## Starter Creation and Maintenance
### Sourdough Bread Guide Approach
The guide provides a straightforward starter protocol using equal
parts flour and water. Creation takes 7-14 days through a
discard-and-feed cycle at 50g flour and 50g water per feeding.
Once active, the starter is maintained at a 1:1:1 ratio either daily
at room temperature or weekly when refrigerated. This method
prioritizes simplicity and reliability for consistent home baking.
