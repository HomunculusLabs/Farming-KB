## Hybrid Approaches
Many experienced growers use both systems in tandem rather than treating them as
mutually exclusive options. Monotubs serve as reliable workhorses for standard bulk
runs of cube varieties, while a Martha tent handles specialty species, experimental
grows, or large-volume harvests of gourmet mushrooms. This division of labor lets you
match the chamber to the crop rather than forcing everything into one system.

Some growers also modify Martha tents with automated controllers (such as Inkbird
thermostats/hygrometers or Arduino-based systems) to reduce the manual tuning burden,
bridging the gap between passive simplicity and active precision. Others use
mini-greenhouses or humidity domes over individual blocks inside a Martha tent to
create microclimates for species that need higher humidity than the rest of the tent.
