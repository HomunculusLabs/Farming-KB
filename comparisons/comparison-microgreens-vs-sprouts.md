## Microgreens vs. Sprouts

## At a Glance

| Feature | Microgreens | Sprouts |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Definition** | Young seedlings harvested 7–21 days after germination, after first true leaves appear | Germinated seeds eaten within 2–7 days, before true leaves develop |
| **What You Eat** | Stem and cotyledon (seed leaf) or first true leaves | Entire sprout — seed, root, shoot |
| **Growing Medium** | Soil, coco coir, hemp mats, hydroponic pads | Water only (no medium) |
| **Light Required** | Yes — needs light for photosynthesis and color | No — grown in dark or low light |
| **Days to Harvest** | 7–21 days | 2–7 days |
| **Food Safety Risk** | Low — clean medium, minimal humidity | Higher — warm, moist conditions promote bacterial growth |
| **Shelf Life** | 5–14 days refrigerated | 3–5 days refrigerated |
| **Nutrient Density** | Very high — 4–40× vitamin levels of mature plants | High in vitamins C and B, enzymes |
| **Flavor** | Intense, concentrated, varies widely by species | Mild, crisp, less variety |
| **Best Species** | Radish, sunflower, pea, basil, cilantro, kale, broccoli | Mung bean, alfalfa, lentil, chickpea, radish, clover |
| **Setup Cost** | $30–150 (trays, medium, light optional) | $10–50 (jar or sprouting bag) |

### Microgreens
1. **Sow** untreated seeds densely on pre-moistened medium in shallow trays.
2. **Cover** for 2–4 days (blackout period) to encourage root establishment.
3. **Expose** to light — plants green up within 24–48 hours.
