# Neem Oil vs Diatomaceous Earth for Cannabis Pest Control

## Overview
Neem oil and diatomaceous earth (DE) are two of the most widely used organic
pest control products in [[diatomaceous-earth-cannabis]]
Diatomaceous earth consists of fossilized skeletal remains of diatoms,
microscopic algae that lived millions of years ago. The silica shells form
razor-sharp particles that control pests through pure mechanical action. The
particles abrade the waxy outer coating on insect exoskeletons, causing body
fluids to leak out and the insect to die from dehydration. When ingested, the
sharp particles rip holes in the pest's digestive tract, providing dual

## When to Choose Diatomaceous Earth
DE is ideal for prevention and early-stage pest control, especially against
soil-dwelling pests like fungus gnat larvae. Its inability to cause resistance
makes it a good rotational partner with neem to prevent pest adaptation. DE is
preferred for growers who want zero chemical or biological residues and are
willing to accept the reapplication labor. The mineral amendment benefits make
it a dual-purpose product for soil-focused organic growers.

## Hybrid Approaches
The most effective organic pest management uses both products in rotation.
Apply DE as a soil topdressing to create a physical barrier against fungus
gnats and crawling pests while using neem oil as a foliar spray during
vegetative growth for systemic protection against mites and aphids. During
flowering, switch exclusively to neem soil drenches and DE topdressing. This
rotation prevents pest adaptation while maintaining continuous multi-modal
protection throughout the entire grow cycle.
