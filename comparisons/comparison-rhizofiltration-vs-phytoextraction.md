# Rhizofiltration vs Phytoextraction

## Overview

Rhizofiltration and phytoextraction are two of the five principal phytoremediation
technologies. Both use plants to remove contaminants from the environment, but they
target fundamentally different environmental matrices: rhizofiltration treats
contaminated water, while phytoextraction treats contaminated soil. Their mechanisms,
timescales, setup requirements, and applications differ substantially. Understanding
these distinctions is critical for selecting the correct approach to any given
contamination scenario, and they are often combined in treatment trains for sites
with complex contamination profiles affecting both soil and groundwater.
