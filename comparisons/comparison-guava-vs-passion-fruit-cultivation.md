## Guava vs Passion Fruit Cultivation

Guava (*Psidium guajava*) and passion fruit (*Passiflora edulis*) are two of the most commercially important tropical and subtropical fruit crops worldwide. Both are valued for their aromatic, vitamin-rich fruit, but they differ markedly in growth habit, cultural requirements, and management practices. This page provides a side-by-side comparison to help growers select and manage these species.
