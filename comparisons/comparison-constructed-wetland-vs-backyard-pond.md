## When to Choose a Constructed Wetland

Choose a constructed wetland when your primary need is water
treatment. This includes treating household wastewater, managing
stormwater runoff from impervious surfaces, polishing effluent from
septic systems, remediating contaminated water from agricultural or
industrial sources, and restoring degraded waterways. Constructed
wetlands are ideal for properties with sufficient land area where
low-energy, chemical-free water treatment aligns with ecological
values.

## When to Choose a Backyard Pond

Choose a backyard pond when your goals center on creating wildlife
habitat, growing ornamental or food fish, storing irrigation water,
enhancing landscape aesthetics, or providing fire suppression water.
Ponds are ideal for smaller properties where the visual and
recreational benefits outweigh treatment requirements. They integrate
well into permaculture zone designs as multi-functional water
features that support overall [[mollison-designers-fish-pond-water-quality-and-aeration]], and the
pond provides additional residence time for biological treatment.
