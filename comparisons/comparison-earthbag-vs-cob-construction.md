## Hybrid Approaches

Many natural builders combine earthbag and cob techniques in the same
building. A common hybrid uses earthbag walls for the lower courses
(foundation to window sill level) where moisture resistance and structural
strength are most critical, then transitions to cob for the upper walls
where sculptural detail and window and door framing are desired. Another
hybrid uses earthbag domes for structural roofs and cob walls for the
living spaces below.

A common hybrid uses earthbag for the first 1-3 courses as a
moisture-resistant base, then transitions to cob above. This eliminates
the need for a conventional concrete foundation and the transition is
straightforward: the bags are tamped and topped with cob that keys into
the irregular bag surface.
