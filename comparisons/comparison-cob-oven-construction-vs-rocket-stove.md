# Cob Oven Construction vs Rocket Stove

## Overview

Cob oven construction and rocket stove design represent two complementary
approaches to wood-fired cooking that serve different culinary needs and
building philosophies. The cob oven is a permanent earthen structure that
stores heat in dense thermal mass for hours of baking after a single firing,
while the rocket stove is an engineered combustion system that maximizes fuel
efficiency through clean-burning technology. Together they form the backbone of
off-grid and homestead cooking infrastructure, with the cob oven handling
bread, pizza, roasting, and slow-cooked dishes through retained heat, and the
rocket stove providing rapid boiling, frying, and daily stovetop cooking with
minimal fuel. Understanding their distinct strengths allows builders to design
outdoor kitchen systems that cover the full spectrum of cooking tasks.
