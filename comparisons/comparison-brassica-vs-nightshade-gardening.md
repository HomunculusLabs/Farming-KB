## Brassica vs Nightshade Gardening

## Overview

Brassicas (cole crops) and nightshades (solanaceous crops) represent
the two most important vegetable families in the home garden, yet they
demand nearly opposite growing conditions. Brassicas thrive in cool
weather while nightshades need warmth. Understanding these contrasts
allows gardeners to plan complementary seasonal plantings that
maximize productivity across the full calendar year.
