# Sprinkler vs Drip Irrigation

## Overview
Sprinkler and drip irrigation are the two most widely used methods of
distributing water to crops, each with distinct advantages depending on farm
scale, crop type, water availability, and soil conditions. Sprinkler systems
distribute water over the soil surface simulating rainfall, while drip
irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone through emitters and
tubing. The choice between them significantly impacts water efficiency, plant
health, disease pressure, and long-term operating costs.

## When to Choose Sprinkler Irrigation
Sprinkler systems are the practical choice for large-scale field crops,
pastures, and orchard floor irrigation where the coverage area makes drip
cost-prohibitive. They excel in situations where frost protection is needed,
