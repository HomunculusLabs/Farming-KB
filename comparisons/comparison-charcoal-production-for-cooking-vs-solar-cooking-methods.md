# Charcoal Production vs Solar Cooking

## Overview

Charcoal production and solar cooking represent two fundamentally different
approaches to off-grid cooking. Charcoal converts biomass into a concentrated
carbon fuel through pyrolysis, yielding a portable, high-heat energy source
that works anywhere, anytime. Solar cooking captures and concentrates sunlight
directly, requiring no fuel at all but depending entirely on weather and
daylight. Understanding their trade-offs helps homesteaders choose the right
method — or combine both — for resilient, low-cost cooking.
