## Drought Gardening vs Rainwater Harvesting

## Overview
Drought gardening and rainwater harvesting address water scarcity from
opposite and complementary directions. Drought gardening, developed by Steve
Solomon, minimizes crop water demand through wide plant spacing, deep root
development, and strategic irrigation timing so vegetables can thrive with
little or no supplemental water. Rainwater harvesting captures, stores, and
redistributes precipitation to ensure a reliable water supply for irrigation
regardless of drought conditions. Both are essential [[drought-tolerant-planting-design]] for plant
selection strategies.
