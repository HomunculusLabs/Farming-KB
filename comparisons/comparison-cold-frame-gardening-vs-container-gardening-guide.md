## When to Choose Cold Frames

Cold frames are the right choice when your priority is extending the growing
season rather than creating growing space. They are essential for gardeners in
colder climates (zones 3-6) who want to start seeds 4-6 weeks earlier in spring,
harvest fresh greens like spinach, lettuce, and kale through winter, or harden
off indoor-grown transplants gradually without the shock of direct outdoor
exposure. Cold frames are also invaluable for overwintering semi-hardy herbs
(rosemary, thyme, sage) and perennials that would otherwise die in the open
garden. They require in-ground garden space but provide the most season
extension per dollar invested of any structure.

## When to Choose Container Gardening

Container gardening is the best choice when you lack in-ground garden space
entirely, such as apartment dwellers with balconies, homeowners with paved
patios, renters who cannot modify the landscape, or anyone with poor,
contaminated, or compacted soil. Containers offer complete control over the
growing medium, making them ideal for acid-loving plants like blueberries and
azaleas that would struggle in alkaline garden soil. They are also portable,
allowing you to move plants to follow the sun, protect from frost by bringing
indoors, or rearrange displays seasonally. Containers are the gateway gardening
method for absolute beginners because the investment risk is minimal.
