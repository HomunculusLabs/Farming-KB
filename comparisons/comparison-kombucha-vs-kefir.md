# Kombucha vs Kefir

## Overview

Kombucha is a tea-based fermented beverage, while kefir is a cultured dairy
or water-based drink, and comparing them helps home fermenters choose the
right approach for their health goals, taste preferences, and available time.

Both beverages are rich in probiotics and organic acids, but they differ
significantly in their fermentation cultures, base ingredients, flavor
profiles, and the daily effort required to produce them.
