## When to Choose Dehydration

Dehydration is the better choice when you need lightweight, portable food for
backpacking, bug-out kits, or long-term calorie-dense storage. It excels with
fruits (apples, bananas, berries), herbs, and lean meats for jerky. Dehydrated
foods take up a fraction of their original volume and weight, making them
ideal when storage space is limited. Solar dehydration provides a truly
zero-energy preservation path in sunny climates. Dehydration is also simpler
to get right for beginners since the main variables are temperature and time,
and failures are usually obvious (mold growth or insufficient drying).
