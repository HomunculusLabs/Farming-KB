## When to Choose Grain Spawn
Grain spawn is the best choice for beginner cultivators and for
projects requiring rapid colonization, such as straw-based oyster
mushroom production or quick compost inoculation. The fast
colonization speed reduces the window of vulnerability to
contamination, and the visual monitoring through glass jars makes it
easy to spot problems early. Grain spawn is also ideal for expanding
mycelial cultures through grain-to-grain transfers, allowing a single
master culture to efficiently multiply into large volumes of spawn.
