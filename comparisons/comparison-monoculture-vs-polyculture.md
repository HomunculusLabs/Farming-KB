## Monoculture vs Polyculture

## Overview

Monoculture grows a single crop species across a field, while polyculture
intermixes multiple species in the same space. The debate between these
approaches touches on yield efficiency, soil health, pest management, and
the philosophical question of whether nature's diversity or human
simplification produces better food systems. Each approach carries trade-
offs that depend on scale, climate, labor availability, and market goals.
