### Expansion Ratios and Generations
A single petri dish can inoculate 6-8 quart jars, designated as G-1 master jars.
Each master jar can subsequently inoculate 10-12 additional quart jars, creating G-2
generation spawn. This exponential expansion chain makes it possible to produce large
volumes of spawn from minimal starting material.
A recommended maximum of 3-4 generations helps avoid senescence, the gradual decline
in mycelial vigor that occurs with repeated subculturing. Exceeding this limit risks
producing weaker, slower-colonizing spawn that performs poorly in fruiting.
### Transfer Protocol and Timing
Timing is critical. Master jars must be transferred within days of reaching full
colonization to ensure maximum vigor. Before transferring, each jar is carefully
scrutinized for contamination indicators including heavy growth in unusual patterns,
slimy kernels, or sour odors.
