# Adobe vs Rammed Earth

## Overview
Adobe and rammed earth are two ancient earth-building techniques that create massive, durable
walls from raw subsoil. Both rely on clay as a binder and produce buildings with outstanding
thermal mass, but they differ fundamentally in their construction process: adobe uses pre-formed
sun-dried bricks laid in courses, while rammed earth compacts damp soil directly between
formwork in monolithic lifts. Choosing between them depends on available labor, soil conditions,
climate, and the desired aesthetic.
