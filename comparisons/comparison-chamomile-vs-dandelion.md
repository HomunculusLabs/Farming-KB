## When to Choose Chamomile

Choose chamomile when you want a dedicated medicinal tea herb with
calming, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties. German
chamomile is ideal for annual herb gardens and cutting gardens
where its prolific self-seeding ensures continuous supply. Roman
chamomile is the choice for permanent ground cover in pathways,
between stepping stones, or as a fragrant lawn alternative. Both
species integrate well into medicinal herb spirals and polyculture
beds where their flowers support pollinators.

## When to Choose Dandelion

plants support overlapping pollinator populations. Harvesting from
both provides a broader range of medicinal compounds and edible
parts throughout the growing season.
