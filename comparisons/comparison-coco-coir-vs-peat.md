## Coco Coir vs Peat

## Overview

Coco coir and peat are two widely used soilless growing media that serve
similar functions in horticulture but differ dramatically in their
environmental impact. Coco coir is a renewable byproduct of coconut
harvesting, while peat is mined from ancient bog ecosystems that take
thousands of years to form. As awareness of peatland destruction grows,
coir has emerged as the leading sustainable alternative for commercial
and home growers alike.

## When to Choose Coco Coir

Coco coir is the clear choice for environmentally conscious growers who
want a high-performance growing medium without contributing to wetland
destruction. It suits hydroponic and container growing systems where
consistent water retention and aeration are critical. Coir is particularly
advantageous for growers who reuse their medium across multiple seasons,
as its lignin-rich fibers maintain structural integrity far longer than
peat. Its near-neutral pH also reduces the need for lime amendments
required by acidic peat-based mixes.
