## When to Choose Bamboo

Choose [[comparison-bamboo-construction-vs-cob-building]] when building in tropical or subtropical climates
where bamboo is locally abundant. It is the superior choice for earthquake-prone
regions due to its flexibility and lightweight nature. Bamboo is ideal when
rapid material turnover is desired, as it regenerates in years rather than
decades.

Bamboo also suits projects prioritizing a minimal carbon footprint, fast
construction timelines, and designs that celebrate natural organic aesthetics.
Its ability to be used for complete structural systems, from foundation to roof,
makes it a comprehensive building solution in the right climate.

## When to Choose Cordwood

Choose cordwood construction when working in temperate climates where thermal
mass and insulation are critical for year-round comfort. Cordwood excels for
owner-builders seeking a simple, learnable technique with low startup costs. It
is the better option when sound insulation, durability, and a solid, permanent
feel are priorities.

Cordwood also suits projects with access to softwood timber and where the heavy,
masonry-like character of the walls is desired. Its thick walls provide natural

incorporate bamboo framing for roof structures, taking advantage of bamboo's
tensile strength for spanning open spaces while using cordwood walls for thermal
performance.
