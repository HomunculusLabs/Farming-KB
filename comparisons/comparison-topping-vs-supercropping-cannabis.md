## Topping vs Supercropping for Cannabis

## Overview
Topping and supercropping are two high-stress training (HST) techniques used
to manipulate cannabis plant structure for increased yield, improved light
distribution, and more manageable canopy height. Topping removes the apical
meristem to create multiple main colas, while supercropping intentionally
damages stems to create strengthened branch joints and horizontal growth. Both
techniques break apical dominance but achieve their goals through
fundamentally different mechanisms, each with distinct advantages, risks, and
ideal application scenarios.
