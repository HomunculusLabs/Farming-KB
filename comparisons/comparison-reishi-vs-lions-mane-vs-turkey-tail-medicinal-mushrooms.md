### Extraction Methods

- **Reishi**: Dual extraction (hot water + ethanol) is essential. Water extracts polysaccharides; ethanol extracts triterpenoids (ganoderic acids). Products using only hot water extraction miss the triterpenoid fraction. Spore oil is a specialty product containing concentrated triterpenoids.
- **Lion's Mane**: Hot water extraction captures β-glucans from fruiting body. Mycelium products (often grown on grain) may contain erinacines but should be tested for β-glucan content. Dual extraction is preferred for full-spectrum products.

## Choosing Between Them

**Choose Reishi for**: chronic stress adaptation, sleep support, liver health, longevity tonification, cardiovascular support, overall immune balancing. Best suited as a daily adaptogen for long-term use.

**Choose Lion's Mane for**: cognitive enhancement, memory support, nerve repair, focus and concentration, mild anxiety/depression, gut-brain axis health. Best for students, aging adults, and those with neurological concerns.

**Choose Turkey Tail for**: robust immune activation, cancer treatment support, chronic viral infections, gut microbiome restoration, post-antibiotic recovery. Best during acute immune challenges or as an adjunct to medical treatment.

**All three together**: Many practitioners recommend stacking these mushrooms for comprehensive immune, cognitive, and adaptogenic support. They work through complementary mechanisms and have no known negative interactions with each other.
