## Establishment Timeline

### Open Pasture

Productive rotational pasture can be established in a single growing season.
Seed-to-graze timelines range from 60-90 days. Infrastructure investments
(fencing, water systems, lane construction) represent the largest upfront
costs. Once established, management focuses on rest periods, stocking rates,
and soil fertility.

### Silvopasture

Establishment is the primary barrier to silvopasture adoption. Tree planting to
functional production typically requires 3-8 years. During establishment, young
trees are extremely vulnerable to livestock damage from browsing, rubbing, and
trampling. Tree shelters, fencing exclusion zones, and careful grazing
management are necessary. Practitioners aligned with [[comparison-rotational-grazing-vs-continuous-grazing]]
principles are understood. Silvopasture adds layers of complexity: tree care
(pruning, thinning, health monitoring), canopy management to balance forage
production with tree growth, and coordination of [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-nutrient-management-ppm]] is also more complex as tree roots compete with
forage for available resources.

## When to Choose Each System

Choose **open pasture** when rapid establishment is needed, capital is limited,
land is flat and open, and livestock production is the sole goal.

Choose **silvopasture** when long-term productivity is a priority, existing
woodland can be integrated, income diversification is desired, climate includes
significant heat stress, and carbon sequestration is a management objective.
