## Fruit Characteristics

**Elderberry** — Clusters of small dark-purple berries. Mild, slightly sweet-tart
flavor; unpalatable raw (must be cooked). Used for syrups, wines, jams,
tinctures, and immune supplements. Yield: 12–15 lbs per mature plant.

**Gooseberry** — Round berries (green, red, or yellow); size of a small grape.
Sharp and tart when underripe; sweet and dessert-quality when fully ripe.
Excellent for pies, preserves, and sauces. Yield: 4–8 lbs per mature plant.

**Currant** — Black, red, or white berries on strigs. [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]]* — improve [[jadam-weed-control-strategies-natural]].

**Currant:**
- *Currant worm / imported currant sawfly* (Nematus ribesii) — primary insect
  pest; multiple generations per season can strip plants. Monitor closely from
  bud break.
- *White pine blister rust* — *Ribes* are an alternate host; black currants are
  the most susceptible. Check local regulations — some areas restrict Ribes
  planting near white pines.
- *Aphids* and *botrytis* (gray mold) can affect developing fruit.
