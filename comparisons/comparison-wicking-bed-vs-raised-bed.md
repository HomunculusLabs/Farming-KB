## Wicking Bed vs Raised Bed

## Overview

Wicking beds and raised beds both elevate the growing surface above ground level,
but they differ fundamentally in how they manage water. Raised beds rely on top-down
watering and free drainage, while wicking beds use a sub-irrigation reservoir that
waters plants from below through capillary action. This distinction makes wicking
beds significantly more water-efficient and lower-maintenance, though they require
more complex construction and are less flexible for certain crops and practices.
