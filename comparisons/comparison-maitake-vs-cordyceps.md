## Maitake vs Cordyceps

## Overview

Maitake (Grifola frondosa) and cordyceps (Cordyceps militaris /
Ophiocordyceps sinensis) are two of the most researched medicinal
mushrooms, yet they occupy distinctly different therapeutic niches.
Maitake is a large, shelf-like polypore that grows on hardwood stumps
in temperate forests, prized as both a culinary delicacy and an immune
potentiator. Cordyceps is a parasitic fungus that colonises insect
larvae, renowned for its energising, adaptogenic, and respiratory
benefits. Understanding their contrasting profiles helps growers and
health practitioners choose the right species for their goals.
