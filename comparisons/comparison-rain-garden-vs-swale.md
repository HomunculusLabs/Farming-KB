A comparison of two water-harvesting earthworks for managing stormwater and rehydrating landscapes.

## Overview

Rain gardens are shallow vegetated depressions designed to capture stormwater runoff from impervious surfaces, filtering pollutants and recharging groundwater through amended soil media. Swales are level channels built on contour that intercept surface water flow across slopes, spreading and infiltrating it to passively irrigate large landscapes.

Both are essential tools in regenerative land management, but they operate at different scales and serve distinct primary functions.
