## Heirloom vs Hybrid Seeds

## Overview

Heirloom and hybrid seeds represent two fundamentally different approaches
to plant breeding, each with distinct advantages for gardeners and farmers.
Heirlooms are open-pollinated varieties passed down through generations,
valued for flavor, genetic diversity, and seed-saving potential. Hybrids
are the result of controlled crosses between two genetically distinct
parent lines, bred for vigor, uniformity, and disease resistance.
Understanding the trade-offs between these two seed types is essential
for anyone making planting decisions, whether for a backyard garden or
a market farm.

Heirloom seeds are the best choice for gardeners who prioritize flavor
above all else. If your goal is to grow the most delicious tomatoes,
peppers, or melons possible, heirlooms offer taste experiences that
