## Bottom Line

- **Want the easiest caffeine plant to grow?** → **Yaupon** — nearly indestructible, cold-hardy, native to the US, minimal care needed
- **Want the strongest caffeine from a holly?** → **Guayusa** — highest caffeine content of the *Ilex* species, plus L-theanine
- **Want a social, cultural beverage tradition?** → **Yerba mate** — millennia of shared drinking culture, distinct smoky flavor
- **Want the greatest variety and processing options?** → **Tea** — unmatched diversity (white/yellow/green/oolong/black/pu-erh), most studied health benefits
- **Want climate resilience?** → **Yaupon** — tolerates extremes that kill all other caffeine plants
- **Want permaculture multi-functionality?** → **Yaupon** (temperate) or **Guayusa** (tropical) — both serve roles beyond beverage production
