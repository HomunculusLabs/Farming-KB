## When to Choose Rammed Earth

Choose rammed earth when architectural refinement and long-term durability are
priorities. The method produces dense, uniform walls with a premium natural
finish that can be left exposed, eliminating the need for render or cladding.
Multi-storey buildings are feasible with rammed earth, making it suitable for
larger homes and small commercial structures where earthbag's single-storey
limitation would be a problem.

Rammed earth performs best in hot, dry climates where its exceptional thermal
mass moderates temperature swings. The method suits owner-builders who want a
refined aesthetic and are willing to invest in proper soil testing, formwork
systems, and compaction equipment. Stabilised rammed earth extends viability
to wetter climates, though this increases cost. For projects where the wall
itself is the finished surface, rammed earth delivers unmatched beauty.

## Hybrid Approaches

Combining elements of both methods is uncommon but possible. Some builders use
earthbag for curved or lower walls and rammed earth for straight, upper
sections where the exposed finish is desired. Another hybrid approach uses
earthbag dome roofs atop rammed earth walls, pairing the structural strengths
of each method. Insulated earthbag variants, using scoria-filled outer bags
with earth-filled inner bags, address the insulation limitation shared by
both methods in cold climates.
