## When to Choose Cob Building

Cob is the right choice when you want maximum design freedom and an organic, sculptural aesthetic. If your building design includes curved walls, rounded corners, built-in shelving, window seats, or other integrated features, cob allows these to be formed directly into the structure without additional framing.

The thick cob walls also provide substantial acoustic insulation, creating quiet, peaceful interior spaces. This makes cob particularly well suited to homes, studios, meditation spaces, and small community buildings where the atmosphere of the space matters.
