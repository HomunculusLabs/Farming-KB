## When to Choose Contour Swale Irrigation Design
Contour swale irrigation design is the better choice when you need engineered, reliable water
delivery to specific crops or areas. Orchard systems, food forests in establishment phase, and
urban stormwater projects all benefit from the additional engineering detail. If your site has
irregular terrain with small valleys or ridges that would break a simple contour line, the
reverse siphon technique alone makes this approach worth considering.

This framework is also essential when your swale installation requires permits or must meet
regulatory standards for stormwater management. The inclusion of overflow structures, sediment
management, and flow rate calculations means the design can be submitted to authorities and
professionally installed. It is the right approach for projects that need to be both functional
and compliant with local water management regulations.
