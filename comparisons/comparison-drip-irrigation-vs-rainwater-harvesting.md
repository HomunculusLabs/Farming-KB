# Drip Irrigation vs Rainwater Harvesting

## Overview
Drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting address the same fundamental challenge of
garden water management from opposite directions. Drip irrigation is an active
delivery technology that distributes water directly to root zones through tubes and
emitters at ten to thirty PSI, reducing water use by thirty to fifty percent compared
to sprinkler systems. Rainwater harvesting is a passive collection and storage system
that captures precipitation from roof surfaces via gutters, stores it in tanks, and
distributes it through gravity or pump with multi-stage filtration. Comparing these
systems matters because they are deeply complementary: harvested rainwater provides
the ideal chlorine-free water source for drip irrigation networks, and together they
form the gold standard for sustainable garden water management.
