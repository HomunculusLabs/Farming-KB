## Gabions vs Check Dams

flow, capture sediment, and rehabilitate degraded landscapes. While they share

Gabions are permeable, allowing water to pass through while trapping sediment

the full width of the gully. The top of the completed gabion must sit above
the maximum observed flood level to prevent water from scouring beneath or
around the structure.

larger stones on the exterior faces and smaller stones filling interior voids.

load. Gabions are built in a stepped sequence from the outlet upstream, with
each subsequent structure placed at the point where the water surface reaches
