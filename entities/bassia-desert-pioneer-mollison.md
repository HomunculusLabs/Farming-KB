## Description

*Bassia* is a genus of flowering plants in the family Amaranthaceae (formerly Chenopodiaceae), comprising numerous species of drought-tolerant shrubs and subshrubs native to arid and semi-arid regions of Australia and elsewhere. Several *Bassia* species function as critical pioneer plants in degraded desert landscapes, playing key roles in soil stabilisation, salt pan reclamation, and [[sisymbrium-officinale]]** (hedge mustard): Of some forage and medicinal value, found mainly in cultivated wheat areas, also spreads dry seed stems as wraiths in dust storms.
