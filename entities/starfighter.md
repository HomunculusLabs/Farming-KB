The strain was developed by Alien Genetics and gained recognition for its exceptionally resinous buds and complex citrus-fuel aroma.

Starfighter typically tests between 18–26% THC with minimal CBD content (<1%).

It has won no major cannabis cups but maintains a devoted following among extract artists and home growers for its heavy trichome production.

The name references both the [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]].

## Lineage

**Parent 1:** Chemdawg 91 × Alien Technology = Alien Dawg (also sold as Chemdawg 91)

**Parent 2:** Lemon OG (Lemon Skunk × OG Kush)

The Chemdawg 91 lineage brings diesel, earthy, and chemically pungent terpenes alongside high THC production.

Lemon OG contributes bright citrus notes and a relaxing indica body effect.

The cross is approximately 60% indica / 40% sativa in its reported effects, though exact ratios vary by phenotype.

Starfighter has been used as a parent in subsequent crosses, most notably **Starfighter OG** and **[[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] to olive, with occasional purple highlights in cooler conditions.

The most notable visual feature is the extraordinary trichome coverage — buds appear frosted or sugar-coated even under modest magnification.

Pistils are orange to amber, relatively sparse compared to the dominant [[cannabis-solvent-extraction]].

## Aroma and Flavor

The dominant aroma is a sharp **lemon diesel** — bright citrus peel layered over pungent fuel and skunk undertones.

Secondary notes include earthy pine, woody spice, and a faint chemical tang inherited from the Chemdawg parent.

When ground, the aroma intensifies significantly, releasing more of the fuel and citrus terpenes.

The flavor mirrors the aroma: lemon and diesel on the inhale, with an earthy, slightly spicy exhale.

Some phenotypes express more of the OG Kush backend, adding notes of pine and soil.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- **Limonene** — primary citrus contributor, typically 0.5–1.0%
- **Myrcene** — sedative, herbal base note
- **Caryophyllene** — peppery, woody spice

Secondary terpenes include pinene, linalool, and humulene in trace amounts.

**Cannabinoid profile:**

- **THC:** 18–26% (average ~22%)
- **CBD:** <1% (typically <0.1%)
- **CBG:** <0.5%
- **THCV:** trace
