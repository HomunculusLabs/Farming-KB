# Do-Si-Dos

## Overview

**Do-Si-Dos** is a premium indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its exceptionally high THC content, sedative body effects, and complex terpene profile. Created by crossing **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** with **Face Off OG**, Do-Si-Dos was bred by **Archive Seed Bank** and has become one of the most sought-after cultivars in the modern cannabis market since its emergence in the mid-2010s. The strain's name is a playful reference to the do-si-do folk dance, evoking its ability to leave users feeling "spun" or deeply relaxed.

Do-Si-Dos is celebrated for its visually striking, frosty, trichome-dense buds that range in color from deep forest green to rich lavender and purple. Its aroma is a pungent blend of sweet floral notes, earthy musk, and sharp fuel undertones, reflecting its prestigious Cookie family lineage. With THC levels routinely testing between 19% and 30%, Do-Si-Dos is considered a heavy-hitter best suited for experienced consumers seeking potent therapeutic effects.

## Genetic Lineage

### Parent Strains

| Parent | Type | Origin | Key Traits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Girl Scout Cookies (GSC) | Indica-dominant hybrid (60/40) | Cookie Family, San Francisco | Sweet, earthy aroma; balanced high; legendary clone |
| Face Off OG | Indica (85/15) | OG Kush phenotype, Southern California | Potent sedative effects; piney-lemon fuel aroma |

### Breeder and History

Do-Si-Dos was created by **Archive Seed Bank**, founded by **Fernando (DJ Short's son)** and based in Portland, Oregon. Archive Seed Bank is renowned for working with elite OG Kush and Cookie Family genetics, and Do-Si-Dos represents one of their flagship crosses. The strain gained significant attention after winning or placing in multiple cannabis competitions in California, Oregon, and Colorado. It has since become a foundational parent in its own right, used to create popular derivatives such as **Do-Si-Dos OG**, **Slurricane** (Do-Si-Dos × Purple Punch), and **Runtz**-adjacent crosses.

### Genetic Stability

Do-Si-Dos is available from multiple seed banks, with some phenotypic variation between breeders. Archive Seed Bank's original cut is considered the gold standard. The strain breeds relatively true, with most phenotypes expressing the characteristic dense bud structure, purple coloration potential, and high resin production. As with most polyhybrid cannabis strains, individual seed-grown plants may show some variation in growth characteristics and exact terpene ratios.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma (Pre-Combustion)

Do-Si-Dos produces a complex, multi-layered aroma that is immediately recognizable to experienced cannabis consumers:

- **Primary:** Sweet, floral, and earthy — a direct inheritance from GSC
- **Secondary:** Pungent, skunky, and musky undertones from Face Off OG
- **Tertiary:** Notes of diesel fuel, pine, and occasionally a subtle lime or citrus sharpness
- **Intensity:** Very strong; the aroma is perceptible from several feet away in unopened containers

### Flavor (Combustion/Vaporization)

- **Inhalation:** Smooth and creamy on the palate, with dominant sweet floral and earthy flavors. The diesel-fuel notes emerge mid-draw.
- **Exhalation:** Lingering sweetness with a slightly spicy, peppery finish and an aftertaste reminiscent of fresh soil and lavender.
- **Smoke characteristics:** Dense, white, and expansive. The smoke is generally smooth but can provoke coughing in larger hits due to high resin content.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Typical Range | Aroma Contribution | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Myrcene | 0.5–1.2% | Earthy, musky, herbal | Dominant terpene; contributes sedative effects |
| Limonene | 0.3–0.8% | Citrus, sweet, fruity | Mood elevation; stress relief |
| Linalool | 0.2–0.6% | Floral, lavender, sweet | Anxiolytic; synergizes with sedative effects |
| Caryophyllene | 0.2–0.5% | Pepper, spicy, woody | Anti-inflammatory; binds CB2 receptors |
| Humulene | 0.1–0.3% | Earthy, woody, hoppy | Appetite suppression; anti-inflammatory |
| Pinene (α + β) | Trace | Pine, fresh, sharp | Counteracts some sedative effects |

The high linalool content, combined with dominant myrcene, is responsible for Do-Si-Dos' characteristic floral-sedative profile and distinguishes it from more purely fuel-scented OG varieties.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 19–30% (most commercial samples 22–28%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3%
- **CBC:** Trace amounts
- **THCV:** Typically not detected

The strain is a high-THC, low-CBD chemotype typical of modern Cookie and OG Kush descendants. Potency can vary significantly based on growing conditions, harvest timing, and curing methods.

## Effects

### Onset and Duration

- **Onset:** Rapid, typically felt within 2–5 minutes of inhalation
- **Peak effects:** 30–60 minutes post-consumption
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours depending on dose and individual tolerance

### Reported Effects Profile

| Effect Category | Intensity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Euphoria | Strong | Uplifting cerebral happiness in early phase |
| Relaxation | Very strong | Deep physical relaxation spreading from limbs to core |
| Sedation | Strong | Progressively couch-locking; may lead to sleep |
| Creativity | Mild to moderate | Brief creative spark during onset before sedation |
| Appetite | Strong | Significant munchies effect |

### Medical Applications (Anecdotal)

Patients and medical cannabis users report using Do-Si-Dos for:

- Chronic pain management (muscle spasms, neuropathic pain, joint inflammation)
- Insomnia and sleep disorders (particularly as a nighttime strain)
- Anxiety and stress relief (in moderate doses; high doses may increase anxiety in sensitive users)
- Nausea and appetite stimulation (useful during chemotherapy or for eating disorders)
- PTSD symptom management (particularly for sleep-related symptoms)

## Cultivation Notes

### Growth Characteristics

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoors
- **Indoor yield:** 12–16 oz/m² under optimal conditions
- **Outdoor yield:** 16–20 oz/plant
- **Plant height:** Medium; tends to stay compact with minimal stretching
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; benefits from experience with Cookie-family genetics

### Environmental Preferences

- **Temperature:** Prefers moderate temperatures (65–80°F / 18–27°C) during flowering. Cooler nighttime temperatures (55–65°F) during late flowering enhance purple coloration.
- **Humidity:** Moderate (40–55% RH during flowering); dense bud structure makes the strain susceptible to bud rot if humidity exceeds 60% in late flowering.
- **Nutrients:** Moderate to heavy feeder; responds well to increased phosphorus and potassium during weeks 4–8 of flowering for resin production.
- **Training:** Responds well to low-stress training (LST), topping, and ScrOG (Screen of Green) methods to maximize light penetration into the dense canopy.

### Pests and Diseases

The dense, resinous bud structure of Do-Si-Dos requires vigilant humidity management. Bud rot (Botrytis cinerea) and powdery mildew are the primary concerns. Adequate airflow, spacing between colas, and dehumidification are essential, especially in the final 2–3 weeks of flowering.

## Popular Derivatives and Crosses

- **Do-Si-Dos OG:** Do-Si-Dos × OGKB (even heavier indica profile)
- **Slurricane:** Do-Si-Dos × Purple Punch ( fruity, deeply sedating)
- **Do-Si-Dos #22:** Select phenotype with enhanced terpene production
- **Peanut Butter Breath:** Do-Si-Dos × Mendo Breath (nutty, sedating)
