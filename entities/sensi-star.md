## Overview

Sensi Star is an indica-dominant cannabis strain originally bred by Paradise Seeds in the Netherlands.

It won the High Times Cannabis Cup in 1999, establishing its reputation among elite legacy varieties.

The strain is known for its exceptionally resinous buds and potent, sedating effects.

Its genetic lineage traces predominantly to Afghani landrace indica varieties.

## Lineage

Sensi Star is derived from an undisclosed Afghani indica selection.

The breeder, Paradise Seeds, has not released the exact parent cross publicly.

Genetic analysis suggests a roughly 90:10 indica-to-sativa ratio.

Afghani heritage contributes the strain's dense bud structure, resin production, and relaxing effects.

## Appearance

Buds are compact, dense, and heavy for their size, typical of Afghani-descended indicas.

The calyxes are tightly packed with a thick coating of trichomes that gives them a frosty appearance.

Colors range from deep green to slightly purple, especially in cooler growing conditions.

Pistils are orange to amber, contrasting against the dark foliage.

The overall impression is of a premium, high-resin flower with significant bag appeal.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is pungent and earthy, with strong notes of citrus and skunk.

A sweet, herbal undertone is present, especially when the buds are ground.

On combustion, the flavor is smooth with earthy, hash-like qualities.

Citrus and pepper notes emerge on the exhale, with a lingering herbal aftertaste.

The terpene profile is dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and limonene.

## Cannabinoid Profile

THC content typically ranges from 18% to 23%, placing it in the high-potency category.

CBD levels are generally low, usually below 0.5%.

Some phenotypes have tested above 25% THC in competitive grows.

The high myrcene content contributes to the strain's sedative character.

## Effects

The onset is relatively fast, beginning with a cerebral euphoria that transitions to deep body relaxation.

Users report significant stress relief, pain management, and appetite stimulation.

The sedative effects intensify over time, making it popular as an evening or bedtime strain.

At higher doses, couch-lock is common, and sleep may follow within 1–2 hours.

Medicinal users value Sensi Star for insomnia, chronic pain, anxiety, and muscle spasms.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness at higher doses.

Inexperienced users may find the potency overwhelming.

## Cultivation

Sensi Star is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, suitable for growers with some experience.

Indoor flowering time is approximately 8–9 weeks from the onset of the 12/12 light cycle.

Outdoor harvest occurs in late September to early October in the Northern Hemisphere.

Yields are above average at 400–500 g/m² indoors under optimal conditions.

The plants are relatively compact and bushy, reaching 80–120 cm indoors.

They respond well to training techniques such as topping and low-stress training.

The strain is resistant to mold but can be sensitive to over-fertilization.

## Notable Phenotypes

Several distinct phenotypes of Sensi Star have emerged through selective breeding.

The "Paradise Seeds" original cut remains the most sought-after for its consistent potency.

Some growers have crossed Sensi Star with Skunk #1 to increase yield and branch stretching.

A purple phenotype is occasionally expressed in cooler nighttime temperatures.

This variant shows enhanced anthocyanin production with comparable cannabinoid profiles.

Sensi Star has also been used as a parent in crosses such as Starberry and Star Killer.

These crosses aim to retain the resin production and potency while modifying the flavor profile.
