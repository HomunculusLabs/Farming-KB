## Runtz (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

**Runtz** is an award-winning, highly sought-after [[girl-scout-cookies]] |
| **Genetic Background** | [[thin-mint-cookies]] × [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]], lavender purple, and electric blue — purple expression varies by phenotype. Heavy trichome coverage gives a frosted appearance
- **Pistils:** Abundant orange-amber pistils curling through the calyxes
- **Resin:** Exceptionally sticky with thick clear-to-milky trichome heads

Several sub-lineages exist: **White Runtz**, **Pink Runtz**, and **Black Runtz**, each with slightly different color profiles and terpene ratios.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Burst of tropical fruit (mango, pineapple, ripe banana) over a creamy, cookie-dough base. Undertones of berry, citrus, and earthy funk
- **Flavor:** Remarkably smooth, sweet smoke — candied tropical fruit, cream, and berry with a slightly herbal, peppery finish from caryophyllene
- **Aftertaste:** Lingering sweet, fruity finish — "smoking candy"

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate % | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| **Limonene** | 0.5–1.2% | Dominant citrus/tropical fruit character |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.8% | Peppery spice, anti-inflammatory |
| **Linalool** | 0.2–0.6% | Floral, lavender — promotes relaxation |
| **Myrcene** | 0.2–0.5% | Herbal, earthy — sedative at high levels |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.3% | Woody, hoppy — appetite suppressant |

## Cannabinoid Profile

## Effects

### Reported Effects (user consensus)
- **Euphoria:** Strong cerebral uplift, mood elevation, giddy happiness
- **Relaxation:** Full-body calm without couch-lock
- **Creativity:** Enhanced creative thinking and sociability
- **[[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]

### Onset and Duration
- **Onset:** 2–5 minutes (smoked/vaped); **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; **Duration:** 2–3 hours

### Potential Side Effects
Dry mouth, dry eyes, anxiety at high doses, dizziness, lethargy with heavy use.
