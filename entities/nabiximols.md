# Nabiximols

**Type:** Chemical / Pharmaceutical / Botanical Drug
**Category:** Whole-plant cannabis extract
**Also known as:** Sativex

## Overview

Nabiximols is a standardized whole-plant cannabis extract developed by GW
Pharmaceuticals (now Jazz Pharmaceuticals). Marketed under the brand name
Sativex, it is one of only three FDA-approved cannabinoid-based
pharmaceuticals alongside dronabinol and nabilone. Unlike those synthetic
compounds, nabiximols contains a full spectrum of cannabis-derived
cannabinoids and terpenes.

Approved in over 25 countries for spasticity associated with multiple
sclerosis, nabiximols represents a pharmaceutical approach to medical
cannabis. It is delivered via an oromucosal spray absorbed through the
buccal mucosa, allowing rapid onset without pulmonary exposure.

## Composition and Chemistry

Nabiximols is formulated as a 1:1 ratio of two principal cannabinoids:
delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) at 27 mg/mL and cannabidiol (CBD) at
25 mg/mL. The extract also contains trace cannabinoids and terpenes
preserved from the original plant material.

The 1:1 THC:[[cannabis-thc-cbd-ratio-guide-for-conditions]] is therapeutically significant. CBD modulates the
psychoactive effects of THC, reducing intoxication, anxiety, and cognitive
impairment while preserving analgesic and anti-spastic properties. This
[[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]] is a key pharmacological advantage over isolated THC
(dronabinol), which produces stronger psychoactive side effects at
equivalent doses.

The formulation uses ethanol and propylene glycol as a vehicle, with
peppermint flavoring to mask the bitter cannabis taste.

## Pharmacology

### Mechanism of Action

Nabiximols acts through the endocannabinoid system, primarily targeting
CB1 receptors (THC) and CB2 receptors (CBD, THC). THC is a partial agonist
at both receptors, producing analgesia, anti-spasticity, and psychoactive
effects. CBD modulates THC activity and acts independently as a 5-HT1A
agonist, GPR55 antagonist, and TRPV1 agonist.

The dual-receptor approach provides broader therapeutic coverage than
single-cannabinoid pharmaceuticals. CB1-mediated effects reduce spasticity
and pain signaling, while CB2-mediated effects address neuroinflammation.

### Pharmacokinetics

- Administration: Oromucosal spray (buccal absorption)
- Onset: 15-40 minutes
- Peak plasma: 1-2 hours
- Duration: 2-4 hours per dose
- Half-life: THC 1-2 hours; CBD 2-5 hours
- Metabolism: Hepatic (CYP2C9, CYP3A4)
- Excretion: Fecal and renal

## Clinical Applications

### Multiple Sclerosis Spasticity

Nabiximols is primarily prescribed for treatment-resistant spasticity in
MS patients. [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]] demonstrate significant reductions in spasm
frequency, severity, and patient-reported spasticity scores.

### Cancer Pain

Approved in several countries (Canada, EU) as an adjunctive treatment for
moderate-to-severe cancer pain unresponsive to opioid monotherapy. Studies
show modest but clinically meaningful pain reduction, often allowing opioid
dose reduction.

### Investigational Indications

- Neuropathic pain (diabetic, HIV-associated)
- Rheumatoid arthritis pain
- Chemotherapy-induced nausea
- Post-traumatic stress disorder

## Administration and Dosage

### Dosing Protocol

- Starting dose: 1 spray (100 µL delivering 2.7 mg THC + 2.5 mg CBD)
- Titration: 1 additional spray every 1-2 hours as needed
- Maximum: 12 sprays per day (32.4 mg THC + 30 mg CBD)
- Administration: Spray onto buccal mucosa; do not swallow for 1-2 minutes

Patients are titrated over 1-2 weeks to identify the optimal dose
balancing symptom relief with tolerability. Most patients achieve
sufficient relief at 4-8 sprays per day.

## Safety and Side Effects

### Common Adverse Effects

- Dry mouth (most frequent, reported in 30-40% of patients)
- Dizziness and fatigue
- Nausea and dysgeusia (taste disturbance)
- Application site discomfort (oral mucosa irritation)

### Psychoactive Effects

The THC component produces dose-dependent psychoactive effects including
euphoria, drowsiness, and mild cognitive impairment. The CBD component
partially mitigates these effects, but some patients experience
intoxication at higher doses. Driving should be avoided during dose
titration.

### Contraindications

Nabiximols is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to
cannabinoids, severe cardiovascular disease, schizophrenia or psychotic
disorders, and pregnancy. Caution with concurrent CNS depressants.
