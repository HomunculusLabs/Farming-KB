## Taxonomy and Morphology

- **Kingdom:** Plantae
- **Division:** Magnoliophyta
- **Class:** Magnoliopsida
- **Order:** Asterales
- **Family:** Asteraceae
- **Genus:** *Tagetes*
- **Species:** *T. minuta* L.
- **Synonyms:** *Tagetes glandulifera* Schrank, *Tagetes porophyllum* Vell.

**Growth habit:** Erect annual, 0.5–2 m tall, much-branched and bushy.
Strongly aromatic foliage with a complex mint-anise-citrus scent.

**Leaves:** Pinnately compound, 5–20 cm long, with 5–13 pairs of
lanceolate to serrate leaflets (2–6 cm × 3–10 mm). Gland-dotted,
releasing aromatic oils when crushed. Dark green, sometimes with
purple-tinged veins.

**Stem:** Angular, ribbed, green to purple, glabrous to sparsely hairy.
Glandular surfaces produce essential oils.

**Flowers:** Small, yellow-orange, in dense corymbose clusters at
branch tips. Each head 8–12 mm diameter, with 3–5 ray florets and
8–15 disc florets. Blooms late summer to autumn. Self-pollinating.

**Root:** Fibrous, 30–50 cm deep. [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]]. Invasive in parts of Australia,
eastern Africa, and India.

**Habitat:** Roadsides, waste ground, cultivated fields, and disturbed
areas. Tolerates wide soil range (sandy loam to [[cannabis-controlled-pollination-seed-production]] and allelopathic root
exudates can suppress native vegetation.
