# Casuarina cunninghamii

## Description

*[[eucalyptus-camaldulensis]]* to utilize surplus sewage or wastewater in lowland areas. It is one of several species identified for strategic planting to lower water tables and prevent [[permaculture]] dryland design: nitrogen fixation for soil improvement, high transpiration for water table management, wastewater processing capability, windbreak function, and timber and fuel production. Its specific recommendation for lowland wastewater sites alongside *E. camaldulensis* provides a proven species combination for designers working with interceptor bank systems where surplus fresh water becomes available.
