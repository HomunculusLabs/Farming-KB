# Biscotti

## Overview

Biscotti is a highly potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain (approximately 80% indica / 20% sativa) created by crossing Gelato #25 with South Florida OG. Developed by the Cookie Family and popularized through the Bay Area cannabis scene, Biscotti has become one of the most sought-after "dessert" strains of the 2020s. Named for the Italian almond biscuit, the strain is known for its intensely sweet, cookie-like aroma, deeply relaxing body effects, and exceptional resin production that makes it a favorite among hash makers and extract artists. THC content typically ranges from 21% to 30%, placing it among the strongest strains available.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Cross:** Gelato #25 × South Florida OG
- **Family tree:** Biscotti inherits the Cookies lineage through Gelato #25 ([[sunset-sherbet]] × Thin Mint GSC), combined with the potent OG backbone of South Florida OG
- **Breeder:** Originally developed by the Cookie Family / Berner's Cookies genetic program
- **Notable offspring:** Biscotti has become a foundational parent strain itself, used to create numerous successful crosses including:
  - **Biscotti Sundae** (Biscotti × Strawberry Sundae)
  - **Biscotti Pancakes** (Biscotti × [[london-pound-cake]])
  - **Gelonade × Biscotti** crosses
  - **Biscotti Breath** (Biscotti × Mendo Breath)
  - **Jealousy** (Sherbet Bx1 × Gelato 41, with Biscotti in extended lineage)
- **[[cannabis-genetic-stability]]:** Relatively stable for F1 hybrids; expresses consistent [[cannabis-terpene-profiles-sativa]] and flower structure across phenotypes, though color variation is significant

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma

- Dominant sweet cookie and diesel notes on initial impression
- Layered complexity: fresh-baked dough, nutty almond, and sweet vanilla underneath the primary cookie aroma
- Undertones of earthy spice, pepper, and subtle herbal notes
- When ground, releases additional sweet cherry and berry aromas inherited from the Sunset Sherbet lineage
- Intensity increases significantly during curing, with peak aroma developing at 4–8 weeks post-harvest

### Flavor

- Smooth, sweet cookie-dough flavor on inhale with a creamy vanilla finish
- Exhale brings out diesel and nutty notes with a lingering sweet aftertaste
- Slightly spicy and herbal on the palate, with a faintly woody finish
- One of the most dessert-like flavor profiles in the Cookies genetic line
- Vaporization at lower temperatures (175–185°C / 347–365°F) best preserves the sweet terpene character

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene** (dominant): Citrus notes, mood elevation, stress relief, potential anti-anxiety effects; contributes to the bright, sweet character
- **Caryophyllene** (secondary): Peppery, spicy undertones; [[beta-caryophyllene-dietary-cannabinoid]] that binds CB2 receptors; anti-inflammatory properties
- **Linalool** (secondary): Floral, lavender-like notes; anxiolytic and sedative properties; enhances the relaxing body effects
- **Myrcene** (supporting): Earthy, musky base notes; synergistic with THC for sedative and analgesic effects; contributes to the "couch-lock" potential
- **Humulene** (trace): Woody, earthy notes; appetite suppressant and anti-inflammatory
- **Pinene** (trace): Piney sharpness; may counteract some cognitive impairment from THC; bronchodilator properties

## Effects

### Onset and Character

- Onset is relatively rapid, 5–10 minutes after inhalation, beginning with a cerebral euphoria that settles into deep physical relaxation
- Initial head high is euphoric, slightly spacey, and mood-elevating without being overwhelming
- Transition to body effects is smooth but pronounced: heavy limbs, warm relaxation spreading from the chest outward, and significant tension relief
- At higher doses, effects become strongly sedative with pronounced couch-lock potential

### Medical Applications

- **Stress and anxiety:** Potent anxiolytic effects make it effective for acute stress relief and generalized anxiety management
- **Pain management:** Strong analgesic properties useful [[cannabis-for-chronic-pain]], muscle spasms, tension headaches, and neuropathic pain
- **Insomnia:** Heavy sedative effects at moderate-to-high doses promote sleep onset and maintenance
- **[[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]:** Reliable appetite enhancer, useful for cachexia, nausea, and appetite loss
