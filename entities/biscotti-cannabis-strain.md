# Biscotti

## Overview

Biscotti is a highly potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain (approximately 80% indica / 20% sativa) created by crossing Gelato #25 with South Florida OG. Developed by the Cookie Family and popularized through the Bay Area cannabis scene, Biscotti has become one of the most sought-after "dessert" strains of the 2020s. Named for the Italian almond biscuit, the strain is known for its intensely sweet, cookie-like aroma, deeply relaxing body effects, and exceptional resin production that makes it a favorite among hash makers and extract artists. THC content typically ranges from 21% to 30%, placing it among the strongest strains available.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Cross:** Gelato #25 × South Florida OG
- **Family tree:** Biscotti inherits the Cookies lineage through Gelato #25 (Sunset Sherbet × Thin Mint GSC), combined with the potent OG backbone of South Florida OG
- **Breeder:** Originally developed by the Cookie Family / Berner's Cookies genetic program
- **Notable offspring:** Biscotti has become a foundational parent strain itself, used to create numerous successful crosses including:
  - **Biscotti Sundae** (Biscotti × Strawberry Sundae)
  - **Biscotti Pancakes** (Biscotti × London Pound Cake)
  - **Gelonade × Biscotti** crosses
  - **Biscotti Breath** (Biscotti × Mendo Breath)
  - **Jealousy** (Sherbet Bx1 × Gelato 41, with Biscotti in extended lineage)
- **Genetic stability:** Relatively stable for F1 hybrids; expresses consistent terpene profiles and flower structure across phenotypes, though color variation is significant

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma

- Dominant sweet cookie and diesel notes on initial impression
- Layered complexity: fresh-baked dough, nutty almond, and sweet vanilla underneath the primary cookie aroma
- Undertones of earthy spice, pepper, and subtle herbal notes
- When ground, releases additional sweet cherry and berry aromas inherited from the Sunset Sherbet lineage
- Intensity increases significantly during curing, with peak aroma developing at 4–8 weeks post-harvest

### Flavor

- Smooth, sweet cookie-dough flavor on inhale with a creamy vanilla finish
- Exhale brings out diesel and nutty notes with a lingering sweet aftertaste
- Slightly spicy and herbal on the palate, with a faintly woody finish
- One of the most dessert-like flavor profiles in the Cookies genetic line
- Vaporization at lower temperatures (175–185°C / 347–365°F) best preserves the sweet terpene character

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene** (dominant): Citrus notes, mood elevation, stress relief, potential anti-anxiety effects; contributes to the bright, sweet character
- **Caryophyllene** (secondary): Peppery, spicy undertones; dietary cannabinoid that binds CB2 receptors; anti-inflammatory properties
- **Linalool** (secondary): Floral, lavender-like notes; anxiolytic and sedative properties; enhances the relaxing body effects
- **Myrcene** (supporting): Earthy, musky base notes; synergistic with THC for sedative and analgesic effects; contributes to the "couch-lock" potential
- **Humulene** (trace): Woody, earthy notes; appetite suppressant and anti-inflammatory
- **Pinene** (trace): Piney sharpness; may counteract some cognitive impairment from THC; bronchodilator properties

## Effects

### Onset and Character

- Onset is relatively rapid, 5–10 minutes after inhalation, beginning with a cerebral euphoria that settles into deep physical relaxation
- Initial head high is euphoric, slightly spacey, and mood-elevating without being overwhelming
- Transition to body effects is smooth but pronounced: heavy limbs, warm relaxation spreading from the chest outward, and significant tension relief
- At higher doses, effects become strongly sedative with pronounced couch-lock potential

### Medical Applications

- **Stress and anxiety:** Potent anxiolytic effects make it effective for acute stress relief and generalized anxiety management
- **Pain management:** Strong analgesic properties useful for chronic pain, muscle spasms, tension headaches, and neuropathic pain
- **Insomnia:** Heavy sedative effects at moderate-to-high doses promote sleep onset and maintenance
- **Appetite stimulation:** Reliable appetite enhancer, useful for cachexia, nausea, and appetite loss
- **Mood disorders:** Euphoric onset can provide temporary relief from depressive symptoms and low mood
- **Cautions:** Due to high THC content, novice consumers may experience anxiety, paranoia, or dizziness; start with low doses (1–2 puffs) and wait 15 minutes before redosing

## Phenotype Variations

Biscotti expresses moderate phenotypic variation, particularly in coloration:

- **Phenotype A (Cookie-dominant):** Dense, rounded buds with deep purple and dark green coloring; most cookie-forward aroma; highest resin production; most commercially common
- **Phenotype B (OG-dominant):** Slightly more elongated bud structure with more green than purple; stronger diesel and earthy notes; more uplifting cerebral effects; slightly taller plant structure
- **Phenotype C (Sherbet-influenced):** Brighter coloration with occasional pink and orange pistils; sweeter berry and cherry notes; balanced hybrid effects; moderate resin production
- All phenotypes share the characteristic dense bud structure, heavy trichome coverage, and high potency

## Cultivation

### Growing Conditions

- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; requires attention to environmental control and nutrient management
- **Indoor:** Best results indoors with controlled environment; flowering time 8–10 weeks; responds well to SCROG (screen of green) training to manage height and maximize light exposure
- **Outdoor:** Suitable for warm, Mediterranean climates; harvest early to mid-October in the Northern Hemisphere; requires protection from excessive rain and humidity during late flowering
- **Yield:** Moderate, approximately 300–400 g/m² indoors; 400–500 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** 90–150 cm indoors; up to 200 cm outdoors

### Environmental Preferences

- Temperature: 20–28°C (68–82°F) during vegetative growth; 18–26°C (64–79°F) during flowering; cooler night temperatures (15–18°C / 59–64°F) during weeks 5–8 of flowering enhance purple coloration
- Humidity: 50–60% vegetative, reducing to 40–45% during flowering to prevent bud rot and powdery mildew
- Light: High-intensity lighting recommended; responds well to LED systems at 600–1000 µmol/m²/s during flowering
- Nutrients: Moderate-to-heavy feeder during vegetative stage; reduce nitrogen and increase phosphorus/potassium during weeks 4–8 of flowering; calcium and magnesium supplementation recommended

### Harvest and Processing

- Optimal harvest window: 8–10 weeks flowering; trichomes should be predominantly milky-white with 10–20% amber for peak potency and flavor balance
- Drying: 10–14 days at 16–18°C (60–64°F), 55–60% humidity; slow drying preserves terpene integrity
- Curing: Minimum 4 weeks in airtight glass jars, burping daily for the first 2 weeks; extended cure (8+ weeks) dramatically improves flavor complexity and smoothness
- Trim level: Hand-trimmed recommended for maximum bag appeal; dense structure requires careful trimming to avoid damaging trichome-covered calyxes

## Extract and Concentrate Applications

Biscotti is highly valued by extract artists due to exceptional resin production and complex terpene profile:
- Produces premium-quality rosin at moderate yields; bubble hash washes well with high melt potential
- Live resin extractions capture the full sweet cookie and diesel terpene spectrum
- Used as a flavor-contributing parent in numerous solventless hash blends
- High terpene content (2.5–4% total terpenes) makes it suitable for full-spectrum extract formulations
