## Overview

*Garcinia atroviridis* is a medium-sized evergreen tree in the family
Clusiaceae, native to Peninsular Malaysia, Thailand, and Myanmar. It is
commonly known as asam gelugur (Malay) or asam keping (Indonesian).

The species is valued primarily for its sour fruit slices, which are sun-dried
and used extensively in [[mollison-specialist-tropical-crops-forest-products]] in Malaysia.

In traditional medicine, the fruit, leaves, and bark are used to treat digestive
disorders, reduce body weight, and manage blood sugar levels. Modern research
has confirmed several of these bioactivities.

## Taxonomy and Morphology

- Kingdom: Plantae; Order: Malpighiales; Family: Clusiaceae
- Genus: *Garcinia*; Species: *G. atroviridis* Griff. ex T. Anderson, 1874

The tree grows 10–25 m tall with a dense, rounded crown. Bark is dark brown to
nearly black, rough and fissured, exuding yellowish latex when cut.

Leaves are opposite, elliptic to oblong, 12–25 × 5–12 cm, with a glossy dark
green upper surface and paler underside. Leaf margins are entire and slightly
revolute.

Flowers are dioecious, borne in axillary clusters of 3–7. Male flowers are
reddish-pink, 8–12 mm diameter, with 4 petals and numerous stamens. Female
flowers are slightly larger and solitary or in pairs.

Fruit is a globose to ovoid berry, 6–8 cm diameter, turning from green to
deep orange-yellow at maturity. The fruit contains 5–7 segments of acidic,
yellowish pulp surrounding several seeds.

Dried fruit slices (asam keping) are flat, dark brown to black, with a
characteristically shiny surface. They are the primary commercial form of the
species, sold in markets throughout Southeast Asia.

## Distribution and Habitat

*G. atroviridis* is native to Peninsular Thailand, Peninsular Malaysia, and
southern Myanmar. It occurs naturally in lowland and hill dipterocarp forests
at elevations up to 600 m.

The tree prefers well-drained soils in humid [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]] with *Garcinia* extracts show mixed results.

Antioxidant activity of fruit extracts is strong, with IC50 values in DPPH
assays comparable to standard antioxidants. Xanthones isolated from the bark
exhibit cytotoxic activity against several [[blesching-cannabis-apoptosis-and-cancer-cell-death]] lines in vitro.
