# Super Boof

## Overview

Super Boof is a modern [[tropicana-cookies]]** (male), combining indica-dominant relaxation with
sativa-influenced cerebral stimulation. It has won or placed in multiple
cannabis competitions, including the Emerald Cup and The Weedmaps
Cannabis Cup.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Black Cherry Punch — [[miracle-alien-cookies]] × (Girl Scout
  Cookies × Tangie). Contributes [[fukuoka-mandarin-orange-citrus]] terpenes, dense bud
  structure, and uplifting mental effects.

The approximate genetic ratio is roughly 60/40 indica-to-sativa,
though phenotypic expression varies significantly between seeds.

## Appearance

Super Boof produces medium-to-large, dense, conical buds with a
chunky, indica-like structure. The most striking visual feature is the
color: deep purples, dark greens, and vivid orange pistils, often with
patches of black or deep magenta. Trichome coverage is heavy, giving
flowers a frosted, almost iridescent appearance.

Color expression is influenced by temperature — cooler nighttime
temperatures during late flowering bring out the deepest purple hues,
while warmer conditions produce green-dominant phenotypes.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Primary notes:** Black cherry, orange citrus, tropical fruit.
**Secondary notes:** Earthy undertones, hashy gas, subtle spice.

The aroma opens with pronounced black cherry sweetness overlaid with
bright orange and tropical citrus. On breaking apart buds, an earthy,
slightly gassy hash note emerges. The flavor on inhalation is sweet
cherry-citrus; the exhale reveals earthy, peppery undertones with
lingering tropical sweetness.

## Terpene Profile

- **Caryophyllene** (dominant, ~0.8–1.2%) — peppery, anti-
  inflammatory properties
- **[[limonene]]** (secondary, ~0.5–0.8%) — citrus brightness, mood
  elevation
- **Myrcene** (secondary, ~0.4–0.7%) — earthy, sedative base
- **Linalool** (trace, ~0.1–0.3%) — floral, anxiolytic contribution
- **Humulene** (trace, ~0.1–0.2%) — woody, appetite suppressant

The high caryophyllene content is unusual for a cherry-forward strain
and contributes to Super Boof's distinctive spicy-peppery finish.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 25–32% (frequently tests above 28%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
