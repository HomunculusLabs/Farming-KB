## Qualitative Effects

At 15 mg (smoked over 75 seconds): Effects noticed within 15 seconds. Described
as "light, no visual, rather pointlessly stoned." Clear within 10 minutes,
fully baseline within 20.

At 20 mg (smoked): "Coming on very fast, quite intense." Fully clear within
30 minutes. The experimenter suspected 30 mg would be more effective.

bonding properties. Thioethers (sulfur between carbons) are true analogues
of ethers (oxygen between carbons), but with different polarity, lipophilicity,

## Expanded Chemical Context

The sulfur/oxygen bioisostere concept is one of the most important principles
in medicinal chemistry. Sulfur and oxygen share the same group number in the
periodic table and can often substitute for one another in drug molecules,
producing compounds with similar but subtly different pharmacological properties.

In the 5-MeS-DMT / 5-MeO-DMT comparison, the sulfur atom's larger atomic
radius (1.80 vs. 1.52 angstroms for oxygen), lower electronegativity (2.58
vs. 3.44 on the Pauling scale), and greater polarizability create meaningful
differences in how the molecule interacts with [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]] sites. The
methylthio group is more lipophilic than the methoxy group, which could
affect membrane penetration and distribution.

The contrasting direction of the sulfur effect in phenethylamines (increased
potency) versus tryptamines (decreased potency) is a pharmacologically
significant observation that suggests fundamentally different receptor binding
geometries between these compound classes. In phenethylamines, the sulfur's
increased size and polarizability may create more productive receptor contacts,
while in tryptamines, the optimal binding pocket geometry may be better served
by the smaller, more electronegative oxygen.
