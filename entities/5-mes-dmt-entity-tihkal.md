# 5-MeS-DMT — TIHKAL #46

## Compound Identity

- Full name: N,N-Dimethyl-5-methylthiotryptamine

- Synonyms: 3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-5-methylthioindole

- Class: Tryptamine, 5-methylthio substituted (sulfur analogue of [[tihkal-introduction-and-methodology]]

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: 15-30 mg (smoked)

- Duration: less than 1 hour

- Active routes: smoked only (oral activity not established)

## Qualitative Effects

At 15 mg (smoked over 75 seconds): Effects noticed within 15 seconds. Described
as "light, no visual, rather pointlessly stoned." Clear within 10 minutes,
fully baseline within 20.

At 20 mg (smoked): "Coming on very fast, quite intense." Fully clear within
30 minutes. The experimenter suspected 30 mg would be more effective.

## Synthesis

From 5-methylthiotryptamine via methylation with methyl iodide and sodium
bicarbonate in methanol (72 hours reflux). The resulting methiodide (mp 177-179°C)
is treated with DABCO in DMF to eliminate the quaternary ammonium, yielding
the free base (mp 97-100°C) after KugelRohr distillation.

## Related Sulfur Analogues

The 4-thio analogues have also been synthesized from 4-methylthioindole via
the oxalyl chloride route:

- N,N-Dimethyl-4-thiotryptamine (4-MeS-DMT, mp 108-110°C)

- N,N-Diisopropyl-4-thiotryptamine (4-MeS-DIPT, mp 92-94°C)

In animal behavioral disruption studies, there was a systematic drop in potency:
5-MeS-DMT > 4-MeS-DMT > 4-MeS-DIPT.

## Expanded Chemical Context

The sulfur/oxygen bioisostere concept is one of the most important principles
in medicinal chemistry. Sulfur and oxygen share the same group number in the
periodic table and can often substitute for one another in drug molecules,
producing compounds with similar but subtly different pharmacological properties.

In the 5-MeS-DMT / 5-MeO-DMT comparison, the sulfur atom's larger atomic
radius (1.80 vs. 1.52 angstroms for oxygen), lower electronegativity (2.58
vs. 3.44 on the Pauling scale), and greater polarizability create meaningful
differences in how the molecule interacts with receptor binding sites. The
methylthio group is more lipophilic than the methoxy group, which could
affect membrane penetration and distribution.

The contrasting direction of the sulfur effect in phenethylamines (increased
potency) versus tryptamines (decreased potency) is a pharmacologically
significant observation that suggests fundamentally different receptor binding
geometries between these compound classes. In phenethylamines, the sulfur's
increased size and polarizability may create more productive receptor contacts,
while in tryptamines, the optimal binding pocket geometry may be better served
by the smaller, more electronegative oxygen.

## Detailed Pharmacological Discussion

5-MeS-DMT is approximately half as potent as 5-MeO-DMT when both are
administered by the smoked route, requiring 15-30 mg versus 6-20 mg for
the oxygen analogue. The subjective effects are also described as less
remarkable -- "light, no visual, rather pointlessly stoned" -- compared to
the often overwhelming psychedelic experience of 5-MeO-DMT.

The reduced potency and muted effects of 5-MeS-DMT could reflect:

- Lower affinity for 5-HT2A receptors due to suboptimal binding geometry

- Reduced ability to activate the receptor (lower intrinsic efficacy)

- Altered metabolic profile leading to shorter effective duration

- Differential engagement of other receptor systems that modulate the
psychedelic response

The compound's activity when smoked but not established orally is consistent
with other simple tryptamines, which are typically metabolized too rapidly
by MAO enzymes for oral activity to be practical.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- 5-MeO-DMT (oxygen analogue): 6-20 mg smoked, intense psychedelic, out-of-body

- 5-MeS-DMT (sulfur analogue): 15-30 mg smoked, lighter, less visual

- DMT (unsubstituted): 20-60 mg smoked, classic short-acting psychedelic

- 4-MeS-DMT (4-position thio): synthesized but less well characterized

The 5-MeS-DMT / 5-MeO-DMT pair provides a clean comparison of the
sulfur/oxygen bioisostere effect in the tryptamine series.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

- Activity has only been established by the smoked route; oral activity is
unknown and unlikely given MAO metabolism

- Smoking any compound carries inherent respiratory risks

- The short duration (<1 hour) may encourage rapid re-dosing, which could
produce cumulative effects

- As with all tryptamines, avoid MAO inhibitors and SSRIs

- The compound has not been extensively characterized for toxicity

- Start at the lower end of the dose range (15 mg) when smoking
