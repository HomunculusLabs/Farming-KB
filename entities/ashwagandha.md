# Ashwagandha (*Withania somnifera*)

## Overview

Ashwagandha (*[[withania-somnifera]]* Dunal), also called **Indian Ginseng**, **Winter Cherry**, or **Asgandh** (Hindi), is an evergreen shrub in the **Solanaceae** (nightshade) family. One of the most important herbs in **Ayurvedic medicine**, it has been classified as a **Rasayana** (rejuvenating) herb for over 3,000 years. The species name *somnifera* means "sleep-bearing" in Latin, reflecting its traditional use as a sedative and nervine tonic. Modern research has validated many traditional applications, particularly in stress management, thyroid function, and athletic performance.

## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom**: Plantae → **Order**: Solanales → **Family**: Solanaceae → **Genus**: Withania → **Species**: *W. somnifera*

## Botanical Description

Ashwagandha is a drought-tolerant perennial shrub growing 35–75 cm tall. Key features:

- **Leaves**: Ovate, dull green, 5–10 cm long, covered in fine stellate trichomes (silvery-grey appearance)
- **Flowers**: Small, bell-shaped, greenish-yellow to creamy white; axillary clusters
- **Fruit**: Small orange-red berry in papery calyx (characteristic of Solanaceae)
- **Roots**: Thick, fleshy, tuberous taproot — the primary medicinal part
- **Seeds**: Small, kidney-shaped, yellowish

Native to dry regions of **India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh**, and parts of **North Africa and the Middle East**. Thrives in arid conditions with well-drained sandy or rocky soil at elevations up to 1,700 m.

## Active Compounds

### Withanolides

The primary bioactive class — naturally occurring steroidal lactones (C₂₈ steroids with a 26-carbon lactone [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]]). Over 40 withanolides isolated:

- **Withaferin A**: Most studied; potent anti-inflammatory, anticancer (induces apoptosis), immunomodulatory. Binds vimentin, inhibits NF-κB.
- **Withanolide D**: Cytotoxic against tumor cells; modulates immune response
- **Withanone**: Neuroprotective; supports DNA repair mechanisms
- **Withanoside IV and VI**: Neuroprotective; promote neurite outgrowth and regeneration
- **12-Deoxywithastramonolide**: Anti-angiogenic properties

### Alkaloids and Other Compounds

- **Somniferine/Somnine**: Mild sedative compounds (sleep-promoting effects)
- **Sitoindosides IX and X**: Anti-stress glycowithanolides (primary cortisol-modulating agents)
- **Iron**: Notable bioavailable iron in root
- **Steroidal saponins**: Adaptogenic and immunomodulatory
- **Choline**: Cognitive support

## Pharmacology and Mechanisms of Action

### HPA Axis Modulation (Stress Response)

1. Reduces circulating **cortisol** levels by 14.5–30%
2. Normalizes **DHEA-S** levels
3. Modulates **CRH** signaling
4. Restores glucocorticoid receptor sensitivity

### GABAergic Activity

Withanolides show affinity for **GABA-A receptors**, functioning as mild GABA-mimetics. Withaferin A modulates GABA transaminase, increasing GABA availability — explaining anxiolytic and sleep-promoting effects without benzodiazepine dependency risk.

### Thyroid Function

- Increases **T3** by ~18–20% and **T4** by ~15–18%
- Decreases **TSH** toward normal range
- Mechanism: enhanced T4→T3 conversion via deiodinase activity
- **Caution**: May exacerbate hyperthyroidism

### Testosterone and Fertility

- **Testosterone increase**: 10–15% in infertile men, ~5% in healthy men
- **Sperm quality**: Improved motility (+8%), count (+53%), volume (+17%)
- Mechanism: reduced cortisol suppression of gonadal steroids + direct Leydig cell support
