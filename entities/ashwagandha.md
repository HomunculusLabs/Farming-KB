## Ashwagandha (*Withania somnifera*)

## Overview

ashwagandha-at-[[query-how-to-grow-rare-tropical-fruits-in-marginal-and-subtropical-climates]] and is drought-tolerant once established. It grows as a perennial in tropical zones and as an annual in [[cancer-medicinal-mushrooms-schizophyllan-spg-clinical-trials]]
- **Neuroprotection**: Protection against β-amyloid and glutamate-induced neurotoxicity
- **Sleep improvement**: Significant improvement in sleep quality and sleep onset latency ( root extract 300 mg bid)

### Musculoskeletal Effects

- Increases VO₂ max and cardiovascular endurance by 13–15%
- Enhances muscle strength and recovery when combined with resistance training
- Increases testosterone levels by 15–40% in men with subclinical deficiency
- Improves sperm quality parameters (count, motility, morphology)

### Immunomodulation

- Increases immunoglobulin production (IgG, IgM)
- Enhances macrophage and NK cell activity
- Paradoxically, acts as both immunostimulant and immunosuppressant depending on context

### Anti-inflammatory

Withaferin A inhibits NF-κB signaling, reducing expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6). COX-2 inhibition has been demonstrated at physiologically relevant concentrations.

## Cultivation

### Growing Conditions

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Climate | Arid to semi-arid, tropical/subtropical |
| Temperature | 20–38°C optimal |
| Rainfall | 500–750 mm annually |
| Soil | Well-drained, [[sandy-loam-texture-cannabis-microbiome-assembly]], pH 7.5–8.0 |
| Altitude | Sea level to 1500 m |
| Propagation | [[clay-seed-pellets-direct-seeding]] or transplanting |

### Agronomic Notes

- Seeds require no special pretreatment; germination in 7–14 days at 25°C
- Plant spacing: 30 × 60 cm (commercial) or 45 × 60 cm ([[cannabis-controlled-pollination-seed-production]])
- Minimal fertilizer requirements; excess nitrogen reduces withanolide content
- Root harvest at 5–6 months, when leaves begin to yellow
- Average root yield: 400–600 kg/ha (dry weight)

### Key Cultivation Regions

India (Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh) produces the majority of the world's ashwagandha supply. Cultivation has expanded to the US, Australia, and parts of Africa as demand has surged.

## Extraction and Standardization

- **KSM-66**: Full-spectrum root extract standardized to 5% withanolides; most clinically studied extract
- **Sensoril**: Root and leaf extract standardized to 10% withanolides; higher potency, more sedating
- **[[kava-traditional-preparation-ceremonial-context]]**: Root powder (churna) — 3–6 g/day in warm milk with honey

## Dosage and Safety

| Form | Dose Range | Frequency |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Root powder | 3–6 g/day | Divided doses |
| Standardized extract (5%) | 300–600 mg/day | 1–2 doses |
| Standardized extract (10%) | 125–250 mg/day | 1 dose |
| Tincture (1:5) | 2–4 mL/day | Divided doses |

### Contraindications

- Pregnancy (emmenagogue and uterotonic activity — risk of miscarriage)
