# Strawberry Banana

## Overview

**Strawberry Banana** (also known as Strawberry Banana Kush) is an indica-dominant hybrid [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and potent sedative effects that transition from uplifting cerebral stimulation to full-body relaxation.

Its [[haze-lineage-and-legacy-in-modern-breeding]] programs, with notable progeny including **Strawberry Banana Kush** crosses and several resin-focused phenotypes.

## Lineage

- **Parent 1:** Banana Kush (Ghost OG × Skunk Haze)
- **Parent 2:** Strawberry Bubblegum (Bubblegum × [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]], with occasional purple hues appearing in cooler growing conditions. The resin glands are large and bulbous, visible to the naked eye.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Primary aroma:** Sweet strawberry and ripe banana, with a tropical creaminess that evokes strawberry-banana smoothies.

**Secondary notes:** Earthy undertones, subtle berry tartness, and a faint peppery spice on the exhale. When ground, the tropical sweetness intensifies considerably.

The flavor closely mirrors the aroma — creamy strawberry and banana dominate on the inhale, with an earthy, slightly herbal finish on the exhale. The smoothness of the smoke is frequently noted in reviews.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene** (dominant): ~0.8–1.2% — contributes sedative body effects and earthy-musky base notes
- **Caryophyllene**: ~0.3–0.6% — adds peppery spice and potential anti-inflammatory properties
- **Limonene**: ~0.2–0.4% — bright citrus undertone, mood elevation
- **Pinene** (trace): subtle pine freshness, counteracts some sedative effects

The high myrcene-to-limonene ratio drives the strain's predominantly relaxing profile while maintaining initial cerebral brightness.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 18–26% (phenotypes from select breeders reported up to 31–32%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3%

The high [[cannabis-pain-management-protocols]], [[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]]:** 8–9 weeks indoors.

**Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors).

**Difficulty:** Moderate — the strain is relatively forgiving but requires attention to humidity during late flowering due to dense bud structure.
