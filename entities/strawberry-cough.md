## Strawberry Cough

## Overview

Strawberry Cough is a sativa-dominant hybrid [[sativa-haze-lineage-breeding-history]], the strain gained widespread fame after appearing in the documentary
*"Super High Me"* (2007). It consistently delivers uplifting, euphoric cerebral
effects paired with a mild body relaxation, making it a popular choice for daytime
use, social settings, and creative endeavors. [[strawberry-banana]] and Strawberry Lemonade, extending its influence across the
modern cannabis market.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 80% sativa / 20% indica)
- **Breeder:** Kyle Kushman
- **Lineage:** Strawberry Fields × Haze (specific cut undisclosed)
- **[[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]]:** 9–10 weeks indoors; mid-October outdoors
- **Yield:** Moderate — approximately 12–14 oz/m² indoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate; requires attention to humidity and training
- **Award:** Best Flower at the 2013 High Times Cannabis Cup (Amsterdam)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Aroma:** Intense fresh strawberries with undertones of earthy pine and subtle
  spicy herbal notes; the scent is immediately recognizable and often described as
  one of the most authentic berry aromas in cannabis.
- **Flavor:** Sweet strawberry on the inhale with a creamy, smooth finish; mild
  peppery and earthy undertones emerge on the exhale.
- **Smoke quality:** Exceptionally smooth — the name "Cough" is somewhat ironic as
  the expansion is gentle, though some phenotypes do induce a light throat tickle.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid cerebral onset within 2–5 minutes; effects build gradually
  to peak over 30–45 minutes rather than hitting all at once
- **Primary effects:** Euphoria, happiness, uplifted mood, enhanced sociability,
  creative inspiration, mild focused energy
- **Secondary effects:** Gentle body relaxation without heavy sedation; light
  physical buzz that complements rather than overwhelms mental stimulation
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours; effects taper smoothly without sharp comedown
- **Commonly reported:** Talkative, giggly, creative, motivated, relaxed but not
  couch-locked

## Terpene Profile

- **[[myrcene]]:** Dominant — contributes to relaxing body effects and herbal aroma
- **[[pinene]]:** Present — adds pine notes and may promote alertness and focus
- **[[limonene]]:** Moderate — enhances mood elevation and contributes citrus brightness
- **Caryophyllene:** Present — adds subtle peppery spice and potential
  anti-inflammatory properties
- **[[linalool]]:** Trace — complements the smooth, calming character of the strain
