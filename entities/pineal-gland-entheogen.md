## Description

The pineal gland is a small endocrine gland located near the center of the brain, often called the "third eye." In entheogenic research, it is theorized to naturally produce DMT (dimethyltryptamine) and pinoline (a beta-carboline MAO inhibitor), creating an endogenous version of the ayahuasca combination. This gland may be responsible for naturally occurring visionary and psychic experiences, particularly during sleep and near-death states.

## Classification

- **Category**: Endocrine organ / neurotheological structure
- **Location**: Epithalamus, near center of brain
- **Size**: ~5–8 mm, pine cone–shaped
