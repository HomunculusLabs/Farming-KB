# Cinex

## Overview

**Cinex** is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Cinderella 99 (C99)** with **Vortex**. Bred by **Resin Seeds** and reportedly originating from the Pacific Northwest cannabis scene (Vancouver, BC area), Cinex has earned a reputation as a potent, clear-headed daytime strain that combines the soaring cerebral effects of C99 with the complex terpene profile of Vortex.
The name is a portmanteau of "Cinderella" and "Vortex."

The strain is known for its pungent citrus-forward aroma, heavy resin production, and energizing effects that have made it a staple among medical patients seeking functional daytime relief. THC content typically ranges from 18–26%, placing it in the high-potency category.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Cinderella 99 (Princess × P94) — a legendary sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Mr. Soul of Brothers Grimm. Known for its rapid 45–50 day flowering, sweet tropical flavor, and powerful but clear cerebral high.
- **Male parent:** Vortex — a sativa-dominant cross of Apollo 13 × Space Queen (both Brothers Grimm lines). Vortex contributes funky, citrus-pine terpenes and adds potency and trichome density.
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 70% sativa / 30% indica.
- **Breeder:** Resin Seeds (Spain), though phenotypic selections and cut-only versions circulate widely in North America.
- **Lineage depth:** Three generations of deliberate breeding from foundational Brothers Grimm stock — Cinderella 99 and Apollo 13 are among the most genetically influential cannabis lines in modern breeding.

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Medium to large, elongated and tapered flowers typical of sativa-dominant varieties. Dense but not rock-hard, with a slightly loose internal structure.
- **Color:** Bright to lime green base with occasional golden-yellow undertones. Pistils are long, thin, and pale orange to copper. Some phenotypes express subtle purple hues in calyxes when exposed to cool night temperatures during late flowering.
- **Trichome coverage:** Heavy — mature flowers are coated in a thick layer of milky-white trichomes with occasional amber heads. Resin production is one of Cinex's defining visual characteristics, giving buds a frosted, almost wet appearance.
- **Leaf:** Moderately broad leaflets with serrated edges; less leafy than many sativa strains, making trimming relatively easy.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Primary aroma:** Sharp, pungent citrus — lemon and grapefruit are the dominant notes, with a bright, almost cleaning-product quality at full maturity.
- **Secondary notes:** Earthy skunk, damp soil, and a faint diesel undertone from the Vortex lineage.
- **Tertiary notes:** Sweet tropical fruit (pineapple, mango) on the finish, especially when grown organically.
- **Flavor:** The smoke is smooth with a sweet citrus forefront that transitions into earthy, skunky mid-palate flavors. The exhale carries lingering lemon and a subtle pine resin quality. The flavor is considered cleaner and less harsh than many high-THC strains.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene (0.8–1.4%):** The dominant terpene; contributes to the sedative baseline and the earthy, herbal base notes.
- **Limonene (0.6–1.2%):** Primary driver of the citrus aroma; enhances mood elevation and stress relief.
- **Caryophyllene (0.3–0.8%):** Adds peppery, spicy undertones; binds to CB2 receptors contributing anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Pinene (0.2–0.5%):** Present in moderate amounts; contributes pine notes and may counteract some of the cognitive fog associated with high THC.
- **Linalool (0.1–0.3%):** Trace levels add floral sweetness and anxiolytic properties.
- **Humulene (0.1–0.3%):** Earthy, woody note; may contribute appetite-suppressant effects.
- **Terpinolene (0.05–0.2%):** Trace; adds complexity to the aroma profile with fruity, floral undertones.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 18–26% (tested flower); averages 20–22% in commercial samples.
- **CBD:** <0.1% — negligible; not a CBD-source strain.
- **THCV:** Low to trace (0.05–0.2%) — insufficient for significant THCV-mediated effects.
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.4% — typical for high-THC cultivars.
- **CBC:** 0.05–0.2%.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid (2–5 minutes when smoked or vaporized); effects build over 15–30 minutes to peak.
- **Cerebral:** Strong, clear-headed mental stimulation. Users report enhanced focus, creativity, and sociability. The high is described as "motivating" rather than "racing" — functional enough for productive tasks, unlike many high-THC sativas that cause scatter-brain.
- **Physical:** Mild body relaxation develops after 30–45 minutes but never becomes couch-locking. Light buzzing sensation in the extremities is common.
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours for smoked; 3–5 hours for edibles.
- **Common applications:** Creative work, social situations, daytime pain management, combating fatigue, managing ADHD symptoms.

## Medical Applications

- **Depression and mood disorders:** The uplifting, euphoric onset provides rapid relief from depressive episodes and anhedonia.
- **Anxiety:** Limonene and linalool content may help some patients; however, high THC at doses above individual tolerance can paradoxically increase anxiety.
- **Chronic pain:** Effective for neuropathic pain, migraines, and inflammatory pain without heavy sedation.
- **Fatigue:** The energizing properties make it suitable for patients dealing with chronic fatigue syndrome, MS-related fatigue, or medication-induced lethargy.
- **ADHD/ADD:** Anecdotal reports suggest improved focus and task completion, though clinical evidence is limited.

## Side Effects

- **Common:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, mild tachycardia.
- **Moderate:** Anxiety and paranoia at higher doses (particularly in THC-sensitive individuals); dizziness on standing.
- **Uncommon:** Headache (usually from dehydration), mild nausea at very high doses.
- **Contraindications:** Use caution with a history of psychosis or severe anxiety disorders. Not recommended as a sleep aid due to stimulating effects.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks (56–63 days) indoors; mid-October outdoors in northern hemisphere.
- **Yield:** Moderate to high — 350–500 g/m² indoors; 400–600 g/plant outdoors.
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate — responds well to training (LST, SCROG) but can be sensitive to overfeeding. Nitrogen requirements decrease sharply after week 3 of flowering.
- **Preferred medium:** Soil or soilless (coco/perlite); organic cultivation maximizes terpene expression.
- **Climate:** Prefers temperate to warm climates; finishes well in Mediterranean conditions. Susceptible to botrytis in high-humidity environments during late flowering.
- **Stretch:** Moderate vegetative stretch (1.5–2× height increase) after flip to 12/12.

## Notable Phenotypes

- **"Citrus pheno":** Expresses dominant limonene profile with intensely lemony aroma and flavor; generally considered the most desirable cut among medical users for its clean, functional effects.
- **"Funky pheno":** More Vortex-dominant expression with stronger skunk/diesel notes; slightly heavier body effects; preferred by recreational users seeking more intensity.
- **"Resin Queen":** Selected for maximum trichome production; popular among hash makers and rosin pressers for its exceptional solventless extract yields. Resin production can exceed 25% by weight.

## Extraction and Processing

Cinex is popular in the solventless extraction community due to its heavy resin production and favorable terpene ratios.
Bubble hash yields are above average at 3–5% by weight of 120μ+ grade material.
Live rosin pressed from fresh-frozen Cinex bubble hash retains the bright citrus terpene profile and commands premium pricing in artisanal concentrate markets.
The strain's moderate cannabinoid-to-terpene ratio (high terpenes relative to total cannabinoids) makes it particularly well-suited for full-spectrum products.
