# 2-TOM (5-Methoxy-4-methyl-2-methylthioamphetamine)

2-TOM is a methylthio-substituted amphetamine and the sulfur analogue of [[dom-entity]]. It replaces the 2-methoxy group of DOM with a methylthio group, resulting in a significant but not catastrophic loss of potency. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #171.

## Chemical Classification

2-TOM belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 5-methoxy-4-methyl-2-methylthioamphetamine.

2-TOM is the sulfur analogue of [[dom-entity]] (STP), with the 2-methoxy oxygen replaced by sulfur.

The compound retains the characteristic 4-methyl group and 5-methoxy group of DOM.

The "TOM" nomenclature indicates the positions of the methyl, oxygen, and methylthio groups.

## Pharmacological Profile

2-TOM is an active psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 60 to 100 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 8 to 10 hours.

This represents a significant loss of potency compared to [[dom-entity]], which is fully active at approximately 5 mg -- a factor of roughly 15 times less potent.

Despite the potency loss, 2-TOM maintains psychedelic character at accessible doses.

## Qualitative Effects

At 60 mg, subjects reported a superb body feeling, with food tasting exceptional.

Confidence was enhanced and the overall experience was described positively.

At 80 mg, one subject described an excellent experience in a down-to-earth, humorous, matter-of-fact universe.

A heaviness below the waist was noted (not erotic, but rather a slight physical weight).

The next day brought a cold, and vivid dreams were reported.

Another subject at 80 mg found the taste completely foul, with quite subtle effects.

The compound was described as a "thinker" rather than a visual compound.

## Potency Comparison with DOM

[[dom-entity]] (STP, 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine) is one of the most potent psychedelic amphetamines, active at 3-10 mg.

2-TOM replaces the 2-methoxy group with a 2-methylthio group, resulting in a 15-fold loss of potency.

This loss is attributed to the different electronic and steric properties of sulfur compared to oxygen.

The duration is also somewhat shorter than DOM.

Despite the potency difference, the experience may be worth the effort for experienced researchers.

Unlike [[5-tom-entity]], 2-TOM does not show the disturbing hints of neurological and physical roughness.

## Structure-Activity Relationships

The TOM series (2-TOM and 5-TOM) demonstrates that replacing oxygen with sulfur at different positions on the ring produces dramatically different compounds.

Position matters enormously: the 2-position substitution produces a compound with reasonable character, while the 5-position substitution produces more problematic effects.

The two-carbon homologue of 2-TOM (2C-2-TOM) has been synthesized but not yet entered into human screening.

2C-2-TOM was prepared by condensation of the appropriate aldehyde with nitromethane and ammonium acetate.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 60 - 100 mg.

Duration: 8 - 10 h.
