## Psilocin (4-HO-DMT)

Psilocin (4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine, 4-HO-DMT) is a
classical serotonergic psychedelic and the primary
pharmacologically active metabolite of [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]].

First isolated from [[albert-hofmann]]
at Sandoz, psilocin was identified as the active principle after
psilocybin was found largely inactive until enzymatically
dephosphorylated. In humans, psilocybin is rapidly converted to
psilocin by alkaline phosphatase enzymes before entering systemic
circulation.

## Chemical Structure and Properties

- **IUPAC name:** 3-[2-(Dimethylamino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-ol
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₂H₁₆N₂O
- **Molecular weight:** 204.27 g/mol
- **CAS number:** 520-53-6
- **Appearance:** White to off-white crystalline powder; darkens
  on air/light exposure due to oxidation to quinones
- **Solubility:** Moderate in water, ethanol, methanol; poor in
  nonpolar solvents. Free base is more lipid-soluble than
  psilocybin, facilitating BBB penetration.
- **Melting point:** 173-176°C (decomposes)
- **pKa:** ~9.5 (amine), ~9.9 (phenol)

The phenolic 4-hydroxyl is the key feature distinguishing
psilocin from DMT, dramatically altering [[glucuronidation]].

## Pharmacology

### Receptor Binding Profile

| Receptor | Ki (nM) | Functional Activity |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| 5-HT2A | 6-12 | Partial agonist |
| 5-HT2C | 12-25 | Partial agonist |
| 5-HT1A | 30-50 | Partial agonist |
| 5-HT1B | 40-80 | Weak agonist |
| 5-HT7 | 20-40 | Agonist |
| 5-HT2B | 50-100 | Agonist |
| D2 | >10,000 | Negligible |

Effects are mediated through 5-HT2A activation in cortical layer
V pyramidal neurons, promoting entropic neural activity. The
partial agonist profile (lower efficacy than serotonin) may
explain favorable safety. 5-HT1A activation contributes
anxiolytic and antidepressant effects. fMRI shows decreased
[[mystical-experience]] intensity and therapeutic outcomes.

### Comparison with Related Tryptamines

| Compound | MW | 5-HT2A Ki | Duration |
|----------|------|-----------|----------|
| DMT | 188 | ~200 nM | 30-90 min |
| Psilocin (4-HO-DMT) | 204 | 6-12 nM | 4-6 hr |
| Psilocybin (4-PO-DMT) | 284 | Inactive | 4-6 hr |
| [[5-meo-dmt-entity]] | 218 | ~10 nM | 15-45 min |

The 4-substituted tryptamine series shares common pharmacological
profiles. 4-AcO-DMT is widely regarded as a psilocin prodrug
producing nearly identical effects. Differences are subtle —
primarily onset time, body load, and visual character.
