# Psilocybe collybioides

**Psilocybe collybioides** is a [[cattle-dispersal-psychoactive-fungi-australia]] in the southern Australian states.

## Taxonomy and Classification

*Psilocybe collybioides* belongs to the family Hymenogastraceae within the order Agaricales. The species epithet "collybioides" refers to its morphological resemblance to mushrooms in the genus *Collybia*, a group of small saprotrophic mushrooms with similar stature and habitat preferences. This resemblance has historically caused confusion in field identification.

The species was documented by Dr. Malcolm Hall, who reported its occurrence from Tasmanian collections. Cribb and Cribb (1975) also referenced the species in their broader surveys of Australian psychoactive fungi. It is listed among the confirmed hallucinogenic fungi identified from Australia and New Zealand.
