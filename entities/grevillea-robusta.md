## Grevillea robusta

## Description

Grevillea robusta, commonly known as silky oak or silver oak, is a large evergreen tree in the family Proteaceae native to the subtropical and warm-temperate rainforests of eastern Australia. It is one of the most widely cultivated Grevillea species globally, naturalised in many tropical and subtropical countries worldwide. Valued for its spectacular golden-orange flower racemes, moderately durable timber, and as a shade and ornamental tree, it is specifically noted in [[silvopasture]] and crop-in-pasture [[gentiana-robusta]]
