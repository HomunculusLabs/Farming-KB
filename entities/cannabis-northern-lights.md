# Northern Lights

Northern Lights is a pure indica cannabis strain widely regarded as one of the most influential genetics in cannabis breeding history. Originating from Afghani landrace stock, it was stabilized in the Pacific Northwest during the late 1970s and early 1980s.

The strain is renowned for its resinous, frosty buds, fast flowering time, and deeply relaxing physical effects. It has served as a foundational parent for countless hybrid strains and remains a staple in both recreational and medical cannabis markets.

Northern Lights won the Cannabis Cup in 1990 and has been inducted into multiple strain halls of fame. Its genetics are estimated to appear in the lineage of over 80% of modern indica-dominant hybrids sold in legal markets.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Pure indica (100% indica)
- **Lineage:** Afghani indica landrace, selectively bred in the Pacific Northwest
- **Breeder:** "The Indian" (anonymous), developed by Neville Schoenmakers
- **THC content:** 16–21% (modern commercial averages)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.1%)

The original genetics are believed to derive from a single Afghani indica plant selected for its exceptional resin production, compact growth habit, and potent psychoactive effects. Neville Schoenmakers further stabilized and popularized the strain through The Seed Bank in the Netherlands during the 1980s.

Eleven numbered phenotypes were reportedly identified from the original seed stock, with NL#1, NL#2, and NL#5 becoming the most commercially significant. NL#5 was selected for its combination of potency, yield, and resin production, and became the basis for most subsequent Northern Lights breeding programs.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Earthy, pine, sweet
- **Secondary notes:** Spicy, woody, citrus undertones
- **Taste:** Smooth, earthy with a sweet pine finish

The aroma is pungent and earthy, characteristic of Afghani indicas. During flowering, Northern Lights produces a strong sweet-and-sour scent. The flavor is notably smooth on inhalation with a lingering spicy pine aftertaste.

The terpene profile is dominated by myrcene, which contributes to the sedative body effects and earthy aroma. Caryophyllene adds peppery warmth, while limonene provides a subtle citrus brightness that balances the heavy earth tones.

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes (smoking/vaping)
- **Duration:** 3–5 hours
- **Primary effects:** Deep physical relaxation, euphoria, sedation
- **Body effects:** Heavy body stone, couch-lock at higher doses
- **Medical applications:** Insomnia, chronic pain, muscle spasms, anxiety, PTSD

Northern Lights delivers a quintessential indica experience. Effects are primarily physical, with a fast-acting body buzz that progresses to deep relaxation and eventual sedation. Cerebral effects are mild, limited to a gentle euphoric haze.

The strain is particularly effective for evening use and sleep aid. Medical patients report significant relief from chronic pain conditions and insomnia. Its anxiolytic properties make it useful for stress and PTSD symptom management.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness, and pronounced couch-lock. Novice consumers should approach with caution due to the sedative intensity.

### Dosage Guidelines

- **Threshold:** 2.5–5 mg THC
- **Light:** 5–10 mg THC
- **Moderate:** 10–15 mg THC
- **Strong:** 15–25 mg THC

Due to the heavy sedative effects, moderate doses are often sufficient. Medical users seeking sleep aid typically consume 15–20 mg THC within one hour of desired sleep onset.

## Growing Characteristics

- **Flowering time:** 6–8 weeks (one of the fastest-flowering strains available)
- **Yield:** 350–500 g/m² (indoor); 400–600 g/plant (outdoor)
- **Height:** 80–130 cm (indoor); 120–180 cm (outdoor)
- **Difficulty:** Easy — recommended for beginners
- **Climate:** Temperate to warm; performs well in controlled indoor environments

Northern Lights is one of the easiest cannabis strains to cultivate, making it a favorite among novice growers. Its compact stature and rapid flowering cycle make it ideal for indoor grows with limited vertical space.

The strain is naturally resistant to mold and pests, a trait inherited from its hardy Afghani landrace genetics. It requires moderate nutrient levels and responds well to both soil and hydroponic growing systems.

Northern Lights performs exceptionally well in Sea of Green (SOG) setups due to its uniform growth and minimal branching. The plants produce large, dense central colas with excellent resin coverage. Trichome production peaks at approximately 7 weeks of flowering.

## Genetic Relatives and Crosses

Northern Lights has been used as a parent in an extraordinary number of crosses. Its genetic contributions are foundational to modern cannabis breeding:

- **Super Silver Haze:** Northern Lights × Haze × Skunk #1
- **Northern Lights #5:** Selected phenotype, widely considered the most potent expression
- **Shiva Skunk:** Northern Lights × Skunk #1
- **Jack Herer:** Northern Lights × Haze × (Northern Lights #5 × Haze)

The NL#5 cut remains one of the most sought-after clone-only genetics in cultivation. Multiple numbered phenotypes (NL#1 through NL#5) exist, with NL#5 being the most commercially significant due to its higher potency and resin production.
