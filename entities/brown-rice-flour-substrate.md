## Description

Brown rice flour (also called brown rice powder) is the primary nutrient source in the PF TEK mushroom cultivation method. Made by grinding whole brown rice into a fine powder, it provides the carbohydrates, proteins, and minerals necessary for mycelial growth and fruiting of *Psilocybe cubensis*. The PF TEK specifically recommends freshly ground brown rice powder over pre-packaged varieties, noting that freshness can significantly affect cultivation results.

## Classification

- **Category:** Organic substrate nutrient component
- **Type:** Ground whole grain (unrefined rice)
- **Source:** Health food stores, co-ops, or homemade via coffee grinder
- **Role in PF TEK:** Primary nutritional source for mycelium
- **Form:** Fine powder

## Key Facts

- The PF TEK standard formula uses 1/8 cup of brown rice powder per 1/2 pint jar.
- The maximum fruiting formula uses 1/4 cup per jar, providing additional nutrition for larger yields.
- Freshly ground brown rice is recommended over pre-packaged powder because "the freshness sometimes makes a big difference."
- To make homemade powder, regular brown rice is ground in a small canister-type coffee bean grinder until it reaches a fine consistency.
- Brown rice is chosen over white rice because the bran layer contains additional nutrients (B vitamins, minerals, oils) that support more vigorous mycelial growth.
- The PF TEK is fundamentally described as "a brown rice method with an improved formula by using vermiculite as a base and adding pulverized brown rice."

## Substrate Formulation

Two formulas are provided in the PF TEK:

**Standard Formula (per 1/2 pint jar):**
- 1/8 cup brown rice powder
- 1/2–2/3 cup vermiculite (medium grade)
- 40–45 cc (ml) water

**Maximum Fruiting Formula (per 1/2 pint jar):**
- 1/4 cup brown rice powder
- 1/2 cup vermiculite
- 60 cc water

Note: When using the maximum fruiting formula with finer vermiculite, the jar lid should be kept loose during incubation to prevent water accumulation at the bottom.

## Preparation and Mixing

- Brown rice powder is placed on top of the measured vermiculite in a mixing bowl.
- Water is added slowly and directly onto the brown rice powder first, wetting it before mixing.
- Ingredients are mixed thoroughly until the mixture feels damp and cohesive.
- Each jar's substrate should be mixed individually to ensure accurate formula rendering.
- Measuring cups must be verified: 1 cup = 237 ml = 1/2 pint = 8 liquid ounces.

## Relevance to Cultivation

Brown rice flour is central to the PF TEK for several reasons:

- **Nutritional profile:** Provides a balanced carbohydrate and protein source suitable for *Psilocybe cubensis* mycelial colonization and fruiting.
- **Steam sterilization compatibility:** Unlike whole grain substrates that require pressure cooking, the flour-based substrate can be effectively steam sterilized at boiling temperature due to its airy, non-dense nature when combined with vermiculite.
- **Accessibility:** Available at health food stores and easily made at home, making the technique accessible worldwide.
- **Potency relationship:** The PF TEK emphasizes that growing on brown rice, harvesting young specimens, and cool-drying with desiccant produces "an entheogen of the highest order."
- **Substrate density:** The powder form, when mixed with vermiculite, creates an ideal balance of nutrition and aeration that grain-only methods cannot achieve.

## Precautions

- If measuring cup specifications are not accurate, the formulas will fail or produce diminished growth.
- Substrate that is too wet or sloppy should have the water content reduced.
- Drying of substrate during sterilization is a common failure mode; overheating should be avoided.
- Any halted growth without contamination is typically a sign of dried substrate, remedied by increasing water content.
