# Zkittlez

**Zkittlez** (also stylized **Skittles** or **Zkittles**) is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by **3rd Gen Family** and **Terp Hogz** in the San Francisco Bay Area of California.
It is a cross between **[[granddaddy-purple]] × Afghani) |
| Male Parent | [[lsd]] or Grapefruit cross (sativa-dominant, unconfirmed) |
| Generation | F1 hybrid (stabilized through selective breeding) |
| Landrace Ancestry | Afghani (indica), Thai/Mexican sativa (via Granddaddy Purple lineage) |

The precise male parent has been the subject of some debate within the cannabis community.
Terp Hogz has described it as a proprietary sativa selection, while some accounts identify it as the strain LSD (a Skunk #1 × Mazar hybrid) or a Grapefruit-dominant selection.
The genetic uncertainty of the male parent has not prevented Zkittlez from becoming a foundational breeding stock in its own right, with dozens of award-winning offspring strains descending from it.
Key descendants include **Runtz** (Zkittlez × Gelato), **OGZ** (Zkittlez × OG Eddy Lepp), **Rainbow Belts** (Zkittlez × Moonbow), and **Cherry Zkittlez** (Zkittlez × Cherry Pie).

## Appearance and Morphology

Zkittlez plants are medium to tall in height, reaching 100–150 cm indoors and potentially 200+ cm outdoors in favorable conditions.
The growth structure is moderately branching with strong lateral development, producing dense, conical flower clusters that are visually striking.
Calyxes are large, chunky, and tightly packed, with a distinctive olive-green to dark-purple coloration that can shift dramatically depending on temperature during the late flowering phase.
Cooler nighttime temperatures (below 15°C) during weeks 5–8 of flowering trigger anthocyanin production, producing vibrant purple, magenta, and deep violet hues throughout the buds.
Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosty, crystalline appearance with a predominantly white to amber trichome profile at maturity.
The pistils are long, thin, and orange to rust-colored, weaving through the dense calyx structure in moderate abundance.
Leaf structure is typical of indica-dominant varieties — broad-fingered, dark green leaves that may also display purple pigmentation.

## Terpene Profile and Aroma

The defining characteristic of Zkittlez is its extraordinary terpene profile, which closely mimics the aroma of mixed fruit candy:

- **Limonene** (35–45% of terpene fraction) — dominant terpene responsible for the citrus, lemon, and tropical fruit notes. Contributes mood-elevating and stress-relieving effects.
- **Caryophyllene** (15–25%) — peppery, spicy undertone. The only terpene known to directly activate the CB2 [[plant-sensory-perception]] and an appreciation for music, food, and visual stimuli.
**Peak (30–90 minutes)**: The indica influence becomes increasingly prominent, transitioning from a heady, cerebral high to a warm, full-body relaxation. Users report a pleasant heaviness in the limbs, muscular tension relief, and a general sense of physical comfort without overwhelming sedation.
**Duration (2–4 hours)**: The experience gradually transitions into a calm, mellow state characterized by gentle sedation, contentment, and relaxation. Sleep may come easily if the user is already tired.
