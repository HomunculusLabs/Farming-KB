## Overview

THC Bomb is an indica-dominant hybrid [[seed-company-supplier-reference]]. Released in the early 2010s, it combines
a potent indica parent with a selected sativa to achieve exceptionally
high THC production while maintaining manageable growth characteristics.

The strain consistently tests between 20–25% THC, with some phenotypes
exceeding 26%. This places it among the most potent commercially
available strains of its generation, earning its "bomb" designation.

THC Bomb is popular among recreational and medical users for its
powerful full-body effects and manageable cerebral high. Short flowering
time and high yields make it a favorite across all experience levels.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by earthy, pungent skunk notes with underlying
citrus and pine undertones. When ground, buds release sharper fragrance
with hints of diesel and spice.

Flavor follows the aroma: thick, earthy smoke with a citrus-laced
aftertaste. Lemon and pepper notes are common on the exhale, with some
phenotypes showing subtle tropical fruit sweetness.

The overall profile is robust and long-lasting, ranking among the more
flavorful high-THC strains. The combination of earthy depth and citrus
brightness appeals to connoisseurs.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

Myrcene is the dominant terpene, contributing sedative, relaxing
properties. Caryophyllene adds peppery spice and potential anti-
inflammatory benefits through CB2 receptor activation. Limonene
contributes citrus brightness and mood elevation.

Supporting terpenes include pinene, humulene, and linalool. The
combined [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] synergizes with high THC for a full-spectrum
effect.
