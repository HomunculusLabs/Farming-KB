# Chicken Tractor

## Description

The chicken tractor is a mobile, floorless poultry enclosure used in
permaculture and sustainable agriculture to integrate chickens into land
management. Designed to be moved regularly across pasture, garden beds, or
orchard floors, the chicken tractor concentrates poultry foraging in a
controlled area, allowing birds to scratch, fertilise, and debug the soil
while being protected from predators. [[chicken-tractor-system]] involving 12-16 pens per hectare rotated over an
18-month cycle, illustrating how this technique becomes a cornerstone of
integrated orchard-poultry management.

## Classification

- **Category:** Permaculture technique / animal husbandry system
- **System type:** Mobile poultry housing and land management tool
- **Integration level:** Zone 1-3 (garden, orchard, pasture)
- **Animal class:** Poultry (chickens primarily; adaptable to ducks and geese)

## Key Facts

- Floorless mobile pen that allows chickens direct ground contact
- Typically houses 20-50 birds per unit, depending on size
- Rotated across land to distribute fertility and control weeds evenly
- Mollison prescribes 12-16 pens per hectare in orchard systems
- Full rotation cycle spans 18 months for complete orchard coverage
- Provides predator protection while allowing natural foraging behaviour
- Combines soil cultivation, pest control, and fertilisation in one operation
- Eliminates or greatly reduces need for mechanical soil preparation
- Can be constructed from lightweight materials: timber frame, wire mesh, corrugated roofing
- Suited to both small-scale backyard systems and commercial operations

## Relevance to Cultivation

### Orchard Management

Mollison specifies chicken tractor rotation as a core technique for
orchard floor management. By moving pens systematically through the
orchard, chickens:

- Clean up fallen fruit that would otherwise host pests and disease
- Consume insect larvae, grubs, and weed seeds from the soil surface
- Scratch and aerate the topsoil, improving infiltration and tilth
- Deposit nitrogen-rich manure directly where trees need it
- Reduce or eliminate the need for mowing between tree rows

The 18-month rotation through 12-16 pens per hectare ensures each area
of the orchard receives poultry attention approximately once per cycle,
maintaining soil health and pest suppression across the entire system.

### Density Guidelines

Mollison provides specific density recommendations for poultry on range:
- Unstressed forage conditions: 800-900 birds per hectare
- Orchard cleanup operations: 120-180 birds per hectare

The lower orchard density reflects the need to prevent excessive soil
disturbance near tree roots while still achieving effective cleanup and
fertilisation.

## Summary

The chicken tractor is a foundational permaculture technique that
transforms poultry from isolated production animals into active land
management agents. Mollison's detailed prescription of 12-16 pens per
hectare on an 18-month rotation demonstrates how this simple technology
can systematically maintain orchard health while producing eggs and meat,
embodying the permaculture ethic of working with nature rather than
against it.
