## Overview

Lactarius chrysorrheus, commonly known as the yellowing milk-cap, is a medium-sized
ectomycorrhizal agaric in the family Russulaceae. It is widespread across temperate
Europe and recognized by its zonate orange-brown cap, abundant white latex that turns
bright yellow on exposure to air, and peppery taste.

The species epithet derives from Greek *chrysos* (gold) and *rheo* (to flow), referring
to the characteristic golden-yellow coloration of the exuded latex. It is inedible due

The most diagnostic feature is the latex — white when fresh, rapidly turning
intense sulfur-yellow within 10–30 seconds on exposure to air. The flesh has a
strongly peppery (acrid) taste and a faintly fruity odor.
