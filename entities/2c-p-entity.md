# 2C-P

2C-P is a psychedelic phenethylamine belonging to the 2C family, with a propyl group at the 4-position of the aromatic ring.

It is documented as entry #36 in PiHKAL by [[alexander-shulgin]].

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propylphenethylamine.

Molecular formula: C13H22ClNO2 (as hydrochloride).

2C-P features a propyl chain at the 4-position, making it the three-carbon member of the 2C series alongside 2c-d-entity|2C-D (methyl), 2c-e-entity|2C-E (ethyl), and 2c-b-entity|2C-B (bromo).

The hydrochloride salt melts at 207-209 degrees Celsius with prior sintering at 183 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 6 - 10 mg.

Duration of effects: 10 - 16 hours.

The onset is characteristically slow, with effects often not becoming noticeable until 2-3 hours after ingestion.

This delayed onset, combined with the long duration, means that re-dosing decisions made during the come-up period may lead to excessive dosing.

The experience is described as building gradually and maintaining a plateau for several hours before declining.

## Qualitative Effects

At 6 mg, some users report physical discomfort including back pain, leg pain, and lower back spasms.

The compound has been described as powerful but physically demanding at this dose.

At 8 mg, the experience becomes more positive, with users describing healing experiences, beautiful eyes-closed visuals, and a sense of internal work being accomplished.

Music is noted as particularly effective for overriding initial physical discomfort.

The long action is appreciated by some users, with reports of beneficial internal processes continuing for a week after the experience.

At 9 mg, effects peak around the third hour and are maintained for approximately four more hours.

The experience is described as "well grounded but rather diffuse" with excellent verbal and interpretive capabilities.

At 12 mg, the experience intensifies to a plus-three level with excellent erotic enhancement, good musical appreciation, and remarkable eyes-closed imagery from "a cool, wise place."

Communication is described as "superb and fun" with a strong sense of physical health and determination.

## Dosage Sensitivity

Shulgin specifically warned about the narrow therapeutic index of 2C-P.

One report described a 16 mg dose as "clearly an overdose" and "a physical disaster, not to be repeated."

There may not be much latitude between a dose that is "modest, or adequate" and one that is "excessive."

Individual titration is considered essential with this compound.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with the preparation of 2,5-dimethoxypropylbenzene from 2,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde through a Wittig reaction followed by catalytic hydrogenation.

The propylbenzene undergoes a Vilsmeier formylation with POCl3 and N-methylformanilide to yield 2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylbenzaldehyde.

The aldehyde is condensed with nitromethane in the presence of ammonium acetate to form the corresponding beta-nitrostyrene.

Reduction of the nitrostyrene with lithium aluminum hydride yields 2C-P as the free base, which is converted to the hydrochloride salt.

The synthesis is notable for the difficulty in obtaining crystalline intermediates, with the aldehyde remaining as an oil.

## Pharmacological Context

2C-P is one of the longer-acting members of the 2C family, comparable in duration to 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2 and 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7.

The propyl chain at the 4-position provides a balance between the shorter-acting methyl and ethyl substituents and the very long-acting higher homologues.

As with other 2C compounds, the primary mechanism of action is believed to be 5-HT2A receptor agonism.

The compound's potency (6-10 mg) is moderate within the 2C series, falling between 2c-d-entity|2C-D (20-60 mg) and 2c-b-entity|2C-B (12-24 mg).

## Comparison with Related Compounds

Compared to 2c-e-entity|2C-E, 2C-P has a longer duration but may be less visually intense.

The propyl chain produces a distinctly different character from the ethyl chain of 2c-e-entity|2C-E, with more emphasis on introspective work and physical sensation.

Among the 2C series, 2C-P is considered one of the more physically demanding compounds, with a higher incidence of body load and physical discomfort at active doses.

The 3-carbon amphetamine analogue would correspond to the DOPR series, which is also discussed in PiHKAL.

## Harm Reduction

The narrow margin between active and excessive doses makes careful measurement essential.

Users should never redose during the come-up period, as the slow onset can mask the actual level of intoxication.

A milligram-accurate scale is required for safe dosing.

The long duration (10-16 hours) means users should have no responsibilities for at least a full day.

2C-P should not be combined with [[psilocybin-vs-other-psychedelics]], MAOIs, or serotonergic substances.

The compound's physical demands mean it may not be suitable for individuals with cardiovascular concerns.
