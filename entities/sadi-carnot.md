## Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot

## Overview
- Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot was a French engineer and physicist.
- He is best known for the 1824 book on the motive power of fire.
- That work introduced the ideal heat-engine reasoning behind the [[query-why-cant-heat-engines-be-100-percent-efficient]].
- His work helped turn practical engine improvement into thermodynamic science.
- The Carnot limit remains central in power generation, refrigeration, and heat-pump analysis.
- His name anchors Carnot efficiency, Carnot theorem, and the Carnot cycle.
- The importance of his work increased through Clapeyron, Kelvin, and Clausius.
- He remains a central person entity for any wiki treatment of thermodynamics.
