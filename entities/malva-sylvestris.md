## Overview

*Malva sylvestris*, commonly known as common mallow or high mallow, is a
perennial herbaceous plant in the family Malvaceae. Native to Europe, North
Africa, and Western Asia, it has naturalized widely across temperate and
subtropical regions worldwide.
