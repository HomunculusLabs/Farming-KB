# Agribon

## Description

Agribon is a brand of [[floating-row-cover]] fabric used extensively in four-season farming systems for both greenhouse inner layer insulation and quick-hoop covering. It is the primary row cover material referenced in Eliot Coleman's *The Winter Harvest Handbook* for extending the growing season and protecting crops from cold temperatures.

## Classification

- **Type:** Product / Agricultural material
- **Domain:** Crop protection, season extension
- **Category:** Floating row cover / frost protection fabric

## Key Facts

- Used as greenhouse inner layers to provide additional insulation within unheated greenhouse structures.
- Used as covering material for [[quick-hoops]] — low tunnel structures made from metal conduit bent into hoops and covered with fabric.
- Creates a microclimate that modifies the three critical temperature factors for winter salad crops:
  1. Rate of temperature drop
  2. Depth of temperature drop
  3. Duration of temperature drop
  4. Frequency of temperature drop
- Works by trapping radiant heat from the soil and creating a still air layer around plants.
- Purchased from Johnny's Selected Seeds (955 Benton Avenue, Winslow, ME 04901).

## Relevance to Cultivation

- Central to the multi-layer protected cultivation system that enables winter harvest in cold climates (Zone 3–5).
- Combined with unheated greenhouse structures, Agribon inner layers can maintain growing temperatures significantly above outdoor conditions even in the depths of winter.
- The quick-hoop system using Agribon provides a lower-cost alternative to full greenhouse structures while still offering meaningful cold protection for overwintering crops.
- Essential for protecting winter salad crops including Claytonia, Mache, Spinach, Asian greens, and other cold-hardy vegetables.
- In Coleman's system, the combination of outer greenhouse glazing plus Agribon inner layer creates two layers of protection, mimicking the effect of moving approximately 1.5 USDA hardiness zones southward.

## Application Notes

When used as greenhouse inner layers, Agribon fabric is suspended on wire wickets (76-inch-long straight lengths of number 9 wire from Ken-Bar Inc.) to create a low tunnel within the greenhouse structure, adding an insulating air gap between the crop and the greenhouse glazing.

## Alternative Products

Coleman also notes experience with Covertan Pro 17, a similar fabric available from Suntex CP, Inc. (Sarasota, FL), suggesting that growers have options in the row cover market.
