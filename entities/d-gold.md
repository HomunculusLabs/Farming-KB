# D. Gold

## Description

D. Gold was the inventor of the Isomerizer, a pioneering consumer-grade [[cannabinoid-isomerization-and-thc-acetate]] accessible to home practitioners for the first time, creating a community of users who shared techniques and innovations through the IsoNews newsletter.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person / Inventor
- **Field**: Cannabis technology, amateur chemistry, consumer product design
- **Era**: 1970s-1980s
- **Associated with**: Thai Power, Inc. (manufacturer), IsoNews newsletter

## Key Facts

- Invented the Isomerizer, a desktop device that combined [[cbd-to-thc-isomerization-chemistry]] published by Roger Adams in the Journal of the American Chemical Society (vol. 63, p. 2211).
- The Isomerizer used [[pittsburgh-doublewash]] system.

## Inventions

### The Isomerizer (Original)
- Combined Soxhlet extraction and acid isomerization in a single tabletop unit.
- Used isopropyl alcohol as the extraction solvent.
- Featured a reaction vessel, Soxhlet basket, condensing unit, and vacuum pump.
- Could process 4-5 ounces of cannabis per cycle (7-8 hours total).

### The ISO-2 (Improved Model)
- Enhanced version of the original Isomerizer.
- More widely distributed and commercially successful.
- Compatible with a planned automatic filtration accessory for finer purification.
- Users reported greater satisfaction with the ISO-2's performance and reliability.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

D. Gold's inventions are historically significant in the cannabis community as early examples of technology designed to maximize the potency and utility of homegrown cannabis. The Isomerizer allowed cultivators to process their own harvests into concentrated, isomerized products, effectively bridging small-scale cultivation with laboratory-grade cannabinoid conversion. The device and its surrounding community of users pioneered many DIY extraction and hash-making techniques that remain influential. For cultivators, the Isomerizer transformed the economics of growing by enabling the production of premium concentrates from standard-grade cannabis at a fraction of the cost of imported products.
