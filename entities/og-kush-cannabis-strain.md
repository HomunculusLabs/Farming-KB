# OG Kush

**Ocean Grown Kush** · **Original Gangster** · **Premium OG**

## Classification

Indica-dominant hybrid (~55/45 Indica-Sativa). Lineage: Chemdawg × Lemon Thai × Hindu Kush (debated). THC 19–26%, CBD <0.3%. Flowering time 8–9 weeks. Yield 300–500 g/m² indoor, 400–600 g/plant outdoor. Moderate difficulty.

The true genetic origin remains one of cannabis's most debated questions. The most accepted account traces it to a cross made in Florida in the early 1990s from a Chemdawg cutting and a Lemon Thai male, with Hindu Kush pollen contributing the "Kush" designation. It migrated west to Los Angeles via Matt "Bubba" Berger, becoming the foundational genetic building block of West Coast cannabis.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene** (0.5–1.2%): Sedative, muscle relaxant, "couch-lock"
- **Limonene** (0.3–0.8%): Uplifting, stress relief, mood elevation
- **β-Caryophyllene** (0.2–0.5%): Anti-inflammatory, CB2 receptor agonist
- **Linalool** (0.1–0.3%): Anxiolytic, calming, sleep-promoting
- **Humulene** (0.05–0.15%): Appetite suppressant, anti-inflammatory

The interplay between sedative myrcene and stimulating limonene creates OG Kush's characteristic "head high with body anchor" — cerebral euphoria settling into physical relaxation without complete incapacitation.

## Aroma and Flavor

Complex earthy-pine foundation with sharp lemon-fuel top notes; distinct "kushy" skunk musk; subtle floral undertones from linalool. Pine and lemon on inhale; earthy, woody spice on exhale with lingering diesel finish. Medium density smoke, smooth with slight expansion.

## Growth Characteristics

### Structure and Environment

- **Plant height**: 80–130 cm indoor; up to 200 cm outdoor
- **Branching**: Moderate lateral branching; responds well to LST and ScrOG
- **Bud structure**: Dense, chunky calyxes with heavy trichome coverage; pale to bright green with orange pistils
- **Temperature**: 22–28 °C day, 18–22 °C night
- **Humidity**: 55–70% vegetative, 40–50% flowering
- **Light**: 400–600 µmol/m²/s vegetative, 600–900 µmol/m²/s flowering
- **pH**: 6.0–6.8 soil, 5.5–6.2 hydro

### Cultivation Notes

- **Veg phase**: 3–5 weeks before flip; allow 6–8 nodes before training
- **Nutrients**: Moderate-to-heavy feeder; appreciates calcium and magnesium in flower
- **Training**: LST, ScrOG, and supercropping all effective; top once or twice
- **Stretch**: Moderate 30–50% flowering stretch; plan headroom
- **Pests**: Susceptible to spider mites and powdery mildew; maintain airflow
- **Living soil**: Thrives with diverse microbial populations; reduced nutrient needs vs. synthetic grows

## Phenotypes

Multiple phenotypes exist within the OG Kush genetic pool:

- **Tahoe OG**: Darker, more indica; shorter flowering, stronger sedation; purple hues in cool temps
- **Ghost OG**: More balanced and cerebral; higher limonene expression
- **SFV OG**: Energetic onset, strong body high; reliable producer
- **Trophy OG**: Dense, frosty buds; high calyx-to-leaf ratio; premium bag appeal

## Effects

Onset 2–5 minutes, peak at 30–60 minutes, duration 2–3 hours. Initial euphoric head rush with enhanced sensory perception and creative spark; progressive body relaxation with muscle tension relief and appetite stimulation. Can be talkative and engaging early, becomes more contemplative.

### Therapeutic Applications

- **Stress/anxiety**: Limonene and linalool provide anxiolytic support; myrcene grounds
- **Pain**: Moderate-to-severe relief; anti-inflammatory via caryophyllene CB2 binding
- **Insomnia**: Effective for sleep onset at moderate doses; heavy indica phenotypes preferred
- **Depression**: Uplifting euphoria provides temporary mood elevation
- **Nausea/appetite**: Reliable appetite stimulation

## Consumption Methods

- **Smoking**: Classic flower in joint, pipe, or bong; full-spectrum terpene experience; combustion at ~800 °C
- **Vaporization**: Dry herb vaporizer at 175–210 °C preserves terpenes and reduces respiratory irritants; preferred for flavor
- **Extraction**: CO₂, ethanol, or hydrocarbon extraction for concentrates; OG Kush is a staple for live resin and sauce production due to complex terpene profile
- **Edibles**: Decarboxylate at 110 °C for 30–45 min; infusion into fats (coconut oil, butter); onset 45–120 min, duration 4–8 hours

## Genetic Legacy

Arguably the single most influential cannabis strain of the 21st century. Contributed genetics to hundreds of named cultivars including Girl Scout Cookies (OG Kush × Durban Poison), Gelato (OG lineage), Wedding Cake (OG lineage), Headband (OG Kush × Sour Diesel), and Tahoe OG. The "OG" designation has become shorthand for premium, kush-adjacent quality regardless of actual genetics.

## Phenotype Selection and Breeding

### Selecting Mothers

When selecting a mother plant from seed, key traits to evaluate:

- **Structure**: Even branching and strong apical dominance for training
- **Trichome maturity**: Plants that express cloudy-to-amber trichomes at 8 weeks are preferred for indoor grows
- **Aroma complexity**: Deeper terpene expression beyond basic lemon-pine indicates better genetics
- **Vigor**: Faster vegetative growth and recovery from training correlate with higher yields
- **Resistance**: Plants showing natural pest/disease resistance are superior mothers

### Breeding Considerations

- **Male selection**: OG Kush males pass on density and trichome production but may reduce stretch; select males with compact internodal spacing
- **Reversal difficulty**: Some OG Kush phenotypes are stubborn to reverse with colloidal silver; extended spraying (21–28 days) may be needed
- **Terpene heritability**: Myrcene and limonene expression is moderately heritable; caryophyllene is strongly dominant in crosses

### Clone Management

OG Kush clones are commercially ubiquitous but genetic drift and mislabeling are rampant. Maintain mother plants under 18/6 light; replace every 6–8 months to prevent senescence. Root cuttings in rockwool, peat, or aeration cubes at 22–25 °C with 70–80% humidity. Rooting hormone optional but speeds strike time to 7–10 days.
