# Northern Lights

## Overview

Northern Lights is one of the most influential indica-dominant cannabis
strains in breeding history. Developed in the late 1970s and stabilized in
the Netherlands during the 1980s, it is a descendant of pure Afghan indica
landrace genetics. Named for the aurora-like shimmer of its crystal-coated
buds, Northern Lights has served as a foundational parent in hundreds of
modern hybrids.

The strain is renowned for its fast flowering time, resin production,
euphoric yet deeply relaxing effects, and its near-legendary status in
cannabis breeding. It has won multiple Cannabis Cup awards and remains a
staple in seed bank catalogs worldwide.

## Lineage

Northern Lights traces primarily to Afghan indica genetics collected from
the Hindu Kush mountain region. The exact original accession is debated, but
most accounts attribute the core genetics to a single Afghan indica cutting
that was brought to the Pacific Northwest of the United States in the late
1970s.

- **Primary parent:** Pure Afghan indica (Hindu Kush landrace)
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 95% indica / 5% sativa
- **Stabilized by:** Neville Schoenmaker (The Seed Bank, later Sensi Seeds)
- **Year of commercial release:** ~1985

Neville acquired the genetics and spent several years inbreeding and
selecting for the most potent, resinous, and fastest-flowering phenotypes.
The resulting stabilized line became the template for "indica" in modern
cannabis breeding. Multiple numbered phenotypes existed historically (NL#1,
NL#2, NL#5, NL#9), with NL#5 often cited as the most potent.

## Appearance and Aroma

**Buds:** Dense, tightly packed, and compact — characteristic of heavy
indica morphology. Calyxes are large and swollen, often appearing as a
solid mass of resin glands with minimal visible leaf material.

**Color:** Deep green to dark forest green, occasionally showing purple
hues in cooler growing conditions. The buds are famously coated in a thick
layer of trichomes, giving them a frosty, almost white appearance under
magnification — the source of the "Northern Lights" name.

**Aroma:** Pungent, earthy, and sweet. Primary notes include:
- Pine and wood (from pinene and terpinolene background)
- Sweet, spicy resin (characteristic of Afghan genetics)
- Subtle citrus and floral undertones

The smoke is smooth with a sweet, earthy aftertaste. The flavor is
relatively simple compared to modern polyhybrids but is regarded as
classic and clean.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

| Terpene | Approximate Range | Character |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| Myrcene | 0.8–1.2% | Sedative, earthy, musky |
| Caryophyllene | 0.4–0.7% | Peppery, anti-inflammatory |
| Pinene | 0.2–0.5% | Pine, mental clarity |
| Limonene | 0.1–0.3% | Citrus, mood elevation |

**Cannabinoid profile (modern stabilized versions):**
- **THC:** 16–21% (some phenotypes reach 24%)
- **CBD:** 0.1–0.3% (negligible in most phenotypes)
- **CBN:** Trace

The myrcene-dominant terpene profile combined with high THC produces the
strain's characteristic fast-onset, body-heavy effects. The relatively low
terpene complexity is typical of pure Afghan genetics.

## Effects and Medical Use

**Onset:** Rapid, typically within 2–5 minutes of inhalation.

**Progression:** Initial cerebral euphoria transitions within 15–30
minutes into a deep, full-body relaxation described as "couch-lock."
The cerebral component is mild compared to the physical sedation.

Common reported effects:
- Deep physical relaxation and muscle tension relief
- Mild euphoria and mood elevation
- Strong sedation, especially at higher doses
- Increased appetite (munchies)
- Drowsiness progressing to sleep

**Medical applications:**
- Insomnia — one of the most consistently effective strains for sleep
- Chronic pain — muscle spasms, neuropathic pain, arthritis
- Anxiety — anxiolytic effects in low-to-moderate doses
- Nausea and appetite stimulation — particularly in chemotherapy patients
- PTSD — nighttime symptom management

**Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness are common. At
higher doses: paranoia (rare due to indica dominance), couch-lock,
lethargy. **Not recommended for daytime use or tasks requiring focus.**

## Cultivation

Northern Lights is widely regarded as one of the easiest cannabis strains
to cultivate, making it a favorite among both beginners and commercial
growers. Its Afghan genetics confer resilience, fast flowering, and
compact growth.

- **Flowering time:** 45–55 days (6–8 weeks) indoors
- **Indoor yield:** 400–500 g/m²
- **Outdoor yield:** 500–600 g/plant
- **Height:** 80–130 cm indoors (naturally compact)
- **Recommended climate:** Temperate to Mediterranean

The plant responds well to all training methods including SCRoG, LST,
and SOG. It is naturally resistant to mold and pests. Nutrient
requirements are moderate — it is not a heavy feeder.

Harvest window is relatively short, reducing the risk of mold in humid
conditions. Trichomes turn from clear to milky at approximately week 6
of flowering.
