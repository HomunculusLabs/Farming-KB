## G-13

**G-13** is a legendary indica cannabis strain shrouded in one of the most persistent myths in cannabis [[cannabis-feminized-seeds]] genetics in existence.

## Genetic Background

The exact origins of G-13 remain unverified, and the government-breeding story is widely regarded as urban legend. No public records confirm the existence of a "G-13" project at the University of Mississippi or any federal facility. However, the strain's genetics are undeniably potent and have been confirmed through multiple independent breeding programs.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Pure indica |
| **Breeder** | Unknown (alleged government origin) |
| **Availability** | Clone-only (no verified true-breeding seed line) |
| **THC content** | 20–28% (historically reported; varies by cut) |
| **CBD content** | <1% |

### Genetic Relatives

G-13 has been used extensively in breeding programs. Many "G-13" crosses on the market are hybrids rather than the pure clone:

- **G-13 Haze** — G-13 × [[blueberry-cannabis]]
- **Super Silver Haze** — reportedly includes G-13 Haze lineage alongside [[skunk-1]]

## Terpene Profile

G-13's aroma is characterized by a pungent, earthy quality with notes of pine and citrus.

| Terpene | Role |
|---------|------|
| **[[limonene]]** | Citrus undertones; mood elevation and stress relief |
| **[[beta-caryophyllene]]** | Pepper accent; anti-inflammatory via CB2 receptor binding |

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

G-13 delivers a heavy, full-body indica experience characteristic of Afghani-type genetics. The onset is relatively fast, beginning with a warm cerebral pressure that quickly transitions into deep physical relaxation.

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes, initial head pressure and warmth
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes, pronounced body sedation and couch-lock
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours with lingering relaxation

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Intense full-body relaxation and euphoria
- Strong sedative effects suitable for evening use
- Pain relief and muscle tension reduction
- Appetite stimulation
- Sleep promotion at higher doses

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes
- Pronounced couch-lock and lethargy
- Potential anxiety or paranoia at high doses
- Dizziness in inexperienced users

### Medical Applications

G-13 is valued in medical cannabis programs for its potent therapeutic properties. Its high THC content and strong indica effects make it particularly effective for severe symptoms.

- **Chronic pain:** Heavy analgesic properties for [[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]]
- **Anxiety:** Moderate doses can provide calming relief
