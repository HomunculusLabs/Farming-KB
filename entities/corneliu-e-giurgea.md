# Corneliu E. Giurgea

## Overview

Corneliu E. Giurgea was a Romanian psychologist and chemist.
He lived from 6 January 1923 to 30 December 1995.
He is best known for synthesizing piracetam in 1964.
He later described piracetam as a member of a new class called nootropics.
Giurgea coined the term nootropic in 1972.
The word combines Greek roots for mind and turning or bending.
