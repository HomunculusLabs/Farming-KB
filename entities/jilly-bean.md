## Overview

Jilly Bean is a sativa-dominant [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain created by
Subcool of [[oner-choke-berry-jack-the-ripper-querkle-tga-subcool]] Seeds (now The Dank). A cross between Space
Queen and Orange Velvet, Jilly Bean was bred for its exceptional
flavor, potency, and uplifting effects. It has won multiple awards
including the 2013 Seattle High Times Cannabis Cup.

The strain is noted for its remarkably sweet, fruity flavor profile
resembling orange and mango candy, making it a favorite among
connoisseurs. Despite its sativa dominance, the indica influence
from Orange Velvet provides enough body relaxation to prevent the
racing thoughts common to pure sativas.

[[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]] typically ranges from 15–22%, with some phenotypes
reaching 25%. CBD content is generally low (<1%). Jilly Bean is
available in both regular and [[cervantes-feminized-seed-production]] forms.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** [[oner-jack-the-ripper-jacks-cleaner-space-queen]] — a Romulan × Cinderella 99 cross
  developed by Vic High of Brothers Grimm Seeds. Contributes
  uplifting cerebral effects, [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and fruity terpene
  profile. Sativa-dominant (approximately 70/30 sativa/indica).

- **Male parent:** Orange Velvet — an unknown indica-dominant
  hybrid selected for its strong orange aroma and relaxing body
  effects. Contributes citrus terpenes, compact [[green-cannabis-plant-anatomy-calyx-pistil-bud-structure]], and
  physical calming properties.

The approximate genetic ratio is 60–70% sativa / 30–40% indica,
though phenotypic variation is common. Some plants express more
indica-like growth patterns while maintaining sativa-dominant effects.

## Appearance and Aroma

**Bud structure:** Medium to large, slightly elongated buds with
moderate density. Calyxes are chunky and well-formed, covered in
a thick layer of trichomes that give the buds a frosty, sticky
appearance. Pistils are orange to rust-colored, abundant and curly.

**Color:** Bright to [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] with occasional purple and blue
hues, especially when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures
during late flowering. The overall impression is colorful and
vibrant, particularly the vivid orange pistils.

**Aroma:** The most distinctive feature. Strong, sweet citrus and
tropical fruit aroma dominates — fresh orange, mango, and
occasionally pineapple or grape. An underlying earthy, musky note
from the Space Queen parent provides depth. When ground, the sweet
candy-like quality intensifies dramatically.

**Flavor:** Mirrors the aroma closely. Sweet orange and mango on
the inhale, with a creamy, almost vanilla-like smoothness on the
exhale. The flavor is remarkably clean and lingering, rated as one
of the best-tasting strains in Subcool's catalog.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- **Myrcene** (~0.8–1.2%) — herbal, musky; contributes sedative
  body effects and synergizes with THC
- **Limonene** (~0.5–1.0%) — citrus, orange; primary contributor
  to the characteristic flavor; elevating and mood-enhancing
- **Caryophyllene** (~0.3–0.6%) — peppery, spicy; anti-inflammatory
  via CB2 [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]]
- **Linalool** (~0.2–0.4%) — floral, sweet; anxiolytic and calming
- **Pinene** (trace) — pine; counteracts THC-induced memory
  impairment
