## Eupatorium perfoliatum

## Overview

**Eupatorium perfoliatum**, known as boneset or common boneset, is a perennial
herbaceous plant in the aster family (Asteraceae). Native to eastern and
central [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]]. It tolerates seasonal flooding and clay soils, making it
adaptable to riparian [[companion-planting]]** — its bitter compounds may deter certain pest
  insects when interplanted with vegetables
