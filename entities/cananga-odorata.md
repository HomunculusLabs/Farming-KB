## Overview

**Cananga odorata** (Lam.) Hook.f. & Thomson, commonly known as ylang-ylang, is
a tropical evergreen tree in the family Annonaceae. Native to the rainforests
of [[comparison-essential-oils-vs-neem-oil-vs-insecticidal-soap]] for perfumery. The species has been naturalized throughout the
tropics and is commercially cultivated in Comoros, Madagascar, Réunion, and
tropical [[fukuoka-natural-farming-tropical-climates]] with peak
production during warm, humid months.

## Distribution and Habitat

Native to lowland tropical rainforests from 0–500 meters elevation in
Indonesia (Java, Sumatra, Kalimantan), Malaysia, and the Philippines. The
species thrives in humid tropical climates with annual rainfall of 1,500–3,000
mm, mean temperatures of 24–30°C, and well-drained fertile soils.

Major commercial production is centered on the Comoro Islands (particularly
Njazidja/Grande Comore), which produce approximately 40 metric tons of
ylang-ylang oil annually. Madagascar, Réunion, and the Philippines are
secondary producers. The tree is widely planted as an ornamental and
agroforestry species throughout Southeast Asia, tropical Africa, and the
[[query-how-do-fungal-volatile-compounds-affect-plant-growth-and-other-microorganisms]]. The oil is unique in perfumery for being produced in
sequential grades based on distillation time: **extra supérieure** (first hour),
**première** (1–2 hours), **deuxième** (2–4 hours), and **troisième**
(4+ hours). Extra supérieure commands the highest price and is used in
premium fragrances.

Major constituents include linalool (15–30%), germacrene D (10–20%),
geraniol (8–15%), acetates of geraniol and linalool (5–12% each),
β-caryophyllene (5–10%), and p-cresyl methyl ether (3–8%). The extra
supérieure grade is richest in the highly volatile, floral esters and
acetates that give ylang-ylang its characteristic heady, sweet-jasmine
scent.

The oil demonstrates antimicrobial activity against Gram-positive bacteria
and Candida species, anxiolytic effects in clinical aromatherapy studies,
and mild spasmolytic properties in smooth muscle tissue preparations.
