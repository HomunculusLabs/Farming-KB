## Four Season Tools

## Description

Four Season Tools is an agricultural equipment company that collaborated with Eliot Coleman on the design of a [[mobile-greenhouse-system]] incorporating drop-down side technology and full-length roof opening for ventilation. Located at 602 Westport Road, Kansas City, MO 64111. Contact: (816) 444-7330, fax (816) 561-5052, email info@fourseasontools.com, website www.fourseasontools.com.

## Classification

- **Entity Type:** Company / Greenhouse and Tool Manufacturer
- **Industry:** Agricultural Structures, Farm Equipment
- **Location:** Kansas City, Missouri, USA
- **Specialization:** Mobile greenhouse design, small-farm tools

## Mobile Greenhouse Design

Four Season Tools developed a mobile greenhouse system in collaboration with Coleman that incorporates several innovative features:

### Drop-Down Side Technology
- Both sides of the greenhouse can be lowered for ventilation and access
- Provides excellent air circulation when temperatures rise
- Allows easy access for planting, cultivation, and harvesting from the sides

### Full-Length Roof Opening
- The entire roof can open for maximum ventilation
- Critical for temperature management during warm periods
- Reduces the need for powered ventilation systems
- Enables natural convection airflow through the structure

### Mobility Options
The greenhouses are available in multiple configurations:

1. **Wheeled models (two options)** — Different wheel configurations for various farm sizes and terrain types
2. **Sled models** — For farms where rail-based wheel systems are not practical; the sled design allows the greenhouse to be dragged to new positions

### Conversion Kits
- Four Season Tools offers conversion kits to transform stationary greenhouses into mobile units
- This allows existing greenhouse infrastructure to be upgraded without complete replacement
- Reduces capital investment for farms transitioning to mobile greenhouse systems

## Small-Farm Tool Development

In addition to greenhouse structures, Four Season Tools designs efficient tools specifically for the small farm:

- Tools focused on practical, labor-saving solutions for intensive production
- Designs informed by working farmers and field-tested in real conditions
- Ongoing development of new tools; the company website features latest developments

## Key Facts

- Collaborated directly with Eliot Coleman on mobile greenhouse design
- Offers both wheeled and sled-based mobility solutions
- Provides conversion kits for existing stationary greenhouses
- Focuses on the needs of small-scale commercial farms
- Actively develops new tools and equipment for intensive farming

## Relevance to Cultivation

Four Season Tools contributes to cultivation through practical innovation:

- **Ventilation Management:** The drop-down sides and roof-opening design address one of the biggest challenges in greenhouse management — overheating during sunny periods even in winter
- **Mobility Access:** Mobile greenhouse systems enable crop rotation and [[soil-health-management]] that stationary structures cannot provide
- **Small-Farm Focus:** Their products are scaled and priced for operations that larger agricultural equipment manufacturers typically ignore
- **Sustainability:** The passive ventilation design reduces or eliminates the need for powered fans and climate control systems
- **Adaptability:** Multiple mobility options allow farms to choose the system that fits their terrain, labor availability, and budget
