# Northern Lights

Northern Lights is one of the most legendary and foundational cannabis strains in the history of the plant. A pure indica (or near-pure indica) originally developed in the Pacific Northwest of the United States in the late 1970s, it has served as the genetic backbone for countless hybrid strains and remains a benchmark indica to this day. Its potent body-centered effects, extraordinary [[fukuoka-ideal-rice-plant-morphology-compact-growth-high-yield]] habit have made it one of the most widely grown, bred, and celebrated strains in global [[cervantes-breeding-program-design-goal-setting]] involved selecting from approximately 11 pure indica plants over several generations of inbreeding and selection
- **Genetic stability:** One of the most genetically stable and true-breeding cannabis lines in existence; seeds produce remarkably uniform offspring
- **Breeder:** Originally developed by an American breeder known only as "The Indian" on the Pacific Northwest scene; later stabilized and popularized by Nevil Schoenmakers of The Seed Bank (later Sensi Seeds) in the Netherlands

The strain's genetic purity is a major factor in its ubiquity as a breeding parent. Unlike many modern hybrids with complex and sometimes disputed pedigrees, Northern Lights has a straightforward and well-documented lineage.

## Effects Profile

- **Onset:** Rapid, typically felt within minutes of consumption
- **Primary effects:** Deep physical relaxation, full-body stone, euphoria, happiness, sleepiness
- **Body effects:** Heavy, warm, enveloping body sensation; muscle relaxation; couch-lock at higher doses
- **Cerebral effects:** Mild, pleasant cerebral uplift in early stages; introspective and dreamy mental state; not typically stimulating or racy
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours depending on dose and individual tolerance
- **Medical applications:** Insomnia (primary), chronic pain, muscle spasms, anxiety, stress, PTSD, nausea, appetite stimulation, restless leg syndrome
- **Negative effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness (at high doses), potential for couch-lock and lethargy, possible paranoia in sensitive individuals (rare due to low sativa content)

Northern Lights is widely regarded as one of the best strains for sleep. Its sedative properties are reliable and profound, making it a go-to recommendation for patients with insomnia.
