     7|     7|# Gordon Wasson
     8|     8|
     9|     9|## Description
    10|    10|
    11|    11|Robert Gordon Wasson (1898–1986) was an American banker, amateur mycologist, and author who, along with his wife Valentina Wasson, is credited with introducing the psychedelic mushroom tradition of Mexico to the Western world. Their 1957 article in LIFE magazine and subsequent book "Mushrooms, Russia and History" documented the [[mazatec-mushroom-velada]] ceremony and Wasson's own experiences with [[psilocybin-mushrooms]] under the guidance of María Sabina. Leary lists Wasson as one of the authors whose "powerful accounts" serve as excellent preparation for psychedelic sessions.

    20|    20|## Key Facts
    21|    21|
    22|    22|- Vice President of Public Relations at J.P. Morgan & Company by profession
    23|    23|- Developed an interest in mushrooms during his honeymoon in the Catskill Mountains in 1927, when his wife Valentina's cultural background (Russian) led her to be a mushroom enthusiast while he was a "mycophobe"
    24|    24|- This cultural difference led to their lifelong research project on "mycophilia vs. mycophobia" across cultures
    25|    25|- In 1955, traveled to Huautla de Jiménez, Oaxaca, Mexico, and participated in a Mazatec mushroom ceremony with curandera María Sabina
    26|    26|- Published "Seeking the Magic Mushroom" in LIFE magazine (May 13, 1957), which introduced psilocybin mushrooms to the American public
    27|    27|- This article directly inspired Timothy Leary to travel to Mexico and experience psilocybin mushrooms himself in 1960
    28|    28|- Co-authored "Mushrooms, Russia and History" (1957) with his wife Valentina

    30|    30|## The Wasson-Leary Connection
    31|    31|
    32|    32|The chain of influence is direct and historically significant:
    33|    33|
    34|    34|1. Wasson publishes LIFE article about Mazatec mushroom ceremonies (1957)
    35|    35|2. Leary reads the article and travels to Cuernavaca, Mexico (1960)
    36|    36|3. Leary experiences psilocybin mushrooms and has a transformative experience
    37|    37|4. Leary begins his research program with psilocybin at Harvard
    38|    38|5. This leads to the Concord Prison Experiment, the Good Friday Experiment, and eventually "The Psychedelic Experience"

    40|    40|## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology
    41|    41|
    42|    42|Gordon Wasson is arguably the single most important figure connecting traditional mushroom use to modern mycology and cultivation:
    43|    43|
    44|    44|- His documentation of Mazatec mushroom practices created the foundation for modern psilocybin mushroom cultivation interest
    45|    45|- He coined the term "entheogen" (with Ruck and Hofmann) to replace "psychedelic" in scholarly contexts
    46|    46|- His ethnomycological work demonstrated the deep historical relationship between humans and psychoactive fungi
    47|    47|- His research directly inspired the psilocybin research program that led to modern cultivation techniques
    48|    48|- The Mazatec tradition he documented remains a touchstone for cultivators and practitioners seeking to understand traditional mushroom use
