## Overview

*Corydalis cava* (formerly *Bulbocapnos cavus*), commonly known as hollowroot or bird-in-a-bush, is a perennial herbaceous plant in the family Fumariaceae. It is native to central and southern Europe and is notable for its tuberous root system and rich isoquinoline alkaloid profile.

The species has a long history of use in European [[magical-illness-peruvian-amazon-folk-medicine]], particularly as a mild sedative, analgesic, and antispasmodic agent.

*Corydalis cava* is closely related to the well-studied Chinese [[corydalis-yanhusuo]] species.

The species is generally pest-free and requires minimal maintenance once established in suitable conditions.
