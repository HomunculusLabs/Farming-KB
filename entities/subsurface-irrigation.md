## Subsurface Irrigation

## Description

Subsurface irrigation uses underground drip and seep systems placed 12-50 cm below
the soil surface, representing the most conservative irrigation method available.
By eliminating surface evaporation entirely, these systems are best suited to arid
sites where solar evaporation is a major factor in water loss. Swales represent
the cheapest form of subsurface water development.
