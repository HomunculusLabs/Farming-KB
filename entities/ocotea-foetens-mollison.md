## Description

*Ocotea foetens*, commonly known as Til, is a large evergreen tree species of the laurel family (Lauraceae) native to the Canary Islands and Madeira. It is a keystone species of the laurisilva (laurel forest) ecosystem and one of the most effective natural condensers of atmospheric moisture known. Mollison cites it as a prime example of how individual trees can dramatically magnify available condensation surface.

## Classification

- **Kingdom**: Plantae
- **Family**: Lauraceae
- **Genus**: *Ocotea*
- **Species**: *O. foetens*
- **Common names**: Til, Stinkwood (referring to the foetens epithet)
- **Growth form**: Large evergreen tree
- **Native range**: Canary Islands, Madeira

## Key Facts

- A single giant Til tree may present 16 hectares of laminate leaf surface to sea air
- There can be approximately 100 such trees per surface hectare in natural forest
- Trees enormously magnify the available condensation surface in laurel forests
- The species is a dominant component of the Canary Islands laurisilva
- The laurisilva forests of the Canary Islands once produced massive condensation-derived precipitation before near-complete deforestation by Spanish colonizers
- Now largely restricted to remnants on the northern slopes of the wettest islands (La Gomera, La Palma, Tenerife)
- Named "foetens" for the unpleasant odor of its fresh wood
- Related to other Lauraceae including *Laurus novocanariensis* and *Persea indica*

## Ecological Role

The Til tree is a living condensation engine:

1. **Moisture capture**: Its enormous laminate leaf surface intercepts and condenses moisture from sea-facing air streams, producing copious soft condensation drip
2. **Cloud forest creation**: Along with other laurisilva species, it creates the conditions for persistent cloud forests on island and coastal slopes
3. **Watershed function**: Condensation drip from Til forests sustains perennial streams and springs on volcanic islands
4. **Microclimate moderation**: The dense evergreen canopy moderates temperature extremes and maintains high humidity
