 Vietnam Black

**Vietnam Black** is a rare pure sativa southeast-asian-mushroom-tourism-bali to landrace genetics

[[shroom-cultural-history-hippie-revolution-and-counterculture]]. Vietnamese landraces
were used traditionally in folk medicine, spiritual ceremonies, and hemp
textile production. During the Vietnam War era (1955–1975), American
soldiers encountered these strains and brought seeds back to the United
States, where they influenced early California breeding programs.

## Appearance

Vietnam Black produces distinctive elongated, finger-like sativa buds:

- **Color:** Deep [[african-landrace-sativa-modern-hybrids]]
- **Leaf:** Very narrow, elongated leaflets — classic [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] distinct from
modern hybrid strains:

### Primary Aromas
- **Deep earth and damp soil** — rich, loamy, and organic
- **Woody and forest-floor** — reminiscent of decaying hardwood and
  tropical undergrowth
- **Spicy [[comparison-black-pepper-vs-cardamom-tropical-spice-cultivation]]** — from elevated caryophyllene content
- **Subtle incense and hashish** — background notes from rare sesquiterpenes

### Flavor
On inhalation: earthy and woody with a sharp peppery quality.
On exhalation: complex spice and incense notes emerge, with a lingering
hash-like aftertaste and faint tropical sweetness.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **THC** | 18–24% | High for a landrace; selected phenotypes reach 26% |
| **CBD** | 0.05–0.2% | Very low, typical of Asian sativas |
| **CBN** | <0.1% | Negligible in fresh flower |
| **THCV** | 0.1–0.4% | Notably present, contributes to energetic onset |
| **CBC** | 0.1–0.3% | Detectable; anti-inflammatory properties |

The elevated THCV content is significant — most modern strains contain
negligible THCV. This contributes to Vietnam Black's distinctive
stimulating onset and appetite-suppressing effects.
