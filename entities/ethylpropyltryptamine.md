# 4-HO-EPT

**4-Hydroxy-N-ethyl-N-propyltryptamine** (4-HO-EPT) is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine belonging to the 4-hydroxytryptamine family ([[psilocin]] analogs). It features an unsymmetrical N-ethyl/N-propyl substitution pattern, distinguishing it from the better-known 4-HO-DMT (psilocin) and 4-HO-DIPT.





## Chemistry

- **IUPAC**: 4-Hydroxy-N-ethyl-N-propyl-1H-indole-3-ethanamine
- **Molecular formula**: C₁₅H₂₂N₂O
- **Molecular weight**: 246.35 g/mol
- The 4-hydroxyindole core is shared with psilocin (4-HO-DMT); the asymmetric N-substitution (ethyl + propyl) is the defining feature
- Structurally sits between 4-HO-DET (diethyl) and 4-HO-DPT (dipropyl) in the N-alkyl substitution series
- Freebase is an oily liquid; typically encountered as the fumarate salt for stability





## Pharmacology

- Believed to act primarily as a **5-HT₂A receptor agonist**, consistent with the classical psychedelic mechanism of 4-substituted tryptamines
- The 4-hydroxy group is essential for psychedelic activity in this series; it is the active metabolite of phosphorolated prodrugs (e.g., 4-PO-EPT would be dephosphorylated to 4-HO-EPT in vivo)
- N-alkyl chain length modulates potency, duration, and qualitative effects — longer chains generally increase duration and alter the tactile/physical component
- The unsymmetrical N-substitution may produce a slightly different receptor binding conformation than symmetrical analogs





## Dosing & Duration

- Limited human data; estimated active dose range approximately 10–25 mg (oral) based on structural analogies
- Expected onset: 20–60 minutes (oral)
- Expected duration: 4–7 hours (oral), intermediate between 4-HO-DMT (~4 hr) and 4-HO-DPT (~8 hr)
- Onset and duration consistent with the intermediate N-alkyl chain length





## Effects

- **Visual**: Color enhancement, geometric patterning, warping/breathing of surfaces — typical of the 4-hydroxytryptamine class
- **Cognitive**: Altered thought patterns, enhanced introspection, philosophical ideation, mild ego modulation
- **Physical**: Body load described as moderate, with some users noting a heavier or more "grounded" somatic sensation compared to psilocin
- **Emotional**: Range from euphoria to anxiety depending on set, setting, and dose; generally empathogenic qualities less pronounced than in 4-HO-MIPT or 4-HO-MET
- The propyl chain is associated with increased tactile and physical effects relative to the methyl or ethyl series





## Context in TIHKAL

- Documented in the "What's New" or later commentary sections of TIHKAL as part of the broader exploration of 4-substituted N-alkyl tryptamines
- Shulgin's research program systematically explored the structure-activity relationships of tryptamine substitutions; 4-HO-EPT represents one data point in the N-alkyl chain length series
- Part of the intellectual lineage from psilocin (4-HO-DMT) → 4-HO-DET → 4-HO-DPT with mixed ethyl/propyl substitution





## Safety & Legal Status

- No toxicological data specific to 4-HO-EPT; general tryptamine precautions apply
- Likely controlled under tryptamine analogue acts in many jurisdictions
- No known deaths or serious adverse events attributed specifically to 4-HO-EPT in the literature
- As with all research chemicals, purity, dose accuracy, and the absence of pharmacokinetic data represent the primary risk factors
