Kingdom: Plantae → Tracheophyta → Magnoliopsida → Fabales → **Fabaceae → Faboideae → Hedysareae → Onobrychis → O. viciifolia**.

The species was described by Scopoli in 1772 and has been cultivated in Europe for centuries under the synonym *O. sativa* Lam.

The genus *Onobrychis* comprises approximately 150 species centered in the Mediterranean and Irano-Turanian floristic regions.

**Growth habit:** Erect to semi-erect perennial, 30–80 cm tall, forming dense tufts from a woody crown and deep taproot system.

**Roots:** Strong taproot reaching 1–2 m depth with numerous lateral roots and effective **Rhizobium** nodules fixing 100–200 kg N/ha/year.

**Stems:** Hollow, branched, slightly angular, sparsely to densely pubescent, arising from a persistent woody base.

**Leaves:** Pinnately compound, 6–12 pairs of leaflets per leaf, leaflets oblong to elliptic, 8–20 mm long, mucronate at the apex.

**Stipules** are membranous and semi-sagittate, a distinctive taxonomic character for the genus.

**Flowers:** Papilionaceous, pink to reddish-pink, borne in dense, axillary racemes 4–10 cm long; standard 10–14 mm, wings 8–10 mm, keel slightly [[gadd-fungal-wood-decay-brown-rot-white-rot-soft-rot-mechanisms]] to dark brown, with a hard seed coat requiring scarification for germination.
