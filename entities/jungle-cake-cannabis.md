## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Wedding Cake (Triangle Kush × [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] with occasional
deep purple undertones, especially when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures
during late flowering.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosted,
silvery-white appearance. Bright orange pistils wind through the calyxes,
contrasting sharply with the resin-coated surface. The overall visual
impression is that of a premium, high-resin modern hybrid.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is complex and layered, dominated by a sweet, creamy vanilla
scent from the Wedding Cake parent. Secondary notes include earthy musk,
subtle diesel fuel, and a faint peppery spice from the White Fire #43 lineage.

On the palate, Jungle Cake delivers a smooth, dessert-like flavor profile.
The primary taste is sweet cream and vanilla, followed by earthy undertones
and a peppery exhale. Some phenotypes express more pronounced citrus or
tropical fruit notes, reflecting variation in terpene ratios.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**
- **Caryophyllene** — peppery, spicy; contributes anti-inflammatory properties
- **Limonene** — citrus; adds mood elevation and stress relief
- **Myrcene** — earthy, herbal; promotes sedation and body relaxation
- **Linalool** — floral; enhances calming effects

**Cannabinoid profile:**
- **THC:** 25–30% (some phenotypes test above 30%)
- **CBD:** <0.1%
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** trace

The high caryophyllene content distinguishes Jungle Cake from many
Wedding Cake crosses, giving it a spicier, more complex aromatic signature.

## Effects and Cultivation

Effects onset is typically within 5–10 minutes of consumption. The initial
experience is cerebral — euphoric and slightly focused — before transitioning
into a deeply relaxing body stone. Most users report a balanced experience
suitable for late afternoon or evening use.
