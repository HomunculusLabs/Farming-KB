## 3-TE (3-Thioescaline; 4-Ethoxy-5-methoxy-3-methylthiophenethylamine)

3-TE is a thio-substituted phenethylamine that replaces one of the oxygen atoms in the [[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #150.

## Chemical Classification

3-TE belongs to the phenethylamine class of [[escaline-entity]] with a methylthio group.

The compound retains the ethoxy group at the 4-position and a methoxy group at the 5-position.

## Pharmacological Profile

3-TE is an active psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 60 to 80 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 8 to 12 hours.

The compound produces effects that include both visual and introspective qualities.

Reports suggest a rich, artistic experience with good potential for creative exploration.

## Qualitative Effects

At 60 mg, subjects reported possible time slowing, with voices on the radio seeming to have a deeper pitch.

Music produced easy flight of imagination and fantasy.

At 70 mg, subjects found themselves in a good, rich place and thoroughly enjoyed introspection.

There was a preference for not talking and interacting, which was acceptable to others present.

Several subjects indicated they would repeat the experience without hesitation.

At 80 mg, art interpretation and imagery with music were described as remarkable.

The material was reported to touch deep creative and aesthetic faculties.

## Potency Relationships

The thio-series of compounds demonstrates interesting structure-activity relationships.

Replacing an oxygen with sulfur at the 3-position of escaline produces a compound with approximately one-third the potency of the parent.

A systematic analysis of thio-substituted mescaline analogues shows that potency changes predictably based on which oxygen is replaced.

The 3-position substitution with sulfur appears to preserve the qualitative character of the experience while reducing the effective dosage.

This pattern of sulfur-for-oxygen substitution (thio-analogues) is a recurring theme in medicinal chemistry.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 60 - 80 mg.

Duration: 8 - 12 h.
