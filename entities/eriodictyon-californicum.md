## Bioactive Compounds

The resinous leaf coating contains a complex mixture of flavonoids and
terpenoids:

- **Eriodictyol** — a flavanone, the signature compound; a selective
  expectorant that increases respiratory fluid secretion while
  reducing its viscosity.
- **Hesperidin and homoeriodictyol** — flavanone glycosides that
  complement eriodictyol's bronchodilatory activity.
