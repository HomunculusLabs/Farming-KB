# Calcium Phosphate

## Description

Calcium phosphate is a critical bionutrient in [[yasushi-inoue]], calcium phosphate is the essential nutrient that must be supplied during this critical transition period to ensure high-quality flower and fruit production. Natural farmers refer to the changeover period as the plant's "morning sickness," where additional nutrition is urgently needed.

## Classification

- **Category:** Bionutrient / Mineral supplement
- **Chemical Formula:** Ca₃(PO₄)₂
- **Function:** Stage-specific plant nutrient for changeover period
- **Source:** Eggshells + vinegar (natural preparation)

## Key Facts

- Calcium phosphate is critical during the changeover period between vegetative and reproductive plant growth stages.
- Under Nutrioperiodism theory, it is the single most important nutrient during the transition phase.
- Plants need nitrogen during vegetative stage, calcium phosphate during changeover, and potassium during reproductive stage.
- Natural preparation method: roast eggshells to generate ash, then soak in an equal volume of vinegar for several weeks until the eggshells break down.
- The resulting solution is diluted 1:20 with water for application as a foliar spray or soil drench.
- Ash from soybean stems is cited as an excellent alternative source of calcium phosphate.
- Application during the changeover period improves overall plant health and productivity.
- The nutrient does not replace but supplements the standard NPK fertilization program.
- Calcium phosphate supports the development of flowers and fruits by providing essential calcium and phosphorus at the critical moment of reproductive organ formation.

## Preparation Method (Natural Farming)

1. Collect eggshells.
2. Roast eggshells until they generate good ashes.
3. Place roasted eggshells in container.
4. Add approximately equal visual volume of vinegar (brown rice vinegar preferred).
5. Allow to sit for a couple of weeks until eggshells are fully broken down by the acetic acid.
6. Dilute the resulting solution 1:20 with non-chlorinated water.
7. Spray or water plants during the changeover period.

## Relevance to Cultivation

Calcium phosphate addresses a commonly overlooked nutritional window in plant development. Most growers focus on vegetative (N) and flowering (K) nutrition but neglect the transition period. For cultivators of fruiting crops — including mushrooms where calcium supplementation is well-known to improve fruiting body formation — the concept of stage-specific mineral nutrition is directly applicable. The DIY preparation from eggshells and vinegar makes this an accessible, nearly free input for any grower.
