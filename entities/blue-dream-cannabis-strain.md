# Blue Dream (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

Blue Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain that has become one of the most popular and widely cultivated strains in the United States and globally. Originally bred in Northern California, it is a cross between the indica **Blueberry** (DJ Short) and the sativa **Haze**. Blue Dream is celebrated for its balanced effects — delivering cerebral stimulation alongside gentle physical relaxation — making it suitable for both daytime and evening use.

The strain's name derives from the blue-purple hues that its buds develop under cooler growing conditions, combined with the dreamy, euphoric quality of its effects.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Female parent:** Blueberry (indica) — DJ Short's legendary indica known for its berry aroma and deep purple coloration
- **Male parent:** Haze (sativa) — A classic sativa landrace-based hybrid with origins in the Santa Cruz Mountains
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 60% sativa / 40% indica
- **Ploidy:** Diploid

The combination produces a plant that inherits Blueberry's bag appeal (color, aroma, trichome production) and Haze's uplifting, creative effects with a significantly shorter flowering time than pure Haze.

## Morphology

### Plant Structure

Blue Dream grows as a tall, vigorous plant with strong lateral branching. In indoor cultivation, it typically reaches 100-150 cm; outdoors it can exceed 200 cm. The plant exhibits sativa-dominant morphology with long internodal spacing and large, fingered fan leaves that may display purple-to-blue pigmentation in cooler temperatures.

### Bud Characteristics

- **Density:** Medium-dense, elongated conical buds
- **Color:** Light to dark green with blue, lavender, and purple undertones; orange pistils woven throughout
- **Trichomes:** Heavy trichome coverage with a mix of cloudy and amber heads at peak maturity
- **Aroma:** Sweet blueberry with earthy, floral, and herbal undertones; subtle vanilla and spice notes
- **Flavor:** Blueberry-forward with creamy, earthy finish; smooth smoke

## Cannabinoid Profile

Typical cannabinoid composition ranges (varies by cultivator and phenotype):

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| THC | 17-24% | Primary psychoactive compound |
| CBD | 0.1-0.3% | Present in trace amounts |
| CBG | 0.1-0.5% | Minor cannabinoid |
| THCV | <0.1% | Trace levels |
| CBC | 0.1-0.3% | Minor cannabinoid |

Some cultivators have developed high-CBD phenotypes of Blue Dream for medical markets, reaching CBD levels of 8-15%.

## Terpene Profile

The strain's distinctive aroma and therapeutic effects are driven by its terpene composition:

- **Myrcene (0.5-1.2%):** The dominant terpene, contributing earthy, musky notes. Enhances THC penetration across the blood-brain barrier. Associated with sedative and muscle-relaxant effects.
- **Caryophyllene (0.3-0.8%):** Peppery, spicy terpene that acts as a selective CB2 receptor agonist. Contributes anti-inflammatory properties.
- **Linalool (0.2-0.5%):** Floral, lavender-like terpene with anxiolytic and sedative properties. Modulates the psychoactive intensity of THC.
- **Pinene (0.1-0.4%):** Piney terpene (α-pinene dominant) that may counteract THC-induced short-term memory impairment. Bronchodilator and anti-inflammatory.
- **Limonene (0.1-0.3%):** Citrusy terpene with mood-elevating and stress-relieving properties.
- **Humulene (0.1-0.2%):** Woody, earthy terpene with appetite-suppressant properties.
- **Ocimene (trace):** Sweet, herbaceous terpene with potential decongestant effects.

## Effects Profile

### Subjective Experience

The onset is typically within 5-15 minutes when inhaled. Effects are characterized by:

1. **Initial phase (0-30 min):** Euphoric cerebral uplift, enhanced creativity, sociability, and sensory appreciation. Users commonly report increased focus and motivation.
2. **Middle phase (30-90 min):** Gradual body relaxation without heavy sedation. A balanced, calm state persists while mental clarity is largely maintained.
3. ** comedown (90-180 min):** Gentle transition to relaxation. May lead to drowsiness in higher doses or later in the experience.

The balanced effect profile makes Blue Dream unusual among high-THC strains — it rarely produces the anxiety or paranoia associated with pure sativas, nor the lethargy of heavy indicas.

### Medicinal Applications

Blue Dream is frequently recommended for:

- **Chronic pain management:** Myrcene and caryophyllene contribute analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects
- **Anxiety and depression:** Linalool and limonene provide anxiolytic benefits; THC elevates mood
- **Fatigue:** Sativa genetics provide energizing, motivating effects at moderate doses
- **Migraine headaches:** Combined analgesic and anti-inflammatory terpene action
- **Nausea:** THC-mediated antiemetic properties
- **PTSD:** Euphoric effects may help with mood while the gentle body relaxation aids with somatic symptoms

## Cultivation

### Growing Conditions

- **Flowering time:** 9-10 weeks indoors
- **Indoor yield:** 400-600 g/m²
- **Outdoor yield:** 500-700 g/plant
- **Harvest (Northern hemisphere):** Mid to late October
- **Optimal temperature:** 20-28°C (vegetative), 18-24°C (flowering)
- **Optimal humidity:** 50-70% (vegetative), 40-50% (flowering)
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder; responds well to phosphorus and potassium during flowering
- **Training:** LST, ScROG, and topping recommended due to tall stature
- **Resistance:** Moderate resistance to mold and mildew; good pest resistance

### Cultivation Tips

- Cooler night temperatures (15-18°C) during the final 2-3 weeks of flowering enhance blue/purple coloration through anthocyanin expression
- Defoliation is recommended in week 3-4 of flowering to improve light penetration to lower bud sites
- The strain is relatively forgiving for novice growers while rewarding experienced cultivators with exceptional quality

### Common Phenotypes

Multiple phenotypes exist within the broader Blue Dream gene pool. The "true" or "original" Blue Dream phenotype exhibits stronger blueberry aroma and more pronounced purple coloration, while later commercial phenotypes may lean more heavily toward Haze characteristics.

## Notable Variants and Crosses

- **Blue Dream CBD:** High-CBD phenotype (8-15% CBD, <1% THC)
- **Dreamweaver:** Blue Dream × White Widow
- **Azure Haze:** Blue Dream × Amnesia Haze
- **Blueberry Dream:** Blue Dream × Blueberry (backcross to intensify berry traits)
- **Dream Queen:** A related strain sometimes confused with Blue Dream
