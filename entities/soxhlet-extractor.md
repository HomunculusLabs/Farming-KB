# Soxhlet Extractor

## Description

The Soxhlet extractor is a piece of laboratory apparatus invented by Franz von Soxhlet in 1879, designed for the continuous extraction of a desired compound from a solid material using a refluxing solvent. In cannabis processing, it is the primary method for extracting cannabinoid-rich oils from plant material.

## Classification

- **Category**: Laboratory [[cannabis-alchemy-book]], overnight soxhleting (8-24 hours) is recommended for complete extraction
- Blending cannabis into fine powder before soxhlet extraction dramatically reduces required time (to as little as 2 hours)

## Variations and Optimizations

- **Pre-soaking**: Extended soaking (24+ hours) before soxhleting reduces machine operation time significantly
- **Cycling extraction**: Multiple solvent changes during soaking phase accelerate extraction
- **Blender method**: Grinding cannabis to fine powder in a blender with alcohol prior to soxhlet extraction greatly increases surface area and extraction speed
- **Multiple baskets**: Layering different grades of material for differential extraction
