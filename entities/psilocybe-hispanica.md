The specific epithet *hispanica* refers to Spain, the country of the type locality.

The species was differentiated from the closely related [[psilocybe-semilanceata]].

The species belongs to section Semilanceata of the genus psilocybe, characterized by species with a peeling gelatinous pellicle on the cap, hygrophanous pileus, and a preference for grassland habitats.

Other members of this section include [[psilocybe-serbica]], [[psilocybe-semilanceata]], with which it may be confused in the field.

Grows solitary to gregarious on rich organic soils in pastures and meadows, particularly in areas grazed by livestock.

## Morphology

### Cap

Conic to campanulate (bell-shaped), 5-25 mm diameter.

Hygrophanous: ochraceous to brown when moist, fading to pale straw or nearly white when dry.

Surface smooth and notably viscid when wet, with translucent-striate margin.

A separable gelatinous pellicle can be peeled from the cap surface, characteristic of section Semilanceata.

Prominent, acute umbo gives a distinctly conical profile when young.

### Gills

Adnate to adnexed attachment, moderately crowded.

Grayish when young, maturing to dark purplish brown with spore development.

Edges typically whitish and fringed.

Lamellulae are truncate and of varying lengths.

Do not show prominent blue staining, unlike some other psilocybin species.

### Stem

40-90 mm long, 1-2 mm thick.

Cylindrical, equal or slightly thicker at the base.

Pale to whitish, smooth or finely pruinose near the apex.

Hollow and somewhat cartilaginous.

Partial veil remnants may form a fibrillose annular zone, often evanescent.

Base may be covered with fine whitish mycelial threads.

### Microscopic Features

Spores ellipsoid to amygdaliform, approximately 12-15 x 7-9 um.

Spore print dark purple-brown to nearly black.

Spores with thick wall and distinct germ pore.

Basidia 2- or 4-spored, clamped at the base.

Cheilocystidia numerous, ventricose to lageniform, often with narrow neck, forming conspicuous sterile band on gill edges.

Pleurocystidia absent or very rare.

Pileipellis a cutis with embedded gelatinous hyphae.

## Chemistry

Chemical analysis confirmed the presence of [[psilocin]].

Concentrations are moderate, generally lower than those of [[psilocybe-azurescens]].

Estimated total alkaloid content in the range of 0.1-0.5% dry weight.

Does not show prominent bluing reactions upon bruising, correlating with lower psilocin concentrations.

No [[norbaeocystin]] definitively reported, though comprehensive alkaloid profiling has not been performed.

Flesh is whitish and does not change color appreciably when cut.

## Similar Species

[[psilocybe-serbica]] — Southeastern European species found in mountainous regions.

Has a more robust stature and grows on decaying wood rather than in grasslands.

## Ethnobotanical Notes

No documented traditional or indigenous uses of psilocybe hispanica exist.

As a recently described species (2000), it was identified through scientific survey rather than ethnobotanical inquiry.
