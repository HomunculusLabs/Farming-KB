# Traveling Sprinkler (Compost Tea Application)

## Description

A traveling sprinkler is a lawn irrigation device that propels itself along a hose laid out on the ground, providing even water coverage across large areas. In the soil food web framework, a traveling sprinkler paired with an inline fertilizer dispenser is one of the best methods for applying compost tea to large lawns. The dispenser tank (normally used for soluble fertilizers) is filled with actively aerated compost tea instead, which is then fed into the water stream as the sprinkler traverses the lawn.

## Classification

- **Type:** Mechanical lawn irrigation device
- **Mechanism:** Self-propelled along a laid-out hose
- **Compost tea adaptation:** Paired with inline fertilizer dispenser
- **Scale:** Suitable for medium to large lawns

## Key Facts

- Self-propelled sprinkler that follows along a hose laid out on the lawn surface
- Provides even, consistent coverage across the entire lawn area
- Paired with an inline fertilizer dispenser (tank designed for soluble fertilizers) connected to the water source
- The fertilizer dispenser is filled with actively aerated compost tea instead of chemical fertilizer
- The dispenser feeds tea into the water stream as the sprinkler travels, diluting and distributing the tea evenly
- Recommended as one of the best ways to apply compost tea to large lawns
- Eliminates the labor of manual spraying or hauling watering cans across large areas
- Alternative application methods for different scales:
  - Concrete sprayer (small areas)
  - Gasoline backpack mist sprayer (medium to large areas)
  - Gas blower on lowest setting (very large areas, acre-scale)
  - Commercial tea sprayer service (professional application)
- The traveling sprinkler method ensures the tea reaches both soil and grass blades for comprehensive coverage
- Can cover an entire lawn in a single pass with consistent application rate

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The traveling sprinkler adaptation demonstrates practical innovation in biological application:

- **Equipment repurposing:** Standard lawn care equipment (fertilizer dispensers) can be repurposed for biological inoculant delivery
- **Scale-appropriate application:** As compost tea use scales from garden beds to lawns to acreages, application methods must evolve
- **Gentle delivery:** The sprinkler provides gentle, rain-like delivery that does not damage microbial populations (unlike high-pressure sprayers)
- **Even distribution:** Consistent coverage ensures all areas receive adequate microbial inoculation
- **Integration with existing infrastructure:** Homeowners can use their existing sprinkler and hose systems, lowering the barrier to compost tea adoption
