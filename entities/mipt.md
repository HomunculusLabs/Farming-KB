## Overview

N-Methyl-N-isopropyltryptamine (MIPT) is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine
compound first synthesized and characterized by [[alexander-shulgin]]. It is
documented in TIHKAL (Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved) with a full
self-experiment report.

MIPT is structurally related to DMT and DPT, differing by the N-alkyl
substitution pattern. The isopropyl group confers unique pharmacokinetic
properties that distinguish it from other short-acting tryptamines.

The compound exists as two positional isomers: 5-MIPT (active psychedelic)
and 7-MIPT (less active). The 5-substituted form is the standard reference
when the unqualified name MIPT is used.

## Chemical Properties

**Molecular formula:** C₁₄H₂₀N₂
**Molecular weight:** 216.32 g/mol
**IUPAC name:** N-methyl-N-propan-2-yl-1H-indol-3-ylmethanamine

**SMILES:** CN(C(C)C)CC1=CNC2=C1C=CC=C2
**CAS number:** 68858-53-3 (freebase)

MIPT is a freebase oil at room temperature with limited stability. It is
typically stored as a crystalline salt (hydrochloride or fumarate) to improve
[[psilocybin-shelf-life-stability]] and handling properties. The freebase is soluble in organic solvents
such as ethanol, DCM, and ether.

The compound is sensitive to oxidation and should be stored under inert
atmosphere at low temperature. UV light accelerates degradation. Salt forms
are significantly more stable for long-term storage.

## Pharmacology

MIPT acts primarily as a 5-HT₂A receptor agonist, the canonical mechanism
underlying psychedelic effects. In vitro binding data indicate moderate
affinity for 5-HT₂A (Ki ≈ 350–500 nM), making it less potent than DMT (Ki ≈
30 nM) but comparable to DPT.

The isopropyl substitution increases lipophilicity compared to DMT, which
affects blood-brain barrier penetration and metabolic stability. This
structural modification is responsible for the compound's longer duration of
action relative to DMT.

Additional receptor interactions include weak 5-HT₁A agonism and minimal
affinity for 5-HT₂C. The compound shows negligible activity at dopaminergic
and adrenergic receptors, contributing to a relatively clean subjective
experience profile with minimal peripheral stimulation.

MAO-A metabolism occurs but at a slower rate than DMT, accounting for the
extended duration. The N-methyl group provides partial protection against
first-pass oxidative deamination.

## Effects

The subjective effects profile is described as warm, gentle, and less intense
than DMT or 5-MeO-DMT. Visual effects include color enhancement, geometric
patterning, and mild visual distortion. Auditory effects are less prominent
than with other tryptamines.

The headspace is characterized by calm, introspective content with reduced
anxiety compared to more potent tryptamines. Emotional openness and
heightened sensory appreciation are commonly reported. [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]] occurs
only at higher dose ranges.

Common dose ranges are 15–30 mg orally (freebase equivalent) and 5–15 mg
via vaporization or insufflation. Onset is 15–30 minutes orally, with peak
effects at 60–90 minutes and total duration of 3–5 hours.

Body load is generally mild. Some users report transient nausea during onset
