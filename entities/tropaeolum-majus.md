---# Tropaeolum majus

## Overview

Nasturtium (*Tropaeolum majus*) is an annual (sometimes short-lived
perennial in frost-free climates) climbing or trailing plant native
to the Andes of Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, and Peru. Widely grown
as both an ornamental and edible plant, nasturtium is celebrated for
its peppery-tasting leaves and flowers, its role as a companion plant
[[mycelium-running-mushroom-antimicrobial-properties-species-chart]]
backed by modern pharmacological research. All above-ground parts are
edible, making it one of the most versatile multi-purpose plants in
permaculture and [[leaf-tissue-analysis-crop-nutrient-status]].

## Medicinal Properties

- **Antibiotic activity:** Benzyl isothiocyanate has shown in vitro
  activity against *[[bloomfield-candida-albicans-yeast-hyphal-dimorphism]]*; historically
  used as a natural antibiotic in [[chiric-caspi-brunfelsia-south-american-hallucinogen]] traditional
  medicine.
- **Respiratory support:** Traditional use as an expectorant for
  bronchitis, coughs, and upper respiratory infections; the
  glucosinolate-derived compounds help thin mucus.
- **Wound healing:** Topical poultices of crushed leaves applied to
  minor cuts and infections; antimicrobial action reduces secondary
  infection risk.
- **Urinary tract:** Diuretic properties support urinary health;
  traditionally used for UTIs and kidney complaints.
- **Antioxidant:** High flavonoid and vitamin C content provides
  systemic antioxidant protection and supports immune function.
