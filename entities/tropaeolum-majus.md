# Tropaeolum majus

## Overview

Nasturtium (*Tropaeolum majus*) is an annual (sometimes short-lived
perennial in frost-free climates) climbing or trailing plant native
to the Andes of Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, and Peru. Widely grown
as both an ornamental and edible plant, nasturtium is celebrated for
its peppery-tasting leaves and flowers, its role as a companion plant
[[plant-disease-management-in-organic-gardens]], and its broad-spectrum [[mycelium-running-mushroom-antimicrobial-properties-species-chart]]
backed by modern pharmacological research. All above-ground parts are
edible, making it one of the most versatile multi-purpose plants in
permaculture and [[holzer-kitchen-garden-herbs-and-medicinal-plants]] design.

## Taxonomy and Botany

- **Family:** Tropaeolaceae (nasturtium family)
- **Genus:** *Tropaeolum*
- **Order:** Brassicales (shares glucosinolate chemistry with
  Brassicaceae)
- **Growth habit:** Annual trailing or climbing vine; stems reach
  1–3 m with support, or form 30–50 cm mounds without.
- **Leaves:** Peltate (shield-shaped), 5–15 cm diameter, on long
  petioles; bright green, slightly waxy surface.
- **Flowers:** Funnel-shaped, 3–6 cm diameter, in warm colors —
  reds, oranges, yellows, and bicolors; long nectar spur on the back.
- **Seeds:** Large (6–8 mm), round, green when immature turning
  grey-brown; each flower produces 3 seeds.

## Chemical Constituents

- **Glucosinolates:** Primarily glucotropaeolin, which hydrolyzes to
  benzyl isothiocyanate (BITC) — a potent antimicrobial compound.
- **Benzyl isothiocyanate (BITC):** Major bioactive metabolite with
  documented antibacterial, antifungal, antiviral, and anti-
  inflammatory activities.
- **Flavonoids:** Quercetin, kaempferol, and myricetin glycosides
  providing antioxidant activity.
- **Vitamin C:** Leaves contain approximately 130 mg/100g fresh
  weight — comparable to citrus fruits.
- **Carotenoids:** Lutein and β-carotene concentrated in flowers.
- **Tropaeolins:** Anthocyanin pigments responsible for flower
  coloration with additional antioxidant properties.
- **Manganese and iron:** Notable mineral content in [[leaf-tissue-analysis-crop-nutrient-status]].

## Medicinal Properties

- **Antibiotic activity:** Benzyl isothiocyanate has shown in vitro
  activity against *[[staphylococcus-aureus]]*, *Escherichia coli*,
  *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, and *[[bloomfield-candida-albicans-yeast-hyphal-dimorphism]]*; historically
  used as a natural antibiotic in [[chiric-caspi-brunfelsia-south-american-hallucinogen]] traditional
  medicine.
- **Respiratory support:** Traditional use as an expectorant for
  bronchitis, coughs, and upper respiratory infections; the
  glucosinolate-derived compounds help thin mucus.
- **Wound healing:** Topical poultices of crushed leaves applied to
  minor cuts and infections; antimicrobial action reduces secondary
  infection risk.
- **Urinary tract:** Diuretic properties support urinary health;
  traditionally used for UTIs and kidney complaints.
- **Antioxidant:** High flavonoid and vitamin C content provides
  systemic antioxidant protection and supports immune function.
