## Overview

Zkittlez is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Grape Ape** and **Grapefruit** with a third, closely guarded proprietary parent, believed by many breeders to be an undisclosed *LSD* phenotype.

The strain was developed in the San Francisco Bay Area by **3rd Gen Family** and **Terp Hogz**, a breeding collective known for producing intensely flavored West Coast genetics.

Zkittlez won first place for Best Indica at the 2016 Emerald Cup, propelling it from regional favorite to mainstream commercial success. It has since become one of the most influential breeding parent strains of the 2018–2024 era.

The name derives from the candy-like fruit aroma and flavor profile, which resembles a bag of Skittles candy. The strain is renowned for its vibrant purple, green, and orange bud presentation.

## Lineage

Zkittlez is a complex polyhybrid. The confirmed parentage includes Grape Ape (indica-dominant cross of Mendocino Purps, Skunk, and Afghani) and Grapefruit (a sativa-dominant hybrid of Unknown Indica × Cinderella 99).

The third parent has never been officially disclosed by the breeders, though community consensus and genetic testing suggest an LSD phenotype. LSD is itself a cross of Skunk #1 and Mazar-I-Sharif.

This three-way cross results in a roughly 60/40 indica-to-sativa ratio, though phenotype expression varies significantly depending on cut and growing conditions.

Zkittlez has served as a parent for dozens of commercially successful crosses, including Runtz, Pink Runtz, and numerous proprietary "Z" lines that dominate modern dispensary menus.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large in size with a dense, chunky structure typical of indica-dominant genetics. The coloration is one of Zkittlez's most distinguishing features.

Calyxes display deep violet and royal purple hues intermixed with forest green, often with vivid orange pistils weaving throughout. The purple coloration is a genuine genetic expression, not temperature-dependent like many purple strains.

Trichome coverage is heavy, with a thick layer of milky-white to amber trichomes giving the buds a frosted, crystalline appearance. The resin production is prolific, making Zkittlez popular for hash and concentrate extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is intensely fruity, dominated by notes of grape, berry, citrus, and tropical fruit. A sweet, candy-like undertone pervades the bouquet, with subtle earthy and herbal background notes.

On the palate, the flavor mirrors the aroma: a burst of mixed berry and grape sweetness with tropical citrus highlights. The smoke is smooth and expansive, with a lingering candied aftertaste.

The terpene profile is dominated by **caryophyllene**, **limonene**, and **linalool**, with secondary contributions from myrcene, humulene, and terpinolene. The caryophyllene provides a subtle peppery spice that balances the sweetness.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

Dominant terpenes include caryophyllene (0.5–1.2%), limonene (0.4–1.0%), and linalool (0.3–0.8%). Myrcene is typically present at 0.2–0.5%, lower than many indicas.

Total cannabinoid content typically ranges from 18–23% THC, with CBD generally below 0.1%. Some phenotypes have tested as high as 26% THC under optimal cultivation.

The terpinolene content, while not dominant, contributes to the strain's complex fruity aroma and may modulate the overall entourage effect. Minor cannabinoids detected include CBC and CBG in trace amounts.

The synergistic interaction between caryophyllene (which binds CB2 receptors) and linalool (GABAergic activity) may contribute to the strain's anxiolytic and analgesic properties independent of THC.

## Effects

The onset is moderately fast, typically felt within 5–10 minutes of inhalation. Initial effects are cerebral and euphoric, characterized by an uplifted mood, creative energy, and heightened sensory perception.

As the high progresses, a deep body relaxation emerges without heavy sedation. Users report feeling calm, content, and physically at ease while maintaining mental clarity.

The strain is well-suited for afternoon or early evening use. At higher doses, the sedative effects become more pronounced, potentially leading to couch-lock and sleepiness.

Medical users commonly employ Zkittlez for stress relief, mild anxiety, chronic pain, insomnia, and appetite stimulation. The mood-elevating effects are particularly noted for depression and PTSD symptom management.

## Cultivation

Zkittlez is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, with specific environmental requirements for optimal terpene expression and color development. It performs well in both indoor and outdoor settings.

Indoor flowering time is 8–9 weeks, with yields of 10–14 ounces per square meter under optimal conditions. Outdoor harvest occurs in mid-October, with yields of 12–16 ounces per plant.

The strain responds well to low-stress training (LST) and scrogging. Nutrient requirements are moderate; overfeeding can diminish the distinctive flavor profile.

Temperature control during late flowering (cooler nighttime temperatures around 15–18°C) enhances the purple coloration. Relative humidity should be maintained at 40–50% during flowering to prevent bud rot.

## Notable Phenotypes

Multiple distinct phenotypes exist within the Zkittlez genetic line. The original "Zkittlez #1" cut, maintained by Terp Hogz, is considered the standard expression of the strain.

Several stabilized crosses have achieved independent recognition. Runtz, a Zkittlez × Gelato cross, became one of the best-selling strains of 2020–2022 and won Leafly's Strain of the Year.

Pink Runtz, a selected phenotype of the Runtz cross, displays more pronounced pink coloration and a sweeter flavor profile. Original Z (also called OZ) is a backcrossed selection with enhanced potency.

Other notable derivatives include Gary Payton, a Zkittlez × Y Griega cross known for its potent cerebral effects, and Black Runtz, a dark-colored phenotype with amplified terpene production.
