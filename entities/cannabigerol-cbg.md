# Cannabigerol (CBG)

**Type:** Chemical / Phytocannabinoid
**Category:** Minor [[cannabis-indica]]. It is often called the "mother cannabinoid" or
"stem cell cannabinoid" because it serves as the biosynthetic precursor
from which all other major cannabinoids are synthesized. In the living
cannabis plant, CBGA (cannabigerolic acid) is enzymatically converted
into THCA, CBDA, and CBCA. As a result, CBG is typically present in
only trace amounts (usually less than 1%) in most cannabis strains.

## Key Details

### Chemical Properties
- Molecular formula: C21H32O2
- Non-psychoactive cannabinoid
- Binds with both CB1 and CB2 receptors
- Biosynthetic precursor to THC, CBD, and CBC
- Exists as CBG-acid (CBGA) in raw, fresh cannabis
- CBGA converts to THCA, CBDA, and CBCA via plant enzymes

### Receptor Affinity
CBG demonstrates dual [[cancer-immune-modulation-mechanisms]].

### Therapeutic Applications
- **Anti-cancer:** Tested alongside CBD, CBC, CBD-acid, and THC-acid in
  Italian research (2006) for anti-tumor properties against breast
  cancer and thyroid cancer. While CBD was the most potent individual
  cannabinoid, CBG contributed to the overall anti-cancer effect of
  whole-plant cannabis extract.
- **Antibacterial:** One of five major cannabinoids (with THC, CBD, CBC,
  and CBN) shown to have potent activity against methicillin-resistant
  Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) in a 2008 international laboratory study.
- **Anti-inflammatory:** CBGA inhibited cyclooxygenase (COX) enzyme in
  a 2011 Uppsala University study, alongside THC, CBD, CBDA, and THCA.
- **Depression:** Tested among cannabinoids evaluated for antidepressant
  properties, with its dual CB1/CB2 binding suggesting potential
  mood-modulating effects.
- **[[cannabis-trichome-development-cannabinoid-biosynthesis-sites]]:
1. Geranyl pyrophosphate + olivetolic acid yield CBGA
2. THCA synthase converts CBGA to THCA (then THC)
3. CBDA synthase converts CBGA to CBDA (then CBD)
4. CBCA synthase converts CBGA to CBCA (then CBC)
5. Remaining CBGA may convert to CBG through decarboxylation

### Strain and Form Considerations
- Typically present at less than 1% in most strains
- Higher CBG content can be found in specific hemp cultivars
- Raw cannabis juice contains CBGA (non-psychoactive precursor)
- Selective breeding can increase CBG yields by reducing conversion
  enzymes for THC/CBD

## Relevance
CBG's role as the biosynthetic precursor to all major cannabinoids makes
it foundational to understanding cannabis chemistry. While often
overlooked due to its low concentrations, CBG's dual CB1/CB2 receptor
binding profile gives it unique therapeutic potential. The growing
interest in "minor cannabinoids" has positioned CBG as a promising
target for breeders and researchers. Its antibacterial properties
against MRSA are particularly noteworthy given the global antibiotic
