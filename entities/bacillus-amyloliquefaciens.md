# Bacillus amyloliquefaciens

## Overview

*Bacillus amyloliquefaciens* is a Gram-positive, endospore-forming soil
bacterium in the family Bacillaceae, widely used as a plant growth-
promoting rhizobacterium (PGPR) [[odonata-dragonflies-damselflies-ecology-and-biological-control]] agent.

The species produces antimicrobial lipopeptides, polyketides, asnow-mold-fungi-cold-temperature-plant-pathogensanic compounds that suppr [[bloomfield-fungal-plant-pathogens]] gens and stimulapermaculturene defenses.

It is a workhorse organism in [[phosphate-accumulators-sustainable-agriculture]], permaculture
[[coleman-greenhouse-soil-management]], and commercial biofungicide formulations.

## Taxonomy

- **Domain:** Bacteria
- **Phylum:** Bacillota (formerly Firmicutes)
- **Class:** Bacilli
- **Order:** Bacillales
- **Family:** Bacillaceae
- **Genus:** Bacillus
- **Species:** B. amyloliquefaciens

Originally described by Fukumoto in 1943 as a source of alpha-amylase.

The type strain FZB42 genome (2007) revealed 8.5% devoted to secondary
metabolite synthesis — one of the largest known arsenals among bacteria.

## Morphology [[plant-anatomy-and-physiology]]

- **Cell shape:** Rod-shaped, 0.7–0.9 × 1.8–3.0 µm.
- **Motility:** Peritrichous flagella, highly motile.
- **Endospores:** Central to subterminal, ellipsoidal; resistant to
heat, desiccation, and UV radiation.
- **Colony:** Cream to off-white, rough surface, 2–5 mm diameter at
24–48 hours on nutrient agar at 30°C.
- **Gram reaction:** Positive.
- **Growth:** 15–45°C (optimum 28–37°C); pH 5.0–9.0.

## Bioactive Secondary Metabolites

### Lipopeptides

- **Surfactin:** Potent biosurfactant; antifungal and antibacterial;
reduces water surface tension to ~27 mN/m.
- **Fengycin (A and B):** Strong antifungal against *Fusarium*,
*Rhizoctonia*, and *Botrytis* species.
- **Bacillomycin D:** Antifungal, effective against *Aspergillus*
and *Penicillium*.

### Polyketides

- **Macrolactin:** Antiviral and antibacterial.
- **Bacillaene:** Broad-spectrum antibacterial.
- **Difficidin:** Active against Gram-positive pathogens.

### Volatile Organic Compounds

- **2,3-Butanediol:** Promotes growth and induces systemic resistance.
- **Acetoin:** Enhances root development.
- **3-Hydroxy-2-butanone:** Contributes to antifungal VOC profile.

## Plant Growth-Promoting Mechanisms

### Direct Growth Promotion

- **Phosphate solubilization:** Organic acids solubilize rock phosphate.
- **Phytohormone production:** Synthesizes IAA, promoting root elongation.
- **ACC deaminase:** Degrades ethylene precursor, improving plant
tolerance to drought, salinity, and heavy metals.
- **Siderophore production:** Bacillibactin sequesters iron from soil.

### Indirect (Biocontrol)

- **Antibiosis:** Lipopeptides directly inhibit pathogens.
- **Induced Systemic Resistance (ISR):** VOCs and root colonization
prime plant immune responses against foliar and soil pathogens.
- **Competitive exclusion:** Rapid rhizosphere colonization outcompetes
pathogens.
- **Lytic enzymes:** Chitinases and glucanases degrade fungal walls.

## Permaculture Applications

- **Seed treatment:** Coating seeds improves germination and vigor.
- **Soil inoculation:** Compost tea or drench applications establish
rhizosphere colonies for season-long protection.
- **Compost additive:** Accelerates decomposition and suppresses
compost-borne pathogens.
- **Foliar spray:** Induces ISR against powdery mildew and leaf spot.
- **Polyculture compatibility:** Compatible with mycorrhizal fungi and
other PGPR.

## Pathogens Suppressed

- *[[fusarium-oxysporum]]* (Fusarium wilt)
- *[[rhizoctonia-solani]]* (damping off, root rot)
- *Botrytis cinerea* (gray mold)
- *Phytophthora capsici* (phytophthora blight)
- *Pseudomonas syringae* (bacterial speck)
- *[[sclerotinia-sclerotiorum]]* (white mold)

## Commercial Formulations

Many products labeled *B. subtilis* are actually *B. amyloliquefaciens*
under modern phylogeny.

- **TAEGRO® (Novozymes/Bayer):** Biofungicide for greenhouse crops.
- **Double Nickel™ (Certis):** Broad-spectrum biofungicide.
- **Serenade® (Bayer):** Contains QST 713 (reclassified to this species).

## Genomics

The FZB42 genome is ~3.9 Mbp with 3,693 predicted proteins, including
13 gene clusters for non-ribosomal peptide and polyketide synthesis.

## Safety

GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) status from the US FDA.
Non-pathogenic to humans, animals, and plants.

## Related Species

- *Bacillus subtilis* — close relative; many strains reclassified.
- *Bacillus velezensis* — sister species; significant taxonomic overlap.
- *Bacillus siamensis* — close relative from tropical soils.
