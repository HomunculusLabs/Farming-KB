## Overview

**Sunset Sherbet** (often shortened to **Sherbet** or **SSH**) is an indica-dominant
[[girl-scout-cookies]]** (GSC)
lineage. Bred in the [[thin-mint]] phenotype)
- **Male parent:** Pink Panties
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 85% indica / 15% sativa
- **Breeder:** Cookie Family (Bay Area, California)

Girl Scout Cookies itself is a cross of OG Kush × [[kanna-sceletium-south-african-narcotic]], and
Californian landrace genetics.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, dense, and conical to irregular in shape. The calyxes
are tightly packed with a thick coating of **trichomes** that gives the flowers
a frosted, silvery appearance under direct light.

Coloration is one of the strain's most distinguishing features — mature flowers
display deep [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] interwoven with shades of dark purple and violet,
accented by bright orange pistils that stand in vivid contrast against the
darker foliage.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Aroma profile** is dominated by a creamy, dessert-like sweetness that blends
ripe berries with a sherbet-like quality. Secondary notes include:
- Earthy, herbal undertones from the OG Kush lineage
- A subtle skunky pungency beneath the sweetness
- Hints of mint and fresh lavender on the exhale

**Flavor** mirrors the aroma closely, presenting a smooth, sweet smoke with
a creamy berry flavor on the inhale and an earthy, slightly peppery finish.
The dessert-like quality is pronounced and lingers on the palate.

## Terpene Profile

- **Caryophyllene** (dominant) — Peppery, spicy notes; CB2 receptor agonist
  contributing anti-inflammatory properties
- **Limonene** — Citrus brightness; mood elevation and stress relief
- **Myrcene** — Herbal, earthy base; sedative and muscle-relaxant effects
- **Linalool** — Floral, lavender notes; anxiolytic and calming contribution
- **Humulene** — Woody, earthy undertones; anti-inflammatory

The combination of high caryophyllene with linalool and myrcene creates the
signature creamy-sweet aromatic profile and contributes to the strain's
relaxing physical effects.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 18–24% (laboratory-verified range)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** Trace

Potency is moderately high, with modern cut-grown specimens consistently
testing above 20% THC. The lack of significant CBD means psychoactive effects
are pronounced and not buffered by cannabinoid antagonism.
