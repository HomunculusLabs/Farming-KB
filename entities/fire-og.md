## Overview

**Fire OG** is an indica-dominant hybrid [[blesching-cannabis-strain-selection-receptor-targeting]] created by crossing **SFV OG** (San
Fernando Valley OG) with an **OG Kush** backcross. Developed in Southern California during
the early 2000s medical cannabis era, Fire OG has become a staple of the [[blue-city-diesel-west-coast-blueberry-nyc-diesel-hybrid-oner]] legacy
strain catalog.

The strain is renowned for its potent sedative effects, often testing between 20-26% THC.
Its name derives from the fiery orange pistils that densely cover its buds. Fire OG has
earned a reputation as a heavy evening strain with strong body-centered relaxation and
has won recognition at multiple cannabis competitions including the High Times Cannabis Cup.

## Lineage

Fire OG is a cross of SFV OG × OG Kush backcross. SFV OG contributes the core OG Kush
chemotype with enhanced Afghan indica influence, while the OG Kush backcross reinforces
the signature fuel, pine, and lemon [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].

The genetic ratio is approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa. Both parent strains trace
back to the original Chemdawg × Lemon Thai × [[cannabis-hindu-kush-strain-description-clarke]] lineage that defines the OG Kush
family. Fire OG is considered a "double OG" cross due to its heavy OG Kush genetic loading.

## Appearance

Buds are dense, chunky, and medium to large in size. The calyxes are tightly packed with
a predominantly bright green coloration. What distinguishes Fire OG visually is the
extraordinary density of bright orange pistils, which often appear to engulf the bud
surface, giving the impression of embers or flame.

Trichome coverage is heavy, with a thick layer of milky-white to amber glandular
trichomes. The [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] is abundant, making buds sticky to the touch with a
slightly oily texture. Interior calyxes may show subtle purple hues in cooler growing
conditions.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by classic OG notes: pungent fuel and pine with an undercurrent of
citrus, particularly lemon. On the exhale, an earthy, woody quality emerges with hints
of spice. The scent is strong and tends to fill a room quickly.

Flavor closely mirrors aroma, with a sharp pine-lemon initial impression followed by
diesel and earth on the palate. A faint sweetness lingers on the finish. The smoke is
thick and expansive, potentially harsh for novice consumers.

## Terpene Profile

Fire OG is dominated by **myrcene** (herbal, musky, sedative), which drives the indica-dominant
body effects. **Limonene** (citrus) and **caryophyllene** (pepper, woody) are the secondary
and tertiary terpenes, contributing the characteristic OG citrus and spice notes.

**Pinene** (pine) is present at moderate levels, adding to the sharp aromatic profile and
providing mild bronchodilatory and alertness-sustaining effects that partially counter
the sedative onset.

**Linalool** may be present at trace levels, adding subtle floral sweetness and
contributing to the strain's anxiolytic properties.
