- **[[sfv-og]] Kush** (pollen parent): An indica-dominant phenotype of OG Kush
  originating from California's San Fernando Valley. Provides the foundational
  Kush [[sensi-star]] and [[sour-diesel]]. Contributes
  additional potency and a subtle diesel pungency to the aroma.

The genetic ratio is approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa, reflecting the
heavy indica dominance of both parent lines. The Death Star parent introduces
a small amount of Sour Diesel sativa influence.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large in size, with a dense and compact structure typical of
indica-dominant varieties. The calyxes are tightly packed and form chunky,
cone-shaped colas with moderate internodal spacing.

Coloration ranges from [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] to deep olive, often with hints of purple
in cooler growing conditions. The leaves are broad and dark, with occasional
twists characteristic of Kush genetics.

Trichome coverage is heavy, giving the flowers a frosted, silvery appearance
under magnification. Pistils are a burnt orange to copper color, contrasting
sharply against the dark green foliage.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by classic Kush terpenes — earthy, pine-scented, and
slightly fuel-like, with an underlying sweetness that emerges as the buds are
broken apart. Secondary notes include damp soil, hashish, and a faint citrus
quality.

On inhalation, the flavor is smooth and earthy with a noticeable pine
presence. The exhale reveals the Death Star influence, with a subtle diesel
sharpness and a lingering sweet, almost grape-like finish.

The overall flavor profile is complex and layered, ranking among the more
distinctive entries in the Kush family for consumers who appreciate nuanced
terpene expression.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- **Myrcene** (~0.5–1.0%): Contributes sedative, musky, and earthy notes.
- **Limonene** (~0.3–0.6%): Adds subtle citrus brightness and mood elevation.
- **Caryophyllene** (~0.2–0.5%): Provides peppery warmth and potential
  anti-inflammatory activity.
- **Pinene** (~0.1–0.3%): Adds pine freshness and may counteract some THC
  cognitive effects.

**Cannabinoid profile:**

- **THC:** 18–24%, with some phenotypes reported up to 26%
- **CBD:** < 0.1% (negligible)
