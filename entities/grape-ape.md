## Overview
Grape Ape is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its deep
purple bud coloration and intensely grape-scented aroma. A three-way cross of
Mendocino Purps, Skunk, and Afghani, it delivers powerful full-body sedation
that has made it a staple for evening and nighttime use. The strain was bred by
Apothecary Genetics in California and has won recognition at multiple cannabis
competitions. Its name references both the pronounced grape terpene profile and
its ability to overwhelm even seasoned consumers with its potency — hence
"ape." THC content typically ranges from 15–23%, with some phenotypes testing
higher. The effects are heavily physical, making it a popular choice among
medical patients managing chronic pain, insomnia, muscle spasms, and anxiety.

## Lineage and Genetics
- **Parent 1:** Mendocino Purps (indica-dominant, purple phenotype, Northern
  California heritage)
- **Parent 2:** Skunk #1 or Skunk lineage (balanced hybrid, pungent aroma,
  cerebral contribution)
- **Parent 3:** Afghani (pure indica, resin production, sedative body stone)
- **Breeder:** Apothecary Genetics, California
- **Genetic Ratio:** Approximately 70–80% indica / 20–30% sativa
- **Ploidy:** Diploid (standard)
The Mendocino Purps parent contributes the deep purple coloration and grape
terpenes, while Afghani provides the heavy resin coat and narcotic body effects.
Skunk adds complexity to the aroma and a mild cerebral uplift that prevents the
experience from becoming entirely sedative at lower doses.

## Appearance and Aroma
### Buds
Dense, chunky, and tightly packed nugs with a rounded indica structure. Colors
range from deep forest green to vivid royal purple, with the most colorful
phenotypes developing near-black hues. Bright orange to rust-colored pistils
wind through the calyxes. A thick, frosty coating of trichomes gives buds a
sticky, resinous texture.

### Aroma and Flavor
- **Primary scent:** Ripe Concord grapes, sweet berry
- **Secondary notes:** Earthy, hashy, subtle skunk
- **Taste:** Sweet grape and berry on the inhale, earthy and herbal on the
  exhale with a lingering skunky finish
- **Smoke:** Thick, smooth, slightly expansive in the lungs
The grape aroma intensifies as buds cure properly, peaking around 4–6 weeks of
drying and curing.

## Effects and Medical Applications
### Recreational Effects
- Deep physical relaxation, full-body calm
- Euphoric head high (mild, secondary to body effects)
- Sleepiness at moderate to high doses
- Increased appetite ("munchies")
- Sociable and relaxed at low doses; couch-lock at higher doses

### Medical Applications
- **Insomnia:** Strong sedative properties make it effective for sleep onset
- **Chronic pain:** Full-body analgesia for neuropathic and musculoskeletal pain
- **Anxiety and PTSD:** Calming effects reduce racing thoughts and hyperarousal
- **Muscle spasms:** Antispasmodic relief for MS, fibromyalgia, and cramps
- **Appetite stimulation:** Useful for cachexia, nausea, and eating disorders
- **Stress relief:** General anxiolytic and mood-stabilizing properties

## Cultivation Notes
- **Flowering time:** 7–8 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate (300–400 g/m² indoors); 400–500 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** Short to medium (80–130 cm), compact indica structure
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate; suitable for beginners
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, dry Mediterranean conditions
- **Resistance:** Good mold and pest resistance due to dense trichome coverage
- **Color enhancement:** Exposing plants to cooler nighttime temperatures (10–15°C)
  during the final 2–3 weeks of flowering triggers anthocyanin production,
  intensifying the purple coloration significantly
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate feeder; watch for nitrogen excess in late flower
- **Pruning:** Benefits from low-stress training (LST) and defoliation for light
  penetration to lower bud sites
- **Harvest indicator:** Milky-white to amber trichomes, 70%+ cloudy

## Cultural Significance and Awards
Grape Ape has been a West Coast staple since the late 2000s and holds a
dedicated following among both recreational and medical consumers. Its vivid
purple buds have made it one of the most visually recognizable strains in
cannabis culture. The strain has been featured in multiple High Times Cannabis
Cup competitions and remains a consistent dispensary favorite. Grape Ape has
also served as a genetic building block in modern breeding, contributing to
crosses like Grape Cream Cake, Grape Stomper, and various purple-leaning hybrid
lines. Its cultural cachet extends into music and media, where the distinctive
grape aroma and purple aesthetics have become synonymous with premium indica
quality.
