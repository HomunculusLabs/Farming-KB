## Antirrhinum majus (*Antirrhinum majus*)

## Overview

Antirrhinum majus, commonly known as the common snapdragon, is a herbaceous
perennial plant in the family Plantaginaceae. Native to the Mediterranean
region, it has been cultivated for centuries as an ornamental and serves as a
major [[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-north-america]]. The specific epithet majus
means "greater," distinguishing it from smaller-flowered relatives. The common
name derives from the Greek anti (like) and rhin (nose or snout), describing
the flower's resemblance to an animal's mouth.

Antirrhinum was traditionally placed in Scrophulariaceae but was reclassified
to Plantaginaceae following molecular phylogenetic analyses in the late 1990s.
The species is a diploid with 2n = 16 chromosomes.

## Morphology

### Growth Habit

Antirrhinum majus typically grows 30–90 cm tall depending on cultivar. The
stems are erect, slightly angular, and glandular-pubescent. The species
behaves as a short-lived perennial in mild climates but is most commonly
cultivated as an annual or biennial. Basal branching produces multiple
flowering stems in most modern cultivars.

### Leaves

Leaves are simple, opposite below and alternate above, lanceolate to ovate,
3–8 cm long and 1–3 cm wide. The margin is entire to slightly crenate. Leaf
color is medium green. A pair of small stipules is present at each node.

### Flowers

The zygomorphic flowers are arranged in terminal racemes, with 8–20 flowers
per inflorescence opening sequentially from bottom to top. Individual flowers
are 3–5 cm long with a distinctive bilabiate structure: two fused upper petals
forming a hood (galea) and three fused lower petals forming a lip (labellum)
that acts as a landing platform.

The flower mechanism requires a relatively heavy pollinator to depress the
lower lip and access the nectar at the base of the corolla tube. Bumblebees
(Bombus spp.) are the primary pollinators. Flower colors include white,
yellow, pink, red, orange, crimson, and bicolor patterns. Wild-type flowers
are typically magenta-pink with a yellow palate marked by darker nectar guides.
