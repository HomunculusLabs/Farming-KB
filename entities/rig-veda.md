## Description

The Rig Veda is an ancient Indian sacred text and one of the oldest known religious writings in any Indo-European language. It contains thousands of hymns devoted to soma, a sacred hallucinogenic plant or beverage. The descriptions of soma in the Rig-Veda have been crucial to identifying its probable botanical identity as [[emcdda-barriers-to-hallucinogenic-mushroom-trend-diffusion]]
- The soma mystery drove ethnomycological research for over a century
- R. Gordon Wasson's identification of soma as A. muscaria was partly based on Rig-Veda descriptions
- The text describes effects consistent with muscimole intoxication
