# Stachybotrys chartarum

Stachybotrys chartarum is a cellulolytic ascomycete fungus commonly
known as black mold or toxic black mold. It is one of the most
notorious indoor moulds, frequently associated with water-damaged
buildings and concerns about adverse health effects in building
occupants.

The species thrives in warm, damp conditions and is notably absent
from cooler climates where dry [[antifungal-secondary-metabolites-coprophilous-fungi]]. Notably, not all strains produce mycotoxins,
and the health significance of exposure remains debated in the
scientific literature.

The potential for mycotoxin production has nevertheless made S.
chartarum a focus of indoor [[fungal-building-material-deterioration]] degradation through
cellulolytic degradation of paper-based materials, discoloration and
staining of surfaces (dark greenish-black patches), and production of
unpleasant musty odors from microbial [[fungal-volatile-organic-compounds-communication]] compounds
(MVOCs).
