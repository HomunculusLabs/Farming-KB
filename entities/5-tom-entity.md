# 5-TOM (2-Methoxy-4-methyl-5-methylthioamphetamine)

5-TOM is a methylthio-substituted amphetamine that replaces the 5-methoxy group of [[dom]], with the 5-methoxy oxygen replaced by sulfur.

The compound retains the characteristic 2-methoxy and 4-methyl groups of DOM.

The name indicates the positions of the methyl, oxygen, and methylthio substituents on the aromatic ring.

## Pharmacological Profile

5-TOM is an active psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 30 to 50 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 6 to 10 hours.

The compound is described as a "pretty heavy-duty experience" with more negative reports than positive ones.

No reports of euphoria or the "Tomso effect" have been received for this compound.

## Qualitative Effects

At 35 mg, subjects reported an awful lot of visual activity.

The experience was characterized by strong visual components.

At 40 mg, subjects described sinking into a deep reverie after about an hour.

The experience was not totally clear, with a quiet sea and horizontal shoreline appearing in visual fantasy.

This was described as perhaps the peak of the experience.

At 50 mg, the body was described as "completely whacked" while the mental effects did not keep pace.

The physical effects outweighed the mental effects at this dose level.

Another subject at 50 mg reported much eyes-closed fantasy with erotic undertones.

The experience was described as heavy going in but rich coming out, with 50 mg rated as a good dosage.
