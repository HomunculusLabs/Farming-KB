# MADAM-6

MADAM-6 is a psychedelic amphetamine that is one of Shulgin's "Classic Ladies," carrying the mythological name THALASSA.

It is documented as entry #84 in PiHKAL by [[alexander-shulgin]].

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2-Amino-1-(3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl)butane.

MADAM-6 is a positional isomer of mescaline-entity|mescaline in which the methoxy groups are at the 3,4,5-positions while the amine-bearing chain has been extended to four carbons with the amine at the 2-position rather than the 1-position.

The compound is a structural hybrid of mescaline-entity|mescaline and the 4-carbon phenethylamine series.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 215-216 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 80 - 160 mg.

Duration of effects: 7 - 10 hours.

The potency is moderate, comparable to mescaline-entity|mescaline on a weight basis but producing a distinctly different character of experience.

## Qualitative Effects

At 80 mg, effects include a "slow, gentle climb" with no stomach awareness.

The experience is characterized as "completely out of body but very clearly in control."

Users describe "completely open, easy, beautiful, magical" states with the eyes-closed world being "everywhere at once."

The compound is described as "a very good visual" with "just the best" for personal introspection.

At 120 mg, the experience is similar to 80 mg but described as "somewhat more like peyote."

The "four-hour valley" characteristic of mescaline-entity|mescaline experiences is noted.

At 130 mg, the compound produces a "deeply pleasant intoxication" that is "totally psychedelic."

Visual effects are rich and eyes-closed imagery is "spectacular."

The body load is described as minimal, making it "a comfortable compound."

## The Classic Lady Thalassa

THALASSA is the Greek primeval spirit of the sea, daughter of Aether and Hemera.

In Greek mythology, Thalassa represents the primordial ocean and the power of the sea.

The name was assigned to MADAM-6 as part of Shulgin's tradition of naming particularly significant compounds after mythological figures.

As a Classic Lady, MADAM-6 joins an elite group of compounds that Shulgin considered to be of special importance.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with 3,4,5-trimethoxybenzaldehyde, which is condensed with butyric acid in the presence of polyphosphoric acid.

The resulting 1-(3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl)butan-1-one is converted to its oxime by reaction with hydroxylamine hydrochloride in pyridine.

The oxime is reduced with lithium aluminum hydride in THF to yield MADAM-6 as the free base.

The ketone intermediate crystallizes as white crystals with a melting point of 73-74 degrees Celsius.

The oxime intermediate has a melting point of 115-117 degrees Celsius.

## Relationship to Mescaline

MADAM-6 shares the 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl core with mescaline-entity|mescaline, the most ancient known psychedelic compound.

However, the amine-bearing chain differs in two ways: it is one carbon longer (four carbons vs three) and the amine is at the 2-position rather than the terminal position.

This structural difference places MADAM-6 outside the traditional phenethylamine family and into the butanamine class.

The 3,4,5-trimethoxy pattern is shared with several other compounds in PiHKAL, including escaline-entity|escaline, proscaline-entity|proscaline, and buscaline-entity|buscaline.

## The 4-Carbon Series

MADAM-6 is part of a series of 4-carbon amine compounds.

The general principle from the MDA series appears to hold: moving the amine from the 1-position (terminal) to the 2-position (one carbon in) consistently produces active compounds.

This pattern has been observed across multiple compound families and may represent a general pharmacological principle.

The 4-carbon chain provides a different spatial arrangement of the amine group relative to the aromatic ring, potentially altering receptor interactions.

## Pharmacological Context

MADAM-6 demonstrates that the 3,4,5-trimethoxy pattern can produce psychedelic activity even when the amine chain is modified beyond the classic phenethylamine structure.

The compound's comfortable character and minimal body load suggest favorable pharmacokinetic properties.

The similarity to peyote at higher doses suggests overlapping receptor targets with mescaline-entity|mescaline.

## Legal Status

MADAM-6 is not specifically scheduled in most jurisdictions.

As a 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl compound, it may fall under analogue drug legislation targeting mescaline-entity|mescaline analogues.

The compound has never been widely available outside of research settings.

## Harm Reduction

The dosage range (80-160 mg) requires accurate measurement.

The 7-10 hour duration means users should plan for most of a day.

The compound's comfortable profile and minimal body load make it relatively forgiving compared to other psychedelics.

As with all psychedelics, [[set-and-setting]] remain important considerations.
