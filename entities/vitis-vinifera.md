## Taxonomy and Morphology

**Kingdom:** Plantae — **Family:** Vitaceae — **Genus:** Vitis — **Species:** *V. vinifera* L.

The plant is a vigorous climbing liana that can reach 15–35 meters in length when supported by trees or trellises.

Leaves are simple, palmately lobed with 3–5 lobes, 5–20 cm long, with coarsely toothed margins.

Tendrils are unbranched and opposite the leaves at nodes, serving as climbing organs.

The fruit is a berry (grape), 6–20 mm in diameter, occurring in dense panicles of 15–300 berries per cluster.

Berry skin color ranges from pale green to yellow-green (white varieties) to red, purple, or blue-black (red varieties) depending on anthocyanin content.

Roots are extensive and can penetrate several meters deep in well-drained soils.
