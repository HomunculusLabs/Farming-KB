## Chemical Identity

5,6-MeO-MIPT is 5,6-dimethoxy-N-isopropyl-N-methyltryptamine, a synthetic tryptamine carrying the methyl-isopropyl nitrogen substitution pattern combined with methoxy groups at both the 5 and 6 positions of the indole ring.

The full chemical name is 5,6-dimethoxy-3-[2-(isopropylmethylamino)ethyl]indole. The compound is also designated as 5,6-dimethoxyindole-3-ethaneamine, N-isopropyl-N-methyl.
