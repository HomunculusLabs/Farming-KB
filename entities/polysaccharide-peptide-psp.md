## Polysaccharide-Peptide (PSP)

**Type:** Biochemical Compound (Fungal Polysaccharide)
**Category:** Mushroom-Derived Immunomodulator
**Also known as:** PSP, Coriolus versicolor polysaccharide,
Yun Zhi extract, I'm-Yunity

## Overview
Polysaccharide-peptide (PSP) is a protein-bound polysaccharide
isolated from the deep-layer mycelial culture of [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]], particularly in China.

## Key Details

### Chemical Properties
- **Classification:** Proteoglycan (polysaccharide-peptide complex)
- **Molecular weight:** Approximately 100,000 daltons
- **Composition:** May contain at least four discrete molecules, all
  true proteoglycans. Differs from PSK in saccharide makeup: lacks
  fucose, contains arabinose and rhamnose
- **Polysaccharide chains:** True beta-glucans with mainly 1-4, 1-2,
  and 1-3 glucose linkages, plus small amounts of galactose, mannose,
  and arabinose linkages
- **Bioavailability:** Orally bioavailable
- **Source organism:** Trametes versicolor (turkey [[lentinan-gastric-cancer-combination-therapy]]:** Demonstrated efficacy in clinical trials for
  stomach cancer
- **Esophageal cancer:** Shown effective in oesophageal cancer
  treatment
- **Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC):** Extensively studied and
  shown efficacy in NSCLC treatment
