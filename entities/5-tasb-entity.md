# 5-TASB (5-Thioasymbescaline; 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-butylthiophenethylamine)

5-TASB is a sulfur-substituted phenethylamine and the butylthio analogue of [[buscaline-entity]]. Like [[tb-entity]], it demonstrates the euphoric "anti-depressant" pattern without classic psychedelic effects. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #148.

## Chemical Classification

5-TASB belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 3,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-butylthiophenethylamine, also known as 5-thioasymbescaline.

5-TASB is closely related to [[tb-entity]] (4-thiobuscaline), which has the same formal chemical name.

The compound is the butylthio analogue of [[buscaline-entity]], with the 4-butoxy group replaced by a 4-n-butylthio group.

The "5-" prefix in the name reflects the position of the sulfur atom in the numbering system.

## Pharmacological Profile

5-TASB is an active compound with a dosage range of 60 to 120 milligrams.

The duration of effects is approximately 8 hours.

Like [[tb-entity]] and [[ariadne-entity]], the compound exhibits the euphoric "anti-depressant" pattern rather than classic psychedelic effects.

## Qualitative Effects

At 35 mg, subjects became aware of something at about one hour.

An attempt to rekindle the state with an additional 20 mg was too little and too late.

At 60 mg, a very subtle threshold effect was noted, with some activity persisting six hours into the experience.

At 80 mg, subjects were vaguely aware of something, with body discomfort possibly reflecting impurity in the sample.

At 120 mg, the course of action was extremely clear, with development beginning at approximately 5 hours.

The experience was characterized by euphoria that did not deepen into full psychedelic effects at any dose level.

## The Dose-Dependent Paradox

5-TASB exemplifies the "dose-dependent" exception seen with several compounds in the PIHKAL collection.

Most drug actions intensify with increasing dose, but euphoria-producing action shows a different pattern.

With increasing dose, euphoria does not increase proportionally.

Instead, there is a perpetual series of "thresholds" -- never the psychedelic or stimulant action that increases with dose.

The result is a simple and ephemeral catalyst of euphoria without substance.

This pattern was first observed with [[ariadne-entity]] and called an "anti-depressant" action.

## Relationship to TB

5-TASB and [[tb-entity]] share the same chemical name (3,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-butylthiophenethylamine) and appear to be the same compound.

Both demonstrate the characteristic euphoric without psychedelic effects pattern.

The compound represents significant synthetic effort that yielded a pharmacologically interesting but therapeutically limited compound.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 60 - 120 mg.

Duration: about 8 h.
