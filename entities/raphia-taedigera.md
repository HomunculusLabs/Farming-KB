## Distribution and Habitat

Raphia taedigera has a disjunct distribution across two continents.

In Africa, it occurs from Senegal eastward through the Gulf of Guinea countries to Angola and the Congo Basin.

In the Americas, it is found along the Caribbean and Pacific coasts from Nicaragua to Colombia and Ecuador.

The species grows in freshwater swamps, tidal estuaries, riverine floodplains, and mangrove-fringe habitats.

It thrives in permanently waterlogged or seasonally inundated soils with high organic content.

Elevation range is from sea level to approximately 200 meters.

The palm tolerates brackish water conditions, allowing it to colonize mangrove transition zones.

It often forms monodominant stands in low-lying swamp forests behind mangrove belts.
