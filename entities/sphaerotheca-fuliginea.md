## Taxonomy and Classification

S. fuliginea belongs to the Erysiphaceae (powdery mildews) within the Ascomycota. It is one of the most economically damaging members of the powdery mildew group, a family of obligate biotrophic parasites that grow exclusively on living plant tissue. The taxonomy of powdery mildews has undergone significant revision in recent decades, and some authorities now place this species within the genus Podosphaera. The Erysiphaceae are among the most heavily parasitized fungus families (Parbery 1978; Hawksworth 1988b), with numerous mycoparasitic fungi exploiting their conidia, hyphae, and cleistothecia as substrates.
