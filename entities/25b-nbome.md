# 25B-NBOMe

## Overview

25B-NBOMe (2C-B-NBOMe, Cimbi-36) is a potent serotonergic psychedelic
in the NBOMe (N-benzylmethoxy) series of phenethylamines.

It acts as a highly potent partial agonist at the 5-HT2A receptor, with
in vivo activity in the low microgram range — one of the most potent
orally active psychedelics known.

Originally developed for 5-HT2A receptor imaging research (Cimbi-36),
it has appeared on the recreational market as blotter paper, often
mislabeled as LSD.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name:** 2-(4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)
methyl]ethanamine
- **Molecular formula:** C19H24BrNO3
- **Molecular weight:** 382.29 g/mol
- **CAS number:** 1445565-86-7
- **Chemical class:** Phenethylamine, NBOMe derivative

The N-(2-methoxy)benzyl substitution dramatically increases receptor
potency and metabolic stability compared to parent compound 2C-B.

## Pharmacology

### Receptor Affinity

- **5-HT2A:** Ki ≈ 0.5–1.5 nM (extremely high affinity)
- **5-HT2C:** Ki ≈ 5–15 nM
- **5-HT1A:** Ki ≈ 100–200 nM (weak)
- **α1-Adrenergic:** Ki ≈ 50–100 nM (moderate)

Partial agonist at 5-HT2A with ~50–80% efficacy relative to serotonin,
contributing to its distinct subjective character versus full agonists.

Primary effects are mediated through 5-HT2A activation in cortical
layer V pyramidal neurons, increasing glutamate release.

The 2-methoxybenzyl group provides metabolic protection against rapid
first-pass deamination.

## Pharmacokinetics

- **Route:** Sublingual/buccal for full effects; oral bioavailability
is very low (~5%) due to extensive first-pass metabolism.
- **Onset:** 15–45 minutes (sublingual).
- **Duration:** 6–10 hours.
- **Metabolism:** Hepatic via CYP2D6 and CYP1A2.
- **Half-life:** ~3–4 hours.

## Effects

### Subjective Profile

- **Visual:** Intense geometric patterns, color enhancement, morphing
surfaces, synesthesia.
- **Cognitive:** Altered thought patterns, introspection, ego
dissolution at higher doses.
- **Emotional:** Euphoria, anxiety, emotional lability.
- **Physical:** Pupil dilation, tachycardia, vasoconstriction, nausea,
tremor, jaw clenching.

### Dose Range (sublingual)

- **Threshold:** 50–100 µg
- **Light:** 100–250 µg
- **Common:** 250–500 µg
- **Strong:** 500–750 µg
- **Heavy:** 750+ µg

Doses above 1000 µg significantly increase risk of serious adverse
effects including serotonin toxicity.

## Toxicity and Adverse Effects

### Common

- Nausea and vomiting (more pronounced than LSD).
- Tachycardia and hypertension.
- Vasoconstriction (cold extremities, numbness).
- Anxiety, paranoia, panic attacks.
- Bruxism and muscle tension.

### Severe

- Prolonged QT interval, cardiac arrhythmia.
- Serotonin syndrome at very high doses.
- Seizures in massive overdose.
- Multiple fatalities reported (typically >1500 µg or combined with
stimulants).

### Harm Reduction

- Use volumetric dosing; blotter absorption is unreliable.
- Never insufflate — significantly higher bioavailability increases
overdose risk.
- Test with Ehrlich reagent (yellow-orange; LSD does not react).
- Avoid combining with stimulants, MAOIs, or serotonergic drugs.

## Legal Status

- **United States:** Schedule I (permanent, 2016).
- **UK:** Class A (Psychoactive Substances Act, 2016).
- **Australia:** Schedule 9 (prohibited).
- **EU:** Controlled under member state NPS legislation.
- **Japan:** Designated substance under Pharmaceutical Affairs Law.

## History

Developed by Ralf Heim at the Free University of Berlin in the early
2000s.

Appeared recreationally on blotter paper around 2010; multiple
fatalities between 2012–2015 prompted emergency scheduling worldwide.

The Cimbi-36 designation refers to its use as a 5-HT2A PET imaging
radioligand (carbon-11 labeled).

## Related Compounds

- **2C-B:** Parent compound; milligram-range potency, oral
bioavailability.
- **25I-NBOMe:** Iodo analogue; most widely encountered NBOMe.
- **25C-NBOMe:** Chloro analogue; slightly lower potency.
- **25D-NBOMe:** Desmethyl analogue; lowest potency in series.
- **DOI:** Related 2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine research compound.
- **LSD:** Often misrepresented on blotter; distinct pharmacology and
lower potency.
