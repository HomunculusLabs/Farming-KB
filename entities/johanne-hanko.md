# Johanne Hanko

## Description

Johanne Hanko is an international consultant and technical specialist in disability matters who served as the author and primary developer of the FAO training manual "[[mushroom-cultivation-for-disabilities-training-program]] People with Disabilities — A Training Manual" (RAP Publication 2001/12). She was the international consultant for the FAO Technical Cooperation Programme TCP/THA/8821(A), "Mushroom Production Training [[holzer-lebenshilfe-permaculture-project-for-disabled-people]]," implemented in Ubon Ratchathani province, Thailand.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person / Author / Development specialist
- **Role**: International consultant for FAO TCP/THA/8821(A)
- **Specialization**: Technical specialist in disability matters
- **Publication**: Author of the FAO training manual on mushroom cultivation for people with disabilities

## Contributions

### Training Manual Development
Hanko authored the comprehensive training manual that covers:
- The complete training methodology for people with disabilities
- Three-in-one training approach: rehabilitation + mushroom cultivation + enterprise development
- Selection criteria and procedures for trainees
- Motivational session design and delivery
- Hands-on mushroom cultivation curriculum covering 20 steps from tissue culture to business management
- Building and equipment adaptations for accessibility
- Monitoring and evaluation frameworks for follow-up support

### Training Methodology
Hanko developed the holistic "three-in-one" training approach that integrates:
1. **Rehabilitation**: Physical, mental, and emotional support for self-acceptance and confidence building
2. **Technical Skills**: Complete mushroom cultivation from laboratory tissue culture through production and processing
3. **Enterprise Development**: Business planning, basic bookkeeping, and marketing strategies

### Disability-Inclusive Design
The manual reflects Hanko's expertise in adapting training and work environments for diverse disabilities:
- Physical adaptations for wheelchair users and people with limited mobility
- Creative problem-solving approaches for task modification
- Recognition of individual differences even among people with the same disability
- Emphasis on capability rather than limitation
- Mental disability inclusion with appropriate support strategies

## Key Facts

- Hanko's manual is one of the most comprehensive resources on disability-inclusive [[mushroom-cultivation-training-program-design]]
- The manual was published as RAP Publication 2001/12 by the FAO Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific
- The project she documented demonstrated that people with both physical and mental disabilities can successfully operate profitable mushroom enterprises
- Her work established a replicable model for disability-inclusive agricultural training programs
- The manual has been used as a reference for similar programs in other countries and regions

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Johanne Hanko's contribution to mycology is not in fungal science but in demonstrating that mushroom cultivation — a technically demanding agricultural practice — can be made accessible to people with widely varying physical and mental capabilities. Her work proves that with appropriate training methodology, adaptive equipment, and supportive environments, mushroom cultivation can serve as a viable pathway to economic self-reliance for some of the most marginalized members of rural communities.

The training methodology she developed has broader applicability beyond disability-inclusive programs, offering a model for participatory, hands-on agricultural education that balances technical skills with personal development and business acumen.
