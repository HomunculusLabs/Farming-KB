## Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)

## Description

The Nutrient Film Technique is a hydroponic method where plants grow with part of
their root mass in earth and part in nutrient solution contained in plastic
gullies. A wick variant uses slotted pipes with hessian or nylon wicks to deliver
nutrients to plant roots. Both prevent water loss to soil absorption and are
classified as hydroponic-intensive systems.

## Classification

- **Category**: Hydroponic growing system
- **Domain**: Intensive horticulture, [[drip-irrigation]] in open fields, true hydroponics in glasshouses

## Limitations

Mollison cautions that maintenance problems are significant. The system requires:
- Regular monitoring of nutrient solution chemistry
- Labour for layout, maintenance, and harvest
- Reliable nutrient solution supply
- Periodic cleaning of gullies and wicks

Without available labour, simpler alternatives may be preferable.

## Advantages

- Maximum water use efficiency
- Dual nutrient access for plants
- Can use moderately saline water
- Suitable for very high-value crop production
- Integrates soil biology with hydroponic precision
