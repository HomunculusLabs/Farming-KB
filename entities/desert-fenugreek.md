# Desert Fenugreek

## Description

Desert fenugreek is a legume intercrop species used in dryland tree establishment,
seeded between tree lines along bulldozer rip-lines. Serves as nitrogen-fixing
ground cover that improves soil conditions for establishing palms, [[tagasaste]], and gourds.

## Classification

- **Kingdom**: Plantae
- **Family**: Fabaceae (legume family)
- **Genus**: Trigonella (fenugreek genus)
- **Type**: Annual or short-lived perennial herb
- **Climate adaptation**: Arid and semi-arid regions
- **Growth habit**: Low-growing, spreading ground cover

## Role in Dryland Tree Establishment

### Companion Crop System
Desert fenugreek is one component of a legume intercrop suite:
- Desert fenugreek (nitrogen fixation, ground cover)
- Lucerne/alfalfa (deep-rooted, nitrogen fixation, fodder)
- Tagasaste (tree lucerne, fodder, nitrogen fixation)
- Gourds (ground cover, soil cooling, food production)

### Companion Trees
Seeded in association with:
- Palms (date, doum) — providing canopy layer
- Casuarina — nitrogen-fixing windbreak companion
- Jujube (Ziziphus) — drought-hardy fruit production

### Establishment Method
1. Bulldozer creates rip-lines for tree rows
2. Tree seedlings planted in the rip-lines
3. Small legume seeds broadcast between trees along the rip-line
4. Legumes establish quickly to protect soil surface
5. Trees grow through the legume ground cover
6. Legumes improve soil nitrogen for tree growth

## Key Functions

### Nitrogen Fixation
As a legume, desert fenugreek fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root nodule
bacteria, enriching poor desert soils and providing nutrition for companion
tree seedlings during their critical establishment phase.

### Ground Cover
Low spreading growth covers the soil surface between tree seedlings, reducing
soil temperature at root level (critical below 30°C threshold), evaporative
water loss, wind erosion of topsoil, and weed competition.

### Soil Cooling
Ground cover helps cool the root area, keeping soil temperatures below the
lethal 30°C threshold for shallow tree roots that can be cooked above this
temperature.

## Key Facts

- Legume intercrop for desert tree establishment
- Seeded between tree lines along bulldozer rip-lines
- Nitrogen-fixing ground cover
- Companions: palms, Casuarina, jujube
- Part of suite including lucerne, tagasaste, gourds
- Keeps soil below lethal 30°C for shallow roots
- Protects soil surface during tree establishment phase

## Relevance to Cultivation

Uses biological interactions to create favorable conditions for desired crops.
Natural nitrogen fixation feeds establishing trees without synthetic fertilizer.
Ground cover addresses temperature, moisture retention, and erosion — all
critical factors in desert tree establishment. Exemplifies [[permaculture]]
principle of using biological solutions rather than chemical inputs.
