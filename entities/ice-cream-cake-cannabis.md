# Ice Cream Cake

Ice Cream Cake is a popular indica-dominant hybrid created by
crossing Gelato #33 with [[wedding-cake]]. Developed by Seed Junky
Genetics in Southern California, it won first place at the
2018 Emerald Cup. Known for dessert-like aroma, deep
relaxation, and high THC (typically 20-25%, some phenotypes
exceeding 30%), it has become a staple in dispensaries. The
strain exemplifies the modern "dessert cannabis" breeding
trend prioritizing rich, sweet [[cannabis-terpene-profiles-sativa]] over raw
potency alone.

## Lineage and Breeding
- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (~75/25 indica/sativa)
- **Breeder:** Seed Junky Genetics (Southern California)
- **Cross:** Gelato #33 × Wedding Cake
- **Year:** Circa 2017-2018
- **Award:** 1st Place, 2018 Emerald Cup
- **Lineage:**
  - Gelato #33 = [[thin-mint-cookies]] × [[sunset-sherbet]]
  - Wedding Cake = [[triangle-kush]] × Animal Mints
  - Combined heritage: Cookie family sweetness + OG
    Kush indica depth via Triangle Kush
- **Genetic tree:** [[girl-scout-cookies]] → Thin Mint →
  Gelato → Ice Cream Cake; OG Kush → Triangle Kush →
  Wedding Cake → Ice Cream Cake

## Appearance
- **Buds:** Dense, tightly packed, medium to large
  chunky calyxes
- **Color:** Deep [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] with purple/violet
  undertones (enhanced by cool night temps); bright
  orange pistils throughout
- **Trichomes:** Extremely heavy frosty white coating;
  snow-capped appearance
- **Leaves:** Broad indica-type; short to medium
  internodes
- **Visual appeal:** Among the most photogenic modern
  strains; purple + trichome coverage is striking

## Aroma and Flavor
- **Primary aroma:** Sweet vanilla, creamy dairy,
  reminiscent of vanilla ice cream
- **Secondary:** Earthy, doughy, sugary with subtle
  lavender undertones
- **Tertiary:** Hints of nuttiness and woody herbal
  quality on the backend
- **Inhalation:** Sweet, creamy, smooth with vanilla
  bean and sugar cookie dominant
- **Exhalation:** Earthy, doughy with lingering vanilla
  and subtle minty finish
- **Aftertaste:** Creamy sweetness lingers; slightly
  cloying for some users
- **Smoke:** Smooth, thick, not harsh despite high THC;
  expansive in lungs

## Terpene Profile
| Terpene | Range | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Myrcene | 0.8-2.0% | Sedative, musky, herbal |
| Limonene | 0.5-1.5% | Citrus lift, mood elevation |
| Caryophyllene | 0.3-1.0% | Pepper, anti-inflammatory |
| Linalool | 0.2-0.8% | Floral, lavender, calming |
| Humulene | 0.1-0.5% | Earthy, appetite suppressant |
| Beta-pinene | Trace | Pine, focus |

High myrcene + linalool + caryophyllene creates the
signature sedative profile. Limonene provides initial
uplift before deeper physical relaxation.

## Cannabinoid Profile
- **THC:** 20-25% typical; range 18-30%+ in select
  cultivars
- **CBD:** Very low (<0.1%)
- **CBG:** Trace (0.05-0.15%)
- **[[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]]:** CBC present; no significant
  THCV
