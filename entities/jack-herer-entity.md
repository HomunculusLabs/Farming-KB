# Jack Herer
**The Jack / JH / Imperial Jack**
- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (~55% sativa / 45% indica)
- **THC content:** 18–24% (modern cultivars up to 27%)
- **CBD content:** <0.1–0.3%
- **Primary terpenes:** Terpinolene, pinene, caryophyllene, myrcene,
  limonene
- **Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate (400–500 g/m² indoor)
- **Breeder:** Sensi Seeds / Green House Seeds (disputed)

## Overview
Jack Herer is one of the most celebrated cannabis strains in the world,
named for Jack Herer (1939–2010), author of "The Emperor Wears No Clothes"
and a pivotal legalization activist. Developed in the Netherlands in the
1990s, it combines Haze genetics with Northern Lights #5 and Shiva Skunk,
delivering an energetic cerebral high with complex spicy-pine aromatics.
The strain has won more than 9 Cannabis Cup awards and is considered a
benchmark cultivar for quality sativa-dominant hybrids.

## Lineage and Genetics
The breeding history is disputed between Sensi Seeds and Green House
Seeds. The generally accepted genetic composition is:
- **Primary sire:** Haze (Colombian, Mexican, Thai, South Indian lineage)
- **Contributing parent 1:** Northern Lights #5 (Afghan indica — adds
  resilience, reduced flowering time, resin density)
- **Contributing parent 2:** Shiva Skunk (Skunk #1 × NL — adds vigor
  and skunky aromatics)
This three-way cross retains Haze's cerebral effects while cutting its
notorious 14–16 week flowering time. Jack Herer genetics appear in
dozens of modern cultivars including Jack Frost, Jack's Cleaner, and
The Joker.

## Growth Characteristics
### Plant Structure
- **Height:** Tall (120–180 cm indoor; 200–300 cm outdoor)
- **Branching:** Long internodal spacing, lanky sativa structure;
  benefits from SCROG, LST, or topping
- **Leaf morphology:** Narrow, serrated sativa-type leaflets, slightly
  broader than pure Haze due to indica influence

### Cultivation Notes
- **Difficulty:** Moderate; requires height management and feeding
  discipline
- **Climate:** Warm, Mediterranean, or semi-arid; sensitive to cold
  and late-flower humidity (bud rot risk)
- **Nutrients:** Moderate feeder; nitrogen drops sharply after week 4
  of flowering. Watch for Ca/Mg deficiency in coco
- **Training:** SCROG highly effective; aim for even canopy at 40–60
  cm before switching to 12/12
- **Pest resistance:** Moderate; watch for spider mites and Botrytis

### Flowering and Harvest
- **Indoor flowering:** 8–10 weeks (most phenotypes finish by week 9)
- **Outdoor harvest:** Early to mid-October (northern hemisphere)
- **Harvest window:** Trichomes cloudy with 10–20% amber for optimal
  cerebral/body balance
- **Cure:** Minimum 2-week cure for full terpene expression

## Terpene Profile
### Dominant Terpenes
- **Terpinolene:** Signature terpene — fruity, floral, herbal, piney.
  Associated with uplifting, creative effects
- **Pinene (α/β):** Sharp pine and wood; promotes alertness and
  bronchodilation. Counteracts THC memory impairment
- **β-Caryophyllene:** Peppery warmth; dietary CB2 agonist with
  anti-inflammatory properties
- **Myrcene:** Earthy base note (lower than indica strains); adds
  gentle body component
- **Limonene:** Citrus brightness; mood elevation and stress relief

### Aroma and Flavor
Complex bouquet of pine, spice, and earth with floral sweetness —
often described as "forest floor meets Christmas tree." Smooth pine
and pepper on inhalation; sweet floral-citrus on exhale. The flavor
profile is widely cited as one of Jack Herer's most prized qualities.

## Effects Profile
- **Onset:** Rapid (5–15 min smoked/vaped); peak at 30–60 min
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours
- **Character:** Clear, focused cerebral high enhancing creativity and
  sociability. Indica component adds gentle body relaxation in the
  comedown. Euphoric without overwhelming; suitable for daytime use
- **Reported:** Euphoria, creativity, focus, energy, mild relaxation
- **Adverse:** Dry mouth/eyes, anxiety at high doses, rare paranoia

## Therapeutic Applications
- **Mood disorders:** Depression, SAD, stress (terpinolene + limonene)
- **Fatigue:** Daytime motivation without heavy sedation
- **ADHD:** Improved focus reported (pinene + THC synergy)
- **Chronic pain:** Moderate analgesia from caryophyllene + THC
- **PTSD:** Terpinolene anxiolytic potential; clear-headed processing
