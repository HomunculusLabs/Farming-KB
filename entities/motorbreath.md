# Motorbreath

## Overview

Motorbreath is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing Chemdog D (also known as Chemdawg D) with SFV OG Kush. Bred by Pisces Genetics in collaboration with Moxie Seeds, it has gained a strong reputation among cannabis enthusiasts for its exceptional potency, distinctive fuel-like aroma, and deeply relaxing effects.

First released in the mid-2010s, Motorbreath quickly established itself as a staple in the "gas" category of cannabis strains — those prized for their pungent, diesel-like terpene profiles.

It has won multiple awards at cannabis competitions and has been used as a parent strain in several notable crosses.

## Lineage

Motorbreath is a cross of two well-established West Coast cannabis lines:

- **Chemdog D (female):** A specific cut of the legendary Chemdawg lineage, known for its intense chemical aromas and potent cerebral effects.

Chemdog D is one of the most sought-after Chemdawg phenotypes and contributes the strain's signature diesel-fuel terpene character.

- **SFV OG Kush (male):** San Fernando Valley OG Kush, a classic Southern California indica-dominant hybrid.

SFV OG brings heavy body effects, earthy pine notes, and a deeply relaxing physical stone that complements the more cerebral Chemdog influence.

The resulting cross is approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa, though individual phenotypes can vary. Motorbreath #15 (also written Motorbreath 15) is the most widely distributed phenotype, while Motorbreath #2 is a second, less common cut.

## Appearance

Motorbreath produces medium to large-sized flowers with a dense, tightly packed structure typical of its kush lineage. The buds are chunky and irregularly shaped, with a slightly elongated form that tapers toward the tips.

The coloration is predominantly deep forest green with occasional flashes of dark purple, especially when exposed to cooler temperatures during late flowering.

The calyxes are large and swollen, densely packed with trichomes that give the buds a frosted, silvery appearance.

Orange to rust-colored pistils wind through the resinous flowers, creating a striking visual contrast against the dark green foliage. The trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, with large, bulbous capitate-stalked trichomes visible to the naked eye.

## Aroma and Flavor

Motorbreath is renowned for its pungent, immediately recognizable aroma that lives up to its name:

- **Primary aroma:** Intense diesel fuel, gasoline, and chemical notes dominate, inherited directly from the Chemdog parent. The scent is sharp and acrid, filling a room quickly when buds are opened.

- **Secondary notes:** Underlying layers of earthy musk, damp soil, and subtle citrus rind add complexity to the overall profile. Some phenotypes express hints of roasted coffee and dark chocolate.

- **Flavor:** On inhalation, the flavor mirrors the aroma with heavy diesel and chemical notes. The exhale reveals a more nuanced profile with earthy, peppery, and slightly sweet undertones. The smoke is thick and expansive, with a lingering aftertaste of fuel and pine.

## Terpene Profile

Motorbreath's distinctive aroma and effects are driven by a dominant terpene profile:

- **Caryophyllene:** The most abundant terpene, contributing peppery, spicy notes and potential anti-inflammatory effects through CB2 receptor activation.
- **Limonene:** Present in moderate levels, adding subtle citrus brightness and potentially contributing to mood elevation and stress relief.
- **Myrcene:** Significant concentration, providing the earthy, musky base notes and contributing to the strain's sedative physical effects.
- **Humulene:** Adds woody, earthy depth and may contribute anti-inflammatory properties.
- **Ocimene:** Present in smaller quantities, adding sweet, herbal nuances.

The high caryophyllene-to-myrcene ratio is characteristic of the "gas" terpene profile and distinguishes Motorbreath from fruitier or more floral strains.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Motorbreath is an exceptionally high-potency strain with THC levels consistently testing between 28% and 34% in licensed market testing, making it one of the strongest strains available in legal markets.

- **THC:** 28–34% (some phenotypes reportedly reaching 35%+)
- **CBD:** Less than 0.1% (negligible)
- **THCV:** Trace amounts detected in some phenotypes

Due to the extreme potency, Motorbreath is generally recommended for experienced consumers. Novice users may find even small doses overwhelming.

## Effects

Motorbreath delivers a powerful, predominantly physical experience consistent with its indica-dominant genetics:

- **Onset:** Effects begin within minutes of consumption, starting with a cerebral pressure behind the eyes and temples that transitions into a warm, spreading body sensation.

- **Body effects:** Deep muscular relaxation and heaviness set in progressively, often described as "couch-lock" in higher doses.

Tension relief and physical sedation are pronounced, making it popular for evening and nighttime use.

- **Mental effects:** Despite the physical dominance, there is a noticeable cerebral component from the Chemdog lineage — a hazy, euphoric head high that can be mildly psychedelic at higher doses.

This can include introspective thought patterns and enhanced sensory perception.

Common applications include chronic pain management, insomnia relief, stress and anxiety reduction, and appetite stimulation. Side effects at higher doses may include dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness, and paranoia in susceptible individuals.

## Cultivation

Motorbreath is considered a moderately difficult strain to cultivate, best suited for growers with some experience:

- **Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors)
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Climate preference:** Warm, Mediterranean-like conditions; performs well both indoors and outdoors

The plants exhibit strong, vigorous vegetative growth with thick, sturdy branches that can support heavy bud development. Training techniques such as low-stress training (LST) and scrogging can improve yield and light penetration.

Motorbreath responds well to moderate nutrient levels but can be sensitive to overfeeding, particularly nitrogen during late flowering. Proper flush during the final two weeks is essential for optimal flavor expression.

The strain is moderately resistant to common pests and molds but benefits from good air circulation due to its dense bud structure. The plants produce a strong odor throughout flowering, requiring adequate odor control in indoor settings.
