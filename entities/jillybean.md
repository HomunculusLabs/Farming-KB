## Aroma and Flavor

Jillybean's defining characteristic is its aroma. The bouquet is overwhelmingly
tropical and fruity, dominated by ripe mango, fresh orange, and sweet apricot
notes with an underlying creaminess.

On the exhale, subtle earthy and slightly skunky undertones emerge, grounding
the otherwise candy-sweet flavor. Some phenotypes display a faint pineapple or
tangerine sharpness that distinguishes them from the more mango-forward cuts.

The flavor closely mirrors the aroma, coating the palate with a sweet citrus
creaminess that lingers. Vaporization at lower temperatures (175–185°C) best
preserves the delicate tropical terpene notes.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Myrcene, limonene, caryophyllene, with notable pinene
and linalool contributions. The limonene content is typically higher than
average, driving the pronounced citrus character.

**[[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]] including CBC and CBG may be present in trace amounts.
Total cannabinoid content generally ranges from 18–25%.
