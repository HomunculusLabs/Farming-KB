## Overview

**Jillybean** is a sativa-dominant [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain bred by **Subcool of TGA
Genetics** (The Green Avengers). It is a cross between **Orange Velvet** and
**[[oner-jack-the-ripper-jacks-cleaner-space-queen]]**, two strains known for their vibrant tropical flavor profiles.

Jillybean has earned a devoted following for its remarkably sweet, mango-orange
aroma and its uplifting, creative effects that remain functional throughout the
day. It has won multiple cannabis competition awards since its release in the
late 2000s.

## Lineage

**Parents:** Orange Velvet × Space Queen.

**Genetic ratio:** Approximately 60% sativa / 40% indica, though phenotypic
variation is notable. Some phenotypes express more indica-dominant growth
patterns while retaining the sativa-leaning effect profile.

**Orange Velvet** contributes the dominant citrus and tropical fruit terpene
profile, along with moderate THC production and a euphoric cerebral quality.

**Space Queen**, itself a cross of Cinderella 99 and a Romulan-Jack Herer male,
adds potency, [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and a subtle diesel-pine undertone to the aroma
complex.

## Appearance

[[green-cannabis-plant-anatomy-calyx-pistil-bud-structure]] is medium to large, with elongated, slightly airy colas typical of
sativa-dominant genetics. Calyxes are densely packed with prominent pistils that
range from bright orange to deep rust-red.

Trichome coverage is heavy, giving the flowers a frosted, crystalline
appearance under magnification. The combination of orange pistils against pale
green calyxes creates a visually striking contrast.

Leaf structure is relatively narrow, and internodal spacing is moderate. Plants
can stretch significantly during the [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-flowering-phase-and-harvest]], particularly in the
sativa-leaning phenotypes.

## Aroma and Flavor

Jillybean's defining characteristic is its aroma. The bouquet is overwhelmingly
tropical and fruity, dominated by ripe mango, fresh orange, and sweet apricot
notes with an underlying creaminess.

On the exhale, subtle earthy and slightly skunky undertones emerge, grounding
the otherwise candy-sweet flavor. Some phenotypes display a faint pineapple or
tangerine sharpness that distinguishes them from the more mango-forward cuts.

The flavor closely mirrors the aroma, coating the palate with a sweet citrus
creaminess that lingers. Vaporization at lower temperatures (175–185°C) best
preserves the delicate tropical terpene notes.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Myrcene, limonene, caryophyllene, with notable pinene
and linalool contributions. The limonene content is typically higher than
average, driving the pronounced citrus character.

**[[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]]:** 15–22%, depending on phenotype and cultivation conditions.
Tested averages cluster around 18%.

**CBD content:** Typically below 0.1%. Jillybean is not a CBD-rich strain.

[[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]] including CBC and CBG may be present in trace amounts.
Total cannabinoid content generally ranges from 18–25%.
