## Description

Tatsoi (Brassica rapa var. rosularis), also known as rosette bok choy, spoon mustard, or tat choy, is a low-growing Asian green in the Brassicaceae family. It forms a tight, flat rosette of dark green, spoon-shaped leaves with thick, crisp pale stems. Tatsoi has a mild, slightly mustardy flavor that sweetens in cold weather. It is one of the key cold-hardy Asian greens cultivated in Eliot Coleman's winter harvest system at Four Season Farm in Harborside, Maine.

## Classification

- **Family**: Brassicaceae
- **Genus**: Brassica
- **Species**: B. rapa
- **Variety**: rosularis
- **Common names**: Tatsoi, rosette bok choy, spoon mustard, tat choy
- **Category**: Cool-season leaf vegetable / Asian green

## Key Facts

- Tatsoi is listed among the approximately 30 cold-hardy vegetables successfully grown in unheated greenhouse winter production at Four Season Farm.
- It is extremely cold-tolerant, surviving temperatures well below 10°F (-12°C) when protected from wind and desiccation by greenhouse covering and inner row-cover layers.
- The plant's flat, ground-hugging rosette form helps it capture available light efficiently during short winter days.
- Tatsoi is included in both the cool-house and cold-house harvest season charts, indicating its productivity across a range of protected cultivation conditions.
- In the Coleman system, tatsoi is succession-planted from late summer through fall for continuous winter harvest.
- The leaves and stems are both edible and can be used raw in salads or cooked in stir-fries, soups, and braising mixes.
- Flavor improves in cold weather as starches convert to sugars, reducing bitterness and enhancing sweetness.
- Tatsoi is botanically related to bok choi and pak choi, sharing similar culinary uses but with superior cold hardiness.
- Growth rate slows significantly when day length drops below 10 hours, requiring earlier sowing to ensure harvestable size before midwinter.

## Cultivation Notes

- Direct sow from late summer through early fall for winter harvest.
- Germinates in 5-10 days at 50-70°F (10-21°C).
- Spacing: Thin to 4-6 inches apart for full rosette development.
- Matures in 40-60 days depending on temperature and light conditions.
- Benefits from floating row cover inside unheated greenhouses in Zone 5 and colder climates.
- Can be harvested as whole rosettes or as cut-and-come-again leaves.
- Relatively pest-free during winter production; flea beetles can be an issue in warmer months.
- Rich in vitamins A, C, and K, calcium, and folate.

## Relevance to Cultivation

Tatsoi is a premier cold-hardy Asian green for winter production in unheated greenhouse systems. Its exceptional frost tolerance, compact growth habit, and flavor improvement in cold conditions make it ideal for the winter harvest model. For market gardeners, tatsoi adds visual interest and culinary versatility to winter CSA shares and farmers' market offerings. Its reliability in cold frames and unheated hoop houses demonstrates that diverse, nutritious greens can be produced year-round without fossil-fuel heating, aligning with deep-organic and low-input farming philosophies.
