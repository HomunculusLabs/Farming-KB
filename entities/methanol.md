## Methanol (Methyl Alcohol)

## Description

Methanol (CH₃OH), also known as methyl alcohol, wood alcohol, or carbinol, is the simplest alcohol and a widely used industrial and laboratory solvent. With a boiling point of 64°C (147°F), it is one of the most volatile common solvents. In cannabis and natural product extraction, methanol is valued for its ability to dissolve cannabinoids efficiently without co-extracting excessive water-soluble tars. It is produced both industrially from synthesis gas and traditionally through destructive distillation of wood.

## Classification

- **Category**: Chemical / Solvent
- **Chemical type**: Primary alcohol
- **Molecular formula**: CH₃OH
- **Molecular weight**: 32.04 g/mol
- **Boiling point**: 64°C (147°F)
- **CAS number**: 67-56-1
- **Appearance**: Colorless liquid with characteristic odor
