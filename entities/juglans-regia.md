## Juglans regia

**Type:** Species (Tree)
**Category:** Nut-bearing tree
**Family:** Juglandaceae
**Also known as:** Persian walnut, English walnut

## Overview
Juglans regia is a slow-growing, broad-crowned deciduous tree reaching
up to 24 m in height. It is one of the most commercially important
walnut species worldwide, prized for both its nutritious nuts and its
exceptionally decorative timber. The species has been cultivated for
thousands of years across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, with
Persia (modern Iran) being its probable centre of origin. The name
"English walnut" is a misnomer arising from its introduction to
England via Roman trade routes.

- **Digestion time:** 3 hours
- **Culinary uses:** Nuts eaten raw, roasted, and in virtually
  every form of cooking; unripe fruits added to jams, preserved
