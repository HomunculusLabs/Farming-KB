## Cinderella 99

**Cinderella 99**, commonly known as **C99** or **Cindy**, is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid bred by Brothers Grimm (Mr. Soul and Sly) in the late 1990s. Derived from a selected phenotype of **[[jack-herer]]** crossed with an unknown male, Cinderella 99 was stabilized through several generations of inbreeding to produce a remarkably consistent, fast-flowering sativa-dominant hybrid. The strain has achieved legendary status among cannabis breeders and growers for its combination of cerebral effects, tropical flavor, and ease of cultivation.

## Genetic Lineage

Cinderella 99's origin story begins with a remarkable stroke of luck. Mr. Soul of Brothers Grimm found three seeds in a batch of Jack Herer purchased in Amsterdam. One of the resulting female plants, designated "Princess," exhibited exceptional qualities and became the foundation of the entire Cinderella 99 line.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Distinctively tropical and fruity with notes of pineapple, grapefruit, and mango. A subtle floral quality underlies the fruit-forward profile.
- **Flavor:** Sweet and fruity on the inhale with tropical citrus dominating. The flavor is clean and smooth, with a light earthy finish.
- **Aftertaste:** Lingering tropical sweetness with hints of pineapple and floral notes.

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

Cinderella 99 delivers a clear, uplifting sativa experience with moderate body relaxation. It is often described as a "thinking strain" that enhances creativity and focus without the anxiety sometimes associated with pure sativas.

- **Onset:** 5–15 minutes; immediate cerebral uplift and enhanced sensory perception
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; sustained mental clarity with mild physical comfort
- **Duration:** 1.5–3 hours; relatively clean comedown

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Clear-headed cerebral stimulation and focus
- Enhanced creativity and problem-solving
- Euphoric mood elevation
- Mild body relaxation without heavy sedation
- Social energy and talkativeness
- Motivation and productivity

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes
- Mild anxiety at high doses (less common than with many sativas)
- Restlessness or jitteriness in some users
- Insomnia if consumed late in the day
