# Cinderella 99

**Cinderella 99**, commonly known as **C99** or **Cindy**, is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid bred by Brothers Grimm (Mr. Soul and Sly) in the late 1990s. Derived from a selected phenotype of **Jack Herer** crossed with an unknown male, Cinderella 99 was stabilized through several generations of inbreeding to produce a remarkably consistent, fast-flowering sativa-dominant hybrid. The strain has achieved legendary status among cannabis breeders and growers for its combination of cerebral effects, tropical flavor, and ease of cultivation.

## Genetic Lineage

Cinderella 99's origin story begins with a remarkable stroke of luck. Mr. Soul of Brothers Grimm found three seeds in a batch of Jack Herer purchased in Amsterdam. One of the resulting female plants, designated "Princess," exhibited exceptional qualities and became the foundation of the entire Cinderella 99 line.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Breeder** | Brothers Grimm (Mr. Soul & Sly) |
| **Year** | Late 1990s (stabilized ~1999) |
| **Type** | Sativa-dominant hybrid (~65% sativa / 35% indica) |
| **THC content** | 18–22% |
| **CBD content** | Low (<0.5%) |

### Lineage Breakdown

| Generation | Cross |
|------------|-------|
| **P1** | Princess (selected Jack Herer phenotype) × Mystery Male |
| **F1** | Cinderella 88 |
| **IBL** | Cinderella 99 (stabilized through several generations of [[cannabis-backcrossing|inbreeding]]) |

The "99" designation refers to the approximate year the strain was sufficiently stabilized. Brothers Grimm used cubing and backcrossing techniques to fix the desirable Princess traits, producing a true-breeding IBL (inbred line).

## Terpene Profile

Cinderella 99 is known for its distinctive tropical and fruity terpene profile, which sets it apart from the more earthy and skunky sativas of its era.

| Terpene | Role |
|---------|------|
| **[[myrcene]]** | Present but less dominant; contributes body relaxation |
| **[[limonene]]** | Prominent citrus and tropical fruit character |
| **[[pinene]]** | Pine and fresh notes; mental clarity |
| **[[beta-caryophyllene]]** | Subtle peppery undertone |
| **[[linalool]]** | Floral sweetness complementing the tropical fruit notes |

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Distinctively tropical and fruity with notes of pineapple, grapefruit, and mango. A subtle floral quality underlies the fruit-forward profile.
- **Flavor:** Sweet and fruity on the inhale with tropical citrus dominating. The flavor is clean and smooth, with a light earthy finish.
- **Aftertaste:** Lingering tropical sweetness with hints of pineapple and floral notes.

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

Cinderella 99 delivers a clear, uplifting sativa experience with moderate body relaxation. It is often described as a "thinking strain" that enhances creativity and focus without the anxiety sometimes associated with pure sativas.

- **Onset:** 5–15 minutes; immediate cerebral uplift and enhanced sensory perception
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; sustained mental clarity with mild physical comfort
- **Duration:** 1.5–3 hours; relatively clean comedown

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Clear-headed cerebral stimulation and focus
- Enhanced creativity and problem-solving
- Euphoric mood elevation
- Mild body relaxation without heavy sedation
- Social energy and talkativeness
- Motivation and productivity

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes
- Mild anxiety at high doses (less common than with many sativas)
- Restlessness or jitteriness in some users
- Insomnia if consumed late in the day

### Medical Applications

Cinderella 99 is valued for daytime medical use due to its functional, clear-headed effects. Patients can often maintain normal activities while experiencing symptom relief.

- **Depression:** Uplifting, motivating effects help counter depressive symptoms
- **Fatigue:** Energizing properties useful for daytime fatigue management
- **ADHD:** Some patients report improved focus and task completion
- **Anxiety:** Anxiolytic at moderate doses without cognitive impairment
- **Mild pain:** Light analgesic properties for [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]
- **[[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]]:** Reliable appetite stimulation

## Growing Information

Cinderella 99 is considered one of the easiest sativa-dominant strains to grow, which contributes significantly to its popularity. Its fast flowering time for a sativa and compact stature make it accessible to growers with limited space.

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Flowering time** | 7–8 weeks (exceptionally fast for sativa-dominant) |
| **Indoor yield** | 14–20 oz/m² |
| **Outdoor yield** | Moderate to high |
| **Height** | Medium (100–150 cm) |
| **Difficulty** | Easy |
| **Preferred climate** | Warm, Mediterranean |

### Cultivation Notes

Cinderella 99's combination of sativa effects with indica-like growing characteristics makes it an excellent choice for indoor growers who want cerebral effects without the challenges of tall, long-flowering sativas.

- **Structure:** Compact and bushy despite sativa dominance; ideal for indoor cultivation
- **Yield:** Heavy yielder with dense, resinous buds
- **Training:** Responds well to [[screen-of-green]], LST, and topping
- **Clone vs seed:** Available as both stable seeds and clones; IBL genetics ensure consistency
- **Nutrients:** Moderate feeder; standard bloom nutrients produce excellent results
- **Harvest:** Monitor [[trichome-development-and-morphology]] — optimal around 7–8 weeks when trichomes are mostly cloudy

## Cultural Significance

Cinderella 99 holds a unique position in cannabis history as one of the most influential breeding projects of the late 1990s. The Brothers Grimm's decision to release seeds publicly (rather than keeping the genetics as a clone-only line) democratized access to exceptional sativa genetics.

C99 has served as a parent strain in countless breeding programs. Its genetics appear in Princess Haze (C99 × [[amnesia-haze]]), Vortex, and numerous other hybrids. The strain's reputation for combining sativa effects with easy cultivation has made it a benchmark in [[cannabis-seed-banks-and-genetics|breeding]] circles.
