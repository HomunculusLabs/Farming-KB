# Gelato

## Overview

Gelato (also known as Gelato #33 or Larry Bird) is a balanced  is-strain-chupacabra created
by San Francisco's Cookie Family in collaboration with Sherbinski. A cross between Sunset
Sherbet and [[girl-scout-cookies]] × Pink Panties)
- **Male parent:** Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies ([[durban-poison]], and an unknown F1 hybrid, making
Gelato a complex polyhybrid with deep California genetics.

## Appearance

- **:** Dense, tightly packed calyxes forming chunky spherical nuggets.
  Moderate leaf-to-calyx ratio with occasional foxtailing.
- **Color:** Deep purple and dark forest green with bright orange pistils. Purple expression
  is highly variable — cooler nighttime temps in late flowering enhance anthocyanin production.
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptionally heavy resin. Buds appear frosty white under
  magnification with dense swollen glandular trichomes. Sticky to the touch.
- **Dry cure:** High resin content makes Gelato preferred for hash and concentrate production.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Primary aroma:** Sweet, creamy, dessert-like — reminiscent of berry gelato or lavender
   with cookie dough undertones.
- **Secondary notes:** Ripe berries (blueberry, blackberry), citrus peel, fresh lavender,
  subtle earthy herbal finish.
- **Inhalation:** Sweet and creamy with pronounced berry and cookie notes.
- **Exhalation:** Smooth, slightly minty and herbal with lingering vanilla and lavender.
  Earthy undertones emerge as the bowl progresses.

## 

- **Limonene** (0.5–1.2%): Dominant; citrus aroma, mood elevation, stress relief.
- **Caryophyllene** (0.3–0.8%): Peppery spice, anti-inflammatory via CB2 binding.
- **Myrcene** (0.3–0.7%): Herbal base note; sedative and muscle relaxant.
- **Linalool** (0.2–0.5%): Floral lavender; anxiolytic, contributes smooth body effects.
- **Humulene** (0.1–0.3%): Woody; appetite suppressant, anti-inflammatory.
- **Ocimene** (trace): Sweet, woody; contributes uplifting head effects.

The linalool + limonene + caryophyllene combination produces the signature sweet-floral-spice
character and balanced psychoactive profile.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 18–25% (modern cuts 25–28%)
- **CBD:** < 0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3% · **THCV:** Trace to 0.2%

Decidedly THC-dominant with minimal CBD. Potency varies by phenotype (#33, #41, #45) and
cultivation practices.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid, 1–3 minutes after inhalation.
- **Head effects:** Euphoric cerebral uplift with enhanced creativity and sociability.
  Thoughts become associative; focus sharpens initially before shifting dreamier.
- **Body effects:** Warm, soothing relaxation building progressively. Muscle tension dissolves
  without heavy sedation — functional rather than couch-locking. Notable pain relief.
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (smoked); extended with edibles or concentrates.
- **Progression:** Euphoric and mentally active first 45–60 minutes, then calm physical
  relaxation in the second hour.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks (56–63 days).
- **Yield:** Moderate to high, 400–500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor.
- **Difficulty:** Moderate. Sensitive to nitrogen burn in late flowering. Responds well to
  LST and SCROG. Multiple colas develop readily.
- **Climate:** Mediterranean outdoors. Prefers 65–80°F flowering. Cool nights (55–65°F) in
  weeks 5–8 enhance purple coloration. Moderate spider mite and powdery mildew susceptibility;
  maintain 45–55% RH during flowering.

## Medical Applications

- **Stress and anxiety:** Linalool-dominant profile provides anxiolytic effects without heavy
  sedation; suitable for daytime.
- **Chronic pain:** Moderate analgesia from caryophyllene + THC; useful for mild-to-moderate
  musculoskeletal pain.
- **Mood disorders:** Euphoric onset alleviates depressive symptoms; some patients report
  PTSD hypervigilance relief.
- **Appetite stimulation:** Reliable munchies effect; helpful in chemotherapy or HIV-related
  appetite loss.
- **Insomnia:** At higher doses, body-relaxation phase promotes sleep onset.

## Side Effects

- **Common:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, mild paranoia at higher doses.
- **Uncommon:** Dizziness, headache, paradoxical anxiety (dose-dependent).
- **Contraindications:** Avoid in patients with psychosis history without medical supervision.

## Notable Phenotypes and Derivatives

- **Gelato #33** ("Larry Bird"): Benchmark cut; balanced high, purple coloration.
- **Gelato #41:** Higher THC, more sativa-leaning; preferred by extractors.
