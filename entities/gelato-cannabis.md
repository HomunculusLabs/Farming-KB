# Gelato (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

**Gelato** is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its dessert-like aroma, potent effects, and visually striking flower structure. A cross between **Sunset Sherbet** and **Girl Scout Cookies (Thin Mint phenotype)**, Gelato was bred by the **Cookie Fam** genetics collective in San Francisco during the mid-2010s. Named for its sweet, creamy flavor profile reminiscent of Italian gelato, the strain rapidly ascended to become one of the most commercially successful and culturally significant cannabis varieties of the modern era. Its balanced 50/50 hybrid genetics deliver a harmonious blend of cerebral euphoria and physical relaxation, appealing to both recreational and medical users.

## Genetic Lineage

- **Type:** Balanced hybrid (~50% indica / 50% sativa)
- **Cross:** Sunset Sherbet × Girl Scout Cookies (Thin Mint)
- **Breeder:** Cookie Fam Genetics, San Francisco, California
- **Lineage Details:**
  - **Sunset Sherbet:** An indica-leaning hybrid descended from Girl Scout Cookies × Pink Panties, contributing sweet berry and tropical fruit aromas alongside relaxing physical effects
  - **Girl Scout Cookies (Thin Mint):** The legendary OG Kush × Durban Poison hybrid that defined the modern "Cookie" genetic line, providing potency, complex terpenes, and cerebral euphoria
- **Notable Phenotypes:** Gelato #33 (Larry Bird), Gelato #41, Gelato #45 — each selected for specific flavor and effect profiles. Gelato #33 is the most widely recognized, noted for particularly vibrant purple coloration and intense dessert aroma.

## Terpene Profile

Gelato's dessert-like aroma is driven by a terpene composition that blends citrus, berry, and floral notes with earthy undertones:

| Terpene | Approximate Content | Aroma / Effect Contribution |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Limonene** | 0.4–1.0% | Citrus, sweet; mood elevation, stress relief, anti-anxiety |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.7% | Peppery, spicy; anti-inflammatory (CB2 agonist), analgesic |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.4% | Woody, earthy; anti-inflammatory, appetite suppressant |
| **Linalool** | 0.1–0.3% | Floral, lavender; anxiolytic, sedative, calming |
| **Myrcene** | 0.1–0.3% | Herbal, earthy; muscle relaxant, sedative |
| **Pinene (beta)** | 0.05–0.2% | Pine; mental clarity, bronchodilator |
| **Ocimene** | Trace | Sweet, herbal; decongestant, uplifting |

The interplay of limonene, caryophyllene, and linalool creates Gelato's signature sweet citrus and berry aroma with pronounced lavender and herbal accents on the finish.

## Cannabinoid Content

- **THC:** 20–25% (some phenotypes test up to 27%)
- **CBD:** 0.01–0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** Trace amounts (<0.1%)
- **THCV:** Trace amounts
- **CBC:** Trace

Gelato's high THC content with negligible CBD produces potent psychoactive effects. The therapeutic benefit is primarily mediated through THC and the entourage effect of its terpene profile rather than CBD.

## Phenotype Description

### Plant Morphology

Gelato plants display a compact, indica-influenced structure with sativa vigor:

- **Height:** Medium, 100–150 cm indoors with training
- **Structure:** Bushy, dense branching with moderate internodal spacing; responds well to LST, SCROG, and topping
- **Leaf:** Broad leaflets with indica dominance; dark green coloration
- **Flowers:** Dense, chunky colas with exceptional trichome coverage; calyxes swell significantly during late flowering
- **Color:** Striking purple-to-dark-violet hues in cooler conditions, contrasting with bright orange pistils and a thick frosty layer of resin glands
- **Resin production:** Very high — among the most resinous strains in the Cookie genetic line

### Cultivation

| Parameter | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| Flowering time | 8–9 weeks |
| Indoor yield | 400–500 g/m² |
| Outdoor yield | 500–600 g/plant |
| Harvest (Northern Hemisphere) | Late September to mid-October |
| Difficulty | Moderate to advanced |
| Resistance | Moderate; monitor for powdery mildew and botrytis |

Gelato thrives in controlled indoor environments where temperature and humidity can be precisely managed. Cooler nighttime temperatures (12–18°C) during the final 2–3 weeks of flowering are critical for expressing the vivid purple coloration. The strain is a moderate-to-heavy feeder, particularly during mid-flowering when calyx swelling accelerates. Growers should ensure adequate calcium and magnesium supplementation to prevent deficiencies. Pruning and defoliation improve airflow through the dense canopy, reducing mold risk.

## Effects Profile

### Onset and Duration

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes (smoking/vaping)
- **Peak:** 20–45 minutes
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours

### Reported Effects

**Cerebral (Sativa-influenced):**
- Euphoric, uplifting head high with enhanced mood
- Creative stimulation and heightened artistic inspiration
- Improved focus and sociability at moderate doses
- Mild sensory enhancement (color perception, music appreciation)

**Physical (Indica-influenced):**
- Deep body relaxation spreading from head to limbs
- Muscle tension relief without heavy sedation at moderate doses
- Warm, comforting body sensation
- Appetite stimulation
- Progressive calm that may lead to couch-lock at higher doses

The effects are notably balanced — Gelato provides enough mental energy and creative spark for daytime use at lower doses while offering sufficient physical relaxation for evening unwinding at higher doses.

## Medicinal Applications

Gelato is commonly recommended by medical cannabis practitioners for:

- **Chronic pain:** Musculoskeletal pain, joint inflammation, neuropathic discomfort — caryophyllene and humulene contribute anti-inflammatory properties
- **Stress and anxiety:** Limonene and linalool provide anxiolytic effects; euphoric onset helps break rumination cycles
- **Insomnia:** Progressive body relaxation promotes sleep onset at moderate-to-high doses, particularly Gelato #33
- **Inflammation:** Caryophyllene acts as a CB2 receptor agonist, providing systemic anti-inflammatory benefit
- **Depression:** Mood-elevating euphoria and creative stimulation help counter low mood and anhedonia
- **PTSD:** Balanced effects may help manage hypervigilance and anxiety without dissociation (patient-dependent)
- **Appetite loss:** THC-driven appetite stimulation useful for cachexia, nausea, or appetite suppression from medication

### Dosage Considerations

New or sensitive users should begin with low doses (1–2 inhalations or 2.5–5 mg THC in edible form). The 20–25% THC range can produce anxiety, paranoia, or dizziness at higher doses in susceptible individuals.

## Cultivation Notes

Gelato is best suited for growers with intermediate to advanced experience due to its dense bud structure and specific environmental requirements:
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean-like conditions; humidity below 50% during flowering is essential to prevent mold in the dense colas
- **Training:** LST and SCROG maximize yield; topping encourages lateral branching and more bud sites
- **Nutrients:** Moderate vegetative feeding increasing to heavy PK (phosphorus/potassium) during weeks 4–7 of flowering
- **Pest management:** Regular inspection for spider mites and fungus gnats; neem oil or integrated pest management recommended
- **Harvest timing:** Trichomes cloudy with 10–20% amber for balanced effects; earlier harvest (mostly cloudy) preserves more cerebral character
- **Curing:** Minimum 4-week cure recommended to fully develop the complex dessert flavor profile
