## Reny Mia Slay

## Description

Reny Mia Slay (born 1953) is an Australian editor and publishing professional who served as the manuscript editor and typescript arranger for [[andrew-jeeves]] (design, illustration, cover, and layout).
- Listed in the National Library of Australia cataloguing-in-publication entry for the manual.
- Her birth year (1953) is recorded in the bibliographic entry, making her approximately 35 years old at the time of the manual's publication.

## Role in Permaculture Publishing

The Designers' Manual is a substantial work of over 570 pages covering topics ranging from soil science and hydrology to legal strategies for land trusts. Slay's editorial responsibilities likely included:

1. **Text Organization**: Arranging Mollison's manuscript into the chapter and section structure that makes the work accessible as both a textbook and reference manual.
2. **Consistency Enforcement**: Ensuring consistent terminology, cross-referencing, and formatting across the 14 chapters and multiple appendices.
3. **Reference Management**: Organizing the extensive reference lists that close each chapter, as well as the consolidated references section at the end of the book.
4. **Index and Appendix Coordination**: Managing the plant lists (by common name and species name), glossary, and comprehensive index that make the manual a practical reference tool.
