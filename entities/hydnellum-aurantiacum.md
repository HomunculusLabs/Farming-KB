## Overview

**Hydnellum aurantiacum** is a species of tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, order Thelephorales. It is a mycorrhizal associate of coniferous and mixed forests, recognized by its orange to reddish-brown cap surface and distinctive spined hymenium.

The species produces tough, corky basidiocarps that are inedible due to their extremely bitter taste and woody texture. It is primarily of ecological interest as an ectomycorrhizal mutualist and as a bioindicator of old-growth forest conditions.
