## Overview

Gelato (also known as Larry Bird or Gelato #33) is a highly influential hybrid
cannabis strain bred by Cookie Family Genetics in the San Francisco Bay Area.
First released around 2015, it rapidly became one of the most popular strains
in [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]] and Europe.

The strain is renowned for its complex dessert-like aroma and potent, balanced
effects that combine cerebral euphoria with deep physical relaxation. Its
[[cannabis-genetic-stability]] and [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] have made it a foundational parent for
dozens of subsequent crosses.

Gelato won first place at the 2018 Emerald Cup and has accumulated numerous
cannabis competition awards. It is considered a modern classic that defined the
dessert-strain category.

## Lineage

Gelato is a cross between [[girl-scout-cookies]] (GSC) and [[sunset-sherbet]]. Both
parents are themselves products of the Cookie Family [[cervantes-breeding-program-design-goal-setting]], making
Gelato essentially a concentrated expression of Cookie genetics.

Girl Scout Cookies contributes OG Kush and Durban Poison heritage, providing
potency and a complex terpene backbone. Sunset Sherbet (a GSC × Pink Panties
cross) adds sweet, fruity terpenes and moderates the sativa influence.

The specific phenotype designated Gelato #33 is considered the most desirable
cut. Multiple numbered phenotypes exist (#41, #45), with variation in color
expression, terpene ratios, and growth characteristics.

## Appearance

Buds are dense, medium to large, with a chunky, cone-shaped structure. The
calyxes are dark forest green with deep purple and violet hues, especially in
cooler growing conditions. Bright orange pistils stand out against the dark
foliage.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosted, almost
sugary appearance. The [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] is among the highest of modern dessert
strains, contributing to both potency and the sticky, tactile quality of cured
buds.

The overall visual impression is dark, colorful, and coated — often described
as resembling the ice cream for which it is named.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by sweet, dessert-like notes with pronounced lavender
and berry undertones. A citrus sharpness (lemon, orange rind) cuts through the
sweetness, complemented by earthy, woody base notes from the OG Kush lineage.

On the palate, Gelato delivers a creamy, dessert-like smoke. Primary flavors
include sweet berries, lavender, vanilla, and fresh citrus. The exhale
typically reveals cookie and dough-like notes inherited from the GSC parent.

The complexity and sweetness of the flavor profile set the standard for an
entire category of dessert strains that followed. Many imitators have attempted
to replicate the Gelato terpene combination.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

Dominant terpenes are limonene (up to 1.0%), caryophyllene (0.8–1.2%), and
linalool (0.5–0.8%). Secondary terpenes include myrcene, humulene,
ocimene, and trace amounts of bisabolol and nerolidol.

THC content typically ranges from 18–26%, with some premium cuts testing above
28%. CBD levels are very low (less than 0.1%), consistent with modern
high-THC hybrid genetics. [[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]] are present in trace amounts.

The limonene-linalool combination is responsible for the citrus-floral aroma
and contributes to the strain's mood-elevating and anxiolytic properties.
