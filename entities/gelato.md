# Gelato

Gelato is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its dessert-like
aroma profile and potent effects. A cross between Sunset Sherbet and Thin
Mint Girl Scout Cookies, it was first bred by the San Francisco Bay Area
collective Cookie Fam (specifically grower Sherbinski) around 2012-2013.
Gelato quickly became one of the most commercially successful and
genetically influential strains of the 2010s, lending its genetics to
dozens of derivative cultivars including Gelato #33, #41, and #45.
The strain typically tests between 20-25% THC, with some phenotypes
exceeding 27%. Its terpene profile is dominated by linalool, caryophyllene,
and limonene, producing a sweet, fruity, and slightly earthy bouquet
reminiscent of its namesake Italian frozen dessert.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Type:** Balanced hybrid (approximately 55% indica / 45% sativa)
- **Breeders:** Cookie Fam / Sherbinski (San Francisco, California)
- **Parent 1:** Sunset Sherbet (Girl Scout Cookies x Pink Panties)
- **Parent 2:** Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies (OG Kush x Durban Poison)
- **Notable phenotypes:** Gelato #33 ("Larry Bird"), Gelato #41,
  Gelato #45, Acai Berry Gelato, Italian Ice
- **Descendant strains:** Biscotti, Jealousy, Kush Mints, Ice Cream
  Cake, Jungle Cake, MAC (Miracle Alien Cookies crosses)

## Growth Characteristics

- **Indoor flowering time:** 8-9 weeks
- **Outdoor harvest:** Early to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere)
- **Height:** Medium-tall; tends to stretch during early flowering
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (400-500 g/m2 indoors)
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate; benefits from training (LST, SCROG)
- **Branching structure:** Dense lateral branching with thick colas
- **Temperature sensitivity:** Prefers warm daytime temperatures
  (22-28C); cool night temperatures (18-21C) can enhance purple
  coloration in the calyxes

## Appearance and Aroma

The buds are dense and chunky with a conical structure, displaying deep
purple and forest green hues interspersed with vibrant orange pistils.
A thick coating of milky-white trichomes gives the flowers a frosted
appearance. The aroma is immediately recognizable: sweet citrus and
berry notes on the inhale, followed by a creamy, lavender-like
undertone attributed to high linalool content. When ground, the
flower releases additional earthy and cookie-like notes inherited
from its GSC lineage.

| Terpene | Typical Range | Aroma Character |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|

## Cannabinoid Profile

Gelato is THC-dominant with minimal CBD content. Typical lab analyses
show THC between 18-26%, depending on cultivation conditions and
phenotype. The acidic precursor THCA accounts for the majority of
total cannabinoids in freshly harvested flower. CBG and CBC are
present in trace amounts (0.1-0.3% each). The low CBD content
means Gelato's therapeutic applications rely primarily on THC and
the entourage effect from its terpene-rich profile rather than
meaningful CBD contributions.

## Effects Profile

- **Onset:** Rapid (2-5 minutes when smoked or vaporized)
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Character:** Euphoric cerebral lift transitioning into a deeply
  relaxing body stone. The initial sativa-dominant head high produces
  creative, uplifting sensations while the indica body effects emerge
  gradually, leading to physical relaxation and potential couch-lock
  at higher doses
- **Common effects:** Euphoria, creativity, relaxation, increased
  sociability, sensory enhancement, mild sedation at high doses
- **Potential side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness with
  overconsumption, anxiety in sensitive individuals at high doses

## Therapeutic Applications

The combination of high THC, linalool, and caryophyllene makes Gelato
popular among medical cannabis patients. Linalool contributes
anxiolytic and sedative properties, while caryophyllene acts as a
dietary CB2 receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory activity.
Common therapeutic uses include chronic pain management, stress and
anxiety relief, insomnia (especially the more indica-leaning
phenotypes), appetite stimulation, and mood elevation for depressive
symptoms. The entourage effect between THC and the linalool-dominant
terpene profile is thought to moderate some of THC's anxiogenic
potential.

## Cultivation Notes

Gelato performs well in both indoor and outdoor environments but
requires attention to humidity control due to its dense bud
structure, which makes it susceptible to bud rot (Botrytis
cinerea) in humid conditions. Low-stress training (LST) and
screen of green (SCROG) methods are recommended to manage its
stretch during the first three weeks of flowering and to maximize
light exposure to lower bud sites. The strain responds well to
defoliation in weeks 3-4 of flower. Nutrient requirements are
moderate; excessive nitrogen during late flowering can reduce
terpene production and delay maturation.

## Commercial Significance

Gelato is among the top-selling cannabis strains in legal North
American markets. Its genetics have become foundational in modern
cannabis breeding, with Sherbinski licensing the Gelato name to
numerous cultivators. The strain's commercial success reflects
broader consumer trends toward dessert-flavored, high-potency
hybrids. Gelato's genetic stability across phenotypes has made it
a reliable parent for breeding programs seeking sweet flavors
and balanced effects. Multiple phenotype-specific cuts command
premium prices in the craft cannabis market.
