# Fish Emulsion

## Description

**Fish emulsion** is a liquid organic fertilizer (N-P-K 5-2-2) made from heat- and acid-processed fish parts. It provides a more balanced nutrient profile than many other natural nitrogen fertilizers, containing nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium in moderate proportions along with a rich complement of micronutrients derived from the diverse diet of fish. Fish emulsion is soluble and quick-acting, making it one of the faster-acting organic liquid fertilizers available.

## Classification

- **Category:** Natural organic fertilizer / Fish by-product / Liquid fertilizer
- **N-P-K:** 5-2-2
- **Nutrient Availability:** Fast-acting, soluble (1–4 months)
- **Application Form:** Liquid, diluted with water
- **Domain:** Organic gardening, horticulture, aquaculture by-product utilization

## Key Facts

- Made from fish parts processed with heat and acid into a concentrated liquid emulsion.
- Provides a relatively **balanced N-P-K profile** compared to single-nutrient organic fertilizers.
- Rich in **micronutrients** because fish accumulate trace elements from their diverse aquatic diets.
- Soluble and quick-acting — must be **diluted before application** due to concentrated nutrient levels.
- Can be applied to soil as a drench or, in some circumstances, as a foliar spray (though foliar feeding has limited value for macronutrients).
- Nutrients available for 1 to 4 months after application.

## Cautions and Considerations

- **Strong odor** — smells like rotting fish; do not apply to lawns or garden areas on the same day as outdoor entertaining.
- **Attracts flies**, which aid in the decay process but may be undesirable near living spaces.
- **Attracts wildlife** — bears, dogs, and other animals may be drawn to treated areas.
- Odor and animal attraction typically subside within a few days.
- Heat processing destroys some enzymes, proteins, vitamins, and micronutrients that remain in cold-processed fish products (hydrolyzed fish).

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- Fish emulsion and other fish-based products have been used as nitrogen supplements in mushroom substrate preparation, particularly in outdoor bed cultivation.
- The micronutrient profile of fish emulsion supports enzymatic processes in both plants and soil microbes, including the metalloenzymes that require trace elements as cofactors.
- As a liquid fertilizer, fish emulsion can be incorporated into compost tea formulations to boost nutrient content.
- Fish-based fertilizers represent a historical agricultural practice: indigenous peoples of North America traditionally placed fish in planting holes with crops like corn, combining the Law of Return with targeted nutrient application.
- The solubility of fish emulsion makes it suitable for fertigation (fertilizer + irrigation) systems in organic growing operations.
