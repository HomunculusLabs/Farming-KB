# 2C-T-2 (Rosy)

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthiophenethylamine

**PiHKAL #40** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +++

## Overview

2C-T-2 is a psychedelic phenethylamine belonging to the 2C-T (thioether) sub-family of the [[2c-family-compounds-overview]]. It was synthesized and bioassayed by [[alexander-shulgin]] as part of the systematic investigation of 4-alkylthio substituted 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamines. The ethylthio group at the 4-position produces a compound with notable potency and extended duration.

Shulgin's published dosage range: **12–25 mg**, with a duration of **6–8 hours**. The compound's dose-response curve is relatively steep — the difference between a manageable experience (12 mg) and an overwhelming one (25 mg) is significant.

## Qualitative Effects

- Strong visual effects with color enhancement and geometric patterning
- Euphoric and emotionally open experience
- Moderate to strong body load
- Enhanced appreciation of music
- Eyes-closed imagery can be rich and detailed
- Combination of psychedelic and mild empathogenic qualities
- Some users report a distinctive "warm" quality to the experience

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

Shulgin noted that 2C-T-2 produces a full psychedelic experience with good visuals and a relatively well-tolerated body load. Reports at common doses describe pleasant, colorful experiences with emotional openness. The compound was found to be reliable in its effects and suitable for repeat experiences.

## Synthesis (from PiHKAL)

The synthesis of 2C-T-2 is more complex than most 2C compounds due to the need to construct the thioether linkage:

1. **Chlorosulfonation:** 1,4-dimethoxybenzene is treated with chlorosulfonic acid in CH₂Cl₂ (vigorous reaction, HCl evolution) to give 2,5-dimethoxybenzenesulfonyl chloride as a yellow crystalline solid, mp 115–117 °C. This intermediate is stable on storage.

2. **Zn/H₂SO₄ reduction:** The sulfonyl chloride is reduced with zinc dust in 25% H₂SO₄ to yield **2,5-dimethoxythiophenol** as a white oil, bp 70–80 °C at 0.3 mm/Hg. This is the key common precursor to all 2C-T compounds — Shulgin called it "a most valuable precursor to all members of the 2C-T family."

3. **S-alkylation:** The thiol is alkylated with ethyl bromide using KOH in boiling ethanol to give 2,5-dimethoxyphenyl ethyl sulfide as a pale amber oil, used without further purification.

4. **Vilsmeier formylation:** Treatment with N-methylformanilide/POCl₃ yields 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylthiobenzaldehyde as almost-white crystals, mp 86–88 °C.

5. **Nitrostyrene formation:** Condensation with nitromethane and ammonium acetate (monitored by TLC). Orange-red crystals of the nitrostyrene, mp 152–154 °C (various recrystallization solvents produced spectacular fluorescent orange crystals from IPA).

6. **LAH reduction:** 24 h reflux with LAH in THF. The free base distills at 120–130 °C at 0.3 mm/Hg. HCl salt: 6.2 g of white crystals.

## Structure-Activity Relationships

The 2C-T series represents a pharmacologically important group of compounds. The sulfur atom at the 4-position creates a distinct pharmacological profile compared to the oxygen-containing (2C-O) and halogen (2C-X) series. The sulfur's larger atomic radius, greater polarizability, and ability to form sulfur-aromatic interactions contribute to both enhanced receptor affinity and distinctive subjective effects compared to oxygen analogues (cf. the near-inactivity of 2c-o-4-entity|2C-O-4 compared to its sulfur counterpart 2C-T-4).

Within the 2C-T series, increasing the alkyl chain on the sulfur generally increases potency and duration, with some variation. Both 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 and 2C-T-2 have been frequently used in therapy as follow-ups to MDMA.

## Tweetio Homologues

All three "Tweetio" (ethoxy-substituted methoxy) homologues of 2C-T-2 were synthesized and bioassayed:

- **2CT2-2ETO** (2-ethoxy homologue): Active at 50 mg oral, rapid onset, intense eyes-closed visuals, ~5–6 h duration. Nasal dose ~10 mg.
- **2CT2-5ETO** (5-ethoxy homologue): Active at 20 mg, slow gentle climb to peak at 3–4 h, "flooding of thoughts and easy conversation" lasting many hours. Some intoxication still noted on day 2. Nicknamed "forever yours."
- **2CT2-2,5DIETO** (2,5-diethoxy homologue): 10–50 mg range; doses not notably different in intensity but progressively longer in duration. At 50 mg, nervousness early, then energy and high attentiveness for several hours. Duration ~9 h.

## Safety Warnings

- Moderate body load and potential nausea during onset
- Gastrointestinal disturbances (diarrhea) reported at higher doses
- Extended duration should be planned for
- The 2C-T series has been associated with some adverse reactions at higher doses
- **Important nomenclature note:** 2C-T-2 should never be abbreviated as "T-2" — T-2 Toxin is a dangerous mycotoxin of the Tricothecene group. These are radically different compounds.
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Scheduled in many countries
