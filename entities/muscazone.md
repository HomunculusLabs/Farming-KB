## Description

Muscazone is a secondary psychoactive compound found in [[amanita-muscaria-and-ibotenic-acid-containing-fungi]] through ultraviolet light exposure and occurs in lesser concentrations than either ibotenic acid or muscimole. Muscazone may contribute to the overall intoxication effects of fly agaric mushrooms.

## Classification

- **Category**: Psychoactive compound / Ibotenic acid derivative
- **Chemical class**: Related to the isoxazole group
- **Source organism**: Amanita muscaria
- **Formation**: UV-mediated conversion of ibotenic acid
