# Casuarina cunninghamiana

## Description

[[permaculture]]
gardens. Produces soft foliage ideal for compost mulch, tolerates drought once
established, and provides excellent shelter in 25-50m windbreak grids around
desert gardens. Used alongside [[yulara-fuel-forest]] with E. camaldulensis
- Soft foliage ideal for compost mulch
- Tall windbreak in 25-50m grids
- Very drought-hardy once established
- Evergreen — year-round protection
- Compatible with wastewater irrigation

## Relevance to Cultivation

Keystone arid-land species for three reasons: nitrogen fixation enriches poor
desert soils, tall evergreen form provides permanent wind shelter, and soft
foliage produces high-quality mulch. These three functions address the primary
limiting factors in desert agriculture simultaneously.
