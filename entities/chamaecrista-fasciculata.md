## Distribution and Habitat

*C. fasciculata* is native to:

- **Eastern and central United States** — from Massachusetts to Florida, west to Minnesota, Nebraska, Colorado, and New Mexico
- **Eastern Canada** — southern Ontario
- **Northern Mexico**

It is adapted to a wide range of habitats:

- **Prairies and open grasslands**
- **Savannas and woodland edges**
- **Old fields and disturbed sites**
- **Roadsides and railway embankments**
- **Sandy or rocky soils** with low fertility
