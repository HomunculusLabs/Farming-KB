# Enlil

## Description

Enlil (also known as Ilu-kur-gal, El Shaddai, YHWH, and Jehovah) is a central deity in the Sumerian pantheon, serving as the god of storms, vengeance, and authoritarian control. In ethnomycological and revisionist historical frameworks, Enlil is identified as the "jealous god" of the Abrahamic traditions — the deity who sought to deny humanity access to entheogenic plants and knowledge, enforcing servitude through fear, law, and punishment.
