### Cannabinoid Content

THC levels in well-grown Thin Mint typically range from 18% to 25%, with
some phenotypic selections testing above 27%.

CBD content is very low, generally below 0.1%, which is typical for
modern dessert-strain genetics.

Minor cannabinoids including CBC and CBG have been detected at trace
levels, though they do not contribute significantly to the overall effect
profile.

## Effects and Medical Applications

Thin Mint delivers a dual-phase effect that begins with a cerebral rush
— euphoria, creative energy, and mood elevation — before transitioning
into deep physical relaxation over the course of 60–90 minutes.

The onset is relatively fast, typically felt within 5–10 minutes of
inhalation, with peak effects lasting 2–3 hours.

Commonly reported recreational effects include uplifted mood, heightened
sensory perception, giggly euphoria, and progressive body stone.

In medical contexts, Thin Mint has been used to manage chronic pain,
nausea, appetite loss, insomnia, and stress-related disorders.

The strain's mood-elevating properties make it particularly well-suited
for patients dealing with treatment-resistant depression and anxiety.

Potential side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness at higher
doses, and in rare cases, heightened anxiety or paranoia — particularly
in users sensitive to THC-dominant strains.
