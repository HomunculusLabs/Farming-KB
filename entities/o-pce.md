## Overview

O-PCE (2-oxo-PCE), also known as [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]] compared to the methylamino group found in [[ketamine]].

Onset of effects is 10–20 minutes via insufflation and 30–60 minutes orally. Duration of effects ranges from 3–6 hours, notably longer than ketamine but shorter than some MXE experiences.

The longer duration may reflect slower metabolic clearance. The ethylamino group likely undergoes N-deethylation as a [[bay-11-medical-sativa-granddaddy-purp-pain-relief-oner]] at dissociative doses.

## Effects

Common doses (10–30 mg insufflated) produce progressive dissociation from physical sensation and environment. Users report a clear-headed dissociation compared to the confusion sometimes associated with ketamine.

The "hole" experience occurs at higher doses (30–60 mg insufflated). This state involves complete dissociation from the body and immersive internal experiences often described as philosophical or introspective.

Physical effects include analgesia, ataxia, and mild stimulation. Unlike ketamine, O-PCE reportedly produces less nausea and spatial disorientation at equivalent levels of dissociation.

Cognitive effects at sub-hole doses include enhanced introspection, altered music appreciation, and mild euphoria. Some users describe a contemplative quality distinct from the more chaotic ketamine headspace.

Potential risks include psychological distress during intense experiences. Prolonged derealization and depersonalization have been reported, particularly with frequent use.

Combining O-PCE with depressants increases the risk of dangerous respiratory depression. The compound should not be mixed with alcohol, opioids, or GHB under any circumstances.

Chronic use may carry similar urinary tract risks as ketamine, though this has not been specifically documented. Users experiencing urinary symptoms should discontinue use and seek medical evaluation.

[[health-risks-and-harm-reduction-for-hallucinogenic-mushroom-use]] O-PCE includes starting with very low doses, using a scale for measurement, and having a trip sitter present. The potency of the compound makes eyeballing doses particularly dangerous.

## History

O-PCE was first synthesized during pharmaceutical research into arylcyclohexylamine anesthetics. Like many research chemicals, it was never commercially developed as a pharmaceutical product.

The compound appeared on the online research chemical market around 2017. Initial availability was limited, but it gained traction following restrictions on ketamine and MXE in various jurisdictions.

Online forums began documenting O-PCE experiences in late 2017. User reports consistently noted the longer duration and clearer headspace compared to ketamine as distinguishing features.

Analytical detection of O-PCE in seized materials was first reported by European laboratories around 2018. The EMCDDA has included it in its monitoring database since 2019.
