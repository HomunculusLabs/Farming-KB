## Penicillium expansum

**Type:** Species (Fungus)
**Category:** Filamentous Ascomycete (Post-Harvest Pathogen)
**Also known as:** Blue mold of apples

## Overview
Penicillium expansum is a major post-harvest pathogen responsible for
blue mold rot of apples, pears, and other pome fruits. It is the most
common cause of fruit decay in [[aspergillus-niger]] when grown in [[vegetable-storage-conditions-by-temperature-and-humidity]] (0 to 4 degrees Celsius),
  unlike most other [[trichoderma-viride]] against A. niger
