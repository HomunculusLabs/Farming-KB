# Kalopanax septemlobus

## Overview

Kalopanax septemlobus, commonly called castor aralia or prickly castor tree, is a large deciduous tree in the family Araliaceae. It is native to East Asia, including Japan, Korea, northeastern China, and the Russian Far East. The species is notable for its spiny bark, massive palmate leaves, and valuable timber.

It is one of the few tree-sized members of the Araliaceae, reaching heights of 25 to 30 meters in mature forests. The tree is planted as an ornamental in botanical gardens and arboreta worldwide for its bold, tropical-looking foliage and striking winter silhouette.

In traditional Korean, Chinese, and Japanese medicine, the bark and roots have been used for centuries. The wood is commercially valued for furniture, construction, and cabinetry across East Asia.
