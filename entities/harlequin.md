# Harlequin

Harlequin is a high-CBD, sativa-dominant [[biscotti-cannabis-strain]] renowned for its balanced cannabinoid profile and reliable [[oner-sativa-medical-applications]].

With a roughly 1:1 CBD-to-THC ratio, it delivers clear-headed functional effects alongside therapeutic relief, making it a staple in medical cannabis programs across [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]].

The strain originated in the early 2000s from the cross of [[cannabis-colombian-gold-landrace-sativa]], Thai, and Swiss landrace genetics, bred specifically to maximize CBD content.

Its name references the harlequin character — colorful, multifaceted, and distinctive — reflecting the strain's complex terpene-driven aroma.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 75% sativa / 25% indica)
- **Lineage:** Colombian Gold × Thai × Swiss (most widely accepted pedigree)
- **Breeder:** Developed by Mr. Nice Seeds and refined by multiple breeders in the Pacific Northwest
- **THC content:** 7–15% (moderate)
- **CBD content:** 8–16% (high, often exceeding THC)
- **CBC content:** Low to moderate (0.5–1%)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Mango, earthy musk, sweet tropical fruit
- **Secondary notes:** Pepper, tea-like tannins, subtle floral undertones
- **Taste:** Smooth, fruity inhale with earthy, slightly spicy exhale
- **Smoke quality:** Light and clean, not harsh; notably less irritating than high-THC strains

The aroma is driven predominantly by myrcene, which contributes the mango-like sweetness, with caryophyllene adding peppery spice and pinene lending subtle pine freshness.

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes when inhaled; 45–90 minutes when ingested
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (smoking/vaporizing); 4–6 hours (edibles)
- **Primary effects:** Clear-headed euphoria, mild relaxation, focused alertness
- **Body effects:** Gentle physical relaxation without sedation or couch-lock
- **Cognitive effects:** Functional, not impairing at typical doses

The high-CBD ratio moderates the psychoactive intensity of THC, producing an effect many users describe as "functional clarity."

CBD acts as a partial agonist at CB1 and CB2 receptors, competing with THC and reducing its anxiogenic and psychotomimetic effects.

This pharmacodynamic antagonism is the basis for Harlequin's medical reputation.

### Medical Applications

- **[[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]:** Effective for neuropathic and inflammatory pain without cognitive impairment
- **[[cannabis-anxiety-and-ptsd-treatment]]:** CBD-dominant profile reduces THC-induced anxiety
- **[[cannabis-for-epilepsy-and-seizure-disorders]]:** High CBD content supports anticonvulsant applications
- **Inflammation:** Caryophyllene and CBD synergize as dual CB2 agonists
- **Daytime medication:** Functional profile allows use during work hours

⚠️ Not a substitute for pharmaceutical epilepsy medications without medical supervision.

## Growing Characteristics

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoors; early to mid-October outdoors
- **Yield:** 350–450 g/m² indoors; 400–600 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** Medium-tall (120–180 cm indoors); can exceed 2 m outdoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — stable genetics but requires attention to CBD expression
