# King Stropharia (Stropharia rugoso-annulata)

## Description
King stropharia (Stropharia rugoso-annulata), also known as the [[wine-cap]] mushroom, is a large edible mushroom that has become a flagship species for research into microbial-triggered fruiting. Its size, culinary quality, and role as a model organism in mycological research make it one of the most significant species in modern [[mushroom-cultivation]] science.

## Classification
- **Kingdom**: Fungi
- **Phylum**: Basidiomycota
- **Class**: Agaricomycetes
- **Order**: Agaricales
- **Family**: Strophariaceae
- **Genus**: Stropharia
- **Species**: S. rugoso-annulata

## Key Facts

### Morphology
- Large reddish-brown to burgundy cap (can reach 30+ cm diameter)
- White to grayish gills that darken with age
- Distinctive thick ring (annulus) with radiating ridges (rugoso-annulata = wrinkled ring)
- Thick, sturdy white stem; white [[spore-print]]

### Cultivation Characteristics
- Can be grown on sterilized sawdust with microbial casing
- Wood chips and straw are suitable outdoor substrates
- Benefits significantly from casing treated with native microbial slurries
- Used as the primary model in dilution-to-extinction research

### Role in Microbial Fruiting Research
- The species that demonstrated the dilution-to-extinction method works
- Wild stem base soil contains the correct microbial complement for fruiting
- Casing with native microbes dramatically improves chances of taming wild specimens
- Serial dilutions identified the fruiting trigger point
- Photographed as model organism for casing dilution assay protocols

### Culinary Value
- Edible with pleasant, slightly nutty flavor and wine-like aroma
- Versatile in cooking — grilled, sauteed, or dried
- Large size makes individual specimens impressive for culinary presentation

### Outdoor Cultivation
- One of the easiest mushrooms to grow outdoors in temperate climates
- Thrives in wood chip beds, garden paths, around perennial plantings
- Can form beneficial relationships with garden plants
- Often fruits in large flushes providing abundant harvest

## Relevance to Cultivation
King stropharia occupies a unique position as both a commercially viable edible mushroom and the primary research model for understanding microbial-triggered fruiting. Protocols developed for this species have provided templates applied to other important species including morels and Agaricus varieties.
