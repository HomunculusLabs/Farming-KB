## Taxonomy and Morphology

**Classification:** Fungi > Basidiomycota > Agaricomycetes > Boletales > Boletaceae > *Xerocomellus*

**Basionym:** *Boletus chrysenteron* Bull. (1791)

**Synonyms:** *[[palmer-mushroom-hunting-field-identification]] feature.

The pore surface on the underside of the cap is yellow to yellow-green, sometimes with olive tones, and does not stain blue when bruised.

Pores are angular and small, 1–3 per mm.

Tubes are 5–12 mm deep, adnexed to subdecurrent, yellow, and do not change color when cut.

The stipe is 4–9 cm tall and 0.8–2 cm thick, cylindrical to slightly ventricose.

Stipe surface is yellow above the ring zone, reddish below, with fine longitudinal striations.

The flesh is yellow, sometimes with reddish tinges in the cap and stipe base.

It is soft and does not change color significantly when cut, though some specimens show faint blueing in the stipe base.

Spore print is olive-brown to olive-buff. Spores are 10–14 × 4–6 µm, fusoid-ellipsoid, smooth, and yellowish-brown in KOH.
