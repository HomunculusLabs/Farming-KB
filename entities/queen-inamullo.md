# Queen Inamullo

## Description
Queen Inamullo (necklace of thorns), also known as Huacra-chukcha (hair of thorns), is a powerful tutelary spirit in Amazonian vegetalismo who serves as the master teacher of the arts of the yachay (knowledge), the virote (magical dart), the marupa (magical animal weapon), and the huampi (magical bone dart). She is the source of the [[mariri]] (magical phlegm) powers that vegetalistas receive.

## Classification
- **Type**: Tutelary spirit / master teacher
- **Origin**: Amazonian mestizo vegetalismo
- **Domain**: Shamanic instruction / power transmission
- **Tradition**: Vegetalismo (Amazonian plant medicine)

## Key Facts
- Queen Inamullo is the master teacher of the fundamental weapons and tools of the vegetalista.
- Her alternative name, Huacra-chukcha (hair of thorns), refers to her distinctive appearance.
- She carries a jar of delicate balsams for the protection of mariris (magical phlegm) able to harmonize with the vibrations of the rainbow.
- Her feathered headdress serves as protection, absorbing any curse or evil spell cast by opponents.
- She transmits powers to shamans through their mouths with the help of tutelary spirits.
- Her tutelary spirits include: the sacha-supay (jungle demon), the supayisnachi (maquisapa ghost), the yura-huacra (white-horned deer), the wiracocha-ailco (dog of the water froth), the canero (*Vandelia plazai* fish), the puka-cakila (red-mouthed fish), the yahaarachi (*Gasterotomus latior*, slime fish), and the boa-sacha plant.
- The mariri is transmitted as a colored cord in different colors representing different powers: black, white, red, and orange.
- Each colored mariri serves a specific role in the vegetalista's *ciencia palística* (plant science).
- She is associated with the killo-caranchi (yellow skins), beings whose hair takes the form of the cobra.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology
- Queen Inamullo represents the teaching function in Amazonian plant medicine, where knowledge is transmitted directly from spiritual beings to human practitioners.
- Her association with the mariri and its various colored forms reflects the sophisticated system of plant-mediated power categorization in vegetalismo.
- The tutelary spirits associated with her include both animals and plants, reflecting the integrated nature of Amazonian ethnobiological knowledge.

## Cultural Context
Queen Inamullo appears in Vision 8 of [[ayahuasca-preparation-pablo-amaringo-ucayali-shamanism]]'s [[amaringo-ayahuasca-visions-shipibo-iconography]] (The Powers of the Mariris). She represents the archetypal teacher figure in vegetalismo, the spiritual source from whom the fundamental tools of the practice are received. Her presence explains how individual vegetalistas acquire their specific powers and specializations.
