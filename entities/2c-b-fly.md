Its name derives from the dihydrofuran ("FLY") ring system replacing the 2,5-dimethoxy groups of 2C-B.
The compound produces a longer-duration psychedelic experience compared to 2C-B, with effects described as more entactogenic and visually rich.
It is not naturally occurring and exists solely as a synthetic research chemical.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name:** 2-(8-Bromo-2,3,6,7-tetrahydrofuro[2,3-f][1]benzofuran-4-yl)ethanamine
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₂H₁₆BrNO₂
- **Molecular weight:** 286.16 g/mol
- **CAS number:** Not registered
- **SMILES:** CNCc1c2c(ccc1Br)OCOC2
- **Structure type:** Benzodifuran core with bromine at the 8-position and ethanamine side chain
- **Appearance:** White to off-white crystalline powder
- **Solubility:** Soluble in ethanol, slightly soluble in water; freebase form is lipid-soluble
- **Stability:** Stable at room temperature when kept dry and away from light

### Relationship to 2C-B

2C-B-FLY replaces the 2,5-dimethoxy groups of 2C-B with a dihydrofuran ring fused across positions 2 and 5 of the benzene ring, creating a rigid benzodifuran system.
This conformational constraint alters receptor binding affinity and metabolic stability compared to the flexible 2C-B structure.

## Pharmacology

### Receptor Activity

- **5-HT₂A agonist:** Primary mechanism of psychedelic action; full or partial agonist
- **5-HT₂C agonist:** Contributes to visual effects and mild stimulant properties
- **5-HT₁A:** Weak partial agonist activity; may modulate anxiety response
- **TAAR1:** Minimal activity at trace amine-associated receptor 1
- The benzodifuran ring system generally confers higher selectivity for 5-HT₂A over 5-HT₂C compared to 2C-B

### Pharmacokinetics

- **Onset:** 45–90 minutes after oral administration (slower than 2C-B's 20–45 min)
- **Duration:** 8–12 hours total experience (significantly longer than 2C-B's 4–6 hours)
- **Peak effects:** 2–3 hours post-ingestion
- **Elimination:** Hepatic metabolism via CYP2D6 and CYP3A4; renal excretion of metabolites
- **Bioavailability:** Moderate oral bioavailability; first-pass metabolism reduces systemic exposure

## Effects

### Subjective Experience

- **Visual:** Rich, colorful, and flowing geometric visuals; less "digital" than 2C-B; enhanced color saturation and pattern recognition
- **Cognitive:** Enhanced introspection, philosophical thinking, mild ego dissolution at higher doses
- **Emotional:** Entactogenic qualities — increased empathy, emotional openness, and social connection
- **Somatic:** Body lightness, mild energy, occasional tingling; less physical tension than 2C-B at comparable doses
- **Sensory:** Enhanced appreciation of music and tactile sensation

### Dosage Ranges

- **Light:** 4–6 mg (subtle visual enhancement, mood elevation)
- **Common:** 8–12 mg (full psychedelic effects with manageable intensity)
- **Strong:** 14–18 mg (intense visuals, significant cognitive alteration)
- **⚠️ WARNING:** Doses above 20 mg significantly increase risk of overwhelming experience and prolonged effects; individual sensitivity varies widely

### Side Effects and Risks

- Nausea during onset (common, transient)
- Anxiety and paranoia at higher doses or in unprepared set/setting
- Tachycardia and mild hypertension
- Insomnia following the experience (due to long duration)
- **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** The 2010 batch contamination incident involved [[2c-b]] — Classical psychedelic with different receptor profile but overlapping effects
- [[2c-b]] — Parent phenethylamine compound with shorter duration
