# Psilocybin

**Psilocybin** (4-PO-DMT, O-phosphoryl-4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is a naturally occurring indolealkylamine psychedelic compound found in over 200 species of mushrooms, primarily in the genus *Psilocybe*. It is a prodrug that is rapidly dephosphorylated in the body to **psilocin** (4-HO-DMT), the pharmacologically active metabolite responsible for its psychedelic effects.

## Pharmacology

### Mechanism of Action
Psilocybin is a serotonin receptor agonist with highest affinity for the **5-HT₂A receptor** (Ki ≈ 6 nM). It also binds to 5-HT₂C, 5-HT₁A, and 5-HT₂B receptors with lower affinity. The psychedelic effects are primarily mediated through 5-HT₂A receptor activation in the prefrontal cortex, particularly in layer V pyramidal neurons.

### Pharmacokinetics
After oral administration, psilocybin is rapidly absorbed (Tmax ≈ 40 minutes) and dephosphorylated by alkaline phosphatase enzymes in the intestinal wall, liver, and blood to form psilocin. Psilocin is further metabolized by monoamine oxidase (MAO) to 4-hydroxyindoleacetic acid (4-HIAA). The elimination half-life of psilocin is approximately 2–3 hours.

## Clinical Research

### Treatment-Resistant Depression
A 2021 randomized controlled trial (COMPASS Pathways, Phase IIb) evaluated psilocybin (25 mg, 10 mg, 1 mg) in 233 patients with treatment-resistant depression. The 25 mg group showed rapid and sustained improvement in depressive symptoms measured by MADRS, with 37% in remission at week 3.

### End-of-Life Anxiety
A 2016 double-blind crossover trial (Ross et al., NYU) in patients with life-threatening cancer diagnoses found that a single dose of psilocybin (0.3 mg/kg) produced significant reductions in depression, anxiety, and death-related distress, with effects sustained at 6-month and 4.5-year follow-ups (Agin-Lieber et al., 2022).

### Substance Use Disorders
- **Tobacco:** A 2014 open-label pilot study (Johnson et al., Johns Hopkins) found 80% abstinence at 6-month follow-up from smoking after 2–3 psilocybin sessions combined with CBT.
- **Alcohol:** A 2022 randomized controlled trial (Bogenschutz et al., NYU) demonstrated significant reduction in heavy drinking days compared to placebo (44% vs. 28% reduction, p=0.02).

### Cluster Headaches
Multiple observational studies and patient surveys report efficacy of psilocybin and other serotonergic psychedelics in interrupting cluster headache cycles and extending remission periods. Mechanism likely involves serotonergic modulation of the hypothalamic trigeminovascular system.

## Natural Occurrence

Psilocybin is biosynthesized via the **tryptamine pathway** in fungal mycelium and fruiting bodies. The pathway involves:

1. **L-Tryptophan** → Tryptamine (via decarboxylase)
2. **Tryptamine** → N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (via methyltransferase)
3. **DMT** → 4-Hydroxy-DMT / Psilocin (via hydroxylase)
4. **Psilocin** → Psilocybin (via kinase)

### Major Psilocybin-Containing Genera
- *Psilocybe* (~150+ species) — most significant genus
- *Panaeolus* — *P. cyanescens* (Blue Meanies)
- *Gymnopilus* — *G. spectabilis* (Big Laughing Gym)
- *Inocybe* — several species
- *Pluteus* — *P. salicinus*
- *Copelandia* — sometimes classified within *Panaeolus*

## Dosage Guide

| Dose Range (dried *Psilocybe cubensis*) | Effect Level |
|-----------------------------------------|-------------|
| 0.25–0.5 g | Microdose (sub-perceptual) |
| 0.5–1.0 g | Mini-dose (light enhancement) |
| 1.0–2.5 g | Low psychedelic (light visual effects) |
| 2.5–5.0 g | Moderate psychedelic (classic experience) |
| 5.0+ g | High psychedelic (ego dissolution, mystical) |

Dried *P. cubensis* typically contains 0.5–1.0% psilocybin/psilocin by dry weight. Potency varies significantly between species, batches, and growing conditions.

## Legal Status

- **United States:** Schedule I under the Controlled Substances Act (DEA). However, Oregon (Measure 109, 2020) legalized psilocybin-assisted therapy; Colorado (Proposition 122, 2022) decriminalized and created a regulated access framework. Several cities have deprioritized enforcement.
- **Canada:** Controlled substance (Schedule III). Authorized clinical trials; special access program for therapy.
- **Netherlands:** Fresh psilocybin mushrooms (truffles) legal; dried mushrooms banned since 2008.
- **Brazil:** Not specifically listed; legal gray area; use by indigenous groups is protected.
- **Jamaica:** Legal; retreat centers operate openly.
- **Australia:** Schedule 9 (prohibited) as of 2026. TGA approved psilocybin for controlled use in treatment-resistant depression (2023).
- **Portugal:** Decriminalized (personal possession, not commercial).

## Harm Reduction

Key harm reduction principles for psilocybin use:

- **Set and Setting:** Mental state (set) and physical/social environment (setting) are the strongest predictors of experience quality
- **Screening:** Contraindicated with personal/family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder
- **Trip Sitting:** Experienced, sober guide recommended for full-dose experiences
- **Substance Interactions:** Dangerous interactions with MAOIs, lithium, tramadol, and stimulants; potentiates SSRIs unpredictably
- **Hydration:** Maintain water intake during the experience
- **Integration:** Post-experience processing and integration significantly improves therapeutic outcomes
