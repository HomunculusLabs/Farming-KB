# 2C-T-4 — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propylthiophenethylamine

PIHKAL entry #41. A member of the 2C-T family of sulfur-substituted phenethylamines, 2C-T-4 carries an isopropylthio group at the 4-position of the classic 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine backbone.

## Chemical Identity

- Full name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-isopropylthiophenethylamine
- PIHKAL number: #41
- HCl salt: white crystals
- Intermediate benzaldehyde mp: 89-90 °C (pale yellow solids, occasionally pale lime-green)
- Nitrostyrene intermediate: lustrous golden orange platelets
- Free base: distilled at 140-145 °C at 0.2 mm/Hg
- Sulfide intermediate has a light, pleasant smell of apples

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: 8-20 mg (estimated full effects as low as 4 mg)
- Duration: 12-18 h
- Onset: gradual, 1-3 hours to reach full effects
- Offset: very gradual and gentle decline matching the slow onset

## Synthesis

The synthesis follows the standard [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]] from 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol:

1. **Sulfide formation**: 2,5-Dimethoxythiophenol + isopropyliodide + KOH in EtOH, heated overnight. Product distilled at 100-110 °C at 0.2 mm/Hg to give 6.9 g pale yellow oil with characteristic apple fragrance
2. **Vilsmeier-Haack formylation**: POCl3 + N-methylformanilide, stirred 1 h at room temperature, then aldehyde added (exothermic, immediate reddening). Heated 30 min on steam bath, quenched in warm water. Product: pale yellow solids (2.35 g), mp 89-90 °C
3. **Nitrostyrene condensation**: Benzaldehyde + nitromethane + NH4OAc, heated 2 h on steam bath. Recrystallized from MeOH to give 6.2 g lustrous golden orange platelets
4. **LAH reduction**: Nitrostyrene reduced with LAH/H2SO4 in THF, stirred overnight at room temperature. Standard workup, distillation at 140-145 °C at 0.2 mm/Hg, converted to HCl salt (2.40 g white crystals)

## Effects and Qualitative Comments

At 8 mg, visual effects set in at about two hours with much color enhancement (particularly green) and flowing colors. Uncomfortable physical sensation with lower legs and hands tending to fall asleep. Not considered a favorite material due to excessive duration. The subject suggested 4-5 mg might reduce physical discomfort while preserving pleasant experience.

At 9 mg, a sense of letting go and flowing was prominent. Growing euphoria, feeling of clearing out body residues, and handling of impressive insights. Thinking grew in clarity with crystal clear visual perception. Peace and centeredness lasted the rest of the evening. Sleep was difficult with continued chemical push.

At 14 mg, described as very rational, benign, and good-humored. Insight and calm common to the 2C-T's present with less of the body-energy push that makes 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2 difficult for some. No particular visuals but excellent eyes-closed imagery. Music was marvelous and body comfortable throughout.

At 14 mg (second report), persistent cold feet and uncertain stomach. Brilliant color trails reminiscent of 2c-b-compound-profile|2C-B, but with dark corners noted — potentially bringing out the worst in social situations.

At 19 mg, deep emotional engagement with music was the dominant theme. Profound introspection about the passage of time and personal growth from the 1960s to the 1980s.

At 22 mg, slow three-hour transition. Initial difficulty with illogical aspects of human experience while reading. Later settling into positive feelings with no self-rejecting aspects. Sleep excellent but next day slow with need for napping.

## Structure-Activity Relationships

2C-T-4 is one of the most variable members of the 2C-T series. Some observers report overwhelming color and visual activity; others note its absence entirely. This variability mirrors the erratic responses seen in the aleph-1-entity|ALEPH family, of which 2C-T-4 is a two-carbon homologue. The isopropylthio group introduces branching at the beta-carbon of the thioether chain, which likely contributes to the wide inter-individual variation in response.

Compared to 2c-t-21-entity|2C-T-21 (fluoroethylthio, 8-12 mg, 7-10 h), 2C-T-4 requires higher doses but produces much longer experiences. The isopropylthio group may slow metabolic clearance compared to smaller thioether chains.

The unusually wide latitude of effective dosages — from ~4 mg to over 20 mg without loss of control — distinguishes 2C-T-4 from most other psychedelics. This same wide dosage range was also observed with the corresponding amphetamine homologue (ALEPH-4).

## Extensions and Commentary

The ethoxy homologue (2CT4-2ETO; 2-ethoxy-5-methoxy-4-isopropylthiophenethylamine) was also explored. Benzaldehyde mp 43-44 °C, nitrostyrene mp 77-79 °C, final HCl mp 153.5-154 °C. Trials at 5 mg increments within the 10-25 mg range produced a consistent gentle plus-two lasting approximately 10 hours. A code name of "tenderness" was assigned due to peaceful meditative inner receptiveness and clarity.

Shulgin noted that 2C-T-4 should not be confused with "T-4" (cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine, a potent explosive known as RDX) or thyroxine (a thyroid hormone). The drug is neither an explosive nor an amino acid.

## Cautionary Notes

Very long duration (12-18 hours) demands careful time planning. Physical discomfort (extremity numbness) reported at lower doses. Individual responses vary considerably — some subjects report dark corners while others find it entirely benign. Sleep disruption is common due to extended chemical push. The next-day lethargy at 22 mg suggests that doses above 20 mg may require a full day of recovery.

The name confusion with T-4 (the explosive RDX/cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine) and thyroxine should be noted — while the compound bears no relationship to either, the shared abbreviation could cause misunderstandings in certain contexts.
