# 2C-T-21 — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(2-fluoroethylthio)phenethylamine

[[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #49. The last of the 2C-T series to be completed, 2C-T-21 carries a 2-fluoroethylthio group at the 4-position and is among the most potent phenethylamines known.

## Chemical Identity

- Full name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(2-fluoroethylthio)phenethylamine
- PIHKAL number: #49
- HCl salt: glistening white crystals, 4.07 g final weight
- Benzaldehyde intermediate: pale ivory crystals
- Nitrostyrene intermediate: bright orange crystals
- Free base: distilled at 145-160 C at 0.4 mm/Hg, white oil
- Fluorine-18 isotope (half-life ~2 h) makes it potentially useful for PET imaging studies

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: 8-12 mg
- Duration: 7-10 h
- Onset: effects noticed within 5-15 minutes; gradual build over ~2 hours
- Plateau: approximately 1 hour at peak
- Aftereffects: after-images intense in the evening; eyes tired the next day; slight introversion noted the following day
