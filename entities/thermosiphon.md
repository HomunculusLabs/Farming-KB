## Description

A thermosiphon is a passive heat-transfer mechanism that uses natural convection to circulate a fluid (liquid or gas) without any mechanical pump. Heated fluid becomes less dense and rises through a closed loop, while cooler, denser fluid descends, creating a self-sustaining circulation driven entirely by temperature differential and gravity.
