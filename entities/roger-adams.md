# Roger Adams

## Description

Roger Adams (1889–1971) was an American organic chemist whose pioneering research on cannabis constituents in the 1930s and 1940s laid the groundwork for understanding cannabinoid chemistry. His work on the [[challenges-neurotropic-fungi-chemical-analysis-taxonomic-identification]] of cannabis oils. Adams developed the potency scale that bears his name, used to rate the relative strength of cannabis extracts and [[pericyclic-reactions-organic-chemistry]]
- **Institution**: University of Illinois
- **Era**: Early-to-mid 20th century
- **Specialty**: Cannabinoid chemistry, organic synthesis

## Key Facts

- Conducted groundbreaking research on cannabis chemistry in **1940**, published on page 198 of volume 62 of the *Journal of the American Chemical Society* (JACS).
- Performed **[[thc-acetate]] rates 14.6 (twice as potent).
- His work demonstrated that cannabis oil could be refined through vacuum distillation to yield products up to twice as strong as the crude ether-phase extract.
- Showed that totally pure THC (a thin transparent oil) could theoretically be produced by chemically isolating pure cannabidiol and then isomerizing it to THC.
