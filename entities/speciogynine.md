## Overview

**Speciogynine** is a corynanthean oxindole alkaloid found in the leaves of
*[[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]] at C-15. The stereochemistry at C-20
differs from its epimer speciociliatine, which accounts for distinct
pharmacological behavior.

The oxindole configuration results from oxidative rearrangement of the
corresponding indole precursor. This structural motif is shared across several
kratom alkaloids including paynantheine and speciociliatine.

## Pharmacology

Speciogynine exhibits weak partial agonist activity at mu-opioid receptors,
though its potency is significantly lower than that of mitragynine.
Pharmacological studies suggest it contributes to the overall opioidergic
activity of kratom but is not a primary driver of the plant's analgesic
effects.

## Related Compounds

Speciogynine is one of several oxindole alkaloids in kratom. Its close
structural relatives include **speciociliatine** (C-20 epimer), **paynantheine**
(methyl ester variant), and the corresponding indole alkaloid **mitragynine**.

The corynanthean skeleton it shares with other kratom alkaloids is also found
in **ajmalicine** and **rhynchophylline** from *Uncaria* species, both of
which have documented activity at adrenergic and serotonergic receptors.
