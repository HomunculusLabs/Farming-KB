# Cajanus cajan (*Pigeon Pea, Arhar, Toor Dal*)

## Overview

Cajanus cajan is a perennial leguminous shrub of the family Fabaceae,
widely cultivated across tropical and subtropical regions as a food
crop, forage, [[coleman-mobile-greenhouse-green-manure-soil-building]], and nitrogen-fixing companion plant. It is
the sixth most important pulse crop globally by production area, serving
as a primary protein source for millions of people in South Asia and
Sub-Saharan Africa. Its deep taproot system, [[dighton-fungal-drought-tolerance-plant-water-relations]], and
multi-use profile make it a keystone species in [[comparison-arid-vs-tropical-permaculture]]
and agroforestry systems.

## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Plantae → **Order:** Fabales → **Family:** Fabaceae
- **Subfamily:** Faboideae → **Genus:** Cajanus → **Species:** C. cajan
  (L.) Millsp.
- **Synonyms:** C. indicus Spreng., Cajanus flavus DC.
- **Common names:** Pigeon pea (English), Arhar/Toor (Hindi), Dal
  (generic), Guandu (Chinese), Pois d'Angole (French), Gandul
  (Spanish)
- **Wild progenitor:** C. cajanifolius, native to peninsular India

The genus Cajanus contains ~32 species, mostly wild relatives native to
the Indian subcontinent and Australia. C. cajan was domesticated in India
at least 3,500 years ago.

## Morphology

### Vegetative Characteristics

- **Growth habit:** Perennial shrub (3–5 year lifespan) grown as annual
  or short-term perennial; height 1–4 m depending on variety
- **Stems:** Erect to semi-erect, woody at base, 3–10 cm diameter;
  green when young, grey-brown at maturity
- **Leaves:** Pinnately trifoliate, leaflets 5–10 cm long, oblong,
  dark green, pubescent beneath
- **Root system:** Deep taproot to 2 m, extensive lateral roots;
  well-nodulated by Rhizobium (Cicer) biovars
- **Biomass:** Dense canopy; annual shoot production 5–15 t/ha

### Reproductive Structures

- **Flowers:** Papilionaceous, 1.5–2 cm, yellow to orange-red with
  red/purple streaking; racemes of 5–10 flowers; predominantly
  self-pollinating with 15–25% outcrossing via bees
- **Pods:** Linear, 5–10 cm, pubescent, green turning brown; 2–9 seeds
- **Seeds:** Globular, 5–8 mm; white to mottled brown/red; 100-seed
  weight: 8–20 g

## Distribution and Cultivation

Grown across 50+ countries in the tropics and subtropics:

- **South Asia:** India (90% of global area), Nepal, Bangladesh,
  Myanmar, Sri Lanka
- **Africa:** Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Malawi, Mozambique
- **Caribbean and Americas:** Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, parts
  of Brazil and [[guzman-allen-gartz-neurotropic-fungi-central-america-caribbean]]
- **Australia:** Northern Territory and Queensland
