## Description

Kelpmeal is a dried, ground seaweed product used as an organic fertilizer and soil amendment. It is remarkably rich in trace elements and plant growth regulators, providing nutrients that few other fertilizer sources can match. Kelpmeal is a key optional ingredient in Steve Solomon's Complete Organic Fertilizer (COF) formula, added at 1/2 part by volume.

## Classification

- **Type**: Organic fertilizer / Soil amendment
- **Source**: Dried and ground seaweed (kelp)
- **Primary nutrients**: Broad spectrum of trace minerals, natural growth regulators, and hormones
- **Form**: Dry meal

## Key Facts

- Kelpmeal contains an extraordinary range of trace elements unavailable from most other organic or chemical fertilizer sources
- It includes natural plant growth regulators and hormones that boost plant fortitude
- Kelpmeal enhances plants resistance to cold, frost, and other environmental stresses
- A 55-pound (25 kg) sack may seem shockingly expensive, but it is cost-effective for small garden use
- Imported kelpmeal from Korea can be less expensive than domestic product due to lower labor costs and favorable exchange rates
- Application rate in COF: 1/2 part by volume
- Can be used as a foliar spray by dissolving in water and spraying directly onto plant leaves
- Foliar feeding with kelp forces the gardener to walk among the plants regularly, providing an opportunity to observe crop health

## Relevance to Cultivation

- Kelpmeal addresses the widespread decline in trace mineral content of agricultural soils documented by USDA data over the past several decades
- Research published in Life Extension Magazine showed that the nutritional content of vegetables has declined approximately 25% over the last 25+ years
- Adding kelpmeal to the garden helps restore the full spectrum of trace minerals that plants need and that humans need from plants
- Trace minerals from kelpmeal contribute to more nutritious produce, not just higher yields
- The foliar application method (dissolving kelp in water and spraying on leaves) is an alternative to mixing dry meal into soil
- Kelpmeal is considered one of the few fertilizer inputs that supplies things nothing else will supply

## Application Methods

1. **Mixed into COF**: 1/2 part by volume, blended with other COF ingredients
2. **Foliar spray**: Dissolved in water and sprayed directly onto plant leaves
3. **Direct soil application**: Sprinkled onto garden beds and worked in

## Substitutes

- Basalt dust (rock dust) can substitute for kelpmeal at 1 part by volume (double the kelpmeal rate)
- However, kelpmeal is preferred because it provides growth regulators and hormones not found in rock dust

## Nutrient Profile

Kelp meal contains over 60 trace minerals, natural growth hormones
(cytokinins, auxins, gibberellins), amino acids, and vitamins.
Its NPK ratio is approximately 1-0.5-2 with significant micronutrient
content including iron, zinc, and manganese.

## Application Methods

Apply kelp meal at 0.5-1 kg per 10 square meters as a soil amendment.
As a foliar spray, steep 50g in 5L of water for 24-48 hours and
apply directly to plant leaves.

## Benefits for Plant Health

Regular kelp meal applications improve plant stress resistance,
enhance root development, and increase frost tolerance. The natural
plant hormones stimulate cell division and nutrient uptake.
