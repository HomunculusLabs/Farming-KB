## Cultivation Notes

- Propagate by seed or division of established clumps
- Plant in full sun with well-drained soil
- Blanch stems 3–4 weeks before harvest by wrapping in burlap or cardboard
- Cut back flower stalks if not harvesting buds, to maintain leaf production
- Protect from severe frost in marginal climates with mulch
