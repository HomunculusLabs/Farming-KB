## Description

Coprameal (also called coconut meal or copra meal) is the protein-rich byproduct remaining after coconut oil is extracted from dried coconut meat (copra). It is a less potent but inherently organic seedmeal option for use in Steve Solomon's Complete Organic Fertilizer (COF) formula. Coprameal is approximately one-third weaker in NPK content compared to other seedmeals, but has the unique advantage of being guaranteed free from chemical fertilizer contamination.

## Classification

- **Type**: Organic fertilizer / Nitrogen source
- **Source**: Coconut oil extraction byproduct
- **Primary nutrients**: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K)
- **Relative strength**: ~1/3 weaker NPK than standard seedmeals
- **Form**: Dry meal

## Key Facts

- Coprameal is the residue after coconut oil is pressed from copra (dried coconut kernel)
- It is the least potent common seedmeal, approximately one-third weaker in NPK than canola, soy, or cottonseed meals
- Coprameal's unique advantage: coconuts are never grown with chemical fertilizers, so coprameal is inherently and guaranteed organic
- In the COF formula, coprameal is used at 4 parts by volume, supplemented with 1/2 part tankage to boost nitrogen content
- When using coprameal as the COF base, Solomon advises being on the side of generous with application rates for medium- and high-demand vegetables
- Most seedmeals analyze at approximately 6-4-2 NPK; coprameal is proportionally lower

## Relevance to Cultivation

- Coprameal offers a truly organic nitrogen source for gardeners who prioritize guaranteed chemical-free inputs
- Its lower potency is easily compensated by slightly higher application rates or by supplementing with tankage
- Being a byproduct of coconut processing, coprameal is widely available in tropical and subtropical regions
- Like other seedmeals, coprameal releases nutrients slowly through microbial decomposition, supporting healthy soil ecology
- When used as the base for COF, the gardener should apply more generously than with standard seedmeals, especially for demanding crops

## Comparison with Other Seedmeals

| Seedmeal Type | Relative NPK | Organic Guarantee | Cost |

## Application Rates

Standard application rate for coprameal is 1-2 kg per 10 square
meters of garden bed. It works best when incorporated into soil
2-4 weeks before planting to allow initial decomposition.

## Advantages Over Synthetic Nitrogen

Coprameal releases nitrogen slowly over 2-4 months, reducing
leaching risk compared to synthetic alternatives. It also contributes
organic matter and stimulates beneficial soil microbial activity.

## Compatibility with Other Amendments

Coprameal pairs well with phosphorus sources like bone meal and
potassium sources like kelp meal to create a complete organic
fertilizer blend.

## Storage and Handling

Store coprameal in a cool, dry location in sealed containers to
prevent moisture absorption and pest infestation. Properly stored
coprameal maintains nutrient value for 6-12 months. The mild
coconut aroma dissipates after soil incorporation.
