# MDPH (a,a-Dimethyl-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine; 3,4-Methylenedioxyphentermine)

MDPH is a substituted phenethylamine compound and the alpha,alpha-dimethyl analogue of mdpea. It is a member of the phentermine family with a methylenedioxy ring. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #116.

## Chemical Classification

MDPH belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named a,a-dimethyl-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine, also known as 3,4-methylenedioxyphentermine.

MDPH is the alpha,alpha-dimethyl homologue of mdpea (homopiperonylamine).

It contains two methyl groups on the alpha-carbon (the carbon adjacent to the aromatic ring), making it a phentermine derivative.

The methylenedioxy substitution pattern at the 3,4-positions is shared with [[mda]], [[mdma-entity]], and related compounds.

## Pharmacological Profile

MDPH is an active psychoactive compound with a dosage range of 160 to 240 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 3 to 5 hours.

The compound produces effects described as similar to but distinct from [[mda]], with a shorter duration and less intensity.

The typical experience is a pleasant stoning effect without dramatic psychedelic features.

## Qualitative Effects

At 120 mg, the alert was felt in 40 minutes and the subject was substantially affected at 1 hour 20 minutes.

The experience was described as quite like [[mda]]: simple, with no lines, no colors, no motion, no fantasy.

Subjects reported being pleasantly stoned, with real anorexia and impotency.

The drop from the 4th to 6th hour was softened by wine, which proved to be extremely intoxicating.

Speech became slurred with amnesia for aggressive and uninhibited behavior, suggesting drug-alcohol interaction.

Dream patterns were disturbingly unreal.

At 160 mg, a very quiet development occurred with no body load and no visuals.

The effects faded quickly. The compound was suggested as a possible promoter, like [[mdpr-entity]].

Subjects felt refreshed and relaxed on the following morning.

At 200 mg, the compound had an inordinately foul taste and produced slight queasiness.

Short daydreams were quickly forgotten.

Physical problems included eye mismanagement, teeth clenching, and a tendency to sweat.

Sleep was possible at only five hours into the experience.

The experience was described as a short-lived plus two that was not as rewarding as [[mda]] and had none of the magic of [[mdma-entity]].

## The Logic of Drug Design

The commentary on MDPH includes an extended discussion of the logical (or illogical) processes that lead from a known active compound to an unknown one that may or may not be active.

The reasoning that led to MDPH is presented as an example of invalid syllogistic reasoning in drug design.

The premises were: some stimulants when given a methylenedioxy ring are MDMA-like; some ring-unsubstituted 1,1-dimethylphenylethylamines are stimulants.

The conclusion: some ring-unsubstituted 1,1-dimethylphenylethylamines when given a methylenedioxy ring are MDMA-like.

This form of reasoning is not one of the 24 valid syllogistic moods, illustrating how drug design often proceeds by intuition rather than formal logic.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with 3,4-methylenedioxybenzyl chloride reacted with the anion of isobutyric acid.

The resulting acid is subjected to a Curtius rearrangement through an azide intermediate.

The isocyanate is trapped with benzyl alcohol to form a carbamate protected amine.

Hydrogenolysis removes the benzyl protecting group to yield MDPH as the hydrochloride salt with a melting point of 180-181 degrees C.

The alpha,alpha-dimethyl group appears as a sharp singlet at 1.30 ppm in the NMR spectrum.

The same carbamate intermediate can be reduced to the methylamine analogue (mdmp).

## Relationship to Phentermine

Phentermine (alpha,alpha-dimethylphenethylamine) is a well-known appetite suppressant and stimulant.

MDPH is the 3,4-methylenedioxy analogue of phentermine.

The addition of the methylenedioxy ring converts the simple stimulant into a compound with both stimulant and mild psychedelic properties.

The alpha,alpha-dimethyl groups protect the compound from oxidative deamination by monoamine oxidase, contributing to its central activity.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 160 - 240 mg.

Duration: 3 - 5 h.

## Safety Considerations

The compound showed some physical side effects including teeth clenching, sweating, and eye mismanagement.

Alcohol interaction appeared significant, with wine producing unexpectedly strong intoxication when combined with MDPH.

The compound was described as having hints of physical problems at higher doses.
