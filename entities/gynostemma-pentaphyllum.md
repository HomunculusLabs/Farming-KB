# Jiaogulan (Gynostemma pentaphyllum)

## Overview
- **Scientific name:** Gynostemma pentaphyllum (Thunb.) Makino
- **Family:** Cucurbitaceae | **Order:** Cucurbitales
- **Designation:** "Southern ginseng" — gypenosides structurally overlap with ginsenosides
- **Primary use:** Adaptogenic tonic; broad-spectrum medicinal herb
- **Key feature:** Broadest range of saponins (gypenosides 1–82) of any known plant
- **Forms:** Dried tea, capsules, tinctures, standardized extracts (≥10% gypenosides)

## Description
- **Habit:** Dioecious climbing vine (liana); perennial subtropical, annual temperate
- **Stem:** Slender, branching, 2–8 m; tendrils at leaf axils
- **Leaves:** Palmately compound, 5 leaflets (4–10 cm × 2–4 cm), serrate margins
- **Flowers/Fruit:** Small greenish-white dioecious flowers; globose berry ~6 mm
- **Roots:** Fibrous, shallow; **Habitat:** Forest margins, thickets, 300–3200 m a.s.l.
- **Native range:** Southern China, N. Vietnam, Korea, Japan | **Hardiness:** USDA 7–10

## Phytochemistry
### Gypenosides (Dammarane-Type Triterpenoid Saponins)
- **82 identified gypenosides** (GP I–GP LXXXII)
- GP III, IV, VIII, XII identical to ginsenosides Rb₁, Rb₂, Rd, F₂
- Core aglycones: protopanaxadiol and protopanaxatriol types

## Pharmacology
### Adaptogenic Mechanisms
- Modulates HPA axis; normalizes cortisol under chronic stress; upregulates HSP70

### Nitric Oxide / Cardiovascular Pathway
- Stimulates eNOS → ↑ NO → vasodilation → ↓ BP
- Inhibits platelet aggregation; ↓ LDL-C 12–15%, ↓ triglycerides
- Anti-atherosclerotic via AMPK/NF-κB signaling

### Antioxidant Activity
- Scavenges O₂⁻, ·OH, DPPH radicals; activates Nrf2/ARE; upregulates SOD, CAT, GPx

### Additional Actions
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Inhibits COX-2, iNOS, TNF-α, IL-6 via NF-κB
- **Anti-diabetic:** ↑ Insulin sensitivity; AMPK activation in skeletal muscle
- **Neuroprotective:** Reduces Aβ aggregation; **Antitumor:** Induces apoptosis, inhibits angiogenesis

## Modern Clinical Research
- **Cardiovascular:** Meta-analysis: ↓ SBP −5.3 mmHg, ↓ DBP −3.5 mmHg; improved FMD
- **Diabetes:** 12-week trial: ↓ fasting glucose and HbA1c in T2DM patients
- **Athletic performance:** ↑ VO₂max, ↓ lactate accumulation; PGC-1α mitochondrial biogenesis
- **Immune modulation:** ↑ NK cell activity, macrophage phagocytosis; T-cell balancing
- **Hepatoprotective:** ↓ ALT/AST; anti-fibrotic via SIRT1/AMPK in stellate cells

## Cultivation and Harvest
| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Propagation | Seed (stratification) or stem cuttings |
| Soil/Light | Loamy, well-drained, pH 6.0–7.5; partial shade (40–60%) best |
| Temperature | Optimal 20–30 °C; ceases below 10 °C |
| Harvest | First cut 90–120 days; repeat every 30–40 days |
| Method | Top 15–20 cm of vine; shade-dry at <40 °C |

## Dosage and Preparation
- **Tea:** 2–4 g dried leaf in 250 mL hot water, 2–3×/day
- **Capsule (extract):** 250–500 mg standardized (≥10% gypenosides), 1–2×/day
- **Tincture (1:5):** 2–4 mL, 2–3×/day | **Powder:** 1–3 g/day
- Best taken morning/afternoon with food (fat-soluble saponins)

## Safety and Contraindications
| Concern | Status |
|---------|--------|
| General | GRAS; well-tolerated at standard doses |
| Pregnancy/lactation | Contraindicated — insufficient data |
| Antihypertensives | Additive BP-lowering — monitor |
| Antidiabetic drugs | Additive hypoglycemia — monitor glucose |
| Bleeding disorders | Mild anticoagulant — use caution |
| High dose (>15 g/day) | Rare: nausea, diarrhea, dizziness |
| Drug interactions | CYP450 modulation in vitro; clinical significance uncertain |
