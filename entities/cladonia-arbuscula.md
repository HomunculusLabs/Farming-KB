## Overview

Cladonia arbuscula is a fruticose lichen in the family Cladoniaceae, belonging to the reindeer lichen complex.

It forms dense mats of intricately branched, greyish-green to brownish podetia that carpet the ground in boreal and subarctic forest floors.

The species is ecologically significant as a major food source for caribou and reindeer across its circumpolar range.

It is closely related to [[antifungal-secondary-metabolites-coprophilous-fungi]].

Some chemotypes of C. arbuscula also contain protocetraric acid and tenuiorin as minor constituents.

The species epithet "arbuscula" derives from the Latin for "small tree," referencing its shrub-like branching habit.

C. arbuscula is distinguished from the similar C. rangiferina by its denser branching, lack of a hollow medulla, and consistently cup-less podetia.
