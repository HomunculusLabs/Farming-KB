# 4-MA

**PIHKAL Entry #97** -- PMA; 4-Methoxyamphetamine

## Chemical Name

4-Methoxyamphetamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 50 - 80 mg

- **Duration:** short

## Qualitative Comments

(with 60 mg) Sudden blood pressure rise at just over an hour (systolic up 55 mm)
maintained for another hour. Effects reminiscent of DET with distinct
after-images and some parasthesia. No residue by early evening (after 5 hours).

(with 70 mg) Hits suddenly with "a feeling of druggedness, almost an
alcohol-like intoxication." Never really high in the psychedelic sense.

## Synthesis

Synthesized from anisaldehyde via nitropropene formation with nitroethane in
benzene using a Dean-Stark trap, followed by LAH reduction. The nitropropene had
a mp of 45-46 degrees C. The hydrochloride salt had a mp of 208-209 degrees C.
The metabolite 4-hydroxyamphetamine (4-HA) was also prepared by heating 4-MA in
concentrated HCl.

## Extensions and Commentary

4-MA is one of the "essential amphetamines" because the 4-methoxy group appears
in two important essential oils: allylbenzene (estragole) and its propenyl
isomer (anethole). The methyl group is allyl in estragole and propenyl in
anethole, and both are found in anise, fennel, and related plants.

A major metabolite of amphetamine is 4-hydroxyamphetamine. The question was
whether chronic amphetamine users might be methylating endogenous 4-
hydroxyamphetamine to produce 4-MA, potentially promoting psychosis. Studies
showed 4-MA produced excitation, adrenergic pressor effects, and was excreted
unchanged in urine, but did not produce amphetamine-like psychosis. Since
administration of up to 600 mg amphetamine produced no detectable 4-MA in urine,
this theory was not valid.

**Social tragedy:** 4-MA was widely distributed in the US (as sulfate) and
Canada (as hydrochloride), promoted as being [[mda-entity]]. Several deaths
occurred following overdose, sold under names "Chicken Power" and "Chicken
Yellow." The ingested quantities were reportedly in the hundreds of milligrams.
The ethoxy homologue 4-EA also appeared on Canadian streets and was promptly
illegalized.

The N-methyl homologue (DOONE) was studied as a separate entry. 4-MA stands as
a cautionary tale about the dangers of selling misidentified or misrepresented
substances.
