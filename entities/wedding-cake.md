The strain won first place in the Hybrid category at the 2018 High Times Cannabis Cup in Santa Rosa, California.

It has become one of the most commercially successful and widely available strains in [[cannabis-terpene-profile]], and potent relaxing effects.

## Lineage and Genetics

**Parent cross:** *[[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], while the [[girl-scout-cookies]] lineage provides sweet, vanilla-tinged terpenes.

Animal Mints adds additional minty and earthy undertones that distinguish Wedding Cake from its GSC parent alone.

The strain is a descendant of the legendary OG Kush and [[durban-poison]] lineages through multiple generational crosses.

## Appearance and Aroma

**Bud structure:** Dense, medium-to-large calyxes with tight internodal spacing, coated in a thick layer of milky-white trichomes.

Colors range from deep forest green to lavender and purple hues, particularly when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering.

**Pistils:** Long, orange to amber stigma hairs woven densely throughout the resinous buds.

**Aroma:** Dominated by sweet vanilla and tangy earthiness on the initial impression, followed by layers of peppery spice and sour cream.

Breaking apart the buds releases a more complex profile with diesel undertones from the [[triangle-kush]] lineage.

**Flavor:** Creamy vanilla [[cake-batter]] dominates the inhale, with a peppery, slightly sour exhale that lingers on the palate.

The flavor is notably smoother than many high-THC strains, contributing to its broad consumer appeal.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- **Limonene** (0.5–1.2%): Citrus notes, mood elevation, stress relief.
- **Caryophyllene** (0.4–1.0%): Peppery spice, anti-inflammatory, binds CB2 receptors.
- **Myrcene** (0.3–0.8%): Earthy, sedating, potentiates THC effects.

**Secondary terpenes:** Linalool, humulene, bisabolol, and nerolidol are commonly detected at lower concentrations.

**Cannabinoid profile:**

- **THC:** 25–30% (some phenotypes test above 30%).
- **CBD:** < 0.1% (negligible).
- **[[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]] profile.

## Effects and Medical Applications

**Onset:** Rapid, typically felt within 2–5 minutes of inhalation, with cerebral effects preceding the body high by several minutes.

**Effects progression:** Initial euphoric and mentally relaxing onset transitions into a deep, full-body physical sedation within 30–60 minutes.

The cerebral component is mildly uplifting but not racy or anxious, even at higher doses, due to the indica dominance.

**Duration:** 2–3 hours for most users, with residual sedation lasting an additional 1–2 hours.

**Medical applications:**

- [[cannabis-sleep-disorders-and-insomnia]], particularly when consumed in the evening.
