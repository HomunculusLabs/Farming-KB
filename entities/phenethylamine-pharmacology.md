# Phenethylamine Class Pharmacology — Overview

The phenethylamine class constitutes one of the largest and most pharmacologically diverse families of psychoactive compounds. The foundational structure is a phenyl ring connected to an ethylamine chain (C6H5-CH2-CH2-NH2), with substitutions at virtually every position producing compounds with dramatically different pharmacological profiles.

## Core Structural Features

The basic phenethylamine skeleton supports five major regions of modification:

1. **Ring substitution** (positions 2, 3, 4, 5, 6): The most pharmacologically impactful region. The 2,5-dimethoxy pattern is consistently the most active for psychedelic effects. The 3,4,5-trimethoxy pattern (as in mescaline) is active but less potent. The 3,4-methylenedioxy pattern produces entactogenic effects (as in MDMA).

2. **Alpha carbon** (adjacent to the amine): Addition of a methyl group converts a phenethylamine to an amphetamine, generally increasing potency and duration. See [[tma-entity]] vs [[mescaline-entity]].

3. **Nitrogen substitution**: N-methylation ([[mdma-entity]]), N-ethylation ([[mdea-entity]]), and N,N-dimethylation each produce distinct pharmacological profiles. The nitrogen substituent profoundly affects whether the compound is psychedelic, entactogenic, or stimulant.

4. **Beta carbon** (middle of the chain): Hydroxylation converts the compound to a catecholamine (e.g., norepinephrine, dopamine). This generally eliminates psychoactive effects but produces endogenous neurotransmitter activity.

5. **Side chain extension**: Homologation (adding carbons) generally reduces psychedelic potency, though exceptions exist.

## Major Subclasses

### Classic Psychedelics (2C Series)

The 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamines (2C family) represent the most thoroughly explored psychedelic phenethylamines. The 4-position substituent determines much of the character:

- Halogens: [[2c-b-entity]] (4-bromo),  (4-iodo) — well-rounded psychedelics
- Short alkyls: [[2c-d-entity]] (4-methyl) — gentle, intellectual
- Longer alkyls:  (4-propyl) — potent, long-lasting
- Sulfur groups: [[2c-t-2-entity]], [[2c-t-4-entity]], [[2c-t-7-entity]], [[2c-t-21-entity]] — the 2C-T series, generally more insight-oriented
- Nitro: [[2c-n-entity]] — yellow compound, consistently underwhelming
- Alkoxy: [[2c-o-4-entity]] — oxygen analogues of 2C-T series, generally less active

### Amphetamine Series (DO, TMA, Aleph families)

Three-carbon chain compounds (amphetamines) are generally more potent and longer-lasting:

- [[tma-entity]] (3,4,5-TMA) — the first synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine
- [[tma-2-compound-profile]] (2,4,5-TMA) — ten times more potent than TMA, a landmark discovery
- [[dob-entity]], [[doi-entity]], [[phloem-sap-composition-and-long-distance-signaling-in-plants]]
- [[aleph-1-entity]] — the Aleph series (thio-substituted amphetamines)

### Entactogens (MDA/MDMA Family)

- [[mda]] — the parent, both psychedelic and stimulant
- [[mdma-entity]] — N-methyl-MDA, the prototypical entactogen
- [[mdea-entity]] (MDE) — N-ethyl-MDA, similar but without MDMA's "magic"
- [[mbdb-entity]] — N-methyl-1,3-benzodioxolylbutanamine, a non-neurotoxic analogue

### Essential Amphetamines

Compounds structurally related to natural essential oils, differing only by elements of ammonia:

- [[mmda-entity]] — from myristicin (nutmeg)
- [[tma-entity]] — from elemicin
- [[mda]] — from safrole
- The essential oils serve as a pharmacological index for their amphetamine counterparts

## Pharmacological Mechanisms

Most psychedelic phenethylamines act primarily as 5-HT2A receptor agonists. Additional receptor interactions include:

- **5-HT2C**: Contributes to visual effects and anxiety
- **5-HT1A**: May modulate the psychedelic experience
- **TAAR1**: Trace amine-associated receptor, particularly for endogenous phenethylamine
- **Serotonin release/reuptake**: Entactogens like MDMA act primarily through SERT activation

## Structure-Activity Relationship Principles

Key findings from Shulgin's systematic exploration:

- The 2,4,5-substitution pattern is consistently the most active
- Relocating a single methoxy group (3,4,5 to 2,4,5) can increase potency ten-fold (TMA to [[tma-2-compound-profile]])
- Sulfur at the 4-position generally produces "insight-oriented" experiences
- Larger 4-position substituents increase potency but also increase physical side effects
- The relationship between molecular weight and duration is not linear
- Ring fluorination or beta-fluoroethyl groups enable PET imaging studies

## Dosage Ranges by Class

| Class | Typical Range | Duration |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| 2C phenethylamines | 5-30 mg | 4-12 h |
| DO amphetamines | 1-5 mg | 12-30 h |
| TMA series | 20-250 mg | 6-12 h |
| MDxx entactogens | 80-200 mg | 3-6 h |
| Aleph amphetamines | 5-20 mg | 8-14 h |

## Safety Considerations

- Potency varies enormously within the class (100x range between least and most potent)
- Duration varies from 3 hours to 30+ hours
- Physical side effects increase with dosage: tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia
- Serotonin syndrome risk with MAOIs or other serotonergic drugs
- [[2c-t-7-entity]] and other long-duration compounds require serious time commitment
- Many compounds in this class have no formal toxicological data
