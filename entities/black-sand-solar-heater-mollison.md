# Black Sand Solar Heater (Mollison's Invention)

## Description

A passive solar water heating system invented by Bill Mollison, inspired by walking barefoot on black sand beaches in Molokai, Hawaii. The system uses black sand in an insulated box topped with glass to heat water pipes with far greater efficiency than conventional metal collectors. Described as Mollison's ultra-sophisticated, cheap, fast, solar heater.

## Classification

- **Category**: Passive solar energy system
- **Type**: Solar thermal collector
- **Materials**: Black sand, glass, water pipes, insulated box

## Key Facts

- **Origin**: Mollison's feet cooked on black sand at Molokai, Hawaii.
- **Efficiency**: Far more efficient than metal collectors due to sand thermal properties.
- **Construction**: Water pipes through a box of black (or blackened) sand, covered with glass.
- **Cost**: Extremely cheap — sand, piping, glass, box.
- **Output**: Endless hot water at no cost.

## Design Details

1. Build insulated box, fill with black sand.
2. Blacken sand if not naturally black.
3. Run water pipes through sand bed.
4. Cover with glass for greenhouse effect.
5. Water absorbs heat from solar-heated sand.

## Advantages

- Superior thermal mass and heat transfer vs metal plates.
- No specialized fabrication required.
- Essentially free materials.
- Scalable from household to community.
