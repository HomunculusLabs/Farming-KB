## Acacia (Wattles)

**Type:** Genus (Plant)
**Category:** Nitrogen-fixing pioneer trees and shrubs
**Also known as:** Wattle, mimosa, thorntree

## Overview
Acacia is a large genus of approximately 850 species, predominantly native to
Australia, that plays a foundational role in permaculture and ecological
restoration. Wattles are nitrogen-fixing legumes that serve as pioneer species,
fodder trees, timber producers, and soil stabilisers across diverse climates
from arid deserts to wet forests. Many species produce edible seeds that were
traditional foods of Aboriginal Australians, and the seed is generally good
poultry fodder.

## Key Details

### Botanical Classification
- **Family:** Mimosaceae (sometimes Fabaceae)
- **Genus:** Acacia
- **Species:** Approximately 850 species
- **Distribution:** Predominantly Australian; some species in Africa, Asia,
  and the Americas
