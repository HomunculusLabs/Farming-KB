## Overview

Ice Cream Cake is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created through a cross between **Wedding Cake** and **Gelato #33**.

First bred by Seed Junky Genetics in Southern California, it rapidly became one of the most popular commercial strains of the early 2020s.

The strain is known for its exceptionally sweet, creamy aroma profile and deeply relaxing physical effects that make it a favorite for evening use.

Ice Cream Cake has won multiple industry awards and consistently ranks among the top-selling flower products in licensed dispensaries across North America.

## Lineage

Ice Cream Cake is a cross of two legendary Cookie-family hybrids: **Wedding Cake** (Triangle Mints × Animal Mints) and **Gelato #33** (Sunset Sherbet × Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies).

This pedigree places it squarely within the "Cookie" genetic lineage that has dominated commercial cannabis breeding since the mid-2010s.

The Wedding Cake parent contributes density, resin production, and a kushy backend, while Gelato #33 adds the sweet, creamy terpene expression and vibrant purple coloration.

The genetic ratio is approximately 75% indica to 25% sativa, reflecting the heavy indica influence from both parent lines.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed, and exhibit a classic indica structure with tight, overlapping calyxes.

The coloration is striking: deep forest green with occasional lavender and purple hues, particularly when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering.

A thick coating of milky-white trichomes covers the flowers, giving them a frosted, sugar-dusted appearance consistent with the "Ice Cream Cake" name.

Bright orange pistils are abundant and curl among the calyxes, creating a visually appealing contrast against the darker foliage.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by sweet, creamy notes reminiscent of vanilla bean and sugar cookies, with an earthy, gassy undertone that emerges as the bud is broken apart.

On the palate, the flavor is rich and dessert-like, with prominent vanilla, cream, and doughy cookie notes complemented by a subtle lavender and herbal finish.

A nutty, slightly woody aftertaste lingers on the exhale, adding complexity to what is otherwise a predominantly sweet flavor profile.

The terpene expression is strong and immediately noticeable, making this a strain that fills a room when the jar is opened.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Limonene (sweet, citrus), Caryophyllene (peppery, earthy), Linalool (floral, lavender), Myrcene (herbal, sedating).

Linalool is particularly abundant in well-grown Ice Cream Cake, contributing both to the floral aroma and the strain's pronounced sedative effects.

**Cannabinoid profile:** THC levels typically range from 20% to 25%, with some phenotype cuts testing above 28%.

CBD content is very low, generally below 0.1%, which is typical for Cookie-family strains bred exclusively for THC output.

Minor cannabinoids including CBG and THCV may be present in trace amounts depending on cultivation conditions.

## Effects

The onset is relatively fast, beginning with a mild cerebral uplift that produces a light, euphoric head change within minutes of inhalation.

This initial mental effect is short-lived and quickly transitions into a deep, full-body relaxation that spreads from the neck and shoulders downward.

Users consistently report heavy physical sedation, couch-lock, and an increased likelihood of sleep at higher doses.

Medical applications include relief from chronic pain, insomnia, anxiety, muscle tension, and stress-related disorders.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness; at higher doses, paranoia or heightened anxiety may occur in sensitive individuals.

## Cultivation

Ice Cream Cake has a flowering time of approximately 8–9 weeks when grown indoors, with outdoor harvest typically ready by mid-October in northern temperate climates.

Indoor yields average 350–450 g/m², while outdoor plants can produce 400–500 g per plant under optimal conditions.

The strain is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, requiring careful management of humidity during the dense flowering phase to prevent bud rot.

It responds well to low-stress training (LST) and scrogging techniques, and exhibits strong resistance to most common pests and pathogens.

The plant tends to develop vivid purple coloration in the final two weeks of flowering if nighttime temperatures drop below 15°C.

## Notable Phenotypes

Several distinct phenotype cuts have emerged from the original Ice Cream Cake cross, with the "Gelato-dominant" cut being the most commercially prevalent.

A "Wedding Cake-dominant" phenotype expresses more earthy, gassy terpenes and produces denser, more resinous buds with a longer flowering time of 9–10 weeks.

The "Lava Cake" cut, sometimes sold separately, is a backcross of an Ice Cream Cake phenotype that amplifies the chocolate and earthy flavor notes.
