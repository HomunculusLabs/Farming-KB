# Animal Face

## Overview

**Animal Face** is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Face Off OG** with **Animal Mints**. Bred by the noted seed company Seed Junky Genetics — the same breeders behind highly sought-after cultivars like Wedding Cake, Jealousy, and Ice Cream Cake — Animal Face has rapidly ascended to cult status among cannabis connoisseurs since its introduction in the late 2010s. The strain is renowned for its extraordinary [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], complex terpene profile blending cookie dough sweetness with sharp diesel and pine undertones, and intensely relaxing effects that make it a favorite among experienced consumers seeking heavy-body sedation. With THC concentrations regularly testing between 23–30% and occasional phenotypes exceeding 32%, Animal Face is considered one of the most powerful strains in the contemporary Cookie Family genetic lineage.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (approximately 70/30 indica-to-sativa ratio, with some phenotypes exhibiting more balanced expression)
- **Breeder:** Seed Junky Genetics (California, USA)
- **Genetic cross:** Face Off OG × Animal Mints
- **Parent — Face Off OG:** An indica-dominant strain of uncertain origin, reportedly named after the 1997 John Travolta–Nicolas Cage film. Face Off OG itself is believed to descend from [[og-kush-cannabis-entity]] lineage, offering deep body relaxation and a characteristically pungent, kush-forward aroma. It has served as a parent in several notable modern crosses.
- **Parent — Animal Mints:** A three-way cross of Face Off animal cookies kies, thin mint Mint GSC. Animal Mints inherits the dense resin production and sweet, minty cookie profile of the GSC line while adding musky, animalistic funk from the Animal Cookies side. Note that Face Off OG appears on both sides of Animal Face's pedigree, creating a degree of inbreeding that concentrates desirable traits — a common strategy in modern [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]].
- **Noted phenotypes:** Multiple cuts circulate, with variation in bud structure, color expression (some phenos exhibit deep purple hues in cooler temperatures), and the balance between the diesel and cookie terpene notes

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range |
|----------|--------------|
| THC | 23–30% (select phenotypes up to 32–34%) |
| CBD | < 0.1% (negligible) |
| CBG | 0.1–0.3% |
| THCV | Trace |

The extreme THC potency places Animal Face firmly in the "heavyweight" category, comparable to strains like Godfat bruce banner e Banner #3, and Chiquita Banana. Novice consumers are strongly advised to approach with caution, as even experienced users report that high doses can produce overwhelming sedation and couch-lock.

The overall aroma is dominated by a pungent, diesel-kush musk overlaid with sweet cookie dough, mint, and subtle notes of pine and earth. When ground, the bouquet intensifies dramatically, releasing layers of complexity that reflect both sides of its lineage — the sharp, gassy OG character from Face Off OG and the sweet, creamy cookie notes from Animal Mints.

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Large, dense, conical to irregularly rounded colas with tightly packed bracteal leaves
- **Coloration:** Deep forest green as the base, often with striking patches of dark purple and violet (especially in phenotypes exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering), with vivid orange pistils weaving throughout
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptionally heavy — buds are typically coated in a thick, milky-white blanket of trichomes that gives them a frosted, almost sugar-dusted appearance; resin production is among the highest of any commercially available strain
- **Calyx-to-leaf ratio:** High; relatively little leaf material, making the buds appear chunky and resinous rather than leafy

## Effects

### Onset and Duration

- **Onset:** Rapid (within 5–15 minutes of inhalation or 30–60 minutes when ingested)
- **Peak:** 1–2 hours post-consumption
- **Duration:** 3–5 hours, with residual sedation persisting for 1–2 additional hours in some users

### Reported Effects

- **Cerebral:** Initial cerebral uplift that is more subdued than typical sativa-dominant hybrids — users describe a hazy, euphoric warmth behind the eyes and forehead that settles into a dreamy, contemplative state. Creative thought is possible at low to moderate doses but tends to fragment at higher doses.
- **Physical:** Deep, full-body relaxation that progresses steadily toward heavy sedation. Muscular tension dissolves, and many users report a "melting" sensation in the extremities. At higher doses, couch-lock is virtually guaranteed.
- **Therapeutic applications:** The strain is popular among medical cannabis patients for managing chronic pain, insomnia, anxiety, PTSD-related hyperarousal, muscle spasms, and nausea. The combination of high THC and sedative terpenes (especially myrcene and linalool) makes it particularly effective as a nighttime medication.
- **[[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]]:** Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness, and at higher doses, significant anxiety or paranoia (especially in inexperienced users or those prone to THC-induced anxiety). The heavy sedative effect may impair motor function and should not be combined with alcohol or other CNS depressants.

## Cultivation

- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; not recommended for first-time growers due to sensitivity to environmental conditions and nutrient requirements
- **Flowering time:** 60–70 days indoors (9–10 weeks)
- **Yield:** Moderate (400–500 g/m² indoors); some phenotypes are lighter yielders due to extreme resin production redirecting metabolic resources away from calyx size
- **Indoor cultivation:** Performs well in soil or hydroponic systems; responds well to low-stress training (LST) and scrog methods; requires adequate airflow to prevent bud rot in the dense colas. Maintaining daytime temperatures at 24–27°C and nighttime temperatures at 18–20°C during flowering encourages optimal terpene production. Some phenotypes express purple coloration more intensely when nighttime temperatures drop below 15°C in the final two weeks.
- **Outdoor cultivation:** Suitable for warm, Mediterranean climates with a long growing season; harvest in mid-to-late October in the Northern Hemisphere. Plants reach medium height (100–150 cm) and benefit from support structures for the heavy terminal colas.
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate-to-heavy feeder during [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]; requires careful management of nitrogen levels in mid-flowering to prevent excessive vegetative stretch. CalMag supplementation is recommended in coco-based media. Phosphorus and potassium should be increased during weeks 4–8 of flowering to support resin and terpene production.
- **[[hamilton-pest-and-disease-control-organic]] resistance:** Moderate; dense bud structure makes it susceptible to botrytis (gray mold) in humid conditions. Regular defoliation and airflow management are essential.

## Market Availability

Animal Face remains a relatively premium and occasionally scarce strain, as Seed Junky Genetics has historically operated through limited seed drops and clone-only distribution. It is most commonly found in licensed dispensaries in California, Oregon, and other US states with established cannabis markets. Prices tend to reflect its boutique status, typically commanding a 20–40% premium over standard top-shelf flower. The strain has gained significant visibility through social media and cannabis review platforms, where its visual appeal and extreme potency have made it a favorite among reviewers and enthusiasts.

## Strain History and Cultural Impact

Animal Face emerged during a period of intense innovation in the Seed Junky Genetics [[cervantes-breeding-program-design-goal-setting]], when the Cookie Family lineage was being extensively explored for novel combinations. Its release was met with enthusiasm from the cannabis community, particularly in Southern California, where it quickly gained a reputation as a "hash-maker's dream" due to the exceptional quality and quantity of its trichome production. The strain has become a staple in competitive cannabis circuits, frequently placing in extraction competitions and Cannabis Cup events.

The name "Animal Face" reflects both its parentage (Animal Mints) and its intensely potent effects, which experienced consumers often describe as "animalistic" in their raw power. The strain has spawned several notable variants and crosses, including Animal Face #10 (a particularly resinous phenotype) and Animal Face BX1 (a backcross used to stabilize desirable traits).
