---# Animal Face

## Overview

**Animal Face** is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Face Off OG** with **Animal Mints**. Bred by the noted seed company Seed Junky Genetics — the same breeders behind highly sought-after cultivars like Wedding Cake, Jealousy, and Ice Cream Cake — Animal Face has rapidly ascended to cult status among cannabis connoisseurs since its introduction in the late 2010s. The strain is renowned for its extraordinary [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] blending cookie dough sweetness with sharp diesel and pine undertones, and intensely relaxing effects that make it a favorite among experienced consumers seeking heavy-body sedation. With THC concentrations regularly testing between 23–30% and occasional phenotypes exceeding 32%, Animal Face is considered one of the most powerful strains in the contemporary Cookie Family genetic lineage.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (approximately 70/30 indica-to-sativa ratio, with some phenotypes exhibiting more balanced expression)
- **Breeder:** Seed Junky Genetics (California, USA)
- **Genetic cross:** Face Off OG × Animal Mints
- **Parent — Face Off OG:** An indica-dominant strain of uncertain origin, reportedly named after the 1997 John Travolta–Nicolas Cage film. Face Off OG itself is believed to descend from [[animal-cookies]] kies, thin mint Mint GSC. Animal Mints inherits the dense [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]].
- **Noted phenotypes:** Multiple cuts circulate, with variation in bud structure, color expression (some phenos exhibit deep purple hues in cooler temperatures), and the balance between the diesel and cookie terpene notes

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range |
|----------|--------------|
| THC | 23–30% (select phenotypes up to 32–34%) |
| CBD | < 0.1% (negligible) |
| CBG | 0.1–0.3% |
| THCV | Trace |

The extreme THC potency places Animal Face firmly in the "heavyweight" category, comparable to strains like Godfat bruce banner e Banner #3, and Chiquita Banana. Novice consumers are strongly advised to approach with caution, as even experienced users report that high doses can produce overwhelming sedation and couch-lock.

The overall aroma is dominated by a pungent, diesel-kush musk overlaid with sweet cookie dough, mint, and subtle notes of pine and earth. When ground, the bouquet intensifies dramatically, releasing layers of complexity that reflect both sides of its lineage — the sharp, gassy OG character from Face Off OG and the sweet, creamy cookie notes from Animal Mints.

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Large, dense, conical to irregularly rounded colas with tightly packed bracteal leaves
- **Coloration:** Deep forest green as the base, often with striking patches of dark purple and violet (especially in phenotypes exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering), with vivid orange pistils weaving throughout
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptionally heavy — buds are typically coated in a thick, milky-white blanket of trichomes that gives them a frosted, almost sugar-dusted appearance; resin production is among the highest of any commercially available strain
- **Calyx-to-leaf ratio:** High; relatively little leaf material, making the buds appear chunky and resinous rather than leafy

## Effects

### Onset and Duration

- **Onset:** Rapid (within 5–15 minutes of inhalation or 30–60 minutes when ingested)
- **Peak:** 1–2 hours post-consumption
- **Duration:** 3–5 hours, with residual sedation persisting for 1–2 additional hours in some users

### Reported Effects

- **Cerebral:** Initial cerebral uplift that is more subdued than typical sativa-dominant hybrids — users describe a hazy, euphoric warmth behind the eyes and forehead that settles into a dreamy, contemplative state. Creative thought is possible at low to moderate doses but tends to fragment at higher doses.
- **Physical:** Deep, full-body relaxation that progresses steadily toward heavy sedation. Muscular tension dissolves, and many users report a "melting" sensation in the extremities. At higher doses, couch-lock is virtually guaranteed.
- **[[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]]:** Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness, and at higher doses, significant anxiety or paranoia (especially in inexperienced users or those prone to THC-induced anxiety). The heavy sedative effect may impair motor function and should not be combined with alcohol or other CNS depressants.

## Cultivation

- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; not recommended for first-time growers due to sensitivity to environmental conditions [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]]:** Performs well in soil or hydroponic systems; responds well to low-stress training (LST) and scrog methods; requires adequate airflow to prevent bud rot in the dense colas. Maintaining daytime temperatures at 24–27°C and nighttime temperatures at 18–20°C during flowering encourages optimal terpene production. Some phenotypes express purple coloration more intensely when nighttime temperatures drop below 15°C in the final two weeks.
