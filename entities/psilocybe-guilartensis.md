# Psilocybe guilartensis

## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Fungi
- **Division:** Basidiomycota
- **Class:** Agaricomycetes
- **Order:** Agaricales
- **Family:** Hymenogastraceae
- **Genus:** *Psilocybe*
- **Species:** *P. guilartensis*

Described by Guzmán and collaborators from collections made in Puerto Rico. The species epithet is derived from the Sierra de Guilarte, a mountainous area in the municipality of Adjuntas in central-western Puerto Rico, where the species was first documented. It belongs to *Psilocybe* Section *Caerulescentes*, the blue-staining neotropical group.

## Description

### Cap (Pileus)
- **Diameter:** 10–25 mm, convex to plano-convex, sometimes with a low, broad umbo
- **Color:** Dark brown to reddish-brown when moist, fading to tan or yellowish-brown when dried
- **Surface:** Glabrous, slightly viscid to subviscid when fresh, hygrophanous
- **Margin:** Even to slightly undulating, sometimes translucently striate at maturity
- **Context:** Thin, bruising distinctly blue

### Gills (Lamellae)
- Adnate to slightly adnexed, close
- Grayish to light brown when young, maturing to dark purplish-brown
- Edges whitish, slightly fimbriate

### Stipe (Stem)
- **Length:** 30–60 mm
- **Thickness:** 1.5–3 mm, cylindrical, equal or slightly tapered downward
- **Color:** White to pale cream, often staining blue-green at the base
- **Surface:** Smooth to faintly pruinose at the apex
- **Partial veil:** Present in young specimens, forming a thin annular zone that may persist as fibrillose remnants
- **Bluing:** Moderate to strong, most prominent at the base and where damaged

## Conservation

While not formally listed as threatened, the restricted island distribution of *P. guilartensis* makes it inherently vulnerable. Puerto Rico's montane forests face pressure from urbanization, agriculture, and climate change. The species' known localities within protected forest reserves (such as Toro Negro and Guilarte State Forest) provide some degree of habitat security, but long-term monitoring would be advisable to assess population trends.
