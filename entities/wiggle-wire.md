# Wiggle Wire

## Description

Wiggle wire (also known as wire-lock, springlock, or poly-lock) is a greenhouse fastening system used to securely attach plastic film covering to greenhouse structural frames. It consists of a low U-shaped aluminum channel mounted to the greenhouse frame, into which a zigzag-shaped spring wire is inserted, trapping the plastic film firmly against the inner surfaces of the channel. It is the primary method used at [[four-season-farm]] for securing greenhouse coverings on both static and mobile structures.

## Classification

- **Category**: Greenhouse hardware / fastening system
- **Type**: Channel-and-spring wire clamp
- **Alternative names**: Wire-lock, Springlock, Poly-lock, Wiggle wire
- **Material**: Aluminum channel (typically); galvanized spring steel wire

## Design and Components

### Channel (Base)
- Low U-shaped metal channel
- Bolted or screwed to the greenhouse frame
- Best designs are deep enough to accommodate two or more zigzag wires
- Dual-wire capacity allows the same channel to secure both end-wall plastic and top-covering plastic simultaneously

### Spring Wire (Insert)
- Zigzag-shaped length of spring steel wire
- Pressed into the channel over the folded edge of the plastic film
- The zigzag pattern creates constant outward pressure against the channel walls
- This pressure clamps the plastic firmly in place

## Advantages

1. **Quick attachment and removal**: Simply press wire in or pry it out — no tools required for routine operations
2. **Reusable**: Both channel and wire can be reused across multiple plastic replacements
3. **Secure hold**: When properly installed, holds plastic firmly against wind loads
4. **Dual-layer capability**: Deep channels allow stacking two wires, securing two separate plastic sheets
5. **Adjustable**: Plastic can be re-tensioned by removing and re-inserting the wire

## Applications at Four Season Farm

### Mobile Greenhouse End Walls
- Rolling houses: 12-foot-wide doorway covered with plastic sheet secured at top and sides with wiggle-wire channel
- Bottom edge held with sandbags (allows roll-up/roll-down operation)
- For winter ventilation: unclip wiggle wire across top and upper sides, drop plastic down
- For summer: roll up door from bottom

### Sliding Houses
- End-wall plastic secured with wiggle wire
- Provides airtight seal against winter cold infiltration

### Compost A-Frame Structure
- Wiggle-wire channel used to secure plastic over temporary A-frame compost shelter
- Holds plastic up and over each end
- Sandbags along the bottom edge

## Installation Notes

- Channel should be deep enough for at least one zigzag wire; two is preferable
- The wire must be the correct width for the channel — too loose and it won't grip; too tight and insertion is difficult
- In cold weather, plastic becomes less pliable; careful insertion avoids tearing
- Channels can be mounted on wooden or metal greenhouse frames

## Comparison to Alternatives

| Method | Security | Ease of Use | Cost | Reusability |
|--------|----------|-------------|------|-------------|
| Wiggle wire | High | Very easy | Moderate | High |
| Lathing strip (batten) | Moderate | Labor-intensive | Low | Low |
| Sandbags | Low–Moderate | Very easy | Very low | High |
| Buried edges | High | Laborious | Free | N/A |
| Snap clamps (PVC) | Moderate | Easy | Low | Moderate |

## Relevance to Cultivation

Wiggle wire is a foundational component of efficient greenhouse management:

- **Rapid ventilation adjustment**: Quick unclip and re-secure is essential for daily temperature management
- **Seasonal covering changes**: Mobile houses covered/uncovered seasonally benefit from speed
- **Cost efficiency**: Reusable hardware reduces per-season infrastructure costs
- **Storm preparedness**: Quick removal or tightening of plastic before severe weather

For any grower building or managing poly-covered structures, wiggle wire is the industry-standard attachment method that balances security, speed, and durability.
