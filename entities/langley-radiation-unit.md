## Langley (Radiation Unit)

## Description

The Langley (Ly) is a unit of solar radiation measurement, defined as one gram calorie per square centimetre of surface area (1 g-cal/cm2). It is named after Samuel Pierpont Langley, a 19th-century American astronomer and physicist who pioneered solar radiation research. In [[passive-solar-design]], [[permaculture-design]]:

1. **Low-grade heat (conduction):** Solid-to-solid contact, trapped by insulation
2. **Medium-grade heat (convection):** Fluid/gas movement, conserved by draught-proofing
3. **High-grade heat (radiation):** Straight-line transmission, conserved by reflective surfaces
