# Pleurotus (Oyster Mushrooms)

## Description

Pleurotus is a genus of gilled mushrooms commonly known as [[oyster-mushrooms]]. They are among the most widely cultivated mushrooms globally, valued for rapid growth, adaptability to diverse substrates, and nutritional qualities. Pleurotus species are primary wood decomposers ([[white-rot-fungi]]) that can also be grown on pasteurized agricultural waste products. The genus includes numerous species with varying colors, temperature preferences, and morphologies, including the pink oyster (P. djamor), king oyster (P. eryngii), and common oyster (P. ostreatus).

## Classification

- **Kingdom**: Fungi
- **Phylum**: Basidiomycota
- **Class**: Agaricomycetes
- **Order**: Agaricales
- **Family**: Pleurotaceae
- **Genus**: Pleurotus

## Key Facts

- Pleurotus can be grown directly on pasteurized agricultural waste products indoors without sterilization
- Grain spawn can be used directly for Pleurotus on pasteurized substrates, similar to Agaricus on compost
- Oyster mushrooms are carnivorous — they trap and digest nematodes using specialized toxins
- All Pleurotus species are white rot fungi, capable of breaking down lignin
- Enormous morphological diversity: P. djamor (pink, tropical), P. eryngii (thick stem), P. ostreatus (cold-tolerant)
- Mycelium is typically fast-growing, linear, and cottony white on agar plates

## Relevance to Cultivation

Pleurotus is often recommended as a starter mushroom for new cultivators because it fruits readily on simple pasteurized substrates (straw, agricultural waste) without requiring sterile technique beyond spawn production. Its fast colonization rate helps outcompete contaminants. Cultivation can proceed from grain spawn directly to fruiting substrate without an intermediate substrate spawn generation.

## Relevance to Mycology

Pleurotus is a model organism for studying lignin degradation, white rot mechanisms, and fungal carnivory. Extensively studied for mycoremediation applications including oil spill cleanup, pollutant degradation, and [[heavy-metal-biosorption]]. Its metabolic flexibility makes it valuable for understanding [[fungal-enzyme-systems]].

## Species of Note

- Pleurotus djamor — pink oyster, tropical
- Pleurotus eryngii — king oyster, thick-stemmed
- Pleurotus pulmonarius — phoenix oyster, warm-tolerant
