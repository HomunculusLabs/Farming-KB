# MEM (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine)

MEM is a substituted amphetamine compound and one of the ethoxy homologues of the TMA series. It played a watershed role in the development of psychedelic phenethylamine chemistry. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #122.

## Chemical Classification

MEM belongs to the amphetamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine.

MEM is one of three possible mono-ethoxy homologues of [[tma-2-entity]], formed by replacing the 4-methoxy group with a 4-ethoxy group.

The naming convention uses M for methoxy and E for ethoxy, arranged by ring position from 2 to 4 to 5.

## Pharmacological Profile

MEM is a potent and active psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 20 to 50 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 10 to 14 hours.

MEM is described as both a valuable and dramatic compound, and it played a critical role in psychedelic research by identifying the 4-position as the key leverage point for potency.

## Qualitative Effects

At 20 mg, subjects experienced some physical discomfort but reported breakthrough insights the following day.

The compound was described as operating with a pattern of energy and expansion on the first day, followed by important insights the next day.

At 20 mg taken 1.5 hours after 120 mg [[mdma-entity]], the transition was very smooth with no loss of the MDMA experience.

The intimate closeness with others was maintained and the experience continued to grow more profound and euphoric.

At 25 mg taken 2 hours after 120 mg [[mdma-entity]], sounds were distracting and annoying, with a desire to be alone.

Some color changes and pattern movement developed, and the material was not anorexic.

At 30 mg, awareness began within 30 minutes and slowly developed to almost a plus three within the following hour.

Visual phenomena included color enhancement and considerable enhancement of brights and darks.

The first signs of decline appeared at about six hours, but effects persisted after twelve hours.

At 50 mg, effects were felt within ten minutes, described as the fastest onset ever experienced with this compound.

The ascent was rapid with an initial hour of inward fantasying with a distinct sensual tinge.

Persistent queasiness contrasted with good outward articulation and lucidity after the introverted first hour.

Sleep was difficult but the next day was calm and clear.

At 50 mg, another subject reported lots of energy, clear imaging and thinking, intensity yet serenity, good pleasantness and euphoria.

A need to keep moving was noted, with difficulty staying still.

At 70 mg in two parts, effects of 40 mg were muted by a previous drug experiment, with an erotic nature and tactile sensitivity.

## The Discovery of the 4-Position

MEM was critical in establishing that the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine scaffold is the key site for modifying psychoactivity and potency.

The three mono-ethoxy homologues of TMA-2 are EMM (2-ethoxy), MEM (4-ethoxy), and MME (5-ethoxy).

Of these three, only MEM was dramatically active, demonstrating that modification at the 4-position is far more productive than at the 2- or 5-positions.

This discovery redirected all subsequent emphasis toward 4-substituted compounds, leading to the many 4-substituted families now known to be highly potent.

Diethoxy and triethoxy possibilities (EEM, EME, MEE, and EEE) were also completed but showed less promise.

## Relationship to TMA Series

[[tma-entity]] (3,4,5-trimethoxyamphetamine) was the original trimethoxy compound.

[[tma-2-entity]] (2,4,5-trimethoxyamphetamine) became the most productive scaffold for modification.

MEM represents the extension of TMA-2 by homologating the 4-methoxy to a 4-ethoxy group.

The discovery that only the 4-position modification produced dramatic results shaped the entire direction of subsequent psychedelic phenethylamine research.

## Emphasis on Potency

Shulgin frequently addressed the question of why potency matters.

There are no reliable guides as to the toxic levels of these compounds in humans.

Most are of relatively low toxicity in mice and relatively high toxicity in rats, but this toxicity appears unrelated to potency in man.

The lower the active dosage, the greater the theoretical safety margin, making this a reasonable operating hypothesis in the absence of factual data.

## MDMA Combination Use

Many reports of MEM effects involved experiments where an effective dose of [[mdma-entity]] had been taken shortly before.

The combination of MDMA followed by MEM appeared to produce particularly profound and long-lasting experiences.

This combination approach was explored to extend and deepen therapeutic or exploratory sessions.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 20 - 50 mg.

Duration: 10 - 14 h.

Many experiments were conducted in combination with [[mdma-entity]].
