## Synthesis and Chemistry

The synthesis of 2C-H begins with 2,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde, which is condensed with nitromethane in the presence of ammonium acetate to form 2,5-dimethoxy-beta-nitrostyrene.

This nitrostyrene intermediate crystallizes as spectacular orange crystals with a melting point of 119-120 degrees C.

The nitrostyrene is then reduced using lithium aluminum hydride (LAH) in THF under an inert atmosphere.

The reduction proceeds over 36 hours at reflux, followed by careful workup to destroy excess hydride.

The free base distills at 80-100 degrees C at 0.4 mm/Hg and is obtained as a water-white oil.
