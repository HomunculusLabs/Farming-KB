## Pinpoint Seeder

## Description

The pinpoint seeder is a lightweight, multi-row precision seeding implement originally manufactured in Germany, used at [[four-season-farm]] for all greenhouse sowing. It was identified by [[eliot-coleman]] as the ideal tool for intensive greenhouse planting, where close row spacing and precise seed placement are economically essential. When the German manufacturer discontinued production in 2004, Coleman collaborated with other growers to develop a six-row successor with improved features. Both the original four-row and newer six-row models remain in use.

## Classification

- **Category**: Seeding equipment
- **Type**: Multi-row precision seeder
- **Use case**: Intensive greenhouse bed seeding
- **Origin**: German-made (original four-row); later US-produced six-row

## Specifications and Configuration

### Four-Row Model (Original)
- **Rows per pass**: 4
- **Row spacing**: 2½ inches between rows
- **Passes per bed**: 3 passes to plant 12 rows on a 30-inch bed
- **Weight**: Lightweight, easy to maneuver

### Six-Row Model (Replacement)
- **Rows per pass**: 6
- **Row spacing**: 2½ inches between rows
- **Passes per bed**: 2 passes to plant 12 rows on a 30-inch bed
- **Improvements**: Multiple features identified as wanting in the original

## Row Spacing Rationale

The 2½-inch row spacing may seem extremely close compared to field standards, but it is common in European greenhouse production. This intensive spacing is economically necessary because:

1. **Space is limited and expensive** in protected cultivation
2. **Close spacing maximizes yield per square foot** of covered area
3. **Baby leaf salad crops** thrive at high density
4. **Greenhouse conditions** support denser planting than field conditions

## Seed Types and Applications

The pinpoint seeder handles seeds for the full range of Coleman's greenhouse crops:

- **Baby leaf salads** — 12 rows per bed at 2½-inch spacing
- **Radishes** — 12 rows per bed
- **Carrots** — 12 rows per bed
- **Spinach** — dense multi-row plantings
- **Other small-seeded crops** — precision placement for uniform germination

## Seeding Technique

The seeder requires specific bed preparation for optimal performance:

1. Soil must be firmed with a 30-inch-wide mesh-surfaced roller before seeding
2. The firm surface provides "purchase" for the toothed wheels of the seeder
3. This ensures consistent seed depth and spacing
4. The seeder is pushed steadily down the prepared bed

## Bed Preparation for Seeding

The complete preparation sequence before using the pinpoint seeder:

1. **Broadfork** — deep aeration
2. **Tilther** — shallow amendment incorporation (top 2 inches)
3. **Compost** — spread and mixed shallowly (top ¼ inch)
4. **Austrian hay rake** — smooth the bed surface
5. **Mesh roller** — firm the surface (critical for seeder operation)
6. **Pinpoint seeder** — sow seeds in precise rows

## Historical Development

Coleman's progression in precision seeding tools:

1. **Early stage**: Homemade model — five EarthWay seeders bolted side by side, planting 5 rows at 4-inch spacing
   - Described in *The New Organic Grower* (p. 73)
   - Functional but awkward and heavy
2. **German pinpoint seeder**: Lightweight four-row model
   - Extremely accurate and precise
   - Became the standard tool for years
   - Discontinued by manufacturer in 2004
3. **Six-row successor**: Collaborative redesign with other growers
   - Incorporated lessons from years of pinpoint seeder use
   - Added features the original lacked

## Comparison to Alternatives

|--------|------|---------|-----------|-------------|

| Homemade 5× EarthWay | 5 rows | 4 inches | Moderate | Heavy, awkward |
| Pinpoint (4-row) | 4 rows | 2½ inches | Extremely high | Greenhouse ideal |
| Six-row successor | 6 rows | 2½ inches | Extremely high | Greenhouse ideal |

## Relevance to Cultivation

Precision seeding is a cornerstone of intensive greenhouse vegetable production. The pinpoint seeder enables:

- **Economic viability**: Maximizing yield from expensive covered space
- **Labor efficiency**: Multi-row seeding in a single pass reduces sowing time
- **Crop uniformity**: Precision placement leads to even germination and harvest timing
- **Succession planning**: Quick, accurate sowing enables tight crop rotation schedules

For any grower practicing intensive raised-bed production, the pinpoint seeder's design principles represent the gold standard for small-scale intensive sowing.
