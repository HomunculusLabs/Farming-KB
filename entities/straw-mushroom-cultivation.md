## Straw Mushroom Cultivation (Volvariella volvacea)

## Description

Straw mushroom cultivation refers to the specific method of growing the straw mushroom (Volvariella volvacea) on [[boil-a-bag-mushroom-cultivation-method]]
- **Species**: Volvariella volvacea
- **Substrate**: Primarily rice straw; also cotton waste, banana leaves, oil palm fiber
- **Temperature requirement**: Tropical (28-35°C optimal)
- **Growth cycle**: Very fast — as short as 10-14 days from spawning to harvest

## Cultivation Process

### Substrate Preparation
1. **Rice straw selection**: Use fresh, clean rice straw free from mold and excessive moisture
2. **Soaking**: Submerge straw bundles in water for 12-24 hours to fully hydrate
3. **Draining**: Remove from water and allow excess moisture to drain

### Bed Construction
1. Lay soaked straw bundles in layers on a prepared platform or ground bed
2. Each layer is spread with spawn (grain spawn or straw-based spawn)
3. Build layers to a height of 15-25 cm, spawning each layer
4. Cover the completed bed with plastic sheeting to maintain humidity and temperature

### Environmental Management
- **Temperature**: 28-35°C optimal (straw mushrooms are heat-loving)
- **Humidity**: 80-95% relative humidity
- **Ventilation**: Moderate air exchange needed, especially after pinning
- **Light**: Some diffuse light beneficial for fruiting

### Fruiting and Harvest
- Pinheads appear 5-7 days after spawning
- Mushrooms are harvested at the "egg" or "button" stage for highest quality
- Multiple flushes occur over a 7-10 day fruiting period
- Total crop cycle from spawning to completion: 10-14 days
