## Overview

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Dense, elongated colas with moderate calyx-to-leaf
  ratio
- **Color:** Bright green with occasional purple hues in cooler growing
  conditions; pistils are pale orange to amber
- **Trichome coverage:** Moderate to heavy; trichomes are predominantly
  clear to milky at harvest maturity
- **[[cannabis-terpene-profile]] is less
  intense than typical THC-dominant Sour Diesel phenotypes

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene:** 0.3–0.6% (moderate, contributes to sedative body note)
- **Caryophyllene:** 0.2–0.5% (peppery, anti-inflammatory)
- **Limonene:** 0.1–0.4% (citrus undertone)
- **Pinene:** 0.1–0.2% (subtle pine freshness)
- **Humulene:** Trace amounts
