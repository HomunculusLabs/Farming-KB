# AK-47

AK-47 is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain celebrated for its
potent, long-lasting cerebral effects and remarkably compact flowering
time for a sativa-leaning cultivar. Developed by Serious Seeds in the
Netherlands during the 1990s, AK-47 has won multiple Cannabis Cup awards
including first place at the 1999 High Times Cannabis Cup. Despite its
aggressive name, the strain delivers a relaxed, euphoric experience
rather than an intense or racy high. Its genetic stability and
consistent performance have made it a staple in cannabis gardens
worldwide for nearly three decades, and it remains widely available
in both seed form and as clones from established genetic libraries.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica)
- **Breeder:** Simon (Serious Seeds, Amsterdam, Netherlands)
- **Genetic background:** Complex Colombian x Mexican x Thai x Afghani
  cross; the exact breeding history is proprietary but represents a
  stabilization of equatorial sativa and landrace Afghan indica genetics
- **Awards:** 1st place High Times Cannabis Cup 1999 (Hybrid category);
  multiple regional Cannabis Cup awards throughout the 2000s
- **Notable descendants:** AK-48 (variant cross), Critical AK, AK
  Auto-flowering versions by multiple seed banks

## Growth Characteristics

- **Indoor flowering time:** 7-9 weeks (notably fast for sativa-dominant)
- **Outdoor harvest:** Late September to early October
- **Height:** Medium; relatively compact for sativa genetics, making
  it suitable for indoor grows with limited vertical space
- **Yield:** High (350-500 g/m2 indoors); prolific producer with
  long, resinous colas
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate; forgiving of minor cultivation
  errors, recommended for beginners transitioning from autoflowers
- **Resistance:** Good resistance to mold and pests; sturdy branch
  structure supports heavy colas without extensive staking

## Appearance and Aroma

AK-47 buds are medium to large in size with a somewhat elongated,
finger-like structure typical of sativa-dominant hybrids. The calyxes
are light to medium green with occasional pale yellow undertones,
covered in a dense layer of sticky trichomes that give the flowers
a frosty, silvery appearance. Orange to amber pistils are abundant
and curl tightly around the bud structure. The aroma is complex:
sour and earthy notes dominate, with undertones of pine, wood, and
a subtle sweetness that becomes more pronounced when the flower is
broken apart. The flavor follows the aroma closely, with a slightly
harsh smoke that carries floral and spicy notes on the exhale.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Typical Range | Aroma Character |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| Myrcene | 0.5-1.0% | Earthy, musky, herbal |
| Limonene | 0.3-0.7% | Citrus, lemon, uplifting |
| beta-Caryophyllene | 0.3-0.6% | Peppery, spicy, woody |
| Pinene (alpha/beta) | 0.1-0.4% | Pine, fresh, woody |
| Humulene | 0.1-0.3% | Hoppy, earthy |
| Linalool | Trace | Floral, sweet |

The relatively high pinene content distinguishes AK-47 from many
contemporary dessert-flavored hybrids and contributes to its
characteristic pine and wood notes. Pinene also has cognitive
effects that may counteract some of THC's short-term memory
impairment, potentially contributing to the strain's reputation
for functional cerebral effects.

## Cannabinoid Profile

AK-47 typically tests between 15-20% THC, with some modern
phenotypes reaching 22-24% under optimized cultivation. CBD
content is generally low (less than 0.1-0.5%), though CBD-rich
phenotypes have been selected by some breeders. The THCA to
THC ratio in cured flower is typical for well-cured material.
The strain's moderate THC levels by contemporary standards
contribute to its manageable, functional effects profile that
has made it enduringly popular with medical patients.

## Effects Profile

- **Onset:** Moderate (5-10 minutes when smoked or vaporized)
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours; notably long-lasting for a sativa hybrid
- **Character:** Immediate cerebral euphoria with a clear-headed,
  creative mental state. Unlike many sativa-dominant strains, AK-47
  does not typically produce racing thoughts or anxiety. The body
  effects are mild and relaxing without being heavily sedating,
  allowing for daytime functionality
- **Common effects:** Euphoria, talkativeness, creative focus,
  relaxation, sociability, gentle body warmth
- **Potential side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, mild paranoia
  at very high doses (rare due to moderate THC levels), headache
  with overconsumption

## Therapeutic Applications

AK-47's balanced effects profile makes it versatile for medical
use. The combination of cerebral uplift and physical relaxation
is particularly valued for chronic pain conditions, including
neuropathic pain and fibromyalgia. The strain's mood-elevating
properties are used in managing depression, PTSD-related symptoms,
and chronic stress. Its appetite-stimulating effects benefit
patients undergoing chemotherapy or dealing with eating disorders.
The relatively low anxiety potential compared to high-THC
contemporary strains makes it suitable for patients sensitive
to THC-induced anxiety.

## Cultivation Notes

AK-47 is considered an excellent strain for novice to intermediate
growers due to its resilience and predictable growth patterns. It
responds well to both soil and hydroponic setups. The strain's
compact stature for a sativa makes it suitable for sea of green
(SOG) configurations. Nutrient requirements are moderate; the
strain is not a heavy feeder and can develop nutrient burn if
over-fertilized, particularly during early flowering. Flushing
for 7-10 days before harvest is recommended to enhance flavor.
AK-47's genetic stability means phenotypic variation between seeds
is minimal, producing consistent results across grows.
