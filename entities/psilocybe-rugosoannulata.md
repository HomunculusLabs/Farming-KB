# Psilocybe rugosoannulata

**Psilocybe rugosoannulata** is a psilocybin-containing mushroom species in the family Hymenogastraceae, native to tropical and subtropical regions of South America. It is one of the lesser-known psychoactive Psilocybe species from the Neotropics, distinguished by its characteristic wrinkled annulus on the stipe.

## Distribution and Habitat

psilocybe rugosoannulata is known from tropical and subtropical regions of South America, with confirmed collections from Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and Brazil. It is likely more widely distributed in the Amazon basin and adjacent montane forests than current records indicate, as many Neotropical Psilocybe species are undercollected.

The species is found in pastures, grassy fields, and forest clearings, often growing directly on or near herbivore dung, particularly that of cattle and horses. It fruits during the rainy season when humidity is high and temperatures are warm, typically from November through March in the Southern Hemisphere. In equatorial regions, it may fruit year-round with peaks corresponding to local wet seasons.
