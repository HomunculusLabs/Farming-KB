# ALEPH-2

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthioamphetamine
**PiHKAL entry:** #4

## Overview

ALEPH-2 is a member of the [[alexander-shulgin]] and his research group.

The ALEPH series is named after letters of the Hebrew alphabet, with ALEPH being the first. The corresponding phenethylamine (2-carbon chain) counterpart is the 2C-T series, which was far more extensively explored. The ALEPH compounds represent some of the earliest sulfur-containing psychoactive phenethylamines ever evaluated.
