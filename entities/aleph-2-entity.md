# ALEPH-2

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthioamphetamine
**PiHKAL entry:** #4

## Overview

ALEPH-2 is a member of the [[aleph-family-overview]] of thio-substituted amphetamines. It carries an ethylthio group at the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine skeleton, making it the amphetamine analogue of 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2. Like other ALEPH compounds, it was synthesized and bioassayed by [[alexander-shulgin]] and his research group.

The ALEPH series is named after letters of the Hebrew alphabet, with ALEPH being the first. The corresponding phenethylamine (2-carbon chain) counterpart is the 2C-T series, which was far more extensively explored. The ALEPH compounds represent some of the earliest sulfur-containing psychoactive phenethylamines ever evaluated.

## Duration

8 - 16 hours. Considered a long-acting psychedelic comparable in duration to doi-entity|DOI or dom-entity|DOM.

## Effects

The qualitative reports from [[pihkal-introduction-and-methodology]] describe a complex and somewhat unpredictable profile:

- **Body effects:** Warm feeling throughout the body, light pressure in the head. Generally comfortable body feel described as "good, healthy." Some reports of muscular tremor and residual shakes at higher doses.
- **Sensory enhancement:** Tactile perception and auditory acuity are enhanced. Color perception may not be dramatically increased, but waves of smooth movement are noted in the visual field.
- **Visual phenomena:** At moderate doses (5 mg), visual distortions and illusions at the edge of hallucination are prominent -- logs in fireplaces appear in continuous motion, writing paper seems to scrunch and deform. At 7 mg, reports describe an "amazing unification of visual hallucination" with retinal-level imagery.
- **Cognitive effects:** At lower doses, paradoxically easier handling of the outer world with none of the jitters of amphetamine. At higher doses (8 mg), extreme intoxication even without obvious visual phenomena -- a sense of being the center of one's own reality.

## Dose-Response Variability

A notable characteristic of ALEPH-2 (shared with the entire ALEPH family) is the **unpredictability of the dose/response relationship**. One subject at 4 mg expressed psychic isolation by maintaining a fetal position for hours with substantial amnesia, while another at twice that dose (8 mg) experienced only a "bare threshold" effect. Shulgin notes this erratic nature was not apparent until after ALEPH-7 had already been synthesized.

## Synthesis Notes

The synthesis begins with 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(ethylthio)benzaldehyde (prepared as described under 2C-T-2), which is condensed with nitroethane in the presence of ammonium acetate to yield the nitrostyrene intermediate. Reduction with lithium aluminum hydride (LAH) in THF provides the final amine as the hydrochloride salt (mp 128-130 deg C with decomposition).

## Pharmacology

As a member of the 2c-t-2-compound-profile|thiophenethylamine family, ALEPH-2's activity likely involves 5-HT2A receptor agonism, consistent with other psychedelic amphetamines. The ethylthio group contributes to both potency and duration compared to the parent compound aleph-1-entity|ALEPH.

## Unmade Analogues

ALEPH-3 (the methallylthio compound) was attempted but never completed -- the aldehyde synthesis produced impure products on two separate occasions. The corresponding phenethylamine, had it been made, would have been called 2C-T-3 or 2C-T-20. ALEPH-5 (the cyclohexylthio analogue) similarly got as far as the nitrostyrene precursor, which sits "on the shelf" awaiting future exploration. The allylthio homologue (ALEPH-16) reached the nitrostyrene stage as well.

## Safety Considerations

The long duration (up to 16 hours) and unpredictable dose-response demand careful dosing. Reports of muscular tremor, difficulty sleeping, and the potential for sensory confusion at higher doses warrant caution. The compound should be approached with the same respect as other long-acting psychedelics.

The extreme inter-individual variability is perhaps the most significant safety concern. The fact that one person can experience a profound psychedelic state at 4 mg while another barely notices 8 mg of the same batch means that any "recommended" dose is merely a starting point. First-time users of ALEPH compounds should begin at the lowest end of the range and have no plans for the remainder of the day or the following morning.
