# Grease Monkey

## Overview

Grease Monkey is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Gorilla Glue #4** with **Cookies and Cream**. Bred by Exotic Genetix, it has gained recognition for its potent sedative effects, high resin production, and distinctive fuel-forward aroma profile.

The strain delivers heavy body-centered effects that make it popular among medical cannabis patients seeking relief from chronic pain, insomnia, and muscle spasms. Its THC content typically ranges from 20–28%, placing it among the higher-potency strains available in legal markets.

Grease Monkey was awarded multiple cannabis competition accolades following its introduction in the mid-2010s, helping establish Exotic Genetix as a prominent breeder in the Pacific Northwest cannabis scene.

## Lineage

Grease Monkey is a cross of two highly regarded parent strains, combining the resin production and potency of Gorilla Glue #4 with the sweet cookie terpene profile of Cookies and Cream.

| Parent | Ratio | Key Contributions |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Gorilla Glue #4 | 50% | Potency, resin volume, sedative effects, fuel aroma |
| Cookies and Cream | 50% | Sweet vanilla terpenes, bud density, cookie flavor |

Gorilla Glue #4 (GG4) itself is a cross of Chem's Sister, Sour Dubb, and Chocolate Diesel. Cookies and Cream descends from the Girl Scout Cookies lineage crossed with a selected phenotype. This combined genetic background produces a complex terpene profile that blends diesel fuel, sweet vanilla, and earthy undertones.

The strain is typically reported as approximately 60–70% indica / 30–40% sativa, though exact ratios vary by phenotypic expression and breeder selection. Most phenotypes exhibit strongly indica-leaning growth patterns and effects.

## Appearance

Grease Monkey buds are medium to large in size, with a dense, chunky structure characteristic of indica-dominant genetics. The calyxes are tightly packed with minimal visible stem space between them.

Coloration ranges from deep forest green to slightly purple-tinted in cooler growing conditions. The buds are heavily coated in a thick layer of white trichomes, giving them a frosted, almost sticky appearance that inspired the strain's name.

Orange pistils are abundant, contrasting sharply against the dark green calyxes. The trichome coverage extends to the surrounding sugar leaves, making this an excellent candidate for hash and concentrate production.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma of Grease Monkey is dominated by pungent diesel fuel notes inherited from the Gorilla Glue lineage, layered over a creamy vanilla sweetness from the Cookies and Cream parent.

Primary aroma notes include diesel, skunk, and sweet earth, with secondary notes of vanilla, coffee, and subtle nuttiness. The scent is strong and lingers, making it relatively pungent compared to many modern hybrid strains.

The flavor profile mirrors the aroma, delivering an initial wave of diesel and earthy hash on the inhale, followed by a creamy vanilla exhale with hints of roasted coffee and dark chocolate.

## Terpene Profile

Grease Monkey's terpene profile is dominated by caryophyllene, myrcene, and limonene, with secondary contributions from humulene and linalool.

- **Caryophyllene** (0.8–1.5%) — Peppery, spicy notes; binds to CB2 receptors for anti-inflammatory effects
- **Myrcene** (0.5–1.2%) — Earthy, musky base; enhances sedation and body effects
- **Limonene** (0.3–0.8%) — Citrus brightness; mood elevation and stress relief
- **Humulene** (0.2–0.5%) — Woody, earthy undertones; appetite suppression
- **Linalool** (0.1–0.3%) — Floral notes; anxiolytic and sedative properties

The high caryophyllene content is notable and contributes to the strain's reported anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects through direct CB2 receptor activation independent of cannabinoid pathways.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range |
|-------------|---------------|
| THC | 20–28% |
| CBD | <0.1% |
| CBG | 0.1–0.3% |
| THCV | <0.1% |

Grease Monkey is a high-THC, low-CBD strain. The dominant psychoactive effects come from delta-9-THC, with minimal modulation from CBD or other minor cannabinoids.

Testing data from licensed dispensaries consistently places Grease Monkey in the 22–26% THC range for well-cultivated indoor flower. Outdoor-grown specimens tend toward the lower end of the THC spectrum.

## Effects

Onset is relatively rapid, with initial effects felt within 5–10 minutes of inhalation. The experience progresses from an initial cerebral uplift to a deep, full-body relaxation that intensifies over 30–60 minutes.

The indica-dominant effects make Grease Monkey well-suited for evening and nighttime use. Common reported effects include deep physical relaxation, couch-lock, euphoria, increased appetite, and drowsiness.

Medical applications include chronic pain management, insomnia, muscle spasms, anxiety, and stress relief. The combination of high THC with caryophyllene-driven CB2 activation provides a dual mechanism for analgesia.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness. At higher doses, users may experience anxiety, paranoia, or excessive sedation. Novice consumers should approach this strain cautiously due to its potency.

## Cultivation

Grease Monkey is considered a moderately difficult strain to cultivate, requiring attention to nutrient management and environmental control to achieve optimal resin production.

**Flowering time** is 8–9 weeks indoors. **Indoor yield** averages 300–400 g/m². **Outdoor yield** reaches 400–500 g/plant, with harvest typically in late September to early October in northern temperate climates.

The strain responds well to training techniques including low-stress training (LST), screen of green (ScrOG), and topping. It benefits from moderate nutrient levels during vegetative growth and reduced nitrogen during flowering.

Grease Monkey is relatively resistant to common molds and pests but can be sensitive to high humidity during late flowering. Maintaining humidity below 50% in the final weeks is recommended to prevent bud rot.

The strain expresses a tendency toward significant stretch during the first three weeks of flowering, so growers should account for vertical space in indoor setups. Pre-flowering height control through topping or supercropping is recommended.

## Notable Phenotypes

Several phenotypic variants of Grease Monkey have emerged in commercial markets. The "Original Cut" from Exotic Genetix remains the most sought-after, characterized by the strongest diesel-cookie terpene expression and highest resin production.

A "Purple Grease Monkey" phenotype has been documented that expresses deeper violet coloration in cooler nighttime temperatures. This variant retains the same cannabinoid and terpene profile but offers enhanced visual appeal.

Cross-breeding derivatives include **Grease Monkey OG** (Grease Monkey × OG Kush), which adds more citrus and pine notes, and **Sticky Monkey** (Grease Monkey × White Widow), which further amplifies resin production.
