## Grease Monkey

## Overview

Grease Monkey is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **[[gorilla-glue]] #4** with **Cookies and Cream**. Bred by Exotic Genetix, it has gained recognition for its potent sedative effects, high [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]] cannabis scene.

## Lineage

Grease Monkey is a cross of two highly regarded parent strains, combining the [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] of Cookies and Cream.

| Parent | Ratio | Key Contributions |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Gorilla Glue #4 | 50% | Potency, resin volume, sedative effects, fuel aroma |
| Cookies and Cream | 50% | Sweet vanilla terpenes, bud density, cookie flavor |

Gorilla Glue #4 (GG4) itself is a cross of Chem's Sister, Sour Dubb, and Chocolate Diesel. Cookies and Cream descends from the [[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]].

Testing data from licensed dispensaries consistently places Grease Monkey in the 22–26% THC range for well-cultivated indoor flower. Outdoor-grown specimens tend toward the lower end of the THC spectrum.
