## Description

Psilocybin alcohol extraction is a technique described in the [[pf-tek]] for concentrating the psychoactive compounds (primarily psilocybin) from dried *[[basidiocarp-four-stage-development-classification-psilocybe-cubensis-badham-1982]]* mushrooms into a potent alcohol-based tincture or "magic mushroom liqueur." The method uses pure 200-proof (anhydrous) ethyl alcohol to extract psilocybin from pulverized dried mushrooms, followed by evaporation to achieve the desired concentration. The entire process uses only mushrooms and food-grade alcohol.

## Classification

- **Category:** Post-harvest processing / extraction technique
- **Type:** Alcohol-based solvent extraction
- **Solvent:** 200-proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol (preferred) or 190-proof ethyl alcohol
- **Product:** Concentrated psilocybin tincture ("psilo-liqueur")
- **Complexity:** Intermediate; requires careful handling of flammable materials

## Key Facts

- The extraction uses pure 200-proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol as the solvent — chosen because it contains no water, which is important for [[pf-tek-evaporation-concentration-extraction]].
- 190-proof alcohol (95% ethyl, 5% water) is acceptable but the 5% residual water is a "negative, especially when evaporated down."
- Mushrooms must be thoroughly dried (rock hard) before extraction to allow proper pulverization.
- The recommended [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]] is at least several grams of dried mushrooms to make the process "worthwhile and effective."
- Only pre-sporulation mushrooms (harvested before or just as caps open, cool-dried with desiccant) should be used for maximum psilocybin content.

## Extraction Procedure

**Step 1 — Pulverization:**
- Place dried mushrooms in a small strong zip-lock freezer bag.
- Cover the bag with a magazine for protection.
- Pound with the rubber heel of a large shoe until pulverized.

**Step 2 — Soaking:**
- Pour pulverized material into a bottle with a tight-fitting, leak-proof cap.
- Add enough alcohol to cover the material, creating a loose slurry.
- Shake well and let sit for 24 hours.
- Shake periodically to activate the extraction.

**Step 3 — Filtration:**
- Use inexpensive dust-pollen masks as filters (available at hardware, drug, and paint stores).
- Fashion the filter mask over the mouth of a drinking glass.
- Pour slurry into the filter and squeeze to extract the alcohol.
- Store the filtered alcohol extract in a fresh bottle.

**Step 4 — Secondary Extraction:**
- Retrieve the mushroom material from the filters.
- Resoak with fresh alcohol.
- Repeat the filtration process for secondary reclamation.

**Step 5 — Evaporation and Concentration:**
- Combine all alcohol extracts into a glass.
- Position a small electric fan (clip-on desk fans ideal) to blow directly down into the glass, rippling the surface.
- Evaporation takes several hours; more extract requires longer time.
- As alcohol evaporates and the level recedes, wash residue from glass walls back into solution.
- Continue until desired concentration is achieved.
- Target: 1–3+ grams of dried mushroom material per 1 cc of final extract.

## Safety Considerations

- **Flammability:** Pure alcohol is very flammable — keep all flames and ignition sources away during the process.
- **Fumes:** Alcohol fumes generated during evaporation are described as harmless because the alcohol is a non-poisonous drinkable spirit, but adequate ventilation is prudent.
- **Accuracy:** Dispense the final tincture with a syringe for accurate dosing. "A slight over calculation could put a slightly reluctant psychonaut on pluto when only the moon was targeted."

## Storage

- Store the concentrated "psilo-liqueur" in small screw-cap bottles or vials.
- Keep in the freezer — alcohol doesn"t freeze solid and remains liquid.
- The extract has a distinctive "pungent mushroomy aroma (like fungi perfume)."

## Procuring 200-Proof Alcohol

- Available at full-service liquor stores that supply hospitals and laboratories.
- In many US states, a special permit from the state liquor board is required (typically $5–10 fee).
- On the permit application, state the use as "non-toxic surface sterilizing plus herb extraction — preservation — tincture — and perfume making."

## Relevance to Cultivation

The alcohol extraction technique is significant for several reasons:

- **Concentration:** Allows precise control over dosage by concentrating variable mushroom material into a standardized liquid form.
- **Preservation:** Alcohol tinctures may have longer shelf life than dried mushrooms when stored properly.
- **Consumption:** Liquid form allows for more controlled and rapid onset compared to eating dried mushrooms.
- **Research connection:** The technique is referenced alongside Jochen Gartz's research on tryptamine techniques, suggesting scientific interest in extraction methods.
- **[[cannabis-potency-maximization]]:** When combined with the PF TEK's emphasis on harvesting young specimens and cool drying, the extraction captures the maximum possible alkaloid content.
