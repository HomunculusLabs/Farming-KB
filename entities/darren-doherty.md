# Darren Doherty

## Overview

**Darren J. Doherty** is an Australian permaculture designer, regenerative agriculture consultant, and educator widely regarded as the world's foremost practitioner and advocate of **Keyline Design®** in the modern era. Based in Bendigo, Victoria, Australia, Doherty is the founder and director of **Regrarians Ltd.** (formerly RegenAG), an organization dedicated to the large-scale adoption of regenerative agriculture practices. Over a career spanning more than three decades, Doherty has designed and overseen the implementation of Keyline-based water management and soil regeneration systems on hundreds of farms and ranches across six continents. His work bridges the gap between P.A. Yeomans' original Keyline principles (developed in the 1950s) and contemporary regenerative agriculture, integrating Holistic Management, carbon farming, planned grazing, and ecological design into a comprehensive framework for broadacre landscape regeneration.

Doherty is perhaps best known for developing the **Regrarians Platform** — a systematic, step-by-step methodology for farm planning and design that organizes the regeneration of agricultural landscapes into ten integrated layers: climate, geography, water, access, forestry, buildings, fencing, soils, economy, and energy. This framework has been adopted by thousands of farmers, ranchers, and land managers worldwide and represents one of the most practical and widely implemented approaches to regenerative agriculture at scale.

## Biography

### Early Life and Education

Doherty grew up in rural Victoria, Australia, where he developed an early connection to the land and an interest in agriculture and natural systems. He completed a Permaculture Design Certificate (PDC) in the early 1990s and rapidly became one of the most active permaculture practitioners in Australia. His early exposure to the degraded landscapes of southeastern Australia — shaped by decades of intensive agriculture, overgrazing, and soil erosion — instilled a deep commitment to landscape restoration and regenerative land management.

### Career Development

- **1990s–2000s:** Established himself as a leading permaculture design consultant in Australia, working on diverse projects ranging from small homesteads to large-scale broadacre properties. During this period, he became deeply interested in the work of P.A. Yeomans and Keyline Design, recognizing its potential for addressing landscape hydration and soil health at a scale that traditional permaculture approaches often struggled to reach.
- **2000s:** Began consulting internationally, bringing Keyline Design to farms and ranches in North America, Europe, Africa, and Latin America. His international work exposed him to a wide range of farming systems, climates, and cultural contexts, which informed the development of his integrated approach to regenerative agriculture.
- **2008–2012:** Founded RegenAG (Regenerative Agriculture), an organization that delivered hands-on workshops and farm planning courses across Australia and internationally. RegenAG became one of the most influential regenerative agriculture training platforms in the Southern Hemisphere.
- **2013–present:** Rebranded and expanded the organization as **Regrarians Ltd.**, developing and refining the Regrarians Platform. The Regrarians platform represents the culmination of Doherty's decades of practical experience, organizing farm design and regeneration into a systematic, replicable methodology. He has since delivered Regrarians workshops and masterclasses in over 50 countries.

## Key Contributions

### Keyline Design Revival and Modernization

Doherty's most significant contribution is the revival, modernization, and global dissemination of Keyline Design — a system of land management developed by Australian engineer and farmer Percival Alfred (P.A.) Yeomans in the 1940s–1950s. Keyline Design uses the topographic features of a landscape (specifically the "keypoint" and "keyline" — the point and contour line where water begins to accumulate and flow on a valley slope) to optimally place roads, fences, tree lines, dams, and irrigation channels in a coordinated pattern that maximizes the retention and even distribution of water across the landscape.

Doherty's contributions to Keyline Design include:

- **Operationalization:** Translating Yeomans' original texts (which were dense, technical, and somewhat inaccessible) into practical, field-ready protocols that farmers and contractors could implement with standard earthmoving equipment
- **Integration with modern practices:** Combining Keyline water management with Holistic Management (Allan Savory), planned grazing, pasture cropping, compost tea application, biofertilizer programs, and carbon farming methodologies
- **Technology adaptation:** Incorporating GPS-guided machinery, GIS mapping, drone surveying, and precision agriculture tools into Keyline design and implementation workflows
- **Climate adaptation:** Adapting Keyline principles for a range of climate zones beyond the Australian context in which they were originally developed, including humid temperate, Mediterranean, semi-arid, and tropical environments

### The Regrarians Platform

The Regrarians Platform organizes farm design and regeneration into ten sequential "layers," each building upon the previous:

1. **Climate:** Understanding the macro-climatic context (rainfall, temperature, evaporation, wind, frost risk) that constrains and enables all subsequent design decisions
2. **Geography:** Analyzing landform, topography, geology, and aspect to understand the inherent patterns and potential of the landscape
3. **Water:** Designing for maximum water retention, infiltration, and distribution using Keyline principles, swales, contour banks, dams, and reticulation systems
4. **Access:** Planning roads, tracks, and laneways to follow landform contours, minimize erosion, and provide efficient movement across the property
5. **Forestry:** Designing integrated tree systems — shelterbelts, woodlots, agroforestry, and perennial polycultures — that serve multiple functions (windbreaks, livestock shade, timber, fodder, biodiversity)
6. **Buildings:** Siting homesteads, sheds, and infrastructure based on the access and water layers, optimizing for solar access, views, drainage, and convenience
7. **Fencing:** Designing fencing systems that facilitate planned grazing, cell grazing, and stock movement aligned with the water and access infrastructure
8. **Soils:** Implementing soil health programs — biological stimulation, cover cropping, minimum disturbance, compost application, and targeted mineral amendment — to regenerate the living soil ecosystem
9. **Economy:** Developing diverse, resilient farm enterprises and financial planning that supports long-term regenerative management
10. **Energy:** Designing for energy efficiency and renewable energy production (solar, wind, biomass) across the farm system

### Carbon Farming Advocacy

Doherty has been a prominent advocate for carbon farming — the practice of managing agricultural land to sequester atmospheric carbon dioxide in soils and biomass. He has worked with governments, industry organizations, and research institutions in Australia and internationally to develop carbon farming methodologies, measurement protocols, and incentive programs. His approach emphasizes that profitable farming and soil carbon sequestration are not only compatible but synergistic — healthier soils produce more forage, support more livestock, generate more income, and store more carbon in a positive feedback loop.

## Notable Projects

- **Broadacre Keyline installations:** Doherty has supervised the design and implementation of Keyline-based water management systems on properties ranging from 50 to 50,000+ hectares across Australia, the United States, Canada, South Africa, Argentina, Chile, and Europe. These projects typically involve the construction of contour-based tree planting strips, subsoiling along keyline contours, and strategic water storage and distribution infrastructure.
- **Farm-scale RegenAG demonstration properties:** Through RegenAG and Regrarians, Doherty has established or contributed to numerous demonstration farms that showcase regenerative practices to local farming communities.
- **International development work:** Has consulted on regenerative agriculture projects for organizations including the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), the World Bank, and various national agricultural research agencies.
- **Regrarians Mega-Scan Project:** A large-scale landscape analysis initiative using drone technology and GIS to create detailed topographic and hydrological maps of farm properties, enabling precise Keyline design and implementation.

## Philosophy and Approach

Doherty's approach to regenerative agriculture is characterized by several key principles:

- **Scale-appropriate design:** While rooted in permaculture ethics, Doherty explicitly focuses on broadacre farming and ranching at scales where many permaculture practitioners feel less comfortable. His work demonstrates that ecological design principles are equally applicable — and critically necessary — at the scale of commercial agriculture.
- **Pragmatism over ideology:** Doherty emphasizes practical, profitable outcomes over dogmatic adherence to any single methodology. He integrates tools and techniques from permaculture, Keyline Design, Holistic Management, conventional agronomy, and other disciplines as appropriate to the context.
- **Economic viability:** A central tenet of Doherty's work is that regenerative agriculture must be economically viable for the farmer. He argues that environmental outcomes (soil health, biodiversity, water retention, carbon sequestration) are most reliably achieved when they are aligned with — rather than opposed to — farm profitability.
- **Landscape-scale thinking:** Doherty advocates for thinking at the watershed and landscape scale, recognizing that individual farm decisions aggregate to determine the ecological health of entire catchments and bioregions.
- **Farmer-led innovation:** He consistently positions farmers as the primary agents of landscape change, providing them with design frameworks, decision-making tools, and technical support rather than prescribing top-down solutions.

## Influence and Legacy

Doherty's influence on the regenerative agriculture movement is substantial and growing:

- He has trained thousands of farmers, ranchers, and agricultural professionals through RegenAG and Regrarians workshops, masterclasses, and consulting engagements
- His work has been instrumental in the revival of interest in Keyline Design, which had declined significantly since P.A. Yeomans' death in 1984
- The Regrarians Platform has become one of the most widely adopted systematic frameworks for regenerative farm design
- His advocacy for carbon farming has contributed to the development of carbon credit methodologies and incentive programs in Australia and other countries
- Through his YouTube channel and social media presence, Doherty has built one of the largest audiences for regenerative agriculture education in the English-speaking world
