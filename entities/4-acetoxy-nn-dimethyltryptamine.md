# 4-Acetoxy-N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (4-AcO-DMT)

4-Acetoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine (4-AcO-DMT), also known as O-acetylpsilocin or synthetically as "psilacetin," is a synthetic tryptamine compound and a prodrug of [[dimethyltryptamine]].

## Chemical Structure and Properties

4-AcO-DMT is an indole-ethylamine compound with the molecular formula C₁₄H₁₈N₂O₂ and a molecular weight of 246.31 g/mol. The IUPAC name is [3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl] acetate. The structure consists of an indole ring with a dimethylaminoethyl chain at the 3-position and an acetoxy group at the 4-position of the benzene ring.

## Forms and Preparations

4-AcO-DMT is encountered in several forms in the research chemical and underground markets:

- **Freebase**: An oily liquid, rarely encountered in commercial preparations due to handling difficulties.
- **Fumarate salt**: The most common crystalline form, appearing as white to off-white crystals or powder. This is the most stable and well-characterized form.
- **Hydrochloride salt**: A crystalline powder, more hygroscopic than the fumarate.
- **Hemifumarate**: Some preparations use a hemifumarate salt, which has different solubility properties.

Purity of commercial preparations varies widely and should be verified by analytical testing before use in research settings. Adulterants and misidentified compounds have been reported in unregulated markets.
