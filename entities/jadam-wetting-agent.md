## JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA)

## Description

The JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) is an environment-friendly surfactant developed by Youngsang Cho as one of the two most important inventions of the JADAM organic farming system. Made from canola oil, caustic potash (potassium hydroxide), and water through a simple process that does not require heating, JWA serves as the essential base ingredient for virtually all JADAM pesticide formulations. Its invention was intentionally not patented, allowing farmers worldwide to produce it themselves at ultra-low cost. JWA enables agricultural sprays to fully wet, envelope, and coat target surfaces, which is essential for effective pest and disease control.

## Classification

- **Type**: Agricultural input / surfactant / wetting agent
- **System**: JADAM Organic Farming
- **Inventor**: Youngsang Cho
- **Ingredients**: Canola oil, caustic potash (KOH), water
- **Process**: Cold process (no heating required)
- **Patent status**: Intentionally unpatented, open knowledge

## Key Facts

- One of the two most important inventions of the JADAM system (along with JADAM Sulfur)
- Made without heating, unlike conventional soap-making processes
- Uses only ingredients permitted under organic production standards
- Serves as the base for combining with JS (JADAM Sulfur), JHS (JADAM Herb Solution), and JMS (JADAM Microorganism Solution) to create various pesticide formulations
- The most important single ingredient in making JADAM pesticides
- Can be produced by any farmer following simple instructions
- Intentionally not patented to allow all farmers of the world to benefit
- Functions by enabling spray solutions to fully wet and coat target surfaces including plant leaves, stems, and pest bodies
- Used in combinations: JS+JWA (germicide), JHS+JWA (insecticide), JMS+JWA (biological germicide), JS+JHS+JWA (combined pesticide)

## Formulation Combinations

JWA is combined with other JADAM inputs to create specific pesticide formulations. JS germicide uses JS+JWA for fungal disease control. JHS pesticide uses JHS+JWA for insect control. JMS pesticide uses JMS+JWA for biological pathogen control. JMS-JHS pesticide combines JMS+JHS+JWA for broad-spectrum control. JS-JHS pesticide combines JS+JHS+JWA for combined fungicidal and insecticidal action. The versatility of these combinations allows farmers to address virtually any pest or disease challenge using a small number of base ingredients.

## Importance in the JADAM System

Hankyu Cho, Youngsang"s father and founder of Natural Farming, predicted that "the making of wetting agent (surfactant) and pesticide at home with organically permitted substances will be a global sensation." Without JWA, the JADAM system of ultra-low-cost pesticide production would not be possible, as commercial wetting agents are expensive and often contain ingredients not permitted under organic standards. By developing an effective organic wetting agent that any farmer can make, JADAM removed one of the major cost barriers to organic pest management.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

JWA is used as a carrier for JADAM Microorganism Solution (JMS), which contains beneficial fungi and bacteria cultured from leaf mold. The wetting agent enables these beneficial microorganisms to effectively colonize plant surfaces when applied as foliar sprays, improving their efficacy as biological control agents against pathogenic fungi. Additionally, JWA's role in enabling effective coverage of plant surfaces is essential for the application of JS (JADAM Sulfur), which functions as a contact germicide against fungal pathogens including powdery mildew, downy mildew, and canker.
