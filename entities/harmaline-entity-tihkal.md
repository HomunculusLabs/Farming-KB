# Harmaline (3,4-Dihydroharmine)

TIHKAL entry #13. A beta-carboline alkaloid and potent monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) found in [[golden-guide-syrian-rue-and-maoi-plants]]) and Banisteriopsis caapi (ayahuasca). Harmaline is both psychoactive in its own right and pharmacologically significant as an enzyme inhibitor that enables [[turner-harmala-alkaloids-preparation-and-dosage]]) |

## Duration

5–8 hours (pure chemical); potentially longer with seed preparations.

## Qualitative Comments — Pure Harmaline

- **100 mg, orally**: "No effects." (on two occasions)
- **150 mg, orally**: "Rapid-onset intoxication. Unusual shimmering in lateral vision. Music missing the higher frequencies. Light food sat heavily. Sex was difficult."
- **175 mg, orally**: "Three areas of disturbance: obvious tracers (bright objects leave afterimages moving in opposite direction), higher frequencies of music attenuated and lower amplified, definite numbing in touch. No appetite."
- **200 mg, orally**: "Nausea and diarrhea. Fortunately they alternated. This is not my trip of choice."
- **300 mg, orally** (psychotherapy setting): "Disconnected images — a girl in front of a church, receiving the Host from an invisible hand. Irritation and disorientation. Facing a reality I cannot accept."
- **400 mg, orally**: "Completely immobilized and sick. Closed-eyed visuals yielded native women, organic colors and shapes, and a black panther."
- **500 mg, orally**: "Intense visual disturbances and complete collapse of motor coordination. Barely able to stagger to the bathroom. Tracers and weird visual ripplings with open eyes. Bothering disjointed eidetic imagery with eyes closed."

## Qualitative Comments — Peganum harmala Seeds

- **2 g seeds**: No effects.
- **5 g seeds**: Tinnitus, nystagmus, nausea and diarrhea. Intense closed-eye hallucinations — geometrical patterns evolving into concrete images, faces, movies, animal presences (snakes). "Fear circuits seemed turned off." Enhanced sex drive.
- **7 g seeds**: Very sick for 24 hours.
- **20 g seeds (extract)**: "The entire experience was a conflict between tripping and being sick."
- **28 g seeds (tea)**: "Zebra-like stripes of light and dark spiraling off window silhouettes. A pronounced auditory buzz. Vivid spontaneous imaginations — weird winged creatures, jungle-like places with vines and fountains."

## DMT Combinations

- **20 mg harmaline + 55 mg DMT**: Minimal — slight hypnogogic abstractions after 3 hours, adrenergic push.
- **50 mg harmaline + 60 mg DMT [20 min]**: No effects noted.
- **80 mg harmaline + 40 mg DMT [60 min]**: Quite a bit of visual activity, subtle onset, quick drop-off.
- **100 mg harmaline + 120 mg DMT [10 min]**: Full [[dmt-entity-tihkal]] — primary tryptamine potentiated by harmaline inhibition
- [[harm-reduction-guide]] — dietary restrictions required with MAOIs
