## Description

**Dano Gorsich** is a permaculture practitioner on **Moloka'i, Hawaii** who
developed a sub-tropical **[[chicken-tractor-system]]** at approximately 21°N
latitude. His integrated system combines mobile chicken pens with papaya
production, vegetable gardens, and egg sales, creating a self-reliant family
farm that generates income from both eggs and fruit. Mollison features Gorsich's
system as a model of tropical smallholder permaculture.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person — Permaculture practitioner / Smallholder farmer
- **Domain**: Tropical agriculture, Integrated animal-crop systems
- **Location**: Moloka'i, Hawaii, USA (Lat. 21°)
- **System type**: Chicken tractor + papaya + vegetable integration

## Key Facts

- Developed a **chicken tractor system** using 5 pens on 0.5 hectares.
- Chickens are **Australorp** breed — a hardy dual-purpose Australian breed.
- Chickens are moved from pen to pen over the year to clear weeds and grasses.
- Chickens derive most food from **fallen papaya, seed, insects, and greens**,
  reducing feed costs significantly.
- **6 weeks of vegetable growth** follows each chicken tractor clearing cycle.
- Maintains a **2.5-year-old papaya system** that provides fruit for market
  and chicken fodder simultaneously.
- Income from **eggs and papaya** at the local cooperative plus home garden
  largely support the Gorsich family.
- Uses the **Busby family** as collaborators in the system.

## System Design

1. **Mobile pens** (5 units) rotated across 0.5 ha throughout the year.
2. Chickens clear grass and weeds in each pen location.
3. After clearing, ground is immediately planted with vegetables.
4. **Vegetable phase** (6 weeks) produces food crops.
5. **Papaya system** (2.5 years) provides long-term fruit production, chicken
   fodder, and market income.
6. Closed-loop: chickens eat papaya waste → manure fertilises vegetables and
   papaya → vegetable waste feeds chickens.

## Relevance to Cultivation / Mycology

- Chicken manure from tractor pens provides **high-nitrogen compost activator**
  ideal for building fungal-dominated compost.
- The rotational clearing and replanting cycle creates repeated disturbance
  phases that can incorporate **mushroom substrate burial** between rotations.
- Papaya waste (stems, leaves, fallen fruit) provides **cellulose-rich organic
  matter** for [[soil-food-web]], accelerating decomposition.
