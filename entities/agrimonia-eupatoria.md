## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Plantae
- **Division:** Magnoliophyta
- **Class:** Magnoliopsida
- **Order:** Rosales
- **Family:** Rosaceae
- **Genus:** *Agrimonia*
- **Species:** *A. eupatoria*
- **Authority:** L. (1753)

The genus name derives from Greek *argemone* (a plant for eye
disorders); *eupatoria* honors Mithridates VI Eupator, king of Pontus,
renowned for herbal knowledge.

## Morphology

**Growth habit:** Erect perennial, 30–120 cm tall, rhizomatous
rootstock producing multiple stems from a single crown.

**Leaves:** Pinnate, 10–30 cm long, 3–6 pairs of serrated leaflets.
Upper surface dark green, underside densely silvery-hairy. Stipules
large and leaf-like at stem nodes.

**Flowers:** Small (6–8 mm), five-petaled, bright yellow, in dense
terminal spikes 5–15 cm long with prominent stamens. Blooms June–September.

## Cultivation

**Propagation:** Seed (stratification 4–6 weeks) or division in
spring/autumn. Seeds require light — surface sow. **Soil:**
Well-drained loam; tolerates poor sandy soils. pH 6.0–7.5.
**Light:** Full sun to light shade. **Water:** Drought-tolerant once
established; avoid waterlogging. **Spacing:** 30–45 cm.

**Harvest:** Aerial parts during flowering (June–August). Dry quickly
out of direct sunlight.

## Permaculture Applications

- **Pollinator support:** Long flowering period attracts bees,
  butterflies, and beneficial insects
- **Ground cover:** Dense basal rosette suppresses weeds
- **Dynamic accumulator:** Deep taproot mines subsoil minerals
  (calcium, potassium, silica)
- **Edge planting:** Thrives in zone 3–4 edges between cultivated
  and wild areas
- **Companion plant:** Non-competitive roots suit fruit tree
  understories
