# Black Domina

**Black Domina** is a pure indica cannabis strain bred by Sensi Seeds of Amsterdam. Created by crossing four distinct indica parent lines — [[afghani-cannabis]], Ortega, and a [[myrcene]]** | Dominant; earthy, musky base with strong sedative properties |
| **[[pinene]]** | Subtle pine accent; may counteract some sedative effects |
| **[[humulene]]** | Woody, earthy depth; appetite suppression |

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Deep, earthy, and pungent with pronounced hash-like qualities. The scent is dark and heavy, reminiscent of Afghani hashish.
- **Flavor:** Rich and earthy on the inhale with spicy, peppery notes. The smoke is thick and smooth with a slightly sweet undertone.
- **Aftertaste:** Long-lasting hash-like finish with hints of dark chocolate and spice.

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

Black Domina delivers a classic heavy indica experience. The effects are predominantly physical, with minimal cerebral stimulation. It is best suited for evening or nighttime use due to its strongly sedative properties.

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes; immediate body warmth and physical heaviness
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; deep relaxation and couch-lock
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours with prolonged body sedation

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Profound full-body relaxation and muscle relief
- Strong sedative effects and sleep promotion
- Euphoric body buzz
- Stress and tension relief
- Appetite stimulation
- Pain management

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes
- Heavy couch-lock and lethargy
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for grogginess the following morning
