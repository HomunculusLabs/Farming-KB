# Black Domina

**Black Domina** is a pure indica cannabis strain bred by Sensi Seeds of Amsterdam. Created by crossing four distinct indica parent lines — [[northern-lights]], [[afghani-cannabis]], Ortega, and a [[hashish-production-methods]] — Black Domina is renowned for its exceptionally dark foliage, dense resin production, and powerful sedative effects. The strain is a staple among indica enthusiasts and medical patients seeking potent physical relief.

## Genetic Lineage

Black Domina represents a carefully constructed indica pedigree, combining some of the most respected Afghan-influenced genetics in cannabis history.

| Parent | Role | Origin |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Northern Lights** | Structure, resin | Pacific Northwest / Netherlands |
| **Afghani** | Potency, density | Afghanistan landrace |
| **Ortega** | Flavor, yield | Unknown indica |
| **Hash Plant** | Resin, aroma | Hindu Kush region |

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Breeder** | Sensi Seeds, Netherlands |
| **Year** | Mid-to-late 1990s |
| **Type** | Pure indica |
| **THC content** | 15–20% |
| **CBD content** | Low (<0.5%) |

The four-way cross produces a genetically stable indica with remarkably uniform phenotype expression. Plants are highly consistent in growth pattern, flowering time, and effect profile across generations.

## Terpene Profile

Black Domina's terpene profile is characteristic of heavy indica genetics, dominated by earthy, hash-like aromatics.

| Terpene | Role |
|---------|------|
| **[[myrcene]]** | Dominant; earthy, musky base with strong sedative properties |
| **[[beta-caryophyllene]]** | Pepper and spice; contributes to the hash-like aroma |
| **[[pinene]]** | Subtle pine accent; may counteract some sedative effects |
| **[[humulene]]** | Woody, earthy depth; appetite suppression |

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Deep, earthy, and pungent with pronounced hash-like qualities. The scent is dark and heavy, reminiscent of Afghani hashish.
- **Flavor:** Rich and earthy on the inhale with spicy, peppery notes. The smoke is thick and smooth with a slightly sweet undertone.
- **Aftertaste:** Long-lasting hash-like finish with hints of dark chocolate and spice.

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

Black Domina delivers a classic heavy indica experience. The effects are predominantly physical, with minimal cerebral stimulation. It is best suited for evening or nighttime use due to its strongly sedative properties.

- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes; immediate body warmth and physical heaviness
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; deep relaxation and couch-lock
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours with prolonged body sedation

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Profound full-body relaxation and muscle relief
- Strong sedative effects and sleep promotion
- Euphoric body buzz
- Stress and tension relief
- Appetite stimulation
- Pain management

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes
- Heavy couch-lock and lethargy
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for grogginess the following morning

### Medical Applications

Black Domina is highly valued in medical cannabis programs for its potent therapeutic effects. Its heavy indica profile makes it one of the most effective strains for physical conditions.

- **Chronic pain:** Deep analgesic properties for [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]
- **Insomnia:** Powerful sleep aid for patients with sleep disorders
- **Muscle spasms:** Significant relief for spasticity and muscle tension
- **Anxiety:** Calming body effects reduce physical anxiety symptoms
- **Appetite stimulation:** Effective for [[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]]
- **PTSD:** Some patients report relief from physical hyperarousal symptoms

## Growing Information

Black Domina is considered a straightforward strain to cultivate, making it accessible to growers of various experience levels. Its compact, indica growth pattern and reliable flowering make it a popular choice for indoor cultivation.

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Flowering time** | 6–8 weeks (very fast for an indica) |
| **Indoor yield** | 14–18 oz/m² |
| **Outdoor yield** | Moderate |
| **Height** | Short to medium (80–120 cm) |
| **Difficulty** | Easy to moderate |
| **Preferred climate** | Temperate to warm |

### Cultivation Notes

Black Domina is one of the faster-flowering heavy indicas available. Its short flowering time and compact stature make it ideal for growers seeking quick turnaround in limited space. The dark, almost black foliage that gives the strain its name becomes most pronounced during late flowering.

- **Foliage:** Leaves darken significantly during flowering; this is normal and not a sign of nutrient deficiency
- **Resin production:** Extremely heavy — one of the most resinous strains from Sensi Seeds
- **Training:** Minimal training needed due to natural bushy structure; responds well to basic pruning
- **Nutrients:** Standard indica feeding schedule; avoid excess nitrogen during flowering
- **Climate:** Low humidity during flowering is critical — dense buds are prone to mold
- **Harvest:** 6–8 week flowering window; monitor [[trichome-development-and-morphology]] for optimal harvest at approximately 50% amber

## Cultural Significance

Black Domina represents the pinnacle of Sensi Seeds' indica breeding program. By combining four legendary indica lines, the strain encapsulates decades of selective breeding into a single cultivar. Its hash-like qualities pay homage to the traditional cannabis products of Central Asia.

The strain has influenced modern indica breeding, particularly in the development of dark-leaf, high-resin cultivars. Its reliable genetics and consistent performance have made it a reference standard for indica potency and production.
