# Zookies

## Overview

**Zookies** is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **Animal Cookies** (a GSC phenotype) with **Gorilla Glue #4 (GG4)**. Bred by **Alien Labs** — a California-based brand renowned for exotic, high-potency cultivars — Zookies was released in the late 2010s and rapidly gained a cult following in the premium craft cannabis market.
The strain is celebrated for its extraordinary resin production, complex "gas meets cookie dough" aroma, and a potent, well-rounded high that balances cerebral euphoria with deep physical relaxation.

The name "Zookies" is a playful portmanteau of "Zoo" (referencing the Animal Cookies lineage) and "Cookies." THC content is high, typically ranging from 20–30%, with select phenotypes exceeding 32%.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Animal Cookies — a selected phenotype of Girl Scout Cookies (OG Kush × Durban Poison) backcrossed or selected for enhanced cookie terpene profile. Contributed cookie-dough sweetness, dense bud structure, and a heavy relaxing body component.
- **Male parent:** Gorilla Glue #4 (Chem's Sister × Sour Dubb × Chocolate Diesel) — a legendary hybrid known for its astronomical resin production, pungent diesel/pine aroma, and knockout potency. Contributed the "gas" terpene notes, extreme trichome coverage, and cerebral intensity.
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 50/50 indica-sativa balance, though the experience skews slightly indica-dominant in physical effects.
- **Breeder:** Alien Labs (Northern California). The brand is known for small-batch, hand-selected phenotypes with distinctive bag appeal and exotic terpene profiles.
- **Genetic influence:** Both parent strains are multi-award winners — GG4 won multiple High Times Cannabis Cups; Animal Cookies is a staple of the West Coast cookie lineage.

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Dense, chunky, and compact with a classic indica-leaning structure. Calyxes are swollen and tightly packed, forming rock-hard nuggets that resist compression.
- **Color:** Deep forest green to dark olive base with prominent violet and lavender undertones, especially in cooler growing conditions. Pistils are long, curly, and amber-orange, weaving through the calyxes in dense clusters.
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptional — one of Zookies' defining characteristics. Flowers are encased in a thick layer of translucent-to-milky trichomes with amber-capped glandular heads. Under magnification, both capitate-stalked and capitate-sessile trichomes are densely packed across calyxes, sugar leaves, and even pistils. This extreme resin density makes Zookies a favorite for solventless extraction.
- **Bag appeal:** Extremely high — the combination of dark coloration, bright pistils, and frosty trichome layer gives a premium, "craft" appearance.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Primary aroma:** Pungent diesel fuel and gasoline ("gas") — the dominant note from the GG4 lineage, immediately apparent on opening a jar.
- **Secondary notes:** Sweet cookie dough and vanilla — contributed by the Animal Cookies parent. This creates a distinctive sweet-and-funky contrast that is Zookies' signature.
- **Tertiary notes:** Earthy, nutty, and slightly chocolatey undertones; hints of skunk and pepper on the back end.
- **Flavor:** Dense, coating smoke with a sweet cookie forefront that transitions into sharp diesel and chemical notes on the exhale. The aftertaste lingers with sweet vanilla and earthy gas. When vaporized at lower temperatures (180–195°C), the cookie and sweetness are more prominent; higher temperatures bring out the harsh diesel.

## Terpene Profile

- **Caryophyllene (0.8–1.6%):** The dominant terpene; responsible for the peppery, spicy notes and the characteristic "gas" quality. As a CB2 receptor agonist, it contributes anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Limonene (0.5–1.2%):** Adds citrus brightness that balances the heavy diesel; contributes mood elevation and stress relief.
- **Myrcene (0.5–1.1%):** Provides the earthy, herbal base and enhances sedative body effects.
- **Humulene (0.2–0.5%):** Earthy, woody note; contributes appetite regulation and anti-inflammatory properties.
- **Linalool (0.1–0.4%):** Adds subtle floral sweetness and anxiolytic properties.
- **Pinene (0.1–0.3%):** Low to moderate; faint pine notes that provide slight mental clarity counterbalance.
- **Geraniol (trace):** Occasionally detected in select phenotypes, adding rosy, fruity complexity.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 20–30% (tested flower); many samples test above 25%. Select cuttings from Alien Labs have tested above 32%.
- **CBD:** <0.1% — negligible.
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%.
- **THCV:** 0.05–0.2% — trace amounts.
- **CBC:** 0.05–0.2%.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid (2–5 minutes smoked/vaporized); cerebral effects manifest first, followed by a spreading body high over 15–30 minutes.
- **Cerebral:** Euphoric, uplifting, and mildly dissociative. Users report enhanced mood, sociability, and creative thinking. The GG4 influence provides a "spacey," floating mental quality without being overwhelming or anxious.
- **Physical:** Deep, relaxing body stone that builds progressively. Muscle tension melts away; couch-lock is common at moderate-to-high doses. The Animal Cookies influence is evident in the heavy, enveloping body component.
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours smoked; 4–6 hours via edibles or concentrates.
- **Progression:** Starts as an uplifting, giggly hybrid high and gradually transitions into a sedative, body-heavy experience. The comedown is smooth and sleep-promoting.

## Medical Applications

- **Chronic pain:** Effective for deep-tissue pain, joint inflammation, neuropathy, and muscle spasms due to the combination of caryophyllene and myrcene with high THC.
- **Insomnia:** The sedative comedown makes it suitable for patients with sleep-onset difficulties. Best consumed 1–2 hours before intended sleep.
- **Stress and anxiety:** The euphoric onset provides rapid relief from acute stress and generalized anxiety, though high doses may paradoxically increase anxiety in susceptible individuals.
- **Appetite stimulation:** Strong munchie effect — useful for patients with appetite loss from chemotherapy, HIV, or eating disorders.
- **PTSD:** Some patients report temporary relief from intrusive thoughts and hypervigilance during the experience.

## Side Effects

- **Common:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, increased appetite, drowsiness.
- **Moderate:** Anxiety at high doses, paranoia (especially in inexperienced users), dizziness, mild tachycardia.
- **Uncommon:** Headache, nausea (rare), headaches from dehydration.
- **Contraindications:** Not recommended for daytime functional use due to sedative effects. Use caution with a history of psychosis or severe anxiety.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks (56–63 days) indoors; early to mid-October outdoors.
- **Yield:** Moderate — 300–450 g/m² indoors. Yield is secondary to quality with this strain; trichome density and terpene production are the primary selection criteria.
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced — requires careful nutrient management (lower nitrogen during flowering), good airflow to prevent mold in dense buds, and patience during the final 2 weeks of flowering for optimal trichome maturation.
- **Preferred medium:** Living soil or organic soilless; organic cultivation dramatically enhances terpene complexity and expression.
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, dry climates with low humidity during flowering. Dense bud structure makes it susceptible to botrytis in humid environments.
- **Training:** Responds well to LST, SCROG, and mainlining. Topping once or twice in early vegetative stage promotes a more even canopy.
- **Flush:** Minimum 2-week flush recommended for best flavor.

## Notable Phenotypes

- **"Gas pheno":** Heavily GG4-dominant expression with intense diesel/fuel aroma and more cerebral, energetic effects. Darker coloration and higher trichome density.
- **"Cookie pheno":** More Animal Cookies-dominant with sweeter vanilla-dough aroma and heavier, more sedative body effects. Purple coloration more pronounced. Considered the most desirable by flavor-focused consumers.
- **"Alien Labs cut":** The breeder's selected phenotype, considered the gold standard. Exceptionally balanced terpene profile with both gas and cookie notes equally expressed; premium resin production and bag appeal.

## Extraction and Processing

Zookies is among the most sought-after strains for solventless extraction due to its enormous resin output.
Fresh-frozen bubble hash yields consistently reach 4–6% by weight at 120μ+ grade, with some phenotypes exceeding 7%.
Live rosin pressed from Zookies hash produces a terpene-rich, golden-brown sap with a complex flavor profile that retains both the cookie sweetness and diesel gas notes.
The strain's balanced terpene-to-cannabinoid ratio and thick glandular trichome heads make it ideal for full-melt ice water extraction.
Premium Zookies rosin commands top-tier pricing in California and Oregon craft concentrate markets, often exceeding $60–80/gram retail for first-press material.

## Market Position and Availability

Zookies occupies a premium market segment, typically priced above average due to Alien Labs branding and limited availability.
Genetically stable seeds are available from select breeders, though the original Alien Labs cut is clone-only and closely held.
The strain's popularity has spawned crosses including Zkittlez × Zookies ("Zoap" lineage), Zookies × Runtz, and various backcrosses.
Competition-level hash makers frequently feature Zookies in extraction competitions due to its reliable yield and terpene preservation through processing.
