# Lambsbread

**Lambsbread** (also spelled Lamb's Bread or Lamb's Breath) is a legendary
sativa [[southeast-asian-mushroom-tourism-bali]] and South
  Indian sativa genetics brought to the Caribbean during the colonial era
- **Breeder:** Landrace — developed through natural selection by Jamaican

### Primary Aromas
- **Herbal, grassy** — fresh-cut vegetation, reminiscent of a tropical
  meadow
- **Earthy, woody undertones** — natural soil and plant matter notes
- **Subtle citrus and spice** — background notes from [[limonene]] and
  caryophyllene

### Flavor
On inhalation: smooth, herbal, and slightly sweet with vegetal notes.
On exhalation: earthy with a lingering spicy-pepper finish and faint
tropical fruit character.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **THC** | 15–22% | Varies by grower and phenotype |
| **CBD** | 0.1–0.5% | Low, though some phenotypes approach 1% |
| **CBN** | <0.1% | Negligible in fresh flower |
| **THCV** | 0.1–0.3% | Occasionally detectable, contributes to energy |

Traditional Jamaican landraces generally test lower in THC than modern
hybrids, but Lambsbread is considered a higher-THC landrace by historical
standards.

The terpinolene content is notable and relatively high compared to most
modern hybrids, contributing to the strain's unique energetic profile.

## Effects

### Onset
Effects begin within 5–15 minutes of inhalation, building gradually over
20–40 minutes. The experience is characteristically long-lasting for a
sativa, persisting 3–5 hours.

### Experience Profile

**Cerebral Effects:**
- Euphoric, mood-elevating onset with mental clarity
- Enhanced creativity and artistic inspiration
- Deepened meditative and spiritual awareness
- Increased sociability and verbal fluency
- Mild visual enhancement at moderate doses
- Absence of racing thoughts or anxiety (lower paranoia risk)

**Physical Effects:**
- Gentle, full-body invigoration without sedation
- Mild muscle relaxation
- Appetite stimulation
- Heightened sensory awareness (music appreciation, tactile sensitivity)

### Spiritual and Cultural Context

In Rastafarian practice, Lambsbread is consumed as a sacrament during
Nyabinghi ceremonies, reasoning sessions, and meditation. The strain's
clarity-promoting effects are valued for facilitating spiritual insight
and communal bond. Bob Marley's association with Lambsbread cemented its
status as the most culturally significant Jamaican strain.

### Medical Applications
- **Depression** — reliable mood elevation and anxiolysis
- **Fatigue** — sustained energy without crash
- **ADHD** — reported focus enhancement (anecdotal)
- **PTSD** — clarity without dissociation
- **Migraines** — both preventative and abortive effects
- **Nausea** — appetite stimulation in chemotherapy patients

## Cultivation

- **Height:** Very tall — can exceed 2.5 m outdoors
- **Flowering time:** 12–14 weeks indoors (long)
