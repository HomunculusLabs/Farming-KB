## Chemical Properties

IUPAC name: (12S)-12-hydroxy-7-ethyl-6,6a,7,8,9,10,12,12b-octahydro-5H-
6,9-methanoquinolizino[3,2,1-ij]pyrido[3,4-b]indole

Molecular formula: C19H24N2O
Molecular weight: 296.41 g/mol
Chemical class: Iboga alkaloid (monoterpene indole alkaloid)

Noribogaine is formed in vivo via O-demethylation of ibogaine at the
12-methoxy position, catalyzed primarily by CYP2D6. It contains a
secondary hydroxyl group at the C12 position, distinguishing it from the
parent compound's methoxy substituent. This structural modification
significantly alters receptor affinity and pharmacokinetic profiles.

## Pharmacology

Noribogaine acts at multiple receptor sites with affinity profiles that
differ meaningfully from ibogaine:

**Serotonergic system:**
- 5-HT2A agonist (Ki ~500 nM) — contributes to psychedelic effects
- 5-HT2C agonist (Ki ~300 nM)
- [[glucuronidation]]. The
CYP2D6 poor metabolizer phenotype results in reduced conversion of ibogaine
to noribogaine, which may affect therapeutic outcomes and toxicity profiles.
