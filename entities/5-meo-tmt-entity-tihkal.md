# 5-MeO-TMT (Indapex) — TIHKAL #45

## Compound Identity

- Full name: 5-Methoxy-2,N,N-trimethyltryptamine

- Synonyms: Indapex, 5-Methoxy-2-methyl-DMT, 3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-5-methoxy-2-methylindole

- Class: Tryptamine, 5-methoxy and 2-methyl substituted

- Source: [[tihkal-introduction-and-methodology]]

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: 75-150 mg orally

- Duration: 5-10 hours

- Active routes: oral

## Qualitative Effects

At 65 mg: First intoxication noted at one hour. Relaxation with subtle day-dreaming.
Sexual stimulation with heightened orgasm intensity. Baseline by three hours.

At 90 mg: Onset at 55 minutes with relaxation and mild motor impairment.
The most notable effect is dramatic sexual potentiation — "orgasm was phenomenal,"
confirmed three more times over three hours. Appetite suppression for five
hours. Music evoked closed-eye thought drifting but no true visuals. Jaw
and finger tremor noted. Effects faded by five hours. Broken sleep with enhanced
dream activity.

At 120 mg: No sexual feelings. Thoughts poorly connected with a drugged/sedated
quality. Moderate chills and cold sensations requiring blankets. Time moderately
slowed, respiration and pulse reduced. No visuals until 100 minutes, when
pronounced closed-eye imagery appeared. Emotions extremely labile — oscillating
between profound crying and calm in twenty-minute cycles. Every defect and
failure in life was vividly recalled. Body temperature fluctuations throughout.
Stomach distress continued for the entire experience.

At 150 mg: Mild intoxication at 55 minutes, progressing to nausea, loss of
fine motor skills, and peripheral visual waviness. Extreme emotional lability
with overwhelming sadness and despair. Pronounced closed-eye visuals by 100
minutes. Inability to eat or drink for eight hours. Residual effects at nine
hours.

## Synthesis from Indomethacin

The synthesis is unusual in that it starts from indomethacin (a pharmaceutical
anti-inflammatory), providing a readily available starting material. The
p-chlorobenzoyl group is removed by basic hydrolysis, then the acetamide
is reduced with LAH. The final product (mp 90-92°C) is obtained as a pale
yellow oil that slowly crystallizes.

## Pharmacological Notes

The dose range (75-150 mg) is extremely high compared to other 5-methoxy
tryptamines ([[5-meo-dmt-entity-tihkal]] is active at 6-20 mg).
The 2-methyl substituent on the indole ring dramatically reduces potency
while adding sedative qualities and inconsistent effects. The sexual potentiation
at moderate doses (90 mg) contrasts with the absence of sexual effects at
higher doses (120 mg) — suggesting a complex, non-linear dose-response relationship.

## Chemical Family Context

5-MeO-TMT is unique among the tryptamines in TIHKAL for bearing a methyl
group on the 2-position of the indole ring. The 2-position is adjacent to
the nitrogen atom of the indole ring, and substitution at this position
creates significant steric and electronic effects that alter the compound's
pharmacological properties.

The 2-methyl group's dramatic reduction in potency (from 6-20 mg for [[5-meo-dmt]]
to 75-150 mg for 5-MeO-TMT) demonstrates that the indole ring is not merely
a passive scaffold but is actively involved in receptor binding. The 2-position
substituent apparently interferes with the optimal orientation of the molecule
at the 5-HT2A receptor binding site.

The synthesis from indomethacin is pharmacologically ironic: a common
anti-inflammatory drug (marketed as Indocin) serves as the starting material
for a psychedelic compound with very different pharmacological properties.

## Detailed Pharmacological Discussion

The most striking feature of 5-MeO-TMT's pharmacology is the non-linear
dose-response relationship. At moderate doses (65-90 mg), the compound
produces relaxation, sexual enhancement, and mild mood alteration. At
higher doses (120-150 mg), the character shifts dramatically to include
emotional lability, profound sadness, temperature dysregulation, and
gastrointestinal distress.

This biphasic dose-response pattern is reminiscent of compounds that affect
multiple receptor systems with different affinity profiles. At lower doses,
a subset of receptors may be preferentially engaged (perhaps those mediating
relaxation and sexual response), while at higher doses, additional receptors
are recruited that produce the dysphoric and somatic symptoms.

The extreme emotional lability at higher doses -- oscillating between crying
and calm in 20-minute cycles -- suggests possible effects on limbic system
serotonin receptors, which are known to modulate emotional processing. The
vivid recall of "every defect and failure in life" is consistent with 5-HT2A
receptor activation in cortical areas involved in self-referential processing.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- 5-MeO-DMT: 6-20 mg, intense psychedelic, much more potent

- [[5-meo-mipt]]: 4-6 mg, mixed complex effects

- 5-MeO-DET: 1-3 mg, vertigo + erotic

- 5-MeO-TMT: 75-150 mg, sedating, sexual at moderate doses, dysphoric at high

The approximately 10-fold reduction in potency compared to 5-MeO-DMT
directly demonstrates the interference caused by the 2-methyl group.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

- The very high dose range (75-150 mg) means large quantities must be ingested

- The dose-response curve is non-linear; effects at 90 mg (pleasant) differ
dramatically from 120 mg (dysphoric)

- Extreme emotional lability at higher doses could be psychologically
harmful, particularly for individuals with depression or anxiety

- The inability to eat or drink for 8+ hours at higher doses creates
dehydration risk

- Body temperature fluctuations suggest autonomic nervous system involvement

- Start at the lower end of the range (65-75 mg) and wait at least 2 hours

- The compound's sedative properties may interact dangerously with other
CNS depressants
