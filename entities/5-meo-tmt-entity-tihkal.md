# 5-MeO-TMT (Indapex) — TIHKAL #45

## Compound Identity

- Full name: 5-Methoxy-2,N,N-trimethyltryptamine

- Synonyms: Indapex, 5-Methoxy-2-methyl-DMT, 3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-5-methoxy-2-methylindole

- Class: Tryptamine, 5-methoxy and 2-methyl substituted

- Source: [[5-meo-mipt]]: 4-6 mg, mixed complex effects

- 5-MeO-DET: 1-3 mg, vertigo + erotic

- 5-MeO-TMT: 75-150 mg, sedating, sexual at moderate doses, dysphoric at high

The approximately 10-fold reduction in potency compared to 5-MeO-DMT
directly demonstrates the interference caused by the 2-methyl group.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

- The very high dose range (75-150 mg) means large quantities must be ingested

- The dose-response curve is non-linear; effects at 90 mg (pleasant) differ
dramatically from 120 mg (dysphoric)

- Extreme emotional lability at higher doses could be psychologically
harmful, particularly for individuals with depression or anxiety

- The inability to eat or drink for 8+ hours at higher doses creates
dehydration risk

- Body temperature fluctuations suggest autonomic nervous system involvement

- Start at the lower end of the range (65-75 mg) and wait at least 2 hours

- The compound's sedative properties may interact dangerously with other
CNS depressants
