# Anunnaki

## Description

The Anunnaki (also spelled Annunaki) are a group of deities in ancient Sumerian, Akkadian, Assyrian, and Babylonian mythology. In the revisionist framework popularized by Zecharia Sitchin and expanded upon in ethnomycological literature, the Anunnaki are described as advanced extraterrestrial beings from the star Sirius who came to Earth and genetically manipulated indigenous hominids to create the human race as a servant species.

## Key Facts

1. **Creators of Humanity**: According to Sumerian clay tablets, the Anunnaki [[singh-genetically-engineered-fungi-bioremediation]] the Adama (Adam and Eve) from indigenous beings they called "beasts," creating humanity as a labor force.

2. **Origin from Sirius**: The tablets describe the Anunnaki originating from the star system Sirius, arriving on Earth with advanced technology including knowledge of astronomy, architecture, agriculture, and genetics.

3. **Internal Conflict**: The Anunnaki were not unified. The central schism between Enki (compassionate toward humans) and Enlil (viewed humans as servants only) drove the major mythological events including the [[allegro-garden-of-eden-and-delight]] narrative, the Great Flood, and the destruction of cities.

4. **Hoarding of Knowledge**: The Anunnaki systematically hoarded advanced knowledge — including the identity and use of entheogenic plants — keeping it exclusively for the elite while suppressing public access through religious prohibition and destruction of records.

5. **Star-Fire**: The Anunnaki possessed a substance called "Star-Fire" (also known as "gold of the gods" or "red gold"), described as nourishment that bestowed enlightenment. This substance was produced from the "lunar essence" or blood of the goddess Ninhursag and is theorized to reference [[allegro-colour-and-consistency-of-the-amanita-muscaria]].

6. **Abandonment and Legacy**: After the fall of Babylon, the Anunnaki reportedly withdrew "like birds taking flight," taking the Star-Fire and its knowledge with them. The priestly castes that remained encoded the knowledge in symbolic form.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The Anunnaki narrative directly connects to mycology through the concept of "Star-Fire" and the "Fire-Stone" — substances with multiple overlapping descriptions (fire, gold, red, blood, nourishment that bestows enlightenment) that align with the physical and psychoactive properties of the Amanita muscaria. The prohibition of these substances by the Enlilian faction of the Anunnaki is presented as the mythological origin of the suppression of entheogenic plant knowledge. The caduceus (Enki's symbol) remains the universal emblem of medicine, linking the Anunnaki legacy directly to modern pharmaceutical and healing traditions.

## Historical Context

The Sumerian civilization is considered the "cradle of civilization," appearing seemingly suddenly in Mesopotamia with advanced knowledge of astronomy, architecture, agriculture, and governance. The cuneiform tablets describing the Anunnaki predate the biblical Genesis account by more than 4,000 years and contain the same foundational narratives (creation, garden, flood, law-giving) that were later adapted into Abrahamic traditions.
