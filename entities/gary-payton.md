# Gary Payton

## Overview

Gary Payton is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain created by Powerzzz Genetics (collaboration with Cookies), named after the NBA Hall of Fame point guard known for his intense defensive play.

The strain gained rapid celebrity status after winning the Leafly Strain of the Year award in 2022, propelled by its unusual combination of potent cerebral stimulation and clear-headed functionality.

It descends from *The Y* (Snow Cap lineage) and *Snowman*, giving it a genetic profile that is distinctly different from the Cookie-family dominant genetics that saturated the market throughout the 2010s.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Balanced hybrid (approximately 50/50 indica-sativa, though slightly sativa-leaning in phenotype)
- **Lineage:** The Y × Snowman
- **Breeder:** Powerzzz Genetics / Cookies, circa 2019–2020
- **THC content:** 20–28% (averaging ~23% in tested samples)
- **CBD content:** Low (<1%)
- **Awards:** Leafly Strain of the Year (2022)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Diesel fuel, burnt rubber, pungent earthiness
- **Secondary notes:** Pepper, subtle sweetness, herbal undertones
- **Taste:** Smooth on the inhale with a sharp diesel and musky exhale; lingering peppery warmth
- **Smoke quality:** Relatively clean and expansive; not overly harsh despite potency
- **Key terpenes:** Limonene, caryophyllene, myrcene, linalool
- **Bag appeal:** Dense, medium-to-large buds with dark olive-green coloration, prominent orange pistils, and a thick coating of milky-white trichomes

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** Rapid, typically felt within 1–3 minutes of inhalation
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours, tapering gradually
- **Primary effects:**
  - Euphoric cerebral rush with heightened sensory perception
  - Clear-headed focus unusual for a strain of this potency
  - Enhanced sociability and conversational engagement
- **Body effects:** Moderate physical relaxation without heavy sedation; subtle buzzing sensation in the extremities
- **Medical applications:**
  - Stress and anxiety relief (anxiolytic without cognitive dulling)
  - Attention deficit support (focus-enhancing properties reported by users)
  - Mild to moderate pain management
  - Fatigue and low mood
- **Side effects:**
  - Dry mouth (common)
  - Dry eyes (common)
  - Anxiety or paranoia at higher doses (moderate risk due to potency)
  - Dizziness in sensitive users

## Growing Characteristics

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoor
- **Yield:** Moderate; 300–400 g/m² indoor, 400–500 g/plant outdoor
- **Height:** Medium; 100–150 cm indoor with training
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; cloning recommended over seed
- **Climate:** Prefers temperate Mediterranean-like conditions; 65–80°F (18–27°C) optimal
- **Resistance:** Good resistance to common molds; moderate pest resistance
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder during mid-flowering; calcium and magnesium supplementation beneficial
- **Training:** LST (low-stress training) and SCROG (screen of green) produce best results; responds well to topping
- **Clone performance:** Clones are the primary propagation method; phenotypic consistency is high when sourced from verified cuts

## Genetic Relatives and Crosses

- **The Y (mother):** Snow Cap descendant providing the signature diesel-pungent aroma and potency backbone; relatively rare genetics not widely available as a standalone strain
- **Snowman (father):** Contributes to the balanced effect profile and dense bud structure; part of the Cookies genetic library
- **Notable crosses:**
  - **Gary Payton × Runtz:** Combines the pungency of Gary Payton with the sugary sweetness of Runtz
  - **Gary Payton × Jealousy:** Potent cross bred by Seed Junky Genetics
  - **Gary Payton × Georgia Pie:** Dessert-flavored cross with improved yield

## Cultural Significance

Named with the endorsement of NBA Hall of Famer Gary Payton, the strain became one of the first modern cannabis varieties to receive a direct athlete name-licensing partnership, signaling a shift in mainstream cannabis branding.

Its 2022 Leafly Strain of the Year award validated a new wave of genetics that moved beyond the OG Kush and Cookies dominance of the previous decade, highlighting consumer demand for novel terpene profiles and functional hybrid effects.

The strain commands premium prices at licensed dispensaries, often selling for $55–75 per eighth ounce in competitive markets.

## Legal Status

Regulated under general cannabis law in jurisdictions where cannabis is legal for medical or recreational use. Gary Payton is not specifically scheduled as a named strain in any jurisdiction. Availability is primarily through licensed dispensaries in California, Oregon, Colorado, Michigan, and other legal US markets; clones and seeds are available from select breeders.
