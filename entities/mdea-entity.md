# MDEA — 3,4-Methylenedioxy-N-ethylamphetamine

PIHKAL entry #106 (listed as "MDE"). Also known as MDE, EVE, or N-Ethyl-MDA. MDEA is the N-ethyl homologue of MDA and the immediate structural cousin of [[mdma-entity]], with which it shares significant pharmacological similarity. It occupies a unique position in the phenethylamine family as the compound that first revealed the "window exploitation" technique later extended to MDMA.

## Chemical Identity

- Full name: 3,4-Methylenedioxy-N-ethylamphetamine
- PIHKAL number: #106
- Also known as: MDE, EVE, N-Ethyl-MDA
- Molecular formula: C12H17NO2
- HCl salt mp: 198-199 C (LAH reduction), 201-202 C (NaBH3CN reduction)
- N-Acetyl intermediate: white crystals from EtOAc/hexane, mp 92-93 C
- Free base: off-white oil, distilled at 145-150 C at 0.5 mm/Hg
- Structural relationship: N-ethyl homologue of [[mda-entity]]; one carbon longer on the nitrogen than [[mdma-entity]] (N-methyl)

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: 100-200 mg
- Duration: 3-5 h
- Onset: 30-60 minutes
- For comparison: MDMA 80-150 mg / 3-5 h; MDA 80-160 mg / 8-12 h

## Effects and Qualitative Comments

At 100 mg, a warm light all about with a gentle, almost alcohol-like intoxication. The drug changes state of awareness without doing much else. The world is as intense or as dull as the user chooses to make it. Effects dropping by 1.5 hours and completely gone by 2.5 hours.

At 160 mg, first effects felt in 40 minutes, fully present by end of first hour. Initial slightly dizzy intoxication followed by a very nice feeling. Good intoxication with mild motor incoordination. Absolutely no appetite. Next morning: still some elation but very relaxed. High marks for quality.

At 160 mg (second report), effects stronger and smoother than MDMA, perhaps partly due to group enhancement. Definitely fewer physiological side-effects than MDMA, particularly reduced urinary discomfort (less burning despite dehydration).

At 160 mg (third report), difficulty verbalizing and following others' thoughts. Cold symptoms (sore throat) disappeared during experience. Felt quite intoxicated and tranquilized.

At 200 mg, very stoned with initial nausea. Extreme difficulty concentrating on thoughts or speech. Noticeable jaw-clenching and "rice crispies in the ears" (tinnitus-like sounds). Described as a meditative material similar to MDMA but with more difficulty forming words and focusing eyes ("eye-romp"). Anorexia was extremely long-lived — possibly a total of 72 hours. This may have been too high a dosage.

## Comparison with MDMA and MDA

MDEA sits between MDA and MDMA in the N-substituted series, and its effects reflect this intermediate position:

- **vs. MDMA**: The particular "magic" and affective transference of MDMA does not appear to be present. The properties of unusually easy communication and positive self-viewing seem to be absent. However, MDEA produces fewer physiological side-effects, particularly reduced urinary discomfort.
- **vs. MDA**: There is a stoning intoxication similar to MDA. Like MDA, increasing the dosage is seemingly unrewarding — higher doses produce confusion rather than deeper insight.
- **Duration**: Shorter than MDA (3-5 h vs. 8-12 h), comparable to MDMA.
- **Isomer studies**: The optical isomers have never been evaluated separately in man. The "S" isomer might retain MDMA-like properties lost in the racemate — this remains an open research question.

## Window Exploitation Technique

The "piggy-back" or "window exploitation" technique was first discovered and explored with MDEA, later extended successfully with MDMA. The procedure involves assaying modest quantities of active materials at the drop-off period of MDEA to exploit the open and benign state present. Usually only a fraction of the standard dosage of the following drug is necessary to evoke a full experience.

In psychotherapy, this technique was used to transition from MDEA to [[2c-b-compound-profile]] with effective results. The technique proved that MDEA's benign afterglow could serve as a platform for combining with other psychedelics at reduced doses — a finding that influenced subsequent therapeutic research methodologies.

## Role in Phenethylamine Research

MDEA is significant in the [[phenethylamine-pharmacology]] landscape for several reasons:

1. **N-substitution SAR**: It demonstrated that the N-ethyl group produces qualitatively different effects from the N-methyl group (MDMA) and N-H (MDA), contributing to understanding of how nitrogen substitution affects the entactogenic vs. psychedelic balance.
2. **Therapeutic exploration**: Like MDMA, MDEA was explored in psychotherapeutic contexts during the brief period before scheduling, though it received far less attention.
3. **Window exploitation**: The discovery of this technique with MDEA had implications beyond the compound itself, influencing how combination sessions were approached in clinical settings.
4. **Street presence**: MDEA appeared on the street in the 1980s as "EVE," sometimes sold as or adulterated with MDMA. The shorter duration and milder empathogenic effects made it a distinct but less popular alternative.

## Synthesis

Three synthetic routes are described in PIHKAL:
1. From MDA via acetylation with acetic anhydride followed by LAH reduction
2. From 3,4-methylenedioxyphenylacetone with aluminum amalgam and ethylamine
3. From 3,4-methylenedioxyphenylacetone with NaBH3CN and ethylamine (reductive amination at pH 6)

## Cautionary Notes

Teeth clenching can occur, especially with supplemental dosages. Supplements have been explored in the 50-75 mg range at the two-hour point. Extreme anorexia reported at 200 mg (up to 72 hours). Jaw-clenching and ear sounds (tinnitus) at higher doses. The absence of MDMA's "magic" makes it less desirable for therapeutic applications. As with all N-substituted MDA derivatives, cardiovascular risk increases with dose.
