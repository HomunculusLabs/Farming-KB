## Overview

**Eth-LAD** (6-ethyl-6-nor-[[blue-light-phototropism-red-light-photoperiodism-dual-plant-vision-system]] with an alkyl group.

Eth-LAD was first synthesized in the late 1940s by [[amanita-muscaria-legal-alternative]] to LSD.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC Name:** (6aR,9R)-7-ethyl-6,6a,7,8,9,10-hexahydro-5H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide
- **Molecular Formula:** C22H27N3O
- **Molecular Weight:** 349.47 g/mol
- **CAS Number:** 23369-16-4 (estimated; multiple isomers may share this number)
- **SMILES:** CC[C@H]1C(=O)NC2[C@@H](CN3C[C@H]1CC3=C2c4cc[nH]c5ccccc45)C(=O)O

The compound exists as a [[hofmann-lsd-pharmacology-serotonin-brain]]

Eth-LAD acts as a potent agonist at the 5-HT2A receptor, the primary [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]] kinetics.

Functional selectivity (biased agonism) at 5-HT2A has been proposed for lysergamides, though specific data for Eth-LAD remains limited.

## Effects

Subjective effects are broadly similar to those of LSD, with users reporting visual distortion, altered time perception, enhanced introspection, and emotional lability.

The visual component is often described as rich and detailed, with fractal geometry, color shifting, and pattern recognition enhancement.

Cognitive effects include philosophical ideation, increased associative thinking, and occasional moments of [[shirota-psilocybin-crystal-form-spectral-data-and-analytical-characterization]] character attributed to LSD.

## Dosage and Duration

- **Threshold dose:** 40–60 μg
- **Light dose:** 60–100 μg
- **Common dose:** 100–150 μg
- **Strong dose:** 150–250 μg

These estimates are approximate and based on self-reported user data, as formal [[cho-morning-sickness-and-plant-cross-over-period]]-tolerance with LSD and other 5-HT2A agonists is complete, persisting for 5–7 days.
