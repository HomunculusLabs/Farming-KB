## Overview

Trichoderma harzianum is a filamentous ascomycete fungus of major agricultural and ecological significance. Widely recognized as one of the most effective biological control agents (BCAs) available for plant [[cryptic-species-fungal-biodiversity]] are morphologically nearly indistinguishable but differ significantly in their ecological niches, biocontrol efficacy, and [[fungal-volatile-organic-compounds-communication]] compounds (VOCs) including 6-pentyl-α-pyrone (6-PAP)
- **Conidiophores:** Highly branched, typically penicilliate (brush-like); phialides arranged in whorls of 2–3 along the branches; phialides are lageniform (flask-shaped) to cylindrical, 5–14 × 2.5–4 µm
- **Conidia:** Subglobose to ovoid, smooth-walled or finely roughened, 2.5–4.5 × 2–4 µm, green, produced in terminal conidial heads (puddles)
- **Chlamydospores:** Globose to subglobose, terminal or intercalary, 8–14 µm diameter, thick-walled, produced in older cultures; important for long-term survival in soil
- **Mycelium:** Hyaline, septate, 1.5–5 µm wide, with rapid apical extension rates (up to 20 mm/hr on favorable media)
- **Teleomorph:** Hypocrea lixii (the sexual stage); stroma pulvinate, greenish to brownish, producing perithecia with ascii containing 16 bicellular ascospores; [[post-harvest-handling-marketing-mushrooms]] treatment
