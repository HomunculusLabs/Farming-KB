# Wedding Cake

## Overview

**Wedding Cake** is a highly regarded indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its potency, complex flavor profile, and resinous bud structure. Originally bred by Seed Junky Genetics in Southern California, it quickly rose to prominence after winning first place in the **Hybrid category at the 2018 Emerald Cup**. The strain is a cross between **Triangle Kush** and **Animal Mints**, combining the heavy, relaxing effects of its kush lineage with the sweet, cookie-like terpene profile inherited from its Cookies family ancestry.

Also known as **Triangle Mints #23**, Wedding Cake has become one of the most commercially successful cannabis strains of the 2020s. It is widely available in both medical and recreational markets across North America and Europe, and has served as a parent or grandparent to numerous derivative cultivars including *Ice Cream Cake*, *Jungle Cake*, and *Birthday Cake*.

## Genetic Lineage

| Generation | Strain | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Parent | **Triangle Kush** (♀) | Indica-dominant; Florida kush lineage; provides structure, potency, and earthy undertones |
| Parent | **Animal Mints** (♂) | Hybrid; crosses Animal Cookies × Thin Mint GSC × Blue Power; contributes sweet cookie terpenes |
| Grandparent | **Animal Cookies** | Hybrid; Girl Scout Cookies × Fire OG |
| Grandparent | **Thin Mint GSC** | Hybrid; OG Kush × Durban Poison |
| Great-grandparent | **Girl Scout Cookies** | Landmark hybrid; OG Kush × Durban Poison |
| Great-grandparent | **OG Kush** | Foundation indica-dominant; Chemdawg × Lemon Thai × Hindu Kush |

The genetic backbone is approximately **70% indica / 30% sativa**, though phenotype expression varies. The Triangle Kush parent contributes dense bud architecture, high resin production, and sedative body effects, while Animal Mints adds layered sweetness, frosty trichome coverage, and a more balanced mental component.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range | Notes |
|------------|--------------|-------|
| **THC** | 20–27% | Some phenotypes test above 30%; consistently high-potency |
| **CBD** | < 0.1% | Negligible; not a CBD source |
| **CBG** | 0.1–0.5% | Minor cannabinoid; anti-inflammatory properties |
| **THCV** | Trace | Notable in some phenotypes |
| **CBC** | Trace | Present in minor quantities |

Wedding Cake is a **THC-dominant cultivar** with consistently high potency. Laboratory analyses across multiple tested samples show average THC content of 23–25%, placing it in the upper tier of commercially available strains. The CBD content is negligible, making it unsuitable for patients seeking CBD-dominant therapeutics.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Percentage | Sensory Contribution |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Limonene** | 0.5–1.2% | Citrus notes; mood elevation; stress relief |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.4–1.0% | Peppery, spicy; anti-inflammatory; binds CB₂ receptors |
| **Linalool** | 0.3–0.8% | Floral, lavender; anxiolytic; sedative enhancement |
| **Myrcene** | 0.3–0.7% | Earthy, musky; sedative; potentiates THC |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.3% | Woody, hoppy; appetite suppressant |
| **Pinene** | Trace – 0.2% | Pine; bronchodilator; counteracts THC memory impairment |

The dominant limonene-caryophyllene combination creates a **sweet, vanilla, and earthy aroma** with undertones of pepper and lavender. The overall sensory profile is often described as resembling vanilla cake batter with cream cheese frosting, lending the strain its popular name.

## Effects Profile

Effects are characteristically **indica-dominant with a balanced onset**:

- **Onset (0–15 minutes):** Initial cerebral euphoria and mood elevation; creative and sociable; mild sensory enhancement
- **Peak (15–60 minutes):** Deep physical relaxation spreading from the head downward; body heaviness and couch-lock potential; pain relief and muscle tension release
- **Duration (2–4 hours):** Gradual taper into sedation; sleepiness common in later stages; [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]

**Reported [[psilocybin-therapeutic-applications]]:**
- Chronic pain management (neuropathic and inflammatory pain)
- Insomnia and sleep disorders
- Anxiety and stress reduction (at moderate doses)
- PTSD [[cannabis-cancer-symptom-management]]
- Appetite stimulation (cachexia, nausea)
- Muscle spasms and tension

**Potential [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin]]:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes (common, dose-dependent)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia in susceptible individuals (especially high-THC phenotypes)
- Sedation and lethargy; not recommended for daytime use in most users

## Cultivation

### Growing Characteristics

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Flowering time** | 7–9 weeks indoors |
| **Outdoor harvest** | Mid to late October (Northern Hemisphere) |
| **Indoor yield** | 400–500 g/m² |
| **Outdoor yield** | 400–600 g/plant |
| **Height** | Medium; 100–150 cm indoors |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate; suitable for intermediate growers |
| **Stretch** | Moderate (1.5–2× during early flower) |

### Cultivation Notes

Wedding Cake performs well in both **soil and hydroponic systems**. The plants develop dense, chunky buds with exceptional resin production, making them popular for hashish and concentrate extraction. Key cultivation considerations:

- **Training:** Responds well to low-stress training (LST), topping, and ScROG; benefits from even canopy management due to dense bud formation
- **Nutrients:** Moderate to heavy feeder; responds well to increased phosphorus and potassium during flowering; [[dynamic-accumulator-plants-calcium-and-magnesium]] supplementation recommended
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, dry conditions; susceptible to bud rot and powdery mildew in high humidity; maintain below 55% RH during late flowering
- **Support:** Heavy buds require staking or trellising to prevent branch breakage
- **Terpene preservation:** Lower growing temperatures (18–20°C) during the final two weeks of flowering enhances terpene production and resin quality

## Phenotype Variation

Multiple phenotypes exist within the Wedding Cake gene pool, ranging from more kush-dominant expressions (denser buds, earthier aroma, heavier sedation) to cookie-dominant expressions (sweeter aroma, slightly more uplifting effects). Selecting a mother plant from clone cuts is common practice to maintain desired phenotype consistency in commercial operations.

## Derivative Strains

Wedding Cake has become one of the most prolific parent strains in modern cannabis breeding. Notable derivative crosses include:

| Strain | Cross | Notable Characteristics |
|--------|-------|------------------------|
| **Ice Cream Cake** | Wedding Cake × Gelato #33 | Extremely sweet; strong indica effects; popular for sleep |
| **Jungle Cake** | Wedding Cake × Jungle Fever | Potent hybrid; frosty resin; tropical cookie terpenes |
| **Birthday Cake** | Wedding Cake selection (phenotype) | Very similar to Wedding Cake; some breeders consider it a distinct phenotype |
| **LA Kush Cake** | Wedding Cake × Kush Mints | Heavy indica; earthy mint terpene profile; high resin yield |
| **Wedding Crasher** | Wedding Cake × Purple Punch | Balanced hybrid; grape and vanilla terpenes; euphoric onset |

## Extraction and Concentrate Quality

Wedding Cake is highly valued in the hash and concentrate market due to its exceptional resin production:

- **Rosin:** High yields of light-colored, flavorful rosin when pressed at appropriate temperatures (180–210°F)
- **Hash:** Ice water extraction produces full-melt bubble hash with excellent flavor preservation
- **BHO/PHO:** Produces high-terpene sauce and badder extracts; limonene and linalool carry through well
- **Testing:** Live resin concentrates typically test 60–80% total cannabinoids with robust terpene profiles (3–6%)
