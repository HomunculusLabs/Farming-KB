# Wedding Cake

## Overview

**Wedding Cake** is a highly regarded indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its potency, complex flavor profile, and resinous bud structure. Originally bred by Seed Junky Genetics in Southern California, it quickly rose to prominence after winning first place in the **Hybrid category at the 2018 Emerald Cup**. The strain is a cross between **Triangle Kush** and **Animal Mints**, combining the heavy, relaxing effects of its kush lineage with the sweet, cookie-like [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] inherited from its Cookies family ancestry.

Also known as **Triangle Mints #23**, Wedding Cake has become one of the most commercially successful cannabis strains of the 2020s. It is widely available in both medical and recreational markets across North America and Europe, and has served as a parent or grandparent to numerous derivative cultivars including *Ice Cream Cake*, *Jungle Cake*, and *Birthday Cake*.

## Genetic Lineage

| Generation | Strain | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Parent | **Triangle Kush** (♀) | Indica-dominant; Florida kush lineage; provides structure, potency, and earthy undertones |
| Parent | **Animal Mints** (♂) | Hybrid; crosses Animal Cookies × Thin Mint GSC × Blue Power; contributes sweet cookie terpenes |
| Grandparent | **Animal Cookies** | Hybrid; [[girl-scout-cookies]] × Fire OG |
| Grandparent | **Thin Mint GSC** | Hybrid; OG Kush × Durban Poison |
| Great-grandparent | **Girl Scout Cookies** | Landmark hybrid; OG Kush × Durban Poison |
| Great-grandparent | **OG Kush** | Foundation indica-dominant; Chemdawg × Lemon Thai × Hindu Kush |

The genetic backbone is approximately **70% indica / 30% sativa**, though phenotype expression varies. The Triangle Kush parent contributes dense bud architecture, high resin production, and sedative body effects, while Animal Mints adds layered sweetness, frosty trichome coverage, and a more balanced mental component.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range | Notes |
|------------|--------------|-------|
| **THC** | 20–27% | Some phenotypes test above 30%; consistently high-potency |
| **CBD** | < 0.1% | Negligible; not a CBD source |
| **CBG** | 0.1–0.5% | Minor cannabinoid; anti-inflammatory properties |
| **THCV** | Trace | Notable in some phenotypes |
| **CBC** | Trace | Present in minor quantities |

Wedding Cake is a **THC-dominant cultivar** with consistently high potency. Laboratory analyses across multiple tested samples show average THC content of 23–25%, placing it in the upper tier of commercially available strains. The CBD content is negligible, making it unsuitable for patients seeking CBD-dominant therapeutics.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Percentage | Sensory Contribution |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Limonene** | 0.5–1.2% | Citrus notes; mood elevation; stress relief |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.4–1.0% | Peppery, spicy; anti-inflammatory; binds CB₂ receptors |
| **Linalool** | 0.3–0.8% | Floral, lavender; anxiolytic; sedative enhancement |
| **Myrcene** | 0.3–0.7% | Earthy, musky; sedative; potentiates THC |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.3% | Woody, hoppy; appetite suppressant |
| **Pinene** | Trace – 0.2% | Pine; bronchodilator; counteracts THC memory impairment |

The dominant limonene-caryophyllene combination creates a **sweet, vanilla, and earthy aroma** with undertones of pepper and lavender. The overall sensory profile is often described as resembling vanilla cake batter with cream cheese frosting, lending the strain its popular name.

## Effects Profile

Effects are characteristically **indica-dominant with a balanced onset**:

- **Onset (0–15 minutes):** Initial cerebral euphoria and mood elevation; creative and sociable; mild sensory enhancement
- **Peak (15–60 minutes):** Deep physical relaxation spreading from the head downward; body heaviness and couch-lock potential; pain relief and muscle tension release
- **Duration (2–4 hours):** Gradual taper into sedation; sleepiness common in later stages; [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]

**Reported [[psilocybin-therapeutic-applications]]:**
- [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]] (neuropathic and inflammatory pain)
- Insomnia and sleep disorders
- Anxiety [[vipassana-mindfulness-and-stress-reduction-healing]] (at moderate doses)
- PTSD [[cannabis-cancer-symptom-management]]
- [[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]] (cachexia, nausea)
- Muscle spasms and tension

**Potential [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin]]:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes (common, dose-dependent)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia in susceptible individuals (especially high-THC phenotypes)
