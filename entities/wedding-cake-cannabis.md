# Wedding Cake

Wedding Cake, also sold under the name Triangle Mints #23, is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its exceptionally high THC content and rich, dessert-like terpene profile.

Bred from Triangle Kush and Animal Mints, it rose to prominence in the California medical and recreational markets after winning Leafly Strain of the Year in 2019.

The strain has become one of the most commercially successful modern cultivars, serving as a parent for numerous crosses including Ice Cream Cake and Jealousy.

## Lineage and Classification

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (~60-70% indica)
- **Lineage:** Triangle Kush × Animal Mints (Girl Scout Cookies × Purple Punch)
- **Breeder:** Seed Junky Genetics, circa 2017
- **THC content:** 20-27% (some phenotypes reported to 30%+)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.1%)

The Triangle Kush parent contributes a heavy indica structure and resin production, while Animal Mints provides the complex cookie-dough terpene expression.

Multiple phenotype cuts circulate commercially, with the "Triangle Mints #23" cut considered the most authentic and potent expression.

## Aroma, Flavor, and Terpene Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Sweet vanilla, earthy dough, sour tang
- **Secondary notes:** Pepper, lavender, fuel on the finish
- **Taste:** Creamy vanilla cake with peppery exhale and lingering sweetness

| Terpene | Approximate Range | Primary Effect |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| Limonene | 0.5-1.2% | Mood elevation, stress relief |
| β-Caryophyllene | 0.4-1.0% | Anti-inflammatory, CB2 agonist |
| Linalool | 0.3-0.8% | Relaxation, anxiolytic |
| Myrcene | 0.3-0.7% | Sedation, muscle relaxation |
| α-Humulene | 0.1-0.4% | Appetite suppression, anti-inflammatory |
| Geraniol | Trace-0.3% | Sweet floral aroma, neuroprotective |

The limonene-dominant terpene profile distinguishes Wedding Cake from other GSC descendants and drives much of its mood-elevating character.

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** Relatively rapid, 3-5 minutes post-inhalation
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Primary effects:** Euphoria, cerebral uplift followed by deep body relaxation
- **Body effects:** Heavy physical sedation in higher doses, couch-lock potential
- **Medical applications:** Chronic pain, insomnia, anxiety, PTSD, appetite stimulation
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness at high doses, potential paranoia in sensitive users

The onset begins with a clear-headed euphoria before transitioning to a full-body stone that intensifies over the first hour.

Medical patients report particular efficacy for evening pain management and sleep induction due to the strong sedative tail.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 7-9 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate to high, 400-500 g/m² indoors
- **Height:** Medium, 100-150 cm indoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate; responsive to training
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, dry conditions; sensitive to high humidity
- **Resistance:** Good mold resistance; moderate pest resistance
- **Nutrient needs:** Heavy feeder during mid-flower, especially calcium and magnesium
- **Training:** Low-stress training (LST) and scrog recommended; responds well to topping

Resin production is exceptionally heavy, with trichomes maturing cloudy to amber by week 8.

The buds are dense, conical, and dark green with purple highlights, coated in a thick layer of frosty trichomes and orange pistils.

## Appearance

Wedding Cake produces dense, medium to large-sized buds with a spade-like conical structure characteristic of its Kush heritage.

The flowers are dark forest green with occasional purple-blue undertones, especially when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flower.

A thick blanket of milky-white trichomes covers the calyxes, giving the buds a frosted, almost sugary appearance.

Bright orange pistils wind through the resinous flowers, becoming more prominent as the plant approaches harvest maturity.

The overall visual impression is of a premium dessert strain — dense, resinous, and visually striking.

## Notable Crosses and Derivatives

- **Ice Cream Cake** — Wedding Cake × Gelato #33; even more dessert-forward, indica-dominant
- **Jealousy** — Wedding Cake × Gelato #41; balanced hybrid with complex fruity profile
- **Secret Weapon** — Wedding Cake × Bubblegum; sweet, potent hybrid
- **Wedding Cheesecake** — Wedding Cake × Cheese; pungent skunky-diesel profile
- **Cake Batter** — Wedding Cake × Hell's Angel OG; dessert-forward with gassy undertones
- **Gushers** — Wedding Cake × Gelato #41 × Triangle Kush; fruity tropical profile
