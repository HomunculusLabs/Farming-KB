# Gorilla Glue

**Gorilla Glue** (also known as **GG4** or **Original Glue**) is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain that gained fame for its extraordinarily high THC content and resin-soaked buds. Developed by GG Strains (Lola Montana and Josey Wales) in Nevada, it swept the 2014 Cannabis Cup circuit with wins in multiple categories.

The strain is named for the sticky resin that coats the buds and readily adheres to scissors, fingers, and any surface it contacts — reminiscent of the polyurethane adhesive. With THC levels regularly exceeding 25–30%, it is one of the strongest strains available in the commercial market.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (approximately 55–60% indica / 40–45% sativa)
- **Lineage:** Chem's Sister × Sour Dubb × Chocolate Diesel (triple cross)
- **Breeder:** GG Strains (Lola Montana), Nevada, circa 2010–2013
- **THC content:** 25–30% (some tests exceed 30%)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.1%)

The triple-cross lineage makes Gorilla Glue genetically complex. Chem's Sister (a Chemdawg phenotype) provides potency and fuel aromatics, Sour Dubb contributes diesel pungency and yield, and Chocolate Diesel adds the chocolate-coffee flavor notes that distinguish Gorilla Glue from other Chemdawg descendants.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Diesel, pine, earth, chocolate, coffee
- **Secondary notes:** Citrus, skunk, fuel, wood
- **Taste:** Diesel-forward inhale with chocolate and coffee undertones; earthy pine on exhale
- **Key terpenes:** Myrcene, β-caryophyllene, limonene, α-pinene, humulene

The chocolate-diesel combination is distinctive and immediately recognizable. The aroma is intensely pungent — a single open jar can fill a room — making discretion difficult during transport or storage. The flavor follows the aroma closely, with the chocolate notes becoming more apparent on the exhale.

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Large, dense, chunky calyxes with exceptional resin production
- **Color:** Deep forest green with occasional purple undertones; thick coating of white trichomes
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptional; buds appear wet with resin even when fully cured
- **Pistil density:** Moderate orange-brown pistils; often obscured by the trichome layer

The resin production of Gorilla Glue is among the highest of any commercially available strain. Buds are so sticky that they can clog grinders and adhere to processing equipment, which is both the strain's namesake characteristic and a practical challenge for extractors.

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** Rapid, within 3–5 minutes
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours
- **Primary effects:** Heavy euphoria, cerebral rush followed by deep body relaxation
- **Body effects:** Progressive full-body relaxation leading to couch-lock at higher doses
- **Medical applications:** Severe chronic pain, PTSD, insomnia, muscle spasms, anxiety
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes; pronounced couch-lock; anxiety in novice users at high doses

Gorilla Glue is classified as a "one-hit wonder" by many consumers — a small amount produces substantial effects. The onset begins with an uplifting cerebral rush that transitions over 30–45 minutes into a heavy, enveloping body stone. This biphasic progression makes it popular for evening and nighttime use.

⚠️ **Potency warning:** At 25–30% THC, Gorilla Glue is among the strongest strains commercially available. Novice users should approach with caution and start with single-inhale doses.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 25–30% (occasionally testing above 30% in top phenotypes)
- **THCV:** Trace (<0.1%); not clinically significant
- **CBD:** <0.1%
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3% (minor amounts detected in some phenotypes)
- **CBC:** Trace amounts present

The cannabinoid profile is heavily THC-dominant with virtually no CBD. This ratio explains both the strain's potent psychoactive effects and its limited application as a medical CBD source. Total cannabinoid content typically exceeds 30% when minor cannabinoids are included.

## Growing Characteristics

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks
- **Yield:** 450–600 g/m² indoors; 500–700 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** 100–150 cm indoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate; high resin production can cause humidity issues
- **Climate:** Warm and dry; humidity below 50% in late flowering to prevent bud rot
- **Resistance:** Good general disease resistance; susceptible to botrytis in humid conditions
- **Nutrient needs:** Heavy feeder; requires elevated calcium and magnesium
- **Training:** LST and SCROG recommended; supports well and benefits from even canopy

Gorilla Glue's resin production peaks in the final two weeks of flowering, when trichome density increases dramatically. Growers should monitor trichome color closely — harvesting when 10–20% are amber maximizes the balance between potency and the full terpene profile.

The strain responds exceptionally well to both organic and synthetic nutrient regimens. In organic grows, compost teas and bat guano during flowering enhance the already pungent aroma. For extraction purposes, live resin production from fresh-frozen Gorilla Glue yields are among the highest per gram of plant material.

## Genetic Relatives and Crosses

- **Chem's Sister:** Contributed the Chemdawg potency and diesel-fuel terpene backbone
- **Sour Dubb:** Added yield, vigor, and additional diesel pungency
- **Chocolate Diesel:** Provided the distinctive chocolate-coffee flavor signature
- **Notable crosses:** GG#1, GG#3, GG#4 (original), GG#5, New Glue, Gorilla Breath, Slurricane, S'mores

## Legal Status

Follows standard cannabis regulations by jurisdiction. Legal in Canada, Uruguay, and numerous U.S. states with adult-use programs. Schedule I under U.S. federal law as of 2026.

The "Gorilla Glue" trademark has been the subject of legal disputes. The adhesive company Gorilla Glue Inc. initiated trademark actions against cannabis companies using the name, leading some breeders and dispensaries to adopt the "GG4" or "Original Glue" designation.
