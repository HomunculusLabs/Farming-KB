## Description

HEPA (High Efficiency Particulate Air) filters are mechanical air filters that remove at least 99.97% of airborne particles measuring 0.3 micrometers in diameter. They are a critical component in laminar flow hoods used for sterile work in mycology, tissue culture, and laboratory settings.

## Classification

- **Category**: Air filtration device
- **Filter class**: H14 according to EN 1822 standard
- **Application**: Laboratory, cleanroom, and [[contamination-prevention-in-mushroom-cultivation]], they are the core component of laminar flow hoods used for:

- **Agar work**: Transferring mycelium cultures between plates
- **[[cultivator-sterile-technique-and-agar-culture]] operations.

## Cost and Availability

Commercial HEPA filters suitable for DIY flow hoods typically cost 100-150 EUR. The specific model referenced in the source (HS-Mikro SF 305 x 610 x 78 mm) was priced at 131.08 EUR from Luftfilterbau.
