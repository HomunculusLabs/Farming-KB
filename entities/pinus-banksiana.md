## Pinus banksiana

## Overview

**Pinus banksiana**, the jack pine, is a small to medium conifer native to
boreal and sub-boreal regions [[pinus-contorta]], with occasional hybridization where ranges overlap.

## Morphology

Jack pine typically reaches 9–18 m in height and 20–30 cm DBH on typical
boreal sites; on favorable sites it can reach 24 m.

The crown is narrow, irregular, and often sparsely branched, with crooked or
leaning stems common.

Bark is thin, dark gray to reddish-brown, divided into irregular scaly ridges.

Needles occur in fascicles of two, 2–4 cm long and 1–1.5 mm wide — among
the shortest of all [[pinus-brutia]]
