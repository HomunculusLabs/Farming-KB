## Overview

*Macrocybe gigantea* (formerly *Tricholoma giganteum*) is a large edible agaric mushroom
in the family Tricholomataceae. It is one of the largest gilled mushrooms known,
with caps reaching 60–100 cm in diameter, earning it the common name giant mushroom.
Found in tropical and subtropical grasslands of Asia, the Caribbean, and parts of
Africa, it is both wild-harvested and commercially cultivated in Southeast Asia.
