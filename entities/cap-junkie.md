## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is intensely complex, dominated by a pungent gassy and diesel
forward note, underlaid with ripe tropical fruit, sour citrus, and cool
mint. On the break, notes of garlic, onion, and fermented funk emerge,
characteristic of the Cookies lineage.

The flavor follows the aroma closely, with a creamy, gassy inhale
transitioning to sweet tropical fruit and sharp mint on the exhale. A
lingering earthy, peppery finish rounds out the experience. The flavor
is dense and coats the palate, reflecting the strain's high terpene
content.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

The dominant terpenes are limonene, caryophyllene, and linalool, with
notable secondary contributions from myrcene, humulene, and bisabolol.
