# Black Tuna

**Black Tuna** is a potent indica-dominant cannabis strain that originated in **British Columbia, Canada**, during the underground grow scene of the late 1990s and early 2000s. Named for its dark, almost black appearance in late flowering and its pungent, oceanic aroma reminiscent of aged fish, Black Tuna earned legendary status among Canadian cannabis enthusiasts as a " connoisseur's indica" prized for its heavy sedative effects and complex terpene profile. It is one of the defining legacy strains of the pre-legalization BC cannabis industry.

## Lineage and Genetics

Black Tuna's exact genetic lineage remains somewhat shrouded in the secrecy of the underground BC grow scene, but the most widely accepted pedigree is:

- **Parent 1: Herijuana** (also known as Holy Juan or Killer New Haven) — A heavy indica bred by Soggy Creek Farms from a cross of Petrolia Headstash and an unknown Kentucky indica. Herijuana contributes Black Tuna's knockout sedative power, earthy-fuel terpenes, and dense bud structure.
- **Parent 2: Lamb's Bread** (also Lamb's Breath) — A legendary Jamaican sativa landrace reputed to be Bob Marley's favorite variety. Lamb's Bread contributes cerebral uplift, spicy-sweet aroma notes, and increased resin production.

The Herijuana × Lamb's Bread cross produces a roughly **80% indica / 20% sativa** hybrid that leans heavily toward full-body relaxation while retaining a subtle mental clarity in lower doses.

### Genetic Stability

Black Tuna is not a stabilized commercial line in the formal breeding sense. It exists as a collection of phenotypically similar cuts that have been passed between BC growers for over two decades. As a result, some phenotypic variation exists between different "Black Tuna" lines:

- **Phenotype A ("The Original")**: Darker buds, more fuel/skunk aroma, heavier body effects
- **Phenotype B ("The Bright")**: Slightly lighter green coloration, more pronounced Jamaican spice notes, marginally more heady

## Appearance

Black Tuna is visually distinctive and true to its name:

- **Bud structure**: Dense, tightly packed, rock-hard calyxes forming chunky, irregular nugs. The structure is classic indica — compact and weighty for their size
- **Coloration**: Deep forest green to nearly black, especially in the final weeks of flowering and after curing. The dark coloration comes from exceptionally high concentrations of **anthocyanins** (purple-black pigments) that express strongly in cooler growing conditions
- **Trichomes**: Extremely heavy trichome coverage — the buds appear frosted or sugar-coated even to the naked eye. Trichomes are large, bulbous, and milky-white to amber at maturity, reflecting the strain's high potency
- **Pistils**: Dark orange to rust-colored, relatively sparse compared to the trichome density
- **Leaf**: Broad, dark indica-style fan leaves during vegetation

## Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma

Black Tuna's aroma is its most polarizing characteristic — users either find it fascinating or off-putting:

- **Primary**: Skunky, diesel/fuel, with a distinctly oceanic, briny quality (hence "tuna")
- **Secondary**: Earthy, damp soil, decaying wood, and musk
- **Tertiary**: Subtle undertones of sweet spice, black pepper, and dark fruit
- **Upon grinding**: The full complexity is released — a pungent wave of skunk, diesel, and ocean air mixed with hashy, incense-like qualities

### Flavor

The flavor softens compared to the aroma: earthy and hashy on inhale, diesel and fuel with briny/salty undertones on exhale, and a long-lasting fishy/skunky aftertaste. The smoke is thick, heavy, and expansive.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Black Tuna is renowned for its high THC content, which has only increased as the strain has been selectively bred over two decades:

| Compound | Typical Range | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| THC | 22–28% | Some phenos reported up to 31% |
| CBD | < 0.1% | Negligible |
| CBG | 0.5–1.2% | Moderate for a THC-dominant strain |
| THCV | Trace | Contributes to the strain's "clean" intoxication |

The strain's high THC levels, combined with its complex terpene profile, produce a **strongly synergistic entourage effect** that users consistently describe as more potent than the THC percentage alone would suggest.

## Terpene Profile

Black Tuna's terpene profile is dominated by bold, pungent compounds:

| Terpene | Approximate % | Contribution |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Myrcene | 0.8–1.4% | Sedative body effects, earthy/musky aroma |
| Caryophyllene | 0.6–1.1% | Peppery spice, anti-inflammatory |
| Limonene | 0.4–0.8% | Citrus undertones, mood elevation |
| Humulene | 0.3–0.6% | Earthy, woody notes, appetite suppression |
| Pinene | 0.2–0.5% | Pine notes, counteracts some sedation |
| Linalool | Trace | Floral, calming |

The unusually high **caryophyllene** content (a CB2 receptor agonist that does not produce psychoactive effects) is thought to contribute to Black Tuna's pronounced body-focused and anti-inflammatory properties.

## Effects

### Onset and Timeline

- **Onset**: Rapid — effects are felt within 2–5 minutes of inhalation
- **Peak**: 30–90 minutes
- **Duration**: 2–4 hours, with lingering body effects for an additional 1–2 hours

### Experience Profile

- **Initial (0–15 min)**: Sudden cerebral pressure behind the eyes with an uplifted, euphoric mood and heightened sensory perception
- **Middle (15–60 min)**: Deep body relaxation progressing to full-body couch-lock. Mental state shifts from uplifted to hazy and introspective
- **Late (60+ min)**: Strong sedation dominates — heavy eyelids and strong desire to sleep. Best reserved for evening use

### Medical Applications

- **Insomnia**: Highly effective for sleep induction and treatment-resistant insomnia
- **Pain management**: Strong analgesic properties for chronic pain, neuropathy, and muscle spasms
- **Anxiety and stress**: Physical relaxation alleviates tension, though high doses may increase mental anxiety in prone individuals
- **PTSD**: Helps with sleep disturbances and nightmares associated with PTSD

### Potential Negative Effects

- **Dry mouth/eyes**, paranoia at high doses, dizziness on standing, and extreme couch-lock
- High potency means overconsumption can lead to nausea and disorientation

## Growing Information

### Cultivation Difficulty

Black Tuna is considered **moderately difficult** to cultivate, suited to intermediate to advanced growers:

- **Flowering time**: 8–10 weeks (56–70 days)
- **Yield**: Moderate — approximately 300–400 g/m² indoors
- **Height**: Medium — reaches 100–150 cm indoors
- **Stretch**: Moderate (1.5–2×) during the first 3 weeks of flowering

### Growing Notes

- **Temperature**: Cooler night temps (15–18°C) during late flowering maximize anthocyanin expression and dark coloration
- **Nutrients**: Heavy feeder during weeks 3–6 of flowering; reduce nitrogen late in cycle
- **Training**: Responds well to LST, SCROG, and topping
- **Humidity**: Keep below 50% RH during flowering to prevent bud rot in dense colas
