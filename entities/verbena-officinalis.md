## Morphology

- **Growth habit:** Perennial, erect herb, 30–70 cm tall
- **Stems:** Square (quadrangular), slender, branched toward apex, appressed hairs
- **Leaves:** Opposite, simple, 3–8 cm long, deeply lobed or pinnatifid,
  coarsely toothed margins
- **Inflorescence:** Slender terminal spikes, 10–30 cm long
- **Flowers:** Small, 5-lobed, lilac to pale blue; calyx tubular 3–5 mm;
  corolla salverform 5–7 mm
- **Fruit:** Schizocarp splitting into 4 nutlets, ~2 mm each
- **Root:** Fibrous taproot system; flowering June–September
