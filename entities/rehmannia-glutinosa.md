## Rehmannia glutinosa (*Di Huang*)

## Overview

*Rehmannia glutinosa* is a perennial herbaceous plant in the family Orophusaceae
(formerly Scrophulariaceae), native to China, Korea, and Japan.

It is one of [[ingham-aact-disease-case-studies-specific-pathogens]] epithet *glutinosa* refers to the sticky, glutinous nature of the
fresh roots when boiled.

## Morphology

Rehmannia is a low-growing herb reaching 15–30 cm in height.

- **Leaves:** Basal rosette, ovate to oblong, 3–10 cm long, wrinkled with
  prominent veins, pubescent surface, crenate-serrate margins.
- **Stem:** Hairy, simple or sparingly branched.
- **Flowers:** Tubular, 3–4 cm long, purplish with yellowish throat, borne
  in terminal cymes; calyx 5-lobed, corolla bilabiate.
- **Fruit:** Ovoid capsule containing numerous small seeds.
- **Root:** Thick, fleshy tuberous root system, the primary [[gadd-fungal-wood-decay-brown-rot-white-rot-soft-rot-mechanisms]]-black.

## Phytochemistry

The bioactive constituents of *R. glutinosa* are dominated by iridoid glycosides,
with additional [[growing-gourmet-medicinal-polysaccharides-mushroom-compounds]] contributing to activity.

**Primary iridoid glycosides:**

| Compound | Content (dried root) | Primary Activity |
|----------|---------------------|------------------|
| Catalpol | 0.5–5.0% | Neuroprotective, anti-diabetic |
| Rehmannioside A | 0.1–1.5% | Anti-inflammatory |
| Rehmannioside C | 0.1–1.0% | Antioxidant |
| Rehmannioside D | trace | Anti-osteoporotic |
| Aucubin | 0.05–0.3% | Hepatoprotective |

**Processing effect:** The traditional nine-fold steaming and sun-drying process
(*Jiu Zhi*) converts catalpol and other iridoids into stachyose, reducing the
cooling properties and enhancing the warming, tonic qualities.
Catalpol content decreases by 60–80% during processing, while 5-HMF
(5-hydroxymethylfurfural) increases significantly.
