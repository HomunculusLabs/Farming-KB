## Overview

Jealousy is a modern indica-dominant [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]] regularly testing between 20–28%, it is considered a high-potency strain best suited for experienced consumers.

## Lineage

Jealousy was created by crossing **Sherbet Bx1** with a select cut of **Gelato 41**.

The Sherbet lineage contributes deeply relaxing body effects and a complex sweet-and-funky aroma profile.

Gelato 41 adds potency, dense [[girl-scout-cookies]] (GSC) genetic line through [[thin-mint-cookies]].

This GSC-dominant pedigree places Jealousy firmly within the "Cookies family" of modern West Coast [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] make Jealousy popular for hash and concentrate extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma of Jealousy is complex and layered, dominated by sweet, creamy notes reminiscent of its Sherbet parentage.

Secondary notes include earthy, peppery, and slightly funky undertones contributed by the Gelato lineage.

Some phenotypes express a distinct grape or berry sweetness, particularly when cured properly.

The flavor profile follows the aroma closely, with a smooth, creamy smoke carrying sweet dessert-like qualities on the inhale.

The exhale often reveals more earthy and peppery notes with a subtle menthol coolness.

## Terpene Profile

- **Caryophyllene:** Dominant terpene, contributing peppery, spicy notes and potential anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Limonene:** Secondary terpene, adding citrus sweetness and mood-elevating properties.
- **Myrcene:** Present at moderate levels, enhancing sedative and relaxing body effects.
- **Linalool:** Contributes floral, lavender-like undertones and calming effects.

Minor terpenes may include humulene, pinene, and trace amounts of ocimene depending on the specific phenotype.
