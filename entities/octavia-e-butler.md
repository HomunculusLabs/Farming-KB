# Octavia E. Butler

## Overview

Octavia Estelle Butler was an American science-fiction and speculative-fiction writer.
She was born in 1947 and died in 2006.
Her fiction is known for its rigor, emotional force, and attention to power.
She wrote about survival, hierarchy, mutation, alien encounter, race, gender, ecology, [[cannabis-tolerance-and-dependence]].
Butler won major genre honors, including Hugo, Nebula, and Locus awards.
In 1995 she became the first science-fiction writer to receive a MacArthur Fellowship.
That recognition helped mark speculative fiction as a serious literary and intellectual field.
