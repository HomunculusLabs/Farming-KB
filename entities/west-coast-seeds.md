## Description

West Coast Seeds is a Canadian seed company based in Ladner, British Columbia. Founded in 1987, it specializes in untreated, non-GMO seeds suited to the Pacific Northwest and West Coast growing conditions. Referenced by Steve Solomon as a recommended seed source in *[[gardening-when-it-counts]]*.

## Classification

- **Type:** Organization (seed company)
- **Location:** Ladner, British Columbia, Canada
- **Founded:** 1987
- **Industry:** Horticultural seeds

## Key Facts

- Specializes in seeds for the Pacific Northwest and maritime climate zones
- Offers only untreated and non-GMO seeds
- Carries over 1,000 varieties of vegetables, herbs, and flowers
- Referenced on page 116 of *Gardening When It Counts* as a recommended seed source
- Known for providing detailed planting charts and growing guides specific to West Coast conditions
- Active in community education, offering workshops and resources for organic gardening
- Serves gardeners in British Columbia, the Pacific Northwest, and across Canada
