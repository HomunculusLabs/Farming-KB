# Green Crack

Green Crack is a sativa-dominant [[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] legal and illicit markets, prized by recreational
users for daytime energy and by medical patients for fatigue,
depression, and attention-related conditions.

The strain is typically classified as 75-80% sativa / 20-25% indica,
though the exact ratio varies by phenotype and breeder. Its genetic
lineage traces to a Skunk #1 phenotype that was selectively bred for
enhanced sativa characteristics, including shorter [[spanish-cannabis-seed-companies]] including Fast Buds, Crop King
