# Hypnodinium

## Description

Hypnodinium is a genus of dinoflagellates found in freshwater environments. Mollison lists Hypnodinium among the typical organisms present in open sewage treatment ponds, where it forms part of the phytoplankton community responsible for biological processing of wastewater. As a dinoflagellate, it contributes to nutrient cycling and oxygen production in faculative and aerobic pond systems.

## Classification

- **Domain**: Eukaryota
- **Phylum**: Dinoflagellata
- **Class**: Dinophyceae
- **Genus**: *Hypnodinium*

## Key Facts

- Hypnodinium is a freshwater dinoflagellate genus commonly found in ponds, lakes, and slow-moving waterways.
- Dinoflagellates are characterized by two flagella that provide motility, allowing them to navigate vertically in the water column.
- Like other phytoplankton, Hypnodinium contributes oxygen through photosynthesis during daylight hours.
- In sewage treatment ponds, dinoflagellates contribute to the diverse microbial community that processes organic waste.
- Some dinoflagellate genera produce resting cysts that survive unfavorable conditions, allowing populations to persist through seasonal changes.
- Dinoflagellates in freshwater systems are generally less studied than their marine counterparts but play significant ecological roles.
