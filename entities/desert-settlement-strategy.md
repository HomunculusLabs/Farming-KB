## Desert Settlement Strategy

## Description

Mollison's comprehensive framework for sustainable human habitation in dryland
environments. Based on the fundamental principle that all settlements must be
limited by their water supply capacity, the strategy addresses site selection,
population limits, [[desertification]].

## Classification

- **Category**: Settlement design framework
- **Domain**: [[permaculture]] design. Establishes the hierarchy of needs:
water first, then shelter, then productive vegetation, then food production.
Without understanding these principles, any desert settlement is ultimately
doomed regardless of agricultural technique employed.

## References

- Mollison, B. (1988). Permaculture: A Designers' Manual, pp. 385-388.
