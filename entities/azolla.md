# Azolla

## Description

Azolla is a genus of small, free-floating aquatic ferns that are among the most efficient nitrogen-fixing organisms known. Each Azolla plant hosts a symbiotic cyanobacterium (Anabaena azollae) within its leaf cavities that converts atmospheric nitrogen into forms usable by plants. In permaculture and sustainable agriculture, Azolla is valued as a living nitrogen factory, [[mushroom-substrate-preparation]]
