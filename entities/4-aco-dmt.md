# 4-AcO-DMT (Psilacetin)

## Overview

**4-Acetoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine** (abbreviated **4-AcO-DMT**, also known as **psilacetin**, **O-acetylpsilocin**, or **synthetic mushrooms**) is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine and a prodrug of **psilocin** (4-HO-DMT). First synthesized by [[albert-hofmann]] at Sandoz Laboratories in the 1960s and later documented by Alexander and [[ann-shulgin]] in TIHKAL (1997), 4-AcO-DMT is the acetylated ester derivative of psilocin. It is metabolized in vivo by esterase enzymes to psilocin, producing subjective effects essentially identical to those of naturally occurring [[cap-versus-stem-alkaloid-distribution-in-psilocybin-mushrooms]].

The compound occupies a unique position in psychopharmacology as a **synthetic psychedelic** that produces the same active metabolite as a natural product. This has made it a subject of interest in clinical [[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] as a potentially superior alternative to psilocybin due to simpler synthesis, greater chemical stability, and more consistent dosing characteristics. It is classified as a controlled substance in many jurisdictions, though its legal status varies internationally.

### Chemical Structure

The molecule consists of an indole ring system with an **acetoxy group** (−OCOCH₃) at the 4-position of the benzene ring and a **dimethylaminoethyl** side chain at the 3-position of the pyrrole ring. The acetoxy group is a metabolic leaving group — it is cleaved by serum and hepatic esterases to yield psilocin (4-HO-DMT), which is the pharmacologically active species.

4-AcO-DMT is an **acetate ester analog** of psilocin, just as psilocybin is the **phosphate ester** of psilocin. The structural similarity extends to 4-AcO-MET (the corresponding metocin analog) and 4-AcO-MiPT (the corresponding 4-acetoxy-miprocin analog), forming a family of acetylated tryptamine prodrugs.

## Pharmacology

### Metabolism and Mechanism of Action

4-AcO-DMT is a **prodrug** with minimal intrinsic pharmacological activity. After oral ingestion, it undergoes rapid deacetylation by esterases in the gastrointestinal tract, blood serum, and liver:

```
4-AcO-DMT → (esterase) → 4-HO-DMT (psilocin) → (MAO-A) → 4-HO-IAA (inactive)
```

The resulting psilocin acts as a **partial agonist at the 5-HT₂A [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]]** (Ki ≈ 5.5 nM), which is the primary receptor responsible for psychedelic effects. Additional receptor interactions include:

| Receptor | Activity | Functional Significance |
|----------|----------|------------------------|
| **5-HT₂A** | Partial agonist (Ki ≈ 5.5 nM) | Primary psychedelic effects; visual hallucinations, [[default-mode-network-and-ego-dissolution]] |
| **5-HT₁A** | Agonist (Ki ≈ 30 nM) | Anxiolytic effects; mood modulation |
| **5-HT₂C** | Agonist | Contributes to perceptual and cognitive effects |
| **5-HT₂B** | Agonist | Minimal clinical significance at psychedelic doses |
| **TAAR1** | Agonist (low affinity) | Possible contribution to stimulant-like peripheral effects |

The pharmacokinetics of 4-AcO-DMT are characterized by rapid absorption and conversion, with psilocin appearing in plasma within 20–40 minutes of oral administration. Peak plasma psilocin concentrations occur at approximately 60–90 minutes. The compound is primarily metabolized by **monoamine oxidase A (MAO-A)** to the inactive metabolite 4-hydroxyindole-3-acetic acid (4-HO-IAA), with renal excretion as the primary elimination pathway.

## Dosage and Effects

### Dosage Ranges (oral)

| Level | Dose (mg) | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Threshold** | 2–4 mg | Subtle mood shift, enhanced colors, mild body sensation |
| **Light** | 5–8 mg | Noticeable visual enhancement, mood elevation, altered thought patterns |
| **Moderate** | 8–15 mg | Pronounced visual and auditory effects, philosophical introspection, time distortion |
| **Strong** | 15–25 mg | Intense visual hallucinations, ego dissolution, mystical-type experiences |
| **Heavy** | 25+ mg | Overwhelming [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]]; significant ego death potential; not recommended for inexperienced users |

### Subjective Effects

The subjective effects of 4-AcO-DMT are reported to be **virtually indistinguishable from psilocybin mushrooms** by experienced psychonauts:

- **Visual:** Enhanced color saturation, geometric patterns (especially with eyes closed), flowing morphing textures, synesthesia at higher doses
- **Cognitive:** Altered thought patterns, enhanced introspection, philosophical and spiritual ideation, increased creativity
- **Emotional:** Euphoria, connectedness, awe; may include challenging emotions or anxiety at higher doses
- **Physical:** Pupil dilation, increased body temperature, nausea (more common at onset than with psilocybin), mild tremor, altered perception of time

## Research Applications

4-AcO-DMT has attracted interest in clinical psychedelic research as a potential alternative to psilocybin for several reasons:

- **Synthetic accessibility:** Simpler and more cost-effective to synthesize than extracting or synthesizing psilocybin
- **Consistent purity:** Synthetic production eliminates batch-to-batch variability inherent in natural mushroom extracts
- **Prodrug mechanism:** Reliable conversion to psilocin provides predictable pharmacokinetics
- **Stability considerations:** Moderate stability requires appropriate [[spawn-storage-conditions]] (cool, dry, dark) but is adequate for clinical supply chains
