---# Ursolic Acid

## Overview

Ursolic acid (3β-hydroxy-urs-12-en-28-oic acid) is a pentacyclic triterpenoid carboxylic acid found abundantly in the epidermal wax coatings of fruits, leaves, and bark of numerous plant species across diverse botanical families. Named for its original isolation from bearberry (Arctostaphylos uva-ursi; *Ursa* = bear in Latin), it is one of the most ubiquitous and extensively studied triterpenoids in phytochemistry and pharmacology. Ursolic acid and its isomer oleanolic acid are among the most prevalent bioactive triterpenoids in the human diet. Over the past three decades, ursolic acid has emerged as a compound of intense research interest due to its remarkably broad pharmacological profile — encompassing anti-inflammatory, anticancer, hepatoprotective, cardioprotective, neuroprotective, antidiabetic, and anti-obesity activities. It is a key active constituent in many traditional herbal medicines and is increasingly recognized for its role in the health benefits of fruit-rich diets, Mediterranean herbal traditions, and adaptogenic botanical preparations.

## Pharmacology and Mechanisms of Action

Ursolic acid modulates a remarkable array of molecular targets, which underlies its diverse pharmacological activities:

**Anti-inflammatory**:
- Inhibits NF-κB nuclear translocation, reducing TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, COX-2, and iNOS expression
- Suppresses MAPK signaling (ERK, JNK, p38) and TLR4/MyD88 pathway
- Activates Nrf2/HO-1 [[blesching-cannabis-oxidative-stress-and-antioxidant-defense]] pathway
- Effective in [[blesching-cannabis-rheumatoid-arthritis]], colitis, and allergic airway inflammation models

**Anticancer**:
- Induces apoptosis via mitochondrial pathway (Bax/Bcl-2 modulation, caspase-3/9 activation)
- Inhibits PI3K/Akt/mTOR and STAT3 [[plant-signaling-pathways]]
- Suppresses angiogenesis (VEGF, HIF-1α downregulation) and metastasis (MMP-2, MMP-9 inhibition)
- Active against breast, prostate, lung, colon, liver, pancreatic, gastric, glioma, and melanoma lines
- IC₅₀ typically 10–50 μM; synergistic with conventional chemotherapeutics at lower doses

**Hepatoprotective**:
- Protects hepatocytes from CCl₄, acetaminophen, and alcohol-induced injury
- Reduces serum ALT, AST, and MDA in hepatic damage models
- Enhances hepatic glutathione and SOD/catalase activity
- Ameliorates NAFLD by reducing hepatic lipid accumulation

**Antidiabetic and metabolic**:
- Enhances insulin sensitivity via AMPK activation and GLUT4 translocation
- Reduces fasting blood glucose and HbA1c in streptozotocin-diabetic rat models
- Promotes skeletal muscle hypertrophy and brown fat activation
- Improves lipid profile: reduces TC, LDL, TG; increases HDL
