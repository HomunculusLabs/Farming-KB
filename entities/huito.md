In Amaringo's visions, huito appears as a key medicinal preparation used in the treatment of *boa chichuchishca* (illness caused by spiritual impregnation by a boa). The fruit is cut in halves, scraped, and prepared with warm water for the patient to consume. The vegetalista sings icaros over the preparation, blows on it, and places *arkanas* (protective defenses) into it, giving the boa's offspring reason to move and exit the body.
