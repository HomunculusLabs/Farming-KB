
# Sajeev John

## Overview
- Sajeev John is a physicist associated with the University of Toronto.

## Nanophotonics and materials
- John's research belongs to the broader rise of nanophotonics.
- Nanophotonics studies optical behavior in structures comparable to the wavelength of light.
- [[query-how-do-photonic-crystals-create-structural-color]] are one of the canonical nanophotonic material classes.
- They use repeated refractive-index contrast to shape optical modes.
- John's work helped establish that these modes can be engineered as deliberately as electronic states.
- His research therefore supports optical circuits, sensors, lasers, and quantum devices.

## Awards and honors
- John has received major Canadian and international recognition.
- He received the Herzberg Medal in 1996.
- He received the Steacie Prize in 1997.
- He received the King Faisal Prize in 2001.
- He received the Killam Prize in 2014.
- He was appointed an Officer of the Order of Canada in 2017.
- He received the Herzberg Canada Gold Medal in 2021.
- He has been identified as a Fellow of the APS, Optica, and the Royal Society of Canada.
- He was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society in 2025.
