## Description

[[bionutrients-fermented-plant-extracts]] (also called bionutrients in [[korean-natural-farming]]) are concentrated liquid preparations made by fermenting plant materials with sugar or molasses. The fermentation process, driven by beneficial microorganisms, breaks down plant cell walls and extracts active ingredients more effectively than simple infusion (tea-making) or decoction (boiling). These extracts serve as foliar fertilizers, pest and disease controls, soil builders, and microbial feeds [[cho-loess-powder-in-natural-farming]] systems.

## Classification

- **Category**: Bionutrient / agricultural input
- **System**: Korean Natural Farming (KNF)
- **Process**: Anaerobic fermentation
- **Application**: Foliar spray, soil drench, compost activator, animal supplement

## Key Facts

- Fermentation is superior to simple extraction (infusion/boiling) because microorganisms break down plant materials biologically, making nutrients more bioavailable.
- The basic formula: add 1/3 crude sugar or molasses to the weight/volume of plant material to be fermented.
- Ferment for 7+ days in a container with 50-75% air gap, loosely covered.
- After fermentation, strain the liquid—this is the pure extract. Dilute 1:20 with water for use (2-4 tablespoons per gallon).
- Plant part potency hierarchy: seed (most potent) > fruit > leaf > other parts. Seeds require more dilution (1:1000 vs 1:500).
- Extracts can be used for plants (foliar spray, soil drench), animals (added to water/feed), compost piles, and as food for beneficial microorganisms.
- The active ingredients available depend on the source plant—fermentation makes even difficult-to-extract compounds (like those in seaweed) accessible.

## Specific Extract Types

### Kangkong (Water Spinach) Fermented Extract
- Growth promotant rich in natural gibberellins, auxins, and cytokinins.
- Made from axillary buds of fast-growing plants.

### BSP (Banana-Squash-Papaya) Fermented Extract
- Flowering and fruiting promotant.
- High in potassium (especially banana) and beta-carotene.
- US substitutes: comfrey, squash, and carrot.

### Papaya Fermented Extract
- Rich in papain enzyme, beta-carotene, and vitamin C.
- Made from ripe, unwashed, unpeeled papaya.

### Seaweed Fermented Extract
- Rich in natural growth hormones [[teaming-with-nutrients-micronutrients-and-trace-elements]].
- Fermentation breaks down tough seaweed cell walls better than simple infusion.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- The fermentation process involves fungi (yeasts, fermenting fungi) and bacteria working together—the same organisms central to mycology.
- Fermented plant extracts can be used to feed beneficial soil fungi, supporting [[mycorrhizal-networks-and-forest-communication]] soil food web health.
- The extraction methodology parallels tincturing and extraction methods used to isolate bioactive compounds from mushroom fruiting bodies.
- Adding these extracts to compost or substrate enriches the microbial food web that supports both plant and fungal growth.
- Plant growth hormones in these extracts may have applications in stimulating mycelial growth and fruiting initiation.
