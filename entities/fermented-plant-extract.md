## Description

[[korean-natural-farming]]) are concentrated liquid preparations made by fermenting plant materials with sugar or molasses. The fermentation process, driven by beneficial microorganisms, breaks down plant cell walls and extracts active ingredients more effectively than simple infusion (tea-making) or decoction (boiling). These extracts serve as foliar fertilizers, pest and disease controls, soil builders, and microbial feeds [[teaming-with-nutrients-micronutrients-and-trace-elements]].
- Fermentation breaks down tough seaweed cell walls better than simple infusion.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- The fermentation process involves fungi (yeasts, fermenting fungi) and bacteria working together—the same organisms central to mycology.
