## Bioactive Compounds

The genus *Hydnellum* is known to produce a range of bioactive secondary
metabolites. Several congeners yield pigmented compounds with antimicrobial and
antioxidant properties, including thelephoric acid and atromentic acid.

Specific chemistry of *H. peatii* remains under-investigated relative to
