# MAC (Miracle Alien Cookies)

## Overview

**MAC**, also known as **Miracle Alien Cookies**, is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain created by breeder Capulator.

It was bred by crossing **Alien Cookies** with **Miracle 15** and **Colombian** genetics.

The strain first gained recognition in the California cannabis market around 2016–2018.

MAC has since become one of the most sought-after modern hybrids in North American dispensaries.

It is widely regarded for its exceptional resin production, complex flavor, and potent effects.

The strain is clone-only, meaning authentic genetics are preserved through vegetative propagation rather than seed.

Multiple phenotypic expressions exist, with variations in color, density, and terpene emphasis.

## Lineage

MAC is a multi-way cross combining three distinct genetic lineages.

The primary parent is **Alien Cookies**, itself a cross of **Girl Scout Cookies** and **Alien Technology**.

**Miracle 15** contributes Afghani indica structure and resin density to the genetic profile.

**Colombian** landrace sativa genetics add uplifting cerebral effects and tropical flavor notes.

The resulting hybrid expresses a roughly 50/50 indica-to-sativa balance.

Genetic testing places MAC firmly within the Cookies family lineage.

It shares genetic markers with other Cookies-dominant strains like Wedding Cake and Gelato.

The Colombian landrace influence distinguishes MAC from many of its Cookies-family siblings.

## Appearance

MAC buds are visually striking and consistently rank among the most attractive modern hybrids.

Nugs are medium to large, with a dense, chunky structure typical of Cookies-dominant genetics.

The calyxes are tightly packed with thick, curling pistils ranging from orange to deep rust.

Colors range from bright lime green to deep forest green, often with purple and blue undertones.

Some phenotypes display striking pink and magenta hues, especially in cooler growing conditions.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving buds a frosted, almost white appearance.

The resin layer is thick and sticky, coating the fingers with a viscous film when handled.

Leaf structure is minimal with well-trimmed sugar leaves, reflecting quality cultivation practices.

## Aroma and Terpene Profile

MAC presents a complex and distinctive aroma that sets it apart from other Cookies crosses.

The primary scent is a sour, diesel-like musk with underlying notes of earth and funk.

Secondary aromas include floral, citrus, and tropical fruit characteristics from the Colombian parent.

The flavor profile mirrors the aroma, delivering a sour, creamy smoke with a floral exhale.

Dominant terpenes include **myrcene**, **caryophyllene**, and **limonene**.

**Linalool** is also present in notable amounts, contributing floral and calming properties.

The terpene profile is more diverse than many modern hybrids, with detectable pinene and humulene.

Terpene content is typically high, often exceeding 2–3% total by weight in well-grown samples.

The unique sour-diesel-floral combination is considered MAC's signature aromatic fingerprint.

## Cannabinoid Profile and Effects

MAC is a high-potency strain with THC levels consistently testing between 20–27%.

CBD content is very low, typically below 0.1%, as is common in Cookies-family hybrids.

Minor cannabinoids such as CBG and THCV may be present in trace amounts.

The onset of effects is relatively fast, beginning with a cerebral uplift and creative energy.

Initial sativa-dominant effects transition into a full-body relaxation as the high progresses.

Users report euphoria, enhanced mood, and increased sociability during the early phase.

The comedown is smooth and relaxing without heavy sedation at moderate doses.

Medical users commonly employ MAC for stress relief, mild pain management, and appetite stimulation.

The balanced effect profile makes it suitable for daytime or early evening use at lower doses.

At higher doses, sedative effects become more pronounced, potentially leading to couch-lock.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and occasional anxiety in sensitive users.

## Cultivation

MAC is considered a moderately difficult strain to cultivate, best suited for experienced growers.

It is available exclusively as cuttings (clones), which limits access to authentic genetics.

The strain performs well in both indoor and outdoor environments.

Indoor flowering time is approximately 9–10 weeks from the onset of the 12/12 light cycle.

Plants exhibit moderate stretch during the early flowering phase before filling out densely.

Yields are above average, with well-managed indoor plants producing 400–500 g/m².

MAC responds well to training techniques including LST, SCROG, and defoliation.

Nutrient requirements are moderate, with careful attention needed during mid-flowering to avoid tip burn.

The strain is moderately resistant to common pests and molds but requires good airflow.

Pruning lower canopy growth improves bud development and reduces disease risk in dense foliage.
