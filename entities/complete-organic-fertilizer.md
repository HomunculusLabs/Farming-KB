## Description

Complete Organic Fertilizer (COF) is a custom-blended organic fertilizer formula developed by Steve Solomon. It is designed to provide a complete and balanced spectrum of plant nutrients including nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium, sulfur, and trace minerals that is not available from commercial chemical or organic fertilizers. COF is mixed by the gardener from bulk agricultural ingredients and applied alongside modest amounts of compost or manure.

## Classification

- **Type**: Organic fertilizer blend
- **Developer**: Steve Solomon
- **Application method**: Broadcast and mixed into soil, or applied as side-dressing
- **Nutrient scope**: Complete (NPK + Ca + Mg + S + trace minerals)

## Key Facts

- COF is measured and mixed by volume (scoop, bucket, or jar), NOT by weight
- The base recipe includes seedmeal, agricultural lime, gypsum, dolomite lime, and a phosphorus source
- Optional but recommended ingredients include kelpmeal or basalt dust for trace minerals
- Application rate: approximately 4-6 liters (1-1.5 gallons) per 100 square feet of raised bed, or per 50 feet of row
- COF takes approximately two months to fully decompose in warm soil, providing a steady release of nutrients
- In hot climates with warm soils, smaller applications decompose faster and last a shorter time
- When used at recommended rates, COF automatically distributes about the right amount of calcium per 1,000 square feet per year
- The formula eliminates the need for separate liming when used consistently
- COF produces vegetables with significantly higher nutritional content than those grown with chemical fertilizers

## COF Formula (by volume)

**Base nitrogen source** (choose one):
- 4 parts seedmeal (any kind except coprameal), OR
- 3 parts seedmeal + 1/2 part tankage (blood-and-bone or meatmeal), OR
- 4 parts coprameal + 1/2 part tankage to boost nitrogen

**Calcium sources** (blend all):
- 1 part agricultural lime (finely ground)
- 1 part gypsum (or double the agricultural lime if gypsum is unavailable)
- 1/2 part dolomite lime

**Phosphorus source** (choose one):
- 1 part finely ground rock phosphate (hard or soft rock)
- 1 part bonemeal
- 1 part high-phosphate guano

**Trace minerals** (optional, choose one):
- 1/2 part kelpmeal, OR
- 1 part basalt dust

## Relevance to Cultivation

- COF addresses the fundamental nutritional deficiency of most garden soils by providing all essential elements in balanced proportions
- Commercial chemical fertilizers (e.g., 16-16-16 complete blends) are actually incomplete since they lack calcium, magnesium, and most trace minerals
- Pushing soil potassium too high (a common commercial practice) increases bulk yield but decreases nutritional content proportionally
- COF combined with modest compost produces incredibly healthy plants with superior taste
- The slow-release nature of COF matches nutrient availability to plant uptake, avoiding the boom-bust cycle of chemical fertilizers
- For high-demand crops, additional side-dressings of COF are applied every few weeks after seedling establishment

## Nutrient Ratios and Analysis

Complete organic fertilizers typically aim for an NPK ratio near
5-5-5 using blended organic materials. Common ingredients include
seed meals, bone meal, kelp meal, and agricultural lime.

## Application Rates

Standard application is 2-3 kg per 10 square meters of garden
bed, worked into the top 15 cm of soil before planting. Side-
dressing at mid-season supports heavy-feeding crops.

## Mixing Your Own Blend

Homemade COF formulations allow customization based on soil test
results. The basic Solomon recipe combines seed meals, lime,
and trace mineral sources in specific proportions.
