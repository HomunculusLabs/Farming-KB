## Lineage and History

Chem 91 is one of several phenotypically distinct cuts propagated from the original Chemdawg seed stock discovered at a 1991 Grateful Dead show in Deer Creek, Indiana.

The original Chemdawg seeds were found in a bag of high-quality cannabis purchased by a person known only as "Chemdog."

Chem 91 was stabilized by Chemdog and his associates and represents one of four principal Chemdawg phenotypes: Chem 91, Chem D (Chemdawg D), Chem 4, and Chem Sis.

Chem 91 is the direct parent of **[[girl-scout-cookies]]** through subsequent crosses.

The cut was originally shared within a small circle of East Coast growers before migrating to California in the early 2000s.

Its influence on modern [[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]]: THCV traces in some phenotypes.

The terpene-to-cannabinoid ratio produces what breeders describe as a "gassy" or "chemmy" chemotype, characteristic of the Diesel/Kush family.

## Effects

Chem 91 delivers a fast-acting, cerebral onset that transitions into deep physical relaxation.

The high begins with an immediate head rush characterized by euphoria, heightened [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], stress, anxiety, and insomnia.
