# Chem 91

## Overview

Chem 91 (also written Chem '91 or Chemdog '91) is a foundational [[cannabis-hybrid-vigor-and-phenotypes-clarke]] that serves as the direct parent or grandparent of dozens of the most influential modern strains.

It emerged from the legendary Chemdawg phenotype lineage that surfaced in the early 1990s at a [[oner-chemdawg-origin-legend-grateful-dead-genetics]] concert in Colorado.

Chem 91 is distinguished from other Chemdawg cuts by its distinctly pungent, chemically diesel aroma and balanced hybrid effects.

It is widely regarded as the most potent of the original Chemdawg phenotypes, with [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]] regularly testing between 22–28%.

## Lineage and History

Chem 91 is one of several phenotypically distinct cuts propagated from the original Chemdawg seed stock discovered at a 1991 Grateful Dead show in Deer Creek, Indiana.

The original Chemdawg seeds were found in a bag of high-quality cannabis purchased by a person known only as "Chemdog."

Chem 91 was stabilized by Chemdog and his associates and represents one of four principal Chemdawg phenotypes: Chem 91, Chem D (Chemdawg D), Chem 4, and Chem Sis.

Chem 91 is the direct parent of **[[sour-diesel]]**, **[[girl-scout-cookies]]** through subsequent crosses.

The cut was originally shared within a small circle of East Coast growers before migrating to California in the early 2000s.

Its influence on modern [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]] is difficult to overstate — most contemporary high-THC hybrid lines trace back to Chem 91 or its immediate descendants.

## Appearance and Aroma

**Bud structure:** Medium to large, densely packed calyxes with a chunky, somewhat irregular formation.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving buds a frosted, crystalline appearance under magnification.

Colors range from bright lime green to darker forest green, with occasional flashes of copper-orange pistils.

**Aroma:** Intensely pungent, dominated by sharp diesel fuel and chemical notes with an undertone of sour citrus and damp earth.

The scent is immediately recognizable and can fill a room — it is one of the defining characteristics of the Chemdawg line.

Breaking open the buds releases additional layers of skunky, peppery, and slightly floral nuances.

**Flavor:** Mirrors the aroma profile, delivering a thick, acrid smoke with diesel, earth, and sour lemon notes on the exhale.

The aftertaste lingers with a chemical tang that gives the strain its name.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- **Limonene** (~1.2–2.0%): Citrus aroma, mood elevation, stress relief.
- **Myrcene** (~0.8–1.4%): Earthy, musky base notes, sedative contribution.
- **Caryophyllene** (~0.5–0.9%): Peppery spiciness, anti-inflammatory properties.

Secondary terpenes include pinene, linalool, and humulene in trace amounts.

**Cannabinoid profile:**

- THC: 22–28% (occasionally testing above 30% in top-shelf phenotype selections).
- CBD: Typically below 0.1% (negligible).
- [[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]]: THCV traces in some phenotypes.

The terpene-to-cannabinoid ratio produces what breeders describe as a "gassy" or "chemmy" chemotype, characteristic of the Diesel/Kush family.

## Effects

Chem 91 delivers a fast-acting, cerebral onset that transitions into deep physical relaxation.

The high begins with an immediate head rush characterized by euphoria, heightened [[plant-sensory-perception]], and creative energy.

This cerebral phase gradually gives way to a heavy body stone that can be profoundly sedative at higher doses.

Users commonly report relief from [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], stress, anxiety, and insomnia.
