# Pongamia

## Description

Pongamia is a leguminous tree genus, with the primary species *[[indian-beech]]. In [[green-manure]]**: Leaf biomass incorporated as nitrogen-rich green manure

## Botanical Characteristics

- **Leaves**: Alternate, imparipinnate with 5-7 leaflets, glossy green above, silvery beneath
- **Flowers**: White to purplish-pink, born in racemes; bee-attracting
- **Fruit**: Elliptical pods containing 1-2 seeds
- **Roots**: Extensive lateral root system with nitrogen-fixing nodules
- **Bark**: Gray-brown, relatively smooth
- **Crown**: Dense, spreading canopy providing good shade

## Environmental Tolerances

- **Rainfall**: 500-2,500 mm annually (survives lower with established roots)
- **Temperature**: Tropical to subtropical; damaged by frost when young
- **Soil**: Wide range including saline, alkaline, and poorly drained soils
- **Altitude**: Sea level to 1,200 m
- **Salt tolerance**: Moderate to high, suitable for coastal and arid saline areas

## Management Notes

- Propagate from seed or vegetative cuttings
- Fast-growing in favorable conditions; 3-5 m in 3-4 years
- Prune to maintain desired shape and encourage bushy growth for windbreak function
- Seed production begins at 4-7 years of age
- Trees can live 80-100+ years
- Coppices readily when cut, regenerating from the stump
- Seed cake (after oil extraction) makes excellent [[complete-organic-fertilizer]]
