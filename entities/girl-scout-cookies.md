The exact genetics have been debated since the strain's commercial
debut. The breeder, known by the pseudonym "Jigga," initially worked
within the Cookie Fam collective in San Francisco. Multiple phenotypes
were released under the GSC umbrella, with Thin Mint, Forum Cut, and
the original GSC being the most sought-after. The Forum Cut, stabilized
by the Cookie Fam, became the genetic basis for much of the subsequent
commercial breeding.

## Cannabinoid and Terpene Profile

### Cannabinoids
- **THC**: 17-28% (tested averages), with select phenotypes exceeding 30%
- **CBD**: Typically below 0.1% (negligible)
- **Primary cannabinoids**: THCA dominates the cannabinoid fraction
- The high THC content was considered exceptional at the time of its
  release and contributed significantly to its reputation

### Terpene Profile

The combined terpene profile produces the strain's signature aroma:
sweet, earthy, and slightly minty with underlying notes of fuel and
forest floor. The name "Cookies" derives from the sweet, doughy quality
of the bouquet, particularly in the Thin Mint phenotype.

## Phenotype Variants

Multiple distinct phenotypes were circulated within the Cookie Fam and
beyond, each with subtle differences:

- **Thin Mint Cookies**: The most awarded and commercially propagated
  phenotype. Darker green-purple foliage, dense trichome coverage, and
  a pronounced minty-sweet aroma. Considered the most potent variant.
- **Forum Cut**: Widely regarded as the most stable commercial cut.
  Vigorous growth, uniform structure, and consistent terpene profile.
  This became the basis for most commercial GSC breeding programs.
- **Original GSC**: The earliest phenotype, slightly less uniform than
  later selections but retaining the classic aroma profile.
- **Platinum Cookies**: A lighter-colored variant with silver-white
  trichome coverage, slightly sweeter aroma, and more sativa-leaning
  effects.

## Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure
GSC grows as a medium-height indica-dominant hybrid. Plants typically
reach 90-150 cm indoors. The structure is bushy with multiple lateral
branches and strong intermodal spacing. The leaf pattern is broad-leafed
with dark green foliage that can develop purple and violet hues under
cool night temperatures, especially in the final 2-3 weeks of flowering.

### Flowering
- **Indoor flowering time**: 9-10 weeks from 12/12 flip
