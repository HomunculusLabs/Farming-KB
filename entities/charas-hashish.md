# Charas (Hand-Rubbed Hashish)

## Description

Charas is a traditional form of hand-made hashish produced primarily in Nepal, India, and Afghanistan by manually rubbing live cannabis flowers between the palms to collect the resin that adheres to the skin. The collected resin is then scraped off and rolled into balls or sticks. Charas is characterized by its sticky, pliable consistency — distinctly different from the dry, pressed hashish commonly produced in Morocco and Lebanon. In *Cannabis Alchemy*, charas is referenced as a benchmark for the desired texture and consistency when preparing hashish from concentrated cannabis oil.

## Classification

- **Category**: [[spagyric-cannabis-preparation]] / Traditional hashish
- **Origin**: Nepal, India, Afghanistan, Himalayan regions
- **Consistency**: Sticky, pliable, soft
- **Production method**: Hand-rubbing live cannabis flowers

## Key Facts

- Produced by rubbing fresh, living cannabis flowers (typically *Cannabis indica*) between the palms or between hands and the plant surface. The glandular trichomes rupture and the sticky resin accumulates on the skin.
- The dark, sticky resin mass is periodically scraped from the hands and rolled into balls, sticks, or "temple balls" — a traditional form in Nepal.
- Unlike dry-sift hashish (which uses dried and sieved material), charas is made from living plants, preserving more of the terpene profile and resulting in a distinctly aromatic and flavorful product.
- The consistency of properly made charas is described as "sticky and pliable" — this is the texture benchmark used in *Cannabis Alchemy* for hashish made with 50-60% oil content.
- Regional varieties include Malana cream (from the Parvati Valley, India), which is considered among the finest charas in the world.
- Traditional production is labor-intensive: a skilled worker may produce only a few grams of charas per day from field-grown cannabis.
- In cannabis alchemy, the charas texture is recreated by mixing concentrated, refined cannabis oil with finely powdered marijuana at 15-60% oil ratios and kneading the mass by hand or in a mortar.

## Preparation Methods (Traditional)

1. Walk through a field of mature, flowering female cannabis plants.
2. Rub the flowers between both palms with moderate pressure for several minutes.
3. A dark, sticky layer of resin accumulates on the palms and between fingers.
4. Periodically scrape the accumulated resin from the hands using a dull blade.
5. Roll the collected resin into balls, sticks, or "temple balls."
6. Store in a cool, dry place. The charas improves with aging as it cures.

## Preparation Methods (Reconstituted, from Cannabis Alchemy)

1. Prepare finely powdered, de-tarred marijuana dust.
2. Heat the dust and purified cannabis oil separately.
3. Mix in a large mortar or knead by hand to combine.
4. Form into patties (Afghanistan style), fingers, temple balls (Nepal style), or press between heated steel plates in a vise for flat sheets.
5. Minimum 15% oil for Moroccan/Lebanese consistency; up to 60% oil for maximum strength with charas-like pliability.
