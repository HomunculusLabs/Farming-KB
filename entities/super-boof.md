# Super Boof

## Overview

Super Boof is a modern cannabis hybrid strain created by crossing Black Cherry
Punch with Tropicana Cookies. Bred by Black Cherry Breeder X, it gained rapid
popularity in the early 2020s for its striking visual appeal and potent
effects.

The strain earned its unusual name from its over-the-top potency and
intensely colored buds, combining "super" with a slang term for an exaggerated,
over-the-top experience. It has become a staple in West Coast dispensaries
and is frequently recognized on curated strain menus.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Black Cherry Punch — a heavy indica-dominant hybrid
  known for dark purple coloration and cherry terpene profile
- **Male parent:** Tropicana Cookies — a balanced hybrid contributing
  citrus and cookie-dough terpenes with moderate THC content

The genetic ratio is approximately 50/50 indica to sativa, though phenotype
expression varies. Some cuts lean slightly indica-dominant with heavier
sedation, while others present more cerebrally focused sativa effects.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large with a dense, chunky structure. The most notable
feature is the intense coloration: deep purple and forest green calyxes
covered in bright orange pistils that create a striking contrast.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosted,
nearly white appearance under magnification. This resin density translates to
high potency and makes the strain popular for solvent extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma opens with strong notes of ripe black cherry and tropical citrus,
underpinned by a pungent, funky earthiness. Secondary notes of grape candy
and subtle diesel emerge when buds are broken apart.

On the palate, Super Boof delivers a complex flavor profile blending sweet
cherry, orange zest, and cookie-dough richness. The exhale brings out earthy,
slightly peppery notes that linger on the tongue.

## Terpene Profile

- **Caryophyllene** (dominant) — peppery, spicy, contributes to the
  strain's anti-inflammatory potential
- **Limonene** (secondary) — citrus notes, mood elevation
- **Myrcene** (secondary) — earthy, sedative undertone
- **Linalool** (trace) — floral, calming complement

## Cannabinoid Profile

THC content ranges from 25% to 30%, placing Super Boof firmly in the
high-potency category. CBD is typically below 0.1%. Minor cannabinoids
including CBG and CBC may be present in trace amounts.

## Effects

Onset is rapid, typically within five to ten minutes of inhalation. Initial
effects are cerebral and euphoric, characterized by elevated mood and
heightened sensory perception.

As the high progresses, a warm body relaxation emerges without heavy
couch-lock sedation. Most users report a balanced experience suitable for
both daytime social use and evening relaxation, depending on dosage.

Common reported effects include euphoria, creativity, and physical comfort.
Higher doses may produce more pronounced sedation and introspection.

## Cultivation

Flowering time is approximately 8 to 9 weeks indoors. Plants exhibit
moderate stretch during the transition phase and respond well to training
techniques including low-stress training and scrog netting.

Indoor yields range from 400 to 500 grams per square meter. Outdoor
cultivation is possible in warm, Mediterranean climates with harvest
occurring in early to mid-October.

The strain is considered moderately difficult to cultivate. It can be
sensitive to nutrient overload, particularly during mid-flowering, and
requires careful monitoring of pH and EC levels.
