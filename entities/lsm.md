## Overview

**[[serotonin-psilocybin-structural-relationship-hallucinogenic-mechanism]] lysergamide and a close [[lysergic-acid-diethylamide]]). It is one of several lysergamide
derivatives explored during the mid-20th century as part of systematic research into
structure-activity relationships of ergoline-based [[mckenna-food-gods-lsd-invention-hofmann-sandoz-psychedelic-era]] Laboratories
and is catalogued in [[alexander-shulgin]]'s *TiHKAL* (Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved)
under entry #26. It is classified as a Schedule I controlled substance in the United
States under the Federal Analogue Act.

## Chemical Properties

- **Molecular formula:** C₂₀H₂₄N₂O₃
- **Molecular weight:** 340.42 g/mol
- **IUPAC name:** (6aR,9R)-7-methyl-4,6,6a,7,8,9-hexahydroindolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxylic acid morpholine amide
- **SMILES:** CC1CN(C2CC3=CNC4=CC=CC(=C34)C2=C1)C(=O)N5CCOCC5
- **Appearance:** [[query-difference-between-white-rot-and-brown-rot-fungi]] from LSD.
In LSD, the amide nitrogen bears two ethyl groups (diethylamide), while in LSM the
nitrogen is incorporated into a morpholine ring system.

This cyclization constrains the conformational flexibility of the amide group and
introduces a polar oxygen atom. The effects on [[taar1-trace-amine-associated-receptor-psychedelic-pharmacology]] section.

LSM is soluble in organic solvents including ethanol, methanol, and DMSO, and insoluble
in water. Like other lysergamides, it is sensitive to light, heat, and oxidation.

## Pharmacology

LSM acts primarily as a partial agonist at the serotonin 5-HT₂A receptor, the same
receptor primarily responsible for the psychedelic effects of LSD and psilocin.

The morpholine ring modification reduces receptor affinity compared to LSD but does not
eliminate psychedelic activity. In animal models and human reports, LSM produces
effects qualitatively similar to LSD but with reduced potency and altered duration.

The compound also shows affinity for 5-HT₁A, 5-HT₂C, and dopamine D₂ receptors,
consistent with the broader lysergamide [[dmt-vs-lsd-pharmacological-comparison-onset-duration-receptor]] profile. The exact binding
constants for LSM have not been as thoroughly characterized as those for LSD.

Metabolism likely proceeds via N-dealkylation and aromatic hydroxylation pathways
common to ergoline derivatives. The morpholine ring may confer greater metabolic
stability than the more labile diethylamide group of LSD, potentially contributing
to differences in duration and onset.

## Effects

- **Onset:** 60–120 minutes (slower than LSD)
- **Duration:** 8–12 hours
- **Visual threshold:** Approximately 100–200 µg (estimated; less potent than LSD)
- **Common dose range:** 150–400 µg

The subjective effects of LSM closely parallel those of LSD and other classical
psychedelics, including visual hallucinations, enhanced pattern recognition,
synesthesia, altered time perception, and emotional amplification.
