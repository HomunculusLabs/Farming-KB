# Miprocin (4-HO-MiPT)

## Overview

**Miprocin**, chemically known as **4-hydroxy-N-methyl-N-isopropyltryptamine** (4-HO-MiPT), is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine compound closely related to psilocin (4-HO-DMT) and the other substituted tryptamines documented by Alexander Shulgin in *TiHKAL* (Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved). It acts primarily as a 5-HT₂A receptor agonist, producing effects broadly similar to those of [[forensic-identification-methods-psilocybin-mushrooms-tlc-gc-hplc]] — including visual hallucinations, altered perception of time, euphoria, and changes in thought patterns — but with a pharmacological and subjective profile that users consistently describe as more "recreational," color-rich, and less introspective than psilocin.

Miprocin is one of the more well-characterized members of the 4-hydroxy-N-substituted tryptamine family. Its isopropyl substitution pattern on the terminal amine nitrogen gives it a distinct pharmacokinetic and subjective character compared to the more familiar psilocin (dimethyl substitution) and 4-HO-MET (ethyl substitution). The compound exists both as a freebase and as a fumarate salt for research purposes.

## Structural Classification

Miprocin belongs to the **4-hydroxy tryptamine** subclass, which can be further characterized:

| Compound | Abbreviation | N-Substitution | Key Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psilocin | 4-HO-DMT | Dimethyl | Natural, benchmark psychedelic tryptamine |
| 4-HO-MET | Metocin | Methyl-ethyl | Slightly less visual, more recreational |
| **4-HO-MiPT** | **Miprocin** | **Methyl-isopropyl** | **Color-rich, less heady, recreational** |
| 4-HO-DiPT | Recin | Diisopropyl | Strong body load, shorter duration |
| 4-HO-DET | Ethocin | Diethyl | More visual, dreamy character |
| 4-HO-PT | — | Methyl-n-propyl | Longer duration, stronger body load |

The methyl-isopropyl substitution pattern is unique to miprocin among the commonly encountered 4-hydroxy tryptamines. The bulky isopropyl group is believed to affect [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]] kinetics and metabolic stability relative to the smaller dimethyl substitution of psilocin.

## Pharmacology

### Mechanism of Action

Like other classical psychedelics, miprocin acts primarily as a **partial agonist at the 5-HT₂A serotonin receptor**, the receptor most responsible for psychedelic effects. It also shows activity at other serotonin receptor subtypes:

- **5-HT₂A:** Primary site of psychedelic action; activation of cortical layer V pyramidal neurons produces characteristic perceptual and cognitive effects
- **5-HT₂C:** Contributes to mild anxiogenic effects and appetite changes
- **5-HT₁A:** Partial agonist activity may contribute anxiolytic and calming components
- The isopropyl group likely reduces affinity for the serotonin transporter (SERT) relative to more polar tryptamines

### Pharmacokinetics

- **Onset of action:** 20–60 minutes (oral); 5–15 minutes (insufflation)
- **Time to peak:** 1.5–2.5 hours (oral)
- **Duration of effects:** 4–6 hours (oral); 3–5 hours (insufflation)
- **Metabolism:** Presumed hepatic via MAO oxidation and CYP450 enzymes. The 4-hydroxy group may undergo glucuronidation similar to psilocin. The isopropyl group may confer some resistance to oxidative deamination.
- **Active metabolites:** Possible dealkylation products have not been well characterized.

## Dosage Ranges

| Route | Threshold | Light | Common | Strong | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oral (mg) | 5–8 | 8–12 | 12–20 | 20–30 | 30+ |
| Insufflation (mg) | 2–4 | 4–7 | 7–12 | 12–18 | 18+ |
**Note:** Dosage information is derived from self-reports and Shulgin's TiHKAL documentation. Miprocin has not been through formal clinical dose-finding studies. Individual sensitivity varies significantly. The fumarate salt contains approximately 70% active compound by weight; salt-based doses must be adjusted upward by ~43% to match freebase-equivalent doses.

## Subjective Effects

### Perceptual Effects

- **Visual alterations:** Enhanced color saturation and vibrancy (consistently reported as more pronounced than psilocin); geometric pattern recognition; flowing, breathing surfaces; closed-eye imagery described as "crystalline" or "jewel-toned"; mild tracers at common doses
- **Auditory effects:** Enhanced music appreciation; mild synesthesia (sound-to-color) at moderate doses; sounds may seem richer and more spatially defined
- **Tactile effects:** Increased tactile sensitivity; pleasant body sensations; less physical discomfort ("body load") compared to 4-HO-DiPT or 5-MeO-MiPT

### Cognitive and Emotional Effects

- **Mood:** Generally euphoric and light-hearted; described as less emotionally intense than psilocin
- **Thought patterns:** Playful, associative thinking with reduced introspective depth; users frequently describe the experience as "fun"
- **Ego effects:** Mild ego softening at moderate doses; full dissolution uncommon at typical doses
- **Social effects:** Enhanced sociability; often considered a good "party psychedelic"

### Comparison to Related Compounds

- **Vs. psilocin (4-HO-DMT):** More colorful visuals, less introspective, shorter duration, lighter headspace
- **Vs. 4-HO-MET (metocin):** Similar recreational character; miprocin is slightly more visual and slightly less stimulating
- **Vs. LSD:** Much shorter duration; less mentally demanding; more colorful but less intricate visuals

## TiHKAL Entry Summary

Alexander Shulgin documented miprocin as **TiHKAL Entry #33**, with an active oral dose range of 12–25 mg. He described the quality as "more colorful and less philosophical than psilocin" and "good for visual appreciation and social interaction." The compound was synthesized from 4-hydroxyindole via reductive amination. No unusual toxicity was noted in animal screening. Shulgin described it as "an excellent example of how subtle changes in N-substitution can dramatically alter the character of a tryptamine experience."

## Legal Status

- **United States:** Not explicitly scheduled federally but may fall under the **Federal Analog Act** as a psilocin analog (Schedule I).
- **United Kingdom:** Controlled as a Class A drug under the Psychoactive [[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] 2016.
- **International:** Not scheduled by the UN Convention on Psychotropic Substances, but many countries have domestic controls covering substituted tryptamines.

## Harm Reduction Notes

- Miprocin has no established [[cannabis-therapeutic-index-overview]] or formal toxicology profile
- [[lsd-set-and-setting-framework]] remain critical determinants of experience quality
- Serotonin syndrome risk is theoretical but low when used alone; combining with SSRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs is strongly discouraged
- Users with personal or family history of psychotic disorders should exercise caution with all classical psychedelics
- Purity and identity of gray-market products cannot be guaranteed without analytical testing; trip sitting is recommended at doses above 20 mg
