## Feijoa

**Type:** Species (Plant)
**Category:** Evergreen fruiting shrub
**Also known as:** Pineapple guava, Feijoa sellowiana, Acca sellowiana

## Overview
Feijoa is a bushy, evergreen shrub native to southern Brazil, northern
Argentina, western Paraguay, and Uruguay, widely cultivated for its uniquely
flavoured fruit. The fruit combines flavours of pineapple, guava, and
strawberry, reflected in its common name pineapple guava. It is valued in
permaculture systems as both a productive fruit crop and an effective windbreak
species, tolerating considerable cold for a subtropical plant.

## Key Details

### Botanical Classification
- **Family:** Myrtaceae
- **Genus:** Feijoa (sometimes classified as Acca)
- **Species:** F. sellowiana

### Growth Characteristics
- **Habit:** Bushy evergreen shrub, 1-6 m tall
- **Growth rate:** Moderate
- **Root system:** Shallow and fibrous
- **Pollination:** Benefits from cross-pollination for best fruit set

### Environmental Requirements
- **Soil:** Prefers deep, rich, organic soil kept moist through summer
- **Water:** [[mollison-designers-mixed-orchard-design-species-selection]] systems. The high
iodine content of the fruit is an unusual nutritional benefit.
