# Dolichos

## Description

Dolichos is a genus of leguminous vines and trailing plants in the family Fabaceae, valued in permaculture systems as multi-functional plants providing edible pods and seeds, nitrogen fixation, shade coverage, mulch biomass, and forage for livestock. In [[lablab-purpureus]]*) — hyacinth bean
- **Growth habit**: Perennial or annual climbing/trailing vines
- **Common names**: Hyacinth bean, lablab bean, dolichos bean, Egyptian bean, Tonga bean

## Key Facts

- **Edible legume**: Produces edible pods, immature seeds (cooked as vegetable), and mature dried seeds as a protein-rich pulse
- **Shade vine**: Listed alongside Mikania and Pelargonium as standing dense evergreen vine crops for western sun exposure in desert home cooling systems
- **Nitrogen fixation**: As a legume, fixes atmospheric nitrogen through rhizobial root nodules, enriching soil fertility
- **Mulch production**: Vigorous vine growth produces substantial biomass for garden mulch when pruned
- **Forage crop**: Provides nutritious forage for small domestic livestock (poultry, rabbits, guinea pigs)
- **Desert adaptation**: Several species tolerate heat and drought conditions, making them suitable for [[dryland-permaculture-strategies]] systems

## Relevance to Cultivation

### Permaculture Functions

Dolichos serves multiple integrated functions in designed permaculture systems:

1. **House cooling vine**: Grown as a standing dense evergreen vine on trellis structures out from western building walls, blocking intense afternoon sun in desert environments
2. **Garden shade**: Trained on trellis above vegetable beds to reduce light saturation and lower ambient temperatures
3. **Living fence**: Rapid vine growth creates effective visual barriers and windbreaks
4. **Soil building**: [[legume-nitrogen-fixation-and-green-manure]] leaf drop [[query-how-does-rock-dust-improve-soil-fertility]] around plantings
5. **Food production**: Edible pods and seeds provide dietary protein

### Desert Garden Applications

In Mollison's dryland garden system, Dolichos is specifically valued for:

- **Western shade trellis**: Planted as an evergreen screen on the western exposure to reduce afternoon heat load on buildings
- **Mulch source**: Regular cutting provides nitrogen-rich mulch for garden beds
- **Leguminous ground cover**: Trails along ground suppressing weeds while fixing nitrogen
- **Seed production**: Provides dried beans as a protein source in desert food systems

### Companion Planting

Dolichos integrates well with:

- **Mikania**: Combined on western shade trellis for dense coverage
- **Pelargonium**: Another shade vine species for building cooling
- **Sunflower and Crotalaria**: As part of windbreak border guilds around vegetable beds
- **Acacia species**: As understory or trellis support in [[bill-mollison]], *Permaculture: A Designer's Manual* — Chapter 11 (Dryland Strategies), vine management and shade systems, mulch source species
