# Entoloma hochstetteri

## Overview
Entoloma hochstetteri is a species of mushroom in the family Entolomataceae, notable for its striking all-blue coloration.
Native to New Zealand, it is one of the few entirely blue fungi known to science and an icon of the country's unique [[plants-without-mycorrhizal-associations]].
DNA barcoding using the ITS region has confirmed its distinctiveness from related species and supports [[arbuscule-isolation-and-nutrient-exchange-mechanisms]].
Fruiting occurs from autumn through early winter (March to June), typically after sustained rainfall when soil moisture is high.
It is found in both lowland and montane forest habitats, from sea level to approximately 1000 m elevation.
The species prefers well-drained soils with moderate [[bloomfield-bioluminescent-fungi-overview]] detected weak bioluminescence in Entoloma hochstetteri.
This makes it one of the few bioluminescent Entoloma species known to science.
