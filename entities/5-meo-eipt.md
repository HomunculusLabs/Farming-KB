## Overview

5-MeO-EiPT (5-methoxy-N-ethyl-N-isopropyltryptamine) is a synthetic substituted tryptamine and psychedelic compound. It belongs to the N-alkylated tryptamine class documented in Alexander and [[savory-short-duration-grazing-and-cell-systems]], moderate potency, and a pharmacological profile distinct from both 5-MeO-DMT and 5-MeO-DIPT. It remains a rare research chemical with limited human pharmacological data.

Its primary mechanism of action is agonism at the 5-HT2A [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]], the canonical target of classical psychedelic tryptamines.

## Chemical Properties

**IUPAC Name:** 3-[2-[(Ethyl)(isopropyl)amino]ethyl]-5-methoxy-1H-indole.

**Molecular Formula:** C16H24N2O.

**Molecular Weight:** 260.38 g/mol.

**Structure:** The molecule consists of an indole backbone substituted at the 5-position with a methoxy group. The amine nitrogen carries both an ethyl and an isopropyl substituent, making it a di-substituted N-alkyl tryptamine.

The N-ethyl-N-isopropyl substitution pattern is relatively uncommon among tryptamine psychedelics, with most compounds featuring symmetrical dialkyl groups (e.g., DMT, DIPT, DPT).

5-MeO-EiPT is a freebase oil at room temperature, with the hydrochloride salt forming a crystalline solid. Solubility data in standard solvents is limited due to the compound's rarity.

## Pharmacology

5-MeO-EiPT acts primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist, the receptor most strongly correlated with classical psychedelic effects. Activity at 5-HT1A and 5-HT2C receptors is likely but has not been formally characterized in vitro.

The N-ethyl-N-isopropyl substitution pattern influences [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]] data from formal pharmacological assays is not available in the published literature, making precise potency characterization impossible.

## Effects

The subjective effects of 5-MeO-EiPT are described in TiHKAL based on the Shulgins' personal experimentation at a threshold dose of 2–5 mg. The active dose range is reported as approximately 2–5 mg orally.

Effects include visual distortions, altered time perception, and mild euphoria. The experience is described as less intense and more controllable than 5-MeO-DMT at equivalent doses.

The onset occurs within 15–30 minutes after oral administration, with effects peaking at 45–90 minutes. Total duration is approximately 2–4 hours, shorter than many orally active tryptamines.

Body load is reported as moderate, with some users noting mild gastrointestinal discomfort. The compound is noted for relatively smooth comedown compared to more potent tryptamines.
