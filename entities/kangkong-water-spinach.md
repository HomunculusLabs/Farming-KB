## Description

Kangkong (Ipomoea aquatica), also known as water spinach or water convolvulus, is a semi-aquatic tropical plant grown as a leaf vegetable. In Korean Natural Farming, kangkong is valued as a source material for fermented extracts used as **natural growth promotants** due to its exceptionally rapid growth rate and consequent high concentrations of natural plant hormones.

## Classification

- **Kingdom**: Plantae
- **Family**: Convolvulaceae (morning glory family)
- **Genus**: Ipomoea
- **Species**: I. aquatica
- **Common Names**: Kangkong (Filipino), water spinach, water convolvulus, swamp cabbage, ong choy
- **Growth Habit**: Semi-aquatic herbaceous vine

## Key Facts

- Kangkong grows in **fresh water** and **highly moist soil** environments.
- Its defining characteristic for natural farming purposes is its **rapid growth rate**, comparable to kelp in marine environments.
- Plants that exhibit extremely fast growth naturally contain higher concentrations of **growth hormones**: gibberellins, auxins, and cytokinins.
- The **axillary buds** (growing tips) of kangkong contain the highest concentrations of these growth-promoting compounds.
- Other fast-growing plants suitable for similar growth promotant extracts include cucumber, squash, watermelon, kelp, and mugwort.

## Fermented Extract Preparation

1. Collect kangkong, preferably the axillary buds and growing tips
2. Chop materials finely (unwashed)
3. Add 1/3 crude sugar or molasses by weight/volume
4. Place in container with 50-75% air gap, cover loosely
5. Ferment for at least 7 days
6. Strain liquid — this is the pure growth promotant extract
7. Store with equal parts crude sugar/molasses
8. Dilute 1:20 with non-chlorinated water for use

## Applications in Natural Farming

- **Foliar spray**: Applied to plants to stimulate vegetative growth
- **Soil drench**: Watered into soil to promote root and shoot development
- The extract enhances the growth rate and vigor of treated plants significantly
- Used as a component of comprehensive natural farming programs alongside BIM and other bionutrients

## Relevance to Cultivation

Kangkong extract represents a **locally producible plant growth regulator** that can replace synthetic growth hormones in natural farming systems. For cultivators:

- Provides a natural source of **gibberellins** (stem elongation, seed germination), **auxins** (root development, apical dominance), and **cytokinins** (cell division, shoot formation)
- Can be produced at near-zero cost from a widely available tropical plant
- The fermentation process amplifies and stabilizes the active compounds
- Particularly useful during the **vegetative growth stage** of the nutrioperiodism framework

The principle extends beyond kangkong: **any plant exhibiting exceptionally rapid growth** can be used as a source of natural growth hormones through the same fermentation method. This allows farmers worldwide to identify and use locally available fast-growing species.

## Nutritional Profile (Edible Use)

- Rich in vitamins A and C
- Good source of iron, calcium, and fiber
- Low in calories
- Widely used in Southeast Asian cuisine (stir-fried, in soups, salads)
