## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: N-Hydroxy-N-methyl-3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine.

FLEA is derived from mdma-entity|MDMA by the addition of a hydroxyl group to the nitrogen atom.

The compound is isolated as the oxalate salt rather than the hydrochloride, because the hydrochloride proved difficult to crystallize.

The oxalate salt melts at 146-147 degrees Celsius after recrystallization from acetonitrile.

The free base is obtained as a white oil by distillation at 110-120 degrees Celsius at 0.2 mm/Hg.
