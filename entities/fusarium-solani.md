# Taxonomy

Fusarium solani (Mart.) Sacc. is a soil-inhabiting ascomycete
fungus
belonging to the phylum Ascomycota, class Sordariomycetes, order
Hypocreales, and family Nectriaceae. The species is a member of
the
Fusarium solani species complex (FSSC), a monophyletic clade
containing numerous morphologically similar but genetically
distinct
lineages including plant pathogens, opportunistic animal
pathogens,
and saprotrophic strains. F. solani is one of the most widely
distributed Fusarium species, recovered from soils on every
continent.
