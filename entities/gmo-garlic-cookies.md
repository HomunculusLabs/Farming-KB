## GMO (Garlic Cookies)

**GMO**, also known as **Garlic Cookies**, is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing **[[vic-high-cannabis-genetics-mendelian-inheritance]].

## Genetic Lineage

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Indica-dominant hybrid (~90/10 indica/sativa) |
| **Breeder** | Originally bred by Mamiko Seeds (attributed) |
| **Female parent** | Girl Scout Cookies (GSC) |
| **Male parent** | Chemdawg |
| **Lineage group** | Chemdawg family / Cookie family |
| **THC content** | 20–30% (average 25%) |
| **CBD content** | < 1% |

GMO sits at the intersection of two of the most influential genetic families in modern [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] along with potent psychoactive effects, while Chemdawg contributes its signature diesel-fuel pungency, skunky sharpness, and heavy body effects. The combination produces a strain with one of the most divisive and memorable aromas in cannabis.

## Aroma and Flavor

GMO is famous for its extreme, polarizing aroma — many users describe it as the most garlic-forward strain ever bred:

- **Aroma:** Intensely pungent, dominated by crushed garlic, roasted onions, and fermented mushroom. Underlying notes of diesel fuel, earthy musk, and a faint sweetness from the GSC parent emerge on closer inspection. The garlic character is so pronounced that the strain has been compared to an Italian kitchen or garlic bread.
- **Flavor:** Rich, savory, and umami-driven on the inhale, with garlic, onion, and herbal notes. The exhale reveals diesel, earthy coffee, and a subtle cookie-like sweetness that hints at the GSC lineage. A lingering spicy, peppery finish is common.
- **Smoke:** Thick, heavy, and expansive. The pungent aroma intensifies when combusted and lingers in the room and on the palate for an extended period.

## Terpene Profile

GMO's distinctive aroma and effects are driven by a unique terpene composition dominated by sulfur-containing and highly aromatic compounds:

- **Caryophyllene** (dominant): 0.5–1.5%. This sesquiterpene contributes spicy, peppery, and woody notes. As the only terpene known to directly activate CB2 [[oner-cannabis-terpene-profiles-effects]], adding earthy, herbal notes and enhancing the sedating, couch-lock effects.
- **Humulene**: Present in moderate amounts. Contributes woody, earthy aromas and may suppress appetite.
- **Linalool**: Trace amounts. Adds a subtle floral-sweetness and may enhance the strain's relaxing qualities.

The garlic and onion character is attributed to volatile sulfur compounds (thiols and sulfides) that are not standard terpenes but are present in the trichome resin. These compounds are also found in allium vegetables and contribute to the strain's unique savory profile.

## Effects

GMO delivers overwhelmingly physical, sedating effects consistent with its indica-dominant genetics:

### Onset (0–15 minutes)
- Rapid onset of cerebral pressure behind the eyes and temples
- Mild euphoric lift that is subdued rather than stimulating
- Sensory enhancement: colors appear slightly more vivid, music sounds richer
- Slight cerebral haziness or "fog" begins to develop

### Peak (15–60 minutes)
- Deep, full-body relaxation spreads from the core outward
- Strong physical heaviness (couch-lock) is typical at moderate to high doses
- Mental state becomes dreamy, introspective, and unfocused
- Muscular tension significantly decreases
- [[nervine-herbs-and-anxiety-relief]]:** Deep physical relaxation helps release tension
- **Pain management:** Effective [[cannabis-for-chronic-pain]], muscle spasms, and inflammation
- **Insomnia:** One of the most reliable strains for promoting sleep
- **Appetite stimulation:** Useful for conditions causing appetite loss
- **Nausea relief:** Anti-emetic properties reported by medical users

### Potential Negative Effects
- Intense couch-lock and lethargy at higher doses
- Dry mouth and dry eyes (very common)
- Dizziness in sensitive individuals or at high doses
- Paranoia or anxiety at very high doses (less common due to sedating nature)
- Not recommended for daytime use or tasks requiring focus
