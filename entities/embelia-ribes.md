## Distribution and Habitat

Native to tropical and subtropical regions of the Indian subcontinent,
ranging from the Himalayan foothills (up to 1,500 m) through peninsular
India, Sri Lanka, and eastward through Myanmar, Thailand, and southern
China.

- **Habitat:** Semi-evergreen to deciduous forests, forest edges,
  thickets, and hill slopes; often found climbing on trees in
  secondary growth
- **Climate:** Tropical monsoon; requires 1,000–2,000 mm annual
  rainfall, warm temperatures (20–35°C optimal)
- **Soil:** Prefers well-drained forest loams with [[antifungal-secondary-metabolites-coprophilous-fungi]]:
