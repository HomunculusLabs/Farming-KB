## Genetic Background

Durban Poison is a pure or near-pure *Cannabis sativa* landrace from the KwaZulu-Natal province of South Africa. The region's subtropical climate — characterized by long warm seasons, high humidity, and abundant sunshine — selected for tall, lanky plants with extended flowering periods and vigorous growth.

## Terpene Profile

Durban Poison is distinguished by an exceptionally rich terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, a less common monoterpene that contributes sweet, fruity, and slightly floral aromas. This is unusual among cannabis strains, where myrcene, limonene, and β-caryophyllene typically dominate.

**Primary terpenes:**
- **Terpinolene (up to 1.5%):** Sweet, piney, floral, and slightly herbal. Contributes to the strain's complex aroma and is associated with uplifting, creative effects in anecdotal reports.
- **Myrcene (0.5–1.0%):** Earthy, musky. Present in lower concentrations than typical for cannabis, contributing to the strain's less sedative profile.
- **Ocimene (0.3–0.8%):** Sweet, woody, herbaceous. Adds complexity to the bouquet.
- **Limonene (0.2–0.5%):** Citrus notes. Moderate levels contribute mood-elevating properties.
- **Caryophyllene (0.2–0.4%):** Peppery, spicy. Adds depth to the flavor profile.

The distinctive aroma combines sweet anise, earthy pepper, ripe pineapple, and subtle pine notes. The flavor is often described as smooth, sweet, and slightly licorice-like, with a clean exhale.
