# Isomerization (Cannabinoid Chemistry)

## Description

Isomerization in cannabinoid chemistry refers to the chemical conversion of one molecular isomer to another — specifically the transformation of cannabidiol (CBD) to tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) and the conversion of lower-rotating (less psychoactive) THC isomers to higher-rotating (more psychoactive) forms. This process can double or even increase the potency of cannabis oil by five to six times, depending on the CBD content of the [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]]. The reaction is catalyzed by sulfuric acid in an alcohol solvent under reflux conditions.

## Classification

- **Category**: Chemical technique / Molecular transformation
- **Field**: [[pericyclic-reactions-organic-chemistry]], cannabinoid chemistry
- **Reaction type**: Acid-catalyzed cyclization and isomerization

## Key Facts

- Converts non-psychoactive cannabidiol (CBD) to psychoactive tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) by acid-catalyzed cyclization of the open terpene ring in CBD to the closed ring structure of THC.
- Simultaneously converts naturally occurring lower-rotating THC isomers to higher-rotating forms, which are more potent and produce a "higher, more psychedelic and spiritual effect."
- Potency increase depends on starting CBD content: experience indicates CBD quantity is usually at least equal to THC, meaning potency can be at least doubled through isomerization alone.
- In optimal cases with high-CBD starting material, potency may increase five to six times after isomerization.
- The reaction uses one drop of 100% sulfuric acid per gram of oil, added slowly with continuous stirring to an alcohol/oil solution (1g oil : 10g absolute ethanol or pure methanol).
- Refluxed for two hours in a boiling water bath using Pyrex glassware (not stainless steel, due to sulfuric acid reactivity).
- After isomerization, the product is purified by partitioning between petroleum ether and water, washing with bicarbonate of soda solution to neutralize residual acid, and evaporating the ether.
- The "rotation" of THC refers to the optical rotation (dextrorotatory vs. levorotatory forms), with higher-rotating forms being significantly more psychoactive.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Isomerization principles have parallels in fungal chemistry:

- **Psilocybin to [[psilocybin-biosynthesis-phosphatase-psilocin-conversion]]**: The dephosphorylation of psilocybin to psilocin is a form of structural isomerization (loss of phosphate group) that occurs naturally in the body and increases psychoactivity. This is analogous to CBD-to-THC conversion in that a less active precursor becomes more active.
- **Ergot alkaloid transformations**: *[[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]]* produces a family of ergot alkaloids that can be chemically isomerized between active and inactive forms (e.g., ergotamine to ergotaminine).
- **Fungal terpenoid chemistry**: Many fungi produce terpenoid compounds that can be isomerized between different structural forms with different biological activities, paralleling the THC isomer concept.
- **Acid catalysis in fungal extraction**: The use of acid catalysts (like the sulfuric acid in isomerization) is also applied in acidified [[cannabis-solvent-extraction]] of [[hplc-analysis-psychedelic-mushroom-alkaloids]] to improve yield.

## Chemical Reaction

The core transformation involves acid-catalyzed cyclization:

**CBD + H⁺ catalyst → Δ⁹-THC (higher-rotating form)**

The sulfuric acid protonates the double bond in CBD, triggering intramolecular cyclization to form the tetrahydropyran ring characteristic of THC. The acid simultaneously promotes isomerization to the more pharmacologically active stereoisomer.

## Protocol Summary

1. Dissolve purified cannabis oil in absolute ethanol or methanol (1:10 ratio by weight).
2. Add one drop of 100% sulfuric acid per gram of oil, slowly with continuous stirring.
3. Use Pyrex glassware — sulfuric acid reacts with stainless steel.
4. Reflux in a boiling water bath for two hours.
5. Cool in ice-water bath before opening apparatus.
6. Pour solution into equal volume of water.
7. Extract with petroleum ether; collect ether/oil layer.
8. Wash ether/oil solution with 5% bicarbonate of soda solution to neutralize acid.
9. Wash twice more with pure water.
10. Evaporate ether to yield purified, isomerized oil with significantly increased THC content.

## Safety Considerations

- **Sulfuric acid**: Causes severe burns. Wear safety glasses, long rubber gloves, and full body coverage. Treat burns with immediate water washing followed by bicarbonate of soda.
- **Flammable solvents**: Ethanol and methanol are flammable. Use sealed reflux apparatus with ice-cooled condenser.
- **Pressure management**: The reaction vessel must allow pressure relief. The plastic bag seal on the stew pot apparatus inflates to indicate pressure buildup and automatically releases.
