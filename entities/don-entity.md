# DON

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-nitroamphetamine
**[[alexander-shulgin]] himself; the qualitative data comes from [[chiric-caspi-brunfelsia-south-american-hallucinogen]] researchers who conducted human trials.

The nitro group makes DON unique among the DOx compounds in several ways -- it is strongly electron-withdrawing (unlike the electron-donating alkyl groups) and may be metabolically reduced to an amino group in vivo, potentially contributing to its pharmacological effects.

## Dosage

| Level | Amount (mg) |
|-------|-------------|
| Threshold | ~3 |
| Light | 3.0 |
| Common | 3.0 - 4.5 |

## Duration

8 - 15 hours. Effects were still noticeable 14 hours after administration at 4.5 mg.

## Effects

The reconstructed qualitative comments (from published summaries of South American research) describe:

- **At 3.0 mg:** Amphetamine-like stimulation apparent within an hour, with considerable anxiety. Stomach cramps were present. "Indications that there might be something hallucinogenic at a higher dose."
- **At 4.5 mg:** Enhancement of color perception and some auditory distortion, still noticeable 8 hours in. Visual changes described as "intense." A feeling of running a slight fever with restlessness, but "almost no physical malaise." Still "somewhat wound up" at the 14th hour.

## Character Assessment

DON appears to have a mixed stimulant-psychedelic profile with notable physical side effects:

- **Stimulant component:** Amphetamine-like stimulation is prominent, particularly at lower doses
- **Anxiety:** Considerable anxiety at the threshold dose, possibly related to the stimulant properties
- **Physical discomfort:** Stomach cramps and feverish feeling
- **Psychedelic component:** Enhancement of color perception and auditory distortion at higher doses
- **Duration:** Effects persist well beyond what might be expected from a simple stimulant

## Unusual Source of Data

Shulgin explicitly states that the qualitative comments "are not true quotations, but have been reconstructed from the published summaries of the human trials reported by several South American researchers." He notes a particularly cryptic published statement: "This action [a strong stimulant action reminiscent of amphetamine] seems to reduce the incidence of insightful, and therefore potentially unpleasant experiences, and thus [DON seems likely] to appear on the market as an illicit recreational drug." Shulgin admits: "I must admit that I have tried, and I am still not able, to interpret this quotation."

## Synthesis

DON is prepared by direct nitration of 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine:

1. **Nitration:** A solution of 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine base in acetic acid is added dropwise to 50% nitric acid at 0 deg C.
2. **Workup:** The reaction is quenched with ice water, made basic, and extracted with benzene-ether.
3. **Isolation:** The residue is dissolved in dilute HCl and evaporated to give nearly colorless crystals. Recrystallization from ethanol/ether provides the hydrochloride salt (mp 206-207 deg C).

The R-isomer hydrochloride has a mp of 231-232 deg C. Both acetamide and formamide derivatives were prepared.

## Pharmacology

The nitro group's role is pharmacologically interesting. It may:
- Act as a steric and electronic mimic of other 4-position substituents
- Be metabolically reduced in vivo to an amino group (potentially generating a different active compound)
- Contribute to the compound's mixed stimulant-psychedelic profile
