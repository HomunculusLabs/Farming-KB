## Spore Syringe

## Description

A spore syringe is a sterile medical-grade syringe filled with a suspension
of mushroom spores in water. It is the primary inoculation tool in the [[pf-tek]]
and many other [[mushroom-cultivation-methods]]. The spore syringe delivers a
controlled volume of spore solution directly into sterilized substrate jars,
initiating the mycelial colonization process. In the PF TEK, a single 10 cc
spore syringe can inoculate approximately 10 half-pint jars.

## Classification

- **Category:** Inoculation tool / spawn delivery system
- **Type:** Sterile liquid spore suspension in medical syringe
- **Components:** Syringe body, plunger, needle, needle guard
- **Standard volume:** 10 cc (mL) per syringe
- **Inoculation rate:** 1 cc per half-pint PF jar

## Preparation Methods

### From Spore Prints

The PF TEK describes a detailed procedure for preparing spore syringes from
spore prints:

#### Equipment Required

1. Spore print in a sterilized KERR 1/2 pint wide mouth canning jar
2. Sterile syringe with water (for injecting into spore print jar)
3. Sterile syringe for loading spore solution
4. Small Pyrex glass stirring rod (lab supply)
5. Alcohol, tequila shot glass, and eye dropper (for flame sterilization)
6. Modified jar lid with two holes (one for stirring rod, one for syringe
   needle)

#### Syringe Sterilization

1. Boil a pot of water
2. Draw boiling water into a syringe and squirt it out several times
3. Refill with boiling water, replace needle guard, wrap in tin foil
4. Drop the wrapped syringes into boiling water and boil for 1 hour
5. Allow to cool before using
6. Alternative: Pressure can at 12 PSI for 10 minutes

#### Loading Procedure

1. Inject sterile water into the spore print jar through the needle hole
2. Flame-sterilize the glass stirring rod and let it cool briefly
3. Insert the rod through the center lid hole and scrape spores into the water
4. Insert a sterile syringe needle through the edge hole
5. Tilt the jar until water covers the needle tip
6. Draw the spore-water solution into the sterile syringe

The key principle is to expose the interior of the jar to as little room air
as possible throughout the procedure. Always cover lid holes with tin foil or
sterile surgical tape before and after.

### Commercial Sources

The PF TEK offered Psilocybe cubensis spore syringes for $10 each, guaranteed
clean (uncontaminated) and viable (germinating). Commercial spore syringes
remain widely available from various vendors for microscopy and research
purposes.

## Inoculation Technique (PF TEK)

1. Ensure the substrate jar is completely cool to the touch
2. Tighten the jar lid
3. Shake the syringe vigorously to redistribute spores in the water
4. Remove tape covering the needle holes
5. Remove the needle guard
6. Insert the needle through a lid hole
7. Tilt the syringe body back toward the center, with the needle tip
   touching the glass
8. Inoculate a few drops down each hole (4 holes per lid)
9. Use approximately 1 cc total per jar
10. As soon as water appears around the needle tip, release plunger pressure
11. Shake the syringe slightly between each hole inoculation

### Important Notes

- The spore solution should flow down the side of the jar, not be absorbed
  by the dry top vermiculite layer
- If the needle plugs on the substrate, draw it back slightly to unplug
- More spore solution per jar speeds colonization but reduces the number
  of jars that can be inoculated

## Storage

- Store at cool temperatures in the dark
- A properly prepared spore syringe remains viable for several months to
  over a year
- Psilocybe cubensis spores begin to degrade a few months after being taken
- After approximately 18 months, germination is greatly reduced or absent
- Germination is massive and quick when spores are fresh
- Do NOT store in a refrigerator (applies to spore prints specifically)
- Protect from extreme heat (airplane cargo holds, hot mail trucks, sun-
  heated mailboxes can kill spores)
- Spore syringes can survive freezing, but extreme cold may be destructive

## Sterile Technique

### Needle Sterilization

If the syringe needle is touched during use, it must be flame-sterilized:

1. Place a tequila shot glass upside down
2. Apply a few drops of denatured alcohol fuel to the hollow bottom
3. Touch with a match or lighter to ignite
4. Heat the needle in the blue alcohol flame for a few seconds

Alcohol flames are preferred over butane cigarette lighters, which leave
an undesirable soot residue. Small "pops" of boiling water may spurt from
the needle during flaming, but spores within the syringe are safe.

### General Sterile Practices

- Wash hands before sterile work (hands are a prime contamination source)
- Sterilize work surfaces with rubbing alcohol
- Minimize drafts; aim for a still-air environment
- Avoid breathing on the work area
- Run a HEPA air cleaner (99.97% rated) for several hours in a closed room
  before sterile procedures

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The spore syringe is the cornerstone tool of amateur mushroom cultivation.
It makes inoculation accessible to beginners by providing a pre-measured,
relatively contamination-resistant delivery method. Understanding proper
spore syringe technique — including preparation, inoculation, storage, and
sterile handling — is fundamental to success in any spore-based cultivation
method. The PF TEK's use of mass spore inoculation (rather than tissue cloning)
through syringes preserves genetic diversity in the crop.
