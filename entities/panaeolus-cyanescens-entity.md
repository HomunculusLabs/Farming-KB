## Cultivation

Cultivation follows a substrate-based approach distinct from wood-decomposing mushrooms:

### Substrate

The species requires **cellulose-rich, nitrogen-enriched substrates**. Common formulations include:
- Pasteurized horse or cow manure (50–70%)
- Straw or vermiculite (20–30%)
- Supplementation with wheat bran or rice bran (5–10%)
- Pasteurized leached cow manure is the traditional standard substrate
