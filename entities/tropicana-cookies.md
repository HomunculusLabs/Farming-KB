# Tropicana Cookies (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

Tropicana Cookies is a sativa-dominant [[girl-scout-cookies]] with Tangie. Bred by Oni Seed Co., it is
celebrated for its extraordinary bag appeal — dense, elongated buds that display
vivid purple and dark orange coloration with a thick coating of white trichomes.
The aroma is a bold, tropical citrus punch dominated by orange and tangerine
notes. With [[green-cannabis-plant-anatomy-calyx-pistil-bud-structure]], and [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]:** Dominated by limonene (citrus), caryophyllene (pepper,
  earth), and myrcene (herbal, sedative baseline); secondary terpenes include
  pinene, linalool, and humulene

## Terpene Breakdown

- **Limonene:** Dominant terpene; responsible for the signature tangerine aroma;
  contributes uplifting mood effects, stress relief, and potential
  anti-inflammatory activity
- **Caryophyllene:** Adds peppery, earthy depth; binds to CB2 receptors for
  anti-inflammatory effects; balances the cerebral high with physical comfort
- **Myrcene:** Provides the foundational herbal, slightly fruity base; enhances
  THC uptake and contributes to the smooth onset
- **Pinene (alpha/beta):** Present in lower amounts; adds freshness and may
  counteract THC-induced memory impairment
- **Linalool:** Trace amounts contribute floral sweetness and mild anxiolytic
  properties

## Effects

- **Onset:** Relatively quick, within 5–10 minutes of consumption
- **Cerebral:** Euphoric, uplifting, and energizing head high; enhances
  creativity, focus, and sociability; mood-elevating without being racy
- **Physical:** Mild body relaxation that does not become sedating; gentle
  warmth and tension relief in the shoulders and neck
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours depending on tolerance and consumption method
- **Common uses:** Creative work, social gatherings, daytime activities,
  combating fatigue and low mood; popular among artists and musicians
