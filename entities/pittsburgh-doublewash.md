# Pittsburgh Doublewash Method

## Description

The Pittsburgh Doublewash is a cannabis hash-making technique developed by an anonymous practitioner from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and published in the *IsoNews* newsletter / *Cannabis Alchemy* letters section. The method involves a two-stage purification process: first, a prolonged hot water wash to remove water-soluble tars, waxes, and impurities from the plant material, followed by a second wash after isomerization to remove residual neutralizer. The result was reported to be hash of exceptional taste, smoothness, and quality superior to expensive imported varieties.

## Key Facts

- The name "Pittsburgh Doublewash" was coined by the inventor to describe the system.
- **Part 1 (Pre-wash)**: Cannabis is pulverized and boiled in water for 1 hour, then boiled again for 3 additional hours in fresh water. This removes approximately 20% by weight of water-soluble tars and waxes.
- Boiling also accomplishes decarboxylation of THC acids to THC during the first hour.
