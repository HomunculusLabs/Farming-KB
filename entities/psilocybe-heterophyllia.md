## Psilocybe heterophyllia

**Psilocybe heterophyllia** is a psilocybin-containing mushroom species in the family Hymenogastraceae, described from subtropical and tropical regions of the Americas. It is a relatively obscure member of the genus, known from limited collections in Central and South American forest habitats.

## Distribution and Habitat

psilocybe heterophyllia is known from tropical and subtropical forests in Central and South America. Confirmed collections come from Colombia, Ecuador, and possibly southern Mexico. The full extent of its distribution is poorly documented due to limited collecting effort in remote tropical forests and the species' inconspicuous appearance.

The species is found in humid forest environments, including cloud forests, tropical rainforests, and lower montane wet forests. It grows on decaying plant material, leaf litter, and woody debris on the forest floor, often among mosses and ferns. It is not strongly associated with open grassland or dung, distinguishing it from many other tropical Psilocybe species. Fruiting occurs during the rainy season when humidity is consistently high and temperatures are moderate.
