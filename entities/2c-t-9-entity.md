# 2C-T-9

**PIHKAL Entry #45** -- 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(t)-butylthiophenethylamine

## Chemical Name

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(t)-butylthiophenethylamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 60 - 100 mg

- **Duration:** 12 - 18 h

## Qualitative Comments

(with 90 mg) 2C-T-9 tastes the way that old crank-case motor oil smells. I was
up to something above a plus two at the third hour. Although there were no
visuals noted, I certainly would not choose to drive. Somehow this does more to
the body than to the head. I feel that the effects are waning at maybe the sixth
hour, but there is a very strong body memory that makes sleeping difficult.
Finally, at sometime after midnight and with the help of a glass of wine, some
sleep.

(with 125 mg) There was a steady climb to a +++ over the first couple of hours.
So far, the body has been quite peaceful without any strong energy push or
stomach problems, although my tummy insists on being treated with quiet respect,
perhaps out of habit, perhaps not. At the fifth hour, the body energy is quite
strong, and I have the choice of focusing it into some activity, such as
love-making or writing, or having to deal with tapping toes and floor-pacing.
For a novice this would be a murderously difficult experience. Too much energy,
too long a time. I suppose I could get used to it, but let me judge by when I
get to sleep, and just what kind of sleep it is. It turned out that sleep was
OK, but for the next couple of days there was a continuing awareness of some
residue in the body -- some kind of low-level poisoning. I feel in general that
there is not the excitement or creativity to connect with, certainly not enough
to justify the cost to the body.

## Synthesis

To a well-stirred ice-cold suspension of 2.8 g p-dimethoxybenzene and 3.2 mL
N,N,N',N'-tetramethylethylenediamine in 100 mL petroleum ether under He, there
was added 13 mL of 1.6 N butyllithium in hexane. After warming to room
temperature, 3.6 g di-(t)-butyldisulfide was added slowly. The yellow color
deepened through brown. Distillation at 0.5 mm/Hg gave 3.7 g of 2,5-dimethoxy-
phenyl (t)-butyl sulfide as a white mobile liquid.

Vilsmeier formylation with POCl3 and N-methylformanilide gave the aldehyde
(12.0 g crude, distilled at 120-130 degrees C at 0.2 mm/Hg). Nitrostyrene
formation gave 3.7 g of fine lemon-yellow crystals (mp 93-94 degrees C). LAH
reduction followed by distillation at 130-160 degrees C at 0.2 mm/Hg gave 1.66
g of pale yellow viscous oil. The hydrochloride salt crystallized from IPA/Et2O
(1.10 g, white crystals).

## Extensions and Commentary

The three-carbon analog of 2C-T-9 (an ALEPH series member) has never been made
and none of the higher numbered 2C-T's have had amphetamine counterparts
synthesized. They are, as of the present time, unknown compounds.

The nifty reaction with di-(t)-butyl disulfide worked so well that three
additional disulfides were immediately tried: 2C-T-10 (2-pyridylthio), 2C-T-11
(4-bromophenylthio), and 2C-T-12 (1-morpholinothio). None were pursued to
completion. The 2C-T-10 intermediate (2,5-dimethoxyphenyl 2-pyridyl sulfide,
mp 66-67.5 degrees C) and the 2C-T-11 intermediate (mp 72-73 degrees C) were
synthesized. The 2C-T-12 effort (N,N-dimorpholinyl disulfide) produced
virtually no product. An additional effort with 2,5-dimethoxybenzenesulfonyl
chloride reacted with diethylamine to give the sulfonamide (mp 41-42 degrees C)
but it proved totally refractory to all efforts at reduction.

Note: 2C-T-4 is sometimes abbreviated "T-4," which could be confused with
cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine (RDX, a potent explosive used by terrorists) or
thyroxine (a body amino acid). 2C-T-9 is neither an explosive nor an amino acid.
