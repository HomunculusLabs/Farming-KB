## Aroma and Flavor

**Aroma:** Dominated by a rich, sweet cake batter profile with prominent vanilla and berry undertones. Secondary notes of grape, nuttiness, and earthy musk emerge on deeper inhalation. The scent is pungent and lingers — a room-filling bouquet.

**Flavor:** Smooth and creamy on the exhale, with a layered dessert profile. Vanilla cake and berry notes dominate, followed by a subtle grapey sweetness. A faint earthy, almost peppery finish rounds out the experience. The flavor is notably sweet without being cloying.

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene** (dominant, ~0.8–1.2%) — Citrus brightness and mood elevation.
- **Caryophyllene** (secondary, ~0.5–0.8%) — Peppery spice, anti-inflammatory.
- **Linalool** (secondary, ~0.3–0.6%) — Floral, lavender notes; promotes relaxation.
- **Myrcene** (moderate, ~0.4–0.7%) — Sedative base, contributes to body-heavy effects.
- **Humulene** (trace) — Earthy, woody undertone.

The high limonene and linalool content creates an unusual tension between cerebral uplift and physical relaxation that defines the London Pound Cake experience.

- **CBD:** < 0.1% (negligible)

## Side Effects

Dry mouth and dry eyes are the most commonly reported side effects, consistent with high-THC strains across the board. Some users experience mild anxiety or paranoia at higher doses, particularly those sensitive to THC-dominant varieties.

Lightheadedness and dizziness can occur during the initial onset, especially when consumed in large quantities or on an empty stomach. Novice consumers should approach this strain with caution due to its potency.
