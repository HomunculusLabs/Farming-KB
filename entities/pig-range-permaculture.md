## Ectoparasite and Health Management

Natural ectoparasite control methods for free-range pigs include: strong diagonal posts wrapped in hessian and kept dampened with vegetable oils mixed with neem or pyrethrum oil for lice control; dust baths (roof over soft dry area) laced with dried neem tree leaves or pyrethrum flowers; and restricted wallows that can be planted to cattails (Typha spp.) for root and shoot forage after the pigs move on. Duck potato (Sagittaria) can be planted in pond margins for summer-dry forage from rested wallows.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The deep soil disturbance and heavy manuring create conditions that can be subsequently planted to crops or inoculated with saprophytic mushroom species, particularly in agroforestry configurations. The integration of pigs with dairy waste and food scrap streams closes nutrient loops on diversified farms. The rested wallow areas, when planted to cattails and Sagittaria, provide additional yields from the same ground after pigs move on. Manure from ranging pigs can be composted for mushroom substrate production, and the rooting activity creates disturbed soil conditions favourable for pioneer fungal colonisation.
