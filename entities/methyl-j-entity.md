# METHYL-J (MBDB; EDEN; 2-Methylamino-1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)butane)

METHYL-J, also known as MBDB or EDEN, is the N-methyl derivative of J (BDB) and the alpha-ethyl analogue of [[mdma]].

It is one of the key compounds in the Muni-Metro series (see below).

## Pharmacological Profile

METHYL-J is an active entactogenic compound with a dosage range of 180 to 210 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 4 to 6 hours.

The compound produces effects very similar to [[mdma]] in some ways.

Empathy and intuition levels were felt to be somewhat lower than with MDMA.

At 210 mg, another subject reported rapid onset with alert at 20 minutes and a plus 2.5 at 30-35 minutes.

No physical symptoms such as teeth clenching or stomach problems were noted.

Good visual enhancement with bright colors was reported but no visuals with eyes closed.

The experience was similar to MDMA but without the "cone of silence" some subjects associate with MDMA.

At 210 mg with a 50 mg supplement, subjects described a full-blown high developing abruptly at 30 minutes.

The high was described as somewhat like an alcohol high without the disabling side effects of confusion, slurring, and staggering.

It was not as stimulating as MDMA and had very little visual activity.

Some jaw-clenching occurred towards the end, with considerable controllable nystagmus at the peak.

At 210 mg with a 70 mg supplement, subjects reported intense euphoria described as a "feeling of grace."

Soft skin, voices, youthful appearance, animated discussions, and feelings of great closeness to others were noted.

The experience was described as very much like MDMA, with the main difference being more quietness and less inclination to talk.

The conclusion was that MBDB is an excellent substitute for MDMA.

## Comparison with MDMA

An observer familiar with group MDMA sessions felt that MBDB lacked some of the spontaneity, warmth, and clear intimacy of MDMA.

Most subjects, however, commented favorably in their comparisons.

MBDB is approximately two-thirds the potency of MDMA on a milligram basis.

The duration and chronology of effects are very similar.

MBDB may offer advantages in therapeutic settings where less stimulation is desired.

## The Muni-Metro Naming System

The H, I, J, K naming convention was inspired by the San Francisco Municipal Railway (Muni-Metro) system.

Several streetcar lines fan across San Francisco to the outer Avenues, named by sequential letters: J Church Street, K Ingelside, L Taraval, M Ocean, N Judah.

In the pharmacological exploration of chain-lengthened MDA analogues, two coincidental benchmarks appeared in the proposed names.

The naming system thus follows the Muni-Metro letter designations for the alpha-alkyl chain length series.

## The Chain-Lengthening Series

METHYL-J occupies a position in the systematic exploration of alpha-alkyl chain extension from the MDA/MDMA scaffold.
