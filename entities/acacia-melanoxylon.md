## Acacia melanoxylon

## Description

Acacia melanoxylon, commonly known as Australian blackwood or blackwood acacia, is a large evergreen tree in the family Fabaceae native to southeastern Australia, including Tasmania, Victoria, New South Wales, and Queensland. It is one of the most valuable timber acacias, producing dark, lustrous heartwood classified among the world's finest decorative hardwoods. The species name melanoxylon derives from the Greek melas (black) and xylon (wood), referring to the dark timber. In permaculture and [[farm-forestry-design]], uniquely combining nitrogen fixation, premium timber production, low pasture competition, and late-winter bee forage value. Its nitrogen-fixing capability improves soil biology and supports the growth of companion species, including fungal associates. The tree forms associations with both ectomycorrhizal and [[food-forest]] systems.
