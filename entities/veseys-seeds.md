## Description

Veseys Seeds is a Canadian mail-order seed company based in York, Prince Edward Island. It is one of the most established seed houses in Canada, serving home gardeners and commercial growers since 1939. Referenced by Steve Solomon as a recommended seed source in *[[gardening-when-it-counts]]*.

## Classification

- **Type:** Organization (seed company)
- **Location:** York, Prince Edward Island, Canada
- **Founded:** 1939
- **Industry:** Horticultural seeds

## Key Facts

- One of Canada's oldest and largest mail-order seed companies
- Offers a wide variety of vegetable seeds, flower seeds, bulbs, and garden supplies
- Specializes in varieties suited to Canadian growing conditions, including short-season and cold-hardy cultivars
- Referenced on page 113 of *Gardening When It Counts* as a recommended source for quality seeds
- Known for rigorous germination testing and seed quality standards
- Serves both home gardeners and small-scale commercial growers across Canada and the northern United States

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Genetic diversity:** Seed companies like Veseys maintain and distribute genetic diversity in crop plants, which is important for resilient food systems
- **Climate-adapted varieties:** Their focus on cold-climate varieties parallels the need for environment-adapted genetics in all forms of cultivation
- **Open-pollinated and heirloom seeds:** Their catalog includes open-pollinated varieties that gardeners can save seed from, supporting self-sufficiency — a principle shared with mushroom cultivation and spore saving
- **Northern growing expertise:** Their knowledge base is valuable for cultivators working in challenging climates, including [[outdoor-mushroom-cultivation]] in cold regions
