## Bioactive Compounds

The principal bioactive constituent of blessed thistle is cnicin, a bitter sesquiterpene lactone of the guaianolide type.

Cnicin concentration is highest in the flowering aerial parts and can reach 0.2–0.5% of dry weight.

The compound is responsible for the plant's characteristic bitter taste and much of its pharmacological activity.

Additional sesquiterpene lactones present include salonitenolide and artemorin.

Flavonoids identified in the aerial parts include apigenin, luteolin, and their glycosides.

Phenolic acids such as chlorogenic acid and caffeic acid derivatives contribute to the antioxidant profile.

[[permaculture-designers-manual-essential-oil-plants]], though present in small quantities, contains spathulenol and caryophyllene oxide.

Tannins and mucilage are also present in the leaves and stems.
