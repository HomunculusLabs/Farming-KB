# MAPS (Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies)

MAPS is a nonprofit research and educational organization founded in 1986 by Rick Doblin, dedicated to developing medical, legal, and cultural contexts for people to benefit from the careful uses of psychedelics and marijuana. It is the leading organization funding and conducting [[psychedelic-therapy-set-and-setting]] for the [[psychedelic-therapeutic-frameworks]] it employs.

## Mission and History

- Founded in 1986 by Rick Doblin, PhD, while he was a graduate student at Harvard
- Originally conceived to pursue FDA-legal research with MDMA after its emergency scheduling in 1985
- Incorporated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit
- Headquartered in Santa Cruz, California
- Evolved from a small advocacy group to a multimillion-dollar research organization conducting Phase 3 [[metzner-psycholytic-psychedelic-therapy-models]] program in the world:

### Phase 2 Trials (2000s-2010s)
- Six Phase 2 studies conducted across the United States, Canada, Israel, and Switzerland
- Consistently demonstrated significant reductions in PTSD symptoms, with many participants no longer meeting diagnostic criteria for PTSD after treatment
- Results published in peer-reviewed journals including The Lancet Psychiatry, Journal of Psychopharmacology, and JAMA Psychiatry
- Effect sizes substantially larger than existing PTSD treatments (SSRIs, prolonged exposure therapy)

### Phase 3 Trials (2021-2024)
- First Phase 3 trial (MAPP1) completed 2021: 67% of participants no longer qualified for PTSD diagnosis after three sessions of MDMA-assisted therapy vs. 32% in placebo group
- Second Phase 3 trial (MAPP2) completed 2024: Confirmed efficacy with 71% of MDMA group achieving clinically meaningful improvement
- Over 200 participants across multiple international sites
- FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation granted in 2017

### Therapeutic Protocol
The MAPS MDMA-assisted [[psychedelic-assisted-therapy-protocol]] involves:
- Three preparatory sessions with a trained therapist pair (one male, one female)
- Two to three MDMA-assisted sessions spaced one month apart
- Doses typically 80-120 mg followed by a supplemental half-dose
- Sessions last 8 hours in a comfortable clinical setting with eyeshades and music
- Integration sessions following each MDMA session
- Follow-up assessments at 2 and 12 months post-treatment
