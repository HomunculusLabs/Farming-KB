## Overview

Rick Strassman (born 1952) is an American clinical psychiatrist and
psychopharmacologist best known for conducting the first federally
approved human research with psychedelic drugs in the United States in
over two decades. Between 1990 and 1995, Strassman administered over

## The DMT Research Project (1990-1995)

### Regulatory Approval

Strassman obtained approvals from UNM IRB, the FDA (IND application),
and DEA Schedule I research license #RS001 — the first such license
issued in over two decades. This made his the first FDA-approved human
psychedelic study since the mid-1970s.

### Study Design

Research was conducted at UNM Hospital with healthy adults who had
prior [[emcdda-psilocybin-cardiovascular-risks-acute-physiological-effects]]: transient heart rate, blood pressure, pupil
  dilation, and temperature increases — all within safe ranges.
- No serious adverse events or lasting psychopathology in controlled
  setting.
- Hormone measurements (melatonin, beta-endorphin, cortisol, DHEA)
  showed complex interactions with DMT.
