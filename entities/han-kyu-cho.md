## Description

Dr. Han Kyu Cho is a Korean agricultural scientist and founder/leader of the [[korean-natural-farming]] Association. He is a pioneering figure in the systematization and teaching of [[cho-poultry-natural-farming-methods]], particularly the cultivation and application of [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] for crop and livestock production. His work, along with collaborator Atsushi Kouama, produced foundational texts on indigenous microorganism vital power for agriculture.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person / Agricultural scientist / Natural farming pioneer
- **Nationality**: Korean
- **Organization**: Korean Natural Farming Association
- **Role**: Founder and mentor
- **Key Publication**: "Korean Natural Farming, Indigenous Microorganism Vital Power for Crop and Livestock" (co-authored with Atsushi Kouama)

## Key Facts

- Founded the Korean Natural Farming Association, which systematized traditional Asian natural farming knowledge into a teachable methodology
- Mentored Gil A. Carandang, who disseminated Natural Farming techniques to the Philippines and the United States
- Co-authored "Korean Natural Farming, Indigenous Microorganism Vital Power for Crop and Livestock" with Atsushi Kouama
- Advocated for farmers recovering their "independent authority" in farming techniques, rather than becoming dependent on purchased inputs
- Emphasized that farmers should make and apply necessary farming materials using locally available resources
- His philosophy centers on the idea that a "new vision of farming techniques is needed to recover the true nature of farming"
- Developed systematic methods for culturing beneficial indigenous microorganisms including lactic acid bacteria, forest microorganisms, bamboo microorganisms, and plant-specific microorganisms
- Created the BIM formulation: 50% lactic acid bacteria combined with 50% other cultured microorganisms
- His Natural Farming system integrates microorganism culture, bionutrient preparation, and environmental management

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Microbial Systems**: Dr. Cho's systematic approach to cultivating diverse [[forest-and-bamboo-beneficial-microorganisms]] from local environments provides a framework applicable to [[mushroom-cultivation-environments]] where managing microbial ecology is critical.
- **Substrate Philosophy**: The emphasis on using locally available materials and farmer-produced inputs parallels the mushroom cultivation principle of working with locally available substrate materials.
- **Fermentation Methods**: The fermentation-based production of inputs (using sugar/molasses as microbial food sources) mirrors techniques used in [[comparison-mushroom-liquid-culture-vs-spore-syringe]] and spawn production.
- **Biodiversity Principle**: The emphasis on microbial biodiversity — mixing multiple microorganism sources for broader ecological function — is relevant to maintaining healthy mushroom cultivation environments.
- **Holistic Approach**: The integration of microorganisms, nutrients, and environmental factors into a unified farming system provides a model for integrated mushroom cultivation operations.
