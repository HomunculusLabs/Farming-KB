# Oidiodendron maius

## Taxonomy

Oidiodendron maius is an ascomycete fungus classified within the
phylum Ascomycota, class Leotiomycetes, order Helotiales, and family
Myxotrichaceae. The genus Oidiodendron comprises fungi that are
primarily recognized as [[gaultheria-shallon]]), a
dominant understory species in Pacific Northwest forests of North
America. As an ericoid mycorrhizal partner, O. maius colonizes the
fine roots of its host plants, forming distinctive coiled hyphal
structures within root epidermal cells that serve as the primary
interface for [[fungal-bacterial-predators-nitrogen-acquisition-soil-ecology]] from
organic sources and pronounced tolerance of heavy metal stress. These
traits make O. maius a key organism for understanding how ericoid
mycorrhizal symbioses enable plants to persist in nutrient-poor and
metal-contaminated environments where other plant-fungal associations
might fail.
