## ProSilva Network

## Description

The ProSilva Network (also written ProSilva or Pro Silva) is a European federation
of foresters, forest owners, and environmental advocates promoting nature-based
[[mycelium-running-forestry-practices-and-mushrooms]]. Founded in 1989 at the first ProSilva European Forest
Convention in Slovenia, the network advocates for a fundamental shift in forest
[[holzer-vole-management-philosophy]] — from even-aged [[plantation-forestry-carbon-dynamics]] with clearfelling to
continuous cover forestry (CCF) that maintains forest structure, biodiversity, and
ecological function while still producing timber.

ProSilva's approach is grounded in the principle that forests managed according to
natural processes are more resilient, more productive in the long term, and provide
greater ecological services than intensively managed plantation systems. The network
draws on centuries of European forestry tradition, particularly from countries like
Slovenia, Switzerland, and Germany, where close-to-nature forestry has been
practised for generations.

In Holmgren's text, the ProSilva Network is cited as a source of the latest practices
in European nature-based forestry being applied at Fryers Forest Eco-Village in
Australia.

## Key Facts

- Founded in 1989 at Robanov kot, Slovenia, by a group of European foresters
  seeking alternatives to intensive plantation forestry.
- Promotes continuous cover forestry (CCF), which maintains forest canopy and
  structure at all times, avoiding clearfelling.
- Advocates for mixed-species, mixed-age forest stands that mimic natural forest
  dynamics rather than creating monoculture plantations.
- The network includes member organisations across most European countries,
  from Ireland to Romania and from Scandinavia to the Mediterranean.
- Core principles include: maintaining soil fertility through continuous forest
  cover, favouring natural regeneration over planting, selective harvesting of
  individual mature trees rather than clearfelling, and maintaining biodiversity
  as a functional component of forest productivity.
- ProSilva's approach is particularly relevant to the transition from industrial
  forestry to ecological forestry in the context of energy descent and climate
  change adaptation.
- Referenced by [[david-holmgren]] as a source of best practices informing forest
  management at Fryers Forest Eco-Village in central Victoria, Australia.
- The network provides a model for how [[permaculture-principles]] of working with
  natural systems can be applied at the forest management scale.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Mycorrhizal Network Preservation**: Continuous cover forestry maintains
  intact underground [[mycorrhizal-networks]] that are destroyed by clearfelling.
  These fungal networks are essential for tree nutrition and forest ecosystem
  health.
- **Woody Debris and Fungal Habitat**: Selective harvesting and natural mortality
  in ProSilva-managed forests create a continuous supply of coarse woody debris
  that supports diverse fungal communities, including saprophytic and parasitic
  species.
- **Soil Structure and Biology**: The absence of clearfelling and heavy machinery
  compaction preserves soil structure and [[soil-food-web]], including fungal
  hyphal networks that aggregate soil particles and improve water infiltration.
- **Mixed-Species Benefits**: Multi-species forest stands support a greater
  diversity of mycorrhizal partners than monocultures, as different tree species
  associate with different fungal species.
- **Forest Floor Ecology**: Continuous canopy cover maintains the moist, shaded
  forest floor conditions that favour fungal fruiting and mycelial growth.
- **Timber and Mushroom Dual Production**: ProSilva-style forests can potentially
  integrate mushroom cultivation (e.g., shiitake on logs, truffle cultivation)
  with timber production in diversified management systems.
- **Carbon [[mollison-designers-fish-pond-fertiliser-and-nutrient-cycling]]**: The network's emphasis on natural decomposition
  processes supports fungal-mediated carbon and [[nutrient-cycling-in-soil]] forest
  ecosystems.
- **Landscape-Scale Mycology**: ProSilva provides a framework for managing
  forested landscapes where wild mushroom harvesting (foraging) can be sustainable
  alongside timber production.
