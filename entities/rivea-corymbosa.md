## Rivea corymbosa

**Rivea corymbosa**, commonly known as **Ololiuqui**, is a woody climbing vine in the [[turbina-corymbosa]] for additional information under that name.

## Botany

Rivea corymbosa is a perennial, evergreen, woody vine native to tropical and subtropical Mexico and [[purple-ohms-argyreia-nervosa-legal-mushroom-substitutes]] flowers but similar in form
- **Seeds:** Small, oval, brown to black, 3–5 mm diameter; contained in small, rounded capsules; each capsule produces typically 1–4 seeds
- **Habitat:** Tropical and subtropical forests, forest edges, and disturbed areas from sea level to 1500 meters

The plant flowers and fruits year-round in tropical conditions. It is closely related to cultivated sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas) and shares many morphological features with other Convolvulaceae.

## Chemistry

The seeds of R. corymbosa contain the following ergoline alkaloids:

| Compound | Concentration | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| Lysergic acid amide (LSA) | ~0.02–0.05% | Primary psychoactive compound |
| Lysergic acid hydroxyethylamide (LSH) | ~0.01–0.03% | May be the dominant active compound |
| Ergometrine | Trace | Uterotonic alkaloid |
| Isoergine | Present | Inactive isomer |
| Chanoclavine | Trace | Biosynthetic precursor |
| Elymoclavine | Trace | Intermediate alkaloid |

The LSA concentration in R. corymbosa seeds is significantly lower than in [[richard-evans-schultes-updated]] proposed R. corymbosa as the true identity of ololiuqui in 1941
- In 1960, Albert Hofmann isolated and identified LSA and LSH from the seeds of R. corymbosa — the same chemist who first synthesized LSD in 1938
- Hofmann's identification confirmed that ololiuqui contained genuine ergoline alkaloids, resolving a 400-year-old botanical mystery

## Traditional Use (Mazatec and Zapotec)

Contemporary indigenous use in Oaxaca:
- **Divination:** Seeds are ground, soaked in water, and the filtrate is consumed during nighttime ceremonies
- **Healing:** Curanderos use ololiuqui to diagnose illness and receive guidance on treatment
- **Dosage:** Typically 12–25 fresh or dried seeds; prepared as a cold water infusion
- **Setting:** Dark, quiet room; the healer sings and prays throughout the experience
- **Co-administration:** Sometimes used alongside [[forensic-identification-methods-psilocybin-mushrooms-tlc-gc-hplc]], tobacco (Nicotiana rustica), or Salvia divinorum

The Mazatec consider ololiuqui a gentler entheogen than psilocybin mushrooms, suitable for certain types of divination and healing work. Its use has declined in recent decades due to the greater availability and cultural preference for mushrooms.

## Effects

- **Onset:** 30–60 minutes after oral consumption
- **Peak:** 2–4 hours
- **Duration:** 6–8 hours
- **Character:** Dreamy, sedating, contemplative; less visually intense than LSD or psilocybin
- **Physical:** Nausea (common), drowsiness, mydriasis, mild vasoconstriction
- **Cognitive:** Enhanced introspection, dream-like imagery, emotional sensitivity

The experience is generally described as gentler and more introspective than that produced by higher-LSA sources like Argyreia nervosa seeds.
