### Morphology

- **Growth habit:** Perennial twining vine, 1–5 m long. Stems are slender,
  green to purplish, climbing clockwise on supports or trailing along the ground.
- **Leaves:** Odd-pinnately compound, 15–30 cm long, with 5–7 ovate leaflets
  5–12 cm × 3–7 cm. Leaflets are hairless above, sparsely pubescent beneath.
- **Flowers:** Dense racemes of pea-like flowers, 8–15 cm long, bearing 12–25
  flowers each. Corolla is reddish-brown to maroon, 10–14 mm long, blooming
  July–September. Flowers are fragrant and attractive to pollinators.
- **Tubers:** The most economically important organ. Tubers are produced in chains
  of 5–20 along rhizomes, each 2–8 cm long and 1–3 cm in diameter. Skin is
  brown and papery; flesh is white, crisp, and starchy. Individual chains
  can weigh 0.5–2 kg. Tubers overwinter in the ground and increase in number
  and size each year.
- **Roots:** Deep taproot system with extensive nitrogen-fixing nodules
  in symbiosis with *Bradyrhizobium* species.

## Distribution and Habitat

*A. americana* is native to eastern North America, ranging from Nova Scotia west
to Minnesota and south to Florida and Texas. It is most abundant in the eastern
deciduous forest biome and the tallgrass prairie-forest ecotone.

The species thrives in moist, partially shaded environments — streambanks, swamp
margins, thickets, woodland edges, and riparian floodplains. It prefers rich,
loamy soils with adequate moisture but tolerates clay, sand, and seasonal
flooding once established. Hardy to USDA zones 3–9.

## Nutritional Profile

The tubers are exceptionally nutritious compared to other [[permaculture-plants-root-crops-and-tubers]]:

- **Protein:** 13–17% by dry weight (3× that of potatoes) — rare for a tuber crop
- **Starch:** 45–55% by dry weight, with a favorable amylose:amylopectin ratio
- **Fat:** <1% by dry weight
- **Minerals:** Rich in iron, potassium, magnesium, and zinc
- **Calories:** Approximately 350 kcal per 100 g dried tuber

The high protein content is attributable to the plant's nitrogen-fixing root
