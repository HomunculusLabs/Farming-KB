# Blue Dream

**Blue Dream** is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid that has become one of the most widely recognized and consumed cannabis strains in the world. A cross between **Blueberry** (indica) and **Haze** (sativa), it delivers a balanced cerebral invigoration paired with full-body relaxation. First emerging from Northern California's medical cannabis scene in the early 2000s, Blue Dream has maintained enduring popularity across both recreational and medical markets.

## Genetic Lineage

| Parent | Type | Origin |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Blueberry** | Indica | DJ Short (Oregon, 1970s) |
| **Haze** | Sativa | Santa Cruz, California (1960s-70s) |

The genetic ratio is approximately **60% sativa / 40% indica**, though this can vary depending on the specific cut and growing conditions. Blue Dream is notable for its genetic stability — multiple verified cuts exist, and the phenotype expression is relatively consistent across generations.

### Notable Cuts and Variants
- **Original Blue Dream** (DJ Short Blueberry × Silver Haze)
- **DJ Blue Dream** (closer to the original DJ Short lineage)
- **Azure Haze** (Blue Dream × Silver Haze backcross, by DJ Short)
- **Blue Dream CBD** (high-CBD phenotype, 1:1 or 2:1 THC:[[cannabis-thc-cbd-ratio-guide-for-conditions]])

## Terpene Profile

Blue Dream's characteristic aroma and effects are driven by a dominant terpene profile:

| Terpene | Approximate % | Aroma Contribution |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Myrcene** | 0.5–1.2% | Earthy, musky, herbal |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.8% | Peppery, spicy |
| **Limonene** | 0.2–0.6% | Citrus, sweet |
| **Pinene** | 0.1–0.4% | Pine, fresh |
| **Linalool** | trace | Floral, lavender |
| **Humulene** | trace | Woody, earthy |
| **Nerolidol** | trace | Woody, citrus |

The **myrcene-dominant** profile contributes to the strain's relaxing body effects, while limonene and pinene provide the uplifting, clear-headed cerebral experience. The caryophyllene content adds subtle anti-inflammatory properties via CB2 receptor interaction.

## Cannabinoid Content

- **THC:** 17–24% (typical average ~19%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (standard phenotype); 5–10% (CBD-rich variants)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **CBC:** trace

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Sweet blueberry with earthy undertones, vanilla notes, and a subtle herbal quality. Breaking apart the buds releases a pronounced berry sweetness.
- **Flavor:** Blueberry dominates on the inhale, followed by earthy, vanilla-like notes on the exhale. The smoke is generally smooth with slight pepperiness from caryophyllene.
- **Aftertaste:** Lingering sweet berry with a hint of lavender and spice.

## Effects

### Onset and Duration
Effects onset within 5–10 minutes when smoked or vaporized. Duration is typically 2–3 hours, making it a moderate-length experience compared to heavier indicas.

### Reported Effects (Consensus)

**Positive:**
- Euphoric uplift with mental clarity
- Creative inspiration and focus
- Gentle full-body relaxation without sedation
- Social comfort and talkativeness
- Mood elevation and stress relief
- Mild pain relief

**Negative (less common):**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes (most common)
- Mild anxiety in high doses or sensitive individuals
- Paranoia (rare, dose-dependent)
- Lightheadedness on standing

### Clinical and Therapeutic Applications
Blue Dream is frequently recommended in medical cannabis programs for:

- **Anxiety and depression:** The balanced sativa effects provide mood elevation without overstimulation. Myrcene and linalool contribute anxiolytic properties.
- **[[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]:** Moderate analgesic effects suitable for daytime use without heavy sedation. Caryophyllene provides additional anti-inflammatory action.
- **PTSD:** Euphoric and grounding effects help manage intrusive thoughts while maintaining functional clarity.
- **ADHD:** Some patients report improved focus and task completion, likely from the sativa-dominant cerebral stimulation.
- **[[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]] stimulation:** Useful for patients undergoing chemotherapy or with appetite disorders.
- **Insomnia (mild):** While not a heavy sedative, the relaxing body effects can help with sleep onset at higher doses.

## Growing Information

Blue Dream is considered a **moderate-difficulty** strain suitable for growers with some experience. Its popularity is partly due to its reliable yields and forgiving nature.

### Cultivation Parameters
| Parameter | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| Flowering time | 9–10 weeks |
| Indoor yield | 17–21 oz/m² |
| Outdoor yield | 15–20 oz/plant |
| Height | Medium-tall (up to 150 cm indoors) |
| Preferred climate | Mediterranean, warm and dry |
| Harvest (Northern Hemisphere) | Mid-October |
