# Fractional Distillation (Cannabinoid Processing)

## Description

Fractional distillation is an [[aminopropyl-silica-gel-chromatography-indole-alkaloid-purification]]**: Fractional distillation under vacuum can separate individual [[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]]* are purified by fractional distillation and chromatography.
- **Solvent recovery**: Fractional distillation is used to recover and recycle solvents (ethanol, petroleum ether) from extraction processes, improving both economics and safety.

## Equipment Required

- **Distillation flask**: Claisen flask or round-bottom flask with side arm for modern setups.
- **Fractionating column**: Packed column (glass beads, helices) or Vigreux column providing multiple theoretical plates for separation.
- **Condenser**: Liebig, Graham, or coil condenser cooled by circulating ice water.
- **Vacuum source**: Vacuum pump capable of achieving 30 mm Hg or lower for cannabinoid work.
- **Temperature control**: Precise heating mantle or oil bath with thermometer.
- **Receiving flasks**: Multiple flasks for collecting different fractions at different temperature/pressure points.
- **Cold traps**: Protect vacuum pump from solvent vapors.

## Process Overview (Cannabinoid)

1. Load crude cannabis oil into Claisen flask with glass wool.
2. Begin heating gradually from 90°C under slight vacuum.
3. Remove ethanol distillate first (discarded or recycled).
4. Raise temperature to 200°C while reducing pressure to 30 mm Hg.
5. Collect cannabinoid-rich "red oil" fraction at target temperature/pressure.
6. Higher-temperature fractions contain heavier cannabinoids and impurities.
