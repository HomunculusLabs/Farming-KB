# Apples and Bananas

Apples and Bananas is a modern indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain resulting from an exceptionally complex multi-way cross within the elite Cookies family lineage. Bred by Compound Genetics, this strain combines the genetics of Platinum Cookies × [[granddaddy-purple]] × Blue Power × Gelatti, creating a cultivar renowned for its intensely fruity, dessert-like aroma profile and deeply relaxing body effects. Released to significant commercial acclaim in the early 2020s, Apples and Bananas exemplifies the contemporary trend toward heavily stacked polyhybrid breeding that maximizes terpene complexity and visual bag appeal.

## Strain Classification

- **Species designation:** *[[cannabis-indica]]* × *[[cannabis-sativa]]* hybrid
- **Type:** Indica-dominant polyhybrid (approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa)
- **Breeders:** Compound Genetics
- **Lineage:** (Platinum Cookies × Granddaddy Purple) × (Blue Power × Gelatti)
- **Generation:** Multi-generational polyhybrid (F4+ stabilized)
- **Also known as:** A&B, Apples n Bananas

## Genetic Lineage

The genetic architecture of Apples and Bananas represents a deliberate convergence of some of the most influential West Coast breeding programs:

### Paternal Line: Platinum Cookies × Granddaddy Purple
- **Platinum Cookies** contributes dense bud structure, high [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and the signature Cookies terpene profile (earthy, sweet, slightly pungent). A phenotype of t girl scout cookies cout Cookies lineage selected for its silvery sheen of trichomes
- **Granddaddy Purple** (Ken Estes, early 2000s) introduces deep purple coloration potential, heavy indica sedation, and a grape-like sweetness that rounds out the fruit profile

### Maternal Line: Blue Power × Gelatti
- **Blue Power** (a DJ Short Blueberry × White Widow derivative) contributes blueberry aromatics, enhanced trichome density, and moderate cerebral stimulation
- **Gelatti** (Gelato #33 × South Florida OG) adds a creamy, dessert-like terpene quality, potency amplification, and a complex backend of gas and citrus notes

The combination of these four parent lines yields a chemotype with exceptional terpene layering — the "apples" come from the fruity esters in the Blue Power and Gelatti lineage, while the "bananas" derive from isoamyl acetate and related acetate esters concentrated through the Cookies and GDP genetics.

## Morphology and Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure
Apples and Bananas produces medium-to-tall plants with a robust, branching growth habit. Under optimal conditions, plants reach 100–150 cm (3.2–5 feet) in height indoors. The structure combines the lateral branching of its indica heritage with moderate internodal stretching from the sativa components. Colas are large, dense, and conical, often developing a tapered "candle" shape at the apical tips.

### Leaves
The leaf morphology reflects its hybrid nature — broad, dark green indica-type leaflets with slightly serrated edges, occasionally displaying phenotypic variation toward narrower sativa-like leaflets in some individuals. Foliage is typically a deep forest green, though temperature fluctuations during late flowering can trigger anthocyanin expression, producing stunning purple, magenta, and dark blue coloration across the bud leaves and calyxes.

### Flowers
Buds are large, densely packed, and extremely resinous, with trichome coverage extending well beyond the calyxes onto the surrounding sugar leaves. The visual presentation is striking: deep green to purple buds coated in a thick layer of frosty white trichomes, interspersed with vivid orange pistils that contrast sharply against the darker foliage. The bud structure is chunky and irregular, with individual calyxes swelling to form a knobby, almost sculptural appearance. Resin production is exceptionally heavy, with observable trichome heads even on shade leaves near the canopy interior.

### Aroma and Flavor Profile
The name is remarkably descriptive — the cured flower emits a complex bouquet that genuinely evokes both fruits:

- **Primary aromas:** Sweet green apple, ripe banana, and tropical fruit punch
- **Secondary notes:** Creamy vanilla, grape (from GDP lineage), and a subtle diesel/gas undertone
- **Tertiary undertones:** Earthy, slightly spicy, with a cookie-dough sweetness on the exhale
- **Smoke character:** Smooth and expansive, with a lingering sweet-cream finish. The fruity terpenes survive combustion well, producing a notably pleasant aftertaste

### Terpene Profile
Dominant terpenes identified through analytical testing:

- **Limonene:** 0.5–1.2% — citrus, apple-like brightness; mood elevation
- **Caryophyllene:** 0.4–1.0% — peppery, spicy warmth; anti-inflammatory
- **Myrcene:** 0.8–1.5% — musky, herbal depth; sedative body effects
- **Linalool:** 0.2–0.5% — floral sweetness; anxiolytic properties
- **Humulene:** 0.1–0.3% — earthy, woody; appetite stimulation
- **Pinene:** Trace — subtle pine freshness; counteracts cognitive fog

## Cannabinoid Profile

Typical laboratory analysis reveals:

- **THC:** 20–30% (average ~24%); some phenotypic selections have tested above 32% THC
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3%
- **THCV:** Trace to 0.2%

The exceptionally high THC content places Apples and Bananas firmly in the "heavy hitter" category, suitable primarily for experienced consumers. Novice users should approach with caution due to the rapid onset of potent psychoactive effects.

## Effects and Medical Applications

### Psychoactive Effects
- **Onset:** Rapid (2–5 minutes when smoked or vaporized), reflecting high bioavailability of both cannabinoids and volatile terpenes
- **Initial phase:** Cerebral euphoria and mood elevation with a warm, spreading body sensation. Creative and sociable effects are common in the first 30–45 minutes
- **Secondary phase:** Deep physical relaxation intensifies progressively, often described as "couch-lock" territory at higher doses. The transition from cerebral to somatic effects is smooth rather than abrupt
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours depending on dose and individual tolerance
- **[[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]]:** Dry mouth, dry eyes; at high doses, anxiety, paranoia, or dizziness may occur, particularly in sensitive individuals
