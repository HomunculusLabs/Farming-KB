## Overview

Gelonade is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by the Cookies Fam genetics group, created by crossing Lemon Tree with Gelato #41.

The strain won first place in the Hybrid category at the 2018 High Times Cannabis Cup in [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] that extends to the sugar leaves.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by sharp lemon and citrus notes on the initial impression, followed by creamy, dessert-like undertones inherited from the Gelato lineage.

Secondary notes include subtle diesel fuel, earthy pine, and a faint sweetness reminiscent of lemon meringue or lemon gelato.

The flavor closely mirrors the aroma, delivering a bright lemon zest on the inhale with a smooth, creamy finish that lingers on the palate.

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene:** 1.2–1.8% — dominant citrus aroma and mood elevation
- **Caryophyllene:** 0.6–0.9% — peppery spice and anti-inflammatory properties
- **Myrcene:** 0.5–0.8% — herbal, earthy base notes with sedative undertones
- **Linalool:** 0.3–0.5% — floral lavender accent, anxiolytic effects
- **Beta-pinene:** trace — fresh pine aroma

The high limonene content is the defining terpen characteristic of Gelonade, setting it apart from other Gelato crosses.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 20–26% (occasionally testing up to 28% in premium phenotypes)
- **CBD:** < 0.1%
- **CBG:** trace

Gelonade is considered a high-potency strain and may be overwhelming for novice consumers at the upper end of its THC range.

## Effects

Onset is relatively rapid, typically within 5–10 minutes of consumption, beginning with a cerebral rush characterized by euphoria, creative energy, and heightened focus.

The initial mental stimulation gradually transitions into a balanced body relaxation that does not typically produce heavy sedation.

Common effects reported include elevated mood, increased sociability, enhanced creativity, and mild physical relaxation without couch-lock.

## Cultivation

Gelonade can be grown indoors, outdoors, or in greenhouse environments, with [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-flowering-phase-and-harvest]] and respond well to [[cannabis-training-techniques-comparison]] such as low-stress training and scrogging.

Yields are average to above-average, with indoor growers reporting 400–500 g/m² under optimal conditions.
