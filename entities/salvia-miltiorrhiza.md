## Distribution and Habitat

Native to China and Mongolia, widely cultivated across central and
eastern China. Major production regions include Shandong, Sichuan,
Shaanxi, and Henan provinces.

- **Habitat:** Sunny mountain slopes, grassy hillsides, forest edges,
  and stream banks at 100–1,500 m elevation
- **Soil preference:** Well-drained, sandy loam soils; tolerates poor
  and slightly alkaline soils but performs best in fertile, deep soils
- **Hardiness:** Hardy to USDA Zone 6; drought-tolerant once established
- **Cultivation status:** Extensively farmed commercially; wild harvesting
  has declined due to overcollection
