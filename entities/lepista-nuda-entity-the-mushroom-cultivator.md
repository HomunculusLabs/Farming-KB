# Lepista nuda (Blewit)

## Taxonomy

Lepista nuda (Bull. ex Fr.) Cooke is a basidiomycete mushroom in the order Agaricales, family Tricholomataceae. The species has several synonymized names:

- Clitocybe nuda (Fr.) Bigelow and Smith
- Tricholoma nudum (Fr.) Kummer
- Rhodopaxillus nudus

The genus name Lepista comes from the Greek "lepis" meaning scale, while the
species epithet nuda comes from "nudus" meaning naked. As [[agar-culture-techniques-detailed]], though it has not achieved
commercial scale production in the [[brassinosteroid-psilocybe-cubensis-mycelial-growth-research]], while
previous researchers found the best fruitings occurred on spent compost that
had been colonized for a year or more. Fruitbodies also form on spawned leaf
mulch mixed with sawdust, offering an alternative cultivation approach.

## Cultivation Parameters

### Spawn Medium
A 4:1 sawdust and bran mixture or rye [[falconer-horse-manure-varieties]] and straw compost mixed with 10% fresh straw at spawning, or
leaf mulch and sawdust mixtures.

### Spawn Run
- Relative humidity: 90% or greater
- Substrate temperature: Fastest growth at 70 to 75 degrees F, with minima
  of 40 degrees F and maxima of 86 degrees F
- Duration: 25 to 60 days for complete colonization
- Carbon dioxide: 5,000 to 10,000 ppm
- Fresh air exchanges: 0 per hour
- Light: Incubation in total darkness

### Casing
Standard peat based casing. An option is the addition of shredded leaf material
and activated charcoal to 10% of total mass. Balance to a pH of 7.0.

### Pinhead Initiation
- Relative humidity: 95%
- Air temperature: 55 to 65 degrees F
- Duration: 7 to 14 days
- Carbon dioxide: Less than 1,000 ppm
- Fresh air exchanges: 2 to 4 per hour
- Light: Ambient natural light or optimally 10 lux in the 370 to 420 nanometer
  range

### Cropping
- Relative humidity: 85 to 90%
- Air temperature: 55 to 65 degrees F
- Duration: 24 to 52 weeks
- Carbon dioxide: Less than 1,000 ppm
- Fresh air exchanges: 2 to 4 per hour
- Harvest stage: While the mushroom caps remain convex
- Flushing interval: 10 to 14 days
- Yield potential: Approximately 1.25 pounds per [[comparison-biointensive-vs-square-foot-gardening]] in 14 weeks

### Moisture and Nutrition
Mushrooms are 88 to 90% water and 10 to 12% dry matter.
