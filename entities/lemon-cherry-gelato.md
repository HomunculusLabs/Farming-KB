## Overview

**Lemon Cherry Gelato** is a balanced hybrid [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]] typically ranging from 19–25%, Lemon Cherry Gelato delivers strong
but manageable potency. Its effects are described as euphoric and relaxing without
being overly sedating at moderate doses.

## Lineage

Lemon Cherry Gelato is a three-way cross combining genetics from several prominent
modern cannabis lines:

- **[[cannabis-strain-super-lemon-haze-greenhouse-seed-co]]** (sativa-dominant parent) — [[cannabis-strain-temple-bodhi-seeds-super-silver-haze-oner]] × Lemon Skunk
- **Cherry Gelato** — [[granddaddy-purple]] descendant crossed
  with [[sunset-sherbet]] → [[girl-scout-cookies]]

The sativa influence from Lemon Haze provides uplifting, creative cerebral effects,
while the heavy indica genetics from the Cherry and Gelato side contribute body
relaxation and physical calm. The resulting hybrid ratio is approximately 50/50.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large with a dense, chunky structure typical of the Gelato family.
The calyxes are tightly packed and covered in a thick layer of milky-white trichomes
with occasional amber caps at full maturity.

Coloration is striking: dark [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] base with deep purple and violet hues,
punctuated by bright orange pistils. The purple intensifies under cooler nighttime
temperatures during the final weeks of flowering.

The [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] is exceptionally heavy. Buds feel sticky and leave significant
residue on fingers when handled, making it popular for hash and concentrate extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma opens with a bright, zesty lemon note immediately upon opening a jar. This
is followed by a sweet cherry undertone and a creamy, dessert-like finish that
recalls the original Gelato's signature profile.

On the palate, the lemon-forward character softens into ripe dark cherry with vanilla
and cookie-dough nuances. The smoke is smooth and expansive, with a lingering sweet
citrus aftertaste. Some phenotypes express more berry and less citrus.

## Terpene Profile

The dominant terpenes in Lemon Cherry Gelato reflect its diverse lineage:

- **Limonene** — primary contributor to the bright citrus aroma; associated with
  mood elevation and stress relief
- **Caryophyllene** — adds peppery, spicy undertones; binds to CB2 receptors
- **Myrcene** — provides earthy, herbal depth and contributes to sedative effects
- **Linalool** — floral, sweet notes from the Cherry and Gelato lineage
- **Terpinolene** — present in smaller quantities, adding subtle pine complexity

The high limonene content is atypical for Gelato-lineage strains and comes directly
from the Lemon Haze parent. This gives Lemon Cherry Gelato a more energetic, focused
character compared to standard Gelato phenotypes.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 19–25% (occasionally testing up to 28% in elite cuts)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
