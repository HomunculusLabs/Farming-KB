# Everclear (Extraction Solvent)

## Description

Everclear is a high-proof grain spirit (typically 95% alcohol by volume / 190 proof) used as the standard ethanol solvent in mushroom extraction processes. In the context of mushroom cultivation and mycology, Everclear serves as the primary alcohol for making dual-extraction tinctures and alcohol-based medicinal mushroom extracts. Its high ABV makes it ideal for extracting both alcohol-soluble and certain water-soluble bioactive compounds from mushroom fruitbodies and myceliated grain. The 95% ethanol concentration provides the maximum solvent capacity for breaking down the chitin matrix that traps beta-glucans and other medicinal compounds inside fungal cell walls.

## Classification

- **Type**: Ethanol-based extraction solvent
- **ABV**: 95% (190 proof) standard; also available in 75.5% (151 proof) in some markets
- **Category**: Potable grain alcohol / extraction medium
- **Application**: Medicinal mushroom extract preparation
- **Alternative names**: Grain alcohol, pure ethanol, rectified spirit
- **Legal status**: Available in most US states; restricted or prohibited in some jurisdictions due to high proof

## Key Facts

- At 95% ABV, Everclear provides the standard baseline for calculating mushroom extraction times and dilution ratios
- The standard steeping time for mushroom extraction in 95% ethanol is 14 days
- When using lower-proof alcohols, extraction time must be recalculated using a specific formula
- For example, using 35% ABV gin requires 22.4 days of steeping instead of 14
- Dilution ratios vary by alcohol strength to achieve the target 20% final ABV
- The target final alcohol concentration for finished extracts is approximately 20% ABV for stability and palatability
- Used for extracting compounds from both dried mushroom fruitbodies and supercolonized grain spawn
- The high proof ensures maximum extraction of beta-glucans and other molecules trapped in the chitin matrix
- Alternative spirits can be substituted including vodka, gin, brandy, and rum with appropriate time adjustments

## Extraction Formulas

The standard extraction calculation using 95% Everclear as the baseline:

**Recalculating extraction time for alternative alcohols:**
- 95% - ABV_ALT = X (written as a decimal)
- (X + 1) x 14 days = total extraction time

**Recalculating dilution ratio for alternative alcohols:**
- ABV_ALT / 20% - 1 = ratio of water to use in dilution

**Example with 35% ABV gin:**
- 95% - 35% = 60% = 0.6
- (0.6 + 1) x 14 = 22.4 days steeping time
- 35% / 20% - 1 = 0.75 dilution ratio (1 part extract to 0.75 part water)

This formula system allows any spirit to be used as the extraction solvent while maintaining consistent extraction efficacy. The lower the alcohol percentage, the longer the steeping time required and the less water needed for final dilution.

## Extraction Methods

### Dried Mushroom Extraction

Dried mushrooms (preferably powdered for maximum surface area) are submerged in 95% ethanol in sealed jars and placed in a dark location for 14 days. The powder has extensive surface area, facilitating exposure to hot water and alcohols, which improves extraction of flavor and medicinal compounds. For even more intense flavor, the powder can be gently roasted and browned just before adding.

### Grain Spawn Extraction

For grain spawn extracts using Everclear, mycelium is allowed to supercolonize grain for three to four weeks beyond normal colonization. During this extended period, the mycelium produces a wave of metabolites that build up in the grain, visible as a clear or colored liquid. Subjecting grain spawn to fluorescent or natural light further increases metabolite production.

The supercolonized grain is blended with 95% ethanol into a watery paste, transferred to sterile jars, sealed, and placed in a dark location for 14 days. When ready to use, the sediment is not strained but instead the jar is shaken before each dose, and the particulates are consumed as part of the therapy.

Supercolonized reishi (Ganoderma spp.) grain spawn extracted in 95% ethanol develops a varnished tone similar to extracts from mature fruitbodies, demonstrating that grain-based extracts can approximate fruitbody extracts in both quality and appearance.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Everclear is central to the medicinal mushroom extract production workflow described by Cotter (2016). Alcohol extracts are preferred over hot water extracts for beer brewing and beverage applications because they better release the beta-glucans and other medicinal molecules trapped in the chitin matrix.

The extraction formulas using Everclear as the 95% baseline are critical knowledge for any cultivator producing medicinal mushroom products. These formulas enable precise calculations regardless of the actual alcohol spirit used, allowing producers to work with locally available spirits while maintaining extraction efficacy. This dual use of ethanol extraction for both tinctures and mushroom-infused beverages makes Everclear a versatile tool in the mycological product development toolkit.
