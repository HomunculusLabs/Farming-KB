## Cyclocybe cylindracea (*Agrocybe cylindracea*)

## Overview

Cyclocybe cylindracea, formerly classified as Agrocybe cylindracea, is an edible
saprotrophic mushroom in the family Strophariaceae. It is widely cultivated in
East Asia, particularly China, Japan, and Korea, where it is valued for its
nutty flavor, firm [[agrocybe-aegerita-pioppino-medicinal-profile]] sensu auct. (misapplied), Pholiota cylindracea (DC.) Quél.

### Macroscopic Features

- **Cap:** 3–10 cm diameter, convex to plano-convex, dry, pale brown to
  yellowish-brown, often hygrophanous. Surface smooth, occasionally fibrillose.
- **Gills:** Adnexed to sinuate, initially white-gray, darkening brown with
  spore maturity. Crowded.
- **Stipe:** 4–12 cm × 0.5–1.5 cm, cylindrical, white, tough and fibrous.
  Partial veil forming a prominent persistent annulus.
- **Flesh:** White to cream, firm, thick. Odor mildly farinaceous.
- **[[spore-print]]:** Dark brown to tobacco brown.
- **Spores:** 10–14 × 6–8 µm, ellipsoid to amygdaliform, smooth, with a
  distinct germ pore.

### Microscopic Features

- **Cheilocystidia:** Ventricose to clavate, thin-walled, 18–30 × 8–14 µm.
- **Pleurocystidia:** Similar to cheilocystidia, occasional.
- **Pileipellis:** Cutis of repent cylindrical hyphae, 3–6 µm wide.
- **Clamp connections:** Present at all septa.

## Distribution and Habitat

Cyclocybe cylindracea is broadly distributed across temperate and subtropical
regions of Europe, Asia, and [[biological-efficiency]] typically 60–100% on supplemented
  sawdust.
- **Yields:** 0.8–1.2 kg fresh per kg dry substrate across 3–4 flushes.
