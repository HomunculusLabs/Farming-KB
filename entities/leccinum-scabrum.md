## Leccinum scabrum

Leccinum scabrum, the brown birch bolete, is an edible ectomycorrhizal
mushroom in the family Boletaceae, found exclusively in association with
birch (Betula spp.) trees across temperate regions of Europe and North
America.

One of the most commonly collected boletes in northern Europe, L. scabrum
is valued as a good [[cryptic-species]].

Exclusively mycorrhizal with birch: B. pendula, B. pubescens, B. papyrifera,
B. alleghaniensis. Fruits June–November in birch forests, woodland edges,
heathland, and parks. Prefers acidic to neutral soils.

## Ecological Role
