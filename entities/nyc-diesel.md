## Lineage

NYC Diesel is the result of crossing a select **[[sour-diesel]]** female (a Chemdawg × Massachusetts [[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]], and introduces earthy undertones that complement the sharp diesel notes.

The genetic ratio is approximately **60% sativa / 40% indica**, though phenotypic variation can skew slightly in either direction depending on the specific cut.

## Appearance and Aroma

Buds are medium to large, with an elongated, somewhat airy structure typical of sativa-dominant genetics. Calyxes are light green with occasional orange pistils and a moderate layer of trichomes.

The aroma is the strain's most defining characteristic — a sharp, acrid **diesel fuel** scent layered over bright **citrus** (grapefruit and lemon) and subtle **earthy** undertones.

When ground, the bouquet intensifies, releasing additional notes described variously as skunky, herbal, and slightly sweet. The diesel quality is immediately recognizable and can fill a room.

Flavor mirrors the aroma closely: an initial sharp diesel hit on the inhale, followed by tangy citrus and a slightly peppery, earthy finish on the exhale.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Caryophyllene (0.5–1.2%), Limonene (0.8–1.5%), Myrcene (0.4–0.9%).

Secondary terpenes include Pinene, Linalool, and Humulene in trace amounts.

Caryophyllene contributes peppery, spicy notes and acts as a CB2 receptor agonist, potentially adding anti-inflammatory effects.

Limonene drives the pronounced citrus character and is associated with mood elevation, stress relief, and anxiolytic activity.

**Cannabinoid profile:** THC typically ranges 16–22%, with some phenotypes reaching 24%. CBD is low, generally below 0.3%.

## Effects

Onset is relatively fast (5–10 minutes), beginning with a pronounced **cerebral rush** — an uplifted, euphoric head high with enhanced focus and creative energy.

As the high progresses, mild body relaxation develops without the heavy sedation typical of indica-dominant strains. Users typically remain functional and sociable.

The effects are long-lasting for a sativa, often persisting 2–3 hours. The comedown is gradual, sometimes transitioning to a mildly relaxed state.

Common applications include daytime use for creative work, social situations, and outdoor activities. Medical users report benefit for depression, fatigue, ADHD symptoms, and chronic stress.

Potential negatives include dry mouth, dry eyes, and in higher doses, mild anxiety or paranoia — particularly in inexperienced users or anxiety-prone individuals.

## Cultivation

NYC Diesel is considered **moderately difficult** to cultivate, best suited for growers with some experience.

Indoor flowering time is **9–11 weeks**, with outdoor harvest in mid-October (Northern Hemisphere).

Plants exhibit strong sativa stretch during the first weeks of flowering, often doubling in height. Topping or LST (low-stress training) is recommended to manage canopy height.
