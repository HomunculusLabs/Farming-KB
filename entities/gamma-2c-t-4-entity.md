## gamma-2C-T-4 (2,6-Dimethoxy-4-isopropylthiophenethylamine)

gamma-2C-T-4 is a phenethylamine compound and a member of the 2C-T family of psychedelic substances. It is notable as a "pseudo-psychedelic," representing the 2,4,6-trisubstitution pattern rather than the classic 2,4,5 pattern. The compound is listed as [[savory-short-duration-grazing-and-cell-systems]].

At 12 mg, subjects noted awareness of activity beginning around 25 minutes with progression over the following quarter hour, but without a distinct "sparkle."

The dosage is listed as "above 12 mg" and the duration is "probably short."

## Qualitative Effects

The effects at threshold levels include pleasant feelings and general awareness of altered state.

There is no prominent body load or nausea reported at the explored doses.

The experience is described as gentle and smooth but lacking in dramatic psychedelic qualities.

The compound appears to produce a subtle intoxication rather than a full [[tma-6-entity]] (2,4,6-trimethoxyamphetamine), which is described as every bit as potent and fascinating as TMA-2.

Z-7 could be called pseudo, and although it is somewhat down in potency, it is an active drug worthy of clinical study.

Shulgin predicted that every 2,4,5-thing has a potentially active 2,4,6-pseudo-counterpart.

## Synthetic Challenges

Chemically, the 2,6-dimethoxy compounds are much more difficult to synthesize than their 2,5-dimethoxy counterparts.

The 2,5-dimethoxy orientation makes the 4-position a natural and easy target for electrophilic substitution.

The 2,6-dimethoxy orientation pushes for 3-substitution, and the 4-position is completely unnatural, requiring chemical tricks to access.

The synthesis of gamma-2C-T-4 demonstrates one such trick using ortho-lithiation chemistry.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with 3,5-dimethoxy-1-chlorobenzene and isopropylsulfide, which undergo a lithium-halogen exchange reaction.

The intermediate is formylated using DMF to produce 2,6-dimethoxy-4-(i-propylthio)benzaldehyde.

This aldehyde is condensed with nitromethane to form the corresponding nitrostyrene.

The nitrostyrene is reduced using LAH to yield the final amine product.

The hydrochloride salt melts at decomposition and the product is obtained in small quantities.
