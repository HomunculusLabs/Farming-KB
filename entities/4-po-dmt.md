# 4-PO-DMT

## Overview

**4-PO-DMT** (4-phosphoryloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine), also known as
**psilocybin**, is the primary psychoactive prodrug found in over 200 species
of psilocybin-containing mushrooms. It is a phosphorylated tryptamine
alkaloid that is dephosphorylated in vivo to the active metabolite
psilocin (4-HO-DMT).

Psilocybin has been used for millennia in Mesoamerican ceremonial contexts
and is currently under investigation as a therapeutic agent for treatment-
resistant depression, end-of-life anxiety, and substance use disorders. It
acts primarily as a serotonin 5-HT2A receptor agonist.

## Chemical Properties

**Systematic name:** 3-[2-(Dimethylamino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl dihydrogen
phosphate.

**Molecular formula:** C₁₂H₁₇N₂O₄P.

**Molecular weight:** 284.22 g/mol.

**Structure:** Indole ring substituted at the 4-position with a phosphate
ester group; N,N-dimethylated ethylamine [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]] at the indole 3-position.
The phosphate moiety renders the molecule highly polar and water-soluble.

**Appearance:** White crystalline solid when synthesized. In fungal tissue,
exists as a zwitterionic salt that is stable and non-volatile.

**Solubility:** Soluble in water; poorly soluble in nonpolar organic
solvents. The phosphate group confers significantly greater aqueous
solubility than the dephosphorylated metabolite psilocin.

## Pharmacology

Psilocybin itself has low receptor affinity. After oral ingestion, it is
rapidly dephosphorylated by alkaline phosphatase enzymes in the gut wall
and liver to form psilocin (4-HO-DMT), the pharmacologically active
compound responsible for the psychedelic effects.

Psilocin is a partial agonist at the 5-HT2A receptor (Ki ≈ 6 nM), with
additional activity at 5-HT2C, 5-HT1A, and 5-HT1B receptors. The 5-HT2A
activation in cortical layer V pyramidal neurons is the primary mechanism
driving the characteristic [[weil-the-natural-mind-altered-states-normalcy]] of consciousness.

Psilocin also binds to serotonin transporter (SERT) and trace amine-
[[taar1-trace-amine-associated-receptor-psychedelic-pharmacology]] 1 (TAAR1), contributing to its complex pharmacological
profile. The drug exhibits functional selectivity (biased agonism) at
5-HT2A, which may explain differences from other serotonergic psychedelics.

## Effects

Onset occurs 20–60 minutes after oral ingestion. Peak effects are reached
at 60–120 minutes, with total duration of 4–6 hours depending on dose and
individual metabolism.

**Common dose ranges:** 10–20 mg (light), 20–35 mg (moderate), 35–50 mg
(strong) of pure psilocybin. Typical dried mushroom preparations contain
0.5–1.5% psilocybin by dry weight.
