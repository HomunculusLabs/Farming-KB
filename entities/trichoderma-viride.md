# Trichoderma viride

**Type:** Species (Fungus)
**Category:** Mycoparasitic Filamentous Ascomycete
**Also known as:** [[aspergillus-niger]], as an antagonist of numerous plant pathogens, and
as a suppressor of [[brassinosteroid-fungal-fruiting-body-morphology]] bodies
  and substrates
- Found as an endophyte in some plant tissues
- Tolerant of a wide range of environmental conditions and
  substrate pH levels

### Mycoparasitic Activity
- Efficient antagonist of numerous plant-pathogenic fungi
  including [[fukuoka-rice-barley-dual-culture]] (Park and Robinson 1964)
- Successfully used as a biocontrol agent against Verticillium
  malthousei in [[lentinus-edodes-entity-the-mushroom-cultivator]]) on log-woods
  (Hashioka and Komatsu 1964; Komatsu and Hashioka 1964)
- Has been investigated for production of gliotoxin and
  viridin (Webster and Lomas 1964)

### Cellulolytic Enzyme Production
- Source of Trichoderma viride cellulase, commercially
  available from BDH Laboratory Supplies (per source text)
- Used in protoplast isolation protocols for molecular
  studies of fungi
- Combined with Penicillium funiculosum cellulase and
  Aspergillus niger hemicellulase to prevent cell clumping
  during protoplast formation
- Plays a key role in [[gadd-fungal-cell-structure]] wall degradation for DNA
