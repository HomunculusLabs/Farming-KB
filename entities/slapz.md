## Overview

Slapz is a modern hybrid [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]].

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Runtz (Zkittlez × Gelato)
- **Male parent:** MAC ([[miracle-alien-cookies]]) (Alien Cookies × Miracle 15 × Colombian)

The Runtz lineage contributes sweet, candy-like terpenes and balanced hybrid effects. The MAC lineage adds potency, dense bud structure, and complex alien-cookie terpene depth.

Both parent strains are themselves modern classics, making Slapz a stacked genetic cross. The combination produces a plant that expresses the best traits of both lineages.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed, and conical in structure. The calyxes are tightly clustered with thick trichome coverage that gives the flowers a frosty, almost white appearance.

Coloration ranges from deep forest green with occasional purple highlights. The pistils are orange to rust-colored and are present throughout the bud, contrasting sharply with the resin coating.

Trichome density is exceptionally high — a trait inherited from the MAC parent. The resin is sticky and tenacious, adhering to fingers and tools with minimal contact.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is the defining characteristic of Slapz. It opens with a sharp, pungent fuel and chemical note that gives way to sweet tropical fruit, sour citrus, and creamy cookie undertones.

On the exhale, the flavor profile reveals layers of ripe mango, grape, and a distinct doughy, cookie-like finish. The combination of sweet and funky creates a complex, multi-dimensional tasting experience.

Some phenotypes express more of the Runtz candy sweetness, while others lean into the MAC's funky, gassy, and earthy notes. The gas-forward phenotypes are particularly sought after by experienced consumers.

## Terpene Profile

Dominant terpenes typically include caryophyllene, limonene, and linalool. Caryophyllene contributes peppery, spicy notes and is also a dietary [[beta-caryophyllene-entourage-effects-cannabis]].

Minor cannabinoids including CBG and THCV may be present in trace amounts, contributing to the overall effect profile beyond what THC alone would produce.

## Effects

The onset is rapid, typically felt within minutes of inhalation. Initial effects include cerebral euphoria, heightened [[plant-sensory-perception]], and an uplifted, giddy mood characteristic of the Runtz lineage.

As the high progresses, a deep body relaxation sets in from the MAC and indica side. This transitions the experience from heady stimulation to full-body comfort without heavy sedation.

Most users report the effects lasting 2–3 hours. The strain is versatile — suitable for daytime social use at lower doses and evening relaxation at higher doses.

## Cultivation

Slapz is considered moderately difficult to cultivate. The plants are vigorous growers with good lateral branching but can be sensitive to nutrient levels and environmental stress.

Flowering time is approximately 8–9 weeks indoors. Yields are moderate to high, typically 400–500 g/m² under optimal conditions. The strain responds well to both SCROG and main-lining techniques.

Outdoor cultivation is possible in Mediterranean climates with harvest in early to mid-October. The dense bud structure requires attention to [[stamets-cultivator-humidity-management-fogging-systems]] in late flowering to prevent mold.

## Side Effects

Common side effects include dry mouth and dry eyes, typical of high-THC cannabis strains. Some users report mild dizziness or paranoia at higher doses, particularly those sensitive to THC.

Anxiety and elevated heart rate can occur, especially with the more potent, gas-forward phenotypes. New users should approach this strain with caution due to its high THC content.

The sedative body effects may interfere with productivity at higher doses. Starting with a small amount and waiting 15–20 minutes before re-dosing is recommended.
