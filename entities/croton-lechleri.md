## Taxonomy and Morphology

Eudicots → Rosids → Malpighiales → **Euphorbiaceae → *Croton → C. lechleri***

Described by John Muirhead Macfarlane in 1891, the species epithet *lechleri*
honors the German botanist and collector Wilhelm Lechler, who extensively
collected in [[agroforestry]] systems and along roadsides throughout the Peruvian
and Ecuadorian Amazon.

## Phytochemistry

The dark red latex of *C. lechleri* is a complex mixture of [[query-what-are-the-main-bioactive-compounds-in-medicinal-plants]]:

- **Proanthocyanidins (OPCs):** 90%+ of the dry latex weight consists of
  oligomeric proanthocyanidins, primarily as dimers and trimers of catechin
  and epicatechin. These are responsible for the latex's potent antioxidant
  and wound-healing properties.
- **Taspine:** An alkaloid identified as the primary cicatrizant (wound-healing)
  compound. Taspine promotes fibroblast migration and collagen deposition
  at wound sites at concentrations as low as 0.001%.
