## Distribution and Habitat

Caulerpa species are predominantly tropical and subtropical, found in shallow
coastal waters from the intertidal zone to depths of 50 m. The genus is
especially diverse in the Indo-Pacific and Caribbean regions.

Species colonize a wide range of substrates including sand, mud, rock, coral
rubble, and seagrass beds. Stolons creep across the substrate, anchoring with
rhizoids and forming dense meadows that can carpet hundreds of square meters
of seabed.

Optimal growth occurs at 22–28°C, salinity of 30–38 ppt, and moderate to high
light intensity. Many species tolerate a broad range of conditions, which
contributes to their success as invasive organisms outside their native range.
