# Nu-Film Sticker-Spreader

## Description

Nu-Film is a commercial sticker-spreader adjuvant used in [[actively-aerated-compost-tea]] (AACT) applications to improve the adhesion and coverage of [[forest-and-bamboo-beneficial-microorganisms]] on leaf surfaces. It is classified as a saponin-based product, derived from natural plant compounds that reduce surface tension and improve film formation on plant foliage.

## Classification

- **Category**: Agricultural adjuvant
- **Type**: Sticker-spreader (surfactant)
- **Active component**: Saponin-based formulation
- **Application**: Foliar spray additive for compost tea

## Key Facts

- **Function**: Acts as both a sticker and spreader for foliar compost tea applications
- **Mechanism**: Saponins reduce surface tension for even distribution across waxy leaf surfaces
- **Compatibility**: Used with AACT to ensure beneficial organisms remain attached after application
- **Rainfastness**: Helps organisms persist on foliage through light rain events
- **Natural origin**: Derived from plant saponins, compatible with organic farming

## Usage in Compost Tea

[[elaine-ingham]] recommends saponin-based sticker-spreaders like Nu-Film when applying AACT as a foliar spray:

1. **Improved coverage**: Ensures compost tea reaches all leaf surfaces including undersides
2. **Organism retention**: Helps beneficial bacteria and fungi stick rather than dripping off
3. **Film formation**: Creates a thin uniform film protecting organisms during establishment
4. **Rain resistance**: Provides rain-fastness, though reapplication may be needed after heavy rain

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Disease prevention**: Better coverage lets beneficial organisms compete with pathogens on leaf surfaces
- **Fungal protection**: Beneficial fungi benefit from improved adhesion when sticker-spreaders are used
- **Economic efficiency**: Less compost tea wasted through runoff
- **Integration**: Works alongside bacterial and fungal-dominated compost teas

## Application Guidelines

- Add to spray tank after compost tea has been diluted to application strength
- Use recommended label rates; over-application reduces effectiveness
- Apply in calm conditions to minimize drift
- Best applied in early morning or late afternoon to avoid UV damage
- Reapply after heavy rain events if disease pressure is high

## Cautions

- Dormant oils should not be used as sticker-spreaders for AACT
- Always test compatibility with specific [[compost-tea-recipes-and-ratios]] before large-scale application
- Avoid application during bloom periods to prevent interference with pollination
