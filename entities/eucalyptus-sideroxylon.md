# Eucalyptus sideroxylon

## Description

Eucalyptus sideroxylon, commonly known as **red ironbark** or **mugga ironbark**, is a medium to tall tree native to eastern Australia.
It is distinguished by its persistent, dark, deeply furrowed bark and is renowned for producing one of the hardest and most durable timbers of any eucalypt.
The species also features showy pink, red, or white flowers, making it valuable as both a timber and ornamental tree.
For permaculture and [[dighton-fungal-drought-tolerance-plant-water-relations]] suit it for shelterbelt plantings
- **[[agroforestry]] plantings
- Growth form improves with space, light, and early [[faires-beekeeping-and-honey-production]].
Because the tree is attractive in bloom, it can also bridge productive and ornamental design goals.

## Management and Harvest

Young trees should be watered only as needed to establish deep roots; long-term over-irrigation is unnecessary and may produce weak growth.
Coppice regrowth can be managed for poles or fuel, while selected stems can be retained for larger timber.
Firewood is excellent but benefits from long seasoning because of its density.
Timber harvest should account for the hardness of the wood, which can be demanding on tools.
In shelter plantings, staggered rows with complementary shrubs reduce [[hemenway-backyard-wildlife-habitat]].
