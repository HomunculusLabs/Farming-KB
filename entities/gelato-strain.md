# Gelato (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

**Gelato** (also known as **Gelato #33** or **Larry Bird**) is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by the Cookie Family (Sherbinskis) in San Francisco, California. A cross between *Sunset Sherbet* and *Thin Mint GSC* ([[girl-scout-cookies]]), Gelato has become one of the most sought-after strains in the modern cannabis market, renowned for its dessert-like flavor profile, vivid coloration, and potent balanced effects. It typically tests between **20–25% THC**, with some premium phenotypes exceeding 27%.

## Origin and Breeding History

### Parental Lineage

| Parent            | Lineage                                        | Contribution                     |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Sunset Sherbet    | Girl Scout Cookies × Pink Panties              | Sweet, fruity [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]    |
| Thin Mint GSC     | OG Kush × Durban Poison (GSC phenotype)        | Potency, minty notes, resin      |

The original breeding project produced multiple phenotypes, numbered sequentially. **#33** became the most famous and widely propagated, earning the nickname "Larry Bird" after the basketball legend's jersey number.

### Breeder

Mario Guzman (Sherbinskis) developed and stabilized the strain in the early 2010s as part of the broader Cookie Family genetic project. Gelato #33 was selected for its exceptional trichome density, color expression, and terpene complexity.

## Morphology and Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

- **Height**: Medium-tall, typically 120–170 cm indoors
- **Structure**: Bushy with moderate lateral branching; indica-dominant stature
- **Leaves**: Broad, dark green with occasional purple hues in cooler temperatures
- **Buds**: Dense, compact, and extremely resinous; displays striking colors ranging from deep purple to fiery orange
- **Trichomes**: Extremely heavy coverage; buds appear frosted white at maturity

### Color Expression

Gelato is prized for its visual appeal. Temperature drops during late flowering (below 18°C nighttime) intensify anthocyanin production, yielding deep purple and blue hues contrasted against bright orange pistils. This dramatic coloration is one of its most marketable traits.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene          | Approx. % | Aroma / Flavor Notes                |
|------------------|-----------|-------------------------------------|
| Linalool         | High      | Lavender, floral, candy              |
| β-Caryophyllene  | High      | Pepper, spice, earthy                |
| Limonene         | Moderate  | Citrus, sweet orange                 |
| Humulene         | Moderate  | Earthy, woody, herbal                |
| Myrcene          | Moderate  | Musky, mango, herbal                 |

The combined terpene expression produces a complex aroma described as **sweet cream, citrus zest, lavender, and mint cookie dough** — directly reflecting its dessert-strain heritage.

## Effects and Therapeutic Applications

### Recreational Effects

- **Onset**: Fast (1–5 minutes inhaled), uplifting cerebral euphoria
- **Peak**: Creative, happy, socially engaging with deep physical relaxation
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours
- **Character**: Described as a "balanced high" — mentally stimulating without anxiety, physically relaxing without heavy sedation

### Reported Therapeutic Benefits

- **Stress and Anxiety**: Linalool and limonene contribute to anxiolytic properties
- **Pain Management**: β-Caryophyllene binds CB2 receptors, providing anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects
- **Insomnia**: Higher doses promote sleep onset; useful for evening use
- **[[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]**: Moderate munchie effect beneficial for cachexia and nausea
- **Mood Disorders**: Uplifting properties may assist with depression and mood regulation

## Cultivation Guide

### Indoor

| Parameter          | Recommendation                        |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Light              | Strong (600W HPS or equivalent LED)  |
| Temperature        | 20–26°C (vegetative), 18–24°C (flower) |
| Humidity           | 50–60% (veg), 40–50% (flower)        |
| Flowering Time     | 8–9 weeks                            |
| Yield              | 400–500 g/m²                         |
| Training           | Responds well to LST, SCROG, topping |
| Nutrient Needs     | Moderate; watch for calcium sensitivity |

### Outdoor

- **Climate**: Mediterranean or warm temperate (zones 8–11)
- **Harvest**: Early to mid-October (northern hemisphere)
- **Yield**: 500–700 g/plant
- **Mold Resistance**: Moderate; ensure good airflow through dense canopy

### Common Challenges

- **Nutrient sensitivity**: Prone to [[dynamic-accumulator-plants-calcium-and-magnesium]] deficiencies in hydroponic systems
- **Dense bud structure**: Susceptible to bud rot (*[[botrytis-cinerea]]*) in high humidity
- **Support needed**: Heavy colas require staking in late flower
- **Odor control**: Extremely pungent; carbon filtration essential indoors

## Market Significance

Gelato has been a consistent top-10 strain in legal US markets since 2018. Its genetics have spawned dozens of derivative strains including **Gello**, **Gelatti**, **Gelato Cake**, and **Lemon Cherry Gelato**. The strain won multiple Cannabis Cup awards and remains a benchmark for flavor-forward breeding programs.

## Legal Status

Legal status varies by jurisdiction. As a high-THC cultivar, Gelato is available in adult-use and medical markets where cannabis is legal. CBD-rich or hemp versions (below 0.3% THC) are available in some markets under the 2018 US Farm Bill.
