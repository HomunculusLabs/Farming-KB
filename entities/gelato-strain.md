## Gelato (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

**Gelato** (also known as **Gelato #33** or **Larry Bird**) is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by the Cookie Family (Sherbinskis) in San Francisco, California. A cross between *Sunset Sherbet* and *Thin Mint GSC* ([[cannabis-terpene-profile]]    |
| Thin Mint GSC     | OG Kush × Durban Poison (GSC phenotype)        | Potency, minty notes, resin      |

The original breeding project produced multiple phenotypes, numbered sequentially. **#33** became the most famous and widely propagated, earning the nickname "Larry Bird" after the basketball legend"s jersey number.

### Breeder

Mario Guzman (Sherbinskis) developed and stabilized the strain in the early 2010s as part of the broader Cookie Family genetic project. Gelato #33 was selected for its exceptional trichome density, color expression, and terpene complexity.

## Morphology and Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

- **Height**: Medium-tall, typically 120–170 cm indoors
- **Structure**: Bushy with moderate lateral branching; indica-dominant stature
- **Leaves**: Broad, dark green with occasional purple hues in cooler temperatures
- **Buds**: Dense, compact, and extremely resinous; displays striking colors ranging from deep purple to fiery orange
- **Trichomes**: Extremely heavy coverage; buds appear frosted white at maturity

### Color Expression

Gelato is prized for its visual appeal. Temperature drops during late flowering (below 18°C nighttime) intensify anthocyanin production, yielding deep purple and blue hues contrasted against bright orange pistils. This dramatic coloration is one of its most marketable traits.

## Effects and Therapeutic Applications

### Recreational Effects

- **Onset**: Fast (1–5 minutes inhaled), uplifting cerebral euphoria
- **Peak**: Creative, happy, socially engaging with deep physical relaxation
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours
- **Character**: Described as a "balanced high" — mentally stimulating without anxiety, physically relaxing without heavy sedation

### Reported Therapeutic Benefits

- **Stress and Anxiety**: Linalool and limonene contribute to anxiolytic properties
- **Pain Management**: β-Caryophyllene binds CB2 receptors, providing anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects
