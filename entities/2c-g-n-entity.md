## 2C-G-N

2C-G-N is a psychedelic phenethylamine based on a naphthalene ring system rather than the typical benzene ring.

It is documented as entry #31 in PiHKAL by [[methanol]] under HCl gas to give 1,4-naphthohydroquinone.

Methylation with HCl and NaOH in methanol yields 1,4-dimethoxynaphthalene, which crystallizes as white needles melting at 87-88 degrees Celsius.

Vilsmeier formylation with POCl3 and N-methylformanilide introduces the aldehyde at the 2-position, yielding 1,4-dimethoxy-2-naphthaldehyde (mp 119-121 degrees C).

The aldehyde is condensed with nitromethane to form the nitrostyrene, which crystallizes as deep gold-colored crystals (mp 146-147 degrees C).

Reduction with lithium aluminum hydride yields 2C-G-N as the free base, a white oil that crystallizes as the hydrochloride salt.

## Pharmacological Context

2C-G-N represents an unusual structural departure from the classic phenethylamine psychedelics.

The naphthalene ring system is larger and more hydrophobic than the typical benzene ring, which may contribute to both the long duration and the character of the effects.

The compound's relationship to the 2C-G series (2c-g-entity|2C-G, 2c-g-3-entity|2C-G-3, 2c-g-4-entity|2C-G-4, 2c-g-5-entity|2C-G-5) is through the shared concept of adding larger alkyl groups to the 4-position.

## Harm Reduction

The 20-30 hour duration is exceptionally long, even by psychedelic standards.

Users should plan for a full day and night of altered consciousness plus residual effects the following day.

Reports of irritability, mild depression, and altered dream patterns for days after the experience suggest a significant recovery period.

Accurate milligram measurement is essential.
