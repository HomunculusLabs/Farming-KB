Seeds require 60–90 days of cold stratification at 4°C for germination. Surface sow in fall for natural stratification, or refrigerate moist seed in sealed bags before spring sowing.

Germination is often irregular, with some seeds germinating in the second year. Transplant seedlings when they develop 3–4 true leaves.

Established plants are relatively low-maintenance. Consistent moisture is the most critical requirement, with drought causing rapid decline.
