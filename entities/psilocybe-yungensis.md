# Psilocybe yungensis

**Type:** Species (Fungus)
**Category:** [[psilocybin]]-containing mushroom
**Also known as:** divinatory mushroom, genius mushroom

## Overview
Psilocybe yungensis is a moderately active psilocybin mushroom found
in subtropical forests and coffee plantations of Central and South
America. Described by Singer and Smith, it is also known by the synonym
Psilocybe acutissima (Heim). The species is distinguished by its
tendency to grow in great numbers on decomposing stumps and wood debris,
its orangish coloration, and caps that remain conic even at maturity.
Its common names suggest cultural significance in indigenous traditions.

## Key Details

### Taxonomy
- **Order:** Agaricales
- **Family:** Hymenogastraceae
- **Genus:** Psilocybe
- **Authority:** Singer and Smith
- **Synonym:** Psilocybe acutissima Heim

### Morphology
- **Cap:** 0.5-1-2 (2.5) cm broad. Conic to campanulate at maturity,
  often adorned with a sharp umbo. Surface smooth, viscid, and
  translucent-striate most of the way to the disc when moist; pellicle
  not separable. Rusty brown to orangish brown to dark reddish brown,
  hygrophanous, fading to dull yellowish brown or dingy straw colored in
  drying. Bruising bluish where injured, then blackish in drying.
- **Gills:** Adnate to adnexed, close to crowded. Dull gray at first,
  soon purplish brown with spore maturity. Edges pale to nearly
  concolorous with gill surface.
- **Stem:** 25-30-50 (60) mm long by 1.5-2.5 (3) mm thick. Equal to
  enlarging towards base. Covered with dense whitish fibrils, pale
  brownish above and reddish brown to reddish brown-black near base.
  Flesh bruising bluish, hollow, fairly brittle.
- **Partial veil:** Cortinate, soon disappearing, leaving whitish
  fibrils along cap margin and scant remnants on upper stem.
- **Spores:** Dark purplish brown in deposit, rhomboid to subrhomboid
  to subellipsoid, (4.4) 5-6 (7) by 4-6 microns.
- **Microscopic features:** Basidia 4-spored. Pleurocystidia 14-25 by
  4.4-10.5 microns, ventricose below, mucronate at apex.
  Cheilocystidia 14-33 by 4.4-7.7 microns, variable, ventricose to
  clavate to strangulated.

### Habitat and Distribution
Most frequently found in clusters or gregariously on rotting wood,
sometimes at bases of stumps, in coffee plantations or subtropical
forests at 1000-2000 meters elevation. Reported from Colombia and
Ecuador, extending north to Mexico (June-July), and from Bolivia
(January). Probably more widely distributed than presently known.

### Chemistry
Moderately active. Exact chemical analyses not available, but the
bluing reaction confirms psilocybin/[[psilocin]] content.

## Relevance
Psilocybe yungensis is notable for its association with coffee
plantations, where it may benefit from the disturbed, enriched soils
and woody debris typical of these environments. The common names
"divinatory mushroom" and "genius mushroom" suggest a history of
traditional or ceremonial use, though documentation is limited. Its
distinctive persistently conic cap shape and prolific fruiting on
decomposing wood make it recognizable in the field.
