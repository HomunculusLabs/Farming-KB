## Cultivation

Edraianthus graminifolius requires well-drained, alkaline, gritty soil in full sun. A mixture of equal parts John Innes No. 2, grit, and crushed limestone provides ideal conditions.

The species is hardy to USDA zone 5 and tolerates temperatures below -20 °C with good drainage. Winter wet is more dangerous than cold; plants may rot in waterlogged conditions.

Propagation is by seed sown in late winter or by careful division of established tufts in spring. Seeds benefit from a period of cold stratification but may germinate without it.

Plants are long-lived in cultivation, forming impressive cushions 15–20 cm across over 8–10 years. They are particularly effective when planted in vertical crevices of a rock wall or tufa garden.
