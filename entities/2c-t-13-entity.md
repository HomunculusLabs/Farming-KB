# 2C-T-13

**PIHKAL Entry #46** -- 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(2-methoxyethylthio)phenethylamine

## Chemical Name

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(2-methoxyethylthio)phenethylamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 25 - 40 mg

- **Duration:** 6 - 8 h

## Qualitative Comments

(with 25 mg) I felt it was somewhat noisy as we went into the experience. This
noisiness lasted only about an hour, then stopped. At the peak, which seemed to
be at about 1 to maybe 1.5 hours, some eyes-closed visuals appeared. There was a
white field with colored visuals, at times geometric in shape. These eye-closed
images were pleasant and I enjoyed them when I did not concern myself with, or
listen to, the conversation. There was an eyes-open change in color. I felt
there was a gradual diminishing of activity starting at 2 to 2.5 hours, and
coming close to baseline at 6 PM. The descent was pleasant and I would say
pleasurable. The experience did not lead to any confusion. There was no problem
with anorexia. We ate constantly during the experience. The grapes and other
fruit were lovely. This is one of the few times I would say that I would try a
higher dose. Maybe 30 or 33 milligrams. It may well be one to try with one's
wife.

(with 28 mg) There was a strange, disturbing twinge exactly eight minutes after
starting this, that asked me, "Should I have done this?" I answered, "Yes" and
the twinge disappeared. And then there was nothing until the expected time of
development, at a half hour when I felt a light head and slight dizziness. There
was a solid plus two for a couple of hours. I paid careful attention for
auditory oddities that I had noted before, but they were not there. I was at a
neutral baseline at about seven hours.

(with 35 mg) There was a quiet climb, but it was marred with some tummy
unquiet, and an annoying persistence of diarrhea. I was very impressed with
eyes-closed patterning, which seemed to do its own thing independently of the
music. I was clearly up to a +++, but there was a feeling that as soon as it
got there it started to go away again. There was no there, there. Yet there
were a couple of touches of introspection, of seriousness which I had to
respect.

(with 40 mg) There were four of us, and the entry was individual for each of
us. Two of us were nauseous. One of us needed his cigarette right now, and then
he saw that he was killing himself, and he swore off. At the two and a half hour
point there is a consensus that this has gone its route and will lose its
impact, so three of us decided to supplement on [[2c-t-2-entity]]. Six
milligrams proves to be a little light so, some four hours later, we each took
another six milligrams. Excellent. In a while we discovered that we were very
hungry, and food tasted marvelous. Headaches acknowledged in the early evening,
but the extension from T-13 to T-2 seemed to be absolutely correct. And as of
the next day, the non-smoker was still a non-smoker.

## Synthesis

To a solution of 3.25 g KOH pellets in 25 mL hot MeOH, there was added 6.8 g
2,5-dimethoxythiophenol followed by 4.73 g 2-methoxyethylchloride. Heated on
steam bath for 0.5 h. Extracted with CH2Cl2, back-washed with 5% NaOH.
Distillation gave 6.65 g of 2,5-dimethoxyphenyl 2-methoxyethyl sulfide (bp
115-125 degrees C at 0.3 mm/Hg).

Vilsmeier formylation gave the aldehyde (3.50 g, pale yellow solid, mp 67-68
degrees C). Nitrostyrene formation gave 3.97 g yellow solid (mp 99-104 degrees
C, recrystallized to mp 107 degrees C from MeOH, burnished gold from IPA, mp
106-107 degrees C). LAH reduction followed by distillation at 155-165 degrees C
at 0.2 mm/Hg gave 1.23 g of clear white oil. The hydrochloride salt weighed
0.89 g.

## Extensions and Commentary

Most of the synthetic adventures of putting a basic something out from the
benzene ring at the four-position have involved subtle things such as unsaturated
bonds or three-membered rings. This was the first try with the actual use of a
different atom (an oxygen). What about other heteroatoms such as sulfur or
nitrogen or silicon or phosphorus?

The sulfur counterpart was named 2C-T-14. The reaction of 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol
with 2-methylthioethyl chloride gave the sulfide, which underwent normal
Vilsmeier to give the aldehyde (mp 64-64.5 degrees C). The nitrostyrene was
prepared as "garish orange-red Las Vegas colored crystals" from acetonitrile
(mp 126-127 degrees C). As of the moment, this is sitting on the shelf waiting
to be reduced to 2C-T-14. "Will it be active? I rather suspect that it will be,
and I'll bet it will be longer-lived than the oxygen model, 2C-T-13."
