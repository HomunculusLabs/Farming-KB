# 3-TME (3-Thiometaescaline; 2,4,6-Trimethoxy-5-methylthiophenethylamine)

3-TME is a sulfur-substituted phenethylamine that replaces one oxygen atom in the trimethoxy pattern of [[tma-2-entity]], with the 5-methoxy group replaced by a 5-methylthio group.

It carries three methoxy groups (at positions 2, 4, and 6) plus one methylthio group (at position 5).

The synthesis begins with 2,4,6-trimethoxybenzaldehyde (phloroglucinaldehyde).

## Pharmacological Profile

3-TME is an active psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 25 to 50 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 12 to 16 hours.

The compound is described as being fully as potent as [[mescaline-entity]].

The long duration is a notable feature, exceeding that of many related compounds.

## Qualitative Effects

At 25 mg, subjects were at an outdoor football game and found the effects difficult to define due to crowd activity.

The experience was described as completely benign, with ease of social interaction.

At 35 mg, subjects reported body tingling all over with unsteady walking at times.

Sleep was difficult the following night, suggesting a preference for lighter dosages.

At 40 mg, one experiment began at noon on a cool rainy day spent indoors.

The experience was characterized by a strange mixture of feelings.

Subjects described being the master within a shell, calling all shots and making all decisions, yet feeling simultaneously disconnected.

At 50 mg, subjects reported alteration of consciousness with a complex interplay of control and surrender.

The compound was described as a simple and easily made material that might justify the extensive synthetic effort.

## Comparison with TMA-2

[[mescaline-entity]] but its activity in humans remains unknown.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 25 - 50 mg.

Duration: 12 - 16 h.
