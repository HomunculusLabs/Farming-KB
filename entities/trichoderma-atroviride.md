## Trichoderma atroviride

## Overview

**Trichoderma atroviride** P. Karst. is a filamentous ascomycete fungus renowned for its biocontrol activity against plant [[endophytic-mycorrhizal-induced-systemic-resistance]] in crops.

The species is widely used in commercial biocontrol formulations and is a [[singh-trichoderma-biocontrol-mechanisms-applications]].

## Taxonomy and Morphology

- **Kingdom:** Fungi
- **Division:** Ascomycota
- **Class:** Sordariomycetes
- **Order:** Hypocreales
- **Family:** Hypocreaceae
- **Genus:** Trichoderma
- **Species:** T. atroviride P. Karst.
- **Teleomorph:** Hypocrea atroviridis Dodd, Lieckf. & Samuels (rarely observed in nature)

Colonies grow rapidly on PDA, reaching 7–9 cm diameter in 7 days at 25°C. Colony color is dark green with a distinctive concentric zonation pattern.

Conidiophores are highly branched, with phialides arranged in whorls. Conidia are ellipsoidal to ovoid, 2.8–4.5 × 2.5–3.5 µm, smooth-walled, and green.

The teleomorph produces dark stromata on wood substrates, 5–15 mm across, with perithecia partially embedded. Ascospores are bicellular, hyaline, and 10–14 × 4–6 µm.

Distinguished from *T. harzianum* by conidial ornamentation (smooth vs. finely warted) and faster growth rate at low temperatures.
