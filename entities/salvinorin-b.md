## Salvinorin B

**Salvinorin B** is a neoclerodane diterpenoid compound found in the
leaves of *[[golden-guide-salvia-divinorum-and-diviner-sage]]* (Epling & Játiva), a psychoactive mint
family plant endemic to the Sierra Mazateca region of Oaxaca, Mexico.

It is the deacetylated metabolite of salvinorin A, the primary
psychoactive component of *S. divinorum*. While salvinorin A is one
of the most potent naturally occurring psychedelics known (active at
200-500 µg), salvinorin B is pharmacologically distinct, showing
markedly reduced affinity for the kappa-opioid receptor.

Salvinorin B is of significant pharmacological interest as a
structure-activity relationship (SAR) reference compound for
understanding the neoclerodane pharmacophore.

## Chemical Properties

- **Chemical class:** Neoclerodane trans-clerodane diterpenoid
- **Molecular formula:** C₂₀H₂₈O₆
- **Molecular weight:** 364.44 g/mol
- **IUPAC name:** (2S,4aR,6aR,7R,9S,10aS,10bR)-9-(acetyloxy)-
  2-(furan-3-yl)-6a,10b-dimethyl-4,10-dioxododecahydro-1H-
  benzo[f]isochromene-7-carboxylic acid
- **Appearance:** White crystalline solid
- **Solubility:** Soluble in acetone, ethyl acetate, chloroform;
  poorly soluble in water

Salvinorin B differs from salvinorin A by the absence of the C-2
acetyl group, bearing a hydroxyl instead. This single modification
dramatically reduces kappa-opioid receptor affinity.

## [[salvinorin-pharmacology|Pharmacology]]

Salvinorin B acts as a weak partial agonist at the kappa-opioid
receptor (KOR), the same receptor target as salvinorin A, but with
substantially lower potency and efficacy.

- **KOR binding affinity (Ki):** Approximately 1,000-5,000 nM,
  compared to ~1-10 nM for salvinorin A — representing a 100-5,000
  fold reduction in affinity
- **Efficacy:** Low partial agonist; does not produce full receptor
  activation even at saturating concentrations
- **Selectivity:** Retains KOR selectivity over mu- and delta-opioid
  receptors, consistent with the neoclerodane scaffold

The dramatic loss of activity upon removal of the C-2 acetyl group
demonstrates that this moiety is critical for high-affinity KOR
binding in the salvinorin pharmacophore.
