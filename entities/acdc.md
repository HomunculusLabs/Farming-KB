# ACDC

ACDC is a high-CBD, low-THC cannabis strain renowned for its
therapeutic potential without significant psychoactive effects. A
phenotype of the legendary Cannatonic line, ACDC typically produces
CBD:THC ratios of 20:1 or higher, making it one of the most reliable
medicinal cannabis strains available. It has become a foundational
strain in the medical cannabis movement, particularly valued for pain
management, seizure reduction, and anxiety relief. Despite its medical
focus, ACDC retains a complex terpene profile that provides a mild,
clear-headed experience appreciated by patients who need daytime
functionality.

## Genetic Background

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | Sativa-dominant hybrid (50/60% sativa / 40/50% indica) |
| Lineage | Select phenotype of Cannatonic (Ruderalis × MK Ultra × G13 Haze) |
| Breeder | Originally selected from Cannatonic seed stock |
| CBD content | 15–20% (commonly 16–19%) |
| THC content | 0.5–2% (rarely exceeding 3%) |
| CBD:THC ratio | Typically 20:1, sometimes 30:1 |

ACDC emerged from the Cannatonic breeding project when a specific
phenotype consistently produced extremely high CBD with minimal THC.
This phenotype was stabilized and named ACDC, and it has since become
one of the most cloned and distributed high-CBD genetics in the world.
It is available from numerous seed banks and clone distributors.

## Morphology and Growing Characteristics

### Plant Structure

- **Height**: Medium to tall (100–150 cm indoors), potentially larger
  outdoors with full-season growth
- **Branching**: Moderate lateral branching, responds well to LST
  (low-stress training) and SCROG (screen of green) methods
- **Node spacing**: Medium-internode spacing, producing well-distributed
  bud sites along branches
- **Leaf structure**: Narrow leaflets reflecting sativa dominance,
  though slightly broader than pure sativa strains
- **Root system**: Moderate root mass; benefits from mycorrhizal
  inoculation and well-aerated growing media

### Flower Characteristics

- **Bud appearance**: Medium to large, moderately dense, somewhat
  leafy. Light green with occasional purple hues under cool
  temperatures. Trichome production is heavy despite low THC,
  reflecting high CBD content.
- **Aroma**: Earthy, piney, and subtly sweet with woody undertones.
  Less pungent than most cannabis strains, which some patients
  prefer for discretion.
- **Flavor**: Mild and smooth, with herbal and slightly citrus notes.
  The flavor is unremarkable compared to high-THC strains but
  palatable and non-harsh when vaporized.
- **Curing**: Benefits from a standard 2–4 week cure at 58–62% RH
  to develop fuller terpene expression and smooth flavor.

### Cultivation Parameters

| Parameter | Optimal Range |
|---|---|
| Photoperiod | 18/6 vegetative; 12/12 flowering |
| Vegetative period | 4–6 weeks recommended |
| Flowering time | 8–10 weeks (63–70 days typical) |
| Temperature (veg) | 22–28°C (72–82°F) |
| Temperature (flower) | 18–26°C (65–79°F) |
| Humidity (veg) | 50–70% |
| Humidity (flower) | 40–55% (lower in final weeks) |
| pH (soil) | 6.0–7.0 |
| pH (hydroponic) | 5.5–6.5 |
| Nutrient needs | Moderate; sensitive to over-fertilization |
| Yield (indoor) | 300–400 g/m² |
| Yield (outdoor) | 400–500 g/plant |

ACDC is considered a moderately easy strain to grow, suitable for
intermediate cultivators. It is relatively pest-resistant and
forgiving of minor environmental fluctuations. The primary
cultivation challenge is maintaining the high CBD:THC ratio — stress
during flowering can sometimes increase THC content, which is
undesirable for patients seeking non-psychoactive medicine.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CBD | 15–20% | Primary therapeutic compound |
| THC | 0.5–2% | Below psychoactive threshold for most users |
| CBDA | 1–4% | Acid precursor; depends on harvest timing |
| THCA | 0.2–0.8% | Minimal psychoactive precursor |
| CBC | Trace | Minor cannabinoid |
| CBG | Trace | Present in small amounts |

The extremely high CBD content with minimal THC is the defining
characteristic of ACDC. Testing from multiple laboratories consistently
confirms the 20:1 ratio, though individual plants can vary. Growers
seeking reliable medical profiles should source verified clones rather
than seeds, as seed-grown plants show greater cannabinoid variation.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene**: Dominant terpene (0.5–1.0%); contributes to relaxing
  effects and is an analgesic and anti-inflammatory compound.
- **Pinene (α and β)**: Significant presence (0.3–0.6%); provides the
  characteristic pine aroma, promotes alertness, and acts as a
  bronchodilator.
- **Caryophyllene**: Moderate (0.2–0.5%); contributes woody and spicy
  notes, binds to CB2 receptors as a dietary cannabinoid.
- **Limonene**: Low to moderate (0.1–0.3%); subtle citrus notes,
  mood-elevating and anti-anxiety properties.
- **Humulene**: Present in trace amounts; earthy aroma contribution
  with anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Linalool**: Trace; adds subtle floral sweetness with anxiolytic
  properties.

## Therapeutic Applications

ACDC is widely used in medical cannabis programs for the following
conditions, supported by both clinical research on CBD and extensive
anecdotal evidence:
- **Epilepsy and seizure disorders**: High CBD content is associated
  with significant seizure frequency reduction, consistent with
  Epidiolex clinical trial data on purified CBD.
- **Chronic pain**: Anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties of CBD
  combined with myrcene provide pain relief without intoxication.
- **Anxiety and PTSD**: Anxiolytic effects of CBD, pinene, and
  linalool without THC-induced paranoia or anxiety amplification.
- **Inflammation**: CBD, caryophyllene, and humulene provide
  multi-pathway anti-inflammatory action via CB2 receptor activation
  and COX-2 inhibition.
- **Neuroprotection**: CBD demonstrates neuroprotective properties in
  preclinical models relevant to Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, and MS.
- **Nausea and appetite**: Mild antiemetic effects without the strong
  appetite stimulation (munchies) associated with high-THC strains.
