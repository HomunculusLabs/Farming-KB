Ergocryptine exists in two epimeric forms: α-ergocryptine and
β-ergocryptine (also called ergocristinine), differing in stereochemistry
at the α-carbon of the cyclol tripeptide moiety. The α-epimer is the
naturally predominant and pharmacologically active form.

The compound is a potent dopamine D₂ receptor agonist and served as
a chemical precursor to the synthetic ergot derivative bromocriptine,
a widely prescribed drug for hyperprolactinemia and Parkinson's disease.
