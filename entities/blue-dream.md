# Blue Dream (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Sativa-dominant hybrid (~60% sativa / 40% indica) |
| **Lineage** | Blueberry × Haze |
| **Breeder** | Unknown (emerged in Northern California, c. 2003–2009) |
| **THC content** | 17–24% (modern avg ~20%) |
| **CBD content** | 0.1–0.5% |
| **Terpene profile** | Myrcene (dominant), Caryophyllene, Pinene, Linalool |
| **Flowering time** | 8–10 weeks indoor; late Sept–mid Oct outdoor |
| **Yield** | 14–21 oz/plant outdoor; 17–21 oz/m² indoor |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate |

Blue Dream is one of the most popular cannabis strains worldwide, consistently in the top 5 in legal US markets for over a decade. Its enduring popularity stems from balanced effects, pleasant flavor, and reliable cultivation.

## Genetic Lineage

### Parent Strains

**Blueberry (indica-dominant):** Bred by DJ Short in the 1970s. Purple Thai × Afghani. Sweet berry aroma, relaxing body effects. Cannabis Cup 2000 Best Indica. Contributes: berry flavor, purple coloration potential, body relaxation.

**Haze (sativa):** Landrace cross of Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian varieties. Developed in Santa Cruz, CA, 1960s–70s. Long flowering (12–14 weeks), energetic cerebral effects. Contributes: mental stimulation, creative energy, tall growth.

The cross balances Blueberry's relaxation with Haze's cerebral uplift — an ideal "daytime hybrid" that eases tension without sedation and engages the mind without anxiety.

## Appearance

Medium-to-large dense conical buds, forest green with occasional blue/purple hues (anthocyanin pigments from Blueberry parent, activated by cool night temperatures). Abundant translucent to milky-white trichomes. Long orange to amber pistils.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Aroma (dry):** Sweet blueberry (primary), earthy/herbal (secondary), vanilla/sugar (tertiary).

**Flavor:** Inhale: sweet blueberry, berry cobbler, light earthiness. Exhale: smooth, creamy, lingering berry sweetness. Aftertaste: light floral with faint vanilla.

### Terpene Breakdown

| Terpene | Approx. % | Character |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Myrcene | 0.5–1.2% | Earthy, musky; sedative, relaxing |
| β-Caryophyllene | 0.3–0.8% | Peppery; anti-inflammatory (CB2 agonist) |
| α-Pinene | 0.2–0.5% | Pine; alertness, bronchodilator |
| Linalool | 0.1–0.4% | Floral, lavender; anxiolytic |
| Limonene | 0.1–0.3% | Citrus; mood elevation |

Myrcene-dominant profile with significant caryophyllene and pinene creates balanced effects: myrcene relaxes, caryophyllene adds anti-inflammatory warmth, pinene maintains mental clarity.

## Effects Profile

**Onset:** Rapid, 1–3 minutes. Builds to peak over 15–30 minutes. Duration: 2–3 hours.

**Cerebral (sativa):** Euphoric uplift, enhanced creativity, mild focus, elevated mood, reduced social anxiety, gentle buzz without overwhelm.

**Physical (indica):** Progressive full-body relaxation, muscle tension relief, mild analgesia, moderate appetite stimulation, light limb warmth.

**Dose-dependent effects:**
- **Low (1–2 puffs):** Mild mood elevation, gentle focus, slight body relaxation
- **Moderate (shared joint):** Full spectrum effects — euphoria, creativity, relaxation, sociability
- **High (solo session):** Stronger cerebral and physical effects; may include couch-lock at very high doses

**Negative effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, mild dizziness at high doses. Rare: anxiety in sensitive individuals.

## Medicinal Applications

Among the most frequently recommended strains in medical dispensaries due to its broad therapeutic utility:

- **Pain:** Mild to moderate chronic pain, neuropathic pain, headaches, migraines, muscle spasms — analgesia without functional impairment
- **Mood disorders:** Depression, anxiety, stress — euphoric and anxiolytic effects via myrcene-linalool combination
- **Fatigue:** Gentle stimulant qualities for daytime fatigue management without overstimulation risk of pure sativas
- **PTSD:** Reduces hyperarousal while maintaining emotional processing capacity
- **Appetite:** Moderate stimulation for chemotherapy-related or eating disorder appetite loss
- **ADHD:** Improved focus and task engagement (pinene counteracts myrcene sedation)

## Cultivation Guide

Blue Dream is relatively forgiving, thriving in Mediterranean and temperate climates.

**Indoor:** Veg 4–6 weeks; flower 8–10 weeks (9 avg). Responds to LST, topping, SCROG. Temp: 20–28°C on / 18–22°C off. Humidity 45–55% flower, reduce to 35–45% final 2 weeks. Moderate-to-heavy feeder; increase P/K during flower.

**Outdoor:** Plant after last frost (May–June). Harvest late Sept–mid Oct. Can reach 150–200 cm. Watch for spider mites, aphids, powdery mildew. Prefers warm, dry autumn for optimal resin.

**Yield tips:** Defoliate weeks 3–5 flower; cool nights (15–18°C) final 2 weeks for purple coloration and terpene boost; flush 7–10 days pre-harvest; optimal at 60–70% milky + 10–20% amber trichomes.

## Notable Variants

- **Blue Dream CBD:** Higher CBD (5–10%) maintaining flavor profile
- **Pink Dream:** Phenotype with pinkish hues
- **Azure Haze:** DJ Short's variation with more Haze influence
- **Blue Dream S1:** Self-pollinated for more consistent phenotypes
