# Blue Dream

**Blue Dream** is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain widely regarded as one of the most popular and commercially successful cannabis varieties in North America. A cross between **Blueberry** (indica) and **Haze** (sativa), it delivers a balanced experience combining cerebral stimulation with gentle physical relaxation.

## Genetic Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Sativa-dominant hybrid |
| **Ratio** | ~60% sativa / 40% indica |
| **Lineage** | Blueberry × Haze |
| **Breeder** | Unknown (emerged Northern California, ~2009) |
| **THC content** | 17–24% |
| **CBD content** | 0.1–0.2% |
| **Terpene profile** | Myrcene-dominant |

## Origin & History

Blue Dream emerged in the Northern California medical cannabis scene around 2009. Its exact breeder remains disputed, though it is widely attributed to the Santa Cruz / Bay Area growing community. The strain was likely bred to combine the euphoric, creative cerebral effects of Haze with the fruity sweetness and body relaxation of DJ Short's Blueberry.

It rapidly became the best-selling strain in California dispensaries and maintained that position for nearly a decade. By the mid-2010s, Blue Dream had become the most searched strain on Leafly and remained in the top three nationally through the legal cannabis market's expansion.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene** (dominant): Herbal, earthy, mango-like. Contributes to the sedating "couch-lock" effect at higher doses. Anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties.
- **Caryophyllene**: Peppery, spicy. Binds to CB2 receptors, adding anti-inflammatory effects. Present in black pepper and cloves.
- **Limonene**: Citrus, bright. Mood elevation, stress relief, and anti-anxiety properties. Enhances absorption of other terpenes.
- **Pinene** (trace): Pine, woody. Counters short-term memory impairment and promotes alertness.

## Effects

### Primary Effects
- **Cerebral:** Uplifting, creative, focused, euphoric. Users report enhanced sociability and conversation flow without the racing thoughts associated with pure sativas.
- **Physical:** Gentle full-body relaxation that is noticeable but not overwhelming. Rarely causes couch-lock at moderate doses.

### Medical Applications
- Chronic pain management
- Daytime anxiety and stress relief
- Depression and mood disorders
- Mild to moderate nausea
- Fatigue (low doses)
- Migraine headaches
- PTSD symptom management

### Onset & Duration
Effects begin within 5–10 minutes when smoked/vaped. Peak at 30–60 minutes. Duration typically 2–3 hours for smoked flower, 4–6 hours for edibles.

## Cultivation

Blue Dream is considered one of the easier strains to grow, making it popular among both commercial operations and home cultivators.

### Growth Characteristics
| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Flowering time** | 9–10 weeks |
| **Yield (indoor)** | 17–21 oz/m² |
| **Yield (outdoor)** | 21–28 oz/plant |
| **Height** | Tall (can exceed 150 cm indoors) |
| **Difficulty** | Beginner-friendly |
| **Resistance** | Good mold resistance; moderate pest resistance |

### Growing Tips
- Responds well to both SCROG and SOG training methods
- Prefers moderate to high nutrient levels during vegetative stage
- Reduce nitrogen during weeks 6–8 of flowering for better terpene development
- Supports heavy bud sites — trellising recommended
- Harvest when 60–70% of trichomes are cloudy with some amber
- Performs well in hydroponic, soil, and living soil setups
- Outdoor harvest: mid-October (Northern Hemisphere)

### Climate Preferences
Thrives in Mediterranean climates similar to its California origins. Optimal temperature range of 20–28°C during the day. Can handle slightly cooler nights during late flowering, which enhances purple/blue coloration in the calyxes.

## Phenotype Variations

Multiple phenotypes exist due to the strain's Haze parentage:

- **Blue Dream (original):** Balanced sativa-dominant with pronounced blueberry aroma
- **Azure Haze:** More Haze-forward, less blueberry sweetness
- **Blue Dream CBD:** High-CBD phenotype bred for medical patients (THC:CBD ~1:1)

## Aroma & Flavor

The aroma is a complex blend of sweet blueberry and earthy herbal notes with a subtle diesel undertone. On inhalation, the flavor is fruity and smooth; on exhalation, earthy and slightly spicy. The blueberry character is most pronounced in well-grown, properly cured flower.

## Potential Side Effects

- Dry mouth (most common)
- Dry eyes
- Mild anxiety at very high doses (rare due to myrcene content)
- Dizziness in sensitive individuals
- Paranoia in inexperienced users at doses above tolerance

## Legal Status

As a cannabis strain, Blue Dream's legal status follows regional cannabis law. It is available through licensed dispensaries in legalized US states, Canada, and several European countries. Classified as a controlled substance in jurisdictions where cannabis remains prohibited.
