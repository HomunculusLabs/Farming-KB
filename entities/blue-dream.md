# Blue Dream

## Overview

Blue Dream is a **sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain** that has become one of the most popular and widely cultivated cannabis varieties in the world since its emergence in the California medical cannabis scene in the early 2000s. A cross between **Blueberry** (indica) and **Haze** (sativa), it delivers a balanced cerebral and physical experience characterized by gentle cerebral stimulation paired with full-body relaxation. Its broad therapeutic appeal and relatively forgiving cultivation profile have made it a staple strain across both recreational and medical markets.

## Genetics & Lineage

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Sativa-dominant hybrid |
| **Ratio** | ~60% sativa / 40% indica (approximate) |
| **Female parent** | Blueberry (indica) — DJ Short |
| **Male parent** | Haze (sativa) — lineage traces to Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and Afghani landraces |
| **Breeder** | Widely attributed to emerging from the Northern California cannabis community (~2003–2006) |
| **Genetic stability** | Moderate; phenotypic variation common without strict selection |

### Genetic Background

- **Blueberry (♀):** An indica-dominant strain bred by DJ Short in the 1970s–80s, known for its distinct blueberry aroma, purple hues, and deeply relaxing body effects. Winner of the 2000 High Times Cannabis Cup.
- **Haze (♂):** A classic sativa landrace cross (Colombian × Mexican × Thai × Afghani), famous for its long flowering time, energetic cerebral high, and complex spicy-sweet terpene profile.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range |
|-------------|---------------|
| **THC** | 17–24% (historically 15–20%) |
| **CBD** | 0.1–0.3% (typically below 0.5%) |
| **CBN** | < 0.1% |
| **THCV** | Trace (< 0.1%) |
| **CBC** | Trace |

**Note:** Modern commercial Blue Dream has trended toward higher THC levels (20–24%) due to selective breeding pressure. Lower-THC phenotypes (14–17%) are still available from heritage breeders and may better represent the original profile.

## Terpene Profile

The characteristic aroma and effects of Blue Dream are primarily driven by the following terpenes:

| Terpene | Percentage | Aroma Character | Known Effects |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Myrcene** | 0.5–1.2% | Earthy, musky, herbal | Sedative, muscle relaxant, anti-inflammatory |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.8% | Peppery, spicy, woody | Anti-inflammatory, gastroprotective, binds CB₂ receptor |
| **Linalool** | 0.2–0.5% | Floral, lavender, sweet | Anxiolytic, sedative, analgesic |
| **Pinene (α+β)** | 0.1–0.4% | Pine, woody, fresh | Alertness, bronchodilator, memory retention |
| **Limonene** | 0.1–0.3% | Citrus, lemon, orange | Mood elevation, anti-anxiety, anti-inflammatory |
| **Humulene** | 0.05–0.2% | Earthy, woody, hoppy | Anti-inflammatory, appetite suppressant |

### Aroma & Flavor

- **Primary:** Sweet blueberry with floral undertones
- **Secondary:** Earthy, herbal, with subtle vanilla and spicy notes
- **Smoke:** Smooth, fruity, with a lingering sweet berry aftertaste
- **The "Blue Dream" signature:** The interplay of Blueberry-derived sweet berry notes with Haze-derived earthy, spicy complexity

## Effects

### Subjective Experience

- **Onset:** 1–3 minutes (vaporization), 15–30 minutes (oral)
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours (smoke/vape), 4–8 hours (edible)
- **Initial phase:** Gentle cerebral uplift, creative energy, enhanced sociability, mild euphoria — described as "motivational" rather than "racing"
- **Body phase:** Gradual physical relaxation without heavy sedation; functional rather than couch-locking
- **Comedown:** Smooth taper; minimal "burnout" compared to heavier indicas

### Common Effects (Self-Reported)

- **Positive:** Happy (90%+), relaxed (85%+), creative (60%+), uplifted (75%+), euphoric (70%+)
- **Negative (rare):** Dry mouth, dry eyes, mild anxiety at high doses, occasional dizziness

## Medical Applications

Blue Dream's balanced profile makes it one of the most versatile medical cannabis strains:

| Condition | Applicability | Rationale |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Chronic pain** | High | Myrcene + THC analgesic synergy; full-body relaxation |
| **Depression** | High | Cerebral euphoria; limonene mood elevation; Haze lineage energy |
| **Anxiety** | Moderate | Linalool anxiolysis; moderate THC avoids overwhelming psychoactivity |
| **Insomnia** | Moderate | Myrcene sedation; body relaxation in later phase |
| **Fatigue** | Moderate | Sativa-dominant cerebral stimulation; daytime functional use |
| **PTSD** | Moderate | Balanced effects reduce hyperarousal while maintaining functionality |
| **Nausea** | Moderate | THC antiemetic; pleasant flavor aids ingestion |
| **Migraines** | Low–Moderate | Caryophyllene anti-inflammatory; analgesic properties |

**Dosage guidance:** Due to moderate THC levels, new patients should start with 1–2 inhalations and wait 15 minutes before redosing. Edible conversions should use a 1:4 ratio (e.g., 5 mg THC inhaled ≈ 20 mg oral).

## Cultivation

Blue Dream is considered an **intermediate-difficulty** strain suitable for growers with basic experience.

### Growing Parameters

| Parameter | Optimal Range |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Flowering time** | 8–10 weeks (indoor) |
| **Outdoor harvest** | Mid-October (Northern Hemisphere) |
| **Yield (indoor)** | 17–21 oz/m² |
| **Yield (outdoor)** | 14–21 oz/plant |
| **Height** | Medium–tall (can exceed 150 cm outdoors) |
| **Stretch ratio** | 2–3× during early flowering |
| **Recommended training** | LST, SCRoG, topping |

### Cultivation Notes

- **Resilience:** Above-average pest and disease resistance; tolerates minor nutrient fluctuations
- **Feeding:** Moderate-to-heavy feeder; responds well to phosphorus and potassium during flowering
- **Climate:** Prefers Mediterranean or warm temperate climates; can handle brief temperature drops
- **Color expression:** Cooler nighttime temperatures (below 15°C/59°F) in late flowering can enhance purple/blue coloration in some phenotypes (anthocyanin expression inherited from Blueberry parent)
- **Clone propagation:** Clones widely available; root easily in 7–14 days; maintains stable phenotype
- **Seed availability:** Feminized and regular seeds from numerous seed banks; phenotype variation more common from seed

## Market Presence

Blue Dream has been the **best-selling cannabis strain** in multiple US legal markets (California, Colorado, Oregon, Washington) for much of the 2010s. It remains consistently in the top 5 across most dispensaries and delivery platforms.

- **California:** Consistently the #1 or #2 strain by volume since legalization (2018)
- **Leafly Strain of the Year:** Won or placed in the top 3 multiple times
- **Price range:** $25–55/eighth (US average, varies by market and quality)
