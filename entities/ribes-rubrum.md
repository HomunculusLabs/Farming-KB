## Overview

**Ribes rubrum**, the red currant, is a deciduous shrub in the family
Grossulariaceae native to western Europe. It produces clusters of
translucent red berries valued for their tart flavor and high vitamin C
content.

The species is widely cultivated commercially in Europe, particularly in
the Netherlands, Poland, Germany, and Russia. Red currants are processed
into jams, jellies, juices, and sauces, and are also grown for home

Flowers are borne in pendulous racemes of 10–20 flowers. Each flower has
five greenish-yellow to reddish petals and a bell-shaped hypanthium.

Fruit is a glossy red berry 6–10 mm in diameter, borne in hanging
clusters. Each berry contains numerous small seeds and has a thin,
translucent skin.
