# Inocybe longicystis

## Taxonomy

| Rank | Name |
|------|------|
| Phylum | Basidiomycota |
| Class | Agaricomycetes |
| Order | Agaricales |
| Family | Inocybaceae |

Inocybe longicystis is a basidiomycete fungus within the
Inocybaceae family, belonging to the order Agaricales. The
genus Inocybe is one of the largest genera of
ectomycorrhizal agarics, containing hundreds of species
worldwide. Members of this genus are generally characterized
by small to medium-sized fruiting bodies with brown spore
prints and the presence of conspicuous cystidia on their
gills. Many Inocybe species contain toxins, including
muscarine, and the genus as a whole plays a significant
ecological role as ectomycorrhizal partners of both
coniferous and deciduous trees in forest ecosystems.
