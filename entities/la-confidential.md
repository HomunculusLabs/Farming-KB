# LA Confidential

LA Confidential is an indica-dominant cannabis strain bred by DNA Genetics,
known for its potent sedative effects, dark dense buds, and classic Afghani
character. Originally developed in Southern California, it gained prominence
after winning multiple Cannabis Cup awards in the mid-2000s. The strain has
become a staple in the medical cannabis community for pain management,
insomnia, and anxiety relief. Its lineage traces to two California landrace
and heirloom selections, producing a plant with straightforward cultivation
needs and consistently high resin production.

## Lineage

LA Confidential is a cross of two well-established California varieties:

- **Female parent:** An Afghani landrace or Afghani-descended indica selected
  for resin production, bud density, and sedative effect. Contributes the
  strain's heavy body high and compact growth structure.
- **Male parent:** An OG Kush-related or South Indian indica selection.
  Contributes some of the complex terpene character, particularly the piney
  and fuel-like undertones that distinguish LA Confidential from pure
  Afghani types.

The exact genetic ratio is approximately 80% indica / 20% sativa, though
phenotypic variation exists. DNA Genetics has not publicly disclosed the
precise breeding history beyond confirming the Afghani foundation.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed, and remarkably hard when properly
cured. Coloration ranges from deep forest green to dark purple, often with
near-black undertones in cooler conditions. Calyxes are swollen and chunky
with minimal visible leaf. Trichome coverage is heavy, giving a frosty
appearance even before peak maturity. Pistils are short, orange to
rust-colored, and tend to retract into the bud mass.

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Primary aroma:** Dense, earthy pine with a distinct skunky undertone.
- **Secondary notes:** Notes of damp soil, leather, and a subtle sweetness
  reminiscent of dark wood or incense on the finish.
- **Flavor:** Smooth, woodsy smoke with pine and earth on the inhale; exhale
  reveals a slightly sweet, almost creamy aftertaste with lingering spice.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene (dominant):** ~0.8–1.2% — contributes sedative, musky,
  earthy character; primary driver of the indica-leaning physical effects.
- **Caryophyllene:** ~0.3–0.5% — adds peppery, spicy warmth and interacts
  with CB₂ receptors for anti-inflammatory modulation.
- **Limonene:** ~0.2–0.4% — subtle citrus lift in the background, offsets
  the heavy earth notes and may contribute mild mood elevation.
- **Pinene:** ~0.1–0.3% — pine needle sharpness in the aroma; may help
  counteract some of the cognitive fog associated with high-myrcene strains.
- **Linalool:** Trace — adds a faint floral sweetness, enhances the
  relaxing and anxiolytic profile.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 17–25% (modern cuts average 19–22%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.2–0.5%
- **THCV:** <0.1%

LA Confidential is not a high-CBD strain and is not recommended for patients
seeking CBD-dominant therapeutic effects. Its medical value comes primarily
from the synergistic entourage effect of THC with its specific terpene
profile, particularly the high myrcene content.

## Effects

Onset is fast, typically within 5–10 minutes of inhalation. Initial cerebral
calm and mild euphoria give way within 30–45 minutes to deep, full-body
relaxation intensifying toward pronounced sedation at higher doses. Users
report significant muscle tension relief, heavy "couch-lock," and reduced
anxiety. At moderate doses the strain remains functional; at higher doses it
strongly promotes sleep. Duration is typically 2–3 hours.

## Medical Applications

- **Pain management:** Effective for chronic pain, neuropathic pain, muscle
  spasms, and tension headaches; the high myrcene content enhances
  analgesic properties.
- **Insomnia:** One of the more reliably sedative strains available;
  frequently recommended for patients with sleep onset difficulties.
- **Anxiety and stress:** The initial cerebral calm combined with physical
  relaxation provides relief from generalized anxiety without the
  paranoid edge some indicas produce.
- **Appetite stimulation:** Moderate munchies effect, useful for patients
  experiencing appetite suppression from medication or illness.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 7–9 weeks (8 weeks average)
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors)
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate; forgiving of minor nutrient errors
- **Preferred climate:** Warm and dry; resistant to mold but sensitive to
  high humidity during late flowering due to dense bud structure.
- **Indoor height:** 80–120 cm; responds well to LST and SCROG methods
- **Outdoor finish:** Early to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere)

The strain branches moderately and benefits from early topping. It is relatively
low-odor during vegetative growth but produces strong aroma during flowering,
requiring carbon filtration indoors. Standard nutrient requirements;
overfeeding nitrogen during flowering reduces terpene expression.

## Side Effects

Common: dry mouth, dry eyes, mild dizziness at higher doses.
Uncommon: paranoia or anxiety in cannabis-sensitive individuals.
Contraindicated for tasks requiring sustained alertness due to sedative profile.

## Notable Phenotypes

Several distinct phenotypes circulate, including a more purple-leaning
variant that expresses deeper coloration in cooler temperatures, and a
slightly more sativa-influenced cut with longer flowering time and more
cerebral effect. DNA Genetics' original cut remains the most widely
available. LA Confidential has been used as a parent in crosses including
Confidential Cheese (with UK Cheese) and LA Con (with various hybrids).
