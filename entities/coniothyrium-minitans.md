# Coniothyrium minitans

**Type:** Species (Ascomycete)
**Category:** Sclerotial Mycoparasite / Biocontrol Agent
**Also known as:** None

## Overview
Coniothyrium minitans is a pycnidial coelomycete and the most
successful [[ingham-trichoderma-biocontrol-fungi-aact]] that operate only in the soil.

Among several potential antagonists, C. minitans and Trichoderma
virens were identified as the most active agents, both singly and
in combination (Whipps and Budge 1990). Once introduced into
soil, the suppressive effect of C. minitans lasts for more than 2
years, conferring long-term suppressive properties on the soil
(Whipps et al. 1993a, 1993b). This persistence is a major
practical advantage for perennial crop systems.

### Biocontrol Efficacy
When abundant in the phyllosphere of oilseed rape, S.
sclerotiorum was effectively suppressed (Whipps et al. 1993a). C.
minitans significantly inhibits S. sclerotiorum in vitro on
nutrient-poor media (Whipps 1987) and is most efficient at
preventing carpogenous germination of sclerotia (Mueller et al.
1985). It colonizes sclerotia more successfully on and in roots
than inside stems of sunflower (Huang 1978).
