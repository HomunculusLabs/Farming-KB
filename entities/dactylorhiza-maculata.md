## Overview

Dactylorhiza maculata, commonly known as the heath spotted-orchid, is a perennial terrestrial orchid in the family Orchidaceae. It is native to Europe and parts of western Asia, where it grows in moist grasslands, heaths, and bog margins.

The species is easily recognized by its dense spike of pink to magenta flowers, each marked with distinctive darker spots and loops on the labellum. Plants typically reach 15–60 cm in height and produce 4–10 leaves arranged along the stem.

Dactylorhiza maculata is one of the most widespread and commonly encountered orchids in northern and central Europe. Despite its relative abundance, local populations have declined in areas where agricultural intensification has destroyed semi-natural grasslands.

The species is widely cultivated by orchid enthusiasts and is available from specialist nurseries. Garden cultivation requires acidic soil, consistent moisture, and a position in full sun to partial shade.
