## 5,6-MDO-MIPT

5,6-Methylenedioxy-N-Isopropyl-N-Methyltryptamine

## Chemical Identity

5,6-MDO-MIPT is N-isopropyl-N-methyl-5,6-methylenedioxytryptamine, a synthetic tryptamine carrying the methyl-isopropyl nitrogen substitution pattern combined with a methylenedioxy bridge across the 5 and 6 positions of the indole ring.

The full chemical name is 5,6-methylenedioxy-3-[2-(isopropylmethylamino)ethyl]indole, also designated as 5H-1,3-dioxolo-[4,5-f]indole-7-ethaneamine, N-methyl-N-isopropyl.

The free base has a melting point of 87-89 degrees Celsius. Mass [[5-6-mdo-dipt-entity-tihkal]]. The methyl-isopropyl nitrogen pattern is the same one found in the much more active compounds [[5-meo-mipt-entity-tihkal]] and [[5-6-mdo-dmt-entity-tihkal]] - the N,N-dimethyl member, also inactive
- [[4-ho-mipt-entity-tihkal]] - the 4-hydroxy analogue, also highly active
