## Description

Catathelasma ventricosa is a spectacular mushroom species found across much [[huckleberries-and-wild-berries-of-north-america]] that is notably covered in [[andrus-voitk]]'s *Common Mushrooms of Newfoundland and Labrador* (2007). It is a large, distinctive basidiomycete that is often absent from standard field guides despite its broad distribution.

## Key Facts

- A large, visually spectacular mushroom species occurring across much of North America.
- Often omitted from standard mushroom field guides despite its wide distribution.
- Featured prominently in Voitk's Newfoundland guide, which notes its spectacular appearance.
- Found in Newfoundland and Labrador, where it is well-documented due to the active local mycological community.
- The genus Catathelasma is relatively small, making C. ventricosa one of its most recognizable members.
