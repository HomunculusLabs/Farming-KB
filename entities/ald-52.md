# ALD-52

**ALD-52** (1-acetyl-N,N-diethyllysergamide), also known as **1-acetyl-LSD** or **1A-LSD**, is a semisynthetic psychedelic substance of the lysergamide class. It is the 1-acetyl derivative of [[lsd-entity-tihkal|TIHKAL]] (Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved).

## Chemical Structure and Properties

**IUPAC name:** (6aR,9R)-N,N-diethyl-7-acetyl-6,6a,8,9,10,10a-hexahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide

**Molecular formula:** C₂₂H₂₇N₃O₂

**Molecular weight:** 349.46 g/mol

**SMILES:** CC(=O)N1[C@@H](C2=CNC3=C2C=C(C=C3)C(=O)N(CC)CC)[C@H](CN1C)C1CCCC1

ALD-52 differs from LSD solely by the presence of an acetyl group (COCH₃) at the 1-position of the indole nitrogen, where LSD bears a hydrogen. This modification adds 42 Da to the molecular weight relative to LSD (C₂₀H₂₅N₃O, 323.43 g/mol). The core tetracyclic ergoline scaffold, diethylamide at C-8, and stereochemistry at C-6a/C-9 are identical to LSD. ALD-52 belongs to the 1-acyllysergamide subclass, which also includes [[1p-lsd]], 1V-LSD, 1cP-LSD) developed by Lizard Labs that emerged in the mid-2010s.

## Legal Status

ALD-52's legal status varies by jurisdiction:

- **[[cannabis-therapeutic-index-overview]] (estimated human LD₅₀ >12,000 µg). Early animal studies found ALD-52 had only ~20% of LSD's toxicity in rabbits
- **Psychological risks:** Anxiety, panic reactions, triggering of latent psychiatric conditions (especially schizophrenia-spectrum), and rare HPPD
- **Vasoconstriction:** Chronic use may carry cardiovascular risk through 5-HT₂B agonism
- **[[ergot-and-lysergic-acid-history]] precursor) at the indole 1-position. As established in the Orange Sunshine trial, known routes proceed through LSD as an intermediate. The compound is hydrolytically sensitive and must be stored anhydrously to prevent conversion to LSD.
