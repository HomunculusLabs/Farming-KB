# ALD-52

**ALD-52** (1-acetyl-N,N-diethyllysergamide), also known as **1-acetyl-LSD** or **1A-LSD**, is a semisynthetic psychedelic substance of the lysergamide class. It is the 1-acetyl derivative of [[lsd-entity-tihkal|LSD]], in which an acetyl group replaces the hydrogen on the indole nitrogen at position 1 of the LSD molecule. ALD-52 acts as a readily converted prodrug of LSD and produces effects that are essentially indistinguishable from LSD. The compound was first synthesized by Albert Hofmann and Franz Troxler at Sandoz in 1957 and later appeared in Alexander Shulgin's [[lsd-entity-tihkal|TIHKAL]] (Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved).

## Chemical Structure and Properties

**IUPAC name:** (6aR,9R)-N,N-diethyl-7-acetyl-6,6a,8,9,10,10a-hexahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide

**Molecular formula:** C₂₂H₂₇N₃O₂

**Molecular weight:** 349.46 g/mol

**SMILES:** CC(=O)N1[C@@H](C2=CNC3=C2C=C(C=C3)C(=O)N(CC)CC)[C@H](CN1C)C1CCCC1

ALD-52 differs from LSD solely by the presence of an acetyl group (COCH₃) at the 1-position of the indole nitrogen, where LSD bears a hydrogen. This modification adds 42 Da to the molecular weight relative to LSD (C₂₀H₂₅N₃O, 323.43 g/mol). The core tetracyclic ergoline scaffold, diethylamide at C-8, and stereochemistry at C-6a/C-9 are identical to LSD. ALD-52 belongs to the 1-acyllysergamide subclass, which also includes [[1p-lsd]], 1V-LSD, and 1cP-LSD.

## Pharmacology

### Mechanism of Action

ALD-52 functions as a **prodrug of LSD**. The acetyl group is metabolically cleaved in vivo to yield pharmacologically active LSD. This prodrug hypothesis, long assumed since the 1960s, was empirically confirmed in the 2010s and 2020s:

- **In vitro studies:** ALD-52 shows dramatically reduced affinities for 5-HT₁A and 5-HT₂A receptors compared to LSD itself. Agonistic activity at 5-HT₂B and 5-HT₂C receptors is largely lost. This indicates that ALD-52 itself has minimal direct pharmacological activity at serotonin receptors.
- **In vivo metabolism:** A large percentage of administered ALD-52 is metabolized into LSD in rodents. Circulating LSD levels following subcutaneous injection are virtually identical whether ALD-52 or LSD is administered.
- **Enzymatic pathway:** Conversion is primarily mediated by **CYP3A4**, as demonstrated by the ability of ketoconazole (a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor) to largely block deacetylation in human liver enzyme systems in vitro.

### Receptor Pharmacology (via LSD Metabolite)

Once converted to LSD, the active compound acts as a **potent partial agonist** at the 5-HT₂A receptor, with additional activity at 5-HT₂B (agonist — cardiac valvulopathy risk at chronic doses), 5-HT₂C (agonist), 5-HT₁A (partial agonist), D₂/D₃ dopamine receptors (partial agonist), and TAAR1 (agonist). In early Sandoz studies, ALD-52 had ~19–20% of the toxicity of LSD in rabbits (IV), 12.5–13% of the pyretogenic effect, and 2.0–2.1× the antiserotonergic activity in isolated rat uterus. By weight, ALD-52 has approximately 90–100% of LSD's potency; on a molar basis (accounting for the ~8% MW difference), the two are roughly equipotent.

### Pharmacokinetics

- **Onset:** 30–90 minutes after oral administration
- **Duration:** 8–12 hours, closely matching LSD
- **Metabolism:** Hepatic via CYP3A4-mediated deacetylation to LSD, then standard LSD pathways
- **HTR potency:** ~45% of molar LSD potency in mouse head-twitch response

## Effects Profile

The subjective effects of ALD-52 are reported as very similar to LSD, with minor qualitative differences noted by some users:

- **Visual effects:** Geometric patterns, color enhancement, trailing, texture flowing, synesthesia — generally comparable to LSD, though one account in TIHKAL described less visual distortion than LSD
- **Cognitive effects:** Enhanced introspection, altered time perception, philosophical ideation, potential for ego dissolution at higher doses. One clinical study reported that ALD-52 seemed to modify cognition and body image to a greater extent than LSD
- **Physical effects:** Pupil dilation, increased heart rate, elevated body temperature, nausea (early phase), reduced appetite, vasoconstriction
- **Emotional effects:** Euphoria, anxiety (set and setting dependent), enhanced empathy, feelings of unity. Per TIHKAL, individual reports varied — some found less anxiety and fewer visuals than LSD, others greater blood pressure effects, and some could not distinguish the compounds at all.

## Dose Range

| Dose Range       | Effect Level                                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| 50–75 µg         | Threshold — subtle perceptual and mood shifts             |
| 75–125 µg        | Light — noticeable visual and cognitive effects           |
| 125–175 µg       | Moderate — full psychedelic experience                    |
| 175–250 µg       | Strong — intense visuals, potential ego dissolution       |
| 250 µg+          | Heavy — overwhelming for most, high anxiety risk          |

Per TIHKAL (50–175 µg) and clinical studies (0.5–1 µg/kg), ALD-52 is roughly equipotent to LSD by weight (~91% by moles).

## History

### Sandoz Research (1950s–1960s)

ALD-52 was first synthesized and described in the scientific literature by **Albert Hofmann** and **Franz Troxler** at Sandoz Laboratories in 1957. Its properties and effects were studied and reported throughout the late 1950s and early 1960s as part of Sandoz's systematic investigation of lysergamide derivatives. It is unclear whether ALD-52 ever circulated as a recreational drug during this period.

### The Orange Sunshine Controversy

ALD-52 became infamous for its alleged connection to **"Orange Sunshine"**, the iconic LSD distributed by underground chemists **Tim Scully** and **Nick Sand** during the 1967 Summer of Love. When arrested in 1973, they claimed in court that they had manufactured ALD-52 rather than LSD, since ALD-52 was not controlled. The prosecution countered that ALD-52 readily hydrolyzes to LSD and that synthesis requires LSD as an intermediate. A sample provided by the defense tested as LSD. Both were convicted — Scully received 20 years, Sand 15 — including for perjury. In the 2000s and 2010s, both publicly admitted Orange Sunshine was always LSD and the ALD-52 defense was fabricated. The story is covered in the 2015 documentary *The Sunshine Makers*.

### TIHKAL Entry

Alexander Shulgin included ALD-52 in [[lsd-entity-tihkal|TIHKAL]] (1997), noting multiple human trials at 50–175 µg with varying subjective reports. He drew an analogy to the [[psilocybin]]-to-[[psilocin]] conversion, describing ALD-52's acetyl group as analogous to psilocybin's phosphate ester.

### Designer Drug Era (2016–present)

ALD-52 was first definitively encountered as a novel designer drug in Europe in 2016, soon appearing in Japan and Brazil. It served as a key parent compound and inspiration for the 1-acyllysergamide prodrugs (e.g., [[1p-lsd]], 1V-LSD, 1cP-LSD) developed by Lizard Labs that emerged in the mid-2010s.

## Legal Status

ALD-52's legal status varies by jurisdiction:

- **United States:** Unscheduled as a specific compound, but potentially prosecutable under the Federal Analog Act as an analog of LSD (Schedule I)
- **United Kingdom:** Class A since January 2015
- **Germany:** Controlled under NpSG since July 2019
- **Switzerland:** Illegal since March 2018 (RS 812.121.11)
- **Singapore:** Class A controlled drug since December 2019
- **Finland:** Controlled since 2014
- **Latvia:** Controlled as LSD structural analog since June 2015
- **Canada:** Not controlled as of 2025
- **Denmark:** Not specifically listed as of 2019

## Safety and Toxicity

ALD-52 carries the same risk profile as LSD once converted in vivo:

- **Toxicity:** LSD has a very high therapeutic index (estimated human LD₅₀ >12,000 µg). Early animal studies found ALD-52 had only ~20% of LSD's toxicity in rabbits
- **Psychological risks:** Anxiety, panic reactions, triggering of latent psychiatric conditions (especially schizophrenia-spectrum), and rare HPPD
- **Vasoconstriction:** Chronic use may carry cardiovascular risk through 5-HT₂B agonism
- **Drug interactions:** Dangerous with lithium (seizure risk), tramadol (serotonin syndrome), MAOIs

## Synthesis

The synthesis of ALD-52 involves acetylation of LSD (or a lysergic acid precursor) at the indole 1-position. As established in the Orange Sunshine trial, known routes proceed through LSD as an intermediate. The compound is hydrolytically sensitive and must be stored anhydrously to prevent conversion to LSD.

## Wiki Relevance

ALD-52 is a notable entity in psychedelic pharmacology as one of the earliest and most historically significant lysergamide derivatives. Its role in the Orange Sunshine legal case makes it a key reference point in drug policy history. As the progenitor of the 1-acyllysergamide prodrug class, it is directly relevant to understanding modern designer lysergamides like [[1p-lsd]], [[al-lad-entity-tihkal]], and [[eth-lad-entity-tihkal]]. Its inclusion in TIHKAL alongside [[lsd-entity-tihkal]] anchors it in the canonical literature of psychedelic chemistry.
