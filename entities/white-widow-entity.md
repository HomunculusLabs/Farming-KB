# White Widow

## Overview

White Widow is an indica-dominant hybrid [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]].

Bred by Shantibaba of Green House Seeds in Amsterdam, White Widow earned
its name from the dense coating of white trichomes that covers mature buds,
giving them a frosted, snow-covered appearance.

The strain became a foundational parent for dozens of subsequent crosses
and remains one of the best-selling coffeeshop strains in Amsterdam
decades after its release. It won the High Times Cannabis Cup in 1995.

## Lineage

White Widow is a cross between a Brazilian [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and the
relaxing body effects characteristic of indica genetics.

Genetic ratio is approximately 60% indica / 40% sativa, though this
varies by cut and phenotype.

White Widow has been used as a parent in numerous notable crosses,
including [[oner-ninja-turtle-white-russian-martian-mean-green]] (White Widow × AK-47), Banana Kush, and
many "White" family strains (White Rhino, White Shark, Great White
Shark).

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed, and conical in shape. The most
distinctive feature is the extraordinary trichome coverage — mature flowers
appear almost white, with a thick crystalline layer of glandular trichomes
extending to the bracts, sugar leaves, and even portions of the stem.

Pistil color ranges from bright orange to copper-brown, providing
contrast against the pale green calyxes and white resin. Leaves are
dark green with occasional purple hues when exposed to cool nighttime
temperatures during late flowering.

The [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]] is exceptional even by modern standards. Hand-rubbing
a bud leaves fingers noticeably sticky with a layer that dries to a
translucent film.

## Aroma and Flavor

White Widow presents a pungent, earthy aroma with distinct pine and
woody undertones. The scent is sharp and skunky, with a slightly sweet
floral note that emerges as buds are broken apart.

On inhalation, the flavor is smooth with dominant pine and earthy
notes, accompanied by a peppery spice on the back of the palate. The
exhale carries a woody, hashish-like quality with subtle floral hints.

The [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] is led by [[limonene]] | 0.1–0.4% | Citrus, uplifting |
| [[tetrahydrocannabinol]] for cannabinoid chemistry.
- **CBD:** 0.1–0.5% (negligible; not a CBD-rich strain)
- **CBN:** Trace amounts in aged flower

The strain is considered high-THC by historical standards, though
moderate by current market benchmarks where 25–30% THC strains are
common. Its reputation derives from the quality and balance of effects
rather than sheer potency.
