# White Widow

## Overview

White Widow is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created in the
Netherlands during the early 1990s, widely regarded as one of the most
influential strains in modern cannabis breeding.

Bred by Shantibaba of Green House Seeds in Amsterdam, White Widow earned
its name from the dense coating of white trichomes that covers mature buds,
giving them a frosted, snow-covered appearance.

The strain became a foundational parent for dozens of subsequent crosses
and remains one of the best-selling coffeeshop strains in Amsterdam
decades after its release. It won the High Times Cannabis Cup in 1995.

## Lineage

White Widow is a cross between a Brazilian sativa landrace and a
South Indian indica landrace.

The Brazilian parent contributes uplifting cerebral effects, vigorous
stretch during flowering, and resistance to mold. The South Indian
parent provides dense bud structure, heavy resin production, and the
relaxing body effects characteristic of indica genetics.

Genetic ratio is approximately 60% indica / 40% sativa, though this
varies by cut and phenotype.

White Widow has been used as a parent in numerous notable crosses,
including White Russian (White Widow × AK-47), Banana Kush, and
many "White" family strains (White Rhino, White Shark, Great White
Shark).

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed, and conical in shape. The most
distinctive feature is the extraordinary trichome coverage — mature flowers
appear almost white, with a thick crystalline layer of glandular trichomes
extending to the bracts, sugar leaves, and even portions of the stem.

Pistil color ranges from bright orange to copper-brown, providing
contrast against the pale green calyxes and white resin. Leaves are
dark green with occasional purple hues when exposed to cool nighttime
temperatures during late flowering.

The resin production is exceptional even by modern standards. Hand-rubbing
a bud leaves fingers noticeably sticky with a layer that dries to a
translucent film.

## Aroma and Flavor

White Widow presents a pungent, earthy aroma with distinct pine and
woody undertones. The scent is sharp and skunky, with a slightly sweet
floral note that emerges as buds are broken apart.

On inhalation, the flavor is smooth with dominant pine and earthy
notes, accompanied by a peppery spice on the back of the palate. The
exhale carries a woody, hashish-like quality with subtle floral hints.

The terpene profile is led by [[beta-caryophyllene]] (peppery, anti-inflammatory), and [[limonene]] | 0.1–0.4% | Citrus, uplifting |
| [[tetrahydrocannabinol-thc]] for cannabinoid chemistry.
- **CBD:** 0.1–0.5% (negligible; not a CBD-rich strain)
- **CBN:** Trace amounts in aged flower

The strain is considered high-THC by historical standards, though
moderate by current market benchmarks where 25–30% THC strains are
common. Its reputation derives from the quality and balance of effects
rather than sheer potency.

## Effects

Onset is relatively rapid (5–10 minutes when smoked), beginning with a
cerebral rush characterized by euphoria, heightened sensory perception,
and mild visual acuity enhancement. Users report feeling creative and
sociable during the initial phase.

After 30–45 minutes, indica effects deepen into a full-body relaxation
that spreads from the neck and shoulders downward. Muscle tension eases,
and a general sense of physical comfort sets in without heavy sedation.

Duration is typically 2–3 hours. The comedown is smooth, with residual
relaxation persisting for another hour. Common applications include
stress relief, mild pain management, and social enhancement.

## Cultivation

White Widow is considered moderately easy to grow and is often
recommended for intermediate cultivators.

Flowering time is 8–9 weeks indoors. Outdoor harvest occurs in late
September to mid-October. Plants produce medium-to-high yields of
400–500 g/m² indoors under optimal conditions.

The strain responds well to both soil and hydroponic systems. It has
good resistance to mold and pests, inherited from its landrace parents.
Plant height reaches 80–140 cm indoors; training techniques (SCROG,
LST) improve yield and light penetration.

White Widow performs best with moderate nutrient levels and benefits
from a slight PK boost during mid-flowering to maximize resin
production. Environmental temperature of 22–28°C during the day is
optimal.
