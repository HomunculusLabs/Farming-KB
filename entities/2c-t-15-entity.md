# 2C-T-15

2C-T-15 is a psychedelic phenethylamine belonging to the 2C-T family, featuring a tert-butylthio group at the 4-position of the aromatic ring.

It is documented as entry #54 in PiHKAL by [[alexander-shulgin]].

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(tert-butylthio)phenethylamine.

2C-T-15 is one of the more unusual members of the 2C-T series, with a bulky tert-butyl (tertiary butyl) group attached to the sulfur atom at the 4-position.

The tert-butyl group creates significant steric bulk near the aromatic ring, which may influence both receptor binding and metabolic stability.

The hydrochloride salt has a melting point of 211-212 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 30 - 60 mg.

Duration of effects: 8 - 12 hours.

The potency is moderate within the 2C-T series, requiring higher doses than compounds like 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2 or 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7.

## Qualitative Effects

At 30 mg, effects are described as gentle and manageable.

Users report a "sensitive" and "good" experience with a feeling of being "open, talkative, comfortable."

Visual enhancement is present but not overwhelming.

At 60 mg, the experience intensifies with rich visual effects and enhanced sensory perception.

The character is described as warm and friendly, with good communication and emotional openness.

Body load is generally reported as minimal, though some gastrointestinal sensitivity during onset has been noted.

## The 2C-T Series

2C-T-15 is one of the longest-chain members of the 2C-T family.

The 2C-T compounds share the core structure of 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamine, with a thioalkyl (sulfur-alkyl) group at the 4-position.

The series progresses from 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2 (methylthio) through 2c-t-4-entity|2C-T-4 (ethylthio), 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 (propylthio), and higher homologues.

The tert-butyl group of 2C-T-15 represents the bulkiest substituent in the series, creating unique steric and electronic properties.

As the alkyl chain lengthens, potency generally decreases while duration tends to increase.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with 2,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde, which is converted to 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol.

The thiophenol intermediate undergoes alkylation with tert-butyl bromide or a similar tert-butylating agent to introduce the tert-butylthio group at the 4-position.

The resulting 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(tert-butylthio)benzaldehyde is condensed with nitromethane in the presence of ammonium acetate to form the beta-nitrostyrene.

Reduction of the nitrostyrene with lithium aluminum hydride yields 2C-T-15 as the free base, which is converted to the hydrochloride salt.

## Pharmacological Context

The bulky tert-butyl group makes 2C-T-15 an interesting pharmacological probe.

The steric bulk may limit access to certain receptor conformations while potentially stabilizing the molecule against metabolic degradation.

The sulfur atom provides polarizability that distinguishes the 2C-T compounds from the purely alkoxy-substituted 2C series.

As with other 2C compounds, 2C-T-15 is believed to act primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist.

## Comparison with Related Compounds

Compared to 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 (propylthio), 2C-T-15 requires higher doses and has a somewhat different character.

The tert-butyl group produces less lipid solubility than a straight-chain alkyl group of equivalent carbon count, which may affect blood-brain barrier penetration.

Among the 2C-T series, 2C-T-15 is considered relatively gentle and forgiving, with less body load than some shorter-chain members.

## Legal Status

2C-T-15 is likely controlled as a member of the 2C-T family in jurisdictions that have scheduled the series.

In the United States, it would fall under analogue drug legislation targeting 2C compounds.

The compound has never been widely available as a research chemical.

## Harm Reduction

The 30-60 mg dose range requires accurate milligram measurement.

The 8-12 hour duration means users should plan for most of a day.

Some gastrointestinal sensitivity during onset has been reported.

As with all psychedelic phenethylamines, 2C-T-15 should not be combined with MAOIs or other serotonergic substances.
