## Description

The Toro Company is a major American manufacturer of irrigation systems, lawn care equipment, and outdoor maintenance products. Founded in 1914 and headquartered in Bloomington, Minnesota, Toro is one of the world's leading providers of innovative solutions for the outdoor environment. Referenced in Steve Solomon's *[[gardening-when-it-counts]]* in the context of sprinkler and irrigation equipment.

## Classification

- **Type:** Organization (manufacturing company)
- **Location:** Bloomington, Minnesota, USA
- **Founded:** 1914
- **Industry:** Outdoor equipment, irrigation systems

## Key Facts

- Global leader in turf maintenance equipment and precision irrigation systems
- Products range from residential lawn sprinklers to large-scale agricultural and golf course irrigation systems
- Referenced on page 172 of *Gardening When It Counts* in relation to sprinkler systems for garden irrigation
- Known for innovations in water-efficient irrigation technology including drip systems, smart controllers, and precision spray heads
- Publicly traded company (NYSE: TTC) with operations in over 125 countries
- Product lines include residential irrigation, professional irrigation, turf equipment, and landscape lighting

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Irrigation technology:** Water management is critical in both gardening and mushroom cultivation; understanding sprinkler and irrigation technology informs misting and humidity control systems used in [[mushroom-fruiting-chambers]]
- **Water efficiency:** Toro's precision irrigation technologies demonstrate principles of efficient water delivery relevant to managing moisture in cultivation substrates
- **Automation:** Their smart irrigation controllers represent approaches to automating environmental control that can be adapted for mushroom growing environments
- **Sprinkler design:** Understanding sprinkler coverage patterns and flow rates is useful for designing outdoor mushroom bed irrigation systems
