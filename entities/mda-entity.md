# MDA (3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine)

**Chemical name:** 3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine

**PiHKAL #100** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +++

**Nicknames:** The "Hug Drug," Mellow Drug of America, Ten Essential Amphetamine

## Overview

MDA is a substituted amphetamine that served as the parent compound for the entire MD-series of empathogenic-psychedelics. It was first synthesized in 1910 but its psychoactive properties were not explored until the 1950s-1960s. MDA combines psychedelic and stimulant properties with a distinctive empathogenic character. It was widely used in the late 1960s counterculture and was explored as a psychotherapeutic tool. MDA is the prototype compound from which mdma-entity|MDMA and mde-entity|MDE were derived.

## Qualitative Effects

- Combination of psychedelic visuals and empathogenic emotional openness
- Euphoric with enhanced tactile sensitivity ("the hug drug")
- Moderate visual effects: color enhancement, pattern recognition, eyes-closed imagery
- Nausea is common, especially at onset (often cleared by vomiting)
- Muscle tension and jaw clenching at higher doses
- Time distortion
- Enhanced appreciation of music
- More psychedelic and "stoning" than mdma-entity|MDMA
- Sexual effects — some numbness at higher doses despite enhanced tactile sensitivity
- Emotional volatility possible

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

At 100 mg: "euphoric and intense," seeing significance in encounters and events. At 128 mg: visual phenomena including smoke rings with complete reality. At 140 mg: abrupt vomiting, then "all around delightful day." At 200 mg (split dose): "complete +++ developed... extreme relaxation... occasional LSD-like moment of profoundness." The "R" isomer is more potent; the "S" isomer is more MDMA-like and peaceful at low doses but shows worrisome physical toxicity at higher levels.

## Extensions and Commentary

MDA has a long and complex history. It was investigated by the US Army as a potential "truth serum" under the code EA-1298. A fatal injection of 500 mg IV into psychiatric patient Howard Blauer in 1953 was one of the earliest known psychedelic-related deaths. It was explored as an anorexic (SKF-5) and for treating psychoneurotic depression. Claudio Naranjo's book "The Healing Journey" describes its psychotherapeutic applications.

MDA is one of the "Ten Essential Amphetamines" — those that can theoretically be synthesized from the ten essential oils found in common spices. The benzylamine isomer (ALPHA) and the propylamine isomer (GAMMA) have both been assayed but are less interesting than MDA itself.

## Safety Warnings

- **Fatal overdose documented** — 500 mg IV injection (Army experiment, 1953)
- Nausea and vomiting common
- Muscle tension, jaw clenching, and nystagmus at higher doses
- More neurotoxic potential than MDMA in animal studies
- The "S" isomer shows "worrisome toxic signs" at higher doses
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Schedule I in the US since 1970; scheduled internationally
