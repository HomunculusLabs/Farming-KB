## Psilocybe merdaria

## Overview

**Psilocybe merdaria** is a small, coprophilous mushroom in the family Hymenogastraceae, known from temperate regions of Europe and [[psilocybe-cubensis-cattle-dung-substrate-introduction-americas-guzman-1998]].

- **deconica coprophila** — A non-psychoactive dung species that may be confused with inactive Psilocybe specimens

## Ethnobotanical and Legal Status

*P. merdaria* has no known ethnobotanical history. It has never been used traditionally or recreationally due to its negligible psychoactive content. The species is of interest primarily to mycologists studying the distribution and chemistry of the genus Psilocybe. Legal status varies by jurisdiction but is largely irrelevant given the species' inactivity.
