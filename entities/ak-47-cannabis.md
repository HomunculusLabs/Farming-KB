# AK-47

AK-47 is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid that has achieved legendary status
in the cannabis community since its creation by Serious Seeds in the 1990s.
Despite its aggressive name, the strain is known for delivering a relaxed,
long-lasting cerebral high with a steady body buzz rather than a racy or
anxious experience. Created by crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and
Afghani landrace genetics, AK-47 represents a four-way landrace fusion that
has remained consistently popular for over three decades. It has won
multiple Cannabis Cup awards and remains a staple in dispensaries and seed
banks worldwide.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica)
- **Lineage:** Colombian × Mexican × Thai × Afghani (four-way cross)
- **Breeder:** Simon, Serious Seeds (Netherlands), circa 1992
- **Seed bank:** Serious Seeds — one of the original Dutch seed companies,
  founded in the mid-1990s after the breeder left Greenhouse Seeds
- **THC content:** 15-20% (some phenotypes up to 22%)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.5% in original; higher-CBD phenotypes exist
  in modern selections)
- **Awards:** Multiple Cannabis Cup placements including 1st place at the
  1999 Cannabis Cup (Soft Secrets category); consistently rated in High
  Times Top 10 Strains of the Year

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

AK-47 is characterized by a complex, pungent, and layered sensory profile:
- **Primary aromas:** Sour, earthy, pine, woody, with a distinct skunky
  undertone
- **Secondary notes:** Floral, sweet, slightly spicy, herbal
- **Taste:** Earthy and sour on the inhale with floral and piney notes,
  finishing with a lingering woody-skunk aftertaste
- **Smoke quality:** Smooth to medium — not as harsh as the name might
  suggest; the smoke is relatively thick and expansive
- **Key terpenes:** [[humulene]],
  [[linalool]]
- The aroma intensity is moderate to strong, making odor control
  important for [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], anxiety, PTSD, depression,
  nausea, appetite stimulation, migraine, muscle spasms, insomnia (at
  higher doses)
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, possible dizziness at high doses,
  rare anxiety in cannabis-sensitive individuals
- The balanced nature of the high makes AK-47 suitable for both daytime
  and evening use, depending on dosage — a versatility that contributes
  to its enduring popularity

## Growing Characteristics

AK-47 is considered one of the easier premium strains to cultivate:
- **Flowering time:** 7-9 weeks (relatively short for a sativa-dominant)
- **Yield:** High — 350-500 g/m² indoors; 500-700 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** Medium to tall, 120-160 cm indoors; can exceed 200 cm
  outdoors in favorable conditions
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate — suitable for beginners despite
  premium genetics
- **Climate:** Adaptable; performs well in temperate and Mediterranean
  climates. Outdoor harvest early to mid-October in the Northern Hemisphere
- **Resistance:** Good pest and mold resistance; the Afghani heritage
  contributes hardiness and disease resistance
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder during [[cannabis-nutrient-regimens-comparison]]
- **Training:** Responds well to SCRoG, LST, and supercropping. Topping
  is recommended to manage height and increase lateral branching
