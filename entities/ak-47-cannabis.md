# AK-47

AK-47 is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid that has achieved legendary status
in the cannabis community since its creation by Serious Seeds in the 1990s.
Despite its aggressive name, the strain is known for delivering a relaxed,
long-lasting cerebral high with a steady body buzz rather than a racy or
anxious experience. Created by crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and
Afghani landrace genetics, AK-47 represents a four-way landrace fusion that
has remained consistently popular for over three decades. It has won
multiple Cannabis Cup awards and remains a staple in dispensaries and seed
banks worldwide.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica)
- **Lineage:** Colombian × Mexican × Thai × Afghani (four-way cross)
- **Breeder:** Simon, Serious Seeds (Netherlands), circa 1992
- **Seed bank:** Serious Seeds — one of the original Dutch seed companies,
  founded in the mid-1990s after the breeder left Greenhouse Seeds
- **THC content:** 15-20% (some phenotypes up to 22%)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.5% in original; higher-CBD phenotypes exist
  in modern selections)
- **Awards:** Multiple Cannabis Cup placements including 1st place at the
  1999 Cannabis Cup (Soft Secrets category); consistently rated in High
  Times Top 10 Strains of the Year

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

AK-47 is characterized by a complex, pungent, and layered sensory profile:
- **Primary aromas:** Sour, earthy, pine, woody, with a distinct skunky
  undertone
- **Secondary notes:** Floral, sweet, slightly spicy, herbal
- **Taste:** Earthy and sour on the inhale with floral and piney notes,
  finishing with a lingering woody-skunk aftertaste
- **Smoke quality:** Smooth to medium — not as harsh as the name might
  suggest; the smoke is relatively thick and expansive
- **Key terpenes:** Myrcene, caryophyllene, limonene, pinene, humulene,
  linalool
- The aroma intensity is moderate to strong, making odor control
  important for indoor cultivation

## Effects and Pharmacology

AK-47 is valued for its balanced and manageable psychoactive profile:
- **Onset:** Moderate, 10-20 minutes
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours, with a long, gradual comedown
- **Primary effects:** Cerebral euphoria, mental stimulation, creative
  focus, uplifted mood, talkativeness, sociability
- **Body effects:** Steady, warm body relaxation that builds gradually;
  not couch-locking at moderate doses
- **Character:** Despite the sativa dominance, the Afghani influence
  provides a grounding body component that prevents the racy,
  overstimulating quality of pure sativas
- **Medical applications:** Chronic pain, anxiety, PTSD, depression,
  nausea, appetite stimulation, migraine, muscle spasms, insomnia (at
  higher doses)
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, possible dizziness at high doses,
  rare anxiety in cannabis-sensitive individuals
- The balanced nature of the high makes AK-47 suitable for both daytime
  and evening use, depending on dosage — a versatility that contributes
  to its enduring popularity

## Growing Characteristics

AK-47 is considered one of the easier premium strains to cultivate:
- **Flowering time:** 7-9 weeks (relatively short for a sativa-dominant)
- **Yield:** High — 350-500 g/m² indoors; 500-700 g/plant outdoors
- **Height:** Medium to tall, 120-160 cm indoors; can exceed 200 cm
  outdoors in favorable conditions
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate — suitable for beginners despite
  premium genetics
- **Climate:** Adaptable; performs well in temperate and Mediterranean
  climates. Outdoor harvest early to mid-October in the Northern Hemisphere
- **Resistance:** Good pest and mold resistance; the Afghani heritage
  contributes hardiness and disease resistance
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder during vegetative growth;
  moderate during flowering. Responds well to standard nutrient regimens
- **Training:** Responds well to SCRoG, LST, and supercropping. Topping
  is recommended to manage height and increase lateral branching
- **Clone performance:** Clones readily and maintains stable genetics;
  mother plants are long-lived and productive
- **Resin production:** Heavy trichome production, making it suitable
  for hash and concentrate production

## Genetic Relatives and Crosses

AK-47's four-way landrace pedigree connects it to some of the most
important cannabis gene pools:
- **Colombian:** Contributes sativa structure, cerebral effects, and
  floral-sweet aroma elements
- **Mexican:** Adds to the sativa backbone with earthy, skunky
  characteristics; Acapulco Gold lineage is often cited
- **Thai:** Provides uplifting, creative qualities and contributes to
  the complex terpene profile
- **Afghani:** Adds indica density, resin production, body relaxation,
  and cultivation hardiness
- **Notable crosses:** AK-47 has been extensively used in breeding:
  AK-48 (AK-47 × Ice), White AK (AK-47 × White Widow), Critical AK
  (AK-47 × Critical Mass), and many others

## Cultural Significance

- **Serious Seeds legacy:** AK-47 was one of the flagship strains that
  established Serious Seeds' reputation in the 1990s Dutch cannabis scene
- **Consistent excellence:** The strain's multi-decade popularity is
  unusual in an industry of rapidly shifting trends — its reliability,
  potency, and balanced effects have kept it relevant
- **Medical cannabis adoption:** AK-47 has been widely adopted in medical
  cannabis programs due to its balanced analgesic and anxiolytic effects
- **Name controversy:** The name has occasionally drawn criticism for
  glorifying firearms, but the breeder has stated the name refers to the
  strain's "one-hit" potency rather than any political statement
- **Stability:** Serious Seeds has maintained AK-47's genetic stability
  over three decades through careful selection, making it one of the most
  consistent hybrid strains available

## Legal Status

As a cannabis strain, AK-47's legal status follows local cannabis
regulations. In jurisdictions where cannabis is legal (Canada, multiple
US states, Germany, Thailand, South Africa, and others as of 2026), AK-47
seeds, clones, and flower are available through licensed channels. In
prohibited jurisdictions, all forms of AK-47 are illegal.
