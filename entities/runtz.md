# Runtz

Runtz is a highly sought-after hybrid cannabis strain celebrated for its
exceptionally sweet flavor profile and potent, balanced effects. A cross
between Zkittlez and Gelato, Runtz emerged from the Los Angeles cannabis
scene in the late 2010s and rapidly became one of the most recognizable
strains in the global market. Its dense, colorful buds and candy-like
aroma have made it a benchmark of modern "dessert" cultivars. Runtz won
first place at the 2020 Emerald Cup and has since spawned an extensive
family of phenotypes and crosses.

## Genetic Lineage

- **Type:** Balanced hybrid (50/50 indica-sativa, phenotype-dependent)
- **Parent 1:** Zkittlez (cross of Grape Ape × Grapefruit)
- **Parent 2:** Gelato #33 (Sunset Sherbet × Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies)
- **Breeder:** Cookies Fam / Berner's network; popularized by Runtz
  cultivation collective in Los Angeles
- **Genetic Family:** Cookie lineage (Girl Scout Cookies descendant)
- **Notable Descendants:** White Runtz, Pink Runtz, Runtz OG, Grape

## Aroma and Flavor

Runtz derives its name from its distinctly candied aroma, reminiscent of
the confectionery. The terpene profile is dominated by fruit-forward and
sweet notes with an underlying creamy earthiness inherited from Gelato.

### Primary Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Range | Aroma Contribution |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Limonene | 0.5–1.2% | Citrus, sweet fruit |
| Caryophyllene | 0.3–0.8% | Peppery, spicy |
| Linalool | 0.2–0.6% | Floral, lavender |
| Myrcene | 0.4–1.0% | Herbal, mango |
| Humulene | 0.1–0.4% | Earthy, woody |

### Flavor Notes
- Dominant: Sweet tropical fruit, ripe banana, sugary candy
- Secondary: Creamy vanilla, berry notes, subtle earth
- Aftertaste: Lingering sweetness with faint pepper

## Appearance

- **Bud Structure:** Dense, tightly packed, medium-to-large nugs
- **Coloration:** Deep forest green base with vivid purple and blue
  undertones; bright orange pistils woven throughout
- **Trichome Coverage:** Extremely heavy — frosted white appearance
  with clear-to-amber glandular trichomes
- **Calyx-to-leaf Ratio:** High calyx density, minimal leaf
- **Visual Appeal:** Strikingly colorful; one of the most
  photogenic strains in modern cultivation

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range |
|----------|---------------|
| THC | 19–29% |
| CBD | <0.1% |
| CBG | 0.1–0.3% |
| THCV | Trace |

Total cannabinoid content typically ranges from 23–33%, with the vast
majority as THC. CBD is negligible. Some phenotypes of White Runtz have
tested above 30% THC in licensed laboratory analyses.

## Effects

Runtz delivers a well-balanced high that begins with cerebral stimulation
before melting into a full-body relaxation. Effects are typically potent
due to high THC levels.

### Onset and Duration
- **Onset:** 1–3 min (smoke), 30–90 min (edible)
- **Peak:** 30–60 min | **Duration:** 2–4 hrs (smoke), 4–8 hrs (edible)

### Reported Effects (by frequency)
- Euphoria — most commonly reported
- Happiness and elevated mood
- Creative inspiration and sociability
- Physical relaxation without heavy sedation
- Mild to moderate body buzz
- Increased appetite (munchies)
- Potential couch-lock at higher doses

### Common Therapeutic Applications
- Stress and anxiety relief (anxiolytic at moderate doses)
- Mild to moderate pain management
- Mood elevation for depressive symptoms
- Appetite stimulation (cachexia, nausea)
- Insomnia support at evening doses
- Temporary relief from muscle tension

### Side Effects (dose-dependent)
- Dry mouth (most common)
- Dry eyes
- Dizziness at high doses
- Paranoia or anxiety in sensitive individuals

## Cultivation

### Growing Parameters

| Parameter | Optimal Range |
|-----------|---------------|
| Flowering Time | 8–9 weeks (indoor), early October (outdoor) |
| Indoor Yield | 400–500 g/m² |
| Plant Height | Medium (80–150 cm indoor) |
| Difficulty | Moderate |
| Resistance | Moderate mold resistance; dense buds need airflow |

### Cultivation Notes
- Benefits from defoliation in weeks 3–4 of flowering to improve
  light penetration to lower bud sites
- Purple coloration intensifies with cooler nighttime temperatures
  (below 18°C) during late flowering
- Feeding: Moderate-to-heavy feeder; responds well to phosphorus
  and potassium boosts in weeks 5–7 of flower

### Phenotype Variation
Runtz exhibits significant phenotypic variation, which has contributed
to its numerous named sub-strains:
- **White Runtz:** Predominantly white trichome coverage, higher THC
- **Pink Runtz:** Distinct pink-purple hue, slightly more indica-dominant
- **Grape Runtz:** Enhanced grape/berry terpene expression

## Legal Status

Legal in jurisdictions permitting recreational or medical cannabis.
THC content above 0.3% remains federally illegal in the United States.
Runtz is not recognized as a distinct cultivar by UPOV or any plant
breeders' rights registry.
