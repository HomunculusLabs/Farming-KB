# Larry OG

## Overview

Larry OG is an indica-dominant [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] that defines the OG lineage while adding
its own subtle earthy sweetness.
Named purportedly after its original grower "Larry," the strain has earned
a reputation for potent full-body relaxation paired with cerebral calm,
making it a popular evening and nighttime choice among medical and
recreational consumers alike.

It is a cross of OG Kush and an unknown indica, though some sources
suggest SFV OG (San Fernando Valley OG) as the second parent.
Larry OG is considered a cornerstone cut of the West Coast cannabis scene
and has served as a parent in several notable crosses.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa)
- **Lineage:** OG Kush × Unknown indica (possibly SFV OG)
- **Breeder:** Anonymous Southern California grower, early 2000s
- **[[oner-sativa-medical-applications]]:**
  - [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]
  - Muscle spasms and cramping
- **Side effects:**
  - Dry mouth and dry eyes (common)
  - Dizziness with overconsumption
  - Anxiety or paranoia at very high doses (uncommon but possible)
  - Lethargy and grogginess the following morning with heavy evening use

## Growing Characteristics
