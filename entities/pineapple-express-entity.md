# Pineapple Express
Pineapple Express is a widely recognized cannabis hybrid strain renowned for
its tropical aroma, vigorous growth, and balanced psychoactive effects that
combine uplifting cerebral stimulation with relaxing body effects. The strain
gained mainstream cultural recognition following the 2008 comedy film of the
same name, which dramatically boosted its popularity despite the movie's
portrayal being largely fictional. Bred by crossing Trainwreck with Hawaiian,
Pineapple Express delivers a distinctly fruity terpene profile dominated by
myrcene, caryophyllene, and pinene, producing aromas of ripe pineapple, mango,
cedar, and pine. With THC content typically ranging from 16–26% in modern
cultivars, it appeals to both recreational users seeking a social, creative
high and medical patients managing stress, pain, and fatigue.
## Lineage and Genetics
Pineapple Express is a cross between two well-established cannabis lines:
- **Trainwreck (female parent):** A legendary Northern California hybrid
  generally accepted as a combination of Mexican, Thai (Sativa), and Afghani
  (Indica) genetics. Trainwreck contributes potent cerebral effects, rapid
  flowering time, and high resin production.
- **Hawaiian (male pollen donor):** A tropical Sativa landrace or landrace-
  derived strain from the Hawaiian Islands. Contributes the distinctive
  tropical fruit aromas, elongated bud structure, and uplifting, energetic
  effects that balance Trainwreck's intensity.
The original breeding is attributed to G13 Labs, though multiple phenotype
selections and subsequent backcrosses have been made by various breeders since
the mid-2000s. The Barney's Farm version is among the most commercially
available, with slight variations in terpene profiles between seed lines.
## Morphology and Appearance
Pineapple Express plants display the following physical characteristics:
- **Plant structure:** Medium to tall stature (90–150 cm indoors), with a
  predominantly Sativa growth pattern featuring long internodal spacing and
  branching that responds well to training techniques (LST, SCROG). Outdoor
  plants can exceed 180 cm in favorable conditions.
- **Bud structure:** Medium to large, conical to slightly spade-shaped buds
  with moderate density. The calyx-to-leaf ratio is favorable for manicuring.
  Buds have a slightly airy structure but compress well during curing.
- **Coloration:** Light to medium green base coloration with occasional hues
  of gold and amber. Pistils are abundant and orange to reddish-brown at
  maturity. Some phenotypes display subtle purple undertones in cooler
  growing conditions.
- **Trichome coverage:** Dense coating of milky-white trichomes with
  occasional amber heads at peak maturity. Resin production is heavy,
  making the strain suitable for hash and concentrate production.
- **Leaf appearance:** Medium-width Sativa-style leaves with serrated edges,
  often displaying yellowish-green pigmentation.
## Terpene Profile and Aroma
Pineapple Express is distinguished by its complex and appealing terpene
profile:
- **Myrcene (dominant, 0.5–1.2%):** Contributes earthy, herbal base notes and
  enhances the sedative body component. Also facilitates cannabinoid
  absorption through cell membranes.
- **Caryophyllene (secondary, 0.3–0.8%):** Adds peppery, spicy undertones and
  provides anti-inflammatory activity through CB2 receptor binding.
- **Pinene (alpha and beta, 0.2–0.6%):** Imparts characteristic pine and cedar
  aromas, contributes mental alertness and bronchodilation, and may
  counteract some of myrcene's sedative effects.
- **Limonene (trace to moderate, 0.1–0.4%):** Adds citrus brightness and
  elevating mood effects. Enhances the tropical fruit character.
- **Ocimene (trace):** Contributes sweet, woody, and herbaceous notes that
  round out the overall aroma profile.
The dominant aromatic impression is tropical fruit — specifically ripe
pineapple, mango, and citrus — with underlying notes of pine, cedar, earth,
and a subtle peppery finish.
## Cannabinoid Profile
Modern Pineapple Express cultivars typically exhibit the following ranges:
- **THC:** 16–26% (some premium phenotypes reported at 28%+)
- **CBD:** < 0.1–0.3% (not considered a CBD-rich strain)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** Trace (< 0.1%)
- **CBC:** Trace (< 0.1%)
The high THC and low CBD ratio places Pineapple Express firmly in the
psychoactive category, with effects driven primarily by THC and modulated by
the terpene entourage.
## Effects Profile
Pineapple Express is valued for its well-balanced hybrid effects:
- **Onset:** Relatively fast (5–15 minutes when smoked or vaporized), with
  initial effects felt in the head and face.
- **Cerebral effects:** Uplifting, euphoric, and mildly energetic. Users
  report enhanced creativity, sociability, and talkativeness. The mental
  stimulation is clear-headed rather than racy or anxious.
- **Body effects:** A pleasant, relaxing body sensation develops gradually,
  complementing the cerebral stimulation without causing heavy sedation or
  couch-lock at moderate doses.
- **Duration:** Moderate duration of 2–3 hours, tapering gradually.
- **Common applications:** Stress relief, mood elevation, mild pain
  management, fatigue, social anxiety, creative enhancement.
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth and dry eyes are most common. Higher doses may
  produce anxiety, paranoia, or dizziness, particularly in novice users.
## Cultivation Parameters
Pineapple Express is considered moderately easy to cultivate:
- **Flowering time:** 7–8 weeks indoors (49–56 days)
- **Indoor yield:** 350–500 g/m² under optimal conditions with training
- **Outdoor yield:** 500–700 g/plant, harvest in early to mid-October
- **Recommended techniques:** Responds well to LST, SCROG, and topping to
  control height and promote lateral branching.
- **Nutrient requirements:** Moderate to heavy feeder during vegetative growth.
  During flowering, balanced NPK with moderate phosphorus and potassium
  supplementation supports bud development.
- **Climate preference:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean-like conditions.
  Tolerates moderate humidity but susceptible to bud rot (Botrytis) in
  high-humidity environments, especially during late flowering.
- **Pest resistance:** Moderate resistance to common pests but susceptible
  to spider mites, powdery mildew, and Botrytis.
## Medical Applications
- **Stress and anxiety:** Uplifting, anxiolytic effects suitable for managing
  generalized anxiety without heavy sedation that impairs daily functioning.
- **Depression:** Euphoric and mood-elevating properties provide temporary
  relief from depressive symptoms for some patients.
- **Chronic pain:** Caryophyllene's anti-inflammatory activity combined with
  THC's analgesic properties offers moderate pain relief.
- **Fatigue:** The stimulating Sativa component helps counter fatigue,
  suitable for daytime medicinal use.
## Cultural Significance
The 2008 film Pineapple Express, starring Seth Rogen and James Franco, is the
primary driver of the strain's mainstream recognition. While the film depicted
the strain as a rare, ultra-potent variety with action-movie effects, the
actual strain is a well-balanced hybrid. The film's cultural impact resulted
in a significant increase in demand, establishing it as one of the most
commercially successful cannabis strain brands worldwide.
