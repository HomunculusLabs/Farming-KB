## Permaculture Design Methods

## Description

[[bill-mollison]] in *Permaculture: A Designer's Manual* (Chapter 3). These methods provide structured frameworks for translating [[deduction-from-nature]] (3.4):** Design by adopting lessons learned from natural ecosystems.
   - **Options and Decisions (3.5):** Design as a selection of options based on decision-making criteria.
   - **Data Overlay (3.6):** Design by map overlays — stacking site factor maps to reveal patterns.
   - **Random Assembly (3.7):** Design by testing unexpected element pairings for beneficial interactions.
   - **Flow Diagrams (3.8):** Design for workplaces — mapping energy, materials, and people movement.
