# 2C-E (Europa)

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylphenethylamine

**PiHKAL #24** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +++

## Overview

2C-E is a potent psychedelic phenethylamine of the [[2c-family-compounds-overview]]. It is widely regarded as one of the most powerful and visually intense compounds in the series. First synthesized and tested by [[alexander-shulgin]], 2C-E has gained a reputation for producing deep, sometimes challenging psychedelic experiences with strong visual and cognitive effects. Shulgin counted it among "the magical half-dozen" of psychedelic phenethylamines.

## Qualitative Effects

- Extremely strong visual effects: complex geometric patterns, color shifting, visual distortion
- Deep cognitive and philosophical introspection
- Intense emotional experiences, both positive and challenging
- Significant body load at moderate to high doses
- Strong time distortion
- Can produce experiences described as "LSD-like" in intensity
- Nausea common during onset, especially at higher doses
- Enhanced appreciation of music and nature
- Potential for difficult or "bad" trips at higher doses

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

Shulgin described 2C-E as "difficult and worthwhile." Reports at 10 mg can give +++ (strongly psychedelic) experiences. At 30 mg, the experience was described as frightening. The compound demands respect and careful dosing. Users report that the experience is significantly more intense than 2c-b-entity|2C-B or 2c-d-entity|2C-D, with deeper introspection and more powerful visuals.

### Selected Experience Reports

**At 16 mg:** "There was a strange devil-angel pairing. As I was being told of the ecstatic white-light ascent of my partner into the God-space of an out-of-body experience, I was fighting my way out of a brown ooze. She saw the young Jesus at the bottom of a ladder drifting upwards step by step to some taking-off place, and I saw all the funny gargoyles around the base of the ladder surrounded by picnic bunting. For me it was the 4th of July, rather than Easter!"

**At 20 mg:** "The view out of the window was unreal. The garden was painted on the window, and every petal of flower and tuft of grass and leaf of tree was carefully sculptured in fine strokes of oil paint on the surface of the glass. It was not out there; it was right here in front of me. The woman who was watering the plants was completely frozen, immobilized by Vermeer. And when I looked again, she was in a different place, but again frozen. I was destined to become the eternal museum viewer."

**At 25 mg (visual animation):** "I have a picture in my living room that is a stylized German scene with a man on horseback riding through the woods, and a young girl coming out to meet him from the nearby trees. But she was not just 'coming out.' He was not just riding through the woods. The wind was blowing, and his horse was at full gallop, and his cape was flapping in the storm, and she was bearing down upon him at full bore. The action never ceased. I became exhausted."

**At 25 mg (toxic psychosis):** "Within minutes I was anxious and sweaty. Each person has his own brand of toxic psychosis — mine always starts with the voices in my head talking to me, about all my worst fears, a jumble of warnings and deep fears spinning faster. Twenty minutes later this complex chaos passed as quickly as it had come. At lower dosages 2C-E has been a truly enjoyable esthetic enhancer. But it really has a steep dose/response curve."

## Extensions and Commentary

2C-E's amphetamine homologue, dom-entity|DOET (2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylamphetamine), is longer acting and more potent. The compound demonstrates that the ethyl group at the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine skeleton produces a highly active compound. The 5-ethoxy homologue (2CE-5ETO) is described as "gentle, forgiving, and extremely long-lived" at 10-15 mg with a 3-4 hour plateau.

As noted in the 2c-d-entity|2C-D entry, the progression from the 4-methyl to the 4-ethyl substitution dramatically increases potency — from a compound that requires heroic doses to one that is fully psychedelic at 15–20 mg. This makes 2C-E a maximally active member of the 4-alkyl series.

2C-E is considered one of the "heavy hitters" of the 2C series and should be approached with caution, especially by inexperienced users. Proper [[psychedelic-therapy-set-and-setting]] is considered essential.

### "One of the Magical Half-Dozen"

Shulgin called 2C-E "another of the magical half-dozen," noting its purposefully broad active range. At 10 milligrams there have been "some pretty rich +++ experiences," and yet a 30 milligram trial from one young lady was "very frightening." Several people have said: "I don't think I like it, since it isn't that much fun. But I intend to explore it again." There is something here that rewards the experimenter. Shulgin concluded: "A difficult and worthwhile material. A very much worthwhile material."

### The Tweetio: 2CE-5ETO ("Eternity")

The 5-EtO-homologue of 2C-E is 5-ethoxy-4-ethyl-2-methoxyphenethylamine, or 2CE-5ETO. The effective level is in the 10–15 milligram range. It is gentle, forgiving, and extremely long-lived. Some 3 to 4 hours were needed to achieve plateau, and on occasion experiments were interrupted with Valium or Halcion at the 16-hour point. After a night's sleep, there were still some effects evident the next day. The dose is comparable to the parent compound 2C-E, but the duration is 2 to 3 times longer. It was given the nickname "Eternity" by one subject.

## Safety Warnings

- **High potency** — doses above 15-20 mg can produce overwhelming experiences
- **Steep dose-response curve** — small increases can dramatically intensify effects
- Significant body load and nausea, especially during onset
- Reports of frightening experiences at higher doses, including transient toxic psychosis
- Long duration means difficult experiences persist for many hours
- Not recommended for inexperienced psychedelic users
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Scheduled in many countries (Schedule I in the US)
