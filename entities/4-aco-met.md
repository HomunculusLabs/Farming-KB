# 4-AcO-MET

## Overview

4-Acetoxy-N-methyl-N-ethyltryptamine (4-AcO-MET), also known as Metocin or
"Colour," is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine and the N-ethyl homologue of
4-AcO-DMT. First synthesized by [[alexander-shulgin]] and documented in TiHKAL
(Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved), it is structurally an acetylated derivative
of 4-HO-MET, functioning as a prodrug that deacetylates to 4-HO-MET in vivo.
4-AcO-MET is widely regarded as one of the most visually rich short-acting
psychedelics, producing vivid color enhancement, geometric patterning, and
entactogenic warmth at common doses while maintaining a manageable headspace
compared to psilocin or LSD. It is not controlled in most jurisdictions and
circulates primarily through online research chemical markets.

## Chemical Identity

- **IUPAC name:** 3-[2-(Ethyl(methyl)amino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl acetate
- **CAS number:** 1108961-81-2 (estimated; not fully confirmed in literature)
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₅H₂₀N₂O₂
- **Molecular weight:** 260.33 g/mol
- **SMILES:** CCN(CC)CCC1=CNC2=C1C=C(C=C2)OC(=O)C
- **Appearance:** White to off-white crystalline powder
- **Solubility:** Soluble in ethanol, slightly soluble in water; commonly
  dissolved in propylene glycol for liquid dosing

## Pharmacology

- **Prodrug mechanism:** 4-AcO-MET is believed to be a prodrug for 4-HO-MET,
  undergoing deacetylation by esterases in the body; some evidence suggests it
  may also have direct pharmacological activity before conversion
- **5-HT₂A receptor agonism:** Primary mechanism of psychedelic action; partial
  agonist at the [[serotonin]] 5-HT₂A receptor, producing altered perception,
  synesthesia, and [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]] at sufficient doses
- **5-HT₁A receptor:** Possible weak agonist activity, contributing to anxiolytic
  and mood-elevating effects
- **TAAR1 receptor:** Minimal activity expected at the trace amine-associated
  receptor 1, unlike amphetamine-class compounds
- **Relative potency:** Approximately 1.5–2× less potent than 4-AcO-DMT by
  weight; slightly more potent than 4-HO-MET due to prodrug efficiency

## Dosage and Administration

- **Common routes:** Oral, insufflated (intranasal); oral is most common
- **Threshold (oral):** 2–4 mg
- **Light dose (oral):** 5–8 mg
- **Common dose (oral):** 10–15 mg
- **Strong dose (oral):** 16–25 mg
- **Heavy dose (oral):** 25+ mg (high risk of overwhelming experiences)
- **Intranasal:** Approximately 1.5× potency of oral; onset 5–10 minutes,
  shorter duration (3–5 hours)
- **Oral onset:** 15–30 minutes
- **Peak:** 1.5–3 hours after oral ingestion
- **Duration:** 4–6 hours oral; 3–5 hours intranasal
- **After effects:** Mild afterglow or residual visual smoothing for 1–3 hours
  following primary effects

## Effects

- **Visual:** Vivid color enhancement, saturation intensification, flowing
  geometric patterns, texture breathing, pattern recognition enhancement,
  tracers, and closed-eye imagery described as more colorful and fluid than
  4-AcO-DMT
