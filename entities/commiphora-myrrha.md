## Distribution and Habitat

*Commiphora myrrha* is native to the Horn of Africa (Somalia,
Ethiopia, Eritrea), eastern Sudan, Kenya, and the Arabian
Peninsula (Yemen, Oman, Saudi Arabia).

It grows in arid and semi-arid environments on rocky slopes,
dry savanna, and desert margins at 200–1,500 m elevation.
The species is highly drought-tolerant, surviving on 150–300
mm of annual rainfall.
