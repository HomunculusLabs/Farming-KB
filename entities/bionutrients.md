# Bionutrients

## Description

Bionutrients are biological nutrients produced through the fermentation of plant and animal materials, used in natural farming systems to feed both plants and beneficial microorganisms. Unlike synthetic fertilizers or simple compost teas, bionutrients are created through controlled fermentation with sugar or molasses, which breaks down raw materials biologically and makes nutrients more bioavailable. Bionutrients serve a dual purpose: they provide direct nutrition to plants and animals while simultaneously feeding beneficial indigenous microorganisms to encourage their proliferation.

## Classification

- **Category:** Agricultural input / Fermented extract
- **Origin:** Korean Natural Farming / Japanese Natural Farming
- **Process:** Fermentation with crude sugar or molasses (1/3 ratio)
- **Function:** Plant nutrition, animal nutrition, microbial food source

## Key Facts

- Bionutrients are created through fermentation, which is superior to simple extraction (like boiling/infusion) because microorganisms break down materials more thoroughly.
- The basic formula: 1/3 crude sugar or molasses mixed with materials to be fermented, with 50-75% air gap, fermented for 7+ days.
- Fermentation produces enzymes, improves nutrition, and makes nutrients more bioavailable — analogous to how yogurt is more nutritious than plain milk and kimchi more nutritious than raw vegetables.
- Bionutrients are commonly mixed with Beneficial Indigenous Microorganisms (BIM) for enhanced effectiveness.
- Specific bionutrient preparations target different growth stages: growth promotants (kangkong, kelp), flowering/fruiting (banana-squash-papaya), high nitrogen (fish amino acid), and calcium supplementation (calcium phosphate from eggshells).
- The part of the plant matters: seed extracts are most concentrated (dilution 1:1000), followed by fruit (1:500), then leaf.
- Bionutrients can be added to animal drinking water and feeds, compost piles, or sprayed/watered onto plant leaves and roots.
- They also serve as food for beneficial microorganisms, encouraging their population growth when co-applied.

## Types of Bionutrients

1. **Growth promotants:** Kangkong (water spinach), kelp, mugwort — rich in natural hormones (gibberellins, auxins, cytokinins).
2. **Flowering/fruiting:** Banana-squash-papaya (BSP) extract — high in potassium and beta-carotene.
3. **High nitrogen:** Fish amino acid — from fermented fish scraps, high protein content.
4. **Stage-specific:** Calcium phosphate — for the changeover period (vegetative → reproductive).
5. **Natural antibiotics:** Ginger-garlic extract — antifungal and antibacterial properties.
6. **General purpose:** Fermented plant juice (FPJ) from any healthy plant material.
