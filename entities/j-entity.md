# J (BDB; 2-Amino-1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)butane)

J, also known as BDB, is a phenethylamine compound and the alpha-ethyl homologue of [[mda]]. It is the primary member of the "Classic Ladies" series of MDA homologues and the direct parent compound of [[mbdb-entity]] (Methyl-J). The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #94.

## Chemical Classification

J belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 2-amino-1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)butane.

The code name BDB derives from the IUPAC nomenclature using 1,3-benzodioxole: 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-butanamine.

J is the alpha-ethyl analogue of [[mda]] (which has an alpha-methyl group), making it one carbon longer on the carbon chain adjacent to the amine.

The N-methyl homologue of J is [[mbdb-entity]], also known as METHYL-J or EDEN.

## Pharmacological Profile

J is an active psychoactive compound with a dosage range of 150 to 230 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 4 to 8 hours.

The compound is generally described as more "stoning" than [[mdma-entity]], more similar in character to [[mda]], but with a similar chronology of effects.

All subjects who explored J accepted it and commented favorably.

## Qualitative Effects

At 175 mg, the first effects are evident within 30 minutes as pleasant feelings without untoward body effects.

The experience reaches a plus two level and levels off there within an hour.

There are no visual distortions, no giddiness, and no significant introspective urges.

The experience does not rise to a full psychedelic intoxication.

After about 90 minutes at this level, the effects gradually decline over two hours, followed by fatigue and languor.

At 200 mg with a 75 mg supplement, subjects reported a very strong climb with a good interior feeling described as having some [[mdma-entity]] properties.

Difficulty concentrating on any single point was noted, with a tendency to slide off.

Excellent emotional affect was reported, with music described as fine but not gripping.

A phenomenon described as "mental nystagmus" was noted.

The supplement at the 2-hour point was noticed within about 30 minutes.

At 230 mg, physically there was some dry mouth but no teeth clenching, some nystagmus, slight dizziness, anorexia, and it is not a decongestant.

Mentally the experience was described as extremely benign and pleasant, funny and good-humored with no visuals.

Easy silences and easy talking were noted.

The consensus was that J is more stoning than [[mdma-entity]].

## Synthesis

The synthesis of J begins with the Grignard reagent of propyl bromide reacted with piperonal.

The Grignard addition produces 1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-2-butanol, which is dehydrated to the corresponding butene.

The olefin is oxidized to the ketone 1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-2-butanone using performic acid (hydrogen peroxide and formic acid).

The ketone is reductively aminated with ammonium acetate and sodium cyanoborohydride to produce J.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 159-161 degrees C.

## The Classic Ladies Series

J is one of the ten "Classic Ladies," the ten possible alpha-homologues of [[mda]] that Shulgin discussed beginning with the [[ariadne-entity]] entry.

Each Lady represents a different length alpha-alkyl chain on the MDA skeleton.

The series explores how changing the alpha-substituent from methyl through various alkyl chain lengths affects psychoactivity.

## Nomenclature

Two nomenclature systems exist for naming J and related compounds.

The traditional system calls the methylenedioxy ring a substituent on phenyl, giving 2-amino-1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)butane.

The modern Chemical Abstracts system uses 1,3-benzodioxole as the parent, giving 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-butanamine, from which the code BDB is derived.

The "J" code name follows Shulgin's alphabetical naming system for MDA homologues.

## Relationship to MDMA and MBDB

J is the alpha-ethyl analogue of [[mda]], while [[mdma-entity]] is the N-methyl derivative of MDA.

[[mbdb-entity]] (METHYL-J) is the N-methyl derivative of J, combining both the alpha-ethyl chain extension and the N-methylation.

J thus occupies a unique position between MDA and MBDB in structural terms.

The pharmacological properties of J are intermediate in some ways, being more stoning than MDMA but with similar timing.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 150 - 230 mg.

Duration: 4 - 8 h.

Supplementation appears effective, mimicking the common [[mdma-entity]] procedure.
