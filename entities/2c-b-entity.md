# 2C-B (Nexus, Venus, Bromo)

**Chemical name:** 4-Bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine

**PiHKAL #20** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +++

## Overview

2C-B is one of the most widely known and used members of the [[2c-family-compounds-overview]] of psychedelic phenethylamines. First synthesized by [[alexander-shulgin]], it gained popularity as a recreational substance in the 1980s and 1990s. It is valued for its combination of visual effects, empathogenic qualities, and relatively manageable duration compared to classical psychedelics.

## Qualitative Effects

- Moderate to strong visual effects: color enhancement, pattern recognition, geometric patterns with eyes closed
- Empathogenic qualities similar to mdma-entity|MDMA but less pronounced
- Increased tactile sensitivity and sensory awareness
- Mild stimulant properties
- Euphoria with emotional openness
- Less introspective than LSD or psilocybin
- Body load can include stomach discomfort, mild nausea at higher doses
- Visuals described as "plastic" or "digital" in quality compared to other psychedelics

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

From PiHKAL qualitative reports at various dosage levels, 2C-B produces a distinctive experience combining visual richness with emotional lightness. At common doses (20-30 mg), users report pleasant color enhancement, moderate visual patterning, and a feeling of well-being. At higher doses, the visual component becomes dominant. The compound is noted for its relatively smooth onset and comedown compared to other psychedelics.

## Three Key Characteristics

Shulgin identified three defining features of 2C-B from accumulated reports:

1. **Steep dose-response curve**: Over the 12–24 mg range, every 2 mg can make a profound increase or change of response. Initial exploration should proceed in small increments.
2. **Shortest duration of major psychedelics**: Wherever you might be, "hang on — in an hour or so you will be approaching familiar territory again." This brevity is a significant practical advantage.
3. **Aphrodisiac potential**: Shulgin wrote that "if there is anything ever found to be an effective aphrodisiac, it will probably be patterned after 2C-B in structure."

## "Museum Level" Dosing

A commonly used term in the 2C-B community for a just-perceptible effect is "museum level" — a slightly-over-threshold dose that allows public activities (such as viewing paintings in a museum or scenery watching as a passenger in a car) without attracting attention. Considerable discomfort can accompany being in public at higher doses.

## Overdose Reports

Shulgin documented two overdose reports that "challenge" (in the classic sense) the compound's safety profile:

**64 mg — intentional (physician):** After finding only mild effects at 20 mg, the physician took the remaining 44 mg. He was "propelled into something not of my choosing." Everything alive became "completely fearsome" — even a bush outside the window was terrifying as an uncontrollable life-form. He assured Shulgin there were no lasting neurological concerns.

**100 mg — accidental (psychologist):** After picking up the wrong vial, the psychologist faced what they believed was imminent death: "A person may believe that he has prepared himself for his own death, but when the moment comes, he is completely alone, and totally unprepared." Two hours later the experience became "really marvelous." They resolved to continue using psychedelics as a probe into the unknown.

## MDMA + 2C-B Combination

Many reports over the years have mentioned combining mdma-entity|MDMA and 2C-B. The most successful approach is sequential rather than concurrent: the optimal time for 2C-B is at, or just before, the final baseline recovery of the MDMA experience. Shulgin described this as if "the mental and emotional discoveries [of MDMA] can be mobilized, and something done about them." This combination has enthusiastic advocates in the psychotherapy world. See [[psychedelic-therapy-set-and-setting]].

## Salt Forms

2C-B forms multiple crystalline salts with distinct properties:

| Salt Form | Melting Point |
|-----------|---------------|
| Hydrochloride (HCl) | 237–239 °C (with decomposition) |
| Hydrobromide (HBr) | 214.5–215 °C |
| Acetate | 208–209 °C |

When too much water is present during hydrochloride formation, a hydrated form is obtained instead. These melting points serve as useful identification markers. See [[phenethylamine-pharmacology]] for salt-form considerations.

## Extensions and Commentary

2C-B is part of the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamine family. The bromine atom at the 4-position is a key pharmacophore. In the halogen series (2C-C, 2C-B, 2C-I), 2C-B occupies a middle position in both potency and character.

### The Tweetios

Two "Tweetios" (ethoxy homologues) of 2C-B are known:

- **2CB-2ETO** (4-bromo-2-ethoxy-5-methoxyphenethylamine) — active at ~15 mg, with higher doses merely prolonging activity from ~3 hours to perhaps 6 hours, without increasing intensity
- **2CB-2,5-DIETO** (4-bromo-2,5-diethoxyphenethylamine) — only restless sleep and strange dreams at 55 mg; active level not yet known

## Safety Warnings

- Dose-dependent increase in physical discomfort at higher ranges
- Can cause nausea, particularly during onset
- Combining with other substances (especially stimulants or MAOIs) increases risk
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Scheduled in many countries (Schedule I in the US)
