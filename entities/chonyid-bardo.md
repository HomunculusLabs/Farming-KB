## Chonyid Bardo

## Description

The Chonyid Bardo is the second of the three bardo phases described in the [[psychedelic-experience-tibetan-book-of-the-dead]] the Dead ([[bardo-thodol]]). Known as the "Bardo of the Experiencing of Reality," it is the phase in which the departed consciousness encounters vivid visionary experiences — what the Tibetans call "karmic apparitions" and what modern [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-vs-psychedelic-terminology-debate]] describes as hallucinations. In this phase, the self partially reconstitutes and encounters external game reality in "sharp exquisite clarity" or in the form of powerful visual, auditory, and somatic phenomena. For most persons undergoing a [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]], this is the longest phase.

## Key Facts

- The Chonyid Bardo is characterized by the appearance of visions, hallucinations, and intense sensory phenomena. In the Tibetan tradition, these include [[leary-peaceful-and-wrathful-visions]] deities, mandalas, and symbolic imagery representing the contents of the consciousness.
- In the psychedelic context, this manifests as visual geometry, color intensification, synesthesia, ego-dissolution with residual self-awareness, emotional amplification, and encounters with archetypal imagery.
- The Bardo Thodol instructs that these visions are projections of one's own mind — "Whether you experience heaven or hell, remember that it is your mind which creates them."
- The key instruction during Chonyid Bardo is to avoid grasping at pleasant visions or fleeing from frightening ones. Attachment or aversion to the visions leads to deeper entanglement in the bardo.
- Leary describes this as the "aesthetic or hallucinatory" stage, noting it is the longest for most persons, while the initiated may maintain the first bardo (Chikhai) longer.

## Relevance to Psychedelic Studies

The Chonyid Bardo [[maps]] onto the middle and most visually intense portion of an LSD or psilocybin experience. Understanding this phase helps guides and facilitators prepare subjects for visionary content and coach them through difficult encounters. The instruction to recognize visions as self-generated is a core principle of [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] and integration work. Modern frameworks like the "challenging experience" protocols used by organizations such as MAPS and Zendo Project echo these ancient instructions.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

[[psilocybin-mushroom]] experiences at moderate doses (2–5 grams dried [[psilocybe-cubensis]]) typically involve an extended Chonyid Bardo phase with vivid closed-eye and open-eye visuals. [[cultivator-strain-selection-and-stock-culture]] potency affect the intensity of this phase. Facilitators should be prepared to guide subjects through this visionary period using bardo-informed techniques: reminding them that visions are self-generated and encouraging surrender rather than resistance.
