## Taxonomy and Morphology

Wahlenbergia albomarginata was described by Joseph Dalton Hooker in 1864 from specimens collected during New Zealand botanical surveys. The specific epithet "albomarginata" refers to the distinctive white, membranous margins of the calyx lobes.
