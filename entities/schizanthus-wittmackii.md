## Overview

Schizanthus wittmackii is a species of flowering annual plant in the family Solanaceae, native to Chile. It belongs to the genus Schizanthus, commonly known as butterfly flower or poor man's orchid, renowned for its showy, irregularly shaped blossoms.

The species produces clusters of bicolor flowers with deeply divided, orchid-like petals that resemble butterflies in flight. The upper petals are typically pink, lavender, or white, with contrasting yellow or orange markings on the lower lip.

Schizanthus wittmackii is cultivated as a cool-season annual in temperate gardens and as a spring-flowering pot plant. The genus Schizanthus is one of the most distinctive members of Solanaceae due to its zygomorphic flower morphology.
