# Proscaline

**Chemical name:** 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propoxyphenethylamine
**PiHKAL entry:** #140

## Overview

Proscaline (P) is a mescaline-entity|mescaline analogue where the 4-position methoxy group is replaced by a propoxy (three-carbon) chain. It occupies an interesting position in the mescaline homologue series as the most potent of the simple alkoxy-substituted phenethylamines, with an effective range starting around 30 mg.

The compound was evaluated both by [[alexander-shulgin]]'s group and independently by Czechoslovakian researchers who explored doses up to 80 mg. The name follows the pattern established by escaline-entity|escaline (mescaline -> escaline -> proscaline).

## Dosage

| Level | Amount (mg) |
|-------|-------------|
| Threshold | ~30 |
| Light | 30 - 40 |
| Common | 40 - 50 |
| Strong | 50 - 60 |
| Maximum (Czech research) | up to 80 |

## Duration

8 - 12 hours. Czechoslovakian research reports residual effects still apparent after 12 hours at higher doses. Sleep was not possible until after the 12th hour at 60 mg.

## Effects

The qualitative reports paint a picture of a gentle, euphoric compound:

- **At 30 mg:** Dulled sense of pain with sharpened other senses. Everything felt "really soft, and clean and clear." Tactile sensitivity greatly enhanced -- "could feel every hair my hand was touching." Deep relaxation. "Under the appropriate circumstances, this material would lead to uninhibited eroticism."
- **At 35 mg:** Very quiet experience. No nystagmus, no anorexia, insignificant closed-eye visuals. Restlessness with slight tremor for the first couple of hours, then drowsiness. "Would I do this again? Probably not. It doesn't seem to offer anything except speculation about the nature of the high."
- **At 40 mg:** Deep feelings of peace and contentment. Euphoria growing in intensity for several hours. "One of the most enjoyable experiences I have ever had." Excellent for socializing. However, "no enhanced clarity and no deep realizations" -- no motivation for serious discussion.
- **At 60 mg:** Intoxication complete in a couple of hours. More physical than mental effect with considerable irritability. "This should probably be the maximum dose." Thinking remained straight despite feeling "quite drunk." Effects waning by the 5th hour.

## Character Assessment

Proscaline's profile can be summarized as:

- **Primarily euphoric** rather than visionary or insightful
- **Gentle body feel** at lower doses, increasingly physical at higher doses
- **Social enhancer** at moderate doses -- good for talking and joking
- **Limited depth** -- "no dark corners" but also no deep realizations
- **Not strongly visual** -- eyes-closed imagery is minimal
- **Individual variation** is significant, from "most enjoyable experience" to "wouldn't do it again"

## Historical Research

A very early report from the Czechoslovakian literature describes experiments at up to 80 mg, noting difficulty with dreams and residual effects persisting beyond 12 hours. This predates Shulgin's own bioassays and represents some of the earliest systematic human research on mescaline homologues.

## Synthesis Notes

The synthesis parallels that of escaline-entity|escaline and buscaline-entity|Buscaline:

1. **Starting material:** Homosyringonitrile (3,5-dimethoxy-4-hydroxyphenylacetonitrile), prepared as described under Escaline.
2. **Alkylation:** Treatment with n-propyl bromide, decyltriethylammonium iodide (phase-transfer catalyst), and anhydrous K2CO3 in acetone under reflux for 10+ hours.
3. **Reduction:** The nitrile is reduced using sodium borohydride/trifluoroacetic acid to give the amine hydrochloride (mp 170-172 deg C). Catalytic hydrogenation (Pd/C, H2) also succeeds.
4. **Alternative route:** From syringaldehyde, via propylation, nitrostyrene formation, and LAH reduction.

## Position in the Mescaline Homologue Series

| Compound | 4-position | Dosage (mg) | Character |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| mescaline-entity|Mescaline | OCH3 | 200-400 | Classic, visual |
| escaline-entity|Escaline | OC2H5 | 40-60 | Analytical, physical |
| Proscaline | OC3H7 | 30-60 | Euphoric, gentle |
| buscaline-entity|Buscaline | OC4H9 | >150 | Inactive |

Proscaline represents the peak of the potency trend -- the propoxy chain is optimal for activity, and extending further to butoxy (Buscaline) causes activity to drop off sharply.

## Significance

Proscaline demonstrates that within the mescaline homologue series, the propoxy substitution at the 4-position represents a "sweet spot" for potency while producing a characteristically different experience from mescaline itself -- more euphoric and social, less visionary. The Czechoslovakian research adds historical depth to its pharmacological profile.
