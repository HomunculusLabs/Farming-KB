# Bergey's Manual of Bacteriology

## Description

Bergey's Manual of Determinative Bacteriology is the definitive reference work for bacterial taxonomy and identification. It provides detailed descriptions of bacterial species including their habitat requirements, metabolic characteristics, and environmental conditions that favor their growth.

## Classification

- **Type:** Scientific reference manual / taxonomic database
- **Subject:** Bacterial systematics and identification
- **Organization:** Bergey's Manual Trust
- **First Published:** 1923

## Key Facts

- Organized alphabetically by bacterial genus across multiple volumes
- Contains detailed habitat descriptions for each bacterial species
- Used by soil biologists to understand environmental conditions that select for specific bacterial species
- Referenced by Dr. [[elaine-ingham]] as the resource for understanding pathogenic Streptococcus habitat requirements
- Provides foundation for understanding why certain disease organisms thrive in degraded soil conditions

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

For cultivators, Bergey's Manual provides the scientific basis for understanding pathogen ecology. By looking up disease organisms, one can identify the exact environmental conditions that allow pathogens to outcompete beneficial organisms. This directly informs [[compost]] tea design and soil [[forest-management-strategies]].

## Relationships

- Reference for understanding Streptococcus pathogen ecology in soil
- Supports [[soil-food-web]] approach by enabling targeted environmental management
- Connected to plate count methodology as a taxonomic foundation
