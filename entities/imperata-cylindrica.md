## Imperata cylindrica

## Overview

Imperata cylindrica, commonly known as cogongrass, is a perennial rhizomatous grass in the family Poaceae. It is native to tropical and subtropical regions of Africa, Asia, and Australia, and is widely recognized as one of the world's most problematic invasive plant species.

Cogongrass forms dense monocultures that suppress native vegetation, alter fire regimes, and reduce biodiversity. Its extensive rhizome system makes eradication extremely difficult once established.

The species is economically important in parts of its native range, where it is used for thatching, [[dighton-fungal-drought-tolerance-plant-water-relations]] and resistance to mechanical removal. Each rhizome segment can produce a new shoot, making fragmentation a potent dispersal mechanism.

Spikelets are paired, 3 to 5 millimeters long, with a silky white callus hair base. Florets are wind-pollinated, and a single inflorescence can produce hundreds of viable seeds.

Seed viability varies significantly between cytotypes. The hexaploid Asian form produces moderate amounts of viable seed, while the African diploid relies more heavily on vegetative spread through rhizome fragmentation.

Ground-nesting birds, small mammals, and insects lose food sources and cover. Monotypic cogongrass stands support dramatically lower arthropod diversity than native grasslands.

In forestry, cogongrass competes aggressively with planted pine seedlings, reducing survival and growth rates. Infestations increase establishment costs and extend the time required for canopy closure.

Pine plantation losses in the southeastern United States are estimated in the millions of dollars annually. Chemical site preparation before planting is often necessary in cogongrass-infested areas, adding substantially to reforestation costs.

## Cultivation and Uses

In parts of Southeast Asia, particularly the Philippines and Indonesia, Imperata cylindrica is intentionally cultivated for thatch. The leaves are harvested, dried, and woven into roofing material that is durable, waterproof, and insect-resistant.
