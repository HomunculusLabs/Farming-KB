## Compost Preparation with Rye Straw

1. Fresh stable manure containing rye straw should be piled **no more than 4 feet high**.
2. The pile must be kept **thoroughly moist** throughout the fermentation process.
3. **Turn the pile** at intervals of 3-5 days, typically requiring 3-4 turnings over about 3 weeks.
4. Target temperature during early fermentation: **140F to 150F**.
5. Signs of readiness: loss of objectionable odor, **oleaginous feel**, **pliability**, and **uniform brown color** of the straw.
6. Final temperature decline to approximately **120F-130F** before spawning.
