# Bentonite

## Description

Bentonite is a naturally occurring absorbent aluminium phyllosilicate clay composed primarily of montmorillonite. In [[desert-gardening]]:

- **Non-wetting sands**: Many desert soils are hydrophobic sands that repel water rather than absorbing it. Bentonite breaks this water repellence by creating zones of water attraction
- **Rapid drainage**: Sandy desert soils drain too quickly for crop roots to access moisture. Bentonite creates micro-reservoirs of water within the soil matrix
- **[[bacterial-biofilms-and-nutrient-retention]]**: By improving [[composting-systems]]
- **Potting mixes**: Added to lightweight potting media for water retention

## Management Notes

- Apply at rates of 1-5 kg per square metre of garden bed, depending on soil sandiness
- Best incorporated into soil rather than surface-applied
- Wet thoroughly after application to activate swelling
- Effects are long-lasting — a single application can improve soil for years
- Use sodium bentonite for maximum water retention; calcium bentonite if soil is already sodic
- Can be difficult to spread in windy conditions due to fine particle size — dampen slightly before spreading
