## Distribution and Habitat

Dodonaea viscosa has one of the widest natural distributions of any woody plant species, occurring on every inhabited continent.

It is found from sea level to over 2,000 m elevation, in climates ranging from arid semi-desert to subtropical and warm temperate.

The species colonizes a remarkable variety of habitats including coastal dunes, rocky hillsides, dry sclerophyll forest, and disturbed sites.

It is particularly common in Australia, where it occurs in every state and territory, and is a dominant component of many arid-zone shrub communities.

The plant shows excellent tolerance of drought, fire, salt spray, and poor soils including serpentine and lateritic substrates.
