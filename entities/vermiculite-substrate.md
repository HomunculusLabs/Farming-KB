## Description

Vermiculite is a naturally occurring mineral (hydrated laminar magnesium-aluminum-iron silicate) that expands dramatically when heated. In mushroom cultivation, specifically the PF TEK method, horticultural-grade vermiculite serves as the primary base material for the substrate. Psylocybe Fanaticus described vermiculite as the "magic" ingredient of the PF TEK, crediting it with transforming the technique from a simple brown rice method into a highly effective cultivation system.

## Classification

- **Category:** Mineral substrate component
- **Type:** Expanded hydrated silicate mineral
- **Grade:** Horticultural (medium grade preferred)
- **Availability:** Garden centers, hardware stores; described as "ubiquitous horticultural product" in the US and Netherlands
- **Role in PF TEK:** Base material and moisture reservoir

## Key Facts

- Vermiculite is the foundational component of the PF TEK substrate, used at a ratio of 1/2 to 2/3 cup per 1/2 pint jar.
- Two particle size types are distinguished in the PF TEK:
  - **Finer type:** Particles averaging ~1 mm across; holds more water; recommended
  - **Coarser type:** Particles averaging 4–5 mm, up to 8 mm; holds less water
- The finer type of vermiculite is recommended over the coarser type because it holds more water, which is critical for fruiting.
- Coarseness varies considerably among brands; there are regional differences in available products.
- When mycelium is grown with vermiculite, "the mycelial threads stretch across space," creating a superior physical structure compared to grain-only substrates where the mycelium "turns into a mass with little air space."
- The particle size directly affects water-holding capacity; if the formulation results in a wet or sloppy substrate, less water should be used.
- The PF TEK advises cultivators to "shop around and try to get both types to compare" and to "keep notes on formulas for replicating the substrate formula that fruits the best."

## Dual Function in PF TEK

Vermiculite serves two critical roles in the PF TEK:

1. **Substrate base:** Mixed with brown rice powder and water to form the nutrient-bearing growing medium. The vermiculite creates air spaces that allow extensive mycelial colonization.

2. **Contamination barrier:** A 1/2 to 3/4 inch layer of **dry vermiculite** is placed on top of the wet substrate. This layer:
   - Protects the wet substrate from airborne contaminants
   - Absorbs and regulates moisture transpiration and condensation
   - Acts as a filter during inoculation (spore solution must be delivered below this layer)
   - Was described as an original discovery by Psylocybe Fanaticus

## Relevance to Cultivation

Vermiculite is indispensable to the PF TEK and to many modified cultivation methods:

- **Structural role:** Creates a porous, airy substrate that promotes robust mycelial growth with extensive rhizomorphic networks.
- **Moisture management:** Excellent water retention provides a reservoir for the growing mycelium throughout the colonization and fruiting phases.
- **Contamination prevention:** The dry top layer eliminates the need for sophisticated sterile technique during inoculation.
- **Availability:** In regions where vermiculite is unavailable (e.g., parts of Italy), modified techniques substitute sand, perlite, whole grain brown rice, and rye, though these are not recommended as equivalent replacements.
- **Relationship to perlite:** While vermiculite is used in the substrate itself, perlite is used separately in the terrarium for humidification. Both are expanded minerals but serve different functions.

## Measurement and Handling

- For the standard PF TEK formula: 1/2–2/3 cup (medium grade)
- For maximum fruiting formula: 1/2 cup
- Particle size assessment can be done using a photo magnifier and millimeter ruler
- Freshly opened vermiculite should be clean and free of contaminants
- Some brands may contain residual moisture; adjust water content accordingly
