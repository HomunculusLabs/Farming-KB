# Scenedesmus

## Description

Scenedesmus is a genus of colonial green algae identified by Mollison as one of the four almost universal sewage lagoon algae. It forms colonies of 2, 4, or 8 cells arranged in flat or curved plates, and plays a critical role in photosynthetic oxygen production in biological wastewater treatment systems.

## Classification

- **Domain**: Eukaryota
- **Kingdom**: Plantae (Viridiplantae)
- **Division**: Chlorophyta
- **Class**: Chlorophyceae
- **Order**: Sphaeropleales
- **Family**: Scenedesmaceae
- **Genus**: Scenedesmus
- **Functional group**: Phytoplankton / colonial green alga

## Key Facts

- One of four universal sewage lagoon algae alongside Euglena, Chlamydomonas, and Chlorella
- Forms distinctive flat colonies of 4-8 cells in a row, sometimes with spines on terminal cells
- Non-motile — relies on water mixing and wind for distribution in ponds
- Highly efficient at nutrient uptake, particularly nitrogen and phosphorus
- Tolerates a wide range of environmental conditions including variable pH and temperature
- Commonly found in freshwater ponds, lakes, and sewage treatment lagoons worldwide
- Reproduces primarily through autocolony formation, enabling rapid population growth
- Dense blooms can reach concentrations sufficient for commercial harvesting

## Role in Sewage Treatment

Scenedesmus contributes to wastewater treatment through several mechanisms:

1. **Photosynthetic oxygenation**: Produces dissolved oxygen that supports aerobic bacterial decomposition of organic matter
2. **Nutrient removal**: Efficiently absorbs dissolved nitrogen and phosphorus, reducing nutrient loads in effluent
3. **BOD reduction**: Converts soluble organic compounds into algal biomass, reducing biological oxygen demand
4. **Heavy metal biosorption**: Cell walls can adsorb trace metals from the water column
5. **Food web support**: Colonies are grazed by zooplankton including Daphnia, Moina, and various rotifers

## Relevance to Cultivation

Scenedesmus has significant applications in sustainable agriculture and permaculture:

- **Wastewater bioremediation**: A key organism in constructed wetlands and sewage lagoon systems
- **Algal protein production**: Can be harvested and processed as animal feed supplement (protein content 50-60% dry weight)
- **Biofertiliser**: Algal biomass is rich in nitrogen, phosphorus, and micronutrients
- **Aquaculture feed**: Supports zooplankton food chains that sustain farmed fish populations
- **Biofuel feedstock**: High lipid content makes it a candidate for biodiesel production
- **Industrial applications**: Used in waste-to-value systems converting sewage nutrients into marketable products

## Environmental Parameters

- **Optimal temperature**: 20-30 degrees C, with reduced growth below 10 degrees C
- **pH range**: 6.5-9.0 (optimal 7.5-8.5, matching aerobic pond conditions)
- **Light**: Requires adequate sunlight for photosynthesis; dense cultures may need mechanical mixing
- **Growth rate**: Can double biomass every 6-12 hours under optimal conditions
