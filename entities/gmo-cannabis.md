## GMO (Garlic Cookies)

## Overview

GMO, also known as "Garlic Cookies" or "[[gmo-cookies]]," is a highly potent indica-dominant [[girl-scout-cookies]] (GSC) with Chemdawg. Bred by Mamiko Seeds and popularized by Divine Genetics, it has become one of the most sought-after strains of the 2020s for its exceptionally high [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]], unique savory pungent aroma, and deeply relaxing body effects. The "GMO" name stands for "Garlic, Mushroom, Onion" — a reference to its complex umami-forward [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]]; indica body effects |
| **Chemdawg** | Balanced hybrid (Nigerian × Thai landrace × Chemdog lineage) | Diesel, chemical pungency; potency; complex terpene depth |

- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 90% indica / 10% sativa
- **Breeder:** Mamiko Seeds (original); Divine Genetics (popularized clone-only cut)
- **Notable descendants:** GMO x [[purple-punch]], GMO x Sherb BX, Jeez-O-GMO

## Appearance

- **[[green-cannabis-plant-anatomy-calyx-pistil-bud-structure]]:** Dense, chunky, and tightly packed indica-typical nugs
- **Color:** Dark [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] to olive, with occasional deep purple hues, especially in cooler growing conditions
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptionally heavy; thick, frosty layer of milky-white trichomes
- **Pistils:** Abundant orange to amber pistils woven throughout the dense calyxes
- **Visual impression:** Strikingly resinous and potent-looking; often described as "sticky" or "wet" in appearance

## Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma
- **Primary:** Pungent garlic and onion — the defining characteristic; immediately noticeable and distinctive
- **Secondary:** Diesel, musky earth, and mushroom-like umami notes
- **Background:** Hints of sweet cookie dough and coffee on the back end
- **Intensity:** Extremely strong and lingering; easily detected through sealed bags

### Flavor
- **Inhalation:** Savory garlic and diesel, with a sharp peppery spice
- **Exhalation:** Smooth earthy and slightly sweet cookie flavor emerges
- **Aftertaste:** Long-lasting garlic and mushroom umami; coats the palate
- The flavor is polarizing — deeply loved by enthusiasts, challenging for those expecting sweet or fruity profiles

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Range | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------|
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.5–1.2% | Dominant; spicy, peppery; binds CB2 receptors |
| **Limonene** | 0.3–0.8% | Citrus undertone; mood elevation, stress relief |
| **Myrcene** | 0.4–1.0% | Herbal, musky; sedative properties, "couch-lock" contributor |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.4% | Earthy, woody; anti-inflammatory |
| **Pinene** | Trace | Subtle pine; counteracts some sedative effects |

The caryophyllene-dominant profile is unusual and directly responsible for the garlic/onion/pungent character.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Range | Notes |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| **THC** | 25–35% | Extremely high; commonly tests above 30% in well-grown samples |
| **CBD** | <0.1% | Negligible |
| **CBG** | 0.1–0.5% | Present in minor amounts |

## Effects

### Onset and Progression
- **Onset:** Rapid, typically felt within 5–10 minutes of inhalation
- **Early phase (0–30 min):** Cerebral euphoria, mood elevation, and mild sensory enhancement
- **Peak (30–90 min):** Deeply relaxing body high begins to dominate; physical tension melts away
- **Late phase (1–3 hours):** Heavy sedation and "couch-lock"; strong urge to recline or sleep
- **Total duration:** 2–4 hours depending on dose and individual tolerance
