The inflorescence is a terminal spike, 10–30 cm long, bearing numerous star-shaped flowers. Each flower is 10–15 mm in diameter with five white petals and a yellow-green center.

Petals are narrowly ovate with a slightly notched apex. The flowers open sequentially from the base of the spike upward, extending the bloom period over several weeks.

The calyx is green with five triangular lobes that persist after petal fall. The fruit is a small spherical capsule containing several seeds.
