## Closed Recycling Irrigation System

## Description

Fully enclosed growing environments — typically glasshouses or plastic tunnel
systems — where water losses are minimized to near-zero through condensation
capture and recirculation. Water transpired by plants condenses on the inside
surfaces of the enclosure and flows back to the root zone, creating a nearly
closed water cycle. The most water-efficient growing approach available.

## Classification

- **Category**: Enclosed growing system
- **Domain**: Intensive horticulture, [[permaculture-design]].
Most appropriate for Zone 1 (home garden) or specialized high-value crop
production where water is extremely scarce. Particularly relevant where only
salt water is available, as the system can distill fresh water passively.
