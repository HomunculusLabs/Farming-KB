## Medicinal Properties

- **Sedative and anxiolytic:** Hops pillows (stuffed with dried cones) are a
  traditional European remedy for insomnia and anxiety; clinical evidence supports
  mild sedative effects, possibly via 2-methyl-3-buten-2-ol (a degradation product
  of humulone) and modulation of GABA_A receptors
- **Estrogenic activity:** 8-prenylnaringenin is one of the most potent known
  phytoestrogens; historically used to manage menopausal symptoms; current evidence
  is mixed regarding clinical significance
- **Digestive bitter:** Stimulates gastric acid secretion and appetite; used in
  bitters formulations for dyspepsia and indigestion
- **Antimicrobial:** Alpha and beta acids inhibit growth of bacteria and some
  fungi; basis for beer preservation and topical antimicrobial applications
