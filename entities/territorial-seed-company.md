# Territorial Seed Company

## Description

Territorial Seed Company is a seed supplier specializing in varieties suitable for winter gardening and cool-season production. Located at PO Box 158, Cottage Grove, OR 97424. Contact: (800) 626-0866, website www.territorialseed.com. The company is notable for publishing a supplementary catalog in June specifically focused on winter gardening — a unique offering among seed companies.

## Classification

- **Entity Type:** Company / Seed Supplier
- **Industry:** Agriculture, Horticulture, Seed Production
- **Location:** Cottage Grove, Oregon, USA
- **Specialization:** Winter gardening varieties, cool-season crops

## Products and Services

### Winter Gardening Focus
Territorial Seed Company stands out among seed suppliers for its dedicated winter gardening catalog:

- **Supplementary winter catalog** published annually in June
- Curated selection of varieties specifically chosen for winter harvest performance
- Varieties tested for cold tolerance, low-light adaptation, and protected-culture suitability
- One of the few seed companies to offer a dedicated winter planting resource

### Seed Varieties
While the full catalog includes standard spring and summer varieties, Territorial's winter-focused offerings make them particularly valuable for:

- Fall-planted crops for winter harvest
- Overwintering varieties that survive and produce through cold months
- Cold-tolerant leafy greens and root crops
- Protected-culture varieties for greenhouse and [[high-tunnel]] production

## Key Facts

- Publishes a supplementary winter gardening catalog in June — timed for fall planting decisions
- Located in Oregon's Willamette Valley, a major seed-producing region with a climate well-suited to trialing winter varieties
- Recognized by [[eliot-coleman]] as one of the key seed sources for winter harvest growers
- Focuses on varieties for shorter growing seasons and cooler climates

## Role in Winter Harvest Farming

Territorial Seed Company complements other seed suppliers in the [[winter-harvest-system]]:

1. **Timing:** Their June catalog publication aligns with the fall planting calendar that is critical for winter harvest success
2. **Variety Expertise:** Their Pacific Northwest location provides natural conditions for testing cold- and damp-tolerant varieties
3. **Supplementary Role:** Alongside Johnny's Selected Seeds and Fedco Seeds, Territorial expands the pool of available winter-adapted genetics

## Relevance to Cultivation

- **Season Extension Support:** Their winter catalog makes it easier for growers to identify and source appropriate varieties for four-season production
- **Regional Adaptation:** Oregon's maritime climate shares characteristics with other cool-season growing regions, making their variety recommendations broadly applicable
- **Knowledge Resource:** The winter catalog serves as both a purchasing guide and an educational resource for growers new to winter harvesting
- **Genetic Diversity:** Access to multiple seed suppliers with different variety selections increases the genetic diversity available to winter growers
