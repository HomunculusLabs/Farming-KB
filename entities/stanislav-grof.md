# Stanislav Grof

Stanislav Grof (born 1931) is a Czech-born American psychiatrist, one of the founding figures of transpersonal psychology, and a pioneer of [[psychedelic-experience-stages]] for his experiential mapping.

## Biography

- Born July 1, 1931, in Prague, Czechoslovakia
- MD from Charles University in Prague (1957)
- Early research at the Psychiatric Research Institute in Prague using LSD
- Emigrated to the [[metzner-psycholytic-psychedelic-therapy-models]] sessions than any other researcher, working with thousands of patients from the late 1950s through the mid-1970s. His [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-legal-prohibition-impact-uk-netherlands]] of LSD in the mid-1970s, Grof and his wife Christina developed Holotropic Breathwork as a non-drug method for accessing non-ordinary states of consciousness. The technique involves:

- Accelerated breathing combined with evocative music
- Bodywork to release physical tension and emotional blockages
- Mandala drawing to integrate the experience afterward
- Facilitated group sessions with trained guides

Holotropic Breathwork is designed to produce states similar to those encountered in psychedelic sessions, including perinatal and transpersonal experiences.

## Transpersonal Psychology

Grof, along with Abraham Maslow and Anthony Sutich, co-founded transpersonal psychology as a discipline that studies experiences extending beyond the boundaries of the individual ego, including:

- Mystical and spiritual experiences
- Archetypal phenomena and collective unconscious material
- Past-life experiences and karmic themes
- Experiences of cosmic unity and consciousness beyond the body
- Near-death experiences

## COEX Systems

Grof proposed the concept of COEX (Condensed Experience) Systems — constellations of memories from different life periods that share a similar emotional theme or physical sensation. COEX systems organize traumatic and meaningful experiences into clusters that can be accessed and resolved together during psychedelic therapy.
