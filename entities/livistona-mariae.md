## Distribution and Habitat

Livistona mariae is endemic to the MacDonnell Ranges and adjacent ranges in central Australia, Northern Territory. Its distribution is centered on the Finke Gorge National Park and the Palm Valley area.

Palm Valley contains an estimated 3,000 to 5,000 mature individuals, making it the largest and most iconic population. Smaller, scattered populations occur in nearby gorges including Ormiston Gorge and Standley Chasm.

## Cultural Significance

Livistona mariae holds profound cultural importance for the Western Arrernte Aboriginal people, who are the traditional custodians of the MacDonnell Ranges. The palm is central to several important Dreaming stories that explain its creation and distribution.

According to Arrernte tradition, the cabbage palm was created by ancestral beings during the Dreaming and planted in the gorges. These stories encode important ecological knowledge about the palm's distribution and habitat requirements.
