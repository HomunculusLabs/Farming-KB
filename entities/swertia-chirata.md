## Morphology

**Habit:** Erect, annual or biennial herb, 30–150 cm tall.

**Stem:** Simple or branched above, cylindrical, purplish at maturity.

**Leaves:** Opposite, lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate, 5–10 cm long,
with 3–5 prominent parallel veins. Upper leaves sessile, clasping.

**Flowers:** Terminal or axillary cymes. Corolla rotate, 2–3 cm,
4–5 deep blue petals with green nectary spots at base.

**Fruit:** Capsule, ellipsoid, 1–1.5 cm, splitting loculicidally,
releasing numerous small, winged seeds.
