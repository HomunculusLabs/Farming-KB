# MMDA-3a

MMDA-3a (2-Methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine) is a psychedelic amphetamine and one of the "Essential Amphetamines."

It is documented as entry #134 in PiHKAL by [[alexander-shulgin]].

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2-Methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine.

The MMDA naming convention: M for methoxy, MD for methylenedioxy, A for amphetamine.

The "3a" designation arises because there are two possible ways to arrange a methoxy and a methylenedioxy group on three adjacent positions of the aromatic ring.

The "a" isomer has the methoxy group at the 2-position and the methylenedioxy bridge at the 3,4-positions.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 154-155 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 20 - 80 mg.

Duration of effects: 10 - 16 hours.

The wide dosage range suggests considerable variation in individual response.

Higher doses (80-100 mg) have been compared to approximately 100 micrograms of LSD in both quantity and quality of effects.

The compound has significant stimulant properties that may persist after the psychedelic effects have subsided.

## Qualitative Effects

At 20 mg, effects include heightened awareness of the natural world, with users describing fascination with insects and a sense of time moving slowly.

The experience is gentle and contemplative.

At 40 mg, the experience develops between one and two hours with considerable body tremors.

Visual sparkle and enhancement are noted, but the effects "drop way too soon."

Residual physical hypersensitivity and light diarrhea may follow.

At 60 mg, soft visuals are present with eyes both open and closed.

Music appreciation is enhanced.

Some lasting stimulation is noted that does not interfere with sleep, though the following morning may still show residual effects.

The compound is described as "gentle" and a "good compound."

## Essential Amphetamine Classification

MMDA-3a qualifies as an "Essential Amphetamine" because its structure can theoretically arise from the amination in vivo of an essential oil.

The essential oil precursor is croweacin (1-allyl-2-methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxybenzene), which occurs in nature.

This classification connects MMDA-3a to Shulgin's concept that the human liver could potentially convert naturally occurring essential oils into psychoactive amphetamines.

In practice, croweacin is not a practical laboratory starting material for the synthesis of MMDA-3a.

## Synthesis

The synthesis begins with 2,3-dihydroxyanisole, which undergoes methylenedioxy bridge formation with methylene iodide and potassium carbonate in acetone.

This yields 2,3-methylenedioxyanisole as white crystals with a sharp spicy smell.

Vilsmeier formylation with POCl3 and N-methylformanilide introduces the aldehyde group.

GC analysis shows three products: the major 2-methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxybenzaldehyde (82%), the positional isomer 4-methoxy-2,3-methylenedioxybenzaldehyde (16%), and a trace of myristicinaldehyde (2%).

The major product is isolated by selective extraction with cyclohexane, yielding pale yellow crystals (mp 103-105 degrees C).

The minor isomer is used in the synthesis of MMDA-3b.

Condensation with nitroethane in acetic acid gives the nitropropene, which is reduced with lithium aluminum hydride to yield MMDA-3a.

## The 2-Carbon and 4-Carbon Analogues

Both the 2-carbon (phenethylamine) and 4-carbon (butanamine) analogues of MMDA-3a have been synthesized and bioassayed.

The phenethylamine analogue (2C-3a; 2-methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine) showed marginal effects at 40-120 mg.

At 40 mg, there was a hint of psychic energizer effects.

At 65 mg, pleasant mood elevation was noted.

At 80 mg, a brief paresthetic twinge was observed.

At 120 mg, similar twinges at one hour, then nothing.

The modest change in effect over a three-fold dosage range led Shulgin to suggest the compound might have merit as an antidepressant.

The 4-carbon analogue (4C-3a) has been tasted at up to 3.5 mg with nothing noted.

No trials at higher doses have been conducted.

## Clinical Reports

Shulgin reported being told of clinical trials at considerably higher doses than those in PiHKAL.

Three trials at 80 mg and one at 100 mg all made comparisons to approximately 100 micrograms of LSD.

However, two events may or may not have been related to these trials: one subject had a spontaneous peak experience five days after the experiment, and another made a symbolic suicide attempt.

These reports highlight the need for careful screening and follow-up in clinical psychedelic research.

## Legal Status

MMDA-3a is likely controlled as a methylenedioxyamphetamine derivative in most jurisdictions.

In the United States, the Federal Analogue Act would likely apply.

The compound has never been widely available outside of research settings.

## Harm Reduction

The wide dose range (20-80 mg) requires careful individual titration.

Reports of residual stimulation the day after dosing suggest users should plan for extended recovery.

The clinical reports of adverse events at higher doses underscore the importance of set, setting, and screening.

The compound has not been studied for long-term toxicity.
