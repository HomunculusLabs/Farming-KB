## Overview

**Vaccinium angustifolium**, commonly known as the lowbush blueberry or wild blueberry, is a low-growing deciduous shrub in the family Ericaceae. It is native to northeastern [[query-how-do-i-prune-brambles-for-maximum-fruit-production]].

Wild blueberry fields in Maine and eastern Canada are typically managed on glacial outwash soils with an organic matter content of 2–8%. These soils are naturally acidic and low in available phosphorus, conditions that favor *Vaccinium* over competing vegetation.
