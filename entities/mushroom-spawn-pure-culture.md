# Pure Culture Mushroom Spawn

## Description

Pure culture [[agaricus-campestris]]* (or related species). Developed around 1900 in both France and the United States, pure culture spawn revolutionized the mushroom industry by enabling reliable strain identification, consistent production, and the propagation of selected varieties.

## Classification

- **Type**: Cultivation technique / [[mushroom-cultivation-history]]:

1. **Scientific foundation**: Pure culture methods brought microbiological rigor to what had been an empirical, tradition-bound practice.
2. **Strain management**: For the first time, specific varieties could be named, characterized, and reliably reproduced -- enabling systematic improvement.
3. **Industry democratization**: Reliable spawn reduced the "mystery" of mushroom growing and opened the industry to new growers.
4. **Market expansion**: The dramatic increase in production following pure culture adoption demonstrates the technique's role in creating modern mushroom markets.
5. **American independence**: Pure culture freed American growers from dependence on imported European spawn, catalyzing domestic industry growth.
6. **Foundation for modern breeding**: Pure culture techniques laid the groundwork for all subsequent strain improvement and breeding programs.
