Ergine is a structural analog of LSD, with an amide group in place of
LSD's diethylamide substituent. It is approximately 10–30 times less
potent than LSD by weight, with generally milder, shorter-lasting, and
more sedative subjective effects.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name:** (6aR,9R)-N,6-dimethyl-9-((S)-oxan-2-yl)-7,8,9,10-
  tetrahydro-6H-6a,9-epimino[1,3]dioxolo[4,5-h]chromen-8-carboxamide
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₆H₁₇N₃O | **MW:** 267.33 g/mol
- **Structure type:** Ergoline (tetracyclic lysergamide)
- **Solubility:** Insoluble in water; soluble in ethanol, methanol, DMSO,
  and acidic aqueous solutions
- **CAS:** 478-94-4

Ergine is biosynthetically produced as the D-isomer in plants. It is
unstable to light, heat, and alkaline conditions, degrading via hydrolysis
of the amide bond.

## Pharmacology

- **Receptor activity:** Partial agonist at 5-HT₂A, 5-HT₁A, and 5-HT₂C;
  also binds dopamine D₂ and adrenergic receptors
- **Relative potency:** ~10–30× less potent than LSD at 5-HT₂A
- **Mechanism:** Classic psychedelic — primarily 5-HT₂A agonism in
  cortical pyramidal neurons; downstream effects on thalamic gating
  and [[psilocybin-default-mode-network-ego-dissolution]] network
- **Metabolism:** Hepatic CYP450; N-dealkylation and oxidative pathways;
  half-life ~3–4 hours
- **Alkaloid synergy:** Co-occurs with ergometrine, isoergine, and
  lysergol in plant seeds, contributing to overall profile

The pharmacological distinction from LSD is significant: additional
adrenergic receptor affinity contributes to ergine's characteristic
physical sedation and vasoconstriction effects.

## Effects

- **Onset:** 30–120 minutes (oral, plant seeds)
- **Duration:** 5–8 hours, residual effects up to 10–12 hours
- **Threshold dose:** 200–500 μg (pure); seeds vary significantly
  (0.05–0.6% ergine by weight)
- **Common dose:** 500 μg – 2 mg (pure); 50–300 seeds (*I. tricolor*),
  4–12 seeds (*A. nervosa*)
- **Visual effects:** Enhanced color, pattern recognition, geometric
  visuals at moderate doses; less intense than LSD
