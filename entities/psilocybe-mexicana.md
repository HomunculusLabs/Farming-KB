## Distribution and Habitat

*P. mexicana* is found in subtropical and tropical highland regions of Mexico, primarily in the states of Oaxaca,
Puebla, and Mexico State, at elevations of 1,000–1,800 meters. It grows in open, grassy areas, along trails, in
meadows, and on disturbed ground — often in rich, humus-laden soils. Fruiting occurs from May through October, during
the rainy season. The species has also been reported from Guatemala and other Central American locations. Unlike some
of its relatives, *P. mexicana* is not strongly associated with dung, instead fruiting from soil enriched with decaying
plant matter. The highland habitat is characterized by distinct wet and dry seasons, with the mushroom's fruiting
closely tied to the onset of summer rains.
