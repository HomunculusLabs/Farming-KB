## Overview

Meisn. ex Nees is an evergreen tree in the family Lauraceae, native to the Indian subcontinent and adjacent parts of [[comparison-nutrient-cycling-vs-nutrient-dense-gardening]] in montane forest ecosystems. The species plays a role in maintaining the structural complexity of multi-layered tropical forests.

The aromatic foliage contains essential oils that may deter browsing by large herbivores. The oil composition varies seasonally, with higher concentrations of monoterpenes during the dry season.
