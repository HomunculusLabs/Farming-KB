## Description

The "Stir-Fry Pak" is a value-added winter product developed by [[eliot-coleman]]'s [[four-season-farm]]. It is a 10-by-15-inch cellophane bag containing all the whole ingredients needed for a stir-fry dinner in a single convenient package. The product exemplifies creative direct-market strategies for winter greenhouse growers.

## Classification

- **Type**: Market product / Value-added package
- **Category**: Convenience vegetable pack
- **Format**: 10 inch by 15 inch cellophane bag
- **Market channel**: Direct market (farmers' market, farm stand)

## Key Facts

- Contains whole, unprocessed ingredients — not pre-cut
- Designed as a complete meal solution for customers
- Beautiful visual presentation with contrasting colors of root, stem, and leaf
- Sold out by mid-December in its trial year, before mature leafy greens received a true cold-weather test
- Customers appreciated finding all ingredients in a single bag
- The product was a market success based on presentation and convenience

## Ingredient List

1. One head of 'Mei Qing Choi' pak choi (baby pak choi)
2. One head of young tatsoi
3. One 'Shunkyo' semi-long pink radish
4. One 'Hakurei' turnip with greens attached
5. One 'Tadorna' extra-hardy leek
6. One large carrot

## Crop Hardiness Notes

- Tatsoi, leek, and carrot are confirmed hardy through the coldest conditions at Four Season Farm
- 'Mei Qing Choi', 'Shunkyo' radish, and 'Hakurei' turnip require further cold-testing
- The trial sold out before extreme cold weather fully tested the latter three ingredients

## Relevance to Cultivation

The Stir-Fry Pak demonstrates how winter greenhouse growers can create value-added products that command premium prices through convenience and presentation. By combining multiple crops into a single meal-ready package, the farm converts commodity winter vegetables into a differentiated product. The concept also serves as a practical test of which crop combinations can be reliably produced together through the coldest months. The sellout success suggests strong market demand for thoughtfully composed winter vegetable packages — an important lesson for growers seeking to maximize revenue from limited protected growing space.
