# Blue Dream

Blue Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain widely regarded as one
of the most popular and commercially successful strains in modern cannabis
history. Known for balanced effects and approachable potency, it is a staple
in both recreational and medical markets across North America and beyond.

## Lineage

| Parent | Type | Origin |
|--------|------|--------|
| Blueberry | Indica | DJ Short (1970s, Oregon) |
| Haze | Sativa | Santa Cruz, CA (1960s–70s) |

Blue Dream is a cross between indica-dominant Blueberry and landrace-sativa
Haze, producing a sativa-dominant hybrid (typically 60–70% sativa / 30–40%
indica). It inherits the sweet berry aroma of Blueberry alongside the cerebral,
uplifting qualities of Haze. The strain emerged in Northern California's
medical cannabis scene in the early-to-mid 2000s.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene** (dominant): Earthy, musky, herbal; sedative body effects;
  enhances cannabinoid absorption across blood-brain barrier
- **Pinene** (secondary): Piney, fresh; counteracts myrcene sedation,
  promotes alertness and memory retention
- **Caryophyllene** (secondary): Peppery, spicy; binds CB2 receptors directly,
  contributing anti-inflammatory effects
- **Limonene**: Citrus, bright; mood-elevating and stress-relieving
- **Linalool**: Floral, lavender; calming and anxiolytic

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| THC | 17–24% | Varies by grower and phenotype |
| CBD | 0.1–0.3% | Low; non-dominant |
| CBG | 0.1–0.5% | Minor presence |
| CBC | Trace | Present in small amounts |

THC can exceed 24% in well-cultivated phenotypes. Effects are driven almost
entirely by THC and terpene interactions given the low CBD content.

## Effects

Blue Dream is celebrated for a well-balanced experience:

- **Cerebral uplift**: Gentle euphoric head high promoting creativity, focus,
  and sociability
- **Body relaxation**: Mild physical ease; rarely couch-locking
- **Mood elevation**: Consistent positive mood shift for stress and mild anxiety
- **Motivation**: Functional daytime effects for creative work and activity

Common benefits include pain relief, stress reduction, improved focus, appetite
stimulation, and mild insomnia assistance at higher doses. Side effects are
generally limited to dry mouth, dry eyes, occasional dizziness, and rare mild
paranoia at high doses.

## Flavor and Aroma

- **Aroma**: Sweet blueberry and berry notes with earthy, herbal, and subtle
  vanilla undertones; light pine quality from pinene when broken open
- **Flavor**: Smooth sweet blueberry on inhale, earthy and slightly spicy on
  exhale with a lingering sugary aftertaste
- **Smoke**: Generally smooth; suitable for less experienced consumers

## Medical Applications

Blue Dream is frequently recommended for:

- **Chronic pain**: Myrcene and caryophyllene provide analgesic and
  anti-inflammatory effects
- **Anxiety and depression**: Balanced THC-to-terpene ratio stabilizes mood
  without heavy sedation
- **PTSD**: Calming yet alert effects for symptom management
- **Migraines**: Anti-inflammatory terpene profile reduces headache frequency
- **Fatigue**: Sativa-dominant effects provide daytime energy
- **Nausea/appetite loss**: Useful for patients undergoing chemotherapy
- **ADHD**: Some patients report improved focus and task completion

## Growing Information

Blue Dream is moderate difficulty, suitable for growers with some experience
but accessible to dedicated beginners.

### Growth Characteristics

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Flowering time | 9–10 weeks (indoor) |
| Harvest time | Mid-October (outdoor, Northern Hemisphere) |
| Indoor yield | 14–21 oz/m² |
| Outdoor yield | 15–24 oz/plant |
| Height | Medium-tall; stretches in early flower |
| Difficulty | Moderate |
| Resistance | Good mold and pest resistance |
| Preferred climate | Mediterranean; 65–80°F (18–27°C) |

### Cultivation Tips

- **Training**: Responds well to LST, SCROG, and topping for canopy control
- **Nutrients**: Moderate feeder; balanced NPK in veg, increased P/K in flower
- **Pruning**: Regular defoliation for airflow and light penetration
- **Humidity**: Maintain 40–50% during flowering to prevent bud rot
- **Support**: Staking or trellising recommended for heavy colas
- **Pest management**: Monitor for spider mites and powdery mildew
- **Flush**: 1–2 week flush before harvest for improved flavor

### Phenotype Variation

- **Sativa-lean**: Taller, elongated buds, stronger cerebral effects
- **Indica-lean**: Denser buds, enhanced body effects, shorter flowering
- Mother plant selection from desired phenotype ensures consistency

## History and Popularity

Blue Dream rose to prominence in Northern California's medical cannabis
community in the mid-2000s, becoming one of the most requested strains at
dispensaries. It consistently ranks among top-selling strains in legal markets
including California, Colorado, Oregon, Washington, and Michigan.

Enduring popularity factors include approachable potency, versatility for
daytime or evening use, consistent yields attractive to cultivators, broad
flavor appeal, and balanced effects rarely causing anxiety or couch-lock.

Blue Dream has served as a parent strain for numerous crosses including Blue
Cookies, Dream Queen, and various auto-flowering varieties, spreading its
desirable flavor and balanced profile into new genetics.
