# Blue Dream

## Overview

Blue Dream is a sativa-dominant [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] that defines the sativa side of Blue Dream.

### Genetic Ratio

Blue Dream is typically classified as approximately **60% sativa / 40% indica**, though this ratio can vary slightly depending on the specific phenotype and breeder. The strain exhibits vigorous growth patterns typical of sativa-dominant hybrids, with tall, stretchy plants that perform well in both indoor and outdoor environments.

## Terpene Profile

The characteristic aroma and effects of Blue Dream are largely determined by its dominant terpenes:

### Myrcene (Primary)

Concentration: 0.5–1.2% of dry weight. Myrcene is the most abundant terpene in Blue Dream and contributes its signature earthy, herbal, and subtly sweet notes. As a known synergist, myrcene enhances the absorption of other cannabinoids through the blood-brain barrier and contributes to the strain's relaxing body effects.

### β-Caryophyllene (Secondary)

Concentration: 0.2–0.6%. This sesquiterpene is unique among terpenes for its ability to directly activate the CB2 receptor, contributing anti-inflammatory effects independent of THC. It adds a subtle pepper and spice note to the aroma.

### α-Pinene and Limonene (Tertiary)

- **α-Pinene:** Contributes pine and forest-floor aromas while promoting alertness and counteracting some of THC's memory-impairing effects through acetylcholinesterase inhibition
- **Limonene:** Adds citrus brightness to the bouquet and contributes mood-elevating and stress-relieving properties

### Overall Aroma and Flavor

Blue Dream presents a complex bouquet of sweet blueberry, earthy herbal notes, subtle vanilla, and a hint of pepper and pine. The flavor closely mirrors the aroma, with a smooth, fruity smoke that is notably less harsh than many high-THC varieties.

## Cannabinoid Profile

### Typical Ranges (modern commercial samples)

- **THC:** 17–24% (occasionally testing higher in select phenotypes)
- **CBD:** <0.1–0.3% (generally considered a low-CBD strain)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** Trace amounts

### Historical Potency

Early versions of Blue Dream from the 2000s typically tested in the 14–18% THC range. Like many legacy strains, selective breeding and improved cultivation practices have pushed THC content upward over the past two decades.

## Effects

### Onset and Duration

Effects typically onset within 5–10 minutes when smoked or vaporized, with peak effects at 30–60 minutes. Total duration is usually 2–3 hours. Edible formulations have a delayed onset (45–90 minutes) and longer duration (4–6 hours).

### Primary Effects

- **Cerebral:** Gentle, uplifting euphoria without the anxiety or racing thoughts that can accompany high-THC sativas. Users commonly report enhanced creativity, sociability, and a generally positive mood shift
- **Physical:** Mild body relaxation that eases tension without causing heavy sedation or couch-lock. The physical effects are more pronounced in higher-dose consumption

### Commonly Reported Uses

- **Stress [[cannabis-for-chronic-pain]], headaches, and muscle tension
- **Mood elevation:** Useful for managing symptoms of mild depression and mood disorders
- **Daytime functionality:** Unlike heavy indicas, Blue Dream typically allows users to remain productive and alert
- **Social situations:** Enhanced sociability and reduced social anxiety make it popular for gatherings
