    18|# Blue Dream
    19|
    20|**Blue Dream** — a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain (*DJ Short's Blueberry* × *Haze*), widely regarded as one of the most popular and commercially significant cannabis varieties in modern cultivation history.
    21|
    22|## Lineage
    23|
    24|```
    25|        DJ Short's Blueberry (♀)
    26|              ×
    27|        Unknown Haze male (♂)
    28|              │
    29|         Blue Dream
    30|```
    31|
    32|| Parent | Type | Origin | Contribution |
    33||--------|------|--------|-------------|
    34|| **DJ Short's Blueberry** | Indica-dominant (80/20) | Pacific Northwest, 1970s | Berry flavor, color, resin production |
    35|| **Haze** | Sativa (90/10) | Santa Cruz, California, 1960s–70s | Cerebral effects, tall growth, complex terpenes |
    36|
    37|The original cross is credited to an anonymous breeder in the **Santa Cruz, California** area, with seeds first circulating in the early 2000s. Blue Dream gained massive popularity on the West Coast medical market before spreading nationally and internationally.

    55|## Cannabinoid Profile
    56|
    57|Blue Dream is a **high-THC, low-CBD type I chemovar**, consistent with its Haze parentage. Typical analytical results:
    58|
    59|| Cannabinoid | Range | Primary Effect |
    60||-------------|-------|---------------|
    61|| Δ⁹-THC | 17–24% | Psychoactive, analgesic |
    62|| THCA | 19–27% (pre-decarboxylation) | Precursor to THC |
    63|| CBGA | 0.5–1.5% | Biosynthetic precursor |
    64|| CBG | < 0.5% | Anti-inflammatory, neuroprotective |
    65|| CBD | < 0.1% | Non-psychoactive therapeutic |
    66|| CBC | < 0.3% | Anti-inflammatory |
    67|| CBN | < 0.1% | Sedative (degradation product) |
    68|| THCV | < 0.2% | Appetite suppressant |
    69|
    70|The **entourage effect** in Blue Dream is driven primarily by the interaction of high THC with its distinctive terpene profile rather than CBD content.
    71|
    72|## Terpene Profile
    73|
    74|Blue Dream's signature terpene profile produces its characteristic sweet berry aroma with earthy undertones.
    75|
    76|### Dominant Terpenes
    77|
    78|| Terpene | Concentration | Aroma Character | Therapeutic Properties |
    79||---------|--------------|-----------------|----------------------|
    80|| **Myrcene** | 0.4–1.2% | Earthy, musky, herbal | Sedative, analgesic, anti-inflammatory |
    81|| **Caryophyllene** | 0.2–0.6% | Peppery, spicy, woody | Anti-inflammatory (CB₂ agonist), analgesic |
    82|| **Linalool** | 0.1–0.4% | Floral, lavender-like | Anxiolytic, sedative, anticonvulsant |
    83|| **Pinene (α+β)** | 0.1–0.3% | Pine, fresh, sharp | Bronchodilator, memory retention, alertness |
    84|| **Limonene** | 0.1–0.3% | Citrus, lemon | Mood elevation, anti-anxiety, GERD relief |
    85|| **Humulene** | 0.05–0.15% | Earthy, woody | Appetite suppressant, anti-inflammatory |
    86|| **Ocimene** | Trace | Sweet, herbaceous | Antiviral, decongestant |
    87|| **Terpinolene** | Trace | Piney, floral, herbal | Sedative, antioxidant |
    88|| **Nerolidol** | Trace | Woody, citrus, floral | Skin penetrant enhancer, sedative |
    89|
    90|The high myrcene content combined with linalool and caryophyllene creates Blue Dream's **balanced psychoactive profile** — the myrcene tempers Haze's racing cerebral effects while linalool provides anxiolytic cushioning.

    92|## Phenotype Variation
    93|
    94|Blue Dream exhibits notable phenotypic variation, with three main expressions:
    95|
    96|### Pheno A — "The Classic"
    97|- Dense, elongated buds with blue-purple hues
    98|- Strong blueberry aroma, moderate stretch
    99|- Most commercially available; balanced head/body effects
   100|- 18–21% THC
   101|
   102|### Pheno B — "The Haze Express"
   103|- Taller, more stretched structure
   104|- More sparse, resinous buds
   105|- More cerebral and energetic
   106|- 20–24% THC, higher limonene/pinene
   107|
   108|### Pheno C — "The Blueberry Heavy"
   109|- Shorter, bushier, indica-leaning growth
   110|- Dense, dark purple buds
   111|- Stronger body relaxation
   112|- 16–19% THC, higher myrcene

   114|## Effects Profile
   115|
   116|### Onset and Duration
   117|
   118|- **Onset:** 1–3 minutes (smoking/vaping); 30–90 minutes (edibles)
   119|- **Peak:** 15–30 minutes (inhalation); 2–4 hours (edibles)
   120|- **Duration:** 2–4 hours (inhalation); 4–8 hours (edibles)
   121|
   122|### Subjective Effects (user-reported consensus)
   123|
   124|**Positive:**
   125|- Euphoric, uplifting cerebral onset
   126|- Creative enhancement and focus
   127|- Gentle full-body relaxation without sedation
   128|- Social comfort and talkativeness
   129|- Mood elevation and stress relief
   130|
   131|**Negative (rare, dose-dependent):**
   132|- Dry mouth and dry eyes (very common)
   133|- Mild anxiety at high doses (rare, due to balanced terpenes)
   134|- Paranoia in anxiety-sensitive individuals
   135|- Dizziness with high-THC phenotypes

   137|### Therapeutic Applications
   138|
   139|Blue Dream is one of the most commonly recommended strains in medical cannabis dispensaries:
   140|
   141|| Condition | Effectiveness | Mechanism |
   142||-----------|-------------|-----------|
   143|| **Chronic pain** | High | THC + caryophyllene (dual anti-inflammatory pathways) |

   145|| **Depression** | High | THC mood elevation + limonene antidepressant effects |
   146|| **PTSD** | Moderate | THC fear extinction; myrcene relaxation without amnesia |
   147|| **Migraines** | Moderate | Caryophyllene + THC analgesia; pinene anti-inflammatory |
   148|| **Fatigue** | Moderate | Haze-derived sativa energy; THCV appetite modulation |
   149|| **Nausea** | Moderate-High | THC antiemetic via CB₁ receptor activation |
   150|| **Insomnia** | Low-Moderate | Myrcene sedation sufficient for some; not heavily sedating |
