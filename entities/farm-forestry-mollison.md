# Farm Forestry (Permaculture)

## Description

Farm forestry in permaculture refers to the deliberate integration of multi-species tree systems into farm landscapes for multiple simultaneous yields including timber, firewood, polewood, forage, honey, mulch, medicinals, shelter, wildlife habitat, and ecosystem services. Emerging from approximately 1972 onwards in mesothermal climates, farm forestry represents a paradigm shift from open cropping and monoculture plantation forestry toward perennial, tree-oriented production that buffers economic changes, provides assured income from diverse products, enhances all other farm yields through shelter effects, and builds long-term ecological resilience. The approach stands in direct opposition to industrial monoculture forestry devoted to single species of eucalypt or pine.
