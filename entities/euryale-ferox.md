### Reproductive Structures

- **Flowers:** Solitary, 4–5 cm, purple to violet petals, 10–20 per
  flower; armed sepals; blooms June–September
- **Fruit:** Spherical capsule, 3–5 cm, densely spiny, 8–20 seeds
- **Seeds:** Spherical, 6–10 mm, black when mature, starchy white
  endosperm

## Distribution and Habitat

Native to tropical and subtropical Asia: India (Bihar, Manipur, Assam —
primary commercial cultivation), China (Yangtze basin), Japan, Korea,
Bangladesh, and Nepal. Thrives in shallow freshwater ponds, oxbow lakes,
flooded fields, and slow river backwaters.

Optimal conditions: water depth 30–150 cm, temperature 25–35°C,
pH 6.0–8.0, nutrient-rich muddy substrate. Intolerant of frost and
salinity.
