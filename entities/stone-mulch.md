## Stone Mulch

## Description

Stone mulch uses rocks placed around plants to condense atmospheric moisture,
[[query-how-do-cover-crops-suppress-weeds]], reduce wind, anchor roots, and moderate temperature. Wherever
stone is plentiful, it provides one of the most effective low-cost establishment
methods for arid conditions. The ground below stone piles is always damp.

## Classification

- **Category**: Mulching technique, water harvesting
- **Domain**: Desert agriculture, arid-land plant establishment
- **Technology level**: Low-tech
- **Materials**: Locally available stones and rocks

## Five Functions

### Condensation Harvester
Stones cool at night, condensing atmospheric moisture that drips to root zones.
Ground below stone piles is always damp.

### Weed Suppression
Stone cover prevents weed germination, eliminating competition for scarce water.

### Windbreak
Stones reduce ground-level wind speed, decreasing desiccation and soil erosion.

### Root Anchor
Stone weight prevents windthrow of newly established plants.

### Temperature Moderator
Stones buffer temperature extremes — absorbing heat by day, releasing slowly
at night.

## Application Methods

### Walled Circles on Rocky Hillsides
Stones in circles with weeds thrown in. Very successful for establishment.
Can be moved after 2-3 years as plants establish.

### Linear Stone Mounds
Stone lines between crops provide field-scale benefits.

### Individual Plant Mulching
Even a few flat stones at a tree base assists soil water condensation.

### Natural Boulder Beaches
Act like swales — springs break out below them. Double lines of trees or
crops can be planted below.

## Ecological Benefits

Small invertebrates take refuge in stone piles, adding nutrients through waste.
Creates a micro-ecosystem supporting plant nutrition alongside water harvesting.

## Key Facts

- Five simultaneous functions from one technique
- No cost beyond labour to collect and place
- Can be relocated as needed
- Ground below always damp
- Invertebrate habitat adds nutrients
- Compatible with all other [[sprinkler-irrigation-methods]]
- Effective wherever stone is plentiful
