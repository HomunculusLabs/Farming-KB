## Description

Shamanism is humanity's oldest known form of natural spirituality, centered on the individual's direct experience of altered [[shamanism-death-and-rebirth]]" through plant-induced altered states, as practice for conquering actual physical death.
- The core practice involves deep psychological introspection and judgment through plant-induced visionary states, preparing the individual for the inevitability of death.
- Shamanism directly opposes patriarchal, dogmatic religious systems that suppress individual spiritual experience, sexuality, and entheogen use.
- *Amanita muscaria* is considered the cornerstone of Shamanism, producing the quintessential death and [[ghouled-field-guide-historical-context]] for the cultivation and ritual use of entheogenic mushrooms, particularly *Amanita muscaria* and *Psilocybe* species.
- Understanding shamanic practice is essential for contextualizing the ethnobotanical significance [[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] trace their cultural lineage to shamanic plant knowledge.
