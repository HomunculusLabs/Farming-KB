## Mortierella alpina

## Overview

mortierella alpina is a filamentous soil fungus belonging to the phylum Zygomycota (subphylum Mucoromycotina). It is a widespread saprotrophic species found in forest soils, grasslands, and various other terrestrial habitats. The species is ecologically notable as an indicator of forest disturbance and management intensity, and it has gained considerable biotechnological importance as a prolific producer of polyunsaturated fatty acids, particularly arachidonic acid.
