## Synthesis Notes

Synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxy-4-((i)-propylthio)benzaldehyde (see 2c-t-4-entity|2C-T-4 synthesis) condensed with nitroethane. The nitrostyrene intermediate crystallizes as orange crystals (mp 99-100 deg C). Reduction with LAH in THF provides the hydrochloride salt (mp 146-147 deg C with prior sintering at 144 deg C). The long reaction times (24 h reflux plus 2 days at room temperature) reflect the steric hindrance of the isopropylthio group.
