## Chemical Family and Context

4-HO-MET is a member of the 4-hydroxytryptamine family, which represents the
pharmacological core of the psychedelic effects found in Psilocybe mushrooms.
The systematic exploration of N-alkyl substitution in this series has been
one of the most productive areas of [[mckenna-baconian-exploration-tryptamine-research-methodology]].

The 4-hydroxytryptamine family demonstrates a remarkable pharmacological
consistency: regardless of the specific N-alkyl groups (within the constraint
of six or fewer total carbons on the nitrogen), all members are active in the
10-20 mg range with durations of 3-8 hours. This suggests that the 4-hydroxy
group is the dominant determinant of both potency and duration, with the
N-alkyl groups providing fine-tuning of the qualitative experience.

4-HO-MET is sometimes called "metocin" or "colour" in informal contexts, and
has appeared sporadically on research chemical markets since the mid-2000s.
Its effects are generally described as slightly more colorful and less
introspective than psilocin, with a comparable duration.

## Detailed Pharmacological Discussion

4-HO-MET acts primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist, consistent with the
established mechanism of action for all 4-hydroxytryptamine psychedelics. The
ethyl group on the nitrogen atom slightly increases lipophilicity compared to
psilocin, which could affect both brain penetration kinetics and metabolic
clearance rates.

The compound is metabolized primarily by MAO-mediated oxidative deamination
and by cytochrome P450 enzymes, particularly CYP2D6. The N-ethyl group may
alter metabolic stability relative to psilocin, potentially contributing to
slight differences in duration and character.

In vitro [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]] profiles of 4-hydroxytryptamine analogues show that
N-alkyl substitution has relatively minor effects on 5-HT2A affinity, with
most members showing Ki values in the 5-50 nM range. The primary effect of
different N-alkyl groups appears to be on functional efficacy and on interactions
with other receptor subtypes, which may explain the subtle qualitative differences
reported between compounds.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- 4-HO-DMT (psilocin): N,N-dimethyl, the reference compound, 10-20 mg, 3-6 h

- 4-HO-MET: N-ethyl-N-methyl, very similar effects, 10-20 mg, 4-6 h

- 4-HO-MIPT: N-isopropyl-N-methyl, slightly more potent, more erotic, 12-25 mg, 4-6 h

- 4-HO-MPT: N-methyl-N-propyl, insufficient data, estimated 10-20 mg

- 4-HO-DET: N,N-diethyl, slightly longer duration, 10-25 mg, 4-6 h

- 4-HO-DPT: N,N-dipropyl, insufficient data, estimated 15-25 mg

The practical similarity of all these compounds supports Shulgin's observation
that they would be difficult to distinguish in blind [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]], though
subtle qualitative differences in color emphasis, emotional tone, and body
feel are consistently reported by experienced users.
