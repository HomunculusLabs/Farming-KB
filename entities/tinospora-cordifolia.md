## Overview

Tinospora cordifolia, known as Giloy or Guduchi in Ayurveda, is a large
deciduous climbing shrub in the family Menispermaceae. It is one of the most
important rasayana (rejuvenating) herbs in traditional Indian medicine.

The species is native to the Indian subcontinent and [[psilocybe-southeast-asia-pacific]]. In India, it is found from the Himalayan foothills to the
southern peninsula.

The species grows in a wide range of habitats from tropical semi-evergreen to
deciduous forests, typically at elevations up to 1200 meters. It is commonly
found climbing on trees along forest edges, in disturbed areas, and near
watercourses.

Propagation is primarily through stem cuttings, which root readily in moist
soil during the monsoon season. The plant is drought-tolerant once established
but grows most vigorously with consistent moisture and partial shade.
