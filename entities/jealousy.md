## Overview

**Jealousy** is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by **Sherbinski Genetics**, the same breeder behind the legendary Gelato and Sunset Sherbet lines.

Released in the early 2020s, Jealousy quickly gained recognition for its potent relaxing effects, complex dessert-like aroma, and visually striking flower structure.

The strain's name references the envy-inducing quality of its bag appeal — dense, resinous buds with deep purple and green coloration under a thick blanket of trichomes.

## Lineage

Jealousy is a cross between **Sherbinski's Gelato #41** and **Sunset Sherbet**, two cornerstone strains of the modern "dessert cannabis" movement.

Gelato #41 contributes high THC production, dense bud structure, and sweet citrus notes. Sunset Sherbet adds creamy, berry-forward terpene complexity and deeply relaxing physical effects.

The genetic ratio is approximately **70% indica / 30% sativa**, though the sativa influence from the Thin Mint Cookies ancestor (via Gelato) is detectable in the initial cerebral onset.

Both parent strains carry awards and widespread recognition: Gelato #41 was selected from the original Gelato cross for its exceptional resin production and balanced effects, while Sunset Sherbet is one of the most influential dessert strains of the 2010s.

This dual-heritage gives Jealousy a complexity that appeals to both connoisseur consumers seeking nuanced flavor and medical patients seeking reliable physical relaxation.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, densely packed with a chunky, tapered structure. Colors range from deep forest green to rich violet, with bright orange pistils woven throughout.

The trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving flowers a frosted, silvery-white appearance even before full maturity. This resin density translates to high essential oil content and potent extracts.

The visual presentation is one of the strain's strongest market attributes — the contrast between dark purple calyxes and white trichomes creates a distinctive bag appeal that commands premium pricing in licensed dispensaries.

## Aroma and Flavor

The dominant aroma is a blend of **creamy vanilla**, **ripe berries**, and **sharp citrus** with earthy undertones and a subtle fuel note on the exhale.

On inhalation, flavors of sweet cream and berry dominate. The exhale reveals earthy, peppery, and slightly skunky notes that ground the initial sweetness.

The flavor profile is consistently described as "dessert-like," placing it firmly in the Gelato family's sensory tradition alongside Runtz, Kush Mints, and Ice Cream Cake.

Side effects are typical of high-THC indica-dominant strains: dry mouth, dry eyes, and potential dizziness at higher doses. Some users report mild paranoia or anxiety with excessive consumption, particularly in low-tolerance individuals.

Due to its sedative properties, Jealousy is not recommended for daytime use or situations requiring sustained focus and alertness. Beginning users should start with low doses (1–2 puffs) and assess tolerance.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Limonene (primary), Caryophyllene (secondary), Myrcene (supporting), with notable contributions from Linalool and Humulene.

**THC content:** 20–28%, with some phenotype selections testing above 30% THC.

**CBD content:** Typically below 0.5%, making Jealousy a high-THC, low-CBD variety.

The limonene-dominant terpene profile contributes to the strain's mood-elevating and stress-relieving character, while caryophyllene provides anti-inflammatory effects via CB2 receptor interaction.

## Effects

Effects onset is relatively fast (5–10 minutes when smoked), beginning with a cerebral uplift — mild euphoria, enhanced mood, and creative stimulation.

This transitions into a deep body relaxation within 30–45 minutes, often described as "couch-lock" at higher doses. The physical sedation builds progressively without sudden onset.

Common medical applications include stress relief, anxiety management, mild pain, and insomnia. The strain is frequently recommended for evening or nighttime use due to its sedative tail.

## Cultivation

Flowering time is **8–9 weeks** indoors, with outdoor harvest in early to mid-October in northern temperate climates.

Indoor yields range from 350–450 g/m². The strain responds well to low-stress training (LST), topping, and SCROG methods. Plants exhibit moderate stretch during early flowering.

Jealousy is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, requiring attention to humidity control (resin-heavy buds are susceptible to botrytis in high humidity) and nutrient management to maximize terpene expression.

The strain expresses its best color and flavor at moderate temperatures (18–24°C during flowering) with a 10–15°C day-night differential. Excessive heat can reduce terpene volatility and diminish aroma intensity.

## Notable Phenotypes and Derivatives

Several Jealousy crosses have emerged since the strain's release, capitalizing on its potency and aroma profile. Notable derivatives include **Jealousy Gelato** (backcross to Gelato), **Jealousy Sherbet** (cross with Sherbet), and **Jealousy Kush** (cross with OG Kush lineage).

The strain has also been used as a parent in several "exotic" market crosses, where breeders seek to combine its dessert terpene profile with other high-potency varieties.

Among cannabis competition circles, Jealousy earned recognition at multiple events in 2022–2023 for its overall quality, including placement in High Times Cannabis Cup categories.
