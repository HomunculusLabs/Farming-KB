## 3,4-DMA

3,4-DMA (3,4-Dimethoxyamphetamine) is an amphetamine derivative with the same oxygen substitution pattern as the natural catecholamine neurotransmitters.

It is documented as entry #55 in PiHKAL by Alexander Shulgin.

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 3,4-Dimethoxyamphetamine.

Molecular formula: C11H17NO2 (free base).

The 3,4-orientation of the two methoxy groups mirrors the positional substitution found in dopamine, norepinephrine, and epinephrine (noradrenaline and adrenaline).

This makes 3,4-DMA a direct analogue of the endogenous catecholamine system, with the hydroxyl groups replaced by methoxy groups.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 144-145 degrees Celsius as beautiful white crystals.

## Dosage and Duration

Oral active dosage: approximately 160 mg and above.

Intravenous dosage producing mescaline-like effects: approximately 0.04 mM/kg.
