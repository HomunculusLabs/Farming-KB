# Tamarillo

**Type:** Species (Plant)
**Category:** Subtropical fruiting shrub
**Also known as:** Tree tomato, Cyphomandra betacea, Solanum betaceum

## Overview
Tamarillo is a fast-growing, erect evergreen shrub native to the temperate
Andes of South America, known primarily for its egg-shaped fruits that
resemble tomatoes. The name tamarillo was adopted in New Zealand in 1967 to
distinguish it from the common tomato. Ideally suited to small gardens in
tropical and subtropical climates, it is a productive and easy-to-grow fruit
crop with significant nutritional value.

## Key Details

### Botanical Classification
- **Family:** Solanaceae (nightshade family)
- **Genus:** Cyphomandra (sometimes placed in Solanum)
- **Species:** C. betacea (syn. Solanum betaceum)

### Growth Characteristics
- **Habit:** Erect, shrubby, fast-growing evergreen to 4 m
- **Lifespan:** Fairly short-lived (typically 5-8 years productive life)
- **Root system:** Very shallow rooted; heavy mulching recommended
- **Frost tolerance:** Slight, to approximately -2C

### Environmental Requirements
- **Soil:** Requires rich, moist, well-drained soil
- **Water:** Will not tolerate either waterlogging or drought; shallow roots
  mean moisture stress causes fruit drop
- **Climate:** Tropical and subtropical; grows in cooler areas if planted in
  sunny, sheltered locations
- **Mulching:** Essential due to shallow root system

### Fruit Characteristics
- **Shape:** Egg-shaped, approximately 5 cm long
- **Colours:** Yellow (sweetest), red, and dark red varieties
- **Flavour:** Yellow fruits are sweetest; red varieties have a harsher,
  more acid flavour
- **Culinary uses:** Best eaten fresh; also made into jams, jellies, and
  preserves
- **Skin:** Easily removed by dipping fruit in hot water

### Nutritional Composition (per 100g edible portion)
- **Water:** 82.7 - 87.8%
- **Fat:** 0.0 - 1.28 g
- **Carbohydrates:** 10.3 g
- **Protein:** 1.5 g
- **Ash:** 0.61 - 0.84 g
- **Calcium:** 3.9 - 11.3 mg
- **Iron:** 0.66 - 0.94 mg
- **Phosphorus:** 52.5 - 65.5 mg (mostly in seeds)
- **Vitamin A:** 540 IU
- **Thiamin:** 0.038 - 0.137 mg
- **Riboflavin:** 0.035 - 0.048 mg
- **Niacin:** 1.1 - 1.38 mg
- **Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C):** 23.3 - 33.9 mg
