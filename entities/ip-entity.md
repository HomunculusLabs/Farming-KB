# IP

**PIHKAL Entry #92** -- Isoproscaline; 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propoxyphenethylamine

## Chemical Name

3,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propoxyphenethylamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 40 - 80 mg

- **Duration:** 10 - 16 h

## Qualitative Effects

IP is described as "a completely fascinating phenethylamine" with complex effects:

- At 75 mg: slow onset with queasiness turning into nausea. Breaking through at two hours. Choosing to dance led to "a marvelous ecstatic experience, flowing with and being the energy, body feeling completely free." Comedown was slow, gentle, euphoric. Sleep impossible but felt good to release to feelings. "Tremendous feeling of confidence in life and the life process. Complete sense of resolution"

- At 80 mg: about two hours for the body to settle down. Emotions were "true and well felt." Much talking, laughing, being silent in great contentment. "It's not a very silent material, though." Slight descent at 6-7 hours, slow and pleasant descent until about 12th hour. Next day, everyone slightly irritable but good mood. Deep, restorative sleep the second night

## Synthesis

Synthesized from homosyringonitrile (see [[escaline-entity]]) via O-alkylation with isopropyl iodide in acetone with potassium carbonate, followed by LAH reduction of the nitrile. The intermediate nitrile was a white solid with mp 33-34 degrees C. The hydrochloride salt had a mp of 163-164 degrees C. Catalytic hydrogenation of the nitrile also works.

## Extensions and Commentary

The prefix "iso" in isoproscaline refers to the branching of the propyl group on the 4-oxygen of mescaline, replacing it with its branched analogue (isopropyl rather than n-propyl). This is distinct from the prefix in "isomescaline" (IM), which refers to a different positional arrangement of the methoxy groups.

The corresponding amphetamine would be 3,5-dimethoxy-4-isopropoxyamphetamine (3C-IP), which Shulgin guessed would be more potent than IP but probably not by much, perhaps in the 30 mg area.

## Chemical Family Context

IP is a member of the mescaline derivative family, specifically the series of compounds where the 4-methoxy group of mescaline is replaced by larger alkoxy groups. This series represents one of the most thoroughly explored structure-activity relationships in psychedelic phenethylamine chemistry.

The alkoxy series at the 4-position follows a clear progression:

- Methoxy (mescaline): 200-500 mg, 6-10 h

- Ethoxy (escaline): 40-60 mg, 8-12 h

- n-Propoxy (proscaline): 30-60 mg, 8-12 h

- Isopropoxy (IP, isoproscaline): 40-80 mg, 10-16 h

- Allyloxy (AL): 20-35 mg, 8-12 h

The branching of the propyl group in IP versus the linear chain in proscaline provides an important data point: branching at the first carbon of the alkoxy chain has relatively modest effects on potency but appears to extend duration. This suggests that the terminal methyl group's orientation is not critical for receptor interaction, while the overall lipophilicity and steric profile may influence metabolic clearance rates.

## Pharmacological Discussion

IP acts as a psychedelic phenethylamine, presumably through agonism at the 5-HT2A receptor, with the characteristic 2,5-dimethoxy-4-alkoxy substitution pattern that is well-established as optimal for psychedelic activity in this compound class.

The compound's effects profile -- combining euphoria, emotional openness, and enhanced sensory perception with minimal visual distortion at moderate doses -- places it in a category sometimes described as "entactogenic-psychedelic." The prominent emotional and somatic effects, coupled with the relatively long duration, make IP more suitable for therapeutic or introspective contexts than for recreational use.

The slow onset (approximately two hours) and extended comedown (up to 12 hours for full return to baseline) are notable features that distinguish IP from shorter-acting phenethylamines like 2C-B. This extended timeline may be related to the compound's metabolic stability, with the branched isopropoxy group potentially resisting oxidative dealkylation more effectively than linear alkoxy groups.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- Escaline (3,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxy): Shorter chain, similar effects profile, somewhat shorter duration

- Proscaline (3,5-dimethoxy-4-n-propoxy): Linear propyl vs. branched, similar potency range

- Mescaline (3,4,5-trimethoxy): Parent compound, significantly less potent, shorter duration

- 3C-E (3,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine): The amphetamine homologue, expected to be more potent

The differences between IP and proscaline are instructive: the branched isopropoxy group slightly increases the dose range (40-80 vs. 30-60 mg) and extends the duration (10-16 vs. 8-12 h), suggesting that branching slows metabolism without dramatically altering receptor binding.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

- The long duration (up to 16 hours) requires advance planning for food, hydration, and rest

- Initial nausea is commonly reported; fasting for 4-6 hours before dosing may help

- The compound's stimulating properties may interfere with sleep for many hours after peak effects subside

- As with all serotonergic compounds, avoid combining with MAO inhibitors, SSRIs, or other serotonergic substances

- The emotional intensity reported at higher doses (75-80 mg) suggests this compound may not be suitable for individuals with anxiety disorders or unresolved trauma

- Residual irritability reported the day after use is a common aftereffect; scheduling recovery time is advisable

## Legal Status

IP is not specifically scheduled in most jurisdictions. As a dimethoxyalkoxyphenethylamine, it could fall under analogue legislation in countries with broad controlled substance analogue provisions. In the US, the Federal Analogue Act could potentially apply given structural similarity to mescaline. The compound's rarity has kept it largely below regulatory attention.

## Cultural and Historical Significance

IP does not have a significant cultural footprint outside of the Shulgin research program. Its primary significance lies in its contribution to the systematic mapping of alkoxy-substituted phenethylamine structure-activity relationships. The detailed subjective reports from PIHKAL, particularly the description of ecstatic dance experiences and emotional resolution, provide valuable qualitative data for understanding the spectrum of psychedelic phenethylamine effects.

The compound's effects profile -- with its emphasis on emotional processing and somatic experience over visual phenomena -- anticipates the modern therapeutic interest in psychedelic compounds that facilitate emotional catharsis and insight without overwhelming sensory distortion.
