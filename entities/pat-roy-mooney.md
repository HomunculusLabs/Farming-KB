## Description

Pat Roy Mooney is a Canadian activist and author known for his foundational work on seed sovereignty and the politics of genetic resource control. His influential book "Seeds of the Earth" (published through the World Council of Churches, Canada) exposed how agro-chemical multinationals were systematically consolidating control over global seed supplies through seed patents, trade contracts, and retail outlet acquisition — using food as a mechanism of geopolitical power.
