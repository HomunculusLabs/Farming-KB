# 2C-O-4 — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propoxyphenethylamine

PIHKAL entry #35. 2C-O-4 is the oxygen-series analogue of 2C-T-4, replacing the isopropylthio group with an isopropoxy group at the 4-position. It represents an early exploration of the 2C-O series that ultimately proved disappointing.

## Chemical Identity

- Full name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-isopropoxyphenethylamine
- PIHKAL number: #35
- Benzaldehyde intermediate: fine off-white crystals, mp 79–80 °C (prior shrinking at 71 °C)
- Nitrostyrene intermediate: orange crystals
- Free base: clear white oil, distilled at 115–125 °C at 0.3 mm/Hg
- HCl salt: white crystals, 0.58 g final weight

## Dosage and Duration

- Dosage: greater than 60 mg
- Duration: unknown

## Effects and Qualitative Comments

At 60 mg, subject became aware of something in the front of the head with a lot of yawning. Body was aware of the experiment. General exhilaration and excitement lasted for a few hours. At best, the experience reached only a plus one. This was the only trial reported and the full activity of 2C-O-4 was never established.

### PiHKAL Trip Report

**60 mg:** "I became aware of something in the front part of my head, and there was a lot of yawning. The body was aware of the experiment. But also there was a general exhilaration and excitement, which lasted for a few hours. At best, I am at a plus one."

## Synthesis

The synthesis of 2C-O-4 follows a multi-step pathway starting from 2,5-dimethoxyphenol. The key steps are:

1. **Williamson ether synthesis:** 2,5-dimethoxyphenol is alkylated with isopropyl iodide using KOH in methanol to give 1,4-dimethoxy-2-isopropoxybenzene as a white oil.
2. **Vilsmeier formylation:** The resulting ether undergoes Vilsmeier-Haack formylation (N-methylformanilide/POCl₃) to yield 2,5-dimethoxy-4-isopropoxybenzaldehyde as fine off-white crystals, mp 79–80 °C.
3. **Nitrostyrene formation:** Condensation with nitromethane in the presence of ammonium acetate gives 2,5-dimethoxy-β-nitro-4-isopropoxystyrene as orange crystals. The reaction progress was monitored by TLC (Rf 0.16 for aldehyde, 0.50 for nitrostyrene in CH₂Cl₂).
4. **LAH reduction:** Reduction with lithium aluminum hydride (with H₂SO₄ quenching) yields the free base, which is distilled and converted to the HCl salt. Final yield: 0.58 g of white crystals.

The three-carbon amphetamine analogue (MIPM) was also synthesized but never bioassayed following disappointing results with the closely related MPM.

## Extensions and Commentary

The full activity of 2C-O-4 was never discovered. It represents an interesting hybrid lying between several fascinating compounds, all carrying the 2,4,5-trisubstitution pattern that has consistently proven the most interesting and active in phenethylamines.

The sulfur analogues in this area (compounds with an alkylthio group at the 4-position) are the 2c-t-7-compound-profile|2C-T series. The replacement of sulfur with oxygen should logically give rise to the 2C-O counterparts, numbered following the same system. 2C-T-4 was the 4-isopropylthio compound, so 2C-O-4 is the oxygen counterpart.

This was "a first shot in the dark" that did not provide much encouragement. The three-carbon amphetamine analogue (MIPM) was synthesized but not explored following disappointing trials of MPM.

### Proposed 2C-O Series Numbering

Shulgin proposed a complete numbering system for the 2C-O series, paralleling the 2c-t-7-compound-profile|2C-T compounds:

| 2C-O Series | 2C-T Analogue | 4-Substituent |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| 2C-O (TMPEA) | 2C-T | methoxy |
| 2C-O-2 | 2C-T-2 | ethoxy |
| 2C-O-4 | 2C-T-4 | isopropoxy |
| 2C-O-7 | 2C-T-7 | (n)-propoxy |
| 2C-O-19 | 2C-T-19 | (n)-butoxy |

These are the exact analogues of 2C-T-2, 2C-T-7, and 2C-T-19, and the 2-carbon homologues of MEM, MPM, and MBM. The simplest member, the methyl counterpart (2C-O, also called 2,4,5-TMPEA), had been explored previously.

### Structure-Activity Relationship: Oxygen vs. Sulfur

The dramatic loss of activity when replacing sulfur with oxygen at the 4-position is a key SAR finding. The sulfur atom in the 2C-T series is larger, more polarizable, and more lipophilic than oxygen. These properties likely contribute to better receptor binding affinity at the 5-HT₂A site. The sulfur atom may also participate in unique receptor interactions (e.g., sulfur-aromatic interactions) that oxygen cannot replicate.

Shulgin concluded that with the probable low eventual potency of 2C-O-4, the 2C-O series would likely not be an exciting one. This prediction appears to have been borne out, as the 2C-O series has received virtually no further research attention.

## Cautionary Notes

Full dosage range and duration unknown. Only a single trial at 60 mg was conducted, reaching a marginal plus one. The oxygen-for-sulfur substitution dramatically reduced activity compared to the 2C-T series. This compound should be considered inactive or minimally active based on available data. Any further investigation would require substantially higher doses, which carries inherent uncertainty.
