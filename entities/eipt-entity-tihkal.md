# EIPT — TIHKAL #10

## Compound Identity

- Full name: N-Ethyl-N-isopropyltryptamine

- Synonyms: 3-[2-(ethylisopropylamino)ethyl]indole

- Class: Tryptamine, N-ethyl-N-isopropyl substituted

- Source: [[det-entity-tihkal]] and [[5-meo-dmt-entity-tihkal]].
Another 40 mg report notes sparkling and an unsure stomach, plus remarkable
sexual functionality despite the experience not feeling erotic. Described
as "barely plus 1" with a diuretic component.

## Research Rationale

EIPT was synthesized to investigate the auditory distortion properties of
the diisopropyl substitution pattern found in [[4-ho-dipt-entity-tihkal]].
DIPT causes extraordinary auditory distortions — Shulgin wanted to determine
whether both isopropyl groups are necessary, or if only one is needed.

The systematic comparison of compounds maintaining one isopropyl group while
varying the other:

- Methyl + Isopropyl (MIPT): Psychedelic at ~25 mg, not fully explored at
40 mg

- Ethyl + Isopropyl (EIPT): Uncomfortable nausea, no auditory disruptions

- Propyl + Isopropyl (PIPT): Not yet evaluated

- Isopropyl + Isopropyl (DIPT): Intense auditory distortion at 40 mg

- Butyl + Isopropyl (BIPT): Not yet evaluated

If the diisopropyl substitution proves absolutely essential for the auditory
phenomenon, DIPT becomes one of the most remarkable tools known for studying
the human auditory association area in the brain. EIPT represents a critical
negative result in this investigation.

## Synthesis

Two routes: from indole via [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-formation-oxalyl-chloride-indole]] and ethylisopropylamine (62%
yield for the glyoxylamide, 90% to the free base, mp 71-73°C), and from N-ethyltryptamine
(NET) via alkylation with isopropyl iodide (59% yield, bp 150-160°C at 0.11
mm/Hg).

## Chemical Family Context

EIPT is a member of the mixed N-alkyl [[tryptamine-family-overview]], specifically the
subgroup that maintains one isopropyl group while varying the other N-substituent.
This series was designed to answer a specific pharmacological question: what
is the minimum substitution pattern required to produce DIPT's unique auditory
distortion effects?

The series represents a textbook example of systematic medicinal chemistry,
where a single variable (the identity of one N-alkyl group) is systematically
altered while holding all other [[metzner-shamanic-ceremony-versus-psychotherapy-structural-features]] constant. This approach
allows clean interpretation of the relationship between molecular structure
and pharmacological effect.

EIPT's role as a negative control -- demonstrating that one isopropyl group
is insufficient for auditory effects -- is scientifically valuable despite
the compound's unremarkable subjective profile.

## Detailed Pharmacological Discussion

EIPT acts as a tryptamine psychedelic with activity at the 24-40 mg range
orally, but its effects are notably dysphoric and uncomfortable compared
to more pleasant tryptamines like DMT or MIPT. The absence of auditory
distortions confirms that the unique pharmacology of DIPT requires both
nitrogen substituents to be isopropyl groups.

The "little elephant" effect -- a sensation of bodily heaviness reminiscent
of 5-MeO-DMT -- suggests some overlap with the pharmacological profile
of 5-methoxytryptamines, despite EIPT lacking a 5-methoxy group. This
cross-class similarity may reflect activity at non-5-HT2A serotonin
receptors that contribute to the somatic aspects of the tryptamine
experience.
