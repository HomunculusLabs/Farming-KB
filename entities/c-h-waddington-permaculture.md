# C. H. Waddington

## Description

Conrad Hal Waddington (1905-1975) was a British developmental biologist, geneticist, and philosopher of science cited by [[permaculture]]: A [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-permaculture-design-principles]]* (Chapter 2). Waddington is best known for his concept of epigenetics and for his book *Tools for Thought* (1977, Paladin, UK), which Mollison references as a foundational text for permaculture thinking.
