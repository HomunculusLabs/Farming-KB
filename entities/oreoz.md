## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is dominated by sweet vanilla and chocolate notes, directly evoking the Oreo cookie inspiration.

Secondary notes include earthy undertones, diesel fuel, and a subtle nuttiness on the exhale.

When ground, the scent intensifies, releasing a creamy, dessert-like profile with hints of roasted coffee.

The flavor mirrors the aroma closely, with a sweet cookie-dough inhale and a smooth vanilla-chocolate finish.

Some phenotypes express more diesel and earth, while others lean heavier into the sweet dessert character.

## Terpene Profile

**Caryophyllene** is typically dominant, contributing peppery spice and anti-inflammatory properties.

**Limonene** is the secondary terpene, providing citrus brightness and mood-elevating effects.

**Myrcene** is present in moderate amounts, adding earthy depth and promoting physical relaxation.

**Linalool** and **humulene** are often detected in smaller quantities, rounding out the flavor profile.

The overall terpene composition contributes to both the dessert aroma and the strain's balanced effect profile.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Total THC ranges from 22% to 33% depending on cultivation and phenotype.

CBD content is typically below 0.1%, consistent with modern Cookies-family breeding priorities.

## Effects

Onset is relatively quick, with initial cerebral euphoria manifesting within minutes of inhalation.

The head high is uplifting and creative, often described as happy and slightly giggly.

As the effects progress, a deep body relaxation spreads from the shoulders downward without being overwhelmingly sedative.

Most users report functional relaxation suitable for late afternoon or evening use.
