# WIN55,212-2

**Type:** Chemical / Synthetic Cannabinoid
**Category:** Research cannabinoid (CB1/CB2 agonist)
**Also known as:** WIN-2, WIN55212-2, AMI-193

## Overview
WIN55,212-2 (commonly abbreviated WIN-2) is a synthetic cannabinoid
primarily used in research settings to study the endocannabinoid
system. It functions as an agonist at both CB1 and CB2 cannabinoid
receptors, with a higher binding affinity for CB2 than CB1. WIN55,212-2
is one of the most widely used [[blesching-cannabis-synthetic-cannabinoids]] in preclinical
research and has contributed significantly to understanding cannabinoid
pharmacology.

## Key Details

### Chemical Properties
- Chemical class: Aminoalkylindole
- Binds both CB1 and CB2 cannabinoid receptors
- Higher affinity ("binds more securely") for CB2 receptors than CB1
- Potential CB1 agonist (classified as such in receptor charts)
- Not naturally occurring; purely synthetic compound
- Used exclusively in laboratory and preclinical research settings

### Receptor Pharmacology
WIN55,212-2's preference for CB2 receptors is significant because CB2
activation produces [[cervantes-terpenoid-pharmacology-therapeutic-effects]] (anti-inflammatory, immune
modulation) without the psychoactive effects associated with CB1
activation. This makes CB2-selective agonists particularly attractive
for drug development, as they could provide medical benefits without
intoxication.

### Research Applications
- **Cognitive enhancement and aging:** Columbus University (Ohio)
  researchers discovered that WIN55,212-2 can enhance cognition and
  produce anti-inflammatory effects in older rats. This effect, found
  in animals, has not yet been confirmed in humans. The CB2 preference
  suggests CB2 may be more important for these desirable effects than
  CB1.
- **[[cannabis-and-depression-research]]:** WIN55,212-2 was among the cannabinoids
  tested for antidepressant properties, alongside THC, CBD, CBG, CBN,
  CBC, HU210, and Dronabinol.
- **[[cannabis-cancer-pain-research]]:** Used extensively in pain pathway studies to
  characterize CB1 and CB2 contributions to analgesia.
- **Inflammation research:** Used to study CB2-mediated
  anti-inflammatory mechanisms.
- **Neuroprotection:** Investigated for neuroprotective properties in
  various neurological disease models.

### Context Among Synthetic Cannabinoids
WIN55,212-2 is one of several synthetic cannabinoids used primarily in
research, classified by receptor preference:

**CB1 agonists:**
- CP 55,940 (equally strong at CB1 and CB2)
- HU 210 (primarily CB1)
- HU 239 (ajulemic acid)

**CB1 antagonists:**
- SR 141716A (Rimonabant, withdrawn)
- AM 251

**CB2 agonists:**
- AM 1241, CP 50556-1, JWH-015, JWH-133, JWH-300, JWH-359,
  JWH-361, GW-405,833, WIN55,212-2

**CB2 antagonists:**
- SR144528, AM 630

### Limitations
- Not approved for human therapeutic use
- Effects observed in animal models may not translate to humans
- Lacks the [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]] of whole-plant cannabinoids
- Purely synthetic with no natural plant equivalent
