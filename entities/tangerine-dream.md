## Overview

Tangerine Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for
its vivid citrus aroma and well-balanced effects. Originally bred by
Barney's Farm in Amsterdam, it is a cross of G13, Afghani, and Neville's
A5 Haze. The strain won the 2010 High Times Cannabis Cup in the
Bio category, establishing its reputation as a premium connoisseur
variety.

With THC content typically ranging from 18–25% and low CBD levels
(usually below 0.5%), Tangerine Dream delivers an uplifting cerebral
high that transitions into gentle body relaxation. Its pronounced
tangerine and orange aroma, attributed to high limonene content, makes it
one of the most recognizable citrus-scented cannabis strains available.

## Lineage

Tangerine Dream is a three-way cross combining distinct genetic
backgrounds:

- **G13** — A legendary indica-dominant clone rumored to originate from
  a U.S. government research program, contributing potency and resin
  production.
- **Afghani** — A pure indica landrace from Afghanistan, providing dense
  bud structure, sedative body effects, and hash-making qualities.
- **Neville's A5 Haze** — A sativa-dominant haze selection from Neville
  Schoenmakers, contributing the citrus terpene profile, cerebral
  stimulation, and extended flowering time.

The resulting genetic ratio is approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica,
though phenotypic variation can shift this balance. Some phenotypes lean
more heavily sativa, with taller stature and more energetic effects.

## Appearance and Aroma

Buds are medium to large, dense, and elongated, with a structure that
reflects both indica and [[cannabis-landrace-sativa-heritage-varieties]]. Colors range from bright green
to yellowish-green, often with orange pistils that complement the
tangerine theme. Trichome coverage is heavy, giving flowers a frosted,
resinous appearance.

The aroma is overwhelmingly citrus-forward, dominated by fresh tangerine
and orange peel notes with undertones of earthiness and skunk. When
ground, the scent intensifies, releasing additional notes of sweet tropical
fruit and a subtle diesel edge from the Afghani parentage.

Flavor closely mirrors aroma, with a sweet tangerine taste on inhalation
that gives way to earthy, herbal notes on exhalation. The smoke is smooth
and expansive, making it popular for vaporization as well as combustion.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

The dominant terpenes in Tangerine Dream are:

- **Limonene** (dominant) — responsible for the characteristic citrus
  aroma; contributes mood elevation and stress relief; also found in
  citrus rinds.
- **Myrcene** — earthy, musky notes; enhances sedative effects and
  promotes relaxation; most abundant terpene in cannabis overall.
- **Caryophyllene** — peppery, spicy notes; binds to CB2 receptors;
  adds anti-inflammatory properties.
- **Pinene** — piney freshness; may counteract short-term memory
  impairment associated with THC.

Cannabinoid profile: THC 18–25%, CBD <0.5%, with trace amounts of
CBC, CBG, and THCV in some phenotypes. The relatively high THC and
minimal CBD produce predominantly psychoactive effects without
significant therapeutic CBD modulation.

## Effects and Medical Applications

Onset is relatively rapid, with initial cerebral effects felt within
minutes. The high begins with an uplifting, euphoric rush accompanied
by enhanced creativity, sociability, and sensory awareness. As the
experience progresses, a gentle body relaxation emerges without heavy
sedation, making it functional for daytime use.

Common medical applications include:

- Stress and anxiety relief due to limonene-driven mood elevation
- Mild to moderate depression, where the uplifting effects provide
  temporary respite
- Fatigue management, as the sativa component promotes alertness
- Mild pain and inflammation, supported by caryophyllene's CB2 activity
- Appetite stimulation, consistent with most THC-dominant strains

Side effects may include dry mouth, dry eyes, mild paranoia at higher
doses, and occasional dizziness. The sativa-dominant profile means it is
less likely to cause couch-lock than indica-heavy strains.

## Cultivation

Tangerine Dream has a flowering time of approximately 65–70 days indoors
and is ready for harvest in early to mid-October outdoors. It is
considered moderately difficult to cultivate, requiring attention to
nutrient needs and [[eclss-environmental-control-life-support]] to express its full terpene
potential.

Indoor yields range from 400–500 g/m² under optimal conditions. Plants
grow to a medium height (80–130 cm) with moderate lateral branching. Low-
stress training (LST) and scrog techniques improve yield and light
penetration. The strain responds well to increased calcium and magnesium
during flowering.

Outdoor cultivation is best suited to Mediterranean or warm temperate
climates. Plants are moderately resistant to mold and pests but benefit
from good [[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]] due to dense bud formation. The citrus aroma
can be pungent during late flowering, requiring odor management in
discreet grows.
