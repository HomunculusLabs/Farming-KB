## Overview

**25C-NBOMe** (2C-C-NBOMe, Cimbi-82) is a synthetic psychedelic
phenethylamine and member of the **NBOMe** (N-benzylmethoxy) series. It
functions as a potent, selective **5-HT2A receptor agonist** and is derived
from the 2C [[shulgin-a-pihkal-the-2c-family-of-phenethylamines]] by addition of a 2-methoxybenzyl
substituent on the amine nitrogen.

First synthesized by Ralf Heim at the Free University of Berlin around
2003–2004, 25C-NBOMe emerged on the recreational drug market circa
2010–2012, often sold on blotter paper misrepresented as LSD. Its extreme
potency (active at 100–400 µg) and history of adverse events have made it
one of the most controversial substances in the NBOMe class.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name**: 2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)
  methyl]ethanamine
- **Molecular formula**: C18H22ClNO3
- **Molecular weight**: 337.83 g/mol
- **CAS number**: 1220230-66-9
- **Structure**: Phenethylamine core with methoxy groups at positions 2
  and 5, chlorine at position 4 of the aromatic ring, and an N-
  (2-methoxybenzyl) substituent.

The N-benzyl substitution dramatically increases both 5-HT2A affinity
and metabolic stability compared to the parent compound 2C-C. The 2-
methoxybenzyl group acts as a pharmacophore that optimizes receptor
binding conformation.

## Pharmacology

**Primary mechanism**: Full or near-full agonist at the **5-HT2A receptor**
(Ki ≈ 0.47 nM at cloned human 5-HT2A). This affinity is orders of
magnitude higher than LSD (Ki ≈ 2.5 nM) and comparable to other NBOMe
compounds.

Secondary binding has been reported at 5-HT2C and 5-HT2B receptors,
though with lower affinity. The 5-HT2A agonism is responsible for the
psychedelic effects, while 5-HT2B agonism raises theoretical concerns
