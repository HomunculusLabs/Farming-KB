## Overview

**25C-NBOMe** (2C-C-NBOMe, Cimbi-82) is a synthetic psychedelic
phenethylamine and member of the **NBOMe** (N-benzylmethoxy) series. It
functions as a potent, selective **5-HT2A receptor agonist** and is derived
from the 2C family of phenethylamines by addition of a 2-methoxybenzyl
substituent on the amine nitrogen.

First synthesized by Ralf Heim at the Free University of Berlin around
2003–2004, 25C-NBOMe emerged on the recreational drug market circa
2010–2012, often sold on blotter paper misrepresented as LSD. Its extreme
potency (active at 100–400 µg) and history of adverse events have made it
one of the most controversial substances in the NBOMe class.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name**: 2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)
  methyl]ethanamine
- **Molecular formula**: C18H22ClNO3
- **Molecular weight**: 337.83 g/mol
- **CAS number**: 1220230-66-9
- **Structure**: Phenethylamine core with methoxy groups at positions 2
  and 5, chlorine at position 4 of the aromatic ring, and an N-
  (2-methoxybenzyl) substituent.

The N-benzyl substitution dramatically increases both 5-HT2A affinity
and metabolic stability compared to the parent compound 2C-C. The 2-
methoxybenzyl group acts as a pharmacophore that optimizes receptor
binding conformation.

## Pharmacology

**Primary mechanism**: Full or near-full agonist at the **5-HT2A receptor**
(Ki ≈ 0.47 nM at cloned human 5-HT2A). This affinity is orders of
magnitude higher than LSD (Ki ≈ 2.5 nM) and comparable to other NBOMe
compounds.

Secondary binding has been reported at 5-HT2C and 5-HT2B receptors,
though with lower affinity. The 5-HT2A agonism is responsible for the
psychedelic effects, while 5-HT2B agonism raises theoretical concerns
about valvular heart disease with chronic exposure.

The compound shows negligible activity at dopaminergic, adrenergic, and
muscarinic receptors at typical doses, contributing to its relatively
"clean" receptor profile compared to classical psychedelics.

## Effects

- **Threshold**: 50–100 µg
- **Light dose**: 100–200 µg
- **Common dose**: 200–400 µg
- **Strong dose**: 400–600 µg

Duration: 6–10 hours, with onset at 20–60 minutes (oral/sublingual),
peak effects at 2–4 hours, and gradual comedown over 3–5 hours. Buccal
or sublingual absorption is more reliable than oral due to extensive
first-pass metabolism.

Effects include visual hallucinations (geometric patterns, color
enhancement, texture distortion), altered perception of time, enhanced
emotional responsiveness, and mild cognitive shifts. Compared to 25I-
NBOMe, 25C-NBOMe is generally described as less stimulating and more
introspective, with a warmer emotional quality.

## Risks and Adverse Effects

25C-NBOMe has been implicated in numerous hospitalizations and deaths,
primarily from:

- **Severe serotonin syndrome** when combined with other serotonergic
  substances (SSRIs, MAOIs, MDMA, other psychedelics).
- **Cardiovascular toxicity**: tachycardia, hypertension, and in rare
  cases, cardiac arrest. Vasoconstriction is a known pharmacological
  effect of 5-HT2A agonism.
- **Seizures**: Reported at high doses or in susceptible individuals.
- **Confusion with LSD**: Many adverse events result from users
  consuming multiple tabs believing they are LSD, leading to massive
  overdose (25C-NBOMe is active at doses 10–30× lower than LSD).

Harm reduction practices include using reagent tests (Ehrlich's
reagent distinguishes LSD from NBOMe compounds), starting with very
low doses with volumetric dosing, and never combining with other
serotonergic drugs.

## Legal Status

Controlled or scheduled in numerous jurisdictions. In the United States,
it is a Schedule I substance under the Federal Analog Act. The UK
classified all NBOMe compounds as Class A drugs in 2014. Many other
countries (Australia, New Zealand, Germany, Sweden) have placed it
under emergency scheduling or permanent control.

## Related Compounds

- **25I-NBOMe**: The most widely used NBOMe compound; more stimulating
  and visual than 25C.
- **25B-NBOMe**: Intermediate in character between 25I and 25C.
- **2C-C**: The parent phenethylamine; much less potent (active at 20–40
  mg oral) and shorter in duration.
- **LSD**: Often sold as counterfeit LSD on blotter paper; key
  pharmacological and dose differences distinguish the two.
