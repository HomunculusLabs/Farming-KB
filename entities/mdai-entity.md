## MDAI — 5,6-Methylenedioxy-2-aminoindane

MDAI is a synthetic phenethylamine analogue developed as a non-neurotoxic entactogen substitute for MDMA. It is not a [[tihkal-oral-activity-mao-inhibition]]: none

Reported effects include:
- Mild to moderate entactogenic effects similar to MDMA but notably gentler
- Increased sociability and empathy
- Mild euphoria
- Enhanced music appreciation
- Less pronounced stimulant effects compared to MDMA
- Generally described as "smoother" and less intense than MDMA

## Pharmacology

MDAI was rationally designed as a non-neurotoxic alternative to MDMA. The indane ring constrains the molecule, preventing the formation of [[mdma-serotonergic-neurotoxicity]]. The mechanism of action is primarily through serotonin transporter (SERT) activation — it acts as a serotonin-releasing agent and reuptake inhibitor.

In animal studies, MDAI showed:
- Potent serotonin release with ED50 values in the low micromolar range
- Minimal dopamine release
- No observable serotonergic neurotoxicity at recreational doses
- No hyperthermia (a dangerous side effect of MDMA)

## History

MDAI was first synthesized by David E. Nichols and colleagues at Purdue University during research into the structural requirements for serotonin release. The goal was to identify compounds that produced MDMA-like entactogenic effects without the neurotoxic and hyperthermic liabilities.

The compound gained popularity as a "legal high" in the late 2000s and early 2010s, particularly in the UK and parts of Europe, before being scheduled as a controlled substance.

## Synthesis

The synthesis typically involves construction of the indane core with appropriate substitution. Starting from piperonal (heliotropin), the route proceeds through a Wittig or Grignard reaction to build the indane skeleton, followed by introduction of the amino group. The exact synthetic route varies in the literature.

## Cautionary Notes

While MDAI was designed to avoid MDMA's neurotoxicity, the absence of long-term human clinical studies means safety is not fully established. Anecdotal reports suggest it is well-tolerated but can produce:
- Mild physical stimulation
- Potential for tachycardia at higher doses
- Post-experience fatigue
- The usual risks associated with unsupervised use of research chemicals

MDAI is a controlled substance in many jurisdictions.
