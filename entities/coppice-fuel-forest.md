# Coppice Fuel Forest

## Description

Sustainable fuel production system for desert settlements using closely-spaced
trees on 4-6 year rotational cutting cycle. Trees at 2-3m spacing on trickle
irrigation, harvested as coppice, regrow from stumps. Combined with perpetual
shelter forest and anaerobic waste processing, provides all essential cooking
and public energy needs.

## Classification

- **Category**: Fuel production system
- **Domain**: Desert forestry, renewable energy, sustainable settlement
- **Rotation**: 4-6 years
- **Tree spacing**: 2-3 meters

## System Design

### Coppice Production Grid
- 2-3m spacing on trickle irrigation pipes
- Preferably automatic irrigation
- 4-6 year rotation harvested as coppice
- One-fourth to one-sixth cut annually

### Perpetual Forest Grid
- Max 300m apart, min 8 trees wide
- Initially trickle-irrigated
- Hardy on rainfall alone once established
- Provides shelter for coppice area

### Waste Integration
- Anaerobic settling pond for methane
- Organic waste shredded and fermented
- Chain: alcohol fuels → biogas → soil amendment
- Clear water routed to fuel forest

## Species Selection

### Criteria
- Vigorous regrowth from cut stump
- Dense wood, high calorific value
- Arid condition adaptation
- Wastewater irrigation tolerance
- Good coppice over many rotations

### Process
- Initially select coppice types
- Continuing field selection for best varieties
- Ongoing improvement through selective seed harvest

## Key Facts

- 2-3m spacing on trickle irrigation
- 4-6 year rotation cycle
- One-fourth to one-sixth cut annually
- Perpetual shelter grid max 300m apart
- Anaerobic ponds for methane fuel
- Organic waste chain: alcohol → biogas → soil
- All fuel needs met from managed system

## Where No Sewage Available

Close-spaced swales (4-8m wide, 3-5m banks between) supplied by interceptor
banks harvesting broadscale runoff support fuel forest without sewage.

## Relevance to Cultivation

Energy backbone of sustainable desert settlement. Demonstrates closing resource
loops: waste → irrigation → trees → fuel → energy → soil. Rotational harvest
ensures perpetual renewable energy while shelter protects settlement.
