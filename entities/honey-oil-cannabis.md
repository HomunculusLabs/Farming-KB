## Description

Honey oil (also called translucent oil or amber oil) is one of the most potent and visually distinctive forms of cannabis extract. It is a thick, translucent, amber-colored oil with the appearance and consistency of dark honey. The translucent quality is achieved by removing colored impurities from petroleum-ether-purified [[cannabis-alchemy-book]]*.

## Classification

- **Category:** Cannabis extract / concentrate
- **Appearance:** Thick, translucent, amber oil
- **Consistency:** Similar to dark honey
- **Potency:** Among the most potent of traditional cannabis oil preparations
- **Origin material:** Traditionally extracted from Afghanistan hashish

## Key Facts

1. **Translucent appearance:** The defining characteristic is its clarity — achieved by removing all colored impurities (chlorophyll, tars, plant pigments) through activated charcoal filtration.
2. **Activated charcoal treatment:** Granulated activated charcoal (Norit brand specified) is added at half the weight of the oil to the oil/alcohol solution. The charcoal adsorbs colored impurities.
3. **Filtration:** The charcoal/oil/alcohol mixture is filtered through fine filter paper to remove the charcoal along with adsorbed impurities.
4. **Alcohol evaporation:** After filtration, the alcohol is evaporated to yield the translucent amber oil.
5. **[[cannabinoid-isomerization-and-thc-acetate]]) before or after the honey oil process.

## Production Process

1. Begin with cannabis oil that has been purified by re-extraction with petroleum ether.
2. Dissolve the purified oil in 10x its volume of pure alcohol.
3. Add granulated activated charcoal (Norit) equal to half the weight of the oil.
4. Mix thoroughly to allow charcoal to adsorb colored impurities.
5. Filter through fine filter paper to remove charcoal.
6. Evaporate the alcohol by distillation or evaporation apparatus.
7. Collect the translucent amber residue — this is the honey oil.

## Historical and Cultural Context

- Honey oil was historically produced from Afghanistan hashish, one of the finest traditional hashish varieties.
- The name reflects the visual similarity to natural honey — thick, amber, translucent.
- It represents the apex of traditional cannabis oil refinement before modern techniques like supercritical CO₂ extraction and BHO (butane hash oil) became common.
- In the modern era, "honey oil" is sometimes used colloquially for various high-quality cannabis extracts, though the traditional product specifically refers to the charcoal-filtered translucent oil.

## Variants and Modern Equivalents

- **Modern BHO/PHO:** Butane or propane hash oil can achieve similar clarity and potency.
- **Distillate:** Modern short-path distillation produces even more refined products.
- **Rosin:** Heat-pressed solventless extracts are now preferred by many for purity.

## Relevance to Cultivation / Mycology

Honey oil is a post-harvest product with no direct cultivation relevance. However, understanding the relationship between starting material quality and final product quality is important for cultivators. High-quality, high-cannabinoid starting material (whether flower or hashish) directly determines the potency and yield of the refined oil.
