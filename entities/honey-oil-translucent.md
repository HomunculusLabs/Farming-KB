# Honey Oil (Translucent Cannabis Oil)

## Description

Honey oil, also called translucent oil or amber oil, is a highly refined cannabis oil preparation characterized by its thick, translucent, amber appearance and consistency similar to dark honey. It is one of the most potent and visually appealing forms of cannabis extract, traditionally produced from Afghanistan hashish. The translucent quality is achieved by removing colored impurities from petroleum ether-purified cannabis oil through activated charcoal filtration.

## Classification

- **Category**: [[spagyric-cannabis-preparation]] / Refined extract
- **Appearance**: Thick, translucent, amber-colored oil
- **Consistency**: Similar to dark honey
- **Potency**: Very high (concentrated cannabinoids)

## Key Facts

- The translucent appearance distinguishes honey oil from opaque or dark-colored crude cannabis extracts, indicating a higher degree of purification.
- Produced by dissolving purified cannabis oil (which may or may not be isomerized or acetylated) in ten times its volume of pure alcohol.
- Granulated activated charcoal (Norit brand specified in source material) equal to half the weight of the oil is added to the solution to adsorb colored impurities.
- After charcoal treatment, the solution is filtered through fine filter paper to remove the charcoal and adsorbed impurities.
- The alcohol is then removed by evaporation, leaving behind a translucent amber oil with the appearance and consistency of dark honey.
- Originally and most classically produced from Afghanistan hashish, which is particularly suitable due to its resin quality.
- May be further enhanced through isomerization (converting CBD to THC) and acetylation (converting THC to THC acetate) before or after the charcoal filtration step.
- Popular both for its potency and its aesthetic appeal — the translucent amber appearance is considered a mark of quality in cannabis oil preparations.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The principles of honey oil production apply to fungal extract refinement:

- **Activated charcoal filtration**: The same Norit-type activated charcoal used to clarify cannabis oil is used in mycological laboratories to purify enzyme solutions, remove pigments from fungal extracts, and decolorize culture media.
- **Mushroom extract refinement**: Dual-extraction processes for [[cancer-medicinal-mushrooms]] (water + alcohol) can benefit from charcoal filtration to remove unwanted pigments while preserving bioactive polysaccharides and triterpenes.
- **Quality indicator concept**: Just as translucent appearance indicates purity in cannabis oil, clarity and color are quality indicators in mushroom tinctures and extracts.
- **[[pf-tek-alcohol-extraction-method]] of fungal compounds**: The alcohol dissolution step used in honey oil production parallels the alcohol extraction phase of medicinal mushroom [[cannabis-reefers-and-tincture-preparation]].

## Production Protocol

1. Begin with cannabis oil that has been purified by petroleum ether extraction.
2. Dissolve the oil in ten times its volume of pure alcohol.
3. Add granulated activated charcoal (Norit) equal to half the weight of the oil.
4. Stir or agitate to ensure complete contact between charcoal and solution.
5. Filter through fine filter paper to remove charcoal and adsorbed impurities.
6. Evaporate the alcohol using a water bath at controlled temperature.
7. Collect the resulting translucent amber oil — the honey oil.

## Variants

- **Non-isomerized honey oil**: Purified but not chemically altered; potency depends entirely on [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]] quality.
- **Isomerized honey oil**: CBD converted to THC before or after purification, increasing potency significantly.
- **Acetylated honey oil**: THC converted to THC acetate, roughly doubling potency. This is the most potent form.
- **Unfiltered**: Some producers skip the charcoal step, resulting in darker, more strongly flavored oil that retains more of the plant's [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].
