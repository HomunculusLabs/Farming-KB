## Honey Oil (Translucent Cannabis Oil)

## Description

Honey oil, also called translucent oil or amber oil, is a highly refined cannabis oil preparation characterized by its thick, translucent, amber appearance and consistency similar to dark honey. It is one of the most potent and visually appealing forms of cannabis extract, traditionally produced from Afghanistan hashish. The translucent quality is achieved by removing colored impurities from petroleum ether-purified cannabis oil through activated charcoal filtration.

## Classification

- **Category**: [[medicinal-mushrooms]] (water + alcohol) can benefit from charcoal filtration to remove unwanted pigments while preserving bioactive polysaccharides and triterpenes.
- **Quality indicator concept**: Just as translucent appearance indicates purity in cannabis oil, clarity and color are quality indicators in mushroom tinctures and extracts.
- **[[cannabis-reefers-and-tincture-preparation]].

## Production Protocol

1. Begin with cannabis oil that has been purified by petroleum ether extraction.
2. Dissolve the oil in ten times its volume of pure alcohol.
3. Add granulated activated charcoal (Norit) equal to half the weight of the oil.
4. Stir or agitate to ensure complete contact between charcoal and solution.
5. Filter through fine filter paper to remove charcoal and adsorbed impurities.
6. Evaporate the alcohol using a water bath at controlled temperature.
7. Collect the resulting translucent amber oil — the honey oil.

## Variants

- **Non-isomerized honey oil**: Purified but not chemically altered; potency depends entirely on [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].
