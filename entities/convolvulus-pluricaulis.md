## Convolvulus pluricaulis

## Overview

**Convolvulus pluricaulis** Choisy, commonly known as **Shankhpushpi**, is a
small perennial herbaceous plant in the family Convolvulaceae. It is one of the
most important *medhya rasayana* (brain tonic) herbs in the Ayurvedic
pharmacopoeia, where it has been used for centuries to enhance memory,
cognition, and mental clarity.

The plant is native to the Indian subcontinent and is found throughout the
plains and foothills of northern and central India, as well as in parts of
Myanmar and Sri Lanka.

## Taxonomy and Morphology

**Classification:** Kingdom Plantae → Division Tracheophyta → Class
Magnoliopsida → Order Solanales → Family Convolvulaceae → Genus *Convolvulus*
→ *C. pluricaulis*.

**Synonyms:** *Convolvulus microphyllus* Sieb. ex Spreng., *Convolvulus
pluricaulis* var. *microphyllus*.

The plant is a prostrate or ascending herb with slender, spreading stems 15–40
cm long. The stems are filiform, hairy, and root at the nodes where they
contact soil, forming dense mats.

Leaves are small, oblong-elliptic to lanceolate, 0.8–2.5 cm long and 0.3–1
cm wide, with entire margins and acute apices. Both surfaces are sparsely
pubescent.

Flowers are solitary or in few-flowered cymes, borne in leaf axils. The calyx
has 5 sepals; the corolla is funnel-shaped, 6–12 mm long, white to pale pink
or pale blue with a darker throat. Flowering occurs during the monsoon season
(July–October).

The fruit is a globose capsule, approximately 4 mm in diameter, containing
1–4 smooth, brown seeds.

## Distribution and Habitat

*C. pluricaulis* is distributed across the Indian subcontinent, from the
Indo-Gangetic plains to the Deccan plateau, at elevations up to 1,500 m. It
also occurs in Myanmar, Nepal, and Sri Lanka.

The plant grows as a weed in cultivated fields, grasslands, and along
roadsides. It prefers sandy or loamy soils with good drainage and thrives in
open, sunny locations with moderate rainfall.

It is commonly found in dry deciduous forests and wastelands, often forming
extensive [[bacopa-monnieri]]*
(Brahmi), *[[ashwagandha-withania-somnifera]]*
(Ashwagandha).
