## Efren Cazares (Mycorrhizal Researcher)

## Description

Dr. [[efren-cazares]] is a mycological researcher who trained under Dr. [[jim-trappe]] at Oregon State University as a graduate student and post-doctoral researcher. He specializes in the identification and measurement of vesicular-arbuscular mycorrhizae (VAM) and [[ectomycorrhizal-fungi]]. Dr. Cazares played a critical role in bridging academic mycorrhizal research with practical soil biology assessment by training [[soil-foodweb-institute]] (SFI) technicians in mycorrhizal identification and measurement protocols.

- **Type**: Person / Researcher

- **Role**: Graduate student / post-doctoral researcher under Jim Trappe

## Key Facts

- Trained directly under Dr. Jim Trappe, the leading authority on ectomycorrhizal fungi at Oregon State University.
- Performed VAM and ectomycorrhizal sample analysis for [[soil-foodweb-institute-sfi]] (SFI), providing expert-level mycorrhizal identification.
- Trained SFI technicians to perform identification and measurement of both VAM and ectomycorrhizal colonization in soil and root samples.
- His work enabled SFI to offer mycorrhizal [[am-fungi-autofluorescence-colonization-assessment]] as part of their comprehensive soil biology analysis, differentiating VAM from ectomycorrhizal and ericoid colonization types.
- The training and protocols he established allowed SFI to move beyond simple presence/absence assessment to quantitative measurement of colonization percentages.
- His contributions helped establish the threshold data showing that VAM colonization below 40% correlates with root disease and root-feeding nematode problems.
- SFI technicians trained by Cazares can also identify disease fungi in roots and warn what percentage of root systems show unknown fungi or necrotic conditions.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Diagnostic capacity**: Cazares's training of SFI technicians made mycorrhizal assessment commercially available to farmers and growers, not just academic researchers.
- **Threshold development**: The [[mycorrhizal-colonization-thresholds]] (below 15% requires inoculation, 15-40% add compost or humic acids, above 40% generally healthy) used by SFI were developed with his input.
- **[[mycorrhizal-inoculation-guide]] guidance**: Accurate identification enables targeted inoculation recommendations, letting growers learn which mycorrhizal type they need (VAM, ecto, or ericoid) and at what rate.
- **[[accessible-mushroom-cultivation-for-disabilities]]**: For cultivators working with ectomycorrhizal species, understanding colonization assessment protocols derived from Cazares's work is essential for monitoring symbiosis establishment.
- **Quality assurance**: His role in training technicians ensured that SFI mycorrhizal assessments met academic standards of accuracy and reproducibility.
