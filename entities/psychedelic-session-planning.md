## Psychedelic Session Planning

## Description

Psychedelic session planning refers to the systematic preparation for a psychedelic experience as described by Timothy Leary in *The Psychedelic Experience* (1964). Leary provides detailed guidance on goals, drug dosages, set (mindset), setting (environment), the role of the guide, and group composition. These recommendations are among the earliest formalized protocols for structured psychedelic use and directly prefigure modern concepts of "set and setting" used in psychedelic-assisted therapy.

## Classification

- **Category**: Psychedelic Practice / Protocol
- **Historical Period**: 1960s Psychedelic Research
- **Related Practices**: Set and Setting, Integration, Trip Sitting

## Key Facts

### Four Classic Goals (from Hindu tradition)

1. **Personal Power** — increased intellectual understanding, sharpened insight, accelerated learning, professional growth
2. **Duty** — help of others, rehabilitation, rebirth for fellow beings
3. **Fun** — sensuous enjoyment, aesthetic pleasure, interpersonal closeness, pure experience
4. **Transcendence** — liberation from ego and space-time limits, mystical union

### Drug Dosages (from 1964 text)

| Substance | Full Dose (transcendental) | Lower Dose (experienced/groups) |
|-----------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|
| LSD-25 (lysergic acid diethylamide) | 200-500 mcg | 100-200 mcg |
| Mescaline | 600-800 mg | 300-500 mg |
| Psilocybin | 40-60 mg | 20-30 mg |
| DMT (intramuscular) | 50-60 mg | ~30 min experience equivalent to 500 mcg LSD |

### Timing

- **Onset**: LSD/psilocybin 20-30 min; Mescaline 1-2 hours
- **Duration**: LSD/mescaline 8-10 hours; Psilocybin 5-6 hours; DMT ~30 minutes
- **Recommended schedule**: 3 days total (day before, session day, follow-up day)

### Set (Mindset)

- **Long-range set**: personality history, fears, desires, defenses, flexibility vs. rigidity
- **Immediate set**: expectations about the session itself
- Pitfalls: medical model expectations, rebellion motive, premature intellectualization, religious expectations, recreational attachment
- Best preparation: meditation practice, talking to experienced voyagers, reading accounts (Huxley, Watts, Wasson)

### Setting (Environment)

- Removed from usual social games and interpersonal dynamics
- Free from unforeseen distractions and intrusions
- Trust in surroundings and privacy are essential
- Consider: music, lighting, food/drink availability
- Night sessions often reported as deeper; natural settings have specific influences
- Indoors: consider room arrangement, symbolic objects, mattresses in star patterns

### Role of the Guide

- Must turn off own ego and social games — "creative quietism"
- Never bored, talkative, or intellectualizing
- Analogized to "ground control at the airport tower" — always available, never imposing
- Should have considerable personal psychedelic experience
- The greatest problem is fear — almost every negative reaction traced to guide fear communicating to subject
- Recommended: one guide takes low dose with subject, one stays sober for practical matters
- Guides should not take large doses with new subjects (risk of being pulled into subject"s hallucinatory field)

### Group Composition

- Small groups preferred initially
- Groups exceeding 6-7 people are "demonstrably less profound" and generate more paranoid hallucinations
- For 5-6 people: at least 2 guides recommended
- An experienced female guide can provide spiritual nurturing and comfort
- Married couples may benefit from separate initial sessions to avoid marriage-game dominance

### Adjunct Medications Mentioned

- **For anxiety**: Librium (chlordiazepoxide) 30-40 mg pre-session
- **For mood**: Methedrine (methamphetamine) for euphoric priming
- **For nausea**: Marezine, Bonamine, Dramamine, or Tigan (especially with peyote/morning-glory seeds)
- **For set-breaking**: DMT 50 mg or Dexedrine/Methedrine 25 mg to "break the set" of repetitive loops
- **Emergency termination**: Thorazine (chlorpromazine) 100-200 mg IM — emphasized that antidotes should NOT be used merely because of fear

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- Leary"s psilocybin dosage range (40-60 mg synthetic psilocybin) corresponds to approximately 4-6 grams of dried Psilocybe cubensis mushrooms, consistent with modern heavy-dose protocols
- The three-day scheduling recommendation (preparation, session, integration) is now standard in clinical psychedelic research at institutions like Johns Hopkins
- The guide-as-ground-control model directly influenced the modern "trip sitting" paradigm and clinical monitor protocols
- The warning about guide fear causing negative reactions has been validated by modern research on the importance of therapist confidence and equanimity
- The recommendation against groups larger than 6-7 has been borne out by clinical practice — intimate settings produce better outcomes
- Adjunct anti-nausea medication remains relevant for peyote and morning glory seeds, and modern alternatives like ondansetron are now available
