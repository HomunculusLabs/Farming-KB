# 3-TM (Thiomescaline; 3,4-Dimethoxy-5-methylthiophenethylamine)

3-TM, also known as thiomescaline, is the sulfur analogue of [[mescaline-entity]] (at the 4-position) with a sulfur atom.

The prefix "thio" means a sulfur-in-place-of-an-oxygen, with everything else left alone.

The name 4-TM is also used in PIHKAL, indicating the position of the sulfur relative to the methoxy groups.

## Pharmacological Profile

3-TM is a highly potent psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 20 to 40 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 10 to 15 hours.

This represents a dramatic potency increase over [[mescaline-entity]] is one of the oldest known psychedelic phenethylamines, requiring 200-400 mg doses.

3-TM achieves similar or greater effects at 20-40 mg, a roughly tenfold increase in potency.

The duration is also extended compared to mescaline.

The commentary suggests that if the chemistry of mescaline is to be more carefully explored, it must be with the shortest possible sulfur chain (TM) or with very long chains.

Intermediate chain lengths tend to show the least interesting pharmacology.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 20 - 40 mg.

Duration: 10 - 15 h.

## Safety Considerations

The 10-15 hour duration requires significant time commitment and planning.

Physical side effects including body tremor and reduced urine output are noted at higher doses.

The potency increase over mescaline means careful dosing is essential.
