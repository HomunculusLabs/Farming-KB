# 3-TM (Thiomescaline; 3,4-Dimethoxy-5-methylthiophenethylamine)

3-TM, also known as thiomescaline, is the sulfur analogue of [[mescaline-entity]] in which the central oxygen atom at the 4-position is replaced by a sulfur atom. It is dramatically more potent than mescaline while maintaining the complexity of its effects. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #156.

## Chemical Classification

3-TM belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 3,4-dimethoxy-5-methylthiophenethylamine, also known as thiomescaline.

The compound is generated by replacing the central oxygen atom of [[mescaline-entity]] (at the 4-position) with a sulfur atom.

The prefix "thio" means a sulfur-in-place-of-an-oxygen, with everything else left alone.

The name 4-TM is also used in PIHKAL, indicating the position of the sulfur relative to the methoxy groups.

## Pharmacological Profile

3-TM is a highly potent psychedelic compound with a dosage range of 20 to 40 milligrams.

The duration of effects is 10 to 15 hours.

This represents a dramatic potency increase over [[mescaline-entity]], which requires 200-400 mg -- approximately a 10-fold increase.

The potency increase is achieved without any apparent decrease in complexity of action.

The compound is one of the most successful thio-analogues in terms of preserving the qualitative character of the parent.

## Qualitative Effects

At 25 mg, subjects first became aware of effects while sitting down, feeling light-headed with unsteady steps.

At 30 mg, subjects were reminded of LSD in the quality of the experience, with deep personal content emerging.

The compound facilitated difficult personal conversations and inward exploration.

Walking and talking were possible without difficulty even during the onset.

Going inward produced sensations that were described as emotionally deep but manageable.

The next-day experience included vivid dreams and residual effects.

At 40 mg, subjects experienced initial physical concern -- not nausea but a generalized uneasiness with distinct body tremor.

Reduced urine production was noted.

Eyes-closed imagery was described as very impressive.

Thought processes became difficult to manage at this higher dose.

A reality distortion was described as similar to a Mobius strip -- continuous yet with no consistent side presented.

## The Chemistry of Sulfur Substitution

The concept of thio-analogues exploits one of the "vertical arrows" of molecular modification.

In organic chemistry, one can replace oxygen atoms with sulfur atoms to create spatially similar analogues.

The term "thio" is simple: it means sulfur-in-place-of-an-oxygen, with everything else left unchanged.

Every "oxy" compound can theoretically have a "thio" analogue.

This is described as "a little awe-inspiring" considering the vast number of oxygen-containing compounds in biology.

The sulfur atom is slightly larger than oxygen, and slightly less polar, which accounts for the pharmacological differences.

## Comparison with Mescaline

[[mescaline-entity]] is one of the oldest known psychedelic phenethylamines, requiring 200-400 mg doses.

3-TM achieves similar or greater effects at 20-40 mg, a roughly tenfold increase in potency.

The duration is also extended compared to mescaline.

The commentary suggests that if the chemistry of mescaline is to be more carefully explored, it must be with the shortest possible sulfur chain (TM) or with very long chains.

Intermediate chain lengths tend to show the least interesting pharmacology.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 20 - 40 mg.

Duration: 10 - 15 h.

## Safety Considerations

The 10-15 hour duration requires significant time commitment and planning.

Physical side effects including body tremor and reduced urine output are noted at higher doses.

The potency increase over mescaline means careful dosing is essential.
