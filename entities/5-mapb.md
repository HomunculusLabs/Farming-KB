# 5-MAPB

## Overview

**5-MAPB** (1-(1-benzofuran-5-yl)-N-methylpropan-2-amine) is a synthetic **benzofuran**
compound with entactogen and empathogen effects, structurally and pharmacologically related
to **MDMA** and **6-APB** (benzofury). It acts primarily as a **[[6-mapb]]** — N-methylated 6-APB analogue; reportedly longer duration.
- **[[psilocin]] — the primary entactogen to which 5-MAPB is compared
- [[psilocin]] — natural serotonergic psychedelic compound
