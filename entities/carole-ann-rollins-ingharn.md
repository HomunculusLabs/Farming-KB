## Description

Dr. Carole Ann Rollins is a soil microbiology researcher and editor who compiled and edited *The Field Guide I for Actively Aerated Compost Tea (AACT)*, published in 2004. She worked closely with Dr. Elaine Ingham to organize years of internet correspondence, field data, and application protocols into a practical reference for the emerging AACT industry.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person / Editor / Researcher
- **Domain**: Soil microbiology, compost tea, sustainable agriculture
- **Role**: Compiler and editor of AACT Field Guide
- **Location**: Novato, California

## Key Facts

- Compiled and edited *The Field Guide I for Actively Aerated Compost Tea (AACT)* from Dr. Elaine Ingham's extensive internet correspondence spanning April 2001 to June 2003.
- Motivated by a desire to "give back to a leader of our industry" — recognizing Ingham's tireless mentoring of practitioners.
- Identified the need for a portable field reference that AACT practitioners could use when working with clients unfamiliar with compost tea benefits.
- Created standardized application rate sheets for a variety of industries, addressing a gap in the field where specific information about application rates was lacking.
- Developed a Test Usage Form for interviewing new clients to standardize data collection.
- Created Data Collection Research Project forms for comparing conventional practices with AACT approaches across industries.
- Acknowledged collaborators including Caprielle Lewis and Gladys Rollins (proofreading), Laurie Keit and Scott Schiable (data collection sheets), and James Eddington (support).
- Based in Novato, California during the compilation period (March 2004).
- Her work demonstrates the importance of knowledge consolidation in emerging fields — transforming scattered practitioner knowledge into accessible reference materials.

## Editorial Methodology

Rollins' approach to compiling the Field Guide involved sorting thousands of email messages and forum posts from Ingham into thematic chapters, verifying technical claims against available research, and organizing practical protocols into step-by-step formats. She maintained Ingham's voice and direct communication style while adding structural coherence to what had been scattered correspondence.

## Contributions to AACT Standardization

Beyond compilation, Rollins contributed original standardized forms and data collection templates that professionalized the AACT consulting process. Her Test Usage Form provided a systematic intake process for new clients, while her Data Collection Research Project forms enabled rigorous comparison between conventional and AACT-based approaches.

## Professional Background

Rollins' academic background in soil microbiology equipped her to understand and accurately represent the technical content of Ingham's correspondence. Her location in Novato, California placed her in the same geographic area as Nature Technologies International, facilitating the co-publishing arrangement.

## Impact on the Field

The Field Guide that Rollins compiled became a foundational reference for the emerging AACT industry, providing practitioners with the first comprehensive portable reference for compost tea biology, brewing, and application. Her work in organizing and standardizing this knowledge base accelerated the professionalization of biological soil consulting.
