# Amnesia Haze

**Amnesia Haze** is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its potent cerebral effects and complex genetic lineage. A staple of European coffeeshop culture, particularly in Amsterdam, it has earned multiple Cannabis Cup awards and maintains a dedicated following among recreational and medical users seeking strong mental stimulation, creativity enhancement, and mood elevation. Despite its name, the strain does not cause amnesia — the moniker refers to its reputation for producing such an immersive, euphoric experience that users temporarily forget their surroundings.

## Genetic Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Sativa-dominant hybrid |
| **Ratio** | ~80% sativa / 20% indica |
| **Lineage** | Southeast Asian × Jamaican × Hawaiian × Afghan (complex haze hybrid) |
| **Breeder** | Disputed — variously attributed to First Gear Seeds, SSSC, and Dutch Coffeeshop breeders |
| **Origin** | Netherlands / California (early 2000s) |
| **THC content** | 20–25% (some phenotypes exceed 27%) |
| **CBD content** | < 0.1% |
| **Terpene profile** | Limonene-dominant with myrcene and caryophyllene |

## Origin & History

Amnesia Haze emerged from the competitive Dutch breeding scene of the early 2000s, a period when "Haze" genetics — originally developed in California in the 1960s and 1970s — were being refined by European seed companies. The strain is believed to be a complex polyhybrid combining landrace sativas from Southeast Asia, Jamaica, and Hawaii with an Afghan indica to reduce flowering time and add resin production.

The strain gained legendary status after winning the **High Times Cannabis Cup** in 2004 (Sativa category) and again in 2012, cementing its reputation as one of the finest sativa-dominant varieties available. It became the most popular strain in Amsterdam coffeeshops during the 2010s, frequently requested by name by tourists and locals alike.

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene** (dominant): Bright citrus, lemon, orange peel. Anxiolytic, mood-elevating, and anti-depressant properties. Enhances absorption of other terpenes and cannabinoids via transdermal and inhalation routes. Promotes focus and creative energy.
- **Myrcene**: Earthy, herbal, slightly fruity. Contributes to the relaxing body component and enhances cannabinoid receptor binding. Anti-inflammatory and analgesic.
- **Caryophyllene**: Peppery, spicy, woody. Selective CB2 receptor agonist — unique among terpenes for direct endocannabinoid system interaction. Anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and gastroprotective.
- **Terpinolene** (trace): Piney, floral, slightly herbal. Sedative at higher concentrations; may counterbalance limonene's stimulating effects.
- **Ocimene** (trace): Sweet, herbal, woody. May contribute to decongestant effects and uplift.

## Effects

### Primary Effects
- **Cerebral:** Powerful, fast-acting head high characterized by intense euphoria, racing creative thoughts, enhanced sensory perception, and a sense of mental expansion. Many users report a "giddy" quality to the onset.
- **Physical:** Light, energetic body sensation that is activating rather than sedating. Users typically remain functional and sociable.
- **Social:** Enhanced conversation flow, laughter, and a sense of shared enjoyment in group settings.

### Medical Applications
- Treatment-resistant depression
- Chronic fatigue and daytime lethargy
- ADHD symptom management (paradoxical focusing effect)
- Stress and anxiety (at moderate doses)
- Migraine headaches
- Mild to moderate pain (anti-inflammatory via terpenes)
- PTSD (mood elevation and present-moment awareness)

### Onset & Duration
Effects onset within 5–15 minutes when smoked or vaped. Peak cerebral effects at 30–60 minutes. Duration typically 2–4 hours for smoked flower. The experience is characterized by a clear onset and relatively gradual comedown without significant crash or lethargy.

### Negative Effects
- Dry mouth (very common)
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or paranoia at high doses — this is a potent strain, and overconsumption can produce uncomfortable racing thoughts
- Dizziness in sensitive individuals
- Insomnia if consumed late in the day (stimulating effects can persist)
- Elevated heart rate (tachycardia), typical of sativa-dominant strains

## Cultivation

Amnesia Haze is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, primarily due to its long flowering time and tall stature. It rewards experienced growers with high-quality, resinous buds but demands patience and adequate space.

### Growth Characteristics

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Flowering time** | 10–12 weeks (can extend to 14 weeks in some phenotypes) |
| **Yield (indoor)** | 21–25 oz/m² |
| **Yield (outdoor)** | 25–35 oz/plant |
| **Height** | Very tall — 150–200+ cm indoors; can exceed 3 m outdoors |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to advanced |
| **Resistance** | Moderate mold resistance; good pest resistance |

### Growing Tips
- **Training essential:** Without training (LST, SCROG, or topping), the plant will rapidly outgrow indoor spaces. Begin training during weeks 2–4 of vegetative growth.
- **Vegetative period:** Keep short (2–3 weeks) to manage final height, unless growing outdoors or in high-ceiling environments.
- **Nutrient requirements:** Moderate to heavy feeder during vegetative stage. Reduce nitrogen during flowering weeks 8–10 for better terpene development.
- **Support structure:** Heavy, elongated bud sites require trellising or staking, especially in later flowering weeks.
- **Harvest window:** Monitor trichomes carefully — harvest when 70–80% are cloudy with some amber for peak potency and balanced effects.
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean conditions. Performs well in Southern European climates for outdoor cultivation. Not suitable for outdoor growing in regions with early autumn frosts (harvest can extend into November).
- **Humidity management:** Maintain 40–50% RH during flowering to prevent bud rot on dense colas.
- **Container size:** Minimum 15–20 L for indoor cultivation; larger containers produce significantly better yields.

### Climate Preferences
Thrives in temperatures of 22–30°C during the day. Can handle slightly cooler nights during flowering. Extremely sensitive to cold — temperatures below 12°C will stunt growth and reduce yield. Requires strong, consistent lighting (1000W HPS or equivalent LED at 600–800 µmol/m²/s PPFD) for optimal indoor results.

## Phenotype Variations

Due to its complex polyhybrid background, Amnesia Haze expresses significant phenotypic variation:

- **Original Amnesia Haze:** The classic Dutch phenotype — tall, citrusy, extremely potent, 12+ week flowering
- **Amnesia Lemon:** Backcrossed with Lemon Skunk for enhanced citrus terpene profile and slightly shorter flowering (9–10 weeks)
- **Amnesia Amnesia:** Selected for maximum potency — THC consistently above 22%
- **Critical Mass × Amnesia:** Indica-influenced cross for faster flowering and heavier yields
- **Auto Amnesia Haze:** Ruderalis-crossed autoflowering version flowering in 10–11 weeks from seed

## Aroma & Flavor

The aroma is immediately striking — a complex blend of sharp citrus (lemon and orange peel), earthy Haze character, and subtle notes of fresh herbs and incense. The bouquet is pungent and fills a room quickly. On inhalation, the flavor is bright and citrus-forward with an earthy undertone; exhalation reveals the classic Haze spice with a slight sweetness. The flavor profile is considered one of the most appealing among sativa-dominant strains.

## Legal Status

As a cannabis strain, Amnesia Haze's legal status follows regional cannabis law. Widely available in licensed Dutch coffeeshops, Canadian dispensaries, and US state-licensed retailers. Classified as a controlled substance in jurisdictions where cannabis remains prohibited.
