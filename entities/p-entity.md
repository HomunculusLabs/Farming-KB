# P

P (proscaline; 3,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-propoxyphenethylamine) is a psychedelic phenethylamine described in PiHKAL entry #140.

It occupies the middle position in the alkoxy series between [[escaline-entity]] (ethoxy) and the butoxy compound, carrying a propoxy group at the 4-position of the mescaline ring.

The progression from methoxy (mescaline) through ethoxy (escaline) to propoxy (proscaline) demonstrates increasing potency with increasing chain length at the 4-position.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage range: 30 to 60 milligrams.

Duration: 8 to 12 hours.

An early Czechoslovakian report describes experiments at up to 80 milligrams, with some difficulty with dreams and residual effects still apparent after 12 hours.

## Qualitative Effects

At 30 milligrams, proscaline dulls the sense of pain while sharpening other senses.

Everything feels "really soft, and clean and clear."

Tactile sensitivity is dramatically heightened: "I could feel every hair my hand was touching."

The subject felt extremely relaxed and at ease, noting that "under the appropriate circumstances, this material would lead to uninhibited eroticism."

At 35 milligrams, the experiment is "very quiet" with no nystagmus, no anorexia, and insignificant eyes-closed visuals.

Restlessness and slight tremor occur for the first couple of hours, followed by drowsiness.

The subject would probably not repeat it, finding the experience pleasant but uneventful.

The high was pleasant, "but quite uneventful," and the subject was "a little disappointed that there was no enhanced clarity and no deep realizations."

At 40 milligrams, a "deep feeling of peace and contentment" develops.

Euphoria grows in intensity for several hours and remains for the rest of the day, making it "one of the most enjoyable experiences I have ever had."

Conversation and humor flow easily. "It was marvelous talking and joking with the others."

The subject was "a little disappointed that there was no enhanced clarity and no deep realizations."

"There was not a problem to be found. There were no motivations to discuss anything serious."

The objection, if any, "would be with the name, not the pharmacology."

At 60 milligrams, intoxication develops over a couple of hours.

There is more physical effect than mental, with considerable irritability.

This is described as "probably the maximum dose."

Despite feeling "quite drunk," thinking remains straight.

Effects wane by the fifth hour but sleep is not possible until the twelfth hour.

There is no hangover the following day.

## Chemical Context

P is the propoxy member of the 3,5-dimethoxy-4-alkoxyphenethylamine series.

The series demonstrates a clear structure-activity relationship: increasing the alkoxy chain length at the 4-position increases potency from mescaline (200-400 mg) through escaline (40-60 mg) to proscaline (30-60 mg).

The amphetamine homologue, 3,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-propoxyamphetamine, would logically be called 3C-P.

This compound is unexplored but predicted to be active in the 20 to 40 milligram range, following the pattern of other 2C-to-3C conversions.

Its synthesis would require propylation of syringaldehyde followed by coupling with nitroethane and reduction of the formed nitrostyrene with lithium aluminum hydride.

## Status

Active psychedelic with a notably pleasant, peaceful character at moderate doses.

The compound is physically gentle at lower doses but becomes irritable at the upper end of the range.

The absence of deep insights or visual fireworks at moderate doses makes it more of a social and sensory enhancer than a tool for psychological exploration, though the 40-milligram report suggests capacity for profound contentment and euphoria.
