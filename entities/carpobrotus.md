# Carpobrotus (Pigface)

## Description

*Carpobrotus* is a genus of succulent groundcover plants in the family Aizoaceae, native primarily to South Africa, Australia, and [[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-south-america]]. Commonly known as pigface, ice plant, or hottentot fig, these low-growing succulents are exceptionally tolerant of salt spray, drought, and poor soils. In [[edible-landscaping]]:** Dual function as ornamental and food plant

## Key Facts

- Succulent groundcover genus in Aizoaceae family
- Native to South Africa, Australia, South America
- Edible fruit (pigface) with traditional food and medicine uses
- Extremely salt- and drought-tolerant
- Used in coastal windbreak systems as living fence cover
- Provides [[comparison-vetiver-grass-vs-bamboo-for-erosion-control]] on dunes and coastal banks

## Cautionary Notes

- C. edulis can be invasive outside native range (California, Mediterranean, New Zealand)
- Monitor spread in sensitive [[dighton-fungi-mangrove-coastal-ecosystems]]
- May outcompete native groundcovers in ideal conditions

## Cultivation

- Full sun
- Any well-drained soil including pure sand
- Extremely [[drought-tolerant-planting-design]] once established
- Propagation from stem cuttings (very easy)
- USDA zones 9-11
- Requires virtually no maintenance
