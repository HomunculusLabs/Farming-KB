# Lion's Mane Mushroom (*Hericium erinaceus*)

## Overview

Lion's Mane Mushroom (*Hericium erinaceus*), also known as the hedgehog mushroom, yamabushitake (山伏茸), or hou tou gu (猴头菇), is a [[holzer-medicinal-and-culinary-herbs-compendium]] fungus belonging to the family **Hericiaceae**. Recognizable by its cascading white spines resembling a lion's mane, this basidiomycete has been used for centuries in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and is now one of the most researched functional mushrooms for cognitive health.

## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom**: Fungi
- **Division**: Basidiomycota
- **Class**: Agaricomycetes
- **Order**: Russulales
- **Family**: Hericiaceae
- **Genus**: Hericium
- **Species**: *H. erinaceus*

## Morphology and Identification

Lion's Mane produces a fruiting body composed of soft, pliable spines (teeth) that hang downward in overlapping tiers. Fresh specimens are white to cream-colored, turning yellowish-brown with age. The spines range from 1–5 cm in length. Unlike gilled mushrooms, *H. erinaceus* releases spores from the surface of these teeth.

In the wild, it is a **saprotroph** and mild **parasite**, found growing on dead or dying hardwood trees—especially oak, walnut, beech, maple, and sycamore. It fruits in late summer through fall in temperate forests [[berry-growing]], Europe, and Asia.

## Active Compounds

### Hericenones and Erinacines

The two primary classes of [[query-what-are-the-main-bioactive-compounds-in-medicinal-plants]] Lion's Mane are:

- **Hericenones** (found in the fruiting body): Hericenones C, D, E, F, and others have demonstrated stimulation of **Nerve Growth Factor (NGF)** synthesis in vitro.
- **Erinacines** (found in the mycelium): Erinacines A–K are cyathane diterpenoids. Erinacine A and S are particularly potent NGF inducers, often more bioavailable than hericenones because they cross the blood-brain barrier more effectively.

### Beta-Glucans

Like many medicinal mushrooms, Lion's Mane contains **β-(1→3)-D-glucans** and **β-(1→6)-D-glucans**, which modulate immune function through activation of macrophages, natural killer cells, and dendritic cells.

### Other Compounds

- **Erinacerins** (protein-bound polysaccharides)
- **Hericium alkaloids** (potential antioxidant activity)
- **Sterols** including ergosterol (vitamin D₂ precursor)

## Pharmacology and Mechanisms of Action

### Neuroprotective Effects

The most studied property of *H. erinaceus* is its ability to stimulate **NGF** and **Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF)** production. These neurotrophins support:

- Survival and maintenance of neurons
- Myelination of peripheral nerves
- Synaptic plasticity and memory consolidation

In animal models, Lion's Mane extract promoted **remyelination** following peripheral nerve injury and improved performance in Morris water maze tests, suggesting enhanced spatial memory.

### Anti-Inflammatory Properties

Polysaccharide extracts suppress pro-inflammatory cytokines (**TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β**) via inhibition of the **NF-κB signaling pathway**. This has implications for neuroinflammation, which is implicated in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease pathology.

### Antioxidant Activity

The mushroom scavenges [[reactive-oxygen-species-and-oxidative-stress]] (ROS) and upregulates endogenous antioxidant enzymes (**superoxide dismutase, catalase, glutathione peroxidase**), reducing oxidative stress in neural and hepatic tissues.

### Gastrointestinal Health

Traditional use in TCM centers on the digestive system. Modern research confirms gastroprotective effects: extracts reduce gastric ulcer formation and promote regeneration of the gastric mucosa. Erinacine S has shown activity against *Helicobacter pylori*.
