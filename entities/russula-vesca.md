The species was originally described by Elias Magnus Fries in 1836.
It is a member of the large genus *Russula*, which contains over 750 species
worldwide. *R. vesca* is considered one of the better edible russulas,
with a nutty, mild taste and flesh that does not become excessively peppery
with age.

**Gills:** Adnexed to slightly decurrent, crowded, white to cream,
occasionally forking near the stipe. Gill edges smooth, becoming slightly

**Stipe:** 4–8 cm tall × 1.5–3 cm thick, cylindrical to slightly clavate,
solid, white, smooth, sometimes with faint pinkish flush at base.

**Flesh:** White, firm, mild-tasting, with a faint fruity or nutty odor.
Does not brown or discolor significantly when cut.
