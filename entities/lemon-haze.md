# Lemon Haze

## Overview

**Lemon Haze** is a sativa-dominant cannabis hybrid created by crossing **Silver Haze** with **Lemon Skunk**. Developed by Green House Seeds, it quickly became one of the most popular cannabis strains worldwide after winning the **High Times Cannabis Cup** in 2008. The strain is celebrated for its **intense citrus aroma and flavor**, clear-headed and uplifting cerebral effects, and vigorous growth characteristics. It typically tests at 17–22% THC with negligible CBD content. The terpene profile is dominated by **limonene**, **terpinolene**, and **myrcene**, which together produce its signature lemon-citrus character.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Silver Haze — a classic sativa bred from Haze × [[northern-lights]] × Skunk #1
- **Male parent:** Lemon Skunk — selected for its pronounced citrus terpene profile
- **Breeder:** Arjan Roskam / Green House Seeds
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 70% sativa / 30% indica
- **Landrace ancestry:** Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and Afghan (through the Haze lineage)

## Appearance

Lemon Haze plants produce medium to large, elongated buds that are moderately dense for a sativa-dominant variety. The flowers are bright green with golden-yellow hues, interspersed with vivid orange pistils (stigmas) that give the buds a warm, sunlit appearance. Trichome coverage is heavy, coating the calyxes and surrounding leaflets with a thick layer of translucent to milky glandular trichomes. The overall visual impression is bright and vibrant, consistent with its citrus character. When properly cured, the buds are slightly sticky and release a strong lemon scent on contact.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

The aroma is immediately recognizable: a sharp, bright **lemon and citrus** nose with undertones of earthy sweetness and subtle herbal notes. Breaking apart the buds releases an intensified lemon zest quality with hints of candied citrus and a slight skunky pungency from the Skunk ancestry. The flavor on inhalation is smooth and citrus-forward — fresh lemon and grapefruit — with an earthy, slightly sweet finish on exhalation. Some phenotypes express additional notes of tropical fruit, pine, or pepper, reflecting variation in the terpinolene and caryophyllene content.

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene (0.8–1.5%):** Dominant terpene, responsible for the primary citrus aroma. Contributes mood-elevating and stress-relieving effects.
- **Terpinolene (0.3–0.8%):** Secondary terpene, adding complex floral, piney, and slightly fruity notes. Known for sedative and antioxidant properties.
- **Myrcene (0.3–0.7%):** Provides herbal, earthy undertones and contributes to the relaxing body component of effects.
- **Caryophyllene (0.2–0.5%):** Adds subtle peppery spice; functions as a CB2 receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory activity.
- **Pinene (trace):** Contributes fresh pine notes that complement the citrus profile.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Typical ranges from commercial and dispensary-tested samples:
- **THC:** 17–22% (occasionally testing up to 25% in premium phenotypes)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3%
- **THCV:** Trace amounts in some phenotypes
- The low CBD and high THC profile places Lemon Haze firmly in the recreational/psychotropic category rather than the medicinal CBD-focused market

## Effects

The effects are characteristically sativa-dominant: **cerebral, uplifting, and energetic** with minimal sedation. Users report:
- **Onset:** Rapid (5–15 minutes when smoked or vaporized)
- **Initial phase:** Euphoric, creative, and mentally stimulating; enhanced focus and sociability
- **Middle phase:** Clear-headed energy and motivation; suitable for daytime use
- **Come-down:** Gradual transition to relaxed but not couch-locked state
- Common recreational applications include social gatherings, creative work, outdoor activities, and daytime functional use
- At higher doses, some users report mild anxiety or paranoia, particularly those sensitive to sativa-dominant varieties
- Medicinal users employ it for: fatigue, mild depression, stress, ADHD-related focus issues, and migraines (due to limonene's reported anti-migraine properties)

## Cultivation

Lemon Haze is considered moderately challenging to cultivate, best suited for growers with some experience:
- **Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks indoor (9 weeks average)
- **Indoor yield:** 400–500 g/m² under optimal conditions
- **Outdoor yield:** 500–600 g/plant
- **Harvest:** Early to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere outdoor)
- **Height:** Can reach 150–200 cm indoors; taller outdoors
- The strain responds well to SCROG ([[screen-of-green]]) and LST ([[low-stress-training]]) to manage height and maximize light exposure
- It is moderately nutrient-hungry, particularly during the flowering phase when phosphorus and potassium demand increases
- The strain is somewhat susceptible to powdery mildew and botrytis in humid conditions; adequate airflow is essential
- **Climate preference:** Temperate to warm Mediterranean climates; performs well in greenhouse environments

## Medical and Therapeutic Applications

Lemon Haze's therapeutic potential derives primarily from its cannabinoid and terpene interactions:
- **Limonene** has demonstrated anxiolytic, antidepressant, and anti-inflammatory properties in preclinical studies. It also enhances topical absorption of other compounds and has been investigated for gastroesophageal reflux disorder (GERD) treatment.
- **Elevated mood and stress relief:** The combination of THC and limonene produces rapid mood elevation that patients report as helpful for treatment-resistant depression and seasonal affective disorder, though clinical evidence remains limited.
- **Focus and ADHD support:** Anecdotal reports from medicinal users suggest cognitive-enhancing effects at moderate doses, though no controlled trials have been conducted.
- **Pain management:** The THC content provides mild analgesic effects suitable for headache and neuropathic pain, though patients with severe chronic pain typically prefer indica-dominant varieties with higher myrcene content.
- **Appetite stimulation:** The sativa-dominant profile provides appetite enhancement without the sedation associated with heavy indicas, making it suitable for patients who need to maintain daytime activity.

## Side Effects and Contraindications

- **Common (dose-dependent):** Dry mouth (cottonmouth), dry eyes, mild tachycardia
- **Less common:** Anxiety, paranoia, dizziness, especially at doses above 15 mg THC or in cannabis-naive users
- **Rare:** Headache, nausea (at very high doses)
- **Contraindications:** Personal or family history of psychosis or schizophrenia; pregnancy and lactation; concurrent use of MAO inhibitors; cardiovascular conditions exacerbated by tachycardia
- The high THC/low CBD ratio means there is no CBD buffer against THC-induced anxiety, making dose titration particularly important for new users
- Vaporization at 175–190°C provides a cleaner delivery method with fewer respiratory irritants than combustion

## Genetic Stability and Breeding Potential

Lemon Haze exhibits moderate genetic stability, with most seeds producing plants expressing the characteristic citrus terpene profile. However, the Haze lineage introduces some phenotypic variability in flowering time, plant height, and bud structure. Breeders have used Lemon Haze extensively as a parent in subsequent crosses due to its desirable terpene profile and vigorous growth. Notable descendant strains include Lemon Haze Auto (autoflowering version), and various crosses with Gelato, OG Kush, and Girl Scout Cookies lineages.

## Legal Status and Market Position

As of 2026, Lemon Haze is available in licensed dispensaries across Canada, most US states with legal adult-use cannabis, Germany, Thailand, South Africa, and Uruguay. It consistently ranks among the top 20 best-selling sativa strains in North American dispensaries. Street prices average $8–12 per gram for flower, $25–40 per gram for premium cured or hand-trimmed product. The strain is particularly popular in the European market, where citrus-flavored varieties command premium pricing.

## Notable Phenotypes and Variations

- **Amnesia Lemon:** A cross of Amnesia Haze × Lemon Skunk, sometimes confused with Lemon Haze; tends to have higher THC and a more complex aroma
- **[[super-lemon-haze]]:** A separate Green House Seeds creation ([[super-silver-haze]] × Lemon Skunk), which has become arguably more famous than the original Lemon Haze
- Individual phenotypes vary significantly in the balance of lemon intensity vs. earthy/hazy undertones, with "lemon-heavy" phenotypes being the most sought after by collectors
- **Lemon Haze Auto:** Autoflowering version bred by crossing with a ruderalis line; completes lifecycle in 10–12 weeks from seed; reduced potency (12–16% THC) but retains characteristic terpene profile
- The strain's enduring popularity across nearly two decades since its introduction reflects the consistent quality of its terpene expression and reliable growth characteristics that appeal to both novice and experienced cultivators.
