# Champignonnière

## Description

A champignonnière (literally "mushroom cave" or "mushroom farm" in French) is a subterranean [[mushroom-house-design]] worldwide.

## Economic Impact

The Parisian champignonnières produced approximately 3–5 million kilograms of mushrooms annually by the early 1900s, with wholesale values of 1.20–2.20 francs per kilogram, generating total values estimated at 7.5 million francs wholesale and 12.5 million francs retail (approximately $2.5 million USD at the time).
