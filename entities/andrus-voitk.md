## Andrus Voitk

## Description

Andrus Voitk is a naturalist and author from Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada, who wrote *A Little Illustrated Book of Common Mushrooms of Newfoundland and Labrador* (2007), published by the Gros Morne Cooperating Association. Voitk is also an authority on wild orchids and previously published a field guide to them. His mushroom guidebook covers nearly 400 species with color illustrations and was praised by *Fungi Magazine* editor [[britt-bunyard]] as a superior resource for mushroomers across the northern half of North America, notable for its current information, warm writing style, and unique organizational approach.

## Key Facts

- Authored *A Little Illustrated Book of Common Mushrooms of Newfoundland and Labrador*, published in 2007 by the Gros Morne Cooperating Association (272 pp., ISBN 978-0-9699509-4-3).
- The guidebook features color illustrations of almost 400 species, making it one of the most comprehensive regional mushroom guides for northeastern North America.
- Also an authority on wild orchids and previously published a well-regarded field guide to the wild orchids of Newfoundland and Labrador.
- His guidebook was the first to caution against picking [[pleurocybella-porrigens]] ("Angel's Wings"), citing a recent spate of poisonings in Japan — demonstrating exceptional currency of information.
- Covers species often omitted from other guidebooks, including the spectacular Catathelasma ventricosa and the striking Amanita wellsii.
- Gives particular attention to the genus Cortinarius, which is abundant in Newfoundland and often challenging for beginners, providing clear breakdowns for identification.
- Also covers lichenized mushrooms, which he describes as "often overlooked little gems," with detailed photographs and descriptions.
- Adopted a non-traditional organizational format, departing from the formulaic species-description approach of most guidebooks in favor of more readable, contextual entries.
- The book is pocket-sized, designed for field use, and praised for its warm, folksy humor.
- Published by the Gros Morne Cooperating Association, with all proceeds supporting Gros Morne National Park.
- Played a significant role in establishing Newfoundland as a leader in mushroomology, building on the success of the annual Foray Newfoundland event.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Andrus Voitk's contributions are relevant to mycology and cultivation in several ways:

1. **Regional species documentation** — His comprehensive coverage of nearly 400 species in Newfoundland and Labrador expands the documented range of many mushroom species, providing cultivators with information about local species that may have cultivation potential.

2. **Safety awareness** — His early warning about Pleurocybella porrigens toxicity demonstrates the importance of staying current with mycological safety information, which is critical for foragers and cultivators working with wild-collected strains.

3. **Cortinarius identification** — His clear treatment of the difficult genus Cortinarius aids in accurate field identification, reducing the risk of misidentification for foragers and helping cultivators understand local mycoflora.

4. **Accessible mycological education** — His warm, humorous writing style and non-traditional format make mycological knowledge accessible to a broad audience, potentially inspiring new cultivators and mycologists.

5. **Conservation through education** — By publishing through the Gros Morne Cooperating Association with proceeds supporting the national park, his work connects mycological education with habitat conservation, supporting the preservation of wild fungal biodiversity that is essential for ongoing cultivation research.
