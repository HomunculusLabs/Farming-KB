## OG Kush

OG Kush is a legendary cannabis strain that originated in Southern California during the early 1990s and became the genetic backbone of modern West Coast [[hindu-kush]] or Lemon Thai pollinator — OG Kush is renowned for its potent, sedating euphoria, distinctive fuel-citrus-pine aroma, and extraordinary influence on contemporary cannabis breeding. The "OG" designation is variously interpreted as "Ocean Grown" (a reference to its coastal California origins) or "Original Gangster" (a nod to its status as a foundational, elite cultivar). OG Kush has served as a parent or ancestor to hundreds of named cultivars includ girl scout cookies kies, Headband, Skywalker, and countless "OG" variants, making it arguably the most genetically influential cannabis strain of the 21st century.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Hybrid (indica-dominant, approximately 55% indica / 45% sativa)
- **Lineage:** Disputed; most widely accepted theory: Chemdawg × [Hindu Kush or Lemon Thai]. Alternative theories include direct selection from a bagseed of unknown origin found in Florida and brought to California.
- **Breeder/originator:** Unknown; earliest documented cuttings circulated in the Los Angeles area circa 1993–1996
- **Geographic origin:** Southern California, likely the San Fernando Valley
- **Landrace ancestry:** Hindu Kush (Afghanistan/Pakistan), Chemdawg (unknown — possibly Thai × US indica), possible Thai sativa contribution
- **Clone-only:** The original OG Kush phenotype was preserved and distributed exclusively as a clone; seeds produce variable offspring with significant phenotypic variation
- **Named phenotypes:** Numerous named cuts exist (Tahoe OG, Ghost OG, Larry OG, SFV OG, Abusive OG, etc.), each representing a selection from the original genetic pool with subtle differences in potency, flavor, and growth characteristics

## Morphology and Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure
OG Kush plants exhibit a classic indica growth pattern with moderate height (90–150 cm indoors), sturdy branching, and dense internodal spacing. The plant produces thick, sturdy stems that support heavy flower clusters. Branching is lateral with a dominant central cola.

### Leaves
Leaves are broad-fingered, dark green with occasional purple-blue pigmentation on leaf margins and petioles, especially under cool night temperatures. Leaf-to-calyx ratio is moderate, simplifying trimming.

### Flowers
Buds are large, dense, and tightly packed, forming chunky, rounded calyx clusters. Color ranges from bright to deep green with vivid orange pistils (stigmas) and a heavy coating of large, bulbous trichomes. Under optimal conditions, flowers develop a frosty, silvery-white trichome layer visible to the naked eye.

### Cultivation Notes
- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate; 350–450 g/m² indoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate; susceptible to [[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]]:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, anxiety/paranoia at high doses (particularly in inexperienced users), couch-lock with heavy consumption, dizziness, potential tachycardia in sensitive individuals
- **Tolerance:** Moderate; regular users develop tolerance over 1–2 weeks of daily use; [[cervantes-cannabis-tolerance-addiction-pharmacology]] patterns
- **Subjective experience:** Widely described as providing a balanced "head high" that transitions into deep body relaxation; the headband-like pressure sensation around the temples is frequently noted (and lent its name to the Headband cross)
- **Medicinal use:** Increasingly studied for PTSD, chronic pain, and anxiety disorders in legal medical cannabis programs; high THC content makes it popular for patients requiring potent symptom relief

## Genetic Legacy and Notable Descendants

OG Kush has been used as a breeding parent more extensively than almost any other modern cannabis strain:

- **Girl Scout Cookies:** OG Kus durban poison ison — one of the most commercially successful strains of the 2010s; Cookies genetics have spawned dozens of sub-crosses
- **Headband:** OG Kus sour diesel esel — named for the pressure sensation around the temples; known for potent, long-lasting effects
- **Skywalker:** Blueberry × OG Kush — indica-dominant with berry-kush flavors; popular in European markets
- **Tahoe OG:** OG Kush phenotype selected for increased potency and shorter flowering time (7–8 weeks); favored by commercial growers
- **Ghost OG:** Cut selected for dense resin production and potent sedation; popular in California medical cannabis community
