## Overview

**God Bud** is a potent indica-dominant cannabis strain renowned for its deeply relaxing effects and pungent, complex aroma. Originally bred in Canada during the late 1990s by **BC Bud Depot** in British Columbia, it has become one of the most iconic Canadian cannabis exports.

The strain consistently produces high THC levels ranging from 18–25%, with some phenotypes reportedly reaching 27%. It is widely regarded as a heavy nighttime strain, favored for stress relief, [[african-landrace-sativa-genetic-contributions-modern-breeding-oner]] from **Blueberry** or an unnamed Afghan indica.

The Purple Skunk parent contributes dense bud structure, purple coloration potential, and a skunky aroma base. The Hawaiian parent adds tropical fruit notes, elevated [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]] and deeply sedating finish.

## Appearance

God Bud produces medium to large, densely packed buds with a classic indica structure — broad, tightly coiled leaves and a chunky, rounded profile. The flowers are exceptionally resinous, often appearing frosted with a thick layer of trichomes.

Coloration ranges from deep forest green to shades of purple and violet, particularly in cooler growing conditions. Bright orange pistils contrast sharply against the darker foliage.

The buds are notably heavy for their size due to dense trichome coverage. When properly cured, they have a slightly sticky texture that leaves resin on the fingers.

## Aroma and Flavor

God Bud's aroma is one of its most defining characteristics. It presents a complex, pungent bouquet that blends earthy, musky, and tropical notes.

The dominant scent is a rich, sweet **musk** with undertones of **tropical fruit** — particularly mango and papaya. Secondary notes include **pine**, **lavender**, and a distinctive **skunky** pungency that becomes more pronounced when the buds are broken apart.

On the palate, God Bud delivers a smooth, sweet smoke with flavors of tropical fruit, berry, and earth. The exhale reveals more herbal and piney notes. The flavor is generally considered pleasant and less harsh than many high-THC indicas.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene** (dominant): 0.8–1.2% — contributes sedative effects and the musky, earthy aroma.
- **Caryophyllene**: 0.3–0.5% — adds peppery spice and potential anti-inflammatory activity.
- **Linalool**: 0.2–0.4% — provides floral lavender notes and anxiolytic properties.
- **Pinene**: 0.1–0.3% — adds pine freshness and may counteract some cognitive fog.
- **Limonene**: trace — subtle citrus undertone, mood elevation.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC**: 18–25% (average ~21%)
- **CBD**: <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG**: 0.1–0.3%
- **THCV**: trace

God Bud is a high-THC, low-CBD strain with virtually no detectable CBD in most tested samples. Its potency comes primarily from [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], muscle spasms, insomnia, anxiety, and stress. The strain's sedative properties make it most suitable for evening or nighttime use.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness. In higher doses, paranoia and anxiety can occur, particularly in users sensitive to THC-heavy indicas.

## Cultivation

God Bud is considered a moderately difficult strain to grow, requiring some experience to achieve optimal results. It performs best indoors or in controlled greenhouse environments.

- **Flowering time**: 8–9 weeks indoors.
- **Yield**: Medium, approximately 350–450 g/m² indoors.
- **Height**: Medium, reaching 100–140 cm indoors with training.
- **Climate**: Prefers warm, slightly humid conditions; resists mold better than many dense-budded indicas.

The strain responds well to techniques like low-stress training and scrogging. It develops significant purple coloration when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering.

## Notable Phenotypes

Several named phenotypes and crosses have emerged from the God Bud lineage. **BC God Bud** is the original BC Bud Depot release and remains the most widely available cut. **God's Green Crack** crosses God Bud with Green Crack, producing a more energetic variant with reduced sedation.
