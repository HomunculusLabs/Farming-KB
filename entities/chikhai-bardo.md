## Chikhai Bardo

## Description

The Chikhai Bardo (also spelled Chikhai Bardo) is the first of the three bardo phases described in the [[psychedelic-experience-tibetan-book-of-the-dead]] the Dead ([[bardo-thodol]]). It represents the moment of ego-loss — the point at which consciousness separates from the ordinary sense of self and enters a state of pure awareness. In the Tibetan tradition, this occurs at the moment of physical death. In the psychedelic context, as mapped by Leary, Metzner, and Alpert, it corresponds to the onset phase of a [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] where the ego dissolves and the subject encounters what the Tibetans call the "Clear Light" — a state of complete transcendence beyond words, beyond space-time, and beyond self.

## Classification

- **Category:** Concept — Consciousness Phase
- **Tradition:** Tibetan Buddhism ([[metzner-leary-bardo-thodol-and-tao-te-ching-adaptation]])
- **Application:** Psychedelic experience framework
- **Duration:** Typically brief (minutes to hours depending on dosage and individual)

## Key Facts

- In the Chikhai Bardo, there are no visions, no sense of self, and no thoughts — only pure awareness and ecstatic freedom from all game and biological involvements.
- The Tibetans describe the primary experience as encountering the "Clear Light" — a luminous, empty, radiant awareness that is the fundamental nature of mind itself.
- For the prepared and experienced practitioner, this state can be maintained and deepened. For the unprepared, it is fleeting and quickly followed by descent into the [[chonyid-bardo]] (hallucinatory phase).
- The key instruction is to recognize the Clear Light as one"s own true nature and to merge with it without fear or resistance.
- In the psychedelic context, this corresponds to the peak onset period of high-dose LSD or psilocybin experiences, particularly with doses above 300–400 μg LSD or 5+ grams dried [[psilocybin-mushrooms]].

## Relevance to Psychedelic Studies

The Chikhai Bardo is the ideal target state in the Leary/Metzner/Alpert model. Their manual instructs that "the prepared person should be able, at the very beginning of his experience, to move directly to a state of non-game ecstasy and deep revelation." The entire purpose of psychedelic session preparation is to maximize the duration and depth of this first bardo phase. When subjects fail to maintain Chikhai Bardo awareness, they descend into Chonyid Bardo (visions) or [[sidpa-bardo]] (ego-reinstatement struggle).

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

For cultivators and facilitators working with [[amanita-muscaria-legal-alternative-psilocybin-mushrooms-europe]], understanding the Chikhai Bardo phase is essential for session preparation. High-potency [[emcdda-european-mushroom-varieties-and-market-products]] (such as [[psilocybe-azurescens]] or high-yield [[cap-and-stem-alkaloid-distribution-in-psilocybe-cubensis-strains]]) are more likely to induce Chikhai Bardo-type experiences. Proper set, setting, and dosing are critical factors in whether a mushroom experience reaches this transcendent first phase.
