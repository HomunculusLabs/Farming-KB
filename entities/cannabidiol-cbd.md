# Cannabidiol (CBD)

**Type:** Chemical / Phytocannabinoid
**Category:** Major [[comparison-cannabis-sativa-vs-cannabis-indica]] and Cannabis indica. It is the second most abundant
cannabinoid in cannabis after THC and has emerged as one of the most
promising therapeutic compounds in medical cannabis research. Unlike THC,
CBD does not produce a "high" but instead modulates the effects of THC
and interacts with multiple biological pathways.

## Key Details

### Chemical Properties
- Molecular formula: C21H30O2
- Non-psychoactive cannabinoid
- Greater affinity for CB2 receptors than CB1 receptors
- Exists as CBD-acid (CBDA) in raw, fresh cannabis
- CBDA converts to CBD through decarboxylation (heat/drying)
- CBD-acid is non-psychoactive even after decarboxylation

### Mechanism of Action
CBD's therapeutic influence largely stems from its ability to suppress
fatty [[blesching-cannabis-inflammatory-bowel-disease]]
  disease, arthritis, periodontitis, and atherosclerosis.
- **Anti-cancer:** Produces significant anti-tumor activity both in
  vitro and in vivo. Selectively produces apoptosis in cancer cells.
  CBD was the most potent anti-breast cancer cannabinoid in Italian
  studies, on par with whole-plant CBD-rich extract. First non-toxic
  exogenous agent to significantly decrease Id-1 expression in
  metastatic breast cancer cells.
- **Antibacterial:** Potent activity against methicillin-resistant
  Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) strains.

### Strain and Form Considerations
- Present in both Cannabis sativa and Cannabis indica
- Indica strains typically have higher CBD profiles (3-4%+ considered
  potent)
- Raw cannabis leaf and juice contain CBDA at concentrations up to 60x
  higher than heated forms (per Dr. William L. Courtney)
- CBD-acid selectively inhibits COX-2 enzyme (discovered 2008)
