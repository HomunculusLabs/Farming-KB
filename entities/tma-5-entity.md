# TMA-5

**PIHKAL Entry #161** -- 2,3,6-Trimethoxyamphetamine

## Chemical Name

2,3,6-Trimethoxyamphetamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 30 mg or more

- **Duration:** 8 - 10 h

## Qualitative Comments

(with 20 mg) A very slight stimulation, and a modest eye dilation. Normal
pulse. No apparent effect upon the spirit, the imagination or the thinking
processes. This is probably a threshold level or less. If this is indeed the
marginal edge, then this is a stimulant, not a psychotomimetic. Go up with
care.

(with 30 mg) Intense introspection. This I would compare to about 75 micrograms
of LSD, or perhaps more. An hour and a half into it, I was aware of being
uncomfortable, but this soon passed. By the third hour I was into a +++ but it
was a thoughtful, gentle, peaceful ++ that had built up to this level. Very
visual, very open.

## Synthesis

A solution of 21 g 1,2,4-trimethoxybenzene in 100 mL petroleum ether was
stirred magnetically under He and cooled to 0 degrees C. 52 mL of 1.6 N
butyllithium in hexane was added, followed by 5.1 g propionaldehyde. After
stirring for 1 h at 0 degrees C, the mixture was allowed to come to room
temperature, quenched with H2O, and extracted with Et2O. Removal of solvent
gave 24.8 g of crude ethyl 2,3,6-trimethoxyphenyl carbinol.

This was converted to the bromide by reaction with PBr3 in petroleum ether at
0 degrees C (yield 18.7 g). Dehydrobromination with 6.3 g KOH in 50 mL EtOH
gave 12.6 g of crude product (containing ethoxy impurity). Hydrolysis with
conc. H2SO4 gave 9.6 g of 2,3,6-trimethoxyphenylacetone.

Ozonolysis in CH2Cl2 at -78 degrees C, followed by treatment with
tetranitromethane in MeOH with KOH, gave 1-(2,3,6-trimethoxyphenyl)-2-
nitropropene as yellow crystals (mp 73-74 degrees C). LAH reduction (Soxhlet
technique, reflux 24 h) followed by distillation at 130-140 degrees C at 0.3
mm/Hg gave 1.5 g of colorless oil. The hydrochloride salt crystallized from
IPA/Et2O (mp 124-125 degrees C, 1.13 g).

## Extensions and Commentary

TMA-5 has only been superficially explored. There are just two qualitative
comments from two different people. They hint at activity in the tens of
milligrams but provide minimal information about the character of the
experience.

Despite the limited data, TMA-5 has been published in pharmacological reviews
as a psychedelic drug with potency "about ten times that of mescaline." Shulgin
cautions that these conclusions must await more extensive trials. The statement
that it is psychedelic was published solely on the basis of the above two
studies, and the comparison to LSD potency is not well supported.

The two-carbon analogue 2,3,6-trimethoxyphenethylamine (2C-TMA-5 or
2,3,6-TMPEA) is a known material. Its properties have been the subject of
some controversy in the literature regarding melting point and physical
properties. The synthesis of 2,3,6-TMPEA proceeds from the same intermediate
(2,3,6-trimethoxybenzaldehyde) via nitrostyrene formation and LAH reduction.

Among the six trimethoxyamphetamine isomers, TMA-5 (2,3,6-) occupies an
intermediate position. The limited data suggest it may have moderate activity,
somewhere between the complete inactivity of [[tma-3-entity]] and the high
potency of [[tma-2-entity]]. However, without systematic dose-response studies,
any conclusions about its relative potency remain speculative.

The 2,3,6-trimethoxy pattern is of interest because it places two adjacent
methoxy groups (2,3-) together with a meta-positioned third group (6-), a
pattern not commonly found in naturally occurring compounds. This may explain
why TMA-5 does not correspond to a known essential oil, unlike [[tma-entity]]
(elemicin) and [[tma-2-entity]] (asarone).
