## NET

N-Ethyltryptamine

## Chemical Identity

NET is N-ethyltryptamine, a monoalkyltryptamine in which a single ethyl group is attached to the primary amine nitrogen of the tryptamine molecule. The full chemical name is 3-[2-(ethylamino)ethyl]indole.

The free base spontaneously crystallizes to a white waxy solid with a melting point of 80-81 degrees Celsius. The hydrochloride salt has a melting point of 181-182 degrees Celsius (from isopropanol/ether).
