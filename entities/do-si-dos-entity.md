# Do-Si-Dos

Do-Si-Dos is a potent indica-dominant [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain bred by
Archive Seed Bank in Oregon, crossing the legendary [[girl-scout-cookies]]
with Face Off OG. Named after the classic spinning dance move, the strain
has earned a reputation for heavy, full-body sedation paired with cerebral
euphoria, making it a favorite among medical patients and evening
recreational users.

The strain took the [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]] by storm after its release in 2016,
winning multiple awards and quickly becoming a parent strain for numerous
successful crosses. Its dense, frosty buds and sweet, earthy aroma make it
instantly recognizable in dispensaries.

## Strain Classification

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (~70% indica / 30% sativa)
- **Lineage:** Girl Scout Cookies × Face Off OG
- **Breeder:** Archive Seed Bank (Portland, Oregon), circa 2016
- **THC content:** 19–30% (test averages 22–25%)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.1%)

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Dense, chunky, and tightly packed; medium to large
  calyxes with minimal leaf exposure
- **Color:** Deep forest green with occasional purple hues; bright orange
  pistils woven throughout; heavy coating of milky-white trichomes
- **Trichome coverage:** Exceptional — frosty appearance visible to the
  naked eye; sticky [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]]
- **Visual appeal:** Often described as "diamond-like" due to crystalline
  trichome density; ranks among the most photogenic strains

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Primary aromas:** Earthy, pungent diesel, sweet floral notes
- **Secondary notes:** Lime, lavender, pepper, nutty undertones
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with a lingering diesel
  and citrus-kissed exhale; smooth smoke with a slightly spicy finish
- **Key terpenes:** Limonene, Caryophyllene, Linalool, Myrcene, Humulene
- The aroma intensifies when buds are broken open, releasing a complex
  blend of OG diesel and Cookie sweetness

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** 5–15 minutes; initial cerebral lift precedes body effects
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours, with lingering sedation for 1–2 additional
  hours
- **Primary effects:** Deep physical relaxation, cerebral euphoria,
  happiness, creative introspection that transitions to heavy sedation
- **Body effects:** Strong body stone, couch-lock at higher doses,
  muscle tension relief, numbing sensation in extremities
- **[[oner-sativa-medical-applications]]:** [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], insomnia, stress, anxiety,
  PTSD, muscle spasms, [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]], nausea
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth (common), dry eyes (common), dizziness
  (moderate doses), paranoia/anxiety (high doses or sensitive users),
  lethargy (prolonged use)
