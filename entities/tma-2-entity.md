# TMA-2

TMA-2 (2,4,5-Trimethoxyamphetamine) is a classic psychedelic amphetamine that played a pivotal role in [[alexander-shulgin]]'s research and in the development of modern psychedelic pharmacology.

It is documented as entry #23 in PiHKAL by Alexander Shulgin.

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2,4,5-Trimethoxyamphetamine.

Molecular formula: C12H19NO3 (free base).

TMA-2 is one of three trimethoxyamphetamine isomers (TMA, TMA-2, TMA-6), with methoxy groups at the 2, 4, and 5 positions of the aromatic ring.

The 2,4,5-trimethoxy pattern is the "classical" psychedelic substitution pattern, shared with dom-entity|DOM, dob-entity|DOB, and many other potent psychedelic amphetamines.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 54-55 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 15 - 30 mg.

Duration of effects: 6 - 8 hours.

TMA-2 is approximately 6-10 times more potent than [[mescaline-entity]]|mescaline on a weight basis.

The compound has a relatively rapid onset compared to longer-acting DOX compounds.

## Qualitative Effects

At 15 mg, effects include gentle visual enhancement, mood elevation, and increased sensory awareness.

The experience is described as comfortable and manageable.

At 20 mg, clear psychedelic effects emerge including visual patterning, color enhancement, and altered perception of space and time.

Cognitive effects include enhanced pattern recognition and philosophical or creative thinking.

At 25 mg, effects intensify with rich visual imagery, both eyes-open and eyes-closed.

The compound is described as producing a "clean" psychedelic experience with moderate body load.

Communication and introspection are both facilitated.

The character is more "psychedelic" and less "stimulant" than dom-entity|DOM.

## Historical Significance

TMA-2 was one of the first compounds that led Shulgin to systematically explore the structure-activity relationships of psychedelic amphetamines.

The discovery that simply adding a methyl group to the 4-position of TMA-2 produced dom-entity|DOM (active at 3-10 mg, a 3-5 fold increase in potency) was a landmark observation.

This finding established the 2,4,5-trimethoxy-4-alkyl pattern as the most productive substitution pattern for psychedelic amphetamines.

The entire DOX family (dom-entity|DOM, dob-entity|DOB, doi-entity|DOI, [[doc-entity]]|DOC) and the 2C family are descendants of this TMA-2 research.

## The Three TMA Isomers

TMA (2,3,4-trimethoxyamphetamine) is the least potent of the three isomers.

TMA-2 (2,4,5-trimethoxyamphetamine) is the most potent and the most thoroughly studied.

TMA-6 (2,4,6-trimethoxyamphetamine) is active but represents a different substitution pattern.

The dramatic difference in potency between the three isomers (TMA-2 being 6-10 times more potent than TMA) demonstrates the critical importance of substitution pattern position.

TMA-2's activity validated the 2,4,5 pattern that became the foundation of psychedelic phenethylamine research.

## Synthesis

The synthesis of TMA-2 begins with asarone (2,4,5-trimethoxy-1-propenylbenzene), a natural product found in plants of the Acorus (sweet flag) genus.

Asarone undergoes nitrostyrene formation by reaction with nitroethane in acetic acid.

The nitrostyrene is reduced with lithium aluminum hydride to yield TMA-2 as the free base.

Alternative routes start from 2,4,5-trimethoxybenzaldehyde, which is more readily available commercially.

The natural product connection to asarone makes TMA-2 one of the "Essential Amphetamines."

## Pharmacological Context

TMA-2 acts primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist, the primary mechanism believed responsible for psychedelic effects.

The 2,4,5-trimethoxy pattern is optimal for this receptor interaction, with the 4-position being particularly sensitive to substitution.

TMA-2 has been used as a reference compound in structure-activity relationship studies throughout psychedelic pharmacology.

The compound's moderate duration and clean effects make it a useful benchmark for comparing new compounds.

## Comparison with DOM

dom-entity|DOM differs from TMA-2 only by the addition of a methyl group at the 4-position.

This single methyl group increases potency 3-5 fold and extends duration from 6-8 hours to 10-12 hours.

The character of the experience also shifts: dom-entity|DOM is more stimulant and "pushy" while TMA-2 is more purely psychedelic.

The DOM/TMA-2 comparison remains one of the most instructive structure-activity relationships in psychedelic chemistry.

## Legal Status

TMA-2 is classified as a Schedule I controlled substance in the United States.

It is also controlled in many other countries due to its classification as a trimethoxyamphetamine.

The compound has been specifically named in scheduling legislation in multiple jurisdictions.

## Harm Reduction

The 15-30 mg dose range requires accurate milligram measurement.

The 6-8 hour duration requires planning for most of a day.

TMA-2 should not be combined with MAOIs or other serotonergic substances.

The compound's relative safety profile compared to longer-acting DOX compounds makes it somewhat more forgiving of dosing errors.
