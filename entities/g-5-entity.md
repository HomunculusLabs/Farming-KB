## G-5

G-5 (3,6-dimethoxy-4-(2-aminopropyl)benzonorbornane) is a psychedelic amphetamine and the most structurally elaborate member of the Ganesha family, described in [[shipibo-san-francisco-ceremonial-center-ucayali]].

The subject could observe without participating. Each speaker appeared to stand in "a cone of light that was beautifully bright and colorful, casting everything else on the stage into obscurity."

The subject mused: "Maybe angels really are illuminated from above, and the aliens lurk out of sight until it is their turn. Where does one look for the archetypes?"

Appetite was severely diminished: only half a cream cheese sandwich could be eaten, and even at dinner that evening there was no hunger.

Sleep was difficult that evening.

At 20 milligrams, onset is very slow but then the effects arrive "all of a sudden."

There is an "unexpected absence of visual activity despite being at a full +++."

Mental activity is excellent, with easy writing and a positive flow of ideas.

However, there is an "absence of the bells and whistles that are expected with a psychedelic in full bloom."

A real drop occurs by the 16th hour, and the next day is free of effects except for occasional catnaps.

A second 20-milligram report describes an unusually long transition period lasting far longer than the typical one to two hours.

This presages the long duration: the "slow-in slow-out rule" generally applies, though there are exceptions.

There is indifference toward the erotic but no separation from personal interactions and emotions.

The subject valued integration over separation: "I believe in integration, not separation of all parts of ourselves, distrusting any drug states (particularly those that have the reputation of being strongly cosmic) which divorce the consciousness from the body."

"With this material there is no separation from feelings, only from my particular color language."

## Chemical Structure

The norbornane group attached to the benzene ring contains a one-carbon bridge and a two-carbon bridge extending at odd angles.

G-5 actually contains two centers of asymmetry: the alpha-carbon on the amphetamine chain and the norbornyl area itself, making it a mixture of two racemic diastereoisomers.

A hypothetical G-6, replacing the one-carbon bridge with a second two-carbon bridge, would still be a racemate but only a single compound rather than a mixture of diastereoisomers.

G-6 would be makeable, but Shulgin noted there was no high priority to do so.

The synthesis of G-5 was notably challenging, with the nitrostyrene intermediate taking approximately three years to crystallize spontaneously.

## Status

Active psychedelic with very long duration.

As potent as any of the three-carbon Ganesha compounds but lacking visual and sensory entertainment.

The absence of bodily threat does not compensate for the limited drive to explore further.

The extreme bulk of the norbornane ring system makes G-5 a landmark in structure-activity relationship studies, though its practical value as a psychedelic tool is limited by the absence of rich sensory experience.
