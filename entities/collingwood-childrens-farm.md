## Collingwood Children's Farm

## Description

Collingwood Children's Farm is a community-based urban farm located in Collingwood, an inner suburb of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. Situated on the banks of the Yarra River, it provides hands-on agricultural and environmental education for children and families. David Holmgren worked with the farm to improve its sustainability and educational value.

## Background and Classification

- **Type:** Place / Organisation — urban community farm
- **Location:** Collingwood, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
- **Setting:** Inner-city urban, riparian zone along the Yarra River
- **Purpose:** Environmental and agricultural education, community engagement
- **Significance:** Practical demonstration site for urban Permaculture and ecological literacy

## Key Facts

- Located in a highly urbanised inner-Melbourne setting, accessible to city-dwelling children
- David Holmgren consulted on improvements to the farm's sustainability and educational programmes
- Holmgren proposed fodder trees and shrubs (tagasaste and willow) that children could lop to feed goats — replacing the practice of feeding goats stale bread
- The bread-feeding practice was criticised for giving children the false message that farm animals eat processed food from plastic bags
- The fodder tree programme succeeded with staff, children, and goats alike

## Relevance to Permaculture and Cultivation

- **Plants come before animals:** Holmgren uses the farm as a teaching example for the ecological succession rule that plants must provide resources to support animals
- **Educational design:** The farm teaches ecological literacy through direct experience — children learn by doing
- **Urban Permaculture:** Demonstrates meaningful food production and ecological education in dense urban environments
- **Appropriate technology:** Using simple fodder trees rather than purchased feed aligns with Permaculture's preference for biological solutions
- **Remedial wholistics:** Holmgren describes Permaculture education as "remedial wholistics" for citizens divorced from nature — the farm serves this function
