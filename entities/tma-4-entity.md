# TMA-4

**PIHKAL Entry #160** -- 2,3,5-Trimethoxyamphetamine

## Chemical Name

2,3,5-Trimethoxyamphetamine

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** unknown

- **Duration:** unknown

## Qualitative Comments

TMA-4 has only been superficially explored and no dosage or qualitative comment
data has been published.

## Synthesis

A multi-step synthesis from 2,4-dimethoxybenzaldehyde:

(1) Peracetic acid oxidation (from 30% H2O2 and acetic acid, with a few drops
of H2SO4 catalyst) at 60-70 degrees C for 8 h gave 2,4-dimethoxyphenyl
formate as a crystalline solid (mp 48-49 degrees C, 86% yield).

(2) Hydrolysis with 10% NaOH gave 2,4-dimethoxyphenol (mp 42-43 degrees C).

(3) Alkylation with allyl bromide and K2CO3 in dry acetone gave 2,4-dimethoxy
allylphenyl ether.

(4) Claisen rearrangement: heating to 215 degrees C produced an exotherm to
270 degrees C (monitored with IR), giving 2-allyl-4,6-dimethoxyphenol.

(5) Methylation with methyl sulfate and KOH gave 2-allyl-1,3,5-trimethoxy-
benzene.

(6) Continuation to the amphetamine via standard methods (ozonolysis,
nitropropene formation, LAH reduction).

## Extensions and Commentary

The synthesis of TMA-4 requires particular care in analytical technique. GC
analysis must be carried out at low column temperatures (below 180 degrees C)
on ethylene glycol succinate substrate. Silicone columns cause considerable
Claisen rearrangement on-column even at low temperatures, producing misleading
analytical results.

TMA-4 has only been superficially explored. The compound represents one of the
six possible trimethoxyamphetamine isomers explored systematically after the
landmark discovery that [[tma-2-entity]] (2,4,5-trimethoxyamphetamine) was
dramatically more potent than [[tma-entity]] (3,4,5-).

The 2,3,5-trimethoxy pattern is unusual in natural products and has not been
extensively studied pharmacologically. Without human assay data, it is
impossible to predict whether this isomer would share the potency of TMA-2 or
the inactivity of [[tma-3-entity]].

The two-carbon analogue 2,3,5-trimethoxyphenethylamine has been synthesized but
has not been systematically evaluated.
