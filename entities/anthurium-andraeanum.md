## Overview

**Anthurium andraeanum** is a species of flowering plant in the arum family, Araceae, native to the rainforests of southwestern Colombia and northwestern Ecuador. It is commonly known as the flamingo flower, laceleaf, or tailflower.

The species is one of the most commercially important ornamental plants in the global cut flower trade. Its glossy, heart-shaped spathe and projecting spadix have made it a popular choice for floral arrangements, interior decoration, and as a long-lasting houseplant.

The distinctive "flower" of Anthurium andraeanum is actually an inflorescence consisting of a colorful spathe (a modified leaf) and a spadix (a spike of tiny flowers). The true flowers are the numerous structures covering the spadix surface.
