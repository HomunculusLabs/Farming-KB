## Description

Lysergic acid diethylamide (LSD) is a powerful semisynthetic hallucinogenic compound derived from ergot alkaloids. While LSD itself is not found in nature, it contains an indole nucleus shared with many natural hallucinogens and is structurally related to the [[sacred-mexican-morning-glories-ololiuqui-and-tlitliltzin]] and ergot fungus ([[schultes-ololiuqui-and-morning-glory]] seeds
- Active in microgram doses (one of the most potent psychoactive compounds known)
- Structurally related to the ergoline alkaloids: ergine and others found in plants
- Part of the indolic hallucinogen family alongside DMT, psilocybin, ibogaine, and beta-carbolines
- The [[hplc-analysis-of-psilocybin-and-psilocin]] from mushrooms
- Understanding LSD helps contextualize the broader family of indole hallucinogens
- Ergot is a fungal parasite of grasses with profound historical significance
