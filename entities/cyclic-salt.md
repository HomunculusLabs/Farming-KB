## Description

Cyclic salt is salt (primarily sodium chloride and other sea salts) that is transported inland from the ocean by wind and atmospheric processes. Sea spray and salt particles are carried aloft by wind, serve as rain nuclei in cloud formation, and are deposited on land through precipitation and dry deposition.
