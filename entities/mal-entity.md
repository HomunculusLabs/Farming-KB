# MAL (Methallylescaline; 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-methallyloxyphenethylamine)

MAL is an allyl-substituted phenethylamine derived from [[dom]].

Nor is it as consistently stimulant as amphetamines.

The olefin double bond in the methallyl group is viewed as a potential source of the mixed character.

## Nomenclature

The name methallylescaline is technically unsound.

There is no union of a methallyl with an escaline -- what is really present is a mescaline with a 2-methylallyl group replacing the 4-methoxy group.

The correct name would be 4-methylallyldesmethylmescaline, which is cumbersome.

MAL is preferred as the practical name.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 40 - 65 mg.

Duration: 12 - 16 h.
