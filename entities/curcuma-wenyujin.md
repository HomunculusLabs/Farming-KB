## Pharmacology

The antitumor activity of *C. wenyujin* extracts is primarily attributed to β-elemene and furanodiene. In vitro IC₅₀ values for β-elemene range from 20–80 μM across various cancer cell lines.

Anti-inflammatory effects are mediated through suppression of COX-2, iNOS, and pro-inflammatory cytokine production in LPS-stimulated macrophages. The essential oil shows analgesic activity in acetic acid writhing and hot plate models in rodents.

Hepatoprotective effects have been demonstrated against CCl₄- and acetaminophen-induced liver injury. The mechanism involves antioxidant enzyme upregulation and inhibition of lipid peroxidation.

## Traditional Medicine

In TCM, *C. wenyujin* rhizome is classified as pungent, bitter, and cold. It enters the heart, liver, and gallbladder meridians. Its primary actions are cooling blood, resolving stasis, clearing the heart, and promoting bile flow.

The tuberous roots (Wen Yu Jin) are differentiated from the rhizome in clinical application. The roots are considered more effective for clearing heart heat and treating psychiatric conditions, while the rhizome is preferred for blood stasis and pain.

Common indications include jaundice, epigastric pain, menstrual disorders, and epilepsy. The daily dose is 3–10 g in decoction. The species is particularly associated with Wenzhou region TCM practice.

## Distribution and Cultivation

*C. wenyujin* is narrowly endemic to Zhejiang Province, China, centered on the Wenzhou (Rui'an) region. It has been cultivated in this area for over 1,000 years and is considered a regional specialty medicinal crop.

Cultivation requires warm, humid conditions with well-drained sandy loam. Propagation is by rhizome division, planted in March–April at spacing of 30 × 50 cm. Harvest occurs 10–12 months after planting.

Wenzhou produces 200–300 tonnes of dried rhizome annually. The species is protected as a geographical indication product. Wild populations are extremely rare due to centuries of cultivation pressure.

## Chemistry and Quality Control

Chinese Pharmacopoeia specifies curdione content ≥ 0.3% and germacrone ≥ 0.1% by HPLC as minimum [[cannabis-harvest-timing-visual-cues]], processing method, and geographic origin. Shade-drying preserves volatile content better than sun-drying, with 20–30% higher curdione retention.
