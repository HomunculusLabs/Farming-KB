# David Hoye

## Description

David Hoye is the author of *[[cannabis-alchemy-book]]*, a seminal counterculture publication that provided detailed instructions for the extraction, purification, and isomerization of cannabinoids from cannabis plant material. The book combined legitimate scientific literature citations (particularly from the Journal of the American Chemical Society and the Lloydia Journal of Natural Products) with practical guidance for home practitioners using the Isomerizer device. Hoye's work served as the bridge between academic cannabis chemistry and the DIY extraction community.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person / Author
- **Field**: Cannabis chemistry, counterculture publishing
- **Era**: 1970s-1980s
- **Notable work**: *Cannabis Alchemy*

## Key Facts

- Authored *Cannabis Alchemy*, which provided step-by-step procedures for [[cannabis-oil-extraction-basics]], cannabinoid isolation, and isomerization of CBD to THC.
- The book cited and adapted peer-reviewed scientific procedures, including Roger Adams' isomerization method from the Journal of the American Chemical Society (vol. 63, p. 2211) and the Lloydia Journal of Natural Products (vol. 33, no. 4, p. 456) cannabinoid isolation procedure.
- Hoye's work was referenced by Isomerizer users who visited college medical libraries to verify the chemical procedures described in the book. One correspondent noted looking up "the formula reference given by David Hoye in Cannabis Alchemy."
- The book covered advanced topics including chromatography using Florisil, isolation of pure cannabidiol via cannabidiol-bis-3,5-dinitrobenzoate intermediate, and conversion of pure cannabidiol to pure THC.
- *Cannabis Alchemy* served as a primary reference text for the community of Isomerizer and ISO-2 users, with Thai Power and its IsoNews newsletter building upon the procedures Hoye documented.
- The book included a comprehensive table of [[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-solvent-properties-and-selection-guide]] (boiling point, density, water solubility, flash point, NFPA hazard ratings) for solvents commonly used in [[vioxx-scandal-evidence-based-medicine-gaps-lessons-cannabis-research-blesching-2015]] and extraction.
- Hoye's approach emphasized understanding the underlying chemistry (isomerization mechanisms, chromatographic separation principles, solvent properties) rather than simply following recipes.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

David Hoye's work is historically significant for the [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-fungi-cannabis-cultivation]] community because it provided cultivators with the chemical knowledge to maximize the value and potency of their harvests. By translating academic research into accessible procedures, Hoye empowered a generation of home growers to process their cannabis into refined products (hash, oil, isomerized preparations) rather than consuming raw plant material. The book represents an important intersection of cannabis cultivation, chemistry, and counterculture, demonstrating how scientific knowledge could be democratized and applied at the hobbyist level.

## Key Scientific Sources Cited

- Roger Adams, Journal of the American Chemical Society, vol. 63, p. 2211: CBD-to-THC isomerization procedure using sulfuric acid in cyclohexane.
- Lloydia Journal of Natural Products, vol. 33, no. 4, p. 456: Hashish cannabinoid isolation using Florisil chromatography and the dinitrobenzoate purification route.
- The National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) supplied 13 kg of confiscated hashish for the research documented in the Lloydia procedure.

## Legacy

*Cannabis Alchemy* is considered a foundational text in the history of [[cannabis-extraction-basics]] science. While the specific procedures have been superseded by modern extraction technology, Hoye's approach of combining scientific rigor with practical accessibility established a template that continues in contemporary cannabis processing literature. The book's influence extended beyond its technical content to inspire a community of practitioners who shared their results, modifications, and innovations through the IsoNews newsletter and informal networks.

## Impact on the Cannabis Community

- Made academic cannabis chemistry accessible to non-scientists.
- Provided the chemical basis for the Isomerizer product line.
- Inspired users to visit libraries and engage directly with primary scientific literature.
- Created a framework for understanding cannabinoid conversion (isomerization) that persists in modern cannabis science education.
