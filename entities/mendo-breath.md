# Mendo Breath

## Overview

**Mendo Breath** is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its
deeply relaxing, sedative effects and complex sweet-earthy flavor profile.
Created by crossing **Mendo Montage** with **OGKB (OG Kush Breath)**, it has
earned a devoted following among medical and recreational users seeking potent
physical relaxation, stress relief, and sleep support. Bred by Symbiotic
Genetics of Northern California, Mendo Breath embodies the craft cannabis
tradition of the Emerald Triangle.

The strain features dense, oversized buds with deep purple hues, fiery orange
pistils, and a generous trichome coating. Its aroma blends sweet vanilla and
caramel with earthy undertones and a subtle fuel-like pungency from its OG
Kush heritage.

## Lineage

| Parent | Type | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| Mendo Montage | Hybrid (indica-dominant) | Female seed parent |
| OGKB (OG Kush Breath) | Indica | Pollen donor |

### Ancestral Breakdown

- **Mendo Montage**: A Mendocino-bred hybrid carrying classic indica lines
  from the region. Contributes structure, yield, and the earthy, forest-floor
  terpene profile characteristic of Humboldt/Mendocino cultivars.
- **OGKB**: A phenotype from the OG Kush Breath lineage (Girl Scout Cookies
  descendant). Known for extreme resin production, cookie-dough aroma, and
  heavy indica effects. Frequently tests above 25% THC.

## Terpene Profile

Mendo Breath is a **myrcene-dominant** chemotype, strongly influencing its
sedative character.

### Primary Terpenes

- **Myrcene** (~0.8-1.2%): Dominant terpene. Earthy, musky, herbal notes.
  Primary compound responsible for cannabis sedation — acts as a GABA-A
  receptor positive allosteric modulator and enhances blood-brain barrier
  permeability to other cannabinoids.
- **Limonene** (~0.3-0.6%): Citrus, sweet, slightly fruity. Mood-elevating
  and anxiolytic effects that balance the heavy sedation.
- **Beta-Caryophyllene** (~0.2-0.5%): Peppery, spicy, woody. A dietary
  cannabinoid activating CB2 receptors, contributing anti-inflammatory
  and analgesic effects without psychoactivity.

### Secondary Terpenes

- **Linalool**: Floral, lavender — enhances relaxation and anxiolysis
- **Pinene**: Pine — may counteract short-term memory impairment
- **Humulene**: Earthy — anti-inflammatory, appetite suppressant

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Compound | Typical Range |
|----------|---------------|
| THC | 18-25% |
| CBD | <0.1% |
| CBG | 0.1-0.5% |

Mendo Breath is a **high-THC, low-CBD** chemotype. Effects are driven by THC
and the entourage effect with its terpene profile. Novice consumers should
exercise caution.

## Effects

Effects onset within 5-15 minutes of inhalation, beginning with cerebral
euphoria that gradually yields to profound body relaxation. Peak effects at
45-60 minutes; total duration 2-3 hours.

| Effect | Intensity | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Body relaxation | Very high | Very common |
| Sleepiness / sedation | High | Very common |
| Euphoria | Moderate-high | Common |
| Appetite stimulation | Moderate | Common |
| Couch-lock | High | Common |

### Potential Negative Effects

- Dry mouth and dry eyes (very common/common)
- Dizziness at high doses or for novice users
- Lethargy next day with evening use

## Therapeutic Applications

1. **Insomnia**: Myrcene-dominant, sedative profile makes it effective for
   initiating and maintaining sleep, especially for cannabis-tolerant patients.
2. **Chronic pain**: THC + beta-caryophyllene (CB2 agonist) + myrcene
   provides multi-modal analgesia for neuropathic pain, arthritis, and
   fibromyalgia.
3. **Anxiety and stress**: Initial euphoric phase with linalool and limonene
   provides anxiolytic effects (high doses may increase anxiety).
4. **Muscle spasms**: Pronounced body relaxation benefits MS-related
   spasticity and menstrual cramps.
5. **Appetite stimulation**: Useful for chemotherapy or HIV-related appetite
   loss.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time**: 8-9 weeks indoors; early-mid October outdoors
- **Yield**: Moderate (300-400 g/m² indoors)
- **Difficulty**: Moderate — requires humidity control for dense buds
- **Ideal climate**: Mediterranean or controlled indoor environment

### Cultivation Notes

- Susceptible to bud rot (*Botrytis cinerea*) in high humidity — maintain
  RH below 50% during late flowering.
- Responds well to LST and ScrOG methods for light exposure.
- Deeper purple coloration with cooler nights (15-18°C) during weeks 6-8 —
  anthocyanin response, not stress.
- Heavy feeder in mid-flowering; reduce nitrogen, increase P/K in weeks 5-8.
- Monitor for spider mites; introduce *Phytoseiulus persimilis* as
  biological control.

## Flavor and Aroma

- **Aroma**: Sweet vanilla and caramel, earthy, with subtle diesel
  undertones. Grinding releases a more pungent, skunky quality.
- **Flavor**: Smooth, sweet smoke with prominent caramel and berry notes on
  inhalation. Exhale reveals earthy, woody, and slightly minty undertones.
