## Chemical Properties

- **Molecular formula:** C₁₇H₂₅N
- **Molecular weight:** 243.39 g/mol
- **IUPAC name:** 1-(1-Phenylcyclohexyl)piperidine
- **CAS number:** 77-10-7
- **Structure class:** Arylcyclohexylamine
- **Appearance:** White crystalline powder

PCP is a lipophilic compound readily absorbed across mucous membranes. It is
frequently applied to leafy material (parsley, marijuana, mint) for smoking,
or dissolved in liquid for oral or intranasal use.

The compound is stable under normal conditions and has a pKa of 9.4 (basic).
It is metabolized primarily by hepatic hydroxylation to inactive glucuronide
conjugates, with a terminal elimination half-life of 7–46 hours.

## Pharmacology

PCP is a potent non-competitive antagonist at the NMDA receptor, binding
within the ion channel pore and blocking calcium influx. This mechanism is
shared with ketamine and other arylcyclohexylamines but PCP's affinity is
significantly higher.

**Receptor profile:**
- NMDA receptor antagonist (Ki ≈ 50–200 nM) — primary mechanism
- Sigma-1 receptor agonist (Ki ≈ 200 nM)
- Dopamine reuptake inhibitor
- Muscarinic acetylcholine receptor antagonist
- Nicotinic acetylcholine receptor antagonist

The broad receptor activity accounts for PCP's complex and unpredictable
effects. Its dopaminergic action contributes to stimulant-like effects
including hypertension and psychomotor agitation.

## Effects

**Low doses (1–5 mg):** Mild intoxication, numbness, slurred speech,
ataxia, euphoria, stimulation. Superficially resembles alcohol intoxication.

**Moderate doses (5–10 mg):** Analgesia, anesthesia, dissociation from
body, nystagmus, marked ataxia, hallucinations, paranoia, aggressive or
violent behavior. Duration 4–8 hours (may persist 24–48 hours in some).

**High doses (10+ mg):** Coma, seizures, hypertensive crisis,
hyperthermia, rhabdomyolysis, acute kidney injury, death. The drug has a
narrow margin between recreational and dangerous doses.

PCP's effects are notoriously unpredictable. The same dose may produce
sedation in one person and extreme agitation, violence, or psychosis in
another. This unpredictability is a major factor in its dangerous profile.
