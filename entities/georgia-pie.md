# Georgia Pie

## Overview

Georgia Pie is a hybrid cannabis strain bred by Seed Junky Genetics, a prominent
breeding operation based in Los Angeles known for developing many of the most
sought-after modern cultivars. It was created by crossing **Girl Scout Cookies
(GSC)** with a **Pie phenotype** from the Cookies genetic line.

The strain has gained a dedicated following for its intensely sweet, fruity aroma
reminiscent of baked peach or cherry pie. Its effects are characterized by a
heavy cerebral euphoria that transitions into deep physical relaxation, making it
popular among both recreational and medical users.

Georgia Pie typically tests between 20–28% THC, placing it among the
higher-potency strains in the contemporary market. It has received notable
attention on social media and cannabis forums since its release in the early
2020s.

## Lineage

Georgia Pie is a cross of **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** × **Pie** (a select
Cookies-family phenotype).

GSC, itself a cross of OG Kush × Durban Poison, contributes dense resin
production, complex cookie-and-earth terpenes, and potent hybrid effects. The
specific Pie parent is a terpene-heavy phenotype expressing dominant sweet, fruity
esters.

The genetic ratio is roughly 50/50 indica-sativa, though phenotypes may lean
slightly indica-dominant in expression. The strain inherits the Cookies lineage's
tendency for vibrant purple and green coloration in cooler growing conditions.

Seed Junky has used Georgia Pie in further breeding projects, and it has
contributed to derivative crosses including Peach Pie and various F1 selections.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, dense, and tightly structured with a classic indica
morphology. The calyxes are chunky and heavily coated in trichomes, giving the
flowers a frosted, almost sugary appearance.

Colors range from deep forest green to shades of lavender and purple,
particularly in the later stages of flowering when exposed to cooler nighttime
temperatures. Bright orange pistils contrast sharply against the darker foliage.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, with large, bulbous glandular
trichomes visible across all surfaces. The resin production makes Georgia Pie
attractive for hash and concentrate extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is the strain's signature trait — an intensely sweet, fruity bouquet
evocative of fresh-baked peach or cherry pie with a graham cracker crust
undertone. Notes of ripe stone fruit, berry, and vanilla are present on the
front end.

Secondary aromas include earthy, doughy, and slightly skunky undertones from the
GSC lineage. The combination creates a complex dessert-like profile that is
immediately recognizable.

On inhalation, the flavor mirrors the aroma — sweet and fruity with a creamy,
biscuit-like finish. Exhale brings out more of the earthy, herbal notes with
subtle peppery spice from [[blesching-caryophyllene-cb2-agonist|caryophyllene]]. The aftertaste lingers with a pleasant
baked-goods sweetness.

## Terpene Profile

The dominant terpenes in Georgia Pie are **caryophyllene**, **[[limonene|limonene]]**, and
**linalool**, with supporting contributions from **[[myrcene|myrcene]]**, **humulene**, and
**ocimene**.

Caryophyllene contributes peppery spice and is the primary driver of the
strain's anti-inflammatory properties. Limonene provides the bright, citrusy
top notes that enhance the fruity perception. Linalool adds floral sweetness and
promotes the relaxing, anxiolytic effects.

The unique "pie" terpene expression is driven by specific ester and lactone
combinations that are amplified in this genetic combination. Terpene content
typically ranges from 1.5–3% total, which is moderately high for modern
cultivars.

## Effects

Onset is relatively fast, typically within 5–10 minutes of inhalation. Initial
effects are cerebral and uplifting, characterized by a rush of euphoria, enhanced
mood, and mild creative stimulation.

As the high progresses (30–60 minutes), effects shift toward deep physical
relaxation and body warmth. The transition is smooth, with the cerebral and
somatic components overlapping rather than sharply dividing.

Common recreational applications include social settings, creative pursuits, and
evening relaxation. Medical users report effectiveness for stress relief, mild
pain management, appetite stimulation, and insomnia when used in larger doses.

Duration is moderate to long, typically 2–4 hours depending on dose and
individual tolerance. The comedown is gradual with minimal residual fog.

## Cultivation

Georgia Pie is considered moderately difficult to cultivate, suited to
experienced growers familiar with Cookies-family genetics.

Flowering time is approximately 8–10 weeks indoors. Outdoor harvest occurs in
mid to late October in northern temperate climates. The plants respond well to
low-stress training (LST) and scrog methods.

Indoor yields average 350–450 g/m² under optimal conditions. The strain is
moderately nutrient-hungry during mid-flowering and benefits from increased
phosphorus and potassium supplementation.

Plants are moderately resistant to common pests and molds but can be sensitive
to high humidity during late flowering due to dense bud structure. Adequate
airflow and dehumidification are recommended. Cooler nighttime temperatures
(around 18°C) during weeks 5–8 of flowering enhance purple coloration.
