# Blueberry

**DJ Short's Blueberry** | **Blueberry Kush** (when crossed with OG)

Blueberry is a legendary indica-dominant cannabis cultivar created by breeder DJ Short in the 1970s–1980s. Widely regarded as one of the most important foundational strains in modern cannabis genetics, Blueberry is celebrated for its distinctively fruity aroma and flavor — evocative of fresh blueberries — and its deeply relaxing, euphoric effects. It was one of the first strains to gain widespread recognition for its terpene-driven sensory profile, winning the High Times Cannabis Cup in 2000 and cementing its status as a classic.

## Genetic Background

### Parentage
DJ Short developed Blueberry through extensive breeding work beginning in the late 1970s, working with landrace genetics sourced from diverse regions:
- **Afghan indica:** Contributed the dense bud structure, resin production, and heavy body effects
- **Thai sativa:** Contributed the sweet, fruity terpene profile and cerebral uplift
- **Purple Thai (Oaxacan × Highland Thai):** A complex sativa landrace cross that contributed both the purple coloration potential and additional fruity terpene complexity

### Breeding History
- DJ Short began working with these genetics in the Pacific Northwest during the late 1970s and early 1980s
- The strain was developed through multiple generations of careful phenotypic selection, with DJ Short selecting for aroma, flavor, color, and effect over approximately 15 years before stabilizing the line
- The original Blueberry genetics were acquired by Dutch Passion Seeds in the 1990s, who commercialized the strain and brought it to the European market
- DJ Short continued maintaining his own line (the "DJ Short Blueberry" or "True Blueberry") separately from the Dutch Passion version, leading to phenotypic divergence between the two lines

### Genetic Legacy
Blueberry has served as a parent or grandparent to hundreds of descendant strains:
- **Blue Dream:** Blueberry × DJ Short's Haze — arguably the most commercially successful hybrid of the 2010s
- **Blueberry Kush:** Blueberry × OG Kush — amplified indica sedation
- **Blue Cheese:** Blueberry × UK Cheese — fruity × pungent cheese terpenes
- **Blue Widow:** Blueberry × White Widow — fruity resin production
- **Berry White:** Blueberry × White Widow variant
- **Aurora Indica:** Blueberry × Northern Lights — heavy indica for pain and sleep
- **Jedi Kush:** Blueberry × SFV OG — potent indica hybrid

## Botanical Characteristics

### Growth Pattern
- **Height:** Short to medium, 60–120 cm indoors; taller outdoors up to 180 cm
- **Structure:** Compact, bushy indica growth with strong lateral branching; responds well to low-stress training (LST) and SCROG
- **Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks indoors (9 weeks optimal); late September to mid-October outdoors
- **Yield:** Moderate to good, 300–400 g/m² indoors; 350–500 g/plant outdoors
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — relatively forgiving for beginners but requires attention to humidity; branches can become heavy with dense buds and may need support
- **Stretch:** Minimal (20–30%) — typical indica behavior

### Morphology
- **Leaves:** Broad, dark green indica leaf structure; many phenotypes develop striking purple, violet, and magenta coloration during flowering, especially when exposed to cooler night temperatures (below 15°C)
- **Buds:** Medium to large, dense and compact with a chunky structure; calyxes swell significantly; colors range from bright green to deep purple and blue with orange to rust-colored pistils
- **Trichomes:** Thick, resinous trichome coverage; glandular trichomes produce copious amounts of the terpenes responsible for the characteristic blueberry aroma
- **Color expression:** The purple coloration is an anthocyanin response to temperature stress — cooler nighttime temperatures during the final 2–3 weeks of flowering maximize color development

## Cannabinoid Profile

Typical laboratory analysis ranges:
- **THC:** 15–22% (classic phenotypes); modern selections 18–24%
- **CBD:** 0.1–0.3% (low but detectable)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.4%
- **CBC:** Trace to low

Blueberry is not a high-THC strain by contemporary standards but is noted for a well-rounded cannabinoid profile that contributes to its smooth, full-bodied experience. The moderate THC levels combined with the rich terpene profile produce effects that users consistently rate as more nuanced than potency alone would predict.

## Terpene Profile

The terpene profile is the defining feature of Blueberry and responsible for its iconic name:

### Dominant Terpenes
- **Myrcene (0.8–1.5%):** The dominant terpene; earthy, musky, herbal notes forming the base of the aroma profile; contributes significantly to the sedative body effects
- **Caryophyllene (0.3–0.7%):** Peppery, spicy, woody; CB₂ receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory properties; adds complexity to the flavor
- **Linalool (0.2–0.6%):** Floral, sweet, berry-like; anxiolytic and sedative; this terpene is a key contributor to the characteristic blueberry aroma and the relaxing quality of effects

### Secondary Terpenes
- **Pinene (α and β):** Pine, fresh forest; counteracts some THC-induced cognitive impairment; contributes subtle freshness
- **Humulene:** Woody, earthy; anti-inflammatory and appetite-suppressant properties
- **Nerolidol:** Woody, fruity, slightly floral; sedative, enhances skin penetration of other compounds
- **Terpinolene:** Piney, floral, herbal; mildly sedative
- **Ocimene:** Sweet, herbaceous; uplifting and decongestant

### Aroma and Flavor
- **Aroma:** Intensely fruity and sweet, dominated by fresh blueberry and mixed berry notes with underlying earthy, hashy undertones
- **Flavor:** Sweet blueberry on the inhale with earthy, herbal, and slightly spicy notes on the exhale; the flavor is remarkably faithful to actual blueberries when well-cured
- **Smoke:** Smooth, expansive, and flavorful; one of the most palatable strains for those sensitive to harsh smoke

## Effects

### Psychoactive Experience
- **Onset:** 1–5 minutes (smoked); 30–90 minutes (edible)
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (smoked); 4–6 hours (edible)
- **Character:** Predominantly physical relaxation with a gentle, warm cerebral euphoria — a "couch-lock" strain with enough mental clarity to remain functional at moderate doses
- **Initial phase:** Warm body sensation, mood elevation, mild cerebral stimulation, heightened sensory appreciation of food and music
- **Later phase:** Deep physical relaxation, heavy eyelids, progressive sedation, sleep facilitation at higher doses

### Therapeutic Applications (anecdotal)
- **Insomnia:** One of the most commonly recommended strains for sleep difficulties
- **Pain management:** Effective for mild to moderate chronic pain, muscle spasms, and tension headaches
- **Anxiety and stress:** The linalool-rich terpene profile provides anxiolytic effects without being overly sedating at low doses
- **Appetite stimulation:** Reliable appetite enhancer
- **PTSD:** Some users report benefits for trauma-related symptoms, though individual responses vary widely

## Cultivation Notes

- **Climate preference:** Thrives in temperate to Mediterranean climates; sensitive to extreme heat
- **Temperature:** Optimal daytime 22–28°C; nighttime temperatures of 12–18°C during late flowering enhance purple coloration
- **Nutrients:** Light to moderate feeder; benefits from calmag supplementation; avoid excessive nitrogen in late flowering
- **Humidity:** Moderate during vegetative growth; reduce to 40–50% during flowering to prevent bud rot in the dense colas
- **Training:** Responds exceptionally well to LST, SCROG, and mild topping; heavy training not recommended due to naturally compact structure
- **Curing:** Extended cure of 4–6+ weeks dramatically enhances the blueberry flavor; terpene development continues well beyond the standard 2-week cure

## Awards and Recognition

- **2000 High Times Cannabis Cup:** 1st Place — Indica category (the award that brought Blueberry to global prominence)
- Widely cited as one of the "top 10 strains of all time" in cannabis media
- Consistently ranked among the best-tasting strains in consumer polls and critic reviews
