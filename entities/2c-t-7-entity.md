# 2C-T-7 (Blue Mystic, T7)

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propylthiophenethylamine

**PiHKAL #43** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +++

## Overview

2C-T-7 is a psychedelic phenethylamine of the 2C-T (thioether) sub-family of the [[2c-family-compounds-overview]]. It was synthesized and bioassayed by [[alexander-shulgin]] as part of the systematic exploration of 4-alkylthio substituted 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamines. The propylthio group at the 4-position produces one of the most potent and longest-acting compounds in the 2C series. Shulgin ranked 2C-T-7 among the most acceptable and intrinsically rich phenethylamines, alongside 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2, 2c-b-entity|2C-B, mescaline-entity|mescaline, and 2c-e-entity|2C-E.

## Qualitative Effects

- Very strong visual effects: rich color enhancement, geometric patterns, visual distortion
- Deep introspective and philosophical experience
- Strong empathogenic qualities at moderate doses — compared to mdma-entity|MDMA but "more grounded"
- Significant body load, including muscular tension (neck, shoulders, legs) throughout the day
- Nausea is common, especially at higher doses
- Enhanced appreciation of music, art, and nature
- Eyes-closed imagery can be elaborate and immersive
- Combination of psychedelic and stimulant qualities
- Very slow onset contributing to redosing risks
- Periodic cycling of nausea and well-being reported
- Increased vocal freedom and musical integration noted in therapeutic contexts

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

Reports describe 2C-T-7 as producing powerful visual and emotional experiences. The compound is noted for its slow onset and very long duration. At common doses, the experience combines strong visuals with emotional openness and introspection. The visuals have been described as "adaptable" — users report being able to shift between different visual styles reminiscent of other psychedelics.

### Selected PiHKAL Trip Reports

**20 mg (first report):** "A wonderful day of integration and work. Took about 2 hours for the onset. Some nausea on and off — that seemed to cycle periodically throughout the day. Visuals were great, much like mescaline but less sparkly. Lots of movement and aliveness — velvety appearance and increased depth perception. Neck and shoulder tension throughout the day along with legs... Working was very integrative. Back and forth constantly between wonderful God-space — similar to MDMA but more grounded — then always back to sadness. I felt that it really showed me where I was unfinished, but with self-loving and tolerance. Tremendous processing and letting go."

**20 mg (second report):** "I lay down with music, and become engrossed with being as still as possible. I feel that if I can be totally, completely still, I will hear the inner voice of the universe. As I do this, the music becomes incredibly beautiful. I see the extraordinary importance of simply listening, listening to everything, to people and to nature, with wide open receptivity. Something very, very special happens at the still point, so I keep working on it. When I become totally still, a huge burst of energy is released. And it explodes so that it takes enormous effort to quiet it all down in order to be still again. Great fun."

**25 mg:** "This was a marvelous and strange evening. This 2C-T-7 is good and friendly and wonderful as I remember it. I think it is going to take the place of 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2 in my heart. It is a truly good material. I got involved with a documentary on television. It was about certain people of Bolivia, people living in the high mountains and about a small village which — perhaps alone among all the places in the country — maintains the old Inca ways, the old traditions, the old language... The shaman faces, male and female, were startling in their intensity and earthy depth."

**30 mg:** "The visuals have an adaptable character to them. I can use them to recreate any hallucinogenic substance I have known and loved. With open eyes, I can go easily into LSD flowing visuals, or into the warm earth world of Peyote, or I can stop them altogether. With closed eyes, there are Escher-like graphics with a lot of chiaroscuro, geometric patterns with oppositional play of sculptured light and dark values. Green light."

## Synthesis (from PiHKAL)

The synthesis of 2C-T-7 begins with 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol (the same key intermediate used for 2c-t-2-entity|2C-T-2):

1. **S-alkylation:** 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol is alkylated with n-propyl bromide using KOH in MeOH. Exothermic reaction with white solid deposition. The sulfide (pale yellow oil, pleasant fruity smell) is used without distillation.

2. **Vilsmeier formylation:** Treatment with N-methylformanilide/POCl₃, followed by quenching in warm water. The aldehyde crystallizes as pale ivory solids. Recrystallized from MeOH: mp 76–77 °C. NMR confirmed correct 4-position substitution (two aromatic singlets at 6.81 and 7.27 ppm).

3. **Nitrostyrene formation:** Condensation with nitromethane and ammonium acetate in steam bath. Orange crystals from IPA: mp 120–121 °C.

4. **LAH reduction:** Using a 1 M LAH solution in THF with 100% H₂SO₄ quenching. The free base distills at 140–150 °C at 0.25 mm/Hg. HCl salt crystallized from IPA/Et₂O as "spectacular white crystals."

## Structure-Activity Relationships

Within the 2C-T series, the progression from ethylthio (2C-T-2) to propylthio (2C-T-7) follows the familiar pattern seen in the 4-alkyl series: increasing chain length on the sulfur increases potency and duration. The propylthio substitution pattern has proven to be one of the most pharmacologically active in the phenethylamine series, with the amphetamine homologue (ALEPH) also being highly potent.

Compared to the oxygen analogue series, the sulfur atom's larger atomic radius and greater polarizability contribute to enhanced receptor affinity. The dramatic contrast between the near-inactivity of 2c-o-4-entity|2C-O-4 and the potency of its sulfur counterpart 2C-T-4 demonstrates sulfur's unique pharmacological contribution.

## Tweetio Homologue

The 2-ethoxy homologue of 2C-T-7 (2CT7-2ETO) was synthesized and bioassayed:

- Benzaldehyde mp: 69–71 °C
- Nitrostyrene mp: 106–106.5 °C
- HCl salt mp: 187–189 °C
- At 20 mg: effects felt quickly, modest but real eyes-closed visuals, very short-lived (~5 h). Uncomfortable headache the following day, intuitively felt to be an after-effect.

## N-Methylated Derivative

Shulgin attempted to create an N-methyl-N-isopropyl derivative of 2C-T-7. The free base was refluxed in butyl formate to form the formamide, which was reduced with lithium hydride to yield METHYL-2C-T-7 (2,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-propyl-N-methylphenethylamine). The product distilled at 150–170 °C at 0.4 mm/Hg, but only 65 mg of an impure brown hydrochloride salt was obtained, and the project was dropped.

## Extensions and Commentary

2C-T-7 gained significant popularity on the research chemical market in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Its widespread availability led to several adverse incidents, including fatalities, particularly when insufflated (snorted) at high doses. These incidents led to emergency scheduling in several countries. The insufflation route appears to dramatically increase the risk of overdose — oral administration is the only route documented in the PiHKAL reports.

The propylthio substitution pattern produces a compound that Shulgin considered among the most rewarding in the entire phenethylamine family. Its combination of visual richness, emotional depth, and therapeutic potential made it a favorite among experimental psychotherapists working with follow-up sessions to MDMA.

## Safety Warnings

- **Very slow onset** (1-2 hours) creates risk of accidental overdose from re-dosing
- **Insufflation is extremely dangerous** — multiple fatalities reported from snorting 2C-T-7
- **Very long duration** — plan for 10-15 hours of effects
- Significant body load: muscular tension (neck, shoulders, legs) and cycling nausea
- Not recommended for inexperienced users
- Oral route only — insufflation and injection are far more dangerous
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Emergency scheduled in many countries; Schedule I in the US
