## Overview

In Australia, the species occurs across most states except the arid interior. Populations in southeastern Australia are notable for producing exceptionally large bladders.

## Ecology and Interactions

*U. australis* provides habitat for various aquatic microorganisms. The dense network of stolons and leaf segments offers refuge for small invertebrates and juvenile fish.

The species can form extensive floating mats that reduce light penetration to the water column below. This can suppress algal growth and influence the composition of submerged plant communities.

Pollinators include small bees, flies, and butterflies that visit the aerial flowers. The flowers are self-compatible but also attract insect visitors for cross-pollination.
