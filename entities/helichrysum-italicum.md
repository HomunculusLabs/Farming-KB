---# Helichrysum italicum (Curry Plant, Immortelle)

**Helichrysum italicum**, commonly known as **immortelle**, **everlasting flower**, **curry plant**, or **Italian strawflower**, is a strongly aromatic perennial herb of the family **Asteraceae**. Native to the Mediterranean basin, it is celebrated for its extraordinary [[childrens-herbal-medicine]].

## Taxonomy and Nomenclature

- **Kingdom:** Plantae
- **Division:** Magnoliophyta
- **Class:** Magnoliopsida
- **Order:** Asterales
- **Family:** Asteraceae
- **Genus:** *Helichrysum*
- **Species:** *H. italicum*

The genus name derives from the Greek *helios* (sun) and *chrysos* (gold), referring to the bright golden-yellow flower heads. The specific epithet *italicum* denotes its Italian origins. The common name "immortelle" (French for "immortal") reflects the flowers' remarkable ability to retain their shape and color indefinitely when dried — a characteristic shared with other *Helichrysum* species. The name "curry plant" comes from the strong curry-like aroma of the foliage, though the plant is not used in actual curry preparations and is unrelated to curry tree (*[[agaricus-campestris-biology-and-life-cycle]]:** Perennial subshrub
- **Height:** 20–60 cm (8–24 inches)
- **Form:** Low, mounding, woody at the base
- **Lifespan:** 5–10 years in cultivation

### Leaves
- **Shape:** Linear to narrowly lanceolate, 10–40 mm long × 1–3 mm wide
- **Color:** Silvery-gray on the upper surface, white-tomentose underneath
- **Aroma:** Extremely strong, warm, spicy, curry-like scent when crushed

### Flowers
- **Type:** Capitula in dense corymbose clusters, 3–5 mm across
- **Color:** Bright golden-yellow with papery involucral bracts
- **Blooming period:** June to August
- **Dried flowers:** Retain color and shape almost indefinitely ("everlasting")

## Distribution and Habitat

*H. italicum* is native to the **Mediterranean region**:

- **Southern Europe:** France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Balkans
- **Mediterranean islands:** Corsica, Sardinia, Sicily, Crete, Balearic Islands
- **North Africa:** Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia
- **Turkey** and the **Middle East** (eastern extent of range)

It inhabits dry rocky hillsides, coastal cliffs, open woodlands, and disturbed sites throughout the Mediterranean basin. Thrives in full sun, tolerates extreme drought, prefers well-drained calcareous soils. Hardy to approximately -5°C; not cold-hardy [[query-how-to-grow-and-use-oca-in-temperate-climates]].

## Essential Oil

*H. italicum* essential oil is among the **most prized and expensive** in aromatherapy, often called "the wonder oil of aromatherapy" or "liquid gold."

### Composition
The essential oil is a complex mixture of over 150 identified compounds. The primary constituents vary significantly by chemotype, subspecies, and harvest location:

- **Neryl acetate** (5–40%) — anti-inflammatory, spasmolytic
- **α-Pinene** (5–25%) — anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial
- **γ-Curcumene** (5–20%) — anti-inflammatory
- **Italidiones** (unique diketones found only in *H. italicum*) — skin-regenerating, anti-hematoma
- **Limonene** (2–10%)
- **Linalool** (trace–5%)
- **β-Caryophyllene** (trace–8%)
