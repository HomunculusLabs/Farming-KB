## Beta-Carboline (9H-Pyrido[3,4-b]indole)

## Overview

Beta-carbolines (β-carbolines, 9H-pyrido[3,4-b]indoles) are a class of
tricyclic nitrogen-containing compounds structurally related to [[tryptamine]],
formed by the fusion of an [[indole]] ring with a pyridine ring.
They occur naturally in a wide range of plants ([[harmala-alkaloids]] —
harmine, harmaline, and harmalol — which are potent reversible inhibitors
of monoamine oxidase A (MAO-A).
Beta-carbolines play a central role in [[tihkal-oral-activity-mao-inhibition]] of DMT, and they have
independent psychoactive effects at higher doses including visual distortions
and oneiric states.

## Chemical Properties

- **Core structure:** Tricyclic 9H-pyrido[3,4-b]indole scaffold
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₁H₈N₂ (norharmane, parent compound)
- **Molecular weight:** 168.20 g/mol (norharmane)
- **Structure types:**
  - **Fully aromatic:** β-Carboline, norharmane, harmane
  - **Dihydro (3,4-dihydro-β-carboline):** Tetrahydroharmine, harmaline
  - **Tetrahydro (1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-β-carboline):** Harmine (partially),
    harmalol, harmaline
- **Solubility:** Basic compounds; soluble in organic solvents and acidic
  aqueous solutions; poorly soluble in neutral water
- **Fluorescence:** Most β-carbolines exhibit strong blue fluorescence under
  UV light — a useful identification feature

## Pharmacology

### Monoamine Oxidase Inhibition

- **Mechanism:** Reversible competitive inhibition of MAO-A (RIMA type)
  - MAO-A preferentially degrades serotonin, norepinephrine, and melatonin
  - Inhibition increases synaptic levels of these monoamines
- **Selectivity:** Harmine and harmaline are 10–50× more potent against
  MAO-A than MAO-B
- **Reversibility:** Unlike irreversible [[ayahuasca-pharmacology]]

### Serotonergic Receptor Activity

- **5-HT₂A:** Weak partial agonist activity — contributes to visual effects
  at higher doses
- **5-HT₂C:** Agonist activity — may mediate anxiogenic effects
- **5-HT₁A:** Variable affinity depending on specific compound
- **Imidazoline receptors:** Harmine binds I₂ sites with nanomolar affinity,
  contributing to its diverse pharmacological profile
