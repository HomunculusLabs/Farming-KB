# Super Silver Haze

## Overview
Super Silver Haze is a legendary sativa-dominant hybrid bred in the mid-1990s by Shantibaba of Green House Seed Company in Amsterdam. Created by crossing three iconic strains — [[cannabis-strain-mango-haze-northern-lights-skunk-haze]] #5, Haze, and Skunk #1 — it dominated the High Times Cannabis Cup, winning three consecutive first-place titles from 1997 to 1999. Known for its intensely euphoric, long-lasting cerebral high and complex spicy-citrus aroma, Super Silver Haze remains one of the most influential [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] [[haze-lineage-and-legacy-in-modern-breeding]] history. Its genetics have been used in hundreds of subsequent crosses.

## Strain Classification
- **Type:** Sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 70% sativa / 30% indica)
- **Lineage:** Northern Lights #5 × Haze × Skunk #1 (three-way polyhybrid)
- **Breeder:** Shantibaba (Green House Seed Company), Amsterdam, Netherlands, mid-1990s
- **THC content:** 18–23% (modern cuts)
- **CBD content:** Low (<1%)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile
- **Primary aromas:** Haze spice, earthy skunk, citrus, pepper
- **Secondary notes:** Sandalwood, incense, sweet tropical fruit, subtle coffee, herbal
- **Taste:** Smooth, expansive smoke; peppery exhale with lingering citrus-sweet aftertaste; complex and layered throughout the session
- **Key terpenes:** Myrcene, β-caryophyllene, limonene, α-pinene, terpinolene
- **Color expression:** Light green to silver buds; heavy trichome coverage gives shimmering appearance under light; orange pistils woven throughout; calyxes densely packed, elongated
- **Smoke quality:** Thick white smoke; smooth on throat despite high resin content; minimal cough reflex; lingering spicy aftertaste on palate
- **Room note:** Pungent and noticeable; skunk-haze hybrid character fills a room quickly; not discreet

## Effects and Pharmacology
- **Onset:** 5–10 minutes after inhalation; gradual cerebral climb
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours (long-lasting for a sativa-dominant strain)
- **Primary effects:** Intense euphoria, creative energy, sociability, focused motivation, mood elevation
- **Body effects:** Gentle body relaxation without sedation; physical energy maintained; mild tremor at higher doses
- **[[oner-sativa-medical-applications]]:** [[cannabis-chronic-fatigue-research]], depression, stress, PTSD, ADHD, migraines, mild pain management, nausea
- **Side effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, paranoia at high doses (>0.5g joint for sensitive users), anxiety in predisposed individuals, mild tachycardia, dizziness in novice users
- **Psychoactive profile:** Clear-headed, non-drowsy stimulation in the Haze tradition; less racy/anxious than pure Haze due to Northern Lights and Skunk stabilization
- **Tolerance curve:** Moderate; regular users report 2–3 week tolerance buildup requiring brief tolerance breaks
- **Time-of-day preference:** Best suited for daytime use; can cause insomnia if consumed within 3 hours of bedtime
- **Strain behavior variation:** Phenotype-dependent — some cuts lean more uplifting/creative, others more physically relaxing; terpene ratios vary significantly between breeders' versions

## Growing Characteristics
- **Flowering time:** 9–11 weeks (10 weeks typical for full maturity)
- **Yield:** 400–600 g/m² indoor; 500–800 g/plant outdoor
- **Height:** 120–180 cm indoor; 200–300 cm outdoor (vigorous stretch in early flowering)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced; stretch requires early training and height management
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean or subtropical; moderate humidity (45–55% RH in flowering); susceptible to bud rot in high humidity
- **Resistance:** Moderate pest resistance; good mold resistance in controlled environments; [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]] risk in humid conditions
- **Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder; responds well to phosphorus and potassium boost in weeks 5–8; watch for [[query-how-to-fix-nitrogen-toxicity-in-cannabis]] in vegetative phase
- **Training:** SCROG (screen of green) highly recommended; LST (low-stress training) effective; topping at 4th–5th node promotes lateral branching
- **Clone performance:** Clones root readily in 7–10 days; mother plants maintain vigor for 3–6 months before re-vegging needed
- **Resin production:** Very high; trichomes turn from clear to milky at 8–9 weeks; amber at 10+ weeks for more sedative effect
- **Vegetative time:** Recommended 3–4 weeks from seed; 2–3 weeks from clone before flip
- **Pot size:** Minimum 15L for indoor; 50–100L+ for outdoor full-season plants
- **Light requirements:** 600W HPS or equivalent LED minimum per m²; responds well to CO₂ supplementation at 800–1200 ppm
- **Common issues:** Stretch in weeks 1–3 of flowering (up to 2–3× height increase); [[cannabis-nutrient-lockout-and-ph]] from pH drift in hydro; calcium/[[query-how-to-fix-magnesium-deficiency-in-cannabis]] common in late flowering

## Genetic Relatives and Crosses
- **Northern Lights #5:** Contributes compact bud structure, resin production, and physical relaxation; stabilizes the Haze genetics and reduces flowering time from Haze's typical 12–16 weeks
- **Haze:** Mexican × Colombian × Thai × Indian landrace composite; contributes the signature cerebral high, complex terpene profile, extended flowering time, and tall stature; the dominant psychoactive contributor
- **Skunk #1:** Afghani × Acapulco Gold × Colombian; contributes vigor, yield, disease resistance, and the pungent skunk aroma backbone; hybrid bridge between indica and sativa parents
- **Notable descendants:** SSH crosses include Super Lemon Haze (SSH × Lemon Skunk), Arjan's Ultra Haze, Amnesia Haze (partially SSH-influenced lineage), and numerous modern haze-dominant hybrids
- **Influence on modern breeding:** SSH terpene profile (high terpinolene + myrcene) has become a target phenotype for "exotic" and "gassy" breeding programs in the 2020s
- **Available versions:** Mr. Nice Seed Bank (original Shantibaba cut), Green House Seed Co. (commercial selection), numerous phenotypic selections from underground breeders; significant genetic drift between sources

## Cultural Significance
- Three-time consecutive High Times Cannabis Cup champion (1997, 1998, 1999) — unprecedented in Cup history at the time; only a handful of strains have achieved back-to-back wins
