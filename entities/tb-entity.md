# TB (4-Thiobuscaline; 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-butylthiophenethylamine)

TB is a sulfur-substituted phenethylamine and the n-butylthio analogue of [[buscaline-entity]]. It is notable for exhibiting a unique euphoria-producing pattern without full psychedelic effects, regardless of dose. The compound is listed as PIHKAL entry #149.

## Chemical Classification

TB belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 3,5-dimethoxy-4-(n)-butylthiophenethylamine, also known as 4-thiobuscaline.

TB is the n-butylthio analogue of [[buscaline-entity]], where the 4-butoxy group of buscaline is replaced by a 4-n-butylthio group.

The compound follows the thio-analogue pattern of replacing an oxygen atom with sulfur.

The n-butyl chain on the sulfur is four carbons long.

## Pharmacological Profile

TB is an active compound with a dosage range of 60 to 120 milligrams.

The duration of effects is approximately 8 hours.

Unlike most psychedelic amphetamines, TB does not produce classic psychedelic effects at any tested dose.

Instead, the compound produces a unique pattern of euphoria without the characteristic visual or cognitive alterations of psychedelics.

## Qualitative Effects

At 35 mg, subjects became aware of something at about one hour.

An attempt to rekindle the state with an additional 20 mg was unsuccessful -- too little and too late.

At 60 mg, a very subtle threshold effect was noted, with some activity persisting six hours into the experience.

At 80 mg, subjects reported a vague awareness of something, with body discomfort possibly reflecting impurity.

At 120 mg, the course of action was described as extremely clear, with development beginning at approximately 5 hours.

The experience was characterized by a clean, simple euphoria without the complexity of classic psychedelic states.

## The Anti-Depressant Pattern

TB exhibits a unique pharmacological pattern that was first observed with [[ariadne-entity]] and called an "anti-depressant" effect.

This action is characterized by euphoria without the psychedelic or stimulant effects that typically increase with dose.

The effect does not deepen or become more psychedelic at higher doses, unlike most compounds in this class.

The commentary notes this is similar to the "Tomso effect," where euphoria is produced without the full psychedelic character.

The term "dose-dependent" applies to most drug actions, but TB represents an exception: euphoria does not scale with dose.

This perpetual series of "thresholds" produces a simple and ephemeral catalyst of euphoria without substance.

The experience never reaches the psychedelic or stimulant level that increases with increased dose.

## Dosage and Duration

Dosage: 60 - 120 mg.

Duration: about 8 h.
