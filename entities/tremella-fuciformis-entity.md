# Tremella fuciformis

**Snow fungus** (银耳, yín'ěr), also known as **silver ear** or **white
jelly mushroom**, is a gelatinous basidiomycete prized in Chinese cuisine
and traditional medicine for over 1,800 years. Its translucent, white,
jelly-like fruiting bodies have a delicate flavor and unique slippery
texture that absorbs surrounding flavors.

Snow fungus is parasitic on *[[tremella-aurantialba]]* (Golden ear) — yellow-orange; premium in
  Chinese market
- *[[lentinula-edodes]]
- [[inonotus-obliquus]]
