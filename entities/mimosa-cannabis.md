## Mimosa (Cannabis Strain)

**Mimosa** is a sativa-dominant [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain renowned for its uplifting, energizing effects and distinctive citrus-forward aroma. A cross between **Clementine** and **[[larry-og]]*
- Famous for sweet grape and blueberry flavors with relaxing body effects
- High caryophyllene and [[myrcene]] content
- Contributes body relaxation, berry undertones, and enhanced [[cannabis-hybrid-vigor-and-phenotypes-clarke]] from the complementary parent profiles
- Several phenotypes exist, ranging from more Clementine-dominant (taller, more cerebral) to Purple Punch-dominant (shorter, more relaxing)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma
- **Primary:** Bright, sweet orange and tangerine — immediately recognizable citrus burst
- **Secondary:** Tropical fruit notes, subtle grape and berry undertones from Purple Punch heritage
- **Tertiary:** Earthy, herbal base with faint floral and spice notes
- **Overall impression:** Like walking through a citrus grove at sunrise — fresh, sweet, and invigorating

### Flavor
- **Inhale:** Sharp, sweet orange and lemon zest with a sugary quality
- **Mid-palate:** Tropical fruit medley — mango, pineapple hints blending with grape
- **Exhale:** Smooth earthy finish with lingering citrus and subtle berry sweetness
- **Aftertaste:** Clean, refreshing citrus that persists pleasantly

### Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Typical Range | Primary Effects |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **[[limonene]]** | 1.0–2.5% | Mood elevation, stress relief, anti-anxiety |
| **Myrcene** | 0.5–1.5% | Relaxation, sedation (at higher levels), anti-inflammatory |
| **β-Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.8% | Anti-inflammatory, [[bay-11-medical-sativa-granddaddy-purp-pain-relief-oner]] via CB2 [[humulene]]** | 0.1–0.2% | Appetite suppression, anti-inflammatory |

The limonene-dominant profile is the defining characteristic — Mimosa consistently ranks among the most citrus-forward strains available.

## Cannabinoid Profile

### Typical Ranges
- **THC:** 17–24% (some premium phenotypes reach 27%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** Trace amounts in some phenotypes

### Dominant Cannabinoids
Mimosa is a **THC-dominant strain** with virtually no CBD. The [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]] arises primarily from the interplay between high THC and the limonene-rich terpene profile. Users seeking CBD benefits should look to companion strains or CBD-dominant products.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid (2–5 minutes inhalation); peak 30–60 minutes
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (smoking/vaping); 3–5 hours (edibles)
- **Come-down:** Gentle, gradual; minimal crash

### Primary Effects
- **Euphoria:** Uplifting, happy, and optimistic mood elevation — the signature effect
- **Energy and motivation:** Stimulating mental energy without jitters; enhances productivity
- **Creativity:** Enhances divergent thinking and artistic expression
- **Focus:** Many users report improved concentration on creative tasks
- **Sociability:** Promotes engaging conversation and social comfort
- **Sensory enhancement:** Heightened appreciation of music, art, and food

### Secondary Effects
- Mild body relaxation from the Purple Punch lineage
- Reduced anxiety and stress in moderate doses
- [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]] (moderate)
