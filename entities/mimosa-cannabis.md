# Mimosa (Cannabis Strain)

**Mimosa** is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its uplifting, energizing effects and distinctive citrus-forward aroma. A cross between **Clementine** and **Purple Punch**, Mimosa has rapidly gained popularity since its introduction for its balanced yet predominantly cerebral high that is well-suited for daytime use. The strain's name reflects both its bright orange-citrus terpene profile and its mood-elevating, "rise and shine" character.

## Genetic Lineage

**Clementine (Mother)**
- Sativa-dominant hybrid
- Cross of *Tangerine* × *Lemon Skunk*
- Known for intense tangerine/citrus aroma and focused, uplifting effects
- High limonene and myrcene content
- Provides Mimosa's energetic, creative mental stimulation

**Purple Punch (Father)**
- Indica-dominant hybrid
- Cross of *Granddaddy Purple* × *Larry OG*
- Famous for sweet grape and blueberry flavors with relaxing body effects
- High caryophyllene and myrcene content
- Contributes body relaxation, berry undertones, and enhanced resin production

### Genetic Ratio
- Approximately **70% sativa / 30% indica** (varies slightly by phenotype)
- Exhibits hybrid vigor from the complementary parent profiles
- Several phenotypes exist, ranging from more Clementine-dominant (taller, more cerebral) to Purple Punch-dominant (shorter, more relaxing)

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma
- **Primary:** Bright, sweet orange and tangerine — immediately recognizable citrus burst
- **Secondary:** Tropical fruit notes, subtle grape and berry undertones from Purple Punch heritage
- **Tertiary:** Earthy, herbal base with faint floral and spice notes
- **Overall impression:** Like walking through a citrus grove at sunrise — fresh, sweet, and invigorating

### Flavor
- **Inhale:** Sharp, sweet orange and lemon zest with a sugary quality
- **Mid-palate:** Tropical fruit medley — mango, pineapple hints blending with grape
- **Exhale:** Smooth earthy finish with lingering citrus and subtle berry sweetness
- **Aftertaste:** Clean, refreshing citrus that persists pleasantly

### Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Typical Range | Primary Effects |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Limonene** | 1.0–2.5% | Mood elevation, stress relief, anti-anxiety |
| **Myrcene** | 0.5–1.5% | Relaxation, sedation (at higher levels), anti-inflammatory |
| **β-Caryophyllene** | 0.3–0.8% | Anti-inflammatory, pain relief via CB2 receptor binding |
| **Linalool** | 0.1–0.4% | Calming, anxiolytic, sleep support |
| **Pinene (α+β)** | 0.1–0.3% | Focus, alertness, bronchodilation |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.2% | Appetite suppression, anti-inflammatory |

The limonene-dominant profile is the defining characteristic — Mimosa consistently ranks among the most citrus-forward strains available.

## Cannabinoid Profile

### Typical Ranges
- **THC:** 17–24% (some premium phenotypes reach 27%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.5%
- **THCV:** Trace amounts in some phenotypes

### Dominant Cannabinoids
Mimosa is a **THC-dominant strain** with virtually no CBD. The entourage effect arises primarily from the interplay between high THC and the limonene-rich terpene profile. Users seeking CBD benefits should look to companion strains or CBD-dominant products.

## Effects

- **Onset:** Rapid (2–5 minutes inhalation); peak 30–60 minutes
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (smoking/vaping); 3–5 hours (edibles)
- **Come-down:** Gentle, gradual; minimal crash

### Primary Effects
- **Euphoria:** Uplifting, happy, and optimistic mood elevation — the signature effect
- **Energy and motivation:** Stimulating mental energy without jitters; enhances productivity
- **Creativity:** Enhances divergent thinking and artistic expression
- **Focus:** Many users report improved concentration on creative tasks
- **Sociability:** Promotes engaging conversation and social comfort
- **Sensory enhancement:** Heightened appreciation of music, art, and food

### Secondary Effects
- Mild body relaxation from the Purple Punch lineage
- Reduced anxiety and stress in moderate doses
- Appetite stimulation (moderate)
- Enhanced tactile and sensory awareness

### Potential Negative Effects
- Dry mouth (cottonmouth) — common with most cannabis
- Dry eyes — manageable with hydration
- Mild paranoia or anxiety at high doses (especially in anxiety-prone individuals)
- Mild headache in rare cases, usually dose-dependent
- **Drowsiness at higher doses

## Medical Applications

- **Depression:** Uplifting effects popular for managing depressive symptoms
- **Chronic fatigue:** Energizing properties combat lethargy and low motivation
- **Stress/anxiety:** Limonene's anxiolytic properties complement THC's mood effects
- **Mild pain:** Caryophyllene and humulene provide moderate anti-inflammatory relief
- **PTSD:** Some report mood stabilization and reduced hypervigilance (individual)

### Dosing
- **Low (1–2 puffs):** Mild elevation, gentle energy, functional daytime
- **Moderate (3–5 puffs):** Full euphoric and creative effects, optimal for most
- **High (6+ puffs):** Risk of anxiety, overstimulation, or paradoxical drowsiness

## Growing Information

### Cultivation
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — suitable for intermediate growers
- **Height:** 100–180 cm indoors; can exceed 200 cm outdoors
- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks (56–63 days)
- **Yield:** 350–500 g/m² indoors; 400–600 g/plant outdoors
- **Stretch:** Moderate (50–100% height increase)

### Environment
- **Temperature:** 20–28°C vegetative; 18–26°C flowering (cooler nights enhance color/terpenes)
- **Humidity:** 50–70% vegetative; 40–55% flowering
- **Nutrients:** Moderate feeder; avoid excess nitrogen in late flowering
- **Training:** LST, ScROG, and topping recommended

## Phenotype Variations

**Clementine-dominant:** Taller, brighter orange aroma, more cerebral, longer flowering (up to 10 weeks).

**Purple Punch-dominant:** Shorter/bushier, purple calyxes, sweeter grape flavor, more body relaxation, faster flowering (7–8 weeks).
