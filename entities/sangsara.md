# Sangsara

## Description

Sangsara (Samsara in Sanskrit) is the Buddhist concept of the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth.

It is the wheel of conditioned existence driven by karma and ignorance.

In Tibetan Buddhist cosmology, Sangsara encompasses six distinct realms of rebirth.

The ultimate goal of Buddhist practice is liberation from Sangsara into Nirvana.

In *The Psychedelic Experience*, Sangsara maps to the cycle of ego-reconstitution during psychedelic re-entry.

## Classification

- **Tradition**: Buddhism (all schools)
- **Category**: Fundamental cosmological concept
- **Sanskrit**: Samsara
- **Tibetan**: Sangsara
- **Nature**: Cyclical existence — the condition of unenlightened being

## Key Facts

1. Sangsara is the endless cycle of birth, death, and rebirth driven by karma and delusion.

2. It encompasses six worlds (lokas) of possible rebirth experience.

3. The six worlds are: devas (gods), asuras (demigods), humans, animals, pretas (hungry ghosts), and hell beings.

4. Each world represents a different psychological state as much as a literal realm.

5. The [[dharma-kaya]] encompasses both Nirvana and Sangsara — they are not ultimately separate.

6. In the [[bardo-thodol]], the [[third-bardo]] involves visions of the six worlds as the consciousness seeks rebirth.

7. Liberation from Sangsara requires recognizing the illusory nature of all six realms.

8. The psychedelic experience temporarily suspends the ordinary Sangsaric mode of consciousness.

9. Upon re-entry, the voyager faces the same six-fold choice of psychological "rebirth."

10. The manual instructs practitioners to avoid the lower realms and seek human rebirth if full liberation is not attained.

## The Six Worlds

### Devas (God Realm)

Pleasure and complacency. The danger of spiritual laziness amid abundance.

### Asuras (Demigod Realm)

Jealousy and constant conflict. Competitive striving without satisfaction.

### Human Realm

The most favorable for spiritual practice — a balance of pleasure and suffering.

### Animal Realm

Ignorance and instinct-driven existence. Predation and survival without reflection.

### Pretas (Hungry Ghosts)

Insatiable craving. Constant desire that can never be fulfilled.

### Hell Beings

Intense suffering, hatred, and rage. The most afflicted state of consciousness.

## Relevance to Psychedelic Studies

In *The Psychedelic Experience*, Sangsara maps to the re-entry phase where ego begins to reconstitute itself.

The six worlds are interpreted as six psychological attitudes the voyager may adopt during reintegration.

The manual warns against "precipitate rebirth" — settling into an inferior psychological state out of fear or confusion.

This framework provides a sophisticated psychology of [[psychedelic-integration]].

It recognizes that the quality of "coming down" determines the lasting value of the experience.

## Relation to Other Entities

- **Dharma-Kaya**: Encompasses both Sangsara and Nirvana.
- **Nirmana-Kaya**: The body that operates within Sangsaric existence.
- **Mara**: The personification of the delusive forces that perpetuate Sangsara.
- **Clear Light**: The state beyond Sangsara, encountered in the First Bardo.
- **Gautama Buddha**: The teacher who discovered the path out of Sangsara.
