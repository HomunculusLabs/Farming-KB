The compound is valued by the research chemical community for its balanced psychedelic profile, which combines visual and cognitive effects with a relatively comfortable physical sensation. It is considered less intense than 4-AcO-DMT but more stimulating and visual than 4-AcO-DET.

## Chemical Properties

- **Chemical class:** Substituted tryptamine
- **IUPAC name:** 3-[2-[Methyl(propan-2-yl)amino]ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl acetate
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₆H₂₂N₂O₂
- **Molecular weight:** 274.36 g/mol
- **CAS number:** 919797-19-6
- **SMILES:** CC(OC1=CNC2=C1C=CC=C2CCN(C)C(C)C)=O

The acetoxy group at the 4-position of the indole ring serves as a prodrug moiety. In vivo, this group is hydrolyzed by esterases to yield the active metabolite 4-HO-MiPT, the same conversion pathway as 4-AcO-DMT to psilocin.

4-AcO-MiPT is a freebase oil at room temperature, moderately lipophilic, and readily soluble in ethanol and DMSO. It is typically encountered as a white to off-white powder salt form for oral administration.

## Pharmacology

### Receptor Activity

4-AcO-MiPT acts primarily as a serotonergic psychedelic through its active metabolite 4-HO-MiPT. The compound shows moderate-to-high affinity for 5-HT₂A receptors, the primary site responsible for psychedelic effects.

- **5-HT₂A:** Partial agonist (primary mechanism of psychedelic action)
- **5-HT₁A:** Moderate affinity, contributes to anxiolytic and mood-elevating effects
- **5-HT₂C:** Low-to-moderate affinity, may influence appetite and anxiety

The N-methyl-N-isopropyl substitution pattern reduces receptor affinity compared to N,N-dimethyl (as in psilocin), resulting in lower potency per milligram. This same substitution increases metabolic stability and may extend the effective duration.

### Pharmacokinetics

Oral absorption is rapid, with onset typically within 15–30 minutes. The acetoxy prodrug is efficiently deacetylated by plasma and hepatic esterases. Peak plasma levels of 4-HO-MiPT are reached within 1–2 hours post-dose.
