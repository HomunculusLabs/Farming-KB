## 3C-E (2C-E-4ETO)

**Chemical name:** 3,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine

**PiHKAL #25** | [[scythian-cannabis-steam-bath-ritual]] for 36 h. Product: canary-yellow crystals of 1-(3,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxyphenyl)-2-nitropropene, mp 84-85 °C (total yield: 2.9 g from two crops)
3. **LAH reduction**: Nitrostyrene + LAH in THF, held at reflux for 48 h, then stirred at room temperature for another 48 h. Standard workup through acid-base extraction. Final product: 1.9 g 3,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine hydrochloride as brilliant white crystals

## Qualitative Effects

- Moderate psychedelic effects with stimulant character (amphetamine backbone)
- Visual enhancement and color intensification
- Mild to moderate eyes-closed imagery
- Increased energy and physical stimulation
- Emotional openness and mild empathogenic qualities
- Less visual intensity than the corresponding phenethylamine (2C compound)
- Body awareness and physical sensations prominent
- For most subjects, a little too heavy on the body

## Shulgin"s Qualitative Comments

At 40 mg, the experience developed into a "strange and indefinable something" — unworldly, with an undertone of unreality reminiscent of high doses of LSD. The subject was very much in control but noted an eerie state of awareness that existed in a narrow window between sensory overload and sensory deprivation. By the 8th hour the subject was sober, with no residue except slight teeth clenching, and was "pretty much disbelieving the whole thing."

At 60 mg, visuals were very strong and insistent. Body discomfort was heavy for the first hour, with a sense of "implacable imposition of something toxic" — faint or pre-nausea, heavy tremor feeling, and general dis-ease. The subject kept lying down for comfort. Fantasy became quite strong: initially anti-erotic with no eyes-closed images, but from the second hour bright colors and distinct jewel-like shapes appeared. The experience then shifted to a full +++, with complex fantasy taking over, continual erotic content, and exquisite sensitivity. Down by approximately midnight. The next morning, faint flickering lights when looking out windows.

## Related Compounds and Commentary

The commentary in PIHKAL also discusses 2C-F (2,5-dimethoxy-4-fluorophenethylamine), which Shulgin noted was difficult to synthesize. Animal studies suggested 2C-F was 4-6 times less potent than DOI or DOB, and no human trials were conducted.

The 3C series represents compounds that bridge the gap between the classic 2C phenethylamines and the DO-series amphetamines. The alpha-methyl group generally increases metabolic stability and duration.

## Safety Warnings

- Amphetamine backbone carries cardiovascular risk (increased heart rate, blood pressure)
- At 60 mg, significant body discomfort was reported during the first hour
- Stimulant effects can mask the psychedelic component, leading to redosing
- Longer duration than corresponding 2C compounds
- Strong fantasy takeover at higher doses may cause difficulty distinguishing reality from imagination
- See [[phenethylamine-synthesis-safety]]
