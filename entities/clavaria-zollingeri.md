## Clavaria zollingeri

## Overview

Clavaria zollingeri, commonly known as the violet coral or magenta coral,
is a striking clavarioid fungus in the family Clavariaceae.
It is immediately recognizable by its vivid violet to purple, simple or
sparingly branched, cylindrical fruiting bodies that arise from forest litter.
Widely distributed in temperate and subtropical regions, it is a saprotroph
found in coniferous and mixed woodlands, often associated with mossy ground.

Despite its dramatic appearance, the species is inedible due to its insipid
taste and rubbery texture. It serves as an indicator of healthy, undisturbed
forest floor ecosystems and is of increasing conservation interest due to
sensitivity to habitat disturbance and [[psilocybe-southeast-asia-pacific]]. The genus name
*Clavaria* derives from Latin *clava* (club), referring to the club-shaped
fruiting bodies characteristic of the group.

## Morphology

### Fruiting Body

- **Height:** 3–8 cm tall, occasionally up to 10 cm
- **Shape:** Simple, unbranched cylindrical clubs or sparsely forking
  from a common base; rarely with 2–3 branches
- **Width:** 3–8 mm diameter per club
- **Color:** Deep violet to lilac-purple when fresh; fading to grayish-purple
  or brownish with age and drying
- **Surface:** Smooth to minutely wrinkled, subgelatinous when moist
- **Base:** Short, rooting basal stipe that merges into substrate debris
- **Flesh:** Soft, rubbery, hollow; same color as exterior or slightly paler
- **Odor:** Indistinct to faintly farinaceous
- **Taste:** Mild, insipid — not acrid or bitter

### Spores

- **Shape:** Broadly ellipsoid to subglobose
- **Size:** 5–7 × 4–6 µm
- **Print:** White
- **Ornamentation:** Smooth, thin-walled, non-amyloid
- **Basidia:** Clavate, 4-spored, sterigmata up to 4 µm long

## Distribution and Habitat

- **Geographic range:** Cosmopolitan in temperate regions — [[decomposition]]:** Contributes to breakdown of coniferous [[dighton-pine-needle-litter-decomposition-fungal-succession]]
  and leaf duff on the forest floor
