## Aspergillus flavus

**Type:** Species (Fungus)
**Category:** Filamentous Ascomycete
**Also known as:** Yellow-green mold

## Overview
Aspergillus flavus is a cosmopolitan saprobic and pathogenic fungus in the
Aspergillaceae family. It is one of the most economically significant fungal
species due to its production of aflatoxins, potent carcinogenic secondary
metabolites that contaminate food crops worldwide. A. flavus is also an
opportunistic pathogen of humans and animals, causing aspergillosis in
immunocompromised individuals. The species produces sclerotia that serve as
overwintering structures in soil, and these sclerotia are themselves subject
to mycoparasitic colonization by other soil fungi.

### Life Cycle
- Reproduces primarily through asexual conidia dispersed by wind
  and insects
- Sclerotia form under stress conditions and can persist in soil
  for extended periods
