# Isomerizer (ISO-2)

## Description

The Isomerizer (and its successor, the ISO-2) was a consumer-grade cannabis extraction and isomerization device invented by D. Gold and manufactured by Thai Power, Inc. It was the first commercially available machine designed to perform solvent extraction of cannabis oil followed by acid-catalyzed isomerization of cannabidiol (CBD) to tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). The device combined a soxhlet extraction system, a reaction vessel, and a solvent recovery pump into a single tabletop unit.

## Classification

- **Category**: Cannabis extraction and processing device
- **Type**: Tabletop solvent extraction + isomerization apparatus
- **Manufacturer**: Thai Power, Inc.
- **Inventor**: D. Gold

## Key Facts

- Combined soxhlet extraction with acid-catalyzed isomerization in a single automated process.
- Used isopropyl alcohol as the primary solvent and sulfuric acid as the isomerization activator.
- The ISO-2 was the second-generation model, featuring improvements over the original Isomerizer.
- Standard batch capacity was 4–5 ounces of cannabis per run, with a total operation time of 7–8 hours.
- The device included a solvent recovery pump, condensing unit, and a reaction vessel with a soxhlet basket.
- Thai Power published a newsletter called *IsoNews* where users shared extraction techniques and recipes.
- The device retailed at a price point accessible to individual consumers rather than just laboratories.
- Baking soda was used as a neutralizer after the isomerization reaction.

## Operating Procedure

1. **Soaking**: Cannabis soaked in isopropyl alcohol for 24+ hours
2. **Soxhlet extraction**: Solvent cycled through the plant material for several hours
3. **Isomerization**: Sulfuric acid activator added; mixture heated for ~45 minutes
4. **Neutralization**: Baking soda added to neutralize the acid
5. **Solvent removal**: Pump evaporates and recovers the isopropyl alcohol
6. **Collection**: Remaining oil or hash product collected from the reaction vessel

## User-Developed Techniques

The letters section of *Cannabis Alchemy* documents numerous user innovations:

- **Cycling extraction**: Multiple 24-hour soaks in fresh solvent to reduce soxhlet time
- **Blender method**: Pre-pulverizing cannabis in a blender with alcohol for faster extraction
- **Doublewash**: Hot water pre-wash to remove water-soluble tars before solvent extraction
- **Pollen screening**: Using nylon mesh to collect resin glands before extraction
- **Multi-on-one hash**: Concentrating oil from multiple ounces onto a single ounce of powdered cannabis

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

The Isomerizer represents a significant chapter in the history of cannabis processing technology. It demonstrated that:

- Post-harvest processing can dramatically increase the effective potency of cannabis
- Isomerization converts non-psychoactive CBD to psychoactive THC, altering the cannabinoid profile
- The quality of the starting material significantly affects the final product's flavor, taste, and experience
- Water-soluble tars and waxes contribute to harshness and can be removed without losing cannabinoids

Understanding the principles behind the Isomerizer informs modern extraction techniques including:

- Supercritical CO2 extraction
- Hydrocarbon extraction (BHO)
- Rosin press technology
- Decarboxylation protocols
