# Common Milkweed (*Asclepias syriaca* L.)

## Overview

*Asclepias syriaca* (common milkweed) is a perennial herbaceous plant native to
eastern and central [[cannabis-for-skin-conditions-detailed]]; leaf
  tea for digestive complaints (after thorough cooking to denature
  cardenolides)
- **Historical European use:** Root tea as an expectorant and diaphoretic
  for respiratory infections
- **Safety:** Internal use is NOT recommended due to cardiac glycoside
  toxicity; can cause nausea, vomiting, cardiac arrhythmias
