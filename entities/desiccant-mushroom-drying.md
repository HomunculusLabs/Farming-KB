## Description

Desiccant drying is the preferred method for preserving harvested *[[psilocybe-cubensis]]* mushrooms in the [[pf-tek]]. The technique uses moisture-absorbing chemical agents (desiccants) to remove water from mushrooms at cool or room temperatures, preserving their psychoactive alkaloid content far better than heat-based drying methods. The PF TEK identifies cool desiccation as "overall, the best drying technique" and a critical step in producing high-quality preserved specimens.

## Classification

- **Category:** Post-harvest preservation technique
- **Type:** Cool/room-temperature desiccant drying
- **Purpose:** Remove moisture while preserving alkaloid integrity
- **Method:** Passive moisture absorption in sealed container
- **Duration:** 4–5 days for fully dried specimens

## Key Facts

- The PF TEK explicitly warns against heat-based drying: "Never dry them in an oven or use hot air dryers. The heat leaches the chemical constituents and reduces their quality."
- Three recommended desiccant sources:
  1. **Drierite** — the universal laboratory desiccant (chemical and science supply retailers)
  2. **Silica Gel granules** — widely available desiccant (chemical and science supply)
  3. **Damp Gone** — inexpensive kitty litter-like product (hardware stores); "works as well as any"
- Desiccant can be reused indefinitely by heating in an oven as instructed by the manufacturer to drive off absorbed moisture.
- Pre-heating purchased desiccant is recommended because it "is usually somewhat damp which will thwart its function."
- Store desiccant in airtight containers between uses to maintain readiness.

## Drying Procedure

**Setup:**
1. Place a 1-inch layer of desiccant on the bottom of a container.
2. Position a wire screen on top of the desiccant layer.
3. Place mushrooms on the wire screen so air reaches all sides.
4. Place the container inside a plastic bag and seal with a wire tie.
5. A clear plastic bag allows observation of the drying process.

**Timeline:**
- **24 hours:** Slight shriveling visible
- **4–5 days:** Mushrooms dried rock hard

**Completion test:** The stem should snap cleanly when bent.

## Advanced Techniques

- **Pre-drying:** Air-drying mushrooms on a wire screen for 1–2 days before desiccant treatment speeds the process, provided room humidity is not high.
- **Cold desiccation:** Placing the desiccant box in the refrigerator dries mushrooms at near-freezing temperatures — "the best alkaloid preservation technique."
- **Frost-free refrigerator:** Can work as a standalone drying method but is time-consuming and not everyone has access to one.

## Harvest Timing for Optimal Drying

The best specimens for drying are immature mushrooms:

- Very light in color with white stems and light-colored caps
- Conical shaped, with unbroken veils or veils just beginning to break
- Light-colored gills (sporulation has not really begun)
- Avoid mature specimens with dark colors, upturned caps, and darkened gills

## Storage After Drying

- Seal in plastic bags or canning jars with rubber-edged lids tightened.
- Freezer storage is excellent for long-term preservation.
- Ensure mushrooms are tightly sealed in containers to protect against freezer moisture.
- Properly dried and stored mushrooms "lose hardly any chemical constituents and their truly desiccated state preserves them in their prime for months."

## Safety Considerations

- Desiccant products may carry toxicity warnings — do not breathe the dust and avoid direct skin contact (it dries skin).
- These precautions apply to handling the dry desiccant material itself, not to the dried mushrooms.
- "Desiccant in an airtight box and under a screen will do nothing to the fungi except dry them. It is completely safe for this use."

## Why Not Heat?

The PF TEK strongly emphasizes that heat destroys the active compounds:

- Oven drying and hot air dryers leach chemical constituents
- Heat reduces the quality and potency of the preserved mushrooms
- The emphasis on "cool desiccation" in the method name itself reflects this principle
- Combined with young harvesting, cool desiccant drying produces specimens that are "an entheogen of the highest order"

## Relevance to Cultivation

Desiccant drying is the final critical step in the PF TEK cultivation cycle:

- **Potency preservation:** Maintains the alkaloid content that makes immature specimens highly potent.
- **Long-term storage:** Properly dried mushrooms remain viable for months.
- **Accessibility:** Requires no specialized equipment beyond inexpensive desiccant and basic containers.
- **Reusability:** Desiccant can be regenerated and reused indefinitely, making the technique cost-effective.
- **Quality indicator:** The ability to snap-cleanly when bent provides a simple, reliable test for complete drying.
