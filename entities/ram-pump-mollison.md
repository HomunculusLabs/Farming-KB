## Ram Pump

## Description

A ram pump (hydraulic ram pump) is a cyclic water pump that uses the kinetic energy of a flowing water column to lift a portion of that water to a higher elevation without requiring any external power source. It operates entirely on the momentum of falling water, making it one of the most appropriate technologies for permaculture and off-grid water systems. In Mollison's permaculture framework, ram pumps are integrated with check dams that provide the necessary "tripping head" to power the pump.

## Key Facts

### Operating Principle
The ram pump exploits the water hammer effect. Water flows downhill through a drive pipe, building velocity. When a waste valve suddenly closes, the resulting pressure surge (water hammer) forces a portion of the water through a delivery valve into an air chamber and up a delivery pipe to a higher elevation. The waste valve then reopens, and the cycle repeats automatically.

### Integration with Check Dams
Mollison describes check dams specifically designed for ram pump operation. These structures provide a tripping head — a minimum vertical drop — that enables the ram pump to function. The check dam creates a small reservoir and head differential sufficient to drive the pump cycle. The same arrangement can power water wheels for mechanical energy.

### Performance Characteristics
- **Lift ratio**: Can lift water to approximately 10 times the drive head, though at reduced volume
- **Flow delivery**: Typically delivers 5–25% of the drive water to the elevated outlet
- **Continuous operation**: Runs 24 hours per day with no fuel or electricity
- **Maintenance**: Low; primarily involves cleaning intake screens and occasional valve replacement
- **Drive head requirement**: Minimum 1 metre of fall typically needed

### Advantages in Permaculture Systems
1. **No external energy**: Powered entirely by water flow
2. **Continuous operation**: Works day and night without attention
3. **Long service life**: Simple construction with few moving parts
4. **Low maintenance**: Minimal mechanical components to fail
5. **Self-regulating**: Automatically adjusts to available flow

## Construction and Installation

A ram pump installation requires:
- A water source with adequate flow and at least 1 m of head
- A drive pipe (typically galvanized steel, sized to the pump)
- The ram pump unit itself (commercially available or fabricated)
- A delivery pipe to the storage or use point
- An air chamber (snifter) to smooth pulsations
- A check dam or weir to create the necessary head

## Relevance to Cultivation and Land Management

Ram pumps are valuable in permaculture systems because they:

1. **Enable gravity-fed irrigation** from lowland water sources to upland gardens and fields
2. **Fill elevated storage tanks** without electricity or fossil fuels
3. **Water livestock** in remote paddocks from stream sources
4. **Support reforestation** by delivering water to newly planted trees on slopes
5. **Drought-proof properties** by continuously filling reserves during flow periods
6. **Reduce infrastructure costs** compared to electric pump systems

In integrated water design, a ram pump fed by a check dam on an intermittent stream can fill an elevated turkey's nest dam or header tank, which then gravity-feeds irrigation to productive areas below. This creates a self-sustaining water distribution system that operates without ongoing energy inputs.
