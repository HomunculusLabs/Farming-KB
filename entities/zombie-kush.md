## Lineage

Zombie Kush has a complex four-way genetic background:

- **Sideshow** (also known as Face Off OG Bx1) — a potent indica-dominant
  phenotype derived from the OG Kush family. Sideshow contributes heavy resin
  production, high THC content, and strong sedative effects.

- **Lavender** (Soma Seeds) — created by crossing [[super-skunk]], Big Skunk Korean,
  and Afghani Hawaiian. Lavender contributes its distinctive floral aroma, deep
  purple coloration potential, and body-focused relaxation.

- **Amnesia** — a Dutch coffee shop classic, likely descended from [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] that balances the heavy indica sedation.

The result is a roughly 80/20 indica-to-sativa ratio, though individual phenotypes
may vary. The Lavender influence makes purple coloration likely during late
flowering, especially under cooler night temperatures.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is complex and layered, reflecting its diverse lineage. Primary notes
include earthy, skunky, and woody undertones from the OG Kush and Lavender
genetics. Secondary notes feature floral, sweet lavender, and a subtle spicy or
peppery quality from the Amnesia parent.

On the exhale, users commonly report a hashy, fuel-like aftertaste mixed with
damp earth and a faint grape or berry sweetness. The flavor profile is generally
described as smooth with a slight harshness on large draws due to the thick
resin content.

## Terpene Profile

The dominant terpenes in Zombie Kush typically include:

- **Myrcene** (0.5–1.2%) — the most abundant terpene, contributing earthy and
  herbal aromas. Myrcene is associated with sedative and relaxing effects and
  enhances THC permeability across the blood-brain barrier.

- **Caryophyllene** (0.2–0.6%) — provides peppery, spicy notes and acts as a
  [[beta-caryophyllene-dietary-cannabinoid]] by binding to CB2 receptors. Contributes anti-inflammatory
  properties.

- **Linalool** (0.1–0.4%) — from the Lavender parent, contributing floral
  sweetness and additional sedative and anxiolytic effects.
