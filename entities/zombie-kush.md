## Overview

Zombie Kush is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by Barney's Farm, a
Netherlands-based seed bank known for award-winning genetics. The strain was created
by crossing Sideshow with a Lavender × Amnesia cross, producing a heavy-bodied
variety with deeply relaxing effects.

The name reflects its powerful sedative properties — users report feeling "couch-
locked" or immobilized, reminiscent of the zombie archetype in popular culture.
Zombie Kush has gained a following among medical patients seeking relief from
insomnia, [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], and anxiety. It has not received major cannabis cup awards
but maintains a strong reputation in the European and [[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] markets.

## Lineage

Zombie Kush has a complex four-way genetic background:

- **Sideshow** (also known as Face Off OG Bx1) — a potent indica-dominant
  phenotype derived from the OG Kush family. Sideshow contributes heavy resin
  production, high THC content, and strong sedative effects.

- **Lavender** (Soma Seeds) — created by crossing Super Skunk, Big Skunk Korean,
  and Afghani Hawaiian. Lavender contributes its distinctive floral aroma, deep
  purple coloration potential, and body-focused relaxation.

- **Amnesia** — a Dutch coffee shop classic, likely descended from Southeast Asian
  and Jamaican landrace lines. Amnesia adds cerebral uplift and a complex
  terpene profile that balances the heavy indica sedation.

The result is a roughly 80/20 indica-to-sativa ratio, though individual phenotypes
may vary. The Lavender influence makes purple coloration likely during late
flowering, especially under cooler night temperatures.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large in size with a dense, tightly packed structure typical of
indica-dominant varieties. The calyxes are chunky and interspersed with curly
orange-to-amber pistils. Colors range from deep [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] to dark violet and
purple, especially in the outer leaves and smaller sugar leaves.

Trichome coverage is heavy, giving the buds a frosty, almost white appearance
under direct light. The [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] is one of Zombie Kush's most notable
visual characteristics — flowers glisten with a thick layer of sticky trichomes
that extend onto the surrounding foliage.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is complex and layered, reflecting its diverse lineage. Primary notes
include earthy, skunky, and woody undertones from the OG Kush and Lavender
genetics. Secondary notes feature floral, sweet lavender, and a subtle spicy or
peppery quality from the Amnesia parent.

On the exhale, users commonly report a hashy, fuel-like aftertaste mixed with
damp earth and a faint grape or berry sweetness. The flavor profile is generally
described as smooth with a slight harshness on large draws due to the thick
resin content.

## Terpene Profile

The dominant terpenes in Zombie Kush typically include:

- **Myrcene** (0.5–1.2%) — the most abundant terpene, contributing earthy and
  herbal aromas. Myrcene is associated with sedative and relaxing effects and
  enhances THC permeability across the blood-brain barrier.

- **Caryophyllene** (0.2–0.6%) — provides peppery, spicy notes and acts as a
  dietary cannabinoid by binding to CB2 receptors. Contributes anti-inflammatory
  properties.

- **Linalool** (0.1–0.4%) — from the Lavender parent, contributing floral
  sweetness and additional sedative and anxiolytic effects.

- **Limonene** (0.1–0.3%) — present in lower quantities, adding subtle citrus
  brightness to the aroma and contributing mood-elevating effects.

- **Humulene** (0.05–0.15%) — earthy, woody aroma with anti-inflammatory
  properties, rounding out the complex bouquet.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Zombie Kush typically tests in the following ranges:

- **THC:** 18–24%, with some phenotypes reaching 26% under optimal cultivation.
  The high THC content is consistent with its heavy, sedative effects.

- **CBD:** Very low, typically below 0.1%. This strain is not recommended for
  users seeking CBD-dominant or balanced cannabinoid profiles.

- **Minor cannabinoids:** Trace amounts of CBG and CBC may be present but are
  not significant contributors to the overall effect profile.

## Effects

The onset is relatively rapid, beginning within 5–10 minutes of inhalation.
Effects progress in distinct stages, starting with a mild cerebral pressure behind
the eyes and a warming sensation that spreads from the chest outward.

As the high deepens, physical relaxation intensifies into heavy body sedation.
Users report deep muscle relaxation, couch-lock, and a general sense of being
anchored to their seat or bed. Mental effects include euphoria paired with
dreamy, hazy thought patterns and reduced anxiety.

Duration typically lasts 2–3 hours. The comedown is gradual, often transitioning
directly into sleep. Zombie Kush is best reserved for evening or nighttime use due
to its strongly sedative character.

## Medical Applications

Zombie Kush has several recognized medical applications:

- **Insomnia** — its most commonly cited medical use. The heavy sedative effects
  make it effective for patients who have difficulty falling or staying asleep.
- **Chronic pain** — the combination of high THC and caryophyllene provides
  meaningful analgesic effects for muscle spasms, joint pain, and neuropathy.
- **Anxiety and stress** — the anxiolytic properties of myrcene and linalool
