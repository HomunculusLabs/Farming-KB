**Flowers** are white to cream-colored, approximately 3–5 cm in diameter, borne in axillary cymes of 1–3 flowers.

The species is functionally dioecious, with separate male and female plants required for fruit production.

Flowers appear in spring (May–June in the Northern Hemisphere) shortly after leaf emergence.

**Fruit** is a large, ovoid to oblong berry, 5–8 cm long and 4.5–6 cm in diameter.

The skin is brown and covered with dense, stiff, tan hairs, giving the fruit its characteristic fuzzy appearance.

Flesh is bright green with a central white core radiating numerous small black seeds.

Seeds are approximately 2–3 mm long, numerous (up to 1,400 per fruit), and edible.

## Distribution and Habitat

*A. deliciosa* is native to the forested mountains of eastern and southern China, particularly in the provinces of Hubei, Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Fujian.

In the wild, it grows as a forest-edge vine at elevations of 600–2,000 meters in mixed deciduous forests.

It prefers deep, fertile, well-drained loamy soils with adequate moisture and partial shade.

It requires a [[pseudomonas]] syringae* pv. *actinidiae*.
