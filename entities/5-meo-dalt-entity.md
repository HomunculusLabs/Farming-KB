# 5-MeO-DALT (N,N-Diallyl-5-methoxytryptamine)

## Overview

5-MeO-DALT is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine first synthesized and characterized by [[indole]] ring and N,N-diallyl groups on the terminal amine — a substitution pattern that is rare among both natural and synthetic tryptamines. It is known for its relatively [[tryptamine-family-overview]].

## Chemical Information

### IUPAC Name
N,N-diallyl-5-methoxy-1H-indole-3-ethanamine

### Chemical Formula
C₁₇H₂₂N₂O

### Molecular Weight
270.37 g/mol

### SMILES
COc1ccc2[nH]c(CCN(C=CC)C=CC)c(C)c2c1

### Structural Features
- **Indole ring** with a **5-methoxy** substitution (positions analogous to [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]] — two allyl groups (CH₂-CH=CH₂) replacing the more common N,N-dimethyl groups
- The allyl groups significantly alter the compound's pharmacokinetics compared to N,N-dimethyl tryptamines

### Physical Properties
- Appearance: White to off-white crystalline powder or freebase oil
- Freebase form is an oily liquid at room temperature; commonly encountered as a hydrochloride or fumarate salt
- Slightly soluble in water (as salt); soluble in [[ethanol]], DMSO, and lipid solvents

## Pharmacology

### Mechanism of Action
5-MeO-DALT acts primarily as a **5-HT₂A receptor agonist**, the primary mechanism underlying its psychedelic effects. Additional receptor interactions likely include:

- **5-HT₂C:** Moderate affinity; may contribute to the body load and anxiety some users report
- **5-HT₁A:** Weak affinity; may contribute to anxiolytic or calming effects
- **TAAR1 (Trace Amine-Associated Receptor 1):** Some tryptamines show activity here, though 5-MeO-DALT has not been specifically characterized

The N,N-diallyl substitution is pharmacologically significant. Compared to N,N-dimethyl groups, the allyl chains:
- Increase lipophilicity, potentially enhancing blood-brain barrier penetration
- Alter metabolic stability — allyl groups are metabolized by cytochrome P450 enzymes, particularly CYP2D6 and CYP3A4
- May confer partial agonist or biased signaling properties at 5-HT₂A that differ from DMT or psilocin

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Onset:** 15–45 minutes after oral administration
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours (notably shorter than psilocin at 4–6 hours or LSD at 8–12 hours)
- **Metabolism:** Presumed hepatic, via N-deallylation and O-demethylation. The allyl groups are susceptible to oxidative metabolism, producing diallylamine and 5-hydroxyindole metabolites.
- **Bioavailability:** Moderate when taken orally. The compound may also be vaporized/smoked, which produces a faster onset (1–5 minutes) and shorter duration (1–2 hours).

## Dosage

### Oral
- **Threshold:** 5–10 mg
- **Light:** 10–15 mg
- **Common:** 15–25 mg
- **Strong:** 25–40 mg
- **Heavy:** 40 mg+

### Vaporized/Smoked
- **Light:** 5–10 mg
- **Common:** 10–20 mg
- **Strong:** 20–30 mg

**Note:** Dosage ranges are derived from self-reporting in the research chemical community and have not been established through [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]]. Individual sensitivity varies considerably.
