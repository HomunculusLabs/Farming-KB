## Araucaria araucana

**Type:** Species (Tree)
**Category:** Nut-bearing conifer
**Family:** Araucariaceae
**Also known as:** Monkey puzzle tree, Araucarian pine nut, Chilean
pine, piñonero

## Overview
Araucaria araucana is an evergreen conifer native to the Andes of
Chile and Argentina, growing 15-35 m tall with a spread of 8 m. It is
the national tree of Chile and has been a vital food source for the
Araucanian (Mapuche) people for thousands of years. The tree produces
large, edible seeds called piñones that are rich in starch and
comparable in size to almonds. The distinctive appearance of the tree,
with its umbrella-shaped crown of sharply pointed leaves, makes it one
of the most recognisable trees in the world.
