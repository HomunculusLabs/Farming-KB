**Synonyms:** Artemisia dracunculoides Pursh; A. glauca Pall. ex Willd.

**Etymology:** The genus honors Artemis, Greek goddess of the hunt.
The epithet *dracunculus* means "little dragon," possibly referring to the
coiled root system. "Tarragon" derives from Arabic *tarkhūn* via Latin *tarchon*.

**Cultivar Groups:**
- **French tarragon** (var. *sativa*) — Sterile, vegetatively propagated,
  60–80% estragole, superior culinary quality
- **Russian tarragon** (var. *inodora*) — Seed-fertile, 5–30% estragole,
  vigorous but milder flavor
