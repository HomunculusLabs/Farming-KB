# Charas

## Description

Charas is a traditional form of hand-pressed cannabis concentrate originating from Nepal and India. It is produced by rubbing live cannabis flowers between the hands, causing resin glands (trichomes) to accumulate on the skin as a sticky, pliable mass that is then scraped off and rolled into balls or sticks. Charas is one of the oldest forms of cannabis concentrate and represents a labor-intensive, artisanal approach to [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-spore-extraction-methods]].

## Classification

- **Category**: Cannabis concentrate
- **Type**: Hand-rubbed hashish
- **Consistency**: Sticky, pliable, dark brown to black
- **Origin**: Nepal, India (Himalayan region)
- **Production method**: Manual hand-rubbing of live cannabis flowers

## Key Facts

- Produced by **hand-rubbing live cannabis flowers**, which causes trichome resin to stick to the palms and fingers. The accumulated resin is periodically scraped off and collected.
- Characterized by its **sticky, pliable** consistency — more malleable than pressed hashish from Morocco or Lebanon.
- Traditional production areas include the Himalayan foothills of **Nepal and northern India**, where cannabis grows wild and has been cultivated for centuries.
- In *[[beta-caryophyllene-entourage-effects-cannabis]].

## Relevance to Cultivation and Processing

Charas represents the traditional endpoint of cannabis resin collection — a technique that relies on the natural stickiness of mature trichomes rather than chemical extraction. In the context of *Cannabis Alchemy*, charas serves as the gold standard for hashish texture. The book's method for preparing hashish from cannabis oil involves mixing purified (and optionally isomerized/acetylated) oil with finely powdered marijuana to achieve a product with the consistency and appearance of charas, but with dramatically higher and more controllable potency. The traditional charas-making regions of Nepal and India remain culturally significant centers of [[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-hashish-preparation]] |

| Method | Hand-rubbing live flowers | Oil + powdered plant mixing |
| Potency | Variable (8–25% THC) | Controllable (up to 60%+ oil) |
| Consistency | Sticky, pliable | Adjustable (Moroccan to charas-like) |

## Regional Variations

- **Nepalese charas**: Often rolled into round "temple balls," typically dark and aromatic
- **Indian charas (Malana cream)**: From the Parvati Valley, known for exceptional quality
- **Afghan charas**: Sometimes pressed into flat, hand-pressed patties
