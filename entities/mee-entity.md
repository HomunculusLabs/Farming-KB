# MEE (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine)

MEE is a substituted amphetamine with ethoxy and dimethoxy substitution on the phenyl ring. It is the amphetamine homologue of [[mem-entity]]. The compound is listed as [[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #121.

## Chemical Classification

MEE belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxyamphetamine.

MEE is the alpha-methylated homologue of [[dom]], [[doi-entity]], and [[2c-e]] is the phenethylamine (two-carbon chain) analogue of MEE.

2C-E is active at 10-25 mg, while MEE requires 20-50 mg.

The alpha-methyl group in MEE provides some protection from metabolic deamination but does not dramatically increase potency over 2C-E.

Both compounds share the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethoxy substitution pattern.
