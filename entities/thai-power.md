# Thai Power (Company)

## Description

Thai Power, Inc. (also referred to as Thai Power or TPI) was the manufacturer and distributor of the Isomerizer and ISO-2 cannabis extraction and isomerization devices invented by D. Gold. The company operated during the 1970s and early 1980s, marketing directly to cannabis consumers through counterculture publications and mail order.

## Classification

- **Category**: Company / manufacturer
- **Industry**: Cannabis processing equipment
- **Products**: Isomerizer, ISO-2, Isomerizer accessories
- **Era**: 1970s–1980s
- **Founder/Key figure**: D. Gold

## Key Facts

- Manufactured and sold the Isomerizer and ISO-2 devices
- Published *IsoNews*, a newsletter for Isomerizer users sharing techniques and recipes
- Provided customer support and responded to user letters about extraction techniques
- Sold consumables including 100% isopropyl alcohol and activator solution
- Advertised in counterculture magazines including *Head*
- Was developing an automatic filtration accessory for the Isomerizer
- Encouraged user experimentation while disclaiming responsibility for accidents from non-standard procedures
- Supplied replacement parts and devices when units were damaged in transit

## Customer Community

Thai Power maintained an active correspondence with Isomerizer users. Letters published in *Cannabis Alchemy* document:

- Users from across the US (Illinois, Texas, Massachusetts, Florida, Pennsylvania, etc.)
- Sharing of novel extraction and hash-making techniques
- Questions about solvent selection, safety, and alternative procedures
- Product feedback and feature requests
- The company provided editorial notes with technical corrections and safety warnings

## Product Line

- **Isomerizer (original)**: First-generation extraction and isomerization device
- **ISO-2**: Improved second-generation model with enhanced features
- **Activator solution**: Concentrated sulfuric acid in measured dropper bottle
- **Isopropyl alcohol**: 100% (anhydrous) grade for extraction solvent
- **Filtration accessory**: In development, announced but not yet released

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Thai Power represents an important chapter in the commercialization of cannabis processing technology:

- Made cannabinoid chemistry accessible to home users
- Created a community of practice around cannabis extraction
- Documented practical knowledge about solvent selection, extraction optimization, and hash production
- The company's customer correspondence provides historical insight into cannabis culture of the era
