# ALEPH-7

ALEPH-7 is the n-propylthio homologue of [[alexander-shulgin]].

## Pharmacological Profile

ALEPH-7 is an extremely potent psychedelic compound with a dosage range of only 4 to 7 milligrams.

The duration of effects is extraordinarily long, lasting 15 to 30 hours.

This makes it one of the longest-duration psychedelic amphetamines in the PIHKAL collection.

The compound produces effects that have been described as distinct from other psychedelics, characterized by a unique neutral state.

## Qualitative Effects

At 4 mg, subjects reported a paraesthetic tingling sensation at the second hour, followed by a relaxed state.

Ears felt "popped" with some physical awareness persisting.

Sleep was difficult the night following, though the subject seemed otherwise recovered the next day.

At 6 mg, an alert was felt within 30 minutes, followed by evolution of an extremely neutral state.

Subjects reported dancing wildly to music, emotional vulnerability with tears and feelings of total loss.

Everything was perceived through a strange wide-angle lens viewing.

Jazz was acceptable but classical music was not.

Residual strangeness persisted for a couple of days.

At 7 mg, the experience reached a full plus three (+++) level between hours 2 and 3, with continued deepening.

No reality loss as with [[aleph-2-entity]]
