## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Fungi
- **Division:** Basidiomycota
- **Class:** Agaricomycetes
- **Order:** Agaricales
- **Family:** Amanitaceae
- **Genus:** *Amanita* Pers.
- **Species:** *A. flavoconia* G.F. Atk.

The specific epithet *flavoconia* derives from Latin *flavus* (yellow) and *conia* (dust or powder), referring to the yellow warty remnants on the cap surface.

Originally described by George Francis Atkinson in 1902 from specimens collected in the eastern [[ectomycorrhizal-associations]] primarily with hardwood trees, especially oaks (*Quercus* spp.), hickories (*Carya* spp.), and beech (*[[tsuga-canadensis]]*) and pines (*Pinus* spp.) in mixed forest stands.

Fruits singly or scattered on the forest floor from June through October, with peak abundance in July and August.

Prefers well-drained, acidic soils in mature forests with rich organic duff layers.
