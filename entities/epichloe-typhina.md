### Hybridization and Evolution
- Origin of a fungal symbiont of perennial ryegrass occurred
  through interspecific hybridization of a mutualist with
  the ryegrass choke pathogen E. typhina (Schardl et al. 1994)
- This hybridization event gave rise to Neotyphodium
  endophytes with novel combinations of host-specificity
  genes
