# Biscotti

## Overview

**Biscotti** is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by the **Cookies Fam** genetics group in the San Francisco Bay Area. A three-way cross of **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** × **Gelato #25** × **South Florida OG**, Biscotti has earned a reputation as one of the most potent and terpene-rich strains in the modern "cookie" lineage.

The strain debuted in the California market around 2016–2017 and quickly gained traction among connoisseurs for its dessert-like aroma, deeply relaxing effects, and exceptionally high THC content. The name references the Italian twice-baked cookie, alluding to the strain's sweet, nutty flavor profile.

Biscotti has served as a parent in numerous high-profile crosses, including **Biscotti Sundae**, **Jealousy**, **Acai Berry Gelato**, and **Georgia Pie**, cementing its influence on contemporary cannabis genetics.

## Lineage

- **Type:** Indica-dominant hybrid (~80/20 indica/sativa)
- **Lineage:** Girl Scout Cookies × Gelato #25 × South Florida OG
- **Breeder:** Cookies Fam (Bernie Sanders and Jigga), circa 2016
- **THC content:** 25–30% (with select phenotypes reported above 32%)
- **CBD content:** Low (<0.1%)

**Girl Scout Cookies** contributes the dense resin production, complex cookie aroma, and balanced body effects that define the Cookies genetic line. **Gelato #25** adds its signature creamy sweetness and vibrant purple coloration. **South Florida OG** provides the heavy indica sedation, fuel-like undertones, and structural density to the buds.

## Aroma, Flavor, and Terpene Profile

**Primary aromas:** Sweet cookie dough, diesel fuel, nutty hash

**Secondary notes:** Lavender, earthy spice, dark chocolate, dried fruit

The flavor mirrors the aroma closely, delivering an initial burst of sweet, doughy cookie on the inhale that transitions to earthy, peppery hash on the exhale. The smoke is thick and expansive, with a lingering sweetness on the palate.

## Effects and Pharmacology

- **Onset:** 5–15 minutes post-inhalation
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours
- **Primary effects:** Euphoria, deep cerebral relaxation, creative introspection
- **Body effects:** Heavy physical sedation, couch-lock at higher doses, full-body warmth
- **Medical applications:** Chronic pain, insomnia, anxiety, PTSD, appetite stimulation, muscle spasms

The experience typically begins with a heady cerebral uplift accompanied by a gentle pressure behind the eyes. This transitions progressively into a heavy, enveloping body stone that is characteristic of the OG-influenced indica side of the lineage.

At moderate doses, Biscotti preserves enough mental clarity for creative pursuits and social interaction. At higher doses, the sedative effects dominate, making it best suited for evening or nighttime use.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and at higher doses, mild anxiety or paranoia in predisposed individuals. Novice consumers should approach this strain cautiously due to its high THC potency.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoor; late September to mid-October outdoor
- **Yield:** Moderate, 10–14 oz/plant indoor; 12–16 oz/plant outdoor
- **Height:** Medium, 90–130 cm indoor with training
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to advanced — sensitive to overfeeding
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean conditions; 65–80°F (18–27°C)

Biscotti produces dense, chunky buds with deep purple and forest green coloration, coated in a thick layer of frosty white trichomes. The resin production is exceptionally heavy, making the strain a strong candidate for hash and rosin extraction.

**Training techniques:** Low-stress training (LST) and scrog (screen of green) are recommended to manage canopy height and maximize light penetration. The plant responds well to topping in the vegetative stage.

**Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder during mid-flowering. CalMag supplementation is recommended, particularly in coco coir or hydroponic media. The strain can be sensitive to nitrogen excess in late flowering, which may diminish terpene production.

Pest and mold resistance is moderate. The dense bud structure requires adequate airflow to prevent bud rot (Botrytis cinerea) in humid environments.

## Phenotype Variation

Biscotti exhibits moderate phenotypic variation across cuts. The most widely circulated phenotypes include:

- **Biscotti #1 (Original Cut):** The original Cookies Fam selection. Dense, purple-tinted buds with the strongest cookie-dough aroma and the heaviest body effects.

- **Biscotti #2 (Miami Cut):** Slightly more sativa-leaning, with taller plant structure and more energetic cerebral effects. Less purple coloration but higher yield.

- **Biscotti LZ:** A select cut propagated by Lemonnade (Cookies sub-brand), known for brighter citrus terpene expression alongside the standard cookie profile.

Phenotype selection significantly influences the final product's cannabinoid and terpene balance, with THC ranging from 22% in less-selected cuts to over 30% in premium bagseed finds.

## Notable Crosses

Biscotti has become a prolific parent strain in the modern cannabis breeding scene. Its most significant descendants include:

- **Biscotti Sundae** (Biscotti × Pink Panties) — enhanced sweetness, balanced effects
- **Jealousy** (Biscotti × Sherbet BX1) — Gelato-esque creamy profile, awarded Strain of the Year 2022
- **Acai Berry Gelato** (Biscotti × Pink Panties) — fruity, berry-forward phenotype
- **Georgia Pie** (Biscotti × Gelati) — peach and baked-goods aroma, extremely popular 2021 release
- **Motorbreath** (Biscotti × Motorbreath #15) — heavy OG diesel character
