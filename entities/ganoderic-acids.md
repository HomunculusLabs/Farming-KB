## Ganoderic Acids

**Ganoderic acids** are a large family of **lanostane-type triterpenoids** found in *Ganoderma lucidum* (reishi/lingzhi mushroom) and related *Ganoderma* species. First isolated by Kubota and colleagues in 1982, ganoderic acids are among the most extensively studied triterpenoids from [[cannabis-therapeutic-index-overview]].

## Pharmacological Activities

### Anti-Tumor Effects
The anti-tumor activity of ganoderic acids is multi-faceted and represents their most clinically significant pharmacological property:

- **Apoptosis induction:** GA-T, GA-Me, and GA-DM activate intrinsic (mitochondrial) apoptotic pathways through upregulation of Bax, downregulation of Bcl-2, and caspase-3/9 activation
- **Anti-angiogenesis:** Suppression of VEGF, FGF-2, and MMP-2/9 expression inhibits tumor vascularization
- **Cell cycle arrest:** G0/G1 and G2/M phase arrest through modulation of cyclin-dependent kinase expression
- **Metastasis inhibition:** Downregulation of MMP-9 and upregulation of tissue inhibitor of metalloproteinases (TIMPs) reduce invasive potential
- **Immunomodulation:** Enhancement of natural killer cell activity, macrophage activation, and dendritic cell maturation

### Hepatoprotective Effects
Ganoderic acids, particularly GA-C and GA-A, protect liver tissue through multiple mechanisms:

- Reduction of serum transaminases (ALT, AST) in models of CCl₄, acetaminophen, and alcohol-induced liver injury
- Inhibition of hepatic stellate cell activation and collagen deposition (anti-fibrotic effect)
- Enhancement of hepatic antioxidant defenses (glutathione, SOD, catalase)
- Modulation of lipid metabolism and reduction of hepatic steatosis

### Anti-Inflammatory Activity
Multiple ganoderic acids inhibit key inflammatory mediators:

- Suppression of NF-κB nuclear translocation and DNA binding
- Reduction of TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, and PGE₂ production
- Inhibition of COX-2 and iNOS expression
- Modulation of MAPK [[plant-signaling-pathways]] (p38, JNK, ERK)
