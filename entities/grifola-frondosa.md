## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Fungi
- **Division:** Basidiomycota
- **Class:** Agaricomycetes
- **Order:** Polyporales
- **Family:** Meripilaceae
- **Genus:** Grifola
- **Species:** G. frondosa (Dicks.) Gray

**Synonyms:** *Boletus frondosus* Dicks. (basionym), *Polyporus frondosus* (Dicks.) Fr., *Coriolus frondosus* (Dicks.) Pat.

**Etymology:** *Grifola* derives from *grifo-* (a mythical creature) + *-folia* (leaf), referring to the frond-like appearance of the fruiting body. The Japanese name *maitake* (舞茸) means "dancing mushroom" — legend holds that foragers danced with joy upon finding it due to its rarity and value.

## Morphology

**Fruiting body:** Large, compound, and composed of multiple overlapping, fan-shaped caps arising from a single branched base. Entire clusters can reach 20–100 cm in diameter and weigh up to 20 kg, though 1–5 kg specimens are more typical.

**Individual caps:** 2–10 cm broad, spatulate to fan-shaped, with wavy margins. Upper surface is grayish-brown to dark brown, smooth to finely velvety. Surface develops radial fibrils with age.

**Hymenophore:** White to cream pore surface on the underside of each cap. Pores are small, angular, 1–3 per mm, and do not stain when bruised — a key identification feature distinguishing it from similar species.

**Stipe/stem structure:** Multiple short, tough, branching stems arising from a common base. Interior is white and marbled, firm but becoming tough with age.

**Spores:** Broadly ellipsoid, smooth, hyaline, 5–7 × 3.5–5 µm. Spore print is white.

**Flesh:** White, firm, and aromatic with a distinctive spicy, earthy fragrance. Taste is described as rich, nutty, and peppery.

## Distribution and Habitat

**Geographic range:** Native to temperate hardwood forests of [[coppice-and-pollard-systems]] in forest ecosystems. It can persist on infected root systems for years, producing annual fruitings.

## Bioactive Compounds and Pharmacology

**β-Glucans (D-Fraction):** The most studied bioactive component. D-Fraction is a protein-bound β-1,6-glucan with β-1,3 branches, isolated from the fruiting body. It activates macrophages, dendritic cells, and NK cells via the Dectin-1 and complement receptor 3 (CR3) pathways.

Documented immunomodulatory activities include:

- Enhanced production of interleukin-1, IL-6, IL-12, and TNF-α by activated macrophages
- Increased NK cell cytotoxicity against tumor cell lines (2–3 fold increase in vitro)
- Activation of T-helper cell and cytotoxic T-cell responses
- Synergistic enhancement of chemotherapy efficacy in murine tumor models

**MD-Fraction:** A refined, acid-soluble β-glucan preparation with higher molecular weight and enhanced bioactivity compared to D-Fraction. Used in commercial maitake supplements.

**Antidiabetic compounds:** Maitake SX-fraction, isolated from the mycelium, demonstrates insulin-sensitizing effects. Clinical studies in Japan (2001–2009) reported improved glycemic control in type 2 diabetic patients at doses of 500–1500 mg/day, with HbA1c reductions of 0.3–0.8% over 12-week periods.

**Other compounds:**

- **Grifolin** — an isoprenoid phenol with antimicrobial and cytotoxic activity
- **Ergosterol derivatives** — anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties
- **Phenolic acids** — chlorogenic, ferulic, and p-coumaric acids with free radical scavenging activity
- **Nucleotides and nucleosides** — adenosine, guanosine, and uridine contributing to umami flavor
