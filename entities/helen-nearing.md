## Helen Nearing

## Description
Helen Nearing (1904–1995) was an American homesteader, author, and musician who, with her husband Scott Nearing, pioneered the modern back-to-the-land movement. Co-author of *Living the Good Life* (1954), Helen was the practical partner in the Nearing homesteading enterprise, managing food production, preservation, and household self-sufficiency. Together with Scott, she hosted and mentored Eliot Coleman in the mid-1960s, and the Nearings sold him a portion of their Maine farm in 1968, enabling his career in organic farming.

## Key Facts

### Partnership with Scott Nearing
- Helen married Scott Nearing in 1947 (Scott was 21 years her senior)
- They built their first homestead in Vermont (1932) before moving to Maine in 1952
- Helen was the practical implementer of their self-sufficient lifestyle
- She managed the food production, preservation, and household operations
- Their partnership combined Scott's economic/philosophical thinking with Helen's practical skills

### The Good Life Center
- The Nearing homestead at Forest Farm in Harborside, Maine became a model of self-sufficient living
- After Scott's death in 1983, Helen continued to manage the property and host visitors
- The Good Life Center was established to preserve and share their legacy
- Helen wrote additional books including * Loving and Leaving the Good Life* (1992)
- The center continues to operate as an educational resource

### Influence on Coleman
- Coleman visited [[scott-and-helen-nearing]] in the mid-1960s as a young aspiring farmer
- *Living the Good Life* had inspired his interest in organic farming
- The Nearings became friends and mentors to Coleman
- In 1968, they sold him the back portion of their farm at their original 1940s purchase price
- This act of generosity was foundational to Coleman's entire farming career

### Writing and Teaching
- Co-author of *Living the Good Life* — the foundational back-to-the-land text
- Author of *Simple Food for the Good Life* (1980)
- Author of *Loving and Leaving the Good Life* (1992)
- Her writing focused on practical aspects of self-sufficient living
- Documented food preservation, cooking, and household management techniques

### Musical and Cultural Interests
- Helen was an accomplished musician (violist)
- Maintained cultural and intellectual life alongside manual labor
- Their approach balanced physical work with artistic and intellectual pursuits
- This holistic approach to rural living influenced the back-to-the-land movement
