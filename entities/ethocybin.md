## Overview

**Ethocybin** (4-phosphoryloxy-*N,N*-dimethyltryptamine; **4-PO-DMT**) is a synthetic psychedelic tryptamine and a close structural analogue of [[psilocin|psilocin]]), the same active metabolite produced by psilocybin.

## Chemical Properties

**Molecular formula:** C₁₄H₂₁N₂O₄P

**Molecular weight:** 312.30 g/mol

**IUPAC name:** [3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl] ethyl phosphate

Ethocybin is a phosphate ester of 4-hydroxy-*N,N*-dimethyltryptamine (psilocin) with an ethyl group on the phosphate oxygen. This structural modification increases lipophilicity relative to psilocybin, potentially altering absorption and blood-brain barrier penetration kinetics.

The freebase form is an oil at room temperature. The compound is typically encountered as a phosphate salt — a white crystalline solid soluble in water. It is stable under acidic conditions but hydrolyzes in alkaline environments to release psilocin.

## Pharmacology

Ethocybin functions as a **prodrug** of psilocin. After oral administration, alkaline phosphatase enzymes in the gut wall and liver cleave the phosphate ester bond, yielding 4-HO-DMT (psilocin) as the primary active metabolite. This metabolic pathway is identical to that of psilocybin.

The active metabolite psilocin is a **serotonin 5-HT₂A receptor agonist**, with additional activity at 5-HT₁A, 5-HT₂C, and 5-HT₇ receptors. The psychedelic effects are primarily mediated by 5-HT₂A receptor activation in cortical layer V pyramidal neurons, leading to increased glutamate release and thalamocortical dysrhythmia.

The ethylphosphoryloxy group may slow the rate of enzymatic dephosphorylation compared to the standard phosphoryloxy group in psilocybin. This could result in a slightly delayed onset and more gradual rise to peak plasma psilocin levels, though comparative pharmacokinetic data is limited.

## Effects

The subjective effects of ethocybin are essentially identical to those of psilocybin, since both compounds produce the same active metabolite (psilocin). Differences in onset speed, duration, and intensity are primarily pharmacokinetic rather than pharmacodynamic.

Typical oral dose range: **15–30 mg** (roughly equivalent to 10–20 mg psilocybin by weight, accounting for the higher molecular weight). Effects onset within 20–60 minutes, peak at 2–3 hours, and last 4–6 hours total.

The experience profile includes visual hallucinations (geometric patterns, color enhancement, morphing surfaces), altered perception of time, synesthesia, emotional lability, and mystical-type experiences at higher doses. The [[lsd-set-and-setting-framework]].

## Synthesis

Ethocybin is synthesized from 4-hydroxyindole via a multi-step sequence. The 4-hydroxyindole core undergoes N,N-dimethylethylenediamine coupling at the 3-position, followed by phosphorylation of the 4-hydroxy group with ethylphosphorodichloridate.

The synthetic route parallels that used for [[dmt-containing-plants-united-states-halpern]],
as it is a positional isomer of psilocybin and is covered by the Federal
Analog Act. In the United Kingdom, it is a Class A controlled substance.
It is also scheduled in many other jurisdictions that control psilocybin
and its analogues, including Australia, Germany, and much of the EU.
