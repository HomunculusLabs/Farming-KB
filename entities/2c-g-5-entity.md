# 2C-G-5

**[[psychedelic-compounds-reference]] (along with 2c-f-entity|2C-T-17) to have a potential optically active center on the side of the ring away from the nitrogen atom.

## Chemical Family Context

2C-G-5 is the most elaborate member of the 2C-G series, which explores the effects of increasingly bulky substituents at the 3 and 4 positions of the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine scaffold. The series progression demonstrates a remarkable trend: as steric bulk increases at these positions, both potency and duration tend to increase, reaching their apex in 2C-G-5.

The benzonorbornane ring system is a bicyclic structure consisting of a benzene ring fused to a norbornane (bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane) framework. This creates a rigid, three-dimensional substituent that projects into space around the aromatic ring, likely affecting [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]] site, increasing affinity. Second, the bulky hydrocarbon structure almost certainly impedes metabolic degradation, particularly by cytochrome P450 enzymes and MAO, leading to the dramatically extended duration.

The receptor binding profile of 2C-G-5 has not been formally characterized, but based on its subjective effects and structural similarity to other 2C compounds, it is presumed to act primarily as a 5-HT2A agonist with possible contributions from 5-HT2C and alpha-adrenergic receptor interactions.

The "area under the curve" concept that Shulgin emphasizes is particularly relevant here. 2C-G-5 delivers more total [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] per milligram than almost any other phenethylamine, but this comes at the cost of commitment -- users must be prepared for a two-day experience.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- 2C-B (10-25 mg, 4-8 h): Similar visual quality but vastly shorter duration

- 2C-G (20-35 mg, 18-30 h): Lower potency, shorter duration, simpler structure

- LSD (60-200 mcg, 8-12 h): While LSD is more potent by weight, 2C-G-5's duration exceeds it

- DOB (1-3 mg, 18-30 h): The amphetamine analogue shows similar duration but different character

The benzonorbornane modification represents a unique approach in psychedelic phenethylamine design, and few compounds share this structural feature.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

The extreme duration of 2C-G-5 (up to 48 hours) creates unique harm reduction challenges:

- Users must be prepared for a full two-day commitment; this is not a compound for casual or spontaneous use

- Adequate food, water, and rest provisions should be arranged in advance

- The long duration increases the risk of sleep deprivation and its associated cognitive impairment
