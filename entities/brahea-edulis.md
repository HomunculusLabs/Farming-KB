**Inflorescence:** Interfoliar, erect, 1.0 to 1.5 meters long, branching to
two or three orders. Flowers small, cream to yellowish, male and female on
the same inflorescence.

**Fruit:** Spherical drupe, 1.5 to 2.0 centimeters in diameter, ripening from
green to brownish-black. Mesocarp is thin and sweet when ripe. Seed spherical,
1.0 to 1.4 centimeters in diameter.

## Distribution and Habitat

Brahea edulis is endemic to Guadalupe Island, approximately 260 kilometers west
of the Baja California peninsula. The island is roughly 35 kilometers long and
9 kilometers wide.

The species occurs primarily on north-facing slopes and in canyon bottoms
between 200 and 1200 meters elevation. It grows in mixed chaparral and pine
forest communities alongside island oak (Quercus tomentella) and island pine
