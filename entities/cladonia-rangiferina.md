# Cladonia rangiferina (Reindeer Lichen)

**Cladonia rangiferina**, commonly called **reindeer lichen** (also known as grey reindeer lichen or Arctic moss), is a fruticose (bushy, branching) lichen species in the family Cladoniaceae. It is one of the most ecologically important [[lichens]] in circumpolar regions, serving as a primary winter food source for caribou and reindeer (*Rangifer tarandus*) across the Arctic tundra
