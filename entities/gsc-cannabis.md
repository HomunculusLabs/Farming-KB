# Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)

## Overview

**Girl Scout Cookies** (GSC) is an iconic hybrid [[blesching-cannabis-strain-selection-receptor-targeting]] that originated in the [[san-francisco-shipibo-ayahuasca-ceremonial-center]] Bay Area in the early 2010s and became one of the most commercially successful strains in modern cannabis history. A cross between **OG Kush** and an early **Durban Poison** phenotype, GSC delivers a balanced yet potent experience characterized by euphoric cerebral effects and deep physical relaxation. Its distinctive sweet, earthy aroma with notes of baked goods spawned an entire family of descendant varieties and set the standard for modern boutique [[vic-high-cannabis-genetics-mendelian-inheritance]].

## Lineage and Genetics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Hybrid (slightly indica-dominant, ~60/40) |
| Breeder | Cookie Fam (San Francisco, CA) |
| Parent 1 | OG Kush |
| Parent 2 | Durban Poison (F1) |
| THC content | 18–28% (phenotype-dependent) |
| CBD content | <0.1–0.3% |

The strain was bred by the "Cookie Fam" collective in the early 2010s Bay Area medical cannabis scene. GSC won multiple High Times Cannabis Cup awards, including first-place Hybrid at the 2013 San Francisco and 2014 Southern California Cups, catalyzing its mainstream spread.

## Chemotype Profile

GSC is a **Type I** [[cannabis-chemotype-classification]] (THC-dominant, low CBD):

### Primary Cannabinoids

- **THC (Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabinol)**: 18–28%
- **THCa**: Present in raw flower; converts to THC through decarboxylation
- **CBG, CBC**: Trace amounts (0.1–0.5%)
- **CBD**: Typically <0.1%

### Dominant Terpenes

| Terpene | Typical Range | Aroma Contribution |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| Caryophyllene | 0.5–1.5% | Peppery, spicy, woody |
| Limonene | 0.5–1.2% | Citrus, sweet, fruity |
| Humulene | 0.3–0.8% | Earthy, woody, hoppy |
| Linalool | 0.2–0.6% | Floral, lavender, sweet |
| Myrcene | 0.2–0.5% | Earthy, herbal, musky |

The interplay of caryophyllene, limonene, and linalool creates GSC's signature aroma — sweet and doughy with undertones of earth, spice, and subtle minty freshness.

## Effects Profile

### Onset and Duration

- **Onset**: Rapid (1–3 minutes when smoked or vaporized)
- **Peak effects**: 15–45 minutes
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours (smoked), 3–5 hours (edibles)

### Reported Effects

**Cerebral (primary, onset phase):**
- Euphoria and elevated mood
- Heightened creativity and sociability
- Mild sensory enhancement (music, visual appreciation)

**Physical (secondary, comedown phase):**
- Deep body relaxation spreading from the shoulders downward
- Muscular tension relief [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]]
- Progressive sedation at higher doses

### Therapeutic Applications

- **Chronic pain**: Muscular pain, [[blesching-cannabis-neuropathic-pain]], [[cannabis-for-inflammatory-conditions]]
- **Stress and anxiety**: Anxiolytic effects at moderate doses (paradoxical anxiety possible at high doses)
- **[[cannabis-for-nausea-and-appetite-stimulation]] loss**: Effective appetite stimulant for cachexia and chemotherapy side effects
- **Insomnia**: Sedative body effects promote sleep onset at higher doses
