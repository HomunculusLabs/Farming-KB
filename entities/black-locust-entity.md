## Black Locust

**Type:** Species (Plant)
**Category:** Nitrogen-fixing leguminous tree
**Also known as:** [[permplant-willows-salix-erosion-control-fodder]], shelter belts, timber, fodder,
and [[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]]:** Yes, leguminous

### Growth Characteristics
- Rapid growth rate, especially in early years
- Coppices readily and regrows vigorously after cutting
- Suckering habit can make it invasive; non-suckering cultivars
  available
- Thorny branches; can be managed by livestock confinement

### Wood Properties
- Hard, heavy, durable, strong, and very rot resistant
- One of the most durable timbers available without treatment
- Excellent firewood: slow to ignite but burns like coal
- Well suited to [[knf-poultry-feed-preparation]]
- **Honey:** Produces honey regarded as one of the finest in the world
- **Erosion control:** Commonly planted for slope stabilisation and
  shelter belts
- **Firewood:** Exceptional quality; burns with coal-like intensity
- **Wildlife habitat:** Provides cover and food for various species
- **Nitrogen fixation:** Improves soil fertility for neighbouring plants

- [[leucaena-alley-cropping-systems]]
- Alder
