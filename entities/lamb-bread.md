## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

### Dominant Terpenes

- **Myrcene:** Primary terpene, contributing herbal and earthy base notes; associated with sedative and muscle-relaxant effects at higher concentrations.
- **Terpinolene:** Secondary terpene, lending floral, fruity, and slightly piney complexity; common in sativa-dominant strains.
- **Caryophyllene:** Pepper-spice terpene; contributes to the strain's distinctive sharp finish and may provide anti-inflammatory activity.
- **Limonene:** Citrus terpene present in moderate amounts, enhancing the tropical fruit notes and mood-elevating properties.

### Cannabinoid Content

- **THC:** Typically 15–22%, though authentic landrace specimens may test lower (12–16%) compared to [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect]].

Onset is relatively rapid, with initial cerebral stimulation felt within 5–15 minutes of consumption.
