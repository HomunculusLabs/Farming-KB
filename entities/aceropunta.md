# Aceropunta

## Description

Aceropunta (literally "steel bow") is a great esoteric ship that appears in deep [[amaringo-ayahuasca-visions-shipibo-iconography]] when called by a well-sung [[icaro]]. It is one of the most elaborate visionary phenomena in Amazonian vegetalismo, described as a truly esoteric vessel that can only be seen under very strong mareación (intoxication). The ship manifests in seven different forms and travels the world visiting those who call it.

## Classification

- **Domain**: Amazonian vegetalismo / mestizo [[shamanism]]
- **Category**: Visionary vehicle, esoteric ship
- **Type**: Multiform spiritual manifestation
- **Function**: Healing deployment, spiritual transport

## Key Facts

- Aceropunta has seven different forms of appearance: battleship, submarine emerging from depths, speedboat with four stories, Viking-style ship, aircraft carrier, trimaran, and airship. In all forms, the bow is always dazzling white steel.
- The crew consists of seamen in white, doctors in violet, nurses in light blue, fairies, mermaids, sibyls, fortune tellers, and great murayas and bancos specialized in high alchemy.
- The ship is commanded by Manuel Huaya and is defended from outer space by great fairies called Llipiann Atun Huarmis (big and brilliant women) with magic high-powered lamps used as weapons.
- Guardian birds used in the science of vegetalistas accompany the ship: sarara (Anhinga anhinga), tibemama (Pandion haliaetus), pompóm, and cumpa-supay.
- Boas serve as arkanas (defenses) around the ship, and huarmi murayas dressed in scaly celestial fish armor perform alchemical healing with pink and white flowers.
- Puka-chukchas (red-haired men) travel in the rear, looking after ropes to prevent enemies from harming the crew during healing operations.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Aceropunta represents the visionary framework through which Amazonian vegetalistas conceptualize the deployment of plant medicine knowledge across space and time. The ship's multi-form nature mirrors the multi-faceted applications of plant medicines — the same plant can serve as battleship (combative defense), submarine (deep exploration), or aircraft carrier (deployment of multiple healing agents). The hierarchical crew structure (doctors, nurses, alchemists) reflects the organized, systematic nature of Amazonian plant knowledge. The ship's connection to the ayahuasca vine growing thick and beautiful in the foreground reinforces the central role of ayahuasca as the medium through which all this knowledge is accessed and deployed.
