## Satureja hortensis

## Overview

*Satureja hortensis*, commonly known as summer savory, is an annual aromatic
herb in the family Lamiaceae. Native to the Mediterranean basin and
southeastern Europe, it has been cultivated for over 2,000 years as a
culinary herb and traditional medicine. The ancient Romans considered
savory a primary flavoring herb alongside pepper and used it extensively
in cooking. The genus name may derive from *satyr* (mythological woodland
creature), referencing the plant's reputed aphrodisiac properties.

## Taxonomy and Classification

- **Kingdom:** Plantae → **Division:** Tracheophyta → **Class:** Magnoliopsida
- **Order:** Lamiales → **Family:** Lamiaceae → **Subfamily:** Nepetoideae
- **Tribe:** Mentheae → **Genus:** *Satureja*
- **Species:** *S. hortensis* L. (1753)

The genus *Satureja* comprises approximately 30 species of aromatic herbs
and subshrubs. Summer savory (*S. hortensis*) is the annual species most
widely used in cooking, while winter savory (*S. montana*) is a perennial
with a stronger, more pungent flavor. The two are sometimes confused but
are readily distinguished by growth habit and [[agaricus-campestris-biology-and-life-cycle]] within one [[oyster-mushroom-log-cultivation-temperate-regions-short-log-method]] worldwide. The plant prefers light, well-drained soils
in full sun and is tolerant of poor, sandy soils. It is sensitive to frost
and does not survive freezing temperatures, which limits its cultivation
to the frost-free [[extending-growing-season]].

## Essential Oil Composition

The essential oil of *Satureja hortensis* is chemically diverse and varies
by chemotype and geographic origin. Major constituents typically include:

  for the herb's characteristic warm, peppery aroma and much of its
