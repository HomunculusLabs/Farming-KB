## Overview

Mochi, also known as Mochi Gelato or Gelato 47, is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain bred by the Cookie Fam in collaboration with Sherbinskis. It is a cross between Thin Mint [[girl-scout-cookies]] and [[sunset-sherbet]], placing it firmly within the Cookie genetic lineage that has dominated West Coast cannabis since the early 2010s.

The strain is named for its dense, trichome-frosted buds that resemble Japanese mochi rice cakes in their pale, powdery appearance. It has earned a reputation for potent, relaxing effects paired with a sweet, dessert-like flavor profile.

Mochi has won or placed in multiple cannabis competitions, contributing to its popularity in legal markets across [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]].

## Lineage

**Female parent:** Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies (Girl Scout Cookies × Durban Poison × OG Kush).

**Male parent:** Sunset Sherbet (Girl Scout Cookies × Pink Panties).

The cross produces a genetically balanced hybrid, typically expressing approximately 50/50 indica-sativa characteristics, though individual phenotypes may lean slightly indica-dominant.

Both parents contribute the signature Cookie [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]: sweet, earthy, and complex with prominent dessert and pastry notes.

The Sunset Sherbet parent contributes enhanced fruitiness and coloration potential (purple hues in cooler temperatures).

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large in size with a dense, chunky structure typical of Cookie-family hybrids.

The calyxes are tightly packed with minimal visible stem space, giving the flowers a rock-hard consistency when properly cured.

Coloration ranges from deep forest green to lavender and purple, with vibrant orange pistils weaving through the bud structure.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosted, almost white appearance that justifies the "Mochi" name.

The resin production is abundant, making Mochi a strong candidate for solvent and solventless extraction.

## Aroma and Flavor

The dominant aroma is sweet and creamy, with notes of vanilla, cookie dough, and fresh pastry.

Secondary notes include ripe berries, earthy undertones, and a subtle floral quality from the Sherbet lineage.

On the exhale, a slight peppery spiciness emerges, attributed to caryophyllene content.

The flavor closely mirrors the aroma: sweet, creamy vanilla with berry undertones and a smooth, dessert-like finish.

The smoke is generally thick and smooth, with minimal harshness when properly flushed and cured.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:** Limonene, caryophyllene, linalool.

**Secondary terpenes:** Myrcene, humulene, pinene.

Limonene contributes the citrus-sweet top notes; caryophyllene adds peppery warmth and potential anti-inflammatory properties.

Linalool provides the floral undertones and contributes to the strain's reported relaxing and anxiolytic effects.

**THC content** typically ranges from 20% to 29%, with some tested batches exceeding 30% in competitive grows.

**CBD content** is generally low, typically below 0.1%, consistent with modern high-THC Cookie-family genetics.

[[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]] present may include CBG and CBC in trace amounts.

## Effects

Onset is relatively quick, with initial cerebral effects felt within 5–10 minutes of inhalation.

Early effects include an uplifting, euphoric head high with enhanced [[plant-sensory-perception]] and mild creative stimulation.

The experience transitions progressively toward a deeply relaxing body sensation that can become sedative at higher doses.

Common reported effects include happiness, relaxation, creativity, and increased appetite (munchies).
