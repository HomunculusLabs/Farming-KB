# ETH-LAD (6-Ethylnor-LSD)

TIHKAL entry #12. A lysergamide analogue of LSD with an ethyl group substituted for the methyl at the 6-position of ring D. ETH-LAD is slightly more potent than LSD but described as much less aggressive in its action — warmer, more allowing, and with reduced visual distortion.

## Chemical Names

- 6-Norlysergic acid, N,N-diethylamide 6-ethyl
- N,N-Diethylnorlysergamide, 6-ethyl
- 9,10-Didehydro-N,N,6-triethylergoline-8b-carboxamide
- N-Ethyl-nor-LSD

## Dosage

| Route | Dose Range |
|-------|-----------|
| Oral | 40–150 micrograms |

## Duration

8–12 hours

## Qualitative Comments

- **20 ug, orally**: "This has a very real effect at this level, whereas I have no response at all from LSD at 20 mikes."
- **50 ug, orally**: "Already coming on in fifteen minutes, and completely developed in another hour. Very few visual changes or distortions but easy eyes-closed imagery. Pretty much out after ten hours."
- **60 ug, orally**: "In about an hour or so, gentle movements of the house plants were noted. The walkway of the painting above the fireplace changed as if the sunny spots were moving ahead. The visual aspects became more LSD-like after a couple more hours, though in a very gentle way. The spider windowpane looked three-dimensional: at first I thought the windows were double-paned, but they were not. Stones, rocks and glass had a magical look to them, but tree bark looked like tree bark. Occasionally, a dark streak (spot) would go through the visual field and a page of a book would move sharply without effort."
- **75 ug, orally**: "I am up to a ++ within the hour and am feeling lazy. It is very diuretic and certainly not anorexic. Body feels balanced. Thinking easy. Concepts easy to follow through. Mind and feelings together as should be. Definitely a plus two, no further."
- **100 ug, orally**: "It sort of sneaks up on you. Certainly not the push of LSD and, sadly, not the sparkle either. Possible time slowing. Easy sleep and no price to pay the next day."
- **150 ug, orally**: "Extraordinary experience, none of the demands of LSD, just a completely together trip. Fine music, and fine sex."

## Synthesis

ETH-LAD is synthesized from LSD in three steps:

1. **6-cyano-nor-LSD**: LSD is treated with BrCN in CCl4/CHCl3 under nitrogen, protected from light, to give 6-cyano-nor-LSD (71% yield, mp 190-191 C).
2. **nor-LSD**: The cyano intermediate is reduced with zinc in acetic acid/H2O to give nor-LSD (61% yield, mp 196-198 C dec). This intermediate is completely inactive even at 500 ug.
3. **ETH-LAD**: nor-LSD is alkylated with ethyl iodide in DMF with K2CO3 to give ETH-LAD (61% yield, mp 108-110 C, [a]D +40.5 in EtOH).

## Extensions and Commentary

ETH-LAD is described as "a remarkable compound" — slightly more potent than LSD but with a dramatically modified character. There is little of the "push" or "taking control" nature of LSD, greatly reduced visual distortion, but retained warmth and humor. The experience is more "allowing rather than demanding."

The compound appears to be somewhat unstable in solution, even as the tartrate stored cold and dark — potency dropped noticeably after a few months. This is an important practical consideration for anyone working with the material.

## The 6-Position Substitution Pattern

The 6-position on ring D is critical for activity in this series:

| Substituent | Compound | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| H | nor-LSD | Completely inactive, even at 500 ug |
| CH3 (methyl) | LSD-25 | Active, 60-200 ug |
| C2H5 (ethyl) | ETH-LAD | Active, 40-150 ug; warmer, less pushy |
| C3H5 (allyl) | AL-LAD | Active, 80-160 ug |
| C3H7 (propyl) | PRO-LAD | Active, with distinct character |

"What a difference a single atom makes" — the progression from inactive (H) to powerfully psychoactive (CH3, C2H5) to distinct variations (allyl, propyl) demonstrates the exquisite sensitivity of the lysergamide receptor binding to this single substitution.

## Comparison with LSD

ETH-LAD differs from LSD in several clinically significant ways:
- **Faster onset** (15 minutes at 50 ug vs typically 30-60 minutes for LSD)
- **Reduced visual distortion** — tree bark still looks like tree bark
- **Less "push"** — no taking-control quality, no boundary dissolution at ++ doses
- **Easier sleep** — subjects report easy sleep and no hangover the next day
- **Anorexia absent** — subjects retained appetite (notable contrast with LSD)
- **Diuretic effect** — reported at several dose levels
- **Physical side effects** — mild tummy discomfort and chills early on, quickly passing

## Physical Properties

- Free base mp: 108-110 C
- Specific rotation: [a]D +40.5 (c 0.46, EtOH)
