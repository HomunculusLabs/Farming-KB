## Johnny's Selected Seeds

## Description

Johnny's Selected Seeds is a prominent seed and farm-tool supply company founded in 1973. Headquartered at 955 Benton Avenue, Winslow, Maine 04901, the company has served as a primary supplier for fresh market growers and avid home gardeners for over five decades. Contact: (877) 564-6697, website www.johnnyseeds.com.

## Classification

- **Entity Type:** Company / Seed Supplier / Farm Tool Distributor
- **Industry:** Agriculture, Horticulture, Organic Farming Supplies
- **Location:** Winslow, Maine, USA
- **Founded:** 1973

## Products and Services

Johnny's Selected Seeds offers an extensive catalog of seeds and specialized farming tools curated for practical small-farm and market-garden use. Their product range includes:

### Seeds
- Wide range of vegetable, herb, and flower seed varieties
- Excellent selection of [[greenhouse-suppliers-and-winter-harvest-varieties]] specifically suited for cold-climate and [[economics-of-winter-greenhouse-production]]
- Varieties tested and proven in northern growing conditions

### Farm Tools
The following tools, recommended by Eliot Coleman and available through Johnny's, are essential for small-scale organic farming:

- **Six-row seeder** — [[coleman-precision-seeding-greenhouse-pinpoint-six-row-seeder]] for dense plantings of salad greens and baby vegetables
- **Greens harvesters** — efficient harvesting tools for [[cut-and-come-again]] salad production
- **Victorinox harvest knives** — durable, sharp knives ideal for clean harvesting cuts
- **Broadfork** — deep soil aeration tool that loosens soil without inverting layers, preserving soil structure
- **The Tilther** — a lightweight, power-driven tiller for shallow [[jeavons-surface-cultivation-technique]]
- **30-inch-wide Austrian grading rake** — for [[soil-tilth-and-seedbed-preparation]] and soil leveling
- **Collineal hoe** — an ergonomic hoe designed for efficient cultivation in tight spacing
- **Wire weeder** — delicate weeding tool for use around small seedlings
- **Three-tooth cultivator** — for between-row cultivation in prepared beds
- **Soil block makers** — tools for creating soil blocks as an alternative to plastic pots for transplant production
- **Right-angle trowel** — designed for efficient transplanting and soil work
- **Propane flame weeder** — for thermal weed control in organic systems
- **Tomato clips and twine** — support materials for indeterminate tomato trellising
- **European-style scythe** — with folding blade for safe storage; used for mowing cover crops and field edges

### Growing Supplies
- Potting and soil blocking mixes, including the full line of **Vermont Compost** products
- **Agribon row covers** for both greenhouse inner layers and quick-hoop field covering
- Quick-hoops conduit bender (was under development at time of publication)

## Role in Winter Harvest Farming

Johnny's Selected Seeds plays a central role in the [[winter-harvest-system]] described by Eliot Coleman in *The [[eliot-coleman-and-the-winter-harvest-handbook]]*. As the primary supplier for [[four-season-farm]], the company provides:

1. **Winter-adapted varieties** specifically bred or selected for cold tolerance, low-light conditions, and protected-culture production
2. **Specialized tools** designed for the intensive, small-scale production methods used in winter greenhouse farming
3. **Row cover materials** (Agribon) essential for creating the double-layer protected environment that enables winter growing without supplemental heat
4. **Growing media** for transplant production that supports the intensive succession-planting schedule of year-round harvesting

## Key Facts

- Founded in 1973, making it one of the longest-serving suppliers to the organic and market-farming community
- Located in Maine, giving them direct experience with cold-climate growing challenges
- Their catalog is a curated selection aimed at practical growers rather than casual gardeners
- They collaborate with growers and researchers on tool development, including the quick-hoops system
- The company serves as a single-source supplier for the complete winter harvest toolkit

## Relevance to Cultivation

Johnny's Selected Seeds is a critical supply-chain partner for deep-organic and winter harvest farming. Their significance to cultivation includes:

- **Variety Selection:** Their seed offerings include cultivars specifically chosen for performance in protected winter culture, reducing the trial-and-error burden on individual growers
- **Tool Innovation:** Many of the specialized tools they distribute were developed in collaboration with working farmers and represent best-in-class solutions for intensive small-farm operations
- **Knowledge Dissemination:** Their catalog and educational materials help disseminate practical growing knowledge to the broader farming community
- **Supply Chain Reliability:** As a single source for seeds, tools, row covers, and growing media, Johnny's simplifies procurement for small farms that lack the purchasing power of larger operations
- **Climate Adaptation:** Their focus on cold-climate varieties supports the expansion of winter harvest techniques to northern regions
