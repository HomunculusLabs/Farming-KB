# Amitabbha (Amitabha)

## Description
Amitabbha (Amitabha) is the Dhyani Buddha representing boundless light and all-discriminating wisdom, associated with the element of fire and the function of seeing. In *The Psychedelic Experience*, Leary identifies Amitabbha as the Peaceful Deity personifying **Vision 3: The Fire-Flow of Internal Unity** — the emotionally toned vision of the Second Bardo characterized by intense feelings of love, unity, and orgastic oneness with all life.

## Classification
- **Category:** Deity — Dhyani Buddha
- **Domain:** Tibetan Buddhism, Pure Land Buddhism, Bardo Thodol
- **Tradition:** Mahayana / Vajrayana Buddhism

## Key Facts
- One of the Five Dhyani Buddhas, associated with the West direction
- Name means "Infinite Light" or "Boundless Light" in Sanskrit
- Associated with the element of fire and the sense of sight
- Represents "life eternal" and discriminating inner vision
- Accompanied by the Bodhisattva Chenrazee (Avalokiteshvara), embodiment of mercy and compassion
- The "deep red light of discriminating inner vision shines forth from his heart" (Lama Govinda)
- Color association: Red
- In Pure Land Buddhism, Amitabha is the Buddha of the Western Paradise (Sukhavati)

## Role in the Psychedelic Bardo Framework
In Leary's adaptation, Amitabbha represents **Vision 3: The Fire-Flow of Internal Unity** (eyes closed, external stimuli ignored, emotional aspects). This vision involves the pure energy of the First Bardo being woven into emotionally charged visionary experiences. The positive expression is ecstatic unity — feelings of intense love, merging with all life forms, the "two-billion-year-old electric sexual dance." The negative trap involves attachment, greed, isolation, and bodily concerns that pull the voyager back into ego-bound consciousness.

## Accompanying Figures
- **Chenrazee (Avalokiteshvara):** Bodhisattva of compassion, "the great pitier ever on the lookout to discover distress"
- **Glorious Gentle-voiced One:** Bodhisattva companion
- Female incarnates of "song" and "light"

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology
The Fire-Flow vision is commonly reported during psilocybin sessions — experiences of overwhelming love, bodily warmth, and felt connection to all living beings. Understanding Amitabbha's archetype helps psychedelic guides recognize this phase and support the voyager through it. The negative counterpart (attachment, greed, bodily anxiety) maps onto challenging experiences where subjects become stuck on physical discomfort or possessive feelings.
