## Description

Earth tube heating is a passive geothermal technique for greenhouse temperature moderation. A smooth-walled, rigid plastic pipe (12-inch diameter) is buried approximately 6 feet deep across a 100-foot field run, then connected into the greenhouse. A fan draws outside air through the buried pipe, where the stable deep-earth temperature (approximately 45°F / 7°C) warms the air before it enters the growing space.

## Classification

- **Type**: Agricultural technique / Passive heating system
- **Category**: Geothermal energy, greenhouse climate control
- **Energy source**: Subsurface earth temperature (free)
- **Complexity**: Moderate (excavation, piping, fan)

## Key Facts

- At 6 feet depth, soil temperature remains approximately 45°F (7°C) year-round in northern climates
- Fan energy consumption is approximately 15% of what would be required to generate equivalent heat artificially
- Warmed air can be distributed under inner row-cover layers through perforated plastic tubes running the length of the greenhouse
- Considered but not yet implemented by [[eliot-coleman]] at [[four-season-farm]]
- Compatible with existing cold-house infrastructure — can feed warmed air directly under floating row covers

## Technical Specifications

- **Pipe material**: Smooth-walled rigid plastic
- **Pipe diameter**: 12 inches
- **Burial depth**: Approximately 6 feet
- **Run length**: Approximately 100 feet (field to greenhouse)
- **Air delivery**: Via perforated tubes under inner row-cover layer
- **Energy requirement**: Fan only (~15% of equivalent fossil-fuel heating)

## Advantages

- Extremely low operating cost after installation
- No combustion, no fuel, no air pollution
- Provides consistent baseline temperature regardless of weather
- Compatible with organic and low-input farming philosophies
- Synergistic with double-cover and inner-layer systems

## Considerations

- Significant upfront excavation cost for trenching to 6-foot depth
- Requires electric fan (can be solar-powered)
- Provides only modest temperature lift — heats to approximately 45°F, not warm enough for heat-loving crops
- Best suited as a supplement to passive systems, not a standalone heating solution

## Relevance to Cultivation

Earth tube heating represents a middle ground between fully unheated and minimally heated greenhouse systems. For growers in Zones 3-6 where winter cold pushes even hardy crops to their limits, earth tubes could provide the few critical degrees of protection needed without the ongoing cost and environmental impact of fossil-fuel heating. The technique aligns with the Four Season Farm philosophy of working with natural energy flows rather than fighting against them. If combined with double-cover outer layers and inner row-cover fabric, earth tube heat delivered under the inner layer could potentially maintain above-freezing conditions with minimal energy input.
