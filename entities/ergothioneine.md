# Ergothioneine

**Ergothioneine** (abbreviated **EGT**, chemical name **2-mercaptohistidine trimethylbetaine**) is a naturally occurring amino acid derivative and potent antioxidant. Unlike most dietary antioxidants, ergothioneine is accumulated in the human body through a dedicated transporter — the **organic cation transporter novel type 1 (OCTN1/SLC22A4)** — suggesting it serves an essential physiological role rather than acting as an incidental dietary scavenger. It is biosynthesized almost exclusively by **fungi** and certain **actinobacteria**, making mushrooms one of the richest dietary sources.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC Name:** 2-[(3S)-3-(Trimethylammonio)-1H-indol-2-yl]-4-sulfanylbutanoic acid
- **Molecular Formula:** C₉H₁₅N₃O₂S₂
- **Molecular Weight:** 229.33 g/mol
- **CAS Number:** 497-30-3
- **Appearance:** White to off-white crystalline powder
- **Solubility:** Highly soluble in water; sparingly soluble in ethanol
- **Stability:** Extremely stable at physiological pH due to the tautomeric equilibrium between thiol and thione forms

### Tautomerism

Ergothioneine exists predominantly in its **thione** (C=S) form at physiological pH (~7.4), with only approximately 0.1–4% present as the thiol (–SH) form. This is in contrast to glutathione, which exists predominantly as the thiol. The thione form makes ergothioneine remarkably stable and resistant to auto-oxidation, allowing it to persist in tissues for extended periods without rapid degradation.

## Biosynthesis

### Fungal Pathway

Ergothioneine biosynthesis in fungi proceeds through a five-step enzymatic pathway:

1. **Histidine methylation:** *EgtD* — S-adenosylmethionine (SAM)-dependent Nα-trimethylation of L-histidine to form hercynine
2. **Cysteine addition:** *EgtB* (or *Egt1* in yeast) — Iron(II)-dependent oxidative coupling of hercynine with cysteine to form γ-glutamylcysteinylhercynine (or hercynylcysteine sulfoxide, depending on the organism)
3. **Glutamylation (optional):** *EgtC* — In some fungi, glutamate is attached as a protective group
4. **Cyclization:** Formation of the thiazolidine ring intermediate
5. **Deconjugation:** *EgtE* — Removal of the glutamyl group (if present) to yield free ergothioneine

### Producing Organisms

Humans and animals **cannot synthesize ergothioneine** and must obtain it entirely from dietary sources. Biosynthesis is confirmed in:

- **Basidiomycota mushrooms:** *Pleurotus* spp., *Agaricus* spp., *[[stamets-reishi-ganoderma-lucidum-cultivation]]*, *[[maitake-grifola-frondosa-comprehensive-profile]]*, *[[neurospora-crassa]]*), molds (*Aspergillus* spp.)
- **Actinobacteria:** *Mycobacterium* spp., *Streptomyces* spp.
- **Cyanobacteria:** Some *Nostoc* and *Anabaena* species
