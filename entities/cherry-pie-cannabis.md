# Cherry Pie

## Overview

Cherry Pie is a balanced indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for
its dessert-like cherry aroma and deeply relaxing body effects. A cross between
Granddaddy Purple and Durban Poison, it inherits a complex terpene profile
that balances sweet fruitiness with earthy, pungent undertones. Since emerging
in the San Francisco Bay Area medical cannabis scene, Cherry Pie has become one
of the most widely recognized strains in North American dispensaries.

The strain typically expresses a 70/30 indica-to-sativa ratio, delivering a
calming cerebral onset that transitions into full-body relaxation. Its
accessibility to novice and experienced consumers alike, combined with its
distinctive flavor, has made it a market staple.

## Lineage

Cherry Pie descends from two foundational cannabis genetics:

- **Granddaddy Purple (female)** — Provides the dense purple bud structure,
  grape and berry terpene base, and heavy indica sedation. GDP descends from
  Purple Urkle and Big Bud, carrying deep Afghani heritage.
- **Durban Poison (male)** — Contributes uplifting cerebral effects, a spicy
  anise undertone, and the signature bud shape. A pure South African landrace
  sativa.

The cross was reportedly stabilized by breeder Ken Estes of Grand Daddy Purp
Genetics in the early 2010s, though origin stories vary among sources.

## Appearance

Cherry Pie buds are medium to large with a dense, compact structure
characteristic of indica-dominant varieties. Deep forest-green calyxes show
occasional purple and violet hues in cooler growing conditions. Bright orange
pistils contrast against dark foliage. Thick, milky-white trichome coverage
gives buds a frosted, crystalline appearance. Leaves shift toward deep purple
during the final flowering weeks, especially with nighttime temperatures below
15°C.

## Aroma and Flavor

The strain's name directly reflects its primary aromatic profile. When ground,
Cherry Pie releases sweet, ripe cherry reminiscent of a baked fruit tart.

Primary notes include sweet dark cherry, tart berry, and subtle vanilla.
Secondary notes are earthy and slightly pungent with a spicy peppery finish.
The aftertaste brings lingering cherry sweetness on the exhale with hints of
damp earth and woodiness. The complexity comes from the interplay between
Granddaddy Purple's grape-berry sweetness and Durban Poison's spicy anise.

## Terpene Profile

Cherry Pie's distinctive aroma and effects are driven by a balanced terpene
profile: **Myrcene** (0.4–0.7%) is the dominant terpene, contributing
sedative, relaxing body effects and earthy aroma. **Caryophyllene** (0.2–0.4%)
adds peppery spiciness and binds to CB2 receptors for anti-inflammatory
action. **Linalool** (0.1–0.3%) provides floral, lavender-like notes and
enhances calming effects. **Humulene** (0.1–0.2%) adds earthy, woody
undertones. **Limonene** (trace–0.15%) contributes subtle citrus brightness.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Typical cannabinoid ranges from licensed laboratory testing: THC 16–24% with
premium phenotypes reaching 26%; CBD 0.01–0.1% (negligible); CBN trace amounts
in aged flower; THCV trace amounts from Durban Poison lineage.

## Effects

The effects follow a clear two-stage progression characteristic of indica-
dominant hybrids with sativa influence.

**Onset (0–15 minutes):** Cerebral euphoria begins in the head with gentle,
uplifting pressure behind the eyes. Mood elevates without racing thoughts;
users report feeling happy, sociable, and mildly creative.

**Peak (15–45 minutes):** Indica effects emerge as a warm, spreading body
sensation starting in the shoulders and moving downward. Muscular tension
dissolves and physical heaviness sets in.

**Duration (2–3 hours):** Deep relaxation continues, potentially progressing
to couch-lock at higher doses. Sleepiness develops in the final phase, making
this strain best suited for evening or nighttime consumption.

## Medical Applications

Cherry Pie is frequently recommended for chronic pain management (myrcene-
dominant analgesic body effects), anxiety and stress relief (linalool and
caryophyllene provide anxiolytic properties without heavy sedation at moderate
doses), insomnia (progressive body relaxation transitions to sleep at higher
doses), appetite stimulation, and PTSD symptom management (balanced head-body
effect helps ground users during anxiety episodes).

## Cultivation

Cherry Pie is moderately difficult to cultivate. Flowering time is 8–9 weeks
indoors with yields of 12–16 oz/m². Outdoor yields reach 15–20 oz/plant, ready
for harvest in late September to early October. Mediterranean or warm, dry
conditions are preferred; the strain is sensitive to high humidity during late
flowering due to mold risk on dense buds. Purple coloration benefits from cool
nighttime temperatures in the final two weeks. It responds well to low-stress
training (LST) and scrog methods; topping is recommended for even canopy.

## Notable Phenotypes and Crosses

Several notable derivative crosses have emerged: Cherry Pie × Girl Scout Cookies
produces exceptionally sweet, dessert-forward phenotypes with amplified
trichome production. Cherry AK crosses with AK-47 for added uplifting energy.
Cherry Garcia enhances cerebral effects while retaining cherry aroma. Black
Cherry Punch crosses with Purple Punch, intensifying color and potency.

## Side Effects

Common side effects include dry mouth and dry eyes, typical of high-THC
cannabis. At higher doses, some users report mild dizziness, paranoia (more
common in anxiety-prone individuals), and lethargy. The sedative properties
impair coordination and cognitive function, making driving inadvisable.
