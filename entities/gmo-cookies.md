## Overview

**GMO Cookies**, also known simply as **GMO** or **Garlic Cookies**, is a potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by **Mamiko Seeds**. It is a cross between **[[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] cannabis market, prized for its unique garlic-onion-pungent aroma profile and deeply relaxing effects. Despite its intimidating name (a play on "Garlic, Mushroom, Onion"), GMO contains no genetically modified organisms.

GMO consistently tests at high THC levels, typically ranging from 25-32%, placing it among the most potent strains available. It has won multiple awards, including first place at the 2020 Emerald Cup and recognition at various High Times Cannabis Cups.

The strain's popularity has surged since approximately 2018, driven by the craft cannabis movement and growing consumer interest in unique, savory [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]]

The genetic ratio is approximately 70% indica / 30% sativa. Chemdawg's influence dominates the [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]] alongside Chemdog, Sour Diesel, and OG Kush, making it a direct descendant of some of the most influential [[haze-lineage-and-legacy-in-modern-breeding]] history.

GMO has itself become a significant breeding parent, used to create numerous progeny strains. Notable crosses include **GMO x Cookies and Cream**, **GMO x Forum Stomper**, and **GMO x (various Gelato lines)**. Its genetics are highly valued by breeders for their potency, resin production, and unique terpene profile.

## Appearance

GMO buds are large, dense, and chunky, with a characteristic spade or rounded cone shape typical of indica-dominant varieties. The calyxes are tightly packed and heavily frosted with a thick layer of white trichomes that extends to the sugar leaves.

The coloration is predominantly dark forest green, often with deep purple and violet hues, especially when exposed to cooler temperatures during late flowering.

Bright orange pistils weave throughout the resinous flowers, creating a striking visual contrast against the dark foliage. The overall appearance is compact and heavy for its size, indicative of the dense resin glands packed throughout the flower structure.

Trichome coverage is exceptional, with large, bulbous capitate-stalked trichomes visible to the naked eye. The buds feel extremely sticky and resinous to the touch, with a weight that reflects their dense internal structure.

## Aroma and Flavor

GMO's aroma is its most distinctive trait and the source of its controversial name. The primary scent notes include:

- **Garlic and onion** — the dominant and most recognizable notes, pungent and savory
- **Mushroom and earthy** — a deep, loamy undertone that adds complexity
- **Diesel and fuel** — inherited from Chemdawg, providing a sharp, chemical edge
- **Sweet cookie** — a subtle backnote from the GSC parent, barely detectable beneath the savory dominance

The flavor follows the aroma closely, with an initial hit of roasted garlic and mushroom that transitions into earthy, diesel-like exhale notes.

A faint sweetness lingers on the palate after exhaling, reminiscent of the [[girl-scout-cookies]] lineage. When vaporized at lower temperatures (around 185°C), the garlic and earthy notes are most pronounced, while higher temperatures bring out the diesel and chemical undertones.

The combination of savory and pungent flavor notes makes GMO a polarizing strain among consumers. Enthusiasts praise its complexity and depth, while those who prefer fruity or sweet profiles may find it challenging. Its flavor has been compared to roasted garlic bread with a diesel finish.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

The dominant terpenes in GMO are **caryophyllene**, **myrcene**, and **limonene**, with significant contributions from **humulene** and **linalool**:

- **Caryophyllene** (~0.8-1.2%): contributes peppery, spicy notes and binds to CB2 receptors
- **Myrcene** (~0.6-1.0%): provides sedative, musky qualities and enhances THC uptake
- **Limonene** (~0.4-0.8%): adds subtle citrus brightness and mood-elevating effects
- **Humulene** (~0.3-0.5%): contributes earthy, woody notes with anti-inflammatory properties
- **Linalool** (~0.2-0.4%): adds floral undertones and anxiolytic effects

Cannabinoid profile typically includes: THC 25-32%, CBD <0.1%, with trace amounts of CBG and CBC.

## Effects and Cultivation

### Effects

Onset is rapid, beginning with a cerebral pressure behind the eyes and temples that quickly gives way to a full-body relaxation. The initial head high is mildly euphoric and stoney, with users reporting significant mood elevation and sensory enhancement.

The body effects are deeply sedating, often described as "couch-lock" in higher doses. Physical tension melts away progressively, starting in the shoulders and neck before spreading through the entire body. These properties make GMO popular among medical patients seeking relief from chronic pain, insomnia, and muscle spasms.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and dizziness. Inexperienced users or those sensitive to high-THC strains may experience anxiety or paranoia at higher doses.

Due to its sedating nature, GMO is best reserved for evening or nighttime use. Novice consumers are advised to start with a low dose and wait at least 30 minutes before redosing, as the effects can intensify gradually over the first hour.
