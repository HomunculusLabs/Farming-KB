# Critical Mass

## Overview

- **Classification:** Indica-dominant hybrid (approximately 80% indica / 20% sativa)
- **Origin:** Bred from an Afghani indica crossed with Skunk #1
- **Name Origin:** Named for massive, heavy yields — buds grow so dense they can reach
  "critical mass" and snap branches under their own weight
- **Reputation:** One of the most recognized medicinal cannabis strains worldwide,
  prized for therapeutic potency and exceptional production
- **Breeder:** Originally developed by Mr. Nice Seed Bank (Shantibaba) in the 1990s
- **General Profile:** Combines the sedating body stone of Afghani genetics with the
  reliability of Skunk #1 — a workhorse strain for patients and commercial growers

## Lineage & Genetics

- **Parent 1 — Afghani:** Pure indica landrace from the Hindu Kush region; contributes
  [[blesching-cannabis-deep-relaxation-healing]], heavy [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], and compact structure
- **Parent 2 — Skunk #1:** Legendary hybrid (Afghani x Acapulco Gold x [[cannabis-colombian-gold-landrace-sativa]]);
  contributes vigor, yield, disease resistance, and signature skunky aroma
- **Breeder:** Shantibaba (Mr. Nice Seed Bank) — same program that produced White Widow
  and Super Silver Haze
- **Alternative Names:** Also sold as **"Critical +"** in some markets (particularly
  Spain); often a slightly sativa-leaning phenotype or separate Dinafem Seeds cross
- **Genetic Stability:** Highly stable and uniform; well-suited for large-scale grows
- **Heritage Significance:** One of the most commercially successful indica crosses in
  modern cannabis history

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 14–22% (phenotype and grow conditions dependent)
- **CBD:** 0.5–2% — notably high for a non-hemp, non-specifically-bred CBD strain
- **THC:CBD Ratio:** Typically 10:1 to 5:1 in standard cuts
- **1:1 Variants:** Critical Mass CBD achieves ~1:1 ratio with 5–8% THC and 5–10% CBD
- **Minor Cannabinoids:** Detectable CBG and CBC in well-cured samples
- **[[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]]:** Moderate-to-high THC with meaningful CBD creates balanced
  psychoactivity with pronounced physical relief
- **Testing Notes:** CBD content varies by breeder; Mr. Nice original trends higher

## Terpene Profile

- **Primary — Myrcene (Dominant):** Sedative, relaxing; earthy/herbal base aroma;
  enhances THC absorption across the blood-brain barrier
- **Secondary — β-Caryophyllene:** Peppery, spicy undertone; binds CB2 receptors for
  added anti-inflammatory properties
- **Secondary — α-Pinene:** Piney, fresh; counteracts sedation slightly, promotes
  mental clarity and bronchodilation
- **Tertiary — Limonene:** Subtle citrus brightness; mood-elevating and stress-reducing
- **Supporting — Humulene:** Woody, earthy depth; mild appetite-suppressant modulation
- **Aroma:** Earthy and skunky with sweet undertones, hints of citrus and pine
- **Flavor:** Smooth smoke, earthy hash-like exhale; slightly sweet aftertaste

## Effects

- **Onset:** Fast-acting, typically felt within 5–10 minutes
- **Body:** Deep, enveloping physical relaxation from limbs inward; significant muscle
  tension relief
- **Mind:** Mild cerebral euphoria, calming rather than stimulating; mental fog
  increases at higher doses
- **Sedation:** Strongly sedative at moderate-to-high doses; progresses to full body lock
- **Couch-Lock Potential:** High — quintessential couch-lock strain above tolerance
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours (moderate dose); up to 4 hours (heavy consumption)
- **Appetite:** Strong stimulation ("the munchies") is a hallmark effect
- **Social Effect:** Generally introspective; not a social strain
- **Tolerance Buildup:** Moderate — regular users may need dose increases over time
- **Negative Effects:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, dizziness at high doses, anxiety in
  sensitive individuals (mitigated by CBD content)

## Medicinal Applications

- **[[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]:** Effective for neuropathic, joint, and back pain; fibromyalgia;
  long-lasting relief suitable for sustained management
- **Muscle Spasms:** Anti-spasmodic properties beneficial for MS, ALS, and general
  cramping
- **Insomnia:** Strong sedative makes it a go-to for sleep onset and maintenance;
  evening/nighttime use only
- **Anxiety & Stress:** CBD tempers THC-driven paranoia; anxiolytic effects promote
  calm without heavy intoxication at lower doses
- **Appetite Loss:** Reliable stimulant for chemotherapy, HIV/AIDS patients, or eating
  disorders
- **Nausea:** Anti-emetic properties help manage chemo-induced and general nausea
- **Migraine:** Vascular relaxation and pain relief reduce severity and frequency
- **PTSD:** Calming, grounding effects useful for managing hyperarousal symptoms

## Cultivation Notes

- **Environments:** Indoor, outdoor, and greenhouse; performs well in all
- **Flowering Time:** 6–8 weeks (49–56 days); among the fastest-finishing high-yield
  indicas
- **Yield — Indoor:** 400–600 g/m² optimal; can exceed 600 g/m² with training
- **Yield — Outdoor:** 500–700 g/plant; harvest late Sept to early Oct (N. Hemisphere)
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly — forgiving of minor nutrient/environment errors
- **Plant Height:** Medium; typically 80–140 cm indoors
- **Odor Level:** Moderate — lower than many Skunk descendants; somewhat discreet
- **Mold/[[cannabis-disease-pest-resistance-breeding-clarke]]:** Good general resistance; dense buds susceptible to botrytis
  in high humidity — ensure adequate airflow
- **Nutrient Needs:** Moderate feeder; avoid overfeeding mid-flower
- **Training:** Highly responsive to SOG and ScrOG; LST recommended for branch support
- **Support:** Bamboo stakes or trellis netting essential in late flower to prevent
  breakage from bud weight
- **Availability:** Widely available as clones and seeds from Mr. Nice, Dinafem, and
  numerous other banks
- **Harvest Indicators:** Milky trichomes with 10–20% amber; pungent aroma intensifies
  at peak ripeness

## Physical Characteristics

- **Plant Structure:** Bushy, compact indica; strong lateral branching
- **Bud Structure:** Extremely dense, heavy, chunky colas; tightly packed calyxes
- **Branch Strength:** Moderate — branches may snap under mature bud weight
- **Color:** Dark forest green foliage; vibrant orange-to-amber pistils at maturity;
  some phenotypes show purple hues in cooler temps
- **Trichome Coverage:** Thick, generous coating — frosty, nearly white appearance;
  excellent for hash and concentrate production
- **Leaf Shape:** Broad indica-typical fan leaves; deep green with slight serration
- **Resin Production:** Very high — suitable for ice water hash, dry sift, and rosin
- **Cure Quality:** Proper cure develops smooth, hash-like flavor with enhanced
  sweetness; minimum 4-week cure recommended

## Variants

- **Critical Mass CBD:** Specialized 1:1 THC/CBD variant for patients needing high CBD
  with reduced psychoactivity; 5–10% CBD
- **Critical +:** Slightly sativa-leaning Dinafem Seeds version; faster flowering and
  slightly uplifting; debated as distinct cross vs. selected phenotype
- **Critical Bilbo:** Spanish-bred variant popular in European markets; similar profile
  with regional selection for European conditions
- **Critical Mass Auto:** Autoflowering ruderalis cross; full lifecycle in 8–9 weeks
  from seed; reduced yield but unmatched speed
- **Critical 2.0:** Updated Mr. Nice Seed Bank version with improved yield and disease
  resistance while maintaining original characteristics
