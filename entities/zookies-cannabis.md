## Zookies

## Overview

Zookies is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing Animal Cookies with Gorilla Glue #4, resulting in a potent cultivar known for its complex cookie-diesel aroma and deeply relaxing effects.

The strain emerged from the Bay Area breeding scene and gained rapid popularity for combining the dessert-like [[girl-scout-cookies]] × Fire OG

**Parent 2:** Gorilla Glue #4 (♂) — Chem's Sister × Sour Dubb × Chocolate Diesel

**Ratio:** Approximately 50/50 indica-sativa, with slight indica dominance in most phenotypes.

**Breeder:** Originated in the California Bay Area, popularized through clonal propagation by Bay Area growers and subsequently distributed by numerous seed companies.

The strain inherits its dense bud structure and cookie terpenes from the Animal Cookies parent, while Gorilla Glue #4 contributes its heavy [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] with occasional purple undertones to nearly black in some phenotypes, especially when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving the flowers a frosted, silvery-white appearance. Pistils are a mix of burnt orange and amber, tightly curled against the calyxes.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is pungent and multifaceted, combining sweet, nutty cookie notes from the Animal Cookies lineage with sharp, fuel-like diesel undertones from the Gorilla Glue parent.

Secondary notes include earthy chocolate, [[cannabis-minor-cannabinoids]]:** Trace CBG, CBC

The high caryophyllene content (often exceeding 1%) is characteristic of the Cookies lineage and contributes to the strain's signature peppery finish.

## Effects

Onset is relatively rapid, typically felt within 5–10 minutes of inhalation. The initial cerebral effects are euphoric and slightly psychedelic, with an uplifted mood and enhanced [[psilocybin-therapeutic-applications]].

Common medicinal applications include [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]].

## Cultivation

**Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks indoors

**Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors)
