## Cordyceps sinensis

**Type:** Species
**Category:** Medicinal fungus (Ascomycetes, Cordycipitaceae)
**Also known as:** Caterpillar Fungus, Tochukaso, Dong Chong Xia Cao,
Yarsa Gunbu

## Overview
Cordyceps sinensis is an entomopathogenic fungus that parasitises
larvae of Lepidoptera (moth caterpillars) in the high-altitude
grasslands of the Tibetan Plateau and Himalayas. The fungus gradually
replaces the caterpillar tissue, and a club-shaped [[ergosterol]] and related compounds
- **Adenosine:** Cardioprotective and antiplatelet agent
- **Cordycepic acid (D-mannitol):** Diuretic and bronchodilatory

### Pharmacological Activities
The main activities of C. sinensis are in oxygen-free-radical
scavenging. Additional documented properties include:
- Anti-cancer polysaccharides with hypoglycaemic activity
- Fatigue reduction and improved motor function
- Respiratory support (traditional use for chronic cough, asthma,
  bronchitis)
- Renal protective effects (nephroprotective)
- Sexual potentiator activity
- Hepatoprotective effects
