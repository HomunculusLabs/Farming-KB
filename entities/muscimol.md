# Muscimol

**Muscimol** is the primary psychoactive constituent of
*Amanita muscaria* and related Amanita species.

Unlike tryptamine psychedelics, muscimol is an isoxazole
alkaloid and a potent GABA_A receptor agonist. Its effects are
sedative-hypnotic, dissociative, and oneirogenic (dream-like)
rather than serotoninergic hallucinations. Muscimol is not
produced directly by the mushroom — fresh tissue contains
ibotenic acid, which decarboxylates to muscimol upon heating.

## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC:** 5-(Aminomethyl)-3-hydroxyisoxazole
- **Molecular formula:** C4H6N2O2
- **Molecular weight:** 114.10 g/mol
- **Structure:** Isoxazole alkaloid; bicyclic system with
  hydroxyl and aminomethyl substituents
- **Appearance:** White crystalline solid
- **Solubility:** Water, ethanol, methanol; poorly soluble
  in nonpolar solvents
- **Stability:** Stable dry; aqueous solutions slowly degrade
  at room temperature
- **pKa:** ~5.0 (hydroxyl group)

## Pharmacology

Muscimol is among the most selective GABA_A agonists known.

- **Primary target:** GABA_A receptor — direct agonist at the
  GABA binding site, distinct from benzodiazepines (allosteric)
  and barbiturates (prolonged channel opening)
- **Selectivity:** Highly selective for GABA_A over GABA_B;
  minimal activity at glutamate, serotonin, dopamine receptors
- **Potency:** EC50 ~1 µM at GABA_A receptors in cortical
  neurons; comparable to GABA but more metabolically stable
- **BBB penetration:** Crosses via neutral amino acid
  transporter (system L)
- **Metabolism:** Partial hepatic oxidation; renal excretion
- **Duration:** 4–10 hours depending on dose and preparation

The GABAergic mechanism produces sedation, ataxia, altered body
perception, and dreamlike visual phenomena fundamentally
different from 5-HT2A-mediated psychedelic hallucinations.

## Effects

- **Onset:** 30–120 minutes after ingestion
- **Duration:** 4–10 hours; residual sedation common 2–4 hours
  after peak effects subside
- **Threshold:** 5–10 mg pure muscimol
- **Common dose:** 10–15 mg
- **Strong dose:** 15–30 mg

**Subjective effects:**

- Dissociative floating or spinning sensations
- Vivid dreamlike imagery with closed eyes (hypnagogic)
- Altered body size perception and spatial orientation
- Pronounced sedation and deep relaxation
- Mild auditory distortion; occasional synesthesia
- Nausea from residual ibotenic acid
- Muscle relaxation and ataxia at higher doses
- Sleep onset with vivid dreams in sedative phase

## Synthesis and Biosynthesis

In the mushroom, muscimol forms by heat-catalyzed
decarboxylation of ibotenic acid (α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-
isoxazoleacetic acid). This conversion is the basis for all
traditional preparation methods.

Laboratory synthesis starts with ethyl acetoacetate and
hydroxylamine to form the isoxazole ring, followed by
5-position functionalization. Several routes exist, but
natural product remains the primary ethnopharmacological
source.

## Traditional and Cultural Use

- **Siberian shamanism:** Central entheogen of Kamchatka,
  Chukchi, Koryak, and Evenki peoples
- **Preparation:** Dried over fire; sometimes consumed after
  reindeer ingestion (reindeer metabolize ibotenic acid but
  excrete intact muscimol)
- **Urine recycling:** Muscimol excreted largely unchanged;
  drinking urine of a consumer produces effects with reduced
  nausea — distinctive feature of Siberian practice
- **Soma hypothesis:** Wasson proposed *A. muscaria* as the
  Vedic Soma; debated but influential
- **European folklore:** Appears throughout Northern European
  and Slavic traditions, associated with midwinter celebrations

## Legal Status

- **United States:** Unscheduled at federal level; not in the
  Controlled Substances Act
- **International:** Not scheduled under the UN Convention
  on Psychotropic Substances
- **Netherlands:** Sold in smart shops until 2019 restrictions
- **Australia:** Restricted in some states, not federally

## Related Compounds

- **Ibotenic acid:** Biosynthetic precursor; glutamate agonist
  with unpleasant stimulant effects; decarboxylates to muscimol
- **THIP (Gaboxadol):** Synthetic analogue developed as a sleep
  aid; GABA_A agonist; reached Phase III trials before
  discontinuation
- **Muscimol hydrochloride:** Water-soluble research salt form
- **Muscimol acetate:** Ester prodrug for improved
  pharmacokinetics
