# Nabilone

**Type:** Chemical / Pharmaceutical / Synthetic Cannabinoid
**Category:** FDA-approved synthetic cannabinoid
**Also known as:** Cesamet

## Overview
Nabilone is a synthetic cannabinoid approved by the FDA and marketed
under the brand name Cesamet. It is structurally related to THC but is
a chemically distinct compound designed to mimic THC's therapeutic
effects. Nabilone is one of three major FDA-approved cannabinoid-based
pharmaceuticals, alongside [[dronabinol]] (Marinol) and Sativex. It is
primarily prescribed for nausea and vomiting associated with
chemotherapy.

## Key Details

### Chemical Properties
- Chemical class: Synthetic cannabinoid (carbazole derivative)
- Structurally related to but distinct from delta-9-THC
- Activates cannabinoid receptors (CB1/CB2)
- Available in capsule form for oral administration
- FDA-approved pharmaceutical product
- Longer half-life than natural THC

### FDA-Approved Indications
- **Chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting:** Primary approved use;
