## Overview

**Maclura tinctoria**, commonly known as fustic, old fustic, or mora, is a medium to large deciduous tree in the mulberry family (Moraceae).

It is native to tropical and subtropical regions of the Americas, from Mexico and the Caribbean to northern Argentina.
The species is best known historically as a source of a durable yellow-orange dye extracted from its heartwood.
Fustic dye was a major commodity in international trade from the 16th through 19th centuries, used extensively in textile manufacturing.

The tree also produces dense, durable timber valued for construction, fence posts, and tool handles.
