## Tangie

## Overview

Tangie is a sativa-dominant cannabis strain renowned for its vivid tangerine citrus aroma and uplifting cerebral effects. Created by crossing [[mckenna-cannabis-culture-history]] before spreading to [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]] — contributes potency, yield, and the skunky undertone that balances the sweetness
- **Breeder:** DNA Genetics (Amsterdam-based seed company)
- **Year introduced:** Approximately 2012–2013, gaining prominence after winning the High Times Cannabis Cup
- **Genetic inspiration:** Tangie was developed as a remake of the legendary Tangerine Dream, a phenotype that circulated in Amsterdam coffeeshops during the 1990s and was prized for its exceptional citrus flavor profile

## Terpene Profile

Tangie's terpene profile is dominated by citrus-forward compounds that produce its signature tangerine aroma:

- **[[linalool]]:** Present in smaller quantities, adding floral sweetness and promoting relaxation that tempers the otherwise energetic sativa effects.
- **Ocimene:** Trace amounts add sweet, woody notes and may contribute to the uplifting quality.

## Cannabinoid Content

- **THC content:** 15–22% (median ~17% from tested samples). Potency is considered higher than average for most cannabis strains. Some well-cultivated phenotypes may exceed 22%.
- **CBD content:** Negligible, typically below 0.1% (not a high-CBD strain)
- **CBG content:** ~1% (notable secondary cannabinoid presence)
- **THCV content:** Negligible

## Phenotype and Growing Characteristics

Tangie exhibits classic sativa growth patterns and is moderately challenging to cultivate:

- **Plant structure:** Medium to tall height with vigorous [[cannabis-training-techniques-comparison]] (LST, SCROG) to manage height and maximize light exposure. Medium to heavy feeder during vegetative and early flowering stages.
- **Resin production:** Produces sticky, resin-coated buds with high trichome density, making it suitable for concentrate extraction.

## Effects Profile

Tangie delivers a quintessential sativa experience characterized by cerebral stimulation and physical lightness:

- **Energetic:** The most prominently reported effect. Provides a clear, motivating energy suitable for daytime activities, creative work, and social engagement.
- **Uplifted:** Strong mood elevation with an immediate sense of well-being and positivity. Users consistently report feeling happier and more optimistic.
- **Focused:** Despite the energetic quality, Tangie maintains cognitive clarity and functional focus, making it suitable for task-oriented activities.
- **Creative:** Enhanced divergent thinking and creative inspiration are commonly reported, making this a favorite among artists, writers, and musicians.
- **Euphoric:** A pronounced sense of euphoria accompanies the initial onset, contributing to the strain's popularity as a social enhancer.
- **Talkative:** Social disinhibition and conversational flow are enhanced, making Tangie well-suited for social gatherings.
- **Duration:** Moderate — 2–3 hours for peak effects with a gentle taper.
- **Onset:** Relatively fast, 5–15 minutes after inhalation.
- **Comedown:** Clean and smooth without significant crash or lethargy.
- **Negative effects:** Most commonly reported are dry mouth (~22%) and dry eyes (~11%). Anxiety occurs in approximately 7% of users, typically at higher doses.

## Medical Applications

Tangie's uplifting, clear-headed effects make it particularly well-suited for daytime therapeutic use:

- **Mood elevation:** The most significant medical application. Tangie is widely used to manage symptoms of depression, with patients reporting substantial relief from depressive episodes and improved outlook.
- **Fatigue:** The stimulating, energizing properties make it effective for combating physical and mental fatigue, including conditions involving chronic tiredness.
- **Stress relief:** The euphoric and calming effects help reduce stress and tension without inducing sedation, allowing patients to remain functional.
- **Anxiety:** Some patients find relief from mild to moderate anxiety, though high doses may exacerbate anxiety in sensitive individuals (approximately 7% of users report anxiety as a negative effect).
- **PTSD:** Patients with PTSD report benefits from the mood-stabilizing and grounding effects, with one of the highest effectiveness scores among sativa strains for this condition.
- **ADHD:** The focusing and clarity-promoting effects have made Tangie a choice for patients managing attention-related challenges.
- **Appetite stimulation:** Standard cannabinoid-mediated appetite enhancement useful for conditions involving appetite suppression.

## Awards and Recognition

- **2013 High Times Cannabis Cup** — 1st Place, Sativa category (Amsterdam)
- Tangie's victory at the 2013 Cannabis Cup cemented its reputation and accelerated its spread from Amsterdam coffeeshops to global markets
- Consistently featured in "Best Citrus Strains" and "Best Sativa Strains" lists across cannabis media
- Has served as a parent strain for multiple award-winning hybrids including [[jack-herer]] — Classic sativa-dominant hybrid with comparable creative, cerebral effects
