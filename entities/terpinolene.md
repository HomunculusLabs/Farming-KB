## Chemical Properties

- **IUPAC name:** 1-Methyl-4-(propan-2-ylidene)cyclohex-1-ene.
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₀H₁₆.
- **Molecular weight:** 136.24 g/mol.
- **CAS number:** 586-62-9.
- **Structure:** Bicyclic monoterpene; isomeric with limonene, terpinene,
  and phellandrene. The systematic name reflects the isopropylidene substituent
  at the 4-position of the cyclohexene ring.
- **Physical state:** Colorless to pale yellow liquid at room temperature.
- **Boiling point:** 185–186 °C at 760 mmHg.
- **Density:** 0.862 g/cm³ at 20 °C.
- **Solubility:** Insoluble in water; soluble in ethanol, ether, and fixed oils.
- **Optical rotation:** Varies by source; [α]D ranges from −2° to +8° depending
  on enantiomeric composition.

## Biosynthesis

Terpinolene is biosynthesized via the mevalonate pathway (MVA) in the
cytosol of plant cells, the same pathway producing all monoterpenes. The
direct precursor is geranyl pyrophosphate (GPP), which is cyclized by
terpene synthase enzymes to form the terpinolene skeleton.

In cannabis, the specific terpene synthase gene responsible (CsTPS) has
been identified and characterized. The relatively low abundance of terpinolene
in most cannabis cultivars reflects the low expression levels of this
particular synthase compared to those producing myrcene, limonene, or
caryophyllene.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

Terpinolene contributes a multifaceted sensory profile:

- **Primary notes:** Fresh pine, wood, herbs.
- **Secondary notes:** Floral (lilac-like), slightly fruity (apple, citrus),
  with a faintly sweet quality.
- **Role in cannabis:** Adds complexity and "lift" to the overall aroma.
  Often described as the most complex-smelling of the common [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect]]
  because of its multi-layered character.
- **Industrial use:** Employed in soaps, lotions, perfumes, and as a flavoring
  agent in food products at low concentrations.
