## Overview

The **Soil Foodweb Institute** (Soil Foodweb Inc. and Soil Foodweb Institute Pty. Ltd.) is a soil biology testing and advisory organization founded by Dr. Elaine Ingham. Referenced throughout *The Field Guide for Actively Aerated Compost Tea*.

## Organization Structure

Ingham founded several related entities: Sustainable Studies Institute (Corvallis, Oregon), Soil Foodweb Institute Pty. Ltd. (Australia), and Soil Foodweb Inc. (United States), providing institutional infrastructure for soil food web assessment, compost tea methodology, and biological soil management.

## Services

### Soil Biology Testing
- Direct microscopy analysis of soil, compost, and compost tea
- Organism group quantification (bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes)
- Fungal-to-bacterial ratio assessment
- Mycorrhizal colonization evaluation

### Compost and Tea Assessment
- Biological quality evaluation of compost
- Tea brew optimization through organism counting
- Troubleshooting of brewing problems

### Advisory Services
- Soil biology management recommendations
- Transition planning from conventional to biological farming

## Methodology

The Institute emphasizes **direct microscopy** over plate counts, examining fresh samples to capture the full diversity of soil organisms. This reveals 10-100x more organisms than culturing methods.

## The Soil Food Web Concept

The soil food web comprises: photosynthesizers (plants), decomposers (bacteria, fungi), mutualists (mycorrhizae, nitrogen fixers), predators (protozoa, nematodes), and higher predators (earthworms, arthropods). The health of this food web determines soil fertility, disease resistance, and plant productivity.

## Relevance to Mycology

- Fungal biomass assessment captures organisms invisible to plate counts
- Fungal-dominant compost tea optimization for perennial crops and orchards
- Mycorrhizal monitoring for establishment and function
- Substrate biology assessment for mushroom cultivation
- Disease suppression through soil food web management
