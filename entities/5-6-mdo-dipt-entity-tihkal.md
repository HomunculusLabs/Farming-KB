# 5,6-MDO-DIPT

5,6-Methylenedioxy-N,N-Diisopropyltryptamine

## Chemical Identity

5,6-MDO-DIPT is N,N-diisopropyl-5,6-methylenedioxytryptamine, a synthetic tryptamine carrying both a methylenedioxy bridge across the 5 and 6 positions of the indole ring and diisopropyl substituents on the side chain nitrogen.

The full chemical name is 3-[2-(diisopropylamino)ethyl]-5,6-methylenedioxyindole. The compound is also known as 5H-1,3-dioxolo-[4,5-f]indole-7-ethaneamine, N,N-diisopropyl. The molecular framework combines the methylenedioxy substitution pattern (famous from the phenethylamine series with compounds like MDA and MDMA) with the diisopropyl nitrogen substitution that has proven effective in compounds like [[4-ho-dipt-entity-tihkal]].

The free base crystallizes with a melting point of 93-94 degrees Celsius.

5,6-MDO-DIPT is documented in [[5-6-mdo-dmt-entity-tihkal]] and [[5-6-mdo-mipt-entity-tihkal]] for its preparation). The indole is dissolved in anhydrous diethyl ether and cooled in an external ice bath. Oxalyl chloride in ether is added dropwise over 20 minutes, forming the glyoxyl chloride as red crystals which are collected by filtration, washed with ether, and dried under vacuum.

The crude acid chloride is dissolved in anhydrous tetrahydrofuran, cooled under nitrogen to 0 degrees Celsius, and treated with a diethyl ether solution of diisopropylamine until the reaction mixture remains basic (pH greater than 9 to external pH paper). After removal of solvents under vacuum, the residue is partitioned between water and chloroform, and the organic phase is dried over anhydrous magnesium sulfate.

The residue is recrystallized from ethyl acetate and hexane to yield 5,6-methylenedioxy-N,N-diisopropylglyoxylamide with a melting point of 278-280 degrees Celsius in 38% yield.

The glyoxylamide is reduced with lithium aluminum hydride in dry tetrahydrofuran under reflux for 2 hours. After careful hydrolysis and workup, the residue is distilled by Kugelrohr and the colorless distillate recrystallized from diethyl ether and hexane to give 5,6-MDO-DIPT with a melting point of 93-94 degrees Celsius in 60% yield.

## Related Compounds

5,6-MDO-DIPT is part of a series of three 5,6-methylenedioxy N,N-disubstituted tryptamines:

- [[5-6-mdo-mipt-entity-tihkal]] - the N-methyl-N-isopropyl member
- 5,6-MDO-DIPT - the N,N-diisopropyl member (this entry)

Three additional heterocyclic analogues have been described but not tested in humans:

- 5,6-MDO-pyr-T (pyrrolidine analogue, mp 110-112 degrees Celsius)

- 5,6-MDO-pip-T (piperidine analogue, mp 150-152 degrees Celsius)

- 5,6-MDO-mor-T (morpholine analogue, mp 117-119 degrees Celsius)

None of these three heterocyclic compounds has ever been administered to human subjects.
