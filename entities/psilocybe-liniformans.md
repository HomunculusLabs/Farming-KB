# Psilocybe liniformans

## Taxonomy

- **Order:** Agaricales
- **Family:** Hymenogastraceae (formerly Strophariaceae)
- **Genus:** Psilocybe
- **Species:** P. liniformans
- **Authority:** Guzmán & Bas (1973)
- **Varieties:** P. liniformans var. liniformans and P. liniformans var. americana

## Description

### Cap (Pileus)
- 0.5 to 2 cm in diameter
- Conic to campanulate when young, expanding to convex with age
- Surface smooth, slightly viscid when moist
- Color reddish brown to chestnut brown when hydrated, fading to ochraceous or pale yellowish when dry
- Hygrophanous, with marked color change between wet and dry states
- Margin sometimes slightly translucent striate

### Gills (Lamellae)
- Adnate to slightly adnexed
- Close
- Grayish when young, becoming dark purplish brown to near black as spores mature
- Distinctive gelatinous threads or lines can be observed connecting the gill edges to the stipe in fresh specimens, giving the species its name

### Stipe (Stem)
- 3 to 6 cm long, 1 to 2 mm thick
- Equal to slightly enlarged at the base
- Hollow, fragile
- Surface whitish to pale brown, silky fibrillose
- Bruises bluish, particularly at the base
- Annular zone often present but fragile and easily lost

### Spores
- Spore print dark purple brown to blackish
- Spores broadly ellipsoid to slightly rhomboid
- 9 to 12 × 6 to 7.5 µm in var. liniformans
- Wall slightly thickened, with a distinct germ pore
- Basidia 2 spored and 4 spored, a variable feature

## Distribution and Habitat

- P. liniformans var. liniformans is found in Europe, particularly in the Netherlands, France, and surrounding countries
- P. liniformans var. americana occurs in the Pacific Northwest of [[psilocin]]
- Considered moderately active
- The American variety, P. liniformans var. americana, is reported to have higher concentrations of psychoactive alkaloids
- [[fungal-microscopic-examination-mounting-media-techniques]] reveals the characteristic pleurocystidia and cheilocystidia morphology
- The bluing reaction, while present, may require careful observation as it can be subtle
- Spore morphology, particularly the combination of size and germ pore structure, aids identification

## Ecology

- Saprotrophic, decomposing organic matter in grassland soils
- Benefits from nutrient enrichment from animal activity
- Mycelium colonizes grass roots and surrounding organic material
- Can form mycorrhizal associations in some contexts
- Plays a role in [[panaeolus-subbalteatus]] — shares grassland habitat but belongs to a different genus with different spore morphology
- [[psilocybe-fimetaria]] — found in similar habitats but larger and more robust
