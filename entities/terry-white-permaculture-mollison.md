## Description

Terry White was the editor of the *Permaculture Journal* and a key collaborator with Bill Mollison in the early permaculture movement in Australia. He is notable for co-founding the S.A.L.T. (Salinity Liaison Team) initiative with Mollison in 1978 and for his subsequent leadership in establishing community-based landcare and salinity remediation programmes in Victoria, Australia. White represents the practical, community-organising side of the early permaculture movement, complementing Mollison's design and writing work.

- **Location**: Maryborough, Victoria, Australia

## Key Facts

- Served as editor of the *Permaculture Journal*, a key publication in the early dissemination of permaculture ideas in Australia.
- In 1978, co-invented the acronym S.A.L.T. (Salinity Liaison Team) with Bill Mollison.
- Together with Mollison, travelled from Maryborough, Victoria in an old Volkswagen to convene meetings addressing dryland salinity.
- Convened diverse stakeholder groups including farmers losing their land to salt, churchmen losing congregations, and local government representatives losing rate income.
- Following the initial S.A.L.T. meetings, took the initiative to form strong action teams and raise grants for practical salinity remediation work.
- Led tree-planting campaigns in the Loddon-Campaspe area of Victoria as part of salinity remediation efforts.
- His work preceded and likely influenced the later national Landcare movement in Australia.
- Demonstrated that permaculture principles could be applied at community and landscape scales beyond individual property design.

## The S.A.L.T. Initiative

The Salinity Liaison Team was founded on the premise that dryland salting was caused by tree removal allowing saline groundwater to rise to the surface. The proposed solution was mass tree planting to pump down water tables and restore the freshwater-saltwater balance.

Mollison and White's grassroots approach — bringing together farmers, clergy, and local government — represented an early model of community-based landcare that influenced subsequent Australian land management policy. The initiative began with a road trip from Maryborough in an old Volkswagen, visiting rural communities to raise awareness of salting as an urgent agricultural and social crisis.

The team convened meetings that brought together stakeholders who would not normally interact, creating cross-sector coalitions for practical action. White's particular skill was in forming strong action teams from these diverse groups and raising the grants necessary for on-ground work.

## Impact on Permaculture Movement

Terry White's contributions helped establish several important precedents:

- **Community-scale action**: Demonstrated that permaculture principles could be applied beyond individual property design to landscape-scale community organising.
- **Cross-sector coalition building**: Showed that environmental remediation requires engaging diverse stakeholders — farmers, clergy, government — not just environmentalists.
- **Grant-funded practical work**: Successfully raised funds for on-ground tree planting and salinity remediation, establishing a model for funded permaculture projects.
- **Media and publishing**: Through the *Permaculture Journal*, ensured that practical results and lessons learned were disseminated to the growing permaculture community.

## Significance in Permaculture History

Terry White's contributions illustrate several important aspects of the early permaculture movement:

- **Practical action over theory**: The S.A.L.T. initiative moved permaculture from design philosophy to on-ground community action.
- **Cross-sector collaboration**: White's convening of diverse stakeholders demonstrated the permaculture principle of integrating rather than segregating.
- **Grassroots organising**: The model of community-led salinity teams preceded and likely influenced the later Landcare movement in Australia.
- **Knowledge dissemination**: Through the *Permaculture Journal*, White played a crucial role in distributing practical permaculture information during the movement's formative years.
- **Adaptive management**: The S.A.L.T. team's willingness to revise the groundwater-rising theory in light of Whittington's soil-collapse evidence demonstrated intellectual honesty.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Permaculture

Terry White's work demonstrates that effective permaculture practice extends beyond individual site design to encompass:

- **Community-scale land management**: Addressing landscape-scale problems like salinity requires coordination across property boundaries.
- **Political engagement**: Salinity is fundamentally a land-use and therefore political problem; community organising is essential for systemic change.
- **Information sharing**: Publications like the *Permaculture Journal* are critical infrastructure for spreading design knowledge and practical experience.
- **Funding models**: White's success in raising grants for practical remediation work provides a template for modern permaculture projects seeking institutional support.
