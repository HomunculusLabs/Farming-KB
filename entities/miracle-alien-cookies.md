# Miracle Alien Cookies (MAC)

## Overview

**Miracle Alien Cookies**, commonly abbreviated **MAC**, is a balanced [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain created by breeder Capulator.

It is a cross between **Alien Cookies** (Alien Technology × [[girl-scout-cookies]]) and a **Colombian** landrace, combining the complex cookie [[cannabis-terpene-profile]] with exotic sativa-influenced vigor.

MAC won First Place Hybrid at the 2016 LA Cannabis Cup and has since become one of the most influential breeding stock strains of the [[mycology-modern-era]].

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Alien Cookies (Alien Technology × Girl Scout Cookies)
- **Male parent:** [[oner-colombian-landrace-sativa-strains]] (sativa-dominant)
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 50/50 indica-sativa, though phenotype-dependent
- **Breeder:** Capulator (California)

The Colombian landrace male contributes increased stretch, [[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]], and a unique tropical aroma layer not found in other Cookies-family crosses.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large with a dense, rounded structure typical of the Cookies lineage.

Coloration ranges from deep [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] to light lime, often displaying striking purple and violet hues particularly in cooler growing conditions.

Trichome coverage is exceptionally heavy, giving mature flowers a frosty, silvery-white appearance.

Pistils are abundant, orange to amber, and curl densely through the calyxes.

## Aroma and Flavor

Primary aroma notes include sour citrus, diesel fuel, and floral undertones with a distinctive earthy pungency.

The flavor profile follows the aroma closely, delivering a smooth smoke with sour orange, floral, and slightly spicy cookie-dough notes on the exhale.

The Colombian parent contributes subtle tropical fruit notes that become more apparent in well-cured flowers.

## Terpene Profile

- **Limonene** (dominant): 0.5–1.0% — citrus aroma, mood elevation
- **Caryophyllene**: 0.3–0.6% — peppery, anti-inflammatory
- **Myrcene**: 0.3–0.5% — earthy, sedative base
- **Linalool**: 0.1–0.3% — floral, anxiolytic
- **Humulene**: trace — woody, appetite suppressant

The high limonene content distinguishes MAC from many other Cookies crosses, contributing to its uplifting character.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 20–23% (tested range 18–27%)
- **CBD:** < 0.1%
- **CBG:** 0.1–0.3%

## Effects

Onset is relatively fast (5–10 minutes), beginning with cerebral euphoria and creative stimulation from the sativa-dominant head effects.

Body effects develop gradually, producing a relaxed physical sensation without heavy sedation at moderate doses.

The experience is typically described as balanced and functional, suitable for daytime or early evening use.

Commonly reported applications include stress relief, mild [[cannabis-pain-management-protocols]], mood enhancement, and creative focus.

## Medical Applications

Patients report effectiveness for chronic stress, generalized anxiety, and mild-to-moderate depressive symptoms.

The balanced cannabinoid-terpene profile provides analgesic potential for headaches, joint pain, and muscle tension without heavy sedation.

The limonene-dominant terpene profile may contribute to anxiolytic and antidepressant effects based on preclinical research on citrus-derived monoterpenes.

Some patients use MAC for [[cannabis-for-cachexia-and-appetite-stimulation]] and nausea management, particularly during chemotherapy recovery.
