# Fedco Seeds

## Description

Fedco Seeds is a cooperative seed company specializing in varieties for northern growers and short growing seasons. Located at PO Box 520, Waterville, ME 04703. Contact: (207) 873-7333, website www.fedcoseeds.com. As a Maine-based cooperative, Fedco is uniquely positioned to serve the needs of cold-climate growers in New England and similar northern regions.

## Classification

- **Entity Type:** Company / Cooperative Seed Supplier
- **Industry:** Agriculture, Horticulture, Cooperative Business
- **Location:** Waterville, Maine, USA
- **Specialization:** Northern-adapted varieties, short-season crops, cold-climate growing

## Cooperative Structure

Fedco operates as a consumer and worker cooperative, which influences their business approach:

- **Mission-driven:** Prioritizes the needs of growers over shareholder returns
- **Regional focus:** Concentrates on varieties that perform in northern and short-season climates
- **Affordable pricing:** Cooperative structure allows competitive pricing for member-growers
- **Community-oriented:** Strong connections to the New England farming community

## Products and Services

### Seed Varieties
Fedco's catalog specializes in:

- **Northern-adapted varieties** — Selected and trialed for performance in cold climates
- **Short-season crops** — Varieties that mature quickly enough for northern growing windows
- **Cold-tolerant selections** — Varieties that withstand frost, cold soil, and short days
- **Open-pollinated and heirloom varieties** — Supporting seed saving and genetic diversity
- **Organic seed options** — Certified organic seed lines for organic growers

### Catalog Approach
Fedco's catalog is known for:

- Detailed growing notes specific to northern conditions
- Honest variety assessments based on regional trial results
- Practical advice from experienced northern growers
- Emphasis on varieties that have proven reliable in challenging climates

## Role in Winter Harvest Farming

Fedco Seeds plays an important supporting role in the [[winter-harvest-system]]:

1. **Cold-Climate Genetics:** Their focus on northern-adapted varieties provides germplasm suited to the challenges of winter and early-spring production
2. **Regional Knowledge:** Being based in Maine, their variety recommendations reflect real-world performance in the same climate where Coleman developed his winter harvest techniques
3. **Accessibility:** Their cooperative pricing makes quality seed accessible to smaller operations
4. **Complementary Source:** Along with Johnny's Selected Seeds and [[territorial-seed-company]], Fedco completes a network of seed suppliers that collectively provide comprehensive variety coverage for year-round production

## Relevance to Cultivation

- **Cold-Climate Adaptation:** Fedco's variety selection directly supports the expansion of four-season growing into northern regions
- **Genetic Preservation:** Their focus on open-pollinated varieties helps maintain genetic diversity in commercially available seed
- **Economic Access:** Cooperative pricing lowers the barrier to entry for new growers experimenting with winter harvest techniques
- **Regional Expertise:** Their trialing in Maine conditions provides reliable performance data for growers in similar climates
- **Community Building:** The cooperative model strengthens connections between northern growers and creates a knowledge-sharing network
