# 2C-D (LE-25)

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-methylphenethylamine

**PiHKAL #23** | [[alexander-shulgin]]'s rating: +

## Overview

2C-D is a psychedelic phenethylamine and a member of the [[2c-family-compounds-overview]]. It was one of the earlier compounds explored by [[alexander-shulgin]]. It is known for its relatively mild effects at low doses but can produce a full psychedelic experience at higher doses. It has been described as one of the more "functional" psychedelics due to its clarity of thought at moderate doses.

## Qualitative Effects

- Mild to moderate visual effects at common doses
- Enhanced color perception and pattern recognition
- Clear-headed, "functional" psychedelic experience
- Euphoric and uplifting mood
- Less body load than many other 2C compounds
- At higher doses, full psychedelic experience with eyes-closed imagery
- Some users report aphrodisiac qualities
- Compared favorably to low-dose mescaline-entity|mescaline

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

Shulgin noted that 2C-D is one of the longer-lived 2C compounds. At 60 mg, the experience is described as being comparable to approximately 300-350 mg of mescaline. Reports mention good humor, appreciation of music, and pleasant eyes-closed imagery. The compound is described as "benign" and suitable for repeat experiences.

### Selected Experience Reports

At 10 mg, a subject reported: "There is something going on, but it is subtle. I find that I can just slightly redirect my attention so that it applies more exactly to what I am doing. I feel that I can learn faster. This is a 'smart' pill!"

At 150 mg (from German therapeutic research, where 2C-D is called LE-25): "A truly remarkable psychedelic, one which could compare favorably with 2c-b-entity|2C-B. There are intense colors, and I feel that more would be too much."

## Extensions and Commentary

2C-D was the first of the 4-alkyl substituted 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamines to be systematically explored. It served as a template for understanding the effects of alkyl chain length at the 4-position. The ethoxy homologues (2CD-2ETO, 2CD-5ETO) were also explored, showing that 2-ethoxy substitution shortens duration while 5-ethoxy increases it without changing potency significantly.

The compound code "LE-25" refers to its early research designation. Despite its relatively mild reputation, 2C-D can produce powerful experiences at higher doses, and Shulgin considered it an underappreciated compound.

### "Pharmacological Tofu"

Shulgin called 2C-D a "pharmacological tofu" — it doesn't seem to do too much by itself, always teasing, until you get to heroic levels. However, a goodly number of experimental therapists have said that it is excellent in extending the action of some other materials. It seems to boost the waning action of another drug, without adding its own color to the experience.

### Potency Loss from Amphetamine to Phenethylamine

2C-D represents perhaps the most dramatic example of the loss of potency from an amphetamine to a phenethylamine. Its amphetamine homologue dom-entity|DOM is active at roughly 3 milligrams, while 2C-D is only about one-tenth as active. As Shulgin noted, the first of a series is not always the most interesting nor the most potent member — the next higher homologue, 2c-e-entity|2C-E, is maximally active at the 15–20 milligram level and is, without any question, a complete psychedelic.

### N-Substituted Homologues

The N-monomethyl and N,N-dimethyl homologues of 2C-D have been synthesized. The N-monomethyl compound was obtained by quaternization of the Schiff's base formed between 2C-D and benzaldehyde with methyl sulfate, followed by hydrolysis (HCl salt, mp 150–151 °C from EtOH). The N,N-dimethyl compound resulted from the action of formaldehyde-formic acid on 2C-D (HCl salt, mp 168–169 °C from EtOH/ether). These two compounds were some ten times less effective in interfering with conditioned responses in experimental rats, and there is no report of their having been explored in man.

### The Tweetio Homologues

Shulgin described an extensive study of ethoxy homologues of compounds in the 2C-X series, collectively called the "Tweetios" (a Sylvester and Tweety-bird allusion arising from pronouncing license plate codes like "2-EtO" as "Tweetio"). In general, the 2-EtO- compounds have shorter duration and lower potency, the 5-EtO- compounds have relatively unchanged potency and longer duration, and the 2,5-di-EtO- homologues are very weak, if active at all.

## Safety Warnings

- Higher doses (80-100+ mg) can produce intense experiences that may be overwhelming
- Long duration at higher doses should be anticipated
- See [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Scheduled in many countries
