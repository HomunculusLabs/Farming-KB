# Petroleum Ether

## Description

Petroleum ether (also called ligroin, petroleum spirit, or naphtha) is a light, volatile hydrocarbon solvent mixture consisting primarily of alkanes (pentane, hexane, and heptane). It has a boiling range of approximately 30–60°C and is widely used as a selective extraction solvent in [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-smartshop-and-retail-outlets]].

## Safety Considerations

- Extremely flammable — flash point below -40°C. All work must be performed away from ignition sources in well-ventilated areas or under fume hoods.
- Vapors are heavier than air and can travel along surfaces to reach distant ignition sources (flashback risk).
- Inhalation of vapors causes dizziness, headache, and respiratory irritation. Chronic exposure may cause neurological effects.
- Proper solvent recovery through distillation is both an economic and safety necessity — the collected pure solvent can be reused for future extractions.
- Fire suppression equipment (water-soaked blankets, CO2 extinguishers) should be immediately available during any petroleum ether work.

## Extraction Protocol (Cannabis Context)

1. Dissolve crude cannabis oil in five times its weight of alcohol.
2. Pour into equal volume of cold water in a glass jug.
3. Add petroleum ether equal to half the water volume.
4. Invert jug 25 times, releasing pressure each time.
5. Allow layers to separate for 30 minutes.
6. Three layers form: bottom (water/alcohol/tars), middle (wax emulsion), top (ether/oil).
7. Siphon off the top ether/oil layer.
8. Repeat with fresh petroleum ether until ether layer remains clear.
9. Evaporate ether at ≤140°F and collect for reuse.
10. The purified oil is significantly more potent than the crude extract.
