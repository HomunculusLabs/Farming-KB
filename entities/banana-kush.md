## Banana Kush
## Overview
Banana Kush is an indica-dominant [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]. Ghost OG contributes the strain's body-
  heavy relaxation, couch-lock potential, and stress-relieving properties.
- **Skunk Haze** — a sativa-leaning hybrid descended from classic Skunk #1 and
  Haze genetics. Skunk Haze introduces tropical fruit terpenes, mild cerebral
  stimulation, and an extended flowering period characteristic of Haze lines.
The interplay between these parents produces a versatile hybrid that can express
a range of effects depending on cultivation conditions and phenotype expression.
## Appearance and Aroma
Banana Kush produces dense, resinous buds with a striking yellow-green coloration
accented by vibrant orange pistils. A thick coating of trichomes gives the flowers
a frosty, sticky appearance. The aroma is the strain's signature trait:
- **Nose:** Ripe banana, tropical fruit, and sweet vanilla with earthy undertones
- **Flavor:** Smooth banana flavor on the inhale, transitioning to herbal and
  skunky notes on the exhale with hints of citrus and spice
- **Smoke:** Thick, expansive, and moderately smooth; recommended for dry herb
  vaporization to fully appreciate the terpene complexity

## Effects and Medical Applications
### Recreational Effects
- Euphoric onset with gentle cerebral stimulation
- Progressive body relaxation and tension release
- Increased sociability in low-to-moderate doses
- Couch-lock and sedation at higher doses
- Heightened [[cannabis-for-chronic-pain]], muscle spasms, and
  inflammation; caryophyllene contributes via CB2 receptor activity
- **Insomnia:** Heavy indica-dominant phenotypes promote sleep onset and
  duration in moderate-to-high doses
- **[[cannabis-training-techniques-comparison]] (LST, SCROG)
- **Flowering Time:** 8–9 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors; 500–700 g/plant outdoor)
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean-like conditions; sensitive to cold
  and excessive humidity
- **Nutrients:** Moderate feeder; watch for [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]]
