# Banana Kush
## Overview
Banana Kush is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain renowned for its distinctive
tropical banana aroma and deeply relaxing effects. First developed in California during
the late 2000s, it has become a staple among both recreational consumers and medical
cannabis patients. The strain typically delivers a THC content of 18–25%, making it
a potent choice for experienced users. Its effects begin with a mild cerebral uplift
before settling into a full-body calm that can range from soothing relaxation to deep
sedation depending on the phenotype.
## Lineage and Genetics
Banana Kush is the result of crossing **Ghost OG** with **Skunk Haze**, combining two
lineages with complementary qualities:
- **Ghost OG** — a potent indica-dominant cut known for its heavy sedative effects
  and earthy, pine-forward terpene profile. Ghost OG contributes the strain's body-
  heavy relaxation, couch-lock potential, and stress-relieving properties.
- **Skunk Haze** — a sativa-leaning hybrid descended from classic Skunk #1 and
  Haze genetics. Skunk Haze introduces tropical fruit terpenes, mild cerebral
  stimulation, and an extended flowering period characteristic of Haze lines.
The interplay between these parents produces a versatile hybrid that can express
a range of effects depending on cultivation conditions and phenotype expression.
## Appearance and Aroma
Banana Kush produces dense, resinous buds with a striking yellow-green coloration
accented by vibrant orange pistils. A thick coating of trichomes gives the flowers
a frosty, sticky appearance. The aroma is the strain's signature trait:
- **Nose:** Ripe banana, tropical fruit, and sweet vanilla with earthy undertones
- **Flavor:** Smooth banana flavor on the inhale, transitioning to herbal and
  skunky notes on the exhale with hints of citrus and spice
- **Smoke:** Thick, expansive, and moderately smooth; recommended for dry herb
  vaporization to fully appreciate the terpene complexity

## Effects and Medical Applications
### Recreational Effects
- Euphoric onset with gentle cerebral stimulation
- Progressive body relaxation and tension release
- Increased sociability in low-to-moderate doses
- Couch-lock and sedation at higher doses
- Heightened sensory perception (taste, touch, sound)
### Medical Applications
- **Stress and Anxiety:** Myrcene and linalool combine to produce pronounced
  anxiolytic and calming effects
- **Pain Management:** Effective for chronic pain, muscle spasms, and
  inflammation; caryophyllene contributes via CB2 receptor activity
- **Insomnia:** Heavy indica-dominant phenotypes promote sleep onset and
  duration in moderate-to-high doses
- **Appetite Stimulation:** Reliable munchies effect useful for patients
  undergoing chemotherapy or dealing with eating disorders
- **Mood Disorders:** Uplifting limonene content can ease symptoms of mild
  depression and PTSD-related stress
## Cultivation Notes
Banana Kush is moderately challenging to grow, best suited for cultivators with
some prior experience. Key parameters:
- **Height:** Medium; responds well to training techniques (LST, SCROG)
- **Flowering Time:** 8–9 weeks indoors
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors; 500–700 g/plant outdoor)
- **Climate:** Prefers warm, Mediterranean-like conditions; sensitive to cold
  and excessive humidity
- **Nutrients:** Moderate feeder; watch for nitrogen toxicity in early veg;
  boost phosphorus and potassium during mid-flower for terpene development
- **Pest Resistance:** Moderate; dense bud structure requires good airflow to
  prevent bud rot and powdery mildew

## Popular Phenotypes
Multiple phenotypes of Banana Kush circulate in the market, expressing variation
in both morphology and effect profile:
- **Indica-Heavy Phenotype (Banana OG lean):** Shorter, bushier plants with
  dense, compact buds. Effects are predominantly physical — strong body lock,
  sedation, and sleepiness. Preferred by medical patients seeking pain relief
  and insomnia management. Most common phenotype in commercial cultivation.
- **Balanced Hybrid Phenotype:** More elongated bud structure with noticeable
  Haze-influenced growth patterns. Effects begin with a clearer cerebral
  uplift before transitioning to body relaxation. Retains more of the tropical
  fruit terpene expression. Popular among recreational users.
- **Sativa-Lean Phenotype (rare):** Taller plants with longer internodal spacing.
  Higher limonene and pinene content. Mental clarity and creative stimulation
  more pronounced; body effects lighter. Less commonly found due to commercial
  preference for indica expression.
## Cultural Significance
Banana Kush emerged during a transformative period in California's cannabis culture,
when legacy breeders began systematically crossing classic indica and sativa lines
to create new flavor profiles. Its distinctive banana aroma made it one of the
first widely recognized "fruit-forward" strains, predating the explosion of
flavor-focused breeding that would define the 2010s cannabis market. The strain
has maintained consistent popularity for over a decade, appearing on dispensary
menus across legal markets in North America and Europe. It has also served as
a parent or genetic contributor to several newer strains in the tropical-
flavored category.
