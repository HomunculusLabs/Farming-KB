## Uses and Applications

Nearly every part of the moringa tree has practical applications.
Leaves are consumed fresh, dried as a powder, or cooked as a
vegetable. Young seed pods are eaten as a vegetable similar to
green beans. Seeds can be pressed for oil (ben oil) used in
cooking, cosmetics, and as a lubricant. Crushed seeds are used
as a natural coagulant for water purification, effectively
removing turbidity and bacteria from contaminated water.
