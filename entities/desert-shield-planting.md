## Desert Shield Planting

## Description

Desert shield planting is a plant establishment technique using bottomless and
topless plastic bags mounted on stake frames around small trees to create
protected microclimates. The shielded environment reduces wind, holds mulch,
protects from small animals, and condenses soil moisture at night, directing it
down the inner bag surface to plant roots. Mollison reports 80-90% improvement
in plant survival using shields versus unprotected plantings.

## Classification

- **Category**: Plant establishment technique
- **Domain**: [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-dryland-strategies-and-desert-agriculture]], arid-land reforestation, [[windbreak-establishment-and-benefits]]
- **Technology level**: Low-tech
- **Materials**: Plastic bags, stakes, loose weeds/mulch

## System Design

### Basic Configuration
- 3 or 4 stakes driven into ground around small tree
- Bottomless and topless bag pulled over the stake frame
- Loose weeds placed around the base inside the shield
- Creates an enclosed protected column around the young plant

### Coastal Variant
- Fine metal screens or fences placed crosswind on coastal ridges
- These condense water from night air flowing inland from the sea
- Passive water harvesting requiring no energy input

## Functions Provided

### Wind Protection
Eliminates direct wind on the young plant, dramatically reducing desiccation
and physical damage from sandblast and wind shear.

### Moisture Condensation
At night, soil moisture condenses on the cooler inner surface of the bag and
runs down to plant roots. Creates a self-watering micro-environment.

### Mulch Retention
Loose weeds placed inside the shield remain in place rather than being blown
away, providing ongoing soil cover [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]].

### Animal Exclusion
The physical barrier protects young plants from small browsing animals.

### Growth Enhancement
Growth (as measured by light interception) may double in tall clear plastic
bag tubes around small trees compared to unshielded controls.

## Performance Data

- 80-90% improvement in survival rate versus unprotected plants
- [[soil-temperature-plant-growth-rate-doubling-rule]] may double in tall clear plastic tube shields
- Effective across multiple arid and semi-arid environments
- Low material cost — plastic bags and simple stakes

## Key Facts

- Materials: plastic bags, 3-4 stakes, loose weeds
- 80-90% survival improvement documented by Mollison
- Growth doubling reported in clear plastic tubes
- Passive condensation harvesting requires no energy
- Applicable to individual trees and coastal ridge installations
- Can be removed once plants are established

## Relevance to Cultivation

Critical technique for establishing the windbreaks, food trees, [[dryland-settlement-vegetation-and-fuel-forests]]
that make [[arid-landscape-scarp-wadi-desert-settlement]] possible. Extremely low cost and high effectiveness
make it accessible to the poorest communities facing [[permaculture]] principle of using biological and simple physical solutions to
create favorable microclimates for plant growth.
