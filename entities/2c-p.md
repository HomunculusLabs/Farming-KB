The compound is distinguished among the 2C series by its exceptionally long duration of action — typically 10–16 hours — and its high potency, with active doses ranging from 6–12 mg. These characteristics make 2C-P one of the longest-lasting and most potent members of the 2C family.

2C-P is a Schedule I controlled substance in the [[phenethylamine-chain-length-effects]] and pharmacokinetic profile is a recurring theme in the 2C series.

## Chemical Properties

**Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylphenethylamine.

**Molecular formula:** C₁₂H₁₉NO₂.

**Molecular weight:** 209.29 g/mol.

**IUPAC name:** 1-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylphenyl)ethan-2-amine.

2C-P exists as a freebase oil at room temperature and is commonly stored and distributed as a hydrochloride salt (C₁₂H₂₀ClNO₂, MW 245.75 g/mol), which is a white crystalline powder. The freebase is sparingly soluble in water but soluble in organic solvents including ethanol, DMSO, and chloroform.

The compound belongs to the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamine class, which is structurally related to the classical psychedelics mescaline (3,4,5-trimethoxyphenethylamine) and the 2C [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]], the same receptor responsible for the psychedelic effects of LSD, psilocin, and mescaline. The 2,5-dimethoxy substitution pattern is well-established as the key pharmacophore for high-affinity 5-HT₂A binding.

Functional assays demonstrate that 2C-P is a full or near-full agonist at 5-HT₂A, with moderate affinity for 5-HT₂C. Activation of cortical 5-HT₂A receptors in layer V pyramidal neurons is the primary mechanism underlying the compound's psychedelic effects, including visual hallucinations, altered cognition, and synesthesia.

The propyl chain at the 4-position increases lipophilicity compared to shorter-chain analogues, which contributes to both increased central nervous system penetration and slower metabolic clearance. This pharmacokinetic property explains the extended duration of action relative to 2C-B and 2C-I.

Metabolism is presumed to occur primarily via hepatic oxidative deamination by monoamine oxidase (MAO-A), followed by O-demethylation by cytochrome P450 enzymes, particularly CYP2D6. The extended duration suggests a slow rate of metabolic clearance and possibly active metabolites.

Limited [[solomon-chemical-vs-organic-fertilizer-slow-release-trace-minerals]], as has been proposed for other long-acting lipophilic phenethylamines. This pharmacokinetic mechanism would explain why blood levels remain above the psychoactive threshold for 10+ hours after a single oral dose.

## Effects

The subjective effects of 2C-P are broadly similar to those of other classical psychedelics but are distinguished by their intensity, duration, and the quality of the visual experience. Onset is slow, typically 2–4 hours, which has led some users to mistakenly redose before the effects have fully manifested.

Visual effects are richly colored, highly detailed, and often described as more "organic" and flowing compared to the geometric patterns associated with 2C-B or 2C-E. Open-eye visuals include color enhancement, pattern recognition, fractal imagery, and at higher doses, immersive hallucinatory scenarios.

Cognitive effects include enhanced introspection, emotional openness, altered sense of time, and dissolution of ego boundaries. The extended duration means users may experience multiple distinct phases: an ascending phase, a prolonged plateau, and a gradual comedown that can itself last several hours.

Physical effects include pupil dilation, increased heart rate, elevated blood pressure, mild nausea (especially during onset), and increased body temperature. These somatic effects are generally manageable at common doses but become more pronounced and uncomfortable at higher doses.

The combination of high potency, long duration, and slow onset creates a significant dose-response hazard. Users unfamiliar with these pharmacokinetic characteristics are at elevated risk of anxiety, panic, and adverse physical reactions from taking excessive doses.

Emotional effects can be intensely cathartic, with some users reporting profound personal insights and emotional release. However, the extended duration means that difficult psychological material may surface and persist for many hours without the option of "waiting it out" in the manner possible with shorter-acting psychedelics.

Reports from Shulgin's original experiments describe 2C-P as having a "contemplative" quality, with less of the stimulant push associated with 2C-E or 2C-I. The experience tends toward a more internal, meditative character rather than the extroverted, visually-driven quality of shorter-duration 2C compounds.

### Comparison with Other 2C Compounds

Compared to 2C-B, 2C-P is approximately 10–20× more potent by weight and lasts 2–3× longer. While 2C-B is often described as playful and sensual, 2C-P tends toward the serious and introspective, with visuals that are more immersive and less controllable.

Relative to 2C-E, 2C-P produces comparable visual intensity but with a distinctly slower onset and more drawn-out timeline. Users frequently report that 2C-E has a sharper, more analytical quality, while 2C-P is more fluid and dreamlike.

## Dosage and Duration

**Threshold:** 3–5 mg. **Light:** 6–8 mg. **Common:** 8–10 mg. **Strong:** 10–14 mg. **Heavy:** 14+ mg.

Doses above 14 mg are strongly discouraged due to the risk of overwhelming [[hallucinogenic-mushroom-acute-psychological-effects-and-bad-trips]] and physical distress. The compound's slow onset (2–4 hours) means that effects may not be apparent for a considerable time after ingestion, leading to the common and dangerous error of taking additional doses prematurely.
