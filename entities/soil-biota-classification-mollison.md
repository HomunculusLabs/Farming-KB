## Classification Categories

Mollison categorized soil biota by size and function: microflora (bacteria,
fungi, actinomycetes), microfauna (protozoa, nematodes), mesofauna
(springtails, mites), and macrofauna (earthworms, beetles, ants). Each group
plays distinct roles in nutrient cycling and soil structure maintenance.

## Functional Roles

Bacteria dominate rapid nutrient cycling, processing simple organic compounds.
Fungi decompose complex materials like lignin and cellulose. Earthworms
create macropores that improve aeration and water infiltration. Each trophic
level supports the next, building soil fertility from the bottom up.
