## Description

WA S.A.L.T. (Whittington Interceptor Salt-Affected Land Treatment Society) is a farmer-led organisation founded in March 1978 in Western Australia to combat [[harry-whittington-mollison]] of Brookton, Western Australia, who developed the "[[interceptor-banks]] correctly, with specific protocols for size, spacing, grading, and construction.

## Core Theory: Soil Collapse Explanation

Whittington"s theory, which Mollison credits as convincing after extensive field inspection, differs from the standard "groundwater rising" theory of dryland salting:

1. **Primary cause**: Clearing, cultivation, and hoofed animal compaction destroy soil structure.
2. **Chemical cascade**: Sodium from surface salt deposits displaces calcium on clay particles, causing deflocculation.
3. **Physical consequence**: Collapsed clay particles migrate and cement the subsoil into a hydrophobic hardpan.
4. **Hydrological result**: Surface soil is sealed, preventing deep infiltration. Overland water flow increases.
5. **Self-reinforcement**: Each cycle worsens the condition (cascade effect).

## Interceptor Bank System

The practical remediation developed by WA S.A.L.T. involves carefully engineered interceptor banks:

- **Modified bulldozers**: Machines are modified to create a 1-metre blade drop for delving ditches and ramming banks to seal them.
- **Precision grading**: Correct size, spacing, and grade are critical — improper construction can worsen the problem.
- **Training requirement**: Farmers and contractors must undergo training with WA S.A.L.T. personnel before building interceptor banks.
- **Proven results**: Mollison inspected "miles of canals and ditches" and found the land therapy effective.

## Relevance to Permaculture

- **Farmer-led science**: WA S.A.L.T. demonstrates that practical, observation-based knowledge from working farmers can challenge and improve upon academic theories.
- **Systems thinking**: The [[soil-collapse]] explanation integrates chemical, physical, and hydrological processes — a genuinely holistic understanding of landscape function.
- **Mollison"s endorsement**: Mollison states that Whittington is "yet another unrecognised "great Australian' with a love of land and good husbandry" and that WA S.A.L.T."s efforts are "worthy of international recognition."
- **Design implication**: The interceptor bank approach shows that successful dryland restoration requires precise earthworks design, not just tree planting.
