## Capsicum annuum (Chili Pepper)

## Overview

**Capsicum annuum** is a flowering plant species in the nightshade family Solanaceae, native to the Americas, and one of the most widely cultivated spice and vegetable crops globally.

The species encompasses an enormous diversity of cultivars, ranging from sweet bell peppers (0 SHU) to extremely hot [[candida-albicans]]* is also documented. These properties contributed to traditional use as food preservatives.

### Cardiovascular Benefits
Regular consumption is associated with reduced platelet aggregation, improved endothelial function, and lower blood pressure.

⚠️ **Safety:** Excessive topical capsaicin can cause severe burning and skin irritation. Oral overdose (>50 g dried chili) may cause gastrointestinal distress. Not recommended for individuals with active peptic ulcers or GERD at high doses.

## Cultivation

**Propagation:** Seeds sown indoors 6-8 weeks before last frost. Germination: 7-21 days at 25-30°C.
**Growing conditions:** Full sun (6-8+ hours), well-drained fertile soil, pH 6.0-6.8. Optimal temperature: 21-29°C. Below 15°C or above 35°C reduces fruit set.
**Nutrient needs:** Moderate to heavy feeder. Calcium prevents blossom-end rot.
**Spacing:** 30-60 cm between plants depending on cultivar.
**Harvest:** 60-90 days from transplanting. Harvest green or allow full ripening (sweeter, higher carotenoids).
**Yield:** 1-3 kg per plant depending on cultivar and conditions.
**Pests:** Aphids, flea beetles, pepper weevils. Companion with [[cervantes-spider-mites-identification-control]].
- **Marigolds:** Repel nematodes in soil.
- **Tomatoes:** Share similar growing requirements (but avoid planting too close to reduce competition).
- **Carrots and onions:** Good understory companions that occupy different root zones.

**Permaculture functions:**

- **Food production:** High-calorie, high-nutrient crop for food forests and kitchen gardens.
- **Medicinal plant:** On-site source of capsaicin for topical pain relief preparations.
- **Pest deterrent:** Capsaicin-based sprays as natural insect repellent for other crops.
- **Edge planting:** Thrives in sunny edge zones between garden areas.

**Notable Cultivar Groups:**
- **Bell peppers** (Grossum group): Sweet, blocky, 0 SHU. Multiple color varieties.
- **Jalapeño:** Medium heat (2,500-8,000 SHU), Mexican cuisine staple.
- **Cayenne:** Hot (30,000-50,000 SHU), slender, dried and ground.
- **Anaheim/New Mexico:** Mild (500-2,500 SHU), large, used for chiles rellenos.
- **Poblano/Ancho:** Mild (1,000-2,000 SHU), heart-shaped; dried form is ancho.
- **Thai bird's eye:** Very hot (50,000-100,000 SHU), small, Southeast Asian cuisine.
- **Serrano:** Hot (10,000-23,000 SHU), smaller than jalapeño, used fresh.
