# gamma-DOM

gamma-DOM (Z-7; 2,6-Dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine) is a psychedelic amphetamine representing the 2,4,6-trisubstitution pattern.

It is documented as entry #69 in PiHKAL by [[dom-entity-pihkal]]|DOM (2,4,5-dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine, also known as STP).

The compound was code-named Z-7 after the Pennzoil oil additive, continuing the petroleum-product naming convention of the DOM series.

Shulgin also referred to it as "pseudo-DOM" using the Greek letter psi, since the 2,4,5-ness of dom-entity|DOM has become a 2,4,6-ness.

The hydrochloride salt has a sharp melting point of 203 degrees Celsius.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 15 - 25 mg.

Duration of effects: 6 - 8 hours.

The potency is notably lower than dom-entity|DOM (which is active at 3-10 mg) but the compound is clearly active.

Effects include a light-headed, starry-eyed stoned state at 18 mg and a full +++ at 25 mg.

## Qualitative Effects

At 14 mg, users report being "really quite spacey" with thoughts that go "straight up into thin air."

The compound is described as an "interpretive drug, not simply an ASC [altered state of consciousness] opening."
