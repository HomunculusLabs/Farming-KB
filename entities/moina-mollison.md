## Moina

## Description

Moina is a genus of small freshwater cladoceran crustaceans (water fleas) that serves as important zooplankton in facultative and aerobic sewage treatment lagoons. Alongside other cladocerans such as Daphnia, Chydorus, and Pleuroxus, Moina feeds on phytoplankton and bacteria, contributing to the biological purification of wastewater through [[nutrient-uptake-and-membrane-transport-in-plants]] biomass conversion.

## Classification

- **Kingdom**: Animalia
- **Phylum**: Arthropoda
- **Class**: Branchiopoda
- **Order**: Anomopoda (formerly Cladocera)
- **Family**: Moinidae
- **Genus**: Moina

## Key Facts

- Moina species are among the smallest cladocerans, typically 0.5-1.5 mm in length.
- They are filter feeders, consuming bacteria, phytoplankton (especially Euglena, Chlamydomonas, Chlorella, and Scenedesmus), and fine organic detritus from the water column.
- In Mollison's description of sewage lagoon ecology, Moina is listed alongside Daphnia, Chydorus, and Pleuroxus as key cladoceran zooplankton in facultative and aerobic ponds.
- Cladocerans including Moina accumulate heavy metals from wastewater: iron (1,200 mg/kg), zinc (152 mg/kg), copper (37 mg/kg), chromium (28 mg/kg), nickel (12.2 mg/kg), lead (103 mg/kg), and cadmium (1.7 mg/kg) in dry weight.
- Zooplankton blooms in productive sewage lagoons can reach densities of 100 mg/l.
- Harvested zooplankton containing Moina can be processed into high-protein poultry feed or used as field manure and trace element amendments.
- Moina is widely cultured as live food for fish fry in aquaculture due to its small size and rapid reproduction.

## Role in Sewage Lagoon Ecology

Moina occupies a middle trophic level in the sewage treatment food web:

1. **Primary producers**: Algae (phytoplankton) fix carbon and release oxygen.
2. **Primary consumers**: Moina and other cladocerans consume algae and bacteria.
3. **Secondary consumers**: Insect larvae, copepods (Mesocyclops), and fish consume zooplankton.
4. **Tertiary consumers**: Waterfowl and larger fish feed on smaller fish and invertebrates.

This food web progressively removes nutrients, metals, and organic matter from the water column.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Aquaculture**: Moina is a premier live food organism for fish larviculture, making sewage treatment ponds dual-purpose water purification and fish production systems.
- **Nutrient recovery**: By concentrating dissolved nutrients and metals into harvestable biomass, Moina enables nutrient recovery from waste streams.
