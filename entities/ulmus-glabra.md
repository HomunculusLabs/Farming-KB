## Ulmus glabra

## Overview

**Ulmus glabra**, the wych elm or Scots elm, is a large deciduous tree native to Europe. It is the most cold-hardy of the European elms, ranging from Ireland to the Urals and north to Scandinavia.

The species name *glabra* means "smooth," referring to the upper surface of its leaves. Wych elm is distinguished from English elm (*U. minor*) by its larger leaves and lack of root suckering.

It can reach heights of 25–30 m with a broad, domed crown. The trunk diameter may exceed 2 m in mature specimens. It is a significant component of mixed deciduous forests across its range.

## Taxonomy

- **Family:** Ulmaceae
