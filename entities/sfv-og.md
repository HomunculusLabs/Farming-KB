## Overview

SFV OG, short for San Fernando Valley OG, is an indica-dominant [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].

The strain is highly regarded among cannabis enthusiasts for its deep body
relaxation and stress-relieving properties. It has become a staple in Southern
California dispensaries and has served as a parent strain for numerous crosses,
contributing its characteristic OG terpene expression to subsequent generations.

SFV OG typically tests between 18–24% THC with minimal CBD content (<0.1%).
Its effects are predominantly physical, making it popular among medical patients
seeking relief from pain, insomnia, and anxiety.

## Lineage

SFV OG is a [[hamilton-vegetative-propagation]].

**Notable descendants:** SFV OG has been used extensively as a breeding parent.
Notable crosses include SFV OG Kush x [[blackberry-kush]].

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large in size with a dense, chunky structure typical of
indica-dominant varieties. Calyxes are tightly packed and covered in a thick
layer of trichomes that gives the flowers a frosted, almost white appearance
under magnification.

Coloration ranges from bright to forest green with occasional yellowish-green
leaves. Pistils are orange to copper-colored and relatively sparse compared
to the dense [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] coverage. The overall visual impression is of
a classic OG Kush phenotype: compact, resinous, and heavy in the hand.

The flowers produce a sticky, resinous coating that clings to surfaces and
grinders, indicating high terpene and cannabinoid content.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Aroma:** Intense and complex, dominated by an earthy, pine-forward profile
with prominent notes of lemon and fuel. The scent is immediately recognizable
as classic OG Kush — pungent skunk and diesel undertones with a bright citrus
high note. Breaking apart the buds releases an amplified musky, woody aroma.

**Flavor:** On inhalation, the flavor is smooth and earthy with pine and
citrus notes. The exhalation reveals deeper flavors of damp earth, hashish,
and a lingering diesel-fuel quality. A subtle lemon sweetness persists on the
palate after exhaling.

The overall flavor profile is less harsh than some OG varieties, with a
smoother smoke that still delivers the characteristic Kush depth.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**

- Myrcene (0.8–1.2%): Sedative, musky-earthy base notes. Primary driver of
the strain's physical relaxation effects.
