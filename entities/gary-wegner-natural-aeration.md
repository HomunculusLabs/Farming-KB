# Gary Wegner

## Description

Gary Wegner of Natural Aeration is a practitioner who has worked with Dr. [[elaine-ingham]] on restoring soil biology in dairy farm environments. His work focuses on demonstrating that correcting environmental conditions to support beneficial biology results in disease problems naturally resolving without chemical intervention.

## Classification

- **Role:** Agricultural consultant, practitioner
- **Organization:** Natural Aeration
- **Field:** Soil biological restoration, dairy farm management

## Key Facts

- Operates under the business name Natural Aeration
- Has collaborated with Dr. Elaine Ingham for an extended period on dairy soil restoration
- Works specifically with dairy soils, manure lagoons, and barn floor systems
- Has repeatedly demonstrated that when soil conditions are corrected to support beneficial biology, disease problems including pathogenic Streptococcus species simply disappear
- His approach focuses on the root cause rather than treating symptoms with chemicals or antibiotics

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

Wegner's work provides real-world validation of [[soil-food-web]] approach to disease management. His success with dairy farm soils demonstrates that biological restoration is achievable even in severely degraded systems. The key lesson is that disease problems are symptoms of imbalanced soil biology. Rather than attempting to kill specific pathogens, the approach should be to create conditions that favor beneficial organisms.

## Approach

1. Soil biology assessment through direct microscopy
2. Identifying which organism groups are missing or deficient
3. Applying appropriate biology ([[compost]], compost tea, inocula)
4. Providing food resources to sustain the introduced organisms
5. Correcting physical conditions (aeration, drainage, compaction)
6. Monitoring biology over time to ensure establishment
