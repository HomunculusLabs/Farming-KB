## Taxonomy and Nomenclature

*Hypholoma popperianum* was first described by the German-born mycologist Rolf Singer in 1973,
originally as *Psilocybe popperiana*. Singer, one of the most prolific taxonomists of
Agaricales in the 20th century, described numerous Psilocybe species from tropical
America during his extensive fieldwork. The species was later elevated to *Hypholoma
popperianum* in subsequent taxonomic revisions by Gastón Guzmán and other mycologists who
recognized its morphological affinity with the genus Hypholoma.
