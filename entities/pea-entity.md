# PEA

PEA (Phenethylamine) is the simplest member of the phenethylamine family and serves as the structural parent of all compounds discussed in PiHKAL.

It is documented as entry #138 in PiHKAL by Alexander Shulgin.

PEA occurs naturally in the human brain and throughout the plant and animal kingdoms.

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: Phenethylamine (2-Phenylethylamine).

Molecular formula: C8H11N.

PEA is the simplest structure that can be called a phenethylamine: a benzene ring connected to a two-carbon chain bearing an amine group.

Every compound in PiHKAL is a substituted derivative of this fundamental structure.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 217-218 degrees Celsius with decomposition.

The free base is a colorless liquid with a boiling point of 197-198 degrees Celsius.
