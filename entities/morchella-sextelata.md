**Mycorrhizal component:** Root-tip analysis suggests facultative
association with conifer root systems. The post-fire nutrient flush
creates a temporary permissive window for fruiting.

**:** Productivity correlates with fire severity, soil
heating depth, and pre-fire forest type. High-severity burns with
substantial duff consumption produce the largest fruitings.

## Cultivation

Commercial cultivation was pioneered in China and adapted for western
strains:

**Substrate:** Two-phase system with cereal  (rye/wheat)
and nutritionally supplemented soil casing (peat, loam, calcium
carbonate, gypsum).

**Parameters:**
- Spawn run: 22–25°C; cold shock at 4°C for 48–72h, then 10–18°C
- Humidity: 90–95% primordia, 85–90% fruiting
- Light: Low ambient (50–200 lux); air exchange critical

**Yield:** 150–350 g fresh/kg substrate over 4–6 weeks. Multiple
flushes possible.

## Culinary Value

*M. sextelata* is a choice edible — rich, earthy, nutty, deeply
umami, with meaty texture. Must be thoroughly cooked (raw morels
contain trace hydrazines). Recommended: sautéing, stuffing, cream
soups, or drying for long-term storage. Dried morels rehydrate well
and concentrate in flavor.

**Nutrition:** High protein, fiber, B vitamins (niacin, pantothenic
acid), vitamin D2, and minerals (iron, phosphorus, potassium).

## Similar Species
