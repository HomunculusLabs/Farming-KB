## Geoff Wallace

## Description

Geoff Wallace is an Australian agricultural innovator and inventor of a highly effective soil conditioning implement referenced extensively by [[soil-life]] — without the need for fertiliser or top-dressing.
- His system enables seed to be dropped into thin furrows created by the machine; beans, corn, and other crops can grow through existing grass cover.
- Recorded as much as 13°C (25°F) increase in soil temperature on treated versus untreated soils in autumn — a result generated by both biological activity and the air pockets left at various depths by the chisel points.
- This temperature increase enables earlier planting and supports more frost-sensitive crops.
- The approach produces permanent soil structure change if the soil is not subsequently compacted by heavy traffic, over-stocking, or chemical treatments.

## Soil Rehabilitation Method

Wallace's soil conditioning follows a sequential graze-or-cut-and-chisel cycle:

1. **First pass:** Chisel plough at 5–10 cm depth after rain when soil will carry a tractor.
2. **Graze or cut:** New growth is hard-grazed or cut and left as [[masanobu-fukuoka]] and P.A. Yeomans.
- Contrast with conventional mouldboard ploughing, which inverts and destroys soil structure.
- Part of the broader movement toward conservation agriculture and minimum tillage.
- Geoff Wallace is referenced as a personal communication source (pers. comm.) by Mollison for temperature data.
