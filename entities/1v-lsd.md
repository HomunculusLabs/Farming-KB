## 1V-LSD

## Overview

1V-LSD (1-valeroyl-lysergic [[lsd-lysergic-acid-diethylamide-experience-guide]]) is a novel psychedelic
compound in the lysergamide class, first identified in European drug
markets in 2021. It is an analogue of LSD in which the amide nitrogen
is substituted with a valeroyl (pentanoyl) group — a modification that
increases molecular weight and changes chromatographic properties,
likely intended to circumvent drug scheduling laws. 1V-LSD is widely
regarded as a prodrug that converts to LSD in vivo via metabolic
hydrolysis of the valeroyl group, producing subjective effects
essentially indistinguishable from LSD itself.

## Chemical Identity

- **IUPAC name:** (6aR,9R)-N-[(1S)-1-methylheptan-1-yl]-7-propyl-6,6a,
  8,9,10,10a-hexahydroindolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide
- **Molecular formula:** C₂₃H₃₁N₃O₂
- **Molecular weight:** 381.53 g/mol
- **SMILES:** CCCCC(=O)N[C@@H]1CCN2C1=C(C3=CC=CC=C3C2=O)C4=CNC5=C4C
  =CC=C5
- **CAS number:** Not formally registered as of 2025.
- **Chemical class:** Ergoline / lysergamide
- **[[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] 2016.

## Pharmacology

- **Mechanism of action:** Partial agonist at 5-HT₂A serotonin
  receptors — the primary mechanism shared with LSD and other
  classical psychedelics responsible for hallucinogenic effects.
- **Prodrug hypothesis:** The valeroyl group is cleaved enzymatically
  (likely by amidases in blood or liver) to yield LSD as the active
  metabolite. This parallels the established prodrug behavior of
  1P-LSD and 1cP-LSD.
- **Affinity:** No published [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]]) at higher doses.
- **Somatic:** Body lightness, tingling sensations, nausea on
  come-up (less common than with psilocybin), and pupil dilation.
- **After-effects:** Mild residual stimulation and emotional
  sensitivity for 12–24 hours post-experience.
