## Overview

Cladonia cristatella, commonly known as the British Soldiers lichen, is a
small fruticose lichen in the family Cladoniaceae. It is one of the most
recognizable lichens in [[dmt-containing-plants-united-states-halpern]].

The species grows on soil, decaying wood, tree bases, and stumps in a
wide range of forest types. It is tolerant of disturbance and is commonly
found along trails, forest edges, and in secondary growth forests.

It prefers acidic substrates and is common on sandy or gravelly soils,
rotting logs, and at the bases of oak, pine, and other trees. Elevational
range is from sea level to approximately 1500 m.

C. cristatella is relatively tolerant of moderate air pollution compared
to many other macrolichens. This tolerance allows it to persist in
suburban and semi-urban environments where more sensitive species have
declined.
