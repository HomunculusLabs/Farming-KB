# Han-Kyu Cho (Master Cho)

> **Note:** Sources unavailable — content from model knowledge. Review and add sources.

## Overview

Han-Kyu Cho (born ~1938, South Korea) is the founder of **Korean Natural Farming (KNF)**, a complete agricultural system that uses [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] and fermented plant/animal inputs to grow crops without reliance on external [[cervantes-chemical-fertilizers-guide]] or pesticides. He is widely known as "Master Cho" among practitioners worldwide.

## Key Contributions

- **Korean Natural Farming (KNF):** Developed a complete farming methodology centered on working with nature rather than against it. KNF uses locally sourced, fermented inputs to support plant health through all growth stages.
- **Indigenous Microorganism (IMO) collection:** Devised the 4-stage IMO collection and cultivation process that captures beneficial microbes from the local environment — the foundation of KNF soil biology.
- **[[bionutrients-lab-fermented-plant-juice-fpj]] (FPJ):** Pioneered the extraction of plant nutrients through lacto-fermentation using fast-growing meristematic plant parts.
- **Fish Amino Acid (FAA):** Developed protein-rich liquid fertilizer from fermented fish waste.
- **Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN):** Created a 5-herb fermented extraction used to boost plant immunity.
- **Water-Soluble Calcium (WCA) and [[bionutrients-lab-calcium-phosphate-calamansi]] (WCP):** Developed methods to extract bioavailable calcium from eggshells and bones using natural vinegar.
- **Nutritive Cycle Theory:** Formulated a growth-stage framework — vegetative, changeover, reproductive — that maps specific inputs to each phase of plant development.
- **Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV):** Created natural acetic acid extraction method used as a solvent for calcium and mineral preparations.
- **Fermented Mixed Compost (FMC):** Developed IMO-enriched [[natural-farming-guide-composting-methods-and-techniques]] for field application.

## Major Works

- **"Korean Natural Farming"** — Foundational manual for the KNF system
- Global teaching and workshops (Hawaii, mainland US, Southeast Asia, Africa)

## Legacy

KNF has spread to over 30 countries and is practiced in [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]], vegetable farming, orchard management, and animal husbandry. It directly influenced the development of [[jadam-methods]] (Young-sang Cho's simplified system). Master Cho's philosophy centers on the idea that all necessary inputs can be found within the farm's ecosystem.
