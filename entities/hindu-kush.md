# Hindu Kush

## Overview

**Hindu Kush** is a pure indica [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]] and the namesake of the entire
"kush" family of strains.

The strain has been cultivated for centuries by indigenous communities in
the region, primarily for [[bubba-kush]], [[dmt-containing-plants-united-states-halpern]] and Europe during the 1970s and 1980s.

## Appearance

Plants are compact, bushy, and short-statured, typically reaching 60–100
cm in height. The structure is characteristically indica — dense branching
with short internodes and broad, dark green leaflets.

Flowers are densely packed, heavy, and coated in thick trichome layers.
Colors range from deep green to purple and blue-black, especially in cooler
growing conditions. The buds are extremely resinous, producing copious
amounts of sticky, aromatic trichomes.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is deeply earthy and pungent, with dominant notes of damp soil,
sandalwood, and hashish. Secondary notes include subtle floral, pine, and
citrus undertones.

The flavor profile is smooth and hash-like, with a woody, earthy
character and a slightly sweet, spicy finish on the exhale. The smoke
is typically thick and expansive.

## Terpene Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**
- Myrcene: 0.5–1.2% — earthy, musky, sedative
- Caryophyllene: 0.3–0.8% — peppery, spicy, anti-inflammatory
- Limonene: 0.1–0.4% — citrus, mood-elevating

**Secondary terpenes:** pinene, linalool, humulene in trace amounts.

The terpene profile is relatively simple compared to modern polyhybrids,
reflecting its landrace genetics.

## Cannabinoid Profile

**THC:** 15–22% (landrace specimens typically lower, 12–18%)
**CBD:** Low, typically <0.1%
