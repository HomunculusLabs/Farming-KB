# Hindu Kush

## Overview

**Hindu Kush** is a pure indica landrace cannabis strain originating from
the mountainous Hindu Kush region spanning eastern Afghanistan, northern
Pakistan, and the border areas of Tajikistan. It is one of the foundational
genetic pillars of modern cannabis breeding and the namesake of the entire
"kush" family of strains.

The strain has been cultivated for centuries by indigenous communities in
the region, primarily for hashish production. Its resinous, compact flowers
are ideally suited to the traditional hand-rubbed charas and sieved hashish
methods practiced throughout Central and South Asia.

Hindu Kush is considered one of the most genetically important landrace
strains, having contributed its genetics to countless modern cultivars
including OG Kush, Bubba Kush, Master Kush, and many others.

## Lineage

Hindu Kush is a pure landrace with no known hybrid parentage. It has been
isolated in the Hindu Kush mountain range for centuries, developing
distinctive characteristics adapted to the harsh, arid, high-altitude
environment.

The genetic lineage of modern "kush" strains traces almost exclusively
back to Hindu Kush or closely related Afghan landraces imported to the
United States and Europe during the 1970s and 1980s.

## Appearance

Plants are compact, bushy, and short-statured, typically reaching 60–100
cm in height. The structure is characteristically indica — dense branching
with short internodes and broad, dark green leaflets.

Flowers are densely packed, heavy, and coated in thick trichome layers.
Colors range from deep green to purple and blue-black, especially in cooler
growing conditions. The buds are extremely resinous, producing copious
amounts of sticky, aromatic trichomes.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is deeply earthy and pungent, with dominant notes of damp soil,
sandalwood, and hashish. Secondary notes include subtle floral, pine, and
citrus undertones.

The flavor profile is smooth and hash-like, with a woody, earthy
character and a slightly sweet, spicy finish on the exhale. The smoke
is typically thick and expansive.

## Terpene Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**
- Myrcene: 0.5–1.2% — earthy, musky, sedative
- Caryophyllene: 0.3–0.8% — peppery, spicy, anti-inflammatory
- Limonene: 0.1–0.4% — citrus, mood-elevating

**Secondary terpenes:** pinene, linalool, humulene in trace amounts.

The terpene profile is relatively simple compared to modern polyhybrids,
reflecting its landrace genetics.

## Cannabinoid Profile

**THC:** 15–22% (landrace specimens typically lower, 12–18%)
**CBD:** Low, typically <0.1%
**Primary cannabinoids:** THC, with trace amounts of THCV and CBC.

The cannabinoid profile is THC-dominant with negligible CBD, consistent
with drug-type Afghan landraces selected for resin production rather than
fiber or seed.

## Effects

Hindu Kush produces deeply relaxing, sedative effects characteristic of
pure indica genetics. Onset is relatively slow (15–30 minutes when smoked),
gradually building into a heavy, full-body calm.

Initial effects include mild euphoria and cerebral fog, transitioning into
profound physical relaxation, couch-lock, and eventually sleep. Duration is
typically 2–4 hours.

The strain is widely used for insomnia, pain relief, anxiety reduction,
and muscle relaxation. It is poorly suited for daytime use or activities
requiring focus and energy.

## Cultivation

Hindu Kush is considered moderately easy to cultivate and is well-suited
to both indoor and outdoor growing. Its compact size makes it ideal for
limited-space indoor operations.

**Flowering time:** 7–9 weeks indoor; late September to mid-October outdoor.
**Yield:** Moderate, 300–400 g/m² indoor.
**Climate preference:** Dry, warm, and arid conditions mimicking its native
mountain environment. Resistant to mold but susceptible to powdery mildew
in humid conditions.

The strain responds well to low-stress training and thrives in soil-based
systems. It is naturally pest-resistant and requires moderate nutrient
levels.
