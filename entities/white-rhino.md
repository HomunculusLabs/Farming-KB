# White Rhino

**White Rhino** is a legendary indica-dominant cannabis strain renowned for its potent
relaxing effects, heavy resin production, and therapeutic value. A staple of the 1990s
European cannabis scene, White Rhino has maintained its reputation as a powerful
medicinal strain favored by patients managing chronic pain, insomnia, and anxiety.

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Indica-dominant hybrid (~80% indica / 20% sativa) |
| **THC Content** | 18–22% |
| **CBD Content** | Low (<1%) |
| **Breeder** | Green House Seeds |
| **Lineage** | White Widow × Unknown North American Indica |
| **Flowering Time** | 8–9 weeks (indoor) |
| **Yield** | High (400–500 g/m² indoor; 600–900 g/plant outdoor) |

## Lineage & Breeding

White Rhino was created by **Green House Seeds**, one of the most prominent seed banks
in Amsterdam and a foundational force in modern cannabis genetics. The strain is the
result of crossing the iconic **White Widow** — itself a legendary Brazilian sativa ×
South Indian indica hybrid — with an **unknown North American indica**. This indica
parent is believed to contribute the strain's heavy body effects and abbreviated
flowering cycle.

The introduction of the North American indica to the White Widow lineage resulted in a
strain that retained its parent's generous trichome production while dramatically
shifting the effect profile toward deep physical relaxation. Green House Seeds has
offered White Rhino in both regular and feminized seed forms since the late 1990s.

## Genetics

White Rhino is classified as an **indica-dominant hybrid** with an approximate ratio of
**80% indica to 20% sativa**. The indica dominance is evident in the plant's compact
growth structure, broad fingered leaves, and fast flowering cycle. Despite the heavy
indica lean, a subtle sativa influence from the White Widow lineage can be detected in
the initial cerebral uplift that precedes the strain's sedative body stone.

The strain is genetically stable and breeds true across generations, making it a
reliable choice for both commercial cultivation and home growing projects.

## Appearance

White Rhino earns its name from its striking visual profile. The buds are **extremely
dense and compact**, characteristic of indica-dominant varieties. Mature flowers are
coated in a thick layer of **white crystalline trichomes** that give the nugs a frosted,
almost silver-white appearance — reminiscent of a rhinoceros hide.

Key visual characteristics include:

- **Dense, tightly packed calyxes** with minimal visible stems
- **Abundant trichome coverage** ranging from cloudy white to amber at harvest
- **Deep green foliage** often accented by hints of lighter green and occasional
  purple hues in cooler growing conditions
- **Moderate pistil density** with orange to brownish-orange hairs weaving through the
  resin-heavy buds

## Aroma & Flavor Profile

White Rhino features a bold, pungent aroma profile that reflects its indica-heavy
genetics:

- **Aroma**: Earthy and woody at the core, with skunky, pungent undertones. Breaking
  open a bud releases a rich organic scent reminiscent of damp soil and pine. Some
  phenotypes exhibit subtle notes of diesel and hashish.
- **Flavor**: On inhalation, the smoke is smooth and earthy with pronounced woody
  flavors. The exhale brings out a slightly sweet, herbal quality with lingering
  hash-like spice. The flavor is generally described as rich and full-bodied rather than
  fruity or floral.

The terpene profile is dominated by **myrcene** (contributing earthy, musky notes and
sedative effects), **caryophyllene** (peppery, spicy), and **pinene** (woody, pine).

## Cannabinoid Profile

White Rhino is noted for its **high THC content**, typically ranging from **18% to 22%**
depending on growing conditions and phenotype. Some particularly well-cultivated
specimens have been tested above 22%. CBD content is **low**, generally below 1%, which
is consistent with its recreational-era breeding lineage.

The high THC-to-CBD ratio makes White Rhino best suited for experienced consumers and
patients seeking strong psychoactive effects alongside physical relief.

## Effects

### Recreational Effects

- **Onset**: The high begins with a mild cerebral euphoria — a gentle uplifting
  sensation inherited from the White Widow sativa genetics.
- **Body High**: Within minutes, a deep, heavy body stone takes over. Muscles relax,
  tension dissipates, and a warm, enveloping sensation spreads throughout the body.
- **Duration**: Effects typically last 2–3 hours.
- **Sedation**: As the high progresses, White Rhino becomes increasingly sedative.
  Higher doses or late-evening use commonly lead to couch-lock and sleep.

### Medicinal Applications

White Rhino is widely regarded as a premier **medicinal strain**, particularly valued
for:

- **Pain management**: Effective against chronic pain, muscle spasms, migraines, and
  neuropathic discomfort due to its potent body-relaxing properties.
- **Insomnia**: The strong sedative effect makes it an excellent choice for patients
  struggling with sleep onset or maintenance.
- **Anxiety and stress relief**: The initial euphoric uplift combined with sustained
  physical relaxation helps calm racing thoughts and ease tension.
- **Appetite stimulation**: Consistent munchies effect can benefit patients dealing with
  appetite loss due to medical treatments or conditions.
- **Nausea relief**: Reported anti-nausea properties, particularly at moderate doses.

## Growing Information

### Cultivation Overview

White Rhino is considered a **beginner-friendly strain** that rewards growers with
heavy yields and forgiving growth characteristics. Its indica dominance makes it
naturally compact and resilient.

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Indoor Height** | 60–100 cm |
| **Outdoor Height** | 100–150 cm |
| **Indoor Flowering** | 8–9 weeks |
| **Outdoor Harvest** | Late September to early October (Northern Hemisphere) |
| **Indoor Yield** | 400–500 g/m² |
| **Outdoor Yield** | 600–900 g/plant |
| **Difficulty** | Easy to moderate |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests |

### Growing Tips

- **Training**: Responds well to low-stress training (LST) and Sea of Green (SOG)
  methods. Topping can increase yield but is not strictly necessary given the plant's
  natural lateral branching.
- **Nutrients**: Moderate feeder; avoid over-fertilizing, particularly with nitrogen
  during the flowering phase.
- **Climate**: Prefers warm, dry climates outdoors. Can tolerate slightly cooler
  temperatures, which may enhance trichome production and bring out purple coloration.
- **Humidity**: Maintain lower humidity (40–50%) during late flowering to prevent mold
  in the dense buds.
- **Support**: The heavy, resinous colas may require staking or trellising in the final
  weeks of flowering.

## Awards & History

- White Rhino was one of the flagship strains in the **Green House Seeds** catalog
  throughout the late 1990s and 2000s, contributing to the coffeeshop culture explosion
  in Amsterdam.
- The strain gained widespread recognition in cannabis competitions during the early
  2000s, cementing its status as a top-tier indica-dominant variety.
- White Rhino was instrumental in establishing the "White" naming convention in
  cannabis (alongside White Widow and White Russian), denoting strains with exceptional
  trichome coverage.
- It remains in active circulation through Green House Seeds and numerous other seed
  banks, with genetics available in feminized, regular, and autoflowering variants.

## Similar Strains

- **White Widow** — Parent strain; more balanced sativa/indica effects
- **White Russian** — White Widow × AK-47; slightly more cerebral
- **Northern Lights** — Classic indica with similar sedative and therapeutic profile
- **Critical Mass** — Comparable yield and indica-dominant relaxation
- **Afghan Kush** — Pure indica with overlapping medicinal applications
