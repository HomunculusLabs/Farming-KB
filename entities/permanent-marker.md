# Permanent Marker

## Overview

Permanent Marker is a balanced hybrid cannabis strain bred by Seed Junky
Genetics and selected by the Cookies brand. Named Leafly's Strain of the Year
for 2023, it has rapidly become one of the most sought-after modern
cultivars, known for its extremely potent aroma profile and heavy resin
production.

The strain exemplifies the contemporary "exotic" trend in cannabis breeding,
combining loud terpene expression with high THC content and bag appeal.
Its name references the pungent, lingering scent that seems to persist on
everything it contacts.

## Lineage

Permanent Marker is a three-way cross of:

- **Biscotti** (female) — itself a Gelato #25 × South Florida OG cross,
  contributing dense bud structure, cookie-dough sweetness, and relaxing
  body effects.
- **Jealousy** — a Gelato #41 × Sherbet cross, lending creamy lavender
  notes, cerebral euphoria, and purple coloration potential.
- **Sherbet BX1** — a backcross of Sherbet reinforcing the dessert
  terpene profile and adding frosty trichome coverage.

The genetic ratio is approximately 50/50 indica-sativa, though phenotype
expression can lean slightly indica-dominant depending on selection.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large with a dense, chunky structure characteristic of
the Biscotti lineage. Colors range from deep forest green to muted purple
with bright orange pistils woven throughout.

The most striking feature is the resin coverage: flowers are typically
coated in a thick layer of milky-white trichomes that gives them a
frosted, almost white appearance. Under magnification, both bulbous
and capitate-stalked trichome heads are abundant.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is intensely pungent and complex, described as a combination of:
- **Sharp, chemical marker-like notes** (hence the name) — gassy, acrid
- **Creamy dessert undertones** — vanilla, cookie dough, sweet cream
- **Earthy funk** — damp soil, skunk, fermented fruit

The flavor follows the aroma closely: initial hits deliver a creamy, sweet
cookie-like inhale followed by an acrid, gassy, peppery exhale. The
aftertaste is long-lasting and lingers on the palate — true to the name.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

**Dominant terpenes:**
- **Caryophyllene** (~0.8–1.2%) — peppery, contributes to the sharp
  aroma; binds CB2 receptors for anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Limonene** (~0.6–1.0%) — citrus notes, mood elevation, stress relief.
- **Myrcene** (~0.5–0.8%) — earthy, sedative body effects.
- **Linalool** (trace–0.3%) — floral, anxiolytic.

**Cannabinoid profile:** THC typically ranges 25–32%, with total cannabinoid
content reaching 33–36%. CBD is very low (<0.1%). THCa content accounts for
most of the total. Minor cannabinoids (CBG, CBC) are present in trace
amounts.

## Effects

**Onset:** Rapid, felt within 2–5 minutes of inhalation.

**Head effects:** Initial cerebral rush characterized by euphoria, creative
energy, and a floating, somewhat disorienting headspace. Thoughts become
associative and free-flowing. Visual perception may sharpen slightly.

**Body effects:** Progressive physical relaxation that intensifies over
30–60 minutes. Muscular tension dissolves without full sedation in most
users. Can transition toward couch-lock at higher doses.

**Duration:** 2–3 hours, with a gradual comedown.

Common applications include stress relief, mood elevation, mild pain
management, and creative stimulation. The balanced profile makes it
versatile for both daytime and evening use at moderate doses.

## Cultivation

**Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoors.
**Yield:** Moderate to high (400–500 g/m² indoors).
**Difficulty:** Intermediate — requires attention to humidity due to dense
bud structure.

Permanent Marker performs well in both soil and hydroponic setups. Plants
are medium height with strong lateral branching. Training techniques
(low-stress training, topping) improve yield significantly.

The dense bud structure makes it susceptible to bud rot (*Botrytis
cinerea*) in high humidity during late flowering. Maintaining relative
humidity below 50% in weeks 7–9 is essential. The strain responds well to
defoliation for airflow improvement.

Optimal temperature range is 20–26°C during flowering. Cooler night
temperatures (18–20°C) in the final two weeks can enhance purple
coloration and terpene production.

## Side Effects

Common: dry mouth, dry eyes, mild anxiety at high doses, dizziness.
Less common: paranoia (in THC-sensitive individuals), headache.
The high THC content means novice users should approach with caution.
