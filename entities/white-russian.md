# White Russian

**White Russian** is an award-winning indica-dominant cannabis hybrid bred by Serious Seeds of Amsterdam. Created by crossing [[white-widow-entity]] and [[ak-47-cannabis]], White Russian won the overall Cannabis Cup at the 1996 High Times Cannabis Cup, one of the most prestigious awards in competitive cannabis. The strain combines the resin production of White Widow with the potency and complex aroma of AK-47.

## Genetic Lineage

| Parent | Type | Origin |
|--------|------|--------|
| **White Widow** | Balanced hybrid | Greenhouse Seed Co., Netherlands (1990s) |
| **AK-47** | Sativa-dominant hybrid | Serious Seeds, Netherlands |

The cross produces a plant that is approximately 60% indica / 40% sativa. White Russian inherits the heavy resin coating of White Widow and the complex, skunky terpene profile of AK-47. The result is a strain noted for its extreme resin production — buds are often described as being "completely coated with white resin."

## Terpene Profile

White Russian's aroma is intensely skunky with earthy and pine undertones. It is considered one of the most pungent strains from Serious Seeds.

| Terpene | Role |
|---------|------|
| **[[myrcene]]** | Dominant; earthy, herbal base with sedative body effects |
| **[[beta-caryophyllene]]** | Peppery, spicy accent; anti-inflammatory properties |
| **[[limonene]]** | Citrus notes; mood elevation and stress relief |
| **[[pinene]]** | Pine undertone; mental clarity and bronchodilation |
| **[[humulene]]** | Woody, earthy depth; appetite suppression |

## Aroma and Flavor

- **Aroma:** Strong skunky smell with earthy and pine notes. The scent is detectable even during the vegetative stage, making odor control important for indoor growers.
- **Flavor:** Skunky and earthy on the inhale, with a smooth, slightly sweet exhale. The smoke is thick and resinous.
- **Aftertaste:** Lingering pine and pepper with a subtle sweetness.

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

White Russian delivers a potent, well-balanced experience that begins with cerebral stimulation and transitions into deep body relaxation. The effects are strong and long-lasting, reflecting the combined potency of both parent strains.

- **Onset:** 5–15 minutes; initial cerebral uplift and euphoria
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes; body relaxation intensifies alongside mental effects
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours with extended body effects

### Reported Effects

**Positive:**
- Powerful euphoria and mood elevation
- Deep body relaxation and stress relief
- Creative stimulation and mental clarity
- Strong analgesic effects
- Appetite stimulation

**Negative:**
- Dry mouth and dry eyes (very common)
- Couch-lock at moderate-to-high doses
- Dizziness in sensitive users
- Potential anxiety at high doses

### Medical Applications

White Russian's balanced effects profile makes it versatile for medical cannabis patients. Its combination of cerebral and physical effects suits a range of conditions.

- **Pain management:** Strong analgesic properties for [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]]
- **Stress and anxiety:** Euphoric onset provides mood elevation and mental calm
- **Insomnia:** Body sedation at higher doses promotes restful sleep
