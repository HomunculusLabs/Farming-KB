# HOT-7

HOT-7 is the N-hydroxy analogue of 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7, a psychedelic phenethylamine.

It is documented as entry #88 in PiHKAL by Alexander Shulgin.

The name follows Shulgin's convention: putting a hydroxyl (HO) group on a thio (T) material gives a HOT compound.

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-N-hydroxy-4-(n)-propylthiophenethylamine.

HOT-7 differs from 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 only by the addition of a hydroxyl group on the amine nitrogen.

The compound is the first of the N-hydroxy 2C-T-X series to be described in PiHKAL.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 15 - 25 mg.

Duration of effects: 6 - 8 hours.

The dosage is closely comparable to that of 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7, consistent with the N-hydroxy hypothesis.

If anything, HOT-7 may be slightly shorter in duration than its parent compound.

## Qualitative Effects

At 15 mg, effects are described as "lightheaded, and maybe a little tipsy" with a centered but socially withdrawn state.

The experience is characterized as having "shades of alcohol woozy."

Effects begin subsiding by the fifth hour and are gone by the seventh.

The overall character is described as "smoothly stoning."

At 22 mg, the transition into effects is described as "a bit difficult, with a faint awareness in the tummy."

By the second hour, the experience becomes "quite psychedelic" and body concerns fade.

The compound is "very rich in eyes-closed imagery" and good for interpretive and conceptual thinking.

Eyes-open visuals are less pronounced than might be expected.

At 22 mg, another evaluator described a "very positive" experience that was "not quite" a complete plus three.

Erotic explorations "somehow just failed to knit by the thinnest of margins."

The experience is characterized as a "truly almost-magnificent" one, tinged with sadness.

## The HOT Family

The HOT compounds represent the N-hydroxy family of psychedelic amines.

The naming convention is straightforward: HO (hydroxyl) + T (thio) = HOT.

The family includes HOT-2 (N-hydroxy-2C-T-2), HOT-7 (this compound), and HOT-17 (N-hydroxy-2C-T-17).

All three HOT compounds were found to be active, with dosages and effects closely comparable to their non-hydroxylated counterparts.

## The N-Hydroxy Hypothesis

HOT-7 provides further evidence for Shulgin's working hypothesis about N-hydroxy compounds.

The hypothesis states: an N-hydroxy amine has approximately the same potency and the same action as its N-hydrogen counterpart.

The hypothesis emerged from the remarkable coincidence that MDOH (mdoh-entity|N-hydroxy-MDA) was equipotent to mda-entity|MDA.

Two possible explanations exist: the N-hydroxy compound reduces to the N-H material in the body, or the N-H material oxidizes to the N-hydroxy material.

Both directions have biochemical precedent.

The N-hydroxy concept has been extended to mdma-entity|MDMA (producing FLEA) and across the 2C-T-X series.

## Synthesis

The synthesis starts from 2,5-dimethoxy-beta-nitro-4-(n)-propylthiostyrene (the nitrostyrene precursor to 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7).

The nitrostyrene is reduced using borane-dimethyl sulfide complex and sodium borohydride in THF.

The reduction is conducted at room temperature over approximately one week.

The product is isolated as the hydrochloride salt after acid-base workup and recrystallization from isopropanol/ether.

## Pharmacological Context

The equivalence of HOT-7 and 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 suggests that the N-hydroxy transformation does not significantly alter the pharmacological profile.

This has implications for understanding the active species at 5-HT2A receptors and other targets.

If N-hydroxylation and de-hydroxylation occur as metabolic processes, the question of which form is the "true" active agent becomes difficult to resolve.

## Comparison with 2C-T-7

HOT-7 and 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 are described as "almost identical" in their effects.

Both compounds center their active dose range around 20 mg.

HOT-7 may be slightly shorter in duration than 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7.

The character of the experience, including the balance between visual and cognitive effects, appears very similar between the two compounds.

## Legal Status

HOT-7 is not specifically scheduled in most jurisdictions.

As an N-hydroxy analogue of 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7, it would likely fall under analogue drug legislation in jurisdictions that have scheduled 2C-T-7 or the broader 2C family.

The compound has never been widely available.

## Harm Reduction

The dosage (15-25 mg) requires accurate milligram measurement.

Some gastrointestinal discomfort during onset has been reported.

As with all psychedelic phenethylamines, HOT-7 should not be combined with MAOIs or other serotonergic substances.

The compound has not been studied for long-term toxicity.
