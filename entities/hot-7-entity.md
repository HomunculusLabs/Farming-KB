## HOT-7

HOT-7 is the N-hydroxy analogue of 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7, a psychedelic phenethylamine.

It is documented as entry #88 in PiHKAL by [[mdma]]|MDMA (producing FLEA) and across the 2C-T-X series.

## Synthesis

The synthesis starts from 2,5-dimethoxy-beta-nitro-4-(n)-propylthiostyrene (the nitrostyrene precursor to 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7).

The nitrostyrene is reduced using borane-dimethyl sulfide complex and sodium borohydride in THF.

The reduction is conducted at room temperature over approximately one week.

The product is isolated as the hydrochloride salt after acid-base workup and recrystallization from isopropanol/ether.

## Pharmacological Context

The equivalence of HOT-7 and 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 suggests that the N-hydroxy transformation does not significantly alter the pharmacological profile.

This has implications for understanding the active species at 5-HT2A receptors and other targets.

If N-hydroxylation and de-hydroxylation occur as metabolic processes, the question of which form is the "true" active agent becomes difficult to resolve.

## Comparison with 2C-T-7

HOT-7 and 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7 are described as "almost identical" in their effects.

Both compounds center their active dose range around 20 mg.

HOT-7 may be slightly shorter in duration than 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7.

The character of the experience, including the balance between visual and cognitive effects, appears very similar between the two compounds.

## Legal Status

HOT-7 is not specifically scheduled in most jurisdictions.

As an N-hydroxy analogue of 2c-t-7-entity|2C-T-7, it would likely fall under analogue drug legislation in jurisdictions that have scheduled 2C-T-7 or the broader 2C family.

The compound has never been widely available.
