## Silky Bantam

## Description

The Silky Bantam (also Silkie) is a small breed of chicken named for its uniquely soft, hair-like plumage that lacks the barbicels (tiny hooks) that normally hold feather barbs together, giving the feathers a silky, fur-like texture. The breed is believed to have originated in China or [[guzman-thailand-southeast-asia-psychoactive-fungi-distribution]], with the first documented European reference by Marco Polo in the 13th century.
