# 4-HO-MPT — TIHKAL #23

## Compound Identity

- Full name: 4-Hydroxy-N-methyl-N-propyltryptamine

- Synonyms: 3-[2-(methylpropylamino)ethyl]-4-indolol

- Class: Tryptamine, 4-hydroxy substituted, N-methyl-N-propyl

- Source: [[psilocin-entity-tihkal]]. Rapid subsidence with ability to eat
normally. Residual insomnia lasting eight hours was reported.

## Synthesis

From 4-acetoxyindole via [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-formation-oxalyl-chloride-indole]] to the glyoxyl chloride, then treatment
with methylpropylamine. The resulting glyoxylamide (mp 94-95°C) is reduced
with LAH in THF. The hydrochloride salt (mp 162-163°C) is obtained in 54%
yield.

## Pharmacological Notes

Only a single trial exists, making it impossible to give a fair estimate
of dose or duration. Shulgin extrapolates that this material is likely active
in the psilocin range of approximately 10-20 mg orally.

A useful generalization emerges from the 4-hydroxy tryptamine series: anything
with up to six carbons attached to the tryptamine nitrogen atom (and bearing
a 4-hydroxy group) appears active in the 10-20 mg range orally. This holds
for methyl-methyl ([[4-ho-met-entity-tihkal]]),
methyl-propyl (4-HO-MPT), methyl-isopropyl ([[det-entity-tihkal]]), dipropyl ([[4-ho-dipt-entity-tihkal]]).

Pushing further — when one N-alkyl group has four carbons — activity drops
dramatically. All four isomeric butyl compounds are known (n-butyl, isobutyl,
sec-butyl, tert-butyl). Only the tert-butyl isomer (4-HO-MTBT) has been tested
in man at 15 mg, producing virtually no effects beyond a possible brief hint
— probably pure placebo.

## Chemical Family Context

4-HO-MPT is a member of the 4-hydroxytryptamine family, positioned in the
systematic exploration of N-alkyl substitution patterns. The compound carries
four carbons on the nitrogen (one methyl + three from propyl = four total),
placing it at the upper boundary of the "six-carbon rule" for active 4-hydroxy
tryptamines.

The "six-carbon rule" is one of the most robust generalizations to emerge
from the Shulgins' systematic exploration of [[psilocybin-tryptamine-chemistry-fungi]]. It states
that 4-hydroxytryptamines with six or fewer total carbons on the nitrogen
atom are active in the 10-20 mg range, while compounds exceeding this threshold
show dramatically reduced activity. 4-HO-MPT, with its four total nitrogen
carbons, falls comfortably within this range.

The compound's mixed N-alkyl pattern (one methyl, one propyl) versus symmetric
substitution (two identical groups) provides data on whether the specific
identity of alkyl groups matters beyond their total carbon count. The
available evidence suggests that the total carbon count is more important
than the specific arrangement, though subtle qualitative differences exist.

## Detailed Pharmacological Discussion

4-HO-MPT's single trial at 8 mg showed mild visual distortion and prominent
vertigo, with relatively rapid subsidence and residual insomnia. The vertigo
component is an interesting feature not typically prominent with psilocin,
and may reflect differential activity at vestibular-related serotonin
receptors or at 5-HT1A receptors (which can produce dizziness).

The residual insomnia lasting eight hours after a mild 8 mg experience is
noteworthy. In standard psilocin experiences, sleep is typically possible
4-6 hours after dosing. The extended sleep disruption could reflect slower
metabolic clearance of the propyl-containing compound compared to the
dimethyl (psilocin) or diethyl (4-HO-DET) variants.

4-HO-MPT presumably acts primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist, though
the prominence of vertigo in the single trial may indicate additional
contributions from other receptor systems. The compound's pharmacological
profile remains largely uncharacterized beyond this single data point.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- 4-HO-DMT (psilocin): N,N-dimethyl, 10-20 mg, 3-6 h, well characterized

- 4-HO-MET: N-ethyl-N-methyl, 10-20 mg, 4-6 h, very similar to psilocin

- 4-HO-MPT: N-methyl-N-propyl, ~10-20 mg (estimated), insufficient data

- 4-HO-MIPT: N-isopropyl-N-methyl, 12-25 mg, 4-6 h, more erotic emphasis

- 4-HO-DET: N,N-diethyl, 10-25 mg, 4-6 h, slightly more sedating

- 4-HO-MTBT (tert-butyl): Essentially inactive at 15 mg, seven carbons on N

The progression from active compounds (up to six N-carbons) to the inactive
tert-butyl variant (seven N-carbons) provides strong evidence for the
six-carbon rule.

## Harm Reduction and Safety Considerations

- Only a single human trial exists; all dose guidance is extrapolated

- The vertigo reported at 8 mg could be hazardous in environments requiring
balance or coordination

- The residual insomnia suggests the compound may have a longer effective
duration than the subjective experience implies

- Standard tryptamine precautions apply: avoid MAO inhibitors, SSRIs, and
other serotonergic medications

- Start with low doses (5-10 mg) and allow adequate time (2+ hours) before
considering any dose increase

- The compound's novelty means no chronic toxicity data exists

## Legal Status

4-HO-MPT is not specifically scheduled in most jurisdictions. As a
4-hydroxy-N-alkyltryptamine, it could potentially fall under analogue
legislation related to psilocin or psilocybin. The compound's extreme
rarity has kept it below regulatory attention. Some countries with broad
psychoactive substance laws may cover it.
