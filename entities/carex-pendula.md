## Related Species

Carex pendula belongs to Carex section Ceratocystis, a group of large forest sedges characterised by pendulous inflorescences and robust tussock-forming habits. The section includes several other European woodland species.

Within its native range, Carex pendula is most frequently confused with [[carex-sylvatica]], another shade-tolerant woodland sedge, is considerably smaller and produces erect rather than pendulous flower spikes.
