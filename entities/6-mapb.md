## Pharmacology

6-MAPB acts as a triple monoamine releaser and reuptake inhibitor, with the
strongest effects on the serotonin system.

Its primary mechanism involves binding to and reversing the direction of the
serotonin transporter (SERT), causing massive serotonin release into the
synaptic cleft. It also inhibits serotonin reuptake, amplifying and prolonging
the effect.

Moderate activity at the dopamine transporter (DAT) and norepinephrine
transporter (NET) contributes to stimulant and prosocial effects. The ratio of
serotonergic to dopaminergic activity is similar to MDMA, though some evidence
suggests 6-MAPB may have slightly more dopaminergic character than 5-APB.

## Effects

Onset occurs within 30–60 minutes after oral administration. Peak effects are
reached at 1.5–2.5 hours, with total duration of 4–6 hours. The after-effects
and comedown period may extend an additional 2–4 hours.

The subjective effect profile includes pronounced euphoria, increased empathy
and emotional openness, enhanced tactile sensation, and mild visual
distortions. Users frequently describe the experience as comparable to MDMA,
with some reporting slightly less "magic" and more stimulant character.
