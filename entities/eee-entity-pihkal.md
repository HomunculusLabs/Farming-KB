## Pharmacology

EEE is a positional isomer in the trialkoxyamphetamine family, where all three
substituent positions (2, 4, and 5) bear ethoxy groups. This makes it the
fully ethoxylated analogue of TMA-2 (trimethoxyamphetamine). The ethoxy group
is larger than methoxy by one methylene unit, which can significantly alter
binding affinity at serotonin 5-HT2A receptors and other monoamine targets.
