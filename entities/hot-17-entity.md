# HOT-17 (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(s)-butylthio-N-hydroxyphenethylamine)

HOT-17 is a phenethylamine compound and the N-hydroxylated derivative of 2C-T-17. It belongs to the HOT (Hydroxylated Ortho-Thio) family of psychedelic substances. The compound is listed as [[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #89.

## Chemical Classification

HOT-17 belongs to the phenethylamine class of chemical compounds.

It is formally named 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(s)-butylthio-N-hydroxyphenethylamine.

The "HOT" designation refers to the N-hydroxylation of the amine group, distinguishing it from the parent compound 2C-T-17.

The (s)-butylthio group indicates a secondary butyl (sec-butyl) sulfur substituent at the 4-position.

It is part of the broader 2C-T family, which features 2,5-dimethoxy-4-alkylthio substitution patterns.
