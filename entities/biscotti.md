## Overview

**Biscotti** is a highly potent indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by the **Cookies** genetics brand (formerly Cookie Fam). It is a three-way cross between **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)**, **Gelato #25**, and **South Florida OG**.

Released in the mid-2010s, Biscotti quickly became a staple of the West Coast exotic cannabis market. Its reputation rests on exceptionally high THC content, a distinctive sweet-and-earthy cookie aroma, and deeply relaxing effects that have made it popular among both recreational and medical consumers.

The strain's name references the Italian twice-baked cookie, alluding to its dessert-like flavor profile and dense, cookie-shaped buds. Biscotti has won or placed in multiple cannabis competitions and is frequently used as a parent in breeding programs for new dessert-type cultivars.

## Lineage

Biscotti's lineage combines three foundational West Coast genetics:

- **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** — the legendary OG Kush × Durban Poison cross that defined an era of exotic cannabis. Contributes sweet, earthy flavor and high resin production.
- **Gelato #25** — a phenotype from the Thin Mint Cookies × Sunset Sherbet cross selected for its creamy, dessert-like terpene profile and balanced effects.
- **South Florida OG** — a potent Florida OG cut known for its fuel-and-citrus terpene profile and heavy sedative effects. Provides the indica backbone of the cross.

The resulting hybrid is typically reported as approximately **80% indica / 20% sativa**, though precise ratios vary by phenotype and breeder. Biscotti itself has become a parent strain for numerous subsequent crosses, including **Biscotti Sherbet**, **Biscotti Pancakes**, **Scotty 2 Hotty**, and **Redemption**.

## Appearance and Aroma

Biscotti produces medium-to-large conical buds that are remarkably **dense and chunky**, living up to the cookie comparison. The nugs are dark forest green with deep purple undertones, interspersed with bright orange pistils.

A thick coating of frosty white trichomes covers the surface, giving the buds a silvery-white sheen when viewed under light. The resin production is exceptionally heavy, making Biscotti a favored choice for solventless extraction (rosin, ice water hash).

The aroma is intensely sweet and complex, dominated by **cookie dough and nutty vanilla** notes with an underlying earthy pungency. Secondary notes of diesel, spice, and sweet berry emerge on the exhale. The flavor closely mirrors the aroma, with a smooth, sweet cookie taste on the inhale and a slightly peppery, herbal finish.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profiles

Biscotti is defined by its dessert-type terpene profile:

- **Limonene** — dominant; contributes citrus brightness and elevating mood effects.
- **Caryophyllene** — prominent; adds peppery spice and potential anti-inflammatory activity via CB2 receptor binding.
- **Myrcene** — significant; provides sedative, relaxing effects and the characteristic herbal musk.
- **Linalool** — present in moderate amounts; adds floral sweetness and anxiolytic properties.
- **Humulene** — trace; contributes earthy, woody undertones.

THC content is consistently high, typically ranging from **25% to 30%** in commercial flower, with select phenotypes and properly grown crops testing above 30%. CBD content is very low, usually below 0.1%.

Total cannabinoid content (THCA + THC + minor cannabinoids) can reach 32–35% in top-tier samples. The low CBD-to-THC ratio produces characteristically psychoactive, euphoric effects.

## Effects

Biscotti's effects are heavily sedative and physically relaxing, consistent with its indica-dominant genetics. The onset begins with a cerebral rush — an uplifted, euphoric head high that may feel slightly spacey or dreamlike.

Within 20–40 minutes, the body effects take over. A deep physical relaxation spreads from the shoulders and neck downward, often described as a "couch-lock" sensation. This transition from mental to physical effects is smooth and gradual rather than abrupt.

Common reported effects include intense relaxation, happiness, sleepiness, appetite stimulation, and mild creative introspection. The strain is particularly well-suited for **evening and nighttime use** due to its sedative finish.

Medical users frequently select Biscotti for chronic pain, insomnia, anxiety, stress, and appetite loss. The combination of cerebral euphoria and physical sedation can provide temporary relief from both physical discomfort and racing thoughts.

## Cultivation

Biscotti is considered a **moderately difficult** strain to cultivate, best suited for experienced growers. It is typically grown indoors or in greenhouses where environmental conditions can be carefully controlled.

Flowering time is approximately **8–9 weeks** indoors. Plants exhibit a medium height and bushy structure typical of indica-dominant genetics. Branching is moderate, and the dense bud formation requires adequate light penetration and air circulation to prevent mold.

Yields are average to above-average, with indoor growers reporting 300–400 g/m² under optimal conditions. The plants respond well to low-stress training (LST) and scrog (screen of green) techniques, which improve light distribution to lower bud sites.

Biscotti is sensitive to temperature fluctuations during late flowering. Cool nighttime temperatures (18–20°C) help bring out the purple coloration that is prized in the market. Nutrient requirements are moderate to high, particularly phosphorus and potassium during the flowering phase.

The strain is moderately resistant to common pests but can be susceptible to **powdery mildew** in humid conditions due to the dense bud structure. Adequate airflow and humidity management (below 50% RH in late flower) are essential.

## Medical Applications

Biscotti's combination of high THC potency and sedative terpene profile makes it a popular choice among medical cannabis patients for managing **chronic pain conditions**, including neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia, and arthritis. The caryophyllene content may contribute additional anti-inflammatory effects.

Insomnia patients report significant benefit from Biscotti's heavy body sedation, which typically induces sleepiness within an hour of consumption. The strain's ability to quiet mental activity while relaxing the body makes it effective for patients whose sleep difficulties are compounded by anxiety or racing thoughts.

Appetite stimulation is another well-documented effect, making Biscotti useful for patients experiencing nausea or reduced appetite from chemotherapy, HIV/AIDS treatment, or eating disorders. The dessert-like flavor can make consumption more palatable for patients with taste alterations.

Patients with treatment-resistant anxiety may find temporary relief from the initial cerebral euphoria, though high-THC strains can paradoxically worsen anxiety in some individuals. New patients should approach Biscotti with caution due to its potency.

## Notable Phenotypes and Crosses

Several distinct phenotypes of Biscotti exist in circulation, varying in color expression (green vs. deep purple), terpene dominance (sweet vs. gassy), and effect profile. The most commercially prevalent phenotype is the purple-leaning cut that produces the visually striking dark buds favored by the exotic market.

Biscotti has become one of the most prolific parent strains in modern cannabis breeding. Notable derivative crosses include:

- **Biscotti Sherbet** (Biscotti × Sherbet BX1) — amplifies the creamy dessert terpenes.
- **Scotty 2 Hotty** (Biscotti × The Menthol) — adds cooling mint notes to the cookie base.
- **Biscotti Pancakes** (Biscotti × London Pound Cake) — increases yield and adds buttery sweetness.
- **Redemption** (Biscotti × Pink Rozay) — a floral, fruity variation with balanced hybrid effects.
