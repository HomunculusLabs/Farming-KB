## Bobinzana (Calliandra angustifolia)

## Description

Bobinzana (Calliandra angustifolia, also spelled *bobinsana*) is a medicinal plant widely used in Amazonian vegetalismo as an admixture to ayahuasca brews. It is a shrubby tree that grows along riverbanks in the Amazon basin, recognized for its pink powder-puff flowers. In vegetalista tradition, the plant's spirit appears as a woman called Bobinzana-sisa (bobinzana flower), and the plant is valued for its ability to strengthen and deepen the purgative effects of ayahuasca.
