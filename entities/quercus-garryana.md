## Quercus garryana

## Overview

**Quercus garryana** Douglas ex Hook., commonly known as Oregon white oak or Garry oak, is a medium-sized deciduous tree native to western [[pseudotsuga-menziesii]]) encroachment), **agricultural conversion** (particularly in the Willamette Valley), **urban development**, and **invasive species** (Scotch broom, Himalayan blackberry, English ivy, and introduced grasses).

Recovery efforts include prescribed burning programs, invasive species removal, oak planting and regeneration projects, and conservation easements on private lands. Several organizations, including the Garry Oak Ecosystems Recovery Team (GOERT) in British Columbia, coordinate restoration across the species' range.
