# Plantago major

## Overview

Broadleaf plantain (*Plantago major*) is a perennial herbaceous plant in the family
Plantaginaceae, native to Europe and northern and central Asia but now naturalized
worldwide. Often dismissed as a ubiquitous lawn weed, it is one of the most
medicinally significant plants in both Western herbalism and traditional medicine
systems globally. Every part of the plant has documented uses — leaves for wound
healing and respiratory complaints, seeds as a mucilaginous laxative, and roots for
toothache relief. In [[fukuoka-citrus-orchard-ground-cover-cultivation]], and indicator species for compacted soils. Its
deep taproot mines calcium, magnesium, sulfur, and [[agaricus-campestris-biology-and-life-cycle]]:** Perennial; hardy to USDA zones 3–12
- **Growth habit:** Basal rosette, leaves radiating from a central crown at
  ground level; flowering spikes rise 10–40 cm tall
- **Leaves:** Broad, oval to elliptic, 5–30 cm long and 4–13 cm wide, with 5–7
  prominent parallel veins, smooth or slightly toothed margins, and a distinct
  petiole
- **Flowers:** Dense greenish-brown spike on a leafless scape; flowers are tiny,
  wind-pollinated, blooming April–October
- **Seeds:** Small, dark brown, contained in a dehiscent capsule; each plant
  produces 10,000–20,000 seeds

## Distribution and Habitat

- **Native range:** Europe, temperate Asia
- **Introduced range:** Global — found on every continent except Antarctica
- **Habitat:** Lawns, roadsides, meadows, pastures, disturbed ground, compacted
  soils, sidewalk cracks, path edges
- **Soil preference:** Highly adaptable; thrives in compacted, nitrogen-rich soils
  but tolerates poor, acidic, and alkaline conditions
- **Indicator value:** Presence often indicates compacted soil; vigorous growth
  suggests [[nitrogen-availability-in-legumes]]

## Active Constituents

- **Iridoid glycosides:** Aucubin (primary), catalpol — anti-inflammatory,
  hepatoprotective, and wound-healing properties
- **Mucilage:** Galacturonic acid, rhamnose, galactose — soothing to mucous
  membranes, demulcent action
- **Flavonoids:** Apigenin, baicalein, luteolin, hispidulin — antioxidant and
  anti-inflammatory
- **Tannins:** Astringent, reduce secretions, tighten tissues
- **Allantoin:** Cell proliferant, promotes tissue regeneration in wounds
- **Vitamins and minerals:** Rich in vitamins A, C, and K; contains calcium,
  iron, zinc, potassium
- **Plantamajoside:** Phenylethanoid glycoside with documented antioxidant and
  anti-inflammatory activity
