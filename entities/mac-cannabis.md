# MAC (Miracle Alien Cookies)

**MAC**, also known as **Miracle Alien Cookies**, is a premium cannabis hybrid strain renowned for its exceptional [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], complex terpene profile, and potent effects. Created by the breeder **Capulator** of **Alien Genetics**, MAC has become one of the most sought-after and commercially successful modern cannabis cultivars, serving as a foundational parent for numerous subsequent crosses.

## Genetic Lineage

MAC is a cross between two legendary cultivars:

- **Female parent: Alien Cookies** — itself a cross of **Girl Scout Cookies × Alien Kush**, contributing cookie-family sweetness, dense bud structure, and high resin content
- **Male parent: Miracle 15 (Colombian × Starfighter × Big Bud)** — contributing increased vigor, yield, unique flavor depth, and enhanced trichome production

This combination produced a strain that expresses the best traits of both lineages: the cookie-dough sweetness and bag appeal of Alien Cookies merged with the exotic complexity and yield potential of the Miracle 15 background.

### Notable Descendant Strains

MAC has been extensively used as a breeding parent, giving rise to numerous popular cultivars:

- **MAC 1** (Miracle Alien Cookies #1) — a selected pheno of the original MAC cross, considered by many to be the superior expression; more stable, with heavier yields and a more refined terpene profile
- **MAC #10** — MAC crossed with an unknown strain, known for intensified colors
- **Miracle Mints** — MAC × SinMint Cookies
- **Alien Mints** — MAC × Thin Mint GSC
- **Cap Junky** — MAC × Alien Kush; extremely high THC and resin content
- **Mochi** (Gelato #33 × MAC) — dessert-like flavor, heavy trichome coverage
- **Permanent Marker** — MAC × Sherb Bx — complex solvent-like aroma
- **Jealousy** — Sherb Bx × MAC — intensely colorful, dessert-forward

## Appearance

MAC buds are visually striking and immediately recognizable:

- **Structure:** Dense, chunky, and tightly packed calyxes with moderate stretching
- **Color:** Light to medium green with occasional flashes of deep violet, especially when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering; some phenotypes display striking pinkish-purple hues
- **Trichomes:** Extraordinarily heavy — a defining characteristic; buds appear coated in a thick layer of milky-white to amber glandular trichomes, giving them a frosted, almost powdered sugar appearance
- **Pistils:** Abundant, long, orange to rust-colored stigmas winding through the bud structure
- **Leaf:** Broad indica-dominant fan leaves during [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]

## Aroma and Terpene Profile

MAC's terpene profile is complex and distinctive, blending sweet, funky, and floral notes:

- **Dominant terpenes:**
  - **Limonene** — citrus brightness, mood elevation
  - **Caryophyllene** — peppery spice, potential anti-inflammatory
  - **Myrcene** — earthy, herbal undertone; contributes to sedative body effects
- **Secondary terpenes:**
  - **Linalool** — floral, lavender-like sweetness
  - **Pinene** — subtle pine notes, mental clarity
  - **Humulene** — earthy, woody depth
- **Aroma description:** A layered bouquet of **sour citrus, diesel fuel, sweet dough, and floral notes** with an underlying earthy musk. The scent is often described as a combination of orange peel, cookie dough, and skunky funk
- **Flavor:** Smooth on the inhale with sweet, cookie-like notes; expanding into sour citrus and diesel on the exhale, with a lingering floral aftertaste

## Cannabinoid Profile

MAC is classified as a **high-THC** strain:

- **THC:** Typically 20–23%, with select phenos and crosses (especially Cap Junky) testing as high as 30–35%
- **CBD:** Generally low (< 1%)
- **THC:CBD ratio:** Very high — predominantly psychoactive

## Effects

MAC delivers a well-rounded experience that bridges cerebral stimulation and physical relaxation:

- **Onset:** Relatively quick, typically felt within 5–10 minutes
- **Initial effects:** Euphoric cerebral uplift, enhanced creativity, heightened sensory perception, and sociable mood
- **Middle phase:** Warm, relaxing body buzz that spreads from the head downward without causing heavy sedation
- **Late phase:** Gentle physical relaxation that may lead to couch-lock in higher doses
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours depending on tolerance and dosage

### Reported Therapeutic Applications

- **Stress and anxiety relief** — balanced calming without excessive sedation
- **Depression** — mood-elevating euphoric properties
- **Chronic pain** — moderate analgesic body effects
- **Fatigue** — initial energizing cerebral stimulation
- **Appetite stimulation** — mild to moderate munchies
- **Insomnia** — higher doses may promote sleep onset

### Potential Side Effects

- Dry mouth and dry eyes (common)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Paranoid or anxious feelings in sensitive individuals
- Lethargy with heavy consumption

## Cultivation

### Growing Difficulty: Moderate

MAC is suitable for intermediate to experienced growers:

- **Flowering time:** 8–10 weeks (9 weeks average)
- **Yield:** Moderate to high (350–450 g/m² indoors)
- **Indoor growing:**
  - Responds well to SCROG (Screen of Green) and LST (Low Stress Training)
  - Prefers moderate nutrient levels — avoid overfeeding, especially nitrogen during late flower
  - Benefits from cooler nighttime temperatures (18–20°C) in weeks 5–8 to enhance anthocyanin production and color development
  - Requires good air circulation due to dense bud structure (mold risk)
- **Outdoor growing:**
  - Harvest: early to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere)
  - Prefers warm, Mediterranean-like climate
  - Medium to tall height (120–180 cm); may require support for heavy colas
- **Resin production:** Exceptional — makes excellent candidates for solventless extraction (rosin, ice water hash) and hydrocarbon extraction
- **[[hamilton-pest-and-disease-control-organic]] resistance:** Moderate; dense buds require attention to [[stamets-cultivator-humidity-management-fogging-systems]]

## History

MAC was created by **Capulator**, a breeder known for his work with Alien Genetics. The strain gained widespread recognition in the **2016–2018 period** when it won multiple awards at cannabis competitions across [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]]. MAC 1, a specific phenotype selected by Capulator himself, became particularly prized for its superior expression of the original cross.

The strain's commercial success helped establish the "cookie family" lineage as one of the most commercially important genetic groups in modern [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]], alongside Kush, Haze, and Skunk families.
