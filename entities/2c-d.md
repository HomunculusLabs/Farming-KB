## Overview

**2C-D** (2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylphenethylamine) is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine and a member of the 2C family of compounds.

It was first synthesized by [[hofmann-lsd-clinical-research-early-studies]] settings.

Its [[serotonin]] 5-HT₂A receptors, the primary receptor responsible for classical psychedelic effects.

It also shows affinity for 5-HT₂C and 5-HT₁A receptors, contributing to its anxiolytic and mood-modulating properties.

Compared to 2C-B and 2C-I, 2C-D has significantly lower 5-HT₂A receptor affinity, correlating with its milder and more controllable subjective effects.

The compound's pharmacokinetic profile includes rapid oral absorption with peak plasma concentrations within 60–90 minutes.

Metabolism occurs via hepatic monoamine oxidase (MAO) and cytochrome P450 enzymes, with N-dealkylation and O-demethylation as primary pathways.

The elimination half-life is estimated at 3–5 hours based on subjective effect duration data.

## Effects

**Onset:** 45–90 minutes after oral administration.

**Duration:** 4–6 hours at common doses, with aftereffects persisting for 1–2 additional hours.

**Dose ranges (oral):**

- **Threshold:** 5–10 mg
- **Light:** 10–20 mg
- **Common:** 20–40 mg
- **Strong:** 40–60 mg
- **Heavy:** 60+ mg

2C-D is characterized by mild visual effects including color enhancement, subtle geometric patterning, and increased [[mckenna-roland-fischer-psilocybin-visual-acuity]].

The cognitive effects are often described as clear-headed and analytical, with enhanced [[pattern-recognition-and-geometric-garden-design-permaculture]] creative thinking.

Users frequently report increased verbal fluency, social ease, and a sense of emotional openness without intense empathogenic effects.

Physical effects include mild stimulation, pupil dilation, and increased tactile sensitivity.

The compound is notably less likely to produce anxiety, confusion, or overwhelming experiences compared to more potent 2C compounds.

At higher doses, visual effects intensify and may include open-eye patterning, while the clear-headed quality is partially diminished.
