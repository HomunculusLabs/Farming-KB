## Description

The [[subsurface-irrigation]] strategies that combat the clothesline effect also create optimal [[outdoor-mushroom-cultivation]] beds in arid regions.

## Design Implications

- Perimeter planting is prioritized over internal windbreaks for maximum clothesline effect mitigation.
- Tree belts must be continuous — gaps create focused desiccation channels.
- Species selection for perimeter belts should emphasize hardy, dense-canopied, drought-adapted trees.
- *Prosopis*, *Casuarina*, Roman cypress, and *Acacia* spp. are recommended for high windbreak in dryland perimeters.
- Internal fields can then be trellised or overshaded with leguminous trees for additional oasis effect mitigation.
