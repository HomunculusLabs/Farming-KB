# BOB

BOB (beta-Methoxy-2C-B; 4-Bromo-2,5,beta-trimethoxyphenethylamine) is a psychedelic phenethylamine with a methoxy group on the beta-carbon of the ethylamine chain.

It is documented as entry #13 in PiHKAL by [[alexander-shulgin]].

BOB is a member of the BOX family of compounds (beta-OXy phenethylamines).

## Chemical Identity

Full chemical name: 4-Bromo-2,5,beta-Trimethoxyphenethylamine.

BOB is 2c-b-entity|2C-B with an additional methoxy group on the beta-carbon of the ethylamine side chain.

The beta-methoxy substitution creates a chiral center at the beta-carbon, meaning the compound exists as two enantiomers that have not been separately resolved.

The hydrochloride salt melts at 187-188 degrees Celsius as fine white crystals.

## Dosage and Duration

Effective oral dosage: 10 - 20 mg.

Duration of effects: 10 - 20 hours.

BOB is the most potent member of the BOX family.

The duration is notably long, significantly exceeding that of 2c-b-entity|2C-B (4-8 hours).

## Qualitative Effects

At 10 mg, effects are polarized between negative and positive reports.

One user reported "a granddaddy of a paranoid, sociopathic snit, without feeling and without emotion" with indifference to everything.

Another user at 10 mg reported initially retreating into garden work, followed by "a magnificent vegetable garden, in Southern France, in my mind's eye" with an "extraordinary zucchini."

The positive phase was followed by "neurological over-stimulation" requiring phenobarbital to manage.

At 15 mg, the experience is described as initially positive with "lustful" erotic qualities, but neurological instability becomes apparent.

Tinnitus was described as "quite bad" and the excitement of the altered state was "certainly worth it all."

Later phases were characterized by exhaustion, fear of sleep, and concern about body integrity.

The overall assessment was that the first part "might have been hard to define, but it was quite positive" but the last part "makes it not really worthwhile."

## The BOX Family

BOB belongs to the BOX series, which places an oxygen atom on the beta-carbon of the phenethylamine side chain.

The BOX family includes BOB (beta-methoxy-2c-b-entity|2C-B), BOD (beta-methoxy-2c-d-entity|2C-D), BOH (beta-hydroxy-2c-b-entity|2C-B), and BOM (beta-methoxy-mescaline-entity|mescaline).

All members of the BOX family share the characteristic of producing "overtones of physical concern" and "some worry as to the integrity of the body."

Shulgin noted that "they can't all be winners" regarding the physical side effects of the BOX series.

## Pharmacological Context

The beta-methoxy group creates a fundamentally different pharmacological profile from the parent phenethylamines.

The additional oxygen atom may influence metabolic stability, [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]], and blood-brain barrier penetration.

The physical concerns consistently reported across the BOX series suggest that the beta-methoxy group may introduce cardiovascular or neurological effects not seen in the parent compounds.

The long duration (10-20 hours) may reflect slower metabolic processing of the beta-substituted compound.

## Unexplored Directions

Shulgin posed several questions about unexplored directions for the BOX family.

Compounds with sulfur instead of oxygen at the beta-position remain unexplored.

Amphetamine derivatives with an alpha-methyl group alongside the beta-oxygen are unknown.

The [[optical-isomers-psychedelic-phenethylamines]] (threo- and erythro- forms) have not been separated and individually tested.

The question of how stereochemistry affects the activity of beta-substituted phenethylamines remains open.

## Harm Reduction

The physical concerns reported across the BOX family are a significant safety consideration.

Neurological overstimulation, tinnitus, and fear of sleep loss have been reported.

The 10-20 hour duration requires planning for a full day of altered consciousness.

Reports of needing phenobarbital to manage overstimulation suggest the compound can produce distressing physical effects.

The compound should not be combined with other stimulants or serotonergic substances.
