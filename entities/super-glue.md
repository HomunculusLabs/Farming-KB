## Overview

Super Glue is an indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain created by crossing GG4 (formerly [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]] sedative effects of its famous parent.

The strain is named for its extraordinary trichome density, which gives buds a sticky, glue-like texture that clings to fingers and scissors. This naming convention follows GG Strains" tradition of adhesive-themed nomenclature, which also includes GG4 and related glue-themed crosses.

Since its release in the mid-2010s, Super Glue has won multiple awards including first place in the 2016 Seattle Hempfest. It is widely regarded as one of the most potent indica-dominant strains in the commercial market, with THC content consistently testing between 25 and 31 percent.

Super Glue has gained particular popularity in the [[cannabis-hashish-production]] for centuries. This genetic heritage is evident in Super Glue's exceptionally high resin output and the deep, earthy undertones that dominate its flavor profile.

## Appearance

Buds are large, dense, and chunky with a classic indica structure. The calyxes are tightly packed and heavily coated in milky-white trichomes that extend to the sugar leaves and even the stems.

This extreme glandular coverage creates the adhesive quality referenced in the strain's name.

Coloration ranges from deep forest green to olive, with occasional purple hues appearing in cooler growing conditions. Pistils are a vibrant orange-amber, contrasting sharply against the dark green foliage.

The resin coverage is among the heaviest of any commercial strain, with glandular trichomes clearly visible to the naked eye even at arm's length. When handled, buds leave a sticky residue on fingers that persists through multiple hand washings. The trichome heads are large and bulbous, often exhibiting a slight amber tint even at harvest, suggesting peak cannabinoid maturation.

Visual appeal is a significant factor in the strain's market success, with well-grown specimens presenting a striking contrast between the dark foliage, bright orange pistils, and frosty white trichome layer. This combination creates a highly photogenic product that performs well in cannabis competitions and retail displays.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is pungent and multifaceted, combining elements of diesel fuel, pine, and earthy chocolate from the GG4 lineage with hashy, peppery notes from the Afghani parent.

When ground, the bouquet intensifies significantly, releasing additional scents of coffee and dark soil. The fragrance fills a room quickly and lingers for hours after the container is sealed, making discreet storage challenging.

Odor control during cultivation is a significant consideration, as Super Glue produces exceptionally pungent terpenes throughout the flowering cycle. Carbon filtration systems rated for 400+ CFM are recommended for indoor grows, and even then, some aroma escape is typical during weeks 5 through 8 of flowering.

The flavor mirrors the aroma closely, with a dominant diesel and pine profile on the inhale. The exhale brings forward chocolate, hash, and a subtle sweetness that lingers on the palate. Smoke is thick and expansive, characteristic of resin-heavy indica strains. The aftertaste is long-lasting, with earthy and peppery notes persisting for several minutes.

When consumed through a vaporizer, the flavor profile shifts noticeably, with the diesel and chocolate notes becoming more pronounced while the hashy undertones recede. This makes Super Glue a popular choice for dry herb vaporizer enthusiasts who appreciate complex terpene expression.

## Terpene and Cannabinoid Profile

The dominant terpenes are myrcene (0.8 to 1.5 percent), caryophyllene (0.4 to 0.8 percent), and limonene (0.2 to 0.5 percent). Secondary terpenes include pinene, humulene, and linalool in smaller concentrations.

THC content ranges from 25 to 31 percent, placing it among the highest-potency strains commercially available. CBD content is typically below 0.1 percent. Minor cannabinoids present include CBC and CBG in trace amounts.
