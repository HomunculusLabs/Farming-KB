## Description

The flat bed is the standard [[ridge-bed-system]].
- Requires less labor in construction and subsequent care than ridge beds.
- Surface area is slightly less than an equal space devoted to the ridge bed system.
- Can be sided with boards to encourage formation of heavier mushrooms.
- Adaptable to shelf-bed systems with multiple tiers, multiplying available space 4-5 times.
- Multi-tier shelf systems sacrifice some convenience but reduce facility investment per unit of bed space.
- The sided flat bed is the primary type of interest to American commercial growers.
- Compression should be firm enough to support a person sinking no more than 1-2 inches.
