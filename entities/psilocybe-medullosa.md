# Psilocybe medullosa

**Psilocybe medullosa** is a rare, little-known species of [[spore]] maturity
- Gills are moderately crowded

### Stipe (Stem)
- Cylindrical, relatively slender
- Surface may be slightly silky to fibrillose
- Color similar to cap or paler, sometimes bruising bluish at the base

### Spores
- Spore print dark purplish-brown to nearly black
- Spores ellipsoid to subellipsoid in face view
- Smooth to finely roughened ornamentation

## Distribution and Habitat

Psilocybe medullosa has been reported from European locales, typically in temperate forest environments. It is a saprotrophic species found on decaying plant material, rich soil, and occasionally in association with forest floor litter. The species is considered rare with very few confirmed collections.

## Chemistry

Like other Psilocybe species, P. medullosa is presumed to contain psilocybin and [[psilocybe-fuscoflocculosa]] — similar species found in neotropical regions
