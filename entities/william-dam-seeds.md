## Description

William Dam Seeds is a Canadian family-owned seed company based in Dundas, Ontario. Established in 1952, it is known for offering untreated, non-GMO seeds and for its commitment to organic and sustainable gardening practices. Referenced by Steve Solomon as a recommended seed source in *[[gardening-when-it-counts]]*.

## Classification

- **Type:** Organization (seed company)
- **Location:** Dundas, Ontario, Canada
- **Founded:** 1952
- **Industry:** Horticultural seeds

## Key Facts

- Family-owned and operated seed company with over 70 years of history
- One of the first Canadian seed companies to commit to offering only untreated (no chemical seed treatments) seeds
- Strong advocate for organic gardening and sustainable agriculture
- Catalog includes vegetables, flowers, herbs, and heirloom varieties
- Referenced on page 113 of *Gardening When It Counts* as a recommended source for quality seeds
- Known for detailed growing instructions and cultural information in their catalog
- Maintains trial gardens to evaluate variety performance under real growing conditions
