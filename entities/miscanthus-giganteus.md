# Miscanthus giganteus

## Overview

*Miscanthus × giganteus* (giant miscanthus) is a sterile, triploid hybrid grass resulting from a cross between *Miscanthus sinensis* and *Miscanthus sacchariflorus*. It is one of the most productive C₄ perennial grasses in temperate climates, capable of producing 15–25 tonnes of dry matter per hectare annually.

Originally developed in Japan, it has become the leading dedicated bioenergy crop in Europe and is increasingly adopted in [[dighton-fungal-biomass-production-aquatic-ecosystems]], [[agroforestry]] systems as a fast-growing understory or boundary species.

The dried stems are useful as mulch, [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]].
