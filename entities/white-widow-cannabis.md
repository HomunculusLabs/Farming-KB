## Morphology and Growing Characteristics

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Plant type | Indica-dominant hybrid, photoperiod-dependent |
| Height | 80–140 cm indoors; 150–200 cm outdoors |
| Structure | Branching, medium density; somewhat open structure allowing good light penetration |
| Leaf shape | Broad-fingered indica leaves with some sativa elongation |
| Flowering time | 8–9 weeks (56–63 days) |
| Yield | 400–500 g/m² indoors; 500–600 g/plant outdoors |
| Harvest month (outdoor) | Late September to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere) |
| Resin production | Exceptional — buds become thickly coated with trichomes by week 6 of flowering |
| Difficulty | Easy to moderate; forgiving for beginners; resistant to common pests and molds |

White Widow is considered an excellent **beginner-friendly strain** due to its vigor, disease resistance, and consistent performance. It responds well to both SOG ([[sea-of-green]]) and ScrOG ([[screen-of-green]]) [[mckenna-shamanic-identification-signs-and-training-methods]]. The plants exhibit moderate stretch during the first two weeks of flowering before settling into bud production. Trichome density increases dramatically in the final two weeks, giving the mature buds their signature white appearance.

## Cannabinoid Profile

Modern White Widow selections typically express the following cannabinoid profile:

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range |
|-------------|---------------|
| **THC** | 18–25% (some selections up to 28%) |
| **CBD** | 0.1–1.0% |
| **CBG** | 0.3–0.8% |
| **CBC** | 0.1–0.3% |
| **THCV** | Trace amounts |

The original 1990s White Widow tested at approximately 12–15% THC. Modern seed bank selections have been bred for significantly higher potency, consistent with the overall trend of increasing cannabis THC content documented in research. The THC:CBD ratio is typically >20:1, placing this firmly in the high-THC, low-CBD category.

## Terpene Profile

White Widow's aroma and flavor are driven by a distinctive terpene combination:

| Terpene | Typical % | Aroma Contribution |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Myrcene** | 0.4–0.8% | Earthy, musky, herbal base note; sedative properties |
| **Pinene (α+β)** | 0.2–0.5% | Pine, fresh forest; mental clarity, bronchodilator |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.1–0.3% | Peppery, spicy; anti-inflammatory, CB2 receptor agonist |
| **Limonene** | 0.1–0.2% | Citrus, lemon; mood elevation, anxiolytic |
| **Humulene** | 0.05–0.15% | Woody, earthy; appetite suppressant |
| **Linalool** | Trace | Floral, lavender; calming, anxiolytic |

The overall aroma is **earthy and pungent** with pine undertones and a subtle woody, floral quality. When combusted, the flavor is smooth with a slightly spicy, peppery finish. The aroma intensifies significantly as buds cure, with well-cured White Widow developing a richer, more complex scent profile.
