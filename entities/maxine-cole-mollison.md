# Maxine Cole

## Description

Maxine Cole is a permaculture practitioner and collaborator who co-compiled the Resource Index for Bioregions with Bill Mollison for the Northern Rivers Bioregional Association of New South Wales, Australia. This index is presented in Chapter 14 of *Permaculture: A Designers' Manual* as a comprehensive template for [[bioregional-resource-index-permaculture]] inventory.

## Classification

- **Category**: Person — Permaculture practitioner and bioregional organiser
- **Region**: Northern Rivers, New South Wales, Australia
- **Association**: Northern Rivers Bioregional Association

## Key Facts

- Co-developed the [[bioregional-resource-index]], a systematic catalogue of regional resources organised into ten major categories (food, shelter, livelihoods, information, community, social life, health, future trends, transport, and appendices).
- The Resource Index was designed to be maintained as a looseleaf notebook or computer database, with local volunteers entering information page by page.
- Each category contains detailed sub-listings; for example, the food category alone covers plant resources (13 subcategories), animal resources (11 subcategories), [[integrated-pest-management]] (6 subcategories), food processing (5 subcategories), markets (9 subcategories), and support functions (9 subcategories).
- The index emphasises practical resources: people, skills, machinery, services, and biological products essential to regional self-reliance.
- Criteria for inclusion focus on conservation of resources, regional cash flow retention, settlement survival, employment, and community security.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- Cole's Resource Index template provides a practical framework for any bioregion to inventory its cultivation and mycological resources — from nurseries and seed exchanges to mycorrhizal inoculant suppliers [[pda-and-tissue-culture]] facilities.
- The index explicitly includes mycorrhiza and inoculants as Category A1 resources under plant propagation.
- Soil, water, and leaf analysis services for micronutrients and soil amendments are included as critical support functions, directly relevant to cultivation and soil mycology.
- The model encourages documenting skilled people (botanists, horticulturists, natural historians) as regional assets — a knowledge-preservation strategy that supports both cultivation and mycological expertise.

## Context in Permaculture Design

Cole's contribution represents the practical implementation arm of [[bioregional-organisation-mollison]] theory — translating Mollison's philosophical framework into an actionable resource survey methodology that any community can adopt and adapt.
