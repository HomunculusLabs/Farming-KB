# Purple Kush

## Overview

Purple Kush is a pure indica cannabis strain from Oakland, California,
renowned for deep purple coloration, heavy body effects, and sweet
earthy aroma.

A cross between Purple Afghani and Hindu Kush — two landrace indica
lines from the Hindu Kush mountains — it won first place in the Indica
category at the 2010 High Times Cannabis Cup.

## Lineage

- **Female parent:** Purple Afghani (Afghanistan landrace indica)
- **Male parent:** Hindu Kush (Afghanistan/Pakistan landrace indica)
- **Genetic ratio:** Approximately 100% indica
- Both parents trace to the Hindu Kush range, making this a nearly
pure landrace cross.

## Appearance

Buds are dense, compact, and tightly coiled in classic indica
structure.

Calyxes display vivid purple to deep violet hues, especially when
exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures during late flowering.

Pistils are dark orange to rust-colored against the purple foliage.

Trichome coverage is heavy, giving buds a frosted appearance.

## Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma

- **Primary:** Sweet grape and berry.
- **Secondary:** Earthy, hash-like undertones (from Hindu Kush).
- **Tertiary:** Subtle sandalwood and spice.

### Flavor

Sweet, fruity inhale with grape and wild berry notes.
Smooth, earthy exhale with hash-like qualities.
Slight skunky aftertaste on the finish.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene (dominant):** 0.5–1.2% — sedative, earthy aroma.
- **Caryophyllene:** 0.2–0.5% — peppery, anti-inflammatory.
- **Linalool:** 0.1–0.3% — floral, calming, anxiolytic.
- **Pinene (beta):** Trace — pine, counteracts THC amnesia.
- **Humulene:** Trace — woody, appetite suppressant.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 17–22% (average ~19%)
- **CBD:** 0.05–0.2% (negligible)
- **CBN:** 0.1–0.3%
- **THCV:** Trace to 0.1%

Low CBD is typical of pure Kush varieties selected for psychoactive
potency rather than therapeutic CBD ratios.

## Effects

### Onset and Progression

- **Onset:** Rapid, 2–5 minutes after inhalation.
- **Peak:** 30–60 minutes.
- **Duration:** 2–3 hours.
- **Progression:** Cerebral euphoria transitions quickly into deep
physical relaxation, then heavy sedation.

### Characteristic Effects

- Deep physical relaxation and body melt.
- Strong sedation ("couch-lock").
- Euphoric, dreamy mental state.
- Increased appetite, pain relief, muscle tension reduction.

## Medical Applications

- **Insomnia:** One of the most effective strains for sleep onset;
potent sedative makes it a popular nighttime medicine.
- **Chronic pain:** Full-body analgesic for fibromyalgia, neuropathy,
and musculoskeletal pain.
- **Muscle spasms:** Anti-spasmodic; aids multiple sclerosis patients.
- **Anxiety and stress:** Indica profile promotes physical calm.
- **Appetite stimulation:** Effective for chemotherapy-induced nausea.

## Side Effects

- **Common:** Dry mouth, dry eyes, drowsiness.
- **Moderate:** Dizziness at higher doses; paranoia in sensitive
individuals.
- **Rare:** Paradoxical anxiety at high doses.

Not recommended for daytime use. Novice consumers should start with low
doses due to potent sedation.

## Cultivation

- **Flowering time:** 8–9 weeks indoors; late September to mid-October
outdoors.
- **Yield:** Moderate, 300–400 g/m² indoors.
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate; pest and mold resistant.
- **Height:** Short and bushy, 60–100 cm indoors.
- **Training:** Responds well to LST and ScrOG.

Purple coloration is induced by anthocyanin accumulation triggered by
cool nighttime temperatures (below 15°C) during the final 2–3 weeks of
flowering.

## Notable Derivatives

- **Granddaddy Purple (GDP):** Purple Kush × Big Bud; one of the most
famous purple strains worldwide.
- **Purple Urkle:** Related phenotype from the same Northern California
genetic pool; often cited as a sibling line.
- **Purple Kush × OG Kush crosses:** Multiple breeders have combined
these for increased potency.

## Related Strains

- **Hindu Kush:** Parent; pure landrace with earthy, hashy profile.
- **Purple Afghani:** Parent; Afghani landrace with purple traits.
- **Granddaddy Purple:** Notable descendant with sweeter profile.
- **Bubba Kush:** Related Kush lineage with similar sedative effects.
- **Northern Lights:** Comparable pure indica with similar therapeutic
applications.
