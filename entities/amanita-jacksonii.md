## Amanita jacksonii

**Amanita jacksonii**, commonly known as the **American caesar's mushroom** or **Jackson's amanita**, is a basidiomycete fungus in the family Amanitaceae.
It is a strikingly beautiful [[polypore-fungi-eastern-north-america]] America, closely related to the European *[[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] fungi.
*Amanita jacksonii* is widely regarded as one of the finest [[north-american-dmt-containing-plants-phalaris-desmanthus-peganum]] forests, though it requires careful identification to distinguish from toxic *Amanita* species.

The species was originally described by Charles Horton Peck in 1908 as *Amanitopsis jacksonii*, before being transferred to the genus *Amanita* by Émile-Jean Gilbert in 1941.
Some authorities have treated it as a variety or subspecies of *[[amanita-caesarea]]*, but molecular phylogenetic studies have confirmed it as a distinct species endemic to [[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-north-america]].
It belongs to *Amanita* section Caesareae, which contains several prized edible species worldwide.

## Description

### Cap

- 5–15 cm diameter, convex when young, expanding to broadly convex or plane with age
- Surface smooth, dry to slightly tacky; deep red-orange to bright orange-yellow
- Margin typically lined/striate in mature specimens; universal veil patches sometimes present

### Gills

- Free from stipe, crowded to moderately close, white to creamy yellow
- Edges often floccose or slightly fringed; lamellulae of varying lengths truncate

### Stipe

- 8–18 cm long, 1–3 cm thick, central, cylindrical or slightly tapering upward
- White to pale yellow, smooth above annulus; scaly/cottony patches below
- Bulbous base with concentric rings of universal veil remnants
- Flesh white, firm, not changing color when cut

### Annulus and Volva

- Annulus membranous, persistent, white to pale yellow, hanging skirt-like
- Volva as concentric rings or zones at bulbous base, white to cream-colored

### Flesh and Odor

- Flesh white throughout, firm and dense
- Odor mild, pleasant, sometimes slightly fruity
- Taste mild and agreeable (edible species)
- Does not stain or discolor when cut or bruised
