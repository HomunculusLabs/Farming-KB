## Distribution and Habitat

psilocybe natmansii is known from scattered locations in northwestern Europe, with confirmed collections from the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, and possibly the British Isles. It is a rare species with a fragmented distribution pattern, likely reflecting both genuine rarity and under-collection due to confusion with more common look-alikes.

The species is found in open grassland habitats, including pastures, meadows, and grassy coastal dunes. It is associated with nutrient-poor to moderately rich soils, often in areas grazed by livestock or in semi-natural grasslands. Unlike some related species, it does not appear to be strongly associated with dung but rather with grassland soils rich in decaying plant material.

Fruiting occurs in late summer to autumn, typically from August through November. The species benefits from cool, damp conditions and may fruit in successive flushes following rainfall.
