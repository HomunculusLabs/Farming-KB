> "The five flavors enter the five organs: sour enters the liver, bitter enters the heart, sweet enters the spleen, acrid enters the lungs, and salty enters the kidneys." — *Bencao Gangmu* (Li Shizhen, 1596)

## Etymology and Common Names

- **Wu Wei Zi** (五味子) — Chinese, "five-flavor fruit"
- **Five-Flavor Berry** — English translation of the Chinese name
- **Omija** (오미자) — Korean, from Sino-Korean roots
- **Gomishi** (ゴミシ) — Japanese
- **Limonnik** (лимонник) — Russian, "lemon tree"
- **Northern Magnolia Vine** — English vernacular

The genus *Schisandra* derives from Greek *schizein* (to split) + *andros* (man), referring to cleft anthers. Epithet *chinensis* denotes Chinese origin.

## Morphology

Deciduous woody climbing vine, 8–10 m long. Functionally dioecious; occasional monoecious individuals.

| Structure | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Stem | Twining, woody, brown bark, 1–3 cm diameter |
| Leaves | Elliptic to obovate, 5–10 cm, serrate margins, alternate |
| Flowers | Dioecious, white to pale pink, 1.5 cm, pendulous |
| Fruit | Bright red drupe, 5–10 mm, in dense pendulous clusters |
| Seeds | Kidney-shaped, 4–5 mm, one per drupe, glossy brown |

Flowers May–June; fruit August–October. Vines live 20+ years.

Native to temperate mixed forests at 200–1700 m. Grows along forest margins, stream banks, and woodland clearings with dappled light.

## Traditional Use in TCM

One of the 50 fundamental herbs; superior herb in the *Shennong Bencao Jing* (~200 CE).

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Nature | Warm |
| Flavor | Sour (primary), sweet, bitter, acrid, salty |
| Meridians | Liver, heart, kidney, lung |
| Dosage | 2–6 g dried fruit (standard decoction) |

**Primary TCM actions:**
- **Astringent** — stops leakage of qi, sweat, semen, fluids
- **Kidney tonic** — consolidates essence (jing), addresses nocturnal emissions
- **Lung tonic** — calms wheezing, stops chronic cough, retains fluids
- **Calms the shen** — insomnia, irritability, dream-disturbed sleep
- **Adaptogen** — qi tonic restoring depleted vitality
- Indications: chronic cough, [[actinidia-chinensis]]
