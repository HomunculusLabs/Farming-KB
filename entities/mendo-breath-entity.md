## Mendo Breath

## Overview

Mendo Breath is a potent indica-dominant [[girl-scout-cookies]] phenotype) with **Mendo Montage**.

Bred in Mendocino County, California, it has earned a reputation as one of the most sedative and relaxing strains available, frequently recommended for evening and nighttime use.

The strain consistently tests between 19–25% THC, with negligible CBD content, and produces dense, visually striking purple-tinted buds coated in trichomes.

## Lineage

| OGKB (OG Kush Breath) | Indica-dominant hybrid | Female seed parent; GSC phenotype |

OGKB is itself a select phenotype of [[oner-cookie-wreck-trainwreck-girl-scout-cookies-hybrid]], tracing back to OG Kush × [[trichoderma-forest-green-mold-guide]] with prominent purple and violet hues, particularly on the calyxes and sugar leaves exposed to cooler growing temperatures.

The flowers are heavily coated in milky-white trichomes, giving them a frosted, almost sugary appearance.

Bright orange pistils contrast sharply against the dark green and purple background, curling inward as maturity progresses.

The density and weight of individual buds are notable, with a firm, resinous texture that leaves fingers sticky after handling.

## Aroma and Flavor

**Aroma profile:** Rich, sweet, and earthy with prominent notes of caramel, vanilla, and brown sugar.

Secondary notes include damp earth, pine, and a subtle berry undertone that emerges as buds are broken apart.

The aroma is pungent and fills a room quickly, with a sweetness that is immediately noticeable.

**Flavor:** The smoke is smooth and sweet on the inhale, dominated by caramel and vanilla flavors with an earthy, woody undertone.

The exhale reveals a more complex profile with hints of [[comparison-black-pepper-vs-cardamom-tropical-spice-cultivation]], herbs, and a lingering sweet aftertaste reminiscent of butterscotch.

The flavor is consistently described as one of the most dessert-like among indica strains.

## Terpene Profile

The dominant terpenes in Mendo Breath reflect its sweet, sedative character:

- **Caryophyllene:** 0.5–1.2% — Peppery, spicy; contributes anti-inflammatory properties
- **Limonene:** 0.3–0.8% — Citrus notes; mood elevation and stress relief
- **Myrcene:** 1.0–1.5% — Earthy, musky; primary sedative terpene
- **Linalool:** 0.2–0.5% — Floral, lavender; anxiolytic and calming
- **Humulene:** 0.1–0.3% — Woody, earthy; appetite suppression

The high myrcene and linalool content synergizes with THC to produce the strain's characteristic body-heavy, sedative effects.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 19–25% (average ~21%)
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible)
- **CBN:** 0.1–0.3%
- **THCV:** <0.1%
