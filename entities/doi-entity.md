# DOI

**Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine

**PiHKAL #67** | [[dob-compound-profile]], it is one of the most potent orally active psychedelics known. The large iodine atom at the 4-position makes DOI particularly valuable as a radiolabeled research tool for studying [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]] distribution and binding in the human brain.

## Dosage

| Level | Amount (mg) |
|-------|-------------|
| Threshold | 0.5-1.0 |
| Light | 1.0-1.5 |
| Common | 1.5-2.5 |
| Strong | 2.5-3.0 |
| Heavy | 3.0+ |

Shulgin's published range: 1.5-3.0 mg. The "R" isomer is the more active enantiomer; the "S" isomer requires approximately 3-4x the dose for comparable effects.

## Duration

| Phase | Time |
|-------|------|
| Onset | 60-120 min |
| Come-up | 60-120 min |
| Peak | 4-10 h |
| Offset | 10-20 h |
| Total | 16-30 h |

At 16 hours into a 3.0 mg experience, subjects were still at +1.5. Residual effects and imagery can persist well into the following day. Sleep is possible by hour 16-18 at moderate doses.

## Synthesis

DOI was synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine (2,5-DMA) via a phthalimide protection strategy:

1. **Phthalimide protection**: 2,5-DMA (19.5 g) + phthalic anhydride (14.8 g), heated to ~150 °C with open flame. After cooling and crystallization from MeOH: 24.6 g N-(1-(2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-2-propyl)phthalimide as off-white crystals, mp 105-106 °C
2. **ICl iodination**: Phthalimide (2.0 g) dissolved in warm acetic acid, treated with iodine monochloride (1.2 g) in acetic acid, stirred 2 h at ~40 °C. Product isolated as reddish glob, recrystallized from MeOH: 1.5 g N-[1-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodophenyl)-2-propyl]phthalimide as fine white crystals with slight purple cast, mp 103-105.5 °C
3. **Hydrazine deprotection**: Phthalimide (0.75 g) + hydrazine hydrate (0.3 mL) in EtOH, refluxed overnight. 1,4-Dihydroxyphthalizine byproduct removed by filtration. Freebase extracted, converted to HCl salt with anhydrous HCl gas in Et2O: 0.35 g white crystals, mp 200.5-201.5 °C

## Qualitative Effects

- Extremely strong visual effects with intense color enhancement
- Geometric patterning and visual distortion
- Deep cognitive and introspective effects
- Strong time distortion
- Physical tremor possible at moderate doses
- Long-lasting after-effects including altered perception
- Very slow onset with continued building of effects for hours
- Emotional range from euphoria to anxiety depending on dose and set
- At higher doses, fantasy takeover with difficulty distinguishing real from imagined

## Shulgin's Qualitative Comments

At 0.6 mg, a nice spacey light-headedness for a few hours with time moving slowly. A generic sadness emerged, reminiscing about earlier pleasures. Mood became present-day and healthy by about the seventh hour.

At 1.6 mg, the experience was generally depressing with a sad view of life and no connection with emotions. The sadness was vague. At about the ninth hour, this disappointing about-plus-two was aborted with 125 µg LSD, which restored emotional presence within a half hour.

At 3.0 mg, a clear, clean psychedelic with excellent eyes-closed imagery — clearly delineated patterns, pictures, and colors. "Perfect for an artist." Total ease for the body. Still at +1.5 at sixteen hours. At 3.5 mg, a full crashing +++ for 3-4 hours with moments of "light-headedness" allowing sideways movement with reality — not quite out-of-body but a strange, authentic shift of perspective.

**R isomer**: At 1.0 mg, a clear ++ from hours 2-8. At 2.3 mg, unquestionable +++ with slight down-mood towards the end; distinctly aware of residual effects well into the next day. The aqueous HCl solution was noted to have a slightly sweetish taste.

**S isomer**: At 6.3 mg, reached only a benign ++ (versus ~2 mg of racemate for similar level). By evening, near enough baseline to drive, but slow-motion imagery persisted when trying to sleep. Approximately 3-4x less potent than the R isomer — consistent with rat discrimination studies showing ~2x potency difference, though Shulgin's human data suggest the factor is closer to 4x.

## Extensions and Commentary

DOI has an extremely high binding affinity for the [[dob-compound-profile]], [[dom-compound-profile]], and MDA. The only exception noted was MDMA (which is more stimulant than psychedelic).

A number of animal studies with DOI have mapped 5-HT2 receptor subtypes, which have been subdivided based on which compounds interact most tightly with each site. Radiolabeled DOI ([¹²⁵I]-DOI) is used extensively in autoradiography and PET imaging to study serotonin receptor distribution in the brain.

The heavy iodine atom and slow pharmacokinetics contribute to the extremely long duration. As with [[psychedelic-harm-reduction]] for general harm reduction guidelines
- Legal status: Schedule I in the US and most countries
