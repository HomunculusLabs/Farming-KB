## Overview

Afghani is a pure indica [[cervantes-landrace-cannabis-populations]] strain originating from the [[cannabis-hindu-kush-strain-description-clarke]] mountain region spanning Afghanistan, Pakistan, and northern India.

It is one of the most genetically foundational strains in cannabis history, having contributed indica genetics to the vast majority of modern indica-dominant and hybrid cultivars worldwide.

Afghani is renowned for its heavy [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]], [[fukuoka-ideal-rice-plant-morphology-compact-growth-high-yield]] structure, and deeply relaxing sedative effects that typify the classic indica experience.

## Lineage and History

Afghani represents one of the original cannabis indica gene pools, naturally adapted to the harsh, arid conditions of the Hindu Kush mountains at elevations of 1,500–3,000 meters.

The strain was first brought to the West in the 1960s and 1970s by travelers along the Hippie Trail and by collectors working with organizations such as the International Association for Cannabinoid Medicines.

Afghani genetics formed the basis of the first indoor-indica breeding programs in California and the Netherlands during the 1970s and 1980s. Its compact stature and short flowering time made it ideal for [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]].

The strain is the direct ancestor of virtually all modern "Kush" varieties, including [[hindu-kush]], and [[sour-diesel]]. Instead, Afghani presents a rich, natural earthiness that is simultaneously musky and slightly sweet.

Flavor follows the aroma closely, delivering a smooth, hashish-like smoke with earthy and woody notes on the inhale. A slightly sweet, herbal aftertaste lingers on the exhale.

The aroma intensity increases significantly as buds cure and age. Well-cured Afghani develops a complex, layered scent profile that is considered among the most appealing of all indica strains.
