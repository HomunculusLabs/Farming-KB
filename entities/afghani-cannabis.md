## Overview

Afghani is a pure indica [[cannabis-hindu-kush-strain-description-clarke]] mountain region spanning Afghanistan, Pakistan, and northern India.

It is one of the most genetically foundational strains in cannabis history, having contributed indica genetics to the vast majority of modern indica-dominant and hybrid cultivars worldwide.

Afghani is renowned for its heavy [[fukuoka-ideal-rice-plant-morphology-compact-growth-high-yield]] structure, and deeply relaxing sedative effects that typify the classic indica experience.

## Lineage and History

Afghani represents one of the original cannabis indica gene pools, naturally adapted to the harsh, arid conditions of the Hindu Kush mountains at elevations of 1,500–3,000 meters.

The strain was first brought to the West in the 1960s and 1970s by travelers along the Hippie Trail and by collectors working with organizations such as the International Association for Cannabinoid Medicines.
