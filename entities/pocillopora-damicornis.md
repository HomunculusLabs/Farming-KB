## Ecology

Cauliflower coral is a foundational habitat species across its range. Its
dense branching structure shelters a remarkably diverse faunal community,
including Trapezia crabs, xanthid crabs, Alpheus shrimp, and numerous
juvenile reef fish. The obligate coral-dwelling crab genus *Trapezia*
defends *Pocillopora* colonies from crown-of-thorns seastar predation.

The species maintains symbiosis with multiple Symbiodiniaceae clades,
primarily clade C but also clade D (thermally tolerant) in warm-water
environments. This symbiont shuffling capacity contributes to its broader
thermal tolerance compared to more specialist corals.
