# A-Frame Level

## Description

The A-frame level is a simple, handmade surveying instrument used for centuries to lay out contour lines and drains at desired slope or flow rates. It consists of two wooden or metal legs joined at an apex, with a cross-piece fixed about two-thirds down their length, and a plumb bob hanging from the center of the apex. The A-frame is approximately 2 meters wide and can be constructed on-site from basic materials.

## Classification

- **Category:** Surveying instrument / Earthworks tool
- **Type:** Gravity-based leveling device
- **Complexity:** Simple (can be handmade)
- **Accuracy:** Sufficient for contour drainage and earthworks layout

## Construction

Materials needed:
- Two wooden or metal legs (approximately 2 m each)
- One cross-piece fixed about two-thirds down the leg length
- Cord or string
- A plumb bob or weight
- Nails, welding, or glue for joining

Assembly:
1. Nail, weld, or glue the two legs firmly together at the apex
2. Fix the cross-piece about two-thirds down the leg length
3. Attach a cord with a plumb bob at the center of the apex
4. Calibrate by marking the cross-piece where the cord hangs, then reversing the frame and marking again — the midpoint between the two marks is the true level point

## Calibration Method

The A-frame is calibrated by finding the midpoint between two cord positions:
1. Place the A-frame on approximately level ground
2. Mark where the cord crosses the cross-piece (Mark A)
3. Reverse (swap) the legs to the same positions
4. Mark where the cord now crosses the cross-piece (Mark B)
5. The exact midpoint between Mark A and Mark B is the true level position

## Usage Technique

1. Plant one leg firmly on the ground
2. Swivel the frame about that leg
3. Move the second leg until the cord hangs at the calibrated level mark
4. Place a peg at the second leg position
5. Pivot on the second leg and repeat to trace a contour line across the landscape

## Applications in Permaculture Design

- **Contour line layout:** Marking contour lines for swales, terraces, and contour banks
- **Drain alignment:** Setting diversion drains at precise slopes
- **Garden bed layout:** Ensuring level growing beds on slopes
- **Earthworks planning:** Preparing sites for machine operators
- **Access road layout:** Rough surveying of road grades

## Relevance to Cultivation

The A-frame level is one of the most accessible tools for small-scale farmers and permaculture designers. It requires no purchased instruments and can be made from scavenged materials. Many kilometers of productive drains and earthworks have been laid out using A-frames. For permaculture practitioners working in developing regions or on tight budgets, the A-frame provides an essential capability for water management earthworks.

## Construction and Use

A Frame Level is a fundamental surveying tool in permaculture design, used to
establish contour lines for earthworks, swales, and terraces. Simple construction
from locally available materials makes it accessible for small-scale applications.
