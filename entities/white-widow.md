# White Widow (Cannabis Strain)

## Overview

**White Widow** is one of the most iconic and influential cannabis strains in modern cultivation history. A balanced **indica-dominant hybrid** (approximately 60% indica / 40% sativa), White Widow earned its name from the extraordinary density of **trichomes** covering its buds, giving them a frosty white appearance. First bred in the Netherlands in the early 1990s, it became a global benchmark strain that has parented countless modern hybrids.

## Lineage and Genetics

### Parent Strains
- **Female parent**: A Brazilian **sativa** landrace — contributing energetic, uplifting cerebral effects and tall growth patterns
- **Male parent**: A South Indian **indica** landrace — contributing resin production, compact bud structure, and relaxing body effects

### Breeder
- **Shantibaba** (Ingemar Blechert) at **Greenhouse Seeds** in Amsterdam, Netherlands
- First stabilized and released commercially in **1994**
- Won the **High Times Cannabis Cup** in 1995, launching its international reputation

### Genetic Legacy
White Widow's genetics have been used in an enormous number of subsequent crosses, including:
- **White Rhino** (White Widow × North American indica)
- **White Russian** (White Widow × AK-47)
- **Blue Widow** (White Widow × Blueberry)
- **Pineapple Widow** (White Widow × Pineapple Chunk)
- **White Widow Automatic** (autoflowering version bred with ruderalis genetics)

## Appearance

The visual profile of White Widow is distinctive and immediately recognizable:

- **Buds**: Dense, medium to large, conical in shape with tightly packed calyxes
- **Color**: Deep forest green with occasional pale yellow-green highlights
- **Trichome coverage**: Exceptionally heavy — buds appear dusted or coated in crystalline white resin; this is the defining visual characteristic
- **Pistils**: Abundant, bright orange to copper-colored hairs weaving through the calyxes
- **Leaf structure**: Broad indica-type leaves during vegetative growth; moderate internodal spacing
- **Resin**: Exceptionally sticky to the touch; scissors and grinders quickly accumulate thick resin

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma
- **Primary**: Earthy, pungent, and woody
- **Secondary notes**: Pine, floral, slightly skunky
- **Terpene signature**: Myrcene-dominant, with significant caryophyllene and pinene contributions
- When ground, releases a sharp, fresh herbal scent with subtle floral undertones

### Flavor
- **Inhale**: Smooth, woody, and slightly piney
- **Exhale**: Earthy with floral and spicy notes; a lingering peppery quality from caryophyllene
- **Aftertaste**: Pleasantly herbal, slightly sweet
- Considered one of the smoother classic strains for smoking and vaporization

## Cannabinoid Profile

Modern White Widow cultivars typically exhibit:

| Compound | Typical Range |
|----------|--------------|
| **THC** | 18–25% (some phenotypes up to 28%) |
| **CBD** | 0.1–0.5% |
| **CBN** | Trace |
| **THCV** | Trace |

Note: Original 1990s White Widow was estimated at 12–15% THC. Modern selective breeding has significantly increased potency, consistent with the global trend in cannabis potency over the past three decades.

## Effects

### Onset and Duration
- **Onset**: Rapid, typically felt within 2–5 minutes of inhalation
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours; effects taper gradually
- **Peak**: 30–60 minutes post-consumption

### Experience Profile
- **Initial (first 30 min)**: Cerebral rush — euphoric, uplifted mood, enhanced creativity and sociability; the sativa influence is most prominent here
- **Middle phase**: Balanced — mental clarity persists alongside emerging body relaxation; conversations flow easily; creative pursuits feel natural
- **Late phase**: Body-dominant — deep physical relaxation sets in; couch-lock potential increases with higher doses; sedative effects become noticeable
- **Comedown**: Smooth and gradual; minimal crash or anxiety

### Therapeutic Applications
- **Stress relief**: Potent anxiolytic effects without heavy sedation at moderate doses
- **Depression**: Mood-elevating properties helpful for treatment-resistant depression
- **Pain management**: Effective for chronic pain, headaches, and muscle spasms
- **Appetite stimulation**: Reliable munchie effect
- **Insomnia**: Higher doses effective for sleep induction
- **PTSD**: Some patients report symptom relief, particularly for anxiety and hypervigilance

### Potential Adverse Effects
- Dry mouth (very common)
- Dry eyes (common)
- Mild paranoia or anxiety at high doses (especially in THC-sensitive individuals)
- Dizziness (uncommon, typically at high doses)
- Increased heart rate (transient)

## Cultivation

### Growing Characteristics
White Widow is considered an excellent strain for beginner and intermediate growers due to its resilience and forgiving nature:

| Parameter | Vegetative | Flowering |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Photoperiod** | 18/6 | 12/12 |
| **Temperature** | 22–28°C | 18–26°C |
| **Humidity** | 50–70% | 40–55% |
| **Flowering time** | — | 8–9 weeks |
| **Indoor yield** | — | 400–500 g/m² |
| **Outdoor yield** | — | 500–600 g/plant |
| **Height (indoor)** | — | 80–140 cm |
| **Difficulty** | Easy | Easy |

### Cultivation Notes
- **Resilience**: Highly resistant to mold and pests; forgiving of minor nutrient fluctuations
- **Training**: Responds well to LST (low stress training), ScrOG, and topping
- **Nutrients**: Moderate feeder; typical NPK schedule during vegetative phase; increase P-K during flowering
- **Flush**: Recommended 1–2 week flush before harvest for smoother flavor
- **Harvest indicator**: Trichomes shift from clear to cloudy/milky; 10–20% amber for balanced effect
- **Outdoor**: Ready for harvest in early to mid-October (Northern Hemisphere)
- **Climate**: Performs well in temperate and Mediterranean climates

### Autoflowering Variant
- **White Widow Auto**: Cross with ruderalis; flowering triggered by age (not photoperiod)
- **Life cycle**: Seed to harvest in 75–85 days
- **Height**: 60–90 cm (compact)
- **Yield**: 300–400 g/m²
- **THC**: 14–18% (slightly lower than photoperiod version)
