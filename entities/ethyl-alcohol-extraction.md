## Ethyl Alcohol Extraction

## Description

Ethyl alcohol extraction is a method for isolating [[psilocybin]] and related
alkaloids from dried mushroom material using 200 proof (100%) anhydrous
ethyl alcohol as the solvent. The technique produces a concentrated
[[psilocybin]] liqueur that can be dosed with precision using a syringe.
This method was popularized in the [[pf-tek]] ([[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]]) community
as a way to create standardized, potent doses from home-grown mushrooms.

## Classification

- **Category**: Alkaloid extraction technique
- **Solvent**: 200 proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol (preferred) or 190 proof
- **Target compounds**: [[psilocybin]], [[psilocin]]
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Yield**: Variable, depending on [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]] potency

## Key Facts

- 200 proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol is the preferred solvent because it
  contains no water, which minimizes degradation of [[psilocybin]] during
  extraction and evaporation.
- 190 proof (95% ethyl, 5% water) is acceptable but the 5% residual water
  is a negative factor, especially during evaporation.
- The extraction process requires fully dried (rock hard) mushroom material
  that has been pulverized into a fine powder.
- Mushrooms should be harvested before or just as caps open and cool-dried
  with [[desiccant]] for maximum alkaloid content.
- The pulverized material is soaked in alcohol for 24 hours with periodic
  shaking to activate extraction.
- Filtration is performed using inexpensive dust-pollen masks as filter media.
- A secondary extraction (re-soaking the filtered material) recovers
  additional alkaloids.
- The combined extracts are evaporated using a small electric fan to
  concentrate the solution.
- Final concentration of 1-3+ grams of dried mushroom material per ice cube
  of final extract is considered a potent dose.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

This [[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-high-volume-extraction-method]] is significant because it allows cultivators to create
standardized, shelf-stable [[psilocybin]] preparations from their harvests.
The concentrated liqueur can be stored in small screw-cap bottles or vials in
the freezer, where the alcohol remains liquid. The precision of syringe
dispensing allows for accurate dosing, which is important for safety given
the variable potency of home-grown mushrooms. The technique demonstrates that
sophisticated extraction can be achieved with common household materials.

## Process Overview

1. **Preparation**: Pulverize thoroughly dried mushrooms in a sealed freezer
   bag using blunt force.
2. **Extraction**: Place powder in a sealed bottle, cover with alcohol to
   form a loose slurry, soak 24 hours with periodic shaking.
3. **Filtration**: Filter slurry through dust-pollen masks, squeezing to
   extract all alcohol.
4. **Secondary extraction**: Re-soak filtered material with fresh alcohol,
   repeat filtration.
5. **Concentration**: Combine extracts, evaporate with fan assistance to
   desired concentration.
6. **Storage**: Store in sealed vials in freezer. Dispense with syringe.

## Safety Considerations

- Pure ethyl alcohol is highly flammable — keep all flames and ignition
  sources away.
- Alcohol fumes from food-grade ethanol are non-toxic but should be used in
  a ventilated area.
- Accurate dosing is critical; slight overestimation can result in
  unexpectedly intense experiences.
- 200 proof ethanol may require a permit in some U.S. states, available
  through state liquor boards.

## Harvest Timing for Maximum Potency

The extraction's effectiveness depends heavily on harvest timing. Mushrooms
harvested before the veil breaks (before or just as the cap opens) contain
peak levels of [[psilocybin]] and [[psilocin]]. After sporulation begins,
alkaloid content declines as the mushroom redirects metabolic energy toward
[[geotropism-and-spore-production-in-mushrooms]]. Proper drying with [[desiccant]] preserves these alkaloids;
heat drying degrades [[psilocin]] rapidly. The [[pf-tek]] method emphasizes
this [[harvest-window-cannabis]] for maximum extraction yield.

## Solvent Selection and Availability

200 proof anhydrous ethanol is available from chemical supply companies and
some liquor control boards. Denatured alcohol must NOT be used — the toxic
additives (methanol, acetone, etc.) make the final product dangerous to
consume. 190 proof Everclear or similar high-proof grain alcohol is a common
substitute when 200 proof is unavailable. The 5% water in 190 proof slows
evaporation and slightly increases [[psilocin]] degradation, but remains
effective for home extraction.

## Storage and Shelf Life

The concentrated [[psilocybin]] liqueur is remarkably shelf-stable when
stored properly. Frozen ethanol remains liquid, preserving the dissolved
alkaloids in a stable state. Amber glass vials protect against light
degradation. Properly stored extract retains potency for months or longer.
Syringe dispensing allows sub-milliliter dose precision, typically
0.5-2.0ml per dose depending on concentration and desired intensity.
