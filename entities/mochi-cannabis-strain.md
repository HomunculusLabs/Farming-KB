# Mochi (Cannabis Strain)

**Mochi**, also known as **Mochi Gelato**, is an indica-dominant [[sunset-sherbet]]** and **Thin Mint [[blue-city-diesel-west-coast-blueberry-nyc-diesel-hybrid-oner]] of the [[blesching-cannabis-deep-relaxation-healing]].

**Thin Mint GSC (♂)** — Original GSC phenotype (OG Kush × [[green-cannabis-plant-anatomy-calyx-pistil-bud-structure]].

### Lineage Tree
```
OG Kush × Durban Poison
         ↓
   Girl Scout Cookies × Pink Panties
         ↓               ↓
  Thin Mint GSC    Sunset Sherbet
         ↓               ↓
           →  MOCHI  ←
```

## Strain Profile

- **Classification:** Indica-dominant hybrid (~70/30)
- **THC:** 21–29% (average 24%); CBD: <0.1%
- **Primary cannabinoids:** Δ⁹-THC, minor THCV and CBC

### Terpene Profile

Mochi's terpene profile is dominated by sweet, dessert-like compounds:

| Terpene | Approximate Range | Aroma Contribution |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Limonene** | 0.5–1.5% | Citrus, sweet, mood-elevating |
| **Caryophyllene** | 0.3–1.0% | Pepper, spice, anti-inflammatory |
| **Linalool** | 0.3–0.8% | Floral, lavender, relaxing |
| **Myrcene** | 0.2–0.6% | Herbal, musky, sedative base |
| **Humulene** | 0.1–0.4% | Earthy, woody, appetite-suppressing |
| **Pinene** | trace | Pine, mental clarity |

The unique combination of high limonene and linalool with a caryophyllene backbone creates Mochi's distinctive sweet, creamy, cookie-like aroma with tropical fruit and mint undertones.

### Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma
- **Primary:** Sweet, creamy, vanilla, cookie dough
- **Secondary:** Tropical fruit (mango, papaya), berry from Sherbet lineage
- **Overall:** Intensely sweet and dessert-like, like a bakery

### Flavor
- **Inhalation:** Sweet vanilla, cookie dough, creamy caramel
- **Exhalation:** Tropical fruit, berry, lingering minty freshness
- **Smoke:** Smooth and thick; relatively low harshness for high-THC

## Appearance

- **Bud structure:** Dense, tightly packed, chunky indica-typical nuggets
- **Coloration:** Deep green with vibrant purple/violet hues, especially in cooler conditions
- **Trichomes:** Exceptionally heavy — buds appear almost white/frosted
- **Pistils:** Bright orange to amber, moderate density

## Effects

### Onset
- **Time to onset:** 1–3 minutes when smoked or vaped; 30–90 minutes when ingested
- **Initial effects:** Cerebral, euphoric, and uplifting — a noticeable head rush or "face melt" sensation
