## Overview

**Skunk** is one of the foundational cannabis hybrids, originally bred in the United States during the 1970s by Sam the Skunkman (David Watson).

It represents a stabilised cross between **Afghan indica** landraces and **Acapulco Gold** and **Colombian Gold** sativa genetics.

Skunk became the genetic backbone for countless modern cultivars and defined the pungent, earthy-sour aroma profile that carries its name.

The term "skunk" is now used generically in some regions for any potent cannabis, but the original Skunk #1 remains a distinct, stabilised line.

## Lineage

Skunk #1's lineage is an indica-dominant polyhybrid derived from three principal parent populations.

**Afghan (indica):** Contributes compact bud structure, resin production, fast flowering (7–8 weeks), and physical sedation.

**Acapulco Gold (sativa):** Adds uplifting cerebral effects, golden-amber bud coloration, and complex aroma layers.

**Colombian Gold (sativa):** Provides tall growth habit, branching vigor, and sweet-citrus terpene notes.

The original cross was made in California, with stabilisation work continued in the Netherlands during the early 1980s.

Skunk #1 was among the first commercially available F1 hybrids sold by seed banks, establishing the modern seed industry model.

Genetic ratio is approximately 65% indica / 35% sativa in most stabilized expressions.

## Appearance

Buds are medium to large, dense, and elongated with a tapered, cylindrical structure characteristic of hybrid morphology.

Color ranges from bright lime green to deeper forest green, often with vivid orange pistils throughout.

Trichome coverage is heavy, producing a frosty, crystalline appearance even before full maturity.

Calyxes are swollen and tightly packed, with moderate leaf-to-bud ratio that simplifies trimming.

## Aroma and Flavor

Aroma is intensely pungent and the source of the strain's name — a sharp, musky, sour-earthy profile with underlying sweetness.

Primary notes include damp earth, skunky sulfur compounds, and fermented citrus.

Secondary notes reveal sweet hash, pine, and subtle tropical fruit on the exhale.

Flavor mirrors the aroma closely, with a creamy-sweet undertone that balances the initial sour pungency.

The terpene profile is dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and limonene, with measurable pinene.

## Terpene Profile

- **Myrcene (0.8–1.4%):** Primary sedative terpene; contributes to the heavy body effects and earthy aroma.
- **Caryophyllene (0.3–0.6%):** Peppery, spicy notes; binds to CB2 receptors for anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Limonene (0.2–0.5%):** Citrus lift; mood elevation and stress relief.
- **Pinene (0.1–0.3%):** Pine notes; counteracts THC-induced memory impairment.
- **Linalool (trace):** Floral undertone; anxiolytic properties.

## Cannabinoid Profile

- **THC:** 15–22% (classic expressions); modern selections reach 18–25%.
- **CBD:** <0.1% (negligible in original line); some CBD-rich Skunk variants bred for medical use reach 5–8% CBD.
- **CBG:** 0.5–1.0%.
- **THCV:** Trace amounts.

## Effects

Onset is rapid (5–10 minutes via inhalation), beginning with an uplifting cerebral buzz that transitions into a warm body stone.

Early phase features mood elevation, mild euphoria, heightened sensory perception, and social chattiness.

Mid-phase shifts toward physical relaxation with mild couch-lock potential at higher doses.

Duration is 2–3 hours, with aftereffects of calm drowsiness in the final hour.

Common applications include stress relief, mild pain management, appetite stimulation, and mood enhancement.

## Cultivation

Skunk #1 is considered one of the easiest strains to cultivate, making it a longtime favorite of beginner growers.

Flowering time is 7–9 weeks indoors; outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere occurs early to mid-October.

Indoor yields average 400–500 g/m² under optimal conditions; outdoor plants yield 500–700 g each.

The strain is resilient to pests, molds, and temperature fluctuations, though high humidity can trigger bud rot in dense colas.

It responds well to both SOG and SCROG training methods and performs well in soil, coco, and hydroponic systems.

## Medical Applications

Skunk's balanced indica-sativa profile makes it versatile for therapeutic use across multiple symptom categories.

Its analgesic properties benefit patients with chronic pain conditions including fibromyalgia, arthritis, and neuropathic pain.

Anxiolytic effects from the limonene and linalool content help manage generalized anxiety without the heavy sedation of pure indicas.

Appetite stimulation is pronounced, supporting patients undergoing chemotherapy or living with HIV-associated wasting syndrome.

The mood-elevating onset phase has antidepressant applications, particularly for treatment-resistant depression with comorbid anxiety.

Common side effects include dry mouth, dry eyes, and at higher doses, mild paranoia or anxiety in susceptible individuals.
