*Panaeolus tropicalis* is another closely related tropical species, differentiated by subtle microscopic features including spore morphology.

Several non-psychoactive Panaeolus species share the coprophilous habitat and mottled gill pattern but lack blue bruising.

*Panaeolus foenisecii* (mower's mushroom) is a common temperate lookalike that does not bruise blue and contains no psilocybin.
