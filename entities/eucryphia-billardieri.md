# Eucryphia billardieri

## Description

Eucryphia billardieri is a species of evergreen tree or large shrub in the family Cunoniaceae, native to the temperate rainforests of Tasmania and southeastern Australia. It is closely related to the more widely known Eucryphia lucida, both of which are the primary sources of the world-renowned leatherwood honey — one of the most distinctive and commercially valuable honey varieties. The genus Eucryphia has a remarkable Gondwanan distribution, with species in Tasmania, southeastern Australia, and South America (Chile), reflecting its ancient origins before continental drift separated these landmasses. In [[mushroom-cultivation]], particularly for moisture-loving species. In diversified forest farm systems, Eucryphia can be incorporated into conservation forests and hedgerow plantings where it provides simultaneous yields of premium honey, potential timber from mature specimens, and [[biodiversity-of-fungi-ecosystem-services-agriculture]] including [[hemenway-backyard-wildlife-habitat]] and [[mycofiltration-watershed-protection]]. The dense canopy creates humid microclimates that support diverse fungal communities in the understory.
