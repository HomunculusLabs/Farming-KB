# Dan Hemenway (Elfin Permaculture)

## Description

Dan Hemenway is a permaculture designer, educator, and founder of Elfin Permaculture, based in Florida and Georgia, USA. He is the editor and publisher of the Yankee Permaculture pamphlet series based on Bill Mollison's 1981 Permaculture Design Course. Along with his wife Cynthia Baxter Hemenway, he operated one of the longest-running permaculture design and education services in North America, active from 1981 through the 1990s and beyond.

## Key Facts

- Founded Elfin Permaculture and operated it as a husband-wife partnership with Cynthia Baxter Hemenway.
- Taught permaculture design across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Pacific Islands.
- Holds five diplomas from the International Permaculture Institute in Australia.
- Operated a homestead self-reliant in food and energy for a decade.
- Has professional experience in food production and alternative energy projects.
- Active in permaculture since 1981; Cynthia active since 1988.
- Edited and published the 15-pamphlet Permaculture Design Course series from Bill Mollison's 1981 course recordings.
- Also directed the Forest Ecosystem Rescue Network (FERN) program.
- Offered courses in multiple formats: weekend workshops, 10-day intensives, two-week workshops, and three-week design courses with certification.
- Developed the Advanced Permaculture Training (APT) program for design course graduates.

## Relevance to Cultivation and Mycology

- **Education**: His publications disseminated permaculture principles including soil mycology, composting, and food forest design to thousands of practitioners.
- **Design Practice**: Professional design work integrated mushroom production (listed as a design component in example designs, e.g., Chapter 9g.iii. Mushroom Production).
- **Forest Systems**: FERN program focused on forest ecosystem health, directly relevant to fungal ecology and mycorrhizal networks.
- **Composting Systems**: Design work included vermicomposting and composting toilet systems relevant to substrate preparation.
