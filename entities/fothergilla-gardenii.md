## Overview

Fothergilla gardenii, commonly known as dwarf fothergilla or coastal
witch-alder, is a low-growing deciduous shrub native to the southeastern
United States. It is a member of the witch-hazel family.

The species is prized in horticulture for its showy, fragrant white flower
spikes in spring and its brilliant fall foliage in shades of orange, yellow,
and scarlet. It is considered one of the finest native shrubs for autumn color.

Dwarf fothergilla was named in honor of Dr. John Fothergill, an English
physician and botanist who maintained an extensive botanical garden in the 18th
century. The specific epithet honors Alexander Garden, a Scottish physician and
botanist who practiced in Charleston, South Carolina.
