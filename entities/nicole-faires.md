## Nicole Faires

Nicole Faires is a homesteader, author, and advocate for sustainable living. She wrote "The Ultimate Guide to Natural Farming and Sustainable Living," a comprehensive handbook covering food production, animal husbandry, food preservation, and off-grid skills.

## Background

- Practicing homesteader with hands-on experience in food production, preservation, and self-reliance
- Author of multiple books on homesteading and sustainable living
- Writes from practical experience rather than purely theoretical knowledge

## Core Contributions

### Comprehensive Homesteading Handbook
Her "Ultimate Guide to Natural Farming and Sustainable Living" covers the full spectrum of homestead skills:

- **Gardening** — Vegetable production, season extension, seed saving
- **Food preservation** — Canning, drying, fermentation, root cellaring
- **Animal husbandry** — Poultry, goats, rabbits for homestead-scale production
- **Permaculture design** — Zone planning, guilds, food forests
- **Natural building** — Cob, straw bale, and other low-cost methods
- **Energy** — Solar, wood heating, off-grid systems
- **Water** — Rainwater harvesting, greywater systems

### Practical Approach
Faires emphasizes:

1. **Start where you are** — Don't wait for the perfect property
2. **Learn by doing** — Hands-on experience beats theoretical knowledge
3. **Design for resilience** — Systems should work even when inputs fail
4. **Integration** — Connect animals, plants, waste streams, and energy systems
5. **Seasonal awareness** — Work with natural cycles rather than against them

### Homestead Design Principles

From her work:

- Design the homestead as an integrated system where outputs from one component become inputs for another
- Zone planning based on frequency of use (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly)
- Multiple yields from each element (chickens provide eggs, manure, pest control, tillage)
- Backup systems for critical functions (water, food storage, energy)

## Key Books

1. **The Ultimate Guide to Natural Farming and Sustainable Living** — Comprehensive homesteading reference
2. **The Ultimate Guide to Permaculture** — Permaculture design for home-scale application
