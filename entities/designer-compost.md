## Description

Designer compost, also known as bokashi in Japan, is an enhanced compost product that goes beyond traditional decomposition by incorporating [[korean-natural-farming]], Japanese Natural Farming
- **Process**: [[fungicide-treated-seed-hazard-mushroom-substrate-preparation]]: organic base materials are inoculated with beneficial organisms and fermented/conditioned.
- The anaerobic fermentation process involves lactic acid bacteria, yeasts, actinomycetes, and fermenting fungi—all relevant to mycological study.
- Charcoal (biochar) as a microbial substrate mirrors its use in [[lactic-acid-bacteria]]
