## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Plantae → **Order:** Alismatales → **Family:** Araceae
- **Genus:** Colocasia → **Species:** C. esculenta (L.) Schott
- **Synonyms:** C. antiquorum, C. esculenta var. antiquorum, Arum esculentum
- **Common names:** Taro (English), Kalo (Hawaiian), Dasheen (Caribbean),
  Arbi (Hindi), Koccham (Malayalam), Sato-imo (Japanese)
- **Etymology:** From Greek *kolokasion*, an ancient name for the plant;
  *esculenta* means "edible" in Latin

Two major cultivar groups are recognized:
- **Dasheen type:** Large central corm, few side cormels; dominant in
  Africa, Caribbean, and South Asia
- **Eddoe type:** Small central corm, many side cormels; dominant in
  Southeast Asia and Oceania

## Morphology

### Vegetative Structures

- **Corm:** Large, spherical to oblong, 10–35 cm diameter, brown
  exterior, white to pink-white interior with purple flecking
- **Cormels:** Secondary corms arising from the parent; size and number
  vary by cultivar group
- **Leaves:** Large, heart-shaped (peltate), 20–60 cm long on thick
  fleshy petioles 30–150 cm tall
- **Petioles:** Green to purple-red, often used as a vegetable themselves
- **Root system:** Fibrous, shallow, adapted to saturated or
  waterlogged soils

### Reproductive Structures

- **Inflorescence:** Spathe and spadix, typical of Araceae, 15–25 cm
  long; rarely flowers under cultivation
- **Propagation:** Almost entirely vegetative via corm pieces (sets)
  or cormels
- **[[biodiversity-fungal-genetic-diversity]] maintained through clonal propagation

## Distribution and Cultivation

Origin is debated — India, Southeast Asia, and New Guinea are candidates.
Domestication likely occurred independently in multiple regions. Now
cultivated pantropically between 20°N and 20°S.

Cultivation spans diverse systems:

- **Upland (dryland):** Rain-fed fields, hilly terraces, intercropped
  with cereals and legumes
- **Lowland (wetland):** Flooded paddies and swamp margins
- **[[chen-maitake-growth-parameters-environmental-control]]: optimal temperature 21–30°C, annual rainfall
1,500–2,500 mm, tolerates 0–60 cm standing water, pH 5.5–7.0,
prefers loamy to clay loam soils with high [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]].

Maturity: 6–15 months depending on variety and conditions. Yields range
from 5–30 tonnes/ha corms depending on cultivation intensity.
