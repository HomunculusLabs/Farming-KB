---# Hydroponics and Soilless Cultivation

Hydroponics is the cultivation of plants without field soil, using
water, dissolved mineral nutrients, inert media, and carefully managed
root environments. The method is often associated with [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] and precise
[[arbuscule-isolation-and-nutrient-exchange-mechanisms]] in soil is mediated by clay particles, humus, and
microbes. In hydroponics it is mediated by dissolved ions, electrical
conductivity, pH, and the grower's willingness to adjust a reservoir
before stress becomes visible.

Temperature buffering is often underestimated. A reservoir that is too
warm loses oxygen and favors pathogens, while a cold solution slows
uptake even when the fertilizer recipe is chemically correct.
