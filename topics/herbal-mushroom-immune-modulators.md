# Herbal and Mushroom Immune Modulators

## Introduction

Herbal and mushroom immune modulators are natural substances derived from
plants and fungi that influence the immune system, not by crudely stimulating
it, but by modulating its activity toward homeostasis. Immunomodulation is a
