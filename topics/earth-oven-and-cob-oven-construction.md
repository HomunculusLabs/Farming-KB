A **ball test** confirms workability: form a golf-ball-sized ball of moist soil, drop it from waist height. If it cracks badly, add clay. If it deforms into a pancake, add sand. If it holds its shape with minor surface cracking, you have good cob soil.

### Fiber Selection

- **Straw** (wheat, oat, rice) is traditional and ideal — long fibers create the best tensile network.
- **Hay** works but contains seeds that may sprout in humid conditions.
- **Chopped weed stems** or dried grass can substitute in a pinch but provide less reinforcement.
