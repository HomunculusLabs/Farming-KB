## Grow Room Setup

The foundation of a successful indoor grow is a well-designed growing space.
For most home growers, this means a grow tent, a self-contained fabric
enclosure with reflective interior walls, ventilation ports, and hanging
points for lights and other equipment. Grow tents offer several advantages:
they are easy to set up and take down, they provide a lightproof environment
that prevents light leaks during the dark cycle, and their reflective
interiors maximize light utilization by bouncing photons back toward the
plant canopy. Tents are available in a wide range of sizes, from small
closet-sized units suitable for one or two plants to large walk-in tents
that can accommodate dozens.

Ventilation is critical for maintaining air quality, temperature, and humidity
within the grow space. A basic ventilation system consists of an inline exhaust
fan connected to a carbon filter for odor control, an intake port that draws
fresh air into the space, and an oscillating circulation fan that moves air
within the tent. The exhaust fan should be sized to exchange the air in the
grow space at least once every one to three minutes during the lights-on
period. Passive intake through open ports is usually sufficient for smaller
setups, though active intake fans may be needed for larger spaces or when
using powerful lights that generate significant heat.

Temperature management is closely tied to ventilation. Cannabis grows best at
temperatures between 20 and 28 degrees Celsius during the lights-on period,
with the ideal range depending on the growth stage and the specific strain.
During the dark period, temperatures should drop by 5 to 10 degrees to
simulate natural diurnal variation. Excessive heat is the most common
environmental problem in indoor grows, particularly during summer months or
in small spaces with powerful lighting. Air conditioning, water-cooled lights,
and CO2 enrichment can all help manage heat, though each adds complexity and
cost to the operation.

## Durban Poison Durban Poison is one of the most important and influential pure sativa landrace strains in cannabis genetics. Originating from the city of Durban, South Africa, located approximately 30 degrees south latitude, this strain has played a pivotal role in cannabis breeding worldwide, particularly in the development of early-finishing sativa hybrids suited for northern hemisphere cultivation. ## Geographic Origin and Significance Durban Poison originates from Durban, South Africa, a coastal city positioned at roughly 30 degrees south latitude. This geographic positioning is critical to understanding the strain's unique flowering characteristics.
