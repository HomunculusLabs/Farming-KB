# KNF [[natural-farming]] departs from this approach. Dr. Cho's
system recognizes that soil is a living ecosystem teeming with
microorganisms that, when properly supported, will maintain soil
structure, fertility, and health naturally.

The KNF approach to soil management involves three primary
strategies working in harmony:
- Earthworms and other soil organisms perform the tilling function
  naturally, creating channels for air and water penetration.
- Straw and leaf mulching suppresses weeds while adding organic
  matter to the soil surface.
- [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]], fixing nutrients, and suppressing
  pathogens.

The SOS is an all-natural soil conditioner that combines multiple
NF inputs to give the soil ecosystem a boost, particularly in fields
that have been degraded by conventional farming practices.

## SOS Recipe for One Acre

The source provides a detailed recipe for treating one acre (0.4
hectare) of land. The following inputs are mixed at their specified
dilutions:

| Input | Quantity |
|-------|----------|
| IMO | 500 ml |
| FPJ | 500 ml |
| OHN | 500 ml |
| BRV | 500 ml |
| LAB | 300 ml |
| WCaP | 300 ml |
| FAA | 300 ml |
| Charcoal | 50 kg |
| Soil | 500 kg |
| Seawater (SW) | 3 liters |
| Water | 200 liters |
| Farm yard manure | 1000 kg |

This comprehensive formula combines microbial inoculants (IMO, LAB),
plant nutrients (FPJ, FAA), mineral supplements (WCaP), herbal
support (OHN), soil acidifier (BRV), trace minerals (seawater), and
organic matter (charcoal, soil, farm yard manure) into a single
powerful soil treatment.

## Using IMO-4 for Soil Treatment

Beyond the liquid SOS, IMO-4 plays a central role in soil
conditioning. The source provides specific guidance for IMO-4 field
application:

1. Scatter IMO-4 compost over the field so that the topsoil is
   barely covered. Use a minimum of 1500 kg per acre.
2. Do not bury the IMO deeply. The source emphasizes that deeper
