## Why Old Varieties

In earlier times, every country, indeed every region, had its own cereal, vegetable, potato, and herb varieties. These were bred over generations through selection to become plants best suited to local conditions. Often they had no names of their own, having been propagated on a single farm. Key advantages:

- Better flavour and higher nutritional value
- Less demanding of soil and inputs
- Better adapted to local climatic conditions
- Greater disease resistance through natural adaptation
- Can cope with the poorest soils and most extreme temperatures
- Nutritional value often far exceeds that of monoculture-grown modern varieties
