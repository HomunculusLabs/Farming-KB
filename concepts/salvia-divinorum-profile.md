# Salvia Divinorum Profile

Salvia divinorum is a psychoactive plant in the Lamiaceae (mint) family, native to the cloud forests of Oaxaca, Mexico. It is the only known natural source of salvinorin A, the most potent naturally occurring psychedelic compound. Salvia produces experiences qualitatively different from classical psychedelics, characterized by intense dissociation and reality dissolution rather than the serotonin-mediated visual and emotional effects of [[5-meo-dmt-compound-profile]] or psilocybin.

## Chemistry

### Salvinorin A
The primary psychoactive compound is salvinorin A, a neoclerodane diterpenoid with the molecular formula C23H28O8. Key characteristics:

- **Most potent natural psychedelic**: Active at 200-500 micrograms (sub-milligram threshold), making it significantly more potent by weight than LSD or psilocybin
- **Kappa opioid receptor (KOR) agonist**: Unlike virtually all other psychedelics, salvinorin A acts primarily as a selective kappa opioid receptor agonist. It has no significant affinity for serotonin receptors (5-HT2A), dopamine receptors, or other typical psychedelic targets
- **Non-nitrogenous**: Unique among major psychedelics for lacking a nitrogen atom in its structure
- **Short molecular life**: Rapidly metabolized, contributing to the extremely short duration of effects

### Other Compounds
Salvinorin B, C, and other divinatorins are present in smaller quantities. Salvinorin B is an inactive or weakly active degradation product.

## Traditional Use

### Mazatec Tradition
Salvia divinorum has been used for centuries by the Mazatec people of Oaxaca, Mexico:

- Known as "ska Maria Pastora" (leaves of Mary the Shepherdess) or "hojas de la Pastora"
- Used in divination and healing rituals by curanderos
- Typically consumed as chewed quids of fresh leaves, sometimes in combination with a tea brewed from the leaves
- Employed for contacting spirits, diagnosing illness, and gaining insight
- Use is typically solemn and contemplative, not recreational
- Traditionally used in dark, quiet settings to enhance inner visions

## Dosage and Administration

### Chewed Quid (Traditional Method)
- 2-8 large fresh leaves (or equivalent rehydrated dried leaves)
- Chewed and held in the cheek for 15-30 minutes, juices absorbed sublingually
- Moderate effects, gentle onset
- Duration: 30-60 minutes

### Tincture (Sublingual)
- Salvia tincture standardized to salvinorin A content
- Held under tongue for 15-20 minutes
- Effects comparable to quid method with more precise dosing
- Duration: 30-60 minutes

### Smoked/Vaporized Extract (Most Common Modern Method)
| Extract Potency | Dose | Effect Level |
|---|---|---|
| Plain leaf (0.25% salvinorin A) | 1 large bowl | Light, threshold |
| 5x extract | 1/10 - 1/5 gram | Moderate to strong |
| 10x extract | 1/20 - 1/10 gram | Strong, immersive |
| 20x-40x extract | 1/40 - 1/20 gram | Breakthrough, complete dissociation |

Smoking requires high heat; a torch lighter is often used with extracts. The experience is extremely intense and comes on rapidly with this method.

## Duration

Smoked salvia has one of the shortest durations of any psychedelic:

| Phase | Duration (Smoked) |
|---|---|
| Onset | 10-30 seconds |
| Peak | 1-5 minutes |
| Main effects | 5-15 minutes |
| Afterglow/residual | 15-45 minutes |
| Total experience | 15-30 minutes |

The rapid onset and intense peak make smoked salvia disorienting — users may lose awareness of having taken anything before effects hit.

## Effects

### Subjective Experience
- Complete dissociation from physical body and consensus reality
- Merging with or being pulled into alternate realities, dimensions, or objects
- Loss of identity and ego dissolution
- Geometric/organic visual landscapes (closed-eye)
- Sensation of being rotated, folded, or divided
- Perception of entities, presences, or intelligences
- Amnesia for the experience immediately after
- Intense confusion during the peak (difficulty understanding what is happening)

The kappa opioid mechanism produces a distinctly different phenomenology from serotonergic psychedelics. Experiences are often described as more bizarre, less controllable, and more alien than those produced by [[5-meo-dmt-compound-profile]] or psilocybin.

## Safety Profile

### Physical Safety
- **No known lethal dose**: No confirmed human fatalities from salvia alone
- **Low physiological toxicity**: Salvinorin A shows no significant organ toxicity at recreational doses
- **No evidence of neurotoxicity**: Unlike some other psychedelics, salvinorin A has not shown neurotoxic effects in research
- **No significant addiction potential**: Kappa agonists are generally dysphoric, reducing abuse potential. No confirmed cases of physical dependence

### Psychological Safety
- **Extreme intensity**: The rapid onset and complete reality dissolution can be terrifying, especially for inexperienced users
- **Fall risk**: Loss of motor coordination during the peak makes falls and injury a real concern — sitting or lying down is essential
- **Psychological distress**: Panic, confusion, and fear are common during the experience, though they resolve quickly due to short duration
- **Not recommended** for those with personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia, or severe anxiety disorders

## Legal Status

Legal status varies significantly by jurisdiction:

- **United States**: Not federally scheduled, though many states have enacted restrictions or bans. DEA has listed it as a "drug of concern" and has considered scheduling.
- **Many countries**: Banned or controlled in Australia, several European nations, Japan, and others
- **Mexico**: Unregulated in most states
- **Legal gray area**: In jurisdictions without specific regulation, salvia and its extracts may be legally sold and possessed

## Harm Reduction

- Always have a sober trip sitter present
- Use in a safe environment, seated or lying down
- Start with low-potency preparations
- Use a torch lighter for efficient vaporization
- Do not mix with other substances
- Do not drive or operate machinery during or immediately after use

Salvia is one of the most intense psychedelic experiences available by weight, and its unique pharmacology makes it fundamentally different from other compounds.
