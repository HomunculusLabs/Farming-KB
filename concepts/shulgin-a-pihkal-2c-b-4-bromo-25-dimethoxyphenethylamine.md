# 2C-B (4-Bromo-2,5-Dimethoxyphenethylamine)

## Overview

2C-B is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine first synthesized and studied by Alexander Shulgin. It is compound #20 in PiHKAL (Phenethylamines I Have Known And Loved). The name "2C" refers to the two-carbon chain of the phenethylamine skeleton, distinguishing it from the three-carbon amphetamine analogues (such as DOB, DOI). 2C-B gained significant popularity in the 1990s and early 2000s as a recreational substance and has been used in psychotherapeutic settings.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name**: 4-Bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine
- **Molecular formula**: C10H14BrNO2
- **Molecular weight**: 244.12 g/mol
- **Structure**: Phenethylamine core with bromine at the 4-position and methoxy groups at the 2 and 5 positions on the aromatic ring
- **Appearance**: White crystalline hydrochloride salt
- **Melting point**: 237-239°C (hydrochloride, with decomposition)
- **Solubility**: Free base picks up CO2 from air rapidly; hydrochloride salt is stable

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage range**: 12-24 mg orally
- **Threshold**: 4-8 mg
- **Light experience**: 8-12 mg
- **Common experience**: 12-18 mg
- **Strong experience**: 18-24 mg
- **Duration**: 4-8 hours
- **Onset**: 20-60 minutes

2C-B is considered a moderately potent phenethylamine. Its dosage range is relatively narrow, and the difference between a light and strong experience can be as little as 8-10 mg. Shulgin noted that the effects are dose-responsive with clear progression from mild perceptual enhancement at lower doses to full [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] at higher doses.

## Synthesis

The synthesis described in PiHKAL proceeds through several steps:

1. **Nitrostyrene formation**: 2,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde is condensed with nitromethane using ammonium acetate as a catalyst, producing 2,5-dimethoxy-beta-nitrostyrene as a yellow-orange crystalline product.

2. **Reduction to phenethylamine**: The nitrostyrene is reduced using lithium aluminum hydride (LAH) in THF, yielding 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine (2C-H). This intermediate picks up carbon dioxide from the air rapidly and must be handled carefully.

3. **Bromination**: 2C-H is dissolved in glacial acetic acid and treated with elemental bromine. The bromine electrophilically substitutes at the 4-position (para to one methoxy group), producing 2C-B. Multiple salt forms exist — the hydrochloride is the preferred form, with the hydrobromide and acetate salts also reported.

Shulgin noted that the salt chemistry of 2C-B is "treacherous" due to the existence of multiple polymorphs and hydrates, making isolation and characterization challenging.

## Qualitative Effects

The subjective effects of 2C-B are characterized by a balance between visual and emotional components:

### Perceptual Effects
- Color intensification, particularly toward golden and rose tones
- Enhanced visual richness without overwhelming distortion
- Geometric patterning with eyes closed, less intense than LSD
- Organic visual imagery — flowers, flowing forms, natural motifs
- Enhanced appreciation of art, music, and natural beauty

### Emotional and Physical Effects
- Sensation of lightness and warmth spreading through the body
- Enhanced tactile sensitivity and erotic experience
- Strongly empathogenic qualities at moderate doses
- Increased appetite (unlike many psychedelics)
- Mild physical stimulation without significant body load at therapeutic doses

### Cognitive Effects
- Increased associative thinking and creativity
- Enhanced appreciation of aesthetics and sensory experience
- Relatively clear-headed compared to classical psychedelics
- Ability to engage with normal activities and conversation

## Overdose and Safety

Shulgin documented two notable overdose cases that illustrate the compound's safety profile:

- **64 mg intentional overdose**: A physician experienced intense fear of living things but showed no neurological damage. Effects were dramatic but resolved without medical intervention.
- **100 mg accidental overdose**: A psychologist who mistakenly consumed 5x the intended dose faced a genuine confrontation with mortality. After two hours of terror, the experience transformed into something "really marvelous."

These cases suggest a high therapeutic index, though they also demonstrate that very high doses can be psychologically overwhelming.

## MDMA Combination

Many reports in Shulgin's files describe combining 2C-B with MDMA. The optimal protocol involves taking 2C-B at or just before the final baseline recovery of the MDMA experience. This timing allows the emotional openness created by MDMA to be channeled into therapeutic exploration with 2C-B's perceptual enhancement. Several psychotherapists have advocated for this combination.

## Legal Status

2C-B was first scheduled in the United States in 1995. It is now controlled internationally under the UN Convention on Psychotropic Substances and is scheduled in most countries worldwide. Prior to scheduling, it was briefly sold commercially as an aphrodisiac under the brand name "Nexus."

The scheduling of 2C-B had the unintended consequence of driving the market toward other members of the 2C family, many of which were not specifically listed in controlled substance legislation. This led to the phenomenon of "research chemicals" — novel [[cultural-evolution-and-psychoactive-substances]] sold in a legal gray area. Eventually, most countries adopted analogue or blanket scheduling laws to address the proliferation of 2C compounds.

## Historical Context

2C-B was first synthesized by Shulgin in 1974. During the 1980s, it saw limited use in the [[metzner-five-fundamental-conclusions-psychedelic-psychotherapy]] community, valued for its relatively [[savory-short-duration-grazing-and-cell-systems]] and controllable intensity compared to LSD or psilocybin. Therapists found that patients could engage with difficult material without being overwhelmed, and the shorter duration made it practical for outpatient sessions.

In the early 1990s, 2C-B became popular in the rave and nightclub scene, where its empathogenic and stimulant qualities at lower doses made it a MDMA alternative. It was often sold in tablet form, sometimes misrepresented as MDMA, which created risks for users expecting MDMA's effects.

## Pharmacological Context

2C-B belongs to the 2C [[shulgin-a-pihkal-the-2c-family-of-phenethylamines]], all sharing the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine core with various substituents at the 4-position. The halogenated members (2C-B, 2C-I, 2C-C, 2C-F) show a clear potency trend correlated with the size and electronegativity of the halogen substituent. The corresponding amphetamine analogues (DOB, DOI, DOC) are significantly more potent and longer-lasting. Research suggests 2C-B acts primarily as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist, with additional activity at 5-HT2C and other serotonergic receptor subtypes, though its full pharmacological profile remains less characterized than classical psychedelics.
