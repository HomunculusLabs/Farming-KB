# PF TEK Substrate Formula and Jar Preparation

## Overview

The **[[pf-tek]]** ([[miniculture-rye-grain-cultivation-method-for-psilocybe-cubensis]] *Psilocybe
cubensis*. Its innovation lies in using **vermiculite as a structural base**
with **brown rice powder** as the nutrient source, eliminating the need for
[[accessible-mushroom-cultivation-for-disabilities]].

## Standard Substrate Formula (per 1/2 Pint Jar)

### Ingredients
- **1/8 cup** brown rice powder (freshly ground preferred)
- **1/2 to 2/3 cup** horticultural vermiculite (medium grade)
- **40–45 cc** (≈3 tablespoons + 1 teaspoon) water

### Maximum Fruiting Formula
- **1/4 cup** brown rice powder
- **1/2 cup** vermiculite
- **60 cc** water

The maximum fruiting formula produces larger yields but requires loose lids
during incubation to prevent water accumulation.

## Vermiculite Selection

Vermiculite coarseness significantly affects water-holding capacity and thus
substrate performance:

| Type | Particle Size | Water Holding |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| Fine | ~1 mm average | Higher (preferred) |
| Coarse | ~4–5 mm, up to 8 mm | Lower |

The **fine type is recommended** because it holds more water, which translates
to better [[mycelial-growth-and-colonization]] fruiting. If the coarse type is the only option,
reduce water proportionally. Store availability varies regionally — PF
recommends shopping around to compare types.

## Brown Rice Powder

Freshly ground brown rice from a coffee bean grinder is recommended over
pre-packaged rice flour:
- Freshness "sometimes makes a big difference" in colonization vigor
- Any standard brown rice works — no special varieties needed
- Grind to a fine powder, similar to flour consistency

## Jar Selection

Jars must meet specific criteria:
- **1/2 pint (8 oz / 250 ml) capacity** — larger jars are unreliable
- **Tapered sides with no shoulder** — essential for cake removal
- Recommended jars: KERR wide mouth 1/2 pint, BALL regular mouth 1/2 pint,
  BALL 1/2 pint jelly jar
- Alternative: 250 ml tapered drinking glasses with tin foil covers

**Note**: Even though KERR and BALL 1/2 pint regular mouth jars look similar,
the KERR version is NOT tapered — the BALL is preferred.

## Lid Preparation

Standard canning jar lids are modified for gas exchange:

1. Place the lid rubber-sealing-edge **upwards** on a flat surface
2. Using a sharpened **3-penny nail** held in vise-grip pliers, punch **4 holes**
   inside the periphery of the rubber sealing edge
