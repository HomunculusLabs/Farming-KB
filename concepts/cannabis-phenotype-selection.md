## Cannabis Phenotype Selection

Phenotype selection is the process of choosing individual cannabis
plants based on their observable traits for use as breeding parents or
cloning stock. It is the core activity of any cannabis [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] all represent different selectable
phenotypes.

All phenotypes result from genes acting within plant cells. Sometimes a
single gene controls a trait (monogenic traits), and sometimes sets of
genes operate together (polygenic traits). Polygenic traits, especially
recessive ones, may produce desirable phenotypes in only 1 in 100 or 1
in 1000 plants, making large population sizes critical for discovery.

## The Phenotype-Genotype-Environment Relationship

Phenotype is the result of an interaction between a plant's genotype
(its genetic constitution) and its environment. A simplified expression
is: Phenotype = Genotype + Environment. Some genotypes are latent and
express only under specific environmental conditions. For example, some
plants have green leaves that turn purple only under cold temperatures,
while others remain green regardless. Different alleles (gene versions)
at the same locus produce these different responses.

## Selection Criteria for Indoor Cultivation

When breeding for indoor cultivation, Cervantes identifies these key
selection priorities:

- Short, squat, bushy growth habit suitable for artificial lighting and
  limited vertical space.
- Large, densely formed buds with high calyx-to-leaf ratio.
- Discernible taste, particular flavors, and desirable aromas.
- High [[solomon-insects-and-diseases-integrated-approach]] common in indoor
  environments.
- Overall vigor and good health as a general rule.

## Selection Criteria for Outdoor Cultivation

Outdoor breeders should perform selection outdoors under their specific
environmental conditions. Key priorities include:

- Early maturation to finish before frost, rain, or pest pressure.
- Mold resistance, particularly botrytis (grey mold) and powdery
  mildew.
- Adaptation to local climate, including temperature, humidity, and
  [[cannabis-disease-pest-resistance-breeding-clarke]] to local insect and pathogen populations.
- Suitable stature for the [[cannabis-harvest-and-curing]]. Plants that require
finishing under 6 feet should be sourced from parents that mature at
less than 6 feet.
