## Reading Weeds as Soil Indicators

"One can understand the nature of the soil more quickly by examining the
weeds growing in it than by examining the soil itself." This principle
inverts the conventional approach. Rather than testing soil chemistry and
prescribing amendments, Fukuoka observes what grows naturally and lets
those plants guide soil management.

Weeds solve the problems of both soil and weeds simultaneously. Each
species that emerges indicates specific soil conditions and addresses
specific deficiencies. A diverse weed community means diverse soil biology
and balanced fertility.
