deified. Language, poetry, ritual, and thought emerged from the

## The Woman and the Magical Plants

McKenna reads the Genesis story as the story of a woman who is mistress
of the magical plants. Eve eats and shares the fruits of the Tree of
Knowledge, fruits which are described as "good to eat, pleasing to the
eye, and pleasing to contemplate." After eating, "the eyes of both of
them were opened and they discovered that they were naked." McKenna
interprets this at the metaphorical level as the attainment of
consciousness of self and other, the emergence of individual awareness
from the undifferentiated oceanic consciousness of the partnership
culture.
