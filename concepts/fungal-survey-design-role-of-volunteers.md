## Convenience vs. Standardized Sampling

Two main approaches exist for fungal surveys, each with strengths and
limitations:

- **Convenience (opportunistic) sampling**: Experienced individuals
  visit locations and document taxa they find; efficient for
  preliminary estimates but precludes rigorous comparisons between
  sites
- **Plot-based sampling**: All fungi within defined areas are
  collected, ensuring standardized effort; allows quantitative
  comparisons but may miss species outside plot boundaries

The volunteer survey at Barlow Pass, Washington, exemplifies
convenience sampling: amateur mycological society members made weekly
visits for 2 years, collecting samples of each taxon encountered.
Although this approach detected many large, showy species, its
limitations for quantitative comparison remain untested.
