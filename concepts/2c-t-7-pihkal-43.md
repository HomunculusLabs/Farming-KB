# 2C-T-7

## Overview

2C-T-7 (2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylthiophenethylamine) is a synthetic [[alexander-shulgin]] ranked it among the most acceptable and intrinsically rich of all phenethylamines, placing it "right up there near the top, along with [[2c-b-compound-profile]], [[2c-e-compound-profile]]." The compound is known for its versatile visual character, emotional depth, and relatively manageable duration of 8-15 hours.

2C-T-7 gained notoriety in the early 2000s as a "designer drug" and was eventually scheduled as a controlled substance in multiple countries following several reported deaths, though the causal role of 2C-T-7 in these fatalities has been debated.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propylthiophenethylamine
- **PIHKAL entry:** #43
- **Family:** 2C-T (thio-substituted 2C phenethylamines)
- **Related compounds:** [[2c-b-compound-profile]], [[2c-e-compound-profile]]
- **Legal status:** Schedule I (US, since 2003) / Controlled in most jurisdictions

## Synthesis (Summary)

2C-T-7 synthesis begins with 2,5-dimethoxythiophenol (prepared as described under 2C-T-2). The thiol is alkylated with n-propylbromide in methanol with KOH, yielding 2,5-dimethoxyphenyl n-propyl sulfide as a pale yellow oil with a light pleasant fruity smell.

The sulfide undergoes Vilsmeier-Haack formylation using POCl3 and N-methylformanilide to give 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(n-propylthio)benzaldehyde (mp 76-77 degrees C). Nitrostyrene formation with nitromethane and ammonium acetate yields 2,5-dimethoxy-beta-nitro-4-(n)-propylthiostyrene (mp 120-121 degrees C).

Reduction is achieved with LAH in THF (using a modified workup with IPA and NaOH). The free base is distilled (140-150 degrees C at 0.25 mm/Hg) and converted to the hydrochloride salt, forming "spectacular white crystals."

| Parameter | Range |

| **Light dose** | 10 - 15 mg |
| **Common dose** | 15 - 25 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 25 - 30 mg |

Shulgin noted that the lower end of the range (10 mg) was intentionally included because many people found this level adequate for their needs.

## Qualitative Commentary (from PIHKAL)

**20 mg (first report):** A "wonderful day of integration and work." Onset in about 2 hours with periodic nausea throughout the day. Visuals described as "much like mescaline but less sparkly" with lots of movement, aliveness, velvety appearance, and increased depth perception. Muscle tension in neck, shoulders, and legs with periodic extreme tightness followed by relaxation. Working described as "very integrative" with oscillation between "wonderful God-space -- similar to MDMA but more grounded" and sadness. Tremendous processing and emotional release including singing and letting go of parental grief. Vocal freedom and facility remained enhanced for several days after.

**20 mg (second report):** Lying with music, becoming engrossed with stillness. Feeling that complete stillness would allow hearing "the inner voice of the universe." Music became "incredibly beautiful." Realization of the importance of deep listening to everything -- people and nature -- with wide-open receptivity. At the still point, "a huge burst of energy is released" that requires enormous effort to quiet again. "Great fun."

**25 mg:** Described as "good and friendly and wonderful." Became deeply engaged with a documentary about indigenous Bolivian people maintaining Inca traditions. The shaman imagery was "startling in their intensity and earthy depth." Noted that 2C-T-7 might "take the place of 2C-T-2 in my heart."

**30 mg:** Visuals with an "adaptable character" -- able to recreate any hallucinogenic substance previously known. With open eyes: [[mescaline-ethnobotany]]: Visuals described as "much like [[2c-b-compound-profile]]: Both are highly ranked by [[2c-t-2-compound-profile]]: [[2c-e-compound-profile]]: Both ranked among the top phenethylamines. 2C-T-7 is somewhat shorter-acting and potentially more emotionally warm.

## Harm Reduction

- Duration of 8-15 hours requires a full day of commitment
- Periodic nausea is common throughout the experience
- Muscle tension may be significant, especially at higher doses
- 2C-T-7 has been associated with several deaths, primarily involving insufflation (snorting) at high doses or dangerous drug combinations
- Oral administration is strongly preferred over insufflation due to steep dose-response curve
- Do not combine with MAOIs or other serotonergic substances
- Start at the low end of the dose range (10-15 mg) to assess individual sensitivity
- See [[harm-reduction-guide]] for general principles

## Related Compounds

The 2-ethoxy homologue (2CT7-2ETO) has been synthesized and tested at 20 mg. It produced modest but real eyes-closed visuals with very rapid onset and short duration (baseline at ~5 hours), followed by an uncomfortable headache the next day.
