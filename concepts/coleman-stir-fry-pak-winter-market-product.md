## Stir-Fry Pak — Winter Market Value-Added Product

## Overview

The Stir-Fry Pak is a value-added farm product concept developed by [[coleman-four-season-farm]]. It packages together all the ingredients needed for a stir-fry meal in a single 10-by-15-inch cellophane bag, creating a complete vegetable kit that customers can purchase in one convenient transaction. The product demonstrated the potential for winter-harvest farms to move beyond salad mixes into main-course offerings and became one of the farm's most successful market innovations.

## Product Contents

Each Stir-Fry Pak contained the following whole ingredients:

- One head of 'Mei Qing Choi' pak choi
- One head of young tatsoi
- One 'Shunkyo' semi-long pink radish
- One 'Hakurei' turnip with greens attached
- One 'Tadorna' extra-hardy leek
- One large carrot

The selection was designed to provide contrasting colors, shapes, and textures across root, stem, and leaf components. The combination of white, green, pink, and orange created visual appeal that attracted customers at point of sale.

## Design Philosophy

### Contrasting Colors and Shapes

The beauty of the Stir-Fry Pak lay in the visual contrast of its ingredients. The round white turnip, elongated pink radish, pale green pak choi, dark green tatsoi, long white leek, and orange carrot created an attractive display that communicated freshness and variety. This visual appeal was an essential marketing element — customers could see at a glance that they were getting a diverse, high-quality selection.

### Whole Ingredients Approach

All ingredients were included whole, not pre-cut or processed. This approach had several advantages:

- Reduced labor compared to processing value-added products
- Extended [[coleman-aphid-management-winter-greenhouse-nitrogen-flushing]] production. The crops and their cold-hardiness characteristics:

- **'Mei Qing Choi' pak choi** — A Chinese cabbage type that performs well in cool conditions
- **Tatsoi** — One of the most cold-hardy Asian greens, capable of withstanding repeated freezing
- **'Shunkyo' radish** — A semi-long pink variety chosen for both flavor and visual appeal
- **'Hakurei' turnip** — A sweet, smooth-skinned Japanese turnip that grows exceptionally well under cool conditions
- **'Tadorna' leek** — An extra-hardy leek variety selected for [[lettuce-and-leafy-greens]] received a true cold-weather testing. This early sell-out indicated strong customer demand for complete meal packages and validated the concept of moving beyond salad into main-course winter products.

## Cold-Hardiness Observations

While the full product never experienced the coldest part of winter due to early sell-out, Coleman noted confidence in the cold-hardiness of several components:

- **Tatsoi** — Proven to withstand the coldest conditions at Four Season Farm
- **Leek** ('Tadorna') — Known to endure midwinter temperatures under protection
- **Carrot** — Stored in-ground under cold houses with proven [[coleman-winter-salad-braising-mix-composition]]
