### Positive Emotions and Longevity
**Danner, Snowdon, and Friesen (2001)**: "Positive emotions in early life and longevity: Findings from the nun study." Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin.

The famous Nun Study found that nuns who expressed more positive emotions in their early-life autobiographies lived significantly longer. Positive emotional content in writing at age 20 predicted survival six decades later.

**Emmons and McCullough (2003)**: "Counting blessings versus burdens." Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

People who kept gratitude journals showed significantly higher well-being, better sleep, more optimism, and more helpfulness toward others.
