## Overview

[[cannabis-tinctures-and-edibles]] are liquid extracts made by [[knf-seed-soaking-cannabis-germination]] flower or concentrate in a solvent (alcohol, glycerin, or oil). They are administered sublingually (under the tongue) for rapid absorption or added to food and beverages. Tinctures offer precise dosing, discreet use, long [[ingham-aact-holding-time-shelf-life-decay]], and a smoke-free consumption method.

## How Tinctures Work

When taken sublingually:

1. Drops are placed under the tongue and held for 30-60 seconds
2. Cannabinoids are absorbed through the sublingual mucosa directly into the bloodstream
3. Bypasses first-pass metabolism in the liver
4. Effects are felt within 15-45 minutes (faster than edibles)
5. Duration: 2-4 hours

When swallowed or added to food, tinctures are processed through the digestive system like edibles, with onset in 1-2 hours and effects lasting 4-8 hours.

## Types of Tinctures

### Alcohol Tinctures (Green Dragon)

Alcohol is the most efficient solvent for cannabis tinctures, extracting a full spectrum of cannabinoids and terpenes. High-proof ethanol (190-proof or Everclear) is ideal.

**Cold Method (Long Extraction)**:
1. Decarboxylate 1 ounce (28g) cannabis at 240 F for 35 minutes
2. Place decarbed cannabis in a glass [[mason-jar-preserving-guide]]
3. Pour 1 pint (16 oz / 475 ml) of high-proof alcohol over the cannabis
4. Seal jar tightly and store in a cool, dark place
5. Shake daily for 4-6 weeks
6. Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter into a dropper bottle
7. Store in a dark glass bottle at room temperature

**Hot Method (Quick Extraction)**:
1. Decarboxylate 1 ounce cannabis at 240 F for 35 minutes
2. Place cannabis in a mason jar
3. Pour 8-16 oz high-proof alcohol over the cannabis
4. Place the sealed jar in a water bath or slow cooker on low (160-170 F) for 20-60 minutes
5. Do NOT use open flame — alcohol is extremely flammable
6. Strain through cheesecloth into a dark glass bottle
7. Store in a cool, dark place

The hot method produces a darker, stronger-tasting tincture in less time. The cold method preserves more terpenes and has a milder flavor.

**[[ingham-compost-tea-holding-time-shelf-life]]**: Alcohol tinctures last 1-2+ years when stored properly. Alcohol acts as a preservative.

### Glycerin Tinctures

Vegetable glycerin is a sweet, thick, alcohol-free solvent. It is ideal for those who cannot or prefer not to consume alcohol.

**Process**:
1. Decarboxylate cannabis as above
2. Combine decarbed cannabis and food-grade vegetable glycerin in a jar (ratio 1:2 cannabis to glycerin by volume)
3. Add 1 teaspoon lecithin to improve bioavailability
4. Heat in a water bath at 160-180 F for 2-4 hours, stirring occasionally
5. Alternative: Use a slow cooker on low for 4-6 hours
6. Strain through cheesecloth
7. Store in a dark glass dropper bottle

**Advantages**:
- Sweet taste (no alcohol burn)
- Safe for alcohol-sensitive individuals
- No intoxicating solvent concerns

**Disadvantages**:
- Less efficient extraction than alcohol (lower cannabinoid yield)
- Shorter shelf life (6-12 months)
- Thicker consistency — harder to use in dropper bottles
- Less potent per volume than alcohol tinctures

### MCT Oil Tinctures

Medium-chain triglyceride (MCT) oil from coconut oil is used as a carrier for cannabinoid-infused tinctures.

**Process**:
1. Decarboxylate cannabis
2. Combine decarbed cannabis and MCT oil in a jar (1 ounce cannabis per 2 cups oil for medium strength)
3. Heat in water bath at 160-180 F for 2-3 hours
4. Add lecithin for bioavailability
5. Strain through cheesecloth
6. Store in a dark glass dropper bottle

**Advantages**:
- Neutral taste (less earthy than alcohol)
- Good bioavailability — MCT oil is efficiently absorbed
- Edible and sublingual compatible
- Pleasant mouthfeel

**Disadvantages**:
- Lower extraction efficiency than alcohol
- Must be kept at room temperature (solidifies if refrigerated)
- Shelf life: 3-6 months

## Dosing

### Calculating Potency

To estimate tincture potency, you need to know the starting cannabis percentage:

**Example**:
- 28g (1 oz) cannabis at 20% THC = 5,600 mg total THC
- Decarboxylation converts ~85-90% of THCA to THC: ~4,760-5,040 mg THC
- Extraction efficiency: 70-80% for alcohol, 50-60% for glycerin
- Assuming 75% extraction efficiency: ~3,570 mg THC extracted
- Dissolved in 475 ml (16 oz) of alcohol
- Concentration: ~7.5 mg THC per ml
- Standard dropper: ~1 ml = ~7.5 mg THC per dropperful

### Dosing Guidelines

- **Beginner**: Start with 1-2.5 mg THC
- **[[ultra-low-dose-brassinosteroid-response-psilocybe-cubensis]]**: 2.5-5 mg THC
- **Moderate dose**: 5-15 mg THC
- **High dose**: 15-30 mg THC
- **Microdosing**: 0.5-2.5 mg, 2-3 times daily

Always start low and wait at least 60-90 minutes before redosing. Sublingual onset is faster than edibles but still requires patience.

### CBD Tinctures

CBD tinctures follow the same process using CBD-dominant flower or hemp. Common dosing:

- **General wellness**: 10-25 mg CBD daily
- **Anxiety**: 15-50 mg CBD
- **Pain and inflammation**: 25-75 mg CBD
- **Sleep**: 25-100 mg CBD before bed

## Flavoring

Tinctures can be flavored with:

- **Peppermint or vanilla extract**: 1-2 teaspoons per batch (alcohol-based extracts are compatible with alcohol tinctures)
- **Stevia or honey**: Sweeten glycerin or MCT tinctures
- **Terpenes**: Add strain-specific or botanical terpenes for flavor and effect enhancement
- **Cinnamon, ginger, or chamomile**: Steep with the cannabis during extraction

## Storage

- Store all tinctures in dark glass bottles (amber or cobalt)
- Keep in a cool, dark place
- Alcohol tinctures: shelf stable at room temperature for 1-2 years
- Glycerin tinctures: 6-12 months at room temperature
- MCT oil tinctures: 3-6 months; do not refrigerate
