## Compassion and the Vagus Nerve

A University of North Carolina experiment identified a novel mechanism
by which kindness and compassion improve health: increased vagal tone.

The vagus nerve communicates parasympathetic impulses to the heart,
reducing heart rate, to arteries, lowering blood pressure, and to
digestive organs, optimizing digestion. Positive emotions of kindness
and compassion significantly increase vagal tone.

Study participants meditated on their own worries and those of friends
with compassion, repeating affirmations like "may you live with ease"
and "may you feel safe." Results showed higher vagal tone and
increased expansive emotions including joy, hope, and amusement.

The authors concluded that "positive emotions, positive social
connections, and physical health influence one another in a self-
sustaining upward-spiral dynamic."
