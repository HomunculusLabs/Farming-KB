### Function

During sterilization, pressure differential forces steam outward through the filter
can. The multi-layer construction of rockwool and vermiculite traps contaminant spores
and bacteria while allowing free gas passage, maintaining a sterile environment
inside the bag without complex laboratory equipment.

## Bag Assembly

Once the substrate is loaded into the boil-a-bag, the filter can is inserted into
the open top. The bag neck is gathered around the filter can and secured with wide
masking tape. Jarrold specifies wrapping the tape **three times** around the
neck to ensure a firm, airtight seal that will not fail under extreme conditions.
