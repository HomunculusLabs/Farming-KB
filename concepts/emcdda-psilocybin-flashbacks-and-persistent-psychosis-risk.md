## Benzodiazepines as First-Line Treatment

The EMCDDA report identifies benzodiazepines as the safest medication of
choice for managing acute adverse reactions to hallucinogenic mushrooms,
effective for most patients. First aid typically aims at reassuring the
affected person and preventing them from harming themselves or others,
then assisting them to an appropriate medical unit. The emphasis on
reassurance and environmental management reflects the understanding that
most adverse mushroom experiences are self-limiting and respond to
calm, supportive intervention rather than antipsychotic medication.
