# ALEPH-4

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propylthioamphetamine

## Overview

ALEPH-4 is a member of the [ALEPH family with an isopropylthio group at the 4-position. It produces a paradoxical mix of physical discomfort and profound beauty, with intense psychedelic effects that can include brief episodes of claustrophobic crisis. The compound is notable for its capacity to produce simultaneously negative and positive experiences, creating deeply transformative states that users describe as among their most profound learning experiences despite significant physical discomfort.

## Chemical Information

- Chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(i)-propylthioamphetamine
- CAS: Not widely registered
- Molecular formula: C14H23NO2S (free base)
- Molecular weight: 269.41 g/mol (free base)
- Class: Substituted amphetamine (ALEPH family, sulfur-substituted DOx variant)
- The isopropylthio isomer of [ALEPH-7; amphetamine counterpart of [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] for aldehyde synthesis) via condensation with nitroethane and ammonium acetate ([[scythian-cannabis-steam-bath-ritual]], 12h + 12h room temp), yielding the nitrostyrene as orange crystals (mp 99-100 C). Reduction with LAH in THF (1 day reflux + 2 days room temp) gives the free base as an almost white oil, converted to HCl salt (mp 146-147 C with sintering at 144 C). Yield is low (0.5 g from 1.2 g nitrostyrene).

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- [ALEPH: Shorter-acting (6-8h) and less intense. ALEPH-4 is more challenging and longer-acting.
- [ALEPH-7: The n-propyl isomer. ALEPH-7 produces the distinctive "Beth state" (emotional neutrality) while ALEPH-4 produces more emotionally intense but paradoxical experiences.
- [ALEPH-2: The ethylthio analog. ALEPH-2 is warmer and more sensual; ALEPH-4 is more intense and physically demanding.
- [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]: The 2-carbon homologue. More thoroughly explored than ALEPH-4.
- [DOM: Both are long-acting DOx-family compounds. DOM is more purely psychedelic without the paradoxical physical discomfort.

## Harm Reduction Notes

- Very low active dose (7-12 mg). Milligram-accurate scales are absolutely essential.
- Long duration (12-20 hours) requires full-day planning with next-day recovery.
- Body toxicity concerns raised by Shulgin himself. Do not exceed 12 mg.
- The compound can produce sudden claustrophobic crisis episodes requiring immediate environmental change.
- Sensory confusion has been reported -- driving is strongly discouraged despite one user reporting they did so successfully.
- The [ALEPH family shows severe inter-individual variability.
