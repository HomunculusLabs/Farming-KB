## Light Schedule for Autoflowers

Since photoperiod does not control flowering, autoflowers can be grown under 18/6,
20/4, or even 24/0 light schedules throughout their entire life cycle. Many growers
use 18/6 or 20/4 to give plants a rest period while maximizing growth rate. There is no
need to switch to 12/12. The flexibility in light scheduling means autoflowers can be
grown alongside photoperiod plants under a vegetative light schedule without
interference.
