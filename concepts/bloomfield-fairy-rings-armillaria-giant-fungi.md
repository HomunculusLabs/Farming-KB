## Hyphal Architecture

The mycelium is constructed from networks of branched hyphae, the
threadlike filaments that constitute the feeding body of the fungus.

non-septate (coenocytic, containing many nuclei in a continuous
tube). In septate species, cytoplasm shuttles from one compartment
to the next through perforated septa, with nuclei actively moving
between compartments.
