## Re-Entry Visions and Judgment in the Psychedelic Experience

## Overview

During the Third Bardo — the period of re-entry into ordinary reality — the voyager encounters a series of vision-types that reveal the character and quality of the ego they are in the process of reconstructing. These re-entry visions, along with the associated phenomena of judgment and sexual hallucination, represent some of the most psychologically significant and potentially transformative elements of the psychedelic experience. The Tibetan [[bardo-thodol]] maps these onto a cosmological framework of six worlds of existence, each corresponding to a different level of consciousness and personality type.

## The Six Worlds of Game Existence

The Tibetan manual conceives of the voyager as returning to one of six worlds (lokas) of game existence (sangsara). The re-entry to the ego can take place on one of six levels, arranged from highest to lowest:

### 1. Devas — The World of Saints
The highest, most illuminated level. Devas are what Westerners would call saints, sages, or divine teachers — the most enlightened people walking the earth: [[gautama-buddha]], Lao Tse, Christ. Existence at this level, while more desirable than others, is concomitant with an ever-recurring round of pleasure and free game ecstasy. The deva world is said to shine with a **white light**, preceded by visions of delightful temples and jewelled mansions.

### 2. Asuras — The World of Titans and Heroes
People with more than human degrees of power and vision. Existence as an asura is concomitant with incessant heroic warfare. The asura world has a **green light** and is signalled by magical forests and fire images.

### 3. Human Existence — The Ordinary Level
Most normal human beings, struggling through game-networks, occasionally breaking free. The characteristic impediments are inertia, smug ignorance, and physical or psychological handicaps of various sorts. The ordinary human world has a **yellow light**.

### 4. Animal Existence — Primitive Incarnations
This category encompasses powerful symbolic animal identities: the **dog and cock** (hyper-sexuality concomitant with jealousy), the **pig** (lustful stupidity and uncleanliness), the **industrious, hoarding ant**, the **insect or worm** (earthy, grovelling disposition), the **snake** (flashing anger), the **ape** (rampaging primitive power), the **snarling "wolf of the steppes,"** the **bird** (soaring freely). In all cultures, people have adopted identities in the image of animals — a process familiar from childhood and from dreams. Helplessness and slavery characterize this level. Animal existence is foreshadowed by a **blue light** and images of caves and deep holes in the earth.

### 5. Pretas — Neurotics and Unsatisfied Spirits
Frustrated, lifeless spirits forever pursuing unsatisfied desires. The torments of unsatisfied needs and wants characterize this existence. The preta world has a **red light** and visions of desolate plains and forest wastes.

### 6. Hell — Psychosis
The sixth and lowest level. The hell world emits a **smoke-colored light** and is preceded by sounds of wailing, visions of gloomy lands, black and white houses, and black roads along which one must travel.

Less than one percent of ego-transcendent experiences end in sainthood or psychosis. Most persons return to the normal human level.

## Determinants of Re-Entry Level

According to the Bardo Thodol, the level one is destined for is determined by one's **karma** — the accumulated residue of past thoughts, actions, and game-involvements. During the Third Bardo, premonitory signs and visions of the different levels appear, with the one for which one is heading appearing most clearly. For example, the voyager may feel full of godlike power (asuras), stirred by primitive impulses (animals), experience all-pervasive frustration (pretas), or shudder at self-created tortures (hell).

The chances of making a favorable re-entry are increased if the process is allowed to take its natural course without effort or struggle. One should avoid pursuing or fleeing any of the visions, but meditate calmly on the knowledge that all levels exist within the Buddha-nature — the unified field of consciousness.

## The All-Determining Influence of Thought

A crucial teaching of the Third Bardo is that the quality of one's thoughts during re-entry directly determines the level of existence one enters. Several specific pitfalls are identified:

### Attachment to Possessions and Old Games
If the voyager feels attachment to material possessions or old games and activities — or feels resentment because others are still involved in pursuits they have renounced — this affects the psychological balance so severely that even someone destined for a higher level will re-enter on the lower level of unsatisfied spirits (neurosis). Conversely, even if one feels attached to old games, one will not be able to play them; they will be of no use. The instruction is to abandon weakness and attachment, to cast them away wholly, and to renounce them from the heart.

### Resentment Toward Companions
When the activities of other session members seem wrong, careless, inattentive, or distracting — when the agreement or contract is broken, when purity of intention is lost by any participant — the voyager (who can perceive all of this with Bardo-clarity) may feel lack of faith and begin to doubt their beliefs. This can lead to extreme depression, disbelief, and loss of faith, which affects the psychological balance and guarantees re-entry on an unpleasant level.

The prescribed response is to recognize that whatever others do is a mirror of one's own impure thoughts — and to put trust in companions, exercising sincere love towards them. Whatever they do will then be to one's benefit.

### The Power of Others' Good Deeds
Even if destined for a lower level, through the good deeds of friends, relatives, participants, and teachers who devote themselves wholeheartedly to beneficent rituals, the resulting feeling of being greatly cheered can so affect the psychological balance that one rises to a higher and happier level despite heading downwards. This underscores the communal dimension of the psychedelic experience.

## The Judgment Vision

The Judgment Vision is one of the most dramatic and psychologically revealing experiences of the Third Bardo — what the manual calls the "blame game."

### The Traditional Form
"Your good genius will count up your good deeds with white pebbles, the evil genius the evil deeds with black pebbles." A judgment scene is central to many religious systems. Westerners are most likely to see it in the Christian version with its throne, book of judgment, and sentencing.

### The Psychological Interpretation
The Tibetans provide a purely psychological reading:

- **The Judge (Lord of Death)** symbolizes **conscience itself** in its stern aspect of impartiality and love of righteousness.
- **The "Mirror of Karma"** (the Christian Judgment Book) is **memory** itself.
- Different parts of the ego come forward — some offering lame excuses, others ascribing baser motives, counting apparently neutral deeds among the black ones, still others offering justifications or requests for pardon.

The mirror of memory reflects clearly; lying and subterfuge will be of no avail. The instruction is: be not frightened, tell no lies, face truth fearlessly.

### The "Executive Furies"
The voyager may imagine themselves surrounded by figures who wish to torment, torture, or ridicule them — the "Executive Furies of the Robot Lord of Death." These merciless figures may be internal hallucinations or may involve the people around you, seen as pitiless, mocking, and superior. The key recognition: fear, guilt, and persecuting figures are your own hallucinations — your own "guilt machine."

The essential teaching is that the personality is a collection of thought-patterns and void. It cannot be harmed or injured: "Swords cannot pierce it, fire cannot burn it." In reality there is no Lord of Death, no justice-dispensing god or demon. Act so as to recognize this.

### The Tri-Kaya Framework
The judgment vision instruction introduces the esoteric Tri-Kaya (Three Bodies) doctrine:

- **Dharma-Kaya** — the "Divine Body of Truth," primordial, formless Essential Wisdom; true experience freed from all error or obscuration; the highest state, including both Nirvana and Sangsara as polar states identical in the realm of pure consciousness.
- **Sambhoga-Kaya** — the "Divine Body of Perfect Endowment"; Reflected or Modified Wisdom, embodied in the five Dhyani Buddhas.
- **Nirmana-Kaya** — the "Divine Body of Incarnation"; Practical or Incarnate Wisdom. All enlightened beings reborn with full consciousness as workers for the betterment of their fellow creatures are Nirmana-Kaya incarnates.

The Tri-Kaya corresponds to the exoteric trinity of: Buddha (your own divinity), the Scriptures (this manual), and the Priesthood (your companions).

## Sexual Visions

Sexual visions are extremely frequent during the Third Bardo. The voyager may see or imagine males and females copulating — corresponding to the psychoanalytic "primal scene" and Oedipus Complex, marking a remarkable convergence of Eastern and Western psychology at this level.

### Characteristics
The vision may be internal or may involve the people around the voyager. Multi-person orgies may be hallucinated, with simultaneous desire and shame, attraction and disgust. The voyager may wonder what sexual performance is expected and have doubts about their ability.

### The Oedipal Dimension
If the voyager becomes conscious of "maleness," hatred of the father together with jealousy and attraction toward the mother will be experienced. If conscious of "femaleness," hatred of the mother together with attraction and fondness for the father is experienced. This self-centered sexuality has little in common with the sexuality of transpersonal experiences, where physical union can be one expression of cosmic union.

### Birth Visions
Visions of sexual union may be followed by visions of conception — the sperm uniting with the ovum — intra-uterine life, and birth through the womb. Some people claim to have re-lived their own physical birth during psychedelic sessions, with occasionally confirming evidence. Whether constructed from memory or fantasy, the birth vision is often symbolic — emergence from a cocoon, breaking out of a shell, etc.

### The Instruction
When sexual visions occur, the voyager should withhold themselves from action or attachment, have faith, and float gently with the stream. Attempting to enter the old ego through attraction or repulsion — trying to join or escape the hallucinated orgy — leads to re-entry on an animal or neurotic level.

## Choosing the Post-Session Personality

The manual teaches that choosing the post-session ego is an extremely profound art requiring impartiality and calm. One should:

1. **Not return fleeing from hallucinated tormentors** — such re-entry brings one to the three lower levels.
2. **First banish fear** by visualizing one's protective figure or the Buddha, then choose calmly.
3. **Use limited foreknowledge** to decode one's own internal road map, following the signs of the three higher types and shunning those of the three lower.
4. **Follow light and pleasant visions** and shun dark and dreary ones.
5. **Enter game existence with good grace**, voluntarily and freely, visualizing it as a celestial mansion — an opportunity to exercise game-ecstasy.
6. **Maintain complete impartiality**, since a game that appears good may later turn out to be bad.

The key to the return voyage: take it easy, slowly, naturally. Enjoy every second. Don't rush. Don't be attached to old games. Every moment can be a joyous discovery.
