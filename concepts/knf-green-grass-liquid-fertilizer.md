## Plant Material Selection

While any green plant material can be used, some are better than others:

- **Preferred:** Young, actively growing weeds (high nutrient content, rapid decomposition)
- **Avoid:** Mature, woody, or seed-bearing weeds (slower decomposition, may introduce weed seeds)
- **Diversity:** Use a mixture of species for the broadest nutrient spectrum
- **Legumes:** Clover, vetch, and other leguminous plants add nitrogen
- **Grasses:** Provide silica and potassium
- **Broadleaves:** Provide calcium, magnesium, and trace minerals
