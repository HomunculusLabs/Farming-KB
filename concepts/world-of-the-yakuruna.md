# World of the Yakuruna

The yakuruna are water people — spiritual beings who inhabit the depths of Amazonian rivers, lakes, and underwater cities. In [[amazonian-ship-aceropunta-and-visionary-cosmology]], they represent a complex civilization beneath the water's surface, with distinct castes, allies, enemies, and magical technologies. Understanding the yakuruna world is essential for murayas (specialist water shamans) who must negotiate with these beings to rescue lost souls, acquire healing knowledge, and defend against aquatic sorcery.

## Types of Yakuruna

### Masha Yakuruna

The masha yakuruna is an ally of the murayas, teaching them how to rescue people lost in the watery depths. In visions, this yakuruna appears in the foreground smoking a pipe made from an encanto (magical stone) in the form of a shoe, with a stem made from the bone of a tuyuyo (Jabiru mycteria, the jabiru stork). The masha yakuruna represents the helpful, teaching dimension of water spirit culture.

### Taksha Yakuruna (Little Yakuruna)

Recognizable by its green hair and the boa it carries, the taksha yakuruna comes out to feed between midnight and dawn. According to Amazonian legend, if captured by a vegetalista, the taksha yakuruna must remain on the earth plane and teach the shaman how to hunt in the waters. This captures the dynamic between human shamans and [[icaro]] is a love chant that can amorously spellbind anyone. It represents the mythical fountain of youth archetype transposed into an Amazonian underwater context.

### Sumillakta — City of the Sumis

Sumillakta is the beautiful and enchanting city of the sumis (a class of powerful shamans). Here one can learn the great magical parapsychic arts. Adjacent to the city is the great bath of the sumis with its gilded crystal stairway and sapphire-blue waters, suggesting ritual purification practices.

### Atunllipian-llakta (Great Brilliant City)

Above the healing hut, a celestial opening appears through layers of dark clouds, revealing the Atunllipian-llakta — the meeting place of the masters where they gather to receive the wisdom of the ancient ones. This city represents the highest aspiration of shamanic learning, accessible through the deepest trance states.

## Aquatic Defenses and Guardians

### Auca-sirenas (War Mermaids)

These mermaids keep watch on the tranquil and resting yakurunas. They live within rocks and can disappear by simply approaching one. The small pools of water atop their rocks function as solar mirrors that can be transformed into strong lasers capable of capturing even the most powerful enemies. This represents a sophisticated defensive technology within the [[icaros]] and heal with them. Behind them appear the great arkanas (defensive protections) that come from these plants. The fairies serve as intermediaries between the underwater plant world and human healers.

## The Angel Taksha

This angel carries a powerful laser used to heal spider and insect bites, and also mal aire. The presence of an angel with laser technology in an underwater context illustrates how Amaringo's cosmology freely mixes traditional Amazonian spirit categories with technological imagery.

## Killo-garza (Golden Heron)

These birds in flight have vigilance equal to that of a curandero. They serve as aerial guardians of the underwater realm, extending the defensive perimeter beyond the water's surface.

## The Aceropunta

The Aceropunta is the great esoteric steamer that can only be seen under a very strong mareación (visionary trance) when called by a well-sung icaro. It arrives from a great distance producing an electrifying sound.

### Seven Forms of Appearance

The Aceropunta manifests in seven different forms:

1. A battleship
2. A submarine emerging from the depths
3. A speedboat with four stories
4. A large Viking-style ship
5. An aircraft carrier
6. A trimaran
7. An airship

Regardless of form, the bow of the ship is always made of dazzling white steel.

### The Crew

The crew includes seamen dressed in white, doctors in violet, nurses in light blue, as well as fairies, mermaids, sibyls, fortune tellers, and great murayas and bancos — shamans specialized in high alchemy. This cosmopolitan crew represents the unity of all healing traditions under the banner of the ship's mission.

### Commander Manuel Huaya

Manuel Huaya stands in front of the steps with his subordinates as the commander of the Aceropunta. The healer on shore prepares to receive the ship and its crew, suggesting a formal diplomatic protocol between the human and spirit worlds.

### Defenses

The Llipian Atun Huarmis (big and brilliant women) are great fairies with magic, high-powered lamps used as weapons. They defend the boat from outer space, accompanied by birds used in vegetalista science: sarara (Anhinga anhinga), tibemama (Pandion haliaetus), pompóm, and cumpa-supay.

### Huarmi Murayas

Resembling mermaids dressed in the scaly armor of celestial fish, these beings water pink and white flowers for their alchemical healing work. The Puka-clukchas (red-haired men) travel in the rear of the ship, tending the ropes to prevent enemies from harming those performing healing arts aboard.

## The Sumiruna's Ascent

The sumiruna represents the highest level of shamanic attainment. In the vision of the sublimity of the sumiruna, this master is transported through an opening to the subaquatic worlds and then launched into space.

### The Three Regions

The subaquatic world is divided into three regions:

1. **First region**: Fair-haired mermaids, pink and black dolphins, white yakurunas
2. **Second region**: Red yakurunas, hidrodos, blue and red mermaids
3. **Third region**: Black yakurunas, pato-bufeos (duck-dolphins), black mermaids

### Levitation

The sumiruna is transported through a glass tube (the [[palm-tree-uses]]:

- **Banco on tortoise**: Swallows virotes of the inchahui palm (Syagrus tessmannii)
- **Pipe smoker**: Swallows virotes of the pona palm (Iriartea exorrhiza)
- **Third vegetalista**: Swallows virotes of the inayuga palm (Maximiliana stenocarpa)
- **Last one**: Swallows virotes of the huasai palm (Euterpe precatoria)

Surrounding plants include the cumaceba tree (Cesalpina equinata), motelo huasca vine (Bauhinia guianensis), sacha-ajos (Mansoa alliacea), siucacasha (sharp spine), and sacha-huiro (Costus cf. cylindricus).

## The Sanguijuela Mama

The [[sanguijuela-mama]] is a giant leech approximately twelve meters long, with four antennae in its head and no mouth. It inhabits cochas bravas (isolated, dangerous jungle lakes) where enormous beasts live.
