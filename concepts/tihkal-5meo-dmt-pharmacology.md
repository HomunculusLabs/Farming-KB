# 5-MeO-DMT Pharmacology and Comparison to DMT

## Chemical Identity

5-MeO-DMT (5-methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is the 5-methoxy analogue of DMT. Like DMT, it is a naturally occurring alkaloid that is not orally active and is almost always smoked.

## Dosage and Administration

| Route | Dosage |
|-------|--------|
| Smoking | 6-20 mg |
| Oral | 35 mg (inactive without MAOI) |
| Intravenous | 0.25-3.1 mg (extremely potent) |
| Oral with harmaline | 10-25 mg |

The intravenous route shows dramatically enhanced potency: effects at 0.25 mg, with full psychedelic experiences at 1.3-3.1 mg. This makes 5-MeO-DMT approximately 10-20x more potent than DMT by the intravenous route.

## Comparison to DMT

### Similarities
- Both are naturally occurring alkaloids
- Both are inactive orally without MAO inhibition
- Both are typically smoked or injected
- Both have extremely short durations (approximately 20 minutes when smoked)
- Both show rapid onset (seconds)

### Differences

| Property | DMT | 5-MeO-DMT |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| IV potency | 4-30 mg | 0.25-3 mg |
| Visual character | Rich visual hallucinations, colors, patterns | Often described as "white-out," [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]], less visual |
| Emotional tone | Can be terrifying, complex imagery | "Cosmic consciousness," "ruthless love," unconditional |
| Somatic effects | Distinct "plastic taste" when smoked | Mild taste when smoked |
| Subjective quality | Complex, narrative visual experiences | Overwhelming singularity, inability to judge, total implosion |
| Duration (smoked) | ~20 min | ~20 min |

### Qualitative Character

5-MeO-DMT is described as fundamentally different from DMT in subjective quality:

- **DMT**: "Terrifying" for some; complex visual landscapes and narratives
- **5-MeO-DMT**: "Like adding the MDMA experience to DMT" -- more relaxed, cosmic consciousness, "receiving grace"

The 5-MeO-DMT experience is characterized by:
- An inability to judge by any method of the mind
- A "white-out" (as opposed to a black-out) -- a singularity of experience
- Feelings described as "deep and profound as a totally unconditional love that is life"
- Complete implosion of the universe through consciousness
- Perceiving all objects, situations, and feelings simultaneously

## Danger and Adverse Effects

At excessive smoked doses, 5-MeO-DMT has produced:
- Near-respiratory arrest (subject stopped breathing)
- Coma-like states
- "Energy of terror" lasting days
- Requirement for antipsychotic medication intervention
- Profound fear of death

## Natural Sources

- **Virola theiodora**: Bark can contain up to 95% 5-MeO-DMT as alkaloid content
- **Virola rufuta**: Root and bark contain ~95% 5-MeO-DMT
- **Dictyoloma incanescens**: First botanical identification (Rutaceae family)
- **Sonora Desert Toad** (Bufo alvarius): Venom contains up to 15% 5-MeO-DMT, along with 5-MeO-NMT and bufotenine
- **[[chiric-caspi-brunfelsia-south-american-hallucinogen]] snuffs**: Yakee, yato, parica, epena, nyakwana

## Ayahuasca and Oral Activity

When combined with harmaline (an MAO inhibitor), oral 5-MeO-DMT becomes active at 10-25 mg, producing effects qualitatively different from either compound alone. This combination is a variant of the ayahuasca/pharmahuasca paradigm discussed extensively in TIHKAL.

## Synthesis

5-MeO-DMT is synthesized from 5-methoxyindole via the standard glyoxylamide-LAH reduction route. The crystalline product has mp 69-70 degrees C after recrystallization from hexane.
