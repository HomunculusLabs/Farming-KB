# 5-MeO-DMT Pharmacology and Comparison to DMT

## Chemical Identity

5-MeO-DMT (5-methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is the 5-methoxy analogue of DMT. Like DMT, it is a naturally occurring alkaloid that is not orally active and is almost always smoked.

## Dosage and Administration

| Route | Dosage |
|-------|--------|
| Smoking | 6-20 mg |
| Oral | 35 mg (inactive without MAOI) |
| Intravenous | 0.25-3.1 mg (extremely potent) |
| Oral with harmaline | 10-25 mg |

The intravenous route shows dramatically enhanced potency: effects at 0.25 mg, with full psychedelic experiences at 1.3-3.1 mg. This makes 5-MeO-DMT approximately 10-20x more potent than DMT by the intravenous route.

## Ayahuasca and Oral Activity

When combined with harmaline (an MAO inhibitor), oral 5-MeO-DMT becomes active at 10-25 mg, producing effects qualitatively different from either compound alone. This combination is a variant of the ayahuasca/pharmahuasca paradigm discussed extensively in TIHKAL.

## Synthesis

5-MeO-DMT is synthesized from 5-methoxyindole via the standard glyoxylamide-LAH reduction route. The crystalline product has mp 69-70 degrees C after recrystallization from hexane.
