### The Fenton Reaction Mechanism
Brown rot fungi employ a non-enzymatic oxidative system based on the Fenton
reaction to depolymerize cellulose:

- The Fenton reaction generates hydroxyl radicals (OH•) from hydrogen
  peroxide (H2O2) and ferrous iron (Fe2+)
- Hydroxyl radicals are among the most powerful oxidants known and can
  cleave cellulose chains non-specifically, rapidly reducing the degree
  of polymerization of wood cellulose
- This initial oxidative depolymerization is critical because it converts
  crystalline cellulose into more accessible, amorphous oligomers that
  can then be further hydrolyzed by conventional cellulase enzymes
