# Phenethylamine vs. Tryptamine Comparison

## Overview

Shulgin documented his exploration of two major families of psychedelic compounds: phenethylamines (in PIHKAL) and tryptamines (in TIHKAL — Tryptamines I Have Known and Loved). Understanding the differences and similarities between these families is essential to appreciating Shulgin's contribution to psychopharmacology.

## Structural Comparison

### Phenethylamine Core
```
Ar—CH2—CH2—NH2
```
A benzene ring connected to an amine via a two-carbon chain. Modifications occur on the ring and on the amine nitrogen. See [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]].

### Tryptamine Core
```
    NH
    |
Ar—C—CH2—CH2—NH2
    |
    CH
```
An indole ring (fused benzene-pyrrole) connected to an amine via a two-carbon chain. The indole nitrogen provides an additional site for substitution.

## Key Differences

### Chemical Diversity
- **Phenethylamines**: Greater diversity of ring substitution patterns; the benzene ring accepts a wider variety of substituents (methoxy, halogen, alkyl, thioalkyl, methylenedioxy)
- **Tryptamines**: More constrained ring substitution; most modifications occur at the 4-position of the indole ring or on the amine nitrogen

### Potency Range
- **Phenethylamines**: Enormous potency range — from hundreds of milligrams ([[mescaline-compound-profile]]) to sub-milligram doses ([[dob-compound-profile]])
- **Tryptamines**: Generally more uniformly potent; most active compounds fall in the 5-30 mg range, with notable exceptions (e.g., 5-MeO-DMT at 5-10 mg, LSD-like compounds at microgram doses)

### Duration Range
- **Phenethylamines**: Wider duration range — 2 hours (some short-acting compounds) to 30+ hours (DOB, DOI)
- **Tryptamines**: Generally shorter durations — 2-8 hours for most compounds, with psilocybin at 4-6 hours

### Effect Character
- **Phenethylamines**: Tend toward greater visual richness, more stimulant character (especially alpha-methylated compounds), wider range of emotional tones
- **Tryptamines**: Tend toward more "organic" or "natural" feeling experiences, stronger body component, more consistent entheogenic quality

### Administration Routes
- **Phenethylamines**: Primarily oral
- **Tryptamines**: More varied — oral, smoked (DMT, 5-MeO-DMT), insufflated, sublingual, and intramuscular

## Shared Features

### Receptor Pharmacology
Both families act primarily as serotonin receptor agonists, with the 5-HT2A receptor being the principal target for psychedelic effects. See [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]].

### SAR Principles
Both families follow similar SAR logic:
- Increasing lipophilicity generally increases potency
- Bulkier substituents can increase duration
- Electron-donating groups on the ring enhance activity
- Alpha-methylation generally increases duration and stimulant character

### Testing Methodology
Shulgin applied the same systematic approach to both families: synthesis, analytical verification, human testing at graduated doses, written experience reports, and SAR analysis.

## Notable Compounds in Each Family

### Phenethylamine Highlights (PIHKAL)
- [[mescaline-compound-profile]] — Natural prototype
- [[2c-b-compound-profile]] — Most famous 2C compound
- [[2c-e-compound-profile]] — Most profound 2C compound
- [[dob-compound-profile]] — Among the most potent
- [[mda-compound-profile]] and [[mdma-in-pihkal-context]] — Empathogenic classics

### Tryptamine Highlights (TIHKAL)
- DMT — The simplest psychedelic tryptamine
- Psilocybin/Psilocin — Natural psychedelics from mushrooms
- 5-MeO-DMT — Extremely potent short-acting compound
- LSD — The most potent psychedelic known (an ergoline, not a simple tryptamine, but included in TIHKAL)

## Cross-Family Analogues

Some substitution patterns produce analogous compounds in both families:
- 4-substitution with halogens produces active compounds in both families
- The 2C vs. 4-HO pattern mirrors the phenethylamine vs. tryptamine ring substitution strategies
- N-methylation has similar effects in both families (increased lipophilicity, altered character)
