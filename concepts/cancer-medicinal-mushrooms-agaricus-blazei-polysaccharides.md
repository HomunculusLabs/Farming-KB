## Research Status

A. blazei has become an important source of antitumour polysaccharides
and is now being artificially grown in Japan and studied
pharmacologically. Extensive research, mostly in Japan, has clearly
demonstrated immunostimulatory activity and antitumour action
(Reshetnikov et al., 2001). In Asia, several medicinal and edible
mushrooms are regularly consumed in the average diet, and the question
of whether the amount consumed through this route can be expected to
have a limited or significant effect on the immune system must be the
subject of future research.

## Polysaccharide Identification

Multiple polysaccharide fractions have been identified from A. blazei,
including FI1-a-beta (a beta-glucan), FIII2-beta (another beta-glucan
fraction), ATOM (a glucomannan-protein complex), and AB-FP (a mannan-
protein complex). The variety of polysaccharide structures contributes
to the mushroom's broad-spectrum immunomodulatory activity.
