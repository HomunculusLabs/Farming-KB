# Fungal Culture Media

Fungal culture media are formulated substrates used to isolate, cultivate, maintain, and study fungi in the laboratory. Media selection is critical for successful isolation and depends on the nutritional requirements and ecological characteristics of the target fungi. This reference covers the major media types and their applications.

## Principles of Media Formulation

### Key Considerations
- **Carbon source**: Mono/disaccharides favor vegetative growth; polysaccharides (cellulose, starch) favor enzyme production
- **Nitrogen source**: Inorganic (NO3, NH4) or organic (peptone, yeast extract, amino acids)
- **pH**: Most fungi prefer pH 4-7; some alkalophiles prefer pH 8-10
- **Water activity (aw)**: High sugar/salt concentrations reduce aw, selecting for osmotolerant/xerophilic species
- **Antibiotics**: Added to suppress bacterial growth during isolation
- **Selective agents**: Cycloheximide, dichloran, rose bengal select for or against specific fungal groups

### Sterilization
- Autoclave at 121°C (15-25 psi) for 15-20 minutes
- Heat-sensitive components (vitamins, antibiotics) filter-sterilized (0.2 um pore) and added after cooling
- Viscous or dirty media (soil, oatmeal) may need 30-45 minutes

## General-Purpose Media

### Malt Extract Agar (MEA)
- **Composition**: Malt extract 2-20g, agar 20g, water 1L
- **Use**: Most versatile medium for isolation and maintenance of filamentous fungi and yeasts
- **Variations**: 
  - MYA: add yeast extract for faster growth
  - MCA (Malt Cyclosporin Agar): add cyclosporin A (2-10 mg/L) for colony control during isolation
- **Applications**: Soil fungi, leaf litter fungi, endophytes, general cultivation

### Cornmeal Agar (CMA)
- **Composition**: Yellow cornmeal 40g, agar 15g, water 1L (boil, simmer 15 min, filter, restore volume)
- **Use**: Culture of myxomycetes, protostelids, acrasids, and filamentous fungi
- **Variations**:
  - CMDA: + glucose 2g, yeast extract 1g (microfungi, coprophilous fungi)
  - CM/2: half-strength (myxomycetes)
  - ACMA: alkaline (pH 9.7) for alkalophilic fungi

### Potato Dextrose Agar (PDA)
- **Composition**: Potato infusion, dextrose 20g, agar 15-20g, water 1L
- **Use**: Most widely used general-purpose medium; favors rapid growth
- **Applications**: Plant pathogens, soil fungi, general cultivation

### Czapek-Dox Agar (CDA)
- **Composition**: Sucrose 30g, NaNO3 2g, K2HPO4 1g, MgSO4 0.5g, KCl 0.5g, FeSO4 0.01g, agar 15g, water 1L
- **Use**: Characterization and maintenance of Aspergillus, Penicillium, and other microfungi
- **Variations**: 0% CZA (no sucrose) for extreme-environment fungi; diluted CDA for sensitive isolates

## Specialized Isolation Media

### Soil Fungi
- **Glucose Ammonium Nitrate Agar (GAN)**: Glucose 10g, NH4NO3 1g, rose bengal 30mg, agar 20g, water 1L. Substitute cyclosporin A (2-10mg) for rose bengal as useful variation. Incubate in dark.
- **Alpha-Cellulose Agar**: For nonsporulating soil isolates; contains alpha-cellulose, coconut milk, oatmeal, tomato paste
- **DRBC (Dichloran-Rose Bengal-Chloramphenicol Agar)**: For soil fungi, dried seeds, food products, osmotolerant fungi. Dichloran 2mg/L, rose bengal 25mg/L, chloramphenicol 0.1g/L.
- **DG18 (Dichloran-Glycerol Agar)**: 18% glycerol for osmotolerant, xerotolerant, and xerophilic fungi. aw = 0.955.

### Dung and Coprophilous Fungi
- **Dung Extract Agar (DEA)**: Air-dried dung (cow or horse) 200g boiled in 1L water 20 min, filter, add 20g agar. Variations replace 500ml water with dung extract in other media.
- **Cornmeal Dextrose Agar**: CMA + glucose 2g

### Extreme-Environment Fungi
- **Czapek-Dox Agar (no sucrose)**: For extreme-environment fungi
- **Alkaline Media**: Horikoshi I and II (Na2CO3 10g/L, pH ~10); Alkaline PDA (NaH2PO4 + Na2CO3 to pH 9.7)
- **Halophilic Media**: Czapek-Dox + NaCl 150-200g/L (rose bengal 60mg/L); incubate in dark at 28°C for 6-8 weeks

### Thermophilic Fungi
- **Emerson Yeast Starch Agar (EYSA)**: Yeast extract 4g, K2HPO4 1g, MgSO4 0.5g, soluble starch 15g, agar 20g, tap water 250ml + distilled water 750ml. Add gentamicin 50mg/L and penicillin G 100 units. For thermotolerant and thermophilic fungi.

### Freshwater and Aquatic Fungi
- **PmTG Agar**: Standard medium for chytrid isolation (peptone, milk, tryptone, glucose, agar)
- **mPmTG**: Weak version (1% agar) with antibiotics
- **KMV Agar**: Glucose 1g, gelatin hydrolysate 1g, crystal violet 10mg, agar 15g, seawater 1L, pH 7.0. For dermatophytes. With streptomycin for Littman Oxgall-Streptomycin Agar.

### Anaerobic Fungi
- **Basal Control Medium (BAM)**: Complex anaerobic medium with mineral solutions, volatile fatty acids, hemin, cellobiose, resazurin, cysteine. Requires anaerobic handling (CO2 atmosphere).

### Endophytes and Plant-Associated Fungi
- **Hypomyces Fruiting Agar (HFA)**: Two-part medium (potato infusion + glucose; malt extract + agar). Stimulates perithecia of Hypomyces polyporinus. For fungicolous fungi.

### Entomopathogenic Fungi
- **Grace's Insect Tissue Culture Medium**: Grace's medium 44.5g/L in distilled water. Add fetal bovine serum (50-100ml/L) and streptomycin (100mg/L) as needed. For fungal parasites of insects and invertebrates.

### Clinical and Medical Fungi
- **Brain-Heart Infusion Agar (BHIA)**: Calf-brain infusion 200g, beef-heart infusion 250g, peptone 10g, glucose 2g, NaCl 5g, agar 15g, water 1L. For clinical samples.
- **Chocolate Agar**: Casein + peptone medium + 5-10% defibrinated blood heated to 80°C. For clinical fungal cultures.
- **Caffeic Acid (Phenol Oxidase) Medium**: Tests for Cryptococcus neoformans melanin production.

### Cellulolytic Activity
- **Carboxymethyl Cellulose (CMC) Agar**: CMC 10g, yeast extract 0.5g, (NH4)2SO4 1g, NaNO3 2g, salts, agar 15g, water 1L. Detects cellulolytic activity.
- **Cellulose Azure Broth Tubes**: Two-layer system (basal medium + overlay with cellulose azure). Detects cellulolytic activity.

## Selective and Differential Media

### For Specific Fungal Groups
- **Creatine Agar**: For Penicillium and Aspergillus characterization; sucrose 30g, creatine 3g
- **Lactose-Yeast Extract Agar (LYA)**: Lactose 0.1-1g, yeast extract 0.1-0.5g, agar 15g. For dictyostelids
- **Lactose-Peptone Agar (LPA)**: Lactose 0.1-1g, peptone 0.1-1g, agar 15g. For dictyostelids
- **Hay Infusion Agar (HIA)**: Dried hay/grass 50g boiled in 1L water, K2HPO4 2g, agar 15g, pH 6.2. For protostelids, myxomycetes, dictyostelids, acrasids

### Antibiotics Commonly Used

| Antibiotic | Concentration | Target | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|--------|-------|
| Chloramphenicol | 50-250 mg/L | Bacteria | Most common antibacterial |
| Streptomycin | 50-100 mg/L | Bacteria | Add after autoclaving |
| Gentamicin | 50 mg/L | Bacteria | Broad-spectrum |
| Tetracycline | 100 mg/L | Bacteria | Dissolve in ethanol |
| Penicillin G | 100 units/L | Bacteria | Add after autoclaving |
| Ampicillin | 250 mg/L | Bacteria | For oomycete medium |
| Cycloheximide | 100-500 mg/L | Fungi (selective) | Suppresses many fungi |
| Thiabendazole | 10 mg/L | Fungi | Halophytophthora selection |
| Amphotericin-B | 2.5 mg/L | Fungi | In A/T oomycete medium |

## Media Storage

- Prepared media stored at 4°C; most viable 2-4 weeks
- Pour plates from molten media reheated in microwave or water bath
- Slant cultures at room temperature: many fungi remain viable for months to years
- Cryopreservation in liquid nitrogen for long-term storage
- Many chytrid cultures retain viability after freezing
