## Habitat and Distribution

Psilocybe quebecensis grows in sandy soils, particularly in
outwashes of streams, and in the decayed wood substratum of alder,
birch, fir, and spruce. It fruits in the late summer and fall. The
species was originally reported from Quebec, specifically in the
Jacques Cartier river valley. As a flood-plain specialist, P.
quebecensis is well adapted to riparian habitats where periodic
flooding deposits organic material and creates the moist, woody
substrates this species prefers.
