## Wind Tunnel Design

### Working Area

- **Dimensions**: 43 × 30 × 20 cm
- **Circuit type**: Closed loop (recirculating air)
- **Flow type**: Low-speed [[brown-rice-flour-substrate]]: 6g rice + 20ml deionized water in 50mm Petri plates. Inoculated with a *P. cubensis* isolate (from Badham 1980) or a sterile mutant strain.

### Growth Phase

1. Allow hyphae to fully cover the [[pf-tek-vermiculite-brown-rice-substrate-dry-barrier]]
2. Transfer colonized rice to 13 cm diameter pie tins
3. Add vermiculite and 25 ml water per tin
4. Cover with glass, place under cool white fluorescent light (2685 lux) for 10 days
5. Water with distilled water when weight loss exceeds 10 ml (determined by weighing)

### Primordia

After ~10 days, primordia reach 15–25 mm height and are ready for wind tunnel transfer.

## Experimental Protocol

1. Place primordia in the wind tunnel working area
2. Set: 12h/day photoperiod, 9 ± 3 cm/sec wind, 94 ± 3% RH, 24.5 ± 1.5°C
3. Remove after 72 hours at maturity

### Conditions Tested

| Variable | Conditions |
|----------|-----------|
| Strain | Fertile vs. sterile mutant |
| Light | 12h photoperiod / constant / darkness |
| Light direction | Above vs. below |
| Rotation | None / 3 rpm perpendicular / 3 rpm parallel to wind |
| Stage | Stages 1–2 vs. Stage 2+ |

### Rotation Experiments

- **Perpendicular (3 rpm)**: Tests gravity vs. wind dominance — upright growth indicates gravity wins
- **Parallel (3 rpm)**: Tests growth with equalized gravity, wind maintained
- **No rotation**: Standard condition showing full tropic response
