## AACT Disease Suppression Recipes

Updated: 2026-04-18

Dr. Elaine Ingham's approach to disease suppression using actively [[comparison-bacterial-dominant-vs-fungal-dominant-tea]] tea (molasses-based, no humic acid)
- Apply as foliar spray at 5 gallons per acre per month during growing season
- Start applications 2 weeks before bud break
- Continue monthly through the growing season
- If disease outbreak occurs, apply immediately every 3-5 days until resolved
- If disease alert is issued by extension service, apply weekly until alert stops, then continue every 10-14 days until harvest

## Fungal Diseases: Mildew

Mildew requires fungal biomass in the tea. Bacterial tea alone is generally not effective against mildew.

Recipe approach:
- Brew fungal dominant tea (humic acid-based, minimal molasses)
- Ensure adequate fungal biomass through pre-activation of compost with oatmeal
- Apply as foliar spray starting early in the season
- "Getting fungal compost tea on later in the season is the only way not to have problems with mildew"
- Apply weekly when mildew alerts are issued
- If outbreak occurs, apply immediately every 3-5 days until resolved

## Root Diseases

Root diseases are managed through soil drench applications that establish beneficial biology in the root zone:

- Apply 15 gallons of compost tea per acre as a soil drench
- Use a balanced tea with both bacterial and fungal biomass
- Include protozoa to cycle nutrients and maintain bacterial populations
- Apply 2 weeks before planting, then as a top-dress drench on compost in fall
- For established disease, apply every 3 days until resolved
