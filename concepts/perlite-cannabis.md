## Perlite for Cannabis

Perlite is a versatile and widely used [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]]. It is made from sand or volcanic glass that has been expanded by heat, resulting in a lightweight, porous material with excellent drainage and aeration properties.

## What is Perlite

Perlite is a naturally occurring siliceous volcanic rock that, when heated to approximately 1600 degrees F (870 degrees C), expands to many times its original volume. The expansion creates a lightweight, white granular material with a closed-cell foam-like structure. Each particle has an irregular surface covered in tiny cavities that hold water and nutrients.

## Properties and Characteristics

- **Lightweight**: One of the lightest growing media available.
- **pH neutral**: Does not affect the pH of the growing medium.
- **Inert**: Does not react with nutrient solutions or decompose over time.
- **CEC of zero**: Perlite has zero cation-exchange capacity, meaning it holds no nutrients on its surface for later root uptake.
- **Drainage**: Excellent drainage properties; drains faster than most other media.
- **Aeration**: Provides outstanding air porosity in soil and soilless mixes.
- **Porosity**: Approximately 90% porosity with about 30% air space.
- **Sterile**: Produced at high temperatures, making it free of pathogens and weed seeds.

## Perlite for Cloning

Coarse sharp sand, fine vermiculite, and perlite work well together to root cuttings. Sand and perlite are fast-draining, while vermiculite adds moisture retention. A mix of fine perlite and fine vermiculite is a common rooting medium for [[coco-coir-cannabis]] retains much more water than perlite and has a higher CEC. Coco is better suited as a primary growing medium, while perlite functions best as an amendment.

## Advantages of Perlite

- **Excellent drainage**: Prevents overwatering and root rot.
- **Improves aeration**: Increases oxygen availability to roots.
- **Lightweight**: Easy to work with and reduces container weight.
- **Inexpensive**: One of the most affordable growing amendments.
- **Sterile**: Free of pests, diseases, and weed seeds.
- **Long-lasting**: Does not decompose or break down.
- **pH neutral**: Does not alter the pH of the growing medium.

## Disadvantages

- **Floats**: Tends to float when flooded, causing problems in flood systems.
- **No nutrient holding**: Zero CEC means it cannot buffer nutrients.
- **Dust**: Dry perlite dust is irritating to lungs; wear a mask when handling.
- **Too light**: Can blow away in outdoor applications.
