## Microscopic Features

- **Spores:** Elliptical to broadly elliptical, roughened with warty ornamentation,
  7–9 × 5–6 µm, rusty brown in deposit, thick-walled
- **Basidia:** 4-spored, clavate, approximately 35 µm long
- **Cheilocystidia:** Ventricose to lageniform, occasionally capitate
- **Pileipellis:** Cutis of cylindrical to slightly encrusted hyphae
