# Psilocybin Phosphorylation and Synthesis

The conversion of psilocin (1) to psilocybin (2) requires phosphorylation of the
4-hydroxy group with a phosphate ester. Shirota, Hakamata, and Goda evaluated
multiple phosphorylation methods before selecting the tetrabenzylpyrophosphate
route, which—combined with their serendipitous discovery of a zwitterionic
intermediate—enabled practical gram-scale synthesis of psilocybin as a
chromatography-free process.

and washed with ethanol to afford the product as a white needle crystalline
powder. No anion-exchange resin or other chromatographic purification is needed.

## Yield and Scale

The isolated yield of psilocybin from psilocin is greater than 72%, even for
gram-scale production. This represents a dramatic improvement over previously
reported yields of 20% (Troxler, Seemann and Hofmann, 1959) and 47% (Nichols
and Frescas). The overall yield from 4-hydroxyindole through to psilocybin
exceeds 37% across six steps, with each intermediate isolated as a crystalline
material without chromatographic purification.

## Psilocybin Characterization Data

The synthesized psilocybin melts at 190-198°C (literature values: 185-195°C and
210-212°C). UV spectrum (MeOH) shows absorption maxima at 221.0 (log epsilon
4.44), 267.5 (3.66), 278.5 (3.57), and 290.0 (3.42) nm. The UV profile is
distinct from that of psilocin, providing a reliable identification marker.

IR spectrum (KBr) shows characteristic bands at 3266, 3034, 2731, 2369, 1620,
1580, 1505, 1439, 1352, 1298, 1244, 1154, 1103, 1061, 926, 858, and 804
cm-1. The presence of phosphate-related absorptions (broad O-H stretch around
3266 cm-1, P-O-C stretches in the 1000-1100 cm-1 region) confirms successful
phosphorylation.

1H NMR (D2O, 400 MHz): delta 7.22 (1H, d, J=7.6 Hz, H-7), 7.18 (1H, s,
H-2), 7.13 (1H, t, J=7.6 Hz, H-6), 6.98 (1H, d, J=7.6 Hz, H-5), 3.44
(2H, t, J=7.2 Hz, H2-2'), 3.28 (2H, t, J=7.2 Hz, H2-1'), 2.86 (6H, s,
NMe2). 13C NMR (D2O + MeOH, 100 MHz): delta 146.4 (C-4, split), 139.4
(C-7a), 124.8 (C-6), 123.3 (C-2), 119.1 (C-3a, split), 109.5 (C-5a, split),
108.6 (C-3), 108.4 (C-7), 59.7 (C-2'), 43.4 (NMe2), 22.4 (C-1').
