# PF TEK Supply Lists and Equipment

## Overview

The [[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]] (PF) TEK is a simplified [[boil-a-bag-mushroom-cultivation-method]] designed for amateur cultivators using readily available materials. All equipment and supplies can be sourced from common [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-smartshop-and-retail-outlets]] — supermarkets, drugstores, hardware stores, pet shops, and scientific supply houses. The method is organized into three stages, each requiring its own set of materials.

## Stage One: Jar Preparation and Culturing

These supplies are needed for preparing substrate jars, sterilizing them, and inoculating with spore solution.

### Substrate Materials

| Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Organic [[brown-rice-flour]] | Organic food stores | Primary nutrient source |
| Horticultural vermiculite | Garden centers | Medium or fine grade; not powdery |
| Distilled or filtered drinking water | Supermarket | Avoid chlorinated tap water |

### Jar Preparation

| Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulderless half-pint jars with lids | Supermarket (Kerr or Ball brand) | Shoulderless design allows easy cake removal |
| Measuring cups and spoons | Supermarket | For precise substrate ratios |
| Large pot for steaming | Kitchen | Must accommodate jars with clearance |
| Heavy-duty tin foil | Supermarket | Covers jar lids during sterilization |
| Heavy-duty masking tape (professional grade) | Hardware store | Seals inoculation holes |
| Ice pick | Hardware store | For punching needle holes in jar lids |

### Inoculation

| Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spore syringe | Specialized supplier | Contains [[query-how-to-store-mushroom-spores-long-term]] in sterile water |
| Butane lighter or alcohol lamp | Hardware store | For flame-sterilizing the needle |
| Rubbing alcohol | Drugstore | For [[challenge-microorganisms-microwave-surface-sterilization]] |

These supplies create the controlled environment needed for mushrooms to fruit from colonized cakes.

### Humidity and Maintenance

| Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "All-purpose" spray bottle with adjustable nozzle | Hardware store | Critical equipment; must deliver strong spray |

## Maximum Formula Note

The PF "maximum formula" — a nutrient-enriched variant of the standard substrate recipe — requires a slightly different jar preparation:

1. Loosely load the half-pint jar and level the substrate.
2. Cover the jar mouth with one hand and lightly slam the bottom against the other palm to settle the mix to approximately 1/2 inch from the top.
3. Clean the inside of the jar walls down to the substrate level.
4. Fill the remaining space to the top with **dry vermiculite** (contamination barrier).

Maximum formula jars should be incubated at room temperature and not heated above 70°F. The lid should be loosened (or lid holes untaped) to allow gas exchange. Watch for loose water accumulation in the jar, which indicates excess moisture.

## Sourcing Philosophy

A core principle of the PF TEK is accessibility. Every item on these lists was chosen because it can be purchased at ordinary retail establishments without raising suspicion. The method was explicitly designed so that no specialized laboratory equipment is required, making it approachable for complete beginners.
