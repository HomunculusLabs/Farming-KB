## Fungal Drought Tolerance and Plant Water Relations

[[fungi-in-the-environment-fungal-endophytes-plant-communities]] can significantly influence
plant water relations and help alleviate drought stress in host plants. This
role may be as ecologically important as the nutritional benefits that fungi
provide, and in some cases may represent the primary rationale for the
persistence of mycorrhizal symbioses in natural ecosystems.

## Mechanisms of Drought Protection

Mycorrhizal associations improve plant water relations through several
mechanisms: direct water flow through fungal hyphae, improvement in plant
phosphate nutrition, and altered hormonal balance in the host plant. These
mechanisms can operate independently or synergistically to enhance plant
survival during periods of water deficit.
