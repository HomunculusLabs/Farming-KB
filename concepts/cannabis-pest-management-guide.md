## Prevention (First Line of Defense)

The most effective pest management is preventing pests from entering your grow space:

### Physical Barriers
- **Sealed grow space**: Close all gaps, cracks, and vents
- **Intake filters**: HEPA or fine mesh filters on all air intakes
- **Screened vents**: Fine window screen on exhaust and intake vents
- **Positive air pressure**: If possible, maintain slightly higher pressure inside the grow room than outside — air flows out, not in

### Hygiene
- **Clean grow area**: Remove dead plant material, debris, and standing water
- **Sterilize between grows**: Clean and sanitize all surfaces, containers, and equipment between crops
- **No outdoor clothing**: Change clothes before entering the grow area if you have been in the garden or around other plants
- **Quarantine new plants**: Isolate any new clones or plants for 1–2 weeks before introducing them to your main grow

### Monitoring
- **Yellow sticky traps**: Hang near plants to catch flying pests and monitor pest populations
- **Regular inspection**: Check plants thoroughly at least 2–3 times per week, especially the undersides of leaves
- **Magnification**: Use a jeweler's loupe or hand lens (30–60x) to inspect closely for tiny pests

## Common Cannabis Pests

### Spider Mites

**Identification**: Tiny (0.5mm) spider-like arachnids. Look for:
- Tiny dots on leaf undersides
- Fine webbing between leaves and branches (advanced infestation)
- Small yellow or white speckling (stippling) on leaves
- Leaves eventually turn yellow and die

**Treatment**:
- **Spray with water**: A strong blast of water can dislodge mites (temporary)
- **[[comparison-neem-oil-cannabis-vs-diatomaceous-earth-cannabis]] on the soil surface to prevent egg-laying

### Thrips

**Identification**: Very small (1–2mm) slender insects. Look for:
- Silver or bronze streaking on leaves
- Tiny black fecal spots on leaves
- Small insects visible on leaves and flowers
- Deformed new growth

**Treatment**:
- Blue sticky traps (thrips are attracted to blue)
- Neem oil or insecticidal soap sprays
- Spinosad-based sprays

### Aphids

**Identification**: Small (1–3mm) soft-bodied insects, often green, black, or white. Found in clusters on new growth.

**Signs**:
- Clusters of insects on stems and new leaves
- Curled, deformed leaves
- Sticky honeydew on leaves and below
- Sooty mold growing on honeydew

**Treatment**:
- Insecticidal soap
- Neem oil

### Whiteflies

**Identification**: Tiny white flying insects that flutter up when plants are disturbed. Found on leaf undersides.

**Signs**:
- White flies rising when plants are shaken
- Yellowing leaves
- Sticky honeydew on leaves

**Treatment**:
- Yellow sticky traps
- Neem oil or insecticidal soap
