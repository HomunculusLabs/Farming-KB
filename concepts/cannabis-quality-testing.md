## Cannabis Quality Testing

## Overview

Cannabis quality testing encompasses the assessment of potency, purity, flavor, and overall desirability of harvested cannabis and its derivatives. According to Cervantes, [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]] (trichomes). Using a 10-30X microscope or jeweler's loupe, growers assess [[african-landrace-sativa-strains]] to reach amber stage; this wastes their unique properties

### What to Look For

- Stalked glandular trichomes with a clear column and round ball on top contain THC
- Hair-like cystolith trichomes contain virtually no THC
- The highest THC concentration is at the base of the resin head
- Senescing glands turn brown, get smaller, and decompose with diminishing THC

## Visual Quality Indicators

### For Dried Flower

- Well-cured buds have an even glow when burned
- Smooth, even smoking indicates proper drying
- Green, harsh taste indicates fast or improper drying
- Resin glands should be visible and intact, not knocked off

### For Hash and Extracts

- Potent resin powder is creamy white to gold in color
- Pressing darkens hash through oxidation
- Dark exterior with blonde interior indicates proper pressing
- Green color in hash indicates contamination with plant matter
- White and clean ash after burning indicates purity

## Microscope Testing

An inexpensive microscope (10-30X) is one of the most valuable tools for quality assessment:

- Inspect trichomes on living plants to determine harvest timing
- Grade resin powder from sieving by examining debris content
- Compare first batch (2-5 minutes turning) with later batches
- Progressively more debris visible in lower-quality batches
- Scan for pests and mold on harvested material

## The Bubble Test (Melt Quality)

For water-extracted hash, the "bubble test" is a key quality indicator:

- Full-melt [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]]
- Non-bubbling hash contains too much plant contamination

## Laboratory Analysis

Professional laboratory testing provides the most accurate quality data:

- Gas chromatography can measure exact THC content of harvested buds
- Testing can quantify individual cannabinoid profiles (THC, CBD, CBN, etc.)
- Residual solvent testing ensures safety of extracts
- Pesticide and contaminant screening identifies health hazards
- Terpene profiling characterizes aroma and flavor compounds

## Taste and Aroma Assessment

Sensory evaluation remains an important quality metric:

- Slowly dried buds taste sweet and smoke smooth
