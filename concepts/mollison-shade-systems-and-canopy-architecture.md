### Shade and Livestock
In the tropics and subtropics, a ridge planting of pines or Casuarinas
with a wind gap left below the crowns affords both shade and an induced
breeze that discourages flies and mosquitoes. Such ridges are also rich
mulch sources for lower slopes.

Blizzards will kill livestock and newborn lambs, and even hardy adapted
animals can lose 30% of their body weight in 3 days of blizzard.
Shelterbelt trees serve to reduce ground temperatures up to 15 degrees C
in desert conditions, and provide critical shade for livestock welfare.

## Creating Shaded Microsites

### Natural Shade Patterns
Gullies, ridgetops, natural shade, and the sides of earth banks all
create shade that invites new species to establish. Every shaded area is
a more moist site. Designers can plan such shaded niches by placing
large rocks, earth mounds, and structures to cast useful shadows.
