## The Case Against the Psychedelic Reading

While this psychedelic reading of Alice remains fashionable in psychedelic
circles, it seems highly unlikely to be true. There is no record of Carroll
using mushrooms or other drugs himself, and no incentive for him to hide any
such habits secretively within the lines of a children's fairy tale. An array
of psychoactive drugs, including opium, were freely available in Victorian
society, purchased over the counter at most pharmacists. Consuming these for
pleasurable ends carried nothing like the stigma nor the legal penalties that
it does today: Conan Doyle, for example, saw no problem with using cocaine.
