# Online Homestead Sales

Selling homestead products online extends reach beyond local markets and creates passive [[homesteading-income-streams]]. Platforms like Etsy, Shopify, and social media enable direct-to-consumer sales of shelf-stable and value-added products. This guide covers platforms, shipping regulations, photography, and marketing.

## Product Categories for Online Sales

### What Sells Online (Shelf-Stable)
- Dried herbs and tea blends (see [[homestead-herb-business]])
- [[hot-sauce-making]] and fermented products (see [[fermented-hot-sauce-guide]])
- Preserves: jam, jelly, canned goods (pH below 4.6 only)
- Baked goods: shipped breads, cookies, granola
- Tinctures and herbal remedies
- Salves, balms, body care products
- Beeswax candles and wraps
- [[cotter-mushroom-products-and-value-added-applications]]: dried, powders, grow kits (see [[homestead-mushroom-business]])
- Seeds and plant starts (seasonal)
- Wool, yarn, fiber products
- Soap (cold process, hot process)
- Eggs: NOT shippable (perishable)
- Fresh meat: NOT shippable without expensive cold-chain logistics

### What Does Not Sell Online
- Fresh produce (perishable, shipping costs exceed value)
- Eggs (fragile, perishable, USPS regulations)
- Fresh meat (requires cold chain, expensive, regulated)
- Frozen products (requires dry ice, insulated shipping)

## Platform Comparison

### Etsy
- Best for: handmade goods, small batches, craft products
- Fees: $0.20/listing (4 months), 6.5% transaction fee + 3% + $0.25 payment processing
- Pros: built-in audience (90+ million buyers), easy setup, good for beginners
- Cons: saturated marketplace, fee structure, limited branding control
- Ideal products: salves, candles, dried herbs, tea blends, soap

### Shopify
- Best for: established brands, higher volume, custom storefront
- Fees: $29-299/month + payment processing (2.9% + $0.30)
- Pros: full branding control, own website, professional appearance
- Cons: monthly cost, you drive all traffic, more technical setup
- Ideal for: farms with established customer base, product lines of 10+ items

### Instagram / Facebook Shops
- Best for: building brand awareness, visual products, community engagement
- Fees: free to post; Instagram shopping requires Shopify or BigCommerce integration
- Pros: visual storytelling, direct connection with customers, free marketing
- Cons: algorithm changes, time-intensive, conversion rates lower than dedicated stores
- Strategy: post daily farm content, stories for behind-the-scenes, link in bio to store

### Local Delivery Platforms
- Barn2Door, FoodHub, Harvie
- Best for: local CSA and [[csa-farm-share-model]] management
- Fees: varies, typically 2-5% transaction fee
- Connects online ordering with local pickup/delivery

## Shipping Regulations

### Food Shipping Requirements
- Labeling: FDA requires product name, net weight, ingredients, business name/address
- Cottage food products: must comply with your state's cottage food law (most restrict to in-state sales)
- Interstate commerce: selling food across state lines generally requires a commercial kitchen and FDA registration
- Alcohol: hot sauces with high vinegar content are generally fine; check ABV (must be non-alcoholic)

### Shipping Best Practices
- Use appropriate packaging: bubble wrap, box inserts, peanuts
- Temperature-sensitive items: avoid shipping in [[extreme-heat-preparedness]] (May-September in hot regions)
- Insulated shipping: foam-lined boxes + ice packs for heat-sensitive products ($3-8 per package)
- Shipping carriers: USPS Priority Mail (2-3 days) for most packages; UPS/FedEx for heavy or large items
- Calculate shipping costs accurately: weigh products + packaging, use carrier calculators
- Offer free shipping on orders over $50-75 (build cost into product prices)

### Organic Claims
- You cannot label products "organic" without USDA certification
- "Naturally grown," "chemical-free," "no-spray" are acceptable alternatives
- "Made with organic ingredients" requires at least 70% certified organic ingredients

## Photography

### Equipment
- Smartphone camera is sufficient (modern phones shoot excellent product photos)
- Optional: lightbox ($30-60) for consistent lighting
- Backdrop: white poster board, parchment paper, wood boards, fabric
- Natural light: shoot near a window on overcast days (diffused light is best)

### Product Photography Tips
- Clean, simple backgrounds (white, wood, marble)
- Shoot from multiple angles: top-down, 45-degree, straight-on
- Include scale references (hand holding product, next to common object)
- Show product in use (salve on skin, tea in a cup, herbs in cooking)
- Consistent style across all product photos builds brand recognition
- Edit for brightness and color accuracy (not heavy filtering)
- Minimum 5 photos per listing: main, detail, in-use, ingredient/close-up, size reference

### Lifestyle Photography
- Farm setting photos build trust and story
- Process photos (harvesting, drying, packing) add authenticity
- Seasonal content keeps feeds fresh
- People in photos increase engagement (show the farmer)

## Social Media Marketing

### Content Calendar
- Post 3-5 times per week minimum
- Content mix: 40% product, 30% farm life/behind-the-scenes, 20% education/tips, 10% personal/story
- Use Reels/short-form video (highest reach currently)
- Hashtags: 10-15 per post, mix of niche (#grassfedbeef) and broad (#homesteadlife)

### Building an Audience
- Consistency matters more than perfection
- Engage with comments and DMs within 24 hours
- Collaborate with complementary accounts (cross-promotion)
- Offer exclusive discounts to followers
- Email list: offer 10% off first order for signups (converts 3-5x better than social)

### Email Marketing
- Mailchimp or similar (free tier for <500 contacts)
- Monthly newsletter: new products, seasonal availability, farm updates
- Segment: buyers vs. browsers; send different content
- Open rate benchmark: 20-30% for small businesses

## Pricing for Online Sales

Online pricing should be higher than [[farmers-market-selling-guide]] to cover shipping and platform fees:

| Cost Factor | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Product cost | $2-8 |
| Packaging | $1-3 |
| Shipping materials | $1-2 |
| Shipping cost (3 lb, Priority) | $8-12 |
| Platform fees (10-12%) | $1-3 |
| Total cost | $13-28 |
| Retail price (online) | $20-40 |

Rule of thumb: online price = farmers market price + $3-5 for shipping/packaging/fees.

## Customer Service

- Ship within 1-3 business days (state this clearly)
- Provide tracking numbers automatically
