# Natural Farming Pest Management

Organic pest management works by building healthy ecosystems where pests are naturally controlled by predators, using cultural practices that reduce pest pressure, and applying targeted organic treatments only when necessary.

## Ecological Approach

The foundation of natural pest management is a healthy ecosystem:
- Healthy soil grows healthy plants that resist pests
- Biodiversity provides [[fukuoka-natural-predator-protection]] populations
- No monocultures = no pest population explosions
- Accept some pest damage — the goal is management, not eradication

## Cultural Practices

### Prevention (first line of defense)
- **Crop rotation** breaks [[hamilton-pest-and-disease-control-organic]] cycles
- **Healthy soil** with adequate nutrients produces resistant plants
- **Proper spacing** ensures good airflow (reduces fungal diseases)
- **Timely planting** avoids peak pest emergence windows
- **Resistant varieties** selected for local pest pressures

### Companion Planting for Pest Control
- Marigolds: deter nematodes and cabbage white butterflies
- Nasturtiums: trap crop for aphids
- Basil: repels tomato hornworms and flies
- Garlic: deters aphids, cabbage maggots, and Japanese beetles
- Borage: attracts [[hamilton-beneficial-insects-and-biological-pest-control]], repels tomato hornworm
- Dill: attracts [[parasitic-wasps-biological-pest-control]] that control caterpillars
- Calendula: attracts hoverflies and aphid-eating insects

### Trap Cropping
- Plant preferred host plants at the garden edge to draw pests away from crops
- Nasturtiums trap aphids away from vegetables
- Radishes trap flea beetles away from eggplant and brassicas
- Blue Hubbard squash traps squash vine borers

## Biological Controls

### Encouraging Beneficial Insects
- **Ladybugs** eat aphids, scale, mealybugs (attract with dill, fennel, yarrow)
- **Lacewings** larvae eat aphids, thrips, mites, whiteflies
- **Parasitic wasps** attack caterpillars, aphids, whiteflies
- **Ground beetles** eat slugs, cutworms, root maggots
- **Hoverflies** larvae eat aphids (attract with alyssum, buckwheat, dill)
- **Praying mantis** eat a wide range of insects (generalist predator)

### Ducks and Chickens for Pest Control
- Ducks eat slugs, snails, and many insect pests
- Chickens scratch up soil-dwelling pests and eat grasshoppers
- Rotate poultry through garden beds between crops
- A small flock (3-5 ducks) can clear a garden of slugs effectively

## Organic Sprays and Treatments

### Neem Oil
- Disrupts insect hormone systems, prevents feeding
- Effective against aphids, whiteflies, thrips, caterpillars
- Apply at dusk to avoid harming beneficial insects
- Mix 1-2 tsp neem oil + 1/2 tsp dish soap per quart of water

### Insecticidal Soap
- Kills soft-bodied insects by disrupting cell membranes
- Effective against aphids, mites, whiteflies
- Must contact the insect directly — no residual effect
- Mix 1 tbsp liquid soap per quart of water

### Diatomaceous Earth
- Abrasive powder damages insect exoskeletons
- Effective against slugs, ants, beetles, flea beetles
- Apply as a dry barrier around plants; reapply after rain
- Food-grade only; wear a mask when applying (silica dust hazard)

### Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt)
- Soil bacterium that kills caterpillars when ingested
- Safe [[query-how-do-i-design-habitat-for-beneficial-insects]], humans, and animals
- Apply to foliage of plants being damaged by caterpillars
- Most effective on young caterpillars
