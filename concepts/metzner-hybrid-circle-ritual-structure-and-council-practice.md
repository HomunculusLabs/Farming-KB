## The Talking Staff and Council Practice

A distinctive feature of these ceremonies was the use of the **talking
staff** or **singing staff** — a practice that "seems to have originated
among the Indians of the [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]]" and is more generally
referred to as "**council**."

In council practice, only the person holding the circulating staff may
speak or sing. There is "no discussion, questioning or interpretation"
of the kind that would occur in conventional group psychotherapy
formats. This creates a space of deep listening and respectful
witnessing, where each person's experience is held without analysis
or judgment.

## Preparation and Integration

The ceremony was embedded in a larger process of preparation and
integration:

**Preparation** (before the circle):
- Sharing of intentions and purposes among participants
- Meditation practice
- Solo time in nature
- Expressive arts modalities: drawing, painting, journal work

**Integration** (after the circle, sometimes the following morning):
- Participants share something of the lessons learned
- Discussion of how insights might be applied in daily life
- Ongoing therapeutic or contemplative support
