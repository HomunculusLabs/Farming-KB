# Cannabis for Cancer-Related Symptoms

## Cancer Overview

Every year, cancer takes credit for 13% of all deaths worldwide. According to
the orthodox model of medicine, 30% of all cancers are attributed to known and
avoidable risk factors. Cancer-inducing materials, called carcinogens, exist
almost everywhere.

Within the orthodox model, cancer (malignant neoplasm) starts with the
corruption of genetic material (a mutation) inside a normally healthy cell.
Evidence suggests that cellular cancer may occur quite frequently in everybody,
but that normally the body's own proper immune defenses can cure these threats.

Since then the scientific evidence of cannabinoids' ability to counteract

preventing and treating specific cancer formations. Studies also lay claim to
cannabinoids' abilities to mitigate common side effects of chemotherapeutic
agents.

confidence that cannabinoids may prevent, inhibit, and destroy cancer cells, as
