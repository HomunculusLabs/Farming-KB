## Smartshops

Smartshops are retail outlets that specialise in psychoactive herbal substances, vitamins,
mineral supplements, and other health products. They are most closely associated with the
Netherlands, where they emerged in the late 1990s as a significant commercial force in the
distribution of [[query-how-can-i-grow-mushrooms-alongside-garden-vegetables]] other natural products in a
retail environment, they created a market infrastructure that normalised access to these
substances. It is estimated that there were approximately 120 to 150 smartshops in the
Netherlands, concentrated particularly in Amsterdam. According to the Dutch Risk Assessment
Report, hallucinogenic mushrooms accounted for approximately 50% of smartshop turnover,
making them the single most important product category.

A 2001 study of young people aged 12 and above in the Netherlands found that 64% of those
aged 18 and older who had used magic mushrooms in the previous year had purchased them in
smartshops. Smartshops also sold related products including sclerotia (marketed as
philosopher's stones or truffles), particularly from [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]]) also sold hallucinogenic mushrooms. In some
areas, paddo cabs operated home delivery services for hallucinogenic mushrooms, reflecting
the degree of commercial sophistication in the Dutch market.
