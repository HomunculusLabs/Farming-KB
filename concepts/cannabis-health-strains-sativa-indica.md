# Cannabis Strains (Sativa vs Indica)

Medicinal cannabis consists of two basic species, [[cannabis-indica]], which look different and contain different ratios of
the primary cannabinoids. These ratios are relevant when selecting a
strain for specific therapeutic purposes. There are hundreds of
varieties of cannabis with similar appearance and characteristics,
growing on every continent except Antarctica.

## Sativa Characteristics

[[african-landrace-sativa-strains]] generally present with a higher THC-to-CBD/CBN ratio.
Key characteristics include:

- Generally stimulating, energizing, and uplifting
- More mental and emotional in effect
- Usually more extrovert in character
- Best suited for daytime use
- Increases alertness
- Consider for depression
- Provides [[bay-11-medical-sativa-granddaddy-purp-pain-relief-oner]] and muscle relaxation
- THC binds both CB1 and CB2 receptors

## Indica Characteristics

Indica strains generally present with a lower THC-to-CBD/CBN ratio.
Key characteristics include:

- Generally sedating, relaxing, and grounding
- More physical in effect
- Usually more introvert in character
- Better suited for after work or bedtime
- Functions as a sleeping aid
- Consider for anxiety
- Provides pain relief and muscle relaxation
- CBD has a greater affinity for CB2 receptors

## Physical Differences

Indica plants tend to be short and stalky, with wider leaves than
sativas. Sativa plants usually grow longer, taller, and display a finer
leaf structure. These physical differences reflect underlying chemical
composition differences that are therapeutically significant.

## THC:CBD Ratios and Therapeutic Effects

The ratio of THC to CBD determines the degree to which the balancing
properties of the whole plant's constituents are additive or cancel
each other out. Sativa strains, with higher [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]], tend to
activate more CB1 receptors (central nervous system). Indica strains,
with higher CBD content, tend to activate more CB2 receptors (immune
system and peripheral tissues).

Raw, fresh leaf plant matter such as cannabis juice has a significantly
higher CBD content in the form of CBD-acid, with a greater affinity to
activate CB2. This makes raw preparations particularly suitable for
immune and [[cannabis-for-inflammatory-conditions]].

## Selecting Strains for Specific Conditions

Choosing the right strain depends on the body's present endocannabinoid
state and the nature of the condition being treated:

- Neurological disorders or mental/emotional problems: Likely CB1-
  deficient; choose sativa or sativa-dominant strains with higher
  THC:CBD ratios
- Immune-compromised states or chronic inflammation: Likely CB2-
  deficient; choose indica or indica-dominant strains with lower
  THC:CBD ratios
- Anxiety: Indica-dominant strains preferred for their sedating,
  relaxing, and grounding effects
- Depression: Sativa-dominant strains preferred for their uplifting
  and energizing properties
- Insomnia: Indica and indica-dominant strains with lower THC:CBD
  combinations encourage sedation and relaxation
- Pain: Both strains provide relief; indica may be preferred for
  nighttime use due to sedating properties
