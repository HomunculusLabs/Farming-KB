### Fish Amino Acids (FAA)
- **Purpose**: Nitrogen and calcium source during vegetation, phosphorus source during early bloom
- **Red fish FAA**: High phosphorus — used during early bloom phase
- **Blue/white fish FAA**: High nitrogen and calcium — used during [[cervantes-vegetative-phase]]

### Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB)
- **Purpose**: Serum for EM-1 ([[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]] (PM)
- **Role**: Enhances the soil microbiome and serves as a carrier for other inputs

### Water Soluble Calcium (WSC)
- **Purpose**: Natural calcium source from brown egg shells
- **Importance**: Plants need calcium for structural growth and cell wall integrity

### Water Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WsCaP)
- **Purpose**: Natural calcium-phosphate source from cooked, crushed, and extracted cow bones
- **Alternative source**: Tobacco plants
- **Timing**: Used heaviest during early flower to prevent plants from getting weak

### Water Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WSPa)
- **Purpose**: Made from coaled sesame straw
- **Timing**: Applied during transition periods; helps build plant strength

### Beneficial Indigenous Microbes (BIM / IMO)
Decompose organics and make nutrients bioavailable — "the tough guys of the microbial world." Added to [[bacterial-vs-fungal-compost-teas]]; mulch and top-feeding support the [[cannabis-cultivar-microbial-community-effects]] in soil bins.

### Brown Rice Vinegar / Apple Rice Vinegar (BRV/ARV)
Carrier solution and acid reducer. Used in seed soak solutions and as a general carrier for other inputs.

## Cannabis Lifecycle Feeding Schedule

### Seedling Stage
- Seeds soaked in Seed Soak Solution: 100 ml water with a drop each of BRV/ARV, FPJ, OHN, WSC
- Soak 24 hours, transfer to clean paper towel, store in dark
- Germination within 24-48 hours

### Vegetative Stage (per gallon of water)

| Input | Rate |
|-------|------|
| FPJ | 8 mL/Gal |
| OHN | 4 mL/Gal |
| FAA | 4 mL/Gal |
| BIM | 1/2 tsp/Gal |
| WSC | 3 mL/Gal |
| WSPa | 3 mL/Gal |
| WSCaP | Transition to flower |
| Beneficial Tea | Weekly or twice weekly as needed |

### Early Flower (weeks 1-4)

| Input | Rate |
|-------|------|
| FPJ | 6 mL/Gal |
| FFJ | 6 mL/Gal |
| OHN | 4 mL/Gal |
| FAA | 4 mL/Gal |
| WSC | 4 mL/Gal |
| WSPa | 3 mL/Gal |
| WSCaP | 4 mL/Gal |
| Beneficial Tea | 2x/week with BIM, LAB, [[ultra-low-cost-mineral-leaching-and-sea-salt-replenishment]] |

FPJ transitions during this period. FFJ uses green/under-ripe fruits. General compost/BIM-rich teas recommended.

### Middle Flower (weeks 4-7)

| Input | Rate |
