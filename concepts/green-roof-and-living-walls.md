# Green Roofs and Living Walls

Green roofs and living walls integrate vegetation into building envelopes, providing insulation, stormwater management, biodiversity habitat, and aesthetic benefits. They are advanced [[water-management]] and [[williams-pdc-ecological-design-principles]] techniques that connect buildings to natural systems.

## Green Roof Types

### Extensive Green Roofs

Lightweight, low-maintenance systems designed for minimal intervention.

- **Depth**: 2-6 inches of growing medium
- **Weight**: 15-30 lbs per square foot (saturated)
- **Plant types**: Sedums, succulents, mosses, drought-tolerant natives
- **Irrigation**: Generally none required after establishment
- **Maintenance**: 1-2 inspections per year; weeding as needed
- **Cost**: Lower installation cost ($15-25 per square foot)
- **Structural requirement**: Most existing roofs can support extensive systems with reinforcement
- **Lifespan**: 40-50+ years (protects underlying membrane from UV and temperature extremes)

### Intensive Green Roofs

Heavy-duty systems that support a wider range of plants, including shrubs and small trees.

- **Depth**: 6-24+ inches of growing medium
- **Weight**: 60-150 lbs per square foot (saturated)
- **Plant types**: Perennials, grasses, shrubs, small trees, vegetables, herbs
- **Irrigation**: Regular irrigation required; may include automated drip systems
- **Maintenance**: Regular gardening — weeding, pruning, fertilizing, watering
- **Cost**: Higher installation cost ($25-60+ per square foot)
- **Structural requirement**: Significant structural engineering required; most roofs cannot support without major reinforcement
- **Use cases**: Rooftop gardens, community spaces, [[edible-landscaping]] on commercial buildings

### Semi-Intensive Green Roofs

A middle ground combining features of both extensive and intensive systems.

- **Depth**: 4-8 inches of growing medium
- **Weight**: 30-50 lbs per square foot
- **Plant types**: Sedums, grasses, herbs, shallow-rooted perennials
- **Irrigation**: Occasional supplemental watering
- **Maintenance**: Quarterly maintenance typical

## Green Roof Installation

### Structural Assessment
- Consult a structural engineer before any green roof installation
- Calculate total saturated weight of all layers (membrane, drainage, soil, plants, water, snow load)
- Existing roofs may need reinforcement or the system may need to be lightweight

### Layer System (Bottom to Top)
1. **Structural deck**: The building roof surface
2. **Vapor barrier**: Prevents moisture from entering the building
3. **Root barrier**: Prevents plant roots from penetrating the waterproof membrane
4. **Waterproof membrane**: Most critical layer — protects the building from water damage (TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen)
5. **Drainage layer**: Ensures excess water flows to drains (dimpled plastic sheets, gravel)
6. **Filter fabric**: Prevents soil particles from clogging the drainage layer
7. **Growing medium**: Engineered lightweight substrate (not garden soil — too heavy and retains too much water)
8. **Plants**: Selected based on roof type, climate, and maintenance capacity

### Plant Selection for Green Roofs

**Extensive roofs** (drought, wind, shallow soil):
- Sedum species (Sedum acre, S. album, S. kamtschaticum)
- Delosperma (ice plant)
- Allium cernuum (nodding onion)
- Talinum calycinum (fameflower)
- Native grasses (little bluestem, prairie dropseed in deeper substrates)

**Intensive roofs** (deeper soil, irrigation available):
- Ornamental grasses, perennials, [[holzer-medicinal-and-culinary-herbs-compendium]]
- Dwarf fruit trees in large planters
- Vegetables in raised beds set on the roof surface
- Native wildflower meadows

### Challenges
- Wind exposure selects for compact, low-growing plants
- Roof temperatures can exceed 150F in summer — select heat-tolerant species
- Winter exposure can be severe without snow cover
- Access for maintenance must be planned during design

## Living Wall Systems

### Panel Systems
- Pre-fabricated panels with built-in planting cells and irrigation
- Mount directly to wall with waterproof backing
- Modular — replace individual panels as needed
- Professional installation recommended
- Higher cost ($75-150 per square foot installed)

### Pocket Systems
- Fabric or felt pockets attached to a frame
- Lightweight and lower cost
- Fill pockets with soil and plants
- DIY-friendly
- Suitable for indoor and outdoor walls

### Trellis Systems
- Plants grow in ground-level containers and climb a wall-mounted trellis
- Lowest cost and simplest maintenance
- Limited to climbing or vining plants
- May take 1-3 seasons for full coverage

### Hydroponic Living Walls
- Plants grow without soil in nutrient-infused water
- Recirculating water system with pump and reservoir
- Clean, no soil mess (good for indoor walls)
- Requires electricity, monitoring, and [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-nutrient-management-ppm]]
- Higher maintenance than soil-based systems

## Living Wall Plant Selection

### Indoor Living Walls
- Pothos (Epipremnum aureum): Low light tolerant, cascading habit
- Spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum): Easy, adaptable, produces offsets
- Peace lily (Spathiphyllum): Shade tolerant, removes air pollutants
- Ferns (Boston fern, bird's nest fern): Require humidity
- Philodendron: Multiple species, various [[growing-gourmet-light-requirements-mushroom-fruiting]]
- Fittonia: Colorful foliage, compact, needs humidity

### Outdoor Living Walls
- Heuchera (coral bells): Shade-tolerant, colorful foliage
- Hostas: Shade, large leaves, dramatic texture
- Sedum and succulents: Full sun, drought-tolerant, shallow roots
- Strawberries: Edible and attractive in full sun
- Herbs: Thyme, oregano, rosemary for sunny walls
- Ferns: For shaded, humid outdoor walls

## Benefits

- **Stormwater management**: Absorbs 50-90% of rainfall, reducing runoff and combined sewer overflows
- **[[fukuoka-textdoc-energy-efficiency-agricultural-labor]]**: Insulates buildings, reducing heating and cooling costs by 10-30%
- **Urban heat island mitigation**: Evapotranspiration cools ambient air
- **Biodiversity**: Provides habitat for pollinators, birds, and invertebrates in urban areas
- **[[cervantes-air-quality-ventilation]]**: Filters particulates and absorbs pollutants
