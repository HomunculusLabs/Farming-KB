# Nicholas P. Money

## Overview

**Nicholas P. Money** is a British-American mycologist and author known for
his engaging, accessible writing about fungal biology. He is a professor
at Miami University in Oxford, Ohio, and has authored several books that
bring the hidden world of fungi to general audiences while maintaining
scientific rigor. His work combines personal narrative with detailed
scientific explanation, making him one of the most effective communicators
of mycological science.

## *Mr. Bloomfield's Orchard*

Money's 2002 book **Mr. Bloomfield's Orchard: The Mysterious World of
Mushrooms, Molds, and Mycologists** is a personal reflection on the
subject of mycology that explores humanity's "profound intimacy with
fungi." Published by Oxford University Press, the book weaves together
scientific exposition with autobiographical elements and biographical
sketches of prominent mycologists.

The book's ten chapters cover a wide range of mycological topics:
1. **Offensive Phalli and Frigid Caps** — [[fungal-mycelial-networks-nutrient-translocation]].
4. **Metamorphosis** — Fungal life cycles and developmental biology.
5. **The Odd Couple** — Fungal symbioses, including mycorrhizae and
   lichens.
6. **Ingold's Jewels** — Fungal [[cordyceps-cancer-bioactive-compounds]].
8. **Angels of Death** — Toxic and hallucinogenic fungi.
9. **Mr. Bloomfield's Orchard** — The title chapter, reflecting on the
   broader significance of mycology.
