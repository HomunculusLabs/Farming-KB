## The Shamanic Universe

The shamanic understanding of the universe is fundamentally different from the materialist worldview of modern science. For the shaman, the world is alive, intelligent, and communicative. Every plant, animal, rock, and river has a spirit. The physical world is interpenetrated by a spiritual dimension that can be accessed through altered states of consciousness.

In this shamanic universe, illness is not merely a physical malfunction but a spiritual imbalance — a disruption of the relationship between the individual and the larger web of life. Healing, therefore, requires not just the treatment of physical symptoms but the restoration of spiritual harmony. The shaman achieves this by entering the spirit world, identifying the source of the imbalance, and performing the necessary rituals and remedies to restore balance.

## Contemporary Shamanism

Despite centuries of suppression, shamanic traditions survive throughout the world — among the indigenous peoples of the Amazon, the Arctic, Siberia, southern Africa, Australia, and Mesoamerica. And in recent decades, a new form of shamanism has emerged — a syncretic practice that combines traditional shamanic techniques with modern psychological understanding and the use of a wide range of psychedelic substances.
