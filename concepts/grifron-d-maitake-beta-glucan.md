# Grifron-D Maitake Beta-Glucan

Grifron-D (also called Maitake D-fraction or GD) is a purified β-glucan extracted from [[medicinal-mushroom-compound-extraction]]

### Direct Cytotoxicity

In a groundbreaking study by Fullerton et al. (2000), Grifron-D demonstrated direct cytotoxic effects on human prostate cancer cells (PC-3, androgen-independent):
- **95% cell death** achieved at ≥480 µg/ml in 24 hours
- Mechanism: oxidative membrane damage leading to apoptotic cell death
- Confirmed by elevated lipid peroxidation and positive in situ hybridization

### Vitamin C Synergy

A remarkable finding with significant clinical implications:
- Vitamin C at 200 µM combined with Grifron-D at only 30-60 µg/ml was as effective as GD alone at 480 µg/ml
- This 8-16 fold dose reduction suggests vitamin C acts synergistically to potentiate GD activity
- Practical implication: lower doses may be effective when combined with dietary vitamin C

### Chemosensitization

When combined with conventional anticancer drugs:
- Most combinations showed little potentiation
- **Exception**: carmustine (BCNU) + GD combination achieved 90% reduction in cell viability
- This specific chemosensitizing effect may have clinical significance for glioblastoma treatment

## Additional Properties

### Anti-Ageing Effects

- Reduced lipid peroxidation
- Enhanced superoxide dismutase (SOD) activity
- Anti-inflammatory effects through cytokine modulation

### Diabetes and Hypercholesterolemia

- Studies have shown blood glucose and cholesterol lowering effects in animal models
- Maitake powder capsules are widely accepted as dietary supplements for metabolic health in Japan

## Commercial Availability

- Available as capsules containing dried maitake powder and purified D-fraction
- Widely sold as a dietary supplement in the US and Japan
- Not approved as a pharmaceutical by FDA or equivalent agencies
- Quality and standardization vary between manufacturers
