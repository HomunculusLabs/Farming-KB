## Chunky Cherry Malawi — African Sativa × Indica Hybrid Landrace Cross

## Overview

Chunky Cherry Malawi is a hybrid cannabis strain that combines **Malawi Gold** landrace sativa genetics with an indica component to create a strain that preserves the legendary African sativa experience while addressing some of the practical challenges of growing pure landrace [[african-landrace-sativa-modern-hybrids]] cannabis and the refined, productive characteristics of modern hybrid breeding. The "Chunky" in the name refers to the improved bud density brought by the indica influence, while "Cherry" describes the sweet, fruity aroma that distinguishes this phenotype.

### Flowering

- **Flowering time**: 10-12 weeks — significantly shorter than pure Malawi Gold but longer than most modern hybrids
- **Stretch**: Significant (2-3x) in the first 3-4 weeks of flowering. This is the primary management challenge
- **Bud structure**: Moderately dense buds that are markedly chunkier than pure Malawi Gold. The sativa influence means buds retain some elongation rather than becoming perfectly round indica nugs
- **Trichome production**: Excellent — the Malawi Gold genetics contribute heavy, sticky resin production
- **Color**: Buds display deep green coloration with occasional purple or pink hues in cooler conditions
- **Aroma development**: The distinctive cherry sweetness emerges in weeks 6-8 and intensifies through harvest

### Yield

- **Indoor**: 300-400g/m² — respectable for a sativa-leaning strain with this flowering time
- **Outdoor**: 400-600g per plant in warm climates with a long growing season
- **Yield quality**: Dense, resinous buds with excellent potency. The combination of landrace potency and improved density makes for a premium product

## Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma Profile

The aroma of Chunky Cherry Malawi is one of its most [[metzner-three-distinctive-features-shamanic-entheogenic-ceremonies]]:

- **Primary**: Sweet cherry and dark berry notes — the "Cherry" in the name is literal and immediately recognizable
- **Secondary**: Floral, tropical, and slightly spicy undertones from the Malawi Gold lineage
- **Tertiary**: Earthy, hash-like base notes that ground the sweet top notes
- **Overall impression**: A complex, sweet, and fruity aroma that is quite different from the skunky, diesel, or pine profiles common in modern hybrids. The cherry character is distinctive enough that experienced users can identify the strain by scent alone
