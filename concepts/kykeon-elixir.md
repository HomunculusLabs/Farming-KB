## The Ergot Hypothesis

Schultes and Hofmann present the hypothesis that the kykeon was prepared from
Claviceps paspali and possibly other species of ergot growing on various
Loliums and other cereal grasses native to Greece. The secret rites of
ancient Greece, which had remained a puzzle for four thousand years, are
associated with intoxication caused by the fungus Claviceps, which grows
parasitically on certain cereals.

The kykeon was the potion consumed during the culminating phase of the
