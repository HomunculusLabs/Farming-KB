# Vegetalista Healing Techniques and Plant Medicines

## Overview

The *vegetalista* is the Amazonian plant healer — a practitioner whose medical knowledge derives entirely from plants and the spirit beings associated with them. Through ayahuasca visions, dietas (plant dieting regimens), and direct communication with plant spirits (*genios* or *madres*), the vegetalista acquires a comprehensive pharmacopoeia and a set of therapeutic techniques that address illnesses spanning the physical, psychological, and spiritual dimensions. Pablo Amaringo's visionary paintings document dozens of specific plants, their medicinal applications, and the spirit entities that govern their use.

## Core Healing Techniques

### Icaros (Sacred Songs)

The *icaro* is the primary therapeutic instrument of the vegetalista. These are sacred songs received during ayahuasca visions or through extended dietas with specific plants. Each icaro has a defined function:

- **Diagnostic icaros**: Enable the healer to perceive the nature and location of illness
- **Extracting icaros**: Direct the removal of pathogenic agents (darts, phlegm, sorcery) from the patient's body
- **Protective icaros** (*arkanas*): Create defensive energy fields around the healer and patient
- **Plant-specific icaros**: Invoke the healing properties of particular medicinal plants
- **Animal icaros**: Channel the powers of spirit animals for healing purposes
- **Funerary icaros** (*sepultura tonduri*): Used by sorcerers to kill — illustrating that icaros themselves are morally neutral technologies

The icaro operates through vibrational resonance: when sung, blown upon (with tobacco smoke), or whistled, it directs specific energies toward the patient or against the disease agent.

### Sucking (*Chupada*)

Sucking is the most direct physical healing technique. The vegetalista places his mouth on or near the affected area and uses his *mariri* (magical phlegm) to extract the disease. The healer's beneficial phlegm neutralizes and draws out the harmful substance introduced by sorcery or environmental exposure. This technique is used for cungatuya, virote extraction, mal aire, and many other conditions.
