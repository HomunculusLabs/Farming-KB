## Why Not Conventional Irrigation?

Fukuoka strongly criticized conventional irrigation approaches to desert
restoration. He observed that in Somalia, modern farms supported by foreign aid
all pumped water from rivers, ran it into irrigation ditches, and watered fields
in succession. They all failed, turning to salt fields and were abandoned. When
irrigation water evaporates quickly, salt precipitates out and builds up in
surface soil layers. The salt must then be drained elsewhere, causing toxic
conditions there instead. These are short-term measures requiring tremendous
energy that create long-term problems.
