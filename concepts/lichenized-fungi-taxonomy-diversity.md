### Basidiomycete Lichens

A few Basidiomycota form lichens, particularly in Agaricales
(*Omphalina*), Cantharellales (*Multiclavula*), and Stereales
(*Dictyonema*).

### Families of Uncertain Position

15 families including Aphanopsidaceae, Baeomycetaceae,
Gomphillaceae, Strigulaceae, Thelenellaceae, Thelocarpaceae,
Umbilicariaceae.

## Taxonomic Principles

Scientific names refer to the fungal partner (mycobiont); photobionts
have independent names. Officially, the lichen association itself has no
name under the International Code of Botanical Nomenclature. When more
than one species of photobiont occurs with the same fungus, the same
fungal name is used for all resulting lichen thalli even if they have
different morphologies.
