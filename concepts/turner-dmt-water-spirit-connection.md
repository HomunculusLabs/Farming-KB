# Turner DMT Water Spirit Connection

Turner's personal discovery of a "magical link" between N,N-DMT and water, based on extensive experimentation and a revelation during a Ketamine experience.

## Background: Loss of DMT Rapport

- After ~4 months of frequent DMT use (2-3x weekly, ~100 total trips), began losing rapport
- Felt "some metaphysical force" preventing progress
- DMT experiences in the Arizona desert (Mt. Lemon, Sedona) were "entirely different" — deep fear, presence of death, despair, loneliness
- At Sedona: "overwhelmed by dread and terror," produced so much sweat on forehead it dripped while body remained dry
- Logged only 15 DMT trips between Arizona vacation and "water" experience over a year later

## The Discovery

### On the Boat (Petaluma River)
- Clear, cool May night under new moon on a boat ride
- Pre-dosed: 250 mics LSD + ecstasy + ground Syrian Rue seeds (smoked, not extracted)
- First DMT hit (~15 mg) in the boat cabin: "beautiful and enchanting visions," all menace absent
- Second hit on deck looking out over water: "truly magical"
- "The profound effect the water imparted to my experience was immediately obvious"
- Patterns on water responsible for visions and the "profoundly magical and harmonious mental/emotional state"

### The Revelation
- Knowledge came not while on DMT, but during a Ketamine + 2C-B-like experience
- An "elfin creature" whispered: "DMT is a Water Spirit plant"
- Transmitted knowledge that DMT spirit likes being near water, bestows peaceful visions near water, detests being away from water, and "those who use DMT removed from water will often experience its wrath"

## Pattern Verification

### Negative (Waterless) Experiences
- Mt. Lemon, Arizona — fear, death, despair
- Sedona, Arizona — dread, terror, unprecedented sweating
- Death Valley — did not even bring DMT (possibly intuition/omen)

### Positive (Water-Adjacent) Experiences
- Petaluma River — magical return
- Hana, Maui (stream audible 30 feet away) — "two of the most luxurious, peaceful, and magical DMT trips"
- Hawaii, Napali Coast (sitting in a stream on waterfall ledge) — water as "grounding force"
- Home during rain — "delightfully enchanting"

## Additional Insights

### Burning vs. Traditional Use
- Leprechaun noted: "DMT is never smoked or burned in the cultures where a shamanic tradition exists"
- Suggests DMT is "adverse to fire"
- Traditional methods: ayahuasca beverage (oral) or concentrated snuffs

### Ingested DMT Protocol
- 4 grams Syrian Rue first (wait for MAO inhibition)
- 160-200 mg DMT orally, consumed over half hour
- Produces 3-4 hour experience with gradual ascent
- "LSD and mushrooms seem like child's play in comparison"
- Scenes "rich, vivid, emotionally charged, and filled with symbols and archetypal images"
- "Degree of realism I've never before encountered — almost taste them"
- Entity contact present, entities "able to metamorphose as a means of communicating"

### Environmental Energy
- Koa trees in Hawaii had "sharp and lively" energy that "shattered" water sensations
- Napali Coast mountains invoked fear of something "so powerful and durable that human frailty is greatly magnified"
- Water acted as grounding force preventing overwhelm from mountain intensity

## Set and Setting Implications
- The natural environment "frequently affects a DMT trip"
- DMT experiences are "never predictable, or subject to fitting into preconceptions"
- Metaphysical forces may try to prevent discovery of this connection
- "Something which exists in a metaphysical realm was trying to prevent me from gaining knowledge"
