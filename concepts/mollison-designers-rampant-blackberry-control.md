# Rampant Blackberry Control in Permaculture Systems

Blackberry ([[rubus-fruticosus]] and related species) is one of the
most persistent and difficult-to-control weeds in cool temperate
and Mediterranean climates worldwide. In the permaculture
approach described by Mollison, blackberry is not merely a weed
to be eradicated with chemicals but a plant that can be managed,
confined, and even productively integrated into farm and garden
systems using biological and mechanical strategies that avoid
dangerous herbicides.

## Confinement Strategies

Where blackberry is not being eliminated but confined, several
strategies work:

1. Regular cropping or mowing of adjacent fields prevents
   spread. Many farmers leave walls of blackberry along fences
   and creeks as cattle barriers; these do not spread where crop
   or slashers are used and the fields are grazed.

2. Blackberry does not penetrate through, or do well under,
   3-5 years of pines or in pine plantations after years 4-7.
   Forest shade is an effective long-term control.
