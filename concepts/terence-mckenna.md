# Terence McKenna

## Overview

**Terence Kemp McKenna** (November 16, 1946 – April 3, 2000) was an
American ethnobotanist, author, lecturer, and psychonaut best known for
his advocacy of [[food-of-the-gods-book]] for a detailed summary. The book's core
argument — that psychoactive plants drove human cognitive evolution and
that their suppression by [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] could provide genuine insights into the
nature of reality, consciousness, and time.

### *True Hallucinations* (1993)

A narrative account of the 1971 Amazon expedition, mixing travelogue
with philosophical speculation. More personal and anecdotal than *Food
of the Gods*, it captures the raw immediacy of McKenna's encounters
with [[psychedelic-consciousness-expansion]].

### *The Archaic Revival* (1991)

A collection of essays and lectures exploring themes of ecological
consciousness, psychedelic experience, and the recovery of prehistoric
ways of knowing. The title became a catchphrase for McKenna's overall
project.
