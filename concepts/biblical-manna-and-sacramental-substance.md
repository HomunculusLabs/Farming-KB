# Biblical Manna and the Sacramental Substance

## Overview

The concept of a sacramental substance — a physical medium through which divine communion is achieved — is central to the world's religious traditions. In the Judeo-Christian context, this substance is identified as **Manna**, described in biblical texts with characteristics that precisely match [[allegro-colour-and-consistency-of-the-amanita-muscaria]] mushroom. Understanding the true identity of this sacramental substance is presented as the key to unlocking the christmas symbolism and hidden meanings of scriptural texts.

## John Marco Allegro and Philological Evidence

**John Marco Allegro** was one of the world's leading philologists and a scholar who took enormous professional risk by publishing *[[allegro-star-of-the-morning-venus-and-the-sacred-mushroom]] and the Cross* (1970). His work made numerous connections between sacred doctrinal enigmas, religious sacraments, and the [[allegro-amanita-muscaria-properties-and-pharmacology]] through linguistic analysis of Hebrew, Greek, Sumerian, and Aramaic texts.

Allegro linguistically linked:

- **Soma** (Greek = "body")
- **Manna** (Sumerian = "mushroom")
- The names **Jesus**, **James**, and **John**
- The **Fruit of the [[stropharia-cubensis-tree-of-knowledge]]**
- The symbol of the **cross**

All were identified as synonyms and wordplay for the hidden identity of the mushroom. The religious establishment responded with intense criticism, though the author of the source material argues that most of Allegro's core associations withstand scrutiny. Regardless of debates over specific claims, Allegro pioneered a field of inquiry that most scholars and laypeople were — and remain — afraid to explore.

## The Two Mannas

A critical distinction in biblical texts is that there are **two kinds of manna**:

1. **The visible manna**: The substance the Israelites ate in the desert (Exodus 16)
2. **The Hidden Manna**: Alluded to in Revelation 2:17, described as the reward for those who "overcome" the lies of the world

Jesus himself makes this distinction explicit in the Gospel of John, describing the manna he gives to his disciples at the Last Supper as the manna that bestows immortality — a higher substance than the manna of the desert.

## Botanical Description Matching Manna

The biblical description of manna in Exodus 16 provides a remarkably precise botanical description of a mushroom:

### Appearance
> *"And when the dew that lay was gone up, behold, upon the face of the wilderness there lay a small round thing, as small as the hoar frost on the ground."* (Exodus 16:14)

Mushrooms appear as small, round objects on the ground after moisture (dew) has fallen.

### Perishability
> *"And Moses said, Let no man leave of it till morning... Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto Moses; but some of them left of it till the morning, and it bred worms, and stank."* (Exodus 16:19-20)

Mushrooms left in warmth rapidly rot, breed worms, and produce a foul odor — a characteristic that distinguishes them from bread or other stored foods.

### Miraculous Origin
Manna was described as being produced miraculously — appearing without visible seed. This is a perfect botanical description of [[evaporative-demand-hypothesis-mushroom-growth]]: mushrooms grow from microscopic spores invisible to the naked eye, giving the appearance of spontaneous generation. To ancient peoples without microscopes, this seedless birth was genuinely miraculous.

## Jesus as the Bread of Life

In the Gospel of John (chapters 6–14), Jesus delivers an extended discourse on the manna/bread theme that takes on new meaning when understood through the entheogenic lens:

- *"Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life"* (John 6:27)
- *"Our fathers did eat manna in the desert"* (John 6:31)
- *"My father giveth you the true bread from heaven"* (John 6:32)
- *"I am the bread of life"* (John 6:35)
- *"He that eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, dwelleth in me, and I in him"* (John 6:56)
- *"Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water"* (John 7:38)

The statement about eating flesh and drinking blood takes on a literal botanical meaning when "flesh" (Soma/body) and "blood" (the mushroom's juice) refer to the physical mushroom substance. The concept of something "dwelling in you" that "teaches you all things" describes the effects of [[pineal-gland-dmt-entheogenic-experience]] rather than mere symbolic metaphor.

## The Comforter and the Anointing

In John 14, Jesus describes a "comforter" or "spirit of truth" that:

- The world cannot receive or see
- Dwells with and within the disciples
- Teaches all things
- Requires no human teacher once received
- Is the "anointing" (Greek: *chrisma*, etymologically connected to "Christ")

This describes a substance that is consumed (hence dwelling "in" the person), opens perception to spiritual realities invisible to the uninitiated ("the world cannot see"), and provides direct gnosis — knowledge that eliminates the need for intercessory religious authority.

The connection to the word *Christ* (from *chrisma*, the anointing substance) reinforces the identification: to be "Christ" means to be anointed with the sacred substance. "Christmas" literally combines this concept with "Mass" — the ceremony of sacramental ingestion.

## The Last Supper as Sacramental Ingestion

The Last Supper is reinterpreted as the literal sacramental ingestion of the entheogenic substance:

> *"Take and eat, This is my Body"* 

When "body" is understood as *Soma* (the Greek word, also meaning mushroom), and the physical substance described is fleshy and round, the event becomes a straightforward ritual of entheogenic communion rather than a metaphor requiring theological elaboration.

## The Doctrine of Transubstantiation

The [[catholic-church-suppression-eleusinian-mysteries]], in the early 1100s under Emperor/Pope Innocent III, established the **Doctrine of Transubstantiation** — the claim that priests possess the holy power to transform ordinary bread into the literal "Body of God" through spoken ritual. This is characterized in the source material as:

- One of the greatest deceptions in religious history
- An undermining of the basic esoteric aspects of the religion
- Arguably the most damaging event in Christendom's history
- A replacement of the original entheogenic sacrament with a placebo (a cracker/wafer)

By removing the actual consciousness-expanding substance and replacing it with symbolic bread, the Church eliminated the possibility of direct individual gnosis — direct communion with the divine — while maintaining the ritual form. This ensured that spiritual experience remained mediated through priestly authority.

## The Ark of the Covenant and the Manna

Moses built the elaborate **Ark of the Covenant** to carry the Manna through the wilderness. The biblical text states that looking inside the ark was punishable by death. The source material argues that the box itself is of little spiritual import — what mattered was what was inside: the Manna. The centuries-long search for the physical Ark is characterized as either completely missing the point or as deliberate misdirection of attention away from the substance it contained.

## The Fruit of the Tree and the Small White Stone

In Revelation, the "Fruit of the Tree," the "Hidden Manna," and the "Small White Stone" are all spoken of separately but in the same context. Each is identified as a symbol for the Amanita muscaria at different stages:

- **Small White Stone**: The button (infant) stage of the mushroom, emerging white and round from its volva
- **Fruit of the Tree**: The mature mushroom as the fruit of its mycorrhizal relationship with the tree
- **Hidden Manna**: The entheogenic substance itself, hidden from the world but revealed to initiates

## The Book of Genesis and the Cuneiform Connection

The oldest known tales of Adam and Eve eating the fruit in the [[allegro-garden-of-eden-and-delight]] come from **Cuneiform clay tablets of Mesopotamia** (at least 4000 BCE). According to Zecharia Sitchin's analysis of these tablets, the original stories portray the eating of the fruit in a completely different light from the biblical version:

- The Father God "Anu" remained on the star Sirius
- Two brothers, **Ea** and **Enlil**, stewarded the colonization of Earth
- **Enlil** wished to control the population and restrict knowledge
- **Ea** wished to expand humanity's consciousness ("multi-dimensionalize" their minds)
- Ea was determined to awaken his creations despite the High Council's preference for the Enlilian position

The original story is described as a glorious narrative about the hope and potential of humanity, which was flipped 180 degrees in the later biblical version — transformed from a story of awakening into a story of fall-from-grace and racial condemnation.

## The Bible as Historical Document

The Bible, as it exists today, is characterized as:

- A re-translation of a re-translation of a re-translation of an initially altered plagiarism
- A compilation created by a governmental council (under Constantine) that examined the world's religious doctrines and selectively compiled them into a "One World Religion"
- A document from which ecumenical councils deliberately excised concepts referring to reincarnation
- A text that, while complex and worthy of study, pales in comparison to the thousands of Hindu or Buddhist writings in complexity and substance

Despite these caveats, the original texts (traced back to their Cuneiform sources) are acknowledged as having been incredible documents — particularly when read in their native language by those who had partaken of "the heavenly gift" (the mushroom).
