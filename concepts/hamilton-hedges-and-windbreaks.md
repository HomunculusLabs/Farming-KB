## Windbreak Design Principles

The effectiveness of a windbreak depends on its height, density, and length. As a general rule, wind is significantly reduced for a distance of 10 to 15 times the height of the windbreak on the leeward side. A 2m hedge therefore provides meaningful protection for 20 to 30m beyond it.

For windbreak design on larger properties, staggered rows of different heights create the most effective shelter. A row of low shrubs in front of taller trees allows wind to be filtered through multiple layers rather than deflected over a single barrier.
