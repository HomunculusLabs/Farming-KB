## Entomopathogenic Fungi and Insect Pathogens

A diverse guild of fungi obtain part or all of their nutrition by
attacking living insects and other arthropods. These entomopathogenic
fungi span the Ascomycota, Basidiomycota, Zygomycota, and
Chytridiomycota, as well as fungus-like Oomycota. The existence of
fungi using this nutritional mode was first revealed in detailed
studies by Charles Drechsler in the 1930s and 1940s. More than 300
recognized species of nematode- and insect-destroying fungi are now
known. They represent three fundamental modes of existence: predation,
endoparasitism of adult invertebrates, and parasitism of invertebrate
eggs and cysts.
