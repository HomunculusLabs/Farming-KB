## A Philosophical Statement

The title is more than an observation — it's a philosophical statement:

- Nature has been managing soil fertility for hundreds of millions of years
- Human intervention (fertilizers, pesticides) is a recent experiment with mixed results
- The smartest thing gardeners can do is to support natural systems rather than replace them
- Trusting the soil food web is both more effective and less work than the chemical approach
