# Finger Hash Collection During Manicuring

Finger hash is one of the most immediate rewards of harvesting
cannabis. As growers handle resinous buds during the manicuring
process, sticky trichome resin accumulates on fingers, gloves,
and tools. This accumulated resin can be collected and pressed
into surprisingly potent hash. Cervantes describes how a single
day of trimming can yield a significant piece of finger hash.

## How Finger Hash Accumulates

During manicuring, the constant handling of resinous buds
transfers trichome heads to the skin. The resin builds up
progressively: first as a light sticky film, then as thicker
layers that can be scraped off. The most resinous strains and
properly grown indoor plants produce the most finger hash
because they exude maximum resin in their protected
environment.

Resin can also be collected by scraping it from tools such as
scissors, clippers, and workspace surfaces. Scissors used for
trimming accumulate visible resin that impairs blade movement
over time and must be cleaned.

## Collection from Rubber Gloves

Wearing inexpensive rubber gloves during manicuring serves
dual purposes: keeping hands clean and creating a dedicated
surface for hash collection. Cervantes provides two methods
for recovering finger hash from gloves:

Alcohol Bath Method:
1. After trimming for a few hours, remove the accumulated
   finger hash from gloves by bathing them in a small portion
   of isopropyl alcohol.
2. Set the hash-laden alcohol on the counter overnight to
   evaporate completely.
3. Scrape up the remaining hash after all the alcohol has
   evaporated.

Freezer Method:
1. Put the rubber gloves in a freezer for a few hours.
2. Cold temperatures make the accumulated hash brittle and
   easier to scrape and rub from the gloves.
3. Collect the cold resin and press it together.

The freezer method avoids the use of isopropyl alcohol and is
preferred by those who want to avoid any solvent residue.

## Collecting from Scissors

Scrape accumulated resin from scissors when buildup impairs
blade movement. Use a small knife to remove built-up resin from
blades. Ball up small bits of scraped resin by rubbing them
together between fingers. The ball of hash will grow as
manicuring progresses throughout the day.
