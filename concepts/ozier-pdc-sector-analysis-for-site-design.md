## Creating the Sector Diagram

Start with a large circle centered on Zone 0. Create concentric
rings equal to the number of sectors. Draw angle lines from
center to the appropriate ring and label each. Put summer sun
on the outermost ring, winter sun second, prevailing winds
third. Color the outermost arc first, then fill wedges to avoid
overlapping colors.
