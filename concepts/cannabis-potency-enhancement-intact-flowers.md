## Cannabis Potency Enhancement of Intact Flowers

## Detailed Steps

### Extraction

1. Place intact marijuana flowers in a cheesecloth bag
2. Reflux in solvent (alcohol) using standard [[cannabis-hashish-from-extracted-oil-techniques]] in alcohol, pour into equal volume of water
2. Extract with petroleum ether (standard purification)
3. Separate ether/oil layer from water/alcohol layer
4. Perform isomerization (sulfuric acid in alcohol, reflux 2 hours)
5. Optionally acetylate for THC acetate (double potency)
6. Purify final product through ether extraction and washing

### Recovering Tars and Resins

The tars and resins left behind from the ether extraction remain dissolved in the alcohol/water layer:
1. Evaporate and collect the alcohol
2. Evaporate the water in an oil bath at 220°F (104°C)
3. The remaining tars and resins are saved for re-infusion

### Re-infusion

1. **Determine flower absorption capacity:** Add clean alcohol to the completely dry flowers until they absorb no more but none pools at the bottom. Measure this amount.
2. **Remove solvent:** Put the saturated flowers in a distillation apparatus and remove/collect all the solvent
3. **Prepare oil solution:** Mix the purified/intensified oil + recovered tars and resins with exactly the measured amount of alcohol
4. **Infuse:** Using an oven-baster-type syringe, equally saturate the dried flowers with the oil-bearing solvent
5. **Remove solvent again:** Place flowers in appropriate apparatus and remove all solvent
6. **Final drying:** Spray a small amount of water on the flowers (steam iron or wrinkle remover works). Place in oven preheated to 250°F, then turned off. Since solvent evaporates at much lower temperature than water, when flowers dry out no solvent traces remain.

## Result

The flowers are now coated with intensified oil and can be over 12× their original potency while appearing visually identical to untreated flowers.

- Creating premium flower products from lower-potency starting material
- Medical applications requiring precise dosing with enhanced material
