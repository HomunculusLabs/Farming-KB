# Cannabis Manicuring Techniques

## Overview

Manicuring is the process of removing leaves from [[cannabis-hashish-production]].

## When to Manicure

Manicuring is easiest when leaves are soft and supple immediately after harvest. Trimming off leaves at this stage also speeds the subsequent drying process. Waiting until foliage is dry to manicure makes the job much slower and more tedious, as dry leaves become brittle and difficult to handle.

Growers have several options for timing their manicuring:

- Manicure fresh buds immediately after cutting branches
- Remove large fan leaves before harvest, manicure small leaves after drying
- Leave outer leaves intact as a protective sheath during drying, then manicure dry
