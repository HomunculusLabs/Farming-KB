# Growing Mushrooms in the Dwelling House

The cellar of a dwelling house is a capital place for
mushroom beds, and can be used in whole or part for
this purpose. In the case of private families who wish to
grow a few mushrooms only for their own use it is not
necessary to devote a whole cellar to it; but partition off
a part of it with boards and make the beds in this.
