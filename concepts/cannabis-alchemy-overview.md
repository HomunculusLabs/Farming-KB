## Cannabis Alchemy

Cannabis alchemy refers to the ancient and modern practice of transforming raw
cannabis plant material into refined, potent extracts and preparations through
processes that parallel traditional alchemical methods. The term bridges
botanical extraction science with the philosophical traditions of alchemy,
emphasizing purification, transformation, and the pursuit of medicinal or
spiritual essences.
