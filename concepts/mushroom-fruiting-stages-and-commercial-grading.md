## Commercial Grading Standards

### The Premium: Pre-Veil-Break Harvest

Commercial mushrooms are graded highest if picked just before the veil breaks. At this stage, the mushroom has achieved maximum size while the cap remains closed, the flesh is firm and dense, and the gills are still protected. Mushrooms harvested at this stage have the longest shelf life, the best texture for cooking, and the most attractive appearance for market display.

### Lower Grades

Mushrooms that are allowed to develop past the veil-break stage are graded lower. Once the cap opens, the mushrooms:
- Lose firmness and become more fragile
- Have a shorter shelf life
- Are more susceptible to damage during packing and shipping
- May develop a less desirable texture when cooked
- Show darkening of the gills from pink to [[open-air-mushroom-culture-and-compost-selection]].

### Shipping Duration

When properly packed and shipped immediately after gathering, mushrooms can withstand 36 to 48 hours of transit during winter months with little danger of deterioration.
