# Grain Spawn Preparation and Inoculation Methods

## Overview

Mushroom spawn is the living fungal culture used to inoculate prepared substrates. It consists of a carrier material fully colonized by mushroom mycelium. The type of carrier varies by species, although rye grain is the choice of most spawn makers. ## Spawn Container Selection

The standard spawn container for home cultivators is the quart mason jar while commercial makers prefer gallon jars. Narrow-mouthed containers are recommended over wide-mouth because they have less chance of contaminating from airborne spores due to their smaller openings. The best combination uses synthetic filter discs with one-piece screw-top lids having a 3/8-1/2 inch hole drilled in center. Polypropylene bags with filter patches are also widely used for larger-scale production.

## Moisture Management

The optimum moisture content for grain spawn is 49-54%. Commercial rye is approximately 11% water by mass. Excessive water favors bacterial growth, causes kernels to explode during sterilization, and makes colonized grain difficult to break into individual kernels. Balanced moisture spawn easily breaks apart, ensuring maximum inoculation points.

### Spawn Formulas

**Quart Jars:** 1 cup rye grain + 2/3 to 3/4 cup water (240ml grain, 170-200ml water)

**Half Gallon Jars:** 3 cups rye grain + 1.75 cups water (600ml grain, 400-460ml water)
