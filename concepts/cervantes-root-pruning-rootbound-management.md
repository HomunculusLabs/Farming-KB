# Root Pruning and Rootbound Management

Root pruning and managing rootbound plants are essential skills for
maintaining [[query-how-to-manage-ph-for-healthy-cannabis-plants]] in containers, especially for long-term
[[query-how-do-i-choose-between-indoor-outdoor-and-greenhouse-cannabis-growing]] grows. [[cervantes-container-gardening-pot-size]] for container selection

## Chemical Root Pruning

Chemical root pruning is an excellent way to control root growth
inside containers. Commercial nursery operations have used chemical
root pruning for many years with outstanding results.

The technique involves coating the inside of containers with a copper
hydroxide product (such as Griffin's Spin-Out). When roots grow to
within a fraction of an inch of the copper hydroxide coating, they
stop and turn away, never touching the compound. The result is similar
to air pruning: a dense, fibrous root system that fills the container
without circling.

To apply, spray-paint the inside of containers with two coats of the
copper hydroxide product. This is most practical for growers who reuse
containers season after season. The coating lasts through multiple
growing cycles and eliminates the need for physical root pruning.

## Prevention Strategies

The best approach is preventing rootbound conditions entirely:

- Start plants in adequately sized containers from the beginning
- [[cervantes-pruning-training]] aboveground can balance the temporary
growth slowdown by redirecting energy to a more manageable canopy.
