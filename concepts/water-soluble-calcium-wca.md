## Water-Soluble Calcium (WS-Ca)

Water-Soluble Calcium is a KNF input made by extracting calcium from eggshells using brown rice vinegar (BRV). Calcium is essential for cell wall strength, disease resistance, fruit quality, and the transition from vegetative to reproductive growth. WS-Ca provides calcium in a form that plants can absorb directly through leaves and roots.

## Why Calcium?

Calcium plays critical roles in plant health:
- **Cell wall structure**: Calcium pectate cross-links cellulose fibers, creating rigid cell walls
- **Disease resistance**: Strong cell walls resist fungal penetration and bacterial invasion
- **Fruit quality**: Prevents blossom end rot, bitter pit, cracking
- **Sugar accumulation**: Calcium promotes sugar transport and storage in fruit
- **Growth regulation**: Acts as a "brake" on excessive vegetative growth
- **Changeover support**: Critical during the transition from vegetative to reproductive growth (see [[nutritive-cycle-theory]])

## Calcium Deficiency Symptoms

- New leaves distorted, curled, or hook-shaped
- Blossom end rot in tomatoes, peppers, cannabis
- Tip burn in lettuce and leafy greens
- Weak stems, lodging (falling over)
- Poor fruit set and quality
- Premature leaf drop
- Cracking fruit during rain after drought

## WS-Ca Recipe

### Materials
- Eggshells (chicken, duck, or any poultry) — thoroughly cleaned
- [[brown-rice-vinegar-brv]] — natural acetic acid extraction solvent
- Glass jar with lid

### Ratios
- Eggshells to BRV: Fill jar 1/3 with eggshells, cover with BRV
- Use enough BRV to fully submerge eggshells (approximately 2:1 BRV to shell volume)

### Process
1. Collect eggshells from kitchen waste — accumulate over days/weeks
2. Clean eggshells thoroughly — remove all egg membrane and residue (membrane contains protein that can rot)
3. Optional: Sun-dry eggshells for 1-2 days to ensure complete dryness
4. Crush eggshells into small pieces (not powder — pieces allow BRV to penetrate)
5. Fill glass jar 1/3 full with crushed eggshells
6. Pour BRV over shells until fully submerged (BRV level should be 2-3x shell volume)
7. Bubbles will form as acetic acid reacts with calcium carbonate: CaCO3 + 2CH3COOH → Ca(CH3COO)2 + H2O + CO2
8. Store in cool dark place
9. Ferment 7-10 days (or until bubbles stop — reaction complete)
10. Strain liquid through cheesecloth — this is WS-Ca
11. Shell residue can be added to compost (still contains some calcium and beneficial compounds)

### Signs of Quality
- Clear to slightly amber liquid
- Mildly acidic smell (from BRV)
- No putrid odor
- Residual shells appear porous and partially dissolved

### Storage
- Glass bottle, cool dark place
- Lasts 6-12 months
- No refrigeration needed (acidic pH prevents microbial growth)

## Alternative Sources of Calcium

From Cho's book, other calcium sources for WS-Ca:
- **Oyster shells** — abundant in coastal areas, higher calcium content than eggshells
- **Clam shells** — similar to oyster shells
- **Snail shells** — small but effective
- **Limestone chips** — slower reaction with BRV

All follow the same process: crush, cover with BRV, wait for reaction to complete.

## Application Rates

| Use Case | Dilution | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Vegetative growth | AVOID or 1:1000 max | Calcium stunts vegetative growth |
| Changeover period | 1:1000 | Critical — supports transition |
| Reproductive growth | 1:1000 | Prevents overgrowth, hardens fruit |
| Pre-harvest | 2 ml/L (with OHN 1 ml/L) | Promotes sweetness |
| Calcium deficiency | 1:500 | Stronger dose until symptoms resolve |
| Crack prevention | 1:500 (with OHN + FPJ + LAB) | Drought-to-rain transitions |
| Seed treatment (SES) | 1 ml/L | For overgrown, soft seedlings |
| Cannabis calyx development | 1:1000 | Weeks 4-8 of flower |
| Soil drench | 1:500-1000 | Slow-release calcium for soil |

Apply as foliar spray early morning or late evening. Calcium is relatively immobile in plants — foliar application is more effective than soil drench for acute deficiency.

## WS-Ca in the Nutritive Cycle

According to [[nutritive-cycle-theory]]:
- **Vegetative stage**: Avoid or use sparingly — calcium suppresses vegetative growth
- **Changeover period**: Apply at 1:1000 — supports the critical transition
- **Reproductive stage**: Apply at 1:1000 — prevents over-luxurious growth, hardens fruit, promotes sugar accumulation
- **Pre-harvest**: OHN 1ml + WS-Ca 2ml + Seawater 30ml per liter — promotes sweetness and maturity

The "brake" function of calcium is one of the most important concepts in KNF. Without calcium, nitrogen-rich vegetative growth continues unchecked, producing soft, disease-susceptible tissue with poor reproductive output.

## Combining with Other Inputs

WS-Ca works synergistically with:
- [[brown-rice-vinegar-brv]] — the extraction solvent, also provides growth energy
- [[oriental-herbal-nutrient-ohn]] — OHN + WS-Ca for pre-harvest sweetness
- [[water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp]] — complementary phosphorus input for changeover
- Seawater — provides additional minerals alongside calcium
- [[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]] — FPJ + WS-Ca for balanced growth

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Shells not dissolving | BRV too weak or old | Use fresh BRV, crush shells smaller |
| Putrid smell | Egg membrane not removed | Clean shells thoroughly, start over |
| Plant stunting | Applied during vegetative | Stop WS-Ca, apply FPJ to recover |
| No effect on deficiency | Dilution too high | Increase to 1:500 |
| Blossom end rot persists | Application too late | Apply preventively during early flower |
