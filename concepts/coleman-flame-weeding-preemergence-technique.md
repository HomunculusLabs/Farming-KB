## Overview

Chapter 14 of *The [[hamilton-organic-weed-management]]. Thermal weed control kills or severely damages weeds through

## The Physics of Flame Weeding

### How Heat Kills Weeds

Flame weeders do not need to incinerate plant tissue. The kill mechanism works through
rapid cellular disruption:

- Cell membranes rupture at approximately 130°F (54°C) sustained exposure
- Brief exposure to 2000°F+ propane flame raises intracellular water temperature rapidly
- Water expansion within cells causes membrane rupture and protein denaturation
- Lethal exposure takes less than one-tenth of a second for seedling-stage weeds
- Visible wilting may take hours to appear, but the damage is already done

### Plant-Stage Sensitivity

| Stage | Sensitivity | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Germinating seeds | Very high | Heat penetrates thin seed coat easily |
| Cotyledon stage (first leaves) | Very high | Thin tissues, minimal vascular development |
| First true leaves | High | Growing point still near soil surface |
| Established weeds (4+ leaves) | Low | Growing point elevated, vascular system developed |
| Deep-rooted perennials | Very low | Heat cannot reach root crowns effectively |

## Equipment: The Flamer

Coleman uses a backpack propane flamer with a 10-foot hose:

- **Fuel**: Standard propane cylinders (20 lb for extended sessions)
- **Pressure regulator**: Adjustable for flame size and fuel economy
- **Burner head**: Produces a broad, soft blue flame—not a sharp pencil flame
- **Heat output**: Adjustable based on weed size and walking speed
- **Safety**: Always carry a fire extinguisher; never use in dry, windy conditions

## Pre-Emergence Flame Weeding

### Principle

Pre-emergence flaming kills weed seeds that have just germinated but not yet emerged
above the soil surface. This technique exploits the timing gap between weed germination
and crop emergence.

### The Critical Timing Window

- Most weed seeds germinate within 3-7 days of soil disturbance (tilling, raking)
- Crop seeds typically take 7-21 days to germinate depending on species and temperature
- The window between weed germination and crop emergence is the optimal flaming window
