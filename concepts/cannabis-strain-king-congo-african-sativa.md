## King Congo — Pure African Sativa Landrace Hybrid

## Overview

King Congo is a pure F1 sativa strain bred by Spain"s Tropical Seeds Company, combining Ciskei P3 and Congo Point Noire genetics. The strain represents the kind of challenging pure sativa work that Tropical Seeds specializes in — taking landrace African genetics and making them accessible to a wider range of growers. King Congo is notable for being one of the rare pure [[comparison-cavendish-vs-east-african-highland-bananas]] sativa from the Pointe-Noire region of the Republic of the Congo. This landrace contributes the strain's tall growth structure, energetic effects, and African sativa character.
- **Ciskei P4 F1** — A [[biodiversity-fungal-genetic-diversity]].

### F1 Hybrid Status

As an F1 hybrid, King Congo expresses strong hybrid vigor from the first generation cross. The combination of two distinct African highland sativas creates a genetically diverse plant with robust growth characteristics. The F1 status means that subsequent generations (F2, F3) will show greater phenotypic variation than the original F1 cross.

## Growing Characteristics

### Indoor Cultivation

King Congo is described as one of the rare pure sativa strains suitable for indoor cultivation. Key indoor traits:
- **Height** — medium stature for a pure sativa, manageable in standard grow rooms
- **Yield** — average to heavy yields indoors, impressive for a pure sativa
- **Structure** — attractive plant with deep green buds covered in snow-white pistils
- **Bud formation** — produces large, dense "fists of flowers" that are visually striking
- **Flowering time** — 65–75 days

### Outdoor Cultivation

Outdoors, King Congo can reach its full potential with significantly greater yields than indoor production. The plant's African highland genetics make it well-suited to warm, sunny climates with long [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]
- Support may be needed during late flowering as buds become large and heavy
- The plant's attractive appearance makes it a favorite among growers who value aesthetics alongside potency

## Aroma and Flavor

### Scent Profile

King Congo's aroma is characterized by:
- **Primary notes** — bright lemon and lime citrus
- **Secondary notes** — sweet undertones
- **Finish** — earthy aftertaste that grounds the citrus sweetness

The combination of bright citrus with earthy base notes creates a well-rounded aromatic profile that distinguishes King Congo from other African sativas, which tend toward more purely earthy or spicy profiles.
