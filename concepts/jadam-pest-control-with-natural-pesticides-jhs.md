### Alternative Methods
- **Cold water extraction:** Tobacco leaves (15 days), hot pepper and gingko fruits (1 month), minced garlic (1 month)
- **Deep frying in oil:** Strong-scented herbs (peppermint, cinnamon bark, rosemary, hot pepper) -- poisonous substances melt into oil
- **Fresh juice method:** Blend fresh herb and use -- JADAM advises against this (hard work, less effective, sticky residue on fruit)
- **Brewing in vinegar/wood vinegar:** Works but does not mix well with JWA
- **Brewing in alcohol:** Mixes well with JWA
