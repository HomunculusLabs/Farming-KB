### Cellulose-Degrading Enzymes

- **Endoglucanases (EG):** Cleave internal beta-1,4 bonds in cellulose chains,
  creating new chain ends for exoglucanases to act on.
- **Exoglucanases / cellobiohydrolases (CBH):** Processively remove cellobiose
  units (glucose dimers) from the reducing and non-reducing ends of cellulose
  chains.
- **Beta-glucosidase:** Hydrolyzes cellobiose and short oligosaccharides into
  individual glucose molecules, relieving end-product inhibition of the other
  cellulase components.

### Hemicellulose-Degrading Enzymes

Hemicellulose is a complex, branched polymer requiring multiple enzymes:
- Xylanases break down xylan (the main hemicellulose in hardwoods)
- Mannanases degrade mannan-based hemicellulose (common in softwoods)
- Acetyl xylan esterases remove acetyl groups
- Alpha-glucuronidases remove glucuronic acid side chains
