# Holzer Pond Construction and Aquaculture

Sepp Holzer is renowned for his water landscapes, which he has developed over decades at the Krameterhof (1,300 metres altitude in the Austrian Alps). His ponds serve multiple functions simultaneously: [[knf-fish-farming-integration]], irrigation reservoir, wildlife habitat, [[mollison-designers-microclimate-creation-frost-protection]], and aesthetic beauty.

## Aquaculture at the Krameterhof

### Fish Species
Holzer raises multiple fish species in polyculture, mimicking natural lake ecosystems:

- Trout (Salmo trutta / Oncorhynchus mykiss) -- cold-water species, require high oxygen. Stocked in the deepest, coldest ponds. Fed naturally on aquatic insects, zooplankton, and smaller organisms.
- Carp (Cyprinus carpio) -- warm-water bottom feeders. Thrive in shallower, warmer ponds. Root in the mud, keeping the bottom loose and fertile.
- Sturgeon (Acipenser sp.) -- kept in some deeper ponds for caviar production
- Tench (Tinca tinca) -- bottom-dwelling, tolerant of low oxygen
- Pike (Esox lucius) -- predator species that controls population balance

### Natural Feeding
- Fish are not fed commercial pellets
- Ponds are designed to produce their own food: aquatic plants, algae, insects, and zooplankton
- Duckweed (Lemna sp.) on the surface provides food for carp and tilapia
- Insect-attracting plants around pond edges (including [[holzer-beekeeping-and-honey-production]] colonies near ponds increase pollination of aquatic and marginal plants
- Manure from [[holzer-urban-farming]] for beauty and pollinator support

## Multiple Functions of Ponds

1. Fish and food production
2. Irrigation reservoir (gravity-fed to lower gardens)
3. Fire protection water supply
4. Microclimate creation (reducing frost, moderating heat)
5. Wildlife habitat (amphibians, birds, insects)
6. Recreation and aesthetic beauty
7. Water purification ([[comparison-constructed-wetland-vs-backyard-pond]] function)
8. Duck habitat (ducks control mosquito larvae and fertilise water)
9. Reflective surfaces that bounce light into nearby gardens
