## Supplementation at Casing (SAC)

### Materials Used

Supplementation at casing employs readily available protein and carbohydrate
sources including soy bean meal, cottonseed meal, ground rye grain, wheat grain,
and kafir corn grains. These materials are chosen for their availability,
nutrient density, and compatibility with mushroom mycelium.

### Application Method

The fully colonized substrate is thoroughly mixed with the supplement at a rate
of 10% of the dry weight. The procedure requires verifying the substrate is
fully colonized and contaminant-free, ensuring the supplement material is clean,
mixing thoroughly to distribute evenly, and then applying the casing layer after
mixing.

### Temperature Considerations

High substrate temperatures should be anticipated on the second to third day
after supplementation. The introduction of fresh nutrients stimulates a surge in
mycelial [[arbuscule-isolation-metabolic-activity-assays]], generating additional heat. The cultivator must
manage this through increased fresh air exchange or cooling to prevent thermal
damage to the mycelium.
