# Compost Tea Application Rates

Application rates for [[lowenfels-earthworms-soil-food-web]]. Rates are provided by Dr. Elaine Ingham and the Soil Foodweb Institute for various agricultural systems.

## General Rates

### Foliar Sprays
- **Standard rate**: 5 gallons of compost tea per acre for every 6 feet of plant height/canopy
- For plants taller than 6 feet, add 5 gallons for each additional 6 feet
- Backpack sprayers can apply tea straight (undiluted) -- water is just a carrier
- Commercial operations add water as needed to cover the area

### Soil Drenches
- **Standard rate**: 15-20 gallons of compost tea per acre
- Applied directly to soil around root zones

### Key Principle
"Water should be regarded only as a carrier. You add enough water to make it easy to cover the area with the tea." -- Elaine Ingham

## Application Frequency

### Year 1 (Rebuilding)
- **Every 7-10 days** for the first 4 months
- Then **monthly** for the remainder of the [[savory-growing-season-and-non-growing-season-management]]
- If a disease outbreak occurs: **every 3-5 days** until resolved

### Year 2+
- After 4-5 months of weekly applications, the food web should be established
- **Monthly** during the growing season
- **Three times per year** (planting, before bud break, after bud break) for maintenance
- When pest/disease alerts are issued: **weekly** until the alert stops

### Reducing Over Time
"Once the critters are in the soil, from plentiful applications of compost and compost tea, won't they continue forever? Yes, the organisms will continue as long as you have plants in the system, or you add foods to feed them." Disturbance (tilling, pesticides, floods, droughts) may require returning to more frequent applications.
