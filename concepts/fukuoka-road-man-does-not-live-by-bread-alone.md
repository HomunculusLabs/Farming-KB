# Man Doesn't Live by Bread Alone

This section consists of excerpts from an interview at
Shunjusha, publisher of the Japanese edition. The
interviewer asks about the future of humanity given famine,
nuclear war fears, and the mixed promises of scientific
progress. masanobu-fukuoka|Fukuoka responds with a radical reframing of what
it means to be alive.

## Science Doesn't Know What Real Food Is

When asked whether man can survive by maintaining food and
energy sources, Fukuoka responds that man has no idea what
it is he lives by, and no idea what to eat and what to
depend on to live. Science, he insists, doesn't have the
vaguest idea what real food is. It can't tell us why, how,
and in what way man eats and should eat. It can't explain
the true source, meaning, and goal of life. See [[fukuoka-road-all-one-has-to-do-is-live]]
