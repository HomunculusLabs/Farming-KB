## Euascomycete Diversity

The Euascomycetes is characterized by numerous well-supported
terminal clades linked by poorly supported basal nodes. The
primitive sporocarp morphology appears to be the apothecium,
characterized by an exposed fertile layer of asci (hymenium).
Major lineages include:

- **Orbilia**: A poorly known basal clade of nematophagous
  ascomycetes, representing the most basal lineage of the
  Euascomycetes.
- **Pezizales**: A large group of apothecial fungi including
  numerous macroscopic forest species (Morchella, Gyromitra,
  Helvella). Characterized by operculate asci, this order
  contains the majority of ectomycorrhizal ascomycete species
  and numerous independent lineages of truffles.
- **Helotiales**: A grossly polyphyletic group including
  endophytes, [[fungal-succession-woody-debris-ecology]].
