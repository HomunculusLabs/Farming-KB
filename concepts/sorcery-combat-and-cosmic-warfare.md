# Sorcery Combat and Cosmic Warfare in Amazonian Shamanism

## Overview

In Amazonian shamanism, particularly as documented by [[amaringo-ayahuasca-visions-shipibo-iconography]]*, sorcery combat and cosmic warfare represent a vast, highly stratified system of esoteric conflict. Shamanic practitioners—both healers (*vegetalistas*, *murayas*) and sorcerers (*brujos*, *sumirunas*)—wield an arsenal of spirit allies, magical vessels, and elemental forces in battles that unfold across multiple dimensions of reality. These encounters, revealed through ayahuasca visions (Visions 43–49), describe warfare conducted not with physical weapons alone but through psychic intrusion, electromagnetic manipulation, and the mobilization of spirit armies. The system encompasses named entities with specialized functions, a graded hierarchy of esoteric power, and a moral architecture distinguishing healers from those who harm.

## The Nature of Shitana (Sorcery)

The primary offensive practice is *shitana*—the art of inflicting harm through sorcery. The term encompasses a broad category of aggressive magical techniques that target the victim's physical health, mental clarity, and spiritual integrity. Sorcerers deploy specialized spirit entities to attack through subtle, insidious means, often working through dreams, food contamination, or direct psychic assault during ayahuasca sessions.

A sorcerer's *supay-masho* (diabolic bat) induces sleepy forgetful weakness, eroding alertness and memory. *Huaira-umas* (forgetful mind snakes) attack cognitive faculties, while *huasi-ukullucos* (house lizards) infiltrate the body during vulnerable moments. *Ayanahais*—"eyes of the dead" appearing as fireflies—serve as surveillance, and *aya-pelejos* (giant sloths of the dead) act as heavy combatants.

Offensive sorcerers command atmospheric forces: *huaira-huacas* (bulls of the wind) and *chusco-rikra* (four-armed people). A *sumi* sorcerer may wield a fire-colored sword producing lightning and thunder, or deploy a *kabbalistic lance* bridging Amazonian and Western esoteric traditions. The *Sepultura Tonduri* icaro—a sad and frightening song—ritually constructs a supernatural coffin around the victim, enlisting *Sachamama* as coffin, *motelo-mama* (tortoise) as wake table, mushrooms as candles, *ayanahuis* as flames, *alipa-cuchis* as grave-diggers, and *rompe-mortaja* bird as shroud, staging a complete symbolic death attended by *huancahui* birds mimicking weeping relatives.

## Offensive Sorcery Techniques

Sorcery attacks target the victim on multiple levels—physical, psychological, and spiritual. The *puka-cunga-shitanero* delivers targeted sorcery bolts. A sorcerer escorted by *puma-machaco* (puma-snake), *yana-simpi* (black foot soldiers), *supay-machin* (diabolic monkey), *pelejo fantasma* (ghost sloth), and *pulpo shitanero* (sorcerer octopus) presents a formidable multi-species assault force. The *banco uma* (black jaguar sorcerer) represents the most dangerous offensive profile—a practitioner fully merged with jaguar spirit. These sorcerers fight through *tingunas* (spiritual atmospheres), requiring defenders to pierce layered veils of hostile energy before engaging directly.

## Defensive Arsenal of the Vegetalista

Healer-shamans maintain an equally elaborate defensive ecosystem operating through diverse mechanisms:

- **Hypnotic and magnetic powers**: The *mantona mama* (large snake) exerts hypnotic magnetism; the *sinchi-ahui* tiger paralyzes with magnetic eyes. Queen Nefina commands hair of fire and a magnetizing mirror that deflects hostile intentions.
- **Intelligence and early warning**: The *chicua* bird reads enemy intentions. Fairy *Sinchinitimushca* ("the one who presses hard") throws a white thread alerting healers to incoming attack.
- **Subterranean defense**: The *supay-yangunturo* (gigantic armadillo) burrows for defensive positions. The *allpa-raya* (land ray) provides aerial mobility.
- **Psychological warfare**: The *ishkay-huahuasupay* (two-child creature) confuses enemies through weeping sounds.
- **Armed spirit entities**: *Guacamayo-machaco* (snake with woman's breasts) throws poisoned daggers. *Sapo-machaco* (snake toad) delivers a deadly bite. *Manilay-rucos*—guardians with crowns of fire knives—serve as elite protectors.

Healers deploy collective formations: electric chains, *pinsha-machaco* spirits, *huacraruna* beings, *tahuicuro* birds, sylphids, *yakurunas*, and mermaids in layered defense. Under extreme threat, vegetalistas transform into wolves through shapeshifting granted by queen allies. This lupine transformation is not merely cosmetic—it grants predatory resilience, heightened senses, and the capacity to meet sorcerous aggression with equivalent ferocity while remaining under the queens' protective authority.

Botanical defenses form another layer: *piñon colorado* (*Jatropha gossypifolia*), *yuca verde*, *achiote* (*Bixa orellana*), and *patiquina* (*Dieffenbachia* sp.) counter sorcerers. *Sacha-ajos* (*Mansoa alliacea*) acts as spiritual disinfectant. *Camuri* fruit is preserved as *mariri*—concentrated spiritual power. These plants are not merely symbolic; they are understood to carry active counter-sorcerous properties deployed through preparation, ingestion, and ritual application.

## Royal and Divine Allies

Queens occupy a central defensive position. Vision 45 names six queens protecting vegetalistas: Cushirima, Silove, Gabat, Manchahuarmi, Allimipaica, and Callpaican-Kapak. These regal beings command vast spirit armies and provide overarching protection. Anacondas form living barriers in their retinue, wrapping around healers and their communities to block incoming hostile forces.

Prince Tesael wields a magical trumpet radiating electrical sounds that disrupt enemy formations, signaling that cosmic warfare extends into a broader celestial hierarchy beyond purely Amazonian spirit ecology. Priest Turmanshe ("messenger of peace") advises against killing even hostile witches, counseling punishment and release—introducing a principle of restraint distinguishing healers from sorcerers. His presence establishes that even in the heat of cosmic battle, a higher ethical jurisdiction prevails.

## The Gradation of Esoteric Powers

Vision 49 reveals a hierarchical spectrum of esoteric knowledge represented by luminous rays:

| Ray Color | Domain |
|-----------|--------|
| Light-green | Humus and earth (basic knowledge) |
| Greenish-yellow topaz | Plants, reptiles, spectres |
| Purple | Sphinxes, black rose, sibyls |
| Neutral grey | Threshold between visible and invisible worlds (diamond) |
| Turquoise-blue sapphire | Angels and messengers with extrasensory wisdom |
| Violet amethyst | Sylphs, fairies, kings, queens |
| Carbon/silicon (opposite polarity) | Strange beings and witches |

Sorcery and healing operate at different points on this spectrum. Witches occupy the carbon/silicon pole—the dark inverse of the violet realm. The neutral grey center marks the liminal diamond zone separating visible from invisible reality. Higher-grade *murayas* operate in sapphire and amethyst ranges, granting access to angelic allies that lower-grade sorcerers cannot match. This positional advantage means a muraya's power fundamentally outclasses a *sumiruna* regardless of the specific entities deployed.

## Battleships and Vessels of Cosmic Warfare

A remarkable feature is the deployment of massive spirit vessels as battleships.

The *yanayakumama* (black boa, mother of the waters) serves as a living battleship with a white forehead stripe. It inhabits black-water lakes, sun-bathes at midday for solar energy, and generates electrified tornadoes liberating flying animals. Behind it march *yana-puma runa* (black tiger-people with red eyes) as infantry. This living vessel demonstrates how [[ayahuasca-tourism-economics-peruvian-amazon]]. Each group possesses named spirit entities and combat techniques, suggesting a highly developed intertribal esoteric geopolitics.
