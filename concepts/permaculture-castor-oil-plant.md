## Castor (Ricinus communis)

The castor oil plant ([[chamovitz-photoperiodism-agricultural-applications]].

## Botany and Growth Habit

A large, dramatic plant reaching 3-12 m in tropical conditions, though
typically 1-3 m as an annual. The large, palmate leaves are up to 60 cm
across and range from green to deep reddish-purple, depending on
variety. The spiny seed capsules are distinctive and ornamental. Growth
rate is extremely rapid -- castor can outgrow most weeds when
conditions are favourable.

## Climate and Soil

Castor is adapted to tropical [[oyster-mushroom-log-cultivation-temperate-regions-short-log-method]]. It is [[cannabis-oil-extraction]] is toxic due to residual
ricin and must be detoxified before use as animal feed or fertiliser.

## Agricultural Uses

- **Insecticide:** Castor oil sprays can deter certain insect pests
- **Mole repellent:** Castor oil applied to soil repels moles and voles
  in some garden applications
- **Green manure:** The fast-growing biomass can be cut and
  incorporated into soil
