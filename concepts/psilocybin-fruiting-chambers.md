# Psilocybin Fruiting Chambers and Stages

Fruiting stage management, growing chamber construction, casing materials, and drying/storage for psilocybin mushrooms, based on Gottlieb's Psilocybin Producer's Guide (1976) and the Magic Mushroom Grower's Guide.

Related: [[psilocybin-mushroom-cultivation]], [[mushroom-indoor-cultivation]], [[pf-tek-guide]], [[mushroom-substrates]]

## Fruiting Stages (Gottlieb, 1976)

Three distinct stages of carpophore development, each with specific environmental requirements:

### Stage 1: Casing Colonization
- Humidity: ~90% (use humidifier or regular misting)
- Temperature: Species-dependent (P. cubensis: 84-86F)
- Light: None
- Fresh air: None
- Casing layer: "Mountains and valleys" surface pattern to increase area and disperse CO2
- Materials: Peat moss + chalk/limestone flour, or sterilized soil/vermiculite

### Stage 2: Pinhead Initiation
- Triggered by: temperature drop + fresh air introduction + light (12h on/off cycle)
- Light: Grow-lux type fluorescent, high in blue spectrum
- Maintain humidity at previous level
- Avoid abrupt changes in humidity or temperature
- Mist carefully — forceful spraying damages developing pinheads

### Stage 3: Primordia Development
- Humidity: Drop to 85-90%
- Primordia grow to pea size, then develop over ~1 week
- Harvest just after partial veil ruptures (peak psilocybin content)
- For spore collection: wait until cap slightly upturns or purplish color appears at base

## Growing Chamber Construction

- Materials: Plastic-lined box, aquarium, or styrofoam cooler — must be insulated and well-sealed
- Humidity: Humidifier or regular misting
- Temperature: Steam, heating pad underneath, or overhead light (note: heating pad/light lower humidity)
- Fresh air: Via humidifier or when opened for misting
- CO2 removal: Holes at bottom of chamber (CO2 sinks); or fan interior when opened
- Alternative containers: Plastic trays, trash bags, baking dishes, plastic-lined cardboard boxes, aquarium tanks

## Casing Materials

| Material | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Peat moss + chalk | Standard mycological recommendation | Requires pH balancing |
| Sterilized soil | Readily available | May contain contaminants |
| Vermiculite | Clean, moisture-retentive | Lower nutrient value |

## Drying and Storage (Gottlieb)

- Dry mycelium/mushrooms in oven no higher than 200F — check periodically
- Turn off heat and leave in oven to cool (removes residual moisture)
- Wet mycelium is ~90% water: 50-100g wet = 5-10g dried
- Psilocin is highly susceptible to oxidation — store in airtight container under refrigeration with silica-gel
- Higher incubation temperature (75F) = faster growth but lower psilocybin; lower temperature (70F) = slower but more potent
