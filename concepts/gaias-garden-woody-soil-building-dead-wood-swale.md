## Hugelkultur Summary (for Context)

For completeness, Hemenway describes the classic hugelkultur technique:

1. Pile branches or brush 1-2 feet deep in a mound 4-8 feet long
2. Stomp on the pile to compact it slightly
3. Add compostable materials: grass clippings, sod, straw
4. Sprinkle compost on the mound
5. Top with an inch or so of soil
6. Plant with seeds or starts

Potatoes particularly love hugelkultur and can be started a month earlier
than in garden beds. Squashes, melons, and other vines do well too. The
decomposing organic matter raises the temperature slightly, boosting plant
growth, and the woody material holds water and releases nutrients slowly.
