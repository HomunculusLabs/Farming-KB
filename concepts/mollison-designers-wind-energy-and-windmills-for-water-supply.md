## Wind and Solar Complementarity

Mollison notes that water often has to be pumped into storage by
windmill or solar systems. In many dryland and island situations,
windmills and solar panels or photovoltaics together provide reliable
water supply. When wind fails, solar may still pump; when solar fails
(in cloudy conditions), wind may still turn the mill. This
complementary approach increases system reliability without the need
for battery storage.
