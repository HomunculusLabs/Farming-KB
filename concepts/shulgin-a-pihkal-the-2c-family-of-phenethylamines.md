# The 2C Family of Phenethylamines

## Overview

The 2C family is a large group of synthetic [[taar1-trace-amine-associated-receptor-psychedelic-pharmacology]].

## Core Structure

The fundamental scaffold is 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine (2C-H):

```
        OCH3
         |
    H3CO-●-CH2-CH2-NH2
         |
        [4-X]
```

Where X is the variable 4-position substituent that defines each member of the family. 2C-H itself is essentially inactive as a psychedelic but serves as the essential precursor for all other 2C compounds.

## Structure-Activity Relationships

### Halogen Substituents (The 2C-X Series)

The halogenated 2C compounds form the most well-characterized subgroup:

- **2C-F** (fluoro): Minimal activity — fluorine is too small and electronegative
- **2C-Cl** (2C-C, chloro): 20-40 mg, 4-6 hours. Gentle, empathogenic quality
- **2C-Br** (2C-B, bromo): 12-24 mg, 4-8 hours. The most popular and well-known member
- **2C-I** (iodo): 14-22 mg, 6-10 hours. More visual and longer-lasting than 2C-B

There is a clear trend: increasing halogen size (Cl → Br → I) corresponds to increasing potency and duration. This correlates with increased lipophilicity and enhanced [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin]], particularly cardiovascular toxicity at higher doses.

### Other Notable Members

- **2C-B-FLY** (difuran ring system): A structural departure from the simple 4-substituted pattern. Extremely potent at sub-milligram doses with unique effects.
- **2C-N** (nitro): The nitro group is electron-withdrawing, producing a compound with different pharmacological properties than the electron-donating halogens.
- **2C-G series** (trimethylene, tetramethylene): Ring-constrained analogues where the ethylamine chain is locked into a ring structure, reducing flexibility and altering receptor interactions.
- **2C-SE** (methylseleno): The selenium analogue of 2C-T. Rarely explored due to selenium's toxicity concerns.
