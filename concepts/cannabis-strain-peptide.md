# Peptide

Peptide is a sativa-dominant hybrid [[durban-poison]], Heavy Duty Fruity, and Sweet
Tooth #3 to create a plant with impressive yields, distinctive citrus-candy
aromas, and a balanced psychoactive experience.

## Genetics and Lineage

Peptide's genetic background brings together three notable cannabis varieties
in a carefully structured cross. The mother plant was an especially large
specimen from a Heavy Duty Fruity and Sweet Tooth #3 cross, selected for her
massive bud production. The father plant was a Durban Poison male chosen for
its resin production and more restrained growth habit.

Durban Poison is a legendary pure sativa landrace from South Africa, known for
its uplifting effects, sweet anise-like aroma, and relatively manageable
flowering time for a landrace sativa. It contributes the sativa dominance and
the energetic qualities of Peptide's high. Heavy Duty Fruity brings intense
fruit flavors and heavy resin production, while Sweet Tooth #3, originally
bred by Spice of Life Seeds, adds sweetness and further yield potential. The
combination creates a genetically diverse plant with a complex terpene profile.

## Breeder Information

Limestone City Seeds operates as part of the Royal Canadian Marijuana
Collective (RCMC), a Canadian breeding organization with over 15 years of
growing and breeding experience. The collective has access to a diverse
library of quality genetics and focuses particularly on creating strains suited
for indoor cultivation. Their breeding philosophy emphasizes creating heavy-
yielding plants with appealing flavor profiles that perform well in Canadian
growing conditions.

## Growing Characteristics

Peptide grows in a Christmas tree shape with moderate stretch, making it
suitable for indoor cultivation with proper light management. Key growing
details include:

- **Indoor flowering time**: 8-9 weeks from forced flowering
- **Outdoor harvest**: End of September
- **Growth pattern**: Christmas tree shape, medium height with some stretch
- **Indoor management**: Keep lights close to plants to control height
- **Climate tolerance**: Suitable for outdoor growing even in colder parts
  of Canada during summer, though extended cold periods should be avoided
- **Support needs**: Staking recommended during vegetative period to prevent
  branches from succumbing to bud weight in mid-flowering
- **Yield**: Heavy yields in both indoor and outdoor environments
- **Resin production**: High, with crystal-covered buds that gleam like a
  disco ball

The strain's adaptability to both indoor and outdoor cultivation makes it
versatile for growers in various situations. Its performance in Canadian
summers demonstrates good cold tolerance for a sativa-dominant plant, though
growers in northern climates should protect plants from unexpected cold snaps.

## Aroma and Flavor

Peptide's aroma and flavor profile is one of its most distinctive features.
The buds emit a fragrance of sweet citrus combined with cotton candy, creating
an immediately appealing scent. The smoke is described as massaging the
insides upon inhalation, suggesting a smooth, full-bodied vapor that carries
the sweet and citrus notes through to the palate. This combination of citrus
brightness and candy-like sweetness makes Peptide a particularly enjoyable
strain for flavor-conscious consumers.

## Effects

Peptide delivers a balanced psychoactive experience that combines mental
engagement with physical comfort. The Durban Poison influence provides a
clear, focused sativa element, while the Heavy Duty Fruity and Sweet Tooth
genetics add a relaxing body component. The overall effect is well-rounded and
suitable for various occasions, from creative pursuits to social gatherings.

## Medical Applications

The balanced nature of Peptide's effects may make it useful for medical
cannabis patients managing conditions that respond to both mental stimulation
and physical relaxation. The smooth, enjoyable smoke is also beneficial for
patients who use cannabis frequently and prefer strains with appealing flavors
that do not cause harshness or irritation.

## Potency

Peptide has a THC content of approximately 16%, placing it in the moderate
potency range. This level provides meaningful therapeutic and recreational
effects while remaining accessible to intermediate consumers.

## Classification

Peptide is classified as a sativa-dominant hybrid. The Durban Poison parent
provides the sativa foundation, while the Heavy Duty Fruity and Sweet Tooth #3
genetics add indica characteristics that moderate the plant's growth and
contribute to its heavy yield potential.
