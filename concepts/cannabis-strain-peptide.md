# Peptide

Peptide is a sativa-dominant hybrid [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] and more restrained growth habit.

Durban Poison is a legendary pure [[african-landrace-sativa-modern-hybrids]]. It contributes the sativa dominance and
the energetic qualities of Peptide's high. Heavy Duty Fruity brings intense
fruit flavors and heavy resin production, while Sweet Tooth #3, originally

combination creates a genetically diverse plant with a complex terpene profile.

## Breeder Information

Limestone City Seeds operates as part of the Royal Canadian Marijuana
Collective (RCMC), a Canadian breeding organization with over 15 years of
growing and breeding experience. The collective has access to a diverse
library of quality genetics and focuses particularly on creating strains suited
for indoor cultivation. Their breeding philosophy emphasizes creating heavy-
yielding plants with appealing flavor profiles that perform well in Canadian
growing conditions.

## Growing Characteristics

Peptide grows in a Christmas tree shape with moderate stretch, making it
suitable for indoor cultivation with proper light management. Key growing
details include:

- **Indoor flowering time**: 8-9 weeks from forced flowering
- **Outdoor harvest**: End of September
- **Growth pattern**: Christmas tree shape, medium height with some stretch
- **Indoor management**: Keep lights close to plants to control height
- **Climate tolerance**: Suitable for outdoor growing even in colder parts
  of Canada during summer, though extended cold periods should be avoided
- **Support needs**: Staking recommended during vegetative period to prevent
  branches from succumbing to bud weight in mid-flowering
- **Yield**: Heavy yields in both indoor and outdoor environments
- **Resin production**: High, with crystal-covered buds that gleam like a
  disco ball

The strain's adaptability to both indoor and outdoor cultivation makes it
versatile for growers in various situations. Its performance in Canadian
summers demonstrates good cold tolerance for a sativa-dominant plant, though
growers in northern climates should protect plants from unexpected cold snaps.
