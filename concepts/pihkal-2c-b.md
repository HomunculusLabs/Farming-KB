# 2C-B

## Overview

2C-B (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine) is one of the most well-known
members of the 2C family of phenethylamines. First synthesized by Alexander
Shulgin in 1974 and documented in PIHKAL as compound #7, 2C-B earned the
street name "Nexus" for its unique position bridging classical psychedelics
and empathogens. It occupies a distinctive niche between the visual intensity
of LSD and the emotional warmth of [[mescaline]]. This dose-dependent shift is one of its most
characteristic features.

## Chemical Structure and Classification

2C-B is a member of the 2C family, characterized by two methoxy groups on
the phenyl ring. Its IUPAC name is 2-(4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethan-1-
amine. The "2C" prefix refers to the two carbons between the phenyl ring
and the amine, distinguishing these from their amphetamine "DO" counterparts.

The bromine atom at the 4-position is the defining substituent and
significantly influences the pharmacological profile. The 2,5-dimethoxy
pattern is critical for 5-HT2A receptor activity, shared across the entire
2C series. 2C-B is the phenethylamine analogue of DOB, with removal of the
alpha-methyl group resulting in reduced potency and duration.

## Dosage and Administration

Shulgin's recommended oral dosage range is 12 to 24 milligrams, with 15 to
20 mg considered standard. The compound has a notably steep dose-response
curve: small increases above 20 mg produce dramatically more intense
effects, making careful measurement essential.

Onset occurs within 30 to 60 minutes. The peak lasts approximately 2 to 4
hours, with total duration of 4 to 8 hours. The effects often come in
distinct waves: a peak, brief plateau, second wave, then gradual tapering.
Insufflation reduces the dose needed (4 to 8 mg) and shortens duration but
causes significant nasal discomfort.

## Effects Profile

At lower doses (12 to 16 mg), 2C-B produces empathogenic effects similar to
MDMA: enhanced tactile sensation, emotional openness, increased sociability,
and brighter colors. At higher doses (18 to 24 mg), visual effects intensify
to include geometric patterning, color shifting, texture enhancement, and
open-eye hallucinations. The visual style is often described as clean,
colorful, and highly aesthetic.

philosophical introspection. Physical effects include pupil dilation, mild to
