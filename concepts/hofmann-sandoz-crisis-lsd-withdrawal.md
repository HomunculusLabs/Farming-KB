## The Growing Problem

statement on the LSD problem and to publish accounts of the corresponding
measures that had been taken. The situation had grown beyond the capacity
of any single pharmaceutical company to control. On August 23, 1965, Dr.
A. Cerletti, then director of the Pharmaceutical Department of Sandoz,
issued a formal "Decision Regarding LSD 25 and Other Hallucinogenic
