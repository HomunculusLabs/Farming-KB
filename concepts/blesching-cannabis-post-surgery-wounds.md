### Key Takeaways

- A 2009 meta-analysis confirmed all classes of cannabinoids effectively
  reduce post-surgical inflammatory pain (CHI: 4)
- Both endogenous and plant-based cannabinoids show anti-inflammatory
  action relevant to surgical recovery
- Whole-plant cannabis with its 200-250 constituents may offer
  synergistic benefits beyond isolated cannabinoid preparations
- Topical, oral, sublingual, and inhaled routes each offer distinct
  advantages for post-surgical pain management
- Combining cannabinoid therapy with mind-body approaches such as
  visualization and meditation may enhance recovery outcomes
- Coordination with surgical team and pain management physicians is
  essential when integrating cannabis into post-operative care
