# Natural Intelligence

Natural Intelligence is the concept that a non-human, purposive intelligence pervades Nature and governs the ongoing formation of stable and enduring patterns of information throughout the fabric of reality.

Rather than viewing the Universe as a mindless accident going nowhere, the Natural Intelligence hypothesis holds that reality is a meaningful and intelligent system -- an immense computational process in which patterns of information naturally organize themselves according to nested hierarchies of logic.

## The Nested Logics of Nature

The forms of logic that govern pattern formation in Nature are language-like, computational, and operate within and upon particular systems of information enfolded within one another in a nested hierarchy:

**Physico-logic:** The language-like logic of physics acts as a substrate in which the logic of chemistry emerges. Physical laws represent the most fundamental computational rules of the system.

**Chemo-logic:** The language-like logic of chemistry embodies the logic of molecular biology. Molecular interactions follow deterministic rules that produce emergent complexity.

**Bio-logic:** Advanced bio-logic leads to brains which embody patterns of information we call minds. Biology represents a vastly more complex computational layer built upon chemistry.

**Psycho-logic:** Conscious minds are subsequently able to reflect on the intelligence governing these processes. Psychology and consciousness represent the most complex informational layer yet known.

All forms of logic must derive from some original and [[mckenna-radio-entelechy-organic-matrix-ripping]] in which countless replicating patterns swarm about the Earth's surface, each organism an informational expression of Natural Intelligence.

Science has failed to see the contextually directed movement of Natural Intelligence, claiming instead that evolution is essentially pointless and mindless. But this can only be a subjective inference likely drawn according to the perceived duration over which evolution works, a duration so great that the intelligence operating over such spans remains all but invisible.

The Natural Intelligence framework is articulated through what can be called the "fantastic hypothesis": reality, or Nature, is a meaningful and intelligent system as opposed to some mindless accident going nowhere.

According to this hypothesis, we are woven into an orchestrational tide of information, interconnected throughout, whose glorious and spectacular purpose awaits us. The natural tendency of the Universe is to foster the integration of more and more information.

## Theories of Natural Intelligence

### The Gaia Hypothesis

The [[gaia-hypothesis]], formulated by [[james-lovelock]] and Lynn Margulis, proposes that Earth's living and non-living components function together as a self-regulating system that maintains conditions suitable for life. This planetary homeostasis -- regulating temperature, atmospheric composition, and ocean salinity over billions of years -- resembles the kind of feedback-controlled intelligence that the Natural Intelligence framework describes at a planetary scale.
