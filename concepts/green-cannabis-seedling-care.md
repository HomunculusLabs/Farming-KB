# Cannabis Seedling Care

From Greg Green's *The [[maitake-fruiting-body-success-rate-management]] -- out of 10
seeds only 7-8 may germinate.

### Seed Towel Propagation

Place seeds on a damp towel or damp piece of cotton wool. Cover with
more damp material. Keep moist at all times. If the material dries out
it may damage the seeds. Check daily for roots. If roots appear,
immediately transfer to a [[query-how-to-prevent-transplant-shock-when-moving-cannabis-plants]].

### Propagation Kits (Rockwool SBS Tray)

The recommended method. Seeds are placed into small rockwool cubes in
a propagation tray. At the bottom is a reservoir where water or
germination hormone is poured. The cubes automatically wick up the
solution. The cover goes on to maintain humidity. Very high success
rate -- all seeds can sometimes germinate.

Cost is about $20 total for tray, rockwool cubes, and grow
fertilizers. The same tray can also be used for rooting clones.

### Scuffing Seeds

Seeds that fail to germinate often have shells too hard to break open.
Line a small box with sandpaper, place seeds inside, cover with more
sandpaper, close and shake for a few minutes. The scuffed shells should
be easier to pop open. Plant in soil afterward.

## Dangers During Germination

**Drafts** are a killer and will stunt germination. Always keep
germinating seeds away from open windows or fans.

**Cold rooms** can inhibit germination rates. Keep the room warm.

**Incorrect fertilizer mixtures** can burn seedlings. In fact, water is
all seedlings should need. Some people use germination solutions but
must keep solution strengths low.

**Disturbing the seeds** -- do not go digging around to check progress.
This can break or damage the seed and root.

## The Seedling Stage

After the first pair of embryonic leaves receive light, the plant begins
to produce new leaves. These leaves are different and may show marijuana
characteristics such as three-rounded finger-shaped points. As the
seedling grows, more leaves form and bush upwards along with a stem.

The seedling stage can last between 1 and 3 weeks. At the end your plant
will have maybe 4-8 new leaves. Some bottom leaves may drop off.
