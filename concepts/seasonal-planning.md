## Seasonal Planning — USDA Zone 7-8

A comprehensive monthly calendar integrating annual vegetables, perennials, cannabis, animal husbandry, composting, and infrastructure tasks. Zone 7-8 last frost: ~April 5-15. First frost: ~October 25-November 5. Growing season: 180-210 days.

## Monthly Task Calendar

### January
- **Planning**: Finalize seed orders (see timeline below). Review last year's notes. Layout garden maps, crop rotations.
- **Cannabis**: Research genetics, order seeds/clone supplies. Maintain mother plants if indoor.
- **Animals**: [[duck-keeping]] — check waterers for freeze damage. [[goat-keeping]] — continue hay feeding, mineral access. Hoof trim if overdue.
- **Compost**: Turn winter piles. [[composting-systems]] — start new pile if space allows.
- **Infrastructure**: Repair fencing, tools. Build new beds or [[greenhouse-design]] structures.

### February
- **Seeds**: Start onions, leeks, celery indoors (10-12 weeks before frost). Start peppers, eggplant indoors (8-10 weeks).
- **Pruning**: Dormant prune fruit trees, berries, grapes. Apply dormant oil spray.
- **Perennials**: Plant bare-root fruit trees, berries, asparagus crowns, rhubarb.
- **Cannabis**: Start cannabis seeds indoors under lights. See [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]].
- **Animals**: Begin spring pasture prep. Order spring chicks/ducklings.
- **Compost**: [[vermicomposting]] bins — check worm activity, add bedding if needed.

### March
- **Seeds**: Start tomatoes, brassicas indoors (6-8 weeks). Direct sow: peas, spinach, radishes, carrots, beets, turnips, potatoes (St. Patrick's Day tradition).
- **[[cover-cropping-guide]]**: Crimson clover and Austrian winter pea planted in fall should be knee-high. Terminate (crimp or mow) 2-3 weeks before planting.
- **Greenhouse**: Begin transplanting cold-hardy crops into tunnels. See [[greenhouse-design]].
- **Animals**: Ducks — increase forage access as pasture greens up. Goats — reduce hay as browse becomes available. Watch for kidding season.
- **Soil**: Take soil samples. Apply lime if needed (takes months to act). [[soil-fertility-management]] amendments.

### April
- **Transplant**: Harden off and transplant tomatoes, peppers, eggplant after last frost (~April 10-15).
- **Direct sow**: Beans, corn, cucumbers, squash, melons after soil temp reaches 60°F.
- **Succession**: Second sowing of radishes, lettuce, spinach.
- **Cannabis**: Move to vegetative stage. Begin training (LST, topping). See [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]].
- **Animals**: Spring pasture rotation begins. Set up electro-net fencing. Ducklings integrate with flock.
- **Compost**: First hot compost batch of spring — mix winter accumulation with fresh greens.

### May
- **Planting**: Everything in ground. Transplant sweet potatoes (slips), successions of beans, corn.
- **[[herbal-medicine]]**: Transplant calendula, chamomile, holy basil, ashwagandha seedlings.
- **Pest management**: Install row covers on brassicas. Set up traps for cucumber beetles, squash bugs.
- **Cannabis**: Full vegetative growth. Defoliate lower growth. Monitor for pests. See [[cannabis-pest-management]].
- **Animals**: Full pasture rotation. Watch for heat stress in ducks (shade, water). Goats — copper bolus if needed.
- **Compost**: Turn piles every 2-3 weeks. Harvest finished compost for garden beds.

### June
- **Summer crops**: Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, squash in full production. Trellis, prune, train.
- **Succession**: Sow fall brassicas indoors (late June). Sow beans, cucumbers for second crop.
- **Herbs**: Harvest chamomile, calendula, yarrow flowers at peak. Begin drying. See [[herbal-medicine]].
- **Cannabis**: Consider flipping to flower (12/12) mid-late June if targeting September harvest. See [[cannabis-flowering-stage]].
- **Animals**: Monitor parasites (FAMACHA scoring for goats). Pasture rest rotation.
- **Compost**: Maintain hot piles. Start bokashi fermentation of kitchen scraps. See [[bokashi]].

### July
- **Maintenance**: Prune tomatoes, harvest daily. Mulch heavily to conserve moisture.
- **Succession**: Direct sow carrots, beets, beans for fall harvest. Start fall lettuce indoors.
- **Heat management**: Shade cloth on greenhouse. Water deeply, early morning.
- **Cannabis**: Early flowering. Support heavy branches. Begin nutrient shift. See [[cannabis-flowering-stage]].
- **Animals**: Peak parasite pressure — pasture rotation critical. Ensure shade and dust bathing areas.
- **Preserving**: Peak preservation season begins. See [[food-preservation-canning]].

### August
- **Fall planting**: Transplant fall brassicas, start direct sowing spinach, radishes, turnips, kale.
- **[[cover-cropping-guide]]**: Sow buckwheat (quick summer cover) on empty beds. Sow oats + crimson clover for winter cover.
- **Cannabis**: Mid-flower. Flush if near harvest. Monitor trichomes.
- **Animals**: Begin evaluating who to cull/replace before winter feed costs. Order winter hay.
- **Preserving**: Canning tomatoes, pickling cucumbers, freezing beans, drying herbs.
- **Seeds**: Begin [[seed-saving-techniques]] — let best plants go to seed. Tag and label.

### September
- **Harvest**: Peak harvest of summer crops. Winter squash cure (80°F, 75% humidity, 10-14 days).
- **Fall greens**: Transplant lettuce, spinach, Asian greens into beds and [[greenhouse-design]] tunnels.
- **Cannabis**: Harvest window (trichomes milky-amber). Dry and cure. See [[cannabis-harvest-and-curing]].
- **Animals**: Final pasture rotation before dormancy. Winterize housing. Bedding prep.
- **Compost**: Build winter piles. Last turning of hot compost before cold weather.
- **Perennials**: Plant garlic cloves (mid-October target but prep now). Divide perennials.

### October
- **Planting**: Plant garlic, shallots, flower bulbs. Transplant final fall greens.
- **[[cover-cropping-guide]]**: Final window for winter covers — winter rye, hairy vetch, crimson clover.
- **Cannabis**: Curing in progress. Clean and sanitize grow space. Start next cycle prep.
- **Animals**: Full winter prep — draft-free housing, heated waterers, hay storage. Goats — breeding season.
- **Preserving**: Root cellaring, final canning, fermenting. See [[food-preservation-canning]].
- **Soil**: Spread finished compost and leaves on beds. Sheet mulch new areas.

### November
- **Cleanup**: Remove dead annuals (leave roots in ground). Mulch perennial beds heavily (4-6 inches).
- **Greenhouse**: Install row covers inside tunnel for winter growing. Direct sow spinach, mizuna, claytonia.
- **Cannabis**: New seedlings or clones under lights. Reset room for next cycle.
- **Animals**: Breeding management. Feed transition to full hay. Monitor body condition.
- **Planning**: Evaluate what worked and what didn't. Start seed and supply wishlists.
- **[[herbal-medicine]]**: Harvest final roots (echinacea, valerian, ashwagandha). Dry and process.

### December
- **Rest**: Light garden work — tool cleaning, inventory, repair.
- **Seed orders**: Early bird discounts from Johnny's, High Mowing, Territorial, Baker Creek. See [[seed-saving-techniques]].
- **Animals**: Routine care. Monitor in extreme cold. Goats — early kidding prep.
- **Compost**: [[vermicomposting]] — minimal feeding, keep bins from freezing.
- **Learning**: Read, plan, take courses. Review and update [[homesteading-infrastructure]] plans.

## Seasonal Flow Overview

**Spring Wake-Up (Feb-Apr)** — Soil prep, early plantings, animal transitions. Energy builds.
**Summer Peak (May-Aug)** — Maximum production, preservation, pest pressure. Labor-intensive.
**Fall Transition (Sep-Nov)** — Harvest, storage, cover crops, winterizing. Abundance processing.
**Winter Planning (Dec-Feb)** — Rest, learning, ordering, indoor growing. Slow season.
