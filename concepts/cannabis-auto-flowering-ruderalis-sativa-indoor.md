## Auto-Flowering Cannabis for Sativa Cultivation

## Overview

Auto-flowering cannabis strains represent one of the most significant breeding innovations in modern [[cervantes-cannabis-ruderalis-autoflowering]] genetics, breeders have created sativa-dominant varieties that flower automatically based on age rather than photoperiod, dramatically reducing the time from seed to harvest and enabling cultivation in conditions that would otherwise be unsuitable for sativa varieties.

## The Ruderalis Contribution

Cannabis ruderalis is a subspecies of cannabis native to Central and Eastern Europe and Russia. Unlike Cannabis sativa and Cannabis indica, which require specific light cycles (12 hours of darkness) to trigger flowering, ruderalis begins flowering automatically after a set number of weeks regardless of light conditions. This trait evolved as an adaptation to the short growing seasons of northern latitudes, where waiting for the proper photoperiod would mean the plant never matures before winter.

The key traits that ruderalis contributes to auto-flowering hybrids:

- **Automatic flowering**: Triggered by plant age, not light cycle
- **Compact size**: Ruderalis genetics keep plants small, typically 1-3 feet tall
- **Rapid maturation**: From germination to harvest in 60-75 days
- **Durability**: Ruderalis evolved in harsh climates, contributing hardiness
- **Simplified [[cannabis-sativa-breeding-evolution]]. Created by crossing a Little Devil plant with a Ruderalis variety, Angelmatic brings auto-flowering characteristics to a sativa-dominant genetic background.

**Breeding goal**: Enable sativa-dominant hybrid cultivation even in very small grow spaces — "smaller than your average fridge."

**Growing profile**:

- Height: approximately 1 foot (30 cm) — extremely compact
- Flowering trigger: approximately 2.5 weeks after germination
- Total time from seed to harvest: approximately 60 days
- Odor: minimal — "she won"t ever get too stinky"
- Pest resistance: resistant to common pests
- Nutrient needs: good soil and generous nitrogen supplementation
- Indoor yield: approximately 50 grams per plant
- Outdoor yield: up to 100 grams per plant in temperate summer conditions

**Target users**: Rookies, light [[supercropping]]
- **No cloning**: Auto-flowering plants cannot be effectively cloned because the flowering timer starts at germination; clones would flower immediately regardless of size
- **Reduced complexity of effect**: The high from auto-flowering strains tends to be simpler and shorter-lasting than that of photoperiod sativas

## The SOG Method

For Angelmatic and similar compact auto-flowering strains, the Sea of Green (SOG) method is recommended. SOG involves growing many small plants close together rather than fewer large plants. With auto-flowering varieties, this means:

- Starting many seeds simultaneously rather than relying on clones
- Minimal vegetative period before automatic flowering begins
- High plant density compensating for lower yield per plant
- Uniform canopy height simplifying light distribution
- Fast turnover from seed to harvest enabling multiple cycles per year

## Medical Applications

Auto-flowering sativas serve specific niches in medical cannabis:

- **Light users**: Patients who need small amounts for symptom management
- **Home growing**: Patients who want to grow their own medicine without complex setups
- **Discreet cultivation**: The minimal odor and compact size allow inconspicuous growing
- **Consistent supply**: The rapid 60-day cycle ensures a steady supply of fresh medicine
- **Beginner-friendly**: Resistance to pests and simple requirements suit inexperienced growers
