following spring. In mild climates (Deep South, California, Australia),

## Sod Removal Methods

### Method 1: Rotary Cultivation

Hire or rent a front-tined walk-behind rototiller of at least 7–8
horsepower. Key instructions:

- Make the first pass straight down the center, the long way (less
  turning = less wasted effort)
- Set the drag bar for **one inch depth only** initially — deeper
  settings cause the machine to skip and jump
- After one pass, go back over the same row 4–5 times. It will seem
  like almost nothing has happened — this is normal
- **Do not try to till deeply on the first pass**. Two inches deep is a
  successful first effort; it kills most of the grass
- **Stop and wait 3–7 days**. Let sun and weather kill the damaged grass
  before tilling deeper — tilling deep immediately relocates grass
  fragments into moist soil where they regrow
- On the second session, cross-till (north–south if first pass was
  east–west), gradually increasing depth
- A powerful front-tined tiller eliminates approximately 1,000 sq ft
  (90 sq m) per hour on moderately difficult ground
- Hand-digging the same area takes approximately 20 hours

### Method 2: Herbicide-Assisted (Optional)

Spraying with glyphosate, then waiting 4–8 weeks of warm weather (or over
winter if sprayed in early autumn) for the sod to die and rot, makes
subsequent digging or tilling proceed at least twice as fast. This assumes
the gardener is not strictly organic.
