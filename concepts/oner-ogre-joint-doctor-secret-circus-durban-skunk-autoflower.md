# OGRE Strain Profile (Joint Doctor × Secret Garden Seeds)

## Overview

OGRE is a sativa-dominant auto-flowering cannabis strain created through a collaboration between Canada's Joint Doctor and the UK's Secret Garden Seeds. Combining the auto-flowering Secret Citrus (also known as Secret Circus) with a classic Durban Skunk, OGRE breaks the typical auto-flower mold by exhibiting an accelerated growth pattern that produces plants taller and wider than most auto-flowering varieties. With a THC content of 15%, OGRE delivers a potent, layered cerebral high that creeps up on the user before launching them into a soaring, uplifting experience. Its name is a playful nod to its large, fat buds with hints of orange. (see [[autoflowering-sativa-development-joint-doctor]] sativa-dominant
- **Genetics**: Secret Citrus (auto) × Durban Skunk
- **Potency**: THC 15%
- **Seed availability**: Regular seeds from jointdoctordirect.com and secretgardenseeds.com
- **Life cycle from seed to harvest**: Approximately 10 weeks (70 days)

## Lineage and Genetics

OGRE's parentage brings together two distinct genetic lines:

- **Secret Citrus (Secret Circus)**: An auto-flowering strain that contributes the automatic flowering trait and the citrus-influenced terpene profile. This parent ensures OGRE flowers without a photoperiod change, making it accessible to growers who lack light-control equipment or who want a simplified grow cycle. (see [[tropicana-cookies]]).

The combination of these two parents creates a strain that bridges the gap between the convenience of auto-flowering genetics and the robust growth patterns normally associated with photoperiod sativas. (see [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-challenges]]).

## Growth Characteristics

### Structure and Habit

OGRE defies the common perception that auto-flowering strains are small and compact:

- **Average height**: Approximately 3 feet (90 cm), significantly taller than most auto-flowers
- **Width**: Grows wider than many auto-growers expect, requiring adequate spacing
- **Vegetative stage**: Extended vegetative period compared to typical auto-flowers, allowing the plant to develop substantial side branching before flowering
- **Resilience**: Particularly resilient to problems encountered during the vegetative stage, making it forgiving for less experienced growers

### Cultivation Method

The breeders provide specific cultivation recommendations for optimal results:

- **Light schedule**: 18 hours of light throughout the entire life cycle for best results
- **Starting containers**: 1-gallon pots in a soil-less substrate from seed
- **Transplant timing**: Transplant into 3–5 gallon pots once sex is shown around day 21
- **Post-transplant growth**: Plants will double or even triple in size after transplanting
- **Nutrient requirements**: Extremely heavy feeder — do not be shy with nutrients
- **Harvest**: Approximately 80 grams per plant at 10 weeks from seed

### Flowering

After transplanting, OGRE enters a vigorous flowering phase where it fills in gaps with dense, succulent-looking buds. The transition from vegetative growth to flowering production is dramatic, with bud development accelerating quickly. Because the plant's vegetative period is extended for an auto-flower, it has more time to develop the branch structure needed to support substantial bud production.

### Growing Tips

- Provide adequate spacing between plants to accommodate the wider growth habit
- Use quality soil-less substrate for the initial 1-gallon pot stage
- Plan for the transplant at day 21 — have larger pots ready
- Feed heavily throughout the cycle, as OGRE is an extremely heavy feeder
- The 18/6 light schedule is recommended over 24/0 for optimal results
- Expect the post-transplant growth explosion and plan headroom accordingly

## Appearance

- **Bud structure**: Fat, dense buds with hints of orange coloring throughout
- **Visual character**: The orange hues and chunky bud structure inspired the Shrek comparison in the source text
- **Resin production**: Buds appear succulent and resinous, with a premium visual appeal
- **Overall impression**: A substantial, impressive plant that looks more like a photoperiod sativa than an auto-flower

## Aroma and Flavor

OGRE's flavor profile is described as having layers, much like an onion — a playful reference to the Shrek theme:

- **First layer**: Licorice taste with a distinct hint of lime
- **Secondary notes**: Citrus undertones from the Secret Citrus parent
- **Earthy base**: Durban Skunk contributes an earthy, skunky foundation
- **Aftertaste**: Lingering licorice and lime combination

The complexity of the flavor profile rewards slow consumption and proper curing.
