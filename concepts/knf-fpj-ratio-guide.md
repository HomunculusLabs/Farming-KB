# KNF FPJ Ratio Guide

[[bionutrients-lab-fermented-plant-juice-fpj]] (FPJ) and [[bionutrients-lab-fermented-fruit-juice-ffj]] Juice (FFJ) both follow the same fundamental osmotic pressure extraction method. This guide covers the correct ratios by plant material type, sugar ratios, and expected fermentation times.

## Base Ratio

The fundamental ratio for all FPJ/FFJ is:

- Plant/fruit material: 1 part by weight
- Brown sugar/jaggery: 1 part by weight (1:1 ratio)

Fill the container to 2/3 of its volume, never more.

## Material Selection by Growth Stage

Choose plant material that matches the growth stage of your target crop:

| Target Stage | Use Material From | Examples |
|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| Early growth (spring) | Fast-growing spring plants | Mugwort, dropwort, bamboo shoot |
| [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] | Summer-growing plants | [[comparison-cassava-vs-taro-vs-sweet-potato-vs-yam]] tips, squash tips, tomato |
| Flowering | Plants that flower in your season | Fruit blossoms, mugwort flowers |
| Fruiting | Mature/fruiting plants | Banana peel, mango, papaya |

## Specific Material Guidelines

### Shoots and Tips

- Use actively growing tips of plants
- Collect between midnight and 2 AM (highest auxin content) or 12-3 PM (highest sugar content)
- Sweet potato vines, bamboo shoots, squash tips, watermelon tips

### Leafy Plants

- Mugwort: good for general vitality
- Dropwort: promotes root growth
- Duckweed: excellent for balanced nutrition

### Fruiting Plants

- Banana peel: use ripe bananas with black spots
- Papaya: contains high enzyme content
- Mango: good for flowering energy
- Tomato: rich in lycopene and nutrients

## Fermentation Times by Material

| Material Type | Fermentation Time | Notes |
|--------------|-------------------|-------|
| Banana peel | 7-10 days | Very fast, check early |
| Papaya | 7-10 days | Fast due to high enzymes |
| Mango | 14-20 days | Standard |
| Apple | 14-20 days | Standard |
| Sweet potato tips | 7-14 days | Fast |
| Bamboo shoots | 14-21 days | Medium |
| Mugwort | 14-21 days | Medium |
| Persimmon | 30+ days | Slow, be patient |

## Sugar Ratio Notes

- 1:1 is the standard ratio
- For very juicy fruits (watermelon), use slightly more sugar to compensate for water content
- For very dry materials (some herbs), you may need slightly less sugar
- Always use brown sugar or jaggery, not white sugar (white sugar lacks minerals)
- Do NOT use honey -- it has its own microorganisms that may interfere

## Container Setup

1. Put stones 10 cm high on bottom for drainage
2. Cover stones with rice straw
3. Pile material and sugar in alternating layers
4. Cover top layer with extra sugar
5. Cover with paper (not sealed)
6. Air must flow through -- set mosquito net over opening

## Dilution Ratios

| Use Case | Dilution |
|----------|----------|
| General foliar spray | 1:1,000 |
| Early growth boost | 1:500 |
| Stress recovery | 1:500 |
| Soil drench | 1:1,000 |
| [[jadam-seed-treatment-and-nursery-management]] | 1:1,000 |

## Temperature

- Optimal fermentation: 23-25C
- Higher temperatures speed fermentation but may cause off-smells
- Lower temperatures slow fermentation
