## Contamination Detection During Colonization

### What to Watch For
During the colonization monitoring period, growers should watch for signs of
contamination that would require discarding the affected bag:
- **Green mould** (Trichoderma): The most common contaminant, appearing as
  rapidly expanding green patches. Trichoderma outcompetes mushroom mycelium
  and will ruin the crop if not caught early.
- **Black mould**: Dark spots or patches that indicate Aspergillus or other
  fungal contaminants.
- **Bacterial slime**: Wet, slimy patches with a foul smell, often appearing
  pink, orange, or brown.
- **Stalled colonization**: If mycelium growth stops expanding before full
  colonization, this may indicate contamination by unseen competitors or
  unfavourable conditions.
