# Phenethylamine Duration and Timing

## Overview

Duration of action is one of the most pharmacologically and practically important properties of phenethylamines. Across the 179 compounds documented in PIHKAL, effective durations range from approximately 2 hours to over 30 hours. Understanding these durations is essential for safe and effective use.

## Duration Categories

### Short-Acting (2-4 hours)
A minority of phenethylamines fall into this category. These tend to be compounds with small substituents or those that are rapidly metabolized. The short duration makes them more manageable but also limits the depth of experience achievable.

### Moderate-Acting (4-8 hours)
The most common duration range, typical of the 2C family without alpha-methylation:
- **2C-B** ([[2c-b-compound-profile]]): 4-6 hours
- **2C-I** ([[2c-i-compound-profile]]): 6-10 hours
- **2C-D**: 4-6 hours
- **MDMA** ([[mdma-in-pihkal-context]]): 4-6 hours

### Long-Acting (8-14 hours)
Compounds with extended durations requiring significant time commitment:
- **2C-E** ([[2c-e-compound-profile]]): 8-12 hours
- **2C-T-7** ([[2c-t-2-compound-profile]]): 8-14 hours
- **MDA** ([[mda-compound-profile]]): 8-12 hours
- **Mescaline** ([[mescaline-compound-profile]]): 10-12 hours

### Very Long-Acting (14-30+ hours)
The alpha-methylated DO family produces the longest durations:
- **DOB** ([[dob-compound-profile]]): 18-30 hours
- **DOI** ([[dob-compound-profile]]): 16-30 hours
- **DOM**: 8-12 hours (shorter than DOB/DOI but still long)

These extreme durations mean that once taken, the experience extends through a full day and potentially into the next, with aftereffects persisting even longer.

## Typical Experience Timeline

For a moderate-duration phenethylamine (4-8 hours):

### Onset (T+0:00 to T+1:00)
- Initial effects begin 20-60 minutes after oral administration
- First signs: mild stimulation, slight visual sharpening, body awareness changes
- Some compounds (notably [[mescaline-compound-profile]]) produce nausea during onset
- This phase can include anxiety or anticipation

### Come-up (T+1:00 to T+2:00)
- Effects intensify rapidly
- Visual effects become clearly established
- Emotional shifts become noticeable
- Peak effects are approaching

### Peak (T+2:00 to T+4:00)
- Full spectrum of effects is active
- Visual effects at their most intense
- Emotional and cognitive effects at maximum
- This is typically the most impactful phase

### Comedown (T+4:00 to T+6:00)
- Effects gradually diminish
- Visual effects fade first
- Emotional and cognitive effects linger longer
- Physical fatigue may become noticeable

### Aftereffects (T+6:00 to T+24:00)
- Residual emotional sensitivity
- Possible fatigue or altered sleep patterns
- "Afterglow" — a pleasant, reflective state experienced by some
- In some cases, mild depressive feelings as serotonin systems recover

## Factors Affecting Duration

### Chemical Structure
- **Alpha-methylation**: The single largest factor in extending duration. Converting a 2C compound to its DO counterpart can extend duration from 4-8 hours to 18-30 hours
- **4-position substituent size**: Larger, more lipophilic groups generally extend duration by slowing metabolic clearance
- **Methylenedioxy bridge**: Can extend duration by blocking oxidative metabolism at adjacent positions

### Route of Administration
PIHKAL compounds are typically administered orally. Other routes (insufflation, sublingual) may alter onset time and duration but are not systematically documented in PIHKAL.

### Individual Factors
- **Metabolism**: Individual variation in hepatic enzyme activity (particularly CYP2D6) can significantly affect duration
- **Body weight**: Larger individuals may clear compounds more slowly or require higher doses
- **Stomach contents**: Food can delay onset but has less effect on total duration
- **Tolerance**: Prior use of serotonergic compounds can reduce both intensity and apparent duration

### Drug Interactions
- **MAOIs**: Can dramatically extend and intensify effects, potentially dangerously
- **CYP2D6 inhibitors**: May extend duration by slowing metabolism
- **Other serotonergic agents**: Risk of serotonin syndrome; not recommended

## Duration and Practical Planning

Shulgin's documentation implicitly emphasizes the importance of duration awareness:

1. **Time commitment**: A 2C-E experience at 8-12 hours requires clearing an entire day; a DOB experience at 18-30 hours requires clearing a day and a half
2. **Sleep planning**: Long-acting compounds will interfere with normal sleep patterns
3. **Aftercare**: The comedown and aftereffects period requires attention to nutrition, hydration, and rest
4. **Driving and responsibility**: Users must remain unfit for driving or responsible tasks for the full duration plus aftereffects

## Duration Across Compound Families

| Family | Typical Duration | Key Factor |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| 2C (no alpha-methyl) | 4-10 hours | 4-position substituent |
| DO (alpha-methylated) | 16-30 hours | Alpha-methylation |
| MD (methylenedioxy) | 4-12 hours | Alpha-methylation, N-substitution |
| Mescaline analogues | 8-12 hours | Ring substitution pattern |
