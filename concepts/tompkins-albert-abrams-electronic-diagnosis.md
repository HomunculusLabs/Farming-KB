## The Reflexophone and Disease Detection

Abrams asked his houseboy Ivor to step onto the lecture platform, strip
to the waist, and face west. As he tapped the boy above the navel, a
student held a specimen of cancerous tissue in light contact with
Ivor's forehead. The class was amazed to hear the resonant note change
to dullness each time the specimen was placed on the forehead. When a
tuberculous specimen was substituted, the same effect appeared at a
different abdominal location.

Abrams found he could differentiate between the wavelengths of
specifically diseased tissues using his "reflexophone" -- an instrument
like a variable electrical resistor that could emit sounds varying in
pitch. Different diseases read from the dial: 55 for syphilis, 58 for
sarcomatous tissue. He could infallibly select or "diagnose" even when
specimens were mixed up.

a person suffered from but to what stage it had advanced.
