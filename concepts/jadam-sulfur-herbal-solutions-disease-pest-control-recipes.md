## JADAM Sulfur and Herbal Solutions — Disease and Pest Control Recipes

## Overview

Beyond the JADAM Wetting Agent, the JADAM pesticide system includes JADAM Sulfur (JS) for disease control and a comprehensive library of JADAM Herbal Solutions (JHS) made from locally available plants. Together, these provide a complete, ultra-low-cost pest and disease management toolkit.

## JADAM Sulfur (JS)

### The Discovery Story

"After nearly 100 experiments, I found the method to completely liquefy sulfur. I still do not forget the rapture I felt at that time. My small kitchen was my lab; it was around 3am that I knew that I finally made it."

Cho"s development of JS was a years-long process of experimentation, driven by the desire to create a sulfur germicide that could be made without heating and in plastic containers.

### No-Heat Innovation

"I wanted a method where you do not need heating at all; do not need steel containers but can use plastic containers; and shorten the making time to less than 10 minutes." The result: a sulfur formulation that dissolves completely in cold water using sodium hydroxide as a solvent.

### Cost and Production

"Cost was less than 50 cents per liter; it could be made simply in less than 10 minutes without having to heat it; and it could be made in plastic containers."

### Target Diseases

"JS is very effective against powdery mildew, downy mildew and fungus. It also works against black spot and canker." These are among the most common and damaging fungal diseases in agriculture.

### Usage Guidelines

- **Greenhouse**: Start from 0.5 L per 500 L water; increase in 0.2 L increments
- **Open field**: Start from 1 L per 500 L water; increase as needed
- **Winter pest control**: Up to 5 L per 500 L water
- **Always combine with JWA**: Without JWA, JS leaves marks on plants and loses effectiveness
- **Minimum JWA**: 2.5 L per 500 L water to prevent concentration problems

### Cautions

- Do not use continuously—only when disease is present or expected
- Can stunt growth if over-applied, especially on young or greenhouse plants
- Some crops (persimmons, walnuts, grapes) are sulfur-sensitive
- Do not spray at noon (except for specific diseases like pear rust)

## JADAM Herbal Solutions (JHS)

### The Concept

JHS are made by extracting active compounds from plants into water, creating liquid pesticide formulations that target specific pest groups. The system is based on traditional botanical pesticide knowledge.

### Available JHS Recipes

JADAM provides recipes for herbal solutions made from:

| Plant | Part Used | Collection Time |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| Tobacco | Leaves | Spring to autumn |
| Red spider lily | Bulbs | Autumn to winter |
| Monkshood (*Aconitum*) | Leaves and roots | Autumn |
| Oleander | Leaves | Spring to autumn |
| Bracken (*Pteridium*) | Leaves | Spring to autumn |
| Sophora root | Roots | Autumn |
| Garlic | Bulbs | Year-round |
| Korean pasque flower | Roots | Autumn |
| Hot pepper | Fruits | Autumn |
| Peppermint | Leaves | Spring to autumn |
| Pyracantha | Fruits | Autumn |

### Extraction Method

"It can also be made by brewing in water for 15 days." The JHS extraction process involves soaking plant material in water for extended periods, allowing water-soluble active compounds to diffuse into the solution.

### Safety Notes

Several JHS ingredients are highly toxic (monkshood, oleander, red spider lily). JADAM provides handling precautions and emphasizes that these should be used with care, even though they are "natural" pesticides.

## Microbial Pesticide from JMS

### Beyond Single Organisms

"Conventional agriculture focuses on selecting a few microorganisms that have antagonistic characteristics against pathogens; and then mass producing them."

JADAM's approach differs: instead of selecting specific antagonistic microorganisms, JMS provides a diverse microbial community that creates competitive conditions unfavorable to pathogens. This approach avoids the resistance problems that arise from single-organism biological pesticides.
