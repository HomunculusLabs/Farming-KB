## Bank Stabilisation Species

For pond edges and waterway banks, stabilisation species include:

- Juncus (rushes)
- Scirpus (bulrushes)
- Various grasses and clovers
- Phyla nodosa (Lippia)
- Bamboo and pampas grass clumps

Pond surface stabilisers include water lilies, Azolla, and water
hyacinth.

## Comfrey for Waste Absorption

Domestically, a comfrey bed is one way to absorb the faecal products
of animals, where wash-water from yards or pens is available. Comfrey
can stand heavy inputs of raw faeces in solution, and the crop may
then be used for fodder or trenched for "instant compost" under other
species such as potatoes. Flow-through systems for methane production
take little plant nutrient from faecal matter, and comfrey or algae
ponds deal with the residues while producing useful by-products for
compost and stock feed.
