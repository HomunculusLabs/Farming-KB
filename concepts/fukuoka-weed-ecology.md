### Straw Mulch
Applying straw mulch (rice straw, wheat straw) suppresses weed germination while
adding organic matter. Straw mulch also moderates soil temperature, retains
moisture, and provides habitat for ground beetles and other beneficial organisms.
Fukuoka applied straw mulch generously — in his rice paddies, he scattered straw
from the previous harvest back onto the fields after seeding. In orchards, he
maintained a thick straw layer under the tree canopy. See
