# Cannabis Tinctures and Edibles

Tinctures and edibles are cannabis consumption methods that deliver cannabinoids through the digestive system (oral) or sublingually (under the tongue). Unlike inhalation, which takes effect in minutes, oral/sublingual methods take 30 minutes to 2 hours but produce longer-lasting effects (4-8 hours).

## Decarboxylation

### Why It's Necessary
- Raw cannabis contains THCA and CBDA (acidic, non-psychoactive precursors)
- Heating converts THCA to THC and CBDA to CBD (decarboxylation)
- This conversion is essential for both edibles and tinctures to be effective
- Occurs naturally during smoking/vaping but must be done deliberately for oral preparations

### Methods
- **Oven**: 220-245°F (105-118°C) for 30-45 minutes. Spread ground flower on baking sheet
- **Sous vide**: 203°F (95°C) for 1.5 hours in sealed vacuum bag. Most precise method
- **Microwave**: Quick but uneven. Risk of burning
- **Boiling in oil/butter**: Simultaneous decarb and infusion. Less efficient (some THC lost to plant material)

### Timing and Temperature
- THCA decarboxylation peaks at ~220°F (105°C)
- Overheating (>300°F/150°C) degrades THC to CBN (more sedative, less psychoactive)
- Target: 70-80% decarboxylation (some THCA retained for [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]])

## Tinctures

### Alcohol Tinctures (Green/Dragon)
- **Green Dragon**: Room temperature extraction. Cannabis soaked in high-proof alcohol (Everclear) for 30+ days. Shaken daily. Strain and use
- **Golden Dragon**: Heat-assisted extraction. Cannabis simmered in alcohol (low heat, double boiler) for 20 minutes. Faster but less full-spectrum
- **Dosage**: 1 dropper (1 ml) = approximately 10mg THC (varies with starting material and extraction efficiency)
- **Sublingual use**: Hold under tongue for 60-90 seconds before swallowing. Faster onset (15-45 min) than swallowing
- **[[psilocybin-shelf-life-stability]]**: 6-12 months at room temperature (alcohol preserves indefinitely)

### Glycerin Tinctures
- Vegetable glycerin instead of alcohol
- Sweeter taste, alcohol-free
- Less efficient extraction than alcohol (glycerin is less solvent)
- Heat-assisted extraction recommended (simmer at 160°F for 4-6 hours)
- Shorter shelf life (3-6 months, refrigerate)
- Good for children, elderly, and alcohol-averse users

### Oil Tinctures (MCT/Coconut)
- Cannabis-infused MCT or coconut oil
- Popular commercial format
- Combine decarboxylated cannabis with oil, heat gently (160°F for 2 hours), strain
- Sublingual use. Standardized dosing easier than alcohol tinctures

### Tincture Dosing Guide

| Effect Level | Alcohol Tincture | Oil Tincture | Onset (sublingual) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microdose | 0.25-0.5 ml | 0.25 ml | 15-30 min |
| Low | 0.5-1 ml | 0.5-1 ml | 15-30 min |
| Moderate | 1-2 ml | 1-2 ml | 15-45 min |
| High | 2-3 ml | 2-3 ml | 30-60 min |

## Edibles

### Cannabutter
- 1 cup butter + 1 cup ground decarboxylated flower (10-15% THC)
- Simmer on low heat (160-180°F) for 2-3 hours
- Strain through cheesecloth
- Approximately 800-1200mg THC per cup of butter (varies with flower potency)
- Use in any recipe calling for butter

### Canna-Oil
- Same process as cannabutter using coconut oil, [[staycare-fungal-treatment-of-olive-oil-mill-wastewater]], or any cooking oil
- Coconut oil preferred: high fat content, good cannabinoid absorption, mild flavor
- Works for vegan recipes

### Infused Honey
- Combine decarboxylated cannabis with honey
- Simmer at low heat (200°F) for 2 hours, stirring
- Strain. Approximately 5-10mg THC per teaspoon
- Add to tea, toast, recipes

### Gummies and Chocolates
- Use cannabis tincture or infused coconut oil as ingredient
- Precise dosing: divide total THC in infusion by number of pieces
- Commercially available in legal markets with standardized doses

### Cooking Tips
- Cannabis binds to fat — use recipes with butter, oil, cream, or coconut milk
- Don't exceed 350°F (175°C) cooking temperature (THC degrades)
- Distribute cannabis evenly in the recipe (stir well)
- Label clearly: "Contains cannabis. Keep away from children and pets"

## Edible Dosing Guide

### First-Time Users
- **Start with 2.5-5mg THC** (many legal markets standardize to 5mg per serving)
- Wait 2 hours before consuming more (oral onset is slow and sneaky)
- Common mistake: eating more after 45 min "because I don't feel anything" → overdose

| Experience Level | Dose (THC) | Effect | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 2.5-5 mg | Mild relaxation, mood elevation | 3-4 hours |
| Moderate | 5-10 mg | Noticeable psychoactive effects | 4-6 hours |
| Experienced | 10-20 mg | Strong psychoactive effects | 4-8 hours |
| Very experienced | 20-50 mg | Very strong, potentially overwhelming | 6-12 hours |

### CBD-Dominant Edibles
- No psychoactive effect (THC < 0.3%)
- Anti-inflammatory, anti-anxiety, pain relief
- 10-50mg CBD per dose typical
- No tolerance buildup or intoxication risk

## Bioavailability

- **Inhalation**: 10-35% bioavailability (fast, efficient)
- **Oral (edibles)**: 4-20% bioavailability (slow, less efficient, but longer duration)
- **Sublingual (tinctures)**: 12-35% bioavailability (moderate speed, moderate efficiency)
- First-pass metabolism in the liver converts THC to 11-hydroxy-THC (more psychoactive than THC itself — this is why edibles feel stronger and different)
