## The First Cataclysmic Change
Fukuoka places the pine rot epidemic in a global context:
- "It is only fitting that the first cataclysmic change to take place in the
  global microbial world occurred in a mycorrhizal fungus"
- Mycorrhizal fungi form "a highly advanced biological community in which many
  microorganisms are concentrated and organically interconnected"
- The red pine was able to become "the strongest plant only because it was

## Proposed Remedies
Fukuoka offered concrete suggestions for addressing the crisis:
- Raise the soil pH (found to be very acidic, about 3.2-4.8, in affected areas)
  to about 5 by administering lime or wood ash.
- Pour large quantities of soil fungicide in water, or spray in stands of red
  pine shortly before heavy rains.
- Once the fungicide has disappeared from the soil, inoculate large amounts of
  matsutake spores into the roots to stimulate new mycorrhiza growth.
- He called for "drastic and far-reaching measures" beyond just emergency
  treatment of the most seriously affected areas.
