### Temperature and Time

- Optimal decarboxylation occurs between 220-245°F (105-118°C)
- Below 220°F: Incomplete conversion, wasted material
- Above 245°F: Cannabinoid degradation and terpene loss
- Typical protocol: 30-40 minutes at 240°F in a preheated oven
- Cannabis should be ground to coarse consistency (not powder) and spread in an even
  layer on parchment paper

### Importance for Edibles

Unlike smoking, where the heat of combustion decarboxylates cannabinoids instantly,
edibles require pre-decarboxylation because baking temperatures and times may not be
sufficient for full conversion. Failing to decarboxylate results in weak or
ineffective edibles.

## Infusion Methods

### Cannabutter

The most traditional edible infusion method:

1. Decarboxylate [[cervantes-cannabis-flower-anatomy]]
2. Melt butter (clarified butter works best) in a saucepan
3. Add decarboxylated cannabis and simmer on low heat (160-200°F) for 2-3 hours
4. Strain through cheesecloth or fine mesh
5. Refrigerate; the cannabutter solidifies and separates from water

### Cannabis Oil (Coconut/Olive)

Similar to cannabutter but using oil as the carrier fat:

- Coconut oil is preferred for its high saturated fat content, which absorbs
  cannabinoids efficiently
