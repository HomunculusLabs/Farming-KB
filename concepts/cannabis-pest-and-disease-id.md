# Cannabis Pest and Disease Identification

Detailed identification guide for cannabis pests and diseases. Covers life cycles, damage patterns, scouting, thresholds, and treatment protocols. See [[cannabis-pest-management]] for IPM strategy and [[cannabis-indoor-living-soil]] for prevention through soil health.

## Scouting Schedule

| Stage | Frequency | Focus Areas |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Clone/seedling | Daily | Soil surface (gnats), leaf undersides (mites) |
| Vegetative | Every 2-3 days | New growth (aphids), leaf undersides, sticky traps |
| Early flower (wk 1-3) | Every 2-3 days | All pests; foliar sprays still safe |
| Mid-late flower (wk 4+) | Weekly | Bud interior (botrytis), leaf spots; no sprays after wk 4 |

**Tools**: 30x loupe, yellow/blue sticky traps, white paper (tap test for mites).

## Pests — Detailed Profiles

### Spider Mites (Tetranychus urticae)

**Lifecycle**: Egg -> larva -> nymph -> adult. 7-10 days at 27C; females lay 100+ eggs. Overwinter as adults.

**Damage**: Fine stippling (yellow dots) starting lower canopy. Silk webbing between branches. Leaves turn bronze, dry up. Can destroy crop in 2-3 days.

**ID**: Tiny (0.5mm) on leaf undersides; tap over white paper. Two dark body spots.

**Treatment**: (1) Isolate plant (2) Neem oil 1 tbsp/gal + soap, cover undersides (3) Release P. persimilis predatory mites (4) Increase humidity 60%+ (5) Repeat every 3-5 days for 2 weeks (eggs survive first spray) (6) If systemic: clean room protocol

### Russet Mites (Aculops cannabicola) & Broad Mites (P. latus)

**Lifecycle**: Both complete cycle in 5-14 days. Invisible to naked eye (0.2mm). Spread by wind, clothing, clones.

**Russet damage**: NO webbing. Starts at stem base, moves up. Leaves taco-curl, bronze/tan edges. Stems look rusty. Confused with nutrient burn.

**Broad damage**: New growth twisted, brittle, wet/shiny. Leaves curl down. Attacks growing tips and bud sites.

**ID**: 30x loupe required. Russet = wedge-shaped, translucent. Broad = eggs with white bumps.

**Treatment**: (1) Sulfur burner veg only (2) Neem oil + suffoil-X, wet stems thoroughly (3) Predatory mites: A. fallacis (russet), N. cucumeris/A. swirskii (broad) (4) Remove affected growth (5) Most difficult mites to eradicate — aggressive scouting

### Fungus Gnats (Bradysia spp.)

**Lifecycle**: Egg -> 4 larval instars -> pupa -> adult. 17-30 days total. Females lay 100-200 eggs in moist soil.

**Damage**: Larvae feed on roots. Seedlings stunt, yellow, wilt. Adults harmless — larvae cause damage.

**ID**: Tiny (3mm) black flies on soil. Larvae = white worms with black heads in top inch of soil.

**Treatment**: (1) Dry soil surface between waterings (most effective) (2) BTi drench (Mosquito Dunks) (3) Yellow sticky traps (4) Nematodes S. feltiae (5) Sand/DE top layer 1/4" (6) [[lactic-acid-bacteria]] drench

### Thrips (Frankliniella spp.)

**Lifecycle**: Egg -> 2 larval instars -> pupa -> adult. Females lay 50+ eggs in leaf tissue.

**Damage**: Silver-gray streaking ("silver damage"), black fecal dots, deformed new growth. Pollen consumption reduces bud set.

**ID**: 1-2mm elongated, yellow-brown. Jump when disturbed. Blue sticky traps.

**Treatment**: (1) Spinosad — very effective (2) Neem oil (3) Blue sticky traps (4) A. cucumeris predatory mites (5) Orius insidiosus for heavy infestations

### Aphids (Myzus persicae + others)

**Lifecycle**: Live birth in summer; 7-10 days to maturity. Asexual reproduction — populations explode.

**Damage**: Clusters on new growth/stems. Sticky honeydew, sooty mold. Curled leaves. Ants present (farming).

**ID**: Soft-bodied, pear-shaped, 1-3mm. Green/black/yellow. Visible to naked eye.

**Treatment**: (1) Insecticidal soap (2) Ladybugs 50-100/plant (3) Aphidius colemani wasps (4) Neem oil (5) Eliminate ants

### Whiteflies (Trialeurodes vaporariorum)

**Lifecycle**: Egg -> crawler -> 4 nymph instars (2-4 weeks) -> adult. Eggs on leaf undersides.

**Damage**: Yellowing, honeydew, sooty mold. Cloud of white when disturbed.

**ID**: Tiny white moths on leaf undersides. Yellow sticky traps.

**Treatment**: (1) Yellow sticky traps (2) Encarsia formosa parasitic wasp (3) Insecticidal soap (4) Neem oil

## Diseases

### Powdery Mildew

**ID**: White powdery spots on leaf surfaces. Cannot be easily wiped off (unlike dust). **Conditions**: 60-80F, 50-70% humidity, poor airflow.

**Treatment**: Potassium bicarbonate (Kaligreen), milk spray 1:9, neem oil, sulfur burner (veg only). Remove leaves, increase airflow, humidity <50%.

### Bud Rot (Botrytis cinerea)

**ID**: Brown mushy spots inside dense buds, gray webbing. Worst late flower. **Conditions**: Humidity >50%, dense buds, cool nights.

**Treatment**: No cure — remove and destroy affected buds. Preventive: humidity 40-50% late flower, defoliate lower canopy, OHN 1:1000 early flower.

### Root Rot (Pythium, Fusarium, Phytophthora)

**ID**: Wilting despite water, brown slimy roots, foul odor. **Conditions**: Overwatering, poor drainage.

**Treatment**: Improve drainage, H2O2 1:4 drench, LAB 1:500 soil drench, beneficial microbes (Trichoderma, Bacillus subtilis). Transplant in severe cases.

### Leaf Septoria

**ID**: Brown circular spots with yellow halos on lower leaves. Black dots (pycnidia) in centers. **Conditions**: Wet foliage, high humidity.

**Treatment**: Remove affected leaves, avoid wetting foliage, copper fungicide (early veg), potassium bicarbonate.

### Tobacco Mosaic Virus (TMV)

**ID**: Mosaic light/dark green mottling, curling, stunting. Not curable. Spread by tools, hands, aphids.

**Treatment**: Destroy plant. Sanitize tools (10% bleach). Wash hands. Don't reuse soil. Control aphids. Use TMV-resistant genetics.

## Action Thresholds

| Pest | Threshold | Escalation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Spider mites | Any webbing or 5+/leaf | Full IPM; consider clean room |
| Russet/broad mites | Any visible damage | Aggressive spray; consider removal |
| Fungus gnats | 5+ adults/trap/day | BTi + dry-out cycle |
| Thrips | 3-5/trap/week | Spinosad + blue traps |
| Aphids | Any colony on new growth | Ladybugs + soap |
| Whiteflies | Any on traps | Encarsia + traps |
| PM | Any white spots | Remove leaf + potassium bicarbonate |
| Bud rot | Any infected bud | Remove + reduce humidity |
