# Cannabis Seedling Care and Early Growth

The seedling stage is a critical period in cannabis
cultivation that lasts from three to six weeks after
germination. During this stage, the root system grows
rapidly while aboveground growth is slow. Proper care
during this vulnerable period establishes the foundation
for healthy, vigorous plants throughout the entire life
cycle.

See also: [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]

The seedling stage is over when rapid foliage growth
starts. At this point, plants need more room to grow
and should be transplanted into larger containers.

See also: [[cervantes-overwatering-underwatering]]

## Selecting and Thinning Seedlings

As seedlings mature, some will grow faster, stronger,
and appear healthier in general. Others will sprout
slowly and be weak and leggy. Cervantes advises culling
sickly, weak plants and focusing attention on the
remaining strong survivors.

Seedlings should be big enough to thin out by the third
to fifth week of growth. Thinning is difficult for
growers who pay premium prices for seeds, but selecting
only the strongest plants ensures better results.
