## Straw Pasteurization Methods for Mushroom Cultivation

Straw is one of the most accessible and versatile substrates for
mushroom cultivation. It is relatively inexpensive, readily
available in most agricultural regions, and serves as a forgiving
substrate for both home and commercial cultivators. Proper
pasteurization is essential to prepare straw for inoculation,
selectively eliminating competitor organisms while preserving
conditions favorable for mushroom mycelium.

## Straw as a Cultivation Substrate

Wheat, rice, oat, and sorghum straws are the best for mushroom
cultivation. Hay, resplendent with abundant seed kernels, should
not be used as the grain kernels tend to contaminate. However,
limited numbers of grain kernels generally boost yields. Straw is
a forgiving substrate for the small to mid-size cultivator,
accepting a limited number of contaminants and selectively favoring
mushroom mycelium.
