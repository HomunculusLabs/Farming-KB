# AACT Applications Guide (Ingham)

Updated: 2026-04-18

Source: The Field Guide I for Actively [[compost-tea-application-rates]], timing, and strategies for different situations.

## Application Goals (Priority Order)

1. **First goal**: [[bioremediation-fungal-biomass-biosorbent-material]] in the tea

### Application Rates
- Standard: 5 gallons of tea per acre
- Mildew alert: 10 gallons per acre
- Soil drench at planting: 15-20 gallons per acre
- Foliar during season: 3 applications at 5 gallons per acre

### Timing
- Start applications two weeks before bud break
- Usually see overnight improvement in plant color and [[extending-growing-season]] is maintenance level

### Ongoing Maintenance
- If any type of disturbance: every 7-10 days if major, can cut back for minimal disturbance
- Disturbances include: air pollution, pesticide drift, plowing, floods, freezes, drought, excessive heat, heavy traffic

### After Chemical Applications
- Apply AACT after any chemical application
- [[ingham-foliar-applications-compost-tea-disease-suppression]]
- Soil drench in spring before planting

## Soil Drench Considerations

- Wait until soil is warm enough for organisms to grow
- If tea is added straight to sand with no [[comparison-soil-food-web-vs-arbuscular-mycorrhizal-fungi]]) inoculation: mix tea and VAM into pancake-batter consistency, dip roots before planting
- Do NOT use gels for root dip — they smother roots and prevent air passage

## Foliar Feeding Mechanism

Plants control what nutrients they take up through leaves:
- If a plant doesn't need nutrients, it doesn't pump out sugars to feed the organisms in the tea — fewer nutrients get taken into the plant
- Plant controls what it needs
