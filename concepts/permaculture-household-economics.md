## Financial Capital in Descent

Financial capital is the most obvious form of non-material wealth in the modern world. The rapid movement of capital and its focus in destructive short-term investment has been recognized as the most dysfunctional element of modern capitalism.

Ethical investment is one of the most rapidly growing areas of financial services. Returns on investment have generally been as good as or better than market averages.

Much of the value of financial capital may be illusory because it has become disconnected from sources of real wealth. Financial crashes have been increasing since the 1980s and more may come before financial wealth realistically represents potential material wealth.

The [[permaculture-community-design]] approach recognizes that cultural wealth, social networks, and local knowledge are forms of energy storage that become increasingly valuable during energy descent.
