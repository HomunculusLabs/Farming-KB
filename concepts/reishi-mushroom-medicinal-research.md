## Ganoderma lucidum (Reishi / Lingzhi) — Medicinal Research Summary

Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum) is the most revered medicinal mushroom in Asian herbal medicine, known as "Lingzhi" in Chinese ("spiritual mushroom") and "Mannentake" in Japanese ("10,000-year mushroom"). It has been used medicinally for over 2,000 years in China, Korea, and Japan.

### Active Compounds

**Polysaccharides** (primarily from fruiting body and mycelium):
- Beta-1,3/1,6-glucans — immunomodulatory, anti-tumor
- Heteropolysaccharides with various sugar compositions

**Triterpenoids** (unique to Ganoderma, concentrated in the bitter-tasting spores and fruiting body):
- **Ganoderic acids** (over 150 identified): A, B, C, D, E, F, and many derivatives
- **Lucidenic acids**: structurally related triterpenoids
- **Ganoderiol**: additional triterpene class
- Responsible for the characteristic bitter taste

**Other**: nucleotides, sterols, fatty acids, proteins, peptides, amino acids

### Pharmacological Activities

**Immunomodulation**:
- Enhances NK cell activity, macrophage function, and dendritic cell maturation
- Modulates cytokine production: increases IL-2, interferon-gamma; can reduce excessive IL-6 and TNF-alpha (anti-inflammatory effect)
- Dual-directional immune regulation: stimulates immune response in immunocompromised states while calming excessive immune activity in autoimmune conditions

**Anti-tumor**:
- Polysaccharides activate immune-mediated tumor cell killing
- Triterpenoids (especially ganoderic acids) show direct cytotoxic activity against cancer cells in vitro
- Inhibits tumor angiogenesis
- Induces apoptosis in cancer cells through multiple pathways
- Enhances efficacy of conventional chemotherapy and reduces side effects

**Cardiovascular**:
- Reduces blood pressure and cholesterol
- Inhibits platelet aggregation (anti-thrombotic)
- Improves blood flow and oxygen utilization

**Anti-inflammatory**:
- Triterpenoids inhibit NF-kappaB signaling pathway
- Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines
- Demonstrates hepatoprotective activity (protects liver)

**Anti-oxidant**:
- Scavenges free radicals, reduces oxidative stress markers
- Protects against radiation-induced damage

### Clinical Evidence

- **Cancer adjunct**: Multiple studies show improved quality of life, reduced chemotherapy side effects (fatigue, nausea, immune suppression), and potential survival benefit when used as an adjuvant
- **Immunomodulation**: Enhanced immune parameters in immunocompromised patients
- **Fatigue and quality of life**: Consistent improvements in cancer-related fatigue and general well-being

### Traditional Use

Classified in TCM as a superior herb that nourishes the spirit (shen), calms the mind, and supports longevity. Traditionally used for: fatigue, weakness, insomnia, cough, asthma, and as a general tonic for vitality. The rare wild specimens were so prized they were reserved for emperors.

### Preparation and Dosage

- Dried mushroom powder: 2-9 g/day
- Hot water extract (decoction): most traditional method, extracts polysaccharides
- Dual extraction (water + alcohol): captures both polysaccharides (water-soluble) and triterpenoids (alcohol-soluble)
- Spore oil: concentrated source of triterpenoids, used in modern preparations

The bitterness is proportional to triterpenoid content and is considered a quality marker.
