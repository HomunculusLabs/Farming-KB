## Humic and Fulvic Acids

Humic substances are the most chemically complex and biologically active components of decomposed organic matter. In AACT brewing, humic acids serve as both fungal food and a chelating agent that improves [[query-what-is-fish-hydrolysate-and-how-do-i-use-it-as-fertilizer]] fish emulsion.

### Fish Hydrolysate vs. Fish Emulsion
- **Fish hydrolysate**: Cold-processed (enzymatically digested) fish that retains proteins, oils, vitamins, and amino acids. This is the preferred form for AACT because the complex protein molecules serve as excellent fungal food.
- **Fish emulsion**: Heat-processed fish that has been partially rendered. The heat processing breaks down proteins into simpler compounds, making it more of a bacterial food. Some emulsions contain sulfuric acid as a preservative, which can harm beneficial biology.
