## Cannabis and Pancreatitis Research

**Source:** [[cannabis-health-index]] (2017)

## Condition Overview

Pancreatitis is inflammation of the pancreas occurring when digestive enzymes are activated while still in the organ, causing cell breakdown. The pancreas is both an endocrine gland (insulin, glucagon, somatostatin) and a digestive organ. A damaged pancreas leads to digestive difficulties and diabetes.

**Primary causes:** alcoholism and gallbladder stones. Other causes: toxic pharmaceuticals (AIDS medications), abdominal surgeries, high mineral/fat concentrations, parathyroid hormones.

**Symptoms:** upper abdominal pain. Diagnosis: blood tests for pancreatic enzymes, stool samples, MRI, CT scans, ultrasound. Treatment: IV fluids, medications, surgery, dietary restrictions (no alcohol, smoking, or fat).
