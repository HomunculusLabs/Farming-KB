## The Principle of Economy in Nature

Nature operates on a principle of maximum economy. A tree does not grow
unnecessary branches. Soil organisms do not waste energy. Natural
ecosystems cycle nutrients with minimal loss. Human agriculture, by
contrast, operates on a principle of excess — adding more inputs than
the system can process, creating waste and pollution.
