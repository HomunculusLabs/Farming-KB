## When to Harvest

Mushrooms are ready to be picked 2 to 3 days after they first appear on the substrate.
Timing is critical:

- If harvested too small, they cannot fetch a good price
- If harvested too big, their conservation period is reduced and they are not as sweet and
  tasty
- Harvesting should be done two or three times per day

The manual recommends harvesting after watering, waiting at least 2 to 3 hours before picking.
This allows surface moisture to evaporate, which conserves mushrooms longer.

## Harvesting Technique

1. Look for mature mushrooms across all bags in the fruiting house

2. Pick mushrooms gently by grasping the stalk and pulling out slowly. Do not use a knife
   or cutting tool; twisting and pulling by hand is the preferred method

3. Place harvested mushrooms in order in a basket, approximately 5 to 8 kg per basket

4. Trim and peel by cutting at the base of the stalk to make the [[oyster-mushrooms]] are:
- 1st flush at 5 weeks after inoculation
- 2nd flush at 8 weeks
- 3rd flush at 11 weeks
- 4th flush at 14 weeks
- 5th flush at 17 weeks

Each subsequent flush typically produces fewer mushrooms than the previous one. After 4 to 5
flushes, bags are generally spent and should be removed from production. [[falconer-mushroom-preserving-and-drying]] techniques, which add value and extend shelf life significantly.
