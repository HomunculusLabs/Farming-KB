## Spent Hops

The residue from the brewing industry, spent hops have a distinctive
strong smell that soon disappears when left in the open. They make an
excellent soil conditioner and are particularly good for mulching. The
challenge is availability, as many breweries now sell on contract to
farmers.

Bought directly from the brewery, spent hops will be wet and can be
dug in fresh or spread as a surface mulch. Keep fresh hops away from
stems and leaves of young plants to avoid scorching. Dry spent hops
are more of a nitrogen-rich fertilizer at about 2.5 to 3.5 percent
nitrogen.

Coverage: 2 to 3 lbs (1 to 1.5kg) per square yard/meter.
