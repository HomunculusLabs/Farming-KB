# Psilocin and Psilocybin Chemical Synthesis

## Overview

### Step 1: Protection — 4-Acetylindole
4-Hydroxyindole is protected by acetylation with acetic anhydride in
pyridine and dichloromethane at 0°C to room temperature. This yields
4-acetylindole as ivory white crystals in high yield. [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-formation-oxalyl-chloride-indole]] in diethyl ether at 0°C,
followed by addition of n-hexane and storage at -20°C. This produces the
glyoxalyl chloride intermediate as yellow crystals, isolated by filtration.

### Step 3: Amidation — Glyoxalylamide
The glyoxalyl chloride is dissolved in tetrahydrofuran and treated with
dimethylamine solution and pyridine. This two-step acylation-amidation
conversion yields 3-dimethylaminooxalyl-4-acetylindole (the glyoxalylamide)
in over 80% yield as ivory white crystalline powder.

### Step 4: Reduction — Psilocin
The glyoxalylamide is reduced by lithium aluminum hydride (LiAlH4) in
anhydrous tetrahydrofuran under argon atmosphere. After reflux for 2 hours
and careful workup with sodium sulfate, psilocin is obtained in over 85%
yield as white crystals. The reduction simultaneously cleaves the acetyl
protecting group and reduces the oxalylamide to the ethylamine side chain.

Psilocin characterization: mp 169–174°C (dec.), UV λmax 222.5, 268.0,
284.5, 294.0 nm; the compound is moderately unstable and decomposes readily.

### Step 5: Phosphorylation — The Zwitterionic Intermediate
The key innovation of the Shirota synthesis. Psilocin is phosphorylated
using tetrabenzylpyrophosphate and n-butyllithium in THF at -78°C to 0°C.
The initially formed O,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative undergoes
intramolecular rearrangement: hydrolytic cleavage of one O-benzyl group
occurs, and the resulting benzyl group migrates to the nitrogen, forming
a zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative.

This zwitterionic intermediate was fully characterized by 2D NMR (HMBC
and NOESY experiments), which confirmed that one benzyl group is directly
linked to the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl moiety as a quaternary ammonium
ion, while the other remains on the phosphate oxygen.

The zwitterionic nature of this compound enables isolation by simple
filtration in over 85% yield, since the excess dibenzyl phosphate is
removed by washing with dichloromethane. This eliminates the need for
chromatographic purification.

### Step 6: Hydrogenolysis — Psilocybin
The zwitterionic intermediate undergoes [[shirota-catalytic-hydrogenolysis-debenzylation-psilocybin]] with
palladium on carbon in methanol at room temperature. This simultaneously
removes both benzyl groups, yielding psilocybin as a white needle
crystalline powder in 87.5% yield from the intermediate (72% overall
from psilocin).
