# Animal Housing (Detailed)
## Overview

## Pig Systems
### Pig-Forage Integration
Mollison describes pig systems integrated with forage crops and tree crops:
- Pigs can be rotationally grazed on fallow land or under fruit trees
- They root and till soil, useful for site preparation before planting
- Fence pigs into sections using electric fencing; rotate every 2-4 weeks
- Provide wallows and shade in hot climates; shelter from wind and cold
### Pig Housing Design
- Strong post-and-rail or electric fencing; pigs test and destroy weak
  barriers
- Shelter needs: windbreak, shade, dry sleeping area, wallow access
- Feed troughs designed to prevent rooting and waste of concentrated feed
- Manure collected from concrete or slatted floor areas for composting
## Ruminant Housing
### Dairy Goats and Sheep
- Milking shed positioned in Zone 2 for twice-daily access
- Fodder trees (Leucaena, tagasaste, mulberry) planted along fence lines
- Night housing with manure collection for compost or biogas
- Mineral blocks and clean water always available
- kidding/judging pens separate from main flock area
### Cattle
- [[carbon-dioxide-retention-jar-lid-mushroom-cultivation]] from respiration benefits plant growth
- Manure falls through slatted floor into compost-active zone below
- The greenhouse provides winter shelter; chickens provide heat and CO2
- Water from roof catchment supplies both poultry and irrigation needs
