### Wood Chips with Castings

When worm castings are combined with wood chips nitrified with urea, the active
organisms in the castings rapidly immobilize nitrogen from the urea into
biomass, preventing leaching. This creates an effective combination where the
castings provide the biological workforce and the urea-wood chip mix provides
substrate. Regular tea applications every 7 days can maintain active
decomposition.
