## Neem Oil and Neem Products for Cannabis

Neem has been used for medicine and pest control for more than four centuries in India and [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]], each with distinct properties:

### Neem Powder

Neem powder is made from dried neem leaves. It contains the active ingredient azadirachtin along with N-P-K nutrients and trace elements. The powder is most effective against young insects and is available in various concentrations. Neem powder works by confusing insect growth hormones, preventing pests from maturing into adults and reproducing.

### Neem Oil

Neem oil is a purified extract from neem seeds. Cold-pressed neem oil is strongly recommended over heat-processed oil because it retains all natural ingredients, including azadirachtin. Brand names include Neemguard, Triact, and Einstein Oil (reported as the most effective brand tested). Neem oil is more effective against spider mites than neem powder alone.

## Active Ingredient: Azadirachtin

Azadirachtin is the primary active compound in neem products. Its mechanism of action:

- Confuses insect growth hormones, preventing larvae from maturing into reproducing adults
- Acts as an antifeedant -- pests stop eating treated foliage
- Disrupts the insect life cycle at multiple stages
- Is most effective against immature insects (larvae, nymphs)

Because azadirachtin works on growth regulation rather than as a direct neurotoxin, it has low toxicity to non-target organisms and is slower-acting than synthetic pesticides.

## Application Frequency and Persistence

- **Contact spray persistence**: Stays on foliage for up to one month, or until washed off by watering or rain
- **Systemic persistence**: Remains in the plant system for up to one month when absorbed via roots
- **Foliar spray frequency**: Every few days for active infestations, to catch hatching larvae
- **Avoid spraying** the last few days before harvest -- some growers report a foul taste when neem is applied just before harvest

## Toxicity and Safety

### Environmental Toxicity

- Not toxic to honeybees, fish, or earthworms (neem powder)
- Not toxic to [[bud-rot-cannabis]].
