### Commercial Compost Method

Commercial compost uses more carefully calibrated ingredients with nitrogen
content between 1.5 and 1.7 percent of total dry weight. Sample formulas
include:

- 49% shredded wheat straw, 48% chicken manure, 3% gypsum
- 96% horse manure, 2.5% gypsum, 1.5% cottonseed meal

Gypsum serves as a calcium source and helps coat particles for aeration. The
process involves wetting and turning daily for five to seven days, then every
other day. Target a consistent internal temperature around 150 degrees F (66
degrees C). After heating, the compost enters the "caramelization" phase,
becoming uniformly brown with a strong ammonia smell.

### Municipal Compost

Municipal compost can be suitable but should be heat-treated to eliminate human
pathogens. Yields may be inconsistent since you cannot control the input
materials.

## Using the Whole Tree

Cotter advocates using the entire tree from leaf to stump in outdoor mushroom
cultivation operations:

1. Wood chip bed: blewit, king stropharia
2. Log trench cultivation: reishi, nameko
3. Log cabin stacks: shiitake
4. Buried rounds: maitake
5. Planted totems: lion's mane
6. Cardboard stacks: nameko, black poplar, oyster
7. Stumps: maitake, reishi, oyster, chicken of the woods
