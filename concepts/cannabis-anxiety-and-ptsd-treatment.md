# Cannabis Anxiety and PTSD Treatment

## Overview

Anxiety disorders and post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) represent significant areas of cannabinoid therapeutic research documented in the [[cannabis-health-index]]. Blesching presents anxiety not merely as a psychological condition but as a state deeply connected to the body's neurochemistry, with the [[blesching-cannabis-epinephrine-norepinephrine-endocannabinoid-system]] (ECS) playing a modulatory role in fear, [[lichen-poikilohydry-and-drought-stress-responses]], and emotional regulation.

## The Neurochemistry of Anxiety

Multiple neurotransmitter systems influence anxiety states:

- **GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid)**: Low levels of GABA are related to irritability, anxiety, panic attacks, lack of empathy, aggression, and attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. The dual capacity of the endocannabinoid system to simultaneously balance GABA and glutamate is one of its most unique and therapeutically promising features.
- **Cortisol**: Elevated cortisol ("I am stressed") is associated with chronic anxiety and can suppress immune function. High cortisol levels are linked to hypertension, cardiovascular disease, and immune suppression.
- **Glutamate**: Excessive glutamate can cause inflammation and neural damage, contributing to anxiety states. The ECS modulates glutamate release.
- **Anandamide**: The endogenous cannabinoid may reduce hypertension, depression, fear, and anxiety. Its presence is associated with the feeling "I am at ease." Anandamide inhibits norepinephrine, which is associated with heightened arousal and anxiety.
- **Oxytocin**: Released by hugs and social bonding, oxytocin reduces anxiety and fear and enhances feelings of trust and bonding. The endocannabinoid system is involved in the production and release of oxytocin.
- **Acetylcholine**: While important for learning and memory, too much acetylcholine may contribute to anxiety, restlessness, or heightened levels of fear.
- **Vasopressin**: Low levels may contribute to social anxiety disorders, autism, low libido, eating disorders, and other conditions.

## Cannabis and Anxiety: A Dose-Dependent Relationship

The relationship between cannabis and anxiety is nuanced and dose-dependent. While cannabis has documented anti-anxiety properties, excessive dosing can paradoxically increase anxiety. Blesching notes that the feeling of apprehension and anxiety may actually multiply if cannabis is consumed in excess of the user's top threshold. Key considerations include:

- Understanding the body's present endocannabinoid state (relative balance or deficiency)
- Patients with mental or emotional problems are most likely lacking CB1 activation
- Those who are immune-compromised or experience chronic inflammation are most likely CB2-deficient
- Cannabis has been used in various healing practices to reduce anxiety and return to a calmer state
- In various healing traditions, cannabis is used to reduce anxiety and return to a state of equanimity

The sativa vs. indica distinction is relevant: indicas (higher CBD to THC ratio) are generally recommended for anxiety, as CBD tames the psychoactive influence of THC, allowing patients to benefit from THC's therapeutic powers at higher concentrations without anxiety-provoking effects.

## PTSD and the Endocannabinoid System

PTSD represents a condition where the endocannabinoid system's role in emotional modulation becomes particularly relevant. The [[cannabis-health-index]] documents several key connections:

- Exposure to abuse resulting in PTSD was identified as a significant co-factor (13.7%) in developing anorexia nervosa in a study of 753 female patients
- Epigenetic research demonstrates that horrific emotions experienced during traumatic events produce physiological changes that can be passed to the [[next-generation-sequencing-hidden-fungal-diversity]], leaving offspring more vulnerable to developing anxiety disorders such as phobia or PTSD
- Vasopressin, a molecule associated with aggression, has been linked to PTSD; patients who suffered from PTSD showed difficulties with emotional connections modulated by vasopressin
- The endocannabinoid-induced modulation of stress-related disorders positions the ECS as a therapeutic target for PTSD treatment
- Insomnia caused by anxiety or use of addictive substances may respond to cannabinoid therapy
- Consider with PTSD a potential similar approach to other fear-based belief patterns or inappropriate trust responses

Synthetic cannabinoid pharmaceuticals including [[nabilone]] are approved by the FDA for treating conditions including PTSD. Studies on cancer patients receiving Nabilone showed improvements in anxiety, depression, insomnia, and [[blesching-cannabis-cancer-night-sweats]] alongside pain reduction.

## The Biology of Fear and Emotional Processing

Blesching frames fear as one of the "constricting emotions" (alongside anxiety, anger, and their variations) that contrast with "expansive emotions" such as bliss, relaxation, and social connection. The ECS serves as a bridge between body and mind in this emotional landscape:

- Changes in frame of mind (e.g., fear vs. relaxation) can similarly affect the nervous and immune systems
- Anandamide enhances pleasure and may mitigate episodes of acute schizophrenia while reducing fear and anxiety
- CBD's ability to suppress the enzyme FAAH that breaks down anandamide keeps "the Bliss Molecule" active at higher concentrations for longer duration
- Consider CB1 activating cannabinoids for calming effects
- Oxytocin released by hugs reduces anxiety and fear, and the endocannabinoid system modulates oxytocin levels

## Clinical Applications

### Anxiety Treatment with Cannabis
- Start with low doses of indica or indica-dominant strains
- CBD-rich products offer anti-anxiety benefits without psychoactivity
- The plant's anti-anxiety properties can ease tension and stress associated with various conditions
- Patients have found benefit for social anxiety, performance anxiety, and generalized anxiety
- Some people who feel anxious before lovemaking have discovered that the plant's anti-anxiety properties can ease the tension and stress often associated with performance problems
- CBD reduced all stress responses including anxiety, high blood pressure, and rapid heart rate in a 2009 University of Sao Paulo animal study

### PTSD Treatment Considerations
- FDA-approved synthetic cannabinoids (Nabilone) are prescribed for PTSD
- Both CB1 and CB2 activation may be relevant to addressing PTSD's multi-system impact
- Integration with psychotherapy and trauma-processing work is recommended
- Mind-body medicine approaches complement cannabinoid therapy
- Cannabinoid therapy may help modulate the memory consolidation and extinction processes relevant to traumatic memories

## Mind-Body Integration for Anxiety and PTSD

Blesching emphasizes that cannabinoid therapy works best when combined with conscious intervention:

- **Mindfulness-based stress reduction** (MBSR) developed by Jon Kabat-Zinn has been extensively studied for positive impact on anxiety, depression, and fibromyalgia
- **Vipassana meditation** practices are employed to contemplate the constructs of the mind (sensation, thoughts, and feelings) and how they contribute to health or illness
- **Samatha (Tranquility) meditation** develops single-point concentration as a way to calm the mind
- **Addressing underlying emotions**: Anxiety may be related to guilt, shame, fear of emotions, or past traumatic events
- **Epigenetic awareness**: Understanding that environmental signals including emotions can switch genes on or off, and that these switches can be inherited
- **Emotional release work**: Anger-coping responses, both suppressive and expressive, influence anxiety levels and cardiovascular health

## Anxiety as a Co-Factor in Other Conditions

The [[cannabis-health-index]] identifies anxiety as an aggravating factor in numerous conditions:

- **Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)**: Introduction of anxiety increases acid-induced esophageal hyperalgesia; anxiety intervention is recommended
- **Hypertension**: Anxiety and depression confirmed as predictive signs for future development of hypertension
- **Insomnia**: Insomnia may produce symptoms of anxiety, creating a reinforcing cycle
- **Fibromyalgia**: Fibromyalgia comorbidities include stress-related disorders such as PTSD, [[blesching-cannabis-irritable-bowel-syndrome]], and depression
- **Eating disorders**: PTSD was identified as a significant co-factor (13.7%) in anorexia nervosa development in a 753-patient study

## The Constricting vs. Expansive Emotions Framework

Blesching presents anxiety as part of a broader emotional taxonomy relevant to treatment:

**Constricting emotions** (associated with sympathetic nervous system activation):
- Fear, anxiety, worry, panic, apprehension
- Anger, rage, hostility, frustration
- Guilt, shame, self-judgment
- Depression, grief, hopelessness

**Expansive emotions** (associated with parasympathetic nervous system activation):
- Bliss, relaxation, contentment, peace
- Love, compassion, empathy, trust
- Joy, gratitude, enthusiasm
- Curiosity, openness, wonder

The ECS modulates the transition between these emotional states. Anandamide (the "Bliss Molecule") is particularly associated with expansive emotional states, while its deficiency may predispose toward constricting emotional patterns. This framework has practical implications: cannabinoid therapy combined with practices that cultivate expansive emotions (meditation, social bonding, meaningful activity) may produce more sustained relief than pharmacological intervention alone.

## GABA-Glutamate Balance and the ECS

The ECS's dual capacity to simultaneously balance GABA (inhibitory) and glutamate (excitatory) is one of its most therapeutically promising features for anxiety:

- Low GABA is related to anxiety, panic attacks, and aggression
- Excessive glutamate contributes to neural excitation, inflammation, and anxiety
- CB1 receptor activation modulates the release of both neurotransmitters
- This dual modulation offers a more balanced approserotoninbenzodiazepines, which primarily target GABA
- CBD has demonstrated anxiolytic effects without the sedation and dependence risks associated with benzodiazepines

## Serotonin and Dopamine Interactions

The endocannabinoid system interacts with serotonin and dopamine pathways relevant to anxiety:

- Serotonin regulates mood, sleep, and anxiety; too little contributes to depression and anxiety
- Dopamine is associated with reward and pleasure; too much may contribute to anxiety and paranoia
- Anandamide influences the release of both serotonin and dopamine
- The interplay between endocannabinoid, serotonergic, and dopaminergic systems provides multiple therapeutic entry points for anxiety treatment

## Practical Dosing Guidelines

1. **Start low**: Begin with the smallest effective dose, particularly for anxiety-prone individuals
2. **Indica preference**: Choose indica or indica-dominant strains with higher CBD ratios
3. **CBD-first approach**: Consider CBD-only or CBD-dominant products before introducing THC
4. **Wait and observe**: Allow adequate time (60-90 minutes for oral) before increasing dose
5. **Mind the biphasic response**: Both THC and CBD can have biphasic dose-response effects
