# Cannabis-Infused Honey and Hempseed Oil Preparations

## Cannabis-Infused Honey

### Historical and Modern Use

Modern medicine has rediscovered honey's ancient use in the care of infected
wounds. Applied to slow-healing skin wounds such as ulcerations, burns, or
infected wounds, honey provides numerous therapeutic benefits.

The Cuban Ministry of Health widely recommends honey as a skin-protective
agent, as an antimicrobial for skin infections where it is prepared as a
topical cream, as an alcohol tincture, and as oral drops.

### Cannabis-Infused Hempseed Oil Topical

Some patients have combined the skin-healing and anti-inflammatory properties
of hempseed oil by infusing it with cannabinoid-rich cannabis to create a
topical ointment.

**Why the skin is an ideal delivery route:**
- The skin is the largest organ of the body
- Provides gentle delivery of biologically active substances
- Allows localized treatment of specific problem areas
- Systemic absorption occurs but tends to be less intense than oral
  or inhaled routes

## Preparation Considerations

For cannabis-infused honey:
- Use raw, unfiltered honey for maximum therapeutic benefit
- Gently warm honey (not above 110°F/43°C) to preserve enzymes
- Add decarboxylated cannabis or cannabis extract
- Store in a cool, dark place
