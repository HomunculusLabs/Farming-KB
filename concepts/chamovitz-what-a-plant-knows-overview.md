The Darwins" five-condition experiment with canary grass (*Phalaris canariensis*) demonstrated that:

- The plant tip detects light direction
- The tip communicates this information to the bending region
- A lightproof cap on the tip eliminates phototropism
- A clear glass cap on the tip preserves it
- Covering only the middle section has no effect

Julius von Sachs (1864) had previously established that blue light is the primary color inducing phototropism.

## Chapter 3: What a Plant Feels

Covers mechanoperception in plants:

- Thigmomorphogenesis — growth changes in response to mechanical stimulation
