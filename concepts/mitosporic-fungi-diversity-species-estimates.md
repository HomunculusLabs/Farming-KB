## Mitosporic Fungi Diversity and Species Estimates
Mitosporic fungi, also known as Fungi Imperfecti or
Deuteromycota, are fungi that reproduce only asexually
through the production of conidia or other mitospores,
without a known or observed sexual (meiotic) stage. This
artificial grouping includes many of the most commonly
encountered fungi in both natural and human-associated
environments, encompassing the hyphomycetes and
coelomycetes. Although the dual nomenclature system that
once separated mitosporic and meiosporic (sexual) states
has been largely replaced by a unified system based on
molecular phylogeny, the mitosporic forms remain the
primary basis for identification and enumeration of fungi
