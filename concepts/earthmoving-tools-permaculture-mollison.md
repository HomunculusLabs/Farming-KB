## Finishing Tools
Rakes and rollers are the tools for finishing excavated earth.
Compaction is only necessary where traffic or water retention is
intended. Care must be taken to compact only 15-30cm (6-12
inches) of soil thoroughly at a time. Even very heavy machines
can seldom compact depths greater than 38cm (15 inches).
