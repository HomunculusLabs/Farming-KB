# 5-MeO-DMT Void Experience

D.M. Turner's treatment of 5-Methoxy-[[dmt]] in "The [[turner-essential-psychedelics-dmt-hyperspace]] Guide"
emphasizes its raw power and the uniquely overwhelming quality of the void
experience it produces. He considers 5-MeO-DMT the more potent of the two
common DMT varieties in terms of dose required, onset speed, and force of
consciousness dissolution.

## Dosage and Physical Effects

The dosage for 5-MeO-DMT is 5 to 10 mg smoked, a remarkably small amount.
Turner notes that 5-MeO-DMT is an MAO inhibitor, requiring dietary precautions.
It increases pulse rate and blood pressure more than N,N-DMT (though less than
amyl nitrite), and can produce breathing irregularities that may take some
getting used to. A sub-threshold dose produces only bodily symptoms and a slight
alteration of consciousness, especially in the visual field.

## The Mind-Expanding Void

The core 5-MeO-DMT experience is one of explosive [[mckenna-consciousness-expansion-and-drug-war]]:

"On 5-MeO I can literally feel my mind exploding and expanding outward to
encompass first the area near me, then the planet, and eventually the cosmos.
This takes place over a span of 15 seconds or so."

Where N,N-DMT enchants and delights, 5-MeO-DMT awes or frightens with its
incredible power. The user's universe is blown apart. Turner describes 5-MeO as
"sheer force" compared to N,N-DMT's "sheer perfection."

## Visual Characteristics

5-MeO-DMT does not produce as spectacular visuals as N,N-DMT. Most frequently,
Turner's 5-MeO visuals are intensely complex, multidimensional, and
quickly-moving geometric designs -- primarily in black and white, checkerboard
style. At times there is some color, and on occasion the visuals become
abundantly colorful, especially with large doses. Turner found taking a hit of
5-MeO just before orgasm to be most enjoyable, often making the visuals
abundantly colorful. However, getting the timing right on this can take some
practice.

By contrast, N,N-DMT comes on a bit slower, less forcefully, enchanting the
smoker with its magical nature. Where 5-MeO obliterates with force, N,N-DMT
delights with hypnotic enchantment. The 5-MeO experience can leave the user
feeling the universe is too small to contain their expanded mind, while
N,N-DMT never produces this boundary anxiety.

## Void and Ego Dissolution

The 5-MeO-DMT experience is characterized by a total dissolution of the sense
of self and universe boundaries. Unlike N,N-DMT, which tends to leave a portion
of the ego intact while granting access to realms of discarnate entities, the
5-MeO experience obliterates self-concept entirely. The physically manifesting
energies -- vibrations felt through the body -- are gentler on N,N-DMT than on
5-MeO-DMT. Turner notes that on 5-MeO one can literally feel the mind exploding
and expanding outward to encompass first the area nearby, then the planet, and
eventually the cosmos, all within about 15 seconds.

On DMT, the perception of time slows to nearly a complete stop. During this
stage one may feel that they are not breathing, or that their heart has stopped.
However, this is due to one's state of perception rather than actual physical
changes. Many users report being aware of millions of vibrations and sounds
going through the universe and their bodies. One can let these feelings flow
through them, or if appropriate, direct the movements to some type of dance,
yogic exercise, or drumming.

## Duration

On 5-MeO-DMT the user returns to normal consciousness within about 20 minutes,
compared to 30 minutes for N,N-DMT. The peak itself lasts only 2-3 minutes,
though this can be extended to 10-20 minutes (total experience 40 minutes to
two hours) when combined with [[turner-harmala-alkaloids-preparation-and-dosage]].

## Combination with Harmala Alkaloids

The combination of 5-MeO-DMT and [[harmala-alkaloids]] produces what Turner calls
"the most powerful experience I have ever felt." However, this tends to be both
physically and mentally overwhelming. He reports becoming violently nauseous,
experiencing extreme discomfort, and witnessing others go through sheer misery.
One friend who ingested 5-MeO plus Harmala felt he had entered an ill-intentioned
pact with a spirit, went into convulsions, and bit the inside of his mouth.
