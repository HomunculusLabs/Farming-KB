## Dosage and Physical Effects

The dosage for 5-MeO-DMT is 5 to 10 mg smoked, a remarkably small amount.
Turner notes that 5-MeO-DMT is an MAO inhibitor, requiring dietary precautions.
It increases pulse rate and blood pressure more than N,N-DMT (though less than
amyl nitrite), and can produce breathing irregularities that may take some
getting used to. A sub-threshold dose produces only bodily symptoms and a slight
alteration of consciousness, especially in the visual field.
