## Greenhouse Suppliers and Winter Harvest Varieties

Successful winter harvest farming requires access to specialized tools, structures, and seed varieties that may not be available through standard garden center channels. Eliot Coleman provides a comprehensive reference of trusted suppliers and proven varieties based on decades of winter growing experience in coastal Maine.

## Row Cover Fabrics

Row covers are a critical component of the multi-layer protection system used in winter harvesting. They provide lightweight, breathable insulation that transmits light while trapping heat.

### Agribon Row Covers
- **Supplier**: Johnny"s Selected Seeds (955 Benton Avenue, Winslow, ME 04901; 877-564-6697; johnnyseeds.com)
- **Uses**: Inner layer protection within greenhouses and covering for quick hoops
- **Notes**: Available in various weights (AG-15, AG-19, AG-30, AG-50, AG-70); lighter weights offer better light transmission for heavier frost protection
- Coleman's farm uses Agribon for both greenhouse inner layers and field quick-hoop covering

### Covertan Pro 17
- **Supplier**: Suntex CP, Inc. (PO Box 21633, Sarasota, FL 34276; 888-786-8391; suntexcp.com)
- **Notes**: An alternative fabric that Coleman's farm has experience with; the "17" refers to grams per square meter

## Mobile Greenhouse Systems

Mobile greenhouses — structures that can be moved between growing areas — are central to Coleman"s [[winter-harvest-system]]. They allow a single structure to serve multiple plots, providing protection when needed and exposure (or cover cropping) when not.

### Rolling Thunder (Rimol Greenhouse Systems)
- **Supplier**: Rimol Greenhouse Systems, Northpoint Industrial Park, 40 Londonderry Turnpike, Hooksett, NH 03106; 603-629-9004; rimolgreenhouses.com
- **Design**: Heavy-duty wheels with bearings at each hoop set, attached to specially designed "base posts" seated on a rail system
- **Sizes**: Available in sizes up to 30×96 feet
- **Movement**: Larger houses movable with an ordinary tractor; smaller houses manageable by two people
- **Construction notes**: The rail is laid flat and does not require pinning or staking, but should be set on a solid base. The first few hoops are the most challenging to erect, requiring several sets of hands. Diagonal bracing provides wind stability. Various height options are available through different-length base posts.
- **Temporary bracing** is used on end walls during construction for stability

### Four Season Tools Mobile Greenhouse
- **Supplier**: Four Season Tools, 602 Westport Road, Kansas City, MO 64111; 816-444-7330; info@fourseasontools.com; fourseasontools.com
- **Design features**: Drop-down side technology on both sides and a full-length roof opening for ventilation
- **Models**: Available in wheeled (two options) or sled configurations
- **Conversion kits**: Available to convert existing stationary greenhouses to mobile
- **Additional**: Also designs efficient tools for small farms

## Ground Anchors

Securing mobile greenhouses and high tunnels against wind requires robust ground anchors.

- **Supplier**: A.M. Leonard (PO Box 816, Piqua, OH 45356; 800-543-8955; amleo.com)
- **Specification**: 45-inch-long, 4,000-pound-rated ground anchors
- Coleman"s farm uses these anchors to secure mobile greenhouses
