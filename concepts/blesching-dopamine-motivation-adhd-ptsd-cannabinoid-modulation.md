## Dopamine Imbalance: Consequences

### Low Dopamine
- Fatigue and lack of energy
- Failure to finish tasks
- Low libido
- Burdensome emotional memory (difficulty releasing past hurts)
- Reduced motivation and productivity

### Excess Dopamine
Too much dopamine, whether from pharmaceuticals (L-dopa drugs) or stimulants
(methamphetamine), is associated with psychosis and aggression. This
illustrates the importance of modulation rather than simple elevation.
