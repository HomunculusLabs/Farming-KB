## Why Values Vary So Widely

Several factors explain the enormous variation:

   [[knf-calcium-deficiency-symptoms]]**: Manures provide relatively little calcium
  compared to what vegetables need for strong cell walls and disease
  resistance.
- **Low phosphorus**: Most manures (except poultry) are notably low in
  phosphorus, essential for root development, flowering, and fruit
  production.
- **Trace mineral gaps**: Manure from animals fed on mineral-deficient
  forage will itself be deficient in trace elements like boron, zinc,
  and copper.
- **Poor balance**: Even if total NPK seems adequate, the ratios may
  not match what vegetables require for optimal nutrition.
