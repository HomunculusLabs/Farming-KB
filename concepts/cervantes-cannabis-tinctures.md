# Cannabis Tinctures

Cannabis tinctures use liquor as a solvent to dissolve resin
glands into a potent liquid extract. You can use any liquor,
but the higher the percentage of alcohol (proof), the quicker
and more efficient the extraction process. You can let the
spirits evaporate so the tincture contains virtually no alcohol
for those who wish to avoid alcohol consumption.

## Choosing the Alcohol

Higher-proof alcohol extracts cannabinoids more efficiently
because THC and other cannabinoids are soluble in ethanol.
Straight spirits like vodka, rum, or everclear work well. If
you prefer a flavored drink with alcohol, use liqueurs like
Kahlua, Cointreau, or Galliano. However, liqueurs contain a
lower percentage of alcohol, so the extraction process takes
longer.

The key factor is alcohol content: 80 proof (40 percent
alcohol) works but requires longer extraction times. Higher
proof spirits (150-190 proof) extract faster and more
completely.

## Tincture Recipe

Ingredients:
- 4 ounces (112 grams) of clean cannabis leaf
- 2 quarts (about 2 liters) of lukewarm water (90 degrees F /
  32 degrees C)
- 1 quart (0.95 L) of 80 proof or higher alcohol

Step-by-step process:

Step 1: Chlorophyll Removal
- Clean 4 ounces of cannabis leaf in 2 quarts of lukewarm
  water (90 degrees F).
- Stir until well-mixed and all leaf is wet.
- Keep leaves whole for easier handling.
- Strain out leaves with a colander, letting the greenish
  water drain into a receptacle.
- This step washes out much of the green chlorophyll,
  improving the final flavor.

Step 2: Alcohol Extraction
- Place the wet foliage in a bowl and cover with 1 quart of
  alcohol of your choice.
- Stir until alcohol and foliage are well-mixed.
- Make sure all foliage is covered with alcohol.
- Put a lid on the bowl so alcohol cannot evaporate.
- Let it sit for 48 hours.

Step 3: Evaporation
- Take the lid off the bowl and let the mix sit uncovered for
  12 hours, until about half the alcohol evaporates.
- Stir the brew again to ensure it is well-mixed.

Step 4: Filtration
- Pour the mixture through a coffee filter into a receptacle.
- Use the coffee filter to wring all liquid out of leaves.
- Pour the alcohol through the leaves again and wring out the
  liquid for maximum extraction.

## Potency and Yield

The resulting tincture will be about two cups of cannabis
tincture concentrate ready to use. You can boil the mixture
down further to concentrate it more. The tincture will contain
60 percent or more of the THC from the entire four ounces of
raw material, making it a very efficient extraction method.

## Usage and Applications

The tincture can be added to recipes in lieu of other liquids
such as water, wine, or other spirits. It can also be consumed
directly by placing drops under the tongue (sublingual
absorption) or adding to beverages.

Be very careful when drinking the tincture; it is potent. The
effects are similar to eating cannabis but come on in less time
because sublingual and gastric absorption bypass some of the
digestive process. Start with small doses and wait to assess
effects before consuming more.

## Storage and Degradation

Store concentrated tinctures in a cool, dark place to avoid
early degradation. Heat and light degrade tinctures quickly.
Use the tincture within one to three months for best quality
and potency. Dark glass dropper bottles provide ideal storage,
protecting the tincture from light while allowing precise
dosing.

## Advantages of Tinctures

- Precise dosing: dropper bottles allow measured doses.
- Fast absorption: sublingual use takes effect faster than
  edibles.
- Discreet: no telltale smell of smoking.
- Versatile: can be added to food or beverages.
- Long extraction time produces high cannabinoid retention.
- No cooking required; simple to prepare at home.

## Historical Context

Cannabis tinctures were widely available as patent medicines
in the 19th and early 20th centuries. They were prescribed for
various ailments before prohibition removed cannabis from
pharmacopeias. Modern medical cannabis programs have
revitalized interest in tinctures as a precise, smoke-free
delivery method.
