- **Grain-to-grain transfer**: Colonized grain from one jar is used to
  inoculate multiple new jars. Extremely fast and efficient but carries
  higher contamination risk since any problem in the parent jar propagates
  to all children. A single colonized jar can expand to 10-20 new jars.

## Colonization and Storage

After inoculation, jars are kept at the species-appropriate temperature
(usually 20-25 degrees C for most gourmet species) in the dark. Full
colonization typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on species, grain type,
and inoculation method. Jars should be shaken once or twice during
colonization to redistribute the mycelium and speed up the process.

Fully colonized spawn can be stored in the refrigerator (2-4 degrees C) for
several months, though viability slowly declines. For long-term preservation,
cultures should be maintained on agar slants or in liquid nitrogen.
