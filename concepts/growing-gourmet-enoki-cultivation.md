# Enoki Cultivation

Enoki ([[ethnomycology-cultural-significance-sacred-fungi]]:
- Enokitake (Japanese: "Snow Peak Mushroom")
- Nametake ("Slimy Mushroom," referring to the mucilaginous surface when cooked)
- Yuki-motase ("Snow Mushroom," from its white cultivated appearance)
- Winter Mushroom (from its natural fruiting season)
- Velvet Foot or Furry Foot (from the dark, velvety stem base)
- Golden Mushroom (in Thailand and Southeast Asia)

## Taxonomy

Formerly classified as Collybia velutipes, the species was reclassified to Flammulina velutipes. The taxonomic revision reflects advances in fungal systematics but has not changed the mushroom's widespread recognition by its traditional common names.

## Description

Wild specimens feature caps 1-5 cm in diameter, convex to plane and often upturned at maturity. The cap surface is viscid when wet, colored bright to dull yellowish-brown to orangish-brown. Gills are white to yellowish, attached to the stem (adnexed to adnate). The stem is 1-3 inches long, yellow to yellowish-brown, darkening with age. The most distinctive feature is the dense covering of fine, velvety brown hairs near the stem base.

Spores are white, measuring 6-8 x 3-4 micrometers, ellipsoid in shape. The spore print is white, distinguishing it from many lookalikes.

## Distribution and Natural Habitat

Enoki is widely distributed across [[duggar-mushroom-environmental-factors-temperature-moisture-light]]:
1. **Elevated CO2**: Maintaining CO2 levels well above 1,000 ppm causes stems to elongate dramatically as the mycelium searches for fresh air
2. **Limited light**: Restricting light exposure prevents cap expansion and maintains the white color by inhibiting pigment development

These conditions are essentially the opposite of optimal growing conditions for most other [[growing-gourmet-light-requirements-mushroom-fruiting]]: minimal (limited for white color)

### Fruitbody Development
- Temperature: 45-55F (7-13C)
- Relative Humidity: 85-90%
- Duration: 7-10 days
- CO2: elevated for elongated stems
- Light Requirements: limited for pale coloration

### Cropping Cycle
- Typically 1-2 crops
