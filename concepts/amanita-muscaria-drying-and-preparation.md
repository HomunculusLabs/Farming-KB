# Amanita Muscaria Drying and Preparation Guide

The preparation of amanita muscaria chemistry and pharmacology contains mainly [[ibotenic-acid]], with only a trace of agarin): alpha-amino-3-hydroxy-5-isoxazoloacetic acid, C5H6N2O4, MW 158.11, water-soluble, mp 150-152 C with [[muscimol-and-amanita-muscaria]] can apparently be reconverted into [[amanita-muscaria-and-ibotenic-acid-containing-fungi]] in the presence of carbonated water. This reverses the effects of drying where ibotenic acid loses CO2 and becomes muscimol. The resulting effect is similar to fresh Amanita muscaria ingestion: nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps, stupor, coma-like sleep, excess salivation, and no hallucinogenic effect.

## Dosage Guidelines

### Active Range

The most pleasurable range for most people is from 3.5 grams (1/8 oz) to 10 grams (3/8 oz), equal to approximately 3 to 10 mg muscimol. People familiar with dried Amanita muscaria can detect faint effects from less than 0.5 grams.

### Upper Limit

Maximum 14 grams (1/2 oz, about 3 specimens) per 200 lb person. Smaller people should use proportionally less: 1/8 oz per 50 lbs body weight maximum. All [[medicinal-herbs-for-livestock]] should be kept out of the reach of children.

### Effect Variables

The effect of a drug plant is subject to several variables:

1. **Absorption rate** of active principles into the body
2. **Physical size of plant pieces** -- smaller pieces release active principles faster, leading to more intense but shorter effects; larger pieces produce lighter, longer-lasting experiences
3. **Individual variation** in potency between specimens -- averaging across multiple plants from a batch produces more predictable results
4. **Preservation** -- all herbs decline in potency over time due to oxygen, moisture, light, and heat

### Onset and Duration

Based on the detailed testimonial of Donald E. Teeter, the progression at increasing doses follows a consistent pattern:

- **1/16 oz (1.75 g):** Slight euphoria, slight increase in ambient light, ~3 hours duration
- **1/8 oz (3.5 g):** General calm, visual illusions of color (especially blue brilliance), waviness of visual field, slight walking impairment, illuminated closed-eye patterns, drowsiness after ~2 hours, extraordinary closed-eye visual illusions, inner peace. Total: ~4.5 hours
- **1/4 oz (7 g):** Much more pronounced effects, quicker onset, room appears brilliant with light, walking like drunk (lying down mandatory after ~1-2 hours), intense Persian carpet closed-eye patterns, great inner peace, auditory effects
- **1/2 oz (14 g):** Very quick onset (minutes), extreme inner calm, indescribable illuminated visions, extremely complex auditory effects including narrated visions and "teachings," spiritual journey to a "kingdom of light." Physical rejuvenation lasting ~14 days after ingestion

## Natural Science: Botanical Description

### Cap
- 1.5 to 8 inches diameter (sometimes much larger, up to 12 inches)
- Color ranges from straw yellow through yellow-orange, orange, orange-red to bright blood red
- Surface smooth with white or off-white warts
- Margin may have striated surface [[spore-print]] lls below, especially in older specimens

### Gills
- Crowded, white, broad, free from stem with extremely fine hairy edges

### Spores
- 8-11 x 6-8 micrometers, ellipsoid, thin-walled
- [[boil-a-bag-spore-print-technique-jarrold]]: white

### Stalk
- 1.5 to 10 inches long, cylindrical, pure white to off-white
- Interior pithy when young, hollow in older specimens
- Full skirt-shaped white persistent veil, sometimes with velvety yellow edge
- Bulbous base covered in concentric ring scales

### Flesh
- White to off-white, firm

### Season and Habitat
- [[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-north-america]]: June to fall frost
- California and Florida: December-January
- Some areas: spring fruiting
- Associated trees: pine, spruce, larch, fir, hemlock, birch, beech, maple, hazel, olive, and rarely oak
- [[worldwide-distribution-psilocybin-mushrooms]] wherever suitable forest habitat exists

### Color Varieties

From yellow to orange to red, all varieties seem to have the same effect and identical taste when dried identically. Several named varieties exist:

- A. muscaria var. muscaria (red fly agaric)
- A. muscaria var. formosa (yellow-orange fly agaric)
- A. muscaria var. alba (white variety)

The experts are not finished classifying all variations. The author recommends gathering only the yellow, orange, or red ones.
