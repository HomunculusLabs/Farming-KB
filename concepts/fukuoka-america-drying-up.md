## The Fallow System

Because the land lacks fertility for continuous cropping:

- One-third to one-half of fields are left fallow each year
- From the air, the land shows alternating green and brown stripes
- This creates a checkerboard pattern visible from great altitude

Farmers working many-thousand-acre farms in central California grow rice one year, leave the land fallow the next simply to [[fukuoka-natural-soil-fertility]], creating a negative cycle
- The negative cycle becomes a basic cause for economic failure
