# Ganoderic Acids

Ganoderic acids are a family of highly oxygenated lanostane-type triterpenoids isolated from [[blesching-cannabis-apoptosis-and-cancer-cell-death]] lines:
- Ganoderic acids T, V, W, X, Y, Z — cytotoxic against various tumour lines
- 3β-Hydroxy-26-oxo-5α-lanosta-8,24-dien-11-one
- Ergosta-7,22-diene-3β,3α,9α-triol

### Enzyme Inhibition

- **FPT inhibition** (farnesyl protein transferase): ganoderic acids A and C — potential anti-cancer mechanism by blocking Ras protein farnesylation
- **PLA2 inhibition** (phospholipase A2): ganoderic acid T — anti-inflammatory mechanism
- **DNA polymerase β inhibition**: 5,8-epidoxy-5α,8α-ergosta-6,22E-dien-3β-ol

### Hepatoprotective Activity

- Ganoderic acids R and S — strong antihepatotoxic activity in galactosamine-induced cytotoxicity tests with primary cultured rat hepatocytes
- Ganosporeric acid A — isolated from ether-soluble fraction of G. lucidum spores

### Cardiovascular Effects

- **Antihypertensive** (ACE inhibitor activity): ganoderic acids B, D, F, H, K, S, Y and ganoderol B
- **Hypocholesterolemic**: ganoderic acid Mf, ganodermic acids B and T-O
- **Anti-platelet aggregation**: ganodermic acid S

### Anti-allergic/ Histamine Release Inhibition

- Cyclooctasulfur and ganoderic acids C and D inhibit histamine release

## Extraction and Isolation

Ganoderic acids are typically extracted from fruiting bodies or spores using organic solvents (ethanol, methanol, ether) rather than the hot water extraction used for polysaccharides. The ether-soluble fraction is particularly rich in triterpenoids. Purification involves column chromatography and recrystallization.
