---# Cannabis and Neuropathies (General)

## Overview

Neuropathies are pains generally caused over time by nerve damage from past
traumatic events (pressure, heat, cold, chemicals, radiation) or a progression
of certain diseases, toxins, pathogens, or lack of nutrients. Deformities,
scar tissue, impaired tissue metabolism, impairment of nerve fiber insulation
(demyelination), and inflammation all individually or together contribute to
neuropathies.
