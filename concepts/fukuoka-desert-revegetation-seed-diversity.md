## The True Cause of Desertification

Fukuoka argued that the land does not become a desert because the water
disappears. The fundamental conditions preceding and underlying the
disappearance of that water are the perishing of vegetation and other
plants. When man cuts down trees and his livestock feeds on grasses,
the varieties of plant life invariably dwindle. A simplified vegetative
cover is easily destroyed.

If the land is taken over by yellow grasses such as foxtail, the
increased reflection of sunlight raises the temperature of the ground.
This in turn upsets weather patterns and causes the accelerated
evaporation of ground moisture, transforming the land into desert. The
vegetation disappears because of the destruction of plant diversity,
not because of lack of water.

## The Failure of Conventional Approaches

Up until now, since everyone has assumed that the vegetation disappears
