## Water Softening for Pesticide Efficacy

Water quality is a critical but often overlooked factor in pesticide
effectiveness. Hard water, containing high levels of calcium, magnesium,
and iron, can dramatically reduce the performance of both natural and
synthetic pesticides by breaking down wetting agents and preventing
proper coating of plant surfaces. The JADAM system addresses this
through water testing, soft water selection, and affordable water
softening technology.

## Understanding Hard Water

Hard water is water with high content of dissolved calcium (Ca),
magnesium (Mg), and iron (Fe). When [[jadam-water-softener-pesticide]] specifications:
- Dimensions: 150 x 1100 mm (6 x 43.3 inches)
- Weight: 27 kg (60 lb)
- Resin capacity: 17 L (4.5 gal) of ion-exchange resin
- Softening capacity: approximately 15 tons (4,000 gal) of water per
  resin charge
- Flow rate: 30 minutes to produce 500 L (132 gal) of soft water
- Operating cost: approximately $5 per month

## Installation and Use

1. Fix the water softener upright on a stable surface.
2. Connect a filter to the input port to prevent debris from entering.
3. Connect a 15 mm hose to the water input.
4. Connect another 15 mm hose to the water output.
5. Discard the brownish water that comes out initially.
6. Always do a water test with JWA before mixing pesticide.

The filter needs frequent cleaning with a toothbrush. The device
produces about 500 L of soft water per 30-minute session, enough for
one pesticide application on a substantial area. Take care in winter
for freeze damage.

## Resin Regeneration

Ion-exchange resin has a limited capacity. After softening approximately
15 tons of water, the resin must be regenerated through salt water
treatment:

1. Lower the output hose and completely drain the device.
2. Raise the hose end higher than the top of the softener.
3. Dissolve 2.5 kg of salt in 7 L of water. Remove impurities.
4. Pour the salt water into the regeneration port. Let sit for over
   1 hour.
5. Lower the hose and drain the machine.
6. Run clean water through for 10 minutes to wash out salt water.
7. Test with JWA to confirm softening power is restored.

Salt water treatment can be repeated about 15 times. Resin lifespan is
approximately 1 year, making the operating cost roughly $5 per month.

## Impact on Pesticide Performance

The difference between hard and soft water in pesticide application is
dramatic. With soft water, JWA at 0.5 L per 500 L provides light
wetting, at 1.5 L provides good coverage, and at 3 L provides
excellent complete coating even on difficult surfaces like taro leaves.
