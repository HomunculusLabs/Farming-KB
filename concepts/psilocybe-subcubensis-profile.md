### Gills (Lamellae)

The gills are adnate to slightly adnexed, with moderate spacing.
They are initially grayish but darken to deep purplish-brown or
nearly black as the spores mature. The gill edges remain paler than
the faces, creating a noticeable contrast. This dark spore coloration
