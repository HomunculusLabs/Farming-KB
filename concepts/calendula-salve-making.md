# Calendula Salve and Herbal Remedies

These traditional herbal remedies were once found in almost every "home pharmacy." Because the potency of medicinal plants can vary from place to place, the recipes should be adapted. With a little experience the correct strength can easily be determined.

## Calendula Salve

### Ingredients
- Two heaped double handfuls of calendula ([[calendula-officinalis]]) — whole plant including stem, leaves and flowers
- Roughly half a litre of lard (available from a butcher), or vegetable oil (e.g. [[olive-cultivation-and-olive-oil-production]])
- If using vegetable oil: 200-250g beeswax per litre of oil as thickening agent

### Method
1. Cut the calendula finely
2. Heat the lard and carefully fry the calendula whilst keeping it moving
3. Cover the mixture and leave it to stand for a day
4. Lightly warm, filter through a cloth, and pour into a container

### If Using Vegetable Oil
1. Follow steps 1-3 above
2. Warm and melt the beeswax separately
3. Mix the melted wax into the filtered calendula oil well
4. Leave to cool

The more wax you use, the stiffer the salve will be. If you prefer a very creamy salve, use less wax.

### Uses
Calendula salve can be used to treat all kinds of injuries because it encourages wounds to heal and keeps inflammation down.

## Lemon Thyme and Thyme Oil

### Ingredients
- Sprigs of thyme (picked during dry weather at midday, when scent is most intense)
- Cold-pressed sunflower or olive oil (enough to cover sprigs by about two fingers)

### Method
1. Place thyme sprigs in a bottle with the oil
2. Leave in a sunny place (e.g. window sill) for 14 days
3. Sieve out the flowers with a cloth
4. Optionally repeat the process with new flowers to make the oil more potent

### Uses
- Recommended for sprains and rheumatism (rub in regularly to affected area)
- Also recommended for people who have suffered strokes
- Use on children with caution — watch for reactions on sensitive skin

## Chicory Tea for Diabetics

### Ingredients
- Equal amounts of: chicory root ([[cichorium-intybus]]), dandelion root, stinging nettles, French lilac, bilberry leaves
- Three tablespoons of the plant mixture per litre of water

### Method
Steep the mixture in hot water. Brew for only a short period. Can be taken every day.

### Uses
Chicory was once used by diabetics. Farmers also made fresh juice from it — only a teaspoonful was taken to lower blood sugar levels.

## Tormentil Powder

### Ingredients
- Dried tormentil roots (Potentilla erecta)

### Method
Grind dried roots into a fine powder (e.g. in a coffee mill). Store in a jar. The finer the powder the better.

### Uses
Applied directly to heavily bleeding wounds. Its ability to stop bleeding means wounds heal very well without leaving large scars.

## Drawing Salve (Traditional)

Made from tree resin (liquid larch pitch) mixed with different [[medicinal-herbs-for-livestock]] and applied as a poultice. The effect was so powerful it could treat inflammation and festering wounds in a very short time.

## Onion, Garlic and Horseradish Poultice

Made with lard and applied with hot cloths. Effective medicinal remedy used traditionally on children.
