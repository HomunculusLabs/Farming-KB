# Cannabis and Pancreatitis

## Overview

Pancreatitis is inflammation of the pancreas, an organ essential for digestion
and blood sugar regulation. Acute pancreatitis is a sudden attack causing
intense abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, and fever. Chronic pancreatitis is
long-standing inflammation that progressively damages the pancreas, leading to
permanent structural changes, loss of function, diabetes, and increased risk of
pancreatic cancer.
