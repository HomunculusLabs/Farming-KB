# The Mushroom Stones of Guatemala

## The First Archaeological Evidence

The mushroom stones of Guatemala and Mexico represent the oldest known archaeological evidence for the use of psychoactive mushrooms in human culture. First brought to scholarly attention by the amateur archaeologist Stephan de Borhegyi and later championed by Gordon Wasson, these small stone sculptures depict mushroom-like forms and date from as early as 1000 B.C.

More than two hundred mushroom stones have been found in Guatemala alone, and others have been discovered in Mexico, Honduras, and El Salvador. They range in height from a few inches to over two feet. Some are plain and abstract; others are elaborately carved with human faces, animal motifs, or geometric designs.
