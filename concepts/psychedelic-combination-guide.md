# Psychedelic Combination Guide

turner-lsd|Turner documents multiple psychedelic combinations and their effects, with important warnings about what works and what doesn't. See also [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]]. These combinations can be very heavy, both physically and mentally.

## Combinations That Work Well

### Harmala + DMT (Ayahuasca-like)
This is the foundational combination — [[beta-carboline-chemistry]] (normally inactive when eaten). N,N-DMT is significantly intensified by Harmala — smoking 15–20 mg while on Harmala produces a much stronger experience. Harmala extends the N,N-DMT experience.

- 5-MeO-DMT + Harmala produces the most powerful psychedelic combination but also the highest danger — Turner experienced both his most spectacular journeys and violent purging
- Some users feel DMT is "warbly" with Harmala and lacks the "Diamond Consciousness" of DMT alone
- See [[harmala-alkaloids-and-ayahuasca-preparation]] for details

### LSD + DMT
DMT after [[lsd-psychedelic-experience-guide]].

### LSD + Ketamine + N,N-DMT
- Start with 200 mcg LSD
- After 2 hours, inject 100 mg [[2c-b-psychedelic-empathogen-guide]] + 5 g [[dmt-dimethyltryptamine-experience-guide]], or [[mescaline-compound-profile]] was "a serious insult" to Mescalito
- Early positive experiences with mushroom + K eventually reversed
- During Ketamine addiction, mushroom trips became static and lethargic

### Harmala + Mescaline
[[2c-b-psychedelic-empathogen-guide]] and other phenethylamines.

### Ketamine Addiction Warning
- Very high potential for psychological addiction
- Large percentage of users consume non-stop until supply exhausted
- Turner himself developed addiction after two years of once-per-week use
- DMT and psilocybin proved to be allies in breaking addiction, providing insights into negative effects
- Negative effects of frequent K use: reduced ambition, reduced healthy mortal fears, reluctance to confront difficult situations
