# Psychedelic Combination Guide

turner-lsd|Turner documents multiple psychedelic combinations and their effects, with important warnings about what works and what doesn't. See also [[psychedelic-experience-stages]] for general context on how combination trips unfold.

## Safety

All combinations listed are from personal experiments. They may affect others differently. Those using multiple combinations should exercise extreme caution, especially with [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]]. These combinations can be very heavy, both physically and mentally.

## Combinations That Work Well

### Harmala + DMT (Ayahuasca-like)
This is the foundational combination — [[beta-carboline-chemistry]] potentiates DMT not only in intensity and duration but also content and quality. [[beta-carboline-chemistry]] (normally inactive when eaten). N,N-DMT is significantly intensified by Harmala — smoking 15–20 mg while on Harmala produces a much stronger experience. Harmala extends the N,N-DMT experience.

- 5-MeO-DMT + Harmala produces the most powerful psychedelic combination but also the highest danger — Turner experienced both his most spectacular journeys and violent purging
- Some users feel DMT is "warbly" with Harmala and lacks the "Diamond Consciousness" of DMT alone
- See [[ayahuasca-preparation]] and [[harmala-alkaloids-and-ayahuasca-preparation]] for details

### LSD + DMT
DMT after [[lsd-compound-profile]] produces intensified effects; this is a common and well-loved combination. See also [[lsd-psychedelic-experience-guide]].

### LSD + Ketamine + N,N-DMT
- Start with 200 mcg LSD
- After 2 hours, inject 100 mg [[ketamine-ultimate-psychedelic-journey]]
- Best to take Ketamine toward the end of the LSD session
- DMT after Ketamine journey is diminished in intensity
- Recovery period extended by 1-2 hours on acid

### LSD + Syrian Rue + N,N-DMT + 5-MeO-DMT + Nitrous Oxide + Cannabis
- "Felt absolutely beautiful"
- Harmala + LSD + DMT as the basis; cannabis and nitrous as add-ons
- Vastly more space or information to explore than typical LSD trip
- Infused with spiritual quality

### Mushrooms + Syrian Rue + N,N-DMT + Nitrous Oxide + Ketamine
- Mushrooms + Syrian Rue as medium strength base
- DMT adds visual enhancement
- Ketamine overpowers remaining high but is pleasantly enhanced
- Semi-conscious chanting and movement occurred
- Turner later decided against this combination (see warnings below)

### LSD + Ludiomil + Nitrous Oxide (+ Ketamine)
- Ludiomil intensifies LSD and produces intense dream activity
- Produces a "platinum plated" acid trip — "every perception and thought coated with silverish translucent light"
- Ludiomil creates a time-delay buffer zone preventing rapid mental responses: when taken 2 hours into the trip, it produced a "thin invisible membrane between myself and anything my senses touched upon"; later experiments revealed the membrane as a spongy, spring-loaded barrier
- Taking Ludiomil 5 hours prior to LSD produced significantly more dream activity in subsequent nights
- Nitrous oxide produces a "totally hilarious, melting through the bottom of the universe" experience

## Ketamine + 2C-B (Best Combination)
Enhanced K journey plus reduced negative effects during recovery. "The widest dosage window for achieving a spectacular Ketamine journey."

## Ketamine + Ecstasy
No enhancement of the K journey, but reduces recovery discomfort.

## Ketamine + Nitrous Oxide
One of Turner's most-used combinations with some 30–40 experiments.

## DMT + Nitrous Oxide
Nitrous pairs well with virtually every psychedelic and combination tested. Turner describes the unique "melting through the bottom of the universe" experience with LSD + Ludiomil + Nitrous.

## Combinations to Avoid

### 2C-B + Mushrooms
Described by Turner as "the first psychedelic combination I tried that does not work." Despite high hopes, neither 25 mg [[2c-b-psychedelic-empathogen-guide]] + 5 g [[psilocybin-mushroom-distribution-worldwide]] nor larger doses produced synergy — results were no better than either substance alone.

### Ketamine + Natural Psychedelics
Turner strongly warns against combining Ketamine with mushrooms, [[dmt-dimethyltryptamine-experience-guide]], or [[mescaline-peyote-and-san-pedro-guide]]:
- "The entities of the natural psychedelic realms are adverse to having their realms polluted"
- Combining Ketamine with [[mescaline-compound-profile]] was "a serious insult" to Mescalito
- Early positive experiences with mushroom + K eventually reversed
- During Ketamine addiction, mushroom trips became static and lethargic

### Harmala + Mescaline
[[beta-carboline-chemistry]] significantly intensifies and may be dangerous with mescaline. The same warning applies to [[2c-b-psychedelic-empathogen-guide]] and other phenethylamines.

### Ketamine Addiction Warning
- Very high potential for psychological addiction
- Large percentage of users consume non-stop until supply exhausted
- Turner himself developed addiction after two years of once-per-week use
- DMT and psilocybin proved to be allies in breaking addiction, providing insights into negative effects
- Negative effects of frequent K use: reduced ambition, reduced healthy mortal fears, reluctance to confront difficult situations
