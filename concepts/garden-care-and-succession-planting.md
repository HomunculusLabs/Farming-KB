## Winter Storage in Ground

Carrots, turnips, leeks, kale, and some root crops are hardy to frost. Leave them in the ground and cover soil with a thick layer of hay to prevent freezing. At end of fall, unripe green tomatoes can be saved by pulling the whole plant and hanging it upside down in a basement or cellar.
