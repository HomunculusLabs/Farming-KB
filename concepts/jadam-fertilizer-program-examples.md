# JADAM Fertilizer Program Examples

## Overview

JADAM fertilizer programs combine [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]], seawater/sea salt, [[jadam-phyllite-solution]], and [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer]] in specific ratios for different growth stages. Below are the complete program examples from Section 17 of the JADAM book. All recipes are for **0.33 ha (0.8 acre)** unless otherwise noted.

## Foliar Application Conversion

For foliar spraying (instead of soil application):
- Reduce total inputs (JMS + JLF) to below 30 L (7.9 gal) per 500 L (132 gal)
- Add over 1.5 L (0.4 gal) of [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] to prevent marks
- If using more than 20 L of JMS, JWA will be broken down (reduced wetting power)
- 20 L of seawater can be replaced with 0.5 kg (1.1 lb) of sea salt
- Seawater and sea salt reduce wetting power of JWA -- apply them to soil
- Use seawater or sea salt 3-4 times per month

## Program 1: Soil Foundation Builder

**Purpose:** Promote root growth, strengthen resistance against cold and frost
**Timing:** Before transplanting and before lowering/sprouting. Repeat 3-4 times.
**Area:** 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 500 L (132 gal)
- Sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb) -- dissolve in plenty of water (at least 1000x)
- [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF ([[jadam-liquid-fertilizer]] from grass/crop residues) 60 L (16 gal)

**Application:** Mix with water or apply undiluted before rain for open fields. For greenhouses, apply with plenty of water to penetrate deep. Can also be applied on tree trunks.

**Note:** 500 L of JMS can cover 0.1 to 3.3 ha (0.25-8.2 ac).

## Program 2: Seed and Seedling Treatment

**Purpose:** Greatly improve root settlement through microbial diversity
**Area:** For seed/seedling batches

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 100 L (26 gal)
  - Can be replaced with 10 kg (22 lb) of leaf mold
- Add water to make 500 L (132 gal)

**Application:** Leave seeds or seedlings in the solution for 2-3 minutes before planting.

## Program 3: Basic Additional Fertilizer

**Purpose:** Provide balanced nutrients and minerals throughout growth
**Timing:** Continue throughout growth period
**Area:** 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 500 L (132 gal)
- Seawater 60 L (16 gal); or sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF 30 L (8 gal)

**Note:** Add as much water as needed. Use seawater 3-4 times per month.

## Program 4: Body Growth Promoter

**Purpose:** Boost vegetative growth during active growing period
**Timing:** When additional growth boost is needed
**Area:** 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 500 L (132 gal)
- Seawater 60 L (16 gal); or sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF 30 L (8 gal)
- Food scrap JLF 60 L (16 gal); or urine, feces, fish JLF

## Program 5: Reproductive Growth Promoter

**Purpose:** Strengthen flowering and fruiting
**Timing:** When flowering/fruiting appears weak
**Area:** 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 500 L (132 gal)
- Seawater 60 L (16 gal); or sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF 30 L (8 gal)
- Calcium phosphate JLF 60 L (16 gal)

## Program 6: Fruit Growth Promoter

**Purpose:** Boost fruit development and quality
**Timing:** During fruit growth period
**Area:** 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)

- [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 500 L (132 gal)
- Seawater 60 L (16 gal); or sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] 60 L (16 gal)
- Calcium JLF 30 L (8 gal)
- Potassium JLF 40 L (11 gal)

**Growth Control Tip:** To control excessive vegetative growth, take out potassium JLF and increase calcium JLF.

## Scaling Guide

For areas larger than 0.1 ha (1/4 acre):
- Increase sea salt, phyllite solution, and JLF in the same ratio

For areas smaller than 0.1 ha (1/4 acre):
- Take out sea salt from the formula
