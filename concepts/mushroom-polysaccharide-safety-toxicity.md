### Genotoxicity (Zhong et al., 1999)

1. Salmonella typhimurium mutagenicity: negative
2. V79 Chinese hamster cell cytotoxicity: negative
3. In vivo micronucleus test: no mutagenic potential
4. Bone marrow chromosome analysis: no changes at 126 mg/kg

### Subchronic Toxicity

Oral PSP at 1.5-6.0 g/kg/day for 62 days in rats showed no differences
in blood, serum biochemistry, or histopathology versus controls (Jiang et
al., 1999). Acute, chronic, genetic, and reproductive toxicity were very
low at 50-100 times clinical dose (Jin, 1999).

## Pre-Clinical: Ganoderma lucidum

Crude extracts showed no clastogenicity, aneugenicity, or genotoxic
chromosomal breakage at any dose tested (Steinmetz et al., 2001; Chiu et
al., 2000). Previous anti-mutagenic claims were not substantiated in Comet
Assays.

## Clinical Safety Data

### Lentinan (469 patients)

- 32 patients (6.8%) had adverse reactions, none serious
- Only 2 required discontinuation
- Most common: rash (1.9%), chest pressure (1.7%), nausea (1.7%)

### PSK

Virtually non-existent adverse effect risk across decades of use. No
drug-drug interactions in clinical trials.

### PSP

Tens of thousands of Chinese patients treated, many for over 10 years
with no serious adverse effects (Yang, 1999). Phase I established doses up
to 6g/day as well tolerated.

### Ganopoly (143 patients)

Only five grade I adverse events (nausea: 2; diarrhoea: 1; other: 2).
No grade II+ toxicity.

## Comparison with Other BRMs

Other BRMs (LAK cells, IL-2, alpha-IFN, TNF) produce severe side-effects at
effective doses: fevers, chills, rashes, arthralgia, hypotension, pulmonary
oedema, congestive heart failure, and CNS toxicities. Mushroom
polysaccharides offer a substantially more favourable safety profile.

## Regulatory Exemptions

- Japan/China: Phase I trials have limited significance (no maximum
  tolerated dose reached)
- US FDA: Exempted Maitake polysaccharides from Phase I due to limited
  side-effects
- Large lentinan phase III trials found no adverse reactions or
  drug-drug interactions
