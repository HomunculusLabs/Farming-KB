## Viability Testing

Before planting large quantities of stored seed, test germination rate. The
standard germination test: place 100 seeds (or 25 for limited seed) on damp
paper towel in a sealed plastic bag at room temperature. Count germinated seeds
after 7-14 days depending on species. Multiply by the appropriate factor to
express as a percentage.

The ragdoll test: wrap a counted sample of seeds in damp cloth, keep warm and
moist, and check for sprouting after the expected germination period.

If germination falls below 70%, increase sowing density accordingly or obtain
fresh seed.
