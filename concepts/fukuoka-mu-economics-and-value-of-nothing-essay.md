## Happiness Without Things

Fukuoka's vision of Mu economics is ultimately a vision of human happiness liberated from the tyranny of value. If things have no intrinsic value, then happiness cannot depend on things. This does not mean asceticism or deprivation; it means liberation from the endless pursuit of objects that can never satisfy.

- In a world of Mu economics, people would not measure their lives by what they possess

- The quarter-acre farm that provides rice, barley, vegetables, and fruit would be sufficient

- The hearthside would be the universe; to own nothing would be never to be without

- Culture and the light of religious teachings are not to be found in the cities and temples of dazzling splendor
