## Comparison with Classical Hallucinogens

The KOR-mediated mechanism of salvinorin-A produces an experience
qualitatively different from classical serotonergic hallucinogens:

- **Onset:** Extremely rapid when smoked (seconds) versus 20-90
  minutes for psilocybin or LSD
- **Duration:** Very short (15 minutes smoked, 1 hour oral) versus
  4-8 hours for most classical hallucinogens
- **Quality:** More dissociative and disorienting, less euphoric,
  more likely to produce feelings of unreality and loss of
  physical coordination
- **Tolerance:** Classical hallucinogens show cross-tolerance with
  each other (LSD-psilocybin-mescaline); salvinorin-A does not
  share this cross-tolerance pattern

The lack of reinforcing properties is notable. Unlike drugs that
activate the mu-opioid receptor, salvinorin-A does not produce
euphoria or compulsive drug-seeking behavior. This is consistent
with the broader KOR pharmacology, where activation tends to be
aversive rather than rewarding.
