## ALEPH Family of Psychedelic Amphetamines

## Overview

The ALEPH family is a series of thio-substituted (sulfur-containing) psychedelic amphetamines synthesized and evaluated by [[shroom-scientific-study]] naming convention. ALEPH is specifically the para-isomer; positional isomers exist as ORTHO-DOT and META-DOT.

## Unfinished Compounds

Several ALEPH compounds were never completed:

- **ALEPH-3** (methallylthio): Attempted twice, years apart. The aldehyde synthesis produced impure products both times. The corresponding 2C-T-16 (allylthio) also reached only the nitrostyrene stage.
- **ALEPH-5** (cyclohexylthio): The nitrostyrene was obtained but "sits there on the shelf to be reduced... some inspired day."
- **ALEPH-16** (allylthio): The nitrostyrene (mp 114-115 deg C) was characterized but never reduced.
