### Depleted Populations

Faulty harvesting methods have seriously depleted populations of peyote cactus in the
wild. Many people uproot the entire plant when harvesting, which is unnecessary and
wasteful since the roots contain no mescaline. Some of these plants have taken a long
time to reach their size -- a cactus three inches in diameter may be more than 20
years old.
