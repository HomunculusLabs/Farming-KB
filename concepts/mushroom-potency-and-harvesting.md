# Mushroom Potency and Harvesting

The potency of [[brassinosteroid-growth-promotion-psilocybe-cubensis]] mushrooms is not fixed — it varies
significantly depending on the [[cervantes-green-algae-growing-medium]], the age of the specimen at
harvest, and the preservation method. The PF TEK identifies these factors
as critical to producing mushrooms of the highest quality, and emphasizes
that the common perception of P. Cubensis as a "weak" mushroom is often
the result of poor [[saffron-cultivation-and-harvesting]] practices rather than any
inherent limitation of the species.

## Immature Specimens Are More Potent Than Mature Ones

It has been scientifically proven that small immature mushroom specimens
are significantly more potent than larger mature specimens. This is one of
the most important principles in the PF TEK and the key to producing
mushrooms of maximum psychedelic quality.

As a mushroom matures, its cap expands like an umbrella, the veil beneath
the cap tears away, the gills darken, and the mushroom increases
dramatically in physical size. However, while the mushroom gets larger,
its potency by weight actually decreases. The same total alkaloid content
becomes distributed across a much larger mass of fungal tissue, resulting
in a lower concentration of active compounds per gram.

Research by Jochen Gartz has shown that P. Cubensis can achieve a baseline
psilocybin content of 1% — comparable to [[guzman-allen-gartz-psilocybe-semilanceata-temperate-cosmopolitan-distribution]] (liberty
cap). But reaching this potential depends entirely on three factors: growing
the mushroom on brown rice, harvesting it young (pre-sporulation), and
preserving it by cool desiccation with a desiccant.

## The Sequence: Primordia to Abhorts to Sporocarps

The fruiting cycle follows a predictable sequence. After the substrate cake
is fully colonized by white mycelium, the fungus begins to form tiny white
pin-like structures — this is called pinning, the beginning of the fruiting
cycle. Soon after, small round fungus growths appear that begin to turn
yellow. Then primordia start to grow — tiny worm-like structures with tiny
reddish heads. These are the first mushrooms.

From this point, the primordia diverge into several developmental paths:

- **Abhorts**: Over half of the small primordia will cease growing,
  becoming convoluted and deformed. These abortive mushrooms are
  ironically the most potent of all.

- **Mutants**: Blobs of fungus with little or no cap also appear. These
  mutants are likewise good for harvesting and high in alkaloids.

- **Sporocarps**: The "perfect" specimens — stately mushrooms with
  well-formed caps and stems that develop into mature [[bloomfield-asterophora-and-mycoparasites-of-fruiting-bodies]]
  capable of releasing spores.

## Identifying Abhorts

Abhorts are recognizable after witnessing the normal growth pattern of the
fungus. They are characterized by convoluted, misshapen caps and gnarly,
stunted stems. While normal mushrooms continue to grow upward with
expanding caps, abhorts stop developing, their growth arrested at an early
stage. Their caps remain small and often take on a warped or wrinkled
appearance.

The tiny "baby mushroom" abhorts are equally valuable for harvesting.
After a few days of observation, recognition of abhorts becomes easy.
As long as the abhorts appear healthy and pure white — free from any
colored contamination — they are primo in potency. A pointed knife blade
works well for removing these high-potency primordia from the cake.

## Harvesting Before Veil Break for Maximum Potency

For normal fruitbodies (sporocarps), the optimal [[query-how-to-read-cannabis-trichomes-for-the-perfect-harvest-time]] is before
the veil under the cap breaks. At this stage, the mushrooms are smaller
with roundish, conical caps that have not yet spread out. The gills on
the underside of the cap will be light in color, and sporulation has not
yet begun. These immature specimens are the best for harvesting in terms
of quality, digestibility, and potency.

The mushrooms in the PF TEK photographs are mostly well-matured —
beautiful and perfect for showcasing and spore collecting — but they are
explicitly noted as weak in psychedelic potency compared to the
diminutive young specimens. Mature specimens with upturned caps, dark
brown gills, and purple spore deposits on the stems are past their prime
as psychedelic material.

For abhorts, the [[harvest-window-cannabis]] extends until their heads begin to turn
black. Aborts should be picked as soon as it is determined they are not
developing into normal specimens. Several cakes will produce a
surprisingly large pile of these small specimens — several dried grams
from the aggregate — and they are described as "the source of the potent
magic."

## Cool Desiccation (Drying) Technique

Proper preservation is the final pillar of maximum potency. Heat is the
enemy of alkaloid preservation — never dry mushrooms in an oven or with
hot air dryers, as heat leaches the [[guzman-magic-mushrooms-chemical-constituents]] and reduces
quality. The PF TEK identifies [[mushroom-desiccant-drying-methods]] as the best overall
technique.

The procedure uses a 1-inch layer of desiccant on the bottom of a plastic
container, with mushrooms placed on a wire screen laid over the desiccant.
The container goes into a plastic bag (clear bags allow observation of
the drying process) sealed with a wire tie. After 24 hours, shriveling
is visible. After 4 to 5 days, the mushrooms are dried rock hard — the
stem should snap cleanly when bent.

Suitable desiccants include Drierite (universal lab desiccant), silica gel
granules, or DAMP GONE (inexpensive, available at hardware stores). Always
preheat desiccant before use, as purchased desiccant is usually somewhat
damp. Desiccant can be reused indefinitely by heating and storing in an
airtight container.

For the best alkaloid preservation, the desiccant box can be placed in
the refrigerator, drying the mushrooms at near-freezing temperatures.
Pre-drying on a wire screen in low-humidity air for a couple of days
before the desiccant stage speeds the process. Mushrooms dried this way
lose hardly any chemical constituents and preserve at their prime for
months. Store dried mushrooms in sealed plastic bags or canning jars in
the freezer, tightly sealed against freezer moisture.

## The PF Maxim

The PF TEK distills its potency philosophy into a single guiding maxim:
"Grow them on brown rice, harvest them when they are young, and cool dry
them with desiccant." When this is done, [[brassinosteroid-morphological-effects-psilocybe-cubensis-fruiting]] is described
as "an entheogen of the highest order," capable of achieving potency
comparable to the most revered [[global-psychedelic-mushroom-species-diversity]].
