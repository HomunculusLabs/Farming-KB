# Psilocybe baeocystis Knobby Tops Guide ## Overview psilocybe-baeocystis Singer and Smith, commonly known as "baeos" or "knobby tops," is a moderately to highly active psilocybin mushroom of the Pacific Northwest. First reported from Oregon, this species is common throughout the Pacific Northwest region of North America. It is named for its association with the discovery of the alkaloid [[psilocybe-cyanofibrillosa]] are found growing side by side.

## Potency and Chemistry

in the dosages commonly consumed. The name "baeocystis" (from the
Greek for "false bladder") is thus somewhat of a misnomer regarding
toxicity concerns.

## Taxonomic Relationships

Taxonomically, the closest relative to P. baeocystis is psilocybe-aztecorum, its subtropical Mexican cousin. Both species fade in
drying to a light color, often bleaching white from sun exposure. The
species is related to the broader group of wood-inhabiting Psilocybes
that includes psilocybe-cyanescens, [[psilocybe-caerulescens]] - Mexican ceremonial species
