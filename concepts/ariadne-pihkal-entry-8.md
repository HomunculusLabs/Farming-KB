# ARIADNE (4C-DOM, Dimoxamine)

**[[optical-isomers-psychedelic-phenethylamines]]:
- "S" isomer via (+)-2beta-nitrotartranilic acid salt
- "R" isomer via (+)-2beta-chlorotartranilic acid salt

The "R" isomer was also prepared independently by reductive
amination of 1-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylphenyl)-2-butanone
(obtained from the nitrostyrene and elemental iron) with
(+)-alpha-methylbenzylamine followed by hydrogenolysis of the
benzyl group.

## Safety and Pharmacology

ARIADNE is not hallucinogenic in cats (unlike DOM, which causes
dramatic tail erection). It showed re-motivation effects in
maze-running monkeys that had previously decided not to run
mazes anymore.

The "R" isomer was patented as an anti-depressant (tradename
Dimoxamine) for restoring motivation in senile geriatric
patients. Despite advocacy for the name "Ariadnamine," the
pharmaceutical tradename Dimoxamine was assigned. The compound
