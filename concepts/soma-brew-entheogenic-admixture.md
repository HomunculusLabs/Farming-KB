## Samsara and Spiritual Death

**Samsara** (the wheel of life) in the Hindu Vedas represents the cycle of death and rebirth. In this framework:

- **Spiritual Death** consumes those disconnected from indigenous spirituality
- Inability to release repressive, culturally conditioned beliefs holds one within the cycle
- Deep conditioning creates fierce resistance to reshaping belief systems
- Individuals often become enforcers of their own oppression
- The Shamanic death-rebirth experience is preparation for breaking this cycle

## Legacy and Significance

Shamanism is described as the **cornerstone of all religions**, though corrupted over time by patriarchal systems. The quest to identify Soma is ultimately a quest to recover humanity's oldest spiritual tradition — one centered on individual direct experience through entheogenic plants rather than institutional mediation.
