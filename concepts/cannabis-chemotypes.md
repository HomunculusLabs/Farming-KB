### Chemotype III — CBD-Dominant

- **Profile**: CBD > 0.3% and CBD > THC (typically THC < 1%)
- **CBD content**: 5-20% in modern high-CBD cultivars
- **Effects**: Non-intoxicating; anti-inflammatory, anxiolytic, anticonvulsant, neuroprotective
- **Therapeutic uses**: Treatment-resistant epilepsy (Dravet syndrome, Lennox-Gastaut syndrome), anxiety, inflammation, autoimmune conditions, neurodegenerative diseases, psychosis
- **Considerations**: Minimal psychoactive effects; may interact with liver enzymes (CYP450 system) affecting medication metabolism
- **Examples**: Charlotte's Web, Harle-Tsu, Sour Tsunami, Ringo's Gift

### Chemotype IV — THCV-Dominant

- **Profile**: Dominated by THCV (tetrahydrocannabivarin) with varying THC levels
- **Effects**: Appetite-suppressing at low doses, mildly psychoactive at high doses, energy-promoting
- **Therapeutic uses**: Obesity [[medicinal-mushroom-antimicrobial-properties]]
- Found in pine needles, rosemary, sage
