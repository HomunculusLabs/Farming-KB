## Energy Descent Action Planning

Energy descent action planning is the permaculture-informed approach to preparing communities and households for the inevitable transition from fossil-fuel-based energy systems to lower-energy futures. Developed primarily through the work of david holmgren, this framework moves beyond sustainability concepts to address the practical reality of declining energy availability.

david holmgren argues that the concept of sustainability contains an ambiguity about whether it describes a state or a process. Once we accept the reality and magnitude of energy descent, we must ask what sustainable systems might actually mean in practice.

## The Energy Descent Reality

The fossil fuel era represents a brief pulse of available energy in the long arc of human history. If it is energetically impossible for high-energy society to be anything more than a pulse, then it cannot by definition be sustainable, no matter how much technology is shuffled.

Holmgren pictures the energy climax as a spectacular but dangerous mountain peak that humanity has succeeded in climbing. The descent to safety is a sensible proposition. The climb involved heroic effort and great sacrifice, but also exhilaration and new views at every step.

The view from the top reconnects us with the wonder and majesty of the world, but we cannot dally. We must take advantage of the view to chart our way down while we have favorable weather and daylight.

The descent will be more hazardous than the climb, and we may have to camp on a series of plateaus to rest and sit out storms. Each step brings us closer to a sheltered valley where we can make a new home.

## Permaculture Strategies for Descent

One key strategy is encouraging household and local economic self-reliance. This rebuilds traditional forms of energy storage in food, fuel, and other resources.

permaculture-disaster-resilience approach recognizes that building self-reliance reduces vulnerability to both gradual energy decline and sudden disruptions.

Energy descent planning also involves creative retrofitting of existing buildings and infrastructure rather than assuming new construction.
