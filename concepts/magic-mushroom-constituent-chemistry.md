"[[tryptamine-chemistry-psilocin-and-psilocybin-synthesis]], the principal active compounds in [[baeocystin-and-norbaeocystin-in-psilocybin-biosynthesis]] are often present as minor constituents.

These compounds are closely related to the neurotransmitter serotonin both structurally and pharmacologically, and their hallucinogenic effects are thought to result from interference with normal brain serotonin signaling.

## Psilocybin (4-PO-DMT)

**Chemical name**: 4-Phosphoryloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine

**Molecular formula**: C₁₂H₁₇N₂O₄P

**Molecular weight**: 284.25 g/mol

Psilocybin is the phosphorylated prodrug of psilocin. It is the more stable of the two principal active constituents and is the primary form in which the compound exists within the fungal tissue.

Upon ingestion, psilocybin is rapidly dephosphorylated by alkaline phosphatase enzymes to yield psilocin, which is the pharmacologically active form.

The phosphate group makes psilocybin significantly more polar than psilocin, which accounts for its higher water solubility and different extraction characteristics.

### Isolation Challenges

Psilocybin has high polarity, making it difficult to isolate from mushroom material on a gram scale using standard extraction techniques.

Chromatographic methods (TLC, GC, HPLC) are typically employed for identification and quantification, requiring pure analytical standards.

### Stability Concerns

Psilocin is significantly less stable than psilocybin:

- It easily decomposes upon storage, particularly when exposed to air, light, or elevated temperatures.
- This decomposition makes isolation from natural sources on a gram scale impractical.
- The Shirota synthesis achieves 85% yield and produces stable crystalline psilocin (mp 169-174°C, dec.).

### Spectroscopic Properties

- **Melting point**: 169-174°C (with decomposition)
- **UV absorption**: λmax at 222.5, 268.0, 284.5, 294.0 nm (in methanol)
- **IR bands**: 3285, 2959, 1620, 1588 cm⁻¹ (KBr pellet)

## Baeocystin

**Chemical name**: 4-Phosphoryloxy-N-methyltryptamine

Baeocystin is a minor constituent of many Psilocybe species.

It is structurally intermediate between psilocybin and norbaeocystin, bearing a single methyl group on the ethylamine nitrogen rather than the dimethyl substitution of psilocybin.
