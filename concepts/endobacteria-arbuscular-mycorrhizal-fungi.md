## Overview

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) harbour obligate intracellular bacteria
within their own cells, creating a remarkable three-tier symbiotic system:
plant, fungus, and bacterium. The discovery that an obligately biotrophic

changed our understanding of the complexity of [[mycorrhizal-inoculum-production]]. Inocula produced
