# Salad Vegetables Organic Growing

Growing your own salad vegetables is particularly beneficial because
their quality and flavor depends on freshness. They take up
comparatively little space and most can be grown all year-round if
some protection against frost is provided. Salad crops are fast-growing
and are not usually troubled by many [[growing-sweet-corn-organically]] starts to turn its
sugar into starch the very instant it is picked. Growing your own
salads ensures peak quality and flavor that cannot be matched by
store-bought produce.

## Successional Sowing

Sow short rows of fast-maturing varieties at frequent intervals for
successional harvesting of peak-condition vegetables. Try to fit some
salads in between wider-spaced, long-term crops like Brussels sprouts
and cauliflower. This intercropping approach maximizes the productive
use of available space.

## Lettuce - *Lactuca sativa*

Lettuce is perhaps the most widely grown salad vegetable. There are
several distinct types, each with its own characteristics.

### Romaine Lettuce

A particularly crisp and refreshing salad vegetable, Romaine lettuce
has erect bright-green leaves with a prominent central vein. It is
well suited to organic cultivation and grows readily in most gardens.

### Red Leaf Lettuce

tinged with red. Like the other lettuces, it is a suitable crop for
