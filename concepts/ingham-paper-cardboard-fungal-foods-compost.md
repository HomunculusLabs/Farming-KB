### Size Specifications for Wood Chips with Paper

When combining paper and cardboard with wood chips in compost, Ingham provides specific size guidance:

- Wood chips should not be smaller than one-eighth inch
- Most chips should not be larger than one-half inch
- Up to 5 percent of the material can be larger than 1 inch
- Precision is not critical; these are approximate targets

These size ranges ensure adequate surface area for fungal colonization while maintaining enough pore space for oxygen diffusion through the compost pile.
