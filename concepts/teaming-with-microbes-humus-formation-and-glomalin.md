## Humic and Fulvic Acids

Humus contains two [[mollison-designers-tropical-soils-and-cation-exchange-management]] capacity (CEC)

### Fulvic Acids

Fulvic acids are smaller molecular weight, more soluble compounds that
remain in solution at all pH levels. They:
- Readily penetrate plant tissues and cells
- Enhance nutrient uptake and transport within plants
- Chelate both macro- and micronutrients
- Improve microbial activity in soil
- Are used as foliar sprays and soil drenches in [[hamilton-geoffhamilton-nick-organic-gardening-companion-planting]]

### Practical Use

In garden management, humic and fulvic acids are applied as:
- Components of compost tea (especially fungal-dominant teas)
- Foliar sprays for trees and perennials in autumn
- Soil drenches to support fungal populations
- Ingredients in organic liquid fertilizers

Both acids are particularly recommended for trees, shrubs, and
perennials -- plants that evolved in fungally dominated soils where
humic substances are most abundant.
