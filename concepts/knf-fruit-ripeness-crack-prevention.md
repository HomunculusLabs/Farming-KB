# KNF Fruit Ripeness and Crack Prevention

[[korean-natural-farming]] provides specific treatment protocols for
enhancing fruit ripeness, sweetness, and preventing fruit cracking.
These targeted treatments are applied at precise timing before
harvest or during adverse weather conditions. The treatments combine
multiple NF inputs at specified dilutions to address the unique
nutritional and protective needs of developing fruits, following the
principles of the [[knf-nutritive-cycle-theory]].

## Ripeness and Enhanced Sweetness Promotion

One of the most valued outcomes of Natural Farming is the production
of sweet, flavorful fruits without chemical inputs. The source
provides a specific treatment formula for promoting sweetness and
maturity in fruits:

Inputs mixed at specified dilutions:
- OHN: 1 ml
- WCA (water-soluble calcium): 2 ml
- SW (seawater): 30 ml

All inputs are mixed together in 1000 ml of water.

This treatment should be sprayed two times before harvesting: once 15
days before harvest and again 2 days before harvest. This dual
application approach ensures that the fruits receive sustained
nutritional support during the critical final ripening period.

The combination of [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient]] for plant
robustness, [[water-soluble-calcium-wca]] for fruit firmness
and nutrient accumulation, and [[cho-seawater-and-fermented-seawater|seawater]] for trace minerals creates a
comprehensive ripening treatment that enhances both sweetness and
overall fruit quality.

## Understanding Fruit Cracking

Fruit cracking is a common problem in many fruit crops. The source
identifies a specific cause-and-effect relationship: fruits crack
when rains arrive after a long drought. The sudden availability of
water after a period of water stress causes rapid fruit expansion
that exceeds the elasticity of the fruit skin, resulting in cracks.

This pattern is particularly problematic in regions with distinct wet
and dry seasons, where extended drought periods are followed by heavy
rainfall. The cracking not only reduces marketability but also
creates entry points for fungal and bacterial infections that can
destroy the fruit.

## Crack Prevention Treatment

The source provides a specific preventive treatment to be applied
during drought conditions to protect fruits from cracking when rain
eventually arrives:

Inputs mixed at specified dilutions:
- OHN: 1 ml
- BRV (brown rice vinegar): 2 ml
- FPJ (fermented plant juice): 2 ml
- LAB (lactic acid bacteria): 1 ml
- SW (seawater): 30 ml

All inputs are mixed together in 1000 ml of water.

This treatment should be applied proactively during the drought
period, before the rains arrive. Each input serves a specific
protective function:

- **OHN** increases plant robustness and helps the plant withstand
  stress conditions. See [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient]] for preparation details.
- **BRV** ([[knf-brown-rice-vinegar|brown rice vinegar]]) improves the flexibility of the tree
  and helps form the wax layer on leaves and fruit surfaces,
  creating thicker skins that resist cracking.
- **FPJ** ([[knf-fermented-plant-juice|fermented plant juice]]) provides enzymes and growth factors that support the
  plant's ability to maintain healthy fruit skin elasticity.
- **LAB** ([[lactic-acid-bacteria]]) reinforces the plant's natural defenses and supports
  overall plant vitality during stress.
- **Seawater** ([[cho-seawater-and-fermented-seawater|sea minerals]]) supplies trace minerals that strengthen cell walls
  and improve the plant's stress resistance.

## The Role of BRV in Fruit Quality

The source highlights that brown rice vinegar in low concentrations
facilitates vegetative growth and affects the initial growth of
leaves. When BRV is applied to leaves, it helps them form the wax
layer, which creates thicker leaves and increases resistance to
diseases and insects. This same wax-forming property applies to
fruit skins, making them more elastic and resistant to cracking.

BRV also improves the flexibility of the tree and increases the
absorption ability when Natural Farming materials are sprayed. This
enhanced absorption means that all the other inputs in the crack
prevention treatment are more effectively taken up by the plant.

## Integration with Growth Stage Treatments

These fruit-specific treatments complement the broader growth stage
treatments described in the source:

1. **Vegetative growth stage**: Treatment promotes vegetative growth
  and increases crop volume and size. All NF inputs diluted at
  1:1000 in water.
2. **Changeover period**: Treatment supplies phosphoric acid demand
  during this critical transition. All NF inputs diluted at 1:1000.
3. **Reproductive growth stage**: Treatment provides additional
  calcium needed during fruit development. All NF inputs diluted
  at 1:1000.

The fruit ripeness and crack prevention treatments build upon these
foundational growth stage treatments with more targeted input
combinations and timing.

## Application Timing Principles

The source emphasizes precise timing for fruit treatments:

- Sweetness promotion requires two applications: 15 days and 2 days
  before harvest.
- Crack prevention requires proactive application during drought,
  before rain arrives.
- All NF foliar treatments should generally be applied in the
  evening or late afternoon to avoid UV damage to microorganisms
  and ensure maximum absorption.
- The basic dilution ratio across all treatments is 1:1000, ensuring
  consistent and safe application rates.

## Key Principles

- Fruit quality treatments require specific input combinations rather
  than single-input applications.
- Sweetness promotion is a two-stage process requiring application
  15 days and 2 days before harvest.
- Crack prevention must be proactive, applied during drought before
  rains arrive.
- BRV plays a key role in forming protective wax layers on fruit
  skins.
- Seawater at 30 ml per liter provides essential trace minerals for
  fruit development and stress resistance.
