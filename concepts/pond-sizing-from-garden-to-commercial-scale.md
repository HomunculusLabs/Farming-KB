## Pond Sizing from Garden to Commercial Scale

## Overview

Pond productivity and appropriate use are fundamentally determined by size. Mollison presents a systematic classification of ponds from 1 square metre to 500+ hectares, demonstrating that every scale has valuable applications — from household food security to full-time commercial income. Understanding the right pond size for a given purpose is essential to effective [[permaculture-design]].

## Ponds from 1–10 Square Metres

Very small garden ponds of 2–60 cm depth can be made from old baths, stock watering tanks, or plastic-lined holes with protective clay or earth covers. Pre-cast ponds in plastic, fibreglass, and concrete are sold in most areas.

### Shallow Pond Crops

In shallow ponds, the following thrive:
- **Chinese water chestnut** (*Eleocharis dulcis*)
- **Kangkong** (*Ipomoea aquatica*)
- **Watercress** (*Rorippa aquatica*)
- **Taro** (*Colocasia esculentus*) — a square metre yields 20–30 kg of starchy food
- **Frogs** — excellent garden predators (along with lizards); Hylid (tree) frogs inhabit plant leaves, feeding on insects by day and night
- **Small fish** — stocks of forage fish for larger ponds can be maintained in house ponds, together with mussels, useful molluscs, basic plant stocks, and shrimp

### Deeper Small Ponds

Deeper ponds will grow:
- **Indian water chestnut** (*Trapa natans*)
- **Lotus** (*Nelumbo*)
- **Arrowhead** (*Sagittaria*)

Boiled taro, cassava, or plantain can be added to fish food.

### The 10 Square Metre Benchmark

At approximately 10 square metres and 2 m deep, clear fibreglass or Kalwall® ponds — as used at the [[new-alchemy-institute]] (NAI, Cape Cod, USA) — produce fish and products valued at $4.50 to $17 per square foot (1984 dollars), amortizing costs in 3–5 years. These ponds yield fish, shrimp, and enriched water for semi-hydroponic crops.

**Design features of productive small ponds:**
- Placed in front of reflective (white) walls on white gravels to maximize solar input
- Increase yields and lengthen seasons of growth
- Useful as heat storage for night and winter heating
- Can be used in greenhouses or outdoors for much of the year
- In glasshouses, they give good yields and moderate temperature extremes while providing algae-rich waters for terrestrial plant beds — fish wastes (nitrates) are reduced in water and routed to plants

The Rodale organization has also made extensive trials on small tank production of fish.

### Market Opportunity

Mollison identifies a clear business opening for standard-sized ponds, equipment, and fish stocks for domestic pond culture — a field with few suppliers, most concentrating on decorative plants. Subsidiary uses include:
- Water stores for fire protection
- Tank supplies for gardens in dry periods
- Productive disposal systems for food wastes fed to omnivorous fish
- Recreational interest (aquariums recommended for workaholics as a relaxing hobby)

## Ponds from 10–100 Square Metres

Ponds of this size serve multiple functions:
- Rearing fish fry for sale or stocking
- Breeding forage fish
- Producing large quantities of vegetable crop
- Supplying aquaculture nurseries and aquariums with plant and animal stocks
- Creating fire-breaks, water reserves, and heat moderation

A **solar pond** of this size can supply house heating and hot water, or be built to heat a larger pond.

For a family, an intensively-managed fish pond of 100 square metres comes close to providing a full protein and vegetable resource if carefully designed and stocked with a beneficial polyculture. Aeration via a photovoltaic cell and air pump may be necessary for high yields, but the modest yields of 300–2,000 kg of protein that can realistically be expected are a significant contribution to food self-reliance.

These are specialty ponds requiring very careful planning and subsequent modification — analogous to the home garden. They can form part of a total wastewater system in line with a biogas or septic tank unit, growing a constant mass of green manures for garden mulch or biogas recycling.

## Ponds from 100–500 Square Metres

Chakroff (1982) and others regard ponds of this size (alone or in series) as **ideal for fish culture**. Easily harvested, netted, or drained, and capable of holding specific age, size, or species assemblies, such ponds give maximum management flexibility.

When devoted to a special crop, even one 500 square metre pond (or five 100 square metre ponds) can provide income from:
- Baitfish
- Aquarium fish
- Special plant crops
- Intensively managed fish (e.g., prawns) of high value

A nursery providing eggs, seeds, fry, or fingerlings to growers need not exceed this size. In 1983, incomes of approximately **$30,000** were possible from open-air ponds of this size stocked with prawns (Hawaii).

## Ponds from 500 Square Metres to 5 Hectares

About 5 hectares is estimated to provide a full (affluent) family income in high-value product. Ten to twenty prawn ponds (with ducks, mussels, and edge plants) constitute a full-time job — akin to but more easily managed than a market garden.

Many successful enterprises never exceed this size (a family farm). The scale permits:
- Multiple use and leaseholds of other activities
- Supply of a relatively large area of land crop
- Complete fire-break for homesteads and fuel forests
- Semi-intensive operations with cell-rearing establishments

Sewage ponds slightly in excess of 5 hectares will provide safe disposal for small towns.

## Ponds Greater Than 5 Hectares

Impoundments of 50–500 hectares are now not uncommon as extensive farms. Even a single impoundment designed for easy management gives a living to a family or families. At 10–20 hectares, with adequate design, harvest and occasional re-stocking are the main activities.

Companies and investors may try intensive management but are often limited by:
- Cost-benefit ceilings
- Food supply limitations
- Disease control challenges

At this scale, innovative leaseholds, recreational fishing, and new planning scales become feasible, including:
- Village or shoreline residence development
- Holiday homes
- Boating
- Sales of water itself

Every size order can include specialty ponds of the previous order, devoted to specific functions.

## Key Principles

1. **Match pond size to purpose** — household food security (10–100 m²), income supplement (100–500 m²), full-time income (0.5–5 ha), commercial enterprise (5+ ha)
2. **Start small and evolve** — ponds can grow from rice paddies or garden features into productive aquaculture systems
3. **Integrate multiple functions** — even the smallest pond serves as water storage, fire protection, food production, and wildlife habitat
4. **Use polyculture** at every scale for maximum yield per unit area
5. **Consider solar design** — reflective surfaces, insulation, and greenhouse integration dramatically increase small-pond productivity
