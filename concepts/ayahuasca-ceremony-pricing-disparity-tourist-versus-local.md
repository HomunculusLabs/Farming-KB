## The Documented Price Gap

The core data points are straightforward:

- **Tourist price**: ~$30 USD per ceremony
- **Local price**: ~$2 USD per ceremony
- **Markup factor**: approximately 15×

This pricing appears to be a community-wide norm in tourist-oriented
areas. The $30 figure represents what the market will bear from
international visitors, while $2 represents what locals can afford and
what the ceremony costs to produce in ingredients and labor.

## Practitioner Pricing Variation

Pricing correlates roughly with ethical orientation and practice style:

### Honorable Practitioner "E" at ~$10

Practitioner "E" charges approximately $10 per tourist — a third of the
standard rate but still five times the local rate. Described as
honorable, he conducts private family ceremonies rather than commercial
spectacles. His pricing reflects a middle ground: acknowledging the
tourist's ability to pay while avoiding pure extraction.

### Market-Rate Practitioners at ~$30

The standard tourist rate of $30 is the community norm charged by most
practitioners serving foreign visitors — the equilibrium price in a
