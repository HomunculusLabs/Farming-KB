### Method 3: Liquid Application

IMO #4 can be converted to liquid form for foliar and soil drench
applications. This is particularly effective for preventing Fusarium
wilt and damping-off in seedlings.

#### Liquidisation Process

1. Place IMO #4 in a hemp cloth bag or nylon stocking
2. Soak in water at a dilution ratio of 1:500 to 1:1,000
3. For 1,000 litres of water, use 1-2 kg of IMO #4
4. Apply to seedlings in the nursery or as a foliar spray
