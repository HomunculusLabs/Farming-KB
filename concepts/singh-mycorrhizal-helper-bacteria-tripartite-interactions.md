## Co-inoculation Effects in Practice

Field and greenhouse studies demonstrate that tripartite inoculations
consistently outperform dual or single inoculations. In forestry
applications, co-inoculation of ECM fungi (e.g., Laccaria bicolor or
Pisolithus tinctorius) with helper Pseudomonas strains improves seedling
survival and growth by 20 to 40 percent compared to fungal inoculation
alone. In agricultural systems, combining AM fungi (Rhizoglomus
intraradices) with Bacillus spp. increases crop yield under low-phosphorus
conditions and enhances resistance to soil-borne pathogens. The timing of
inoculation matters: MHB applied before or simultaneously with the fungal
inoculant generally produces the best results.
