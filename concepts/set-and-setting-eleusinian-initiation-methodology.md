# Set and Setting — Eleusinian Initiation Methodology

**Source:** The [[mckenna-food-of-the-gods-kykeon-and-eleusinian-mysteries]] exemplified what modern psychedelic researchers call "set and setting" — the deliberate preparation of psychological mindset and physical environment to maximize the therapeutic and spiritual potential of the [[lesser-and-greater-eleusinian-mysteries]] systematically optimized both variables.

## Set Preparation at Eleusis

The preparatory period for the Mysteries extended over months:

1. **The Lesser Mysteries**: Held in spring (Anthesterion), these were a mandatory preliminary that introduced candidates to the basic ritual framework

2. **Fasting and dietary restrictions**: Candidates abstained from certain foods (notably eggs) and fasted before the culminating night — see [[pelanos-barley-cake-and-fasting]] at Eleusis served a dual purpose: religious purification and pharmacological optimization. Modern [[sacred-road-pilgrimage-to-eleusis]] from Athens to Eleusis served as an extended liminal experience — a deliberate transition between the ordinary world and the sacred realm. Walking for hours alongside fellow initiates, crossing symbolic boundaries (the narrow bridge, the obscene insults), and arriving at a landscape saturated with mythological meaning prepared the initiates psychologically for the visionary experience to come.

This use of physical journey as psychological preparation parallels the [[sacred-road-pilgrimage-to-eleusis]]
