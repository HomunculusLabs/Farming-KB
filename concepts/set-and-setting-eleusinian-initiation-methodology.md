# Set and Setting — Eleusinian Initiation Methodology

**Source:** The [[the-road-to-eleusis-wasson-hofmann-ruck]], Chapters I, III — Wasson, Ruck

## Overview

The [[mckenna-food-of-the-gods-kykeon-and-eleusinian-mysteries]] exemplified what modern psychedelic researchers call "set and setting" — the deliberate preparation of psychological mindset and physical environment to maximize the therapeutic and spiritual potential of the [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]]. The [[lesser-and-greater-eleusinian-mysteries]] systematically optimized both variables.

## Set Preparation at Eleusis

The preparatory period for the Mysteries extended over months:

1. **The Lesser Mysteries**: Held in spring (Anthesterion), these were a mandatory preliminary that introduced candidates to the basic ritual framework

2. **Fasting and dietary restrictions**: Candidates abstained from certain foods (notably eggs) and fasted before the culminating night — see [[sacred-road-pilgrimage-to-eleusis]]**: The 20-kilometer walk from Athens to Eleusis created a liminal state, physically and psychologically separating initiates from ordinary life

4. **Sacred bathing**: Ritual purification at the sea upon arrival

5. **Secrecy and anticipation**: The oath of silence created powerful expectations and curiosity

## The Oath of Silence as Set Preparation

The requirement that initiates swear never to reveal the contents of the Mysteries functioned as a powerful set-preparation technique. By creating an aura of profound secrecy, the oath amplified expectations and created a psychological state of heightened receptivity. Initiates approached the Telesterion knowing that something extraordinary awaited them — a belief that itself contributed to the transformative power of the experience.

Modern research on [[sacred-road-pilgrimage-to-eleusis]] from Athens to Eleusis served as an extended liminal experience — a deliberate transition between the ordinary world and the sacred realm. Walking for hours alongside fellow initiates, crossing symbolic boundaries (the narrow bridge, the obscene insults), and arriving at a landscape saturated with mythological meaning prepared the initiates psychologically for the visionary experience to come.
