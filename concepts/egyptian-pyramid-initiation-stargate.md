# Egyptian Pyramid Initiation and the Stargate

The Great Pyramid at Gizeh served as the supreme site of initiation in [[allegro-plants-and-drugs-in-the-ancient-world]]—a multi-dimensional instrument used in conjunction with the amanita-muscaria mushroom to produce the ultimate death-rebirth experience. This rite, lasting three days, was the culmination of a lifetime of study within the Egyptian mystery schools and was later imitated (in degraded form) by the Rosicrucians, Freemasons, Templars, and other [[entheogenic-knowledge-secret-societies]].

## The Sacred Mushroom in Egyptian Symbolism

[[andrija-puharich]], in his book "The Sacred Mushroom (Key to the Door of Eternity)," described hieroglyphic and linguistic links between Egyptian symbolism and [[allegro-colour-and-consistency-of-the-amanita-muscaria]]. The typical mushroom pictogram and the **Ankh** are theorized as mushroom symbols. The **Aten** (winged sun disc) symbolizes the mushroom cap, with the gills stylized as wings.

The Egyptian **scarab beetle** contains [[allegro-cross-and-mushroom-symbolism]]: the wings represent gills, the sun-disc and moon-disc correspond to the mushroom cap, and the scarab is considered hermaphroditic or self-generating—much like the mushroom, which contains both male and female characteristics within a single organism. This is an appropriate symbol for the self-regenerating fungal kingdom.

## The Central Chamber as Sensory Deprivation Tank

The central chamber of the Great Pyramid—the King's Chamber—contained a large **tub** that functioned as a sensory deprivation tank. Enigmatic **salt deposits** found on the walls of the chamber suggest the use of buoyant salt water, creating an environment analogous to the isolation tanks used in modern consciousness research (as depicted in the film "Altered States").

The tub was part of a ceremony designed to prepare the initiate for entry into the heavens. Small portals in the chamber are theorized to have aligned with specific regions of the sky at particular times, potentially directing the initiate's spirit toward **Sirius** or **Orion**. The astronomical alignment of these openings with specific celestial positions at specific times suggests the pyramid was designed as a directional instrument for spiritual ascension.

## The Mystery Schools and Tiers of Learning

The Egyptian mystery schools employed a graduated system of advancement that became the model for all subsequent initiatory orders. The base period of study was **22 years** (according to Masonic tradition), encompassing mathematics, geometry, astronomy, and **astro-theology**—not merely astronomical observation but the esoteric principles of human evolution and the mythology of the gods.

Advancement required proving worthiness through scientific proficiency and passing severe trials, with secret oaths taken at each level and rewarded through the revealing of hidden knowledge. The ultimate motivation for this lifetime commitment was the hoped-for culmination in the highest initiation: the rite that could only take place in the central chamber of the Great Pyramid.

Later societies—Rosecrucians, Freemasons, Templars—elaborated upon this tiered system but in a degraded state, using a room instead of the pyramid and a simple coffin instead of the sarcophagus. These imitations are described as "only a shadow of the real thing, completely void of the key mechanisms that make this rite what it really is."

## Exclusionary Nature of the Highest Initiation

Despite the promise of the ultimate rite to those who dedicated their lives and swore oaths to proceed along the prescribed path, the Egyptian mystery schools were "supremely exclusionist in nature." Those who devoted their lives to advancement through the lower tiers may have served primarily as "servile knowledge gatherers and developers" rather than genuine candidates for the highest ceremony.

Egyptian hierarchies appear to have conferred the culmination ritual based on bloodline rather than merit. The initiate pools were essentially a support system for the priestly class, gathering and developing knowledge on behalf of those who were born into the position to receive the actual supreme initiation.

## The Pyramid as Multi-Dimensional Instrument

The Great Pyramid is described as the permanent record and physical representation of Khemitian (Egyptian) accomplishments in mathematics, geometry, astronomy, and astrology—all encoded in the structure itself. Beyond these known encodings, **anomalous energy fields** associated with the pyramidal structure indicate what the author calls "multi-dimensional fallout."

These energy fields are not merely architectural curiosities but functioned as mechanical instruments in the highest initiation rite. The full understanding of these phenomena "is NOT available to us—and in fact our understanding can only be termed as infantile in our times." The surface of the enigma remains barely scratched after thousands of years of study.

## The Three-Day Ordeal

The highest initiation rite was a **three-day ordeal** conducted in the central chamber:

**Day 1-2 (Death):** The initiate was placed in the sarcophagus and consumed the sacred Amanita muscaria, entering a death-like trance. The word **sarcophagus** literally translates as "flesh-eating"—and since the mushroom is referred to as "flesh" in most [[sacred-meal-ancient-traditions]], the initiate lay in the "flesh-eater" consuming the "flesh" of the gods. During this death-like state, the initiate descended into Hades (called **Amenta**—linguistically connected to **Amanita**) and performed the work of ministering to lost souls through charity and good deeds.

The experience of the abyss is described as every atom of the self being "dashed to pieces in the self-introspection and judgment that accompanies the plunge into that vortex." One's entire life flashes before consciousness, "leaving no stone unturned." The initiate learns "the humility of the adept, the infallibility of perfection and the inevitability of error." The Egyptian concept of the "heart light as a feather" becomes "the most valuable element in the universe."

**Day 3 (Rebirth):** At the close of the third night, the initiate was taken to the entrance of a gallery. At sunrise (or at a specific astronomical moment), the entranced initiate was brought out of the pyramid where "the beams of the rising sun struck directly the face" of the initiate. The sunlight was described as producing a "Born into the Light" experience: "Each atom of the body can be felt to change somehow as the light traveling through the body can be distinctly sensed each micrometer of the way." Once the light finished its course, "the complete body is engulfed" and "an incredible 'stepping through' experience" unfolds, "wherein the glorious beauty and ecstasy of the heavens" are revealed.

## The Slingshot Effect

The author compares this guided experience to a **slingshot**: the further back (down) you pull the projectile (initiate), the further (higher) it goes. The Amanita experience naturally oscillates between indefinable heavenly realms and sudden plunges to the depths, but the Egyptian rite deliberately held the initiate in the depths for three days before engineering the ascent through sunlight exposure at a precisely calculated moment.

This "slingshot" effect, combined with the pyramid's multi-dimensional energy fields, produced an experience of profound uniqueness. The author states: "As far as I know, it is the only Entheogen that produces this effect, and in fact this IS that proscribed experience that all the ecstatic religious writings refer to."

## The Hieroglyphic Symbol of the Priest

The hieroglyphic symbol for the Egyptian priest consists of: a horizontal line with a vertical line to the right, a connecting 45-degree line, a pitcher balancing atop the vertical line pointing left, and a jagged line (like a river) pouring from the pitcher. This symbol closely resembles the astronomical sign for **Aquarius**. The priest is the "water-bearer"—the one who knows and understands the true nature of the waters of life ([[soma]]) and can dispense this knowledge to those worthy of receiving it.

## Manley P. Hall's Description

Manley P. Hall (echoed by Peter Tompkins) described the rite: "The candidate passed through the mystic passageways and chambers of the great pyramid, entering its portals as men and coming forth as Gods. The candidate was laid in the great stone coffin and for three days his spirit—freed from its mortal coil—wandered at the gateways of eternity. His KA (as a bird) flew through the spiritual spheres of space. He discovered that the entire universe is life, the entire universe is progress, the entire universe is eternal growth. Realizing that his body was a house that he could slip out of and return to without death, he achieved actual immortality."

## Christianity's Debt to Egyptian Initiation

Christianity, which "owes most of its myths to Egyptology/Astro-theology," depicts this Egyptian ritual as: the mushroom-induced death of the god on the cross, burial in the tomb for three days (with descent into Hades to minister to souls), and resurrection when the stone is rolled away. The three-day death-rebirth-resurrection cycle in Christian theology [[maps]] directly onto the Egyptian pyramid initiation.

## Khemitology vs. Egyptology

The author notes that "Egyptology" is a dogma-entrenched, prejudiced study of Egyptian theology. The true name of the land is **KMT** or **Khemit**, and the proper term for its study should be **Khemitology**. Egypt (Khemit) is considered by many to be the true "Cradle of Civilization," predating Mesopotamia and containing advanced technological relics that have never been reproduced.

## Motivations: Compassion vs. Power

The initiatory system could be motivated by either compassion (the desire to understand life and help humanity progress) or the desire for power. The "sinister motive" of power-seeking has always been a threat to the integrity of the mystery schools. The same knowledge that could liberate humanity could also be hoarded as a mechanism of control—a tension that has defined the entire history of esoteric traditions.
