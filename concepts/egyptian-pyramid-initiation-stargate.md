# Egyptian Pyramid Initiation and the Stargate

The Great Pyramid at Gizeh served as the supreme site of initiation in [[entheogenic-knowledge-secret-societies]].

## The Sacred Mushroom in Egyptian Symbolism

[[allegro-colour-and-consistency-of-the-amanita-muscaria]]. The typical mushroom pictogram and the **Ankh** are theorized as mushroom symbols. The **Aten** (winged sun disc) symbolizes the mushroom cap, with the gills stylized as wings.

The Egyptian **scarab beetle** contains [[sacred-meal-ancient-traditions]], the initiate lay in the "flesh-eater" consuming the "flesh" of the gods. During this death-like state, the initiate descended into Hades (called **Amenta**—linguistically connected to **Amanita**) and performed the work of ministering to lost souls through charity and good deeds.

The experience of the abyss is described as every atom of the self being "dashed to pieces in the self-introspection and judgment that accompanies the plunge into that vortex." One's entire life flashes before consciousness, "leaving no stone unturned." The initiate learns "the humility of the adept, the infallibility of perfection and the inevitability of error." The Egyptian concept of the "heart light as a feather" becomes "the most valuable element in the universe."

**Day 3 (Rebirth):** At the close of the third night, the initiate was taken to the entrance of a gallery. At sunrise (or at a specific astronomical moment), the entranced initiate was brought out of the pyramid where "the beams of the rising sun struck directly the face" of the initiate. The sunlight was described as producing a "Born into the Light" experience: "Each atom of the body can be felt to change somehow as the light traveling through the body can be distinctly sensed each micrometer of the way." Once the light finished its course, "the complete body is engulfed" and "an incredible 'stepping through' experience" unfolds, "wherein the glorious beauty and ecstasy of the heavens" are revealed.

## The Slingshot Effect

The author compares this guided experience to a **slingshot**: the further back (down) you pull the projectile (initiate), the further (higher) it goes. The Amanita experience naturally oscillates between indefinable heavenly realms and sudden plunges to the depths, but the Egyptian rite deliberately held the initiate in the depths for three days before engineering the ascent through sunlight exposure at a precisely calculated moment.

This "slingshot" effect, combined with the pyramid's multi-dimensional energy fields, produced an experience of profound uniqueness. The author states: "As far as I know, it is the only Entheogen that produces this effect, and in fact this IS that proscribed experience that all the ecstatic religious writings refer to."

## The Hieroglyphic Symbol of the Priest

The hieroglyphic symbol for the Egyptian priest consists of: a horizontal line with a vertical line to the right, a connecting 45-degree line, a pitcher balancing atop the vertical line pointing left, and a jagged line (like a river) pouring from the pitcher. This symbol closely resembles the astronomical sign for **Aquarius**. The priest is the "water-bearer"—the one who knows and understands the true nature of the waters of life ([[maps]] directly onto the Egyptian pyramid initiation.

## Khemitology vs. Egyptology

The author notes that "Egyptology" is a dogma-entrenched, prejudiced study of Egyptian theology. The true name of the land is **KMT** or **Khemit**, and the proper term for its study should be **Khemitology**. Egypt (Khemit) is considered by many to be the true "Cradle of Civilization," predating Mesopotamia and containing advanced technological relics that have never been reproduced.

## Motivations: Compassion vs. Power

The initiatory system could be motivated by either compassion (the desire to understand life and help humanity progress) or the desire for power. The "sinister motive" of power-seeking has always been a threat to the integrity of the mystery schools. The same knowledge that could liberate humanity could also be hoarded as a mechanism of control—a tension that has defined the entire history of esoteric traditions.
