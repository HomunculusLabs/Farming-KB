### Germany

Germany addressed hallucinogenic mushrooms through its Narcotics Act (Betäubungsmittelgesetz):

- The Federal Institute for Drugs and Medical Devices issued guidance classifying mushrooms containing psilocybin as controlled substances under existing law
- No new legislation was required — the existing scheduling of psilocybin was interpreted to cover the mushrooms that contain it
- This administrative approach avoided the need for parliamentary action while achieving the same regulatory outcome
- Enforcement focused on retail outlets and internet suppliers
