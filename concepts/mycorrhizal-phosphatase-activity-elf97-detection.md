## Mycorrhizal Phosphatase Activity Detection by ELF-97 Substrate
## Overview
The ELF-97 endogenous phosphatase detection kit (Molecular Probes) is a
fluorescence-based method for assessing phosphatase activity associated with
[[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-fungal-diversity-patterns-distribution]] tissues at high resolution.
## Principle of the ELF Method
The ELF-97 phosphatase substrate
[2-(5-chloro-2-phosphoryloxyphenyl)-6-chloro-4-(3H)quinazolinone (CPPCQ)]
is normally slightly fluorescent in the blue range. Once its phosphate is
enzymatically removed, the substrate forms a crystalline precipitate that is
bright green fluorescent, indicating the site of phosphatase activity.
The precipitate has maximum emission at 530 nm, separated by more than 100 nm
from its maximum excitation at 345 nm, making the signal clearly
distinguishable from the autofluorescence of biological material such as
mycorrhizal hyphae.
## Detection of Alkaline vs. Acid Phosphatase
- **Alkaline phosphatase**: Detected using the alkaline detection buffer
  from the ELF kit. Note that the precipitate dissolves at pH above 8.0-8.5.
- **Acid phosphatase**: Detected by combining the substrate with an acid
  buffer (citrate buffer or acetate buffer). Citrate buffer can be set at
  specific pH values between 4.0-6.0; acetate buffer at pH 5.5.
## Protocol Summary
1. **Prepare buffers**: Acetate (pH 5.5, 0.2M) or citrate (pH 4.0-6.0,
   0.1M) for acid phosphatase; alkaline detection buffer for alkaline
   phosphatase
2. **Dilute ELF substrate**: Typically 20-fold dilution; test 1:10-1:100
   for different sample types
3. **Filter**: Vortex and pass through 0.22 mm syringe filter to remove
   pre-formed crystals
4. **Incubate**: 20-50 ml for mycelium, 40-100 ml for root sections,
   100-500 ml for root pieces; optimal reaction time approximately 15-30
   minutes (first 10 minutes typically show no precipitate)
5. **Wash**: Three changes of wash buffer (30 mM Tris, 1.5M NaCl, 0.05%
   Triton X-100, pH 8.0), 5 min each; pH must not exceed 8.0 or crystals
   dissolve
6. **Mount**: In kit mounting medium on slides; seal with nail varnish
   after drying
7. **Visualize**: Hoechst/DAPI longpass filter set; ELF precipitates appear
   as yellow-green fluorescent crystals; non-active tissue shows light blue
   fluorescence
## Sample Preparation
### Roots and Intraradical Mycelium
- Collect roots or [[fungal-hyphae]]
- The fluorescent precipitate is very photostable, supporting long
  visualization periods
- Do NOT mount samples in glycerol (ELF crystals dissolve within hours)
- Store prepared slides in the dark; signal is stable for extended periods
- Mounting medium may crack after several days, especially if viscous/aged
- Mini-sieves can be made from cut Eppendorf tubes with nylon mesh for
  gentle washing
