## JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) — Recipe and Natural Surfactant Science

## Overview

JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) is the cornerstone of the JADAM natural pesticide system. It is a plant-based surfactant made from canola oil and potassium hydroxide that serves as the critical delivery mechanism for all JADAM pesticide formulations.

## The Simplicity Principle

### Einstein"s Rule

"Albert Einstein said, "If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.' I realized that the closer you get to the truth, the simpler it becomes."

Cho applies this principle to pesticide formulation: the effective system must be simple enough for any farmer to use without specialized knowledge.

### Against the 400-Pesticide Model

"There are over 400 types of chemical pesticide for sale in Korea; they are all specified for each pest and disease. How can a single farmer make 400 different kinds of pesticides?"

The conventional approach—different products for different pests—creates an impossible knowledge burden for farmers. JADAM"s system uses a small number of versatile formulations that handle most pest situations.

## JWA Chemistry

### Ingredients

JWA is made from:
- **Canola oil**: 18 L (4.76 gal)
- **Caustic potash** (potassium hydroxide): 3.2 kg (7.05 lb)
- **Soft water**: 2.5 kg (0.66 gal)

The reaction between the oil and potassium hydroxide produces potassium salts of fatty acids—essentially a natural soap that functions as a surfactant.

### Production Process

1. Measure and pour water and caustic potash into a heat-resistant container
2. Melt the caustic potash by rolling the container on its side
3. Add canola oil and mix with electric drill until it reaches a "thin mayonnaise" consistency (~10 minutes)
4. Close lid and let stand for 1-2 days to complete saponification

### Cost

"It only costs 50 cents to make 1 L (33.8 oz). The amount of JWA you get by following below instructions is enough to be used for around 30 times (diluted in water to make pesticide of 500 L or 132 gal)."

## Biodegradability and Safety

### Natural vs. Synthetic Surfactants

"99.9% of natural soap were shown to biodegrade in just 5 days." JADAM"s plant-based surfactants biodegrade rapidly, unlike synthetic surfactants that "when released to the environment, cut off sunlight and block oxygen, killing many creatures in water."

### Health Concerns

Synthetic surfactants "accumulate in the human body; cause cancer, asthma, atopy or other chronic diseases." JADAM argues that the cumulative exposure to synthetic surfactants through repeated pesticide spraying is a significant but under-researched health risk.

### USDA Organic Compliance

"JADAM natural pesticides all comply with USDA National Organic Program. They are made from safe ingredients included in the National List."

## Usage Guidelines

### Dilution Rates

- **Mild problems**: 3 L JWA per 500 L water (0.6%)
- **Growing problems**: 5 L JWA per 500 L water (1.0%)
- **Serious problems**: Up to 15 L JWA per 500 L water (3.0%)
- **Minimum effective**: 1.5 L JWA per 500 L water (below this, dispersion fails)

### Application Notes

- **Soft water required**: JWA dissolves clearly only in soft water
- **Temperature sensitivity**: In cold temperatures, mix JWA in a small amount of water first before adding to the main tank
- **Growth suppression risk**: Continuous use above 10 L per 500 L may suppress growth in some crops
- **Fruit coating**: High concentrations may damage the white powder coating of some fruits

### Standalone Effectiveness

"JWA alone can control aphids, mites, and powdery mildew in their early stages." The wetting agent itself has pesticide properties, not just carrier functions.

### Multi-Purpose Use

"You can use JWA as detergent, dishwashing liquid, shampoo, bath soap, and other cleaning purposes." JWA"s versatility extends beyond agriculture to household cleaning.

### No Expiry Date

"JWA does not have an expiry date; in fact, its quality improves with age."
