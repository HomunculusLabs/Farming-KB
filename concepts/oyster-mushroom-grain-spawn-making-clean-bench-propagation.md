## Clean Bench (Laminar Flow Hood) Operation

Inoculation must be performed in a sterile environment to prevent contamination.
The clean bench (laminar flow hood) is the standard tool:

- HEPA-filtered air flows horizontally or vertically across the work surface,
  creating a particle-free zone
- The operator works within this zone, minimizing arm movement that might
  create turbulent air currents
- All tools (scalpels, loops, forceps) are flame-sterilized before each use
- Container openings are exposed for the minimum time necessary
- The operator wears clean gloves and a mask to prevent contamination from
  skin flora and respiratory droplets

Proper clean bench technique is more important than the sophistication of the
equipment. A well-maintained simple laminar flow hood in the hands of a
careful operator will outperform an expensive facility used carelessly.
