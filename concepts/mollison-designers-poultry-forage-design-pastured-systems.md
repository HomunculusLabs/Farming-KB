## Poultry Forage Design and Pastured Systems

Mollison presents poultry as the most accessible and productive livestock for
small-scale permaculture systems. Chickens convert forage, insects, and
kitchen waste into eggs and meat while providing manure, pest control, and
soil cultivation services. The key to productive poultry systems is designing
forage resources that meet their nutritional needs with minimal imported feed.

## Chicken Forage Species

Mollison identifies numerous plants that chickens actively forage:

**Herbaceous forage:**
- Comfrey ([[cichorium-intybus]]): Deep-rooted perennial providing protein and
  minerals through summer drought when grasses fail.
- Lucerne/alfalfa ([[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]]. Chickens prefer white clover for grazing.
- Plantain ([[caragana-arborescens]]): Nitrogen-fixing shrub
  producing edible seeds that poultry harvest directly.

## Pasture Management

Mollison advocates diverse multi-species pastures rather than monoculture
ryegrass:

- Seed poultry pastures with 10-15 species including grasses, legumes, and
  forbs to provide year-round nutrition
- Include deep-rooted species (chicory, plantain, lucerne) that stay green
  through dry periods
- Overseed annually with seasonal species to maintain diversity
- Rest pastures during wet periods to prevent pugging and soil damage

## Rotational Grazing Systems

Rotational grazing is essential to prevent overstocking and parasite buildup:

- Divide poultry forage areas into paddocks of 200-500 square metres
- Rotate flocks through paddocks on a 2-4 week cycle depending on stocking
  density and season
- Allow at least 30 days rest between rotations for pasture recovery and
  parasite life cycle interruption
- Use portable electric fencing to create flexible paddock boundaries
- Place mobile housing and water in each paddock to reduce movement stress
- Follow poultry with other livestock (cattle, sheep) to harvest the
  remaining forage and break parasite cycles

## Insect Protein from Compost and Mulch Piles

omnivores adapted to scratching for insects and seeds. Designing insect
