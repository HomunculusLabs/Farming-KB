# Geese as Grass Control and Integrated Poultry in Permaculture

## The Role of Geese in System Cycles

Mollison emphasises that every animal participates in nutrient
cycles. Geese eat grass, digest it, moult, produce waste products, and
add parasites, digestive enzymes, acids, and alkalis to the soil. The

## Geese in Orchard and Crop Systems

In the evolved permaculture system, geese are introduced at the
semi-hardy stage of establishment, alongside fish and shellfish. At
this stage, crops include some aquatic plant species and the system
begins to provide forage, firewood, and animal products.

Geese are particularly effective at:

- **Pond-edge grass control**: In established tropical systems, geese
  control pond-edge grasses that would otherwise encroach on water
  storage areas and reduce infiltration capacity.

- **Orchard understorey management**: Geese clip short swards beneath
  [[root-competition-plant-spacing-solomon]] from invasive grasses that
  weaken most deciduous [[gadd-mycelial-network-resilience-grazing-pressure]] where it is most needed.

- **Seasonal rotation**: Larger foragers such as geese can be grown
  seasonally in the evolving system. They require management rather
  than energy input, and have a variety of marketable yields.

- **Water access**: Geese need access to water for foraging and
  digestion. In dryland systems, they can be grazed on swale-planted
  forage and watered from earth tanks or shallow ponds.

## Geese in Arid and Dryland Systems

Mollison documents that geese thrive in arid areas, provided adequate
food and good nutrition are available. In desert systems, the main
limitation is not climate but the denudation of the total environment
