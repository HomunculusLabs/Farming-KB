# JADAM Mineral Liquid Fertilizer

Mineral JLF improves crop quality by providing trace elements and broad-spectrum
minerals. JADAM uses humus soil, phyllite rock powder, and sea water/salt as
mineral sources.

## Humus Soil JLF

Humus soil was formed at the bottoms of swamps or reservoirs millions of years
ago. It contains a wide range of nutrients for plant growth.

### Recipe
- Humus soil: 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- Some [[leaf-mold-composting]]

### Method
1. Place phyllite dust, wild grass, [[comparison-leaf-mold-composting-vs-hot-composting-method]] in container
2. Add water 10x the weight of phyllite powder
