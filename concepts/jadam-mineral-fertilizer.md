# JADAM Mineral Liquid Fertilizer

Mineral JLF improves crop quality by providing trace elements and broad-spectrum
minerals. JADAM uses humus soil, phyllite rock powder, and sea water/salt as
mineral sources.

## Humus Soil JLF

Humus soil was formed at the bottoms of swamps or reservoirs millions of years
ago. It contains a wide range of nutrients for plant growth.

### Recipe
- Humus soil: 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- Some wild grass
- A handful of [[leaf-mold-composting]]

### Method
1. Place humus soil in container with 10x its weight in water
2. Add leaf mold and wild grass
3. Close lid, leave at ambient temperature
4. Time: **3+ months**
5. Can also be used without long fermentation -- just soak in water

### Analysis (per 100 L: humus soil 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 8.1 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 0.42 |
| CaO % | 0.021 |
| Fe mg/kg | 4.757 |
| Mn mg/kg | 0.154 |

## Phyllite JLF

Phyllite is a metamorphic rock formed from deposits of plant and animal remains
from millions of years ago. It contains diverse minerals and is particularly
effective for improving taste and aroma.

### Recipe
- Phyllite dust: 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- Some wild grass
- Sea salt
- A handful of [[leaf-mold-composting]]

### Method
1. Place phyllite dust, wild grass, sea salt, and leaf mold in container
2. Add water 10x the weight of phyllite powder
3. Close lid, leave at ambient temperature
4. Time: **3+ months**
5. Stir occasionally (phyllite sinks)

### Analysis (per 100 L: phyllite 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 7.2 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 7.24 |
| CaO % | 0.354 |
| Fe mg/kg | 9.302 |
| Mn mg/kg | 0.571 |

## Sea Water / Sea Salt as Mineral Source

Sea water contains over 83 identified minerals -- the broadest spectrum
available. It is the most important mineral input in JADAM.

- Use seawater: 20-60 L per 500 L water
- Or sea salt: 0.5-1.5 kg per 500 L water
- Apply throughout growth, 3-4 times per month
- See [[jadam-sea-salt-use]] for detailed application

## General Application

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dilution | x30 to x500 |
| When to apply | Throughout growth |
| How to apply | Soil or foliar |
| What to expect | Improves quality |

## Using Many Rock Powders

Using the humus soil method, many different rock powders can be turned into
liquid fertilizers. The general ratio is 1 part rock powder to 10 parts water,
with leaf mold and wild grass added.
