## Leucaena leucocephala (Primary Species)

The most commercially exploited species, it takes three distinct forms:

- Common form: low shrub rarely exceeding 5 m, producing abundant
  foliage, flower, and seed
- Giant or Salvador type: tall forest tree over 20 m with little seed
  but fastest biomass rate (varieties include Hawaiian giants K8, K28,
  and El Salvador K67)
- Peru type: medium tree to 10 m branching close to the ground with
  abundant foliage and seed
