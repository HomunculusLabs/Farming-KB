### Cage vs. Free-Range
Caged systems allow intensive production but require regular feeding and cleaning. Free-range or colony systems reduce labour but need predator protection and more space. Guard dogs, fed and kennelled in the rabbit area, are sufficient control for fox predation.

## Rabbit Forage

### Green Forage
Rabbits thrive on a variety of green forages:
- Fresh grasses and clovers
- Dandelion
- Comfrey leaves
- Plantain
- Chickweed
- Raspberry and blackberry leaves
- Lucerne (alfalfa)
- Sweet potato vines

### Supplementary Feed
- Grains (oats, barley, wheat)
- Bran
- Root vegetables (carrots, beets)
- Hay (in winter or when fresh forage is scarce)
