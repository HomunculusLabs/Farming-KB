# Cannabis and Seborrhea

Seborrhea (seborrheic dermatitis) is a common, chronic inflammatory skin
condition that primarily affects areas of the body where sebaceous (oil)
glands are most abundant, including the scalp, face, and upper body. It is
characterized by excessive production of sebum, the oily/waxy substance
secreted by sebaceous glands, along with inflammation and flaking skin.
