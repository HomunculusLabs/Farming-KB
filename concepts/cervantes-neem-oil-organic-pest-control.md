## Mixing and Application

Mix neem oil just before using in water with a pH below 7. Use a
spreader-sticker to help the solution adhere to foliage. Agitate
constantly while using to keep the solution emulsified. Throw out any
excess mixture, as it does not store well once mixed.

Spray on foliage, especially under leaves, where mites live. Apply
every few days so hatching larvae will eat it immediately. Spray
heavily so mites have little choice but to eat it. For persistent
