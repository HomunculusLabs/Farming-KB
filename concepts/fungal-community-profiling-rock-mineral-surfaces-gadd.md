# Fungal Community Profiling on Rock and Mineral Surfaces

## Overview
Rock and mineral surfaces support complex and diverse fungal [[fungal-mineral-transformations-biogeochemical-cycles]] cycling, rock weathering, and soil formation. Modern
molecular techniques — particularly culture-independent methods — have revealed that
the true diversity of rock-dwelling (lithic) fungi far exceeds what traditional
culturing methods suggested. These communities are shaped by the specific mineralogy
and chemistry of their substrate, with distinct fungal populations inhabiting
different mineral types.

## Why Rock-Dwelling Fungi Matter
Fungi on rock and mineral surfaces are important agents of:
- **Bioweathering**: The biological breakdown of rocks and minerals, releasing
  nutrients into the soil ecosystem.
- **[[staycare-phytochelatins-and-intracellular-metal-detoxification-in-fungi]]**: Some lithic fungi can transform toxic metals, relevant
  to bioremediation of [[biodiversity-fungi-soil-fungal-communities]]:
- DNA is extracted directly from rock samples (e.g., sandstone).
- Fungal-specific primers amplify ribosomal DNA regions.
- The resulting banding patterns serve as community fingerprints.
- Considerable variation in banding patterns between samples from the same
  geographical origin indicates that rock-dwelling populations are highly diverse
  and their selection is a complex process.

### Ribosomal Intergenic Spacer Analysis (RISA and ARISA)
RISA exploits length differentiation in the ITS (Internal Transcribed Spacer)
region of ribosomal DNA to generate community profiles.

**Automated RISA (ARISA)** is a high-throughput version:
- Uses fluorescently labelled primers to amplify ITS region length variants.
- Products are analysed by automated capillary electrophoresis.
- Each peak in the electrophorogram corresponds to a DNA fragment called a
  ribotype or operational taxonomic unit (OTU).
- Community complexity ranges from ~30 to over 200 fungal peaks per profile.

**Advantages**:
- Highly sensitive and reproducible.
- Suitable for environmental [[cannabis-cultivar-microbial-community-effects]] nucleic acids:
- Fluorescently labelled PCR products are amplified from total environmental DNA.
- Products are digested by restriction enzymes.
- Fragments are analysed by a gene fragment sequencer.
- Produces an electrophorogram for rapid sample comparison.
