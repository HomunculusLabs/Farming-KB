## Cannabis and Atherosclerosis

**Zhao et al. (2010)**: "Activation of cannabinoid CB2 receptor ameliorates atherosclerosis associated with suppression of adhesion molecules." Journal of Cardiovascular Pharmacology.

CB2 receptor activation reduced atherosclerosis by suppressing adhesion molecules -- the molecular signals that cause inflammatory cells to stick to blood vessel walls. This is relevant because rheumatoid arthritis patients have significantly elevated cardiovascular risk due to systemic inflammation.

**CBD-2',6'-dimethyl ether** (Takeda et al., 2009): A cannabidiol derivative that is a highly potent and selective 15-lipoxygenase inhibitor. This enzyme pathway is involved in atherosclerotic plaque formation.

## Food-as-Medicine for Arthritis

### Curcumin (Turmeric)
- "From kitchen to clinic" -- extensive evidence for arthritis treatment (Goel et al., 2008)
- Natural products as a gold mine for arthritis treatment (Khanna et al., 2007)

### Black Cumin (Nigella sativa)
**Tekeoglu et al. (2007)**: "Effects of thymoquinone (volatile oil of black cumin) on rheumatoid arthritis in rat models." Thymoquinone, the active compound in black cumin seed oil, reduced arthritis severity in animal models.

### Myrrh
**Haffor (2010)**: "Effect of myrrh (Commiphora molmol) on leukocyte levels before and during healing from gastric ulcer or skin injury." Myrrh modulates immune response, relevant to autoimmune arthritis.

### Spices with Anti-Inflammatory Action
- **Cardamom**: Inhibits human platelet aggregation (Suneetha and Krishnakantha, 2005)
- **Garlic**: Antithrombotic effects (Fukao et al., 2007), antioxidant allicin (Okada et al., 2006)
- **Rosemary**: Potent antiglycative effects (Hsieh et al., 2007)
- **Ginger**: Protective effects on vascular endothelium (Wu et al., 2006)

## Psychosomatic Research

### Psychological Factors in Osteoarthritis
**Wise et al. (2010)**: "Psychological factors and their relation to osteoarthritis pain." Osteoarthritis and Cartilage 18(7):883-87.

Psychological factors significantly influence osteoarthritis pain experience, suggesting that mind-body approaches complement cannabinoid therapy.

### Psychosomatic Model for Rheumatoid Arthritis
**Schiel (1999)**: "A proposed psychosomatic etiologic model for rheumatoid arthritis." Medical Hypotheses 53(4):305-14.

Proposes a model connecting psychological stress, personality factors, and the onset of rheumatoid arthritis.

### Hostility and Atherosclerosis
**Miller et al. (1996)**: Meta-analytic review of hostility and physical health. **Whiteman et al. (2000)**: Personality and social predictors of atherosclerotic progression.

These studies document the connection between hostility, anger, and cardiovascular disease -- relevant because RA patients already have elevated cardiovascular risk.

### Optimism and Inflammation
**Roy et al. (2010)**: "Association of optimism and pessimism with inflammation and hemostasis in the Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis." Optimism was associated with lower inflammatory markers, suggesting that psychological interventions may reduce systemic inflammation.
