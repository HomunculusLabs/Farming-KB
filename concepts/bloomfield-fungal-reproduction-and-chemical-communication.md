### Hermaphroditism vs. Heterothallism

- **Heterothallic species**: require a mate for reproduction
- **Self-fertile hermaphrodites**: produce both antheridia and oogonia independently
- **Parthenogenetic species**: form oospores without any male contribution
- **"She-males"**: Achlya ambisexualis includes strains that offer oogonia or antheridia depending on the gender of available partners

Don Thomas compared sex distribution in seasonal ponds (dry in summer, freeze in winter) versus permanent ponds:
- Seasonal ponds: 10x more self-fertile hermaphrodites than heterothallic strains
- Permanent ponds: heterothallic strains far more common
- Conclusion: self-fertility is favored in unstable environments where fast reproduction outweighs genetic recombination benefits

### Wall Mechanics During Mating

The emergence of antheridia involves localized wall loosening:
- Endoglucanase enzymes are secreted in response to antheridiol
- These enzymes clip wall polymers, causing the hyphal wall to relax
- Relaxed wall allows new branches (antheridia) to emerge close to the hyphal tip
- Money and Hill demonstrated strong correlation between enzyme activity and wall strength

## Chytrid Sexual Reproduction

Chytrids have motile gametes (sperm and eggs) with flagella:
- **Pheromones**: manufactured from sesquiterpenes (not steroids)
- **Parisin**: male pheromone
- **Sirenin**: female pheromone — when detected, sperm cells stop wandering and swim in a corkscrew path toward the egg
- The sperm cell inactivates sirenin molecules as it swims through the pheromonal cloud, allowing it to take frequent directional bearings
- The parallel with the Sirens of Greek mythology — whose song lured sailors to their deaths — gives Chapter 7 its title

## Zygomycete Sexual Reproduction

### Zygospore Formation

When two compatible zygomycete strains meet on a food source:
1. Both strains cease producing asexual sporangia
2. Each produces hyphal branches that extend into the air and grow toward one another (a "cobra-like dance")
3. The branches fuse and a zygospore forms at the junction
4. Zygospores range from colorless spheres to jet-black warty nuggets
5. They are survival structures: melanin and other pigments render walls impervious to UV, heat, chemicals, and microbes

### Trisporic Acid and Cooperative Biosynthesis

Zygomycete pheromones use a unique signaling mechanism:
- Each strain releases a minute amount of a carotenoid-derived precursor
- Only the partner strain possesses the enzyme to convert this precursor into the active pheromone (trisporic acid)
- This "cooperative biosynthesis" ensures both partners must be present for sexual development
- Trisporic acid functions in water and is also transmitted through air
