## Active Constituents

A. blazei contains multiple bioactive beta-glucan structures:

- **Beta (1,3)-D-glucan** -- antitumour and immune enhancing
- **Beta (1,4)-D-glucan** -- antitumour and immune enhancing
- **Beta (1,6)-D-glucan** -- antitumour and immune enhancing
- **Proteoglucans** -- antitumour

Additional compounds include:
- **ATOM** -- a polysaccharide-protein complex (glucomannan-protein) prepared from Iwade Strain 101, with demonstrated activity against xenografts. "A novel polysaccharide-protein complex has been shown to be highly active against a variety of xenographs" (Ito et al., 1997).
- **AB-FP** -- a mannan-protein complex
- **FI1-a-beta and FIII2-beta** -- beta-glucan fractions

The variety of bioactive structures in A. blazei distinguishes it from other medicinal mushrooms that may rely on a single primary polysaccharide. This structural diversity may contribute to the breadth of its reported effects.
