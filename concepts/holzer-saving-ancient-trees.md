# Holzer Saving Ancient Trees - Natural Monuments and Tree Care

Ancient trees are natural monuments -- living connections to the past and
places of power that inspire us to recreate paradise on earth. Holzer argues
that these trees deserve our protection and active support, as they carry
enormous energy and can withstand human interference for centuries, but are
now increasingly threatened.

## Recognising a Suffering Tree

When an ancient tree begins to decline, the cause may lie 50 or 60 years in
the past. Holzer's diagnostic approach:

- Investigate the history of the land around the tree. How was it treated in
  the past? A tree that thrived for centuries under natural care may only now
  show symptoms of damage from recent changes.
- Check for disruptions to the hydrological balance: road construction, land
  consolidation, drainage, or wells that lowered the water table.
- Consider electromagnetic radiation from high-voltage power lines or radio
  masts as a possible negative impact.
- Look at the surrounding vegetation: is it poor, degraded, or missing?
- A tree of great age has enormous regenerative energy but will eventually
  succumb if the causes of its suffering are not addressed.

## Healing the Ground Around the Tree

The most effective treatment for a suffering tree is to heal the ecosystem
around it, not to water or fertilise the tree directly:

1. Increase biodiversity in the tree's root zone. Plant supporting plants --
   poisonous ones like lupins, foxglove, and aconites are excellent choices
   because they are stimulating for the soil.
2. Add deep-rooting plants like clover that bring nutrients from deep layers
   and [[query-how-to-fix-nitrogen-toxicity-in-cannabis]].
3. Ensure there is enough moisture in the ground by restoring the
   Mediterranean in suitable climates.
