## JADAM Vegetable Farming Techniques

## Overview

JADAM vegetable farming applies the same foundational principles as all [[jeavons-natural-pest-control]] icides -- with specific attention to [[jeavons-transplanting-techniques]], and vegetable-specific pest control.

## Soil Foundation for Vegetables

### Pre-Transplanting (Critical)
Apply soil foundation builder 3-4 times before transplanting. For 0.33 ha (0.8 ac):

- JMS -- 500 L (132 gal)
- Sea salt -- 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- [[jadam-methods]] ensure "root settlement will be robust as never before."

### Seedling Treatment
- Soak seedlings in JMS dilution or leaf mold solution for 2-3 minutes before planting
- Or use: JMS 100 L + water to make 500 L; soak 2-3 minutes

### Root Zone Problems
- In conventional farming, pepper roots are often found "coiled up" -- desperate to stay in the nursery soil
- This happens because the field soil is compacted and hostile to roots
- JADAM soil foundation eliminates this problem by creating ideal conditions before transplanting

## Crop Residue Management

Vegetable crop residues are the best fertilizer for that vegetable:
- **Cucumber leaves** are not a source of disease -- they contain nutrients for cucumbers. Thin thread-like roots approach decomposing leaves to absorb nutrients.
- **Tomato vines, pepper plants** -- all should be returned to the soil
- Do NOT remove or burn crop residues
- The practice of "field sanitation" (removing all residues) removes nutrients from the soil and increases pesticide dependency

## Greenhouse Management

### Key Differences from Open Fields
- No rainwater falls -- must add JMS with every irrigation
