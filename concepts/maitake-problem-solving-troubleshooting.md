## Surface Mycelial Coat Problems

The surface mycelial coat should not be grayish brown or excessively
thick. Proper light levels and appropriate spawn run duration are
essential for forming a coat conducive to primordia initiation.

## Failure of Primordia Initiation

Verify that your strain has the correct genotype capable of producing
well-formed fruiting bodies. Note that cold shock is not required to
initiate maitake primordia. Failure can often be traced to improper
light management during spawn run (see above).
