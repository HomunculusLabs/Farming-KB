# Rooting Hormones for Cannabis Cloning

Rooting hormones are essential tools for successful cannabis clone propagation.
They accelerate the formation of undifferentiated cells at the cut site, which
then transform into root cells. According to Jorge Cervantes, understanding the
[[22s-23s-homobrassinolide-chemistry-and-synthesis]] proper application of rooting hormones is critical to achieving
high clone survival rates.

## How Rooting Hormones Work

When a cutting is taken, the plant begins dispatching its own natural rooting
hormones to the wound site. These arrive in full force after about one week.
Artificial rooting hormones fill the gap until natural hormone production takes
over. The hormones stimulate undifferentiated cells to form root tissue,
dramatically speeding up the rooting process and improving success rates.

Three primary synthetic substances stimulate undifferentiated cell growth:

- **NAA** (naphthaleneacetic acid)
- **IBA** (indolebutyric acid)
- **2,4-DPA** (2,4-dichlorophenoxyacetic acid)

Commercial rooting products contain one, two, or all three of these active
ingredients and often include a fungicide to prevent damping-off disease.

## Forms of Rooting Hormones

### Liquid Hormones

Liquid rooting hormones are among the most versatile and consistent. They
penetrate stems evenly and can be mixed at different concentrations. Always use
the most dilute concentration for softwood cannabis cuttings. Liquid hormones
can be applied as a quick dip or as a soaking solution.

Apply any rooting hormone containing IBA only once. If exceeded in
concentration or duration, IBA applications impair root formation rather than
helping it. Give cuttings a 5 to 15 second dip in concentrated solutions of
IBA and NAA (500 to 20,000 ppm).

### Gel Hormones

Gels have become very popular because they keep root-inducing hormones evenly
distributed along the subterranean stem. Once applied, gels hold and stay with
the stem longer than liquids or powders. They are easy to [[use-and-value-diversity-permaculture]] practical,
but are not [[water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp]]. Some gels seal cutting tissues, reducing the chance
of infection and air embolisms.

### Powder Hormones

Rooting powders are a mixture of talc and IBA and/or NAA. They are the least
expensive option but adhere inconsistently to stems and penetrate poorly,
which can cause uneven root growth and lower survival rates. To use, roll the
moistened [[allegro-end-of-a-road-motivation]] the cutting in powder. Apply a thick, even coat. Tap or
scrape off excess powder, as too much hormone can hinder root growth.

## Popular Rooting Products

Cervantes documents several well-known products:

- **Clonex** - The first cloning gel; contains IBA, NAA, and anti-bacterial
  agents
- **Dip 'N Grow** - IBA-based liquid; very high success rate
- **Olivia's Cloning [[ultra-low-cost-jadam-microorganism-solution-and-soil-foundation]] Gel** - Popular liquid and gel options

- **Rootox** - Known for reliable rooting results
- **Nitrozyme** - Seaweed extract with cytokinins, auxins, enzymes, and
  gibberellins; spray on [[cervantes-mother-plants]] two weeks
  before taking cuttings

## Concentration Guidelines

To determine rooting hormone concentration in parts per million (ppm), multiply
the percentage listed by the manufacturer by 10,000. For example, a product
with 0.9% IBA contains 9,000 ppm IBA.

Recommended approaches:
- **Quick dip**: 500 to 20,000 ppm IBA/NAA for 5 to 15 seconds
- **Foliar spray**: Dilute IBA at 50 to 90 ppm; spray just enough to cover
  leaves without dripping (overdose slows growth and can kill clones)
- **Soak method**: 20 to 200 ppm IBA/NAA for 24 hours (less commonly used)

## Natural Alternative: Willow Water

An all-natural root-inducing substance is willow water. The specific substance
in willow trees that promotes rooting is unknown, but experiments have proven
that willow water promotes about 20 percent more roots than plain water. It
can be mixed with commercial rooting hormones [[staycare-fungal-consortia-for-enhanced-pollutant-removal]] results.

To make willow water:
1. Find any willow tree and remove branches about 1.5 inches in diameter
2. Remove leaves and cut branches into one-inch lengths
3. Place sticks upright in a jar and fill with water
4. Let soak for 24 hours
5. Pour off the willow water and soak marijuana clones for 24 hours
6. Plant cuttings in rooting medium

## Application [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-inoculum-production-best-practices]]

1. **Keep the work area clean** - Wash surfaces and tools before starting
2. **Prepare the medium first** - Have rooting cubes or medium ready
3. **Make holes in advance** - Prevent scraping off hormone during insertion
4. **Use the right concentration** - Softer cuttings need less hormone
5. **Apply only once** - Excess hormone impairs root formation
6. **Avoid contamination** - Pour powder into a separate container; discard
   excess after use

## Trichoderma and Root Enhancement

Several commercial products such as Clonex Root Matrix contain Trichoderma
bacteria. This beneficial bacterium causes roots to grow faster and absorb
nutrients more efficiently. Products containing Vitamin B1 also help ease
transplant shock and promote root development.
