## Practical Identification Tips

Based on Gartz's extensive field experience, several practical guidelines
emerge for safe mushroom identification:

**Habitat knowledge is essential.** Knowing what species typically grow in
a given environment -- pasture, wood debris, bark mulch, dung -- is a
fundamental first step in identification. Panaeolus species in particular
tend to invade commercial cultivation areas, fruiting before the
cultivated species and creating opportunities for misidentification.

**Spore prints provide important but limited information.** A spore print
is made by placing the cap on black or white paper (depending on expected
spore color) for several hours under a glass vessel. However, spores from
