## Alpha and Beta Diversity in Cannabis Root Microbiomes

## Overview

Diversity analysis of Cannabis-associated [[cannabis-alpha-diversity-gradient-bulk-soil-rhizosphere-endorhiza]] peaks in bulk soil and declines
progressively through the rhizosphere and into the endorhiza, while beta
diversity shows that the greatest community differences occur between
soil compartments rather than between cultivars within the same compartment.
These patterns are consistent with the two-tier [[cultivar-cannabis-microbiome-two-tier-selection-model]] experiment, alpha diversity showed a clear
declining gradient across the three sample types:

- **Bulk soil**: chao1 mean = 4947, SD = 717
- **Rhizosphere**: chao1 mean = 4525, SD = 542
- **Endorhiza**: chao1 mean = 3321, SD = 420

The decline from bulk soil to rhizosphere was modest (~8.5%), while the
decline from rhizosphere to endorhiza was dramatic (~26.6%). This indicates
that the root tissue boundary imposes a much stronger selective filter than
the rhizosphere transition from bulk soil.

### Soil Type Effects on Alpha Diversity

Alpha diversity was significantly higher in the Mo-Bio (MB) soil compared
to the Orange County (OC) soil for bulk soil and rhizosphere samples:

- MB bulk soil: chao1 = 5597 (SD = 89) vs OC bulk soil: chao1 = 4296
  (SD = 85)
- MB rhizosphere: chao1 = 4859 (SD = 286) vs OC rhizosphere: chao1 = 3913
  (SD = 290)

However, endorhiza diversity was not significantly different between soil
types (MB: chao1 = 3325, SD = 517 vs OC: similar). This suggests that
while the soil determines the diversity of the available microbial pool,
the endorhiza imposes a relatively consistent ceiling on diversity
regardless of soil origin.

### Interpretation

The declining alpha [[weighted-vs-unweighted-unifrac-cannabis-strain-microbiome]]
distances consistently showed that endorhiza and bulk soil samples were
the most distinct from other sample types, while rhizosphere samples were
intermediate:

- Endorhiza vs all others: ADONIS R² = 0.26, p = 0.001 (unweighted)
- Bulk soil vs all others: ADONIS R² = 0.14, p = 0.001 (unweighted)
- Rhizosphere vs all others: ADONIS R² = 0.07, p = 0.07 (not significant)

This pattern held across both experiments and both [[weighted-unifrac-strain-clustering-cannabis-endorhiza-community-structure]] showing even stronger differentiation for endorhiza
(R² = 0.59, p = 0.001) and bulk soil (R² = 0.29, p = 0.004).

### Cultivar Differentiation

Division of communities by strain was only significant within endorhiza
samples, not in rhizosphere or bulk soil:

- Endorhiza by strain: weighted R² = 0.59, p = 0.004; unweighted R² =
  0.39, p = 0.003
- All samples by strain: weighted R² = 0.11, p = 0.25 (not significant)
