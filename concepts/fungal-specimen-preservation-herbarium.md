## Preservation Methods for Different Fungal Groups

### Sporocarps (Mushrooms, Bracket Fungi, Puffballs)

Sporocarps are typically air dried. Fresh specimens are sliced thinly to
facilitate rapid drying and prevent internal decomposition. Moderate
temperatures (20–30°C) with adequate airflow preserve both morphological
integrity and viable DNA. Oven drying at higher temperatures damages specimens
and degrades DNA.

## Specimen Transactions

Herbaria manage specimen flow through several transaction types:

- **Loans**: Temporary transfer for research, governed by formal agreements
  specifying use conditions, duration, and return requirements.
- **Exchanges**: Reciprocal transfers of duplicates between institutions.
- **Gifts**: Permanent transfers without expectation of return material.
- **Purchases**: Acquisition through commercial or private channels.
- **Staff accessions**: Specimens collected by herbarium staff during field
  work or received through research collaborations.
