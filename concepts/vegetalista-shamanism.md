# Vegetalista Shamanism

The vegetalista tradition is a system of Amazonian plant medicine and
[[planetary-spirits-spiritual-healing]] practiced throughout the Peruvian Amazon, particularly
along the Ucayali River. A vegetalista is a healer who derives their
knowledge and power from plants — specifically from the plant-teachers
that reveal their medicinal and spiritual properties through ayahuasca
visions and dietary discipline. The tradition represents a sophisticated
system of ethnobotanical knowledge developed over centuries by
indigenous and mestizo peoples of the Amazon basin.

## The Three Degrees of Vegetalista Medicine

The vegetalista tradition recognizes three hierarchical degrees of
mastery, each requiring progressively more demanding dietary discipline
and plant knowledge:

1. **Muraya**: The first advanced degree. A muraya has mastered the
   basic plant medicines and can perform standard healings. They have
   established relationships with several plant spirits and learned
   their [[icaros-power-songs]]).

2. **Sumi** (sumiruna): The second degree, representing deeper mastery.
   Sumis command greater spiritual power and can perform more complex
   healings, including those involving sorcery and spiritual warfare.

3. **Banco** (banco runa): The highest degree of traditional
   vegetalista medicine. Bancos possess the most extensive knowledge
   of plant spirits and the greatest spiritual authority. They are
   sought for the most difficult cases of illness and sorcery.

Progression through these ranks requires years of strict dietary
isolation (dieta) under the guidance of an established vegetalista.
There is no shortcut — the knowledge cannot be purchased or learned
from books; it must be earned through direct spiritual experience with
the plant-teachers.

## The Dieta: Foundation of Vegetalista Training

The dieta is the central practice through which vegetalistas acquire
their knowledge. During a dieta, the practitioner isolates themselves
in the forest and consumes a specific plant while following strict
rules: sexual abstinence, avoidance of salt, sugar, fat, pork, and
alcohol, minimal social contact, and purification through tobacco and
bathing. The dieta typically lasts weeks or months.

During this period, the plant spirit appears in dreams and ayahuasca
visions, teaching the practitioner the plant's [[fukuoka-eastern-western-medicine-healing-world-crisis]]. They exchange stories
about supernatural encounters. The vegetalista advises on dietary
requirements for the ceremony.

**Drinking**: The vegetalista sings an icaro over the brew to give it
strength, then serves it in a gourd. Each person prays to the
ayahuasca spirit before drinking: "Grandfather, please, let me see all
those things I want to see." The person drinks from the specific spot
where the vegetalista blew on the gourd.

**The Session**: Participants sit in ritual order in complete darkness.
The vegetalista blows tobacco smoke on each person's head, sometimes
adding perfume or camphorated water. Yawning indicates the onset of
visions — a single yawn means beautiful visions; multiple yawns
suggest strong visions or approaching enemies. Contractions in the
left arm signal sorcery threats; in the right arm, the visions will
reveal what was asked.

**Icaros**: The vegetalista begins [[metzner-shamanic-singing-icaros-rhythm-and-entrainment]] to bring forth the
visions, sometimes preceded by a prayer to Jesus or another divinity
depending on their religious affiliation. The icaros guide and shape
the visionary experience.
