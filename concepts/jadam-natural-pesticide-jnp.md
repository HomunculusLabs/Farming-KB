## JADAM Natural Pesticide (JNP)

JNP is the combined pesticide system of [[jadam-methods]]. It is built from four ingredients -- [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]], [[jadam-sulfur-js]], [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]], and [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] -- mixed in different proportions for different targets. One pesticide system replaces hundreds of commercial products. Costs ~$7-9 per acre per year (vs ~$350 for chemical pesticides).

## Core Principle

Without JWA, there is no pesticide. JWA is the center of everything -- it provides coating, penetration and delivery. Increasing JWA makes any formulation stronger. Controlling powdery mildew and aphids is the touchstone: succeed at those two and you can control 90% of all pest and disease problems.

## Essential Rules

1. **Always use soft water** -- test with JWA (clear + lots of foam = good)
2. **Always do mixture test** -- 0.5L water + 5cc of each ingredient, shake, check for foam + no clotting
3. **Always do concentration test** -- spray small area first, check after 2-3 days
4. **Spray when humid** -- morning dew, evening, cloudy days. Dry conditions = rapid evaporation = no effect
5. **Filter everything** -- use only clear liquids, no particles or sediments
6. **Mix order**: Half-fill tank with water, add ingredients ONE BY ONE stirring each time, top up with water. Never mix undiluted JWA and JS directly (they clot)
7. **Spray thoroughly** -- fully wet the target. Use fine-particle nozzles
8. **Use up same day** -- never store leftover spray, especially with JS

## Formulation Quick Reference (per 500L / 132 gal)

### Disease Control (Germicides)

**Powdery mildew, downy mildew, fungus**:
- JWA 3L + JS 1.2L

**Canker, black spot, pear rust, brown blotch**:
- JWA 5L + JS 1.5L
- Severe canker: JWA 10L + JS 2L, every 2 days, 2-3 applications

### Insect Control (Insecticides)

**Aphids and mites**:
- JWA 5L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L
- Add JS 1-2L for simultaneous disease control
- Add 0.5kg fine red clay powder for extra effect

**Moths** (tobacco moth, armyworm, diamondback, fruitworm):
- JWA 8L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 15L
- Spray at sunset, re-spray next morning for heavy infestation
- Hairy pests (slug moth): spray repeatedly, wet thoroughly

**Planthoppers, leafhoppers, mulberry suckers**:
- JWA 10L + gingko JHS 15L

**Stinkbugs, thrips, whiteflies, fruit flies** (strongest JNP):
- JWA 10L + KPFR JHS 15L
- Emergency: increase to JWA 15L + JHS 20L

### All-Purpose (Pests + Diseases)

**Standard all-purpose**:
- JS 1.5L + JWA 6L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L + KPFR JHS 10L

**Rice comprehensive** (planthopper, borer, armyworm, blast, sheath blight):
- JWA 5L + JS 2L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L + KPFR JHS 5L

### Prevention (Before Outbreak)

**JMS prevention spray**:
- JMS 20L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L + gingko JHS 5L + JWA 3L
- Use regularly. Replace JMS with JS if disease appears; replace JHS with KPFR if pests appear

### Winter Dormant Spray (Fruit Trees)

- JWA 10L + JS 5L + fine red clay powder 2kg per 500L
- Apply after leaf fall, 1-2x between late Feb and budding
- For evergreens, reduce JWA to below 2L

### Slugs and Snails

- JWA 3L + NaOH 1.5kg (dissolve in small water first) per 500L
- **DANGER: do not let contact skin when spraying**
- Add JHS for aphid/mite/moth control too

### Rice Water Weevil (Surface-Spreading Agent)

- JWA 3L + JS 0.5L + canola oil 3L (drill-mix 10 min) in 5L water
- Sprinkle undiluted on water surface at dawn
- 1L covers 0.1 ha (1/4 acre)

## What Goes With What

| Ingredient | Mixes well with | Does NOT mix well with |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| JWA | JS, JHS, JMS, alcohol, chemical pesticides (test first) | Vinegar, wood vinegar, sugar/molasses liquids, seawater, Delan-family fungicides |
| JS | JWA, JHS | Never mix undiluted with JWA -- add to water separately |
| JHS | JWA, JS, JMS | Vinegar/wood vinegar extracts (use boiled JHS instead) |
| JMS | JWA, JHS | Over 20L JMS breaks down JWA |

## Application Timing

- **Frequency**: Apply in 2-day shorter cycles than chemical pesticides
- **Season**: Intensive in early growth (soft leaves are vulnerable), before rain for open fields
- **Time of day**: Early morning (spring/autumn), evening (summer), humid/cloudy anytime
- **Multiple applications**: For heavy infestation without JS, can spray 2-3x per day or every 1-2 days

## Resistance Management

JADAM pesticides do not develop resistance in pests because:
- Multiple active compounds from different herbs (not a single synthetic molecule)
- JMS adds microbial competition (crowding out, not killing)
- JWA itself has pesticide action that pests do not adapt to
- Rotate between different JHS types (Jerusalem artichoke, gingko, KPFR)

## Sensitivity Guide

**Sulfur-sensitive** (start JS at 0.5L, increase carefully): grapes, persimmons, walnuts, perilla, greenhouse crops
**Generally resistant**: apple, pepper
**JWA-sensitive** (don't exceed 10L continuously): crops with white powdery fruit coating

## Cost Reference

Bongha village, Korea (320 acres, 160 households): $7/acre/year for 4 applications of JNP. That is ~1/50 the cost of chemical pesticides.
