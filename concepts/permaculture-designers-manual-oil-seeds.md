# Oil Seeds

updated: 2026-04-18

From: [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-pattern-understanding-in-permaculture]]



## Overview

Oilseed crops provide edible oils, fuel, and industrial raw materials in permaculture systems. Bees carry out the essential service of pollination for a wide variety of food, oil, fruit, and seed crops such as mustard, clovers, buckwheat, most smallfruit, apples, and grain legumes. The Designer's Manual references oil crops as part of regional plantation systems and specialist crop potential.



## Principal Oilseed Crops

### Tropical and Subtropical Oil Crops
- **Coconut** (Cocos nucifera) - copra production for coconut oil; a staple food and trade crop in the humid tropics alongside plantain, banana, cassava, yams, and taro
- **Oil palm** (Elaeis guineensis) - palm oil production; noted as a trade crop in tropical Africa
- **Peanut/Groundnut** (Arachis hypogaea) - both food and oil; a staple and plantation crop in African savannahs
- **Sesame** (Sesamum indicum) - oil and food seed; suited to tropical and subtropical climates
- **Castor** (Ricinus communis) - industrial oil; chalmougra oil noted among specialist crop potential
- **Chalmougra** (Hydnocarpus spp.) - specialist oil crop for remote locations

### Temperate Oil Crops
- **Sunflower** (Helianthus annuus) - oil and food seed; used as a garden hedge species alongside lavender and rosemary
- **Rapeseed/Canola** (Brassica napus) - edible oil and biodiesel
- **Linseed/Flax** (Linum usitatissimum) - linseed oil and fibre
- **Mustard** (Brassica spp.) - oil and condiment; pollinated by bees
- **Buckwheat** (Fagopyrum esculentum) - oil and grain; bee forage
- **Walnut** (Juglans spp.) - walnut oil from tree crops
- **Olive** (Olea europaea) - Mediterranean climate oil tree
- **Pecan and macadamia** - high-value nut oils in tree crop systems



## Specialist Crop Potential

There is a wide range of specialist crop potential for oils, from rubber (Hevea), betel-nut, chalmougra oil, and chicle to essential oils and medicinals. Many are suited to primary processing in remote locations, or conversion to commercial-quality end-products. The high value of processed product often justifies small-scale production.



## Oil Processing

Permaculture systems emphasise on-site or community-scale processing of oilseeds:
- Cold pressing for edible oils
- Seed cleaning using vibrating tables with screens
- Fukuoka-style seed-clay mixes pressed through mincers
- Community-scale processing facilities in bioregional planning



## Integration with Other Systems

Oil crops integrate with other permaculture elements:
- Oilseed meals as animal feed after pressing
- Press cakes as fertiliser or compost input
- Oil crops in rotation with legumes and cereals
- Oil-producing trees in food forest systems
- Bee pollination services essential for many oilseed crops
- Sunflower hedges providing garden windbreak and food



## Design Considerations

- Select oil crops suited to local climate (tropical palms vs temperate seeds)
- Plan for processing equipment and storage
- Integrate oilseed crops into polyculture rotations
- Use oil-producing trees in Zone 3-4 food forests
- Consider dual-purpose crops (oil + feed, oil + fibre)
- Plan for pollinator habitat near oilseed fields
