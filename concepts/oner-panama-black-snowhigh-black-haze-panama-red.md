## Phenotype Variation

Panama Black expresses several distinct phenotypes that span
across the whole spectrum of its genetic makeup. All phenotypes
will grow tall, but the specific expression varies significantly
between plants, making each grow a unique exploration of the
strain's genetic potential.

### Panama Red Phenotype

The Panama Red-dominant phenotypes grow especially tall and
lanky, with many bud sites distributed all along their branches.
These phenotypes most closely resemble the classic Latin American
sativas of the 1960s and 1970s. The tall structure means these
plants may need height management in indoor environments. The
bud sites eventually fill out into very dense, resinous flowers
that may require staking or support during the late flowering
stage to prevent branches from bending under the weight.

### Purple Haze Phenotype

The Purple Haze phenotypes yield large, incredibly purple buds
that look like they are crusted in sugar. Along with their
diamond shape and glittering crystal covering, the buds appear
as though they could be set into a gold ring and sold as
jewelry. These phenotypes are the most visually striking and
likely to attract attention from collectors and connoisseurs.

### Recessive Red Phenotype

Some phenotypes exhibit recessive red traits, making them look
just like the original Panama Red strains from the 1960s and
1970s. These plants carry the heritage of the old-school
Panama Red in both appearance and effect, offering a nostalgic
connection to the landrace sativas that defined an era of
cannabis culture.
