# Cannabis for Neurological Conditions

Neurology is one of the most promising areas for medical cannabis research. The strongest evidence exists for epilepsy (specifically CBD), with growing evidence for multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's, and other conditions.

### Treatment-Resistant Epilepsy
- **Evidence level: Strong** (FDA-approved Epidiolex for Dravet and Lennox-Gastaut syndromes)
- CBD reduces seizure frequency by 30-50% in treatment-resistant patients
- Mechanism: CBD modulates GABA and glutamate signaling, reduces neuronal excitability
- Does not produce psychoactive effects
- Dosage: 10-25mg/kg/day CBD (pharmaceutical grade)
- Whole-plant CBD extracts show additional benefits beyond isolated CBD (entourage effect)

### Multiple Sclerosis
- **Evidence level: Strong** for spasticity (Sativex approved in many countries)
- Moderate evidence for pain, bladder dysfunction, sleep quality
- THC:CBD 1:1 ratio is most studied (Sativex)
- Reduces muscle spasticity and spasms
- May slow neurodegeneration via neuroprotective properties

### Parkinson's Disease
- **Evidence level: Mixed**
- May reduce tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia in some patients
- THC can worsen motor symptoms in others — highly individual
- CBD shows neuroprotective properties in preclinical models
- Best for non-motor symptoms: sleep, anxiety, pain

### Alzheimer's Disease
- **Evidence level: Preclinical only**
- THC may reduce amyloid-beta plaque accumulation
- May reduce agitation and behavioral symptoms in patients
- No evidence for prevention or disease modification

### Tourette Syndrome
- **Evidence level: Moderate**
- THC reduces tic frequency and severity
- 5-10mg THC shows rapid reduction in tics
- May be useful when conventional medications fail

### Migraine
- **Evidence level: Moderate**
- Both prevention and acute treatment
- Cannabis may reduce migraine frequency by 50%+
- Best evidence for migraine with cannabis is for chronic migraine

### Spinal Cord Injury
- **Evidence level: Limited**
- May reduce spasticity, neuropathic pain below injury level
- THC:CBD combinations most studied
