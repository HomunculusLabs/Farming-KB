## Wildlife Corridors

Zone 5 can serve as a wildlife corridor that may cross other zones:

- **Observe animal patterns**: If deer or other wildlife regularly cross a specific spot, fence off their path separately from the rest
- **Create an "animal highway"**: Leave the path wild with natural forage
- **Separation from gardens**: Fencing the corridor keeps animals from eating crops while respecting their movement patterns
- **Coexistence**: Rather than constantly fighting animals eating gardens, accommodate their natural routes

## The Temptation to Intervene

The biggest challenge with Zone 5 is resisting the urge to manage it:

- **Do not "improve" it**: Let nature take its course
- **Do not harvest**: Leave dead wood, fallen trees, and all natural processes intact
- **Do not plant**: Allow natural succession to occur
- **Do not clear trails**: Unless absolutely necessary for fire access
