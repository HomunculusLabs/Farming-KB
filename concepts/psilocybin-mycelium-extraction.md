# Psilocybin Mycelium Extraction and Dosage

Methanol extraction of psilocybin/psilocin from cultivated mycelium, dosing guidelines, and potency factors, from Gottlieb's Psilocybin Producer's Guide (1976). See [[psilocybin-mushroom-chemistry]] for analytical chemistry details and [[psilocybin-mushroom-effects-and-safety]] for clinical safety data.

Related: [[psilocybin-liquid-culture]], [[psilocybin-mushroom-cultivation]], [[psilocybin-mushroom-potency]]

## Extraction Method (Gottlieb, 1976)

### Materials
- Dried mycelial material
- Absolute methanol
- Hot water bath
- Buchner funnel with filter paper
- Celite (filter aid)
- Suction filtration apparatus
- Keller's Reagent (glacial acetic acid, ferrous chloride, concentrated sulfuric acid)
- p-dimethylaminobenzaldehyde
- 1N hydrochloric acid

### Procedure

1. **Extraction**: Crumble and pulverize 100mg dried mycelium. Combine with 10ml absolute methanol in a flask. Place in hot water bath for 4 hours.

2. **Filtration**: Filter liquids with suction through filter paper in a Buchner funnel with Celite to prevent clogging. Collect filtrate.

3. **Repeated extraction**: Heat the remaining slurry (filter cake) two more times in fresh methanol. Combine all three filtrates.

4. **Completeness test**: Extract a small portion of used slurry and test with Keller's Reagent. A violet indication means alkaloids remain — continue extraction. No violet = extraction complete.

5. **Evaporation**: Evaporate combined liquids to total dryness using a hot water bath or hair dryer in an open beaker. All traces of methanol must be removed.

6. **Yield**: Each 100g dried mycelium yields approximately 2g of extracted material containing 25-50% psilocybin/psilocin mixture (at least 500mg active alkaloids, approximately fifty 10mg doses).

### Storage
- Psilocybin is fairly stable but psilocin is highly susceptible to oxidation
- Store extracted material in a dry airtight container under refrigeration
- Include silica-gel desiccant packets to capture any moisture
- Protect from light

## Dosage Guidelines (Gottlieb, 1976)

### Standard Doses
- Standard dose for a 150 lb person: 6-20 mg psilocybin/psilocin
- Average dose: 10 mg
- Each quart Mason jar yields approximately 50-100g wet mycelium = 5-10g dried
- Crude extraction from one jar: 25-35 mg active alkaloids (roughly 2-3 doses)

### Dose Variability Factors
- **Species**: P. cubensis is the most potent commonly cultivated species; other species may contain less
- **Strain**: Alkaloid content varies between strains of the same species
- **Temperature**: Higher incubation temperature (75F) produces faster growth but lower psilocybin content; lower temperature (70F) produces slower growth but higher potency
- **Extraction quality**: Efficiency of the methanol extraction process varies
- **Individual sensitivity**: Dose varies for different individuals; always start with small doses

### Crude Extract Dosing
- Crude alkaloid extraction yields approximately 25% pure material
- Depending on potency and extraction efficiency, crude dose ranges from 25-100 mg
- Each new batch must be tested individually to determine proper dosing
- Caution: crude extracts may contain high concentrations of baeocystin alkaloids, which may produce more intense visual effects — start with small doses and progress gradually

## Paper Chromatography Verification (Gottlieb)

A simple home test for verifying psilocybin/psilocin presence:

1. Dry and pulverize mushroom sample
2. Extract in unheated methanol (shake 30 minutes)
3. Spot the top fluid on paper in a 2mm zone
4. Develop with water-saturated butanol for 2 hours (solvent front 7-8 cm from origin)
5. Dry paper with hair dryer on warm setting
6. Spray with saturated p-dimethylaminobenzaldehyde in alcohol
7. Spray with 1N hydrochloric acid
8. Dry again
9. Results: psilocybin = reddish color zone; psilocin = blue-violet zone

**Caveat**: The blueing reaction alone is not diagnostic. Eastwood Boletus also bruises blue. The Metol (photo developer) test is unreliable because any organism containing indole-based amino acids (like tryptophan) will respond positively.
