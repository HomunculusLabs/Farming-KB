# Dual Chambered Terrarium Design

## Overview

The [[gottlieb-psilocybin-cultivation-fruiting-chamber-design]] at the heart of the PF
TEK. Built from a standard 10-gallon aquarium, it uses a [[fungal-mycelial-networks-nutrient-translocation]]. Direct contact drowns the fungal micro-structures and
can inhibit or contaminate growth. Only airborne molecularized water (evaporated
water) should reach the fungus.

## Terrarium Components

### The Aquarium

A standard 10-gallon aquarium serves as the main body. The most critical
requirement is that the aquarium must be sealable with no air leaks, as any
leak will allow humidity to escape.

### The Airtight Lid (Top)

The lid is constructed from a wooden frame with clear polyethylene plastic
film stretched across it. For a standard 10-gallon aquarium:

- **Outside dimensions**: 24 inches x 14.5 inches
- **Inside dimensions**: approximately 21.25 inches x 12 inches (variable)

The frame can be made from flat, unwarped 1/4-inch thick board or from four
wood strips connected by screws. The inner rectangular cutout of the frame
must be larger than the top of the aquarium.

Clear polyethylene plastic film is tacked to the underside (or upper side) of
the frame so that the frame holds it tightly onto the aquarium top. The frame
essentially hangs by the plastic film, creating an airtight seal.

A simpler alternative is to cover the aquarium top with plastic wrap or
something similar. The key point is that the aquarium must be sealable with
no air leaks for humidity retention.

### The Spray Shield/Chamber Partition

The spray shield divides the aquarium into primary and secondary chambers. It
is made from 1/8-inch thick clear acrylic (Plexiglas) window insulation,
available at most hardware stores.

- **Approximate dimensions**: 15 inches x 18 inches (check the aquarium first)
- A tight fit is good

The shield serves multiple purposes: it breaks down the initial spray into a
finer mist, acts as a drip shield, and functions as a roof that directs
condensation away from the fungus cakes below.

## How the Dual Chamber Works

The primary chamber receives the initial spraying. When spray strikes the
shield, it is broken down into a finer mist that flows around the sides of
the shield into the secondary chamber, where the fungus cakes are bathed in
fine humidity safely away from large water droplets. Condensation on the
slanted shield drips downward, acting as a roof that protects the cakes.

## Spraying Procedure

1. Before placing cakes in the terrarium, spray all inside surfaces including
   the spray shield and lid.
2. Insert fungus cakes, then the spray shield and lid.
