# Pressure Cooker Sterilization of Grain Jars

Pressure cooker sterilization is essential for fungal-spores.

## Why Pressure Sterilization is Required

Grain substrates contain abundant carbohydrates, proteins, and
minerals that support the growth of a wide range of microorganisms:

- **Bacterial endospores**: Species such as *Bacillus* and
  *Clostridium* form heat-resistant endospores that survive boiling
  (100°C). These require temperatures of 121°C for 15–30 minutes
  to ensure destruction.
- **Fungal spores**: Most mold spores are killed at 100°C, but some
  species produce thermotolerant spores that survive brief boiling.
- **Actinomycetes**: These bacteria-like organisms form resistant
  spores that require extended exposure at elevated temperature.

A normal pot at atmospheric pressure can only reach 100°C, which is
insufficient to guarantee sterility. Pressure cookers achieve 121°C
at 15 psi, providing the temperature margin needed for reliable
sterilization.

## Equipment Requirements

### Pressure Cooker
- Minimum capacity: Must fit jars with space for water underneath
- Pressure rating: Must reach 15 psi (1 atmosphere gauge pressure)
- Lid with sealing gasket and pressure regulator
- A standard stovetop pressure cooker is sufficient for most home
  cultivation
