## Aroma and Flavor

### Terpene Profile

Golden Goat's aroma is a complex blend reflecting its diverse parentage:

- **Tropical sweetness** — from the Island Sweet Skunk and Hawaiian lineage
- **Skunky pungency** — the characteristic Island Sweet Skunk musk
- **Subtle earthy notes** — contributed by the Romulan genetics
- **Overall impression** — sweet, tropical, and skunky with an exotic quality

### Flavor

The flavor follows the aroma closely:

- Sweet tropical fruit on the inhale
- Skunky, herbal undertones developing mid-palate
- Smooth finish with lingering sweetness
- The Hawaiian influence adds a bright, almost citrus quality to the overall profile

## Effects and Experience

Golden Goat is classified as a **hard hitter** with a powerful, mood-enhancing sativa-dominant high:

### Effect Profile

- **Onset** — rapid, with effects felt within minutes
- **Cerebral** — intense uplifting and euphoric stimulation
- **Energy** — significant energizing effect, suitable for daytime activities
- **Mood** — pronounced mood enhancement and anxiety reduction
- **Body** — subtle relaxing body component from Romulan heritage, preventing overstimulation
- **Duration** — moderate to long, consistent with 24% THC content
