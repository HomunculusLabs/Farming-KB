# Soil Microorganism Ecology in Natural Farming

## Overview

Masanobu Fukuoka's natural farming system places soil microorganisms at the
center of agricultural productivity. Rather than managing soil chemistry through
fertilizer inputs, Fukuoka's approach relies on maintaining the living soil
ecosystem — the bacteria, fungi, actinomycetes, and other microorganisms that
cycle nutrients, fix nitrogen, decompose organic matter, and maintain soil
structure. His farm demonstrated that healthy, self-sustaining soil biology can
replace all external agricultural inputs.
