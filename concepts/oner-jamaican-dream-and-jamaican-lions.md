# Jamaican Dream and Jamaican Lions — Sativa Strain Profiles

Two sativa-dominant strains with Jamaican heritage represent
different approaches to working with Caribbean landrace
genetics: Jamaican Dream as a quick-finishing commercial
variety, and Jamaican Lions as a high-CBD medical cultivar.

## Jamaican Dream

Jamaican Dream was bred by Eva Female Seeds of Spain from a
Jamaican heirloom. As a 90% sativa plant, it delivers the
cerebral effects sativa enthusiasts seek while maintaining an
indica-style growth pattern.

The plant grows in a Christmas tree shape, making it
straightforward to cultivate even for newer growers.

Indoors, flowering should be forced at around 2 feet for a
final height of approximately 3 feet. Outdoors, plants can
reach 6 feet or more.

The breeder recommends growing under sodium lights with
medium to heavy nutrient levels to maximize yield.

The flowering time of just 42 days is exceptionally fast for
a predominantly sativa strain, translating to an end-of-
September outdoor harvest.

Indoor yields average around 70 grams per plant, while
outdoor plants can produce at least 500 grams each.

The aroma is fresh limes with a burnt wood undertone, and the
effects deliver a gorgeous sativa high despite the indica-like
growth habit.

## Jamaican Lions

Jamaican Lions was created by Natural Ganjahnetics, a USA-
based breeder collective catering to [[cervantes-medical-marijuana-pharmacology]]
patients. The cross combines a Mountain Lion strain with a
landrace Jamaican "Yarders" strain.

The result is a hybrid that is 94% sativa and only 6% indica,
making it one of the most sativa-heavy hybrids in Volume 3.

With its landrace genetics, Jamaican Lions is best grown by
experienced cultivators familiar with pure sativa management.

Plants can reach 4 to 18 feet tall depending on conditions,
making [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]] challenging without extensive Low
[[low-stress-training]] and tying down.

Even Sea of Green or [[cannabis-screen-of-green-scrog-guide]] setups may struggle with
this plant's vigor.

Outdoors, in West Coast US environments, Jamaican Lions
develops into a magnificent plant of monstrous proportions.

It shows good resistance to pests and mold when grown in
high-quality organic soil. Given the plant's height and the
weight of its sizable buds, staking is essential.

## Medical Profile

Jamaican Lions stands out for its medical properties, with
THC at 6.8% and CBD at 9.7% — a CBD-dominant profile that is
unusual for such a sativa-heavy plant.

The high CBD content makes this strain particularly valuable
for patients seeking relief without significant psychoactive
effects.

Medical users report effective pain relief and anti-
inflammatory benefits from the clear, alert mental state the
strain provides.

The buds feature hints of red and orange among bright green,
with a visual appeal that landrace enthusiasts particularly
appreciate.
