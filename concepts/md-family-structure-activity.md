# Methylenedioxyamphetamine Family Structure-Activity

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

The methylenedioxyamphetamine (MD) family is one of the most extensively explored structural families in PiHKAL. Built on the 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl core, systematic variation of the amine nitrogen, the aliphatic chain, and the methylenedioxy bridge produced a rich pharmacological landscape spanning classic psychedelics, empathogens, and inactive probes.

## Core Structure

The MD-family shares a 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl group attached to an amphetamine backbone. The three-carbon chain carries the amine function on the alpha-carbon (beta-position from the ring). This parent compound is MDA (3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine).

## N-Substitution Series

### Hydrogen (MDA)
The parent compound. Active at 80-160 mg, 4-6 hours. Described as both "euphoric and intense" and a "stoning intoxicant." The R-isomer is more potent, consistent with the classical psychedelic pattern.

### N-Methyl (MDMA)
Active at 80-150 mg, 4-6 hours. The S-isomer is more potent -- a reversal of the psychedelic pattern and consistent with amphetamine-like mechanism. Shows no cross-tolerance with MDA. Called "Adam" or "Ecstasy."

### N-Ethyl (MDE)
Active at 100-200 mg, 3-5 hours. Called "EVE" -- "which carries some perverse logic in light of the nickname used occasionally for MDMA, which was ADAM." Effects are similar to MDMA but "the particular magic, and affective transference, does not appear to be there."

### N-Propyl (MDPR)
Inactive at up to 180 mg as a standalone psychedelic, but functions as an "inactive probe" that can serve as a "primer" to LSD -- "an inactive level of 180 milligrams of MDPR is a rather fantastic enhancer of LSD action."

### N-Butyl (MDBU)
Inactive at up to 40 mg. Part of a consistent pattern: N-alkyl chains longer than two carbons on MDA are "probably not active." Mouse assays showed "a consistent decrease in action as the alkyl chain on the nitrogen atoms was lengthened."

### N-Allyl (MDAL)
Inactive at up to 180 mg. Despite having "identical carbon atoms, identical oxygen atoms, and an identical nitrogen atom" as MDPR (simply C13H18ClNO2 rather than C13H20ClNO2), it does not enhance LSD and "actually seems to somewhat antagonize the action of LSD." This illustrates how minor structural changes produce dramatic pharmacological differences.

### N-Benzyl (MDBZ)
Explored but found inactive. Part of a collection of N-substituted homologues that showed no psychoactivity.

## Positional Isomers

### ORTHO-MDA (2,3-methylenedioxyamphetamine)
Moving the methylenedioxy group from 3,4- to 2,3-position produces a "stimulant rather than another MDA." At 50 mg, subjects reported being "awake and alert all night" but "no MDA-like effects."

### MMDA (3-methoxy-4,5-methylenedioxyamphetamine)
Adding a methoxy group creates MMDA, derived from nutmeg's myristicin. Active at 100-250 mg. Known for producing "brain movies" -- rich closed-eye imagery described as a "link between the subconscious and the conscious." Despite similar initials, "totally separate and completely different drugs" from MDMA.

### MMDA-2 (2-methoxy-4,5-methylenedioxyamphetamine)
The other possible methoxy-MDA isomer. Active at 25-50 mg, 8-12 hours. Compared to a "slow MDA, perhaps 80 milligrams of it."

## Ring Expansion and Modification

### MDMC (3,4-ethylenedioxy-N-methylamphetamine)
Expanding the 5-membered methylenedioxy ring to a 6-membered ethylenedioxy ring. Required 200+ mg for threshold effects. Described as "a strange and completely unsatisfactory compound" with inconsistent responses between subjects.

### EDA and IDA (Ethylidene and Isopropylidene MDA)
Homologues with modified methylenedioxy bridges:
- EDA: 2-3 fold decreased potency from MDA; human trials at 150 mg produced "at best a threshold light-headedness"
- IDA: 2-3 fold further decreased potency; untested in humans

## Chain-Length Homologues

- H (homopiperonylamine / MDPEA): The phenethylamine (2-carbon) counterpart of MDA
- J (alpha-ethyl MDA): 4-carbon chain; "bending back" creates an aminoindane
- The aminoindane and aminotetralin derived from this series "react in animal discrimination studies identically to MDMA, and they appear to be free of neurochemical toxicity"

## The SAR Lessons

The MD-family SAR reveals several principles:
1. N-substitution beyond ethyl eliminates psychoactivity
2. The 3,4-position of the methylenedioxy group is optimal for empathogenic/psychedelic activity
3. Expanding the ring system (ethylenedioxy) greatly reduces potency
4. Rigidifying the chain into indane/tetralin ring systems retains MDMA-like discrimination but may reduce toxicity
5. Stereochemistry differentiates mechanisms (R-preference = psychedelic, S-preference = empathogenic)
