## KNF Agricultural Mineral Water (AMW)

Agricultural Mineral Water is KNF's approach to creating vital, mineral-rich water for irrigation. It uses the waterfall principle — rocks and IMO #4 are combined in a flowing water system to produce water with abundant dissolved oxygen, minerals, and microorganisms. AMW replaces regular irrigation water with enhanced water that promotes plant growth and livestock health.

## Why AMW?

Water quality significantly affects crop yield and quality. AMW strengthens absorption by making water particles smaller, adds minerals from rocks, and inoculates water with beneficial microorganisms from IMO #4. One watering with AMW is more effective than five waterings with regular water.

## Recipe

### Materials
- Various rocks (granite preferred — break into pieces with sharp edges)
- IMO #4
- Rust-free gauze sacks (2 — one for rocks, one for IMO)
- Pond or pool (120 cm deep)
- Vinyl film (for pond bottom)
- Water pump (electrical motor)
- Pipe or hose

### Process

**Step 1 — Build the pond**
1. Dig a pond 120 cm deep
2. Cover bottom with vinyl film to prevent water permeation
3. Use natural topography if possible — dig a pool in a low area for natural water flow
4. On flat ground, install pipe/hose at a high level to create artificial waterfall

**Step 2 — Set up rock sack**
5. Break various rocks into pieces with sharp edges
6. Put rocks into a rust-free gauze sack
7. Fix the sack so only the bottom dips in the water (do NOT submerge fully)
8. Position where falling water flows through the sack, creating a whirlpool

**Step 3 — Set up IMO sack**
9. Put IMO #4 in a separate bag
10. Hang alongside rock sack where waterfall flows through
11. The IMO bag floats initially, then sinks slowly
12. When it floats again, it is time to refill with fresh IMO #4

**Step 4 — Circulate**
13. Pump water up and let it fall through pipe over the rock and IMO sacks
14. Install circulation pump to increase AMW concentration over time
15. Continuous flow creates oxygenation and mineral extraction

## Usage

There is no special dilution — use AMW like regular water:

| Use Case | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Irrigation | Direct replacement for regular water |
| Livestock drinking | Especially for pigs/chickens with diarrhea |
| Manure deodorization | Reduces odor |
| Plant growth | Promotes overall growth |
| Crop quality | Produces mineral-rich crops, improves storability |

## Effects

- Water particles become smaller — better plant/soil absorption
- Dissolved oxygen increases from waterfall aeration
- Minerals leach from rocks into water
- Microorganisms from IMO colonize the water
- One AMW watering equals 5 regular waterings in effectiveness
