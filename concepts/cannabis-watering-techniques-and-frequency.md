### Treatment

1. **Stop watering immediately** — let the soil dry out completely
2. **Improve drainage**: Ensure containers have adequate drainage holes
3. **Add perlite**: Increase the perlite content in your soil mix (25–40% is ideal)
4. **Increase air flow**: Better ventilation around the soil surface speeds drying
5. **Check container size**: Small plants in large containers stay wet too long
6. **For severe cases**: Gently remove the plant, inspect roots for rot (brown, slimy roots), trim affected roots, and repot in fresh, well-draining soil

## Underwatering

### Symptoms of Underwatering

- Leaves droop and feel dry and crispy to the touch
- Leaf tips and edges turn brown and brittle
- Soil pulls away from the container edges
- Plant recovers quickly (within hours) after watering
- Growth slows and new leaves are small
- In severe cases, leaves dry up completely and fall off

### Treatment

1. **Water thoroughly**: Apply enough water so that runoff comes from the drainage holes
2. **Water slowly**: Pour gradually to allow the soil to absorb rather than running off
3. **Do not overcorrect**: After underwatering, return to a proper schedule — do not flood the plant
