## Cannabis Methods of Administration

Cannabis may be eaten raw, cooked into foods, drunk as juice, inhaled
after vaporization, smoked in a pipe, wrapped in paper and smoked, or
rubbed into the skin. Each method of administration has distinct
pharmacokinetic properties affecting onset time, duration of effect,
and [[psk-breast-lung-cancer-clinical-trials]] risk. However, to minimize any potential risk of
negative consequences, many patients prefer alternative methods.

## Vaporization

Many patients prefer the use of a vaporizer to heat plant material to a
precise temperature that evaporates the cannabinoids just below the
burning point of the plant matter. Vaporization eliminates the
inhalation of carbon compounds from burned paper and burned plant
material. Inhalation of cannabinoids via vaporization quickly reaches
the bloodstream, and effects are often experienced in minutes or even
seconds. However, effects are of shorter duration than other modes of
administration.
