# Maitake (Grifola frondosa) and D-Fraction Research

## Overview

significant anti-cancer and immunomodulatory properties. Beta-glucan fractions
from Maitake are used by over 3,000 health professionals in the US for
[[query-what-are-the-main-bioactive-compounds-in-medicinal-plants]] from G. frondosa include:

  - Grifolan: a (1-3)-beta-glucan similar to Schizophyllan in primary
    structure; activates macrophages increasing IL-6, IL-1, and TNF-alpha
    mRNA levels
  - Maitake D-Fraction: a highly active beta-D-glucan with 1-6 main chains
    and 1-4 branchings plus 1-3 main chains with 1-6 branching; obtained
    from hot water crude extract by deproteination
  - Grifron-D (GD): a highly purified beta-1,6 glucan branched with
    beta-1,3-linkage; orally bioavailable
  - Heteroglucan proteins, manogalactofucan, and other polysaccharides
  - Fa-1a-beta (acidic beta-glucan), FIII-2c (hetero-beta-glucan),
    xyloglucan, mannoglucan, fucomannoglucan

## Grifolan: Macrophage Activation

Grifolan is a novel macrophage activator confirmed by RT-PCR to increase
mRNA levels of IL-6, IL-1, and TNF-alpha. It operates through stimulation
of acute phase proteins and colony-stimulating factors, which in turn
affect proliferation of macrophages, polymorphonuclear cells, and
lymphocytes, and activate the complement system.

## D-Fraction: Clinical Evidence

The D-Fraction has been tested in both animal models and human clinical
studies:

Animal studies:
  - Remarkable inhibition of metastasis in immuno-competent mouse models
  - Prevention of hepatic metastases: 81% (Maitake powder) to 91%
    (D-fraction) reduction (Nanba, 1995)
  - Strong antitumour activity in xenographs (Kurashiga et al., 1997)

Human clinical studies:
  - Chinese pilot study (n=63): 95% response rate against solid tumours,
    90% for leukaemia (Jones, 1998)
  - Japanese non-randomised study (n=165): patients took oral D-fraction
    plus crude Maitake tablets or placebo plus chemotherapy (Nanba, 1997a)
  - Tumour regression or significant improvement in 11 of 15 advanced
    hepatocellular carcinomas with D-fraction plus Maitake
  - Overall response rates increased by 12-28% when D-fraction plus Maitake
    combined with chemotherapy across all cancer types

## Grifron-D: Cytotoxic Effects and FDA Approval

Grifron-D demonstrates both immunomodulatory and direct cytotoxic effects:

  - Achieved >95% [[blesching-cannabis-prostate-cancer]] PC-3
    cells in 24 hours at >=480 ug/ml
  - Vitamin C acts synergistically: 30-60 ug/ml GD plus 200 uM vitamin C
    matched the effect of 480 ug/ml GD alone
  - Causes oxidative membrane damage and apoptotic cell death
  - Chemosensitises carmustine (90% reduction in cell viability with
    carmustine/GD combination)
  - May inactivate glyoxalase I, a detoxifying enzyme in cancer cells,
    potentially enhancing chemotherapy efficacy
  - Approved by FDA for trial under Investigational New Drug Application
    (IND) for [[mushroom-cancer-clinical-trials-overview]] (Phase I/II) in breast, prostate, lung, liver, and
gastric cancers are underway in the US and Japan.

## Substrate Preferences

Different fungal species show distinct substrate affinities based
on their enzymatic capabilities. Lignicolous species degrade wood,
while coprophilous species specialize in dung. Soil-dwelling
species form complex relationships with plant root systems.

## Temperature Sensitivity

Growth rates respond predictably to temperature gradients within
species-specific ranges. Most cultivated species grow optimally
between 20-28°C. Fruit body formation often requires a distinct
temperature shift from vegetative growth conditions.
