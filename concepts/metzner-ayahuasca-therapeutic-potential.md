## The Brazilian Ayahuasca Churches
In Brazil, three organized churches use ayahuasca as their main sacrament: Santo
Daime, Uniao de Vegetal (UDV), and Barquinia. Each was founded by rubber tappers
working in the Amazon region in the 1950s who came into contact with the
hallucinogenic vine through mestizo or Indian ayahuasceros. The founder of each
reported a significant vision which instructed them to organize a church using the
brew as the principal sacrament.
### Santo Daime
Founded by a Brazilian of African descent, Santo Daime involves the singing of hymns
by the entire congregation, led by a small group of women singers. Some ceremonies
also involve dancing in simple rhythmic, swaying steps, reminiscent of
African-American gospel services. The communal singing and dancing create a powerful
group resonance that amplifies the individual's experience.
### Uniao de Vegetal (UDV)
The largest of the Brazilian ayahuasca churches, UDV is also the most formal:
participants sit in rows in straight-backed chairs during the ceremony, listening to
sermons and songs given by the maestres who sit around a central table. There is also
