## Rare Micronutrients for Cannabis

Several micronutrients are seldom found deficient
in cannabis gardens but can cause significant
problems when deficiencies or toxicities do occur.
These rare micronutrients include boron, chlorine,
cobalt, nickel, molybdenum, and sodium. Cervantes
provides practical guidance on identifying and
treating issues with these often-overlooked
elements.

## Boron (B) - Immobile

Boron must be available during the entire life of
a plant. It helps with calcium uptake and numerous
plant functions. Scientists have collected evidence
that boron helps with synthesis, nucleic acid
formation, cell division, differentiation,
maturation, respiration, and pollen germination.

### Deficiency

Stem tip and root tip grow abnormally. Root tips
often swell, discolor, and stop elongating. Growing
shoots look burned and may be confused with a burn
from being too close to HID light. First leaves
thicken and become brittle, top shoots contort
and/or turn dark. When severe, growing tips die,
and leaf margins discolor and die back. Necrotic
spots develop between leaf veins.

Treat boron-deficient plants with one teaspoon of
boric acid per gallon of water as a soil drench.
Hydroponic gardeners should keep boron dosage
below 20 parts per million (ppm), because boron
quickly becomes toxic if concentrated.

### Toxicity

Leaf tips yellow first, and as conditions progress,
margins become necrotic toward the center of the
leaf. Avoid using excessive amounts of boric acid-
based insecticides.

## Chlorine (Cl) - Immobile

Chlorine is found in many municipal water systems.
Cannabis tolerates low levels of chlorine. It is
usually not a component of fertilizers and is almost
never deficient in cannabis gardens. Chlorine is
fundamental to photosynthesis and cell division in
roots and foliage.

### Toxicity

Young leaves develop burned leaf tips and margins.
Very young seedlings and clones are most susceptible.
Later symptoms progress throughout the plant,
producing characteristic yellowish-bronze leaves
that are smaller and slower to develop. Treat
toxic signs by letting heavily chlorinated water
sit out overnight so chlorine volatizes.
