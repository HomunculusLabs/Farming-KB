# Ayahuasca and Amazonian Shamanism

## The New World Richness in Hallucinogens

Indole-containing plant hallucinogens and their cults cluster in the tropical New World. The New World subtropical and tropical zones are phenomenally rich in hallucinogenic plants. Similar ecosystems in [[sacred-mexican-morning-glories-ololiuqui-and-tlitliltzin]].

## The Discovery of Ayahuasca

In the 1850s, botanists Richard Spruce and Alfred Russel Wallace traveled in the Amazon drainage. On the upper reaches of the Rio Negro, Spruce observed Indians prepare an unfamiliar hallucinogen. He noted that the main ingredient was a liana (woody climbing vine) which he named Banisteria caapi. Several years later in western Ecuador, he saw the same plant used to make a hallucinogen called ayahuasca.

## The Pharmacology of Ayahuasca

The word ayahuasca is a Quechua word that roughly translates as "vine of the dead" or "vine of souls." The tissues of Banisteriopsis caapi are rich in beta-carboline alkaloids, the most important being harmine. Harmine is not overtly psychedelic unless taken at near-toxic levels. However, well below that level, harmine is an effective short-acting monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI).

A hallucinogen such as DMT, which would normally be inactive if taken orally (because MAO enzymes in the gut destroy it), is rendered highly psychoactive if taken orally in combination with harmine. Native peoples of the Amazon have brilliantly exploited this pharmacological mechanism, which was not described by Western science until the 1950s.

## The DMT-Harmine Synergy

In the presence of harmine, DMT becomes a highly psychoactive compound that enters the bloodstream, passes the blood-brain barrier, and very effectively competes with serotonin for synaptic bonding sites. The experience of the slow release of DMT lasts four to six hours and is the basis of the magical and shamanic view of reality that characterizes the ayahuasquero.

## Ayahuasca vs. Smoked DMT

The experience of ingesting ayahuasca has characteristics that set it apart from smoking DMT. Ayahuasca is gentler and of much greater duration. Its themes and hallucinations are oriented toward the organic and natural world, in marked contrast to the titanic, alien, and off-planet motifs that characterize the DMT flash. Why such major differences should exist between compounds that appear so structurally similar is an uninvestigated problem.

## Ayahuasca as Healing Elixir

In native areas of its use, ayahuasca is regarded as a general-purpose healing elixir and is called la purga ("the purge"). Its effectiveness in combating intestinal parasites has been proven. Its effectiveness in killing the malaria organism is being investigated. Its long history of effective shamanic use in folk psychiatry has been documented by Naranjo, Dobkin de Rios, Luna, and others.

Immigrants into the Amazon basin have also accepted ayahuasca and have created their own ethnobotanical-medical system for using the psychedelic visions it imparts to promote healing. Ayahuasca has continued to the present day to be a part of the spiritual life of many tribes in the montane rain forest of South America.
