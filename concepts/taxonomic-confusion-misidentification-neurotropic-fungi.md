### Sectional Classification Disputes

The classification of *Psilocybe* into sections has been repeatedly
revised. Key disputes include:

- **Section Chrysocystidiatae**: Guzmán (1983) initially included this
  group within *Psilocybe*, but later questioned its placement. Some
  authors argue these species belong in a separate genus based on the
  distinctive chrysocystidia (inflated cells that stain yellow in
  Melzer's reagent) and their typically non-neurotropic chemistry
- **Section Stuntzii**: The status of this section has been debated,
  with some authors merging it into Section *Cyanescens* based on
  overlapping morphological characteristics
- **Panaeolus vs. Psilocybe**: Some bluing *Panaeolus* species have
  been moved to *Psilocybe* and back again, reflecting disagreement
  about the importance of [[gartz-panaeolus-subbalteatus-taxonomy-and-intoxication-history]]* or other
  neurotropic *Panaeolus* species
