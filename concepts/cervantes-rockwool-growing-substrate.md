## Moisture Management

Rockwool holds a lot of moisture, which is both an advantage and a
challenge. Overwatering in rockwool can reduce oxygen availability to
roots. The top of rockwool blocks can dry out while the interior
remains moist. Adding a thin layer of perlite or expanded clay to the
top of rockwool blocks helps keep the surface from drying out too
quickly.

## Rockwool vs. Other Substrates

Rockwool is one of several popular growing substrates. Compared to
soil, rockwool provides more precise control over nutrient delivery
but requires more frequent irrigation. Compared to coconut coir,
rockwool drains faster but holds less organic material. Compared to
perlite, rockwool retains more water while still providing good
