# Cannabis THC Acetate Preparation

THC acetate has approximately twice the potency of THC on the Adams scale (THC = 7.3, THC acetate = 14.6). There is also a 25% increase in weight after adding the acetate structure. The effect of THC acetate is more spiritual and psychedelic than ordinary THC, with a unique property: a delay of about thirty minutes before effects are felt. ### Distillation (Removing Acetic Anhydride)

1. Use Pyrex distillation flask and Erlenmeyer receiving flasks
2. Pour solution from boiling flask into distillation flask using a long-stem funnel
3. Receiving flask immersed in ice water bath
4. Heat in oil bath at same temperature as refluxing
5. Collect evaporated acetic anhydride in receiving flask (save in safety container)
6. Maintain temperature 1 hour after last traces removed
7. Cool to room temperature

### Washing the Product

1. Dissolve the THC acetate in several volumes of pure alcohol (one drop at a time)
2. Pour into 5 volumes of water
3. Extract with petroleum ether (as in purification step)
4. Evaporate the ether using stew pot apparatus
5. Redissolve in alcohol, pour into water again
6. Extract with petroleum ether again, evaporate
7. The resulting oil contains THC acetate

## Important Notes

- Acetic anhydride fumes are extremely flammable and poisonous
- All operations must be conducted under nitrogen atmosphere
- The product volume will be up to 25% more than the starting oil volume
- THC acetate can be consumed in any customary manner (smoked, oral, etc.)
