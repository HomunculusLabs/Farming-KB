# Cannabis THC Acetate Preparation

THC acetate has approximately twice the potency of THC on the Adams scale (THC = 7.3, THC acetate = 14.6). There is also a 25% increase in weight after adding the acetate structure. The effect of THC acetate is more spiritual and psychedelic than ordinary THC, with a unique property: a delay of about thirty minutes before effects are felt. The pharmacological profile places it alongside other [[psychedelic-consciousness-models]] as a compound that blurs the line between cannabis and classic psychedelics. Understanding the parent compound's chemistry in [[tetrahydrocannabinolic-acid-thca]] is essential since the quality of starting material directly affects acetate yield and purity.

## Safety Requirements

This conversion uses acetic anhydride, an extremely dangerous chemical. A safety glove box is mandatory:

1. Construct a box with thick reinforced front boards and hinged thin plywood side panels
2. Side panels blow outward in case of explosion, protecting the operator
3. Replace air in the sealed box with anhydrous nitrogen gas (making combustion impossible)
4. Test by attempting to strike a match — if it won't light, oxygen is removed
5. Equipment includes: exhaust fan with sparkless motor, fire extinguisher, safety-glass window, long sealed gloves
6. All operations performed through gloves from outside the box

## Equipment

- 500ml Pyrex round-bottom boiling flask with ground glass fitting
- Tubular condenser with matching ground glass fitting
- Metal pot (2000ml+) as hot oil bath
- Thermometer for monitoring oil bath temperature
- Sparkless electric hotplate
- Rheostat to control hotplate from outside the box
- Ring stand and proper clamps
- Cottonseed oil (for oil bath)
- Acetic anhydride (reactant)
- Immersible water pump, bucket, and hoses for condenser coolant

## Conversion Process

### Refluxing

1. Place measured cannabis extract in the boiling flask
2. Add acetic anhydride equal to 3× the volume of the cannabis oil
3. Assemble apparatus: flask in oil bath, condenser secured above, thermometer visible through safety glass
4. Fill condenser with circulating ice water via immersion pump
5. Heat oil bath slowly, monitoring temperature
6. Note the temperature where solution begins to fume and droplets form in condenser
7. Raise temperature slightly until solution boils; stabilize at this point
8. Reflux for 3 hours, supplying ice as needed to coolant
9. Turn off hotplate, let cool to room temperature with circulating coolant (2+ hours)
10. Check rubber stopper seal on top of condenser
11. Turn off immersion pump, let sit another hour
12. Remove flask, wipe clean of oil, set in empty container

### Distillation (Removing Acetic Anhydride)

1. Use Pyrex distillation flask and Erlenmeyer receiving flasks
2. Pour solution from boiling flask into distillation flask using a long-stem funnel
3. Receiving flask immersed in ice water bath
4. Heat in oil bath at same temperature as refluxing
5. Collect evaporated acetic anhydride in receiving flask (save in safety container)
6. Maintain temperature 1 hour after last traces removed
7. Cool to room temperature

### Washing the Product

1. Dissolve the THC acetate in several volumes of pure alcohol (one drop at a time)
2. Pour into 5 volumes of water
3. Extract with petroleum ether (as in purification step)
4. Evaporate the ether using stew pot apparatus
5. Redissolve in alcohol, pour into water again
6. Extract with petroleum ether again, evaporate
7. The resulting oil contains THC acetate

## Important Notes

- Acetic anhydride fumes are extremely flammable and poisonous
- All operations must be conducted under nitrogen atmosphere
- The product volume will be up to 25% more than the starting oil volume
- THC acetate can be consumed in any customary manner (smoked, oral, etc.)
