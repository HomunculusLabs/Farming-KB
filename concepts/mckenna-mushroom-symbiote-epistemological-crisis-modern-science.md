McKenna reported that the alien intelligence encountered through the mushroom trance claimed to possess "a huge body of information drawn from the history of the galaxy." According to this intelligence, the galaxy has been an organized society for billions of years, and humanity's conceptions of organized intelligence and the dispersion of life are "hopelessly culture-bound."

## The Epistemological Crisis

As McKenna became more familiar with the epistemological assumptions of modern science, he "slowly realized that the structure of the Western intellectual enterprise is so flimsy at the center that apparently no one knows anything with certitude." This realization made him less reluctant to discuss his experiences, since they are "experiences, and as such they are primary data for being."

McKenna challenged several specific epistemological assumptions:

**Scientific exclusivity:** Only that which is validated through consensus via "certain sanctioned instrumentalities" is accepted as valid data. Meanwhile, psychics, shamans, mystics, and schizophrenics have heads filled with information ruled irrelevant "a priori."

**Search for extraterrestrials:** The assumption that contact with alien intelligence requires radio signals is "probably as culture-bound a presumption as to search the galaxy for a good Italian restaurant."

**Scientific materialism:** The "Hellenistic-Christian-Judaic tradition" built a "triumph of mechanism" that conquered the New World and drove [[mckenna-history-shockwave-of-eschatology]]. Something is at the end of time and is casting an enormous shadow over human history." He envisioned a future where the mind is exteriorized and the body internalized, producing "a superconducting lens of translinguistic matter generated out of the body of each of us at a critical juncture."

The tryptamine state, he suggested, is "transtemporal"—an anticipation of this future, "a stepping out of the moving image and into eternity."

## The Extraterrestrial Versus Endopsychic Interpretation

McKenna explicitly acknowledged the ambiguity in interpreting the entity encountered in the DMT state. The literal extraterrestrial reading—that an alien intelligence from elsewhere in the galaxy is communicating through the mushroom—coexists with what he called the "endopsychic" reading: that the alien quality reflects the self's alienation from its own depths within a culture that has systematically denied the reality of inner experience.

This dual interpretation has become a central question in [[ceremonial-shamanic-use-psychoactive-mushrooms]] in the mountains of Oaxaca by R. Gordon Wasson.

The irony that the same civilization that destroyed indigenous shamanic traditions then "rediscovered" them through the very plants those traditions venerated was not lost on McKenna. He saw this as evidence that the repressed always returns—that the knowledge systematically excluded from the Western intellectual canon cannot be permanently suppressed.

## The "Black Hole Effect" and Communicative Failure

McKenna described what he called the "black hole effect" surrounding DMT: "once one knows about it, it is very hard for others to understand what one is talking about. One cannot be heard. The more one is able to articulate what it is, the less others are able to understand." He connected this communicative impasse to the broader problem of enlightened beings who "are silent because we cannot understand them." The phenomenon of tryptamine ecstasy has not been seriously examined by scientists despite its profound implications for understanding consciousness.

This communicative failure is both a personal and cultural phenomenon. Individually, the experiencer finds that language is inadequate to convey the content and quality of the experience. Culturally, the epistemological assumptions that render the experience inadmissible as data also prevent the development of a shared vocabulary that could make it communicable. The black hole effect is thus both a consequence and a reinforcement of the epistemological crisis McKenna identified.
