The drug war context created both risks and protections for ayahuasca
tourists in early-2000s Peru. On one hand, the presence of narcotraffickers
and corrupt officials created genuine safety concerns for travelers in the
Amazonian regions. The closure of major highways by traffickers could strand

On the other hand, ayahuasca's explicit legal acceptance as an herbal tonic
provided a degree of protection. Tourists participating in ayahuasca
ceremonies were not, in the normal course of events, at risk of arrest or
prosecution. The government's active promotion of ayahuasca festivals and the
president's own participation in ceremonies signaled that the practice was
not merely tolerated but officially sanctioned as part of Peru's cultural
heritage.
