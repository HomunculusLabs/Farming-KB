### Spot Treatment Protocol

1. Sprinkle mold-contaminated area with agricultural lime.
2. Fill a spray bottle with 10:1 dilution of bleach.
3. Spray one or two hard blasts directly onto the contaminated area.
4. For holes in columns or bags, wipe the surface with 70% isopropyl alcohol.
5. Allow to dry, then tape the hole with clear packing tape to monitor whether
