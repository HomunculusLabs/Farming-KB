## Fukuoka's Personal Journey

In Chapter 4 of The Road Back to Nature, Masanobu Fukuoka offers his most
intimate and detailed account of the philosophy and practice that defined his
life's work. Having spent decades developing and refining his methods on his
farm in Shikoku, Japan, he presents natural farming not merely as an
agricultural technique but as a way of understanding humanity's proper
relationship with the natural world. His testimony is both a practical guide
and a philosophical meditation on what it means to live in accordance with
nature's wisdom.
