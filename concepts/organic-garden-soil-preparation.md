## Organic Garden Soil Preparation

Soil preparation sets the foundation for a productive garden. Organic methods focus on building soil biology, maintaining soil structure, and providing nutrients through natural amendments. The approach chosen -- no-till, double-dig, or [[soil-food-web-structure]], and [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]] per year to maintain soil organic matter levels.

## Mineral Amendments

Apply based on soil test results:

- **Calcium**: ag lime (raises pH), gypsum (neutral pH, adds Ca + S, improves clay structure)
- **Phosphorus**: rock phosphate (slow-release), bone meal (moderate speed), fish bone meal
- **Potassium**: kelp meal, greensand, wood ash (also raises pH), sulfate of potash
- **Trace minerals**: azomite, basalt rock dust, kelp meal -- add broad-spectrum trace elements
- **Boron**: borax (use sparingly, 1-2 oz per 100 sq ft; easy to over-apply)

Apply rock minerals in fall to allow slow breakdown over winter.
