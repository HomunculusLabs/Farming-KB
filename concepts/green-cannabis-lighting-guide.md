# [[greg-green]]'s *The Cannabis Grow Bible* (4th ed.), Chapter 5.

Light is the most important factor in bud development. The two most
fundamental factors in high-yield growth are Strains (good genetics)
and Light. Optimal light along with good strains will lead to great
yields and bud-plentiful plants.

## Light Bands and Spectrum

Light can be separated into a spectrum: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green,
Blue, Indigo, Violet. Each mixes down to form white light. Artificial
lights tend to lean towards a tint of one of these colors.

Plants absorb light at the middle of the spectrum (Orange to Indigo).
Cannabis plants need this part of the [[cannabis-light-spectrum-and-color-bands]] to reach full
growing potential. The intensity of the light is also important. A
light that feeds the full bandwidth with all light spectrums will work
very well -- like sunlight. When dealing with artificial lights it is
better to choose a light that concentrates its intensity on the top
part of the spectrum.

## Lighting Systems Ranked

### Poor Lighting Systems

**Domestic/Incandescent Lights:** 15W-150W. Not suitable for growing
due to low light intensity and bad color spectrum. Avoid entirely.

### Average Lighting Systems

**Fluorescent Tube Lights:** Long industrial lights, 2-10 feet, 10W-
300W. Okay for growing but provide little light, hard to set up, and
not in the best light spectrum for cannabis. Good for clones and
seedlings.

**Halogen Lights:** 75W-4000W. Extremely hot, dangerous for indoor
growing. Not recommended. Also wrong light spectrum.

**Fluorescent White Tube Lights:** In the correct growing spectrum but
very weak (not much above 100W). Only recommended for growers who do
not want to grow full flowering cannabis plants.

### Best Lighting Systems (HID)

Horticultural lights are professional HID (High Intensity Discharge)
lights developed by horticultural lighting companies. They come as kits
with bulb, reflector, ballast, and timer. **If you want to grow good
bud then you need a HID.** A HID is the second most important purchase
you will make, next to choosing your strain.

**[[cervantes-metal-halide-lighting-systems]] (MH):** used for seedling and [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] stages.
Can also be used for flowering. Range: 75W-4000W. Very common and a
good kit for indoor growers. Gives off a blue tone. Better veg lamp.

**Mercury Vapor (MV):** Similar to MH but almost obsolete. MV has a
tendency to be slightly out of the optimal spectrum range. If you have
a choice, stay with MH.

**High [[cervantes-high-pressure-sodium-lighting]] (HPS):** The lighting choice of many cannabis
cultivators. Range: 75W-4000W. In the perfect spectrum for growing
cannabis. **Highly recommended.** Gives off an orange tone. Better
flowering lamp.

**MH vs HPS Recommendation:** If you can only afford one system, get
HPS. Some growers run MH for seedlings/veg and HPS for flowering. Both
MH and HPS can be used for both stages, but if growing for bud, get
the best HPS you can.

## Wattage and Lumens

Lumens are the correct way of measuring how much light per square foot
a bulb emits. Lumens and wattage go hand in hand but can vary between
systems.
