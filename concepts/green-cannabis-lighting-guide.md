## Lighting Systems Ranked

### Poor Lighting Systems

**Domestic/Incandescent Lights:** 15W-150W. Not suitable for growing
due to low light intensity and bad color spectrum. Avoid entirely.

### Average Lighting Systems

**Fluorescent Tube Lights:** Long industrial lights, 2-10 feet, 10W-
300W. Okay for growing but provide little light, hard to set up, and
not in the best light spectrum for cannabis. Good for clones and
seedlings.

**Halogen Lights:** 75W-4000W. Extremely hot, dangerous for indoor
growing. Not recommended. Also wrong light spectrum.

**Fluorescent White Tube Lights:** In the correct growing spectrum but
very weak (not much above 100W). Only recommended for growers who do
not want to grow full flowering cannabis plants.

### Best Lighting Systems (HID)

Horticultural lights are professional HID (High Intensity Discharge)
lights developed by horticultural lighting companies. They come as kits
with bulb, reflector, ballast, and timer. **If you want to grow good
bud then you need a HID.** A HID is the second most important purchase
you will make, next to choosing your strain.
