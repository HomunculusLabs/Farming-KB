## The HEPA Filter Revolution

The invention of High Efficiency Particulate Air (HEPA) filters made
sterile tissue culture achievable for cultivators at every scale. When
air is forcibly pressed through HEPA filters, all contaminants down to
0.3 microns are eliminated with 99.99% efficiency. This means only 1
of every 10,000 particulates exceeding 0.3 microns passes through.
For all practical purposes, a sterile wind is generated downstream
from the filter. A newer generation of ULPA filters screens out
particulates down to 0.1 microns with 99.9999% efficiency.

A three-stage filtration system is ideal: a coarse prefilter
capturing 30% of particles at 10 microns, an electrostatic filter
capturing 99% at 1 micron, and finally the HEPA filter. This staged
approach extends the life of the expensive HEPA filter while
providing progressively cleaner air. The cultivator works within this
airstream, operating under a unique set of rules where the violation
of sterile protocol invites contamination that can cascade through
an entire production cycle.
