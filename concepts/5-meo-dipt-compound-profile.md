# 5-MeO-DIPT Compound Profile

5-MeO-DIPT (N,N-diisopropyl-5-methoxytryptamine) is a synthetic tryptamine first synthesized by Alexander Shulgin. In TIHKAL it is compound #37. Street names include "Foxy" and "Foxy Methoxy." It was briefly sold legally in the US before being emergency-scheduled in 2003. The compound was co-developed with Michael Carter in England, with whom Shulgin had previously published on [[tihkal-introduction-and-methodology]].

## Safety Notes
- Nausea is common at onset, especially with food in stomach
- Body load and muscle spasms reported at higher doses (12+ mg)
- Some users report anxiety, paranoia, or "unusable" [[weil-the-natural-mind-altered-states-normalcy]] at higher doses
- Combining with MAOIs may dangerously increase potency
- Long-term effects unknown
- Variable experience quality: some found it "outstanding" while others found it "awful" at the same dose
- Fast-acting with rapid drop-off — shorter than most psychedelics

## Comparison with Related Compounds

- The diisopropyl substitution pattern (two isopropyl groups on the basic nitrogen) appears to be the key structural feature shared with DIPT that produces both the erotic enhancement and the auditory distortions

## Historical Context

The compound was first published in 1981 by Shulgin and Michael Carter. After initial correspondence and sample exchange, Carter became unreachable — letters returned as undeliverable with no forwarding address. Shulgin eventually tracked down 20 "Michael F. Carter" listings through the London telephone company but received no response. The paper was published using Shulgin's address for both authors. Carter's fate remains unknown; Shulgin included a note in TIHKAL hoping Carter might read it.
