## Smoke and Mirrors Ritual

This ritual explicitly employs mirrors for reflective and banishing work under the influence of cannabis. While simpler in its visionary component than the Vortex, it creates a more elaborate physical scrying environment.

### Scrying Chamber Setup

The ritual requires:
- **Three mirrors**: one placed across from the practitioner on the altar, one on each side
- **Five white candles** arranged in a pentagram on the altar
- **A small altar** as the focal point
- **A vaporizer** for cannabis consumption
- **A fog machine** to create atmospheric density

The three-mirror arrangement creates a classic scrying chamber where reflections multiply infinitely in the darkness. Combined with the fog machine filling the room with mist and the vaporizer adding cannabis vapor to the air, the physical environment becomes saturated with obscured light and atmospheric haze -- conditions that traditional scrying practitioners have long sought for inducing visionary states.
