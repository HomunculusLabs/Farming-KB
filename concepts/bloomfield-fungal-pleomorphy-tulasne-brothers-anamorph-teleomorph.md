### Cleistothecia

The simplest ascomycete fruiting bodies are closed spheres called cleistothecia that contain one or more asci. The minute fruiting bodies of *Eurotium* are built from a jacket of hyphae that become yellowed with age. The asci inside are flaccid bags that dissolve, turning the cleistothecium into a spore-filled globe that eventually breaks open to free the spores.

### Perithecia

Flask-shaped perithecia characterize genera like *Cordyceps*. These fruiting bodies have a narrow opening (ostiole) through which spores are discharged. The elongated asci of *Cordyceps* act as quivers that contain eight elongated spores, each capable of fragmenting into multiple infectious cells upon discharge.
