## Cannabis for Stroke Recovery

Stroke (cerebrovascular accident, CVA) is a medical emergency in which blood flow to part of the brain is interrupted, causing brain [[blesching-cannabis-prostate-cancer-chi-value-3]]:** 8

## Types of Stroke

- **Ischemic stroke:** Blockage of blood flow to the brain (most common)
- **Hemorrhagic stroke:** Rupture of blood vessels in the brain
- Both involve [[cannabinoid-receptors-cb1-cb2]]
- Scientists concluded: "Cannabidiol will have a palliative action and open new therapeutic possibilities for treating cerebrovascular disorders"

## Proposed Clinical Applications

If the Japanese results translate to humans:

### Prophylactic (Prevention)
- CBD and THC could potentially be used as neuroprotective agents in patients prone to stroke
- Patients with known cerebrovascular risk factors might benefit from regular cannabinoid therapy

### Acute Treatment
- CBD could serve as a first-line drug treatment during acute stroke
- CBD's ability to work independently of CB1 receptors and provide neuroprotection after the event is particularly valuable
- CBD's lack of psychoactive effects makes it suitable for emergency administration

### Recovery Phase
- Anti-inflammatory properties may reduce post-stroke inflammation
- Neurogenesis stimulation may support brain repair
- Antioxidant properties may limit ongoing oxidative damage

## Mechanism Summary

| Cannabinoid | Timing | Mechanism | Key Feature |

| THC | Before stroke | CB1-mediated body temperature reduction, infarct size reduction | Requires pre-treatment |
| CBD | Before AND after stroke | CB1-independent anti-inflammatory mechanism | Works post-event |

## Cardiovascular Considerations

Cannabis also shows cardioprotective properties:
- CBN and THC significantly reduced heart attack size in animal studies
- THC protects cardiac cells against hypoxia damage
