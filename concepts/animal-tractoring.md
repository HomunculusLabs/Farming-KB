## Design Considerations

Key design considerations for animal tractor systems include fencing that is secure, movable, and affordable; water supply for animals in each paddock; shade and shelter from sun, wind, and rain; the appropriate stocking density for the desired land preparation effect; the rotation timing to match the cropping cycle; predator protection, especially for chickens; and integration with the overall farm layout and water management system.

## Limitations

Animal tractor systems have some limitations. They require regular management attention to move animals and maintain fencing. They may not be suitable for very rocky or steep terrain. They require a minimum number of animals to be effective. They may create odour and dust if close to residential areas. They need careful management in wet weather to prevent soil damage from hooves.
