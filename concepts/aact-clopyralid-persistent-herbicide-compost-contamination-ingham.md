## Elaine Ingham's Position on Feedstock Sourcing

Dr. Ingham recommends a cautious, source-aware approach to compost feedstocks:

- **Avoid grass clippings from treated lawns entirely.** It is nearly impossible
  to verify whether a lawn has been treated with persistent herbicides.
- **Use hay, not straw — but verify the source.** Hay contains more diverse
  biology and nutrients than straw. However, hay from fields treated with
  aminopyralid or picloram is dangerous. Know your supplier and ask about
  herbicide applications specifically.
- **Be cautious with manure.** Ask livestock owners about deworming products
  and pasture treatments. Avoid manure from animals on recently treated pasture.
