title: Nutrified Agar Media for Mushroom Mycelium Culture
created: 2026-04-26
tags: [[cotter-agar-tissue-culture-techniques]]. Agar is a seaweed-derived compound that
gelatinizes water. Nutrients are added to the agar/water base which,
after growing gourmet sterilization, promote healthy
mushroom mycelium. The agar medium most commonly used with the greatest
success is a fortified version of Malt Extract Agar (MEA). Other
recommended nutrified agar media include Potato Dextrose Agar (PDA),
Oatmeal Agar (OMA), and Dog Food Agar (DFA).

## Recommended Agar Media Recipes

### Malt Extract, Yeast Agar (MYA / MYPA)

  - 1000 ml water (1 liter)
  - 20 g agar agar
  - 20 g barley malt sugar
  - 2 g yeast (nutritional)
  - 1 g peptone (optional, soybean derived)

With peptone, which is not critical for most species described in the
book, this medium is designated MYPA.

### Potato, Dextrose, Yeast Agar (PDYA / PDYPA)

  - 1000 ml water (1 liter)
  - 300 g potatoes (broth from boiling in 2-3 liters water for 1 hour)
  - 20 g agar agar
  - 10 g dextrose
  - 2 g yeast
  - 1 g peptone (optional, soybean derived)

Note: Only the broth from boiling the potatoes is used; the potatoes
are discarded. The total volume should equal 1 liter.

### Oatmeal, Malt, Yeast Enriched Agar (OMYA)

  - 1000 ml water (1 liter)
  - 80 g instant oatmeal
  - 20 g agar agar
  - 10 g malt sugar
  - 2 g yeast

This is a rich medium. The oatmeal does not have to be filtered out,
although some prefer to do so.

### Dog Food Agar (DFA)

  - 1000 ml water (1 liter)
  - 20 g dry dog food
  - 20 g agar agar

Dog food was first used as a component for agar medium by the late Dr.
[[fungal-culture-preservation-methods]].

## pH Considerations

The pH of the above media formulations, after sterilizing, generally
falls between 5.5-6.8. This is suitable for most gourmet mushroom
species.

## Supplements and Fortification

By supplementing these formulas with yeast and peptone, essential
vitamins and amino acids are provided. These supplements not only
greatly stimulate the rate of growth but the quality of the projected
mycelial mat. A variety of nitrogen and carbohydrate-based supplements
can be added to 1 liter of MEA or PDA:

Nitrogen and carbohydrate supplements:
  - 2 g yeast
  - 1-2 g peptone
  - 2 g oatmeal or oat bran
  - 2 g rye or wheat flour
  - 1 g soybean meal
  - 1 g spirulina
  - 2 g high quality dry dog food

End-substrate supplements (for lag period reduction):
  - 3-5 g sawdust
  - 3-5 g powdered straw

If soil or dung samples are desired, they must first be pre-boiled for
1-2 hours before adding to any formula.

## The Importance of Complex Sugars

Sugars are essential for the healthy growth of mycelium. Complex sources
of carbohydrates and polysaccharides are recommended. Cornsteep
fermentative, cooked potatoes, wood, and barley malt extracts provide
sugars and an assortment of basic minerals, vitamins, and salts helpful
in the growth of mushroom mycelium.

From Stamets' experience, simple sugars, while they may support growth,
are not recommended as strains cannot be maintained for long without
promoting mutation factors, senescence, or loss of vitality.

## Preventing Media Specificity

Strains grown repeatedly on mono-specific media for prolonged periods
risk limiting the repertoire of digestive enzymes to just that
formulation. A strain grown on one medium adapts to it and may lose its
innate ability to digest larger, more complex and variable [[cotter-pasteurization-sterilization-methods]] for details.

Pressure cookers that do not release pressure during the sterilization
cycle are ideal. The old-fashioned pressure canners, those having
weights sitting upon a steam valve, cause media to boil as steam is
vented.
