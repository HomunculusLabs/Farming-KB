## Fungal Pathogens and Crop Loss

Plant pathogenic fungi cause enormous economic losses in agriculture worldwide. Key pathogenic strategies include:

- **Necrotrophy**: Killing host cells and feeding on dead tissue.

- **Biotrophy**: Feeding on living host cells while keeping them alive.

- **Hemibiotrophy**: An initial biotrophic phase followed by necrotrophic phase.

- **Stealth biotrophy**: Minimizing host detection through suppression of plant defences.
