## The Molech Cult

King Josiah's reform desecrated Topheth, "that no one might burn his son or daughter as offering to
Molech" (II Kings 23:10). The traditional reading assumes child sacrifice, but the Levitical
prohibition states: "You shall not give your SEED to devote it to Molech" (Leviticus 18:21, 20:2-5).
The word "seed" (*zeraʿ*) is literal: semen, not children. The Molech cult involved sexual offering,
not infanticide.

Molech connects linguistically to Mallow, Moly (the herb Hermes gave Odysseus), and Greek *Mukēs*
("mushroom"). The root denotes the erect penis. The cult of Molech was another expression of the
same fertility religion that produced the Bacchic and Dionysian rites.
