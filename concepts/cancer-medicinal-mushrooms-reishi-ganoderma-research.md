## Anti-Cancer Activity

Both in vitro and in vivo studies show water-soluble extracts from
G. lucidum stimulate immune responses with antitumour effects. The action
of G. lucidum as an anti-cancer and anti-inflammatory agent is mediated
through cytokines released from activated immune effector cells. Triterpenoids
also contribute to anti-cancer activity. A Phase I/II study of G. lucidum
extract has been conducted (Zhou et al., 2001). No pure beta-glucan product
from G. lucidum has been commercially available to date, though beta-glucan
is one of the first BRMs for which the cellular mechanism of action has been
defined at the specific receptor level.
