# DOB and DOI Compound Profile

## The DO Series

The DO family (DimethOXY-amphetamine) consists of alpha-methylated phenethylamines with a 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted pattern. These are among the most potent psychedelic compounds known, with active doses measured in hundreds of micrograms rather than milligrams. The alpha-methylation converts them to amphetamines, dramatically extending their duration compared to their 2C counterparts.

## DOB (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-bromoamphetamine)

### Chemical Identity

**DOB**: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-bromoamphetamine. This is the alpha-methylated version of [[2c-b-compound-profile]].

Molecular formula: C11H16BrNO2

### Dosage

- **Threshold:** 0.5 mg (500 micrograms)
- **Light:** 1-2 mg
- **Common:** 1-3 mg
- **Strong:** 3-5 mg

DOB is extremely potent — the active dose is in the milligram range, with threshold effects at half a milligram.

### Duration

Effective duration: 18-30 hours (one of the longest durations of any psychedelic compound).

### Qualitative Effects

Shulgin describes DOB as producing intense and prolonged psychedelic effects:

- Powerful visual effects including complex geometric patterns, color enhancement, and visual distortion
- Significant stimulant effects due to the amphetamine structure
- Deep cognitive shifts and philosophical introspection
- Extended duration requiring significant time commitment
- A "heavy" body load compared to other psychedelics

The extended duration means that the experience can persist through a full day and into the next, with residual effects potentially lasting even longer. This makes careful dose selection and planning essential.

## DOI (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine)

### Chemical Identity

**DOI**: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine. The iodine analogue of DOB.

Molecular formula: C11H16INO2

### Dosage

- **Threshold:** 0.5 mg
- **Light:** 1-2.5 mg
- **Common:** 1.5-3 mg
- **Strong:** 3-5 mg

### Duration

Effective duration: 16-30 hours.

### Qualitative Effects

DOI produces effects similar in character to DOB but with the iodine atom's contribution to the subjective quality. Shulgin's notes describe it as a powerful, long-acting psychedelic with rich visual effects and a significant cognitive component.

### Research Utility

DOI has found particular use as a pharmacological research tool. It is one of the most selective 5-HT2A receptor agonists available and is widely used in laboratory research to study the 5-HT2A receptor system. Its receptor selectivity makes it valuable for understanding the mechanisms of psychedelic action.

## DOB vs. DOI Comparison

- **Potency**: Both are extremely potent, with active doses in the low milligram range
- **Duration**: Both have very long durations (16-30+ hours), making them among the longest-acting psychedelics
- **Stimulation**: Both carry significant stimulant effects from the amphetamine structure
- **Receptor selectivity**: DOI is particularly valued for its 5-HT2A selectivity
- **Safety**: Both require extreme caution in dosing due to their high potency

## DOM (STP)

Also in the DO family, DOM (2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine, nicknamed "STP") is historically significant as the compound involved in widespread distribution in the late 1960s. DOM has a shorter duration (8-12 hours) compared to DOB and DOI but shares the general character of the DO series. Shulgin documents DOM as compound #68 in PIHKAL.

## Safety Considerations

The extreme potency and extended duration of DOB and DOI make them particularly hazardous for casual use. Dose errors of even one milligram can result in dramatically different — and potentially overwhelming — experiences. The long duration means that once taken, the experience cannot be shortened. See [[shulgin-dosage-guidelines]] and [[psychedelic-mushroom-set-and-setting]].
