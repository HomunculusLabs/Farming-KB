## Not Being Led Astray by Circumstance

To look at things from a perspective that transcends time and place is to
prevent oneself from becoming captive to circumstance. Even science
constantly tries to avoid becoming too wrapped up in details and losing
sight of the larger picture. However, this "larger picture" is not the true
picture. There is another view that is broader and more all-encompassing.

In nature, a whole encloses the parts, and a yet larger whole encloses the
whole enclosing the parts. By enlarging our field of view, what is thought of
as a whole becomes, in fact, nothing more than one part of a larger whole.

on to infinity.
