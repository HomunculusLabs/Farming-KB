# Mushroom Cultivation Equipment and Sourcing

## Essential Equipment Overview

The Jarrold boil-a-bag mushroom cultivation method requires a specific set of

## Primary Containers: Boil-a-Bags

The foundational equipment is the "boil-a-bag"—plastic bags capable of withstanding
[[gottlieb-psilocybin-cultivation-spore-syringe-preparation]]. These must be oven-safe for sterilization before use.

## Spore Handling Tools

### Spore Print Jar
Clean glass jars with metal screw lids for storing spore prints. Sterilized by
placing on a baking tray under the oven grill at half maximum heat for 30 minutes.

### Syringe and Needle
A standard syringe with needle is used for inoculation. The guide recommends
creating a longer needle by removing the original needle from its plastic holder
and replacing it with thin stainless steel tubing. This modification allows the
needle to pass completely through the filter can for the long-needle inoculation
method.

### Stainless Steel Tubing for Extended Needle
- **Source:** Model shops, craft shops, or metal engineering companies
- **Tip:** Heat the tip before inserting into the syringe holder

### Metal Scraper (Flat-Edged)
A flat-edged metal scraper is used to loosen spores from the bottom of the spore
print jar. It must be flame-sterilized before each use with a spirit burner.

## Inoculation and Handling Tools

### Tweezers
Metal tweezers are used for handling sterile equipment and transferring mushroom
caps for spore printing. They are flame-sterilized before each use.

### Scissors
Metal scissors for cutting mushroom caps from stems during spore printing.
Also flame-sterilized before use.

### Cotton Wool Buds
Used for swabbing inoculation points with propyl alcohol before and during the
inoculation process. Essential for maintaining sterility at injection sites.

### Propyl Alcohol
Used as a surface sterilant for inoculation points, tools, and working surfaces.
Applied with cotton wool buds before needle insertion.

### Spirit Burner
Used as the heat source for flame-sterilizing metal tools including the scraper,
tweezers, scissors, and syringe needle. Must be fuelled with methylated spirits.
