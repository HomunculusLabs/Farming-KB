## The Quantum Basis of Hallucinatory States

McKenna argued that the major quantum mechanical phenomena experienced by
human beings, aside from waking consciousness itself, are dreams and
hallucinations. These states occur when the large amounts of radiation
conveyed into the body by the senses are restricted. Under these conditions,
interior images and interior processes become visible, and these processes
definitely arise at the quantum mechanical level.

The key insight comes from structure-activity relationship studies: if one
atom on the molecular ring of an inactive compound is moved, the compound
becomes highly active. For McKenna, this is a perfect proof of the dynamic
linkage at the formative level between quantum mechanically described matter
and mind. The extreme sensitivity of psychoactive response to molecular
geometry suggests that quantum-level interactions between the compound and
neural tissue are responsible for the observed effects.
