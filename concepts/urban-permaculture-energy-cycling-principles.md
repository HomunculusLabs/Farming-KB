# Urban Permaculture Energy Cycling Principles

Urban permaculture applies the same principles as [[permaculture-design-course-methodology]] to city environments, focusing on cycling energy and resources through small-scale systems that mimic natural ecosystems.

## Energy Cycling

In the forest, water falls from the sky, soaks into soil, moves through the land, rises through trees, and returns to the atmosphere. Urban permaculture mimics this by cycling flows of nutrients and energy (sun, water, wind, organic wastes) through systems:

- Kitchen waste recycled into [[cho-fermented-mixed-compost-methods]]
- Household graywater-systems|graywater channelled into gardens
- Leaves raked around trees as mulch
- Hand tools (scythes, wheelbarrows, forks, spades) replace expensive petroleum-dependent machinery
- Energy expended in food cultivation returns through eating

## Water Catchment

Water is intercepted before it runs off the property:
- Spring or rooftop water piped to ferrocement storage tanks
- Gravity-fed distribution to houses and gardens
- Goal: catch, store, and use all resources before they leave the property

## Closed Circle Economy

The aim is a closed circle where the community feeds itself without dependence on the global market:
- Supporting neighbourhood businesses over megamalls
- Keeping trade flowing locally
- Buying from family markets rather than distant corporations

## Accelerated Succession

Mimicking forest succession in urban settings:
- Pioneer species (like alder) fix nitrogen and build soil
- Each stage creates conditions for the next
- Shrub and ground cover layers develop
- Climax species eventually dominate

## Reclaiming Lost Knowledge

The urban permaculture movement emphasises reclaiming food-cultivation and land stewardship skills that are rapidly becoming lost arts:
- Seed saving and propagation
- [[grafting-fruit-trees]]
- Rooting hardwood cuttings
- Food preservation (jam, drying, threshing, wine making)
- Drip irrigation and biofertiliser
- Getting the timing right with soil, seeds, water, and sun

"Anyone can learn how to grow food and take care of themselves and the world around them."

## Species Lists for Urban Sites

The urban permaculture reader provides extensive species lists for various conditions:

### Very Dry Sites
Almond, black locust, carob, fig, mesquite, olive, pomegranate, rosemary, most aromatic herbs

### Nitrogen-Fixing Plants
**Temperate trees**: Alder, black locust, autumn olive, Siberian pea shrub, tagasaste
**Tropical trees**: Acacia, albizia, gliricidia, leucaena, pongamia

### Pest Control Plants
Sann hemp (nematodes), marigold (nematodes), pyrethrum daisy (insecticide), neem, tobacco (insecticide), derris root, rhubarb

### Water/Wetland Plants
Azolla, watercress, duckweed, water lily, wild rice, cattail, willows, cranberry

### Bee Forage
Almond, apple, bergamot, lavender, lucerne, rosemary
