## Safety Warnings

### UV Light Danger
UV light is very dangerous. In a flash, intense UV light can burn your
skin and the retinas in your eyes beyond repair. Never look at the UV
lamp in an ozone generator. Sneaking a peek has cost more than one
aspiring grower their eyesight.

### Ozone Exposure Limits
Legal exposure for humans is about 0.1 ppm for a maximum of 8 hours.
Most grow room ozone generators produce about 0.05 ppm at timed
intervals. Ozone is capable of burning your lungs at higher levels.
Never use too much ozone.

### Nitric Acid Byproduct
When UV light encounters moisture in the air, nitric acid is produced
as a byproduct. This white, powdery nitric acid collects around the
lamps at connection points. It is a very corrosive acid that will
severely burn skin and eyes.
