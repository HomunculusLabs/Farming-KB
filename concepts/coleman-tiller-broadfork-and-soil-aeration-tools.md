This approach contrasts sharply with conventional rotary tilling, which inverts the soil to the full depth of the tines and brings buried weed seeds throughout the profile.

## The Broadfork for Soil Aeration

Before using the Tilther to incorporate amendments, the soil is aerated by making a pass down the bed with a broadfork. The broadfork used at Four Season Farm has the following specifications:

- Two handles, each four feet long, positioned upright at either end of a 30-inch-wide metal crossbar
- Seven soil-penetrating tines, each ten inches long, attached to the crossbar

The operating technique requires the operator to apply foot pressure to press the tines fully into the soil, then pull back slightly on the handles to lift and loosen the lower soil. The tines are then raised out of the soil, the operator moves six inches further back, and the sequence is repeated down the length of the bed.

The broadfork serves a fundamentally different purpose than the Tilther. While the Tilther mixes amendments into the top two inches, the broadfork aerates and loosens the soil at greater depth (up to ten inches) without inverting it. This deep aeration:

- Relieves compaction from foot traffic and previous crops
- Improves drainage and water infiltration
- Creates channels for deeper root penetration of subsequent crops
- Allows air to reach deeper soil organisms
- Does not bring deep weed seeds to the surface because the soil is lifted rather than turned

## The Complete Bed Preparation Sequence

The full soil preparation process between succession crops in the greenhouse follows this order:

1. Clear away the old crop residues
2. Make a pass with the broadfork to aerate the deeper soil
3. Use the Tilther to incorporate any needed amendments such as alfalfa meal into the top two inches
4. Apply a layer of finished compost to the surface
5. Rake the bed smooth and mix in the compost shallowly with a 30-inch-wide Austrian hay rake (a wooden-tooth grading rake from a hardware store is a good alternative)
6. Firm the bed surface lightly with one pass of a 30-inch-wide mesh-surfaced roller
