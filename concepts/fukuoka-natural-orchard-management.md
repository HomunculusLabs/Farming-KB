### How Nature Builds Soil

The process of natural soil formation:
1. Wind and snow weather rock into boulders, then stones, then sand and earth
2. Microbes are nurtured, grasses appear, eventually towering trees grow
3. A layer of humus forms and deepens with annual leaf fall
4. Microorganisms multiply, turning soil rich black
5. Soil aggregates and softens, increasing water retention
