# KNF Fermented Seawater Preparation

Fermented seawater is a key Korean [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]]. Developed by Master Han-Kyu Cho, fermented
seawater leverages the concept of the brackish zone where freshwater meets
seawater, one of the most biologically productive environments on Earth. This
input provides abundant energy to crops and livestock, helps control fungal
diseases, and enhances fruit sugar content.

## The Science Behind Seawater in Agriculture

Fish live off seawater microbes. The top 3 cm of seawater depth from the
surface is an area swarming with microbial life. The deeper the seawater, the
higher the salt content, so surface seawater is preferred for agricultural use.
Fermented seawater creates an effect similar to the confluent point where
freshwater and seawater meet. Brackish conditions provide a favorable
environment for microbes, which in turn support plankton growth and the entire
aquatic food chain.

When the microbes of land and the minerals and microbes of seawater are
combined, organic matter in seawater produces an excellent effect. Master Cho
explained that this combination mirrors the productivity of estuarine
ecosystems, where the meeting of fresh and salt water creates conditions that
support extraordinary biological abundance.

## Seawater Collection and Storage

### Surface Seawater Collection

For best results, collect surface seawater rather than deep water. The deeper
seawater has higher salt content and fewer of the surface-dwelling microbes
that make fermented seawater effective. Collect from clean ocean areas away
from pollution sources, river mouths, and industrial discharge.

### Storage Before Fermentation

For storage, pour seawater in a large bowl and leave it uncovered for one day
to let airborne microbes colonize the water. This pre-fermentation step
introduces wild microorganisms that will participate in the fermentation
process. The seawater should be stored in a non-metallic container in a shaded
area.

## Fermented Seawater Recipe

### Ingredients

- Seawater: diluted with fresh water in ratio of 1:30
- Rice-washed water: diluted 1:200
- [[water-soluble-calcium-wca]] 15 to 20 days before
harvest. The minerals in seawater, particularly trace elements, contribute to
improved flavor and nutritional density of fruits and vegetables.

### Ripening Enhancement

Use seawater at dilution ratios of 1:30, 1:25, or 1:20 to enhance ripening.
The timing and concentration depend on the crop and [[brown-rice-vinegar-brv]] (BRV). If salt water is used
instead of natural seawater, dilute to 0.2% salinity. This treatment
strengthens seedlings against disease and improves establishment rates.

## Livestock Applications

### Poultry Health

To prevent feather-loss in chickens during summer, provide seawater at 1:10
to 1:15 dilution (or salt water at 0.1%), combined with BRV at 1:200 and
water-soluble calcium at 1:500 every other day. The minerals in seawater
support feather development and overall poultry health during [[master-cho]]
