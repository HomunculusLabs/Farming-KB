## Mushroom Substrate Bag Production

## Definition

Substrate is the material used to grow mushrooms — a mixture of all ingredients or "food" necessary for [[contamination-prevention-in-mushroom-cultivation]], as contamination or improper nutrient balance at this stage can ruin an entire crop.

## Materials

The following materials are required for producing substrate bags:

- **Plastic bags** — Polypropylene, 7" x 12.5" (for sawdust substrate); 8" x 12" (for rice straw substrate)
- **Plastic necks** — Approximately 4 cm diameter, used to form the bag opening
- **Cotton plugs** — Placed inside the plastic caps for gas exchange and contamination filtration
- **Cover filters** — Square filter paper placed over the neck opening for additional contamination protection
- **Rubber bands** — Used to secure the bag around the plastic neck/ring
- **Sawdust** — Primary base ingredient; cover the sawdust pile with rice sacks to maintain proper humidity during storage
- **Rice bran** — Provides supplemental carbohydrates and nitrogen
- **Calcium sulfate** — Source of calcium and sulfur; improves substrate structure
- **[[dighton-fungal-nutritional-composition-animal-diets]]
- **Center hole depth** — must reach nearly to the bottom but never puncture it, as this is the primary channel for spawn inoculation and mycelium spread
- **Timing** — complete the bagging process as quickly as possible once substrate is hydrated to reduce the window for contamination
- **Visual inspection** — check every bag for tears, holes, or improperly sealed necks before loading into racks; reject any defective bags

## Pasteurization and Next Steps

After the bags are loaded onto racks and transferred to the pasteurization chamber, they undergo sterilization to eliminate competing microorganisms. Once pasteurization is complete and the bags have cooled to room temperature, they are ready for inoculation with mushroom spawn. Proper handling during and after pasteurization is essential — any lapse in hygiene at this stage can undo all the careful work of [[bulk-substrate-preparation]].

## Safety Notes

- Work in a well-ventilated area when handling fine sawdust to avoid respiratory irritation
- Wear gloves when mixing additives, especially lime and mineral supplements
- Ensure pasteurization chamber is properly vented before opening to release steam safely
