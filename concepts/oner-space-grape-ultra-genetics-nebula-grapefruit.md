# Space Grape — Ultra Genetics

## Overview

This cross represents Ultra Genetics' strategy of combining their favorite fast-

result is a plant that is greater than the sum of its parts — retaining sativa
effects while gaining a terpene complexity that pure sativas rarely achieve.

## Plant Structure and Growth

Space Grape exhibits an interesting duality in its growth pattern. The default
structure is more typically indica — compact, bushy, with dense green buds. However,
the sativa influence can express itself under the right conditions:

- **Indoor growth:** When kept relatively small, Space Grape maintains its indica-like
  growth pattern. The plant is undemanding and "keeps itself to itself," requiring
  nothing special to reach its potential. This makes it an excellent choice for
  growers who want sativa effects without managing tall, lanky plants.
- **Outdoor growth:** Given adequate space and a long vegetative period, Space Grape
  can express more of its sativa heritage, potentially developing into a sativa-style
  tree. Outdoor plants in favorable conditions can become quite large, producing
  harvests that may require physical support.

Visual characteristics include slim, sharp green leaves that reveal the sativa
influence, contrasting with the dense, compact bud formation that reflects the
indica parent. This combination of sativa foliage with indica bud density is visually
distinctive and aesthetically appealing.

## Cultivation

Space Grape is bred for ease of cultivation. It is an excellent choice for growers
at all experience levels:

- **Flowering time:** Approximately 70 days (10 weeks).
- **Yield:** Above average to heavy. Outdoor plants with extended vegetative periods
  can produce massive harvests requiring assistance to bring down.
- **Nutrient needs:** Standard. The plant is not particularly demanding and responds
  well to regular feeding schedules.
- **Climate adaptability:** Performs well both indoors and outdoors. Not particularly
  sensitive to temperature fluctuations.
- **Difficulty:** Low. Space Grape "doesn't require anything special to grow to its
  potential," making it an ideal strain for less experienced cultivators.

The combination of manageable height, above-average yield, and low maintenance
requirements makes Space Grape one of the most practical sativa-dominant choices
in the Oner guide for home growers with limited space.

## Aroma and Flavor

Space Grape's flavor profile is one of its strongest attributes. The dominant notes
are fruity and berry-forward, with a sweetness that has a slight tang on the finish.
The Grapefruit parent's influence is immediately apparent — bright citrus and grapefruit
rind notes complement the deeper berry flavors. The smoke is notably smooth, making
it easy to consume in larger quantities.

The name "Space Grape" perfectly captures this flavor experience: the grape/berry
notes are grounded and fruity, while the effect is "spacey" and cerebral. The terpene
profile likely features myrcene (berry), limonene (citrus), and caryophyllene
