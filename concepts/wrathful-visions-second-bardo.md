## The Third Bardo: The Period of Re-Entry (Sidpa Bardo)

## Introduction

The [[third-bardo]], the *[[sidpa-bardo]]* or "Bardo of Seeking Rebirth," begins when the voyager cannot hold on to the knowledge that all visions were projections of his own mind. Consciousness struggles to regain routine reality and the ego — the transition from transcendent reality to ordinary waking life.

## Time and Impact

The Tibetans estimate approximately **50% of the entire psychedelic experience** is spent in the Third Bardo by most normal people. The types of re-entry can **profoundly color subsequent attitudes and feelings** about oneself and the world for weeks or even months. A negative session can be redeemed by positive re-entry; a happy experience can be made valueless by fearful re-entry.

## Key Instructions

1. **Do nothing** — stay calm, passive, and relaxed, no matter what happens
2. **Recognize where you are** — failure leads to fear-driven premature and unfavorable re-entry

Only through recognition can the voyager maintain the calm, passive concentration necessary for favorable re-entry. Multiple recognition-points are provided so failure at one can be compensated by success at another.

## Three Purposes of Third Bardo Teachings

1. Free the voyager from Third Bardo traps
2. Prolong the session, ensuring maximum degree of illumination
3. Select a favorable re-entry — returning to a wiser, more peaceful post-session personality

Only persons of extremely advanced spiritual development can accomplish complete liberation by exercising the [[transference-principle]] at ego-death. For most, return to game reality is inevitable.

## Characteristic Third Bardo Experiences

The person descends step by step into lower, more constricted states of consciousness. Each step may be preceded by swooning into unconsciousness. Reality seems dull, static, hard, angular, and puppet-like by contrast with earlier phases. Narrow, primitive elements from repressed aspects of personal history take over — powerful impulses made overwhelming by Bardo suggestibility. The more enlightened consciousness of the first two Bardos and the civilized elements of ordinary waking life are shelved in favor of obsessive, incoherent instinctual parts of the voyager's total personality.

Key signs include:

- **Supernormal perception** — feelings of clairvoyance, telepathy, ESP; the manual warns against fascination with these powers, as they impede higher development until the selfish nature is completely mastered. The Tibetan text attributes paranormal faculties to Bardo consciousness, encompassing both past and future elements, though this remains an open empirical question.

- **Panic, torture, and persecution** — involving the person's own "skin-encapsulated ego"; mind-controlling figures and demons symbolize the evil passions (anger, lust, stupidity, pride, jealousy, control-power) binding mankind to game-networks ([[samsara]]). The person may plead or demand to be brought "out of it" through antidotes and tranquillizers, may see themselves falling down terrifying precipices. One should neither flee the pain nor pursue the pleasure — recognition alone is necessary.

- **Restless, unhappy wandering** — driven by "winds of karma," with desperate desires to phone family, doctors, or friends and appeal to them to pull you out. This should be resisted; the guide and fellow voyagers provide the best assistance. Involving outsiders in one's hallucinatory world will fail since they cannot understand what is happening. Merely to recognize these desires as Third Bardo manifestations is already the first step toward liberation.

## Summary

The wrathful visions represent the intellect's final resistance to consciousness expansion — the dynamic, terrifying face of the same enlightenment that appears peaceful in its completed form. Recognition transforms these horrors into liberation. For those who cannot recognize, the Third Bardo awaits: a prolonged re-entry where return quality profoundly shapes the voyager's psychological and spiritual life for weeks or months to come. Preparation, the guide's presence, and the manual remain the essential safeguards.

## Key Takeaways

- The 58 wrathful [[herukas]] are the peaceful deities in dynamic aspect — not enemies but manifestations of enlightenment
- Westerners experience them as technological and bureaucratic horrors rather than mythological demons
- The wrathful visions originate from the Brain chakra — the intellect defending its boundaries
- Recognition ("welcome them like old friends") is the sole mechanism of liberation
- The manual ("The Great Liberation by Hearing") can liberate through hearing alone
- The Third Bardo occupies roughly 50% of the psychedelic experience for most people
- The quality of re-entry determines post-session psychological impact for weeks or months
- Key Third Bardo instruction: do nothing, stay calm, recognize where you are
