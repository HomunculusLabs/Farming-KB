## PAH Metabolism by Non-Ligninolytic Fungi

The vast majority of fungi grow on substrates other than wood and do not
produce extracellular lignin peroxidases. Many of these non-ligninolytic
fungi have been found to metabolize PAHs through cytochrome P450
pathways. Genera include Cunninghamella, Mucor, Rhizopus, Mortierella,
Aspergillus, Penicillium, Fusarium, and many others. These fungi typically
cannot use PAHs as carbon or energy sources but co-metabolize them while
growing on other substrates, reducing their toxic or mutagenic properties.

## Specific Metabolites Produced

Different fungi produce characteristic PAH metabolite profiles. C. elegans
metabolizes acenaphthene to 6-hydroxyacenaphthenone,
1,2-acenaphthenedione, and various dihydrodiol derivatives. Fluorene is
metabolized to 9-fluorenol, 9-fluorenone, and 2-hydroxy-9-fluorenone.
Anthracene is oxidized to anthracene trans-1,2-dihydrodiol and
1-anthryl sulfate. Phenanthrene yields specific trans-dihydrodiol
enantiomers. Benzo[a]pyrene is hydroxylated through cytochrome P450-
mediated reactions to form the 7,8-diol-9,10-epoxide.
