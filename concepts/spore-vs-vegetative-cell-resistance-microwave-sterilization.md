## Elastomeric Penetration and Enclosed System Sterilization

A notable feature of the NASA technique is its ability to penetrate elastomeric
materials. Microwave energy passes through rubber and polymer components,
enabling sterilization of fully enclosed systems inaccessible to conventional
surface treatments. This has significant implications for spacecraft hardware
sterilization (sealed components without disassembly, critical for planetary
protection under COSPAR guidelines), medical device reprocessing, and
pharmaceutical containment systems.
