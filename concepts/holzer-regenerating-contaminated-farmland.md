# Holzer Regenerating Contaminated Farmland

When farmers decide to become self-sufficient, they must heal ground
contaminated by decades of artificial fertilisers and pesticides. Every
country has legal procedures and time spans regulating the transition from
conventional to certified organic agriculture, but Holzer's methods can
dramatically reduce this transition period.

## The Ukraine Example

The Ukraine, once Europe's breadbasket, illustrates both the potential of
rich soil and the damage caused by over-exploitation. Fields with
world-famous black soil (chernozem), several metres deep and composed of
loose dark humus, have been damaged by unchecked use of toxins. Crops are
failing and the produce is chemically contaminated. The ground is saturated
with toxins and groundwater has become polluted.

## Two Rules of Thumb for Regeneration

1. The rougher the structure of the soil, the more air will get in, and
   the quicker the soil will heal actively. [[holzer-catch-crop-and-polyculture-design]]

   ploughing
3. If the soil is compacted with high clay content, plough before winter

### The Science of Soil Detoxification

The degradation of toxins in soil requires three elements:
- Oxygen (from air reaching the soil)
- Good root systems (reaching deep into contaminated layers)
- Active [[lupinus-angustifolius]]):**
- Roots can grow several metres into the soil
- Perennial plant with abundant flowers and seeds in its second year
- Seeds can be harvested and sold
- Very hardy and will self-seed
- Legume that fixes nitrogen, boosting neighbouring plant growth

**Red Clover:**
- Excellent nitrogen fixer
- Attracts bees and other [[comparison-beneficial-insects-guide-vs-integrated-pest-management]]
- Supports root growth of neighbouring plants

**[[growing-root-vegetables-guide]]:**
- Carrots, daikon, and Jerusalem artichokes
- Left to rot and decompose in fields for the first few years
- Activate soil life and aid the purification process

### How Deep Rooting Helps

A diverse root system reaching far and deep will:
- Air and loosen compacted soil
- Activate dormant soil life
- Speed up the detoxification process
- Bring nutrients from deep layers to the surface
- Create channels for water infiltration

## Dealing with Acid Soil

Farmers typically use lime to treat acidic soil, but this treats the
symptom rather than the cause. It requires repeated application and creates
dependency.

### Understanding the Root Causes

High acidity can result from:
- Overuse of fertilisers (the ground becomes oversaturated with nutrients,
  like eating bacon without bread day after day)
- Over-cultivation of soil
- Too much nitrogen in the ground causing crops to rot in the field

### Holzer's Natural Approach

Good rooting and aeration of the ground are the best measures against
high acidity:

1. **Mechanical loosening:** Dig 50cm deep with a mini-digger and mix,
   bringing unaffected soil higher up to help balance the soil profile

2. **Biological balancing:** Plant red and [[fukuoka-white-clover-cover-crop-system]], foxglove,
   comfrey, and lupin to root and activate the soil

3. **Polyculture approach:** Add vegetables, especially root vegetables,
   so plants support each other and provide all required nutrients

## Managing Insect Overpopulation

Insect overpopulation is a visible sign of incorrect cultivation, usually
indicating the use of agricultural chemicals. The fukuoka [[fukuoka-straw-pest-control-natural-balance]] has been
disturbed.

### The Pesticide Trap

- Using pesticides at the first sign of pests is a short-term solution
- It treats the symptom, not the cause
- Natural predators (ladybirds, earwigs) are at the end of the food chain
- Toxins accumulate in predator bodies, killing them
- Pests develop resistance and thrive without predators
- Monoculture crops are eventually doomed

### Holzer's Alternative

There is only one way to protect crops from pests: do not plant
monocultures. Instead:
- Encourage natural predators by providing habitats
- Watch and learn from nature
- Ask what nature is telling you about the site
