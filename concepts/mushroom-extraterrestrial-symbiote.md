## History as Eschatological Shockwave

McKenna's broader cosmological vision frames human history as the shockwave
of eschatology. Something is at the end of time and is casting an enormous
shadow over human history, drawing all human becoming toward it. All the wars,
the philosophies, the migrations, the cities, the civilizations — all of this
is occupying a microsecond of geological, planetary, and galactic time as the
monkeys react to the symbiote.

McKenna did not belong to the school that attributes all human accomplishments
to knowledge given as a gift from friendly aliens. He described something he
hoped was more profound: as nervous systems evolve to higher and higher levels,
they come more and more to understand the true situation in which they are
embedded, and the true situation is an organism, an organization of intelligence
on a galactic scale.
