# Mushroom Growing Room Design

Professional growing room design from Stamets' Mushroom Cultivator. For equipment lists, sterile workspace setup, and fruiting chambers, see [[mushroom-grow-equipment]]. For indoor cultivation methods, see [[mushroom-indoor-cultivation]].

## Air-to-Bed Ratio

The growing room air volume must be matched to the substrate type:
- **Thin grain layers**: 4:1 air-to-bed ratio (or less)
- **Compost beds**: 5:1 air-to-bed ratio
- Design to take full advantage of the substrate's own heat-generating capability to reduce energy consumption

## Ventilation System

Minimum 3-5 air exchanges per hour while maintaining 85-92% humidity. Stagnant air pockets cause Trichoderma blooms and deformed fruitbodies. Design for fresh air intake and recirculation mixing. Place thermostats/humidistats in the recirculation inlet air stream.

## Cooling

- **Commercial**: Cooling coils (cold water or glycol) placed before the fan, supplied by central chiller or underground tank
- **Home cultivator**: Fresh air cooling — close vents during hot day, open fully at night when temperatures are lowest
- Avoid air conditioning that dehumidifies excessively

## Humidification (3 Methods from Stamets)

1. **Steam injection**: Steam into the air duct downstream of fan and filter. Household vaporizers well-suited for small rooms
2. **Atomizing nozzles**: Fine mist into air stream; nozzle emits 0.5-1.0 gal/hr at 20-30 psi; filter water supply to prevent clogging
3. **Evaporative pad ("swamp cooler")**: Air passes through coarse mesh absorbent saturated with water; also provides cooling

## Lighting (Stamets)

- **Fluorescent**: Most practical and broadest coverage; mount vertically on walls or horizontally on ceiling above center aisle; remove ballasts outside the room to eliminate heat
- **Best tubes**: "Daylite 65" (6500K) or "Vita-Lite" (5500K) — provide 140+ microwatts/10nm/lumen of blue spectra (440-495 nm) needed for primordia formation in Pleurotus, Psilocybe, and other photosensitive species
- Cool-white has only 100-110 microwatts/nm/lumen; warm-white only 40-50
- Mount at least 18 inches above cropping surface

## Pressure Canner Specifications (Stamets)

For grain spawn and supplemented substrate sterilization at 15-18 PSI:
- **23-quart pressure canner** (e.g., Presto): Standard for home growers; fits 7 quart jars or 14 pint jars
- **41-quart pressure canner** (All-American 941): Serious hobbyist; fits 10-14 quart jars; weighted gauge, no rubber gasket to replace
- **Sterilization at 15 PSI**: 60-90 min for quart grain jars; 90-120 min for half-gallon jars; 2-2.5 hours for substrate bags
