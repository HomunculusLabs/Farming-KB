## Fungal Endophytes in Grass and Crop Systems
Fungal endophytes of grasses and crop plants represent one of the
most important plant-fungal interactions in both natural and
[[tropical-rainforest-seedling-mycorrhiza-ecological-restoration]].
## Two Major Types of Grass Endophytes
### Clavicipitaceous Endophytes (Systemic)
These endophytes colonize virtually all tissues of grass hosts and
are found in the seed coat and near the embryo:
- Systemic throughout the plant body
- Vertically transmitted through seeds
- Often mutualistic, providing herbivore deterrence through
  alkaloid production
- Well-studied in genera Neotyphodium and Epichloe
- The basis of endophyte-enhanced tall fescue and perennial
  ryegrass pastures
### P-Endophytes (Non-Systemic)
Species such as Phialophora and Gliocladium represent a different
colonization strategy:
- Nonsystemic but also seed-borne (An et al. 1993)
- More restricted tissue colonization
- Less studied than clavicipitaceous endophytes
- Found in diverse grass species worldwide
## Endophytes in Non-Grass Hosts
Scattered but significant reports exist of systemic seed-borne
endophytes in nongrass hosts:
- **Casuarina** — Phomopsis casuarinae (Bose 1947)
- **Helianthemum** — systemic colonization (Boursnell 1950)
- **Ericaceae** — systemic colonization (Rayner 1915, 1929)

- **Disease resistance** — endophytes can suppress pathogenic fungi
  and bacteria through antibiosis or induced host defenses
- **Growth promotion** — certain endophytes produce plant growth
  hormones such as auxins and gibberellins
## Alkaloid Classes and Their Effects
Endophyte-produced alkaloids fall into several classes with distinct
effects on herbivores:
- **Ergot alkaloids** — affect mammalian physiology; cause fescue
  toxicosis in livestock grazing endophyte-infected tall fescue
- **Lolitrems** — cause ryegrass staggers in sheep and cattle
- **Peramine** — deters insect herbivores without affecting mammals
- **Loline alkaloids** — toxic to insects, relatively safe for
  livestock; important for pasture endophyte selection
## Endophyte-Enhanced Cultivars
Commercial development of endophyte-enhanced grass cultivars has
been a major success story. Novel endophytes (Neotyphodium and
Epichloe strains selected for beneficial alkaloid profiles) are
introduced into elite pasture grass germplasm. These selected
endophytes provide insect deterrence and stress tolerance while
minimizing the animal health problems associated with wild-type
endophytes. The technology is widely used in New Zealand,
Australia, and the United States for improved pastoral productivity.
