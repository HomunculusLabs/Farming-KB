## No Reflective Hood Lighting (Bare Bulb)

One option for [[jorge-cervantes]] explores in Chapter 9
of *Marijuana Horticulture*.

## How Bare Bulb Lighting Works

Without a reflective hood, the lamp emits direct light downward only.
No reflected light reaches the canopy from above. This means less
total light reaches the plants compared to a properly hooded setup,
but the light that does reach them is entirely direct (unreflected).

The key characteristic is that the bulb runs cooler without the
confined heat of a hood. This can allow the lamp to be positioned
closer to the plant canopy without causing heat damage.

## Reflective Hood Study

Cervantes constructed a test room (10 x 10 feet / 3 x 3 m) with
everything painted black inside. The floor was covered with black tar
paper, reflecting less than three percent of light. Foot-candle
readings were taken every 12 inches (30 cm) on a floor matrix.

Five different lamps were tested, all positioned three feet from the
floor:
- 1000W super [[cervantes-reflective-hoods-light-distribution]] from different
  manufacturers. Some companies do not test their hoods before market.
- When a reflector distributes light evenly, the lamp can be placed
  closer to plants for more effective illumination.
- Horizontal reflectors deliver many more lumens to the canopy than
  vertical setups.
- The larger the wattage, the more efficient the bulb, but light
  intensity diminishes quickly with distance. Multiple lower-wattage
  lamps provide better distribution.

## Adding Light Without More Electricity

Any unused light is wasted. Cervantes suggests several ways to
maximize light utilization without adding wattage:

- Use several 400W or 600W lamps instead of 1000W units
