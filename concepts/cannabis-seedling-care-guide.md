## Common Seedling Problems

### Stretching (Leggy Seedlings)

**Cause**: Insufficient light intensity causes the seedling to stretch toward the light source

**Fix**:
- Move lights closer
- Add supplemental light
- Ensure the light is on 18+ hours per day

### Damping Off

**Cause**: [[cervantes-green-algae-growing-medium]]
- Do not overwater — keep moist, not soaked
- Ensure good air circulation
- Avoid splashing water on the stem
- Some growers sprinkle cinnamon or use chamomile tea as natural antifungal treatments

**Treatment**: Once damping off occurs, the seedling usually cannot be saved. Prevention is key.

### Nutrient Burn in Seedlings

**Cause**: Feeding nutrients too early or in too-high concentration

**Symptoms**: Leaf tips turn yellow or brown, curling, scorched appearance on new growth
