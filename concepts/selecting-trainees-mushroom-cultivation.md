Trainers must ensure the candidate is able, committed, and ready to leave

Trainers sit together and discuss their visits, reviewing each candidate
and deciding whether to select them, giving reasons for each decision.

## Group Size and Trainer Ratio

The number of trainees depends on the number of trainers. A ratio of 5:1
or 6:1 has proven successful. With only five to six trainees per trainer,
it is possible to become closer to trainees and better understand their
physical, psychological, and emotional needs during training.
