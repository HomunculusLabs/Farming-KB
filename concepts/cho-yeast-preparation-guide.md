# Cho's Yeast Preparation and Applications

Yeast is a microorganism that plays a critical role in the final stage of fermentation within Dr. Cho's Korean Natural Farming system. It breaks down sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide, enhances metabolism, and helps resynthesize decomposed organic substances into amino acids, hormones, vitamins, and other [[query-what-are-the-main-bioactive-compounds-in-medicinal-plants]]. In KNF, yeast is primarily used as a recovery agent for stressed or diseased plants and livestock.

## Functions of Yeast

Yeast performs several essential biological functions that make it valuable in natural farming:

1. **Fermentation completion** — Yeast handles the last step of fermentation, converting sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide. This is essential in producing inputs like [[master-cho-knf-ffj-fermented-fruit-juice-guide]].

2. **Metabolic enhancement** — Yeast expands capillary vessels and enhances overall [[arbuscule-isolation-metabolic-activity-assays]] in plants and animals.

3. **Nutrient resynthesis** — Yeast resynthesizes organic substances decomposed by various microbes into amino acids, hormones, vitamins, and other compounds that plants and animals can readily utilize.

4. **Chemical fertilizer decomposition** — Yeast has the excellent ability to decompose [[cervantes-chemical-fertilizers-guide]], making it useful during the [[query-how-to-transition-from-conventional-to-no-till-farming]] to organic farming.

5. **Vitamin and mineral production** — Yeast produces various substances including vitamins, nucleic acids, minerals, hormones, and fatty acids that organisms cannot synthesize for themselves.

## Yeast Collection and Cultivation

### Source Materials

The preferred culture media for yeast collection in Cho's system are:

- **Grapes** — The richest natural source of surface yeast. Do not wash the grapes before use, as this would remove the wild yeast.

- **Strawberries** — Another excellent source of natural yeast. Same no-wash principle applies.

### Cultivation Guidelines

The following protocols ensure successful yeast cultivation:

1. **Do not wash** — Use the fruit as-is. Washing removes the natural yeast colonies on the fruit surface.

2. **Sterilize tools** — All tools and containers must be sterilized before use to prevent contamination by unwanted microorganisms.

3. **Do not seal** — Cover the container loosely with its lid. Complete sealing would create anaerobic conditions unfavorable to yeast.

4. **Separate location** — Keep the container in a dedicated area away from other fermentation activities to prevent unwanted microbial cross-contamination and odor transfer.

5. **Temperature control** — Maintain 23-25 degrees Celsius and 65-70% relative humidity.

6. **Daily agitation** — Shake the container 1-2 times daily to protect against fungal contamination on the surface.

### Harvesting

After approximately 7 days, the yeast culture is ready. The liquid is strained and can be used directly as a plant spray (diluted) or added to livestock feed. For longer storage, the yeast can be preserved with -treated soil.

- **Recovery agent** — For stressed or diseased plants, increase concentration to 1:200 and apply as both foliar spray and soil drench for 3-5 consecutive days.

### For Livestock

- **Appetite recovery** — When livestock lose appetite or vigor, yeast added to feed or water can stimulate digestive function.

- **Digestive support** — Yeast aids in breaking down feed components and improving nutrient absorption.

- **General health** — Regular low-dose yeast supports overall gastrointestinal health in all livestock species.
