# Ecstasy - The Heart Opening Psychedelic

D.M. Turner rates ecstasy (MDMA) at intensity 2 to 4, making it the mildest psychedelic in the guide. It is described as a "selective psychedelic" or empathogen.

## Material

Ecstasy is a synthetic compound (3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine, MDMA) developed in 1914 as a potential dietary aid. Psychoactive effects were not discovered until the mid-1970s. Used widely in therapy from that time until it was made illegal in 1985. Street names include "X," "E," or "Adam." Usually comes as white crystalline powder or tablets.

## Dosage

- **125 mg (1/8 gram):** Standard single dose; experience lasts ~4 hours, intensity drops off after ~2 hours
- **Double dosing:** Additional 125 mg when first dose starts wearing off (common recreational pattern)
- **Turner's preference:** Single large dose of 150-170 mg for intense but shorter experience, reducing side effects
- Should be taken on an empty stomach; a full stomach may prevent the high entirely or delay it

## The Ecstasy Experience

### Onset and Phases
Coming on feels fantastically exhilarating: blissed out, energetic, emotionally opened and loving. The ride up lasts 30 minutes to an hour, followed by a plateau of 1-2 hours, then a slow drift back to baseline.

### Key Characteristics

**Not truly psychedelic:** Unlike LSD or mushrooms, ecstasy does not produce fine-level hallucination detail. Many users experience no visual phenomena at all. It does not heighten senses to infinite sharpness.

**Empathogenic qualities:** Called an empathogen for its ability to facilitate emotional empathy and communication. Called a "selective psychedelic" because it opens the mind in a psychedelic way but leaves much of the personality and perceptual structure intact. This makes it usable by people who could not handle LSD.

**Consistent experiences:** More than any other psychedelic, ecstasy produces very similar experiences across different people, generally described as loving and emotionally opened.

**First-time magic:** Most users report their first couple experiences are like being in heaven. Subsequent experiences are still enjoyable but cannot match the initial ride.

### Deep Bonding
Lasting beneficial experiences derive primarily from deep bonding with other people while high. Best results occur with one other person or a small group of close friends, paralleling therapeutic use. The atmosphere of nonjudgment and acceptance is conducive to deep bonding and healing. The more general "connectedness" feeling at large events like raves tends to dissipate, leaving one feeling hollow.

### Negative Aspects
- One of the hardest psychedelics to come down from (especially double-dosed)
- Depression as blissful feelings slip away
- Personality feels "sticky" during return; fewer options choosing return personality than traditional psychedelics
- Side effects: speedy feeling, jaw clenching (amphetamine family), loss of appetite, possible hangover

## Safety Factors

**Heat Stroke Risk:** Several deaths from heat stroke in dance clubs where users danced for hours without drinking fluids. Stay hydrated.

**Neurotoxicity:** Large doses produced neurotoxicity (reduced serotonin levels) in lab animals. Damage was significantly less to non-existent with smaller doses equivalent to 100-150 mg for humans. A single dose of Prozac taken up to 6 hours after ecstasy completely blocked neurotoxic effects in lab animals.

**Harm Reduction:**
- Take a single dose rather than double-dosing to reduce hangover
- Take amino acids (DL-Phenylalanine/DLPA) or other neurotransmitter precursors before and after
- Anti-oxidant formulas may help prevent free radical damage
- Prozac at trip's end may reduce next-day negative effects (controversial; test reaction to Prozac alone first)
- Some users report no negative next-day effects with single doses

## Pharmacology

MDMA acts primarily as a serotonin releaser and reuptake inhibitor, with additional effects on dopamine and norepinephrine systems. The empathogenic effects are largely attributed to the massive serotonin release, which activates 5-HT1A, 5-HT2A, and 5-HT2C receptors. The dopaminergic release contributes to the stimulant properties and the "speedy" feeling users report.

## Therapeutic Applications

MDMA-assisted psychotherapy is currently in Phase 3 clinical trials for treatment of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). Results from MAPS-sponsored trials have shown significant reductions in PTSD symptoms, with 67% of participants no longer qualifying for PTSD diagnosis after three sessions of MDMA-assisted therapy. The drug's empathogenic properties appear to reduce the fear response associated with traumatic memories, allowing patients to process trauma that would otherwise be overwhelming. The FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy designation to MDMA-assisted psychotherapy for PTSD in 2017.

## Combinations

- **LSD ("Candyflip"):** Much deeper and more visual than ecstasy alone, but ecstasy's heavier signature dominates. Residual acid provides smoother recovery.
- **Nitrous Oxide:** Growing in popularity, especially in rave scenes.
- **2C-B:** In therapeutic use, 2C-B is taken at the tail end of ecstasy to develop and retain insights.
- **MAO Inhibitors:** May be significantly intensified and possibly dangerous.
