# Auto-Flowering Sativa Development

Auto-flowering [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] represent one of the most significant
breeding innovations in modern [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-fungi-cannabis-cultivation]]. By incorporating
*[[cannabis-ruderalis]]* genetics, breeders created plants that flower
based on age rather than photoperiod, dramatically reducing the time
from [[query-how-to-grow-chili-peppers-from-seed-to-harvest]]. The challenge has been preserving desirable
sativa characteristics — tall stature, cerebral highs, complex flavors
— within the auto-flowering framework.

## The Ruderalis Foundation

*Cannabis ruderalis* is a subspecies native to Central and Eastern
Europe and Russia, characterized by its small stature (typically under
2 feet), auto-flowering habit, and relatively low THC content. In its
native range, ruderalis evolved to complete its entire life cycle
during the short [[extending-growing-season]], flowering automatically after reaching
a certain age regardless of day length.

The auto-flowering trait is controlled by specific genes that trigger
the flowering cascade independently of photoperiod. When crossed with
photoperiod-sensitive *C. sativa* or *C. indica* strains, this trait
can be inherited as a dominant or semi-dominant characteristic,
producing hybrids that combine the auto-flowering habit with the
potency and flavor of their non-ruderalis parent.

## The Joint Doctor and Lowryder

Canada's Joint Doctor is credited with revolutionizing auto-flowering
cannabis with the release of Lowryder. This groundbreaking strain
proved that auto-flowering genetics could produce worthwhile cannabis
in an extremely compressed timeline.

Lowryder's impact on the worldwide cannabis community cannot be
overstated. For the first time, growers could produce harvestable bud
in approximately 60-70 days from seed, without any need to manage
light cycles. This opened cannabis cultivation to people with limited
space, limited time, and limited growing experience.

The Joint Doctor continued refining the concept with Lowryder #2,
which improved upon the original with increased [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]]
better flavor profiles. Lowryder #2 became the foundation for
numerous subsequent auto-flowering crosses.

## Easy Ryder: Sativa-Dominant Auto-Flowering

Easy Ryder, bred by High Bred Seeds (the Joint Doctor's company),
represents a significant step in bringing sativa qualities to
auto-flowering genetics. It is a stabilized F1 cross of Lowryder #2
and Auto AK-47.

The Auto AK-47 parent contributes a typically indica-style growth
pattern — squat stature and abundant side branching — while the
carefully selected Lowryder #2 phenotype brings phenomenal resin
production and a fruity aroma.

Despite being slightly sativa-dominant, Easy Ryder stays compact,
rarely exceeding 2 feet in height. It completes flowering in just
56 days, with a total life cycle of approximately 70 days from
germination to harvest. The recommended [[light-schedule-cannabis]] is 20 hours
of light and 4 hours of darkness through the entire life cycle.
Being an auto, it does not need a photoperiod change to trigger
flowering, but more light produces more bud in the compressed
timeline.

Outdoor growers can manage multiple harvests per season, with the
best results from May through August. The strain's short lifecycle
makes it possible to achieve several complete grows in a single
outdoor season.
