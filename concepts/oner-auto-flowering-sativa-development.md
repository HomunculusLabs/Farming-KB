## Easy Ryder: Sativa-Dominant Auto-Flowering

Easy Ryder, bred by High Bred Seeds (the Joint Doctor's company),
represents a significant step in bringing sativa qualities to
auto-flowering genetics. It is a stabilized F1 cross of Lowryder #2
and Auto AK-47.

The Auto AK-47 parent contributes a typically indica-style growth
pattern — squat stature and abundant side branching — while the
carefully selected Lowryder #2 phenotype brings phenomenal resin
production and a fruity aroma.
