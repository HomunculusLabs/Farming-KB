# Cannabis for Chronic Pain

Chronic pain is the most common reason patients use medical cannabis, and it has the strongest evidence base of any therapeutic application. Multiple systematic reviews and meta-analyses support cannabis as effective for neuropathic pain and chronic non-cancer pain.

## Types of Pain Treated

### Neuropathic Pain
- Diabetic neuropathy, post-herpetic neuralgia, HIV neuropathy, chemotherapy-induced neuropathy
- Evidence level: Strong (multiple RCTs)
- THC-dominant products generally more effective than CBD alone
- Mechanism: CB1 activation in pain pathways reduces hyperexcitability of damaged nerves

### Inflammatory Pain
- Rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis
- Evidence level: Moderate
- CBD plays a larger role due to anti-inflammatory properties via CB2 modulation
- Topical applications effective for localized joint pain

### Nociceptive Pain
- Musculoskeletal pain, headaches, menstrual cramps
- Evidence level: Moderate
- Whole-plant products (with terpenes like myrcene and caryophyllene) enhance analgesic effects

### Cancer Pain
- Evidence level: Moderate to strong
- Particularly effective for breakthrough pain not controlled by opioids
- Opioid-sparing effect: patients using cannabis require lower opioid doses

## Dosing Strategies

### Acute Pain
- Higher THC ratio (1:1 to 3:1 THC:CBD)
- Inhalation for rapid onset (smoking or vaporizing)
- 2.5-10mg THC per dose as needed

### Chronic Maintenance
- Lower THC, higher CBD (1:3 to 1:10 THC:CBD)
- Oral tinctures or edibles for sustained effect
- Microdosing: 1-2.5mg THC 2-3 times daily
- Avoid psychoactive impairment during daily activities

### Topical Application
- CBD-dominant topicals for localized pain
- No psychoactive effects from topical use
- Effective for arthritis, muscle soreness, neuropathic pain in hands/feet

## Key Terpenes for Pain
- **Myrcene**: Muscle relaxant, sedative (found in indica strains)
- **Beta-caryophyllene**: Anti-inflammatory, CB2 agonist (found in black pepper, cannabis)
- **Pinene**: Anti-inflammatory, bronchodilator
- **Linalool**: Analgesic, anxiolytic (found in lavender, cannabis)

## Opioid Crisis Context
Cannabis shows significant promise as an opioid alternative or adjunct. States with medical cannabis laws see 25% fewer opioid overdose deaths. Patients using cannabis for chronic pain report reducing or eliminating opioid use in many cases.
