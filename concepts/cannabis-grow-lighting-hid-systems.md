# Cannabis Grow Lighting — HID Systems, Spectra, and Setup

## Overview

Lighting is the single most critical factor in indoor cannabis cultivation. The choice of light system determines growth rate, yield potential, and plant health. High Intensity Discharge (HID) lights — specifically Metal Halide (MH) and High Pressure Sodium (HPS) — are the traditional standard for cannabis cultivation.

## HID Light Types

### Metal Halide (MH)

- **Spectrum**: Blue-white light.
- **Best for**: Vegetative growth and seedlings.
- **Why**: Blue light promotes compact, bushy vegetative growth with short internodal spacing.
- **Visual appearance**: Gives off a blue tone.

### High Pressure Sodium (HPS)

- **Spectrum**: Orange-red light.
- **Best for**: Flowering.
- **Why**: Red/orange spectrum promotes bud development and flowering.
- **Visual appearance**: Gives off an orange tone.

### Optimal Setup

Many experienced growers use a **two-stage approach**:
- **MH for vegetative growth** — promotes healthy vegetative structure.
- **HPS for flowering** — maximizes bud production.

Some growers use MH throughout, and some use HPS throughout. Both approaches work, but the two-stage method optimizes each growth phase.

## Light Specifications

- **Wattage range**: 75W to 4000W.
- **Coverage area**: Depends on wattage — roughly 50W per square foot as a rule of thumb.
- The lights come in the **perfect spectrum for growing cannabis** and are highly recommended.

## Buying Considerations

- Keep a **spare bulb** at all times — bulbs fail, and being without light for extended periods stresses or kills plants.
- Ballasts and bulbs must match — check the type rating on both.
- **Safety**: Water + electricity = extremely dangerous. Keep all electrical outlets away from plants and liquids.

## Reflective Surfaces

- **Mylar** or white paint maximizes light utilization by reflecting escaped light back to the canopy.
- Flat white paint provides ~85% reflectivity; Mylar provides ~95%.
