Cosmetic products are a mixture of hundreds of different chemicals. When absorbed through the skin they accumulate in the body. Minimize use and try refraining from soap. Apply cosmetics only to the face and hands if necessary.

### 5. Use Sun-Dried Sea Salt Instead of Toothpaste

Toothpaste contains numerous chemicals. After a few weeks of using sun-dried sea salt, gums become noticeably healthier. Lightly wet the toothbrush, apply a small amount of salt, and brush as normal. Do not drink too much alcohol.

### 6. Exercise and Bathing for Skin Health

Skin is a major detox organ. Exercise and sweat regularly for better detoxification. Lower-body warm bathing and hot/cold alternate bathing strengthen the skin. Avoid smoking.

### 7. Maintain Spine Health and Good Posture

Do sit-ups 30 times daily. If experiencing backache or spinal curvature, roll a towel and lie on it for 20 minutes. Weak back muscles lead to chronic problems; sway back eventually causes internal organ issues.

### 8. Keep Shoulders Free from Stiffness

Holding and hanging from a horizontal bar for 30 seconds daily is an effective remedy. Repeated exercise puts bones in correct position and keeps shoulders pain-free.

## The Three Changes That Could Transform National Health

JADAM identifies three changes that could drastically reduce medical expenses:
1. Replace white rice with brown rice and mixed grains
2. Replace toothpaste with sun-dried sea salt
3. Drink water until urine is clear

These simple steps could prevent most modern diseases. JADAM notes that Korea has more clinics and hospitals than ever, yet the number of patients keeps increasing -- evidence that the current approach to health is failing.

## The Body-Soil Connection

These guidelines embody JADAM's philosophical principle of "I and others are one." Just as crops need:
- Balanced nutrition (not excessive of any single element)
- Good water quality and quantity
- Good "circulation" (no soil compaction blocking flow)

Humans need:
- Balanced nutrition (whole foods, not refined)
- Pure water in sufficient quantity
- Good blood circulation (exercise)

Health is not coincidence -- it is the result of effort. "How you eat, drink, exercise, move your body, and operate your mind all matter."
