## Zwitterionic N,O-Dibenzyl Phosphate Intermediate

## Overview

Compound 8, formally named {benzyl[2-(4-oxyindol-3-yl)ethyl]dimethyl-
ammonio}-4-O-benzyl phosphate, is a novel [[psilocybin]] without chromatographic
purification.

## Discovery and Formation

### Initial Observation

During the phosphorylation of [[psilocin]] with tetrabenzylpyrophosphate and
n-BuLi, the expected O,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative (compound 7) was
formed. However, after the standard aqueous workup to remove excess
reagents, the 1H NMR spectrum of the crude product in CDCl3 showed
complicated, uninterpretable signals. Re-examination of the TLC plate
revealed an additional spot at the origin (the baseline), and the
whitish material no longer dissolved in dichloromethane.

### Prior Observation by Nichols and Frescas

A similar observation had been reported by Nichols and Frescas (1999),
who attributed the insoluble material to hydrolytic cleavage of one
O-benzyl group, forming a zwitterionic O-monobenzyl phosphate mixture.
However, the Shirota team's more detailed investigation using
preparative reversed-phase HPLC and comprehensive 2D [[indole]] nitrogen (N-1), creating a quaternary ammonium
ion (positive charge) while retaining a benzyl-protected phosphate
monoester (negative charge). This rearrangement is easily achieved by
suspending the worked-up reaction mixture in CH2Cl2 overnight.

The driving force for this rearrangement is the formation of a stable
zwitterion. The quaternary ammonium ion is permanently charged and
stable, while the phosphate monoester provides the negative charge. The
benzyl group on nitrogen cannot participate in hydrogen bonding with the
phosphate, making the zwitterion insoluble in organic solvents like
dichloromethane.

## Structural Elucidation by NMR

The structure of compound 8 was determined using a combination of 1D and
2D NMR experiments. The key evidence came from HMBC (heteronuclear
multiple bond correlation) and NOESY (nuclear Overhauser effect
spectroscopy) experiments.

### 1H NMR Evidence (CD3OD, 400 MHz)

The 1H NMR spectrum showed signals for two distinct benzyl groups:

- **N-benzyl methylene**: 4.56 ppm (2H, s) -- shifted to high field
  relative to typical benzyl protons due to the adjacent quaternary
  ammonium center
- **O-benzyl methylene**: 4.98 and 4.96 ppm (each 1H, s) -- an AB
  system indicating diastereotopic protons, typical of benzyl esters

The aromatic protons of the two benzyl groups appeared as separate sets:
the N-benzyl group at 7.56-7.45 ppm and the O-benzyl group at
7.31-7.20 ppm. The psilocin core protons and the dimethylaminoethyl
[[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]] were shifted to low field compared to free psilocin,
consistent with the positive charge on the nitrogen.
