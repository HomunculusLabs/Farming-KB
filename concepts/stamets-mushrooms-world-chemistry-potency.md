# Psilocybin Chemistry and Potency Variation

Psilocybin mushrooms produce several tryptamine-derived compounds, most
notably psilocybin (4-PO-DMT), psilocin (4-HO-DMT), baeocystin, nor-baeocystin,
and aeruginascine. These indole alkaloids are concentrated primarily in the
genus Psilocybe (more than 80 known or suspected active species), with
additional active species in Panaeolus, Gymnopilus, Conocybe, Inocybe, and
Pluteus.

## Chemical Compounds

Psilocybin (O-phosphoryl-4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is a stable
prodrug that is dephosphorylated during digestion to its active metabolite
psilocin (4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine). This means that when you ingest
psilocybin, you are ultimately feeling the effects of psilocin — the
dephosphorylated form.

Baeocystin (4-PO-NMT) and nor-baeocystin are closely related to psilocin
and may be active (Gartz 1992), though they are present in lesser
concentrations than psilocybin and psilocin. The name baeocystin derives
