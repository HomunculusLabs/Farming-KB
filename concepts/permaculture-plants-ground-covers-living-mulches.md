## Perennial Grasses as Ground Covers

### Pampas Grass (Cortaderia selloana)
Clumping perennial to 3 m, forms a fountain of cascading leaves. Suited to cool areas yet thrives in heat; does well in acid and alkaline soils, including waterlogged areas. Fast to recover from grazing. Good windbreak and shelter plant for stock. Controls soil erosion. In New Zealand, 1 acre planted at 1.8m x 1.8m spacing is equivalent to 3000 bales of hay. Deep roots bring up trace elements which control grass tetany.
