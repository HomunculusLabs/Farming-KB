# Haze Lineage and History

The Haze family is perhaps the most celebrated and influential lineage in [[cervantes-hybrid-varieties-types]]. From coffee
shop menus to medical dispensaries, the Haze name carries an almost mythic weight in cannabis
culture, representing the pinnacle of [[cannabis-training-techniques-comparison]] were developed.
The Haze Brothers' creation would go on to influence virtually every major sativa-dominant
strain developed in the subsequent decades. Dutch breeders, particularly Neville Schoenmaker and
the team at Positronics Seeds, obtained Haze genetics in the 1980s and began incorporating them
into European breeding programs, which is how Haze genetics spread globally.

## Original Haze and Its Derivatives

### Tom Hill's Original Haze

Grown by Trip Seeds in Chile but originally created by Tom Hill in Northern California, this
strain descends from Positronics Seeds' Original Haze from the mid-1990s. As a four-way hybrid
of narrow-leaf varieties from Mexico, Colombia, Thailand, and southern India, it represents one
of the purest expressions of the original Haze lineage available today.
The plant grows to 8 feet indoors with ease, requiring high-ceiling grow rooms and significant
experience to manage. Flowering runs a full 14 to 16 weeks, demanding patience that many growers
simply do not possess. The buds are wispy and loose but full of character, yielding about 300
grams per plant at approximately 13% THC.
The sweet but spicy Haze taste is unmistakable, but no one smokes Haze for the taste alone — it
is all about the high, a cerebral experience to end all cerebral highs that will pick you up,
throw you through the air, and not particularly care where you land. This is the kind of stuff
that Steve Jobs smoked before he redefined geek cool.

### Santa Muerte

Bred by BlimBurn Seeds in Spain, Santa Muerte crosses a Mexican sativa with Original Haze.
Named after the Mexican folk saint of death, this sativa-dominant hybrid reaches 9 to 10 feet
outdoors and produces 500 to 600 grams of high-quality bud. The plant flowers in about 75 days
with a vegetative period that is relatively short but full of vigorous growth.
Near the end of flowering, the buds develop a deep, dark green color that inspired the strain's
ominous name. The Haze parent contributes an unmistakable spicy aroma and a deeply cerebral
high. THC content is approximately 16%.

## Amnesia Lineage

The Amnesia family represents one of the most commercially successful branches of the Haze
tree, particularly in European markets where it dominates coffee shop menus.
