# Desert Settlement Species Compendium

This compendium consolidates the species recommended for desert and arid-zone settlement gardens, orchards, and broadscale plantings. It draws from Mollison's fieldwork at Aboriginal settlements in central Australia and complementary tropical and coastal trials.

## Established Tree Crops

### Fruit and Nut Trees
| Species | Status | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Mulberry (Morus spp.) | Est. | Red/black varieties; poultry forage; high vitamin C; resists white ant, fire |
| Carob ([[ceratonia-siliqua]]) | Est. | 68% sugar beans; needs 5% male trees; careful site selection (termites) |
| Citrus spp. | Est. | Oranges, lemons; need limes, grapefruit; Tagetes understorey for eel-worm |
| Fig (Ficus spp.) | Est. | Needs more varieties; hybrid potential with native figs |
| Mango ([[mangifera-indica]]) | Est. | At Mt. Isa; wider trials needed |
| Pomegranate | Est. | Major summer fruit; Pakistani spp. keep into winter |
| Grape Vine | Est. | 26 varieties flourish; fruit from cuttings in 18 months |
| Paw Paw | Trials | Small selected areas |
| Guava (Psidium, Ugni) | Est. | True guava, feijoa, strawberry guava in sheltered areas |
| Passionfruit | Est. | Banana passionfruit; frost hardy; good poultry fodder |
| Cape Gooseberry | Trials | With native tomato, kangaroo apple, tamarillo; high vitamin C |
| Cashew Nut | Est. | Wider trials needed |
| Cherries (Prunus spp.) | Trials | Near watercourses |
| Plums (Prunus spp.) | Est. | Especially prunes and gages; wider trials |
| Almond ([[prunus-dulcis]]) | Trials | |
| Pistachio (P. vera) | Trials | Seedlings survive well |
| Sweet Chestnut | Trials | Frosty areas, stream beds |

### Multi-Purpose Trees
| Species | Uses |
|---------|------|
| Black Locust ([[robinia-pseudoacacia]]) | Durable timber; poultry seed; spiny (toxic to stock—advantageous); fire-resistant |
| Honey Locust ([[gleditsia-triacanthos]]) | Timber; edible beans; pole timbers |
| Olive (Olea europaea) | Oil; shade; poultry forage; cuttings propagate easily |
| Karreeboom (Rhus lancea) | Fodder; small edible berries; frost and termite resistant |
| Jujube (Zizyphus jujuba) | Est.; hedge potential |
| Cacti | Fruiting species; hedgerow; non-irrigated crop |
| Stone/Nut Pines (Pinus, Araucaria) | Pinon, desert nut pines; some established |

### Palms
Coconut, Cotton, Canary, Date palms established. Queen palm, borbassus, oil palm need trials. Hardy and useful along watercourses.

### Vine Crops
Kudzu, hops, kiwi fruit and other vine crops help with shade and forage.

## Vegetable Categories

### Perennials
Globe artichoke, asparagus, sweet potato, cassava (Manihot), comfrey, beans, herbs (chives, sage, thyme, marjoram, mints)

### Biennials
Parsley, fennel, celery

### Annuals
Sweet corn, tomato, melons, borage, lettuce, chard, spinach, sprouts, cabbage, peas, beans

### Tubers and Roots
Potato, sweet potato, turnip, carrot, onion, cassava, [[comparison-jerusalem-artichoke-vs-potato]], oca, native wapiti, yala

|---------|----------|

| Paulownia spp. | Surface moisture provision |
| Leucaena | Frost-free areas |
| Tree Lucerne | Fire/frost resistant; stock and poultry fodder |
| Mulga (Acacia aneura) | Multi-purpose native |

| Mesquites (Prosopis spp.) | Deep-rooted nutrient pumps |

## Hedgerow Species
- [[coprosma-repens-entity]]: Fire-resistant; poultry/stock fodder
- Bamboo spp.: Structural needs; larger varieties needed
- Cacti, Wormwood, Jujube: Formidable barriers

## Shade and Shelter Only
- White Cedar ([[melia-azedarach-entity]]): Use with care—fruits poisonous to pigs and poultry
- Jacaranda (J. mimosifolia)
- Tamarind (Tamarindus indicus): Marginal food use
- Tamarisk (Tamarix articulata): Also for mulch

## Aquatic Species for Desert Dams
- Native wild rice
- Water lily (Nymphea gigantea): stalks, tubers, seeds
- Nardoo (fern)
- Native arrowroot
- Trapa / Eleocharis (water chestnuts)
- Lotus
- Sweet rush: shoots and bulbs
- Freshwater mussels (water filters, food, shell grit for poultry)
- Goldfish and grass carp (algae and [[backswimmer-mosquito-control-mollison]])
- Native snails (grit for ducks)
