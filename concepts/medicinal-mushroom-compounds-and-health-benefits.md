## Triterpenoids

Triterpenoids are a large class of lipophilic compounds particularly abundant
in Ganoderma species. Over 150 distinct ganoderic acids have been identified,
each with slightly different biological activities. Key effects include:

- **Hepatoprotective activity** — ganoderic acids protect liver cells from
  toxin-induced damage and show promise in treating non-alcoholic fatty liver
  disease.

- **Anti-inflammatory effects** — inhibition of NF-kB signaling pathways
  reduces production of pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha,
  interleukin-6, and COX-2.

- **Antitumor activity** — triterpenoids induce apoptosis in cancer cells
  through multiple mechanisms including cell cycle arrest, mitochondrial
  disruption, and inhibition of angiogenesis.

Triterpenoids are alcohol-soluble, which is why traditional reishi preparations
involve hot water extraction (for water-soluble beta-glucans) followed by
ethanol extraction (for alcohol-soluble triterpenoids). Dual extraction
products contain both compound classes.
