## Steam Penetration Depth

The steam generated by flash vaporization has limited penetration depth compared to autoclave steam delivered under pressure. In an open surface configuration, the steam rapidly dissipates into the surrounding atmosphere. This means the sterilization effect is primarily a surface phenomenon, affecting organisms directly exposed at the surface. Organisms protected within deep crevices, biofilm matrices, or under particulate debris may receive sub-lethal exposure. This limitation underscores the importance of surface preparation (cleaning) prior to microwave sterilization and the need for adequate antenna coverage to ensure all surfaces receive sufficient [[microbial-kill-curve-microwave-exposure-dose-response]] for steam generation.
