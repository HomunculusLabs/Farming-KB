## Steam Penetration Depth

The steam generated by flash vaporization has limited penetration depth compared to autoclave steam delivered under pressure. In an open surface configuration, the steam rapidly dissipates into the surrounding atmosphere. This means the sterilization effect is primarily a surface phenomenon, affecting organisms directly exposed at the surface.
