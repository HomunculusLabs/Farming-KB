## Permaculture Composting Philosophy

In permaculture, composting is not waste disposal — it is [[comparison-nutrient-cycling-vs-nutrient-dense-gardening]]. The goal is to keep all organic matter on-site, returning it to the soil to build soil health and close nutrient loops. "Waste" is simply a resource in the wrong place.

Permaculture composting differs from conventional approaches by:
- **Diversity of inputs** — combining materials from many sources creates more biologically active compost
- **Multiple concurrent systems** — rather than one large pile, use several smaller systems suited to different waste streams
- **Integration with design** — compost bins placed at zone interfaces where waste is generated and where compost is needed
- **Minimal turning** — some systems work with minimal intervention, saving labor while still producing quality compost

### Vermicomposting

### Compost Toilets
Humanure systems compost human waste with carbon cover materials, returning valuable nutrients to the soil cycle. After 12+ months of thermophilic composting, the result is safe, high-quality compost for fruit trees and ornamental plantings.
