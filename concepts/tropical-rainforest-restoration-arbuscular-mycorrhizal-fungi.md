## Native Versus Introduced AMF

The use of native AMF inocula is generally preferred in restoration to maintain regional fungal diversity and ensure adaptation to local conditions. Native AMF are adapted to local soil types, climate, and host plant communities. However, commercial AMF inocula may be useful when native AMF communities have been severely depleted or when rapid establishment of plant cover is the primary objective. The choice between native and introduced AMF should be based on restoration goals, the degree of site degradation, and the availability of native inoculum sources.
