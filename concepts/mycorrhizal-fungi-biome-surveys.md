## EMF Community Dynamics
Host specificity ranges from generalist to specialist.
Fruiting is highly variable year-to-year. Below-ground
diversity exceeds sporocarp surveys 2-10x. Molecular
methods reveal far more species than visual surveys.

## Molecular Tools in Surveys
Modern surveys rely increasingly on DNA-based methods:
- NGS of root and soil samples provides community profiles
- ITS region sequencing detects unculturable species
- Metagenomics reveals functional gene diversity
- qPCR quantifies AMF abundance across gradients

Challenges include incomplete reference databases, primer
bias, and difficulty linking sequences to functional traits.
