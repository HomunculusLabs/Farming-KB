# Decentralizing Bender and the Perceiving Self as Primary Datum

## Overview

In "[[mckenna-tryptamine-hallucinogens-and-consciousness]] and Consciousness" (1983), [[terence-mckenna]]
identified what he called the "decentralizing bender" — a five-century-
long intellectual trajectory that progressively displaced human beings
from the center of the cosmos. He argued that this trajectory has gone
too far, and that the [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]], particularly the DMT
state, reveals the perceiving self to be the primary datum of reality.

## The Decentralizing Trajectory

McKenna traced the progressive displacement of human centrality:

1. **Copernicus**: Earth is not the center of the solar system
2. **Galileo and Newton**: The heavens obey the same mechanical laws
   as Earth
3. **Darwin**: Humans are not specially created but evolved from
   common ancestry with all life
4. **Freud**: The conscious mind is not even the master of itself;
   unconscious drives govern much of behavior
5. **Modern cosmology**: The universe is billions of years old and
   unimaginably vast; humanity occupies an infinitesimal fraction
   of spacetime

Each step further displaced humans from centrality. McKenna observed
that "Western societies have been on a decentralizing bender for five
hundred years, concluding that the Earth is not the center of the
universe and man is not the beloved of God. We have moved ourselves
out toward the edge of the galaxy."

## The Overcorrection

McKenna argued that this trajectory, while scientifically valid in
its particulars, has produced a cultural pathology of self-negation.
The conclusion that humans are cosmically insignificant is, in his
view, a philosophical overreach from scientific facts:

"The fact is that the most richly organized material in the universe
is the human cerebral cortex, and the densest and richest experience
in the universe is the experience you are having right now."

This is not an anthropocentric claim about physical centrality but
a phenomenological claim about the centrality of experience. No
matter where humans sit in physical space, experience itself is the
medium through which the universe knows itself.

## The Perceiving Self as Primary Datum

McKenna's counter-argument to the decentralizing bender is simple
but radical: "Everything should be constellated outward from the
perceiving self. That is the primary datum."

This position — that consciousness is the starting point of all
inquiry rather than a derivative phenomenon to be explained — aligns
with philosophical traditions including:

- **Phenomenology** (Husserl, Merleau-Ponty): Consciousness is the
  ground of all experience and cannot be reduced to object-level
  explanation
- **Idealism** (Berkeley, Schopenhauer): The world as we know it
  is fundamentally experiential
- **Whitehead's process philosophy**: Experience ("prehension") is
  the fundamental unit of reality

McKenna explicitly referenced Whitehead's concept of "the formality
of actually occurring" — the idea that consciousness participates in
the actualization of events rather than passively observing them.
"Consciousness is somehow able to collapse the state vector and
thereby cause the stuff of being to undergo what Alfred North
Whitehead called the formality of actually occurring."

## The Psychedelic Revelation

The [[lsd-psychedelic-experience-guide]], particularly the tryptamine state,
reinforces the primacy of the perceiving self by demonstrating that
consciousness can access modes of being utterly foreign to ordinary
waking awareness. The [[mckenna-dmt-experience-and-hyperspace]]:

- Reveals dimensions of reality not accessible to ordinary perception
- Demonstrates that consciousness is far more plastic and expansive
  than the decentralizing narrative allows
