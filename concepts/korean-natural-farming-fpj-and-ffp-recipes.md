# Korean Natural Farming FPJ and FFP Recipes

Fermented Plant Juice (FPJ) and Fermented Fish Protein (FFP/FAA) are key Korean Natural Farming inputs that provide plants with bioavailable nutrients, growth hormones, and enzymes. Both are simple to make from locally available materials.

## Fermented Plant Juice (FPJ)

### Purpose
- Provides natural plant hormones (auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins)
- Supplies sugars and amino acids for quick plant energy
- Stimulates vegetative growth
- Enhances flowering and fruiting (depends on plant used)

### Plant Selection
- Choose fast-growing plants with abundant sap (high "chi" energy)
- Collect before sunrise when plant sugars are highest
- Best plants by growth stage:
  - **Early spring**: Japanese mugwort (Artemisia princeps), dropwort (Oenanthe javanica)
  - **Summer**: Bamboo shoots, sweet potato vines, fast-growing weeds
  - **Flowering**: Flowering branches of any plant (for reproductive growth stimulation)
  - **Fruiting**: Unripe fruit of the target crop (tomato FPJ for tomatoes)

### Method
1. Collect plant material before sunrise (when dew is still present)
2. Don't wash (surface microorganisms aid fermentation)
3. Cut into small pieces (1-2 inches)
4. Weigh plant material
5. Add equal weight of brown sugar (1:1 ratio)
6. Mix thoroughly in a clay jar (fill to 2/3 — fermentation produces gas)
7. Place a weight on top to keep plant material submerged
8. Cover with breathable cloth
9. Ferment at room temperature (20-25°C) for 5-7 days
10. Done when liquid separates and bubbles stop rising
11. Extract the liquid by pressing through a cloth strainer

### Storage and Use
- Store in cool, dark place in glass bottles
- Shelf life: 6-12 months at cool temperatures
- Dilution: 1:500 to 1:1000 with water for foliar spray or soil drench
- Apply every 7-14 days during active growth
- Apply early morning or evening (avoid midday heat)

## Fermented Fish Amino Acid (FFP/FAA)

### Purpose
- Provides nitrogen and amino acids for vegetative growth
- Excellent for crops needing a nitrogen boost
- Faster-acting than compost nitrogen
- Fish oil byproduct acts as insect repellent

### Materials
- Fish waste: heads, bones, entrails, whole small fish (fresh or frozen)
- Brown sugar: 1:1 ratio with fish by weight
- Clay jar

### Method
1. Crush fish waste to increase surface area
2. Mix with brown sugar 1:1 by weight
3. Pack into clay jar (fill to 2/3)
4. The sugar draws moisture from the fish, creating a liquid extract
5. Cover with breathable cloth
6. Ferment for 6+ months (longer = more complete extraction)
7. Strain the liquid for use

### Storage and Use
- Stores indefinitely at room temperature (high sugar content preserves it)
- Dilution: 1:1000 with water (very concentrated)
- Apply as soil drench or foliar spray
- Best applied during vegetative growth stage
- Apply every 2-3 weeks
- Fish oil that separates to the top can be used as insect repellent (dilute 1:1000)

### Notes
- Strong fish smell during fermentation — keep away from living areas
- Works best combined with FPJ in a spray program
- Can be made with specific fish for different crops (local tradition)

## Application Schedule (Combined FPJ + FAA)

### Vegetative Stage
- FPJ from fast-growing plants: 1:500 dilution, every 7-10 days
- FAA: 1:1000 dilution, every 2-3 weeks
- Combine in same spray tank (compatible)

### Flowering Stage
- FPJ from flowering branches: 1:500 dilution, every 10-14 days
- Reduce or stop FAA (too much nitrogen reduces flowering)

### Fruiting Stage
- FPJ from unripe fruit of the target crop: 1:500 dilution
- OHN (Oriental Herbal Nutrient): 1:1000 dilution for disease resistance
