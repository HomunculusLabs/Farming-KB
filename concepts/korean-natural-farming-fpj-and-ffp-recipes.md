## Korean Natural Farming FPJ and FFP Recipes

- Enhances flowering and fruiting (depends on plant used)

### Plant Selection
- Choose fast-growing plants with abundant sap (high "chi" energy)
- Collect before sunrise when plant sugars are highest
- Best plants by [[coleman-overwintered-onions-and-early-spring-crops]]**: Japanese mugwort (Artemisia princeps), dropwort (Oenanthe javanica)
  - **Summer**: Bamboo shoots, knf-reproductive-growth-stage stimulation)
  - **Fruiting**: Unripe fruit of the target crop (tomato FPJ for tomatoes)

### Method
1. Collect plant material before sunrise (when dew is still present)
2. Don't wash (surface microorganisms aid fermentation)
3. Cut into small pieces (1-2 inches)
4. Weigh plant material
5. Add equal weight of brown sugar (1:1 ratio)
6. Mix thoroughly in a clay jar (fill to 2/3 — fermentation produces gas)
7. Place a weight on top to keep plant material submerged
8. Cover with breathable cloth
9. Ferment at room temperature (20-25°C) for 5-7 days
10. Done when liquid separates and bubbles stop rising
11. Extract the liquid by pressing through a cloth strainer

### Storage and Use
- Store in cool, dark place in glass bottles
- Shelf life: 6-12 months at cool temperatures
- Dilution: 1:500 to 1:1000 with water for foliar spray or soil drench
- Apply every 7-14 days during active growth
- Apply early morning or evening (avoid midday heat)

## Fermented Fish Amino Acid (FFP/FAA)

### Purpose
- Provides nitrogen and amino acids for [[knf-vegetative-growth-stage]]
- Apply every 2-3 weeks
- Fish oil that separates to the top can be used as insect repellent (dilute 1:1000)

### Notes
- Strong fish smell during fermentation — keep away from living areas
- Works best combined with FPJ in a spray program
- Can be made with specific fish for different crops (local tradition)

## Application Schedule (Combined FPJ + FAA)

### Vegetative Stage
- FPJ from fast-growing plants: 1:500 dilution, every 7-10 days
- FAA: 1:1000 dilution, every 2-3 weeks
