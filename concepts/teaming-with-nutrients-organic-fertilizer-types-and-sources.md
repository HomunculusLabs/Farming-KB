## Why Organic Fertilizers Work Differently

[[chamovitz-gravitropism-plant-knows-where-it-is]].
In essence, organic fertilizers feed the soil, and the soil feeds the plants.
Organic fertilizers are, by definition, full of organic matter, which
supports the creation and maintenance of the soil food web. Adding organic
fertilizer is like constructing a savings account for nutrient cations. The
addition of organic matter in natural fertilizers helps create a structure in
which the carbon that supports the soil food web is stored. Organic
fertilizers add organic bulk to soils, so everything is of benefit. Chemical
fertilizers don't contain organic matter but rather fillers, which may or may
not be of benefit -- but are never as beneficial as organic matter.

## Plant-Based Fertilizers

Plant-based materials are derived from plants and generally provide moderate
nitrogen with a balance of other nutrients. Their carbon-to-nitrogen (C:N)
ratios are relatively low, meaning they break down reasonably quickly.

## Animal-Based Fertilizers

Animal-derived materials tend to be higher in nitrogen than plant-based
sources and release nutrients more quickly due to their lower C:N ratios.

### Blood Meal (12-0-0)

Dried, powdered blood from slaughterhouses, blood meal is one of the
highest-nitrogen organic fertilizers available. It releases nitrogen quickly
(over 1-4 weeks) and is excellent for nitrogen-hungry crops. Blood meal also
contains some iron. It should be used carefully -- excessive application can
burn plants and attract animal pests. On the plus side, deer and moose hate
its smell and normally avoid it.

### Bone Meal (3-15-0)

Steamed and ground animal bones, bone meal is the classic organic phosphorus
source. Its high phosphorus content makes it valuable for root crops, bulbs,
and flowering plants. Bone meal releases phosphorus slowly (over 1-4 months).
It also contains calcium (about 24%) and some nitrogen. Bone meal is most
effective in slightly acidic soils (pH below 7) where phosphorus solubility
is highest. It may attract animals until the odor disappears.
