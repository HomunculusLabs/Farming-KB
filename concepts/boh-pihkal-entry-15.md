# BOH (beta-Methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine)

**[[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #15**

## Chemical Identity

- **Full name:** beta-Methoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine
- **Class:** Phenethylamine (beta-methoxy derivative of MDPEA;
  member of the BOX family)
- **Molecular formula:** C10H13NO3 (free base)
- **Hydrochloride salt:** fine white crystalline product
  - Hydrate form: mp 105-106.5 °C (with bubbling and darkening)
  - Anhydrous form: mp 152-153 °C (from CH3CN or heating to
    100 °C in toluene)
- **Boiling point:** 103-112 °C at 0.5 mm/Hg (free base)

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 80-120 mg
- **Duration:** 6-8 hours

## Qualitative Effects

### At 90 mg
Distinct body awareness in an hour. Effects were mostly physical:
faint inner warmth, skin prickling, cold feet, loose bowels,
anorexia. By the fifth hour, the subject was on the downslope,
retrospectively describing the experience as good-humored but not
insightful. The threshold is mostly physical.

### At 100 mg
Vague nausea and chilling of the feet occurred. The experience
reached a genuine +2 with dilated pupils and marked thirst. The
state was described as not even stoned, "just very turned on,"
with no visual component. Return to baseline by hour six.

### Lower dose reports
Several reports of nice, mild mood enhancement in the 20-40 mg
range, but searches for psychedelic effects at higher levels gave
an uncomfortable mix of altered state with bodily discomfort.

## Synthesis Notes

### Step 1: Nitrostyrene
Piperonal (30 g) was condensed with nitromethane (20 mL) and
cyclohexylamine (10 mL) in acetic acid (100 mL) on the steam
bath for 1.5 h. The product, 3,4-methylenedioxy-beta-
nitrostyrene, crystallized from the reaction mixture as
canary-yellow crystals (24.5 g, mp 158-160 °C from MeOH).
Reduction of this with LAH gives MDPEA.

### Step 2: Michael addition
The nitrostyrene (20 g) was suspended in anhydrous MeOH (100 mL)
and treated with sodium methoxide (from 5.5 g Na in 100 mL MeOH).
The bright orange color faded to light cream as the nitrostyrene
dissolved over 5 minutes. Acetic acid (50 mL) was added, then
water (300 mL). The product was extracted into CH2Cl2 and
distilled (128-145 °C at 0.4 mm/Hg) to give 16.6 g of
1-methoxy-1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-2-nitroethane as bright
yellow crystals (mp 58-59 °C).

### Step 3: Reduction
LAH (100 mL 1 M in THF) was activated with H2SO4 (2.5 mL) at
0 °C, then the nitroethane (12 g) was added. After an immediate
loss of color, the temperature was raised to reflux. Unlike the
BOD, BOH, and BOM reductions which showed vigorous exothermic
reactions, this one did not show the characteristic exotherm.
The product (2.5 g) was isolated as the hydrochloride salt.

## The Pro-Drug Hypothesis

BOH may act as a "pro-drug" to norepinephrine in the body. The
polar hydroxyl group is masked by a methoxy ether, allowing
brain penetration. Once in the brain, it could be metabolically
stripped of this shield and play a direct neurological role as
norepinephrine.
