## Sativa vs Indica Profiles

| Property | Sativa | Indica |
|----------|--------|--------|
| THC:CBD Ratio | Higher THC:CBD | Lower THC:CBD |
| Effect | Stimulating, energizing, uplifting | Sedating, relaxing, grounding |
| Focus | Mental/emotional | Physical |
| Social | Extrovert | Introvert |
| Timing | Daytime | Evening/bedtime |
| Alertness | Increases | Decreases (sleep aid) |
| Depression | Consider | -- |
| Anxiety | -- | Consider |
| Receptor | Activates CB1 and CB2 | Greater CB2 affinity |

### Strain Selection by Condition
- Neurological disorders / mental-emotional problems: likely CB1-deficient; choose **higher THC:CBD ratio** (sativa)
- Immune-compromised / chronic inflammation: likely CB2-deficient; choose **lower THC:CBD ratio** (indica)

## Whole-Plant vs Synthetic

Pharmaceuticals like Dronabinol (synthetic THC) and Nabilone contain single cannabinoids without the other 200-250 biologically active compounds found in the whole plant. Sativex, a plant-derived THC:CBD spray, represents a middle ground. Blesching argues that complete cannabinoid profiles from whole-plant preparations likely produce superior therapeutic outcomes through synergistic effects not yet fully understood.
