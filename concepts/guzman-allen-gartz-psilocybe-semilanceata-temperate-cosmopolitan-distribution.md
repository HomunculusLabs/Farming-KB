# Psilocybe semilanceata: Temperate Cosmopolitan Distribution

## Overview

Psilocybe semilanceata, commonly known as the "liberty cap," occupies a uniquely
anomalous position within the biogeography [[oyster-mushroom-log-cultivation-temperate-regions-short-log-method]] across an extraordinarily broad range:

- **Europe**: Widespread and abundant throughout western, central, and northern
  Europe, where it is by far the most commonly encountered and foraged
  psilocybin-containing mushroom.
- **Asia**: Recorded from India and Russia, extending across significant
  portions of the Eurasian landmass.
- **[[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-south-america]]**: Present in Chile and Peru, representing a southern
  hemisphere extension of its temperate range.
- **Oceania**: Documented in New Zealand, Australia, and Tasmania.

This distribution makes P. semilanceata one of the most widely distributed
neurotropic Psilocybe species known to science, rivaling or exceeding the range
of even P. cubensis, which is restricted to tropical and subtropical dung
habitats.
