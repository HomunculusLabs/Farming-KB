# Rejuvenation and Re-vegging Harvested Cannabis

Rejuvenation (also called re-vegging) is the process of returning a harvested
cannabis plant back to [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] for a second [[cannabis-sativa-flowering-cycle-management]]. Jorge
Cervantes documents this technique as a way to get additional harvests from
favorite plants without starting entirely from seed.

## How Rejuvenation Works

When a harvested female plant is given an 18/6 day/night photoperiod (18
hours of light, 6 hours of darkness), it stops flowering and reverts to
vegetative growth. The process requires leaving several undeveloped lower
branches with foliage on the harvested plant. These remaining growth points
serve as the foundation for new vegetative growth.

## Step-by-Step Rejuvenation Process

1. **Harvest carefully** - Leave several lower branches with foliage intact
   when harvesting the main colas and upper buds
2. **Change photoperiod** - Switch to 18/6 day/night schedule the day after
   harvesting to trigger vegetative growth
