## Cage Fungi: Structural Innovation

Cage fungi such as Clathrus and Anthurus produce receptacles that unfold
into geometric cage or star shapes. The spore slime coats the inner
surfaces, maximizing exposure to insects. The Ileodictyon cage from
New Zealand and Australia forms a polyhedral structure resembling a
geodesic dome, providing strength with minimal material investment.

The detached cage of Ileodictyon can blow across grasslands like a
tumbleweed, scattering spores along its path. The Maori people
recognized this fungus with nine different names and consumed its eggs.
