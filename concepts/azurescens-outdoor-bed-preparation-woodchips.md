# Azurescens Outdoor Bed Preparation with Woodchips

Outdoor bed preparation for [[corylus-avellana]]): Recommended as excellent for P. azurescens expansion. Fresh cut twigs and chips work well
- **Beech** ([[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]], suitable for P. azurescens
- **General hardwood**: Any fresh hardwood chips are acceptable; avoid conifers and treated lumber

For [[growing-gourmet-growing-room-ventilation-air-exchange]], and longer-term decomposition.

## Bed Construction

### Site Selection
A half-shaded location beneath shrubs or bushes is ideal. The site should have:
- Protection from direct afternoon sun
- Natural windbreaks
- Proximity for regular monitoring (a 2-hour drive makes maintenance impractical)
- Well-draining soil that does not waterlog

### Digging the Bed
- Excavate a hole **15 to 20 cm deep**
- Width and length are flexible; even small patches (from 0.3L of spawn) can produce results
- The hole is filled entirely with the wood chip and spawn mixture

### Spawn Ratio
Two sources provide different recommendations reflecting different approaches:

| Source | Minimum Spawn Ratio | Preferred Ratio |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Azurescens guide | 1:5 (at least 1/5th spawn by volume) | Higher if available |
| Woodlovers guide | 1:20 | Higher rates preferred |

The azurescens guide's higher ratio (1:5) reflects the species' slower colonization compared to more aggressive woodlovers like P. cyanescens. Using more spawn increases the probability of fruiting in the same year, especially for later plantings.

## Chip Preparation

### For the Bed (Non-Sterile)
1. Place coarse [[brassinosteroid-fungal-growth-promotion]])
2. Drain completely — no standing water
3. Optional supplementation: 1/2 cup oatmeal or boiled grass seed per bag
4. Sterilize at 121C for the appropriate duration

## Covering and Moisture Management

After filling the hole with the spawn/chip mixture:

1. **Water thoroughly** — use several liters depending on bed size
2. **Cover with wet cardboard** — the cardboard itself becomes an additional growth medium for the mycelium
3. **Add perforated plastic** on top of the cardboard (or plain plastic sheeting)
4. Keep the bed **humid but not wet** for the first two weeks
5. After two weeks, no additional water is typically needed until September

### Critical Watering Rule
It is better to water lightly and repeat after a week than to overwater initially. Overwatering during summer causes the mycelium to grow aggressively vegetatively but produces no mushrooms in autumn.
