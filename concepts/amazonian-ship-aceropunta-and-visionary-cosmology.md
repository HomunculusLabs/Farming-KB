## The Three-Temple Initiation

Vision 36 describes a three-tiered initiation system:
1. **First temple**: Initiates ascending colored steps for ordination. A lilac horse with wings descends for those who have been admitted.
2. **Second temple**: Graduates receive deeper esoteric knowledge. A spaceship leaves, symbolizing increased protection.
3. **Third temple**: Ordination as sublime masters with light-blue stairs. Two spaceships depart -- one upward, one downward -- signifying full mastery of esoteric sciences.
