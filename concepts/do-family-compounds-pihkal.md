**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

The DO-family (DimethOxy-amphetamines) represents one of the most pharmacologically significant families of psychedelic amphetamines documented in PiHKAL. These compounds share a core 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine skeleton with various substitutions at the 4-position, producing some of the most potent and longest-lasting psychedelic substances known.

## Core Structural Pattern

All DO compounds derive from 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine (2,5-DMA), which itself is relatively inactive as a psychedelic. The critical modification occurs at the activated 4-position of the aromatic ring, where various groups are introduced via electrophilic attack. The 2,5-dimethoxy orientation keeps the 4-position activated and available for substitution.
