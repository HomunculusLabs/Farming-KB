# PF TEK Introduction and History

## Overview

The PF TEK, created by **Psilocybe Fanaticus** (PF), is a revolutionary mushroom cultivation technique first introduced in September 1991 via a classified advertisement in *High Times Magazine*. Originally sold as a complete kit (prepared jars with PF substrate, syringes, and instructions), the technique was fully published in 1992, making the kits obsolete. The complete instructions empowered anyone to cultivate psilocybin mushrooms using common kitchen utensils and readily available supplies.

The PF TEK is fundamentally a **brown rice method** improved by using **vermiculite as a base** with added **pulverized brown rice**. PF described vermiculite as the "magic ingredient" of the technique. The key innovation is that when mycelium is cultured in vermiculite rather than straight grain, the mycelial threads stretch across open space rather than forming a dense mass with little air space.

## Core Innovation: Mass Spore Inoculation

Perhaps the most significant conceptual contribution of the PF TEK is its departure from conventional mycological practice. Instead of the usual approach of **cloning mushroom tissue** and growing mushrooms from that clone, the PF TEK employs **mass spore inoculation** directly onto the fruiting substrate. This has several important consequences:

- **Complete genotype preservation**: The full genetic diversity of the spores remains intact throughout the life cycle
- **No senescence**: The problem of senescence (mutating and ceased fruiting that plagues cloned cultures) is entirely avoided
- **Perpetual succession**: Spores ensure a never-ending cycle of fungus with full reproductive capacity

## Global Adoption

The PF TEK was rapidly copied worldwide by numerous organizations and individuals. Notable adopters include:

- **HEMP BC** (Vancouver, Canada)
- **HEMP NATION** (Canada)
- **The Florida Mycology Research Center**
- **Psilo-grow** (Amsterdam, Holland)
- **Smart Botanics** (Gouda, Holland)
- **Perfect Fungi** (Europe, Wageningen, Holland)
- Various online resources including the **Lycaeum**, **EROWID**, and the **Hyperreal Magic Mushroom Site**

The technique was widely distributed on the internet as the "Magic Mushroom Growers Guide" Version 3.2, which incorporated PF TEK methods along with various modifications and alternatives. This widespread distribution prompted PF to publish the original technique online so cultivators could see the source material.

## Legal Context

PF noted that *Psilocybe cubensis* mushrooms contain the controlled compounds **psilocybin** and **psilocin**. While possession of the mushrooms themselves is illegal under U.S. federal law, there are no federal laws prohibiting the spores. PF distributed spores and documentation explicitly for "scientific and entheogenic research only."

## The PF Philosophy

PF advocated for responsible use, stating that "only mature humans need experiment with these entheogens" and urging practitioners to "protect the children." The philosophy emphasized adult exploration of consciousness — "exercise your rights as an adult, to explore the other place and bring back knowledge."

## Supplies and Discreet Distribution

The original PF operation distributed:

- **PF TEK booklet** (2nd edition): $10 postpaid, with detailed photos and drawings covering the entire process from growing to harvesting and preserving
- **Psilocybe cubensis spore syringes**: $10 each, guaranteed clean (uncontaminated) and viable (germinable), sourced from the finest fruiting genotype

Orders were fulfilled with maximum discretion — no customer or mailing lists were kept, syringe boxes and mailers were hand-addressed, and order letters were destroyed immediately after addressing.

## The Six-Step PF TEK Process

The complete PF TEK can be summarized in six fundamental steps:

1. **Substrate preparation**: Brown rice powder, vermiculite, and distilled water are mixed and loaded into half-pint jars
2. **Sterilization**: Jars are steam-sterilized (no pressure cooker required)
3. **Inoculation**: Jars are inoculated with spore syringe through lid holes
4. **Incubation**: Jars incubate at approximately 70°F until fully colonized
5. **Birthing**: Colonized cakes are removed from jars and placed into a dual-chambered terrarium
6. **Fruiting**: Mushrooms develop and are harvested, with mature specimens used for spore collection

## Significance in Mycology

The PF TEK democratized mushroom cultivation by eliminating the need for specialized equipment like pressure canners, laminar flow hoods, and laboratory-grade supplies. Its use of commonly available materials — small canning jars, brown rice, vermiculite, and a standard aquarium — made cultivation accessible to virtually anyone. The technique's emphasis on simplicity, reliability, and the dry vermiculite contaminant barrier (a PF original discovery) represented genuine innovations in amateur mycology that remain influential to this day.
