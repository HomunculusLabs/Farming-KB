practical advice on this topic, making claims about decades-long potency
retention that remain relevant to contemporary discussions of [[psilocybin-shelf-life-stability]].

The chemistry supports the general claim. Psilocybin itself is a relatively
stable compound compared to psilocin, its dephosphorylated counterpart.
Psilocin is more prone to oxidation and degradation over time. Since dried
mushrooms contain both compounds in varying ratios depending on species,
strain, age, and drying conditions, some potency shift is expected over very
long storage periods. However, the bulk of the psychoactive effect comes
from psilocybin, which is indeed quite stable when protected from light,
heat, and moisture.
