## Key Genera

### Rhodotorula
Rhodotorula species are among the most commonly encountered
basidiomycetous yeasts, characterized by pink to red colony
pigmentation due to carotenoid production. They are ubiquitous
in soil, water, air, and on plant surfaces. Some species are
opportunistic pathogens of immunocompromised patients, causing
catheter-related fungemia and other infections. Population
levels in the open ocean are usually low (around 10 cells/g)
but may increase dramatically near surface slicks, pollutants,
or plankton blooms.
