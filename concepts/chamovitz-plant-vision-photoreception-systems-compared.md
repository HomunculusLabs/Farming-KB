definitive demonstration of plant vision. The Darwins grew canary
grass in total darkness, then exposed it to a gas lamp so dim they
"could not see the seedlings themselves." After three hours, the
plants had curved toward the invisible light.

The critical experiment showed that the "eye" of the plant is at
the shoot tip:
- Untreated seedling: bent toward light
- Tip removed: no bending (blind)
- Tip covered with lightproof cap: no bending (blind)
- Tip covered with clear glass cap: bent toward light (can see)
- Middle covered with lightproof tube: bent toward light (only
  tip needs to see)

This elegantly proved that the tip detects light direction and
sends a signal to the stem to bend accordingly — a functional
visual system.
