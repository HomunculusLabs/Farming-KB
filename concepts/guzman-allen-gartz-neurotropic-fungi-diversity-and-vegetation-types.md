## Implications for Future Discovery

The strong correlation between vegetation type and species diversity provides
a predictive framework for future discovery. Regions with extensive mesophytic
and cloud forests that have not been systematically surveyed for neurotropic
fungi represent the highest-priority targets. These include the Andean cloud
forests, Central African montane forests, Himalayan broadleaf forests, and
remaining intact cloud forests of Southeast Asia and Mesoamerica. The
pattern observed in Mexico, where mesophytic forests harbor far more species
than better-studied coniferous forests, likely applies globally and should
guide future collecting strategies.
