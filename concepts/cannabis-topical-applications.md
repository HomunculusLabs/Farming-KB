# Cannabis Topical Applications

Topical cannabis products — salves, balms, creams, oils, and patches — deliver cannabinoids and terpenes directly to the skin for localized relief of pain, inflammation, and [[cannabis-for-skin-conditions-detailed]]. Unlike inhaled or ingested cannabis, topicals do NOT produce psychoactive effects because cannabinoids do not enter the bloodstream through the skin in significant quantities.

## How Cannabis Topicals Work

### The Endocannabinoid System in Skin
- Human skin contains CB1 and CB2 [[cannabinoid-receptors-cb1-cb2]]
- CB2 receptors are concentrated in immune cells, hair follicles, and sebaceous glands
- Topical cannabinoids activate these local receptors without systemic absorption
- This produces localized anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and anti-proliferative effects

### Bioavailability Through Skin
- Cannabinoids are lipophilic (fat-loving) — they penetrate the stratum corneum (outer skin layer) effectively
- They do NOT penetrate deeply enough to reach the bloodstream (no psychoactive effect)
- Exception: transdermal patches use permeation enhancers to deliver cannabinoids systemically
- Maximum penetration occurs with alcohol-based tinctures and lipid-based formulations

## Types of Topical Products

### Salves and Balms
- Oil-based: cannabis-infused coconut oil, [[olive-cultivation-and-olive-oil-production]], shea butter, beeswax
- Thick, protective barrier on skin
- Best for: arthritis, joint pain, muscle soreness, dry/irritated skin
- Easy to make at home (see recipe below)
- [[ingham-aact-holding-time-shelf-life-decay]]: 3-6 months

### Creams and Lotions
- Water-based emulsions with [[cervantes-hash-cannabis-oil-production]]
- Lighter texture, absorbs more quickly
- Best for: general skin care, mild inflammation, daily use
- Commercial products widely available in legal markets

### Oils and Serums
- Pure cannabis-infused carrier oil (coconut, jojoba, hemp seed)
- Concentrated, versatile
- Best for: facial care, scalp treatment, targeted [[bay-11-medical-sativa-granddaddy-purp-pain-relief-oner]]
- Can be added to baths (cannabis bath bomb/soak)

### Transdermal Patches
- Adhesive patches that deliver cannabinoids through the skin into the bloodstream
- Provide systemic effects (unlike other topicals)
- Controlled, sustained release over 8-12 hours
- Available with THC (psychoactive) or CBD only
- Best for: [[cannabis-chronic-pain-management]], fibromyalgia, neuropathy

### Cannabis Bath Products
- Bath salts, bombs, soaks infused with [[cannabis-oil-capsules-preparation]] or Epsom salt + cannabis
- Full-body absorption through warm water
- Relaxing, anti-inflammatory
- Not psychoactive (unless very large amounts used)

## Conditions Treated with Topicals

### Pain and Inflammation
- **Arthritis**: CBD + THC balms applied to affected joints. Reduces inflammation and pain
- **Muscle soreness**: Post-workout recovery. Anti-inflammatory and analgesic
- **Neuropathy**: Cannabis oils applied to affected areas. CBD particularly effective
- **Headaches/migraines**: Temple and forehead application. Peppermint + cannabis oil
- **Menstrual cramps**: Lower abdominal application. CBD + THC balms

### Skin Conditions
- **Psoriasis**: Anti-proliferative effect of cannabinoids slows skin cell overgrowth. CBD-rich topicals
- **Eczema/dermatitis**: Anti-inflammatory, reduces itching and redness
- **Acne**: CBD reduces sebum production and inflammation. Topical serums
- **Sunburn**: Aloe + cannabis salve for cooling relief
- **Minor wounds/burns**: Antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties aid healing

### Other Uses
- **Insect bites**: Anti-itch and anti-inflammatory
- **Nail health**: Cuticle oil with cannabis for nail strengthening
- **Hair care**: Scalp treatments for dandruff, inflammation, hair growth stimulation

## Making Cannabis Topical Salve

### Recipe
**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup carrier oil (coconut oil, olive oil, or combination)
- 1/4 cup decarboxylated [[cervantes-cannabis-flower-anatomy]] (CBD-rich for non-psychoactive, THC-rich for stronger effect)
- 1/4 cup beeswax (adjust for harder/softer consistency)
