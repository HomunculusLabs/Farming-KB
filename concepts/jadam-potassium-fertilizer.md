# JADAM Potassium Liquid Fertilizer

Potassium (K) controls osmotic pressure in cells and activates enzymes for
respiration and photosynthesis. JADAM provides three methods for potassium JLF:
Sul-Po-Mag, potassium sulfate, and wood ash.

## Role of Potassium in Plants

- Controls osmotic pressure in cells
- Activates enzymes for respiration and photosynthesis
- Deficiency: leaf margin chlorosis and necrosis; leaves roll and shrink
- Symptoms start on mature leaves, progress toward base
- Critical for fruit size and quality

## Recipe 1: Sul-Po-Mag JLF (K + Mg + S)

Sulfate of potash magnesia (Sul-Po-Mag, K-mag) provides potassium, magnesium,
and sulfur simultaneously. It does not dissolve easily in water.

### Ingredients
- Sul-Po-Mag powder: 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)

## Recipe 3: Wood Ash JLF (traditional method)

The simplest and most accessible potassium source.

### Ingredients
- Wood ash: as much as available
- Water: sufficient to cover
- Gunny sack (burlap bag) for containing ash

### Method
1. Gather wood ash in a gunnysack
2. Place the sack in a container of water
3. Soak for **15 days**
4. Potassium and other minerals leach from ash into water
5. Remove sack, use the liquid
6. This is the traditional Korean [[jadam-calcium-fertilizer]] -- calcium for growth control
