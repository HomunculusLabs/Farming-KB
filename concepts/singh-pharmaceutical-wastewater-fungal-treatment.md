## Anaerobic Biodegradability Testing

Methanogenic activity tests are used for the determination of anaerobic
biodegradability of concentrated pharmaceutical wastes. Specific waste
streams studied include waste biomass from threonine production, mycelium
after Penicillium extraction, and excess activated sludge from the
treatment of other pharmaceutical wastewaters (Zabranska et al., 1994).
Anaerobic biodegradability of each waste and mixtures of wastes was
evaluated. An optimum substrate concentration and methane yield for each
waste and mixture were determined. These tests provide essential data for
designing anaerobic treatment stages following aerobic fungal treatment.
