## Pest Control and Companion Planting Roles

Mollison emphasizes that essential oil plants serve critical pest management functions within permaculture systems. Many aromatic species produce [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-permaculture-design-principles]]. Mollison positions them as ideal for smallholders because they require relatively little land to produce a meaningful income. A single hectare of lavender can yield 10-15 kg of essential oil annually, with wholesale prices varying widely by species and quality. Rosemary oil commands premium prices for both aromatherapy and culinary markets.

The key economic advantage noted in the Designers Manual is that essential oils are stable, concentrated products that store well without refrigeration, making them far more practical for remote producers than fresh herbs or perishable crops. Processing can be done on-farm using simple [[permaculture-food-forest-design-principles]] integration. Vetiver's deep root system (documented to 3-4 metres) provides [[biodiversity-of-fungi-ecosystem-services-agriculture]].

## Harvesting and Post-Harvest Handling

Timing of harvest significantly affects essential oil yield and quality. Most aromatic plants reach peak oil concentration just before or during flowering, when volatile compounds are at their highest concentration. Mollison notes that morning harvest, after dew has dried but before the heat of the day causes volatile loss, produces the best results. For tree-based oils like eucalyptus, younger leaves typically contain higher concentrations of essential oil than mature foliage.

Post-harvest handling is critical for leaf and flower crops. Material should be distilled as quickly as possible after harvest — wilting reduces oil content. If distillation must be delayed, plant material should be dried in shade with good airflow rather than in direct sun, which volatilizes the most valuable aromatic compounds. For root-based oils such as vetiver, roots are typically harvested at 18-24 months, washed, chopped, and dried before distillation.
