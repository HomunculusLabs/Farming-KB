## The UK Market and Prohibition Impact

The UK market was substantial, with an estimated 300 shops and market stalls
selling hallucinogenic mushrooms until the change in legal status in July 2005.
UK Treasury figures estimated annual tax revenue from mushroom sales at up to
£175,000 on a turnover of approximately £1 million. The Mixmag 2004 survey
found that 54.6% of respondents purchased their last batch from a club, shop,
or stall, 16% from a friend, and 4.2% via the internet.

The UK prohibition of psilocybin-containing fungi in July 2005 had an immediate
market impact. The Mixmag 2005 survey, conducted shortly after the ban, found
that 67% of respondents reported mushrooms were less available than before.
However, the prohibition also stimulated market innovation: retailers began
