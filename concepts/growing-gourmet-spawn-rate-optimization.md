## Fundamentals of Spawn Rate

Spawn rate is typically expressed as a percentage of the substrate wet weight:

- **Low spawn rate**: 2-5% of substrate weight
- **Standard spawn rate**: 5-15% of substrate weight
- **High spawn rate**: 15-30% of substrate weight

Grain spawn at 10% means 100 grams of colonized grain added per kilogram of
prepared (wet) substrate. Since grain is approximately 50% moisture, this
translates to roughly 50 grams of dry grain equivalents per kilogram of wet
substrate.

## How Spawn Rate Affects Cultivation

### Colonization Speed

Higher spawn rates dramatically reduce colonization time. Each grain kernel
serves as a point of inoculation, so more kernels mean mycelium can leapfrog
across the substrate rather than growing continuously from a few origin
points. Doubling the spawn rate from 5% to 10% can cut colonization time by
30-50%. Faster colonization reduces the window during which contaminants can
establish.

### Contamination Resistance

Higher spawn rates provide better biological containment of the substrate.
The mushroom mycelium, once established, produces antimicrobial compounds and
physically occupies space that competitors would otherwise colonize. With
sufficient spawn, the mycelium can overwhelm and exclude many contaminants
that land on the substrate surface.

### Biological Efficiency and Yield

The relationship between spawn rate and yield is not linear. Below the minimum
effective rate, colonization is slow and yields suffer significantly. At
standard rates (5-15%), most species achieve 80-95% of their maximum
potential yield. Beyond optimal rates, yield improvements plateau while cost
increases linearly.

## Species-Specific Spawn Rate Guidelines

### Oyster Mushrooms (Pleurotus spp.)
Oyster mycelium is aggressive and fast-growing. Standard spawn rates of 5-10%
are usually sufficient for straw and sawdust substrates. Higher rates (10-15%)
are justified when using large-format straw logs, cultivating during warm
months with higher contamination pressure, or growing slower-colonizing
species like [[cultivator-sterile-technique-and-agar-culture]].

### Button Mushroom (Agaricus bisporus)
Commercial Agaricus growers typically use 3-5% spawn rate because the
composted substrate is already selectively favorable. Grain spawn is mixed
into compost at approximately 1 quart per 15 square feet of bed surface.
