### Ground Covers

Low-growing species that cover the soil surface protect against erosion,
retain moisture, and suppress weed competition. Strawberry, clover, thyme,
nasturtium, and sweet potato serve as living ground covers in guild designs,
maintaining soil health and filling ecological niches that might otherwise be
occupied by weeds.
