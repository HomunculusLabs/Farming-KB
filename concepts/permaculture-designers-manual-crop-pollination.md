# Crop Pollination
#permaculture #pollination #bees #insects #insectary

Updated: 2026-04-18

> From: Bill Mollison, *Permaculture: A Designers Manual* (1988), Chapters 3, 12.

## Overview

Pollination is an essential service in food production. In permaculture design, pollinator habitat is created through deliberate planting of insectary species, windbreak design, and integrated [[permaculture-designers-manual-rabbit-housing]]. Crops within a mile of hives will out-yield crops in bee-deficient areas by a factor of 3-10 times.

## Bee-Dependent Crops

Bees carry out essential pollination for a wide variety of food, oil, fruit, and seed crops:
- Mustard and Brassicas
- Clovers
- Buckwheat
- Most smallfruit (berries)
- Apples and pome fruits
- Grain legumes
- Kiwifruit
- Bramble berries
- Stone pine (Pinus pinea)
- Acacia species

## Insectary Plants for Pollinators

### Umbelliferae (Carrot Family)
These are the primary insectary plants, hosting predatory wasps, robber flies, ladybirds, and pollinator bees:
- Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare)
- Dill (Anethum graveolens)
- Queen Anne's lace (Daucus carota)
- Carrot flowers
- Parsnip flowers
- Coriander (Coriandrum sativum)

### Compositae (Daisy Family)
Small-flowered daisy species attract predatory insects and pollinators:
- Tansy (Tanacetum vulgare)
- Echium fastuosum (pride of Madeira) -- excellent bulk bee forage
- Marigolds (Tagetes) -- also fumigate soils against nematodes

### Flowering Shrubs for Insectivorous Birds and Bees
- Kniphofia (red hot poker)
- Banksia
- Salvia species
- Buddleia (butterfly bush)
- Fuchsia
- Echium fastuosum
- Quillaja (a small tree attracting many wasps)
- Photinia
- Some Tamarix species
- Some Acacia species

### Ground Cover Flowers
- Catnip (Nepeta cataria)
- Phyla (Lippia) nodiflora
- Nasturtium -- soft ground cover, prevents soil drying, provides mulch
- Flowering ground covers generally attract wasps, pollinator bees, and insectivorous birds

### Small-Fruited Trees
- Rosemary (classic early-season bee forage)
- Thyme (classic honey source)
- Catmint (classic honey source)

## Frog Ponds for Insect Control

Small ponds or troughs throughout orchards will breed frogs for leaf insect control. Frogs are among the most effective insect predators in garden and orchard systems. Troughs free of dragonflies give fewer bee losses than large ponds.

## Windbreak Design for Pollinators

Cross-wind forage hedgerows shelter bee-dependent seed crops, herbs, and vines. Cold winds most restrict bee foraging, so hedgerow (even low hedgerow of 0.5-1 m) is essential cover, preferably leading from the hive site to the forage. Such hedges can be made of rosemary, Acacia, or even soil ridges with catmint, capeweed, thyme, or field daisies.

## Design for Pollination

### Maximise Flowering Components
In general, aim to maximise the floristic or flowering components of orchard interplant. This attracts pollinators and predatory insects, reduces or eliminates grasses, and provides ground cover for frogs and insectivorous lizards.

### Clumped vs. Scattered Plantings
Bees more efficiently harvest clumped rather than scattered nectar sources. Hedgerows, fields, or clumps of preferred forage species are better than a scatter of the same species in a mosaic of individual plants.

### Seasonal Spread
Divide forage species into early, mid, and late season for a good spread of yield. It is best to have 35 main forage species over the whole season.

## Pest Reduction Through Pollinator Habitat

Pest species in orchards can be reduced to 4-7% of monocrop orchard by a combination of strategies:
- Selection of disease resistant stock
- Minimal pruning
- Predation by birds, frogs, lizards, wasp and predatory insects attracted by insectary plants
- Interplant of leguminous trees
- Reducing crop stress with [[permaculture-designers-manual-windbreak-species]] and [[permaculture-designers-manual-mulch-systems]]
- Ground foraging by poultry

## Integration with Food Forests

In [[permaculture-designers-manual-food-forest-guilds]], the herb layer provides critical pollinator habitat. Every orchard and food forest should include dedicated insectary plantings integrated into the [[mollison-designers-edge-effect-design]] and windbreaks.
