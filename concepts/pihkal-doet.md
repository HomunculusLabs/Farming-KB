## Dosage and Administration

Shulgin's recommended oral dosage for DOET ranges from 2 to 6 milligrams.
A threshold dose is reported at approximately 1 to 2 mg, with doses above 6
mg producing increasingly intense and potentially overwhelming effects. The
dosage range is somewhat wider than those of DOB or DOI, providing a bit
more room for individual variation in sensitivity.

Onset occurs within 1.5 to 3 hours after oral administration. The
experience peaks at approximately 3 to 5 hours, with total duration ranging
from 8 to 14 hours. This makes DOET one of the more manageable members of
the DO family in terms of time commitment, comparable to DOM in overall
duration.

The relatively moderate onset time and duration make DOET somewhat more
forgiving than the longer-acting DO compounds, though the delayed onset
still poses a risk of premature redosing.
