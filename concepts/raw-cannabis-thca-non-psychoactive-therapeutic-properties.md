## Integration with Decarboxylated Cannabis Therapy

Raw and decarboxylated cannabis preparations are not mutually exclusive but
can be integrated into a comprehensive therapeutic approach. A patient might
use raw cannabis juice during the day for anti-inflammatory and CBD-rich
effects while reserving decarboxylated cannabis for evening use when
psychoactive effects are acceptable or desired. This layered approach
maximizes the therapeutic versatility of the cannabis plant while managing
the practical constraints of psychoactivity.
