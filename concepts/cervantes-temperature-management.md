## Day and Night Temperature Differentials

A natural day-to-night temperature drop is beneficial.
Most cannabis strains perform well with a 5 to 10
degree F (3 to 6 degrees C) difference between the
lights-on and lights-off periods. This differential:

- Mimics natural outdoor conditions
- Promotes proper metabolic cycling
- Can enhance terpene and resin production during
  flowering
- Reduces stress on the plant

Excessive temperature swings, however, can stress
plants and should be avoided.

## Temperature and Sex Expression

Environmental temperature during the seedling stage
influences sex expression in cannabis grown from seed.
Low temperatures increase the proportion of female
plants, while warm temperatures tend to produce more
male plants. This effect is most pronounced during the
first two weeks after seedlings have three pairs of
true leaves.
