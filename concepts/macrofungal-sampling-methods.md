## Species Richness Estimation

Because complete inventories are rarely achievable, researchers rely on estimators to extrapolate total richness from observed data.

### First-Order Jackknife

**S\* = Sobs + [L(n−1) / n]**

Where:
- Sobs = observed number of species
- L = number of species found in only one sample (singletons)
- n = number of samples

### Chao 2

**S\* = Sobs + (L² / 2M)**

Where:
- L = number of singletons
- M = number of species found in exactly two samples (doubletons)

### Limitations

**No extrapolation technique is robust when applied to all data** (Schmit et al. 1999). Estimators perform variably depending on community structure, sampling intensity, and the degree of rarity in the assemblage. Extrapolation to total richness is **problematic but necessary** for meaningful comparisons across sites, treatments, or time periods.
