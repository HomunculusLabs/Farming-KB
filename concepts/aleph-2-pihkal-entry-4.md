# ALEPH-2

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthioamphetamine; meta-DOT

## Overview

ALEPH-2 is the second member of the [[aleph-family-overview]] of sulfur-containing psychedelic amphetamines, featuring an ethylthio group at the 4-position. Compared to [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]] (which has a methylthio group), ALEPH-2 is slightly more potent and significantly longer-acting (8-16 hours vs 6-8 hours). It produces remarkable visual phenomena, body warmth, and enhanced tactile/auditory perception, but exhibits the characteristic ALEPH family unpredictability in dose-response relationships.

## Chemical Information

- Chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthioamphetamine
- CAS: Not widely registered
- Molecular formula: C13H21NO2S (free base)
- Molecular weight: 255.39 g/mol (free base)
- Class: Substituted amphetamine (ALEPH family, sulfur-substituted DOx variant)
- Synonym: meta-DOT
- The ethylthio homologue of [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]]; amphetamine counterpart of [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Light dose | 4 - 5 mg |
| Common dose | 5 - 7 mg |
| Strong dose | 7 - 8 mg |
| Duration | 8 - 16 hours |
| Onset | 30 - 60 minutes |

WARNING: The ALEPH family shows extreme inter-individual variability. One person at 4 mg experienced fetal position hibernation with amnesia for hours, while another at 8 mg felt only bare threshold effects.

## Effects and Qualitative Commentary

### 4 mg -- Body Warmth and Enhanced Perception
A warm feeling spreads throughout the body with light pressure in the head that evolves into "the feeling of a balloon without any anatomical definition." Color perception is not dramatically enhanced, and vision is not sharpened as with [[shroom-scientific-study]]. Instead, users notice "waves of movement, very smooth and not too busy." Both tactile perception and auditory acuity are enhanced. The main effect is "paradoxically, an easier handling of the outer world." No amphetamine jitters. The body feeling is described as "good, healthy," with peace regarding "the body-mind dualism."

### 5 mg -- Visual Distortions at the Edge of Hallucination
An extraordinary day of visuals and interpretations. About two hours in, effects are still climbing with a "marvelous onset of visual distortions and illusions, right at the edge of hallucination." Logs in a fireplace appear in continuous motion. Notepaper scrunches and deforms under pen pressure. Nothing stays still; everything is always moving. A phase of "unabated inflation" occurs. Intensity drops at the fifth hour, with considerable residual shakes and muscular tremor. Tooth-rubbiness persists even towards midnight, but sleep is eventually achievable.

### 5 mg -- Magical and Unworldly
New environments are difficult to categorize -- separating what was "seen differently" from "seen for the first time." The Santa Cruz Mystery Spot "should have been bizarre but it was simply hokey. And yet the boardwalk that should have been depressing was totally magical." The day feels "unworldly" and ends with considerable muscular weakness.

### 7 mg -- Retinal Hallucination and Philosophical Insight
"An amazing unification of visual hallucination seen only in the very fine detail of something, and what must be considered retinal hallucination." The user describes how the mind picks and chooses from different retinal cells of the high-resolution part of the eye, sometimes from the right eye, sometimes from the left. A small curve or bump becomes "whatever you wish. For a moment. And then it chooses again, but differently." This leads to a profound question: "Is all of our perceived world as subjective as this?"

### 8 mg -- Extreme Intoxication Without Visuals
"Extreme intoxication, but almost no visual phenomena." Despite the absence of visual effects, driving would be impossible because the user doesn't trust that "the reality I see is the same reality that the other driver might see." The user becomes "very much the center of the world about me."

## Extensions and Commentary

### ALEPH Family Unpredictability

The entire [[aleph-family-overview]] is characterized by extreme inter-individual variability in dose-response. One person at 4 mg took and maintained a fetal position in "relative hibernation" for several hours with substantial amnesia, while another at 8 mg felt only bare threshold effects. This erratic nature was discovered only after ALEPH-1 through ALEPH-7 had already been synthesized and assigned.

### Missing ALEPH Numbers

Several ALEPH numbers were never completed:
- **ALEPH-3** (2,5-dimethoxy-4-beta-methallylthioamphetamine): The aldehyde synthesis was attempted twice and abandoned both times. The corresponding phenethylamine would have been 2C-T-3 or 2C-T-20.
- **ALEPH-5** (2,5-dimethoxy-4-cyclohexylthioamphetamine): The aldehyde was obtained in tiny yield (0.4 g, mp 95-96 C) but was never pursued further.

### Relationship to 2C-T Family

ALEPH-2 is the amphetamine (3-carbon) homologue of [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]. The [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] (2-carbon homologues) proved more predictable in dosing and richer in subjective effects than the ALEPH series, leading to their more thorough exploration.

## Synthesis Summary

Synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(ethylthio)benzaldehyde (see [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] for aldehyde synthesis) via condensation with nitroethane and ammonium acetate (steam bath, 3h), yielding 1-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylthiophenyl)-2-nitropropene as pale orange crystals (mp 112-113 C). Reduction with LAH in THF (24h reflux) gives the free base as a pale amber oil, converted to HCl salt (mp 128-130 C with decomposition).

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]]: The methylthio analog. ALEPH is less potent (5-10 mg vs 4-8 mg) and shorter-acting (6-8h vs 8-16h). Both share the characteristic inter-individual variability.
- [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]]: The isopropylthio analog (entry #5), active at 7-12 mg.
- [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]]: The heptylthio analog (entry #7), active at 4-7 mg.
- [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]: The 2-carbon (phenethylamine) homologue. More thoroughly explored, with a dose range of 12-25 mg.
- [[shroom-scientific-study]]: The methyl-substituted (non-sulfur) analog. DOM produces sharper visual enhancement and is much longer-acting (14-20 hours).
- adobe-construction|DOB: The bromo (non-sulfur) analog. Far more potent (1-3 mg) and much longer-acting.

## Harm Reduction Notes

- Extremely low active doses (4-8 mg). Milligram-accurate scales are absolutely essential.
- Severe inter-individual variability -- what is threshold for one person may cause extreme intoxication in another.
- Long duration (8-16 hours) requires full-day planning.
- Muscular tremor, shakes, and tooth-rubbiness are common even as effects diminish.
- Driving is absolutely contraindicated even in the apparent absence of visual effects.
- The [[aleph-family-overview]] is relatively unexplored compared to other psychedelic families.
- See [[harm-reduction-guide]] for general psychedelic safety principles.
- The [[lsd-set-and-setting-framework]] framework is essential.
