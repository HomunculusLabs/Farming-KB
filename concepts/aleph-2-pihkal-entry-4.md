# ALEPH-2

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthioamphetamine; meta-DOT

## Overview

ALEPH-2 is the second member of the [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Light dose | 4 - 5 mg |
| Common dose | 5 - 7 mg |
| Strong dose | 7 - 8 mg |
| Duration | 8 - 16 hours |
| Onset | 30 - 60 minutes |

WARNING: The ALEPH family shows extreme inter-individual variability. One person at 4 mg experienced fetal position hibernation with amnesia for hours, while another at 8 mg felt only bare threshold effects.

## Effects and Qualitative Commentary

### 4 mg -- Body Warmth and Enhanced Perception
A warm feeling spreads throughout the body with light pressure in the head that evolves into "the feeling of a balloon without any anatomical definition." Color perception is not dramatically enhanced, and vision is not sharpened as with [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] (2-carbon homologues) proved more predictable in dosing and richer in subjective effects than the ALEPH series, leading to their more thorough exploration.

## Synthesis Summary

Synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(ethylthio)benzaldehyde (see [[aleph-7-pihkal-entry-7]]: The heptylthio analog (entry #7), active at 4-7 mg.
