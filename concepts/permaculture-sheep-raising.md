## Seasonal Management Calendar

Managing sheep effectively means anticipating the needs of each season and preparing in advance.

Spring (March-May):

- Shear ewes before lambing begins

- Prepare lambing pens with clean, dry bedding

- Begin pasture rotation as grass starts growing; introduce sheep gradually to prevent bloat on lush new growth

- Vaccinate lambs at 6-8 weeks

- Check and repair fencing before turnout

Summer (June-August):

- Move sheep to fresh pasture frequently; parasite pressure peaks in warm, wet weather

- Monitor for fly strike and treat promptly

- Wean lambs at 8-12 weeks of age

- Provide shade and plenty of fresh water; sheep can suffer [[sheep-keeping-guide]]

## Fencing Design and Rotation

Proper fencing is essential for containing sheep and implementing
effective rotational grazing within a permaculture design.
Woven wire or high-tensile electric fencing with 4-6 strands works
well; sheep are less likely than cattle to test electric fences
once trained, but the bottom wire should be close enough to the
ground (8-12 inches) to prevent lambs from crawling under.
Permanent perimeter fencing combined with temporary interior
electric fencing allows flexible paddock subdivision for rotation.
A typical rotation moves sheep to fresh pasture every 3-7 days,
with each paddock resting for 30-60 days depending on season and
stocking density.
This rest period allows regrowth and breaks parasite cycles,
making rotational grazing both a productivity and health strategy.
See [[pasture-management]] for rotation planning principles.

## Sheep Breeds for Permaculture Systems

Breed selection should match the farm's climate, forage base, and
production goals within the permaculture framework.
Hair sheep breeds (Katahdin, St. Croix, Dorper) shed their wool
naturally and are excellent choices for systems where shearing is
impractical or where meat production is the primary goal.
These breeds are generally more parasite-resistant than wool breeds
and tolerate heat better, making them well-suited to warmer climates.
