# Cannabis, Motion Sickness, CB1 Receptors, and Anandamide

## Motion Sickness Mechanism

Motion sickness — seasickness, airsickness, and carsickness — manifests through
nausea, vomiting, dizziness, and vertigo. The core mechanism is **visual-vestibular
discord**: sensory conflict between movements perceived by the eyes versus those
perceived by the inner ear's vestibular system. When unresolved, the brain's
nausea center activates and nausea may progress to vomiting. An estimated 7–28%
of travelers experience acute motion sickness.
