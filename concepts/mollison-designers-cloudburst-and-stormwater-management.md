## Vegetated Drainage and Riparian Buffers

Natural and constructed drainage lines must be stabilised with vegetation
to handle high-velocity flows without eroding. Mollison recommends planting
riparian buffers along all watercourses with dense, deep-rooted native
species that bind soil, filter sediments and pollutants, and slow water
movement. Bamboo, willow, and various fast-growing native grasses are
particularly effective for this purpose.

On larger properties, vegetated waterways serve as broad, shallow drainage
channels that convey overflow from swales and detention basins to the
primary drainage network. These waterways are designed to carry water as
sheet flow rather than concentrated channel flow, minimising erosive
energy. The combination of vegetated drainage with contour earthworks
creates a cascading system that progressively reduces runoff energy as
water moves down the catchment.
