## Building Soil Fertility Long-Term

updated: 2026-04-18

Building long-term soil fertility is the ultimate goal of the organic gardener. Rather than treating soil as an inert substrate that needs annual chemical inputs, the organic approach views soil as a living ecosystem that, once established and properly maintained, can largely feed itself. The strategies for building this kind of lasting fertility are biological, not chemical.
