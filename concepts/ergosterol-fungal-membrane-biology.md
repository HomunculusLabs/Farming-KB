## Industrial and Biotechnological Applications

Ergosterol and its derivatives have several commercial applications beyond antifungal drug targeting:

- **Steroid hormone synthesis**: Ergosterol serves as a microbial feedstock for industrial production of progesterone, cortisone, and other steroid intermediates through microbial transformation
- **Phytosterol standard**: Used as a reference compound in analytical chemistry for sterol profiling
- **Biofuel precursor**: Ergosterol content affects biodiesel quality from oleaginous fungi and yeast
- **Cosmetic ingredient**: Ergosterol derivatives are used in skin care formulations for their purported moisturizing and antioxidant properties
