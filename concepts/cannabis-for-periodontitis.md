## Mechanism of Action

### CB2 in Bone Metabolism
CB2 receptors on osteoclasts (bone-resorbing cells) inhibit their activity. In periodontitis:
1. Bacterial biofilm triggers chronic inflammation in the gums
2. Inflammatory cytokines activate RANK/RANKL signaling
3. Osteoclasts are stimulated, destroying the alveolar bone
4. CBD activates CB2 receptors, which suppresses RANK/RANKL
5. Bone destruction is slowed or halted

### Anti-Inflammatory
CBD reduces the inflammatory cytokines that drive periodontal tissue destruction, addressing both the cause (inflammation) and the effect (bone loss).
