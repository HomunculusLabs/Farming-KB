## The Jewel in the Lotus

The Buddhist chant "Om mani padme hum" encapsulates this philosophy in a mnemonic pattern:

- **Om**: The fundamental harmonic — the sound of all stillness and all time, recalling the universe itself
- **Mani**: The unchanging diamantine essence or truth at the core of all phenomena
- **Padme**: "Enfolded in the heart of the lotus" — the visible unfolding of events revealing the essential core; the lotus represents the implicate order of tessellated events
- **Hum**: "It is here, now" — a declaration that all events are present now, and forever repeated in their form

This ancient mnemonic encodes the same understanding that modern physics and biology are converging upon: that all phenomena are expressions of a single, enfolded reality.
