## Freshness Counts

If you use sawdust and chips for indoor or outdoor cultivation,
freshness is critical -- or else competitors may have already taken
hold. Lumber mills, pulp mills, furniture manufacturers, and many
other wood product companies generate waste usable to the mushroom
cultivator. However, industries that run mixed woods and do not
separate their sawdust into identifiable piles are not recommended
as substrate suppliers.
