## Rising Sea Levels

Rising sea levels threaten vast areas of farmland. Some forecasts predict sea level rise as high as seven meters (23 ft). Just one meter would devastate over 80% of rice paddies in the Philippines, Vietnam, and Thailand. Half of Bangladesh would go underwater. One-sixth of the world population lives at one meter or less above sea level.

JADAM's conclusion: "Fertile, arable land will become more and more valuable. The land you own will be your wealth."

## Financial Market Volatility

Global financial markets will become more volatile, economies stuck in low-growth. Stocks, bonds, insurance, pensions, and typical real estate may no longer serve as safe forms of wealth. Running and investing in businesses including farms based on debt is dangerous.

JADAM's alternative: "Arable land that returns continued produce and profit will be more valuable. Let us do a farming that is free from money; ultra-low cost; and self-sufficient."

## The Core Strategy

JADAM's response to all these challenges is the same: reduce farming cost to near-zero through self-sufficient input production. When farming costs almost nothing:
- Free trade competition becomes irrelevant
- Energy price spikes don't matter
- Food is produced regardless of global supply chains
- Land retains its productive value regardless of financial markets
- Farmers are independent of debt and corporate inputs

## Government Policy Critique

JADAM critiques government agricultural policy: when the state sees farmers as people in need of help, it increases spending while farmers lose self-supporting capability. Taxpayer money flows into the pockets of fertilizer, pesticide, and machine companies, leaving farmers poor and excluded.
