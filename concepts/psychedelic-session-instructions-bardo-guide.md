# Psychedelic Session Instructions — The Bardo Guide Texts

Part IV of *The Psychedelic Experience* by Timothy Leary, Ralph Metzner, and Richard Alpert contains the actual instruction texts designed to be read aloud to a person undergoing a psychedelic session. These texts are adapted from the *Tibetan Book of the Dead* (Bardo Thodol) and serve as a practical guide for navigating the expanded states of consciousness encountered during the experience. The instructions correspond to the three Bardos — periods of transition between states of being — and are structured to help the voyager recognize, accept, and move through each phase without attachment or fear. Each major descriptive section of the original Tibetan text has an accompanying instruction passage, designed to lead the voyager back to the original First Bardo transcendence whenever they become lost.

## Session Preparation and Group Composition

The setting and personnel of a psychedelic session are critical to its outcome. Small groups are preferable, with a maximum of six or seven participants. At least two guides should be present — individuals who are experienced, calm, and familiar with the Bardo maps. One guide takes the psychedelic substance while the other serves as practical ground control, managing recordings, food, and reading from the manual as needed. One experienced woman is recommended as part of the guiding team, as her presence contributes a stabilizing and nurturing energy to the session environment. Married couples are advised to have their initial sessions separately, so that each person can undergo the experience without the interpersonal dynamics of the relationship influencing or distorting the process. Unexpected or undesired manipulations by one participant can easily trap others into paranoid Third Bardo delusions. The guides read the appropriate instructions at the proper moments, matching the text to the phase of experience the voyager is undergoing.

## First Bardo Instructions

The First Bardo corresponds to the initial phase of ego-loss and the encounter with the [[clear-light]] — the primordial, undifferentiated ground of awareness. The instructions prepare the voyager for ego-death by urging them to seek new levels of reality and to recognize the Clear Light directly. The voyager is addressed by name and told that their ego and familiar game are about to cease. In the ego-free state, all things are like the void and cloudless sky, and the naked intellect is like a transparent vacuum. The voyager is instructed to know themselves and abide in that state.

The teaching emphasizes that the Clear Light is the true nature of mind — radiant, empty, and self-luminous — and that recognizing it is the purpose of the session. The voyager is told to concentrate on the unity of all living beings and to hold onto the Clear Light, using it to attain understanding and love. The instruction frames ego-death as an opportunity — "the hour of death and rebirth" — a temporary death to be used for obtaining the perfect state of enlightenment. If the voyager cannot maintain the bliss of illumination and begins slipping back into contact with the external world, they are reminded that the hallucinations and visions will teach them about themselves and the world. The veil of routine perception will be torn from their eyes, and the memory of friends and the power of admired persons should be called upon if confusion arises.

Beyond the restless flowing electricity of life lies the ultimate reality — the Void. The voyager's own awareness, not formed into anything possessing form or color, is naturally void. This is the Final Reality, the All Good, the All Peaceful, the Light. The Void is not nothingness but beginning and end itself — unobstructed, shining, thrilling, blissful. Diamond consciousness. Knowing this is sufficient. Recognizing the voidness of one's own consciousness as Buddhahood, and maintaining that recognition, allows the voyager to sustain the divine mind.

## Second Bardo — Preliminary Instructions

The Second Bardo is the period of game hallucinations — the rich, varied, and sometimes overwhelming visionary content that arises when consciousness is no longer anchored to the ego. The preliminary instructions explain the structure of the three Bardos to orient the voyager:

1. **The Bardo of the Clear Light** — the initial flash of ego-transcendence.
2. **The Bardo of Game Illusions** — the hallucinatory visions that follow if the Clear Light is not recognized.
3. **The Bardo of Re-entry** — the return to ordinary consciousness and the regaining of an ego.

Ego-transcendence is described as a universal experience — not unique to any individual, culture, or era. The voyager is addressed as "friend" and told that this experience comes to all at some time, and that they are fortunate to have this gratuitously given rebirth experience. They must not cling in fondness and weakness to their old self. Even though they may cling to their mind, they have lost the power to keep it. Nothing can be gained by struggling in the hallucinatory world.

The natural sound of reality may be heard — compared to a thousand thunders simultaneously sounding — and the voyager must not be alarmed. Thought-forms will become visible, appearing as visions, sounds, and sensations that seem utterly real. The voyager is reminded: "These realms are not come from somewhere outside yourself. They come from within and shine upon you. The revelations too exist from eternity within the faculties of your own intellect."

The key teaching of the Second Bardo is: **relax, merge, be neither attached nor afraid, do nothing about the visions**. The voyager should blissfully accept the wonders of their own creativity, recognizing that all visions exist only within them. Above all, they should do nothing about the visions.

## Instructions for Vision 1 — The Source

The first vision of the Second Bardo is the encounter with the Source — the radiant seed energy from which all living forms emerge. This energy shoots forth and strikes against the voyager with a light so brilliant they can scarcely look at it. The voyager is told not to be frightened, not to try to intellectualize it, not to play games with it. They should merge with it, let it flow through them, lose themselves in it. The instruction leads them to fuse in the Halo of Rainbow Light and obtain Buddhahood in the Central Realm of the Densely Packed — the heart of all creation.

---

## Instructions for Physical Symptoms

Physical symptoms — nausea, trembling, temperature changes, bodily distortions, and other somatic experiences — are explicitly stated to be "not drug-effects." They indicate that the voyager is struggling against awareness of feelings surpassing normal experience. These are universal energy-waves that cannot be controlled. The voyager should:
- Relax and not struggle against the symptoms
- Allow themselves to pulsate with the surrounding vibrations
- Accept the symptoms as the message of the body
- Welcome them and enjoy them

The symptoms will disappear as soon as all trace of ego-centered striving disappears.

## Instructions for Vision 2 — The Internal Flow of Archetypal Processes

The second vision presents the internal flow of archetypal processes — an endless parade of pure forms and sounds, dazzlingly brilliant and ever-changing. The life flow whirls through the voyager, presenting ancient cosmic myths of creation and manifestation. The instruction is to not try to control it or understand it ("there is plenty of time for that later"), but to merge with it and let it flow. The voyager is being taught the great lessons of evolution, creation, and reproduction. If they try to stop the flow, they may fall into hell-worlds of unbearable misery generated by their own mind. They should avoid game interpretations, avoid thinking, talking, and doing. The instruction leads them to merge in Rainbow Light into the Heart of the River of Created Forms and obtain Buddhahood in the Realm called Pre-Eminently Happy.

---

## Instructions for Vision 3 — The Fire-Flow of Internal Unity

The third vision is the fire-flow of internal unity — an organic fire ecstasy in which the hard, dry, brittle husks of the ego are washed out into the endless sea of creation. The ecstasy of organic fire glows in every cell. This vision is associated with the red [[buddha-amitabha]], who sweeps the voyager along in a tide of emotional unity. The voyager is instructed not to fear the ecstasy, not to resist the flow, and to recognize that all exultant power comes from within. They should release their attachment, recognize the wisdom of their own blood, trust the tide-force, and let their heart burst in love for all life. The instruction leads them to merge into the Heart-Glow of Buddha [[amitabha]], float in the Rainbow Sea, and attain Buddhahood in the Realm named Exultant Love.

---

## Instructions for Vision 4 — The Wave-Vibration Structure of External Forms

The fourth vision reveals the wave structure of the world — everything dissolves into energy vibrations. The voyager tunes in on the electric dance of energy. There are no longer things and persons but only the direct flow of particles. Consciousness leaves the body and flows into the stream of wave rhythm. The voyager's brain becomes a receiving set for the radiance. As the three-dimensional world fragments, panic may arise, or a fondness for the heavy dull world of objects being left behind. The instruction is to not fear the transparent, radiant, dazzling wave energy, to let the intellect rest, and to experience directly the revelation of primal forms — the basic structure of matter and wave communication.

---

## Instructions for Vision 5 — The Vibratory Waves of External Unity

The fifth vision presents the unity of all living forms. People may seem rubbery and lifeless, like plastic puppets — this is only the attempt of the ego to maintain its separate identity. The voyager is instructed to feel the unity of all, merge with the world around them, and enjoy the dance of the puppets created by their own mind. They should relax and feel the ecstatic energy-vibrations pulsing through them. The glowing radiance is a reflection of their own consciousness — one aspect of their divine nature. They should not be attached to their old human self or alarmed at new feelings. If attracted to the old self, they will be reborn for another round of game-existence. The instruction leads them to merge into the heart of the Blessed [[ratnasambhava]] in a Halo of Rainbow Light and attain liberation in the Realm Endowed with Glory.

---

## Instructions for Vision 6 — The Retinal Circus

The sixth vision is the Retinal Circus — ecstatic kaleidoscopic patterns exploding around the voyager. All possible shapes come to life: the ceaseless play of elements — earth, water, air, fire — in ever-changing forms and manifestations. The voyager should relax and enjoy the rushing stream without becoming attached to any vision or revelation. If unpleasant experiences come, they should let them flit by with the rest. The voyager should meditate calmly on the knowledge that all these visions are emanations of their own consciousness — this is the great lesson in the creativity and power of the brain, freed from its learned structures. Through this recognition, the voyager can obtain self-knowledge and be liberated.

---

## Instructions for Vision 7 — The Magic Theatre

The seventh vision is the Magic Theatre — the appearance of mythical superhuman figures representing the highest reaches of human knowledge. Demons, goddesses, celestial warriors, giants, angels, Bodhisattvas, dwarfs, crusaders, elves, devils, saints, sorcerers, infernal spirits, goblins, knights, and emperors all populate this divine theatre. Specific archetypal figures are named: the Lotus Lord of Dance, the Wise Old Man, the Divine Child, the Trickster, the Shapeshifter, the Tamer of Monsters, the Mother of Gods, the Witch, the Moon King, and the Wanderer. The voyager is told not to be afraid — these figures are within them. Their own creative intellect is the master magician of them all. The voyager should recognize the figures as aspects of themselves, understanding that the whole fantastic comedy takes place within. They should not become attached to the figures but remember the teachings to still attain liberation.

---

## Instructions for the Wrathful Visions

The wrathful visions are the Second Bardo's nightmares — terrifying, overwhelming, and seemingly hostile hallucinations that arise when the ego fights against its own dissolution. The voyager is told that they were unable to maintain the perfect Clear Light of the First Bardo or the serene peaceful visions of the Second, and are now entering the nightmares. They must recognize these visions. They are the voyager's own thought-forms made visible and audible — products of their own mind with its back to the wall. They indicate that the voyager is close to liberation. No harm can come from these hallucinations. The instruction tells the voyager to welcome them, merge with them, join them, lose themselves in them: "They are yours."

Whatever is seen, no matter how strange and terrifying, the voyager must remember that it comes from within. As soon as they recognize this, they will obtain liberation. If they do not recognize them, torture and punishment will ensue — but these too are but the radiances of the voyager's own intellect, and they are immaterial. The core instruction is: **voidness cannot injure voidness**. None of the peaceful or wrathful visions, blood-drinking demons, machines, monsters, or devils exist in reality — only within the skull. This understanding dissipates fear.

## Third Bardo — Preliminary Instructions

The Third Bardo is the period of re-entry — the return of consciousness to the body and to ordinary, game-playing reality ([[sidpa-bardo]]). The voyager is told that while they could not recognize the archetypal forms of the Second Bardo, and became unconscious through fear, their consciousness is now rising up "like a trout leaping forth out of water, striving for its original form." The former ego has started to operate again. The instruction is: do not struggle to figure things out. If through weakness the voyager is attracted to action and thinking, they will wander amidst the world of game existence and suffer pain. They should relax their restless mind and float back to the unobscured, primordial, bright, void state of the intellect.

If unable to relax the mind, the voyager is instructed to meditate on their protective figure or on their friends, thinking of them with profound love and trust. The voyager may feel the power to perform miraculous feats, perceive with extrasensory power, or change shape and traverse space and time. These feelings come naturally and should not be desired or exercised — they are merely signs of being in the Third Bardo. Frightening visions may come: gusts of wind, humming and clicking of controlling machinery, mocking laughter, imagined taunts of "guilty," "stupid," "inadequate," "nasty." These are the residues of selfish, ego-dominated game-playing. The voyager is reminded that they are struggling to re-enter the denser atmosphere of routine game existence, and should let the re-entry be smooth and slow without force of will-power. The winds of karma — the accumulated momentum of past actions and tendencies — may drive the voyager here and there, and they are instructed to let this happen without resistance.

## The Seven Visions: A Structural Overview

The Second Bardo's seven visions follow a structured progression from internal to external, intellectual to emotional:

| Vision | Name | Mode | Aspect | Tibetan Realm |
|--------|------|------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | The Source | Internal | — | Central Realm of the Densely Packed |
| 2 | Archetypal Processes | Internal | Intellectual | Pre-Eminently Happy |
| 3 | Fire-Flow of Internal Unity | Internal | Emotional | Exultant Love |
| 4 | Wave-Vibration of External Forms | External | Intellectual | — |
| 5 | Vibratory Waves of External Unity | External | Emotional | Realm Endowed with Glory |
| 6 | The Retinal Circus | External | Perceptual | — |
| 7 | The Magic Theatre | External | Mythological | — |

Visions 1–3 occur with eyes closed, ignoring external stimuli. Visions 4–7 involve open eyes and engagement with external forms. This progression mirrors the voyager's gradual re-engagement with the sensory world after the ego-dissolution of the First Bardo. Each vision is associated with a specific meditative instruction designed to prevent the voyager from becoming trapped in any single visionary state and to guide them back toward the liberating recognition that all phenomena are projections of consciousness.

## Summary: The Core Teaching

The Bardo guide texts form a complete navigational system for the psychedelic experience. From the initial encounter with the Clear Light, through the visionary landscapes of the Second Bardo, to the re-entry phase of the Third Bardo, the instructions provide a consistent teaching:

1. **Recognize** each experience as a product of your own consciousness
2. **Do not cling** to any vision or flee from any terror
3. **Maintain awareness** that all phenomena are empty of inherent existence

The guide's role is to read the appropriate text at the appropriate moment, providing the voyager with a verbal anchor that reconnects them to the liberating perspective at each stage of the journey. The texts can be pre-recorded and played back, or read aloud by a companion. The aim is always to lead the voyager back toward the original First Bardo transcendence and to help maintain that state for as long as possible.
