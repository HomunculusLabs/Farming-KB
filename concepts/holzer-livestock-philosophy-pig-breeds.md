Old domestic breeds are:
- Significantly hardier
- More intelligent and adaptable
- Natural instincts are good enough that they don't "fall over their own feet"
- Produce is of far higher quality
- Can be kept under near-natural conditions

## Pigs in Permaculture

### Why Pigs Are Central

"For me there is no more versatile or more helpful an animal. If I were to close them away in a pen, however, I would have to work for my pigs instead."

### Pigs as Workers

- **Soil loosening:** When searching for food they plough through top layers of earth, loosening and aerating soil
- **Directed tilling:** Easily directed by scattering loose feed (peas, grain, maize) in appropriate places
- **Scale:** Works for small- to large-scale tilling with minimal human effort
- **Stone ground:** Indispensable as "living ploughs" on stony ground that cannot be ploughed mechanically
- **Orchard cleanup:** Do not damage [[dmt-containing-plants-united-states-halpern]] from crossing Spanish pig breeds (mid-19th century)
