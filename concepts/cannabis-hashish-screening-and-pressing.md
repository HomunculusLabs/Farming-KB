Hashish (hash) is a concentrated cannabis product made by collecting and compressing the resinous trichomes from the plant surface. Greg Green covers screening and pressing techniques in detail.

## What Is Skuff

Skuff is the collected trichome material (glandular heads and stalks) separated from the plant matter using screens. It is the raw material used to make hashish. The stalked capitate trichomes — the mushroom-shaped resin glands found on female flowers and nearby leaves — are the target of collection.

## Hashish Grades

Green explains the traditional Moroccan grading system for hashish:

- **00 (Zero Zero)**: The purest grade. Made from the finest, most potent trichome collection with excellent technique.
- **0**: Very high quality, slightly less refined than 00.
- **1**: Good quality hashish.
- **2**: Average quality.
- **3**: Lower grade hashish.

The grade depends on both the THC level of the source plant AND the quality of the collection technique. Excellent technique with low-THC plants still produces better results than poor technique with high-THC plants.

## Screening Methods

### Dry Sifting (Flat Screen Method)

The simplest approach uses flat screens of varying mesh sizes:

1. **Prepare material**: Use dried, cured trim, small buds, and sugar leaves. Fresh-frozen material also works.
2. **Screen sizes**: Use multiple screens to separate different grades:
   - 150–160 micron: Collects the finest, most potent material (premium grade)
   - 100–120 micron: Medium grade
   - 70–80 micron: Coarser material with more plant contamination
   - Working screen (larger, ~200+ micron): Removes large leaf material first
3. **Process**: Gently rub or tumble the plant material across the screen. The trichomes fall through while leaf matter stays on top.
4. **Temperature**: Cold temperatures make trichomes more brittle and easier to separate. Some growers freeze their material before screening.
5. **Work gently**: Aggressive rubbing pushes plant matter through the screen, contaminating the collection.

### Ice Water Extraction (Bubble Hash)

A water-based method that uses cold temperatures and agitation:

1. Fill a bucket with ice water (just above freezing)
2. Add plant material
3. Agitate with a mixer or by hand for 10–15 minutes
4. Pour the mixture through sequential bubble bags (screen bags of decreasing micron sizes)
5. Trichomes collect in the bags while water and plant material pass through
6. Scrape the collected material from each bag
7. Dry thoroughly before pressing

This method produces very clean, high-quality hash with minimal plant contamination.

## Pressing Skuff into Hash

Once you have collected skuff, it needs to be pressed into hashish:

### Heat Press Method
1. Preheat skuff slightly (warm to the touch, not hot)
2. Place skuff between parchment paper
3. Apply firm, even pressure with a press or clamp
4. The warmth and pressure cause the trichome heads to rupture and fuse together
5. Continue pressing until the material forms a solid mass
6. Let cool and cure for best texture and flavor

### Hand Press Method
1. Warm the skuff in your hands or in a warm location
2. Roll and compress between your palms repeatedly
3. The friction and body heat help the material bind together
4. Continue working the material until it forms a cohesive mass
5. This produces softer, more pliable hash than mechanical pressing

### Factors Affecting Quality
- **Source material quality**: The more trichome-rich the starting material, the better the hash
- **Collection temperature**: Cold separation produces cleaner results
- **Screen quality**: Consistent mesh sizes give better separation
- **Pressing technique**: Even pressure without overheating preserves terpenes and potency
- **Curing**: Like buds, hash improves with proper curing

## Equipment

- **Screens**: Stainless steel mesh screens in various micron sizes
- **Bubble bags**: Screen bags designed for ice water extraction
- **Pollinator/tumbler**: Mechanical devices that tumble plant material over screens
- **Press**: A hash press, c-clamp, or even a hair straightener for small quantities
- **Parchment paper**: For pressing and storage
- **Collection cards**: Credit cards or similar for gathering sifted material

## Storage

Store hashish in:
- Airtight glass containers
- Cool, dark location
- Refrigeration for long-term storage
- Properly cured and stored hash can remain potent for months to years
