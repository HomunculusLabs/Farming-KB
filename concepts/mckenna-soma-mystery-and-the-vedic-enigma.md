## Soma as Male Moon God

The Rig Veda connects Soma with the moon in complex ways. Soma is
described as dwelling on the mountains, being brought by eagles, and
associated with the celestial realm. McKenna explored the possibility
that the moon-god association reflected the cyclical, visionary nature
of the Soma experience — a journey to the lunar, reflective dimension
of consciousness distinct from the solar, rational mode.

## Soma and Cattle

A recurring theme in the Rig Veda is the connection between Soma and
cattle. Soma is pressed in the same manner as milk is churned, and the
language surrounding both processes shares significant vocabulary. This
connection suggests a pastoral cultural context for the original Soma
ceremonies, consistent with the nomadic, cattle-herding lifestyle of the
early Indo-European peoples.

## The Indo-European Context

The Soma/Haoma question cannot be separated from the broader question of
Indo-European origins. The similarity between Vedic Soma and Iranian
Haoma suggests a common Indo-Iranian plant sacrament that was carried by
migrating peoples from a Central Asian homeland. The identity of this
original sacramental plant would illuminate the spiritual life of the
people whose language family gave rise to most European languages.
