## Cannabis Reflux Extraction Apparatus

Reflux extraction is the foundational technique in [[comparison-essential-oils-vs-neem-oil-vs-insecticidal-soap]] of cannabis into a solvent. The reflux apparatus, as described in Ben Lemon's Cannabis Alchemy, is designed to be constructed from common household and hardware store items, making cannabis oil extraction accessible without specialized laboratory equipment. The design prioritizes safety while enabling the extraction of cannabinoids from large quantities of plant material.

## Principles of Reflux Extraction

Reflux extraction works by boiling a solvent in contact with cannabis material and continuously returning the condensed solvent vapors to the extraction vessel. As the solvent boils, cannabinoid-rich vapors rise, contact a cooled surface, recondense into liquid, and drip back down through the plant material. This continuous cycling ensures thorough extraction while preventing the loss of solvent through evaporation.

The key safety advantage of reflux is that the solvent is contained within a closed system. Unlike open-vessel evaporation, reflux prevents the release of flammable solvent fumes into the surrounding environment. The entire extraction occurs within the apparatus, with the only safety concern being the potential for pressure buildup, which is managed through proper apparatus design.

## Basic Apparatus Design

The classical reflux apparatus for [[snow-mold-fungi-cold-temperature-plant-pathogens]] causes rapid condensation of the solvent vapors. The ice must be replenished as it melts to maintain condensation efficiency.

The stainless-steel pot containing the cannabis and solvent sits in the stew pot, elevated above the bottom by the screen. The boiling water in the outer pot heats the inner pot indirectly, preventing the solvent from reaching its flash point through direct contact with the heat source.
