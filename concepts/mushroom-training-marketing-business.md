# [[mushroom-cultivation]] is ultimately a business enterprise, and the FAO training manual
emphasizes that marketing and business management skills are as important as cultivation
techniques. The manual's three-in-one training approach combines rehabilitation, mushroom
cultivation, and enterprise development into a single integrated program. This page covers
marketing strategies, sales outlets, quality control, and business startup procedures from
the perspective of farmers with disabilities.
## Why Mushrooms as a Business
Mushrooms were selected as an enterprise opportunity because of their high demand in the Thai
diet and other Asian cuisines. Key business advantages include:
- Low start-up costs with short-term returns on investment (2 to 4 months)
- Mushrooms can be produced and sold within 2 to 4 months
- Spawns and substrate bags can be bought at reasonably low cost, allowing an acceptable
  profit margin
- Spawn bags can be produced for self-use and also sold, increasing profit margins
- Mushrooms can be processed into various value-added products
- The business offers a wide range of activities suitable for people with various
  disabilities
## Sales Outlets
The manual identifies multiple channels for selling mushrooms:
- Direct from the farm: make sure people know you cultivate mushrooms
- Community sales: neighbors and local residents
- Local market: fresh produce markets in nearby towns
- Neighboring markets: expanding to markets in adjacent areas
- Restaurants: establishing regular supply agreements
- Hotels: higher-end markets that pay premium prices
- Supermarkets: for larger scale producers with consistent supply
Additionally, inoculated mushroom bags can be sold to other farmers, generating supplemental
income beyond fresh mushroom sales.
## Quality Control for Market Success
Quality directly affects price and repeat customers:
- Mushrooms must be clean; trimming is very important before sale
- Collect young mushrooms for longer shelf life and better taste
- Harvest after watering with at least 2 to 3 hours wait for surface moisture to evaporate
- Proper packaging prevents damage and extends shelf life
- Constant monitoring of competitors is necessary to ensure market share
Prices of mushrooms depend on the season and the type. Different seasons give better yields
for certain mushrooms, so farmers should plan their production calendar accordingly.
## Starting the Business
The manual provides a step-by-step procedure for launching a mushroom production farm:
1. Identify location for the mushroom house: make sure the land is above flooding level
   and offers good ventilation
2. Set up [[mushroom-training-cost-profit-analysis]] for detailed cost

- Organizations for people with disabilities

- Local government administrations
- International agencies such as FAO, ILO, and UNDP
