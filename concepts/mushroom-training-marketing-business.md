## Sales Outlets
The manual identifies multiple channels for selling mushrooms:
- Direct from the farm: make sure people know you cultivate mushrooms
- Community sales: neighbors and local residents
- Local market: fresh produce markets in nearby towns
- Neighboring markets: expanding to markets in adjacent areas
- Restaurants: establishing regular supply agreements
- Hotels: higher-end markets that pay premium prices
- Supermarkets: for larger scale producers with consistent supply
Additionally, inoculated mushroom bags can be sold to other farmers, generating supplemental
income beyond fresh mushroom sales.
## Quality Control for Market Success
Quality directly affects price and repeat customers:
- Mushrooms must be clean; trimming is very important before sale

breakdowns per bag and justification of all calculations.
