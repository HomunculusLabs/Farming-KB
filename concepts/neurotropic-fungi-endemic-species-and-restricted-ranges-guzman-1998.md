### Section Aztecorum Disjunct Distribution

Three Section Aztecorum species show striking northern distributions:

- **P. baeocystis**: Northwestern North America (Oregon, Washington, British Columbia)
- **P. quebecensis**: Quebec, Canada
- Section Aztecorum is otherwise primarily Mexican

This disjunction suggests ancient vicariance events or long-distance dispersal during glacial cycles.

## Caribbean and Island Endemics

- **Psilocybe portoricensis**: Known only from Puerto Rico
- **Panaeolus moellerianus** and **P. olivaceus**: Restricted to the Faeroe Islands

## African Species

Africa remains the most underexplored continent for neurotropic fungi, with only 6-8 species of neurotropic *Psilocybe* known:

- **Psilocybe natalensis**: Only confirmed neurotropic Psilocybe from South Africa (Gartz et al. 1995)
- **Psilocybe mairei**: Northern Africa (Morocco, Algeria)
- **Psilocybe aquamarina**: Kenya (Pegler 1977), closely related to *P. cubensis*

This paucity contrasts with Africa's high biodiversity and reflects insufficient exploration.
