# Sterile Spore Print Collection Using Glass Jar Technique

Collecting [[spore-syringe-preparation-jarrold-method]] uses a sterile glass jar technique that
produces clean, contaminant-free spore deposits.

These prints can be stored for months and used to create
[[mushroom-spore-printing-and-storage]] on paper or foil exposes spores
to open air and contamination.

The glass [[fungicide-treated-seed-contamination-risk-mushroom-substrate]].

Glass jars can be sterilised in a domestic oven and double
as both collection vessel and storage container.

The sealed jar protects spores from airborne bacteria, mould
spores, and dust during the collection period.

## Jar Sterilisation

Small clean glass jars with metal screw lids are placed on
a metal baking tray with lids beside them.

The tray goes under a domestic oven grill at half maximum
temperature for 30 minutes.

After cooling, lids are loosely placed on each jar but not
tightened.

The tray is removed from the oven and lids tightened fully
to maintain sterility during transport.

## Equipment and Preparation

Required tools: tweezers, scissors, methylated spirits
burner.

All tools are flame-sterilised immediately before use.

A protective breathing mask must be worn to prevent the
operator's breath from contaminating the work area.

The [[duggar-mushroom-growing-history-and-methods]] tank serves as the workspace.

## Cap Harvesting

Mature caps with fully opened gills are selected — the veil
should have torn naturally.

Using flame-sterilised tweezers, the operator grips the cap
top and cuts the stem with flame-sterilised scissors.

The cap is placed gills-down into the sterilised jar.

The lid is replaced immediately but left loose so moisture
can evaporate from the spores.

## Collection and Drying Periods

Jars stand undisturbed for at least 24 hours while spores
settle from the gills onto the glass bottom.

After settling, caps are removed with sterile tweezers.

Jars then stand for a further 24 hours in a clean, draught-
free area to allow spores to dry completely.

Once dry, lids are tightened firmly for storage.

Jars are labelled with species, date, and parent mushroom
characteristics, then stored cool, dark, and dry.

## Integration with Syringe Preparation

Jar-collected spore prints feed directly into the spore
syringe preparation process.
