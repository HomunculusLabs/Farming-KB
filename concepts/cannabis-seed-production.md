## Cannabis Seed Production

Seed production in cannabis is the process of deliberately pollinating
female plants to create viable seeds for future cultivation or breeding
programs. Cervantes provides detailed guidance on making seeds at both
small and commercial scales, covering parent selection, pollination
techniques, seed crop care, and [[bloomfield-fungal-life-cycle-spiral-versus-circle]], harvests bud
samples from each plant, and evaluates growth and smoking
characteristics. Post-harvest evaluation allows additional inspection
of aromas and flavors as flowers dry and cure.

Choosing males is more difficult since males do not produce female
flowers. The most reliable method is the progeny test: take pollen from
a candidate male, make seeds with chosen females, grow out the progeny,
and evaluate the male's genetic contribution (combining ability). Some
breeders rub the stem to check for pungent, resinous odor, but this is
only a crude measure.

## Step Two: Collecting Pollen

One branch of male flowers supplies sufficient pollen for small-scale
breeding. Strip away other branches to prevent accidental pollination.
Isolate the male as soon as anthers show, since airborne pollen can
travel miles. Just before anthers open, place a clean paper or plastic
bag over the branch, secure it at the bottom, and leave it for several
days. When enough pollen has collected, cut the branch and shake
remaining pollen into the bag. See also
