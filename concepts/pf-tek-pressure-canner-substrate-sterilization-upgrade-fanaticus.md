# PF-TEK Pressure Canner Substrate Sterilization Upgrade

## Overview

While the standard PF-TEK relies on steam sterilization using a common kitchen
pot, cultivators seeking higher reliability can upgrade to pressure canner
sterilization. A pressure canner achieves temperatures above the boiling point
of water (240–250°F at 10–15 PSI), providing more thorough elimination of
contaminant organisms, particularly bacterial endospores that survive standard
boiling. This upgrade is especially valuable for cultivators working in humid
environments or experiencing persistent contamination issues.

## Steam Sterilization vs. Pressure Canning

### Standard Steam Sterilization (PF Method)

The original PF-TEK uses a large kitchen pot with a tight lid to steam
sterilize substrate jars. PF recommended a sterilization time of approximately
60–90 minutes, with some practitioners extending to 2 hours for reliability.
Steam at atmospheric pressure reaches 212°F (100°C), which kills most
vegetative bacteria and fungal spores but may not eliminate resilient bacterial
endospores. The method works because the [[grass-seed-substrate-for-mushroom-cultivation]] substrates.

### Jar Preparation

Substrate jars are prepared identically to the standard PF-TEK method:

1. Mix brown rice flour, vermiculite, and water in the standard ratios.
2. Fill jars to the correct level with a dry vermiculite barrier on top.
3. Install lids with inoculation holes and filter tape (or leave holes open
   and cover with foil).
4. Place jars on a rack inside the pressure canner to prevent direct contact
   with the bottom.

### Sterilization Cycle

1. Add 1–2 inches of water to the canner bottom.
2. Load jars on the rack, ensuring they do not touch each other or the
   canner walls.
3. Seal the canner lid and bring to pressure over medium-high heat.
4. Once the target pressure (15 PSI) is reached, begin timing the cycle.
5. Maintain pressure for 30–45 minutes (45 minutes recommended for half-pint
   jars).
6. Remove from heat and allow pressure to drop naturally to zero — do not
   force-cool or open the vent.
7. Wait an additional 10–15 minutes after pressure equalization before
   opening the lid.
