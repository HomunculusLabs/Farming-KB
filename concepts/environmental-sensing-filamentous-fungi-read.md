flexibility related to its pathogenic lifestyle.

GPCRs regulate second messengers through heterotrimeric G-proteins
consisting of G-alpha, G-beta and G-gamma subunits. Neurospora has three
G-alpha subunits (GNA-1, GNA-2, GNA-3), one G-beta and one G-gamma.
Ligand binding causes GDP-GTP exchange on G-alpha, dissociating the
heterotrimer into active units that interact with downstream effectors.
G-protein signalling in Neurospora regulates hyphal growth, conidiation,
female fertility and stress responses.
