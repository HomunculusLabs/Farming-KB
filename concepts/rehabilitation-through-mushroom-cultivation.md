## Rehabilitation Through Mushroom Cultivation

The FAO [[grass-seed-substrate-for-mushroom-cultivation]] by people with
disabilities introduces a "Three-in-One" training approach that combines
rehabilitation, mushroom cultivation skills, and [[motivational-sessions-trainees]] teach trainees about
  positive thinking, problem-solving, and setting their own limits rather
  than letting other people determine what they can and cannot do.

## Rehabilitation of the Spirit

The second pillar addresses emotional and social acceptance:

- **Self-acceptance** - Trainees must learn to accept who they are, how
  they are, and their differences from others.
- **Understanding others** - They must learn about other people and
  their disabilities, building empathy and mutual support within the
  training group.
- **Community belonging** - Sessions are designed to make trainees feel
  like they are part of a large family, reducing isolation and fostering
  social bonds.
- **Learning to love** - The curriculum includes activities focused on
  love, family relations, anger management, and interpersonal skills.

## Rehabilitation of the Body

The third pillar develops physical capabilities and task mastery:

- **Learning new tasks** - Trainees must learn to do things, to do things
  differently, to control their body, and to accomplish new tasks.
- **Alternate methods** - People with disabilities can accomplish
  everything needed [[query-how-to-make-a-diy-still-air-box-for-mushroom-cultivation]] by using different methods
  and developing personal ways of performing various tasks. For example,
  a person unable to use two hands can use their feet to make substrate
  bags.
- **Physical strengthening** - Several trainees in the program actually
  became physically stronger through regular work activity.
- **Individual capability assessment** - Trainers evaluate capacities
  including use of hands (or artificial limbs), free movement (with legs
  or wheelchair/tricycle), vision, hearing, speech, basic understanding,
  and decision-making ability.

## The Training Approach

Training is conducted as a hands-on experience with basic theory for
better understanding. Trainers closely monitor procedures and actions to
help trainees develop ease in accomplishing all required tasks. Not all
trainees will be capable of fully understanding each step, and many will
prefer to focus on cultivation and selling rather than complex tasks like
spawning and inoculation. The program accommodates these differences.

For most trainees, the objective is to cultivate mushrooms and sell them
on the fresh market to generate income. This means buying inoculated bags
ready for fruiting, which can be sourced from nearby mushroom farms or
the training center. Some trainees will want to make their own bags and
sell [[mushroom-cultivation-disabilities-substrate-bag-production]] for higher profit.
