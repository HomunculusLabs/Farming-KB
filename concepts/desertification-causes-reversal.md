# [[sepp-holzer]] argues that what are commonly called "natural disasters" such as
floods, droughts, desertification, and fires are not natural at all but the
logical consequences of erroneous human behaviour over generations. He has
observed and documented desertification processes across multiple continents
and developed practical methods for reversing them.

## Understanding Desertification as a Process

Desertification does not happen overnight. It follows a predictable sequence of
degradation that Holzer has witnessed in countries [[fungi-around-the-world-species-distribution]]:

1. Original vegetation (mixed forests, diverse grasslands) is removed through
   deforestation, overgrazing, or conversion to monoculture
2. The [[savory-bare-ground-and-soil-exposure]] is exposed to direct sunlight and hardens like an oven
3. Where the forest once stored water like a sponge, dry heat builds up
4. Dew and humidity disappear; the ground begins to radiate heat into the air
5. Changing thermal conditions create new air currents, leading to storms and
   unpredictable weather patterns
6. Remaining moisture is stripped away by unobstructed winds
7. Nutrients wash out in rainy seasons, followed by erosion as topsoil
   is lost
8. The body of the earth can no longer hold or store water
9. The area becomes desert

## Human Causes of Desertification

Holzer identifies several primary human activities that drive desertification:

**Overgrazing.** Intensive animal husbandry with cattle, sheep, and goats
destroys vegetation faster than it can regenerate. This is visible across
Spain, Portugal, North Africa, and the Middle East. The animals eat most of
the vegetation, preventing natural reseeding, and their hooves compact the
soil.

**Deforestation and monoculture forestry.** Replacing mixed forests with
monoculture plantations of eucalyptus, pine, or spruce eliminates the
diverse root systems that held water and nutrients in the soil. The 2010
Russian forest fires burned an area twice the size of Austria, directly
caused by a century of wrong [[mollison-pdc-forest-management-products]].

**Land consolidation and drainage.** Making fields machinery-friendly by
removing hedges, ponds, lakes, and wetlands eliminates the humid habitats
that maintained the hydrological-balance. Roads and railways with
immense drainage systems take water away from the soil.
