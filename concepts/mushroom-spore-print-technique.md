# Mushroom Spore Print Collection Technique

## Overview

## Jar Sterilization

Small clean glass jars with metal screw lids are sterilized
before use:
1. Remove lids and place jars and lids on a metal baking tray
2. Place under the grill/broiler of an oven
3. Sterilize at half maximum heat for 30 minutes
4. Allow jars to cool in the grill
5. Place lids loosely on each jar
6. Remove tray from grill and tighten lids

Tight lids are loosened just before use to allow moisture to
evaporate during the [[flammability-hazard-alcohol-lysol-flame-sterilization-mycology]]
- Protective breathing mask (to prevent breath contamination)

## Spore Print Procedure

### Step 1: Preparation
Wear a protective breathing mask covering nose and mouth.
This prevents the cultivator's breath from contaminating the
spore print. Take all equipment to the [[mushroom-humidity-chamber-design-operation]] where
the mushrooms are growing.

### Step 2: Cap Removal
Flame-sterilize tweezers and scissors. Gently insert tweezers
into the top of a mature, spore-loaded cap. Cut the cap from
the stem using the scissors.

### Step 3: Placement
Carefully place the cap gills-down into the sterile jar.
Immediately replace the lid loosely — do not tighten, as
moisture on the spores needs to evaporate naturally.

### Step 4: Spore Release
Leave the jar undisturbed for at least 24 hours. During this
time, spores will drop from the gills onto the bottom of the
jar, creating a visible spore print pattern.

### Step 5: Cap Removal and Drying
After 24 hours, use flame-sterilized tweezers to remove the
cap from the jar. Place jars (without caps) in a clean, draft-
free area for an additional 24 hours to allow spores to fully
dry.

### Step 6: Storage
Once spores are completely dry, tighten the jar lids firmly.
Spore prints can be stored at room temperature in a cool, dark
place. Properly dried and sealed spore prints remain viable for
months to years.

## Spore Syringe Preparation from Prints

To convert stored spore prints into inoculum:

1. Sterilize 80 mL of water in a Pyrex jug (15 psi, 20 min)
2. Sterilize syringe and needle alongside the water
