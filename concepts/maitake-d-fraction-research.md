### D-Fraction
The Maitake D-fraction and crude Maitake powder have demonstrated remarkable inhibition of metastasis in immuno-competent mouse models, especially in prevention of **hepatic metastases** -- reduced by 81% (Maitake powder) to 91% (D-fraction) in experimental studies. This level of metastasis inhibition is among the highest reported for any [[mushroom-polysaccharide-chemistry]] preparation.

## Direct Cytotoxic Effects

Unlike many [[clinical-trials-of-mushroom-polysaccharides-in-cancer-treatment]] that act purely through [[blesching-cannabis-oxidative-stress-and-antioxidant-defense]] (elevated lipid peroxidation, oxidative membrane damage)
- **Vitamin C acts synergistically** with Grifron-D -- combinations as low as 30-60 micrograms/ml GD with 200 microM vitamin C were as effective as GD alone at 480 micrograms/ml
- **Carmustine/GD combination** showed 90% reduction in cell viability, suggesting chemosensitising potential
- GD may potentiate anticancer drugs through inactivation of **glyoxalase I**, a vital detoxifying enzyme

The dual mechanism of both immune activation AND direct cytotoxicity makes Maitake unique among [[lentinan-pre-clinical-antitumour-models]]) operate primarily through [[cancer-immune-modulation-mechanisms]] rather than direct tumour cell killing.

## Clinical Trials

### Japanese Non-Randomised Study (Nanba, 1997)
A study of 165 advanced cancer patients receiving oral D-fraction plus crude Maitake tablets, or D-fraction plus placebo, in addition to chemotherapy:
- **11 out of 15 advanced hepatocellular carcinomas** showed tumour regression or significant symptomatic improvement with D-fraction plus Maitake
- When D-fraction plus Maitake was combined with chemotherapy, overall response rates increased by **12-28%** across all cancer types
- Patients reportedly recovered from severe chemotherapy side-effects when D-fraction was given

### Chinese Pilot Studies
Early studies involving 63 cancer patients reported response rates (partial and complete) of:
- **95% for solid tumours**
- **90% for leukaemia** (type not specified)

These response rates are remarkably high and warrant further controlled investigation.

### FDA Approval
The US FDA has approved Grifron-D for trial under an Investigational New Drug Application (IND) for patients with advanced cancer. The FDA has also exempted Maitake polysaccharides from Phase I study because of limited side-effects. Clinical trials are underway in breast, prostate, lung, liver, and gastric cancers in the US and Japan.

Grifolan's cytokine induction profile (IL-1beta, IL-6, TNF-alpha) activates the immune cascade at the macrophage level, which then communicates with T-cells and NK cells. The T-cell dependence of antitumour activity has been confirmed through experiments with cyclosporin A.

## Cardiovascular and Metabolic Effects

Beyond cancer, Maitake has demonstrated beneficial effects in other areas:
- **Cholesterol reduction**: Listed among six species that reduce total cholesterol levels
- **Blood pressure**: One of three species shown to reduce arterial blood pressure
- **Blood sugar**: Active in non-insulin-dependent diabetes; can diminish glycemia, insulemia, and blood triglyceride levels
- **Blood platelet binding**: Reduced platelet aggregation (in vitro)

## Safety Profile

Grifron-D has few side effects. Anecdotal clinical reports suggest it may alleviate some side-effects of chemotherapy. The apparent success of crude Maitake powder by oral administration supports its suitability as a nutriceutical. The FDA's exemption from Phase I studies reflects the safety profile.
