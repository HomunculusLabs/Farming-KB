# DMT Phenomenology and the Hyperspace Experience

## Overview

Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) is a powerful endogenous [[mckenna-tryptamine-hallucinogens-and-consciousness]] and Consciousness" provides one of the most
detailed first-person phenomenological accounts of the DMT experience,
describing it as a visit to a radically alien dimension that exists "one

## Pharmacological Profile

DMT (N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is distinguished from other classical
psychedelics by several pharmacological properties:

- **Onset**: Extremely rapid — effects begin within 15-45 seconds when
  smoked
- **Duration**: Very brief — the full experience lasts approximately
  5-15 minutes
- **Intensity**: Among the most intense of all psychedelic experiences
- **Endogenous status**: Present in small amounts in the human brain,
  suggesting a natural neurochemical role
- **[[mckenna-dmt-onset-phenomenology-ripping-cellophane]], like someone
   crumpling up plastic film and throwing it away" — described by a
   friend as "our radio entelechy ripping out of the organic matrix"
2. **Ascending tone**: A rising pitch accompanies the transition
3. **Geometric visuals**: The normal hallucinogenic modality — shifting
   geometric surfaces of migrating, changing coloured forms
4. **Synaptic saturation**: "All available bond sites are being occupied"
5. **Mode shift**: Complete transition occurring over approximately
   thirty seconds

### The Hyperspace Environment

After the transition, one arrives in what McKenna calls a space that:

- Has a feeling of being "underground" or "somehow insulated and domed"
- Is reminiscent of the "merry go raum" (from German *raum*, "space")
  described in Joyce's *Finnegans Wake* — "The room is actually going
  around"
- Produces a feeling of being "like a child, though one has come out
  somewhere in eternity"
- Invokes Heraclitus's 24th fragment: "The Aeon is a child at play
  with colored balls"

### Entity Encounters

The hallmark of the DMT experience is contact with entities that McKenna
describes as:

- **Self-transforming machine elves**: Dynamically contorting topological
  modules distinct from the continuously transforming background
- **Fractal reflections** of a "previously hidden and suddenly autonomous
  part of one's own psyche"
- **Hyperdimensional beings** that "bathe one in love" — "not erotic but
  open-hearted"
- Speaking entities that say "Don't be alarmed. Remember, and do what
  we are doing"
