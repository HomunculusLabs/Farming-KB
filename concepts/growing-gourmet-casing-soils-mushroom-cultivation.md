---# Casing Soils for Mushroom Cultivation

## Overview and Purpose

Button mushroom growers long ago discovered that by placing a layer of
peat moss over compost grown through with mushroom mycelium, yields were
greatly enhanced. The casing serves several critical functions:

1. **Moisture Bank:** The casing layer acts as a water reserve that can
   be replenished through the course of each crop.
2. **Humidity Buffer:** It limits damage to the mycelium from
   fluctuations in relative humidity.
3. **Microbial Stimulus:** The casing provides stimulatory
   micro-organisms, essential salts and minerals.
4. **Primordia Environment:** These combined properties make casing a
   perfect environment for the formation and development of primordia.
