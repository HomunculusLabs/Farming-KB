# JADAM Insect Pest Management

JADAM organizes insect [[ultra-low-jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] for wetting power, these control virtually all crop pests.

## Sucking Insects

### Aphids

**Standard program (Program 6, per 500L):**
- JWA: 5L (1.3 gal)
- [[cho-plant-growth-stage-treatment-protocols]] when new sprouts appear

**Stronger:** Increase both JWA and JHS to 10L each. Add 0.5 kg fine red clay powder.

**Replacements:** Gingko, KPFR, or pokeweed can replace Jerusalem artichoke.

### Mites (Two-Spotted Spider Mite)

**Standard program (Program 6):**
- JWA: 5L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L per 500L

**Key facts:**
- Mites thrive in hot, dry conditions (especially greenhouses)
- They spin fine webbing on leaf undersides
- JS 1-2L added to the program provides additional miticidal effect
- Mites develop resistance to synthetics rapidly; JADAM's multi-component approach avoids this
- [[ultra-low-cost-jadam-microorganism-solution-and-soil-foundation]] soil application -- increase microbial diversity and population to crowd out nematodes. Not controlled by direct pesticide application.

### Soil Foundation (Program 14)

- JMS 500L for up to 3.3 ha
- Sea salt 0.5 kg per 0.1 ha
- Phyllite solution 20L per 0.1 ha
- Wild grass JLF 20L per 0.1 ha
- Controls: root knot nematodes, verticillium wilt, virus diseases, phytophthora blight, canker

## General Application Principles

- Spray thoroughly to fully wet the target -- incomplete coverage = poor control
- Apply in morning/evening or on humid days
- For heavy infestation: spray evening + re-spray next morning
- When JS is not used, you can spray consecutively (even twice daily)
- Fine-particle nozzles give best results with less volume
- Always use soft water (test with JWA)
- Always do mixture test and concentration test before full application
