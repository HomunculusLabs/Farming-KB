## Water Power on the Krameterhof

The Krameterhof spans 1,100-1,500m elevation, providing approximately 100m (10 bars) of height difference. Holzer exploits this for multiple energy and water supply systems without external energy inputs. Every system serves multiple functions simultaneously -- this cascading use principle is central to Holzer's energy philosophy.

### Pelton Wheel Generators

Two small generators built from old Pelton wheels:

- Fed from ponds through a penstock pipe
- 100m height difference generates enough electricity for the entire farm
- Water exits the generators cool and oxygen-rich
- This oxygenated water flows into lower, warmer fish ponds
- Allows keeping trout (a cold-water species) in ponds that would otherwise be too warm
- Simple, mechanical, with very few moving parts -- extremely reliable and maintainable

### Hydraulic Ram

A hydraulic ram pump operates without any external energy source:

- Powered by water flowing from the ponds
- Uses pressure differences to pump drinking water to the mountain pasture
- Provides up to 15 bars of pressure
- Flow rate ratio: 1:10 (10 litres of drive water pump 1 litre of drinking water)
- A membrane inside pulses constantly as drive water stretches it
- Delivery pipe carries pumped water to the high-altitude pasture
- No maintenance beyond occasional membrane replacement

### Water Wheels

Two traditional water wheels drive mills for grain processing. These are simple, reliable, and have been used for centuries. They can be built from locally available timber and require minimal maintenance.

## Earth-Sheltered Construction

While not energy generation per se, Holzer's building methods dramatically reduce energy needs:

### Roundwood Shelters
- Built into slopes using excavated earth as insulation
- Soil temperature provides stable 8-10C year-round
- No heating required for livestock shelters
- Roof covered with earth and vegetation provides thermal mass
- Construction cost: 700-800 euros for a 3x6x2m shelter
- Build time: one day with excavator and one additional person

### Stone Cellars
- Earth provides passive temperature regulation (8-10C, 80-90% humidity)
- Ideal for storing fruit and vegetables without refrigeration
- No heating or cooling energy required
- 10m underground air inlet pipe pre-conditions incoming air
- Air outlet at highest point prevents condensation
- Gravel floor provides drainage

The energy systems on the Krameterhof demonstrate that a modern farm can operate entirely without fossil fuel inputs. The key is to work with natural forces (gravity, water flow, thermal mass) rather than against them.
