# [[water-management]] and [[tompkins-electroculture-plant-growth]] is like building a house -- you cannot build with just
cement (N). You need diverse materials. Fish JLF provides nitrogen plus diverse
micronutrients. Use [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful
- Phyllite powder: optional (reduces smell)

### Method
1. Fill half the container with mackerel
2. Fill up with water, add [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Put fish meal in container, add water 10x weight
2. Add leaf mold
3. Solid sinks -- stir every 7-10 days for first 3 months
4. After 3 months, stir once a month
5. Longer aging = higher nitrogen content
6. Can heat to speed process
7. Time: **3+ months**

### Analysis (per 100 L: fish meal 10 kg + leaf mold)

## Recipe 3: Fresh Anchovy JLF (fastest decomposition)

### Ingredients
- Fresh anchovies: fill **half** the container
- Water: fill to top
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Fill half container, add water and leaf mold
2. No need to stir
3. If oil layer forms on top, add more water and leaf mold
4. Extended fermentation dissolves even bones -- enriches with Ca and P
5. Dilute x1,000 in early growth
6. Time: **3+ months**

### Analysis (per 100 L: fish by-product 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 7.7 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 99.40 |
| T-N % | 7.44 |
| P2O5 % | 0.127 |
| CaO % | 0.035 |
