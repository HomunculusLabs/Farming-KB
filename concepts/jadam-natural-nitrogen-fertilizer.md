# [[jadam-natural-pesticide-recipes]] Nitrogen Liquid Fertilizer

Protein is broken down into amino acids by microorganisms. These amino acids are
a source of nitrogen for plants. Any nitrogen-rich ingredient from nature can be
fermented with [[water-management]] and [[leaf-mold-composting]] to produce nitrogen JLF. Fish JLF has
approximately 1/5 the nitrogen of urea fertilizer, but is much higher quality
because it contains a wide variety of other nutrients.

## Philosophy

Building [[tompkins-electroculture-plant-growth]] is like building a house -- you cannot build with just
cement (N). You need diverse materials. Fish JLF provides nitrogen plus diverse
micronutrients. Use [[ultra-low-cost-wild-grass-cover-cropping-and-symbiotic-weed-management]] and fruit JLF as the main fertilizer; add fish
JLF when growth needs a boost.

## Recipe 1: Mackerel JLF (highest N content)

### Ingredients
- Mackerel: fill **half** the container
- Water: fill to top
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful
- Phyllite powder: optional (reduces smell)

### Method
1. Fill half the container with mackerel
2. Fill up with water, add [[ultra-low-leaf-mold-as-ideal-soil-model]]
3. No need to stir
4. If too much mackerel, decomposition slows -- ensure enough water
5. Add phyllite to reduce smell
6. Time: **3+ months**
7. Dilute x1,000+ in early growth stage (very concentrated)

### Analysis (per 100 L: mackerel + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 6.8 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 135.15 |
| T-N % | 10.98 |
| P2O5 % | 0.332 |
| K2O % | 0.729 |

## Recipe 2: Fish Meal JLF (convenient dried form)

### Ingredients
- Fish meal (dried powder): 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Put fish meal in container, add water 10x weight
2. Add leaf mold
3. Solid sinks -- stir every 7-10 days for first 3 months
4. After 3 months, stir once a month
5. Longer aging = higher nitrogen content
6. Can heat to speed process
7. Time: **3+ months**

### Analysis (per 100 L: fish meal 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 6.1 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 8.16 |
| T-N % | 4.69 |
| P2O5 % | 0.002 |
| K2O % | 0.174 |

## Recipe 3: Fresh Anchovy JLF (fastest decomposition)

### Ingredients
- Fresh anchovies: fill **half** the container
- Water: fill to top
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Fill half container with anchovies, fill with water, add leaf mold
2. No need to stir
3. If too much fish, water becomes too thick -- slows microbial activity
4. Superior nutritional balance (entire fish body used)
5. Time: **1+ month** (fastest decomposing)

### Analysis (per 100 L: anchovy 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 9.2 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 69.60 |
| T-N % | 5.08 |
| P2O5 % | 0.107 |
| K2O % | 0.194 |

## Recipe 4: Fish By-Product JLF (bones dissolve over time)

### Ingredients
- Fish by-product (heads, bones, guts): fill **half** the container
- Water: fill to top
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Fill half container, add water and leaf mold
2. No need to stir
3. If oil layer forms on top, add more water and leaf mold
4. Extended fermentation dissolves even bones -- enriches with Ca and P
5. Dilute x1,000 in early growth
6. Time: **3+ months**

### Analysis (per 100 L: fish by-product 10 kg + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 7.7 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 99.40 |
| T-N % | 7.44 |
| P2O5 % | 0.127 |
| CaO % | 0.035 |

## Recipe 5: Perilla Pulp JLF (press cake method)

### Ingredients
- Perilla pulp: 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- [[leaf-mold-composting]]: a handful

### Method
1. Put perilla pulp in container, add water 10x weight
2. Add leaf mold, stir every 7-10 days
3. Husks take several years to break down completely
4. Rice bran, chicken manure, pig manure, all press cakes work the same way
5. Rice bran JLF is especially great for rice
6. Time: **3+ months**
