### Agaricus Blazei NK Mechanism

Fujimiya et al. (1999) demonstrated that the anti-tumor effect of *Agaricus blazei* in murine models was mediated primarily through NK cell activation. When NK cells were depleted using anti-asialo GM1 antibodies, the anti-tumor effect was significantly reduced, confirming the central role of NK cells in the observed response. The active proteoglucan fraction was shown to stimulate NK cell production of interferon-gamma and TNF-alpha, creating a potent anti-tumor immune microenvironment.

## Complement System Activation

Several mushroom polysaccharides activate the complement system through the alternative pathway, generating anaphylatoxins (C3a, C5a) that:

- Recruit and activate additional immune cells to the tumor microenvironment
- Increase vascular permeability, enhancing immune cell infiltration into tumors
- Opsonize tumor cells for phagocytosis by macrophages
