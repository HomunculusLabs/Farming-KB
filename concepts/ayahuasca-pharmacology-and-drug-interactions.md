## Ayahuasca Pharmacology and Drug Interactions

Ayahuasca is a psychoactive brew whose pharmacological complexity
creates significant risk of dangerous drug interactions, particularly

they inhibit MAO-A in the gastrointestinal tract and liver, preventing
the enzymatic breakdown of DMT and allowing it to reach the
bloodstream and cross the blood-brain barrier when taken orally.
Without this MAOI action, orally ingested DMT would be rapidly
metabolized and produce no psychoactive effects. Second, the harmala
alkaloids contribute their own psychoactive effects including
sedation, mild visual distortion, and introspective qualities.

### DMT (N,N-Dimethyltryptamine)

Psychotria viridis (or occasionally Diplopterys cabrerana) provides
DMT, a potent serotonergic psychedelic compound structurally related
to serotonin and psilocin. DMT acts primarily as a partial agonist at
5-HT2A receptors, producing intense visionary experiences, altered
perception of time and space, and profound changes in consciousness.
DMT is also an endogenous compound found naturally in mammalian
brains, though its physiological role remains debated.

## Mechanism of Dangerous Drug Interactions

The MAOI component of ayahuasca is the primary source of drug
interaction risk. MAOIs affect the metabolism of several classes of
neurotransmitters and pharmaceuticals, creating potentially dangerous
synergistic effects when combined with certain medications.

### Serotonin Syndrome Risk

The most dangerous interaction occurs between ayahuasca's MAOIs and
serotonergic drugs. When MAO-A is inhibited, serotonin breakdown is
impaired. If a person is also taking a drug that increases serotonin
levels (such as an SSRI antidepressant), the resulting serotonin
accumulation can trigger serotonin syndrome—a potentially
life-threatening condition. Symptoms include:

- **Mild**: Tremor, sweating, dilated pupils, diarrhea, agitation
- **Moderate**: Confusion, rapid heart rate (tachycardia), high blood
  pressure, muscle rigidity, hyperreflexia
- **Severe**: Hyperthermia (body temperature above 40°C), seizures,
  irregular heartbeat, delirium, coma, and potentially death

This interaction is well-documented in pharmacological literature and
represents the single most dangerous risk for ayahuasca users taking
prescription medications.

### Sympathomimetic Interactions

MAOIs impair the breakdown of catecholamines including norepinephrine
and dopamine. Combining ayahuasca with sympathomimetic drugs
(stimulants, decongestants, certain asthma medications) can cause
dangerous hypertensive crises—sudden severe increases in blood
pressure that may lead to stroke or cardiac events.
