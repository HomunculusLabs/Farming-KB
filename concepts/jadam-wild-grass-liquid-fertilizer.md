# JADAM Wild Grass Liquid Fertilizer

## Overview

Wild grass [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer]] (JLF) is the backbone of JADAM's zero-cost fertilization system. The principle is simple: "Crops are plants. Use plants as feed." Instead of buying expensive organic fertilizers, farmers use the wild grass and crop residues already available on or near their farms. This is described as the "most optimized fertilizer" because plants naturally contain the balanced nutrition that other plants need.

## The Fundamental Principle

An egg is single-celled. If kept warm for a certain period, cell division occurs and a perfect form of life is born. An egg has all the nutrients needed to create life -- it is called the "perfect food." Similarly, a fruit contains all the nutrients needed by a seed to sprout and grow into a perfect form of life. Just as an egg is the child of a chicken, a fruit is the child of a crop. So fruit is equivalent to the egg -- do not turn them into waste.

## Basic Wild Grass JLF

**Recipe**: Fill the container with wild grass, fill up with water, add a handful of leaf mold, close lid.

- Dilution: 20-300 times
- When to apply: Throughout growth
- How to apply: Soil or foliar application
- What to expect: Balanced growth
- How long to make: More than 10 days

Wild grass contains 1.5 to 2.5% nitrogen (N) for its dried weight. Grass collected in spring has higher nitrogen content. Crop residues can also be added.

## Purslane JLF

Purslane is one of the fastest-disintegrating wild grasses. It has a high nitrogen content compared to other wild grasses, and the tissue is soft, so decomposition is almost complete in 10 days. It is an effective fruit color enhancer. Purslane is a very common plant growing anywhere, yet makes excellent fertilizer.

## Customized JLF: Same-Fruit Fertilizer

JADAM introduces a radical concept: to grow tomatoes, make fertilizer from tomatoes. To grow strawberries, use strawberry liquid fertilizer. For melons, use melon liquid fertilizer.

**The nutritional perspective**: That particular fruit has the best nutritional composition for that fruit. Left-over, unsold, diseased, ugly, or malformed fruits become precious fertilizer for next year's farming.

### Strawberry JLF
- Gather unsold, left-over, diseased, ugly, malformed strawberries
- Fill half the container with water, add a handful of leaf mold
- Put in strawberry residues whenever produced
- How long to make: More than 1 month
- Strawberry has what strawberry needs

### Cherry Tomato JLF
- Same process with cherry tomato residues
- The peel is slow in disintegrating; tomato leaves or branches can be added
- How long to make: More than 10 days

## Filter System

JADAM provides practical filter designs for liquid fertilizer:

**For 500 L containers**: Prepare a plastic box, put it in a fine net bag, fix the net tight with a thick rubber, cut the top for opening, put bricks to sink the box, and use a rope on the side to control sinking depth so the box does not entirely submerge.

**For 2-10 T containers**: Drill a hole on the side of a plastic box, place a pipe so the opening is in the middle (connected to an electric motor), tie nylon string above the open side to prevent collapse, put the box in a fine net bag, and connect the motor.

## Nutrient Analysis Data

JADAM provides laboratory analysis data for various JLF types, showing measurable nutrient content across pH, EC, organic matter, total carbon, total nitrogen, carbon-nitrogen ratio, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium, sodium, iron, manganese, zinc, copper, and heavy metals (cadmium, chromium, nickel, lead, arsenic -- all below detection limits).

## Why This Is Ultra-Low-Cost

The entire system costs nothing:
- Wild grass is free and abundant
- Crop residues are a byproduct of farming
- Water is free
- Leaf mold is collected from nearby mountains
- No special equipment needed beyond basic containers

This eliminates the need for purchased organic fertilizers (about 20 dollars per sack for certified organic products), making the system genuinely ultra-low-cost while providing comprehensive, balanced plant nutrition.
