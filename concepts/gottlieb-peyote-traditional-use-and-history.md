## Tarahumara Indian Uses

### Endurance Running

The Tarahumara Indians consume small amounts of peyote to combat hunger, thirst and
exhaustion, especially while hunting. They have been known to run for days after a
deer with no food, water or rest.

### Folkloric Medicine

Peyote has many uses in Tarahumara folkloric medicine including treatment of:

- Arthritis
- Consumption (tuberculosis)
- Influenza
- Intestinal disorders
- Diabetes
- Snake bites
- Scorpion bites
- Datura poisoning
