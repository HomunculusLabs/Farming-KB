## The Delinquent Landscape

Mollison critiques the Western pattern:
- Suburban plots under lawns and cosmetic flowers
- Urban blight around cities
- More land cleared at the frontier
- Desperate misuse of land in between

"We risk the double jeopardy of sterile city and delinquent landscape, a fatal combination, where city, forest and farm are all neglected and lack even the basic resources for self-sufficiency."

Nearby lands are often a result of the delinquent use of the plough, heavy machinery, and clean tillage practices that destroy soil structure. "This system is not, in any future terms, sustainable."

## The Strategy

"Planning for high labour-intensive food production at the doorstep is the only way out of future crises."

- Vegetables can largely supplant monoculture grains for human food
- Tree crops can largely supplant grains for animal forage
- The energy savings of both strategies are obvious and necessary
- Grain only if grown in small plots on permanent beds, using no-tillage methods

### Specific Recommendations
- 3-4 hens plus a pig and 5-6 large wattles per 1000 m2, with many fruit trees and some field crop
- One main crop that needs little attention after establishment (potatoes, corn, pumpkin)
- Permanent grain plots kept small — 750-1000 m2 is ideal for the home garden
- Use [[permaculture-zones-in-practice]] — zone-based design
