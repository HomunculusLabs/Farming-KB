# 2C-T-8 — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-cyclopropylmethylthiophenethylamine

## Overview

2C-T-8 is a member of the 2C-T family of [[pihkal-beta-substituted-phenethylamines]],
featuring a cyclopropylmethylthio group at the 4-position of the
2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine core. It was synthesized and evaluated by
[[alexander-shulgin]] as part of his systematic exploration of sulfur-containing
phenethylamine analogues. The compound demonstrated significant psychoactivity
at doses of 30–50 mg with a long duration of 10–15 hours, though reports were
highly variable — ranging from profoundly positive introspective experiences
to severely uncomfortable adverse reactions lasting over a week. This
unpredictability led Shulgin to conclude the cyclopropylmethyl substitution
was not compelling for the 2C-T series.

## Chemical Classification

2C-T-8 belongs to the 2C-T series of 2,5-dimethoxy-4-alkylthiophenethylamines.
The defining substitution places methoxy groups at the 2- and 5-positions of
the benzene ring, with a sulfur atom at the 4-position bearing a
cyclopropylmethyl group (-S-CH2-cyclopropyl). The cyclopropylmethyl moiety is
a strained three-membered ring attached to the sulfur via a methylene bridge.
The compound is a phenethylamine (two-carbon chain), distinguishing it from the
ALEPH series (three-carbon amphetamine homologues). Molecular formula:
C14H21NO2S. The cyclopropane ring introduces approximately 27 kcal/mol of ring
strain, influencing both conformational preferences and metabolic interactions.

## Synthesis

The synthesis follows the standard 2C-T route. 2,5-Dimethoxythiophenol is
alkylated with cyclopropylmethyl bromide in potassium hydroxide/methanol,
producing the thioether intermediate, which is extracted, washed, and
vacuum-distilled. Vilsmeier-Haack formylation (POCl3/N-methylformanilide)
introduces the aldehyde at the 4-position, yielding
2,5-dimethoxy-4-(cyclopropylmethylthio)benzaldehyde as white crystals
confirmed by proton NMR. Condensation with nitromethane and ammonium acetate
produces the beta-nitrostyrene as orange-yellow crystals. Final reduction with
LiAlH4/THF (H2SO4-moderated) gives the free base, which is distilled and
converted to the hydrochloride salt as white crystals. The ALEPH-8 amphetamine
counterpart was partially synthesized from the benzaldehyde intermediate but
not pursued.

## Dosage and Duration

Active dosage: 30–50 mg. Duration: 10–15 hours. Onset occurs within 45
minutes to 1.5 hours, with peak effects at 2–3 hours. The long duration
places it among the longer-acting 2C-T compounds, often extending into the
night and interfering with sleep.

## Qualitative Effects

At 30 mg: smooth onset, pleasant body warmth, good manual dexterity, easy
creative capacity. Described as non-overstimulating with mild sensual quality.
Subject was willing to try a higher dose.

At 40 mg (positive report): extraordinary experience beginning at 1.5 hours
with tremendous insight, [[metzner-empathogen-discovery-mdma-psychotherapy-and-emotional-opening]], and profound internal journey. A
heavy, dense feeling dissolved, leading to "one of the most remarkable
experiences I have ever had" involving extensive self-review of personal
relationships. Subject concluded they were "the better for all of this."

At 40 mg (adverse report): one of the worst experiences in the PiHKAL series.
Severe sinus pain, head "split in two," sensation of existing in two universes
simultaneously. Hypersensitivity to light, noise, and motion. Nausea, severe
vertigo (intolerable with eyes closed), boundary dissolution, visual-spatial
distortions with eyes working independently. Medical intervention required
(Tranxene). Buzzing, balance problems, and disorientation persisted three
days, still faintly present after a week.

At 43 mg: enjoyable experience with good humor, strong grounding, and creative
energy reminiscent of 2C-T-2. Subject slept thoroughly and awoke refreshed.

At 50 mg: negative, self-doubting, and paranoid. No constructive insights.
Subject was relieved to sleep at twelve hours with no next-day deficit.

## Pharmacological Notes

2C-T-8 is a potent 5-HT2A receptor agonist consistent with its hallucinogenic
classification. Onset is slow (1–2 hours). Body load is variable and
significant. Visual effects range from subtle color enhancement to dramatic
spatial distortions (vertical field splitting). Cognitive effects include deep
introspection and enhanced creativity at moderate doses, escalating to identity
fragmentation and boundary dissolution at higher doses. The 10–15 hour
duration suggests slow metabolic clearance, likely due to cyclopropylmethyl
stability against oxidative metabolism. Dramatic inter-individual variability
at identical doses suggests significant pharmacogenetic factors, possibly
involving metabolic enzyme polymorphisms or receptor isoform differences.

## Structure-Activity Relationships

The cyclopropylmethylthio group is the distinguishing feature. The strained
three-membered ring constrains bond angles to ~60° (vs. 109.5° tetrahedral),
creating unusual orbital overlap and partial double-bond character. While the
cyclopropylmethyl group proved effective on [[mescaline]]'s oxygen atom, it was
"less appealing on the 2C-T-X sulfur atom." Compared to straight-chain
2C-T compounds (2C-T-2 ethylthio, 2C-T-7 heptylthio), the cyclopropylmethyl
group provides similar steric bulk but with fundamentally different
conformational freedom. The mixed results and abandoned ALEPH-8 development
suggest this substitution pattern does not reliably produce desirable profiles.

## Extensions and Commentary

Shulgin observed that "there are as many negatives as there are positives,"
and the substitution pattern is "not one to set the world on fire." The
ALEPH-8 amphetamine homologue was started (benzaldehyde + nitroethane) but not
pursued. Shulgin reflected that while cyclopropylmethyl was "quite something
on the mescaline oxygen atom," it generates "even less enthusiasm" for the
ALEPH series. He concluded: "That's the way it is, and who could have guessed!"
This illustrates the empirical, discovery-driven nature of the [[hofmann-lsd-sandoz-research-program]]
where surprising results were accepted as valuable data.

## Safety Considerations

The [[blesching-cannabis-safety-profile]] is concerning due to extreme response variability at
identical doses. The 40 mg dose produced both a transformative positive
experience and a severe multi-day adverse reaction. Adverse symptoms included
neurological effects (headache, vertigo, spatial distortion), autonomic
effects (nausea, hypersensitivity), and prolonged aftereffects (>1 week). The
long duration (10–15 h) increases risk of prolonged distress with no practical
way to shorten the experience. Medical intervention was required in at least
one case. The 50 mg dose produced paranoia without compensatory insights.
Narrow [[cannabis-therapeutic-index-overview]] and extreme individual variation make this compound
particularly risky.
