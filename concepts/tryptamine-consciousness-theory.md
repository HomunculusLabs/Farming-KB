## Tryptamine Consciousness Theory

[[terence-mckenna]]'s 1983 Esalen lecture "Tryptamine Hallucinogens and Consciousness" (published in *The Archaic Revival*, 1992) presents his most detailed phenomenological account of DMT and psilocybin experiences, with theoretical implications for consciousness studies.

## The Tryptamine Family

McKenna focuses on three tryptophan-derived hallucinogens:
- **DMT** (N,N-dimethyltryptamine): Endogenous compound, 45-second onset, 5-minute duration
- **Psilocybin** (4-phosphoryloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine): ~1-hour onset, hours duration
- **Ayahuasca**: Oral DMT + MAO inhibitor (beta-carboline from Banisteriopsis caapi)

Key biochemical note: Psilocybin is 4-phosphoraloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine; serotonin (the major neurotransmitter) is 5-hydroxytryptamine. The structural similarity means "the brain is absolutely at home with this compound."

## Quantum Mechanical Correlates

McKenna references work by John Smythies and [[alexander-shulgin]] showing quantum mechanical correlates to hallucinogenesis: "If one atom on the molecular ring of an inactive compound is moved, the compound becomes highly active." He interprets this as "a perfect proof of the dynamic linkage at the formative level between quantum mechanically described matter and mind."

## The Logos Hypothesis

Central to McKenna's theory: DMT and psilocybin "invoke the Logos" -- they work directly on language centers, making interior dialogue a primary feature of the experience. This means:

- An important aspect is the interior dialogue with an apparent alien intelligence
- The experience presents information as "three-dimensionally deployed fourth-dimensionally, coded as light and as evolving surfaces"
- The question becomes whether to enter into this dialogue and make sense of the incoming signal

## DMT Phenomenology

### The Breakthrough Sequence
1. **Onset** (15 sec): Sound like "ripping cellophane," ascending tone, shifting geometric surfaces
2. **Mode shift** (~30 sec): All available bond sites occupied; arrival in an "underground, insulated, domed" space
3. **The "Merry Go Raum"**: From Finnegans Wake -- the room is actually going around; one feels like a child
4. **Entity contact**: "Self-transforming machine elves" -- hyperdimensional, dynamically contorting topological modules
5. **The message**: "Don't be alarmed. Remember, and do what we are doing."

### The Machine Elves
- Appear as "fractal reflections of some previously hidden and suddenly autonomous part of one's own psyche"
- "Bathe one in love" -- not erotic but open-hearted
- Emit sounds "like music, like language" that pass "without any quantized moment of distinction"
- Convey "alien meaning that cannot be Englished"
- The cognitive dissonance of encountering a "translinguistic object" causes wonder, astonishment, and "awe at the brink of terror"

### Glossolalia and the Primal Language
DMT-induced glossolalia differs from religious speaking-in-tongues:
- It is a conscious brain state allowing expression of "the assembly language that lies behind language"
- McKenna relates this to Robert Graves' "primal language" in *The White Goddess* and the Kabbalistic language of the Zohar
- One discovers the ability to "make the extra-dimensional objects -- the feeling-toned, meaning-toned, three-dimensional rotating complexes of transforming light and color"

## Psilocybin vs DMT

| Feature | DMT | Psilocybin |
|---------|-----|------------|
| Onset | ~15 seconds (smoked) | ~1 hour |
| Duration | ~5 minutes | Several hours |
| Intensity | Maximum ("bastante") | Gradual build |
| Entity contact | Immediate | Same but slower |
| Integration | Extremely difficult | More accessible |

Both invoke the Logos and convey into "the world of the tryptamine hyperdimension."

## Consciousness Theory

### The Perceiving Self
- "The most richly organized material in the universe is the human cerebral cortex"
- "The densest and richest experience in the universe is the experience you are having right now"
- "Everything should be constellated outward from the perceiving self"
- Western science has decentralized humans to "the edge of the galaxy" when consciousness is the primary datum

### Time and Eternity
- The tryptamine state is "transtemporal -- an anticipation of the future"
- Plato's metaphor: "time is the moving image of eternity"
- The tryptamine ecstasy is "a stepping out of the moving image and into eternity, the nunc stans of Thomas Aquinas"
- In that state, "all of human history is seen to lead toward this culminating moment"
- Visible acceleration: fire (millions of years ago), language (35,000 years ago), measurement (5,000), Galileo (400), Watson-Crick and DNA (recent)

### The Future of Consciousness
- "The future is bound to be psychedelic, because the future belongs to the mind"
- McKenna's vision: "exteriorize the soul and internalize the body" -- the exterior soul as "a superconducting lens of translinguistic matter generated out of the body of each of us at a critical juncture at our psychedelic Bar Mitzvah"
- Language as "self-replicating information systems that reflect functions of DNA: learning, coding, templating, recording, testing, re-testing, re-coding"

## Shamanism as Technology

McKenna argues shamanism is "primarily techniques, not ritual":
- Signs of shamanic potential: oddness/uniqueness, epilepsy, survival of unusual ordeals (lightning strikes, near-death illness)
- Distinguishing characteristic of shamans: "extraordinary centeredness" combined with intellectual sophistication
- Shamans are "the true phenomenologists of this world; they know plant chemistry, yet they call these energy fields 'spirits'"
- In traditional societies, shamans are peripheral to ordinary social life, called on only in crisis (illness, psychological difficulty, weather prediction)
- "We do not live in that kind of society" -- McKenna positions himself as an "explorer" doing "Baconian collecting of data"

## The Black Hole Effect

McKenna describes DMT as an "intellectual black hole": "once one knows about it, it is very hard for others to understand what one is talking about. One cannot be heard. The more one is able to articulate what it is, the less others are able to understand." This explains why enlightened beings are "silent" -- "they are silent because we cannot understand them."

## Borges and the Secret
McKenna relates his experiences to Borges' "The Sect of the Phoenix" and "The Aleph": the "Secret" is a trivial, momentary act "transmitted from generation to generation," always "somewhat ridiculous" yet "all words name it or rather inevitably allude to it." In the Amazon, the mushroom answered why McKenna and Dennis were chosen: "Because you did not believe in anything. Because you have never given over your belief to anyone."

## Cautionary Notes
- "Experimenters should be very careful. One must build up to the experience."
- "These are bizarre dimensions of extraordinary power and beauty."
- "All the compounds are potentially dangerous, and all compounds, at sufficient doses or repeated over time, involve risks."
- "The library is the first place to go when looking into taking a new compound."
