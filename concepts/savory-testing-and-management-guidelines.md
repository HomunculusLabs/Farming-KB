## The Whole Ecosystem Guideline

This particular guideline reminds practitioners that the whole is the
only reality in nature and thus the only unit that can be managed.
Management of a rare species without regard to the whole will doom
that species. Management of a monoculture crop will also fail eventually
as it damages the whole.

In practice, when using the model in any of its modes, one need not
go through the testing guidelines in any particular order, but should
go through them all unless a tool fails one or more outright. Over
time, practitioners develop a feel for which guidelines a tool is least
likely to pass and consider those first.

## The Weak Link Guideline

A chain stretched to breaking will fail at its weakest link, and at
any moment every chain has only one weakest link that alone accounts
for the strength of the entire chain. To strengthen a chain when
resources are limited, one must always attend only to the weakest link.

The ecosystem, humanity's only source of true wealth, functions as a
chain of processes, and practitioners cannot afford to waste the
wealth it generates by investing in any but the weakest of them.
