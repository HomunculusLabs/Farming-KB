## Harpellales-Kickxellales Relationship

One of the few well-supported relationships, based on SSU rDNA
analysis, is that between the Harpellales and Kickxellales. These
groups also share the ultrastructural feature of plugged, flared
septal pores. A suggestion that the Harpellales were derived from
the Kickxellales was rejected by O'Donnell and colleagues (1998).

The enlarged Harpellales-Kickxellales clade also contains
Zoopagales and Spiromyces. Most species of both groups are
parasites of fungi or invertebrate animals.

## Amoebidiales Exclusion

The Amoebidiales has been determined to represent a protozoan
lineage rather than a fungal one, and has been excluded from the
Trichomycetes. This reclassification, based on molecular evidence,
removed one of the traditional groups from the Zygomycota entirely.

## Mucorales Revisions

O'Donnell and colleagues (2001) included 54 genera in their
phylogenetic analyses of Mucorales, using an expanded molecular
data set of SSU rDNA, LSU rDNA, and EF-1a gene exons plus 11
morphological traits. Their tree is at odds with morphological
phylogenetic hypotheses:

- Supports a monophyletic grouping of Mucorales
- Identifies members of the Mortierellales as a basal outgroup
- Finds that traditionally recognized families Mucoraceae,
  Thamnidiaceae, and Pilobolaceae are polyphyletic
