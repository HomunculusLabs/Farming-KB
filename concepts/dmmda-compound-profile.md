# DMMDA — 2,5-Dimethoxy-3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine

## Overview

DMMDA (PIHKAL #58) is a psychedelic amphetamine with a uniquely crowded substitution pattern — methoxy groups at the 2 and 5 positions plus a methylenedioxy bridge spanning the 3 and 4 positions. This five-substituent pattern (four substituents on four positions of the ring, with one bridge covering two) makes DMMDA one of the most heavily substituted amphetamine derivatives in PIHKAL. It is derived from apiole, a constituent of parsley oil.

DMMDA holds historical significance as the first tetraoxygenated amphetamine ever explored in man, with initial testing dating back to 1962. Its natural origin from parsley essential oil — specifically the compound apiole — provides a fascinating connection between the botanical spice world and synthetic psychopharmacology. See [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]] for background on substitution patterns.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #58
- **Class:** Substituted amphetamine
- **HCl melting point:** 165-175 °C (broad range)
- **Natural precursor:** Apiole (from parsley essential oil)

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Threshold** | ~25 mg |
| **Common dose** | 30-75 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 75 mg+ |
| **Duration** | 6-8 hours |
| **Onset** | ~1 hour |

For Shulgin's approach to dose determination, see [[shulgin-dosage-guidelines]].

## Effects

- At 25 mg: rapid onset with nausea, mild depersonalization reminiscent of [[lsd-compound-profile]], slight time slowing, mild ataxia — effects diminish within a few hours
- At 32 mg: "2 1/2 plus" at two hours with no apparent visuals, no push, no erotic — "felt completely safe, and without any conspicuous psychedelic action"
- At 50 mg (gradual dosing): no effects at all when taken as 10 mg increments every 30 minutes
- At 50 mg (single dose): reports of getting into a "very remarkable place" (entry truncated in PIHKAL)

The variable response at 50 mg (no effects with gradual dosing but notable effects with single dose) suggests possible first-pass metabolism issues or enzyme saturation kinetics.

## Experience Reports (from PIHKAL)

### At 25 mg (1962 — First Human Trial)

> The intoxication was there at an hour and a quarter, hit with nausea with no particular warning. Shaky, a little dilated in the eyes, modest depersonalization (reminding me of [[lsd-compound-profile]]). Time slightly slowed, mild ataxia in the legs. A couple of hours later, all effects going away fast.

As the first tetraoxygenated amphetamine ever tested in humans, this report from 1962 is historically significant. The rapid onset of nausea, brief duration, and mild character are consistent with what would later be understood about the pharmacokinetics of heavily substituted amphetamines.

### At 32 mg

> 2 1/2 plus at two hours with no apparent visuals, no push, no erotic. Quietly slipping away. Felt completely safe, without any conspicuous psychedelic action.

The "2 1/2 plus" rating on Shulgin's scale indicates a moderate experience — above threshold but well below a full psychedelic level. The absence of visuals, "push" (stimulant drive), or erotic enhancement at this dose places DMMDA at the milder end of the psychedelic amphetamine spectrum.

### At 50 mg (Graded Dosing)

> Took graded doses of 10 mg every thirty minutes for a total of 50 mg, no effects at all.

The complete absence of effects with gradual dosing — despite 50 mg total — is a striking finding. This pattern is consistent with rapid first-pass metabolism: small doses are cleared before they can accumulate to active levels, and the metabolizing enzymes are not saturated by incremental administration.

### At 50 mg (Single Dose)

> Getting into abstract thinking, maybe some imagery. Effects were disappointingly light.

The contrast between graded and single-dose administration at the same total amount is dramatic. With a single bolus, at least some threshold of active compound reaches the CNS before metabolic clearance, producing effects that gradual dosing entirely fails to achieve.

### At 75 mg

> Equal to somewhere between 75 and 100 micrograms of [[lsd-compound-profile]]. Caught up with the imagery, overriding religious aspect to the day. Esthetic value. Liked it.

At 75 mg, DMMDA finally delivers a fully psychedelic experience, comparable in intensity to a moderate LSD dose. The "overriding religious aspect" and emphasis on "esthetic value" suggest a qualitatively rich experience at this level, though the high dose required indicates that DMMDA is not among the more potent psychedelic amphetamines.

Shulgin assigned DMMDA a potency of 10 [[mescaline-compound-profile]] units (M.U.), though he noted this was "probably too low" — a judgment consistent with the need for 75 mg to achieve a full experience.

## The Ten Essential Amphetamines

DMMDA occupies a special position in Shulgin's body of work as "one of the ten essential amphetamines." These ten compounds are each derived from one of ten essential oils — botanical sources that bridge the worlds of natural products chemistry and synthetic psychopharmacology.

The natural precursor for DMMDA is apiole, isolated from commercial Oil of Parsley by fractional distillation (bp 165-167 °C at 27 mm/Hg). Shulgin reflected that this connection "ties together the spice world and the amphetamine world" — a poetic framing of the relationship between culinary herbs and synthetic psychedelics. The concept of "essential amphetamines" parallels and extends the classical "essential oils" of perfumery and herbalism, reframing botanical chemistry through the lens of psychopharmacology.

## Synthesis (from PIHKAL)

DMMDA is derived from apiole (1-allyl-2,5-dimethoxy-3,4-methylenedioxybenzene), isolated from commercial Oil of Parsley by fractional distillation. Apiole undergoes base-catalyzed isomerization to isoapiole (the propenyl isomer), which is then oxidized with tetranitromethane to the nitrostyrene intermediate and reduced with LAH via Soxhlet extraction technique. The synthesis is notable for starting entirely from a natural product. For more on synthesis methodology see [[pihkal-introduction-and-methodology]].

## Pharmacology

The heavily substituted aromatic ring creates steric constraints that likely affect receptor binding. The broad melting point range (165-175 °C) suggests possible crystallization issues related to the molecule's conformational flexibility. DMMDA acts as a serotonin receptor agonist. See [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]] and [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]].

The unusually high doses required for full psychedelic effects (75 mg) relative to other substituted amphetamines suggest that the tetraoxygenated substitution pattern may reduce receptor affinity compared to tri-substituted analogues like [[mda-compound-profile]].

## Analogues and Derivatives

### METHYL-DMMDA (DMMDMA)

The N-methylated derivative of DMMDA, also designated DMMDMA, represents an [[mdma-compound-profile]] analogue. The addition of an N-methyl group to the amphetamine backbone converts the compound from a classical psychedelic amphetamine into an entactogenic/empathogenic profile, paralleling the well-known MDA → MDMA transformation.

### Beta-Methyl Homologues (S-1495 and S-1496)

Two beta-methyl homologues were synthesized as the threo- and erythro- diastereomers (designated S-1495 and S-1496 in the Somorit series). These compounds incorporate an additional methyl group at the beta position of the phenethylamine chain. Despite being synthesized and presumably tested, they were never assigned trivial names in the PIHKAL system.

### DMMDA-2

DMMDA-2 (2,3-dimethoxy-4,5-methylenedioxyamphetamine) is a positional isomer of DMMDA, with the two methoxy groups shifted to the 2 and 3 positions. This repositioning of the oxygen substituents around the aromatic ring can produce significant differences in both potency and subjective character. It is closely related to the [[mmda-compound-profile]] and [[mmda-2-compound-profile]] family of compounds, which share the dimethoxy-methylenedioxy substitution motif.

## Harm Reduction

- Variable dose-response — individual sensitivity varies considerably
- Some nausea on onset
- Relatively short duration (6-8 hours) for an amphetamine-based psychedelic
- The natural origin from parsley oil does not imply safety
- High dose requirements (up to 75 mg) increase the risk of dosing errors
- The graded-dosing paradox (no effects from gradual administration) complicates titration approaches
