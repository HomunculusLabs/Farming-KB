# DMMDA — 2,5-Dimethoxy-3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine

## Overview

DMMDA (PIHKAL #58) is a psychedelic amphetamine with a uniquely crowded substitution pattern — methoxy groups at the 2 and 5 positions plus a methylenedioxy bridge spanning the 3 and 4 positions. This five-substituent pattern (four substituents on four positions of the ring, with one bridge covering two) makes DMMDA one of the most heavily substituted amphetamine derivatives in PIHKAL. It is derived from apiole, a constituent of parsley oil.

DMMDA holds historical significance as the first tetraoxygenated amphetamine ever explored in man, with initial testing dating back to 1962. Its natural origin from parsley [[permaculture-designers-manual-essential-oil-plants]] — specifically the compound apiole — provides a fascinating connection between the botanical spice world and synthetic psychopharmacology. See [[shulgin-dosage-guidelines]].

## Effects

- At 25 mg: rapid onset with nausea, mild depersonalization reminiscent of [[lsd-compound-profile]]). Time slightly slowed, mild ataxia in the legs. A couple of hours later, all effects going away fast.

As the first tetraoxygenated amphetamine ever tested in humans, this report from 1962 is historically significant. The rapid onset of nausea, brief duration, and mild character are consistent with what would later be understood about the pharmacokinetics of heavily substituted amphetamines.

### At 32 mg

> 2 1/2 plus at two hours with no apparent visuals, no push, no erotic. Quietly slipping away. Felt completely safe, without any conspicuous psychedelic action.

The "2 1/2 plus" rating on Shulgin's scale indicates a moderate experience — above threshold but well below a full psychedelic level. The absence of visuals, "push" (stimulant drive), or erotic enhancement at this dose places DMMDA at the milder end of the psychedelic amphetamine spectrum.

### At 50 mg (Graded Dosing)

> Took graded doses of 10 mg every thirty minutes for a total of 50 mg, no effects at all.

The complete absence of effects with gradual dosing — despite 50 mg total — is a striking finding. This pattern is consistent with rapid first-pass metabolism: small doses are cleared before they can accumulate to active levels, and the metabolizing enzymes are not saturated by incremental administration.

### At 50 mg (Single Dose)

> Getting into abstract thinking, maybe some imagery. Effects were disappointingly light.

The contrast between graded and single-dose administration at the same total amount is dramatic. With a single bolus, at least some threshold of active compound reaches the CNS before metabolic clearance, producing effects that gradual dosing entirely fails to achieve.

### At 75 mg

> Equal to somewhere between 75 and 100 micrograms of [[mescaline-compound-profile]] units (M.U.), though he noted this was "probably too low" — a judgment consistent with the need for 75 mg to achieve a full experience.

## The Ten Essential Amphetamines

DMMDA occupies a special position in Shulgin's body of work as "one of the ten essential amphetamines." These ten compounds are each derived from one of ten [[comparison-essential-oils-vs-neem-oil-vs-insecticidal-soap]] — [[dmt-botanical-sources-pharmacology]] that bridge the worlds of natural products chemistry and synthetic psychopharmacology.

The natural precursor for DMMDA is apiole, isolated from commercial Oil of Parsley by [[cannabis-fractional-distillation-cannabinoids]] (bp 165-167 °C at 27 mm/Hg). Shulgin reflected that this connection "ties together the spice world and the amphetamine world" — a poetic framing of the relationship between [[holzer-medicinal-and-culinary-herbs-compendium]] and synthetic psychedelics. The concept of "essential amphetamines" parallels and extends the classical "essential oils" of perfumery and herbalism, reframing botanical chemistry through the lens of psychopharmacology.

## Synthesis (from PIHKAL)

DMMDA is derived from apiole (1-allyl-2,5-dimethoxy-3,4-methylenedioxybenzene), isolated from commercial Oil of Parsley by fractional distillation. Apiole undergoes base-catalyzed isomerization to isoapiole (the propenyl isomer), which is then oxidized with tetranitromethane to the nitrostyrene intermediate and reduced with LAH via Soxhlet extraction technique. The synthesis is notable for starting entirely from a natural product. For more on synthesis methodology see [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]] and [[ayahuasca-preparation-and-ceremony]].

## Analogues and Derivatives

### METHYL-DMMDA (DMMDMA)

The N-methylated derivative of DMMDA, also designated DMMDMA, represents an [[mmda-compound-profile]] and [[mmda-2-compound-profile]] family of compounds, which share the dimethoxy-methylenedioxy substitution motif.

## Harm Reduction

- Variable dose-response — individual sensitivity varies considerably
- Some nausea on onset
- Relatively [[savory-short-duration-grazing-and-cell-systems]] (6-8 hours) for an amphetamine-based psychedelic
- The natural origin from parsley oil does not imply safety
- High dose requirements (up to 75 mg) increase the risk of dosing errors
- The graded-dosing paradox (no effects from gradual administration) complicates titration approaches
