## Ganoderma: The Reishi

### The Parasitic Potential

The reishi mushroom (Ganoderma lucidum), prized in traditional medicine, can function as a parasite:
- It attacks living trees, particularly hardwoods
- Produces distinctive shelf-like fruiting bodies on tree trunks
- Can cause heart rot in infected trees
- Despite its medicinal value for humans, it can be devastating to host trees
