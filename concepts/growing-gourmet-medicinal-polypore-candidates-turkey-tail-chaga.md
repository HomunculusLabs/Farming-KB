## Cultivation Approaches for Polypores

The cultivation of polypore species can take several
tracks:

1. **[[gourmet-medicinal-mushroom-shiitake-cultivation-logs]]. By burying the
   inoculated logs in sawdust or soil, moisture is better
   preserved and fruitings extend over several years.

2. **Stump culture**: Stumps can also be inoculated,
   although if other fungi have already captured that
   niche, production is inhibited. First flushes are
   often delayed, not showing for several years after
   inoculation.

3. **Indoor controlled environment**: By far the most
   dependable and rapid production system is indoor
   cultivation under controlled environmental conditions.
