# Shamanic Powers Through Ayahuasca

In Amazonian vegetalismo, [[banco-sumiruna-muraya]] is transported in a
glass tube (the lupuna colorada) by ancash sylphs (blue sylphs),
standing on a ball of high-pressure gas. He wears skins of the boa,
trousers of ray-fish, feet of yangunturo (giant armadillo), and a
macaw as his hat. Flying saucers accompany him during astral travel.
Vegetalistas may also visit planets -- Saturn, Jupiter, Venus, Mars,
and even distant galaxies -- with relative ease.

## Powers of Transformation

Vegetalistas can transform themselves and others. In one vision, a
group of vegetalistas under sorcerous attack transform into wolves,
drinking ayahuasca from a gourd and accompanied by their duenas (wise
queens of great paranormal knowledge). A Campa shaman is capable of
transforming himself into any kind of snake. The queen Bobinzana-sisa
gives the [[amazonian-ayahuasca-purge-and-diet-practices]] more power to cleanse impurities in the
stomach.

## The Tingunas

Tingunas are luminous, colorful formations raised by the vegetalista
during healing. They function as protective barriers and can be
transformed into persons or cities. Strong tingunas of surprising
colors are posted around the healing circle along with predatory
animals to prevent intrusion by sorcerers or disease-causing spirits.
