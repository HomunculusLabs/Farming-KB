## Spectrum of Effects

### Perceptual Effects

At common recreational doses, psilocybin mushrooms produce a range of perceptual alterations:

- Visual enhancements: Colors appear more vivid, patterns become more complex
- Geometric patterns: Closed-eye and open-eye visual patterning
- Enhanced appreciation of music and art
- Altered perception of time: Time may appear to slow down or speed up

It is [[query-what-are-the-most-important-medicinal-mushrooms-and-how-do-i-choose-one]] to note that, at common recreational doses, psilocybin mushrooms typically do not produce true hallucinations (seeing objects that are not present). Instead, they alter the perception of objects that are actually present.

### Cognitive and Emotional Effects

The cognitive and emotional effects of psilocybin mushrooms can include:

- Changes in mood, ranging from euphoria to anxiety
- Altered thought patterns and unusual associations
- Enhanced introspection and self-reflection
- Changes in the perception of personal identity
- Increased emotional sensitivity and reactivity

### Variability

The effects of psilocybin mushrooms are highly variable and influenced by the user's mental state (set), physical environment (setting), prior experience with psychedelics, and individual neurochemistry. The same dose can produce markedly different experiences in different individuals or even in the same individual on different occasions.
