# JADAM Animal By-Product Liquid Fertilizers

JADAM has developed methods for producing high-quality liquid fertilizers from animal by-products including fish, fish meal, and fish waste. These fertilizers provide a diverse nutrient profile superior to single-nutrient synthetic fertilizers, at a fraction of the cost.

## Philosophy of Quality Over Quantity

JADAM emphasizes that the quality of nitrogen matters more than quantity:

- Fish JLF has approximately 1/5 the nitrogen contained in urea fertilizer
- However, it is of much higher quality because it contains a wide variety of other nutrients
- The analogy: building a house requires more than just cement (nitrogen); you need diverse materials
- Similarly, plants need a diversity of nutrients, not just concentrated nitrogen
- Liquid fertilizer is considered high-quality when it contains a variety of nutrients

## Recommended Usage Pattern

- Use [[comparison-leaf-mold-composting-vs-hot-composting-method]]
- No need to stir
- If too much mackerel, decomposition will be significantly slower; ensure enough water
- To reduce smell, add phyllite powder
- Can make JLF from any animal by-products using this approach
- Dilution: 50-500 times
- Application: soil or foliar
- Duration: more than 3 months to prepare
- Expected effect: promotes growth

### Analysis (per 100 liters: mackerel + leaf mold)
- pH: 6.8
- EC (1:5): 135.15 ds/m
