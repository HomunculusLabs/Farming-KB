# Caterpillars and Budworms on Cannabis

Caterpillars and budworms are chewing insect pests that
can cause significant damage to cannabis foliage and
flowers. While they are seldom a problem indoors, they
are common in [[bud-rot-cannabis]]

## Borers

Borers are beetle larvae that tunnel into cannabis
stems and roots. They are more common outdoors than
indoors. Signs of borer activity include an entry hole
and dead growth on either side of the entry, often
accompanied by sawdust-like frass. Tunnels inside the
stem curtail fluid flow and cause plant parts to wilt.
If a borer severely damages the main stem, fluid flow
to the entire plant can stop, causing death.

## Detection

One effective way to check for caterpillars and
loopers is to spray one plant with pyrethrum aerosol
spray and shake the plant afterward. The spray has a
quick knockout effect, and most caterpillars will fall
from the plant, making them easy to spot and count.

Droppings that fall out when buds are hung to dry are
another way to discover an infestation. Inspect below
hung buds for poop droppings to identify previously
undetected caterpillar activity.

## Cultural and Physical Control

- **Manually remove** caterpillars by hand. Check
  plants regularly, especially during and after
  outdoor moth activity periods.
- **Inspect buds carefully** during flowering for
  signs of entry holes or frass.
- **Remove and destroy** infested buds to prevent the
  spread of disease.
- **[[cervantes-bacillus-thuringiensis-bt-control]] (Bt)** is the most
  effective biological control for caterpillars. Btk
  (B. thuringiensis var. kurstaki) is toxic to many
  moth and caterpillar larvae including most species
  that feed on flowers and vegetables. Bta (B.
  thuringiensis var. aizawai) is effective against
  hard-to-kill budworms, borers, and armyworms.

See also: [[comparison-beneficial-insects-guide-vs-integrated-pest-management]].
  Microencapsulated forms (M-Trak, Mattch) extend
  effective life on foliage to more than a week.
- **Pyrethrum** provides a quick knockdown effect for
  detection and immediate control.
- **Homemade sprays** of hot pepper and garlic can
  repel caterpillars.
- **Rotenone** is effective against caterpillars when
  other methods fail.
