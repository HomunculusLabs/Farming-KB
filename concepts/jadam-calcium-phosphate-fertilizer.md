# JADAM Calcium Phosphate Liquid Fertilizer

## Tips for Success

- Bone powder sinks to the bottom -- regular stirring is essential
- Finer powder = faster decomposition and higher mineral extraction
- Chicken bones decompose fastest due to their small size
- Fish bones decompose naturally in fish by-product JLF over extended time
- If oil layer forms, add more water and leaf mold to continue decomposition
- Reuse the bone residue: refill with water multiple times until depleted
