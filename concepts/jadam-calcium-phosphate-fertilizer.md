# JADAM Calcium Phosphate Liquid Fertilizer

Animal bones consist of approximately 20% phosphate (P) and 20% calcium (Ca).
Bone meal JLF provides both elements simultaneously, making it an excellent
dual-purpose input for mid-to-late stage [[mushroom-crop-management-flush-harvesting]].

## Role in Plant Growth

- **Phosphorus (P):** Component of cell membranes (phospholipids), energy
  metabolism (ATP), DNA and RNA
- **Calcium (Ca):** Forms spindle fibers for cell walls and cell division (Ca2+);
  signal transmitter for hormone responses
- Together these elements **control [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]** and **stimulate
  [[knf-reproductive-growth-stage]]** (flowering, fruiting)
- Apply in later stages of fruit growth to strengthen lower buds

## Recipe: Bone Meal JLF

### Ingredients
- Bone meal (powdered): 1 part by weight (10 kg per 100 L)
- Water: 10 parts by weight
- [[jadam-natural-nitrogen-fertilizer]])

## Tips for Success

- Bone powder sinks to the bottom -- regular stirring is essential
- Finer powder = faster decomposition and higher mineral extraction
- Chicken bones decompose fastest due to their small size
- Fish bones decompose naturally in fish by-product JLF over extended time
- If oil layer forms, add more water and leaf mold to continue decomposition
- Reuse the bone residue: refill with water multiple times until depleted
