# Cannabis for Cachexia and Appetite Stimulation

updated: 2026-04-18

Cachexia (from Greek "kakos" meaning bad and "hexis" meaning condition) is a complex metabolic syndrome associated with underlying illness, characterized by loss of muscle mass with or without loss of fat mass. It is distinct from starvation, anorexia, and age-related muscle loss. Cachexia commonly accompanies cancer, [[cannabis-for-hiv-and-aids]], chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, congestive heart failure, and chronic kidney disease.

## Cannabis and Cachexia

**CHI Value: 28** (11 studies reviewed) -- one of the highest CHI values in the Cannabis Health Index.

### Key Studies

| Drug | Study | Year, Place, Results | CHI |
|------|-------|---------------------|-----|
| Oral THC (Marinol/dronabinol) | Various | Multiple studies: Significant appetite improvement in cancer and AIDS patients | 3-5 |
| Smoked cannabis | 82 patients | 2007, San Francisco General Hospital: Significant appetite increase in HIV patients | 5 |
| Megace (megestrol acetate) vs dronabinol | 469 patients | 1997, multiple sites: Both effective; megestrol superior for weight gain but dronabinol better tolerated | 3 |
| Nabilone | Various | Several studies: Appetite stimulation confirmed | 3 |
| Oral cannabis extract | 243 patients | THC:CBD extract improved appetite and quality of life | 5 |

### Evidence-Based Confidence Level: 2.54
With 11 studies reviewed and a combined CHI of 28, cannabis-based therapies rank among the most evidence-supported applications, particularly for HIV/AIDS-related anorexia and cancer-related appetite loss.

## Anorexia (Non-Cancer, Non-HIV)

**CHI Value: 2** (Limited evidence)

While THC stimulates appetite, clinical evidence for non-disease-related anorexia remains limited. One study on dronabinol showed modest weight gain in elderly patients with anorexia of aging.

## Standard Treatments

Allopathic treatments include:
- Megestrol acetate (progesterone derivative)
- Corticosteroids (short-term appetite stimulation)
- Dronabinol (synthetic THC, FDA-approved for AIDS wasting)
- Nutritional supplementation and tube feeding

## Strain-Specific Considerations

Indica strains generally provide stronger appetite stimulation (the classic "munchies" effect) compared to sativas. THC is the primary cannabinoid responsible for appetite stimulation through CB1 receptor activation in the hypothalamus. Raw cannabis (juiced) does not stimulate appetite as THC-acid (THCA) does not activate CB1 receptors the way decarboxylated THC does.

## Endocannabinoid System Role

The [[cannabis-endocannabinoid-system]] plays a central role in regulating appetite through CB1 receptors in the hypothalamus. Ghrelin (the hunger hormone) signals through endocannabinoid pathways. Leptin resistance in obesity involves disrupted endocannabinoid signaling.

## Mind-Body Perspective

Cachexia and appetite loss may reflect a loss of appetite for life itself -- a withdrawal from engagement with the world. Chronic illness can create a sense of futility that manifests as physical wasting. Addressing the emotional and spiritual dimensions of appetite -- nourishment, pleasure, desire, engagement with life -- is integral to comprehensive treatment.
