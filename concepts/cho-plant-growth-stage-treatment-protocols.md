Dr. Cho Han Kyu's Natural Farming system provides specific
treatment protocols for each stage of plant growth. These
protocols are derived from the Nutritive Cycle Theory and are
designed to supply precisely the nutrients that plants need at
each developmental phase. The treatments address the specific
needs of plants at the vegetative, cross-over, and reproductive
stages, with additional specialized treatments for fruit
ripening and crack prevention.

All treatment inputs are diluted at a ratio of 1:1000 ml of
water as a base. Farmers can adjust combinations based on the
specific conditions observed in their crops. This flexibility
within a structured framework is characteristic of Cho's
approach: the system provides clear guidelines while
encouraging farmer observation and judgment.

## Vegetative Growth Stage Treatment

The vegetative growth treatment promotes vigorous leaf, stem,
and root development while increasing the overall volume and
size of crops. During this phase, plants are building the
structural foundation that will support later reproductive
effort. The treatment prioritizes inputs that supply nitrogen
and growth enzymes.

FPJ (Fermented Plant Juice) is the primary input during the
vegetative stage, providing readily available amino acids,
growth hormones, and enzymes from fermented plant material.
LAB (Lactic Acid Bacteria) supports nutrient cycling in the
soil rhizosphere and helps suppress pathogenic organisms that
might attack young, rapidly growing tissue.

Additional inputs may be included depending on crop condition.
If vegetative growth is insufficient, FAA can supplement the
nitrogen supply. If growth is excessive and plants are becoming
too soft, WsCa can be added to strengthen cell walls and slow
excessive elongation. The diagnostic approach ensures that
treatments match actual plant needs rather than following a
rigid calendar.

## Cross-Over Period Treatment

The cross-over period treatment addresses the plant's heightened
demand for phosphoric acid compounds as it transitions from
vegetative growth to flowering. This is the most critical
transition point in the plant's life cycle, and Cho considered
proper support during this phase essential for achieving good
fruit set and yield.

Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-PA) is the key input during
this stage. Phosphorus is required for energy transfer reactions
that drive flower initiation, pollen development, and early
fruit formation. FFJ complements WS-PA by providing organic
acids and natural sugars that support the metabolic transition.

BRV may also be included in the cross-over treatment. The acetic
acid in brown rice vinegar helps the plant access soil-bound
phosphorus and other minerals that become more available in
slightly acidified conditions. The timing of this treatment is
critical: it should begin when the first signs of flower bud
formation appear.

## Reproductive Growth Stage Treatment

During the reproductive stage, the plant's nutritional
priorities shift toward calcium and potassium as it develops
and ripens fruit. The reproductive treatment supplies these
nutrients along with continued microbial and enzymatic support
to ensure full fruit development.

Water-Soluble Calcium (WsCa) is the primary input, providing
calcium needed for cell wall integrity in developing fruit.
Calcium deficiency during reproductive growth leads to blossom
end rot, poor fruit texture, and reduced shelf life. The calcium
input is combined with Ws-K (Water-Soluble Potassium) for color
development and sugar accumulation in maturing fruit.

FPJ continues to be used during this stage, though at reduced
proportions compared to the vegetative phase. The fermented
plant compounds support the plant's energy demands during the
metabolically expensive process of fruit production. OHN provides
broad-spectrum herbal support that helps the plant resist disease
during its most vulnerable period.

## Ripeness and Sweetness Promotion

Cho developed a specialized treatment for enhancing fruit
ripening and sweetness in the final days before harvest. This
treatment is applied twice: fifteen days before harvest and
two days before harvest. The dual application ensures that the
plant receives late-stage nutritional support during the period
when sugar accumulation and color development are most active.

The formula combines OHN at one milliliter, WCA at two
milliliters, and seawater at thirty milliliters, all mixed in
one thousand milliliters of water. OHN provides herbal nutrients
that support the plant's final metabolic push. WCA supplies
calcium for fruit firmness and shelf life. Seawater contributes
a broad spectrum of trace minerals that enhance flavor complexity.

This treatment exemplifies Cho's precision approach: rather
than applying a single pre-harvest application, the two
timed doses target different phases of the ripening process.
The fifteen-day application supports the onset of ripening,
while the two-day application provides a final boost to
sugar concentration and color development.

## Crack Prevention Treatment

expansion of the fruit, leading to surface cracks that reduce
market value and create entry points for pathogens. Cho's
preventive treatment addresses this by strengthening the fruit's
