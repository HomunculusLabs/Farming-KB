# Harlequin

Harlequin is a high-CBD sativa-dominant hybrid [[blesching-cannabis-strain-selection-receptor-targeting]] developed by
Green Haven Genetics in the United States. One of the most significant medical
cannabis strains in the modern era, Harlequin is notable for its unusual
cannabinoid profile featuring CBD levels approximately double those of THC,
making it a staple for patients seeking therapeutic benefits without strong
psychoactive effects.

## Genetics and Lineage

Harlequin's lineage is notably diverse, comprising four distinct genetic lines:
Colombian Gold from the 1970s, Thai sativa, Swiss sativa, and Nepalese indica.
This multi-national heritage gives the strain a complex genetic foundation that
contributes to both its unique cannabinoid profile and its resilient growth
characteristics.

The Colombian Gold parent, a legendary sativa from the 1970s that helped define
the [[mckenna-cannabis-culture-history]] of that era, provides the foundational sativa energy and
uplifting qualities. The Thai genetics add to the sativa dominance, while the
Swiss and Nepalese lines contribute structural stability and the indica
influence that helps keep the plant manageable. The combination of these four
landrace-influenced lines is responsible for the strain's balanced cannabinoid
ratio.

## Breeder Information

Green Haven Genetics is a US-based [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]] company that has focused
on developing strains with specific therapeutic applications. Harlequin
represents one of their most important contributions to the medical cannabis
community, having been bred specifically to produce high-grade hash and oils
for medical patients. The strain is currently available in clone form only,
with Bay Area residents being the most likely to find it in circulation.

## Growing Characteristics

Despite being sativa-dominant, Harlequin is described as an incredibly hardy
plant that benefits from the [[mycelium-running-rhizomorphic-mycelium-structural-strength-erosion-control]] provided by its indica
genetics. It can be kept to a manageable height or allowed to reach its full
potential, giving growers flexibility in their approach. Key cultivation
details include:

- **Flowering time**: 70 days from the flip to 12/green [[comparison-autoflowering-cannabis-vs-cannabis-photoperiod-flowering-cycle]]
- **Drying time**: Approximately 3 weeks due to heavy resin content and dense
  green cannabis plant
- **Growth pattern**: Taller than indica but easily managed; strong structure
- **Bud formation**: Flowers begin forming as early as 2 weeks after the flip
  to flowering, with finger-length flowers reported by the breeders
- **[[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]]**: Extremely heavy; buds become caked in resin to the
  point where they appear sugar-coated
- **Support needs**: Staking required as resin-heavy buds become too heavy for
  branches to support unaided

The exceptional resin production makes Harlequin particularly attractive for
hash and concentrate production. Growers can expect significant quantities of
finger hash during the trimming and manicure process. The long drying period is
essential to properly preserve the terpene and cannabinoid content of the
resin-heavy flowers.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma and flavor profile of Harlequin buds is enhanced by the remarkable
resin production. When used for hash making, the resulting concentrate is
described as among the best available, indicating a rich and complex terpene
profile. The combination of Colombian, Thai, Swiss, and Nepalese genetics
suggests a diverse palette of tropical, earthy, and spicy notes.

## Effects

Harlequin's effects are defined by its high-CBD profile rather than the
intense psychoactivity typical of sativa-dominant strains. With approximately
4% CBD and 2% THC, the strain provides therapeutic benefits while minimizing
the intoxicating effects. Users can expect clear-headed functionality with
mild relaxation, making it suitable for daytime medical use without impairment.
