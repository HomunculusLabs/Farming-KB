# Catalytic Hydrogenolysis for [[psilocybin-synthesis-zwitterionic-intermediate-filtration-method]] (compound 8) carries two benzyl groups that
must be removed to reveal the final psilocybin structure:

1. **N-benzyl removal**: Cleavage of the benzyl group from the quaternary
   ammonium nitrogen (N-1 of the [[cho-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid-preparation]] group (-OPO3H2), giving
   the complete phosphate ester of psilocybin.

Both debenzylation events occur under the same catalytic hydrogenolysis
conditions, making this a highly convergent and efficient final step.

## Reaction Conditions

The hydrogenolysis is performed under the following conditions:

- **Substrate**: Compound 8 (10.5 g, 22.5 mmol)
- **Solvent**: Methanol (225 mL), chosen for its ability to dissolve
  the [[oxidative-addition-organometallic-chemistry]] to Pd(0) or through a surface-mediated
process, yielding toluene and the free phosphate acid. This is the same
mechanism by which benzyl esters are routinely deprotected in organic
synthesis.

### Quaternary Ammonium Debenzylation (N-Benzyl)

Cleavage of the N-benzyl group from a quaternary ammonium ion is less
common but well precedented for benzylic quaternary ammonium salts. The
benzylic carbon-nitrogen bond is activated toward hydrogenolysis because
the benzylic position stabilizes developing carbocation character during
bond cleavage, and the palladium surface facilitates the cleavage of the
C-N bond. The products are toluene and the tertiary amine (N,N-dimethyl-
indole nitrogen).

The net transformation converts the zwitterionic N-benzyl quaternary
ammonium phosphate to the neutral N-H indole with a phosphate ester,
which is psilocybin itself.

## Workup and Isolation

The workup procedure is straightforward:

1. After 2 hours under hydrogen atmosphere, water (approximately 50 mL)
   is added to the reaction mixture because psilocybin begins to
   deposit as a crystalline solid.
2. Stirring continues for one additional hour under hydrogen to ensure
   complete reaction.
