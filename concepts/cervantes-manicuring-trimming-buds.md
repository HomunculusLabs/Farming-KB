## Manicuring and Trimming Cannabis Buds

Manicuring is the process of removing leaves from harvested cannabis buds to
produce clean, potent, visually appealing flowers. Jorge Cervantes considers
proper manicuring essential to preserving quality and maximizing the value of
every harvest.

## Why Manicuring Matters

Leaves around buds have much lower THC potency than the resin-covered bud
tissue itself. Removing them concentrates the psychoactive material and
improves the appearance, flavor, and smoothness of the final product. Proper
manicuring also speeds drying and reduces the risk of mold, since leftover
leaf petioles attract moisture and can become sites for [[cervantes-finger-hash-manicuring]]"
  that accumulates during trimming

## Collecting Resin During Manicuring

Manicuring produces valuable byproducts:

- **Scissor hash** - Scrape accumulated resin from scissors with a small
  knife when buildup impairs blade movement. Ball up the bits by rubbing
  between fingers
- **Finger hash** - Remove accumulated resin from rubber gloves by bathing
  them in isopropyl alcohol. Let the alcohol evaporate overnight, then scrape
  up the remaining hash. Alternatively, freeze the gloves for a few hours
  to make scraping easier
- **Table resin** - Manicure over a fine silkscreen or glass table. Scrape
  up fallen resin glands and press into blocks of hash
- **Trim for hash** - All trimmed leaf material can be processed into
  [[cannabis-odor-control-and-carbon-filters]] during manicuring
