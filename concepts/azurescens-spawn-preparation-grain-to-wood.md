# Azurescens Spawn Preparation — Grain to Wood Transfer

Producing reliable spawn for [[holzer-mushroom-cultivation-outdoor-beds]] is possible but not recommended:

- **Pest attraction**: Grain attracts bugs and rodents, which consume the nutritious kernels
- **[[stamets-outdoor-wood-chip-bed-mycelium-transplantation]] chip bed
- **Colonization advantage**: Mycelium already adapted to wood substrate colonizes outdoor beds faster

## Stage 1: Agar Culture

### Malt Extract Agar Recipe
- 100 ml water
- 4 g malt extract
- 2 g agar

### Procedure
1. Combine ingredients, bring to boil until fully dissolved
2. Pour approximately 1 cm depth into jars (no taller than 10 cm)
3. Loosely cap jars; cover with tin foil
4. Sterilize at maximum pressure (121C) for **20 minutes**
5. Cool to room temperature

### Spore Germination
1. Clean workspace with 70% ethanol
2. Sterilize inoculation loop or tweezers in gas flame
3. Cool loop on sterile agar surface (spores stick to the wet surface)
4. Pick up spores and transfer to agar
5. Incubate at **18-24C (65-75F)**
6. Spores germinate within a few days; visible cottony white mycelium in about three weeks

### Strain Isolation
1. Transfer small pieces of white mycelium to fresh [[double-layer-tinfoil-jar-covering-pressure-cooker-method]] to prevent bags from blocking vent
- Sterilize at 121C for **3 full hours** — do not cut corners
- Cool in [[mushroom-cultivation-jar-lid-management-loose-fit-vacuum-gas-exchange]]; cover with tin foil
4. Incubate — growth visible within a few days
5. Shake again after a few days to accelerate expansion

**From grain spawn (bag method)**:
1. Open cooled bags in flow cabinet (touch only the outside)
2. Pour colonized grain spawn into each bag
3. Seal with impulse sealer; test seal for leaks
