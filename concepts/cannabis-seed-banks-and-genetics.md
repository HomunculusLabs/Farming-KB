## Cannabis Seed Banks and Genetics

## Overview

Cannabis seed banks are companies that produce, store, and distribute cannabis seeds to growers worldwide. They serve as the primary source of [[biodiversity-fungal-genetic-diversity]] for both commercial operations and home cultivators. Understanding seed banks, strain preservation, and genetic terminology is essential for anyone serious about [[cannabis-breeding-basics|breeding]] or selecting quality genetics for [[cannabis-indoor-growing-basics|indoor]] or [[cannabis-outdoor-growing|outdoor]] cultivation.

## Types of Seeds

### Regular Seeds

Regular seeds can produce either male or female plants (approximately 50/50 ratio). They are essential for breeding programs but require sex identification and male removal for sinsemilla production.

- Full genetic diversity preserved
- Required for creating new crosses
- Both male and female plants needed for seed production
- More work for growers who only want buds (sinsemilla)
- Generally less expensive than feminized seeds

### Feminized Seeds

Feminized seeds are bred to produce only female plants (approximately 99% female). Created through techniques that induce female plants to produce pollen containing only X chromosomes.

- No need to identify and remove males
- More efficient for bud production (every seed produces a bud-bearing plant)
- Not suitable for breeding (no male plants available)
- Slightly higher cost than regular seeds
- Some debate about slightly lower vigor compared to regular seeds

### Autoflowering Seeds

Seeds containing ruderalis genetics that flower automatically based on age. See [[cannabis-autoflower-guide|autoflower guide]] for detailed information.

### Clone-Only Genetics

Some elite strains are maintained only as living clones — no seeds are available. These genetics are preserved through [[hamilton-vegetative-propagation]]. Examples include original OG Kush, Gelato (certain cuts), and various Cookies phenotypes. Clone-only genetics maintain exact genetic copies of the mother plant.

## Seed Bank Categories

### Dutch Legacy Seed Banks

The Netherlands was the birthplace of the modern commercial seed bank industry:

- **Sensi Seeds:** Founded in 1985 by Ben Dronkers. One of the oldest and most respected seed banks. Home to legendary genetics including Northern Lights, Skunk #1, and Haze. Extensive strain catalog and genetic preservation program.
- **Dutch Passion:** Established in 1987. Pioneered feminized seed technology. Known for reliable, stable genetics and autoflower development.
- **Royal Queen Seeds:** Modern Dutch bank with extensive catalog. Good beginner-friendly options and competitive pricing.
- **Greenhouse Seeds:** Founded by Arjan Roskam. Won multiple Cannabis Cups. Known for powerful, high-THC strains like Super Lemon Haze and White Rhino.
- **Barney's Farm:** Based in Amsterdam. Extensive catalog with strains from their world travels and breeding programs.

### Spanish Seed Banks

Spain has emerged as a major seed bank hub due to favorable laws:

- **Sweet Seeds:** Known for innovative crosses and creative breeding. Strong catalog of modern varieties.
- **Dinafem:** One of the first banks to focus heavily on autoflowering genetics. Good selection of both autoflowers and photoperiod strains.
- **Kannabia:** Accessible pricing with solid genetics. Good beginner-oriented catalog.

### North American Seed Banks

- **Exotic Seed Shop / Exotic Genetix:** Based in Washington state. Created numerous popular strains including Grease Monkey, Tropical Cookies, and Papa's Punch. Highly sought-after genetics.
- **Symbiotic Genetics:** Creators of the famous Purple Punch and its derivatives. Known for flavorful, potent crosses.
- **In House Genetics:** Popular American breeder known for Cake and Gelato crosses. High demand and limited availability.
- **Massive Seeds:** Known for unique crosses and stable genetics. Growing reputation in the North American market.
- **Fast Buds:** Specialized exclusively in autoflowering strains. Extensive catalog with high-quality auto genetics.

### Boutique and Craft Breeders

- **DJ Short:** Blueberry, Flo, Blue Moonshine. Old-school genetics with devoted following.
- **Subcool's The Dank:** Jesus OG, Jack the Ripper, Space Queen. Organic growing advocate.
- **Serious Seeds:** AK-47, Bubble Gum, Chronic. Smaller catalog, exceptional quality.

## Landrace Genetics

[[cannabis-landrace-strains]] are pure, naturally evolved cannabis varieties from specific geographic regions. They represent the original genetic diversity before modern breeding.

### Important Landrace Regions

**Asia:** Thai (sativa), Nepalese (highland indica), Hindu Kush (pure indica, foundation of most modern indicas), Lashkar Gah (Afghan hashish strain).

**Americas:** Colombian (Gold, Red, Black — used in Skunk #1), Mexican (Acapulco Gold, Oaxacan), Jamaican sativas.

**Africa:** Durban Poison ([[cannabis-strain-swazi-landrace-south-african-sativa-oner]] — one of few pure landraces widely in seed form), Malawi, Swazi.

### Landrace Preservation

Many landrace populations are threatened by introduced hybrid genetics. Seed banks maintain living collections, and organizations like the Cannabis Genetics Institute work on [[cannabis-breeding-basics|breeding programs]] and preservation efforts.

## Breeding Programs

### Commercial Breeding Goals

High THC, unique [[cannabis-terpene-profiles]], fast flowering, high yields, pest resistance, visual appeal, and [[cannabis-genetic-stability]].

### Breeding Techniques

- **Stabilization:** Inbreeding over multiple generations (F1-F6+) to fix traits
- **Backcrossing:** Crossing offspring back to a parent to reinforce traits
- **Cubing:** Three successive backcrosses to a single parent (87.5% genetics)
- **Selfing:** S1 seeds through self-pollination (feminized seeds from one plant)
- **Phenotype hunting:** Growing large numbers to find exceptional individuals

## Seed Storage and Viability

- **Storage:** Airtight container in refrigerator with silica gel packets. Label with strain, date, source.
- **Viability:** Fresh (<1 year) = 90-100% germination. 1-3 years = 70-90%. 5+ years drops significantly.
- **Germination test:** Place 10 seeds between damp paper towels at 70-80F. Check after 7-10 days. 80%+ indicates healthy seeds.

## Evaluating Seed Bank Quality

**Quality indicators:** Breeder reputation, genetic stability, germination rates, transparent lineage info, customer service.

**Red flags:** Unreasonably high THC claims (30%+), stolen genetics, inconsistent phenotypes, poor germination, no customer service.

Seed bank legality varies by jurisdiction:

- Check [[cannabis-regulations-by-state|local regulations]] before ordering
- Seeds may be legal to purchase but illegal to germinate
- International shipping carries legal risks
- Medical programs may have specific seed sourcing requirements
- [[cannabis-testing-and-quality|Quality testing]] of seeds and resulting plants helps ensure genetic integrity
