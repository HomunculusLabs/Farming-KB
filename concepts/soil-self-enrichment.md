## Soil Self-Enrichment

Soil self-enrichment is the principle that soil naturally improves its own
fertility, structure, and biological activity without human intervention.
Masanobu Fukuoka demonstrated this principle over decades of no-tillage,
no-fertilizer farming, showing that soil left to its own devices becomes
progressively richer, more porous, and more productive.

## The Living Soil

Fukuoka emphasized that soil is not dead mineral matter but a living ecosystem.
One gram of ordinary topsoil contains approximately 100 million bacteria,
yeasts, molds, diatoms, and other microorganisms. These organisms do not exist
without reason; each lives for a purpose, struggling, cooperating, and carrying
on the cycles of nature. Far from being dead and inanimate, the soil teems
with life.
