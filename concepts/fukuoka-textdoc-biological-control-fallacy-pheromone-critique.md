### Indirect Harm Is Invisible

Many insects that appear harmless in a direct sense are harmful indirectly through
their effects on other organisms. An insect that seems beneficial because it preys
on a crop pest may simultaneously harm other species that play important ecological
roles. The ladybug that eats aphids may also compete with or prey upon other
organisms that serve equally important functions in the ecosystem. Evaluating any
single species in isolation inevitably misses these indirect effects.
