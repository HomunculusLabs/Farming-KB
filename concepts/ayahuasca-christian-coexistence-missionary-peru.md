# Christian Coexistence with Ayahuasca Practice in Peru

## Overview

The relationship between Christianity and indigenous ayahuasca practice in the Peruvian Amazon is complex and often contradictory. R. Stuart's 2001 observations in the Ucayali region revealed that while Pentecostal, Adventist, and Catholic missionaries have gained many converts among indigenous communities, the public disavowal of shamanism often coexists with private continued practice of native religious traditions including ayahuasca consumption. This syncretic reality challenges simplistic narratives of religious conversion in the Amazon.
