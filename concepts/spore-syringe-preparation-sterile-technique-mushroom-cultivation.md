# Spore Syringe Preparation - Sterile Technique for Mushroom Cultivation

## Overview

Spore syringe preparation is the critical bridge between harvesting spore
prints and inoculating sterile substrate [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-bacterial-and-fungal-dominant-soil]] competitors. The
Jarrold method provides a detailed, low-cost approach using household and
readily available materials, emphasizing flame sterilization and respiratory
protection to maintain spore solution purity.

## Equipment and Materials

The following equipment is required for spore syringe preparation:

- **[[challenge-microorganisms-microwave-surface-sterilization]]
- **Pyrex or heat-safe glass container** for sterilizing water
- **Aluminum foil** for wrapping equipment
- **Protective breathing mask or clean handkerchief** to prevent
  contamination from the operator's breath

## Custom Long Needle Construction

The standard 4 cm syringe needle is often too short for inoculation through
filter cans. A longer needle can be fabricated by:

1. Removing the original needle from its green plastic holder using pliers
   or grips.
2. Obtaining thin stainless steel tubing from model shops, craft shops,
   or metal engineering suppliers.
3. Heating the tip of the steel tube before inserting it into the needle
   holder, which softens the plastic and creates a tight seal.
4. Ensuring the finished needle is long enough to pass completely through
   the filter can and reach the substrate below.

## Water Sterilization

The carrier water for the spore suspension must be sterilized before use:

1. Fill the Pyrex container with 80 mL of water and cover tightly with
   aluminum foil.
2. Place the syringe and needle (unassembled) on foil, wrap completely,
   and place alongside the water container.
3. Process in a pressure cooker at 15 psi for 20 minutes to sterilize
   both the water and the syringe components.
4. Allow all items to cool completely before proceeding. Handling
   equipment while hot risks thermal damage and can compromise sterility.

## Spore Suspension Preparation

### Loosening Spores from the Print

Spores deposited on the bottom of a glass jar do not readily disperse into
water. The scraper is used to free them:

1. Flame-sterilize the flat-ended metal scraper by passing it through the
   spirit burner flame until glowing.
2. Allow the scraper to cool briefly (a few seconds) to avoid heat-killing
   the spores.
3. Insert the sterilized scraper into the spore jar and gently scrape the
   bottom to loosen the deposited spores.
4. Add the cooled sterile water to the jar and swirl to disperse the spores
   into suspension.

### Loading the Syringe

1. Assemble the syringe and flame-sterilized needle.
2. Pass the needle through the flame to re-sterilize before each jar access
   and before each inoculation.
3. Draw the spore suspension into the syringe through the sterile needle.
4. Replace the lid on the spore jar immediately after each refill to prevent
   contamination.
