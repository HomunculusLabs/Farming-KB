# PF TEK Mushroom Extraction Techniques

## Overview of Psilocybin Extraction

Psilocin, being the dephosphorylated form, is less polar and more readily
soluble in organic solvents including ethyl alcohol. When mushrooms are
consumed, the body's alkaline phosphatase enzymes cleave the phosphate group
from psilocybin, converting it to psilocin — the form that crosses the
blood-brain barrier and produces psychoactive effects.

The alcohol extraction process draws out both psilocybin and psilocin from the
dried fungal matrix. Because 200 proof (100% pure) ethyl alcohol contains no
water, it avoids introducing moisture that could promote degradation of the
alkaloids during the extraction and concentration phases. PF specifically
recommends 200 proof over 190 proof (95% ethyl, 5% water) for this reason.

## The PF Alcohol Extraction Process

### Step 1: Material Preparation

Begin with quality [[4-hydroxyindole-tryptamine-synthesis-building-block]] technique that involves
treating growing mushrooms with tryptamine precursors, but the basic solvent
extraction principles remain similar.

Compared to acid-base extraction methods commonly used for other tryptamines
(DMT, mescaline), the PF technique is notably simpler. Acid-base extractions
typically involve defatting with a nonpolar solvent, acidifying the aqueous
layer to convert freebase alkaloids to water-soluble salts, extracting with
water, basifying, and finally pulling with a nonpolar solvent. The PF method
skips this complexity because psilocybin and psilocin are already readily
soluble in ethyl alcohol without pH manipulation.

The role of acidity in extraction efficiency is noteworthy. Psilocybin, as a
phosphate ester, is naturally acidic and water-soluble. In acid-base
extractions of other tryptamines, acidification converts the freebase form
into a water-soluble salt. With psilocybin/psilocin, the phosphate ester
group already provides this water solubility, which is why a simple alcohol
soak achieves effective extraction without needing acidification steps.

## Safety Considerations

Pure ethyl alcohol is highly flammable — keep all flames and ignition sources
away from the extraction and evaporation areas. Perform evaporation in a
well-ventilated space. Despite the flammability, any fumes generated are
harmless because the alcohol is a non-poisonous, drinkable spirit (unlike
denatured alcohol or other solvents used in chemistry).

Use only 200 proof ethyl alcohol — not isopropyl, methanol, or denatured
alcohol. These other solvents are toxic and should never be used for
extractions intended for human consumption. PF notes that 200 proof anhydrous
ethyl alcohol can be procured from well-stocked liquor stores in the United
States, sometimes over the counter and sometimes with a simple state permit.
