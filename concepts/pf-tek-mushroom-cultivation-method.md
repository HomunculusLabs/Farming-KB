## Substrate Preparation

The substrate preparation follows a precise procedure:

1. Measure 2 parts vermiculite by volume into a mixing bowl. Add 1 part water
   and mix thoroughly until the vermiculite is evenly moistened but not
   waterlogged. Excess water should be drainable but the mix should feel damp.

2. Add 1 part brown rice flour to the moistened vermiculite. Mix gently but
   thoroughly to distribute the flour evenly. Avoid overmixing, which compacts
   the substrate and reduces air space.

3. Fill each jar to within 1/2 inch of the rim with the substrate mixture.
   Level the surface without compressing. Wipe the rim clean with a paper towel.

4. Fill the remaining 1/2 inch with dry vermiculite as a contamination barrier.
   This dry layer prevents airborne contaminants from reaching the moist
   substrate during inoculation.

5. Place the metal lid on the jar with the rubber seal facing up. Secure with
   the metal ring band. Poke 4 small inoculation holes through the lid,
   equally spaced near the edges. Cover the holes with breathable tape
   (micropore tape or masking tape).
