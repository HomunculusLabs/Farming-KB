### Effects on Artistic Expression

Janiger's data revealed several consistent patterns in the effects of LSD on artistic production:

- **Enhanced color perception**: Artists consistently reported and depicted more vivid, saturated color experiences during their LSD sessions.
- **Changes in spatial perception**: Distortions of perspective, proportion, and spatial relationships were commonly observed in the artwork produced during sessions.
- **Increased fluidity and spontaneity**: Many artists reported that LSD reduced their habitual patterns of artistic production and allowed more spontaneous, less self-critical expression.
- **Symbolic and archetypal imagery**: The artwork produced during sessions frequently incorporated symbolic, mythological, and archetypal elements consistent with Jungian depth psychology.

### The Celebrity Dimension

The location of Janiger's practice in Los Angeles and his willingness to work with entertainment industry professionals meant that his study attracted participants from the highest levels of American popular culture. This celebrity dimension had several consequences:

The Southern California location of the Cohen and Janiger program was significant for several reasons:

- **Climate of experimentation**: The relative social liberalism of the Los Angeles area created a permissive environment for unconventional research.
