## NMR Characterization of Psilocin and Psilocybin

## Overview

The Shirota, Hakamata, and Goda (2003) paper provides comprehensive
NMR characterization of psilocin (compound 1), psilocybin (compound 2),
the [[jadam-ambient-temperature-principle]].

## General Experimental Conditions

- **1H and 13C NMR**: TMS was used as the internal standard for
  compounds measured in CDCl3. For psilocybin measured in D2O, the
  solvent residue peak (HDO) was used for the 1H NMR reference, and
  one drop of methanol was added as the 13C NMR reference.
- **31P NMR**: [[shirota-glyoxalylamide-side-chain-construction-psilocin-synthesis]]:

- 7.90 ppm (1H, br s): Indole NH (H-1)
- 7.05 ppm (1H, d, J = 8.0 Hz): H-6
- 6.86 ppm (1H, dd, J = 0.8, 8.0 Hz): H-7
- 6.84 ppm (1H, d, J = 2.4 Hz): H-2
- 6.56 ppm (1H, dd, J = 0.8, 8.0 Hz): H-5
- 2.94 ppm (2H, m): H2-1' (alpha-methylene of side chain)
- 2.70 ppm (2H, m): H2-2' (beta-methylene, adjacent to nitrogen)
- 2.38 ppm (6H, s): N(CH3)2

The H-2 proton shows small meta-coupling to H-1, while the benzene
ring protons show ortho and meta couplings consistent with a
1,2,3-trisubstituted benzene pattern.

### 13C NMR (CDCl3, 100 MHz)

Key carbon resonances for psilocin:

- 152.1 ppm: C-4 (bearing the hydroxyl group) -- strongly deshielded
- 139.0 ppm: C-7a (ring junction)
- 123.5 ppm: C-6 (CH)
- 120.8 ppm: C-2 (CH, adjacent to NH)
- 117.5 ppm: C-3a (ring junction)
- 114.6 ppm: C-3 (quaternary, bearing side chain)
- 106.4 ppm: C-5 (CH)
- 102.4 ppm: C-7 (CH)
- 61.6 ppm: C-2' (CH2, adjacent to nitrogen)
- 45.3 ppm: N(CH3)2 (2 x CH3)
- 25.1 ppm: C-1' (CH2, alpha to ring)

The C-4 resonance at 152.1 ppm is characteristic of a phenolic carbon.
The side chain carbons appear at typical aliphatic positions.

## Psilocybin (Compound 2) NMR Data

### 1H NMR (D2O, 400 MHz)

- 7.22 ppm (1H, d, J = 7.6 Hz): H-7
- 7.18 ppm (1H, s): H-2
- 7.13 ppm (1H, t, J = 7.6 Hz): H-6
- 6.98 ppm (1H, d, J = 7.6 Hz): H-5
- 3.44 ppm (2H, t, J = 7.2 Hz): H2-2' (beta-methylene)
- 3.28 ppm (2H, t, J = 7.2 Hz): H2-1' (alpha-methylene)
- 2.86 ppm (6H, s): N(CH3)2

Notable changes from psilocin include downfield shift of the side chain
protons due to the phosphate group at C-4. The H-2 proton appears as a
singlet because exchange with D2O eliminates the NH coupling.

### 13C NMR (D2O + MeOH, 100 MHz)

- 146.4 ppm: C-4 (split, P coupling, bearing OPO3H2)
- 139.4 ppm: C-7a
- 124.8 ppm: C-6
- 123.3 ppm: C-2
- 119.1 ppm: C-3a (split, P coupling)
- 109.5 ppm: C-5a (split, P coupling)
- 108.6 ppm: C-3 (quaternary)
- 108.4 ppm: C-7
- 59.7 ppm: C-2' (CH2, shifted downfield vs. psilocin)
