## Contamination in Mushroom Cultivation

Contamination by competing microorganisms is the most common cause of failure in
mushroom cultivation at all scales from hobby to commercial. Understanding
contaminant biology, prevention strategies, and remediation approaches is
essential for achieving consistent results. The cultivation environment is
inherently favorable for microbial growth: warm temperatures, high humidity,
and nutrient-rich substrates attract a wide range of competitor organisms.

## Fungal Contaminants

### Trichoderma (Green Mold)

Trichoderma species are the most destructive contaminants encountered in
mushroom cultivation facilities worldwide. They produce prolific green spores
that spread rapidly through air movement and can quickly colonize an entire

population management in commercial facilities.
