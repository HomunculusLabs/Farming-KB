## Construction Materials

Readily available and appropriate materials include:

- **Rice straw** — thatched roofing and wall material, excellent insulation
- **Grass** — thatch for roofing
- **Dried leaves** — additional roofing layer over old rice bags
- **Bamboo** — structural framing and shelf supports
- **Tree branches** — alternative structural framing
- **Old rice and grain bags** — woven polypropylene bags used as roofing underlayment
- **Shade mesh** — external shade cloth to regulate light and temperature

## Roof Construction

Layer materials from outside in:

1. Shade mesh or palm fronds (outermost, for sun protection)
2. Rice straw or grass thatch (insulation layer)
3. Old rice bags or plastic sheeting (waterproofing)
4. Dried leaves (additional insulation)

This multi-layer approach provides shade, insulation from tropical heat, and protection from rain while allowing airflow.

## Wall Construction

Walls should be semi-permeable to allow ventilation while excluding pests. Options include:

- Bamboo lattice with rice straw or grass infill
- Woven bamboo mat walls
- Old rice bags stretched over bamboo frames

Leave openings for doors and windows that can be covered with mesh or removable panels for airflow control.

## Flooring

- **Cement floor** (preferred for hygiene and durability) — 4m x 5m x 200mm
- **Compacted earth** (lowest cost, functional but requires more maintenance)
- A water trench around the perimeter limits entry of ants and other insects while allowing wheelchair access

## Interior Shelving Systems

Several rack systems work for tropical mushroom houses:

- **Traditional bamboo shelves** — split bamboo poles on bamboo uprights, cheapest option
- **Suspended systems** — ropes or chains hanging from the roof structure, useful for bag cultivation
- **Steel racks** — heavier duty, used in commercial operations but higher cost

Shelf height must allow seated workers to access the highest level. Aisles must accommodate wheelchair or tricycle movement.

## Pasteurization Setup

A country-style pasteurization system can be made from:

- Two 200-liter oil drums arranged as a steam chamber
- Old rice bags for insulation and cover
- Gas burner or wood fire beneath (many farmers use branches instead of purchased fuel)

## Site Selection

- Land above flood level
- Good natural ventilation
- Access to clean water
- Shade from trees or structures
- Close to the cultivator's residence for easy monitoring
