## Common Bacterial Contaminants

### Bacterial Blotch (Pseudomonas)

- **Appearance:** Slimy, wet-looking patches on the mushroom cap surface, often
  brownish or yellowish discoloration
- **Cause:** Excess moisture on mushroom surfaces combined with Pseudomonas bacteria
- **Prevention:** Reduce surface moisture, improve air circulation, avoid direct
  spraying on mushroom caps
- **Action:** Affected mushrooms are still edible after thorough cooking, but the
  blotch cannot be reversed

### Wet Spot / Sour Rot

- **Appearance:** A foul-smelling, wet, discolored area in the substrate, usually
  appearing during early colonization
- **Cause:** Bacteria such as Bacillus species that survive steam sterilization,
  particularly in overly wet substrates
- **Prevention:** Ensure proper substrate moisture content and adequate sterilization
  time
- **Action:** The jar is lost — bacterial contamination cannot be treated. Dispose
  of the contaminated jar without opening it to prevent [[fruiting-chamber]]
- **Prevention:** Maintain proper FAE (fresh air exchange), avoid excessive humidity
  condensation on mushroom surfaces
