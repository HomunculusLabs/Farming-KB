## Implications for National Parks

The Mana Pools experience raises difficult questions for park
management worldwide. If we cannot control overgrazing by controlling
numbers, how do we control time in fenced or bounded areas? What
territories and migration routes do animals require? How can predator
functions be maintained or simulated? Should tourism priorities be
subordinated to ecological health?

## Lessons for Game Ranching

The [[savory-game-ranching-and-wildlife-conversion]] wildlife conversion concept -- substituting
game for livestock -- was one of Savory's early ideas. However, game
without natural predators does not behave naturally and can be as
damaging as domestic stock. Game ranching must account for the time
factor and the need for herd movement. Simply removing cattle and
replacing them with game does not automatically improve land health.
