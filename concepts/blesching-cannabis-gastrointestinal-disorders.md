## Gastro-Esophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)

### Overview
Commonly known as heartburn and acid reflux disease, GERD is due to damage of
the esophageal mucous membrane and esophageal sphincter. When the closing
action of the sphincter is temporarily incomplete, stomach acid reaches
unprotected tissue. Symptoms include heartburn, regurgitation, pain when
swallowing, nausea, vomiting (especially in children), and chest pains.

### Cannabis and GERD
Dutch researchers (2009) noted that 10-mg doses of THC given to dogs and
humans significantly reduced symptoms of GERD such as meal-induced transient
lower esophageal sphincter relaxations and spontaneous swallowing. However,
dosage levels of 20 mg caused some volunteers to experience nausea, vomiting,

### Cannabis and Appetite
- 11 studies reviewed, combined CHI Value: 28
- It is now confirmed that endocannabinoids, acting at brain CB1 cannabinoid
  receptors, stimulate appetite and ingestive behaviors
- 117 people living with HIV/AIDS received Dronabinol -- majority experienced
  improved appetite and reduced weight loss
- Dronabinol study (Tulsa, Oklahoma): 139 AIDS patients -- increases in
  appetite (38% vs. 8% for placebo), improvement in mood (10% vs. -2%), and
  decreased nausea (20% vs. 7%). Body weight stable with Dronabinol vs. mean
  loss of 8.8 pounds with placebo
- THC is an effective appetite stimulant in patients with advanced cancer at
  well-tolerated low doses (2.5 mg orally, three times per day)
