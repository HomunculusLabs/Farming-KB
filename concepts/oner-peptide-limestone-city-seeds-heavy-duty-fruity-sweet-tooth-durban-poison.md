## Peptide — (Heavy Duty Fruity × Sweet Tooth #3) × Durban Poison

## Overview

Peptide is a sativa-dominant hybrid originally bred by Limestone City Seeds, part of the Royal Canadian Marijuana Collective (RCMC) based in Canada. With over 15 years of growing and breeding experience, the RCMC team created Peptide as an indoor-focused strain that also performs well outdoors, even in the colder Canadian climate. The strain combines three notable parent lines — Durban Poison, Heavy Duty Fruity, and Sweet Tooth #3 — into a heavy-yielding, resinous plant with a distinctive sweet citrus and cotton candy aroma profile.

## Breeder Information

- **Breeder**: Limestone City Seeds
- **Collective**: Royal Canadian Marijuana Collective (RCMC)
- **Country**: Canada
- **Experience**: 15+ years growing and breeding
- **Focus**: Creating great indoor strains with access to premium genetics

## Genetic Lineage

### Parent Cross
Peptide's genetics are structured as a three-way cross:

```
Heavy Duty Fruity × Sweet Tooth #3  →  Mom plant
                    ×
              Durban Poison         →  Dad plant
                    ↓
                  Peptide
```

### Parent Characteristics
- **Mom plant (Heavy Duty Fruity × Sweet Tooth #3)**: Described as an especially monstrous plant with huge nugs of exceptional potency
- **Dad plant (Durban Poison)**: Slightly smaller and more restrained than the mother, but still a capable yielder with notable [[query-how-do-i-grow-figs-in-cold-climates]] should be cautious about particularly cold nighttime temperatures
- **Support requirements**: Stake plants during the vegetative period — buds become heavy enough to cause branches to bend or break during mid-flowering if unsupported

### Feeding
Not specifically detailed in the source, but given the heavy-yielding genetics (Heavy Duty Fruity, Sweet Tooth #3), a robust feeding program during flowering is likely beneficial.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

- **Aroma**: Sweet citrus and cotton candy — a distinctive and appealing combination
- **Smoke quality**: Described as "the kind that massages your insides as you inhale" — smooth and rich
- **Resin contribution**: The heavy crystal coating from the Durban Poison father likely enhances both the aromatic intensity and the smoothness of the smoke

## Potency and Effects

- **THC content**: 16%
- **Dominance**: Sativa-dominant
- **Expected effects**: While not explicitly detailed, the Durban Poison lineage suggests an uplifting, energetic, and cerebral high typical of African sativa genetics. The Sweet Tooth #3 contribution may add a relaxing body component that balances the pure sativa effect.

## Comparative Notes

### Within the RCMC/Limestone City Seeds Catalog
Peptide represents the RCMC's approach of combining proven [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]]. The three-way cross structure is relatively uncommon and suggests intentional selection for combining yield (Sweet Tooth/Heavy Duty Fruity), resin (Durban Poison), and potency.
