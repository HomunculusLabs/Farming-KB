## Forensic Identification Methods

Shirota and colleagues noted that the identification of magic mushrooms using morphologic and microscopic analyses is "quite difficult without experts."

[[psilocybin-mushroom-identification]] requires specialized mycological training and is subject to the inherent variability of biological specimens.

Visual identification alone is unreliable because many non-psychoactive mushroom species closely resemble psychoactive ones.

Chromatographic methods provide more reliable and objective identification.

TLC is the simplest and most accessible method, suitable for rapid screening of seized material in field laboratories.

GC and HPLC offer higher resolution and quantification capability but require more sophisticated instrumentation.

All chromatographic methods require comparison with authenticated reference standards of known purity.
