## Compost and Organic Matter Management

## Why Compost Quality Matters

digging it in -- is riskier than heap composting because the C/N is harder to

The closed-system ideal is a myth for gardens, though a well-managed farm with
livestock can slowly increase overall soil organic matter. Every bit of organic
material must be recycled, including humanure, if a garden is to approach
self-sufficiency in organic matter.

## Manure Values and Handling

Manure quality varies enormously. Horse manure can range from 0.5 percent
nitrogen to 1.0 percent; chicken manure from poor to over 2 percent. Solomon
recommends using only the minimum amount of manure necessary to supply the soil
ecology's requirement for organic matter, and supplementing with COF for actual
plant nutrition. Sacked steer manure from garden centers has little fertilizing
value but does feed soil microbes and improve structure. Sacked chicken manure
(NPK approximately 4-3-2) is far better but should not be overused.
