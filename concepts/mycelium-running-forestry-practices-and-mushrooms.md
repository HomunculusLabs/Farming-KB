# Mycoforestry: Forest Management with Mushrooms

clear-cut portion was divided into test plots with 35,000 Douglas fir
and cedar seedlings. Treatments included:

- Trees replanted with and without mycorrhizal spore-mass inoculation
  (Glomus intraradices for cedars, Rhizopogon parksii for firs)
- With and without wood chip topdressing (approximately 1 gallon per
  tree)

After 10 months, mycorrhizal-treated trees showed a net 5 and 7 percent
increase in overall height and girth respectively -- significant given
the short timeframe.
