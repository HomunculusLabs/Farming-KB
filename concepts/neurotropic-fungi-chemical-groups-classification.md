# Neurotropic Fungi: Chemical Groups Classification

## Overview

Neurotropic (hallucinogenic, psychotropic, or "magic") fungi are
classified into four major chemical groups based on their active
compounds. Guzmán, Allen, and Gartz (2000) catalogued 216 species across
these groups, drawing on more than 450 bibliographic references. The
classification reflects fundamental differences in both chemistry and
pharmacology, with the first group (psilocybin-containing fungi) being
by far the largest and most diverse.

## Group 1: Psilocybin and Related Indoles

### Chemistry

This group contains fungi with **psilocybin** (4-PO-DMT), **psilocin**
(4-HO-DMT), **baeocystin** (4-PO-NMT), and **norbaeocystin**
(4-HO-NMT). These are tryptamine derivatives that act primarily as
serotonin 5-HT2A [[cannabinoid-receptor-agonists-antagonists-synthetic-reference-blesching]]. Psilocybin is a prodrug that is
dephosphorylated to the active psilocin after ingestion.

### Taxonomic Distribution

The 186 species span 13 genera, dominated by *Psilocybe* (116 species),
followed by *Gymnopilus* (14), *Panaeolus* (13), *Copelandia* (12),
*Hypholoma* (6), *Pluteus* (6), *Inocybe* (6), *Conocybe* (4),
*Panaeolina* (4), *Gerronema* (2), and single species each of
*Agrocybe*, *Galerina*, and *Mycena*. Not all species in these genera
are neurotropic; many Panaeoloideous fungi lack chemical studies.

with specimen age. This has practical consequences:

  0.84% in 1976 specimens

- Specimens kept for extended periods may produce no effects even at
  normal doses
- Exposure to fresh collections in enclosed spaces can produce
  psychoactive effects through [[mycovolatiles-fungal-volatile-compounds]] in the air

## Group 2: Ibotenic Acid Mushrooms

### Chemistry

These fungi contain **ibotenic acid** and **muscimol**. Ibotenic acid is
a glutamate receptor agonist that acts on the brain differently from
psilocybin — producing effects that include agitation, confusion,
visual distortions, and in some cases delirium. Muscimol, formed by the
decarboxylation of ibotenic acid, is the primary psychoactive compound.

### Species

Three *Amanita* species are recognized:

- **A. muscaria** — the fly agaric; red cap with white spots;
  mycorrhizal with *Pinus* and *Betula* in northern hemisphere forests;
  also found in pine plantations [[fukuoka-natural-farming-in-tropical-regions]] (Brazil, Colombia,
  Tanzania, Australia, New Zealand)
- **A. pantherina** — the panther cap; brown cap with white warts;
  [[psilocybe-bohemica-central-european]] forests
- **A. regalis** — the royal fly agaric; rare, yellowish-brown cap

These species are culturally significant as the sacred mushroom of
[[harner-amanita-muscaria-siberian-shamanism]] among the Chukchee and Koryak peoples, where the
practice of drinking the urine of *A. muscaria* consumers concentrates
and purifies the active compounds.
