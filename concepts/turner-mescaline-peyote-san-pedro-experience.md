## Material and Classification

cactus species, most notably Peyote (Lophophora williamsii) and
San Pedro (Trichocereus pachanoi). Several other members of the
Trichocereus family also contain mescaline, including T.

peruvianus, T. taquimbalensis, T. tersheckii, T. validus, and T.
werdermannius. T. peruvianus is reported to be several times as
