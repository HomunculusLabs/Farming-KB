# Turner on Mescaline: Peyote, San Pedro, and Shamanic Sacraments

## Material and Classification

Mescaline is a naturally occurring psychedelic found in several
cactus species, most notably Peyote (Lophophora williamsii) and
San Pedro (Trichocereus pachanoi). Several other members of the
Trichocereus family also contain mescaline, including T.
bridgesii, T. cuzcoensis, T. fulvinanus, T. macrogonus, T.
peruvianus, T. taquimbalensis, T. tersheckii, T. validus, and T.
werdermannius. T. peruvianus is reported to be several times as
potent by weight as other Trichocereus species.

## The Mescaline Experience

Turner considers mescaline his favorite of the traditional
psychedelics (LSD, psilocybin, mescaline). Key qualities:

- **Lucidity of acid without the metallic edge:** Mescaline
  provides a focused, penetrating ability of mind while feeling
  totally relaxed, even calmer than mushrooms.
- **Visual richness:** Mescaline tends to be more visual than
  mushrooms or acid, especially with synthetic mescaline.
- **Auditory hallucinations:** Unlike LSD or psilocybin which
  heighten hearing, mescaline can produce auditory
  hallucinations and alter sounds significantly.
- **Olfactory enhancement:** Sharpens smell more than other
  traditional psychedelics.
- **Evolutionary connection:** Like psilocybin, mescaline links
  one with collective evolutionary consciousness more than
  synthetics like LSD. The experiences feel "significant,"
  ancient, spiritual, and personal.
- **Unique signature:** A feeling that gods or protective
  allies are smiling down. Duration: 6 to 14 hours.
- **Smooth return:** The comedown is smoother than other
  traditional psychedelics, without the "neural overload"
  feeling possible after intense acid trips.

### Whole Cactus vs. Synthetic

Whole cactus produces a more body-oriented high. San Pedro is
very smooth and flowing. Peyote is quite different due to its
unique alkaloid mixture: the first hours are dream-like and
almost delirious. Some element acts as an emetic, making most
people nauseous about two hours in.

### The Extraterrestrial Feeling

When overtaken by full-strength mescaline, Turner felt more than
with any traditional psychedelic that he was an extraterrestrial
being experiencing new sensory phenomena for the first time.
This quality, along with aphrodisiacal properties, distinguishes
mescaline from other psychedelics.

## Combinations

- **Belladonna alkaloids** are reputed to potentiate mescaline.
- Mescaline may be significantly intensified and possibly
  dangerous if combined with MAO inhibitors.
