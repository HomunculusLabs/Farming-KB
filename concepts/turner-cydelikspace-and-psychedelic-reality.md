# Turner CydelikSpace and Psychedelic Reality

Turner's metaphysical framework describing "CydelikSpace" — a universal state of consciousness accessible through deep psychedelic experiences.

## What is CydelikSpace?

- A state visited through psychedelics, correlated to but distinct from Gibson's fictional "cyberspace"
- "Not a fictional dimension. It is accessible now, and even appears to be the underlying reality behind all existence"
- Vast — "appears to contain all matter and energy in all of its manifestations since the beginning of time"
- Contains thought; "it may be thought that gives birth to matter"

## Personal Experience

### Complete Life Review
- "Every thought that has been formulated in my mind throughout my entire lifetime"
- Each perspective through which life was viewed, each experience had
- "My entire life laid out in suspension before me" — could wander through previous perspectives as detached observer
- Four-dimensional view: "easily recalling in full detail perspectives and perceptions back through early childhood"
- "With the precision of someone analyzing graph charts displaying data, yet with full emotional connection"
- Like the near-death experience of life flashing before eyes, but lasting for hours and allowing reflection

### Universal Repository
- Contains "all thoughts and experiences of every human, animal, plant, and molecular life form"
- Including individual cells and galactic star systems
- Other lives experienced with almost as much detail as one's own
- Examples: child's wonder feeling dew, extraterrestrial body during lovemaking, planet's soul over millions of years

### Unmanifest Possibilities
- "Also contains all thoughts which did not occur but could have"
- The possible actions that did not manifest "are infinite, and branch out endlessly"
- Contains visions the artist "never dreamed in the wildest fits of imagination"

## Key Properties

### Non-Duality
- "In CydelikSpace it is clear that you and I are not two"
- "The detailed dreams of our separate lives are entirely contained within it"

### Dimensionless Origin
- "All matter, all energy, all movement, is still contained in a dimensionless point"
- "The Big Bang and the unfolding of the universe has not yet occurred"
- "A seething mass of unlimited possibilities contained within a nucleus"

### Paranormal/Pre-cognitive Elements
- Occasional experiences that later manifested in consensus reality
- Events experienced at times/places where not present — confirmed to have actually occurred
- "Only a very small percent" have parallels in consensus reality
