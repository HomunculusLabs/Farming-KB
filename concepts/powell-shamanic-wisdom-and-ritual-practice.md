 Shamanic Wisdom and Ritual Practice

Simon G. Powell examines shamanic traditions as sophisticated systems of
knowledge that have been built up over millennia through the careful,
ritualised use of entheogenic plants and fungi. Far from being "primitive"
practices, Powell argues that indigenous shamanic cultures have developed a
profound understanding of consciousness, ecology, and the relationship
between the human mind and the natural world.
