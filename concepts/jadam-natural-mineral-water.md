# JADAM Natural Mineral Water

## Overview

JADAM considers water the single most important input in farming. Since 95% of crops like cucumber, tomato, watermelon, and strawberry consist of water, water quality directly determines 95% of crop quality. Similarly, 95% of microorganisms are water, so water determines 95% of microbial health. JADAM provides a simple method for [[comparison-leaf-mold-composting-vs-hot-composting-method]] and rocks.

## The Importance of Water

Water is the "door to life" and the primary indicator for the existence of life. Key facts:
- 70% of the human body is water (90% for internal organs and brain)
- 80% of fish is water
- 95% of microorganisms are water
- 95% of high-water-content crops (cucumber, tomato, watermelon, strawberry) is water
- Water molecules have strong polarity, making them excellent solvents

JADAM asserts: "If you use the wrong water, your farming will fail." Yet most farmers pay no attention to water quality despite spending money on expensive bottled water for their own health.

## The Body-Plant Connection

Experience shows that drinking spring water for a few days makes skin smoother -- cells become healthier with the change in water. JADAM's principle: "Our bodies and plants are one." The same water quality that benefits human cells benefits plant cells.

## Farm-Made Natural Mineral Water

JADAM's method for creating mineral water on the farm:

1. Use existing large water containers on the farm
2. Fill about 1/3 of a gunnysack with leaf mold
3. Hang the bag in the container so contents sink into the water
4. Bring rocks from the vicinity and cover 20-30 cm (8-12 in) at the bottom
5. Change the leaf mold bag 1-2 times per year

Cotton gunnysacks are not suitable because cotton breaks down from microbial activity. Indigenous microbes from the leaf mold purify the water just as microbes purify contaminants in natural water bodies. After 2-3 days, microbes act on polymers (contaminants) and the water becomes excellent.

## Uses

The treated water can be used for:
- Culturing microorganisms (JMS)
- Irrigation
- Applying pesticides
- All farming water needs

- Water container with valves for controlling volume
- Separate containers for microorganism culture and [[jadam-animal-byproduct-liquid-fertilizer]] by providing trace minerals that support the fermentative microorganisms involved in JLF production. When preparing [[ph-and-nutrient-availability-garden-soils]] and light, but excessive algae can be managed by partially shading the container or using it more quickly. If plants show no improvement after switching to mineral water, check that the leaf mold is fresh and biologically active, and consider whether other factors such as soil compaction or nutrient imbalances may be limiting plant health independently of water quality.
## Mineral Water for Seed Treatment

JADAM recommends soaking seeds in natural mineral water before planting to improve germination rates and seedling vigour. Seeds soaked for 6-12 hours in mineral water absorb trace minerals that support the initial growth phase before the seedling develops its root system sufficiently to access soil minerals. This practice is particularly beneficial for seeds of [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] -- rock-based mineral supplementation
