# Blanching Vegetables for Freezing

Updated: 2026-04-18

Blanching is a critical step before freezing vegetables. It slows or stops the enzymes that cause vegetables to lose flavor, color, and nutritional value during frozen storage. Without blanching, frozen vegetables quickly become brown, wilted, and off-flavored. The process involves briefly cooking vegetables in boiling water or steam, then rapidly cooling in ice water.
