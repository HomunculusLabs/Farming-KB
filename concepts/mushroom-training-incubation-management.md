## Daily Monitoring Procedures

During incubation, the following monitoring tasks should be performed:

1. Visually check mycelium on a daily basis looking for abnormal mycelium (black spots, green
   spots, brown spots, orange or red spots)

2. Try to identify the causes of any abnormal mycelium, which could indicate pest or disease

3. Keep bags clean at all times

4. Allow natural pest control where possible

5. Identify and separate contaminated bags immediately

6. Manually remove all garbage from the incubation area

7. Keep good and clear records of mushroom production observations

## Handling Contaminated Bags

When contamination is detected, several options are available:

- Separate contaminated bags and pasteurize again
- For partially contaminated bags, open them to expose the contaminated area
- Tear the plastic bags off and reuse the sawdust substrate
- Ferment substrate as compost for gardening
