## Handling Contaminated Bags

When contamination is detected, several options are available:

- Separate contaminated bags and pasteurize again
- For partially contaminated bags, open them to expose the contaminated area
- Tear the plastic bags off and reuse the sawdust substrate
- Ferment substrate as compost for gardening
