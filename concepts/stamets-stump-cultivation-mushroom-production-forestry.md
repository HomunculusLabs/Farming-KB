## Published Research on Stump Cultivation

Pagony (1973) described the cultivation of Oyster mushrooms (Pleurotus ostreatus) on large diameter poplars with a 100% success rate. Inoculations occurred in the spring for fruitings which began in the ensuing fall, and continued for several years hence. An average of four pounds of Oyster mushrooms were harvested over four years (approximately 1 lb./year/stump).

Hilber (1982) reported on the utility of using natural wood (logs and stumps) for growing Oyster mushrooms, finding that per cubic meter of elm wood, the yield from one season averaged 17-22 kilograms.
