## Reported Results

### Neurotic Character Disorders

The most consistently reported results were for neurotic character disorders:

- **Success rate:** Approximately 85%
- **Duration of treatment:** Typically 20-40 sessions over several months
- **Follow-up:** Improvements maintained at follow-up periods of 1-5 years
- **Comparison:** Far superior to conventional psychotherapy for comparable conditions

The 85% success rate for neurotic disorders is one of the most impressive outcomes ever reported in [[psilocybin-psychotherapy-research-history]]. For comparison:

- Conventional psychoanalysis: ~40-60% success rate (depending on criteria)
- Cognitive-behavioral therapy: ~50-70% success rate (depending on condition)
- Psycholytic therapy: ~85% success rate

### Other Conditions

Psycholytic therapy was also applied to:

- **Depression:** Good results, particularly for treatment-resistant depression
- **Anxiety disorders:** Excellent results for anxiety related to neurotic character structure
- **Obsessive-compulsive patterns:** Moderate to good results
- **Personality disorders:** Mixed results; some personality types responded better than others
- **Sexual dysfunction:** Good results when related to psychological factors

### Terminal Illness

The treatment of terminal patients with high-dose psychedelic therapy produced particularly dramatic results:

- Dramatic reduction in fear of death
- Improved quality of remaining life
- Better relationships with family members
- Greater acceptance of mortality
- Some patients reported [[psilocybin-mystical-experiences-personality-change]] that transformed their relationship to death

These results have been replicated in modern trials by researchers at Johns Hopkins and NYU.
