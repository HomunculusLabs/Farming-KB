---# Cannabis and Wound Care

## Overview

Historically cannabis has been applied topically in the form of poultices,
plasters, salves, tinctures, and oils to treat slow-healing wounds and
skin ulcers (as in "diabetic foot" in turn-of-the-century American
medicine). The [[blesching-cannabis-dermatological-conditions]] for additional
dermatological applications.

### Key Takeaways

- Cannabis has a long historical tradition of topical use for wound
  healing, with CHI value of 32 across 10 studies
- Cannabis-infused honey combines honey's antibacterial and
  tissue-regenerating properties with cannabis's anti-inflammatory effects
- Hempseed oil provides omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids that reduce
  dryness, itching, and inflammation in atopic dermatitis
- The endocannabinoid system modulates inflammatory responses to both
  acute and chronic injuries, supporting a role in wound care
- Topical cannabis applications offer targeted delivery with minimal
  systemic effects
- Individual therapeutic windows vary; starting low and increasing slowly
  is the recommended approach
