Primordia have low tolerance to CO2 and need ample fresh air exchange.
During spawn running, CO2 levels of 10,000-15,000 ppm are beneficial,
but for pinning this must be reduced to below 1,000 ppm for most
species. The drop in CO2 is accomplished by increasing fresh air

pinning. While mycelium has no light requirement, many species need

stimulation at specific wavelengths for as little as half a millisecond
per day can cause primordia to form in photosensitive species.
