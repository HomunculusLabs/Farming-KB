- **Purpose**: Verify compound identity by confirming that HPLC peak fractions co-migrate with authentic psilocybin and psilocin standards on TLC

### Role of TLC in the Analytical Workflow

TLC serves as a rapid, low-cost orthogonal confirmation method. While HPLC provides precise quantification based on retention time and peak area, TLC adds a layer of confidence by separating compounds using a completely different mechanism (adsorption chromatography on silica rather than partition chromatography on C18). Agreement between the two methods provides strong evidence that the HPLC peaks are correctly assigned to psilocybin and psilocin rather than to co-eluting impurities or degradation products.
