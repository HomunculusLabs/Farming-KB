## Smartshop and Online Mushroom Markets

## Overview

The [[legal-responses-to-hallucinogenic-mushrooms-in-the-european-union]] Europe was dominated by two countries: the **Netherlands** and the **United Kingdom**. Smartshops, market stalls, and online retailers created the infrastructure that drove the [[mckenna-distillation-and-the-birth-of-synthetic-drugs]] not yet placed under legal control.

### Dutch Smartshop Landscape

- Estimated **120 to 150 smartshops** in the Netherlands (exact number unknown)
- Concentrated in Amsterdam
- Sell legal, predominantly natural products including [[1960s-counterculture-and-magic-mushrooms]] estimated to constitute **50% of smartshop turnover** (2000 Dutch Risk Assessment)
- 64% of Dutch users aged 18+ who used mushrooms in the last year purchased them in smartshops (2001 survey)

### Geographic Distribution

- **82%** of online shops were based in the **Netherlands**
- **18%** from other countries: 4 Austrian shops (growkits and sporeprints), 2 German sites, 1 UK site (linked to a Dutch shop), 1 Polish site (primarily cannabis, mushrooms unavailable at time of survey)

### Website Traffic (One Dutch Shop, May 2004 to February 2006)

- Netherlands: 73%
- Belgium: 14%
- Germany: 2%
- France: 2%

### Products Sold

Online shops offered a range of products:

- Fresh mushrooms (various species and varieties)
- Spore prints and spore syringes
