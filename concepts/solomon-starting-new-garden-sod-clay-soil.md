## Converting Sod to Garden

New gardens are almost always made in areas previously growing
grasses, which form dense networks of tough roots called sod that
firmly hold soil together. Grass is tenacious — it won't die and rot
from being turned over once and will attempt to regrow.

### Timing

In most climates, the best season for starting a new garden is
not spring but late summer/early autumn, anticipating the next
spring. This allows turned sod time to decompose over winter. In
mild climates (Deep South, California, Australia), the work can be
done any time of year when soil moisture is right.
