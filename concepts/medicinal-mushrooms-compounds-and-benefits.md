## Key Medicinal Mushroom Species

### Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum)

Known as Lingzhi in Chinese medicine, reishi has the longest documented
history of medicinal use among mushrooms. Primary effects include immune
modulation, anti-inflammatory activity, and potential anti-cancer
properties. Reishi is typically consumed as a hot water extract (tea)
or dual extract (water plus ethanol) to capture both water-soluble
beta-glucans and alcohol-soluble triterpenes.

### Shiitake (Lentinula edodes)

Shiitake produces lentinan, a beta-glucan approved as an adjuvant
cancer treatment in Japan. It also contains eritadenine, which may help
lower cholesterol. Regular consumption is associated with improved
immune function and cardiovascular health.

### Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus)

Primarily studied for neurological benefits. The hericenones and
erinacines it contains stimulate NGF production. Studies suggest
potential benefits for cognitive function, nerve repair, and mood
regulation.

### Turkey Tail (Trametes versicolor)

Turkey tail is one of the most researched medicinal mushrooms. Its
beta-glucan polysaccharopeptide (PSP) and polysaccharide-K (PSK) are
used as cancer adjuvant therapies in several countries. PSK is
prescribed in Japan as an approved cancer treatment adjunct.
