## Microclimate Zones Within the Spiral

The genius of the herb spiral is that it creates multiple distinct
microclimates within a single structure. The top of the spiral is the
driest and warmest area, receiving the most sun and having the best drainage.
The bottom is the coolest and wettest, with moisture collecting at the base.
The north side (in the northern hemisphere) is shaded and cooler, while the
south side receives full sun exposure. This variation allows herbs with very
different growing requirements to coexist in a compact space.
