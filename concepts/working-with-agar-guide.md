# Working with Agar Guide

Agar work is the foundation of mushroom strain selection, cloning, and long-term culture maintenance. This guide covers the complete workflow from media preparation through culture storage.

## What Is Agar

Agar is a polysaccharide extracted from red algae cell walls. When dissolved in boiling water and cooled, it forms a stable gel. It is used in mycology as a gelling agent for [[laminar-flow-hood-diy-construction]] or still-air box:

## Preparing Agar Slants (for Storage)

1. Dissolve ingredients in a pot (mix agar with cold water first to prevent lumps)
2. Fill tubes with 5-6 ml of medium
3. Cap loosely
4. Sterilise 25 minutes at 121°C
5. Cool on a sloped surface to maximise surface area
6. Solidified slants are ready for inoculation
