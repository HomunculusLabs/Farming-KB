# Cropping Containers and Growing Systems

Choosing the best type of cropping container depends on several
variables: the mushroom species being cultivated, the cultivator's
experience and resources, and the equipment and facility available.
Different growing systems have evolved for different mushroom types,
each with distinct advantages and limitations.

## Wood-Based Cultivation Systems

For outdoor cultivation, logs and stumps serve as natural cropping
containers. Hardwood logs are inoculated with sawdust or plug spawn
and incubated in shaded, moist environments. This low-technology
approach can produce mushrooms for several years with minimal
maintenance. Burying logs in sawdust or soil helps preserve moisture
and extends the fruiting period.

Stumps can also be inoculated, although if other fungi have already
captured that niche, production is inhibited. In outdoor
environments, the first flushes are often delayed, not showing for
several years after inoculation. However, since polypores are
naturally lower in moisture and require less water, outdoor patches
require less maintenance than indoor methods.
