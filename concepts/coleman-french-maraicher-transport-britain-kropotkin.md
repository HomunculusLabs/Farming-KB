## Joseph Fels and the Mayland Experiment

A wealthy American businessman, Joseph Fels (of Fels-Naptha soap
fame), became a key promoter of the French system in Britain. Fels
believed firmly in the benefits of small farming and easier access
to the land for ordinary people. He purchased six hundred acres at
Mayland, Essex, England, to be subdivided into numerous mini-farms
for intensive French-style production.

Fels's foreman, Joseph Smith, wrote one of the best instruction
