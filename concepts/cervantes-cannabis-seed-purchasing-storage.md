## Choosing a Seed Company

The most secure approach is to order from a company you can contact by
telephone. A qualified representative should provide good answers to your
questions about specific strains. Key evaluation criteria:

- Companies with email addresses and websites are usually okay to order
  from, but verify they respond promptly
- Call several companies and ask specific questions about the strains they
  sell
- Check strain guides and grow reports for real-world performance data
- Research seed company ratings on independent review sites

## Security Precautions for Ordering

If you live in a country where seeds are illegal, take extra precautions:

- **Telephone calls** - call from a public telephone using a calling card.
  Never call the seed company from a telephone located at a grow house
- **Email communication** - can leave a trail to your computer if you do
  not use a proxy server. Set up proxy server software for anonymous
  browsing
- **Payment** - do not use a credit card if security is a concern. While
  companies normally bill discreetly and do not charge cards as "Cannabis
  Seeds," cash tells no tales and leaves no trails
- **Cash orders** - wrap cash in carbon paper so that it cannot be seen
  through the envelope
- **International money orders** - provide an alternative to credit cards
  with some anonymity

## Germination Strategy

Buy ten seeds, and germinate half of them at once. Then, when and if they
germinate successfully, start the rest of the seeds. This staggered
approach preserves backup genetics if the first batch fails.

### Optimal Conditions
