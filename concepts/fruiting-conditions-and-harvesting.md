# Fruiting Conditions and Harvesting

## Overview

The fruiting stage begins when colonized substrate cakes are transferred from
their jars into the dual chambered terrarium. This is where the actual
mushrooms develop and mature. Proper environmental conditions, particularly
humidity, temperature, and light, are essential for successful fruiting.
Understanding when and how to harvest is equally important for maximizing both
yield and potency.

## The Birthday: Transferring to the Terrarium

The moment the fungus cake is removed from the jar is referred to as the
"birthday." The best time for this is when primordia (tiny worm-like structures
with reddish heads) appear on the cake while still inside the jar. The cake is
slid out onto an old jar lid that serves as a base, then any water droplets on
the cake are daubed with tissue paper. The cake goes immediately into the
terrarium for the fruiting cycle.

## Environmental Requirements

### Humidity

High humidity is the single most important factor for fruiting. The fungus
needs to bathe in a shroud of floating water molecules. Near 100% humidity is
the target. The dual chambered terrarium achieves this through the spray shield
system, which molecularizes water spray into a fine mist that envelops the
cakes without directly wetting them.

Symptoms of low humidity include:
- Fuzzy white [[spore]] collection and display.

### Aborts

Abhorts are mushrooms that cease growing, developing convoluted caps, gnarly
stems, and stunted growth. Despite their unappealing appearance, they are
described as primo in magic alkaloids and even more powerful than the normal
stately specimens. They should be harvested before their heads turn black.

### Mutant Blobs

Along with abhorts, blobs of fungus with little or no cap will appear. These
mutants are also good for harvesting and contribute to the overall yield of
potent material.

### Primordia

Over half of the small primordia that first form will abort. These tiny baby
mushrooms are among the most potent specimens. A pointed knife blade works
well for removing them.

## Harvesting for Potency

### The Golden Rule

The secret to potent mushrooms is harvesting them young. Scientific evidence
shows that small immature specimens are significantly more potent than larger
mature ones. The recommended approach is to harvest all specimens while their
caps are still round and before the veil under the cap breaks.

### Identification of Young Specimens

Young, potent mushrooms are characterized by:
- Very light color with white stems and light colored caps
- Conical shape with caps that have not yet spread out
- Light-colored gills on the underside of the cap
- Sporulation has not yet begun

### Identification of Mature Specimens

Mature specimens, which are past their prime for consumption, show:
- Darker colors with dark bluish tones on caps and stems
- Cap margins that turn up like an inverted umbrella
- Gills darkening to deep brown
- Purple spore deposits visible on the stem

### When to Harvest

Just before or right after the veil breaks is a good time to harvest. The cap
will begin to spread after the veil breaks. The mushroom will be smaller and
its head will be roundish. The immature specimens are the best in quality,
digestibility, and potency.
