## Overview

Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is defined by symptoms of inattention, poor concentration, distractibility, hyperactivity, and poor impulse control. The CDC estimates 1 in 10 children (ages 3-17) have been diagnosed with ADHD, with boys three times more likely to be diagnosed. Up to 5% of the US adult population has ADD. - 76 cannabis-using patients with ADHD: men used cannabis to treat inattention, women to improve sleep quality

## Therapeutic Considerations

### Strain Selection
- ECS modulation suggests balanced cannabinoid profiles may be optimal
- CBD normalizes social behavior and reduces hyperactivity
- THC (Dronabinol) appears to calm without sedating
- Individual titration essential — effects on attention span are less clear than effects on hyperactivity

### Dosing
- CBD at 3 mg/kg showed effects in animal studies
- Human dosing not yet standardized
- Start low, go slow approach recommended

## Conventional Treatment Concerns

- Standard ADHD medications (Adderall, Desoxyn, Ritalin, Strattera) are essentially legal stimulants
