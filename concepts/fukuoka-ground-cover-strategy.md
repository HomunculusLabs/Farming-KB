# Fukuoka's Ground Cover Strategy

Ground cover is the backbone of Masanobu Fukuoka's [[cotter-outdoor-mushroom-garden-mulch-beds]]

## The Scientific Case Against Bare Soil

Fukuoka anticipated modern soil science by decades:

- Bare soil loses carbon to the atmosphere through oxidation
- Bare soil is vulnerable to erosion by rain and wind
- Bare soil loses moisture through evaporation
- Bare soil heats up, killing beneficial microorganisms near the surface
- Bare soil loses its crumb structure and becomes compacted
- Bare soil is colonized by pioneer weed species that are difficult to manage

By maintaining continuous ground cover, all of these problems are prevented simultaneously, without any additional inputs or labor.
