## Sativex

### Composition and Formulation

Sativex is unique among prescription cannabinoids because it is not a synthetic compound but rather a standardized whole-plant extract. Developed by GW Pharmaceuticals, Sativex is formulated as an oromucosal spray containing approximately equal parts THC and CBD, delivered in a 1:1 ratio. This formulation preserves the broad spectrum of cannabinoids and terpenes found in the cannabis plant, making it the pharmaceutical preparation most similar to whole-plant medicine.

Blesching specifically notes that "with the exception of Sativex, which is essentially a plant-derived tincture, pharmaceuticals that contain THC do not contain any of the other biologically active components of cannabis that may play an important therapeutic role in the human body."
