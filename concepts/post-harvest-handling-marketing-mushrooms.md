### Heavenly Mushrooms (Hed Sawan)

Ingredients: 300g dried shredded mushrooms, 400g palm sugar, white soya
sauce, 100g sesame seeds, 1 liter vegetable oil, 2 teaspoons ground
pepper. Dry fresh oyster mushrooms in sun. Fry in hot oil until browned.
In a separate pan, cook soya sauce with palm sugar until sticky. Add
pepper, fried mushrooms, and sesame seeds. Mix, cool, and store.

### Pickled Mushrooms with Soya Sauce

Ingredients: 1 kg fresh mushrooms, 700g water, 50g salt, 50g white soya
sauce. Clean mushrooms, cut large ones into pieces, and blanch. Place
loosely in glass jars. Add salt and soya sauce to brine, pour into jars,
steam to remove air, and tightly close lids.

## Seasonal Business Considerations

During rice harvesting season and the rainy season, demand for cultivated
mushrooms drops as [[edible-wild-mushrooms-by-region]] become available. Many farmers stop
production during this period, but certain mushroom types still have
market demand. Processing can be profitable during the rainy season.
For continuous year-round income, stagger production so bags are always
at different stages of the cultivation cycle.
