## Frost Protection Methods

### Cloches

Cloches are individual protective coverings that
keep plants warm at night. A simple cloche is a
milk container with the bottom cut off and the lid
removed. Placed over a plant, the plastic captures
and retains heat while allowing ventilation through
the open top. You can make cloches out of wax
paper, glass, and jars, or buy commercial units
made of rigid transparent plastic or heavy-duty
wax paper.
