## When to Collect Pollen

Pollen collection begins just prior to anther dehiscence, when the
pollen sacs on male flowers are swollen but have not yet opened. One
large, healthy male top provides all the pollen necessary for small-scale
breeders to produce ample seed for their own use.

Strip away unnecessary branches from the selected male to guard against
accidental random pollination. Isolate the male as soon as anthers show,
because airborne [[cannabis-sativa-flowering-cycle-management]]. Indica-dominant males tend to flower faster than
sativa-dominant males. A breeder working with both types must time
pollen collection carefully to have viable pollen available when the
female plants are receptive.

Fresh pistils are ready to pollinate from two to twelve weeks after
flowering is induced, depending on the variety. The more pistils on
the bud at the time of pollination, the more seed will be produced.

## Sieving and Cleaning Pollen

After collection, the pollen should be cleaned to remove leaf matter and
anther debris that may have fallen into the bag. Pass the collected
pollen through a fine screen or sieve. Place wax paper under the screen
to catch the clean pollen. This debris can cause the pollen to spoil if
not removed.

## Storage Containers

Clean pollen is placed in a small coin envelope or sterile test tube for
storage. A sterile scraper should be used to handle the pollen.
Cleanliness is essential throughout the process to prevent contamination
and premature spoilage.
