## Food Production in Suburbia

Suburban food production can supply a significant portion of household needs. Intensive vegetable gardens, fruit trees, berry bushes, and small animal systems (chickens, rabbits, bees) can transform a suburban block into a food-producing asset.

The [[urban-farming-methods]] addresses scaling up beyond the single household.

Home food production tends to foster rather than compete with small commercial organic growers serving local markets. This synergistic relationship strengthens the overall local food system.

## Energy Systems for Suburbia

Retrofitting suburban energy systems includes installing solar photovoltaic panels, solar hot water heaters, and improving household energy efficiency through insulation, draft sealing, and efficient appliances.
