## Backcross Breeding Methods

Backcross breeding is a type of breeding that involves repeated crossing
of progeny with one of the original parental genotypes. Cannabis
breeders most often cross progeny to the mother plant. This parent is
known as the recurrent parent. The non-recurrent parent is called the
donor parent. Backcross breeding has become one of the staple methods
clandestine cannabis breeders use, mainly because it is a simple, rapid
method when using greenhouses or grow rooms, and requires only small
populations.

## Goals of Backcross Breeding

The principal goal of backcross breeding is to create a population of
individuals derived mainly from the genetics of one single parent (the
recurrent parent). The donor parent is chosen based on a trait of
interest that the recurrent parent lacks. The idea is to introgress this
trait into the backcross population, such that the new population is
comprised mainly of genetics from the recurrent parent, but also
contains the genes responsible for the trait of interest from the donor
parent.

The backcross method is suitable for adding new desirable traits to a
mostly ideal, relatively true-breeding genotype. The recurrent parent
should be a highly acceptable or nearly ideal genotype (for example, an
existing commercial cultivar or inbred line).

## Incorporating a Dominant Trait

When the desired trait from the donor parent is dominant, backcrossing
is straightforward:

- **Step 1:** Recurrent Parent x Donor Parent = F1 Hybrid generation
- **Step 2:** Select desirable plants showing the dominant trait, and
  hybridize selected plants to the recurrent parent. The resulting
  generation is denoted BC1 (some cannabis breeders denote this BX1)
- **Step 3:** Select plants from BC1 and hybridize with the recurrent
  parent; the resulting generation is denoted BC2
- **Step 4:** Select plants from BC2 and hybridize with the recurrent
  parent; the resulting generation is denoted BC3

After three backcrosses (BC3), approximately 93.75% of the genome comes
from the recurrent parent, with only 6.25% from the donor parent.
