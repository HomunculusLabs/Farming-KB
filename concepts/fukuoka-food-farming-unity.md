## Vegetables Closest to Wild Ancestors

Vegetables that are biologically closest to their wild ancestors are the best in flavour and the highest in food value. In the lily family (which includes nira, garlic, Chinese leek, green onion, pearl onion, and bulb onion), the nira and Chinese leek are highest in nutrition, good as herbal medicine, and also useful as a tonic for general well-being.
