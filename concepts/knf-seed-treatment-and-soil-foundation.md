    10|# Seed Treatment and Soil Foundation with Microbial Activator (M-A)
    11|
    12|**Updated:** 2026-04-18
    13|
    14|Microbial Activator (M-A) is the [[knf-preparations]] input used for establishing the biological foundation of soil and preparing seeds for planting. M-A is made from a combination of [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture]] and other biological inputs, diluted and applied at a standard ratio of 1:1000. It serves as the bridge between the microbial inputs and the growing crop, activating enzymes, vitalizing germination, and building soil health before planting begins.
    15|
    16|## What Is M-A
    17|
    18|M-A is a microbial-rich liquid preparation that combines:
    19|
    20|- [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture]] (the indigenous microorganism culture)
    21|- Additional biological activators (yeast, LAB, or similar)
    22|- Water for dilution
    23|
    24|The term "Microbial Activator" reflects its primary function: activating the biological processes in soil and seeds that enable healthy plant growth. By inoculating both the soil environment and the seed itself with beneficial microorganisms, M-A establishes the conditions for vigorous, disease-resistant crops from the very start.
    25|
    26|## Soil Foundation Application
    27|
    28|### Purpose
    29|
    30|Applying M-A to the soil before planting establishes a living biological foundation:
    31|
    32|- Introduces diverse beneficial microorganisms into the soil
    33|- Activates enzyme systems that break down organic matter and release nutrients
    34|- Creates conditions favorable for root colonization by beneficial microbes
    35|- Suppresses soil-borne pathogens through competitive exclusion
    36|- Improves soil structure through microbial polysaccharide production
    37|
    38|### Application Rates
    39|
    40|- **Standard application:** 1-2 tons of M-A (diluted 1:1000) per 100a (approximately 1000 square meters)
    41|- **Degraded or depleted soil:** Increase to 3-4 tons per 100a for the first application
    42|- **Maintenance application:** 1-2 tons per 100a for established soils with existing microbial populations
    43|
    44|### Method
    45|
    46|1. Prepare the M-A solution at 1:1000 dilution
    47|2. Apply as a soil drench, saturating the top 5-10 cm of soil
    48|3. Apply 2-3 hours before sunset or on a cloudy day to protect microorganisms from UV radiation
    49|4. Allow 1-2 weeks for microbial populations to establish before planting
    50|5. Can be combined with [[knf-fermented-mixed-compost]] (FMC) application for maximum soil building
    51|
    52|### When to Apply
    53|
    54|- Before planting season, as part of general soil preparation
    55|- After soil disturbance (tillage, grading) to restore microbial populations
    56|- After periods of flooding or drought that may have damaged soil biology
    57|- As an emergency treatment when soil shows signs of biological degradation
    58|
    59|## Seed Treatment
    60|
    61|### Purpose
    62|
    63|Soaking seeds in M-A before planting:
    64|
    65|- Activates enzymes within the seed that initiate germination
    66|- Vitalizes the germination process for faster, more uniform emergence
    67|- Inoculates the seed surface with beneficial microorganisms that will colonize the emerging root system
    68|- Provides initial protection against soil-borne pathogens
    69|- Promotes vigorous root generation from the earliest stage of growth
    70|
    71|### Seed Treatment Procedure
    72|
    73|1. Prepare the M-A solution at 1:1000 dilution
    74|2. Place seeds in the solution, ensuring they are fully submerged
    75|3. Soak for the appropriate duration based on germination speed (see table below)
    76|4. Remove seeds from the solution and plant immediately
    77|
    78|### Soaking Duration by Germination Speed
    79|
    80|| Germination Speed | Example Seeds | Soaking Time |
    81||------------------|--------------|-------------|
    82|| Very fast | Potato, taro, ginger, garlic | 30 minutes to 1 hour |
    83|| Fast | Chinese cabbage, radish, bean | Approximately 2 hours |
    84|| Average | Cucumber, melon, squash | 4-5 hours |
    85|| Slow | Rice, barley, tomato | 7-8 hours |
    86|
    87|**Note:** Do not exceed the recommended soaking times. Over-soaking can damage seeds by allowing water to penetrate too deeply, causing the embryo to suffocate or the seed coat to rupture prematurely.
    88|
    89|### Seedling Treatment
    90|
    91|For transplanted seedlings:
    92|
    93|1. Soak seedling roots in the M-A solution (1:1000) for 10 minutes before transplanting
    94|2. After transplanting, water the seedlings with the same M-A solution used for treatment
    95|3. This double application ensures both root colonization and soil inoculation around the newly planted seedling
    96|
    97|### Results of Seed Treatment
    98|
    99|- **Uniform germination:** Seeds treated with M-A emerge at roughly the same time, creating an even stand
   100|- **Vigorous root generation:** Treated seeds develop stronger, more extensive root systems
   101|- **Good establishment:** Seedlings establish more quickly and with less transplant shock
   102|- **Even growth:** Plants grow more uniformly throughout the season, making management easier
   103|
   104|## Disease Control Application
   105|
   106|M-A can also be used as a disease intervention during the growing season:
   107|
   108|- If disease occurs while plants are growing, apply 1-2 tons of M-A (1:1000 dilution) per 100a along with other natural farming materials
   109|- The beneficial microorganisms compete with and suppress pathogenic organisms
   110|- Apply as both soil drench and foliar spray for comprehensive treatment
   111|
   112|## Emergency Weather Response
   113|
   114|M-A serves as an emergency response plan for abnormal weather conditions:
   115|
   116|- **Low temperature protection:** Apply M-A when unexpected cold threatens crops. The microbial activity generates slight warmth in the root zone and helps plants maintain metabolic activity during cold stress.
   117|- **Abnormal weather during harvest:** Apply M-A when unusual weather patterns threaten the harvest. The microbial support helps plants maintain vigor and complete their reproductive cycle.
   118|
   119|## Specific Crop Disease Control
   120|
   121|M-A has demonstrated effectiveness against specific diseases:
   122|
   123|- **Apple tree canker:** Apply 2 tons of M-A (1:1000) per 100a as a soil drench around affected trees
   124|- **Fusarium wilt of melon and watermelon:** Apply M-A as a soil treatment before planting and as a foliar/soil drench if symptoms appear
   125|
   126|## Fruit Tree Application
   127|
   128|For fruit trees, M-A provides both soil foundation and disease protection:
   129|
   130|- Apply 2 tons of M-A (1:1000) per 100a immediately after harvest (post-harvest application)
   131|- This timing allows the microbial populations to establish during the dormant season
   132|- The soil foundation supports root growth and nutrient cycling in preparation for the following growing season
   133|- Combine with [[knf-fermented-mixed-compost]] for comprehensive post-harvest soil care
   134|
