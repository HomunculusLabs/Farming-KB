# ALEPH (DOT) — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Methylthioamphetamine

## Overview

ALEPH (PIHKAL #3), also designated DOT (para-DOT), holds a special place in psychedelic chemistry history as "probably the first sulfur-containing phenethylamine to have been evaluated as a potentially active CNS stimulant or psychedelic." The introduction of sulfur (as a methylthio group) into the phenethylamine framework was a completely novel concept at the time, and Shulgin's extreme caution during initial testing — starting at 0.25 micrograms — reflects both the unknown territory and the anxiety of discovery.

ALEPH pioneered the exploration of sulfur-containing psychedelics, which eventually led to the [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]].

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylthioamphetamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #3
- **Code names:** ALEPH, DOT (para-DOT)
- **Class:** Substituted amphetamine (thioether series)
- **HCl melting point:** 204-205 °C (recrystallized from IPA)

## Experience Reports (from PIHKAL)

### At 5 mg

> The initial hints of action were physical — warming of first the legs, and then a comfortable warmth spread over the entire body. Intense intellectual stimulation...14 pages of handwritten notes...almost entirely non-verbal. The afterglow was benign and rich in empathy.

The production of 14 pages of notes, described as "almost entirely non-verbal," suggests that ALEPH's cognitive effects operate in a mode distinct from ordinary analytical thought — perhaps closer to a form of non-linguistic ideation or pattern recognition. The benign, empathic afterglow contrasts with the intense intellectual stimulation of the peak.

### At 7 mg

> Uneventful +1.

A minimal response, suggesting that the threshold between 5 mg and 10 mg is quite steep for this compound.

### At 10 mg

> There was a rapid shift of frame of reference that made simple tasks such as reading and tuning the radio quite alien...mechanical things, such as arranging flowers or alphabetizing names, are beyond me. Driving would be impossible.

The disruption of mechanical and routine cognitive tasks at 10 mg is notable. The fact that telephone conversation remained coherent while physical-world tasks became impossible suggests a dissociation between social cognition and procedural motor planning — an effect profile not commonly emphasized in other psychedelic compounds.

### Independent Assay at 10 mg

A colleague independently assayed ALEPH at 10 mg and reported "only mild effects which he found relatively uninteresting." This inter-individual variability is common among psychedelic phenethylamines and underscores the importance of [[lsd-set-and-setting-framework]].
