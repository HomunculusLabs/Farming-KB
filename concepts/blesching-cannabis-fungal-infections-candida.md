## Candida albicans

Candida albicans is a fungus implicated in vaginal yeast infections and
oral thrush. In immunocompromised patients, particularly those with
AIDS, oral Candida albicans infections are common and can be resistant
to standard pharmacological treatments.

### Allopathic Treatment Challenges

U.S. doctors routinely prescribe Fluconazole, a pharmacological
antifungal agent, to AIDS patients suffering from oral Candida albicans
inflammations. However, they found it often ineffective, leaving patients
with no relief. In 2002, scientists at Detroit's Wayne State University
School of Medicine discovered that oral solutions of tea tree oil appeared
to be effective when Fluconazole failed to work.
