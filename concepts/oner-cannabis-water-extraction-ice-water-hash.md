## Bubble Hash Grades

Water extraction naturally separates hash into different grades based on micron size:

- **Full-melt** (lower micron bags, typically 25-73 micron): Contains primarily
  intact trichome heads with minimal plant contamination. Melts completely when
  heated.
- **Half-melt** (mid-range bags, 90-120 micron): Contains some plant matter mixed
  with trichomes.
- **Cooking grade** (higher micron bags, 160-220 micron): Contains the most plant
  material; suitable for edibles rather than smoking.
