# AHCC (Active Hexose Correlated Compound)

AHCC is a proprietary extract derived from the mycelia of several
basidiomycete mushroom species, primarily cultured through submerged
fermentation. Unlike most mushroom anticancer polysaccharides, AHCC
contains low molecular weight alpha-1,3 glucan structures rather than

high molecular weight beta-glucans. This aligns with research on

## Dosage and Administration

Standard oral dosage: 3g per day. The oral route is a practical advantage
over lentinan and schizophyllan requiring parenteral
administration. Oral bioavailability is likely superior due to lower
molecular weight alpha-glucan composition.
