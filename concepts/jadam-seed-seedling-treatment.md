# JADAM Seed and Seedling Treatment

## Overview

JADAM uses [[comparison-leaf-mold-composting-vs-hot-composting-method]] as a seed and seedling treatment to boost root settlement and prevent disease. This simple, nearly free method replaces commercial root promoters by leveraging the millions of [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] and their excreta found in leaf mold.

## The Principle

Leaf mold is a reservoir of millions of different kinds of indigenous microorganisms, plus millions of different microbial excreta. It is a super nutrient-rich material. Using leaf mold treatment:
- Boosts biodiversity in the seed and seedling environment
- Effectively suppresses outbreak of diseases through microbial competition
- Promotes strong root settlement
- Costs essentially nothing (collected from nearby mountains)

## How to Collect Leaf Mold

Collect from mountains near your farm. The places with a thick layer of soil formed from leaf mold are best:
1. Remove the undecomposed leaves at the very top
2. Take the soft soil mixed with decomposing leaf debris from the underparts
3. Put in a black plastic bag or gunnysack
4. Store in shade; keep loosely closed (tight sealing not necessary)
5. Better if kept moist, but becoming dry is not a serious problem

Use about 500 grams each time. Collect every changing season, or bring a large amount for one year's use. Leaf mold from conifers is acidic -- if collecting from conifer areas, mix with leaf mold soil from two or three other places.

## Seed Treatment Method

1. Put seeds in a porous bag
2. Submerge in leaf mold solution (water + leaf mold) for approximately 30 minutes
3. Remove and plant immediately

## Seedling Treatment Method

### Small Scale
1. Prepare a large bucket
2. Put in about 3 kg of leaf mold
3. Add water up to the level where the root part of the seedling will be submerged
4. Allow seedlings to rest in the solution for 1-2 minutes so they are fully soaked
5. Transplant immediately

### Large Scale
1. Dig out a wide area of soil
2. Cover with plastic
3. Fill with water and add leaf mold
4. Dip seedlings as above

## If You Cannot Collect Leaf Mold

You can make it yourself:
1. Find an uncontaminated piece of land
2. Cut [[jadam-cover-crop-wild-grass-symbiosis]] and cover the soil with it
3. Keep it moist by frequently watering
4. Microorganisms in soil and air will propagate on the grass and soil surface
5. Soil becomes soft and usable as leaf mold in approximately 3 weeks (optimum conditions)

Using wet grass is better than straw because straw has low moisture content. This method works anywhere on earth.

## Disease Prevention Through Biodiversity

JADAM's view: canker and athlete's foot are essentially the same thing. Chemical pesticide cannot control canker; medicine rarely fixes athlete's foot. However, when you tackle them with microbial diversity, the disease-causing organisms are neutralized.

This is the fundamental JADAM principle applied to seed treatment: maximize microbial diversity so no single pathogen can dominate. The same microorganisms that protect soil health protect seed and seedling health.

## Connection to Soil Foundation Work

Seed and seedling treatment is the first step in establishing the microbial diversity that continues throughout the [[savory-growing-season-and-non-growing-season-management]]. It pairs with soil foundation work using JMS, seawater, phyllite solution, and JLF to create a comprehensive system of biological protection and nutrition.
