## JADAM Methods

JADAM (Jayondamul Agac Yeongu Moe) is an organic farming system developed by Youngsang Cho in South Korea. Its core principle: all inputs are self-made from locally available materials at near-zero cost. The goal is $100/acre/year total input cost. JADAM shares [[knf-preparations]] philosophy but uses different recipes and emphasizes ultra-low-cost pest control.

## Core Inputs

### JMS — JADAM Indigenous Microorganism Solution

The heart of JADAM. Cultures microbes from forest leaf mold using cooked potato as food.

**Recipe (500L / 132 gal batch)**:
- Leaf mold (from forest floor): 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
- Boiled potato: 1 kg (2.2 lb)
- Sea salt: 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
- Water: 500L (132 gal) — non-chlorinated
- Optional: 1 kg chopped wild grass/crop leaves (diversifies microbes)

**Process**:
1. Boil potato until soft, put in cotton bag with rocks, knead until fully dissolved into water
2. Put leaf mold in separate cotton bag with rocks, knead to extract microbes into water
3. Add sea salt to water
4. Cover container (not airtight — needs to breathe)
5. Ferment at ambient temperature (same as crops will experience)
6. 24 hrs at 28C (82F), 72 hrs at 25C (77F), 96 hrs at 19C (66F)
7. Ready when foam forms a clear circular disc at peak (pizza-like layer)
8. Filter through fine net before applying

**Application**:
- Dilute 1:10 minimum (more is safer)
- Apply with every watering — 3-4 times per month
- Also use with sea water and phyllite solution for full mineral program
- 500L serves 0.1 to 3.3 ha (0.25 to 8.2 acres)
- ph ~6.5 (potato medium, not acidic like sugar/molasses methods)

**Key principle**: Culture microbes at the same temperature as your crops — not at a fixed 32C. This produces microbes suited to your actual conditions.

### JWA — JADAM Wetting Agent

Natural surfactant for pesticide/foliar applications. Replaces chemical surfactants.

**Recipe**:
- Canola oil
- Caustic potash (KOH)
- Water
- (Specific ratios in JADAM book — process involves no heating)

**Use**: 3L per 500L water (0.6%) for foliar applications. Ensures even coverage and penetration. Has its own pesticide/germicide effect.

### JS — JADAM Sulfur

Natural germicide made from sulfur without heating.

**Recipe**:
- Sulfur
- Caustic soda (NaOH) with water
- Red clay powder, phyllite powder, sea salt

**Use**: Mix with JWA to make JS germicide. Effective against powdery mildew, downy mildew, canker, and fungal diseases. Takes 20 minutes to make.

### JHS — JADAM Herb Solution

Boiled herb extract used for insect control.

**Process**: Boil specific herbs in water. The herb solution is combined with JWA and/or JMS to make pesticides for specific pests (aphids, mites, thrips, stinkbugs, etc.).

### JNP — JADAM Natural Pesticide

Combination of JWA + JS + JHS (and optionally JMS). Can target:
- Powdery mildew, downy mildew (JS + JWA)
- Aphids, mites (JHS + JWA)
- Stinkbugs, thrips (specific JHS formulations)
- Slugs, snails (specific JHS formulations)

### JMW — JADAM Mineral Water

Made by immersing leaf mold in water with rocks. Slow mineral extraction for soil drench.

## JADAM Soil Management Philosophy

The core of JADAM is: **apply JMS, sea water, and phyllite solution every time you water.**

### Sea Water / Sea Salt
- Contains 83+ minerals including S, Mg, Ca, K, B, P, I, Se, Ge
- Dilute 30:1 to 100:1 with water (sea water is ~3% salt, plants want ~1%)
- 20L sea water (or 500g sea salt) per 0.1 ha (1/4 acre)
- Apply 3-4 times per month with JMS
- Improves sweetness, coloring, storage quality, and product value

### Phyllite Solution
- Fine-powdered phyllite rock contains nearly all inorganic nutrients
- Add 60 kg (132 lb) phyllite to 500L water, stir, let settle, use upper water
- Refill water and reuse for 1 year
- Direct application: 1 kg per 3.3 sq meters for severely mineral-depleted soils

### The JADAM Principle
"Shift from disinfection to diversity." Instead of trying to kill pathogens, maximize the diversity and population of beneficial soil microorganisms. When micro-ecology recovers its natural diversity, no single pathogen can dominate.

## JADAM vs. KNF

| Feature | JADAM | KNF |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Microbe source | Leaf mold + potato | Cooked rice in forest |
| Sugar source | Potato (no sugar/molasses) | Brown sugar/molasses |
| Cost focus | Ultra-low ($100/acre/yr) | Low (local materials) |
| Pest control | Core of the system (JNP) | Secondary (OHN, FPJ) |
| Fertilizer | Sea water + phyllite + JLF | FPJ, FAA, OHN, etc. |
| pH approach | Potato keeps ~6.5 | Brown sugar acidifies |

Both systems are compatible — many growers combine techniques from both.
