hardwood species include oaks, beech, elm, and various other hardwoods.
However, paper-barked hardwoods such as alder and birch are not recommended.
For outdoor logs, bury approximately one-third of the log into the ground in
a shady, humid location to maintain moisture levels. Walnut decomposes
relatively slowly, which can provide multiple years of continued fruiting
from a single inoculation.

substrate can be susceptible to contamination.
