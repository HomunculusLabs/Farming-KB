## Sterilization and Bag Preparation

Substrate-filled bags must be sterilized before inoculation. Proper sterilization is essential since G. frondosa is highly sensitive to contamination. Polypropylene bags with microfilter windows (such as those from Unicorn Imp. and Mfg. Corp.) are the standard container. The microfilter allows gas exchange while maintaining sterility. Bags should not be filled to the rim; ample air space above the substrate is necessary for oxygen supply and air exchange during the prolonged spawn run. Coarser sawdust or wood chips in the substrate must not puncture the bag.

## Spawn Run (30-42 days)
