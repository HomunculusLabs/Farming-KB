# Plant Stage Treatments

## Overview

Korean [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]] prescribes specific treatments at different stages of a plant's [[bloomfield-fungal-life-cycle-spiral-versus-circle]], based on the [[knf-preparations]].

## Stage 1: Vegetative Growth Treatment

## Stage 3: Reproductive Growth Treatment

## Stage 4: Sweetness Promotion

A specialized treatment for promoting ripeness and enhanced sweetness in fruits is applied near harvest time:

- [[water-soluble-calcium-wca]]: 2 ml per 1000 ml of water.

- [[oriental-herbal-nutrient-ohn]]: 1 ml per 1000 ml of water.

- [[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]]: 2 ml per 1000 ml of water.

- [[knf-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]] (SW): 30 ml per 1000 ml of water.

All ingredients are mixed in 1000 ml of water and applied during the drought period, before expected rains.

## General Dilution Guidelines

The following principles apply across all KNF stage treatments:

- Standard dilution is 1:1000 ml of water for most single-input applications.

- Specialized formulations follow specific multi-input recipes as detailed above.

- Always dilute inputs with clean, non-chlorinated water.

- Apply [[query-how-to-make-and-use-plant-based-foliar-sprays-for-pest-and-disease-prevention]] during early morning or late evening.

- For preparation details, refer to [[knf-troubleshooting]] for diagnostic support.
