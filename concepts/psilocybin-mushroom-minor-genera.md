## The Genus Hypholoma (Strophariaceae)

Six species listed in the Guzman/Allen/Gartz worldwide checklist.

Hypholoma naematoliformis is considered endemic.

The original "Hypholoma cyanescens" described by Maire from Algeria was reclassified as Psilocybe mairei.
