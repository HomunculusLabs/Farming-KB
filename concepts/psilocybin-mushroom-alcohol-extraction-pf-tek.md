# Psilocybin Mushroom Alcohol Extraction (PF TEK Method)

## Overview

The PF TEK alcohol extraction is a simple liquid extraction technique for
[[hplc-analysis-of-psilocybin-and-psilocin]] from dried mushrooms, described by Psilocybe Fanaticus
(Robert McPherson). It uses **200 proof ethyl alcohol** to dissolve active
alkaloids from pulverized dried mushroom material, producing a concentrated
liquid extract often called **"psilo-liqueur."** This approach offers advantages
in precise dosing, long-term storage, and more palatable consumption compared
to eating raw or dried mushrooms.

## Required Materials

- **200 proof ethyl alcohol** (absolute ethanol) — the critical solvent
- **Dried [[amanita-muscaria-legal-alternative-psilocybin-mushrooms-europe]]** — fully desiccated for maximum potency
- **Coffee grinder** — for pulverizing dried material into fine powder
- **Glass jars with lids** — for soaking mushroom material
- **Dust-pollen masks** (or fine coffee filters) — for filtering the extract
- **Glass baking dish** — flat surface for evaporation
- **Small electric fan** — to accelerate evaporation
- **Small glass vials with lids** — for final storage

## Step-by-Step Extraction Process

### Step 1: Pulverize the Dried Mushrooms

Start with fully desiccated mushrooms — any remaining moisture dilutes the
alcohol and reduces efficiency. Use a coffee grinder to pulverize into the finest
possible powder. Maximizing surface area is essential for thorough solvent
penetration and maximal alkaloid extraction.

### Step 2: Soak in Alcohol for 24 Hours

Place the powder into a clean glass jar and cover with 200 proof ethyl alcohol,
using enough to fully submerge the material with some excess. Seal tightly and
store in a cool, dark place for at least 24 hours. Agitate gently several times
to improve solvent contact. The alcohol dissolves both psilocybin and psilocin.

### Step 3: Filter Through Dust-Pollen Masks

Pour the mixture through a dust-pollen mask to separate liquid from solid
material. The mask captures fine particles while allowing the extract to pass.
Collect the filtered liquid in a clean glass container and squeeze the mask
gently to recover as much liquid as possible from the spent material.

### Step 4: Resoak the Extracted Material

Return the filtered mushroom material to the jar and cover with fresh 200 proof
ethyl alcohol for a second extraction cycle. Soak another 24 hours with periodic
agitation. Double-extraction significantly improves yield and ensures maximum
recovery of active compounds from the [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]].

### Step 5: Combine Extracts

Filter the second soak through a fresh dust-pollen mask. Combine with the first
extract in a clean container. You now have a raw alcohol extract containing the
full complement of alkaloids from your mushroom material.

## Evaporation and Concentration

Pour the combined liquid onto a clean, flat glass surface such as a baking dish.
Position a **small electric fan** to blow a gentle stream of air across the
surface. The fan accelerates evaporation without heat, which could degrade the
sensitive psilocin compound. Monitor closely — the goal is to concentrate to
your desired potency, not evaporate to dryness. Less remaining liquid means more
alkaloids per unit volume. Stop when the extract reaches a convenient dosing
concentration.

## Storage Recommendations

Store the finished psilo-liqueur in **small glass vials with tight-fitting lids**
in the **freezer**. Ethyl alcohol does not freeze at standard freezer
temperatures, so the extract remains liquid and ready after extended freezing.
Cold storage slows psilocin degradation, which is oxidation-sensitive. Properly
stored, the extract remains viable for months to over a year.

## Dosing Considerations

Track how much dried material went into the extract versus final liquid volume:
**1 gram per 1 oz (30 mL)** for a lighter dose, **2 grams per 1 oz** for
moderate, and **3+ grams per 1 oz** for a strong dose. Individual sensitivity
varies, and source potency fluctuates by strain, growing conditions, and flush.
Start smaller with a new batch. Liquid extract enables more precise and
consistent dosing than dried mushroom pieces.

## Procurement of 200 Proof Ethyl Alcohol

Obtaining 200 proof ethanol is critical — lower-proof alcohol contains water
that reduces extraction efficiency and complicates evaporation:

- **Over-the-counter** in some states (e.g., Washington) where 190-200 proof
  grain alcohol is sold legally at liquor stores
- **Permit required** in most states, through state liquor control boards or approved chemical supply vendors
- **190 proof Everclear** is widely available and acceptable, though 200 proof
  is preferred for maximum efficiency

Always verify local regulations before purchasing high-proof alcohol.

## Safety Notes

- **Flammability:** 200 proof ethyl alcohol is extremely flammable. Perform all
  steps in a well-ventilated area away from flames, sparks, or ignition sources.
  Never use heat to evaporate — the electric fan method is safe and sufficient.
- **Dosing accuracy:** Liquid extracts deliver concentrated doses in small
  volumes. Always label vials with the concentration ratio (grams per oz).
- **Secure storage:** Keep in child-proof containers in the freezer, out of
  reach of children and pets.
- **Legal status:** Psilocybin and psilocin are Schedule I in the United States
  and controlled in many countries. This information is for educational purposes.

## Comparison to Other Consumption Methods

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh mushrooms** | Full-spectrum, traditional | Unpredictable dosing, nausea |
| **Dried mushrooms** | Potent, storable | Unpleasant taste, imprecise dosing |
| **Mushroom tea** | Faster onset, less nausea | Shorter duration, heat degrades actives |
| **Alcohol extract** | Precise dosing, long storage | Requires prep, flammable solvent |

Alcohol extraction eliminates consumption of fungal tissue — the primary source
of gastrointestinal discomfort. The liquid form enables precise dose titration
compared to dried mushrooms, where potency varies cap-to-stem and batch-to-batch.

## The Concept of "Psilo-Liqueur"

Psilocybe Fanaticus coined the term **"psilo-liqueur"** to describe the finished
extract. The name reflects the liqueur-like appearance and intent as a measured,
sip-able preparation. Properly concentrated, it appears as a clear to slightly
amber liquid with the earthy aroma of psilocybin in an alcohol base. Some
practitioners add flavoring agents or herbal tinctures to mask the taste, though
the alcohol itself provides a familiar profile many prefer over raw mushroom
tissue.
