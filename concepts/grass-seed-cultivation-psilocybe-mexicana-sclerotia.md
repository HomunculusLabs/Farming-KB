# Grass Seed Cultivation of *Psilocybe mexicana* and Related Species

## Overview

Grass seed provides an excellent substrate for cultivating *[[psilocybe-mexicana]]*, *P. tampanensis*, and *[[panaeolus-subbalteatus]]*. This method is particularly notable because it supports the formation of **sclerotia** (truffle-like underground masses that contain psychoactive compounds) in *P. mexicana* and *P. tampanensis*, in addition to normal mushroom fruiting bodies.

## Substrate Preparation

### Ingredients (per 720 ml jar)

- **110 grams** grass seed (rye grass, *Lolium perenne*, or mixtures of species).
- **180 ml** water.

### Seed Selection

- Use **untreated** grass seed — fungicide-treated seed will not work.
- Check packaging for treatment indicators.
- Inexpensive seed can be found at animal feed stores (sold as bird feed).

### Procedure

1. Fill jars with grass seed and water.
2. Place lids on jars but **do NOT screw tight** — lids must remain loose.
3. Cover with a **double layer of crumpled tinfoil** over the lid and top of the jar.
4. **Sterilise** by autoclaving at 15 psi pressure.
5. Allow to cool completely before inoculation.
6. Inoculate with mycelium from agar culture.
7. Incubate at appropriate temperature until fully colonised.

## Sclerotia

Sclerotia are dense, hardened masses of mycelium that serve as survival structures. In cultivation:

- They form **underground** within the substrate.
- Contain psilocybin and psilocin (psychoactive compounds).
- *P. mexicana* and *P. tampanensis* sclerotia are commonly sold as "magic truffles" in countries where they are legal.
- Harvest by breaking open the colonised jar and separating sclerotia from the grain substrate.
- Sclerotia can be stored for extended periods when dried.

## Advantages of Grass Seed

- Readily available and inexpensive.
- Good water retention.
- Supports both fruiting body and sclerotia production.
- Smaller grain size allows efficient colonisation.
