# Companion Planting Reference

Companion planting leverages synergistic relationships between plants to improve growth, repel pests, attract [[comparison-beneficial-insects-guide-vs-integrated-pest-management]], and maximize space. This reference covers classic combinations, pest-repelling partnerships, and incompatible pairings.

## Classic Combinations

### Three Sisters (Corn, Beans, Squash)
- **Corn** provides a trellis for beans to climb.
- **Beans** fix atmospheric nitrogen, feeding corn and squash.
- **Squash** shades soil, suppresses weeds, and its prickly vines deter pests.
- Plant corn first (6 in tall), then beans 2 weeks later, squash 1 week after beans.
- Spacing: corn 12 in apart, beans at base of each corn, squash every 3-4 ft.

### Basil + Tomato
- Basil repels tomato hornworms, aphids, and whiteflies.
- Some gardeners report improved tomato flavor when basil is interplanted.
- Both love heat and full sun; compatible nutrient needs.
- Space basil 10-12 in from tomato base.

### Marigolds + Everything
- French marigolds (Tagetes patula) release compounds that repel root-knot nematodes in soil.
- Strong scent deters whiteflies, aphids, and cabbage moths.
- Plant throughout the garden, especially near tomatoes, peppers, and brassicas.
- Avoid planting marigolds near beans (allelopathic).

## Pest-Repelling Companions

| Companion Plant | Repels | Plant Near |
|----------------|--------|------------|
| Basil | Aphids, whiteflies, hornworms | Tomatoes, peppers |
| Borage | Tomato hornworms, cabbage worms | Tomatoes, squash, strawberries |
| Chives | Aphids, Japanese beetles | Roses, tomatoes, carrots |
| Dill | Aphids, spider mites, squash bugs | Cucumbers, lettuce, cabbage |
| Garlic | Aphids, cabbage loopers, Japanese beetles | Roses, brassicas, fruit trees |
| Marigold | Nematodes, whiteflies, Mexican bean beetles | Tomatoes, peppers, beans |
| Mint | Aphids, cabbage moths, flea beetles | Cabbage, tomatoes (contain in pots) |
| Nasturtium | Aphids, squash bugs, whiteflies | Tomatoes, squash, cabbage |
| Rosemary | Cabbage moths, bean beetles, carrot flies | Beans, cabbage, carrots |
| Sage | Cabbage moths, carrot rust flies | Cabbage, carrots, tomatoes |
| Thyme | Cabbage worms, whiteflies | Brassicas, tomatoes |
| Yarrow | Aphids, attracts predatory wasps | Near any [[hamilton-vegetable-bed-preparation]] |

## Trap Cropping

Trap crops attract pests away from your main crop:

| Trap Crop | Attracts | Protects |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Nasturtium | Aphids, whiteflies | Tomatoes, peppers, brassicas |
| Radish | Flea beetles | Eggplant, cabbage, broccoli |
| Chervil | Slugs | Lettuce, leafy greens |
| Blue Hubbard squash | Squash vine borers | Zucchini, summer squash |
| Mustard | Harlequin bugs | Cole crops |

## Incompatible Pairings

| Plant A | Plant B | Reason |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Beans | Onions, garlic, chives | Alliums stunt bean growth |
| Beans | Beets | Mutual antagonism, reduced growth |
| Tomatoes | Corn | Tomato fruitworm = corn earworm; share pest |
| Tomatoes | Fennel | Fennel is allelopathic to tomatoes |
| Tomatoes | Potatoes | Same pest complex (late blight, Colorado potato beetle) |
| Tomatoes | Cabbage | Tomatoes stunt cabbage growth |
| Cabbage | Strawberries | Mutual antagonism |
| Cabbage | Tomatoes | Growth inhibition |
| Peppers | Beans | Peppers can stunt bean growth |
| Carrots | Dill (when young) | Dill can cross-pollinate and stunt carrots |
| Onions | Beans, peas | Alliums inhibit [[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]] |
| Sunflowers | Potatoes | Sunflowers inhibit potato growth |
| Cucumbers | Sage | Sage stunts cucumber growth |
| Lettuce | Celery | Celery can inhibit lettuce growth |

## Beneficial Insect Attractors

These plants attract pollinators and predatory insects that control pest populations:

- **Dill, fennel, parsley** (umbellifers): Attract ladybugs, parasitic wasps, lacewings.
- **Alyssum, clover**: Attract hoverflies (aphid predators).
- **Yarrow**: Attracts ladybugs, parasitic wasps, lacewings.
- **Zinnias, cosmos**: Attract pollinators and predatory wasps.
- **Sunflowers**: Attract ladybugs and provide habitat for predatory insects.

## Spatial Combinations

### Vertical + Low-Growing
- Trellised cucumbers with lettuce underneath (shade for lettuce in summer).
- Pole beans climbing corn with squash as ground cover (Three Sisters).

### Deep-Rooted + Shallow-Rooted
- Carrots (deep) with radishes (shallow) — radishes harvest first, loosen soil.
- Tomatoes (deep) with lettuce (shallow) — lettuce shades tomato roots.

### Nitrogen Fixer + Heavy Feeder
- Beans/peas ([[query-how-to-fix-nitrogen-toxicity-in-cannabis]]) with corn, squash, or brassicas (heavy feeders).
- Clover as living mulch under fruiting vegetables.
