## Treatment Approach

### Cannabis for GERD
- **Low-dose THC** before meals to prevent TLESRs
- **Sublingual tincture** 15-20 minutes before eating
- **CBD** for esophageal inflammation and anxiety reduction
- Avoid high doses of THC which may slow gastric emptying

### Dosing
- Start with very low THC (1-2.5mg) before meals
- Monitor response over 1-2 weeks
- Increase gradually if needed
- Consider adding CBD for anti-inflammatory effect

### Food-as-Medicine
- **Ginger**: Proven anti-nausea and aids digestion
- **Licorice root (DGL)**: Coats and protects esophageal lining
- **Aloe vera juice**: Soothes esophageal inflammation
- **Slippery elm**: Traditional demulcent for GI inflammation
- **Melatonin**: Has been shown to reduce GERD symptoms (regulates LES tone)

### Lifestyle Modifications
- Eat smaller meals (reduce gastric distension)
- Don't lie down after eating (2-3 hours)
- Elevate head of bed
- Avoid trigger foods (caffeine, alcohol, chocolate, mint, fatty foods)
- Stress management (meditation, relaxation response)

## Conventional Treatment Limitations

### Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs)
PPIs (omeprazole, etc.) are the most commonly prescribed GERD medications. Long-term use concerns:
- Reduced nutrient absorption (magnesium, calcium, B12, iron)
- Increased fracture risk
- Increased infection risk (C. difficile, pneumonia)
- Kidney disease with long-term use
- Rebound acid hypersecretion when discontinued

### H2 Blockers and Antacids
Less potent than PPIs but with fewer long-term risks. Antacids provide only temporary relief.

### Cannabis vs. Conventional
Cannabis addresses the root cause (TLESRs) rather than just suppressing acid. It may allow reduction or elimination of PPIs under medical supervision.
