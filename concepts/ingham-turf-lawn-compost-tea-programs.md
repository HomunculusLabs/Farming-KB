## Assessing What Biology Is Lacking

You need to know what biology is lacking in the lawn to determine
whether AACT should be bacterial or fungal. Ingham notes that most
lawns need fungal tea, since conventional lawn care practices
(fertilizers, pesticides, irrigation) selectively destroy fungi
while favoring bacteria.

A soil foodweb analysis is the best starting point. Without testing,
you are guessing. Dr. Eric Nelson at Cornell University has worked
on turf grass health approaches correlated with direct microscopic
measures, showing the relationship between biology and turf
performance.
