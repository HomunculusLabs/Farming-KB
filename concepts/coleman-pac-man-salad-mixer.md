## Design and Construction

### Physical Description

The mixer consists of a barrel 48 inches long and 36 inches in diameter, constructed from wood slats with plywood ends. It is mounted horizontally in a frame so that a handle at one end can rotate it. The barrel is divided into two halves, hinged on the long side, allowing it to open like a clamshell.

### The "Pac-Man" Name

When the top half is opened upward for loading and unloading, the shape of the wooden barrel with its circular plywood end and open mouth closely resembles the Pac-Man video game character. The farm crew gave it this name, and it has been used ever since.

### Mixing Mechanism

After the bottom half is filled with salad ingredients (up to 80 pounds capacity), the top half is closed down and latched securely. The operator then turns the handle to rotate the barrel ten slow revolutions in each direction — first clockwise, then counterclockwise. Inside the barrel, the ingredients are raised as the side goes up and gently dropped to the bottom again as it rotates. This tumbling action produces a thorough but gentle mixing of the various leaf types without crushing, tearing, or bruising them.

## Place in the Wash-Pack Process

The Pac-Man mixer occupies the central position in the salad mixing workflow:

### Step 1: Harvest

Salad ingredients are harvested separately by crop type using sharp Victorinox knives and placed in food-grade five-gallon buckets. Each ingredient is cut and collected individually.

### Step 2: Wash

Each salad ingredient is washed separately. Coleman emphasizes that it is easier to spot bad leaves or occasional detritus when there is only one shape and color of leaf to distinguish them from. Ingredients are washed in sinks and spun dry.

### Step 3: Mix

The individual washed and dried ingredients are combined in the Pac-Man mixer. The ten-revolution cycle in each direction ensures even distribution of all leaf types throughout the mix.

### Step 4: Pack

The mixed salad is packed for delivery. Depending on the customer, it may go into the standard reusable cedar boxes or into quarter-pound cellophane bags for retail accounts.

## Importance of Separate Washing

The decision to wash each ingredient separately before mixing, rather than washing the mixed product, reflects careful attention to [[coleman-wash-pack-infrastructure-four-season-farm]]. Coleman notes that the farm has continually analyzed all aspects of the harvesting, washing, and packing operation for efficiency improvements. One notable success was cutting by 75 percent the time required to drain and refill the sinks between washing each leaf type by installing larger diameter pipes and raising water pressure. For maximum efficiency, the sinks need to be in continual use, so one person begins washing as soon as the first buckets of salad arrive from the greenhouse.

## Quality Standards Supported by the Mixer

The Pac-Man mixer helps maintain several key quality standards for Four Season Farm's salad mix:

- **Uniform distribution** — Each bag or box contains a representative mix of all ingredients rather than clumps of single types
- **Leaf integrity** — The gentle tumbling preserves the whole-leaf quality standard; leaves are not chopped or torn
- **Freshness** — Rapid processing from harvest through mixing to packing minimizes time between cutting and delivery
- **Consistency** — Repeatable mixing ensures that customers receive a predictable product each time
