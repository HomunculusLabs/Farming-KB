---# Tarragon Oyster Mushroom ([[agar-culture-techniques-detailed]].

## Microscopic Features

Spores pale pinkish lilac, oblong and narrow, measuring 12-14 x 4-5
micrometers. Otherwise similar to P. ostreatus.

## Mycelial Characteristics

White, longitudinally linear, soon cottony, aerial, fast-growing, and
classically Oyster-esque. The mycelium tears off in thick sheets,
virtually identical to P. ostreatus in appearance and behavior.

## Fragrance Signature

Sweet, pleasant, slightly anise-like. This distinctive tarragon aroma
is the primary feature separating this species from P. ostreatus.
