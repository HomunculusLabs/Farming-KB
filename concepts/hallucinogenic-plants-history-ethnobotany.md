# Hallucinogenic Plants: History and Ethnobotany

## Overview

Hallucinogenic plants have played a profound role in human civilization for
thousands of years. In the history of mankind, hallucinogens have probably been
the most important of all the narcotics. Their fantastic effects made them
sacred to primitive humans and may even have been responsible for suggesting
the idea of deity. These plants distort the senses and usually produce
hallucinations: experiences that depart from reality. Although most
hallucinations are visual, they may also involve hearing, touch, smell, or
taste, and occasionally several senses simultaneously.
