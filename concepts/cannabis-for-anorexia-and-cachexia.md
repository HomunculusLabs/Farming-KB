## Understanding Anorexia and Cachexia

**Anorexia nervosa** is a psychological condition involving lack of interest in or refusal to eat sufficient food to maintain healthy body weight. It is distinct from bulimia nervosa (binge eating followed by purging).

**Cachexia** (wasting syndrome) is loss of body mass that cannot be replaced through nutrition, presenting as generalized weakness, poor health, malnutrition, and emaciation. Cachexia is typically a secondary condition in chronic diseases including:
- End-stage cancer
- AIDS
- Terminal tuberculosis
- Multiple sclerosis
- COPD/emphysema

Cancer-related anorexia-cachexia involves weight loss from limited appetite, nausea, and weakness. AIDS-related anorexia involves appetite loss leading to fat and muscle tissue loss.
