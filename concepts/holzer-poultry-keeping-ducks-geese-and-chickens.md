## Poultry Breeds and Natural Breeding

Holzer keeps birds extensively and is mostly self-sufficient:

- Preferred breeds: mallards, Indian runner ducks, crested ducks, Muscovy ducks, and Styrian chickens
- These breeds are hardy, adaptable, and help control snail populations
- Naturally hatched birds are preferred over incubator-hatched -- they retain maternal instincts for raising broods independently
- Naturally hatched birds that fail to brood successfully are accepted as genetic selection; successful mothers balance out losses
- Over time, reliable breeding pairs establish themselves naturally

### Protective Hedge Design

For poultry enclosures, Holzer plants thorny hedge barriers using:
- Multiflora rose (Rosa multiflora) -- strong climber, abundant flowers, intense perfume, popular with bees
- Dog rose (Rosa canina) -- easy to cultivate, excellent medicinal plant, fruit rich in vitamin C, makes delicious jam and fruit tea
- Japanese rose (Rosa rugosa) -- large red shiny rose hips, very tasty, corrugated leaves

These hedges serve multiple functions: shelter from predators, food source (rose hips), beauty, fragrance, and [[holzer-pond-construction-and-aquaculture]], irrigation, and [[mollison-designers-microclimate-creation-frost-protection]]
