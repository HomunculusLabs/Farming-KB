# Mushroom Pest and Disease Management

Comprehensive troubleshooting guide from the FAO training manual covering contamination, pests, diseases, and environmental problems in mushroom cultivation. The guiding principle: "Preventing is better than solving the problems."

See also: [[mushroom-training-adapted-cultivation]], [[mushroom-training-oyster-straw-cultivation]], [[mushroom-training-spawn-production]], [[fungicolous-fungi-cultivated-mushroom-diseases]]

## General Prevention

- Keep hygiene management: clean everything (person, area, tools, equipment, surroundings) at every step
- Stop using contaminated areas for 1-2 weeks to break contaminant life cycles
- For serious contamination: spray area with chemicals
- Use black-light with water or sticky-traps to decrease insects
- Keep mushroom houses clean: less insects, less disease
- Check bags individually at least twice weekly
- Never use chemicals during the fruiting stage
- Place lemongrass plants around mushroom house as natural pest deterrent

## Mycelium Fails to Form

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Improper initiation strategy | Alter moisture, temperature, light, CO2. If substrate too moist, decrease moisture |
| Chlorinated or contaminated water | Use activated charcoal water filters |
| Bad substrate | Check substrate; spread, remix, repackage. Pasteurize immediately after bagging (fermentation gas slows/stops mycelium) |
| Bad pasteurization | Release all air, ensure continuous steam before starting 3-hour timing |
| Substrate too hot at inoculation | Ensure substrate is cool before inoculation |
| Bad strain or spawn | Obtain younger strain of known vitality |
| Spawn contaminated | Pasteurize and reinoculate with good spawn |
| Forgot to inoculate | Ensure inoculation is done |

## Poor Mycelium Spread, Bad Spots, Mites

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Temperature dropped too fast after pasteurization | Cool chamber slowly; don't open cover too quickly; check cotton plug is tight |
| Poor inoculation hygiene | Inoculate in clean conditions with no air movement |
| Too high density, poor ventilation in incubation | Spread bags, improve ventilation, maintain 25-35C |
| Too high CO2 | Not more than 5% CO2; check ventilation |
| Poor incubation house hygiene | Improve overall hygiene |
| Uneven substrate (patches of mycelium) | Mix substrate thoroughly |
| Bacteria/fungi contamination | Separate contaminated bags immediately; remix separately, remake bags, pasteurize longer |
| Mite contamination | Separate contaminated bags, pasteurize again |

## Mycelium Grows But No Mushrooms

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Substrate formula unsuitable | Adjust formula; check pH, sawdust quality, additives |
| Mites, mold, virus, bacteria, insects | Check pasteurization, inoculation, house management for hygiene |
| Environmental toxins | Remove toxin source |
| Bad strain | Acquire new strains |

## Mushrooms Form But Abort or Delay

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Poor primordia/growth conditions | Check temperature and humidity; adjust doors/windows |
| Mold, bacteria, insects, worms, mites | Adjust light, temperature, humidity, ventilation. For severe cases: 0.5 tsp sulfur in 3.5L water, mist bags and surfaces |
| Chemical contamination (solvents, gas, chlorine) | Remove toxins |
| Bad strain | Acquire new strain or new supplier |

## Abnormal Mushroom Development

### Long Stems, Underdeveloped Caps
- **Inadequate light**: increase or adjust light wavelength
- **Excessive CO2**: increase air exchange, open doors/windows appropriately

### Massive Numbers Form, Few Develop
- **Too long incubation**: shorten primordia formation period
- **Lack of oxygen / inadequate light**: increase ventilation and light

### Deformed, Decaying Mushrooms
- **Germs or competing microorganisms**: adjust house to favor mushrooms, not competitors
- **Dirty substrate surface**: clean surface
- **Poor ventilation, too high humidity**: increase circulation, reduce humidity; surface water must evaporate several times daily
- **Bad strain**: acquire better strain
- **Chemicals used during fruiting**: never use chemicals during fruiting stage

## Single-Flush Production Failure

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Inadequate substrate nutrition | Reformulate |
| Competitors | Check hygiene, adjust all environmental parameters |
| Poor management | Improve overall management |
| Bad strain | Acquire new strain |

## Small Mushrooms

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Too many mushrooms at once | Reduce opening size |
| Lack of nutrients in substrate | Review substrate quality |
| Weather changes | Beware of wide temperature fluctuations |
| Unhealthy spawn | Check spawn origin |

## Pests and Insects

### General Pest Control
- Place lemongrass plants around mushroom house
- Spread lime on shelves, poles, and ground
- Keep mushroom house and surroundings clean
- Remove all waste promptly
- Use black-light traps with water or sticky traps

### Ants
- Mix detergent with water, place on their paths
- Do NOT put detergent on mushrooms

### Mites
- Major pest for ear mushrooms (beware after 5th flush)
- Separate contaminated bags immediately, pasteurize again
- Check all bags at least twice weekly

### Flies
- Use sticky traps and maintain cleanliness
- Reduce humidity to appropriate levels

## Post-Harvest Problems

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Light weight mushrooms | Water shortage | Check humidity |
| Quick spoilage | Too mature at harvest | Harvest younger |
| Quick spoilage | Too warm before packaging | Chill before packaging |
| Quick spoilage | Too wet at harvest | Reduce humidity before harvesting |
| Quick spoilage | Stored beyond shelf life | Sell faster |

## Bacterial Rot (Oyster Mushrooms)

- **Pathogens**: Pseudomonas tolaasii, Pseudomonas fluorescens
- **Symptoms**: Rotting spots on fruiting body during flush
- **Prevention**: Control humidity at 80-85%; allow water to evaporate from surfaces before further watering
- **Treatment** (severe cases): 113g chlorine in 45L water (or 4 oz/gallon)
