### 3. Setting Limits to Population and Consumption

*By governing our own needs, we can set resources aside to further the above principles.*

The third ethic recognizes that unlimited growth is incompatible with a finite planet. It calls for voluntary self-regulation of consumption and population, creating surpluses that can be reinvested in [[query-how-do-i-use-knf-inputs-through-the-plant-growth-cycle]] composting, mulching, green manures, or mineral amendments.
