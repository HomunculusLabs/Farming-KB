## Gymnopilus purpuratus: A South American Specialty

Gymnopilus purpuratus holds particular biogeographic interest. It
was originally described from South America and is well documented
from Chile, where it has been the subject of extensive chemical
studies by Gartz and colleagues. The species is also reported from
