| Germination | 1:1,000 | Promote sprouting and early root development |
| Seedling establishment | 1:1,000 | Support transplant recovery |

- Use during early vegetative stage for comprehensive nutrition
- FAA provides nitrogen; FPJ provides growth hormones
- Together they support rapid, healthy vegetative growth
