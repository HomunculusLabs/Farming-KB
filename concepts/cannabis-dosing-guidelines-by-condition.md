## Dosing Evidence from Clinical Studies

### Pain Management
- **Smoked cannabis** at 2%, 4%, and 8% THC: Medium dose (4%) reduced capsaicin-induced pain, while high dose (8%) increased pain (San Diego, 2009)
- **Nabilone** (synthetic THC) 2 mg orally: Significant pain reduction in fibromyalgia (Winnipeg, 2007)
- **Nabilone** 0.5-1.0 mg at bedtime: Superior to amitriptyline for sleep in fibromyalgia (Montreal, 2008)
- **Sativex** oromucosal spray (2.7 mg THC + 2.5 mg CBD per spray): Average use 22-32 mg/day THC and 20-30 mg/day CBD for MS spasticity and pain

### Neurological Conditions
- **CBD** 100 mg/kg: Significant reduction in seizure frequency in animal models (Reading, UK, 2009)
- **Delta-9-THC** 0.04-0.14 mg/kg body weight: Positive effects in pediatric neurological patients (Germany, 2003)
- **Dronabinol** 5 mg three times daily: Reduced hyperkinetic movement disorder (Michigan, 2009)
- **Nabilone** 0.5-1.0 mg: Dramatic reduction in Alzheimer's agitation (British Columbia, 2008)

### Mental Health
- **Dronabinol** 2.5 mg twice daily: 10% mood improvement in AIDS patients with depression (Tulsa, 1995)
- **Nabilone**: 72% of PTSD patients experienced cessation or significant reduction of nightmares (Ottawa, 2009)
