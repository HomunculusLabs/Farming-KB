# Medicinal Mushroom Extraction and Preparation

[[anti-cancer-polysaccharides-from-medicinal-mushrooms]] contain [[cordyceps-cancer-bioactive-compounds]] that require proper
extraction to become bioavailable. Different compounds are extracted by
different methods: water for polysaccharides, alcohol for triterpenoids.
Understanding these differences is critical for effective preparation.

## Key Bioactive Compounds by Species

The bioactive compounds discussed below — particularly [[functional-foods-medicinal-mushrooms]]. Understanding their extraction requirements is essential for maximising health benefits. See also the individual species guides: [[medicinal-mushroom-guide-shiitake]], and [[medicinal-mushroom-guide-lions-mane]].

### Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum)
- Polysaccharides: immune-stimulating beta-glucans (water-soluble)
- [[ganoderic-acids]]: anti-coagulating, cholesterol-lowering
  (alcohol-soluble triterpenoids)
- Immuno-modulating proteins (Kino et al., 1989)
- Anti-inflammatory activity comparable to hydrocortisone
  (Stavinoha, 1990)
- Low molecular weight polysaccharide fraction prolongs HIV-exposed
  lymphocyte survival (Kim et al., 1994)

### Shiitake (Lentinula edodes)
- Lentinan: beta-1,3 glucan with beta-1,6 branchings (water-soluble),
  approved anti-cancer drug in Japan
- KS-2: anti-tumor polysaccharide active via oral administration
- Activates killer and helper T-cells
- Eritadenine: cholesterol-lowering compound unique to shiitake

### Maitake (Grifola frondosa)
- D-fraction: beta-glucan extract with potent immune-modulating
  activity
- MD-fraction: approved adjunct cancer therapy in Japan
- Anti-HIV activity demonstrated by NIH drug screening
- Blood sugar regulation effects for diabetes management

### Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus)
- Erinacines and hericenones: stimulate nerve growth factor (NGF)
  synthesis
- Unique compounds found only in this genus
- Neuroprotective and potentially neurogenerative
- Promising research for cognitive decline and peripheral nerve
  regeneration

### Turkey Tail (Trametes versicolor)
- PSK (polysaccharide-K): approved cancer drug in Japan since 1970s
- PSP: protein-bound polysaccharide approved as medicine in China
- Strong [[cancer-immune-modulation-mechanisms]] through T-cell activation

### Enoki (Flammulina velutipes)
- FVP ([[flammulina-velutipes]] polysaccharide): anti-tumor
- 80-100% effective against Sarcoma 180 and Ehrlich carcinoma in
  laboratory studies

### Nameko (Pholiota nameko)
- Water extracts: 60% effective against Sarcoma 180
- NaOH extracts: 90% effective against Sarcoma 180
- Improves resistance to Staphylococcus bacteria

## Extraction Methods

### Hot Water Extraction (Tea/Decoction)
Best for: polysaccharides, beta-glucans, water-soluble compounds.

Process:
- Dried mushroom pieces boiled in water for 5 to 60 minutes
- Steeped for 30 minutes to several hours for milder extraction
- Strain and serve as tea or reduce to concentrate

Traditional method for Reishi tea: 3 to 5 grams fresh per person
daily. Simmering at high heat develops stronger flavour in Nameko.
Multiple extractions from the same material increase total yield.
A second extraction typically yields 30-50% of the first extraction.

### Alcohol Extraction (Tincture)
Best for: triterpenoids, ganoderic acids, alcohol-soluble compounds.

Process:
- Dried mushroom material soaked in ethanol (40-95%) for weeks
  to months
- Glycerin tinctures serve as alcohol-free alternatives
- Often combined with water extract for full-spectrum preparations
- Typical ratio: 1 part dried mushroom to 5 parts solvent (1:5)
