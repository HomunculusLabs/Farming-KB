## Cohoba Snuff

Cohoba is the Taíno name for a psychoactive snuff prepared from the
seeds of [[yopo-snuff]] for the [[kava-traditional-preparation-ceremonial-context]] of cohoba snuff involves a careful
multi-step process that reflects an empirical understanding of the
chemistry involved:

1. Harvesting the ripe seed pods from the Anadenanthera tree
2. Toasting or roasting the seeds over a fire to develop the psychoac
  tive properties
3. Grinding the toasted seeds to a fine powder using a mortar and pes
  tle
4. Mixing the powder with calcined lime or shell ash (a source of cal
  cium hydroxide)
5. The resulting snuff is stored in ornate containers, often carved f
  rom wood, bone, or ceramic

The addition of lime serves a critical chemical purpose: it converts
the free-base tryptamine alkaloids into a form more readily absorbed
through the nasal mucosa. This technique parallels the preparation of
other South American snuffs like [[ethnomycology-cultural-significance-sacred-fungi]]
