## From Extravagant Consumption to Addictive Necessity

Holmgren traces a concerning progression in modern consumer culture: what
begins as extravagant consumption becomes habitual norm and eventually
transforms into addictive necessity. Technologies and products initially
regarded as luxuries --- private automobiles, air conditioning, packaged
foods --- gradually become seen as necessities, creating dependency on
resource-intensive systems that generate enormous waste. This progression
mirrors addiction at a civilisational scale, making the refusal and reduction
strategies of the waste hierarchy not merely practical but psychologically
and culturally transformative.
