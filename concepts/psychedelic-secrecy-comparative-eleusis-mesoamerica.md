## Why Secrecy?

The parallel secrecy served several overlapping functions in both traditions:

1. **Protection of the experience**: Public knowledge of the sacrament's nature would invite casual, recreational use, degrading the ritual context

2. **Preservation of authority**: The [[the-profanation-of-415-bc-and-evidence-for-eleusinian-psychoactivity]], in which Alcibiades was accused of imitating the Mysteries in a private drinking party, led to his arrest and exile from Athens.

The severity of the penalty underscores the functional importance of secrecy: the Athenian state recognized that the Mysteries' power depended on the preservation of the initiate's ignorance about what would occur.
