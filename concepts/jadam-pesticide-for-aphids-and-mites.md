# JADAM Pesticide for Aphids and Mites

Aphids and mites are the most common and earliest pests in organic farming. JADAM's approach uses [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] as the delivery mechanism combined with [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] for the active insecticidal compound. Developed by Jeongho Choi and Youngsang Cho.

## Formulation (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

**Standard recipe**:
- JWA: 5L (1.3 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L (1.3 gal)
- Water: to 500L (132 gal) total volume

**Strong recipe** (heavy infestation):
- JWA: 10L (2.6 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 10L (2.6 gal)

**With disease control** (aphids + fungal disease present):
- JWA: 5L + JS 1-2L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L

## Preparation Steps

1. Test water with JWA first -- must be soft water (clear, lots of foam)
2. Do mixture test: 0.5L water + 5cc each ingredient, shake, check for foam + no clotting
3. Half-fill spray tank with water
4. Add ingredients one by one, stirring each time
5. Top up with water to 500L
6. Filter through fine net before spraying
7. Use same day -- never store leftover

## Application Method

- Use fine-particle nozzles for thorough coverage
- Spray entire plant -- aphids hide on undersides of leaves and at growing tips
- For leaf-rolling aphids: target curled leaves especially, spray thoroughly into crevices
- Mites require complete leaf coverage including undersides

## Timing

- **Best time**: morning dew, evening, or cloudy/humid days
- **Avoid**: hot dry midday (rapid evaporation = no effect)
- **Frequency**: spray every 1-2 days until controlled; can spray 2-3x per day if no JS is used
- **Season**: intensive control from early growth when new sprouts appear (soft leaves are most vulnerable)

## Scouting Before Treatment

- Check undersides of young leaves at growing tips
- Look for curling or yellowing leaves (aphid damage)
- Look for fine webbing or stippling on leaf surfaces (mite damage)
- Inspect 10-20 plants across the field, especially borders and stressed plants
- Action threshold: treat when colonies are found on more than 10% of plants

## Follow-Up

- Re-spray evening and again next morning for heavy infestations
- After control achieved, switch to preventive JMS spray (JMS 20L + JHS 5L + JWA 3L per 500L)
- Rotate JHS types periodically (Jerusalem artichoke, gingko, KPFR) to prevent any adaptation

## Effectiveness Notes

- JS is also directly effective on mites -- add 1-2L for dual pest+disease control
- Adding 0.5kg fine red clay powder increases pesticidal effect
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS can be replaced with pokeweed, gingko, or Korean pasque flower root
- Controls: cotton aphid, turnip aphid, mealy plum aphid, spider mite, earth mite, silverleaf whitefly, broad mite, and many others
