# Von Karman Trails and Flow Patterns

## Overview

Von Karman trails are stable, repeating spiral patterns generated downstream from fixed bodies in fluid flow. Named after the aerodynamicist Theodore von Karman, these patterns emerge when water, wind, or even clouds pass a bluff (non-streamlined) body at certain modest velocities. They represent one of the fundamental ways that nature organizes flow, and understanding them is essential for [[permaculture-design]], we can provide many such receptors or triggers:
- Catching nutrients in flow
- Ensuring events for future growth
- Creating starting-places for biological events
- Providing resting or settling places for entities dependent on flow

Matter flows predictably along streamlines, analogous to corks floating on the sea, which have a predictable path to shore perpendicular to the wave fronts. Drift lines form at sea and end on shorelines, where they deposit or remove material.

### Concentration and Dispersal

Streamlines diverge as wave fronts and disseminate into open media, but are strong, concentrated, and visible at:
- **Constrictions** in the flow
- **Near origins** of flow events
- **In powerful or refracted flows**

A small restricted orifice in the wave front acts as a secondary origin. Just as a grub encircling a tree causes it to branch at that point, constrictions in the flow of events generate secondary origins. Designers can impose small constricting events or place fixed objects in flow to produce specific branching results.
