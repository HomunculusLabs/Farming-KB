## The Giving Shame Back Technique

This technique addresses the shame-cortisol loop by consciously identifying and releasing shame that does not genuinely belong to the individual:

### Preparation
1. Retreat to a quiet, safe place
2. Close your eyes and enter a relaxed state
3. Breathe deeply — slow inhalations and exhalations
4. Bring your attention to your heart center

### The Process
1. Identify a shame that you carry — notice where it lives in your body
2. Ask yourself: "Whose shame is this really?"
3. Consider: Did this shame originate with you, or was it given to you by someone else?
4. Common sources include parents, teachers, religious institutions, cultural norms, peer groups, and romantic partners
5. If the shame does not genuinely belong to you, imagine gathering it up
6. See it, feel it, give it shape and form
