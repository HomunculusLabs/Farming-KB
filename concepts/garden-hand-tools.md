# Garden Hand Tools

## Overview

The basic techniques of practical [[disposable-tray-casing-method-grass-seed-substrate]].

### Hoes

Own two hoes minimum:
- **Dutch (push) hoe:** Push backward and forward while walking backward to avoid treading weeds back in. Keep blade no more than 1/2in below surface
- **Swan-necked (draw) hoe:** For weeds too large for the Dutch hoe, earthing-up vegetables, and making seed rows
- **Onion hoe:** Small, ideal for working between closely planted items on [[holzer-grafting-fruit-tree-propagation]] work.

### Edging Knife

Half-moon-shaped tool for cutting lawn edges. Some gardeners manage fine with just a spade.

### Secateurs

Two types: anvil and parrot-bill. Both work well for most [[deep-bed-cultivation]], make a 4ft board with 6in divisions.

### Dibber

Made from a broken spade or fork handle, cut to 12in and shaved to a point. Used for transplanting seedlings.

### Garden Line

Two pegs and a length of thick nylon string for marking seed rows and digging areas. Make at least two.

### Tool Cleaner

Wooden spade-shaped scraper carried in pocket. Use regularly while digging to remove soil from blades. Clean all tools before putting away and rub with oily cloth to prevent rust.

### Firming Board

1/2in thick wood cut slightly smaller than seed trays with a handle. For leveling and firming compost in trays before sowing.

## Power Equipment

### Rotary Cultivator

Invaluable for large gardens: creates fine seedbeds quickly and incorporates [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]] into soil. However, hand dig at least part of the garden annually -- constant rotary cultivating can create an impermeable "glazed" layer. Most cultivate to 6-9in depth.

### String Trimmer

Nylon line cuts grass right up to walls and trees without damage. Available as gas, electric, or battery-powered.

### Shredder

Electrically or gas-driven; shreds woody prunings finely. The resulting material is excellent for mulching-guide|mulching -- a cheap alternative to bark chips.

### Hedge Trimmer

Useful for large hedges. Available as battery, electric, or gas models. Does almost as well as hand shears but not quite.

### Lawn Mower

Cylinder mowers give the best finish. Rotary mowers perform better on tall grass. Electric mowers are cheap and safe but the cord is a disadvantage in large gardens.
