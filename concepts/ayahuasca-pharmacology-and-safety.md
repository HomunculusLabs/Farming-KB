## Dangerous Drug Interactions

The MAOI activity of ayahuasca creates the most significant safety
concern. MAOIs interact dangerously with a wide range of common
medications and certain foods. The primary categories of dangerous
interactions include:

- **Serotonergic drugs**: SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclic antidepressants,
  and other serotonergic medications can precipitate serotonin syndrome
  when combined with MAOIs—a potentially fatal condition characterized
  by hyperthermia, agitation, tremor, and autonomic instability.
- **Sympathomimetic drugs**: Stimulants including amphetamines, cocaine,
  and some decongestants can cause hypertensive crises when combined
  with MAOIs, potentially leading to stroke or cardiac events.
- **Opioids**: Meperidine (Demerol) and certain other opioids can
  cause serotonin syndrome or excitory toxicity when combined with
  MAOIs.
- **Antiretroviral medications**: Some AIDS medications may interact
  adversely with ayahuasca. At least one fatality has been documented
  in the literature—an AIDS patient who died during an ayahuasca
  ceremony in Bolivia despite appearing healthy enough to dance at
  the beginning of the service.
