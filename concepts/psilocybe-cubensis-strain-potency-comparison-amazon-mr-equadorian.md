conditions and flush number than by strain genetics.

## Implications for Dosage Estimation

The approximately 1.6-fold concentration difference between caps and stems in two of three strains
means that consuming whole mushrooms versus caps-only preparations would yield meaningfully
different alkaloid intakes. For the M.R. and Equadorian strains, caps-only consumption would deliver
approximately 1.3 times the total psilocybin per gram compared to whole mushroom consumption. The
strain-dependent nature of this distribution means generalized potency estimates based on one strain
cannot be reliably applied to another.
