## Haze Lineage and Genetic History

## Overview

Haze is arguably the most influential cannabis lineage in modern breeding history. Developed in Northern California in the late 1960s and early 1970s, Haze became the genetic backbone for countless sativa-dominant hybrids and established the template for what consumers expect from a "sativa" experience: cerebral, uplifting, creative effects accompanied by complex spicy, citrus, and earthy aromas. The Haze genetics documented in Cannabis Sativa Vol. 3 — including Original Haze, Amnesia Haze, Super Lemon Haze, Neville's Haze, and dozens of Haze crosses — represent the pinnacle of sativa breeding.

## Origins: The Haze Brothers (1960s–1970s)

### The Breeders

The original Haze was created by a group of three brothers (known only as "the Haze Brothers") working in the Santa Cruz Mountains of Northern California in the late 1960s. At the time, the California cannabis scene was dominated by imported [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]] compactness.

### The Breeding Process

The Haze Brothers worked through multiple generations of crossing and selection:

- **F1 generation**: Crossing the Colombian with the Thai produced vigorous but extremely slow-flowering hybrids.
- **Backcrossing and selection**: Over several years, they repeatedly selected the most desirable individuals and crossed them back to the parent lines or to each other, gradually stabilizing the combination of traits.
- **Stabilization**: By the early 1970s, they had produced a reasonably stable line that combined Colombian vigor, Thai potency and aroma, and a flowering time of 10–14 weeks.

The total breeding effort spanned approximately 5–7 years, an unusually long commitment for [[advanced-cannabis-breeding-ibl-strains-hash-making]] at the time.

## Characteristics of the Original Haze

### Morphology

Original Haze plants display the classic extreme sativa phenotype:

- Height: 8–15+ feet outdoors, requiring significant management indoors
- Internode spacing: Very long (4–8 inches), creating an open, airy structure
- Leaf structure: Long, narrow leaflets with 5–7 fingers per leaf
- Bud structure: Elongated, fluffy buds with prominent pistils and moderate resin production
- Flowering time: 10–16 weeks, with some phenotypes requiring up to 20 weeks
- Yield: Low to moderate, reflecting the long flowering investment and airy bud structure

### Chemical Profile

- **THC content**: Moderately high by historical standards (12–16% in the original), though modern selections have pushed this significantly higher
- **CBD content**: Very low (<0.1%), typical of drug-type sativas
- **Dominant terpenes**: Myrcene, limonene, caryophyllene, pinene, and humulene. The terpene profile is complex and multi-layered, contributing to the strain's reputation for nuanced aroma.

### Effects Profile

Haze is renowned for a specific pattern of psychoactive effects:

- **Onset**: Slow, building gradually over 15–30 minutes
- **Character**: Cerebral, uplifting, creative, and energetic
- **Duration**: Long-lasting (3–5 hours), reflecting the complex cannabinoid and terpene profile
- **Body effects**: Minimal physical sedation; the experience is predominantly mental

## Key Haze Variants and Derivatives

### Original Haze / Haze A / Haze B

The Haze Brothers produced several distinct lines. The most commonly referenced are:

- **Haze A**: More Colombian-dominant, slightly faster flowering (10–12 weeks), sweeter aroma
- **Haze B**: More Thai-dominant, longer flowering (12–16 weeks), more potent and psychedelic
- **Haze C**: A stabilized cross between A and B, representing the canonical "Original Haze"

### Neville's Haze

Neville Schoenmakers, founder of The Seed Bank (later Sensi Seeds), obtained Haze genetics from the Haze Brothers and worked extensively with them in the Netherlands during the 1980s. Neville's Haze is a cross of Original Haze (1/4 NL#5, 3/4 Haze) that significantly improved yield and flowering speed while retaining the Haze character. It is considered one of the finest sativa varieties ever created and remains a benchmark strain.

### Amnesia Haze

One of the most popular Haze derivatives in Europe, Amnesia Haze is believed to be a cross of Southeast Asian, Jamaican, and Hawaiian genetics with Afghan indica. It combines Haze-like cerebral effects with a faster flowering time (9–11 weeks) and higher yield than pure Haze lines. Its aroma profile features citrus, lemon, and earthy notes.

### Super Lemon Haze

Created by Green House Seed Co., Super Lemon Haze is a cross of Lemon Skunk and Super Silver Haze (itself a Haze derivative). It won the High Times Cannabis Cup in 2008 and 2009. The strain is notable for its intense lemon aroma, moderate flowering time (9–10 weeks), and balanced energetic effects.

### Super Silver Haze

A cross of Haze, Northern Lights #5, and Skunk #1, created by Neville Schoenmakers. This three-way hybrid combined Haze potency with NL#5 yield and Skunk#1 vigor. It won the Cannabis Cup three consecutive years (1997, 1998, 1999) and became one of the most awarded strains in history.

## Haze's Influence on Modern Breeding

### As a Parent Strain

Haze has been used as a parent in hundreds of crosses. Its genetic contribution is valued for:

- **Potency**: Haze contributes high THC levels and complex cannabinoid profiles
- **Terpene complexity**: Haze offspring typically have rich, layered aromas
- **Effect profile**: The uplifting, cerebral "sativa high" that consumers associate with Haze is a primary breeding target
- **Hybrid vigor**: Haze crosses often show pronounced heterosis (hybrid vigor)

### Common Haze Cross Patterns

1. **Haze × Indica**: The most common pattern, adding indica yield, flowering speed, and physical relaxation while retaining sativa cerebral effects. Examples: Bruce Banner, Purple Haze Thai.
2. **Haze × Skunk**: Combining Haze potency with Skunk vigor, yield, and reliability. The Skunk#1 × Haze cross is one of the most foundational hybrid combinations.
3. **Haze × Haze**: Sibling crosses within the Haze family, used to stabilize specific traits or create novel combinations. Examples: Original Amnesia, various "Haze" selections.
4. **Haze × Ruderalis**: Autoflowering Haze crosses, combining Haze effects with the convenience of auto-flowering. Example: Easy Ryder (Lowryder × Joint Doctor × Haze).

## Cultivation Considerations for Haze-Type Strains

### Indoor Growing

Haze strains present specific challenges for indoor cultivation:

- **Height management**: Essential techniques include SCROG (Screen of Green), LST ([[fukuoka-natural-farming-tropical-climates]] with long growing seasons. [[query-what-is-seed-starting-and-when-should-i-start-seeds-indoors]] in early spring and transplant after last frost.
- **Finishing**: May not finish before frost in climates north of 40° latitude without [[double-layer-greenhouse-protection]].
- **Support**: The tall, branchy structure requires staking or trellising, especially when heavy with resin-laden buds.
