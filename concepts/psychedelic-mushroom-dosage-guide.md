# Psychedelic Mushroom Dosage Guide

## Overview

Accurate dosing [[archaeology-of-psychedelic-mushrooms]] is complicated by significant variability in potency between species, within species, and even between individual fruiting bodies. This guide provides general dosage frameworks based on Psilocybe cubensis as a reference standard, with conversion factors for other species. All dosages refer to dried mushroom material unless otherwise noted.

## Critical Caveats

- Potency varies 3-5x within the same species depending on genetics, growing conditions, and handling
- No dose is guaranteed to produce a specific level of effects
- Individual sensitivity varies based on body weight, metabolism, tolerance, and neurochemistry
- This guide is for informational purposes; it does not constitute medical advice
- Always start lower than you think you need, especially with an unfamiliar batch
- See [[psychedelic-mushroom-set-and-setting]] for principles that affect subjective intensity

## Fresh vs. Dried Mushrooms

Fresh mushrooms contain approximately 90% water by weight. The standard conversion is:

- **1 gram dried ≈ 10 grams fresh** (average)
- Range: 1g dried ≈ 8-12g fresh depending on species and drying completeness

Fresh mushrooms are often considered slightly different in character — some users report a more "organic" or "wilder" experience, possibly due to the presence of psilocin that degrades during drying. However, fresh material is harder to dose accurately and has a much shorter shelf life.

## Dosage Levels by Dried P. cubensis Equivalent

### Threshold Dose: 0.25 - 0.5g

- Subtle mood enhancement and sensory enrichment
- No significant visual or cognitive alterations
- Useful for gauging sensitivity to a new batch
- May not be noticeable for experienced users

### Light Dose: 0.5 - 1.5g

- Noticeable effects without significant impairment
- Enhanced colors, music appreciation, and mild euphoria
- Suitable for first-time users in comfortable settings
- Closed-eye visuals may begin
- Manageable in most social settings with trusted individuals

### Moderate Dose: 1.5 - 3.0g

- Classic psychedelic experience
- Significant visual and cognitive effects
- Suitable for experienced users seeking full psychedelic effects
- Not recommended for first-time users without experienced guidance
- Requires dedicated time and space (4-6 hours)
- A trip sitter is strongly recommended

### Strong Dose: 3.0 - 5.0g

- Intense psychedelic experience
- Significant ego dissolution likely
- Requires substantial preparation and an experienced trip sitter
- Not recommended without prior psychedelic experience
- Set aside 6-8 hours with no responsibilities

### Heroic Dose: 5.0g+

- Full [[mystical-experience]] in the "hero's journey" framework (as described by Terence McKenna)
- Complete ego dissolution probable
- Extreme vulnerability; requires expert guidance or extensive personal experience
- Minimum 8 hours with no responsibilities; ideally a full day with nothing scheduled the following day
- Not recommended without significant prior experience

## Species-Specific Conversion Factors

Potency varies dramatically between species. The following conversion factors adjust the P. cubensis reference doses for other common species. Multiply the P. cubensis dose by the factor to get the equivalent dose for the target species:

### High-Potency Species

| Species | Potency Factor | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Psilocybe azurescens | 0.25-0.40x | Extremely potent; 1g cubensis ≈ 0.3g azurescens |
| Psilocybe cyanescens | 0.40-0.60x | Very potent; 2g cubensis ≈ 1g cyanescens |
| Psilocybe bohemica/serbica | 0.50-0.70x | Potent European species |
| Psilocybe semilanceata | 0.50-0.70x | Small but potent; potency varies by region and season |
| [[panaeolus-cyanescens-bali-omelette]] | 0.40-0.60x | "Blue meanies"; dung-inhabiting tropical species |

## Dosage by Body Weight

Body weight has a moderate influence on dose response. The following adjustments to the base dose are suggested:

- **Under 60 kg (130 lbs)**: Reduce base dose by 20-30%
- **60-80 kg (130-175 lbs)**: Use base dose as reference
- **80-100 kg (175-220 lbs)**: Increase base dose by 10-20%
- **Over 100 kg (220 lbs)**: Increase base dose by 20-30%

These are rough guidelines; individual metabolism and sensitivity are more important than body weight alone.

## Preparation Methods and Bioavailability

### Whole Mushroom Consumption

Eating dried mushrooms directly is the most common method. Onset is 20-60 minutes, peak at 60-120 minutes. Nausea is more common due to chitin content in the cell walls.

### Lemon Tek

Chopped mushrooms are soaked in fresh lemon or lime juice for 15-20 minutes before consumption. The acidic environment:

- Pre-converts some psilocybin to psilocin, potentially faster onset
- Begins breaking down chitin, reducing nausea
- May increase perceived intensity by 10-25% — reduce dose accordingly
- Onset may be as fast as 10-30 minutes

### Mushroom Tea

Mushrooms are steeped in hot (not boiling) water for 10-20 minutes, optionally with added tea or flavorings. The liquid is strained and consumed, leaving behind solid material:

- Reduced nausea (chitin is not consumed)
- Faster onset (15-45 minutes)
- Slightly shorter duration
- More consistent dosing (thorough extraction)
- Potential loss of some alkaloids in discarded material

### Capsules

Dried, powdered mushrooms encapsulated for precise dosing. Effects are similar to whole consumption but with more predictable dosing and easier storage.
