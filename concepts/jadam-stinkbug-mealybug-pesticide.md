## Stinkbug and Mealybug Pesticide (JADAM Recipe #9)

## Overview

JADAM pesticide recipe #9 is the **strongest of all JADAM pesticides**. It uses [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] 15 L (4 gal)
- Water to make 500 L (132 gal)

## Target Pests

- Bean bug (Riptortus clavatus)
- Stinkbug (Arma custos, Homalogonia obtusa, Eysarcoris spp.)
- Silverleaf whitefly and greenhouse whitefly
- Citrus mealybug (Planococcus citri)
- Thrips (including tangerine thrips)
- Turnip moth larvae
- Pear psylla
- [[jadam-red-clay-powder-method]] to increase effectiveness
- See jadam red clay powder method for making fine red clay powder

## Combined Coverage

Recipe #9 includes the effects of recipes #6 (aphid/mite), #7 (moth), and #8 (planthopper/leafhopper). It is the broadest-spectrum JADAM pesticide.

## Application Timing

- Apply when humid for best results (early morning or sunset)
- Pesticide must coat the target and remain wet as long as possible
- If evaporation is rapid, pesticide becomes ineffective
