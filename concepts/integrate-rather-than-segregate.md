## The Power of Relationship

In nature, productivity and resilience arise from the complex web of
relationships between species. [[permaculture-design]] seeks to replicate these integrated relationships in
human-designed systems. The goal is to place every element so that it
performs multiple functions and is supported by multiple other elements.
This creates a web of beneficial connections that makes the system more
productive, resilient, and self-reliant.

## Kropotkin's Mutual Aid

Peter Kropotkin's work "Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution" provides an
important intellectual foundation for this principle. Writing in response to
the social Darwinists who used Darwin's ideas to justify competitive
individualism, Kropotkin documented numerous examples of cooperation and
mutual aid in both nature and human societies. He showed that cooperation
rather than competition has been a primary driver of evolutionary success.
