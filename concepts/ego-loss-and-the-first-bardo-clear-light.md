## Overview

The First Bardo ([[chikhai-bardo]]) in the Tibetan Buddhist framework describes the initial phase of [[mckenna-consciousness-expansion-and-drug-war]]: the moment of ego-loss and the encounter with the [[clear-light]]. In Leary"s psychedelic adaptation of the *[[bardo-thodol]]*, this phase represents the most critical juncture of the [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] — the point at which liberation from game-playing (karmic conditioning) is immediately possible, or from which consciousness descends into increasingly complex hallucinatory states.

## The Doctrine of Immediate Liberation

The foundational teaching of the First Bardo holds that any individual who has received practical preparation can, if the instructions are remembered, "be set face to face with the ecstatic radiance and win illumination instantaneously, without entering upon hallucinatory struggles and without further suffering on the age-long pathway of normal evolution."

This represents the highest potential outcome of consciousness expansion: bypassing the entire cycle of game existence in a single moment of recognition. The sequence follows a three-step path: **faith** → **illumination** (with certainty) → **emancipation**. Success requires both unusual preparation in consciousness expansion and "much calm, compassionate game playing (good karma)."

## Liberation as Neural Silence

Liberation is defined with striking precision: "the nervous system devoid of mental-conceptual activity." This is framed not as a mystical abstraction but as a neurological state — the mind in quiescence, alert, awake but not active. The text explicitly connects this to the highest state of *dhyana* (deep meditation) while still united to a human body.

The author draws a remarkable parallel between Buddhist metaphysics and modern astrophysics, citing [[george-gamow]]"s concept of "ylem" — the primordial mixture of dissociated matter that existed before the formation of galaxies, stars, and atoms. At this first point of [[powell-omega-point-and-cosmic-evolution]], there existed "only the Unbecome, the Unborn, the Unformed." The Tibetan Buddhists suggest that the uncluttered intellect can experience what astrophysics confirms theoretically. The cosmological awareness exists in the cortex; neurons "know" because they are linked directly to the process.

The [[buddha-vairochana]], the Dhyani Buddha of the Center, is associated with the "Central Realm of the Densely-Packed" — described as the seed of all universal forces densely packed together. This convergence of modern cosmology and ancient lamaism is presented as requiring no complicated explanation: the awareness is neurologically embedded.

## Symptoms of Ego-Loss

The text provides a detailed taxonomy of physical sensations that herald the onset of ego-loss, mapped to Tibetan elemental symbolism:

1. **Bodily pressure** — "earth-sinking-into-water"
2. **Clammy coldness followed by feverish heat** — "water-sinking-into-fire"
3. **Body disintegrating or blown to atoms** — "fire-sinking-into-air"
4. **Pressure on head and ears** — colloquially called "rocket-launching-into-space"
5. **Tingling in extremities**
6. **Feelings of body melting or flowing as if wax**
7. **Nausea**
8. **Trembling or shaking**, beginning in pelvic regions and spreading up the torso

These symptoms are to be recognized as signs heralding transcendence. The instruction is explicit: avoid treating them as symptoms of illness; accept them, merge with them, enjoy them. The text notes that nausea occurs often with morning-glory seeds or peyote, rarely with mescaline, and infrequently with LSD or psilocybin.

## The Primary Clear Light

The Primary Clear Light is the first flash of ego-transcendent awareness — "the infallible mind of the pure mystic state." This is awareness of energy transformations with no imposition of mental categories. Its duration varies with the individual depending on experience, security, trust, preparation, and surroundings. In those with even a little practical experience of tranquil non-game awareness, this state can last from thirty minutes to several hours.

For those heavily dependent on ego games and who dread giving up control, the illuminated state endures only "so long as it would take to snap a finger" or "as long as the time taken for eating a meal." The text emphasizes that the consciousness-expansion process is the reverse of birth: birth is the beginning of game life, while ego-loss is a temporary ending of game life.

## The Needle and Thread Simile

The *[[metzner-leary-bardo-thodol-and-tao-te-ching-adaptation]]* employs a vivid metaphor for the instability of the Clear Light state: a needle balanced on a thread. So long as the needle retains its balance, it remains on the thread. Eventually, however, "the law of gravitation (the pull of the ego or external stimulation) affects it, and it falls." Similarly, consciousness in the ego-transcendent state momentarily enjoys perfect equilibrium, but the average human being "lacks the power to function in it." Karmic (game) propensities becloud consciousness with thoughts of personality, individualized being, and dualism.

## The Secondary Clear Light

If the Primary Clear Light is not recognized, a Secondary Clear Light dawns. The first flash produces a state of ecstasy of the greatest intensity — "every cell in the body is sensed as involved in orgastic creativity." The Secondary Clear Light is less intense but still accessible through remembrance of the instructions.

Three characteristic phenomena accompany this phase:

### Wave Energy Flow
The individual becomes aware of being part of and surrounded by a charged field of energy, almost electrical in character. The prepared person relaxes and allows forces to flow through. Two dangers must be avoided: the attempt to control or to rationalize this energy flow — either reaction indicates ego-activity and the loss of First Bardo transcendence.

### Biological Life-Flow
The person becomes aware of physiological and biochemical processes: rhythmic pulsing activity within the body, sensed as powerful motors or generators continuously throbbing and radiating energy. An endless flow of cellular forms and colors flashes by. Internal biological processes may be heard with characteristic swooshing, crackling, and pounding noises. The critical insight: these processes are a billion years older than the learned conceptual mind, and the ego cannot be dragged into molecular processes of life.

### Kundalini Energy
Ecstatic energy movement felt in the spine — the base of the backbone seems to be melting or on fire. If quiet concentration is maintained, energy flows upward through several ganglionic centers (*chakras*) to the brain, where it is sensed as a burning sensation in the top of the cranium. Tantric adepts devote decades to releasing these energies, called **Kundalini** (the Serpent Power). These sensations are accompanied by the most intense feelings of joy and illumination in prepared persons.

## The Flashback to Ego

The first ecstasy usually ends with a momentary flashback to the ego condition, accompanied by a concern with identity: "Who am I now? Am I dead or not dead? What is happening?" The surroundings and companions appear as before, but with penetrating sensitivity — the ego grasp is not quite as sure. Neither frightening apparitions nor heavenly visions have begun. This is described as "a most sensitive and pregnant period" where the remainder of the experience can be pushed one way or another depending on preparation and emotional climate.

## Guidance Protocols

The text provides detailed guidance protocols for different types of participants:

- **Experienced persons / introverts**: Remember the situation, stay calm, let the experience take you where it will. The experienced person can usually turn off external pressure and return to illumination.
- **Extroverts**: May become pleasantly distracted by colors, sounds, and people. If transcendental awareness is desired, concentrate on an ideal contemplative personage (Buddha, Christ, Socrates, Einstein, Lao Tse) and follow their model.
- **Novitiates**: The best procedure is to make a reassuring sign and do nothing. They will have read the manual and will have some guidepost. If guidance is requested, repeat the instructions, explain what is happening, and urge them to release ego struggle.

The text includes a crucial warning: "no words can communicate experience." Reading the manual is extremely useful, but the analogy is given of a person who has heard a detailed description of swimming yet never been in the water — suddenly diving in, they find themselves unable to swim.

## The Mystic Non-Self vs. Mystic Self

The Secondary Clear Light produces a fascinating dialogue between pure transcendence (which knows no self, no concepts) and a state of conceptual lucidity where the knowing self hovers within transcendent terrain. The flashing between these states — pure ego-less unity and lucid, non-game selfhood — produces "an intellectual ecstasy and understanding that defies description. Previous philosophic reading will suddenly take on living meaning."

This illuminates one of the oldest debates in [[fukuoka-western-eastern-philosophy-agriculture]]: "Is it better to be part of the sugar or to taste the sugar?" The psychedelic experience, the text suggests, allows experimental mysticism that may let one experience both states and the flashing between them — knowledge that academic monks could only theorize about.
