## Types of Contaminants

Contaminant invaders appear in various colors from pastels to black. Common
molds include green, blue-green, pink, orange, and black varieties, typically
appearing as fuzzy or powdery growths. If any colored growth appears, the
culture is doomed and the jar should be discarded.
