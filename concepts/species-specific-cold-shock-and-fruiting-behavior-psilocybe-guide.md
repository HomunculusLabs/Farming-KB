The general fruiting timeline for casings placed into fruiting bins is as follows:

1. **Pinning phase**: First pinheads appear **6–15 days** after casings are
   introduced to the fruiting environment. The wide range reflects differences in
   species, strain vitality, and environmental conditions such as humidity, fresh
   air exchange, and light exposure.
2. **Maturation phase**: Once pinheads have formed, mature mushrooms are ready for
   harvest in approximately **5–7 days**. This rapid growth period requires
   consistent environmental conditions to ensure healthy development.
