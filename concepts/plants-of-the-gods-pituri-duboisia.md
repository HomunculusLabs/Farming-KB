## Botanical Description

Duboisia hopwoodii F. von Mueller, known as Pituri Bush or Poison
Bush, belongs to the Solanaceae (Nightshade Family). It is a shrub
native to the arid interior of Australia, where it grows in the dry
regions of central and western Australia. The plant produces narrow
leaves that are harvested for their psychoactive properties.

## The Pituri Trade

The trade networks surrounding Pituri were among the most extensive
in Aboriginal Australia. Leaves harvested from Duboisia plants in
central Australia were traded along established routes to distant
communities. The trade was so significant that Pituri from the
best-known source areas became a form of currency and a marker of
social status. The plant was sometimes referred to simply as "the
drug" in early anthropological accounts.

## Duration of Use

The book notes that Pituri leaves have been used for at least 40,000
