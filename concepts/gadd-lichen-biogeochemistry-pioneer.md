## Mechanisms of rock weathering by lichens

Lichens weather rocks through multiple mechanisms. Physical
weathering occurs through penetration of hyphae into rock crevices and
intergranular spaces, expansion and contraction of the thallus with
wetting and drying cycles, and formation of oxalate crystals that
pry apart mineral grains. Chemical weathering involves production of
organic acids (oxalic acid, usnic acid, and other lichen acids) that
solubilise minerals, and acidification of the microenvironment at the
rock-lichen interface.
