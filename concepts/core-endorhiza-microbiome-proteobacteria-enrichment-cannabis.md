## The Two-Tier Selection Model in Cannabis

The two-tier selection model predicts that [[methylophilus-cannabis-endorhiza-strain-specificity]]
involves dramatic restructuring at the phylum level. The most prominent
change is a sharp decline in Acidobacteria and corresponding increase
in Proteobacteria and Actinobacteria. The most significant OTU
abundance difference was the decrease in Acidobacteria order iii1-15 in
endorhiza samples (Bonferroni-corrected ANOVA p = 1.12e-7), reflecting
the oligotrophic adaptations of Acidobacteria poorly suited to the
nutrient-rich root interior. Of 51 OTUs differing between sample types,
17 that increased in the endorhiza were predominantly Proteobacteria,
including several from Rhizobiales.

Mean abundance correlations tracked increasing selective pressure:
bulk soil to rhizosphere showed Pearson's rho of 0.92, rhizosphere to
endorhiza showed rho of 0.63, and bulk soil to endorhiza showed rho of
0.42.
