Cannabis is a tall, erect, annual herb in the family
Cannabaceae. As described by Robert Connell Clarke, it is one
of the oldest cultivated plants, with thousands of years of
co-evolution alongside human [[comparison-cannabis-sativa-vs-cannabis-indica]] L.,
with several subspecies: C. sativa subsp. sativa (tall,
narrow-leafleted plants adapted to tropical and subtropical
latitudes), C. sativa subsp. indica (shorter, broader
leafleted plants from Central Asia), and C. sativa subsp.
ruderalis (small, weedy plants from Central Europe and Russia).

## Propagation Methods

Cannabis can be propagated sexually through seeds or asexually
through cloning (cuttage, layerage, or root division). Sexual
propagation involves recombination of genetic material from two
parents, producing variation among offspring. Asexual propagation
