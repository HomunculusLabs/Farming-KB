## Indigenous Use and Cultural Significance

Mexico harbors the greatest diversity of neurotropic fungi in the world, with
76 species of which 44 belong to Psilocybe — representing 39% of all known
Psilocybe species globally. Guzmán (1997) documented more than 200 common names
for these fungi in Mexican indigenous languages, including Nahuatl, Mazatec,
Mixtec, Zapotec, Chinantec, and Trique, reflecting their deep cultural
integration across dozens of ethnic groups.
