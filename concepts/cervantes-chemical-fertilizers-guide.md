## Soluble Chemical Fertilizers

Soluble chemical fertilizers are an excellent choice for indoor
container cultivation. They dissolve in water and offer precise control
over [[cervantes-green-algae-growing-medium]]
- Allow precise adjustment of available nutrients
- High-quality hydroponic fertilizers using food-grade nutrients are
  the best value

### Recommendations
- Use high-quality hydroponic fertilizers with completely soluble
  food-grade nutrients
- Avoid low-quality fertilizers that do not list all necessary
  micronutrients on the label
- Local grow shop owners often know which formulations work best with
  local water quality
- Always read the entire fertilizer label and follow directions

## Granular Chemical Fertilizers

Chemical granular fertilizers work well but carry significant risks:

- Can easily be over-applied, creating toxic soil conditions
- Are almost impossible to leach out fast enough to save the plant
