# Amanita Muscaria Eucharist Wafer Preparation

The Eucharist wafer preparation is a method of creating shelf-stable, portable sacrament from dried [[amanita-muscaria-drying-and-preparation]] mushroom material and its [[amanita-muscaria-ambrosia-preparation]] or [[amanita-muscaria-drying-and-preparation]] pieces (properly dried and aged)
- Clean coffee grinder or mortar and pestle
- Fine mesh strainer or screen (window screen works well)
- Sealable container for storage

### Procedure

1. **Place dried Amanita muscaria pieces into a clean, dry coffee grinder**
2. **Grind to the finest possible powder** -- The finer the grind, the more efficient the extraction
3. **Sift through a fine mesh strainer** -- Collect the powder that passes through; return larger pieces to the grinder
4. **Repeat grinding and sifting** until all material passes through the strainer
5. **Store herb flour in an airtight container** away from heat, light, and moisture

### Important Notes

- Do not grind the mushroom before it is thoroughly dried -- moisture will gum up the grinder
- A clean grinder is essential; cross-contamination with coffee or other substances affects taste
- The powder degrades faster than intact pieces due to increased surface area exposure to air
- Only powder as much as will be used within a few weeks
- Herb flour has a mild, sweet, nutty taste

### Uses for Herb Flour

Once the herb flour is prepared, it serves as the base material for several sacramental preparations:

- **Direct consumption** -- Encapsulate in gelatin capsules or mix with food
- **Honey drops** -- Mix herb flour with honey to create sweet, portable sacrament
- **Wafer preparation** -- Press herb flour into solid forms
- **Magic food** -- Add to soups, stews, or oatmeal (Siberian method described by Wasson)

## The Sacred Wine (Blood of Christ)

The wine component of the Eucharist is the  preparation. The most historically relevant version substitutes grape juice for water in the extraction:

### Preparation

1. Use approximately 1 oz (28 grams) of dried Amanita muscaria per quart of grape juice
2. Soak for 8-12 hours
3. Strain and press the mushroom pieces to recover more juice
4. Add a few drops of vinegar per cup of juice at the start (seems to increase potency)

### The Dilution Paradox

This wine has a unique property: it gets more potent (up to a point) from being diluted with water. For best results, dilute 2-4 cups water per cup of wine before drinking.

This explains the ancient practice of always diluting wine before drinking:

- **Greek practice:** 2:1 to 3:1 water-to-wine ratio
- **Roman practice:** 2:1 to 4:1 ratio
- **[[allegro-fertility-philosophy-of-the-ancient-near-east]] East:** Up to 20:1 ratio

These dilution ratios are impossible for alcoholic wine but perfectly consistent with a water-soluble psychoactive extract. See [[amanita-muscaria-drying-and-preparation]] -- Essential pre-processing
