## Ancient Origins of the Fungal Kingdom

Fungi are among the oldest organisms on Earth, with fossil evidence and
molecular clock analyses suggesting their origin dates back approximately one to
two billion years. The earliest undisputed fungal fossils appear in the
Ordovician period, roughly 450 million years ago, though controversial
microfossils from the Precambrian may push the fungal lineage back much further.
Molecular phylogenetics, using ribosomal RNA sequences, indicates that fungi
diverged from animals roughly one billion years ago, making the fungal and
animal kingdoms sister groups.
