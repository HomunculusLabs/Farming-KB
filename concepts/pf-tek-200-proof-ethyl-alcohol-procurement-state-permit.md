# PF TEK 200-Proof Ethyl Alcohol Procurement and State Permit Process

## Overview

The [[pf-tek]] [[emcdda-free-spore-ring-europe-spore-distribution-network]] for laboratory and hospital alcohol supplies.

### The State Liquor Board Permit Process

If a permit is required in the purchaser's state, the following procedure applies:

1. **Contact the state liquor board** — usually located in the state capital
2. **Request an application** for an ethyl alcohol permit
3. **Pay the fee** — typically $5 to $10
4. **Complete the application** — which includes a question about the intended use

### Suggested Use Description

The application asks what the alcohol will be used for. PF advises writing something the licensing authority would find acceptable:

> "Non-toxic surface sterilizing plus herb extraction — preservation — tincture — and perfume making"

This description positions the use within legitimate, recognized applications that do not raise regulatory concerns.

PF describes the poison warnings on alcohol bottles and the permit requirements as "bureaucratic nonsense" that results in the state collecting revenue from the sale. ## Role in the Extraction Process

### Initial Extraction

1. Thoroughly dried (rock hard) mushrooms are pulverized in a ziplock freezer bag using the rubber heel of a large shoe
2. Mushroom material is placed into a bottle with a tight-fitting leak-proof cap
3. Enough 200-proof ethyl alcohol is added to cover the material and make a loose slurry
4. The bottle is shaken well and left to sit for 24 hours, with periodic shaking to activate extraction

### Secondary Extraction

After filtering off the first alcohol extract, the mushroom material is retrieved from the filters and resoaked with fresh alcohol for a second extraction cycle. This double-extraction maximizes alkaloid recovery from the mushroom material.

### Evaporation and Concentration

Combined extracts are placed in a glass with a small electric fan directed down into the surface to create ripples. The evaporation process takes several hours depending on volume. As alcohol evaporates, residue on the inside of the glass is washed back into the solution.

PF notes that fumes are harmless because the alcohol is a drinkable spirit, but warns to keep flames away — pure alcohol is highly flammable. This safety consideration is critical: the extraction should be performed in a well-ventilated area away from any ignition sources, stoves, or open flames. The fan setup should not be placed near any electrical equipment that could spark.

The target concentration is 1 to 3 or more grams of dried mushroom material per 1 oz of final extract alcohol, described as "a potent brew." The more concentrated the extract becomes, the less alcohol needs to be consumed per dose, though this also increases the risk of dosing errors.

## Storage of Final Product

The concentrated mushroom liqueur has a pungent mushroomy aroma ("like fungi perfume"). It is stored in small screw-cap bottles or vials in the freezer. Since ethyl alcohol does not freeze solid, it remains liquid at freezer temperatures, maintaining the extract in solution.

## Advantages of Ethyl Alcohol as Extraction Solvent

PF specifically chose 200-proof ethyl alcohol over other solvents because:
- **Non-toxic**: Unlike methanol or other industrial solvents, ethyl alcohol is safe for human consumption
- **Obtainable**: Available through legal retail channels with minimal paperwork
- **No chemical contamination**: The alcohol is untainted with chemicals and poisons
- **Drinkable**: Can be acquired from a liquor store (with permit in most states)
- **Safe fumes**: Evaporation produces non-toxic fumes unlike petroleum-based solvents

## Dosage Precision

PF emphasizes accuracy when dispensing the concentrated liqueur. A syringe is used for precise measurement because "a slight over calculation could put a slightly reluctant psychonaut on Pluto when only the moon was targeted." This underscores the importance of careful dosing with concentrated extracts compared to eating whole dried mushrooms, where the active compound concentration is more predictable.

### Dosing Considerations

The concentrated form of the extract means that small volume differences translate to significant dose differences. PF recommends:
- Using a graduated syringe (10cc) for accurate measurement
- Starting with a small test dose when using a new batch
- Accounting for the fact that concentration may vary between extraction batches
- Noting that the extraction efficiency can depend on mushroom quality, age at harvest, and drying method

## Relationship to PF TEK Harvesting Standards

The quality of the final extract depends entirely on the quality of the [[shirota-4-hydroxyindole-acetyl-protection-psilocin-synthesis-starting-material]]. PF's extraction technique is designed to work with mushrooms that have been:
- Harvested young (before or as the veil breaks)
- Cool-dried using desiccant (see pf-tek-desiccant-types-sourcing-cool-drying-technique.md)
- Stored properly to prevent moisture reabsorption
- Pulverized thoroughly to maximize surface area for solvent contact

Using mature, sporulating, or improperly dried mushrooms will produce a weaker extract regardless of the extraction solvent quality.
