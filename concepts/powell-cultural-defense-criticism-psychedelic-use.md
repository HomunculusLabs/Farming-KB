## Baudelaire and the Pattern of Rejection

The pattern of initial enthusiasm followed by vehement rejection is not
unique to Koestler. The 19th century French poet Baudelaire eventually
came to oppose psychoactive substances after having once used opium and
cannabis for creative purposes. Like other writers of his era including
Byron, Shelley, Balzac, De Quincey, and Coleridge, Baudelaire had
experienced psychoactive plant products but later came to despise them as
the root of evil and misleading, treating them as the cunning tools of
the Devil for thwarting mankind from reaching God.
