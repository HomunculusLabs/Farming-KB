Practical implication: harvesting all mushrooms at the same stage of development (just as the veil tears) ensures the most consistent potency across the harvest. It also prevents spores from dropping onto the substrate, which can complicate subsequent flushes.

#### Multiple Flushes

A single PF TEK cake can produce multiple "flushes" (crops) of mushrooms:
- **First flush:** Typically the largest yield and highest average potency.
- **Second flush:** Moderate yield, slightly reduced potency.
- **Third flush and beyond:** Decreasing yields. Potency may be variable.

Most cultivators harvest 2-4 flushes before the cake is exhausted or contamination sets in. Total yield from a single half-pint PF TEK cake is typically 3-7 grams dried across all flushes.

## Dosage Guidelines

### Paul Stamets' Dosage Scale (P. cubensis, Dried)

The following dosage guidelines, attributed to Paul Stamets, are widely cited in cultivation and use communities. They refer to dried P. cubensis mushrooms, which are moderate in potency relative to other species:

| Dose (dried) | Effect Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 0.25 - 0.75 g | Sub-threshold | Subtle mood enhancement, no significant visual effects |
| 1.0 g | Mild | Noticeable perceptual changes, enhanced colors, light euphoria |
| 1.5 - 2.0 g | Light to moderate | Enhanced visual perception, mood elevation, mild introspection |
| 2.5 - 3.0 g | Moderate | Visual waves and patterns, altered time perception, enhanced introspection |
| 3.5 - 4.0 g | Strong | Intense visuals, significant cognitive alteration, 4-5 hour experience |
| 5.0 g | Very strong | Overwhelming experience, ego dissolution possible, approximately 6 hours |
| 7.0+ g | Not recommended | Extreme dose, high risk of psychological distress, not advised |

### Important Caveats

- These guidelines are for P. cubensis specifically. More potent species (P. semilanceata, P. azurescens) require proportionally lower doses.
- Individual sensitivity varies. First-time users should start with low doses (0.5-1.0 g).
- Tolerance develops rapidly. The same dose taken within 3-7 days produces significantly diminished effects. Wait at least 7-14 days between experiences for full sensitivity.
- Fresh mushrooms are approximately 90% water. Multiply the dried dose by 10 for fresh equivalent (e.g., 1g dried ≈ 10g fresh).
- Body weight has modest influence; set (mindset) and setting (environment) are far more important determinants of the experience quality.
