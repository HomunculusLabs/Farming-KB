### Perennials Over Annuals
If at all possible, substitute perennial for annual crop,
and never practice frequent cultivation. Perennial root
systems maintain soil structure and continuously cycle
nutrients, while annual cultivation exposes soil to
leaching rains.

## Mineral Amendments for Tropical Soils

### Basalt Dust
Basalt provides essential silica, calcium, iron, magnesium,
and [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]].

### Coral and Shell
On coral cays and near coasts, coral and shell provide
calcium carbonate. However, on calcium-rich tropical soils,
fine rock phosphate yields more slowly and is therefore
more likely to provide long-term benefits than soluble
superphosphate, which rapidly forms insoluble calcium
triphosphate (platin or calcrete).

### Bamboo Mulch
Bamboo mulch supplies essential silica, which is often
deficient in heavily leached tropical soils. Silica
strengthens plant cell walls and improves resistance to
