possible causes exist for insomnia, it is interesting to note that its
preceding symptoms mirror the condition's effects. Depending on the severity,

### The Farnborough Study (2004)

The Farnborough study revealed an apparent self-balancing mechanism between
CBD and THC. Dose-dependent THC appears to have sedative effects, while
dose-dependent CBD appears to have some alerting properties.

In this study, 8 healthy human volunteers (4 males, 4 females; 21 to 34
years old) received an oromucosal spray of either placebo or 15 mg THC or 5 mg
THC with 5 mg CBD or 15 mg THC with 15 mg CBD.

The study concluded that THC appears to have some sedative effects while CBD
appears to have some alerting properties.

### Clinical Implications of the THC/CBD Balance

This self-balancing mechanism between THC and CBD is significant. It suggests
that whole-plant cannabis preparations, which contain both cannabinoids in
varying ratios, may provide a more balanced sleep effect than isolated
cannabinoids alone. The sedating properties of THC combined with the modulating
properties of CBD may explain why many patients report improved sleep with
whole-plant cannabis use.

## Strain-Specific Considerations

Practical reports from patients using cannabis to improve their sleep have
shown that the use of indica and indica-dominant strains with their particular
mix of cannabinoid ratios, namely a relatively lower THC:CBD combination,
encourages sedation, relaxation, and grounding effects.
