### Entoloma lividum (Leaden Entoloma)

Known around Dijon as "le grand empoisonneur de la Cote d"Or." Causes violent sickness, diarrhoea, and occasional death. The symptoms are very severe and may be fatal, attributed to definite resinoid substances acting on the mucous lining of the intestine.

### Gyromitra esculenta

An Ascomycete with a characteristically brain-like, dark chestnut-brown cap. Perfectly safe when cooked or dried but has caused serious accidents and death when eaten raw. Poisoning attributed to helvellic acid acting on red blood corpuscles, though German cases suggest the action is not simple haemolysis. Large quantities are exported annually from Poland, principally to Germany.

## Species Often Mistaken for Poisonous but Harmless

- **Amanita mappa (False Death Cap):** Long thought poisonous; now known harmless though worthless due to offensive flavor. Distinguished from A. phalloides by lemon-yellow or white cap without green or dark fibrils.
- **Lactarius torminosus:** Not really poisonous; acrid principle destroyed by cooking. Favorite toadstool in Finland.
- **Russula emetica, R. fragilis, R. fellea:** Acrid taste but irritant dispersed by cooking; best avoided.
- **Boletus luridus, B. erythropus:** Both edible despite vivid blue color changes; flesh becomes yellow during cooking.
- **Boletus Satanas:** Powers of evil much overrated; probably very indigestible rather than truly poisonous. Eaten in Czechoslovakia and parts of Italy.
- **Volvaria gloiocephala:** Formerly considered deadly; harmless and much eaten in Portugal and Algeria.

## General Safety Principles

1. Learn to recognise individual species and learn their reputations -- there is no shortcut.
2. Eat fungi fresh -- as they age they may become harmful through bacterial or mould action. Ramsbottom criticises the London shop practice of exposing mushrooms for sale so long as they retain their shape.
3. Be aware of personal allergy or idiosyncrasy (no more frequent with fungi than with milk, eggs, or strawberries, but more alarming when it occurs).
4. Some species safe when cooked are discomforting when not. Abroad some toadstools are eaten raw in salads but this is not a practice to follow without proper consideration.
