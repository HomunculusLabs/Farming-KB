## Supplement Groups

Supplements are grouped by nitrogen content:

### Group I: High Nitrogen, No [[animal-manure-guide]] s
- Cow manure (0.5% N)
- Pig manure (0.3-0.8% N)
- Rarely used except where horses and chickens are unavailable.

### Group VI: Hay
- Alfalfa (2.0-2.5% N) and clover (2% N), used at 20% of dry weight.

### Group VII: Minerals
- **Gypsum** (calcium sulfate): Essential for all composts at 50-100
  lbs per dry ton. Improves physical structure, increases water
  holding capacity, counteracts harmful concentrations of K, Mg, P,
  and Na, and supplies calcium for mushroom metabolism.
- **Limestone flour** (calcium carbonate): Used to buffer acidic
  supplements like grape pomace (pH 4).

## Carbon:Nitrogen Ratio

A well-balanced compost holds an optimum nutritional level. The C:N
ratio gradually decreases: approximately 30:1 at make-up, 20:1 at
filling, and 17:1 at spawning. Over-supplementation with nitrogen
causes prolonged ammonia release, while excess carbohydrates leave
residual carbon compounds that feed competitors.
