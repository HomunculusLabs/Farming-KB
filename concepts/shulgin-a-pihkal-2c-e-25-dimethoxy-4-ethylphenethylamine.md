# 2C-E (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Ethylphenethylamine)

## Chemical Identity

2C-E is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine and compound #24 in Shulgin's PiHKAL. Its full
chemical name is 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylphenethylamine. It belongs to the 2C family and features an
ethyl group at the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine scaffold. Shulgin regarded 2C-E
as one of the "magical half-dozen" phenethylamines — a small group of compounds with
particularly remarkable properties.

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage**: 10-25 mg orally
- **Duration**: 8-12 hours
- **Onset**: Within minutes (initial awareness), building gradually to peak over 2-4 hours

2C-E has a notably steep dose-response curve. At 10 mg, rich +++ experiences have been reported,
while a 30 mg trial was described as "very frightening" by one subject. This narrow therapeutic
window demands careful dose measurement and respect for individual sensitivity.

## Synthesis

The synthesis of 2C-E proceeds through 2,5-dimethoxyacetophenone, which is converted to
2,5-dimethoxy-1-ethylbenzene via Wolff-Kishner reduction. This intermediate undergoes
formylation (via dichloromethyl methyl ether and stannic chloride) to yield
2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylbenzaldehyde. A Henry reaction with nitromethane produces the
nitrostyrene intermediate (2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethyl-beta-nitrostyrene, mp 99-100°C), which is
reduced with lithium aluminium hydride (LAH) to give 2C-E as the free base.

Two reduction routes were documented: one using LAH with [[fukuoka-textdoc-fertilizer-soil-destruction-sulfuric-acid]] in THF, and another
using LAH in THF alone. Both produced the hydrochloride salt, described as "magnificent white
crystals" and "lustrous white crystals" in different preparations. The malononitrile derivative
of the aldehyde intermediate formed red crystals (mp 123-124°C), useful for identification.

## Qualitative Effects

### Visual and Aesthetic Effects

2C-E is renowned for its powerful visual and aesthetic enhancement properties. At 20 mg, one
subject described the garden outside their window appearing as if painted directly on the glass
— "every petal of flower and tuft of grass and leaf of tree was carefully sculptured in fine
strokes of oil paint." Another reported a picture on their wall coming to life with continuous,
exhausting motion — "the wind was blowing, and his horse was at full gallop, and his cape was
flapping in the storm."

These reports illustrate 2C-E's capacity to transform ordinary visual perception into immersive,
cinematic experiences. The compound is often described as a "truly enjoyable esthetic enhancer"
at lower doses.

### Emotional Range and Depth

2C-E produces a wide emotional range that can include both ecstatic and challenging states:

- At 16 mg, users report clear, insightful conversation with remarkable honesty and a centered
  feeling of well-being
- At higher doses, the experience can include anxiety, especially during onset. One subject
  described "voices in my head talking to me, about all my worst fears, a jumble of warnings and
  deep fears spinning faster" — though this passed within twenty minutes
- The "devil-angel pairing" is notable: one partner experienced an ecstatic white-light ascent
  while another fought through a "brown ooze," illustrating the compound's capacity for
  simultaneous divergent experiences in different individuals

### Cognitive Effects

2C-E is characterized by its intensity rather than its euphoria. Several subjects noted that
they "don't think I like it, since it isn't that much fun" but "intend to explore it again" —
suggesting a compound that rewards contemplative, serious engagement rather than casual
recreational use.

## The Amphetamine Homologue: DOET

The 3-carbon amphetamine analog of 2C-E is DOET (4-ethyl-2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine), which is
considerably more potent and much longer in action than 2C-E. The extension from phenethylamine
to amphetamine consistently increases duration and potency in the 2C/DO series.

## Chemical Extension: 2CE-5EtO ("Eternity")

One Tweetio (5-ethoxy homologue) of 2C-E is known: 5-ethoxy-4-ethyl-2-methoxyphenethylamine,
designated 2CE-5EtO. Key properties include:

- Nitrostyrene intermediate melting point: 110-110.5°C
- Hydrochloride melting point: 184-185°C
- Effective dosage: 10-15 mg (comparable to parent 2C-E)
- Duration: Extremely long — 3 to 4 hours to reach plateau, with effects still evident the
  next day after a night's sleep. Experiments were sometimes interrupted with Valium or Halcion
  at the 16-hour mark
- Nickname: "Eternity" — given by one subject due to its extraordinarily long duration

## Shulgin's Assessment

Shulgin's personal first experience with 2C-E was described as "really profound" and became the
subject of a chapter in the narrative portion of PiHKAL. He characterized 2C-E as "a difficult
and worth-while material. A very much worth-while material." The compound rewards careful,
intentional exploration but demands respect for its steep dose-response curve and long duration.

## Safety Considerations

Key safety considerations for 2C-E include:

- Steep dose-response curve: small increases above the effective range can produce overwhelming
  effects
- Long duration of 8-12 hours (or much longer with the 5EtO homologue) requires substantial
  time commitment
- Onset anxiety is possible, particularly at higher doses, though typically resolves within
  20-30 minutes
- As with all [[optical-isomers-psychedelic-phenethylamines]], [[lsd-set-and-setting-framework]] significantly influence the quality
  of the experience
