## 2C-E (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Ethylphenethylamine)

## Chemical Identity

2C-E is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine and compound #24 in Shulgin's PiHKAL. Its full
chemical name is 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylphenethylamine. It belongs to the 2C family and features an
ethyl group at the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine scaffold. Shulgin regarded 2C-E
as one of the "magical half-dozen" phenethylamines — a small group of compounds with
particularly remarkable properties.

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage**: 10-25 mg orally
- **Duration**: 8-12 hours
- **Onset**: Within minutes (initial awareness), building gradually to peak over 2-4 hours

2C-E has a notably steep dose-response curve. At 10 mg, rich +++ experiences have been reported,
while a 30 mg trial was described as "very frightening" by one subject. This narrow therapeutic
window demands careful dose measurement and respect for individual sensitivity.

## Synthesis

The synthesis of 2C-E proceeds through 2,5-dimethoxyacetophenone, which is converted to
2,5-dimethoxy-1-ethylbenzene via Wolff-Kishner reduction. This intermediate undergoes
formylation (via dichloromethyl methyl ether and stannic chloride) to yield
2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylbenzaldehyde. A Henry reaction with nitromethane produces the
nitrostyrene intermediate (2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethyl-beta-nitrostyrene, mp 99-100°C), which is
reduced with lithium aluminium hydride (LAH) to give 2C-E as the free base.
