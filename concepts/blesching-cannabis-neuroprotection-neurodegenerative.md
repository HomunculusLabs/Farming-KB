## Multiple Sclerosis (MS)

MS involves autoimmune destruction of myelin sheaths surrounding
nerve fibers. Cannabis addresses MS through: (1) CB2-mediated
reduction of autoimmune inflammation, (2) CB1-mediated reduction
of spasticity and muscle spasms, (3) neuroprotection of remaining
myelin-producing oligodendrocytes, and (4) pain management for
neuropathic pain components. Sativex, a whole-plant cannabis extract,
is specifically approved for MS spasticity in numerous countries.
