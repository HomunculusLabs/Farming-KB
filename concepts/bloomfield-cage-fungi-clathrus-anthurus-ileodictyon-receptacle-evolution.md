# Cage Fungi — Clathrus, Anthurus, Ileodictyon, and Receptacle Evolution

forms among cage fungi is extraordinary, ranging from spherical cages to
geodesic nets, all produced from what is fundamentally the same developmental
starting point.

## Clathrus — The Spherical Cage

The genus *Clathrus* produces perhaps the most recognizable cage form among
these fungi. When its egg hatches, the receptacle expands into a roughly
spherical lattice, a delicate cage of interconnected bars. The spores are
smeared on the inner surfaces of these bars, positioned so that any insect
crawling inside or probing the structure will inevitably contact the fetid
spore slime. *[[fungal-ecology-forest-floor]] or
[[fungal-kingdom-diversity]]. Rather than forming a closed cage, its receptacle
separates into four or more bright orange arms that curl backward over the
egg from which they emerged. The result resembles a star or, as Nicholas
Money vividly describes, "the agonized contortions of a horribly injured
lobster." The inner surfaces of the arms are coated with the spore-bearing
gleba. A time-lapse recording of *Anthurus archeri* hatching reveals an
almost unsettling performance — the arms unfurl with a slow, organic
deliberateness that seems almost animate. The orange coloration likely
serves as an additional visual attractant alongside the carrion-mimicking
odor, directing insects toward the spore-laden surfaces.

## Laternea — The Vaulted Lantern

*Laternea* elaborates a form that occupies a middle ground between the
closed cage of *Clathrus* and the open arms of *Anthurus*. Its receptacle
forms long arms similar to those of *Anthurus*, but these arms are fused
at their tips, creating a vaulted chamber. Inside this vault, the stinking
gleba hangs like a reeking lantern. This configuration may offer some
protection for the spore mass from rain or desiccation while still
maintaining enough openness to release volatile attractant compounds. The
fused-tip architecture demonstrates how small modifications to the same
basic arm-forming developmental program can produce functionally distinct
structures.
