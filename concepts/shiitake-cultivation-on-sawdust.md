## Bag Culture Method

The sawdust substrate is packed into autoclavable polypropylene bags,
typically 5 to 8 pounds of wet substrate per bag. Bags are fitted with
filter patches for gas exchange during colonization. The bags are then
autoclaved at 15 psi for 90 to 120 minutes, cooled, and inoculated with
grain spawn at a 5-10% rate.
