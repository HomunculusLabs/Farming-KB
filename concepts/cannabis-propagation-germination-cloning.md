# Cannabis Propagation — Seeds, Germination, and Cloning

## Overview

Propagation is the process of creating new cannabis plants from seeds or cuttings. This chapter covers the complete propagation cycle from seed selection and germination through seedling care and cloning techniques. Proper propagation is the foundation of a successful grow.

## Indica vs. Sativa Flowering Times

Understanding the flowering characteristics of different cannabis types is essential for planning:

| Type | Flowering Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Indica | 6–8 weeks | Compact, bushy, shorter |
| Sativa | 6–9 months | Tall, leggy, longer flowering |
| Indica/Sativa hybrid | 2–4 months | Intermediate |

## Seed Germination

### Methods

Common germination methods include:
- **Paper towel method**: Seeds placed between moist paper towels.
- **Rockwool cubes**: Seeds placed directly in pre-soaked rockwool.
- **Direct soil planting**: Seeds planted ¼–½ inch deep in moist medium.

### Critical Factors

- **Temperature**: Keep warm (70–85°F / 21–29°C). Cold rooms inhibit germination.
- **Moisture**: Consistently moist but not waterlogged.
- **Darkness**: Seeds germinate best in darkness.
- **No drafts**: Drafts are a "killer" — keep germinating seeds away from windows and fans.

### Dangers During Germination

1. **Drafts** — Stunt or kill germination.
2. **Cold temperatures** — Inhibit germination rates.
3. **Excessive fertilizer** — Seedlings need only water; incorrect mixtures cause burn.
4. **Disturbing seeds** — "Digging up your seeds" to check progress can break or damage the root.
5. **Weak stems** — Some strains produce seedlings with weak stems; support with small sticks and thread (never tie above a growing shoot).

## Seedling Care

- **Water only** — No fertilizer needed for the first 1–2 weeks.
- **Light**: Moderate light intensity; seedlings are sensitive to intense light.
- **Support**: Use stakes for weak-stemmed seedlings; tie below growing shoots.

## Cloning

Cloning (taking cuttings from a mother plant) produces genetically identical copies:

- Select healthy, vigorous mother plants.
- Take cuttings from lower branches with at least 2–3 nodes.
- Use rooting hormone and place in rooting medium (rockwool, peat pellets, or water).
- Maintain high humidity (80–90%) and moderate temperature.
- Clones typically root in 7–14 days.

## Outdoor vs. Guerrilla Growing

### Outdoor Growing

- Growing on your own property.
- **Security** is the biggest concern — plants can be ripped off by thieves (including professionals in areas with a booming cannabis industry).
- Consider visibility from neighbours, roads, and aerial surveillance.

### Guerrilla Farming

- Growing away from your property in public areas or on others' land.
- Requires finding remote, hidden locations with good sunlight, water access, and soil.
- Higher risk of discovery by hikers, landowners, and law enforcement.
- Plants must be self-sufficient (no daily visits).

## Security Considerations

### Purchasing Equipment

- **Pay cash** for all grow supplies — credit card purchases at grow shops can be tracked by law enforcement with court orders.
- Seed banks are sometimes monitored through mail tracking.
- Never have seeds sent to your grow address.
- Use seed banks that practice stealth shipping without company headers.

### Indoor Security

- Prepare for unexpected visits (repairmen, gas workers, landlords).
- Some growers maintain rooms that can be "swept clean" in under a minute.
- Odour control (carbon filters) is critical for preventing detection.
