# Psilocybin Liquid Culture and Species Parameters

Mycelial liquid culture method, pure culture technique, strain maintenance, and detailed species cultivation parameters for psilocybin-containing mushrooms, from Gottlieb's Psilocybin Producer's Guide (1976).

Related: [[psilocybin-mushroom-cultivation]], [[mushroom-cultivation-basics]], [[mushroom-substrates]], [[psychedelic-compounds-reference]]

## Mycelial Liquid Culture Method (Gottlieb, 1976)

Adam Gottlieb's Psilocybin Producer's Guide describes a broth-based mycelial cultivation approach that predates modern grain spawn and monotub methods. Instead of fruiting mushrooms, this technique grows mycelium in liquid broth and extracts alkaloids directly. Key advantages: faster (10-12 days), simpler equipment, scalable.

### Broth Media Recipes
- **PDA (Potato Dextrose Yeast broth)**: 250g unpeeled potatoes boiled, strained liquid to 1L, add 10g dextrose + 1.5g yeast extract (no agar)
- **MEA broth**: 20g malt extract + 100mg K2HPO4 + 100mg CaCO3 per liter distilled water

### Culture Initiation
- Spore germination: hydrate spore print in 10ml sterile water, dilute to 100ml, spot 3-4 drops per petri dish of agar medium. Wait 3-5 days for monokaryotic mycelia, then somatogamy (mating) occurs where mycelia contact each other
- Tissue culture: surface-sterilize cap with 7% iodine solution, excise inner tissue fragments (match-head size) with flame-sterilized blade, press into agar surface
- Dikaryotic tissue from a mature mushroom can skip the mating step entirely

### Broth Cultivation
1. Transfer mycelial fragments from agar stock to Mason jars of broth using flame-sterilized inoculation loop
2. Tighten lids, shake to disperse inoculum and aerate
3. Loosen lids, incubate 10-12 days at 70-75F (21-24C)
4. Shake jars every 2-3 days for aeration and dispersion
5. Harvest at maximum growth: ~4 days after all sugar is consumed (monitor with saccharimeter)
6. Each quart jar yields 50-100g wet mycelium (5-10g dried)

## Pure Culture Technique (Gottlieb)

Critical sanitary measures for pre-flow-hood era cultivation:
- Scrub arms and hands with disinfectant soap, wear short-sleeve T-shirt
- Gargle with antiseptic mouthwash, wear surgical mask and cap
- Eliminate drafts: close doors, stuff door jambs, no animals or unnecessary people
- Work 3 feet from equipment; no swift movements
- Spray room and work surfaces with disinfectant before each session
- Autoclave: 15-20 PSI (250F) for 30 minutes in pressure cooker; higher temps caramelize sugars and inhibit growth

## Strain Maintenance and Senescence

- Stock cultures refrigerated last ~1 year
- Continual subculturing from crop cultures causes gradual weakening (senescence factor)
- Mitigate by alternating agar media types: PDA then MEA then PDA
- Perpetual farm: reinoculate fresh agar from healthy crop cultures, maintain rotation

## Psilocybin/Psilocin Testing (Paper Chromatography)

Gottlieb describes a simple home test: dry and pulverize mushroom sample, extract in unheated methanol (shake 30 min), spot on paper, develop with water-saturated butanol for 2 hours. Spray dried paper with p-dimethylaminobenzaldehyde in alcohol, then 1N HCl. Psilocybin = reddish zone; psilocin = blue-violet zone. Note: the blueing reaction alone is not diagnostic — many non-psilocybin fungi also bruise blue (e.g., Eastwood Boletus), and the Metol test is unreliable because any indole-containing organism responds.
