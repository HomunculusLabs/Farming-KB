## Composting Without a Compost Heap

Holzer's fundamental insight: "It is by no means necessary for a high-yield garden to have a compost heap." The combination of year-round mulching and skilful polyculture use provides sufficient fertility without concentrated composting.

## The Raised Bed Trench Method

An innovative composting system that integrates compost production with [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-vermicompost-and-traditional-compost]])

### Critical Factors
- Smaller material decomposes faster
- More active soil life = faster humus production
- Partial shade creates optimal conditions
- Plants chosen so compost gets light but also sun protection

## Surface Composting (Mulching)

### The Principle
"Mulching is nothing other than surface composting." A layer of organic material spread over the soil serves as [[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]]
- **Never pack down**: soil pores must remain open for soil life
- **Do not shred**: unshredded material releases nutrients more slowly and compacts less
- **Vary materials**: same nutrients repeatedly = unhealthy for soil

### Working With Mulch Seasonally
- Spring: scoop mulch aside to sow/plant; sown areas remain mulch-free
- Year-round: regularly introduce new material
- Result: hardly any weeding needed; no spring digging required after initial years
- Also effective under shrubs, trees, hedges (mirrors natural forest floor)
