## The Asarone Connection

TMA-2's natural precursor, asarone, occurs in three isomeric forms found in [[sweet-flag-acorus-calamus-ethnobotany]], sweet flag):

- **Alpha-asarone** — the trans-propenyl isomer
- **Beta-asarone** — the cis-propenyl isomer
- **Gamma-asarone** (euasarone) — the allyl isomer

Shulgin reported tasting asarone at up to 70 mg without any effects, illustrating how the propenyl/allyl side chain of the natural compound must be converted (via nitrostyrene synthesis) to the amphetamine skeleton before psychedelic activity emerges.

## The Nature Article and the Endogenous Psychotogen Hypothesis

About twenty years before PIHKAL, alexander shulgin co-authored a review article in the journal *Nature* on structure-activity relationships among one-ringed psychotomimetics. As part of this review, the authors proposed a hypothetical compound — 2,beta-dihydroxy-4,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine — as a possible "endogenous psychotogen," a molecule the brain might produce internally that could underlie naturally occurring psychotic states. This compound incorporated features of both dopamine (the 6-hydroxy group) and norepinephrine (the beta-hydroxy group). A respected scientist subsequently sent a synthesized sample, but Shulgin's own graded dosing up to only 250 micrograms (the compound was unstable and decomposed) never produced definitive results, and the project was abandoned.

## Optical Isomer Studies

Studies on the resolved optical isomers of TMA-2 reached assays of 6 mg on each separate isomer but were never taken higher. The R-isomer proved much more potent in rabbit assays, but human comparisons remain unknown — a gap that still exists in the pharmacological literature.
