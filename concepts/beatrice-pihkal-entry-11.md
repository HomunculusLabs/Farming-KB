# BEATRICE (N-Methyl-DOM)

**[[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #11**

## Chemical Identity

- **Full name:** N-Methyl-DOM; 2,5-dimethoxy-4,N-dimethylamphetamine
- **Class:** Amphetamine (N-methyl homologue of DOM)
- **Hydrochloride salt:** pale pink crystals, mp 136-137 °C
  (from ethyl acetate recrystallization)
- **Formyl intermediate:** 2,5-dimethoxy-N-formyl-4-
  methylamphetamine, fine white crystals from MeOH
- **Named for:** Beatrice of Florence, immortalized by Dante in
  the Divina Commedia (not the Dutch Beatrijs nor Berlioz's
  Beatrice)

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** Above 30 mg (DOM is active at 3 mg)
- **Duration:** 6-10 hours (though wakefulness persisted to
  24 hours at 30 mg)

## Qualitative Effects

### At 20 mg
A gentle and demanding rise over one to three hours led to an
extremely open, erotic, and responsive state. The kitchen served
as a familiar orientation point. As the experience progressed,
it showed more and more of a stimulant response, with tremor,
restlessness, and trouble sleeping. Notably, no anorexia was
observed, unlike most psychedelic amphetamines. Described as an
"OK experience."

### At 30 mg
Real physical effects predominated: diarrhea, restlessness, and
continuous body awareness of having been impacted by something.
The last few hours were spent talking, and the subject was still
awake 24 hours after the start. Mental effects did not reach a
+++ level, yet the physical disruption exceeded any mental reward.
When sleep finally came, dreams were OK but not rich. The
conclusion was: "Why go higher?"

## Synthesis Notes

### Primary route
DOM free base (5.0 g) was formylated with ethyl formate (10 mL)
at reflux for several hours, giving 2,5-dimethoxy-N-formyl-4-
methylamphetamine (5.5 g crude, 3.8 g recrystallized from MeOH).
Reduction with LAH (4.0 g in 250 mL anhydrous ether, shunted
Soxhlet technique, 24 h reflux plus several days at room
temperature) yielded the N-methyl amine. Workup required careful
addition of dilute H2SO4 followed by potassium sodium tartrate
and NaOH basification. The hydrochloride was recrystallized
from ethyl acetate (3.5 g, mp 136-137 °C).

### Alternative route
Methyl sulfate methylation of the benzaldehyde adduct of DOM
gave an initially different polymorph (mp 125-126 °C) with a
different infrared spectrum. After recrystallization from ethyl
acetate, it proved identical to the higher-melting form.

### N-cyclopropyl analogue
Efforts to fuse free-base DOM with ethyl cyclopropane carboxylate
failed, but the acid chloride in pyridine gave the amide (mp
156-157 °C from MeOH, Anal. C16H23NO3). This reduced smoothly to
N-cyclopropyl-2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine hydrochloride
(mp 153-156 °C). This compound was never tasted.

## Safety and Pharmacology

BEATRICE is approximately 10x less potent than DOM based on
human testing (3 mg DOM vs >30 mg BEATRICE). This is broadly
consistent with rabbit hyperthermia studies showing approximately
25x reduction. The stimulant side effects are prominent and
