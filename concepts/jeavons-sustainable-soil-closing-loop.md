### Humus Sustainability

Ecology Action has been getting closer to achieving closed-system soil humus
sustainability. The current approach uses compost materials grown primarily
within the limits of the growing beds that receive cured compost. The goal is
to eventually grow all carbonaceous compost material within the mini-farm.
