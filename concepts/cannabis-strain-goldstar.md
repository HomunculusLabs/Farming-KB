# Goldstar

Goldstar is a sativa-dominant hybrid [[african-landrace-sativa-modern-hybrids]] from southeastern Africa, specifically the
Malawi region. African landrace sativas are prized by breeders for their
vigorous growth, long flowering times, and powerful psychedelic effects. The
Malawi Gold parent contributes these traits to Goldstar, along with its
characteristic trippy head high. After the initial cross, the resulting plants
were backcrossed over multiple generations to stabilize the desirable traits
and create a reliable seed line.

## Breeder Information

Bodhi Seeds is an American seed company that has earned a strong reputation
in the cannabis community for sourcing and working with exceptional genetics
from [[old-world-hallucinogenic-plants-overview]] sativas and contemporary breeding.

## Growing Characteristics

Goldstar is a vigorous plant that performs well in both indoor and outdoor
environments, though the breeder recommends outdoor cultivation for optimal
results. Despite the heavy sativa influence from the Malawi Gold parent, the
strain exhibits almost no stretch, which is unusual for sativa-dominant
varieties and beneficial for indoor and greenhouse growers. Key traits include:

- **Flowering time**: 10-14 weeks depending on environment (indoors vs.
  outdoors)
- **Yield**: Described as "nothing short of incredible," with heavy bud
  production
- **Growth pattern**: Very vigorous [[dom]]
