## Chemical Identity

22S,23S-Homobrassinolide is a polyhydroxylated steroid with the
following defining features:

- **Carbon skeleton**: 29-carbon ergostane-type structure (homologated
  relative to the 28-carbon brassinolide due to an additional methyl
  at C-24)
- **Stereochemistry at C-22 and C-23**: Both S-configuration
  (22S,23S-diol), which is critical for biological activity
- **A-ring hydroxylation**: 2α,3α-diol pattern characteristic of
  bioactive brassinosteroids
- **B-ring**: Contains the characteristic 7-oxolactone moiety
- **Side chain**: Extended C-8 side chain with hydroxylation at
  C-22, C-23, and an additional methyl group at C-24

The molecular formula is C29H50O6. The S-stereochemistry at positions
22 and 23 distinguishes it from the corresponding R-isomers, which
typically show reduced or absent biological activity.
