# EMCDDA Hallucinogenic Mushroom Online Retail Shops

## Overview

The emergence of online retail shops selling hallucinogenic mushrooms
represented a significant development in the European drug market during the
early 2000s. As traditional smartshops and market stalls expanded their reach
through e-commerce, consumers gained easy access to mushroom products from
a wide range of countries, including those where domestic sales were
prohibited. The EMCDDA conducted a systematic survey of online shops in
January 2006 to characterise this emerging market.

## Market Survey Methodology

To assess the scope of online mushroom retail, the EMCDDA conducted searches
via Google and AltaVista in January 2006 using four English-language keyword
combinations:

- "magic mushrooms shop"
- "buy magic mushrooms"
- "psilocybe cubensis shop"
- "buy psilocybe cubensis"

For each search engine and keyword, the first 300 results were analysed. The
searches yielded millions of hits, but after filtering, a total of 39 unique
online shops selling hallucinogenic mushroom products were identified.

## Geographic Distribution

The geographic distribution of online shops was heavily concentrated:

- **Netherlands**: 82% of all identified online shops (approximately 32 out
  of 39) were based in the Netherlands, reflecting the country's permissive
  legal environment for fresh mushrooms at the time.
- **Austria**: Four online shops selling growkits and spore prints.
- **Germany**: Two identified sites.
- **UK**: One site, linked to a Dutch online shop.
- **Poland**: One site viewed, primarily selling cannabis products, noting
  that hallucinogenic mushrooms were not currently available.

## Customer Access Patterns

Website statistics for one Dutch online shop revealed access patterns between
May 2004 and February 2006:

| Country    | Percentage of Site Traffic |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Netherlands| 73%                       |
| Belgium    | 14%                       |
| Germany    | 2%                        |
| France     | 2%                        |
| Other      | 9%                        |

No data were available on the total number of customers or sales volumes
through online channels. However, the traffic patterns suggest significant
cross-border demand, with the majority of international customers coming
from neighbouring Belgium.

## Products Sold

Online shops offered a diverse range of hallucinogenic mushroom products:

- **Fresh mushrooms**: Including Psilocybe cubensis (Mexican) and Copelandia
  cyanescens varieties, sold in quantities typically ranging from 10 to 30
  grams.
- **Truffles/sclerotia**: Psilocybe mexicana sold as "Philosopher's stones,"
  typically in 15 gram portions.
- **Spawnbags**: Containing sterile hardwood chips inoculated with mushroom
  mycelium (e.g., Psilocybe azurescens) for outdoor cultivation.
- **Growkits**: Complete cultivation kits containing colonised substrate, a
  bag with air filter, and paperclips, available for various species.
- **Spore prints**: Created by pressing mature mushroom gills onto paper, or
  as spore syringes, for home cultivation on a substrate of nutrients.

## Pricing (January 2006)

| Product                                   | Quantity   | Price Range (EUR) |
|-------------------------------------------|------------|--------------------|
| Fresh Psilocybe cubensis (Mexican)        | 30 gr      | 8.50 - 45.28       |
| Fresh Copelandia cyanescens               | 10 gr      | 13.00 - 17.50      |
| Psilocybe mexicana (Philosopher's stones) | 15 gr      | 10.00 - 17.50      |
| Spawnbag (Psilocybe azurescens)           | 1 bag      | 24.00 - 25.00      |
| Growkits                                  | 1 kit      | 25.00 - 100.00     |
| Spore prints                              | 1 print    | 9.00 - 28.00       |

Prices for growkits and spore prints varied according to the species
available. Users could also obtain various strains for approximately 1 EUR
per print from the non-profit "Free Spore Ring Europe" organisation.

## International Shipping and Legal Compliance

The majority of online shops offered international shipping. However, many
sites employed compliance measures:

- Refusing to ship to countries where sales were prohibited.
- Advising customers to verify the legal status of products before ordering.
- Providing multiple language versions (mainly English, French, and German)
  to attract foreign customers.

## Impact of Legal Changes

Legal changes had immediate impacts on online availability:

- **UK (July 2005)**: Internet sites stopped selling hallucinogenic mushrooms
  following reclassification as Class A drugs. One UK site
  (allsalvia.co.uk) was "sold out" of growkits in July 2005, with a notice
  that fresh mushrooms, truffles, and growkits were about to become
  controlled substances.
- **Ireland (January 2006)**: The online headshop THC placed a notice that
  hallucinogenic mushroom products were no longer available since becoming
  illegal on 31 January 2006.
- **Market adaptation**: UK retailers such as EveryOneDoesIt.com and
  Allsalvia.co.uk switched to selling legal alternatives including Fly Agaric
  (Amanita muscaria) and Amanita pantherina.
