## Psychedelic Session Instructions (Part IV)

Part IV of *The [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]]* by Timothy Leary, Ralph Metzner, and [[richard-alpert]] contains the actual instructions read aloud to a person during a psychedelic session. These instructions are adapted from the *Bardo Thodol* ([[jung-and-the-tibetan-book-of-the-dead]] the Dead) and organized according to the three bardos — stages of consciousness encountered after ego dissolution.

The instructions are designed to be read by a guide or companion during the session itself. They serve several functions: orienting the voyager when they become confused, preventing descent into paranoid or fearful states, and returning the voyager to First Bardo transcendence whenever possible. The manual recommends pre-recording selected passages or having a trusted companion read them at appropriate moments. Each instruction begins with "O nobly-born, listen carefully" or "O friend" — a direct, personal address that cuts through the abstract quality of the psychedelic state.

## First Bardo Instructions

The First Bardo is the experience of the Clear Light — the initial moment of ego loss and direct encounter with unconditioned reality. The instructions urge the voyager to:

- **Seek new levels of reality** beyond the ego, which is about to cease.
- **Face the Clear Light** directly: "concentrate on the unity of all living beings."
- **Hold the Clear Light** by recognizing that "the light is the life energy, the endless flame of life."
- **Surrender to the surging turmoil of color** as "ceaseless transformation of energy."
- **Recognize the Void** as the ultimate reality beyond all restless flowing electricity of life: "Your own awareness, not formed into anything possessing form or color, is naturally void."
- **Achieve Diamond consciousness** — "The All-Good Buddha" — by recognizing "the voidness of your own consciousness to be Buddhahood."

If the voyager begins slipping back toward ego-consciousness, they are reminded that hallucinations teach about self and world. If confused, they should call upon memories of friends and an admired person for grounding.

The First Bardo instructions contain some of the most poetic language in the entire manual. The voyager is told that beyond the "restless flowing electricity of life" lies "the peaceful silence of the void — the quiet bliss beyond all transformations." The text identifies this void not with nothingness but with "beginning and end itself — unobstructed, shining, thrilling, blissful." This is the state of "Diamond consciousness" or the "All-Good Buddha." The final instruction is: "Recognize the voidness of your own consciousness to be Buddhahood. Keep this recognition and you will maintain the state of the divine mind of the Buddha."

## Second Bardo Preliminary Instructions

The Second Bardo involves game hallucinations and visionary experiences. The instructions explain the three bardos clearly:

1. **Clear Light** — the primordial unstructured reality.
2. **Game hallucinations** — structured visions and symbolic content.
3. **Re-Entry** — return to ordinary ego-consciousness.

The central advice is: **"Do not cling in fondness and weakness to your old self."** The key secret is *recognition* — all apparitions are one"s own thought-forms projected from within. The text warns: "If you do not recognize your own thought forms, if you forget your preparation, the lights will daunt you, the sounds will awe you, the rays will terrify you, the people around you will confuse you." The essential instruction is:

> "The key to enlightenment: Relax. Merge yourself with them. Blissfully accept the wonders of your own creativity. Become neither attached nor afraid."

The Second Bardo instructions further emphasize that "these realms are not come from somewhere outside yourself — they come from within and shine upon you." The voyager is reminded that "the revelations too exist from eternity within the faculties of your own intellect." Above all, the instruction is: "Do nothing about the visions. They exist only within you."

The Seven Visions of the Second Bardo form a progressive sequence from internal to external, intellectual to emotional:

| Vision | Name | Mode | Focus |
|--------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | The Source | Eyes closed | Primal energy |
| 2 | Archetypal Processes | Eyes closed | Intellectual/mythic |
| 3 | Fire-Flow of Unity | Eyes closed | Emotional/organic |
| 4 | Wave-Vibration of Forms | Eyes open | Intellectual/structural |
| 5 | Vibratory Waves of Unity | Eyes open | Emotional/ecstatic |
| 6 | [[retinal-circus-and-magic-theatre]] | Both | Sensory kaleidoscope |
| 7 | Magic Theatre | Both | Mythical figures |

This structure shows the manual"s systematic approach: the first three visions are introverted (eyes closed, attending to inner processes), the next two are extroverted (eyes open, engaging with external stimuli), and the final two integrate both modes.

### Vision 1 — The Source (Radiant Energy of the Seed)

The voyager encounters the primal source of energy, described as "radiating for billions of years, ever manifesting itself in different forms." The instruction is to accept it without intellectualizing, merge with it, and "fuse in the Halo of Rainbow Light into the core of the energy dance." The goal is to "obtain Buddhahood in the Central Realm of the Densely Packed."

### Instructions for Physical Symptoms

The text emphasizes that "bodily symptoms are not drug-effects" in the ordinary sense but indicate struggle against feelings that surpass normal experience. The voyager should let feelings melt, relax, and not struggle against somatic sensations: "Your symptoms will disappear as soon as all trace of ego-centered striving disappears. Accept them as the message of the body. Welcome them. Enjoy them."

### Vision 2 — Internal Flow of Archetypal Processes

The internal life flow becomes whirling and mythic, described as "an endless parade of pure forms and sounds, dazzlingly brilliant, ever-changing." The voyager should not attempt to control it but instead experience cosmic myths of creation and manifestation directly. They are told: "Do not try to understand; there is plenty of time for that later." The warning is stark: "If you try to stop it, you may fall into hell-worlds and endure unbearable misery generated by your own mind." They are told to "avoid game interpretations" and merge in the Rainbow Light, attaining Buddhahood in "the Realm called Pre-Eminently Happy."

### Vision 3 — Fire-Flow of Internal Unity

An ecstasy of organic fire arises, described as "the ecstasy of organic fire glows in every cell." The "hard, dry, brittle husks of your ego are washing out to the endless sea of creation," associated with the red [[buddha-amitabha]]. The instruction is to release attachment: "Recognize the wisdom of your own blood. Trust the tide-force pulling you into unity with all living forms." The voyager is told to "let your heart burst in love for all life" and "merge into the Heart-Glow of the Buddha Amitabha," attaining Buddhahood in "the Realm named Exultant Love."

### Vision 4 — Wave-Vibration Structure of External Forms

External reality dissolves into energy vibrations. The voyager is told: "Everything you see dissolves into energy vibrations... There are no longer things and persons but only the direct flow of particles." They should "let your brain become a receiving set for the radiance" and remember that "all interpretations are the products of your own mind. Dispel them." If panic arises as the three-dimensional world fragments, they are warned against "a fondness for the heavy dull world of objects you are leaving" and told to "abide in the state of quietude."

### Vision 5 — Vibratory Waves of External Unity

The unity of all living forms becomes apparent. If people seem "rubbery and lifeless, like plastic puppets, be not afraid" — this is merely the ego attempting to maintain its separate identity. The voyager is told to "relax and feel the ecstatic energy-vibrations pulsing through you" and to "enjoy the feeling of complete one-ness with all life and all matter." The glowing radiance is described as "a reflection of your own consciousness — one aspect of your divine nature." The instruction warns: "Do not be attached to your old human self. Do not be alarmed at the new and strange feelings you are having." If attracted to the old self, the voyager "will be reborn shortly for another round of game-existence." They should instead "exercise humble trust and remain fearless," merging "into the heart of the Blessed Ratnasambhava in a Halo of Rainbow Light."

### Vision 6 — Retinal Circus

Kaleidoscopic patterns and the ceaseless play of elements unfold — "Earth, water, air, fire, in ever-changing forms and manifestations" that "dazzle you with its complexity and variety." The voyager is told to "relax and enjoy the rushing stream" and "do not become attached to any vision or revelation." This is described as "a great lesson in the creativity and power of the brain, freed from its learned structures." By meditating calmly on the knowledge that all visions are emanations of one"s own consciousness, the voyager "can obtain self-knowledge and be liberated."

### Vision 7 — Magic Theatre

Mythical superhuman figures appear — demons, goddesses, celestial warriors, giants, angels, Bodhisattvas, dwarfs, crusaders, elves, devils, saints, sorcerers, and archetypal figures like the Wise Old Man, the Divine Child, the Trickster, and the Shapeshifter. The instruction reminds the voyager: "The whole fantastic comedy takes place within you." These figures represent "the highest reaches of human knowledge" and should be recognized as aspects of the self.

### Instructions for Wrathful Visions

Terrifying visions are addressed directly:

- "They are your own thought-forms made visible and audible."
- "They are old friends. Welcome them."
- "None of the peaceful or wrathful visions... exist in reality. Only within your skull."

## Third Bardo Preliminary Instructions

The Third Bardo begins the return to ego-consciousness and re-entry into the game of life. The instructions describe consciousness as rising up "like a trout leaping forth out of water, striving for its original form." The voyager should:

- **Not struggle** against the rising current — "there is nothing to do, nothing to think."
- **Float back** to the "unobscured, primordial, bright, void state of your intellect."
- **Meditate on a protective figure and friends** for stability — think of them "with profound love and trust, as overshadowing the crown of your head."
- **Be aware** of feelings of supernormal power — "Do not desire them. Do not attempt to exercise them. Recognize them as signs that you are in the Third Bardo."
- **Remain calm** through frightening visions of wind, icy blasts, humming machinery, and mocking laughter — "Such imagined taunts and paranoid nightmares are the residues of selfish, ego-dominated game-playing."

The instructions conclude with guidance for the re-entry: "Let this re-entry be smooth and slow. Do not attempt to use force of will-power."

## Core Principles Across All Instructions

Several principles recur throughout every stage of the session:

1. **Non-attachment** — do not cling to any vision, identity, or interpretation.
2. **Non-fear** — nothing encountered can harm the essential self.
3. **Recognition** — all experiences are self-generated projections.
4. **Surrender** — merge with whatever arises rather than resisting.
5. **Creative acceptance** — blissfully embrace the mind's own creative power.

The instructions function as a verbal guide through unfamiliar territory, providing the voyager with a framework for interpreting and navigating ego-dissolution, visionary content, and re-entry into ordinary consciousness. Their power lies in their simplicity and directness — they do not explain *why* but tell the voyager *what to do*: recognize, relax, surrender, and trust. This mirrors the Tibetan Buddhist approach where liberation comes through recognition rather than analysis.
