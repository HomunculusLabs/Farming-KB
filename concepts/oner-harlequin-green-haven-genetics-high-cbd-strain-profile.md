# Harlequin Strain Profile (Green Haven Genetics)

## Overview

Harlequin is a high-CBD sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Green Haven Genetics that represents an important milestone in medical cannabis breeding. With a remarkable 4% [[cannabidiol-cbd]] content alongside 2% THC, Harlequin delivers therapeutic benefits with minimal psychoactivity, making it one of the most significant medical cannabis strains of its era. Its complex multi-landrace genetics—combining Colombian Gold, Thai, Swiss, and Nepalese lines—give it a rich heritage that connects modern medical breeding with classic sativa genetics.

## Breeder Information

- **Breeder**: Green Haven Genetics, USA
- **Type**: Sativa-dominant hybrid
- **Genetics**: Colombian Gold (1970s version) × Thailand × Switzerland × Nepal
- **Availability**: Clone-only (not available as seed at time of publication)
- **Location**: Bay Area residents most likely to find this strain [[oner-super-lemon-haze-greenhouse-strain-profile]]
- **Potency**: THC 2% / CBD 4%

## Lineage and Genetics

Harlequin's four-way lineage is notable for combining some of the most legendary sativa genetics from different regions. For more on cannabis breeding, see [[cervantes-cannabinoids-thc-cbd-chemistry]].

- **Colombian Gold (1970s version)**: A classic Colombian landrace that provides the foundational sativa structure and contributes to the strain's CBD production. The 1970s-era Colombian Gold genetics are distinct from modern Colombian hybrids. (see [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]]).
- **Thai**: Southeast Asian sativa genetics that contribute to the strain's uplifting effects and complex terpene profile. Thai landraces are historically associated with creative, energetic highs. (see [[tropicana-cookies]]).
- **Switzerland**: Swiss sativa genetics that may contribute to the strain's CBD-dominant profile, as Swiss hemp breeding programs have historically selected for high CBD content.
- **Nepal**: Himalayan sativa genetics from the Nepalese highlands that add resilience and complexity to the cannabinoid profile.

The combination of these four geographically diverse sativa lineages creates a genetically rich strain with a cannabinoid profile optimized for medical use.

## Cannabinoid Profile

### THC:CBD Ratio

Harlequin's most distinctive feature is its inverted THC:CBD ratio:
- **THC: 2%** — Very low by modern standards, well below the psychoactive threshold for most users
- **CBD: 4%** — Twice the THC content, placing it firmly in high-CBD territory
- **Ratio**: Approximately 1:2 THC to CBD

This profile means that Harlequin provides the therapeutic benefits of cannabis—particularly CBD's anti-inflammatory, anxiolytic, and analgesic properties—without significant intoxication. Patients can function normally while experiencing symptom relief. See [[cannabis-thc-therapeutic-profile]] for more on THC effects.

### Significance of the CBD Dominance

The high CBD content of Harlequin places it among the pioneering medical strains that demonstrated cannabis could be bred specifically for therapeutic rather than recreational use. At the time of its development, most available strains were THC-dominant, making Harlequin's CBD-forward profile revolutionary for patients who needed relief without impairment.

## Growth Characteristics

### Structure and Habit

Despite being a sativa-dominant strain, Harlequin displays surprising structural strength:
- **Height**: Taller than a typical indica but manageable; can be kept to a reasonable height with standard techniques
- **Hardiness**: Described as "incredibly hardy" for a sativa, clearly benefiting from the indica influence on structural strength
- **Vegetative period**: Relatively short vegetative period before being ready to flip into flowering
- **Flowering onset**: Blooms "a lot quicker than you might expect," with flowers "as long as your finger" appearing just 2 weeks after the flip

### Flowering

- **Flowering time**: 70 days to full maturity
- **Resin production**: By the end of flowering, buds are "absolutely caked in resin" to the point where individual buds are barely visible beneath the crystal layer
- **Branch support**: The weight of resin-heavy buds requires staking; do this sooner rather than later to prevent branch breakage
- **Drying time**: Requires a full three weeks to properly dry due to the weight and resin density of the buds

### Cultivation Notes

- Available only in clone form, which limits accessibility but ensures genetic consistency
- The short vegetative period makes it suitable for growers with space constraints
- Heavy resin production makes it attractive for hash and concentrate production
- Finger hash from post-harvest manicure is described as plentiful and high quality

## Appearance

- **Bud structure**: Long, finger-like flowers with exceptional resin coverage
- **Trichome coverage**: So heavy that buds appear "like someone just threw a bag of sugar at a tree"
- **Visual impression**: The extreme resin production gives Harlequin a premium, artisanal appearance that signals quality to experienced consumers

## Medical Applications

### Primary Medical Uses

Harlequin was bred specifically to produce high-grade hash and oils for medical patients. Its primary applications include:

- **Pain management**: CBD's analgesic properties provide effective pain relief without sedation
- **Anxiety relief**: High CBD content reduces anxiety without the paranoia sometimes associated with THC-dominant strains
- **Anti-inflammatory**: CBD's well-documented anti-inflammatory effects address chronic inflammation
- **Daytime medication**: The low THC content allows patients to medicate during work or daily activities
- **Hash and oil production**: Exceptional resin production makes it ideal for extract-based medicine

### Patient Population

Harlequin is particularly valuable for:
- Medical patients new to cannabis who are sensitive to psychoactive effects
- Patients who need to maintain cognitive function while managing symptoms
- Patients who have found THC-dominant strains too intoxicating for regular use
- Patients seeking CBD-rich medicine for specific therapeutic applications
- Elderly patients who may be more sensitive to THC's psychoactive effects
- Patients with seizure disorders who benefit from CBD's anticonvulsant properties

## Historical Significance

Harlequin represents an important milestone in cannabis breeding history:
- It demonstrated that intentional CBD-dominant breeding was possible and valuable
- It helped shift the conversation from recreational to medical cannabis genetics
- It showed breeders that landrace genetics could be combined for specific therapeutic outcomes
- It paved the way for the many high-CBD strains that followed
- Its multi-landrace heritage demonstrated the value of preserving and combining diverse genetic resources
- The success of Harlequin helped legitimize CBD as a primary therapeutic compound in cannabis

## Comparison to Other High-CBD Strains

Harlequin stands out from other CBD-rich strains in several ways:
- **Rarity of 1:2 ratio**: Most "high-CBD" strains offer a 1:1 ratio; Harlequin's CBD-dominant 1:2 profile is less common
- **Four-way landrace complexity**: Unlike many modern high-CBD crosses, Harlequin draws from four distinct landrace lineages
- **Clone-only preservation**: The genetics have been maintained clonally, preserving the original phenotype rather than being diluted through seed production
- **Resin production**: Harlequin's resin output exceeds that of many THC-dominant strains, making it particularly valuable for extraction

## Summary

Harlequin is a landmark medical cannabis strain that combines Colombian Gold, Thai, Swiss, and Nepalese genetics into a high-CBD sativa-dominant hybrid with a 4% CBD to 2% THC ratio. Bred by Green Haven Genetics specifically for medical hash and oil production, it delivers therapeutic relief without significant psychoactivity. Its incredible resin production, manageable growth characteristics, and consistent clone-only genetics make it a prized strain for medical patients and extract artists. While clone-only availability limits its reach, Harlequin remains one of the most important reference strains in the history of medical cannabis breeding.
