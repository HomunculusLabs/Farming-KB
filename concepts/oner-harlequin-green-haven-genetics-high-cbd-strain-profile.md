# Harlequin Strain Profile (Green Haven Genetics)

## Overview

Harlequin is a high-CBD sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Green Haven Genetics that represents an important milestone in medical cannabis breeding. With a remarkable 4% [[oner-super-lemon-haze-greenhouse-strain-profile]]
- **Potency**: THC 2% / CBD 4%

## Lineage and Genetics

Harlequin's four-way lineage is notable for combining some of the most legendary sativa genetics from different regions. For more on cannabis breeding, see [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]]).
- **Thai**: Southeast Asian sativa genetics that contribute to the strain's uplifting effects and complex terpene profile. Thai landraces are historically associated with creative, energetic highs. (see [[cannabis-thc-therapeutic-profile]] for more on THC effects.

### Significance of the CBD Dominance

The high CBD content of Harlequin places it among the pioneering medical strains that demonstrated cannabis could be bred specifically for therapeutic rather than recreational use. At the time of its development, most available strains were THC-dominant, making Harlequin's CBD-forward profile revolutionary for patients who needed relief without impairment.

## Growth Characteristics

### Structure and Habit

Despite being a sativa-dominant strain, Harlequin displays surprising structural strength:
- **Height**: Taller than a typical indica but manageable; can be kept to a reasonable height with standard techniques
- **Hardiness**: Described as "incredibly hardy" for a sativa, clearly benefiting from the indica influence on structural strength
- **Vegetative period**: Relatively short vegetative period before being ready to flip into flowering
- **Flowering onset**: Blooms "a lot quicker than you might expect," with flowers "as long as your finger" appearing just 2 weeks after the flip

### Flowering

- **Flowering time**: 70 days to full maturity
- **Resin production**: By the end of flowering, buds are "absolutely caked in resin" to the point where individual buds are barely visible beneath the crystal layer
- **Branch support**: The weight of resin-heavy buds requires staking; do this sooner rather than later to prevent branch breakage
- **Drying time**: Requires a full three weeks to properly dry due to the weight and resin density of the buds

### Cultivation Notes

- Available only in clone form, which limits accessibility but ensures genetic consistency
- The short vegetative period makes it suitable for growers with space constraints
- Heavy resin production makes it attractive for hash and concentrate production
- Finger hash from post-harvest manicure is described as plentiful and high quality

## Appearance

- **Bud structure**: Long, finger-like flowers with exceptional resin coverage
- **Trichome coverage**: So heavy that buds appear "like someone just threw a bag of sugar at a tree"
- **Visual impression**: The extreme resin production gives Harlequin a premium, artisanal appearance that signals quality to experienced consumers

## Medical Applications

### Primary Medical Uses

Harlequin was bred specifically to produce high-grade hash and oils for medical patients. Its primary applications include:

- **Pain management**: CBD's analgesic properties provide effective pain relief without sedation
- **Anxiety relief**: High CBD content reduces anxiety without the paranoia sometimes associated with THC-dominant strains
- **Anti-inflammatory**: CBD's well-documented anti-inflammatory effects address chronic inflammation
- **Daytime medication**: The low THC content allows patients to medicate during work or daily activities
