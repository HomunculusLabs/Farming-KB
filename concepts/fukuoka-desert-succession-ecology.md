## Long-Term Ecological Outcomes

When succession is allowed to proceed naturally, the eventual outcome is a self-sustaining ecosystem that maintains its own soil fertility, regulates its own water cycle, supports diverse plant and animal communities, and requires no human management once established.

Fukuoka connected this vision to his broader philosophy: "Reviving the earth, halting the growth of deserts, conserving the environment all can be achieved not by doing something, but by seizing an opportunity for returning to nature that requires nothing to be done."
