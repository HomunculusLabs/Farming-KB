## Chemistry

### Muscimol
Muscimol (C4H6N2O2) is the primary psychoactive compound in prepared Amanita muscaria:

- **GABA-A receptor agonist**: Muscimol binds to GABA-A receptors as a selective agonist, producing sedative, hypnotic, and dissociative effects. This is the same receptor class targeted by benzodiazepines and alcohol, though muscimol's binding profile differs
- **Not a serotonin agonist**: Muscimol has no significant activity at 5-HT2A or other [[psilocybin-pharmacology-and-serotonin-receptors]], making its effects fundamentally different from classical psychedelics
- **Also acts on GABA-C receptors**: Contributing to visual and perceptual effects
- **Orally active**: Effective when consumed orally after proper preparation

### Ibotenic Acid (Toxic Precursor)
[[ibotenic-acid]] (C5H6N2O4) is a glutamate receptor agonist present in fresh or improperly prepared mushrooms:

- **Excitotoxic**: Activates NMDA and metabotropic glutamate receptors, causing neuronal excitation that produces nausea, confusion, and potentially seizures at high doses
- **Prodrug to muscimol**: Ibotenic acid decarboxylates (loses a CO2 group) to form muscimol through heat, drying, or aging
- **The key preparation step**: Converting ibotenic acid to muscimol while removing excess ibotenic acid is essential for a safe and pleasant experience
- **Water-soluble**: Can be largely removed through hot water extraction/decanting

## Preparation Methods

Proper preparation transforms ibotenic acid to muscimol and removes excess ibotenic acid. This is the single most important factor in safe Amanita use.

### Method 1: Oven Drying
1. Slice fresh caps thinly
2. Dry at 150-175F (65-80C) for several hours until completely crisp
3. The heat converts ibotenic acid to muscimol
4. Consume dried material directly

### Method 2: Hot Water Extraction (Recommended for Safety)
1. Dry caps as above
2. Crush dried caps and simmer in water at 170-190F (76-88C) for 30-60 minutes
3. Strain and discard solids (ibotenic acid is more water-soluble than muscimol)
4. Drink the liquid (tea)
5. This method removes much of the residual ibotenic acid while retaining muscimol

### Method 3: Traditional Siberian Reindeer Urine Method
In traditional Siberian practice, the mushroom was sometimes consumed after being processed through reindeer, whose metabolism converts ibotenic acid to muscimol. The reinerdeer's urine, collected after the animals ate fly agarics, was consumed for its muscimol content.
