# JADAM Seed Treatment and Storage

Seed treatment in conventional agriculture typically involves synthetic
fungicides and chemical coatings to protect seeds from soil-borne
pathogens. JADAM replaces these chemical treatments with natural methods
that are equally effective, cost nothing, and can be performed by any
farmer using locally available materials. Seed self-sufficiency is a
pillar of [[query-what-is-jadam-farming-and-how-does-it-work]]:

### Practical Immersion Protocol

A practical seed treatment protocol combines these elements:

1. Prepare treatment water at the target temperature
2. Add natural antimicrobial agents (plant extract, sea salt, or diluted
   JS) to the water
3. Place seeds in a mesh bag for easy handling
4. Immerse the bag in the treatment water for the specified time
5. Remove and immediately transfer to cold water for rapid cooling
6. Spread seeds on a clean surface to dry
7. Store dried seeds in a cool, dry location

## Bakanae Disease Prevention

Bakanae disease (caused by Fusarium fujikuroi) is one of the most
serious seed-borne diseases in rice production. Infected seeds produce
abnormally tall, thin seedlings with yellowish leaves that eventually
lodge and die. The fungus spreads through seed and can contaminate
entire rice paddies.

JADAM's approach to bakanae prevention is integrated across multiple
stages:

1. **Seed selection**: Choose seeds from healthy, disease-free mother
   plants. Avoid seeds from fields with known bakanae history.
2. **Hot water treatment**: Immersion at the correct temperature and
   duration to kill Fusarium spores on the seed surface.
3. **Natural antimicrobial treatment**: Herbal extracts or diluted JS
   provide additional protection against the fungus.
4. **Post-treatment handling**: Ensure seeds are dried completely and
   stored properly to prevent post-treatment contamination.

- **Cool, dark location**: Store seeds away from direct sunlight and
  heat sources. Temperature fluctuations reduce seed viability.
- **Airtight containers**: Glass jars or sealed containers prevent
  moisture absorption and pest infestation.
- **Natural pest repellents**: Dried pesticidal herbs can be placed
  near stored seeds to deter insect pests.
- **Regular inspection**: Check stored seeds periodically for mold,
  insects, or moisture problems.

## Seed Self-Sufficiency

JADAM encourages farmers to save their own seeds rather than purchasing
from seed companies. This practice eliminates seed costs and allows
farmers to select for traits adapted to their specific local conditions.

- Maintaining genetic purity by preventing cross-pollination
- Selecting seeds from the healthiest, most vigorous plants
