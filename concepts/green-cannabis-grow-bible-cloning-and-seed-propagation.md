# Cloning and Seed Propagation

Greg Green's Cannabis Grow Bible covers both seed-based propagation
and cloning techniques for maintaining desirable [[maitake-fruiting-body-success-rate-management]]:

1. **Propagation kits (highest success)**: Rockwool SBS propagation
   trays with grow cubes, a reservoir for water or germination
   hormone, and a cover. Nearly 100% germination rate. Cost
   approximately $20 for tray, rockwool, and fertilizers. The same
   tray can be used later for rooting clones.

2. **Towel/wool germination (moderate to high)**: Seeds placed on
   damp cheesecloth, cotton wool, or paper towels and covered. Keep
   moist at all times. Check daily for roots. Transfer immediately to
   [[query-how-to-prevent-transplant-shock-when-moving-cannabis-plants]].

3. **Soil propagation (moderate)**: Seeds placed 3mm deep (or seed
   length) in moist soil, sprinkled with water daily. 7-8 out of 10
   seeds typically germinate.

### Seed Viability

- Never use white seeds (immature)
- Look for seeds with white and grey markings or other colors
- Crushed seeds will not germinate
- Old seeds may have trouble germinating

### Dangers During Propagation

- **Drafts**: Kill or stunt germination; keep away from open windows
  and fans
- **Cold rooms**: Inhibit germination rates
- **Fertilizer burn**: Do not use high-dose fertilizers on seedlings;
  water alone is sufficient
- **Disturbance**: Do not dig up seeds to check progress
- **Weak stems**: Support leaning seedlings with sticks and thread;
  never tie thread above a growing shoot. Simulate wind by gently
  shaking the stem morning and evening to develop stronger stems
- **Outdoor sticks**: Never bring sticks from outdoors for support --
  they may carry [[cannabis-perpetual-grow-cycle]] cycles (SOG, ScrOG, cabinet grows all
  depend on clones)

### Standard Cloning Technique

1. Select a healthy mother plant grown to sufficient height
2. Take cuttings above a node with at least 2 nodes above it; minimum
   3 inches in length
3. Use a clean or sterile cutting instrument
