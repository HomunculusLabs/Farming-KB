## Historical Development

Dr. Cho Han Kyu was born in 1935 in South Korea. After studying
agriculture and observing [[fukuoka-critique-green-revolution]]'s heavy chemical reliance. Dr. Cho
observed that while chemicals initially boosted yields, they gradually
degraded soil health, increased costs, reduced quality, and created
dependency. He sought an alternative maintaining productivity while
restoring [[solomon-root-exudates-and-allelopathy]]. The farm is a living ecosystem the farmer stewards.

## No Chemical Inputs

The most visible expression of Cho's philosophy is the complete
elimination of synthetic chemical inputs. Dr. Cho argued that chemicals
disrupt biological networks in soil and on plant surfaces, actually
increasing [[query-how-do-i-design-habitat-for-beneficial-insects]] organisms,
and contribute organic matter as mulch. [[natural-farming-philosophy]]
