# Natural Farming: Water-Soluble Minerals, Seawater, and Brown Rice Vinegar

This concept covers Dr. Cho's water-soluble mineral inputs (WS-K, WS-P, WS-Ca, WS-CaPo), seawater and [[cho-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]], brown rice vinegar (BRV), yeast, and [[loess]] powder. These inputs provide essential mineral nutrition and [[cervantes-damping-off-seedling-disease-prevention]] without [[cannabis-chemical-fertilizers]].

## Water-Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WS-CaPo)

### When to Use
1. When crops overgrow (prevents excessive vegetative growth)
2. When initial growth is poor
3. When flower buds have weak differentiation
4. As drinking water (1:500) for pregnant or ovulating livestock
5. During pregnancy or breeding time for animals

### Storage: 23-25°C, cool and shaded

## Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV)

### Characteristics
- Acetic acid bacteria affect liver cells, supporting sterilization, neutralization, diuresis, and synthesis
- **Weakly acidic but becomes alkaline** when decomposed in the body ("alkali food")
- Contains **phytic acid** for poison neutralization and sterilization
- Has **more amino acids and organic acids** than any other food
- Raises calcium absorption ratio when combined with citric acid

### Functions in Farming
1. **Low concentration**: Facilitates vegetative growth, initial leaf formation, wax layer formation
2. **Improves tree flexibility** and increases absorption of NF materials when sprayed
3. **Sterilizes** and restrains bacterial growth via acetic acid bacteria activity
4. **Maximizes calcium effect** when used with water-soluble calcium
5. **High concentration**: Accelerates reproductive growth

### Preparation
Same method as Korean rice wine (Makgeolli) — fermented from brown rice.

## Seawater and Fermented Seawater

### Principle
Seawater contains the full spectrum of minerals and trace elements. When combined with land microbes, "it produces an excellent effect for crops and livestock."

"The estuary, the merging point where freshwater and seawater meet, provides a favorable condition for microbes which in turn support many plankton and many fish that eat the plankton."

### Preparation
- Pour seawater in a large bowl and leave for a day to allow airborne microbes to enter
- Fermented seawater provides abundant energy to crops and livestock

### Application Rates
| Input | Dilution |
|-------|----------|
| Seawater | 1L seawater : 30L water |
| Sun-dried salt | 2ml per 1000ml water |

### Uses
1. **Sweeten fruits**: WS-Ca added to diluted seawater, sprayed twice per month + 20 days before harvest
2. **Soil application**: Sun-dried salt at 5kg per 10 acres
3. **Prevent feather loss in chickens**: BRV (200) + WS-Ca (500) mixed with diluted seawater, fed once every 2 days in summer

## Water-Soluble Potassium (WS-K)

### Key Functions
1. Activates starch-synthesizing enzyme, facilitates photosynthate translocation
2. Regulates stomata and water use
3. Grows meristematic tissues
4. Promotes CO2-fixing enzyme synthesis
5. Transport carbohydrates, vitalize carbon assimilation
6. Improves resistance to drought and freezing
7. Reduces lodging, increases yield, improves quality

### Deficiency Symptoms
- Chlorosis starts from old leaves (edges become yellowish-brown)
- Branch growth deterioration, stunted maturity
- Symptoms typically appear after initial growth stages

### Preparation
Extracted from plant materials rich in potassium using brown sugar fermentation.

## Water-Soluble Calcium (WS-Ca)

### Source
Extracted from eggshells ([[peat-calcium-carbonate-perlite-casing-psilocybe-cubensis-miniculture]]) using brown rice vinegar. Eggshells are "very cost-effective materials."

### Functions
1. Better utilization of carbohydrates and protein
2. Major component in cell membrane formation
3. Prevents crop overgrowth
4. Makes fruits firm, prolongs storage
5. Promotes phosphoric acid absorption
6. Carries and accumulates nutrients (carbohydrates) to storage organs

### Preparation Method
1. Collect eggshells, remove inner peel
2. Crush into small pieces (not powder)
3. Lightly roast to remove organic substances
4. Soak in brown rice vinegar — shells bubble and dissolve
5. Done when no more bubbles/movement
6. **Caution**: Add shells slowly to prevent overflow from CO2 reaction

## Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-P)

Extracted from animal bones using brown rice vinegar, similar principle to WS-Ca extraction.

## Fermented Mixed Compost (FMC)

### Method 1: Full Compost
- Mix IMO-4 with various organic materials
- [[cervantes-reservoir-temperature-management]] critical: **50°C = turn**, **70°C = nutrients lost**
- Turn on Day 2, Days 5-6, and Days 8-10
- Complete in ~20 days when sweet smell develops
- Store in sacks/gunny bags in cool shade for up to 1 year

### Method 2: Quick Mix
- Mix IMO-4 with compost materials (oil cake, fowl droppings, pig/cow manure, fish meal) at 1:10 ratio
- Ferment 7-14 days
- Adding bone powder, shrimp/crab shell creates IMO-5

### Application
- Apply 2-3 hours before sunset or on cloudy day (UV damages microorganisms)
- Cover with soil immediately or lightly rotary hoe 1-2 inches
- Partial topsoil application preferred
