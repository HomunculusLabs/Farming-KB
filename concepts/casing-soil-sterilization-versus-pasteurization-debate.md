### Why Pasteurization Can Fail

- **Incomplete heat penetration** — Casing soil is insulating. Core
  temperatures may not reach the target range, leaving pockets of viable
  competitor organisms.
- **Variable starting loads** — If raw ingredients carry heavy [[trichoderma]], Aspergillus) prefer acidic conditions, so limestone

## Moisture Calibration

The guide describes the target moisture as **"as much water as possible without
turning to mud."** This is a practical field calibration rather than a precise
measurement. The casing should hold together when squeezed but not drip water,
and crumble apart easily when rubbed between fingers.

### Achieving the Right Moisture Level

1. Combine dry ingredients (peat, vermiculite, limestone) thoroughly.
2. Add water gradually while mixing continuously.
3. Squeeze a handful — it should form a loose ball that breaks apart with
   minimal pressure.
4. If water drips from the squeezed ball, add more dry ingredients.
5. If dusty and will not hold shape, add more water.
