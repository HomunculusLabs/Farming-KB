## Proton NMR Spectroscopy

The proton NMR spectrum of [[indole]] ring. The meta-coupled doublet at 6.84 ppm (J = 2.4 Hz) for H-2
is diagnostic for the unsubstituted position adjacent to the ring nitrogen.
The ortho-coupled doublet of doublets for H-5 and H-7, combined with the
doublet for H-6, completes the ABCD spin system of the benzene portion of the
[[indole]] ring. This value is characteristic of a
carbon bearing a phenolic hydroxyl group in an aromatic system and
distinguishes the 4-hydroxy substitution from the 5-hydroxy pattern found in
serotonin (which shows the deshielded carbon at a different position). The
beta-methylene of the side chain (C-2 prime) at 61.6 ppm is shifted downfield
relative to typical aliphatic methylenes due to the electron-withdrawing
