# 2C Family Compounds Overview

## Definition and Naming

The "2C" family consists of 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamines. The name "2C" was coined by Shulgin to denote the "two carbons" of the phenethylamine chain (as opposed to the three carbons of the amphetamine chain). The letter following "2C" indicates the substituent at the 4-position:

- **2C-B**: 4-bromo ([[2c-b-compound-profile]])
- **2C-C**: 4-chloro
- **2C-D**: 4-methyl (a longer name was not needed)
- **2C-E**: 4-ethyl ([[2c-e-compound-profile]])
- **2C-F**: 4-fluoro
- **2C-I**: 4-iodo ([[2c-i-compound-profile]])
- **2C-N**: 4-nitro
- **2C-P**: 4-propyl
- **2C-T-2**: 4-ethylthio ([[2c-t-2-compound-profile]])
- **2C-T-7**: 4-propylthio ([[2c-t-2-compound-profile]])
- And many others

## Core Structure

All 2C compounds share the basic scaffold:

```
    OCH3
     |
    / \
   |   |
   |   |      CH2-CH2-NH2
    \ /
     |
    OCH3
```

2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine with a variable substituent at the 4-position (between the two methoxy groups).

## Common Effects Across the Family

Despite differences in specific character, the 2C compounds share several general properties:

### Visual Effects
- Enhanced color perception and color saturation
- Geometric pattern recognition (trails, fractals, geometric forms)
- Visual distortion of objects and surfaces
- Closed-eye imagery, often described as colorful and flowing

### Physical Effects
- Increased sensory awareness
- Mild to moderate body sensation (described as "body load")
- Pupil dilation
- Mild temperature sensitivity
- Changes in heart rate and blood pressure (generally moderate)

### Emotional and Cognitive Effects
- Enhanced emotional openness
- Altered thought patterns and perspective shifts
- Enhanced appreciation of music and art
- Varying degrees of introspection and philosophical thinking

## SAR Trends

### Halogen Series (B, C, F, I)
- Potency generally increases with atomic number: F < Cl < Br < I
- Iodine (2C-I) and bromine (2C-B) produce the most favorable potency-to-tolerability ratios
- Fluorine (2C-F) is relatively weak

### Alkyl Series (D, E, P)
- Increasing chain length generally increases potency up to ethyl, then decreases
- 2C-D (methyl) is notably mild and gentle
- 2C-E (ethyl) is among the most powerful and profound
- 2C-P (propyl) is active but less reliable

### Thioalkyl Series (T-2, T-7, etc.)
- Sulfur introduces a distinctive subjective quality
- Potency increases with chain length
- Duration tends to be longer than carbon-chain analogues

### Other Substitutions
- **2C-N** (nitro): Active but with an unpleasant body load
- **2C-G** (glycine-derived): Explores unusual bioisosteric substitutions
- **2C-SE** (selenoethyl): Selenium-containing analogue, demonstrating the breadth of Shulgin's exploration

## What Makes the 2C Family Unique

1. **Lack of alpha-methylation**: Unlike the DO family amphetamines, 2C compounds are not alpha-methylated, resulting in shorter durations and less stimulant character
2. **Consistent scaffold**: The 2,5-dimethoxy pattern provides reliable activity, allowing systematic exploration of the 4-position
3. **Tunable properties**: By varying only the 4-position substituent, a remarkable range of potencies, durations, and effect profiles can be achieved
4. **Manageable duration**: Most 2C compounds have durations of 4-10 hours, making them more practical for many users than the 18-30 hour durations of DOB/DOI
