# DMT Smoking Technique and Preparation

DMT (N,N-dimethyltryptamine) is a powerful short-acting psychedelic compound found in numerous plants and animals, including the human brain. When smoked, it produces an intense, rapid-onset experience lasting 5-20 minutes. This page covers preparation, technique, and [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin]].

## Freebase Preparation

### From Plant Material
DMT is found in the bark of [[entheogen-jurema-mimosa-hostilis-ethnobotany]], the leaves of [[chacruna-psychotria-viridis]], and other plants. [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-spore-extraction-methods]] vary:

### Basic Acid-Base Extraction (Overview)
1. Powder plant material
2. Soak in acidic solution (vinegar or [[citric-acid-cycle-tca-krebs-cycle]]) to extract alkaloids
3. Filter to remove plant matter
4. Basify the solution with sodium hydroxide (NaOH) to convert DMT to freebase
5. Extract with nonpolar solvent (naphtha or DCM)
6. Evaporate solvent to yield crystalline DMT freebase

### Safety Warning
- Sodium hydroxide is extremely caustic — wear gloves, eye protection
- Work in well-ventilated area
- Naphtha is flammable — no open flames
- This is illegal in most countries

## Changa

### What Is Changa
- DMT-infused smoking herb
- Typically 20-50% DMT by weight, mixed with herbs
- Provides a smoother, longer experience than pure DMT
- MAOI-containing herbs ([[ayahuasca-and-banisteriopsis-caapi]] leaf, passionflower) extend and modify effects

### Making Changa
1. Dissolve DMT freebase in a solvent (isopropyl alcohol or acetone)
2. Add herb mixture (mullein, mint, caapi leaf, passionflower)
3. Allow solvent to evaporate completely
4. DMT crystals deposit evenly throughout the herb
5. Dry thoroughly before use

### Advantages Over Pure DMT
- Smoother smoke (herbs buffer harshness)
- Longer experience (15-30 min vs 5-15 min)
- MAOI herbs alter the experience quality (more grounded, less "rush")
- Easier to dose consistently
- Can be rolled into a "joint" or smoked in a pipe

## Smoking Methods

### The Machine (Glass Vapor Genie Style)
1. Place steel wool or mesh screen in a glass pipe
2. Add DMT freebase on top of the screen
3. Apply heat gently from below (not directly on the DMT)
4. DMT vaporizes at ~160°F (71°C) — much below combustion temperature
5. Inhale slowly and steadily
6. Hold smoke for 10-15 seconds before exhaling

### Direct Pipe
1. Place DMT in the bowl of a glass pipe
2. Carefully apply flame to the side of the bowl (indirect heat)
3. Don't let the flame touch the DMT directly
4. Inhale the white vapor (not black smoke — that's combustion)

### Dab Rig
1. Use a quartz nail or banger
2. Heat nail with torch
3. Apply DMT to hot nail
4. Inhale vapor through water filtration

## Dosage

### Breakthrough Dose
- **Pure DMT**: 40-60 mg (heavy), 20-40 mg (moderate)
- **Changa (25% DMT)**: 150-250 mg of changa blend
- Individual sensitivity varies enormously

### Experience Levels
- **Threshold** (5-10 mg): Slight visual shimmer, body sensation
- **Light** (10-20 mg): Open-eye visuals, geometric patterns, mild dissociation
- **Moderate** (20-40 mg): Intense visuals, entity contact, ego softening
- **Breakthrough** (40-60 mg+): Complete [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]], alternate realities, "hyperspace"
