## Routes of Administration

### Oral Ingestion

The most common method. Mushrooms are:

- **Chewed raw or dried**: held in the mouth briefly to begin breakdown, then swallowed. Chopping into smaller pieces is recommended due to unpleasant taste.
- **Brewed as tea**: steeped or stewed in hot (not boiling) water. Tea may reduce nausea compared to eating whole mushrooms.
- **Mixed with food**: cooking into food may cause additional nausea and is generally not recommended

### Smoking

Some user reports describe smoking dried mushrooms, said to produce faster but milder effects. It is not clear whether combustion temperatures destroy psilocybin/psilocin before they can produce psychoactive effects via this route.

The experience is most commonly described by users as "tripping."

## Acute Psychological Effects

### Positive Effects ("Good Trips")

- Mild relaxation (comparable to cannabis at lower doses)
- Giddiness and uncontrollable laughter
- Increased energy and euphoria
- Visual enhancement (brighter colours, enhanced patterns)
- Visual disturbances (moving surfaces, waves, geometric patterns)
