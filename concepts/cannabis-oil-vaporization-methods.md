# Cannabis Oil Vaporization Methods

The direct vaporization of cannabis oil for inhalation represents one of the most efficient methods of cannabis consumption, delivering high concentrations of cannabinoids directly to the lungs for rapid absorption into the bloodstream. Unlike smoking plant material, where much of the active material is destroyed by combustion or lost in sidestream smoke, oil vaporization targets precise temperatures that volatilize cannabinoids without burning them.

## Principles of Oil Vaporization

Cannabis oil vaporization relies on heating the oil to a temperature that causes the cannabinoids and terpenes to vaporize without reaching the point of combustion. THC vaporizes at approximately 157 degrees Celsius (315 degrees Fahrenheit), while CBD vaporizes at slightly higher temperatures around 160-180 degrees Celsius. Plant material, by contrast, begins to combust at approximately 230 degrees Celsius, producing tar, carbon monoxide, and other harmful byproducts.

Because cannabis oil is a concentrated extract, much smaller quantities are needed compared to smoking flower. A dose the size of a pinhead can deliver effects equivalent to several times that amount of smoked cannabis. This efficiency makes oil vaporization both economical and potent.

## Glass Oil Pipe Method

The most common traditional method for vaporizing cannabis oil is the glass oil pipe, also called a vapor pipe. The oil is placed in a glass bowl and the pipe is heated from below with a flame, similar to the traditional method for smoking opium. This is a very efficient method because very small amounts of oil may be vaporized at one time.

The glass pipe allows the user to control the temperature by adjusting the distance and duration of the flame application. An experienced user learns to apply just enough heat to produce vapor without causing the oil to burn. The visual cue of seeing vapor form in the pipe chamber helps the user gauge the correct temperature.

Glass oil pipes come in various designs, from simple glass tubes with a bulb at one end to elaborate artistic pieces with multiple chambers and water filtration. The basic design requires a glass surface that can be heated to vaporize the oil and a pathway for the resulting vapor to travel to the user's mouth.

## Aluminum Foil Method

Another method for smoking oil involves placing a tiny dab of oil on a piece of aluminum foil. The foil is heated from below with a match or lighter, and the smoke is inhaled from above the boiling oil through a tube or funnel. This is essentially the same as smoking in a glass pipe, except that a new spot on the foil can be used each time, preventing residue buildup at the point of vaporization.

Before use, the foil should be heated in a gas flame to burn off any coating or manufacturing residue that might also vaporize and be inhaled. This pre-burning step is important for safety, as aluminum foil often has a non-stick coating or other surface treatments.

A tube or funnel (often made from rolled foil, a pen tube, or a glass tube) is positioned above the heating point to capture the rising vapor. The user inhales through the tube as the oil is heated, drawing the vapor into their lungs.

## Safety Considerations

All methods of oil vaporization involve heating flammable or combustible materials and should be performed with appropriate caution. The use of water filtration, as in a bong or dab rig, cools the vapor and removes some particulate matter. Glass and quartz tools should be handled carefully to avoid breakage and burns.
