# Mushroom Spore Syringe Inoculation

[[spore-syringe-inoculation]] is the process of introducing fungal spores into a sterilized
substrate to initiate [[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]], this step transforms a jar of inert substrate into a living fungal
culture. The technique emphasizes mass [[pf-tek-maximum-fruiting-substrate-formula]],
which preserves complete [[mushroom-spore-syringes]] must be stored in a cool, dark location to maintain viability. Before use,
the syringe should be shaken vigorously to redistribute spores that may have settled to
the bottom during storage.

## Pre-Inoculation Requirements

Before any inoculation begins, several conditions must be met:

1. **Substrate jars must be cool to the touch** — hot jars will kill spores on contact.
   After [[growing-gourmet-atmospheric-steam-sterilization]], allow jars to cool for several hours or overnight.
2. **Jar lids must be tight** — ensure the canning ring is firmly screwed down.
3. **Tape covering needle holes must be removed** — these coverings protected the holes
   during sterilization but must be removed for inoculation.
4. **Work area should be as clean as possible** — while the PF TEK's dry vermiculite
   barrier provides significant protection, working in a still-air environment improves
   success rates.

## Inoculation Procedure

### Standard Lid Inoculation

1. Shake the spore syringe well to redistribute spores evenly in the suspension
2. Remove the needle guard and discard the tape covering the lid's needle holes
3. Insert the needle through one of the four punched holes in the canning lid
4. Tilt the syringe body back toward the center of the lid, with the needle tip touching
   the inside glass surface of the jar
5. This angling distributes the spore solution down the side of the jar rather than
   injecting it deep into the substrate, providing better surface coverage
6. Press the plunger slowly, watching the needle tip against the glass — as soon as
   liquid appears around the tip, stop pressing. This delivers just a few drops per hole
7. Repeat for each of the four holes, shaking the syringe slightly between holes
8. Use approximately 1 cc total per jar (10 jars per standard syringe)

### Needle Plugging

If the needle becomes plugged with substrate as it is inserted through the vermiculite
barrier layer, slowly draw the needle back a small distance. This usually clears the
blockage without requiring removal and reinsertion.

### Open-Glass Inoculation

For jars using drinking glasses without punched lids, inoculation is performed without
a lid entirely. This technique is more advanced and should only be attempted after
mastering the standard lid method. The critical precaution is to disturb the dry
vermiculite contaminant barrier as little as possible while introducing the needle.

## Alcohol Flaming Technique

Whenever the syringe needle is touched or potentially contaminated, it must be
re-sterilized before reinsertion. The PF TEK uses an alcohol flame rather than a
butane lighter because:

- Alcohol burns cleanly with no soot residue (butane leaves carbon deposits)
- The flame is short and controlled, suitable for small tools

### Creating an Alcohol Flame

1. Place a tequila shot glass upside down
2. Using an eyedropper, place a few drops of denatured alcohol on the hollow bottom
3. Touch the alcohol with a match or lighter to ignite
4. A short blue flame results — this is the sterilizing flame

### Flaming the Needle

1. Pass the needle tip through the blue alcohol flame for several seconds
