# PF TEK Ethyl Alcohol Procurement for Psilocybin Extraction

The PF TEK alcohol extraction method requires pure 200 proof ethyl alcohol (ethanol) as the solvent for dissolving psilocybin and related alkaloids from dried mushroom material. Procuring this reagent in the United States involves navigating state liquor regulations, but the process is straightforward and inexpensive once the proper channels are understood.

## Why 200 Proof Ethyl Alcohol Is Required

The PF TEK extraction technique relies on pure ethyl alcohol as the sole solvent. The choice of solvent grade directly affects the quality and concentration of the final extract:

- **200 proof (100% pure ethanol):** Contains no water whatsoever. This is the preferred grade because the complete absence of water means no dilution of the final extract. When the alcohol is evaporated and concentrated down, only the extracted alkaloids remain in solution without water interference. The PF TEK explicitly recommends 200 proof over all alternatives.
- **190 proof (95% ethyl, 5% water):** Acceptable but inferior. The 5% residual water content becomes a significant negative factor when the extract is evaporated down to a concentrated form. Water does not evaporate as readily as alcohol, leading to a more dilute and less predictable final product.

The alcohol must be ethyl (drinkable) alcohol, not denatured alcohol or isopropyl alcohol. Denatured alcohol contains toxic additives (such as methanol, pyridine, or bittering agents) that would contaminate the extract and make it dangerous. Isopropyl alcohol is also toxic and unsuitable for this purpose.

The PF TEK emphasizes a key safety advantage of using ethyl alcohol: because it is a non poisonous drinkable spirit, any fumes generated during the extraction and evaporation process are harmless to the operator, unlike the toxic fumes produced by industrial solvents.

## Sourcing from Liquor Stores

Full service liquor stores in the United States supply hospitals and laboratories with 200 proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol as part of their institutional sales. The procurement process follows these steps:

1. Call a well stocked liquor store and ask if they carry 200 proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol
2. Many stores will have it in stock or can order it through their institutional supply channels
3. If the store does not carry it, the salespeople will typically know which local suppliers stock this product
4. Full service stores that serve institutional and laboratory clients are the most likely to have 200 proof ethanol readily available

The PF TEK notes that some states allow over the counter purchase of 200 proof ethanol without any special permit, simplifying the process considerably.

## State Alcohol Permit Requirements

In most states, purchasing 200 proof ethyl alcohol requires a permit from the state liquor board. The PF TEK describes the permit application process:

1. Identify and contact the state liquor board (usually located in the state capital)
2. Request an application for an ethyl alcohol permit
3. The permit fee is typically 5 to 10 dollars, making it an inexpensive formality
4. Complete the application, including the required description of intended alcohol use

## Permit Application Language

The PF TEK provides guidance on how to describe the intended use of the alcohol on the permit application. The recommended language describes legitimate, non controversial purposes:

- Non toxic surface sterilizing
- Herb extraction
- Preservation
- Tincture making
- Perfume making

The PF TEK characterizes the poison warnings on pure ethanol bottles and the regulatory permit requirements as bureaucratic obstacles that primarily serve to generate state revenue from the sale, rather than genuine safety measures.

## Practical Considerations for Procurement

Several practical factors affect the procurement process and should be considered before beginning:

- **Store selection:** Full service liquor stores that serve institutional clients (hospitals, laboratories, universities) are far more likely to stock 200 proof ethanol than small neighborhood retail outlets
- **Geographic variation:** Regulations and availability vary significantly by state. Some states may allow over the counter purchase while others strictly require permits
- **Quantity planning:** Estimate the extraction batch size before purchasing to avoid acquiring more alcohol than needed. A single extraction typically requires enough alcohol to cover the mushroom material with excess for multiple soaking cycles
- **Storage of purchased alcohol:** Store in its original sealed container away from open flames and heat sources, as pure ethanol is highly flammable
- **Timing:** Allow time for the permit application to be processed if required, which may take several weeks depending on the state

## Safety During Extraction and Evaporation

The PF TEK provides specific safety guidance for working with pure ethyl alcohol throughout the extraction process:

- **Fire safety:** Keep all flames, sparks, and ignition sources away from the alcohol solution during extraction and evaporation. Pure alcohol is extremely flammable and its vapors can ignite at room temperature.
- **Ventilation:** Work in a well ventilated area. Although the fumes from ethyl alcohol are non toxic (unlike denatured alcohol or other solvents), adequate ventilation prevents vapor accumulation and reduces fire risk.
- **Evaporation method:** Use a small electric fan to accelerate evaporation. The fan should be positioned to direct airflow directly down into the evaporation vessel, creating ripples on the alcohol surface. Never use open flame or heat elements for evaporation.
- **Aroma indicator:** A pungent mushroomy aroma (described by the PF TEK as "fungi perfume") in the concentrated extract indicates successful extraction of the active compounds.

## Supply List for Complete Extraction

The PF TEK provides a complete materials list for the extraction process beyond the alcohol itself:

- Harvested and dried mushroom material (several grams minimum, thoroughly desiccated)
- 200 proof ethyl alcohol
- Drinking glass, spoon, and knife for handling materials
- Funnel for transferring liquids
- Bottles with tight fitting caps (whiskey bottles, pop bottles, or similar)
- Dust pollen masks for filtration
- Small electric desk fan for evaporation
- 10cc syringe for measurement and dispensing of the final extract

## Role in the Complete Extraction Workflow

Ethyl alcohol procurement is the foundational first step in the PF TEK extraction method. The complete workflow proceeds as follows:

1. Procure 200 proof ethyl alcohol from a liquor store (with permit if required)
2. Acquire and desiccant dry mushroom material harvested before or at cap opening
3. Pulverize the dried mushrooms to a fine powder using a plastic bag and hammer
4. Soak the pulverized material in alcohol for 24 hours with periodic shaking
5. Filter the slurry through dust pollen masks to separate liquid from solids
6. Resoak the extracted mushroom material with fresh alcohol and repeat
7. Combine all alcohol extracts and evaporate using a fan to desired concentration
8. Store the concentrated liqueur in small screw cap vials in the freezer

## Evaporation and Concentration Process

The PF TEK describes the evaporation phase in detail, which follows the alcohol extraction and filtration steps:

1. Combine all filtered alcohol extracts into a clean glass vessel
2. Position a small electric fan (desk clip on type is ideal) near the glass
3. Direct the airflow straight down into the glass until the surface of the alcohol ripples
4. The fan accelerates evaporation without introducing heat, preserving alkaloid integrity
5. As the alcohol level recedes, wash any residue adhering to the inside of the glass back into the solution
6. Continue until the desired concentration is reached

The PF TEK recommends a target concentration of 1 to 3 or more grams of dried mushroom material per 1cc of final extract alcohol. At higher concentrations, less liquid needs to be consumed per dose. The concentrated extract develops a pungent mushroomy aroma described as "fungi perfume," which indicates successful alkaloid extraction.

A 10cc syringe is used for accurate measurement and dispensing of doses. The PF TEK warns that accuracy is critical because a slight overestimation of dose could result in an unexpectedly intense experience.

## Final Extract Storage

The concentrated mushroom liqueur should be stored in small screw cap bottles or glass vials in the freezer. Ethanol does not freeze solid at standard freezer temperatures and will remain liquid, making it easy to dispense individual doses from frozen storage.
