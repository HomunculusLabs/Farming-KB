Two primary categories of assays are used: immunological and molecular methods. ## Immunological Assays Immunological methods detect specific fungal antigens or proteins using antibody-based technologies. These approaches are valuable for identifying and quantifying target fungi in complex environmental samples. ### Principles Immunological assays exploit the specificity of antibody-antigen interactions: - **Polyclonal antibodies**: Raised against whole-cell preparations or crude extracts; recognize multiple epitopes - **Monoclonal antibodies**: Highly specific to single epitopes; more reproducible - **Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA)**: Quantitative detection using enzyme-conjugated antibodies - **Immunofluorescence**: Microscopic detection using fluorescently labeled antibodies ### Applications in Mycoremediation - Monitoring survival and spread of inoculated white-rot-fungi-effluent-decolorization in field applications ### Advantages - Relatively rapid turnaround time - Can be designed for field-portable formats - Quantitative capabilities with proper calibration - Cost-effective for routine monitoring ### Limitations - Cross-reactivity with non-target organisms possible - Antibody production requires purified antigens - May not distinguish between viable and non-viable cells - Environmental matrix interference (soil humic acids, etc.) ## Molecular Assays Molecular methods detect fungal DNA or RNA, providing highly specific identification and quantification capabilities. ### PCR-Based Methods - **Species-specific PCR**: Uses primers targeting unique DNA sequences for identification - **Quantitative PCR (qPCR)**: Real-time amplification enabling quantification of fungal biomass - **Nested PCR**: Two-stage amplification for increased sensitivity in low-biomass samples - **Multiplex PCR**: Simultaneous detection of multiple fungal species - **RT-PCR**: Detects RNA (expressed genes), indicating [[rhizosphere-fungal-community-analysis-rrna-rdna]] - **Denaturing gradient gel electrophoresis (DGGE)**: Community fingerprinting - **Terminal restriction fragment length polymorphism (TRFLP)**: Community structure analysis - **Clone libraries**: Identification of community members through sequencing - **Metagenomics**: Comprehensive community characterization ### Genetic Markers for Fungal Detection -
