## Purdue Vegetable Planting Reference Table

A quick-reference for planting [[wide-row-spacing-intensive-gardening-comparison]]:** 1 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 3 inches between plants
- **Seed rate:** 1 oz per 50 ft of row
- **Planting depth:** 1/2 to 1 inch
- **[[coleman-second-spring-august-planting-window]]:** April 1 to June 25
- **Days to harvest:** 55 to 65
- **Expected yield:** ~1 bushel per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Tops make excellent greens at 1-inch diameter; main harvest at 2-3 inches

### Carrots

- **Row spacing:** 1 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 2 inches between plants
- **Seed rate:** 1/2 oz per 50 ft of row
- **Planting depth:** 1/2 inch
- **Planting window:** April 10 to June 20
- **Days to harvest:** 70 to 75
- **Expected yield:** ~1 bushel per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Spring crop before hot weather; fall crop before first moderate freeze

### Irish Potatoes

- **Row spacing:** 3 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 10 inches between plants
- **Seed rate:** 60 seed pieces per 50 ft of row
- **Planting depth:** 4 inches
- **Planting window:** April 20 to June 1
- **Days to harvest:** 90 to 130
- **Expected yield:** ~1 bushel per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Harvest when tops yellow or die; cure 1 week in shaded, ventilated area

### Radishes

- **Row spacing:** 1 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 1 inch between plants
- **Seed rate:** 1/2 oz per 50 ft of row
- **Planting depth:** 1/2 inch
- **Planting window:** March 20 to June 15 (late: Aug 15 to Sep 15)
- **Days to harvest:** 25 to 30
- **Expected yield:** ~500 roots per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Fastest crop from seed to table; harvest at 1/2 to 1 inch diameter

## Cole Crops (Brassicas)

### Broccoli

- **Row spacing:** 3 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 18 inches between plants
- **Plant rate:** 36 transplants per 50 ft of row
- **Planting window:** April 15 to June 15
- **Days to harvest:** 40 to 55
- **Expected yield:** ~36 heads per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Harvest terminal head while florets tight and green-blue; side shoots follow

### Cabbage

- **Row spacing:** 3 ft between rows
- **Plant spacing:** 18 inches between plants
- **Plant rate:** 36 transplants per 50 ft of row
- **Planting window:** April 5 to June 5
- **Days to harvest:** ~55
- **Expected yield:** ~36 heads per 50 ft row
- **Notes:** Harvest when heads are solid; stores up to 2 months cold
