# Roundwood Shelter Building

Roundwood shelters [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-earthworking-and-earth-resources]] cellars are practical buildings that can be constructed all over the world. They make use [[mckenna-food-of-the-gods-exopheromones-and-plant-communication]] soil's balancing effect — creating a pleasant, cool retreat in hot places [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-a-monotub-and-a-shotgun-fruiting-chamber]] warm one in cold places.

## Construction Method

### Foundation Posts

Rough timber logs are placed at least one metre deep into the ground, then cut to the same height. These serve as the vertical support posts for the structure.

### Notched Trunks and Roof

Tree trunks are notched and used to construct the roof. The notched trunks are laid across the support posts to form the roof structure. To improve stability, the back wall should fit closely with the top edge of the roof (the back wall can also have a cap log).

### Roof Layering

The roof is built up in layers from the inside out:
1. Fine soil (innermost layer)
2. Membrane (waterproof barrier)
3. Sheeting
4. Membrane
5. Soil and vegetation (outermost layer)

This layered approach provides insulation, waterproofing, and integrates the structure into the landscape.

## Materials

- **Robinia logs**: Particularly durable wood species suitable for storage rooms
- **Rough timber**: Used for foundation posts
- **Notched trunks**: Form the roof structure
- **Membranes**: Provide waterproofing between layers
- **Local soil and vegetation**: Top layer for insulation and aesthetics

## Maintenance

Roundwood shelters [[mollison-designers-water-purification-and-storage-principles]] rooms must be checked regularly like any other building. This is necessary to repair any damage promptly and to guarantee safety.

## Advantages

- Uses natural, locally available materials
- Soil provides temperature regulation (cool in summer, warm [[weed-control-in-winter-greenhouses]])
- Can be built with simple tools and techniques
- Integrates naturally into the landscape
- Works in all climates when properly designed
