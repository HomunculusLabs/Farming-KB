## Cannabis Soil Growing — Containers, Flushing, and Medium Management

## Overview

Soil growing is the most traditional and accessible method of indoor cannabis cultivation. Proper container selection, soil flushing, and medium management are essential for healthy root development and nutrient uptake.

## Container Selection

### Pot Size

- Cannabis develops long root systems and is best kept in **1.5–3 gallon pots**.
- Larger pots allow more root space, supporting bigger plants and higher yields.
- Under-potted plants become root-bound, stunting growth.

### Drainage

- Pots should have **perforations (holes) at the bottom**.
- Place pots on dishes/saucers to catch runoff.
- **Empty dishes** after watering to prevent waterlogging.
- Advanced growers may use non-draining pots, but this requires precise watering discipline.

### Over-Watering Risks

- Excess water drains through perforated pots, carrying nutrients with it (nutrient leaching).
- Over-watering wastes time, nutrients, and can kill plants.
- If using non-perforated pots, never over-water — there is no safety drain.

### Preparation

- Clean pots thoroughly before use to remove unwanted chemicals or dust.

## Soil Flushing

Flushing removes accumulated nutrient salts and excess fertilizer from the root zone. It is critical both during growth (to fix nutrient problems) and before harvest (to improve taste and smoothness).

### Method A — Non-Perforated Pots

1. Tilt the plant on its side over a sink.
2. Pour water slowly over the soil surface.
3. Watch the colour of the liquid that runs out.
4. Continue until all liquid has poured through.

### Method B — Perforated Pots

1. Pull the plant up and sit it in the sink.
2. Pour lots of water on top of the soil.
3. Wait for water to flow out the bottom; observe the colour.
4. Repeat until the runoff water becomes clearer.
5. The soil will become very muddy — some mud will pass out with the water.
6. Once water is clear, tilt plant on its side and let all water drain.

### Flushing Indicators

- If feeding solution has **no colour**, watch the **texture and smell** of the runoff.
- Continue flushing until runoff resembles clean water in colour and odour.

## Humidity Management

### Ideal Range

- Cannabis plants work best at **40–80% relative humidity**.
- Optimal target: **~60%**.

### Control Methods

- **Fresh air exchange** is the best humidity control method.
- Use an rH measuring device to gauge humidity levels.
- **Dehumidifiers** are available but generally unnecessary unless growing at very large scale.
- Simple ventilation is sufficient for most setups.

## Temperature

- **Normal household temperatures** are suitable for cannabis growth.
- **Target**: ~75°F (24°C).
- Simple rule: If it's too cold for you, it's too cold for your plants.
- Temperature is controlled by heating units or the natural thermal environment of the room.
