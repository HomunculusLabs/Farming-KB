# PF TEK Fruiting and Harvesting Techniques

## Overview

The fruiting phase of the PF TEK begins when the colonized substrate cake is
removed from the jar and placed into a **[[dual-chambered-terrarium]] um]]** (DCT). This
environment provides the high humidity and fresh air exchange needed for
mushroom formation. Proper harvesting technique directly affects both yield and
potency.

## The Dual Chambered Terrarium

The DCT is PF's original [[fruiting-chamber]] er]] design, built around a standard
**10-gallon aquarium**:

### Construction
- **Base chamber**: Lower half contains perlite or water for humidity
- **Upper chamber**: Elevated platform holds the fungus cakes above the
  moisture source
- **Airtight lid**: Custom wooden frame (24" × 14.5") with clear polyethylene
  plastic film tacked to the underside, creating a seal over the aquarium top

### Critical Requirements
- The aquarium **must be sealable with no air leaks** for humidity retention
- Saran wrap is a [[simple-plastic-bin-fruiting-chamber-passive-humidity-mushroom-cultivation|Simple]] r alternative to the custom frame
- The lid must allow some passive gas exchange while retaining moisture

### Terrarium Techniques
- **Spraying**: Mist the terrarium walls (not the cakes directly) to maintain
  humidity. Spraying should be light and frequent.
- **Heating**: Ambient room temperature is usually sufficient. If heating is
  needed, a low-wattage light above the terrarium works.
- **Low humidity symptoms**: cakes turn blue, mushrooms abort, caps crack —
  increase misting frequency.

## Cake Removal ("Birthday")

The optimal time to remove the cake from the jar is when **primordia** (tiny
worm-like structures with reddish heads) appear while the cake is still in
the jar.

### Removal Procedure
1. Remove the jar lid
2. With a clean fork, scrape away the **majority of the dry top vermiculite**
3. Place an old jar lid over the jar mouth and invert the jar
4. Lightly slam the jar down on a **table cushioned with a magazine**
5. The fungus cake slides out onto the jar cap (this moment is called the
   **"birthday"**)
6. Daub the cake with loose tissue paper to absorb water droplets
7. Immediately place into the terrarium

### Handling Precautions
- **Handle with care** — do not squeeze or bruise the cake
- Bruising appears as **bluish marks** but the fungus is resilient
- Minimize handling — the less, the better
- The cake has a distinct, pleasant mushroomy aroma

## Fruiting Cycle Timeline

| Stage | Timeframe | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Pinning | Within 2 weeks of full colonization | White pin-like structures appear |
| Primordia | Within a week of pinning | Tiny worm-like structures with reddish heads |
| Mushroom growth | Days | Primordia develop into mature fruiting bodies |
| First flush peak | ~4–6 weeks from inoculation | Maximum harvest from first fruiting cycle |
| Second flush | Variable | Possible if cake is cleaned; usually sparse |

The **total process from [[sterile-spore-print-collection-glass-jar-technique|Spore]] inoculation to fruiting takes 4–6 weeks**.

After the initial flush, the mycelium cake begins to turn blue and no more
mushrooms form. Secondary flushes are possible but usually sparse and small.

## Harvesting for Maximum Potency

PF emphasized that **potency depends critically on harvest timing**:

### The Potency Principle
"The secret to potent mushrooms is in their age when picked. It has been
scientifically proven that the **small immature specimens are significantly
more potent** than the larger mature specimens."

### Types of Fruiting Bodies

| Type | Description | Potency |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Primordia** | Tiny baby mushrooms, first to form | **Highest potency** — harvest before heads turn black |
| **Aborts** | Convolutioned caps, gnarly stems, stunted growth | **Very high potency** — "primo in magic alkaloids" |
| **Mutants** | Blobs with little or no cap | High potency |
| **Normal sporocarps** | Perfect specimens with well-formed caps | Lower potency — good for spore collection, weak in alkaloids |

### Harvesting Guidelines
1. Pick **aborts and primordia** with a pointed knife blade before their heads
   turn black
2. Harvest normal mushrooms **before the veil breaks** under the cap
3. Early-harvested mushrooms have **roundish heads** and smaller size
4. Mature specimens (beautiful but fully opened) are **good for spore
   collecting and display** but are weak in psychedelic potency

### PF's Potency Formula
> "Grow them on brown rice, harvest them when they are young and cool dry
> them with desiccant. When this is done, they are an entheogen of the
> highest order."

## Drying (Cool Desiccation)

Proper preservation maintains potency:

1. **Cool desiccant drying** is PF's recommended method
2. Place harvested mushrooms in a sealed container with a desiccant (e.g.,
  silica gel, Drierite)
3. The desiccant absorbs moisture without heat, preserving alkaloids
4. Heat-based drying (food dehydrators, ovens) can degrade psilocybin
5. Store dried mushrooms in a **cool, dark, airtight container**

## Spore Printing

Mature specimens with open veils are ideal for spore collection:

1. Remove the cap from a mature mushroom
2. Place it gills-down on a piece of [[sterile-spore-print-collection-glass-jar-technique|Sterile]] paper or foil
3. Cover with a glass or bowl to prevent air current disturbance
4. Leave for 12–24 hours — spores will deposit in a radial pattern
5. The **spore print** can be used to prepare new spore syringes

This closes the life cycle: spore → inoculation → colonization → fruiting →
spore print → new syringes.

## The Complete Life Cycle

The PF TEK uses **mass spore inoculation** directly to the fruiting substrate,
rather than tissue cloning. PF considered this an advantage:

- The **complete genotype** is preserved with each generation
- **Senescence** (mutating and ceased fruiting from repeated cloning) is no
  longer a problem
- Spores ensure a "never ending succession of fungus, with all the power of
  the spores' reproductive ability intact"
