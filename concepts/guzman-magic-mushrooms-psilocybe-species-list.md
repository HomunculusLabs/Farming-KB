# Psilocybe Species Taxonomy and World Checklist ## Taxonomic Framework The genus Psilocybe (Fr.) P. Kumm. belongs to the family Strophariaceae and is the largest genus of [[psilocybin-potency-dosage-guide]] and related indole alkaloids. This
follows the taxonomic concept of Kuhner (1980), which treats Psilocybe,
Hypholoma, and Stropharia within the same genus. Species placed in
Psilocybe s. Noordeloos (1995) are not accepted.

## Taxonomic History

The genus was first studied systematically by Schultes (1939), Singer
(1949, 1958), and Heim (1956-1958). Guzman's 1983 world monograph
provided a comprehensive revision. The taxonomic concept follows
Kuhner (1980), which treats Psilocybe, Hypholoma, and Stropharia within
the same genus. Several species have problematic definitions: P.
coprinifacies was considered doubtful by Guzman (1983), and P. lazoi was
considered a synonym of P. zapotecorum but claimed as independent by
Singer (1986).

## Infrageneric Classification

Within the neurotropic Psilocybe species, the following sections are
recognized:
- **[[psilocybe-aztecorum-section-aztecorum-alpine-neurotropic-fungi-guzman-1998]]**: P. aztecorum and allies
- **Section Brunneocystidiatae**: Species with brown cystidia
- **Section Cordisporae**: Species with heart-shaped spores
- **[[psilocybe-zapotecorum-section-zapotecorum-americas-pacific-distribution-guzman-1998]]**: P. zapotecorum and allies (a large group of
  subtropical species)
- **Section Mexicanae**: P. mexicana and allies
- **Section Semilanceatae**: P. semilanceata and temperate allies
