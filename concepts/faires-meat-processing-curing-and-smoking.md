## Salting Meat

Salting is a practical and efficient method of preserving meat with very little effort. While smoking produces delicious results, it requires a smokehouse and several weeks of constant vigilance. Salting, by contrast, needs only salt, a cool space, and patience.

### Dry Salt Curing Process

1. Clean the meat and cut off anything undesirable. Save the fat to make clarified fat. Dry the meat with a clean cloth and cut it into smaller strips so that the salt can penetrate fully, ensuring the meat in the middle is preserved. Rub spices into the strips, then rub large quantities of salt into them until no more can be absorbed.

2. When as much salt as possible has been rubbed in, cover the meat in a layer of salt to coat it completely. Hang it in a place that stays consistently at 59 degrees F (15 degrees C) for at least three weeks, checking often for spoilage. A basement or cold storage is ideal. The meat should stay edible for at least a few months.

3. When ready to cook, wash the salt off well. It may be necessary to soak the meat briefly to remove excess salt.

### How Salt Preservation Works

Salt dissolves into the water in the meat, preventing bacteria from growing if the salt-to-water balance is greater than 3.5%. Ideally the concentration should be over 10% salt. While precise control is difficult when hand-rubbing, rubbing so much salt into the meat that it will not accept any more ensures adequate preservation.

### Salt Curing Products

Commercial salt curing products containing sodium nitrite are available, but these are not recommended. Natural salt curing produces a different colour and flavour than nitrite-cured products, but avoids the health concerns associated with nitrites. The meat will be greyer rather than pink, but will taste excellent and be completely safe.

## Smoking Meat

Smoking is one of the oldest methods of meat preservation and produces delicious results. The smoke both dries the meat and deposits compounds that inhibit bacterial growth.

### Smokehouse Requirements

A proper smokehouse is needed for reliable meat smoking. This is a dedicated structure separate from living areas, with:
- A fire pit or smoke source at the bottom or side
- Hanging racks for the meat above the smoke
