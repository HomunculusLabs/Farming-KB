Rock phosphate is a naturally slow-release phosphorus source. It
becomes available to plants gradually through the action of soil
microbes and mild soil acidity. Bone meal decomposes faster but is
more expensive. The choice depends on availability, cost, and how
quickly you need phosphorus available to your crops.

## Sulfur

Sulfur is an essential nutrient that many chemical fertilizers do
contain, but organic sources may vary. Gypsum (calcium sulfate) is a
good source of both calcium and sulfur. Seedmeals also contain sulfur
as part of their protein content. In areas with alkaline soil, elemental
sulfur can be used to lower pH while also supplying this nutrient.
