## pH Management Cannabis

pH management is one of the most critical aspects of [[query-how-to-build-healthy-garden-soil]].

## Ideal pH Ranges

- **Soil and soilless mixes:** 6.5 to 7.0 (optimal around 6.5)
- **Hydroponic systems:** 5.5 to 6.5 (optimal around 5.8-6.0)
- **Rockwool and cloning medium:** 5.0 to 6.0
- **Aeroponic clone systems:** 5.0 to 5.5

Within these ranges, cannabis can properly absorb and process available nutrients most
efficiently. Deviations outside these ranges cause [[cervantes-salt-buildup-leaching-management]] that limits water intake by roots can
also develop. Deviations in pH levels often affect element solubility.

## Measuring pH

pH can be measured with:

- **Electronic pH meters:** Most accurate and convenient; require regular calibration
- **Soil test kits:** Mix soil with chemical solution and compare color to a chart
- **Litmus paper:** Simple and inexpensive but less precise

Electronic pH testers are preferred for their accuracy and ease of use. Less expensive
pH meters are accurate enough for casual use; more expensive models are quite accurate.
Once purchased, you can measure pH thousands of times with an electronic meter, while
chemical test kits are good for about a dozen tests. Combination meters that measure pH,
