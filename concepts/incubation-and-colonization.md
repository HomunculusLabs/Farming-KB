## Aborts and Mutants

Some of the first mushrooms to form are "abhorts," characterized by convoluted
caps, gnarly stems, and stunted growth. Despite their appearance, abhorts are
described as primo in magic alkaloids, even more potent than the stately
normal specimens. Another form of mutant, blobs of fungus with little or no
cap, also appears and is good for harvesting.

The fruiting cycle lasts approximately 2 weeks. After the initial flush, the
mycelium cake begins to turn blue and no more mushrooms form. Secondary
fruiting may occur if the cake is thoroughly cleaned, but yields are usually
sparse and small.
