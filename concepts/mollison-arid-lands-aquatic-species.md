## Fish in Arid Water Systems

Fish have multiple uses in arid environments, including the reduction of algae and mosquito larvae in tanks. Together with yabbies and mussels in dams, they also offer protein potential. Native fish species may be recommended by regional hatcheries such as the Narrandera facility in NSW.

Importantly, there is no risk of fish escapes via the desert and salt pans that buffer arid areas from permanent streams. For this reason alone, Mollison considers deserts to be important trial sites for water polyculture species. The isolation provided by arid landscapes makes them ideal for experimental aquaculture without risk of introducing exotic species into natural waterways.
