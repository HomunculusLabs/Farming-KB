## Vertical Wall Culture

In the progression of cultivation techniques, the Agaricus tray has been modified for growing Oyster mushrooms by turning it vertically so mushrooms fruit from both faces. These vertical surfaces are typically screened with tight wire or plastic mesh. Perforated plastic positioned between the substrate and the wire mesh allows the formation and development of mushrooms on both sides while retaining moisture.

Racks having a breadth of 12–16 inches support full flushes and generally do not become anaerobic near the core, a problem seen when racks are 20 inches or more in breadth. Properly designed individual rack frames can be stacked upon each other to construct continuous mushroom walls, with one side hinged for easy filling.
