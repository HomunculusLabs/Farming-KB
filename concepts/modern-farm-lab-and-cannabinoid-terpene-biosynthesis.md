## Overview

Lactic Acid Bacteria Serum (LABS) has been observed to increase trichome-to-calyx
ratio and terpene production in Cannabis when applied as a soil drench or foliar
spray. This article proposes a biochemical mechanism involving hexanoate, a
short-chain fatty acid byproduct of LAB fermentation of ruminant milk fats,
as a catalyst for increased cannabinoid and terpene biosynthesis.

### Hexanoyl-CoA as the Critical Intermediate

Hexanoyl-CoA, derived from hexanoate, serves as a primer for a polyketide
synthase (PKS) enzyme that forms olivetolic acid. The biochemical pathway:

- Hexanoate is formed via esterification of hexanoic acid
- Hexanoate stimulates hexanoyl-CoA production
- Hexanoyl-CoA feeds the PKS enzyme forming olivetolic acid
- Increased olivetolic acid availability increases cannabinoid precursor supply
