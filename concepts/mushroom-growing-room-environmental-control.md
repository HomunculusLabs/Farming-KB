## Overview

Mushroom growing room environmental control encompasses the heating, cooling,
humidification, and air handling systems required to maintain optimal conditions
for mushroom cultivation. The growing room must maintain a selected temperature
at high relative humidities through adequate insulation and environmental control.

### Air Recirculation

A mixing box with adjustable damper proportions fresh and recirculated
air. During spawn running, the damper seals the fresh air inlet for full
recirculation. As fresh air is introduced, room air is displaced through
exhaust vents or cracks around the door.

## Air Filtration

Filters screen atmospheric dust, spores, bacteria, and plant pollen:

-   **High-efficiency extended surface filters** — 0.3 micron at 90-95%
    efficiency; 5.0 micron at 99% efficiency.
-   **HEPA filters** — 0.1-0.3 microns at 99.96-99.99% efficiency;
    0.75-1.00 inches static pressure. Special waterproof filters operate
    in 100% humidity.
-   Protect HEPA filters with a 1-inch prefilter of open-celled foam or
    fiberglass.

## Heating Systems

Two approaches:

-   **Dry heat** — hot water or steam through closed pipe systems,
    resistance coils, or baseboard heaters. Lowers air moisture content.
-   **Live steam** — maintains [[blesching-cannabis-fever-temperature-regulation]].
