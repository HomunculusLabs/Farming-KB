## Random Assembly

Random assembly is a creative brainstorming technique used to generate
unexpected connections between elements. The designer lists all possible
elements for the design (species, structures, water features, animals) on
cards or slips of paper, then draws them at random and challenges themselves
to find beneficial connections between the randomly paired elements.
