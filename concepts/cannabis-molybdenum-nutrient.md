# Cannabis Molybdenum (Mo)

## Overview

Molybdenum is a [[organic-fertilizer-sources-guide]] of molybdenum include:

- Well-rotted manures (small amounts)
- Compost
- Seaweed and kelp products
- Legume-based composts (legumes accumulate molybdenum)
- Lime (indirectly, by raising pH and increasing molybdenum availability)

Because molybdenum is required in such tiny quantities (0.01-0.05 ppm), most organic soils and composts contain adequate amounts. Raising soil pH with lime is often the most effective way to improve molybdenum availability in acidic soils.

## Deficiency

Molybdenum deficiency is extremely rare in [[knf-calcium-deficiency-symptoms]], making it easy to misdiagnose.

### Symptoms

- Older and middle-aged leaves yellow first
- Some leaves develop interveinal chlorosis
- Leaves develop cupped or rolled-up margins as deficiency progresses
- Acute symptoms cause leaves to become severely twisted, die, and drop
- Overall growth is stunted
- Nitrogen shortage symptoms may appear as a secondary effect

### Deficiency Progression Timeline

1. Older and middle-aged leaves begin to yellow
2. Interveinal chlorosis develops on some leaves
3. Leaf margins cup or roll upward
4. Leaves become severely twisted and die
5. Overall growth is stunted; plant resembles nitrogen-deficient specimen

### Contributing Factors

Deficiencies are worst in acidic soils. The availability of molybdenum decreases as soil pH drops, making it less accessible to plant roots in acidic conditions. This is the opposite of most other micronutrients, which become more available in acidic conditions. If growing in acidic media and experiencing unexplained nitrogen-like deficiency symptoms, molybdenum should be considered as a possibility.

### Treatment

Since deficiencies are extremely rare, treatment is seldom needed. If deficiency is diagnosed:

- Raise the soil pH to improve molybdenum availability
- Apply a complete micronutrient solution containing molybdenum
- Most high-quality hydroponic fertilizers contain adequate molybdenum
- Use a chelated micronutrient mix for best availability
- Avoid lowering pH too much when correcting other [[query-how-to-identify-and-fix-cannabis-iron-deficiency]]
