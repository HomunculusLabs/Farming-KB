### Internet Shops

Online retailers expanded the market beyond geographic areas with physical smartshops. Internet shops sold fresh and dried mushrooms, cultivation supplies (spawn bags, growkits, mushroom prints), and spore syringes. The anonymity and reach of internet commerce made mushrooms accessible to consumers in countries where no physical retail outlets existed. This channel also facilitated information exchange through forums, trip reports, and cultivation guides.

### Market Stalls

In some countries, particularly the UK during the early 2000s, mushrooms were sold at music festivals, alternative markets, and from informal stalls. This channel was more seasonal and less reliable than dedicated shops but contributed to the normalization of mushroom use within certain subcultures.
