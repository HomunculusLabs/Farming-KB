### DNA-Based Methods
- Quantitative PCR (qPCR) of universal 16S rRNA genes (bacteria) and ITS regions (fungi) provides estimates of microbial community size.
- Metagenomic sequencing gives taxonomic and functional gene abundance data.
- Cell counting by flow cytometry after soil cell extraction provides direct cell counts.

## Factors Affecting Soil Microbial Biomass

### Soil Properties
- **Organic matter**: Strongest positive correlation with SMB. Each 1% increase in SOM typically increases MBC by 200-500 μg/g.
- **Clay content**: Clay protects microbial cells from predation and desiccation, increasing SMB.
- **Soil moisture**: Optimal at 50-60% water-holding capacity. Both drought and waterlogging reduce SMB.
- **pH**: Most microbial groups prefer near-neutral conditions; acidification reduces total biomass and shifts community composition.
