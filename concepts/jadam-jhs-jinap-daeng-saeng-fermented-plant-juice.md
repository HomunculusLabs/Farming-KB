## JADAM Herb Solution (JHS) - Fermented Plant Juice for Pest Control

JADAM Herb Solution (JHS) is a boiled herbal extract used primarily for
its insect-controlling effect in the JADAM [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]]"s [[query-how-to-manage-pests-and-diseases-in-mushroom-cultivation]]." Fresh leaves, fallen leaves, and fruits are
all used for JHS production.

### Strong Level: Korean Pasque Flower Root (KPFR)

Korean pasque flower (Pulsatilla koreana) provides the strongest
pesticidal effect of all JHS herbs. The root is used; seeds are
collected in mid-May, sprouted in trays (approximately 10 days), and
transplanted. It requires full sun. Commercially available dried KPFR
can also be purchased.

### Other Common JHS Herbs

- **Red spider lily** (Lycoris radiata): roots used, repels moles and
  snakes; traditionally used in oriental medicine for tumors
- **Foxglove** (Digitalis purpurea): leaves collected spring to autumn
- **Tobacco**: leaves collected spring to autumn; can also be extracted
  by soaking in water for 15 days instead of boiling
- **Garden balsam**: leaves and roots collected spring to autumn
- **Monkshood**: roots collected autumn to winter, highly poisonous
- **Oleander**: leaves and roots collected in autumn, highly poisonous
- **Pokeweed**: common plant with strong repelling properties
- **Water pepper** (Persicaria hydropiper): readily available
- **Bracken** (Pteridium): fern with pesticidal properties

## Application Rates

Mix 3 to 20 liters of JHS with water to make 500 liters (132 gal) of
finished spray solution. The concentration is adjusted based on pest
severity. For fog machines, use 10 L of JHS per 17 L of water.
