## Hydroponic Growing Systems for Cannabis

## Overview

Hydroponics is the science of growing plants without soil, using inert soilless media and nutrient solutions to deliver all essential elements directly to the root zone. Cervantes defines hydroponics broadly — even growing in soilless mixes like rockwool, peat moss, or coconut fiber qualifies. The key distinction is that all nutrients are supplied via the nutrient solution rather than being present in the growing medium. Cannabis is exceptionally well-suited to hydroponic cultivation due to its fast growth rate and high nutrient demands.

## Advantages of Hydroponics

- **Faster growth**: Plants take up nutrients as fast as they can use them, resulting in accelerated growth rates compared to soil
- **Earlier harvest**: Hydroponically grown cannabis typically finishes flowering several days earlier than soil-grown plants
- **Greater control**: Nutrient concentration, pH, and oxygen levels around roots can be precisely managed
- **Higher yields per watt**: Efficient [[cannabis-growing-environment-setup]]**: No soil-borne pests, diseases, or weed seeds

## Disadvantages and Challenges

- **Less forgiving**: Problems manifest faster and more severely than in soil gardens
- **More maintenance required**: Faster growth means more frequent monitoring and adjustment
- **Higher initial cost**: Pumps, timers, reservoirs, [[capillary-action-in-soils]] to transfer nutrient solution from a reservoir to the growing medium. No pumps or moving parts are involved.

**Wick Systems** use cloth or fiber wicks that absorb nutrient solution and transport it to the growing medium via capillary action. Key characteristics:

- Lowest initial cost and simplest design
- Very reliable — nothing to break or malfunction
- Growing medium stays wet, so substrate selection is critical to avoid oxygen deprivation
- Ideal growing media: vermiculite, peat moss, coconut coir
- The Dutch have perfected passive systems, flooding the floor with 2-3 inches of nutrient solution that plants absorb over 2-5 days
- Suitable for growers who are away for several days at a time
