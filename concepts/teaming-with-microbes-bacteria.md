## Biofilm Formation

Bacteria create slime layers (biofilms) that serve multiple functions:
- The slime raises soil pH
- It traps pathogens, preventing them from reaching plant surfaces
- Biofilms coat root surfaces in the rhizosphere and leaf surfaces in the phyllosphere
- Some bacteria work with fungi to form protective layers around roots
