## Field Crops vs. Vegetables

The distinction between field crops and vegetables is not merely botanical but
practical:

- **Field crops** (grains, dry legumes, potatoes) are planted in rows wide
  enough to permit cultivation with a hoe or tractor, require lower soil
  fertility per unit of yield, and can be stored for months or years without
  refrigeration. They produce high calories per area.
