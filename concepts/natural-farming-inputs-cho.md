# [[natural-farming]] Inputs

## Overview

Dr. Cho Han Kyu's [[korean-natural-farming]] system relies on a suite of
naturally fermented inputs that replace synthetic fertilizers, pesticides,
and growth regulators. These inputs are produced on-farm from locally
available materials using simple fermentation techniques. Each input
serves a specific purpose in the nutritive cycle, and together they
provide plants with a complete nutritional and protective program.

## [[fermented-plant-juice]] (FPJ)

FPJ provides plants with readily absorbable amino acids, enzymes,
vitamins, and natural growth hormones. It supports vegetative growth,
enhances photosynthesis, and improves overall vigor. Different plant
materials produce FPJ with different properties: FPJ from young shoots
of fast-growing plants supports vigorous growth, while FPJ from
reproductive plant parts supports flowering and fruiting.

To produce FPJ, healthy plant material is collected in the early morning,
lightly wilted, and mixed with an equal weight of brown sugar. The mixture
is placed in a ceramic or glass container, weighted down, covered, and
fermented in a cool, dark place for 7 to 10 days. The liquid extract is
filtered and stored. Standard dilution rates are 500:1 to 1000:1.

## Fermented Fruit Juice (FFJ)

FFJ enhances fruit quality by increasing sugar content (brix), improving
color and flavor, and extending shelf life. It is particularly important
during flowering and maturation stages. FFJ provides readily available
sugars and minerals that support energy-intensive reproductive processes.

FFJ is produced similarly to FPJ but uses ripe fruits such as banana,
papaya, mango, pumpkin, or melon. The fruit is mashed and mixed with an
equal weight of brown sugar, then fermented for 7 to 10 days. Dilution
rates are 500:1 to 1000:1.

## [[oriental-herbal-nutrient]] (OHN)

OHN is a broad-spectrum plant tonic that strengthens immunity, improves
disease resistance, and supports overall plant health. It is considered
one of the most important inputs due to its wide-ranging benefits and is
valuable during environmental stress such as extreme temperature or
drought.

OHN is a multi-herb preparation typically including garlic, ginger,
licorice, cinnamon, and angelica (Angelica gigas). Each herb is crushed
and soaked in rice wine or vodka separately for a specific period. The
individual tinctures are then combined and aged. The final concentrated
extract is diluted 1000:1 for application.

## [[fish-amino-acid]] (FAA)

FAA provides a rich source of nitrogen and amino acids. It supports rapid
vegetative growth and is particularly valuable for leafy crops. FAA also
contains trace minerals from fish that support enzymatic processes. It is
one of the most nitrogen-rich natural farming inputs available.

FAA is produced by fermenting fish scraps (heads, bones, internal organs)
with an equal weight of brown sugar. The fish and sugar are layered in a
container, with extra sugar on top to prevent air exposure. The container
is sealed and fermented for 30 days or longer. The liquid extract is
filtered and diluted 500:1 to 1000:1.

## [[lactic-acid-bacteria]] (LAB)

LAB strengthens plant immunity, improves soil health, enhances nutrient
availability, and suppresses pathogenic microorganisms. LAB produces
lactic acid, which lowers pH and creates conditions unfavorable to many
pathogens. It is used both as a foliar spray and a soil drench.

LAB is collected from rice wash water left at room temperature for 2 to
3 days until slightly sour. This is mixed with milk at 10:1 ratio and
left for 5 to 7 days. LAB bacteria consume the lactose and produce lactic
acid. A yellow curd forms on top; the liquid below is pure LAB serum,
diluted 1000:1 for application.

## Water-Soluble Calcium (WCA)

WCA strengthens cell walls, improves fruit firmness, prevents cracking,
and enhances disease resistance. Since calcium is immobile in plants,
consistent external supply through foliar application is essential during
rapid cell division such as fruit set and early fruit development.

WCA is produced from eggshells, animal bones, or oyster shells. The source
material is cleaned, dried, and lightly roasted, then soaked in rice wine
or apple cider vinegar at 1:10 ratio for 7 to 14 days. The acid dissolves
calcium into a water-soluble form, diluted 500:1 to 1000:1 for use.

## Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV)

BRV serves multiple functions: it lowers soil and leaf surface pH,
enhances absorption of other inputs as a tank mix adjuvant, improves fruit
quality and sugar content, and suppresses certain fungal pathogens. It is
particularly valuable during the maturation stage of the nutritive cycle.

BRV is produced through a two-stage fermentation. Brown rice is cooked and
inoculated with a yeast starter to produce rice wine, then acetic acid
bacteria convert the alcohol to acetic acid over 2 to 3 months. The
resulting vinegar is diluted 500:1 for application.

## Input Combination and Application

Natural farming inputs can be combined in tank mixes for simultaneous
application. A typical foliar spray might combine FPJ, WCA, and BRV.
However, not all inputs are compatible. OHN should generally be applied
separately. Practitioners should observe plant responses and adjust
combinations and rates based on local conditions.
