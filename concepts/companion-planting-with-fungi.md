## Mycorrhizal Fungi as Plant Partners

Most vascular plants, especially trees, can host diverse populations of mycorrhizae. In the lifetime of a single Douglas fir, more than 200 species of mycorrhizal fungi live symbiotically in and on its roots.

Most accessible mycorrhizal genera for companion planting:

- **Rhizopogons**: Preferred mycorrhizae for conifer trees in temperate forests
- **Pisolithus**: Puffball-like mushrooms mycorrhizing with approximately 80% of all temperate trees
- **Glomus**: Endomycorrhizal species including G. intraradices for broadleaf plants and garden vegetables
