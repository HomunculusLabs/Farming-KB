## The Mycorrhizal Economy

Mollison frames the mycorrhizal relationship as an underground economy that
underpins all above-ground productivity. The exchange is remarkably efficient:

- **Carbon flow**: Plants allocate 10-30% of their photosynthetically fixed
  carbon to mycorrhizal fungi. In forest ecosystems, this represents the
  single largest carbon flux from plants to soil.
- **Nutrient return**: In exchange for carbon, fungi provide phosphorus,
  nitrogen, zinc, copper, and water. The fungal hyphal network is far more
  efficient at nutrient acquisition than roots alone.
- **Water relations**: Mycorrhizal hyphae access water in soil micropores
