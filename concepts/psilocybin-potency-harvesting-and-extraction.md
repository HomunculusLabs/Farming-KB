# Psilocybin [[psilocybin-mushroom-harvesting-potency|Potency]], [[psilocybin-mushroom-potency-and-harvesting|Harvesting]], and Alcohol Extraction

## Overview

The potency of psilocybin-containing mushrooms is not static — it changes dramatically throughout the mushroom's life cycle and is influenced by substrate, harvest timing, [[oss-oeric-drying-and-preservation-methods]]. Understanding these factors is essential for maximizing the quality of harvested specimens. Additionally, techniques exist for extracting and concentrating the active alkaloids into a more compact and dose-controllable form.

## Potency Through the Life Cycle

Psilocybin content in mushrooms peaks before sporulation begins and declines steadily afterward. When a mushroom cap opens, the veil tears away, gills turn dark brown to purple, and spore deposits gather on the stem, the specimen has passed its prime for psychedelic use. This principle appears to hold true across all [[emcdda-psilocybin-mushroom-species-and-identification]], whether cultivated or wild-harvested.

Research by Jochen Gartz demonstrated that *Psilocybe cubensis* can achieve a baseline psilocybin content of 1% by dry weight — comparable to the more famously potent *[[guzman-allen-gartz-psilocybe-semilanceata-temperate-cosmopolitan-distribution]]* (liberty cap). However, reaching this level depends on several converging factors:

- **Substrate**: Brown rice provides an excellent base for potency development
- **Age/maturity**: Young, pre-veil-break specimens contain the highest concentrations
- **Sporulation state**: Pre-sporulation specimens retain maximum alkaloid content
- **Harvest and preservation**: Cool [[gottlieb-psilocybin-cultivation-harvesting-and-drying|drying]] (with desiccant) preserves potency best

## Harvesting Strategy for Maximum Potency

A deliberate harvest strategy focuses on collecting the smallest, youngest specimens:

1. **Abortive specimens**: Tiny primordia that stop developing (aborts) and small mutant growths (round blobs without proper cap formation) should be harvested as soon as it's clear they won't develop normally. Though individually tiny, multiple cakes produce a substantial combined weight of these specimens — often several dried grams total.

2. **Young normal specimens**: Harvest both small and large mushrooms while their caps remain round and before the veil beneath the cap breaks and the cap begins to expand outward.

### Why Early Harvest Works

As the mushroom cap expands like an umbrella, the physical size increases dramatically, but by weight the concentration of active alkaloids decreases proportionally. The mushroom is essentially diluting its psychoactive content as it allocates energy to spore production and cap expansion. By harvesting early, you capture the alkaloids at their peak concentration.

The trade-off is clear: harvesting only young and abortive specimens yields significantly lower total weight, but the quality and potency per gram can rival *P. semilanceata* — widely considered one of the most potent [[amanita-muscaria-legal-alternative-psilocybin-mushrooms-europe]] in the world.

## Consumption Guidelines

The recommended approach is deliberate and ritualistic:

- Eat all fungi at one sitting — do not piece-meal the dose
- The lag time between ingestion and onset can undermine peak effects if the dose is spread out
- A single, complete dose at one time ensures the full psychedelic potential is realized

## Alcohol Extraction Technique

The alcohol extraction method produces a concentrated psilocybin liqueur using only mushrooms and pure ethyl alcohol. This creates a dose-controllable preparation where potency can be precisely measured.

### Materials

- Quality dried *P. cubensis* specimens (harvested pre-sporulation, cool-dried with desiccant)
- 200 proof (anhydrous) ethyl alcohol — preferred over 190 proof because the 5% water content in 190 proof becomes a negative factor during evaporation
- Drinking glasses, funnels, bottles with tight caps
- Dust-pollen masks (for filtration)
- Small desk fan (for evaporation)
- 10cc syringe (for accurate dosing)

### Extraction Process

**Step 1 — Pulverization and Soaking**

Thoroughly dried (rock-hard) mushrooms are placed in a heavy-duty freezer bag, covered with a magazine for protection, and pulverized with the rubber heel of a shoe. The resulting powder is transferred to a bottle with a tight-fitting leak-proof cap. Enough alcohol is added to cover the material and create a loose slurry. The bottle is shaken well and left to soak for 24 hours, with periodic shaking to activate extraction.

**Step 2 — Filtration**

After 24 hours, the slurry is filtered using inexpensive dust-pollen masks (available at hardware, drug, and paint stores). The mask is fashioned over the mouth of a drinking glass. The slurry is shaken, poured into the filter, and the filter is squeezed to extract the alcohol. The filtered alcohol is stored in a fresh bottle.

**Step 3 — Secondary Extraction**

The mushroom material remaining in the filters is retrieved and re-soaked with fresh alcohol. Steps 1 and 2 are repeated to extract any remaining alkaloids.

### Evaporation and Concentration

All alcohol extracts are combined into a glass. A small electric fan (clip-on desk fans work well) is positioned to blow air directly down into the glass until the surface ripples, accelerating evaporation. The process takes several hours depending on volume.

As the alcohol level drops, residue adhering to the inside of the glass should be washed back into the solution. The fumes are harmless since the alcohol is a drinkable spirit, but open flames must be avoided — pure alcohol is highly flammable.

**Concentration targets**: 1 to 3+ grams of dried mushroom material per 1 ounce of final extract creates a potent preparation. The concentrate has a pungent mushroomy aroma and should be stored in small screw-cap bottles or vials in the freezer (alcohol does not freeze solid).

### Dosing Precision

A syringe is used for accurate dispensing. Precision is critical — slight overestimation can produce a dramatically stronger experience than intended. The concentrated form allows for very controlled dosing compared to eating dried mushrooms directly.

## Sourcing 200 Proof Ethyl Alcohol

In the United States, 200 proof anhydrous ethyl alcohol can be obtained from well-stocked liquor stores that supply hospitals and laboratories. If a store doesn't carry it, staff typically know where it can be found. Some states require a permit, obtainable from the state liquor board for a nominal fee ($5–10). The permit application asks about intended use — appropriate responses include "non-toxic surface sterilizing, herb extraction, preservation, tincture, and perfume making."

## Perlite Humidification Technique

The perlite humidification method is a low-tech alternative to electric humidifiers for maintaining proper growing conditions:

- A layer of wetted perlite is placed at the bottom of the growth chamber
- Perlite absorbs water through its porous structure and evaporates it gradually into the air above
- Unlike vermiculite, perlite does not absorb and hold water — instead it wicks moisture upward over an extended period (weeks)
- Cakes placed directly on the perlite benefit from both ambient humidity and direct wick-like moisture transfer at the contact point
- The technique works effectively even without supplemental air bubbling
- Perlite (bright white, expanded volcanic glass) is available at any plant shop as a potting soil aeration ingredient

This method produces excellent flush quality, often surpassing electric humidifier setups in both simplicity and effectiveness.

- Micro curved cuticle scissors (cosmetics/drug store)
- Denatured alcohol (fuel/hardware stores)
- Tequila shot glass and eye dropper (for sterilizing and flaming)
- Glass stirring rod (scientific supply)
- Plastic syringes (10cc or larger) and 18 gauge 1.5 inch needles
- Large syringes (20cc–65cc) and extra-long needles when available

### Maximum Formula Notes

The maximum substrate formula requires slight tamping (unlike the regular PF formula):

1. Loosely load the half-pint jar and level the top
2. Cover the mouth with one hand and grasp the jar
3. Lightly slam the bottom on the other palm a couple of times to lower mixture to 1/2 inch from the top
4. Clean the inside jar walls down to substrate level and fill to the top with dry vermiculite

Maximum formula jars should be incubated at room temperature without exceeding 70°F. Loosen the lid or untape the lid holes. Watch for loose water accumulation in the jar.
