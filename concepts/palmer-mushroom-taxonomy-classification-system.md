Globular fungi with enclosed spores rather than external ones.

## The Agaricini Subdivisions

Palmer could not generalize about the Agaricini as he could about the
other groups. Within this family he discussed several important genera:

**Agaricus (Psalliota):** The meadow mushroom (A. campestris) and
horse-mushroom (A. arvensis) were so similar that cooks and gardeners
did not recognize any distinction. Palmer theorized the horse-mushroom
was propagated from spores of the meadow mushroom after passing through
the horse's digestive system.
