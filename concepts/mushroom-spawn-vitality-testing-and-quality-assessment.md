# Mushroom Spawn Vitality Testing and Quality Assessment

## Overview

The ability to assess the quality and vitality of [[brick-spawn]] is properly inoculated and has undergone adequate growth, it will show a characteristic **mold-like growth throughout the brick**. This uniform, dense, white [[flake-spawn]]**. This is attributed to the compact, low-moisture form of the brick, which better protects the mycelium during storage. This fact explains the early general use of English brick spawn rather than French flake spawn in America—the imported French product proved unsatisfactory time and again due to its more rapid loss of vitality during importation.

### Material Experiments

Experiments aimed at reducing the weight of the brick by employing a more spongy material in its preparation gave **unsatisfactory results from the vitality standpoint**. The denser, more compact brick formulation provided better long-term viability.

## Spawn Pricing

### Standard Pricing (Early 1900s)

The price of mushroom spawn varied considerably depending upon quality. Standard spawn was available at commodity rates, while spawn of higher quality—where all bricks had been inoculated directly from pure culture—commanded premium prices. English brick spawn could be purchased from importers at a few cents less per brick than American spawn, but had been subjected to longer shipment and storage.

### Purchasing Strategy

It is a mistake for the grower to purchase spawn in a hurry and be forced to take whatever the market affords. Best practices include:

1. **Place orders well in advance**: If spawn is needed in quantity, order from the manufacturer several months before needed to secure the best quality
2. **Avoid reliance on advertisements alone**: So much false advertising had been practiced that no inexperienced grower should depend upon advertisements alone to determine where to purchase spawn
3. **Seek reliable sources**: Appeal to the director of the experiment station of the state, or to the U.S. Department of Agriculture for information and to be put in touch with reliable dealers or manufacturers

The consistent advice: above everything else, the spawn maker should realize that **vigorous spawn, free from insects and disease**, is required. Any practical method of manure fumigation that can be employed in [[spawn-production]] would be a step in advance.
