# High-Volume Cannabis Extraction and [[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-high-volume-extraction-method]] scales to industrial volumes using apparatus built from 55-gallon oil drums. At this scale, the same chemical principles apply — refluxing, soxhleting, distillation, and solvent collection — but the engineering challenges of handling large volumes of flammable solvents demand more robust safety systems. Beyond extraction, [[hashish]]
- Perform all operations: refluxing, soxhleting, distilling, and solvent collection
- Incorporate necessary safety features for working with large volumes of flammable solvents

Despite safety features (pressure relief valves, [[mushroom-house-construction-design]] to contain minor mishaps), the large volumes of flammable solvents pose a serious fire hazard. The potential scale and violence of a major accident warrant extreme caution. Such a device is **always tended during operation**, and emergency shutdown measures are ready at all times.

### Condenser Drum (Upper Drum)

The upper drum acts as a giant condenser, filled with circulating ice water. When solvent fumes from the lower drum contact its cooled surfaces, they recondense into liquid and fall back down.

**Construction:**

1. Mark corresponding **four-inch grids** on the top and bottom surfaces of the oil drum
2. Drill a hole at each grid intersection
3. Run pieces of **one-inch copper tubing** lengthwise through the drum
4. Each tube protrudes about one inch from the surface on both top and bottom
5. Watertight seals are soldered or welded around each tube where it passes through the surface
6. Inlet and outlet fittings for circulating ice water coolant are fitted to the top
7. Two metal straps with large holes are attached to the top for chain lifting with a small overhead crane

### Boiling Drum (Lower Drum)

**Construction:**

1. The top is removed first
2. Sturdy legs are attached to maintain a space between the drum bottom and the boiling water bath
3. A **copper drain tube with on/off valve** is attached to the bottom for siphoning solvent/cannabis solution
4. Four metal straps protrude above the top edge for securing the upper drum
5. A fitting with on/off valve is installed through the side, approximately halfway between top and bottom

### Soxhlet Chamber

A removable chamber sits between the falling recondensed solvent drops and the boiling solution below:

- Many holes are punctured in the bottom with a nail
- A hole is cut in the side at a point slightly higher than the side valve on the lower drum
- A plate closes the hole when necessary
- During soxhleting, the chamber contains marijuana or hashish; clean solvent runs through it, washing out remaining oil

### Solvent Collection System

Solvent is removed from solution and collected by:

1. A large funnel (nearly the same diameter as the drum) catches recondensed drops falling from the copper tubes
2. A tube from the funnel runs through the soxhlet chamber to the side valve on the lower drum
3. On the outside, another hose takes the pure distilled solvent to a receiving barrel

## High-Volume Extraction Operations

### Refluxing

1. The lower drum is filled to approximately **one-third full** with powdered cannabis
2. Solvent is added to **half-fill** the lower drum; side valve is closed
3. The upper drum is secured; the joint between drums is sealed with a trash bag (bottom cut out) secured by inner-tube rubber bands above and below — this arrangement acts as a **safety pressure relief valve** (plastic puffs out under pressure)
4. The entire apparatus is placed in the tub on the hotplates
5. Immersion pump supplies ice water coolant to the condenser drum
6. All copper tube tops are sealed with rubber stoppers except one — a garden hose runs from this tube to a jug (if fumes or liquid appear in the jug, the bath must be cooled immediately)
7. The hotplates are turned on; the water bath is brought to a boil
8. Refluxing continues for **3 hours**
9. The hotplates are turned off and disconnected; boiling water is removed
10. The tub is refilled with ice and water; the apparatus cools for at least **30 minutes**
11. The oil-saturated solvent is removed via siphon through the drain tube

### Fractional Distillation

Fractional distillation requires heating the oil to a high temperature under **reduced pressure** (vacuum). This causes THC and related cannabinoids to vaporize at lower temperatures than would be required at atmospheric pressure, reducing thermal degradation. The vapors are condensed back into oil on a cooled surface. The desired fraction is collected by selecting the appropriate temperature and pressure combination. Many impurities do not vaporize and remain in the heating flask.

Fractional distillation can yield a product up to **twice as strong** as the ether-phase oil and can approach nearly pure THC.
