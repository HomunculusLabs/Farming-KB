## Ducks
### Functions
- Egg and meat production
- Snail and slug control (superior to chickens for this)
- Pond fertilization (manure feeds aquatic plants and fish)
- Mosquito control (eat larvae from water surface)
- [[fukuoka-textdoc-weed-control-with-weeds-rice-barley-clover-discovery]] in wet areas
### Integration Patterns
- **Duck pond**: Ducks in a pond system — manure feeds plants, plants filter water
- **Rice paddies**: Traditional Asian integration (ducks in rice fields eat pests and weeds)
- **[[fukuoka-orchard-floor-management]]**: Control [[query-how-to-get-rid-of-slugs-and-snails-organically]] better than chickens
- **With fish**: Duck manure feeds fish, fish waste feeds plants
## Pigs
### Functions
- Meat production
- Land clearing (pigs root up brush, small trees, and sod)
- Tilling (natural plow — root through soil, incorporate [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]])
- Compost turning (root through piles)
- Manure (hot, nitrogen-rich)
### Integration Patterns
- **[[cover-crops]], then crop residue after harvest
## Bees
### Functions
- Honey and wax production
- Pollination (essential for fruit trees, vegetables, and seed production)
- Biodiversity support (wild bee populations)
- Income (honey, beeswax, pollination services)
### Integration
- Place hives near diverse forage (flowering trees, herbs, wildflowers)
- Position near orchards and vegetable gardens (pollination service)
- Avoid pesticide use in bee forage area
- Provide water source near hives
## Rabbits
### Functions
- Meat production (fast reproduction, efficient feed conversion)
- Manure (high nitrogen, excellent for compost and garden)
- Wool (Angora breeds)
- Small space requirements (suitable for urban/backyard)
### Integration
- Deep litter system under rabbit hutches (creates compost directly)
- Rabbit manure tea as [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer-nutritional-perspective]]
- Can be fed garden waste and forage
## Designing Multi-Species Systems
### Sequential Integration
- Sequence animals through the same space over time
- Example: Cattle graze → chickens follow (spread manure, eat larvae) → pigs root → plant crop
- Each animal prepares the ground for the next
### Spatial Integration
- Different animals in different zones of the same paddock
- Ducks on the low/wet area, chickens on the high/dry area
- Trees for shade and forage shared by all
### Forage Design
- Plant diverse forage: grasses, legumes, herbs, browse
- Multi-species pasture supports multiple animal types
