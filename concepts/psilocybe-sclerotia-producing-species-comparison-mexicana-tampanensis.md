### Sclerotia Characteristics

*P. tampanensis* sclerotia are similar in appearance to those of *P. mexicana*
but tend to be somewhat larger and more irregularly shaped. They have a hard,
dark exterior and a firm interior. The sclerotia of this species are among the
most sought-after in cultivation due to the species' extreme rarity in nature.
