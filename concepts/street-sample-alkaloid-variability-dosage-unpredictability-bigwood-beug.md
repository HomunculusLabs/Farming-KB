## Dosage Estimation Challenges

The variability data makes reliable dosage estimation from dry mushroom
weight extremely difficult. A standard recreational dose of psilocybin is
often cited as approximately 10 milligrams, which would correspond to roughly
1.5 to 14 grams of dried mushroom material depending on the actual potency
of the specific sample. This wide range means that a person consuming what
they believe to be a moderate dose could receive anywhere from a threshold
sub-perceptual amount to a strongly psychedelic dose.

Bigwood and Beug concluded that users of Psilocybe cubensis have no way of
predicting the amount of they are ingesting with a
given dry weight of mushroom material. They further noted that variations
in subjective experience will stem not only from the well-established effects
of set and setting but also from large dosage differences inherent in the
material itself.
