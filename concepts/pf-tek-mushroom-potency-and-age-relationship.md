## PF Tek Mushroom Potency and Age Relationship

## Overview

One of the most important and counterintuitive findings in [[cannabis-yield-optimization]].

## The Potency-Age Principle

### Scientific Basis

"It has been scientifically proven that the small immature specimens are significantly more potent than the larger mature specimens." This is not a matter of subjective perception but a measurable difference in [[abort-harvesting-alkaloid-concentration-pf-tek]] per unit of dried weight.

As a mushroom matures and its cap expands "like an umbrella," the mushroom's size increases substantially, but "by weight the potency will decrease." The total alkaloid content may increase slightly with size, but the concentration (mg of psilocybin/psilocin per gram of dried material) decreases significantly.

### Visual Indicators of Maturity

Young, potent mushrooms have:
- Very light coloration
- White stems
- Light-colored caps
- Conical shape (cap has not expanded)
- Intact veil under the cap
- Light-colored gills

Mature, weaker mushrooms show:
- Darker bluish colors on caps and stems
- Cap margins turning up (umbrella shape)
- Dark brown gills
- Purple spore deposits visible on the stem
- Torn or absent veil

## Abortive Mushrooms

### What Are Aborts?

"Over half of the small primordia that first form will abhort (cease growing, convolute and deform)." Aborts are characterized by "convoluted caps, gnarly stems and stunted growth." Despite their unappealing appearance, "they are primo in magic alkaloids" and "are even more powerful in magic than the stately beauties that will soon dominate the cake."

### Other Mutant Forms

Along with typical abhorts, "blobs of fungus with little or no cap" also appear. These mutant growths are likewise good for harvesting and consumption.

### Harvesting Aborts

"Pick these before their heads turn black. A pointed knife blade works well for removing these high potency primordia." Recognition comes easily after witnessing the growth cycle: "After witnessing the growth of the fungus, recognition of these abhorts is easy."

### Yield from Aborts

While individual aborts are tiny, "several cakes will produce quite a pile of these little ones (several dried grams!)." The cumulative harvest of abhorts from multiple cakes represents a significant portion of total potent material.
