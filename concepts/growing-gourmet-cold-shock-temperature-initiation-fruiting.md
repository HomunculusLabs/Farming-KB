## Practical Temperature Management

For cultivators managing multiple species or strains, the key
principles of temperature initiation include:

- Allow adequate thermal lag time (24-72 hours) for substrate
  temperature to equilibrate with air temperature changes
- Monitor substrate core temperature, not just air temperature
- Time the cold shock to coincide with full colonization
- Cycle strains seasonally to minimize energy costs for heating
  and cooling
