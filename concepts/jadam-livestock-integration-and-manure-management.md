# JADAM Livestock Integration and Manure Management

## Overview

JADAM's approach to livestock and manure management has two distinct aspects: (1) an innovative **no-feces-removing** [[jadam-organic-livestock-no-feces-removing]] system using microorganisms, and (2) a critical position against the conventional use of commercial livestock manure as fertilizer due to antibiotic contamination and soil degradation.

## No-Feces-Removing Organic Livestock Farming

### Concept
JADAM has developed an organic livestock system (building on the Yamagishi movement from Japan, further developed by Hankyu Cho) where animal manure on the shelter floor is not removed. Instead, microorganisms (JMS) are introduced to decompose the manure in-place, converting it back into feed.

### How It Works
1. **Shelter design:** Faces south for sunlight; air enters from sides and exits through top opening (galvanized steel roof promotes air circulation)
2. **Floor preparation:** Lay straw and rice husk on the floor
3. **JMS application:** JMS is sprayed on floors and/or fed to animals (diluted 20x with water)
4. **Natural cycle:** Animal feces mix with sawdust, straw, and microbes; microbes decompose the manure; it dries out from sunlight and air circulation; animals eat the fermented material again
5. **Result:** Very little smell, few flies; manure does not pile up; only needs to be cleaned about once a year

### Benefits
- Solves most problems of [[fukuoka-critique-modern-livestock]] farming
- Almost free of smell
- No daily manure removal needed (once per year)
- Feed conversion ratio improves
- Animals live in comfortable environment
- Feces becomes feed -- essentially a closed-loop system

### JMS for Livestock
- Dilute JMS 20x with water
- Feed to animals OR spray on shelter floors
- Significantly reduces odor
- Increases feed conversion ratio
- The system is "astonishing" in its effectiveness

### Animal Welfare Philosophy
JADAM extends its "I and others are one" philosophy to livestock:
- For pigs, there is a way of life that they need
- Chickens too shall be allowed a life that tends to their nature
- Organic livestock farming respects the nature of each animal

## JADAM's Critical Position on Livestock Manure as Fertilizer

### The Antibiotic Problem
- Commercial livestock operations routinely use antibiotics
- Antibiotics accumulate in animal manure
- When this manure is applied to soil, antibiotics destroy soil microorganisms
- This leads to further soil hardening and compaction
- Root vegetables (potatoes, carrots, radish) are especially vulnerable to antibiotic contamination

### Research Evidence
A USDA-supported study by University of Minnesota scientists found:
- Crops grown with compost containing antibiotics accumulate the chemicals in their tissues
- Antibiotics do move into plants
- Concentrations became higher as manure use increased
- Root vegetables in direct contact with soil were far more vulnerable

### Government-Supported Manure Programs Criticized
- In Korea, livestock manure is supplied to farms at very cheap prices due to government support
- JADAM sees this as contaminating soil with antibiotics and chemicals
- "Food going into my mouth determines my health; manure going into the soil determines the health of the crops"
- [[coleman-deep-organic-farming-philosophy]] bans the use of such [[cotter-pet-livestock-waste-fungal-remediation-systems]]

### Soil Compaction from Manure
- Livestock manure with antibiotic residues aggravates soil compaction ([[plow-pan-formation-and-remediation]])
- Habitual input of livestock manure with unclear origin inhibits soil microbial activity
- Combined with [[comparison-parasitoid-wasps-vs-chemical-pesticides]] and fertilizers, creates a hardened layer that blocks root growth

## When Manure Is Acceptable

### Herbivore Manure
If growing cover crops is difficult, JADAM suggests using manure from grass-eating (herbivorous) animals. Manure from herbivores contains optimum [[jadam-nutritional-balance-fertilizer]] for crops because the animals eat the same types of plants.

### Human Manure JLF
JADAM supports the traditional practice of using human manure as liquid fertilizer:
- Our ancestors used it for millennia and produced excellent food
- The "bad smell equals bad fertilizer" dogma is a marketing scam
- Human manure JLF is made using the standard anaerobic JLF method (container + ingredient + water + leaf mold)
- This is one of the traditional inputs that modern [[fukuoka-critique-organic-certification]] has abandoned but JADAM embraces
