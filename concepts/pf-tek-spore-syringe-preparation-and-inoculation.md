### Process

1. Sterilize the syringe by drawing boiling water and expelling it
2. Allow the syringe to cool with sterile water inside
3. Scrape a small amount of spores from the print into sterile water
4. Draw the spore suspension into the syringe
5. Cap the syringe needle with the protective cover

The resulting suspension contains millions of individual spores distributed
throughout the liquid. When injected into substrate, these spores germinate
and colonize the available nutrients.

## Inoculation Technique

Proper technique is critical for preventing contamination and ensuring even
substrate colonization.

### The Four-Hole Method

Each [[carbon-dioxide-retention-jar-lid-mushroom-cultivation]] has four evenly spaced holes. Inoculation proceeds as follows:

- Shake the syringe well to redistribute spores in suspension
- Insert the needle through the first hole at a slight angle
- Tilt the needle toward the center of the jar
- Deposit approximately 1cc of spore solution per hole
- Repeat for each remaining hole (4cc total per jar)

The angled injection spreads spores across a wider substrate surface, promoting
faster and more even colonization.

### Between Jars

The syringe should be shaken between each hole and between each jar. Spores
settle quickly in liquid, so redistribution ensures even spore delivery.

Each syringe inoculates approximately 10 jars at 4cc per jar.
