# PF TEK Spore Syringe Preparation and Inoculation

## Overview

[[spore-syringe]] preparation and inoculation are fundamental techniques in the PF
TEK method. The spore syringe serves as both the inoculation tool and the
primary means of preserving and distributing [[mushroom-genetics-strain-selection]].

The PF approach emphasizes simplicity and accessibility. All materials are
common household or drugstore items, and the techniques can be learned quickly.

## Spore Print Technique

Creating a spore print is the first step in generating inoculation material.

### Collection Method

The spore print process begins with a mature mushroom specimen:

- Select a mushroom with an open cap and visible gills
- Remove the stem close to the cap
- Place the cap gills-down on clean paper or aluminum foil
- Cover with a jar or glass for protection from contamination
- Allow to sit undisturbed for approximately 24 hours

After 24 hours, lifting the cap reveals the spore print — a dense deposit of
millions of spores in the gill pattern. A good print is dark and symmetrical.
Store prints dry and dark; properly dried prints remain viable for months.

## Syringe Preparation

Once a spore print is obtained, it must be transferred into liquid medium.

### Materials

- Sterile syringe (10cc or 12cc capacity)
- Sterile water (boiled and cooled distilled water)
- Sterile working surface or [[query-how-to-make-a-diy-still-air-box-for-mushroom-cultivation]]
- [[spore-print]] and alcohol lamp for [[flammability-hazard-alcohol-lysol-flame-sterilization-mycology]]

### Process

1. Sterilize the syringe by drawing boiling water and expelling it
2. Allow the syringe to cool with sterile water inside
3. Scrape a small amount of spores from the print into sterile water
4. Draw the spore suspension into the syringe
5. Cap the syringe needle with the protective cover

The resulting suspension contains millions of individual spores distributed
throughout the liquid. When injected into substrate, these spores germinate
and colonize the available nutrients.

## Inoculation Technique

Proper technique is critical for preventing contamination and ensuring even
substrate colonization.

### The Four-Hole Method

Each [[carbon-dioxide-retention-jar-lid-mushroom-cultivation]] has four evenly spaced holes. Inoculation proceeds as follows:

- Shake the syringe well to redistribute spores in suspension
- Insert the needle through the first hole at a slight angle
- Tilt the needle toward the center of the jar
- Deposit approximately 1cc of spore solution per hole
- Repeat for each remaining hole (4cc total per jar)

The angled injection spreads spores across a wider substrate surface, promoting
faster and more even colonization.

### Between Jars

The syringe should be shaken between each hole and between each jar. Spores
settle quickly in liquid, so redistribution ensures even spore delivery.

Each syringe inoculates approximately 10 jars at 4cc per jar.
