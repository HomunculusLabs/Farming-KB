## Organic Fungicides

Organic fungicides occupy a grey area. They are not strictly organically
derived but are non-persistent and considered safe for the gardener and
beneficial wildlife when used correctly.

### Copper Fungicide

Several types of copper fungicide are used to control mildews and blights.
Bordeaux mixture combines copper sulfate and slaked lime. Burgundy mixture
uses copper sulfate and washing soda. All are based on copper sulfate and
coat the leaves, remaining active for several weeks. New growth formed
during this period is uncoated and therefore vulnerable, so repeat
applications are needed.
