**Symptoms**:
- Leaves curl upward at the edges (taco shape)
- Leaf tips and edges turn brown and crispy
- Leaves may feel dry and brittle
- New growth is small and deformed
- Flowers in late stage may "foxtail" — elongated, airy growth from the bud

**Recovery**:
- Reduce temperature (increase ventilation, add fans, move lights further away)
- Affected leaves will not recover but new growth will be healthy
- If heat stress occurred during flowering, foxtailing is permanent for that cycle

### 2. Cold Stress

**Causes**:
- Temperatures below 60°F (15°C)
- Cold drafts
- Frozen or very cold water
- Rapid day-to-night temperature drops

**Symptoms**:
- Growth slows dramatically
- Leaves may develop purple or dark coloring (especially stems)
- Leaves curl downward and become dark
- Root uptake slows, mimicking [[cho-plant-growth-stage-treatment-protocols]] (especially seedlings)
- Too many hours of light (rare in standard 18/6 or 12/12)
- Light leaks during dark period in flowering

**Symptoms**:
- Seedlings: bleaching, yellowing, upward leaf curling
- Bleached or white patches on leaves closest to light
- Stretching (insufficient light)
- Hermaphroditism (from light leaks during dark period in flowering)

**Recovery**:
- Adjust light distance and intensity
- Eliminate all light leaks during dark period
- See [[cannabis-transplanting-guide]].
