# PF TEK Ethyl Alcohol Psilocybin Extraction and Liqueur Concentration

## Overview

The PF TEK describes a complete ethyl [[1960s-counterculture-and-magic-mushrooms]] and concentrating it into a "[[australian-magic-mushroom-common-names]] liqueur" of controllable potency. The technique uses only mushrooms and pure ethyl alcohol, avoiding any toxic chemical solvents. The entire process is designed for home practitioners and results in a storable, doseable liquid extract that can be precisely metered using a syringe.

The quality of the final extract depends entirely on the [[brassinosteroid-morphological-effects-psilocybe-cubensis-fruiting]] specimens

## Reagent: 200 Proof Ethyl Alcohol

### Procurement

200 proof (100% pure, anhydrous) ethyl alcohol is the preferred solvent:

- Available at well-stocked liquor stores that supply hospitals and laboratories
- 190 proof (95% ethyl, 5% water) is acceptable but the residual water is a negative factor, especially when evaporating down to concentrate
- If a permit is required, state liquor board applications typically cost $5 to $10
- Stated use on permit applications: "non-toxic surface sterilizing plus herb extraction, preservation, tincture, and perfume making"

### Why 200 Proof Matters

The absence of water is critical because the extraction targets psilocybin, which is alcohol-soluble. Residual water in 190 proof alcohol introduces complications during evaporation and concentration, potentially degrading the final product quality. Pure ethyl alcohol also eliminates the need for any separation steps.

## Extraction Procedure

### Step 1: Pulverization

1. Ensure mushrooms are thoroughly dry (rock hard) to enable complete pulverization
2. Place dried mushrooms into a small, strong zip-lock freezer bag
3. Cover the bag with a magazine for protection against tearing
4. Pound the bag with the rubber heel of a large shoe until the material is reduced to fine particles

Complete pulverization maximizes surface area for solvent contact, which directly impacts extraction efficiency.

### Step 2: Initial Soak

1. Pour the pulverized mushroom material into a bottle with a tight-fitting, leak-proof cap
2. Add enough 200 proof ethyl alcohol to cover the material and create a loose slurry
3. Shake the bottle well to fully suspend the material
4. Let it sit for 24 hours at room temperature
5. Shake periodically during the soaking period to activate and maintain extraction

### Step 3: First Filtration

1. Fashion an inexpensive dust-pollen mask (available at hardware, drug, and paint stores) over the mouth of a drinking glass
2. Shake the bottle of slurry thoroughly and pour some through the filter
3. Squeeze the filter and slurry to extract the alcohol
4. Store the filtered alcohol extract in a fresh, clean bottle

The dust-pollen mask material makes "excellent filters" for this purpose according to PF. The mask is typically white or tan, fits over the nose and mouth, and is held by a rubber band.

### Step 4: Secondary Extraction

1. Retrieve the mushroom material remaining in the filter
2. Return it to the extraction bottle
3. Cover with fresh 200 proof ethyl alcohol
4. Repeat the 24-hour soak and filtration process

## Evaporation and Concentration

### Setup

1. Combine all alcohol extracts into a single glass container
2. Position a small electric fan (desk clip-on fans are ideal) near the glass
3. Direct the airflow straight down into the glass until the alcohol surface ripples

### Process

- Evaporation takes several hours depending on the volume of extract
- As the alcohol level recedes, wash the residue adhering to the inside of the glass back into the solution
- Any fumes generated are harmless because the solvent is drinkable ethyl alcohol
- Keep all flames away from the solution — pure alcohol is highly flammable
