# Mushroom Species and Cultivation Methods

Mushroom cultivation can be found in forests around the world. Given the
proper environment, mushrooms grow and offer a good source of natural
vitamins and minerals. The most commonly and easily cultivated mushrooms
in Thailand and Southeast Asian countries are oyster mushrooms, ear
mushrooms, and straw mushrooms. Selecting the right type must be based on
climatic conditions and market demand.

## Nature of Mushrooms

Mushrooms or fungi do not contain chlorophyll and must feed on plants or
animal matter. They need a controlled environment with appropriate
humidity, light, temperature, ventilation, air pressure, pH, and
nutrients, plus a disease-free environment. Three groups exist:

1. **Saprophytes** - Feed on dead plants or animals. Pleurotus
   ostreatus (oyster mushroom) is an example. Saprophytes help break down
   dead matter.
2. **Parasites** - Feed on living plants or animals, sometimes damaging
   or killing their host.
3. **Symbiotic fungi** - Grow on living plants without damaging them.
   Both organisms benefit from the relationship.

Mushrooms develop from mycelium (loosely arranged thread-like hyphae)
living in soil or substrate. Hyphae develop from spores produced in
mushroom gills. A mushroom forms only when hyphae from positive and
negative spores join. If conditions are right with enough food and
moisture, hyphae grow into a tangled mass forming a "button" that
creates a mushroom.

## Major Cultivation Steps

The complete mushroom cultivation process follows twenty major steps:
learning about mushrooms, producing PDA medium, selecting tissue culture,
multiplying spawn on sorghum seeds, producing substrate bags, pasteurizing
bags, inoculating with sorghum seeds, incubating bags, opening bags,
maintaining and monitoring, harvesting, cultivating straw mushrooms,
packaging, marketing, processing, waste management and recycling,
troubleshooting, preparing the mushroom house, starting the business, and
keeping records.

## Key Species for Cultivation

### Oyster Mushrooms (Pleurotus species)

Multiple varieties are cultivated in substrate bags with temperature
ranges of 20 to 37 degrees Celsius depending on variety. Common types
include:

- **[[pleurotus-citrinopileatus]]** (yellow) - 24-34 degrees C.

Oyster mushrooms are the most commonly cultivated and recommended for
beginners due to ease of cultivation and commercial viability.

### Ear Mushrooms (Auricularia species)

- **[[auricularia-auricula]]** (pale brown) - 26-34 degrees C.

### Straw Mushrooms (Volvariella species)

Cultivated using a plot method rather than substrate bags:

- **[[ganoderma-lucidum]]** (reishi) - 24-37 degrees C, summer and rainy.
- **Agrocybe cylindracea** - 20-34 degrees C, mid-rainy to early winter.
- **[[flammulina-velutipes]]).
