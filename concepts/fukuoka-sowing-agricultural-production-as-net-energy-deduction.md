# Agricultural Production as Net Energy Deduction

In *Sowing Seeds in the Desert*, Masanobu Fukuoka presented a devastating
energy accounting of modern industrial agriculture, arguing that what appears
to be increased food production is actually a net loss when all energy inputs
are tallied. This analysis undermines the fundamental premise of the Green
Revolution and challenges claims that industrial farming can feed a growing
world population.
