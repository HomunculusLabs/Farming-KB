### Key Observations

- **Psilocybin range**: 0.7 to 6.2 mg/g dry weight — a nearly 9× range.
- **Consistently low psilocin**: All samples had very low psilocin levels
  (0 to 0.3 mg/g), with three of five samples containing 0.3 mg/g and two
  containing none at all.
- **Bimodal distribution**: Samples 1 and 2 had psilocybin levels
  comparable to the best controlled-culture results (5.1-8.3 mg/g), while
  samples 3-5 had dramatically lower levels.

used a single Amazonian strain, standardized rye-grain substrate, controlled
temperature and humidity, and optimized timing.
