# High-Volume Extraction Method

## Overview

The high potency and small volume of cannabis oil, combined with
anti-marijuana laws, has created high potential profits for smugglers
and new problems for law enforcement. The U.S. DEA (Drug Enforcement,
1973) has described various seized devices. The situation is
reminiscent of the traditional moonshiner-revenuer chase. The
apparatus described here is constructed using two 55-gallon oil drums
and equipment from hardware or surplus stores. Very large amounts of
marijuana or hashish may be extracted with this setup, which performs
refluxing, soxhleting, distilling, and solvent collection.

## Safety Considerations

Even with safety features (pressure relief valves and construction
design preventing minor mishaps from becoming disastrous), the large
volumes of inflammable solvents pose a serious fire hazard. The large
scale and violence of a major accident warrant all possible caution.
The device is always tended during operation, and shutdown measures
are ready at all times. See
[[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-reflux-apparatus-and-laboratory-setup]] for
basic safety principles that apply at any scale.

## Operating Principle

The solvent and cannabis mixtures are heated via a tub of boiling
water in the lower oil drum. The upper drum acts as a giant condenser,
filled with circulating ice water so solvent fumes recondense into
liquid and fall back into the lower drum. Tube tops are closed with
rubber stoppers except for one acting as a safety pressure relief
valve. Solvent fumes also condense inside the copper tubes on contact
with the bottom surface of the condenser drum.

## Condenser Drum Construction

Mark corresponding four-inch grids on the top and bottom surfaces of
the oil drum. Drill holes at each grid intersection and run pieces of
one-inch copper tubing through the drum lengthwise, protruding about
one inch from both surfaces. Solder or weld watertight seals around
each tube where it passes through the drum. Fit inlet and outlet
fittings for circulating ice water coolant to the top. Attach metal
straps with large holes on opposing sides for chain lifting with a
small overhead crane.

## Lower Drum Preparation

Remove the top of the drum. Attach sturdy legs to the bottom for
maintaining a space above the boiling water bath. Attach a copper
drain tube with on/off valve to the bottom for siphoning the
solvent/cannabis solution. Attach four metal straps to the top,
protruding above the drum, to secure the upper drum. Run a fitting
with on/off valve through the side of the drum approximately halfway
between top and bottom.

## Soxhlet Chamber

During extraction, a chamber containing marijuana or hashish is placed
between the recondensed solvent drops from the condenser and the
boiling solvent below. The distilled solvent runs through the cannabis
material, washing out the oil. The soxhlet chamber has many holes
punctured in the bottom. A hole cut in the side of the drum, slightly
higher than the side valve, allows solvent removal via a funnel
catching recondensed drops from the copper tubes.

## Required Equipment

1. A large, deep tub, at least twice the diameter of the oil drums,
   for a boiling water bath.
2. An immersion pump and a large pail for ice and water.
3. A long hose running from a copper tube to an empty jug.
4. Rubber stoppers for the remaining copper tube tops.
5. Large polyethylene trash bags and giant rubber bands from inner
   tubes to seal the drum junction.
6. A large funnel matching the drum diameter with tubing to connect
   to the side valve.
7. An overhead chain winch or locking block and tackle.
8. Three heavy-duty sparkless electric hotplates.

## Refluxing Procedure

Grind the marijuana or hashish to a fine powder. Fill the lower drum
approximately one-third full with powdered cannabis material, then add
solvent to half fill the drum. Close the side valve. Secure the upper
drum and seal the junction with a large trash bag (bottom cut out)
held by inner-tube rubber bands above and below the joining. This
acts as a safety valve; pressure buildup causes the plastic to puff
out. Place the apparatus in the tub on the three hotplates.

Run ice water coolant through the condenser drum via immersion pump.
Seal all copper tube tops with rubber stoppers except one, attaching
a garden hose running to a large jug in a safe location. If fumes or
liquid appear in the jug, immediately remove the boiling water and add
ice and water, then check the condenser system.

## Draining and Collecting

Heat the water bath to boiling and reflux the mixture for three hours.
Turn off and disconnect the hotplates. Remove boiling water, refill
the tub with ice and water, and stand for at least one-half hour.
After cooling, use a siphon pump to draw the oil-saturated solvent
through the drain tube. Remove all solvent, drain the condenser, open
the drums, and scoop out all cannabis material into a closed drum.

## Distillation and Solvent Recovery

Replace the cannabis oil/solvent mixture in the lower drum and
assemble for distillation. The funnel held by the empty soxhlet
chamber collects recondensed pure solvent drops from the tubes. The
liquid runs through a hose to the side fitting, then outside via
another hose to a receiving barrel. When all solvent evaporates, a
heavy oil film coats the drum bottom. Redissolve in a small amount of
solvent and store in an unbreakable container. Replace the damp
cannabis in the drum with collected solvent and repeat the three-hour
reflux cycle. After soxhleting, combine all collected extracts for
further processing or [[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-isomerization]].
