# High-Volume Extraction Method

## Overview

The high potency and small volume of [[cervantes-hash-cannabis-oil-production]], combined with
anti-marijuana laws, has created high potential profits for smugglers
and new problems for law enforcement. The U.S. DEA (Drug Enforcement,
1973) has described various seized devices. The situation is
reminiscent of the traditional moonshiner-revenuer chase. The
apparatus described here is constructed using two 55-gallon oil drums
and equipment from hardware or surplus stores. Very large amounts of
marijuana or hashish may be extracted with this setup, which performs
refluxing, soxhleting, distilling, and solvent collection.

## Safety Considerations

Even with safety features (pressure relief valves and construction
design preventing minor mishaps from becoming disastrous), the large
volumes of inflammable solvents pose a serious fire hazard. The large
scale and violence of a major accident warrant all possible caution.
The device is always tended during operation, and shutdown measures
are ready at all times. See
[[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-isomerization]].
