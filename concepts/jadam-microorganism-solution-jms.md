# JADAM Microorganism Solution (JMS)

JMS cultures [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms]] from forest leaf mold using boiled potato as food. Simpler than [[indigenous-microorganisms-imo]] -- no multi-stage collection, no sugar/molasses needed. At peak fermentation, 1ml contains ~0.1-1 billion microbes. The core of [[jadam-methods]] soil management. 500L batch costs nearly nothing.

## Collecting Leaf Mold (Starter)

Collect from mountains near your farm. The best spots have thick layers of soft, dark soil formed from decomposed leaves. Remove the undecomposed top leaves, take the soft soil mixed with decomposing debris. Conifer areas are acidic -- mix with leaf mold from 2-3 other locations.

Store in black plastic bags or gunnysack in shade. Keep loosely closed. Becoming dry is not serious. You will use ~500g per batch.

**No leaf mold available?** Pile wild grass on bare soil, keep moist. Microbes propagate on the grass/soil surface. Soil becomes soft in ~3 weeks. Use as starter.

## Recipe: 500L (132 gal) Batch

**Starter**: Leaf mold 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
**Medium**: Boiled potato 1 kg (2.2 lb) + sea salt 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
**Optional**: 1 kg chopped crop residues (fruits, leaves, branches) or wild grass for crop-specific microbes
**Water**: 500L -- non-chlorinated preferred; soft water for foliar use

**Equipment**: 500L container with lid, 2 cotton/nylon bags (socks), rocks (to sink bags), stick, string. For cold weather: heater + insulation wrap.

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Boil potato until soft, put in bag with rocks, knead until fully dissolved into water
2. Put leaf mold in separate bag with rocks, knead to extract microbes into water
3. Dissolve sea salt in the water
4. (Optional) Blend crop residues, put in bag, knead into water
5. Hang bags underwater so contents are submerged
6. Close lid, place in sun, at [[jadam-ambient-temperature-principle]] matching your crops
7. **Fermentation time**: 24 hrs at 28C (82F), 72 hrs at 25C (77F), 96 hrs at 19C (66F)
8. Bubbles appear first, then foam forms a circular disc (pizza-like layer)
9. **Ready when foam is at peak** -- clear disc with vigorous foam, edges well-defined
10. Filter through fine net before applying
11. Use completely at peak -- do not store (see troubleshooting)

## Cold Weather Cultivation (water below 18C / 64F)

- Wrap container in insulation on all sides (bottom, sides, top)
- Use electric heater: 3kW for 500L, 1.5kW for 100-300L
- Set to 20C (68F) for cold-adapted microbes, 25C (77F) for vigorous growth
- Use ELCB (earth leakage circuit breaker) for safety
- Fermentation: ~72 hrs at 20C

## Application

**Soil drench** (primary use):
- Dilute 1:10 minimum with water
- Apply with every watering, 3-4 times per month
- Before transplanting or before lowering/sprouting of fruit trees: apply abundantly
- 500L serves 0.1 to 3.3 ha (0.25 to 8.2 acres)
- Combine with sea water/salt and [[jadam-phyllite-solution]] for full mineral program

**Foliar spray** (prevention):
- JMS 10-20L + JWA 3L per 500L water
- Dilute JMS over 20x for foliar -- higher amounts break down JWA
- Filter very thoroughly (finer than fine net, use fabric) to avoid marks on fruit
- Increases microbial diversity on leaf surface, preventing pathogen dominance

**JMS Pesticide** (prevention formula, per 500L):
- JMS 20L + [[comparison-jerusalem-artichoke-vs-potato]] JHS 5L + ginkgo JHS 5L + JWA 3L
- Use regularly before disease/pest outbreaks
- If disease breaks out, replace JMS with JS; if pest occurs, use KPFR JHS instead

## JMS-JHS Pesticide (for active pest control)

When pest pressure requires more than prevention, add JHS to JMS:
- JMS 10-20L + JHS (any type) 5-10L + JWA 3-5L per 500L
- Increase JHS and JWA together for stronger effect
- Can kill insects when JHS is included
- Always use JWA or marks will form on plants

## Key Principles

- **Culture at crop temperature**, not at a fixed 32C -- produces locally-adapted microbes
- **Use at peak foam** -- microbe count drops 10,000x after foam dies
- **Embrace diversity** -- do not select specific "good" microbes; use the whole leaf mold community
- **Do not use air pumps** -- produces too much foam, makes it impossible to judge peak
- **pH ~6.5** (potato medium, unlike sugar/molasses which acidify)

## Quality Indicators

- **Good**: Vigorous circular foam disc, clear edges, active bubbling
- **Ready**: Foam at maximum coverage, pizza-like appearance -- lasts ~12 hours
- **Too late**: Foam edges crumble, bubbles dying down -- use as liquid fertilizer (dilute 1:10)
- **Failed**: All foam gone, film on surface -- microbes dead, use as JLF only

## Troubleshooting

- **No bubbles after 48 hrs**: Temperature too low (below 18C) -- add heat
- **Small weak bubbles**: Normal in cold weather (smaller cold-adapted microbes)
- **Bad smell / mosquito larvae**: Missed peak use window -- dilute 1:10, use as fertilizer
- **Marks on leaves after foliar spray**: Filter more thoroughly, increase JWA, ensure soft water
