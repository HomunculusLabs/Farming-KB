## Seaweed and Sea Minerals in Compost Tea Brewing

## Seaweed as a Food Source

Seaweed and kelp products are commonly added to compost tea as food
sources for the microorganisms. They provide a wide range of
micronutrients, trace minerals, growth stimulants, and complex
carbohydrates. Different species of seaweed contain different
profiles of these compounds, so the source and processing method
matter significantly for the quality of the tea produced.

## Kelp Meal vs. Liquid Kelp

Kelp meal is dried, ground seaweed that provides a slow-release food
source. Liquid kelp extracts are more immediately available to
microorganisms in the tea. The processing method used to create
liquid kelp matters: cold-processed or enzymatically-digested kelp
retains more of the beneficial compounds compared to heat-processed
or chemically-extracted products. Heat can destroy many of the
