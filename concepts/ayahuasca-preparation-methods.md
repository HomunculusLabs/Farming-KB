# Ayahuasca Preparation Methods

Ayahuasca is a brew made from two principal plants: Banisteriopsis caapi (the MAOI-containing vine) and Psychotria viridis or Diplopterys cabrerana (the DMT-containing leaf). The combination allows oral DMT to be psychoactive — normally DMT is inactive orally because monoamine oxidase (MAO) in the gut destroys it. See [[beta-carboline-chemistry]] for the biochemistry.

## The Two Plants

### Banisteriopsis caapi (Ayahuasca Vine)
- The "vine of souls" — provides the harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine)
- These are reversible MAO-A inhibitors that block gut MAO, allowing DMT to reach the bloodstream
- The vine also contributes its own psychoactive effects: visions, emotional catharsis, purgative effects
- Different varieties: "Cielo" (sky — mild), "Thunder" (intense), "Indian" (dark, strong)

### DMT-Containing Plants (Chacruna/Chaliponga)
- **Psychotria viridis** (Chacruna): Leaves containing 0.1-0.6% DMT. Most common admixture in Peru
- **Diplopterys cabrerana** (Chaliponga/Oco-Yaje): Leaves containing DMT + 5-MeO-DMT. Stronger, more visionary. Used in Ecuador and Colombia
- Both plants are added to the brew for their DMT content

## Traditional Preparation

### Materials
- Banisteriopsis caapi vine sections (fresh or dried)
- Psychotria viridis leaves (fresh or dried)
- Large pot (aluminum, stainless steel, or clay — avoid iron/copper)
- Water
- Wood fire (traditional) or stove

### Process
1. **Clean and prepare**: Wash the vine, scrape or pound to break fibers. Strip leaves from stems
2. **Shred**: The vine is shredded by pounding with a mallet or scraping with a machete
3. **First boil**: Place vine in pot, cover with water, boil for 4-8 hours. The water becomes dark and bitter
4. **Add chacruna**: After 2-3 hours, add the DMT-containing leaves
5. **Reduce**: Continue boiling, reducing the liquid. Periodically remove plant material, add fresh water
6. **Concentrate**: Boil until the liquid is reduced to a thick, dark syrup (1-2 liters from 50+ liters of water)
7. **Strain**: Remove all plant material, strain through cloth
8. **Repeat**: Often the same plant material is boiled 2-3 times, combining the extracts
9. **Final reduction**: Combine all extracts, reduce to desired concentration

### Traditional Additions
- **Mapacho** (ayahuasca-preparation|Nicotiana rustica): Strong jungle tobacco added for flavor and effect
- **Toé** (Brugmansia): Datura-like plant, sometimes added but dangerous (scopolamine)
- **Camalari** ( Justicia pectoralis): Mild MAOI, flavoring
- **Chiric Sanango** (Brunfelsia): Added in some traditions

## Dose

- **Standard dose**: 100-200 ml of concentrated brew
- **Effect depends on**: Strength of the brew (how concentrated), ratio of vine to chacruna, individual sensitivity
- **Experienced practitioners** often consume larger doses (300-500 ml)
- **First-timers**: Start small (50-100 ml) — the effects can be overwhelming

## Modern/At-Home Preparation

### Aqueous Extraction
1. Shred 50-100g B. caapi and 50g P. viridis
2. Simmer in 2-3 liters water with lemon juice or vinegar (acid helps extraction)
3. Simmer for 2-3 hours, strain
4. Repeat 2 more times with fresh water
5. Combine all liquid, reduce to 100-200 ml on low heat
6. Store in dark glass bottle, refrigerate (lasts 1-2 weeks)

### Freeze Precipitation (Pharmahuasca)
- Not traditional ayahuasca — this is a modern approach using purified plant extracts
- Extract harmala alkaloids from B. caapi (or Syrian rue) using acid-base extraction
- Extract DMT from Mimosa hostilis or P. viridis using acid-base extraction
- Take harmala extract first (MAOI), wait 20-30 min, then take DMT extract
- More predictable dosing than brew but lacks the traditional ceremonial context

### Ayahuasca Analog (Pharmahuasca with Syrian Rue)
- Syrian rue (Peganum harmala) seeds as MAOI substitute for B. caapi
- 2-4g ground Syrian rue seeds brewed as tea (MAOI)
- DMT extracted from M. hostilis or purchased (illegal in many countries)
- Same pharmacology, different plants

## Preparation Safety

### Critical Warnings
- **Toé/Datura**: Avoid preparations containing Brugmansia (toé). Scopolamine causes dangerous delirium, not psychedelic effects. Many hospitalizations and deaths
- **Purification**: Traditional diets (dieta) prepare the body — modern users should avoid tyramine-rich foods, SSRIs, and medications
- **MAOI interactions**: See [[psychedelic-safety-and-contra-indications]] for full interaction list
- **Adulteration**: Only prepare with verified plant material from trusted sources

### Set and Setting
- Traditional preparation is always ceremonial, with an experienced facilitator (curandero/ayahuasquero)
- Icaros (medicine songs) are integral to the experience
- Dietas (restrictive diets) are observed before and after
- See [[psychedelic-therapy-set-and-setting]] for modern adaptation
