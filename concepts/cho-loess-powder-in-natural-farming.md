## Preparation Method

### Materials

-   Raw loess soil (from ant hills or clean deposits)
-   Water
-   Containers for settling and drying

### Steps

1.  Put 2-3 kg of raw loess into 20 liters water; stir thoroughly.
2.  Allow to settle. Heavy particles and stones sink; fine particles
    float.
3.  Collect floating fine particles into a separate container — these
    are the most biologically active fraction.
4.  Allow collected particles to precipitate over several hours.
5.  Drain surface water. Dry precipitated loess in a cool, shaded place.
    **Critical: direct sunlight drying prevents redispersal in water.**
6.  Well-prepared loess should be flour-fine in texture.

### Seedling Treatment
