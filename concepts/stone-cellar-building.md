# Stone Cellar Building

Stone cellars provide permanent storage [[query-how-do-i-grow-elderberry-for-fruit-and-medicine]] vegetables with the same basic characteristics as wooden earth cellars but are designed to last indefinitely, requiring a great deal more work to construct.

## Storage Conditions

An optimal [[holzer-stone-cellar-construction]] maintains humidity between 80 and 90 percent and temperature between 8 and 10°C using just the warmth [[mckenna-food-of-the-gods-exopheromones-and-plant-communication]] soil — no additional energy required.

## Construction Details

### Drainage

Gravel is laid on the floor for drainage. Good air circulation is essential.

### Ventilation Pipes

- **Air inlet pipe**: Must be at least ten metres long and reach a depth of around one metre underground. On its way through the pipe the air is brought to the same temperature as the soil. If incoming air temperature differs from inside, condensation builds up, dripping water can cause crops to rot or moulder.
- **Pipe diameter**: For a 10m³ earth cellar, ventilation pipes should have a diameter of at least 15cm.
- **Air outlet pipe**: Placed at the highest point [[query-what-are-the-benefits-of-straw-mulch-in-the-garden-and-how-do-i-use-it]] room to prevent condensation build-up.
- **Dual function**: If the air inlet pipe is sloped slightly downwards, it can also serve [[query-can-bamboo-be-used-as-a-building-material-and-how-do-i-get-started]] drain pipe when cleaning the cellar.

## Key Principle

The diameter of both inlet and outlet pipes must be adjusted according to the size of the room. Proper ventilation ensures oxygen supply while maintaining the stable temperature that the soil provides naturally.
