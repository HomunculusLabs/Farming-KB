### Thermal Compost

Pathogens are killed by heat. The compost must reach **55 degrees Celsius (131 degrees Fahrenheit) or higher for at least 3 consecutive days** in all parts of the pile. No part of the pile should exceed 70 degrees Celsius (158 degrees Fahrenheit). The pile must be turned to achieve adequate temperature distribution throughout. Temperature should be monitored daily and turning performed each time the compost approaches 68 to 70 degrees Celsius.

The key risk with thermal compost is creating anaerobic pockets within the pile. If portions of the pile become compacted or waterlogged, temperatures may not reach pathogen-killing levels in those zones, creating refugia for surviving pathogens.
