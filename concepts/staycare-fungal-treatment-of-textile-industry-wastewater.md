## Fungal Treatment of Textile Industry Wastewater

## Overview

Textile dyeing and manufacturing generate large volumes of colored wastewater containing [[mollison-designers-wastewater-treatment-wetlands]]. Dyes of concern include azo dyes, triphenylmethanes, anthraquinones, metal phthalocyanins, and indigoids. White rot fungi have emerged as promising bioremediation agents due to their non-specific ligninolytic enzyme systems capable of degrading a wide range of chromophoric structures.

## Types of Textile Effluents and Chromophores

| Effluent Category | Examples | Chromophore Types |
|-------------------|----------|-------------------|
| Chemical industry effluents | Various | Wide range including dyes |
| Dyes and textile dyeing effluents | Dye house effluents | Azo, triphenylmethane, metal phthalocyanin, anthraquinone |
| Paper making and pulping effluents | Kraft effluent, sulfite liquor | Lignin fragments, lignin sulfonate, chlorolignins, chlorinated phenolics |
| Cotton bleaching effluents | Cotton black liquor | Melanoidins or similar |
| Molasses-based effluents | Molasses spent wash | Melanoidins |
| Olive oil milling effluents | - | Polyphenolics |
