## Potato Growing - Organic Methods

Potatoes are one of the most rewarding crops for the organic gardener.
Geoff Hamilton considered the potato to be a vegetable that truly
justifies organic methods, as the difference in taste between
organically grown early potatoes and their inorganic counterparts
is remarkable. Main also serve as an excellent
cleaning crop in new gardens, helping to break up compacted soil
with their vigorous root systems.

## Choosing Varieties

Always buy certified disease-free seed potatoes. Select small tubers
with few sprouts to avoid undue competition, which produces a larger
number of inferior tubers. Seed companies sometimes supply potato
eyes rather than whole tubers; these are sold packed in moist
vermiculite and can be potted in the greenhouse until planting time.

Early varieties such as Yukon Gold, Irish Cobbler, and Adirondack Red
are dug and eaten fresh. Main crop varieties like Red Pontiac are
lifted in autumn and stored for winter use. There are numerous
varieties that perform differently in different areas, so experiment
with a few tubers of a different type each year.
