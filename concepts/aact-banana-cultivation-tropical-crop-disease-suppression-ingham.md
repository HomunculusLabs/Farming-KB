### 2. Foliar Application for Black Sigatoka

AACT applied as a foliar spray creates a biological barrier on banana leaf surfaces. The beneficial bacteria and fungi in the tea:

- Occupy the leaf surface (phyllosphere), leaving no space for Black Sigatoka spores to germinate
- Produce antibiotics and competitive exudates that inhibit pathogen establishment
- Trigger systemic acquired resistance (SAR) in the plant tissue
- Accelerate decomposition of infected leaf tissue, reducing inoculum

Foliar AACT applications should begin before the wet season when disease pressure increases. Ingham recommends applications every 7-14 days during high-risk periods, applied with adequate coverage to both upper and lower leaf surfaces.

### 3. Root Zone Inoculation

Soil drench applications of AACT directly inoculate the root zone with beneficial organisms:

- Mycorrhizal fungi colonize banana roots, extending the effective root surface area for nutrient and water uptake
- Beneficial fluorescent Pseudomonas bacteria produce antifungal compounds that suppress Fusarium
- Trichoderma species directly parasitize Fusarium hyphae and compete for root exudate resources
- [[aact-brewing-ingredients-ingham]] for fungal dominance**:

- Compost: 2-3 cups per 5 gallons of water
- Fish hydrolysate: 1 tablespoon (provides amino acids and fungal food)
- Kelp meal: 1 tablespoon (trace minerals and cytokinins)
- Humic acid: 1 tablespoon (fungal food and pH buffering)
- Rock dust: 1 tablespoon (minerals and fungal stimulation)
- Molasses: minimal or none (molasses strongly favors bacteria over fungi)
- Chitin (crab waste): 1 teaspoon (stimulates chitinolytic fungi that suppress Fusarium)

**Brew duration**: 24-36 hours for fungal-dominant teas. Fungi require longer to grow and reproduce compared to bacteria. The longer brew time allows hyphal fragments to develop in the tea.

## Application Protocol for Bananas

### Soil Drench

- Apply 5-10 gallons of AACT per mature banana plant per application
- Apply to the root zone, extending to the drip line
- Frequency: Monthly during the growing season; every 2 weeks during periods of disease stress
- Apply when soil moisture is adequate but not waterlogged
- Avoid application during heavy rain to prevent runoff

### Foliar Spray

- Apply 2-5 gallons per acre using fine mist nozzles
- Ensure complete coverage of both leaf surfaces, especially the undersides where Black Sigatoka establishes
- Apply in the early morning or late evening to avoid UV damage to organisms
- Add a sticking agent (diluted molasses or yucca extract) to improve adhesion
- Frequency: Every 7-14 days during high disease pressure periods
