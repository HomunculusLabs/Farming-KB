## Black Plastic Mulch

Black plastic mulch increases soil temperature and is ideal for warm
season crops such as tomatoes, eggplants, peppers, and vine crops.
It is applied before planting so the soil has time to warm. Black
plastic nearly eliminates weed growth, conserves moisture, and keeps
fruit clean by preventing soil splash. Lay plastic tightly over a
smooth seedbed, secure edges by burying them in soil, and cut holes
for planting.
