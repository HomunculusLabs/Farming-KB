# Immune-Modulating Mushrooms and Herbs

Immunomodulators regulate and optimize immune function — enhancing
underactive responses and calming overactive ones. They restore balance
rather than simply stimulating or suppressing.

## Mechanism: Beta-Glucans

Beta-glucans are polysaccharides in fungal cell walls (beta-1,3/1,6-glucans
are most biologically active).

1. **Recognition:** Macrophages, NK cells, and dendritic cells detect beta-
   glucans via Dectin-1 and CR3 receptors.
2. **Activation:** Macrophages increase phagocytosis, NK cells enhance
   cytotoxicity, cytokine production is modulated.
3. **Trained immunity:** Beta-glucans induce innate immune memory for
   enhanced future responses.
4. **Balancing:** Upregulates Th1 cytokines (cellular immunity) while
   modulating excessive Th2 responses (allergic/inflammatory).
5. **Anti-tumor:** NK cell activation enhances tumor surveillance.

**Bioavailability:** Raw mushrooms have limited bioavailability (beta-glucans
locked in chitin). Hot water extraction breaks down chitin. Dual extraction
(water + alcohol) provides the fullest spectrum.

## Medicinal Mushrooms

### Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum)
Deep immune tonic, anti-inflammatory, adaptogen. Beta-glucans + triterpenoids
(ganoderic acids). Enhances NK cell activity, modulates cytokines, inhibits
NF-kB. 1-3g powder or dual extract daily. "Mushroom of immortality" in TCM.

### Shiitake (Lentinula edodes)
Culinary with medicinal properties. Lentinan (beta-glucan) enhances immune
cells; approved as cancer adjunct in Japan. 6-16g fresh daily; 1-3g extract.
Easy to grow at home on logs or sawdust.

### Maitake (Grifola frondosa)
Immune modulation, blood sugar, anti-tumor. D-fraction beta-glucan enhances
macrophages and NK cells. 3-5g dried or 200-400mg D-fraction extract. "Hen of
the woods" — excellent edible.

### Turkey Tail (Trametes versicolor)
Potent immune modulator, cancer adjunct, gut microbiome support. PSK/PSP are
among the most researched mushroom compounds (approved in Japan since 1970s).
2-3g dried powder daily. Decoct 45-60 min. One of the most common mushrooms
worldwide.

### Chaga (Inonotus obliquus)
Antioxidant powerhouse, immune support. Beta-glucans, betulin/betulinic acid,
SOD, melanin. Highest ORAC scores. 1-2g dual extract daily; simmer 30-60 min.
Grows on birch in cold climates. Sustainable harvesting is critical.

### Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus)
Cognitive enhancement, nerve regeneration (NGF stimulation), immune support.
Hericenones, erinacines, beta-glucans. 500-3000mg dual extract daily. Also see
[[adaptogenic-herbs-overview]].

### Cordyceps (Cordyceps militaris)
Energy, endurance, respiratory health, immune support. Cordycepin, adenosine,
beta-glucans. 1-3g powder or extract daily. C. militaris is cultivated and
affordable.

## Immune-Modulating Herbs

### Echinacea (Echinacea purpurea, E. angustifolia)
Acute immune stimulant for colds/flu. Alkylamides stimulate phagocytosis and
NK cells. 300-600mg extract 3x daily at onset; most effective at first sign
of infection. Not for long-term daily use.

### Elderberry (Sambucus nigra)
Antiviral for colds and flu. Anthocyanins inhibit viral replication, reduce
duration and severity. 1 tbsp syrup daily for prevention; 3-4x daily during
illness. Raw berries contain cyanogenic glycosides — always cook.

### Astragalus (Astragalus membranaceus)
Deep immune tonic, adaptogen. Polysaccharides enhance immune cells and
interferon production. 1-2g root decoction daily; 2-4ml tincture 2-3x daily.
Best preventively long-term; traditionally not during acute illness (TCM).

### Andrographis (Andrographis paniculata)
Acute immune stimulant, anti-infective. Andrographolides are antiviral,
antibacterial, anti-inflammatory. 400mg standardized extract 3x daily during
acute illness. Extremely bitter — use capsules.

## Extraction Methods

| Method | Solvent | Extracts | Best For |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| Decoction | Water | Beta-glucans, polysaccharides | Mushrooms, roots, bark |
| Tincture | Ethanol | Alkaloids, terpenoids, flavonoids | Herbs, resins |
| Dual extract | Water + ethanol | Full spectrum | Medicinal mushrooms |
| Glycerite | Glycerin/water | Mild constituents | Children's preparations |
| Acetum | Vinegar | Minerals, some alkaloids | Mineral-rich herbs |
