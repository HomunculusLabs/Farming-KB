## Immune-Modulating Mushrooms and Herbs Immunomodulators regulate and optimize immune function — enhancing underactive responses and calming overactive ones. They restore balance rather than simply stimulating or suppressing. ## Mechanism: Beta-Glucans Beta-glucans are polysaccharides in fungal cell walls (beta-1,3/1,6-glucans are most biologically active). 1. **Recognition:** Macrophages, NK cells, and dendritic cells detect beta- glucans via Dectin-1 and CR3 receptors. 2. **Activation:** Macrophages increase phagocytosis, NK cells enhance cytotoxicity, cytokine production is modulated. 3. **Trained immunity:** Beta-glucans induce innate immune memory for enhanced future responses. 4. **Balancing:** Upregulates Th1 cytokines (cellular immunity) while modulating excessive Th2 responses (allergic/inflammatory). 5. **Anti-tumor:** NK cell activation enhances tumor surveillance. **Bioavailability:** Raw mushrooms have limited bioavailability (beta-glucans locked in chitin). Hot water extraction breaks down chitin. Dual extraction (water + alcohol) provides the fullest spectrum. ## Medicinal Mushrooms ### Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum) Deep immune tonic, anti-inflammatory, adaptogen. Beta-glucans + triterpenoids (ganoderic acids). Enhances NK cell activity, modulates cytokines, inhibits NF-kB. 1-3g powder or dual extract daily. "Mushroom of immortality" in TCM. ### Shiitake (Lentinula edodes) Culinary with medicinal properties. Lentinan (beta-glucan) enhances immune cells; approved as cancer adjunct in Japan. 6-16g fresh daily; 1-3g extract. Easy to grow at home on logs or sawdust. ### Maitake (Grifola frondosa) Immune modulation, blood sugar, anti-tumor. D-fraction beta-glucan enhances macrophages and NK cells. 3-5g dried or 200-400mg D-fraction extract. "Hen of the woods" — excellent edible. ### Turkey Tail (Trametes versicolor) Potent immune modulator, cancer adjunct, gut microbiome support. PSK/PSP are among the most researched mushroom compounds (approved in Japan since 1970s). 2-3g dried powder daily. Decoct 45-60 min. One of the most common mushrooms worldwide. ### Chaga (Inonotus obliquus) Antioxidant powerhouse, immune support. Beta-glucans, betulin/betulinic acid, SOD, melanin. Highest ORAC scores. 1-2g dual extract daily; simmer 30-60 min. Grows on birch in cold climates. Sustainable harvesting is critical. ### Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus) Cognitive enhancement, nerve regeneration (NGF stimulation), immune support. Hericenones, erinacines, beta-glucans. 500-3000mg dual extract daily. Also see [[herbal-medicine-making-basics]] — [[medicinal-mushroom-antioxidant-anti-inflammatory]]
