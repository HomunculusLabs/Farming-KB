# Fungal Growth Kinetics and Mass Transfer in Bioremediation Understanding fungal growth kinetics and mass transfer processes is essential for designing and optimizing fungal-bioreactor-types-for-pollutant-removal design and operation. The specific growth rate of fungi is influenced by temperature, pH, nutrient availability, dissolved oxygen concentration, and the presence of toxic pollutants. Unlike bacteria, which typically exhibit exponential growth followed by a stationary phase, fungal growth in submerged culture often shows a linear phase as hyphae extend and branch. The morphology of fungal growth, including the formation of [[fungal-bioremediation-mechanisms]] can increase the apparent solubility of hydrophobic pollutants, enhancing mass transfer and degradation rates. However, surfactants can also be toxic to fungi or inhibit enzyme activity at high concentrations.
