## Urban Planning Critique

"Modern zoning is the antithesis of energy-conservative planning." Life, work, and recreation should be integrated in dwellings. Older cities -- Florence, Vienna, pre-1930 villages -- are better models where:
- Housing is above workplaces
- Gardens and food production are integrated
- Streets serve multiple functions
- No separation between residential and commercial zones

## Village Development Methods

### Land Trust Gifts
The most common method: Land is gifted to a tax-deductible trust for community use. Many people with large incomes benefit from purchasing, improving, and gifting land at improved value for paper profit.

### Options to Buy
Trusts can obtain options to purchase land at agreed prices, giving time to raise funds and plan development.

### Tribal/Trust Lands
Traditional indigenous models where land is held in common by clans or tribes.

### Investor Groups
Groups of people pool capital to purchase and develop land for community use.

### Self-Convened Groups
People find each other and form intentional communities, often using [[community-finance-and-alternative-economies]]

## Out-Migration and Community Resilience

Historical waves of out-migration occurred in the 1890s, 1930s, and 1960s-1990s. Communities that survived maintained:
- Diverse local economies
- Strong social bonds
- Connection to land and place
- Cultural traditions and celebrations
- Inter-generational knowledge transfer

## Extended Families and Social Structure

"Extended families, and bioregional networks are the alternative to nation-states." The critique of the "Toyota-Coca Cola-McDonald-Hilton monoculture" calls for:
- Cross-cultural enrichment rather than homogenization
- Local identity rooted in place
- Inter-village networks for trade and mutual aid
- Respect for traditional knowledge systems

## The Designer's Role

The permaculture designer working with communities must:
1. Understand local culture, climate, and resources
2. Facilitate community visioning, not impose designs
3. Start with water and food systems as foundation
4. Design for energy efficiency and self-reliance
5. Create [[permaculture-land-access-and-trusts]]
