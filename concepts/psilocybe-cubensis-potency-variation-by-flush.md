## Desiccant Drying Method

Desiccant drying uses chemical drying agents to absorb moisture from mushrooms in a sealed container. Silica gel beads are the most common desiccant. Mushrooms are placed in a container with a layer of desiccant, separated by a screen or paper towel to prevent direct contact. The desiccant absorbs moisture from the air within the sealed container, gradually drying the mushrooms over several days to weeks.

Desiccant drying is very gentle and preserves the appearance and structure of delicate specimens. It is particularly useful for specimens intended for identification or display purposes. However, it is slow and has limited capacity, making it impractical for large harvests. Silica gel can be regenerated by heating it in an oven at 250°F for 2-3 hours, which drives off absorbed moisture and restores its drying capacity.
