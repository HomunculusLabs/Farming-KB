## Three Parallel Responses to Environmental Crisis

In a world losing forests, species, and whole ecosystems, Mollison identifies three concurrent and parallel responses:

1. **Care for surviving natural assemblies**: Leave wilderness to heal itself. This means implacable opposition to further disturbance of any remaining natural forests where most species are still in balance.

2. **Rehabilitate degraded or eroded land**: Use complex pioneer species and long-term plant assemblies—trees, shrubs, ground covers—to restore stability to damaged systems.

3. **Create our own complex living environment**: Incorporate as many species as we can save or have need for, from wherever on earth they come. This is the primary domain of [[permaculture-landscape-society-anthropocentric-nature-centered-design]]
