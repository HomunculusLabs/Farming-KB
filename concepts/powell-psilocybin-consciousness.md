## The Epistemological Promise

Powell identifies two virtues of investigating entheogenic agents:

1. Through their dramatic action within the brain, we might perceive Nature in a new and arguably
   more enhanced way.
2. We might understand more about the underlying chemistry bound up with normal conscious
   processes — the modus operandi of entheogenic substances reveals the delicate chemical
   mechanisms governing consciousness and our perceptions of reality.

If through the study of entheogens we can understand more about the interface between the mind
and the "world out there," then we shall know more clearly what consciousness is, how it is
formed, and how it can come to experience transcendence. Powell argues that entheogenic agents
unleash a form of consciousness better able to grapple with ultimate questions about the reality
process than our normal frames of perception.
