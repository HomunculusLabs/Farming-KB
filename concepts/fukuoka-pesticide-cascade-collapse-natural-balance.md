## The Forest Ecosystem Cascade

Fukuoka traced a parallel cascade in Japanese pine forests:

1. Atmospheric pollution and acid rain increase soil acidity.
2. Acidity kills beneficial [[dighton-fungus-cultivation-ants-termites-bark-beetles]].
4. Dead pines open space for bamboo grass invasion.
5. Bamboo grass berries feed a mouse population explosion.
6. Mice attack cedar saplings planted as pine replacements.
7. Man applies rodenticide; weasels and snakes that eat mice decline.
8. To protect weasels, man begins raising mice to restore rodents.

"This is the stuff of crazed dreams," Fukuoka wrote. Each intervention creates
a new problem requiring a new intervention, in an endless spiral.

## The Apple Tree Case

1. Pesticides kill pollinating bees and gadflies.

3. More pesticides are applied to control new pests in the ecological vacuum.
