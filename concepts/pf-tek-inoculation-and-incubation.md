## Spore Syringe Preparation

A standard 10 cc syringe can inoculate **10 jars** at 1 cc per jar. Shake well
before each use. Store unused syringes **dark and cool** with needle guard on.
Syringes survive freezing but extreme cold kills spores. Resterilize the needle
in an alcohol flame before each reuse.
