## Germination Soil

Good germination soil should be sifted with no compost added.
Ordinary loam soil with pH of 7 and NPK where N is equal to or higher
than P and K is good for starting seeds. Ratios like 5:1:1 or 8:4:4
are good. Ensure N is equal to or higher than P and K factors.

## Pot Selection

Plants are best kept in pots that are somewhat large (1.5-3 gallon
pots) because cannabis does grow long roots.

**Perforated pots** (with holes at bottom): Sit on a dish. Some water
drains through. Empty the dish to avoid water spills. Good for
beginners who may over-water. However, drainage can leech nutrients.

**Non-perforated pots:** Used by advanced growers who never over-water.
No nutrient leeching occurs. Better control over moisture and feeding.
