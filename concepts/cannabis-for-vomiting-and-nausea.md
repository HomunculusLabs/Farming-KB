# Cannabis for Vomiting and Nausea (CINV)

updated: 2026-04-18

## Key Clinical Studies

### THC vs. Prochlorperazine (Comparative Studies)
Multiple studies compared oral THC directly against prochlorperazine, the standard antiemetic. Results consistently showed THC to be at least as effective, and in many cases superior, particularly for patients who had failed standard antiemetic therapy.

### Dronabinol Studies
- Dronabinol (synthetic THC) at 15mg doses demonstrated significant antiemetic effects
- Particularly effective for patients experiencing nausea refractory to other medications

### Pediatric Application (Jerusalem 1995)
One remarkable study administered 18mg THC as an edible oil to children undergoing chemotherapy. THC successfully prevented vomiting in pediatric patients, demonstrating safety and efficacy even in younger populations.

### Nabilone Combinations
Nabilone (synthetic cannabinoid) in combination with other antiemetics showed enhanced efficacy compared to single-agent therapy.

## Mechanism of Action

Cannabinoids reduce nausea and vomiting through:
- **CB1 receptor activation** in the brainstem's vomiting center (nucleus tractus solitarius)
- **Serotonin modulation**: Cannabinoids interact with 5-HT3 receptors, the same target as modern antiemetics like ondansetron
- **Anandamide pathways**: The endocannabinoid system naturally regulates nausea responses

## Morning Sickness

**CHI Value: 3.00** (1 study reviewed)

Cannabis has been traditionally used for pregnancy-related nausea. Modern research has examined cannabinoid antiemetic effects for morning sickness, though clinical studies remain limited due to ethical considerations around pregnancy.

### Food-as-Medicine Alternatives
Blesching suggests ginger (Zingiber officinale) as a well-established alternative:
- Ginger root tea or crystallized ginger
- German Commission E monograph approves ginger for prevention of motion sickness
- Pimpinella anisum (anise) has antispasmodic and relaxant effects on smooth muscle

### Mind-Body Considerations
Morning sickness may reflect ambivalence about pregnancy, unresolved fears about motherhood, or physical sensitivity to hormonal changes. The nausea-vomiting reflex is deeply connected to the brain's threat-detection systems.

## Motion Sickness

**CHI Value: 2.00** (2 studies reviewed)

Cannabinoids have been studied for motion sickness prevention. The endocannabinoid system modulates vestibular processing and the nausea response to motion stimuli.

## Endocannabinoid System and Nausea

The endocannabinoid system is intimately involved in nausea regulation:
- **Anandamide** levels in the brainstem correlate with nausea states
- CB1 receptor antagonists (like rimonabant) actually induce nausea -- confirming the system's natural anti-nausea role
