## The Endocannabinoid System and Brain Repair

The ECS is deeply embedded in the central nervous system. CB1 receptor sites
are especially prominent in brain regions related to motor control, cognition,
emotional responses, motivation, and homeostasis. CB2 receptor sites are found
primarily in the periphery but also exist in brain tissue, particularly in
microglia, the immune cells of the central nervous system. This widespread
distribution positions the ECS as a key regulator of brain function and repair.

### Neural Stem Cells and Neurogenesis

Neurogenesis, the birth of new neurons, occurs primarily in two regions of the
adult brain: the subventricular zone of the lateral ventricles and the
subgranular zone of the hippocampus. The hippocampus is critical for learning,
memory, and emotional regulation, making hippocampal neurogenesis directly
relevant to conditions such as depression, anxiety, and age-related cognitive
decline.

A landmark 2005 international study examined the potent synthetic cannabinoid
HU210 in rats and found that chronic cannabinoid treatment promoted
neurogenesis in the hippocampal regions. This finding was particularly
significant because most illegal substances have been reported to decrease the
growth of new nerve cells in the hippocampus. The researchers concluded that
cannabinoid-induced hippocampal neurogenesis likely produces both anxiolytic
