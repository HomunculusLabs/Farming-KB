### Decapitation Experiments

When the cap is removed from a developing mushroom:

- Normal geotropic responses of the stipe are disrupted
- The stipe may continue elongating but loses proper directional orientation
- The effect can be partially restored by applying gill diffusates

These experiments strongly support the existence of a cap-produced, gill-associated growth-regulating substance.

## Relationship to Higher Plant Hormones

Several researchers have investigated whether mushrooms contain the same hormones found in higher plants:

### Hormones Detected in Mushrooms

- **Auxins** (IAA — indole-3-acetic acid): Found in some mushroom species
- **Gibberellins**: Detected in certain basidiomycetes
- **Cytokinins**: Present in some fungal tissues
- **Ethylene**: Produced by some fungi, suggested as a signaling molecule in *Phycomyces*
