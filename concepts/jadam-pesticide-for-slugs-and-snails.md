# JADAM Pesticide for Slugs and Snails

JADAM's slug/snail control uses [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] combined with sodium hydroxide (caustic soda/NaOH). This is a corrosive formulation -- the only JADAM pesticide that requires skin protection during application. Developed by Youngsang Cho.

## Formulation (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

**Standard slug/snail control**:
- JWA: 3L (0.8 gal)
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH): 1.5kg (3.3 lb)
- Water: to 500L (132 gal)

**With insect control** (slugs + aphids/mites/moths):
- JWA: 3L + NaOH 1.5kg + JHS 5L ([[comparison-jerusalem-artichoke-vs-potato]])

**With [[hamilton-pest-and-disease-control-organic]]**:
- JWA: 3L + NaOH 1.5kg + JS 1-2L

## Preparation Steps

1. **Dissolve NaOH first**: dissolve caustic soda in a small quantity of water separately
2. Add dissolved NaOH solution to half-filled spray tank with water
3. Add JWA, stir thoroughly
4. Top up with water to 500L
5. Filter through fine net
6. Use same day

## SAFETY WARNINGS

- **Do not let contact skin when spraying** -- NaOH is highly corrosive
- **Wear protective gloves** when handling caustic soda
- Wear eye protection, long sleeves, and waterproof boots
- Wash thoroughly with water if skin contact occurs
- NaOH can be added to any JNP for increased strength -- always do concentration test first
- Never touch caustic soda with bare hands

## Application Timing

- **Best time**: evening or after rain when slugs/snails are active
- Slugs and snails are nocturnal -- spray in late afternoon/evening for maximum contact
- Moist conditions are ideal (slugs are more active, JNP stays wet longer)
- Re-apply after heavy rain (rain washes away residue)

## Application Method

- Spray thoroughly on ground surface around plants, under leaves, near plant bases
- Focus on areas where slugs hide: under mulch, near debris, along borders
- Direct spray on visible slugs/snails for immediate contact kill
- Ensure good soil drench in problem areas

## Cultural Controls Alongside JADAM

Integrate with JNP for best results:
- **Remove hiding places**: clear debris, reduce thick mulch in problem areas
- **Beer traps**: shallow dishes of beer attract and drown slugs (supplementary)
- **Copper barriers**: copper tape around beds deters slugs (they get electric shock)
- **[[comparison-neem-oil-cannabis-vs-diatomaceous-earth-cannabis]]**: sharp particles damage slug soft bodies (supplementary)
- **Hand-picking**: early morning or evening patrols, drop in soapy water
- **Reduce moisture**: improve drainage, avoid overhead watering at night
- **Encourage predators**: [[ground-beetles-carabidae-and-biological-pest-control]], ducks, frogs, toads

## Pests Controlled

Slugs (Acusta despecta and others), snails, and various soft-bodied molluscs. Also effective on some mites and aphids when combined with JHS.

## NaOH as General Strengthener

Sodium hydroxide can be added to any JNP formulation for increased pesticidal strength. However:
- Always perform concentration test first
- Start with low amounts and increase gradually
- Some crops are sensitive -- test on small area
- The NaOH + JWA combination is already quite strong on its own
