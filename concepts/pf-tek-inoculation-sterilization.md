## Inoculation Without Lids (Drinking Glasses)

For growers using 1/2 pint tapered drinking glasses instead of canning
jars (covered with tin foil rather than pierced lids), a modified
technique is used. This method is less reliable than the lid method and
should only be attempted after successfully inoculating with the standard
punched-lid technique:

1. Remove the tin foil cover from the glass.
2. Tilt the glass at approximately 30 degrees.
3. Insert the needle and inoculate spore solution down the inside wall
   of the glass, targeting the substrate surface.
4. The critical precaution is to disturb the dry top vermiculite
   contamination barrier as little as possible.
5. Replace the tin foil cover immediately after inoculation.
