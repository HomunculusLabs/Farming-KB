# PF TEK Inoculation and Sterilization

## Overview

Successful inoculation in the PF TEK requires careful [[cultivator-sterile-technique-and-agar-culture]]
to prevent contamination of the substrate jars. The process involves
introducing spore solution from a syringe into the prepared substrate
jars through pre-punched lid holes. [[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]] emphasized that
this stage is where most cultivation failures occur, primarily due to
breakdown in sterile procedure. The technique was designed to be
accessible using common household materials while maintaining adequate
[[mushroom-contamination-control]].

## Pre-Inoculation Checklist

Before beginning inoculation, ensure:

1. Jars have fully cooled to room temperature (cool to the touch).
  Introducing spores into hot jars will kill them.
2. The work area is as clean as possible. A still-air environment
  (closed room, no fans or drafts) significantly reduces contamination.
3. The [[comparison-mushroom-liquid-culture-vs-spore-syringe]] has been recently shaken to redistribute spores
  evenly in the water.
4. Hands and surfaces have been cleaned with alcohol.
5. The syringe needle is sterile and the guard has been removed.

## Inoculation Procedure with Canning Jars

1. Verify the [[carbon-dioxide-retention-jar-lid-mushroom-cultivation]] is tight and the jar is cool.
2. Shake the spore syringe well to redistribute spores.
3. Remove the tape covering the needle holes on the jar lid.
4. Remove the needle guard from the syringe.
5. Insert the needle through one of the lid holes.
6. Tilt the syringe body back toward the center of the lid with the
   needle tip touching the inside glass. This angle distributes the
   spore solution down the side of the jar for good inoculation coverage.
7. Press the plunger gently. As soon as water appears around the
   needle tip, release plunger pressure. Do not over-inoculate.
8. Inoculate a few drops down each of the four needle holes.
9. Between each hole, shake the syringe slightly to keep spores
   distributed in solution.
10. Use approximately 1 cc (1 ml) of spore solution per jar, allowing
    one 10 cc syringe to inoculate 10 jars.

## Syringe Needle Plugging

If the syringe needle plugs as it is inserted into the substrate, draw
the needle back slightly — this will typically unplug it. Needle plugging
occurs when vermiculite particles are drawn into the needle tip. Using
less than the full insertion depth can help prevent this issue.

## Alcohol Flaming Technique

If the syringe needle touches any non-sterile surface, it must be
flamed to resterilize before continuing. An alcohol flame is preferred
over a butane cigarette lighter because it produces a clean blue flame
without the soot residue left by butane:

1. Place a tequila shot glass upside down.
2. Using an eyedropper, put a few drops of denatured [[permaculture-on-farm-alcohol-fuel-production]]
   (available at hardware stores) on the hollow bottom of the glass.
3. Touch the alcohol with a match or lighter to produce a short,
   clean-burning blue flame.
4. Heat the needle in the flame for a few seconds.
5. Small "pops" of boiling water may spurt from the needle, but the
   spores within the syringe body remain safe from the brief heat
   exposure.

## Inoculation Without Lids (Drinking Glasses)

For growers using 1/2 pint tapered drinking glasses instead of canning
jars (covered with tin foil rather than pierced lids), a modified
technique is used. This method is less reliable than the lid method and
should only be attempted after successfully inoculating with the standard
punched-lid technique:

1. Remove the tin foil cover from the glass.
2. Tilt the glass at approximately 30 degrees.
3. Insert the needle and inoculate spore solution down the inside wall
   of the glass, targeting the substrate surface.
4. The critical precaution is to disturb the dry top vermiculite
   contamination barrier as little as possible.
5. Replace the tin foil cover immediately after inoculation.
