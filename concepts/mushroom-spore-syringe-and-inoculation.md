### Method 1: Paper Print
1. Select a mature mushroom with fully opened cap and darkening gills
2. Cut the stem close to the cap with a sterilized knife
3. Place cap gill-side down on clean white paper
4. Cover with a bowl to retard desiccation
5. Leave for 24 hours in a draft-free area
6. Remove cap -- a dark purple-brown radial deposit of spores will remain
7. Fold paper and seal in an envelope

### Method 2: Sterile Glass Jar Print (PF Method)
1. Sterilize a 1/2 pint wide-mouth canning jar and lid in a toaster oven at ~300F for 30 minutes (lid loose)
2. Cool, tighten lid until use
3. Wash hands; sterilize work surfaces with rubbing alcohol
4. Flame-sterilize cuticle scissors with an alcohol flame
5. Snip the cap from the stem, cutting as high into the cap as possible
6. Place cap gill-side down on the jar bottom
7. Cover loosely, leave 1-2 days in a draft-free area
8. Remove cap with minimal air disturbance
9. Let jar dehumidify for a few days, then seal with tape
10. Store at room temperature in a dark place (not refrigerator)

### Enhanced Sterility Method
Place four alcohol-swabbed and flamed microscope slides side by side. Place the cap centered across them. After 24 hours, each slide will have a spore deposit. Seal slides individually in plastic.

## Spore Print Storage

- Store at room temperature, dark, dry location
- Do not refrigerate (condensation risk)
- P. cubensis spores begin degrading after a few months
- Germination is "greatly reduced or won't occur at all" after approximately 1.5 years
- Fresh spores germinate massively and quickly

## Spore Syringe Preparation

### Materials
- Spore print in sterile jar
- Sterile syringe with sterile water (for injection)
- Sterile syringe for loading spore solution
- Pyrex glass stirring rod
- Alcohol, tequila shot glass, eyedropper
- Two-hole lid (one for stirring rod, one for syringe needle)

### Syringe Sterilization
1. Boil a pot of water
2. Draw boiling water into syringe, squirt out, repeat several times
3. Fill with boiling water, replace needle guard, wrap in foil
4. Drop syringes into boiling water, boil for 1 hour
5. Cool before use

### Loading Procedure
1. Inject sterile water into the spore print jar through the needle hole
2. Flame-sterilize the glass stirring rod, let cool
3. Insert rod through center lid hole, scrape spores into water
4. Insert sterile syringe through the second lid hole
5. Tilt jar until water reaches needle tip, draw spore solution into syringe
6. Protect holes with foil or surgical tape before and after

### Storage
- Store at cool temperatures in the dark
- Properly prepared syringes remain viable for several months to over a year
