## Taxonomic Background

Psilocybe semilanceata was first described by Elias Fries in the early 19th
century (Sowerby, 1797-1809; Cooke, 1881-1906). The species belongs to
Section Semilanceatae within the genus Psilocybe, following Guzmán's
classification system (Guzmán, 1983, 1995). It is characterized by its
distinctive conical to bell-shaped pileus with a prominent papilla, its
reliance on grassland habitats, and its strong [[field-guide-psilocybin-bluing-reaction-identification]] when bruised.
