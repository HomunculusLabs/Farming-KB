The Ustilaginomycetes (smut fungi) are a major class within the
phylum [[bloomfield-fungal-life-cycle-spiral-versus-circle]]. Teliospores are the defining feature of the Ustilaginomycetes.
These diploid resting spores are typically darkly pigmented,
thick-walled, and capable of surviving adverse environmental
conditions for extended periods. Upon germination, teliospores
undergo meiosis to produce a promycelium (basidium) bearing
haploid basidiospores (sporidia). The sporidia may germinate
to produce a yeastlike saprobic phase or, upon encountering a
suitable host plant, fuse to form a dikaryotic mycelium that
invades host tissues.
