## Biology of Cannabis Pollen

Cannabis is a wind-pollinated species. Pollen grains are nearly spherical,
slightly yellow, and 25 to 30 microns in diameter, with a smooth surface
exhibiting 2 to 4 germ pores. Just prior to dehiscence (pollen release),

## Pollen Collection Methods

### Direct Collection

If the propagator has an isolated area where staminate plants can grow
separate from each other to avoid mutual contamination, direct collection
may be used. A small vial, glass plate, or mirror is held beneath a
recently opened staminate flower that appears to be releasing pollen, and
the pollen is dislodged by tapping the anthers gently.

### Drying and Scraping

Pollen may also be collected by placing whole limbs or clusters of
staminate flowers on a piece of paper or glass and allowing them to dry
in a cool, still place. Pollen will drop from some of the anthers as they
dry, and this may be scraped up and stored.

### Desiccant Storage Method

A simple method involves placing the open pollen vial or folded paper in a
larger sealable container with fresh, dry soda crackers or a cup of dry
white rice as desiccant. The sealed container is stored in the
refrigerator, extending pollen viability by absorbing moisture.

### Pre-Opening Collection

Staminate flowers often open several hours before pollen release. Flowers
collected at this pre-opening stage can be placed in a covered bottle
where they will release pollen within two days. A paper cover allows air
circulation while preventing mold.

## Timing and Conditions for Collection

- Early morning is the best time to collect pollen, as it has not been
  exposed to the heat of the day
- All equipment must be cleaned before continuing to the next source
- If pollen becomes moist, it will germinate and spoil; dry storage is
  imperative
