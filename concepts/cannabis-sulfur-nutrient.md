# Cannabis Sulfur (S)

## Overview

Sulfur is a [[cannabis-calcium-nutrient]] in an "A" container and a "B" container. If combined in a concentrated form, sulfur and calcium will form crude, insoluble gypsum (calcium sulfate) and settle as residue to the bottom of the tank. Always dilute nutrient concentrates before combining the A and B solutions.
