# Haze Genetics and Lineage

The Haze lineage [[haze-genetics]] is one of the most important and influential family trees in modern
[[cannabis-breeding-basics]] [[haze-genetics]]. Originating in Northern California in the 1970s, Haze and its
countless descendants have shaped the genetic landscape of sativa-dominant cannabis
worldwide. Understanding Haze genetics is essential for anyone studying cannabis
[[haze-cannabis-lineage-and-breeding-history]] or selecting sativa strains for cultivation.

## Origins of the Original Haze

The original Haze was created in the Santa Cruz, California area in the early to
mid-1970s by a group of brothers known as the "Haze Brothers." The exact pedigree
has been debated, but the most widely accepted version is that Haze was created by
crossing several [[landrace-sativa-varieties]]: a Colombian Gold (or Colombian sativa),
a Thai sativa, a Mexican (possibly Oaxacan) sativa, and possibly a South Indian or
other South Asian sativa. The cross involved multiple generations of selection and
inbreeding over several years to stabilize the desirable characteristics.

The Haze Brothers were working with some of the most potent [[cannabis-genetics-and-breeding]]
available in the 1970s — an era before the widespread introduction of indica
genetics from Afghanistan that would later dominate commercial breeding. Their goal
was to create the most potent sativa possible, and by most accounts they succeeded.
The original Haze developed a reputation as one of the most powerful and
psychoactively intense cannabis varieties ever created.

## Characteristics of Original Haze

Original Haze is a pure or near-pure sativa with extreme characteristics in almost
every dimension. Plants are very tall with long internodal spacing, thin leaflets,
and extensive branching. Flowering time is extremely long — 14-16 weeks or more,
with some phenotypes reportedly requiring up to 20 weeks. The aroma is complex,
often described as spicy, peppery, and incense-like with earthy undertones. The
effect is profoundly cerebral and can be overwhelmingly psychoactive for
inexperienced users, with many reports of visual and auditory enhancement, creative
inspiration, and in some cases borderline hallucinogenic effects.

The long flowering time and tall growth made original Haze impractical for most
commercial operations, which is why it was primarily preserved by dedicated
collectors and breeders rather than grown widely. However, its extraordinary
genetic qualities made it an irresistible parent for breeding programs seeking to
introduce potency, sativa characteristics, and complex [[cannabis-terpene-profiles]] into more
manageable hybrid lines.

## Key Haze Descendants in This Guide

### Amnesia Haze

Amnesia Haze is one of the most popular [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] in the world and a direct
descendant of the Haze lineage. The Royal Queen Seeds version documented here
combines Amnesia, Skunk, and Haze genetics into a sativa-dominant hybrid that
retains much of the Haze effect profile while flowering in approximately 12 weeks
and producing yields of up to 600 grams per square yard indoors. Amnesia Haze
typically tests at 19% THC and produces a "hefty, cerebral high" that can cause
genuine amnesia in high doses.

### Amnesika 2.0

From Spain's Philosopher Seeds, Amnesika 2.0 combines [[cannabis-strain-temple-bodhi-seeds-super-silver-haze-oner]], G-13
Haze, and Skunk genetics. The inclusion of both Super Silver Haze and G-13 Haze
means this strain has a triple dose of Haze lineage from different breeding
directions. It finishes in 65 days of flowering with THC levels of 16-18%, making
it a more manageable expression of Haze genetics.

### Super Lemon Haze

Super Lemon Haze (Green House Seed Co.) is a cross of Super Silver Haze with
Lemon Skunk, representing another branch of the Haze family tree. Super Silver
Haze itself is a cross of Haze with [[cannabis-strain-mango-haze-northern-lights-skunk-haze]] and Skunk #1, making Super
Lemon Haze a grandchild of the original Haze. This lineage demonstrates how Haze
genetics were combined with [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-sativa-indica-and-hybrid-cannabis]] parents to create faster-finishing,
higher-yielding plants while retaining sativa characteristics.

### Tom Hill's Original Haze

Tom Hill's Original Haze represents one of the most carefully preserved direct
lines to the original 1970s Haze. Tom Hill, a breeder known for his work with
[[heritage-and-landrace-crop-varieties-for-small-farmers]] crop varieties for small farmers genetics, maintained and stabilized an authentic Haze line
that retains the full expression of the original — including the extreme flowering
time, towering height, and uncompromising psychoactive power. This strain is
primarily of interest to preservationists, breeders, and serious connoisseurs.
