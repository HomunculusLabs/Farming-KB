## Origins of the Original Haze

The original Haze was created in the Santa Cruz, California area in the early to
mid-1970s by a group of brothers known as the "Haze Brothers." The exact pedigree
has been debated, but the most widely accepted version is that Haze was created by
crossing several [[cannabis-genetics-and-breeding]]
available in the 1970s — an era before the widespread introduction of indica
genetics from Afghanistan that would later dominate commercial breeding. Their goal
was to create the most potent sativa possible, and by most accounts they succeeded.
The original Haze developed a reputation as one of the most powerful and
psychoactively intense cannabis varieties ever created.

## Characteristics of Original Haze

Original Haze is a pure or near-pure sativa with extreme characteristics in almost
every dimension. Plants are very tall with long internodal spacing, thin leaflets,
and extensive branching. Flowering time is extremely long — 14-16 weeks or more,
with some phenotypes reportedly requiring up to 20 weeks. The aroma is complex,
often described as spicy, peppery, and incense-like with earthy undertones. The
effect is profoundly cerebral and can be overwhelmingly psychoactive for
inexperienced users, with many reports of visual and auditory enhancement, creative
inspiration, and in some cases borderline hallucinogenic effects.

The long flowering time and tall growth made original Haze impractical for most
commercial operations, which is why it was primarily preserved by dedicated
collectors and breeders rather than grown widely. However, its extraordinary
genetic qualities made it an irresistible parent for breeding programs seeking to
introduce potency, sativa characteristics, and complex [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] in the world and a direct
descendant of the Haze lineage. The Royal Queen Seeds version documented here
combines Amnesia, Skunk, and Haze genetics into a sativa-dominant hybrid that
retains much of the Haze effect profile while flowering in approximately 12 weeks
and producing yields of up to 600 grams per square yard indoors. Amnesia Haze
typically tests at 19% THC and produces a "hefty, cerebral high" that can cause
genuine amnesia in high doses.
