## Organic Soil Enrichment — Compost, Manure, and Green Manures

## Overview

Organic soil enrichment is the foundation of organic gardening. Rather than applying synthetic chemical fertilizers, organic gardeners build soil fertility through natural methods: [[composting]], animal manures, green manures, and crop rotation. These methods feed the soil ecosystem (microorganisms, earthworms, fungi) which in turn nourish plants.

## The Problem with Chemical Fertilizers

- Plants need certain nutrient levels for healthy growth.
- Chemical fertilizers are poured on year after year to maintain these levels.
- Result: plants are filled with "alien chemicals" and waterways become polluted.
- Traditional crop rotation and mixing practices have been abandoned for short-term profit.
- Pests and diseases build up to uncontrollable proportions without rotation.
- More powerful chemicals are needed as resistant strains develop.
- Chemicals previously thought safe (e.g., DDT) are regularly banned.

## Composting

[[compost]] is decomposed organic matter that returns nutrients to the soil in a plant-available form. It improves soil structure, water retention, and microbial activity.

## Animal Manures

Various animal manures provide different nutrient profiles. All should be well-rotted before application to avoid burning plants and to kill weed seeds and pathogens.

## Green Manures

Green manures are [[cover-crops]] grown specifically to be dug back into the soil:
- Add organic matter.
- Fix nitrogen (legumes).
- Prevent soil erosion.
- Suppress weeds.

## Soil Types and Management

Different soil types require different management approaches:
- **Clay soils**: Difficult to work; sticky when wet, hard when dry. Improve with organic matter.
- **Sandy soils**: Free-draining but poor at holding nutrients. Improve with organic matter.
- **Loam**: Ideal balance of sand, silt, and clay. Easy to cultivate.

## Key Principle

"Feed the soil, not the plant." Healthy soil with active microbial life will provide plants with all the nutrients they need in the right forms and at the right times.
