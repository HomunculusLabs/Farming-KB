## The Hierarchy of Aggregate Formation

Tisdall and Oades (1982) proposed the hierarchical model of
aggregate formation, which remains widely accepted. According
to this model, different binding agents operate at different
scales. Persistent binding agents (plant roots and fungal
hyphae, humic substances) stabilize macroaggregates (>2 mm).

activity) stabilize macroaggregates at smaller scales. Temporary
