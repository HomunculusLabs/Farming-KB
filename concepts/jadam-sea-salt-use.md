# JADAM Sea Salt Use

Sea water and sea salt are fundamental mineral supplements in the JADAM system. Sea water contains 83 different minerals including S, Mg, Ca, K, B, P, I, Mn, Mo, Co, Se, and Ge -- nearly all essential elements for crop growth.

## Why Sea Water

Sea water contains approximately 100 million marine microorganisms per mL. When combined with JADAM microorganism solution (JMS) cultured from leaf mold, you essentially combine all the diversity Earth has to offer: microorganisms from land and from sea. Foliar application of JMS mixed with sea water eliminates powdery mildew.

## Mineral Replenishment

Modern farming causes continuous mineral leaching from soil. JADAM's approach is not to try to identify which specific mineral is deficient, but to recover the diversity and balance of minerals holistically -- similar to eating a wholesome diet rather than taking individual vitamin pills. Sea water with its 83 minerals, combined with phyllite rock powder, ensures soil never lacks mineral diversity.

## Dilution Rate

Sea water is about 3% salt, while plant body fluid is about 1%. You must dilute sea water with 30 to 100 times more plain water to prevent salt damage and Na/Cl accumulation in soil.

- JADAM recommends: 20 L (5.28 gal) of sea water, or 600 g (1.3 lb) of sea salt, per 0.1 ha (0.25 ac)
- If you cannot obtain sea water, use sea salt: 20 L of sea water equals approximately 500 g of salt
- Sea water salinity differs by region, normally 3.1-3.8%

## How to Apply

Mix sea water with JMS and continue applying 3-4 times per month. Apply every time you water. Use together with phyllite solution for maximum effect.

## Benefits

Using sea water:
- Supplements minerals leached from soil
- Improves sweetness, coloring, storage period, and product value
- Combined with JMS, controls powdery mildew
- Supports microbial diversity in soil

## Critical Warnings

- Do NOT apply sea water diluted with less than 30 times water -- salinity will be too strong and can damage crops
- Do NOT sprinkle salt directly onto soil
- Do NOT spray undiluted sea water
- "Good and bad are one. If a good were excessive, it will turn bad."

## Historical Evidence

Fields, rice paddies, and orchards near the sea have been showered with sea water elements for hundreds and thousands of years. They consistently produce better quality food than inland farms. Even the Korean National Institute of Agricultural Sciences has confirmed the benefits of sea water and sea salt.

## Combination with Phyllite

JADAM uses sea water together with phyllite solution (fine-powdered phyllite rock). Add 60 kg of phyllite powder to 500 L of water, stir, let settle, and use the upper water. Phyllite contains almost all inorganic nutrients required by plants. Together with sea water, the soil will never be richer in minerals.
