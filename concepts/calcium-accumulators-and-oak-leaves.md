## Calcium Accumulators and Oak Leaves

Calcium is an essential plant nutrient that plays critical roles in cell
wall structure, membrane integrity, root development, and enzyme
activation. While many gardeners address calcium deficiencies by adding
lime or gypsum, certain plants can serve as biological calcium
accumulators, concentrating this mineral in their tissues from soils
where calcium availability is low. Oak trees are among the most notable
calcium accumulators, and their leaves offer a valuable, often
overlooked resource for soil building.
