## Distribution and Habitat

### Primary Range

The species is concentrated in the Pacific Northwest, specifically:

- **Washington State:** Most common, particularly in the Seattle area
- **British Columbia, Canada:** Present in the Vancouver area
- **Oregon:** Western portions of the state

### Habitat Preferences

Psilocybe stuntzii is remarkably adaptable:

- Bark mulch in landscaped areas
- Lawns in parks and gardens
- Golf courses
- Football fields
- Garden beds

The species' ability to colonize bark mulch is particularly notable, as this is a relatively uncommon substrate for psychoactive mushrooms. This adaptability may explain its rapid spread across the Pacific Northwest after initial introduction.

### Fruiting Season

Under favorable conditions, Psilocybe stuntzii can fruit year-round, making it unique among Pacific Northwest psychoactive species. Most other species in the region fruit only during fall and early winter, ceasing when temperatures drop below freezing.
