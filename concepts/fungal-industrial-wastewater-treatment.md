### Silage Wastewater

Silage effluent is problematic due to high organic load, acidity, and regulatory constraints. Fungal treatment approaches include:
- On-farm treatment systems
- Fungal biomass production
- Compliance with [[fungi-in-composting-and-agricultural-waste-conversion]] legislation

### Acidogenic Wastewater

Highly acidic waste streams can be treated by acid-tolerant fungi using bioreactor systems with appropriate modeling for process optimization.

### Olive Mill Wastewater

Olive mill effluent is among the most challenging industrial wastewaters, containing phenolic compounds, high organic load, and antimicrobial constituents. Treatment approaches include:
- Fungal fermentation with species selection for phenolic tolerance
- Mathematical modeling of treatment processes
- [[singh-immobilized-fungal-bioreactors-wastewater-treatment]] cell systems
- Enzyme treatment approaches
- Toxicity testing protocols
- Significant economic importance in Mediterranean regions

## Bioreactor Types for Fungal Wastewater Treatment

Multiple bioreactor configurations are employed for fungal wastewater treatment:

- **Batch stirred-tank reactors**: Simple operation, suitable for small-scale treatment
- **Continuous-flow stirred-tank reactors**: Steady-state operation for consistent effluent quality
- **Fed-batch bioreactors**: Controlled substrate addition for improved treatment efficiency
- **Bubble column bioreactors**: Good gas-liquid mass transfer for aerobic fungi
- **Fluidized-bed bioreactors**: High surface area for biofilm attachment
- **Immobilized bioreactors**: Enhanced fungal retention and stability
- **Membrane bioreactors**: Cell retention with effluent separation
