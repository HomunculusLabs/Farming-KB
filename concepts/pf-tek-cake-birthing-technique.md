## PF TEK Cake Birthing Technique

## Overview

The "birthing" process is the critical transition from the colonization phase inside the jar to the fruiting phase inside the dual [[gadd-mycelial-network-dynamics]] and any primordia that have begun forming. Proper birthing minimizes damage, prevents contamination, and sets the stage for productive fruiting.

## Optimal Timing for Birthing

The ideal moment to birth a fungus cake is when primordia become visible while the cake is still inside the jar. Primordia appear as tiny worm like structures with small reddish heads growing on the surface of the colonized cake. This timing ensures:

- The mycelium has fully colonized the substrate
- The fungus has committed to the reproductive cycle
- The transition to the humid terrarium environment will trigger immediate fruiting
- No development time is lost waiting for pinning to begin in the terrarium

Birthing before primordia appear is possible but may delay the onset of fruiting. Waiting too long after primordia form risks damaging the developing structures during handling.

## Jar Requirements for Successful Birthing

The PF TEK requires jars with tapered sides and no shoulders. This design is specifically intended to facilitate cake removal. Suitable containers include:

- Kerr wide mouth [[pf-tek-canning-jar-lid-configuration]] (most versatile)
- Ball regular mouth half pint canning jar
- Ball half pint jelly jar
- 1/2 pint (250 ml) capacity drinking glasses with tapered sides

Jars larger than 1/2 pint are unreliable for the PF TEK and fail easily. The tapered design ensures the cake can slide out freely without mechanical assistance that could damage the mycelium.

### Step 4: Invert and Strike

Turn the jar upside down with the old lid held against the jar mouth. Lightly slam the jar down onto a table surface that has been cushioned with a magazine or similar padding. The fungus cake will slide out of the jar and land on the old jar cap, which now functions as the cake's base.

### Step 5: Inspect and Daub

As soon as the fungus cake emerges from the jar, inspect it for water droplets that may have deposited on the surface during ejection. Daub the cake gently with a piece of loose tissue paper to absorb any surface moisture. Excess moisture on the cake surface can promote bacterial growth in the terrarium.

## The Aroma Indicator

When the fungus cake is birthed, a distinctly mushroomy and very pleasant aroma is released. This aroma is a positive indicator of healthy colonization. Any sour, rancid, or otherwise unpleasant odor suggests bacterial contamination within the cake, and the affected cake should be discarded.

## Post Birthing: First Growth Forms

After the cake is placed in the terrarium, several types of growth forms may appear:

### Aborts

Some of the first mushrooms to form are abhorts, characterized by convoluted caps, gnarly stems, and stunted growth. These are primo in magic alkaloids despite their unusual appearance. The tiny baby mushroom abhorts are likewise highly potent. Aborts should be picked before their heads turn black, using a pointed knife blade for clean removal.
