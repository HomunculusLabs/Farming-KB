## Implications for Consumption and Preparation

Practical relevance for use and preparation:

- **Whole-body consumption is ideal** — Caps and stems carry
  different alkaloid profiles; whole fruiting bodies deliver the
  full compound spectrum.
- **Cap-only preparation reduces yield** — Discarding stems loses
  all [[psilocybin]] by roughly 35-40%.
- **Dosage remains unpredictable** — Strain-dependent partitioning
  plus fourfold overall variation means predicting effects from
  appearance or preparation alone is not feasible.
