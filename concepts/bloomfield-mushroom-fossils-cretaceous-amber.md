## Exquisitely Preserved Cretaceous Mushrooms

Among the most remarkable fungal fossils ever discovered are
exquisitely preserved mushrooms found in Cretaceous amber from New
Jersey. These tiny fruiting bodies were immortalized when a glob of
tree resin suffocated the fungus and preserved an instant in
geological history. The amber specimens are more than 90 million
years old, dating to the middle Cretaceous period when dinosaurs
still dominated terrestrial ecosystems and pterosaurs wheeled in the
sky above.

Despite their extreme age, the Cretaceous amber mushrooms are so
similar in appearance to certain living species that they are
instantly recognizable to a trained mycologist. They look almost
identical to modern mushrooms, suggesting that the basic mushroom
body plan has remained remarkably stable over tens of millions of
years. This morphological conservatism is striking when compared
with the dramatic evolutionary transformations seen in many animal
and plant lineages over the same time interval.
