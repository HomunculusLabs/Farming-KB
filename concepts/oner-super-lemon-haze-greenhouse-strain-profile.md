# Super Lemon Haze — Strain Profile

## Overview

Super Lemon Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Amsterdam's Green House Seed Co., widely regarded as one of the most commercially successful and recognizable cannabis strains of the modern era. Derived from a cross between Lemon Skunk and Super Silver Haze, this strain delivers an intensely lemony aroma, energetic cerebral effects, and reliable yields that have made it a staple in both recreational and medical markets worldwide. With a THC content of approximately 19.33% and CBD at 0.21%, Super Lemon Haze represents a quintessential example of Dutch breeding expertise applied to Haze genetics.

### Parent Strain Analysis

**Lemon Skunk** contributes the dominant citrus terpene profile — the sharp, sweet lemon aroma that defines this strain. Lemon Skunk itself is a stable, reliable line that passes on its distinctive flavor and moderate growth characteristics.

**Super Silver Haze** brings the legendary Haze lineage — contributing cerebral potency, creative stimulation, and the complex "Hazey" undertones that complement the upfront lemon character. Super Silver Haze also adds structural vigor and yield potential.

## Growth Characteristics

### Indoor Cultivation

Super Lemon Haze is classified as a **medium-height plant**, making it accessible to indoor growers who might otherwise avoid sativa-dominant strains. Key cultivation considerations:

- **Height management** — plants can reach 6+ feet if untrained; LST or ScrOG recommended
- **Branching** — long, wide branches respond well to training and manipulation
- **Structural support** — towards end of flowering, colas become heavy; stake during vegetative stage
- **Resilience** — deals well with rookie errors, making it forgiving for less experienced growers
- **Flowering period** — 10 weeks, reasonable for a Haze-dominant strain

### Yield Expectations

- **Indoor**: approximately 800 grams per square yard under standard lighting
- **Outdoor**: up to 1200 grams per individual plant in ideal conditions

The high outdoor yields make this strain particularly attractive to outdoor growers in warm climates.

### Outdoor Cultivation

Outdoor growers, especially those in warmer regions (Australia, Africa, Central America, California), can achieve outstanding results. The mid-October harvest date provides flexibility for various latitudes. However, the strain is noted as being **fairly sensitive** to environmental stress — extra care against inclement weather, pests, and mold is warranted. Outdoor yields can reach up to 1200 grams per plant, making it one of the heavier-yielding sativa-dominant options available.

### Environmental Preferences

- **Climate** — thrives in Mediterranean and warm temperate conditions
- **Humidity** — moderate; avoid excessive humidity during late flowering
- **Light** — responds dramatically to increased light intensity
- **Nutrients** — moderate feeder; standard organic or synthetic regimens work well

## Aroma, Flavor, and Appearance

### Terpene Profile

The defining characteristic of Super Lemon Haze is its **intense lemony smell** that is immediately recognizable. The aroma is described as:

- Sharp, bright citrus (lemon peel and lemon zest)
- Sweet undertones with a slightly skunky edge
- Hazey aftertaste that becomes apparent on the exhale

### Flavor Experience

When combusted or vaporized, the flavor mirrors the aroma with exceptional clarity:

- The lemon flavor "cuts through" immediately, coating the palate
- A sweeter, almost candied citrus quality develops
- The Haze influence appears as a subtle, complex aftertaste
- Clean finish without harshness

### Visual Characteristics

- Dense, well-formed buds with excellent bag appeal
- Bright green coloring with orange pistils
- Heavy trichome coverage giving a frosty, crystalline appearance
- Colas are substantial and resinous

## Effects and Experience

Super Lemon Haze delivers a **balanced yet predominantly cerebral high**:

- **Onset** — immediate uplifting and energizing effects
- **Cerebral** — enhanced creativity, mental clarity, and sociability
- **Physical** — gentle body relaxation without sedation
- **Duration** — moderate to long-lasting for a sativa-dominant strain
- **Social** — promotes conversation and positive mood

The balanced nature of the high — energetic yet physically relaxed — makes this strain versatile for both daytime and evening use.

## Medical Applications

- **Mood disorders** — the uplifting effects may help with depression and anxiety
- **Fatigue** — energizing properties combat daytime tiredness
- **Stress relief** — cerebral relaxation without heavy sedation
- **Social anxiety** — the sociable, talkative effects can ease social situations
- **Mild pain** — moderate body relaxation provides gentle analgesia

## Growing Tips

1. **Light intensity** — pack as much light as physically possible into the grow room for heaviest yields
2. **Training methods** — ScrOG and LST both work excellently with this strain's branching structure
3. **Staking** — install supports during vegetative stage before colas become heavy
4. **Mold prevention** — despite good resistance, maintain proper ventilation and humidity control
5. **Curing** — minimum 2 weeks recommended by the breeder to fully develop flavors
6. **Organic vs. hydro** — suitable for both organic soil and hydroponic setups

## Cultural Significance

Super Lemon Haze has become one of the most awarded and commercially important cannabis strains of the 21st century. Green House Seed Co. is described in the source as "basically the Jay-Z of the seed world," and Super Lemon Haze is one of their flagship offerings. The strain's appeal extends beyond experienced cannabis consumers — the distinctive lemon flavor makes it one of the few strains that even non-regular users can identify by taste alone.
