## How Does It Feel?

McKenna provides detailed phenomenological descriptions of the DMT
experience, drawing on both his own experiences and the reports of
hundreds of users. The experience typically unfolds in phases:

1. **Onset** (0-30 seconds): A rushing sensation, often described as
   a high-speed vibration or hum. Visual field begins to dissolve.
2. **Breakthrough** (30 seconds - 5 minutes): The most intense phase.
   Ordinary reality is completely replaced by an extraordinary
   landscape or space. Users report encountering complex geometric
   structures, alien environments, and autonomous entities.
3. **Peak** (5-15 minutes): Full immersion in the DMT space.
   Sense of self dissolves. Time perception becomes meaningless.
   Communication with entities or direct gnosis occurs.
4. **Return** (15-30 minutes): Gradual re-establishment of ordinary
   consciousness. Residual visual effects fade. Profound sense of
   having visited somewhere "real" persists.

exclusive authority over the nature of reality.

The suppression of DMT research is particularly revealing. DMT is
an endogenous human compound, present in the brain and bloodstream.
The fact that the government criminalizes investigation of a
substance that naturally occurs in the human body demonstrates,
for McKenna, that the issue is not pharmacological but ideological.
