plants to bring spacing closer to final target. This is an opportunity
to select for vigor, leaf color, and growth habit. Again, cut rather
than pull.

**Stage 3 — Final thinning** (3-5 weeks after sowing, or when plants
begin to crowd): Thin to the final intended spacing. By this point,
plants are well-established and can tolerate the root disturbance of
removing neighbors. The strongest, healthiest individuals should remain.

**Stage 4-5** (for long-season crops): Some crops benefit from a
progressive harvest-thin approach where the largest individuals are
pulled for eating as "baby" vegetables, giving remaining plants more
space to mature.
