## Carbon Crops vs. Green Manures

| Factor | Carbon Crops | Green Manures |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon production | High (full crop grown to maturity) | Low (~1/4 of carbon crop output) |
| Humus production | High | Low (decompose rapidly, may deplete humus) |
| Food production | Yes (dual-purpose) | No (land tied up during growth + 1-month decomposition) |
| Nitrogen contribution | From interplanted legumes | From legume root nodules |
| Soil building | Through compost | By digging residues into soil |
