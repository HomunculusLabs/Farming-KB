# Harmala Alkaloids and Ayahuasca Preparation

Harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine) are [[dmt-dimethyltryptamine-experience-guide]].

## Sources

### Banisteriopsis caapi ("Vine of the Soul")
- Native to the Amazon region
- Active ingredient of ayahuasca/yage brews
- Bark contains 0.2-1.3% mixed Harmala alkaloids
- Normally procured in the Amazon jungle; occasionally sold by specialty herb dealers

### Peganum harmala (Syrian Rue)
- Most concentrated source: seeds contain 2-7% mixed Harmala alkaloids
- Grows in Africa, Middle East, India, South America, Mexico, and southern US
- Hardy plant that can take over other vegetation (prohibited in California as "noxious weed")
- Can be picked wild or procured from specialty seed/herb dealers
- Most potent and readily available source of Harmala

## Dosage and Preparation

### Syrian Rue
- **Potentiating dose**: Approximately 3 grams of seed (1.25 teaspoons finely ground)
- Acts as a "switch" — larger doses increase nausea but not potentiating effect
- 3 grams sufficient to potentiate without excessive stomach discomfort

**Methods:**
1. **Capsules**: Grind seeds in coffee grinder, pack into gelatin capsules. Takes ~2 hours for effect.
2. **Tea**: Boil powder for a few minutes. Takes ~1 hour but tastes bitter.
3. **Spread consumption**: Consume over an hour to further reduce stomach disturbances.
4. **Extract**: Vodka solvent extraction. Can be smoked (nice high, comes on in minutes) or ingested. Produces less nausea for same intensity.

## The Harmala Experience Alone

Intensity: 1-2 (mild). Not potent psychedelics when taken alone.

- Feelings of subtle energy flowing and slight perception enhancement
- Smoked concentrated Harmala: more mind/less body, often euphoric and relaxed
- "A nice alternative to a cannabis high, more meditative and relaxed"
- Some obtain full psychedelic effects by consuming small amounts over a day to circumvent nausea
