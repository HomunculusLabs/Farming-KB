## Classification of Mycoreactors

Mycoreactors fall into two principal categories based on growth
configuration:

1. **Biofilm or immobilized systems**: Fungi are attached to or entrapped
   within a solid support matrix, providing high cell density and easy
   biomass-liquid separation.

2. **Suspended growth systems**: Fungal mycelium grows freely in the
   liquid phase, requiring subsequent separation steps.

Within these categories, numerous specific configurations exist for
pollutant removal applications.
