## Alternative Media Formulations

### MEA (Malt Extract Agar) -- Solid Substrate

- 20 g malt extract, 20 g agar (added slowly to prevent boil-over), 100 mg
  potassium phosphate dibasic (K2HPO4), 100 mg calcium carbonate per liter
  of distilled water.
- Used for stock cultures and species that perform better on malt-based
  media. Agar must be added carefully to prevent boiling over.
- Poured hot into culture dishes, filled halfway.

### PDA (Potato Dextrose Yeast Agar) -- Solid Substrate

- Same potato extraction as PDY, plus 15 g agar, 10 g dextrose, 1.5 g
  yeast extract per liter.
- Used for stock culture maintenance and spore germination on agar.

## Rye Grain Substrate

Several species are noted as growing on sterilized rye medium:
- [[psilocybe-caerulescens]]: grows on sterilized rye but it is almost
  impossible to produce carpophores on rye alone.

Rye is mentioned as a substrate option but is not the primary medium in
Gottlieb's liquid culture system. The guide is geared toward broth
extraction rather than grain-to-bulk fruiting methods.

## Bulk Substrate References by Species

### Psilocybe cubensis

- Wild substrate: cow dung, horse dung, rich pasture soil, straw, and
  sawdust/dung mixtures.
- Cultivation substrate: vegetable compost in clay pots, inoculated with
  agar-grown mycelium.
- Casing layer: silica sand/limestone mix.
- Incubation: 4-6 weeks in daylight at 68 degrees F.
- Carpophores larger than wild specimens can be produced this way.
- Does poorly in darkness.
- Very resistant to contaminants.
- Optimum growth on MEA: 86 degrees F. Thermal death: 104 degrees F.

### Copelandia cyanescens

- Habitat: cow dung or rich soil in Florida and other southern states.
- Spores germinate easily on all agar media.
- Optimum growth on MEA: 80 degrees F.
- Carpophores produced on uncased compost or on rye.
