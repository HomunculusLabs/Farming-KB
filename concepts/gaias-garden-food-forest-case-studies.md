## Jerome Osentowski's Mountain Food Forest (Colorado)

Jerome Osentowski's garden at 7,400 feet in Aspen,
Colorado, demonstrates that food forests can
thrive even in harsh mountain climates where
frosts can occur during any month.

### The Transition from Annuals to Perennials

After years of growing organic salad greens for
upscale Aspen markets, Jerome was ready for a
change. The food forest was a natural evolution:
"After years of growing annual vegetables, a lot
of fertility had leached down to where the short
roots of salad greens couldn't get it. So I went
to fruit trees, with their deep roots, to get at
all those nutrients."

### The Companion Plant System

Jerome calls his guild companion-plants
"companieros." The rich array includes:
- **Nitrogen fixers**: Pea shrubs, fava beans,
  clovers, fenugreek, alfalfa
- **Bee plants**: Borage and comfrey
- **Insectary species**: Fennel, celery, dill,
  coriander
- **Pest confusers**: Horseradish, Mexican
  marigold, garlic mustard, walking onions

### Species Palette

The mountain food forest includes:
- **Trees**: Apples (multi-grafted), apricots,
  plums, Douglas firs, New Mexico locusts
