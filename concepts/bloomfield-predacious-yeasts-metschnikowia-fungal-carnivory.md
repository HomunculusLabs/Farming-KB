## Named for a Pioneer

The genus Metschnikowia is named after Elie Metchnikoff, the Russian zoologist who
discovered phagocytosis — the process by which specialized cells in animals engulf and
destroy pathogens — and who is considered one of the founders of immunology. The
naming is fitting: just as Metchnikoff's phagocytes attack invading microorganisms
from within the animal body, the yeast named in his honor attacks animals from within,
reversing the predatory relationship.
