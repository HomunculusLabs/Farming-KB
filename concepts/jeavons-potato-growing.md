### The Flat Sprouting Alternative

Whole sweet potatoes may also be sprouted side by side in a flat
rather than using the jar method. This can be more convenient when
large numbers of slips are needed. Monitor moisture levels carefully
to prevent rotting while encouraging root and shoot formation.

## Planting Sweet Potatoes in the Growing Bed

When the seedlings are 7 to 9 inches tall, transplant them into the
growing bed. A critical technique: plant them so at least 6 inches of
the stem is beneath the soil surface. This [[coleman-leek-deep-planting-technique]] encourages
additional root formation along the buried stem, significantly
increasing the harvest.
