# Water Harvesting in Arid Lands

## Overview

Water is the dominant design theme for arid landscapes. The quantity of fresh water (less than 700 ppm salt) is the final arbiter of successful settlement and sustainable agriculture in drylands. To obtain and retain fresh water, designers must intercept it before it washes salt from soils, mixes with deeper saline groundwaters, or runs off roof areas and rocks. This requires a comprehensive understanding of rainwater conservation, tank storage, [[swale-design]] and construction
