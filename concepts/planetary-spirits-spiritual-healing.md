The [[ayahuasca-preparation-pablo-amaringo-ucayali-shamanism]], extends far beyond the Amazon rainforest to encompass planetary realms, extraterrestrial civilizations, ancient Andean cultures, and elaborate spiritual [[blesching-cannabis-cardiovascular-heart-disease-evidence-review]].

The brew was so strong that Amaringo was at the edge of screaming. The visions were vivid enough that he felt contact with something physical and real. He saw sphinxes and visited Africa, Europe, and the Americas.

### The Operation

An American doctor dressed in grey-violet, accompanied by his wife in emerald-green and their daughter in matching green, appeared as surgeons. They carried scalpels, scissors, pincers, hooks, cotton, needles and thread, and various medicines.

The doctor:
1. Opened Amaringo from clavicle to last rib on the left side with a large broad knife
2. Broke the ribs with a hammer and opened the chest
3. Placed the heart on a dish
4. Operated on the arteries, joining them with soft plastic tubes
5. Showed the location of arterial damage
6. Put the heart back, closed the chest, and cleansed and sewed the wound

The doctors instructed Amaringo to fast for a week, after which he felt perfect.

### Interdimensional Guardians

A group of Adonitas — guardians of the most sacred temples of the universe — surrounded the house where the ceremony took place. Their bodies are made of tiny, dynamic, iridescent particles.

### Other Present Beings

- **Chaicunis**: Hidden people of the Pano group (related to Shipibo, Shetebo, and Cashibos) who hid when the Spaniards arrived. People of great wisdom.
- **Yaku-pato** (water duck): Very knowledgeable on occult matters with beautiful songs
- **Amahuaca couple, Campa-Ashaninka woman, and Ayar**: An Inca master of great knowledge
- **Woman from the Himalayas**: Normally lives in a galaxy called Mazzarot, where she is a netafita — a being who lives in the great nebulous mountains of the universe

### Royal Healers

- **Princess Jerusela**: About to receive a balsam from her father, a Baruh from Persia. Powerful enough to travel anywhere in the world where people call her. The woman sleeping under a red blanket symbolizes rejuvenating sleep after healing.
- **Durela**: A princess accompanied by a spirit who teaches healing prayers
- **White-dressed king**: Cures mal aire and other illnesses
- **Throne-seated king**: Cures sorcery, knows millions of icaros
- **Queen Galmana** (in blue): Defends the vegetalista during fights with enemies, absorbing evil from sorcerers
