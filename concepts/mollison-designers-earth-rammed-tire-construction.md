## Plastering and Finishing

Exposed rubber is not aesthetically desirable and degrades under UV light.
Mollison describes the plastering process:

- A cement-lime or lime-based render is applied over the tyre wall in
  two or three coats
- Chicken wire or expanded metal lath is fastened to the tyres with
  screws or wire to provide a mechanical key for the plaster
- The first (scratch) coat is a coarse mix that fills the irregular
  surface
- Subsequent coats are smoother and can be finished with lime wash,
  [[comparison-lime-plaster-vs-earthen-plaster]], or coloured renders
- Interior finishes can include [[mollison-designers-house-design-climate-zones]],
[[mollison-designers-wool-insulation-and-natural-textiles]].
