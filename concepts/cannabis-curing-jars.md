# Cannabis Curing Jars

## Overview

Curing jars are airtight glass containers used to slowly and evenly dry harvested [[growing-gourmet-growing-room-ventilation-air-exchange]]
- Some growers avoid plastic bins (Rubbermaid, Tupperware) saying plastic imparts undesirable flavor
- However, for large quantities (10+ pounds), plastic bins may be the only practical option

### Specialized Options

- "Reflection anti-detection barrier foil bags" that are airtight and infrared-proof
- Vacuum-sealed jars using inexpensive or professional-grade vacuum sealers
- Chests of drawers with screens on the bottom for drying before jarring

## The Curing Process

### Initial Drying Phase

Before placing buds in curing jars, they must be partially dried:

1. After harvest, hang branches or place buds on screens for 5-7 days
2. Ideal [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]] bruise easily)

### Moisture Management

If stems fold instead of snapping and buds feel moist:

- Remove buds and place in a paper bag
- Stack up to 6 inches (15 cm) high in the bag
- Fold top once or twice to close
- Check 2-3 times daily, turn buds to expose different sides
- Return to curing jar when dry enough

If buds have low moisture and stems snap:

- Leave in container, open lid for 5-10 minutes every few hours
- Let excess moisture escape before closing
- Optionally add an orange or lemon peel for citrus aroma
- Check several times daily
- Total curing time: a few days to two weeks

### Final Storage

Once evenly dry, seal buds in airtight containers:

- Vacuum seal jars for best preservation
- Place in refrigerator for storage (refrigerators have ~65% humidity, so seal must be airtight)
