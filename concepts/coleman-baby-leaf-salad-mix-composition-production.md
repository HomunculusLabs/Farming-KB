# Coleman Baby Leaf Salad Mix Composition and Production Methods

## Overview

Baby leaf salad mix (often called mesclun) is one of the most profitable and productive crops in Coleman's winter harvest system. Rather than growing individual greens as separate crops, the salad mix approach combines multiple species into a single planting, harvesting at the juvenile leaf stage for maximum tenderness, flavor diversity, and rapid turnover. At Four Season Farm, salad mix is a cornerstone product, featured in winter farmers' market displays and restaurant sales throughout the cold months. (see [[coleman-chicory-family-winter-greens-endive-escarole-radicchio]]).
- **Flavor is mild to moderate**: Many greens become increasingly bitter or pungent as they mature. Baby leaves capture the sweet, mild phase. (see [[coleman-winter-harvest-cut-and-come-again-techniques]]).
- **Nutrient density is high**: Young leaves concentrate vitamins and minerals relative to their size.
