# Holzer Livestock Integration

Livestock play a large role in Sepp Holzer's permaculture system. They provide high-quality produce and are also industrious and pleasant workers. Holzer breeds robust, hardy animals suited to the terrain — old domestic breeds as well as wild animals.

## Philosophy

Old domestic breeds are significantly hardier, more intelligent and adaptable than over-bred modern varieties. Their natural instincts are sufficient for them not to fall over their own feet, and their produce is of far higher quality. These breeds can be kept under near-natural conditions, which is Holzer's main requirement. Intensive livestock farming amounts to torture and produces food of very low nutritional value.

## Keeping Conditions

On the Krameterhof, livestock are always kept outside in families. Great care is taken to meet the needs of different animal species. The proportion of male and female animals is adjusted appropriately. The size of the area depends on the space naturally required by each species.

## Pigs as Workers

Pigs are indispensable as living ploughs on rough, stony terrain that cannot be ploughed mechanically. When they search for food they plough and dig through the soil, improving it with their digging activity. Holzer keeps pigs using a paddock system — moving the paddocks ensures the land is never overgrazed, and the varied diet prevents livestock diseases almost entirely.

### Old Pig Breeds

- **Mangalitza**: Hardy old breed
- **Swabian-Hall Swine**: Hardy old breed
- **Duroc**: Hardy old breed
- **Turopolje**: Hardy old breed

Pigs are also used to regulate problem plants: Holzer puts pigs out to graze in areas with unwanted plant growth and supports the process by sowing peas, beans or sweetcorn between the problem plants to make the pigs concentrate there.

## Wild Cattle and Old Domestic Cattle Breeds

Holzer has successfully bred mouflon, chamois, ibexes and red, roe and fallow deer as well as various species of wild cattle such as yaks, water buffalo and American and European bison. Old cattle breeds are kept for their hardiness and the superior quality of their produce.

## Poultry

Birds are kept humanely on the Krameterhof. Holzer advocates for bird conservation and keeping poultry under natural conditions with access to outdoor space.
