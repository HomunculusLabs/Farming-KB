# Rhizosphere Bacteria and Plant Interaction

The rhizosphere is the narrow zone of soil immediately surrounding plant roots
that is directly influenced by root secretions and associated soil microorganisms.
Coined by German microbiologist Lorenz Hiltner in 1904, the term describes one
of the most dynamic and biologically active interfaces on Earth. In "Teaming
With Microbes," Lowenfels and Lewis emphasize that the rhizosphere is where
the plant exerts the most control over its microbial environment, essentially
farming its own microbiome to secure nutrients and protection.
