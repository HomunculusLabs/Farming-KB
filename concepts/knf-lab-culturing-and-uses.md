# KNF LAB Culturing and Uses

## Ten Specific Uses of LAB

1. Improves soil ventilation: Fields recover fertility and soil
   becomes soft and fluffy when IMO mixed with LAB is sprayed on the
   field.

2. Enhances livestock digestion: LAB (1:500) with FPJ (1:300) used
   as drinking water for livestock helps recover their digestive
   function.

3. Enlarges fruits and leaves: LAB is extremely effective in making
   fruits and leaves large, though the amount should be reduced
   approaching later growth stages.

4. Accelerates fermentation: When used together with [[knf-fermented-mixed-compost]] compost from decaying by maintaining proper
   fermentation conditions.
