# Wildlife Gardening and Biodiversity

### Ladybugs
Both the adult ladybug and its slate-gray larvae eat vast quantities
of aphids. They cannot be attracted by specific plants but will be
encouraged by cultivating a varied selection of plants throughout the
garden.

### Hoverflies
It is the larvae of these creatures, which resemble "thin wasps,"
that are very useful in controlling aphids. Adult flies lay eggs
directly in aphid colonies. Attract them by planting marigolds
(Tagetes and Calendula) and nasturtiums.
