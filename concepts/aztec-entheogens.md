# Aztec Entheogens in Plants of the Gods

Schultes and Hofmann document the rich pharmacopoeia of psychoactive plants
employed by the Aztec civilization of pre-Columbian Mexico. The Aztecs
incorporated a remarkable diversity of entheogenic plants into their
religious, medicinal, and divinatory practices, establishing one of the most
complex ethnomedical systems in the indigenous Americas.
