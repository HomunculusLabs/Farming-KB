## Optimal Levels

The optimum CO2 concentration widely agreed upon by professional growers is 0.12-0.15
percent (1200-1500 ppm). At these levels, foliage becomes measurably thicker and more
turgid. Stems and branches grow faster with more densely packed cells. Flower stems
carry more weight without bending. Plants also develop more flower initiation sites.
Plants that sometimes do not bear from the first flower set are more likely to set flowers
early if CO2 enrichment is used.

## Temperature Interaction

degrees C) stimulates more rapid metabolism. However, when temperatures exceed 85 degrees F
