## Making Spore Prints

Mushroom spores are collected by taking a spore print. The
procedure is straightforward:

1. Sever the cap from the stem
2. Place the cap, gills down, upon a piece of typing paper
   or glass
3. Cover the mushroom cap with a bowl or plate to lessen
   evaporation and disturbance from air currents
4. Within 24 hours, spores fall in a beautiful pattern
   according to the radiating symmetry of the gills
