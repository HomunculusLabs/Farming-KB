# Compost Tea (AACT) — Actively Aerated Compost Tea

AACT is a liquid extract of compost, actively aerated to multiply beneficial microorganisms. Developed by Dr. Elaine Ingham, it's used to restore and boost the [[soil-food-web-nutrient-cycling]] in soils and on plant surfaces. AACT is NOT the same as anaerobic compost tea or leachate — it must be aerated.

## Why AACT

- Multiplies beneficial bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and nematodes from quality compost
- Applies concentrated biology directly to soil or foliage
- Can be tailored: bacterial-dominated for vegetables, fungal-dominated for trees/perennials
- Suppresses disease organisms through competitive exclusion
- Works faster than applying raw compost

## Core Recipe (5-Gallon Batch)

### Materials
- 5-gallon bucket or brewer
- High-quality aerobic compost or vermicompost (1-2 cups per 5 gal)
- Air pump with air stones (must run continuously — minimum 0.05 CFM per gallon)
- Dechlorinated water (let tap water sit 24 hrs or use well/rain water)
- Unsulfured blackstrap molasses (1-4 tablespoons per 5 gal) — bacterial food
- Optional: fish hydrolysate (1-2 Tbsp), kelp meal (1-2 Tbsp), humic acid (1 Tbsp)

### Process
1. Fill brewer with dechlorinated water
2. Start air pump — water must be actively bubbling before adding anything
3. Add compost in a mesh bag (paint strainer works) — do NOT squeeze the bag
4. Add molasses and any optional nutrients
5. Brew for 18-24 hours at 65-75F (18-24C)
6. Keep air running the entire time — oxygen is critical
7. Use immediately after brewing (biology peaks at 18-24 hrs, then declines)

### Warning Signs
- Foul/putrid smell = anaerobic — discard, do NOT apply
- Surface foam is normal; thick bad-smelling foam is not
- Temperature above 85F risks anaerobic conditions

## Bacterial vs. Fungal Tea

### Bacterial-Dominated (vegetables, annuals, cannabis in veg)
- Use worm castings or thermophilic compost
- Food: molasses (1-4 Tbsp), fish hydrolysate (1-2 Tbsp)
- Shorter brew: 18-20 hours

### Fungal-Dominated (trees, shrubs, perennials, cannabis in flower)
- Use forest humus or fungal compost (visible white hyphae)
- Food: kelp meal (1-2 Tbsp), humic/fulvic acid (1 Tbsp), fish hydrolysate
- Less molasses (favors bacteria)
- Longer brew: 20-24 hours
- Add oatmeal, cornmeal, or finely chopped oat straw as fungal food

## Application Rates

### Soil Drench
- **Vegetables/flowers**: 15 gal per acre, every 10-14 days
- **Orchard/trees**: 10-15 gal per acre, spring and fall
- **Indoor beds**: 1-2 cups per square foot, monthly
- Apply to [[no-till-gardening-method]] beds during watering

### Foliar Spray
- 5 gal per acre per 6 feet of canopy height
- Apply every 10-14 days during growing season
- Start 2 weeks before bud break
- Spray at dusk or early morning — UV kills microbes
- Drench foliage and stems thoroughly
- Do NOT spray when bees are pollinating

### Disease Response
- Apply immediately when disease is detected
- Repeat every 3-5 days until controlled
- Then resume every 10-14 days

## Compost Quality Matters

The quality of your AACT depends entirely on the compost:
- Must be aerobic (no foul smell)
- Should contain visible fungal hyphae
- Worm castings are excellent bacterial source
- Forest humus is excellent fungal source
- SFI (Soil Foodweb) lab can test your compost biology
