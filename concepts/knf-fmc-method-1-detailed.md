## Overview

FMC is made from a mixture of organic materials fermented with [[cervantes-reservoir-temperature-management]]:

- **Target fermentation range:** 40-50C
- **Turning trigger:** Turn when temperature reaches 50C
- **Maximum temperature:** NEVER allow temperature to exceed 70C
- **Danger zone:** Above 70C, beneficial microbes die and the [[sterilization]] occurs -- the compost becomes biologically dead
Above 70C: irreversible damage, potential fire hazard

## The Turning Schedule

The pile must be turned at specific times based on temperature readings:

### Day 2: First Turn

- By day 2, the pile should reach 50C from initial microbial activity
- Turn the entire pile thoroughly, moving material from outside to inside and bottom to top
- This reintroduces oxygen and redistributes moisture and microbes
- After turning, temperature will drop temporarily, then rise again

### Days 5-6: Second Turn

- The pile reaches 50C again approximately 3-4 days after the first turn
- Turn thoroughly as before
- Check moisture: the pile should feel like a wrung-out sponge
- If too dry, add water while turning; if too wet, add dry material

### Days 8-10: Third Turn

- The pile reaches 50C again approximately 2-4 days after the second turn
- This is typically the last required turn
- After this turn, the fermentation rate slows

### Days 15-20: Completion

- Temperature should gradually decline
- By day 20, fermentation is essentially complete
- The compost should have a sweet, earthy smell
- If the compost smells sour, rotten, or like ammonia, something went wrong

## Signs of Success and Failure

### Successful FMC (Sweet Smell)

- Sweet, earthy aroma (like [[decomposition]] by wrong organisms -- pile may need to be restarted
- **Dry and powdery:** Insufficient moisture -- add water during next turning
- **Still hot after 20 days:** Fermentation not complete -- may need additional turns
