# Enterprise Development for Mushroom Growers

Starting and managing a successful [[oyster-mushrooms]] yielding 250 grams per bag over 3 months at 25 Baht
per kg, each bag generates 6.25 Baht in total income. Cost of buying one
spawn bag is approximately 3.75 Baht.

### Profit by Number of Bags

| Bags | Monthly Income | Profit (buying bags) | Profit (making bags) |
|------|---------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| 1,000 | 2,083 Baht    | 750 Baht/month       | 1,333 Baht/month    |
| 2,000 | 4,167 Baht    | 1,500 Baht/month     | 2,667 Baht/month    |
| 3,000 | 6,250 Baht    | 2,250 Baht/month     | 4,000 Baht/month    |
| 6,000 | 12,500 Baht   | 4,500 Baht/month     | 8,000 Baht/month    |

Further income can be generated by selling spawn bags to other growers.
No labor costs are included since family labor is assumed, and
electricity and water are generally free in remote areas.

## Three Levels of Operation

1. **First level** - Buy bags, one fruiting house, 2,000 bags, 3 months
   harvest with 1 month rest.
2. **Second level** - Buy bags, three fruiting houses, 6,000 bags, 3
   months harvest per house with 1 month rest.
3. **Third level** - Make own bags with one incubation and three
   fruiting houses, 6,000 bags, continuous harvest. Can also sell bags.

## Record Keeping

Keeping records of income and expenses allows verification of profit.
If profit is low, identify how to reduce expenses without reducing
quality. If profit is high, consider expanding the farm.

### Essential Financial Records

1. **Monthly cash flow statement** - Daily record of expenses, income,
   balance, and comments (DATE, EXPENSES, INCOME, BALANCE, COMMENTS).
2. **Inventory book** - All raw materials with purchase prices, dates,
   and supplier names.
3. **Mushroom sales record** - All daily mushroom sales.
4. **Raw materials sales record** - All materials sold to ex-trainees
   or other buyers.
5. **Credit control system** - Credit notes with accounts receivable,
   including date of purchase, buyer name, item, quantity, price, total,
   and payment due date.
6. **Petty cash voucher system** - For quick purchasing of necessary
   goods.
7. **Cash book** - Following bank inflows and outflows.

Simple yet accurate accounting should be maintained daily. It is highly
advisable that all money goes through a bank account for close monitoring.

## Financial Assistance

In many countries, governments have set up special funds for people with
disabilities in enterprise development. These vary as loans with interest,
interest-free loans, or grants. Information can be found at municipal or
provincial offices, or through ministries of labor, social welfare, and
health. Trainers should help trainees prepare necessary documents and
follow up on applications.
