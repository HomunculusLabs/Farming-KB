### Mushrooms That Reduce Triglycerides
- [[grifola-frondosa]]
- Lentinus edodes (via eritadenine)

## Mechanisms of Cholesterol Reduction

### Lovastatin Production (Pleurotus)
Pleurotus species produce mevinolin (lovastatin), a specific HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor:
- P. ostreatus produces the highest amount in fruit-bodies, especially in gills
- P. saca produces lovastatin in submerged fermentation
- P. sapidus produces lovastatin in surface fermentation

### Eritadenine (Lentinus edodes)
- Lowers blood serum cholesterol not by inhibiting biosynthesis but by accelerating excretion of ingested cholesterol
- Decreases phosphatidylcholine to phosphatidylethanolamine ratio in liver microsomes
- Clinical studies in Japan show positive decreases in serum cholesterol in young women and elderly

### Dietary Fibre
High fibre content of mushrooms contributes to cholesterol reduction by binding bile acids and reducing cholesterol absorption.

### Clinical Evidence
- 4% dried Pleurotus added to high-cholesterol diet reduced serum and liver cholesterol in rats
- 15-20g dried Pleurotus supplement daily for one month reduced hypercholesterolemia in humans (though not all patients)
- PSK decreases LDL cholesterol in hyperlipidemia patients

## Blood Pressure Reduction

Three species demonstrated arterial blood pressure lowering:
- Ganoderma lucidum (ganoderic acids B, D, F, H, K, S, Y act as ACE inhibitors)
- [[agaricus-bisporus]]
- Agrocybe aegerita (polysaccharides AG-HN1 and AG-HN2)
- Cordyceps sinensis (CS-F30 polysaccharide)
- Tremella aurantia (TAP - Tremella acidic polysaccharide)

### Non-Insulin-Dependent Diabetes
- Grifola frondosa: diminishes glycemia, insulinemia, and blood triglycerides

### Anti-Coagulation Warning
Water extracts of L. edodes lessen blood platelet effectiveness in coagulation. People who bleed easily or take anticoagulants should use caution with L. edodes extracts therapeutically.

## Ideal Functional Food Profile

Mushrooms are almost ideal for cardiovascular disease prevention diets because of:
- High fibre content
- Sterols
- Proteins
- Microelements
- Low calorific value
