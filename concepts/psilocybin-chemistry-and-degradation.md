## Structural Similarity to Serotonin

Both compounds closely resemble serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine, 5-HT),
the endogenous neurotransmitter regulating mood, perception, and
cognition. The shared indole ring system is the key structural feature
allowing binding to serotonin receptors. The primary difference is
dimethyl substitution on the terminal amine, increasing lipophilicity
and altering the binding profile relative to serotonin.

## Metabolic Conversion

Psilocybin is not directly psychoactive in the brain. After ingestion,
alkaline phosphatase enzymes rapidly dephosphorylate it, converting
psilocybin to active psilocin. This conversion occurs in the digestive
tract and liver. Psilocin then crosses the blood-brain barrier to
exert its pharmacological effects.

## 5-HT2A Receptor Mechanism

Psilocin acts primarily as a partial agonist at the serotonin 5-HT2A
receptor. By binding to and partially activating this receptor, psilocin
modulates serotonergic signaling in the prefrontal cortex, producing
the characteristic alterations in perception, cognition, and emotion.
The 5-HT2A receptor is densely expressed in cortical layer V pyramidal
neurons, and its activation increases cortical excitability and cross-
modal neural communication.

## Minor Alkaloids

### Baeocystin
Baeocystin (4-phosphoryloxy-N-methyltryptamine) is a mono-methylated
analog of psilocybin, present in smaller quantities. Its individual
pharmacological contribution remains incompletely characterized, though
it may contribute to the overall psychoactive profile.

### Norbaeocystin
Norbaeocystin (4-phosphoryloxytryptamine) is the N-desmethyl analog of
baeocystin, typically present in trace amounts. Its pharmacological
activity is poorly understood and it is generally considered to have
minimal psychoactive contribution at natural concentrations.

## Volatility and Airborne Exposure

A remarkable anecdote from Guzmán's fieldwork underscores compound
potency. During a collecting trip to Oaxaca in 1958, Guzmán experienced
hallucinatory effects simply from being in a closed room with a large
quantity of fresh psilocybin mushrooms. This suggests psilocybin or
psilocin may be volatile enough under certain conditions to produce
effects through inhalation, though this is not a typical exposure route.
