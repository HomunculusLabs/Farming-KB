## Yakuruna and the Aquatic Spirit World

## Overview

The aquatic spirit world in Amazonian vegetalismo is a vast, multi-layered realm beneath the rivers, inhabited by the yakurunas (water people), mermaids, and a bewildering variety of water spirits. These beings are accessed through [[ayahuasca-preparation-pablo-amaringo-ucayali-shamanism]]'s paintings provide the most detailed visual documentation of this complex spirit ecology.

## Types of Yakurunas (Vision 19)

The yakuruna (from Quechua: yaku = water, runa = person) exist in several distinct forms, each with different powers and moral orientations:

### Masha Yakuruna

The masha yakuruna is an ally of the murayas (aquatic specialist shamans), teaching them how to rescue people lost in the watery depths. He rests on a serpent-hammock of beautiful color in a grove of golden trees, smoking a pipe made from an encanto (magical stone) in the form of a shoe, with a stem made from the bone of a tuyuyo (*Jabiru mycteria*).

### Taksha Yakuruna (Little Yakuruna)

A green-haired yakuruna carrying a boa. According to Amazonian legend, this type emerges to feed between midnight and dawn. If captured by a vegetalista, the taksha yakuruna must remain on the earth plane and teach how to hunt in the waters — a form of forced spirit alliance.

### Yana-Sacraruna (Black Evil People)

This yakuruna is a practitioner of black magic whose sole purpose is to kill. He takes great pleasure in causing riverbanks to collapse. He lives in the deepest, darkest caves of the underwater world and uses a red conch as a fan to cast his evil spells. He is an ally of sorcerers.

### Puka-Ninaruna (People of the Red Flame)

This type lives in the largest of the underwater cities and is invoked by murayas as an ally to control the anguila mama (electric eel), sea monsters, thunder, and storms. Their association with fire and thunder suggests they command the more volatile and dangerous aspects of the aquatic realm.

### Children of the Mermaids

These beings emerge to rest by riverbanks, along lakes, and by ravines. They represent a peaceful, intermediary class of aquatic spirits connected to mermaid lineages.

## The Subaquatic Realms

### Enchanted Underwater Forest

One of the realms beneath the waters is an enchanted forest of aquatic plants and seashells containing precious pearls and aquamarines. This realm represents the natural beauty and material wealth hidden within the aquatic spirit world.

### City of the Huiracucha Mermaids

The huiracucha (white people) mermaids inhabit one of the most splendid underwater cities. Here live the most sublime and beautiful mermaids, possessing great magical power. Their designation as "white people" connects them to the broader Andean cosmological category of huiracocha, associated with light, creation, and divine beings.

### Oasis of Youth

The miraculous oasis of youth is resplendent with aromatic fragrances. Its icaro is a love chant capable of amorously spellbinding anyone. This represents a fountain-of-youth motif within Amazonian spirituality — the idea that certain spirit places can restore or preserve vitality.

### Bath of the Sumis and Sumillakta

The great bath of the sumis features a gilded crystal stairway and sapphire-blue waters. Adjacent is Sumillakta, the beautiful and enchanting city of the sumis, where one can learn the great magical parapsychic arts. The sumis represent an advanced class of aquatic beings with access to esoteric knowledge.

### Atunllipian-llakta (The Great Brilliant City)

The Atunllipian-llakta (atun = great, ilipian = brilliant, llakta = city) is the meeting place of the masters, where they gather to receive the wisdom of the ancient ones. It is revealed through a celestial opening in layers of dark clouds above a hut where medicine men are in full trance.

## Aquatic Guardians and Defenses

### Auca-Sirenas (War Mermaids)

Mermaids keeping watch on the tranquil and resting yakurunas. They live within rocks and disappear by approaching rocks when anyone tries to capture them. The small pools of water atop their rocks function as solar mirrors that can be transformed into strong lasers capable of capturing even the most powerful enemies.

### Fierce Dogs of the Yakuruna

The yakuruna's fierce dogs wear the heads of piranha fish, combining aquatic predation with terrestrial guarding instincts. Behind them are cats with the heads of canero (*Vandelia plazai*, a type of fish often invoked by evil sorcerers), representing the darker aquatic guardians.
