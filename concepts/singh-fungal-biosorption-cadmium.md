## Cadmium Biosorption by White-Rot Fungi

White-rot fungi exhibit notable cadmium biosorption capabilities and
additional enzymatic mechanisms for cadmium interaction. Trametes
versicolor is an excellent cadmium biosorbent, removing all Cd(II) ions
within 2 hours at a rate of about 2 mg Cd(II) per gram of mycelial dry
weight. This appears to be an energy-independent surface binding process.
