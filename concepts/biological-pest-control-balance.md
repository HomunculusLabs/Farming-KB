## The Rice Leafhopper Investigation

When an expert in insect damage prevention from the Kochi Prefecture Testing
Centre investigated why rice leafhoppers were so scarce in Fukuoka's fields
despite no insecticide use, he discovered a complex web of natural
relationships. While harmful insects were few, their natural predators were
far more numerous than in chemically sprayed fields. The professor acknowledged
that if Fukuoka's method were generally adopted, the problem of crop
devastation by leafhoppers could be solved, then returned to Kochi where no
further changes were implemented.
