## IMO-1: Collection from the Environment
IMO-1 is the initial collection of microorganisms. The source
describes three [[cho-imo-collection-bamboo-stump-and-paddy-field]]**: A bamboo stump in a bamboo grove is cut
10 cm from the ground and filled with boiled rice. After 3 to 5 days,
red, white, yellow, and black microorganisms collect along with
bamboo juice. The stump is cut and rice transferred to a jar as
IMO-1.
**Method 3 - Paddy Field**: After paddy harvest, rice-filled wooden
boxes are placed face-down on rice stumps. After approximately one
week, mostly anaerobic microorganisms collect, including Bacillus
licheniformis (breaks down protein, fat, carbohydrates) and Bacillus
subtilis (breaks up strong fibers like straw and reeds).
## IMO-2: Sugar Preservation
IMO-2 is created by mixing IMO-1 with jaggery or brown sugar at a
1:1 ratio. The container is covered with porous paper. The sugar
preserves the microorganisms and provides a food source. The source
emphasizes that crude, unrefined sugar (jaggery) is best; white
refined sugar is not recommended. Fermentation takes 3 days in summer
and 5 days in winter. Black molds indicate the collection has
exceeded optimal timing.
