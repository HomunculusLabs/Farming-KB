## McKenna's Phenomenological Distinction

McKenna argued that the critical difference is not merely duration
but the **quality of consciousness** each compound evokes:

- **LSD**: Produces a sustained, gradually unfolding psychedelic
  state where the ego progressively dissolves. The long duration
  allows for psychological processing and integration during the
  experience itself.

- **DMT**: Produces an immediate, overwhelming breakthrough into
  what McKenna called "a universe of active intelligence that is
  transhuman, hyperdimensional, and extremely alien." The brevity
  of the experience provides no time for psychological preparation
  or processing — one is simply thrust into the state and returned.
