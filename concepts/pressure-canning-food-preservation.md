# Pressure Canning for Food Preservation

## Overview

Pressure canning is a food preservation method that uses elevated temperature under steam pressure to safely process low-acid foods in sealed glass jars. It is the only recommended method for canning vegetables, meats, poultry, seafood, soups, stews, and other low-acid foods (pH above 4.6). ## Common Safety Mistakes to Avoid

- Using a pressure cooker (not canner) for pressure canning
- Skipping the 10-minute venting step before building pressure
- Modifying tested recipes (adding ingredients, changing proportions, or omitting acid)
