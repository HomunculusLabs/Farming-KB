# Hawaiian and Tropical Sativa Strains

The Pacific islands and [[psilocybe-southeast-asia-pacific]] have produced some of the most
prized [[cannabis-sativa-landrace-strains]] genetics in cannabis history.

Hawaiian strains in particular have been celebrated since the 1970s
for their unique [[cannabis-terpene-profiles-sativa]], cerebral effects, and vibrant
visual appeal.

This page covers tropical and island-origin sativa strains featured
in Cannabis Sativa Volume 3, including Hawaiian Wave, Wild Thailand,
and South Star.

## Hawaiian Wave

Hawaiian Wave is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Spain's Ripper Seeds.

The strain combines a pure Hawaiian landrace with Double Glock, a
100% indica from Afghan valleys.

This pairing tames the extreme height and long flowering time of the
pure Hawaiian sativa while preserving its tropical character.

### Genetics and Lineage

The Hawaiian landrace parent contributes the strain's signature
fruity terpenes and uplifting, euphoric effects.

Double Glock, a sweet but powerful indica, provides structure,
shorter flowering time, and mold resistance.

The result is a balanced plant that can be grown both indoors and
outdoors, unlike its pure landrace parent.

### Growing Characteristics

Indoor growers should limit the vegetative period to 1-2 weeks
before flipping. Plants double or triple in height after the flip.

The strain shows good mold resistance and handles humidity well,
though it struggles with extended cold.

Flowering time is approximately 10-11 weeks indoors.

Outdoor growers can expect higher yields with large, pale-colored
colas in gorgeous greens.

### Potency and Effects

Hawaiian Wave tests at approximately 14% THC.

The effects are characteristic of Hawaiian genetics: flying,
euphoric highs with a crisp, fruity flavor profile.

The smoke carries notes of fresh ocean air and tropical fruit,
making it a favorite among island-strain enthusiasts.

## Wild Thailand

Wild Thailand is a pure Thai sativa from World of Seeds, sourced
from the Ko Chang archipelago in Thailand.

This strain represents authentic landrace genetics that are highly
prized despite Thailand's severe anti-cannabis penalties.

Thai landraces are among the most historically significant sativas,
having influenced countless hybrid strains since the 1960s.

### Growing Profile

Wild Thailand is an extremely tall plant with vigorous, almost
uncontrollable growth.

It is designed for outdoor cultivation where roots can spread freely.

[[comparison-cannabis-indoor-growing-basics-vs-cannabis-outdoor-growing]] is possible but requires substantial room and careful
training including staking and low-stress techniques.

The strain shows very high mold resistance and moderate pest
resistance, with some forgiveness for overwatering.

Expected yields are 300 grams indoors and up to 500 grams per
plant outdoors.

A light breeze in the grow room helps stems grow thick rather
than spindly, improving structural support for heavy buds.

Wild Thailand tests at an impressive 22.3% THC.

The aroma is overbearing with a spicy, Haze-like Asian character.

The high is spectacularly euphoric, catapulting mood into an
intensely elevated state — a pinnacle of pure Asian landrace
expression.

## South Star

South Star is a sativa-dominant hybrid from Holland's Kiwiseeds,
combining Haze and Northern Lights genetics.

Kiwiseeds was founded in 2002 specifically to preserve old-school
Kiwi genetics brought to New Zealand from Mexico, Thailand, Vietnam,
and India during the 1970s.

### Compact Sativa Structure

Despite being a Haze cross, South Star reaches only 3-4 feet in
most conditions, making it exceptionally indoor-friendly.

The plant grows in a Christmas-tree structure with balanced
branching — a significant achievement for indoor cultivators.

### Cultivation Details

South Star performs well in most non-tropical climates.

Large, fairly loose colas develop quickly once flowering begins.

Plants reach full maturity 70 days after forced flowering, yielding
around 400 grams per square yard.

### Effects Profile

At 16% THC, South Star delivers a potent but manageable experience.

The high is described as a creeper — slow to build but energetic
and long-lasting once established.

Effects persist for at least two hours, making it suitable as a
daytime smoke.

The flavor is pleasant and complements the uplifting effects.
