## Natural Fertilizer Sources and Application Guide

Natural fertilizers should be selected based on soil test results. No two gardens are alike, so fertilizer choice must be individual.

### Timing Applications

- Apply slow-release fertilizers in autumn or early spring so they break down before peak growing season
- Side-dress heavy-feeding crops (tomatoes, corn, squash) monthly during growth
- Foliar feed during critical growth stages (flowering, fruit set) for quick nutrient boosts
- Avoid fertilizing dormant plants or frozen soil

## Key Principle

Perennials, trees, and shrubs in natural systems with the Law of Return operating usually don't need much fertilizer. Exceptions: at planting time, after heavy pruning, or when fruit is removed (preventing nutrient return to soil).
