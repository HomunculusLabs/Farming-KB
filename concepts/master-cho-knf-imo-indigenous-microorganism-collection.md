## Collecting IMO (IMO #1)

### Collection Site

It is important to collect IMO in a place with characteristics most similar to the land being worked. Vegetation differs by altitude because microorganisms and their environments differ.

- Prefer areas around plants whose roots contain sugar: near bamboo or broadleaf tree roots, leaf moulds near the field, or lawn grass roots.
- Rice stumps are excellent: after harvest, sap rises to the stump top. Fungi and bacteria such as [[bacillus-subtilis]] gather on the stump.
- Collect in a slightly barren area, 150-200m higher than the field altitude.
- **South-facing slopes**: effective for summer crops (more sunshine).
- **North-facing slopes**: effective for winter crops (less sunshine).
- Do not collect during rainy season. Avoid moist areas where anaerobic microorganisms (red mold, blue mold) dominate.

### Materials

- Wooden lunch box (Japanese cedar or bamboo), 10cm tall
- Hard-cooked rice (less moisture to collect aerobic microbes) -- fill to 2/3 of box height
- Porous paper and rubber band or straw for covering
- Container box to protect from animals

### Collection Period

- Spring and fall: 7-10 days
- Summer: 4-5 days
- Winter: more than 10 days (bring leaf moulds into greenhouse)
- Collect every year

### Method

1. Fill the wooden box with hard-cooked rice up to 7cm (2/3 height).
2. Cover with porous paper, tie with rubber band.
3. Bury in bamboo field or leaf moulds nearest the field, in loose/soft soil.
4. Cover with more leaf moulds, then the container box.
5. In dry weather, water the leaf moulds around the box.

### Successful Collection Signs

The empty 1/3 volume of the lunch box is fully covered by white microorganisms (aerobic). A few spots of red/blue mold are acceptable. If the whole box is covered by them, collect again.

## Cultivating IMO

### IMO #2 (Foundation Stock)

1. Mix IMO #1 with brown sugar in 1:1 ratio (by weight).
2. Fill 2/3 of a clay jar with the mixture.
3. Cover with paper, tie with rubber band.
4. Maintain temperature at 23-25 deg C. Temperature rises above 40 deg C inside; consistency becomes like gruel.
5. Process takes over 7 days. Stir in early morning during spring/fall.
6. Storage: keep at 1-15 deg C with good [[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]]. Watch for bubbles (signal of losing effectiveness).

### IMO #3 (Pure Stock)

1. Dilute IMO #2 by 500-1000x with water and mix with rice bran.
2. Target moisture: 65-70%. Add nutrient liquids (OHN x1/1000, FPJ of mugwort/dropwort x1/500, BRV x1/500, Mineral A x1/1000).
3. Stack rice bran mixture 35-40cm high on soil floor (not concrete).
4. Cover with straws, straw mats, or fallen leaves. 70% shade, 30% light.
5. When temperature reaches 40-50 deg C, overturn evenly. Takes about 7 days.
6. White colonies visible when uncovered. Fragrance when complete.
7. Storage: 1-15 deg C, ventilated container, moisture drops to 20-30% (dormancy).

### IMO #4 (Final Product)

1. Mix IMO #3 with soil (field soil 50%, mountain soil/loess 50%) in 1:1 ratio.
2. Adjust moisture to 65-70% using OHN, FPJ, BRV, Mineral A, and seawater (x1/30).
3. Same fermentation process as IMO #3. Takes 4-5 days.
