### Home Test Kits
Home kits like the La Motte are limited to testing nitrogen,
phosphorus, potassium, and pH. While useful, they cannot reveal
trace mineral deficiencies or excesses. Plants grown in soil
lacking major or trace minerals show deficiency through yellowed
leaves, stunted growth, purple veins, or other symptoms. A home
test kit may not provide the solution when plants grow poorly.
