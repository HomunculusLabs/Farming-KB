## Ancient History of Psychedelic Use

## Overview

The use of psychoactive plants and fungi in religious, healing, and divinatory contexts extends back to the earliest human civilizations and possibly to prehistoric times. Evidence comes from archaeology, ethnobotany, philology, and comparative mythology. This page covers the major theories and evidence for ancient psychedelic use, from cave paintings to classical mystery cults.
