# Eucalypts (continued) and F-Section Permaculture Plants

This page continues the eucalypt coverage and covers plants from the letter F as catalogued in *Permaculture Plants: A Selection*.

## Eucalypts — Additional Species

### River and Floodplain Eucalypts
- **Eucalyptus pauciflora** (Snow gum) — page 117. Cold-hardy eucalypt of the Australian high country; distinctive smooth white bark.
- **[[eucalyptus-occidentalis]]** (Yate) — pages 95, 117, 130, 135. Western Australian species useful for saline landscapes; good timber and nectar source.
- **Eucalyptus largiflorens** (Black box) — pages 20, 21, 93, 116. Inland tree of heavy clay floodplains; important drought fodder.
- **Eucalyptus moluccana** (Grey box) — page 21, 94. Eastern Australian box eucalypt for general timber.
- **Eucalyptus crebra** (Narrow-leaved ironbark) — pages 21, 94. Hardy inland species producing dense timber.

### Lesser-Known Eucalypts
- **Eucalyptus macrocera** — page 93. Western Australian mallee with distinctive large buds.
- **Eucalyptus rudis** (Flooded gum) — pages 93, 95. Western Australian riverine species.
- **Eucalyptus gomphocephala** (Tuart) — pages 93, 116, 135. Tall coastal tree of Western Australia; dense durable timber.
- **Eucalyptus calophylla** (Marri) — pages 23, 92, 93, 95, 96, 116. Large Western Australian tree with distinctive fruit capsules; excellent nectar source.
- **Eucalyptus wandoo** (Wandoo) — pages 94, 96, 117. Fine-grained hardwood for furniture and flooring.
- **Eucalyptus sargentii** (Salt river gum) — pages 96, 130. Salt-tolerant species for saline land restoration.
- **Eucalyptus spathulata** — pages 130, 135. Mallee eucalypt useful for alkaline soils.
- **Eucalyptus loxophleba** (York gum) — pages 94, 95. Multi-purpose inland eucalypt of Western Australia.
- **[[foeniculum-vulgare-entity]]*) — pages 114, 115, 119. Perennial herb with edible leaves, stems, seeds, and bulbs. Attracts beneficial insects; companion plant.
- **Fenugreek** (*Trigonella foenum-graecum*) — page 114. Annual legume for culinary spice and green manure; nitrogen fixing.
- **Fig** (*Ficus carica*) — pages 57, 118. Deciduous fruit tree producing sweet figs; [[fukuoka-natural-farming-in-tropical-regions]].

### Fish and Aquatic Systems
- **Fish** — page 121. Aquatic plants in integrated farming systems support fish populations through habitat and food.
- **Fish food** — page 123. Duckweed, azolla, and other aquatic plants serve as fish food in polyculture systems.
- **Fish pole bamboo** — page 97. Thin-walled bamboo species traditionally used for fishing poles.

### Flora and Forage
- **Flax** (*Linum usitatissimum*) — page 114. Multipurpose plant for linen fibre, linseed oil, and edible seeds.
- **Flavoring** — pages 133, 136. Many herbs and spices serve as flavoring agents in permaculture kitchen gardens.
- **Floating plants** — page 121. Duckweed, water hyacinth, and azolla are floating aquatic plants used in wastewater treatment and animal feed.
- **Fodder** — pages 17, 18, 19, 22, 24, 26, 28, 37, 62, 83, 97, 114, 127, 130. Livestock fodder species include tagasaste (26), honey locust pods (28), leucaena (24), various grasses (124-128), and tree lucerne (28).
- **Forage** — pages 62, 67, 83, 102, 104, 106, 108, 114, 133. Browse species for free-range livestock; includes acacia pods, tree lucerne, and various palms.

### Flooring and Framework
- **Flooring** — pages 20, 67, 81, 83, 85, 94, 95, 96, 101. Premium flooring timbers include jarrah (93), spotted gum (94), and various oaks (80-82).
- **Framework** — page 85. Bamboo provides excellent framework material for construction and scaffolding.

### Fodder Trees and Shrubs
- **Forsythia** (*Forsythia* spp.) — page 135. Ornamental shrub with early yellow flowers; hedgerow plant.
- **Fraser's sheoak** (*Allocasuarina Fraseriana*) — pages 23, 111. Western Australian sheoak for timber, shelter, and nitrogen fixation.
- **Forest sheoak** (*Allocasuarina torulosa*) — pages 24, 111. Ornamental and timber tree of eastern Australia; nitrogen-fixing root nodules.
- **Four-winged saltbush** (*Atriplex canescens*) — page 128. North American halophyte for livestock fodder on saline rangelands.
- **Frankia spp.** — page 22. Nitrogen-fixing actinobacteria that form symbiotic associations with casuarinas and other non-legume species.

### Fruits
- **Feijoa** (*Acca sellowiana*, syn. *Feijoa sellowiana*) — pages 73, 118. Also called pineapple guava; produces aromatic fruit with tropical flavor. Hardy evergreen shrub.
- **Field pea** (*Pisum sativum*) — page 15. Cool-season legume for food and green manure; nitrogen fixing.
- **Flinders Range wattle** (*Acacia iteaphylla*) — page 19. Ornamental wattle with weeping habit; drought tolerant.
- **Flindersia maculosa** (Leopardwood) — page 20. Australian dry-zone tree with distinctive mottled bark.
- **Fuchsia** (*Fuchsia* spp.) — pages 76, 135. Ornamental flowering shrubs; some produce edible berries.
- **Fuel** — pages 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 28, 75, 89, 97, 100, 104, 105. See also firewood.

### Frost-Tolerant Species
Frost tolerance is noted for numerous species across pages 15-133. Key frost-hardy plants include:
- Alpine ash and snow gum — pages 18, 117
- Common beech and hornbeam — pages 82, 134
- European ash and oak — pages 80-82
- Hawthorn and blackthorn — pages 112, 115
- Many wattles and eucalypts — pages 17-23
