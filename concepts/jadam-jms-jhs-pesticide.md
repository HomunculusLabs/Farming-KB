# JMS-JHS Pesticide (JADAM Combination)

**Updated:** 2026-04-18
**Source:** JADAM Organic Farming, pp.298-300 (Youngsang Cho, 2016)

## Overview

The JMS-JHS pesticide combines [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] and [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] 10-20 L (2.6-5.3 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] 3-5 L (0.8-1.3 gal)
- Water to make 500 L (132 gal)

## How It Works

### Two Mechanisms, One Spray

| Component | Mechanism | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| JMS (microbes) | Crowds out pathogens on leaf surface | Disease prevention |
| JHS (herbs) | Direct insecticidal compounds | Pest killing |
| JWA (surfactant) | Coats all surfaces evenly | Enhances both mechanisms |

### Synergy
The combination is synergistic:
1. JMS creates a healthy microbial barrier on leaf surfaces
2. JHS directly kills insects that feed on the plant
3. JWA ensures both JMS and JHS reach every part of the plant
4. Dead insects decompose and feed the JMS microorganisms
5. Healthy microbial ecology reduces future pest pressure

## JHS Options

You can use different JHS types depending on target pests:

| JHS Type | Best Against |
|---|---|
| Jerusalem artichoke | Aphids, mites, moths |
| Ginkgo | Planthoppers, leafhoppers, mulberry suckers |
| Korean pasque flower root | Broadest spectrum -- almost all pests |
| Oleander | Can replace KPFR |
| Bracken | General insecticide |

You can use both JHS types or just one. For broader coverage, use KPFR JHS which has the widest pest spectrum.

## Critical JMS Limitations

### Do Not Exceed 20 L of JMS
- Over 20 L per 500 L will cause JMS microorganisms to break down JWA
- Broken-down JWA loses its wetting/surfactant properties
- Result: uneven coverage, marks on plants, reduced effectiveness

### Use JMS at Peak Foam
- JMS is most potent when foam production is at its peak
- This indicates maximum microorganism population
- Peak lasts approximately 12 hours

### Soft Water Required
- Use soft water for culturing JMS intended for foliar application
- Hard water compromises both JMS viability and JWA effectiveness

## Application Schedule

### Prevention Phase
- Apply weekly during growing season
- Use 10 L JMS + 5 L JHS + 3 L JWA per 500 L
- This maintains microbial diversity and basic pest deterrence

### Active Pest Pressure
- Increase to 20 L JMS + 10 L JHS + 5 L JWA per 500 L
- Apply every 2-3 days
- If disease appears, add 1-2 L of [[jadam-jms-pesticide]] - JMS-focused prevention recipe (#11)
