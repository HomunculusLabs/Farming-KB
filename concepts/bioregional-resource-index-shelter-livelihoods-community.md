## Bioregional Resource Index — Shelter, Livelihoods, and Community Services

This concept covers Categories B through H of the [[bioregional-resource-index]], a comprehensive inventory system for regional self-reliance developed within [[permaculture]]'s social design framework. While Category A addresses food systems, these categories address the full spectrum of shelter, economic livelihood, information, community security, social life, and health services needed for a functioning bioregion.

## Category B: Shelter and Buildings

**Criteria**: Energy-efficient house design and non-toxic materials only.

### B1: Construction Materials

The materials subcategory emphasises local, non-toxic, and energy-efficient building resources:

- **Timber**: growers and suppliers, community timber plantations
- **Stone and gravel**: earth materials sourced locally
- **Plumbing and piping**: drainage and roofing systems
- **Bricks and concrete products**: tanks, blocks, and related items
- **Tiles and surfaces**: paints (specifically non-toxic varieties)
- **Furniture and fittings**: locally made where possible
- **Tools and fasteners**: sharpening services, repairs, glues and tapes
- **Library and research resources**: building design knowledge
- **Housing assessment**: current state of housing in the region, numbers seeking housing, rentals available
- **Toxin sources**: identification of unsafe materials in buildings, appliances, furnishings, paints and glues, high-voltage equipment

### B2: Energy Systems

Energy resources for domestic and regional use:

- **Energy-efficient appliances**: home appliances for energy conservation
- **Hot water systems**: solar hot water and related technologies
- **Space heating**: house design appropriate for the region's climate
- **Power generation**: current and proposed systems for the region
- **[[appropriate-technology]]**: research centres and demonstrations
- **Low-energy design**: designers specialising in efficient home systems and buildings
- **[[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-internet-information-sources]]**: publications, trade literature, library resources
- **Reliable contractors and builders**: vetted professionals

### B3: Wastes and Recycling

Closed-loop waste management systems:

- **Sewage and greywater**: domestic disposal and treatment systems
- **Compost systems**: organic waste cycling
- **Solid waste disposal**: collection systems for boxes, bottles, plastics
- **Recycling occupations**: livelihoods based on [[agricultural-waste-recycling]]

## Category C: Livelihoods and Support Systems

**Criteria**: Concept of [[effective-working-groups-right-livelihood]] or socially useful work. Durable and well-made items.

### C1: Community Finance and Recycling

Economic systems that keep value circulating within the region:

- **Barter and exchange**: non-monetary trade systems
- **Small business loans**: local lending mechanisms
- **[[mollison-local-currency-systems-and-community-banking]]**: investment systems that serve the region
- **Land access systems**: commonworks, leases, trusts
- **Legal and information services**: accessible community-level support

### C2: Livelihood Support Services

Infrastructure supporting local employment and self-sufficiency:

- **Small business service centres**: shared resources for entrepreneurs
- **Skills resource bank**: business, legal, and financial advisory services, volunteer and retired people contributing expertise
- **Self-employment**: fulfilling regional needs through job vacancy lists and needs assessments
- **Training courses**: educational opportunities within the region

### C3: Crafts and Manufacturing

Local production of essential goods and services:

- **Clothing and cloth**: spinning, weaving, textile production
- **Footwear and accessories**: leatherwork
- **Basketry and weaving**: mats and screens from local materials
- **Functional pottery**: ceramics for everyday use
- **Steelwork**: fitting and turning, smithing, casting, welding
- **Functional woodwork**: furniture, tools, building components
- **Engines and repairs**: mechanical maintenance skills
- **Functional glasswork**: local glass production
- **Paper recycling and manufacture**: book trades, printing, binding
- **Catering and cooking**: food preparation services
- **Draughting and illustrating**: design and communication services
- **Soaps and cleaning materials**: non-toxic local production

## Category D: Information, Media, Communications, and Research

**Criteria**: Essential community information, aids, and research.

### D1: Communications Infrastructure

- **Regional radio and CB**: ham radio networks
- **Regional news**: newspapers and newsletters
- **Audio-visual services**: photography, television, film
- **Business and research communications**: fax, telex, modem, card files, computer, journals, libraries, graphics, telephone answering services
- **Computer services and training**: digital literacy and access
- **Libraries and data collections**: regional knowledge repositories
- **Maps**: cartographic resources
- **Bioregional contacts**: local and overseas network connections
- **Standard documents**: data sheets available via the bioregional centre

## Category E: Community and Security

**Criteria**: Cooperative neighbourhood and ample, sustainable resources for people.

- **House and livestock security**: house sitting arrangements
- **Neighbourhood watch**: community safety networks
- **Cattle and livestock watch**: agricultural asset protection
- **Flood response**: cleanup services and equipment ("rubber duckies")
- **Bush, cliff, beach rescue**: emergency services for natural environments
- **Communication systems**: emergency reporting and response
- **Report centre**: centralised emergency coordination
- **Emergency communications**: reliable crisis communication networks

## Category F: Social Life

**Criteria**: Assistance for isolated people to meet people of like mind.

- **Introductory services**: connecting newcomers and isolated individuals
- **Think tanks**: forums for ideas and discussion
- **Expeditions**: group activities and exploration
- **Work groups**: collaborative labour for community projects

## Category G: Health Services

**Criteria**: Basic preventative and common ailment treatment, necessary hospitalisation, accident treatment, local resources.

- **Medical and dental services**: accessible healthcare providers
- **Alternative health services**: ecological and midwifery services, birth support
- **Morbidity assessment**: understanding regional health priorities including:
  - Accidents and injuries
  - Infectious diseases
  - Addictions and drugs
  - Genetic and birth defects
  - Nutritional problems

The health assessment emphasises that until a comprehensive listing of regional health issues is made, no region can properly assess its health priorities.

## Category H: Future Trends and Potential Threats

This category addresses emerging challenges and opportunities for the region, serving as a forward-looking assessment that complements the inventory of existing resources.
