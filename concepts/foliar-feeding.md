# Foliar Feeding

Applying liquid nutrients directly to plant leaves for rapid absorption. Foliar feeding bypasses soil chemistry and root uptake limitations, delivering nutrients directly into the plant through stomata and leaf cuticle.

## How It Works

Plants can absorb nutrients through their leaves. The nutrients enter through:
- **Stomata:** Tiny pores on leaf surfaces that open during the day
- **Cuticle:** The waxy outer layer of leaves, which can absorb dissolved nutrients
- Apply in early morning or late evening when stomata are open and temperatures are moderate

## When to Use Foliar Feeding

Foliar feeding is not a replacement for good soil fertility, but it serves important purposes:
- **Rapid correction of deficiencies** when soil minerals are insufficient
- **Stress recovery** during drought, transplant shock, or pest damage
- **Growth boost** during critical growth stages
- **Low-water situations** when fertigation (fertilizer through irrigation) isn"t possible

## Effective Foliar Sprays

### Kelp Tea
One of the best organic foliar sprays. Kelp contains:
- A full range of trace mineral nutrients
- Natural plant growth hormones (cytokinins, auxins)
- "Phytamins" that boost plant immune response
- Apply once per week for best results

### Fish Emulsion + Kelp
A mixture of kelp tea and fish emulsion makes plants grow much faster. The fish provides nitrogen and phosphorus while kelp supplies micronutrients and growth hormones.

### Soluble Mineral Solutions
Complete soluble chemical mixes with trace minerals can be used as foliar sprays when the soil lacks specific minerals. Ensure the solution contains all trace elements, not just NPK.

### Other Options
- **Half-strength Coca Cola:** Contains significant phosphoric acid and sugars that help plants. Used by farmers in India as a foliar spray.
- **Compost tea:** [[compost-tea-aact]] can be foliar-applied to deliver beneficial biology to leaf surfaces (the phyllosphere)

## Application Guidelines

1. **Filter thoroughly** — any particles will clog sprayers
2. **Apply to leaf undersides** — more stomata on the underside
3. **Spray early morning** — stomata are open, less evaporation
4. **Don't spray in full sun** — solution can burn leaves
5. **Use fine mist** — smaller droplets absorb more readily
6. **Add a surfactant** — a drop of mild soap helps solution spread and stick

## Foliar vs. Soil Application

| Method | Speed | Duration | Best For |
|--------|-------|----------|----------|
| Foliar | Hours | 1-2 weeks | Quick fixes, stress recovery |
| Soil | Days-weeks | Season-long | Long-term fertility |
| Fertigation | Days | Season-long | Irrigated gardens |
