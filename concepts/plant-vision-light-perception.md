## Light as Information

Plants do not merely use light as energy for photosynthesis. They
extract rich
information from light signals. The red to far-red ratio reveals whether
neighbors are shading a plant. [[blue-light-phototropism-red-light-photoperiodism-dual-plant-vision-system]] intensity indicates whether the plant is above or
below
ground. Light direction guides growth orientation.

This informational use means plants effectively construct a visual
representation of their environment. A plant "knows" the time of day,
the
season, the presence of competitors, and its position in space -- all
from
light cues alone.
