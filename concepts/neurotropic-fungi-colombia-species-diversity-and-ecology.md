Colombia occupies a critical position in the biogeography of neurotropic
fungi, serving as a bridge between Central American and Amazonian mycoflora.
The country's extraordinary range of altitudes, from sea level to permanent
snow, combined with its position at the intersection of major biogeographic
realms, supports a remarkably diverse assemblage of psychoactive mushroom
species.

Colombia is situated at the northwestern corner of South America, where the
Andes mountain range splits into three cordilleras (Western, Central, and
Eastern), creating an extraordinary diversity of microclimates and
