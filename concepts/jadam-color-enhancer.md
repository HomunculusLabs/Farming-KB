# JADAM Natural Color Enhancer

When microorganisms [[query-can-fungi-break-down-plastic-pollution]] material containing large quantities of iodine
(I), the result is a natural color enhancer for fruits. Most seaweeds are good
sources; kelp is particularly effective.

## What It Enhances

- **Fruit color** -- improves red, blue, purple pigmentation
- Works throughout the growth period
- Enhances visual appeal and market quality of produce

## Recipe: Kelp JLF

### Ingredients
- Dried kelp: 10x its weight in water (10 kg per 100 L)
  - OR fresh kelp: fill **half** the container, then add water to fill
- A handful of [[leaf-mold-composting]]

### Method
1. Fill container with purslane, fill with water
2. Add [[comparison-leaf-mold-composting-vs-hot-composting-method]], close lid
3. Decomposition almost complete in **10 days**
4. High nitrogen content (0.20%) accelerates breakdown
5. Very common plant growing anywhere

### Analysis (per 100 L: purslane + water + leaf mold)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| pH | 8.4 |
| EC (1:5) ds/m | 13.89 |
| T-N % | 0.20 |
| K2O % | 0.133 |

## Application

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dilution | x30 to x500 |
| When to apply | Throughout growth |
| How to apply | Soil or foliar |
| What to expect | Enhances color |

## Key Notes

- Most types of seaweeds work as color enhancers (iodine-rich)
- Kelp is the most effective seaweed source
- Purslane works as a dual-purpose input: color enhancer + nitrogen source
- The white bubbles and stickiness from kelp fermentation are normal and
  temporary

## Other Color-Enhancing Materials

Any iodine-rich plant or seaweed works as a color enhancer. Beyond kelp and
purslane:
- Dulse, nori, wakame, and other edible seaweeds
- Sea lettuce (Ulva species)
- Bladderwrack
- Any wild seaweed gathered from clean coastal waters

The key is high iodine content -- when microorganisms break down
iodine-containing materials, the result enhances pigmentation in fruits.

## Combining with Other JLF

Color enhancer JLF can be mixed with any other JLF type. For best results:
- Apply alongside [[jadam-potassium-fertilizer]] for size + color
- Use with [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer]] -- base JLF method
- [[jadam-mineral-fertilizer]] -- mineral-based JLF (seaweed is mineral-rich)
