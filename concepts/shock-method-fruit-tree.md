# Shock Method for Fruit Tree Establishment

Sepp Holzer's "shock method" is an emergency technique that allows badly rooted trees without root balls to be replanted even when they are already fully in leaf, in flower, or bearing fruit. It originated from a childhood experience.

## Origin Story

As a child, Holzer found small wild apple trees on a pile of stones and brought them home. His mother scolded him, saying that with fully-grown leaves they would not take root at that time of year. Despite this, he planted them and removed all the leaves since they seemed to be stopping the trees from taking root. Weeks later, one tree produced new shoots — to everyone's surprise. This experience inspired the shock method.

## Procedure

1. **Lay trees in the sun**: Place the trees so that leaves dry out. Cover the roots with a wet jute sack — roots cannot tolerate sun.
2. **Do not water**: The wet sacks ensure roots do not dry out but will not provide enough water to supply the leaves.
3. **Wait about a day**: Until the leaves have dried out.
4. **Replant**: Do not soak the soil before planting or water the trees afterwards.
5. **Mulch**: The only protection is a layer of mulch to keep the soil moist.

## How It Works

Trees planted using this method quickly develop new, fibrous roots which supply the trees with nutrients and water again. They can survive the initial lean period because they no longer have any leaves or fruit to support. If a tree in full leaf and fruit were planted and not watered, all its energy would go to maintaining the leaves — the roots would not get enough attention.

Trees treated using the shock method concentrate on taking root and do not produce shoots until they have the energy to do so. The trees are raised to be independent. Trees planted this way grow best between [[holzer-raised-bed-design|raised beds]] where a large amount of moisture collects.

## Practical Application

Holzer has cultivated thousands of trees using this method. He buys remainder stock from tree nurseries at very good prices — stock that would otherwise be chopped up or burnt — and plants them using the shock method. After two to three years the trees have developed so well that they can be replanted or sold.
