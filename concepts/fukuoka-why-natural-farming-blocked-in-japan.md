Fukuoka is sometimes asked why no other farmers nearby have thought to try farming the way he does. A farmer does the same thing year after year, so even a period of forty or fifty years passes by in a flash. Fukuoka has never had the time to indulge in idle conversation on the pathways between rice paddies. The farmer next door simply says, "Muggy today, is it not" or "Chilly weather we are having," as he walks by. When he does take a sidelong glance at the field, he notices that it is always full of weeds, that it is lumpy and uneven with high and low spots.
