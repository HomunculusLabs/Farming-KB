---# Plant Nursery Establishment in Permaculture Systems

The plant nursery is the engine of permaculture establishment. In
*Permaculture: A Designers' Manual*, Bill Mollison repeatedly
emphasises that a small plant nursery is necessary to supply the
4,000-8,000 plants that can be placed on a hectare in a designed
system. Without nursery capacity, the designer cannot implement the
complex species assemblies that characterise mature permaculture.

## Why a Nursery Is Essential

When we construct a system of mixed tree, shrub, and vegetable crop
utilising livestock as foragers, so many species and individuals of
each species are needed that it is usually necessary to first create
a small plant nursery to supply them. While these are growing in
their pots, we can fence and prepare the soil, and then plant them
out to a carefully-designed long-term plan.
