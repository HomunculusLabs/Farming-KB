# Choke Berry — Jack the Ripper × Querkle (TGA Subcool)

Choke Berry is a hybrid strain created by Subcool of TGA Genetics that
combines the legendary potency of Jack the Ripper with the colorful, flavorful
Querkle. The resulting cross produces a strain with dark purple buds and a
complex flavor profile that blends the sharp lemon-haze terpenes of Jack the
Ripper with the grape and berry notes of Querkle. Despite the indica-leaning
Querkle parent, Choke Berry retains a sativa-dominant effect profile that makes
it suitable for daytime medical use.

## Breeder and Genetics

- **Breeder**: Subcool / TGA Genetics, USA
- **Genetics**: Jack the Ripper × Querkle
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant
- **Potency**: THC 20%

### Parent Strains

**Jack the Ripper** contributes extreme potency and citrus terpenes:

- Tested at 26.6% THC — one of the highest-potency sativa-dominant hybrids
- Lemon Haze aroma with tropical and pine undertones
- Intense, soaring cerebral high with THCV content
- Genetics trace through Jack's Cleaner, Space Queen, Romulan, Lamb's Bread,
  [[northern-lights]] #5, Purple Haze, and Jack Herer

**Querkle** brings color, berry flavor, and indica density:

- A TGA Genetics creation combining Space Queen with a purple indica male
- Known for deep purple coloration and grape-like aroma
- Provides bud density and weight that complements Jack the Ripper's airy
  structure
- The "purple" parent that gives Choke Berry its dark coloration

The cross is somewhat incestuous from a breeding perspective — both parents
share Space Queen genetics — which helped Subcool stabilize the desired traits
more quickly than with an outcross to an unrelated line.

## Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

Choke Berry exhibits a **medium height** growth habit that is more manageable
than the extremely tall Jack the Ripper:

- More compact than Jack the Ripper thanks to Querkle's indica influence
- Branching is strong with multiple bud sites
- Leaf structure shows a mix of sativa thinness and indica width
- Overall vigor is excellent — the hybrid cross produces robust, healthy plants

### Cultivation Method

TGA Genetics recommends organic soil growing for Choke Berry:

- Organic soil produces superior flavor development compared to hydroponic
  methods
- The strain performs well in both container and raised bed systems
- Moderate [[cannabis-soil-and-nutrient-requirements]] — avoid overfeeding which can reduce flavor
  complexity
- Querkle parentage means the plant can handle slightly cooler nighttime
  temperatures during late flowering, which enhances the purple coloration

### Flowering

- **Flowering time**: 8 weeks — relatively short for a sativa-dominant
  strain, inherited from the Querkle parent
- **Color development**: Dark purple hues emerge during weeks 5–8 of
  flowering, especially with cooler night temperatures (55–65°F)
- **Bud density**: Dense, heavy buds — a significant improvement over the
  Jack the Ripper parent's tendency toward airy bud structure
- **Trichome production**: Heavy [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] coating the dark purple
  buds creates a striking visual contrast
- **Indoor harvest**: Week 8
- **Outdoor harvest**: Late September to early October

The combination of purple coloration with heavy resin production makes Choke
Berry one of the more visually striking strains in the TGA Genetics catalog.
The dark purple buds covered in white trichomes create a dramatic appearance.

### Yield

- **Indoor yield**: Moderate to heavy — the improved bud density from Querkle
  means higher weight per plant than Jack the Ripper
