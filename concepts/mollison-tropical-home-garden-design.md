## Structural and Craft Materials

Bamboos, balsa, teak, palms, and mahogany provide structural
and craft materials. Rattans can be encouraged along waterways
and in mangrove edges. Oil palm, jelly palm, Bactris, Mauritia,
salak palm, and doum palm provide trusses of useful fruits.
The garden thus provides not only food but the materials for
shelter, tools, and trade goods.

## Pest Management

Large insect pests (locust, cicadas, sucking bugs) are plentiful;
guinea-fowl or chickens on range provide some defense. Native
rodents and pigs can be damaging, and pythons rather than foxes
take poultry. Termites and ants largely replace worms in
soil-building, and buildings must be constructed to resist them.

## Grass Suppression

The value of surface mulch in weed suppression is a major factor
in lowering garden work. Any mulch should be thickly applied
20-25 cm (8-10 inches) deep when first establishing home
gardens. Later mulch can be derived from green herbage and
borders or windbreaks. A thick mulch almost totally eradicates
