## Natural Farming Pest Control

Natural Farming pest control, as developed by Dr. Cho Han Kyu, takes a
fundamentally different approach from conventional pesticide-based
methods. Instead of attempting to eradicate pests with toxic chemicals,
the system focuses on coexistence, plant health as [[cho-nutritive-cycle-theory]] — when
plants receive incorrect nutrition at the wrong growth stage, they
become weak and emit [[nutritive-cycle-theory]]. When plants receive the correct balance
of nutrients at each growth stage, they develop strong cell walls,
produce natural defensive compounds, and are less attractive to
herbivorous insects and disease organisms.

Key inputs that strengthen plant defenses include:
- **[[nutritive-cycle-theory]]. When plants are over-fertilized with
nitrogen during the reproductive stage, they produce soft, succulent
growth that is highly attractive to aphids and other sucking insects.
When phosphorus is deficient during the cross-over period, plants
become stressed and emit volatile signals that attract pests. By
correcting the nutritional imbalance, the pest problem often resolves
itself without any direct intervention.
