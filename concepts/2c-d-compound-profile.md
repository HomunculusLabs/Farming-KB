# 2C-D (LE-25) — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Methylphenethylamine

## Overview

2C-D (PIHKAL #23), also known as LE-25, is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine in the 2C family. Shulgin held 2C-D in particularly high regard, calling it the "pharmacological tofu" of psychedelics -- it mixes well with other substances and is remarkably forgiving. Its gentle nature and wide [[blesching-cannabis-therapeutic-window-dosing]] make it one of the safest and most controllable members of the 2C series.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylphenethylamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #23
- **Code name:** LE-25
- **Class:** 2C family (substituted phenethylamine)
- **HCl melting point:** 213-214 deg C
- **Hydrobromide salt mp:** 183-184 deg C
- **Acetamide mp:** 116-117 deg C (from free base in pyridine treated with acetic anhydride)

## Synthesis (from PIHKAL)

Synthesized from toluhydroquinone, which is dimethylated with dimethyl sulfate to yield 2,5-dimethoxytoluene. The minor phenolic byproduct (2-methyl-4-methoxyphenol, mp 66-68 deg C) can be ethylated to yield 2-ethoxy-5-methoxytoluene, which leads to the ethoxy homologue 2CD-5ETO (one of the Tweetios) and the three-carbon Classic Lady IRIS.

The dimethoxytoluene is formylated via Vilsmeier reaction (POCl3 + N-methylformanilide) to yield 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylbenzaldehyde (mp 83-84 deg C). The Gattermann aldehyde synthesis gave better yield (60% of theory) but required hydrogen cyanide gas. The aldehyde undergoes nitrostyrene condensation with nitromethane and ammonium acetate (mp 117-118 deg C for the nitrostyrene), followed by LAH reduction to yield 2C-D as the hydrochloride salt.

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Threshold** | ~10 mg |
| **Common dose** | 20-60 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 60-150 mg |
| **Duration** | 4-6 hours |
| **Onset** | ~45 minutes |

2C-D has an unusually wide active dose range (20-150 mg) -- a remarkable window compared to the typical 2x window of most psychedelics. This forgiving dose-response curve contributes to its [[cannabis-safety-profile-and-overdose-risk]].

## Effects

- Gentle, controllable [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]]
- Enhanced color perception and visual clarity without intense hallucinations
- Calm, centered mental state
- Useful for creative work and problem-solving
- Can be used as a "primer" at low doses to enhance the effects of other psychedelics
- Minimal body load
- Easy to navigate -- described as "smooth"
- Shulgin recommended it for those new to the 2C family

## Qualitative Comments (from PIHKAL)

**(10 mg):** "There is something going on, but it is subtle. I find that I can just slightly redirect my attention so that it applies more exactly to what I am doing. I feel that I can learn faster. This is a 'smart' pill!"

**(20 mg):** "Butterflies in stomach whole time. OK. This is about the right level. In retrospect, not too interesting. Primarily a stimulant, not entirely physically pleasant."

**(30 mg):** "I was into it quite quickly (not much over three-quarters of an hour) and got up to a ++ by the end of an hour. There is something unsatisfactory about trying to classify this level."

**(45 mg):** "There was a rocket from the half-hour to the one and a half hour, from nothing up to a +++. Somehow the intimacy and the erotic never quite knit. Talking is extremely easy, but something is missing. I am down by the fifth hour, and sleep is comfortable. This compound will take some learning."

**(75 mg):** "This is a +++, but the emphasis is on talking, not on personal interacting. I am putting out, but my boundaries are intact. Communication was excellent. This is fast on, but not too long lived. Maybe a therapy tool?"

**(150 mg):** "A truly remarkable psychedelic, one which could compare favorably with 2C-B. There are intense colors, and I feel that more would be too much."

## Extensions and Commentary

Shulgin called 2C-D "pharmacological tofu" -- it doesn't seem to do too much by itself, always teasing, until you get to heroic levels. But experimental therapists said it was excellent in extending the action of other materials, boosting the waning action of another drug without adding its own color to the experience. The 150 mg report above comes from therapeutic research in Germany (where it is called LE-25).

This compound is the most dramatic example of the loss of potency from an amphetamine (DOM, active at maybe 3 milligrams) to a phenethylamine (only one tenth as active). The next higher homologue, 2c-e-compound-profile|2C-E, is maximally active at 15-20 milligrams and is, without question, a complete psychedelic.

The N-monomethyl and N,N-dimethyl homologues have been synthesized. The N-monomethyl compound had a mp of 150-151 deg C; the N,N-dimethyl compound had a mp of 168-169 deg C. These two were some ten times less effective in interfering with conditioned responses in experimental rats and were never explored in humans.

## Tweetios (Ethoxy Homologues)

The ethoxy homologues of the 2C-X series have been collectively called the "Tweetios." In general, the 2-EtO- compounds have shorter duration and lower potency, the 5-EtO- compounds have relatively unchanged potency and longer duration, and the 2,5-di-EtO- homologues are very weak, if active at all. The 2-EtO-homologue of 2C-D (2CD-2ETO) showed feelings of closeness between couples at 60 mg without appreciable visual effects.

## Pharmacology

2C-D is a 5-HT2A agonist with relatively low affinity compared to more potent 2C compounds. Its gentle profile and wide therapeutic window reflect moderate [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]]. As a phenethylamine, it lacks stimulant properties. See [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]].

## Harm Reduction

- One of the safest 2C compounds due to wide therapeutic window
- No stimulant effect -- less cardiovascular concern
- Short to moderate duration (4-6 hours)
- Often recommended as an introductory psychedelic phenethylamine
- Related compounds: [[2c-e-compound-profile]], [[shulgin-mda-compound-profile]]
