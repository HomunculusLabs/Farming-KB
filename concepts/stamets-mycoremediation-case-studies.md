## Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs)

### PAHs (Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons)

White rot fungi produce manganese peroxidases that are particularly efficient at breaking down PAHs:
- These compounds are common products of incomplete combustion
- They contaminate former industrial sites (gasworks, smelters, refineries)
- Conventional remediation requires excavation and incineration
- Fungal approaches can treat PAHs in situ, dramatically reducing cost

### Pesticides and Herbicides

The similarity between the hydrogen-carbon bonds in plant materials and those in synthetic pesticides means fungal enzymes can degrade many agricultural chemicals:
- DDT, aldrin, chlordane, atrazine
- The bonds holding plant material together are similar to those in petroleum products
- Fungal metabolism is the reverse of photosynthesis — more than 50% of organic mass becomes CO2 and water

### Application Strategy

The mycoremediation approach is elegantly simple:
1. Overlay straw or wood chips infused with the appropriate mycelium to create a living membrane of enzymes
2. This membrane rains enzymes down on toxins in the topsoil
3. Replenish annually with additional mycelium-treated substrate
4. Several sequential applications may be necessary to reduce toxins to acceptable levels
