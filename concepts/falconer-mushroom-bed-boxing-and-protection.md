# Boxing and Protecting Mushroom Beds

The boxing of mushroom beds is a practice employed to
protect the [[mushroom-growing-temperature-and-moisture]], and to drying out. Boxing or
curtain-covering mitigates these problems.

## Mulching with Straw and Hay

Covering the beds with hay or straw is another protection
method, particularly valuable for maintaining proper
[[mushroom-house-construction-duggar]], so that they may retain
their heat for a long time. This is essential because the
fermenting manure provides the primary heat source, and
without adequate depth and insulation, cold will penetrate
and stagnate the crop.

## Covering Against Condensation Drip

One of the chief troubles with flat-roofed [[sustaining-mushroom-crop-flushing-harvesting]] from
cold drips that can chill and check the growth of the
mushrooms.

## The Importance of Ventilation with Boxing

While boxing confines heat and moisture, it must not
completely exclude fresh air. The cellars can be ventilated
without interfering with the welfare of the mushrooms when
proper boxing is employed. The lid arrangement allows the
grower to ventilate the general cellar space while keeping
the mushroom bed itself protected from cold draughts that
would harm the crop.
