# 2C-D

LE-25; 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-methylphenethylamine

## Overview

2C-D is a member [[allegro-death-and-resurrection-in-the-mushroom-cult]] phenethylamine pharmacopeia as a communication-facilitating and therapy-oriented tool. At heroic doses (150 mg), it becomes a fully-fledged psychedelic comparable to 2c d [[beatrice-pihkal-entry-11]] 23.

## Effects and Qualitative Commentary

### 10 mg ("Smart Pill" Level)
Subtle but noticeable. Users report an ability to "slightly redirect attention" more precisely to the task at hand. A feeling of enhanced learning capacity. Described as a "smart pill" -- one [[shulgin-a-pihkal-2c-t-7-25-dimethoxy-4-propylthiophenethylamine]] of potency loss from the amphetamine to the phenethylamine form. [DOM (the amphetamine homologue) is active at approximately 3 mg, while 2C-D requires 20-60 mg for comparable effects -- roughly one-tenth the potency. This pattern of reduced potency in the 2-carbon vs 3-carbon form is common but particularly pronounced in the methyl-substituted series.

### Tweetio Homologues (Ethoxy Variants)

The "Tweetio" compounds (2-ethoxy variants) of 2C-D have been evaluated:

- **2CD-2ETO** (2-ethoxy-5-methoxy-4-methylphenethylamine): At 60 mg, produces closeness between couples without appreciable intoxication. Duration ~4 hours. Shorter duration, lower potency than parent.
- **2CD-5ETO** (5-ethoxy-2-methoxy-4-methylphenethylamine): At 40-50 mg, slow gradual climb over ~2 hours. Experience is free from excitement but with friendly openness, easy talk, humor, and healthy appetite. Duration ~12 hours.
- **2CD-2,5-DIETO** (2,5-diethoxy-4-methylphenethylamine): At 55 mg, only a +1. Effects gone after 4 hours. Very weak.

### N-Alkyl Homologues

The N-monomethyl and N,N-dimethyl homologues have been synthesized but are approximately ten times less effective than 2C-D in interfering with conditioned responses in experimental rats. Neither has been explored in humans.

## Synthesis Summary

Synthesized from toluhydroquinone via methylation with dimethyl sulfate to give 2,5-dimethoxytoluene. Vilsmeier-Haack formylation (POCl3/N-methylformanilide) gives 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylbenzaldehyde (mp 83-84 C). Condensation with nitromethane gives the nitrostyrene (mp 117-118 C), which is reduced with LAH in THF. The product is distilled (bp 90-115 C at 0.3 mm/Hg) and converted to HCl salt (mp 213-214 C).
