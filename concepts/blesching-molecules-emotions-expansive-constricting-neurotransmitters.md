# Molecules of Emotion — Expansive vs Constricting Neurotransmitters

## Overview

In Chapter II of The Cannabis Health Index, Blesching maps specific
neurotransmitters to emotional states, positioning the endocannabinoid
system (ECS) as the critical bridge between molecular biology and
emotional experience. The model divides neurochemicals into two
categories: **expansive** (openness, healing, well-being) and
**constricting** (stress, defense, disease progression).

## The ECS as Molecular-Emotional Bridge

trigger neurochemical cascades; neurochemical levels shape emotional

the brain govern emotion, memory, and perception, while CB2 receptors in

### Dopamine — "I Am Motivated"
Essential for motivation and reward, but in excess or certain contexts
can become constricting — compulsive, anxious, or externally directed,
interfering with the receptive state needed for deep healing.

### Norepinephrine — "I Am Attentive!"
Governs alertness and vigilance. Chronic elevation sustains the
sympathetic stress response, diverting resources from healing toward
threat detection.
