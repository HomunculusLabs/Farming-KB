## Duck Keeping Detailed Guide

Ducks are the gentlest and most versatile poultry. They eat algae and weeds from ponds, slugs, snails, grubs, soft greens, grasses, water plants, small tree greens, and grains while fertilizing water and soil. They need less care and feeding than chickens but more planning.

## System Integration

- Best in enclosed area around small pond with island for nesting (Zone 2)
- 25 ducks per acre of pond surface
- Forage garden around the pond works well
- Duck manure feeds algae-eating organisms and helps grow pond plants
- Can be opened into Zone 1 garden to eat slugs/pests when plants are at least duck-sized
- Must separate from other animals' water sources (ducks make water too dirty)

## Housing

- 4 square feet (0.37 sq m) per duck
- Three-sided shelter near pond during day
- Night: barn room with straw bed, or simple shed
- Shed needs human-access door for harvesting fertilized straw
- Ducks can fly -- clip one wing's long feathers when they first grow and after each molting
- Use big scissors; do not cut during molting (risk fatal bleeding)

## Feeding

- With adequate water, grassy yard, and forage garden: minimal extra feed needed
- Ducks need young grass; will quickly destroy a small unvaried pasture
