## The Carbon-Nitrogen Dynamic and Humus Formation

When you amend soil with organic material having a carbon-nitrogen ratio
higher than 12:1, the soil microbial population burns the excess carbon
for fuel while preserving the nitrogen. At the end of this process,
there will be a higher level of humus in the soil than there was before
the amendment. In simple terms, the soil becomes healthier and
develops better tilth.

Conversely, if you put something into the soil with a C/N below 12:1,
the resulting formation of nitrates encourages soil microbes to
multiply. More of them means they more aggressively attack existing
humus. The end result is a soil with a slightly lower quantity of
