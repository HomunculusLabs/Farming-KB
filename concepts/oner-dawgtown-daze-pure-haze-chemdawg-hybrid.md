# Dawgtown Daze: Pure Haze × ChemDawg Hybrid

## Overview

Dawgtown Daze is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by California Cannetics
for the SoCal Seed Collective. It represents a significant breeding
achievement: the combination of a pure, original Haze female with a
ChemDawg male. For cannabis enthusiasts and breeders, the parentage
alone makes this strain noteworthy — both Haze and ChemDawg are
foundational genetics that have shaped modern cannabis.

## Genetics and Lineage

The female parent is a pure Haze — one of the most legendary sativa
lines in cannabis history, originally developed in Northern California
in the 1970s from a complex mix of Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and
Afghani genetics. Haze is renowned for its extremely long flowering
time (often 12–16 weeks), tall lanky growth, and powerful psychedelic
cerebral high.

The male parent is a ChemDawg — a strain whose origin is itself the
subject of legendary stories involving [[oner-chemdawg-origin-legend-grateful-dead-genetics]] followers and
found seeds at a Grateful Dead concert. ChemDawg is known for its
intense chemical/fuel aroma and potent effects.

The resulting Dawgtown Daze is over 90% sativa, inheriting the
cerebral intensity of both parents. The ChemDawg male contributes
a more manageable [[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]] and adds density to the typically
airy Haze buds.

## Growth Characteristics

As a 90%+ [[dts-multi-line-sativa-hybrid-cheeze-mk-ultra-diesel-haze]], Dawgtown Daze grows tall and flowers long.
It is best suited to outdoor cultivation where its natural stature
can be accommodated, though it can be grown indoors with proper Low
[[low-stress-training]] (LST). The plant exhibits significant stretch after
the flip to flowering, so indoor growers should start flowering at a
smaller size than they might with indica-dominant strains.

The plant has classic Haze morphology during [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]: long
internodal spacing, serrated leaflets, and vigorous upward growth. As
flowering progresses, Haze traits become more pronounced with the
development of foxtailed bud structures that accelerate toward harvest.
Support is essential — the tall, lanky branches need staking or
trellising to prevent collapse under bud weight.

Multiple phenotypes are expressed, with flowering times ranging from
10 weeks to as long as 16 weeks. Breeders recommend patience and
allowing each plant to fully mature rather than rushing the harvest.

## Aroma and Flavor

The aroma is distinctly Haze-dominant: that familiar pungent, spicy,
and slightly sweet scent that Haze enthusiasts recognize instantly.
The ChemDawg influence adds an undertone of fuel and chemical
sharpness that complements the Haze character.

Bud appearance features white, green, orange, and occasional purple
hues. The foxtailed bud structure is visually distinctive and becomes
more pronounced as plants approach full maturity.

## Potency and Effects

With THC tested at 19.23% and minimal CBD (0.22%), Dawgtown Daze is
a potent, almost exclusively THC-driven experience. The high is
described as powerful and soaring — a long journey through cerebral
space that reflects both the Haze and ChemDawg heritage. This is not
a strain for the inexperienced or those seeking mild effects.

The ChemDawg influence may add a subtle body component to what is
otherwise a very heady, cerebral experience. Duration of effects is
extended, consistent with both parent strains. Onset is relatively
rapid, building in waves that peak approximately 20–30 minutes after
consumption. The experience is long-lasting and deeply immersive.

## Cultivation Notes

- Best suited to outdoor cultivation in warm climates
- [[cannabis-indoor-growing-basics]] requires LST and adequate vertical space
- Multiple phenotypes with variable flowering times (10–16 weeks)
- Low odor during late flowering — unusual for such potent strains
- Check grow room exterior daily for escaping aroma
- Stake or trellis early to support branch development
