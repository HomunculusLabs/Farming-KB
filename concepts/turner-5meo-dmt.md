# 5-MeO-DMT

> Derived from the DMT chapter of The Essential Psychedelics Guide. Intensity: 9 to 10.

## Overview

5-Methoxy-Dimethyltryptamine (5-MeO-DMT) is one of two primary forms of DMT discussed by Turner. It is a naturally occurring psychedelic found in a variety of plants and is also produced in the human pineal gland. It is an **MAO inhibitor**.

## Safety

5-MeO-DMT is an MAO inhibitor - see contraindicated substances list. Since it takes effect almost immediately, users should lie down or recline before taking a hit.

## Dosage

- **5 to 10 mg** - full psychedelic dose
- The tiny amount required can only be weighed on a sophisticated scale
- Frequently sold soaked into parsley flakes or cannabis (must burn the plant material to consume)
- The entire dose must be consumed within ~60 seconds

## Comparison to N,N-DMT

### 5-MeO-DMT: "Sheer Force"
5-MeO-DMT is the **more potent** of the two in terms of:
- Size of dose required (5-10 mg vs ~40 mg)
- How quickly it comes on
- How forcefully it blows apart one's universe

On 5-MeO, one can literally feel the mind **exploding and expanding outward** to encompass first the area nearby, then the planet, and eventually the cosmos - over a span of about 15 seconds.

### Visuals
5-MeO does not produce as spectacular visuals as N,N-DMT. Most frequently the visuals are:
- Intensely complex, multidimensional, quickly-moving **geometric designs**
- Primarily **black and white, checkerboard style**
- At times some color, and on occasion abundantly colorful (especially with large doses)
- Taking a hit just before orgasm can make visuals abundantly colorful

### Physical Effects
- Increases **pulse rate and blood pressure** more than N,N-DMT (but not as much as amyl nitrite)
- Can produce **breathing irregularities** which may take getting used to
- "Physically manifesting energies" are more forceful/gentler on N,N-DMT
- If one doesn't get a good dose, they will feel only bodily symptoms and slight visual alteration

### Duration
- Peak: ~2-3 minutes (alone)
- Back to baseline: ~20 minutes (alone)
- With Harmala: peak extends to 10-20 minutes, total 40 min to 2 hours

## Key Differences from N,N-DMT

| Attribute | 5-MeO-DMT | N,N-DMT |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Character | Sheer force | Sheer perfection |
| Potency | More potent (smaller dose) | Less potent (larger dose) |
| Onset | Extremely rapid, forceful | Slightly slower, enchanting |
| Visuals | Geometric, B&W checkerboard | Spectacular, colorful, kaleidoscopic |
| Entity contact | Less common | More common (gnomes, elves, discarnate entities) |
| Ego dissolution | Total, immediate | Leaves portion of ego intact |
| Fear potential | Can awe or frighten with power | Can delight but also produce deepest fear |
| Anesthetic quality | Less pronounced | Unique "selective anesthetic" property |
| Physical energy | More forceful | Gentler |

## 5-MeO-DMT with Harmala Alkaloids

Harmala only **slightly** intensifies 5-MeO-DMT but significantly **prolongs** the experience:
- Peak extends from 2-3 minutes to 10-20 minutes
- Total experience: 40 minutes to 2 hours
- Still need ~5 mg for a powerful high

**Warning:** The combination of 5-MeO-DMT and Harmala produces the **most powerful experience** Turner has ever felt - both physically and mentally overwhelming. High percentage of intensely unpleasant experiences, including violent nausea, extreme discomfort, convulsions. Turner and most acquaintances reverted to taking 5-MeO-DMT alone.

Since Harmala slows the onset so drastically, much of the brilliance experienced with 5-MeO alone is lost. Some users feel the DMT high is "warbly" in combination with Harmala and lacks the "Diamond Consciousness" of DMT alone.

## Small-Dose Experience

A small dose of 5-MeO produces only bodily symptoms and a slight alteration of consciousness, especially in the visual field. In contrast, a small dose of N,N-DMT will still produce a psychedelic trip (without the intensity and depth of a full dose).
