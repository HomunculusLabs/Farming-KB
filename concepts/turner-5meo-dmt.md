## 5-MeO-DMT with Harmala Alkaloids

Harmala only **slightly** intensifies 5-MeO-DMT but significantly **prolongs** the experience:
- Peak extends from 2-3 minutes to 10-20 minutes
- Total experience: 40 minutes to 2 hours
- Still need ~5 mg for a powerful high

**Warning:** The combination of 5-MeO-DMT and Harmala produces the **most powerful experience** Turner has ever felt - both physically and mentally overwhelming. High percentage of intensely unpleasant experiences, including violent nausea, extreme discomfort, convulsions. Turner and most acquaintances reverted to taking 5-MeO-DMT alone.

Since Harmala slows the onset so drastically, much of the brilliance experienced with 5-MeO alone is lost. Some users feel the DMT high is "warbly" in combination with Harmala and lacks the "Diamond Consciousness" of DMT alone.

## Small-Dose Experience

A small dose of 5-MeO produces only bodily symptoms and a slight alteration of consciousness, especially in the visual field. In contrast, a small dose of N,N-DMT will still produce a psychedelic trip (without the intensity and depth of a full dose).
