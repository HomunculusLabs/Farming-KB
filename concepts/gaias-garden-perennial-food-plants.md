## Roots and Tubers

Perennial root crops provide calorie-dense food with minimal effort:

- **[[oxalis-tuberosa]]*): tangy, colorful tubers; productive in cool
  climates and short seasons.
- **Yacon** (*Smallanthus sonchifolius*): sweet, crisp tubers related to
  sunflowers; contains inulin, making it suitable for diabetics.

These crops are adapted to harsh mountain conditions and may perform well in
temperate gardens. They represent a largely untapped resource for perennial food
production.

## "Perennialized" Annuals

Some annual plants can be managed as quasi-perennials through self-seeding or
vegetative propagation:

- **Leeks** (*Allium ampeloprasum*): some varieties produce bulbils that can
  be replanted indefinitely.
- **Self-seeding greens:** arugula, cilantro, lettuce, mustard, and
  lamb's quarters will naturalize in disturbed soil and return year after year
  without replanting.
- **Chard** (*Beta vulgaris* var. cicla*): biennial that often persists for
  two or more years in mild climates.
