### D-Handled Spading Fork
The spading fork is used for loosening soil to 12 inches depth,
aerating compacted subsoil, and sifting in fertilizers. To loosen
soil, dig the fork in to its full depth and push the handle
downward so the tines lever through the soil, loosening and
aerating it. For compacted soil, lift out tight sections on the
fork pan, then jerk upward to break them apart as they fall
against the tines.

### Digging Board
A 3/4-inch-thick plywood board, 2-3 feet long by 3-5 feet wide,
is placed on top of the bed to stand on while digging. This
displaces your weight over a larger area, preventing unnecessary
soil compaction. Advance the board along the bed 1 foot at a time
as you prepare each new trench. The board also serves as a
planting board during [[jeavons-double-digging-bed-preparation]], [[jeavons-transplanting-techniques]], [[jeavons-seed-propagation-techniques]]
