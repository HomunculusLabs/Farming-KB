# Permaculture Community Structures and Legal Organizations

updatebill-mollison

Pamphlets XIII and XIV of the [[permaculture-design]] Course provide detailed models for organizing permaculture communities, including [[holmgren-permaculture-land-tenure-and-legal-structures]], trust systems, financial management, and practical enterprise models. Bill Mollison presents a working model used by [[mollison-permaculture-two-philosophy-and-tagari-community]] in Australia.

## The Community Model

### Core Structure
A permaculture community is organized around three ethical principles:
1. **Earth care**
2. **People care**
3. **Non-profiteering** -- not accumulating wealth beyond one's needs

The community forms a **company** (a perfectly legal entity existing in all nations). Part of the function of all companies is to act as a **trustee**. The company does not trade, nor does it take any cash flow in or out. It issues shares of one dollar to each member. All members can be directors.

### The Institute Trust (Public Trust)
The Permaculture Institute (the name of this company) exists to work in areas of **health, education, and agriculture**. This gives the company a broad spectrum basis of acting.

Under trust drawn up according to the laws of your country, it adds "for the good of all Americans" (or equivalent). This publicly oriented trust confers advantages:
- Immunity from land taxes, rates, and local government charges
- Automatic membership in [[hallucinogenic-plants-of-the-world]] Free [[permaculture-designers-manual-seed-exchange]]** (4,600 institutes, 680 botanical gardens)
- Joint research with any other institute of its kind
- Links with funding staff arrangements
- Insulated from corporate risk -- if this fails, so do schools, churches, and public institutions, making it nearly unheard of for it to be raided

### The Trading Trust (Non-Profit Trading Corporation)
This is a **second trust**, not connected with the Institute Trust, under the company's trusteeship. It is the key innovation of the model:

- Occupies structures loaned to it by the Institute at no cost
- Staffed free of charge by members of the community
- Owns no buildings, employs no staff, has no chattels
- Borrows all moveable fixtures, furnishings, and usable objects from a political party or small external society

**At the Trading Trust, there is nobody and nothing [[query-how-to-make-cannabis-tinctures-at-home]].** No risk is incurred. This mirrors the structure of merchant banks worldwide.

## Trading Trust Businesses

Good businesses for the Trading Trust include:

### Travel Agencies
- Many people in the network need to travel
- Agents may get 15% discount, free tickets, or free flights
- Export remissions: Australian law refunds 75% of fares for exporters, plus accommodation costs

### Publishing Company
- Information is vital -- it is what the community lives on
- Pre-order model: solicit pre-orders, spend all pre-order money on printing
- Every book sold on pre-order funds additional copies

### Consultancy
- Transmit information on a global scale by people
- Government remits fares for "knowledge exports"

### Real Estate
- Control what happens across a lot of land without necessarily owning it
- Agents can manage properties, earning management fees ($1,000-$2,000/year per property)
- Qualified agents can oversee properties without taking risk

### Seed Company
- Deals only in open-pollinated seeds from which people can save their own seed
- Distributed growing network: growers collect seed, stamp envelopes, return to cataloger
- Catalog lists plants by function: Bee Forages, Chicken Forages, Cattle Forages, Deer Forages, Fireproof Plants, by zone
- Every seed packet includes instructions on how to save your own seed
- "We are selling self-reliance"

### Manufacturing
- Plow manufacturing: components out to three small manufacturers, assembly by a central fabricator
- "Twigger" (bent wire tool): give manufacturing to a small group in an urban center
- Contract out specialized components to repetition engineers

## Financial Flow

### Income Sources
1. Trading efforts (businesses run by the Trading Trust)
2. Government business aids (automatic, no forms required):
   - Export fare remissions (up to 90% of travel costs)
   - Full refund on development of any salable invention
   - Government grants for consultancy/knowledge exports
3. Public donations (occasionally substantial amounts)
4. Political party flow (equals cost of typewriters, desks, vehicles)

### Profit Distribution
The Trading Trust distributes profits in four ways:
1. **Far the greater amount** to the Research Institute (double tax exemption: not taxable, and tax deductible)
2. To public charity (another tax deduction) -- aboriginal ethnic groups, etc.
3. Minor flow to political party (exactly equals equipment costs)
4. "Thin miserable stream" to workers: $21/week per person (Tagari model)

### Tax Advantages
- Both trusts are tax immune
- Donations to them are often tax deductible
- Money can route through any other institute with tax deductibility (2-4% handling charge)
- Workers live below minimum income level, so they pay no tax
- The community is "the most heavily self-taxed group" -- all money beyond $21/week goes to the public

## Land Acquisition

### How Land Comes In
- Trading operations buy properties, pay them off, then gift to the Institute
- Public donations of land
- Government grants (land local government doesn't want)
- Cooperative/community shares (e.g., $1,000 share plus a contour design job)
- Offers from people in Britain, France, Africa, Australia, and islands

"There is absolutely no problem in 12 months in getting hold of several million dollars worth of land -- I mean hundreds of square miles of land."

### Land Goes Out
- To communities, cooperatives, or small groups to administer
- To single individuals (limited tenancy, life tenancy inheritable, or transferable tenancy)
- As public land, open to the public
- For health retreats, educational purposes, preservation, or development

### Risk Management
- All land donations go to the Trading Trust, not the Institute
- Trading Trust pays all transfer costs
- Part-owned properties lie in the risk area
- Only fully owned, unencumbered property passes into the Institute
- The Institute will not accept properties with any attachments/claims

## Legal Support
- In Australia, the legal profession makes no charge to public interest groups
- Excellent international lawyers available free through the lawyer association
- Free typist, offices, duplicating equipment in all capital cities

## Housing
- Unpaid houses in the Trading Trust are rented to community members
- Rent is remitted if members work within the Trading Trust
- If they leave, rent is incurred even if they donated the house
- Houses may be given to community members on life lease (inheritable or transferable)

## The Permaculture Alternative (Pamphlet XIV)

### Philosophy
Mollison grew up in a village with no rich or poor: "You had no fear of starving, nor any hope of becoming rich." The alternative movement failed because "it didn't offer an alternative." People asked, "Can you employ me?" and the answer was, "No, I can't, because what I am in isn't employment."

The shift: "While I cannot employ you, I can give you a job in which you can earn your way, and you can leave your present job right now."
