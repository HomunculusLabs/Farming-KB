## Multi-Functional Benefits

Natural swimming pools serve many purposes beyond recreation:

- Fire-fighting reserves for the property
- Potential heat sources for [[mollison-fire-control-design]] spread
- Emergency water supplies rechargeable from the roof
- Recycled by photovoltaic pumps

This multi-functional approach eliminates the "endless servicing" of
chemical pools and replaces it with an ecologically productive system.

## Construction Principles

The design involves water pumped down through base gravels, cleansed in
reed beds, aerated via a [[mollison-sewage-treatment-natural-processes]] palette:

- Phragmites communis and Typha species: flocculate colloids,
  dry out sludges, eliminate pathogens
- Schoenoplectus species: take up copper, cobalt, nickel, manganese
- Scirpus species: break down phenols including toxic pentachlorophenol

## Maintenance Considerations

Unlike chemical pools, natural swimming pools require:

- Monitoring of E. Coli levels (which are naturally lower than in
  chlorinated systems)
- Occasional plant harvesting to remove accumulated nutrients
- Flowform or aeration maintenance to ensure oxygenation
- Leaf skimming and gravel bed care
- Seasonal plant management
- Gravel bed cleaning every 12-18 months (surface sands can be
  washed or roasted)

The result is a self-regulating system that becomes more stable and
productive over time, as opposed to conventional pools that require
constant chemical input and deteriorate without it. Mollison concludes
with a simple farewell: "Goodbye to the endless servicing, and perhaps
hello to an occasional" productive swim in living water.

## Health Benefits

Natural swimming pools avoid the documented health risks of chlorinated
water. Chlorine forms carcinogenic chloroform (CHCl3) when it contacts
organic matter in water, a risk entirely eliminated by biological
filtration. The skin-friendly water, free of chemical irritants, makes
natural pools particularly beneficial for children and those with
sensitivities. The presence of fish, crayfish, and diverse aquatic life
provides an educational resource and psychological benefit that sterile
blue pools cannot offer.
