## Conclusion

The evidence is clear: aerobic compost tea delivers measurable benefits across all five criteria, while anaerobic compost tea fails every one. Anaerobic brews may appear to offer short-term plant protection through toxicity, but this comes at the cost of nutrient loss, soil degradation, and potential health hazards. For growers seeking to build long-term [[query-what-is-the-role-of-actinomycetes-in-soil-health-and-decomposition]] sustainable fertility, actively aerated compost tea is the only recommended approach.
