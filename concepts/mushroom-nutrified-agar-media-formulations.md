# Mushroom Nutrified Agar Media Formulations

## Overview

Many formulations have been developed for cultivating mushrooms on semi-solid agar media. Agar is a seaweed-derived compound that gelatinizes water. Nutrients are added to the agar-water base which, after sterilization, promote healthy [[grass-seed-substrate-for-mushroom-cultivation]].

## The Role of Agar

Agar coagulates water when added in excess of 10 grams per 1000 ml H₂O. Only high-grade agar should be used. Various agars differ substantially in their ability to gelatinize water, their mineral and salt content, and their endemic populations of microorganisms including bacteria. Bacteria, if surviving sterilization, can de-gelatinize the media.

Increasingly, pollution has affected the refinement of tissue-grade agar, causing prices to rise. Agar substitutes such as Gelrite™ are widely used by the plant tissue culture industry, although only a few grams are needed per liter and it does not result in a media firm enough for most mushroom cultivators. Mushroom cultivators desire a media with a semi-solid, firm surface upon which the mycelium will grow, in contrast to plant tissue culturists who seek a softer, gelatinous form.

## Primary Agar Media Formulations

### Malt Extract, Yeast Agar (MYA / MYPA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1000 ml (1 liter) |
| Agar agar | 20 grams |
| Barley malt sugar | 20 grams |
| Nutritional yeast | 2 grams |
| Peptone (optional, soybean derived) | 1 gram |

This is the most commonly used [[mushroom-mycelium-growth-monitoring-colonization-cues]].

### Potato, Dextrose, Yeast Agar (PDYA / PDYPA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1000 ml (1 liter) |
| Agar agar | 20 grams |
| Dextrose | 10 grams |
| Nutritional yeast | 2 grams |
| Peptone (optional, soybean derived) | 1 gram |
| Potatoes (for broth) | 300 grams |

**Important:** Only the broth from boiling potatoes is used — the potatoes are discarded. Boil 300g potatoes in 2-3 liters of water for 1 hour, then use this broth to bring the total media volume to 1 liter. This medium is designated **PDYA**, or **PDYPA** if peptone is added.

### Oatmeal, Malt, Yeast Enriched Agar (OMYA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1000 ml (1 liter) |
| Agar agar | 20 grams |
| Instant oatmeal | 80 grams |
| Malt sugar | 10 grams |
| Nutritional yeast | 2 grams |

This is a rich medium. The oatmeal does not have to be filtered out, although some cultivators prefer to do so for a cleaner appearance.

### Dog Food Agar (DFA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1000 ml (1 liter) |
| Agar agar | 20 grams |
| Dry dog food | 20 grams |

Dog food was first used as a component for [[agar-medium-preparation]] by the late Dr. [[steven-pollock]]. This unusual formulation provides a broad spectrum of nutrients.

### Corn Meal, Yeast, Glucose Agar (CMYA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1000 ml (1 liter) |
| Agar agar | 20 grams |
| Cornmeal | 10 grams |
| Malt or glucose | 5 grams |
| Nutritional yeast | 1 gram |

CMYA is widely used by mycological laboratories for storing cultures but is not as nutritious as the other formulations described above.
