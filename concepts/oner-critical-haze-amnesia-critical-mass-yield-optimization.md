# Critical Haze — Amnesia × Critical Mass Hybrid for Yield Optimization

## Overview

Critical Haze is a cross between two legendary [[oner-southeast-asian-sativa-heritage-thai-vietnamese-cambodian]] × Jamaican × Hawaiian × Afghan (likely through Neville's Haze or [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] for high trichome coverage
- Distinctive [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]]
- Greater overall [[cannabis-super-cropping-technique]]. The sativa influence means plants can stretch significantly if not controlled
- **Height management**: Expect a 2-3x stretch after flipping to 12/12. Indoor growers should begin flowering at 30-40cm for a final height of 60-100cm
- **Nutrient needs**: Moderate to heavy feeder during [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]. The Critical Mass parentage means it can handle substantial nitrogen without tip burn

### Flowering Stage

- **Flowering time**: 8-9 weeks (shorter than pure Amnesia Haze's 10-12 weeks, longer than Critical Mass's 6-8 weeks)
- **Stretch**: Moderate sativa stretch during the first 2-3 weeks of flowering
- **Bud development**: Buds begin as typical sativa foxtails but fill in with Critical Mass density as flowering progresses
- **Support**: May require trellis netting or staking in the final weeks as buds become heavy
- **Trichome production**: Excellent — the Amnesia parent contributes heavy resin production
- **Nutrient shift**: Reduce nitrogen and increase phosphorus/potassium during weeks 3-6. Watch for nutrient sensitivity in the final two weeks

### Yield Expectations

- **Indoor**: 400-500g/m² under optimal conditions — significantly above average for a sativa-leaning hybrid
- **Outdoor**: 500-700g per plant in warm climates with a long growing season
- **Yield quality**: Unlike many high-yielding strains, Critical Haze maintains excellent potency and flavor. The yield comes from genuine bud mass, not leafy, low-quality flowers
