# JADAM Fungal Disease Management

JADAM controls fungal diseases through a two-tier approach: [[ultra-low-jadam-sulfur-js]] for treatment of active outbreaks. The philosophy: do not rely only on pesticide -- change environmental conditions favorable for fungus.

## Prevention: JMS Microbial Occupation

The core preventive strategy is regular JMS foliar application. JMS contains 0.1-1 billion microorganisms per milliliter at peak foam. Spraying JMS on leaves increases microbial diversity and prevents any single pathogen from gaining dominance.

**Preventive spray (per 500L):**
- JMS: 20L (5.3 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L (1.3 gal)
- Gingko JHS: 5L (1.3 gal)
- JWA: 3L (0.8 gal)

Use this regularly before disease breaks out. If disease appears, replace JMS with JS and replace gingko JHS with KPFR JHS.

## Powdery Mildew

The single most important fungal disease to master -- if you control powdery mildew, you can control most other pathogens.

**Standard treatment (Program 4, per 500L):**
- JWA: 3L (0.8 gal)
- JS: 1.2L (0.3 gal)

**Environmental conditions:** Powdery mildew thrives in warm days + cool nights with high humidity but dry leaf surfaces. Improve airflow, reduce plant density, and avoid excess nitrogen.

**Crops affected:** Melon, strawberry, rose, sesame, pepper, cucumber, grape, apple

## Downy Mildew

Controlled with the same Program 4 as powdery mildew. Downy mildew prefers cool, wet conditions and attacks from the undersides of leaves.

## Canker

**Standard treatment (Program 5, per 500L):**
- JWA: 5L (1.3 gal)
- JS: 1.5L (0.4 gal)

**Severe pepper canker:**
- JWA: 10L (2.6 gal)
- JS: 2L (0.53 gal)
- Apply every 2 days for 2-3 total applications

Canker is analogous to athlete's foot in humans -- both are caused by a dominant pathogen that chemical treatments struggle to control. The JADAM approach of microbial diversity (via JMS and leaf mold) addresses the root cause.

## Black Spot (Citrus, Pear, Plum)

**Treatment (Program 5):**
- JWA: 5L + JS: 1.5L per 500L

## Pear Rust

**Treatment (Program 5):**
- JWA: 5L + JS: 1.5L per 500L
- **Special timing:** Effective to apply around 11am on a clear day (unusual -- most JNP is applied morning/evening)

## Brown Blotch

**Treatment (Program 5):**
- JWA: 5L + JS: 1.5L per 500L

## Rice Blast and Rice Sheath Blight

**Treatment (Rice Program 3):**
- JWA: 5L + JS: 2L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L + KPFR JHS: 5L per 500L
- For leaf blight specifically: increase JWA to over 12L and JS to over 2.5L

## Grey Mold (Botrytis)

Found on strawberries and other soft fruit. Controlled with Program 4 (JWA 3L + JS 1.2L).

## Green Mold (Citrus)

Controlled with Program 4 or 5 depending on severity.

## White Rot (Mulberry)

Controlled with Program 5 (JWA 5L + JS 1.5L).

## All-Purpose Disease + Pest Program (Program 12)

When both diseases and pests are present:
- JS: 1.5L + JWA: 6L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L + KPFR JHS: 10L per 500L

## JS Usage Rules

- Maximum 2-3 applications per season
- Do NOT use preventively throughout the season
- Reserve for actual disease outbreaks
- Overuse kills beneficial microbes and disrupts leaf ecology
- Do not re-use leftover pesticide containing JS
- Never apply at noon (except pear rust)
- For greenhouse/protected crops: start at JS 0.5L
- For open fields: start at JS 1L
- Increase in 0.2L increments only
- Sensitive crops: grapes, persimmons, walnuts, perilla

## Environmental Disease Management

JADAM emphasizes changing conditions that favor fungus:
- Reduce humidity in greenhouse (ventilate)
- Prune for airflow and sun penetration
- Avoid excess nitrogen (promotes soft, susceptible tissue)
- Water at base of plants, not overhead
- Remove infected plant material

## Seed Disinfection

Prevent soil-borne and seed-borne fungal diseases at planting:
- Rice: JS 5L per 500L cold water, soak seeds 48 hours, rinse thoroughly
- Garlic/onion: soak sets ~30 minutes in JS solution
- All seeds: soak in leaf mold water 30 minutes for microbial protection
