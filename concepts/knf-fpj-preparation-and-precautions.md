# KNF FPJ Preparation and Precautions

[[knf-fermented-plant-juice]] alongside [[brassinosteroid-fungal-growth-promotion]].

## Storage Guidelines

1. Use polyethylene, glass, or clay jars. Brown glass containers are
   preferred over clear glass.
2. Store in a cool, shaded area with no direct sunlight and stable
   temperature.
3. Optimum storage: 1 to 15 degrees C (refrigerator) for up to one
   year. At room temperature, use within 30 days.

## Important Precautions

1. Molasses is not recommended for FPJ because it contains too much
   moisture to raise osmotic pressure as high as brown sugar does for
   proper fermentation.

2. Small bubbles or fungi in the jar indicate insufficient brown
   sugar or unbalanced volume between empty space and ingredient. Add
   a little brown sugar, stir, and preserve after filtering.

3. Avoid excessive sunshine when picking plants. If hot weather
   continues but FPJ production must proceed, water the plant one day
   before picking, then harvest before sunrise.

4. Avoid picking plants during or after rainfall. Rain washes away
   lactic [[lacto-fermentation-and-food-preservation-methods]] well. This is
   the same reason ingredients should not be washed before
   fermentation. Pick plants 2 to 3 days after rain.

5. Avoid picking plants near roadsides to prevent using polluted
   plants contaminated with vehicle emissions and dust.
