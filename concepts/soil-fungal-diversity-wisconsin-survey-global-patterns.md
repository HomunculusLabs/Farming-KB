## Species-Isolate Curves

### Why Simple Ratios Fail
The relationship between species richness and number of isolates is **nonlinear**:
- First isolates yield many new species
- Later isolates increasingly represent already-seen species
- Simple species-to-isolate ratios cannot compare studies with different sample sizes

### Graphical Methods
1. **Species-isolate curves:** Plot cumulative species against isolates examined
2. **Rank-abundance plots:** Rank species by abundance; plot rank vs. count
3. **Rarefaction:** Mathematical technique for projecting richness from unequal sample sizes

## Microhabitat and Environmental Factors

### Factors Influencing Soil Fungal Diversity
1. **Plant diversity:** Greatest influence; fungal diversity correlates with host/substratum variety
2. **Soil chemistry:** pH, calcium, [[fukuoka-textdoc-composting-critique-futility-prepared-organic-matter]] content
3. **Moisture and temperature:** Most fungi grow/sporulate best >15-20C; water critical
4. **Disturbance:** Burning, logging, cultivation — effects usually quantitative
