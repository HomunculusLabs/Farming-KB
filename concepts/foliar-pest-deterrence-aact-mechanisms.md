## Plant-Microbe Communication on Leaf Surfaces

A critical insight from the Ingham framework is that the plant is largely in control of its interactions with foliar microorganisms. The plant regulates these interactions through the sugars it exudes onto the leaf surface:

- When the plant needs nutrients, it pumps sugars onto the leaf surface
- These sugars feed beneficial microorganisms, which grow and elevate CO2
- Elevated CO2 causes stomata to open, allowing more nutrient uptake
- The plant absorbs the nutrients it needs from the microbial community

If the plant does not need nutrients, it does not pump sugars out, the organisms do not grow as vigorously, and fewer nutrients are taken up.
