# Permaculture Two Species Compendium

updated: 2026-04-18

A comprehensive index of species mentioned in Permaculture Two (1979) with their roles and characteristics. This compendium supports [[tree-vine-crops-arid-tropical-mollison]], [[free-range-poultry-forage-design-mollison]], and [[urban-permaculture-fodder-trees-livestock]].

## Trees

### Food and Forage Trees
- Mulberry (Morus spp.) -- Poultry forage, high vitamin C, resists white ant and fire, fruit ground to flour
- Carob (Ceratonia siliqua) -- 5% male trees needed, termite risk, high nutrient forage and people
- Honey locust (Gleditsia triacanthos) -- Non-toxic leaves, edible beans
- Black locust (Robinia pseudoacacia) -- Toxic spiny leaves, non-inflammable bark
- Olive (Olea europaea) -- Oil crop, shade, easily from cuttings, poultry forage
- Fig (Ficus spp.) -- Hybrids with native figs possible
- Mango (Mangifera indica) -- Established Mt. Isa
- Cashew (Anacardium occidentale) -- Needs wider trials
- Indian oil nut (Calophyllum inophyllum)
- Almond (Prunus dulcis)
- Sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa) -- Frosty areas, stream beds
- Pistacia (P. vera)
- Jujube (Zizyphus jujuba) -- Hedge plant
- Pomegranate -- Pakistani spp. keep into winter
- Plums, cherries -- Many varieties established
- Paw paw, guava (true, feijoa, strawberry, ugni, Pisidium)
- Cape gooseberry, tamarillo
- Grape -- 26 varieties flourish
- Black walnut (Juglans nigra) -- 2,920 P.F.D. value
- Persian walnut (J. regia)
- Oak (Quercus spp.) -- Acorns edible for poultry
- Beech (Fagus spp.) -- Oil-rich nuts
- Mesquite (Prosopis spp.) -- Salt and drought resistant

### Shelter and Hedgerow Trees
- Coprosma repens, C. retusa, C. kirkii -- Fire resistant, poultry forage
- Bamboo spp. -- Structural uses, poultry forage
- Tree lucerne (Chamaecytisus proliferus) -- Fire/frost resistant, stock fodder
- Mulga (Acacia aneura)
- White cedar (Melia azedarach) -- Fruits poisonous, use with care
- Jacaranda (J. mimosifolia)
- Tamarind (Tamarindus indicus)
- Tamarisk (Tamarix articulata) -- Also for mulch
- Kurrajong (Brachychiton gregorii) -- Nectar, pollen, fodder, fibre
- Bottletree (Apophyllum anomalum) -- Fodder, spice
- Ebony (Bauhinia carronii)
- Lime bush (Eremocitrus glauca)
- Karreboom (Rhus lancea) -- Frost/termite resistant

### Nutrient Pump Trees
- Prosopis cineraria -- Roots to 30 m
- Acacia tortilis -- Root net 40-50 m
- Acacia albida -- Deciduous in wet season
- Ailanthus excelsa -- Hedgerow, windbreak, fodder

## Vines
- Passionfruit (Passiflora mollissima) -- Banana passionfruit frost resistant
- Kudzu (Thunbergia) -- Free-seeding rambler for poultry
- Chayote (Choko)
- Gabizura (Actinostemma lobatum) -- Oily seeds
- Dolichos spp. -- Temperate to tropical
- Kiwi fruit, grape

## Herbaceous Plants
- Comfrey -- Perennial, multifunctional
- Tagetes (marigold) -- 90% eelworm control
- Datura Lily (Brugmansia) -- Cabbage moth trap
- Pyrethrum daisy -- Termite control
- Wallflower -- Cabbage moth spray
- Shepherd's purse (Capsella bursa-pastoris) -- Stimulates egg production
- Cleavers (Galium aparine) -- Iron and iodine content
- Plantain (Plantago major, P. lanceolata) -- Absorbs chicken manure, duck forage
- Chard -- First choice green (needs protection from poultry)
- Pokeweed (Phytolacca Americana) -- Especially pigeons
- Wild rice (Zizania aquatica) -- Duck forage in ponds
- Asparagus, chicory, fennel, plantain

## Grasses and Ground Covers
- White clover -- Leguminous intercrop, nitrogen fixation
- Rye grass -- Duck and goose seed heads
- Pampas grass -- Hedgerow, forage
- Kennedia prostrata -- Leguminous coastal mulch
- Mesembryanthemum -- Sand drift prevention
- Lupins -- Coastal scrubs, soil fertility
- Centrosema, Lab-lab -- Tropical ground covers

## Root Crops
- Jerusalem artichoke (Helianthus) -- Under oak forest
- Nut-grass (Eleocharis) -- Poultry eat tubers
- Sour-grass (Oxalis) -- Poultry eat all parts
- Oca -- Special culture of Oxalis
- Potato, sweet potato, turnip, carrot, onion, manioc

## Aquatic Plants
- Water chestnut (Trapa) -- Floating aquatics
- Lotus, water lilies -- Edible roots
- Wild rice (Zizania) -- Edge species for ducks
- Watercress, mint -- Seepage plantings
- Duckweed, algal pond weeds

## Australian Native Species
- Wiltja brother trees: stringy bark, ironbark, native cherry, cider gum
- Acacia spp.: A. aneura, A. kempeana, A. victoriae, A. cowleana, A. peuce, A. oswaldia, A. binervata, A. longifolia
- Eremophila longifolia (Berrigan)
- Owenia acidula (Emu apple)
- Capparis mitchelli (Wild orange)
- Canthium latifolium (Native currant)
- Banksia spp. -- Coastal windbreak
- Melaleuca spp. -- Stakes, poles, coastal hedges
- Casuarina spp. -- Mulch, fire resistant
- Callitris spp. -- Coastal pines

## Pest Control Species
- Moloch horridus (Mountain Devil) -- Ant control
- Guinea fowl -- General pest control
- Ducks -- Fruit fly and termite control
- Pygmy perch, galaxids, minnows -- Mosquito control in ponds

## Sources

- Mollison, B. Permaculture Two: Practical Design for Town & Country in Permanent Agriculture. Tagari Books, 1979.
