# Rice Water Weevil Surface Spreading Agent (JADAM Recipe #2)

## Overview

Rice water weevils (Lissorhoptrus oryzophilus) inhabit the dikes and water surface of rice paddies. JADAM recipe #2 is a water surface-spreading agent -- not a spray -- that forms a thin film on the water surface to control weevils. This is a unique application method that exploits the weevils' surface habitat.

**Developed by:** Seongmin Bak, Youngsang Cho

## Surface Spreading Agent Recipe (Concentrated)

- [[jadam-canola-oil-surfactant-method]] 3 L (0.8 gal)

### Mixing Instructions
1. Mix JWA in 5 L of water and stir well
2. Add JS and stir
3. Add canola oil and mix with drill (manual or electrical) for 10 minutes
4. The result is a concentrated surface-spreading agent

## Coverage

- 1 L (0.26 gal) of surface spreading agent covers 0.1 ha (1/4 acre)
- Use warm/soft water for best results
