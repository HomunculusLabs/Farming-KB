# Anaerobic Digestion and Biogas Production

Anaerobic digestion is a biological process that breaks down organic matter in the absence of oxygen, producing biogas (primarily methane and carbon dioxide) as a byproduct. In [[permaculture]] designs, several principles apply:

- **Cascade placement**: Position ponds on contour so effluent flows by gravity through each stage.
- **Buffer zones**: Maintain vegetated buffer areas around ponds for nutrient absorption and wildlife habitat.
- **Gas utilisation**: Connect sealed digester covers to storage or direct-use systems for methane.
- **Sludge harvesting**: Periodically harvest metal-rich sludge from anaerobic pond bottoms; this material may be suitable for non-food soil amendment after testing.
- **Multiple functions**: Design pond systems to provide energy (biogas), water treatment, nutrient recycling, and aquatic habitat simultaneously.
