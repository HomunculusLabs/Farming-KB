# Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum) Indoor Cultivation Parameters #mycoremediation #mushroom-cultivation #medicinal-mushrooms #polypore-cultivation Updated: 2026-04-18 Source: Paul Stamets, Growing Gourmet and Medicinal Mushrooms (1993), Ch. 21 Growth Parameters ## Overview Ganoderma lucidum is known as Reishi (Japanese), Ling Chi or Ling Zhi (Chinese, "Mushroom of Immortality"), and Mannentake (Japanese, "10,000 Year Mushroom"). It is the most renowned medicinal mushroom in Asian pharmacopeia, with over two millennia of documented use. The earliest mention dates to Emperor Shih-huang of the Ch'in Dynasty (221-207 BC). Claimed to address cancer, heart disease, diabetes, arthritis, high altitude sickness, and chronic fatigue syndrome. ## Mycelial Characteristics

Longitudinally radial, non-aerial, initially white, rapid growing, becoming densely matted and appressed, yellow to golden brown, often zonate with age. Some strains produce brown hymenophore on MEA. 1 cm square inoculum colonizes 100x15 mm petri plate in 7-10 days at 75F (24C). Becomes difficult to cut after 2 weeks. Culture slants store 5 years at 35F (1-2C).

## Fragrance Signature

Musty, mealy, not sweet, not pleasant.

## Indoor Cultivation Method (Rapid Cycle System)

### Substrate Preparation

- 50:50 (by volume) hardwood sawdust/wood chip mixture
- Soak wood chips 3-4 days in molasses-enriched water (50 ml molasses/5 gallons water)
- Fill bags (17.50 x 8.25 x 4.75 in) to 3 lbs wet weight
- Sterilize 2 hours at 15 psi
- Inoculate with grain or [[oyster-mushroom-clean-room-design]]
- Heat-seal bags (domed/inflated appearance creates idealized positive-pressure humidified environment)

### Growth Parameters

**Spawn Run:**
- Temperature: 75F (24C)
- Relative Humidity: 95-100%
- Duration: 14-21 days colonization; 30-40 days total to first mushrooms
- CO2: >20,000 ppm (2%) within sealed bag
- Fresh Air Exchanges: 0 (sealed bag, microporous filter patch)
- Light: Not needed

**Primordia/Antler Formation:**
- Temperature: 75F (24C)
- Duration: 30-40 days post-inoculation
- CO2: >20,000 ppm (critical for antler/stalk formation)
- Light: Minimal
- Note: Whitish to golden yellow tongue-like formations emerge, becoming branched, reaching 4 inches by day 50

**Cap Development (Bag Opening):**
- Temperature: 75-85F (24-30C)
- Relative Humidity: 85-95%
- CO2: <1000 ppm (drop from >20,000 to ~350 ppm signals cap formation)
- Fresh Air Exchanges: 4-8 per hour
- Light: 500-1000 lux
- Duration: 14-21 days
- Note: CO2 drop from sealed bag to ambient triggers transition from antler to conk form. Analogous to stalk emerging from high-CO2 underground environment into open air.

### Cropping Cycle

Every 3-4 weeks for maximum of two flushes.

## Key Cultivation Insights

- The CO2 transition is the critical trigger: high CO2 produces antlers, low CO2 triggers cap development
- If bag is not opened at the right time, opportunity for conk development is lost
- Caps form above the CO2-sensitive stalk and orient towards directional light
- New growth indicated by white band at cap margin
- Polypores do NOT require heavy watering like gilled mushrooms
- Compressed substrate blocks can improve fruiting for some species
- Polypores are sensitive to CO2 and light conditions for proper development

## Natural/Outdoor Cultivation

Traditional method: inoculate logs, lay on ground or shallowly bury in shady, moist locations. Cover with shade cloth on hoop-frames. 6 months to 2 years before substantial harvests begin, continuing 4-5 years. Also grown on stumps, especially in humid southeastern US climates. Quasi-natural method: inoculate short hardwood logs, place into nursery pots filled with sawdust, top with soil, house in shade-cloth-covered greenhouses. See also stamets-shiitake-and-stropharia-cultivation.

## Yield and Marketing

Marketed as fresh, dried, powdered, and in tablet form. Dried Reishi is 70-80% water (woody texture). Can be stored for months. Used in teas, extracts, and traditional preparations. The mushroom is more valued in compact fruitbody form than in mycelial form.
