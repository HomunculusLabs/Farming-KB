## Enzyme Harvesting from Mycorrhizal and Fungal Systems

Fungi function as enzymatic factories, producing a wide array of
primary, secondary, and tertiary metabolites. Critically, fungi
can shift their gene expression to manufacture different spectra
of enzymes in response to specific chemical and biological
triggers. This capacity enables researchers to construct enzyme
harvesting systems that yield significant volumes of unique
metabolites for applications including novel antibiotics,
industrial enzymes, and bioremediation cocktails.
