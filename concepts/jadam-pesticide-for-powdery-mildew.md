# JADAM Pesticide for Powdery Mildew

Powdery mildew and downy mildew are among the most common and destructive fungal diseases. JADAM controls them with [[jadam-sulfur-js]] combined with [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]]. Early intervention is critical -- JS is a germicide, not a cure for advanced infection.

## Formulation (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

**Standard powdery/downy mildew**:
- JWA: 3L (0.8 gal)
- JS: 1.2L (0.3 gal)
- Water: to 500L (132 gal)

**With insect control** (mildew + pests present):
- JWA: 5L (1.3 gal)
- JS: 1.2L (0.3 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 5L (1.3 gal)

**Severe outbreak**:
- JWA: 5L + JS 1.5L (stronger, but watch for concentration damage)

## Preparation Steps

1. Test water with JWA -- must be soft water
2. Do mixture test: 0.5L water + 5cc each, shake, check foam + no clotting
3. Half-fill tank with water, add JS first, stir thoroughly
4. Add JWA, stir, top up with water
5. Never mix undiluted JWA and JS directly (they clot)
6. Filter through fine net
7. Use same day -- never store leftover with JS

## Application Timing

- **CRITICAL**: apply in early stage of disease outbreak for best results
- Do not wait until mildew has spread widely
- Spray in morning dew, evening, or humid/cloudy conditions
- For pear rust specifically: effective to spray around 11am on clear day
- Apply before rain for open fields (rainwater carries inputs into soil)

## Number of Applications

- Use JS only 2-3 times per season when disease is present
- Do NOT use JS regularly throughout the season as preventive
- For preventive management, use JMS regularly instead (JMS 20L + JWA 3L per 500L)
- Re-apply every 1-2 days during active outbreak

## Resistance Prevention

- JS is a broad-spectrum germicide with multiple modes of action from sulfur -- resistance does not develop the way it does with single-molecule synthetic fungicides
- Rotate between JS treatments and JMS microbial diversity approach
- JMS maintains microbial diversity on leaf surfaces so no single pathogen dominates
- Do not over-rely on JS -- change environmental conditions favorable for fungus (reduce humidity, improve airflow)

## Concentration Safety

- JS can cause concentration damage to young/soft leaves, greenhouse crops, grapes, persimmons, walnuts, and perilla
- Always do concentration test: spray small area, check after 2-3 days
- When increasing JS, increase in 0.2L (0.05 gal) increments
- Start sulfur-sensitive crops at JS 0.5L and increase carefully

## Diseases Controlled

Powdery mildew (melon, strawberry, rose, sesame, pepper), downy mildew, grey mold (botrytis on strawberry), green mold, black spot, leaf fungus
