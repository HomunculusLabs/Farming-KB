## PF TEK Maximum Fruiting Substrate Formula

## Overview

The [[pf-tek]] offers two substrate formulations for half-pint jars: the standard formula
and the maximum fruiting formula. The maximum fruiting formula increases the nutrient
content of the substrate by doubling the [[mushroom-casing-moisture-management]] during incubation and a slightly
more involved preparation process.

## Standard vs Maximum Formula Comparison

### Standard Formula (Per Half-Pint Jar)

- 1/8 cup of brown rice powder (from health food stores and co-ops)
- 1/2 to 2/3 cup of horticultural vermiculite, medium grade
- 40 to 45 cc (milliliters) of water, approximately 3 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon

### Maximum Fruiting Formula (Per Half-Pint Jar)

- 1/4 cup of brown rice powder
- 1/2 cup of vermiculite
- 60 cc water

The maximum formula doubles the brown rice powder from 1/8 cup to 1/4 cup while
keeping vermiculite at 1/2 cup and increasing water from approximately 45 cc to
60 cc. The additional water is necessary to properly hydrate the increased rice
flour and maintain the correct moisture content for vigorous [[pf-tek-vermiculite-type-comparison-water-holding-capacity]] and require formula adjustments. If the formulation results in a
really wet or sloppy substrate, use less water. Keep notes on formulas for
replicating the substrate formula that fruits the best.

### Fine Vermiculite (Recommended)

Fine vermiculite has particles averaging around 1 millimeter across, with some larger
and some smaller particles mixed in. It holds more water than coarse vermiculite
and is recommended for both the standard and maximum fruiting formulas.

The finer grade provides better moisture retention and more uniform distribution
throughout the substrate cake. The mycelial threads can stretch across the smaller
air spaces more effectively, promoting faster and more complete colonization.

### Coarse Vermiculite

Coarse vermiculite has particles averaging around 4 to 5 millimeters across, with
some up to 8 millimeters in size. It holds less water than the fine type, which
means the water content in the formulas will need to be reduced to avoid a wet or
sloppy substrate.

If using coarse vermiculite with the maximum formula, the [[carbon-dioxide-retention-jar-lid-mushroom-cultivation]] should be kept
loose during incubation to allow excess moisture to escape, as the combination of
higher water content and lower water retention can lead to pooling.

### Selecting and Comparing Vermiculite

Stores usually carry one type or the other, and there will be regional differences
between brands and suppliers. The PF TEK advises shopping around and trying to
obtain both types for comparison experiments.

To ascertain particle size accurately, observe the vermiculite under a photo
magnifier next to a millimeter ruler. The visible difference between 1 millimeter
fine particles and 5 millimeter coarse particles is dramatic and easy to identify.

## Brown Rice Powder Preparation

Homemade brown rice powder is recommended over prepackaged types. To prepare it,
place regular brown rice in a small canister-type coffee bean grinder and grind it
to fine powder.
