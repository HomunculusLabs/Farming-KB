## LSD (Lysergic Acid Diethylamide)

## Overview

LSD (D-lysergic acid diethylamide, LSD-25) is a semi-synthetic ergoline alkaloid and one of the most potent psychoactive substances known. It is classified as a classical psychedelic and acts primarily as a partial agonist at serotonin 5-HT2A receptors. Discovered by albert hofmann at LSD Compound Profile Pharmaceuticals in 1938, LSD is active at microgram doses -- making it thousands of times more potent by weight than [[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]])
- **Synthesis route:** Hofmann developed a coupling procedure to combine lysergic acid with diethylamine, producing LSD along with the natural ergot alkaloid ergobasine
- LSD is a semi-synthetic compound: the lysergic acid core is derived from natural ergot, but the diethylamide substitution is synthetic
