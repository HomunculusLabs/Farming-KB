# Cho's Natural Pest Control Methods

Dr. Cho's Korean [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]] approaches pest control through preventive
plant health management and targeted physical interventions. The system
avoids chemical pesticides entirely, relying instead on soil health, balanced
nutrition, and biological methods.

## Philosophy

Cho's pest control rests on several principles:

1.  **Healthy plants resist pests** — Soil managed with
    [[cho-plant-growth-stage-treatments]] ensures appropriate nutrition at each stage.
3.  **Microbial diversity suppresses pathogens** — A diverse soil
    microbiome prevents any single pathogen from dominating. IMOs produce
    antibiotic substances and enzymes that suppress diseases.
4.  **Minimal intervention preferred** — Physical traps and biological
    methods are used only when necessary.

## Soil Health Foundation

In Cho's "Non-Chemical" approach, farmers nurture the soil rather than
feed plants directly. IMOs perform two critical functions for pest
management:

1.  **Decomposition** — Breaking complex [[fungal-volatile-organic-compounds-communication]] into
    absorbable nutrients for vigorous, resistant growth.
2.  **Pathogen suppression** — Producing antibiotic substances, enzymes,
    and lactic acids that suppress diseases.

The [[cho-fermented-plant-juice-preparation]]
    diluted 300 times, and [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient]] functions as both plant tonic and
pest deterrent. It is used in making IMO-3/IMO-4, soil treatment
solution, seed/seedling treatment, and foliar applications at 1:500 to
1:1000 dilution throughout all growth stages.

-   Prevention through soil health and balanced nutrition is primary.
