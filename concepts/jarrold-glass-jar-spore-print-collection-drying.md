## Spore Print Collection Procedure

### Selecting Suitable Mushroom Caps
Not all mushroom caps are equally suitable for spore printing. Ideal candidates are:
- Mature mushrooms with fully opened caps and exposed gills
- Specimens at or near peak sporulation (not over-mature and decomposing)
- Healthy specimens free from contamination or disease
- Caps with intact gill structures that have not been damaged

### Step-by-Step Collection Process
1. Bring sterilized jars, tweezers, scissors, and spirit burner to the mushroom
   growing tank area.
2. Put on the breathing mask to prevent breath contamination.
3. Select a suitable mushroom cap and unscrew the lid of a sterile jar (leave it
   on but loosened).
4. Flame sterilize the tweezers and scissors using the methylated spirit burner.
5. Gently insert the flame-sterilized tweezers into the top of a mature,
   spore-laden cap.
6. Cut the cap from the stem using the flame-sterilized scissors.
7. Carefully place the cap gills-down into the sterile jar.
8. Immediately replace the lid — loosely, not tightly — so that any moisture on
   the cap or spores can evaporate naturally.

### Critical Hygiene Notes
- Flame sterilize tweezers and scissors between each cap to prevent cross-
  contamination between specimens.
- Work quickly to minimize the time the jar is open and exposed.
- Do not touch the inside of the jar or the gill surface of the cap with bare hands.
- The breathing mask must be worn throughout the entire procedure.
