## Pleurotus cornucopiae

The branched oyster or trumpet oyster produces clustered, branching fruiting
bodies with a distinctive funnel shape. Found in temperate hardwood forests,
it fruits in spring and autumn. It has a more complex, aromatic flavor than P.
ostreatus and is considered a delicacy in some European and East Asian
cuisines. Cultivation is less common due to more specific environmental
requirements and slightly lower yields, but it fills a specialty market niche.
