### Panning and Watering Between Flushes

The most common watering mistake is applying too much water at once,
which seals the casing surface ("panning"). Repair by scratching the top
1/4 inch with a nail-studded board. After each flush harvest, water
immediately to replenish moisture, then reduce during mid-break when few
pins are visible.

### Water Quality

- Use clean, non-chlorinated water when possible.
- Water temperature should be close to the room temperature to avoid
  shocking the mycelium.
- Some growers add a small amount of calcium (from gypsum or lime) to
  the water to maintain casing pH.

## Key Points

- Flushes normally occur in 7-10 day intervals with decreasing yields.
- Lower air temperature by 2°F before flush peaks for quality control.
- Remove entire flush quickly to accelerate the next flush cycle.
- Even pinning during initiation produces uniform flushing patterns.
- CO2 from crop metabolism still requires fresh air exchange.
- Water 2-4 times per day rather than once for slow absorption.
- Pick before veil tears; spore release inhibits further pinning.
