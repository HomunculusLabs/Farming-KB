## How to Culture LAB from Milk

The KNF method for culturing LAB is straightforward and
uses readily available materials:

**Ingredients needed:**
- Rice-washed water
- Milk (unprocessed, not boiled; cow's milk is best)
- Jaggery or brown sugar
- Clay jar or [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]**: When used during the vegetative
   growth period, higher quality plants result that can
   be stored for longer periods

3. **Fertilizer solubility**: LAB increases the
   solubility of fertilizers, making nutrients more
   available to plants

4. **Gas damage reduction**: LAB neutralizes ammonia gas
   produced by immature compost applications

5. **Phosphate solubilization**: LAB solubilizes phosphate
   at 100 to 200 ppm, helping plants absorb insoluble
   phosphates and overcome saline disorders
