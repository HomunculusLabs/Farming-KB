# Permaculture Chicken Forage Systems (Design Course)

**Source:** Permaculture Design Course, Bill Mollison (1981, Pamphlet IX)
**Updated:** 2026-04-18



## Overview

No one in history has ever designed and implemented animal forage systems until permaculture. The system integrates chickens with tree crops, forage plants, and pest management in zone two.



## Key Forage Species

### White Mulberry
- As good as a double crop of grain — 17% protein
- Excellent chicken food during and beyond bearing period; chickens get seed long after mulberries are gone
- Plant 4-foot cuttings of 1.5-inch diameter; full production in second year
- Multiple varieties extend harvest season; US has some of the world's best black mulberries

### Siberian Pea Tree
- Excellent nitrogen fixer producing large seed quantities
- Siberian peasants fed poultry exclusively on this tree
- Chickens forage on it when other seed is scarce; they eat greens from it in summer
- One of the best chicken forage trees for cold climates

### Other Forage Plants
- **Hawthorn and mountain ash:** Great winter forage
- **Comfrey, cleavers, chard:** Summer drought greens
- **Black locust:** 10,000 lb seed drop per acre minimum (also best fence post material)
- **Black raspberry:** Excellent cover for brooding hens, grows at apple tree drip lines
- **Canary Island tree lucerne:** Number one chicken seed forage in old Tasmania



## Flock Management

### Numbers and Density
- **Maximum:** 400 chickens per acre (above this, nitrogen weakens pasture, chickens eat it out)
- **Ideal:** 300 chickens per acre — still entire herb cover, no bare soil
- **Per flock:** 50–60 chickens (suits social conditions best), never exceed 80
- **Roosters:** 5 per flock — fewer roosters causes hens to wander
- **Separation:** Houses 150 feet apart prevents flock mixing

### Breeds and Seasons
- Four entirely different breeds on one acre (four flocks of 60)
- Heavy breeds lay better in winter; light breeds are spring/summer layers
- Consult chicken fanciers, not poultrymen

### Pest Control Functions
- Chickens eat encysted parasite eggs, roadkill (converted to larvae), insects
- Chickens under plum/cherry trees sharply reduce crawling and flying pests
- All soil pests that go underground and re-emerge are sharply reduced
- Chickens in tree shelter = practically nil depredation
- Hang roadkill in chicken run → larvae drop in pen → eaten before hatching into flies
- Ducks are great fly catchers; bait flies and ducks catch them

### Forage Calculations
- Half acre of mixed forage: ~5,000 lb chicken forage per year (higher protein than wheat)
- Plus 2,000–3,000 lb additional protein from forages, grasses, and insects
- Keep 40 chickens year-round on half acre without grain purchase
- Hang grain sheaves in straw yards; chickens do their own husking and threshing
