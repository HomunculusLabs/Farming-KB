# Moth Pesticide (JADAM Recipe #7)

**Updated:** 2026-04-18
**Source:** JADAM Organic Farming, p.316 (Youngsang Cho, 2016)

## Overview

JADAM pesticide recipe #7 targets moth larvae and scale insects using high concentrations of [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] and Jerusalem artichoke [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]]. This is a stronger formulation than recipe #6, designed for chewing pests that are more difficult to control than sucking insects.

**Developed by:** Hyunho Cha, Youngsang Cho

## Recipe

For 500 L (132 gal) of spray solution:

- [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] 8 L (2.1 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] 15 L (4 gal)
- Water to make 500 L (132 gal)

## Target Pests

- Tobacco moth larvae
- Beet armyworm (Spodoptera exigua)
- Diamondback moth larvae (Plutella xylostella)
- Oriental fruit moth
- Slug moth larvae (Thosea sinensis)
- Citrus mealybug (Planococcus citri)
- Arrowhead scale (Unaspis yanonensis)
- Fruitworm moth larvae
- Perilla leaf moth larvae
- Cabbage moth larvae

## Application Strategy

### Standard Application
- Apply thoroughly, coating all plant surfaces
- The more JWA and JHS, the stronger the pesticide
- When JS is not used, spray consecutively for better results

### Heavy Infestation Protocol
- Apply at **sunset**, followed by another application **early next morning**
- The 12-hour gap allows pests to be active and contact fresh pesticide
- Two applications significantly increase kill rate

### Nocturnal Moths
- For some nocturnal moth species, evening application may be more effective than morning
- Moths are active at night and more likely to contact wet pesticide

## Special Pest Instructions

### Hairy Pests (Slug Moth Larvae)
- Slug moth larvae have protective hairs that repel liquid
- **Repeatedly apply and wet them thoroughly**
- Multiple applications overcome the hair barrier
- Ensure complete saturation of the pest body

## Herb Substitutions

Jerusalem artichoke JHS can be replaced with:
- **Pokeweed** JHS
- **Ginkgo** JHS
- **Korean pasque flower root** (KPFR) JHS

## Adding Disease Control (JS)

To also control fungal diseases:
- Add [[jadam-sulfur-js]] 1-2 L (0.26-0.53 gal) per 500 L
- When JS is added, you lose the ability to spray consecutively

## Red Clay Enhancement

- Add 0.5 kg (1.1 lb) of very fine red clay powder per 500 L
- Increases effectiveness against all target pests
- See [[jadam-red-clay-powder-method]] for preparation

## Relative Strength

- Recipe #7 is **stronger than** recipe #6 (aphid/mite)
- Recipe #7 is **weaker than** recipe #8 (planthopper/leafhopper)
- For maximum strength against all pests including stinkbugs, see [[jadam-stinkbug-mealybug-pesticide]] (recipe #9)
