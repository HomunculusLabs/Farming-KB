# Mango Haze

**Breeder:** Mr. Nice Seedbank (Holland)
**Genetics:** [[northern-lights]] #5/Haze × Skunk/Haze
**Potency:** THC 19%
**Ratio:** ~50% Sativa / 50% Indica (sativa-dominant classification)
**Flowering Time:** 66 days (indoor)
**Yield:** ~500g per square yard (indoor)

## Overview

Mango Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid from Holland's Mr. Nice Seedbank, one of the most
respected seed companies in cannabis history, founded by Neville Schoenmaker and Shantibaba.
The strain's lineage is nothing short of extraordinary: a quadruple dose of Haze genetics
combined with Skunk #1 and Northern Lights #5, making its family tree among the most
prestigious in cannabis breeding. Named for the state of bliss its creator finds in during
mango season — buying whole boxes of mangos in the midst of a munchies attack — this strain
delivers an experience that lives up to its tropical namesake.

The complex breeding history means Mango Haze carries genetics from the foundational pillars
of modern cannabis: [[haze-lineage-and-history]] (the original sativa masterpiece), Skunk #1 (the first true
commercial hybrid), and Northern Lights #5 (one of the most influential indicas ever bred).
This genetic diversity results in a plant that is both a joy to grow and a pleasure to
consume.

## Growth Characteristics

At just over 50% sativa, Mango Haze occupies a favorable middle ground for indoor cultivation.
The indica influence keeps the plant manageable while the sativa genetics contribute vigor,
yield potential, and the celebrated cerebral effects. Key growing traits include:

- **Indoor height:** Finished plants reach approximately 4–5 feet, making them suitable for
  standard grow tents and rooms
- **Outdoor potential:** Can grow significantly larger outdoors, taking full advantage of
  natural light and root space
- **Space-saving option:** For growers with limited vertical space, the breeder recommends
  going straight to 12/12 flowering with a rooted clone or seedling to minimize final height
- **Resin production:** Described as "very, very resinous" — exceptional trichome coverage
  throughout flowering

## Flowering and Yield

Mango Haze flowers in approximately 66 days indoors, placing it in the moderate range. The
yield potential is impressive: around 500 grams per square yard under standard conditions.
Experienced growers who optimize their lighting layout and nutrient programs can extract
significantly more from each grow cycle. The breeder specifically recommends pruning lower
branches to redirect energy to the upper canopy and encourage greater overall yields. Mold
resistance is notable — the breeder states it "won't pose a threat to your crop," a
significant advantage for humid environments.

## Cultivation Versatility

One of Mango Haze's strengths is its adaptability to different growing methods:

- **Organic soil:** Performs excellently in living soil setups, where the complex terpene
  profile can be further enhanced by microbial activity
- **Hydroponic systems:** Equally at home in hydro, with no special requirements or
  sensitivities reported
- **Feeding requirements:** Standard sativa-oriented nutrient schedules are sufficient; the
  strain is not particularly demanding or finicky

## Effects Profile

Mango Haze delivers "a riot of cerebral rushes and gorgeous vibes" — a classic Haze-influenced
experience characterized by:

- **Euphoric onset:** Rapid mood elevation with an uplifting, happy quality
- **Cerebral stimulation:** Mental energy and creative engagement without anxious overstimulation
- **Duration:** The Haze genetics contribute to a longer-lasting experience than many
  contemporary hybrids
- **Body component:** The Northern Lights influence adds a subtle physical relaxation that
  prevents the experience from becoming purely heady

## Aroma and Flavor

The defining characteristic of Mango Haze is, unsurprisingly, its exceptional mango aroma. The
scent is described as genuinely fruity and delicious, with the tropical fruit notes being the
dominant olfactory experience. The flavor follows through on the aromatic promise, delivering
a rich, sweet mango taste that distinguishes it from the many strains that claim tropical
profiles but deliver generic sweetness.

## Terpene Profile

The mango-dominant aroma suggests a terpene profile likely featuring:

- **[[myrcene]]**: High levels contribute to the tropical fruit aroma and synergize with THC for
  enhanced effects
- **Terpinolene**: May contribute to the complex, layered fruitiness beyond simple mango
- **Caryophyllene**: Adds subtle spice and pepper notes to complement the sweetness
- **Linalool**: Possible floral undertones that add depth to the aroma profile

## Medical Applications

The balanced yet uplifting profile makes Mango Haze suitable for various therapeutic uses:

- **Mood disorders:** The euphoric, uplifting effects can temporarily alleviate symptoms of
  depression and anxiety
- **Fatigue:** Cerebral energy and motivation without jitters or overstimulation
- **Appetite stimulation:** The munchies-inducing qualities are well-documented by the
  breeder's own admission
- **Pain management:** Moderate body relaxation from the indica lineage helps with mild to
  moderate pain

## Parent Strain Heritage

The Northern Lights #5/Haze mother contributes the legendary NL#5's resin production, yield
potential, and flowering reliability, while the Skunk/Haze father adds vigor, disease
resistance, and the complex aromatic profile that Skunk genetics are known for. The double
Haze contribution from both parents ensures the celebrated cerebral effects remain prominent
despite the indica influence.

## Cultivation Tips

1. **Prune lower branches** — redirect energy to upper colas for improved yields
2. **Consider 12/12 from clone** — saves vertical space in constrained environments
3. **Try organic growing** — may enhance the already-exceptional terpene production
4. **Monitor trichome development** — the long resin production window means optimal harvest
   timing is important
5. **Support heavy colas** — dense, resinous buds may need staking in later flowering
6. **Extended flush recommended** — clean flush enhances the delicate mango flavor profile
