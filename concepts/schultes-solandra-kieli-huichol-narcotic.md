## Cultural Significance

The Huichol classification of Solandra as a "god-narcotic" reflects
the indigenous understanding that psychoactive plants are not merely
intoxicants but are imbued with spiritual power and agency. The term
*Kieli* denotes a being with its own will and intentions, and the
Huichol approach its use with appropriate reverence and caution.
