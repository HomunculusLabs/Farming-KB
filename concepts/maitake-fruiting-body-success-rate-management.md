# Maitake Fruiting Body Success Rate and Environmental Management

## Overview

Successful fruiting body production in [[fruiting-body-development]], with 2000-5000 ppm (0.2-0.5%)

- **Chinese practice**: High O2 maintained with bag tops opened

### Effects of Oxygen Deficiency

- **Low O2 with high CO2**: Produces antler-form fruiting bodies
  instead of normal capped fruiting clusters.
- **Extreme O2 deficiency**: Completely arrests fruiting body
  development and leads to decay of the substrate block.

For bag opening: transfer bags to the fruiting environment, allow
2-3 days for adjustment, then open tops or cut slits for oxygen.
Never remove the entire bag; retain it to preserve moisture.
