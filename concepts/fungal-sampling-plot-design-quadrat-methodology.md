## Plot Shape: Circles vs Rectangles

### Circular Plots

In grasslands, shrublands, or even forests, **circular plots** are delineated easily and quickly using:
- A center pole
- A freely swinging radius line

**Advantages of circular plots:**
- Minimize edge effects that can strongly bias estimates of density
- Quick to establish in the field
- No orientation decisions required
- Equal distance from center to all edges

**Disadvantages:** Less effective at detecting habitat heterogeneity and may miss patchy distributions of fungi within the sampling area.

### Rectangular Quadrats

Krebs (1989) suggested that **elongated or rectangular quadrats** provide more accurate estimates of species composition than circular or square plots of the same area.

**Key advantages of rectangular plots:**
- More effective in detecting habitat heterogeneity
- Accurately estimate patchy distribution of organisms within a sampling scheme
- A long quadrat potentially covers more patches than a circular one of the same area
