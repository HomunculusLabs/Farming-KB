## Jun Kombucha

Jun is a variant of kombucha made with green tea and honey instead of black tea and white sugar. The Jun culture is a different SCOBY that has adapted to the honey and green tea environment. Jun ferments faster than regular kombucha and has a lighter, more delicate flavor with floral notes from the honey.

## Health Considerations

Kombucha contains live probiotic bacteria, organic acids (acetic acid, gluconic acid, lactic acid), B vitamins, and antioxidants from tea polyphenols. Proponents claim various health benefits including improved digestion, immune support, detoxification, and increased energy.
