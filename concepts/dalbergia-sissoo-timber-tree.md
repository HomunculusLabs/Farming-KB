# Dalbergia sissoo (Sissoo) — A Multipurpose Timber Tree

Dalbergia sissoo, commonly known as sissoo or Indian rosewood, is one of the most valuable multipurpose trees in tropical and subtropical permaculture systems. A fast-growing leguminous tree in the Fabaceae family, it provides premium timber, excellent firewood, erosion control, nitrogen fixation, and fodder — making it an outstanding example of a high-yield permaculture tree that performs multiple functions simultaneously.

## Botanical Profile

**Family**: Fabaceae (Leguminosae)  
**Common names**: Sissoo, shisham, tahli, Indian rosewood  
**Form**: Fast-growing deciduous tree to 30 meters (may only reach 10–15 meters without irrigation)  
**Native range**: Indian subcontinent, naturally occurring along riverbanks and streams

## Climate and Site Requirements

Sissoo is remarkably adaptable across a wide range of conditions:

- **Temperature range**: From below 0°C to nearly 50°C, considered frost hardy.
- **Rainfall range**: 500–2,000 mm annually. Can tolerate arid conditions within this broader range, though growth is naturally slower without irrigation.
- **Altitude**: From sea level up to 1,500 meters.
- **Soil preference**: Alluvial soils along riverbanks and stream beds. Does not grow well on heavy clay soils. Prefers well-drained, deep, moist soils.
- **Root system**: Long superficial roots that send up suckers when damaged. This vigorous suckering habit makes it valuable [[riparian-zone-restoration]]

### Nitrogen Fixation

As a legume, Dalbergia sissoo fixes atmospheric nitrogen through symbiotic relationships with rhizobial bacteria. This nitrogen fixation:

- Improves soil fertility for interplanted crops
- Reduces the need for external nitrogen inputs
- Supports the growth of understory species
- Contributes organic nitrogen through leaf litter decomposition

### Fodder Production

Young branches and leaves of sissoo are used as fodder. Deer and cattle browse young plants. The protein content and palatability make it a valuable dry-season fodder source in areas where other forage is scarce.

## Integration in Permaculture Systems

### Agroforestry Applications

Sissoo integrates well into several agroforestry configurations:

- **Riparian buffer strips**: Planted along waterways to filter runoff, stabilise banks, and provide shade that reduces water temperature for aquatic ecosystems.
- **Windbreaks and shelterbelts**: Fast growth and height make it effective as a windbreak component.
- **Alley cropping**: Nitrogen fixation benefits interrow crops while providing timber income.
- **Silvopasture**: Provides shade for livestock while yielding fodder and eventually timber.
- **Woodlot systems**: Managed for sustainable timber production on a rotation basis.
