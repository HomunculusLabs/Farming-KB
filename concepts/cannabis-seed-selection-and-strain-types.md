## Strain Categories

Seed producers primarily offer these strain types:

- **Pure Sativa**: A pure Sativa species — tall plants, long internodes, pointy leaves, total head high
- **Sativa**: Mostly Sativa with some Indica influence — taller plants, cerebral effects
- **Pure Indica**: A pure Indica species — short plants (6 inches to 4 feet), short internodes, rounded leaves with marble patterns, total body stone/couch-lock
- **Indica**: Mostly Indica with some Sativa — compact plants, relaxing body effects
- **Indica/Sativa (50/50)**: Equal cross between Sativa and Indica — balanced head and body effects

## Indica vs Sativa Comparison

| Trait | Sativa | Indica |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Height | 4–15 feet | 6 inches–4 feet |
| Internodes | Long (3–6 inches) | Short (under 3 inches) |
| Leaf shape | Pointy, no patterns | Rounded, marble patterns |
| High type | Cerebral head high | Body stone, couch-lock |
| Flowering time | Longer (8–16 weeks) | Shorter (6–9 weeks) |
| Growing factors | Space-intensive | Compact, good indoors |

## The High Type Spectrum

The ratio of head high to body stone depends on the genetic mix:

- Pure Sativa = 100% head high
- Indica-dominant Sativa cross = 60% body stone, 40% head high
- 50/50 cross = 50/50 balance
- Sativa-dominant Indica cross = 60% head high, 40% body stone
- Pure Indica = 100% body stone/couch-lock
