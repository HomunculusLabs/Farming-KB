# Ketamine Anaesthetic Realm

Turner dedicates a full chapter to [[ketamine]], titled "The [[ketamine-ultimate-psychedelic-journey]]
Journey," rating its intensity at "10 -- Infinity." He calls it "the most
intense, bizarre, and enjoyable psychedelic I've tried," a view shared by most
of his experienced acquaintances.

## Nature and Clinical Background

Ketamine (marketed as Ketalar by Parke Davis) is a general anesthetic
considered "gentle" enough for children and [[cannabis-elderly-patients]]. Its psychedelic
properties were discovered after recipients reported "reemergence" experiences
while coming out of anesthesia. A dose much smaller than the anesthetic dose
produces an experience of incredible intensity. Turner notes that psychedelic
self-experimenter John Lilly injected ketamine 24 times a day for several
consecutive months without significant difficulties.

## Dosage and Administration

The standard [[psychedelic-dose-response]] is approximately 100 mg intramuscularly. Turner
describes a spectrum:
- 10-20 mg: non-psychedelic dissociative altered state
- 50 mg: semi-conscious halfway zone
- 75-125 mg: highly desirable ketamine trip
- Above this: tendency toward unconsciousness

It can also be snorted after evaporating the liquid to powder, but this
produces a less intense experience. Taking ketamine while already high on a
psychedelic such as 2C-B or LSD significantly widens the "dosage window" for
achieving a spectacular journey.

## The Anaesthetic Realm Experience

The ketamine high comes on about two minutes after injection. Turner describes
a break in the continuity of consciousness, after which he finds himself in a
swirling psychedelic universe with no concept of being high on a drug, no
recollection of ever having been born, had a personality, or known of planet
earth.

Key elements of the anaesthetic realm:

- **Hyperspace immersion**: "I feel like I'm in hyperspace, simultaneously
  connected to all things. Billions of images and perceptions are simultaneously
  flowing through my circuits."
- **Fourth-dimensional freedom**: Not bound into three dimensions. Experiences
  backwards and forwards in time, with the current moment as center of
  intensity.
- **Total dissolution**: The experience is one of being in total orgasm with
  the universe. Supports of reality, identity, and stability are dissolved at
  the speed of light, yet no fear is experienced.
- **The apex**: Some 30 minutes to an hour in, Turner reaches an apex where his
  will determines whether he and the universe exist, toggling between existence
  and non-existence many times within a second.
- **Alien rebirth**: The return to regular consciousness is experienced as an
  alien rebirth, with spectacular hallucinatory visuals resembling DMT visuals.

## The "GO Button" Sensation

On his first ketamine experience, Turner felt that his entire life up to that
point had been preparation, particularly his other psychedelic experiences, and
taking ketamine was like pressing the GO button. It felt as though the states
of mind he had broken through to in previous psychedelic peaks had become the
base reality.

## Personality Correlation

Dr. Igor Kungurtsev's research treating alcoholics with ketamine found a strong
correlation between personality type and experience quality. People who are
very controlled and have difficulties letting go often have negative
experiences -- the dissolving of the individual self is horrible for them. For
patients more relaxed and able to surrender, with a deep capacity to love, the
experience is usually blissful, even ecstatic.

Kungurtsev also found ketamine produced spiritual experiences and long-term
changes in spiritual outlook. Many patients reported contact with an ocean of
brilliant white light filled with love, bliss, and energy -- the Clear Light
experience.
