### Enzyme Diversity

Different mycorrhizal species exhibit different enzyme capabilities.
Conn and Dighton (2000) showed that the mycorrhizal communities on
pine roots exploiting oak and oak-pine mixed leaf litters had a higher
proportion of phosphatase-producing mycorrhizae than on pine alone.
This reflects adaptation to local nutrient conditions, as oak litter
immobilizes phosphorus during initial decomposition stages.
