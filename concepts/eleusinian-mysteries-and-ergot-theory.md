## The Annual Athenian Pilgrimage

Each year in the month of Boedromion (September–October), thousands of initiates —
including the most prominent citizens of Athens and, later, the entire Greek world —
embarked on a sacred procession from Athens to Eleusis, a distance of approximately
twenty kilometers along the Sacred Way. The pilgrimage was one of the defining events of
Athenian civic and religious life, and participation in the Mysteries was considered
essential for a meaningful existence. The procession culminated in a multi-day
initiation ceremony held within the Telesterion, a large hall at Eleusis where the
central secret rites were performed.
