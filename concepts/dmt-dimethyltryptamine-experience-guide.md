# DMT (Dimethyltryptamine) Experience Guide

DMT is one of the most powerful psychedelics known, rated intensity 9-10 by Turner. It exists as N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT, both naturally occurring and found in the human brain. Turner summarizes: "5-MeO-DMT feels like sheer force, whereas N,N-DMT feels like sheer perfection."

## Chemistry and Endogenous Presence

N,N-Dimethyltryptamine and 5-Methoxy-Dimethyltryptamine are naturally occurring psychedelics found in many plants worldwide, especially in the Amazon region. These alkaloids are also found in the human brain as neurotransmitters, and in blood, urine, and spinal fluid. DMT is produced in the human pineal gland, correlated with the "third eye" or Ajna Chakra in Indian spiritual systems. Meditative states attained by yogis concentrating on the third eye may result from increased DMT levels. DMT is also produced in high concentration in the glands of tropical toads (Bufo Alvarius) — some people make a practice of squeezing out this venom, drying and smoking it.

N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT are also manufactured synthetically as separate compounds and sold on the underground market. Synthetic DMT is used much more often than DMT from natural sources.

### Traditional Use
- **Yopo** (Anadenanthera peregrina): Made into snuff by Amazon natives. The most effective method is a two-person snorting device with tubes from each person's mouth to the other's nose. One "blows" the DMT snuff deep into the other's nasal cavity, then the receiver must quickly blow a dose back before falling into a tryptamine trance
- **Epena** (Virola theiodora): Another snuff preparation
- **Ayahuasca**: Oral preparation combining DMT-containing plants (Psychotria viridis) with Banisteriopsis caapi vine (source of [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]] for dangerous drug and food combinations
- Effects onset is nearly instantaneous; users should lie down or recline before taking a hit
- Users will neither be able to stand nor recognise their surroundings on a full dose
- The only reported case of physical harm involved the combination of Harmaline and 5-MeO-DMT
- 5-MeO increases pulse rate and blood pressure more than N,N-DMT, and can produce breathing irregularities

## The Experience

DMT is the most intense psychedelic experience commonly available. The onset is instantaneous (seconds when smoked), and the experience typically lasts 5-15 minutes for N,N-DMT, though subjective time dilation can make it feel much longer. In the Sixties, DMT was called "The businessman's lunch trip."

### N,N-DMT Characteristics
- Psilocybin's active component (psilocin) is 4-OH-DMT — explaining the chemical similarity of mushroom experiences
- Extremely intense visual and cognitive effects
- Complete dissolution of normal reality
- Comes on a bit slower and less forcefully than 5-MeO, enchanting the smoker with its magical nature
- Spectacular visuals — more swirling, kaleidoscopic universes per square millimeter than anything else
- Mixture of kaleidoscopic-geometric forms, archetypal symbols, and outlandish images
- Unique "selective anesthetic" property — body and mind feel simultaneously stimulated and anesthetized
- Encounters with seemingly autonomous entities reported by many users (gnomes, discarnate spirits, "skull clowns")
- Turner: "I've often found myself transported to a fairy tale world where little gnomes pop out of nowhere, laugh, and smile at me"
- Visuals best in diffused sunlight (unlike [[psilocybin-mushroom-distribution-worldwide]] share a quality absent from [[ketamine-ultimate-psychedelic-journey]]: a sense of interacting with an intelligent entity vastly superior in knowledge and breadth of consciousness, aware of what's transpiring in one's mind, able to instruct and tailor the experience personally

### Guiding the Experience
- On a full dose of DMT, one cannot guide the trip with the mind or thoughts the way an LSD trip can be guided
- One can, however, direct the trip by what Turner calls the "intent of the soul" or will
- What one will experience is impossible to predict — it can range from heaven to hell, cyberspace to jeweled palaces, contacts with extraterrestrials to adventures with fairies

## DMT with Harmala Alkaloids

Harmala alkaloids potentiate DMT not only in intensity and duration, but also in content and quality. The Harmala alkaloids also make DMT orally active — the method by which DMT is traditionally consumed in ayahuasca-preparation|ayahuasca brews of the Amazon region.

### Preparation
Prior to smoking or ingesting the DMT, one should wait until feeling the effects of the Harmala alkaloids. This prevents the DMT from being metabolized prematurely.

### Effects on N,N-DMT
- Smoking 15-20 mg of N,N-DMT while on Harmala produces an experience as intense as smoking 40 mg alone
- This is especially useful because 40 mg of harsh-tasting DMT is difficult to smoke in one hit
- Harmala extends the N,N-DMT experience to about 30-40 minutes total
- The experience becomes a much slower, more personal process — rather than feeling "blown out of my mind," one feels personally involved
- Increased awareness of mind, body, and subtle energy
- Often feels as though body and Being are "embraced" by an ancient earth spirit
- The DMT high does not "disappear" suddenly — more time to absorb and integrate the experience
- No degradation of the N,N-DMT quality with Harmala — it remains like a rocket to another dimension

### Effects on 5-MeO-DMT
- Harmala only slightly intensifies 5-MeO-DMT — still need ~5 mg for a powerful high
- Prolongs the normally 2-3 minute peak to 10-20 minutes, with total experience lasting 40 minutes to 2 hours
- Slows the onset drastically, potentially losing some of the brilliance experienced with 5-MeO alone
- Some users feel the combination is "warbly" and lacks the "Diamond Consciousness" of DMT alone

### Safety Warning
The combination of 5-MeO-DMT and Harmala produces the most powerful experience Turner has ever felt — both physically and mentally overwhelming. There is a high percentage of intensely unpleasant experiences: Turner has become violently nauseous, witnessed extreme discomfort, and one friend felt he had entered an ill-intentioned pact with a spirit, went into convulsions and bit the inside of his mouth. Turner and most experienced users have reverted to taking 5-MeO-DMT alone.
