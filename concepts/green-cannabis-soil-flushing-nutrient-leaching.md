# Soil Flushing and Nutrient Leaching

Soil flushing is an emergency corrective technique used when cannabis
plants develop severe [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-nutrient-management-ppm]] where it cannot access essential elements.
- pH levels have drifted far outside the acceptable range and cannot be
  corrected through normal pH adjustment methods.
- Contaminants or unknown substances have been introduced to the soil.
- An emergency transplant reveals severely compromised root conditions.

## The Soil Flush Procedure (Standard Pot with Drainage)

The step-by-step process for flushing a plant in a pot with drainage
holes:

1. Tilt the plant on its side and slowly pour water into the pot until
   the soil is completely saturated.
2. Wait until the water flows out the bottom of the pot, then examine
   the color of the runoff.
3. Repeat this process until the color of the water becomes clearer. The
   soil will get very muddy during flushing, and some mud will pass out
   with the water.
4. Once the water runs relatively clear, tilt the plant on its side again
   and wait for all water to drain. If the feeding solution does not have
   a noticeable color, watch the texture and smell of the runoff instead
   -- when these change, the flush has worked.
5. Quickly move the plant to a warm, dry area and wait for the soil to
   dry out.
6. Use a pH meter to test the pH of the soil and proceed with pH
  correction if needed.

## Emergency Flush (Pot Without Drainage Holes)

For containers without drainage, a more involved procedure is required:

1. Use a screwdriver to create drainage holes if the plastic is soft
  enough, then follow the standard procedure.
2. If holes cannot be made, perform an emergency transplant: cut around
   the soil edge with a knife, extract the root ball carefully, and hold
   it under running water to flush contaminants directly.
3. When the water running through the root ball changes color or
   quality, quickly place the plant into a new pot with fresh soil.
4. Fill gaps with new soil and use a stick for support if needed.
5. Move to a warm, dry area to recover.
