## Balanced Mineral Nutrition

### Albrecht's Insight
- Plants need balanced minerals, not just NPK
- Excess of one mineral can block uptake of another (antagonism)
- Calcium:magnesium ratio is critical for soil structure
- Ideal base saturation: Ca 65-75%, Mg 10-20%, K 2-5%

### Deficiency Symptoms by Nutrient

| Nutrient | Deficiency Symptom | Mobile in Plant? |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | Yellowing of older leaves, stunted growth | Yes |
| Phosphorus | Purplish leaves, poor root development | No |
| Potassium | Brown leaf edges, weak stems | Yes |
| Calcium | Blossom end rot, distorted new growth | No |
| Magnesium | Interveinal chlorosis (yellow between veins) | Yes |
| Iron | Interveinal chlorosis on NEW leaves | No |
| Zinc | Small leaves, short internodes | No |
| Boron | Hollow stems, cracked roots, poor fruit set | No |
