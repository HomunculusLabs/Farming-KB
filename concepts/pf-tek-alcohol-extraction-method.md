pulverization. Place the dried mushroom material into a small, strong zip-lock
freezer bag. Cover the bag with a magazine for protection and pound it
thoroughly with the rubber heel of a large shoe until the material is reduced
to a fine powder.

Transfer the pulverized material into a bottle with a tight-fitting, leak-proof
cap. Add enough 200 proof ethyl alcohol to cover the mushroom material and
create a loose slurry. Cap the bottle securely, shake it well, and let it sit
for 24 hours. Shake the bottle periodically during the soaking period to
activate the extraction process and keep the alcohol in contact with all of the
mushroom material.
