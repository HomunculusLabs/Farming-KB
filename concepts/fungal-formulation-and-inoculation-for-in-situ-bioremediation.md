### Key Findings from Field Trials

- White rot fungi growth is frequently limited by carbon and nitrogen in soil
- Nutrient supplementation in unsterile soils often benefits indigenous fungi more than inocula
- Benomyl (115 ppm) selectively benefits white rot fungi, which are relatively resistant
- Adding [[cervantes-organic-amendments-chart]] alone can sometimes improve degradation more than fungal inoculation
- In one study, a colonizing fungus actually impeded pyrene degradation
- 45 white-rot and 4 brown-rot fungi were screened; 22 white-rot species could not colonize soil

### Critical Success Factors

- Matching fungal species to substrate and environmental conditions
- Adequate carbon source supplementation
- Protection from competition (selective biocides like benomyl)
- Sufficient moisture maintenance
- Appropriate inoculum density
- Realistic expectations about degradation rates
