# Garlic Growing - Organic Methods

Sometimes classified as an herb, garlic is one of the easiest
vegetables to grow, needing only a warm, sunny location. It is sown
from cloves, the individual segments of the bulb, and can be dried
and stored for year-round use. Garlic is a member of the allium
family alongside onions, shallots, and leeks, and shares many of
their growing requirements. Its reputation as a companion plant
that helps deter pests makes it especially valuable in the organic
garden.

## Choosing Varieties

Garlic is grown from cloves rather than seed. California White
performs well in most climates, while Silver Rose stores very well.
For something big and beautiful, try Elephant Garlic, which produces
large bulbs with a milder flavor. Select plump, firm cloves from
reliable sources for the best results. Avoid using supermarket
garlic, which may be treated with sprout inhibitors or unsuited to
local conditions.
