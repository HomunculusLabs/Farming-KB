## Grazing in Zone 4

Zone 4 can support seasonal or opportunistic grazing. Mollison describes
several grazing patterns:
- Silvopasture: cattle or sheep grazing under a managed forest canopy
- Seasonal grazing: moving livestock to Zone 4 during specific periods
  (acorn season for pigs, autumn pasture for cattle)
- Emergency grazing: using Zone 4 forage during drought when Zone 3
  pastures are depleted

Stocking rates must be conservative to prevent overgrazing of wild plant
communities. Electric fencing allows temporary paddock subdivision. Livestock
in Zone 4 require regular inspection but less frequent management than in
Zone 3.

## Water Harvesting in Zone 4

Zone 4 catchments feed dams and water storage in lower zones. Mollison
emphasizes maintaining forest cover in upper catchments to ensure clean,
reliable water flow. Springs emerging in Zone 4 should be protected and
developed for stock water or gravity-fed supply to lower zones. Swales and
check dams in Zone 4 gullies slow runoff and increase infiltration, reducing
erosion and improving downstream water quality. This relates to
[[backyard-water-harvesting]] principles.
