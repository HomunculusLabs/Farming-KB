## Super Lemon Haze

> *"A true modern classic."* — S.T. Oner, *Cannabis Sativa Vol 3*

Super Lemon Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by [[skunk-1|lemon-skunk]] and [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] of the modern era. Known for
its intense lemony aroma, balanced social effects, and generous yields, Super Lemon Haze
made an immediate splash when first introduced and continues to be widely popular among
both recreational and medical consumers.

- **Indoor Yield:** 800g per square yard

## Terpene Profile

Super Lemon Haze lives up to its name with a limonene-dominant terpene profile
responsible for its signature intense lemony aroma. Supporting terpenes include:

- **[[myrcene]]** — Herbal, musky; contributes to sedative body effects
- **[[pinene]]** — Piney; promotes mental clarity and focus

The interplay of these terpenes produces a scent and flavor that is unmistakably
lemon-forward with a complex Hazey aftertaste. S.T. Oner recounts that even his
mother could taste a distinct lemon flavor cutting through other strains — a
testament to the intensity of its terpene expression.

## Aroma and Flavor

The smell is an intense lemony aroma that fills a room quickly. On the palate,
users report bright citrus notes followed by a smooth, earthy Haze finish.
The flavor lingers, making it a favorite for those who appreciate terpene-rich
varieties. The lemon character is so pronounced that it stands out even in blind
tastings among other citrus-forward strains.

## Effects

Super Lemon Haze delivers a balanced high that is both cerebral and physically
relaxing. Key characteristics include:

- **Social and Euphoric** — Described as "incredibly social," making it a popular
  choice for gatherings and creative collaboration
- **Physically Relaxed** — Body effects are present but not overpowering, avoiding
  the couch-lock associated with heavy indicas
- **Balanced** — The sativa-dominant genetics provide mental energy while the Haze
  lineage smooths the experience into a sustained, even plateau
- **Duration** — Effects are long-lasting, typical of Haze-dominant varieties
