# Critical Haze Strain Profile

## Overview

Critical Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Spain's Resin Seeds of Barcelona. It represents a deliberate effort to combine the soaring cerebral effects of Haze genetics with the heavy yields that indica-dominant Critical Mass provides. The result is what Resin Seeds describes as "a near-perfect marriage of sativa highs and indica yields." Critical Haze exemplifies the modern European breeding approach of selecting sativa-dominant plants that yield like indicas while retaining the psychoactive complexity that sativa enthusiasts demand.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Breeder**: Resin Seeds, Barcelona, Spain
- **Genetics**: Amnesia Haze (HP reverted) × Critical Mass (female clone)
- **Sativa/Indica Ratio**: Sativa-dominant
- **THC Content**: 18-22%
- **Family Lines**: Haze → Amnesia → Amnesia Haze on one side; Afghani → Skunk → Big Bud → Critical Mass on the other

The Amnesia Haze parent is specifically the "HP" reverted version (referring to the original Dutch Haze line, not Soma's Amnesia Haze). Critical Mass is a particularly vigorous selection known for its enormous yields and resistance to mold.

## Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

- Medium height with bushy growth habit — more compact than typical Haze-dominant strains
- Vigorous [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] inherited from the Critical Mass parent
- Side branching is more developed than pure sativa lines
- Structure is bushier and more manageable than heavier [[african-landrace-sativa-strains]]
- Responds well to [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-training-techniques]] that maximize light exposure

### Cultivation Requirements

- **Light**: Performs best with maximum available light — pack extra T5s or HPS bulbs
- **Indoor**: Well-suited to [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]] due to manageable height
- **Outdoor**: Excellent in Mediterranean climates; large plants possible
- **Pest resistance**: Good general resistance to pests
- **Mold awareness**: The one vulnerability — monitor closely as buds fatten to maturity
- **Flowering time**: 10-11 weeks
- **Nutrient demands**: Moderate to heavy feeder during flowering

### Yield Performance

- **Indoor**: Minimum 500 grams per square yard of grow space
- **Outdoor**: Minimum 400 grams per plant
- Yield is one of the standout features — significantly above average for sativa-dominant genetics
- The Critical Mass inheritance is clearly expressed in bud size and density

## Bud Morphology

Critical Haze produces visually distinctive colas:

- **Shape**: Spear-like, elongated colas characteristic of Haze lineage
- **Structure**: Stunning foxtail buds that form in slow motion during the long flowering period
- **Appearance**: Deep green with a silky-soft velvet texture at maturity
- **Leaf pattern**: Tear-shaped serrated leaves fan out from the buds in an ornamental display
- **Trichome coverage**: Heavy [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] consistent with both parent lines
- **Density**: Buds are dense and weighty — the Critical Mass influence is unmistakable

The visual contrast between the Haze-like spear shape and the indica-like bud density is a hallmark of this cross. The buds look like sativas but feel like indicas when handled.

## Aroma and Flavor

### Aroma

- Primary character is "Pure Haze" — spice and tang dominate
- A subtle hint of something sour and fruity underneath the Haze spice
- Complex and layered — not a one-note strain
- Aroma intensity increases significantly during the final weeks of flowering

### Flavor

- Haze-forward: all spice and tang on the inhale
- The fruity undertone becomes more apparent during the exhale
- Slightly sour finish that lingers on the palate
- Smoke is smooth for a sativa-dominant strain with this potency level

## Effects

### Psychoactive Profile

- **Cerebral intensity**: Strong, long-lasting, and decidedly cerebral
- **Duration**: Extended effect — the Haze lineage provides sustained psychoactivity
- **Body component**: Just enough body buzz to prevent the high from becoming racy or anxious
- **Functional potential**: The body relaxation component makes this more versatile than pure sativas
- **Potency**: 18-22% THC provides significant psychoactive impact

The balance between the soaring Amnesia Haze cerebral effects and the grounding Critical Mass body influence creates a high that is powerful but not overwhelming. This is a strain that can be enjoyed socially or in focused creative pursuits.
