## Confirmatory Thin-Layer Chromatography

All HPLC results were qualitatively confirmed by thin-layer chromatography
(TLC) using butanol-acetic acid-water (12:3:5) as the mobile phase. TLC
served as an independent orthogonal verification method, providing a
secondary line of evidence that the HPLC peaks being quantified were
indeed psilocybin and psilocin rather than co-eluting contaminants or
degradation products. The dual-method approach strengthened confidence
in the reported quantitative values and was standard practice in the
era before mass spectrometric detection became widely available.
