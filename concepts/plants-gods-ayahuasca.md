# Ayahuasca and Banisteriopsis caapi

**Source:** Plants of the Gods (Schultes & Hofmann) | **Updated:** 2026-04-24

## Distribution and Habitat

Banisteriopsis caapi is found throughout the tropical zones of northern South America, including the Amazon basin of Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Ecuador, Bolivia, and Venezuela, as well as parts of the West Indies and the Pacific coastal slopes of the Colombian and Ecuadorean Andes. The liana thrives in lowland tropical rainforest, often climbing high into the forest canopy. It is frequently cultivated by indigenous communities near their settlements to ensure a ready supply for ceremonial use.

The plant is propagated both from seeds and from stem cuttings. Mature lianas can be harvested for their bark, which is the primary psychoactive part. The bark is typically scraped, beaten, or mashed before being boiled for extended periods — sometimes for many hours or even days — to produce the finished Ayahuasca beverage. The long preparation time is itself often a ceremonial activity, accompanied by prayers and songs.

## Overview

Ayahuasca — known variously as Caapi, Natema, Pinde, Yaje, and Yage — is the most important psychoactive preparation of the Amazon basin and one of the most widely used hallucinogenic beverages in the world. Prepared from the bark of forest lianas of the genus banisteriopsis caapi (family Malpighiaceae), the drink has been employed ceremonially for centuries by indigenous peoples throughout the western Amazon and on the Pacific slopes of the Colombian and Ecuadorean Andes.

The name Ayahuasca derives from the Kechwa language, meaning "tendril of the soul" — a reference to the frequent experience during intoxication that the soul separates from the body, communicating with ancestors and the forces of the spirit world. As described by the anthropologist Reichel-Dolmatoff, the drinking of Caapi represents a return "to the maternal womb, to the source and origin of all things," during which participants report seeing "all the tribal divinities, the creation of the universe, the first human beings and animals and even the establishment of the social order."
