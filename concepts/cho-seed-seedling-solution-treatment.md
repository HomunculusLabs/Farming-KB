## Cho's Seed and Seedling Solution Treatment

The Seed/Seedling Solution (SES) treatment is a fundamental practice in
Dr. Cho's Korean [[cho-fermented-plant-juice-preparation]]: 2 ml
-   [[cho-water-soluble-calcium-and-potassium]]: 1 ml — when
    seedlings are overgrown and soft (calcium firms tissue).

This diagnostic choice embodies a key KNF principle: weak seedlings need
nitrogen (FAA), while overly lush seedlings need calcium to strengthen
structure.

## Treatment Duration by Seed Type

-   **Fast-germinating** (potato, taro, ginger, garlic): 30 min to 1 hour
-   **Moderately fast** (cabbage, radish, bean): 2 hours
-   **Average speed** (cucumber, melon, squash): 4-5 hours
-   **Slow-germinating** (rice, barley, tomato): 7-8 hours

The deliberately brief period preserves internal nutrient reserves while
activating metabolic processes and beneficial microbial colonization.

### Application Protocol

1.  Mix all liquids and set aside 3-5 hours for microorganism propagation.
2.  Combine with charcoal, soil, and manure.
3.  Normal fields: apply 7 days before seeding/transplanting.
4.  Cultivation disorder fields: apply 14 days before seeding.
5.  Scatter IMO-4 in small lumps (minimum 1500 kg/acre) to build
    aggregated soil structure.
6.  Apply 2-3 hours before sunset for optimal microbial establishment.
7.  Follow up with additional applications at middle and late growth
    stages.
