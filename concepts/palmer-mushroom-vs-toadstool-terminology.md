# Palmer on Mushroom vs Toadstool Terminology

Julius Auboineau Palmer devoted considerable effort throughout his writings
to correcting what he called the most fundamental error in popular
understanding of fungi: the idea that "mushrooms" and "toadstools" are
two different kinds of organisms. In his view, this misconception lay at
the root of most dangerous ignorance about fungi.

## The Core Distinction

Palmer stated emphatically: "Every mushroom is a toadstool, and every
toadstool is a mushroom." He compared the relationship to that between
synonymous terms like "plant" and "vegetable," "bush" and "shrub," or
"rock" and "stone." The two words, he argued, are entirely
interchangeable when speaking of fungi in popular language.
