## Hybrid Classifications

Seed producers categorize strains into several hybrid groups:

- Pure Sativa: A pure species with no crossbreeding.
- Sativa: Mostly Sativa species with some Indica influence.
- Pure Indica: A pure species with no crossbreeding.
- Indica: Mostly Indica species with some Sativa influence.
- Indica/Sativa: A 50/50 cross between the two species.
- Ruderalis: Pure species or Ruderalis crosses.

## The High Spectrum

- Pure Sativa = Total head high
