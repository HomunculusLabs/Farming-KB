# Mobile Greenhouses and Winter Harvest Seed Varieties

## Overview

[[mobile-greenhouses]] represent one of the most innovative approaches to year-round growing, allowing a single structure to serve multiple plots of land through seasonal movement. Combined with carefully selected cold-tolerant varieties, mobile greenhouses enable growers to extend the harvest season dramatically. This page covers two [[mobile-greenhouse]] systems, row cover suppliers, seed companies specializing in winter harvest varieties, and Coleman's recommended variety list.

## Mobile Greenhouse Systems

### Rimol "Rolling Thunder"

Coleman collaborated with [[rimol-greenhouse-systems]] to design and develop a mobile greenhouse on wheels, sold under the name "Rolling Thunder." This system uses heavy-duty wheels with bearings attached to each hoop via specially designed "base posts" that ride on rails, allowing the greenhouse to move along desired growing areas.

**Design Features:**

- Heavy-duty wheels with bearings at each hoop position
- Base posts seated on rails that guide movement
- Can accommodate large structures (up to 30×96 feet)
- Smaller greenhouses can be moved by just two people
- Larger structures can be moved with an ordinary tractor
- Significant internal bracing to prevent wind-induced movement
- Temporary bracing on end walls for stability during movement
- Flexible height options through different length base posts

**Installation:**

- Rails are laid flat according to desired greenhouse width
- Rails do not need to be pinned or staked but should sit on a solid base to prevent sinking
- The first few hoops are the trickiest, requiring several people since wheels, base posts, and hoops are erected simultaneously
- Once several hoops are up and diagonally braced, the rest of the greenhouse goes up quickly

**Supplier:** Rimol Greenhouse Systems, Hooksett, NH

### Four Season Tools

Coleman also worked with [[four-season-tools]] on their mobile greenhouse design, which incorporates drop-down side technology on both sides plus a full-length roof opening for ventilation. These houses are available in both wheeled and sled models, and the company offers conversion kits to make existing stationary greenhouses mobile.

**Design Features:**

- Drop-down sides on both walls for maximum ventilation access
- Full-length roof opening for superior summer airflow
- Two wheeled options available
- Sled model alternative (slides on ground rather than rolling on rails)
- Conversion kits for retrofitting stationary greenhouses
- Developing efficient small-farm tools alongside greenhouse systems

**Supplier:** Four Season Tools, Kansas City, MO

## Row Cover Fabrics

### Agribon

The primary row cover used for both greenhouse inner layers and quick-hoop covering. Available in different weights providing varying degrees of frost protection and light transmission.

**Supplier:** Johnny's Selected Seeds, Winslow, ME

### Covertan Pro 17

An alternative row cover fabric that Coleman has also used successfully in his operation.

**Supplier:** Suntex CP, Inc., Sarasota, FL

## Packaging Materials

Pure cellophane box liners and bags are used for packaging harvested greens. These must be ordered ahead in sufficient quantities to justify the special sizes. Cellophane is preferred over plastic for its breathability and environmental profile.

**Supplier:** ATLAPAC, Columbus, OH

## Seed Suppliers for Winter Harvest

### Johnny's Selected Seeds (Winslow, ME)

Since 1973, Johnny's has supplied a wide range of seeds and tools to fresh market growers and home gardeners. They maintain an excellent selection of winter harvest varieties and are Coleman's primary seed source. Their catalog is particularly strong on cold-tolerant greens, root crops, and season-extension varieties.

### Territorial Seed Company (Cottage Grove, OR)

Territorial publishes a supplementary catalog in June specifically focused on winter gardening. This specialized catalog is a valuable resource for growers planning fall and winter plantings, offering variety recommendations and timing guidance for mild-winter climates.

### Fedco Seeds (Waterville, ME)

Fedco specializes in varieties for northern growers and short growing seasons. Their selection is particularly relevant for growers in cold climates who need varieties that mature quickly and tolerate cool conditions.

### Wood Prairie Farm (Bridgewater, ME)

Supplier of organic 'Rose Gold' seed potatoes, a variety that Coleman grows and recommends for winter greenhouse production.

### Graines Baumaux (Nancy, France)

The most extensive seed listing of any catalog Coleman knows, with particularly strong representation of old-time French varieties. Their full listing is available online. This source provides access to European varieties not commonly available from American seed companies.
