## Garden Soil Terminology and Molecular Biology Glossary

A comprehensive reference of key terms used in soil science, plant biology, and molecular biology as applied to gardening and plant nutrition.

## Molecular and Cellular Biology

- **actin filaments:** Threads that serve as structural support for the cell, part of transportation infrastructure and communication network
- **adenosine triphosphate (ATP):** Energy currency of life; two phosphate-to-phosphate bonds that when broken provide energy
- **[[staycare-degradation-of-plant-cell-wall-polymers-by-fungi]]:** Strong, lattice-like structure surrounding plant cells; primarily cellulose
- **chloroplasts:** Organelles that contain chlorophyll
- **chlorophyll:** Molecule absorbing energy from light; gives plants their green color
- **chlorosis:** Inability of leaves to produce enough chlorophyll; yellow leaves surrounding greener veins
- **chromoplasts:** Cells containing red, yellow, and orange pigments in flowers, fruits, and some roots
- **collenchyma:** Thick-walled cells providing support and flexibility
- **cytoplasm:** Liquid and organelles (minus nucleus) that make up a cell
- **cytosol:** Clear, jelly-like substance that is the major component of cytoplasm
- **dermal tissue:** Cells forming the "skin" of plants (epidermis and periderm)
- **ground tissue:** Cells providing most of the mass/bulk of a plant and its support
- **leucoplasts:** Colorless cells for storage of lipids and proteins in unexposed plant parts
- **meristematic tissue:** Undifferentiated plant cells with ability to become any type of cell
- **parenchyma cell:** Specialized cells for metabolic functions that can become further specialized

## Soil Chemistry

- **anion:** Negatively charged molecule
- **cation:** Positively charged molecule
- **Casparian strip:** Single layer of wax-clogged cells at the endodermis of a root
- **humus:** Extremely stable [[diffusion-osmosis-and-active-transport-in-plants]]:** Using energy to move a molecule against its concentration gradient
- **aquaporin:** Membrane protein allowing water molecules to pass through
- **channel proteins:** Tunnel-like, gated proteins for passive ion movement across membranes
- **diffusion:** Passive movement of molecules from high to low concentration
- **integral membrane proteins:** Specialized proteins embedded in plasmalemma for transport
- **osmosis:** Diffusion of water across a semi-permeable membrane
- **passive transport:** Movement of molecules without energy input

## Nutrients

- **macronutrients:** Required in largest quantities: C, H, O, N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S
- **micronutrients:** Needed in minute quantities: Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, Mo, B, Cl, Ni
- **biofertilizer:** Fertilizer provided by [[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]]
- **nitrogen fixation:** Breaking strong bonds holding nitrogen molecules to make nitrogen biologically available
- **Law of Return:** Recycle all plant and animal waste to keep producing humus
- **Law of the Minimum:** Plant growth is limited by the least abundant mineral
- **hypha (pl. hyphae):** Thread-like filament formed by a fungus
- **mycorrhizal fungus:** Fungus forming symbiotic relationships with plants

## Other Terms

- **adhesion:** Tendency of dissimilar molecules to attach (e.g., water and glass)
- **cohesion:** Tendency of same type of molecules to stick together
- **carbohydrate:** Carbon-based molecules with H2O for each carbon atom
- **compound:** Two or more molecules of different elements
- **disaccharide:** Two glucose molecules linked together
- **Golgi apparatus:** Organelle responsible for protein transport
- **lipids:** Organic molecules insoluble in water (fats, waxes, steroids, triglycerides)
- **oxidation:** Loss of electrons in a chemical reaction
