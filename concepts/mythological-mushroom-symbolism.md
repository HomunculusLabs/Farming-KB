## Mythological Mushroom Symbolism

The symbolic vocabulary through which mushrooms — particularly [[amanita-muscaria]] — were encoded in world mythology, religious art, and cultural traditions. [[james-arthur]] catalogs 40+ names and symbols that he argues all refer to the mushroom across cultures, representing different views of the same organism at various stages of its lifecycle.

## The Mushroom Lifecycle as Mythological Template

The Amanita muscaria lifecycle provides a natural template for mythological narratives:

### Stage 1: The Egg/Button (Infant State)
- Small, white, round — resembles a stone or egg
- "Born from a stone" — Mithra's birth myth
- The Cosmic Egg, the Easter Egg (Easter = mushroom hunt)
- The "Small White Stone" of Revelation 2:17
- "Pulling the sword from the stone" — Arthurian legend (wielding power through knowledge of the mushroom)

### Stage 2: Emergence (Snake-Like)
- Pushing upward from the bulbous base like a snake hatching
- Shedding the universal veil = shedding skin
- The serpent in the Garden of Eden — reveals secret knowledge
- The [[feathered-serpent]] ([[quetzalcoatl]]/Kukulcan) — cosmopolitan symbol
- After full growth and decay, leaves a hole with white spore powder — a "snake's hole" or lair

### Stage 3: Full Growth (Mushroom Form)
- Cap and stem resemble vulva and phallus — the hermaphroditic/alchemical union
- The Holy Grail — upturned cap shape, "vessel containing the blood of the God"
- The Fountain of Youth, the Fountain of Living Waters (90% water, fountain-shaped)
- The "One-Eyed Howler" — round eye shape representing cosmic vision

### Stage 4: Upturned Cap (Mature/Decaying)
- Gills exposed, resembling feathers or wings
- The Phoenix — gold and red cap as bird, gills as feathers; dissolves in heat (sun), leaving only spores (ashes), then regenerates
- The Thousand-Armed Bodhisattva (gills as arms)
- Winged Isis (gills as wings)
- The Egyptian Scarab (gills as wings, disc body)
- The Aten (winged disc of Egypt)

### Stage 5: Dissolution and Return
- Mushroom dissolves, leaving spores
- "From the ashes" — Phoenix regeneration cycle
- The World Tree — mushroom as world-creator in many cultures

## The Complete Symbolic Catalog

Arthur catalogs these cross-cultural names/symbols for the mushroom:

### Food and Drink Names
- Soma (Hindu plant-god; also Greek for "body")
- Amrita (Buddhist magical sacrament / elixir of immortality)
- Ambrosia (Greek "food of the gods")
- Haoma (Zoroastrian sacrament)
- Manna (Hebrew; means "mushroom" per Arthur; two kinds in Bible — John 6-14)
- Bread of Life / Loaf of Bliss
- Hidden Manna (Revelation 2:17)
- Celestial Food / Divine Food / Tchefau (Egyptian Book of the Dead)
- Waters of Life (Ankh = waters and life)
- Fountain of Living Waters

### Object and Artifact Symbols
- The Holy Grail (cup shape = upturned cap; "vessel containing blood of God")
- The Golden Fleece
- The Fountain of Youth (Amanita grows under Longleaf Pine in northern Florida in December — Ponce de Leon "likely kicked over" what he sought)
- The Prima Materia / Philosopher's Stone (alchemical secret substance)
- The Hammer of Thor (mushroom-shaped; thrown to earth in lightning)
- The [[djed-pillar]] (Egyptian, shaped like mushroom not cedar)
- The Ankh (Egyptian symbol for waters of life)
- The Cosmic Egg / Easter Egg
- The Eye of Horus (Djed-Eye)

### Deity and Being Names
- Rudra (Hindu red god of the forest)
- The Feathered Serpent (Quetzalcoatl)
- The Phoenix
- Celestial Food (personified as Horus — "Horus is both the divine food and the sacrifice")

### Title and Role Names
- Flesh of the God / Divine Flesh
- Fruit of the Tree of Life / Fruit of the Tree of Knowledge
- Flesh of Jesus ("Take and eat, this is my body/soma")
- The God of a Thousand Names (Egyptian reference)

### Nature and Element Associations
- Lightning (mushrooms mythologically grow where lightning strikes)
- Snake/Serpent (growth pattern from button, dissolution leaving holes)
- Stone/Rock/Egg (button stage)
- Fire (red cap, burning/dissolution)
- Blood (red color, Amanita's red juices)

## Cross-Cultural Blood-Origin Myths

A recurring motif across traditions: a deity's blood falls to earth and produces the mushroom:
- **Egyptian**: Hu and Saa created from drops of blood when Ra cut his phallus
- **Norse**: Odin's horses' blood-mingled spit hitting the ground sprouts Amanita
- **Hindu**: Rudra murdered, severed red penis returns yearly as the mushroom
- **Egyptian**: Osiris's severed phallus replaced by Isis using a living tree (Cedar/Djed)

## The Androgynous Mushroom
The mushroom's stem (phallus) and cap (vulva) in a single organism made it a natural symbol for the union of opposites:
- The alchemical hermaphrodite
- Hari Hari (Shiva + Vishnu combined, depicted as androgynous)
- Bas-reliefs at Angkor showing mushroom disc separated from stem as "separation of the sexes"
- Nymphs worshipping the phallus to provoke union with the universal female disc
