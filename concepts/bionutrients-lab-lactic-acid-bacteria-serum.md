**Updated:** 2026-04-24

Lactic acid bacteria (LAB) serum is one of the major workhorse beneficial [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]]. LAB is popularly used in composting to arrest foul odors associated with anaerobic decomposition. It thrives and feeds on the ammonia released during decomposition, converting wastes into harmless forms through natural biological processes.
