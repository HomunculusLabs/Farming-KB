### The Farmer's Poverty

Fukuoka found that California farmers selling fruit and vegetables
at roadside stands offered a bag of oranges or three large melons
for one dollar, yet complained about how hard it was to make ends
meet. The farmers growing for export were not getting rich. The
wealth was captured by the middlemen, the processors, and the
corporations that controlled the distribution system.

The real reason the American farmer had to sell crops to other
countries was poverty, not abundance. A farmer with a better diet
and a richer, happier life would have no need to export crops.
