# Cannabis Seed Selection and Seed Banks

Greg Green's [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-breeding-genetics-and-true-breeding]] devotes Chapter 2 to the critical topic
of [[solomon-choosing-seeds-and-catalogs]] and selecting a reputable seed bank. Selecting the
right seeds is one of the most important decisions a grower makes, as
genetics fundamentally determine the potential of every plant.

## The Seed Market

There are approximately 450 seed varieties of Cannabis on the market.
Of these, about 200 are worth considering, and roughly 50 are truly
outstanding. Each strain is either a pure species type (two plants of
the same species crossed together) or a crossbreed of two or more
species. Many unstable hybrid strains are mixed so extensively that they
cannot be classified as either Sativa or Indica.

Seed producers tend to offer the following strain categories:

- Pure Sativa
- Sativa (mostly Sativa with some Indica)
- Pure Indica
- Indica (mostly Indica with some Sativa)
- Indica/Sativa (50/50 cross)
- Ruderalis and Ruderalis crosses

## Choosing a Seed Bank

When selecting a seed bank, Green advises growers to consider several
factors. Some seed banks and breeders exaggerate THC levels, while
others provide accurate measurements. It is best to consult your seed
bank or breeder directly for details about the strains they offer.

Seed banks are not responsible for male/female ratios in their seeds,
as this is not under their control. However, if a grower receives very
poor ratios (such as all males from 15 seeds), a sincere letter to the
seed bank may result in free seeds or a discount on the next order.

## What to Look For in Choosing Seeds

Growers should check seed catalogues for the following information:

- THC levels (to understand potency potential)
- Strain type (pure species or hybrid ratio)
- Flowering times
- Expected height at maturity
- High type (head high, body stone, or ratio thereof)
- Indoor or outdoor suitability
- Yield expectations

The goal is to choose a plant that fits your needs in terms of height,
potency, and high type. There is no point trying to grow an 8-foot
Sativa in a small indoor space if the strain requires it.

## Seed Viability

[[seed-quality-and-selection-solomon]] matters enormously for germination success:

- White seeds are immature and will not germinate. Avoid them.
- Look for seeds with white and grey markings or other colors.
- Crushed seeds will not germinate.
- Old seeds may have trouble germinating.
- Always try to use the best seeds available.

## The Life Cycle and Timing

Understanding the life cycle of the Cannabis plant is essential when
planning your grow:

- Germination: 1 to 2 weeks
- Seedling stage: 2 to 3 weeks
- [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]: 3 weeks to several months (grower controlled)
- Pre-flowering: 1 to 2 weeks
- Flowering: 6 to 10 weeks ([[strain-dependent-alkaloid-tissue-distribution-cap-stem-psilocybe-cubensis]])
