## JADAM Pesticide for Sucking Insects

Stinkbugs, thrips, whiteflies, leafhoppers, planthoppers, and fruit flies require the strongest JADAM pesticide formulations. These tough-shelled insects resist basic treatments and need high concentrations of [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] combined with potent [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] (Korean pasque flower root or gingko).

## Formulations (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

### Stinkbugs, Thrips, Whiteflies, Fruit Flies (strongest)

**Standard**:
- JWA: 10L (2.6 gal)
- Korean pasque flower root (KPFR) JHS: 15L (4 gal)

**Emergency** (heavy infestation):
- JWA: 15L (4 gal)
- KPFR JHS: 20L (5.3 gal)

**Soil application** (half-strength):
- JWA: 5L + KPFR JHS: 7.5L per 500L

### Planthoppers, Leafhoppers, Mulberry Suckers

**Standard**:
- JWA: 10L (2.6 gal)
- Gingko JHS: 15L (4 gal)

**Soil application** (half-strength):
- JWA: 5L + Gingko JHS: 7.5L per 500L

### Rice Comprehensive (planthopper + borer + armyworm + blast + sheath blight)

- JWA: 5L + JS 2L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L + KPFR JHS 5L

## Pests Controlled

Bean bug (Riptortus clavatus), black stinkbug, silverleaf whitefly, greenhouse whitefly, tangerine thrips, pear psylla, winter cherry bug, fruit fly, flea beetle, onion fly, mosquito, grasshopper, citrus flatid planthopper, mulberry sucker, rice green leafhopper, brown planthopper, turnip moth

## Preparation Steps

1. Test water with JWA -- must be soft water
2. Mixture test: 0.5L water + 5cc each, shake, check foam + no clotting
3. For stinkbugs/black stinkbugs: use JWA 10L+ and KPFR JHS 15L+
4. Half-fill tank, add ingredients one by one, stir each
5. Top up with water, filter, use same day

## Application Timing

- Spray when insects are active: early morning or evening
- Can spray 2x per day or every 1-2 days when JS is not in the mix
- For black stinkbugs: JWA 10L+ and KPFR JHS 15L+
- Intensive early in growth season (soft new growth attracts suckers)
- Do not continue use with JWA exceeding 10L -- concentration can damage plants

## Trap Crop Integration

JADAM recommends cultural controls alongside pesticide:
- Plant trap crops (e.g., sorghum for stinkbugs, mustard for flea beetles) around field borders
- Monitor trap crops -- they concentrate pests for targeted treatment
- Treat trap crops heavily while reducing pesticide on main crop
- Remove and destroy trap crops after pest peak

## Systemic vs. Contact Action

JADAM pesticides are primarily contact-action:
- JWA provides the wetting and penetrating power to reach insects hidden in plant crevices
- JHS delivers plant alkaloids that act on contact
- Soil application at half-strength can provide some systemic protection as plants absorb JHS compounds
- KPFR JHS is the strongest herb available -- controls almost all pests including those resistant to Jerusalem artichoke or gingko

## Substitutions

- KPFR can be replaced with oleander (for stinkbugs)
- Gingko can be replaced with bracken or KPFR
- Both formulations (no. 8 and no. 9) are stronger than aphid/mite (no. 6) and moth (no. 7)
- No. 9 includes the effects of no. 6, 7, and 8

## Concentration Warning

Adding 0.5kg fine red clay powder increases effectiveness but do NOT exceed JWA 10L for continuous use. High JWA can damage the white powdery coating of fruits and stun growth.
