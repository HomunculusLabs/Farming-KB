# Water-Soluble Potassium (WP)

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

Water-Soluble Potassium (WP) is a KNF mineral input extracted from tobacco stems. Potassium regulates water use, activates enzymes, facilitates photosynthate translocation, and is critical for fruit quality, disease resistance, and stress tolerance.

## Source
Master Cho's Korean Natural Farming Recipe Book

## Functions of Potassium in Plants

1. Activates starch-synthesizing enzyme and facilitates translocation of photosynthate
2. Helps relocate storage substances in starch crops
3. Principal function: growing meristematic tissues
4. Regulates water use and plant stomata -- potassium content in guard cells controls opening/closing
5. Promotes synthesis of CO2-fixing enzyme; decreases diffusive resistance of CO2 in leaves
6. Activates various enzyme reaction systems
7. Enhances absorption of moisture; improves resistance to drought and freezing
8. Reduces lodging problems; increases yield and improves crop quality
9. Bean plants require comparatively high amounts of potassium

## Potassium Mobility

- Potassium is a highly mobile element in the plant (always exists as an ion or easily ionizable form)
- Potassium content in leaves decreases rapidly during fruit enlargement because much is translocated to fruit
- Abundant in leaves, stem, and root tips as salt forms
- Can be translocated again from old tissues to young tissues

## Materials

- Tobacco stems (dried, ground -- not too finely)
- Water (100L per 1-2 kg tobacco stems)
- Hemp cloth bag
- Clay jar or Japanese cedar bucket

## Environmental Conditions

- Temperature: 23-25C
- Cool, shaded place with no direct sunlight

## Preparation Method

1. Dry tobacco stems thoroughly
2. Ground the dried stems (do NOT grind too finely -- avoid making powder)
3. Put 1-2 kg of ground tobacco stem into a hemp cloth bag
4. Dip the bag in 100L of water to dissolve potassium
5. This produces the "crude liquid of natural potassium"
6. Steeping takes about 7 days

## Usage

- WP must be used ALONE (not combined with other KNF mineral inputs in the same application)
- Dilution: 0.7L of crude potassium liquid per 20L of water

## Weather Precautions

This is critical: if WP is applied with nitrogen in hot and humid conditions, or on a rainy day, a synergy effect occurs that helps the plant absorb excessive nitrogen, resulting in overgrowth. Avoid applying WP:
- On rainy days
- During hot, humid weather
- In combination with nitrogen-rich inputs like [[knf-fish-amino-acid]] during warm periods

## Symptoms of Potassium Deficiency

1. Branch shows growth deterioration; maturity is stunted; yield/quality deteriorates
2. Rare in early growth stages -- symptoms typically appear after plants reach a certain size
3. Chlorosis starts from old leaves whose edges become yellowish-brown
4. In some plants, chlorosis appears as a spot in the middle of leaves
5. Roots and stems become thin; lignification of vascular bundles is suppressed, reducing cold resistance
6. Seed of fruits becomes small and matures late
7. Leaf tip may discolor and dry out even at peak growth

## Causes of Potassium Deficiency Beyond Soil Depletion

- Even with enough soil potassium, excess lime and magnesium suppress potassium absorption
- Easily occurs in sandy soil with low humus because potassium leaches like nitrogen
- Similar to nitrogen in mobility -- easily lost from sandy soils

## Symptoms of Excessive Potassium

1. Magnesium deficiency occurs if potassium level is excessive
2. Symptoms similar to gas disorder, appearing in the middle part of leaves
