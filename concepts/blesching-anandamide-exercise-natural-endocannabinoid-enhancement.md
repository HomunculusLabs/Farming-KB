## Anandamide Biochemistry

- **Production:** Synthesized in human cell membranes on demand
- **Receptor affinity:** Binds relatively equally to both CB1 and CB2 receptors
- **Degradation:** Broken down by fatty acid amide hydrolase (FAAH); inhibiting
  FAAH keeps anandamide active longer
- **Emotional keyword:** "I am at ease" — encompassing bliss, relaxation, and
  social connection

## The Runner's High: Anandamide, Not Endorphins

The traditional explanation for the "runner's high" attributed the effect to
endorphins. However, high-intensity endurance running has been shown to
significantly increase anandamide levels, not endorphins. This discovery
reframed exercise as an endocannabinoid-enhancing activity.

## Natural Anandamide Enhancement Strategies

### Exercise
High-intensity endurance running is the most well-documented method for
boosting anandamide. The effect is dose-dependent: longer and more intense
exercise produces greater anandamide increases. Other forms of aerobic exercise
likely produce similar effects.

### Social Connection
Meaningful social interactions may increase anandamide levels naturally. This
finding aligns with research showing that social isolation reduces endocannabinoid
tone while positive social bonding enhances it. The anandamide-oxytocin
connection suggests these systems work synergistically.

### Meditation
Meditation practices may increase anandamide levels naturally. Combined with
the stress-reducing effects of meditation on cortisol, this creates a dual
pathway for endocannabinoid enhancement through contemplative practice.
