# Cannabis and Bipolar Disorder

## Overview

Psychiatry considers manic-depressive disorder (bipolar affective disorder, or
BAD) to be a chronic mental illness characterized by dramatic and sudden mood
swings ranging from manic to depressed and back. Onset of the illness is
usually observed in late adolescence or early adulthood but has also been
noted in younger people. While normal ups and downs in mood are part of being
human, pathological mood swings severely reduce quality of life and the ability
to function and maintain social relations.

The scientific community believes that bipolar disorders have a variety of
causes. Origins could be genetic, environmental (childhood trauma or abuse),
or physiological (brain, endocrine irregularities). Diagnosis is based on
observation and clinical evaluation. Western treatment consists of
pharmacological interventions and talk therapy. The most commonly used
pharmaceutical is lithium, a potent mood stabilizer that has been proven to
significantly prevent suicides in bipolar patients.
