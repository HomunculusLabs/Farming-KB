## Overview

Uveitis is an inflammation of the uvea, the middle layer of the eye. It is
primarily defined by which side of the uvea is affected: anterior uveitis
(also called iritis) refers to inflammation on the outermost side, while
posterior uveitis (choroiditis) describes inflammation of the inside layer.
When both sides of the uvea are inflamed, the condition is called pan-uveitis.
