# Organic Fertilizer Recipes

A collection of complete organic fertilizer recipes from multiple traditions. Each provides all essential plant nutrients using organic or mineral inputs.

## 1. Steve Solomon's Complete Organic Fertilizer (COF)

For 100 square feet of garden bed:

| Ingredient | Amount | Nutrients Provided |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Seed meal (cottonseed, canola, or soybean) | 4 quarts (~4 lbs) | N, P, trace minerals |
| Agricultural lime OR dolomite lime | 1 quart (~1 lb) | Ca (and Mg if dolomite) |
| Bone meal OR rock phosphate | 1 quart (~1 lb) | P, Ca |
| Kelp meal | 1 quart (~1 lb) | K + all trace minerals |

**Application**: Mix thoroughly, broadcast 4-6 quarts per 100 sq ft, work into top 2-3 inches before planting. Side-dress heavy feeders at midseason with additional 1-2 quarts.

**Seed meal options by availability**:
- Cottonseed meal (6-2-1) — most common in the US
- Canola meal (5-1-1) — good alternative
- Soybean meal (7-2-1) — highest N
- Alfalfa meal (3-1-2) — moderate N, good K

**Lime decision tree**:
- Soil pH below 6.0 AND Mg adequate → use agricultural lime
- Soil pH below 6.0 AND Mg deficient → use dolomite lime
- Soil pH above 7.0 → use gypsum instead of lime (adds Ca without raising pH)

## 2. KNF Fish Amino Acid (FAA)

For nitrogen and amino acids during vegetative growth:

1. Collect fish waste (heads, guts, bones) — 1 part by volume
2. Add brown sugar — 1 part by volume (1:1 ratio)
3. Layer in a container: fish, sugar, fish, sugar
4. Cover with breathable cloth (not airtight)
5. Ferment 2-4 weeks, stirring daily
6. Drain liquid, filter through cloth
7. Dilute 1:1000 for foliar spray or soil drench

**Notes**: Smells strong during fermentation. Use during vegetative growth phase per [[nutritive-cycle-theory]]. Can substitute with fish scraps from market or processed fish waste.

## 3. KNF Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN)

Plant immune booster and pest deterrent:

**Step 1 — Garlic extract**: Crush garlic, soak in vodka or rice wine (1:2 ratio) for 7 days. Drain.

**Step 2 — Ginger extract**: Crush ginger, soak in vodka/rice wine (1:2 ratio) for 7 days. Drain.

**Step 3 — Licorice extract**: Crush dried licorice root, soak in vodka/rice wine (1:2 ratio) for 7 days. Drain.

**Step 4 — Combine**: Mix garlic extract + ginger extract + licorice extract in equal parts.

**Step 5 — Ferment**: Add brown sugar (1:1 with combined extracts). Ferment 5-7 days.

**Application**: Dilute 1:1000 for foliar spray. Apply during changeover period (transition from vegetative to reproductive). Boosts plant immunity and deters pests.

## 4. KNF Fermented Plant Juice (FPJ)

Growth stimulant from plant sap:

1. Collect fast-growing plant tips in early morning (before sunrise)
2. Chop finely, mix with brown sugar (1:1 by weight)
3. Pack tightly into a container, leaving 1/3 headspace
4. Place weight on top to press out liquid
5. Cover with breathable cloth, store in cool dark place
6. Ferment 7-10 days
7. Strain liquid — this is FPJ concentrate

**Dilution**: 1:500 to 1:1000 for soil drench or foliar spray.

**Plant choices by growth stage**:
- Early vegetative: fast-growing weeds, comfrey, mugwort
- Changeover: willow, angelica, arrowroot
- Fruiting: banana, pumpkin, sweet potato vines
- See [[nutritive-cycle-theory]] for detailed plant selection

## 5. Dynamic Accumulator Liquid Feed

From common garden weeds:

**Nettle tea**: Fill bucket with nettle tops (wear gloves!), cover with water, let steep 1-2 weeks. Dilute 1:10 for soil drench. Rich in N, Fe, K.

**Comfrey tea**: Fill bucket with comfrey leaves, cover with water, let steep 2-4 weeks (WARNING: very smelly). Dilute 1:10. Rich in K, excellent for fruiting plants.

**Dandelion tea**: Steep dandelion leaves and flowers in water 1-2 weeks. Dilute 1:10. Provides Ca, K, Fe.

## 6. Simple Kelp Foliar Spray

Trace mineral boost for any stage:

1. Dissolve 1 tablespoon kelp meal in 1 gallon warm water
2. Let steep 2-4 hours
3. Strain through fine cloth
4. Add 1 teaspoon mild liquid soap (as surfactant)
5. Spray on leaves in early morning or late evening

**Provides**: All trace minerals, natural growth hormones (cytokinins, auxins), stress resistance.

## Safety Notes

- Always dilute concentrated extracts before applying to plants
- Test on a few leaves before spraying entire crop
- Apply foliar sprays in early morning or evening (not in hot sun)
- Fermented products can smell strongly — ferment in a well-ventilated area
- Store all concentrates in cool, dark conditions
