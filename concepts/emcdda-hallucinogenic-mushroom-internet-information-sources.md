## Types of Information Provided

These websites are typically created by non-profit organisations, mushroom
users, or lobby groups. They commonly provide:

- **Effects and dosage information**: Detailed descriptions of expected
  effects at various dose levels, including subjective experiences.
- **Chemistry and classification**: Information on active compounds
  (psilocybin, psilocin), chemical properties, and taxonomic classification.
- **Legal information**: Current legal status in various jurisdictions, though
  this information may become outdated.
- **Trip reports**: First-hand accounts of user experiences, contributing to
  collective knowledge about effects and risks.
- **Identification guides**: Photographic and descriptive guides for
  identifying [[falconer-growing-mushrooms-in-sheds]] at home using
  spore prints, growkits, or other methods.
- **Location sharing**: Some sites allow users to share information about
  known locations where hallucinogenic mushrooms grow wild.
