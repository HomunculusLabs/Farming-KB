# cannabis dosing guidelines

## Overview

Cannabis dosing is inherently more complex than dosing conventional pharmaceuticals. The [[cervantes-cannabis-flower-anatomy]] potency ranges from less than 5% to over 30% THC. Concentrates can exceed 90% THC. Without lab testing, potency is unknown
- **Individual sensitivity**: Genetic differences in [[cannabis-terpene-profiles-sativa]] produce different effects even at the same THC dose

## Consumption Methods and Bioavailability

### Inhalation (Smoking/Vaporizing)

- **Onset**: 1-5 minutes
- **Peak effects**: 15-30 minutes
- **Duration**: 2-4 hours
- **Bioavailability**: 10-35% (varies with [[cannabis-topical-applications]] for details.

## THC Dosing Guidelines

### Microdose (0.5 - 2.5 mg THC)

- Sub-perceptual to very mild effects
- May enhance mood, creativity, and focus without impairment
- Used for anxiety reduction, mild pain, and daytime functional use
- Many experienced users report this as their preferred daily dose
- Excellent starting point for new or sensitive users

### Low Dose (2.5 - 10 mg THC)

- Mild to moderate psychoactive effects
- Noticeable euphoria, relaxation, sensory enhancement
- Therapeutic for moderate pain, insomnia, appetite stimulation
- Standard dose in many legal market edibles (5-10 mg per serving)
- May cause mild anxiety in sensitive individuals

### Moderate Dose (10 - 25 mg THC)

- Strong psychoactive effects
- Significant euphoria, altered perception, impairment of motor skills
- Effective for severe pain, nausea, muscle spasms
- Not recommended for driving or operating machinery
- May cause anxiety, paranoia, or discomfort in inexperienced users
- This is considered a "high" dose for most occasional consumers

### High Dose (25 - 50+ mg THC)

- Very strong psychoactive effects
- Significant impairment of cognitive and motor function
- May cause anxiety, paranoia, nausea, disorientation
- Used therapeutically only by highly tolerant patients (e.g., chronic pain, cancer)
- Not recommended for casual or new users
