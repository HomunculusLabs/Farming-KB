## Overview

Cloning is the process of taking a cutting from a mature cannabis plant and encouraging it to grow its own root system, creating a genetically identical copy. Cloning preserves desirable traits from mother plants and eliminates the genetic variability of seeds.

## Why Clone

- **Genetic consistency**: Every clone is an exact genetic copy of the mother
- **Known sex**: No need to sex plants — female clones produce female plants
- **Preserved phenotypes**: Lock in a specific expression of a strain
- **Faster than seed**: Clones skip germination and seedling stages
- **Perpetual harvests**: Maintain mother plants for ongoing cuttings

## Selecting a Mother Plant

The mother plant is the source of all clones. Choose mothers based on:

- **Health and vigor**: No pests, diseases, or nutrient deficiencies
- **Growth structure**: Branching pattern suitable for your space
- **Yield and quality**: Proven bud production and potency
- **Rooting ability**: Some strains clone more easily than others

Mother plants are typically kept in perpetual vegetative state (18/6 light) and maintained at a manageable size through regular pruning. A dedicated mother tent or area is ideal.

## Taking Cuttings

### Tools Needed

- Sterile razor blade or scalpel
- Rooting hormone (gel, powder, or liquid)
- Rooting cubes (Rockwool, peat, or coco)
- Propagation dome or cloning machine
- Clean cutting surface
- Spray bottle with plain water

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Prepare the workspace**: Clean all tools with isopropyl alcohol. Have rooting cubes pre-soaked (pH 5.5-6.0 for Rockwool).

2. **Select branch material**: Choose healthy branches from the lower to mid canopy. Ideal cuttings are 3-6 inches long with 2-4 nodes.

3. **Make the cut**: Cut at a 45-degree angle below a node. The angled cut increases surface area for water uptake and root formation. Some growers make a second perpendicular slit up the stem (splitting) to expose more cambium layer.

4. **Trim lower leaves**: Remove the bottom set of leaves. Trim remaining fan leaves in half to reduce transpiration while the cutting has no roots.

5. **Apply rooting hormone**: Dip the cut end into rooting hormone. Gels tend to adhere better than powders. Ensure the bottom 1-2 nodes are coated.

6. **Plant the cutting**: Insert the cutting 1-2 inches deep into the rooting cube. Ensure the cut end makes firm contact with the medium.

7. **Place under dome**: Put the rooted cutting into a propagation dome or cloning machine.

### Cloning from [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]] Plants

The best time to take cuttings is during the vegetative stage when the mother is actively growing. Avoid taking cuttings from flowering plants unless reverting, as this adds stress and time. Some growers take cuttings 2-3 weeks before the flip to ensure clones root while the mother transitions.

## Rooting Mediums

### Rockwool Cubes

- Industry standard for hydroponic growers
- Must be pre-soaked in pH-adjusted water (5.5-6.0)
- Good water-to-air ratio for root development
- Inert — requires careful pH and EC management

### Peat / Coco Cubes

- More forgiving pH range
- Organic option compatible with living soil transitions
- Rapid Rooter and Root Riots are popular brands
- Naturally buffered for young cuttings

### Aeroponic Cloning Machines

- Root in 5-10 days (faster than medium-based methods)
- Cuttings are suspended in a chamber with misting nozzles
- Higher success rate with proper maintenance
- Require regular cleaning to prevent pathogen buildup
- Popular models: TurboKlone, Clone King, EZ-Clone

## Environment for Rooting

### Temperature

Root zone temperature should be 72-80 F (22-27 C). A seedling heat mat under the tray helps maintain warmth. Air temperature should be 75-82 F (24-28 C).

### Humidity

Cuttings without roots rely entirely on humidity for water uptake. Maintain 85-100% RH inside the dome:

- Keep dome vents closed for the first 3-4 days
- Gradually open vents over days 5-7 to harden off
- Mist inside the dome (not directly on cuttings) 2-3 times daily
- Remove dome entirely once roots are visible and cuttings show new growth

### Light

Use low-intensity light during cloning:

- CFL or T5 fluorescent at 18/6
- LED at 20-30% intensity
- No direct intense light — this stresses unrooted cuttings
- 18/6 schedule is standard; some use 24/0 for faster rooting

## Rooting Timeline

| Day | Expected Progress |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | No visible change; cutting may wilt slightly |
| 4-7 | Callus tissue forms at cut site |
| 7-10 | First white roots emerge from cube |
| 10-14 | Root system visible, new growth at tips |
| 14-21 | Ready for transplant |

## Success Rate Tips

- **Work quickly**: Cuttings should be planted within seconds of being cut to prevent air embolisms in the stem
- **Use fresh rooting hormone**: Old hormone loses potency
- **Sanitize everything**: Contamination is the primary cause of failure
- **Don't overwater cubes**: Soggy cubes promote rot. Moist, not wet.
- **Don't pull on cuttings** to check roots — this damages fragile new root tissue
- **Maintain warmth**: Cold rooting zones are the most common cause of slow or failed rooting
- **Use a clean razor for each mother plant**: Prevents cross-contamination

## Transplanting Rooted Clones

Once roots are 1-2 inches long and visible outside the cube:

1. Harden off by removing the dome for increasing periods over 2-3 days
2. Transplant into final container or veg system
3. Begin with light nutrient solution (EC 0.6-0.8)
4. Gradually increase light intensity over 3-5 days
5. Feed per [[cannabis-nutrient-recipes]] vegetative schedule
