### Putative Additional Cannabinoid Receptors

Several non-CB1/non-CB2 receptors have been proposed:

- **GPR55**: An orphan GPCR that responds to certain cannabinoids. May mediate some of the effects attributed to "non-CB1/non-CB2" receptors. Its exact role remains controversial—some evidence suggests it is not a true cannabinoid receptor.
- **GPR18**: Proposed as a cannabinoid receptor activated by abnormal cannabidiol (abn-CBD). May be involved in immune function and intraocular pressure regulation.
- **GPR119**: Activated by oleoylethanolamide (OEA) and other lipid signaling molecules. Primarily expressed in the pancreas and GI tract, with potential roles in metabolism and appetite.
- **TRPV1 (vanilloid receptor)**: While not a classical cannabinoid receptor, TRPV1 is activated by anandamide, CBD, and other cannabinoids, linking the endocannabinoid system to pain and thermoregulation pathways.
