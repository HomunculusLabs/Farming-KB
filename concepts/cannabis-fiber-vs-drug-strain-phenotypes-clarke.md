### Cannabinoid Profile
Most fiber strains contain CBD as the primary cannabinoid and little if any THC. This makes them useful for CBD production but unsuitable for psychoactive drug use.

## Drug Strain Gross Phenotypes

### Characteristics
Drug strains are characterized by Delta1-THC as the primary cannabinoid, with low levels of other accessory cannabinoids such as THCV, CBD, CBC, and CBN. This results from selective breeding for high potency or natural selection in niches where Delta1-THC biosynthesis favors survival.

### Geographic Distribution of Drug Strains
Drug strains come from specific regions, each with distinctive characteristics:

**Equatorial and Subtropical (0-15 degrees latitude):**
- Colombia (highland and lowland)
- Southern India
- Congo
- Kenya/Kisumu
- Malawi
- Jamaica (Caribbean hybrids)
- South Africa
- [[cannabis-hindu-kush-strain-description-clarke]] (Afghanistan and Pakistan)
- Russia (ruderalis, uncultivated)

### General Drug Strain Traits
- Selected for strong dioecious tendencies (separate male and female plants)
- Sex ratio often skewed toward pistillate plants in drug strains
- Calyx-to-leaf ratio is the primary determinant of floral cluster quality
- Higher calyx-to-leaf ratio = more adapted to calyx and [[hindu-kush]]
