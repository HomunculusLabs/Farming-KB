# KNF Large-Scale Farming

updated: 2026-04-18

While Korean [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture]] are collected locally and multiplied through a staged process:

- **IMO-1:** Collected on steamed rice in the field (small scale)
- **IMO-2:** IMO-1 mixed with equal weight of jaggery/brown sugar
- **IMO-3:** IMO-2 mixed with expanded rice bran and soil (3:1 ratio with soil)
- **IMO-4:** IMO-3 mixed with field soil and formed into lumps for field application

At each stage, the volume increases dramatically. For large farms, IMO-3 and IMO-4 production can be scaled to any needed quantity using simple mixing equipment. The key ingredients -- rice, sugar, rice bran, and soil -- are all locally available in bulk.
