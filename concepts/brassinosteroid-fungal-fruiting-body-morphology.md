## The Annulus in Psilocybe cubensis

*Psilocybe cubensis* normally develops a membranous annulus on the
stipe (stem) as part of its standard fruiting body morphology. The
annulus is a remnant of the partial veil, a tissue that connects the
margin of the pileus (cap) to the stipe during early development.

As the cap expands, the partial veil ruptures and remains as a ring
on the upper stipe. The annulus serves as an important taxonomic
character for species identification within the genus *Psilocybe* and
related genera. Its presence, position, form (membranous vs.
fibrillose), and persistence are all diagnostic features used in
mycological keys.

The absence of an annulus in brassinosteroid-treated cultures
therefore represents a significant morphological deviation from the
species norm.
