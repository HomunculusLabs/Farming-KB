# DMT and 5-MeO-DMT Reference

N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) and 5-Methoxy-DMT (5-MeO-DMT) are among the most powerful psychedelics known. Found naturally in plants, animals, and the human body, they produce experiences of extraordinary intensity lasting only minutes. See [[psychedelic-compounds-reference]] for the broader compounds table.

## Chemistry

| Compound | Formula | Source | Duration |
|----------|---------|--------|----------|
| N,N-DMT | C12H16N2 | Plants, animals, human pineal gland | 5-15 min (smoked), 4-6 hr (oral with MAOI) |
| 5-MeO-DMT | C13H18N2 | Plants, Bufo alvarius toad venom | 5-20 min (smoked) |
| Bufotenin (5-HO-DMT) | C12H16N2O | Anadenanthera, Bufo toads | 15-30 min (snorted) |

DMT is a tryptamine (indole family). The active metabolite of psilocybin, psilocin, is 4-OH-DMT — essentially DMT with a hydroxyl group at the 4-position. This structural similarity explains why high-dose mushroom experiences resemble DMT.

## Natural Sources

### Plant Sources
- **Psychotria viridis** (Chacruna) — DMT-rich leaves, primary DMT source in ayahuasca
- **Mimosa hostilis** (Jurema) — Root bark contains 0.5-1% DMT; used in Brazilian ayahuasca analogs
- **Diplopterys cabrerana** (Chaliponga) — DMT and 5-MeO-DMT
- **Anadenanthera peregrina** (Yopo/Cohoba) — Seeds contain DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, and bufotenin; snuffed traditionally
- **Virola theiodora** (Epena) — Bark resin snuffed by Amazonian tribes
- **Acacia species** — A. maidenii, A. phlebophylla, and others contain DMT in bark and leaves
- **Desmanthus illinoensis** (Prairie Mimosa) — Root bark contains DMT
- **Phalaris grass** — P. arundinacea contains DMT and 5-MeO-DMT (also contains toxic gramine)

### Animal Sources
- **Bufo alvarius** (Colorado River Toad) — Venom glands secrete 5-MeO-DMT and bufotenin; dried and smoked
- **Human body** — DMT found in pineal gland, blood, urine, and spinal fluid (Strassman research)

## History

### Amazonian Ayahuasca
DMT-containing plants are combined with Banisteriopsis caapi (containing MAO-inhibiting harmala alkaloids) in brews called ayahuasca or yage. The MAO inhibition allows DMT to be orally active. How indigenous Amazonians discovered this specific combination — among thousands of plant species — is a matter of wonder. Shamans claim the "plant spirits" told them which plants to use.

### Snuff Traditions
Yopo (Anadenanthera peregrina) and Epena (Virola theiodora) are blown into the nostrils using elaborate snuffing tubes. Some devices allow two people to simultaneously blow snuff into each other's nostrils before losing consciousness.

### Modern Discovery
- DMT first synthesized in 1931 by Richard Manske
- Identified in ayahuasca in 1957 by Hochstein and Paradies
- Popularized in the underground psychedelic scene in the 1960s; called "the businessman's lunch trip"
- Rick Strassman's clinical DMT research (1990-1995) at University of New Mexico documented effects in human volunteers

## Administration

### Smoking/Vaporization (N,N-DMT)
- Dose: 30-60 mg freebase
- Onset: 15-30 seconds
- Peak: 1-3 minutes
- Duration: 5-15 minutes
- The entire dose must be consumed within ~60 seconds or the user won't "break through"
- Vaporization is preferred over burning — burning destroys some DMT and tastes harsh
- Use glass pipe with fine mesh screen; keep flame 1/2 inch away from DMT crystals
- Place a small amount of ash in the bowl to hold melted DMT before vaporization

### Smoking (5-MeO-DMT)
- Dose: 5-10 mg (much less than N,N-DMT)
- Often infused into parsley or cannabis for accurate dosing (too little to weigh on most scales)
- Faster and more intense onset than N,N-DMT
- MAO inhibitor — see safety notes

### Oral (Ayahuasca)
- Dose: 60-120 mg DMT (combined with MAOI from Banisteriopsis caapi or Peganum harmala)
- Onset: 30-60 minutes
- Duration: 4-6 hours
- Requires MAO inhibition to prevent gastrointestinal breakdown
- Includes purgative effects (nausea, vomiting — considered part of the cleansing process)

### Snuff (Yopo/Epena)
- Dose: Variable (depends on preparation)
- Onset: Rapid (minutes)
- Duration: 15-30 minutes
- Harsh on nasal passages; burning sensation common

## The Experience

### Intensity Rating: 9-10 (Turner scale)

The DMT experience is an order of magnitude more intense than traditional psychedelics (LSD, psilocybin, mescaline). Key characteristics from Turner's Essential Psychedelics Guide:

- **Breakthrough**: On a full dose, the user is propelled to a high as intense as the peak of a 1000+ mcg LSD trip within 60 seconds
- **Reality replacement**: Conventional reality is completely replaced. Users frequently feel out of body, with no recollection of having a body, personality, or even knowledge of planet Earth
- **Unpredictable content**: Ranges from heaven to hell, cyberspace to jeweled palaces, entity contact, extraterrestrials, ancient spirits, jungle animals
- **Time distortion**: Perception of time slows to nearly a complete stop; users may feel they are not breathing or that their heart has stopped (perceptual, not actual)
- **Energy phenomena**: Users report awareness of millions of vibrations and sounds; physical energies can manifest through spontaneous movement, dance, or yogic postures
- **Facial expressions**: DMT produces distinctive changes in facial musculature, suggesting latent communication abilities
- **Cannot be guided**: Unlike LSD, the DMT experience cannot be directed by thought; can only be directed by "the intent of the soul or will"

### N,N-DMT vs 5-MeO-DMT

| Aspect | N,N-DMT | 5-MeO-DMT |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Visuals | Rich, complex, often described as "hyperspace" | Often described as "the void" or "white light" |
| Entity contact | Common (elves, aliens, spirits) | Less common; more abstract |
| Dose | 30-60 mg | 5-10 mg |
| Duration | ~30 min return | ~20 min return |
| Character | "Candy for the mind" | More overwhelming, less visual content |

## Harmala Alkaloids (MAO Inhibitors)

The harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine) from Banisteriopsis caapi or Peganum harmala (Syrian Rue) are essential for oral DMT activity.

- **Mechanism**: Inhibit monoamine oxidase (MAO) enzymes in the gut that would otherwise destroy DMT before it reaches the brain
- **Synergistic effect**: Harmala adds its own signature — connection to collective unconscious, earth spirit, evolutionary archetypes
- **Peganum harmala seeds**: Contain 2-7% mixed harmala alkaloids; approximately 3g of ground seed for potentiating dose
- **Preparation**: Grind seeds, encapsulate (onset ~2 hr), or brew as tea (onset ~1 hr)
- **Smoking**: Harmala extract can be smoked for milder effects with faster onset
- **Safety**: MAO inhibitors require dietary restrictions (avoid tyramine-rich foods, certain medications, other drugs)
- **Combinations**: Harmala potentiates DMT, psilocybin, LSD, and ketamine. WARNING: May be dangerous with phenethylamines (ecstasy, mescaline, 2C-B)

## Ketamine Comparison

Turner rates ketamine at "Intensity: 10 - Infinity" — the only substance rated higher than DMT:

- Both produce complete reality replacement and ego dissolution
- Ketamine lasts longer (30-90 min IM) and has a smoother onset (2 min)
- Ketamine described as "the ultimate psychedelic journey" — cosmic orgasm, connection to all things, non-verbal realization of existence/non-existence
- Both are dissociative; ketamine is more consistently described as "blissful" while DMT is more "overwhelming"
- See [[psychedelic-compounds-reference]] for ketamine details

## Safety

- 5-MeO-DMT is itself an MAO inhibitor — additional MAO inhibitors increase risk
- Users should lie down before taking a hit — inability to stand is immediate
- Smoking technique is critical; burning destroys DMT and produces harsh smoke
- Set and setting are extremely important due to intensity
- Integration time is needed after the experience despite its short duration
- Contra-indicated with SSRIs, MAOIs, and many psychiatric medications
- Cardiovascular effects: transient increases in blood pressure and heart rate
- Psychological risk: overwhelming experiences can be traumatic without proper preparation
