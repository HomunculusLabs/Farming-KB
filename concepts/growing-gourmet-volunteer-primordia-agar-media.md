# Volunteer Primordia Formation on Agar Media

The voluntary and spontaneous formation of miniature mushrooms
in a petri dish is a delightful experience for all cultivators.
These volunteer primordia, forming on nutrified agar media,
provide valuable information about a strain's fruiting
potential and can be strategically used to accelerate
cultivation cycles.

## What Are Volunteer Primordia?

Volunteer primordia are miniature mushrooms that form
spontaneously on the surface of agar culture media, without
any deliberate environmental triggering by the cultivator.

## Species Known to Form Volunteer Primordia

enriched malt extract agar, supplemented with 0.2% yeast
and 0.005% gentamycin sulfate. The formation of primordia
on this medium is often strain-specific. Known species
