### Materials
Any organic material that decomposes is suitable:
- Grass clippings
- Chipped material
- Leaves
- Hay and straw
- Algae or mud from pond
- Kitchen waste
- Cardboard

### Principles
- Smaller material + more active soil life = faster humus production
- Space between raised beds is protected from drying out and retains heat
- Plants on beds chosen so compost gets enough light but is protected from full sun
- Partial shade creates optimal decomposition conditions
- Earthworms breed abundantly -- be careful when digging
