# Amazonian Shamanic Battles and Defensive Powers

A major theme in [[amaringo-ayahuasca-visions-shipibo-iconography]] is the shamanic battle -- direct confrontations between healers (curanderos/vegetalistas) and sorcerers (brujos). These battles take place entirely in the visionary realm and involve complex arsenals of magical weapons, spirit allies, and defensive barriers. Understanding these battles is essential to understanding Amazonian [[query-how-do-plants-defend-themselves-chemically]]. "Huaira" means air, "uma" means head.

### Ayanahais (Eyes of the Dead)
Fireflies that move in flocks. Sorcerers use them as cigarettes -- they embody the spirits of the dead and carry destructive intent.

### Huasi-Ukullucos (House Lizards)
They hide in bathrooms and quickly enter a person's anus during defecation, bringing about death. A form of intrusion sorcery.

### Virotes
Magical darts made from various materials: palm thorns (chonta, pona, inchahui, huasai), fish horns, snake teeth, bird beaks. Projected through mariri tubes.

### Chonta Sorcery
The most feared form of sorcery. The sorcerer places thorns from specific palms in their mariris (magical phlegm tubes) to be used as virotes. The huiririma palm (Astrocaryum jauari) is particularly associated with this -- its thorns carry electromagnetic force.

## Healer's Defensive Powers

### Tingunas
Colored energy barriers that the healer raises as defense. Tingunas come in various colors and intensities:
- White and pink: Apprentice-level tingunas
- Blue: Muraya (healer) level tingunas
- Red and yellow: Banco (master) level tingunas
- Black and red: Evil banco tingunas

Tingunas can be transformed into persons or cities. They form the primary magical shield.

### Arkana
Spiritual defenses given to vegetalistas by [[icaros]], perfumes, clairvoyance, warfare, healing, and rapid travel.

### Anaconda Protection

Gigantic black boas protect healing groups with hypnotic powers. Anacondas are the owners of mama cochas (enormous forest lakes) and are central to shamanic defense.

## Pulsations as Diagnostic and Combat System

Queen Pulsarium Coya teaches vegetalistas to diagnose illness through pulsation -- an intuitive ability connecting the hands with the brain. When a vegetalista touches a patient, their brain immediately gives a picture of what is wrong. The pulsation system has levels:
- White and pink waves: Apprentice pulsations
- Blue waves: Muraya healer pulsations
- Red and yellow waves: Banco master pulsations
