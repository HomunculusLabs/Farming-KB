The NAC developed from the peyote traditions of multiple tribes. The Huichol

## Brazilian Ayahuasca Churches

Three major organized churches use ayahuasca as their primary sacrament, all
founded in the 1950s by rubber tappers working in the Amazon who encountered
the hallucinogenic vine through mestizo or Indian ayahuasceros:

### Santo Daime
- Founded by a Brazilian of African descent
- Ceremonies involve the entire congregation singing hymns led by women
- Some ceremonies include dancing in simple rhythmic, swaying steps
- Resembles African-American gospel services in structure
- Several thousand members in Brazil; satellite centers in [[ayahuasca]]
