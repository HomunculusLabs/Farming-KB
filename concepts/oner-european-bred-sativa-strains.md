# European-Bred Sativa Strains

European breeders have played a crucial role in adapting wild
sativa genetics for controlled cultivation environments.

From Poland to Spain to the UK, European seed companies have
developed innovative crosses that preserve sativa character
while improving manageability and yield.

This page covers notable European-bred sativa strains from
Cannabis Sativa Volume 3.

## Polm Gold

Polm Gold is a fascinating sativa from Poland's Satindi Seeds,
originally created by Holland's Spliff Seeds.

The strain incorporates rare Moroccan landrace genetics through
a parent called [[polm-gold-moroccan-chefchaouen-sativa-hybrid]], named after a city in
northwestern Morocco.

### Three-Way Genetic Blend

Polm Gold combines Viking, Early Pearl, and the Moroccan
Chefchaouen landrace — named after a city in northwestern Morocco.

The Moroccan parent is a heat-loving sativa stabilized over
many generations for European climates.

This stabilization produces uniform crops with a single phenotype,
now thriving even in regions with extreme temperature drops.

### Growing Profile

Polm Gold is unsuitable for [[comparison-cannabis-indoor-growing-basics-vs-cannabis-outdoor-growing]] due to its extreme
height and leggy structure.

Outdoors, the plant reaches 12-13 feet and develops phenomenal
root growth even on rocky soil.

Clones flower within 2-3 weeks under 24-hour light.

With an 8-week vegetative period and late-May planting, harvest
arrives mid-September at up to 600 grams per plant.

The strain requires significant experience to cultivate properly.

### Flavor and Effects

Polm Gold buds taste like blueberry with apple-pie sweetness.

Hints of earth and [[hamilton-citrus-fruit-growing]] emerge on the exhale.

The high is exceptionally strong — simultaneously trippy and stoney.

At 17% THC, this strain represents a unique fusion of Moroccan
and European genetics.

## White Bhutanese

White Bhutanese is a rare landrace variety from Mandala Seeds,
originating in the Wangdi Province of Bhutan.

The strain is only one generation removed from its wild source,
making it among the most authentic landrace offerings available.

### Authentic Landrace Status

White Bhutanese is approximately 80% sativa, only one generation
removed from its wild source in Bhutan's Wangdi Province.

It requires outdoor or greenhouse cultivation due to massive height.

The strain shows high mold resistance and prefers warm to tropical
climates.

Overpruning decreases landrace yields; light LST is sufficient.

### Unique Trichome Expression

Even in late October, when plants are nearly ripe, White Bhutanese
trichomes remain shining white rather than turning amber.

This white-trichome persistence is a trademark of the strain and
distinguishes it from most other varieties.

The plant requires heavy nutrient feeding despite its wild origins.

### Potency and Medical Potential

White Bhutanese tests at 12-13.8% THC with 0.16% CBD.

The moderate THC with CBD presence suggests medical utility.

Effects include creative focus with social anxiety reduction,
delivered through a lemony, minty smoke.

## Y Griega

Y Griega is a powerful sativa-dominant hybrid from Spain's
Medical Seeds Company, combining Kali Mist and Amnesia genetics.

The strain name is the archaic Spanish form of the letter "y"
(before modern spelling reform to "ye").

### Haze Heritage and Indoor Adaptation

At 27.12% THC, Y Griega is among the most potent strains in
Volume 3, with strong Haze grandparent expression.

The plant reaches 11 feet outdoors but can be managed indoors
with LST and ScrOG methods. Force flowering at half desired
final height.

The 90-day flowering period is long but rewards patience with
extraordinary [[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]].

### Yield and Quality

Indoor ScrOG yields approximately 550 grams per square yard;
outdoor plants produce about 550 grams each by early November.

Every inch of stem is covered in thick white-haired buds at
harvest. Medical Seeds Company notes excellent hash and oil
production from the extraordinary resin density.

## European Breeding Innovation

These strains demonstrate Europe's contribution to cannabis
genetics: adapting wild and landrace material for modern growers
with stability, uniformity, and manageability.
