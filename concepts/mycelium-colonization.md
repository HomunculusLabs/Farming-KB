## Mycelium Colonization Mycelium colonization is the phase where fungal mycelium spreads through the substrate, consuming nutrients and establishing the network that will eventually produce mushrooms. This stage requires careful environmental control and patience. ## The Biology of Colonization Mycelium is the vegetative body of a fungus, consisting of a network of thread-like hyphae. Each hypha is a single cell-wide tube that grows at its tip, secreting enzymes to break down organic matter. As hyphae branch and interconnect, they form a dense white mat called the mycelium. This network is capable of extraordinary biological activity: decomposing lignin and cellulose in wood, forming symbiotic relationships with plant roots, and transmitting chemical signals across meters of substrate. ## Spawn Types mycelium-network densifies.
