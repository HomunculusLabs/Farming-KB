# Reference Trees and Plant Species

**Source:** Permaculture: A Designers' Manual, Bill Mollison (1988)
**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

The Designers' Manual contains extensive plant species references throughout its chapters, culminating in a comprehensive Appendix listing plants mentioned in the text by common name. This page organises the key species cited across multiple contexts -- as forage, windbreak, soil builders, food crops, and structural materials.

## Appendix Plant List

The Appendix (starting Page 596) lists plants by common name with botanical names. Below is a representative selection organised by function. The full list contains hundreds of species.

- Moringa (Moringa oleifera) -- leaves for food, pods for water purification
- Neem (Azadirachta indica) -- insecticide, medicinal, shade
- Bamboo (Bambusa, Phyllostachys, Dendrocalamus) -- construction, food, erosion control

## Species Selection Principles

Mollison emphasises several principles for species selection:
1. **Local adaptation:** Choose species suited to climate, soil, and site conditions
2. **Multiple functions:** Every plant should serve 3+ functions in the system
3. **Native and exotic:** Use both; evaluate on merit, not origin
4. **Design by analogy:** If a species works in one biome, try related species in similar conditions
5. **Pioneer to climax:** Plan succession from fast pioneers to long-term climax species
6. **4,000-8,000 plants per hectare:** Species-rich systems require nursery support
