# Water Hash and Bubble Hash Extraction

Hash extracted using cold water is known as water hash,
Ice-O-Lator hash, ice hash, THC crystal, and other names.
When very pure, hash will bubble, releasing volatile resins
when exposed to a flame, hence the name Bubble Hash. This
boiling effect is called "full-melt bubble" hash, the highest
quality grade achievable through water extraction.

## Sadu Sam's Secret

Modern [[cervantes-15-bag-water-hash-extraction]] started with "Sadu Sam's Secret"
published in HASHISH! by Robert Connell Clarke. The principle
is simple physics: resin is oil-based and cannabis foliage is
water-based. This difference makes separating the two in
aqueous solution easy. Heavy, oil-soluble [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]] will not
dissolve in water; they are heavier than water so they sink.
Water-soluble material dissolves in liquid, and foliage is
lighter than water, which makes it float.

The method involves stirring pulverized flowers or freshly
sieved resin powder into a tall container of cool water
containing ten to twenty times the volume of water to dry
powder. Cool or cold water is essential because warm water
softens the resin, which tends to stick together forming an
unmanageable lump. The mixture must be stirred vigorously for
several minutes until lumps of powder disperse. Once stirring
ceases, plant particles and light debris float to the surface,
while mature resin glands and dense debris sink to the bottom.

## Ice-O-Lator System

Mila (the Pollinator) in Amsterdam refined Sadu Sam's method
by combining dry sieving with ice-cold water to create
Ice-O-Lator bags. These are waterproof nylon bags with
progressively smaller micron silk screens sewn into the bottom.
Clean cannabis is chopped and placed in ice-cold water in the
bags. The water is mixed, and when the slurry settles, resin
glands pass through the screens while foliage and debris are
retained in bags.

## Bubble Man's Refinements

Bubble Man further refined the process by adding more bags
with progressively smaller mesh, eventually using seven
different screens. He found that each screen separates unique
sediments, some of which contain exceptionally pure THC. This
hash is so pure it bubbles when heated, leading to his famous
saying: "If it don't bubble, it ain't worth the trouble."

## Water Hash Instructions

The basic Ice-O-Lator process requires:

- Ice-O-Lator bag set with progressively smaller screens
- 5-gallon (20-25L) bucket with lid
- Kitchen mixer with paddles (not a chopping blade)
- Paper towels, dinner plate, plastic card, large mixing
  spoon
- 4.5-11 pounds (2-5 kg) of ice cubes
- Temperature target: 41 degrees F (5 degrees C)

Step-by-step procedure:

1. Fill bucket with ice-cold water to six inches below rim.
2. Place bags into bucket in proper order, nested inside one
   another. Pull each bag's top edge over the rim and lock.
3. Add up to 7 ounces (200 grams) of frozen cannabis. Do not
   overload; plant material needs space to mix and for resin
   to separate.
4. Add more ice on top to help leaves get wet.
5. Fill with more water to within two inches of bucket top.
6. Let mix soak for 15 minutes so foliage becomes as cold as
   the water.
7. Cut two holes in the lid for the mixer shafts. Mix on low
   speed for 15 minutes.
8. Use a spoon to move peripheral plant material to center.
   Let soak 5 more minutes, then mix again.
