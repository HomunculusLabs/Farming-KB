# Water Hash and Bubble Hash Extraction

Hash extracted using cold water is known as water hash,
Ice-O-Lator hash, ice hash, THC crystal, and other names.
When very pure, hash will bubble, releasing volatile resins
when exposed to a flame, hence the name Bubble Hash. This
boiling effect is called "full-melt bubble" hash, the highest
quality grade achievable through water extraction.
