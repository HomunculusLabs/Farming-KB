### Depth Control

Correct planting depth is essential — too deep and seeds exhaust their
energy reserves before reaching light; too shallow and they dry out or
are displaced by rain or irrigation:

- **General rule**: Plant seeds at a depth approximately 2-3 times their
  diameter
- **Heavy clay soils**: Plant slightly shallower, as clay is more
  resistant to seedling emergence
- **Sandy soils**: Plant slightly deeper, as sandy soils drain faster
  and may dry out at shallow depths
- **Hot, dry conditions**: Plant slightly deeper to reach moisture
- **Cool, wet conditions**: Plant slightly shallower for faster emergence

### Spacing Within Rows

In-row spacing determines final plant density, which affects individual
plant size, overall yield, and disease susceptibility:

- **Overseeding**: Always sow more seeds than the desired final stand.

  times. This accounts for germination failure, insect damage, and
  disease losses
