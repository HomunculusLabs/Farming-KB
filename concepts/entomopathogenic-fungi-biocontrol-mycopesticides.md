# Entomopathogenic Fungi: Biocontrol and Mycopesticides

## Overview

Entomopathogenic fungi are fungi that infect and kill insects, serving as natural population regulators in virtually every terrestrial ecosystem. Paul Stamets' work on mycopesticides — using these fungi as targeted biological control agents — represents one of the most practical applications of mycological research for agriculture and structural pest management.
