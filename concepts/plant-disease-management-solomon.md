# Plant Disease Management (Solomon)

Updated: 2026-04-18

Source: Gardening When It Counts, Steve Solomon, Chapters 9-10

Solomon takes a preventive approach to plant diseases, emphasizing that the home gardener has few effective cures once disease strikes. The focus must be on prevention through healthy growing conditions.

## The Prevention Philosophy

"Sadly, the home gardener has no effective cure for plant diseases. However, we can often prevent the problem by growing healthy and naturally disease-resistant plants."

This means:
- Using appropriate, well-adapted varieties
- Maintaining high soil fertility with balanced nutrition
