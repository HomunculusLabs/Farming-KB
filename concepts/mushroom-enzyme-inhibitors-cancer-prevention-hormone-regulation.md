## 5-Alpha-Reductase Inhibition and Prostate Health

5-alpha-reductase is the enzyme that converts testosterone to
dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT stimulates growth of the
prostate gland, and elevated DHT levels are associated with
benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) and prostate cancer.
Pharmaceutical 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors such as finasteride
and dutasteride are used to treat these conditions but can
cause sexual dysfunction and other side effects. Several of the
mushroom species tested by Chen also inhibited 5-alpha-reductase,
suggesting a potential dietary approach to prostate health
management.
