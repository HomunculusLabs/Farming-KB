## Limitations and Adaptations

While Fukuoka presented seed balls as simple and accessible, he acknowledged certain challenges:

- Pellets that crumble too early expose seeds to predation before germination
- The clay composition must be matched to local rainfall patterns -- too hard and seeds cannot emerge, too soft and protection is lost
- Broadcasting pellets requires some skill to achieve even distribution
- Not all seed types are equally suited to the pellet format; very large seeds may not need protection
