Richard Howard at DuPont Company is responsible for much of the
groundbreaking research on appressorial function, discovering that
Magnaporthe appressoria can puncture bulletproof vest material. His
electron microscopic studies revealed the detailed ultrastructure of the
melanized wall and penetration peg. The DuPont team quantified the
forces involved and demonstrated melanin's critical role using albino
mutants and biosynthesis inhibitors.
