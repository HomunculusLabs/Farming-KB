### Bag Placement

Once mycelium has fully colonized the substrate bag, the filter can is removed and bags are placed into the chamber. Jarrold recommends cutting bags down the sides when mushrooms begin forming, leaving approximately 2 cm of the bottom intact. This allows the bags to stand upright in the chamber without the substrate becoming waterlogged from contact with the humid base.

## Self-Humidifying Chamber Design

### Components Required

The automated design adds active humidity control to the basic aquarium chamber:

### Assembly

The self-humidifying chamber is built by modifying the basic fish tank:

1. A small hole is drilled near the top of the tank for the airline tubing
2. The tubing is fed through to a water container at the bottom of the tank
3. An air stone is attached to the tubing end inside the water
4. The air pump is connected to the external end of the tubing
5. Inner angled drip lids are fabricated from 2mm plastic sheet

The angled drip lids serve to distribute condensation evenly back into the chamber rather than allowing it to pool on one surface. The car registration plate nuts and bolts are used as mounting hardware for the drip lids — an ingenious repurposing of readily available, cheap materials.

### How It Works

When the air pump runs, it pushes air through the airline tubing and out through the submerged air stone. The rising bubbles agitate the water surface, dramatically increasing the rate of evaporation. This moisture-laden air rises into the main chamber volume, maintaining high humidity without manual spraying or misting. The system is essentially a small-scale fog chamber powered by the same principle as aquarium aeration.

### Advantages Over Passive Design

- **Consistent humidity:** Continuous operation eliminates humidity fluctuations
- **Reduced maintenance:** No need to manually mist or top up water as frequently
- **Better gas exchange:** Air pump provides gentle air circulation
- **Lower contamination risk:** Sealed system with filtered air input reduces [[greg-green-temperature-and-humidity-control]]
