# KNF Seed Soaking Protocol for Cannabis Germination

Seed soaking with fermented biological inputs is a Korean Natural Farming
(KNF) technique used to prime cannabis seeds before germination. Rather
than simply hydrating seeds in plain water, the KNF approach inoculates
the seed coat with beneficial microorganisms, enzymes, and plant-derived
compounds that support the earliest stages of plant development. This
protocol was documented by Geo Farms and Country Roots as part of their
Modern Natural Farming Technology (MNFT) system for cannabis, and
represents one of the first applications of KNF principles in the
cannabis life cycle.

## The Seed Soak Solution

The MNFT seed soak solution combines four KNF inputs in a small volume
of water:

- **100 mL water** (non-chlorinated, ideally filtered or spring water)
- **1 drop of BRV or ARV** (Brown Rice Vinegar or Apple Rice Vinegar)
- **1 drop of FPJ** ([[cho-water-soluble-calcium-and-potassium]])

The solution is mixed in a small container, and cannabis seeds are added
directly. The seeds soak for 24 hours in the solution before being
removed and placed into a clean, moist paper towel for germination.

## Role of Each Input in Seed Priming

### Brown Rice Vinegar / Apple Rice Vinegar (BRV/ARV)

The vinegar serves as both a carrier and an acid reducer. BRV and ARV
contain acetic acid and organic acids from rice fermentation that
slightly lower the soak pH, improving seed coat permeability and water
uptake. The organic acids have mild [[nutrient-dynamic-accumulator-reference]] plants such as comfrey, nettle,
or dandelion, it also introduces trace minerals for early seedling
development.

### Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN)

OHN provides medicinal compounds from herbs and roots that strengthen
the seedling's immune system from the beginning. The traditional recipe
includes garlic, ginger, licorice, cinnamon, and Angelica gigas
(Korean angelica). During seed soaking, these compounds permeate the
seed coat and provide systemic protection against soil-borne pathogens
such as Pythium and Fusarium that commonly attack germinating seeds.
OHN also contains plant growth regulators that stimulate cell division
during initial radicle emergence.

### Water Soluble Calcium (WSC)

Calcium is essential for cell wall construction, cell membrane integrity,
and the signaling pathways that trigger germination. WSC provides
immediately bioavailable calcium for the emerging seedling from its
first moments of growth. [[doc]]
