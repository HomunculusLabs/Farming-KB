Soil is not a uniform substance but a layered system of distinct zones called
horizons. In *Teaming With Microbes*, Lowenfels and Lewis explain that
understanding soil horizons helps gardeners appreciate where the biological
activity that supports plant life actually occurs and why the top layers are so
critical. A soil profile is essentially a map of these layers, and for
gardeners, the top horizons are the ones that matter most.

active A horizon that supports robust plant growth. Protecting existing topsoil
from erosion, compaction, and inversion is always preferable to rebuilding it.

## Summary

organic matter accumulation, and biological activity over time. The O and A
horizons at the surface contain the vast majority of organic matter and soil
