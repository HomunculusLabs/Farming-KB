# Soil Freezing Requirements for Sustainable Vegetable Gardening
**Source:** Gardening When It Counts - Steve Solomon (2005)

## Overview

One of the most overlooked factors in long-term vegetable garden planning is whether the soil freezes solid in winter. Steve Solomon emphasizes that in climates where the ground does not freeze to a depth of at least 18 inches (45 cm) for several continuous months, it becomes progressively more difficult to grow vegetables on the same piece of land year after year. The absence of a deep, sustained freeze allows soil-borne diseases and root-dwelling insect pests to accumulate to problematic levels within just 3-5 years of continuous cultivation.
