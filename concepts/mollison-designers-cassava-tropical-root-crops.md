## Cyanide Detoxification

All cassava contains some level of cyanogenic glycosides, primarily linamarin.
Processing to remove these compounds is essential, especially for bitter
varieties. Traditional processing methods include peeling, grating, fermenting,
and cooking. The fermentation process, in which grated cassava is left for
several days, allows enzymes to break down the glycosides. The resulting product
can be dried as flour (farinha in Brazil, gari in West Africa) or cooked as a
porridge or flatbread.
