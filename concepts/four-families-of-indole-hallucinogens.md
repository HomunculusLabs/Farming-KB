### Exopheromones and Cross-Species Communication

McKenna proposed that psychoactive plant compounds may function as
exopheromones - chemical messengers that act across species lines
rather than within a single species. Some exopheromones allow a small
group of individuals to affect an entire community or biome. This
suggests nature operates as an organismic whole that mediates its own
development through chemical messages, challenging the classical
Darwinian view of nature as endless warfare among competing species.
