Photosynthesis is the fundamental biological process by which plants, algae, and certain bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in sugars. ### Calvin Cycle (Stroma)
Also called the "dark reactions" or "light-independent reactions":
1. CO2 is "fixed" by the enzyme RuBisCO, combining with ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP)
2. The resulting 6-carbon compound immediately splits into two 3-carbon molecules (3-PGA)
3. ATP and NADPH from light reactions convert 3-PGA into glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P)
4. Some G3P exits the cycle to form glucose and other carbohydrates
5. Remaining G3P is used to regenerate RuBP, continuing the cycle
6. For every 3 CO2 fixed, one net G3P molecule is produced
