## Key Terpenoids in Cannabis

### Limonene

Limonene is a citrus-scented terpenoid found in many cannabis
varieties. It has significant [[cannabis-terpene-entourage-effect-profiles]].

### Pulegone

Pulegone is another terpenoid that increases brain acetylcholine
activity. It has a minty aroma and is found in higher concentrations in
certain cannabis varieties, contributing to their unique therapeutic
profiles.

### Citronellol

Citronellol has a citrus-floral aroma and possesses sedative
properties when inhaled. It is one of the terpenoids that helps
mitigate the anxiety that pure THC can provoke.
