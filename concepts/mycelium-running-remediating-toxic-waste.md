## Safety Warnings

Many hazardous waste sites contain multiple toxins. Although mushroom
enzymes break down many chemical contaminants, mushrooms can concentrate
heavy metals. If a site contains heavy metals, mushrooms should not be
eaten until determined safe through testing.
