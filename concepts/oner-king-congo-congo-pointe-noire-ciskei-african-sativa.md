## King Congo: Congo Pointe Noire × Ciskei African Pure Sativa

## Overview

King Congo is a pure F1 sativa bred by Spain"s Tropical Seeds Company.
It combines Congo Pointe Noire and Ciskei P4 F1 genetics — both
African landrace-derived lines — into a single strain that captures
the raw energy and creativity of pure equatorial sativa genetics. For
growers and connoisseurs seeking unhybridized sativa experiences, King
Congo represents an accessible entry point into African landrace
genetics.

## Genetics and Lineage

The parent strains are both African in origin:

**Congo Pointe Noire**: Sourced from the Congo region of Central
Africa, this genetics line carries the characteristics of equatorial
cannabis — tall growth, long flowering, and potent cerebral effects.
Pointe Noire is a major city in the Republic of the Congo, and the
cannabis from this region has historically been valued for its potency
and energetic effects.

**Ciskei P4 F1**: A South [[african-landrace-sativa-strains]]. The P4 F1 designation indicates this
is a stabilized filial generation, offering more consistency than
raw landrace material.

The combination of Central and Southern African genetics creates a
strain with broad [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]] — a rare quality among pure sativa strains.
Most landrace-derived pure sativas are impractical for indoor grows
due to extreme height and flowering time, but King Congo manages to
remain a medium-sized plant that is manageable in grow rooms.

Indoors, the plant yields average to heavy harvests. Outdoors is
where King Congo truly excels, producing significantly more bud than
its indoor counterpart. The outdoor yield potential exceeds
expectations for a pure sativa, making it attractive to commercial
and personal growers alike.

The plant is visually striking during flowering, producing deep green
buds covered in snow-white pistils. The bud formation is described as
"fists of flowers" — dense, substantial clusters that develop
progressively through the flowering period.
