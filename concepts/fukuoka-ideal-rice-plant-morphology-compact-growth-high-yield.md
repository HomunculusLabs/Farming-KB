## Overview

Fukuoka's theory of ideal rice plant morphology is a counterintuitive principle that challenges conventional rice cultivation wisdom. ## The Compact Plant Principle

### Keep the Head Small

Fukuoka does not try to raise tall, fast-growing plants with big leaves. Instead, he keeps the plants as compact as possible. The key principles are:

1. **Keep the head small**: Do not over-nourish the plants
2. **Grow true to natural form**: Let the rice plant develop its natural shape
3. **Sow thickly**: About 250–300 grain-bearing stalks per square yard (20–25 plants)
4. **Do not force large size**: Allow the plant to mature at its own pace
