## The Water Molecule Hypothesis

One proposed mechanism for plant vibration sensing involves water transport.
Plants rely on a continuous column of water in their xylem vessels, pulled
from roots to leaves by transpiration. Vibrations could potentially affect
the movement of water molecules within these vessels, creating pressure waves
that are detected by mechanosensitive proteins. This would mean that the
plant's water transport system doubles as a vibration-detection system,
an elegant example of biological multifunctionality.
