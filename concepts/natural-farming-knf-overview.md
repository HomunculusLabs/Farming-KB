# [[korean-natural-farming]] (KNF) Overview

Korean Natural Farming (KNF) is an innovative agricultural system developed by Dr. [[cho-han-kyu]] in the 1960s at the Janong Natural Farming Institute in South Korea. It was originally intended to change the chemical-based and harmful farming methods being practiced in South Korea, and has since spread to over 15 countries.

## History and Development

Dr. Cho Han Kyu developed natural farming as a student of agriculture and veterinary technology. Even as a final year graduate student (1965), he had the unusual courage and conviction about the irrelevance of Green Revolution Technologies (GRT) for the Asian region. He and his friends boycotted the special paper on GRT and dedicated themselves to examining the best practices adopted by farming communities across three generations in South Korea and Japan.

When Dr. Cho promoted this technology in South Korea, it appeared too subversive to the chemical lobby and government. He was jailed several times, dubbed as a communist. However, when the results were too transparent for everybody to see, truth prevailed and the Korean government accepted the technology. It has since spread to Japan, Mongolia, China, Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines, USA, India, and beyond.

## The [[nutritive-cycle-theory]]

The foundation of KNF is the **Nutritive Cycle Theory**, which states that plants and livestock need different nutrients during different stages of growth. Like humans, plants need different kinds of food at different stages. Just as a baby can only eat baby food, young plants can only be fed with nutrients appropriate for their age.

### Three Growth Stages

1. **Vegetative Growth Stage**: Plants develop roots and shoots, consuming carbohydrates (C) and converting them to nitrogen (N), their main growth requirement at this stage.

2. **Cross-Over Period (Morning Sickness)**: Plants begin flowering in preparation for reproduction. Like pregnant women craving sour foods, plants need "sour nutrients" in the form of phosphoric (P) compounds. This is the most critical period that determines the quality of the final reproductive stage.

3. **Reproductive Growth Stage**: From flowering to ripening, plants begin storing carbohydrates in fruits or storage organs. Potassium (K) is needed for proper color development of fruits.

## Natural Farming Inputs

KNF has a complete system of natural inputs that replace all chemical fertilizers and pesticides:

### Core Inputs
- **Indigenous Microorganisms (IMO)**: The foundation -- collected locally, cultured, and applied to soil
- **Fermented Plant Juice (FPJ)**: Fermented extract of plant sap and chlorophylls; rich enzyme solution
- **Fermented Fruit Juice (FFJ)**: Made from sweet, ripe fruits; nutritional activation enzyme
- **Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN)**: Made from garlic, ginger, cinnamon, and angelica; increases plant robustness
- **Fish Amino Acid (FAA)**: Rich in nitrogen and amino acids from fish
- **Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB)**: Anaerobic bacteria that break sugar into lactic acid; improves soil ventilation

### Mineral Inputs
- **Water-Soluble Calcium (WS-Ca)**: From eggshells dissolved in brown rice vinegar
- **Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-PA)**: From sesame stem charcoal
- **Water-Soluble Potassium (WS-K)**: From tobacco stems
- **Water-Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WS-CaPo)**: From animal bones dissolved in vinegar
- **Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV)**: Used to dissolve minerals and as sterilizer
- **Seawater (SW)**: Provides trace minerals

### Supporting Inputs
- **Yeast**: Enhances metabolism and resynthesizes organic substances
- **Loess Powder**: Uncontaminated soil with trace elements and undefined growth factors
- **Fermented Mixed Compost (FMC)**: Potent compost fortified with NF inputs and IMO

## Key Practices

### Seed/Seedling Treatment (SES)
Seeds are treated briefly with a solution of FPJ, BRV, OHN, and water to restore natural vitality before planting.

### Soil Treatment Solution (SOS)
A comprehensive soil conditioner combining IMO, FPJ, OHN, BRV, LAB, WCaP, FAA, charcoal, soil, seawater, and farmyard manure.

### Stage-Specific Treatments
Different NF input combinations are applied at vegetative growth, changeover period, and reproductive growth stages, all at a basic dilution ratio of 1:1000.

## KNF vs Organic Farming

While similar to organic farming in using non-chemical ingredients, KNF has its own systematic approach:

| Aspect | KNF | Conventional Organic |
|---|---|---|
| Microorganisms | Indigenous, locally collected | Often purchased |
| Fertilizer theory | Nutritive Cycle Theory (right nutrient, right time) | General NPK approach |
| Cost | Very low (homemade inputs) | Often high (purchased inputs) |
| Philosophy | Maximize natural potential | Avoid synthetic chemicals |
| Livestock integration | Integral to the system | Often separate |

## Global Impact

KNF is practiced in over 15 countries and recognized for its strength to produce more at better quality with lower cost. It is also practiced in underdeveloped countries and communities to give them a self-supporting tool that can improve their living. The system is designed to be adaptable -- ingredients used in South Korea can be substituted with natural resources distinct to each region or ecosystem.
