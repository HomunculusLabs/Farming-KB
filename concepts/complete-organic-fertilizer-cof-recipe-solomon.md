## COF vs. Chemical Fertilizers

Chemical fertilizers (typically 16-16-16 or 10-10-10) provide only NPK
and sometimes sulfur, but lack calcium, magnesium, and trace minerals.
Russian investigator Krasil'nikov demonstrated that plants produce
biologically active substances beneficial to human health, and that this
production is strongly influenced by soil microbiology and mineral
availability. COF provides complete nutrition at a fraction of the cost
of commercially packaged organic blends.

## Potassium and Yield vs. Nutrition

Boosting potassium beyond COF levels increases bulk yield but decreases
nutritional content by a similar proportion. Higher potassium triggers
starch and fiber production, not protein, vitamins, enzymes, and minerals
that humans need. Most commercial growers push potassium for profit.
