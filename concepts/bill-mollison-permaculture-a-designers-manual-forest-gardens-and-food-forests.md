Mollison described seven distinct layers in a mature food forest, each

## Tropical vs. Temperate Food Forests

Mollison drew a clear distinction between tropical and temperate food forest
design. Tropical systems, based on traditional practices in Southeast Asia,
the Amazon, and West Africa, achieve higher total productivity because the
growing season is year-round and species diversity is naturally greater.
Temperate food forests have a dormant season that reduces annual production
but can still be highly productive when designed with species adapted to local
conditions. Both systems follow the same structural principles of layered
