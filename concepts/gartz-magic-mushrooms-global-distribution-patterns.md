## Cosmopolitan and Widespread Species

Two species dominate global distribution: [[psilocybe-semilanceata]] ("Liberty Cap") represents the temperate-zone
counterpart, common across Europe and also found throughout the Pacific
Northwest of North America. It is a nitrophilic grassland species that fruits
in autumn, particularly after heavy rainfall.

## Climatic Corridors

Gartz identifies several climatic corridors supporting psychoactive mycoflora:

- **Tropical/subtropical belt**: Home to P. cubensis, P. cyanescens, and
  numerous Panaeolus species. Found in Central America, northern South America,
  sub-Saharan Africa, South and Southeast Asia, and northern Australia.

- **Temperate oceanic zones**: Support P. semilanceata, P. cyanescens (the
  European wood-debris species), and [[psilocybe-stuntzii]], P. baeocystis, P. pelliculosa,
