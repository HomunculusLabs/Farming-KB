# Cannabis Seed Germination and Viability in Breeding Programs (Clarke)

## Seed Germination Basics

[[query-how-to-store-cannabis-seeds-for-long-term-viability]] are planted in spring and usually germinate in 3 to 7 days. The seedling emerges from the ground by pushing through the soil surface. Fresh seeds approach 100% viability, but this decreases with age, [[vegetable-storage-conditions-by-temperature-and-humidity]], and handling. Seeds cannot be stored forever -- even under the best storage conditions, viability eventually declines.

## Germination Conditions

- **Temperature**: Spring planting conditions; warm soil temperatures promote rapid germination
- **Moisture**: Consistent moisture required; soil should not be waterlogged
- **Soil**: High-quality soil with good drainage
- **Depth**: Seeds planted at appropriate depth for seedling emergence
- **Light**: Not required for germination itself but needed immediately after emergence

## Seedling Selection for Breeding

Early selection is a key breeding strategy. Seedling plants take up much less space than adults, allowing thousands to be germinated in a flat. This enables mass screening of seedlings for desirable traits before committing resources to growing full plants.

### Selection Criteria for Seedlings
- Rapid germination rate is a selected trait
- Vigorous early growth indicates strong genetics
- Leaf morphology characteristics can be observed early
- Disease resistance can be screened at seedling stage
- Sex cannot be determined until prefloral stage

## Seed Storage

Seeds are allowed to dry completely, and all vegetable debris is removed before storage. This prevents spoilage caused by molds and other fungi. Seeds should be stored in a cool, dark, dry place. For breeding programs, seed banks must be carefully maintained:

### Breeding Program Seed Management
- Half of the original seed collection should be retained until the viability of further generations is confirmed
- This preserves the original gene pool in case later generations prove inferior
- Seeds are labeled and stored systematically
- Regular viability testing should be performed

### Long-Term Considerations
- Cannabis seeds do not remain viable forever
- Gene pool preservation requires ongoing seed production and refreshment
- Imported strain seeds may represent irreplaceable genetics if the source population is lost
- Colchicine-treated seeds show reduced germination rates

## Colchicine and Seed Viability

When seeds are treated with colchicine (used to induce polyploidy), germination rates drop significantly. If 100 viable seeds are treated and only 40 germinate, it is unlikely that the remaining 60 seeds would have produced desirable polyploid plants. This represents a significant loss of genetic material and highlights the trade-offs of induced polyploidy breeding.

## Seed Production in Cultivated Crops

Both fiber and drug production interfere with the natural cycle and block pathways of inheritance. Special techniques must be used to produce viable seed for the following year without jeopardizing product quality:

### Fiber Crops
- Commercially produced high-fiber monoecious strains of even maturation
- Farmers may leave a portion of the crop to develop mature seeds
- If a hybrid variety is grown, offspring will not all resemble the parent crop
- Desirable characteristics may be lost in subsequent generations

### Seeded Marijuana
- Growers of seeded marijuana for smoking or hashish collect seeds falling during harvesting, drying, and processing
- A mature pistillate plant can produce tens of thousands of seeds if freely pollinated

### Sinsemilla Operations
- Sinsemilla cultivation eliminates pollination entirely
- Seed production requires separate [[cannabis-controlled-pollination-seed-production]] events
- Pollen from a selected male plant is applied to specific pistillate plants
- Resulting hybrid seed is stored for later planting

## Role of Seeds in Strain Preservation

Because [[cannabis-sexual-propagation-genetics]] introduces variation, imported Cannabis strains usually exhibit great seedling diversity. To minimize variation and ensure preservation of desirable parental traits:
1. Careful selection of parent plants
2. Controlled pollination techniques
3. Systematic record-keeping of crosses
4. Proper [[cannabis-seed-storage-and-viability]] viability monitoring
5. Maintenance of backup seed collections

The genetic diversity within imported seed populations is both a challenge (unpredictable offspring) and an opportunity (potential for discovering novel phenotypes).
