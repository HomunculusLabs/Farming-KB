For straw substrates, pasteurization can be carried out with live steam

## Hot Water Bath Pasteurization

The method best suited to home cultivators is the hot water bath. A
55-gallon drum half-filled with water is heated to 160-170 degrees F.
Chopped dry straw is placed into a wire mesh basket and submerged for
30-45 minutes. After removal, all loose water must be allowed to drain
before the straw is spread out to cool to 80 degrees F or less, at
which point it can be spawned. Some compression of straw into the
container increases cropping efficiency.
