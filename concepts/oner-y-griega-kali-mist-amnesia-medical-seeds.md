# Y Griega

**Breeder:** Medical Seeds Co. (Spain)
**Genetics:** [[kali-mist]] st]] × Amnesia
**Potency:** THC 27.12%
**Ratio:** Sativa-dominant
**Flowering Time:** 90 days (indoor)
**Outdoor Harvest:** Early November
**Yield:** ~550g per plant (outdoor) / ~550g per square yard (indoor ScrOG)

## Overview

Y Griega is a high-THC sativa-dominant hybrid from Spain's Medical Seeds Company, a breeder
that, while perhaps less internationally recognized than Dutch or American seedbanks, produces
strains of exceptional quality. The strain's name comes from the former Spanish word for the
letter "Y" (before the Real Academia Española officially changed it to "ye"), giving this
strain a distinctly Spanish cultural identity.

The lineage is a pairing of two legendary sativas: Kali Mist and Amnesia (also known as
Amnesia Haze). Both parent strains are renowned among sativa enthusiasts, and the Haze
grandparent genetics come through strongly in Y Griega. This makes it particularly appealing
to Haze fans, especially those who grow indoors — a combination that would have been
unthinkable a few decades ago when Haze was considered strictly an outdoor or greenhouse
variety.

## Growth Characteristics

Y Griega is a vigorous, powerful-growing sativa that demands respect from cultivators:

- **Outdoor height:** Can reach up to 11 feet in full sun, making it one of the taller
  strains available to outdoor growers
- **Indoor management:** [[low-stress-training]] Training (LST) and ScrOG methods, the strain
  can be successfully managed indoors despite its natural tendency toward significant
  vertical growth
- **Vegetative vigor:** Described as starting life "much like the universe, with a huge
  surge of power and dramatic force" — expect explosive early growth
- **Bud coverage:** Nearly every inch of stem is covered in buds by the end of flowering,
  with thick white hairs creating a striking "white Christmas" appearance

## Flowering and Yield

The 90-day indoor flowering period is long — typical of heavily Haze-influenced sativas — but
the results justify the wait. Y Griega delivers impressive yields in both indoor and outdoor
settings:

- **Indoor (ScrOG):** ~550g per square yard us [[screen-of-[[green-cannabis-grow-bible-hash-making-and-trichome-extraction|green]]]] en]] of Green method
- **Outdoor:** ~550g per individual plant, harvested around early November
- **Bud density:** Moderate density with exceptional resin coverage and visual appeal

The extended flowering time means Y Griega is best suited to growers with patience and
dedicated flowering space.

## Height Management

Controlling Y Griega's height is the primary cultivation challenge. The breeder provides
specific guidance:

1. **Force flowering early** — trigger 12/12 when the plant is at half the desired final
   height or even smaller, as the significant stretch during early flowering can double
   the plant's size
2. **Use ScrOG** — the breeders "highly recommend" Screen of Green setups, as the mesh
   naturally controls height while spreading the plant horizontally, making the vigorous
   growth more manageable
3. **LST throughout vegetative stage** — bending and tying branches helps control vertical
   growth and encourages the bushy structure needed for ScrOG success

## Effects Profile

Y Griega delivers a classic, powerful Haze-influenced experience:

- **Onset:** A bolt of energy hits rapidly, described as sending the user soaring
- **Peak:** Effects are intensely cerebral, euphoric, and mentally stimulating — the kind
  of high that "throws you right up in the air"
- **Duration:** Long-lasting, consistent with heavy Haze genetics
- **Come-down:** A gradual descent into a "relaxing bubble" a few hours later, providing
  a smooth transition rather than an abrupt crash

## Aroma and Flavor

The scent profile is described as "classic Haze" — earthy, spicy, and slightly sweet with
the signature complexity that Haze genetics are known for. The aroma sets high expectations,
and according to the breeder's assessment, the experience "super smashes them into a million
pieces." Medical Seeds Co. also notes that Y Griega makes excellent hash and oil, suggesting
high trichome density and resin quality suitable for solvent and solventless extraction.

## Terpene Profile

The classic Haze aroma points to a terpene profile likely dominated by:

- **Terpinolene:** The signature Haze terpene, contributing complex floral, fruity, and
  slightly herbal notes
- **Myrcene:** Adding depth and body to the aroma while modulating THC effects
- **Caryophyllene:** Contributing peppery spice notes and potential anti-inflammatory
  benefits
- **Humulene:** Earthy, woody undertones complementing the Haze character

## Medical Applications

With 27.12% THC and a clear-headed, energetic profile, Y Griega has significant therapeutic
potential:

- **Treatment-resistant conditions:** The high THC content may benefit patients who have
  developed tolerance to lower-potency strains
- **Fatigue and lethargy:** Powerful energizing effects counter physical and mental
  exhaustion
- **Depression:** The euphoric onset can provide significant temporary relief
- **Concentration and focus:** Despite the potency, the Haze influence tends toward
  mental clarity rather than confusion
- **Extraction patients:** The excellent resin production makes this strain suitable for
  creating concentrated medicines (hash, oil, rosin)

## The Hybrid Revolution Context

Y Griega represents the success of what the breeder terms the "hybrid revolution" — the
breeding movement that brought traditionally outdoor-only sativa genetics like Haze into
forms manageable for indoor cultivation. A few decades ago, when indoor growing was
dominant, "no one would have ever hoped that they could grow Haze plants indoors." Strains
like Y Griega, with their carefully balanced sativa-dominant genetics, represent the
culmination of this breeding philosophy.

## Cultivation Tips

1. **ScrOG is strongly recommended** — the breeder explicitly endorses this method for
   managing Y Griega's vigor
2. **Flip to flowering early** — force 12/12 when plants are 2.5–3 feet tall for a final
   indoor height of 5–6 feet
3. **Plan for the long flowering time** — 90 days means careful scheduling to avoid
   conflicts with other grow cycles
4. **Support heavy branches** — the extensive bud coverage can weigh down stems; use
   trellis netting in addition to ScrOG
5. **Maximize light penetration** — defoliate strategically to ensure lower bud sites
   receive adequate light during the extended flowering period
6. **Consider for extraction** — excellent resin profile makes Y Griega suitable for hash, rosin, or oil production
