## Growth Characteristics

All three phenotypes grow to a medium height with a good amount of stretch. The breeders recommend topping out the plants and rounding them out into a bush shape to manage the stretch and encourage lateral branching. This technique helps maximize yield by creating an even canopy with multiple bud sites that receive adequate light exposure.

A soil grow is perfect for this strain, and it will not require much in the way of additional feeding or special treatment. Mickey Kush is surprisingly hardy, making it accessible to growers with moderate experience who want a potent strain without demanding cultivation requirements.

The plant is relatively low-maintenance compared to other high-THC varieties, which often require precise nutrient management and environmental controls.
