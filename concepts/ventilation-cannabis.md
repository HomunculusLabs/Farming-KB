# Ventilation Cannabis

Ventilation is as important as water, light, heat, and fertilizer for a healthy indoor
cannabis garden. Fresh air is the least expensive essential component required to produce
a heavy harvest. A proper ventilation system removes stale, humid air and replaces it
with fresh CO2-rich air, maintaining optimal growing conditions.

## Night Ventilation

Plants still respire during darkness, producing moisture. If humidity rises too high at
night, ventilation may be needed. However, some growers reduce or stop ventilation during
dark hours to preserve CO2 buildup from plant respiration, running fans only when humidity
exceeds a threshold.
