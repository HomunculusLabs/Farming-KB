# cannabis disease pest resistance breeding clarke

Disease and pest resistance are among the most important traits for
Cannabis breeders to consider, yet they are often overlooked in favor of
more visible characteristics like potency and yield. [[cannabis-breeding-traits-selection-criteria-clarke]]. A strain may be bred for resistance to a specific disease through
natural selection under controlled conditions.

### Damping-Off Fungus Resistance

Clarke provides damping-off fungus as a detailed example of disease
resistance breeding. If flats of seedlings are infected by damping-off
disease and nearly all of them die, the surviving few will possess some
degree of resistance. The breeding procedure follows these steps:

1. Infect flats of seedlings with damping-off fungus
2. Most seedlings die; the surviving few carry inheritable resistance
3. Cross the surviving plants to pass resistance to offspring
4. Inoculate flats of seedling offspring with damping-off fungus
5. Select the most resistant individuals for further crossing
6. Repeat over several generations until a pure-breeding resistant strain
   is established

Each generation is tested by inoculation to verify that resistance is
being strengthened. This approach relies on the natural genetic variation
present within the population to provide the raw material for selection.

### Greenhouse Mold Resistance

With the popularity of greenhouse [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]], Clarke identifies
a need for strains with increased resistance to mold. Molds usually
reproduce by wind-borne spores, so negligence can rapidly lead to epidemic
disaster in the confined greenhouse environment. Selection and breeding of
the least infected plants from affected populations should result in
strains with increased resistance over successive generations.

## Breeding for Pest Resistance

Resistance to pest attack works through many of the same mechanisms as
disease resistance. It is common to find stands of Cannabis where one or a
few plants are infested with insects while adjacent plants remain untouched.

### The Role of Cannabinoid and Terpenoid Resins

Cannabinoid and terpenoid resins are most probably responsible for repelling
insect attack in Cannabis. Levels of these defensive compounds vary from
plant to plant, [[fukuoka-creating-natural-farm-site-selection-establishment]] variation that breeders can exploit.
Cannabis has evolved defenses against insect attack in the form of
resin-secreting [[cannabis-breeding-traits-selection-criteria-clarke]] such
as potency, maturation rate, and yield. A currently unrecognized gene may
control resistance to a pest or disease, accessible only by maintaining
broad genetic diversity in breeding populations.
