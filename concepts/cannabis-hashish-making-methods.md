# Cannabis Hashish Making Methods

Hashish (hash) is a concentrated cannabis product made by separating and collecting the resinous trichomes from the plant material. Trichomes contain the highest concentrations of cannabinoids (thc, cbd) and terpenes. Hash has been produced for thousands of years — the earliest methods involved simply rubbing the plants with hands.

## What Hashish Is

### Trichomes
- Tiny [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]] on cannabis flowers and leaves
- Contain cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids
- Three types: bulbous, capitate-sessile, and capitate-stalked (the largest, most potent)
- When separated and pressed, trichomes form hashish

### Types of Hash
- **[[green-cannabis-dry-sift-water-extraction]]**: Trichomes separated by sieving dry plant material
- **Ice water ([[cervantes-water-hash-bubble-hash]])**: Trichomes separated using ice water and agitation
- **Hand-rubbed (charas)**: Trichomes collected by rubbing living plants
- **Chemical extraction**: Solvent-based (bho, CO2, ethanol) — technically not traditional hash

## Dry Sift Method

### Equipment
- Screens/micron bags (various mesh sizes)
- Collection surface (glass or mirror)
- Card or scraper
- Dry, cured cannabis (optimal moisture: 8-10%)

### Process
1. Place dried cannabis on a screen (100-150 micron for quality, 25-75 micron for maximum yield)
2. Gently agitate — don't force material through
3. Trichomes fall through the screen onto the collection surface
4. Collect the fallen material (kief)
5. For higher quality: re-sift through progressively finer screens
6. Press kief with a pollen press or by hand with warmth to form hash

### Grading by Screen Size
- **Full melt (90-120 micron)**: Highest quality, melts completely when heated, clear smoke
- **Half melt (73-90 micron)**: Good quality, partially melts
- **Cooking grade (25-73 micron)**: Contains some plant material, best for edibles/cooking

### Tips
- Work in cool, dry conditions (trichomes are brittle and separate easily)
- Don't over-grind (plant material contamination)
- Fresh frozen material can be sifted for higher terpene content
- Multiple sifts at different micron sizes produces graded product

## Ice Water Extraction (Bubble Hash)

### Equipment
- Bubble bags (set of 5 bags with different micron screens: 220, 160, 120, 73, 25)
- Bucket
- Ice
- Water
- Dry, frozen cannabis (fresh frozen produces best results)
- Wooden spoon or paint mixer

### Process
1. Line bucket with bubble bags (largest micron inside, smallest outside)
2. Fill bucket with ice and water (cold separates trichomes by making them brittle)
3. Add cannabis material (30-200g per batch)
4. Stir vigorously for 15-20 minutes (the "wash")
5. Let settle for 10 minutes
6. Lift bags one at a time, letting water drain
7. Collect material from each bag screen
8. Each bag contains a different grade of hash
9. Air-dry the collected hash on cardboard (2-7 days)
10. Cure in [[sterile-spore-print-collection-glass-jar-technique]] for 2-4 weeks

### Grading
- **Micron 120-220**: Lower quality, more plant material, cooking grade
- **Micron 73-120**: Mid-quality, good for pressing
- **Micron 25-73**: Highest quality ("full melt"), minimal plant contamination

### Tips
- Use frozen cannabis (fresh frozen preserves terpenes better than dried)
- More ice = colder water = better trichome separation
- Don't over-agitate (damages trichomes, increases plant contamination)
- Multiple short washes produce better quality than one long wash
