## Effects of Peyote

The mescaline in peyote produces characteristic effects:

- Brilliant, kaleidoscopic visual hallucinations with vivid colors
- Enhanced color perception and visual acuity
- Altered sense of time and space, with time seeming to slow down
- Deeply introspective and contemplative states
- Feelings of unity, connectedness, and love
- Physical sensations including nausea (often considered purifying)
- Enhanced auditory perception, particularly sensitivity to music and 
  singing

The experience typically lasts 10-14 hours. The ceremony's structure
--
including singing, prayer, and the communal setting -- provides a
supportive
framework for navigating the intense effects.
