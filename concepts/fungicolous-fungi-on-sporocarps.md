## Fungicolous Fungi on Sporocarps

Fungi living on fresh or decaying sporocarps of other nonlichenized fungi (SCIF — sporocarp-inhabiting fungi) represent one of the best-studied groups of mycoparasites. Their substrata include dry or soft macromycete sporocarps, anamorphs, conidiomata, conidiophores, and conidia. SCIF are mainly Ascomycota or their anamorphs, but also include some Zygomycota and many Basidiomycota (particularly Tremellales and some Agaricales).

## Fungi on Slime Molds (Myxogastrea)

Sporocarps of [[slime-molds]] are colonized by specialized fungi. Most obligate myxomyceticolous conidial fungi smother the sporangia, usually turning them white. Hyphae penetrate the spore mass, killing spores and preventing their release. Typically, only a single colonizing species is present per collection, and most fructifications of the host are colonized.

Two groups are distinguished:

- **Parasites of calcareous Physarales**: *Gliocladium album*, *Nectriopsis violacea*, *[[sesquicillium-microsporum]]*
- **Parasites of noncalcareous myxomycetes**: *Aphanocladium album* sensu stricto, *Byssostilbe stilbigera*

*Nectriopsis violacea* is found only on *Fuligo* species; *Blistum tomentosum* only on Trichiales. *Nectriopsis exigua* (anamorph *Verticillium rexianum*) is ubiquitous across all major myxomycete groups, as is *Stilbella byssiseda*.

Less specific colonizers include the hemiascomycete *Dipodascus macrosporus*, which can live in slime trails of *Badhamia utricularis* and resist digestion if ingested by the plasmodium, acting as a facultative parasite.

## Fungi on Ascomycota

### On Discomycetes (Cup Fungi)

The most characteristic colonizers of ascomata of discomycetes (Pezizales, Leotiales) include:

- *Hypomyces cervinigenus* (anamorph *Mycogone cervina*) on *Mycolachnea hemisphaerica*
- *Hypomyces stephanomatis* (anamorph *Stephanoma strigosum*) on *Helvella* species
- *Sphaeronaemella helvellae* on *Gyromitra* species
- *Hormiactis* species on *Morchella* and *Peziza* species
- *Hypomyces leotiicola* (anamorph *Sepedonium leotiarum*) on *Leotia lubrica*

*Xenolachne longicornis* (heterobasidiomycete) grows on *Cudoniella* and *Disciniella* where it replaces the hymenium.

### On Sequestrate Ascomycetes

*Tuber* and other tuberoid hypogeous Pezizales are colonized by *Acremonium*, *Verticillium* sect. *Prostrata*, and *Mortierella*. *Nodulisporium tuberum* was described from *Tuber maculatum*. *Battarrina inclusa* (Bionectriaceae) occurs inside ascomata of *Tuber puberulum*.

The subterranean sporocarps of *Elaphomyces* often are detected by the conspicuous epigeous ascomata of their parasites: *Cordyceps ophioglossoides*, *C. capitata*, and *C. canadensis*.

### On Coriaceous Discomycetes (Phacidiales, Rhytismatales)

A specific competitive mycoparasitic relationship exists between *Hendersonia pinicola* or *H. acicola* and *Lophodermium concolor* or *Lophodermella sulcigena*. *H. pinicola* causes needle cast in *Pinus contorta* and can prevent ascoma formation in *L. sulcigena*, naturally controlling epidemics.

### On Pyrenomycete Stromata

Common colonizers of old carbonized stromata include *Polydesmia pruinosa* and *Cosmospora* species (*C. episphaeria*, *C. purtonii*). *Hypomyces triseptatus* is unique in growing on carbonized perithecia. *Tubeufia cerea* (with *Helicosporium* anamorph) occurs on old stromata of *Diatrype stigma* and species of *Eutypa*, *Eutypella*, and *Hypoxylon*.

### On Soft Perithecia (Hypocrealean Fungi)

*Nectria cinnabarina* is commonly overgrown by the hyphomycete *Tympanosporium parasiticum*. *Nitschkia parasitans* grows only on *N. cinnabarina*. *Circinoniesslia nectriae*, *Valetoniella crucipila*, and *V. pauciornata* have been reported on *Nectria* species.

### On Anamorphic Ascomycota

- **Hansfordia pulvinata** — destructive parasite on many dematiaceous conidial fungi, particularly *Cercospora* and *Passalora fulva* on tomato, producing the fungistatic sesquiterpene deoxyphomenone
- **Cladosporiella cercosporicola** — parasitizes *Passalora personata* on peanut leaves
- **Verticillium luteo-album** — highly susceptible to hyphal interference, but also antagonizes *Alternaria brassicae*, *Pleospora* species, and *[[trichothecium-roseum]]*
- **Penicillium funiculosum** — smothers various fungi in culture, preventing pineapple fruit diseases
- **Fusarium udum** — parasitizes *Aspergillus luchuensis*, producing a diffusible toxin inducing vesicular deformations

## Fungi on Basidiomycota

### On Aphyllophorales

Most colonizers are Ascomycota. *Sphaerostilbella* species grow on wood-decaying aphyllophorales; *S. aureonitens* and *Gliocladium polyporicola* are particularly common on *Stereum* species. *Hypocreopsis lichenoides* grows with *Hymenochaete tabacina*.

**Destructive necrotrophic mycoparasites** of ascomycetes (e.g., *Ceratocystis*) occur among *Bjerkandera*, *Lenzites*, *Trichaptum*, *Trametes*, *Schizophyllum*, and *Pleurotus*. *[[schizophyllum-commune]]* coils around and penetrates host hyphae.

### On Polypores

A considerable range of fungicolous species grow on polypores, with *[[hypomyces-aurantius]]* being one of the most common. Key species include:

| Species | Primary Hosts |
|---------|--------------|
| *Hypomyces aurantius* (anamorph *Cladobotryum varium*) | Various aphyllophorales |
| *Hypomyces polyporinus* (*C. clavisporum*) | *Trametes versicolor* |
| *Cosmospora vilior* (*A. berkeleyanum*) | Various polypores |
| *[[ophiostoma-polyporicola]]* | *[[fomitopsis-pinicola]]*, *[[piptoporus-betulinus]]* |
| *[[sporophagomyces-chrysostomus]]* | Ganodermataceae |
| *[[hypocrea-pulvinata]]* | *Fomitopsis pinicola*, *Piptoporus betulinus* |

### Succession on Sporocarps

A special group of wood-rotting fungi (successors) inhabits material previously decayed by predecessors. *Antrodiella citrinella* grows on sporocarps of *Fomitopsis pinicola*; *A. hoehnelii* on *Inonotus radiatus*. Sporocarps of successors may develop on those of predecessors because mycelial channels left by dead fungi act as canals for hyphae of succeeding species.
