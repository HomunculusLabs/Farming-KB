## Key Takeaways for Design

- Design for beneficial connections, not species counts
- Use information actively — it is only a potential resource until acted upon
- Create stress mosaics for maximum richness
- Understand that stability is dynamic self-regulation, not a fixed state
- Recognize that very old systems can be fragile, not robust
- Note survivor species as indicators of long-term regional resilience
