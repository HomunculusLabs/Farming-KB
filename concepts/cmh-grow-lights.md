## Efficiency

CMH lamps are more efficient than standard metal halide in lumens-per-watt. The 315-watt
CMH is the most common size and offers efficiency competitive with 400-watt HPS while
producing a superior spectrum for plant growth. Pulse-start metal halides (including CMH)
are brighter and more efficient than their predecessors. The 315-watt CMH covers roughly
the same area as a 400-watt HPS while using less electricity.

## Ballasts for CMH

CMH lamps require a compatible electronic ballast designed specifically for ceramic
metal halide technology. Standard MH or HPS ballasts are not suitable for CMH lamps.
The ballast should match the wattage of the lamp. Quality electronic ballasts run
cooler and more efficiently than magnetic ballasts.
