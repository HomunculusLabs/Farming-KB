# Cover Crops and Soil Biology

Cover crops are plants grown primarily to benefit the soil rather than for
harvest. In "Teaming With Microbes," Lowenfels and Lewis present cover crops
as one of the most powerful tools for building and maintaining soil biology.
Living roots are the engine of the soil food web, providing the carbon
exudates that fuel microbial communities. Cover crops ensure that this engine
runs continuously, even when cash crops are not growing, preventing the soil
food web from starving during fallow periods.
