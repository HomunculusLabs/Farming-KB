# Lactic Acid Bacteria Serum (LAB) Preparation

Lactic acid bacteria are anaerobic microorganisms that break sugar into lactic acid in the absence of oxygen. LAB is very effective in improving soil ventilation, promoting rapid growth of [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]].

## What LAB Does

- Improves soil ventilation and aeration
- Promotes rapid growth of [[jarrold-glass-jar-spore-print-collection-drying]]
- Porous paper (paper towel)
- Rubber band or thread

### Step 1: Collect from Air via Rice Wash
1. Put rice-washed water 15-20 cm deep in a jar
2. Cover mouth with porous paper, leave in shade
3. At 23-25C, lactic acid bacteria will propagate in 5-7 days
4. Solution will start to smell sour

   - **Middle**: Clear yellow liquid -- this is the [[cannabis-lab-serum-cultivation]]
   - **Bottom**: Debris/waste
2. Strain and collect the yellow liquid (pure lactic acid bacteria serum)

## Storage

### Short-term (Room Temperature)
1. Mix equal amount of crude sugar or molasses with the serum
2. Sugar serves as food to keep bacteria alive
3. Do NOT use refined white sugar (chemically bleached, harms bacteria)
4. One-to-one ratio with sugar is suggested

### Long-term (Refrigerator)
- Keep refined LAB serum at 1-15C
- No direct sunlight

## Usage

### Basic Dilution
- **Standard**: 1:1000 in water
- Ensure water is not chemically treated (chlorine kills bacteria)

### For Plants
- Spray diluted LAB on plant leaves and soil
- Reinforces anabolism of microbes on plant stems and leaves
- More effective when used with FPJs
- Extremely effective for making fruits and leaves large
- Reduce LAB amount in later growth stages to prevent overgrowth

### For Soil
- Fields recover fertility and soil becomes soft/fluffy when IMO mixed with LAB is sprayed
- Increases phosphate solubility in phosphate-accumulated soil
- Helps overcome saline disorders from phosphate decomposition
