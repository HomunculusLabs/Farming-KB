## The Operational Group Concept

Mycological survey designers use "operational groups" — assemblages
defined by shared habitat, substrate, ecological function, or [[bloomfield-mushroom-cells-stem-cells-totipotency-fungal-tissues]]s, bracket fungi,
puffballs, coral fungi, and other large fruiting bodies. Surveys involve
systematic walking of transects or plots during fruiting seasons. Both
ascomycetes (cup fungi, morels, truffles) and basidiomycetes (gilled
mushrooms, boletes, polypores) are included. Specimens are dried and
vouchered for herbarium deposition. Collection is seasonal and requires
repeated visits to capture the full complement of species.

and microfungi (detected by microscopy or isolation). Divided by decay
