## Pest and Disease Management

Hops are susceptible to molds and insects, which must be prevented proactively rather than treated reactively:

- **Drip irrigation:** Essential for keeping foliage dry and preventing mold problems. Overhead watering that wets the leaves invites fungal disease.
- **Organic pesticides:** Used as a preventive measure against insect pests.
- **Air circulation:** Adequate spacing and trellis design ensures good airflow through the bines.

## Growth Cycle

Hops take approximately 4 months from planting to flower production. They die back to the ground in winter and regrow from the root crown each spring. First-year plants produce minimal yield; production increases significantly in the second and third years.

**Storage:** Seal dried cones in a freezer bag and put them in a freezer until ready to use. Properly dried and frozen hops can maintain quality for a year or more.
