# Jack the Ripper — Subcool TGA Genetics

## Overview

Jack the Ripper is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Subcool of Team Green Avengers (TGA
Genetics). Named for its devastatingly potent effects, this strain has earned legendary
status among both growers and connoisseurs for its extraordinarily high THC content,
complex [[cannabis-terpene-profile]], and the presence of THCV (tetrahydrocannabivarin), a rare
cannabinoid with potential [[psilocybin-therapeutic-applications]]. With tested THC levels reaching
26.6%, Jack the Ripper is one of the most potent sativa-dominant strains documented in
S.T. Oner's Cannabis Sativa series. (see [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]]).

## Genetic Lineage

Jack the Ripper is a cross of Jack's Cleaner P1 and Space Queen. The Jack's Cleaner
parent itself derives from Jack Herer crossed with The Cleaner, a Skoosh-bred strain
containing [[cannabis-strain-mango-haze-northern-lights-skunk-haze]] #5 and Purple Haze genetics. The Cleaner earned its name
from its distinctive Mr. Clean-like odor. The Jack's Cleaner line was ultimately
pollinated by a Jack Herer female to stabilize the prized characteristics. (see [[skywalker-og]]).

The full genetic pedigree includes Romulan, Lamb's Bread, Pluton, and Cindy99 BCGA
in its extended family tree. Space Queen, the pollen parent, contributes additional
potency [[query-how-do-i-maximize-terpene-and-resin-production-in-cannabis]]. This deep genetic heritage from some of the most
celebrated cannabis lines gives Jack the Ripper an exceptionally complex and layered
effect profile.

## Plant Structure and Growth

Jack the Ripper expresses two main phenotypes, both carrying a dominant Lemon Haze
aroma. The phenotypes can be differentiated by scent and height:

- **Phenotype 1** carries additional pine notes and tends to be the shorter of the two
  variants.
- **Phenotype 2** leans more purely into the Lemon Haze terpene profile and grows
  somewhat taller.

Neither phenotype will reach excessive heights unless given extended vegetative time
outdoors. The plant develops a bushy, well-branched structure when allowed an adequate
vegetative period. Subcool recommends growing Jack the Ripper untopped to preserve
the natural apical dominance and cola formation.

## Cultivation

Jack the Ripper performs best in organic soil, where its terpene profile and resin
production reach their full potential. The Rev (cultivation editor for SKUNK Magazine)
specifically recommends organic cultivation for sativa-dominant strains, noting that
these "wild-ish and highly adaptable plants" do not respond well to force-fed
[[organic-nutrients-vs-synthetic-nutrients]]. However, the strain is also manageable in hydroponic systems
and will not present significant difficulties for experienced hydro growers.

Key [[mushroom-species-cultivation-parameters]]:
- **Vegetative period:** Allow a good long vegetative period for the plant to develop
  its full bushy structure before flowering.
- **Flowering time:** Approximately 8 weeks, though some phenotypes may run slightly
  longer toward 9 weeks.
- **Yield:** Moderate to heavy. Buds are not excessively leafy, reducing trimming time.
- **Nutrient needs:** Relatively light feeding requirements in organic soil. Avoid
  over-fertilization which can diminish terpene expression.
- **Bud development:** Spear-shaped white buds begin forming around day 12 of flowering,
  with production ramping up significantly through mid-flower.

## Aroma, Flavor, and Appearance

The harvested buds produce a complex bouquet of tropical fruit with undertones of
burning hash. Some phenotypes add piney, resinous notes to the profile. The dominant
Lemon Haze terpenes are immediately apparent on opening a jar, with layers of citrus,
tropical sweetness, and an earthy hash finish developing on the palate.

## Effects and Cannabinoid Profile

Jack the Ripper delivers a powerful, soaring cerebral high that hits with surprising
intensity. The onset is rapid and disorienting in the best possible way — an "amazing
head high" that lifts the user into an intensely focused, euphoric state. The name
is apt: this strain catches you unawares and then "sends you straight towards the
light."
