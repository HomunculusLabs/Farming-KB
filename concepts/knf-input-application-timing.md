# KNF Input Application Timing

The [[cho-nutritive-cycle-theory]] Theory states that plants need different nutrients during different stages of growth. Like humans, plants need different kinds of food at different stages. This guide covers when to apply each KNF input during the plant growth cycle.

## The Three Growth Stages

### 1. Vegetative Growth Stage

Plants develop roots and shoots. They consume Carbohydrates (C) and convert them to Nitrogen (N), their main growth requirement.

**Primary inputs:**
- [[knf-fish-amino-acid|FAA]] -- rich in nitrogen, promotes vegetative growth
- [[knf-fermented-fruit-juice|FFJ]] / [[jadam-fermented-plant-juice]] -- provides hormones and enzymes for growth
- [[knf-lactic-acid-bacteria-serum|LAB]] -- improves soil ventilation, promotes rapid growth
- [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient|OHN]] -- strengthens plants, used throughout all stages

**Dilution:** All inputs at 1:1000 with water (unless otherwise noted)

**Purpose:** Promote vegetative growth and increase volume and size of crops.

### 2. Changeover Period (Cross-Over / "Morning Sickness")

Plants begin flowering in preparation for reproduction. They crave "sour nutrients" in the form of [[cho-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid-preparation]] (P) compounds.

**Primary inputs:**
- [[knf-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid|WPA]] -- supplies phosphoric acid demand
- [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient|OHN]] -- supports the transition
- [[knf-fermented-fruit-juice|FFJ]] -- provides energy for flowering

**Dilution:** All inputs at 1:1000 with water

**Purpose:** Supply the demand for phosphoric acid during the transition from vegetative to [[knf-reproductive-growth-stage]].

### 3. Reproductive Growth Stage

Period from flowering to ripening. Plants store carbohydrates in fruits or other storage organs. More calcium is required. For proper color development of fruits, Potassium (K) is also needed.

**Primary inputs:**
- [[knf-water-soluble-calcium|WSC]] -- more calcium required at this stage
- [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient|OHN]] -- continued support
- [[knf-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid|WPA]] -- continued phosphorus support

**Dilution:** All inputs at 1:1000 with water

**NOT recommended:** [[knf-fish-amino-acid|FAA]] during reproductive growth -- may induce overgrowth.

## Special Treatments

### Ripeness and Enhanced Sweetness (Fruits)

Spray 15 days and 2 days before harvesting (2 times):

| Input | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| OHN | 1 ml |
| WCA (WSC) | 2 ml |
| Seawater (SW) | 30 ml |

Mixed in 1000 ml of water.

### Crack Prevention in Fruits and Vegetables

Fruits crack when rains arrive after a long drought. Apply during drought:

| Input | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| OHN | 1 ml |
| BRV | 2 ml |
| FPJ | 2 ml |
| LAB | 1 ml |
| Seawater (SW) | 30 ml |

Mixed in 1000 ml of water.

### Reinvigorating Weakened Crops

| Input | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| OHN | 1:1000 |
| FPJ | 1:500 |
| BRV | 1:500 |

For [[staycare-soft-rot-fungi-in-pollutant-degradation]] or anthracnose, add WCA at 1:1000.

## General Application Rules

- All inputs diluted at 1:1000 in water unless otherwise specified
- Apply in early morning or late afternoon (avoid hot sun)
- Additional inputs can be mixed in the solution depending on necessity
- OHN can be used throughout all stages at 1:500 to 1:1000
- FAA is NOT recommended during reproductive growth
- LAB makes fruits and leaves large but reduce amount approaching later stages (decreases sweetness)

## Key Principle

Dr. Cho's view: providing nutrients in excess to plants is not needed -- it will only bring disease. Use the right material at the right amount and at the right stage.
