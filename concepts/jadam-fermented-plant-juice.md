# JADAM Liquid Fertilizer (JLF)

JADAM liquid fertilizer (JLF) is a simple, ultra-low-cost liquid fertilizer made from crop residues, wild grass, and leaf mold. Unlike many organic liquid fertilizers, it requires no sugar, no molasses, and no complicated fermentation management.

## Core Principle: The Nutritional Perspective

JADAM's approach to liquid fertilizer is radically simple: "Things similar to my crops are good food for my crops." The best fertilizer for tomatoes is made from tomatoes. Use crop residues and wild grass -- these materials already contain the perfect balance of N, P, K, Ca, Mg, B and other nutrients your crops need.

This stands in contrast to the expert approach of analyzing and adjusting each nutrient separately, which is complicated and nearly impossible for farmers to practice.

## How to Make JLF

The process is extremely simple:

1. Fill a container with crop residues, wild grass, and/or fruit waste
2. Fill up with water
3. Add a handful of leaf mold (about 2-3 kg for 500 L)
4. Close the lid
5. Done

No sugar. No molasses. No stirring needed (unless ingredients sink). No pH adjustment needed -- JADAM inputs stay around pH 7 because no sugar/molasses is used.

## Fermentation Time

- Summer: ready in about 7 days
- Spring preparation for next year's use is recommended
- The older the liquid fertilizer, the better (like soy sauce)
- Can be used at ANY stage of fermentation -- just dilute above x30

## Standard Dilution

- Standard: x100 dilution (1 part JLF to 100 parts water)
- Range: x20 to x300
- Calculate based on 1 ton of water per 0.03 ha (0.08 ac)
- Start at x100 and adjust based on plant response

## Key Ingredients

**Wild grass JLF:**
- Wild grass contains 1.5-2.5% nitrogen (dried weight), higher in spring
- Fill container with wild grass, fill with water, add leaf mold, close lid
- Dilution: x20 to x300
- Apply throughout growth period, to soil or foliar

**Crop residue JLF:**
- Use leaves, branches, and fruits of the crop itself
- Tomato leaves for tomatoes, grape residues for grapes
- Add wild grass as supplementary material

**Nitrogen-rich ingredients** (food waste, fish) should be made separately from other materials.

## Additions

- Add sea salt up to 0.1% salinity (0.5 kg per 500 L) -- except for human feces, urine, and food waste
- If smell is too strong, add phyllite powder to 0.2% (1 kg per 500 L)
- For foliar application: filter well and mix with [[jadam-herbal-pest-control|JADAM wetting agent (JWA)]]

## Continuous Use System

JLF containers can be used indefinitely:
1. After using all the liquid, do NOT remove solids
2. Fill with new crop residues and wild grass on top of used solids
3. Fill with water, add leaf mold, close lid
4. Container interior never needs cleaning
5. For long-term storage, tie lid tightly to minimize evaporation

## Foliar Application

For foliar spraying, JLF must be well-filtered and mixed with JADAM wetting agent (JWA) to coat evenly and not leave marks on plants. For 500 L of spray, use more than 3 L of JWA.

## Why No Sugar or Molasses

Commercial liquid fertilizers use sugar/molasses, which turns the solution strongly acidic (low pH). JADAM's method using water and leaf mold keeps the end solution around pH 6.5, which is best for the soil. The C/N ratio theory and pH adjustment theories promoted by input manufacturers are rejected by JADAM as unnecessarily complicated.
