### Why Plowing Compacts Soil

Conventional wisdom holds that plowing loosens soil. Fukuoka argues the opposite:

- The effect of plowing is like kneading bread: soil is broken into smaller particles with increasingly regular arrangement and smaller interstitial spaces
- The result is a harder, denser soil
- This was confirmed when herbicide use reduced plowing frequency and farmers found yields improved -- demonstrating that intertillage had only been effective for weeding, not for soil loosening
