## Tracing Contamination Sources

Observing where contamination appears in a petri dish reveals its source. Mold
growing beside a transferred wedge suggests the contaminant was associated with
the previous culture. Contamination along the outer periphery indicates airborne
spores entered during incubation, settling nearest their point of entry.
