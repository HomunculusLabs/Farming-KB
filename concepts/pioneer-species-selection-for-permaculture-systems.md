## Tree Lupin as a Pioneer

The Tree Lupin (Lupinus arboreus) is an evergreen perennial shrub or
small tree to about 2 m, native to California. It is nitrogen fixing
and a good nurse crop, tolerant of frost, preferring deep loose soil.
It provides good bee forage, flowering from late winter to early
summer with a second flowering in autumn. It can be cut hard to
supply nitrogen-rich mulch. Notably, hungry kangaroos will not eat
it, which is an advantage in many situations. It can be grown from
cuttings or seed.
