Reduction was carried out with LAH in anhydrous ether (5.9 g LAH
in 310 mL ether, 6 h reflux). After careful quenching with 1.8 N
H2SO4, the amine was isolated via picrate salt formation (8.0 g,
mp 176-181 °C from water), then liberated as the hydrochloride
(4.72 g white crystals).
