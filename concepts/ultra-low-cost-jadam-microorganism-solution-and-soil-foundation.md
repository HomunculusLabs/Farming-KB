## JADAM Indigenous Microorganism Solution and Soil Foundation

JADAM indigenous microorganism solution (JMS) is a cornerstone input in the

## Autumn Application Strategy

JADAM recommends fertilizing in autumn like nature does, not in spring. Nature
makes it a rule to apply fertilizer on the surface and in raw form during
autumn. If adding organic materials, mix them together and scatter on the field
in autumn. Apply JMS so microbes can start feeding. Use a tiller at this stage
to speed up the process. After autumn, winter and spring pass, compost will be
fully prepared.

Another merit of autumn application is preventing larvae damage in spring
because there is no food for them in organic matter that has decomposed
completely through fall, winter and spring.

## JMS Preparation Method

To make JADAM microorganism solution, collect leaf mold soil from a local
mountain or forest. The darker and softer the soil, the better. Mix roughly 1
part leaf mold with 10 parts water in a container. Stir thoroughly and let sit
at [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] already present in the leaf mold. Fermentation occurs
at ambient temperature, not the high temperatures (75 C / 167 F) that some
systems claim are necessary.

## Monitoring Microbial Activity

After applying JMS, farmers can observe visible signs of microbial activity.
Strong application results in visible hyphae covering the soil surface. The
soil becomes softer and more crumbly. Earthworm activity increases. These are
all indicators that the microorganism population is thriving and actively
processing organic matter into plant-available nutrients.
