# Contaminant and Pesticide Safety in Cannabis

Cervantes and Dr. John McPartland emphasize that the most common
contaminants of marijuana are molds and pesticides. For medical cannabis
patients, these contaminants pose serious health risks. Optimal
cultivation eliminates the possibility of contamination, making prevention
the highest priority.

## Primary Contaminants

### Mold and Fungi

Mold is the most prevalent contaminant in cannabis. It can develop during
growth, harvesting, drying, and storage. Key mold risks include:

- **Bud mold (Botrytis)** - the most destructive mold, attacks dense
  flower buds especially in humid conditions
- **[[cannabis-root-rot-prevention-and-treatment]] fungus that flourishes in high temperatures and
  excessive moisture, the worst threat to clones and seedlings
