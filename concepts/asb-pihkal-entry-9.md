# ASB (Asymbescaline)

**[[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #9**

## Chemical Identity

- **Full name:** Asymbescaline; 3,4-diethoxy-5-methoxyphenethylamine
- **Class:** Phenethylamine ([[mescaline]] analogue with asymmetric
  diethoxy substitution)
- **Molecular formula:** C13H21NO3 (free base)
- **Hydrochloride salt:** white crystals, mp 142-143 °C
- **Boiling point:** 110-135 °C at 0.4 mm/Hg (free base)

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** 200-280 mg
- **Duration:** 10-15 hours

## Qualitative [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin|Effects]]

### At 240 mg
A pleasant and easy flow of day-dreaming thoughts, described as
friendly and somewhat erotic. A gentle drift to baseline by
midnight from a 9:00 AM start. Never quite reached a +++.
The experience was characterized as pleasant but ultimately
disappointing in depth.

### At 280 mg (Subject 1)
The plateau was evident by hour two but lacked the visual and
interpretive richness expected. Sleep was fitful even after
effects largely dropped. It was hard to relax guard even while
up and about the next day, with a residual plus one. Few if any
open interactions resembling 2C-B or [[lsd]] were noted, with some
negative side effects present.

### At 280 mg (Subject 2)
The experience was described as being in a corridor outside the
lighted halls where a mescaline experience was taking place,
sensing light from behind a grey door, unable to find the way
in from the dusky passageways. The compound was "sort of a
gentle sister of mescaline" but tended to emphasize the negative,
the sad, the struggling.

Sleep was impossible before the fifteenth hour. Hypnagogic
imagery included visions of moonlight in the desert with green
vampire-werewolf figures. During the peak, pulse was thready,
somewhat slowed, and uneven with a faint feeling of physical
weirdness. The subject had to sit quietly and wait until the
disturbing imagery settled back.

## Synthesis Notes

The synthesis is a complex multi-step sequence:

### Step 1: Ethylation
5-Bromobourbonal was ethylated with ethyl iodide (31 g) and KOH
(32 g, 85%) in DMF (150 mL), reflux 2 h, yielding 36 g of crude
3-bromo-4,5-diethoxybenzaldehyde as an amber liquid.

### Step 2: Imine formation
The bromoaldehyde was condensed with cyclohexylamine (17 mL) by
heating with open flame until water-free, then vacuum distilled
(135-145 °C at 0.4 mm/Hg) to give 42 g of
3-bromo-N-cyclohexyl-4,5-diethoxybenzylidenimine as a viscous
light greenish oil, mp 60-61 °C. This intermediate serves as a
branch point for compounds with sulfur (5-TASB, 3-T-TRIS) or
oxygen substitution at the 3-position.

### Step 3: Lithium-halogen exchange and borate
The imine was treated with butyllithium (36 mL, 1.5 M in hexane)
at dry ice temperature, followed by butyl borate (30 mL). After
returning to room temperature, the borate was oxidized with H2O2
(15 mL, 35%) in [[methanol]]. Acid workup gave the hydroxyaldehyde.

### Step 4: Methylation
3,4-Diethoxy-5-hydroxybenzaldehyde (8.3 g, mp 70.5-71.5 °C) was
methylated with methyl iodide (5 mL) and KOH (3.0 g) in EtOH
(75 mL) at room temperature for 5 days to give 8.2 g of the
final aldehyde as a pale yellow liquid.
