## Overview Chlorinated monoaromatic compounds and BTEX (benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, and xylene isomers) represent major classes of soil and groundwater contaminants. Fungi, particularly white-rot basidiomycetes, offer promising degradation capabilities through both ligninolytic and non-ligninolytic enzymatic pathways.
