## Biochemical weathering

Biochemical weathering operates through the excretion of metabolites that
chemically attack mineral structures. The two primary biochemical
mechanisms are proton-promoted dissolution and ligand-promoted dissolution.

Proton-promoted dissolution occurs when fungi excrete protons (H+),
lowering the pH at the hyphal-mineral interface. Acidification weakens
metal-oxygen bonds in silicate and oxide minerals, accelerating their
dissolution. Fungi generate protons through several metabolic pathways,
