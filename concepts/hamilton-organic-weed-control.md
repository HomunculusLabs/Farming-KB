## Overview

## Prevention Through Soil Management

### Organic Matter and Mulching
- Heavy applications of bulky organic matter (compost, manure) suppress weed germination by blocking light to the soil surface
- Mulch between plants during the [[green-cannabis-soil-flushing-nutrient-leaching]] is most rapid
- Green-manure crops (mustard, phacelia) on vacant ground prevent weed establishment
- In the ornamental garden, [[coleman-overwintered-onions-and-early-spring-crops]] onwards
- Hoe between ornamental plants throughout [[solomon-sheet-composting-mulch-gardening]]: use surplus compostable material directly on empty beds as a weed-suppressing mulch

### Perennial Weeds
- More challenging; require persistent removal of root systems
- Hand-pulling or digging out with as much root as possible
- Repeated cutting weakens perennial weeds over time
- For severe infestations, covering ground with black plastic for a full growing season can exhaust root reserves

### Lawn Weeds
- Maintain healthy turf through proper feeding, mowing, and watering — vigorous grass outcompetes weeds
- Rake with spring-tine rake in early spring to remove debris and thatch
- Feed lawns with blood, fish, and bone meal
- Reseed bare patches promptly

## Seasonal Weed Control Calendar

### Spring
- Hoe regularly as soil warms and weed seeds germinate
- Mulch between plants as they establish
- Hoe between ornamental plantings when preparing for annual planting
- Lightly loosen soil surface before planting

### Summer
- Continue hoeing; critical period as many weeds set seed
- Cut down perennial weeds near the garden
- Mulch to retain moisture and suppress weeds
- Sow vacant ground with green-manure cover crops
- Top off compost bins; start new bins if first is full

### Autumn
- Lightly fork over vacant ground in borders and mulch with compost or manure
