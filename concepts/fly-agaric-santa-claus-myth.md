## Fly-Agaric Santa Claus Myth and Siberian Shamanism

## The Popular Myth

The modern-day figure of Father Christmas, or Santa Claus, has been proposed by
psychedelic enthusiasts as an attenuated folk memory of Siberian fly-agaric
shamanism. In this reading, Santa's red-and-white costume represents the
parti-coloured mushroom, his nocturnal flight from the north on a
reindeer-driven sleigh represents the shamanic spirit-journey, and the
practice of entering through the smoke-hole in the ceiling explains why Santa
drops down the chimney. The gifts he bears represent presents from the
spirit-world.

This rather delightful story has gained a particular appeal among contemporary
members of the psychedelic underground, in exactly the same way that Alice in
Wonderland did for the sixties generation: the idea of a drug-taking shaman
lying behind the beloved Christmas figure so loved by adults and children alike
was irresistible.
