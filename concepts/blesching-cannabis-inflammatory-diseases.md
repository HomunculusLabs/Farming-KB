## Spices That May Aid Inflammatory Conditions

Blesching highlights several spices with anti-inflammatory properties that
may complement cannabinoid-based therapy:

### Cayenne

Cayenne stimulates peripheral circulation. In Cuba, topical tinctures and
creams are used to treat chronic aches and pains of lumbago, arthritis, and
rheumatism.

### Garlic

The garlic bulb is used in Cuba for the treatment of thrombophlebitis and
other inflammations, supporting cardiovascular health alongside
anti-inflammatory effects.

### Myrrh

A study from the University of Cincinnati confirmed myrrh's physiological
benefits for arthritis treatment, demonstrating that myrrh modulates
inflammatory responses.

### Nigella (Black Seed)

An experiment from Yuzuncu Yil University, Turkey, demonstrated that the
volatile oil of nigella can suppress artificially induced arthritis in rats.
Nigella has historically been used for arthritis and chronic inflammatory
conditions.
