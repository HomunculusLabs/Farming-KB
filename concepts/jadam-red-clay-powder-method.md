# Fine Red Clay Powder Method (JADAM Technique)

## Overview

Fine red clay powder is used in several JADAM pesticide recipes to enhance effectiveness. When added at 0.5 kg per 500 L of spray solution, it improves pesticide adhesion and coverage. For winter [[comparison-berry-growing-vs-fruit-tree-pruning]] spraying ([[jadam-fruit-tree-winter-pest-control]] (recipe #13):
- Slice 2 kg lump and dissolve in water
- Filter out any remaining large particles
- Use air pump/aerator to keep clay suspended during spraying

## Spraying with Red Clay

When spraying clay-enhanced pesticide:
- **Keep stirring continuously** while spraying, OR
- **Run an aerator/air pump** in the spray tank
- Clay particles settle quickly and will clog sprayer lines if not agitated
- Uneven distribution reduces effectiveness

## Why Red Clay Works

- Provides additional physical coating on pest bodies
- Enhances adhesion of herbal active compounds
- Contains minerals (iron oxide) that may have supplementary pesticidal properties
- Improves spray coverage on waxy or hairy plant surfaces
- Adds trace minerals as incidental foliar nutrition
