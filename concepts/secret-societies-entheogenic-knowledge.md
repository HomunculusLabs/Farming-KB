# Secret Societies and the Suppression of Entheogenic Knowledge

## Overview

Entheogenic knowledge — particularly regarding the [[freemasonry]] and many other secret societies throughout history. The systematic hoarding, suppression, and eventual destruction of this knowledge is presented as a primary mechanism by which religious, governmental, and corporate power structures have maintained control over humanity, replacing direct spiritual experience with dogmatic belief systems.

## The Nature of Secret Society Secrecy

### Hierarchical Knowledge Restriction

Secret societies operate on a principle of tiered knowledge access. Information about entheogenic substances exists within these organizations, but it is reserved for those who fit a certain profile at the highest levels. Lower-ranking members are permitted only to believe they possess the full truth. This structure creates what is described as "Fascist agenda societal morals and dogma of the big lie."

### Oaths of Secrecy

The binding nature of oaths prevents even willing initiates from discussing what they know. High Priests and Bishops (identified as Secret Society Initiates) have reportedly confirmed knowledge of entheogenic sacraments but refused to discuss them further due to vows of secrecy. The reaction described when a layman demonstrates knowledge of these secrets is one of shock and fear — not because the information is wrong, but because it threatens the deepest foundations of the initiate's spiritually conditioned identity.

### The Catch-22 of Revelation

The argument is made that revealing entheogenic secrets can only be done by a layman (non-member), because initiation at the relevant level prohibits further discussion. Secret societies are called "secret" precisely because they keep certain secrets, and the entheogenic rites are described as "so far up the totem pole" that those who know are genuinely frightened by anyone else possessing the information.

## The Pattern of Knowledge Destruction

### Acquisition, Hoarding, and Erasure

The operational pattern attributed to secret societies follows a consistent three-phase process:

1. **Acquisition** — gathering information, technology, and spiritual knowledge
2. **Hoarding** — keeping it exclusive to the initiated elite
3. **Destruction of public remnants** — systematically erasing all public traces so the knowledge cannot be independently rediscovered

This pattern is compared to Christians burning the libraries of the East — after pillaging what they wanted — in an attempt to erase knowledge and history from the public domain permanently.

### Historical Parallels

- **Library burnings** — the destruction of the great libraries of antiquity as a model for knowledge suppression
- **[[patriarchal-law-and-social-control]]. The King James version of the Bible, edited by **Sir Francis Bacon** (a 33rd degree Freemason), is described as specifically crafted to create social order through the implementation of a belief system aligned with Fascist ideologies.

### Ordo Ab Chao — Order Out of Chaos

The Masonic motto "Ordo Ab Chao" describes a methodology of social engineering:

1. **Agenda formulation** — designed to give the powerful more power
2. **Chaos creation** — crises are deliberately created or exploited
3. **Media manipulation** — public fear and confusion are amplified through media coverage
4. **Solution imposition** — laws and restrictions are passed that could never have been accepted without the preceding chaos
5. **Order achieved** — the pre-planned agenda is accomplished under the guise of solving the manufactured crisis

### Escalating Moral Legislation

The increasing preaching of "morality" by politicians is interpreted as evidence of this agenda in action — each moral panic creates justification for more restrictive laws that incrementally destroy personal freedom. The end goal is described as a "New World Order" that will force dogma and beliefs on the entire world.
