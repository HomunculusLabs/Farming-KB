# Amnesia Haze — Origins, Genetics, and Cultivation Profile

**Source:** S.T. Oner, *[[cannabis-sativa]] Vol. 3: The Essential Guide to the World's Finest Marijuana Strains*

---

## Strain Overview

Amnesia Haze is one of the most popular sativa-dominant [[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] in modern European cannabis culture. Bred by Royal Queen Seeds, Amnesia Haze has become a staple of Dutch coffee shops and a favorite among European growers and consumers. The strain is known for its potent cerebral effects that can leave even veteran smokers momentarily disconnected from their surroundings—hence the "Amnesia" name.

The strain's genetic background combines Southeast Asian, Jamaican, and Hawaiian landrace genetics with Afghan indica influences, creating a complex sativa-dominant hybrid that expresses both the soaring cerebral effects of its tropical sativa parents and the [[cannabis-resin-production-and-quality-traits-clarke]] growing vigor of its Afghan ancestor.

---

## Genetic Lineage and History

The exact genetic composition of Amnesia Haze involves multiple landrace and hybrid lines:

- **Southeast Asian genetics:** Contributing the potent, psychedelic sativa effects typical of Thai, Laotian, and Cambodian varieties
- **Jamaican genetics:** Adding tropical fruit flavors and the energetic, euphoric qualities associated with Caribbean sativas
- **Hawaiian genetics:** Providing additional tropical terpene complexity and the characteristic Hawaiian "brightness"
- **Afghan indica:** Contributing resin density, flowering speed, and structural stability

This multi-continental genetic heritage makes Amnesia Haze a true world hybrid, combining some of the most celebrated [[cannabis-genetics-and-breeding]] from four distinct geographic regions. The Afghan indica influence is subtle but important—it provides enough structural stability and flowering speed to make the strain manageable for indoor cultivation, something that pure tropical sativas often struggle with.

---

## Growth Characteristics

Despite its sativa-dominant genetics, Amnesia Haze is described as well-suited to indoor cultivation and accessible to growers with moderate experience. The plant does not present the extreme height and flowering-time challenges of pure tropical sativas, thanks to the Afghan indica influence that tames some of the more difficult sativa growth traits.

Key cultivation characteristics include:

- **Container size:** Recommended 2-gallon pots for indoor cultivation
- **Plant density:** Up to 12 plants per square yard of grow space
- **Pruning:** Recommended to prune at the 5th internode for optimal structure
- **Vegetative period:** Approximately 3 weeks before switching to flowering
- **Outdoor height:** Can easily exceed 6 feet when grown outdoors

The strain's adaptability to high-density indoor cultivation makes it popular among commercial growers in Europe, where space constraints and the desire for multiple harvests per year drive cultivation decisions.

---

## Flowering Time and Yield

Amnesia Haze has a moderate-to-long flowering period of approximately 65 days when grown indoors under forced flowering conditions. This places it in a middle ground—longer than indica-heavy hybrids but significantly shorter than the 12 to 16 weeks required by pure Haze or tropical sativa varieties.

For outdoor growers, the strain is typically ready for harvest around the first two weeks of October in the Northern Hemisphere. This mid-October finish makes it suitable for Mediterranean and warm [[fukuoka-natural-farming-temperate-climates]] but risky in regions prone to early autumn frost or persistent autumn rainfall.

Yield figures from the breeder indicate:

- **Indoor:** Approximately 450 grams per square yard of grow space
- **Outdoor:** Approximately 450 grams per individual plant

These yields are described as moderate to good, reflecting the strain's balance between sativa flowering behavior and the productivity enhancements from its Afghan lineage.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

Amnesia Haze is noted for its light, fruity flavor that contrasts with the heavy, pungent profiles common to many Kush and Diesel varieties. The breeder specifically recommends a minimum 2-week curing period to fully develop the flavor, noting that the fresh, fruity smoke with tangy sweetness emerges best after proper curing.

The flavor profile includes:

- **Primary:** Fresh, fruity notes reminiscent of tropical citrus and berries
- **Secondary:** Tangy sweetness with floral undertones
- **Tertiary:** Subtle earthy notes from the Afghan parent

The light, accessible flavor makes Amnesia Haze appealing to a broad range of consumers, including those who find heavy, diesel-flavored strains overpowering. The fruity character is particularly well-suited to vaporization, where the nuanced terpene profile can be fully appreciated.

---

## Effects and Experience

The effects of Amnesia Haze are where the strain truly earns its name. The high is described as intensely cerebral, capable of leaving users "unaware of your surroundings and incapable of remembering your own name." This description, while humorous, accurately conveys the potency of the strain's psychoactive effects.

The effect profile includes:

- Powerful cerebral rush immediately upon onset
- Euphoric, uplifting mental state with enhanced [[plant-sensory-perception]]
- Potential for short-term memory impairment at higher doses
- Creative and introspective thought patterns
- Duration that extends for several hours

Oner notes that even veteran smokers will find that a full joint or a large bong hit of Amnesia Haze produces a profoundly disorienting experience. The strain is not recommended for novice consumers or situations requiring mental clarity and focus.
