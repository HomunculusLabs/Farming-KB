## Overview

Endogenous opioids are naturally occurring pain-blocking molecules produced by
the human body. Five distinct groups have been identified: beta-endorphins,
enkephalins, dynorphins, endomorphins, and nociceptin. These molecules bind to
opiate receptors throughout the central nervous system (CNS) and pituitary
gland, reducing stress and pain perception while influencing emotion formation,
interpersonal relationships, and hunger states.
