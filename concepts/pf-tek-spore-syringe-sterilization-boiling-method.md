# PF TEK Spore Syringe Sterilization and Preparation Technique

## Overview

The spore syringe is the foundational inoculation tool in the PF TEK cultivation
method developed by Psilocybe Fanaticus. A spore syringe is a sterile syringe
containing a suspension of mushroom spores in water, which is used to introduce
fungal spores into [[mushroom-spore-printing-syringe-preparation-pf-tek]]:

1. **Spore print in jar**: A mushroom spore print deposited on a sterile surface
   inside a glass jar with a modified lid
2. **Sterile syringe with water**: For injecting sterile water into the spore
   print jar to hydrate and suspend the spores
3. **Sterile syringe for collection**: For drawing the spore solution out of the
   jar after the spores have been suspended
4. **Small Pyrex glass stirring rod**: A lab-grade glass rod for scraping spores
   from the print surface into the water
5. **Alcohol, tequila shot glass, and eye dropper**: For [[flammability-hazard-alcohol-lysol-flame-sterilization-mycology]] of
   the glass rod and general hygiene purposes
6. **Modified jar lid**: A lid drilled with two holes — one in the center (to fit
   the Pyrex stirring rod) and one near the edge (to fit a syringe needle)

## Jar Lid Preparation

The jar lid is a critical component of the [[spore-syringe-preparation]] apparatus.
The PF TEK specifies the following lid design:

- A center hole drilled to fit the Pyrex glass stirring rod, allowing it to be
  inserted into the jar while maintaining a reasonable seal
- A second hole punched near the edge of the lid (rubberized edge facing up) to
  accommodate a syringe needle

The purpose of the two-hole lid design is to enable manipulation of the spore
print within the jar while minimizing exposure to ambient air. The holes should
be protected by tin foil or sterile surgical tape both before and after the
procedure to maintain sterility.

## Boiling Water Sterilization Method

The PF TEK describes a boiling water method for sterilizing syringes, which is
an accessible alternative to autoclaving or pressure cooking for this purpose.
The procedure is as follows:

1. Boil a pot of water on a stovetop
2. Draw boiling water into a syringe and squirt it out several times, rinsing
   the interior
3. Refill the syringe with boiling water
4. Replace the needle guard (cover) on the syringe
5. Wrap the syringe in tin foil
6. Prepare several syringes using this method
7. Drop the foil-wrapped syringes into the boiling water
8. Boil the syringes for one hour
9. Allow the syringes to cool before using

This boiling sterilization method, while less rigorous than autoclaving, was
considered adequate by Psilocybe Fanaticus for home cultivation purposes. The
extended one-hour boil ensures sufficient heat penetration to kill most
contaminant organisms. The tin foil wrapping prevents contamination of the
syringe exterior during the boiling process.

## Spore Solution Preparation Procedure

Once the syringes are sterilized and the apparatus is prepared, the actual spore
solution preparation follows these steps:

**Step 1 — Hydration**: Inject sterile water into the spore print jar through
the needle hole in the lid. The water hydrates the dried spores on the print
surface, loosening them from the substrate.

**Step 2 — Spore scraping**: Flame sterilize the Pyrex glass stirring rod
using alcohol, and allow it to cool for approximately one minute. Insert the
rod through the center hole in the lid. Using the rod end, gently scrape the
spores from the print surface into the water, creating a spore suspension.
