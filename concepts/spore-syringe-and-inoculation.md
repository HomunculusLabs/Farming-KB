## Inoculation Techniques

### Grain Jar Inoculation

The most common inoculation method for grain spawn. Working in a still-air box
or under a laminar flow hood:

1. Shake the spore syringe vigorously to distribute spores evenly throughout
   the suspension.
2. Flame-sterilize the needle tip until it glows red-orange, then allow it to
   cool for 10–15 seconds (touching a sterile surface briefly to cool without
   contaminating).
3. Insert the needle through the jar's inoculation port (self-healing silicone
   port or micropore tape covering a drilled hole).
4. Inject 1–2 cc of spore solution per quart jar, aiming to distribute drops
   across the grain surface for multiple colonization points.
5. Remove the needle and flame-sterilize the tip again before proceeding to the
   next jar or capping the syringe.
