## Fenitrothion and Fenitrooxon Degradation

Fenitrothion and fenitrooxon are degraded enzymatically by Trichoderma
viride in the presence of alternative nutrients. The presence of
alternative nutrients appears to support fungal growth and enzyme
production, thereby enhancing organophosphate degradation capacity. This
nutrient-dependent enhancement suggests that biostimulation strategies
could be combined with bioaugmentation for improved field remediation of
organophosphate-contaminated soils. The enzyme systems responsible for
fenitrothion degradation by T. viride include both hydrolases and
oxidative enzymes that act on different moieties of the molecule.
