# Seed Quality and Selection (Solomon)

Updated: 2026-04-18

Source: Gardening When It Counts, [[cervantes-seed-production-storage]] fields
- Do not hire skilled plant breeders to maintain pure lines
- Do not patrol surrounding areas to prevent cross-pollination
- Do not hand-select the best plants for seed production
- Let the field grow unsupervised, harvest, and re-use seed from the same bag year after year

Each generation grown this way becomes increasingly variable and less productive. If a variety degrades too badly, the cheap producer simply buys a small quantity of expensive commercial-quality seed of a similar-looking variety and uses it to start their production field — then mislabels the harvest with the heirloom name.

This is why many "heirlooms" from cheap producers bear no resemblance to the original variety.
