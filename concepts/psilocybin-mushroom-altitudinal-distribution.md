# Psilocybin Mushroom Altitudinal Distribution

The elevational range and altitudinal patterns of psilocybin-containing mushroom species, from sea level to high alpine habitats above 4,000 meters.

## Mid-Elevation Subtropical and Temperate (500-2,500 m)

Mexico City stands at 2,220 meters altitude where Copelandia cyanescens grows in disturbed zones, showing that even tropical species can adapt to high-altitude urban environments.

The majority of Mexico's 44 neurotropic Psilocybe species grow in mesophytic forests, many at mid-elevations.

In Mexico, 34 of 42 reported neurotropic Psilocybe species are from mesophytic forests found at intermediate elevations.

The mesophytic forests represent a conjugation zone where both northern and southern mycobiotas meet, creating extraordinary species diversity.

## High Elevation and Alpine (2,500-4,000 m)

Psilocybe aztecorum var. aztecorum grows exclusively in subalpine and alpine habitats of the high mountains of Mexico, reaching approximately 4,000 meters elevation.

This is one of the highest-elevation psilocybin mushroom species known.
