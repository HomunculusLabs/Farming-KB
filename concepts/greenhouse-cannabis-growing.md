## Growing in Ground vs. Containers

Planting directly in the earth floor of the greenhouse allows organic methods and bigger
plants with less maintenance. Container-grown plants are portable but require more
attention. Growing in the ground is always better than pots for plant size and health,
but plants cannot be moved easily.

## Covering Materials

The best greenhouse films are UV-resistant and transmit plenty of light. Lexan
(polycarbonate) is rigid, thermally insulating, and transmits almost as much light as
glass while retaining heat. Glass provides excellent light transmission but is heavy,
expensive, and breakable. Polyethylene film is economical and easy to install but must be
replaced periodically. Vinyl plastic is a flexible film that installs easily against wet
glass inside the structure and is reusable.

### Heating Systems

Cannabis grows well with night temperatures of 60-65 degrees F (16-18 degrees C), but
colder nights require additional heat. Small greenhouses can be heated with an electric
space heater. Larger units may use forced air, coal, natural gas hot-water, or steam
systems. Heat cables provide 10-15 watts per square foot of growing area and are useful
for warming seedlings and clones without heating the entire structure. A low-tech method
uses compost stacked on the sides of the greenhouse to a height of about five feet; as
it decomposes, it gives off heat.

### Evaporative Cooling

Evaporative cooling eliminates excess heat and adds humidity. Properly installed, a
cooler can reduce interior temperature as much as 30-40 degrees F in hot, dry climates.
The cooler must be installed on the outside of the greenhouse; otherwise, it simply
humidifies without dropping the temperature. Size the cooler to equal the total cubic
space of the structure plus 50 percent.

## Security

Security is always a concern. Growers camouflage greenhouses by growing other plants
alongside cannabis, painting walls with sun-blocking paint, or attaching artificial
flowers to visible branches. Low-profile structures are less conspicuous. Building the
greenhouse yourself will be cheaper and more secure than hiring a contractor.
