# Pressing Hash - Methods and Techniques

Once collected, resin powder is often pressed to facilitate
[[coleman-post-harvest-handling-and-storage]]. Bulky resin powder is awkward to handle;
it is easily spilled, blown away, and contaminated by dust and
dirt. Resin powder is also more difficult to smoke, especially
if no screen is available. Once pressed into a piece of hash,
the resin is easy to handle, store, transport, and consume.
Proper pressing is essential for storage and to slow
[[decomposition]].

## What Pressing Does

Pressing ruptures [[greg-green-trichomes-and-resin-glands]] and warms the resin, causing
many volatile aromatic terpenoids to release their aromas and
flavors. The pressure exerted to form a cohesive block is
contingent upon the volume of contaminants in the powder. The
more pure the resin powder, the less pressure it takes to form
it into a block.

When pressed, heat and friction cause the outer layer of
pressed hash to oxidize and turn darker than the interior. Hash
can have a dark exterior and a blonde interior packed with
creamy resin glands. Potent resin powder is a creamy white to
gold in color. Pressing and working it in your hands ruptures
and oxidizes resin glands, making the mass turn darker.

## Hand Pressing

By hand pressing, you experience the transformation of resin
powder into your very own piece of fragrant, dense hash.

1. Collect one to four grams of resin powder in the palm of
   your hand.
2. Apply pressure, working the powder between your palms.
3. Push your thumb into your palm full of resin powder to work
   it into a piece of hash.
4. Continue this process for 10-30 minutes until the piece is
   completely pliable and whole.
5. Heat will be generated and help rupture and meld the resin
   glands together.

Relatively pure resin powder will congeal faster than
less-potent powders that contain impurities. A little vegetable
matter and debris gives hash different flavors and more body.

## Bottle Pressing

Press small amounts of resin powder between cellophane to form
a pancake. Fill a long cylindrical bottle with warm water and
use it as a rolling pin to press the pancake of hash into an
even wafer that will burn evenly.

## Shoe Hash

Press small amounts of hash by putting resin powder in
cellophane and placing it under your heel inside your shoe.
Walk around for an hour or so, doing what you would normally.
Take your shoe off to find freshly pressed hash. This method
uses body heat and constant pressure to form a cohesive piece.

## Laminator Pressing

A photo laminator can press hash. Sprinkle the best resin
powder onto a piece of cellophane, lay another piece on top,
and roll with a bottle full of hot water to get preliminary
shape. Remove from cellophane and laminate it like a document.
If the laminator can control heat level, even better results
are possible.

## Mechanical Hydraulic Pressing

Place resin powder in a plastic bag or cellophane wrapper to
contain it. Poke a few small holes in the bag before pressing
to allow trapped air to escape. The bag goes into a
heavy-duty steel mold, and pressure is applied with a hydraulic
jack.
