## Straw Mulch as Weed Control

The spread of threshed straw across the field after harvest serves multiple purposes:

- Physically blocks light from reaching weed seeds, preventing germination
- Retains soil moisture, favoring crop seedlings over weed seeds
- Provides organic matter as it decomposes, feeding soil microbiology
- Creates habitat for ground beetles and other weed-seed predators
