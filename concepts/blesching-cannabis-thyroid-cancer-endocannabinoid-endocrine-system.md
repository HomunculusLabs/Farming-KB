# Thyroid Cancer and the Endocannabinoid System

## Thyroid Gland Anatomy and Function

The thyroid (Greek for "shield") is an integral part of the human endocrine
system. Its butterfly-shaped structure embraces the front of the Adam's apple
in both males and females. The thyroid produces thyroid hormones, primarily
triiodothyronine (T3) and thyroxine (T4), which are intricately involved in:

- Physiological development and growth
- Cellular metabolism regulation
- Enhancement and extension of catecholamine effects (alertness and fight-or-flight
  responses)

The thyroid produces T3 and T4 by utilizing dietary iodine. Its affinity for
iodine also makes it vulnerable to ill-effects from iodine isotopes such as
iodine-131, released during nuclear accidents and explosions.
