## Phylogenetic Diversity and Taxonomic Placement
Rock-inhabiting fungi are polyphyletic, arising from multiple
evolutionary lineages rather than a single common ancestor.
Molecular phylogenetic studies place them across several orders
within the Ascomycota, particularly Dothideomycetes and
Eurotiomycetes. The black yeast genus Knufia and the
meristematic genus Coniosporium are frequently encountered in
rock-colonizing surveys worldwide. Despite their phylogenetic
diversity, these fungi converge on remarkably similar
morphological and physiological strategies, making them a
textbook example of convergent evolution in response to
environmental stress.
