## Nitrogen Saturation Threshold

In N-limited ecosystems, additional nitrogen acts as a fertilizer and
increases plant growth (McNulty and Aber, 1993). As N loading continues,
ecosystems progress through stages of nitrogen saturation. Initially,
added N is retained in plant and [[legume-nitrogen-dynamics]] through several mechanisms. Saprotrophic
fungi decompose [[edaphic-determinants-cannabis-microbiome-community-structure]] and activity under N deposition alter
soil respiration patterns. When N deposition stimulates saprotrophic
fungal activity on recalcitrant litter, as shown by Ruhling and Tyler
(1991), increased decomposition rates can transiently increase CO2
release. However, the net effect on carbon sequestration depends on
the balance between enhanced decomposition and increased primary
production under N fertilization.
