## Prevention

Prevention is far more effective than treatment once the disease
takes hold. Key preventive measures include:

- Increase air circulation and ventilation throughout the grow
  space
- Ensure light intensity is high and consistent
- Space containers far enough apart so air freely flows between
  plants
- Allow foliage to dry before turning off lights at night
- Avoid excess nitrogen that produces soft, susceptible tissue
- Keep [[pf-tek-low-humidity-symptoms-and-remediation]],
good ventilation, and healthy plants.
