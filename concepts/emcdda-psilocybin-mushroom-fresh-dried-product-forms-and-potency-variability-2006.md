## Route of Administration

Mushrooms were typically chopped and ingested directly, or brewed into a tea.
Chewing and swallowing fresh or dried mushroom material was the most common
method. Brewing into tea was reported as an alternative that could reduce
nausea for some users, though it introduced additional variability in the
extraction efficiency of active compounds. The onset of effects typically
occurred within 20-60 minutes following ingestion, with the experience
lasting approximately 4-6 hours.

## Market Implications of Potency Variability

The unpredictable potency of mushroom products had significant implications
for both public health and market dynamics. The EMCDDA noted that users could
not reliably predict the strength of their experience based on the weight
consumed or the variety name of the product. This unpredictability
contributed to both under-dosing (resulting in disappointment and potential
re-dosing at inappropriate intervals) and over-dosing (resulting in
intensified or distressing [[hallucinogenic-mushroom-acute-psychological-effects-and-bad-trips]]).

The lack of standardized dosing information on retail products was identified
as a risk factor. Some retailers provided general dosage guidelines, but
these were necessarily approximate given the inherent variability of the
products. The EMCDDA case study highlighted this as an area where improved
consumer information and harm reduction messaging could potentially reduce
adverse outcomes.
