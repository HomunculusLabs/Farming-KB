# Electricity Consumption and Cost Management

Managing electricity consumption is a critical aspect of [[query-how-to-set-up-an-indoor-cannabis-grow-on-a-budget]]
cultivation. It affects both the economics of the operation and
personal security. This page covers cost calculation, consumption
[[weed-management-strategies]], and security considerations. See also
[[cervantes-grow-room-security-law-enforcement]].

## Legal Considerations

It is not a crime to use electricity that has been legally purchased.
No sensible judge would issue a search warrant on the basis of
suspicious electricity consumption. However, not all judges are
sensible, and small communities with bored police officers or special
marijuana task forces often investigate whatever information they are
able to obtain from electric company employees.

Larger police forces do not have the desire, time, or money to look
for small-time marijuana growers. Electric company employees may share
consumption data and personal information fairly freely. If a simple
question and a smile can reveal years of consumption history and
personal details, imagine what enforcement officials can do with
intimidation.

## Understanding Your Electric Bill

Electric bills are controlled with and generated by a computer system.
Monthly energy consumption is often displayed on a bar graph for the
previous 12 months. This graph makes it easy to see fluctuations in
electricity consumption.

The amount of electricity consumption and the size of the home are
proportional. Often, an increase in electric consumption is normal.
For example, electric bills always increase if there is a baby in the
home or if there are more residents living there.

## Strategies to Reduce Consumption

### Switch Heating Sources
One grower moved into a home with all-electric heat. He installed three
HID lamps in the basement that generated heat, dispersed via a vent fan
attached to a thermostat/humidistat. He turned off the electric heat
and started heating with wood. Even running three lamps at three
kilowatts per hour, the electric bill was less than it had been with
electric heat.

### Optimize Water Heating
Some friends bought a new, efficient water heater and saved $17 per
month, enabling them to add another 600-watt lamp. Another grower set
her water heater to 130 degrees F (54 degrees C) instead of 170 degrees
F (77 degrees C), saving about 25 kWh per month. Do not set water
heaters below 130 degrees F, as harmful bacteria can grow below this
safe point.

### Switch to Gas Appliances
Changing to gas or wood heat and a gas stove and water heater will
lower the electricity bill significantly, freeing capacity for grow
room lighting.
