# Wind Protection for Outdoor Cannabis

Wind is one of the strongest forces affecting
outdoor cannabis gardens. Sustained wind will
suck moisture from plants, causing them to draw
water from roots and shed it through leaves in a
defensive mechanism to regulate internal
temperature and chemistry. Without proper
protection, wind can devastate crops through
moisture loss, physical damage, and temperature
stress.

## How Wind Affects Cannabis

Cannabis virtually stops growing at 85F (29C),
and hot winds compound this stress by increasing
transpiration rates beyond what the root system
can supply. Moderate sustained winds will dry out
container- and field-grown crops within a few
hours. Container crops suffer the most because
their limited soil volume cannot compensate for
rapid moisture loss.

Plants grown in five-gallon (20 L) containers on
a terrace that receives full sun and constant
moderate winds use about two gallons (7.5 L) of
water daily. Indoors, the same plant would use 75
percent less water. This dramatic difference
illustrates the enormous impact wind has on plant
water needs.
