# Psilocybin Mushroom Effects and Safety

Pharmacological effects, dosage guidelines, toxicity profile, and safety considerations for psilocybin-containing mushrooms. Based on [[jochen-gartz]]'s comprehensive survey and clinical literature. See also [[psilocybin-mushroom-chemistry]] for chemical composition and [[psychedelic-mushroom-history]] for historical context.

## Active Compounds

### Psilocybin (O-phosphoryl-4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine)

- **Molecular weight:** 284.2 g/mol
- **Discovery:** Isolated by [[albert-hofmann]] in 1958 at Sandoz Laboratories
- **Mechanism:** Prodrug — dephosphorylated by alkaline phosphatase enzymes to psilocin after ingestion
- **Receptor activity:** 5-HT2A receptor agonist (primary), also active at 5-HT1A, 5-HT2C, and other serotonin receptors
- **Onset:** 20-60 minutes after ingestion
- **Duration:** 4-6 hours
- **Half-life:** Approximately 2-3 hours for psilocin

### Psilocin (4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine; 4-HO-DMT)

- The active metabolite of psilocybin
- Chemically similar to DMT (4-HO-DMT vs. N,N-DMT)
- Also present in fresh mushrooms in small amounts
- Unstable; oxidizes rapidly (responsible for bluing reaction in mushrooms)

### Baeocystin (4-phosphoryloxy-N-methyltryptamine)

- Present in most psilocybin-containing species in smaller quantities
- Pharmacological activity less well characterized
- May contribute to overall effects; some researchers suggest synergistic role
- Named after Psilocybe baeocystis

### Norbaeocystin (4-phosphoryloxytryptamine)

- Minor constituent in many species
- Pharmacological significance unclear
- May be an intermediate in the psilocybin biosynthetic pathway

### Aeruginascin (4-phosphoryloxy-N,N,N-trimethyltryptamine)

- Discovered by [[jochen-gartz]] in Inocybe aeruginascens
- Unique quaternary ammonium compound not found in other genera
- Gartz hypothesized it may modify the psychedelic experience (see below)

## Dosage Guidelines

Dosage varies enormously depending on species potency, individual sensitivity, set and setting. All values below are approximate for dried P. cubensis (0.5-1.0% total alkaloids):

| Level | Dried Weight | Fresh Equivalent | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 0.25-0.5g | 2.5-5g | Subtle mood enhancement, mild sensory changes |
| Light | 0.5-1.5g | 5-15g | Enhanced colors, pattern recognition, mild visual effects |
| Moderate | 1.5-3.5g | 15-35g | Clear visual hallucinations, altered time perception, introspection |
| Strong | 3.5-5g | 35-50g | Intense visuals, ego dissolution possible, mystical experiences |
| Heroic | 5g+ | 50g+ | Complete ego dissolution, visionary states, strong catharsis |

For more potent species (P. cyanescens, P. azurescens, P. semilanceata), reduce dosage by 50-75%. P. azurescens is roughly 3-4x more potent than P. cubensis by dry weight.

## Subjective Effects

### Common Effects (Positive)

- Visual hallucinations: geometric patterns, color enhancement, trailing, morphing objects
- Synesthesia: "Seeing" sounds, "hearing" colors
- Altered time perception: time dilation or compression
- Enhanced introspection and self-reflection
- Feelings of connectedness and unity
- Enhanced appreciation of music and art
- Euphoria and well-being
- Spiritual or mystical experiences (especially at higher doses)
- Enhanced creativity and novel thinking patterns

### Common Effects (Challenging)

- Anxiety and panic (especially at high doses or in unfamiliar settings)
- Nausea (common in first 30-60 minutes)
- Confusion and difficulty processing information
- Paranoia and fear of losing control
- Difficulty distinguishing internal from external experiences
- Emotional catharsis (can be both positive and negative)

### After-Effects

- Residual visual phenomena ("visual snow") for hours to days after
- Integration period: new perspectives on personal issues, relationships, life direction
- Improved mood that may persist for days to weeks
- Possible fatigue the following day
- Flashback phenomena (extremely rare at recreational doses)

### Dependence and Withdrawal (EMCDDA)
- **No psychological or physical dependency** occurs with psilocybin mushrooms
- **No withdrawal symptoms** reported
- Tendency for experimental use only; most users discontinue after a few experiences
- Users report mushrooms lack the positive sociable (entactogenic) effects of ecstasy, which may limit appeal of regular use

### Acute Health Incidents (EMCDDA Europe Data, 2003-2005)
- Medical assistance-seeking rates are very low across EU
- Czech Republic: 4 cases (2003), 10 cases (2004)
- Poland: 2 cases (2003), 3 cases (2004) at one toxicological center
- Slovenia: 2 cases (2005)
- Sweden: 30-40 calls annually to Poisons Information Centre (stable)
- One confirmed death attributed directly to mushroom ingestion (France)
- One death with mushrooms detected in autopsy (Czech Republic, 2004 — suicide)
- UK fatality: 31-year-old leapt from tower block after consuming Hawaiian mushrooms + alcohol (2004)
- Ireland fatality: 33-year-old fell from fourth floor after consuming mushrooms (2005)

### Emergency Treatment (EMCDDA)
- Benzodiazepines reported as safest medication of choice, effective for most patients
- First aid focuses on reassuring, preventing self-harm, and assisting to medical unit
- Diagnosis difficult unless ingestion history available from user/friends
- No systematic research on chronic toxicity; no evidence of mutagenicity or teratogenicity to date
- No irreversible organ damage from psilocybin reported
