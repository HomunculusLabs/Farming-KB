## The Bushman's Cheerful Face

Back a couple of years ago, some of the major television networks in Japan ran a number of shows and features on the Bushmen of southern Africa. Fukuoka was struck by the Bushman himself, who is fine with a dazzlingly bright, cheerful face. Civilized society may mock and make fun of him, but all that is of no concern to him. This is the face of a natural man able to immerse himself in his own joy.

But what bothered Fukuoka was the environment in which the Bushman lives. He saw the Bushman digging up some kind of root with a stick. He had to go quite a ways to find that root and also to fetch firewood. That means that nature is scarce there.

## The Bushman's Environment Is Unnatural

The nature in the background of the Bushman's life is just too poor. Fukuoka would say that this is an unnatural existence. In other words, the balance of the Bushmen and the animals and plants with nature has been destroyed.
