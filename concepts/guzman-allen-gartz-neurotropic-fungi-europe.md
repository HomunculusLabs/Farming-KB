America and Europe, suggesting either ancient shared ancestry or
relatively recent dispersal across the Atlantic.

Species restricted to Europe include Psilocybe serbica, known
primarily from Central Europe, and Psilocybe bohemica, first
described from Czechoslovakia. The taxonomic status of P. bohemica
has been contentious, with Krieglsteiner (1984, 1986) considering it
a synonym of P. cyanescens, while other European authors have
maintained it as a distinct species. Psilocybe coprinifacies, another
European species, has a complex nomenclatural history with multiple
misidentifications and synonyms.
