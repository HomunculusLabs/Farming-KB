## Cannabis: Historical Use, Chemistry, and Effects

Cannabis, undoubtedly the most widespread of all the hallucinogens, is one of
the oldest cultivated plants. It is also one of the most widely spread weeds,
having escaped cultivation, appearing as an adventitious plant everywhere,
except in the polar regions and the wet, forested tropics. Also called
marihuana, hasheesh, hemp, kif, bhang, or charas, Cannabis is the source of
hemp fiber, an edible fruit, an industrial oil, a medicine, and a narcotic.
Despite its great age and its economic importance, the plant is still poorly
understood.

## Botany and Classification

Cannabis is a rank, weedy annual that is extremely variable and may attain a
height of 18 feet. Flourishing best in disturbed, nitrogen-rich soils near
human habitations, it has been called a "camp follower," going with man into
new areas. It is normally dioecious; the male or staminate plant is usually
weaker than the female or pistillate plant. The intoxicating constituents are
normally concentrated in a resin in the developing female flowers and
adjacent leaves and stems.
