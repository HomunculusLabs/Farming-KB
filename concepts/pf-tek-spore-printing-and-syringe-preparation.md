### Storage

Store the spore print jar at room temperature in a dark place away from sunlight. Do not store in a refrigerator, as condensation can promote contamination or degrade the spores.

## Spore Syringe Preparation

### Equipment and Materials

1. [[spore-print]] jar (prepared as described above)
2. Sterile syringe with water for injecting into the spore print jar
3. Sterile syringe for loading spore solution out of the jar
4. Small Pyrex glass stirring rod (scientific and lab supply)
5. Alcohol, tequila shot glass, and eye dropper
6. Custom lid with two holes: one centre hole for the stirring rod, one edge hole for the syringe needle

### Syringe Sterilisation

1. Boil a pot of water
2. Draw boiling water into each syringe and squirt it out several times
3. Refill the syringes with boiling water, replace the needle guard, and wrap in tin foil
4. Drop the syringes into boiling water and boil for one hour
5. Allow to cool before use

### Lid Preparation

The custom two-hole lid is prepared by:

1. Drilling a centre hole to fit the Pyrex glass stirring rod
2. Punching a second hole near the rubberised edge to fit a syringe needle
3. Keeping the rubberised edge facing upward

### Spore Harvesting into Syringe

1. Inject sterile water into the spore print jar through the needle hole
2. Flame sterilise the glass stirring rod, allow to cool for a minute, then insert through the centre lid hole
3. Using the rod end, scrape spores into the water
