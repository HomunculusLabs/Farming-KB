## Stable Isotope Fundamentals

### What Are Natural Abundance Isotopes?

Most elements exist as mixtures of isotopes — atoms with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons. The "heavy" isotopes ¹⁵N and ¹³C occur naturally at low abundances:

- ¹⁵N comprises approximately 0.366% of total nitrogen
- ¹³C comprises approximately 1.1% of total carbon

Isotope ratios are expressed in delta (δ) notation as parts per thousand (‰) deviation from a standard:

- δ¹⁵N values are reported relative to atmospheric N₂
- δ¹³C values are reported relative to the Pee Dee Belemnite (PDB) standard
