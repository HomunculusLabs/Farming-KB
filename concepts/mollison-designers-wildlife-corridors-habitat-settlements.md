## Habitat Patches Within Settlements

Mollison advocates setting aside dedicated habitat patches within
settlements at multiple scales:

- Road verges planted with native species instead of lawn
- Undeveloped rocky outcrops, gullies, and steep slopes left wild
- Cluster housing layouts that preserve central commons as habitat
- Rooftop gardens and green walls providing vertical habitat in urban areas
- Stormwater retention areas designed as wetland habitat

These patches function as stepping stones that allow wildlife to move
through developed areas. When connected by corridors, even small patches
become part of a functional ecological network.
