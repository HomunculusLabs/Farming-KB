## Growing Chicories - Endive Escarole and Radicchio

Updated: 2026-04-18

Chicories (Cichorium endivia and Cichorium intybus) are remarkably cold-hardy salad greens that Solomon considers underrated for [[cervantes-hybrid-varieties-types]] are available but expensive. Most open-pollinated varieties are also fine.

## Pests and Diseases

In maritime climates, chicories tend to develop mold in winter. The heads may rot back to stumps. In real cold, they will rot back to stumps in winter. In milder, dryer years they grow steadily right through winter and into spring, going to seed with higher leaf production. They shrug off nighttime lows under 10 degrees F (-12 degrees C).

## Overwintering in Cold Climates

In climates where the soil does freeze:

1. Dig up heads a week or two before the soil starts freezing.
2. Remove their carrot-like roots with soil attached.
3. Transplant them into tubs or beds in a [[winter-gardening-season-extension]]
