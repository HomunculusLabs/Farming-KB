## Distribution and Natural Habitat

asexual spores, effecting a simultaneous "spore mass" inoculation.
Colonization is comparatively fast. If the fermentation is continued
for 5-7 days, asexual conidia form, facilitating the direct inoculation
of bulk substrates.

## Mycelial Characteristics

Initially white, racing linearly, soon fluffy white and aerial. As the
mycelium matures, distinctive black droplets (coremia) form and radiate
outward from the center of the culture. These coremia produce asexual
conidia (spores) that appear as black liquid droplets.

## Fragrance Signature

Musty, farinaceous, not pleasant, not anise-like.
