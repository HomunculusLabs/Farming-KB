## Overview of Neurodegenerative Diseases

Neurodegenerative diseases encompass a range of conditions characterized by
progressive loss of structure or function of neurons, including death of
neurons. Among the most prominent are Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's
disease, HIV-associated dementia, and the effects of stroke.

These conditions represent some of the most challenging areas of medicine,
with orthodox approaches offering limited treatment options.
