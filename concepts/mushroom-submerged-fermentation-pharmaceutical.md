### Compounds from Mycelial Biomass
Mycelial biomass harvested from fermenters provides:
- **KS-2** — α-mannan peptide from L. edodes mycelium (containing serine, threonine, alanine, proline)
- **LEM** — glycoprotein from L. edodes mycelium (glucose, galactose, xylose, arabinose, mannose, fructose + nucleic acid derivatives, vitamin B, ergosterol)
- **LAP** — from L. edodes culture media filtrate
- **Proflamin** — glycoprotein (MW 16,000, 90% protein, 10% saccharide) from Flammulina velutipes mycelium
- **ATOM** — glucomannan-protein from Agaricus blazei mycelium

### Exopolysaccharide Extraction
Culture broth exopolysaccharides are extracted by:
1. Adding 96% ethanol (1:1 volume ratio) to culture supernatant
2. Collecting precipitate by centrifugation
3. Dissolving in distilled water
4. Dialyzing against distilled water for 2 days
5. Analyzing homogeneity by gel filtration (Sephadex G-200)

## China's Leadership in Fermentation Technology

China dominates world mushroom production (over 64% of total), and is investing heavily in fermenter technology:
- Wolfiporia cocos: 10,000 tons from fermentation
- Ganoderma lucidum: 4,000 tons from fermentation
- At least 10 new edible/medicinal species brought into fermentation cultivation
- Historical expertise in both traditional mushroom medicine and modern biotechnology

## World Production Context

Total world production of cultivated edible and medicinal mushrooms reached 6.16 million metric tons in 1997, valued at over $14 billion. The shift from fruiting body to fermenter production is accelerating as pharmaceutical companies require GMP-grade consistent products.

## Mixed Polysaccharide Products

An emerging area is the use of mixed medicinal mushroom extracts for synergistic immunomodulation:
- **AHCC (Active Hexose Correlated Compound)** — proprietary extract from co-cultivation of L. edodes, T. versicolor, and S. commune on rice; hot water extract after enzyme treatment; contains low MW (c. 5,000 Da) α-1,3 glucans
- Studies suggest greater immune stimulation from polysaccharide mixtures than from single-species extracts
- Cold-alkaline extraction from premixed edible mushroom powders yields conformationally stable (1-6)-branched (1-3)-β-glucans

## The Way Forward

Increasingly, industrial producers are moving or have already moved to fermenter-produced pharmaceutical-grade compounds. The logical progression is from dietary supplement to pharmaceutical application, with fermenter technology providing the consistency and quality control that regulatory authorities require. It is only a matter of time before most major medical products from medicinal mushrooms are produced this way.
