# Medicinal Mushrooms: Beyond Cancer — Cardiovascular, Antidiabetic, and Antimicrobial Effects

While [[medicinal-mushrooms]] are best known for their anticancer properties, their therapeutic applications extend far beyond oncology. This concept covers the cardiovascular, antidiabetic, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties of [[medicinal-mushroom-compounds]].

## Cardiovascular and Cholesterol-Lowering Effects

### Lovastatin from Pleurotus
"The highest amount of lovastatin [is found] in the fruit-body, especially in the lamellae or gills." Mevinolin (lovastatin) has been detected in both submerged and surface fermentation broths of [[pleurotus-species]].

### Pleurotus Cholesterol Reduction
"The addition of 4% dried Pleurotus to a high cholesterol diet effectively reduced cholesterol accumulation in the serum and liver of experimental rats" by:
- Redistributing cholesterol in favour of **HDL**
- Reducing production of **VLDL and LDL** cholesterol
- Reducing cholesterol absorption
- Reducing **HMG-CoA reductase** activity in the liver

Clinical trials with 15–20g dried Pleurotus supplement daily over one month "reduced hypercholesterolemia in many but not all patients." Similar results achieved with **[[grifola-frondosa]]** and **Auricularia auricula**.

### Eritadenine from Lentinus edodes
"Eritadenine reduces blood serum cholesterol in mice not by inhibition of cholesterol biosynthesis but by the acceleration of the excretion of ingested cholesterol and its metabolic decomposition."

Eritadenine lowers cholesterol by:
- Decreasing the ratio of phosphatidylcholine (PC) to phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) in liver microsomes
- Impairing secretion of VLDL cholesterol at high dosages
- Similar mechanism to soybean protein

### Additional Cardiovascular Benefits
- **Lentinus edodes**: Lowers blood pressure and free cholesterol; accelerates lipid accumulation in liver (removing from circulation)
- **Nucleic acids from L. edodes**: Significant platelet agglutinating inhibitory effects (antithrombotic activity)
- **PSK**: Causes decreases in LDL cholesterol in hyperlipidemia patients
- **Tremella fuciformis and T. aurantia**: Antilipemic polysaccharides lower plasma cholesterol levels

### Clinical Evidence
Several small studies in Japan showed "positive decreases in serum cholesterol in young women and people older than 60 years of age" using Lentinus extracts.

## Antidiabetic Effects

### Blood Glucose Lowering
"Several mushroom species including Tremella aurantia, Cordyceps sinensis, Ganoderma lucidum and Auricularia auricula-judae have been shown to lower blood glucose."

### Streptozotocin-Induced Diabetic Model
"The blood glucose and triglyceride (TG) lowering effects of water soluble extracts from Lentinus edodes, Pleurotus ostreatus and Phellinus linteus in the streptozotocin-induced diabetic model have been clearly demonstrated."

"These results strongly suggest that these mushrooms have potential preventive and therapeutic action in diabetes mellitus (type I and II)."

## Antimicrobial Effects

### Context of Growing Antibiotic Resistance
"The recent increase in the occurrences of drug-resistant bacterial strains is creating serious treatment problems." Mushroom antimicrobial polysaccharides function by mobilising humoral immunity to ward off infections.

### Opportunistic Infections
"Many cancer and AIDS patients die of opportunistic infections because of immunosuppression." Mushroom polysaccharides show antiviral activity against:
- Ectromelia virus
- Cytomegalovirus
- HIV

### Lentinan Antimicrobial Activity

#### Antiviral
- Used with AZT: **Suppressed HIV surface expression on T-cells** more than AZT alone
- Sulphated lentinan: Potent anti-HIV activity, inhibiting viral replication and cell fusion
- Activity against VSV, encephalitis virus, Abelson virus, adenovirus type 12
- **Complete protection** against LD75 challenge of virulent mouse influenza A/SW15
- Stimulated non-specific resistance against respiratory viral infections

#### Antibacterial
- Activity against drug-resistant **Mycobacterium tuberculosis**
- Effective against Bacillus subtilis, Staphylococcus aureus, Micrococcus lenteus

#### Antifungal
- Effective against Candida albicans, Saccharomyces cerevisiae

#### Antiparasitic
- Increased resistance to Schistosoma japonicum and S. mansoni
- Increased resistance to potentially lethal Listeria monocytogenes

### LEM and JLS-18
"LEM and a new lignan-rich compound JLS-18 derived from LEM block the release of infectious Herpes simplex virus in animals." JLS-18 has been suggested as potentially valuable for treating hepatitis B and AIDS patients.

### PGG-Glucan Surgical Trials
Three separate multicentre (including Harvard Medical School), randomised, placebo-controlled, double-blind clinical trials:

| Study | Key Finding |
|-------|------------|
| **Babineau 1994a** | High-dose β-glucan (2.0 mg/kg): significantly fewer postoperative infections |
| **Babineau 1994b** | β-glucan group: 1.4 infections/patient vs. placebo: 3.4 infections/patient |
| **Dellinger 1999** | 1,249 patients: **39% reduction** in serious infections and death |

The Dellinger study was terminated early due to adverse effects in the PGG-glucan group. The recommendation is to repeat trials with mushroom-derived β-glucans that have demonstrated no adverse human side effects.

## Antioxidant and Anti-Inflammatory Effects

### Role of Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS)
"A wide variety of pathological damage, such as DNA [damage], carcinogenesis and cellular degeneration, related to the ageing process and ageing itself can be caused by reactive oxygen species (ROS)" produced by sunlight, radiation, chemical reactions, and metabolic processes.

### Mushroom Antioxidant Compounds
Medicinal mushrooms contain various compounds with antioxidant activity:
- **Phenolic compounds**
- **Tocopherols**
- **Ascorbic acid**
- **Carotenoids**
- **Polysaccharides** with free radical scavenging properties

### Anti-Inflammatory Activity
Mushroom-derived compounds reduce inflammation through:
- Inhibition of prostaglandin synthesis
- Modulation of cytokine production
- Reduction of oxidative stress markers
