## Limitations

- Only two timepoints were compared; a full time-series would better

- The first experiment had only 9 plants vs 18 in the second, reducing
  statistical power for direct comparisons
- Other decomposer taxa beyond Cellvibrio were not individually analyzed for
  their post-harvest dynamics
- The study could not distinguish between colonization of decaying root tissue
  and mere presence of decomposer DNA from surrounding soil
