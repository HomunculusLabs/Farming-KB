## Long-Term Water Security

The only long-term insurance of good water supply to a settlement is
by rigorous control of a forested catchment, including a total ban on
biocides and metallic processing. As there are few such clean areas
left in the world, house roof tanks must serve for the foreseeable
future. The 30 to 40 additives commonly introduced into water supplies
are often pollutants in themselves. These additives represent the end
point of the technological fix: pollution is "fixed" by further
pollution.

## The Duty of Downstream Release

The wastewaters of upstream settlements are the drinking waters of
downstream areas. Our duty is to release from any settlement only water
of sufficiently good quality to be safely usable by others. This
principle of downstream responsibility is fundamental to permaculture
water ethics and should guide all settlement water management decisions.

## Problem Contaminants in Drinking Water

The main contaminants likely to affect drinking water are:

- **Turbidity**: silt and fine particles suspended in water
- **Bacterial or organic pollution**: from sewage, including E. coli,
  disease organisms, and viral or protozoan pathogens
- **Chemical pollutants**: heavy metals, agricultural chemicals,
  and industrial byproducts

## The Prevention Principle

The only water safe for us is also safe for other living things. For
millennia we have existed on water supplies containing healthy plants
and fish, and if we keep natural waters free of faecal and industrial
contaminants, we can continue to do so. This is not so much a matter
of water treatment as the prevention of polluting activities. The
biological treatment of common contaminants is stressed throughout
Mollison's approach.
