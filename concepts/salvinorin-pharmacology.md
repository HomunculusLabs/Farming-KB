# Salvinorin Pharmacology

Salvinorin A is the principal psychoactive compound of
[[psilocybin]] and DMT, or phenylethylamines such as mescaline),

subsequently isolated and characterized through [[challenges-neurotropic-fungi-chemical-analysis-taxonomic-identification]] of

Research on the pharmacology of salvinorin A has accelerated since
the late 1990s, when its unique mechanism of action was discovered.
The revelation that salvinorin A acts on kappa-opioid receptors
rather than serotonin receptors was unexpected and opened new avenues
of research into the neurobiology of consciousness and perception.

## Mechanism of Action

Salvinorin A acts primarily as a potent and selective agonist at the
kappa-opioid receptor (KOR). This mechanism of action is entirely
different from that of other classes of hallucinogenic compounds:

- **Classical hallucinogens** (LSD, psilocybin, DMT, mescaline): Act a
  s agonists or partial agonists at serotonin 5-HT2A receptors, produc
  ing their effects primarily through modulation of the serotonin syst
  em
- **Dissociative anesthetics** (ketamine, PCP): Act as NMDA receptor a
  ntagonists, blocking glutamate signaling
- **Deliriants** (atropine, scopolamine from [[belladonna-plant]]): Act as anticholinergics, blocking acetylchol
  ine muscarinic receptors
- **Salvinorin A**: Acts as a kappa-opioid receptor agonist, modulatin
  g the endogenous opioid system

The kappa-opioid receptor system is involved in the modulation of
perception, mood, consciousness, and the body's response to stress
and pain. Activation of KOR produces a distinctive state
characterized by profound alterations in sensory perception, spatial
awareness, and the sense of self, along with dissociative effects and
changes in interoception.

## Potency

Salvinorin A is remarkable for its extraordinary potency, which ranks
among the highest of any known naturally occurring psychoactive
substance:

- **Active dose (inhaled)**: 250-500 micrograms -- a remarkably small 
  amount
- **Active dose (oral/sublingual)**: Higher doses required, typically 
  1-2 mg, due to lower bioavailability through the oral mucosa
- By comparison, LSD is active at doses of 25-100 micrograms, making s
  alvinorin A somewhat less potent than LSD by weight but the most pot
  ent naturally occurring hallucinogen

The extremely low effective dose means that very small amounts of
[[golden-guide-salvia-divinorum-and-diviner-sage]] leaf material can produce profound psychoactive
effects. A single leaf typically contains sufficient salvinorin A for
multiple psychoactive doses.

## Pharmacokinetics

When inhaled (smoked or vaporized), salvinorin A produces effects
within seconds, with peak intensity reached within 1-2 minutes. The
rapid onset reflects the compound's high lipophilicity and efficient
absorption through the lungs. The duration of effects is relatively
short, typically 15-30 minutes when inhaled, although subjective time
distortion can make the experience seem considerably longer.

When absorbed through the oral mucosa (sublingual or buccal
administration), onset is slower (5-15 minutes) and duration longer
(30-60 minutes or more). Oral ingestion of salvinorin A is generally
ineffective due to rapid metabolism in the digestive tract, where it
is deactivated before reaching the central nervous system.

## Effects

The psychoactive effects of salvinorin A are distinctive and often
described as more "physical" or "disruptive" than those of classical
hallucinogens:

- Profound alterations in perception of reality and the boundaries of 
  self
- Complete dissociation from the physical body, sometimes described as
   "[[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]]"
- Vivid, immersive visionary experiences with complex imagery
- Uncontrollable laughter or emotional catharsis
- Synesthetic experiences blending different sensory modalities
- Altered sense of time and space, with time appearing to slow or stop
- Temporary amnesia of the experience after it ends
- A sense of merging with objects, environments, or other entities
- Physical sensations of being pulled, twisted, or transported

The effects come on extremely rapidly when inhaled, which can be
disorienting or frightening to unprepared users. The Mazatec, who
have long used the plant, provide a structured ceremonial context
that helps navigate the intensity of the experience.
