## Passive and Wick Hydroponic Systems for Cannabis

Passive hydroponic systems rely on capillary action rather
than pumps to transfer [[cervantes-growing-medium]] draws moisture
upward from the reservoir, keeping root zones consistently
moist. Absorbent mediums ideal for passive systems include:

- Vermiculite, which holds and wicks moisture effectively
- Peat moss, which has excellent water retention
- Sawdust, though it can compact over time
- Coconut coir, which combines good drainage with moisture
  retention
- Soilless mixes containing these components

## Tray-Based Passive Irrigation

A simple but effective approach uses containers set in a
watertight tray with low sides (approximately two to four
inches or 5-10 cm). The tray is filled with mild nutrient
solution, and the growing medium in the pots wicks up
moisture as needed. One grower reported leaving his indoor
garden under a 400-watt HPS lamp for five days per week.
He filled the tray with nutrient solution on Monday morning
and returned Friday to find plants still strong and healthy.
