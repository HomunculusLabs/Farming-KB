# JADAM Natural Farming Philosophy

JADAM (Jayonul Danggan Nong-eop Moe-dan, meaning "Research Association of Nature Farming") is a Korean ultra-low-cost organic farming movement founded by Young-sang Cho in 1991. JADAM extends Fukuoka's [[natural-farming]] with specific, practical techniques and a focus on self-sufficiency.

## Core Philosophy

### Self-Sufficiency
- Farmers should produce ALL inputs on-farm — seeds, fertilizer, pest control
- No purchased inputs means zero input cost
- Knowledge replaces purchased products
- A farmer who depends on external inputs is not truly free

### Ultra-Low Cost
- JADAM farming costs 1/100th to 1/1000th of conventional farming
- No synthetic fertilizers, no pesticides, no machinery needed
- Labor replaces capital investment
- The goal is maximum profit per unit area, not maximum yield

### Nature Is the Teacher
- Study natural ecosystems and mimic their processes
- Forests grow without human intervention — learn from them
- Soil biology does the work of fertilizers and pesticides
- The farmer's job is to create conditions for biology to thrive

### Ultra-Low Tech
- Simple tools and methods accessible to anyone
- No expensive equipment or proprietary products
- Recipes use locally available materials
- Scalable from backyard garden to commercial farm

## Comparison with Other Methods

| Method | Inputs | Cost | Philosophy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional | Synthetic fertilizers, pesticides | High | Maximum yield |
| Organic | Organic fertilizers, allowed pesticides | Medium | No synthetics |
| [[korean-natural-farming]] | Fermented plant/animal inputs | Low | Specific fermented preparations |
| JADAM | On-farm inputs only | Ultra-low | Total self-sufficiency |
| Fukuoka | No inputs at all | Zero | Do nothing |

## Key Innovation: JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA)
- Made from unrefined canola oil and potassium hydroxide (ash water)
- Acts as a surfactant — allows water-based sprays to penetrate insect bodies
- Replaces commercial soap/surfactant sprays
- Cost: pennies per liter
- Used as the base for all JADAM pest control sprays
