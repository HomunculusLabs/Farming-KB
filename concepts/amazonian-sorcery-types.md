## The Forest Spirits: Three Powers

The vision of the three powers establishes the fundamental tripartite structure of Amazonian spiritual ecology:

- **Land powers**: Represented by the Sachamama (giant earth serpent) and the various practitioners seated in different plants, each demonstrating their alignment (healer, sorcerer, or both)
- **Water powers**: Embodied by the Yakumama (water mother serpent), who sometimes manifests as a steamboat, and the various aquatic beings
- **Sky powers**: Represented by the Huairamama (wind serpent), who moves with great winds and produces thunder-like sounds without rain — a phenomenon called supay-cato (ghost bath)

## Extraterrestrial Beings in Shamanic Cosmology

Amaringo's visions consistently include extraterrestrial beings and spacecraft. Beings arrive from Antares, Andromeda, Saturn, Venus, Ganymede, and the Pleiades. These are not random additions but integral parts of the cosmology — extraterrestrials bring specialized knowledge, serve as allies or adversaries, and interact with human shamans on equal or superior terms. The Inca priest received direct contact with beings from Andromeda whose vision was "very much superior to ours" and who gave specialized knowledge to the Tahuantinsuyo shamans.
