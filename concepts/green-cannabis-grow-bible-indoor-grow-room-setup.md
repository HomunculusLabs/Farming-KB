# Indoor Grow Room Setup

Greg Green's Cannabis Grow Bible provides detailed guidance on
assembling an indoor grow environment, from lighting and ventilation to
reflective surfaces and grow methods.

## Choosing a Grow Space

Common indoor locations include attics, closets, basements, spare
bathrooms, and purpose-built cabinets. The space must accommodate
plant height, allow for light-proofing during flowering, and permit
[[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]]. Security considerations include [[cannabis-odor-control-and-carbon-filters]], heat
signatures, and visibility to visitors or service personnel.

## Lighting

Light is the single most important factor after [[blesching-cannabis-strain-selection-receptor-targeting]]. Green
covers the full range of lighting options:

### Poor Lighting (not recommended)
- Domestic incandescent bulbs: low intensity, bad spectrum
- Halogen lights: extremely hot, dangerous indoors, wrong spectrum

### Average Lighting
- Fluorescent tubes: acceptable for seedlings but low intensity and
  poor spectrum for flowering
- Fluorescent white tubes: correct spectrum but weak (under 100W)

### Best Lighting -- HID (High Intensity Discharge)
A full HID kit contains a bulb, reflector, ballast, timer, and
electrical connections. Two main types:

- **[[cervantes-metal-halide-lighting-systems]] (MH)**: Better for [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]; gives off a
  blue tone. Range 75W-4000W.
- **High [[cervantes-high-pressure-sodium-lighting]] (HPS)**: Better for flowering; gives off an
  orange tone. Considered the lighting choice of most cannabis
  cultivators. Range 75W-4000W.

If only one light can be purchased, get HPS. A switchable ballast
allows both MH and HPS bulbs on the same system.

### Wattage and Lumens

- Minimum: 2500 lumens (even for one plant)
- Target: 45,000+ lumens
- A 600W HPS produces approximately 45,000 lumens and covers a 3x3
  foot area (6-9 plants)
- A 1000W HID is the maximum single-bulb wattage recommended; use
  multiple 1K bulbs for larger areas
- 4000W bulbs bleach cannabis and are too hot
- As a rule: when in doubt, buy 400W HPS or better; below 250W HPS
  yields below-average results

Lumens vary between kits of the same wattage due to design quality.
Always compare lumens ratings.

### Light Placement and Reflection

- Keep the light close but not close enough to burn leaves (use the
  hand test: if you feel discomfort, the plant will too)
- Plants can grow up to an inch per day -- monitor spacing
- **White flat paint** is the best reflective surface, better than
  mirrors (mirrors absorb light)
- Mylar sheets are a popular reflective alternative
- Never use the shiny side of tin foil; use the white side
- Air-cooled hoods reduce room temperature and extend bulb life

### Light-Proofing for Flowering

The flowering room must be completely light-tight. Even a small desk
lamp on the other side of the room will prevent proper 12/12 response.
Green recommends studying photography dark room techniques for
light-proofing door frames and openings. A 100% light-tight room
increases yields by 30% compared to 99% light-tight.

## Ventilation and Air

- Always keep a window open or use extractor and intake fans
- Plants need fresh air during both [[green-cannabis-vegetative-growth]] and flowering
