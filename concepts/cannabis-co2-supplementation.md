## Common Mistakes with CO2

One of the most common errors is supplementing CO2 in an unsealed room. If exhaust fans run continuously, any CO2 injected is immediately evacuated. The room must have a controlled ventilation cycle where CO2 can accumulate.

Another frequent mistake is inadequate light. Some growers expect dramatic results from CO2 with weak lighting. The plant cannot process additional CO2 without the photon energy to drive photosynthesis. CO2 supplementation is most effective when the existing grow setup is already optimized.

Over-fertilization is common when CO2 causes plants to grow faster than the grower anticipates. The increased metabolic rate means plants consume nutrients more rapidly, and what was previously an appropriate feeding schedule may become insufficient or, conversely, excess nutrients may accumulate if the grower overcompensates.

Neglecting to monitor CO2 levels leads to either waste (too much CO2 released) or ineffectiveness (too little). Guessing at CO2 levels is unreliable and inconsistent.
