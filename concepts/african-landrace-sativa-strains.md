# African Landrace Sativa Strains
Africa is one of the primary centers of cannabis genetic diversity, home to numerous landrace
sativa varieties that have been cultivated for centuries. These strains represent some of the
purest expressions of the [[colombian-gold]]

Gage Green Genetics' Colombian Gold is a pure sativa from Colombia representing one of the
foundational equatorial sativa varieties. Colombian landrace genetics were among the most
celebrated cannabis strains of the 1960s and 1970s, when Colombian cannabis flooded the American
market and defined what premium marijuana meant to an entire generation of consumers.

### Rio Negro Colombian Sativa

Bred by Centennial Seeds in the USA, this pure Colombian sativa is an equatorial variety
harking back to the glory days of 1970s cannabis quality. It displays vigorous growth and a
bushy structure, flowering in just 9 to 10 weeks — remarkably fast for an heirloom sativa
variety that typically requires much longer to mature.
The plant features sharp serrated leaves and gorgeous deep green buds with light foxtailing. The
fragrance is full of pine and citrus, with darker sandalwood notes appearing when the buds are
burned. The high is energetic, stimulating, and deeply pleasing — a true keeper for sativa
enthusiasts.

### Chunky Cherry Malawi

Bred by Kingdom Organic Seeds, Chunky Cherry Malawi combines pure Malawi landrace sativa
genetics with indica influence to produce massive, dense buds on tall plants. The strain is
known for its huge cherry flavor and extremely long flowering period, making it a significant
challenge for indoor growers. It represents the African landrace tradition at its most extreme
and rewarding.
