
# Cannabis Indoor Growing Basics

## Overview

Indoor cannabis cultivation provides complete environmental control, allowing growers to optimize every variable for maximum yield, potency, and quality. Unlike [[cannabis-outdoor-growing|outdoor growing]], indoor systems eliminate dependence on weather and seasonal constraints. This guide covers the fundamentals of setting up and managing an indoor cannabis grow room or tent.

## Grow Tent vs. Dedicated Room

### Grow Tents

Grow tents are the most common entry point for indoor growers:

- Quick setup (1-2 hours)
- Reflective interior maximizes light efficiency
- Built-in ports for ventilation, electrical, and ducting
- Available in standard sizes (2x2, 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, 4x8)
- Light-proof and waterproof
- Relatively affordable ($100-500 depending on size)

Recommended tent sizes:
- **2x2 or 2x4:** 1-2 plants (beginner, autoflowers, or small photoperiod plants)
- **3x3:** 2-4 plants (most popular size, good balance of space and cost)
- **4x4:** 4-8 plants (serious hobbyist level)
- **4x8:** 8-16 plants (advanced grower, SOG or SCROG methods)

### Dedicated Grow Rooms

Converted rooms offer more space and customization but require more work:

- Higher initial cost (insulation, wall covering, waterproofing)
- More complex ventilation design
- Greater yield potential
- Better environmental stability
- Permanent installation (less portable)
- More options for custom lighting and irrigation

## Lighting

### Light Types

**LED:** Most popular modern choice. Energy efficient, full-spectrum, long lifespan (50,000+ hours). Higher upfront cost but lower operating cost. Quantum board LEDs with Samsung LM301B/H diodes recommended.

**HPS:** Traditional flowering light with excellent bud spectrum. High heat output, lower initial cost but higher operating cost. Needs MH for vegetative stage. Still used by many commercial growers.

**CMH/LEC:** Full-spectrum with good UV output, moderate heat, superior color rendering. Good all-in-one solution.

### Light Scheduling

- **Vegetative stage:** 18 hours on / 6 hours off (standard)
- **Flowering stage:** 12 hours on / 12 hours off (triggers flowering)
- Use a timer for consistency — light leaks during dark periods cause stress and hermaphroditism

### Light Intensity

Measured in PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density):

- Seedlings: 100-300 PPFD
- Vegetative: 300-600 PPFD
- Flowering: 600-1000+ PPFD

Hang lights adjustable — raise as plants grow to maintain optimal distance and prevent light burn.

## Ventilation

### Exhaust System

- **Inline fan:** Sized to exchange room/tent volume every 1-3 minutes
- **Carbon filter:** Removes odors (attached to exhaust fan)
- **Ducting:** Connects fan to filter and vents outside
- **Passive intake:** Opening opposite exhaust (3-4x exhaust size)

Fan sizing: Calculate volume (L x W x H), aim for exchange every 1-3 minutes. Example: 4x4x7 tent = 112 cu ft; a 200-400 CFM fan is appropriate.

### Temperature Control

Optimal: Vegetative 70-85F day / 60-70F night. Flowering 65-80F day / 55-65F night. Control with AC, heaters, fan speed controllers, or thermostat controllers.

## Humidity Control

Target ranges: Seedlings/clones 65-80%, vegetative 50-70%, early flowering 40-55%, late flowering 30-45%. Equipment: dehumidifier (essential for flowering), humidifier (dry climates/seedlings), hygrometer, and automated controller.

## Growing Mediums

### Soil and Living Soil

- [[cannabis-living-soil-guide|Living soil]] provides slow-release organic nutrition
- pH buffering capacity reduces management complexity
- Best flavor and terpene expression (widely reported)
- Heavier containers require strong support

### Coco Coir

Made from coconut husk fibers. Excellent water retention and drainage, pH neutral (requires cal-mag), lighter weight than soil. Good balance of accessibility and quality.

### Hydroponics

Water-based growing systems with faster growth, precise nutrient control, and higher yields in experienced hands. More complex setup; requires pH, EC/TDS, water temperature, and dissolved oxygen monitoring. Systems include DWC, [[ebb-and-flow]], drip, and NFT.

## Environmental Monitoring

Essential equipment: thermometer/hygrometer (at canopy level), pH meter (for hydro/coco), EC/TDS meter (nutrient concentration), timer (lights/pumps), infrared thermometer (canopy/light surfaces).

### Space Planning

Training methods and spacing: Natural growth 1 plant per 2-3 sq ft, LST 1 per 2 sq ft, SCROG 1 per 2-4 sq ft, SOG 4-9 per sq ft, Topping 1 per 3-4 sq ft. Check [[cannabis-regulations-by-state|local regulations]] for plant count limits.

## Water Quality

- **Filtered water:** Reverse osmosis (RO) or carbon-filtered (preferred)
- **Tap water:** May contain chlorine/chloramine (harmful to microbes). Let sit 24-48 hours to off-gas, or use a dechlorinator.
- **Well water:** Test for mineral content, pH, and contaminants

## Basic Grow Schedule

### Seedling Stage (Weeks 1-2)

- Maintain 70-80% humidity, moderate light intensity
- No nutrients in soil (seed has reserves); light feeding in hydro

### Vegetative Stage (Weeks 3-6+)

- 18/6 light schedule, train plants for even canopy (LST, topping)
- Begin nutrient regimen, monitor pH, temperature, and humidity

### Flowering Stage (Weeks 7-12+)

- Switch to 12/12 light schedule, reduce humidity progressively
- Support heavy branches, reduce nitrogen and increase P and K

### Pre-Harvest (Final 1-2 Weeks)

- Monitor trichomes with a jeweler's loupe (60-100x)
- Reduce watering, prepare drying and curing space

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- Overwatering (most common — let soil dry between waterings)
- Overfeeding (nutrient burn), inadequate ventilation, light leaks
- Harvesting too early, not monitoring pH in coco/hydro systems
