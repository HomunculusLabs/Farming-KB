Choosing and managing cash crops is essential for farm profitability. A permaculture approach to cash cropping emphasizes polyculture, value-added products, and direct sales channels to maximize income while maintaining ecological integrity.

## Choosing Cash Crops

### Selection Criteria

- **Low bulk**: Reduces transportation and storage costs (e.g., honey, berries, herbs)
- **Easy processing**: Minimal equipment needed
- **Value-added potential**: Raw materials processed into higher-value products (jam, butter, dried fruit, smoked meat)
- **Non-perishable backup**: A second cash crop that stores well for year-round sales (firewood, nuts)
- **Polyculture compatibility**: Crops that work together in a diverse growing system

### Cash Crop Ideas

| Category | Examples |
