## The Alkoxy Homologue Series

B illustrates the chemical adage "there's ethyl and propyl, but
butyl is futile" as applied to the 4-position alkoxy homologues
of mescaline. Shulgin notes that the 5-, 6-, and 7-carbon chain
analogues would be:
- A (amylescaline/pentescaline): from amyloxy
- H (hexescaline): from hexyloxy (careful: H has also been used
  for DMPEA, homopiperonylamine)
- S (heptescaline/septescaline): from heptyloxy (Greek "hepta"
  shifted to Latin "septem" since H was consumed)

These would certainly be makeable, but little value could be
anticipated from testing them. The toxic symptoms accompanying
higher homologues would likely discourage use at the dosages
needed to affect higher brain centers. The same applies to the
amphetamine counterparts 3C-B, 3C-A, 3C-H, and 3C-S.
