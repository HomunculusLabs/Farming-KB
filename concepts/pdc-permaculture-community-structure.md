# PDC Permaculture Community Legal Structure

Pamphlet XIII of the 1981 Permaculture Design Course presents Mollison's detailed model for a legal and financial structure that enables permaculture communities to function effectively. This is "the same methods that integrate the elements of a garden system" applied to social systems, "attaining surprisingly similar benefits accruing to the whole social system."

## The Three-Layer Structure

### Layer 1: The Community
A group of people who "agree to a set of principles and ethics that guide them." The ethics are earth care, people care, and non-profiteering -- "not accumulating wealth beyond one's needs."

### Layer 2: The Company (Public Trust)
The community forms an ordinary company. "Part of the function of all companies is to act as a trustee." It does not trade or take cash flow. It issues one-dollar shares to each member. Called the Permaculture Institute, it exists to work in "health, education, and agriculture."

Advantages:
- Immunity from land taxes, rates, and local government charges
- Automatic membership in the World Free Seed Exchange (4,600 institutes, 680 botanical gardens)
- Joint research links with other institutes
- Insulated from government raiding -- if it fails, "so do all schools, churches, and most other important public institutions"
- Donations are tax deductible

### Layer 3: The Trading Trust
A separate non-profit trading corporation, not connected to the Institute Trust. "Within the Trading Trust no risk is incurred." It borrows fixtures, furnishings, and equipment from the Institute at no cost. It is staffed free by community members.

"Good businesses are: travel agencies, a publishing company, a consultancy, a real estate operation, and a seed company."

The Trading Trust distributes profits four ways:
1. Greater amount to the Research Institute (double tax exemption)
2. To public charity (tax deduction)
3. To a political party (equals cost of typewriters, desks, vehicles, tractors)
4. To workers and dependents -- at Tagari: $21 per week, "whether one year old or ninety, male or female"

## Government Business Aids

Australia's export incentives: "All businesses engaged in exporting are automatically remitted certain portions of all fares of exporters traveling overseas." The government regards "consultancy and knowledge as an export." Remission approaches 90% for established exporters.

Export agents in any country also get fare remission plus full remission of accommodation costs. "There is a fantastic amount of potential in that alone for shifting people around the face of the Earth at very little cost."

Australia also "funds and fully refunds on the development of any invention or device that might be salable. You don't have to be under any bureaucrat... these are normal channels."

## Land Strategy

"All land donations go to the Trading Trust, not the Institute." Land comes in from the public, government, local government. "Land accumulates, and can accumulate with extraordinary rapidity."

"There is absolutely no problem in 12 months in getting hold of several million dollars worth of land, no problem whatsoever. I mean hundreds of square miles of land."

Fully owned property passes to the Institute. It can be given to communities on limited tenancy, life tenancy, or life tenancy inheritable. Houses not paid off are rented to members; rent is remitted if they work within the Trading Trust.

## The Philosophy

"We are a group of friends who agree to a set of principles and ethics. As people, we can have no power over any other person, over any capital or indeed over any material goods. So what we have divested ourselves of is power, only power."

"We give enablements away. We will fund a college to teach an aboriginal gardener. We will not give the aborigines money. We will give orange trees to aborigines; we won't give them the money to buy orange trees. We give fishhooks, not fish."

## Self-Reliance Seed Company

Deals only in open-pollinated seeds "from which people can save their own seed." The catalog lists plants under "Bee Forages, Chicken Forages, Cattle Forages, Deer Forages, Fireproof Plants" by zone.

Growers receive stamped envelopes, collect seed, and receive the grower's margin plus the packager's margin. "Nobody else gets a large margin."

"Every seed we sell has instructions on how to save your own seed. We are selling self-reliance."
