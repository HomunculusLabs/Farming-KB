Cannabinoids have biphasic effects on anxiety: low doses can be anxiolytic (reducing anxiety) while high doses can be anxiogenic (increasing anxiety). This is critical for dosing guidance.

### CBD as Anxiolytic
CBD is consistently anxiolytic across a wider dose range than THC. It reduces anxiety without the biphasic complication.

### Memory and Trauma
**Redondo et al. (2014)**: "Bidirectional switch of the valence associated with a hippocampal contextual memory engram." Nature 513(7518):426-30.

This Nature paper demonstrated that the emotional valence (positive/negative) of memories can be switched using optogenetic manipulation of hippocampal engrams. This provides a scientific basis for understanding how trauma memories might be modified.
