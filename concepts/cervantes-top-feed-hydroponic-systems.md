## Multiple Bucket Systems

Multiple bucket top-feed systems connect several buckets to a
central reservoir. Key design elements include:

- Flexible drain hose attached near the bottom of each
  bucket connects to a drainage manifold
- Runoff [[cannabis-nutrient-solution-preparation]] shuttles back to the central
  reservoir for reuse
- Each reservoir below the growing container holds an inch
  or two of water
- Regular irrigation cycling prevents stagnation in the
  bottom of buckets
- Square containers make the most efficient use of space
- Plants are fed with irrigation tubing attached to a
  manifold running between rows

## Top-Feed Slab Systems

Top-feed slab systems are popular among indoor and
[[cervantes-drip-irrigation-systems]]. Runoff
drains through the growing medium and back to the reservoir
for recirculation. This configuration maximizes light
utilization and floor space efficiency.

## Management Tips

Successful top-feed systems require attention to several
factors:

- Maintain proper [[cervantes-ec-tds-nutrient-solution-strength]]
  to avoid [[cervantes-nutrient-burn-over-fertilization]]
- Ensure drainage is rapid to prevent root zone saturation
- Screen drain openings in reservoirs to prevent roots
  from blocking them
- Use a pre-filter on pumps to prevent emitter clogging
