## Level One: Increased Visual Acuity

At low levels of usage, small amounts of psilocybin — consumed with no awareness of psychoactivity while browsing for food — impart a noticeable increase in visual acuity, especially edge detection. As visual acuity is at a premium among hunter-gatherers, the discovery of "chemical binoculars" could not fail to have an impact on hunting and gathering success.

Partnership groups containing individuals with improved eyesight will be more successful at feeding their offspring. Because of the increase in available food, the offspring within such groups will have a higher probability of reaching reproductive age. The outbreeding (or decline) of non-psilocybin-using groups would be a natural consequence.
