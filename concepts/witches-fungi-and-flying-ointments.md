### Connection to Folk Medicine

Many accused witches were, in fact, village healers and midwives who possessed extensive knowledge of medicinal and toxic plants and fungi. Their familiarity with psychoactive substances may have come from legitimate medical and herbal practices that were subsequently reinterpreted as diabolical witchcraft. The knowledge of which mushrooms and plants produced which effects was part of the folk pharmacopoeia that village wise women maintained.

## The Broader Context of Fungi and Witchcraft

### Mushroom Imagery

Mushrooms appeared in various forms of European folk art and symbolism connected to witchcraft. The sudden overnight appearance of mushrooms was seen as evidence of supernatural activity. The bizarre forms of some fungi — particularly stinkhorns and other Phallales — were interpreted as demonic or obscene manifestations.
