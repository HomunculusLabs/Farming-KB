### Aborts and Mutants

"Aborts" — mushrooms that cease growing, becoming convoluted with gnarly stems —
are ironically among the most potent specimens, with concentrated alkaloids in
small mass. Over half of initial primordia abort naturally; these should be picked
before their heads turn black using a pointed knife blade. Mutant blobs with little
or no cap also retain high potency.

## Harvesting Technique

### Optimal Harvest Window

The best specimens are very light in color with white stems, conical shape with
unbroken or just-breaking veil, light-colored gills, and minimal sporulation. As
mushrooms mature beyond this point, caps spread, gills darken, and potency
decreases significantly. Mature specimens are good for spore collection but weak
in psychedelic potency.

### Handling

Remove the fungus cake from the jar when primordia (tiny worm-like structures with
reddish heads) appear. Handle carefully to avoid bruising (blue marks). Dab the
cake with tissue paper to absorb water droplets and immediately place into the
fruiting terrarium.

## Drying Methods

### Critical Principles

Heat is the enemy of alkaloid preservation. Never dry mushrooms in an oven or
with hot air dryers — heat leaches [[pf-tek-low-humidity-symptoms-and-remediation]]. After a couple of days, finish in a desiccant
chamber.

### Desiccant Drying (Recommended)

The overall best technique for preserving alkaloid content.

**Materials**: Desiccant (Drierite, silica gel, or "Damp Gone"), wire screen,
plastic tub, plastic bag with tie-off.

**Process**:
1. Pre-heat desiccant in an oven per manufacturer instructions
2. Place 1-inch layer of desiccant in container bottom
3. Place mushrooms on wire screen above the desiccant
4. Put container in sealed plastic bag
5. After 24 hours, visible shriveling occurs
6. After 4-5 days, mushrooms are rock-hard (stem should snap cleanly)
