ground by late October is unlikely to establish sufficiently

## Succession Planting Intervals

Coleman uses short succession intervals for salad greens and baby
leaf crops. Typical intervals are:
- **Spinach:** Every 2 to 3 weeks from August through October.
- **Lettuce mix:** Every 2 weeks from September through March.
- **Arugula:** Every 2 to 3 weeks from September through March.
- **Radishes:** Every 3 weeks from September through April.
- **Mache:** Two to three plantings in September and October.

For heading crops like kale and cabbage, a single planting is
sufficient for the entire winter. These crops hold well in the
field and can be harvested leaf-by-leaf over many months.

## Transplant Production

Many winter crops are started as transplants in protected seedbeds
or cell trays. Coleman starts brassica seedlings in July for August
transplanting, ensuring large, well-rooted plants by winter. Cell
trays with 128 or 200 cells per flat are used for greens transplants,
with seedlings moved to the field at the 4 to 6 leaf stage.

Transplanting is preferred for crops that require a long growing
season to reach full size. [[coleman-leek-deep-planting-technique]]
