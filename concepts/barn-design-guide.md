## Permaculture Barn Design (Two-Story)

An innovative barn design optimized for permaculture principles:

- **Built on a slope**: Animals enter the main floor upstairs without needing a ramp
- **Slatted floors**: Behind each stall, slats in the floor allow manure to be pushed through to the lower level
- **Manure collection**: The lower level collects manure, which decomposes in place or is moved to the [[sheep-keeping-guide]] — sheep housing requirements
