## Principles of Good Chunking

### Start Close to Home
Begin with Zone 1 (closest to the house). Successes here are most visible and most frequently used, providing daily reinforcement.

### Build on Success
Each chunk should build confidence and skills. Don't take on complex projects (like greywater systems or large earthworks) until you've completed simpler ones.

### Work with What You Have
Use available materials, existing plants, and current resources. Don't wait for the perfect conditions -- start with what's available.
