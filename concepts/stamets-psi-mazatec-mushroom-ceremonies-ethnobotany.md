## Aztec Mushroom Ceremonies

Sahagun recorded detailed descriptions of Aztec mushroom rituals:
mushrooms were served when shell trumpets were blown. Participants ate
no other food and drank only chocolate during the night. The mushrooms
were consumed with honey. When the effects took hold, participants
danced, wept, and some sat in contemplation. After the effects
subsided, they consulted among themselves and shared what they had seen
in visions. The Aztec emperor Moctezuma held an annual "feast of the
revelations" where the cognoscenti would consume green mushrooms.
