# KNF Seawater and Fermented Seawater

Seawater is KNF's full-spectrum mineral supplement. Surface seawater (top 3 cm) swarms with microbes and contains all essential trace minerals in balanced, bioavailable form. Fermented seawater combines seawater minerals with land-based microbes and [[hamilton-composting-and-organic-matter-management]], creating a potent biological-mineral input.

## Why Seawater?

Fish live off seawater microbes. Surface seawater (top 3 cm) has the highest microbial density and lowest salt concentration. Deeper water has higher salt content and fewer microbes. All 92 naturally occurring elements are present in seawater in proportions similar to blood plasma.

## Collection

- Collect surface seawater (top 3 cm) from clean ocean water
- Avoid polluted areas, river mouths, harbors
- For storage: pour in a large bowl, leave for a day to let airborne microbes colonize
- This begins the fermentation process

## Fermented Seawater Recipe

### Materials
- Surface seawater
- Rice-washed water (x1/200)
- FPJ of Japanese mugwort and dropwort (x1/500)
- Pear FPJ (optional)

### Process
1. Dilute seawater 1:30 with fresh water
2. Add rice-washed water at 1:200 ratio
3. Add FPJ (mugwort + dropwort) at 1:500 ratio
4. Keep for half a day in summer (2-3 days if cloudy)
5. Use when mixture becomes whitish like haze
6. **Cannot be stored** — use immediately when ready
7. If it goes bad: haze clumps like cotton balls — discard

### Why It Works
Combining land microbes (rice wash, FPJ) with ocean minerals and microbes creates a brackish-water effect similar to an estuary. Estuaries are among the most biologically productive environments — plankton thrive, fish feed on plankton, and this energy transfers to crops and livestock.

## Application Rates

| Use Case | Dilution | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Seawater alone | 1:30 | General mineral supplementation |
| Pre-harvest sweetness | Seawater + WS-Ca | 15-20 days before harvest |
| Seedling treatment (onion/leek/garlic) | 1:20 seawater + FPJ + BRV | Or dilute salt water to 0.2% |
| Ripening enhancement | 1:30, 1:25, or 1:20 | Stronger = faster ripening |
| Chicken feather-loss prevention | 1:10-1:15 + BRV 1:200 + WS-Ca 1:500 | Every other day in summer |
| Anthracnose control | Fermented seawater + IMO #4 + OHN + WS-CaPo | Increased microbial activity |

## Key Benefits

- Full-spectrum mineral supplementation (all trace elements)
- Controls fungal diseases: scab, rust, anthracnose
- Effective during hot weather and tropical nights
- Provides abundant energy to crops and livestock
- Enhances fruit sugar content when combined with WS-Ca
- Rice-washed water acts like yeast in the fermentation
- Even more effective when OHN is mixed in
