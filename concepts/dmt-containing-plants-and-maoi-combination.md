## Overview

hallucinogenic tryptamine found in numerous plant species worldwide.

### Psychotria viridis (Chacruna)

Psychotria viridis provides the DMT component of ayahuasca, with DMT
content ranging from 0.1% to 0.66% by dry weight. Like Banisteriopsis
caapi, it is native to the Amazon and not found wild in the US.

### Phalaris Species (Canary Grass)

DMT can be found in common plants growing throughout much of the
United States. Phalaris arundinacea, Phalaris tuberosa, and Phalaris
aquatica (canary grass) contain appreciable amounts of DMT obtainable
through simple juice extraction. These grasses are found in fallow
fields, cracks in sidewalks, and many lawns across the entire United
States. DMT content is highly variable, but high percentage strains
can be ordered through the Internet.
