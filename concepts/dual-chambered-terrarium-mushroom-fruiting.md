# Dual Chambered Terrarium Mushroom Fruiting

The Dual Chambered Terrarium (DCT) is the fruiting chamber design developed by
[[bloomfield-mushroom-formation-fruiting-body-development-cultivation]].

## Design Principle

The DCT exploits a simple physical principle: a moisture differential between two
chambers creates a [[stamets-cultivator-humidity-management-fogging-systems]] points:

- The water level in the lower chamber must be maintained — it gradually evaporates
  and must be topped up periodically
- Covering the aquarium with plastic wrap or a fitted lid reduces moisture loss
- Spraying the walls of the upper chamber with water can boost humidity when needed
- A fine mist spray bottle is used — never spray directly onto the mushroom cakes

### Fresh Air Exchange (FAE)

While high humidity is essential, mushrooms also require fresh air to supply oxygen
and remove metabolic CO2. Without adequate gas exchange, fruiting deforms or stalls.
The DCT manages this through:

- Periodic fanning — the cover is removed and air is fanned across the chamber
- The frequency of fanning depends on the stage of fruiting and ambient conditions
- During active fruiting, 2-3 fanning sessions per day may be necessary

### Temperature

Fruiting temperatures are slightly lower than colonization temperatures:
- Optimal range: 70-75°F (21-24°C)
- Temperature drops of a few degrees from incubation temperature can actually
  trigger [[dual-chambered-terrarium]]
