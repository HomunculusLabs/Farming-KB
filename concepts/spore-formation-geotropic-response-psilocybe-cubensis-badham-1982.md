## Light as Indirect Factor

Light's role in geotropic expression is almost certainly indirect:

- **Darkness suppresses spores:** Few spores form, no geotropic response.
- **Light direction irrelevant:** Light from below still produces upward
  recurvature — ruling out direct phototropism as the cause.
- **Photoperiod independence:** Identical results in 12h and constant
  light, as long as spore production is enabled.
- **Threshold model:** Light enables spore production, which generates
  the tropic signal. Below a minimum light threshold, insufficient
  spores form to trigger the geotropic switch.
