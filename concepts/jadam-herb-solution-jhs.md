# JADAM Herb Solution (JHS)

JHS is a boiled herb extract that provides insecticidal and germicidal power to [[jadam-methods]] pesticides. Different herbs target different pests. The basic herb is Jerusalem artichoke (controls aphids, mites, moths). Stronger options include gingko (adds leafhopper/planthopper control) and Korean pasque flower root (strongest -- controls stinkbugs, thrips, and almost everything else). Always combined with [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]].

## Herb Selection Guide

| Herb | Parts Used | Collect | Strength | Primary Targets |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|----------------|
| Jerusalem artichoke | Leaves, stems, roots | Spring-autumn | Basic | Aphids, mites, most moths |
| Gingko | Fresh/fallen leaves, fruits | Spring-autumn | Strong | Everything JA does + planthoppers, leafhoppers, mulberry suckers |
| Korean pasque flower root (KPFR) | Roots | Autumn | Strongest | Almost all pests including stinkbugs, thrips, whiteflies, fruit flies |
| Red spider lily | Roots | Autumn | Strong | Moles (soil), snails -- highly poisonous |
| Tobacco | Leaves | Spring-autumn | Medium | Broad insecticide -- also steep in water 15 days |
| Pokeweed | Leaves, stems | Spring-autumn | Medium | Replaces Jerusalem artichoke |
| Garlic | Bulbs | Autumn | Medium | General insecticide (steep 1 month) |
| Hot pepper | Fruits | Autumn | Medium | General insecticide (steep 1 month) |
| Bracken | Leaves | Spring-autumn | Medium | Insecticide |
| Foxglove | Leaves | Spring-autumn | Strong | Broad insecticide -- highly poisonous |
| Oleander | Leaves | Spring-autumn | Strong | Replaces KPFR for stinkbugs |

Grow Jerusalem artichoke, gingko, and Korean pasque flower around your farm for self-sufficiency.

## Recipe: Boiled JHS

### Water Ratios

| Material | Pressure Pot | Ordinary Pot |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 kg fresh plant | 4L water | 5L water |
| 1 kg dried plant | 20L water | 25L water |

Must use soft water (test with JWA).

### Process

1. Cut fresh plant into pieces (or use dried)
2. Put plant material in fine mesh bag with a rock to sink it
3. Place bag in pot with measured water
4. For pressure pot: heat on high until boil, reduce to medium, maintain gentle boil
5. Boil 4 hours (pressure pot) or 5 hours (ordinary pot)
6. Keep lid slightly open so contents don't spill
7. After boiling, remove bag and drip remaining liquid
8. You get JHS at ~70% of the water volume added
9. Filter if not clear before use

### Storage (sterile bottling)

1. While JHS is still boiling, pour into heat-resistant bottles until completely full
2. Squeeze slightly until no air remains, seal tightly with lid
3. **Immediately lay bottle on its side** -- hot liquid sterilizes the air pocket around lid
4. Store in cool, dark place or cold storage
5. Properly sterilized JHS keeps 1-2 years
6. Use small bottles (easier to fill completely, use up quickly)
7. Once opened, use within 1-2 days
8. If bottle inflates during storage = sterilization failed (microbes feeding on it)

### Alternative Methods

**Water steeping** (no boiling): Tobacco 15 days; hot pepper, gingko fruits 1 month; garlic 1 month. Leave plant material in water, strain when ready.

**Oil infusion** (for H-JWA): Deep-fry strong-scented herbs (peppermint, cinnamon bark, star anise, rosemary, hot pepper, mustard) in canola oil. Use this herbal oil instead of plain canola oil when making JWA.

## Usage Rates (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

| Situation | JHS Volume |
|-----------|-----------|
| Mild pest pressure | 3L (0.8 gal) |
| Moderate infestation | 5-10L (1.3-2.6 gal) |
| Heavy infestation | 10-15L (4 gal) |
| Emergency (KPFR only) | 15-20L (4-5.3 gal) |
| Fog machine (17L tank) | 10L (2.6 gal) |

**Critical rule**: When increasing JHS, always increase JWA proportionally.

## JHS Pesticide Formulations

**Aphids and mites** (per 500L):
- JWA 5L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 5L

**Moths** (tobacco moth, armyworm, diamondback):
- JWA 8L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 15L

**Planthoppers, leafhoppers, mulberry suckers**:
- JWA 10L + gingko JHS 15L

**Stinkbugs, thrips, whiteflies, fruit flies** (strongest):
- JWA 10L + KPFR JHS 15L (increase to JWA 15L + JHS 20L for emergency)

## Important Notes

- JHS mixes well with JWA (lots of foam, no clotting)
- Do NOT use sediments -- only clear top portion; filter when particles present
- JHS also functions as fertilizer (contains N, P, K, Ca, Mg, Fe, Mn, Zn)
- Little concentration stress compared to JS
- Can spray consecutively when JS is not in the mix
