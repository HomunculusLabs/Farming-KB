### Vermicompost

Vermicompost is produced through the action of earthworms processing organic materials at room temperature. For vermicompost to qualify as high quality, at least 75–80% of the material must have passed through the worm digestive system. Weed seeds must not be added, or materials must be pre-composted before being fed to worms. Key habitat requirements include:

- Room temperature throughout the process
- Adequate earthworm population per unit volume
- Adequate oxygen availability
- Monitoring of temperature and oxygen levels per batch

### Worm Castings vs Worm Compost

Ingham draws an important technical distinction: worm castings are specifically the fecal pellets produced by worms. If any non-pellet material is mixed in, the product is properly called worm compost, not worm castings.
