# Holzer Traditional Farm Medicine and Medicinal Herbs

Sepp Holzer's farm at the Krameterhof has always functioned as both a food source and a pharmacy. His knowledge of medicinal and culinary herbs draws on Alpine farming traditions passed down through generations. This complements the [[composting-humus-building-holzer]] page with additional detail from the plant lists and practical descriptions.

## The Farm as Pharmacy

Traditional Alpine farms were largely self-sufficient in medicine. Every farm had its collection of medicinal herbs, and knowledge of their preparation and use was passed from parent to child. Holzer's mother maintained the family herb garden, using herbs for both cooking and healing. The family medicine cabinet included herbal teas, tinctures, salves, and syrups made from garden and wild plants.

## Medicinal and Culinary Plant Compendium

The following is drawn from Holzer's reference plant list for town gardens, which specifies growing conditions, frost hardiness, and uses for each species:

### Digestive and Culinary Herbs

- Sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) -- good loose soil, sunny, annual culinary plant
- Chives (Allium schoenoprasum) -- good soil, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, culinary
- Garlic chives (Allium ramosum) -- wonderful flavour, undemanding, good soils, frost hardy, culinary
- Coriander (Coriandrum sativum) -- good moist soil, annual, sunny, culinary (fresh leaves and seeds)
- Dill (Anethum graveolens) -- good moist soil, sunny to partial shade, annual culinary plant
- Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare) -- good soils, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, culinary and medicinal
- Horseradish (Armoracia rusticana) -- undemanding, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, culinary
- Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia) -- well-drained soils, sunny, frost hardy, discourages aphids, medicinal
- Lovage (Levisticum officinale) -- good soils, hardy, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, culinary and medicinal
- Marjoram (Origanum majorana) -- good soils, sunny, medicinal and culinary
- Oregano (Origanum vulgare) -- good moist soil, sunny, frost hardy, culinary plant
- Parsley (Petroselinum crispum) -- good soil, partial shade, biennial, culinary
- Rosemary -- not listed but referenced in Holzer's broader work
- Sage (Salvia officinalis) -- undemanding, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary (particularly intense flavour on poor, dry soils)
- Southernwood (Artemisia abrotanum) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary, tastes of lemon
- Tarragon (Artemisia dracunculus) -- undemanding but prefers good soils, sunny, sensitive to frost, culinary with very intense flavour
- Thyme (Thymus vulgaris) -- undemanding, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary (particularly intense flavour on poor, dry soils)
- Broad-leaved thyme (Thymus pulegioides) -- undemanding, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary
- Winter savoury (Satureja montana) -- good loose soil, sunny, hardy and frost resistant, culinary, keeps aphids away from beans
- Wild garlic (Allium vineale) -- very aromatic, sunny, frost hardy, culinary

- Herb hyssop (Hyssopus officinalis) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary
- Hound's-tongue (Cynoglossum officinale) -- undemanding, good moist soils, biennial, sunny to partial shade, decoy plant for voles
- Lady's mantle (Alchemilla erythropoda) -- very undemanding, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal
- Masterwort (Peucedanum ostruthium) -- good moist soils, partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal

- Motherwort (Leonurus cardiaca) -- undemanding and hardy, sunny to partial shade, frost resistant, medicinal
- Mugwort (Artemisia vulgaris) -- very undemanding, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary (good with pork)
- Musk mallow (Malva moschata) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal
- Narrow-leaved inula (Inula ensifolia) -- good soil, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal
- Peppermint (Mentha piperita) -- undemanding but prefers good soil, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal, tea
- Pennyroyal (Mentha pulegium) -- undemanding but prefers good soil, sunny to partial shade, sensitive to frost, medicinal, tea
- Pot marigold (Calendula officinalis) -- undemanding but prefers good soil, annual, sunny, discourages nematodes in mixed planting, medicinal
- Purple coneflower (Echinacea purpurea) -- good well-drained soil, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal (particularly in homeopathy)
- Rue (Ruta graveolens) -- prefers loose soil, sunny, sensitive to frost, discourages insects, medicinal and culinary
- Safflower (Carthamus tinctorius) -- thrives on poor soils, annual, sunny, dye plant (yellow/orange flowers)
- Scented mayweed / chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla) -- undemanding, annual, sunny, medicinal

- Small-flowered willowherb (Epilobium parviflorum) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal
- St. John's wort (Hypericum perforatum) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal
- Sweet cicely (Myrrhis odorata) -- good moist to wet soil, partial shade, frost hardy, culinary
- Sweet flag (Acorus calamus) -- marshy areas, on banks, sunny to partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal and culinary
- Sweet woodruff (Galium odoratum) -- good moist soil, partial to full shade, frost hardy, culinary, sweet woodruff punch
- Tansy (Tanacetum vulgare) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, frost hardy, can be used in liquid fertiliser
- Valerian (Valeriana officinalis) -- undemanding but prefers moist soil, partial shade, frost hardy, medicinal
- Watercress (Nasturtium officinale) -- moist to wet areas (on banks), partial shade, frost hardy, added to salads
- Wormwood (Artemisia absinthium) -- thrives on poor soils, sunny, does not get along with many other plants, frost hardy, medicinal
- Yellow chamomile (Anthemis tinctoria) -- undemanding, also on poor and dry soils, sunny, frost hardy, dye plant (yellow flowers)
- Yellow gentian (Gentiana lutea) -- undemanding, sunny, frost hardy, medicinal

### Dual-Purpose Plants

Several plants serve as both decoy plants for [[holzer-problem-plant-regulation-with-livestock]] and medicinal/culinary herbs:
- Hound's-tongue -- decoy for voles, medicinal
- Pot marigold -- discourages nematodes, medicinal
- Winter savoury -- keeps aphids away from beans, culinary
- Lavender -- discourages aphids, medicinal
- Rue -- discourages insects, medicinal and culinary
- Borage -- improves heavy soils, attracts pollinators, medicinal and culinary
- Garden nasturtium (Tropaeolum majus) -- added to salads, decoy plant for aphids

## Liquid Fertilisers from Medicinal Plants

Several medicinal herbs are used to make liquid fertilisers that double as plant tonics and pest deterrents:

- Comfrey (Symphytum officinale) -- potassium-rich liquid manure for fruiting plants
- Nettle (Urtica dioica) -- nitrogen and iron-rich general growth stimulant
- Tansy (Tanacetum vulgare) -- can be included in liquid fertiliser mixes
- Oak leaf tea -- fungal disease prevention
- Horsetail (Equisetum arvense) -- silica-rich, strengthens cell walls against fungal attack

## Connection to the Broader Farm System

Medicinal and culinary herbs are integrated throughout the farm:
- Near the kitchen garden gate for quick access (lovage, chives, garlic)
- On dry stony slopes where they develop more intense flavour (thyme, lavender, sage)
- In moist nutrient-rich areas (mint, lemon balm, motherwort)
- Around fruit trees as companion plants (pot marigold, borage, garlic)
- In permanent pastures as self-seeding medicinal plants
- In [[sepp-holzer-terrace-systems-and-raised-beds]] for household use
