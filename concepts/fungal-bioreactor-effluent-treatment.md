## Immobilization Technologies

Fungal immobilization is critical for continuous reactor operation:

- **Polyurethane foam**: Provides high surface area, good porosity, and mechanical stability. T. versicolor immobilized on polyurethane shows effective decolorization over extended operation.
- **Calcium alginate beads**: Simple entrapment method; mild conditions preserve enzyme activity. Pallerla and Chambers (1997) reported 61-72% decolorization using alginate-immobilized T. versicolor.
- **Jute rope**: Low-cost natural support material. Marwaha et al. (1998) achieved 86% decolorization with P. chrysosporium immobilized on jute rope, although performance deteriorated after 10 cycles.
- **Membrane bioreactors**: Semipermeable hollow-fiber bioreactors (Chambers and Cheng 1991) separate fungal biomass from treated effluent while allowing metabolite exchange.
