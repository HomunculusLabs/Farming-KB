# Earth Cellar Construction

Earth cellars use the natural temperature-balancing properties of soil to create ideal storage conditions [[query-how-do-i-grow-elderberry-for-fruit-and-medicine]] vegetables without any additional energy input.

## Temperature and Humidity

The high humidity of around 80 to 90 percent in earth cellars and their fairly steady temperature between 8 and 10°C is ideal for storing the majority of crops. Modern houses with heated cellars and concrete floors are disastrous for storing produce — the low humidity causes apples to wither and wrinkle [[query-what-are-the-benefits-of-straw-mulch-in-the-garden-and-how-do-i-use-it]] shortest amount of time.

## Construction Method

Roundwood shelters and earth cellars are built using notched tree trunks:

1. **Foundation**: Rough timber logs are placed at least one metre deep and cut to the same height.
2. **Walls**: Notched tree trunks are used to construct the roof and walls. For stability, the back wall should fit closely with the top edge [[mckenna-food-of-the-gods-exopheromones-and-plant-communication]] roof.
3. **Roof layers**: The roof is built up in layers — membrane, sheeting, fine soil, another membrane, then soil and vegetation on top.
4. **Storage**: The interior serves [[query-can-bamboo-be-used-as-a-building-material-and-how-do-i-get-started]] storage room with the natural insulation of earth maintaining stable conditions.

## Advantages

Building roundwood shelters and earth cellars is possible and practical all over the world. By making use of the balancing effect of the soil, a pleasant cool retreat can be created in hot places [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-a-monotub-and-a-shotgun-fruiting-chamber]] warm one in cold places. The key is understanding [[query-how-to-make-and-use-plant-based-foliar-sprays-for-pest-and-disease-prevention]] make use of nature's resources properly. Regular inspection and prompt repair of any damage is necessary to guarantee safety.
