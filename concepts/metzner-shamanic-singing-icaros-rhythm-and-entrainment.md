## Icaros — The Shamanic Songs of the Amazon

Among mestizo and indigenous ayahuasceros in the Peruvian and Brazilian
Amazon, the icaros constitute the shaman's primary therapeutic tool.

Luis Eduardo Luna documented that vegetalistas claim to learn their
icaros directly from the plant spirits during their dieta periods of
isolation in the jungle. Each plant teacher imparts its own song, which
the shaman then uses to invoke that plant's specific healing power.

An experienced vegetalista may know dozens or even hundreds of icaros,
each one associated with a particular plant ally and its particular
medicinal application.

chanting combined with the consumption of natema (ayahuasca) to enter a
state he called the "shamanic state of consciousness" (SSC).

Metzner argued that the rhythmic, percussive driving quality of shamanic
singing acts as a kind of technology of consciousness, sustaining and
deepening the altered state produced by the entheogenic plant.

This entrainment effect may explain why music is so universally present
in entheogenic ceremonies and why the absence of music is often reported
as making the experience more difficult or fragmented.
