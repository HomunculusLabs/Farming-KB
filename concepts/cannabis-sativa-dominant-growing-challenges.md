## Light Requirements and Photoperiod Sensitivity
Sativa-dominant strains often have more demanding light requirements than
indicas. Their tropical and subtropical origins mean they evolved under
conditions of intense, equatorial light with relatively consistent day
lengths. Indoors, sativas typically require higher light intensity to achieve
their full potential -- experienced growers recommend 50-75 watts per square
foot of actual canopy coverage from high-quality LED fixtures, compared to
35-50 watts for indicas. Light stress from insufficient intensity can cause
sativas to stretch excessively as they search for light, compounding the
height management
challenges.
