Perhaps the most interesting research conducted in Paris throughout the
1960s, largely unreported, was that investigating the effects of
psilocybin upon creativity. The question as to whether drugs unleashed or
provoked artistic inventiveness was not new, for artists, poets and
musicians had been experimenting with drugs since at least the early
days of Romanticism.
