## Climatic Zones: Tropical
**Source:** Permaculture: A Designers' Manual, Bill Mollison (1988)
## Overview
Chapter 10 of the Designers' Manual covers tropical and subtropical
climates in detail. Mollison divides the tropics into several sub-types,
each with distinct design challenges and species assemblages. This page
covers the humid tropics, wet-dry tropics, and monsoon tropics.
## Humid Tropics
### Characteristics
- High rainfall (150-400+ inches annually), evenly distributed
- High temperatures year-round (25-35 C / 77-95 F)
- High humidity; persistent cloud cover
- Rapidly leached, low-nutrient soils (except on recent volcanics)
- Dense [[permaculture-designers-manual-animal-housing-detailed]]
- Fuelwood plantation with windbreak
### Evolving a Polyculture
Start by ripping, swaling, pitting, damming from the highest point.
Co-plant:
- Tree legumes at 3x3m spacing
- Bananas at 2x2m
- Fruits at 5x8m
- Palms at 10x10m
- [[comparison-cassava-vs-taro-vs-sweet-potato-vs-yam]], cassava, arrowroot, comfrey at 1-1.5m
- Seed holes: nasturtium, Dolichos, clover, broad bean, buckwheat,
  Umbelliferae, lupin, vetch, dun peas, chilies, pigeon pea
Aim to carpet and overshade ground in 18-20 months. Complex polycultures
work best at small scale with close human attention.
### Key Species
- **Tree crops:** Coconut, breadfruit, mango, citrus, avocado, durian,
  jakfruit, rambutan, lychee, mangosteen
- **Legumes:** Leucaena, Gliricidia, Acacia, Inga, Albizia, Erythrina,
  Sesbania
- **Ground covers:** Sweet potato, cassava, taro, yam, kang kong,
  xanthosoma
## Wet-Dry Tropics (Savannah)
### Characteristics
- Distinct wet and dry seasons
- High temperatures in wet season; cooler in dry
- Erratic rainfall; drought common in dry season
- Soils generally more fertile than wet tropics (less leached)
- Savannah and grassland with [[permaculture-designers-manual-island-climate]] for related information from the Permaculture Designer's Manual.
