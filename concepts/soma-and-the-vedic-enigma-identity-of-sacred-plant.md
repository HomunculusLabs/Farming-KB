## Soma as Male Moon God

The Vedic texts contain mythological descriptions of Soma that go beyond
botanical identification. Soma is described as a celestial substance that
resides on a high mountain, that is brought to earth by an eagle, and that
is associated with the moon — Soma is literally identified with the moon god
Chandra. The waxing and waning of the moon were understood as the drinking
and replenishing of the Soma reservoir in the heavens. The plant was said to
be guarded by demons and could only be obtained through heroic effort. These
mythological layers complicate any purely botanical identification and suggest
that Soma may have been a cultural construction that combined a real
psychoactive plant with layers of symbolic meaning accumulated over
centuries of ritual use.
