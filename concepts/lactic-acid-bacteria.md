# Lactic Acid Bacteria (LABS)

Lactic Acid Bacteria Serum (LABS) is a homemade biological inoculant used in organic cannabis cultivation and Korean Natural Farming. LABS contains beneficial Lactobacillus species that function as bio-fertilizers, exhibit antifungal activity, and may enhance terpene and cannabinoid production.

## What LABS Does

- **Bio-fertilizer**: Increases nutrient availability and uptake
- **Antifungal activity**: LABS suppresses plant pathogens and diseases
- **Soil microbial balance**: Part of the effective microorganisms (EM) approach
- **Mineral solubilization**: Helps make soil minerals available to plants
- **Photosynthetic efficiency**: May improve plant energy production
- **Biological nitrogen fixation**: Supports nitrogen cycling in the [[soil-food-web]]

## LABS and Cannabis Quality

Research suggests LABS may increase trichome-to-calyx ratio and terpene production through two mechanisms:

### 1. Hexanoic Acid Precursor
LABS fermentation of ruminant milk produces hexanoic acid (caproic acid) as a byproduct. Hexanoate is the direct precursor for hexanoyl-CoA, which feeds the cannabinoid biosynthesis pathway via olivetolic acid production in glandular trichomes.

### 2. Plant Defense Priming
Hexanoic acid activates broad-spectrum plant defenses:
- Induces callose deposition
- Activates salicylic acid (SA) pathway (systemic acquired resistance)
- Activates jasmonic acid (JA) pathway (induced systemic resistance)
- Primes redox-related genes for antioxidant protection
- Both SA and JA pathways are involved in trichome production as a defense response

## Making LABS (Step by Step)

### Ingredients
- Fresh rice
- Water (chlorine-free)
- Milk (raw or pasteurized, NOT ultra-pasteurized)
- Molasses
- Glass jar with one-way fermentation valve (airlock)

### Process

**Phase 1: Rice Wash (5-7 days)**
1. Soak rice in jar, shake vigorously, then strain out rice
2. Keep the milky rice water
3. Cover loosely and store in a dark cupboard
4. Wait until a skin forms on top and three layers are visible
5. Siphon the middle layer — this is your rice wash culture

**Phase 2: Milk Fermentation (7-8 days)**
1. Mix the rice wash culture with 10 parts milk in a jar
2. Seal with one-way fermentation valve
3. Wait 7-8 days as pH drops
4. Milk fats and carbohydrates separate into a floating curd (cheese)
5. The liquid below is probiotic whey — this is your LABS

**Phase 3: Stabilization**
1. Strain the liquid whey
2. Add equal parts molasses (1:1 ratio) to stabilize
3. Store in a sealed container at room temperature

## Application Rates

### Soil Drench
- Dilute 1:1000 (1ml LABS per liter of water)
- Apply every 1-2 weeks during vegetative and early flowering stages
- Compatible with compost tea applications

### Foliar Spray
- Dilute 1:500 (2ml LABS per liter of water)
- Apply during vegetative stage and early flower (stop by week 3-4 of flower)
- Spray in early morning or just before lights off for best absorption

## Connection to Effective Microorganisms (EM)

LABS is one component of the EM approach developed by Teruo Higa. The original EM1 blend included:
- Lactic acid bacteria
- Purple bacteria
- Yeast
- Actinomycetes
- Fermenting fungi

Higa later revised to: lactic acid bacteria, purple bacteria, and yeast. Homemade LABS replicates the lactic acid bacteria component minus the purple bacteria, which are difficult to culture at home.
