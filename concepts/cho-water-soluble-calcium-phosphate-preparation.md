Water-Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WS-CaPo) is extracted from the bones
of vertebrate animals using [[cho-brown-rice-vinegar-preparation]]. WS-CaPo is an essential substance for plant
growth and is widely distributed in the soil. Calcium phosphate is
normally insoluble in water but soluble in acids, and this property is
exploited in Natural Farming. Calcium phosphate can also dissolve
slightly in water containing CO2.

Phosphoric acid and calcium are both beneficial for plant cultivation.
Their results are said to be effective, but with improved methods of
production and correct usage, the benefits may be seen much faster.

## Materials Needed

1. Animal bones (200 grams): cow, pig, chicken, or fish
2. Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV): 1 liter
3. Clay jar / Glass jar
4. Porous paper (paper towel)
5. Rubber band

## Preparation Steps

### Step 1: Cleaning and Drying Bones

To remove flesh, boil the bones and dry under sunlight. Do not use
raw bones with meat and fat still attached. Clean bones are essential
for proper extraction.

### Step 2: Low-Temperature Charring

Turn the cleaned bones into a charcoal state by burning them at a low
temperature. This process is necessary to burn off organic and fatty
substances that would interfere with the extraction process. Do not
over-burn or use high heat.

**Note**: Cow, pig, or fish bones contain much calcium phosphate.
After burning at low temperature to remove organic substances, they
are placed in vinegar or BRV for extracting the inorganic substance
calcium phosphate.

### Step 3: Extraction in BRV

Use the bone charcoal as-is or pound it lightly (do not pound too
much or it will become powder). Put the bones in the jar with BRV.
Calcium phosphate dissolves from the bones into the vinegar.

### Step 4: Monitoring Completion

Small bubbles will appear during the extraction process. When there
is no more movement or bubbling, it means the process of solution
is completed. This typically takes about 7 days.

## How to Use WS-CaPo

### Dilution Ratio

WS-CaPo is diluted 1:500 to 1:1000 times for crops. A stronger
dilution rate can be used when needed. The solution is then sprayed
on leaves.

### When to Apply

WS-CaPo is used on plants before and after the
[[cho-nutritive-cycle-theory]]:

1. Use after dilution with water. The basic dilution ratio is 1:1000.
2. Spray WS-CaPo on leaves during the periods of cross-over and
   vegetative growth.
3. Use when the crop overgrows, to check excessive vegetative growth.
4. Use when the initial growth is poor, to stimulate early vigor.
5. Use when flower buds have weak differentiation.
6. Use as drinking water (1:500) for livestock that are pregnant or
   ovulating.
7. Can be fed to animals during pregnancy or breeding time.

## Storage Conditions

1. Optimum temperature ranges from 23 to 25 degrees C.
2. Store in a cool and shaded place where no direct sunlight can
   penetrate.

## Relationship to Other Inputs

WS-CaPo occupies a unique position in the Natural Farming input
system because it combines two essential nutrients:

- **Calcium**: Prevents overgrowth, hardens fruit, promotes
  absorption of phosphoric acid, and helps crops accumulate
  nutrients. See [[cho-water-soluble-calcium-and-potassium]].
- **Phosphoric acid**: Substance of the cell nucleus, essential for
  cell division and reproductive growth. See
  [[cho-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid-preparation]].

This combination makes WS-CaPo particularly valuable during the
transition between vegetative and reproductive growth stages in the
[[cho-nutritive-cycle-theory]].

## Alternative Production Method

Fish bones left over from creating
[[cho-fish-amino-acid-preparation]] can be put
into BRV at a volume 10 times larger than the bones. The bones will
decompose and produce good quality water-soluble calcium phosphate.
This recycling approach maximizes the use of all materials.
