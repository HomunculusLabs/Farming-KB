### Fish Food Generation

In raising silver salmon, Stamets found that mature King Stropharia mushrooms thrown into fish-holding tanks would float. Fly larvae soon emerged from the mushrooms. The fish learned to strike the large mushrooms to dislodge swollen larvae into the water. After several days, salmon would excitedly strike at King Stropharia in anticipation. King Stropharia serves as a base medium for generating fish food.

### Soil Building and Bacterial Dependence

King Stropharia depends upon bacteria for growth. It flourishes in gardens devoid of wood debris, benefiting neighboring plants. Acclimated to northern latitudes, it fruits when air temperatures range 60-90F (15-32C), translating to ground temperatures of 55-65F (13-18C). The species thrives in soils supplemented with chopped straw.

### Edibility and Cautions

Excellent edible when young. Edibility quickly declines as mushrooms mature. Fly larvae proliferate inside developing mushrooms. Wood chips from trees along busy roadways should be avoided -- automobile exhaust and asphalt leachate contaminate soil with lead and aluminum, which mushrooms can bioconcentrate.

## Substrate Matrix Concept

The substrate matrix concept was pioneered with King Stropharia. A 50:50 mixture (by volume) of sawdust and chips, of varying particle sizes, provides the ideal habitat. Smaller particles stimulate quick growth ("leap-off"). Larger particles encourage rhizomorph formation, forcefully penetrating between cells. Larger chips become nutritional bases and fruiting platforms, giving rise to super-large mushrooms. This concept resulted in the 5 lb. specimens pictured in the book.
