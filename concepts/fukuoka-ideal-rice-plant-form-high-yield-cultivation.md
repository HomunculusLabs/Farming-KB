With this approach, Fukuoka reported: "I was able to obtain yields of over one

conditions has large heads on a short plant, with only three to four nodes above

Fukuoka described the ideal form as a "sturdy, masculine, short-stalked,
panicle weight type shape"—contrasting it with a "feminine" high head-to-body
ratio. The masculine form concentrates energy in the panicle rather than
expanding vegetative structure.

In his characteristically poetic summary: "Concentrated rice carries a
