## Smartshop Origins

The smartshop model originated in the Netherlands during the late 1990s.
These retail establishments sold psychoactive natural products, including
herbal stimulants, relaxants, and hallucinogenic mushrooms. Dutch tolerance
policy toward soft drugs created a unique regulatory environment where
smartshops could operate openly, establishing a commercial template that
later spread across Europe.

## Dutch Smartshop Market

At peak operation, an estimated 120 to 150 smartshops operated across the
Netherlands. Mushrooms represented approximately 50% of total smartshop
turnover, making them the flagship product of the Dutch smartshop industry.

### Paddo Cabs

A distinctive feature of the Dutch market was the "paddo cab' delivery
service. These specialized vehicles transported mushrooms directly to
customers, demonstrating the commercial sophistication the mushroom market
achieved in the Netherlands.

## Brand Names and User Terminology

The commercial mushroom market developed its own vocabulary and branding:
- **Paddos** (Dutch, from paddestoel = mushroom)
- **Shrooms** (English, most widespread term)
- **Zauberpilze** (German, meaning "magic mushrooms")
- **Mushies** (English colloquial)
- **Boomers** (English slang)
- **Philosopher"s stone** (marketing term for sclerotia/truffles)
- **Teonanacatl** (from Nahuatl, used in ethnobotanical marketing)

These terms reflect both local linguistic traditions and cross-cultural
marketing strategies employed by retailers.
