# Cannabis Reefers and Tincture Preparation

A "reefer" is a marijuana cigarette that has been impregnated with cannabis extract. There are several methods for preparing reefers, each producing a different result in terms of potency, flavor, and burning characteristics.

## Tincture Preparation: Standard Method

Cannabis tincture is an alcohol-based extract:
1. Pulverize cannabis material — flower, trim, or both
2. Soak in alcohol (ethanol or high-proof spirits) in a sealed container
3. Shake daily for 2-4 weeks
4. Strain through filter paper or cheesecloth
5. The resulting green liquid is the tincture
6. Can be used for dipping/injecting reefers, sublingual use, or as a base for edibles

## Green Dragon Variations

The "Green Dragon" is a colloquial term for cannabis tincture. Several preparation variations exist:

- **Room temperature extraction:** The traditional method described above. Produces a full-spectrum extract but requires 2-4 weeks of patience.
- **Heat-assisted extraction:** The sealed jar of cannabis and alcohol is placed in a warm water bath (not exceeding 77°C/170°F) or a slow cooker for several hours. This dramatically reduces extraction time to as little as 1-2 hours but may degrade some terpenes and heat-sensitive cannabinoids. Some practitioners perform multiple short heat cycles of 20-30 minutes each, with cooling periods in between.
- **Decarboxylated Green Dragon:** The cannabis is first decarboxylated by heating at 105-115°C for 30-45 minutes before adding alcohol. This converts THCA to THC, producing a more potent tincture. Without decarboxylation, the tincture will contain primarily non-psychoactive THCA, which provides medical benefits but minimal intoxication.
- **Quick wash method:** A rapid 1-3 minute agitation of cannabis in cold ethanol, then immediate straining. This selectively extracts cannabinoids while leaving behind chlorophyll and waxes, producing a cleaner-tasting product. The tradeoff is somewhat lower total cannabinoid yield.

## Ethanol vs. Glycerin Extraction

The choice of solvent significantly affects the final tincture's properties:

- **Ethanol extraction:** The most efficient and traditional solvent. Ethanol dissolves a broad range of cannabinoids, terpenes, and other cannabis compounds. High-proof grain alcohol (190 proof / 95% ABV) is preferred because water-soluble impurities like chlorophyll are less soluble in pure ethanol. Ethanol tinctures are potent, fast-acting, and shelf-stable for years. The primary drawback is the alcohol content, which some users find unpleasant for sublingual administration. The alcohol can be evaporated partially after extraction to concentrate the tincture.
- **Glycerin extraction:** Vegetable glycerin produces a sweet-tasting, alcohol-free tincture that is easier on the tongue for sublingual use. However, glycerin is a much less efficient solvent — it extracts approximately 30-50% of the cannabinoids that ethanol would recover from the same material. ## Modern Applications

In legal cannabis markets, tinctures have experienced a renaissance as a delivery method:

- **Precision dosing:** Commercial tinctures typically include a measured dropper marked in milliliters, allowing precise dose control. This is particularly valuable for medical patients managing chronic conditions.
- **Ratio formulations:** Products are formulated with specific THC:CBD ratios (1:1, 2:1, 20:1 CBD-dominant) to target different therapeutic outcomes. High-CBD, low-THC tinctures provide anti-inflammatory and anti-anxiety benefits without significant intoxication.
