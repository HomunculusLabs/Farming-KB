# 2C-G

2,5-Dimethoxy-3,4-dimethylphenethylamine

## Overview

2C-G is a unique member of the [[phenethylamine-pharmacology]] as the first example where the phenethylamine (2-carbon) and amphetamine (3-carbon) forms have approximately equal potency -- breaking the usual pattern where amphetamines are significantly more potent. Its extremely long duration (18-30 hours) and insight-enhancing qualities make it both intriguing and impractical for casual use.

## Chemical Information

- Chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-3,4-dimethylphenethylamine
- CAS: 207740-22-5
- Molecular formula: C12H19NO2
- Molecular weight: 209.28 g/mol
- Class: 2C family phenethylamine (unusual 3,4-dimethyl substitution)
- The phenethylamine homologue of GANESHA (2,5-dimethoxy-3,4-dimethylamphetamine)

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Light dose | 20 - 25 mg |
| Common dose | 25 - 32 mg |
| Strong dose | 32 - 35 mg |
| Duration | 18 - 30 hours |
| Onset | 1 - 2 hours |

WARNING: Extremely long duration. At 32 mg, effects can persist for 18+ hours, with complete baseline not returning until the evening of the following day. Plan for a full 48-hour recovery window.

## Effects and Qualitative Commentary

### 22 mg -- Functional Psychedelic
The user remains completely functional -- able to write and answer the telephone -- but with altered [[plant-sensory-perception]] (coffee tastes "most strange"). Mental effects reach only a ++ level. Body retains memory of the experience longer than the mind. Sleep is achievable and desirable in the early evening.

### 32 mg -- "True Psychedelic" / "Insight-Enhancer"
Described as a "superb material, to be classified as a 'true psychedelic' unless one is publishing, in which case it could be best described as an 'insight-enhancer' and obviously of potential value in psychotherapy (if one would wish to spend 30 hours in a therapy session!)." Notable for its anorexic and non-sexual aspects. The user concludes: "The length of the experience is against its frequent use, of course, which is a pity, since this one is well worth investigating as often as possible."

### 32 mg -- Indole-Like Onset
At the very beginning, a "feeling of non-physical heat in the upper back" reminiscent of [[psilocybe-semilanceata-eight-indole-compounds]] (like LSD or psilocybin), "which this ain't." Energy tremor is quite strong throughout, yet the body remains generally at ease.

### 32 mg -- Extended Duration
Plateau at two hours with mild stomach queasiness, persisting at plateau for several more hours. Sleep finally achievable at the 18th hour. Even after sleeping and resuming normal activities the next day, complete baseline doesn't return until that evening. "A couple of days more for what is certainly complete repair. That is a lot of mileage for a small amount of material."

## Extensions and Commentary

### Phenethylamine-Amphetamine Potency Parity

2C-G represents a landmark in Shulgin's thinking about phenethylamine pharmacology. It was the first compound where the 2-carbon (phenethylamine) and 3-carbon (amphetamine) forms showed approximately equal potency in both dosage, duration, and nature of activity. This challenged Shulgin's long-held assumption that phenethylamines are inherently weaker than their amphetamine counterparts.

Shulgin reflected that this assumption was "a totally prejudiced point of view, an outgrowth of my earliest comparisons of mescaline and TMA." He proposed an alternative framing: "It is equally valid to think of the phenethylamines as the prototypes, and that the amphetamines are somewhat stronger than the corresponding phenethylamines." This shift in perspective -- from asking what makes phenethylamines different to asking what makes amphetamines different -- reflects the deeper pharmacological insights that emerged from systematic exploration.

### The G-Series

2C-G is the first of the 2C-G series, which features the unusual 3,4-dimethyl substitution pattern rather than the typical 2,5-dimethoxy-4-X pattern. Subsequent members include 2C-G-3, 2C-G-4, 2C-G-5, and 2C-G-N, each with different alkyl group modifications.

## Synthesis Summary

Synthesized from 2,3-xylenol via methylation to 2,3-dimethylanisole, oxidation to 2,3-dimethyl-4-methoxybenzaldehyde (mp 55-55.5 C), Baeyer-Villiger oxidation to the phenol, re-methylation to 2,3-dimethyl-1,4-dimethoxybenzene (mp 78-79 C), and Vilsmeier formylation to 2,5-dimethoxy-3,4-dimethylbenzaldehyde (mp 59-60 C). Condensation with nitromethane gives the nitrostyrene (mp 120-121 C) which exists in both yellow and tomato-red crystalline forms with identical spectra. Reduction with LAH/H2SO4 in THF gives the free base (bp 110-120 C at 0.2 mm/Hg), converted to HCl salt (mp 232-233 C) as "beautiful white crystals."

## Comparison to Related Compounds

## Harm Reduction Notes

- EXTREMELY LONG DURATION (18-30 hours). This is one of the longest-acting phenethylamines known. Clear your schedule for at least 48 hours.
- Complete baseline may not return until 2+ days after dosing. Plan recovery time.
- Energy tremor persists throughout the experience despite general physical ease.
- Anorexic effects -- eat well before dosing.
- The compound's psychotherapy potential is noted but the 30-hour duration makes therapeutic use impractical in most settings.
- Milligram-accurate scales essential (20-35 mg active range).
