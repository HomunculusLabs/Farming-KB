## Genetic Considerations

### Hybrid Vigor

Crossing genetically distinct parents (such as a sativa and an indica) typically produces offspring with hybrid vigor, also known as heterosis. These F1 hybrids often grow faster, produce more resin, and show greater disease resistance than either parent. However, this vigor typically diminishes in subsequent generations as the hybrid is inbred.

### Trait Heritability

Not all cannabis traits are equally heritable. Some traits that breeders commonly select for:

- **Flowering time**: Highly heritable, tends to express in predictable patterns
- **Plant height**: Moderately heritable, influenced by multiple genes
- **Cannabinoid profile**: Complex genetic basis, but high-THC traits tend to be dominant
- **Terpene profile**: Highly complex, influenced by many genes and [[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] Kush varieties simultaneously, creating combinations that would have been impossible in the era of regional isolation.
