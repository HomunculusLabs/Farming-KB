## Salt-Tolerant Plants in Permaculture

Salt-tolerant (halophytic) plants are essential for permaculture systems in coastal areas, saline soils, and arid regions where salt accumulation is a problem. These plants enable productive land use where conventional crops would fail, and many serve additional functions including forage, timber, honey production, [[atriplex-halimus]]* — tree saltbush
- *Atriplex rhagodioides*
- *Atriplex undulata*
- *Atriplex vesicaria* — bladder saltbush

## Bluebushes (Maireana spp.)

### Cotton Bush (*Maireana aphylia*)
A much-branched perennial shrub reaching 1 m in height and width. It prefers clay and clay loam soils. The cotton bush is an Australian native adapted to arid and semi-arid conditions.

### Black Bluebush / Shrubby Bluebush (*Maireana pyramidata*)
Growing to a height of nearly 2 m, this species is confined to calcareous red and red-brown soils. It provides valuable forage with crude protein content of 15.1–22.0% and digestibility of 58%.

### Pearl Bluebush (*Maireana sedifolia*)
Another valuable forage species adapted to alkaline, saline conditions in arid Australia.

## Samphires (*Halosarcia* spp.)

Native to waterlogged saltlands of Western Australia, samphires are succulent halophytes that thrive in conditions lethal to most plants. They are important components of saltmarsh ecosystems and can be used for land rehabilitation.

## Salt-Tolerant Trees

### Athel Tamarisk (*Tamarix articulata*, syn. *Tamarix aphylia*)
A vigorous, bushy, pine-like tree that tolerates most soils in frost-free districts with a minimum of 350 mm rainfall. **WARNING**: most tamarisks cause salinisation of the upper soil. Their extensive roots extract all soluble salts from the soil, excrete the salts on the leaves, and return them to the topsoil with litter fall. They reduce crop yields for up to 50 m. This is an important consideration when siting tamarisk in permaculture designs.

## Sissoo (*Dalbergia sissoo*)

A fast-growing leguminous tree reaching 30 m, which adapts well to semi-arid conditions (may only reach 10–15 m without irrigation). Key characteristics:

- **Temperature range**: Below 0°C to nearly 50°C; considered frost hardy
- **Rainfall**: 500–2,000 mm; tolerates arid conditions
- **Altitude**: Sea level to 1,500 m
- **Soils**: Alluvial soils along river banks and stream beds; does not grow well on heavy clay
