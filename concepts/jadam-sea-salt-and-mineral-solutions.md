# JADAM Sea Salt and Mineral Solutions

Mineral supplementation is a cornerstone of JADAM soil management. Sea water (or sea salt) and phyllite rock powder provide the broad mineral spectrum that crops need. JADAM considers mineral diversity as essential as microbial diversity for healthy soil.

## Sea Water and Sea Salt

### Why Sea Water

Sea water contains all elements that make up the earth -- not only Mg, Ca, K, and P essential for crop growth, but also I, Mn, Mo, Co, Se, Ge and many trace minerals. About 100 million marine microorganisms live in 1 mL of sea water. Combined with JADAM [[jadam-microorganism-solution-culturing]] (JMS), this provides diversity from both land and sea.

### Application Rate

- 20 L (5.28 gal) of sea water per 0.1 ha (1/4 ac), or equivalently 600 g (1.3 lb) of sea salt
- Sea water must be diluted 30-100x with plain water (sea water is ~3% salt; plant body fluid is ~1%)
- 20 L sea water equals approximately 500 g (1.1 lb) of sea salt
- Apply 3-4 times per month mixed with JMS

### Benefits

- Replenishes minerals leached from soil
- Improves sweetness, coloring, storage period, and product value
- Contains S, Mg, Ca, K, B, P, I (essential for growth) plus Se and Ge (highly beneficial)
- [[ultra-low-cost-foliar-application-methods]] of JMS + sea water eliminates [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]]
- Even the Korean National Institute of Agricultural Sciences confirms benefits

### Cautions

- Never sprinkle salt directly onto soil or spray undiluted sea water
- Do not dilute less than 30x (salinity too strong, can damage crops)
- Salinity of sea water varies by region (normally 3.1-3.8%)
- "Good and bad are one" -- excess of a good thing becomes bad

### Historical Evidence

Fields, rice paddies, and orchards by the sea have been showered with sea water elements for hundreds and thousands of years. They produce better quality food than inland farms. Farms near the sea historically had superior soil mineral content.

## Phyllite Solution

### What is Phyllite

Phyllite is a sedimentary rock formed from the accumulation of flora and fauna over geological time. Higher pressure and temperature convert it to metamorphic rock and eventually granite. Rocks made from organic accumulation contain materials closest to the mineral requirements of plants.

### Preparation

1. Add 60 kg (132 lb) of fine-powdered phyllite to 500 L (132 gal) of water
2. Stir and let settle for a few hours
3. Scoop out the upper water for use ("phyllite solution")
4. Refill water when depleted; reuse for one year
5. For serious [[jadam-natural-mineral-leaching-and-replenishment]], sprinkle phyllite powder directly on soil at 1 kg (2.2 lb) per 3.3 sq meters (36 sq ft)

### Benefits

- Contains nearly all inorganic nutrients required by plants
- Inexpensive and widely available
- Combined with sea water, the effect is dramatic
- Replenishes mineral diversity in depleted soils

### Alternatives

If phyllite is not available, other rock powders can be used. The key is to use rocks derived from organic accumulation, as their mineral composition most closely matches plant requirements.

## The Core Technology

JADAM's most important soil management instruction: "Apply JADAM [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture]] solution (JMS), sea water, and phyllite solution every time you water." This is the path to:
- Strong root settlement
- High quality and high yield
- Reversal of mineral deficiency
- Breaking down of insolubilized nutrients in soil

### Expected Results

Crops will show visible improvements in growth and root settlement. Taste, aroma, and market value of fruits will soar. However, the method is so powerful it can suddenly break down insolubilized nutrients and cause water sprouts or delay fruit ripening. JADAM recommends starting before transplanting or growth period.

## Mineral Deficiency Philosophy

Modern science cannot precisely determine which mineral is deficient or in what quantity. It cannot clearly distinguish between insolubilized and solubilized nutrients. JADAM's approach mirrors human dietary wisdom: rather than trying to identify individual mineral deficiencies, recover mineral diversity and balance through a wholesome approach. Sea water plus phyllite provides that wholesome mineral spectrum.
