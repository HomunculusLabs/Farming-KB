## Fungi on Sporocarps in Nature

### Hypomyces Complex

The most commonly observed mycoparasitic ascomycete genus on wild mushrooms:

- Approximately 55 species described
- Anamorphs in Cladobotryum and several aleurioconidial genera
- Comprehensive descriptions available for species growing on:
  - Discomycetes (5 species)
  - Boletes (10 species)
  - Aphyllophorales (23 species)
  - Agarics (13 species)
- Anamorphs often more strongly differentiated than teleomorphs,
  facilitating identification

### Boletus parasiticus

Consistently fruits on sporocarps of Scleroderma species, particularly S.
citrinum. May be parasitic or merely coincidental fruiting stimulated by the
host. Observed in association with Scleroderma mycorrhiza in Picea abies.
