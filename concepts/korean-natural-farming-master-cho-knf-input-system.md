## Korean Natural Farming — Master Cho"s KNF Input System

Korean Natural Farming (KNF), developed by Master Han-Kyu Cho, is a system of sustainable agriculture that uses locally-sourced, naturally-fermented inputs to build soil health, enhance plant vitality, and reduce or eliminate dependence on external chemical inputs. The core principle: strengthen plants through their own natural vitality rather than attacking [[mushroom-pests-and-diseases]] with toxins.

## The KNF Philosophy

KNF operates on the principle that **healthy plants grown in living soil do not get sick**. Rather than treating symptoms (diseases, pests, nutrient deficiencies), KNF focuses on building robust ecosystems where:
- [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms]] (IMO) create living, fertile soil
- Plants are fed fermented plant and mineral extracts that enhance their own immune systems
- The farm ecosystem is self-sustaining with minimal external inputs

## Core KNF Inputs

### 1. Indigenous Microorganisms (IMO)

IMO are collected from the local environment — they are organisms that have evolved to thrive in that specific region and soil type. They purify soil water, decompose organic matter, catalyze chemical processes, revitalize ecosystems, and suppress diseases.

**IMO Collection Stages:**

| Stage | Name | Method | Duration |
|-------|------|--------|----------|
| IMO #1 | Collection | Bury hard-cooked rice in bamboo grove/leaf mold, collect white aerobic fungi | 4–10 days (season-dependent) |
| IMO #2 | Foundation Stock | Mix IMO #1 with brown sugar (1:1), ferment in clay jar at 23–25°C | 7+ days |
| IMO #3 | Pure Stock | Mix IMO #2 with rice bran (1:500–1:000 dilution), stack 35–40cm on soil floor | ~7 days |
| IMO #4 | Final Product | Mix IMO #3 with field soil + mountain soil (1:1), ferment with nutrients | 4–5 days |

**Collection Guidelines:**
- Collect near roots of bamboo, broadleaf trees, or lawn grass (plants whose roots contain sugar)
- Prefer slightly barren areas; avoid nutrient-rich zones
- South-facing sites: effective for summer crops; North-facing: winter crops
- Collect at altitude 150–200m higher than the field
- Avoid rainy season and excessively moist areas
- White colonies = success; red/blue mold = anaerobic — try again if predominant

**Application:**
- Scatter IMO #4 over field (minimum 150kg/ha), barely covering topsoil
- Apply 7 days before seeding (normal soil) or 14 days before (successive cropping disorder)
- Apply 2–3 hours before sunset
- Can be liquidized (1:500–1,000 dilution) for seedling treatment and foliar application
- Hay bacillus ([[bacillus-subtilis]]) from rice stumps prevents damping-off and Fusarium wilt

### 2. Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN)

Made from five herbs, OHN increases plant robustness, sterilizes, and keeps plants warm.

**Ingredients:**
- Gigantic angelica root (*Angelica gigas*) — used 2× amount of others
- Cinnamon bark
- Licorice root (*Glycyrrhiza glabra*)
- Garlic (raw, crushed with skins)
- Ginger (raw, crushed, unwashed)
- Brown sugar, Makgeolli (Korean rice wine), Soju (Korean distilled liquor, 30–35%)

**Preparation:**
1. Soak dry herbs in Makgeolli until fully wet (1–2 days)
2. Add brown sugar equal to ingredient weight; ferment 4–5 days (fill jar 2/3)
3. Fill remaining 1/3 with Soju; stir clockwise daily for 2 weeks
4. Filter and store in dark container at 1–15°C
5. Herbs can be rebrewed 4–5 times (total ~330L from full recipe)

**Dilution ratio:** 1:1,000
**Mix ratio:** Angelica 2 : Licorice 1 : Cinnamon 1 : Garlic 1 : Ginger 1

### 3. Fermented Plant Juice (FPJ)

Extracts the essence, chlorophyll, lactic acid bacteria, and yeast from plants through [[fungal-fruiting-body-mechanics-osmotic-pressure]] using brown sugar.

**Best Plant Materials:**
- **Japanese mugwort** (*Artemisia princeps*) — vitality, cold/heat resistance, iron; apply in spring to revitalize plants after dormancy
- **Dropwort** (*Oenanthe javanica*) — iron, manganese, blood circulation, nervous system stimulation
- **Bamboo shoots** — rapid growth hormones; fast fermentation (~5 days)
- **Kudzu vine** (*Pueraria lobata*) — fast fermentation
- **False acacia flowers** (*[[robinia-pseudoacacia]]*) — promotes flower bud formation, increases fruit sugar
- **Seaweeds** — growth reinforcement, fruit color enhancement
- **Thinned-out fruits** — rich in gibberellins, thicker foliage
- **Same-crop materials** — tomato lateral buds, squash/sweet potato vines, outer cabbage leaves

**Collection Rules:**
- Collect just before sunrise (maximum nutrients, dew present)
- Snap growing points quickly; if slow, dip in FPJ 1:500 to prevent nutrient escape
- Avoid excessive sunshine or rainfall periods; pick 2–3 days after rain
- Do not wash ingredients (preserves surface lactic acid bacteria)
- Use only one plant type per container

**Preparation:**
1. Mix plant material with brown sugar (1:1 to 1:0.5 ratio by weight)
2. Press with stone weight, cover with porous paper
3. After 12 hours, remove stone (materials should fill 2/3 of jar)
4. Ferment at 23–25°C for 7 days
5. Filter and store with added brown sugar; darker glass container

**Fermentation stages:** Recognition → Harmony → Fermentation → Affection (stabilization) → Natural farming material

### 4. Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV)

Made from fermented brown rice, BRV adjusts pH and enhances nutrient absorption.

### 5. Fish Amino Acid (FAA)

Protein-rich input from fermented fish waste. Provides nitrogen and amino acids for plant growth.

### 6. Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB)

Collected from rice wash water, LAB suppresses harmful microorganisms and aids decomposition.

### 7. Water Soluble Calcium (WSC)

Made from eggshells or sea shells in brown rice vinegar. Strengthens plant cell walls.

### 8. Water Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WSCP)

Made from animal bones and brown rice vinegar. Essential for flowering and fruiting.

### 9. Water Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WSPA)

Phosphorus source for root development and energy transfer.

### 10. Water Soluble Potassium (WSK)

Potassium source including flower mycelium for enhanced flower/fruit production.

### 11. Fermented Mixed Compost

Combines IMO #4 with oil cake, fowl droppings, pig/cow manure, fish meal, and commercial organic fertilizer (1:10 ratio). Ferment 7–14 days before use. Add cow bone powder, shrimp/crab shell for calcium.

## Key KNF Principles

1. **Use indigenous microorganisms** — locally adapted, resilient
2. **Ferment, don't synthesize** — all inputs are naturally fermented, not chemically manufactured
3. **Strengthen the plant, not kill the pest** — robust plants naturally resist disease
4. **Minimal external inputs** — most materials are free or very low cost
5. **Respect the 2/3 rule** — fill containers 2/3 with materials, leave 1/3 for air/water/heat (the "3 chi")
6. **Follow seasonal rhythms** — collect materials at their peak vitality
7. **Dilution ratios matter** — precise dilution (1:500, 1:1,000) prevents plant damage
