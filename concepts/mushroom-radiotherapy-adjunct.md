## Mushroom Radiotherapy Adjunct Use

## Overview

Mushroom-derived polysaccharides have shown significant potential as
adjuncts to radiotherapy in cancer treatment. Radiotherapy, while
effective at eradicating loco-regional disease, invariably damages the
patient's immunological defenses and produces significant side effects.
[[cancer-immune-modulation-mechanisms]].

### PSK and Radiotherapy

PSK has been studied in combination with radiotherapy in multiple
settings. In non-controlled, retrospective analyses of combined
radiation, chemotherapy, and immunotherapy (using PSK or OK-32),
results have shown prolonged survival, especially for patients with
gastric and colorectal carcinomas.
