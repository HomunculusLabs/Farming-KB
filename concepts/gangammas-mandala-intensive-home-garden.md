## Gangamma's Mandala Intensive Home Garden

Gangamma's Mandala is a sophisticated permaculture design for small, intensively-planted home gardens capable of feeding a family of five year-round. Named after a Karnataka (India) permaculture design graduate, the system draws on the East-West Institute's work in Hawaii and the Samaka gardens of the Philippines, arranged in a permaculture "least-path" layout. Despite its simplicity and speed of construction, its underlying design is remarkably sophisticated.

## The Central Banana Circle

The mandala begins at the center of a 100 square metre (1,075 square foot) or larger area. A circle 2 meters (6 feet) across is described and excavated to a dish shape, ridged on the perimeter, about 0.6–1 meter (2–3 feet) deep from hollow to rim. This becomes the banana/sweet potato/papaya circle garden.

### Construction Process

1. The whole circle is covered with wet paper, wet cardboard, banana leaves, or any mulch material
2. The hollow is filled (or over-filled as a dome) with rough mulch: short logs, coarse twigs, hay, rice husks, sawdust, or any humus-creating materials
3. A scatter of manure, ash, lime, dolomite, or fertilizer is added
4. If stones are available, they are banked to the outside of the rim

### Planting the Center

The rim is planted with:
- 4–5 papaya (tall variety)
- 4 or so bananas (dwarf types)
- 8–10 sweet potato
- If available, yams or taro can be placed inside the rim
- Later, beans climb the papaya and banana stalks

A grid or platform of wood over the mulch transforms the center into an outside shower or wash-up area, integrating household water use with garden irrigation. Greywater from washing directly nourishes the dense planting.

## The Keyhole Bed System

### The Sunken Path

A circular sunken path 0.6–1 meter (2–3 feet) wide is covered with sawdust or gravel around the central circle. Off this path, 5–6 "keyholes" or indentations are made, giving the system its mandala-like appearance in plan view.

### Bed Construction

Around each keyhole, a bed 1.5–2 meters (5–6 feet) wide is constructed:
1. Edge-banked with soil 10–20 cm (4–8 inches) high to prevent water run-off
