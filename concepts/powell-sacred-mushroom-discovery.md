## Eunice Pike's Testimony

Through associates, the Wassons corresponded with Eunice Pike, an
American linguistic student who had lived among Mazatec Indians in
Huautla, Mexico for over fifteen years. Pike reported that Indians
referred to the mushroom as a gift from Jesus, "no less than the blood
of Christ." They said it helped "good people" while killing "bad
people" or making them crazy. Indians claimed Jesus spoke to them in
the "bemushroomed" state, and everyone Pike asked agreed they were
"seeing into Heaven itself" through the mushroom.
