# 2C-T-2 (PIHKAL Entry #40)

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthiophenethylamine

## Chemical Classification

- Family: [[mdma-compound-profile]].

## Potential for Misidentification

2C-T-2 has occasionally been called, simply, T-2. This abbreviated nickname
has also been used for T-2 Toxin, a mycotoxin of the Trichothecene group,
formed mainly by Fusarium spp. This is the infamous "warfare agent" in
Southeast Asia, which was finally identified as bee feces rather than a
Soviet military adventure. T-2 and 2C-T-2 are radically different compounds.

## Tweetio Homologues

All three Tweetio homologues of 2C-T-2 have been synthesized and evaluated:

- 2CT2-2ETO (2-ethoxy-4-ethylthio-5-methoxyphenethylamine): 50 mg orally
  was completely effective. Effects felt very quickly with blurred vision,
  intense eyes-closed visuals, and a pleasant contemplative mood. Baseline
  in 5-6 hours. Nasal administration: typical dose approximately 10 mg with
  considerable individual variation.

- 2CT2-5ETO (5-ethoxy-4-ethylthio-2-methoxyphenethylamine): 20 mg produced
  a slow, gentle climb to full effect at the 3rd-4th hour. Flooding of
  thoughts and easy conversation lasted for many hours; sedative sometimes
  needed at 16 hours. A feeling of being drained persisted for 1-2 days.
  Some intoxication noted on the second day. Nicknamed "forever yours."

- 2CT2-2,5DIETO (2,5-diethoxy-4-ethylthiophenethylamine): 10-50 mg range
  showed progressively longer duration with similar intensity. At 50 mg:
  nervousness and edginess initially, then energy and high attentiveness.
  Few sensory alterations. No negative aftereffects. Duration approximately
  9 hours.

## Common Precursor

2,5-Dimethoxythiophenol, prepared from 2,5-dimethoxybenzenesulfonyl chloride
via zinc dust reduction, is the key common precursor to all members of the
2C-T family. This valuable intermediate is prepared by chlorosulfonation of
1,4-dimethoxybenzene followed by zinc reduction in sulfuric acid.
