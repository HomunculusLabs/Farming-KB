## Traditional Preparation Notes

From Amaringo's detailed account of ayahuasca preparation:

- **Pot material**: Must be clay, not aluminum or other metals — essential for the purge to be effective
- **Chacruna placement**: Sun-receiving side placed downward, "spurs" upward
- **Layering**: Ayahuasca vine crushed and layered with chacruna leaves
- **Water**: Filled to top of pot
- **Tobacco**: A handful added to make the mixture boil
- **Solitude**: No one may see or speak to the preparer
- **Icaro**: The vegetalista whistles an icaro and blows on the pot before adding chacruna
- **Admixtures**: Add one plant at a time; "it is not always good to mix in so many things"
- **Boiling duration**: 3-4 hours across multiple pots; juice periodically transferred
- **Quantity**: 12-15 pieces of ground ayahuasca vine; two handfuls of chacruna leaves

## Omen System

The chicua bird (Piaya cayana) serves as a living oracle when harvesting ayahuasca:
- "Chis, chis" — favorable; the vine can be taken
- "Chicua" — danger; must leave it for another day
- "Chis, chis, chicua" — things will start well but go wrong afterward
