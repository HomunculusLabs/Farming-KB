# [[hashish]] Preparation and Cannabis Products

Beyond crude extraction oil, [[cannabis-alchemy-book]] encompasses a range of refined products — from traditional hashish preparations to intensified flowers, oral capsules, vaporization techniques, translucent [[honey-oil]], and infused cigarettes. Each product represents a different approach to delivering the psychoactive constituents of cannabis, with varying potency, onset characteristics, and [[cannabis-methods-of-consumption]].

## Hashish from Extracted Oil

Hashish can be prepared from extracted cannabis oil at any stage of refinement. The potency of the final product depends on two factors:

1. **The potency of the oil itself** — whether it has been purified, isomerized, and/or acetylated
2. **The ratio of oil to powdered marijuana** — the blending proportion

### Oil-to-Powder Ratios

| Oil Content | Consistency | Analog |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **15% minimum** | Powdery | Moroccan or Lebanese hash |
| **50–60%** (~equal parts) | Sticky, pliable | Nepalese/Indian [[charas]] |

Fifty to sixty percent oil represents the maximum amount that can be used while still maintaining hashish consistency. Beyond this, the product becomes too soft to handle as traditional hashish.

### Preparing the Marijuana Powder

The finest possible powder is essential for quality hashish:

1. Clean, very dry marijuana is pulverized in a **high-speed blender**
2. Material may be dried in a preheated oven (250°F) for fifteen-minute intervals between blending
3. A very fine dust collects on the blender top during processing
4. This dust is sifted through a piece of **nylon stocking** or a very fine mesh screen

### Removing Taste and Smell Compounds

Many times the taste of the hashish is improved by removing the oils that give marijuana its characteristic flavor and aroma from the dust:

1. Extract the powder with alcohol using the stew-pot apparatus
2. Remove all solvent from the dust first (fumes might present a fire hazard in subsequent steps)
3. Boil the dust in water to further extract taste/smell compounds
4. Filter the water from the dust and repeat with fresh water until the water remains **clear** — indicating all soluble substances have been leached
5. Thoroughly dry the cannabis dust

### Mixing and Forming

The oil and powder are combined by:

1. Heating both the dust and oil
2. Working them together in a **large mortar**, or kneading by hand
3. Forming into traditional shapes:
   - **Thin, flat, hand-pressed patties** (Afghanistan style)
   - **Clumps of "fingers"** or **round "temple balls"** (Nepal style)
   - **Flat sheets and blocks** by pressing between two heated steel plates in a vise

## Increasing Potency of Intact Marijuana Flowers

A specialized technique allows whole cannabis flowers to be enhanced far beyond their natural potency — up to **twelve times their original strength**.

### Process Overview

1. [[cannabis-potency-enhancement-intact-flowers]] are placed in a **cheesecloth bag** and refluxed in the standard manner
2. The extracted oil is purified with [[petroleum-ether]], then **isomerized and acetylated**
3. The tars and resins remaining from the ether extraction (dissolved in the alcohol/water layer) are recovered:
   - Alcohol is evaporated and collected
   - Water is evaporated in an oil bath at 220°F
4. The recovered tars and resins are mixed with the intensified, purified oil
5. The combined product is dissolved in the **exact amount of alcohol** that the completely dry flowers will absorb

### Determining Alcohol Absorption Capacity

- Clean alcohol is added to the dried flowers incrementally
- The point at which the flowers absorb no more alcohol, with **none lying in the bottom** of the pan, determines the saturation volume
- This amount of alcohol is used to dissolve the intensified oil and tars/resins

### Re-Saturating the Flowers

1. Using an **oven-baster-type syringe**, the flowers are equally saturated with the oil-bearing solvent
2. The saturated flowers are placed in the distillation apparatus and all solvent is removed and collected
3. A small amount of **water is sprayed** on the flowers (steam iron or Sears wrinkle remover works well)
4. Flowers are placed in an oven preheated to 250°F and then **turned off**
5. Since solvent evaporates at a much lower temperature than water, when the flowers begin to dry out, no traces of solvent remain
6. The flowers are now coated with intensified oil at over twelve times original potency

## Oil Capsules (Pot Pills)

Capsules for oral ingestion are prepared by:

1. Mixing the purified oil with an **equal amount of butter**
2. Butterfat carries the oil through the membranes of the stomach and intestine (lipid-based absorption enhancement)
3. The oil/butter mixture is cut into two volumes of:
   - Marijuana powder, or
   - Parsley, or
   - Lactose, or
   - Any edible inert powder
4. The mixture is stuffed into **large gelatin capsules**

The butter serves as a critical carrier — cannabinoids are highly lipophilic and require dietary fat for efficient gastrointestinal absorption.

## Smoking Oil by Direct Vaporization

### Glass Oil Pipe (Vapor Pipe)

- Oil is placed in the glass bowl of a specially designed pipe
- The pipe is heated from below with a flame, similar to opium [[dmt-smoking-technique-and-preparation]]
- Very efficient — very small amounts of oil may be vaporized at one time
- Allows precise dosing

### Aluminum Foil Method

1. A tiny dab of oil is placed on a piece of aluminum foil
2. The foil is heated from below with a match
3. Smoke is inhaled from above the boiling oil through a tube or funnel
4. Essentially the same principle as the glass pipe, but:
   - A new spot may be used each time (no residue buildup)
   - The foil must be pre-heated in a gas flame to burn off any coating that might also vaporize

## Translucent (Honey) Oil

One of the most potent and popular cannabis oil preparations is a thick, translucent, amber oil with the appearance and consistency of dark honey. This is achieved through activated charcoal filtration.

### Production Method

1. Start with purified cannabis oil (from petroleum ether re-extraction)
2. The oil may or may not be isomerized or acetylated before this step
3. Dissolve the oil in **ten times its volume of pure alcohol**
4. Add **granulated activated charcoal** (Norit brand) equal to **half the weight of the oil**
5. Filter the solution through **fine filter paper**
6. Remove the alcohol by evaporation
7. The residue is a **translucent amber oil** — dark honey consistency

The activated charcoal adsorbs the remaining colored impurities (chlorophyll derivatives, oxidation products, and other pigmented compounds) while allowing the cannabinoids to pass through in solution.

## Reefers (Oil-Infused Cigarettes)

The term "reefer" describes a marijuana cigarette that has been impregnated with cannabis extract. Several methods exist:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Working in** | Cannabis oil is mixed directly with the marijuana or tobacco before rolling |
| **Painting** | The rolling paper is painted with oil before the cigarette is rolled |
| **Dipping** | The rolled joint is dipped in tincture of cannabis and allowed to dry |
| **Injecting** | [[cannabis-tincture-preparation]] is injected into the rolled joint with a syringe and allowed to dry |

Each method produces a different distribution of oil within the cigarette, affecting burn characteristics and the evenness of cannabinoid delivery during smoking.
