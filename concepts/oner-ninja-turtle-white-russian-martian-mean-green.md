## Growth Structure and Christmas Tree Form

The plants grow into Christmas tree-shaped beauties, although
they can be bushed out if the grower wishes them to be shorter
or wishes to grow them in a SOG setup. This natural Christmas
tree structure is a hallmark of well-bred sativa-dominant
strains, featuring a strong central cola with graduated lateral
branches that decrease in length toward the base of the plant.

The Christmas tree shape is not just aesthetically pleasing but
also functionally efficient for light distribution. The structure
allows light to penetrate evenly to all bud sites, maximizing
photosynthesis and ensuring that lower branches receive adequate
exposure. For indoor growers, this means a single light source
positioned above the canopy can effectively illuminate the entire
plant without requiring supplemental side lighting.

## SOG Suitability and Training Response

Ninja Turtle is particularly well-suited for SOG (Sea of Green)
growing systems. The plant's structure and growth patterns make
it adaptable to this high-density cultivation method, where many
small plants are grown in close proximity for maximum yield per
square foot of growing space.

Growers who wish to bush out the plants for shorter stature or
SOG arrangements will find that Ninja Turtle responds well to
training techniques. The natural branching can be encouraged or
controlled depending on the grower's preferred method, making the
strain versatile for different cultivation approaches and grow
room configurations.
