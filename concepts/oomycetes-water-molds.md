## Ecological Roles

### Plant Pathogens

Oomycetes include some of the most devastating plant pathogens known. Most famously, *Phytophthora infestans* caused the **Irish Potato Famine** of the 1840s, resulting in approximately one million deaths and mass emigration. Other significant genera include *Phytophthora*, *Pythium*, and *Peronospora*, which cause root rots, damping-off, downy mildews, and blights across a wide range of crop and native plant species.

### Freshwater Saprobes

Many oomycetes function as saprobes in freshwater ecosystems, playing important roles in the decomposition of organic material. Their zoospores allow rapid colonization of submerged substrates, making them significant contributors to [[cannabis-disease-pest-resistance-breeding-clarke]] and chemical control.
