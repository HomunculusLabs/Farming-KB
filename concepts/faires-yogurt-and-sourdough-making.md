## Sourdough

### Principle
- Wild yeasts captured from the air, combined with lactobacillus bacteria
- Only needs flour, water, and time
- Starters can be maintained for 100+ years
- Creates bread with complex flavor and longer shelf life than commercial yeast bread
- Natural leavening replaces commercial yeast entirely
- Sourdough is more digestible than commercial bread because fermentation breaks down gluten and phytic acid

### Creating Starter (5 days)
- Day 1: Mix 1/2 cup any wheat flour + 1-2 tbsp unchlorinated water. Knead into small dough. Put in glass/ceramic (not metal) jar, cover with damp cloth/cheesecloth. Leave 2 days — hard wrinkled crust forms.
- Day 3: Pull crust off, add 1 cup flour + little water. Cover 24 hours.
- Day 4: Remove crust, add 1 cup flour + water. Cover 24 hours.
- Day 5: Dough will have risen. Poke it — hole won't spring back. Starter is ready.
- Simplified: Check every morning for 4 days, add 1 cup flour + ~1/2 cup water each time. Ready by day 5.
- Store in fridge until needed. NEVER use all of it.
- After 1-2 weeks of daily feeding, the starter will be consistently bubbly and active
- The "discard" portion from feedings can be used to make pancakes, waffles, and crackers

### Daily Feeding Routine
- Remove starter from fridge, let come to room temperature
- Discard half (or use in other recipes)
- Feed with equal parts flour and water by weight
- Leave at room temperature for a few hours until bubbly, then return to fridge
- In warm conditions, feed daily; in cool conditions, feeding every 2-3 days may suffice

### Bread Recipe

Ingredients:
- 1 handful starter + 2 cups flour + 2 cups water (sponge)
- 2 cups flour + 1 tbsp salt + 1 cup warm water + little oil (dough)

Process:
1. Morning before needed: mix starter + flour + water, cover with damp towel, leave all day (8-12 hour slow rise)
2. Evening: mix rest of flour, salt, water into nice dough. Knead very well. Let rise overnight in biggest bowl, covered
3. Morning: punch down, divide in half, form two round loaves. Rise 3-6 hours (second rise)
4. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C). Slash tops, spray with water, spray inside oven. Bake ~30 minutes until browned and sound hollow when tapped
5. Cool before cutting

### Baking in a Dutch Oven

For a superior crust, bake inside a preheated Dutch oven:
1. Preheat Dutch oven with lid at 450°F for 30 minutes
2. Transfer shaped dough into hot pot, slash the top
3. Cover and bake 20 minutes (steam creates crust)
4. Remove lid, bake 10-15 more minutes until deeply browned
