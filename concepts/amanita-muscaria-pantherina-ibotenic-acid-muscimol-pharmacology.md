## Dosage and Intoxication

### Active Doses
- **Muscimol**: approximately 6 mg for noticeable effects
- **Ibotenic acid**: 30-60 mg for noticeable effects
- **100 g of dried A. muscaria** contains approximately 180 mg total of these
  compounds, of which ~25 mg may be ibotenic acid

### Time Course
- Onset: within 30 minutes of ingestion
- Peak: 2-3 hours post-ingestion
- Duration: variable; some effects (headache, pupillary dilation) may persist
  for days or weeks

### Intoxication Profile
Effects are variable and dose-dependent, including:

- Mild sedation and euphoria at lower doses
- Visual hallucinations and perceptual distortions
- Slurred, incoherent speech
- Severe nausea and vomiting
- Seizures (rare but documented)
- Transient deep sleep or coma
- Headache persisting for days to weeks
- Notable reddening of the skin (the primary "muscarinic" effect observed)

## The Ibotenic Acid to Muscimol Conversion

A critical practical consideration is that **drying converts ibotenic acid to
muscimol** through decarboxylation:

- Fresh Amanita muscaria contains more ibotenic acid (excitatory, less
  desirable)
- Properly dried specimens contain proportionally more muscimol (sedative,
  primary psychoactive agent)
