# Amazonian Medicinal Plants in Amaringo's Visions

Pablo Amaringo's ayahuasca visions are filled with plant spirits (genies or guardians) who appear inside or alongside their corresponding plants and teach the vegetalista how to use them. Each plant has a spirit that explains its medicinal properties and proper preparation methods. The following plants appear prominently across multiple visions.

## Ayahuasca Additives (Admixtures)

The ayahuasca brew (Banisteriopsis caapi) is frequently combined with other plants, each adding its own spirit and medicinal properties:

- **Chacruna** (Psychotria viridis): Adds light and visions to the brew
- **Clavohuasca** (Tynanthus panurensis): Used for various purposes
- **Bobinzana** (Calliandra angustifolia): Healing properties
- **Ajosquiro** (Gallizia corazema): Produces kapukiri when rotten; also teaches sorcery
- **Motelo huasca** (Bauhinia guianensis): Vine used in healing
- **Albahaca** (Ocimum micranthum): Aromatic, used in multiple remedies
- **Alcanfor** (Zanthoxylum sp.): Aromatic, contributes to visionary experience
- **Toe** (Brugmansia sp.): Powerful additive; also used in sorcery
- **Murachuasca** and **Mureohuasca**: Additional vine additives
- **Altaruna**: Unidentified additive plant
- **Lucero ayahuasca**: Star ayahuasca, with leaves like boats and hummingbirds, carrying people from Antares

## Bark and Wood Medicines

- **Capirona** (Calycophyllum spruceanum): Bark used in treatment of diabetes
- **Muena** (Endlicheria williamsii): Used for snake bites
- **Timareba** (Loetia sp.): Ashes cooked and used in baths for children with poliomyelitis
- **Pajaro bobo / Hiporuro** (Dendrobangia boliviana): For diarrhea and sexual potency
- **Chonta** (various palm species): Used for virotes (magical darts); also provides protective wood
- **Renaco tree**: Queens or mothers of the renaco have interweaving dancing roots
- **Punga tree** (Bombax munguba): Makes the mariri (magical phlegm) stronger

## Leaf and Herb Remedies

- **Achiote** (Bixa orellana): Indispensable in healing any kind of mal aire; leaves used for fanning patients
- **Pucunucho** (Capsicum sp.): Red-fruited chili used to stun snakes during extraction
- **Rocoto** (Capsicum sp.): Also used to stun snakes
- **Maramara**: Unidentified medicinal plant; appears near Sachamama
- **Puka-gramalote**: Red plant whose leaves are gathered by pink dolphins; sorcerers use it to inflict nausea and headaches
- **Sacha-ajos** (Mansoa alliacea): Garlic vine, one of the plants behind the palm trees in vision 21
- **Cumaceba** (Cesalpina equinata): Tree near palm species in shamanic visions
- **Michiquipanga** (Renealmia alpina): Medicinal plant with spirit guardian
- **Santa Maria** (Pothomorpha peltata): Medicinal plant with spirit guardian
- **Una de gato** (Uncaria tormentosa): Cat's claw, medicinal plant with spirit guardian

## Fruit and Seed Medicines

- **Jagua / Huito** (Genipa Americana): Fruit cut in halves, scraped, mixed with warm water for treating boa pregnancy; also used in body painting with piripiri for strength
- **Cacao** (Theobroma cacao): Present in sorcery contexts
- **Macambo** (Theobroma quinquenervia): Present in disease contexts

## Root and Rhizome Medicines

- **Jergon-sacha** (Dracontium loretense, D. longipes): Coiled snake plant that heals snakebites
- **Papastrueno** (Dioscorea sp.): Very difficult to obtain; gives power to control rain, wind, and thunderbolt when ingested with special diet
- **Piripiri** (Cyperus sp.): Mixed with jagua and applied to body for strength; also smoked
- **Yaku-pichana**: Roots purify water, making it good for drinking or preparing medicines
- **Siucacasha**: Sharp spine tree, unidentified

## Special Purpose Plants

- **Raya balsa**: Aquatic plant with round leaves; dried leaves mixed with tobacco and smoked to learn how to enter the underwater realm
- **Shikshi huama** (Dictyoloma Peruvianum): Cures putanerta (calms women's sexual desires); cooked with albahaca. Mixed with camphor and pucunucho for cancerous ulcers.
- **Yaku-yarinilla**: Poultice for rheumatic pain and hemorrhoids
- **Lobo-chupa**: Used when children are magically harmed by dolphins; mixed with cinnamon and burned as smoke to expel mal aire del agua
- **Casha-sacha**: Thorny plant growing on rocks; its genie is used in sorcery to guard victims
- **Huamas** (Eichhornia crassipes): Water hyacinth; intensifies pain of malignant diseases
- **Pushco-yuyo**: Thorny vine used as a guardian during curing
- **Ayahuman** (Couroupita gujanensis): Cannon ball tree; its spirits help cure gastritis and stomach ache by extracting illness with their tongues
- **Ayah-uman** (Couroupita gujanensis): From this plant one can learn both medicine and sorcery

## Geotherapy and Earth Medicines

The spirits taught Amaringo that Egyptians, Mayans, pre-Incas, Incas, and other ancient peoples used earth to cure many illnesses (geotherapy). Flint stones are used in treating pulsano. Earth brought from caves is applied in healing preparations.

## Hydrotherapy

Ceramic jars absorb sun rays and purify water. Drinking from ceramic jars is considered very beneficial. The ancients -- Tiahuanaco, Chanca, Chimu, and Inca cultures -- used water therapy extensively.
