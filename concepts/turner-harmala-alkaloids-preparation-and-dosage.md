# Harmala Alkaloids Preparation and Dosage

## Overview

Harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine) are beta-carboline compounds that function primarily as MAO (monoamine oxidase) inhibitors. They are found in several plants, most notably [[cashinahua-banisteriopsis-use]] (the Ayahuasca vine) and [[syrian-rue-peganum-harmala-ethnobotany]] (Syrian Rue). Their main psychedelic value lies not in their own mild psychoactivity but in their ability to potentiate other psychedelics, particularly DMT-containing plants.

## Plant Sources and Alkaloid Content

### Banisteriopsis caapi (Ayahuasca Vine)
- Native to the Amazon region of South America
- Primary active ingredient of traditional Ayahuasca and Yage brews
- Bark contains between 0.2% and 1.3% mixed harmala alkaloids
- Must normally be procured in the Amazon jungle or from specialty herb dealers
- See also: [[harmala-alkaloids-and-ayahuasca-preparation]]

### Peganum harmala (Syrian Rue)
- The most concentrated natural source of harmala alkaloids
- Seeds contain 2-7% mixed harmala alkaloids (significantly higher than B. caapi)
- Grows wild in Africa, the Middle East, India, South America, Mexico, and the Southern U.S.
- A hardy, invasive plant that will take over vegetation if planted in the same area
- Some seed dealers will not ship to California due to "noxious weed" laws
- Available from specialty seed and herb dealers, and occasionally as dye stock

## Dosage

### Potentiating Dose
- Approximately 3 grams of Syrian Rue seed (1.25 teaspoons finely ground)
- Acts as a "switch" for potentiating other psychedelics: a certain threshold amount activates the potentiating effect, and larger doses do not increase this effect but only increase physical symptoms (nausea)
- 3 grams provides sufficient potentiation without excessive stomach discomfort

### Intensity Rating
- When taken alone: 1 to 2 on a standard psychedelic intensity scale
- Harmala alkaloids are usually not potent psychedelics by themselves
- Their primary value is as potentiators for other substances

## Preparation Methods

### Capsule Method
1. Grind Syrian Rue seeds in a coffee grinder to a fine powder
2. Pack the powder into gelatin capsules
3. Onset: approximately 2 hours
4. This is the most common preparation method

### Tea Method
1. Boil the ground seed powder for a few minutes
2. Strain and drink the tea
3. Onset: approximately 1 hour (faster than capsules)
4. Taste is quite bitter

### Gradual Consumption Method
- Spread consumption of the 3g dose over about one hour
- Further reduces stomach disturbances compared to taking all at once
- Turner's preferred method in later experiments

### Extraction Method
- Gracie and Zarkov used vodka as a solvent
- The extract offers several advantages over seed powder:
  - Can be smoked for rapid onset (within minutes)
  - Produces a pleasant meditative high with euphoria and relaxation
  - Produces less nausea and physical symptoms for equivalent intensity
- Disadvantage: smoked harmala does not produce as long-lasting effects as ingested harmala, both alone and when used to enhance other psychedelics

## The Harmala Experience Alone

- Generally quite mild as a standalone psychedelic
- Produces feelings of subtle energy flowing
- Slight enhancement of perception
- Some users report full psychedelic effects by consuming small amounts over the course of a day to circumvent nausea
- Smoked concentrated harmala: more mind-oriented, less body-oriented, with euphoria and relaxation
- Described as a nice alternative to cannabis: more meditative and relaxed

## Safety Considerations

- Harmala alkaloids are MAO inhibitors: see [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]] for dietary and drug restrictions
- The harmala alkaloids are emetics and can produce intense nausea in large amounts
- May significantly intensify and possibly be dangerous when combined with phenethylamine-family drugs: ecstasy (MDMA), mescaline, and 2C-B
- Works well and safely with: N,N-DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, LSD, psilocybin, and ketamine

## Combinations and Potentiation Mechanism

Harmala potentiates other psychedelics through two primary mechanisms:
1. Inhibits enzymes (MAO) that normally destroy compounds like DMT in the stomach before they reach the brain
2. Reduces the speed at which these compounds are metabolized within the brain

This makes orally inactive DMT become orally active (the basis of Ayahuasca), and significantly intensifies and extends the effects of smoked DMT. See [[turner-dmt-water-spirit-connection]] for details on DMT potentiation by harmala alkaloids.
