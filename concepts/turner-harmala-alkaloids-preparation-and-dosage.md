     9|# Harmala Alkaloids Preparation and Dosage
    10|
    11|## Overview
    12|
    13|Harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine) are beta-carboline compounds that function primarily as MAO (monoamine oxidase) inhibitors. They are found in several plants, most notably [[cashinahua-banisteriopsis-use]] (the Ayahuasca vine) and [[syrian-rue-peganum-harmala-ethnobotany]] (Syrian Rue). Their main psychedelic value lies not in their own mild psychoactivity but in their ability to potentiate other psychedelics, particularly DMT-containing plants.
    14|
    15|## Plant Sources and Alkaloid Content
    16|
    17|### Banisteriopsis caapi (Ayahuasca Vine)
    18|- Native to the Amazon region of South America
    19|- Primary active ingredient of traditional Ayahuasca and Yage brews
    20|- Bark contains between 0.2% and 1.3% mixed harmala alkaloids
    21|- Must normally be procured in the Amazon jungle or from specialty herb dealers
    22|- See also: [[harmala-alkaloids-and-ayahuasca-preparation]]
    23|
    24|### Peganum harmala (Syrian Rue)
    25|- The most concentrated natural source of harmala alkaloids
    26|- Seeds contain 2-7% mixed harmala alkaloids (significantly higher than B. caapi)
    27|- Grows wild in Africa, the Middle East, India, South America, Mexico, and the Southern U.S.
    28|- A hardy, invasive plant that will take over vegetation if planted in the same area
    29|- Some seed dealers will not ship to California due to "noxious weed" laws
    30|- Available from specialty seed and herb dealers, and occasionally as dye stock
    31|
    32|## Dosage
    33|
    34|### Potentiating Dose
    35|- Approximately 3 grams of Syrian Rue seed (1.25 teaspoons finely ground)
    36|- Acts as a "switch" for potentiating other psychedelics: a certain threshold amount activates the potentiating effect, and larger doses do not increase this effect but only increase physical symptoms (nausea)
    37|- 3 grams provides sufficient potentiation without excessive stomach discomfort
    38|
    39|### Intensity Rating
    40|- When taken alone: 1 to 2 on a standard psychedelic intensity scale
    41|- Harmala alkaloids are usually not potent psychedelics by themselves
    42|- Their primary value is as potentiators for other substances
    43|
    44|## Preparation Methods
    45|
    46|### Capsule Method
    47|1. Grind Syrian Rue seeds in a coffee grinder to a fine powder
    48|2. Pack the powder into gelatin capsules
    49|3. Onset: approximately 2 hours
    50|4. This is the most common preparation method
    51|
    52|### Tea Method
    53|1. Boil the ground seed powder for a few minutes
    54|2. Strain and drink the tea
    55|3. Onset: approximately 1 hour (faster than capsules)
    56|4. Taste is quite bitter
    57|
    58|### Gradual Consumption Method
    59|- Spread consumption of the 3g dose over about one hour
    60|- Further reduces stomach disturbances compared to taking all at once
    61|- Turner's preferred method in later experiments
    62|
    63|### Extraction Method
    64|- Gracie and Zarkov used vodka as a solvent
    65|- The extract offers several advantages over seed powder:
    66|  - Can be smoked for rapid onset (within minutes)
    67|  - Produces a pleasant meditative high with euphoria and relaxation
    68|  - Produces less nausea and physical symptoms for equivalent intensity
    69|- Disadvantage: smoked harmala does not produce as long-lasting effects as ingested harmala, both alone and when used to enhance other psychedelics
    70|
    71|## The Harmala Experience Alone
    72|
    73|- Generally quite mild as a standalone psychedelic
    74|- Produces feelings of subtle energy flowing
    75|- Slight enhancement of perception
    76|- Some users report full psychedelic effects by consuming small amounts over the course of a day to circumvent nausea
    77|- Smoked concentrated harmala: more mind-oriented, less body-oriented, with euphoria and relaxation
    78|- Described as a nice alternative to cannabis: more meditative and relaxed
    79|
    80|## Safety Considerations
    81|
    82|- Harmala alkaloids are MAO inhibitors: see [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]] for dietary and drug restrictions
    83|- The harmala alkaloids are emetics and can produce intense nausea in large amounts
    84|- May significantly intensify and possibly be dangerous when combined with phenethylamine-family drugs: ecstasy (MDMA), mescaline, and 2C-B
    85|- Works well and safely with: N,N-DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, LSD, psilocybin, and ketamine
    86|
    87|## Combinations and Potentiation Mechanism
    88|
    89|Harmala potentiates other psychedelics through two primary mechanisms:
    90|1. Inhibits enzymes (MAO) that normally destroy compounds like DMT in the stomach before they reach the brain
    91|2. Reduces the speed at which these compounds are metabolized within the brain
    92|
    93|This makes orally inactive DMT become orally active (the basis of Ayahuasca), and significantly intensifies and extends the effects of smoked DMT. See [[turner-dmt-water-spirit-connection]] for details on DMT potentiation by harmala alkaloids.
