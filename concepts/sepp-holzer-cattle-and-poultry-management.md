### Dehorning
Holzer strongly opposes dehorning:
- Unbelievably painful for the animals
- Animals act in a completely different and disturbed way afterwards
- Animals butt each other in the stomach, potentially causing premature or stillbirths in pregnant cows
- Animals may store and dispose of harmful substances in claws and horns
- Dehorning, docking tails, and cropping ears are "nothing more than mutilation"
