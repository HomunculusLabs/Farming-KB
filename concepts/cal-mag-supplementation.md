## Cal Mag Supplementation

Calcium (Ca) and magnesium (Mg) are [[query-how-does-no-till-cannabis-cultivation-work]]. Supplementation is often necessary, especially in
hydroponic systems and when using certain growing media.

## Calcium's Role

Calcium is an essential part of plant cell walls and must be present for cell division
and growth. Calcium is relatively immobile in plants -- it does not readily move from
older tissue to newer growth. Once deposited in cell walls, calcium stays there. This
means new growth (growing tips, young leaves) is the first to show deficiency symptoms
when calcium supply is inadequate.

Calcium is classified as an immobile nutrient. Immobile nutrients -- calcium (Ca),
boron (B), chlorine (Cl), cobalt (Co), copper (Cu), iron (Fe), manganese (Mn),
molybdenum (Mo), silicon (Si), and sulfur (S) -- do not translocate to new growing
areas as needed. They remain deposited in their original place in older leaves. This is
the reason [[co2-enrichment-cannabis]] accelerates growth, which increases demand for all nutrients
including calcium and magnesium. CO2-enriched plants may require higher Ca/Mg levels than
non-enriched plants. Monitor [[cannabis-flowering-stage-management]], nitrogen takes a backseat to potassium, phosphorus, and calcium
intake. Using a super bloom fertilizer with less nitrogen and more potassium,
phosphorus, and calcium promotes fat, heavy, dense buds. Calcium demands increase
significantly during flowering as bud structures develop rapidly.
