# Geese and Duck Weed Control

Mollison highlights geese and ducks as specialised weed control agents that
perform functions difficult or impossible to achieve with machinery or
herbicides. Their different feeding behaviours make them complementary rather
than competing species in integrated permaculture systems.

## Geese as Weeders

Geese are primarily grazers, preferring grasses and broadleaf weeds over
most crops. This makes them ideal for weed control in specific contexts:

**Orchard integration:**
- Geese graze grass competition from beneath fruit trees without damaging
  tree bark (unlike sheep or goats which will strip bark from young trees)
- They prefer grasses over most [[greenhouse-cool-climates-mollison]] but less active
- Toulouse geese are docile but less effective as weeders
- Mollison recommends Chinese geese for orchard work and Embden crosses
  for general farm duty

## Ducks as Aquatic Weed Controllers

Ducks serve a complementary role managing aquatic vegetation in ponds,
waterways, and wetland areas:

**Aquatic [[savory-growing-season-and-non-growing-season-management]]
- Muscovy ducks are particularly effective, consuming large quantities of
  aquatic vegetation and insects
