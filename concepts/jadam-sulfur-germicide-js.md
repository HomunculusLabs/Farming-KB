JADAM sulfur (JS), also called red clay-sulfur, is a low-cost sulfur-based germicide developed by Youngsang Cho. It is highly effective against most fungal diseases including powdery mildew, downy mildew, black spot, pear rust, and canker, without requiring heating equipment. Making 1 liter of JS (25% sulfur content) costs only about 50 cents.

## Advantages Over Lime Sulfur

Traditional lime sulfur requires very high heat, takes a long time to make, can damage plastic and steel greenhouse pipes, and is mostly limited to winter dormant season use due to concentration problems during active growth. JS overcomes all these limitations:

- Made without heating (exothermic chemical reaction generates the needed heat)
- Can be made in plastic containers (no steel needed)
- Completed in under 10 minutes of active work (plus 24 hours settling)
- Does not damage greenhouse pipes
- Causes far fewer concentration problems during the growing season
- Can replace lime sulfur for winter dormant sprays

## Ingredients for 100L Batch

- Sulfur: 25 kg (99.9% purity)
- Caustic soda (NaOH): 20 kg (98%)
- Water: 82 L total (50 L first addition + 32 L second)
- Phyllite powder: 0.5 kg
- Red clay powder: 0.5 kg (or very fine rock powder substitute)
- Sea salt: 1.5 kg

Equipment needed: 110L heat-resistant plastic container, 1.2m wooden stick, mask, boots, jacket, gloves, safety goggles.

## Making Process

1. Add sulfur (25 kg), red clay powder (0.5 kg), phyllite powder (0.5 kg), and sea salt (1.5 kg) to the dry container
2. Add caustic soda (20 kg) slowly to minimize dust
3. In a separate container, measure 50L of water (use 54L if temperature exceeds 27C/80F)
4. Pour water in all at once -- extreme heat will be generated
5. Stir slowly and thoroughly with wooden stick (never metal/aluminum)
6. Temperature will exceed 80C (176F) and sulfur will begin melting
7. Keep 1-2L of water handy to add if over-boiling occurs
8. Continue stirring for about 10 minutes until all sulfur is dissolved
9. Add second water (32L, or 28L if first water was 54L)
10. Stir well, close lid, let sit for 24 hours
11. After settling, take out the clear upper portion into thick-walled plastic bottles
12. Do not use bottom sediments; filter through felt if particles remain

## Storage and Safety

- Store in thick-walled plastic bottles, sealed air-tight
- Do not store in the making container (sulfur concentration stratifies)
- Do not use thin-walled water bottles
- Do not store below freezing (can thaw and reuse)
- No expiry date
- Never use aluminum containers
- Strong heat is produced -- always wear full safety gear
- Work in well-ventilated area

## Usage Rates

Standard dilution: 0.5-2L JS per 500L water

- Greenhouses: start at 0.5L, increase in 0.2L increments
- Open fields: start at 1L, increase in 0.2L increments
- Fog machines: 0.1L per 17L
- Maximum: do not exceed 2.5L per 500L

For winter dormant spray on fruit trees: use approximately 5L per 500L before sprouting or leaf fall. During growth period when buds are enlarging: approximately 1L per 500L.

## Key Usage Rules

- Use ONLY when disease has broken out or is imminent -- do not use preventively throughout the season
- Always use with [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] (otherwise JS leaves marks and reduces germicide effect)
- For increased power, raise JWA to over 5L and JS up to 2L per 500L
- Grapes, persimmons, walnuts, and greenhouse crops are sensitive -- increase doses cautiously
- Do not spray at noon (exception: pear rust)
- Always do mixture test and concentration test before combining with commercial pesticides
- Do not mix JWA and JS together directly -- add each separately to water first

## Disease Targets

JS is effective against:
- Powdery mildew (all crops)
- Downy mildew
- Black spot
- Pear rust
- Canker
- Most fungal diseases

## The "Detoxified Sulfur" Scam

JADAM warns against marketing claims for "detoxified sulfur." The sulfur used in JS is already 99.9% pure -- there are no heavy metal toxins (lead, cadmium, chrome, arsenic) to remove. Companies selling "detoxified sulfur" at premium prices are perpetrating fraud.

## Role in JNP System

JS is a core component of the [[jadam-natural-pesticide-jnp]] system. Combined with JWA, it forms "JS germicide." Adding [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] creates "JS-JHS pesticide" for even broader pest and disease control. Rather than increasing sulfur (which risks concentration damage), increase JWA content for stronger effect.
