# Jack the Ripper — Jack's Cleaner × Space Queen (TGA Subcool)

Jack the Ripper is a potent sativa-dominant hybrid created by Subcool and his
Team Green Avengers (TGA Genetics). Named for its "absolutely killer"
potency, this strain combines a P1 of Jack's Cleaner with Space Queen to
produce one of the most powerful sativa-dominant hybrids in modern cannabis
genetics. With a tested THC content of 26.6%, Jack the Ripper ranks among
the highest-potency sativa-dominant strains available.

The strain's genetics trace back through an impressive family tree that includes
Romulan, Lamb's Bread, Pluton, Cindy99 BCGA, [[northern-lights]] #5, Purple Haze,
and Jack Herer — making it almost a living museum of legendary cannabis
genetics.

## Breeder and Genetics

- **Breeder**: Subcool / TGA Genetics / Team Green Avengers, USA
- **Genetics**: Jack's Cleaner P1 × Space Queen
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant
- **Potency**: THC 26.6%
- **Website**: tgagenetics.com

### Lineage Breakdown

The Jack's Cleaner parent itself is a complex hybrid:

- Jack's Cleaner was created by crossing Jack Herer with a strain called The
  Cleaner (bred by Skoosh)
- The Cleaner contained both Northern Lights #5 and Purple Haze genetics
- The Cleaner earned its name from its aroma, which resembled a cleaning
  product called Mr. Clean
- This unique parent proved too exceptional to ignore as a breeding stock

The Space Queen parent contributes additional vigor and complexity to the cross.
The resulting Jack the Ripper inherits the best traits of both parents while
achieving potency levels that exceed either parent individually.

## Growth Characteristics

### Phenotypes

Jack the Ripper expresses two main phenotypes:

1. **Larger phenotype**: Taller stature, tends toward pine-scented buds
2. **Shorter phenotype**: More compact, similar lemon-haze aroma to the larger
   pheno but without the pine undertone

Both phenotypes share the characteristic Lemon Haze aroma that defines the
strain's [[cannabis-terpene-profile]]. The primary distinguishing feature between phenos is
the pine scent and the height difference.

### Cultivation Method

The best approach to growing Jack the Ripper is **untopped**:

- Allow the plant to develop its natural structure without topping
- Provide a good long vegetative period so the plant can grow into its full
  bushy form
- Organic soil grow is recommended as the ideal cultivation method
- The strain does not require heavy nutrient feeding — it performs well with
  moderate nutrition programs
- Hydroponic cultivation is also viable, though organic soil is preferred for
  flavor development

### Flowering and Harvest

After flipping to 12/12 flowering:

- Bud production becomes visible from approximately **day 12** of flowering
- The emerging buds are **spear-shaped** and white, developing density
  [[query-how-do-i-maximize-terpene-and-resin-production-in-cannabis]] through the [[cannabis-photoperiod-flowering-cycle]]
- Indoor harvest: approximately **week 8** of flowering (some phenotypes may
  run slightly longer)
- Outdoor harvest: late September to early October
- Yield: **moderate to heavy** depending on phenotype and growing conditions
- Trimming requirements are minimal — the bud structure is relatively clean

### Height Management

None of the Jack the Ripper phenotypes will grow excessively tall unless given
extended outdoor vegetative periods. This makes the strain manageable in indoor
environments, though providing adequate vertical space during the vegetative
phase is still important for maximizing yield.
