## Kumquats

Kumquats (Fortunella japonica) are dwarf evergreen trees that produce
good crops of miniature oranges. They are among the hardiest citrus
and can be grown outside provided the temperature will not drop lower
than 15 degrees F (-10 degrees C). Two suitable varieties are
available, Marumi and Nagami. They are always grown as bush trees.
Plants are available balled or container-grown so can be planted at
any time of year. Plant them 15ft (4.5m) apart to avoid competition.
After harvesting, cut back the current season's growth. Harvest when
the fruits begin to turn orange. They can be eaten raw or made into
marmalade. Watch for gall wasp, little leaf, and lemon scab as well
as red spider mite, aphids, and scale insects.
