A synergy between at least four kingdoms, fungus, plant, bacterium, and animal, denatures toxins into derivatives useful to myriad species and fatal to few. ## Multi-Kingdom Synergy

When working with fungi on toxic waste sites, it soon becomes clear
that many other organisms are being affected. The introduction of a
single fungus, for instance oyster mycelium, into a nearly lifeless
landscape triggers a cascade of activity by other organisms. Bacteria
use rotting mushrooms as a rich base for growth, further freeing
nutrients and releasing cascades of microbes that destroy the
structure of mushrooms as they melt into the soil.

This bacterial influx predisposes habitats for the emergence of plant
communities. Ultimately, nature fosters complex partnerships of
interdependence, with fungi blazing the path to ecological recovery.
Once these predecessor organisms are engaged, nature will steer the
habitat on the path toward self-healing. To maintain the site over
time, you may need to reintroduce follow-up populations of mediating
mycelia.

## Practical Site Management

Several factors drive the decision-making process for remediation
projects. The habitat you want to inoculate predetermines which
mushrooms are suitable. Your ability to obtain mycelium, in the form
of spores or commercial spawn, or by gathering naturally growing
mycelium, also influences your decisions. Other factors including
experience, labor, ease, and commercial or recreational intent all
come into play. The best results come from listening to the mycelium
and adapting methods to each site's unique conditions.

## Key Principles

- Sequential recovery: Fungi lead the way, followed by bacteria,
  then plants, then animals in a predictable succession
- Aged mycelium superiority: Farm-aged mycelium pre-exposed to
  wild microbes outperforms sterile pure culture spawn
- Wood chip matrix: A one-and-a-half-foot-deep mulch layer
  provides the aerobic conditions mycelium needs to thrive
- Cardboard and straw capping: Covering mulch beds prevents
  anaerobic competitors and maintains moisture levels
