- **Strawberry Diesel**: A hybrid known for its sweet strawberry aroma combined with diesel

purple coloration potential through anthocyanin expression.

The combination produces a mostly sativa-dominant plant expressing characteristics from both
lineages. Strawberry Diesel contributes the energetic onset and sweet aroma, while Ghost OG
provides structural backbone, resin coverage, and physical relaxation. This strategic pairing of
