## Uredial Stage — The Fungal Photocopier

The most destructive phase of wheat rust is the uredial stage. Uredospores
are produced in brick-red pustules (uredinia) that erupt through the surface

photocopy of the parent — and can infect another wheat plant within hours

## Haustoria — The Placenta of Fungi
