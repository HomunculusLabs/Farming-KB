## Lollipopping

Lollipopping is a defoliation and pruning technique performed just before or during the early weeks of flowering. The lower portion of the plant's branches is stripped of fan leaves and small bud sites, leaving the upper canopy full of foliage and bud development while the lower stems are bare like a lollipop stick. The technique redirects the plant's energy toward top colas that receive the most light, and eliminates the low-quality "popcorn" buds that form in shaded lower areas.

## Core Concept

In a typical cannabis plant grown under artificial lighting, the upper canopy receives intense direct light while the lower branches exist in deep shade. These lower bud sites develop slowly and produce small, airy, underdeveloped buds with lower potency. Lollipopping removes this unproductive lower growth entirely, forcing the plant to channel all its energy into the top portion of the canopy where light is abundant and bud quality is highest.

Cervantes supports the principle behind lollipopping when he advises to "remove spindly, dimly lit lower branches" and notes that doing so "stresses plants much less than removing leaves to speed growth of upper foliage." He also recommends pruning "lower branches that do not receive light" so that "plants will direct energy into buds."

## How Lollipopping Works

The technique is straightforward but requires careful judgment:

1. Begin 1-2 weeks before switching to 12/12 flowering, or during the first two weeks of flowering
2. Identify the "lollipop line" -- the point on each branch where light penetration drops off significantly
3. Remove all fan leaves below this line on every branch
4. Remove all small secondary branches and bud sites below the line
5. Leave the main stem and upper branches intact with all their foliage
6. Clean up any dead or yellowing leaves at the base of the plant
7. Optionally remove the lowest 1-2 sets of branches entirely for a cleaner look

## Timing

The optimal timing for lollipopping depends on the grower's approach:

- Pre-flower lollipopping (1-2 weeks before 12/12): allows the plant to recover before flowering begins, with minimal impact on bud production
- Early flowering lollipopping (weeks 1-2 of flower): removes lower growth before it develops into popcorn buds, but causes some stress during the critical flowering transition

Cervantes advises that pruning shortly before flowering delays peak maturation by a week or longer, as it takes a month or more for hormones to rebuild to pre-pruning concentrations.
