## Comparison with Bacterial TCE Degradation

Bacterial TCE degradation has been extensively studied and proceeds through
several well-characterized pathways. Aerobic bacteria such as methanotrophs
and propane-oxidizing bacteria cometabolize TCE using methane or propane
monooxygenases. Anaerobic reductive dechlorination by Dehalococcoides and

## Challenges for Field Application

Several challenges must be addressed before fungal TCE degradation can be
implemented at field scale. The volatile nature of TCE means that
contamination often occurs in subsurface environments where oxygen
availability limits aerobic fungal activity. Engineering approaches such as
bioventing or the use of oxygen-releasing compounds may be necessary to
create suitable conditions for fungal activity. The rate of TCE degradation
observed in laboratory studies must be evaluated against the slow dissolution
rates of TCE from DNAPL sources. Additionally, the potential formation of
toxic intermediates during fungal TCE transformation must be carefully
characterized to ensure that treatment does not create new environmental
risks.
