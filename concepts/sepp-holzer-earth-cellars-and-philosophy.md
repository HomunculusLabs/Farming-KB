## Earth Cellars and Shelters

Earth cellars are a fundamental element of Holzer's permaculture infrastructure, providing low-energy storage for produce through the seasons without refrigeration.

### Design Principles
- Positioning and equipment make earth cellars ideally suited for storing fruit and vegetables
- Can offer fresh produce on site until late in winter
- Can also serve as open shelters for livestock if needed (particularly pigs)
- Use locally available clay and loam as primary [[bamboo-as-building-material]]
- Simple construction technique using local materials
- Very low construction cost
- Traditional [[holzer-lebenshilfe-permaculture-project-for-disabled-people]] in Bad Aussee, Styria, designed to integrate disabled people with the help of permaculture. Holzer worked on the concept with Lebenshilfe Ausseerland (under Roland Kalft) in June 2003.

### Project Components

**Mediterranean Garden**
- Indigenous and non-indigenous plants in experimental series
- Terraced and protected from elements to store heat
- Raises average annual temperature
- Enables growing grapes, figs, kiwifruit, and other sunny-climate fruits in Austrian climate

**Water Garden**
- Brings disabled visitors closer to water with its plant and animal inhabitants
- Direct contact as focus
- Ford that can be crossed in a wheelchair
- Planting possible from a wheelchair
- Aquatic [[permaculture-plant-propagation-guide]] and fish breeding

**Sensory Garden**
- Heavily scented and aromatic flowers and herbs
- Delicious berries and fruit for smell and taste
- Brightly coloured flowers and eye-catchers leading through labyrinth
- Different [[building-with-natural-materials]] (stone, wood, water) for touch
- Currently under construction with view of Mediterranean garden

**Activity Garden**
- Stimulates visitors' mobility
- Balance, coordination, and fine motor function taught using natural aids
- Different rock formations and wooden structures

**Rest Area and Rocky Area**
- Spiritual wellbeing through naturally available "positive vibrations"
- Physical wellbeing (eating outdoors, picnics)
- Rocky area becomes cultural meeting point for music and poetry

**Integration Goals**
- Disabled visitors included from the outset
- Help with practical planting and creating environments
- Entire area planned so planting and harvesting achievable from wheelchair
- Marketing produce at local weekly market or farmers' market
- Groundbreaking took place 22 April 2004

## Water Treatment and Purification

Holzer consistently recommends natural water treatment systems across his projects:

### Reed Bed Systems
- Created to purify waste water
- Regenerate and use surface and ground water
- Treatment ponds with appropriate plants, aeration, and filtering
- Deep wells for clean water access
