# Forest Spirits and the Subaquatic World

## Overview

In Amazonian vegetalismo as documented by [[sachamama]] to the mischievous Chullachaki — while beneath the rivers lie golden cities inhabited by yakurunas (water people), mermaids, and extraterrestrial visitors. These realms are accessed through [[icaro]] comes from the Kima constellation, emanating wisdom as heavenly light.

**Hidden knowledge**: The text suggests that within vegetalismo lies knowledge that could benefit humanity, including the hidden secret of longevity, believed to be found within the plants of the forest.

### The Huairamama (Mother of the Wind)

The Huairamama is a great snake that moves with a powerful wind. According to jungle legend, when this snake takes a bath, thunder is heard among the clouds but no water falls — a phenomenon called supay-cato (ghost bath). The Huairamama can be called by a healer during trance and arrives with a sudden great wind born from one moment to the next, without any visible clouds, passing like a whirlwind. It can be heard by both those taking the purge and those who have not.

**Physical description**: Its eyes shine with white lights and its mouth radiates violet-colored waves that make perceivers feel like giants capable of sensing anything approaching.

**Associated beings**: On the Huairamama ride sylphs who teach sidereal defense and esoteric sciences. One sylph cleverly corners enemies and delivers them to another sylph dressed in yellow who kills them. A princess instructs in esoteric sciences, and a king's goats (chusco-huacra), jumping and running, produce the wind that accompanies this snake. Four flying objects always accompany the sylphs as guardians.

**Healing function**: The Huairamama throws its rainbow to the jungle to give strength to the medicine a healer prepares for a patient bitten by a snake.

## Vision of the Snakes (Vision 17)

The snake vision presents an elaborate ecosystem of serpent beings and their associated healers and protectors:

### Key Serpent Beings

- **Huairamama**: Throws rainbow to strengthen medicine
- **Sachamama**: Throws rainbow charged with waves of light
- **[[icaros]], coming from a distant city in the midst of the river — a beautiful place full of aromatic perfumed air, where the most rapid ships (such as the supay-lancha) can be found.

### Royal Palace

A great royal palace above houses muses who dance pompously back and forth while musicians play lutes producing charming and emotional melodies. A queen and her court-ladies observe. This occurs on Callisto, a satellite of Jupiter. A monument honours Hashi, a great juggler. Nearby is the spirit of the papastrueno plant (*Dioscorea sp.*), an Inca called Yana-huya — a person who ingests this plant can summon rain or prevent it from falling.
