## Changa — Smokeable DMT-Harmala Herb Blend

## Overview

Changa is a smokable herbal blend containing N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) infused into a plant base that has been enriched with beta-carboline alkaloids, primarily harmine and harmaline derived from [[peganum-harmala]] ([[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] without the need for the complex preparation required for ayahuasca. Changa was developed in the early 2000s by Australian ethnobotanists and has since gained global popularity as a more accessible and user-friendly alternative to smoked freebase DMT.

## Comparison with Other DMT Administration Routes

Changa represents one of several methods for consuming DMT, each with distinct pharmacokinetic and experiential profiles:

**Smoked freebase DMT** provides the most rapid and intense onset, with effects beginning within seconds and a duration of approximately 5–15 minutes. The experience is often described as being launched into an entirely different reality, with minimal transition time. However, the intensity can be overwhelming and the brevity of the experience leaves little time for integration during the peak.

**Oral ayahuasca** produces a slower onset (30–60 minutes) and a much longer duration (4–8 hours). The experience is more gradual and integrative, with significant purgative effects and deep emotional processing. Preparation is complex and time-consuming, and the taste is notoriously unpleasant.

**Vaporized changa** occupies an intermediate position in nearly all parameters. The onset is rapid but slightly more gradual than freebase DMT, the duration is extended by the MAOI potentiation, and the experience tends to be warmer and more emotionally accessible. The herbal matrix also makes changa easier to smoke for those who find vaporizing freebase crystals technically challenging.

**Pharmahuasca** refers to the oral or sublingual administration of pharmaceutical-grade DMT combined with pharmaceutical MAOIs such as moclobemide or harmine extract. This approach provides precise dosing control but lacks the full-spectrum plant chemistry that many users find contributes to the therapeutic quality of natural preparations.

## Changa Formulations and Recipes

The diversity of changa formulations reflects the creativity of the global ethnobotanical community. Some well-known formulations include:

**"1:1 Changa"** refers to a blend where the DMT content by weight is roughly equal to the weight of the herbal base. This produces a very potent preparation and is typically reserved for experienced users. The high DMT concentration means that even small amounts produce strong effects.

**"Light Changa"** contains approximately 10–20% DMT by weight and is suitable for beginners and those seeking a more gentle, controllable experience. The lower concentration allows for larger bowl sizes and longer smoking sessions without reaching overwhelming intensity.
