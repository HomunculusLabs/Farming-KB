## Overview

The endocannabinoid system (ECS) is a biological regulatory mechanism present
in all mammals that plays a fundamental role in maintaining physiological and
psychological homeostasis. Identified scientifically in 1990 through research
into the cannabis plant, the ECS is involved in initiating physiological and
psychological changes needed to adjust to ever-changing internal and external
environments.
