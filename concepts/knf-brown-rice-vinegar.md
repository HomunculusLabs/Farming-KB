# Brown Rice Vinegar (BRV)

### Livestock Application

- Add BRV to livestock drinking water at low dilution rates
- Helps with digestion and nutrient absorption in animals
- Reduces manure odor when added to feed
- Particularly useful for poultry and pigs

## BRV as a Mineral Extraction Solvent

One of BRV's most important functions in KNF is as a solvent for mineral extraction:

- **Water-soluble calcium (WCA):** Eggshells are roasted and soaked in BRV; the acetic acid dissolves calcium carbonate into bioavailable calcium acetate
- **Water-soluble calcium phosphate (WCP):** Bones are charred at low temperature and soaked in BRV to extract calcium phosphate
- **Water-soluble phosphoric acid:** Rock phosphate can be dissolved using BRV as a mild acid extractant

The organic acids in BRV (particularly acetic acid and citric acid) effectively break down mineral compounds into plant-available forms without the harshness of industrial chemical solvents.

## Quality Indicators

- **Color:** Deep amber to brown
- **Aroma:** Sharp, clean vinegar smell with subtle rice notes
- **Taste:** Sour but not harsh; should have a rounded, complex flavor
- **Clarity:** May be slightly cloudy due to suspended amino acids and minerals; this is normal
- **Bad signs:** Putrid smell, mold on surface, or extremely harsh bitter taste indicate contamination

## Storage

- Store in glass, clay, or food-grade plastic containers
- Keep at 1-15 degrees C in a dark location
- Airtight seal after fermentation is complete
- Properly made BRV has an essentially unlimited shelf life
- Avoid metal containers as acids will corrode them

## Relationship to Other KNF Inputs

BRV is a core input that interacts with many others in the KNF system. It serves as a solvent for mineral inputs ([[knf-water-soluble-calcium-phosphate]]), a growth promoter during vegetative stages, and a feed supplement for livestock. It is produced alongside [[knf-yeast-preparation-detailed]] as both are outputs of the same fermentation cascade. BRV can be thought of as the bridge between the biological inputs (IMO, FPJ, LAB) and the mineral inputs (WCA, WCP, WS-K).
