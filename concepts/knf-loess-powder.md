# Loess Powder Preparation and Use

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

Loess powder is a [[jadam-seed-treatment-and-nursery-management]] timing guidelines

The loess powder in the seed treatment solution provides several benefits:
- Minerals from the loess are absorbed by the seed coat, providing initial nutrition for the germinating seed
- Fine loess particles coat the seed, creating a physical barrier against soil-borne pathogens
- The mineral coating may stimulate early root growth and disease resistance

### Disease Prevention

- Mix loess powder with seedling treatment liquid for preventing diseases such as canker in [[knf-fermented-mixed-compost]] (FMC) as a mineral supplement
- Apply as a foliar dust in very small quantities (mixed with water as a suspension)

## Storage

- Store dried loess powder in a sealed container in a cool, dry location
- Protect from moisture to prevent clumping
