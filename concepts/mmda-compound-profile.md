# MMDA Compound Profile

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 3-methoxy-4,5-methylenedioxyamphetamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #132
- **Class:** Substituted amphetamine (tetraoxygenated)
- **HCl melting point:** 190-191 °C
- **Picrate salt melting point:** 179 °C (decomposition)
- **Molecular formula:** C11H15NO3
- **Essential oil precursor:** Myristicin (from nutmeg, parsley, dill)
- **Classification:** Essential Amphetamine

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Threshold** | ~100 mg |
| **Common dose** | 100-250 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 250+ mg |
| **Duration** | 8-12 hours |

MMDA requires significantly higher doses than most other substituted amphetamines in PIHKAL, with an active range of 100-250 mg. The long duration (8-12 hours) and high dose requirement place it in a distinct pharmacological niche. Shulgin noted that MMDA has "a particular beauty to it" despite the high dosage requirement.

## Qualitative Effects (from PIHKAL #132)

- **Taste:** Bitter alkaloid taste followed by a distinctive odor, sometimes described as unpleasant.
- **Character:** MMDA appears to bring dreams to the conscious level, acting as a link between the waking and sleeping states. The recurring description from early trial subjects was "brain movies" — vivid imagery generated with eyes closed that had a dreamlike, narrative quality.
- **Visuals:** Eyes-closed imagery was prominent, with colorful flowing patterns and dream sequences. Visuals tended to be less geometric than other psychedelics and more narrative or story-like.
- **Motor effects:** No profound motor coordination problems were noted. Users remained physically capable despite the intensity of the inner experience.
- **Comparison to LSD:** MMDA's effects were distinctly different from LSD — less anxious, more dreamy, with a warmth and intimacy that Shulgin compared to the content of dreams rather than the analytical nature of LSD experiences.
- **Duration:** Effects began within 1-2 hours, peaked around 3-4 hours, and persisted for 8-12 hours with residual sleeplessness common.

## Synthesis (from PIHKAL #132)

The synthesis begins with myristicin (5-allyl-1-methoxy-2,3-methylenedioxybenzene), the principal essential oil of nutmeg:

1. **Amination of myristicin:** Myristicin is reacted with ammonia in a sealed tube at elevated temperature and pressure. The ammonia adds across the allyl double bond to produce MMDA directly. This is one of the simplest syntheses in PIHKAL, as the essential oil already contains the complete ring substitution pattern and only needs the amine function added.
2. **Alternative route:** The reaction can also be performed by passing myristicin through a liver preparation, demonstrating that this conversion can occur biologically — a key piece of evidence for the "Essential Amphetamines" concept.
3. **Purification:** The crude product is isolated as the picrate salt (mp 179 °C, decomp.), then converted to the hydrochloride salt (mp 190-191 °C).

This synthesis route is notable for its simplicity: myristicin plus ammonia equals MMDA. The biological feasibility of this reaction (liver conversion) provides one of the strongest arguments for Shulgin's theory that essential oils serve as natural precursors to psychoactive amphetamines.

## Myristicin Connection

The relationship between MMDA and myristicin is fundamental to understanding both compounds:

- **Myristicin** is the major component of Oil of Nutmeg (from *Myristica fragrans* seed; mace is the fibrous covering of the same seed, virtually identical in chemical composition)
- Myristicin is also found in parsley oil and dill
- The biological conversion of myristicin to MMDA has been demonstrated *in vitro* using rabbit liver preparations
- This led to MMDA being classified as an "Essential Amphetamine" — a compound that can be theoretically produced from a naturally occurring essential oil by the addition of ammonia
- The confusion between myristicin (the essential oil) and myristin (the fat, trimyristin/glyceryl trimyristate) is common even in the scientific literature and should be carefully avoided

## Essential Amphetamine Context

MMDA is the archetype of the "Essential Amphetamines" concept — the idea that nature provides, through essential oils, chemical structures that are one step (addition of ammonia) away from psychoactive amphetamines. Shulgin's son suggested the term "essential" (from essence, something with an odor) to replace the earlier "natural" amphetamines label. There are approximately ten essential oils that have a three-carbon chain and each lacks only a molecule of ammonia to become an amphetamine. See [[soma-vedic-history]] for the complete list and detailed discussion of each.

## Historical Context

MMDA was first synthesized independently and simultaneously in 1962 by both Alexander Shulgin and Gordon Alles. Both researchers named it MMDA and had begun exploring it when they discovered the other's work. At the time of the FDA's mid-1960s proclamation of dangerous drugs, MMDA was specifically named and controlled.

The close resemblance of the initials "MMDA" to the currently notorious "MDMA" has led to considerable confusion in both the scientific literature and popular culture, with MMDA sometimes being incorrectly discussed as if it were MDMA. This initial confusion persists to the present day and has been a source of regulatory and scientific misunderstanding.

## FDA and Legal Status

MMDA was specifically listed in the FDA's mid-1960s proclamation of dangerous drugs, making it one of the earliest controlled phenethylamine derivatives. This pre-dated the Controlled Substances Act of 1970 and reflected the era's growing concern with psychoactive amphetamines.

## SAR Context

MMDA's substitution pattern (3-methoxy-4,5-methylenedioxy) is one of three possible methoxy-methylenedioxy amphetamine orientations. The methylenedioxy bridge can be viewed as a replacement for two adjacent methoxy groups (in TMA, the trimethoxy compound), with the bridge constraining the oxygen atoms into a fixed five-membered ring. This constraint affects the molecule's three-dimensional shape and its interaction with receptors. The high dose requirement compared to other substituted amphetamines may reflect less efficient receptor binding or faster metabolic clearance. ## Comparison to Related Compounds

- **MDMA** ([[mdma-compound-profile]]): N-methyl-MDA; dramatically different character — empathogenic, shorter duration, much more potent at lower doses
- **MDA** ([[ayahuasca-and-dmt-plant-medicine]]): Parent compound without the methoxy; more stimulant and empathogenic qualities
- **MMDA-2** ([[mmda-2-compound-profile]]): Different methoxy position (2-methoxy); more potent and more visual
- **DMMDA** ([[dmmda-compound-profile]]): Additional methoxy at position 2; lower dose range (30-75 mg)
- **TMA** ([[soma-vedic-history]]): All three positions as methoxy (no methylenedioxy); similar dose range, more stimulant character
