### Prevention Strategies

- Maintain lowest possible generation numbers
- Track and maintain cell lines closest to wild isolates
- Vary media between transfers to exercise metabolic "muscles"
- Keep records to identify which generation levels produce best results
- Periodically return to generation 0 or 1 cultures for fresh expansions

### Freezing (Cryopreservation)

Freezing is excellent for indefinite backup storage, but requires precise
execution. Freezing without a cryoprotectant damages and kills mycelium.

#### Testing Viability

Before freezing an entire collection, test species for freezing tolerance:
1. Prepare identical liquid cultures of a single strain
2. Freeze them
3. Remove one tube monthly and transfer to fresh agar
4. Thaw in warm water bath (77-86 degrees F / 25-30 degrees C) for 2-5
   minutes before transferring
5. Evaluate recovery speed and growth quality
