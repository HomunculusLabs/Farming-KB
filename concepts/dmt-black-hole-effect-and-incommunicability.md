## The Incommunicability Problem

McKenna observed that DMT occupies a peculiar position among psychoactive
compounds. Despite living in a society obsessed with every conceivable form of
sensation, therapy, intoxication, and media overload, people consistently find
DMT to be "too much." The Spanish expression "bastante" captures this, as
something so extreme that it exceeds the threshold of what people are willing to
engage with repeatedly. This cultural avoidance creates an information vacuum
around the experience.

The black hole metaphor operates on several levels simultaneously. First, the
experience itself is so overwhelming that the person undergoing it struggles
to form coherent memories or descriptions even for themselves. Second, any
attempt to articulate the experience to others seems to generate less
understanding rather than more. McKenna noted that the more precisely one is
able to describe what happens during a [[mystical-experience]]. If the silence of sages across traditions reflects the

propositional language.
