## Fruiting Chamber Types

### Shotgun Fruiting Chamber (SGFC)

The classic beginner chamber: a clear plastic tote with 1/4-inch holes drilled
on all six sides (every 2 inches) and filled with 4-5 inches of wet perlite.
The holes provide passive FAE while the perlite maintains humidity. Best for
[[mushroom-home-cultivation-history]], offering simplicity, scalability, and
reliable results.

Common modifications include:
- **Polyfill or micropore tape** over holes to filter contaminants while
  allowing gas exchange.
- **Liner** (trash bag or plastic sheet) between substrate and tub walls to
  prevent side pinning.
- **Lighting** mounted in the tub lid or provided externally.

### Martha Tent (Greenhouse)

A freestanding grow tent with shelving, a cool mist humidifier inside, and
active exhaust. Suited for cultivators running multiple substrates
simultaneously. Requires more equipment and monitoring than monotubs but offers
greater capacity and environmental control.

### Automated Chambers

Advanced setups use environmental controllers (Inkbird, Arduino-based systems)
to monitor and regulate temperature, humidity, CO2, and light automatically.
