## Cannabinoids and Sleep Architecture

### THC: Sedative Properties
THC is the primary cannabinoid responsible for cannabis's sleep-promoting effects:
- Reduces sleep onset latency (time to fall asleep)
- Increases total sleep time
- May increase deep slow-wave sleep in some users
- Reduces REM sleep duration
- Indica strains, with their generally higher CBD and sedating terpene content, are preferred for sleep

### CBD: Alerting Properties
CBD presents an interesting paradox for sleep:
- At low doses, CBD can be alerting and wake-promoting
- At higher doses, CBD may have sedative effects
- CBD alone may actually worsen sleep onset for some users
- CBD's anxiolytic (anti-anxiety) properties may indirectly improve sleep by reducing pre-sleep worry

### The THC-CBD Balance
The interplay between THC and CBD in sleep is critical:
- THC-dominant preparations generally better for insomnia
- CBD may moderate unwanted THC effects (anxiety, racing thoughts) that interfere with sleep
- Some users find that too much CBD counteracts THC's sedative properties
- The optimal ratio is highly individual

## The Self-Balancing Mechanism

A notable phenomenon documented in cannabis sleep research is what might be called a self-balancing mechanism. A 2004 Farnborough, UK study found that a cannabis extract normalized sleep architecture: subjects who had been sleep-deprived experienced longer sleep duration, while subjects who had been sleeping excessively experienced shorter sleep duration. This suggests cannabinoids may help restore healthy sleep patterns rather than simply forcing sedation.
