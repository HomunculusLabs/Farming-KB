## Overview

Agar media form the foundation [[mushroom-cultivation-industry-directory]]. To one liter of water, add 20 grams agar-agar, 20 grams barley malt
sugar, 2 grams nutritional yeast, and optionally 1 gram peptone derived from soybean. When
peptone is included, the medium is designated MYPA. This formulation provides complex
carbohydrates from the malt sugar, nitrogen from the yeast, and additional amino acids
from the peptone, supporting robust mycelial growth across most gourmet and medicinal
mushroom species.

Potato Dextrose Yeast Agar (PDYA) uses the broth from boiling 300 grams of potatoes in two
to three liters of water for one hour. Only the broth is retained and the potatoes are
discarded. To this potato broth, add 20 grams agar-agar, 10 grams dextrose, 2 grams yeast,
and 1 gram peptone. The total volume should equal one liter. PDYA is particularly favored
for species that thrive on starchy substrates and provides a different carbohydrate
profile than malt-based formulations, which helps maintain the metabolic versatility of
strains cultured on it.

Oatmeal Malt Yeast Agar (OMYA) combines 80 grams instant oatmeal, 20 grams agar-agar, 10
grams malt sugar, and 2 grams yeast per liter of water. The oatmeal does not need to be
filtered out, though some cultivators prefer to do so for a cleaner appearance. Dog Food
Agar (DFA), first developed by the late Dr. Steven Pollock, uses 20 grams dry dog food and
20 grams agar-agar per liter of water, representing an economical alternative with
surprising effectiveness. Corn Meal Yeast Glucose Agar (CMYA) consists of 20 grams agar-
agar, 10 grams cornmeal, 5 grams malt or glucose, and 1 gram yeast per liter, and is
widely used by mycological laboratories for long-term culture storage.

## Media Supplementation and Adaptation

Beyond the standard formulations, agar media can be fortified with a variety of nitrogen
and carbohydrate supplements to stimulate growth rate and mycelial quality. Two grams of
yeast, one to two grams of peptone, two grams of oatmeal or oat bran, two grams of rye or
wheat flour, one gram of soybean meal, one gram of spirulina, or two grams of high-quality
dry dog food can be added to one liter of MEA or PDA, often in combinations. These
supplements broaden the nutritional profile and help maintain the metabolic versatility of
cultivated strains across successive transfers.

substrate on which mushrooms will ultimately be produced. For sawdust-based cultivation,
