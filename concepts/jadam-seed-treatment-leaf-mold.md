## Rice Seed Treatment with JS

For rice seeds specifically, JADAM has a sulfur-based seed disinfection protocol (Program 1):

**Recipe:**
- JS: 5L (1.3 gal) per 500L (132 gal) cold water
- For thick-husked rice: use 7L JS
- For waxy rice and colored rice: use 7L JS

**Procedure:**
1. Put rice seeds in small porous bags
2. Immerse in the cold JS solution for 48 hours
3. A white film will form on the surface -- this is normal
4. Rinse very thoroughly with water
5. For government-supplied seed varieties: rinse and rub 3 times before immersion
6. Start sprouting

This replaces chemical seed disinfectant. Also effective for garlic and onion sets (immerse ~30 minutes).

## Seedbed Disease Control

If rice seedlings show disease in the seedbed:
- JS 1.5L + JWA 3L per 500L water
- Spray on seedbed
