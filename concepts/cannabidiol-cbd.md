# Cannabidiol (CBD)

## Overview

Cannabidiol (CBD) is a non-psychoactive cannabinoid sourced from
cannabis. Unlike THC, CBD does not produce a "high" but has demonstrated
profound therapeutic effects across a wide range of conditions. CBD has a
greater affinity for CB2 receptors than CB1 receptors, though much of
its therapeutic influence stems from indirect mechanisms. (see [[blesching-cannabis-fibromyalgia-tai-chi-and-trauma]]).

## Mechanism of Action

### FAAH Inhibition
CBD's most significant mechanism is its ability to suppress fatty acid
amide hydrolase (FAAH), the enzyme that breaks down anandamide. By
inhibiting FAAH, CBD keeps the "Bliss Molecule" active at higher
concentrations and for longer durations, amplifying the body's own
endocannabinoid signaling. (see [[cannabis-and-copd-research]]).

### THC Modulation
CBD tames the psychoactive influence of THC, allowing patients to benefit
from THC's therapeutic powers at higher concentrations without
overwhelming psychoactive effects. This "entourage effect" is one reason
whole-plant cannabis preparations often outperform isolated THC
pharmaceuticals.

### Serotonin Receptor Engagement
CBD calms autonomic responses to stress (such as rapid heart rates) by
engaging receptors that modulate serotonin, producing a calming anxiolytic
effect through pathways distinct from cannabinoid receptors.

## Neuroprotective Properties

CBD is considered one of the most promising agents for treating
neurodegenerative illness:

- **Epilepsy**: Oil-based CBD solutions have proven effective for
  pediatric patients with refractory epileptic seizures who failed
  traditional anti-seizure medications
- **Alcohol neurotoxicity**: May prevent nerve damage associated with
  alcohol poisoning
- **General neuroprotection**: Supports brain cell health across multiple
  neurodegenerative conditions

## Psychiatric Applications

CBD's therapeutic potential in psychological disorders is based on three
key properties:

- **Antipsychotic**: Reduces symptoms of acute paranoid schizophrenia
  as effectively as the pharmaceutical Amisulpride, without significant
  adverse side effects
- **Anxiolytic**: Reduces anxiety through serotonin pathway modulation
- **Antidepressant**: Demonstrates measurable antidepressant effects in
  clinical studies

## Anti-Inflammatory and Immune Effects

CBD has shown clear therapeutic impact on inflammatory regulation:

- **Inflammatory bowel disease**: Reduces gut inflammation
- **Arthritis**: Decreases joint inflammation and associated pain
- **Periodontitis**: Reduces inflammatory markers in gum disease
- **Atherosclerosis**: Modulates inflammatory components of arterial
  plaque formation

These effects are primarily mediated through CB2 receptor engagement in
immune tissues.

## Anti-Tumor Activity

CBD produces significant anti-tumor activity both in vitro and in vivo:

- **Selective oxidative stress**: Produces oxidative stress specifically
  in cancer cells, triggering apoptosis (programmed cell suicide) without
  impacting normal, healthy cells
- **Multiple cancer types**: Demonstrated efficacy against breast, brain,
  colon, and other cancer cell lines
- **Non-toxic to healthy tissue**: The selectivity of CBD's anti-tumor
  action represents a significant advantage over conventional chemotherapy

## Comparison with THC Pharmaceuticals

Synthetic THC drugs (Sativex, Dronabinol, Marinol, Nabilone) are
FDA-approved but contain no other biologically active cannabis components.
Whole-plant CBD preserves the entourage of compounds that may play
important therapeutic roles — including terpenoids, flavonoids, and other
minor cannabinoids.

## Therapeutic Profile

| Property | CBD |
|----------|-----|
| Psychoactive | No |
| Primary receptor | CB2 (greater affinity) |
| Key mechanism | FAAH inhibition |
| Anti-tumor | Yes (selective apoptosis) |
| Anti-inflammatory | Yes |
| Antipsychotic | Yes |
| Anxiolytic | Yes |
| Neuroprotective | Yes |
| THC moderation | Yes |

## Clinical Significance

CBD represents a paradigm shift in cannabis-based medicine because it
delivers therapeutic benefits without intoxication. This makes it
acceptable to patients, physicians, and regulators who may be reluctant
to use THC-dominant preparations. Its safety profile and broad-spectrum
efficacy position it as one of the most important cannabinoids for
clinical development.
