# JADAM Element Function Reference

Summary of what each element does for plants, deficiency symptoms, and natural
JADAM sources. In a typical plant: organic elements (C, H, O) comprise ~96% of
dry mass; major inorganic elements ~3.5%; trace elements ~0.5%.

## Organic Elements (from water and CO2)

| Element | Symbol | Conc. (%) | Role |
|---------|--------|-----------|------|
| Hydrogen | H | 6 | Component of water and organic compounds |
| Carbon | C | 45 | Backbone of all organic molecules |
| Oxygen | O | 45 | Component of water, organic compounds, respiration |

## Macronutrients (from soil)

### Nitrogen (N) -- 1.5%
- **Role:** Component of amino acids and nucleic acids; needed most by crops
- **Deficiency:** Growth rapidly deteriorates; chlorosis on leaves near roots;
  stems become woody; nitrogen transfers from old to young leaves first
- **Excess:** Excessive vegetative growth; soft tissue; disease susceptibility;
  delayed fruiting
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-food-waste-liquid-fertilizer]]; [[jadam-wild-grass-liquid-fertilizer]]; cover crops (hairy
  vetch, milk vetch)

### Potassium (K) -- 1.0%
- **Role:** Controls osmotic pressure in cells; activates enzymes for
  respiration and photosynthesis
- **Deficiency:** Leaf margin chlorosis and necrosis; leaves roll and shrink;
  starts on mature leaves, progresses to base
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-potassium-fertilizer]]

### Calcium (Ca) -- 0.5%
- **Role:** Forms spindle fibers for cell walls and cell division (Ca2+); signal
  transmitter for hormone responses
- **Deficiency:** Necrosis at tips of young leaves (fast cell division areas);
  new leaves roll downward showing malformation
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-calcium-fertilizer]];
  [[jadam-chitosan-fertilizer]]; [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

### Magnesium (Mg) -- 0.2%
- **Role:** Activates enzymes for respiration, photosynthesis, DNA/RNA synthesis;
  major component of chlorophyll
- **Deficiency:** Chlorosis between veins of leaves; starts on old leaves due
  to mobility; severe deficiency turns leaves yellow/white
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-soil-mineral-replenishment]]; Sul-Po-Mag JLF

### Phosphorus (P) -- 0.2%
- **Role:** Component of cell membranes (phospholipids), energy metabolism
  (ATP), DNA and RNA
- **Deficiency:** Growth delay in young plants; dark blue leaf hue; purple
  coloration from anthocyan; thin stems; old leaves die; delayed maturity
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-calcium-phosphate-fertilizer]];
  fish by-product JLF; [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

### Sulfur (S) -- 0.1%
- **Role:** Component of vitamins and amino acids essential to coenzymes and
  metabolism
- **Deficiency:** Similar to nitrogen (both are protein components); chlorosis,
  undergrowth, anthocyan accumulation; unlike N, symptoms appear on mature and
  young leaves (sulfur cannot retransfer easily)
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-potassium-fertilizer]]; sea water

### Silicon (Si) -- 0.1%
- **Role:** Stimulates growth and reproduction; accumulated in cell walls and
  intercellular space as silica; alleviates heavy metal toxicity
- **Deficiency:** Plants easily topple over; vulnerable to pathogens
- **JADAM source:** Phyllite JLF; sea water

## Micronutrients (from soil)

### Iron (Fe) -- 100 ppm
- **Role:** Component of enzymes for oxidation-reduction reactions; electron
  transport (Fe2+ to Fe3+)
- **Deficiency:** Chlorosis between veins; starts on young leaves (iron cannot
  transfer from old leaves); severe: entire leaves turn white
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-soil-mineral-replenishment]]

### Boron (B) -- 20 ppm
- **Role:** Cell elongation, nucleic acid synthesis, hormone response, cell
  membrane function
- **Deficiency:** Black necrosis on young leaves and terminal buds; abnormally
  stiff/crumbly stems; internal tissue destruction in fruit and roots
- **JADAM source:** Sea water (contains trace B); [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

### Manganese (Mn) -- 50 ppm
- **Role:** Activates decarboxylase and dehydrogenase in TCA cycle; important
  for oxygen-generating photosynthesis
- **Deficiency:** Small necrotic spots; necrosis between veins of leaves
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-soil-mineral-replenishment]]

### Zinc (Zn) -- 20 ppm
- **Role:** Activates various enzymes; synthesizes chlorophyll; auxin
  production
- **Deficiency:** Decreased intercalary growth; small deformed leaves;
  chlorosis between veins in some species
- **JADAM source:** Sea water; [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

### Copper (Cu) -- 6 ppm
- **Role:** Binds to enzymes for oxidation-reduction (Cu+ to Cu2+)
- **Deficiency:** Dark green leaves with necrotic spots; starts at leaf tips,
  extends along margins; twisted/deformed leaves
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-chitosan-fertilizer]]; sea water

### Chlorine (Cl) -- 100 ppm
- **Role:** Water division during photosynthesis; cell division in leaves and
  roots
- **Deficiency:** Withered leaf tips; chlorosis/necrosis; bronze coloring;
  rare because wind brings Cl from seawater
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

### Molybdenum (Mo) -- 0.1 ppm
- **Role:** Component of nitrate reductase and nitrogen-fixing enzymes
- **Deficiency:** Chlorosis between veins; necrosis on old leaves; flower
  formation inhibited; flowers drop before maturity
- **JADAM source:** Sea water; needed in extremely small amounts

### Nickel (Ni) -- 0.1 ppm
- **Role:** Enzymes for reprocessing hydrogen in nitrogen-fixing microbes
- **Deficiency:** Urea accumulation in leaves; necrosis of leaf tips; rarely
  seen in soil-grown plants
- **JADAM source:** Sea water; [[jadam-soil-mineral-replenishment]]

### Cobalt (Co) -- trace
- **Role:** Component of cobalamine in nitrogen-fixing microorganism enzymes
- **Deficiency:** Stops development of nitrogen-fixing tubercles
- **JADAM source:** Sea water; humus soil

### Sodium (Na) -- 10 ppm
- **Role:** Carbon fixation in most species; stimulates growth through cell
  expansion; substitutes for potassium in osmotic activation
- **Deficiency:** Chlorosis or necrosis; prevents flowering
- **JADAM source:** [[jadam-sea-salt-and-mineral-solutions]]

## JADAM's Preventive Approach

JADAM recommends using sea water (83+ elements), rock solutions (phyllite,
humus), and [[ultra-low-cost-jadam-microorganism-solution-and-soil-foundation]] throughout the year to
prevent any deficiency. This broad-spectrum approach is simpler and more
effective than trying to diagnose and correct individual element deficiencies.
