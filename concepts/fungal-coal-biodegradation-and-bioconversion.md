## Overview

The role of fungi in coal transformation has been recognized for over a decade. Of more than 750 fungal strains screened, only six have been shown to modify the physicochemical properties of hard coal. Coal bioconversion involves two distinct processes:
- **Solubilization**: Typical for molds, yeasts, actinomycetes, and pseudomonad bacteria
- **Depolymerization**: Limited to ligninolytic Basidiomycetes (wood-decaying and litter-decomposing fungi)

## Fungal Species for Coal Transformation

### Molds

- **[Coprinus sclerotigenis C-142-1**: Attack on powdered hard coal releases 2-hydroxybiphenyl, alkylated benzenes, and PAHs
- **Fusarium oxysporum**: Solubilizes low-rank coal (LRC)
- **[Trichoderma atroviride**: Solubilizes LRC through multiple mechanisms:
  - Secretes a partly inducible heat-sensitive agent with hydrolytic properties
  - Produces an unusual esterase for cleavage of ester bonds in lignite
  - Produces alkaline substances and chelators assisting coal humic material dissolution
- **Trichoderma sp. M2**: Modifies and solubilizes hard coal without tarlike product formation
- **Penicillium sp.**: Also solubilizes hard coal without tarlike products
- **Aspergillus sp. (indigenous Indian culture)**: Eliminated 70-80% of total sulfur from high-sulfur Assam coal

### White-Rot Basidiomycetes

- **[Phanerochaete chrysosporium**: Depolymerizes and decolorizes alkali-soluble acid-precipitate LRC macromolecules (AS coal); nitrogen-limited media with hyperbaric oxygen enhance transformation, partly through MnP and LiP activity
- **Basidiomycete strain RBS 1k**: Produces MnP before coal solubilization onset
- **Basidiomycete strain RBS 1b**: Secretes oxidative enzymes with laccase and peroxidase activity
- **Clitocybula dusenii**: MnP catalyzes and optimizes depolymerization of coal humic substances
- **Nematoloma frowardii b19**: Depolymerizes low-rank-coal-derived humic acids, releasing fulvic acid-like compounds; produces high levels of laccase mRNA in the presence of coal humic substances

## Enzymatic Mechanisms

Three [ligninolytic enzymes appear involved in coal depolymerization:

1. **Lignin peroxidase (LiP)**: Oxidative depolymerization of coal macromolecules
2. **Manganese peroxidase (MnP)**: Catalyzes and optimizes depolymerization of coal humic substances
3. **Laccase**: Cell-free laccase alone is unable to promote water solubility of low-rank coals, suggesting synergistic action with other enzymes

### Enhancement Conditions
- Nitrogen-limited media enhance AS coal transformation
- Hyperbaric oxygen increases depolymerization efficiency
- Coal humic substances induce laccase mRNA production

## Products of Coal Bioconversion

- **Fulvic acid-like compounds**: Released from coal humic acids by depolymerization; potentially valuable as soil conditioners
- **2-hydroxybiphenyl**: Released from hard coal by fungal attack
- **Alkylated benzenes**: Products of coal solubilization
- **PAHs**: Released during coal transformation
- **Desulfurized coal**: Reduced sulfur content valuable for cleaner combustion
