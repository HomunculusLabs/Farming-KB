## Desulfurization Mechanisms
Fungal desulfurization targets both inorganic pyritic sulfur and organic
sulfur compounds bound within the coal matrix, important because sulfur
content directly affects environmental impact of coal combustion.
- Pyritic sulfur removal occurs through fungal production of
  organic acids that solubilize iron sulfide crystals, releasing
  sulfate and iron ions into the surrounding medium.
- Organic sulfur removal involves cytochrome P450 and other
  oxidative enzyme systems that cleave sulfur-carbon bonds in
  thiophenic and sulfidic structures within the coal matrix.
- Aspergillus species have shown particular promise, with some
  isolates achieving up to 80 percent total sulfur removal.
