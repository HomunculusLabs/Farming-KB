| pH | Affects fungal growth and enzyme stability |
| Agitation and aeration | Critical for oxygen transfer in aerobic systems |
| Inoculum size | Affects colonization speed and treatment efficiency |
| Effluent dilution | Reduces toxicity but increases volume to treat |

## Bioreactor Types for Distillery Waste

- **Fed-batch bioreactors**: Controlled substrate addition for improved efficiency
- **Bubble column bioreactors**: Good gas-liquid mass transfer
- **Fluidized-bed bioreactors**: High surface area for biofilm attachment
- **Immobilized bioreactors**: [[surfactant-enhanced-fungal-bioremediation]] retention and stability
- **UASB reactors**: Upflow anaerobic sludge blanket for primary treatment

## Economic Importance

### Co-Product Recovery
- **Single-cell protein (SCP)**: Fungal biomass from treatment can be used as animal feed
- **Ethanol production**: Recovery of fermentable sugars
- **Bioproducts**: Enzymes, organic acids, and other value-added compounds
- **Algal production**: Treated effluent can support algal cultivation
