# Holzer Intercropping by Height and Suntraps

Communities work best for humans, plants, and animals. In a polyculture,
plants support and protect each other. Water and nutrients are utilised
most efficiently because all soil layers contain roots. Holzer calls this
interactive symbiosis.

## The Principle of Height-Based Intercropping

Growing plants of varying heights strengthens the polyculture process. The
taller plants protect smaller ones from hail, wind, and direct sunlight.
This creates a multi-layered growing system that mirrors natural forest
structure.
