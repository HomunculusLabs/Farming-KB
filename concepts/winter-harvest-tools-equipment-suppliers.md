# Winter Harvest Tools and Equipment Suppliers

## Overview

Successful winter harvesting requires specialized tools for planting, cultivating, harvesting, and protecting crops in cold weather. [[eliot-coleman]]'s operation relies on a carefully selected set of hand tools and equipment, each chosen for efficiency in the intensive, small-scale farming environment. This page catalogs the essential tools and their suppliers.

## Essential Hand Tools

### Seeding and Cultivation

- **Six-row seeder** — Precision seeder for efficiently planting multiple rows of salad greens and other small-seeded crops in one pass. Dramatically speeds up direct seeding compared to hand broadcasting.
- **The Tilther** — A specialized tool for shallow tillage of bed surfaces. Creates a fine seedbed without deeply disturbing soil structure. Essential for preparing beds for successive plantings in the intensive winter harvest schedule.
- **[[soil-block]] makers** — Produce compressed soil blocks for seed starting without plastic pots. Roots air-prune naturally at block edges, producing stronger transplants. Available in multiple sizes for different crop stages.
- **Three-tooth cultivator** — For shallow cultivation between rows. Loosens soil crust and disrupts small weeds without damaging crop roots.

### Harvesting

- **Greens harvesters** — Specialized cutting tools for efficiently harvesting salad greens and baby leaf crops. Speeds up the harvest process significantly compared to knife-only methods.
- **Victorinox harvest knives** — High-quality Swiss knives for precise harvesting of individual heads and specialized crops. The preferred cutting tool for careful, selective harvests.
- **30-inch-wide Austrian grading rake** — Used for cleaning and leveling beds, removing debris, and preparing surfaces for seeding.

### Weed Control

- **[[collinear-hoe]]** — A thin-bladed hoe that cuts weeds just below the soil surface with minimal soil disturbance. The collinear design keeps the blade parallel to the soil surface for consistent cutting depth.
- **Wire weeder** — A precision weeding tool that fits between closely spaced crop rows. The thin wire blade slices weeds at soil level without disturbing crop roots.
- **Propane flame weeder** — Uses directed heat to kill weeds before crop emergence or between rows. Effective for stale seedbed technique where weeds are germinated and flamed before planting.

### Bed Preparation

- **Broadfork** — A wide-tined fork used to loosen compacted soil without inversion. Essential for maintaining good soil structure and aeration in intensively cultivated beds. The broadfork lifts and aerates soil while preserving the natural layering created by biological activity.
- **Right-angle trowel** — A specialized trowel with a perpendicular blade for transplanting and bed work in tight spaces.

### Support and Protection

- **Wire wickets** — Made from 76-inch lengths of number 9 wire, bent into U-shapes to support row covers over quick hoops. Hold fabric securely above crops without crushing them.
- **Tomato clips and twine** — For trellising and supporting tall crops in the greenhouse.

### Miscellaneous

- **European-style scythe** with folding blade for safe storage — For managing cover crops and grass pathways around the farm
- **Potting and soil blocking mixes** — Including the full line of Vermont Compost products for seed starting and transplant production

## Row Cover Fabrics

### Agribon

Agribon row covers are used for both inner layers within the greenhouse and for covering quick hoops in the field. Available in different weights (thicknesses) that provide varying levels of frost protection and light transmission. Heavier weights offer more protection but transmit less light.

### Covertan Pro 17

An alternative row cover fabric that Coleman has also used successfully. Different fabrics may be preferred depending on the specific crop, season, and protection level required.

## Vole Control

Voles can be a significant pest in winter greenhouse operations, damaging crops by feeding on roots and stems. Coleman recommends two approaches:

1. **"The Better Mouse Trap" from Intruder Inc.** — A specialized trap designed for vole control that is more effective than standard mouse traps for these larger rodents.

2. **Predator urine granules** — Granular pellets soaked in predator urine (such as coyote or fox urine) that create a scent barrier around growing areas, deterring voles from entering.

## Ground Anchors for Mobile Greenhouses

The 45-inch-long, 4,000-pound-rated ground anchors used to secure mobile greenhouses against wind uplift are a critical safety component. These heavy-duty anchors must be properly installed to prevent greenhouse movement or damage during storms.

## Quick-Hoop Equipment

A conduit bender designed specifically for bending metal conduit (EMT) into quick-hoop shapes was in development at the time of Coleman's writing. Quick hoops are a low-cost alternative to full greenhouses, consisting of metal conduit bent into hoops and covered with row cover fabric or plastic.

## Tool Selection Philosophy

Coleman's tool selection reflects several principles of small-scale intensive farming:

1. **Quality over quantity** — Invest in well-made tools that last and perform precisely, rather than accumulating cheap alternatives
2. **Ergonomic efficiency** — Tools that reduce repetitive strain and increase speed are worth their cost in labor savings
3. **Appropriate scale** — Tools matched to the scale of the operation (walk-behind rather than tractor-mounted)
4. **Multi-functionality** — Tools that serve multiple purposes reduce the total inventory needed
5. **Soil-friendly design** — Tools that minimize soil compaction and structural damage support long-term soil health
