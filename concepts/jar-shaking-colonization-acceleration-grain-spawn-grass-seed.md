### Mechanism of Acceleration

Without shaking, the inoculant is deposited at a single point (syringe
injection) or in a small cluster (agar square). The mycelium must then
grow outward from that point to colonize the entire jar, a process that
can take weeks. With shaking, the inoculant is distributed across dozens
or hundreds of individual kernels throughout the jar. Each colonized kernel
becomes an independent growth point, and mycelium spreads from all of them
simultaneously. This parallel colonization approach can reduce total
colonization time by 50% or more.

After closing the lid, the jar is shaken vigorously for 10-15 seconds.

not so violent that it damages the jar. After shaking, the lid is loosened
