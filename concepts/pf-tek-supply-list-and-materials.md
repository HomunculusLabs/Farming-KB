# PF Tek Supply List and Materials

The PF Tek ([[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]] Technique) is organized into three distinct stages, each requiring its own set of materials and equipment. Most items are common household or readily available retail products. This comprehensive supply list covers everything needed for the complete cultivation cycle from spore syringe to harvest.

## Stage One: Jar Preparation and Culturing

These materials are needed to prepare the substrate jars and inoculate them with spores. Everything listed here can be sourced from supermarkets, department stores, drug stores, and hardware stores.

### Substrate Ingredients

- **Organic brown rice flour**: Available at health food stores and organic grocers. Organic is preferred to avoid potential contamination from [[agricultural-chemicals-mycorrhizal-impact]].
- **Horticultural vermiculite**: Medium or fine grade — avoid powdery vermiculite which compacts too tightly. Available at garden centers and hardware stores.
- **Distilled or filtered drinking water**: Tap water can introduce contaminants. Filtered or distilled water is strongly recommended.

### Jar Preparation Equipment

- **Shoulderless half-pint jars with lids**: Kerr or Ball brand canning jars. The "shoulderless" design (straight-sided) is critical because it allows the colonized substrate cake to slide out easily. Wide-mouth jars are preferred.
- **Measuring cups and spoons**: For precise substrate ratio measurement.
- **Large pot for steaming**: Must be tall enough to hold the jars with a layer of water at the bottom for steam sterilization.
- **Heavy-duty tin foil**: For covering jar lids during steaming to keep them dry.
- **Heavy-duty masking tape**: Professional grade, for sealing needle holes in jar lids after inoculation.
- **Ice pick**: For punching needle holes in the culture jar lids. Two holes per lid are standard — one for inoculation and one for gas exchange.

## Stage Two: Mushroom Growing (Fruiting)

These materials are used to create the [[fruiting-environment-parameters]] where colonized cakes produce mushrooms. Items are sourced from pet shops and hardware stores.

### Terrarium Construction

- **10-gallon aquarium**: The standard fruiting chamber. A 10-gallon size provides adequate space for multiple cakes while maintaining manageable humidity levels.
- **Cut piece of transparent plastic (Plexiglas)**: Used as a partition to create a dual-chambered terrarium, which improves [[pf-tek-dual-chambered-terrarium-design-humidity-management]].
- **Strips of wood with connectors and screws**: For constructing the terrarium lid frame.
- **Plastic film and thumb tacks**: For sealing the terrarium lid while allowing some gas exchange.

### Environmental Monitoring

- **Small wall-type thermometer**: For monitoring the temperature inside the fruiting chamber. Temperature control is critical for optimal fruiting.

### Humidity Management

- **"All-purpose" water spray bottle with adjustable nozzle**: This is described as a critical piece of equipment. A good quality sprayer from a hardware store (not a recycled kitchen product sprayer) is essential for immediately supercharging the terrarium with high humidity. The adjustable nozzle should be capable of delivering a strong, fine mist.

### Harvesting and Drying

- **Wire screen**: For placing harvested mushrooms during air drying.
- **Plastic containers and plastic bags**: For intermediate storage and containment during the drying process.
- **Desiccant**: For final drying to rock-hard preservation state. Available from scientific, chemical, and lab supply sources. Silica gel is the most common desiccant used.

## Stage Three: Spore Printing and Spore Syringe Making

These materials are needed for harvesting spores from mature mushrooms and creating new spore syringes for future cultivation cycles.

### Spore Print Materials

- **Micro curved cuticle (fingernail) scissors**: For precisely removing the cap from the stem when making a spore print. Available at cosmetics counters and drug stores.

### Sterilization Equipment

- **Denatured alcohol**: For sterilizing tools and work surfaces during the spore collection [[pf-tek-spore-printing-and-syringe-preparation]] process. Available at hardware stores.
- **Tequila shot glass**: Used as a small container for flaming and sterilizing tools.
- **Eye dropper**: For handling small volumes of sterilizing alcohol.

### Syringe Preparation

- **Glass stirring rod**: For stirring spore collections. Available from scientific supply houses.
- **Plastic syringes (10 cc or larger)**: For drawing up and storing spore solution. Large syringes (20 cc to 65 cc) are particularly useful because they hold more spore solution and reduce the number of syringes needed.
- **18 gauge 1.5-inch needles**: Standard needle size for [[mushroom-spore-syringe-preparation]]. Extra-long needles are advantageous if available, as they reach deeper into the substrate during inoculation.

Syringes and needles can be sourced from retail medical supply stores, health supply shops, pharmacies, drug stores, and scientific/lab supply companies.

## Maximum Formula Modification

For cultivators using the "maximum formula" substrate recipe, an additional step is required during jar preparation: after loosely loading the substrate, the jar is lightly slammed on the palm to settle the mixture to approximately 1/2 inch below the jar top. The inside walls are then cleaned down to the substrate level and the jar is filled to the top with dry vermiculite as a contamination barrier. Maximum formula jars should be incubated at room temperature without exceeding 70°F, with loosened lids or untaped holes to ensure adequate gas exchange.

## Sourcing Strategy

Nearly all materials for the PF Tek are available locally. The recommended approach is to visit a well-stocked hardware store, a garden center, a health food store (for organic brown rice flour), and a pharmacy (for syringes and needles). Scientific supply items (desiccant, glass stirring rods) may require ordering from a lab supply catalog or visiting a specialty store.
