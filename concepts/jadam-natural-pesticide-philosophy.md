## Core JADAM Pesticide Components

### JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA)

The most important ingredient in any pesticide is the wetting agent or
surfactant. Only when pesticide has this wetting agent will it effectively
penetrate the target pest. JWA is a natural surfactant developed without
heating. It increases wetting, coating, and penetrating of the pesticide
solution and has its own pesticide and germicide effect. JWA is the key
innovation that makes JADAM pesticide effective at ultra-low cost.

### JADAM Sulfur (JS)

JADAM sulfur is a germicide made from sulfur without heating, taking only
20 minutes to prepare. JS is no less effective than chemical pesticides and
does not damage plastic or steel pipes like traditional lime-sulfur mixtures.
It controls [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]], downy mildew, and
anthracnose without the corrosive properties of conventional sulfur
preparations.
