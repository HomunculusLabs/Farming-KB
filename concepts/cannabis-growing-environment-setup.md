# Cannabis Growing Environment Setup

Creating the right indoor environment is the foundation of successful cannabis cultivation. The grow room must provide optimal conditions for every stage of the plant's life cycle, from seedling through harvest.

## Lighting

Light is the single most important factor in indoor cannabis cultivation. Greg Green's Cannabis Grow Bible covers lighting systems in depth, from poor to best systems.

### Light Types
- **HID (High Intensity Discharge)**: The gold standard for cannabis. MH (Metal Halide) for vegetative growth, HPS (High Pressure Sodium) for flowering.
- **LED**: Modern LEDs can provide full-spectrum light with lower heat output and energy consumption.
- **Fluorescent (CFL/T5)**: Good for seedlings and clones but insufficient for flowering mature plants.

### Key Metrics
- **Lumens**: Measure of visible light output. More lumens = more light energy available to plants.
- **PAR/PPFD**: Photosynthetically Active Radiation measures the specific wavelengths plants use (400-700nm). PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density) measures how many photons reach the canopy.
- **Light Spectrum**: Blue light (400-500nm) promotes vegetative growth; red light (620-700nm) promotes flowering. UV-B light stimulates olivetolic acid production, increasing trichome development.

### Light Placement
- Maintain proper distance between lights and canopy to prevent burning while maximizing intensity.
- Use reflective walls (mylar, white paint) to maximize light distribution.
- Rotate plants regularly for even light exposure.

## Ventilation and Air Flow

Proper air exchange is critical for plant health and prevents mold, mildew, and pest problems.

### Exhaust and Intake
- An inline fan with carbon filter for odor control and heat removal.
- Passive or active intake to bring fresh CO2-rich air into the room.
- Aim for a complete air exchange every 1-3 minutes.

### Air Circulation
- Oscillating fans should create gentle movement of leaves and branches.
- This strengthens stems, prevents hot spots, and reduces humidity buildup.
- Avoid direct strong wind on young plants.

## Temperature

Temperature directly affects metabolic rate, nutrient uptake, and terpene profiles.

### Ideal Ranges
- **Vegetative stage**: 70-85°F (21-29°C) during lights on; 65-75°F (18-24°C) lights off.
- **Flowering stage**: 65-80°F (18-27°C) day; 60-70°F (15-21°C) night. Cooler night temps during late flowering can enhance purple coloration and terpene production.
- **Root zone**: 65-70°F (18-21°C) for optimal nutrient uptake.

### Temperature Management
- HID lights generate significant heat; LED runs cooler.
- Air-cooled hoods or water-cooled systems can manage heat from HID fixtures.
- Thermostat-controlled ventilation prevents overheating.

## Humidity

Relative humidity (RH) must be managed throughout the plant's life cycle.

### Target RH by Stage
- **Seedlings/Clones**: 65-80% (roots not yet established, need atmospheric moisture)
- **Vegetative**: 50-70% (supports vigorous leaf growth)
- **Early Flowering**: 40-55% (reduces risk of mold)
- **Late Flowering**: 40-50% or lower (prevents bud rot and powdery mildew)

### Humidity Control
- Dehumidifiers for high-humidity environments.
- Humidifiers when growing in dry conditions.
- Proper ventilation is the first line of defense.

## CO2 Supplementation

Elevating CO2 from ambient (~400 ppm) to 1000-1500 ppm can increase growth rates by 20-30%, but requires sealed rooms and matching light intensity.

### Requirements
- Sealed grow room (no air exchange during CO2 enrichment).
- Strong lighting (CO2 only helps when light is not the limiting factor).
- CO2 generator or tank with regulator and controller.
- Temperature should run slightly higher (75-85°F) when supplementing CO2.

## Environmental Controllers

Automated systems can monitor and adjust temperature, humidity, and CO2 levels:
- Analog controllers: Simple thermostat/humidistat switches.
- Digital controllers: Programmable with setpoints and data logging.
- Smart controllers: App-connected, with remote monitoring and alerts.
