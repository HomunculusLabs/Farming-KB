Two protein-bound polysaccharides isolated from this fungus -- PSK (Krestin) and PSP -- have undergone decades of clinical trials and have become standard adjunct cancer therapies in Asia. PSK once accounted for more than 25% of total national expenditure for anti-cancer agents in Japan.

## The Organism

Trametes versicolor is not an edible mushroom, but since ancient times its extracts have been used in traditional Chinese medicine for therapeutic effects including cancer treatment. The multicoloured cap resembles a turkey tail. Today two compounds are purified by deep tank fermentation of the mycelium using a variety of strains.

## PSK (Krestin)

### History and Production
PSK (polysaccharide-K) was first isolated in Japan in the late 1960s from mycelial strain CM-101. It is extracted from deep-tank fermentation of the mycelium. The polysaccharide component consists of a beta-1,4 main chain and beta-1,3 side chain, with beta-1,6 side chains. PSK has a molecular mass of approximately 100 kDa and contains fucose among its sugar components.

### Clinical Trials - Gastric Cancer
PSK has been used as immunotherapy for more gastric cancer patients than any other cancer type:

- **Kaibara et al. (1976)**: PSK at 3g/day added to Mitomycin C and 5-FU chemotherapy after gastrectomy (n=66) doubled 2-year survival rates
- **Fujimoto et al. (1979)**: Larger prospective study (n=230) confirmed the finding
- **Kodama et al. (1982)**: Study of 450 patients suggested PSK gave protection against immunosuppression associated with surgery and chemotherapy
- **Kondo and Torisu (1985)**: Double-blind randomized controlled trial (n=144) found significant increase in disease-free and overall survival
- **Nakazato et al. (1994)**: Phase III trial by the Study Group of Immunochemotherapy with PSK for Gastric Cancer showed combining PSK with chemotherapy significantly improved survival

### Clinical Trials - Other Cancers
- **Oesophageal cancer**: PSK improved one-year and two-year survival in combination with radiation and chemotherapy (Okudaira et al., 1982)
- **Lung cancer**: Study of 185 patients showed almost four times more patients with PSK had significant disease-free survival improvements after radiotherapy (Hayakawa et al., 1993); PSK was more effective with Stage III disease and patients over 70
- **Breast cancer**: Large trial (914 patients) suggested PSK significantly extended survival in ER-negative Stage IIA patients (Toi et al., 1992); HLA B40 antigen may be a predictive factor for PSK response (Yokoe et al., 1997)
- **Nasopharyngeal carcinoma**: Small trial in Taiwan showed small but significant impact on five-year survival (Go and Chung, 1989)

## PSP (Polysaccharide-Peptide)

### History and Chemistry
PSP was isolated in China in 1983 from Trametes versicolor mycelial cultures. PSP differs from PSK in its sugar composition: PSP lacks fucose and contains arabinose and rhamnose instead. Both have molecular weights of approximately 100,000 daltons. PSP may contain at least four discrete molecules, all true proteoglycans.
