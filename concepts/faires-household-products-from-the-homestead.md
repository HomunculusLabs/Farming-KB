### Neem-Based Products

The neem tree provides a range of household products beyond its garden use as a pesticide:

- **Toothpaste:** Neem extracts are used in natural toothpaste formulations
- **Skin creams:** For treating various skin ailments
- **Sprays:** Antifungal and antibacterial household sprays
- The entire tree has antifungal, antibacterial, sedative, and antiviral properties

### Tea Tree Oil Products

Similar to neem, tea tree oil serves as a base for household products:

- Skin treatments and antiseptic applications
- Pest control sprays
- Cleaning solutions
- Antibacterial surface treatments

## Animal Fat Products

### Tallow and Lard

Animal processing yields fats that serve multiple household purposes:

- **Cooking:** Traditional cooking fat for frying and baking
- **Soap making:** Tallow and lard from processing support soap-making
- **Candle making:** Rendered fats provide candle material
- **Pastry:** Goose grease traditionally used for pastry making
- **Preservation:** Fat can seal and preserve cooked foods (potted meats)
