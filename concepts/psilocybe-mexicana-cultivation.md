## Sclerotia Production

For sclerotia-cultivation|sclerotia, no special treatment beyond full
colonization is needed. The jar continues incubating in a clean,
preferably dark location. Sclerotia form as white to tan hardened knots
pressing against the glass within the grain mass.

While the source gives specific timeline data for *P. tampanensis*
(sclerotia enlarge until approximately 4 months post-inoculation),
*P. mexicana* follows a similar though potentially shorter curve.
Expect 2 to 4 months for maximum yield. No light, fresh air exchange
beyond the loose lid, or humidity management is required during this
period. Sclerotia also form within the casing layer of cased trays
intended for mushroom production, providing a bonus harvest.

## Casing for Mushroom Production

When the goal is mushroom fruiting rather than (or in addition to)
sclerotia production, fully colonized grass seed is cased:

1. Shake colonized grain loose from the jar and pour into a 1-litre
   disposable plastic tray. Break up any lumps with the clean rim
   of the jar.
2. Level the grain surface evenly using a large spoon and fork.
3. Apply a 1.5 to 2.0 cm layer of [[comparison-laminar-flow-hood-vs-fruiting-chamber]], *P. mexicana*
follows the general timeline described in the source:

1. **Pin initiation:** First pinheads appear 6 to 15 days after
   placing the casings in the bins. Exact timing depends on the
   specific strain and environmental conditions.
2. **Maturation:** Mushrooms mature in 5 to 7 days after pin
   formation, after which they can be picked at the desired stage.
3. **Post-harvest:** Increase watering directly after picking a flush,
   as maturing mushrooms pull substantial moisture from the casing
   soil.
