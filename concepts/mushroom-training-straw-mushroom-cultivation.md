Straw mushrooms must be harvested promptly as they mature quickly. The "button" stage
(before the veil opens) is the premium harvest time. If allowed to open fully, the mushrooms
are still edible but command a lower market price.

## Advantages of Straw Mushroom Cultivation

- Extremely fast production cycle (7 to 23 days total)
- Recycles [[fungi-in-composting-and-agricultural-waste-conversion]] materials
- Can serve as a complementary crop alongside bag-based cultivation

## Cultivation Challenges

- Very short shelf life after harvest; must be sold or processed immediately
- Requires consistent high temperatures (28 to 38 C)
- More susceptible to pest damage in outdoor settings
- Multiple production cycles require careful scheduling
- Heavy rainfall can disrupt outdoor beds despite drainage provisions
