## Chemical Profile

As a confirmed member of the neurotropic fungi catalog, P. weldenii contains
psychoactive [[hplc-analysis-of-tryptamine-alkaloids]]. The species was included in the definitive
Guzmán, Allen, and Gartz monograph specifically because it was verified to
produce psilocybin, psilocin, or related compounds. Exact alkaloid
concentrations and profiles for this species have not been as extensively
studied as for more common species like P. cubensis or P. semilanceata.

## Related Species

P. weldenii is one of several Psilocybe species described from the Veracruz
region during the Welden and Guzmán collecting expeditions of the late 1970s.
Related species from the same region and collecting program include:

- **Psilocybe uxpanapensis** Guzmán — another Veracruz species from the same
  surveys
- **Psilocybe xalapensis** Guzmán & A. López — named near the city of Xalapa
- **Psilocybe veraecrucis** Guzmán & Pérez-Ortiz — also from Veracruz state
- **Psilocybe singerii** — collected during the same expeditions

These species collectively illustrate the remarkable diversity of the
Psilocybe genus in the Gulf Coast tropical forests of Mexico.
