## Stock Culture Preservation Methods

Once a pure strain is created and isolated, saving it as a
"stock culture" or "slant" is wise. Stock cultures are media-
filled glass test tubes sterilized and inoculated with mushroom
mycelium. A suitable size is 20mm x 100mm with a screw cap.
Every experienced cultivator maintains a "species bank"
collection.
