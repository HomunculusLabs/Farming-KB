## Equipment and Materials

### 1. Dried Amanita Muscaria Caps

- Low-temperature dried (120 F or less) caps preferred
- Higher grades worth the extra money if purchasing
- Inspect carefully for dirt, pine needles, and debris
- Remove all foreign material before grinding
- Grind to fine powder (like flour) so it floats on juice surface
- Small coffee grinders work well; dedicate one to Amanita only

### 2. Glass Bottles or Jugs

- 1 pint to 1 gallon capacity
- Juice bottles, beer bottles, wine bottles all work
- Bottles with tapered necks better for polyfill plugging
- Clear glass preferred (easier to monitor culture)
- 1-gallon clear glass jugs ideal (hold up to 3 quarts juice)
- Clean new bottles with detergent and hot water
- Recycled bottles: soak in bleach solution (1 oz bleach per quart water) for several days
- Inspect for cracks and chips before use

### 3. Polyfill (Polyester Fiber)

- Superior modern substitute for Pasteur's waterproof cotton
- Available from craft and fabric stores as pillow stuffing
- Waterproof, not biodegradable, easily sterilized
- Roll into ball 3-4x the bottle mouth size
- Push in with twisting motion; plug should extend into bottle
- Practice on empty dry bottles first
