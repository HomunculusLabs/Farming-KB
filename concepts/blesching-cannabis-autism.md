## Dosing Considerations for ASD

- Most clinical reports involve **CBD-dominant** preparations with ratios
  of 20:1 or higher CBD:THC
- Starting doses in pediatric populations have ranged from 1-5 mg/kg/day
  of CBD, titrated based on response
- THC content is typically kept very low or eliminated entirely in
  pediatric applications
- Whole-plant extracts with preserved terpenes may offer additional
  benefit through the [[cannabis-drug-interactions-and-contraindications]] with commonly prescribed ASD medications (SSRIs,
  antipsychotics, stimulants) must be evaluated
- Long-term effects of cannabinoid use during neurodevelopment are not
  fully understood
- Quality and consistency of cannabis products is critical, especially
  for pediatric patients
- Behavioral therapies should continue alongside any cannabinoid
  intervention

## The Endocannabinoid System in Autism

Research into the ECS and ASD has revealed:
- Altered expression of CB1 and CB2 receptors in some ASD populations
- Differences in endocannabinoid metabolite levels compared to
  neurotypical controls
- Potential genetic links between ECS-related genes (FAAH, CB1R) and
  ASD susceptibility
- These findings suggest that ECS modulation may address underlying
  neurobiological dysfunction rather than merely managing symptoms
