# Psilocybin Mushrooms of Africa

Africa has a relatively poorly documented neurotropic mycobiota compared to other
continents. Guzman, Allen & Gartz (2000) noted that many regions of Africa lack
mycological surveys, and records are sparse outside of North Africa, South
Africa, and a few scattered tropical locations. The continent likely harbors
many more species than currently recorded.

## Widely Distributed African Species

tropicalis, Gymnopilus spectabilis (northern Africa), Inocybe corydalina
(northern Africa), [[guzman-panaeolina-foenisecii-psilocybin-controversy-conflicting-chemical-analyses]], Panaeolus africanus, P. fimicola, P.

mairei (southwest).

## Distribution by Country/Region

### Algeria
[[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]], Psilocybe mairei (originally described from North Africa).

### Chad
Panaeolus africanus.

### Ethiopia
Claviceps purpurea.

### Ivory Coast
Claviceps paspali, Conocybe sp. (?), Psilocybe sp. (?) -- Samorini (1995)
reported unidentified neurotropic fungi.

### Kenya
Panaeolus sp., P. aquamarina, Psilocybe cubensis (?) (reported as Stropharia
sp. cf. cubensis by Vedcourt & Trump 1969), Psilocybe sp. (reported as
Stropharia sp. by Charters 1957, 1958). The Kenyan records remain tentative.

### Madagascar (Malagasy Republic)
Copelandia cyanescens, Dictyophora indusiata.

### Mauritius Island
Claviceps paspali, C. purpurea.

### Morocco
Morocco has the richest documented North African mycobiota:
- **Amanita muscaria, A. pantherina**
- **Copelandia bispora**
- **Inocybe calamistrata, I. corydalina**
- **Panaeolus fimicola, P. papilionaceus**
- **Pluteus cyanopus, P. atricapillus, P. salicinus, P. villosus**
- **Psilocybe mairei** -- Originally described from North Africa; well-documented
  in Morocco.

### Republic of Central Africa
Panaeolus africanus, P. microsporus, Pluteus atricapillus.

### Rhodesia (Zimbabwe)
Claviceps paspali.

### South Africa
South Africa has the best-documented subtropical African mycobiota:
- **Amanita muscaria, A. pantherina**
- **Panaeolina foenisecii, Panaeolus papilionaceus, P. retirugis, P.
  subbalteatus**
- **Pluteus salicinus**
- **Psilocybe natalensis** (endemic to Natal/KwaZulu-Natal region), P.
  semilanceata (?)
