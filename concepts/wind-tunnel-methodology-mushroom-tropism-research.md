# Wind Tunnel Methodology for Mushroom Tropism Research

## Overview

The study of [[darwin-five-seedling-phototropism-experimental-conditions]]. Edmond R.
Badham's 1982 study of tropisms [[bloomfield-mushroom-evaporative-cooling-and-thermal-regulation]]. Water
surrounding the working area served as both light filter and thermal
insulation, with a dedicated heater maintaining temperature stability.

Lighting was a "cool white" fluorescent fixture at 60 cm on a 12-hour
photoperiod at 0.002 cal/cm²/min (Eppley radiometer Model PSI) —
approximately one-tenth of field conditions. Humidity was monitored via wet/dry
copper constantan thermocouples connected to a two-pen strip-chart recorder
with automatic humidifier relay control.

## Controlled Environmental Parameters

**Wind velocity**: 9 ± 3 cm/sec (TSI hot wire anemometer Model 1650),
simulating gentle indoor air movement sufficient for measurable tropic
responses over 72 hours.

**Humidity**: 94 ± 3% RH, preventing desiccation while minimizing confounding
evaporative gradients unrelated to wind stimulus.

**Temperature**: 24.5 ± 1.5°C, within optimal P. cubensis growth range,
stabilized to prevent temperature-gradient-induced curvature.

**Light**: Cool white fluorescent at deliberately low intensity to minimize
phototropic interference while supporting normal development and sporulation.

## Mushroom Culture Preparation

An isolate from Badham (1980) was inoculated onto autoclaved brown rice (6 g
per 20 mL deionized water) in 50 mm Petri plates. After colonization, the
mycelial mass was transferred to 13 cm pie tins with vermiculite (25 mL
water), covered with glass, and maintained under 2685 lux cool white light
for approximately 10 days until primordia formed.

Cultures were watered with distilled water when weight loss exceeded 10 mL.
A sterile mutant was cultured in parallel for comparative experiments on the
spore production-geotropism relationship. Primordia of 15-25 mm height were
transferred to the wind tunnel.

## Developmental Staging System

**Stage 1** (0 h): Slight pileus swelling. Active [[spore-formation-geotropic-response-psilocybe-cubensis-badham-1982]]
emerges in fertile basidiocarps — the critical transition between wind-
directed and gravity-directed growth.

**Stage 3** (~48 h): Maturity with open hemispherical pileus and active
spore production. Full reorientation for optimal spore dispersal.

**Stage 4** (~72 h): Late maturity with plane pileus. Growth essentially
ceased.
