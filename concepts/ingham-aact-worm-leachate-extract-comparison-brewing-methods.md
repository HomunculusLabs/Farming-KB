## Actively Aerated Compost Tea (AACT)

### Definition

AACT is a brewed product that goes through both an extraction phase AND a growth phase.
Organisms are extracted from compost into water, then actively grown during the brewing
period using added food sources while maintaining aerobic conditions through mechanical
aeration.

### Key Characteristics

- **Two-phase process**: Extraction (pulling organisms from compost) + Growth (multiplying
