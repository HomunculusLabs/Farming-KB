### Callus and Suspension Culture Initiation

Stem portions from 30-day-old in vitro germinated plants are cut into 1 cm
squares and placed on MS medium supplemented with 0.4 mg/L NAA (naphthalene
acetic acid) for callus initiation under a 16h/8h light/dark cycle at 1,200 lux.
Fresh, friable callus cells (20-day-old) are transferred to MS medium with
NAA in Erlenmeyer flasks on a gyratory shaker (125 rpm) for suspension culture.
After 12 days of growth, cells are centrifuged and used to inoculate co-culture
setups.

## Co-Culture Establishment and Optimization

Viable fungal cells are added to *L. album* suspension cultures at 0.5-10% w/v
concentrations. Critical optimization parameters include:

- **Time of fungal addition**: Fungal cells can be added at the start of
  cultivation, end of lag phase, middle/late log phase, or early stationary
  phase. Different addition times test various co-cultivation durations (24-96
  hours).
- **[[cannabis-harvest-timing-visual-cues]]**: Flasks are harvested in duplicate for analysis of
  cumulative biomass, podophyllotoxin accumulation (mg/L), volumetric
  productivity (mg/L/day), and PAL enzyme activity.

## Analytical Methods

### Biomass Determination

Total biomass (plant plus fungal) is determined by filtering, washing, and
drying cells at 25 degrees C to constant weight (~48 hours). Fungal biomass
within the total is estimated through acid hydrolysis and colorimetric chitin
assay using MBTH (3-methyl-2-benzothiazolone hydrazone hydrochloride). Plant
biomass is calculated by subtracting fungal biomass from total. The chitin assay
involves sulfuric acid digestion, HCl hydrolysis, autoclave hydrolysis,
neutralization, and absorbance measurement at 650 nm.

### Podophyllotoxin Quantification

Dried cells are sonicated in methanol at 4-6 degrees C for 15 minutes,
extracted for 24 hours, centrifuged, and the supernatant evaporated and re-
dissolved in HPLC-grade methanol. Quantification uses reverse-phase HPLC
(Nova Pak RP-C18 column, 250 x 4.6 mm) with 0.01% [[staycare-fungal-organic-acid-production-metal-leaching]] measured at 290
nm. Activity is expressed as mkat/kg protein, with protein determined by the
Bradford method.

## Results and Significance

The co-culture system demonstrated significant enhancement in both plant cell
biomass and podophyllotoxin productivity compared to *L. album* monocultures.
The fungal elicitation effect activates the phenylpropanoid pathway through PAL
upregulation, channeling metabolic flux toward lignan biosynthesis. This
approach provides a biotechnological alternative for large-scale production of
podophyllotoxin that does not depend on endangered plant species. The system is
protected under Indian patent application 1266/DEL/2007.
