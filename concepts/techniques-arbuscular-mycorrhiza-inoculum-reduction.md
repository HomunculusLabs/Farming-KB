## Gamma Irradiation

### Method
Soil is irradiated with gamma rays (typically from a cobalt-60 source) at doses sufficient to eliminate microbial propagules.

### Effects
- Effective at eliminating AM fungal propagules
- Less impact on soil chemistry than autoclaving
- May not eliminate the most resistant spores at lower doses
- Equipment access is limited (specialized facilities required)

## Chemical Fumigation (Methyl Bromide and Alternatives)

### Method
Soil fumigation with volatile chemicals to eliminate microbial life.

### Methyl Bromide
- Historically the most effective soil fumigant
- Now being phased out under the Montreal Protocol due to ozone depletion
- Highly effective at eliminating AM fungi but also kills beneficial organisms

### Alternatives
- Metham sodium, dazomet, chloropicrin, and other fumigants
- Variable effectiveness against AM fungi
- Some alternatives less effective than methyl bromide
