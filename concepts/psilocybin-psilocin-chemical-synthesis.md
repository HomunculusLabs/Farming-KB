# Psilocybin and Psilocin Chemical Synthesis

The total synthesis of psilocin and psilocybin is important for producing analytical reference standards needed for forensic and clinical chromatographic identification of "magic mushroom" materials. Shirota, Hakamata, and Goda (2003) developed a concise large-scale synthesis route that achieves gram-scale production of both compounds without any chromatographic purification steps.

## Background

Psilocin (4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine, 4-HO-DMT) and psilocybin (4-phosphoryloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine) are the principal hallucinogenic constituents of Psilocybe mushrooms. They are structurally similar to the neurotransmitter serotonin, and their hallucinogenic effects likely result from interference with normal brain serotonin actions.

Isolating psilocin and psilocybin from mushroom material on a gram scale is impractical because:
- Psilocin readily decomposes
- Psilocybin has very high polarity, making isolation difficult
- Natural yields are low

Several synthetic routes to psilocin had been published, but reports on psilocybin synthesis were few, with yields of only 20% and 47% in prior methods.

## Psilocin Synthesis (4 steps from 4-hydroxyindole)

### Step 1: Acetylation
4-Hydroxyindole (3) is treated with acetic anhydride in pyridine and CH2Cl2 (0°C to room temperature) to give 4-acetylindole (4) as ivory white crystals.

### Step 2: Oxalylation
4-Acetylindole (4) is treated with oxalyl chloride in diethyl ether at 0°C, then n-hexane is added and the mixture stored at -20°C overnight. Yellow crystals of the glyoxalyl chloride intermediate (5) form and are collected by filtration.

### Step 3: Aminolysis
The glyoxalyl chloride (5) is dissolved in THF and treated with dimethylamine solution and pyridine in an ice bath. 3-Dimethylaminooxalyl-4-acetylindole (6) is obtained as an ivory white crystalline powder in over 80% yield.

### Step 4: Reduction
Compound 6 is reduced with LiAlH4 in anhydrous THF under argon atmosphere (reflux, 2 hours) to afford psilocin (1) as white crystals in over 85% yield.

**Overall psilocin yield**: ~68% (80% x 85%) without chromatographic purification.

## Psilocin Characterization Data

- **Appearance**: White crystals
- **Melting point**: 169-174°C (dec.) (lit. 173-176°C dec.)
- **UV (MeOH)**: λmax 222.5, 268.0, 284.5, 294.0 nm
- **ESI-MS**: m/z 205.1 [M+H]+, 227.1 [M+Na]+
- **HRESIMS**: m/z 205.1303 [M+H]+ (calcd C12H17N2O: 205.1341)

## Psilocybin Synthesis (2 steps from psilocin)

### Step 5: Phosphorylation
Psilocin (1) is dissolved in anhydrous THF and cooled to -78°C. n-Butyllithium is added, followed by tetrabenzylpyrophosphate. The reaction is stirred while warming to 0°C over 1 hour.

### Discovery of the Zwitterionic Intermediate (8)

After the phosphorylation reaction, the expected O,O-dibenzyl phosphate (7) undergoes spontaneous intramolecular rearrangement. One benzyl group migrates from oxygen to the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl group, forming a quaternary ammonium ion. This zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate (8) was characterized by:
- **1H NMR**: Two sets of benzyl signals — OCH2Ph at δ 4.98/4.96 and NCH2Ph at δ 4.56 (shifted upfield due to positive charge)
- **13C NMR**: Confirmed quaternary ammonium structure
- **31P NMR**: Confirmed phosphate moiety at δ -5.45
- **HMBC**: Key correlation showing benzyl linked directly to nitrogen
- **NOESY**: Supported N,O-dibenzyl linkage assignments

The zwitterionic nature of 8 enables large-scale isolation by simple filtration in CH2Cl2 (since excess dibenzyl phosphate washes away). Yield: over 85%.

### Step 6: Hydrogenolysis
The zwitterion (8) is dissolved in MeOH with 10% palladium-activated carbon and stirred under hydrogen atmosphere at room temperature. Both benzyl groups are removed to yield psilocybin (2) as white needle crystals. Yield: over 72% (87.5% reported for gram-scale production).

**Overall psilocybin yield from psilocin**: >72%, representing a dramatic improvement over prior methods (20%, 47%).

## Psilocybin Characterization Data

- **Appearance**: White needle crystals
- **Melting point**: 190-198°C (lit. 185-195°C, 210-212°C)
- **UV (MeOH)**: λmax 221.0, 267.5, 278.5, 290.0 nm
- **IR (KBr)**: νmax 3266, 3034, 2731, 1620, 1580 cm-1
- **31P NMR**: δ -4.48 (OPO3H2)
- **ESI-MS**: m/z 285.1 [M+H]+, 307.1 [M+Na]+
- **HRESIMS**: m/z 285.0991 [M+H]+ (calcd C12H18N2O4P: 285.1004)

## Key Innovation

The critical innovation is the isolation of the zwitterionic intermediate (8), which allows purification by simple filtration rather than chromatography. This makes the synthesis practical for producing gram quantities of analytical standards for law enforcement and clinical use.
