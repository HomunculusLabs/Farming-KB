## Acute Physiological Effects

The somatic effects of [[hallucinogenic-mushroom-acute-psychological-effects-and-bad-trips]]:

- **Nausea**: Most commonly reported physical effect, typically during onset
  (first 30–60 minutes)
- **Pupil dilation** (mydriasis): Consistent and dose-dependent
- **Increased heart rate** and blood pressure: Mild to moderate
- **Increased body temperature**: Mild hyperthermia
- **Muscle weakness** and ataxia: Impaired coordination
- **Dizziness**: Particularly during onset
- **Increased reflexes**: Hyperreflexia
- **Tremor**: Fine motor tremor at higher doses
