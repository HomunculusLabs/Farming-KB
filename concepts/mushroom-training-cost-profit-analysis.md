## Financial Management Records

The manual recommends seven essential records for
[[mushroom-training-marketing-business]]:
1. Monthly cash flow statement (daily: date, expenses, income, balance, comments)
2. Inventory book (raw materials, prices, dates, suppliers)
3. Mushroom sales record (all daily sales)
4. Raw materials sales record
5. Credit control system (with payment due dates)
6. Petty cash voucher system
7. Cash book (bank inflows and outflows)

## Checking Profitability

To verify sustainability, compare total income from mushroom sales against total costs,
including contamination losses (target less than 5%). If net monthly profit exceeds the cost
of buying new bags for the next production cycle, the farm is financially sustainable.
Trainers should encourage clear record keeping and help identify causes when the farm is not
profitable.
