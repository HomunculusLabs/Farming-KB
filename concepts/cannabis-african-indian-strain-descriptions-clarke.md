# African and Indian Cannabis Strain Descriptions (Clarke)

## Indian Central Southern Strains (Kerala, Mysore, Madras: 10-20 degrees N)

### Historical Context
Ganja (flowering Cannabis tops) has been grown in India for hundreds of years. These strains are usually grown in a seedless fashion and are cured, dried, and smoked as marijuana rather than being converted to hashish as in many Central Asian areas. Medicinal Cannabis of the late 1800s and early 1900s was usually Indian ganja.

### Morphological Characteristics
- **Growth form**: Tall and broad, central stalk up to 12 feet tall, spreading highly-branched limbs
- **Leaves**: Medium green, 7 to 11 leaflets of moderate size and serration, arranged in a circular array
- **Flowers**: Small, slender, and curved floral clusters from extensive compound branching (tertiary or quaternary limbs)
- **Seeds**: Usually small and dark

### Qualities
- Many spicy aromas and tastes
- Extremely resinous and psychoactive
- High yield of floral clusters due to extensive branching
- Frond-like limbs result from compound branching
- These strains are finding their way into domestic American Cannabis crops through Europeans and Americans living in these areas

## Malawi Strains (10-15 degrees S)

Malawi is a small country in eastern central Africa bordering Lake Nyasa.

### Morphological Characteristics
- **Growth form**: Dark green, large plant of medium height, strong limb growth
- **Leaves**: Dark green, coarsely serrated, large, slender leaflets in a narrow, drooping, hand-like array; usually lack serrations on the distal 20% of each leaflet
- **Flowers**: Sometimes airy from long internodes; large calyxes with relatively few leaves
- **Seeds**: Large, shortened, flattened, ovoid with dark grey or reddish brown mottled perianth; caruncle (point of attachment) uncommonly deep with sharp-edged lip
- Some individuals turn very light yellow-green in flowering clusters when mature under exposed conditions

### Qualities
- Nearly seedless flowers that are spicy in taste and powerfully psychoactive
- Large calyxes are very sweet and resinous, as well as extremely psychoactive
- Has become incorporated into several domestic hybrid strains
- Phenotypes vary considerably, though many resemble Thai strains
