## The Composting Fallacy in Hokkaido

Before Fukuoka demonstrated otherwise, farmers in Hokkaido were all told to burn their straw because of the pathogens remaining on it. Everyone thought that straw was crawling with disease.

- The authorities saw scattering rice straw back onto the fields as tantamount to sowing the seeds of blast and sclerotium disease

- They instructed farmers to either burn it or bring it home and turn it into compost

- Incentive money was even handed out to farmers for composting

- Every month a competition was held, with judges from the local agricultural testing station

- A hundred pounds of straw converted to compost weighs two hundred pounds

- That straw had to be taken home and turned repeatedly to prepare the compost
