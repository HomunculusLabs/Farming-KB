## Overview

Osmotolerant and osmophilic fungi are defined by their ability to grow on
substrata whose low water activities result from [[xerophilic-xerotolerant-fungi]], which tolerate low water

The water-activity value (aw) ranges from 0 to 1, with most
microorganisms restricted to aw >= 0.95. Osmophilic fungi can grow at
significantly lower aw values.

## Key Genera and Species

Important osmophilic fungi are confined to genera including:
*Aspergillus*, *Aureobasidium*, *Chrysosporium*, *Cladosporium*,
*Fusarium*, *Geotrichum*, *Myrothecium*, *Oidiodendron*, *Paecilomyces*,
*Penicillium*, *Rhizopus*, *Scopulariopsis*, *Trichoderma*, *Wallemia*,
and *Xeromyces* (Pitt 1981; Pitt and Hocking 1997).
