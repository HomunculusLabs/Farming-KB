### From Piperonal (via Nitrostyrene)

Piperonal is condensed with nitroethane in glacial acetic acid using cyclohexylamine as
base, heated at steam-bath temperature for 6 hours to produce 1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-
2-nitropropene (mp 93-94 °C, recrystallized to 97-98 °C). The conventional method using
excess nitroethane as solvent and anhydrous ammonium acetate gives poor yields. The
nitrostyrene has also been made in cold methanol with aqueous NaOH as base.

The nitrostyrene is reduced using LAH in anhydrous THF under inert atmosphere at reflux
for 36 hours. After workup, the product is distilled (bp 80-90 °C at 0.2 mm) and
converted to the hydrochloride salt (mp 187-188 °C) in isopropanol with HCl.

### From 3,4-Methylenedioxyphenylacetone

MDA can also be produced by reductive amination of 3,4-methylenedioxyphenylacetone using
ammonium acetate and sodium cyanoborohydride in methanol, maintaining pH 6-7 with HCl
over several days. Product distills at 80-90 °C at 0.2 mm/Hg (HCl salt mp 187-188 °C).
