### Soil Conditioning

Mollison describes the use of the Wallace soil conditioner -- a sophisticated implement that opens compacted soil to a depth of nine inches, creating crevices for water storage and root penetration. The results are dramatic:
- "A fantastic amount of water storage within the landscape"
- Soil temperatures 15-19 degrees Fahrenheit above surrounding soils in winter
- "Soil conditioning sharply decreases frost" and extends the growing season at both ends
- Trees that might bear in 17-18 years "will normally bear within three years in conditioned soil"

The conditioner includes a seed box that drips seeds at pre-regulated rates into the crevices, allowing direct conversion of pasture to crop without cultivation. On stubborn compacted soils, the process is repeated: go down four inches, let grass recover, then recondition to nine inches.
