### Short Needle Method

For standard 4cm needles, inoculation is through the bag wall below the filter.
Two inoculation points are prepared per bag, taped on both sides.
Points and needle are swabbed with propyl alcohol.
Needle is flame-sterilized until red-hot before each use.
5cc of spore solution injected at each point;
tape is rolled down over the hole as needle is removed.

### Long Needle Method

Modified long needles (thin stainless steel tubing) are passed
through the filter can directly into the substrate.
10cc of spore solution is deposited,
and the filter can lid is closed immediately.
This method is described as easier, quicker, and more trouble-free.

## Colonization and Fruiting

After inoculation, bags are placed in a cardboard box in a warm location.
Visible mycelium appears within a few days.
Once fully colonized, filter cans are removed
and bags are transferred to a [[mushroom-humidity-chamber-design-operation]].
When mushroom primordia form, bags are cut down the sides
leaving approximately 2cm of bag at the bottom for support.
