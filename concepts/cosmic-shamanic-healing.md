# Cosmic Shamanic Healing

In the [[amazonian-ship-aceropunta-and-visionary-cosmology]] of [[ayahuasca-preparation-pablo-amaringo-ucayali-shamanism]], healing is not confined to the physical body or even the earthly realm. Shamans routinely interact with extraterrestrial beings, travel to distant planets, receive knowledge from cosmic civilizations, and perform surgery in visionary space with the assistance of beings from other galaxies. This article explores the intersection of [[turner-essential-psychedelics-lsd-cosmic-consciousness]] and shamanic healing practice as revealed through [[amaringo-ayahuasca-visions-shipibo-iconography]].

## Vision of the Planets

Vegetalistas are able to visit the planets of our solar system and beyond through ayahuasca trance. The planets are not distant, inaccessible objects but relatively easy destinations for the experienced shaman.

### Mars

A spaceship comes from Mars, a planet composed of four distinct regions: the great volcanoes, deep canyons, great craters, and terraces full of deep caves. This detailed planetary geography suggests direct visionary exploration rather than abstract symbolism.

### Jupiter and Guibori

Jupiter appears with the fairy Guibori and her magic blue star. Two comets travel very fast nearby. Vegetalistas can call upon these comets to travel to distant places in the universe.

### The Outer Planets

Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto appear in the center of the vision. The text notes that vegetalistas may visit all these planets "rather easily, because they are not very far away" — a statement that refers to spiritual distance rather than physical distance, emphasizing the accessibility of cosmic consciousness through [[glossolalia-and-altered-states]].

### Venus

From Venus, a spaceship arrives. A horned snake called J supay is capable of creating tremendous whirlpools. Hikko, a magician, appears nearby, along with another planet.

### Nina-runa and Maullayruna

Nina-runa (fire person) with hair of fire serves shamans as protection. Maullayruna is described as a great shaman associated with the planetary vision. Two beings with an old document in their hands serve as keepers of a tunnel leading to the Supreme Divinity.

## The Tiahuanaco Realm

The ancient Tiahuanaco culture of Lake Titicaca maintains a living presence in the visionary world. Two ancestral figures — Papamtu'a (father that takes care of everybody) and Mamamtu'a (mother of all human beings) — maintain contact with huaira-cuchas, beings from distant galaxies with skin as white as paper.

### Round Pyramids of Persivann

Several ruiro-piramides (round pyramids), also called allpahuichcan (round tombs), come from a mystical city called Persivann in the magical esoteric triangle of the radiant Pleiades. People of great wisdom emerge from these pyramids, expert in cryptesthesia — the ability to perceive without using the ordinary physical senses.

### Transformative Flames

Flames rise from a pot and become expressive beings who strengthen the power of vegetalistas. Monkeys of different colors accompany different types of practitioners:

- **White monkey**: Accompanies healers
- **Black monkey**: Accompanies sorcerers
- **Red monkey**: Accompanies those who can both heal and cause harm

### Huatanruna and Muyuyruna

The Huatanruna (people that tie) are snakes that spiritually take apart the vegetalista's body. The Muyuyruna (people that turn) move like discs and take care of the vegetalista's heart. These beings represent the restructuring and strengthening processes that occur during deep trance.

### Nitimushcanpoma

The jaguars with human heads and hair made of snakes are called nitimushcanpoma, meaning "crushing tigers." They serve as powerful guardian beings within the Tiahuanaco spiritual landscape.

### Kuri-toro (Golden Bull)

The icaro of the golden bull is used to cure manchari (fright/soul loss). Behind it grows the vine of lucero ayahuasca (star ayahuasca), whose leaves resemble boats and hummingbirds, carrying people from Antares who teach new medicinal techniques through their songs.

### Manchay Barayuc

In the lower right corner, a being whose body is made of triangles serves as a giant soldier from a city in the Pleiades. The triangular body composition suggests a crystalline or geometric being from an advanced civilization.

## Spirits Descending on a Banco

The banco is a specialized type of shaman who receives visits from spiritual beings while lying beneath his mosquito net. These beings descend a heavenly blue spiral to conduct their work.

### The Three Kings

Three beings sit on the banco during the vision:

- **Maucanuelotz** ("the oldest king"): A wise man with white hair, expert in occult science. He sits centrally as the authority figure.
- **Chaimibaco**: Prince seated to Maucanuelotz's right
- **Huambrarrah**: Prince seated to Maucanuelotz's left

These three collaborate to devise powerful medicine and sublime love magic, and can bestow great fortune upon those they favor. The other beings present play instruments and sing icaros to execute the banco's will.

### Fadanat and the Subterranean Mines

A magical vessel shows the face of Fadanat, a great magician woman with a thousand faces who guards immense subterranean mines. Beside her vessel is a jergón — a snake coiled in jergón-sacha (Dracontium loretense / D. longipes) — which serves to heal snakebites.

### Puka-purahua

The great water snake Puka-purahua lies in the lake beyond, with magnetic rays shooting from her eyes capable of attracting anything from above. This snake can transform into boats of various shapes, connecting to the Aceropunta tradition of shape-shifting spirit vessels.

### Papastrueno Plant

A plant called papastrueno is extremely difficult to obtain. When ingested with a special diet, it grants power to control rain, wind, and thunderbolt. Found in high jungle hills, it resembles a golden heart. The name derives from "papa" (potato/father) and "strueno" (thunder), suggesting its association with storm control.

### Mannhuaira

The terrible mannhuaira dwells on the far side of the lake with its spirit Killo-runa (golden man), wearing a red hat with a yellow ribbon. This spirit takes the soul of anyone who fears him. The victim suffers headaches and other bodily illnesses. Only a vegetalista can heal the person with the appropriate icaro.

## Spiritual Heart Operation

One of the most remarkable visions is a literal spiritual surgery performed on Amaringo himself in 1959 at Tamanco. Suffering from heart disease, he was given very strong ayahuasca by his father.

### The Surgical Team

An American doctor dressed in a grey-violet suit appeared, accompanied by his wife in an emerald-green dress and their daughter in a matching color. They carried surgical instruments: scalpels, scissors, pincers, hooks, cotton, needles and thread, and various medicines.

### The Procedure

1. The patient was asked to remove his shirt
2. A large, broad knife opened the body from clavicle to last rib on the left side
3. A hammer broke the ribs to open the chest
4. The heart was placed on a dish
5. The doctor operated on damaged arteries, joining them with soft plastic tubes
6. The heart was returned to its place
7. The chest was closed and the wound cleansed and sewn

The doctor showed the patient the location of the arterial damage. After the operation, the patient was instructed to fast for one week, after which he felt perfect.

### Guardian Beings

The Adonitas — guardians of the most sacred temples of the universe — surrounded the house during the ceremony. Their bodies are made of tiny, dynamic, iridescent particles, suggesting a quantum or subatomic level of spiritual existence.

### Supporting Beings

- **Chaicunis**: Hidden people of the Pano group who retreated when the Spaniards arrived
- **Yaku-pato** (water duck): Very knowledgeable on occult matters with beautiful songs
- **Amahuaca couple** and **Campa-Ashaninka woman**: Indigenous spiritual presences
- **Ayar**: An Inca master of great knowledge
- **Woman from the Himalaya caves**: Normally lives in a galaxy called Mazzarot as a netafita — a being dwelling in the great nebulous mountains of the universe

## Princess Jerusela and Healing Royalty

### Jerusela

Princess Jerusela receives a balsam from her father, a Baruh from Persia. She is powerful enough to travel to any part of the world where people call her. A woman sleeping under a red blanket symbolizes the rejuvenating sleep following healing through Jerusela's icaros.

### Durela

A princess covered with a yellow mantle, Durela is accompanied by a spirit who teaches prayers for healing.

### Two Healing Kings

- **White-dressed king**: Cures mal aire and other illnesses
- **Throne-seated king**: Cures sorcery, knows millions of icaros

### Queen Galmana

The queen in a blue dress defends the vegetalista during fights with enemies. Her name means she can absorb evil from sorcerers, functioning as a spiritual shield or filter.

### The Pregnant Woman's Wisdom

A pregnant woman appears in the foreground, embodying the principle that all the mother's experiences — passions, desires, interests — transfer to the unborn child. If the mother appreciates art and music, the foetus learns from these experiences. The mother must educate her child from the moment of conception, reflecting a sophisticated understanding of prenatal influence.

## Types of Sorcery and Defense

### King Kundal

Master of the Huairamama (great snake mother of the air), King Kundal wears a red cape symbolizing that no enemy can escape him. His umbrella is made of meteors — special ships with a psychomagnetic nucleus.

### Queen Samhadi

The "illuminated" queen offers King Kundal a kettle of medicinal balsam extracted from aromatic plants of vegetalismo. She carries her talents in a coffer shaped like a serpent holding its tail (an ouroboros). When she approaches an ayahuasca session, visions take on a purplish color. If an initiate whose body is not yet clean takes ayahuasca during her presence, they may vomit heavily — even blood — as the body purifies.

### The Angash-machohualipa

This blue cock appears when an initiate breaks the rigorous diet necessary to become an efficient vegetalista. Hen's meat is particularly dangerous during the diet: if a treated patient eats hen's meat, the sickness returns with greater force and may be fatal if not treated in time.

### Chai Cuilkimama (Mother of Silver)

She spreads white silver plates and her icaro helps people prosper in business and become rich. Her chant also cures the mal aire de difunto (illness from the spirit of a deceased person).

### Millaipa-huarmi (Ugly Women)

These two women perform a critical role in curanderismo by gathering the sickness the curandero extracts from patients:

- **Younger woman** (sky-blue dress): Picks up sickness and contains it in her hands. If the patient doesn't diet, she returns the sickness and the patient worsens.
- **Older woman** (lilac dress): Receives the sickness from the younger woman if the patient cooperates with the curandero and maintains their diet. She permanently contains the evil spell.

This system illustrates the Amazonian understanding that healing is a collaborative process — the curandero extracts the illness, but the patient must participate through dietary discipline, or the treatment fails.

## Beings from the Divine Kingdom

Beings from the Divine Kingdom descend from above to give perfect foundation to the earth, whose nucleus appears behind them. A six-headed serpent and the rumi salamandra (salamander of the rock) with ultra-physical powers appear nearby. The ailpa-puma (jaguar of the earth) has bristles and two horns, representing a chthonic guardian force.

The great sylph Mesafel is an expert in hyperchemical transformation in both directions — spiritual to physical and physical to spiritual — working with aromatic ointments and perfumes.
