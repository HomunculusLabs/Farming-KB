# JADAM Herbal Smoker for Livestock and Greenhouse Pest Control

## Overview

The JADAM herbal smoker is a simple device that burns plant material to
produce pesticidal smoke inside enclosed spaces. While primarily developed
for greenhouse pest management, the smoke-based approach has broader
implications for livestock shelter hygiene and [[jadam-methods]].

## Applications

### Winter Greenhouse Pest Management

The herbal smoker fills a critical gap in winter greenhouse pest control
when spray-based methods are impractical. Regular smoke treatments during
the winter season can prevent pest populations from building to damaging
levels before the spring growing season begins.

### Pre-Harvest Pest Cleanup

When liquid pesticide residues are unacceptable close to harvest, the
herbal smoker using non-tobacco herbs provides a residue-minimal
alternative. The smoke dissipates within hours and leaves no chemical
residue on produce when appropriate herbs are selected.

### Exploratory Livestock Applications

Preliminary investigation into using herbal smoke for pest control in
livestock shelters shows promise. Flies, mites, and lice in barns and
poultry houses could potentially be managed through periodic smoke
treatments. Further research is needed to identify optimal herbs and
treatment frequencies for different livestock species and shelter types.
