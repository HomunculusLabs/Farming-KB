## Combining Cover Crops

For maximum soil improvement, JADAM recommends combining multiple cover
crop species. A common combination is cereal rye and hairy vetch,
planted together in fall. The rye provides carbon-rich organic matter
and weed suppression, while the vetch fixes nitrogen. Together they
produce a balanced fertilizer that supports the subsequent cash crop.
