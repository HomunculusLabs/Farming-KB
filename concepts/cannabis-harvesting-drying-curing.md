# Cannabis Harvesting, Drying, and Curing

## Overview

Proper harvesting, drying, and curing are essential for maximizing the quality, potency, and flavour of cannabis buds. The difference between poorly and properly cured cannabis is dramatic — well-cured bud smokes smoother, tastes better, and preserves more of the active compounds.

## Nutrient Burn vs. Nutrient Deficiency

### Chemical (Nutrient) Burn

- Looks like a cigarette burn pattern: damage starts at **leaf tips** and slowly moves toward the centre.
- Leaves behind crispy matter that flakes away between fingers.
- The plant distributes water and nutrients from bottom to top; damage mirrors this pattern.

### Nutrient Deficiency/Problem

- Does **not** look burnt.
- Appears as **cell collapse with discolouration**.
- The affected part may wither and die but should not have a burnt appearance.
- This is the major distinguishing feature from chemical burn.

### Troubleshooting

1. Check if symptoms look burnt or like cell collapse.
2. Check pH — incorrect pH can mimic nutrient problems.
3. Assess recent feeding schedule.

## Drying

- Hang plants **upside down** to dry.
- Drying period: approximately **3 weeks**.
- Ensure good air circulation around hanging plants.
- Avoid excessive heat or light during drying.

## Curing

### The Canning Method

The most common curing technique:

1. After drying, place buds in airtight containers (glass jars).
2. Open jars briefly each day to release moisture and allow fresh air exchange ("burping").
3. Continue for **3–4 weeks** minimum.

### Curing Timeline

| Time | Quality |
|---|---|
| 3–4 weeks canned | Good — minimum recommended |
| 8 weeks total (3 weeks hanging + 5 weeks canned) | Excellent — "pinnacle point of cured bud" |
| 1 year old | "Vintage" but may lose potency |

### Chemistry of Curing

- During curing, some THC cannabinoids **change composition** and become different cannabinoid compounds.
- Fresh bud (8 weeks canned curing) is considered the peak — fresher is better than aged.
- Year-old bud can still be very good but will have lost some potency.

## Smoking Quality Indicators

- Well-cured bud has:
  - Smooth, even burn.
  - Grey/white ash (not black).
  - Full flavour profile.
  - Less harshness on the throat.
