Fukuoka was assigned to an eastern district of the prefecture where his job was to

### The Fundamental Limitation

Fukuoka key insight from this experience was that "the purpose of scientific
agriculture is always and everywhere convenience to man." No matter how large and
complete the technology amassed, it will "never amount to more than a temporary
expedient." The post-season cultivation program, representing a cross-section of
the most advanced agricultural technology of the time, succeeded only in
preventing further losses, demonstrating this principle conclusively.
