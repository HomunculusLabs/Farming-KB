### Gender Relations

The proprietary attitude of men toward women, centrally characteristic of dominator models, is much diminished in partnership societies.

Women are not driven to seek reproductive security under the protection of older men, because power does not lie exclusively with aging and powerful males.

Couples may bond for any number of reasons related to themselves and the welfare of the group. Such bonding may be lifelong but is not necessarily so.

Sexuality is rarely taboo in such societies, only becoming so as a result of contact with dominator values.

### Relationship to Nature

Partnership societies maintain a relationship of dynamic equilibrium with the natural world.

McKenna argued this was reinforced by regular use of psychedelic plants, which produced ego-dissolution and a sense of oneness with the larger web of life.

Our culture, self-toxified by the poisonous by-products of technology and egocentric ideology, is the unhappy inheritor of the dominator attitude that alteration of consciousness by plants is somehow wrong and perversely antisocial.
