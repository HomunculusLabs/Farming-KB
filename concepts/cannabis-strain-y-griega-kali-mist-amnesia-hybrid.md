# Y Griega

**Y Griega** is a sativa-dominant [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-indica-sativa-and-hybrid-cannabis]] strain bred by [[oner-y-griega-kali-mist-amnesia-medical-seeds]] of
Spain. A cross between the legendary [[kali-mist]] and the iconic [[amnesia-haze]], Y Griega
delivers extraordinary potency at **27.12% THC**, a soaring cerebral high, and generous yields
that have earned it a devoted following among sativa enthusiasts and medical patients alike.

## Name Origin

The name "Y Griega" (literally "Greek Y") refers to the former Spanish letter "y," which was
officially known as "y griega" before the Real Academia Española changed its recommended name
to "ye" in 2010. The strain's naming reflects its Spanish heritage — a distinctly Iberian
take on global sativa genetics.

## Genetic Lineage

### Kali Mist (Pollination Parent)

[[kali-mist]] is one of the most celebrated pure sativas in cannabis history, bred by
Serious Seeds in the Netherlands. A multiple High Times Cannabis Cup winner, Kali Mist is
renowned for its tall stature, 90+ day flowering period, and clear, uplifting cerebral effects.
It has been a cornerstone of [[cannabis-sativa-breeding-evolution]] programs worldwide due to its vigor, resin
production, and energetic psychoactive profile.

### Amnesia (Seed Parent)

[[amnesia-haze]] stands as one of Europe's most popular strains. A Dutch coffeeshop staple
since the late 1990s, Amnesia Haze combines Southeast Asian, Jamaican, and Afghan genetics
into a potent, sativa-leaning hybrid. Its massive commercial success made it an ideal
crossbreeding candidate, contributing both yield potential and a complex [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].

### The Haze Heritage

Both parent strains carry deep [[Haze]] lineage, and Y Griega's Haze grandparent comes through
strongly in every aspect — from towering growth and extended flowering to pungent aroma and
powerful psychoactive effects. This dual Haze inheritance gives Y Griega its unmistakable
character.

## Growth Characteristics

Y Griega is a vigorous, tall-growing sativa that demands respect from cultivators. Outdoors,
plants can reach up to **11 feet**, producing massive colas along every inch of stem. Each
branch is covered in dense buds with thick white hairs, creating a striking display during
late flowering.

### Indoor Cultivation

Indoor growers must manage Y Griega's stretch carefully. The key strategy is to **force
flowering when the plant reaches half the desired final height** — typically at 3 feet or
less to achieve a 6-foot maximum at harvest. A [[screen-of-green|ScrOG]] setup is strongly
recommended, controlling height, maximizing light penetration, and distributing bud sites
evenly across the canopy for yields of 550g per square yard.

### Outdoor Cultivation

Outdoors, Y Griega thrives in warm, Mediterranean-style climates with long [[jeavons-climate-adaptation-growing-seasons]].
Plants reach their full towering potential in open air and are typically ready for harvest
at the **beginning of November**. The extended flowering period means outdoor cultivation
is best suited to regions with mild autumns.

## Aroma and Flavor

Y Griega produces a **classic Haze scent** — pungent, earthy, and floral with sharp spicy
undertones. The [[terpene-profile-chemotaxonomy]] is likely dominated by:

- **Terpinolene** — floral, fruity, and slightly herbal notes
- **Myrcene** — earthy, musky base notes
