## Cannabis and AIDS-Related Neuropathy

## Overview

AIDS-related neuropathy is a common and debilitating complication of HIV
infection. HIV-associated sensory neuropathy (HIV-SN) affects a significant
proportion of people living with HIV/AIDS and can severely impact quality of
life. The condition involves damage to peripheral nerves, leading to pain,
tingling, numbness, and burning sensations, typically in the hands and feet.
