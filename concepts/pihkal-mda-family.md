# MDA Family — Empathogenic Phenethylamines from PIHKAL

The MDxx family represents the most culturally significant group of
compounds from [[entheogenic-ceremony-preparation]].

## MDA (3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine)

MDA was first synthesized in 1910 but remained obscure until the 1960s
counterculture. Known as "Sass" before MDMA overshadowed it, MDA
occupies a unique position between classical psychedelics and empathogens.

**Dosage:** 80-150 mg (oral)
**Duration:** 8-12 hours
**[[tihkal-shulgin-rating-scale]]:** +++

Shulgin described MDA as having more pronounced psychedelic character
than MDMA, with stronger visual effects and more intense cognitive
experience. The emotional warmth is present but accompanied by greater
perceptual alteration. MDA's amphetamine backbone produces more
stimulation than MDMA, contributing to its longer duration. At higher
doses, MDA can produce experiences indistinguishable from classical
psychedelics, complete with [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]].

The distinction between MDA and MDMA became one of Shulgin's most
important observations: the N-methyl group in MDMA dramatically shifts
the experiential profile from psychedelic-dominant toward
empathogen-dominant. This single structural change transforms the
compound while preserving the core methylenedioxy framework that drives
serotonin release.
