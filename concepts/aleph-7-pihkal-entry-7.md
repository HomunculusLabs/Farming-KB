# ALEPH-7

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propylthioamphetamine

## Overview

ALEPH-7 is the most pharmacologically distinctive member of the [ALEPH family, notable for defining the "Beth state" -- a unique altered consciousness characterized by uncaring, anhedonia, and emotionlessness. It is the most potent of the ALEPH series (4-7 mg) and the longest-acting (15-30 hours). The compound produces profound, neutral psychedelic states unlike anything else in the phenethylamine repertoire, and its effects are memorable enough that several experienced users reported they would never take it again.

## Chemical Information

- Chemical name: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-(n)-propylthioamphetamine
- CAS: Not widely registered
- Molecular formula: C14H23NO2S (free base)
- Molecular weight: 269.41 g/mol (free base)
- Class: Substituted amphetamine (ALEPH family, sulfur-substituted DOx variant)
- The propylthio homologue of [ALEPH; amphetamine counterpart of [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] (2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylthiophenethylamine). The 2C-T-7 became far more widely known and controversial due to several fatalities associated with its use, leading to emergency scheduling. ALEPH-7's profile suggests the propylthio substitution pattern produces particularly long-acting and unusual psychological states regardless of backbone length.

## Synthesis Summary

Synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxy-4-((n)-propylthio)benzaldehyde (see [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]] for aldehyde synthesis) via condensation with nitroethane and ammonium acetate ([[scythian-cannabis-steam-bath-ritual]], overnight), yielding the nitrostyrene as orange crystals (mp 83-84 C). Reduction with LAH in THF requires extended reaction time (2 days reflux + 2 days at room temperature). The product is isolated via extraction and converted to HCl salt as an off-white powder.

## Comparison to Related Compounds

- [ALEPH: Shorter-acting (6-8h), warmer, more intellectual stimulation. ALEPH-7 is colder, longer, and uniquely neutral.
- [ALEPH-2: Ethylthio analog with 8-16h duration. ALEPH-2 is warmer and more visually engaging.
- [[2c-d-pihkal-entry-23]]: The 2-carbon homologue. 2C-T-7 became notorious for associated fatalities; ALEPH-7 shares the propylthio group but has an amphetamine backbone.
- [DOM: Both are long-acting DOx-family compounds, but DOM is classically psychedelic while ALEPH-7 produces the distinctive neutral "Beth state."

## Harm Reduction Notes

- EXTREMELY LOW active dose (4-7 mg). Milligram-accurate scales are absolutely essential.
- EXTREMELY LONG DURATION (15-30 hours). Clear 48+ hours.
- The "Beth state" (emotional neutrality/uncaring) can be deeply unsettling. Having a trusted sitter is strongly recommended.
- Driving under the influence was attempted and described as "completely stupid." NEVER drive on ALEPH-7.
- Multiple experienced psychedelic users stated they would not take this compound again. Approach with extreme caution.
- The [ALEPH family shows severe inter-individual variability.
