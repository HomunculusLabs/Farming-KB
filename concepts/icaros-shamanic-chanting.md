# Icaros — Shamanic Chanting in Ayahuasca Ceremonies

Icaros (also spelled *ikaros*) are sacred melodic chants sung by Amazonian shamans during ayahuasca ceremonies. They serve as one of the most distinctive and essential elements of the ayahuasca experience, functioning simultaneously as [[fukuoka-textdoc-weeds-as-soil-diagnostic-tools-orchard-restoration]], healing instruments, energetic protection, and maps of the visionary landscape encountered under the influence of the brew.

## Definition and Cultural Context

The term "icaro" derives from the Quechua word *ikaray*, meaning "to blow smoke" — a reference to the practice of blowing tobacco or other sacred smoke over a patient while singing. Icaros are integral to the shamanic traditions of numerous Amazonian indigenous groups, but are most elaborately developed among the Shipibo-Conibo people of the Ucayali River basin in Peru, whose icaros are renowned throughout the Amazon for their complexity and power.

Among the Shipibo-Conibo, icaros are understood not merely as songs but as living energetic patterns — vibrational codes that can directly influence physical, emotional, and spiritual reality. Each icaro is believed to contain specific healing properties, and experienced shamans may know hundreds of distinct icaros for different purposes, from curing specific illnesses to calling protective spirits to managing the intensity of the ayahuasca experience.

Icaros are typically received by shamans through visionary experiences during their own ayahuasca ceremonies. A shaman may hear a new icaro in a vision and then learn to reproduce it, or they may inherit icaros from their teachers along a lineage of transmission that can span generations. The acquisition of icaros is a central component of shamanic training and expertise.

## Musical and Structural Characteristics

### Melodic and Rhythmic Features

Icaros vary enormously in their musical characteristics, reflecting the diverse traditions from which they originate and the specific purposes they serve. They range from simple, repetitive melodic phrases of just a few notes to elaborate compositions with complex structures and wide vocal ranges.
