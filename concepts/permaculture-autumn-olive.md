## Botanical Description

Autumn Olive is a deciduous shrub or small tree typically growing 10-20 feet (3-6 m) tall with a similar spread. It has silvery-green foliage and produces abundant small, red-silver berries in autumn. The plant can live 30-50 years and forms an extensive root system with nitrogen-fixing nodules from symbiotic actinorhizal bacteria.
