- **Temperate species**: Primarily autumn-fruiting; P. semilanceata
  fruits September through November in the northern hemisphere
- **Tropical species**: Fruiting year-round, often during rainy seasons
- **Pacific Northwest**: P. azurescens fruits September through
  December; P. cyanescens from October through January
- **Australia**: P. subaeruginosa fruits May to July (southern autumn)
