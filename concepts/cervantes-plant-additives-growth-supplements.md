## Plant Additives and Growth Supplements

Numerous additives and growth supplements have entered the cannabis
growing market. Generally, additives contain a cocktail of beneficial
elements. Most came from the greenhouse industry or were developed for
organic growers. Many do what they claim, but when growing a short
eight to ten-week crop, some additives do not have time to work
properly if added near the end of flowering. See also
[[cervantes-rooting-hormones-cloning]].

### Cytokinins
Plant hormones derived from the purine adenine, the most common being
Zeatin. Synthesized in the roots, they promote cell division,
chloroplast development, leaf development, and delay leaf senescence.
As an additive, cytokinins are most often derived from seaweed
(Ascophyllum nodosum). They help plants make more efficient use of
nutrients and water even in drought conditions.

### Gibberellins (Gibberellic Acid, GA)
A natural plant growth hormone that acts with auxins to break
dormancy, stimulate [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-fungi-biology-symbiosis]] and rhizobacteria are extremely beneficial in
[[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]]
while providing an additional nitrogen source. They are most effective
when added to irrigation water. Benefits depend on proper crop and
rhizobium match.

### Trichoderma
Fungi that colonize the root zone, crowding out negative fungi and
microorganisms while stimulating root development and resistance to
environmental stress. See [[hamilton-organic-fertilizers-and-plant-nutrition]].

### Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C)
Thought to build tighter, heavier buds and act as an antioxidant. Often
combined with fructose, molasses, or sugar and added to the nutrient
solution during the last two weeks before harvest.

### Aspirin (Salicylic Acid)
A naturally occurring plant hormone associated with willow. It is
effective in preventing pathogens by speeding up "systemic acquired
resistance" (SAR), reducing the need for pesticides. A 1:10,000
solution used as a spray will stimulate the SAR response lasting weeks
to months. "Willow water" also makes a popular rooting bath.
