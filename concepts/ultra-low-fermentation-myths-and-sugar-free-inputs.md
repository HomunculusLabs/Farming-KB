## The Sugar and Molasses Problem

Modern organic farming workshops and publications frequently promote
the use of sugar, molasses, vinegar, wood vinegar, and commercial
microorganisms in making [[fukuoka-microbes-scavengers-natural-decomposition-biosystem]] processes.

Our ancestors farmed with human manure, food waste, and green manure
liquid fertilizers for thousands of years. They all smelled. Those
farmers even had the ability to judge nitrogen content by smell and
adjust dosage accordingly. The labeling of smell as "bad" drove
traditional farming practices into disrepute and opened the door for
commercial input manufacturers to capture the market.

## The JADAM Alternative: Water and Leaf Mold

JADAM's method for making all organic farming inputs is radically
simple: prepare a container, add the organic ingredient, fill with
water, and add a handful of leaf mold. That is all. No sugar, no
molasses, no commercial microorganisms, no temperature control, no
aeration, no C/N ratio calculations, no pH monitoring.
