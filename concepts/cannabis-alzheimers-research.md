# Cannabis and Alzheimer's Disease Research

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder and the most common cause of dementia. Cannabis research suggests potential neuroprotective properties of cannabinoids that may slow disease progression, reduce inflammation, and manage behavioral symptoms.

## Condition Overview

### Definition
Alzheimer's disease is a progressive brain disorder that gradually destroys memory, thinking skills, and eventually the ability to carry out simple tasks. It is characterized by the accumulation of amyloid-beta plaques and neurofibrillary tangles in the brain.

### Symptoms
- **Early stage:** Short-term memory loss, difficulty finding words, mood changes
- **Middle stage:** Increased confusion, wandering, behavioral changes, difficulty with daily activities
- **Late stage:** Loss of communication, complete dependence on others, inability to recognize loved ones

### Prevalence
- Over 6 million Americans have Alzheimer's; worldwide prevalence exceeds 55 million
- Risk doubles every 5 years after age 65
- Women are disproportionately affected

### Orthodox Treatment
No cure exists. Current treatments focus on:
- Cholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine)
- NMDA receptor antagonist (memantine)
- Management of behavioral symptoms (antidepressants, antipsychotics)
- Lifestyle modifications (diet, exercise, cognitive stimulation)

### Key Research Findings

1. **Amyloid-beta reduction:** THC was shown to reduce the accumulation of amyloid-beta plaques, which are a primary pathological marker of Alzheimer's disease

2. **Neuroinflammation reduction:** Activation of CB2 receptors reduced neuroinflammation, which is increasingly recognized as a major contributor to Alzheimer's progression

3. **Neuroprotection:** Both THC and CBD demonstrated neuroprotective properties, potentially slowing the death of brain cells

4. **Antioxidant effects:** CBD has well-documented antioxidant properties that may protect neurons from [[cannabis-cancer-symptom-management]]:** Anecdotal and clinical evidence suggests cannabis may help manage agitation, aggression, sleep disturbance, and appetite loss in Alzheimer's patients

### Mechanism of Action

The endocannabinoid system plays multiple roles relevant to Alzheimer's:
- **CB1 receptors:** Modulate memory, learning, and neuronal survival
- **CB2 receptors:** Primarily involved in inflammation regulation in the brain
- **Neurogenesis:** Cannabinoids may promote growth of new neurons in the hippocampus (memory center)
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Both CB1 and CB2 activation reduce neuroinflammation
- **Antioxidant:** CBD scavenges free radicals and reduces oxidative stress
