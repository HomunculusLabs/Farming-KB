## Holzer Brandroggen (Fire Rye) Growing and Processing

Brandroggen literally means "fire rye" -- rye varieties sown after plant matter has been burnt on meadow land. This is a traditional Austrian mountain [[oyster-mushroom-spawn-types-and-quality-grain-sawdust-plug-liquid]] for milling and [[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]]

## Barn Storage and Arrangement

1. Bring sheaves into barn after field ripening
2. Put sheaves into troughs
3. Arrange carefully: lean sheaves in a circle working from middle outward
4. All ears point to the centre
5. Make best use of available space
6. When one circle is full, start the next
7. Continue until all grain is in place and troughs are full
8. Grain stored there until other autumn tasks (wood cutting) are completed

## Threshing (November)

- Performed during bad weather when outside work is impossible
- Threshing machine powered by old paraffin or petrol engine
- Laborious, dusty, and exhausting work
- Ears are chopped up and separated from the straw
- Tidy threshing machine between different varieties

## Winnowing

- Hand-operated winnowing machine produces a stream of air
- Separates material by weight:

| Output | Material | Use |
|--------|----------|-----|
| Light (blown away) | Blind ears, straw pieces | Livestock bedding; mixed with bran + hot water for feed paste |
| Fine (through sieve) | Broken grains, sand | Chicken feed |
| Bulky (left over) | Stalks, stones, droppings | Chickens scratch out remaining grains |
| Main chute | Clean seed and milling grain | Seed saving and flour milling |

## Traditional Wisdom

Every single product of the threshing and winnowing process is used on the farm. Nothing is wasted -- the blind ears become bedding or feed, broken grains feed chickens, stalks provide scratching material, and only the finest clean grain is used for seed and bread. This complete utilization serves as an example against modern throw-away culture.
