# KNF Poultry Feed Preparation

Korean Natural Farming (KNF) emphasizes the use of homemade chicken feed as a cornerstone of its poultry management system.

Developed by [[knf-poultry-natural-farming]].

## Brown Rice and Bamboo Leaves for Chicks

In Natural Farming, newly hatched chicks are fed whole brown rice grains in unlimited quantity.

This replaces the enriched commercial feed that is standard in conventional hatcheries.

The whole grains require the chicks to actively digest their food, which strengthens their developing intestinal tracts.

After three days for layer breeds and one day for broiler breeds, bamboo leaves are added to the diet.

Bamboo leaves provide additional fiber and natural compounds that support digestive health.

On Day 50, rice husk is introduced into the feed mixture.

Gradually, the proportion of rice husk is increased over time.

By six months of age, rice husk constitutes 20 to 25 percent of the total feed, at which point the egg-laying rate reaches approximately 60 percent.

## Homemade Feed Composition

One of the key insights of KNF poultry feeding is that any material except mineral matter can be given as feed.

This opens up a wide range of locally available, low-cost feed options.

Fresh green grass is an excellent feed source and makes up approximately one-third of the total feed for adult chickens.

Green grass provides essential vitamins, minerals, and roughage that commercial feeds cannot replicate.

Feed can also be found directly on the housing floors of the NF poultry shed.
