## Companion Planting Within Beds

The spot rotation system naturally integrates companion planting. Since
different crop families are interspersed throughout each bed, the
opportunity for beneficial plant interactions increases. Mollison
describes several classic companion combinations:

- Tomatoes interplanted with basil and marigolds, which deter nematodes
  and whitefly
- Carrots and onions planted in adjacent spots, as each repels the
  other's primary pests
- Beans climbing corn stalks, fixing nitrogen for the heavy-feeding
  maize
- Nasturtiums at bed edges to trap aphids away from vulnerable crops
