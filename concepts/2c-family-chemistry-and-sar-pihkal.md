## Overview

The 2C-family represents the phenethylamine (two-carbon chain) counterparts to the DO-family amphetamines. Named for the two-carbon chain between the aromatic ring and the amine group (as opposed to the three-carbon chain of amphetamines), these compounds form one of the most extensively explored chemical families in PiHKAL.
