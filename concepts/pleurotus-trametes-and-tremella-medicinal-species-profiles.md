# Pleurotus, Trametes, and Tremella Medicinal Species Profiles

Three distinct mushroom genera—Pleurotus ([[oyster-mushrooms]]), Trametes (turkey tail), and Tremella (jelly fungi)—represent important medicinal species with diverse bioactive compounds ranging from cholesterol-lowering statins to powerful immunomodulators.

## Pleurotus ostreatus (Oyster Mushroom)

### Description and Habitat
The fruit-body is oyster-shaped, growing in layered clusters on deciduous trees worldwide. Referred to in the Sung dynasty (A.D. 420-479) as "the mushroom of flower heaven." One of the easiest mushrooms to grow, typically on straw or sawdust logs.

### Edible and Medicinal
Popular edible mushroom with pleasant odour and taste. First artificially cultivated in USA. Production now worldwide.

### Cross-Cultural Medicinal Discovery
"The medicinally beneficial effects of Pleurotus spp. were discovered independently on different continents." Awareness of medicinal properties comes not only from Asia but from the folklore of **central Europe, South America, and Africa**.

### Cholesterol-Lowering: Natural Lovastatin
"The major cause of death in the Western hemisphere is coronary artery disease with hypercholesterolemia as a primary risk factor." Species of Pleurotus are **excellent producers of lovastatin** (mevinolin) and could be considered as a **functional food with natural cholesterol-lowering ability**.

#### Commercial Limitations
- Large-scale lovastatin production from fruit-bodies is not commercially viable due to variability in composition
- Lovastatin found mainly in lamella and basidiospores, not in stipe and cap
- Mycelial cultivation could be the way ahead

### Other Medicinal Activities
- Anti-cancer activity
- Immunomodulating effects
- Antiviral activity
- Antibiotic activity
- Anti-inflammatory activity

### Key Active Constituents
| Compound | Properties |
|----------|-----------|
| **β-glucans** | Antitumour, immunomodulation |
| **Lovastatin** | Cholesterol-lowering |

## Grifola frondosa (Maitake / Hen of the Woods)

### Clinical Evidence
"Recent studies have shown that polysaccharides and polysaccharide-protein complexes from this mushroom have significant anti-cancer activity." Limited clinical studies in Japan and the USA have shown purified polysaccharide fractions highly effective against cancers of the **breast, lung, liver, prostate, and brain**.

### Immunological Properties
β-glucan fractions now used by over **3,000 health professionals** in the US for prevention and treatment of:
- Flu and common infections (bacteria and viruses)
- AIDS (HIV)
- Diabetes mellitus
- Hypertension
- Hypercholesterolemia
- Urinary tract infections (particularly for women)

### Additional Properties
Other fractions exhibit immunological enhancement with anti-HIV, antihypertension, antidiabetic, and antiobesity properties.

### Commercial Products
- Capsules with dried Maitake (widely accepted dietary supplements)
- Maitake tea
- Whole Maitake powder
- Maitake drink

### Key Active Constituents
| Compound | Properties |
|----------|-----------|
| **1,3 and 1,6 β-glucans** | Antitumour and immunomodulating |
| **Commercial product "Grifolan"** | Standardized extract |

## Flammulina velutipes (Enokitake)

### Description
One of the most popular edible mushrooms in China and Japan. In nature, grows on stumps or decayed hardwood. Now mostly produced artificially from jars of sawdust mix. A plastic collar around the jar neck produces elongated stipes and tiny caps.

### Traditional Use
Used in traditional Chinese medicine to treat **liver diseases and gastric ulcers**. Slightly salty and bitter in taste.

### Antitumour Research
- **Flammulin**: A basic simple protein that markedly inhibits tumour cells; purified to crystalline state; clinical trials in progress
- **Proflamin**: New antitumour glycoprotein from cultured mycelium; useful in combination therapy with chemotherapy agents
- "The first scientific paper stating that edible mushrooms were effective against a solid tumour was with Flammulina"

### Epidemiological Evidence
"An epidemiological study in Nagano Prefecture, Japan showed that the cancer death rate among farmers producing F. velutipes was remarkably lower than that of other people in the Prefecture and in Japan overall."

### Key Active Ingredients
| Compound | Properties |
|----------|-----------|
| **β-glucan-protein** | Antitumour and immunomodulating |
| **β-glycoprotein (Proflamin)** | Antitumour |

## Trametes versicolor (Turkey Tail)

### Description
Multicoloured cap resembling a 'turkey tail'; occurs as overlapping clusters on dead logs worldwide. **Not edible** but hot water extracts used in traditional Chinese medicine from historical times.

### PSK ("Krestin") and PSP
Modern studies have produced two extremely important compounds:
- **PSK ("Krestin")**: Water-soluble protein-bound polysaccharide from mycelial cultures
- **PSP**: Polysaccharide-peptide from mycelial cultures

### Mechanisms of Action
- **PSK acts directly on tumour cells** (cytostatic and cytotoxic)
- **PSK boosts host cellular immunity** (indirect action)
- **Antiviral activity through stimulation of interferon production**
- **PSP is a powerful immunostimulant and anti-cancer agent**

### Clinical Significance
- Wide range of clinical trials for various human cancers
- When taken with chemotherapy or radiotherapy: **significant increases in patient longevity**
- In 1987, "Krestin" accounted for **25% of total expenditure on anti-cancer agents in Japan**
- Polysaccharopeptide shown to **inhibit HIV-1** (Collins and Ng, 1997)

### Key Active Constituents
| Compound | Properties |
|----------|-----------|
| **β-glucan-proteins** | Antitumour, antiviral, immunomodulating |
| **Ergosterol** | Provitamin D2 |

## Tremella mesenterica and T. fuciformis (White Jelly Fungus)

### Description
Known as "white auricularia" or "white jelly fungus." In Japan: **Shirokikurage**. Jelly-like, translucent fruiting-body on deciduous trees in warm climates worldwide.

### Traditional Use
Long history in traditional Chinese medicine as:
- **Immune tonic**
- Treatment for debility and exhaustion
- **Skin-care** (cosmetic applications)

### Bioactive Polysaccharides
Contains acidic polysaccharides, especially **glucuronoxylomannan**, readily extracted with hot water giving a smooth, stable solution used in Oriental cuisine.

### Clinical Evidence
- Anti-cancer activity
- Enhanced immune functions
- Effective in treating **radio- and chemo-therapy-induced leukopenia**
- Boosts immunological functions
- Stimulates leukocyte activity

### Fermenter Production
Med Myco Ltd. (Israel) developed submerged fermentation to produce **Tremellastin** from T. mesenterica mycelium containing:
- 50% glucuronoxylomannan
- Proteins rich in amino acids
- Dietary fibre
- B group vitamins

"Dietary supplements from Tremella are only now beginning to expand into the Asian market, and they will certainly be of special significance in the cosmetic industry."
