## Lignin Decomposition

Lignin is a complex, non-carbohydrate polymer that provides structural
rigidity to plant cell walls. The bonds holding lignin's chains of
alcohols together are among the strongest in biological materials.

### White Rot Fungi

White rot basidiomycetes are the primary lignin decomposers. They
produce phenol oxidase, a powerful enzyme that dissolves lignin. White
rot fungi are unique in their ability to completely mineralize lignin to
carbon dioxide and water.
