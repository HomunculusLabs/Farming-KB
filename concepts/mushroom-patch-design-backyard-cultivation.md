## Step-by-Step Bed Construction

### Step 1: Site Preparation

Clear the site of debris down to mineral earth, which in many
locations is clay. Lay down fresh wood chips to a depth of 2
inches, spreading evenly. Fresh chips without pre-existing
fungal contamination are preferable to chips already colonized
by other fungi, which would compete with introduced spawn.

Moisten the wood chips briefly before applying spawn. For every
100 square feet of bed, use 5 to 10 pounds of spawn broadcast
