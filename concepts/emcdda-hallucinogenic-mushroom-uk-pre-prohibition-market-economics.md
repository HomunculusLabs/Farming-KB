## Market Visibility and Political Salience

The UK magic mushroom market was notable for its visibility in mainstream commercial environments. Unlike most illicit drug markets that operate through hidden or informal channels, hallucinogenic mushrooms were sold openly in high street shops, weekend markets, and festival stalls.
