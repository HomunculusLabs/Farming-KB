## Maga: The Great Gift and Root of Magic

In "Amanita Muscaria: Herb of Immortality," Donald E. Teeter identifies the Proto-Indo-European word "Maga" (The Great Gift) as the original name for amanita-muscaria-living-vessel-grail), produced healing and visionary experiences, and transformed simple food and water into a communion meal with a "Living God." The "Magic" was real natural phenomena that appeared miraculous to people who did not understand mycology.

## Maga in the Soma Ceremony

In the Vedic Soma ceremony, dried Amanita muscaria earned the name Soma "the pressed one" by being rehydrated with water and then juiced by being pressed or pounded between two stones to release the golden colored Amrita -- the legendary Nectar of the Gods. To filter, the juice was poured onto a wooden trough lined with "the eternal fleece" (mycelium resembling sheep's wool), falling into a large carved wooden vat called the "Drona" into which was added barley gruel, milk, and honey. The result was poured into individual cups for consumption, generating "Divine rapturous joy."

Every vessel involved became colonized by Amanita muscaria mycelium, becoming a "living vessel" -- what later cultures called the Holy Grail. The Maga thus gives rise not only to the concept of magic but to the entire technology of the magic vessel: a container that transforms simple food and water into a communion meal with a Living God. Teeter writes: "It is this symbiosis that makes the Holy Grail truly 'Holy,' a Living Vessel that transforms the simple shared food and water into a Communion meal with a Living God."

## Maga and the Hebrew Prophets

Teeter argues that the Hebrew Prophets, as part of the ancient Persian Empire, were influenced by the Persian Prophets (Magi) who used the dried fungus. The Hebrew Prophets "rehydrated the dried fungus and pressed out a magic Elixir or used it to create the Living Bread and by its power they healed the sick, found lost objects and foretold the future." The Hebrew Prophets called this one wondrous life form by more than twenty names including "the Anointed One" -- which in Greek is "Christos," Latin "Chrestus," English "Christ." The Maga thus lies at the root not only of Western magic but of the prophetic traditions of Judaism and Christianity.

## The Living Bread: Maga's Greatest Trick

The "Living Bread" of the New Testament is Teeter's name for the ancient technology of growing Amanita muscaria mycelium on cooked grain in a shallow living vessel. When cooked grain (especially barley, which works best) is placed into a suitable vessel, it is quickly colonized by the fungus, which grows into a solid fleshy loaf covered in long wool-looking mycelium. In four to seven days this loaf can be removed, dried at low heat, and used as if it were the dried mushroom. The vessel can then be refilled to start the cycle again.

Some calculations based on experiments show that using the right techniques, an ancient person could start with a single medium-sized dried Amanita muscaria cap and in less than a month create several pounds of dried living bread and at least a dozen small living vessels. The living bread multiplies rapidly and can "easily feed a fair number of people with the Eucharist Sacrament in quantities large enough to manifest its dramatic healing effects and allow a profound spiritual awakening to be generated in the participants."

## The Roots of Western Civilization

Teeter positions the Great Gift as the foundation of Western civilization itself: "in its original discovery by ancient western man are the roots of our western magic, religion, science and medicine." The ancient Magi who used it were "masters at employing drug plants for healing without knowledge of the underlying chemistry" -- morphine was not isolated from opium until 1806. The Magi's empirical knowledge of the Great Gift's properties preceded modern pharmacology by thousands of years.

The stories of the ancients say the Maga "may be the Secret to the Greatest Gift of them all, that of an extra long, healthy, and happy life." The Great Gift "just keeps giving its own flesh and blood and by doing so, heals the sick and inspires human minds with its unique Wisdom."

## Magic as Natural Phenomena

Teeter's analysis reframes the entire concept of magic. What ancient people called "magic" was not supernatural but the observable effects of a real biological organism. A mushroom that appeared to die when dried yet resurrected when rehydrated; that could live indefinitely in a suitable container; that produced healing and visionary experiences; that transformed simple food and water into a communion meal with a "Living God"; whose extract became more potent when diluted with water rather than weaker -- "The 'Magic' was real natural phenomena that appeared miraculous to people who did not understand mycology."

The Magi were the original scientists of the ancient world -- healers, seers, and keepers of empirical knowledge about a remarkable organism. Their "magic" was technology, their "miracles" were repeatable experimental results, and their "God" was a real, visible, physically immortal life form of immense benefit to mankind.

## The Grail as Maga's Vessel

The Grail is one of Amanita muscaria's "greatest Gifts to mankind, a magical never ending source of inspiration, enlightenment, healing, comfort, joy, and by its very existence, astonishment!" The creation of a Grail involved the Maga -- the Great Gift -- being crushed and consumed, then resurrecting in a suitable container. The resulting symbiosis between mortal man and immortal plant is the "real Natural Magic" behind all Eurasian stories of magic cups, cauldrons, and bowls.

Teeter concludes that "Amanita Muscaria is NATURAL MAGIC" -- the only logical explanation for the ancient legends of magic vessels across Eurasia. The Grail "matches the Grail of ancient legends perfectly," from its never-ending supply of wisdom and inspiration to the spicy smell described in Grail legend.
