# Cold Climate Gardening

Growing food in cold climates (zones 3-6) requires specific strategies: season extension, cold-hardy varieties, [[succession-planting]], and protected cultivation. Drawn from [[nicole-faires]]' comprehensive manual.

## Zone 5-6 Growing Season

- Last frost (spring): May 10-20
- First frost (fall): September 20 - October 10
- Frost-free days: 120-160
- Coldest temperatures: -20F to -10F (-29 to -23C)

The key challenge is maximizing production within a short growing season while extending it on both ends.

## Season Extension Techniques

See [[season-extension-techniques]] for detailed construction guides.

### Cold Frames

- Simple wooden box with a glass or polycarbonate lid
- Extends season 4-6 weeks on each end
- Can grow greens through winter in zones 5-6
- Dimensions: 3x6 feet, 12-18 inches tall at back, 8-10 inches at front
- Prop open on sunny days above 45F, close at night
- Ideal for: spinach, lettuce, radishes, Asian greens, scallions

### Row Covers (Floating Row Cover)

- Spun-bonded polypropylene fabric laid directly over crops
- Lightweight (0.5 oz): protects to 28F (-2C), 90% light transmission
- Medium (1.0 oz): protects to 24F (-4C), 70% light transmission
- Heavy (1.5 oz): protects to 20F (-7C), 50% light transmission
- Wire hoops or PVC supports keep fabric off plants
- Water and air pass through
- Must be removed during flowering for insect-pollinated crops (tomatoes, squash)

### Low Tunnels (Hoop Houses)

- Metal or PVC hoops covered with polyethylene film
- 6-8 feet wide, 4-6 feet tall
- Extends season 6-8 weeks on each end
- Can grow cold-hardy crops through winter
- Ventilate on sunny days (open ends or roll up sides)
- Cost: $100-300 for a 20-foot tunnel

### Wall-o-Water

- Plastic water-filled teepee around individual plants
- Protects to 16F (-9C)
- Water absorbs heat during day, releases at night
- Most commonly used for tomatoes and peppers
- Install 1-2 weeks before transplanting to warm soil
- Remove once plants outgrow the unit

### Greenhouses

See [[greenhouse-design]] for detailed construction.

- Freestanding: 14x24 feet minimum for efficiency
- Attached (lean-to): heats house, smaller footprint
- Unheated: zone 7-8 inside during winter
- Minimal heat (compost or small heater): zone 8-9
- Year-round growing possible with proper design

### Mulching for Winter Protection

- Apply 6-12 inches of straw or leaves after first hard freeze (not before -- plants need to harden off)
- Straw mulch: best insulation, stays loose
- Leaves: good but can mat and smother
- Evergreen boughs: excellent for protecting perennials
- Do not mulch warm-season crops (tomatoes, peppers) -- they will rot

## Succession Planting

### Spring Planting Schedule (Zone 5)

| Date | Crop | Method |
|------|------|--------|
| Mar 1 (indoors) | Peppers, eggplant, tomatoes | Seed indoors, 8-10 weeks before last frost |
| Mar 15 (indoors) | Broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower | Seed indoors, 6-8 weeks before last frost |
| Apr 1 (outdoor, [[cold-frame]]) | Spinach, lettuce, radishes, peas | Direct sow under cold frame |
| Apr 15 (outdoor) | Potatoes, onion sets, asparagus crowns | Direct sow/plant |
| May 1 (outdoor) | Beets, carrots, Swiss chard, lettuce | Direct sow (row cover ready) |
| May 10-20 | Tomatoes, peppers, eggplant | Transplant after last frost |
| May 15 | Beans, corn, cucumbers, squash | Direct sow |
| Jun 1 | Succession beans, cilantro, dill | Second planting |

### Fall Planting Schedule (Zone 5)

| Date | Crop | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Jul 1 | Fall broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower | Start indoors, transplant Aug 1 |
| Jul 15 | Carrots, beets, turnips | Direct sow |
| Aug 1 | Spinach, lettuce, radishes | Direct sow, cover later |
| Aug 15 | Asian greens, mache, claytonia | Direct sow under cold frame |
| Sep 1 | Garlic (plant) | Plant cloves 2-3 inches deep, mulch heavily |
| Sep 15 | Spinach (overwintering) | Direct sow under cold frame |
| Oct 1 | Mulch all winter crops | After first hard freeze |

### Crop Rotation by Year

Rotate crop families to prevent disease and pest buildup:

- Year 1: Nightshades (tomatoes, peppers, potatoes, eggplant)
- Year 2: Legumes (beans, peas)
- Year 3: Brassicas (cabbage, broccoli, cauliflower, kale)
- Year 4: Roots (carrots, onions, beets, parsnips)
- Year 5: Squash family (cucumbers, squash, melons, pumpkins)
- Year 6: Grains/corn or fallow with [[cover-crops]]
