## When Liming Might Still Be Needed

- **Extremely acidic soils** (pH below 5.0) may need a one-time
  corrective lime application before starting the COF maintenance
  program.
- **Sandy soils** with very low CEC may need more frequent calcium
  attention than COF alone provides.
- **Magnesium-excessive soils** (from years of dolomite application)
  benefit from using high-calcium lime rather than dolomitic in the
  COF formula.

In all cases, the COF formula can be adjusted for specific conditions,
but the principle remains: apply a balanced mineral amendment rather
than chasing pH numbers.
