## Fungal Biodegradation of Coal

Fungi play a significant role in the transformation and biodegradation of
coal, particularly low-rank coal (LRC) such as lignite and brown coal.
These processes involve both solubilization by molds and yeasts and
depolymerization by ligninolytic Basidiomycetes, offering potential
pathways for coal desulfurization, humic substance production, and
environmental remediation of coal-contaminated sites.
