## The Lignocellulose Challenge

Standing-dead smooth cordgrass is composed largely of lignocellulose (70-75%), posing a significant decomposition challenge. Early testing suggested cordgrass ascomycetes were weak lignocellulose degraders (less than 0.1% per day mineralization), but this was likely an artifact of unfavourable microcosm conditions, particularly violent shaking and absence of non-lignocellulose carbohydrates.
