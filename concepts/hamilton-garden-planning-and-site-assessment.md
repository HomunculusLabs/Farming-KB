### Soil Types and Their Management

| Soil Type | Characteristics | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|-----------|----------------|------------|---------------|
| Clay | Heavy, cold, sticky when wet, hard when dry | Well-supplied with plant food, supports wide variety | Poor drainage, difficult to work, slow to warm |
| Sand | Light, gritty, free-draining | Easy to work, warms quickly in spring | Hungry, loses water and nutrients rapidly |
| Silt | Smooth, silky | Fertile | Packs down, poor drainage when wet |
| Chalk | Pale, alkaline, often rocky | Free-draining | Thin, hungry, alkaline limits plant choice |
| Peat | Dark, spongy, rich in organic matter | Very fertile, easy to work | Acidic, prone to waterlogging, can dry out and shrink |
