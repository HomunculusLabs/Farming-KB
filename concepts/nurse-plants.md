## When to Remove Nurse Plants

Nurse plants are often short-lived pioneers. Their decline is natural
and expected. Sometimes it is possible to save a desirable nurse plant
by pruning back its competitors. Other times, the nurse can be slashed
for mulch when it begins to compete with the established plant.
