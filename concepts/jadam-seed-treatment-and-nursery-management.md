# JADAM Seed Treatment and [[nursery-management]]

## Overview

JADAM seed treatment and nursery management focus on establishing strong microbial diversity from the very beginning of a plant's life. Rather than using chemical seed disinfectants, JADAM uses natural methods -- JADAM sulfur (JS) immersion and JADAM microorganism solution (JMS) soaking -- to protect seeds and seedlings while promoting vigorous root settlement.

## JADAM Seed Treatment

### Rice Seed Disinfection (Bakanae Disease Control)

Developed by Seonseop Jeong, Youngsu Hyeon, Seonsu Gim, and [[youngsang-cho]].

**Recipe:**
- JADAM sulfur (JS) -- 5 L (1.3 gal)
- Add water to make 500 L (132 gal)
- For smaller batches (20 L): use JS 0.2 L (0.053 gal)

**Procedure:**
1. Put rice seeds in small porous bags
2. Immerse in cold JS solution for 48 hours
3. A white film will form on the surface (normal)
4. Rinse very thoroughly with water
5. Start sprouting

**Variations:**
- For government-supplied varieties: rinse and rub 3 times before immersing
- For rice seeds with thick husks: increase JS to 7 L (1.85 gal)
- For waxy rice and colored rice: use 7 L of JS
- This method can replace chemical seed disinfectant
- Also applicable to garlic and onion seeds (immerse ~30 minutes, time can vary)

### General Seed/Seedling Treatment with JMS

**Recipe for 500 L (132 gal):**
- JMS -- 100 L (26 gal) -- can be replaced with 10 kg (22 lb) of leaf mold

**Procedure:**
- Soak seeds or seedlings in JMS dilution or leaf mold solution for 2-3 minutes before planting
- Greatly improves root settlement
- Diversity of microbes prevents diseases

## Nursery Soil Foundation

The key to successful nursery management in JADAM is building the soil foundation **before** planting or transplanting. High yield is determined at the early stage of plant growth. How well the root settles right after transplanting decides the yield.

### Soil Foundation Builder (for 0.33 ha / 0.8 ac)

Apply mixed with water or undiluted before rain for open fields. For greenhouses, apply with plenty of water so inputs go deep (>1 meter). Repeat 3-4 times before transplanting/lowering.

- JMS -- 500 L (132 gal)
- Sea salt -- 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- Phyllite solution -- 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF (wild grass/crop residue JLF) -- 60 L (16 gal)

### When to Apply

- For annual crops: before transplanting
- For fruit trees: before lowering/sprouting
- 500 L of JMS can cover 0.1 to 3.3 ha (0.25-8.2 ac)
- Promotes root growth; strengthens resistance against cold and frost
- For trees, can be applied directly on trunks

## Nursery Disease Control

### If Seedlings Are Diseased in Seedbed

**Recipe for 500 L (132 gal):**
- JADAM sulfur (JS) -- 1.5 L (0.4 gal)
- JADAM wetting agent (JWA) -- 3 L (0.8 gal)
- Add water to make 500 L
- Spray on affected seedlings

## Key Principles

1. **Root settlement is everything:** The moment of transplanting is critical. Strong root foundation means the plant has secured a stable supply of water and nutrients for its entire life.

2. **Microbial diversity from day one:** Seeds and seedlings treated with JMS carry diverse microorganisms that protect against pathogens.

3. **Soil application before planting:** Apply JMS + seawater + phyllite solution + JLF generously and often before sprouting or transplanting. This is the most important technique in JADAM organic farming.

4. **Foliar as secondary:** For greenhouse nursery plants, if using foliar application, reduce JMS + JLF inputs to below 30 L per 500 L total solution and add over 1.5 L of JWA to prevent marks on leaves.

5. **Temperature-matched microbes:** Culture JMS at ambient temperature matching the nursery environment. Microbes adapted to the same conditions as the seedlings are most beneficial.
