# Reflux Apparatus and Laboratory Setup

## Overview

The essential oil of cannabis is extracted by refluxing (boiling) the
plant material in a solvent. The oil, containing THC and related
substances, chlorophyll, and taste/smell compounds, dissolves in the
solvent (usually alcohol), while cellulose does not. The leached
material is strained out and the solvent evaporated, leaving the
essential oil as residue. Since boiling solvents is dangerous (fumes
and liquid are highly flammable), specialized methods are necessary
for safe operation.

## Apparatus Components

1. A small stainless-steel pot for powdered cannabis and solvent (not
   over two-thirds full when covered with half again its volume in
   solvent).
2. A large stew pot with lid at least fifty percent wider and twice
   as deep as the smaller pot. Both pots must have flat bottoms.
3. A large, deep tub for boiling water, at least twice as wide as
   the stew pot.
4. A heavy-duty electric hotplate with two burners.
5. Several yards of one-inch hemp or manila rope.
6. Large, thick polyethylene trash bags (three-mil thickness best).
7. Inner-tube cut into one-inch-wide rubber bands for the stew pot.

## Assembly Instructions

Place the large tub on the hotplate. Lay rope lengths in the tub
bottom to keep the stew pot from resting directly on the tub. Place
the small pot with cannabis and solvent inside the stew pot, and set
the lid on top inverted (upside down). Cut a plastic trash bag to
cover the inverted lid, extending halfway down the side. Secure it
with two large inner-tube rubber bands positioned several inches
down, allowing slack. Force out trapped air by loosening bands and
flattening the bag. Pile ice on the plastic covering the lid. Fill
the tub half full of water and bring to boil (max ~212 degrees F).

## Condensation and Reflux Principle

As the small pot is heated by boiling water, the solvent boils.
Fumes rise and contact the ice-cooled inverted lid, condense to
liquid, and drop back into the pot. By refluxing this way, there is
no danger of explosion or toxic fumes escaping. The cannabis and
solvent are not placed directly in the stew pot because the
condensing surface area must be larger than the boiling solution's
surface area.

## Safety Features

The plastic sheeting completely seals the reaction from the
atmosphere, preventing fume escape or ignition. A rigid seal (like a
pressure cooker) is avoided because it would prevent pressure
buildup from inflating the plastic bag. This inflation serves two
purposes: it notifies the chemist of pressure increase, and causes
ice to fall into the boiling water bath, cooling the rig safely.

Pressure builds dangerously only if ice is neglected or the
apparatus heats too fast before the lid is sufficiently cooled.
Reflux for three to four hours dissolves most essential oils into the
solvent. See
[[lemon-cannabis-alchemy-high-volume-extraction-method]] for the
55-gallon drum apparatus performing refluxing, soxhleting,
distilling, and solvent collection at large scale. The fundamental
approach of water-bath heating, ice-cooled condensation, and
plastic-sheet pressure relief remains constant regardless of scale.
