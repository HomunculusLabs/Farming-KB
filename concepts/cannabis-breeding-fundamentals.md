# Cannabis Breeding Fundamentals

Cannabis breeding is the science and practice of developing new cannabis
varieties through controlled [[vic-high-cannabis-genetics-mendelian-inheritance]]
and breeding methodology available to growers.

## Sexual vs. Asexual Propagation

Cannabis can be reproduced in two fundamentally different ways. Asexual
propagation, commonly called cloning, involves taking cuttings from a
donor plant and inducing root formation. These rooted cuttings are
genetically identical replicas of the parent, enabling growers to plant
uniform crops with predictable growth, yield, and product consistency.
Gardens propagated solely from cannabis-cloning|clones are the most
productive and consistent.

Sexual propagation involves the union of male and female sex cells
(gametes) from separate parents within the female plant. Pollen from a
staminate (male) parent fertilizes an ovule in the pistillate (female)
parent to create an embryo, which matures into a seed. Each seed is
genetically unique, containing some genes from each parent. This
variation allows breeders to combine positive traits from different
parental lines through recombination. See also
[[cannabis-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium]].
