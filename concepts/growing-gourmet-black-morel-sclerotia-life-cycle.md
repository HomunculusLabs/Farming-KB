## The Morel Life Cycle

Morel spores germinate quickly. The hyphae race through the
environment -- up to four inches per day. Morel mycelium can
colonize a vast territory in a relatively short time. But when
they encounter a physical boundary, a non-nutritional zone, or
competitors, the mycelium stops expanding.

## Differences Between Black and Yellow Morel Sclerotia

The sclerotia of the Black Morel are uniquely different from all
the other Morel species:

**Black Morel sclerotia (M. angusticeps):**
- Abrasive, golden yellow to orange
- Light in weight when fresh
- Pumice-like texture
- Form by the thousands per cubic foot
- Dense clusters

**Yellow Morel sclerotia (M. esculenta, M. crassipes):**
- Dense, slippery, and heavy when fresh
- Dark and walnut-like
- Comparatively few in number

The differences between these two groups are soon seen after the
clones or spores are put into culture.
