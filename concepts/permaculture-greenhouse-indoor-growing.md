# Permaculture Greenhouse and Indoor Growing (Design Course)

**Source:** Permaculture Design Course, Bill Mollison (1981, Pamphlet IX)
**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

Greenhouses provide opportunities for growing foods otherwise imported from distant peasant economies at great human cost. Mollison emphasizes growing tropical and subtropical species that are normally imported.

## Key Tropical/Subtropical Crops for Greenhouses

### Ginger
- Put a green ginger root in a small tub — continuous ginger supply
- One plant gives all the ginger needed; grows easily from cuttings

### Papaya (Papaw)
- Self-seeding; comes up all over the place
- In cool areas: lasts 30–40 years; in true tropics: only ~4 years

### Banana
- Sends up four shoots — give extras to friends
- Cut off spice plants and stick in ground — they regrow

### Pineapple
- One person might eat 20 per year — very easily grown in greenhouses
- Quail nest ideally under pineapple plant curves; they eat tiny insects and whitefly

### Coffee
- Very shade-tolerant indoor shrub with beautiful flowers and aroma
- Lives well in offices under artificial light; bears heavy crops of berries
- Roast your own — pounds and pounds from a single plant

### Tea (Camellia sinensis)
- Grows in open up to latitude 40 degrees
- One plant gives all the Camellia tea you want
- Better as green tea, but can be fermented

### Turmeric
- Equally easy to grow as ginger

### Vanilla
- Hand-pollinated with a feather (3 trips per bean: cut/bend, pollinate, clip)
- Sweat beans in woolen blanket — one plant gives hundreds of beans
- Better commercial crop than tomatoes

## Design Features

- **Attach kitchen to greenhouse** with connecting entrance and direct view from dishwashing area
- **Internal pond** connected by pipe to outside pond — fish overwinter inside
- **Quail** for cockroach and whitefly control
- **Azolla** (nitrogen-fixing fern) — spreads across water surface in 1–2 weeks; 18 species from Canadian border to equator
- **Taro** production in indoor water — frost prevents outdoor growing in many climates
- **Salable bulbs** rooted on mesh suspended above water in ponds

## Swimming Pool Retrofit

- Rent or retrofit unused swimming pools for spring bulb production
- Grow bulbs on rafts; add blueberry edges and frogs
- One Melbourne grower rents people's swimming pools for bulb production before summer use

## Highest Returns

- Flowers, bulbs, ferns, and indoor plants provide highest greenhouse income
- The goal is reducing dependence on imported foods from exploited peasant economies
