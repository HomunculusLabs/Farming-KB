### Reaction Conditions

The phosphorylation of psilocin (1) to the dibenzyl-protected
intermediate (7) proceeds under the following conditions:

- **Starting material**: Psilocin (5.4 g, 26.4 mmol)
- **Solvent**: Anhydrous tetrahydrofuran (THF, 265 mL)
- **Base**: 2.6M n-butyllithium in n-hexane (11.5 mL, 29.9 mmol,
  approximately 1.13 equivalents)
- **Phosphorylating agent**: Tetrabenzylpyrophosphate (18.0 g, 33.4
  mmol, approximately 1.26 equivalents)
- **Temperature**: -78°C for addition, warming to 0°C over 1 hour
- **Atmosphere**: Inert (argon or nitrogen)

### Procedure

The procedure involves dissolving psilocin in anhydrous THF and cooling
to -78°C. n-Butyllithium is added to deprotonate the phenolic hydroxyl,
generating the phenolate anion. After stirring for 5 minutes,
tetrabenzylpyrophosphate is added all at once. The reaction mixture is
stirred for 1 hour while the temperature is allowed to warm to 0°C.
