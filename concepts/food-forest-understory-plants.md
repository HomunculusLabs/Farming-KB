### Chicory (Cichorium intybus)
Deep-rooted perennial providing both nectar and pollen for bees. Medicinal herb for livestock. Drought tolerant once established.

### Ribwort Plantain (Plantago lanceolata)
Common perennial herb providing good poultry forage and bee forage. Medicinal properties.

### Nasturtiums (Tropaeolum spp)
Excellent companion plant for apples, cabbages, cauliflowers, and many other crops. Edible leaves and flowers with peppery flavour. Attracts beneficial insects.

### Mint (Mentha spp)
Spreading herbs well suited to moist understorey conditions. Bee forage. Medicinal and aromatic. Grow in containers to prevent excessive spreading.

### Borage (Borago officinalis)
Excellent companion for strawberries. Long flowering season provides bee forage. Edible flowers.

### Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale)
Deep-rooted perennial. Long season bee forage. Edible leaves and flowers. Medicinal for livestock.

## Nitrogen-Fixing Understorey

### Albizia lophantha (Crested Wattle)
At home either as an understorey species or as a major species. Fast-growing nitrogen fixer, useful as a nurse tree.

### Acacia pulchella
Valued companion for avocados, Chinese chestnuts, and macadamias in the understorey.

### Lupinus arboreus (Tree Lupin)
Nitrogen-fixing pioneer shrub. See [[zone-and-sector-analysis]] to place understorey plants where they receive appropriate light levels
- Layer nitrogen-fixing species throughout the understorey to maintain soil fertility without external inputs
- Include [[chop-and-drop-mulching]] with understorey prunings to build soil organic matter
- Include [[garden-care-and-succession-planting]] principles for synergistic plant relationships
