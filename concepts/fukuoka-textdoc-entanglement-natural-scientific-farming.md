elimination of human labor; it compresses and freezes time and space. Modern
scientific farming, on the other hand, seeks to appropriate time and space
through complex and diverse means; it proceeds centrifugally outward toward
"something-ness," expanding and developing as it goes.

Both can be understood as existing in a relative relationship in the same
dimension or plane. But although the two may appear identical at a given
point, they move in opposite directions, the one headed for zero and the
other for infinity.
