# Nature Has No Cause and Effect

One of Masanobu Fukuoka's most radical philosophical claims is that
nature operates without cause and effect. This assertion, which seems

rhythms rather than by human theories about what should work.

"All begins by relinquishing human knowledge," Fukuoka wrote. Not
because knowledge is worthless, but because the knowledge we have is
always incomplete, and acting on incomplete knowledge in complex living
systems causes more harm than good.
