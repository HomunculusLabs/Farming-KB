## Cannabis Health Index Methodology

The Cannabis Health Index (CHI) is an evidence-based rating system developed by Uwe Blesching, PhD, that compiles and evaluates available scientific literature on medical cannabis for over 100 chronic diseases and symptoms. Published in *The Cannabis Health Index* (2015, North Atlantic Books), the CHI rates the therapeutic evidence for cannabis on a scale ranging from "possible" to "probable" to "actual," providing patients and healthcare providers with a quick-reference framework for making informed decisions about cannabinoid-based therapy.

## Purpose and Context

The CHI was developed to address a critical gap in medical cannabis: while the scientific literature on cannabis had grown to more than 15,000 entries at the U.S. National Library of Health, no systematic evidence-based model existed for evaluating the plant's [[cancer-medicinal-mushrooms-schizophyllan-spg-clinical-trials]] supporting therapeutic use |

This three-tier system allows users to quickly assess the strength of evidence supporting cannabis use for any given condition, from preliminary laboratory findings to well-established clinical results.
