## Doubtful Reports

Several species previously reported as neurotropic have been excluded due
to unreliable analyses. Bresinsky and Besl (1990) considered studies on
Stropharia cyanea, S. coronilla, Mycena pura, and Amanita gemmata to be
doubtful. Stijve and Kuyper (1988) failed to find psilocybin in several
species including Psathyrella candolleana, Rickenella swartzii, Gerronema
fibula, Gymnopilus fulgens, G. spectabilis, and Hygrocybe psittacina.
