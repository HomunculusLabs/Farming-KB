## KNF Mineral Inputs — Calcium, Phosphorus, Potassium Solutions

Master Cho"s [[korean-natural-farming]] system uses water-soluble mineral solutions derived from natural sources to provide plants with bioavailable calcium, phosphorus, and potassium. These are made by dissolving minerals in brown rice vinegar (BRV), which converts insoluble forms into plant-available forms.

## Water Soluble Calcium (WSC)

**Source:** Eggshells or sea shells dissolved in brown rice vinegar.

**Preparation:**
1. Collect eggshells (or sea shells), clean, and remove inner membrane
2. Broil/roast shells to sanitize (not burn to charcoal)
3. Crush shells and place in clay jar
4. Add BRV at 1:10 ratio (shells:BRV)
5. Small bubbles appear as [[peat-calcium-carbonate-perlite-casing-psilocybe-cubensis-miniculture]] converts to water-soluble calcium (~7 days)
6. When bubbles stop, the process is complete

**Calcium's Role in Plants:**
- Major component of cell membranes; enables smooth cell division
- Removes harmful substances by binding with organic acids
- Prevents overgrowth
- Makes fruits firm; prolongs storage period
- Promotes phosphoric acid absorption and nutrient accumulation
- Carries carbohydrates from branches/leaves to storage organs (ovaries)
- Critical for overall plant health

**[[knf-calcium-deficiency-symptoms]]:**
- Underdeveloped roots and feeble root hairs
- Leaves discolor to brown, then dry out
- Empty bean pods
- Poor fruit ripening, excessive moisture, lack of sugar, soft flesh
- Root vegetables become pithy/hollow
- Rhizoctonia disease in leafy vegetables, poor heading
- Rice/barley lodging and disease susceptibility

**Usage:** Dilute 1:1,000 with water. Apply during reproductive growth period and when calcium deficiency symptoms appear.

## Water Soluble Calcium Phosphate (WSCP)

**Source:** Animal bones (boiled, defatted) converted to charcoal, dissolved in BRV.

**Preparation:**
1. Boil bones to remove meat and fat
2. Burn bones to charcoal at low temperature (removes remaining organic matter)
3. Use whole or lightly pounded charcoal bones
4. Place in jar with BRV at 1:10 ratio (bone charcoal:BRV)
5. Small bubbles appear; process completes in ~7 days

**Usage:** Dilute 1:1,000 with water.
- Spray during cross-over and vegetative growth periods
- Use when crops overgrow
- Use during bad weather conditions
- Use when initial growth is poor or flower bud differentiation is weak
- Use as livestock drinking water (1:500) for pregnant/ovulating animals

## Water Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WPA)

**Source:** Sesame stems (high in phosphoric acid).

**Phosphorus Functions:**
- Present in every plant cell; composes the cell nucleus
- Moves to vigorous buds, root tips, and seed fruit to increase cell production
- Essential for energy transfer (ATP) and root development
- Critical during flowering [[mushroom-spawn-and-fruiting-stages]]

## Water Soluble Potassium (WSK)

**Source:** Tobacco stems soaked in water.

**Preparation:**
1. Dry tobacco stems, grind coarsely (not to powder)
2. Put 1–2kg in hemp cloth bag, soak in 100L water for ~7 days
3. Dilute 0.7L crude liquid in 20L water for application

**[[knf-water-soluble-potassium-functions]]:**
- Essential for sugar/starch synthesis and translocation
- Enhances fruit quality and disease resistance
- Regulates water balance and stomatal function

**[[knf-potassium-deficiency-and-excess]] Symptoms:**
- Marginal chlorosis and necrosis of older leaves
- Weak stems, lodging tendency
- Small fruit seeds, late maturity
- Chlorosis from old leaves; tip discoloration even at peak growth

**Potassium Excess Symptoms:**
- [[query-how-to-fix-magnesium-deficiency-in-cannabis]]
- Gas disorder symptoms in middle leaves

**Cautions:**
- Use potassium alone (not with nitrogen) in hot, humid conditions
- Avoid application on rainy days — synergy with nitrogen can cause overgrowth
- Deficiency can occur even with adequate soil potassium if lime/magnesium levels are high (absorption suppression)
- Sandy soils with low humus lose potassium easily

## Fish Amino Acid (FAA)

**Source:** Fish waste fermented with brown sugar.

**Preparation:**
1. Layer fish waste and brown sugar alternately in container
2. Cover top with extra brown sugar
3. Add IMO #4 to decompose fish oil/meat
4. Cover with rice straw lid (not sealed — needs airflow)
5. Ferment 6+ months until fully liquidized
6. Extract liquid only for use

**Usage:** Dilute 1:1,000 with water.
- Rich in nitrogen; enhances vegetative growth
- Apply to both soil and foliage
- Use with other KNF materials during vegetative period
- Add to IMO/mixed compost to activate microorganisms

## FPJ Usage by Growth Stage

| Growth Stage | Best FPJ Materials | Dilution |
|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| Germination/early growth | Japanese mugwort, dropwort, bamboo shoot | 1:500 |
| Vegetative growth | Mugwort, dropwort, bamboo, reed, kudzu vine | 1:500 |
| Cross-over (flowering) | False acacia flowers, "child liquid," mulberry, grape | 1:500 |
| Reproductive growth | Fruit enzymes (ripe fruits: chocolate vine, apple, peach) | <1:2,000 |
| Maturity/harvest | Purslane (matured FPJ) | <1:5,000 |

**Important:** Do not use FPJ when fungal diseases appear in vinyl houses — poor ventilation can spread fungi further. In reproductive period, use well-ripened FPJ at low dilution — sugar content acts as a spreader/sticker for leaf applications.
