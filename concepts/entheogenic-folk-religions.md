NAC ceremonies follow a consistent format: participants sit on the ground in
a circle around a blazing central fire in a tipi. A "roadman" presides,
assisted by a drummer, firekeeper, and cedar-man. A staff and rattle are
passed around, and participants sing traditional peyote songs throughout the
night. There is virtually no discussion of healing processes or visions
during or after the ceremony. Ceremonies are legally limited to persons with
25% or more Indian ancestry, though some leaders have opened ceremonies to
non-Indians.

## Brazilian Ayahuasca Churches

In Brazil, no less than three organized churches use ayahuasca as the main
sacrament: the Santo Daime, Uniao de Vegetal (UDV), and Barquinia. Each was
founded by rubber tappers working in the Amazon region in the 1950s, who came
into contact with the hallucinogenic vine through mestizo or Indian
ayahuasceros. The founder of each reported a significant vision instructing
them to organize a church using the brew as the principal sacrament.

The UDV, the largest, is the most formal: participants sit in rows in
straight-backed chairs during ceremonies, listening to sermons and songs by
maestres around a central table, with a question and answer period.
Sometimes testimonials of life-transformations are offered, reminiscent of
AA confessions.

The Santo Daime, founded by a Brazilian of African descent, involves the
singing of hymns by the entire congregation, led by a small group of women
singers. Some ceremonies also involve dancing in simple rhythmic steps,
reminiscent of African-American gospel services.

The Barquinia church has incorporated elements of Umbanda, an
Afro-Brazilian religious movement involving contact with deceased ancestors
and deities known as orixas. These ceremonies involve both singing and
dancing.

Research by Charles Grob, Dennis McKenna, and others confirmed that
membership in these churches and long-term drinking of ayahuasca tea is
associated with no adverse health effects — and indeed with a reduction of
addictions and other psychopathologies.
