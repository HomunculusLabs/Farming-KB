# Secret Societies and Entheogen Suppression

## Overview

The suppression of entheogenic knowledge by secret societies and organized religion is presented in [[james-arthur]]'s *[[mushrooms-and-mankind]]* as one of the most consequential cover-ups in human history. Secret societies such as Freemasonry are described as having preserved knowledge of entheogenic plants — particularly **Amanita muscaria** — at the highest levels of their initiatory systems while systematically obscuring this information from the general public and even from lower-ranking members.

## The Structure of Secrecy

Secret societies operate on a hierarchical system where knowledge is compartmentalized. Only those who fit a certain profile and ascend to the highest ranks gain access to the most closely guarded secrets. Lower-ranking members are permitted to believe they possess special knowledge, but the most profound truths — including the identity and use of sacred entheogenic plants — remain reserved for an elite few.

The oaths of secrecy taken by initiates create what Arthur describes as a "Fascist agenda" of societal control. These vows prevent members from discussing entheogenic knowledge even if they personally wish to share it. Arthur recounts personal encounters with high-ranking clergy and secret society initiates who acknowledged knowing about Amanita muscaria but refused to discuss it further due to their binding oaths.

## Knowledge Hoarding and Destruction

Secret societies are characterized by a pattern of acquiring knowledge and technology, hoarding it exclusively, and then systematically destroying all public remnants. Arthur draws a parallel to the historical destruction of libraries by religious authorities — notably the burning of Eastern libraries by Christians after pillaging their contents. This systematic erasure of entheogenic knowledge from the public domain is described as an "abomination" that leaves the rest of the world desolate.

The motivation behind this suppression is tied to power dynamics. Revealing the truth about entheogens would, in Arthur's view, relinquish churches, governments, and secret societies of their power over people. The control of spiritual experience through the monopoly on sacramental substances is seen as a cornerstone of authoritarian control.

## Symbolism in Religious Art

### Catholic Communion Symbolism

Catholic communion robes and vessels contain mushroom-like symbolism that is not intended to be understood beyond the dogmatic level. The symbolism of the cup (chalice), a central element of the Eucharist, shares visual characteristics with mushroom imagery — its shape, colors, and associated iconography all evoke fungal forms. Catholic first-communion robes are noted as being "full of symbolism" with the cup prominently displayed. This symbolism is described as knowledge reserved for the highest elite, not for local clergy. The cup itself, from ancient times, was considered a representation of the female genital organ — paralleling the mushroom cap's anthropomorphization. The **[[holy-grail-entity]]** legend, in this interpretation, encodes the search for the lost entheogenic sacrament, with the "grail" being the original mushroom cup from which the waters of life were drunk.

### Alpha and Omega

The declaration "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end" found in Biblical texts is connected to mushroom imagery. The mushroom, as a life form that emerges from spore, grows, dies, and returns to the earth in a cyclical pattern, embodies this concept of beginning and end in a single organism.

### Mithra and the Cosmic Egg

Mithraic imagery depicts the god inside a **cosmic egg**, a symbol of potentiality and creation that parallels the mushroom's role as a gateway between worlds. The cosmic egg represents the primordial state from which all things emerge and to which they return.

### The Sacred Initiatory Sacrament

One significant artwork depicts an angel holding a mushroom being placed into a sacramental cup with fire emanating from it. A second angel holds a fire container resembling a cooking apparatus used for preparation. This imagery parallels Egyptian depictions of preparing the "celestial food" and shares visual vocabulary with **alchemical** representations. The preparation of entheogenic substances through fire-based processes (such as drying or decoction) is a recurring theme across multiple ancient traditions, suggesting a shared understanding of the necessary preparation methods. Notably, the five colors present in the angels — red, blue, green, yellow, and white — constitute a recurring motif across multiple mystical traditions and serve as a visual code identifying the sacred plant combination.

## The Five-Plant Soma Theory

A key artwork depicts Jesus as the "lord of magickal plants" above four specific plants underscored by a green vine. Arthur proposes this represents an **alchemical recipe** for the true Soma — the legendary elixir of the gods described in Vedic traditions but never definitively identified by any single plant candidate.

### The Five Plants

1. **Amanita muscaria** (red) — Produces the "quickening of the spirit," the foundational entheogenic experience
2. **Psilocybe mushroom** (blue) — Opens the "third eye" and enhances visionary capacity
3. **Syrian Rue** (*Peganum harmala*) — Contains **telepathine** (harmine), an MAO inhibitor that potentiates the effects of other psychoactive compounds, paralleling the function of **pinoline** naturally produced by the pineal gland
- **Opium Poppy** (*Papaver somniferum*) — Induces euphoria and relaxation, enabling one to release control and fully enter the visionary state. Arthur notes this plant has been surprisingly absent from most Soma candidate discussions despite the suggestive etymological link between *Soma* and *somniferum*
5. **Green plants** including **Cannabis** and various DMT-containing grasses — Used to prevent nausea, enhance other compounds, and reactivate neural pathways opened by entheogens

### The Pineal Gland Connection

The combination of Syrian Rue (MAO inhibitor) with DMT-containing plants mimics the natural DMT-pinoline combination produced by the **pineal gland** in the human brain. This endogenous psychochemical system may be responsible for naturally occurring psychic experiences and what are described as UFO contact visualizations. The pineal gland, long associated with the "third eye" in Eastern traditions, produces both DMT (dimethyltryptamine) and pinoline (a natural MAO inhibitor), and the simultaneous release of both compounds during deep meditation, sleep, or near-death experiences could account for spontaneous mystical states. The Ayahuasca brew of South America, researched by Jace Callaway and others, uses the same pharmacological principle: combining DMT-containing plants with MAO inhibitor-containing plants (*[[banisteriopsis-caapi]]*, Syrian Rue) to produce entheogenic effects. Oral DMT is normally inactive because the MAO enzyme in the gut breaks it down before it can reach the brain; the MAO inhibitor temporarily disables this defense, allowing the DMT to cross the blood-brain barrier and produce its visionary effects.

### Linguistic Evidence

The linguistic connection between **Soma** and **Papaver somniferum** is noted: *Somnambulism* (sleep-walking) occurs when DMT and pinoline are excreted by the pineal gland during sleep. The name *somniferum* itself suggests "sleep-bringing," connecting the opiate poppy to the broader Soma complex.

### Previous Candidates

Arthur acknowledges the contributions of multiple researchers to the Soma debate:
- **R. Gordon Wasson** — Made a convincing case for Amanita muscaria alone
- **Flattery and Schwartz** — Proposed Syrian Rue as the primary candidate
- **Terence McKenna** — Exhaustively documented the ecstatic states produced by [[psilocybin-mushrooms]]

Each candidate individually falls short of producing the full range of effects described in religious texts. However, a combination of all five plants may produce the "long sought after and mysterious experience" that has remained an enigma.

## The Five-Color Theme

The five-color motif (red, blue, green, yellow, white) recurs throughout mystical traditions and is visually represented in multiple artworks associated with sacred plants and initiatory knowledge. This chromatic symbolism connects to the five-plant theory and represents a unifying visual language across otherwise disparate spiritual traditions.

In the artwork depicting the sacred initiatory sacrament, the five colors appear on the angels themselves — each embodying a different chromatic frequency that corresponds to one of the five plants. Red connects to Amanita muscaria, blue to the Psilocybe species, green to the Cannabis and DMT-containing plants, yellow to the golden dried cap of the mushroom, and white to the purified essence of the completed brew. This color coding system served as a visual mnemonic for initiates who understood the correspondence.

The recurrence of these five colors across diverse cultural artifacts — from Christian illuminated manuscripts to Hindu tantric art to Mesoamerican codices — suggests a shared initiatory tradition with common roots in the use of psychoactive plant combinations. The consistency of this chromatic vocabulary across thousands of years and vast geographical distances points to either direct cultural transmission or a universal human response to these specific entheogenic experiences that naturally produces this symbolic vocabulary.

## The Gateway Symbolism

The sacred gateway or archway appears repeatedly in religious architecture as an allusion to the mushroom as the literal "door" to other worlds. Arthur describes one such archway as an "incredible, obvious symbolic allusion" to the entrance to the "Holy of Holies." The real secret of the doorway, in this interpretation, is that it represents the entheogenic substance that takes one literally into the other dimension — the realm where the gods are said to reside. This architectural symbolism encodes, in stone, the function of the mushroom as a portal between ordinary reality and the sacred dimension.

## The Mushroom as Divine Food

Throughout the religious texts and artistic traditions surveyed, the mushroom consistently appears as the "food of the gods" — a substance that must be physically consumed to produce its effects. The Biblical insistence that "unless you eat and drink you have no life in you" is contrasted with the modern Eucharistic practice of consuming ordinary bread and wine. Arthur argues that the **transubstantiation** — the supposed magical transformation of bread into flesh and wine into blood — has "no merit" when the substance being consumed lacks the pharmacological properties to produce the described spiritual effects. The substitution of the real entheogenic sacrament with an inert placebo is described as one of the most consequential deceptions in religious history.

## The Suppression of Knowledge

The systematic pattern of knowledge suppression by secret societies follows a consistent historical template. Societies acquire advanced knowledge — spiritual, technological, or pharmacological — then hoard it exclusively for their elite membership. The next step is the systematic destruction of all public remnants of this knowledge, ensuring it remains exclusive. Arthur draws a direct parallel to the Christian destruction of Eastern libraries, where valuable texts were pillaged for their useful contents and then burned to erase the knowledge from public access forever.

This pattern extends beyond religious institutions to encompass governmental and corporate entities. The criminalization of entheogenic plants in the 20th century is viewed as the modern continuation of this ancient suppression — using legal frameworks to maintain the monopoly on spiritual experience that religious institutions once held through theological authority.

The consequences of this suppression are described in apocalyptic terms. When the truth about entheogens is revealed, Arthur argues, "those formerly oppressed humans would not take it lightly that their lives have been manipulated through the false morals and dogma of Fascism." The fear of this revelation is what drives the continued suppression.

## The Fascist Agenda

Arthur uses the term "Fascist" to describe the societal model created by the alliance of secret societies, organized religion, and government. This model operates through:
- **Information control** — Restricting access to knowledge that would empower individuals
- **Moral manipulation** — Creating guilt and shame around natural human behaviors
- **Hierarchical authority** — Establishing systems where some are permitted to know and others are not
- **Oath-bound secrecy** — Using binding vows to prevent knowledge from spreading even among those who possess it

The big lie, in Arthur's framework, is that the substitute sacraments (bread, wine, placebo rituals) are equivalent to the original entheogenic experience. This substitution allows the power structures to maintain control while appearing to provide spiritual nourishment. Those who convince themselves they truly feel something after eating priest-blessed bread and wine may, according to Arthur, be "creating an illusion that is eventually something that is damaging to them beyond comprehension."

The revelation of entheogenic truth would simultaneously liberate individuals and destroy the authority structures that depend on the suppression of this knowledge — which is precisely why that revelation has been so fiercely resisted throughout history.

## The Myth of the Serpent and Knowledge

The Biblical serpent, far from being a symbol of evil, is reinterpreted in this framework as the bringer of knowledge and enlightenment. In the Sumerian original, the entity who fed humanity the fruit of knowledge was Enki — humanity's benefactor and protector. The later Biblical inversion, which transforms this benefactor into a tempter and deceiver, represents the successful propaganda campaign of the controlling faction. The serpent's ancient association with wisdom ("be ye wise as serpents") and its representation in the caduceus (the staff of Enki/Ea) confirm its original role as a symbol of enlightenment rather than deception. This inversion of the knowledge-bringer into the villain of the story is, according to Arthur, the foundational deception upon which patriarchal religious control was built.
