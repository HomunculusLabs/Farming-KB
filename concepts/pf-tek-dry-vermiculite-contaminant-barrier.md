# PF TEK Dry Vermiculite Contaminant Barrier

The dry vermiculite contaminant barrier is one of the key innovations of the
PF TEK. Discovered by [[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus-cultivation-method]] as
"an original discovery." Prior to this innovation, home cultivators relied
heavily on pressure canners to achieve truly sterile substrate conditions. The
PF TEK's approach of using a non-nutritive dry layer at the jar opening provided
a passive filter that allowed gas exchange while blocking contaminant organisms
from reaching the wet substrate below. This was a paradigm shift in amateur
mycology.

## How the Barrier Works

The dry vermiculite layer serves two primary functions:

1. **Contaminant protection**: The dry layer acts as a physical barrier between
   the wet substrate and the outside air. Airborne mold spores, bacteria, and
   other contaminant organisms that land on the dry vermiculite cannot thrive
   because the layer contains no moisture or nutrients. Without these
   essentials, contaminants cannot germinate or colonize.
