degrees Celsius). Strain and dilute to 20 to 23 percent alcohol by volume to

extract.

Grain spawn can also be used for extracts, allowing large-volume production of
mycelium without fruiting. Supercolonize grain for three to four weeks until a
clear or colored liquid (metabolites) becomes visible. Subject to fluorescent
or natural light to increase metabolite production. Blend grain spawn with
enough alcohol to make a watery paste, transfer to sterile jars, and steep for
fourteen days. Shake before dosing and consume particulates as part of the
therapy. This method is especially useful for slow-to-fruit species like
agarikon.
