## Planning the Earthworks Process

It is best to plan all aspects of the earth-moving process
before machines or labourers arrive on site:

1. Make an initial decision where to place the road, dam,
   house site, drains, etc., using contour map and plan if
   necessary.
2. Test the soil by auger holes, soil samples, and soil pits
   to determine if the soil is good enough to suit plans (a
   good clay soil for dams is essential).
3. Peg out the site, using a level, measuring tape, and stakes
   with red or white cloth attached so the earth-moving machine
   can follow them.
4. Plan a place to store all topsoil removed during
   excavations. Never allow topsoil to be mixed with subsoil,
   but carefully remove it to be later returned to the site as
   a [[permaculture-plants-food-forest-ground-covers-bamboos]], pioneers, and long-term plants
can be set out in new earth, a great deal of time and work is
saved. A broadscale scatter of mixed seed, raked in, will
prepare the way for permanent placements. Having planned the
planting and stabilisation, plant materials must be on standby
to implement the plan as soon after disturbance as possible.
