## The laccase pathway for lignocellulose breakdown

Ascomycetes were not expected to possess lignin or manganese peroxidases
(the enzymes best known for lignolytic capabilities). How do cordgrass
ascomycetes carry out lignocellulolysis? These fungi possess laccase, a
copper-containing enzyme capable of oxidising phenolic molecules. A
key discovery by Eggert et al. (1997) showed that if a redox-mediator
molecule is present alongside laccase, non-phenolic portions of lignin
can be oxidised, enabling extensive lignin breakdown. The basidiomycete

## Laccase activity screening

Eight strains of the most common smooth-cordgrass ascomycetes were
grown on agar containing ground cordgrass leaves and flooded with
syringaldazine solution. All eight strains immediately presented a pink
colour, indicating universal laccase activity among the dominant
species. This result prompted investigation of laccase gene diversity
using molecular techniques.
