## Method 6: Freezing

Eggs can be frozen but must be removed from the shell first:
- Whole eggs: beat together, add 1/2 tsp salt or 1 tbsp sugar per cup of eggs (to prevent graininess), freeze in ice cube trays
- Whites only: freeze in ice cube trays
- Yolks only: beat with 1/8 tsp salt or 1.5 tsp sugar per 4 yolks to prevent gelation
- Frozen eggs last up to 1 year

## Using Preserved Eggs

Before using preserved eggs, test by placing in water. Discard any that float. Preserved eggs are best used in cooking and baking rather than for dishes where egg freshness is critical (like fried eggs or poached eggs).
