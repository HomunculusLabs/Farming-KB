# Earthworks for Water Conservation

## Overview

Earthworks are the physical infrastructure of water management in permaculture design. They encompass dams, tanks, swales, diversion channels, and irrigation layouts that capture, store, and distribute water across the landscape. While engineers have long focused on the technical aspects of dam construction, permaculture emphasises the placement, linking, and biological integration of earthworks within the total designed landscape.
