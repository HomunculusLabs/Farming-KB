Proper seed preparation improves germination rates and speeds up the sprouting process. Greg Green describes seed scuffing and handling techniques as part of the propagation chapter.

## Why Scuff Seeds

Cannabis seeds have a hard outer shell designed by nature to protect the embryo inside. In the wild, this shell would be gradually worn down by soil microbes, moisture, and natural abrasion. For the indoor grower, scuffing (scarification) mimics this natural process and:

- Allows moisture to penetrate the seed shell more quickly
- Helps the embryo crack through the outer layer
- Can reduce germination time from weeks to days
- Improves overall germination success rates

## Scuffing Methods

### Sandpaper Method
1. Obtain fine-grade sandpaper (220–400 grit)
2. Place the seed between two pieces of sandpaper
3. Gently rub back and forth a few times — just enough to scratch the surface
4. Do NOT sand aggressively — you want to lightly score the coating, not damage the embryo inside
5. The seed should look slightly duller but remain intact

### Matchbox Method
1. Place seeds in a small box (a matchbox works well) lined with sandpaper
2. Close the box and shake gently for 30–60 seconds
3. The abrasion scores the seed coat lightly

### Nail File Method
1. Use a fine metal nail file or emery board
2. Gently file one spot on the seed edge
3. Just remove the shiny outer layer in one small area
4. This creates a weak point where moisture can enter

## Soaking (Pre-Germination)

After scuffing, soaking seeds can further speed germination:

1. Place seeds in a glass of room-temperature water (68–77°F / 20–25°C)
2. Add a few drops of hydrogen peroxide (3%) to prevent mold — optional but recommended
3. Seeds that sink within a few hours are generally viable
4. Seeds that float after 12–24 hours may be non-viable (though some viable seeds float initially)
5. Soak for 12–24 hours maximum
6. Do not soak longer — the embryo can drown
7. Remove seeds and plant immediately into your germination medium

## Handling Seeds

Cannabis seeds are living organisms and must be treated carefully:

- **Avoid touching with dirty hands** — oils and contaminants from your fingers can introduce pathogens
- **Use tweezers** when possible for handling
- **Do not crush or squeeze** — the embryo inside is fragile
- **Store properly before planting**: In a cool, dark, dry place. Refrigeration in an airtight container extends viability for years.
- See [[cannabis-seed-germination-methods]] for detailed instructions on each method.

## Post-Germination Care

Once seeds have sprouted:
- Move seedlings to a light source immediately (but not too intense — fluorescent or LED at low intensity)
- Do not add nutrients for the first 1–2 weeks
- Keep the growing medium moist but not waterlogged
- Maintain warm temperatures (70–80°F / 21–27°C)
- Provide gentle airflow (small fan on low) to strengthen stems
- Transplant when roots are visible through the container or growing medium
