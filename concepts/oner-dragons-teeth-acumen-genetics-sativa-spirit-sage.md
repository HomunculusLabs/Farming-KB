# Dragon's Teeth

**Breeder:** Acumen Genetics (USA)
**Genetics:** Sativa Spirit × SAGE
**Potency:** THC 18%
**Ratio:** 90% Sativa / 10% Indica
**Flowering Time:** 70 days (indoor)
**Yield:** Medium-high

## Overview

Dragon's Teeth is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by Acumen Genetics, a USA-based seed company
founded by Gene E. Yuss. Unlike many boutique breeders who started as growers-turned-breeders,
Acumen assembled a team of experts from various Agricultural Sciences disciplines to approach
cannabis breeding with scientific rigor. Their stated mission is to improve the quality of seed
stock available in the United States, and Dragon's Teeth represents a successful application of
that philosophy.

The strain results from crossing a Sativa Spirit mother plant with an exceptional sage (Sativa
Afghan Genetic Equilibrium) male. The breeding goal was straightforward: use SAGE's pest
resistance and elevated THC production to enhance the already-impressive Sativa Spirit lineage.
The result is a plant that combines indica-influenced growing habits with a distinctly sativa
psychoactive profile — a combination the breeders describe as achieving "something quite
spectacular."

## Growth Characteristics

Despite being 90% sativa, Dragon's Teeth exhibits remarkably manageable growth patterns thanks
to the SAGE influence, which tempers the typically wild sativa growth habit. Key cultivation
traits include:

- **Height:** Reaches 6–8 feet if left untopped, but responds well to training and topping for
  indoor cultivation
- **Structure:** Grows more as a bush than a Christmas tree shape, with stronger stems and
  branches than most sativas
- **Stability:** The SAGE parent contributes significant structural strength, reducing the
  tendency toward spindly, weak-stemmed growth common in sativa-dominant plants
- **Resistance:** Exceptional resistance to pests — especially mites — and mold is rarely an
  issue, though proper ventilation and regular bud inspection remain advisable

## Flowering and Yield

Dragon's Teeth requires a 70-day indoor flowering period, which is relatively moderate for a
strain of this sativa content. The buds are medium-dense and tend to fill out toward the end
of the growing cycle rather than swelling early. Appearance-wise, the buds display deep green
serrated leaves with hints of white throughout — a visually sativa presentation through and
through. Yields are classified as medium-high, making this strain productive enough for both
personal and small commercial grows.

## Effects Profile

The high from Dragon's Teeth is described as incredibly energetic and motivating, with strong
creative stimulation and mental clarity. This is a functional, task-oriented sativa — the kind
of strain suited for productive work rather than passive relaxation. The effects align with
the classic sativa profile: cerebral, uplifting, and mentally engaging without significant
body sedation.

## Aroma and Flavor

The flavor profile is notably distinctive: blackberry notes dominate, complemented by a hint
of gasoline (fuel-like terpene character). This combination of sweet fruitiness and pungent
fuel creates an unusual but appealing tasting experience that reviewers describe as making you
"want to partake more often than not."

## Terpene Profile

Based on the reported aroma and flavor characteristics, Dragon's Teeth likely features:

- **[[myrcene]]**: Contributing to the berry fruitiness and relaxing undertones
- **Caryophyllene:** Likely source of the fuel/gasoline notes and contributing to anti-
  inflammatory properties
- **Limonene:** Supporting the energetic, mood-elevating effects

## Medical Applications

The clear-headed, motivating effects make Dragon's Teeth potentially useful for:

- **Fatigue and low energy:** The uplifting, stimulating profile counters lethargy
- **ADHD/ADD:** Mental clarity and focus enhancement may aid concentration
- **Depression:** The energizing euphoria can provide temporary mood elevation
- **Stress management:** Functional clarity without sedation allows continued productivity

## Growing Environment

Dragon's Teeth is versatile and can be grown both indoors and outdoors. Indoor growers will
appreciate the manageable height when trained, while outdoor growers benefit from the strain's
natural pest resistance. The strong stems mean less structural support is needed compared to
other sativas of similar height. The mold resistance also makes it suitable for humid climates
where other sativa strains might struggle.

## Cultivation Tips

1. **Top early** — given the potential 6–8 foot height, topping in the vegetative stage is
   recommended for indoor grows
2. **Train aggressively** — LST works well to control height and promote lateral branching
3. **Feed moderately** — the strain is not particularly nutrient-sensitive, making it
   suitable for intermediate growers
4. **Monitor late-stage flowering** — buds fill out late in the cycle; don't harvest
   prematurely
5. **Ventilation still matters** — despite mold resistance, proper airflow prevents any
   issues in dense canopies
