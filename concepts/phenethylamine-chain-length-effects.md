## Phenethylamine Chain Length Effects

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

One of the most systematic structure-activity relationship studies in PiHKAL concerns the effect of aliphatic chain length on psychoactivity in phenethylamine-type compounds. Shulgin explored the continuum from 2-carbon (phenethylamine) through 3-carbon (amphetamine) to 4-carbon and beyond, documenting how each additional methylene unit affects potency, duration, and qualitative character.

## The Chain Length Continuum

### Two-Carbon Chain (2C Series / Phenethylamines)

The basic phenethylamine structure has a two-carbon chain between the aromatic ring and the amine nitrogen. These compounds:
- Are generally less potent than their amphetamine counterparts
- Often have shorter durations
- Tend to have a "cleaner" or more purely psychedelic character with less stimulant overlay
- Include the entire 2C-X family (2C-B, 2C-I, 2C-D, 2C-E, etc.)

The most dramatic potency loss is seen with 2C-D: its amphetamine counterpart DOM is active at approximately 3 mg, while 2C-D requires 20-60 mg -- roughly one-tenth the potency.

### Three-Carbon Chain (Amphetamines)

The addition of a single alpha-methyl group (creating the amphetamine structure) dramatically increases potency in most cases:
- DOM: active at 3-10 mg (vs 2C-D at 20-60 mg)
- DOB: active at 1-3 mg (vs 2C-B at 12-24 mg)
- DOI: active at 1.5-3 mg (vs 2C-I at 12-25 mg)
- MDA: active at 80-160 mg (vs MDPEA at ~300 mg)

The alpha-methyl group also typically increases duration and adds stimulant qualities.

### Four-Carbon Chain

Extending to four carbons produces varied results. The compound J (alpha,alpha-dimethyl-3,4-methylenedioxyphenethylamine) and related 4-carbon homologues show intermediate or reduced activity. The aminoindanes and aminotetralins (where the chain is "bent back" to the ring) are a special case -- they "react in animal discrimination studies identically to MDMA" and "appear to be free of neurochemical toxicity," despite having the chain length incorporated into a ring structure.

### Extended Chains (5+ Carbons)

Beyond four carbons, activity generally drops off sharply. The Muni-Metro series (K, L, etc.) with progressively longer chains showed decreasing psychoactivity.
