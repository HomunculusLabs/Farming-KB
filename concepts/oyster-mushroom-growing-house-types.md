## Growing House Types

### Traditional Growing Houses

Traditional mushroom houses in Asia are often simple structures adapted from agricultural buildings:
- Single-story buildings with insulated walls and roof
- Equipped with shelves or racks for holding substrate bags or bottles
- Natural or forced ventilation systems
- Humidity maintained through manual misting or simple humidification

### Modern Controlled Environment Rooms

Commercial operations increasingly use purpose-built controlled environment rooms:
- Insulated panels (sandwich panels with foam core)
- Automated climate control systems
- HEPA-filtered air intake
- Computerized monitoring and adjustment of temperature, humidity, and CO2
