## AMF Functional Diversity in Grasslands

Different AMF taxa provide distinct functional benefits to host plants
in grassland settings.

Species in the Glomeraceae family tend to produce dense intraradical
hyphal networks and are effective phosphorus scavengers, while species
in the Gigasporaceae invest more heavily in extraradical hyphae that
extend far from the root zone.

## Saprotrophic Fungal Communities

Grassland saprotrophic fungal communities differ from forest communities
in several important ways:

- Greater proportion of microfungi (ascomycetes and mitosporic fungi)
  relative to basidiomycetes
- Higher fungal biomass per unit organic matter due to rapid root litter
