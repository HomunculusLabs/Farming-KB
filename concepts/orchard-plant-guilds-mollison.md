## Orchard Plant Guilds (Mollison)

Source: Permaculture: A Designers' Manual, [[mollison-geese-grass-control-and-integrated-poultry]].

### Spike Roots (Comfrey, Dandelion, Globe Artichoke)
Cover ground, encourage worms, yield mulch and crop. Soil
below foliage is soft, free-draining, and cool.

### Insectary Plants
Umbelliferae: fennel, dill, Queen Anne's lace, tansy.
Attract tachinid wasps, robber flies, ladybirds, and
pollinators. Quillaja, Photinia, and Acacia serve as
insectary trees in windbreaks and interplant rows.

### Herb Layer Insectary
Catnip, fennel, dill, small daisies, Phyla nodosa, and
flowering [[fukuoka-citrus-orchard-ground-cover-cultivation]] for frogs
and lizards. Kniphofia, Fuchsia, and Salvia attract
insectivorous birds. Small ponds breed frogs. Nasturtium
prevents soil drying and adds mulch.

## Pest Reduction Strategy

Pest species can be reduced to 4-7% of monocrop levels through:
disease resistant stock, minimal pruning, predation by birds
and insects, leguminous interplants, windbreaks and mulch,
and poultry for windfall cleanup.

## Animals in the Orchard

Bantams at 120-240/ha scavenge windfalls and reduce codlin
moth and [[fukuoka-australia-fruit-fly-incident]]. Pigs handle windfalls when trees reach
3-7 years. Sheep and cattle graze pruned orchards aged 7-20
years as older trees tolerate grass competition.
