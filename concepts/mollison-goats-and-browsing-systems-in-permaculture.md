## Goats in the Broader Animal System

Mollison places goats within a multi-species grazing system that
exploits different feeding preferences:

- Cattle graze tall grasses
- Sheep follow cattle on shorter grasses
- Horses follow sheep
- Goats browse shrubs and thorny vegetation

This sequential or multi-species approach maximises the use of
available forage while preventing any single species from
over-exploiting preferred plants. The result is better overall
pasture utilisation and reduced weed problems.
