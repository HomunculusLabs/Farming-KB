# Mushroom Environmental Parameters

Each mushroom species has specific environmental requirements for mycelial growth (spawn run), primordia formation (pinning), and fruitbody development (cropping). The parameters below are synthesized from Chapter XI of The Mushroom Cultivator.

## Agaricus bisporus / brunnescens (Button Mushroom)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 74-81 | 90-100 | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 64-68 | 95-100 | <1,000 | 2-4/hr | Ambient |
| Cropping | 57-62 | 85-92 | <1,000 | 4-6/hr | Ambient |

Thermal death: 93°F prolonged. Flush interval: 7-10 days. Yield: 3-5 lbs/sq.ft. over 4-5 weeks.

## Agaricus bitorquis (Rodman's Agaricus)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 84-86 | 90-100 | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 64-68 | 95-100 | <1,000 | 2-4/hr | Ambient |
| Cropping | 60-66 | 85-92 | <1,000 | 2-4/hr | Ambient |

Thermal death: 93°F prolonged. Virus resistant. Half the fresh air requirements of A. brunnescens. Longer shelf life.

## Pleurotus ostreatus (Oyster Mushroom)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 78-84 | 90-100 | 20,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 55-60 | 95 | <600 | 4/hr | 2000 lux |
| Cropping | 55-60 | 85-92 | <600 | 4-6/hr | 2000 lux |

Thermal death: 104°F for 48 hours. Growth stimulated up to 28,000 ppm CO2. No casing needed. Flush interval: 10 days. Duration: 4-5 weeks. Strongly phototropic.

## Pleurotus ostreatus var. florida

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 82-86 | 90-100 | 20,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 72-77 | 95 | <600 | 4/hr | 2000 lux |
| Cropping | 72-77 | 85-92 | <600 | 4-6/hr | 2000 lux |

Warmer variety -- no cold shock needed. First flush in 10 days (vs 20 for P. ostreatus). 1 kg fresh mushrooms per kg dry straw.

## Psilocybe cubensis

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 84-86 | 90 | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Case Run | 84-86 | 90+ | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 74-78 | 95-100 | <5,000 | 1-3/hr | 12-16h/day |
| Cropping | 74-78 | 85-92 | <5,000 | 1-3/hr | 12-16h/day |

Thermal death: 106°F. Flush interval: 5-8 days. Casing pH: 6.8-7.2. Light: blue spectra at 480nm wavelength.

## Flammulina velutipes (Enoki)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 72-77 | 90-100 | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 50-55 | 85 | <1,000 | 4/hr | None needed |
| Cropping | 50-55 | 85 | <1,000 | 4/hr | 12h cycle |

No casing needed. Duration: 2-3 weeks. Cold-weather species. Sawdust/bran substrate (4:1 vol). 160-220g per 800ml bottle commercially.

## Coprinus comatus (Shaggy Mane)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 76-80 | 90-100 | 5,000-10,000 | 0-1/hr | Dark |
| Case Run | 76-80 | 90-100 | 5,000-10,000 | 0-1/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 62-65 | 95-100 | <1,000 | 4/hr | 12h cycle |
| Cropping | 62-65 | 85-92 | <1,000 | 4/hr | 12h cycle |

Harvest before gills deliquesce. Submerge in water post-harvest to slow deliquescence. Yield: 2-3 lbs/sq.ft. over 4 weeks.

## Lepista nuda (Blewit)

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 70-75 | 90+ | 5,000-10,000 | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 55-65 | 95 | <1,000 | 2-4/hr | 10 lux |
| Cropping | 55-65 | 85-90 | <1,000 | 2-4/hr | 10 lux |

Very long cropping period: 24-52 weeks. Light: 370-420nm range preferred. Purplish mycelium.

## Panaeolus cyanescens

| Phase | Temp (°F) | RH (%) | CO2 (ppm) | Fresh Air | Light |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Spawn Run | 79-84 | 90+ | 10,000+ | 0/hr | Dark |
| Primordia | 75-80 | 95+ | <5,000 | 2/hr | Diffuse |
| Cropping | 75-80 | 85-92 | <5,000 | 2/hr | Diffuse |

Very fast: less than 1 week from casing to first flush. Flush interval: 5-7 days. Thin casing: 1/2-1 inch.

## Key Principles

- Lower temperatures during cropping produce higher quality mushrooms with longer shelf life
- Higher temperatures speed development and shorten cropping period but increase disease/insect risk
- Commercial growers lower air temperature 2°F prior to peak flush to maximize quality
- The progressive build-up of contaminant organisms eventually halts mushroom growth -- maximize early flush yields
