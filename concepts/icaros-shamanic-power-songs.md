## Nature and Origin of Icaros

Every icaro originates from a plant spirit. During dieta, when a
vegetalista apprentices with a specific plant, the spirit mother reveals
her song through dreams and visions. The vegetalista learns the melody,
rhythm, and words, which may be in Spanish, Quechua, or the plant's own
spirit language — a tongue outside ordinary human speech. Some icaros
contain words with no literal meaning in any known language; their power
lies in their vibration and connection to the specific spirit who
composed them.

The number and quality of icaros a vegetalista possesses directly
measures their knowledge and power. Experienced healers may know
hundreds of icaros, each corresponding to a different plant teacher,
spirit entity, or healing function. Acquisition of new icaros continues
throughout a vegetalista's life as each dieta and ceremony presents
opportunities for deeper spirit communication.

## Functions of Icaros

### Calling and Invoking Spirits

The primary function of an icaro is to call specific plant spirits and
supernatural entities into the ceremonial space. Each song establishes a
vibrational connection with its corresponding spirit, drawing that
spirit's presence and power into the healing work. When a vegetalista
sings during an [[spirit-mothers-of-plants]] the brew's
plants and the dieted plants respond by making their presence known.
Amaringo's paintings vividly depict this — spirits emerging from
darkness as luminous beings, animals, and geometric patterns in response
to the shaman's song.

### Healing and Purification

Icaros direct healing energy toward patients. During ceremonies, the
vegetalista sings specific icaros for particular illnesses or spiritual
conditions. The song acts as a vehicle for the plant spirit's healing
intelligence, concentrating and directing it with precision. Icaros can
extract harmful energies, restore energetic balance, and facilitate the
purge essential to [[icaros]]
