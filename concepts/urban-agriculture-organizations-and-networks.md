# [[urban-rural-linkages-and-direct-marketing|Urban]] Agriculture Organizations and Networks

The global urban agriculture movement has spawned a vast network of organizations, research institutions, and community initiatives dedicated to food production within cities. These organizations span continents and operate at scales from neighborhood garden plots to international policy advocacy, collectively forming the institutional infrastructure that supports urban food systems worldwide.

## North American Organizations

### United States

**City Farmer** (Vancouver, BC, Canada) — Office of Urban Agriculture, produces publications on urban home [[composting]] (rodent-resistant bins), gardening with disabilities, school garden guidelines for teaching children about nutrition, and maintains a color urban gardens poster.

**American Community Gardening Association (ACGA)** — Based in Philadelphia, ACGA's Community Greening Review publication connects volunteers and professionals who "promote gardening and greening in urban, suburban, and rural America."

**Urban Harvest** (Houston, TX) — Occupies the former Dow School building, focused on "building healthy communities through gardens."

**Green Guerillas** (New York, NY) — Operating from Broadway, this organization has been a driving force in New York City community gardening since the 1970s.

**NYC Department of General Services — Operation Greenthumb** — Runs a "Land Reclamation Program" that converts vacant lots into meadows, providing free technical assistance, design help, fencing, tools, lumber, soil, fruit trees, and shrubs, with special assistance for educational garden projects.

**Bronx Green-Up Program** (NY Botanical Garden) — Provides technical assistance, resource referrals, training, truck service for garden materials, and free soil and plants when available for Bronx-based groups.

**Boston Urban Gardeners (BUG)** — Based in Jamaica Plain, Massachusetts.

**Seattle Tilth Association** — Trains composting teachers, operates demonstration [[compost]] systems, gardens, and greenhouses. Produces printed guides for establishing similar programs and distributes slide shows. Publishes a monthly newsletter and several [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]] gardening books.

**Center for Neighborhood Technology** (Chicago, IL) — Publishes *Neighborhood Works*, focused on sustainable urban development.

**Center for Urban Education about [[phosphate-accumulators-sustainable-agriculture]] (CUESA)** — Based in Berkeley, CA, maintains a Bay Area Urban Gardening and Greening Directory listing over 100 projects and organizations.

**Urban Agriculture Network** (Washington, DC) — An initiative of the United Nations Urban Development Programme, networking more than 6,500 NGOs across 65 countries around urban food production issues.

**Hartford Food System** (Hartford, CT) — Works on food access and community food security.

**Sustainable Resources Center** (Minneapolis, MN) — A non-profit involved in energy, land, and food issues, including community gardens in the Twin Cities.

**Institute for Sustainable Cities** (Sanibel, FL) — Developing strategies for environmentally sound, socially just urban redevelopment, with key areas including urban farming, sustainable transportation, proximity policies, urban wildlife, and community economics.

**Center for Sustainability** (Penn State University) — University-based research center.

**Center for Popular Economics** (Amherst, MA) — Provides "economics education for activists."

### Canada

**FarmFolk/CityFolk Society** (Vancouver, BC) — Connects rural and urban food systems.

**Toronto Food Policy Council** — Municipal government body addressing food system planning.

## European Organizations

### United Kingdom

**National Federation of City Farms** (Bristol) — An extensive network of urban food systems (both plants and animals) set up and managed by local communities. City farms occupy very deprived areas where children have never visited working farms, providing exposure to cows being milked, piglets, goats, geese, and rabbits.

**Neighbourhood Use of Buildings and Space (NUBS)** (London) — Established in 1975 to help voluntary groups develop embryonic ideas for using derelict land and buildings in inner city areas. Has worked with over 90 voluntary groups.

**Parks Council** (New York/London) — Protects and enhances parks, open space, and shorelines.

**Arboricultural Association** — Publishes the *Arboricultural Journal* and *Journal of Urban Forestry*.

**Henry George Foundation** (London) — Promotes land value taxation philosophy as a solution to land allocation challenges, maintaining a library and publishing research on the application of Henry George's economics to contemporary problems.

**[[permaculture-community-design]] Action — Worknet** (Ontario, Canada) — A selective listing of permaculture-related organizations.

### Austria

**Permakultur Austria** (Vienna) — Based at the University of Natural Resources and Life Sciences, offering consultancy, lectures, introductory and design courses, a seed service, library, book sales, and plant exchanges. Has sub-groups working on bioregional development and urban permaculture, with demonstration sites. Translates and publishes permaculture texts and produces a quarterly newsletter.

### Netherlands

**Greentown Group** — Operates an Urban Studies Center in Milton Keynes focused on neighborhood use of buildings and space.

## Permaculture-Specific Organizations

**[[permaculture-institute]] of New Zealand (PINZ)** — National permaculture organization based in Auckland.

**Permaculture in Action** (South Africa) — Based in Noordhoek, offers training and support in permaculture and "city villages" across Southern Africa, including Malawi and Namibia.

**Permaculture Implementation Guild of Seattle (PIGS)** — Regional implementation group.

**Vancouver Permaculture Network** — Hosts permaculture courses and maintains regular meeting schedules at Cotton Wood Gardens.

**Yankee Permaculture** — Publisher and distributor of the [[permaculture-design]] Course pamphlet series and the International Permaculture Solutions Journal (TIPS), operating from Sparr, Florida.

## Ecological Building and Design

**Environmental Building News** (Brattleboro, VT) — Bimonthly newsletter on environmentally responsible building.

**Eco-Home** (Los Angeles, CA) — Demonstration home of urban ecological living, offering tours.

**Earth Connection** (Cincinnati, OH) — Educational center for solar design construction, [[water-conservation]], and eco-sensitive living.

**Los Angeles Eco-Village** — An urban ecovillage at White House Place.

**Projeto Aldeia Global** (Brazil) — Non-profit supporting the development of eco-villages.

**IMAGO** (Cincinnati, OH) — Ecological [[bioregionalism-and-community-organization]].

## Urban Forestry and Green Space

**American Forestry Association** — Publishes *American Forests* and runs the Urban Forests program.

**NYC Street Tree Consortium** — Provides street tree care training and pruning courses.

**Wave Hill** (Bronx, NY) — Focused on urban reforestation.

**Urban Forest Institute** (Hawaii) — Research and advocacy for urban tree canopy.

**Open Space Institute** (New York, NY) — Protects open space in urban environments.

**Neighborhood Open Space Coalition** (New York, NY) — Advocacy for community access to open space.

**Green City Program** (San Francisco, CA) — Calendar and coordination of urban greening events.

**Trust for Public Land — NYC Land Project** — Legal support for community garden preservation.

## Research and Education Institutions

**Center for Urban Horticulture** (University of Washington, Seattle) — Research, teaching, and public service on plant selection, management, and ecosystem roles in urban landscapes.

**International Society of Arboriculture** — Publishes curricula in arboriculture and urban forestry.

**National Association for Urban Studies** — Academic network for urban research.

**Urban Ecology** (Oakland, CA) — Publishes *The Urban Ecologist* journal.

**Urban Environment Centre** (Toronto) — Canadian urban environmental organization.

**Urban Ecology Australia** (Adelaide) — Centre for Urban Ecology promoting ecological city design.

**EarthRight Institute** (Norwich, VT) — Environmental hot line and municipal energy-planning resources.

**International Water and Sanitation Centre** (The Hague, Netherlands) — WHO-affiliated center with publications on water and sanitation in [[low-cost-mushroom-growing-house-construction-developing-countries]].

## Community Development and Economic Organizations

**Center for Citizen Initiatives** (San Francisco, CA) — Agricultural initiative promoting self-sufficiency and increased food production in the former Soviet Union through rooftop gardens and small-scale agricultural enterprises.

**Shelterforce / National Housing Institute** (Orange, NJ) — National cross-disciplinary magazine for community development activists.

**Biological Urban Gardening Services (BUGS)** (Citrus Heights, CA) — International membership organization devoted to reducing synthetic chemical use in urban landscapes through education.

**GREEN Institute** (Minneapolis, MN) — Runs an "ecoindustrial" park, business incubator, urban [[plant-memory-environmental-learning]] center, materials exchange, and energy technology programs.

**Agricultural Initiative for the Former Soviet Union** — Promotes self-sufficiency through rooftop gardens and small-scale agricultural enterprises, providing horticultural technical assistance and workshops for school children.
