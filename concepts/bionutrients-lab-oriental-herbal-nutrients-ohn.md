# Bionutrients Lab — Oriental Herbal Nutrients (OHN)

**Updated:** 2026-04-24

Oriental Herbal Nutrients (OHN) is a natural antibiotic and tonic preparation used in Korean Natural Farming, originally derived from a recipe using ginger, garlic, and traditional Chinese herbs. OHN serves as both a preventive medicine and a treatment for fungal, bacterial, and pest problems in plants and animals. The preparation method involves alcohol extraction similar to herbal tinctures in homeopathy, which extracts the final active ingredients after an initial fermentation stage.

Related preparations include [[bionutrients-lab-lactic-acid-bacteria-serum]], [[bionutrients-lab-fermented-plant-juice-fpj]], [[bionutrients-lab-fermented-fruit-juice-ffj]], [[bionutrients-lab-fish-aminos]], and [[bionutrients-lab-calcium-phosphate-calamansi]].

## Original Korean Recipe

The original recipe from natural farmers of Korea uses not only ginger and garlic but also Chinese herbs:

- **Ginger (Zingiber officinale):** Highly medicinal, aids digestion, warming herb
- **Garlic (Allium sativum):** High sulfur content makes it a potent fungicide and natural antibiotic
- **Angelica acutiloba (Dong Quai):** Traditional Chinese herb good for digestion and blood circulation
- **Glycyrrhiza uralensis (Chinese Licorice):** Used in traditional Chinese medicine for digestion and harmonizing herbal formulas
- **Cinnamomum loureirii (Vietnamese Cinnamon):** Aromatic bark used in traditional medicine for digestive support

The common denominator among all these herbs is their benefit for digestion. This reflects the natural farming principle that healthy digestion and nutrient assimilation are fundamental to plant and animal health.

## Simplified Preparation — Ginger-Garlic Extract

The practical preparation uses equal amounts of ginger and garlic (with or without the Chinese herbs):

### Stage 1: Initial Soak

1. Chop up ginger and garlic
2. Soak in beer or wine overnight (approximately 12 hours)
3. The beer or wine provides a mild alcoholic medium that begins extracting active compounds

### Stage 2: Sugar Fermentation

1. Add 1/3 crude sugar or molasses to the soaked ginger-garlic mixture
2. Let ferment for 5-7 days
3. During this stage, microorganisms begin breaking down the plant material
4. Active ingredients start being extracted through microbial action

### Stage 3: Alcohol Extraction (Tincture Stage)

1. Add alcohol (minimum 40% proof) to the fermented mixture
2. The alcohol stabilizes and arrests fermentation
3. Active ingredients of ginger and garlic are extracted in finale, similar to herbal tincture preparation in homeopathy
4. The alcohol pulls out the remaining medicinal compounds that water-based fermentation cannot reach

### Why Two-Stage Extraction

The two-stage process (fermentation followed by alcohol extraction) is deliberate:

- **Fermentation stage:** Microbial action breaks down cell walls and begins extracting water-soluble compounds
- **Alcohol stage:** Extracts alcohol-soluble compounds (essential oils, resins, alkaloids) that water alone cannot reach
- **Combined effect:** Produces a more complete extraction than either method alone
- **Preservation:** The final alcohol content preserves the preparation for long-term storage

## Properties and Active Compounds

### Garlic

- **High sulfur content:** Allicin and other sulfur compounds provide potent antifungal and antibacterial properties
- **Natural antibiotic:** Effective against a broad spectrum of pathogens
- **Fungicide:** Particularly useful against fungal problems in plants
- **Nutritious:** Rich in minerals and vitamins

### Ginger

- **Digestive aid:** Stimulates digestion and nutrient absorption
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Reduces inflammation in plants and animals
- **Warming herb:** In traditional medicine, promotes circulation and vitality
- **Antimicrobial:** Contains gingerol and other compounds with antimicrobial properties

### Chinese Herbs (when used)

- **Angelica acutiloba:** Blood tonic, supports circulation and nutrient transport
- **Glycyrrhiza uralensis:** Harmonizes the formula, supports digestive function
- **Cinnamomum loureirii:** Aromatic, antimicrobial, supports digestive warmth

## Applications

### Plant Applications

- **Fungal control:** Applied to plants with fungal problems — garlic's sulfur compounds are particularly effective
- **Pest and disease prevention:** Regular application strengthens plant defenses
- **General tonic:** Improves overall plant health and vigor
- **Combined with BIM:** Mixed with beneficial indigenous microorganisms for enhanced pest and disease control
- **Whole-farm application:** For persistent pest/disease problems, spray not only on affected plants but across the entire farm to restore ecological balance

### Animal Applications

- **Chickens and poultry:** Used throughout the life of chickens to maintain health; has kept flocks healthy without vaccines and antibiotics
- **Preventive medicine:** Regular small doses prevent disease before it starts
- **Treatment:** Used when animals show signs of weakening or sickness
- **Digestive support:** Improves feed conversion and nutrient assimilation
- **Human use:** The ginger-garlic concoction has helped people with coughs, fever, and flu; also used for rheumatism

### Observed Effects

- Chicks and chickens maintained health throughout their lives when given OHN regularly
- Plant fungal problems controlled without synthetic fungicides
- Combined with BIM, has arrested persistent pest and disease problems that other methods could not control
- The combination works by restoring ecological balance — addressing the oriental medicine principle that disease results from imbalance between yin and yang

## Integration with BIM and Bionutrients

OHN is one of several bionutrients that natural farmers combine with beneficial indigenous microorganisms:

- **Standard combination:** BIM + fermented plant extracts (FPJ/FFJ) + ginger-garlic extract (OHN) + brown rice vinegar + fish amino acid (FAA)
- **Synergistic effect:** The combination is more effective than any single input
- **OHN's role:** Provides antibiotic and tonic properties to the overall formulation
- **Ecological balance:** When combined with BIM proliferation, OHN helps restore the microbial balance that keeps pathogens in check

## Broader Philosophy

OHN exemplifies several natural farming principles:

- **Self-reliance:** Made from common kitchen ingredients (ginger, garlic, beer/wine, sugar, alcohol)
- **Prevention over cure:** Used preventively to maintain health rather than only as a treatment
- **Multi-species application:** Same preparation works for plants, animals, and humans
- **Traditional knowledge:** Draws on centuries of oriental herbal medicine
- **Fermentation technology:** Uses biological processes (fermentation + tincture) rather than chemical extraction
- **Balance and harmony:** Disease is viewed as imbalance; OHN helps restore balance rather than simply killing pathogens
