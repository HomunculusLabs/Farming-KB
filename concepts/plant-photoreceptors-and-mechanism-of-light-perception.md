## Plant Photoreceptors and the Mechanism of Light Perception

Plants perceive light through a sophisticated array of photoreceptor
proteins that detect not only presence and absence but color, intensity,
direction, and duration. These photoreceptors operate through mechanisms
analogous to, yet fundamentally different from, animal visual systems,
enabling coordinated responses touching every aspect of growth,
development, and survival.
