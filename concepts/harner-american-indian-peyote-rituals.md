## Power Without Intrinsic Morality

Mescalero Apache religious philosophy holds that the world is permeated
by supernatural power with no intrinsic attribute of good or evil; its
virtue resides in its potency. Power approaches people through a plant,
animal, or natural phenomenon by means of a dream or hallucinatory
experience; its acceptance is frequently accompanied by an ordeal. Any
person is a possible power recipient, leading Opler to describe the
Mescaleros as "a tribe of shamans, active or potentially active."

considered bad. An individual who uses power for purposes not oriented
