## Modern Context: Spore vs. Liquid Culture

Since the PF TEK's introduction, home cultivators have developed additional methods that blur the line between spore inoculation and cloning. Liquid culture (LC) involves growing mycelium in a nutrient broth and using that to inoculate substrates — effectively a liquid form of cloning that is faster than spore inoculation but still subject to senescence over many generations.

The spore syringe remains the simplest and most senescence-free starting point for cultivation. Many experienced growers use a hybrid approach: beginning with spores, selecting the best-performing cultures, and then propagating clonally for a limited number of generations before returning to spores to refresh the genetic line.
