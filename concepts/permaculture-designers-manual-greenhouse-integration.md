## Energy Efficiency

Slope and elevation permit the placement of hot air or hot water collectors below their storages, where the thermosiphon effect can operate without external energy inputs. The simple physics of flow and thermal movements can be applied to greenhouse placement:

- Cold air sinks, so low vents release cold air and high vents release hot air
- Thermal mass (water barrels, stone walls) stores daytime heat for night release
- Reflective surfaces bounce additional light into the greenhouse
- Attached greenhouses share walls with heated buildings

## Greenhouse as Part of the Whole System

The glasshouse is one node in a network of connections:

- House HEATS (and is HEATED BY) glasshouse
- Glasshouse HEATS house by day
- Chickens RANGE from glasshouse to orchard
- Compost in glasshouse provides heat and CO2
