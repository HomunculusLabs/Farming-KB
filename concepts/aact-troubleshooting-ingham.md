# AACT Troubleshooting (Ingham)

Updated: 2026-04-18

Source: The [[ghouled-field-guide-historical-context]] I for [[elaine-ingham]], Soil Foodweb Inc.

Ingham provides systematic troubleshooting guidance based on years of Soil Foodweb Inc. testing. The approach: identify the symptom, test the variables, and fix the root cause.

## The Systematic Approach

AACT production is like making milk or baking a cake — things can go wrong at every step:
- The compost can be "sick" (lacking beneficial organisms)
- The tea machine can be dirty
- The aerator may be inadequate
- Water quality can kill organisms
- Food additions can be wrong (too much, too little, wrong type)
- Temperature can be too high or too low

## Symptom: Low Fungal Biomass in Tea

If your compost has documented adequate fungi but they aren't appearing in the brew:

### Check the Machine
- The brewer may not be extracting fungi from the compost
- Check water movement through the compost during brewing
- Visible hyphae in compost should produce visible hyphae in tea — if not, something is destroying them

### Check Aeration
- Fungi are lost when oxygen drops below 5.5 ppm
- Fine bubbles may be physically destroying [[aact-definitions-and-types-ingham]], [[aact-ph-water-quality-ingham]].
