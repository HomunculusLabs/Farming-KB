## Plant Symbiosis in Agriculture

Joseph Cocannouer, professor of soils and horticulture at the
University of the Philippines, showed that plants considered noxious --
ragweed, pigweeds, purslane, and nettles -- bring up minerals from the
subsoil, especially those depleted from topsoil, and help domesticated
plants reach food otherwise beyond their reach. He warned that "the
whole of world agriculture was beginning to ignore" the "law of
togetherness of all things."
