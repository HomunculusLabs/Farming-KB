## The Phosphate Revelation

Fukuoka cites the belated discovery that phosphate fertilizers applied to fields
only penetrate about two inches into the soil surface. Mountains of phosphates that
farmers had religiously spread on their fields year after year were essentially
being dumped on the topsoil, useless. This came out only after decades of use —
what Fukuoka calls an unspeakable injury and injustice to countless farmers.
