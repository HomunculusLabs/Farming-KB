## Major Psilocybin Genera

Psilocybin-containing mushrooms span several genera. The major groups include
Psilocybe, Panaeolus, Gymnopilus, Conocybe, Inocybe, and Pluteus. Each has
distinctive morphological features that aid [[macrofungal-documentation-photography-spore-prints]].

## Habitat Identification

Understanding habitat is crucial for identification. Curiously, the most
potent psilocybes are scarce in deep wilderness but prolific in urban and
suburban areas. The woodland Psilocybe, P. pelliculosa, is one exception,
thriving in disturbed grounds such as trails, abandoned forest roads, and
similar habitats. However, the majority of wood-decomposing psilocybes thrive
in landscaping beds of densely populated areas, especially around buildings
where decorative wood chips for landscaping have become common.
