### Blue City Diesel

Jordan of the Islands' Blue City Diesel is another Chemdawg descendant that incorporates
Blueberry genetics into the Diesel lineage, adding fruity sweetness to the characteristic fuel
aroma. This demonstrates how breeders have used Chemdawg as a backbone for introducing diverse
flavor profiles into new hybrid combinations. The strain combines the best elements of its

## Notable Chemdawg Descendants

### Chemdawg x Amnesia

Bred by Ultimate Seeds, this sativa-dominant hybrid combines an indica-heavy Chemdog IX-III with
an Amnesia plant. The resulting strain produces THC levels around 22% and delivers a soaring,
long-lasting high described as clean and paranoia-free — a notable quality for such a potent
strain.

The flowering period runs about 10 weeks, and the indica influence helps keep the plant
manageable for indoor growers while still producing above-average yields. The combination of
Chemdawg potency with Amnesia cerebral clarity creates a uniquely balanced experience that works
well for both daytime and evening use.
