3. The stem of sesame (Zingiber) contains large amounts of phosphoric

5. Phosphoric acid is involved in photophosphorylation and electron
   transport in photosynthesis, making it essential for energy
   production in all green plants.

6. It affects transporting anabolites and protein synthesis
   throughout the plant, influencing virtually every metabolic
   process.

## Five Deficiency Symptoms

1. Mobile nutrient with old-leaf symptoms: WS-PA can easily travel
   within the plant and moves to vigorous new leaves first. As a
   result, symptoms of deficiency appear mainly on the petiole and
   veins of the old leaf, which are the first to be depleted.

2. Stunted new leaves: The new leaf does not grow well and becomes
   smaller and dark green in color. This darkening occurs because
   phosphorus-deficient plants accumulate sugars that stimulate
   anthocyanin production, giving leaves a purplish or dark green
   appearance.

3. Suppressed flowering: The growth of flowers is noticeably
   suppressed, and the plant naturally bears fewer fruits. Flower
   buds may abort before opening, and pollination success rates
   decline significantly.

4. Dormant internal symptoms: The symptoms are usually dormant
   within the body. It is therefore difficult to recover even
   considering the ability of the plant to cope with the disease
   when the symptoms finally appear on the exterior. Early
   detection and prevention are essential.

5. Thick fruit skin and high acid: The fruit skin becomes thick and
   the acid content increases, making fruits less palatable. Sugar
   content drops while acidity rises, producing tart, thick-skinned
   fruit with poor market quality.

## Preparation from Sesame Stem Charcoal

### Materials Needed
1. Charcoal from sesame stems
2. Water
3. Jar or [[sterile-spore-print-collection-glass-jar-technique]]
4. Porous paper (paper towel)
5. Rubber band or thread

### Procedure
1. Make charcoal out of sesame stems, which contain natural
   phosphoric acid abundantly. Burn the sesame stems and put out
   the fire when large flames appear. The goal is charcoal, not
   complete ash.

2. Put 1 kg of sesame stem charcoal in a cloth bag and dip it in
   5 liters of water to dissolve phosphoric acid (crude liquid of
   natural phosphoric acid).

3. Allow approximately 7 days for extraction, though the time varies
   depending on the temperature of the surroundings.

4. Blow air into the water with a tube once every two days to
   introduce oxygen. This aeration promotes phosphoric acid
   dissolution in the water.
