# Developmental and Property Trusts

Developmental and property trusts are specialized legal instruments designed to purchase real estate for improvement, lease, or rental on behalf of many small investors who cannot afford to individually own or develop properties. In [[permaculture-design]], these trusts channel investment away from destructive industries (coal, oil, uranium, agrochemicals, mining) toward truly rehabilitative ends — reafforestation, preservation of existing forests, sane village development, and rehabilitation of eroded and misused lands.

## Why Ethical Property Trusts?

No investment in "glamour stocks" is likely to yield anything but more pollution and to hasten global collapse. The evidence on acid rain alone should convince any sane person that further destructive "progress and development" will cause social and environmental upheaval. Developmental trusts provide a vehicle for turning money resources toward earth repair while also ensuring community survival through community involvement.

With good management and skillful work, there is no reason why such trusts cannot pay for themselves or show a financial advantage to investors. It is an invaluable experience to model a property trust and teach others how to follow any successes achieved.

## Trust Structure and Operations

The management group obtains backing from investors (via a public prospectus) to float a Property Trust on the investment market. The prime purpose is to give every person a chance to do more than object to or protest inappropriate land sales, land misuse, and poor planning — and to actively invest in saving critical or endangered national resources while rehabilitating eroded lands.

### Investment Parameters
- **Capital cap**: The first trust aims to stop accepting investors at $2 to 5 million
- **Unit price**: Low ($100) to enable even poorer people to invest
- **Partnership units**: A single unit can be held by a partnership, society, or corporate group
- **No holding limits**: No limit to the number of units any person can hold

### Investor Involvement
Unlike other property trusts, investors should be given every opportunity to involve themselves in their investments through:
- On-site work
- Consultancy
- Leasehold arrangements
- Tree nursery supply
- Preference in sales of village titles
- Access to products or services
- Controlled recreational access to lands and buildings

### Fund Allocation
The precise amount used for administration should not exceed 4 to 8% of funds (based on figures from other property trusts). The remainder is devoted to purchase and development of properties as outlined in the prospectus. Costs reduce as trust income grows.

Funds are used for:
- Setting up the trust deed and management company
- Paying for the work of the trustee
- Paying management a retainer
- Normal running costs: office expenses, printing, bookkeeping, accounting, valuation, and management travel

Surplus funds can be invested in ethical systems including housing cooperatives, inventory for development projects, and shares in ethical businesses.

## Tax Advantages

By improving properties so purchased, their value increases. Under typical rulings, taxation is not incurred on that increase in value if the property is held for 10 or more years, nor is the trust itself taxed on its income from investors.

## Project Areas for Ethical Trusts

| Project Area | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Wildlife and forest habitat | Purchase of threatened habitat, managed for aims stated in prospectus |
| Land rehabilitation | Purchase and rehabilitation of eroded, salted, deforested, or misused land |
| Sustainable villages | Development of energy-efficient, sustainable villages on trust lands |
| Special interest developments | Trout fisheries, aquaculture, seed orchards for lease or sale |
| Bioregional development | Purchase of community resources in specific regions for residents and unit holders |
| Overseas outposts | Purchase of selected properties for assisting developing countries |
| Clean energy systems | Bioregional clean energy systems or clean transport methods |

## Staged Development and Returns

The trust stages development sequentially as funds become available:

1. **Formation**: Purchase, prospectus writing, and design
2. **Development**: Projects started in sequence as funds allow
3. **Management**: Annual re-evaluation of properties
4. **Investor engagement**: Investors informed of and encouraged to assist in developments and sales
5. **Maturity**: Possible sale or disposal of trust properties after 10 years, with increase in real value of units to investors

## The Development Cycle

```
Investors → Trustee → Management (4-8% overhead)
                      ↓
              Purchase & Design
                      ↓
              Sequential Projects
                      ↓
        Work/Consultancy/Services (active investments)
                      ↓
              Annual Re-evaluation
                      ↓
        Sale after 10+ years OR re-investment
```

## Small Business Centers

A "small business centre" can itself be a property trust. While many property trusts concentrate on city office properties or rural monocultures, ethical trusts can concentrate on village development, land rehabilitation, and community resource acquisition — areas that serve both people and planet.

## Key Principles

1. **Community involvement** is as important as financial return
2. **Low entry cost** enables broad participation
3. **Active investor engagement** creates deeper commitment and better outcomes
4. **Staged development** manages risk while building capacity
5. **Ethical investment criteria** exclude destructive industries entirely
6. **Long-term holding** (10+ years) maximizes tax advantages and property value appreciation
