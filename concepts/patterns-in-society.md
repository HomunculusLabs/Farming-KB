## Designer's Checklist for Pattern Analysis

1. **Identify the pattern** — What recurring form or process is present?
2. **Determine the function** — What does the pattern achieve in the system?
3. **Map the boundaries** — Where does it begin and end?
4. **Assess the scale** — At what order of magnitude does it operate?
5. **Locate the edges** — Where is the greatest interaction and exchange?
6. **Identify the flows** — What moves through (energy, water, materials,
   people, information)?
7. **Find the niches** — Where are underutilized spaces or resources?
8. **Apply the pattern** — Can it be transplanted or modified for the site?
9. **Evaluate the result** — Does the applied pattern improve system function?
10. **Connect to larger patterns** — How does it integrate regionally?
