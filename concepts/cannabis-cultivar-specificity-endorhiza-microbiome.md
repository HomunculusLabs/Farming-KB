County, California) with substantially different soil types. The Orange County soil

## Mechanisms of Cultivar-Specific Selection

### Immune Recognition

Plant innate immune systems recognize microbial-associated molecular patterns
(MAMPs) through pattern recognition receptors (PRRs). The specificity of this
recognition varies between cultivars due to genetic differences in receptor
sequences and downstream signaling cascades. Cultivars with different immune
profiles will permit colonization by different bacterial assemblages, effectively
acting as selective filters on the rhizosphere community pool.

### Secondary Metabolite Interactions

Cannabis produces hundreds of secondary metabolites, including cannabinoids,
terpenes, flavonoids, and alkaloids. Many of these compounds have antimicrobial
properties that vary in their spectrum of activity. A cultivar producing high
levels of certain terpenes may suppress specific bacterial groups while permitting
others, creating a distinctive endorhiza fingerprint.
