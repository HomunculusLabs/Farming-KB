## Duckweed (*Lemnaceae*)

The family Lemnaceae comprises approximately 40 known perennial, free-floating species. These tiny plants form a green mat on still or slow-moving water surfaces.

### Water Purification

Duckweeds are valuable for removing excess nutrients from water. Tests on *Lemna minor* and *Lemna trisulca* have demonstrated their ability to concentrate boron, aluminium, manganese, iron, titanium, copper, and cobalt from water into their tissue. This makes them effective biological filters for:
- Farm dam [[mollison-designers-greywater-treatment-reedbeds]]
- Aquaculture water [[pollinator-habitat]].

## Integration Design

Aquatic plant systems should be integrated with:
- **Duck and waterfowl systems**: Ducks fertilize water, aquatic plants feed ducks
- **Aquaculture**: Fish waste fertilizes plants, plants filter water and provide fish food
- **Irrigation return flows**: Capture nutrients from irrigation runoff before they leave the property
- **Wetland margins**: Gradual transitions from deep water to emergent plants to margin plants
- **Composting**: Excess aquatic plant biomass added to compost systems
