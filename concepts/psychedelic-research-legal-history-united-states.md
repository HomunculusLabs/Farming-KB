## The Resurgence (2000s-present)

Beginning in the 2000s, a gradual reopening of psychedelic research has occurred:

- FDA approval of MDMA-assisted psychotherapy studies through [[hofmann-lsd-psychiatric-research]], NYU, and other institutions

- DEA registration for researchers studying Schedule I psychedelics

- Growing recognition of the therapeutic potential of psychedelics

- FDA Breakthrough Therapy designations for both MDMA and psilocybin
