## Treatment-Resistant Depression (TRD)

### COMPASS Pathways Phase IIb Trial (2021)

The largest randomized controlled trial of psilocybin to date, published in the New England Journal of Medicine:

- **Design**: 233 participants with TRD (failed ≥2 antidepressants), randomized 1:1:1 to 1 mg (control), 10 mg, or 25 mg COMP360 psilocybin
- **Sessions**: Single 25-mg session with psychological support, plus preparation and integration sessions
- **Primary endpoint**: Change in MADRS (Montgomery-Åsberg Depression Rating Scale) at week 3
- **Results**: 25 mg group showed -12.0 point MADRS reduction vs -7.9 in 1 mg control (p=0.002). Response rates (≥50% reduction): 37% vs 19%. Remission rates: 29% vs 8%
- **Adverse effects**: Headache (most common), nausea, fatigue. Suicidal ideation occurred in all groups but was slightly more frequent in the 25 mg group. No serious treatment-emergent adverse events attributed to psilocybin.

### COMPASS Pathways Phase III (2023–2025)

Two pivotal Phase III trials (COMP001 and COMP002) enrolled approximately 900 participants with TRD across 100+ sites in 20+ countries:

- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group comparison of single-dose 25 mg COMP360 vs 1 mg (active control) with psychological support
- **Primary endpoint**: MADRS change at week 6
- **Key features**: Standardized therapist training program, centralized dosing protocols, diverse international population
- **Results**: Mixed — COMP001 met its primary endpoint with statistical significance, while COMP002 did not achieve statistical significance on its primary endpoint. The divergent results raised questions about the reproducibility of single-session [[psilocybin-pharmacokinetics-bioavailability]]
