### Approach Options

Ingham identifies three paths to sustainable strawberry production:

1. **Plug plants with AACT**: Treat transplants with compost tea at planting and through
   the season
2. **AACT throughout season**: Regular applications to established plantings
3. **Full soil replacement with compost**: Replace diseased soil entirely with quality
   compost—in mere minutes going "from sick soil to healthy"

- Fungal-dominant tea is essential—match the forest understory ecology
