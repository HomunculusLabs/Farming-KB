## Organic Greenhouse Management

updated: 2026-04-18

A greenhouse greatly extends the growing season and allows you to grow plants that would not survive outdoors in your climate. In the organic garden, the greenhouse is managed without synthetic chemicals -- pest control relies on biological methods, ventilation, and healthy plant culture. The greenhouse can be used year-round with careful management.

## Greenhouse Uses

- Starting seeds and raising seedlings (early spring)
- Growing heat-loving crops (tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, aubergines)
- Extending the season for salads and herbs
- Overwintering tender plants
- Propagating plants from cuttings
- Growing exotic or out-of-season crops

## Seasonal Management

### Spring

Spring is the busiest time in the greenhouse. The main tasks are:

**Seed sowing**: Sow seeds in seed trays using a good quality compost. Most seeds need warmth to germinate -- a heated propagator or warm windowsill works well. Once seedlings emerge, move them into good light to prevent leggy growth.

**Hardening off**: Before moving plants outdoors, they must be acclimated to outside conditions. Start by placing them in a closed cold frame, then gradually increase ventilation over a week or so. This process is called "hardening off."

**Temperature management**: Open vents and doors on warm days to prevent overheating. Close them at night to retain warmth. Most plants prefer temperatures between 55-75 degrees F (13-24 degrees C).
