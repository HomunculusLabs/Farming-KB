# Neo Blizzard — Matanuska Tundra × Congolese by Evil Seeds

## Summary

Neo Blizzard is a sativa-dominant strain bred by Spain's Evil Seeds, created by crossing the epic Matanuska Tundra with a Congolese sativa. The resulting plant is 75% sativa and 25% indica, producing an incredibly powerful and fairly balanced high. Notable for its unusual steak aroma, heavy production, excellent calyx-to-leaf ratio, and 70-80 day flowering period, Neo Blizzard is a force to be reckoned with for both indoor and outdoor growers.

## Growth Characteristics

In terms of cultivation, the little indica influence in NeoBlizzard serves to take the edge off what would otherwise be a seriously severe sativa variety. As it stands, NeoBlizzard will not grow as tall as pure sativa plants will, but might still pose a problem for indoor growers unless they take steps to keep it nice and tamed while still in the vegetative state.

The indica genetics add some bushiness to the sativa growing style, creating a plant that fills out nicely without reaching the extreme heights that pure sativas can achieve. This bushiness also contributes to yield, as the plant develops multiple bud sites throughout its branching structure rather than concentrating production in a single main cola.

Your finished buds will be incredibly easy to manicure thanks to the great calyx-to-leaf ratio. This is a significant practical advantage at harvest time, as less time and effort is required to trim the buds to a premium standard. The high calyx-to-leaf ratio also means that more of the plant's energy is directed toward resin and trichome production rather than foliage development.

## Indoor Cultivation

Indoor growers will need to manage the plant's height during the vegetative stage, as even with the indica influence, Neo Blizzard can stretch significantly once flowering begins. Techniques such as topping, low-stress training (LST), or scrogging can help keep the plant within manageable bounds while still maximizing yield potential.

The bushy growth pattern inherited from the Matanuska Tundra parent makes Neo Blizzard responsive to training techniques that encourage lateral branching. Indoor growers who invest time in vegetative training will be rewarded with a more even canopy and improved light penetration to lower bud sites.

Nutrient requirements for Neo Blizzard are moderate — the plant responds well to standard feeding schedules but does not demand the heavy feeding that some sativa-dominant strains require. Overfeeding should be avoided, as the indica genetics make the plant somewhat sensitive to nutrient burn if pushed too hard during the flowering phase.

The 70-80 day flowering period means that outdoor growers in Mediterranean and similar climates can expect to harvest their Neo Blizzard plants in the middle of October, making it suitable for regions with long growing seasons.

Outdoor plants will benefit from full sun exposure and well-draining soil. The plant's natural resilience, inherited from both parent strains, helps it withstand the variable weather conditions that outdoor growers often face during the extended flowering period.

In regions with shorter growing seasons, Neo Blizzard may need to be started early indoors and transplanted outside once the risk of frost has passed. The plant's vigorous vegetative growth means it will quickly catch up once established outdoors, but the long flowering period requires careful timing to ensure harvest before cold weather arrives.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

When they say that NeoBlizzard is a force to be reckoned with, they are not messing around. There is something about this strain that just screams "manly" — not only do both parent strains sound like places you would go on a year-long trip around the globe during which you would grow a full beard and get bitten by venomous things a number of times, but the smoke itself has an aroma of cooked steak.

Yes, you read that right — a juicy sirloin just being tossed on the grill for a few seconds before being served up almost raw to a buck hunter with hairy palms. It is that manly. The unusual steak aroma is one of Neo Blizzard's most distinctive characteristics, setting it apart from virtually every other cannabis strain on the market.

The savory, meaty terpene profile is believed to originate from the unique combination of Matanuska Tundra and Congolese genetics. While many cannabis strains feature fruity, earthy, or diesel aromas, Neo Blizzard's steak-like scent is genuinely unusual and has become one of its most talked-about attributes among those who have had the pleasure of experiencing it.
