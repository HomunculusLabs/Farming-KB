## Cannabis Health Index (CHI) Method

## Purpose

The Cannabis Health Index (CHI) is an evidence-based compilation and
review of available scientific literature relevant to more than 100
different chronic diseases and stubborn symptoms. Developed by Uwe
Blesching, PhD, the CHI model provides a systematic framework for
evaluating cannabis as a therapeutic intervention, addressing a critical
gap in evidence-based cannabis medicine. (see [[blesching-cannabis-chi-scoring-evidence-based-medicine]]).
The demand for evidence-driven healthcare has led to the establishment of
13 evidence-based practice centers funded by the U.S. Department of
Health, including institutions like the Mayo Clinic, Duke University,

## Strain-Specific Considerations

The CHI framework incorporates evidence-based strain and form
considerations:

- **Cannabinoid profile**: THC-to-CBD ratios relevant to specific
  conditions
- **CB1 vs. CB2 deficiency assessment**: Patients with neurological
  symptoms likely lack CB1 activation; those with immune conditions
  likely are CB2-deficient, guiding strain selection
- **Form of administration**: Tinctures, edibles, vaporization, topical
  applications — each with different bioavailability profiles
