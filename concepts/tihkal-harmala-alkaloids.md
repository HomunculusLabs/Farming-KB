# Harmala Alkaloids: Harmine, Harmaline, and Tetrahydroharmine

## Overview

The harmala alkaloids are beta-carboline compounds that serve as **monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs)**. They are the essential pharmacological component that enables orally inactive tryptamines (particularly DMT) to become active when consumed by mouth -- the basis of ayahuasca.

TIHKAL covers three principal harmala alkaloids:

## Harmaline (Entry #13)

**Chemical name**: 3,4-Dihydro-7-methoxy-1-methyl-beta-carboline

**Dosage**: 150-300 mg, orally
**Duration**: 5-8 hours

### Effects of Harmaline Alone
Harmaline is far from pharmacologically inert as a mere MAO inhibitor:
- **Visual disturbances**: Tracers, shimmering in lateral vision, weird visual ripplings, eidetic imagery with closed eyes
- **Auditory changes**: Higher frequencies attenuated, lower frequencies amplified
- **Tactile effects**: Pronounced numbing
- **Gastrointestinal**: Nausea and diarrhea (dose-dependent)
- **Motor effects**: At high doses (500 mg), complete collapse of motor coordination
- **Psychological**: Clouding of thoughts, disorientation, intoxication

At 500 mg: "Intense and annoying visual disturbances, and complete collapse of motor coordination. I could barely stagger to the bathroom." The subject felt they were "struggling to metabolize a chemical disruption of physiological functions."

### Harmaline Combined with DMT (Pharmahuasca)

This is where harmaline's [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] at 80 minutes |
| 150 mg | 35 mg | "Highly effective MAO blocker" at the 35 mg DMT level |

**Key finding**: 150 mg harmaline is highly effective as an MAO blocker, making as little as 35 mg DMT orally active. The effects are qualitatively different from either compound alone and can last 4+ hours.

### Harmaline Combined with 5-MeO-DMT

| Harmaline | 5-MeO-DMT | Result |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 70 mg | 10 mg | Pressure changes around eyes, visual activity, nausea |
| 80 mg | 10 mg | "Conceptually very active. Extremely rewarding." |
| 150 mg | 25 mg | Full 5-MeO-DMT experience beginning at 15 minutes |

## Harmine (Entry #14)

**Chemical name**: 7-Methoxy-1-methyl-beta-carboline

Harmine is the other major alkaloid found in [[mckenna-food-gods-peganum-harmala-soma]], present alongside harmaline and tetrahydroharmine. It is closely related to harmaline (the dihydro form) and shares MAO inhibitory properties.

## Tetrahydroharmine (Entry #54)

**Chemical name**: 7-Methoxy-1-methyl-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-beta-carboline

### Known Activity
- **Single trial**: 300 mg orally produced effects "similar to what I had experienced with 100 milligrams of harmaline"
- **MAOI**: Effective monoamine oxidase inhibitor
- Natural isolate is the **dextrorotatory isomer** ([alpha]D +32 degrees)

### Importance in Ayahuasca
Tetrahydroharmine may be an extremely important factor in [[mckenna-peganum-harmala-soma-substitute-psilocybin-synergy]]
- Reported at levels **equal to those of harmine** in analyses of ayahuasca samples
- Harmaline itself is usually present in "rather small amounts" in ayahuasca
- Yet it remains essentially **unexplored** pharmacologically in controlled settings

### Historical Discovery
First isolated from [[ayahuasca-and-banisteriopsis-caapi]] in the 1950s during careful chromatographic analysis. Initially misidentified as 6-methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine (6-MeO-DMT) before the correct structure (racemic tetrahydroharmine) was confirmed spectroscopically.

## Peganum harmala (Syrian Rue) Seeds

Seeds contain a mixture of harmala alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine, and others). Their effects differ significantly from pure harmaline:

| Dose | Effects |
|------|---------|
| 2 g | No effects |
| 5 g | Tinnitus, nystagmus, nausea, intense closed-eye hallucinations, enhanced sex drive |
