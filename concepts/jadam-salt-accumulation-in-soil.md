## JADAM Salt Accumulation in Soil

## Overview

Soil salinization is one of the most serious problems in modern agriculture. JADAM addresses it through its fundamental "ask nature" approach, identifying the root cause and proposing a natural solution that costs nothing.

## The Problem

High salinity afflicts farmlands worldwide. Farmers apply irrigation water containing dissolved substances, and when water evaporates, salts are left behind. Acid rain carries pollutants that similarly accumulate. Year after year, these salts build up, making farming increasingly difficult.

## Ask Nature: Does Nature Have This Problem?

JADAM's signature approach is to consult nature. When JADAM asks nature whether it suffers from salt accumulation, the answer is unequivocally no -- despite receiving the same acid rain that falls on farms.

Why? Because in natural soil:
- Moisture seeps deep into the ground when it rains
- As water percolates downward, it encounters a huge population of diverse microorganisms
- These microbes act on pollutants, breaking them down and purifying the water
- Purified water flows into aquifers, reaches rivers and oceans, then returns to the sky
- Material circulation functions properly, so pollutants cannot accumulate
- Sky and soil, heaven and earth are mutually open and interact seamlessly
