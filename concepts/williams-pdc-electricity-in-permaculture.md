## Electricity in Permaculture
While electricity exists abundantly in nature, electrical systems as humans
use them are not natural. We use machinery to convert other forms of
energy into electricity, then convert that electricity into radical heat
or work. This is why electricity is considered something we truly "take"
from nature, since there is no natural loop by which we return it.
Modern humans need electricity, and the goal of a permaculture designer
is to take electricity from renewable sources in a way that does not
degrade ecosystems.

## The Primary Strategy: Reduce, Reduce, Reduce

The first and foremost strategy in any permaculture electrical design is
reduction. The best way to generate electricity without harming the
environment is to not need as much in the first place. Every watt saved
is a watt saved. A permaculture electrical designer should use every trick
available to reduce the electricity requirement of the design. See
[[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-ethical-foundations-in-permaculture]] of this approach.

## Natural Lighting Strategies

During the day the sun provides all needed light, yet buildings often
require power-burning light bulbs even in daylight. Design tricks for
natural lighting include:

- **Skylights**: Well-known design elements allowing sunlight in
- **Light Tubes**: Metal tubes in the roof that concentrate and focus
  sunlight into a room
- **Lightshelves**: Features added to windows that bounce light from the
  window up to the ceiling, which then reflects deeper into the room
- **Fiber Optics**: Using fiber optic cables to harness natural sunlight
  and distribute it through a building
- **Interior Design**: Color choices and surface materials affect how well
  natural light is distributed within a space

## Solar Water Heaters

Solar water heaters do not use any electricity; the heat from the sun
directly heats the water. They work even in moderate to cold climates. The
trick is to allow sunlight to hit the water storage while keeping the
storage in an insulated envelope so ambient cold does not draw the heat
out.
DIY solar water heaters can be made from water tanks held within
refrigerators with glass fronts. You open the door in the morning,
sunlight enters through the glass and heats the water, and you close the
door at night to keep the water insulated. Even when used as a
pre-heater, solar water heaters reduce the energy needed by standard
water heating systems.
