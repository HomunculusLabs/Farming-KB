## Ecological Niches

Psilocybe species occupy diverse ecological niches:

- **Dung-inhabiting species** (coprophilous): P. cubensis, many Panaeolus and
  Copelandia — found on bovine dung in tropical and subtropical pastures
- **Grassland species** (geophilous): P. semilanceata, P. strictipes — in wet
  meadows, pastures, and fields, often associated with livestock
- **Lignicolous species** (wood-inhabiting): P. cyanescens, P. azurescens,
  P. pelliculosa — on decaying wood, wood chips, and forest debris
- **Soil-inhabiting species**: P. mexicana, P. zapotecorum — in disturbed soils,
  trails, and roadbanks in subtropical forests
