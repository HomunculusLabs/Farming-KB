## Early Status

Before the mid-twentieth century, psychoactive mushrooms were not specifically
regulated anywhere in the world. In Mexico, indigenous use of sacred mushrooms was
practised with a degree of cultural tolerance, though colonial authorities had
periodically suppressed what they viewed as pagan practices.

In Europe and North America, magic mushrooms were simply not on the legislative
radar. Their use was so minimal and so poorly understood that no specific laws
addressed them. The few people who knew of their psychoactive properties were mostly
anthropologists, mycologists, and the indigenous communities of Mesoamerica.

## Modern Developments

The twenty-first century has seen growing challenges to the prohibitionist
consensus. In the United States, cities including Denver, Oakland, and Santa Cruz
have decriminalised psilocybin mushrooms. Oregon has legalised therapeutic use.
