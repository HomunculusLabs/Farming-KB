## The STP Incident of 1967

### Distribution in Haight-Ashbury

DOM was distributed widely in [[mescaline]], and amphetamines.

### The Dosage Problem

Two critical complications arose during this first appearance:

1. **No identity known**: No connection was made between STP and DOM. No one knew what drug had been distributed, making overdose treatment impossible.

2. **Excessive dosage**: Initial tablets contained 20 mg of DOM per tablet, later reduced to 10 mg. Either dose is now known to be extremely high — the active dose range is only 3-10 mg.

### The Slow Onset Trap

The overdose situation was compounded by DOM's slow onset of action. Users familiar with LSD (which shows effects in 15-20 minutes) expected similar rapid onset from STP. When little was felt at the 30-minute mark, many users assumed the dose was weak and took one or two additional tablets. Some overdose victims may have consumed as much as 30 mg of DOM.

The timeline was treacherous:
- 0.5 hours: First subtle awareness
- 1 hour: + or ++ effects
- 2 hours: Full impact, often overwhelming

Combined with the 14-20 hour duration, many innocent users were caught unprepared for an experience far more intense and prolonged than they expected.

## Qualitative Experience

### Low Doses (1-3 mg)

At 1 mg, effects include slight mouth dryness, noticeable mydriasis (pupil dilation), and an "eerie" overall feeling. At 2.3 mg, mood elevation begins at 2-3 hours, with considerable color enhancement but minimal perceptual distortion. The emotional content and empathy for others was described as closer to mescaline than to amphetamine.

### Moderate Doses (3-5 mg)

At 3 mg, users report enhanced sharpness of observation with the ability to focus at different depths of concepts. Colors become more numerous rather than merely brighter. At 4 mg, one subject ranked the visual experience as surpassing LSD, mescaline, cannabis, and peyote — "THE CLOUDS!!! No visual experience has ever been like this." At 5 mg, the experience becomes challenging, with users alternating between joy and discomfort, sometimes confronting difficult personal truths.
