Autopsy findings include marked gastro-intestinal edema, hemorrhagic gastro-enteritis, fatty degeneration of the heart and liver, tubular necrosis of the kidneys, swollen brain with multiple hemorrhages, and degenerative nerve cell damage. Death is primarily from liver and kidney failure. ## A Cautionary Tale The case of a sixteen-year-old girl who died six days after consuming misidentified mushrooms underscores the life-and-death importance of proper identification.
