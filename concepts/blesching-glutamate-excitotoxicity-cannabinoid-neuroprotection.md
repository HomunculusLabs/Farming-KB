## The Endocannabinoid System as Glutamate Regulator

### CB1-Mediated Modulation
Activation of CB1 receptors modulates synaptic glutamate transmission and
plasticity. The ECS has a dual capacity:

- **In healthy conditions:** CB1 modulates glutamate for normal synaptic
  plasticity, learning, and memory
- **In pathological conditions:** The ECS reduces excessive glutamate release,
  protecting neurons from excitotoxic damage

### Cannabinoid Therapy

1. Presynaptic CB1 activation reducing excessive glutamate release
2. Anti-inflammatory effects breaking the inflammation-glutamate feedback loop
