## Discovery of the Zwitterionic Intermediate

During workup, an unexpected observation was made. The 1H NMR spectrum of
the remaining substance in CDCl3 showed complicated signals. TLC revealed an
additional spot at the origin, and the whitish material no longer dissolved
in dichloromethane. A similar observation had been reported by Nichols and
Frescas, who concluded that hydrolytic cleavage of one O-benzyl group had
occurred, yielding a zwitterionic O-monobenzyl phosphate.

Purification by preparative reversed-phase HPLC afforded a single compound
(compound 8), which was analyzed by NMR. The 1H and 13C NMR spectra in CD3OD
showed signals for two sets of benzyl groups and a psilocin core. Notably,
the proton signal of the methylene on one benzyl group was shifted to high
field (delta 4.56, 2H, s) compared to the other (delta 4.98, 1H, s; 4.96,
1H, s), while the proton signals of the methylene and N,N-dimethyl parts
on the psilocin core were shifted to low field.

The 31P NMR spectrum confirmed the presence of the phosphate moiety. HMBC
and NOESY experiments revealed that one benzyl group was directly linked at
the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl part, forming a quaternary ammonium ion.
This confirmed that compound 8 was a zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate
derivative — a new chemical entity not previously characterized.

## Mechanism of the Intramolecular Conversion

The conversion of the O,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative (compound 7) into
the zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative (compound 8) occurs
through an intramolecular process. During the aqueous workup for removing
excess reagents, hydrolytic cleavage of one O-benzyl group takes place. The
resulting phosphate anion then attacks one of the N-methyl groups
intramolecularly, forming the quaternary ammonium center.

This conversion is easily achieved by suspending the worked-up reaction
mixture in CH2Cl2 overnight. The zwitterionic nature of compound 8 is what
makes large-scale isolation possible — its insolubility in dichloromethane
allows it to be separated by simple filtration.

## Large-Scale Isolation

The zwitterionic nature of compound 8 made possible its large-scale isolation
by filtration in over 85% yield. The excess remaining dibenzyl phosphate was
removed by washing with CH2Cl2, exploiting the differential solubility
between the zwitterionic product and the neutral phosphate byproduct.
