## Making Good Compost

Making good compost is something of an art because each batch of
starting materials is different. Key requirements for both methods:
- Maintain 5.5 ppm O2 or higher during compost cycle
- Pleasant smell (no anaerobic conditions)
- Moisture 45-75%
- The required set of organisms to compete with, inhibit, and
  consume diseases or pest organisms
- Match the fungal:bacterial ratio to the requirements of the
  target plant
