## Molecular Methods for In-Situ Fungal Diversity Monitoring

Molecular biological techniques have revolutionized the ability to
assess fungal diversity directly from environmental samples, bypassing
the need for culturing or observing fruiting structures. These in-situ
approaches are particularly valuable for fungi because of the
limitations of traditional methods, including the inability to culture
many species, the ephemeral nature of sporocarp production, and
intracellular variation in morphology and biochemistry.
