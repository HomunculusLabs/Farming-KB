## Shaman

Shaman is a sativa-dominant hybrid [[oner-cannabis-breeding-fundamentals]], contributes its characteristic vigor, yield
potential, and the pungent aroma that has made Skunk varieties famous since
the 1970s.

The combination of these two parent strains creates a genetic profile that is
approximately 75% sativa and 25% indica. The indica influence primarily
manifests in the tamed growth structure and the enhanced coloration, while the
sativa genetics dominate the effects and overall plant morphology.
