# Shaman

Shaman is a sativa-dominant hybrid cannabis strain bred by Dutch Passion of
Holland. Dating back to the 1980s, Shaman is a classic variety that combines
Purple #1 and Skunk genetics, resulting in a plant prized for its stunning
coloration, resilient growth, and thoughtful cerebral effects. Named after the
traditional spiritual guides who lead journeys of consciousness, Shaman lives
up to its name by delivering a deeply contemplative sativa experience.

## Genetics and Lineage

Shaman was created by crossing Purple #1 with Skunk genetics. Purple #1 is a
well-known Dutch cannabis variety celebrated for its ability to develop vivid
purple coloration, particularly when exposed to cooler nighttime temperatures
during flowering. The Skunk parent, one of the most foundational strains in
modern cannabis breeding, contributes its characteristic vigor, yield
potential, and the pungent aroma that has made Skunk varieties famous since
the 1970s.

The combination of these two parent strains creates a genetic profile that is
approximately 75% sativa and 25% indica. The indica influence primarily
manifests in the tamed growth structure and the enhanced coloration, while the
sativa genetics dominate the effects and overall plant morphology.

## Breeder Information

Dutch Passion is one of the oldest and most established seed companies in the
Netherlands, with a history dating back several decades. The company was among
the first to regularize [[cannabis-seed-production]] and has been instrumental in
developing many of the genetic lines that form the foundation of modern
cannabis breeding. Shaman represents one of their classic offerings, a strain
that has stood the test of time since its development in the 1980s.

## Growing Characteristics

Shaman was bred specifically for outdoor and greenhouse cultivation, though it
can be grown indoors with reduced results. The 25% indica genetics help tame
the characteristic sativa stretch, resulting in a plant that reaches only 5 to
6 feet at its tallest. Key cultivation details include:

- **Flowering time**: Approximately 8 weeks
- **Outdoor harvest**: End of September to early October
- **Height**: 5-6 feet maximum
- **Growth pattern**: Classical sativa style with moderated stretch; responds
  well to careful trimming and training for bushier structure
- **Resistance**: Highly resistant to spider mites and mold; bud rot is
  possible in very humid conditions but unlikely
- **Coloration**: While breeders claim approximately 50% of plants turn blue,
  many crops end up dark purple with minimal green or white
- **Bud quality**: Outdoor buds are described as dripping with resin and
  intensely colorful at harvest time
- **Indoor suitability**: Can be grown indoors but with less impressive
  results and lower yields compared to outdoor cultivation

The strain's hardiness and resistance to common outdoor growing problems make
it an excellent choice for outdoor cultivators, particularly those in European
climates. The dramatic purple coloration that most plants develop adds
significant aesthetic value to the garden.

## Aroma and Flavor

Shaman's aroma and flavor profile is characterized by a typical "purple"
taste, which generally refers to a combination of sweet, fruity, and
grape-like notes. The smoke has a sharp sweetness that is thoroughly
enjoyable and benefits from a good, long curing period to fully develop.
The well-rounded flavor experience is enhanced by the strain's generous
resin production, which adds depth and smoothness to each inhalation.

## Effects

The psychoactive effects of Shaman are described as "heady, thoughtful, and
cerebral," aligning with the strain's spiritual namesake. The experience is
one of enhanced contemplation and mental exploration, making it suitable for
meditative practices, creative pursuits, or deep conversations. The effects
are typically sativa in character, promoting mental clarity and creative
thinking without the anxiety or racing thoughts that can accompany more
intense sativa varieties.

## Medical Applications

The contemplative and mentally engaging effects of Shaman may be beneficial
for patients dealing with stress, mild depression, and conditions that respond
to enhanced creative thinking. The moderate THC content provides therapeutic
effects without being overwhelming, making it suitable for patients who prefer
a gentler approach to medical cannabis.

## Potency

Shaman has a tested potency of 13.7% THC. This moderate potency level makes
it accessible to a wide range of consumers, including those who may find
high-potency strains too intense. The moderate THC level also means that the
strain's other qualities, such as flavor and aroma, can be fully appreciated
without being overshadowed by excessive psychoactive intensity.

## Classification

Shaman is classified as a sativa-dominant hybrid at approximately 75% sativa.
The indica influence from the Purple #1 parent is primarily expressed in the
plant's physical characteristics, including its manageable height and striking
purple coloration. The effects remain firmly in the sativa camp, delivering a
cerebral experience befitting the strain's evocative name.

## Historical Significance

As a strain dating back to the 1980s, Shaman represents an important piece of
cannabis breeding history. It stands as an early example of breeders
successfully combining purple coloration traits with desirable sativa effects,
a combination that has become increasingly sought after in modern cannabis
culture.
