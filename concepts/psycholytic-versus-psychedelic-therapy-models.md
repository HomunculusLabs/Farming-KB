### Dosage and Method

The psycholytic approach is characterized by low to moderate doses
administered in multiple sessions over an extended treatment period.
Typical protocols involved 50-200 micrograms of LSD, with doses
increasing gradually across ten to twenty sessions. The low-dose
approach was designed to facilitate a therapeutic dialogue in which
the patient could maintain sufficient ego function to verbally
process the emerging material.

## The Psychedelic Model

### Origins and Development

The psychedelic model emerged more or less simultaneously with the
psycholytic approach but in Anglo-American rather than European
contexts. The English psychiatrist Humphrey Osmond, working in
Canada with Abram Hoffer on the treatment of alcoholism with LSD,
coined the term "psychedelic" in a famous exchange of letters with
Aldous Huxley (who provided the alternative term "phanerothyme").
