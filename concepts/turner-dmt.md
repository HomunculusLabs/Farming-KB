## DMT - Candy for the Mind

> Intensity: 9 to 10

## Safety Factors

Since DMT takes effect almost immediately, users normally lie down or sit in a reclining position prior to taking a hit. One will neither be able to stand up, nor recognize their surroundings if they get a good hit.

## Material

N,N-Dimethyltryptamine and 5-Methoxy-Dimethyltryptamine are naturally occurring psychedelics found in a variety of plants [[5-meo-dmt-and-bufo-alvarius]]** (some people dry and smoke the venom)

N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT are also manufactured synthetically and sold on the underground market. Synthetic DMT is used much more often than DMT from natural sources.

## History

DMT is not active if taken orally - it must be smoked or snorted for psychoactive effects. To obtain a full-strength experience, the entire dose must be consumed in a short period.

### Snuff Sources
Two sources - **[[virola-theiodora]]** - are made into snuffs known as **Yopo** and **Epena**. Amazon natives use ingenious methods including paired snorting devices where one person blows the DMT snuff into the other's nose.

### Ayahuasca
Elsewhere in the Amazon, DMT-containing plants like **Psychotria veridis** are included in brews known as **Ayahuasca** or **Yage**, combined with **[[turner-harmala-alkaloids-preparation-and-dosage]]). The [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]]) intensify the [[mckenna-dmt-experience-and-hyperspace]] and allow it to be active orally. The healers claim that plant spirits tell them which plants to use in their brews.

## Dosage and Administration

| Compound | Dosage |
|----------|--------|
| **5-MeO-DMT** | 5 to 10 mg |
| **N,N-DMT** | ~40 mg |

Smaller amounts produce perceptual changes but won't reach the unique dimensions. The entire dose must be consumed within about 60 seconds - smoking more after that won't get one much higher.

### Smoking Technique
- Load into a glass pipe with fine mesh stainless steel screen
- Keep flame about 1/2" away from the DMT (never touch it - burning destroys some)
- Toking very slowly, draw in enough heat to melt then vaporize
- The slower the vaporization, the more one gets out of it
- Place a small amount of ash in the bowl bottom to hold melted DMT

The smoke has a harsh, plastic-like taste. It doesn't expand like cannabis and cools quickly.

## The High

### Onset and Duration
- Comes on within about **30 seconds**
- Within the next 30 seconds, propelled to intensity equal to the peak of a **1000+ mcg acid trip**
- Generally completely out of body and unaware of physical surroundings
- Intense part lasts only **2 to 5 minutes** (seeming timeless while in it)
- Back to baseline: **20 minutes** on 5-MeO, **30 minutes** on N,N-DMT
- Called "The businessman's lunch trip" in the Sixties

### Unpredictability
Experiences range from heaven to hell, cyberspace to jeweled palaces, fear or personified evil, visions of jungle animals, contacts with extraterrestrials, links with ancient spirits, or adventures with fairies and elves. On a full dose, one cannot guide the trip with the mind - only by the "intent of the soul" or will.

### Entity Contact
Many users have felt possessed by various spirits, as if becoming a medium and channeling alien thoughts. This has been generally positive for Turner and his circle, but others have experienced the opposite extreme.

### Physical Sensations
- Perception of time slows to nearly a complete stop
- May feel that one is not breathing or that the heart has stopped (perceptual, not actual)
- Awareness of millions of vibrations and sounds through the universe and body
- Physically manifesting energies (gentler on N,N-DMT than 5-MeO-DMT)
- Changes in facial expression that can appear bizarre to onlookers
- Potential for shamanic dance

### The "Weirder" Factor
DMT gets progressively "weirder" as you keep smoking it. Very few people use it frequently after a short period of initial use - either they have an extremely frightening/intense experience, or the progressive weirdness intimidates them.

## N,N-DMT vs 5-MeO-DMT Comparison

### 5-MeO-DMT: "Sheer Force"
- **More potent** in terms of dose size, onset speed, and force
- Can literally feel the mind exploding and expanding outward: area → planet → cosmos, over ~15 seconds
- Visuals: intensely complex, multidimensional, quickly-moving **geometric designs**, primarily **black and white checkerboard** style
- Increases pulse rate and blood pressure more than N,N-DMT
