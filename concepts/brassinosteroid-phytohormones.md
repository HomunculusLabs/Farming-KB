## Mode of Action

The molecular mechanism involves binding to a plasma membrane
receptor kinase called BRI1 (Brassinosteroid Insensitive 1).
Upon binding, BRI1 forms a complex with co-receptor BAK1,
initiating a phosphorylation cascade including the GSK3-like
kinase BIN2. In the absence of brassinosteroid, BIN2
phosphorylates and inactivates transcription factors BES1 and
BZR1.

When brassinosteroid levels are sufficient, BIN2 is inactivated,
allowing dephosphorylated BES1 and BZR1 to accumulate in the
nucleus and regulate gene expression. These transcription factors
influence hundreds of genes involved in cell growth, division,
and stress responses. The signaling pathway exhibits extensive
crosstalk with auxin and gibberellin pathways. BZR1 directly
regulates expression of auxin transporters (PIN proteins),
