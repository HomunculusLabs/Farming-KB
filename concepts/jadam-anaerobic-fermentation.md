# JADAM Anaerobic Fermentation

## Overview

Anaerobic fermentation (cutting off oxygen) is the cornerstone principle of JADAM's input-making philosophy. Youngsang Cho argues that the modern [[cho-natural-farming-inputs-overview]] are food prepared for crops. The most important question is: how rich is the input in nutrients, and in what balance?

From nutritional science, the three biggest destroyers of nutrients are:
1. Sunlight
2. Air
3. Heat

All cooking processes involve blocking off light and air and using minimal heat. There is no cooking method in the world that deliberately inputs air. Yet modern organic farming routinely uses air pumps when making inputs.

## Why Aerobic Fermentation Is Harmful

Aerobic fermentation destroys vitamins and amino acids. When companies put air into their fermentation processes, they are destroying the very nutrients the inputs are supposed to provide. If crops are fed with food deprived of vitamins and amino acids, the plants will fall to diseases.

Despite this, aerobic fermentation became the standard because:
- Sir Albert Howard's "An Agricultural Testament" over-complicated compost-making
- The focus was on eliminating odor rather than preserving nutrition
- Machinery manufacturers promoted conditions requiring their products
- "Experts" declared conditions that necessitated buying equipment

## The Case for Anaerobic Fermentation

Anaerobic fermentation is natural and universal:
- The entire human digestive system breaks down food anaerobically
- Korean kimchi, alcohol, and yogurt are all created anaerobically
- Photosynthetic bacteria, lactobacillus, and yeast -- all considered beneficial microbes -- are anaerobic
- Leaf mold in mountains is anaerobic below the surface
- For thousands of years, farming used anaerobic processes with just a jar or container

## The Gunnysack Fermentation Method

JADAM's practical approach for solid fertilizer:
1. Place a pallet on the ground
2. Fill gunnysacks with wild grass, leaves, and sawdust as main ingredients
3. Add food waste, livestock manure, fish waste, etc.
4. Pile gunnysacks in about six layers on the pallet
5. Apply generous [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer]] -- the [[jadam-rejection-of-sugar-molasses]] and molasses -- why JADAM rejects common fermentation additives
