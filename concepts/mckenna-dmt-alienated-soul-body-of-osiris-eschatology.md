## The Hellenistic-Christian-Judaic Tradition and Ignorance

McKenna identified what he called the "Hellenistic-Christian-Judaic tradition" as the force of "ignorance" that had systematically suppressed the psychedelic dimension of human experience.

This ignorance was not mere lack of knowledge but an active epistemological narrowing—a refusal to acknowledge forms of knowing that fell outside the sanctioned instrumentalities of Western rationalism.

This tradition had burned the libraries of the Hellenistic world and dispersed the ancient knowledge, shattering the stellar and astronomical machinery that had been the work of centuries.

The inheritors of this tradition built a triumph of mechanism.

They realized the alchemical dreams of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries—and the [[christus-eros-osiris-mckenna-mythological-framework-suppression-psychedelic-knowledge]].

Dick discussed "the long hibernation of the Logos"—a creature of pure information that was buried in the ground at Nag Hammadi along with the burying of the Chenoboskion Library circa 370 A.D.

As static information, it existed there until 1947, when the Nag Hammadi codices—Gnostic gospels including the Gospel of Thomas—were discovered and translated.

As soon as people had the information in their minds, the symbiote came alive, for, like the [[mckenna-stropharia-cubensis-extraterrestrial-hypothesis-spore-dispersal]].

Having described the [[stropharia-cubensis]] mushroom as potentially extraterrestrial in origin, he proposed an alternative reading that was in some ways more radical:

The human soul is so alienated from us in our present culture that we treat it as an extraterrestrial.

To us, the most alien thing in the cosmos is the human soul.

Aliens Hollywood-style could arrive on earth tomorrow and the DMT trance would remain more weird and continue to hold more promise for useful information for the human future.

It is that intense.

This interpretation reframes the entire extraterrestrial encounter narrative of the DMT experience.

The "alien intelligence" encountered in hyperspace is not a being from another planet but the human soul itself, rendered unrecognizable by centuries of cultural alienation.

The Self must disguise itself as an extraterrestrial in order not to alarm us with the truly bizarre dimensions that it encompasses.

When we can love the alien, then we will have begun to heal the psychic discontinuity that has plagued us since at least the sixteenth century, possibly earlier.

## History as the Shockwave of Eschatology

McKenna's eschatological framework linked these mythological reflections to a broader philosophy of history.

History, he argued, is the shockwave of eschatology—something is at the end of time and is casting an enormous shadow over human history, drawing all human becoming toward it.

All the wars, the philosophies, the migrations, the cities, the civilizations—all of this is occupying a microsecond of geological, planetary, and galactic time as the monkeys react to the symbiote.

The symbiote is in the environment and is feeding information to humanity about the larger picture.

This is not an attribution of all human accomplishment to knowledge given as a gift from friendly aliens.
