## Trees in Permaculture Design

Trees are the cornerstone species of the ecology. While the
micro-organisms within the soil provide the [[williams-pdc-soils]], healthy soil is the ecological engine which
creates the biosphere.

One of the main ways healthy soil creates the biosphere is by creating
more trees, which in turn create more healthy soil, on and on in a
self-reinforcing cycle. By growing close together the healthy
microclimates of trees overlap, interact, and resonate, creating an even
healthier microclimate that eventually gives rise to forests. Trees are
one of the greatest examples of stacking of functions in permaculture
design, performing all of these ecological services simultaneously.

## Coniferous and Deciduous Trees

There are basically two types of trees: coniferous and deciduous.
Coniferous trees are pine trees, also called evergreens. They stay
green all [[passive-solar-design]], providing summer shade while allowing winter sunlight to
reach structures.

## Food Production from Trees

In addition to microclimate effects, trees provide food in the form of
fruits, nuts, edible leaves, flowers, and even bark. When looking for
trees that provide foodstuffs it is important to open the mind to
greater possibilities than the usual apples, pears, and oranges. The
full range of edible tree products is far broader than most people
realize. Food forests, discussed in

## Buildings Like Trees

Architect William McDonough coined the concept of buildings like trees,
not in the sense of form with trunks and leafy canopies, but in terms
of function. What if buildings were powered by the Sun, created healthy
microclimates, created healthy soil, cleaned water, improved air
quality, produced food, and provided habitat for diverse species? This
concept aligns with [[williams-pdc-ecological-design-principles]] principles where ecological
impact and function guide the style and aesthetic of the design. To
make a beautiful permaculture design, one need only design it to its
maximum ecological potential, pay attention to the quality of
craftsmanship, and the beauty will present itself.
