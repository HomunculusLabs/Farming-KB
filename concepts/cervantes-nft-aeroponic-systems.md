### NFT Advantages and Risks

NFT offers very high performance but practically no buffering
ability. In the absence of growing medium, roots must be kept
perfectly moist by the nutrient solution at all times. If a pump
fails, roots dry and die. If the system dries out for a day or
longer, small feeder roots die with grave consequences. Cervantes
recommends that only growers with several years of experience try an
NFT system if working alone. A filter is often necessary to prevent
debris from blocking gullies and the pump.
