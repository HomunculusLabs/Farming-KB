### Key Four: Role of Herding Animals and Predators

The fourth key concerns the vital role that large herding animals and their
predators play in maintaining the health of brittle environments. The removal
of predators disrupts herd behavior, causing animals to become localized and
calm rather than constantly moving. This change in behavior eliminates the
periodic disturbance and compaction that brittle environment soils require,
leading to soil exposure, capped surfaces, and eventual desertification.
