# Psilocybin Crystalline Product from Catalytic Hydrogenolysis

The final step in the Shirota, Hakamata, and Goda (2003) synthesis of
[[psilocybin]] converts the zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl [[hmbc-noesy-nmr-analysis-zwitterionic-phosphate-intermediate-psilocybin]]
to crystalline [[psilocybin]] via catalytic hydrogenolysis over palladium on
carbon. This transformation removes both benzyl protecting groups
simultaneously, yielding [[psilocybin]] as a white needle crystalline powder
without any chromatographic purification.

## The Zwitterionic Starting Material

The hydrogenolysis substrate is the zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate
derivative (compound 8 in the synthesis scheme). This intermediate was
isolated in 85.2 percent yield as a white powder by filtration after
suspension of the crude reaction mixture in dichloromethane overnight.

The zwitterionic nature of this compound, with one benzyl group attached to
the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethylamino group (forming a quaternary ammonium
ion) and the other benzyl group on the phosphate oxygen, makes it insoluble in
dichloromethane and allows simple filtration isolation.

The zwitterionic structure was fully characterized by HMBC and NOESY NMR
experiments, which confirmed that one benzyl group had migrated from the
phosphate oxygen to the nitrogen during the workup. This unexpected
intramolecular rearrangement converted the originally formed O,O-dibenzyl
phosphate into an N,O-dibenzyl phosphate zwitterion.

The rearrangement proved advantageous because it enabled isolation of the
intermediate by simple filtration rather than chromatography.

The intermediate weighs 10.5 g from 5.4 g of [[psilocin]] starting material
(22.5 mmol scale), demonstrating efficient conversion even at gram scale.

## Hydrogenolysis Conditions

The debenzylation is carried out under the following conditions:

1. The [[psilocybin-phosphorylation-via-zwitterionic-intermediate]] (10.5 g, 22.5 mmol) is dissolved in methanol (225 mL, giving a 0.1 M solution)
2. Ten percent palladium-activated carbon (approximately 1 g, roughly 10 percent by weight of substrate) is added under an argon atmosphere
3. The atmosphere is purged and switched to hydrogen, and the suspension is stirred at room temperature
4. After two hours of hydrogenation, water (approximately 50 mL) is added because product deposition was observed
5. Stirring continues for one additional hour under hydrogen to ensure complete debenzylation

The addition of water after two hours is a critical practical detail. As
[[psilocybin]] forms during the reaction, it begins to deposit from the
methanol solution because [[psilocybin]] has limited solubility in pure
methanol at this concentration.

Adding water increases the polarity of the solvent mixture, which helps keep
the product in solution longer and ensures complete debenzylation of any
remaining intermediate. Without this water addition, the deposited
[[psilocybin]] could coat the catalyst surface and prematurely terminate the
hydrogenation before all benzyl groups are cleaved.

## Catalyst Selection and Mechanism

Palladium on carbon (Pd/C) is the standard catalyst for hydrogenolytic
cleavage of benzyl ethers and benzyl amines. The mechanism involves:

- Adsorption of the benzyl-protected substrate onto the palladium surface
- [[oxidative-addition-organometallic-chemistry]] of hydrogen to the palladium substrate complex
- Reductive elimination of toluene and the deprotected product

The reaction simultaneously cleaves both the O-benzyl group on the phosphate
and the N-benzyl group on the quaternary ammonium, regenerating the free
N,N-dimethylamino group and the [[cho-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid-preparation]] moiety.

The use of 10 percent Pd/C rather than 5 percent provides faster reaction
rates, which is important for minimizing the time that the sensitive
[[tryptamine]] product spends in solution. Room temperature conditions avoid
thermal degradation that could occur at elevated temperatures.

The methanol solvent serves dual purposes: it dissolves the zwitterionic
intermediate adequately, and it is a good solvent for hydrogen gas, ensuring
efficient transfer of hydrogen to the palladium surface.

## Workup and Crystallization

After the hydrogenation is complete, the reaction mixture is filtered through
a Celite pad by suction to remove the palladium catalyst. Celite (diatomaceous
earth) provides a fine filtration medium that traps the carbon-supported
catalyst particles while allowing the product solution to pass through.

The filtrate volume is reduced by evaporation, which concentrates the solution
and promotes crystallization. The [[psilocybin]] product forms crystalline
material directly upon concentration, and is collected by filtration and
washed with ethanol to afford the final product.

This workup is notably simple compared to the anion-exchange resin
purification method previously used by Nichols and Frescas (1999). Their
O,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative did not form a convenient zwitterionic
intermediate, so the deprotected [[psilocybin]] required ion-exchange
chromatography.

The zwitterionic intermediate route eliminates the ion-exchange requirement
entirely.

## Yield and Scale

The isolated yield of [[psilocybin]] from the zwitterionic intermediate is
87.5 percent (5.6 g from 10.5 g of intermediate). When calculated from the
starting [[psilocin]], this represents an overall yield greater than 72
percent for the two-step phosphorylation and hydrogenolysis sequence.

This yield is described as quite gratifying when compared to previously
reported yields:

- 20 percent (Hofmann, 1958; Troxler, 1959)
- 47 percent (Nichols and Frescas, 1999)
- 72 percent (Shirota, Hakamata, and Goda, 2003)

The synthesis was demonstrated at gram scale (5.6 g of [[psilocybin]]
produced), confirming its practical utility for producing reference standards.
The 87.5 percent isolated yield at this scale demonstrates that the procedure
does not suffer from the yield losses typically encountered when scaling up
heterogeneous catalytic reactions.

## Physical Properties of the Crystalline Product

The [[psilocybin]] obtained is a white needle crystalline powder with the
following characteristics:

**Melting point:** 190 to 198 degrees Celsius. Literature values are 185 to 195 degrees Celsius and 210 to 212 degrees Celsius, indicating some polymorphism or batch variation in the crystalline form. The broad melting range of 8 degrees is typical for crystalline [[chamovitz-plant-scent-communication-volatile-organic-compounds]].

**Crystal habit:** White needles, which is the characteristic crystalline morphology of [[psilocybin]] from methanol-based crystallization. The needle shape suggests preferential growth along one crystal axis, commonly observed for polar molecules that form hydrogen-bonded chains in the solid state.

**Solubility:** The product deposits from methanol during hydrogenolysis, and is washed with ethanol, indicating moderate solubility in both polar protic solvents. The need for water addition during the reaction confirms limited solubility in methanol alone at the 0.1 M reaction concentration.

## Spectroscopic Confirmation

The crystalline product was confirmed as [[psilocybin]] by multiple
spectroscopic techniques:

**UV spectrum (MeOH):** Lambda max at 221.0 (log epsilon 4.44), 267.5 (3.66), 278.5 (3.57), and 290.0 nm (3.42). The UV profile is similar to that of [[psilocin]] but slightly shifted due to the electron-withdrawing effect of the phosphate group on the [[indole]] chromophore.

**IR spectrum (KBr):** Key absorptions at 3266 cm-1 (O-H and P-OH stretching), 1620, 1580, 1505, 1439 cm-1 (aromatic and phosphate vibrations), 1352, 1298, 1244 cm-1 (P=O and P-O-C stretching), and 1154, 1103, 1061 cm-1 (phosphate group modes).

**Proton NMR (D2O, 400 MHz):** Aromatic pattern of the 4-hydroxyindole ring system plus side chain signals at 3.44 ppm (2H, t, J = 7.2 Hz) and 3.28 ppm (2H, t, J = 7.2 Hz), and N,N-dimethyl singlet at 2.86 ppm (6H, s).

**Phosphorus-31 NMR (CD3OD, 162 MHz):** A single signal at minus 4.48 ppm confirming the OPO3H2 phosphate moiety. The absence of additional phosphorus signals confirms complete debenzylation.
