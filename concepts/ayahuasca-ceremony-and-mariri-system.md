# Ayahuasca Ceremony and the Mariri System

## Overview

The ayahuasca ceremony, as described in [[powell-shamanic-wisdom-and-ritual-practice]] of Amazonian vegetalismo (plant-based shamanism). The ceremony involves the ingestion of the ayahuasca brew under the guidance of a vegetalista (plant healer), accompanied by the singing of icaros (sacred melodies), and incorporates a complex system of magical substances called mariris. These ceremonies serve both therapeutic and initiatory purposes, connecting participants with an extensive spirit world populated by serpents, nymphs, extraterrestrial beings, and elemental forces.
