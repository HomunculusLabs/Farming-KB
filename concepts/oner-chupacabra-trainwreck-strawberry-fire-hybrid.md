## Chupacabra — Trainwreck × Strawberry Fire Hybrid Strain Profile

## Overview

Chupacabra is a [[mckenna-cannabis-culture-history]], naming a strain after this creature suggests:

- **Potency**: Something that "hits hard" and can overwhelm the unprepared
- **Uniqueness**: A distinctive, memorable experience unlike more common strains
- **Mystery**: The exact effects and character of the strain are not immediately predictable from its parent genetics alone

The choice of name reflects the breeder's sense of humor and the strain's tendency to deliver unexpectedly powerful effects that catch users off guard.

## Parent Strain Analysis

### Trainwreck (Mother)

Trainwreck is one of the foundational American [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] — plants can double or triple in height during flowering stretch
- **Effect profile**: Powerful, fast-hitting cerebral high with a euphoric onset that transitions to relaxed body effects. True to its name, the high can "derail" you if you are unprepared

Trainwreck has been a staple of the Northern California cannabis scene since the 1980s and remains popular for its reliability, potency, and distinctive character. It has served as a parent for numerous crosses, lending its vigor and potency to offspring.

### Strawberry Fire (Father)

Strawberry Fire is a lesser-documented strain that contributes aromatic and flavor characteristics to the Chupacabra cross:

- **Character**: Fruity, berry-forward aroma with notes of strawberry and sweet earth
- **Effect**: Balanced hybrid effects with a moderate body stone complementing its fruity, uplifting character
- **Contribution to Chupacabra**: Primarily aromatic complexity and a sweeter flavor profile that tempers Trainwreck's harsh, spicy edge
- **Growth traits**: Adds [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]]

### Flowering

- **Flowering time**: Approximately 8-9 weeks
- **Stretch**: Expect 1.5-2x stretch in the first 2-3 weeks of flowering
- **Bud structure**: Dense, resinous buds with good calyx-to-leaf ratio. The Strawberry Fire parent contributes tighter bud formation compared to pure Trainwreck
- **Trichome production**: Heavy — both parents contribute to excellent resin coverage
- **Aroma during flowering**: Intense and complex. The strawberry notes from Strawberry Fire emerge in mid-flowering, layered over Trainwreck's characteristic spicy-pine base
- **Support requirements**: Moderate. Buds become heavy but branch structure from both parents provides adequate support in most cases

### Yield

- **Indoor**: 350-450g/m² — solid yields that reward proper training and canopy management
- **Outdoor**: Performs well in Mediterranean and warm [[fukuoka-natural-farming-temperate-climates]]
- **Yield quality**: Dense, resinous buds with excellent bag appeal. The combination of potency and aroma makes this a premium-quality harvest

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

### Aroma
Chupacabra's aroma is one of its most distinctive characteristics, representing a successful fusion of both parents:

- **Primary notes**: Fresh strawberries and sweet berries from the Strawberry Fire lineage
- **Secondary notes**: Spicy pine and earthy undertones from Trainwreck
- **Tertiary notes**: Subtle fuel-like hints that emerge when buds are broken apart
- **Overall impression**: A sweet, fruity aroma with enough complexity and depth to distinguish it from simple "strawberry" strains

### Flavor
The flavor follows the aroma closely:

- **Inhale**: Sweet strawberry and berry notes dominate, with a pleasant sweetness
- **Mid-palate**: The Trainwreck influence emerges — spicy, piney, and slightly peppery
- **Exhale**: Earthy, smooth finish with lingering berry sweetness
