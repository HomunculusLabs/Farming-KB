## Double-Layer Climate Modification and Winter Temperature Data

## Overview

The technical foundation of Eliot Coleman's winter harvest system is the climate
modification achieved by combining two layers of protection — an outer layer of
[[coleman-four-season-farm]] are 30 feet wide and
96 feet long. Key characteristics:

- Single-layer UV-resistant polyethylene covering.
- Gothic arch design sheds snow effectively.
- No heating system, water ballast, buried insulation, or complicated
  technology — completely passive.
- The greenhouse itself provides the first level of climate modification by
  blocking wind and trapping radiant heat from the soil.

## The Inner Layer: Floating Row Cover

Inside the greenhouse, a second layer of protection is created by laying
floating row cover material directly over the crops:

- Supported by flat-topped wire wickets approximately 12 inches (30 cm) above
  the soil surface.
- Wickets spaced every 4 feet (120 cm) along 30-inch-wide (75 cm) growing
  beds.
- Any lightweight, breathable row cover material is suitable — it must allow
  light, air, and moisture to pass through.

## Measured Temperature Performance

Coleman provides specific temperature data from Four Season Farm that
demonstrates the effectiveness of the double-layer system:

### Outdoor to Inner Layer Temperature Differential

When the outdoor temperature drops to -15°F (-26°C), the temperature under the
inner layer of the cold house drops only to 15°F to 18°F above zero (-10°C to
-8°C) on average. This represents a temperature gain of approximately 25-30°F
(14-17°C) from the double-layer system alone.
