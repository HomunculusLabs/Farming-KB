## Biogeographic Patterns

The Canadian distribution reveals several clear patterns:
1. **Pacific coast concentration**: British Columbia hosts the highest diversity, with over 15 documented neurotropic species
2. **Psilocybe semilanceata ubiquity**: The liberty cap is the most widely distributed neurotropic species, recorded in six provinces
3. **Amanita muscaria as the northernmost species**: Found across all provinces and territories, reflecting its association with boreal and subarctic birch/pine forests
4. **Eastern scarcity**: Atlantic provinces have very limited documented diversity, with only P. semilanceata and A. muscaria recorded
5. **Psilocybe quebecensis endemicity**: This species is named for Quebec and appears largely restricted to eastern Canada
