# 2C-E (4-Ethyl-2,5-Dimethoxyphenethylamine)

## Chemical Identity

2C-E is the common name for 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylphenethylamine hydrochloride, compound #24 in Alexander and [[ann-shulgin]]'s *PiHKAL* (Phenethylamines I Have Known And Loved). It belongs to the 2C family of phenethylamines, characterized by a 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine backbone with a substituent at the 4-position — in this case, an ethyl group.

- **Chemical formula**: C₁₂H₂₀ClNO₂ (hydrochloride salt)
- **Molecular weight**: 245.74 g/mol (HCl salt)
- **Appearance**: Magnificent white crystals (hydrochloride)
- **Melting point**: Not explicitly stated in PiHKAL
- **CAS**: Not listed in PiHKAL

2C-E occupies a notable position in the 2C series. The ethyl group at the 4-position places it between the shorter methyl of 2C-D and longer propyl of 2C-P in terms of lipophilicity and duration. The amphetamine homologue of 2C-E is DOET (4-ethyl-2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine), which is considerably more potent and longer-acting.

## Synthesis Overview

The synthesis of 2C-E as described in PiHKAL proceeds through several well-defined steps:

1. **Acylation**: p-Dimethoxybenzene is reacted with acetyl chloride in the presence of aluminum chloride (Friedel-Crafts acylation) to produce 2-hydroxy-5-methoxyacetophenone and 2,5-dimethoxyacetophenone.

2. **Wolff-Kishner reduction**: The 2,5-dimethoxyacetophenone undergoes Wolff-Kishner reduction using hydrazine and KOH in triethylene glycol to yield 2,5-dimethoxy-1-ethylbenzene. An alternative Birch-type reduction pathway through a nitrostyrene is also described.

3. **Vilsmeier-Haack formylation**: The ethylbenzene intermediate is formylated using dichloromethyl methyl ether and stannic chloride to produce 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylbenzaldehyde (mp 47-48°C after purification through the bisulfite complex).

4. **Nitrostyrene formation**: The aldehyde is condensed with nitromethane in the presence of ammonium acetate to give 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethyl-beta-nitrostyrene (mp 99-100°C).

5. **Reduction to amine**: The nitrostyrene is reduced with lithium aluminum hydride (LAH) in THF, either with or without [[fukuoka-textdoc-fertilizer-soil-destruction-sulfuric-acid]] catalysis, to yield 2C-E hydrochloride as lustrous white crystals.

Yields of the final step range from approximately 3.9 g (with H₂SO₄ catalysis) to 7.7 g (without) from the corresponding nitrostyrene quantities.

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Threshold** | ~5-8 mg |
| **Light dose** | 10-15 mg |
| **Common dose** | 15-20 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 20-25 mg |
| **Duration** | 8-12 hours |
| **Onset** | 30-90 minutes |

The dosage range of 10-25 mg is notably broad, and Shulgin deliberately kept it so because of the compound's steep dose-response curve. At 10 mg, rich +++ (strong psychedelic) experiences have been reported, while a 30 mg trial was described as "very frightening."

## Qualitative Effects

### At 10-16 mg (Moderate Range)

Effects at this level are described as genuinely enjoyable aesthetic enhancement. One subject at 16 mg reported:

- The garden appeared "painted on the window," with every petal and leaf "carefully sculptured in fine strokes of oil paint"
- A woman watering plants appeared "frozen, immobilized by Vermeer"
- The visual field was transformed into an "eternal museum" experience

### At 20-25 mg (Strong Range)

At higher doses, 2C-E becomes more intense and potentially challenging:

- At 20 mg: Vivid, dynamic visual experiences — a German scene with a man on horseback became animated with wind blowing, cape flapping, and perpetual motion that "never ceased"
- At 25 mg (positive): A profoundly aesthetic experience with rich hallucinatory content
- At 25 mg (negative): Acute anxiety, sweating, and what the subject described as "toxic psychosis" with voices, racing fears, and chaotic internal dialogue that passed after 20 minutes. The same subject noted that "at lower dosages 2C-E has been a truly enjoyable aesthetic enhancer"

### Character Profile

2C-E produces primarily visual and aesthetic effects rather than empathogenic or insight-oriented ones. The visuals are described as rich, detailed, and painterly — more like a museum or gallery experience than the flowing, organic visuals of LSD or the warm, earth-toned visuals of mescaline. Emotional effects are present but secondary to the visual-aesthetic experience.

## Pharmacological Considerations

### Dose-Response Curve

The most notable pharmacological feature of 2C-E is its steep dose-response curve. The difference between a "truly enjoyable" experience at 15 mg and a "toxic psychosis" at 25 mg is surprisingly narrow for a phenethylamine. This steepness demands careful dose measurement and conservative titration.

### Comparison to DOET

The amphetamine homologue DOET (4-ethyl-2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine) is both longer-acting and considerably more potent than 2C-E. The addition of the alpha-methyl group in DOET extends duration significantly, as is typical when comparing phenethylamines to their amphetamine counterparts. This SAR (structure-activity relationship) pattern is consistent across the 2C/DOx families.

### Receptor Activity

While formal [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]] data is not presented in PiHKAL, the pharmacological profile (visual-dominant effects, 8-12 hour duration, steep dose-response) is consistent with 5-HT₂A receptor agonism, the primary mechanism of action for classical psychedelics. The ethyl group's moderate lipophilicity likely contributes to both the duration and the blood-brain barrier penetration.

## Extension: 2CE-5ETO ("Eternity")

One homologue (Tweetio) of 2C-E has been synthesized and evaluated. 2CE-5ETO (5-ethoxy-4-ethyl-2-methoxyphenethylamine) is the 5-EtO variant where one methoxy group has been replaced with an ethoxy group:

- **Nitrostyrene mp**: 110-110.5°C
- **Hydrochloride mp**: 184-185°C
- **Effective dose**: 10-15 mg (comparable to parent 2C-E)
- **Duration**: 16+ hours (2-3 times longer than 2C-E)
- **Onset**: 3-4 hours to reach plateau
- **Character**: "Gentle, forgiving, and extremely long-lived"

The extreme duration earned it the nickname "Eternity" from one subject. Experiments were sometimes interrupted with Valium or Halcion at the 16-hour mark, with residual effects still evident the next day after sleep.

## Safety Considerations

- The steep dose-response curve makes accidental overdose a real risk with imprecise measurement
- At higher doses, 2C-E can produce acute anxiety, sympathetic activation (sweating, racing heart), and transient psychotic-like symptoms
- The 8-12 hour duration means that uncomfortable effects persist for a significant period
- As with all [[optical-isomers-psychedelic-phenethylamines]], set, setting, and mental state are critical determinants of experience quality
- Shulgin's safety principle of self-experimentation applies: know the substance, start low, and never exceed tested ranges
- The long-acting homologue 2CE-5ETO carries additional risks due to its extreme duration, requiring multiple days of recovery time

## Historical Significance

2C-E is described by Shulgin as "one of the magical half-dozen" phenethylamines — placing it alongside mescaline, 2C-B, and 2C-T-2 in the highest tier of the 2C family. Despite this ranking, Shulgin noted that several people said "I don't think I like it, since it isn't that much fun. But I intend to explore it again," suggesting that 2C-E rewards patient, intentional exploration rather than casual recreational use. The full character of 2C-E, Shulgin wrote, "will be understood" in the future — but for the moment it stands as "a difficult and worthwhile material. A very much worthwhile material."

## Legal Status

2C-E is scheduled as a controlled substance in many jurisdictions. In the [[dmt-containing-plants-united-states-halpern]], it falls under the Federal Analog Act as an analogue of 2C-B (Schedule I). It is also controlled in the UK (Class A), Canada (Schedule III), Australia, and numerous other countries. The legal status varies internationally and should be verified for current regulations.
