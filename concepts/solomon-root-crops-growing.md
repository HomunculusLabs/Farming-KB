## Potatoes (Solanum tuberosum)

The single most important [[fukuoka-natural-farming-temperate-climates]]. Solomon devotes more space to potatoes than any other vegetable.

### Seed Preparation (Chitting)

About 6 weeks before planting, spread uncut seed potatoes on a tray in bright indirect light (not direct sun). By planting time they will be light green with sprouts emerging. Cut larger potatoes into chunks, each containing at least two sprouting eyes and weighing at least 2 ounces. Do not save your own seed -- virus diseases accumulate and reduce yields. Buy certified disease-free seed.

### Planting

- Plant late enough to emerge after last frost (frost burns vines but regrowth follows)
- Prepare rows: spread compost/manure in 1-foot bands, deeply dig the rows
- For highest yield: excavate trenches, fill with compost/manure/COF, cover with soil
- Set seed pieces 4 inches deep, 8-12 inches apart in rows 36 inches apart
- Wider spacing: larger tubers, slightly lower yield, better [[holmgren-mineral-balance-soil-fertility]] affects potato [[blesching-cannabis-fever-temperature-regulation]]
- Potatoes last for months; rub off sprouts to extend storage

### Pests and Diseases

- Colorado potato beetle: see [[query-what-is-biofumigation-and-how-does-it-work]] page
- Scab: some advise against liming potatoes; Solomon found no difference
- Rotate potatoes at least 3 years away from any solanum crop (tomatoes, peppers, eggplant)

### Varieties

Use late-maturing varieties for main storage crop. Early varieties grow for less time, make fewer leaves, and yield less. Lates yield more. In long-season climates, delay planting main crop to ensure vines die back just before frost -- reduces premature sprouting in storage.

## Sweet Potatoes (Ipomoea batatas)

Require light, well-drained soil. Need 3-4 months of warm growing weather (90+ days). Require soil temperatures above 60F to grow well.

### Growing

- Buy virus-free shoots or grow your own from certified seed stock
- Form raised beds 4 feet apart, 6-8 inches above paths
- Set shoots 15 inches apart on ridges, planted nearly horizontally 2 inches deep
- Keep soil moist but never soaked; encourages root development
- Hill gradually as vines grow (reduces insect problems per Louisiana extension)
- Vines should cover between-row spaces within 2 months
- Do not repeat on same beds without 3-4 year break

### Harvest and Curing

- Monitor closely near expected maturity; size up rapidly in last few weeks
- Dig promptly when ready; avoid skinning damage
- Light irrigation before digging helps if soil is dry
