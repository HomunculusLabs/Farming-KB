## Discovery of the Zwitterionic Intermediate

When the phosphorylation reaction was run and the usual aqueous workup was performed, the 1H NMR spectrum of the remaining substance showed complicated signals. TLC revealed an additional spot at the origin — a whitish material that no longer dissolved in dichloromethane. This observation had been reported by Nichols and Frescas, who concluded that hydrolytic cleavage of one O-benzyl group had occurred.

Purification by preparative reversed-phase HPLC afforded a single compound. Analysis by NMR (1H, 13C, 31P) and 2D techniques (HMBC, NOESY) revealed the structure: one benzyl group had migrated from the phosphate oxygen to the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl group, forming a **quaternary ammonium ion** paired with a negatively charged phosphate group — a zwitterion.

### Structural Evidence

- **HMBC correlations** showed one benzyl group directly linked at the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl part
- **NOESY spectrum** supported these linkages
- **31P NMR** confirmed the presence of the phosphate moiety
- **Chemical shift differences**: The methylene proton of the N-benzyl group shifted to high field (δH 4.56) compared to the O-benzyl protons (δH 4.98, 4.96), while the psilocin core methylene and N,N-dimethyl protons shifted to low field relative to free psilocin

The zwitterion, named {benzyl[2-(4-oxyindol-3-yl)ethyl]dimethylammonio}-4-O-benzyl phosphate (compound 8), forms readily by suspending the worked-up reaction mixture in CH2Cl2 overnight.

## Practical Significance of the Zwitterion

The zwitterionic nature of compound 8 made large-scale isolation possible by **simple filtration** rather than chromatography:

1. The excess remaining dibenzyl phosphate was removed by washing with CH2Cl2
2. The zwitterionic product precipitated as an insoluble white powder
3. Isolation yield exceeded 85%
4. The material could be used directly in the next step

## Final Deprotection to Psilocybin

[[shirota-catalytic-hydrogenolysis-debenzylation-psilocybin]] of the zwitterionic intermediate using 10% palladium-activated carbon in methanol under a hydrogen atmosphere at room temperature removed both benzyl groups, yielding psilocybin as a white needle crystalline powder. The overall yield from psilocin to psilocybin exceeded 72%, even at gram scale — dramatically better than the previous best of 47%.
