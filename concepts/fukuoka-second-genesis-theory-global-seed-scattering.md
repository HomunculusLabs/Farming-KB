# Second Genesis Theory

## Overview

The **Second Genesis** is Masanobu Fukuoka's most radical and controversial proposal for healing the planet's deserts and degraded landscapes. Rather than attempting to restore ecosystems to some prehuman baseline, Fukuoka advocated broadcasting the seeds of all plant species — trees, vegetables, grains, legumes, grasses, ferns, mosses, and lichens — along with fungi, bacteria, and other soil microorganisms, across vast desert areas simultaneously. By providing nature with a full palette of species and stepping back, nature would establish a new [[comparison-pruning-fruit-trees-vs-grafting-fruit-trees]]**
- **Perennials**, **vegetables**, **grasses**, and **legumes**
- **Ferns**, **mosses**, and **lichens**
- **Fungi**, **bacteria**, and other **soil microorganisms**
- **Black forest soil** as a rich source of microorganisms and their spores
