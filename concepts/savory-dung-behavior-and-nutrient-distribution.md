## Dung and Parasite Management

Animals naturally avoid fouled ground, which has implications for
parasite management. Parasite eggs in dung are concentrated in areas
animals avoid, reducing re-infection rates. At low density where
animals remain on the same ground, this natural avoidance breaks down
and parasite loads increase.
