## Preparation Failures

### IMO Won't Culture

See [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture]] for full IMO collection and culturing instructions.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No growth on rice after 7 days | Too cold (below 15C), wrong site | Move to warmer area (20-35C ideal); collect from deep humus under old tree |
| Black mold on rice | Left too long (>7 days) | Discard, start over — black = spoilage |
| Slimy, putrid rice | Anaerobic (too wet or sealed) | Use drier rice, breathable paper cover, not airtight |
| Only white growth, slow | Low microbe diversity | Collect from multiple sites/forests; mix bamboo + forest IMOs |
| IMO-3 won't heat up | Too dry or too wet | Adjust to 65-70% moisture (squeeze test: balls then crumbles) |
| IMO-3 exceeds 70C | Thermophiles dominating | Turn immediately; nutrients evaporating above 70C |

### FPJ/FFJ Smells Bad

See [[knf-fermented-plant-juice]] and [[knf-fermented-fruit-juice]] for proper preparation methods.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Putrid/rotten smell | Anaerobic fermentation, wrong plant | Discard. Ensure breathable cover first 1-2 days. Avoid cucumber, melon, strawberry (too much sugar) |
| Alcohol smell | Too warm, yeast overgrowth | Move to cooler location (under 25C) |
| Weak/no smell | Not enough fermentation time | Wait longer or move to warmer spot (20-25C) |
| Too much liquid | Plant too watery | Use less sugar ratio next time (1:0.8) |
| White mold on surface | Normal early fermentation | Stir gently. White = ok. Black = discard |

### FAA Smells Rotten

See [[knf-fish-amino-acid]] for the complete FAA production guide.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Strong putrid/rotten | Fat not dissolving, anaerobic | Add IMO-3 (lipase bacteria dissolve fat), more sugar, burp daily |
| Excessive gas/pressure | Active fermentation normal | Burp container daily to prevent explosion |
| Weak nitrogen effect | Too diluted or old | Increase concentration to 1:500; FAA lasts 1-2 years separated from solids |
| Burn on leaves | Too concentrated or wrong stage | Reduce to 1:1000; never apply during flowering |

### OHN Mold

See [[knf-oriental-herbal-nutrient]] for the full OHN recipe and preparation process.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Surface mold on extract | Low alcohol content, contamination | Ensure beer/wine is 5%+ ABV; add more sugar as preservative |
| Weak herbal smell | Insufficient extraction time | Ferment each phase full 7 days |
| Separation in final product | Normal — herbs settling | Shake before use |

### WCA Won't Dissolve

See [[knf-water-soluble-calcium]] for the standard WCA recipe.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Eggshells intact after 10 days | BRV too weak or old | Use fresh, properly fermented BRV (pH <4); crush shells smaller |
| Putrid smell | Egg membrane not removed | Clean shells thoroughly, remove all membrane; start over |
| Cloudy liquid with chunks | Normal — fine particles | Strain through cheesecloth; cloudy is ok |

### LAB Weak / Won't Separate

See [[knf-lactic-acid-bacteria-serum]] for the complete LAB culturing procedure.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No curd separation after 8 days | Ultra-pasteurized milk used | Use raw or regular pasteurized milk (UP milk lacks enzymes) |
| No skin on rice wash | Too cold or chlorinated water | Use non-chlorinated water; keep at 20-25C |
| Foul smell | Bad bacteria dominated | Discard; sanitize jar; ensure anaerobic with airlock for phase 2 |

### WS-CaPo Won't Extract

See [[knf-water-soluble-calcium-phosphate]] for the standard WS-CaPo method.

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Bones intact after 14 days | BRV too weak | Use fresh BRV; char bones first (optional) to increase porosity |
| Very slow bubble formation | Large bone pieces | Smash bones into smaller pieces; increase surface area |

## Plant Symptom Diagnosis

### By Visual Symptom

| Symptom | Likely Cause | KNF Response |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Yellowing lower leaves (veg) | N deficiency or natural senescence | FAA 1:1000 foliar; check if old growth (normal) |
| Yellowing upper/new leaves | Fe, S, or general [[nutrient-lockout]] | [[knf-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]] 1:30 (trace minerals); check pH |
| Leaf curl (upward) | Ca deficiency, heat stress | WS-Ca 1:500 foliar; improve airflow |
| Leaf curl (downward) | Overwatering, P toxicity | Reduce water; check drainage |
| Interveinal chlorosis | Mg or Fe deficiency | [[chlorosis]] treatment: Seawater 1:30; Epsom salt soil drench |
| Purple stems/leaves | P deficiency (cold) | WS-CaPo 1:500; WS-PA 1:30; raise temps |
| Brown spots on leaves | Fungal disease, Ca deficiency | OHN 1:500 + WS-Ca 1:500; improve airflow |
| Soft, sappy growth | Excess N, insufficient Ca | Stop FAA; apply WS-Ca 1:500 |
| Stunted growth | Ca applied during veg, root issues | Stop WS-Ca; apply FPJ 1:500; check soil drainage |
| Blossom end rot | Ca deficiency during fruiting | WS-Ca 1:500 foliar; apply preventively early flower |
| Poor flowering/fruit set | N too high during changeover | Stop all N inputs; apply WS-CaPo 1:500 |

### Environmental Stress

| Stress | Signs | Response |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Heat (>35C) | Wilting, leaf curl, tip burn | Shade cloth; seawater 1:30 (osmotic adjustment); OHN 1:1000 |
| Cold (<10C) | Purple leaves, slow growth | Move indoors; WS-PA 1:30; reduce watering |
| Drought | Wilting, leaf drop | Deep water; mulch; crack prevention formula (OHN+BRV+FPJ+LAB+Seawater) |
| Overwatering | Drooping, yellowing, root rot | Improve drainage; LAB 1:500 soil drench; dry out between waterings |
| Transplant shock | Wilting after moving | OHN 1:500 root dip or drench; shade for 2-3 days; FPJ 1:500 |
