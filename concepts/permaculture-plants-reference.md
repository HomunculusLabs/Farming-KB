# Permaculture Plants Reference

A curated reference of plants commonly used in permaculture design, organized by function. Plants are selected from multiple permaculture sources for their multi-functionality and suitability for homestead-scale systems. For detailed species entries, consult *Permaculture Plants: A Selection* (Clerk and Costa).

## Nitrogen-Fixing Trees and Shrubs

These plants convert atmospheric nitrogen into plant-available form through symbiosis with rhizobium bacteria. They are essential for building soil fertility without external inputs.

| Plant | Type | Height | Zone | Notes |
|-------|------|--------|------|-------|
| Black locust (*Robinia pseudoacacia*) | Tree | 40-80 ft | 4-9 | Fast growing, rot-resistant timber, bee forage (flowers), chicken fodder (pods), fixes 100+ lb N/acre/year |
| Honey locust (*Gleditsia triacanthos*) | Tree | 30-70 ft | 4-9 | Edible pods for livestock, dappled shade, good stock fodder |
| Siberian pea shrub (*Caragana arborescens*) | Shrub | 6-15 ft | 2-7 | Extremely cold hardy, edible peas for chicken feed, windbreak |
| Sea buckthorn (*Hippophae rhamnoides*) | Shrub | 6-20 ft | 3-8 | Edible berries (vitamin C), nitrogen fixer, windbreak, soil stabilizer |
| Autumn olive (*Elaeagnus umbellata*) | Shrub | 8-20 ft | 3-8 | Edible berries, nitrogen fixer, wildlife habitat (can be invasive -- check locally) |
| Goumi (*Elaeagnus multiflora*) | Shrub | 6-10 ft | 4-8 | Edible berries, nitrogen fixer, less invasive than autumn olive |
| Russian olive (*Elaeagnus angustifolia*) | Tree | 15-30 ft | 2-8 | Edible fruit, nitrogen fixer, drought tolerant, windbreak |
| Alder (*Alnus spp.*) | Tree | 30-60 ft | 3-9 | Excellent for wet areas, fast growing, nitrogen fixer |
| Mesquite (*Prosopis spp.*) | Tree | 20-40 ft | 7-11 | Edible pods, nitrogen fixer, drought tolerant, firewood |
| Acacia (*Acacia spp.*) | Tree | 20-50 ft | 9-11 | Fast growing, nitrogen fixer, livestock fodder (non-hardy in most of US) |
| Silver wattle (*Acacia dealbata*) | Tree | 30-60 ft | 8-10 | Fast growing pioneer, windbreak, nitrogen fixer |

## Dynamic Accumulators

Plants with deep taproots that mine minerals from subsoil and bring them to the surface. When cut and used as mulch, these minerals become available to other plants.

| Plant | Accumulates | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| Comfrey (*Symphytum officinale*) | Potassium, calcium, magnesium, iron | THE premier accumulator. Chop-and-drop 4-6x per year. High protein forage. Medicinal. |
| Yarrow (*Achillea millefolium*) | Phosphorus, potassium, copper | Medicinal, attracts beneficial insects, drought tolerant |
| Stinging nettle (*Urtica dioica*) | Iron, nitrogen, potassium, calcium | Excellent compost activator, liquid fertilizer (tea), edible (cooked), butterfly host |
| Dandelion (*Taraxacum officinale*) | Calcium, potassium, iron, copper | Edible (leaves, flowers, roots), deep taproot breaks compaction |
| Chicory (*Cichorium intybus*) | Potassium, calcium, magnesium | Deep taproot, drought tolerant, chicken forage, coffee substitute |
| Burdock (*Arctium lappa*) | Calcium, iron, magnesium | Edible root, medicinal, deep taproot |
| Borage (*Borago officinalis*) | Potassium, calcium | Bee forage, edible flowers, compost accelerator |
| Plantain (*Plantago major*) | Zinc, calcium | Medicinal (wound healing), edible, mineral accumulator |

## Poultry Forage Plants

Plants that produce food for ducks and chickens. See [[companion-planting]] for more details.

**Trees**: Mulberry, black locust (pods), oak (acorns), fruit trees (fallen fruit), honey locust (pods)
**Shrubs**: Elderberry, autumn olive, aronia, currant, gooseberry, Siberian pea shrub
**Herbaceous**: Comfrey, alfalfa, clover, chickweed, dandelion, plantain, lamb's quarters, amaranth, buckwheat, sunflower, Swiss chard

## Bee Forage Plants

Plants that provide nectar and pollen for honeybees and native pollinators:

**Trees**: Black locust (one of the best honey sources), linden/basswood, maple, willow, fruit tree blossoms, chestnut
**Shrubs**: Blueberry, raspberry, blackberry, clover, Russian sage, butterfly bush
**Herbaceous**: Borage, buckwheat, clover, dandelion, lavender, thyme, oregano, sunflower, phacelia, yarrow, echinacea

## Water Plants

For pond edges, constructed wetlands, and greywater treatment:

**Floating**: Duckweed (*Lemna minor* -- high protein chicken feed), water hyacinth (tropical only)
**Emergent**: Cattail (*Typha* -- edible shoots/roots, water purification), bulrush (*Scirpus*), reed canary grass (*Phragmites*), canna lily, iris, pickerel weed
**Marginal**: Willow, dogwood, mint, watercress, marsh marigold, sweet flag

## Difficult Site Plants

**Salt-tolerant**: Sea buckthorn, rosemary, fig, date palm, pomegranate, carob, saltbush, yarrow
**Wet soil**: Willow, alder, elderberry, cattail, iris, mint, skunk cabbage, swamp milkweed
**Dry soil**: Rosemary, lavender, thyme, yarrow, sage, Russian sage, sedum, prickly pear
**Shade**: Hosta, ferns, Solomon's seal, wild ginger, sweet woodruff, mint, comfrey (tolerates partial shade)
**Wind**: Sea buckthorn, rugosa rose, juniper, pine, caragana, Russian olive
