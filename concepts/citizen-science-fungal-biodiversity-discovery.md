## Limitations and Challenges

Citizen science fungal data are subject to well-documented biases. Observers
tend to cluster in accessible areas, producing spatial gaps in coverage. Large,
colorful, or edible fungi are over-represented relative to small, inconspicuous,
or cryptic species. Seasonal sampling bias is also significant, with most
records concentrated in autumn in temperate regions. Data quality varies
considerably, and misidentifications are common even with automated image
recognition assistance. Despite these limitations, citizen science remains an
