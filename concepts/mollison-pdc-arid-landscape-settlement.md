## Village Location: Box Canyons

"The most satisfactory place to locate a village is in these box
canyons, up on cliffs within the box canyons." The advantages are:

- A single fence across the canyon entrance keeps out all large feral
  animals
- Box canyons are 300 to 400 yards wide with abrupt cliffs rising from
  pediments
- Little box canyons branch off the main canyon, providing multiple
  microsites
- A trickle of water runs at the bottom where pediments meet
