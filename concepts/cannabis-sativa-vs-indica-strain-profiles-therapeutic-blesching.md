## Indica Profile

Indica strains feature a lower THC to higher CBD/CBN ratio. The higher CBD content
modulates the psychoactive effects of THC:

- **Physical emphasis**: Indica effects tend to manifest more in the body, producing
  sensations of heaviness, warmth, and deep physical relaxation.

- **Sedating and relaxing**: Indicas generally produce a calming, grounding experience
  that can promote sleep and reduce physical tension.

- **Introverted quality**: The indica experience tends toward introspection and inner
  calm, making it suitable for evening use and solitary reflective activities.

- **Best for evening and bedtime use**: The sedating nature of indicas makes them
  appropriate for nighttime therapeutic applications and sleep aid.
