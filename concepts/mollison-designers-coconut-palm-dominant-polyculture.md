## Varietal Selection

For coconuts, as for many other species (guava, yam, taro, banana,
papaya), there are dozens of varieties developed for specific sites, soils,
microclimates, or uses. There are coconut palms ideally suited to oil
production, others for fine quality cup copra, others for shredded coconut,
coconut milk, or fresh nut markets. Dwarf, medium-height, and tall
varieties exist.

Dwarf varieties (Philippine, Samoan) are ideal for village surrounds,
especially in windy areas where falling coconuts cause deaths each year.
They are easily accessible, have large nuts, good eating characteristics,
and will not damage people or buildings if nuts fall.
