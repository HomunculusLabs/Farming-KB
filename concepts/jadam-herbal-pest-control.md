# JADAM Herbal Pest Control (JHS)

JADAM herb solution (JHS) is a boiled extract of insect-repelling plants, used as a natural pesticide. Combined with [[jadam-wetting-agent]] (JWA) and optionally JADAM sulfur (JS), it forms a complete pest [[vegetable-insect-and-disease-control]] system that costs a fraction of commercial pesticides.

## Why Herbal Pest Control

Trees want their fruits eaten to spread seeds -- they do not produce anti-insect substances in autumn. Farming's purpose is opposite to nature's: humans want to take all the fruits. Pesticide is therefore essential for fruit production. JADAM's natural pesticides are permitted under organic regulations, environment-friendly, not harmful to human health, effective, cheap, and easy.

[[jadam-natural-pesticide-jnp]] costs about 1/50 of buying chemical pesticides.

## Recommended Herb Plants

Common plants with strong insect-repelling effects:

- **[[comparison-jerusalem-artichoke-vs-potato]]** ([[helianthus-tuberosus]]) -- Easily grown anywhere, grows tall (no weeding needed). Boil leaves, branches, and roots. Controls aphids, mites, and most moths. Assumed that inulin in the plant acts as pesticide.
- **Gingko** -- Tree has perfect defense mechanism against pests and diseases. Use fresh leaves, fallen leaves, and fruits.
- **Korean pasque flower** (Pulsatilla koreana) -- Root is used. Strongest pesticide effect of all. Used in oriental medicine for blood circulation.
- **Red spider lily** ([[lycoris-radiata]]) -- Root is used. Used in oriental medicine for tumors. Repels moles and snakes when planted.
- **Pokeweed, water pepper, bracken, foxglove** -- Also effective

Other options: tobacco leaves (steep 15 days), hot pepper (steep 1 month), garlic (steep 1 month), gingko fruits (steep 1 month).

## Why Boiling

JADAM recommends boiling herbs rather than brewing in vinegar, wood vinegar, or alcohol. Boiling is cheap, easy, and produces strong solutions. Boiled herb solutions mix well with JWA and form much foam. High-pressure double pots (like those used in oriental medicine) produce even stronger solutions faster.

For herbs with strong scent (peppermint, cinnamon bark, star anise, rosemary, lavender, hot pepper, mustard, pepper), deep frying in canola oil is another method -- the poisonous substances melt into the oil.

## How to Make JHS

Using Korean pasque flower root as example:

1. Add 3 kg dried herb and 75 L water to a 100 L container with gas burner
2. Place herb in fine mesh bag with rocks to sink it
3. Bring to boil on high heat
4. Reduce to medium heat once boiling
5. Maintain gentle boil for approximately 5 hours
6. Remove the bag
7. Keep water slightly boiling for disinfection while bottling
8. Pour into heat-resistant bottles, fill completely, squeeze out air, seal immediately
9. Lay bottles on their side (hot liquid disinfects the air pocket around lid)
10. Store in cold storage for long-term keeping

## Storage and Shelf Life

- Store in cool, dark place. Cold storage is ideal.
- Well-sterilized JHS keeps 1-2 years
- Do NOT use sediments; filter if not clear
- If a bottle inflates during storage, fermentation has started -- the JHS has lost effectiveness
- Once opened, use within 1-2 days
- Use small bottles for storage (easier to use up quickly, less prone to microbial attack)
- After autumn harvest, prepare JWA, JS, and various JHS for next year

## Dilution and Application

- Mix 3-20 L of JHS with water to make 500 L of spray
- For fog machines: 10 L per 17 L
- Can be applied to all crops
- Causes little concentration stress
- JHS also functions as fertilizer (unlike chemical pesticides)

## JHS as Fertilizer

Analysis of Korean pasque flower root JHS shows: pH 6.9, contains organic matter, T-N, P2O5, K2O, CaO, MgO, Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu. It provides germicide, insecticide, and fertilizer effects simultaneously.

## Complete JADAM Pesticide Combinations

- **JHS pesticide:** JHS + JWA
- **JMS-JHS pesticide:** JMS + JHS + JWA (pesticide, nutrient, and fertilizer in one)
- **Complete pesticide:** JWA + JS + JHS combined
