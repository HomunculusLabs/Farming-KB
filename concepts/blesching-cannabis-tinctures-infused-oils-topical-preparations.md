### Dosing with Tinctures

Blesching provides clear guidance on tincture dosing: "Infused oils or tinctures often come in dropper bottles, which allow for precise dosing. As always, start slowly with a few drops, and wait at least one hour to feel the effect. Then slowly increase the dosage by a couple of drops, repeating the process until you have achieved the desired therapeutic effect."

Key dosing principles from the Cannabis Health Index:

- **Start low** — Many patients begin with 3–4 drops
- **Go slow** — Wait at least one hour before increasing
- **Track your response** — Document dose, timing, and effects
- **Recalibrate with each batch** — Since most products are not standardized, each new bottle may differ in potency
- **Consistency is key** — Once an effective dose is found, maintain it

### Routes of Tincture Administration

1. **Sublingual (under the tongue)** — Fastest absorption; cannabinoids enter the bloodstream through the mucous membranes, bypassing first-pass metabolism in the liver. Effects typically begin within 15–30 minutes.

2. **Oral ingestion** — Tinctures can be added to food or beverages. Absorption occurs through the gastrointestinal tract, producing delayed onset (45–90 minutes) but longer duration of effects.

3. **Topical application** — Tinctures can be applied directly to the skin for localized effects.

## Cannabis-Infused Oils

### Production Methods

Cannabis-infused oils are made by extracting cannabinoids into a carrier oil through gentle heating. Blesching notes: "hemp oil, almond oil, or coconut oil (preferably of the virgin, organic variety) may be mixed with cannabis as an additive to create topical skin creams and food products."

Common carrier oils include:

- **Coconut oil** — High in saturated fat, which efficiently absorbs cannabinoids; solid at room temperature; anti-inflammatory properties
- **Olive oil** — Readily available; good bioavailability; rich in beneficial fatty acids
- **Hempseed oil** — Optimal balance of omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids; complementary to cannabis therapeutics
- **Almond oil** — Light texture; good skin penetration for topical use
- **MCT oil** — Rapid absorption; neutral flavor; long shelf life
