## Overview

Lycoperdonosis is a rare respiratory illness caused by the inhalation of
massive quantities of puffball (Lycoperdon) spores. The condition was brought
to clinical attention following a 1994 incident in Wisconsin in which a group
of teenagers was hospitalized after snorting puffball spores at a party. The
name derives from Lycoperdon, the puffball genus. The condition demonstrates
the hazards of inhaling fungal spores in concentrated doses and illustrates
the immune system's response to chitinous fungal particles lodged deep in the
lungs.

squeezing the fruiting body — releases a cloud of spores.

The spores are small (typically 3–5 micrometers in diameter), spherical or
nearly so, and covered with minute spines or warts. This small size allows
them to become airborne easily and to penetrate deep into the respiratory
tract when inhaled. A single mature puffball can release enough spores to
expose an individual to millions or even billions of particles in a single
