## Identification Challenges

### Morphological Limitations

The identification of psilocybin-containing mushrooms using traditional
mycological methods proved problematic for several reasons:

- **Morphological similarity**: Many psilocybin-containing species closely
resemble non-psychoactive species in the genera Inocybe, Galerina, and
others
- **Microscopic overlap**: Spore size, shape, and ornamentation patterns
overlap significantly between psychoactive and inactive species
- **Expertise requirement**: Reliable morphological identification requires
considerable mycological expertise that front-line law enforcement officers
and customs officials typically lacked
