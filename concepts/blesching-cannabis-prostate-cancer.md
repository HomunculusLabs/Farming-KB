---# Cannabis and Prostate Cancer

## Overview

The prostate gland is part of the male reproductive system, located at the
outlet of the urinary bladder. About the size of a walnut in its healthy state,
the urethra leads through the prostate. The gland produces and stores seminal
fluids in which sperm move during ejaculation. Females have analogous tissue
called Skene's glands that produce similar fluid.

Prostate cancer is one of the most common cancers in elderly males, generally
very slow-growing. Many patients never have particular symptoms, but prostate
enlargement commonly underlies cancer pathologies, exerting pressure on the
urethra and making urination difficult. Other possible symptoms include sexual
dysfunction, bloody urine, painful urination, or regional pain.

An eight-year study of nearly 30,000 males confirmed that increased sexual
activity (more than 21 ejaculations per month) correlated to a 30% decreased
risk of developing prostate cancer compared to males ejaculating four to seven
times monthly. Specific causes remain largely unknown, and traditional treatment
includes chemotherapy, radiation, and/or surgery.

### CB1 in Healthy Prostate Tissue

In 2009, researchers from Debrecen, Hungary, identified CB1 in epithelial and
smooth muscle cells of the healthy human prostate for the first time. They
confirmed study results and strongly argued that CB1 possesses a promising
future role in prostate cancer treatment.
