# Soil Amendment Reference

This reference covers common organic soil amendments, their nutrient profiles, application rates, and best use cases. Always base amendments on soil test results rather than routine application.

## pH Adjusters

### Agricultural Lime (Calcium Carbonate)
- **Raises soil pH** (sweetens acidic soil).
- Primary nutrient: calcium (30-40% Ca), magnesium (if dolomitic lime).
- Application rate: 5-10 lbs per 100 sq ft (follow soil test).
- Apply in fall; takes 3-6 months to fully react.
- Use dolomitic lime if soil is also low in magnesium.
- Do NOT use if soil pH is already above 6.5.

### Gypsum (Calcium Sulfate)
- Does NOT significantly change soil pH — adds calcium without raising pH.
- Also provides sulfur (15-18% S), which lowers pH very slightly.
- Application rate: 1-2 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Improves soil structure in clay soils by flocculating particles.
- Good for treating blossom end rot in tomatoes and peppers (calcium deficiency).
- Can help displace excess sodium in sodic soils.

## Phosphorus Sources

### Rock Phosphate (Colloidal or Hard Rock)
- Slow-release phosphorus source (0-3-0 to 0-2-0).
- Application rate: 2-5 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Best applied in fall or mixed into compost piles for microbial activation.
- Extremely slow breakdown — benefits show over 1-3 years.
- Good for building long-term phosphorus reserves.
- Not effective in alkaline soils (pH above 7.0).

### Bone Meal
- Higher phosphorus availability than rock phosphate (3-15-0).
- Also provides calcium (12-16%).
- Application rate: 1-2 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Effective for bulbs, root crops, and flowering plants.
- Can attract animals — work into soil well.
- Moderate release speed — available within weeks.

## Nitrogen Sources

### Blood Meal
- Very high nitrogen (12-0-0 to 13-0-0).
- Fast-acting nitrogen source from dried animal blood.
- Application rate: 1-2 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Also provides trace iron.
- Use sparingly — excess can burn plants.
- Good side-dress for heavy-feeding crops (corn, brassicas).
- Keep away from dogs and wildlife (attractive scent).

## Potassium and Trace Mineral Sources

### Greensand (Glauconite)
- Slow-release potassium (0-0-3 to 0-0-7).
- Also contains iron, magnesium, silica, and 30+ trace minerals.
- Application rate: 2-5 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Improves soil moisture retention and structure.
- Very slow release — lasts 3-5 years.
- Safe to use generously; will not burn plants.
- Excellent for sandy soils needing mineral diversity.

### Azomite (Hydrated Sodium Calcium Aluminosilicate)
- Volcanic ash-derived trace mineral amendment (0-0-0.2).
- Contains 67+ major and trace minerals.
- Application rate: 1-2 lbs per 100 sq ft (light) or 5-10 lbs (remedial).
- Re-mineralizes depleted soils.
- Does not significantly affect pH.
- Can be added to compost piles, potting mixes, or directly to soil.
- Apply once per year; slow and steady release.

### Kelp Meal
- Derived from dried seaweed.
- Low NPK (1-0.1-2) but rich in trace minerals, cytokinins, and auxins.
- Application rate: 1-2 lbs per 100 sq ft.
- Stimulates root growth and plant immune response.
- Contains alginic acid — improves soil structure and water retention.
- Can be used as a foliar spray (kelp extract).
- Benefits soil microbial populations.
- Cold-water kelp extracts preserve more hormones than heat-processed products.

## When to Apply

- **Fall**: Lime, rock phosphate, greensand (slow-release amendments need time).
- **Early spring**: Gypsum, bone meal, general mineral mixes.
- **Planting time**: Bone meal in planting holes for transplants and bulbs.
- **Growing season**: Blood meal as side-dress for heavy feeders; kelp meal as foliar or top-dress.
- **Compost pile**: Rock phosphate, kelp meal, azomite to enrich finished compost.

## Safety Notes

- Wear a mask when applying fine powders (lime, azomite, blood meal).
- Wash hands after handling blood meal or bone meal.
- Keep blood meal away from pets and wildlife.
- Store all amendments in a dry, rodent-proof container.
