## The Bean Test Protocol

A practical efficacy test uses common beans as a bioassay. Beans
are planted in soil amended with unfiltered water versus filtered
water from the mycofiltration unit. Differences in germination
rate, seedling vigor, root development, and overall growth over
several weeks provide a biological indicator of contaminant
removal. This bioassay is accessible even without laboratory
equipment and gives a clear visual indication of filtration
effectiveness.
