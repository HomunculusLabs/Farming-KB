### When to Use WS-Ca
- Cross-over period (vegetative to reproductive transition)
- After fruits have grown large (spray on leaves for solid fruits)
- When plants overgrow (prevents excessive [[bionutrients-lab-calcium-phosphate-calamansi]] (WCaP)
- FPJ, OHN, and seawater for better taste and more aromatic fruits
- Basic dilution ratio: 1:1000

### Calcium Deficiency Symptoms
- Underdeveloped roots and feeble root hairs
- Leaves discolor, become brownish, then dry out
- Empty bean pods
- Poor ripening, soft flesh, insufficient fragrance
- Root vegetables become pithy and hollow
- Low starch accumulation in grains

## Water-Soluble Potassium (WS-K)

### Source Material
Potassium is extracted from tobacco stems, which naturally contain
high amounts of this element.

### Preparation
1. Dry tobacco stems and cut into pieces (not too fine -- avoid
   powder)
2. Put 1 kg of tobacco stems in a cloth bag
3. Soak in 5 liters of water for approximately 7 days
4. This produces crude liquid of natural potassium

### Functions of Potassium in Plants
- Activates starch-synthesizing enzyme
- Facilitates translocation of photosynthate
- Relocates storage substances in starchy crops
- Regulates stomata and water use
- Promotes synthesis of CO2-fixing enzyme
- Enhances metabolic enzyme reactions
- Improves resistance to drought and freezing
- Reduces lodging problems, increases yield and quality
