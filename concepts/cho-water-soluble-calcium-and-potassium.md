Water-soluble mineral inputs are a distinctive feature of Dr. Cho's
Natural Farming. By converting normally insoluble minerals into
plant-available forms through acid extraction, NF makes calcium,
potassium, phosphorus, and other minerals readily absorbable. The two
most important are [[knf-water-soluble-calcium-wsca]]
(WS-Ca) and [[knf-water-soluble-potassium-detailed]] (WS-K), both corresponding to key stages of the
[[knf-nutritive-cycle-theory]].

## Water-Soluble Calcium (WS-Ca)

### Source Material
Calcium carbonate (CaCO3), the main component of eggshells and
seashells, is one of the most abundant substances on Earth. However,
calcium carbonate is insoluble in water. Natural Farming extracts
water-soluble calcium by dissolving eggshells in [[knf-brown-rice-vinegar]] (BRV).

### Preparation
1. Collect eggshells; remove the inner peel (clears non-calcium
   matter)
2. Crush into small pieces (not powder -- larger pieces are faster
   and more effective)
3. Lightly roast to remove organic substances that could rot
4. Put roasted shells in BRV-filled container. Bubbles appear as
   CO2 is released (acetic acid reacts with calcium carbonate)
5. When bubbles stop, the solution is saturated. Fluffy shells mean
   CO2 is dissolving into vinegar
6. Add shells slowly to prevent overflow from the reaction
7. If shells sink without fully dissolving, the solution is saturated
   -- extract the liquid and add more BRV

### Functions of Calcium in Plants
- Contributes to carbohydrate and protein utilization
- Major component of cell membranes; enables smooth cell division
- Removes harmful substances by binding with organic acids
- Prevents overgrowth of crops
- Makes fruits firm; prolongs storage period
- Promotes phosphoric acid absorption
- Carries and accumulates nutrients from branches/leaves to final
  storage organs (ovary, fruit)
- Transitions plants from vegetative to reproductive growth

### When to Use WS-Ca
- Cross-over period (vegetative to reproductive transition)
- After fruits have grown large (spray on leaves for solid fruits)
- When plants overgrow (prevents excessive vegetative growth)
- When initial growth is poor
- When leaves discolor, lack luster, or flower buds differentiate
  poorly
- When physiological drop is severe, fruit enlargement is slow, or
  sugar content decreases
- Do NOT use when vigorous vegetative growth is needed

### Combinations for Enhanced Effect
WS-Ca works synergistically with:
- Water-soluble calcium phosphate (WCaP)
- FPJ, OHN, and seawater for better taste and more aromatic fruits
- Basic dilution ratio: 1:1000

### Calcium Deficiency Symptoms
- Underdeveloped roots and feeble root hairs
- Leaves discolor, become brownish, then dry out
- Empty bean pods
- Poor ripening, soft flesh, insufficient fragrance
- Root vegetables become pithy and hollow
- Low starch accumulation in grains

## Water-Soluble Potassium (WS-K)

### Source Material
Potassium is extracted from tobacco stems, which naturally contain
high amounts of this element.

### Preparation
1. Dry tobacco stems and cut into pieces (not too fine -- avoid
   powder)
2. Put 1 kg of tobacco stems in a cloth bag
3. Soak in 5 liters of water for approximately 7 days
4. This produces crude liquid of natural potassium

### Functions of Potassium in Plants
- Activates starch-synthesizing enzyme
- Facilitates translocation of photosynthate
- Relocates storage substances in starchy crops
- Regulates stomata and water use
- Promotes synthesis of CO2-fixing enzyme
- Enhances metabolic enzyme reactions
- Improves resistance to drought and freezing
- Reduces lodging problems, increases yield and quality

### Deficiency Symptoms
- Chlorosis starting from old leaves (edges become yellowish-brown)
- Branch growth deterioration, stunted maturity
- Thin roots and stems, suppressed lignification
- Reduced cold resistance
- Seeds/fruit become small and mature late

### When to Use
- Dilute 0.7 liters of crude WS-K with 20 liters of water
- CAUTION: In hot, humid conditions, WS-K combined with nitrogen
  can cause synergy-induced overgrowth. Also avoid on rainy days.

### Excess Symptoms
- Excessive WS-K causes magnesium deficiency
- Symptoms similar to gas disorder, appearing in the middle of leaves

## Potassium and Calcium: The K-Ca Balance

WS-K and WS-Ca represent the reproductive growth stage of the
Nutritive Cycle. Potassium is needed for proper color development of
fruits; calcium prevents overgrowth and hardens fruits. Together
they ensure quality harvests. Weather conditions must be considered
when applying these inputs -- synergy with nitrogen in hot, humid
conditions can cause unwanted vegetative growth.
