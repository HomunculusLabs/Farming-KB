## Sheds

Sheds ranked second only to pits or frames for mushroom
cultivation. Beds in sheds were convenient to access and
easily covered and uncovered as needed. Cuthill grew
mushrooms under open sheds for extended periods, though he
encountered difficulties with [[accessible-mushroom-cultivation-for-disabilities]], suggesting that orchard
houses, with their glass covering and shade management,
would provide excellent growing conditions.

## Economic Considerations for Cottagers

Cuthill specifically addressed cottagers and working-class
growers, noting that a good little bed of mushrooms would
save a shilling or two per week during winter for family
use, with additional income possible from selling surplus to
neighbours. He remarked that "mushrooms grow faster than
pigs" and require no feed, only attention, making them an
economical choice for households with limited space and
resources.
