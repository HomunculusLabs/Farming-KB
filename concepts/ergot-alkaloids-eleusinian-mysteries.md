# Ergot Alkaloids and the Eleusinian Mysteries

The Eleusinian Mysteries were the most important religious initiation rites in
[[psychedelic-use-in-ancient-greece]], conducted annually for over two thousand years at Eleusis near
Athens. A persistent and intriguing hypothesis, advanced by [[terence-mckenna]]
among others, holds that the central visionary experience of the Mysteries was
mediated by ergot alkaloids derived from the fungus *[[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]]*.

## The Eleusinian Mysteries

The Mysteries were celebrated every September for initiates who had undergone a
period of preparation and purification. The culminating rite, held in the
Telesterion (a large underground hall), involved the revelation of sacred
objects and a visionary experience that participants consistently described as
life-changing. The [[demeter-and-persephone-the-homeric-hymn-and-eleusinian-foundation-myth]] to Demeter, our primary source for the mythic
framework of the Mysteries, describes the abduction of Persephone to the
underworld and her return, a cyclical narrative of death and rebirth that
paralleled the agricultural cycle and likely informed the experiential content
of the initiation.

Famous initiates included Plato, Aristotle, Cicero, Pindar, and Marcus
Aurelius. Cicero wrote that the Mysteries taught people "to live with joy and
to die with a better hope." Despite their cultural importance, the central
secret — the *epopteia*, or the visionary revelation — was guarded so closely
that its true nature remains unknown.

## The Kykeon Question

Central to the Mysteries was the ritual consumption of *kykeon*, a barley-based
drink described in the Homeric Hymn. The composition of kykeon has been the
subject of intense scholarly debate, as it may have contained psychoactive
ingredients that produced the transformative visionary experience reported by
initiates.

In 1978, chemist Albert Hofmann (discoverer of LSD) and classical scholar Carl
Ruck proposed that kykeon was prepared from ergotized barley contaminated with
*[[claviceps-purpurea]]*. Hofmann, drawing on his intimate knowledge of ergot
chemistry, argued that a water-soluble preparation could extract the
psychoactive alkaloid ergine ([[ergot-and-lysergic-acid-history]] amide, also known as LSA) while
leaving behind the toxic ergopeptine alkaloids responsible for ergotism
(St. Anthony's Fire).

## Ergot Chemistry

*Claviceps purpurea* is a parasitic fungus that infects cereal grains,
especially rye but also [[ergot-of-barley-and-wheat-alkaloid-composition]]. It produces a complex mixture of
alkaloids that can be broadly categorized into:

- **Ergopeptines** (ergotamine, ergocristine, ergocryptine, ergocornine):
  These are responsible for the vasoconstrictive and neurotoxic effects of
  ergotism. They cause intense vasoconstriction, hallucinations, and in severe
  cases, gangrene and death.
- **Ergoline derivatives** (ergine/LSA, [[lysergic-acid-hydroxyethylamide]]):
  These are water-soluble, less toxic, and possess known psychoactive
  properties. LSA is structurally similar to LSD but approximately 10-50x less
  potent.

The key insight of the Hofmann-Ruck hypothesis is that a simple water
extraction of ergotized barley would preferentially dissolve the water-soluble
[[morning-glory-lsa-and-ergoline-alkaloids]] (including LSA) while leaving the lipid-soluble, more toxic
ergopeptines behind. This would produce a drink capable of inducing altered
[[mckenna-food-gods-designer-states-of-consciousness]] without the severe toxic effects associated with
ergotism.

## Evidence and Arguments

### Supporting Evidence

- **Consistent descriptions**: Ancient accounts of the Eleusinian experience
  consistently describe it as a vision of profound beauty and emotional
  intensity — language more suggestive of a genuine psychoactive experience
  than mere suggestion or theatrical effect.
- **Secrecy**: The extraordinary secrecy maintained for two millennia suggests
  the experience was repeatable and reliably induced, consistent with a
  pharmacological mechanism rather than purely symbolic ritual.
- **Barley as substrate**: Barley was a primary ingredient of kykeon and is a
  known host for *Claviceps purpurea*.
- **Hofmann's expertise**: Hofmann's intimate knowledge of ergot chemistry
  lent significant credibility to the feasibility argument.
