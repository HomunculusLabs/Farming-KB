## Cannabis and Fibromyalgia

## Overview

Fibromyalgia is a chronic, complex pain syndrome characterized by widespread musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, sleep disturbances, cognitive dysfunction, and multiple tender points throughout the body. It affects an estimated 2-8% of the population, with a significantly higher prevalence in women (approximately 80-90% of diagnosed patients). The condition was formally recognized by the American College of Rheumatology in 1990 with the publication of classification criteria based on the presence of widespread pain in combination with tenderness in at least 11 of 18 specific tender point sites.

Despite decades of research, fibromyalgia remains poorly understood and often inadequately managed. Conventional pharmacological treatments include pregabalin, duloxetine, milnacipran, and cyclobenzaprine, but these provide only partial relief for many patients and are associated with significant side effects including weight gain, dizziness, sedation, and nausea. The limited efficacy of conventional medications has led many patients to explore cannabinoid-based therapies.

## Pathophysiology

Fibromyalgia is now understood as a disorder of central pain processing rather than a condition of peripheral inflammation or tissue damage. Key pathophysiological features include:

- **Central sensitization:** Amplified pain signaling within the central nervous system leads to hyperalgesia (increased sensitivity to painful stimuli) and allodynia (pain from normally non-painful stimuli).
- **Neurotransmitter imbalances:** Elevated levels of substance P, glutamate, and nerve growth factor in cerebrospinal fluid, combined with reduced levels of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine.
- **Neuroendocrine dysfunction:** Dysregulation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, altered [[blesching-endocannabinoid-autonomic-nervous-system-adrenal-glands]] dysfunction:** Sympathetic hyperactivity and reduced parasympathetic tone.
- **Sleep architecture disruption:** Reduced slow-wave sleep and increased alpha-wave intrusion during deep sleep stages.

- **Altered endocannabinoid levels:** Some studies report lower cerebrospinal fluid levels of anandamide in fibromyalgia patients compared to healthy controls.

This theoretical framework suggests that supplementation with phytocannabinoids could help restore endocannabinoid system function and alleviate fibromyalgia symptoms.
