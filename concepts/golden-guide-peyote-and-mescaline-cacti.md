## Religious Significance Among Mexican Indians

Religious importance of peyote persists among the Tarahumare, Huichol, and
other Mexican Indians. The Tarahumare believe that when Father Sun left earth
to dwell above, he left peyote, or hikuli, to cure man's ills and woes; that
peyote sings and talks as it grows; and that God speaks through the plant.
Many legends about the supernatural powers of peyote underlie its religious
importance. The peyote-collecting trip of the Huichols is highly religious,
requiring pilgrims to forego adult experiences, especially sexual, for it
reenacts the first peyote quest of the divine ancestors.
