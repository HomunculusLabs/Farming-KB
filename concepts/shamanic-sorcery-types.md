# Shamanic Sorcery Types

## Overview

Amazonian vegetalismo encompasses not only [[ayahuasca-preparation-pablo-amaringo-ucayali-shamanism]]'s visionary paintings document three primary types of sorcery practiced by evil vegetalistas (brujos or sorcerers), each employing distinct weapons and methods. Healers (curanderos) must be able to identify which type of sorcery has afflicted a patient in order to administer the correct treatment. The diagnostic method involves observing the pattern of lights surrounding the patient during an [[ayahuasca-vision-art-and-shipibo-tradition]].

## The Three Types of Sorcerers (Vision 12)

### 1. The Chontero (Dart Sorcerer)

The chontero is typically a Shipibo shaman who inflicts harm using chontas or virotes — magical darts made from:

- Thorns of various plants and trees
- Beaks of certain birds
- Fangs of snakes
- Hair of the casha-cushillo (porcupine, *Coendu bicolor*)

**Method of attack**: The chontero takes a chonta from his mann (magical phlegm stored in the body) and blows toward the victim. The dart strikes the victim's body — for example, in the right arm — causing great pain.

**Diagnosis**: If a curandero sees zigzagging lights surrounding the patient, it indicates the patient has been struck by a chontero.

**Defense**: If the victim's body has been previously treated by a good vegetalista (with protective icaros and blows), the victim immediately feels the attack and can seek rapid remedy. An untreated victim may suffer great pain without knowing the cause and may even die.

### 2. The Marupa Sorcerer

The marupa sorcerer (depicted as a Cocama) manipulates marupas — living creatures including insects, snakes, scorpions, bats, rays, and toads — which he keeps in his mann. This technique is called hechicería marupa ([[huani-sorcery]].

**Critical distinction**: No vegetalista can cure this kind of sorcery. Only God can save the victim, making the huani the most lethal form of magical attack documented in the tradition.

## Sorcery Plants

Several plants are associated with harmful magical practices:

- **Marannara** (unidentified): Used by sorcerers
- **Tungara** (unidentified): Tree whose icaros are used to cause harm
- **Cocona-sacha** (unidentified): Thorny plant used in sorcery
- **Siuca-huito** (*Solanum kioniotrichum*): Used by sorcerers
- **Alambre-casha** (unidentified): Used by sorcerers
- **Ishanga** (*Laportea aestuans*): Used by sorcerers to cause harm

## The Powers of the Pipes (Vision 11)

The shamanic pipe is a central implement in Amazonian vegetalismo, serving as a conduit for [[metzner-spirit-beings-multiple-realities-entheogens]]. Different trees produce pipes with different spiritual properties:

### Pipe Types and Their Spirits

| Tree Species | Spirit Being | Beings Released |
|---|---|---|
| Tahuari (*Tabebuja sp.*) | Human and black bull | Yura-cukchas mailcas (old snowy-haired wise ones), renowned miracle-working doctors; Talabat (a king of great stature) |
| Huacamayo-caspi (*Coutarea hexandra*, *Sickingia tinctoria*) | Prince Yana-huya (black face) | Curikiros (golden teeth), forest nymph |
| Shihuahuaco (*Dipteryx sp.*) | Machaco-runa (snake people) | Maya-machaco (snake ancestors), sylph Pichamui shipash (maiden that cleans), belonging to the tobacco flower |
| Cumaceba (*Cesalpina equinata*) | Giant named Maun with golden crown | Ampishcashamuy (those who come to cure), serpent tamer Alli Urman |

### Shamanic Specializations by Pipe

The text describes six native shamans in trance, each with distinct capabilities:

- **White cushma shaman**: Dedicated solely to healing. His arkana (defence) is the white serpent of the white rainbow.
- **Woman healer**: Specialises in curing mal aire. Her power manifests as a circle of red [[cryptochrome-and-blue-light-photoreception]].
- **Lower left healer**: Average ability. His arkana is an ochre-yellow boa.
- **Dark cape healer**: Can both heal and kill. His red boa can journey into waters.
- **Ochre cushma Conibo**: Has learned from various trees and ayahuasca. Possesses many powers, evidenced by gold in ceremonial dress. His golden boa reflects both healing and killing abilities.
- **Black cushma Shetebo sorcerer**: Exclusively a killer who works with the darkest beings of hell, including black yakurunas, black mermaids, black boas, the sarara, the black rainbow, the black ape, and the black aquatic bird. Possesses complete knowledge of witchcraft and curses.

### Celestial Beings Connected to the Pipes

- **Binop**: The elder spirit of the pinn, known for wisdom and strength
- **Churay Allinushki**: Forest nymph of the violet palm, associated with perfumes and sweet smells
- **Sanango flower spirits**: Queen spirits sitting upon flowers like thrones, possessing the gift of expelling cold using this powerful curative plant
- **Fedikel**: A celestial spirit (alpha-wave cherub) showing the movements of rotation and translation of Planet Earth, capable of instantaneously travelling through thousands of billions of stars
