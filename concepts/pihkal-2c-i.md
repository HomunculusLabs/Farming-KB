# 2C-I

## Overview

2C-I (2,5-[[shulgin-a-pihkal-doi-25-dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine]]-4-[[shulgin-a-pihkal-2c-i-25-dimethoxy-4-iodophenethylamine]]) is a halogen-substituted
phenethylamine and a prominent member of the 2C family documented in PIHKAL
as [[doc-compound-profile]] #22. The iodine analogue of 2C-B, 2C-I produces longer-lasting
and more visually intense [[mckenna-dmt-onset-phenomenology-and-mode-shift-experience]]s. It gained popularity in the early
2000s as a research chemical after 2C-B was scheduled, earning a reputation
for producing colorful, enjoyable visual effects described as "clean" and
"sparkling."

Despite its popularity, 2C-I is considered more challenging than 2C-B, with
longer du[[phytochrome-red-far-red-ratio-shade-detection-plants]]n and greater cognitive intensity. Users describe it as more
"psychedelic" and less "empathogenic" than 2C-B, occupying a space between
2C-B and the more intense 2C-E in overall character.

The iodine atom at the 4-position follows a well-documented trend in
Shulgin's work: heavier halogens increase both potency and duration, from
2C-F (fluorine, least potent) through 2C-Cl, 2C-Br (2C-B), to 2C-I
(iodine, most potent among simple halogenated 2C compounds).

## Chemical Structure and Classification

2C-I's IUPAC name is 2-(4-iodo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethan-1-amine. It
features the characteristic 2,5-[[shulgin-a-pihkal-2c-i-4-iodo-25-dimethoxyphenethylamine]] backbone shared by
all 2C compounds, with an iodine atom at the 4-position. The iodine's large
atomic radius and high polarizability influence [[tihkal-receptor-binding-serotonin]] profile
and metabolic stability, contributing to longer duration compared to smaller
halogen-substituted analogues.

2C-I is the phenethylamine analogue of DOI (the alpha-methylated version),
with removal of the alpha-methyl group reducing potency and duration.

## Dosage and Administration

Shulgin's recommended oral dosage is 14 to 22 milligrams, with 16 to 18 mg
considered standard. The dose-response curve is relatively steep, and small
increases above the comfortable range can produce significantly more intense
effects. Onset occurs within 30 to 60 minutes, peaking over 1 to 2 hours.
Total duration ranges from 6 to 10 hours.

The comedown is generally smooth, though lingering stimulation may interfere
with sleep if taken late. Insufflation is possible at 5 to 10 mg but is
particularly painful and not recommended.

## Effects Profile

2C-I is renowned for its visual effects, often described as its defining
characteristic. Users report intense color enhancement with vibrancy and
saturation exceeding most other 2C compounds. Geometric patterns, flowing
visual distortions, and color shifting are common, with a distinctive
"sparkling" or "shimmering" quality.

Cognitive effects include enhanced creativity, altered thought patterns, and
a positive, contemplative mental state. The headspace is clearer and less
confusing than 2C-E, though more cognitively engaging than 2C-B. Emotional
effects tend to be positive but less pronounced than 2C-B's empathogenic
warmth.

Physical effects include moderate pupil dilation, increased energy, mild
body load (muscle tension, restlessness), and enhanced tactile perception.
Nausea during onset is reported by some users but is generally less severe
than with [[mescaline]] or 2C-E.

## Shulgin's Commentary

Shulgin documented 2C-I as having a "goodly amount of visual activity" and a
pleasant overall character. He noted its similarity to 2C-B while
acknowledging [[query-difference-between-white-rot-and-brown-rot-fungi]]s from the heavier iodine substituent. He
appreciated its visual richness, describing colors as particularly vivid
with a unique quality not found in other 2C compounds.

He emphasized the importance of accurate dosing, noting that lower doses
produced subtle sensory enhancement while higher doses became significantly
more psychedelic and introspective, with a narrow range between comfortable
and overwhelming experiences.
