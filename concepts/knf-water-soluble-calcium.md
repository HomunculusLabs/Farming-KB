# KNF Water Soluble Calcium (WSC)

Water Soluble Calcium is extracted from eggshells, animal bones, or seashells using [[brown-rice-vinegar-brv]] (BRV). It strengthens plant [[alpha-1-3-glucan-fungal-pathogen-cell-walls]], improves fruit quality, and helps prevent disease.

## Why Calcium Matters

- Calcium makes cell walls stronger and thicker
- When cell walls are strong, plants resist insects, disease, and physical damage
- Essential for fruit sweetness and firmness
- Prevents blossom-end rot in tomatoes, peppers
- Deficiency causes [[holmgren-long-rotation-forestry-slow-growth]], tip burn, and rot in many crops

## Materials

- Eggshells, animal bones (chicken bones, fish bones), or seashells
- Brown rice vinegar (BRV)
- Clay jar, wooden box, or PE container

## How to Make WSC

### From Eggshells

1. Collect eggshells from chicken, duck, or quail eggs
2. Wash eggshells thoroughly
3. Remove inner membrane
4. Dry in sunlight
5. Roast eggshells in a pot until they turn white or slightly brown (charred)
6. Crush roasted eggshells into small pieces
7. Put crushed shells into a container
8. Pour brown rice vinegar to cover (shells should be submerged)
9. The vinegar will bubble as it extracts calcium
10. Wait until bubbles stop (no more un-dissolved shells)
11. If all shells dissolve, add more roasted shells
12. When shells no longer dissolve, WSC is complete

### Ratio

- Eggshells and BRV at approximately 1:10 ratio (shells to vinegar by weight)
- Keep the solution at 1/3 the jar volume (not more)

## Fermentation Time

- Usually 7-14 days
- Ready when no more bubbles are produced and all shells are dissolved

## Dilution and Use

- Basic dilution ratio: 1:1,000
- Foliar spray during [[knf-reproductive-growth-stage]] period
- Apply to flowering and fruiting plants
- Good to use with OHN for enhanced effect
- Can be mixed with FPJ for balanced nutrition
- Apply in early morning or late afternoon

## When to Use

- When plant cell walls appear weak
- During fruiting stage for firmer, sweeter fruit
- When insects attack (calcium-strengthened walls resist pests)
- For [[cervantes-damping-off-seedling-disease-prevention]]
- During rapid growth periods when calcium demand is high

## Storage

- Keep in cool, shaded place
- No direct sunlight
