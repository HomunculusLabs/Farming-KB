## IBS Classification

IBS is classified according to the primary symptoms displayed:

- **IBS-D**: Diarrhea-predominant
- **IBS-C**: Constipation-predominant
- **IBS-A**: Alternating diarrhea with constipation
- **IBS-PI**: Post-infectious IBS
