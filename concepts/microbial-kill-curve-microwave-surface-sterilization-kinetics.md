## Differential Susceptibility of Challenge Organisms

The three test organisms displayed clearly stratified susceptibility:

- ***Escherichia coli:*** Most susceptible. As a vegetative Gram-negative
  rod lacking a protective spore coat, *E. coli* showed significant kill
  at intermediate exposure levels.

- ***Pseudomonas cepacia:*** Intermediate susceptibility. Although also
  a vegetative Gram-negative, *P. cepacia* exhibits marginally greater
  thermal tolerance, likely due to membrane lipid composition differences.

- ***Bacillus pumilus:*** Most resistant. A spore-forming Gram-positive
  bacterium whose endospores required the highest cumulative energy dose
  to achieve complete inactivation, defining the practical sterilization
  endpoint for the protocol.
