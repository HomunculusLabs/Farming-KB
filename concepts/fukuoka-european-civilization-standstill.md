## Churches and God

The state of European churches reveals civilizational exhaustion:

- Empty churches across Europe symbolize the death of the religious framework that sustained Western civilization
- Europeans have not lost their spiritual impulse but have lost faith in institutional religion
- The church buildings remain as monuments to a worldview that no longer commands belief
- This spiritual vacuum is part of why Europeans are turning to Eastern philosophies
