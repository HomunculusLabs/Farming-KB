# PF TEK: Brown Rice Vermiculite Mushroom Cultivation

## Overview

The PF TEK ([[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]] Technique) is a revolutionary mushroom cultivation
method introduced by "[[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus-cultivation-method]]" (Robert McPherson) in 1991. Published
initially as a kit in High Times Magazine and later as complete instructions in
1992, the PF TEK enabled easy cultivation of [[cap-versus-stem-alkaloid-distribution-in-psilocybin-mushrooms]] using common
kitchen utensils and readily available supplies: small canning jars, brown rice
powder, vermiculite, and an aquarium. It has been copied worldwide and forms
the basis for most amateur [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-cultivation-techniques]] used today.

## Core Innovation: Vermiculite-Based Substrate

The PF TEK is fundamentally a brown rice method improved by using vermiculite as a
base matrix with pulverized brown rice added as nutrition. The key insight is that
when mycelium is cultured in grain alone, it turns into a dense mass with little
air space. When grown with vermiculite, the mycelial threads stretch across the
aerated spaces, producing a healthier, more vigorous colonization.

Unlike conventional cultivation that clones [[hplc-analysis-psilocybin-psilocin-mushroom-tissue]] (leading to senescence
— genetic degradation and eventual cessation of fruiting), the PF TEK employs mass
[[mushroom-spore-inoculation-technique]] directly to the [[pf-tek-maximum-fruiting-substrate-formula]]. This preserves the complete
genotype and ensures a never-ending succession of fungus, with all of the spore's
reproductive ability intact. Senescence is no longer a problem.

## PF Substrate Formula

### Standard Formula (per half-pint jar)

- 1/8 cup brown rice powder (freshly ground from regular brown rice in a coffee
  grinder; freshness makes a significant difference)
- 1/2 to 2/3 cup horticultural vermiculite, medium grade
- 40-45 cc (ml) water — slightly less than 1/4 cup

### Maximum Fruiting Formula

- 1/4 cup brown rice powder
- 1/2 cup vermiculite
- 60 cc water

Note: Vermiculite coarseness varies between brands. Finer vermiculite (particles
averaging ~1 mm) holds more water than coarser types (particles averaging 4-8 mm).
If the substrate is too wet, reduce water. Keep notes on formulas for replicating
the best results.

## Jar Preparation

### Required Materials

- 1/2 pint (250 ml) tapered canning jars — Kerr wide-mouth or Ball regular-mouth
  half-pint. The jars must have tapered sides with no shoulders so cakes slide
  out easily. Jars larger than 1/2 pint are unreliable for the PF TEK.

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **[[query-how-to-grow-mushrooms-on-masters-mix-substrate]]**: Place vermiculite in a bowl, add brown rice powder on top,
   slowly add water directly onto the rice powder first. Mix thoroughly. The
   mixture should feel damp and cohesive. Mix each jar's substrate individually.

2. **Load jars**: Fill loosely, leaving 1/2 to 3/4 inch at the top. Level the
   substrate and wipe the jar interior clean. Fill the top with plain dry
   vermiculite and level it off. This [[pf-tek-dry-vermiculite-contaminant-barrier]] layer acts as a
   contaminant barrier — it protects the wet substrate from airborne
   contaminants and absorbs moisture transpiration.

3. **Prepare lids**: Punch 4 holes inside the periphery of the rubber sealing
   edge using a 3-penny nail and vise grips. Place lid with rubber edge up.

4. **Sterilize**: Steam sterilize in a covered pot with the jars elevated above
   the pot bottom (use a vegetable steamer insert). Keep lid bands loose during
   steaming. Steam at the lowest possible gentle boil for 1 hour. Avoid
   overheating, which dries the substrate.

## Inoculation

Jars must be cool to the touch before inoculation. Tighten lids and shake the
[[comparison-mushroom-liquid-culture-vs-spore-syringe]] well to redistribute spores. Remove tape from needle holes and
insert the needle, tilting the syringe body toward the jar center with the needle
tip touching the glass. This distributes spore solution down the side of the jar.
Inoculate a few drops per hole, using 1 cc total per jar (allowing one syringe
to inoculate 10 jars).

### Alcohol Flaming Technique

Use an alcohol flame (not a butane lighter, which leaves soot) to sterilize the
needle. Place a tequila shotglass upside down, add a few drops of denatured alcohol
to the hollow bottom, and ignite. Heat the needle in the blue flame for a few
seconds. Store syringes in a dark, cool place between uses.
