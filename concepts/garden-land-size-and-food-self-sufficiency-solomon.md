## Potatoes vs. Cereal Grains: A Caloric Efficiency Comparison

Solomon strongly advocates the potato (and sweet potato) as the foundation
crop for food self-sufficiency. Potatoes and sweet potatoes produce far more
calories per unit area than cereal grains. To produce the same amount of
nutrition by growing cereal grains (wheat, oats, barley, corn) would require
five to ten times as much land per person as relying on potatoes or sweet
potatoes.
