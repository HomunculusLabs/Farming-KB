# DOM (STP) — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Methylamphetamine

## Overview

DOM (PIHKAL #68) is a potent substituted amphetamine and classic psychedelic phenethylamine. It was one of the most notorious psychedelic amphetamines of the 1960s counterculture. Its street name "STP" allegedly stood for "Serenity, Tranquility, and Peace." DOM is a member of the DOx family (2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted amphetamines) and is notable for its exceptionally long duration of 14-20 hours, which contributed to many difficult experiences when it was distributed in the 1960s at dosages far above the effective range.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylamphetamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #68
- **Molecular formula:** C12H19NO2
- **HCl melting point:** 190.5-191.5 °C
- **Sulfate melting point:** 131 °C
- **Class:** Substituted amphetamine (DOx family)
- **CAS:** 6882-68-4
