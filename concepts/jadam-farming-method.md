## JADAM Farming Method

JADAM (Jayonul Damun Saramdul, meaning "people who are
like nature") is a revolutionary [[jadam-natural-pesticide-institute-and-research]]
Institute in 2002, systematically developing ultra-low-cost
methods through decades of research and field testing.

Cho's philosophical roots draw from Jesus Christ, Karl
Marx, liberation theology, Leonardo Boff's ecological
theology, and Laozi. Laozi's teaching of relaxation and
water-like persistence shaped JADAM's core design
principle: SESE (Simple, Easy, Scientific, Effective).

## Core Philosophy

JADAM's ultimate objective is to bring farming back to
farmers. Cho observed that agriculture worldwide is being
taken over by fertilizer and pesticide corporations. Farmers
have become mere consumers who buy all necessities from the
marketplace, losing control over their own fate.

JADAM reverses this by teaching farmers to make all their
own inputs. The system is designed so that anybody can
follow it, with costs ten to one hundred times cheaper than
conventional farming. Cho chose not to patent his
inventions, instead sharing all knowledge publicly.
