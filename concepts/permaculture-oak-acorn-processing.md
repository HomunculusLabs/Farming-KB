Updated: 2026-04-18 Oaks (Quercus spp.) are massive, long-lived trees providing timber, wildlife habitat, and a traditional food source — acorns — that predated grain flour by thousands of years. ## Species - Holm Oak (Quercus ilex) — warm climate, dryland tolerant - Swamp White Oak (Quercus bicolor) — moist areas - Burr Oak (Quercus macrocarpa) — hardiest, large sweet acorns with less tannin - Schuette's Oak (Quercus x schuettei) — hybrid with sweeter acorns - Cork Oak (Quercus suber) — evergreen, source of cork (bark regrows in 150-200 years) ## Uses - Wood and timber - Edible acorns (processed into flour) - Wildlife habitat ## Growing Conditions - Full sun - 75-100 feet (23-31 m) tall and wide - Most soil types - Select species appropriate to climate ## Propagation Oaks can be grown from acorns planted in the fall, either directly in the ground or in deep containers. Select acorns that sink in water — floaters are typically infested or empty. Plant acorns 1-2 inches deep with the pointed end down. Cold stratification occurs naturally in temperate climates; in milder regions, refrigerate moist acorns for 30-60 days before planting. Protect young seedlings from rodents with wire cages. Transplanting is best done when trees are young, as mature oaks develop extensive taproots that make relocation difficult. Grafting is possible but rarely practiced by home growers. ## [[comparison-companion-planting-reference-vs-jadam-monoculture-critique]] In  by providing late-season pollen from their catkins. ## Pests and Diseases

Acorn weevils are the most common pest, laying eggs inside developing acorns. Infested acorns typically float in water and should be discarded during processing. Oak wilt (Ceratocystis fagacearum) is a serious [[bloomfield-amphibian-decline-fungal-disease-climate-interactions]] that can kill mature trees — it spreads through root grafts and sap-feeding beetles. [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]] and anthracnose can affect leaves but rarely kill established trees. Oak leaf blister and gall wasps cause cosmetic damage. Management focuses on maintaining tree health through proper spacing, avoiding root damage during construction, and promoting biodiversity.

## Acorn Processing

1. **Collect** when fully formed and beginning to fall; discard acorns without caps or with holes (weevils)

2. **Dry** in oven, dehydrator, or sun

3. **Shell** — crack off the shell to access nutmeat

4. **Remove tannins** (critical — tannins are toxic in quantity):

   - Boil method: Boil in water, pour off dark water, repeat 5-6 times until water stays clear

   - Cold [[overnight-soak-method-homogeneous-grass-seed-substrate]]: Grind into flour, soak 1 part water to 3 parts flour in fridge for 1 week, changing water daily

5. **Dry** again after leaching

6. **Grind** into fine flour — use to replace up to half the flour in any recipe
