## Psilocybe coprophila

Psilocybe coprophila is a psilocybin-containing mushroom species
whose name reflects its distinctive ecological niche. The specific
epithet "coprophila" comes from the Greek words "kopros" meaning
dung and "philos" meaning loving, directly translating to
"dung-loving." This coprophilous (dung-inhabiting) lifestyle
defines much of the species' ecology and distribution.

### Fruiting Patterns

Coprophilous fungi like P. coprophila typically fruit relatively
quickly after dung is deposited, often within days to weeks. The
fruiting is stimulated by warm temperatures and high humidity, and

## Substrate Ecology

The [[edaphic-factors-microbial-community-structure]] within the dung is
dynamic, with different fungal species colonizing at different
stages of decomposition. Psilocybe coprophila is one of the earlier
colonizers, fruiting while the dung still retains significant
