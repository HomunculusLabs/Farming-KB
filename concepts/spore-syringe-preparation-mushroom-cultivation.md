## Step-by-Step Spore Syringe Preparation

### Step 1: Sterilize the Syringe and Water

Connect the syringe and needle and wrap them in tin foil. Fill a glass Pyrex container with approximately 80 mL of water and cover with a tin foil lid. Place both in the [[mushroom-cultivation-disabilities-substrate-bag-production]] — flame-sterilize the needle. Pass it through the spirit burner flame until the tip glows red. This kills any contaminants that may have contacted the needle between uses. Allow the needle to cool for a few seconds before injecting to avoid heat-killing the spores.

## Inoculation Methods

### Short Needle Method (Through the Bag)

This method is used when only a standard 4 cm needle is available:

1. Prepare the sterilized bag by taping support strips on each side (from can to bag base)
2. Swab both inoculation points with isopropyl alcohol using cotton wool buds
3. Place a piece of tape over each inoculation point, with a tab folded back for quick sealing after injection
4. Heat the needle tip until red-hot to penetrate the bag easily
5. Insert the needle fully into the bag below the filter level
6. Inject 5 mL of spore solution at each inoculation point
7. Swiftly roll the pre-placed tape over the hole as the needle is withdrawn

### Long Needle Method (Through the Filter Can)

This is the preferred method when a long needle is available:

1. Tape the filter can on both sides of the bag for security
2. Flame-sterilize the long needle
