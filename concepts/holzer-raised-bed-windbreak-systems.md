## Slope Considerations

On slopes, raised beds should be positioned at an angle to the slope rather
than parallel to it. When beds run parallel to the slope, the raised beds at
the top receive an excessive amount of water (danger of landslides), whilst
the lower ones begin to dry out. When beds are placed at an angle to the
slope, the water is distributed evenly and absorbed without channelling.

This principle is important for windbreak bed placement on slopes. The beds
