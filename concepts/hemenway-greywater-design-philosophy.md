## What is Greywater?

While most water leaving a home goes down the toilet as
"blackwater," the rest exits via sink, shower, and laundry
drain, contaminated only by a few drops of soap, dead skin,
and harmless bacteria. This is greywater: nearly pure but just
dirty enough to be unfit for direct human reuse. Plants and
soil organisms will gratefully accept the watery part of
greywater and eagerly consume the solid and dissolved contents
as food.

## Closing the Loop

clean water, mixes it with soap and food, and sends the dirty
result through a massive sewage system to an expensive
treatment plant and eventually into a river or ocean. This
converts valuable resources into garbage, yielding only a
fraction of their potential value.
