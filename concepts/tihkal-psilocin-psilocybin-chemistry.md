# Psilocin and Psilocybin Chemistry

## Chemical Identity

- **Psilocin** (4-HO-DMT): 4-hydroxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine, also known as PSOH, 4-indolol-3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]
- **Psilocybin** (4-PO-DMT): The phosphate ester of psilocin, also known as PSOP, 4-phosphoryloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine

These two compounds are entry #18 in TIHKAL, presented together because of their intimate chemical and pharmacological relationship.

### Psilocybin Synthesis
Historical syntheses used the O-benzyl ether protecting group approach:
1. 4-Hydroxyindole converted to 4-benzyloxyindole
2. Standard glyoxyl chloride/dimethylamine sequence
3. LAH reduction to 4-benzyloxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine
4. Catalytic debenzylation (H2, Pd/Al2O3) to yield psilocin
5. Additional steps for phosphate ester: dibenzyl chlorophosphonate + catalytic debenzylation

The phosphate ester conversion yields are "very bad, often less than 10%." Psilocybin is much more stable in air than psilocin and is water-soluble, but pharmacologically equivalent.

### Spectral Properties
- Psilocin MS: C3H8N+ m/z 58 (100%), parent ion m/z 204 (15%), indolemethylene+ m/z 146 (3%)
- Psilocybin IR: P=O stretch at 1110 cm-1; acidic OH stretches at 2400, 2700, 3200 cm-1
- Psilocybin MS: Identical to psilocin
