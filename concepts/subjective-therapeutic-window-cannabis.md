# Subjective Therapeutic Window for Cannabis Dosing

The subjective therapeutic window is the optimal dose range at which
cannabis produces desired [[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]] are directly related
to consuming cannabis in excess of this range. The concept applies the
principle that more is not better—with cannabis, taking too little is
sub-optimal while taking too much can actually increase the very symptoms
one is attempting to treat.

## The Core Principle

Cannabis follows an inverted dose-response curve for many of its
[[bay-11-medical-sativa-granddaddy-purp-pain-relief-oner]]. This biphasic response pattern makes precise
personal dosing critical.

The therapeutic window is "subjective" because it varies from person to
person based on individual [[blesching-cannabis-strain-selection-receptor-targeting]] and batch, and the mode of administration.

## Factors That Determine the Therapeutic Window

### Endocannabinoid State

The body's present endocannabinoid balance or deficiency influences how
cannabis will be experienced:

- **CB1 deficiency**: Patients with neurological disorders or mental
  health challenges may be lacking CB1 activation and would benefit from
  strains with a higher THC:[[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]] and batches contain different ratios of THC,
CBD, CBN, terpenes, and other constituents. Each batch may require
re-establishing the therapeutic window. A dose that works well with one
batch may be too strong or too weak with another.

### Mode of Administration

The method of consumption dramatically affects onset time, duration, and
the dosing process:

- **Inhalation** (smoking or vaporization): Near-instant onset; effects
  are shorter in duration; requires waiting 5-10 minutes between doses
- **Ingestion** (edibles, oils, tinctures): Delayed onset (45 minutes
  to 2 hours); effects last much longer and are noticeably different;
  requires waiting 1-2 hours between doses
- **Topical application**: Local effects; minimal systemic absorption;
  different dosing considerations

## Step-by-Step Dosing Protocol

### For Inhaled Cannabis

1. Take one short to medium inhalation
2. Wait 5 to 10 minutes
3. Assess: Did this dose achieve the desired effect?
4. If not, take another inhalation of about the same length
5. Wait another 5 to 10 minutes
6. Continue until the desired effect is achieved
7. Stop—do not inhale more once the therapeutic effect is reached

The line between an effective therapeutic dose and an adverse effect is
very thin with inhaled cannabis. Patience between doses is essential.
If [[adverse-effects-and-harm-reduction-psilocybin]] occur, wait a full day and start again at a lower
dosage.

### For Ingested Cannabis

1. Use an exact, measured amount, preferably on an empty stomach
2. Wait at least 1 hour (2 hours if taken on a full stomach) before
   increasing the dose
3. If no effect is felt, increase by the same measured amount
4. Wait another hour
5. Repeat until the desired effect is achieved

The most common mistake with edibles is impatience—thinking "I'm not
feeling anything" and continuing to eat until adverse effects occur.
