## Additional Problems

**Pot-bound (Root-bound):** Plant has outgrown the pot. The root mass
has reached maximum capacity, causing stress and various problems.
Solution: transplant to a bigger pot.

**Bad genetics:** Crap genetics exist with mutations, warping, flowering
problems, or poor germination. These can cause nutrient symptoms even
when no real deficiency exists. Solution: get new genetics.
