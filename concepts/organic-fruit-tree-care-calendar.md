## Organic Fruit Tree Care Calendar

Month-by-month guide to organic fruit tree care [[comparison-grafting-vs-budding-fruit-trees]], bushes, and canes (while dormant)
- **Spraying**: Winter wash (plant oil-based) to control overwintering pests and eggs on bark
- **Mulching**: Apply compost or well-rotted manure around trees (keep away from trunk)

## February

- **Pruning**: Continue winter pruning of apples and pears. Prune autumn-fruiting raspberries to ground level
- **Planting**: Last chance for bare-root planting
- **Forcing**: Bring potted rhubarb and strawberries under cover for early harvest
- **Spraying**: Apply copper spray if canker or bacterial canker is present

## March

### Bud Break
- **Pruning**: Finish winter pruning before buds swell. Prune blackcurrants (remove old wood)
- **Feeding**: Apply general [[hamilton-beneficial-insects-and-biological-pest-control]]**: Hang pheromone traps for [[holzer-frost-protection-techniques]]**: Cover blossoms with fleece on frost nights (critical for stone fruits)
- **Pest control**: Start monitoring for aphids. Encourage ladybugs
- **Weeding**: Keep area around trees clear of grass (grass competes for nutrients)
- **Feeding**: Apply high-potash organic feed to [[query-how-do-i-graft-fruit-trees-in-a-home-orchard]] dry spells (critical when fruit is swelling)
- **Soft fruit**: Pick summer-fruiting raspberries, strawberries, gooseberries
- **Feeding**: Liquid feed fruit in containers weekly (seaweed or [[comparison-comfrey-tea-vs-compost-tea]])
- **Pest control**: Monitor for codling moth, apply nematode sprays if needed

## July

### Harvest Begins
- **Summer pruning**: Continue pruning trained forms. Prune cherry plums after harvest
- **Harvest**: Early apples, plums, apricots, peaches, summer raspberries, currants
- **Propagation**: Take semi-hardwood cuttings of fruit bushes
- **Watering**: Critical month — fruit swelling rapidly
- **Pest control**: Remove any fruit with brown rot immediately (don't compost)

## August

### Main Harvest
- **Summer pruning**: Final summer pruning of apples and pears. Prune plums after harvest (avoid winter pruning — silver leaf risk)
- **Harvest**: Maincrop raspberries, blackberries, late gooseberries, early apples, pears
- **Planting**: Plant strawberry runners for next year
- **Training**: Tie in new growth on trained fruit

## September

### Late Harvest
- **Harvest**: Maincrop apples, pears, damsons, autumn raspberries
- **Storage**: Pick unblemished apples/pears for storage. Wrap individually in newspaper, store cool and dark
- **Planting**: Prepare ground for autumn planting
- **Pruning**: Prune blackcurrants (remove old dark wood, keep young light wood)
