## Soap Water

Soap water is a simple, effective pest control method.

### How to Make
1. Use natural, biodegradable soap (not detergent)
2. Dilute soap in water (ratio varies by soap type and pest)
3. Add a small amount of cooking oil to help the solution stick to leaves

### Application
- Spray directly onto affected plants
- Focus on undersides of leaves where pests hide
- Apply in early morning or late evening (avoid midday sun)
- Reapply after rain

### How It Works
- Soap breaks down the waxy coating of soft-bodied insects
- Causes dehydration and death
- Effective against: aphids, mites, whiteflies, thrips, mealybugs

## Hot Pepper Spray

### How to Make
1. Crush or blend hot peppers (cayenne, habanero, etc.)
2. Soak in water for 24+ hours
3. Strain through fine mesh or cheesecloth
4. Dilute with water (1:10 or as needed)
5. Add a small amount of soap to help adhesion

### Application
- Spray on plants as a repellent
- Reapply after rain
- Can be combined with garlic spray for stronger effect

### Effectiveness
- Repels most chewing insects
- Deters mammals (rabbits, deer)
- Fungal properties from capsaicin

## Integrated Pest Management in KNF

### Beneficial Insects
- Plant flowers that attract predatory insects (ladybugs, lacewings, parasitic wasps)
- Avoid broad-spectrum sprays that kill beneficials
- Provide habitat (ground cover, flowering plants)

### Physical Barriers
- Netting for birds and larger pests
- Row covers for seedlings
- Sticky traps for flying insects

### Cultural Practices
- Crop rotation
- Proper spacing for [[knf-fluorescent-insect-attractant]], [[knf-soap-water-and-hot-pepper]].
