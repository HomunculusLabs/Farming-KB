---# Psilocybe subaeruginosa in Australia and New Zealand

## Overview

Psilocybe subaeruginosa Cleland is one of the most significant psychoactive
mushroom species native to Australasia. First reported by Cleland in 1927 from
South Australia, New South Wales, and Victoria, this species has become one of
the most commonly used recreational mushrooms in southeastern Australia. The
presence of psilocybine in this species was detected in 1970, and three years
later, Dr. Malcolm Hall reported its use as a recreational drug.

## Distribution in Australia

psilocybe subaeruginosa australia has been documented from the following locations in
Australia:

- South Australia: Adelaide, Adelaide Hills, Mt. Lofty
- Australian Capital Territory: Canberra
- New South Wales: various locations including National Park and Belair
- Victoria: widespread occurrence
- Tasmania: Mt. Field National Park (on the pathway to Russell Falls)
