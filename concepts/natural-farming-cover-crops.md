### Leguminous Green Manures
The pea family provides excellent green manures including kudzu,
common vetch, bur clover, and various bean species. Leguminous green
manure suppresses summer weeds and enriches the soil through nitrogen
fixation. These crops are particularly valuable because they address
both weed suppression and soil fertility in a single planting.

### Combination Planting
Rotational cropping in combination with various green manure crops is,
in a sense, a mimicry of nature. Green manure crops combined with root
vegetables can improve and maintain soil fertility. When a field has
lost its fertility, rotation with green manure crops and root
vegetables can gradually restore productivity. A leguminous green
manure suppresses summer weeds and enriches the soil while the root
crops penetrate and aerate the soil profile.

Trees and their understory cover crops form a symbiotic community.

## Soil Building Through Ground Covers

Without grasses growing over the surface of the ground, rainwater
washes away tons of topsoil each year. In gently sloping areas, this
can result in the loss of several tons to over a hundred tons of soil
per year. In twenty to thirty years, topsoil washes entirely away,
reducing soil fertility to essentially zero. A ground cover of green
manure crops and the combination planting of vegetables and grains
maintains soil structure and prevents this catastrophic loss.

The roots of grasses and green manure crops work the soil down to
twelve inches or more, creating channels for air and water
penetration that no mechanical tillage can match. When combined with
