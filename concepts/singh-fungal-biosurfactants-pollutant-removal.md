# Fungal Biosurfactants for Pollutant Removal

Biosurfactants are amphiphilic compounds produced by microorganisms that
reduce surface and interfacial tension between immiscible phases. Fungal
biosurfactants play a crucial role in
[[singh-fungal-exopolysaccharides-biofilm-soil-aggregation]] including carbohydrate-protein
complexes act as bioemulsifiers. Liposan from *Candida lipolytica*
effectively emulsifies petroleum hydrocarbons.

### Fatty Acids and Lipopeptides

Many fungi secrete extracellular fatty acids and phospholipids with
surfactant properties. *Aspergillus* and *Penicillium* release free
fatty acids with emulsifying activity during growth on hydrophobic
substrates.

## Mechanisms of Pollutant Removal

### Solubilization and Micellization

Above their critical micelle concentration (CMC), biosurfactants
self-assemble into micelles with hydrophobic cores that solubilize
contaminants including [[singh-fungal-degradation-of-chlorinated-compounds]], and
[[singh-fungal-oxidation-petroleum-enzymes]]. Particularly
important for complex petroleum mixtures and
[[singh-fungal-bioaugmentation-contaminated-soils]] fungal strains.

### Cell Surface Modification

Fungal biosurfactants modify cell surface hydrophobicity, enhancing
direct contact between fungal cells and hydrophobic substrates.

## Key Fungal Biosurfactant Producers

| Fungal Species | Type | CMC (mg/L) | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Starmerella bombicola* | Sophorolipids | 50-100 | Hydrocarbon solubilization |
| *Candida lipolytica* | Liposan | Variable | Oil emulsification |
| *Pseudozyma aphidis* | MELs | 10-50 | PAH solubilization |
| *Aspergillus niger* | Fatty acids | Variable | Soil washing |
| *Yarrowia lipolytica* | Liposan-like | Variable | Oil degradation |

## Remediation Applications

### Enhanced Hydrocarbon Biodegradation

Fungal biosurfactants significantly enhance
[[singh-fungal-consortia-synergistic-biodegradation]],
biosurfactant producers support hydrocarbon-degrading partners.
Sophorolipid addition increases PAH degradation 2-10 fold.

### Soil Washing and Flushing

Biosurfactant-enhanced soil washing removes hydrophobic contaminants
through in-situ flushing or ex-situ washing. Fungal biosurfactants
offer advantages due to biodegradability and lower soil toxicity.

### Heavy Metal Removal

Anionic biosurfactant head groups complex with cationic metals; micelles
solubilize metal-organic complexes. Sophorolipids effectively remove
copper, zinc, and cadmium from contaminated solutions.

### Combined Biosurfactant-Enzyme Systems

Combining biosurfactant solubilization with
[[singh-fungal-biodegradation-of-polycyclic-aromatic-hydrocarbons]] and
[[singh-mushroom-cultivation-on-agricultural-wastes]] as carbon sources. Fed-batch
fermentation maximizes yield. Alternatively, in situ production through
direct inoculation eliminates separate production steps.

## Advantages Over Synthetic Surfactants

1. **Biodegradability**: Readily degraded by soil microorganisms
2. **Lower toxicity**: Non-toxic to plants, animals, and beneficial
   microbes at effective concentrations
3. **Extreme condition stability**: Effective across wide pH, temperature,
   and salinity ranges
4. **Structural diversity**: Tailorable for specific contaminants
