## Amnesia Haze: Genetics and History

Amnesia Haze is one of the most commercially successful sativa-dominant [[oner-southeast-asian-sativa-heritage-thai-vietnamese-cambodian]],
Jamaican,  and Afghan lineages,
with Haze genetics incorporated through one or more of these pathways.
The most commonly cited genetic breakdown is a cross between a Southeast Asian sativa (often described as a Thai or Laotian variety),
a Jamaican sativa (likely a Lamb's Bread descendant),  and an Afghan indica.

The Haze influence in Amnesia Haze comes primarily through the Southeast Asian sativa parent,
as the original Haze was itself a complex hybrid of Colombian,  Mexican,
Thai,  and South Indian landrace varieties.
This means that Amnesia Haze carries a diluted but significant portion of the original Haze genetic heritage,
which contributes to its tall stature,  long flowering period,
and powerful cerebral effects.

The Afghan indica parent provides counterbalancing characteristics that make Amnesia Haze somewhat easier to cultivate than pure tropical sativas.
The Afghan genetics contribute reduced flowering time,
increased bud density,
and improved resistance to mold and pests — all significant advantages in the European climate where the strain was developed and popularized.

## Morphological Characteristics

Amnesia Haze plants are tall and vigorously growing,
reflecting their predominantly sativa genetic background.
In indoor cultivation,  they typically reach 4 to 6 feet without training,
and considerably taller if given adequate vertical space.
The plant structure features substantial lateral branching with long internodal spacing.
The leaves are characteristically sativa in morphology — narrow,
finger-like leaflets with long,  thin blades.

The buds are moderately dense for a sativa-dominant variety,
reflecting the Afghan genetic influence that adds some indica characteristics to the bud structure.
Individual calyxes are medium to large and covered in a generous layer of resin glands.
The pistils are typically long and orange-brown,
creating attractive color contrast against the light to medium green foliage.
