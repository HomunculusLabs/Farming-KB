### Modern Caveat
Know what was used to make the compost. Many chemicals we seek to avoid do not break down quickly enough in compost. Compost should ideally be tested for nutrient content and microbial activity.

## Matching Compost to Plant Needs

You can best satisfy a plant's nutrient needs by adding compost with the right microbial domination:

- **Vegetables and annuals**: Prefer bacterially dominated soils (bacteria release nitrogen in nitrate form, preferred by these plants)
- **Trees, shrubs, and perennials**: Prefer fungally dominated soils (fungi release nitrogen in ammonium form, preferred by woody plants)
- **Lawns**: Bacterial dominance
- **Orchards and forest gardens**: Fungal dominance

## Compost Application Methods

### Surface Application (Recommended)
Applying compost to the soil surface allows the organisms in the compost to spread into the soil naturally. The biology travels downward, establishing populations throughout the root zone.
