# Mescaline: Peyote and San Pedro Cactus Guide

Mescaline is a naturally occurring psychedelic found in several cactus species, most notably Peyote (Lophophora williamsii) and San Pedro (Trichocereus pachanoi). Mescaline belongs to the [[ecstasy-mdma-guide]], [[peyote-ceremony]].

## Dosage

| Form | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Mescaline sulfate | ~500 mg | Full experience; take in two half-doses 30 min apart |
| Mescaline HCl | ~350 mg | ~25% more potent than sulfate |
| Peyote buttons | 3-15 | Varies widely; some need 24+ |
| San Pedro | 10" x 3" section | One piece approximately this size |

Cactus is most potent when harvested during hotter months. See [[lsd-psychedelic-experience-guide]]
- More visual than mushrooms or acid (especially synthetic mescaline)
- Links user with collective evolutionary consciousness
- Feels more ancient, spiritual, and personal than analytical LSD
- Enhanced sense of smell (perceiving individual plant scents)
- Can produce auditory hallucinations (unlike LSD which mainly heightens hearing)
- Duration: 6-14 hours
- Smoother comedown than LSD or mushrooms
- No "neural overload" or drained feeling afterwards

### Peyote vs. San Pedro
- **San Pedro**: Very smooth, flowing experience
- **Peyote**: First 2 hours are dream-like, drifting, almost delirium; groggy and sleepy; emetic quality causes nausea ~2 hours in; overall more body-oriented
