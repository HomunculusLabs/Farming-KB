## Soil Food Web Assessment

Soil food web assessment is the process of analyzing the living biology in soil, compost, or [[savory-living-organisms-as-tools]] from dormant ones
- Can identify bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and nematodes by morphology
- Can differentiate active from total biomass
- Can assess [[aact-disease-suppression-mechanisms-ingham]]: "If conditions are perfect for mildew, black spot, etc., then the fungal component appears to be critical for prevention"

### Protozoa
- Three groups: **flagellates** (smallest), **amoebae** (medium), **ciliates** (largest)
- Protozoa consume bacteria and release plant-available nutrients (primarily nitrogen)
- **Ciliates too high** indicates anaerobic or compacted conditions -- they are an indicator of poor soil health
- Flagellates and amoebae are the primary nutrient cyclers

### Nematodes
- Four functional groups: **bacterial-feeders**, **fungal-feeders**, **root-feeders**, **predators**
- Bacterial- and fungal-feeders cycle nutrients
- Root-feeders indicate soil health problems (compaction, anaerobic conditions)
- Predators regulate populations of other nematodes
- Must be extracted from soil (not observed by direct microscopy of suspensions)

## Assessment Process

1. **Collect soil sample** from the root zone (0-6 inches for annuals, deeper for perennials)
2. **Send to SFI or qualified lab** for direct microscopy analysis
3. **Review report**: Note the desired ranges on the bottom row. Are all organism groups in range or higher?
4. **Determine corrections**: If any group is too low, add the organisms or their food resources
5. **Check ratios**: Bacterial:fungal ratio should match the desired plant type
6. **Monitor over time**: Re-test seasonally or after major interventions

## Bacterial:Fungal Ratios by Plant Type

| Plant/Succession Stage | Bacterial:Fungal Ratio |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| Annual vegetables, brassicas | Bacterial dominated (~10:1 or higher) |
| Grasses, pasture | Bacterial dominated |
| Row crops (corn, wheat) | Slightly bacterial (~3:1 to 10:1) |
| [[comparison-perennial-vegetables-vs-cover-crops]] | Balanced (1:1 to 3:1) |
| Shrubs, berries | Fungal dominated (~1:3 to 1:10) |
| Deciduous trees | Fungal dominated |
| Conifers | Strongly fungal dominated |
