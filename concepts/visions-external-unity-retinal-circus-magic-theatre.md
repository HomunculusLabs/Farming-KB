# Visions of External Unity, the Retinal Circus, and the Magic Theatre

Visions 5 through 7 of [[the-second-bardo-and-hallucinatory-visions]] represent the mind's progressive departure from
one-pointed concentration into increasingly dramatized displays of consciousness content.
As the voyager's awareness loosens its grip on unity, the content of mind externalizes
into vivid sensory and archetypal panoramas. Each vision offers both a liberation and a
trap — freedom for those who flow without attachment, confusion for those who grasp.

## Vision 5: The Vibratory Waves of External Unity

The fifth vision confronts the voyager with radiant unity — an overwhelming sense of
oneness with all energy. Reality is experienced as a single pulsating field of vibration,
and the boundary between self and cosmos dissolves entirely. Every form is understood as
a temporary pattern within this undifferentiated energy ocean.

### The Plastic Doll Phenomena

Within this vision, familiar forms may appear inorganic, dull, and mass-produced — what
Leary calls the "plastic doll" phenomena. People, objects, and settings lose their
customary vitality and reveal themselves as hollow shells, inert configurations of energy
rather than genuinely solid entities. This strips away the comforting illusion of
permanence that ordinary consciousness depends upon.

### Connection to Ratnasambhava and the Earth Element

The Tibetan Buddhist tradition associates this vision with [[ratnasambhava]], the Buddha of
the south, and the earth element. Ratnasambhava embodies the wisdom of equality — the
recognition that all phenomena share the same essential nature. The earth element
grounding suggests that even in the most destabilizing experiences, there is an underlying
stability and foundational equanimity the voyager can rest upon through surrender.

### The Terror of Transience

The epistemological revelation of Vision 5 is that everything — every thought, every
object, every identity — is merely a momentary cluster of energy, fleeting and
insubstantial. This is the ultimate fear: the discovery that there is nothing to hold
onto, no permanent self, no enduring reality beneath the dance of appearances. For the
ego-attached mind, this groundlessness is terrifying.

### The Solution: Swinging with the Wave Dance

The instruction for Vision 5 is to swing with the wave dance — to cease resisting the
vibratory flow and instead become the dance itself. Liberation comes not from intellectual
understanding but from fully participating in the vision, moving with the rhythms of
energy rather than struggling to maintain a fixed position. The voyager who lets go
discovers that transience is not a source of dread but of profound freedom.

## Vision 6: The Retinal Circus

Vision 6 unfolds as a kaleidoscopic extravaganza of game-reality. The closed eyes become
screens upon which the entire drama of sensory experience plays out — hence "the retinal
circus," a carnival of perception where the usual rules of sensory processing no longer
apply.

### Synesthesia and Primary Process Thinking

A hallmark of Vision 6 is the complete interchangeability of senses — synesthesia in its
purest form. Colors are heard, sounds are felt, tastes have shapes. This collapse of
sensory boundaries reflects primary process thinking, where logical categories dissolve
and everything becomes metaphorically connected to everything else.

### Transformation of Subjects and Objects

Within the retinal circus, the distinction between subject and object breaks down. Inner
thoughts externalize into visual forms and outer stimuli fold inward into felt sensations.
Personal memories and racial memories bubble up from the unconscious, surfacing as vivid
scenes and characters that carry emotional charge far beyond their literal content.

### The Computer with Unlimited Programs

Leary offers a powerful metaphor: the brain as a computer with unlimited programs. Every
possible configuration of experience, every game that consciousness has devised, can run
simultaneously. The retinal circus is the display screen for this computational
extravagance, showing the voyager the staggering range of reality-constructions available
to the mind.

### The Negative Trap and the Motto

The unprepared voyager interprets the retinal circus as confused chaos — a meaningless
flood of disconnected images. The ego tries to organize the kaleidoscope into familiar
categories and fails, producing anxiety. The guiding instruction is: "Relax and accept
whatever comes." The retinal circus rewards only those who watch without grasping.

## Vision 7: The Magic Theatre

Vision 7 elevates the psychedelic drama to its mythological dimension. The Magic Theatre
is the stage upon which heroic figures, demigods, and archetypal beings enact the cosmic
drama of consciousness encountering its own depths. Here the personal merges with the
supra-individual, and the voyager witnesses the archetypal patterns underlying all human
culture and experience.

### Knowledge-Holding Deities and Dakinis

In the Tibetan framework, Vision 7 corresponds to the appearance of Knowledge-Holding
Deities (Vidyadharas) and Dakinis — fierce and radiant beings embodying the wisdom
energies of the awakened mind. These are projections of the voyager's own deepest
capacities for insight, appearing in terrifying and beautiful forms to test equanimity.

### Archetypal Figures from World Mythologies

The Magic Theatre draws upon the entire treasury of human mythological imagination. Gods
and goddesses, heroes and monsters from every tradition may appear as living presences
with immediate emotional force — Greek, Hindu, Buddhist, Christian, indigenous — each
a doorway into a different mode of being and knowing.

### The Cosmic Theatre and Divine Comedy

Leary frames Vision 7 as the Cosmic Theatre, a grand Divine Comedy in which the voyager
is simultaneously audience, actor, and playwright. The images of great painters — Blake's
[[amazonian-ship-aceropunta-and-visionary-cosmology]], Bosch's teeming hells, Tibetan thangkas — may flood consciousness,
as though the entire history of [[mnemonic-patterns-and-sacred-art]] were projected onto the screen of awareness.

### The Merging of Personal and Supra-Individual Consciousness

The culmination of Vision 7 is the merging of personal consciousness with supra-individual
awareness. The mythological drama is not separate from the voyager's mind but the very
structure of mind itself revealed. The boundary between individual psyche and collective
unconscious dissolves: all stories are one story, all consciousness a single vast theatre.

## The Structure of the Later Second Bardo Visions

Visions 5 through 7 chart the mind's progressive inability to maintain one-pointed
concentration after the initial unity experiences. As concentration weakens, consciousness
externalizes and dramatizes: first into vibratory energy unity (Vision 5), then into the
sensory carnival of the retinal circus (Vision 6), and finally into the full mythological
spectacle of the Magic Theatre (Vision 7). Each stage is both a descent from pure
awareness and a further unfolding of creative potential. The key at every stage is the
same: non-attachment, surrender, and the willingness to let each vision arise and pass
without grasping or fleeing.
