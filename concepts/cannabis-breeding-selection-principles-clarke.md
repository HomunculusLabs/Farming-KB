## The Danger of Unconscious Selection

A grower who selects the best seeds each year and grows them under the
same conditions may see improvement the first year but subsequent
degeneration. This illustrates unconscious selection for undesirable
traits. The hypothetical cultivator began well by selecting the best

## Hybrid Vigor and Its Pitfalls

The tendency is for many of the dominant characteristics from both
parents to be transmitted to the F1 offspring, resulting in particularly
large and vigorous plants. This increased vigor due to recombination of
dominant genes often raises the cannabinoid level of the F1 offspring,
but hybridization also opens up the possibility that undesirable
(usually recessive) genes may form pairs and express their
characteristics in the F2 offspring.

Hybrid vigor may also mask inferior qualities due to abnormally rapid
growth. During the second season, random pollinations again account for

traits while selecting for favorable ones, the unconscious breeding of
