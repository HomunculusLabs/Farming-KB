## Water-Soluble Potassium (WS-K)

WS-K is a KNF input made from tobacco stem charcoal steeped in water. Potassium activates starch-synthesizing enzymes, regulates stomata and water use, and translocates photosynthates to fruit. It is critical during reproductive growth for fruit quality, sugar content, and disease resistance.

## What Potassium Does

- Activates starch-synthesizing enzymes
- Facilitates translocation of photosynthate to storage organs
- Regulates stomata opening/closing (potassium levels in guard cells)
- Promotes CO2-fixing enzyme synthesis
- Controls turgidity of protoplasm
- Improves resistance to drought and freezing
- Reduces lodging, increases yield, improves crop quality
- Exists as highly mobile ion in plants — translocates from old to young tissue

## Deficiency Symptoms

- Appears on older leaves first (K is mobile)
- Branch growth deterioration, stunted maturity
- Rare in early stages — usually appears after plants reach a certain size
- Chlorosis from old leaf edges turning yellowish-brown
- In some plants, chlorosis appears as spots in the middle of leaves
- Roots and stems become thin, vascular bundle lignification suppressed
- Seeds small, mature late
- Exacerbated by high lime/magnesium in soil or sandy low-humus soils

## Excess Symptoms

- Induces magnesium deficiency
- Similar to gas disorder — appears in middle of leaves

## Recipe

### Materials
- Tobacco stems (dried)
- Water
- Clay jar or Japanese cedar bucket
- Hemp cloth bag
- Porous paper, rubber band

### Process
1. **Dry and grind**: Dry tobacco stems and grind coarsely (not powder)
2. **Bag**: Put 1-2 kg of ground tobacco stem in a hemp cloth bag
3. **Soak**: Submerge in 100L of water
4. **Wait**: About 7 days — produces crude liquid of natural potassium
5. **Strain**: Remove bag; liquid is WS-K

### Environmental Conditions
- Optimum temperature: 23-25C
- Cool, shaded area, no direct sunlight
- Minimal environmental fluctuation

## Application Rates

| Use Case | Dilution | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| WS-K alone | 0.7L crude in 20L water | Standard dose |
| Use ALONE only | — | Do not mix with nitrogen sources |

## Critical Warning

WS-K must be used alone. If applied with nitrogen in hot/humid conditions or on rainy days, the synergy effect causes plants to absorb excessive nitrogen, leading to overgrowth. Always consider weather conditions before applying.

## When to Apply

- **Reproductive growth stage**: Primary use — fruit quality and sugar content
- **Fruit enlargement period**: Potassium demand peaks
- **Pre-harvest**: Improves starch/sugar accumulation
