### Nameko Log Culture
Similar to Shiitake but logs should be partially buried in a high peat moss soil base due to Nameko's higher moisture requirements. Broad-leaf hardwoods, especially beech, poplar, and deciduous oaks.

### Reishi Log Culture
Hardwood logs laid directly on ground or shallowly buried in shady, naturally moist locations. Cover with hoop-frames and shade cloth. First harvests: 6 months to 2 years. Production continues 4-5 years. Works best in high-humidity climates (e.g., southeastern US).

### Lion's Mane Log Culture
Walnut logs are particularly suitable. Logs 3-4 feet long buried 1/3 in shaded ground. Hardwood logs of oak, maple, and beech also viable. Long production cycle but low maintenance.

## Stump Cultivation
Hardwood stumps can be inoculated using plug or sawdust spawn. Reishi grows prolifically on stumps. If other fungi have already colonized the stump, production is inhibited. Best with fresh-cut stumps. Species suited: Reishi, Shiitake, Oyster, Maitake.
