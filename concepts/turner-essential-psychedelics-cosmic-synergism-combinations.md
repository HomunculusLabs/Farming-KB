## Key Combinations Documented

### LSD + Ketamine + N,N-DMT

Turner started with 200 mcg of acid, injected 100 mg of ketamine two hours
later, then smoked DMT after the ketamine journey. The ketamine produced one of
his best journeys, lasting about 1.5 hours. However, DMT was diminished in
intensity by lingering ketamine, and the ketamine recovery period was extended
by another 1-2 hours by the acid. On subsequent attempts, Turner took ketamine
toward the end of the acid session, finding the recovery period much less
unpleasant, particularly if remaining in bed until falling asleep.

### LSD + Syrian Rue + N,N-DMT + 5-MeO-DMT + Nitrous Oxide + Cannabis

Described as "absolutely beautiful." The combination of Harmala + LSD + DMT (in
either form) forms the basis. Compared with a typical LSD trip, there was
vastly more space or information to explore, and it felt infused with a
spiritual quality.

### Mushrooms + Syrian Rue + N,N-DMT + Nitrous Oxide + Ketamine

The mushrooms + [[syrian-rue]] was medium strength and pleasant. DMT added
especially to the visual context. The ketamine at the end overpowered what
remained of the previous high but was pleasantly enhanced with a wider range of
feelings than ketamine alone. Turner was semi-consciously aware of his body
chanting and moving about -- common with DMT + Syrian Rue but previously unseen
on ketamine for him. He later decided against combining ketamine with natural
psychedelics.

### LSD + Ketamine in Death Valley

On 350 mcg of LSD while backpacking in Death Valley, Turner experienced a
remarkable evolutionary vision. Observing lizards and geological formations, he
began seeing creatures from progressively earlier eras. His mind progressed
back through the evolutionary line until time spread to infinity: "I had become
the One Mind onto which all the experiences of time have been etched." Later,
on ketamine at the salt flats, he felt he became part of the earth and
penetrated to levels deep within the "mind" of planetary consciousness.

### 2C-B + Mushrooms (+ Ketamine)

This was the first psychedelic combination Turner tried that did not work well.
The universes of both 2C-B and mushrooms seemed to compete rather than
complement. With a larger second attempt (50 mg 2C-B + 10g mushrooms), the
experience was described as "two massive entities at war." Turner concluded that
2C-B + mushrooms has a tendency to push the user into a state of extreme
insanity.
