# Turner Psychedelic Combination Experience Reports

Detailed experience reports from [[turner-lsd]]'s experimentation with combining different psychedelics. These complement the general [[psychedelic-combination-guide]] with specific trip accounts. See [[psychedelic-safety-and-harm-reduction]] for risk awareness and [[psychedelic-crisis-response-protocol]] if difficulties arise during combinations.

## LSD + Ludiomil (Maprotiline)

### First Experiment
- Ludiomil taken 2 hours into the [[lsd-compound-profile]] trip; took effect ~15 minutes
- Produced a "platinum plated" acid trip — "every perception and thought coated with silverish translucent light"
- Described as a "thin invisible membrane between myself and anything my senses touched upon"
- Ludiomil taken after LSD produced "the most distinct nitrous high"
- Nitrous oxide during this combination produced "totally hilarious, melting through the bottom of the universe" — most distinct nitrous high ever experienced
- Significantly increased dream activity during subsequent nights

### Second Experiment (Ludiomil 5 hours before LSD)
- Allowed closer look at the "membrane" between mind and world
- Perceived membrane as "spongy, or even spring-loaded barrier"
- Ludiomil seems to create a "time-delay buffer zone" preventing rapid responses and changes in mind

### LSD + Ludiomil + Ketamine (45 mg IM)
- Produced "quite unique Ketamine experience" with more freedom to shift between individual and infinite perspectives than ever before

## LSD + Ketamine (Death Valley)

### Setting
- Backpacking in Death Valley, 350 mcg LSD
- 350 mcg normally only produced peripheral visual phenomena for Turner (high tolerance)
- Observing lizards and volcanic rock formations influenced the experience
- Observing lizards led to trying to synchronize with their mind; their time sense and information flow was "worlds apart from that of a human"
- Volcanic rock formations in canyon — web-shaped mineral ridges revealed terrain history
- Warning: psychedelics in wilderness can be dangerous; they can disrupt survival instincts like sense of direction and time

### Experience
- Became aware of evolutionary time — saw saber tooth tigers, bear-like forest creatures, dinosaurs
- "I began to feel that in times past I had been each of the animals that I saw"
- Time spread out to infinity; all experiences simultaneously present
- "I had become the One Mind onto which all the experiences of time have been etched"

### Ketamine at Salt Flats
- Administered at the lowest elevation in the United States
- "I felt that I became part of the earth and penetrated to levels deep within the 'mind' of the planetary consciousness"

## 2C-B + Mushrooms

### First Attempt (25 mg 2C-B + 5 g mushrooms)
- Produced feeling of "dopey" or "drugged" — unfocused state
- Brief lucid moment: "two different parallel realities coexisting in the same physical space-time"
- Added Ketamine (final dose): produced less spectacular K trip than with either substance alone

### Second Attempt (50 mg 2C-B + 10 g mushrooms) — WARNING
- **Strongly negative experience**
- Felt as though "a mesh of interference patterns was placed before my awareness"
- Also had a feeling of being hexed — "some force preventing me from venturing into an expanded state of awareness"
- 2C-B and mushrooms "trying to alter consciousness in opposing directions"
- Felt like "a serious slap in the face, an infusion of the states of mind and reality tunnels that some of life's less fortunate people have to endure"
- Longest lasting and most unpleasant trip in years
- Ketamine initially relieved it via ego death, but negativity spread into the Ketamine domain
- **Conclusion: "2C-B + mushrooms is a combination not to be messed with"**
- Turner notes a powerful shaman might control this combination (South American shamans control Belladonna combinations), but since 2C-B is modern synthetic, no centuries-old shamanic tradition exists
- An omen appeared: large black spider on the center of his marble coffee table
- Previous omens: bat flying into his home on Halloween

## 2C-B + Ketamine

### General
- The combination has been valuable for creating a "bridge" between normal reality and the bizarre world of the [[ketamine-compound-profile]] experience
- Two streams crossing at right angles, producing "uniquely new universe of experience"
- Works well with small (25 mg) or large (100 mg) Ketamine doses
- With smaller K doses: no Ketamine journey, but relaxed immersion in flowing psychedelic mental fireworks
- Ketamine recovery period diminished — ease of movement, no nausea
- Sometimes "incredible physical energy" emerging from K journey
- "Frequently emerged from the Ketamine journey and immediately begun dancing about like a whirling dervish, or assuming yogic asanas"
- Significantly increased recollection of Ketamine domain when combined with 2C-B — useful for understanding and integrating K experiences

## Mushrooms + Syrian Rue + DMT + Nitrous + Ketamine

### Experimental Session
- Experimental session exceeded expectations
- Mushrooms + [[beta-carboline-chemistry]] (an [[mao-inhibitors-and-psychedelics]]) was medium strength, pleasant but not overwhelming
- DMT added especially in visual context
- Nitrous blasts felt great
- Ketamine (75 mg) overpowered remaining high but was pleasantly enhanced with wider range of feelings
- Semi-consciously aware of body chanting and moving — common with DMT + Syrian Rue but not previously with Ketamine
- Turner later decided this combination was not appropriate (K + natural psychedelics warning)
