## Harvesting

Pick cherries as they ripen from early to mid-summer depending on species and variety. Eat raw, dry for storage, or process into pies and preserves. Nanking cherries are typically ready in July. Use bird netting to protect ripe fruit.

## Yields

A mature Nanking cherry bush can produce 8-15 pounds of fruit per year. Dwarf varieties produce slightly less but are easier to manage and harvest. Yields increase significantly after the third year.

## Pests and Diseases

Birds are the primary pest — they will strip bushes clean when fruit ripens. Netting is the most effective protection. Cherry aphids, leaf spot, and powdery mildew can affect some varieties. Good air circulation and proper pruning help prevent disease. See [[permaculture-tagasaste]]
