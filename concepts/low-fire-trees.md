## Overview

Trees and shrubs with low combustibility can be incorporated into permaculture designs as fire barriers and to protect structures, livestock, and sensitive plantings. Species listed below are generally considered to have low fire potential; an asterisk (*) denotes conflicting reports.

## Trees and Shrubs

### Acacia (Wattles)
- Acacia baileyana* (Cootamundra wattle)
- A. cyanophylla (western wattle)
- A. cyclops (coastal wattle)
- A. glandulicarpa (hairy pod wattle)
- A. howittii (sticky wattle)
- A. ileaphylla (Gawler Range wattle)
- A. melanoxylon* (blackwood)
- A. pravissima (Ovens acacia)
- A. prominens (golden rain wattle)
- A. saligna (golden wreath wattle)

### Acer (Maples)
- A. campestre, A. ginnala, A. negundo (box elder), A. opalus, A. palmatum, A. platanoides, A. pseudoplatanus (sycamore, recovers)

- C. cunninghamiana (recovers), C. fraserana, C. glauca (recovers), C. obesa, C. stricta (recovers), C. torulosa
