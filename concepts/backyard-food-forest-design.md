# Backyard Food Forest Design

A food forest is a multi-layered edible garden modeled on forest ecosystems. Even a small backyard (1/8 to 1/4 acre) can support a productive food forest that provides fruit, nuts, vegetables, herbs, and medicinal plants year-round with minimal maintenance.

## The Seven Layers

### Canopy (30-50+ ft)
- Large fruit and nut trees
- Species: standard apple, pear, walnut, pecan, chestnut, mulberry
- Spacing: 25-40 ft apart
- Backyard consideration: most backyards can only fit 1-2 canopy trees

### Understory (15-30 ft)
- Smaller fruit trees, dwarf fruit trees
- Species: dwarf apple, plum, cherry, peach, apricot, persimmon, pawpaw
- Spacing: 10-20 ft apart
- These are the backbone of most backyard food forests

### Shrub Layer (5-15 ft)
- Berry bushes and fruiting shrubs
- Species: blueberry, raspberry, blackberry, currant, gooseberry, elderberry
- Place at edges and between trees for sun/partial shade

### Herbaceous Layer (1-5 ft)
- Perennial vegetables, herbs, flowers
- Species: asparagus, rhubarb, comfrey, artichoke, mint, oregano, thyme
- Fill all gaps between shrubs and trees

### Ground Cover (0-1 ft)
- Spreading plants that suppress weeds and retain moisture
- Species: strawberry, creeping thyme, clover, sweet potato, nasturtium
- Living mulch — replaces wood chip mulch in established forests

### Root Layer
- Edible roots and tubers
- Species: potato, sweet potato, Jerusalem artichoke, garlic, onions, shallots
- Place in dedicated sunny areas between trees

### Vine Layer (vertical)
- Climbing plants on trellises, fences, or trees
- Species: grape, kiwi, passionflower, hops, climbing beans, cucumbers
- Use vertical space — espalier fruit trees combine canopy and vine functions

## Design Process

### Site Assessment
- Sun mapping: track sun/shade patterns through the year
- Soil test: pH, nutrients, drainage
- Water: irrigation needs, rainfall, greywater potential
- Existing vegetation: what to keep, what to remove
- Zone 1 placement: herbs, salad greens, daily harvest items near the house

### Plant Selection for Small Yards (1/8 acre)
1. 1-2 dwarf fruit trees (apple, plum, or cherry)
2. 3-5 berry bushes (blueberry, raspberry, elderberry)
3. 10+ perennial herbs and vegetables
4. Ground cover (strawberry, clover)
5. 1-2 vines (grape on fence, kiwi on arbor)
6. Nitrogen fixers (comfrey, seaberry, Siberian pea shrub)

### Guild Design
Plant support groups around each tree:
- Fruit tree (center)
- Nitrogen fixer (comfrey, seaberry, or Siberian pea)
- Dynamic accumulator (comfrey, yarrow, borage)
- Pest confuser (alliums, aromatic herbs)
- Ground cover (clover, strawberry)
- Pollinator attractor (flowers in umbel and composite families)

## Maintenance
- Year 1-2: Heavy mulching, regular watering, weeding
- Year 3-5: Reducing inputs, ground covers fill in
- Year 5+: Minimal — mostly harvesting, occasional pruning, mulch top-up
- Chop-and-drop: cut comfrey and dynamic accumulators 3-4 times per year as mulch
