## Long-Term Storage: Drying

Drying removes enough water to inactivate enzymes and microorganisms. Fresh
mushrooms have 70-95% moisture content; dried mushrooms approach 10%.
Dried mushrooms have good flavor, convenient long-term storage, and easier
transportation.

### Sun Drying

Mushrooms are spread on shelves with gills facing upward, directly exposed to
sunlight. Drying time varies with weather conditions. Quality is generally
lower than thermal or hot-air methods. Moisture content remains higher,
increasing susceptibility to molds and pests. This method is most accessible
to small-scale growers without specialized equipment.

### Thermal Power Drying

Begin at 30-35C depending on weather conditions. After 5-7 hours, gradually
raise temperature to 40-60C and maintain for 12-18 hours. Initial low
temperature prevents case-hardening and enzyme damage. Drying enhances both
flavor and appearance of the preserved product.
