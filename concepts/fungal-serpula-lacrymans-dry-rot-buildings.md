## Entry Points and Building Invasion

The most favorable condition for dry rot establishment is direct contact
between wooden structural elements and soil. Posts sunk into the ground
without concrete foundations, soil accumulation beneath stucco exteriors,
and exterior particle board in contact with earth all provide entry
points. Cracks in concrete slabs and holes for pipes or electrical
conduits also serve as gateways. Even in regions without dry rot,
piling soil against foundations invites problems from both fungi and
termites.
