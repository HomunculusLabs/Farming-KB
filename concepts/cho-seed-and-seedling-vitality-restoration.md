Seed quality is the foundation of farming success in Cho's Natural
Farming system. A strong seed produces a healthy plant throughout
its entire life cycle. Cho observed that seeds grown under harsh
conditions develop superior toughness, strength, and adaptability
compared to seeds bred in controlled artificial environments.

Modern commercial seeds, by contrast, are bred in artificial
conditions and treated with chemicals. Cho argued that these
seeds cannot grow without human protection and fail to reach
their full potential even when given scientific treatments. They
have been weakened by dependency on chemical inputs and
controlled environments that shield them from natural selection
pressures.

## The Problem with Commercial Seeds

Cho identified several problems with the conventional seed
industry. Seeds in the market are bred for uniformity and
yield under chemical-intensive conditions, not for resilience
or [[teaming-with-nutrients-natural-vs-synthetic-fertilizers]] and pesticides.

The conventional practice of soaking seeds for long periods
before broadcast causes seeds to lose nutrients through
leaching. Seeds that have been pre-soaked in water for extended
durations lose vitality because essential compounds dissolve
into the soak water. This is a particular problem for farmers
who follow traditional seed [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]] seed treatment is not to add
foreign substances but to restore the seed's innate vitality
that has been suppressed by industrial processing.

When seedlings are overgrown and soft, one milliliter of
Water-Soluble Calcium (WsCa) is added instead. The calcium
strengthens cell walls and promotes structural rigidity in
plants that have grown too rapidly under high nitrogen
conditions. This corrective application prevents lodging and
improves the seedling's ability to withstand transplanting
stress and environmental challenges.

These conditional additions illustrate Cho's principle of
diagnosing the plant's condition before applying inputs. The
same base formula serves different purposes depending on what
the seedling needs, demonstrating the flexibility within the
Natural Farming system.
