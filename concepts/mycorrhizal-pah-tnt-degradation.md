# Mycorrhizal PAH and TNT Degradation

Mycorrhizal fungi contribute to the degradation of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and 2,4,6-trinitrotoluene (TNT) through their enzymatic capabilities and symbiotic relationships with host plants.
