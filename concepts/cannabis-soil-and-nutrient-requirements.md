# Cannabis Soil and Nutrient Requirements

Cannabis is a heavy feeder with specific nutritional demands that change throughout its life cycle. Understanding soil composition, [[ph-and-nutrient-availability-garden-soils]], and the soil food web is essential for maximizing growth and quality.

## Growing Mediums

### Soil (Living Soil)
- Most traditional and forgiving medium.
- Contains [[forest-and-bamboo-beneficial-microorganisms]] that cycle nutrients.
- Ideal pH: 6.0-6.8.
- "Super soil" recipes create a nutrient-rich, living medium that requires minimal additional feeding.

### Coco Coir
- Made from coconut husk fibers.
- Excellent water retention and aeration.
- Near-inert medium (no nutrients); requires complete nutrient program.
- Ideal pH: 5.5-6.5.
- Often buffered with [[dynamic-accumulator-plants-calcium-and-magnesium]].

### Hydroponics
- Plants grow in inert media (clay pebbles, rockwool, perlite) or directly in [[cannabis-nutrient-solution-preparation]].
- Fastest growth rates but least forgiving of mistakes.
- Requires careful monitoring of pH (5.5-6.0), EC, and [[cervantes-ec-tds-nutrient-solution-strength]] temperature.

### Perlite and Vermiculite
- Amendments used to [[query-how-do-fungi-improve-soil-structure]].
- **Perlite**: Improves drainage and aeration.
- **Vermiculite**: Improves water and nutrient retention.

## Macronutrients

### Nitrogen (N)
- **Role**: Essential for chlorophyll, amino acids, and protein synthesis. Primary driver of [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]].
- **Deficiency**: Lower leaves yellow (chlorosis), starting from tips and moving inward. Stunted growth.
- **Toxicity**: Dark green leaves, "clawing" (leaf tips curling downward), weak stems, susceptibility to pests.
- **Sources**: Blood meal, fish meal, bat guano (organic); calcium nitrate, ammonium sulfate (synthetic).

### Phosphorus (P)
- **Role**: Root development, energy transfer (ATP), flower production. Critical during flowering.
- **Deficiency**: Dark or purplish leaves, stunted growth, poor root development.
- **Sources**: Bone meal, rock phosphate (organic); [[monopotassium-phosphate-kh2po4-mushroom-substrate-buffer-nutrient]] (synthetic).

### Potassium (K)
- **Role**: Water regulation, enzyme activation, disease resistance. Important throughout all stages.
- **Deficiency**: Brown/yellow leaf edges (scorching), weak stems, poor flower development.
- **Sources**: Wood ash, kelp meal, langbeinite (organic); potassium sulfate (synthetic).

## Secondary Nutrients

### Calcium (Ca)
- **Role**: Cell wall structure, root development, [[mycelial-network-nutrient-transport-imaging-gadd]].
- **Deficiency**: Deformed new growth, brown spots on leaves, root tip dieback.
- **Sources**: Dolomite lime, gypsum, eggshells.

### Magnesium (Mg)
- **Role**: Central atom in chlorophyll molecule, enzyme activation.
- **Deficiency**: Interveinal chlorosis (yellowing between veins while veins stay green), common in coco grows.
- **Sources**: Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate), dolomite lime.

### Sulfur (S)
- **Role**: Protein synthesis, enzyme function, terpene production.
- **Deficiency**: Similar to nitrogen deficiency but affects new growth first.
- **Sources**: Gypsum, Epsom salt, elemental sulfur.

## Micronutrients

Required in trace amounts but essential for plant health:
- **Iron (Fe)**: Chlorophyll synthesis. Deficiency: interveinal chlorosis on new leaves.
- **Manganese (Mn)**: Enzyme activation, photosynthesis. Deficiency: interveinal chlorosis similar to iron.
- **Zinc (Zn)**: Growth hormone production, enzyme function. Deficiency: small, distorted leaves.
- **Boron (B)**: Cell wall formation, pollen viability. Deficiency: brittle, distorted growth.
- **Copper (Cu)**: Photosynthesis, lignin synthesis. Rarely deficient.
- **Molybdenum (Mo)**: [[comparison-biological-nitrogen-fixation-vs-synthetic-fertilizer]] and enzyme function.
- **Chlorine (Cl)**: Osmosis and ion balance.
- **Nickel (Ni)**: Urease enzyme function.

## NPK Ratios

The NPK ratio on fertilizers indicates the percentage by weight of nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.

### Vegetative Stage
- High nitrogen, moderate phosphorus, moderate potassium.
