# Mushroom Spore Syringe Preparation — Jarrold Boil-a-Bag Method

## Overview

The Jarrold boil-a-bag [[challenge-microorganisms-microwave-surface-sterilization]]
- **Cotton wool buds:** For applying alcohol to inoculation points
- **Protective breathing mask or clean handkerchief:** To prevent breath contamination

## Water Sterilization

### Preparation

1. Pyrex jug filled with 80 mL of water
2. Tin foil lid placed on top of the jug
3. Syringe (with needle attached) wrapped in tin foil
4. Both items placed in the [[query-what-is-the-difference-between-bacterial-and-fungal-dominant-soil]] spores — from contaminating the spore solution.

### Spore Suspension Process

Once the sterilized water has cooled to room temperature:

1. **Transfer water** to the spore print jar (which contains dried spores from a previous print)
2. **Observe** that spores do not readily mix with water — they tend to float or clump
3. **Flame sterilize** the flat-edged metal scraper using the spirit burner
4. **Scrape** the bottom and sides of the jar to loosen spores into the water
5. **Agitate** gently until spores are dispersed into a visible suspension
6. **Flame sterilize** the syringe needle before drawing spore solution

### Flame Sterilization Protocol

The flame sterilization protocol is repeated before every syringe operation:

- Pass the needle through the methylated spirit flame until the tip glows red
- Allow the needle to cool briefly (a few seconds) before contacting spore solution
- This sterilization is performed before every refill from the spore jar and before every bag inoculation
- The spore jar lid must be replaced immediately after each refill

## Inoculation Methods

### Short Needle Method

For standard 4 cm needles that cannot pass through the filter can:

1. **Tape support strips** on each side of the bag (from can to bag base) for [[comparison-silicon-vs-calcium-in-plant-defense-and-structural-support]]
2. **Add tape** at inoculation points (2 per bag, one on each side) to prevent hole enlargement
3. **Add looped tape** at each point for quick sealing after needle withdrawal
4. **Swab** inoculation points with propyl alcohol using cotton wool buds
