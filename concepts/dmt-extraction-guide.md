# DMT Extraction Guide

## Overview

N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) is a naturally occurring tryptamine compound found in hundreds of [[blue-light-phototropism-red-light-photoperiodism-dual-plant-vision-system]] species and produced endogenously in mammals, including humans. It is one of the most potent psychedelics known — a full visionary experience can be achieved with doses as low as 20–60 mg when vaporized.

This guide covers the extraction of DMT from plant material, primarily Mimosa hostilis root bark (MHRB), which is the most common and efficient source for at-home extraction. DMT is a Schedule I controlled substance in many countries — this information is presented for educational and harm-reduction purposes.

## The Chemistry

DMT is a tryptamine base — meaning it is not soluble in water but is soluble in nonpolar solvents when in its freebase form. In its salt form (e.g., DMT citrate, DMT fumarate), it is water-soluble but not soluble in nonpolar solvents. This differential solubility is the basis of all extraction methods.

### Key Concepts
- **Freebase DMT**: Nonpolar, soluble in naphtha, insoluble in water, vaporizes at ~60°C
- **DMT salt**: Polar, soluble in acidic water, insoluble in nonpolar solvents, higher vaporization point
- **pKa of DMT**: ~8.68 — this determines the pH ranges used in extraction

## Plant Sources

### Mimosa hostilis (Mimosa tenuiflora) Root Bark
- The most popular extraction source
- DMT content: 0.5–1.0% by dry weight
- Available as shredded bark or powder from botanical suppliers
- Native to northeastern Brazil

### Other Sources
- **Acacia species** (A. confusa, A. obtusifolia, A. phlebophylla) — Australian and Asian acacias, variable DMT content
- **Psychotria viridis** — DMT in leaves (0.1–0.6%), used in ayahuasca
- **[[north-american-dmt-containing-plants-phalaris-desmanthus-peganum]] illinoensis** — Illinois bundleflower, root bark contains DMT
- **Phalaris species** — grasses containing DMT and 5-MeO-DMT, also contain toxic gramine

## STB (Straight-to-Base) Tek

The Straight-to-Base method is the simplest and most popular extraction technique. It skips the initial acid extraction step of A/B (acid-base) extraction.

### Materials Needed
- Mimosa hostilis root bark: 50–200 g
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH / lye): 50–100 g
- Naphtha (pure, unscented): 250–500 ml (Ronsonol lighter fluid or VM&P naphtha)
- Distilled water
- Glass jars (1 quart mason jars work well)
- Glass pipette or turkey baster
- Freezer
- Safety equipment: gloves, goggles, long sleeves, well-ventilated area

### Step 1: Basify
1. Powder the MHRB as finely as possible (coffee grinder)
2. In a large jar, mix the powdered bark with 200 ml of warm distilled water per 50 g of bark
3. Slowly add sodium hydroxide — approximately 50 g NaOH per 50 g of bark
4. Add carefully — the reaction is exothermic and produces heat
5. Stir thoroughly. The mixture should be very basic (pH 13+)
6. Let sit for 1 hour, stirring occasionally

### Step 2: Add Naphtha
1. Add naphtha to the basified bark mixture — approximately 100 ml per 50 g of bark
2. Seal the jar and shake vigorously for several minutes
3. Let the jar sit undisturbed. The mixture will separate into layers:
   - Bottom layer: dark, soapy water + bark (aqueous layer)
   - Top layer: clear naphtha containing dissolved DMT freebase (nonpolar layer)
4. Let separate for 10–30 minutes

### Step 3: Separate the Naphtha Layer
1. Use a glass pipette or turkey baster to carefully draw off the top naphtha layer
2. [[transcendent-other-and-plant-human-information-transfer]] to a clean glass jar
3. Leave behind any aqueous layer contamination (dark material)

### Step 4: Repeat
1. Add fresh naphtha to the bark mixture
2. Shake and separate again
3. Combine all naphtha pulls (typically 3–4 pulls total)

### Step 5: Freeze Precipitation
1. Place the combined naphtha in a glass jar
2. Put it in a freezer for 24–48 hours
3. DMT freebase will precipitate out as white crystals as the naphtha cools
4. Pour off the naphtha, leaving the crystals behind
5. Let the crystals dry completely

### Step 6: Optional Recrystallization
1. Dissolve the crude crystals in a minimal amount of warm naphtha
2. Filter through a coffee filter to remove impurities
3. Place the filtered solution in the freezer again
4. The resulting crystals will be purer and more well-formed

## Defatting

Some plant sources (particularly acacia species) contain significant amounts of plant fats and oils that can contaminate the final product. Defatting removes these:

1. After the basify step but before adding naphtha, add a small amount of nonpolar solvent (pre-pulled naphtha or heptane)
2. Shake and separate — the fats will dissolve into the solvent
3. Discard this solvent (it contains fats, not DMT)
4. Proceed with the standard STB tek

## Yield Expectations

- From 100 g of MHRB: expect 0.5–1.0 g of DMT freebase
- Yield depends on bark quality, extraction technique, and skill
- Crude yields from the freeze precipitation are typically 70–90% of theoretical maximum
- Recrystallization reduces weight but increases purity

## Forms of DMT

### Freebase
- White to yellow crystals or powder
- Smoked/vaporized (onset: seconds, duration: 5–20 minutes)
- Not orally active without an MAOI
- Volatile — melts and vaporizes at relatively low temperatures

### DMT Fumarate
- Made by reacting DMT freebase with fumaric acid
- Water-soluble salt form
- Suitable for oral, sublingual, or rectal administration
- More stable for storage

## Safety Considerations

- Sodium hydroxide is extremely caustic — chemical burns, permanent eye damage
- Always wear gloves, goggles, and work in a ventilated area
- Naphtha is flammable — no open flames, sparks, or heat sources nearby
- DMT is a powerful psychedelic — even small amounts produce overwhelming experiences
- A sitter is essential when using DMT
- DMT is a Schedule I controlled substance in the US and many other countries — see [[psychedelic-plants-legal-status]]
