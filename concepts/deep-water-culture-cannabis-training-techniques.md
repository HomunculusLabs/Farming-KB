# Deep Water Culture and Cannabis Training Techniques

## Deep Water Culture (DWC) — "The Bubbler"

DWC is one of the simplest and most cost-effective hydroponic systems for cannabis cultivation. Out of 100+ possible hand-built hydroponic system types, approximately 15 have proven useful for cannabis, with DWC being among the most famous.

### System Description

- Plants are suspended with roots directly submerged in oxygenated nutrient solution.
- An air pump and air stone provide constant oxygenation ("bubbling").
- Very cheap to assemble.
- Provides excellent growth rates — "there is nothing like it for the price."
- Produces massive root systems in the nutrient-rich, oxygenated water.

## Plant Training Techniques

### Topping

- **Method**: Remove the top of the plant just above the last branch formation during the 3rd or 4th week of vegetative growth by shearing at the stem.
- **Result**: The main stem splits into 2+ directions, creating a V shape. After flowering, this produces **2+ top colas instead of 1**.
- **Caveat**: Does not always work as expected — results can vary by strain and technique.

## Advanced Feeding

- Advanced feeding products can produce **greater amounts of bud** but may **reduce potency** and alter taste.
- The relationship between feeding products, yield, and potency requires experimentation.
- "Through FAILURE learn MORE" — experimentation is essential to discovery in cannabis cultivation.

## Cloning

- Cloning replicates plants by taking cuttings from a mother plant.
- The clone contains the **exact same genetic code** as its mother plant.
- Enables preservation of desirable genetics and ensures consistent results.

## Pests and Diseases

### Fusarium Oxysporum

- A fungal pathogen that **wilts and eventually kills** cannabis plants.
- Rare but problematic in some regions.
- Requires specific **fungicide treatment** from a grow shop or online supplier.
- Prevention is better than cure — maintain sterile conditions.

### Deer (Outdoor)

- Deer are extremely curious and can bypass electric fences over time.
- Physical barriers (tall fencing) are the most effective deterrent.
