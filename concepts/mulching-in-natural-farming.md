## Principles of Effective Mulching

1. **Return everything to the soil**: All crop residues
   should be returned, not removed or burned
2. **Keep soil covered**: Bare soil is vulnerable to
   erosion, desiccation, and weed invasion
3. **Use diverse materials**: Straw, green manure, clover,
   and vegetable residues each contribute differently
4. **Minimize disturbance**: No-tillage allows soil
   organisms to work undisturbed
5. **Observe natural patterns**: Nature mulches forests
   continuously with leaf fall

## Fukuoka's Key Discovery

Fukuoka's most important insight about mulching was that
all rice and barley straw and hulls should be returned to
the land. During his entire period of continuous no-tillage
farming, he never saw any sign of yield decline due to
reduced soil fertility. This sealed his conviction that
straw mulch alone, combined with [[green-manure-cover-crops]],
could sustain high productivity indefinitely.
