### Mango in Polyculture Systems
Mango integrates into tropical food forests as a large canopy species:
- **Canopy role**: Mature mango trees reach 15-30 metres, providing shade

### Design Considerations
- **Climate**: Optimal at 15-25 degrees Celsius with 1000-2000 mm rainfall.
  Tolerates light frost when mature. Requires protection from strong winds,
  especially during flowering and nut set.
- **Soil**: Deep, well-drained, slightly acidic soils (pH 5.5-6.5).
  Shallow-rooted compared to other tree crops; sensitive to drought and
  waterlogging.
- **Harvest**: Nuts fall when ripe; ground collection or catching nets.
  Dehusking within 24 hours prevents mould.
- **Guild integration**: Macadamias combine well with coffee, citrus, and
  subtropical berries as an understory. Nitrogen-fixing companions include
  silky oak (Grevillea robusta) as a tall nurse tree.
