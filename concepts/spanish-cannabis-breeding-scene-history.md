# Spanish Cannabis Breeding Scene

## Europe's Cannabis Hotbed

Spain has emerged as the epicenter of European cannabis breeding and cultivation,
particularly for sativa-dominant strains. As documented in Cannabis Sativa Volume 3,
Spain is described as "Europe's hotbed of marijuana activity" and "the south of Spain
has embraced outdoor [[cannabis-landrace-sativa-growing-challenges]] and provides some of the best sativa genetics
around." The country's climate, legal environment, and cultural attitude toward
cannabis have combined to create ideal conditions for a thriving breeding community.

## Climate Advantages for Sativa Cultivation

Spain's Mediterranean climate is particularly well-suited to sativa cultivation.
The long, hot summers and abundant sunshine mirror the equatorial and subtropical
conditions where many sativa landraces evolved. This means Spanish growers can
cultivate pure and near-pure [[african-landrace-sativa-strains]] outdoors without many of the challenges
that northern European or [[growing-gourmet-north-american-reishi-species]] indoor growers face.

The climate advantage allows Spanish breeders to work with genetics that would be
extremely difficult to finish in more northern latitudes. Strains with flowering
times of 12, 14, or even 16 weeks can be grown to full maturity outdoors in southern
Spain, where [[query-how-to-protect-plants-from-frost-and-extend-the-growing-season]] extends late enough into autumn to accommodate even
the latest-finishing sativas.

## Key Spanish Seed Companies

Cannabis Sativa Volume 3 features numerous Spanish seed companies, demonstrating the
depth and breadth of the Spanish breeding scene:

### Royal Queen Seeds (Holland/Spain)
Known for their Amnesia Haze, one of the most popular sativa-dominant strains in
Europe. Their version produces around 600 grams per square yard indoors under 600
watts of light after a 12-week flowering period. Outdoors in good conditions, the
strain can produce up to 200 grams per plant.

### Resin Seeds (Barcelona)
Founded by Jaime, who opened "one of the first grow shops in Spain back in the day."
Active since the late 1990s, Resin Seeds are described as "experts in the field" who
specialize in crossing stellar sativas with high-yielding indicas. Their Critical
Haze—crossing Amnesia Haze with [[critical-mass]]—is noted as a "near-perfect marriage
of sativa highs and indica yields."

### Sweet Seeds
Creators of Jack 47, a cross of Jack Herer and AK-47 that produces "nothing short
of phenomenal" yields. The strain is 75% sativa, growing fairly tall but still
manageable indoors. Their breeder used a particularly proven male AK-47 as the
"stud" plant, selecting for huge yields and strong highs.

### Positronics Seeds
Founded by Wernard Bruining, who "started the first ever grow shop in the
Netherlands." When the original grow shop hit hard times, the seed company was taken
over by employees and friends, keeping the Positronics name alive. They created Jack
Diesel, crossing Jack Herer with New York City Diesel.

### Philosopher Seeds
Creators of Amnesika 2.0, highlighting the "ever-growing cannabis scene in Spain."
Their breeding work exemplifies the Spanish approach of "bringing the best of indica
strains into their sativa plants, meaning that the yields are higher and the
flowering times shorter."

### Ripper Seeds
Founders discovered their flagship strain, Ripper Haze, on a trip to Holland in
2002. The strain—also known as Amnesia Haze—is described as capable of felling
"a rhino in a biker gang in one short toke." [[thc-content-factors-cultivation-freshness-storage-degradation-blesching]] reaches 24%.

### Kaliman Seeds
Self-described "Cheesemasters" who created Rockster's Cheese by crossing a cloned
1989 Exodus Cheese strain with Skunk #1, then backcrossing three times with the
Exodus clone mother. The result is a mostly-sativa Cheese strain that grows under
18 hours of HPS lighting.

### Eva Female Seeds
Described as "the nicest cannabis breeders you could ever hope to meet," Eva Female
Seeds created [[oner-jamaican-dream-and-jamaican-lions]] from a Jamaican heirloom. The 90% sativa plant is hardy
and easy to grow, suitable for both indoor and outdoor cultivation.

### Medical Seeds Co.
Creators of Y Griega, one of the standout Spanish-bred sativas featured in the book.
The company exemplifies Spain's focus on both recreational quality and medical
application.
