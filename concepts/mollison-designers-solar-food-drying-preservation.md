## Nutritional Retention

Mollison cites research showing that properly conducted solar drying
retains 70-90 percent of vitamins and minerals in most foods, compared
to 40-60 percent retention for canning and 50-70 percent for freezing
(when accounting for storage losses). The key factors for nutrient
retention are:

- Moderate temperatures (45-60 degrees C for most foods)
- Protection from direct UV exposure
- Rapid drying to minimize the period of enzyme activity
- Proper storage in airtight containers away from light
