## Aquatic Plants

### Deep Water Plants
- **White water lily** (Nymphaea alba): classic pond plant, roots in deep water
- **Yellow water lily** (Nuphar lutea): similar to white, yellow flowers
- **Water soldier** ([[sagittaria-sagittifolia]]): edible tubers, shallow water
- **Sweet flag** ([[typha-latifolia]]): dense stands, water purification
- **Yellow iris** (Iris pseudacorus): ornamental, water purification

### Floating and Submerged Plants
- **Duckweed**: fast-growing surface cover, nutrient absorption
- Various submerged oxygenators

### Functions of Aquatic Plants
- **Oxygenation**: submerged plants [[comparison-nutrient-cycling-vs-nutrient-dense-gardening]]**: absorb nutrients, prevent algal overgrowth
- **Habitat structure**: provide shelter for fish, amphibians, invertebrates
- **Water purification**: filter pollutants, trap sediments
- **[[blesching-cannabis-fever-temperature-regulation]]**: shade water surface, reduce evaporation
- **Food**: some species edible for humans and animals

## Fish and Aquatic Animals

### Fish Species (Krameterhof)
- **Trout**: cold, clean water; top predator
- **Arctic char**: cold water, similar requirements to trout
- **Tench**: bottom feeder, tolerant of warmer water
- **Rudd**: omnivorous, shallow water
- **Wels catfish** (Silurus glanis): large predator, warm water
- **Zander**: predatory, prefers moderate temperatures
- **Crayfish** (Astacus astacus): freshwater crayfish, water quality indicator
- **Swan mussels** (Anodonta cygnea): filter feeders, water purification

### Amphibians
- Frogs, toads, newts: breed in ponds
- Important predators of insects
- Indicators of healthy aquatic ecosystem

### Stocking Principles
- Match species to water temperature and quality
- Provide habitat structure (stones, plants, depth variation)
- Natural feeding (no artificial feed needed in balanced system)
