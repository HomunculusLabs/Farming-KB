# PF TEK Fruiting, Harvesting, and Potency

After substrate colonization in the jar, the PF TEK progresses to the
fruiting stage. This covers incubation, pinning, birthing the cake into
the terrarium, harvesting for maximum potency, and drying for
preservation.

## Inoculation

Jars are inoculated through the 4 needle holes in the lid:
1. Shake the spore syringe well to redistribute spores
2. Insert needle through lid hole, tilt syringe body toward center
3. Touch needle tip to the inside glass wall
4. Deposit a few drops per hole — stop as soon as water appears around
   the needle tip
5. Shake syringe between holes
6. Use 1 cc per jar (allows one syringe to inoculate 10 jars)

If the needle plugs, draw it back slightly to unplug. Sterilize the
needle with an alcohol flame if touched. An alcohol flame is cleaner
than a butane lighter, which leaves soot residue.

## Incubation

After inoculation:
- Tighten lid bands and retape needle holes
- Store in a safe place out of direct sunlight; indirect light is fine
- Optimal temperature: ~70°F (21°C)
- [[growing-gourmet-spore-germination-strain-isolation]] begins within 3-5 days as white fuzzy spots
- Mycelium spreads with cottony white growth and strandy rhizomorphs
- Any room temperature works; overheating is the main risk
- Overhead light can provide gentle warming — monitor with a thermometer

## Colonization to Pinning

1. **Full colonization**: 2-3 weeks after inoculation, substrate turns
   white with mycelium
2. **Consolidation**: Leave jars sitting in indirect light; mycelium
   continues infiltrating substrate until food triggers fruiting
3. **Pinning**: Within 1 week to several weeks after surface
   colonization, tiny white "pin" structures appear
4. **[[growing-gourmet-primordia-formation-pinning]]**: Small worm-like structures with reddish
   heads — these are the first mushrooms

## Birthing the Cake

Remove the fungus cake from the jar when primordia appear:
1. Remove lid; scrape away majority of dry top vermiculite layer
2. Place an old jar lid over the jar mouth and invert
3. Lightly slam jar down on a table cushioned with a magazine
4. The cake slides onto the old lid (called the "birthday")
5. Daub the cake with tissue paper to absorb any water droplets
6. Immediately place into the dual [[dual-chambered-terrarium-design]]

Handle with care — bruising causes blue marks. The cake is resilient
but should be disturbed as little as possible.

## The Dual Chambered Terrarium

A standard 10-gallon aquarium serves as the [[gottlieb-psilocybin-cultivation-fruiting-chamber-design]]:

- **Top lid**: Airtight seal using clear polyethylene plastic film
  stretched over a wooden frame — essential for humidity retention
- **Spray shield**: 1/8" clear acrylic partition separates the
  spraying area from the cake chamber
- **Cakes sit elevated** above standing water in the lower chamber
- **Misting**: Spray the walls of the terrarium, not the cakes directly
- **Heating**: An aquarium heater in the lower water chamber maintains
  ~72°F; a thermometer is essential

## Harvesting for Maximum Potency

### The Potency Principle
Small immature specimens are **significantly more potent** than larger
mature specimens. This is a scientifically established finding and the
core of the PF potency strategy.

### Types of Fruiting Bodies
- **Abhorts**: Convoluted caps, gnarly stems, stunted growth. These
  are "primo" in alkaloid content — more powerful than the beautiful
  mature specimens.
- **Baby mushroom abhorts**: Tiny primordia that cease growing. Also
  extremely potent. Harvest before their heads turn black.
- **Mutant blobs**: Fungal masses with little or no cap. Harvestable.
- **Normal sporocarps**: Perfect specimens. Best for spore collecting
  and display, but weakest in psychedelic potency.

### Harvesting Guidelines
1. Pick abhorts and mutants when young, before caps turn black
2. Harvest normal mushrooms **before the veil breaks** under the cap
3. Young mushrooms have roundish caps and are smaller — that's optimal
4. A pointed knife blade works well for removing primordia

### The PF Potency Formula
> "Grow them on brown rice, harvest them when they are young, and cool
> dry them with desiccant. When this is done, they are an entheogen of
> the highest order."

## Drying with Desiccant

Cool [[mushroom-desiccant-drying-methods]] preserves potency far better than heat-based
methods:
1. Place harvested mushrooms in a container with silica gel or Drierite
2. Keep in a cool, dry, dark location
3. Replace or regenerate desiccant as needed
4. Dried mushrooms should be cracker-dry for long-term storage
5. Store in airtight containers in a cool, dark place

## Time Scale Summary

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Spore inoculation → germination | ~1 week at 70°F |
| Germination → full colonization | 2-3 weeks |
| Colonization → fruiting begins | ~2 weeks |
| Fruiting cycle | ~2 weeks |
| **Total: spore to harvest** | **4-6 weeks** |

After the initial flush, cakes may produce sparse secondary fruitings
if thoroughly cleaned, but the first flush is typically the largest.

## Spore Printing

Mature specimens (those allowed to open fully) are used for spore
collection. Place the cap gills-down on paper or foil, cover with a
glass, and leave for 12-24 hours. The resulting spore print can be
used to prepare spore syringes for the next cultivation cycle.
