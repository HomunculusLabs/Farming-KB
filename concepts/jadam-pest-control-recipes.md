# JADAM Pest Control Recipes

JADAM pest control relies on simple, on-farm preparations that are inexpensive, non-toxic, and effective. The foundation is the [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] (JWA), combined with plant extracts and biological agents.

## JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) — The Foundation

### Recipe
- Unrefined canola/rapeseed oil: 1 part
- Potassium hydroxide (KOH): 1/10th part by weight
- Water: 20 parts

### Method
1. Dissolve KOH in water (use non-metal container)
2. Add oil slowly while stirring
3. Stir until the mixture becomes translucent (saponification)
4. Dilute 1:500 to 1:1000 with water for use as spray
5. Stores indefinitely

### Purpose
- Surfactant that allows sprays to penetrate insect exoskeletons
- Replaces commercial insecticidal soaps
- Base for all other JADAM pest sprays

## JADAM Herb Solution (JHS) — General Pest Control

### Recipe
- Any strong-smelling herb plant: garlic, ginger, hot pepper, neem, mugwort, etc.
- Water: enough to cover plant material
- JWA: 1:500 dilution

### Method
1. Collect herb plant material (leaves, stems, roots)
2. Chop finely or blend
3. Soak in water for 1-3 days
4. Strain and dilute with water (1:100 to 1:500)
5. Add JWA at 1:500 dilution
6. Spray on affected plants, covering both sides of leaves

### Most Effective Herbs
- **Garlic**: Antibacterial, antifungal, repels most insects
- **Hot pepper**: Repels mammals, ants, aphids
- **Neem**: Broad-spectrum insecticide, fungicide
- **Mugwort**: Repels caterpillars, mites
- **Ginger**: Antibacterial, antifungal

## JADAM Sulfur (JS) — Fungal Disease Control

### Recipe
- Sulfur powder: agricultural grade
- JWA: 1:500 dilution
- Water

### Method
1. Mix sulfur powder with a small amount of water to make a paste
2. Dilute to final concentration (0.1-0.3% sulfur)
3. Add JWA
4. Spray on affected plants

### Effective Against
- [[cervantes-powdery-mildew-prevention-treatment]]
- Rust
- [[cervantes-downy-mildew-fungal-blight]]
- Mites

## JADAM Sea Water (JSW) — Micronutrient Supplement

### Method
- Dilute seawater 1:100 to 1:500 with fresh water
- Spray as foliar feed or soil drench
- Provides complete trace mineral spectrum

## Application Guidelines
- Spray early morning or evening (avoid midday heat)
- Cover both sides of leaves
- Repeat every 3-7 days for serious infestations
- Rotate different herb solutions to prevent resistance
- Always test on a few leaves first before full application
