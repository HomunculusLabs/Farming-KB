# Master Cho's OHN ([[knf-ohn-oriental-herbal-nutrient-recipe]] Herb Nutrient) Recipe

The [[bionutrients-lab-oriental-herbal-nutrients-ohn]] Nutrient (OHN) is made from herbs full of energy that increase [[what-a-plant-knows-phytochrome-red-far-red-light-switch]] robustness, sterilize, and keep [[phytochrome-red-far-red-ratio-shade-detection-plants]] warm. OHN revitalizes crops and activates their growth. It is one of the most frequently used [[query-how-do-i-use-knf-inputs-through-the-plant-growth-cycle]] in Korean [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]].

## [[cho-oriental-herbal-nutrient-preparation]] [[cho-natural-farming-inputs-overview]]

- **Optimum season**: Spring or fall
- **Fermentation [[soil-temperature-plant-growth-rate-doubling-rule]]**: 23-25 deg C
- **Storage temperature**: 1-15 deg C
- **Ingredients**: Gigantic angelica root (Angelica gigas), cinnamon bark, licorice root ([[glycyrrhiza-glabra]]), garlic, ginger, Makgeolli (Korean rice wine), brown sugar, Soju (Korean distilled liquor, 30-35% alcohol)
- **Tools**: 20L jars (one per herb), porous paper, vinyl film, wooden sticks, rubber bands

## Five Herbs

### Dry-State Herbs

Three main herbs are typically sold dry: gigantic angelica root, cinnamon bark, and licorice root.

### Raw-State Herbs

Garlic and ginger are used raw. Do not wash either with water -- shake off soil only. Crush everything including skins and roots (which contain moisture).

## Preparation Method for Dry Herbs (Cinnamon Example)

1. Put 2kg cinnamon bark into a 20L jar with 6L of Makgeolli. Fill jar 2/3 full. Herbs must be completely soaked. Cover with porous paper and rubber band. Let absorb moisture for 1-2 days.
2. Add brown sugar equivalent to total weight of herb plus Makgeolli (cinnamon 2kg + Makgeolli 6L = 8kg sugar). Cover and tie. Leave 4-5 days. Contents must occupy 2/3 of the jar for optimum fermentation.
3. Pour 11-12L of Soju into the remaining 1/3 of the jar. Cover with vinyl film. Stir clockwise every early morning for 2 weeks.
4. Filter the contents and keep in a colored glass bottle or PE container for long-term storage.

## Preparation Method for Raw Herbs (Garlic Example)

1. Put 8kg of crushed garlic (whole bulb, including skins and roots) in a jar.
2. Add 8kg brown sugar (1:1 ratio). Cover with porous paper. Contents occupy 2/3 of jar. Leave 4-6 days.
3. Pour 11-12L of Soju into the remaining 1/3. Cover with vinyl film. Stir clockwise every early morning for 2 weeks.
4. Filter and store extraction in a dark container.

## Rebrewing

Each herb can be reused 4-5 times:

1. After filtration, put the herb remnants back in a jar.
2. Pour 4/5 of the previously extracted liquid into the jar. No Makgeolli needed this time.
3. Optionally add 1/10 of original brown sugar amount (e.g., 800g from original 8kg). Divide into 4-5 parts. Leave 1 day.
4. Fill remaining 1/3 of jar with Soju. Same process as first extraction.
5. Maximum 5 extractions per herb. Combine all extracted liquid.

## Yield Estimates

After 5 extractions per herb:
- Cinnamon: ~55L total
- Licorice: ~55L total
- Garlic: ~55L total
- Ginger: ~55L total
- Gigantic angelica: ~110L (used in double quantity)
- **Grand total: ~330L of OHN**

## Storage

- Use jars, glass bottles, or PE containers that block light penetration.
- Temperature 1-15 deg C, no direct sunlight, enough ventilation, airtight.
- The longer OHN is stored, the thicker the concentration and smaller the particles become, resulting in higher absorption rate.
- When using years-old OHN, dilute more.

## Dilution and Usage

### Dilution Ratio

Base dilution: 1:1,000 (OHN to water). Adjust depending on weather and plant condition.

### Mixing Ratio of Five OHNs

Combine the five [[korean-natural-farming-imo-collection-fermented-plant-extracts]] just before use in this ratio:
**2 (Angelica) : 1 (Licorice) : 1 (Cinnamon) : 1 (Garlic) : 1 (Ginger)**

Example: Dilute OHNs 1000x with 18L of water, then add 6cc Angelica, 3cc licorice, 3cc cinnamon, 3cc garlic, 3cc ginger.

### Applications

- **IMO #2 and IMO #4 preparation**: OHN (x1/1000) used as nutrient liquid.
- **[[knf-soil-treatment-solution]]**: Combined with other inputs.
