# Mycoheterotrophic Plants and Mycorrhizal Carbon Theft

Updated: 2026-04-18

Mycoheterotrophs are achlorophyllous (non-photosynthetic) plants that depend exclusively on fungi for their carbon, exploiting mycorrhiza-like relationships in their underground roots and tubers. Over 400 mycoheterotrophic plant species are known, distributed across approximately 90 genera ranging from liverworts to dicotyledons and monocotyledons (Leake, 1994, 2004). These plants have originated from multiple independent lineages of green plants.
