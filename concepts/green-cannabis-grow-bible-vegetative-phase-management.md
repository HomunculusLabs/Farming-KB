## Watering

Green recommends watering every second or third day, or when the soil
dries out. A weight test is effective: lift the pot when dry versus
when watered to learn the difference. The pattern:

- Day A: water, Day B: let dry, Day C: let dry, Day D: check and water
  if needed

Signs of overwatering include leaves wilting downward in a claw shape
while soil is wet. Signs of underwatering include wilting with dry
soil. Overwatering kills plants if continued; use a fan near the soil
surface if needed.
