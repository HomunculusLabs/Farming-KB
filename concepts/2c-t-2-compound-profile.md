# 2C-T-2 and 2C-T-7 Compound Profile

## The 2C-T Series

The 2C-T series represents a family of 2,5-dimethoxy-4-(thioalkyl)phenethylamines — compounds where the 4-position substituent contains a sulfur atom. The "T" stands for "thio" (sulfur). Shulgin explored multiple chain lengths in this series, creating compounds with a range of potencies, durations, and effect profiles.

## 2C-T-2

### Chemical Identity

**2C-T-2**: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-ethylthiophenethylamine. The sulfur atom is bonded to an ethyl group at the 4-position.

### Dosage

- **Threshold:** 5 mg
- **Light:** 8-12 mg
- **Common:** 12-22 mg
- **Strong:** 22-30 mg

### Duration

Effective duration: 6-8 hours.

### Qualitative Effects

Shulgin's notes describe 2C-T-2 as a visually active compound with a warm, friendly character. At moderate doses (15-20 mg), users report colorful visuals, enhanced sensory perception, and a relaxed but alert mental state. The compound is noted for a relatively smooth onset and a gentle comedown compared to some other 2C compounds.

## 2C-T-7

### Chemical Identity

**2C-T-7**: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-propylthiophenethylamine. The sulfur atom is bonded to a propyl group.

### Dosage

- **Threshold:** 2 mg
- **Light:** 5-10 mg
- **Common:** 10-20 mg
- **Strong:** 20-30 mg

### Duration

Effective duration: 8-14 hours (one of the longest-acting 2C compounds).

### Qualitative Effects

2C-T-7 is noted for its exceptional potency and extended duration. Shulgin described it as a powerful psychedelic with strong visual effects and a significant body component. At moderate doses, the experience is characterized by:

- Rich, elaborate visual effects
- Enhanced sensory perception across all modalities
- A dreamy, contemplative quality
- Extended duration requiring careful planning

### Safety Concerns

2C-T-7 has been associated with several fatalities, often involving co-administration with other substances (particularly MAOIs). Shulgin did not document these incidents in PIHKAL, but subsequent research has highlighted the dangers of combining 2C-T-7 with other serotonergic agents.

## Series SAR Observations

Across the 2C-T series, Shulgin observed clear structure-activity relationships:

- **Increasing chain length** generally increases potency up to a point, then decreases
- **Longer chains** extend duration significantly
- **The sulfur atom** contributes a distinctive quality to the subjective effects not present in the purely carbon-chain analogues
- **S-methyl** (2C-T) is the shortest and least potent; activity increases through S-ethyl (2C-T-2) and S-propyl (2C-T-7)

## Synthesis

The 2C-T series compounds are synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde via electrophilic substitution with the appropriate thiol reagent, followed by the standard nitrostyrene formation and reduction pathway common to all 2C compounds.
