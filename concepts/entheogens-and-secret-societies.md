# Entheogens and Secret Societies

## Overview

Entheogens — psychoactive plants and fungi used in sacred contexts — are among the most closely guarded secrets of Freemasonry and many other secret societies throughout history. The suppression of knowledge about these substances represents, according to James Arthur's analysis in *[[arthur-mushrooms-and-mankind-ethnomycology-and-religion]]*, one of the primary mechanisms by which religious and political institutions have maintained control over human consciousness and spiritual development.

## The Structure of Secrecy

Secret societies operate on hierarchical principles where knowledge is compartmentalized. Lower-ranking members are permitted to believe they possess the full truth, while the deepest secrets — including the nature and use of entheogenic sacraments — are reserved for those at the highest levels. This structure creates a system where:

- **Information is hoarded**: Advanced knowledge, technology, and spiritual practices are acquired and then deliberately kept exclusive to the inner circle.
- **Public remnants are destroyed**: After extracting valuable knowledge, organizations systematically erase evidence from the public domain.
- **Oaths of silence enforce compliance**: Initiates at higher levels swear binding oaths preventing disclosure, even when they recognize the value of sharing the information.

This pattern mirrors historical events such as the destruction of libraries in [[allegro-plants-and-drugs-in-the-ancient-world]] by religious authorities who first pillaged their contents.

## Entheogens as the Core Secret

Among the most significant of these guarded secrets is the knowledge of entheogenic plants, particularly the **Amanita muscaria** (fly agaric mushroom). According to accounts from those who have reached high levels within secret society initiations, the entheogenic rites are positioned at the very top of the initiatory hierarchy. When individuals outside these orders demonstrate knowledge of these practices, the reaction from insiders is reportedly one of shock and fear — suggesting the information strikes at the core of their most deeply held indoctrination experiences.

## The Problem of Secrecy for Human Development

The hoarding of [[mckenna-sect-of-the-phoenix-and-secret-entheogenic-knowledge]] is characterized as an "abomination" that has left the world "desolate." The argument proceeds on several levels:

1. **Suppression of truth**: Just as revealing the truth would relinquish religious institutions of their power over people, so too would governments and secret societies lose their authority.
2. **Inhibition of evolution**: Open debate and access to knowledge enhance human evolution; secrecy inhibits it.
3. **Perpetuation of false systems**: The Fascist agenda of societal morals and dogma depends on keeping the public ignorant of the actual mechanisms of spiritual experience.
4. **Repression of indigenous spirituality**: The natural human drive toward expanded consciousness, heightened awareness, and direct spiritual experience is replaced with societal models that condemn these expressions as sin.

## Symbolism in Religious Art

Religious artwork contains extensive [[allegro-cross-and-mushroom-symbolism]] that operates on multiple levels:

- **The sacramental cup**: Depicted in Catholic communion robes and other religious contexts, the cup symbol is noted for its mushroom-like shape and coloring. This symbolism is understood fully only by the elite, not by local clergy.
- **Alpha and Omega imagery**: The statement "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end" is connected to mushroom imagery, as all things are seen as created in this form.
- **The sacred gateway**: Arched doorways in religious architecture allude to the entrance to the "Holy of Holies" — the passage into the other world where divine beings are said to reside.
- **Jesus as lord of magical plants**: One depiction shows Jesus above four specific plants (Amanita muscaria, a Psilocybe species, Syrian Rue, and Opium Poppy), underscored by a green vine representing Cannabis and other DMT-containing plants.

## The Soma Mixture Theory

The depiction of Jesus above the four plants suggests an alchemical recipe for the true **Soma** — the legendary elixir of the gods described in Vedic tradition. Each plant serves a specific function:

1. **Amanita muscaria** (red): Produces the "quickening of the spirit"
2. **Psilocybe mushroom** (blue): Opens the "third eye" — visionary capacity
3. **Syrian Rue** (*[[mckenna-food-gods-peganum-harmala-soma]]*): Contains harmine/harmaline, MAO inhibitors that potentiate other compounds, paralleling pinoline naturally produced by the pineal gland
4. **Opium Poppy**: Induces euphoria and relaxation necessary to fully experience the intensive visionary state
5. **Green plants** (Cannabis, DMT-containing grasses): Prevent nausea, enhance other effects, and reactivate neural pathways opened by the entheogens

This five-plant combination mimics the DMT-pinoline mixture naturally produced by the pineal gland during sleep. The theory addresses a longstanding mystery: why no single candidate plant for Soma has satisfied researchers. Each proposed candidate (Amanita by Wasson, Syrian Rue by Flattery and Schwartz, Psilocybe by McKenna) produces compelling but incomplete results individually. A multi-plant admixture may produce the full spectrum of effects described in ancient religious texts.

## Linguistic Connections

Several linguistic threads support the entheogenic interpretation:

- **Soma/Somniferum**: *[[papaver-somniferum]]* (opium poppy) shares a root with Soma; "somnambulism" (sleepwalking) occurs when DMT and pinoline are released by the pineal gland during sleep.
- **Manna**: In ancient tongues, manna means mushroom.
- **Shem-an-na**: The Sumerian term for their version of the sacred substance, made from "highward fire-stone."
- **Ambrosia** (Greek), **Scheffa-food** (Egyptian), **Amrta** (Hindu/Buddhist): All terms for the food/drink of the gods that confers immortality.

## Implications

The secrecy surrounding entheogens is presented not as a neutral cultural feature but as an active force of oppression. The replacement of genuine psychoactive sacraments with symbolic substitutes (bread and wine) is characterized as a "broken cistern that can hold no water" — a placebo that fails to produce the spiritual effects prescribed by the original texts. This substitution, whether intentional or resulting from the loss of knowledge, has profound implications for the spiritual development of humanity.
