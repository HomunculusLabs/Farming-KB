## Krameterhof - Sepp Holzer's Model Farm

updated: 2026-04-18

The Krameterhof is the 45-hectare (110-acre) family farm of [[holzer-tamera-water-landscape-project]], waterways, wetlands, tracks, shelterbelts, and mixed plantings. Every element serves multiple functions, and the connections between elements are as important as the elements themselves.

The design follows natural contours and works with gravity to move water, nutrients, and heat across the landscape. Ponds at different elevations create a cascading water system that can generate power, breed fish and crayfish, grow aquatic plants, provide irrigation, and [[comparison-pruning-fruit-trees-vs-grafting-fruit-trees]] of many different varieties are planted throughout the Krameterhof, integrated with other crops and livestock rather than segregated into orchards. Wild fruit trees are interspersed with cultivated varieties to provide pollination partners and genetic diversity.

## Water Systems

Water management is central to the Krameterhof design. The farm contains numerous ponds of varying sizes and depths, connected by waterways and wetlands. These serve multiple purposes simultaneously:

- **Aquaculture**: Breeding of fish species including trout, tench, pike, wels catfish, zander, koi carp, roach, rudd, and minnows, as well as European crayfish
- **Aquatic plant cultivation**: Different pond zones support different plant communities
- **[[permaculture-integrated-animal-systems]], Duroc pigs, Turopoije pigs, poultry, and other species. Animals are managed using [[csa-farm-share-model]]
