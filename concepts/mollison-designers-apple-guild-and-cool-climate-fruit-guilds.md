### Mulch Producers
Species that generate abundant biomass for surface mulching:
- **Comfrey**: Cut 4-6 times per season, yielding 2-5 kg of fresh material
  per plant per cut. High potassium content supports fruit development.
- **Clover**: Self-generating living mulch that fixes nitrogen, suppresses
  weeds, and protects soil surface. Cut or grazed to cycle nutrients.
- **Rhubarb**: Large leaves provide summer mulch; edible stalks; shade-
  tolerant understorey crop.
