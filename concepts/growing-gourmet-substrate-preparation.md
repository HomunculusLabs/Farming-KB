## The Supplemented Sawdust Fruiting Formula

The standard supplemented sawdust formula for [[psilocybin-north-america-pacific-northwest]]. The addition of gypsum serves multiple purposes: it keeps grain kernels separated after sterilization, provides calcium and sulfur (essential elements for mushroom metabolism), and helps buffer pH.

## Grain Formulas for Spawn Production

Moisture content is critical for successful grain spawn. The optimal range falls within 45-55%, with an ideal around 50%. Since grain comes with inherent moisture content of 8-15%, less water is added than might be expected.

### Standard Grain Formulas

For 16 oz bottles:
- 100 grams rye + 150 ml water + 0.5g gypsum (approximately 60% moisture)

For quart/liter jars:
- 200 grams rye + 200 ml water + 1g gypsum (approximately 50% moisture)

For 1/2 gallon/2 liter jars:
- 480 grams rye + 400 ml water + 2g gypsum (approximately 45% moisture)

For gallon/4 liter jars:
- 800 grams rye + 600 ml water + 4g gypsum (approximately 43% moisture)

### Key Moisture Principles
- Grain that is too dry produces slow, wispy [[growing-gourmet-phase-ii-steam-pasteurization]] at 190-194F (88-90C) for 14 hours. Remote sensing thermometers at various depths should be used to verify that the coolest point reaches target temperature.

## Steam Pasteurization (Phase II) for Straw

### Chamber Design
- Raised false floor with steam pipes beneath
- Insulated walls and ceiling with water-resistant materials
- Multiple thermometers at low (4-6 inches), mid, and high (12-24 inches from top) positions
- Positive-pressure HEPA-filtered air system for cooling phase
- Floor drain with gate valve

### Process
1. Fill chamber with chopped, hydrated straw to 1/4 to 1/8 of air volume (7:1 air-to-substrate ratio)
