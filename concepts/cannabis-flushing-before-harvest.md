Flushing is the practice of ceasing nutrient feeding and watering with plain water in the final days or weeks before harvest. The goal is to allow the plant to use up stored nutrients, resulting in smoother-tasting, cleaner-burning bud.

## Why Flush

As cannabis plants grow, they accumulate excess nutrients in their tissues. If not flushed:

- **Harsh smoke**: Accumulated nitrogen and other nutrients produce a harsh, chemical taste when burned
- **Poor ash color**: Unflushed bud produces dark, black ash instead of clean white/grey ash
- **Headache and cough**: Excess nutrients can cause headaches and excessive coughing when consumed
- **Masked flavor**: Nutrient residue masks the natural terpene flavors and aromas of the strain

Flushing gives the plant time to metabolize and use up these stored nutrients, improving the final product quality.

## When to Flush

### Standard Flush Timeline

- **Soil grows**: Begin flushing 1–2 weeks before planned harvest (7–14 days)
- **Hydroponic grows**: Begin flushing 5–7 days before planned harvest
- **Soilless grows (coco, perlite mixes)**: Begin flushing 7–10 days before harvest

The hydroponic timeline is shorter because nutrients are more immediately available and there is less buffer in the growing medium.
