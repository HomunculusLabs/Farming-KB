# Mollison Woodlot Timber Durability and Hedgerow Barriers

Mollison's detailed species lists for on-farm woodlot management, timber durability ratings, and hedgerow barrier systems including livestock exclusion and windbreaks.

## Woodlot Functions

On-farm woodlots serve multiple purposes beyond timber:
- Fuel (from high sugar crops for alcohol production)
- Structural (fences and buildings)
- Forage (winter and summer feed)
- Shelter (species suited to forage or structural use)

## Timber Durability in Ground

### Very High Durability (70+ years in ground)
- Strawberry Jam Acacia (Acacia acuminata)
- Black Locust (Robinia pseudoacacia)
- Catalpa (Catalpa speciosa)
- Almost all cedars (Cedrus spp.)
- Juniper (Juniperus communis)
- River Red Gum (E. camaldulensis)
- Huon Pine (Athrotaxis franklinii)

### High Durability (30-70 years)
- Chestnut (Castanea dentata)
- Red Mulberry (Morus rubra)
- Osage Orange (Madura punifera)
- Bald Cypress (Taxodium distichum)
- Redwood (Sequoia sempervirens)
- Honey Locust (Gleditsia triacanthos)
- White Oak (Quercus alba)
- Tasmanian Tallowood (Pittosporum bicolor)
- Macrocarpa (Cupressus macrocarpa)
- Oyster Bay Pine (Callitris timmanica)
- Desert Oak (Casuarina decaisnea)
- Celery-top Pine (Phyllodadus rhomboidalis)

From these lists, farmers choose shelter-belt, hedgerow, forage, bee forage, and distillation species. Some suit arid, riverine, or coastal conditions. Conifers mainly useful as timber but slow-growing and less versatile.

## Hedgerow Barrier Functions

1. Contain or exclude livestock
2. Shelter gardens and houses from wind
3. Increase efficiency of wind and sun
4. Prevent re-invasion by unwanted plants
5. Screen unwanted views and sounds

## Livestock Exclusion Hedges

### The Ultimate Barrier: African Boxthorn
- Lycium ferocissimum — stops bulls, lions, and weed invasion
- Resists salt spray and gales
- Feeds poultry (which disappear into it without recourse)
- In coastal sands: crown spreads to ~7 m wide
- Needs uprooting of seedlings every ~50 years
- Stable, wide fence — no tendency to spread on heavy soils
- Dead branch "bomas" protect more useful tree plantings
- Clipped branches deter rabbits around seedlings

### Other Formidable Barriers
- Jujube
- Rosa multiflora and Rosa rugosa (need hard stopping/pruning in first years)
- Osage orange (O.G.F.S.)
- Red cedar (never-browsed)
- Honey locust and shingle oak (Quercus imbricaria)
- Cacti in dry areas

### Traditional European Hedge
- Hawthorn (Crataegus) live-set at 60-90 cm, later layered or interwoven with dead trimmings
- Needs cutting to shape every 4-6 years
- More efficient with barbed wire through infancy, or ditch-and-bank, or electric wire

### Woven Palisades
- Woven or netted for small livestock exclusion
- Caspian Sea area: living papyrus uprights interwoven with dead stems
- Middle-European woven fences: local wattle, ti-tree (Melaleuca), whipstick regrowth, bamboo

## Windbreak Systems

### Design Principles
- Shape should follow the "sun-trap" configuration
- Must break wind force while performing multiple functions

### Multi-Function Windbreaks
- Act as firebreaks
- Store emergency fodder for animals
- Make cultivation easier
- Provide bee forage and insectivorous bird cover/nesting
- Supply cull wood for construction
- Contain emergency diversification species
- Conserve soil and clean run-off water, prevent erosion

### Productive Wind Shelter (cattle excluded)
Mixed hedgerow of Prunus, Crataegus, Coprosma, Malus (crab apple), hazel, bamboo, fuchsia, and vines — wildlife and forage habitat.

### Winter Shelter for Livestock
Bamboo, pampas grass, paspalum, sudax, ti-tree, and thickset species for newly-shorn sheep, ewes in lamb, emergency snow retreats.

### Dry Area Windbreaks
Tall tamarisk, casuarina, mulga (Acacia aneura), eucalypt — protect soil from desiccating winds, reduce pond evaporation.

### Salt Wind Defense
Coprosma, Euonymus japonicas, sea buckthorn (Hippophae rhamnoides), Tetragonia implexa fedges, tall pine stands.

### Temporary Summer Windbreaks
Sunflower, Jerusalem artichoke, pole bean belts (not rows), corn belts, autumn-winter broad bean clumps.

### Perennial Garden Hedges
Clipped Coprosma, tree lucerne, Leucaena — wind protection plus manurial mulch from leaves and branchlets.

### Seaside Garden Edges
Escallonia macrantha or wormwood for soft silver edge. Rosemary for small herb gardens.

## Weed-Exclusion Barriers

Plants that halt invading couch grass, twitch, oxalis:
- Matted roots: bamboo, pampas, lemongrass
- Dense foliage: comfrey, Coprosma, Lycium
- Reinforce with marigolds (against twitch) inside, oaks and pines outside
