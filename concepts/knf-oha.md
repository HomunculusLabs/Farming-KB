# KNF Oriental Herbal Adjust (OHA)

The Oriental Herbal Adjust (OHA), more commonly referred to as Oriental Herbal Nutrient (OHN), is a very important input in Natural Farming. It is made from herbs which are full of energy and function to increase plant robustness, to sterilize and keep plants warm. OHN revitalizes crops and activates their growth.

## Overview

OHN is made from popular oriental herbs such as Angelica, Acutiloba, Licorice and Cinnamon that are fermented (not boiled) to maintain the vigorous growth of crops. The herbs are full of energy and provide multiple benefits:

- Increase plant robustness and vitality
- Sterilize and keep plants warm
- Revitalize crops and activate growth
- Strengthen plant immune systems
- Help plants overcome stress from weather, pests, or chemicals

## Herbs Used

### Cinnamon

- Warms and stimulates plant growth
- Antimicrobial properties
- Made by soaking cinnamon bark in rice wine/beer at 1:3 ratio (bark to liquid)
- Add jaggery equivalent to weight of ingredients
- Ferment 3-5 days, stir daily with stick each morning for two weeks
- After fermentation, add distilled liquor to remaining 1/3 space for long storage
- If using within 45 days, no need to add liquor -- water will do

### Garlic

- Natural antibiotic, pest deterrent
- Select freshly harvested garlic (do not wash with water)
- Crush entire bulb (1 kg) including skins and roots
- Mix with equal weight jaggery (1:1 ratio) in jar
- Fill only 2/3 of jar for good fermentation
- Cover with tight lid or vinyl film, stir gently clockwise every morning
- Leave 4-6 days, then filter and keep extraction

### Ginger

- Stimulates circulation, warms plants
- Collect ginger, shake soil off (do not wash)
- Crush (not too finely)
- Same process as garlic: 1:1 ratio with jaggery, 4-6 days fermentation
- Filter and keep extraction

### Angelica (Dong Quai)

- Restores plant vitality and promotes recovery
- Rich in bio-active compounds

### Licorice

- Harmonizes effects of other herbs
- Supports plant metabolism

## Dilution and Use

- The three kinds of OHNs (ginger, garlic, cinnamon) are mixed just before using
- Mix ratio: 1:1:1:1000 (equal parts of each OHN in 1000 parts water)
- Can be used throughout the entire Nutritive Cycle
- Basic dilution ratio: 1:500 to 1:1000

## When Crops Are Weakened

To reinvigorate weakened crops, apply a mixture of:
- OHN at 1:1000
- FPJ at 1:500
- BRV at 1:500

For plants with soft rot or anthracnose, add WCA at 1:1000 to the mixture.

## Uses in Other Preparations

OHN is used in making:
- [[knf-indigenous-microorganism-culture|IMO-3 and IMO-4]]
- Soil treatment solutions
- Seed treatment solutions

## Storage

- Filtered extractions stored in sealed jars
- Add distilled liquor for long-term storage
- Without liquor, use within 45 days
