## The Ancient World View of Gods

Understanding the Greek and Roman context requires understanding the ancient Indo-European worldview. In the ancient world, only those things that could be seen by the human eye, were physically "immortal," and were of use or frightening to mankind were considered "Gods" -- the Sun, Moon, Earth, Sky, and Fire. The Sky Father (Jupiter, Zeus) was a real, visible entity that brought sunshine, rain, and storms. Modern people must remember that the ancient Gods were always real things, and that stories about these real Gods are describing something real. The ancients always personified these real Gods in their stories, so naive literal reading of the "person" in the story leads to misunderstanding.
