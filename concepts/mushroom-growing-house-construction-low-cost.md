## Standard House Specifications

- **Dimensions**: 3m wide x 6m long x 3m high at roof peak
- **Capacity**: 1000 substrate bags
- **Frame**: 8 bamboo poles (3m each) for roof supports, plus side poles
- **Walls**: Thatch or agricultural netting (sak yong) on sides
- **Floor**: [[reciprocal-roof-frame]] supports |
| Rope/cord | 5 rolls | 50 | Binding frame |
| Thatch panels or netting | Variable | 100 | Side walls |
| Palm leaf thatch | Variable | 80 | Roof covering |
| Nails | 1 kg | 30 | Frame connections |
| Plastic sheeting | 6m x 3m | 80 | Ground moisture barrier |

Total: approximately 500 Baht (US$12) for a basic 1000-bag [[fao-accessible-mushroom-house-design-disabilities]].

## Construction Steps

### 1. Site Selection
Choose a location that is:
- Near the trainee's home for daily management
- Close to water source
- On flat or gently sloping ground
- Shaded by trees where possible (existing shade reduces construction costs)

### 2. Ground Preparation
Clear the area of weeds and level the ground. Create a shallow drainage channel (20cm deep) around the perimeter. Lay plastic sheeting on the floor if moisture from ground is excessive.

### 3. Frame Construction
- Set 4 main bamboo poles at corners, 3m apart, buried 50cm into ground
- Set intermediate poles every 1.5m along the sides
- Build the roof frame with bamboo rafters forming an A-shape or gable roof
- Secure all connections with rope or wire

### 4. Walls and Roof
- Cover side walls with thatch, palm leaves, or agricultural netting
- Leave openings for doors at both ends for airflow
- Cover roof with thatch or palm leaves, ensuring no rain enters

### 5. Ventilation
Critical for mushroom production:
- Create openings near the top of walls for hot air escape
- Ensure cross-ventilation through door openings
- Adjustable openings allow control of humidity and fresh air

## Shelving Systems

### Wooden Shelf System
For higher-capacity houses:

- Use wood planks for shelves, 80cm wide
- Space shelves vertically at 60cm intervals
- Support with wooden posts and cross-beams
- Each shelf holds 3 rows of bags side by side
- A house of 3m x 6m x 3m can accommodate 5 levels of shelving

Shelf costs:
- Wood planks (80cm x 6m x 10 pieces): 500 Baht
- Wood posts (3m x 10 pieces): 300 Baht
- Total shelving: approximately 800 Baht

### Suspended Bag System
An alternative that saves floor space:

- Hang bags from the roof frame using rope or string
- Space bags 15-20cm apart horizontally
- Hang in rows with 60cm vertical spacing
- Ideal for people who cannot bend to floor level

This system is particularly suitable for wheelchair users as bags hang at accessible heights.
