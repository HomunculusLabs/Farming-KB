## Limitations

- Root-organ cultures are not whole plants — some physiological processes
  differ from intact plant systems
- The artificial environment may not reflect field conditions
- Not all AMF species are amenable to in vitro culture
- Long-term experiments may be limited by medium exhaustion
- Scale is small compared to soil-based systems
