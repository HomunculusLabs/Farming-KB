## Building Up Orchard Earth Without Fertilizers

The deeper purpose of the natural orchard is to promote the conversion of weathered bedrock into living, organic soil. Fukuoka regarded modern soil management as fundamentally destructive:

> "clean cultivation that turns the soil into mere mineral matter."

He dismissed the common practice of hauling straw from paddy fields to hillside orchards as merely moving biomass around without addressing the underlying problem:

> "Soil management divorced from the field, garden, and hillside is meaningless."
