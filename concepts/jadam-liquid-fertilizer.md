# JADAM Liquid Fertilizer (JLF)

JADAM liquid fertilizer is the simplest fertilizer system in organic farming. The key difference from [[knf-preparations]]: **no sugar, no molasses, no purchased microorganisms**. Only three ingredients: plant material, water, and leaf mold. This is the ultra-low-cost approach that makes [[jadam-methods]] possible at $100/acre/year.

## Core Philosophy

JADAM's nutritional perspective: "Things similar to my crops are good food for my crops." The best fertilizer for tomatoes is made from tomatoes. Crop residues contain the exact nutritional balance that crop needs.

## Three Principles of JADAM Fertilizer Making

1. **Anaerobic** (not aerobic) -- close the lid, minimize air exposure
2. **Water and leaf mold** (not sugar and molasses) -- indigenous microbes from leaf mold do the work
3. **Ambient temperature** (not heated) -- culture at same temperature as crops

## Basic Recipe

**Ingredients**:
- Crop residues or wild grass: fill container
- Water: fill to top
- Leaf mold: 1 handful (about 500g for 500L container)

**Process**:
1. Fill container with crop residues, wild grass, or both
2. Fill with water to the top
3. Add a handful of leaf mold
4. Close lid tightly (anaerobic)
5. Place in same environment as crops (ambient temperature, under sun)
6. Ready in 7 days (summer), but JADAM recommends preparing in spring for use next year -- the older the better
7. JLF is like soy sauce: the longer it ages, the better

## Types of JLF

### Wild Grass JLF
- Fill container with any wild grass, add water, leaf mold, close lid
- Dilution: 20-300x (standard: 100x)
- ph 6.9, balanced growth throughout season
- Spring grass has higher nitrogen (1.5-2.5% N dried weight)

### Purslane JLF
- Fastest decomposing wild grass (ready in 10 days)
- Higher nitrogen than other wild grasses
- Effective fruit color enhancer
- Dilution: 20-300x

### Crop Residue JLF (Customized)
- Use fruits, leaves, branches of the target crop
- Tomato JLF for tomatoes, strawberry JLF for strawberries
- Diseased fruits are fine -- microbial diversity prevents pathogen dominance
- Best nutritional match for the crop
- Fill half container with water, add leaf mold, add crop residues as produced

### Nitrogen-Rich JLF
- Food waste JLF (2.93% N), human feces JLF (2.48% N), urine JLF (3.92% N), fish JLF (5.08-7.44% N)
- Use when GC-JLF feels weak and growth needs boosting
- Make separately from plant-based JLF

## Dilution and Application

| Method | Dilution | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Standard soil drench | x100 | Start here, adjust by observation |
| Light feeding | x300-500 | Strong root settlement |
| Heavy feeding | x20-50 | Poor soil, rapid growth needed |
| Foliar | x30+ | Must be well-filtered + mixed with JWA |

Calculate based on 1 ton (264 gal) water per 0.03 ha (0.08 ac).

## Cost Comparison with KNF

| Factor | JADAM JLF | KNF FAA/WCA |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Sugar/molasses | None required | Required for all inputs |
| Microbe source | Free leaf mold | Purchased or collected IMO |
| Fermentation vessel | Any container with lid | Specific vessels, aeration |
| Heating | None (ambient) | Often recommended |
| Time to make | 7 days usable, better aged | 7-14+ days per input |
| Cost | Near zero | Low but higher |
| Number of inputs | One (JLF) | Multiple (FPJ, FAA, OHN, etc.) |
| pH | ~6.5-7.0 (neutral) | Often acidic from sugar |

## Storage and Refill

- Never remove solids from container
- When liquid is used up, refill with water, add more plant material and leaf mold
- Container interior does not need cleaning
- Tie lid tightly to minimize evaporation for long-term storage
- Use filter system: net-covered box with rock, sunk in JLF, pump out filtered liquid

## Optional Additions

- Sea salt: up to 0.1% salinity (0.5kg per 500L) -- except for human waste JLF
- Phyllite powder: 0.2% (1kg per 500L) -- reduces smell
- Foliar application: always filter well + mix with JWA (3L per 500L)
