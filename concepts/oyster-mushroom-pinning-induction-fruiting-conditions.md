## Humidity Management During Fruiting

High relative humidity of 80-95% is essential during fruitbody formation. Humidity is
maintained by frequent light spraying on substrate blocks and the growing room floor.
Spraying must be light and frequent — water pooling on pins or caps causes bacterial
rot. During pinning, higher humidity (90-95%) encourages primordia. As fruitbodies
develop, humidity can be reduced slightly (85-90%) but must never drop below 80%.

In tropical regions, high ambient humidity is an advantage but temperature control is
challenging. In temperate regions, supplemental humidification may be needed. The
growing room should be checked multiple times daily based on hygrometer readings and
[[pf-tek-substrate-moisture-diagnosis-and-correction]] and even light and air exposure.
