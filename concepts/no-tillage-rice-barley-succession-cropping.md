Masanobu Fukuoka's direct-seeded, no-tillage rice/barley succession
cropping system is one of the most remarkable achievements in
agricultural history. For over thirty years, his fields on the Dogo
Plain in Ehime Prefecture, Japan, were never plowed or turned, yet
they consistently produced close to 22 bushels (1,300 pounds) of
winter grain and 22 bushels of rice per quarter-acre. His goal was
to eventually harvest 33 bushels per quarter-acre. The system
