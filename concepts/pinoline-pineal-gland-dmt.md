# Pinoline, the Pineal Gland, and Endogenous DMT

## Overview

Pinoline (6-methoxytetrahydro-beta-carboline) is a naturally occurring monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) produced by the **pineal gland** in the human brain. It plays a central role in the neurochemistry of sleep, visionary experiences, and potentially in naturally occurring psychic phenomena. The pineal gland is also known to produce **dimethyltryptamine (DMT)** — a powerful psychoactive compound — and the combination of endogenous DMT with pinoline's MAO-inhibiting action has been proposed as a biological mechanism underlying dream states, [[psilocybin-mystical-experiences-personality-change]], and the visionary effects of certain entheogenic plant brews like **Ayahuasca**.

## The Pineal Gland as the "Third Eye"

The pineal gland, a small endocrine gland located deep within the brain between the two hemispheres, has been associated with spiritual vision across numerous mystical traditions — from the Hindu concept of the "third eye" (ajna chakra) to Descartes' designation of it as the "seat of the soul." Modern neurochemistry has revealed that the pineal gland is indeed a remarkably active neurochemical factory, producing melatonin, pinoline, and potentially DMT.

Research by Jace Callaway and others has demonstrated that the pineal gland synthesizes pinoline during sleep. Pinoline functions as a beta-carboline MAO inhibitor, meaning it temporarily disables the monoamine oxidase enzymes that would normally break down tryptamine compounds in the brain. This creates a biochemical window in which endogenous DMT can remain active in neural synapses rather than being rapidly metabolized.

## DMT and Pinoline: The Endogenous Entheogenic Combination

The DMT-pinoline combination produced naturally by the pineal gland during sleep is considered to be the endogenous equivalent of the Ayahuasca brew. Ayahuasca, used traditionally by indigenous peoples of the Amazon basin, combines:

1. **DMT-containing plants** (e.g., *[[chacruna-psychotria-viridis]]*, *Diplopterys cabrerana*)
2. **MAOI-containing plants** (e.g., *Banisteriopsis caapi*, Syrian Rue/*[[mckenna-food-gods-peganum-harmala-soma]]*)

The MAOI component is essential — without it, DMT consumed orally is rapidly deactivated by monoamine oxidase enzymes in the digestive tract and liver, producing no psychoactive effect. The beta-carboline harmala alkaloids in *Banisteriopsis caapi* serve the same function that pinoline serves in the brain: inhibiting MAO to allow DMT to reach and activate neural receptors.

This parallel between the indigenous Amazonian brew and the brain's own biochemistry is striking. It suggests that the visionary states experienced during sleep — particularly during REM dreaming and deep meditative states — may be mediated by the same receptor systems (primarily 5-HT2A [[psilocybin-pharmacology-and-serotonin-receptors]]) activated by entheogenic plants.

## Somnambulism and the Pineal Secretions

The connection between pineal secretions and altered consciousness is further supported by the phenomenon of **somnambulism** (sleepwalking). The linguistic connection between "Soma" — the ancient Vedic entheogenic sacrament — and "somniferum" (the opium poppy species *[[papaver-somniferum]]*) reflects an ancient recognition that sleep states and entheogenic states share common neurochemical territory. During sleep, particularly in deep states, the pineal gland excretes both DMT and pinoline, producing the vivid visionary experiences we call dreams.

## The Five-Plant Soma Theory

Research into the identity of the ancient Vedic sacrament **Soma** has produced numerous candidates, none of which fully accounts for the descriptions found in the Rig Veda when considered individually. The candidates include:

- **Amanita muscaria** (fly agaric mushroom) — Gordon Wasson's influential hypothesis
- **Psilocybin mushrooms** — supported by Terence McKenna and others for producing ecstatic visionary states
- **Syrian Rue** (*Peganum harmala*) — proposed by Flattery and Schwartz, containing the MAOI harmine/harmaline
- **Opium Poppy** (*Papaver somniferum*) — producing euphoric relaxation that facilitates visionary states
- **Cannabis and other DMT-containing green plants** — used to prevent nausea and enhance other entheogens

The emerging synthesis is that **Soma was not a single plant but a multi-plant admixture**. When these plants are understood in combination — the MAOI properties of Syrian Rue allowing oral DMT activity, the visionary mushroom compounds, the relaxing euphoria of opium, and the nausea-preventing and pathway-reactivating properties of cannabis — they together produce an experience matching the Vedic descriptions far better than any single candidate.

The five-color theme (red mushroom, yellow opium, green vine, and the four specific plants against a fifth green background) recurs across many mystical traditions, suggesting a cross-cultural recognition of this multi-plant entheogenic formula.

## Implications for Understanding Consciousness

The pineal gland's production of pinoline and DMT has profound implications for our understanding of consciousness:

1. **Dreams as entheogenic experiences**: The vivid, sometimes profoundly meaningful content of dreams may be the result of endogenous DMT activation moderated by pinoline's MAO inhibition.

2. **Meditation and visionary states**: Deep meditative practices, sensory deprivation, and certain breathing techniques may upregulate pineal activity, producing experiences phenomenologically similar to those induced by exogenous entheogens.

3. **Naturally occurring psychic experiences**: The theory proposes that pinoline may be responsible for spontaneous visionary or "psychic" experiences reported across cultures — experiences that have historically been attributed to divine intervention or spiritual revelation.

4. **The death experience**: Both the pineal chemistry and the effects of entheogenic plants produce experiences that users consistently describe as involving a "death and rebirth" — a dissolution of ego-boundaries followed by reconstruction of self-understanding. This parallels the core practice of Shamanism, which uses the death experience as preparation for actual death.

## Neurotransmitter Pathways

The primary receptor system involved is the **serotonin 5-HT2A receptor**, which both DMT and classical psychedelics (psilocybin, LSD) activate. Pinoline's role as an MAOI increases the availability of serotonin and other monoamine neurotransmitters at synaptic clefts, potentially sensitizing these receptors. The interaction creates a unique neurochemical state characterized by:

- Enhanced sensory perception and pattern recognition
- Dissolution of [[default-mode-network-and-ego-dissolution]] activity (associated with ego/self-referential processing)
- Increased connectivity between normally segregated brain regions
- Heightened emotional and cognitive flexibility
