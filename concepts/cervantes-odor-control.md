# Odor Control in Cannabis Gardens
A good exhaust fan, vented outdoors, is the first step
in [[cervantes-ozone-generators-negative-ion-odor]], activated charcoal filters,
or a combination of two or more of these solutions.
See also: [[cervantes-ventilation-fan-carbon-filter-setup]] to further scrub the air.
### Ozone Generator Selection
When shopping for an ozone generator, look for one that
has been on the market for a few years with an
established track record. Important features include
self-cleaning (or easy to clean) and safe bulb
replacement. Ozone generators are rated by the number
of cubic feet they are able to treat.
### Safety Warnings
UV light is very dangerous and can burn skin and
retinas beyond repair. Never look at the UV lamp in an
ozone generator. Legal exposure for humans is about 0.1
ppm for a maximum of 8 hours. Most grow room ozone
generators produce about 0.05 ppm at timed intervals.
When UV light encounters moisture, nitric acid is
produced as a white, powdery byproduct. This corrosive
acid can severely burn skin and eyes. Ensure the
generator has proper safety features such as a
maintenance switch.
### Ozone Damage to Plants
For best results, keep the ozone generator in another
room or isolated from growing plants. Ozone causes
chlorotic spots on leaves that may first appear as a
[[stamets-growing-room-air-circulation-co2-management]] through it before evacuation.
Once generated, ozone has a life of about 30 minutes.
It takes a minute or two for the O3 molecules to
combine with oxygen to neutralize odors.
## Activated Carbon Filters
Activated charcoal filters are fantastic and effective
at removing odors. The charcoal is "activated" with
oxygen, which opens millions of pores in the carbon.
The activated charcoal absorbs odor molecules and
other pollutants from the air.
### Three Key Rules for Carbon Filters
1. **Keep humidity below 55 percent.** At about 65 to
   70 percent relative humidity, charcoal absorbs
   moisture and clogs. At 80 percent humidity, it
   stops removing odors entirely.
2. **Air must move slowly** through charcoal filters
   to extract odors. The fan on professional units
   allows just enough airflow for adequate dwell
   time so odors are absorbed.
