The [[comparison-natural-farming-vs-permaculture]] system. It is made from herbs that are full of
energy and function to increase plant robustness, to sterilize, and
to keep plants warm. OHN revitalizes crops and activates their growth.
It is made from popular oriental herbs such as Angelica, Acutiloba,
Licorice, and Cinnamon that are fermented, not boiled, to maintain
the vigorous growth of crops.

## The Three OHN Components

OHN consists of three separate preparations that are mixed just before
use. The ingredients may be classified as:

- **Raw state**: Garlic and Ginger
- **Dry state**: Cinnamon bark

Each component is prepared separately and then combined at the time of
application.

## Cinnamon OHN Preparation

### Materials Needed

1. Cinnamon bark (250 grams)
2. Rice wine or Beer (750 ml)
3. Jars / Bottles
4. Porous paper
5. Rubber band

### Preparation Steps

1. Put cinnamon bark (250 g) in a jar and fill with beer so that
   the bark is completely soaked.
2. Fill the jar to 2/3 capacity. The amount of beer should be such
   that it fully wets the ingredients but is not excessive. Let it
   absorb moisture for 1 or 2 days.
3. Add jaggery equivalent to the weight of the ingredients.
4. Cover the jar using porous paper and tie with a rubber band.
5. Leave for 3 to 5 days for initial fermentation.
6. Stir every day with a stick in the morning for two weeks.
7. After fermentation, pour distilled liquor into the remaining 1/3
   space of the jar for long storage.
8. If using within 45 days, no need to add liquor; water will do.

## Garlic / Ginger OHN Preparation

### Materials Needed

1. Fresh garlic (whole bulb, 1 kg) or fresh ginger (1 kg)
2. Jaggery / Brown sugar (1 kg, 1:1 ratio)
3. Separate jars for garlic and ginger
4. Porous paper
5. Rubber band / vinyl film

### Selecting Ingredients

- **Garlic**: Select freshly harvested garlic. Do not wash with water.
  Crush everything including the skins and roots, which contain
  moisture. Do not crush too finely.
- **Ginger**: Collect ginger. Do not wash with water. Shake the soil
  off. Crush (do not crush too finely).

### Preparation Steps

1. Put 1 kg of crushed garlic or ginger in a separate jar.
2. Add the same amount of jaggery (1 kg, 1:1 ratio) and cover with
   porous paper. The mixture should occupy only 2/3 of the jar. This
   is very important for good fermentation.
3. Cover with a tight lid or vinyl film. Stir the mixture gently
   clockwise every morning for a week. Leave for 4 to 6 days.
4. Filter the content and keep the extraction in another jar for
   long-term storage.
5. If the extracting process is difficult, add water to extract the
   juice. This can be used within 45 days.
6. To preserve for a longer period, add liquor to extract the juice
   more easily.
