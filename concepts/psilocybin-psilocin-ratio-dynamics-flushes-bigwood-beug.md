## Street Sample Ratios

variable ratios:

- Some samples showed **high psilocybin, moderate psilocin** —
  consistent with later-flush harvested material
- Other samples contained **very low psilocin** relative to psilocybin
  — possibly first-flush material or samples dried at low
  temperatures
