# 2C-C — 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Chlorophenethylamine

## Overview

2C-C (PIHKAL #22) is a synthetic psychedelic phenethylamine and a member of the 2C family. The 4-chloro substituent gives 2C-C moderate potency and a relatively short, manageable duration. It is considered one of the gentler members of the 2C series, often described as producing warm, colorful visuals with less intensity than [[2c-b-compound-profile]] or mescaline.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name:** 2,5-dimethoxy-4-chlorophenethylamine
- **PIHKAL number:** #22
- **Class:** 2C family (substituted phenethylamine, NOT amphetamine)
- **HCl melting point:** 218-221 deg C (220-222 deg C recrystallized)

## Synthesis (from PIHKAL)

Two synthetic routes are described:

**From 2C-H:** The free base of 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine is dissolved in glacial acetic acid, cooled to 0 deg C, and treated dropwise with liquid chlorine. After quenching with water, washing, and treatment with sodium dithionite (to discharge color), the reaction is made basic and extracted. The free base is crystallized as the hydrochloride from IPA. NMR confirmed the 4-position chlorination by the presence of two para-protons at 7.12 and 7.20 ppm.

**From 2C-B:** 2C-B is phthalimide-protected, then subjected to a Rosenmund-von Braun reaction using cuprous chloride in DMF to replace bromine with chlorine. The phthalimide is removed with hydrazine, yielding 2C-C. This route demonstrates the synthetic interconvertibility within the 2C series.

## Effects

- Warm, colorful visual enhancement
- Moderate psychedelic experience -- less intense than [[lsd-compound-profile]] or [[2c-b-compound-profile]]
- Mild body sensations without heavy body load
- Enhanced appreciation of art and music
- Relaxed, contemplative headspace
- Relatively easy comedown
- Some users report it as a "beginner-friendly" 2C compound

## Qualitative Comments (from PIHKAL)

**(20 mg):** "This is longer lived than 2C-B, and there is a longer latency in coming on. It took an hour and a half, or even two hours to get there. It had a slight metallic overtone."

**(24 mg):** "I was at a moderately high and thoroughly favorable place, for several hours. It seemed to be a very sensual place, but without too much in the way of visual distraction."

**(40 mg):** "There were a lot of visuals -- something that I had noted at lower levels. There seems to be less stimulation than with 2C-B, and in some ways it is actually sedating. And yet I was up all night. It was like a very intense form of relaxation."

**(50 mg):** Reports mention usage of up to 50 milligrams which seems to increase yet further the intensity and the duration. One report of an intravenous administration of 20 milligrams described the response as overwhelming, with effects peaking at about 5 minutes and lasting for perhaps 15 minutes.

## Extensions and Commentary

The halogens represent a small group of atoms unique for being monovalent and negative, meaning they can be stable when attached to an aromatic nucleus but cannot be modified or extended. They represent a "dead end" in the 2C-X series. The heaviest, iodine, was explored as 2C-I (phenethylamine) and DOI (amphetamine) -- the most potent of the halides. The next lighter, bromine, gives 2C-B and DOB -- the most broadly explored. 2C-C represents the chlorine counterpart, with the corresponding amphetamine being DOC. These are less potent still, and much less explored, perhaps because chlorine is a gas and troublesome to handle (bromine is a liquid, iodine is a solid). The fluorine analogue is yet harder to make and requires indirect procedures.

## Pharmacology

2C-C acts as a 5-HT2A receptor agonist. As a phenethylamine (not amphetamine), it lacks the alpha-methyl group that gives the DOx compounds and [[mdma-compound-profile]] their stimulant properties. The chloro substituent at the 4-position provides moderate receptor affinity. See [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]] and [[phenethylamine-chemistry-basics]].

## Comparison within the 2C Family

2C-C is the phenethylamine analogue of [[doc-compound-profile]] -- both share the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-chloro pattern, but 2C-C lacks the alpha-methyl group, resulting in shorter duration and less stimulant effect. It is less potent than [[2c-p-compound-profile]] but longer-lasting than [[2c-d-compound-profile]]. Compared to [[2c-b-compound-profile]], it has a longer onset (1.5-2 hours vs 30-60 minutes) and slightly less intensity. Users have expressed a preference for 2C-C over 2C-B due to its gentler stimulation profile.

## Detailed Effects Profile

The subjective effects of 2C-C can be organised by category:
- **Visual**: Enhanced colour saturation, pattern recognition, geometric patterning (less intense than [[2c-b-compound-profile]]), mild visual drifting and breathing of surfaces, increased appreciation of art and visual media. Visuals tend to be warm-toned with a gentle quality rather than the sharp, high-contrast visuals of [[lsd-compound-profile]].
- **Cognitive**: Mild introspection, enhanced creative thinking, contemplative mood. The headspace is generally described as clear and lucid, with less confusion or disorientation than more potent psychedelics. Some users report a meditative quality at moderate doses.
- **Physical**: Mild body lightness or warmth, slight tactile enhancement. Body load is notably low compared to other 2C compounds -- this is one of 2C-C's most appreciated qualities. Nausea is uncommon at moderate doses but may occur at higher doses.
- **Emotional**: Gentle mood elevation, increased empathy, relaxed and open emotional state. Anxiety is rare at common doses, contributing to its reputation as a beginner-friendly compound.
- **Sensory**: Enhanced music appreciation, heightened tactile sensitivity, richer taste perception. The sensory enhancement is smooth and pleasant rather than overwhelming.

## Pharmacology in Detail

2C-C acts primarily as a serotonin 5-HT2A receptor agonist, the mechanism shared by all classical psychedelics. The 2,5-dimethoxy substitution pattern on the phenethylamine ring is critical for 5-HT2A affinity -- this pattern is conserved across the entire 2C series and the DOx series. The 4-chloro substituent provides moderate lipophilicity and receptor affinity, positioning 2C-C between the more potent 2C-B (4-bromo) and the less potent 2C-F (4-fluoro) in terms of receptor binding.

Additional receptor interactions likely include 5-HT2C (contributing to mild appetite suppression), 5-HT1A (potentially contributing to the relaxed, anxiolytic quality), and trace affinity for adrenergic receptors. Unlike the DOx compounds (which have an alpha-methyl group), 2C-C is not a substrate for monoamine oxidase (MAO), giving it a shorter duration of action.

The lack of an alpha-methyl group is pharmacologically significant. This group in the DOx series protects against MAO degradation, extending duration to 12-24 hours and adding significant stimulant character. Without it, 2C-C is metabolised relatively quickly, accounting for its 4-8 hour duration and gentle stimulation profile. See [[phenethylamine-receptor-pharmacology]] for receptor binding data across the 2C family.

## Synthesis Notes and Safety

The synthesis of 2C-C requires handling of chlorine gas or reactive chlorine donors, which introduces hazards beyond those of other 2C compounds. The PIHKAL route from 2C-H involves direct chlorination with liquid chlorine in glacial acetic acid at 0 deg C, followed by careful quenching and treatment with sodium dithionite to discharge the characteristic green colour of chlorinated intermediates. The alternative route from 2c-b-compound-profile|2C-B via Rosenmund-von Braun reaction uses cuprous chloride in DMF at elevated temperatures. Both routes require familiarity with organic synthesis techniques, proper fume ventilation, and protective equipment. The chlorination step must be carefully controlled to avoid over-chlorination or substitution at unintended positions on the aromatic ring.

2C-C occupies a unique niche in the 2C family as the most physically comfortable and least stimulating member. While [[2c-b-compound-profile]] remains the most popular and widely available 2C compound, users seeking a gentler, more relaxing experience often prefer 2C-C. [[2c-i-compound-profile]] is significantly more potent and visually intense, making it less suitable for inexperienced users. The trend in the 2C halogen series is clear: as halogen atomic weight increases (F to Cl to Br to I), potency increases, duration may extend, and stimulant character becomes more pronounced.

## Harm Reduction Guidelines

- **Dose carefully**: 2C-C has a slower onset than most psychedelics. Wait a full 2 hours before considering any redosing. Taking a second dose too early is the most common cause of overwhelming experiences with 2C-C.
- **Start low**: First-time users should begin at 15-20 mg. Even experienced psychedelic users should respect the dose-response curve, as individual sensitivity varies considerably.
- **Test your material**: Reagent testing with Marquis, Mecke, and Simons reagents can help identify 2C-C and distinguish it from other substances. 2C-C typically shows a yellow reaction with Marquis reagent.
- **Set and setting**: While 2C-C is gentler than most psychedelics, it still produces genuine altered states. A calm, familiar environment and a trusted trip sitter are recommended, especially at doses above 30 mg.
- **Avoid mixing**: Do not combine with other serotonergic substances (SSRIs, MAOIs, other psychedelics, [[mdma-compound-profile]]) due to the theoretical risk of serotonin syndrome. Avoid combining with alcohol or stimulants.
- **Health contraindications**: Individuals with a personal or family history of psychosis, severe cardiovascular conditions, or who are pregnant or breastfeeding should avoid all psychedelics including 2C-C.
- **Hydration**: Maintain normal fluid intake. 2C-C is not typically dehydrating, but altered states of consciousness can lead to forgetting to drink water.

## Legal Status by Jurisdiction

- **United States**: Schedule I under the Federal Analog Act. 2C-C is not separately scheduled but is treated as a controlled substance analog of 2C-B.
- **United Kingdom**: Class A under the Misuse of Drugs Act, as a phenethylamine substituted in the 2-position. Covered by the generic tryptamine/phenethylamine catch-all provisions.
- **European Union**: Many EU member states have scheduled the 2C family under generic legislation or individual scheduling. Germany classifies it under BtMG Anlage I.
- **Canada**: Schedule II under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act.
- **Australia**: Schedule 9 (Prohibited Substance). Australia was an early adopter of 2C family scheduling, adding many 2C compounds in the early 2000s.
- **Netherlands**: Despite the Netherlands' permissive approach to some substances, 2C-C is listed as a Schedule I hard drug under the Opium Law.
- **Japan**: Controlled under the Pharmaceutical Affairs Law. Japan has been particularly aggressive in scheduling novel psychoactive substances.

As with all [[psychedelic-safety-and-harm-reduction]], legal status varies by jurisdiction and is subject to change. Users should verify current local laws.
