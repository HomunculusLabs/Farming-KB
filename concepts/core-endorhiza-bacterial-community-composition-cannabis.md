## Core Versus Cultivar-Specific Communities

The core community represents taxa colonizing any cannabis root regardless of genotype. Cultivar-specific additions represent host genotype-dependent selection outcomes. Methylophilus exemplifies this pattern.

Methylophilus comprised 13% of the Bookoo Kush endorhiza community, 0.13% in Burmese, and was absent in Sour Diesel. Statistical evidence supports the layered model: 690 OTUs differed between soil types by presence/absence, 71 between cultivars by abundance only, and zero by presence/absence.

Cultivars modulate abundances within the existing soil-derived community rather than introducing new taxa. The core provides structural and functional foundation, while cultivar-specific adjustments fine-tune composition.
