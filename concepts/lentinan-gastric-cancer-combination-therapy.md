# Lentinan Gastric Cancer Combination Therapy

Lentinan, the beta-glucan polysaccharide from [[shiitake-medicinal-compounds]], has shown significant clinical efficacy in gastric cancer treatment, particularly when combined with the chemotherapeutic agent tegafur.

## Lentinan Overview

Lentinan was first isolated by Chihara et al. (1970) and demonstrated anti-tumour effects greater than other mushroom polysaccharides for some, but not all, tumour types. It is classified as a medicine in Japan (unlike LEM and LAP from the same mushroom, which are food supplements).

## Clinical Trial: Lentinan Plus Tegafur

### Study Design
A randomised controlled study compared tegafur alone versus Lentinan plus tegafur in patients with inoperable or recurrent gastric cancer.

- Total patients: 145
- Tegafur alone: 68 patients
- Lentinan plus tegafur: 77 patients
- Lentinan administered intraperitoneally

### Survival Results
- Tegafur alone: 50% survival at 92 days
- Lentinan + tegafur: 50% survival at 173 days
- Near doubling of median survival time

### Long-Term Survival
- 19.5% survived more than one year
- 10.4% survived more than two years
- 6.5% survived more than three years

### Response Rate
Using Japan Society for Cancer Therapy criteria:
- Lentinan group: 14.9% response rate
- Control group: 2.0% response rate

## Sub-Group Analysis by Prognostic Factor

### Tumour Extension
- Abdominal localization: tegafur alone 166 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 237 days

### Metastasis Status
- Hepatic or peritoneal metastasis: tegafur alone 68 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 169 days (P < 0.01)
- Distant metastasis: tegafur alone 170 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 133 days

### Histology
- Well-differentiated adenocarcinoma: tegafur alone 105 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 223 days (P < 0.01)
- Poorly-differentiated adenocarcinoma: tegafur alone 91 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 169 days (P < 0.01)

### Borrmann Classification
- Types 1 and 2: tegafur alone 119 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 391 days
- Types 3 and 4: tegafur alone 100 days vs. Lentinan + tegafur 163 days (P < 0.01)

## Immune Correlates

When patients responded well to Lentinan:
- Significantly larger response (2.5x) in killer T cell/suppressor T cell ratio (CD11- CD8+/CD11+ CD8+)
- Higher ratio of NK cells with higher activity to NK with moderate activity (CD57- CD16+/CD57+ CD16+)
- These ratios correlated well with survival times

However, a later study suggested lymphocyte subset changes in peripheral blood did not necessarily correlate with changes occurring in the tumour itself.

## Safety Profile

In a detailed study of 469 patients:
- 32 patients (6.8%) experienced adverse reactions
- None were serious
- Only 2 patients required treatment discontinuation
- Total episodes: 46 (9.8%)

### Adverse Reactions (>0.5% incidence)
- Rash/redness: 1.9%
- Chest pressure: 1.7%
- Nausea/vomiting: 1.7%
- Headache: 0.6%
- Feeling of warmth: 0.6%
- Diaphoresis: 0.6%

Other reactions (<0.5%): fever, transient hot flushes, anorexia, leukopenia, dizziness, decreased RBC, decreased haemoglobin, pharyngitis.

## Chemotherapy Side-Effect Reduction

Lentinan appears to greatly reduce debilitating chemotherapy effects including nausea, pain, hair loss, and lowered immune status. This anecdotal evidence has been noted as a feature of many mushroom polysaccharides.
