## Third Bardo Sensations of Limitation

### Stupidity and Incoherent Thought

During the [[third-bardo]], the voyager may experience a profound sense of **stupidity** and incoherent thinking. This is a natural and expected result of the limited perspective imposed by the re-emerging ego. Having experienced the expansive, visionary clarity of the earlier bardos, the return to conceptual, language-bound thought feels conspicuously inadequate. The mind gropes for words and categories that cannot contain what has been witnessed. This sensation is not a failure but a structural feature of the re-entry process — the ego is too small a vessel for the experiences it is trying to re-integrate.

### Feelings of Death and Separation

A common experience is the feeling of being **dead** or cut off from surrounding life. The voyager may perceive other people as **robots performing meaningless gestures** — going through the motions of life without authentic presence. This vision reflects the voyager's own partial disconnection from the embodied, social world. The ego has not yet fully re-engaged, and from the transitional perspective, ordinary human activity appears mechanical and hollow. This is a temporary stage, not a permanent perception.

### Oppression and Crushing Sensations

Feelings of **oppression** and crushing weight frequently occur. The voyager may envision **metallic nets, cages, or mechanical devices** closing in. These images symbolize **premature ego re-entry** — the ego-structure attempting to reassert itself before the consciousness is ready. The metallic, industrial quality of these visions contrasts sharply with the organic, radiant imagery of earlier phases. They represent the rigidity of conditioned mental patterns clamping down on the free-flowing awareness of the bardo state.

### Grey Twilight Light

The visual field may shift from the **brilliant radiance** characteristic of [[the-second-bardo-and-hallucinatory-visions]] to a subdued **grey twilight**. This dimming of light mirrors the dimming of consciousness as the voyager approaches consensus reality. The loss of luminous intensity can be frightening and may be interpreted as a regression, but it is a natural part of the descent back into the shared human world.

## The Six Worlds of Game Existence (Sangsara)

The Tibetan Buddhist cosmology describes **six realms** or **worlds of game existence** (sangsara) into which consciousness may be drawn during re-entry. Each world represents a different **level of game existence** — a particular style of ego-involvement, attachment, and suffering. The level into which one enters is determined by **karma** — the accumulated tendencies, attachments, and patterns of thought carried by the individual.

### 1. The World of Devas (Saints and Sages)

The **devas** inhabit the highest of the six worlds — a realm of pleasure, ecstasy, and spiritual refinement. Beings here experience extraordinary bliss and aesthetic satisfaction. However, their characteristic thraldom is **pleasure itself** — the attachment to ecstasy becomes a trap, preventing further liberation. Those with strong spiritual inclinations and positive karma may experience re-entry visions of saintly or sage-like existence, but even this exalted state remains within sangsara.

### 2. The World of Asuras (Titans and Heroes)

The **asuras** are warrior-like titans inhabiting a realm of **warfare, competition, and heroic struggle**. Their thraldom is **conflict** — an addiction to combat, rivalry, and the exercise of power. The voyager drawn to this world may experience visions of battles, contests, or struggles for dominance. This corresponds to personality types driven by ambition, aggression, and the need to prove themselves against adversaries.

### 3. The World of Human Beings

The **normal human** world represents the middle ground — balanced between the extremes of pleasure and torment. Its characteristic thraldom is **inertia and smug ignorance** — a comfortable, unquestioning acceptance of conventional reality. Humans are considered the most fortunate realm for achieving liberation because the suffering here is neither so extreme as to overwhelm nor so pleasant as to lull one into complacency. Yet most remain trapped in habitual patterns.

### 4. The World of Animals (Primitive Incarnations)

The **animal realm** represents regression to instinctual, primitive modes of being. The voyager may experience visions of being or encountering various animals: **dog, cock, pig, ant, snake, ape, wolf, bird**. The thraldom here is **helplessness and slavery** to instinct, driven by fear, hunger, and biological necessity without the reflective awareness that distinguishes human consciousness. These visions indicate a re-entry dominated by survival-level concerns.

### 5. The World of Pretas (Neurotics and Frustrated Spirits)

The **pretas** are hungry ghosts — beings tormented by **unsatisfied needs, cravings, and frustrated desires**. Their thraldom is **insatiable wanting** — they can never obtain what they seek. In psychological terms, this corresponds to neurotic patterns of compulsive desire, addiction, and perpetual dissatisfaction. The voyager may experience visions of gnawing hunger, thirst, or craving that can never be fulfilled.

### 6. The World of Hell (Psychosis)

The lowest realm corresponds to **hell** — a state of **torment, agony, and psychological fragmentation**. In the context of the [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]], this maps to **psychotic episodes** — complete ego-disintegration experienced as terrifying rather than liberating. The thraldom is pure suffering. Re-entry into this level represents the most difficult and dangerous outcome, where fear, paranoia, and loss of self dominate the experience.

### Karma and Premonitory Visions

The **karma** accumulated through one's life — thought patterns, emotional habits, attachments, and actions — determines which of the six worlds the voyager is drawn toward during re-entry. Each level produces characteristic **signs and premonitory visions** that signal the direction of re-entry. The text emphasizes the critical importance of **allowing the natural course without effort** — struggling against or reaching for particular visions only strengthens the karmic pull toward that level. The liberated approach is to remain detached, witnessing all visions without attachment or aversion.

## The All-Determining Influence of Thought

### Attachment to Material Possessions and Old Games

During re-entry, **attachment to material possessions** or involvement in **old ego games** can severely affect the voyager's psychological balance. The pulling force of familiar desires — status, property, social role, habitual pleasures — acts as a gravitational field drawing consciousness back into specific karmic patterns. The more tightly one clings to these, the more constrained and painful the re-entry becomes.

### Effects of Other Participants' Behavior

The behavior of **other participants** in the session has a powerful influence on the voyager's re-entry. Companions who are calm, loving, and present help stabilize the return; those who are anxious, judgmental, or intrusive can destabilize it. The voyager in the vulnerable re-entry state is highly permeable to the emotional atmosphere created by those around them.

### Love, Trust, and the Power of Good Companions

The text stresses the profound importance of **love and trust** toward companions during the re-entry phase. **Good deeds and positive intentions of friends** can actually **raise the voyager's re-entry level** — pulling them toward a higher, more liberated world rather than a lower one. This is one of the key justifications for having experienced, compassionate guides present during a [[psychedelic-session-instructions]]. The compassionate presence of others functions as a form of spiritual assistance, directly influencing the karmic trajectory of re-entry.

## The Judgment Visions

### The Blame Game and the White and Black Pebbles

A common re-entry vision involves a **judgment scene** reminiscent of the Egyptian weighing of the heart. The voyager may perceive a tribunal where **white and black pebbles** are used to tally good and bad deeds. The **Lord of Death** appears as a terrifying authority figure. This is interpreted psychologically: the Lord of Death is one's own **conscience**, and the **Mirror of Karma** is **memory** — the accumulated record of one's actions and their consequences reflected back with unforgiving clarity. The judgment is self-judgment.

### The Tri-Kaya Doctrine

The text presents the **[[tri-kaya]]** (Three Bodies) doctrine of Tibetan Buddhism as a framework for understanding the structure of reality revealed during the experience:

- **[[dharma-kaya]]** (Truth Body): The ultimate, unconditioned reality — the ground of being beyond all form and concept.
- **[[sambhoga-kaya]]** (Enjoyment Body): The realm of visionary bliss and radiant form — the celestial or archetypal dimension.
- **Nirmana-Kaya** (Emanation Body): The physical, manifested world of ordinary appearance — the world of ego and consensus reality.
- **Adi-Kaya** (First Body): An additional designation (per [[lama-kazi-dawa-samdup]] Dawa-Samdup's interpretation) referring to the primordial, uncreated ground from which the other three bodies emanate.

### Lama Kazi Dawa-Samdup's Interpretation

**Lama Kazi Dawa-Samdup**, the translator whose work underlies the text, interpreted the Tri-Kaya as describing a **hierarchy of reality** that the voyager traverses in reverse during the psychedelic experience — starting from the Nirmana-Kaya (everyday reality), moving through visionary states (Sambhoga-Kaya), approaching the unconditioned (Dharma-Kaya), and potentially touching the primordial source (Adi-Kaya). Re-entry reverses this journey, and the judgment visions mark the point at which the voyager re-encounters the moral and psychological structures of the Nirmana-Kaya.

## Sexual Visions

### The Primal Scene and Psychoanalytic Correspondence

**Sexual visions** during re-entry bear a direct correspondence to the **primal scene** of psychoanalysis. The voyager may experience vivid images of sexual intercourse, often involving parental figures. These visions correspond to Freudian concepts of the **[[oedipus-complex-psychedelic]]** — the foundational sexual and emotional dynamics that structure the ego during early childhood. The appearance of these visions signals that consciousness is approaching the point of ego-construction, tracing the psychological origins of selfhood back to its earliest formative experiences.

### Dangers of Entering the Old Ego Through Sexual Attraction or Repulsion

The text warns that **sexual attraction or repulsion** can serve as a powerful lure drawing the voyager back into the **old ego structure**. The intense charge of sexual imagery creates a gravitational pull toward the familiar self — the personality as it was constructed around early sexual experiences and family dynamics. Both attraction to and rejection of these images reinforce the ego-pattern. The liberated response is to recognize sexual visions as **natural phenomena** of the re-entry process and to avoid engagement with either pole.

### Visions of Conception and Birth

Voyagers may experience symbolic or realistic **visions of conception and birth** — the actual biological process of entering embodied existence. These can range from abstract cellular imagery to vivid experiential re-enactments of the birth process itself. Such visions represent the final stage of re-entry, where consciousness re-anchors in the physical body and resumes its identification with the separate self.

### Symbolic vs. Memory-Based Birth Visions

Birth visions may be **symbolic** (archetypal imagery of emergence, passage through tunnels, breaking through shells or membranes) or **memory-based** (apparent reliving of one's actual birth). Both serve the same psychological function — the re-establishment of ego-boundaries and the return to individual identity. The text advises the voyager to witness these visions with equanimity, understanding them as the natural conclusion of the re-entry process rather than engaging with them as literal truths or occasions for attachment.

## Key Principles for Re-Entry

1. **Non-attachment**: Do not cling to any vision or world, however pleasurable or terrifying.
2. **Non-resistance**: Allow the natural course of re-entry to unfold without effort or struggle.
3. **Recognition**: Understand each vision as a product of one's own karma and mental patterns.
4. **Compassionate companionship**: The presence of loving, trusted guides can elevate the re-entry experience.
5. **Equanimity toward judgment**: The judgment visions are self-generated reflections of conscience and memory.
6. **Awareness of sexual imagery**: Recognize sexual visions as ego-lures tied to the primal construction of selfhood.
