## Basidiocarp Developmental Stages in *Psilocybe cubensis*

## Overview

Badham (1982) divided the development of *Psilocybe cubensis* basidiocarps into four distinct morphological stages. This staging system provides a framework for understanding the temporal sequence of developmental events, including the dramatic tropic switches that reorient the mushroom during its growth from primordium to mature sporulating fruitbody.

## Stage 1: Pileus Initiation

### Morphological Characteristics

- The pileus (cap) shows a **slight swelling** — a barely perceptible enlargement of the primordium tip
- The primordium at this stage is 15-25 mm in total height
- The stipe is the dominant structure; the cap has not yet differentiated significantly
- No gill tissue is visible

### Duration

Approximately 0-24 hours after placement in the wind tunnel (under [[spore-formation-geotropic-response-psilocybe-cubensis-badham-1982]] in some individuals
- When cultures are rotated perpendicular to the wind at this stage, growth is upward rather than into the wind
- This suggests that by Stage 2, the balance between evaporative-demand-driven growth and gravity sensing is shifting

### Key Transition

Stage 2 represents the **transition point** between Phase 1 (anemotropism-dominated) and Phase 2 (geotropism-dominated) growth. The exact timing of this switch varies between individuals and strains.

### The Partial Veil's Role

During Stage 2, the partial veil is fully intact, covering the gill surfaces. Badham proposed that the veil protects the unexpanded upper stipe from wind and light, preventing premature tropic responses that could misorient the developing cap before the geotropic switch at Stage 3.

## Stage 3: Maturity and Sporulation

### Morphological Characteristics

- The pileus opens to a **hemispherical** shape — the cap has expanded and the gills are now exposed
- The partial veil ruptures, often leaving a ring (annulus) on the stipe
- Lamellae are fully developed and facing outward and downward
- Spore production is actively underway
- The stipe may show some curvature from the tropic reorientation

### Duration

Approximately 48-72 hours after Stage 1 onset under controlled conditions.
