## Cannabis Contaminants, Testing, and Quality Control

## Overview

The unregulated nature of cannabis in many jurisdictions raises important
concerns about external toxins and biological pathogens that may be present on
the plant material. Patients and practitioners must be aware of contaminant
risks and take proactive steps to ensure the safety of their medicine.

## Types of Contaminants

### Pesticides

Pesticides on any consumed plant material may increase the body's toxic load
and can contribute in numerous and unpredictable ways to ill health. Cannabis
plants are susceptible to the same pests as other agricultural crops, and
growers may apply insecticides, fungicides, or miticides during cultivation.

**Risk reduction:** Purchasing or growing organic cannabis eliminates this risk
entirely. Organic cultivation avoids synthetic pesticide application and relies
on [[cannabis-elderly-patients]] with compromised immunity

**Potential effects of aspergillus-contaminated cannabis:**
- Aspergillosis (fungal infection of the lungs)
- Allergic reactions
- Respiratory complications
- Systemic infection in severe immunocompromise

## Decontamination Claims vs. Evidence

Many cannabis patients believe that heating cannabis at temperatures of 300°F
(149°C) for a period of 5 minutes will kill aspergillus and other pathogens.
However, Blesching notes that no studies have verified this suggestion for
cannabis specifically.

One relevant study suggested that aspergillus fungus was not present in any
samples of ten medicinal herbs dried and stored with a water activity of less
than 0.81 at temperature ranges of 25 ± 2°C (77 ± 3.6°F). This highlights
the critical importance of proper drying and storage practices for all herbal
medicines, including cannabis.

## Proper Drying and Storage

Key factors in preventing mold growth on cannabis:

1. **Moisture content** — Maintain water activity below 0.81
2. **Temperature** — Store at 25 ± 2°C (77 ± 3.6°F) or cooler
3. **Humidity control** — Use desiccants or climate-controlled storage
4. **Air circulation** — Avoid sealed containers with residual moisture
