## PF Tek Substrate Formula Details

The Psylocybe Fanaticus Tek (PF TEK) revolutionized amateur mushroom
cultivation with its simple, reliable substrate formula. This page
covers the exact ratios, ingredient specifications, and rationale
behind the PF substrate.

## The Basic Formula

The standard PF TEK substrate formula per jar:

| Ingredient | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| [[pf-tek-dry-vermiculite-contaminant-barrier]] (top layer) | 15-20 mL | Contamination barrier |

This produces a 1:2:1 ratio of BRF to vermiculite to water by volume.
The formula fills a standard 240 mL (½ pint) wide-mouth [[sterilization]]
- **Avoid** softened water (high sodium), mineral water with
  excessive calcium, or water with known contaminants
- **Temperature**: Room temperature or slightly warm water mixes
  more easily than cold water

## Mixing Procedure

The PF TEK mixing procedure, in Fanaticus's own words:

1. Measure 120 mL fine vermiculite into a mixing bowl
2. Add 60 mL water and mix thoroughly until the vermiculite is
   evenly moistened but not dripping
3. Add 60 mL brown rice flour and mix until evenly distributed
4. The final mixture should be moist but not wet — when squeezed
   gently, a few drops of water should appear but no streams
5. Load the mixture into jars, filling to approximately 60-70% of
   jar volume
6. Wipe the inside rim of each jar clean with a paper towel
7. Top with a 15-20 mL layer of dry vermiculite — this is the
   critical contamination barrier
8. Cover with foil before sterilization

## The Dry Vermiculite Barrier

The dry vermiculite top layer is a key innovation of the PF TEK:

- Creates a physical barrier between the sterile substrate and
  the inoculation point
- Any contaminant spores that land on the surface must penetrate
  the dry vermiculite to reach the moist, nutritious substrate
- The dry layer lacks moisture and nutrients, preventing most
  contaminant germination
- This barrier allows inoculation through the foil without a
  glovebox or [[comparison-laminar-flow-hood-vs-fruiting-chamber]] in many cases
- The barrier should be loose, not packed, and level with or
  slightly below the jar rim
