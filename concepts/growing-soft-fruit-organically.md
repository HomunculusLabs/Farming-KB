## Black Currants (Ribes nigrum)

**Type:** Bush (5ft).
**Site:** Tolerant of most conditions; heavy, rich soil preferred.
**Time to crop:** 2 years.
**Growing method:** Grown as "stools" -- all stems cut to ground level after harvest to encourage new growth from the base. Prune in winter.

## Raspberries (Rubus idaeus)

**Type:** Canes on wire support, 18in spacing.
**Site:** Tolerant; heavy, rich soil.
**Time to crop:** 1 year.
**Support:** Wire system with posts at each end of the row, wires at 2-3ft intervals. Tie canes to wires as they grow. Cut autumn-fruiting canes to ground level in late winter; summer-fruiting canes are tied to one side while new canes grow on the other, then the old ones are cut out after fruiting.

## Blackberries and Hybrid Berries (Rubus fruticosus)

**Type:** Bramble, 10ft on wire support.
**Site:** Tolerant; any well-drained soil.
**Time to crop:** 1-2 years.
**Propagation:** Tip layering -- in late summer, pull down shoots, pin tips into holes in the soil, cover with soil. Sever from parent when leaves fall, lift and transplant.

## Gooseberries (Ribes uva-crispa)

**Type:** Bush (5ft) or cordon (1-3ft).
**Site:** Sunny, heavy moisture-retentive soil.
**Time to crop:** 1-2 years.
**Pests:** Sawfly (pick off caterpillars by hand), mildew, birds.
**Pruning:** For bushes, create an open goblet shape. For cordons, train as single stems with fruiting spurs.

## Blueberries (Vaccinium corymbosum)

**Type:** Bush (6ft).
**Site:** Sunny, acid soil essential (pH 4.5-5.5).
**Time to crop:** 3-8 years.
**Soil preparation:** If soil is not acid enough, grow in large pots of ericaceous compost. Mulch with pine needles or [[organic-fruit-tree-care-calendar]]
