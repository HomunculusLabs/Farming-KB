## D.T.S. Multi-Line Sativa Hybrid Complex Genetics Cultivation

## Overview

D.T.S. is a sativa [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]]).
- **MK-Ultra**: An indica-dominant [[haze-plum-purple-passion-medical-sativa-strain]] that combines the fuel-heavy terpene profile of Diesel genetics with the legendary potency and flowering characteristics of Haze. Silver Diesel Haze contributes tall stature, extended flowering time, and a complex aromatic profile blending fuel, citrus, and spice.
- **Head Banger**: A potent hybrid known for its hard-hitting cerebral effects, contributing additional THC potency and a sharp, acrid aroma to the D.T.S. profile. (see [[blue-city-diesel-west-coast-blueberry-nyc-diesel-hybrid-oner]] vigor.

## Breeder: BillBerry Farms

BillBerry Farms operates outside the mainstream cannabis seed market, maintaining what S.T. Oner describes as a small but dedicated fanbase. The comparison to the Pixies—the influential alternative rock band that achieved cult status without mainstream commercial success—is apt. BillBerry Farms focuses on tissue culture propagation and genetic refinement rather than volume production, and D.T.S. represents one of their most noteworthy achievements.

Their approach emphasizes:
- **Genetic complexity**: Breeding with multiple parent lines to create novel combinations.
- **Adaptability**: Selecting for plants that perform well across diverse growing conditions.
- **Clone performance**: D.T.S. clones readily, making it accessible to growers who propagate from cuttings.

## Growth Characteristics

### Height and Structure
D.T.S. is remarkably adaptable in terms of height, capable of growing anywhere from 2 to 9 feet depending on cultivation conditions. This extreme range makes it suitable for both indoor grows with height restrictions and outdoor gardens where taller plants are welcome. The plant tends toward bushiness rather than the Christmas-tree shape typical of many sativas, requiring adequate spacing between plants.

### Environmental Adaptability
One of D.T.S."s most notable traits is its lack of fussiness. The strain adapts readily to the conditions it finds itself in, making it forgiving for cultivators who may not have optimized their growing environment. It is not particularly demanding of specific nutrient regimens, light schedules, or temperature ranges, though like all cannabis plants it performs best when given adequate light, nutrition, and ventilation.

### Cloning and Propagation
D.T.S. clones with exceptional ease, producing strong roots quickly from cuttings. This trait makes it popular with commercial growers and those who maintain mother plants for continuous production. Reliable cloning also means that once a desirable phenotype is identified, it can be preserved indefinitely.

### Screen of Green (ScrOG) Suitability
The strain is particularly well-suited to Screen of Green cultivation, where a screen or net is placed above the plants to create an even canopy and maximize light penetration to lower bud sites. D.T.S.'s bushy growth pattern and flexible branches respond well to ScrOG training, producing an even canopy of bud sites that all receive adequate light exposure.

### Flowering Period
Under optimal conditions, D.T.S. completes flowering in approximately 75 days (roughly 10.5 weeks). This places it in the moderate-to-long flowering category for sativa-dominant strains—longer than many indica hybrids but shorter than pure Haze or landrace sativas that can require 14+ weeks.

### Yield
D.T.S. produces a good-to-above-average yield, with dense, resinous buds that carry the complex aroma profile inherited from its diverse parentage. The combination of yield quantity and quality makes it a favorite among growers who prioritize both weight and potency.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

D.T.S. produces a complex aroma that reflects its multi-line heritage:
- **Earthy base notes**: From the Cheese/Skunk and Kush lineage.
- **Citrus undertones**: Contributed by the Diesel and Haze genetics.
- **Sharp, pungent cheese**: The Cheese influence is detectable but not overwhelming, blending with rather than dominating the overall profile.

The flavor on consumption follows the aroma closely, with an earthy-citrus character that is more nuanced than the sometimes one-dimensional profiles of simpler crosses.

## Effects

D.T.S. delivers a strong, intense head high accompanied by a pleasant body buzz. S.T. Oner describes it as "perfect weed for kicking back and talking about Lord of the Rings for hours with your buddies"—capturing the strain's combination of cerebral engagement and physical comfort. The MK-Ultra influence provides a grounding body component that prevents the Haze and Diesel elements from becoming overly racy or anxiety-inducing.

The onset is moderately rapid, with the head high arriving first followed by the body relaxation building over 30–60 minutes. Duration is typical for sativa-dominant hybrids, lasting 2–3 hours depending on dosage and individual tolerance.

## Medical Applications

D.T.S.'s balanced effects profile makes it potentially useful for:
- **Stress relief**: The combination of mental uplift and body relaxation addresses both psychological and physical tension.
- **Mild to moderate pain**: The body buzz provides analgesic effects without heavy sedation.
- **Social anxiety**: The euphoric head high can facilitate social interaction and conversation.
- **Mood enhancement**: Useful for temporary relief from mild depression or low mood.
