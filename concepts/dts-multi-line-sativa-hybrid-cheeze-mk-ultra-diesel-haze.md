# D.T.S. Multi-Line Sativa Hybrid Complex Genetics Cultivation

## Overview

D.T.S. is a sativa [[sativa-haze-lineage-breeding-history]]-dominant hybrid bred by BillBerry Farms of the United States. What makes D.T.S. remarkable among modern cannabis strains is its extraordinarily complex genetic background, combining no fewer than four distinct and highly regarded genetic lines into a single cultivar. The name D.T.S. stands as a testament to BillBerry Farms' approach to breeding: meticulous, small-scale, and quality-focused, eschewing mainstream acclaim in favor of a dedicated following of knowledgeable cultivators.

## Genetic Background

D.T.S. is the product of a four-way cross:

**Line 1: Cheeze × MK-Ultra**
- **Cheeze** (also spelled Cheese): One of the most famous strains to emerge from the UK cannabis scene. Cheese is a Skunk #1 phenotype selected in the late 1980s for its intensely pungent, cheese-like aroma. The Cheese clone became one of the most widely circulated phenotypes in European cannabis culture, known for its distinctive smell and balanced relaxing effects. (see [[east-coast-sour-diesel-clone-lineage-chemdawg-family-tree]]).
- **MK-Ultra**: An indica-dominant [[haze-plum-purple-passion-medical-sativa-strain]] strain created by crossing G-13 with OG Kush. Named after the CIA's notorious mind-control program, MK-Ultra is known for its extremely potent, hypnotic effects and deep body relaxation. The strain won first place in the Indica category at the 2003 High Times Cannabis Cup.

**Line 2: Silver Diesel Haze × Head Banger**
- **Silver Diesel Haze**: A Diesel-family hybrid [[haze-plum-purple-passion-medical-sativa-strain]] that combines the fuel-heavy terpene profile of Diesel genetics with the legendary potency and flowering characteristics of Haze. Silver Diesel Haze contributes tall stature, extended flowering time, and a complex aromatic profile blending fuel, citrus, and spice.
- **Head Banger**: A potent hybrid known for its hard-hitting cerebral effects, contributing additional THC potency and a sharp, acrid aroma to the D.T.S. profile. (see [[cannabis-sativa-vs-indica-guide]]).

The crossing of these two composite lines (Cheeze × MK-Ultra) × (Silver Diesel Haze × Head Banger) creates a genetic tapestry that draws from Skunk, Kush, Diesel, Haze, G-13, and Cheese lineages, among others. This extraordinary genetic diversity contributes to D.T.S.'s notable phenotypic variation and hybrid [[blue-city-diesel-west-coast-blueberry-nyc-diesel-hybrid-oner]] vigor.

## Breeder: BillBerry Farms

BillBerry Farms operates outside the mainstream cannabis seed market, maintaining what S.T. Oner describes as a small but dedicated fanbase. The comparison to the Pixies—the influential alternative rock band that achieved cult status without mainstream commercial success—is apt. BillBerry Farms focuses on tissue culture propagation and genetic refinement rather than volume production, and D.T.S. represents one of their most noteworthy achievements.

Their approach emphasizes:
- **Genetic complexity**: Breeding with multiple parent lines to create novel combinations.
- **Adaptability**: Selecting for plants that perform well across diverse growing conditions.
- **Clone performance**: D.T.S. clones readily, making it accessible to growers who propagate from cuttings.

## Growth Characteristics

### Height and Structure
D.T.S. is remarkably adaptable in terms of height, capable of growing anywhere from 2 to 9 feet depending on cultivation conditions. This extreme range makes it suitable for both indoor grows with height restrictions and outdoor gardens where taller plants are welcome. The plant tends toward bushiness rather than the Christmas-tree shape typical of many sativas, requiring adequate spacing between plants.

### Environmental Adaptability
One of D.T.S.'s most notable traits is its lack of fussiness. The strain adapts readily to the conditions it finds itself in, making it forgiving for cultivators who may not have optimized their growing environment. It is not particularly demanding of specific nutrient regimens, light schedules, or temperature ranges, though like all cannabis plants it performs best when given adequate light, nutrition, and ventilation.

### Cloning and Propagation
D.T.S. clones with exceptional ease, producing strong roots quickly from cuttings. This trait makes it popular with commercial growers and those who maintain mother plants for continuous production. Reliable cloning also means that once a desirable phenotype is identified, it can be preserved indefinitely.

### Screen of Green (ScrOG) Suitability
The strain is particularly well-suited to Screen of Green cultivation, where a screen or net is placed above the plants to create an even canopy and maximize light penetration to lower bud sites. D.T.S.'s bushy growth pattern and flexible branches respond well to ScrOG training, producing an even canopy of bud sites that all receive adequate light exposure.

### Flowering Period
Under optimal conditions, D.T.S. completes flowering in approximately 75 days (roughly 10.5 weeks). This places it in the moderate-to-long flowering category for sativa-dominant strains—longer than many indica hybrids but shorter than pure Haze or landrace sativas that can require 14+ weeks.

### Yield
D.T.S. produces a good-to-above-average yield, with dense, resinous buds that carry the complex aroma profile inherited from its diverse parentage. The combination of yield quantity and quality makes it a favorite among growers who prioritize both weight and potency.

## Aroma and Flavor Profile

D.T.S. produces a complex aroma that reflects its multi-line heritage:
- **Earthy base notes**: From the Cheese/Skunk and Kush lineage.
- **Citrus undertones**: Contributed by the Diesel and Haze genetics.
- **Sharp, pungent cheese**: The Cheese influence is detectable but not overwhelming, blending with rather than dominating the overall profile.

The flavor on consumption follows the aroma closely, with an earthy-citrus character that is more nuanced than the sometimes one-dimensional profiles of simpler crosses.

## Effects

D.T.S. delivers a strong, intense head high accompanied by a pleasant body buzz. S.T. Oner describes it as "perfect weed for kicking back and talking about Lord of the Rings for hours with your buddies"—capturing the strain's combination of cerebral engagement and physical comfort. The MK-Ultra influence provides a grounding body component that prevents the Haze and Diesel elements from becoming overly racy or anxiety-inducing.

The onset is moderately rapid, with the head high arriving first followed by the body relaxation building over 30–60 minutes. Duration is typical for sativa-dominant hybrids, lasting 2–3 hours depending on dosage and individual tolerance.

## Medical Applications

D.T.S.'s balanced effects profile makes it potentially useful for:
- **Stress relief**: The combination of mental uplift and body relaxation addresses both psychological and physical tension.
- **Mild to moderate pain**: The body buzz provides analgesic effects without heavy sedation.
- **Social anxiety**: The euphoric head high can facilitate social interaction and conversation.
- **Mood enhancement**: Useful for temporary relief from mild depression or low mood.

## Cultivation Tips

Based on the breeder's recommendations and grower reports:
- **Spacing**: Allow adequate room between plants for the bushy growth pattern.
- **Training**: ScrOG is recommended but not required; LST and topping also work well.
- **Nutrients**: Not demanding; standard cannabis nutrient regimens are sufficient.
- **Cloning**: Take advantage of the easy cloning trait for reliable propagation.
- **Harvest timing**: Watch trichome development rather than relying solely on calendar days, as the complex genetics can produce varied maturation rates.

## The Cheese Lineage in Detail

The Cheese genetics in D.T.S. trace back to one of the most significant cannabis selections in European history. In the late 1980s, a group of growers in the United Kingdom obtained seeds from a Skunk #1 lineage and grew out numerous plants. Among them, one phenotype exhibited an extraordinarily pungent aroma reminiscent of aged cheese—so strong that it could be detected from a considerable distance. This "Cheese" phenotype was cloned and shared among UK growers, becoming the foundation of an entire subfamily of strains.

Cheese genetics typically contribute:
- **Intense pungent aroma**: The signature smell that defines the Cheese family.
- **Relaxing body effects**: Skunk-derived indica characteristics that promote physical calm.
- **Resilience**: Cheese strains tend to be hardy and disease-resistant.
- **Moderate flowering time**: Typically 7–9 weeks, shorter than many sativa-dominant strains.

The inclusion of Cheese in D.T.S. provides a counterbalance to the more racy, cerebral elements from the Diesel and Haze lineage, contributing to the strain's balanced and well-rounded effect profile.

## MK-Ultra: A Potency Legend

MK-Ultra, the other contributor to D.T.S.'s first parent line, is itself a cross of two legendary strains: G-13 and OG Kush. G-13 is perhaps the most famous clone-only indica in cannabis history, allegedly originating from a government research program at the University of Mississippi in the late 1960s or early 1970s. While the government origin story is debated, G-13's reputation for extraordinary potency is well-established.

OG Kush contributes its own legendary status as the cornerstone of West Coast cannabis genetics. The "OG" designation (variously claimed to stand for "Ocean Grown," "Original Gangster," or simply be a reference to the original Kush phenotype) has become one of the most frequently appearing names in cannabis breeding.

MK-Ultra won the High Times Cannabis Cup in 2003, establishing its credentials as one of the most potent indicas of its era. Its contribution to D.T.S. includes deep body relaxation, enhanced resin production, and a layer of Kush-derived complexity to the terpene profile.

## Multi-Line Breeding Philosophy

D.T.S. exemplifies a breeding philosophy that values genetic complexity over simplicity. While many commercial breeders work with two-parent crosses for predictability and ease of marketing, BillBerry Farms' approach of combining four established lines creates the potential for:
- **Hybrid vigor**: The combination of diverse genetic backgrounds often produces offspring that exceed either parent in vigor, yield, or potency.
- **Phenotypic diversity**: Multiple phenotypes within a seed lot allow growers to select the expression that best suits their preferences and growing conditions.
- **Complex terpene profiles**: More parent lines mean more potential terpene combinations, resulting in richer and more nuanced aromas and flavors.
- **Breeding potential**: The genetic diversity within D.T.S. makes it valuable as breeding stock for further crosses.

This approach is not without risks—complex crosses can produce unpredictable results, and stabilizing desirable traits across multiple parent lines requires many generations of selection. BillBerry Farms' success with D.T.S. suggests careful selection and stabilization work went into the strain's development.
