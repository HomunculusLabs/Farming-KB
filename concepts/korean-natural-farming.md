# Korean Natural Farming

Korean Natural Farming (KNF) is an innovative agricultural
system developed by Dr. Cho Han Kyu at the Janong Natural
Farming Institute in South Korea during the 1960s. It
represents a nature-respecting approach to farming that
uses locally available natural materials instead of
chemicals, producing high yields at dramatically lower
cost while improving soil health and crop quality.

## Origins and Philosophy

Dr. Cho Han Kyu developed KNF at a time when chemical
intensive agriculture was beginning to spread across South
Korea. He observed that conventional farming was
ecologically unsustainable, economically unviable, and
environmentally ruinous. Together with like-minded farmers,
he converted lifelong studies and practical experience into
a system that promotes respect and care for the environment
while producing more with less cost and labor.
