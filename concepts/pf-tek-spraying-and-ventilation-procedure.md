# PF TEK Spraying and Ventilation Procedure

## Overview

The spraying and ventilation procedure is the primary maintenance activity during the
fruiting phase of PF [[pf-tek-cultivation-method]]. The dual [[dual-chambered-terrarium-design]] relies on manual
spraying to maintain the high humidity environment that mushrooms require, combined
with regular ventilation to provide fresh [[growing-gourmet-growing-room-ventilation-air-exchange]].

Understanding the correct spraying technique is critical because improperly applied
water droplets can drown the delicate mycelial structures and inhibit or contaminate
growth, defeating the entire purpose of the carefully designed system.

## The Physics of Fungal Humidification

The mushrooms receive water from two sources: the substrate they grow on and the
humidified air that surrounds them. The fungus needs to bathe in a shroud of
floating water molecules.

At 100% humidity, the maximum number of water molecules float amongst the air atoms,
creating the ideal environment for fruiting. The [[dual-chambered-terrarium]] easily
achieves these conditions through its [[pf-tek-dual-chambered-terrarium-spray-shield-humidity-physics]] system.

A critical distinction must be made between water droplets and molecularized water.
The spray from a hand sprayer initially produces water droplets that are, in relation
to the fine [[fungal-mycelial-networks-nutrient-translocation]] of fungal threads, like giant ponds of water.

These droplets will drown the micro-world of fungal structures and thereby inhibit or
contaminate growth. Molecularized water, which has evaporated into the air, is what
the fungus actually needs. It floats into the fine structures and provides humidity
at the microscopic level without the damaging physical impact of liquid droplets.

Molecularized water is another way of describing water that has evaporated into the
air. In a matter of time, humidity will condense out onto surfaces inside the
terrarium and drip down. The spray shield acts as a drip shield and roof, so the
more condensation the better.

## The Spray Shield: Multi-Function Component

The spray shield is the key innovation that converts coarse spray into fine humidity.
It serves multiple functions in the dual chambered terrarium system, acting
simultaneously as a humidification diffuser, a drip shield, and a chamber partition.

### Humidification Diffuser

The primary chamber receives the initial direct spraying. As the spray strikes the
shield, it is broken down into a finer mist. This refined mist flows around the
sides of the spray shield into the secondary chamber where the fungus is bathed in
fine humidity, safely away from damaging water droplets.

The shield effectively transforms coarse water spray into the molecularized moisture
that the fungus requires for healthy development. Without the shield, direct spraying
would expose the cakes to harmful water droplets.

### Drip Shield and Roof

Over time, the humidity inside the terrarium condenses onto surfaces. The spray
shield is slanted, so it acts as a drip shield and roof directing water away from
the fruiting cakes.

Condensation collects on the shield and drips down to the sides of the terrarium
rather than onto the fungus cakes below. More condensation is actually beneficial
in this system, as it indicates that high humidity is being maintained successfully.

### Chamber Partition

The shield creates two distinct chambers within the terrarium. The primary chamber
acts as a buffer zone where spraying occurs, while the secondary chamber provides
the calm, humid environment where fruiting takes place.

This separation protects the developing mushrooms from physical disturbance during
spraying and creates a humidity gradient that more closely mimics natural growing
conditions found in the wild.

## Spray Bottle Selection

The PF TEK emphasizes that the spray bottle is a critical piece of equipment, not an
afterthought. An all-purpose water spray bottle with an adjustable nozzle should be
procured from a hardware store.
