## Polysaccharide Structural Diversity

Polysaccharides are structurally diverse biological macromolecules -- polymers
of monosaccharide residues joined by glycosidic linkages. They differ from
proteins and nucleic acids in having the greatest potential for structural
variability. While four amino acids can form only 24 different permutations,
four different sugar monosaccharides could form up to 35,560 unique
tetrasaccharides. This enormous variability allows precise regulatory mechanisms
for cell-cell interactions in higher organisms.

## Types of Anti-Tumour Polysaccharides

Anti-tumour polysaccharides from mushrooms occur as: water-soluble beta-D-
glucans; beta-D-glucans with heterosaccharide chains of xylose, mannose,
galactose, or uronic acid; and beta-D-glucan-protein complexes (proteoglycans).
Protein-linked glucans generally have greater immunopotential activity than
corresponding glucans alone.

## Key Structural Features Affecting Activity

Anti-cancer activity levels relate to molecular weight, degree of branching,
solubility in water, and glycosidic linkage types. The most pharmacologically
active forms are typically beta-(1,3)-D-glucans with beta-(1,6)-D branches.
See beta-glucan [[schizophyllum-commune-schizophyllan-cancer]] for clinical data.

### PSK (Trametes versicolor / Turkey Tail)

A polysaccharide-protein complex (proteoglycan) isolated in Japan in the late
1960s. Molecular weight approximately 100 kDa. Contains fucose. Marketed as
Krestin, approved as an adjuvant cancer therapy in Japan since the 1970s.

### PSP (Trametes versicolor)

Similar to PSK but lacks fucose and contains arabinose and rhamnose instead.
Developed in China from 1983. Used extensively in Chinese hospitals as
complementary cancer treatment.

### Grifron-D (Grifola frondosa / Maitake)

Highly purified beta-1,6 glucan branched with beta-1,3 linkage. Notably orally
bioavailable, distinguishing it from Lentinan and Schizophyllan.

### AHCC (Proprietary Blend)

Low molecular weight alpha-1,3 glucan structures -- contrasting with the
beta-glucans of other compounds. Retains tumour-static activity and is one of
the most commercially successful [[medicinal-mushroom-compound-extraction]] for practical procedures.
