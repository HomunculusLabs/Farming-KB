## Overview

Mushroom spores are among the most remarkable dispersal structures in the
biological world, enabling fungi to travel vast distances through air,
water, and by hitchhiking on animals. [[growing-gourmet-polypore-mushrooms-cultivation-overview]] coexist
within such trees, creating multi-species [[core-endorhiza-bacterial-community-composition-cannabis]].

### Mycoremediation Seeding

For mycoremediation projects, understanding spore dispersal
dynamics helps practitioners design effective inoculation strategies.
Combining multiple vectors - water-based slurries, insect attraction,
and strategic placement near animal corridors - maximizes the
probability of successful mycelial establishment in contaminated or
degraded sites. The inverse square relationship of spore density to
distance from parent means that saturation inoculation near target
sites yields the highest colonization rates.
