# Composted Biomass as Soil Amendment Remediation

## Overview

Composted fungal biomass and spent mushroom substrate (SMS) represent
valuable byproducts of fungal cultivation repurposed for soil remediation.
Singh's treatment of [[cancer-medicinal-mushrooms-pleurotus-ostreatus]], Agaricus bisporus, [[staycare-fungal-degradation-of-polycyclic-aromatic-hydrocarbons]]: Organic matter adsorbs PAHs,
  reducing bioavailability and toxicity
- **Pesticide residues**: Compost organic matter adsorbs and partially
  degrades pesticides through retained microbial activity

### Radionuclide Binding

- Fungal cell wall melanin and chitin contribute to radionuclide
  biosorption (cesium-137, strontium-90, uranium)
- Application at contaminated sites can immobilize radionuclides,
  reducing leaching and plant uptake

## Soil Health Improvement

### Physical Properties

- **Soil structure**: Organic matter improves aggregation, reducing
  compaction and improving porosity
- **Water retention**: Increased water-holding capacity (2-10%
  improvement) reduces irrigation needs
- **Erosion resistance**: Improved aggregate stability reduces erosion

### Chemical Properties

- **pH buffering**: Compost moderates acidic or alkaline conditions
  common at contaminated sites
- **Cation exchange capacity**: Increased CEC improves nutrient retention
  and reduces metal leaching
- **Nutrient supply**: Slow-release NPK and micronutrients support plant
  establishment
- **Organic matter**: Increases soil organic carbon for microbial activity

### Biological Properties

- **Microbial inoculation**: Composted SMS contains diverse microbial
  communities enhancing biological activity
- **Residual enzyme activity**: [[mycorrhizal-fungi]]: Some SMS contains residual mycorrhizal
  propagules for plant colonization

## Application in Mycoremediation

### Soil Amendment and Biofilters

- **Direct amendment**: 10-50 metric tons per hectare, tilled into top
  15-30 cm of soil before planting remediation crops
- **Compost biofilters**: SMS as biofilter media for contaminated water
  or air; effective for volatile organic compound biodegradation
- **Vermicomposting**: SMS processed through earthworms produces
  vermicast with enhanced nutrient availability

### Combined Remediation Approaches

- **Phytoremediation support**: SMS improves plant growth on contaminated
  soils, enhancing [[biosorption-isotherms]] -- metal binding mechanisms of fungal biomass
- [[singh-phosphate-solubilizing-fungi-and-nutrient-mobilization]] -- nutrient mobilization
- [[singh-radionuclide-accumulation-by-fungi]] -- radionuclide binding
