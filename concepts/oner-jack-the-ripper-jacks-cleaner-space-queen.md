## Jack the Ripper: Jack's Cleaner × Space Queen

## Overview

Jack the Ripper is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by Subcool and
his Team Green Avengers. Named for its potency — "absolutely killer
weed" — this strain combines Jack's Cleaner (P1) with Space Queen to
produce a plant with remarkable lineage depth. With THC measured at
26.6% and the presence of THCV, Jack the Ripper is one of the more
significant strains in the Subcool catalog.

## Flowering and Yield

After flipping to flowering, bud production becomes visible around day
12, when spear-shaped white buds begin to form. Indoor flowering
typically completes around week 8, though some phenotypes may run
slightly longer. Outdoor growers should plan for a September harvest
in Northern Hemisphere [[lemon-haze]] — bright, citrusy, and immediately
recognizable. Some phenotypes overlay this with pine notes. The cured
buds carry tropical fruit aromas with an undertone of burning hash.

The flavor profile follows the aroma closely: citrus and tropical
fruit forward, with a hashy finish that adds depth. This combination
makes Jack the Ripper appealing to connoisseurs who appreciate
complex, layered [[cannabis-terpene-profiles]].

## Potency and Effects

At 26.6% THC, Jack the Ripper is a high-potency strain that delivers
a blindingly strong head high. The effects are described as catching
users "totally unawares" — the onset is rapid and the cerebral
experience is intense. The high is clear and long-lasting, sending
users "straight towards the light" in a metaphorical sense.
