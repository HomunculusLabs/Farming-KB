## Contraindicated Conditions

### Absolute Contraindications (Do Not Use Psychedelics)

| Condition | Risk | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Schizophrenia spectrum disorders | Very High | Psychedelics can trigger or exacerbate psychotic episodes. Risk persists even during remission |
| Active psychosis or mania | Very High | Acute psychiatric crisis |
| Severe cardiovascular disease | High | LSD, MDMA, and others increase heart rate and blood pressure |
| Aortic aneurysm or dissection | Very High | Risk of rupture from blood pressure spikes |
| Uncontrolled hypertension | High | Stroke risk from acute blood pressure elevation |

### Relative Contraindications (Use with Extreme Caution or Avoid)

| Condition | Risk | Details |
|---|---|---|
| [[blesching-cannabis-bipolar-disorder]] (history of mania) | High | Can trigger manic episodes |
| PTSD with dissociative features | High | Re-traumatization risk without proper therapeutic support |
| Borderline personality disorder | Moderate-High | Emotional instability can be amplified |
| Epilepsy or [[cannabis-for-epilepsy-and-seizure-disorders]] | Moderate | LSD lowers seizure threshold |
| Pregnancy or breastfeeding | High | Unknown fetal effects; avoid all [[cultural-evolution-and-psychoactive-substances]] |
| Liver disease | Moderate | Impaired metabolism of compounds |
| Diabetes | Moderate | Appetite changes and altered glucose awareness |
| Personal/family history of psychosis | High | Elevated risk of psychotic reactions |

### Cardiac-Specific Considerations
- **Long QT syndrome**: Contraindicated with MDMA, ibogaine, ketamine
- **Recent heart attack**: Minimum 6-month recovery before consideration
- **Heart failure**: Avoid compounds with significant cardiovascular effects
- **Arrhythmias**: Many psychedelics can trigger arrhythmias in susceptible individuals
- **Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy**: Risk from tachycardia and increased cardiac output

## Trip Sitting Guidelines

### Role of a Trip Sitter
A trip sitter (or guide) remains sober and provides a safe, supportive presence:

1. **Before the session**: Help set intentions, review dosage, prepare the environment, discuss potential challenges
2. **During the session**: Maintain calm presence, offer reassurance, redirect if needed, monitor physical safety, provide water/snacks
3. **After the session**: Support the comedown, help ground the person, assist with basic needs, be available for processing

### Key Principles
- Never leave the person alone during the peak
- Remain sober — no alcohol, cannabis, or other substances
- Do not attempt to control or direct the experience
- If the person is distressed: change the music, dim/brighten lights, offer water, move to a different room, suggest breathing exercises
- Do not physically restrain unless there is immediate danger of self-harm
- Be familiar with the specific substance's effects and duration
- Know when and how to call for medical help
