## Major International Resources

The EMCDDA identified several major US-based websites that served as comprehensive
repositories of information on hallucinogenic mushrooms:

- **Erowid**: Perhaps the most well-known psychoactive substance information
  resource globally. Erowid provided detailed information on mushroom species,
  effects, dosages, chemistry, trip reports, and legal status across multiple
  jurisdictions. It served as a primary reference for both casual users and
  researchers.
- **Lycaeum**: An early online community and information resource focused on
  psychoactive plants and fungi. Alongside Erowid, Lycaeum was one of the
  foundational internet resources for [[forensic-identification-methods-psilocybin-mushrooms-tlc-gc-hplc]], featuring extensive cultivation guides, identification
  resources, and user forums.
- **MushroomJohn**: A specialized website providing information on mushroom
  species and their effects.
- **The Entheogen Review**: A publication and website covering a range of
  entheogenic substances including hallucinogenic mushrooms.

## European National and Regional Websites

In addition to the major international resources, the EMCDDA documented numerous
country-specific websites that focused on magic mushrooms in their local and
regional contexts. These websites reflected the linguistic and cultural diversity
of [[hplc-analysis-of-psilocybin-and-psilocin]]), taxonomic classification of mushroom species, and legal
status in various jurisdictions. This information served both harm reduction and
educational purposes.

**Trip Reports**: Many websites hosted collections of user-submitted experience
reports, allowing visitors to read about the effects of different mushroom species,
dosage levels, and set and setting considerations. The website magic-mushrooms.net
was specifically cited as an example of a site hosting trip reports.

**Cultivation Guides**: Some sites provided detailed instructions on how to
cultivate hallucinogenic mushrooms at home, including techniques derived from the
PF TEK method and other cultivation approaches. This information contributed to
the availability of mushrooms beyond natural sources.

**Photo Galleries**: Visual [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-species-classification-and-chemistry]] in their natural habitats, aiding in field
identification for foragers.

**Community Features**: Discussion forums, events listings, and other community
tools enabled visitors to share personal experiences, cultivation tips,
information about local mushroom seasons, and supply channel information.
