# Beer Styles Overview

The world of beer encompasses an enormous range of styles, from light, crisp lagers to dark, complex ales, from sour wild beers to strong barrel-aged monsters. Beer style guidelines provide a framework for understanding and categorizing this diversity. The most widely used reference is the Beer Judge Certification Program (BJCP) style guidelines, which organize beer into families and subcategories.

## Lager Styles

### Light Lagers

The most widely consumed beers in the world are light lagers. These include American lager, Czech pilsner, German pilsner, and Munich helles. They are characterized by clean fermentation, subtle malt sweetness, and restrained hop bitterness. The focus is on balance, drinkability, and refreshment.

Czech pilsner (Pilsner Urquell style) is the original pilsner, with a slightly fuller body and more pronounced hop character than German pilsner. Munich helles is a malt-forward light lager with minimal hop presence.
