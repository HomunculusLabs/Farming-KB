## Four Types of Diet

Fukuoka classifies human eating into four progressively deeper stages, each
reflecting a different relationship between the individual, food, and nature.

### 1. Empty Diet

"A lax, self-indulgent diet, influenced by the external world that submits to
cravings and fancies. This diet, directed by the mind, might be called an empty
diet."

### 2. Scientific Diet

"The physically centered diet of most people, where nutritional food is consumed
to sustain the body. This is a scientific diet that spins centrifugally outward
with increasing desires."

### 3. Diet of Principle

"The diet of natural man based on spiritual laws. Extending beyond Western
science and centered on [[cho-loess-powder-in-natural-farming]], natural diet, and natural healing are all part of one whole."
These three domains arise together from a single orientation toward life.

"Both natural diet [[fukuoka-textdoc-three-stages-of-human-collapse-and-natural-farming-future]] can be achieved only by natural people.
This trinity begins and is realized at once. The goal of all three elements is
the creation of ideal man."

He closes with urgency: "Unless we succeed very soon in clarifying what nature
is and what man should and should not do, there will be no turning back." The
choice between natural and artificial diet is not merely a matter of personal
health but of civilizational direction — whether humanity will find its way back
to nature or continue down the path of estrangement.
