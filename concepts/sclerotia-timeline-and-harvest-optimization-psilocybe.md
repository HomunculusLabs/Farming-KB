### Why Patience Matters

Harvesting sclerotia before the 4-month mark yields smaller, less dense
trichomes. The difference in both wet and dry weight can be substantial between
a jar harvested at 2 months versus one left for the full 4 months. The mycelium
invests continued metabolic energy into sclerotia consolidation over this
extended period, resulting in harder, more compact masses.
