## Ketamine - The Ultimate Psychedelic Journey

D.M. Turner rates Ketamine at intensity "10 to Infinity," the highest rating in the guide, describing it as the most intense, bizarre, and enjoyable psychedelic he has tried.

## Material

Ketamine is a general anesthetic manufactured by Parke Davis (marketed as Ketalar) and distributed by several smaller pharmaceutical companies. Comes in multi-use sealed vials generally containing 500 mg. Also used as a veterinary anesthetic and occasionally sold in powder form on the underground market.

## History

Ketamine is used clinically as a "gentle" anesthetic, often given to children and elderly people. Psychedelic characteristics were discovered after many recipients reported "reemergence" experiences while coming out of anesthesia. Further experiments showed a dose much smaller than the anesthetic dose produces incredibly intense psychedelic experiences.

## Dosage and Administration

**Intramuscular injection:**
- **10-20 mg:** Non-psychedelic dissociative altered state
- **50 mg:** Semi-conscious halfway zone
- **75-125 mg:** Highly desirable Ketamine trip (~100 mg average)
- **100 mg IM:** Standard intense [[fukuoka-textdoc-production-costs-not-coming-down]]. Frequently no recollection of ever having a personality, body, or knowing of planet Earth.

### Core Experience
- Total orgasm with the universe
- Feeling of being in hyperspace, simultaneously connected to all things
- Billions of images and perceptions flowing simultaneously
- Not bound to three dimensions; experiences backward and forward in time
- First experience felt like pressing a "GO button" -- all previous psychedelic experiences had been preparation
- Permanent shift in the "fabric of reality" -- previous peak breakthrough states became base reality

### Peak (30 min to 1 hour in)
- Will determines whether self and universe exist
- Can toggle between existence and non-existence multiple times per second
- Impression of ability to crystallize the universe in any desired format

### Return
- Begins with one perception in a million fitting familiar perceptual structure
- "Personal perceptions" increase: 1 in 100,000, then 1 in 10,000
- Return is NOT disappointing (unlike ecstasy comedown)
- Feels novel: "Wow, I'm coming back, I wonder what life will be like"
- Part of mind still running circles around the cosmos while another part reintegrates identity
- Often experienced as an "alien rebirth"
- Upon returning to body, DMT-like visuals continue with eyes opened

### Recovery
45 min to 1 hour after injecting, user is "back" but retains strange bodily sensations: light/anti-gravity feeling, slight dizziness, poor motor coordination, mild nausea if moving. Best to relax for a couple of hours.

### Memory and Integration Problem
Within hours after coming back, 99% of the experience is inaccessible to conscious mind. The experience is so bizarre and otherworldly that a normal mind cannot conceive of experiencing in this manner. Some part of the mind protectively closes off access. Taking Ketamine while already high on 2C-B provides a "bridge" between ego and ego-less states, helping with integration.

### Personality Correlation
Dr. Igor Kungurtsev found that controlled people who have difficulty letting go or relationship problems often have negative Ketamine experiences (dissolving of self is horrible for them). Relaxed people with deep capacity to love usually have blissful, even ecstatic experiences. Most reported spiritual experiences and long-term changes in spiritual outlook.

## Safety

- **Purity:** Ketamine comes in sealed pharmaceutical vials, free of street drug impurities
- **Overdose:** Little risk -- routinely administered at much higher doses as an anesthetic
- **Needle safety:** Use only new, sealed hypodermic syringes; never share
- **Movement:** Do NOT get up during the experience; reaction abilities are suspended
- **Sitter:** Having a friend present is one of the safest methods
- **Precautions alone:** Extinguish all cigarettes, remove glass objects, lock doors, clear floor of obstacles
- **Aftermath:** Stay indoors away from traffic for several hours afterward
