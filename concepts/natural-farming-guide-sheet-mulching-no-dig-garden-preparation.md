## Soil Type Considerations

Different soil types benefit from sheet mulching in different ways:

- **Clay soil:** Adding gypsum in step 2 helps break up clay particles.
  The organic layers improve drainage and prevent crusting.
- **Sandy soil:** Sheet mulching dramatically improves water retention in sandy
  soils. The organic layers act as a sponge, holding moisture that sand alone
  cannot retain.
- **Compacted soil:** The layers gradually soften compacted earth as roots
  grow downward and organisms create channels. Over multiple seasons, even
  heavily compacted areas can be restored.
