# KNF Soap Water and Hot Pepper Spray

Soap water and hot pepper water are KNF"s direct pest control methods for aphids and mites. Unlike most KNF inputs that focus on plant nutrition, these are physical/mechanical controls. Soap water kills aphids through evaporation-induced rupture, while hot pepper repels them with capsaicin.

## Soap Water

### How It Works
When soap water is sprayed on plants, sunlight evaporates the water. Heat loss and condensation cause aphids to burst and die. The soap acts as a surfactant that penetrates the insect's waxy coating.

### Recipe
1. Cut 200 g of laundry soap into small pieces
2. Boil 20L of water with soap pieces until it becomes thick soup
3. Cool — this concentrated liquid is called "turbid water"
4. For **aphid control**: Mix 0.7L turbid water with 20L water
5. For **mite control**: Mix 0.5L turbid water with 20L water

### Application
- **Time**: Spray when sunlight is strongest, 1-2 PM
- **Target**: Back of leaves where aphids congregate
- **Caution**: Can be harmful on cloudy days — do NOT apply when cloudy

## Hot Pepper Water

### How It Works
Capsaicin in hot peppers repels aphids through its irritating taste/smell. Aphids avoid treated plants.

### Recipe
1. Cut 10-12 hot peppers into pieces
2. Put in 1L of water
3. Boil until volume reduces to 1/2 (500 ml)
4. Cool and mix with 20L water for spraying

## Important Philosophy

Cho emphasizes: it is more important to grow plants that do not attract aphids and mites than to control the insects after they appear. Aphids and mites appear because plants become unhealthy from excessive nitrogen and nutrient imbalance. The plant"s "body constitution" changes to favor pests.

**Prevention over cure**: Manage nutrition and plant health from the start to avoid pest pressure.

## Cautions

| Caution | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Do not spray soap water on cloudy days | Evaporation is the killing mechanism |
| Do not combine with other KNF biologicals | Soap can kill beneficial microbes |
| Test on small area first | Some plants sensitive to soap/capsaicin |
| Apply in early morning or late afternoon for pepper spray | Avoid midday heat stress |
| Do not over-apply nitrogen | Root cause of aphid attraction |

## Combining with KNF

- Fix the underlying nutritional imbalance causing pest susceptibility
- Reduce FAA ([[fish-amino-acid-faa]]) if over-applying — excess nitrogen attracts aphids
- Apply WS-Ca ([[water-soluble-calcium-wca]]) to harden plant tissues against pests
- Use OHN ([[oriental-herbal-nutrient-ohn]]) for disease prevention alongside soap/pepper
- For severe infestations, use JADAM methods — see [[jadam-natural-pesticide-jnp]]
