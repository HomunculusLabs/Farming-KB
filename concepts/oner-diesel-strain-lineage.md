## Sour Diesel and Its Progeny

From the ChemDawg lineage came Sour Diesel (also known as Sour
D), which became one of the most popular and recognizable cannabis
strains in the world. The Diesel name comes from its distinctive
fuel-like aroma, a pungent, acrid smell that is immediately
identifiable to anyone familiar with it. The strain delivers an
energetic, cerebral high that makes it a favorite daytime choice.

The Sour Diesel genetics branched into numerous offspring. East
Coast Sour Diesel, featured in Volume 3, represents a specific
regional expression of the lineage. Ringo's Sour Diesel is another
notable variant. Sour Diesel #2, White Diesel, and Jack Diesel (a
cross with Jack Herer genetics) demonstrate how the Diesel
lineage has been crossed with diverse genetic backgrounds to create
new expressions while preserving the characteristic fuel aroma and
uplifting effects.
