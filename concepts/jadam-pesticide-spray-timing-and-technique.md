JADAM specifically recommends spraying when humidity is high. There is a
common misconception that spraying pesticide when morning dew is present is
harmful; JADAM states the opposite is true. The presence of dew means
humidity is nearly 100 percent, so pesticide will evaporate very slowly.
Furthermore, the bodies of aphids and mites do not accumulate dew, meaning
the pesticide stays concentrated on the pest bodies.

## Seasonal Spray Timing

In spring and autumn, JADAM recommends spraying in the early morning. In
summer, when mornings are short, spray in the evening of a humid day. To
check humidity levels, spray water on plants and observe how fast it dries.
If evaporation is very slow even during daytime, daytime spraying is
acceptable.

For greenhouses, pesticide can be sprayed even during rainy weather since
there is no risk of wash-off. In open fields, avoid spraying just before
rain. The general rule is to apply pesticide early morning or at sunset
to avoid hot times of day and maximize contact time.
