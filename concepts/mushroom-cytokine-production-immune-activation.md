# Mushroom Cytokine Production and Immune Activation

Mushroom polysaccharides exert anticancer effects primarily through immune
system modulation. A central mechanism is stimulation of cytokine production
by immune cells, orchestrating coordinated anti-tumour responses.

## Cytokine Classes Relevant to Mushroom Activity

- Interleukins (IL-1, IL-2, IL-3, IL-6, IL-10)
- Interferons (INF-alpha, INF-gamma)
- Tumour necrosis factors (TNF-alpha)
- Colony stimulating factors (CSF)
- Macrophage and NK cell activating factors

## Lentinan Cytokine Cascade

lentinan triggers a multi-level cytokine cascade:

- Stimulates IL-2 production from peripheral blood lymphocytes
- IL-2 activates LAK cells (lymphokine-activated killer cells)
- Augments IL-1, which induces IL-2 maturation
- Increases IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, and TNF-alpha in gastric cancer patients
- Increases CSF and IL-3 production correlating with macrophage activity
- Stimulates TNF production for direct anti-tumour effects
- Promotes interferon production for immune surveillance
- Activates acute phase inflammatory responses

Lentinan can activate NK cells in vitro at concentrations achievable in
patient blood plasma, and inhibits prostaglandin synthesis that slows
T-cell differentiation.

## Ganoderma lucidum Cytokine Responses

### Broad Cytokine Induction

Water-soluble ganoderma lucidum extracts activate T lymphocytes,
inducing IL-1beta, INF-gamma, TNF-alpha, IL-2, IL-6, and IL-10 (Wang et
al., 1997; Mao et al., 1999).

### GLB Polysaccharide

A polysaccharide fraction (GLB) promotes IL-2 in a dose-dependent manner,
augmenting cytotoxic T lymphocyte toxicity by up to 100% at 200
micrograms/ml (Lei and Lin, 1992).

### LZ-8 Protein

LZ-8 mediates T cell activation through IL-2 up-regulation, IL-2 receptor
expression, ICAM-1 expression, and increased INF-gamma, TNF-alpha, and
IL-1beta production. Neutralising antibodies to IL-2R and TNF-alpha block
aggregate formation and proliferation.

### CR3-Mediated Macrophage Activation

Beta-D-glucans bind CR3 receptors on macrophages, triggering IL-1beta,
IL-6, INF-gamma, and TNF-alpha production causing anti-proliferation and
apoptosis in leukemic cells. INF-gamma and TNF-alpha act synergistically
(Li et al., 2000).

## PSP and PSK Cytokine Effects

[[psk-psp-polysaccharide-peptides]] increases IL-2 secretion, induces
gamma-interferon at twice control levels, alpha-interferon at 2-4x
control, and promotes IL-6 gene expression in peripheral blood lymphocytes.
[[psk-psp-polysaccharide-peptides]] increases WBC counts, INF-gamma, IL-2, and
delayed-type hypersensitivity.

## Cell Types in Cytokine Responses

### Macrophages

Primary producers of IL-1, TNF-alpha, IL-6. Activated through
[[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]] at CR3 (CD11b/CD18). Produce cytotoxic
mediators, present antigens via MHC class II, release nitric oxide.

### Natural Killer Cells

Activated by polysaccharides; produce IFN-gamma and TNF. First line of
defence eliminating transformed cells before adaptive immune responses.

### T Lymphocytes

Both helper (CD4+) and cytotoxic (CD8+) cells stimulated. PSP augments
T-helper activation and increases CD4+/CD8+ ratio.

### Dendritic Cells

Lentinan stimulation of dendritic cells (in lymph nodes, spleen, thymus)
has important immunomodulatory impact. Dendritic cell tumour-infiltration
with cytotoxic T cell activation governs tumour elimination (Chihara,
1997).

## CR3 Receptor-Mediated Induction

Beta-glucans bind iC3b-receptors (CR3, CD11b/CD18) on phagocytes and NK
cells (Ross et al., 1999). The binding site maps to the CD11b C-terminus.
After binding, the complex is internalised with NF-kB activation (Battle et
al., 1998). Beta-D-glucan can override tumour immune evasion by enabling
CR3-mediated cytotoxicity against iC3b-opsonized tumour cells.

## Cytokine Responses as Biomarkers

In Lentinan-treated patients, the ratio of activated to cytotoxic T cells
and NK cell activity correlated with survival times, suggesting cytokine
activation is a mechanistic predictor of clinical benefit.
