## Olive Mill Wastewater Fungal Treatment

Olive mill wastewater (OMW) is a highly polluting effluent generated
during olive oil extraction, particularly in Mediterranean countries that
account for approximately 98% of global olive production. The wastewater
contains high concentrations of [[fukuoka-textdoc-composting-critique-futility-prepared-organic-matter]], and
toxic substances that resist conventional treatment, making fungal
bioremediation an attractive alternative approach.

## Wastewater Characteristics

Olive mill wastewater exhibits challenging properties for treatment:
- COD values ranging from 43,000 to 82,000 mg/L
- Phenolic content of 1.2 to 10 g/L depending on extraction process
- High phytotoxic and antibacterial activity from phenolic compounds
- Stable emulsion from vegetation water, process water, and olive pulp
- Production of 1 to 2 tons of OMW per ton of olives processed
- Release of approximately 2.5 liters of wastewater per liter of oil

Conventional physicochemical and anaerobic methods are often ineffective
due to high toxicity, low biodegradability, and reactor acidification.
Aerobic biological treatment with fungi tends to diminish phenol content
and reduce toxicity more effectively.

## Fungal Species for OMW Treatment

### White-Rot Fungi
- **[[staycare-veratryl-alcohol-as-lignin-peroxidase-mediator]] and oxygen
  flushing achieves 70% color and 80% COD removal
- **Phanerochaete flavido-alba**: Decolorizes OMW in static or
  semistatic cultures at 30 degrees Celsius, eliminating up to 70%
  toxicity as measured by Microtox assay. Produces MnP and laccase
  simultaneously
- **[[lentinus-edodes-entity-the-mushroom-cultivator]]**: Achieves 90% phenolic reduction and 85%
  decolorization of 20% diluted OMW over 12 days; highly significant
  correlation between color reduction, TOC, and total phenols

### Molds and Yeasts
- **[[fungal-lignin-peroxidase-veratryl-alcohol]] activity
- Ultrafiltration pretreatment removing polyphenolic fractions above 60
  kDa significantly improves subsequent [[biogas-digester-basics]]
