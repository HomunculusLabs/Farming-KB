## Potency and Maturity: The Inverse Relationship

### Small Immature Specimens Are More Potent

A critical insight from [[geotropism-and-spore-production-in-mushrooms]]
- **Intact veil**: Partial veil connecting cap to stem is still present

### Post-Optimal Indicators

- **Flat or upturning cap**: Expanded beyond optimal harvest point
- **Dark gills**: Spore production well underway, indicating maturity
- **Broken veil with annulus**: Past peak potency

## Abortive Mushrooms: Primo in Alkaloids

Abortive mushrooms — those that stop developing prematurely — are exceptionally
potent and should be harvested separately:

### Identifying Aborts

- **Convoluted caps**: Irregular, wrinkled, or twisted surfaces
- **Gnarly stems**: Distorted, thickened, or twisted stems
- **Stunted growth**: Initiated but failed to develop normally
- **Color change**: Often bluish due to concentrated alkaloids

### Why Aborts Are Special

Aborts are **"primo in alkaloids"** — exceptionally high active compound
concentrations. Because growth halted early, alkaloids are concentrated in the
small remaining tissue rather than distributed across a larger body.

## Drying Methods and Principles

### Critical Rule: Never Use Heat

> **Never use an oven, hot air, or any heat source to dry mushrooms.**

Heat actively **leaches and degrades** [[pf-tek-spore-viability-degradation]] follows a predictable decline:

- **Begin degrading** after a few months of storage
- **Greatly reduced germination** after approximately 1.5 years
- Fresh syringes or prints should be used whenever possible
- Refrigerated storage extends viability versus room temperature

## The Complete Growth Cycle Timeline

The [[pf-tek]] cycle from start to finish:

- **Inoculation**: Day 0 — spore solution injected into substrate jars
- **Colonization**: 2–4 weeks — mycelium spreads through substrate
- **Birthing**: Jars opened, cakes placed in terrarium
- **Fruiting**: Mushrooms form within days of birthing
- **Total**: 4–6 weeks from mass spore inoculation senescence avoidance pf tek to first harvest
