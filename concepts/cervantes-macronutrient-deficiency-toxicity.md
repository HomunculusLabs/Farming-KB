## Potassium (K) -- Mobile
### Role
Helps combine sugars, starches, and carbohydrates. Essential to growth by
cell division. Increases chlorophyll in foliage and helps regulate
stomata openings. Essential in accumulation and translocation of
carbohydrates. Necessary for proteins that augment oil content and improve
flavor. Encourages strong root growth and is associated with disease
resistance and water intake.
### Deficiency
Initially plants appear healthy but are susceptible to disease. Older
leaves (first tips and margins, then whole leaves) develop spots, turn
dark yellow, and die. Stems become weak and sometimes brittle. Potassium
is usually present in soil but locked in by high salinity.
**Progression:**
- Plants appear healthy with dark green foliage
- Leaves lose their luster
- Branching may increase but branches are weak and scrawny
- Leaf margins turn grey, progress to rusty-brown, curl up and dry
- Yellowing of older leaves accompanied by rust-colored blotches
- Leaves curl up, rot sets in, older leaves drop
- Flowering retarded and greatly diminished
**Treatment:** First leach toxic salt out of soil, then apply a complete
N-P-K fertilizer. Organic growers add soluble potash (wood ashes) mixed
with water. Be careful: wood ash pH is normally above 10. Use a
pH-lowering mix to bring pH to around 6.5 before application. Foliar
feeding for [[cervantes-micronutrient-deficiency-chelation]]
