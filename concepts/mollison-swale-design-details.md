## Swale Construction

- Cut shallow blade trenches on true contours, **no water movement along trenches**
- At least 4 feet wide, often much wider
- Moderate to shallow slope only (not steep slopes)
- Walk it out on foot; walk the pegs, bulldozer follows
- **In clay:** deepen swale in profile
- **In sand/gravel:** widen the whole water system for large surface area
- Can be done with kids and a spade; "no work repairing drainpipes; no pipes; no gutters; no curbs -- cheap!"

## Orchard Swale System

- Plant white clover on swale; mow grass strip and throw all grass on swale
- When rich, regrade -- bring topsoil up to trees
- Grade 2-3 times; creates great mound of black earth with tree roots
- Stagger trees down slope; alternate species (narrow-leafed up top: peaches/apricots; broad-leafed below: wetter)
- Walking platform for ladders; easy watering via siphons

## Flatland Irrigation from Swales

- Side channel drain with slight fall, blocked at intervals
- Short 4-6 inch pipes through blocks into graded channels
- Plugs with handles to control water flow section by section
- Irrigate hundreds of trees with very little effort
- Switch by pulling plugs sequentially

## Urban Swale Applications

- "A swale is a critical technology for winter-wet America that is not much used"
- Run hard-top road, swales, little bridges, houses back-to-back, footpaths, downpipes
- All roof run-off and road run-off into swales; no guttering, no curbs
- Swales sometimes pass under roads
- Treatment options: stepping stones, gravel areas, little ponds, frogs croaking
- Trees along swale edge (not in front of house, not on sunny side)

## Davis, California Case Study

- Village project on plain near Sacramento
- "An oasis in a desert of disaster"
- Initially absorbed ~40% of water, then 85%, now **100%**
- Now absorbs water from off-site; collects run-off from neighbors
- Efficiency increases as swales age and tree roots penetrate subsurface
- "Nothing quite like Davis has ever happened in America"
- Key: whole-set design orientation, not individual homes

## Swale Benefits Beyond Water

1. **Fire control:** collect fuel and rot it quickly; "greatly decrease risk of forest fire"
2. **Forest moisture:** far more moist forest than existed before; very few trees need removal
3. **Ginseng cultivation:** swales below pine forest are ideal
4. **Blueberries:** swale below pine forest
5. **Composting system:** leaves rot quickly in moist swale; can take from swale for garden
6. **Mulch generation:** plant trees above swale for continuing mulch input; "immaturity exploits maturity"
7. **Salamander habitat:** moist sites with leaf accumulation
8. **Fence/road integration:** fencing and access roads follow water systems naturally

## Gley Pond Sealing

- Pack green sappy material (lawn clippings, chipped green leaves, second-cut hay) 6 inches thick
- Cover with sand, plastic, or old carpets
- When it goes slimy (ferments), fill with water -- fills without trouble, never leaks again
- If leaks persist: find missed spots and re-gley
- Bentonite alternative: swells 14x, spread and roll in, costly but satisfactory
- Water dynamite: 3 packages bump bank, seal cracks with water pressure

## Net and Pan (Steep Slopes)

- Very shallow guide lines for drainage; hand-cut drains
- Clear vegetation at junctions; make little flat planting platforms
- Dribbles of water come down, gathering in absorbing pans
- Particularly effective in rocky country not normally used for orchards
