## Homemade Pest and Disease Sprays

## Overview

Homemade pest and disease sprays use common household
ingredients to create effective, low-cost control options for
the cannabis garden. Cervantes notes that a strong, hot taste,
smelly odor, and a desiccating powder or liquid are the main
ingredients in home-brewed pesticide and fungicide potions.

These sprays discourage and control many pests including
[[cervantes-mealybugs-scale-insect-control]], and many
others. They are particularly valuable for organic growers who
want to avoid synthetic chemicals on consumable crops.

## Safety Precautions

Always test a new spray on a single plant first. Wait a few
days to learn the outcome before applying to all plants. Wear
a mask and gloves, and cover skin and hair when mixing and
applying. Avoid contact with eyes, nose, lips, and ears. While
usually not toxic to humans in dosages lethal to pests, these
concentrated preparations can cause irritation.

## Key Ingredients and Their Uses

Several common ingredients form the basis of effective homemade
sprays:

- Alcohol (isopropyl/rubbing): Add to sprays to dry out pests
  on contact. Also helps emulsify oil in water-based sprays
- Bleach: Use a 5 percent solution as a general disinfectant
  for tools and growing surfaces, not as a foliar spray
- Cinnamon: Dilute cinnamon oil with water, just a few drops
  per pint, as a pesticide
- Citrus: Citrus oils make great ingredients that kill insects

  into the mix. Use liberal amounts for maximum repellent
  effect
