## Mulberry (Morus spp)
## Description
- Family: Moraceae
- Small to medium, long-lived (300 years), deciduous trees
- Hardy, drought resistant, frost tolerant, suited to cool areas
- Tolerant of shade; suited to most soils and sites
- Heavy bearers: 19-24 t/ha; regular fruiting
- Good companion plant for grapes; forms trellis for them

## Propagation

Mulberries propagate readily from hardwood cuttings, softwood cuttings,
and seed. They are among the easiest fruit trees for
[[permaculture-guild-design]] around other fruit trees such as
persimmon.
