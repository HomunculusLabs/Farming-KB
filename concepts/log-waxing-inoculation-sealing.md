# Log Waxing and Inoculation Sealing Techniques Sealing inoculation holes after spawning is a critical step in log-based [[brassinosteroid-psilocybe-cubensis-mycelial-growth-research]].

## Hot Wax Sealing

The most common and effective sealing method uses hot wax applied
directly over each inoculation hole. Spawn-filled holes are
sealed with hot wax or a mixture of hot wax and resin. The wax
must be applied while hot enough to flow into and fill the
drilled cavity, creating an airtight and watertight seal. The
heat also provides a secondary pasteurization effect at the
inoculation site.
