# CBD Therapeutic Effects

## Overview

Cannabidiol (CBD) is a non-psychoactive cannabinoid sourced from cannabis. Unlike THC, CBD does not produce intoxicating effects, making it therapeutically valuable for patients who need symptom relief without cognitive impairment. CBD has a greater affinity for CB2 receptors than CB1 receptors, but much of its therapeutic influence stems from its ability to suppress the enzyme fatty acid amide hydrolase (FAAH) that breaks down anandamide, thus keeping the "Bliss Molecule" active at higher concentrations and for longer duration.

At the same time, CBD tames the psychoactive influence of THC, allowing patients to benefit from THC's other therapeutic powers at higher concentrations without unwanted psychological side effects.
