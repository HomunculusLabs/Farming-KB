# JADAM Soil Hardening Prevention

## Overview

Soil compaction and hardening -- the formation of an impenetrable layer ([[jadam-salt-accumulation-in-soil]]. JADAM provides both diagnosis and treatment methods for this widespread condition.

## Causes of Soil Hardening

### Heavy Machinery

Use of heavy machinery is a direct cause of soil compaction. Tractors are the main culprit. The heavy wheels of a tractor press down on the soil, and their effect can be measured as deep as 7 meters (23 ft). Repeated use of heavy vehicles turns the soil layer at 15 cm depth rock-hard.

### Chemical Accumulation

Antibiotics, chemical pesticides, herbicides, and fertilizers accumulate in the 15 cm belt below the surface because of the compacted soil layer. Normally, if these substances were diffused and decomposed in soil, the problem would not be as serious. Unfortunately, they actually accumulate because the compacted layer prevents deep penetration.

### Antibiotic Contamination

When factory-produced livestock manure containing antibiotics is supplied to farms, the antibiotics build up in the soil and destroy microorganisms. This leads to further hardening of soil. The use of hormones and antibiotics in raising animals has become the norm; their excrement is turned into manure and supplied to farms at very cheap prices due to government support.

## Consequences of Soil Compaction

The consequences of soil hardening cascade through the entire farming system:

**Chemical trapping**: Agricultural chemicals cannot penetrate deep into the soil and enter a breakdown cycle. Instead, they linger at the 15 cm sub-surface level after water has evaporated. This residue is highly detrimental to crop roots. Crystallized chemicals are clearly observable on top of the hardened layer.

**Root restriction**: Crops cannot develop deep root systems. In the past, pepper plants extended roots 1.5 m into the soil and were so robust that farmers needed sickles to chop them down. Now, a small tug pulls the roots out immediately. The roots are coiled up in the nursery soil, desperate to stay.

**Salt accumulation**: When [[comparison-nutrient-cycling-vs-nutrient-dense-gardening]] and [[jadam-phyllite-water-method]] help restore soil structure. Powerful root activity will ease soil compaction from below as roots push through the hardened layer.
