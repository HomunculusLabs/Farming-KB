# Extremophilic Fungi

Fungi inhabit virtually every environment on Earth, including extreme conditions that would kill most organisms. Extremophilic fungi have evolved specialized adaptations to survive and thrive in conditions of extreme temperature, pH, salinity, radiation, desiccation, and toxic metal concentrations.

## Acidophilic Fungi

### Habitat
- Acid mine drainage (pH 1-3)
- Acidic soils (pH < 4)
- Volcanic hot springs
- Stomachs of ruminants

### Adaptations
- Cell membranes with altered lipid composition maintain integrity at low pH
- Proton pumps actively exclude H+ from cytoplasm
- Cytoplasm maintained at near-neutral pH despite external acidity

### Species
- Acidomyces richmondensis: abundant in acid mine drainage
- Aspergillus niger: grows at pH as low as 1.5
- Various yeasts from volcanic soils

### Applications
- Bioleaching: extraction of metals from low-grade ores using acidophilic fungi/bacteria
- Bioremediation of acid mine drainage
- Industrial enzyme production (fungal cellulases, xylanases work optimally at low pH)

## Thermophilic Fungi

### Habitat
- Compost piles (55-70°C during thermophilic phase)
- Hot springs
- Solar-heated soils
- Grain silos (self-heating)

### Adaptations
- Heat-stable enzymes (cellulases, amylases, proteases)
- Thermostable membrane lipids (saturated fatty acids)
- Efficient DNA repair mechanisms
- Rapid growth rate at high temperatures

### Species
- Thermomyces lanuginosus: common in compost, produces industrial lipase
- Myceliophthora thermophila: thermophilic cellulose degrader
- Talaromyces thermophilus: produces thermostable enzymes

### Applications
- Industrial enzyme production (detergent enzymes, biofuel production)
- Composting: thermophilic fungi drive the hot phase
- Biofuel: thermophilic cellulases break down plant biomass

## Psychrophilic (Cold-Adapted) Fungi

### Habitat
- Arctic and Antarctic soils and ice
- Alpine snowfields
- Cold ocean water
- Refrigerated foods

### Adaptations
- Enzymes with high activity at low temperatures
- High membrane fluidity (unsaturated fatty acids)
- Production of antifreeze compounds
- Pigmentation for UV protection

### Species
- Various species of Cladosporium, Penicillium, and Mortierella from polar soils
- Snow molds (Microdochium nivale) that grow under snow cover
- Cryptoendolithic fungi inside Antarctic rocks

## Halophilic (Salt-Tolerant) Fungi

### Habitat
- Salt lakes and saline soils
- Marine environments
- Salted foods
- Saline industrial waste

### Adaptations
- Osmotic adjustment through compatible solutes (glycerol, arabitol)
- Cell wall modifications
- Efficient ion exclusion mechanisms

## Xerophilic (Desiccation-Tolerant) Fungi

### Habitat
- Arid soils and deserts
- Dried foods, spices
- House dust
- Indoor environments

### Adaptations
- Ability to grow at low water activity (aw < 0.70)
- Production of compatible solutes for osmotic adjustment
- Dormant spores survive extreme desiccation for decades
- Rapid germination when moisture returns

## Radiation-Resistant Fungi

### Habitat
- Chernobyl reactor
- High-altitude environments
- Space (survive on spacecraft exteriors)

### Species
- Cladosporium sphaerospermum: grows toward ionizing radiation (radiotrophic)
- Cryptococcus neoformans: melanin provides radiation protection
- Wangiella dermatitidis: melanized, highly radiation-resistant

### Applications
- Bioremediation of radioactive sites
- Space biology research
- Melanin-based radioprotection research
