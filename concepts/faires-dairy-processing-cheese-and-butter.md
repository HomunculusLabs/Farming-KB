### Basic Yogurt Process

1. Heat a quart of milk to 180 degrees F (82 degrees C) to pasteurise
2. Cool the milk to 100 degrees F (38 degrees C) -- use a thermometer for precision
3. Stir in 3 tablespoons of plain yogurt with active cultures (either from a previous batch or store-bought with live cultures)
4. Keep the mixture warm at approximately 100 degrees F for 8-12 hours

### Maintaining Temperature

Several methods work for keeping the yogurt warm during fermentation:
- Thermos: Heat milk directly in the thermos before adding starter
- Oven: Preheat the oven, turn it off, and place the yogurt inside (turn on periodically to maintain warmth)
- Crockpot: Use the low setting or preheat and turn off
- Woodstove: Set at the back when the stove is cooling
- Sun: In warm climates, a sunny windowsill may provide enough warmth
