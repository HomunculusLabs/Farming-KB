### Puerto Rico

Puerto Rico has the richest documented neurotropic mycoflora in the Caribbean,
with nine species including two endemics:

- **Claviceps paspali** — Reported by Grasso (1955).
- **Copelandia cyanescens** — Reported by Navarro & Betancourt (1992) and
  Gerhardt (1996).
- **Panaeolus fimicola**, **P. papilionaceus**, and **P. sphinctrinus** —
  Reported by Gerhardt (1996) and Navarro & Betancourt (1992).
- **Psilocybe cubensis** — Extensively documented by Heim (1956b, 1978),
  Singer & Smith (1958), and Guzmán et al. (1997b).
