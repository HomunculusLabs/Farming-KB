# Water Activity and Osmophilic Fungal Media Preparation

Updated: 2026-04-18

Water activity (aw) is a physiochemical index of water availability ranging from 0 to 1. It is a critical factor in [[biodiversity-xerophilic-fungi-dry-environments]] can grow below aw 0.85
- Some fungi grow at aw as low as 0.62

## Water Activity vs Water Potential

Water potential (Y, MPa) is related to water activity by:
Y = 8.314T ln(aw) / Vw

Where T is absolute temperature (K) and Vw is the partial molar volume of water.

Approximate relationships at 25C:
- aw 0.995 = -0.138 MPa
- aw 0.95 = -7.06 MPa
- aw 0.85 = -22.4 MPa
- aw 0.70 = -59.3 MPa
- aw 0.50 = -95.4 MPa

## Solutes for Adjusting Water Activity

### Glycerol
Glycerol is preferred for lowering aw because NaCl at high concentrations may be toxic to some fungi. Glycerol amounts for specific water potentials at 25C:
- -0.5 MPa: 8.36 g/L
- -1.0 MPa: 19.02 g/L
- -2.0 MPa: 35.59 g/L
- -5.0 MPa: 91.08 g/L

### Sodium Chloride
NaCl molalities and corresponding water activities:
- 0.15 molal NaCl = aw ~0.995
- 0.607 molal NaCl = aw ~0.98
- 2.31 molal NaCl = aw ~0.90
- 5.15 molal NaCl = aw ~0.75

### Sucrose
Sucrose molalities and corresponding water activities:
- 0.272 molal sucrose = aw ~0.995
- 1.03 molal sucrose = aw ~0.98
- 3.48 molal sucrose = aw ~0.90
- 5.98 molal sucrose = aw ~0.80

## Recommended Media for Xerophilic and Osmophilic Fungi

### Dichloran-Glycerol-18 (DG18)
The recommended medium for isolating and enumerating xerophilic fungi from low-moisture foods and arid environments. Dichloran (2,6-dichloro-4-nitroaniline) at 0.002% restricts colony spreading, while 18% glycerol reduces water activity.

### Dichloran-Rose Bengal-Chloramphenicol (DRBC)
General-purpose medium with rose bengal to restrict colony size and chloramphenicol to suppress bacteria. Suitable for rock-inhabiting fungi and other stress-tolerant isolates.

### Alkaline Cornmeal Agar (ACMA)
For general isolation of alkalophilic and alkali-tolerant fungi. Prepared with sodium carbonate or bicarbonate to pH 8.5-11.

### Horikoshi-I and Horikoshi-II Media
Alkaline media for isolating alkalophilic fungi, containing sodium carbonate (0.5-2%) at pH 8.5-11.
