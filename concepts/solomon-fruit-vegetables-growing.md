### Dry and Shelling Beans

- Same species as snap beans (P. vulgaris); grown to maturity for dry seeds
- Fresh dry beans (less than 1 year old) cook much faster than store-bought
- Grow exactly as snap beans until crop dries in the field
- Harvest when pods are dry and brittle; pull entire plants, dry under cover
- Thresh by flailing, tarp dancing, or other methods; winnow in breeze
- Store fully dry in breathable containers (cloth bags, feed sacks)
