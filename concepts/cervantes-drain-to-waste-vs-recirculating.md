### Recirculating Maintenance

Cervantes recommends checking EC of the reservoir, growing medium,
and runoff nutrient solution at the same time every day. Smart
growers leach the entire system with weak nutrient solution for an
hour or more between reservoir changes. Wipe down the entire system
with the solution. Never let the nutrient solution go more than four
weeks before draining and adding fresh solution.

## Drain-to-Waste Systems

In drain-to-waste (run-to-waste) systems, nutrient solution is
applied to the growing medium and the excess is allowed to drain
away, typically to a floor drain or collection point, rather than
being returned to the reservoir. Cervantes notes that commercial
growers commonly use run-to-waste systems.

### Advantages of Drain-to-Waste

- No pathogen recirculation: diseases cannot spread from one plant
  to another through the nutrient solution
- Simpler [[cervantes-salt-buildup-leaching-management]]
- Easier to isolate and treat individual plants with problems
- Reduced risk of catastrophic system-wide failure

### Challenges and Risks

- Higher water and nutrient consumption since runoff is discarded
- Requires a drain or waste disposal system
- Precise irrigation timing is critical to avoid waterlogging or
  drying out
- More expensive per crop due to nutrient solution that is not
  recovered
- Environmental considerations for nutrient-rich wastewater
