## Raking and Rolling

After broadforking and applying amendments, the bed surface is prepared
for seeding. Coleman uses a 30-inch-wide Austrian hay rake (or a
wooden-tooth grading rake as a second choice) to level and smooth the
bed. Finally, the bed surface is lightly firmed with one pass of a
30-inch-wide mesh-surfaced roller. The mesh roller is preferred because
it does not glaze the soil as a smooth-surface roller would. The firming
provides purchase for the toothed wheels of the precision seeders.
