difficult matter to obtain reliable information regarding the species and

and Switzerland. In the United States, there is practically no market for

## Swiss Markets

At Geneva, Switzerland, a special quarter of the market is reserved for
mushroom sales, constituting a veritable fungous exposition in season.
Inspection is carefully organized, and even private collectors are
encouraged to have their collections examined.

At Lausanne, the sale of seventy-eight species has been authorized, with
the total amount sold being perhaps 60,000 pounds. The Swiss system of
market inspection represents an early model for consumer protection that
balances commercial activity with public safety.
