# Washing Machine Hash Production

Using an everyday washing machine to make hash saves hours
of labor when processing large volumes of leaf material.
Cervantes documented how expert Colombian growers adapted
Mila's Ice-O-Lator technology for commercial-scale
production. Following all the steps and paying attention to
water temperature in this simple process will extract all
remaining cannabinoids from the leaves.

The best approach is to purchase proper bags that have been
tested. Mila and other manufacturers make different bags for
outdoor and indoor crops. Plants grown outdoors have smaller
resin heads than plants grown indoors and require a smaller
mesh bag to collect the resin effectively.

## Equipment Needed

- Standard washing machine
- Zipped Ice-O-Lator bags (silkscreen mesh)
- Ice (chunks the size of your fist)
- Cold water
- Large collection container
- Additional Ice-O-Lator bag for final filtration

## The Colombian Method

The Colombians developed a streamlined approach to process
large quantities efficiently:
