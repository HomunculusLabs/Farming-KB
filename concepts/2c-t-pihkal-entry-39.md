# 2C-T (PIHKAL Entry #39)

2,5-Dimethoxy-4-methylthiophenethylamine

## Chemical Classification

- Family: [[2c-b-compound-profile]]|2C-B.
The one true negative, which has been pretty consistent with this drug, is
that there is a certain emotional removal. One teeny step removed. One is
connected with feelings, certainly, but there is a tendency for the intellect
to be more evident, in me, than the heart. Pretty good material, but there

later."

## Extensions and Commentary

The earliest work with the sulfur atom was with the three-carbon chain
materials, the [[doi-compound-profile]], 2C-N from DON, etc. Since the
original name for ALEPH-1 was DOT (desoxy- with a thiomethyl group at the
4-position), the 2C-T naming followed this general pattern. The subsequent
numbering of 2C-T compounds (2C-T-2, 2C-T-4, etc.) is assigned in strict
historical order of conception, with no structural significance in the
number.

## Tweetio Homologues

Both mono-ethoxy Tweetios of 2C-T have been synthesized and evaluated:

- 2CT-2ETO (2-ethoxy-5-methoxy-4-methylthiophenethylamine): Effects felt
  very quickly with blurred vision. 50 mg was unable to produce greater than
  +1; duration approximately 4 hours.

- 2CT-5ETO (5-ethoxy-2-methoxy-4-methylthio-phenethylamine): Body awareness
  and modest eyes-closed visuals at 30 mg. Quiet, peaceful, contemplative,
  and insightful. Duration approximately 15 hours; Halcion needed for sleep.

## Related Compounds
