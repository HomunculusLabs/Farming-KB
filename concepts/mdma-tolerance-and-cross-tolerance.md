# MDMA Tolerance and Cross-Tolerance Patterns

**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

PiHKAL documents detailed tolerance and cross-tolerance studies for key phenethylamine compounds, particularly MDMA and MDA. These studies provide crucial evidence for understanding mechanisms of action and the relationship between different pharmacological classes.

## MDMA Tolerance Development

### Daily Dosing Study

In one of the most informative studies documented in PiHKAL, MDMA was consumed daily at 9:00 AM for nearly a week:
- Day 1: 120 mg
- Days 2-6: 160 mg each day
- By day 5: "there were no effects from the drug except for some mydriasis"
- By day 6: even the mydriasis "appeared to be lost"

This demonstrates rapid and nearly complete tolerance development to MDMA's subjective effects over approximately 5 days of consecutive use.

### Tolerance Reversal

A complete drug holiday of 6 days "led to the reversal of this tolerance, in that 120 milligrams of MDMA had substantially the full expected effects." ## Clinical Implications

### Supplemental Dosing Protocol

The tolerance data informed clinical MDMA protocols. A common procedure in early psychotherapeutic sessions was:
- Initial dose of 120 mg MDMA
- Supplemental dose of 40 mg at approximately 1.5 hours
- Rarely, a second supplement of 40 mg at 2.5 hours

The supplements extended the experience "with only a modest exacerbation of the usual physical side-effects, namely, teeth clenching and eye twitching." The rapid tolerance development suggests that even within a single session, the second and third doses produce diminishing returns.

### Post-Session Effects

Reports of "tiredness and lethargy on the day following the use of MDMA" were common enough that "this factor should be considered in the planning of clinical sessions." This post-session fatigue may relate to serotonin depletion following the drug's releasing action.

## Comparison with Classical Psychedelics

The tolerance patterns documented in PiHKAL differ from classical psychedelics like LSD, where:
- Tolerance develops rapidly (within hours) but also reverses rapidly (2-3 days)
- Cross-tolerance exists between LSD, psilocybin, and mescaline (all acting as 5-HT2A agonists)
- MDA shows cross-tolerance with classical psychedelics but MDMA does not

This pattern places MDA in the classical psychedelic category while MDMA occupies a distinct pharmacological space.
