# JADAM Microbial Solution (JMS) -- Preparation and Use

## Overview

JADAM Microbial Solution (JMS) is the cornerstone input of JADAM [[coleman-deep-organic-farming-philosophy]]. It is a culture of [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] collected from local leaf mold, cultured using simple ingredients (potato and sea salt) at [[jadam-ambient-temperature-principle]]. JMS is used for soil application, foliar spraying, [[jadam-seed-treatment-and-nursery-management]], and even livestock management. At peak fermentation, 1 mL contains approximately 0.1 billion microorganisms.

## Philosophy Behind JMS

All microorganisms are beneficial. The leaf mold under decomposing leaves in forests is "black gold" -- teeming with indigenous microorganisms adapted to the local environment, making them the best workers for nearby farms. Commercial microorganism products are unnecessary; the microbes you need are already in nature, free for the taking. JMS is the input that should not be commercialized because effective microbes for a region can only come from that region.

## JMS Preparation -- 500 L (132 gal) Recipe

### Ingredients
- **Starter:** Leaf mold -- 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
- **Medium:** Boiled potato -- 1 kg (2.2 lb) + sea salt -- 0.5 kg (1.1 lb)
- **Optional diversification:** Blend 1 kg of crop leaves/fruits, wild grass from surroundings

### Equipment
- Plastic container with lid (500 L / 132 gal)
- 2 socks or cotton bags, 2 rocks, stick, string

### Steps
1. Prepare 1 kg of boiled potato for 500 L of water
2. Put potatoes and rocks in cotton bag; knead until potatoes fully melt into water (or use blender)
3. Put leaf mold and rocks in another cotton bag; knead so leaf mold melts into water
4. Optionally, add blended crop leaves/fruits/wild grass in a cotton bag
5. Cover container with lid; leave at ambient temperature

### Fermentation Timeline
- At 28C (82F): ~24 hours to completion
- At 25C (77F): ~72 hours
- At 19C (66F): ~96 hours
- Below 18C (64F): use heating and insulation

### Reading the Fermentation
- Bubbles start rising as microbes feed on potatoes and sea salt (~21 hrs)
- Bubble size increases (~32 hrs)
- Foam forms a circle in center (~46 hrs)
- Foam becomes vigorous (~48-55 hrs)
- Clear disc forms ("pizza-like layer") -- **this is peak, use now** (~67-72 hrs)
- Disc starts crumbling -- microbes dying (~84 hrs)
- All foam gone, film remains -- not suitable as JMS but usable as [[jadam-liquid-fertilizer-nutritional-perspective]] (~144 hrs)

**The peak-bubble period lasts about 12 hours.** At peak, microorganisms reach ~1 billion per mL. 500 L of JMS is equivalent to 1,000 bottles of commercial microorganism products.

## Alternative Mediums

### Grain Medium
- Brown rice (7 parts) + mixed grains (3 parts)
- Soak in water 1-2 hours before cooking; add lots of water
- Cook, then blend with water and grind in electric blender
- Put in cotton bag and knead into water
- No need to worry about C/N ratio

### Customized Medium
- Add crop-specific ingredients to potato base (bell pepper for bell pepper farm, grapes for vineyard, etc.)
- For 500 L: blend 1 kg of ingredient into fine particles; put in cotton bag; knead in water
- Crop residues and waste fruits make even better JMS
- Using only crop residues (no potato) works but takes much longer

## Winter Cultivation

When water temperature drops below 18C (64F):
- Use a 3kW heater for 500 L, 1.5kW for 100-300 L
- Wrap container tightly with insulation on all sides (bottom, side, top)
- Inside a greenhouse is an excellent location
- At 25C: vigorous foam; at 20C: cold-surviving microbes also propagate
- Always use ELCB (Earth leakage circuit breaker) for safety

## How to Use JMS

### Soil Application (Primary Method)
- Dilute with water (at least 10x)
- Apply every time crops are watered
- For greenhouses: mix JMS with irrigation water, wet soil fully each time
- For open fields: apply before rain so microbes reach deep into soil
- Apply 3-4 times per month during growth
- 500 L of JMS can cover 0.1 to 3.3 ha (0.25-8.2 acres)

### Foliar Application
- Mix 10-20 L of JMS + over 3 L of JWA in 500 L of water
- **Must use JWA** for even coating and to prevent marks on plants
- Filter JMS thoroughly (finer than a fine net, such as fabric)
- If using more than 20 L of JMS, JWA becomes less effective (microbes break it down)
- Always use soft water that passes the JWA test

### Seed/Seedling Treatment
- Soak seeds or seedlings in JMS dilution or leaf mold solution for 2-3 minutes before planting
- Greatly improves root settlement
- Microbial diversity prevents diseases

### Livestock Use
- Dilute 20x with water; feed to animals or spray on shelter floors
- Significantly reduces odor
- Increases feed conversion ratio

## Key Warnings

- Do not apply JMS undiluted during active growth -- can radically increase microorganism population and damage roots
- Always filter before applying to soil or plants
- If peak is missed, dilute 10x and use as liquid fertilizer
- Do not use air pumps for culturing -- produces too much foam, prevents judging peak
- Do not leave unused JMS sitting -- it will smell bad and attract mosquito larvae

## Effects of JMS

- Solves most soil-borne disease problems (nematodes, wilt, virus diseases)
- Increases microbial population and diversity in soil
- Suppresses particular pathogens from gaining dominance
- Raises soil temperature (less chill/freeze damage, earlier harvest)
- Breaks down insolubilized nutrients and contaminants in soil
- Promotes robust root settlement
- Combined with seawater/salt and phyllite solution, it is the "most important technology in JADAM organic farming"
