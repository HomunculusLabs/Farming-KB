### Singing and Chanting

The shaman's singing is invariably considered essential to the success of the
healing or divinatory process. The singing typically has a fairly rapid beat,
similar to the rhythmic pulse in shamanic drumming journeys found throughout
Northern Hemisphere shamanic traditions. Psychically, the rhythmic chanting
provides support for moving through the flow of visions and minimizes the
likelihood of becoming stuck in frightening or seductive experiences. The
songs themselves, known as [[icaros-power-songs]] through which healing and diagnosis takes place. Shamanic sucking
