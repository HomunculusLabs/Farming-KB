--- Dry Sift and Water Extraction Methods
The collection and processing of trichomes -- the tiny [[greg-green]] provides detailed instructions for effective screening:
1. Use a metal screen rated between 100 to 140 lines per inch (120
   lines is most common for home hash makers).
2. Construct a wooden frame to hold the screen.
3. Place the screen over small wooden blocks, resting on a mirror or
   sheet of glass with about an inch of gap.
4. Place small amounts of skuff on the screen.
5. Gently roll the skuff back and forth using a credit card or similar
   flat plastic object. Apply very little pressure.
6. It may take a hundred passes before visible resin glands appear on
   the mirror below.
7. Sweep the collected glands off the mirror with the card.
8. For a second pass, apply slightly more pressure to dislodge
   remaining glands -- this produces a lower quality grade since some
   leaf and branch particles will also pass through.
The objective is to collect as much resin from the skuff as possible,
producing different grades that can be used to make hash.
## Drum Machines
A drum machine is an automatic screening device that can be built at
home. Key features:
- Diameter typically ranges from 1.5 to 2 feet.
- A screen cylinder rotates slowly (about 2 rotations per minute) via
  a small motor.
- Skuff is placed inside the rotating drum.
- Trichomes fall through the sieve onto a glass or mirror surface
  below.
- The tumbler can run for up to 1 hour for maximum extraction without
  pressure.
- A small wooden cylinder placed inside the barrel applies pressure
  for faster extraction (at the cost of some quality).
