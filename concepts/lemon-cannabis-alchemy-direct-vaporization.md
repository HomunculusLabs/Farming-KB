# Smoking Cannabis Oil by Direct Vaporization

Chapter 7 of *Cannabis Alchemy* by Ben Lemon describes the various
methods for smoking cannabis oil by direct vaporization with heat. This
is one of the most direct ways to consume purified cannabis oil
preparations, offering rapid onset of effects and efficient use of small
quantities of oil.

## Overview

There are several customary methods for smoking oil by direct
vaporization with heat. The chapter focuses on techniques that vaporize
the oil without combusting it, which is considered more efficient and
potentially less harmful than methods that involve burning the oil
alongside plant material.

## The Glass Oil Pipe (Vapor Pipe)

The most common method described is the glass oil pipe, or vapor pipe.
The oil is placed in the glass bowl of the pipe and the pipe is heated
from below with a flame, similar to the method for smoking opium. This
is a very efficient method, as very small amounts of oil may be
vaporized at one time.

The glass pipe design allows the user to observe the oil as it
vaporizes, making it easier to control the temperature and avoid
combustion. When heated gently, the oil melts and then vaporizes,
producing a smooth stream of vapor that can be inhaled through the
pipe's mouthpiece.

The efficiency of this method lies in its directness. Unlike a joint or
water pipe filled with plant material, where much of the THC is lost to
side-stream smoke or trapped in the unburned material, the vapor pipe
delivers nearly all of the vaporized cannabinoids directly to the user.

## Aluminum Foil Method

Another method for smoking oil straight is to place a tiny dab of it on a
piece of aluminum foil. The foil is then heated from below with a match
and the smoke is inhaled from above the boiling oil through a tube or
funnel.

This is essentially the same as smoking in a glass pipe, except that a
new spot may be used each time and there is no build-up of residue at
the point of vaporization. The foil method avoids the problem of
residue accumulation that can occur with repeated use of a glass pipe.

### Foil Preparation

Before using, the foil is heated in a gas flame to burn off any coating
or impurity on the foil which might also vaporize. This is an important
safety step, as aluminum foil often has a thin coating or residue from
manufacturing that could produce toxic fumes when heated.

The use of a tube or funnel to inhale the vapor allows the user to
position their mouth at a comfortable distance from the heated foil,
preventing burns and allowing better control of the vapor stream.

## Advantages of Direct Vaporization

Direct vaporization of cannabis oil offers several distinct advantages
over other consumption methods:

- **Efficiency**: Very small amounts of oil produce significant effects
  since nearly all of the material is vaporized and inhaled.
- **Rapid onset**: Effects are felt within seconds to minutes, as the
  vaporized cannabinoids are absorbed through the lungs directly into
  the bloodstream.
- **No plant material**: The user consumes only purified cannabinoids
  without the tar, particulate matter, and other combustion products
  associated with smoking plant material.
- **Dose control**: Small amounts can be vaporized incrementally,
  allowing the user to titrate their dose precisely.
- **Potency**: Purified and refined oils, especially those that have
  been isomerized or acetylated, are extremely potent when vaporized
  directly.

## Temperature Control

A key skill in direct vaporization is controlling the heat applied to
the oil. Too little heat and the oil will not vaporize; too much heat
and the oil may combust, producing harsh smoke and destroying some of
the active cannabinoids.

The ideal temperature range for vaporizing THC is approximately 157
degrees C (315 degrees F), though the optimal temperature can vary
depending on the specific cannabinoids present. Gentle, indirect
heating allows the user to find the right temperature through practice
and observation.

## Equipment Considerations

The chapter implies that specialized equipment, while not strictly
necessary, improves the experience. Glass pipes designed specifically
for oil vaporization have bowls that hold small quantities of oil and
are shaped to concentrate the vapor. The foil method requires minimal
equipment but demands more skill and attention from the user.

## Comparison with Other Methods

Direct vaporization differs from the other consumption methods described
in *Cannabis Alchemy* in several ways. Unlike capsules (which provide
delayed, long-lasting effects through oral ingestion), vaporization
provides immediate but shorter-duration effects. Unlike reefers or
tinctures, direct vaporization uses only the purified oil without any
carrier material.
