## Brassica Growing - Organic Methods

The cabbage family, or brassicas, includes some of the most important
vegetables in the organic garden: cabbages, broccoli, Brussels sprouts,
kale, cauliflower, and kohlrabi. These leaf vegetables are especially
rich in iron and vitamins. Brassicas generally follow legumes in the
rotation scheme, so there will be a reserve of nitrogen in the soil
from the bacteria in the root nodules of peas and beans. Strict crop
rotation is essential to deter the build-up of clubroot disease.
