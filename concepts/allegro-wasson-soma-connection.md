## Allegro's Response

Allegro published The Sacred Mushroom and the Cross just two years after SOMA,
in 1970. The timing was significant. Both books proposed that the fly-agaric had
played a central role in ancient religion, but Allegro's claims were far more
radical.

While Wasson argued that the fly-agaric was the Vedic Soma (a claim restricted
to ancient Indian religion), Allegro extended the mushroom theory to encompass
