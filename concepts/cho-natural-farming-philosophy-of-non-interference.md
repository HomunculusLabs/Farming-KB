At the core of Dr. Cho Han Kyu's Natural Farming system lies a
philosophy of non-interference with natural processes. Cho
believed that the best way to achieve top quality yield is to
respect the nature of life rather than forcing crops to produce
more than they naturally can. This philosophy distinguishes
Natural Farming from both conventional agriculture and standard
[[cervantes-chemical-fertilizers-guide]], genetic modification, or
controlled environments that eliminate natural selection
pressures.

The practical implication is that farmers should focus on
creating conditions where natural potential can express itself
rather than trying to manufacture outcomes through external
inputs. In crop production, this means building healthy soil
ecosystems, using locally adapted microbial inoculants, and
timing inputs to match the plant's natural growth cycle rather
than overriding it with [[cho-nutritive-cycle-theory]] Theory, and
its own set of inputs made from locally available materials.
