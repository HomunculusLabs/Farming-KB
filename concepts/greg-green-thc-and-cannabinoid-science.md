## THC Levels in Seed Bank Catalogues

When examining a strain in a seed bank catalogue, growers are guided to
check for the THC levels of that plant to understand how potent it is.
Many seed sellers and breeders measure their plants' THC levels and give
accounts on how much THC their plants have. However, Green cautions that
many breeders like to exaggerate their THC claims, while some do not.

If a grower wishes to know more about THC levels, it is best to consult
the seed bank or breeder directly for details. Independent testing
through laboratories provides the most accurate results, though this can
be expensive and is not practical for most home growers.
