## Overview
Herpes simplex is a common virus belonging to the same family as the chickenpox
virus. There are currently eight known herpes viruses. According to orthodox
medicine, all herpes viruses can exist in the body without outward signs or symptoms
until a period of depressed immunity results in an outbreak. Oral herpes (cold sores
or fever blisters), called HSV-I, usually appears above the waist, in contrast to
genital herpes (HSV-II). No orthodox cure exists; standard [[blesching-dark-night-of-soul-meditation-adverse-effects-mind-body]].
