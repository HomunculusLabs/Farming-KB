## Soil Physical Composition

All soils in the study were classified as sandy loam with similar proportions
of sand (62-66%), silt (16-18%), and clay (17-21%). Despite this similar
texture classification, significant differences in edaphic chemistry existed
between the two main soil types used in the second experiment:

- **Mo-Bio soil**: Higher salinity (5.12-7.44), higher total nitrogen
  (0.26-1.51%), lower organic carbon (3.02-5.00%), and lower water content
  (0.101-0.178).
- **Orange County soil**: Much lower salinity (1.73), moderate nitrogen
  (0.53%), very high organic carbon (20.0%), and higher water content
