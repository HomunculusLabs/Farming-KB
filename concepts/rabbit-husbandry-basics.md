# Rabbit Husbandry Basics

Rabbits are among the most efficient homestead livestock: high feed
conversion (3-4 lb feed per lb gain), minimal space, excellent garden
manure, and quiet.

## Why Rabbits for Meat

- Market weight (4-5 lbs) in 8-12 weeks
- Breeding trio (1 buck, 2 does) fits in ~4x8 ft
- One doe can produce 6-8 litters/year, 6-10 kits each
- High protein, low fat, all white meat
- Quiet and clean — suitable for suburban settings

## Breeds

### New Zealand White
Most popular meat breed. 4-5 lbs at 8-10 weeks. 8-10 kits/litter. Calm,
good mothers, clean white appearance for processing. Industry standard.

### Californian
Dual-purpose meat and fur. 4-5 lbs at 10-12 weeks. 6-8 kits/litter. Good
meat-to-bone ratio, hardy. Black points on nose, feet, ears, tail.

### Rex
Meat and fur (velvety plush coat). 4-5 lbs at 10-12 weeks. 6-8 kits.
Docile. Exceptionally soft pelts valuable for crafts.

### Other: Flemish Giant (15-20 lbs, slower growing), Silver Fox (gentle,
good mothers), Satin (good meat yield, beautiful sheen).

## Housing

### Individual Hutch System
- **Cage:** 30x36 in for doe, 24x30 for buck. 18-24 in height. Welded wire
  (14-gauge 1x2 mesh floor). Provide resting board to prevent sore hocks.
- **Nesting box:** 12x12x12 in, provided days before kindling.
- **Protection:** Predator-proof, weather-proof, elevated off ground.

### Colony Housing
- Larger enclosed area (10x10 ft minimum for 1 buck + 2-3 does), secure
  from diggers, with shelters, hay feeders, water.
- More natural behavior but harder to manage breeding and health.
- Remove buck after breeding (may kill kits).

## Feed

- **Pellets:** 16-18% protein grower for kits/breeding does; 12-14%
  maintenance for bucks/non-breeders. 1-2 oz per lb body weight daily.
- **Hay:** Unlimited timothy or grass hay always available — critical for
  dental and digestive health. Alfalfa for kits/pregnant does only.
- **Greens:** Introduce gradually. Safe: [[dandelion]], plantain, clover, kale,
  parsley, raspberry leaves, comfrey (small amounts). Toxic: rhubarb,
  potato tops, tomato leaves, foxglove.
- **Treats (5% max):** Rolled oats, black oil sunflower seeds, apple,
  banana, carrots.
- **Always provide unlimited water.**

## Breeding

- **Maturity:** Small breeds 4-5 months; medium/large 5-7 months.
- **Take doe to buck's cage** (does are territorial). Repeat mating 2-3
  times.
- **Gestation:** 30-32 days (average 31). Provide nest box day 27-28 with
  clean bedding.
- **Kindling:** Usually early morning, 6-10 kits. Check within hours; remove
  dead kits. Foster extras if doe has more than 8 nipples.
- **Kits:** Eyes open 10-12 days, nibble solids at 2-3 weeks.
- **Weaning:** Separate at 6-8 weeks, gradually over several days.
- **Schedule:** 4 litters/doe/year for sustainability. Replace does after 2-3
  years.

## Processing

- **Dispatch:** Cervical dislocation or rabbit wringer. Verify death before
  proceeding.
- **Skin:** Hang by hind legs, cut around each leg, peel down like a sock.
- **Eviscerate:** Remove organs. Save liver, heart, kidneys.
- **Chill:** Ice water 1+ hour, age in fridge 24-48 hours before freezing.
- **Yield:** ~60% dressed weight (5 lb live = ~3 lb dressed).
