# PF Tek Alcohol Flaming Sterilization Method

## Overview

[[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus-cultivation-method]] described a simple but effective method for
sterilising inoculation needles and other small metal tools using
an alcohol flame. This technique is central to maintaining sterility
during the PF [[pf-tek-inoculation-technique-and-syringe-management]] process and offers advantages over other
flame sources.

## The Alcohol Flame vs Butane Lighter

### The Problem with Butane

Fanaticus explicitly recommended against using butane cigarette
lighters for needle sterilisation. The reason: butane lighters
produce an undesirable soot residue that deposits on the needle
surface. This carbon soot can contaminate the substrate when the
needle is inserted, potentially introducing foreign material and
affecting colonisation.

### The Alcohol Advantage

An alcohol flame produces a clean burn with no soot residue. The
blue flame of burning denatured alcohol provides adequate heat for
sterilisation while leaving the needle surface clean. This is
particularly important for the [[pf-tek]] because the needle is inserted
directly into the substrate through the lid holes, and any residue
on the needle will be carried into the sterile substrate.

## The Tequila Shotglass Setup

Fanaticus described a specific apparatus for producing a controlled
alcohol flame:

### Materials
- One tequila shotglass
- Denatured [[permaculture-on-farm-alcohol-fuel-production]] (available at hardware stores)
- An eyedropper or pipette
- A match or lighter (only used to ignite the alcohol)

### Assembly
1. Place the tequila shotglass upside down on a stable surface
2. Using an eyedropper, apply a few drops of denatured alcohol
   fuel onto the hollow bottom of the inverted glass
3. Touch the alcohol pool with a match or lighter to ignite

The inverted shotglass creates a small, stable platform that holds
the fuel and produces a short, controlled blue flame. The amount
of alcohol can be adjusted to control flame size and duration.

## Sterilisation Procedure

### For Needle Sterilisation

1. Produce the alcohol flame using the shotglass method
2. Hold the needle in the blue flame for a few seconds
3. The flame will heat the stainless steel to sterilising
   temperature
4. Allow the needle to cool briefly before inserting into the
   substrate jar — the residual heat should not be enough to
   damage spores, but a brief cooling period is prudent

### What Happens During Flaming

When a needle containing spore solution is flamed, Fanaticus noted
there may be "a few pops of boiling water spurting out of the
needle." This is normal and harmless — the spores within the
syringe body are protected from the brief heat exposure. The
boiling only affects the small amount of solution in the needle
itself.

## When to Flame the Needle

Fanaticus specified that needle flaming is necessary when:

- The syringe needle has been touched by hands or any surface
- Re-using a syringe after a previous inoculation session
- The needle has been exposed to potential contamination
- Beginning a new inoculation batch after any significant pause
