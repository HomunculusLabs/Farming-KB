## The Etymology of Soma and Haoma

"Soma" and "haoma" are different forms of the same word, derived from a root meaning
"to squeeze out liquid" — su in Sanskrit, hu in Avestan. This etymology indicates that
the preparation of the sacrament involved pressing or squeezing the plant to extract its
active juice. Both traditions describe a ritual pressing of the plant, with the
resulting liquid being consumed by priests during ceremonies.
