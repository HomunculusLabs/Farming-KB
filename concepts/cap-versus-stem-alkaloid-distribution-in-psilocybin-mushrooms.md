## Relationship to Flush Number

These cap-versus-stem measurements were taken specifically from the first
flush, when psilocin levels are at or near zero in most strains. Bigwood
and Beug's broader research demonstrated that psilocin levels generally
increase with subsequent flushes, reaching maximum by the fourth flush.
Whether the cap-versus-stem distribution pattern observed in the first
flush persists in later flushes when total psilocin content is higher
remains an open question.

Psilocybin levels, by contrast, showed no consistent upward or downward
trend across flushes but varied unpredictably by a factor of four even
under controlled conditions. Whether the cap-to-stem ratio for psilocybin
remains constant across flushes is also not fully established, though the
first-flush data from three strains suggests it may vary genetically.
