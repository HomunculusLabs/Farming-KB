## Designing for Multiple Functions

When selecting plants for the ecological garden, evaluate each candidate against multiple functional categories. Ask these questions for every species under consideration:

1. What food does this plant produce for people, and when?
2. What habitat does it create for wildlife?
3. How does it build soil or cycle nutrients?
4. Does it modify microclimates favorably for nearby plants?
5. What pest management benefits does it offer?
6. How does it manage water on the site?
7. Does it control erosion or stabilize soil?
8. What aesthetic qualities does it contribute?
9. Does it produce useful materials?
10. Can it serve as a nurse plant for more desirable species?
