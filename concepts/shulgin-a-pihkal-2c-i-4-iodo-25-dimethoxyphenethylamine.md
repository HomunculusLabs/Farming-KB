# 2C-I (4-Iodo-2,5-Dimethoxyphenethylamine)

## Chemical Identity

2C-I is compound #33 in PiHKAL, the common name for 2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodophenethylamine hydrochloride. It is a member of the 2C family of [[pihkal-beta-substituted-phenethylamines]], distinguished by the presence of an iodine atom at the 4-position of the 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine skeleton. Iodine is the heaviest of the stable halogens, and its presence gives 2C-I unique properties, particularly its suitability for radio-isotopic labelling.

- **Chemical formula**: C₁₀H₁₅ClINO₂ (hydrochloride salt)
- **Molecular weight**: 327.58 g/mol (HCl salt)
- **Appearance**: White microcrystalline solid (hydrochloride)
- **Melting point**: 246-247°C (hydrochloride)
- **CAS**: Not listed in PiHKAL

The [[serotonin-psilocybin-structural-relationship-hallucinogenic-mechanism]] between 2C-I and 2C-B is chemically elegant: both are halogen-substituted at the identical 4-position, with iodine directly below bromine in the periodic table. This periodic relationship makes them "truly analogous" in Shulgin's words, and the frequent comparisons between the two compounds in user reports stem from this structural suggestion.

## Dosage and Duration

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| **Threshold** | ~10 mg |
| **Light dose** | 12-14 mg |
| **Common dose** | 14-18 mg |
| **Strong dose** | 18-22 mg |
| **Duration** | 6-10 hours |
| **Onset** | 20-40 minutes (alert within minutes) |
| **Offset** | Abrupt drop-off at 5.5-6 hours, then gentle decline |

The effective range is relatively narrow compared to some 2C compounds, making dose accuracy important. The onset is described as relatively quick — one subject reported "an immediate alert within minutes," though full effects build gradually over 1-2 hours to peak at approximately 2 hours.

## Qualitative Effects

### Visual Effects

The most consistently reported feature of 2C-I is its visual richness. Reports describe:

- "Persian carpet type" patterning, very colorful and active
- Strong color enhancement throughout the visual field
- Darkened or shadowed areas that shift position in the visual field — a characteristic shared with 2C-B
- Detailed color and pattern movement in pictures and artwork
- Focusing on walls or the outside of a house produces less activity than focused attention on interior objects

The visual character is frequently compared to 2C-B, with observers noting similarities in the type of patterning while acknowledging differences in the overall "frequency" of the experience.

### Emotional and Cognitive Effects

- "Much more balanced emotional character" compared to some other phenethylamines
- "No feeling of insight, revelation, or progress toward the true meaning of the universe" — a self-aware assessment from one subject
- Enhanced conversational clarity: "conversations were extremely clear and insightful"
- "The degree of honesty was incredible" — one subject reported being unafraid to say anything
- Felt "centered" with good self-regard
- Easy communication without the empathogenic warmth of [[mdma]]
- Body effects are minimal: "Good on the body," "No body problems"

### Physical Effects

- Mild to no body load at common doses
- One subject at 20 mg reported slight excess energy, suggesting sympathetic activation
- No difficulties with sleep reported at standard doses
- Appetite was normal to increased: "I ate like a horse" (15 mg)
- Next-day aftereffects: excellent sleep, unusually good energy the following day

## Comparison to 2C-B

The comparison between 2C-I and 2C-B dominates the literature on both compounds. Shulgin notes:

- **Visuals**: Similar "Persian carpet" patterning, though 2C-I's are described as slightly less immersive or "sensual"
- **Body feel**: 2C-I's transition to the peak is "not as strongly dark as 2C-B"
- **Emotional tone**: 2C-I feels like "getting used to a slightly different frequency" rather than being inherently less engaging
- **Duration**: 2C-I lasts 6-10 hours versus 2C-B's 4-8 hours
- **Offset**: 2C-I has a notably abrupt drop-off followed by gentle decline

One experienced observer noted: "I suspect it's more a matter of getting used to the language of 2C-I and the feelings — getting tuned to a slightly different frequency, really — rather than that the material is less sensual or less easy to use sensually."

## The Contact High Phenomenon

PiHKAL includes a remarkable report of a "contact high" at 0 mg. An observer who was present at a group session but consumed no 2C-I whatsoever reported reaching a "delightful plus 2.5" experience within five minutes of arriving, simply by "absorbing the ambience" of those who had actually taken the compound. This experience lasted about four hours and came down at approximately the same time as the other participants.

Shulgin includes this report as "an instructive observation of what has been called a contact high." Whether this represents a genuine psychosomatic response, expectancy effects, or subtle [[duggar-mushroom-environmental-factors-temperature-moisture-light]] remains undetermined, but its documentation in PiHKAL has made it a frequently discussed phenomenon in [[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] circles.

## Radio-Labelling Applications

The iodine atom at the 4-position makes 2C-I and its 3-carbon analogue DOI natural candidates for radio-isotopic labelling experiments. Radio-iodine can be readily incorporated into the molecule, allowing researchers to trace the compound's distribution, [[beta-glucan-receptor-binding]], and metabolic fate using techniques such as PET (positron emission tomography) or SPECT (single photon emission computed tomography).

The most impressive radio-labelling findings, according to Shulgin, have been made with DOI (2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine), the amphetamine homologue of 2C-I. DOI has been extensively used as a radioligand for studying 5-HT₂A receptor distribution and density in brain tissue, contributing significantly to the understanding of [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]] pharmacology.

## Extension: 2CI-2ETO

One Tweetio (2-EtO homologue) of 2C-I has been synthesized: 2CI-2ETO (2-ethoxy-4-iodo-5-methoxyphenethylamine).

- **Hydrochloride mp**: 175-175.5°C
- **Threshold**: ~5 mg
- **Duration**: 2-4 hours (very short compared to parent 2C-I)
- **Maximum effect**: Threshold level only, even at doses up to 50 mg

The most interesting finding was that doses above 5 mg produced no more intense effects but simply extended the threshold experience by a couple of hours. At no dose tested did 2CI-2ETO produce even a plus-two response. This represents a significant departure from the parent compound's activity profile and suggests that the 2-ethoxy substitution dramatically alters receptor interaction.

## The Astatine Speculation

Shulgin engages in a characteristically speculative discussion of a theoretical 2C-A (2,5-dimethoxy-4-astatophenethylamine), which would replace the iodine with astatine — the fifth halogen, discovered in the nuclear age. All astatine isotopes are radioactive. Shulgin makes two predictions about this hypothetical compound:

1. **Practical impossibility**: The most that has been produced at one time is approximately 0.05 micrograms — about one millionth of the estimated 5-10 mg active dose. Accumulating enough astatine would be virtually impossible.

2. **Lethality**: "You would not survive the screaming radiation that would bombard you if you could get the needed 5 or 10 milligrams of radio-astatine onto that magic 4-position."

The longest-lived astatine isotope has a half-life of about 8 hours, while the longest-lived natural isotope decays in less than a minute. The speculation serves as both a pharmacological thought experiment and a reminder of the practical limits of chemical exploration.

## Safety Considerations

- 2C-I has a relatively gentle body profile at common doses, making it one of the more physically tolerable 2C compounds
- The abrupt offset at 5-6 hours can be disorienting — users should be prepared for the transition
- Next-day energy is notably good, suggesting minimal serotonergic depletion at standard doses
- The contact high report, while intriguing, should not be relied upon as evidence of safety or lack of pharmacological activity at 0 mg
- As with all psychedelics, set, setting, and mental health screening are essential precautions
- [[ayahuasca-tourism-legal-status-by-country]] varies by jurisdiction; 2C-I is controlled in many countries
