## Mushrooms as a Farm Enterprise

Mushroom cultivation was one of Holzer's most important income sources in the 1980s. He grew button, oyster, shiitake, and king stropharia mushrooms on the Krameterhof and sold them throughout Lungau and neighbouring regions. The Chernobyl disaster (1986) ended this trade overnight despite the mushrooms being uncontaminated -- a lesson in the dangers of specialisation.

## Understanding Fungi

Fungi are not plants; they belong to a separate kingdom and are among the most important decomposers (saprotrophs) in soil:

- The visible mushroom is only the fruiting body (like an apple on a tree)
- The mycelium is the main organism -- thread-like cells (hyphae) that spread through the substrate
- Fungi cannot photosynthesise -- they absorb nutrients from organic material via the mycelium
- Many form mycorrhizae: symbiotic associations with plant roots, helping absorb water, nitrogen, and phosphates
- [[dighton-mycorrhizal-networks-carbon-transfer]] connect different plants, allowing [[gourmet-medicinal-mushroom-substrate-preparation]]
- Water in a tray below is wicked up through the trunk, maintaining moisture
- Drill and inoculate the trunk at multiple points
- Mushrooms fruit from the shaded sides of the trunk
