## Processing Hardware

### Essential Processing Equipment
Mollison identifies key hardware needs for a permaculture community:
- **Pressers**: Oil seed extractors, fruit presses, cider presses
- **Grinders/Mills**: Grain mills, nut butters, spice grinders
- **Juicers**: For fruits and vegetables
- **Stills**: For essential oils, distilled water, and spirits
- **Dryers**: Solar dehydrators, food dryers for preservation
- **Fermentation vessels**: For wine, beer, vinegar, lacto-fermented foods
- **Canning equipment**: For long-term food preservation
- **Bakery ovens**: For bread and baked goods
- **Cheese-making equipment**: For dairy processing

### Shared Equipment Models
Processing equipment is expensive individually but affordable when shared:
- **Community tool libraries**: Equipment available for community use
- **Cooperative ownership**: Groups purchase and share equipment
- **Mobile processing units**: Shared between communities on rotation
- **Ripple Flow rights**: The right to use equipment when not in use by owner (see below)
