## Coprinus comatus — Shaggy Mane

### Overview

The shaggy mane (inky cap) is a cosmopolitan mushroom famous for spectacular
autodigestion — fruiting bodies liquefy into black ink within hours. Rates
**1/4** outdoor and **3/4** indoor difficulty, though indoor cultivation is
rarely practical due to extreme perishability.

### Ecology and Fruiting

Fruiting is triggered by **microbial soil interactions** rather than
temperature alone. Optimal conditions: cool nights below **60°F**, foggy
mornings, and proximity to decomposing [[fukuoka-textdoc-composting-critique-futility-prepared-organic-matter]]. The entire fruiting
cycle spans just **1–2 days**, demanding daily monitoring.

### Mycelium Characteristics

- **Appearance:** White, cottony, irregular growth
- **Odor:** Sweet, pleasant smell
- **Tolerance:** Handles **high ammonia** levels uncommonly well

### Spawn Collection and Lab Isolation

**Wild spawn:** Wash a mature mushroom in water to create a spore solution
slurry, then apply directly to substrate.

**Lab cloning** is easy from stem tissue. Notably, **antibiotic media should
not be used** — the [[mycelium]] is sensitive to these compounds.

### Substrates

As secondary decomposers: [[comparison-wood-chip-mulch-vs-leaf-mold-vs-composted-manure-soil-building]], cereal straws, and agricultural
by-products. **[[casing-soil]] is required** for [[fruiting-initiation]].

### Outdoor Cultivation

Colonization: **6–12 months**. Preferred site: **shade/sunlight interface**
(e.g., hedgerows, [[compost]] edges). Place under **rotting wheat bales** to mimic
natural habitat and sustain moisture. Yields are **extremely low** regardless
of technique. Check daily during fruiting season.

### Harvesting

Timing is critical due to the 1–2 day window. A unique technique: **harvest
at night with a blacklight** — shaggy manes glow bright white under UV,
making them easy to locate before autodigestion begins.

### Key Challenges

- Harvest window measured in hours, not days
- Very low yields even under optimal conditions
- Cannot be stored or transported — must be consumed or processed immediately
- Indoor cultivation impractical for commercial purposes
