## The Oral Administration Imperative

Delivering anticancer agents by oral methods is becoming increasingly important for several reasons:

- **Cost reduction**: Oral formulations are less expensive than intravenous administration for diseases requiring protracted treatment
- **Outpatient preference**: Patients increasingly prefer outpatient treatment
- **Quality of life**: Self-administered oral formulations improve patient autonomy and well-being
- **Protracted treatment**: Cytostatic therapy often requires long-term drug administration, making oral delivery far more practical

## Compounds with Confirmed Oral Bioavailability

### PSK (Polysaccharide-K)

PSK is confirmed to be orally bioavailable (Sakagami and Aoki, 1991). With a molecular weight of 94,000-100,000 daltons, PSK can be absorbed through the gastrointestinal tract and exert systemic immune-modulating effects. This has been demonstrated in numerous [[cancer-medicinal-mushrooms-turkey-tail-psk-and-psp]] achieve oral bioavailability despite their large size:

1. **Protein moiety-facilitated absorption**: The protein component may interact with gastrointestinal transport mechanisms
2. **M-cell uptake**: Specialized M-cells in the gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) can transport macromolecules
3. **Paracellular transport**: Some passage between epithelial cells may occur
4. **Microbial degradation**: Gut microorganisms may partially digest polysaccharides into smaller, absorbable fragments
5. **Local immune activation**: Some immune effects may occur locally in the gut before systemic absorption

The finding that oral PSK can improve the impaired antitumor CD4+ T-cell response in gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) suggests that oral administration may have important local immune effects in addition to systemic ones.
