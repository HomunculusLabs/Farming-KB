# Cannabis and Hemorrhoids

## Overview

Hemorrhoids are swollen and inflamed veins in the rectum and anus that cause
discomfort and bleeding. They are extremely common, affecting approximately 75%
of people at some point in their lives. Hemorrhoids may result from increased
pressure in the lower rectum due to straining during bowel movements, sitting
for long periods, chronic constipation or diarrhea, obesity, pregnancy, or
aging.

Symptoms range from painless bleeding after bowel movements to protruding
tissue, itching, irritation, swelling, and severe pain. External hemorrhoids
can thrombose (form blood clots), causing intense pain. Internal hemorrhoids
may prolapse through the anal opening. Orthodox treatment includes over-the-
counter creams, sitz baths, rubber band ligation, sclerotherapy, and in severe
cases, surgical removal (hemorrhoidectomy).
