## Nature Cannot Be Dissected

Fukuoka extends his critique to the methodology of science itself. Nature, he argues, is an
organic whole — not an assembly of parts that can be separated, classified, and studied in
isolation. The act of dissection, whether literal or intellectual, destroys the very thing
being studied. Once nature is broken down into its components, it dies. What remains is only
an empty skeleton devoid of true essence.
