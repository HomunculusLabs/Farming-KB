# JADAM Mineral Water (JMW)

JMW is made by immersing leaf mold in water with rocks. It slowly extracts minerals and microbial diversity from both the rock and the decomposed leaf matter. Used as a nutrient source and soil drench in the [[jadam-methods]] system. Part of the foundational trio alongside [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] and sea water/phyllite solution.

## Why Mineral Water?

Plants are ~95% water. The quality of water directly impacts cell formation and plant health. Purified water cannot sustain fish life; spring/mineral water can. JMW brings inorganic minerals (Ca, Mg, Fe, K, trace elements) in a form accessible to both plants and soil microorganisms. Combined with JMS and sea water, it provides complete nutritional balance.

## Making JMW

### Ingredients

- Leaf mold: 0.5-1 kg (from forest floor -- see [[jadam-microorganism-solution-jms]] for collection method)
- Rocks: Several fist-sized rocks from your local area (see selection below)
- Water: Non-chlorinated, preferably soft
- Optional: sea salt 0.5 kg per 500L

### Rock Selection

Choose rocks that are locally available. Good candidates:
- Phyllite / shale (finely layered sedimentary rock -- excellent mineral content)
- Granite, basalt, or other igneous rocks
- Limestone (adds calcium)
- Avoid rocks with visible metal oxidation or unusual coloring

The principle: use rocks from your local environment, as their mineral profile matches what your soil evolved with.

### Process

1. Collect leaf mold from forest floor (remove undecomposed top layer)
2. Collect rocks, clean off loose dirt (do not scrub -- surface biofilm has microbes)
3. Fill container with water (200-500L)
4. Put leaf mold in a mesh/cotton bag with rocks to sink it; hang in water
5. Add additional rocks directly to the water
6. Cover loosely, place in sun or ambient temperature
7. Let steep for 1-2 weeks
8. The water will gradually darken as minerals and humic substances extract
9. Filter through fine net before use
10. Top up with fresh water and continue extracting -- can be reused for months

### Shortcut: Combined with JMS

When making JMS, you can add rocks directly to the culturing container. The rocks contribute minerals while the microbial fermentation helps chelate them into plant-available forms. Add 1 kg phyllite or rock powder in a mesh bag alongside the leaf mold bag.

## Usage

**Soil drench**: Use undiluted or diluted 1:1 with water. Apply 3-4 times per month alongside JMS.

**With JMS program**: Add JMW to your regular JMS watering rotation. The [[jadam-methods]] recommendation is to apply JMS + sea water + phyllite solution (or JMW) every time you water.

**Foliar**: Dilute 20x minimum, mix with JWA (3L per 500L). Filter thoroughly.

## Dilution Rates

| Use | Dilution | Frequency |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Soil drench (maintenance) | Undiluted to 1:5 | 3-4x/month |
| Soil drench (transplant) | Undiluted | Once at planting |
| Foliar | 1:20+ | With JMS rotation |
| Greenhouse irrigation | Mix into irrigation water | Every watering |

## When to Apply

- Before transplanting seedlings (soil foundation)
- Before lowering/sprouting of fruit trees
- Throughout growth with every watering (greenhouse) or before rain (open field)
- Combined with JMS for maximum soil biology activation

## Storage

Keep in covered container at ambient temperature. No strict expiration if kept aerobic. If it smells bad (anaerobic), dilute 1:10 and use as soil drench -- the minerals are still valuable. Replenish water as you use it.

## Role in JADAM Nutrition Program

JMW provides the inorganic mineral component of the JADAM nutrition triangle:
1. **JMS** -- microbial workforce and nutrient cycling
2. **Sea water / sea salt** -- 83+ trace elements from ocean
3. **JMW / phyllite solution** -- local rock minerals, humic substances

This combination provides both organic (microbe-processed) and inorganic (direct mineral) nutrition -- the balance that [[jadam-methods]] emphasizes.
