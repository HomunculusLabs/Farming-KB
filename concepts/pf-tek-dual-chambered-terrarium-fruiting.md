# PF TEK Dual Chambered Terrarium Fruiting

## Overview

The Dual Chambered Terrarium (DCT) is the [[fruiting-chamber-design-and-environmental-control]] introduced by
Psilocybe Fanaticus as part of the original PF TEK. It creates a simple,
self-contained humid microclimate for [[bacteria-and-microorganisms-in-mushroom-fruiting]] using two clear plastic
containers — one inverted over the other.

It requires no special equipment beyond what can be found at any discount store.

## Design and Construction

### Bottom Chamber

The bottom container holds the humidity source, typically wet perlite or
standing water. This provides continuous evaporative moisture that fills the
enclosed space with humidity.

The bottom chamber creates the moisture reservoir that drives the entire system.
As water evaporates from the perlite surface, it raises ambient humidity to
levels favorable for mushroom fruiting.

### Top Chamber

The top container is inverted and placed over the bottom one, creating the
actual [[fruiting-environment-parameters]]. Colonized substrate cakes are elevated above the
moisture source on a platform or rack.

This elevation is critical — direct contact with standing water or saturated
perlite can waterlog the cakes and promote bacterial contamination. The gap
between containers provides passive air exchange.

## Humidity Management

### The Role of Perlite

Wet perlite in the bottom chamber provides sustained humidity through surface
evaporation. The expanded volcanic glass has enormous surface area, holding
significant water while releasing it gradually.

When properly prepared, perlite alone maintains 90%+ relative humidity without
additional spraying or misting.

### Spraying Procedure

Occasional misting may supplement the passive system:

- Use a fine mist spray bottle
- Spray terrarium walls rather than directly onto cakes
- Light misting on cake surfaces is acceptable when needed
- Avoid heavy spraying that pools water on the surface

The PF TEK spraying approach is conservative. Over-misting is considered more
harmful than under-misting because excess moisture promotes bacterial growth
and can damage developing primordia.

## Temperature Considerations

Room temperature is sufficient for fruiting in the DCT. The PF TEK does not
require heated fruiting environments. Adding direct heat is explicitly
discouraged because it dries out the cakes and lowers humidity.

### Avoiding Direct Heat

Heat lamps, heating pads, and direct heat sources are problematic:

- They evaporate moisture faster than perlite can replace it
- They create temperature gradients within the chamber
- They can cause cakes to dry and crack
- They increase contamination risk through stress

The preferred approach is maintaining the entire room at a comfortable
temperature, typically 70-75°F (21-24°C).

### Temperature and Humidity Relationship

Higher temperatures increase evaporation rates and can overwhelm the perlite's
ability to maintain humidity. Keeping temperatures moderate ensures the system
works as designed.

## Symptoms of Low Humidity

Low humidity is the most common DCT problem. Recognizing symptoms early allows
corrective action before the crop is lost.
