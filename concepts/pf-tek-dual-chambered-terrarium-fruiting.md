# PF TEK: Dual Chambered Terrarium Fruiting

## Overview

The dual chambered terrarium is the [[fruiting-environment-parameters]] designed for the PF TEK.
It provides high humidity and [[psilocybe-cubensis-strain-potency-variability-controlled-conditions-bigwood-beug]] for [[bloomfield-mushroom-formation-fruiting-body-development-cultivation]] while
solving the fundamental problem: maintaining near-100% humidity without directly
wetting delicate fungal structures.

---

## Terrarium Construction

### Base: Standard 10-Gallon Aquarium

The foundation is a standard glass aquarium with clear walls for visibility. The
rectangular shape provides ample surface area for fruiting cakes, and the glass
allows monitoring progress without opening the chamber and disturbing conditions.

---

## Airtight Lid Construction

The lid must create a fully sealed environment:

- **Frame**: Wooden frame fitted snugly over the aquarium opening
- **Film**: Clear polyethylene plastic film stretched across the frame
- **Seal**: Must seal completely with **no air leaks** — even small gaps allow
  humidity to escape and compromise the environment

The airtight seal allows the terrarium to maintain 100% humidity. Any compromise
directly reduces humidity and yields.

---

## The Spray Shield: Core Innovation

The spray shield is the defining feature of the dual chambered design:

- **Material**: 1/8 inch clear acrylic (Plexiglas)
- **Dimensions**: Approximately 15 x 18 inches
- **Position**: Slanted inside the aquarium as drip shield and roof

The shield divides the aquarium into two zones:

1. **Primary chamber** (upper): Receives direct water spray, serves as the
   humidification zone
2. **Secondary chamber** (lower): Where the fungus lives — receives only
   evaporated, molecularized moisture, never direct water contact

---

## The Core Principle: Never Directly Spray the Fungus

The most important rule of PF TEK fruiting:

> **Never spray water directly onto the mushrooms or mycelium.**

Direct water contact drowns fine mycelial structures, promotes bacterial
contamination, can physically damage developing pins, and creates breeding
grounds for mold. The spray shield prevents this by breaking droplets into fine
mist, which then evaporates into molecularized water vapor that drifts into the
secondary chamber, maintaining near-100% humidity through evaporation alone.

---

## Humidity Management

### Target and Procedure

- **Goal**: 100% relative humidity — condensation visible on all surfaces
1. **Pre-spray** all interior surfaces before inserting cakes
2. Insert colonized cakes into the secondary chamber
3. **Spray vigorously** into the middle of the shield for ~5 seconds
4. Immediately **seal the lid** tightly
5. **Repeat at least 2x daily** — morning and evening is standard

---

## Ventilation and Maintenance

Stagnant air promotes contamination and CO2 buildup. During regular sessions:

1. **Remove the lid** completely
2. **Fan the interior** with cardboard to circulate fresh air
3. **Re-spray** the shield to restore humidity
4. **Seal the lid** tightly

**Water management**: Siphon pooled water from the bottom regularly using a
turkey baster or small hose. Standing water becomes a contamination source.

---

## Temperature Guidelines

### Do NOT Heat the Terrarium

[[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus]] explicitly warns against heating the [[comparison-laminar-flow-hood-vs-fruiting-chamber]].
Heating works against humidification — warm air holds more moisture but reduces
relative humidity percentage, making it harder to maintain 100% conditions.

### Acceptable Temperature Range

- **Optimal range**: 60–85°F (15–29°C)
- **Ideal**: Mid-70s°F for balanced growth rate and fruit quality
- Standard indoor room temperatures work without modification

---

## Identifying Low Humidity Problems

Visible symptoms when humidity drops below optimal:

- **Fuzzy white mycelium on caps**: "Crown of white hair" indicates mycelium
  extending outward seeking moisture from the air
- **Deformed mushrooms**: Irregular caps that fail to open properly
- **Withering**: Mushrooms dry out, shrivel, or abort entirely
- **Cracked caps**: Surface drying causes radial cracks in cap surface

If these symptoms appear, increase spraying frequency and verify lid seal
integrity.

---

## Lighting

A low-wattage fluorescent plant light provides phototropic stimulus directing
mushroom growth upward. Without light, mushrooms may grow randomly or fail to
develop properly. Standard fluorescent plant grow lights on a 12-hour cycle are
sufficient for this purpose.
