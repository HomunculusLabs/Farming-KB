## Theoretical Foundation

The two-tier selection model posits a hierarchical filtering process. Edaphic
factors, including soil type, pH, salinity, nitrogen content, carbon levels,
and water content, define the structure of the local soil microbiota. This
soil community serves as the source pool for the first community shift into
the nutrient-rich rhizosphere environment. Following this initial soil-driven
selection, migration from the rhizosphere into plant tissues is governed by
plant genotype-dependent selection of the endorhiza environment. The model
predicts several broad changes in phylum-level taxon abundance associated
with the shift from soil to root interior.
