## CBD — Cannabidiol

CBD is non-psychoactive with greater CB2 than CB1 receptor
affinity. Much of its therapeutic influence stems from suppressing
FAAH, the enzyme that breaks down anandamide, extending the bliss
molecule's activity and duration. CBD tames THC's psychoactive
effects, enabling patients to access THC's therapeutic benefits at
higher concentrations without excessive intoxication.
