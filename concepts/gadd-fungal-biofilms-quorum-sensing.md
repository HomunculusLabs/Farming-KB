# Fungal Biofilms and Quorum Sensing

**Source:** Gadd, Watkinson & Dyer, *Fungi in the Environment* (2006), Chapters 3, 10 (Read; Liu et al.)

## Overview

Filamentous fungi form complex multicellular structures through hyphal aggregation, producing tissues analogous to biofilms in bacteria. These aggregated structures include fruit bodies, sclerotia, rhizomorphs, and mycelial cords. Quorum sensing (cell density-dependent signaling) regulates key developmental transitions in fungi, including dimorphism, [[fungal-life-cycles-sexual-reproduction-metamorphosis]]
- Choice between sexual and asexual reproduction
