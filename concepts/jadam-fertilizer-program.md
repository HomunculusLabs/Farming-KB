# JADAM Fertilizer Program

JADAM rejects the conventional approach of dividing growth into N-P-K stages and applying different fertilizers for each. Instead, use wild grass and crop residue JLF (GC-JLF) throughout the entire season -- it already contains the optimal nutritional balance. The real focus is on JMS soil foundation before planting.

## Core Principle

"Give plants what is the same (or similar) as the plants." Crop residues and wild grass contain all N, P, K, Ca, Mg, B, and trace elements in the right proportions. No need to adjust individual elements.

## Growth Stage Programs

### 1. Pre-Planting: Soil Foundation Builder

The most important step in JADAM. Apply 3-4 times before transplanting (annuals) or before lowering/sprouting (perennials).

**Per 0.33 ha (0.8 ac)**:
- JMS: 500L (132 gal)
- Sea salt: 1.5kg (3.3 lb)
- Phyllite solution: 60L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF: 60L (16 gal)

Apply with plenty of water. For open fields, apply before rain. For greenhouses, apply with generous irrigation. Promotes root growth, strengthens resistance to cold/frost, reduces nematodes and wilt.

### 2. Seed/Seedling Treatment

- JMS: 100L (or 10kg leaf mold) per 500L water
- Soak seeds or dip seedling roots for 2-3 minutes before planting
- Greatly improves root settlement

### 3. Basic Additional Fertilizer (Throughout Growth)

**Per 0.33 ha (0.8 ac), 3-4 times per month**:
- JMS: 500L
- Seawater: 60L (or sea salt 1.5kg)
- Phyllite solution: 60L
- GC-JLF: 30L

Add as much water as needed. Use seawater 3-4 times per month. This is the everyday program -- the backbone of JADAM fertility.

### 4. Body Growth Promoter (When Extra Growth Needed)

Same as basic + add:
- Food scrap JLF: 60L (or urine, feces, fish JLF)

### 5. Reproductive Growth Promoter (Weak Flowering/Fruiting)

Same as basic + add:
- Calcium phosphate JLF: 60L

### 6. Fruit Growth Promoter (Fruit Development)

- JMS: 500L
- Seawater: 60L
- Phyllite solution: 60L
- Calcium JLF: 30L
- Potassium JLF: 40L

To control excessive growth: remove potassium JLF, increase calcium JLF.

## Crop Categories

### Leafy Crops (lettuce, spinach, herbs)
- Focus on wild grass JLF and nitrogen-rich JLF
- GC-JLF x100 throughout growth
- Harvest continuously means continuous nutrient replacement needed

### Fruiting Crops (tomato, pepper, cucumber, strawberry)
- Use crop residue JLF from that specific crop
- Add calcium JLF during fruiting to prevent blossom end rot
- Add potassium JLF for fruit sizing
- Fruit color enhancement: purslane JLF

### Root Crops (radish, carrot, potato, sweet potato)
- Less nitrogen, more potassium
- GC-JLF is still the base -- wild grass has balanced nutrients
- Loose soil from JMS soil foundation is critical for root development

## Seasonal Calendar

**Spring**: Prepare JLF containers, apply soil foundation builder, begin basic additional fertilizer program

**Summer**: Peak growth -- apply basic additional fertilizer 3-4x/month, increase JLF if growth is slow, pest control intensifies

**Autumn**: Continue fertilizer through harvest, prepare JLF for next year (fill containers now, age through winter)

**Winter**: JLF ages in containers, apply dormant spray on fruit trees (JWA 10L + JS 5L + clay 2kg)

## Foliar Application Notes

When applying foliar (instead of soil drench):
- Reduce JMS + JLF to below 30L total per 500L solution
- Add JWA: over 1.5L per 500L for even coating
- If JMS exceeds 20L, JWA breaks down -- limit JMS to 20L for foliar
- Seawater/sea salt reduce JWA effectiveness -- apply to soil only

## Dilution Guide

Calculate inputs based on 1 ton (264 gal) water per 0.03 ha (0.08 ac):
- x100 dilution = 10L JLF per ton of water
- x300 dilution = 3.3L JLF per ton of water
- x500 dilution = 2L JLF per ton of water

Start at x100 and adjust: stronger if growth is weak, weaker if plants are vigorous. Root settlement strength varies required JLF by 10x.

## Key Rules

1. Always add JMS when applying JLF
2. Focus fertilizer money and effort on soil foundation -- everything else follows
3. Foliar is secondary -- give nutrients to roots primarily
4. Partial fertilizers (rice bran, perilla pulp, oil cake) should be max 1/10 of total
5. GC-JLF + JMS + seawater + phyllite = complete nutrition, cost near zero
