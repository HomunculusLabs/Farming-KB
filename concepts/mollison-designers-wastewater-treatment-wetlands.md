## Treatment Process and Nutrient Cycling

[[comparison-constructed-wetlands-vs-bioswales]] purify water through a combination of physical,
chemical, and biological processes:

1. **Physical filtration**: Particulate matter is trapped in the gravel
   media and on plant root surfaces.
2. **Sedimentation**: Heavy solids settle to the bottom of the basin.
3. **Microbial decomposition**: Bacteria and fungi on root surfaces and
   in the substrate break down organic matter and transform nutrients.
   Aerobic zones convert ammonia to nitrate (nitrification); anaerobic
   zones convert nitrate to nitrogen gas (denitrification).
4. **Plant uptake**: Aquatic plants absorb nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium,
   and trace minerals, incorporating them into biomass.
5. **Pathogen die-off**: UV radiation, predation by soil microorganisms,
   and extended retention time reduce pathogen populations significantly.
