Mollison emphasises that gully control weirs need splash aprons and
must be keyed into gully sides. Low fences of 0.5 to 1.0 m are more
likely to survive floods than taller structures. Where interceptor
banks can be made above the gully-head, and water diverted, the lower
volume of flow permits weirs of 1.0 to 1.5 m high without scour damage.
