## Trichoderma — Green Mold

### Macroscopic Appearance

Green mold blotch that appears as aggressive greenish colonies on substrate
and casing surfaces. Affected mushrooms may be deformed or discolored. On
malt agar, colonies appear bright green with a woolly texture.

### Microscopic Characteristics

- **Spores**: Single-celled, approximately 4 x 5 microns.
- **Mycelium**: Initially white, rapidly turning green as sporulation begins.

### Medium of Spread

Primarily through casing materials and airborne spores. Trichoderma thrives
in slightly acidic conditions and can persist in growing rooms for extended
periods. It is one of the most common contaminants in both agar and grain
culture. See [[det]]
