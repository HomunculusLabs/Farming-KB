## The Soil Exposure Advantage

When the mobile greenhouse moves away from a section of growing beds, those
beds are exposed to the open air for an extended period. This exposure provides
several soil health benefits:

- **Sunlight**: Direct solar radiation helps sanitize the soil surface,
  reducing pathogen populations that may have built up during the covered
  growing period.
- **Rain**: Natural rainfall flushes accumulated salts and soluble nutrients
  that can build up under greenhouse cover, and restores aerobic conditions
  throughout the soil profile.
- **Wind**: Air movement across the soil surface helps dry out and disrupt
  disease cycles that favor moist, protected environments.
- **Snow**: Winter snow cover provides slow-release moisture and a gentle
  insulating effect that protects soil organisms during cold periods.
- **Freezing and thawing**: Natural freeze-thaw cycles help break up compacted
  soil layers and improve aggregate structure without mechanical intervention.
