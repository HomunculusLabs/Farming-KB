## Fungal Laccase-Mediator Systems
Laccase-mediator systems (LMS) represent one of the most significant
advances in fungal enzymatic bioremediation, extending the substrate
range of [[singh-fungal-biodegradation-of-polycyclic-aromatic-hydrocarbons]] hydrocarbons (PAHs), dye decolorization, and
degradation of other recalcitrant pollutants.

## Mediator Compounds

Several mediator compounds have been identified and characterized for
use with fungal laccases:

### ABTS (2,2'-Azinobis-(3-ethylbenzthiazoline-6-sulfonate))

ABTS is the most widely studied [[growing-gourmet-turkey-tail-trametes-versicolor-cultivation]], and ABTS is essential for the
oxidation of benzo[a]pyrene by these laccases. In the presence of 2 mM
ABTS at acidic pH, laccase supports the cleavage of diketonitrile.
ABTS also enhances oxidation of anthracene, with degradation rates
significantly increased compared to laccase alone.

### HBT (1-Hydroxybenzotriazole)

HBT is another important mediator compound. Laccase of Trametes
versicolor in the presence of HBT achieved 99% degradation of
anthracene, 91% degradation of benzo[a]pyrene, 92% degradation of
fluorene, and 96% degradation of perylene, all within 72 hours.
HBT at 1 mM concentration reduced methoxychlor by 23%, producing
identifiable metabolites. Laccase-HBT treatment achieved 28%

## PAH Degradation by Laccase-Mediator Systems

Laccase-mediator systems have been applied to the degradation of
numerous polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons:

### Trametes versicolor LMS

Laccase of T. versicolor oxidized most of 14 PAHs in vitro when
mediators were present. With HBT as mediator, the system achieved
near-complete degradation of acenaphthene, acenaphthylene, anthracene,
and perylene. Benzo[a]pyrene was degraded to 91% with formation of
benzo[a]pyrene 1,6-, 3,6-, and 6,12-diones.
With ABTS as mediator, laccase isozyme I achieved 84% degradation of
anthracene and 100% degradation of benzo[a]pyrene. Laccase isozyme II
achieved 70% anthracene degradation and 90% benzo[a]pyrene degradation.
Purified laccase isozymes without mediators showed limited oxidation
capacity, highlighting the essential role of mediators in extending the
substrate range of laccases.

### Other Fungal LMS

[[pycnoporus-cinnabarinus]] laccase with ABTS achieved 95% degradation of
benzo[a]pyrene in a bench-scale reactor within 24 hours, producing
benzo[a]pyrene 1,6-, 3,6-, and 6,12-quinones in a 2:1:1 ratio.
