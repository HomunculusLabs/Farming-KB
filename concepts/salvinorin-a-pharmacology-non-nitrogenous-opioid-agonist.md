## Overview

Salvinorin-A is the principal psychoactive compound in [[mazatec-people]] of Oaxaca, Mexico.
Salvinorin-A is remarkable in pharmacology because it is the first known naturally occurring
non-nitrogenous opioid receptor agonist.
Unlike virtually all other classical hallucinogens, which act at the 5-HT2A [[psilocybin-pharmacology-and-serotonin-receptors]] and instead acts as a potent kappa-opioid receptor (KOR) agonist.

## Chemical Properties

Salvinorin-A is a neoclerodane diterpene with the molecular formula C23H28O8.
Its classification as a non-nitrogenous compound is pharmacologically significant:
nearly all other known hallucinogens contain at least one nitrogen atom
in their molecular structure (tryptamines, phenethylamines, ergolines).
Salvinorin-A's lack of nitrogen distinguishes it fundamentally from other psychoactive compounds
and places it in a unique pharmacological category.

The compound is lipophilic and can be effectively absorbed through the oral mucosa
when fresh leaves are chewed, or through the lungs when smoked.
It is relatively unstable at high temperatures,
which affects the efficiency of smoked administration.

## Receptor Pharmacology

### Kappa-Opioid Receptor Agonism

Salvinorin-A acts as a highly selective and potent agonist at the kappa-opioid receptor (KOR).
It shows no significant binding to mu-opioid receptors (responsible for morphine-like effects)
or delta-opioid receptors.
The KOR is a G-protein coupled receptor
that modulates perception, consciousness, and interoception.
Activation of KOR produces dissociative effects,
altered perception of reality, and changes in sensory processing.

Roth et al. (2002) first demonstrated the KOR agonism of salvinorin-A,
establishing it as the first non-nitrogenous opioid receptor agonist discovered.
The EC50 for KOR activation is in the low nanomolar range,
making salvinorin-A one of the most potent naturally occurring psychoactive compounds known.

### Lack of Serotonergic Activity

Unlike LSD, psilocybin, mescaline, and DMT,
salvinorin-A shows no affinity for serotonin 5-HT2A receptors,
which are the primary site of action for classical hallucinogens.
This fundamental difference in mechanism of action
explains why the [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]]
- Visual distortions and closed-eye imagery
- Uncontrollable laughter or crying
