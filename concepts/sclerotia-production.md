- **Sclerotia initiation**: Small sclerotia begin forming as hard knots within

- **Maximum development**: Sclerotia continue to enlarge for up to **4 months**

Harvesting too early results in small, less potent sclerotia. Waiting the full
4 months maximizes both mass and potency, though diminishing returns set in
after approximately 3 months for *P. tampanensis*.

## Environmental Requirements

Unlike fruiting, sclerotia production requires minimal [[eclss-environmental-control-life-support]]:

- **Temperature**: Room temperature (20-25°C) is ideal. No cold shocking or
  temperature cycling is needed.
- **Light**: Darkness is preferred. Light exposure does not harm development
  but provides no benefit for sclerotia formation.
- **Air exchange**: Minimal. The loose jar lids provide sufficient gas
  exchange. No additional fresh air exchange (FAE) is required.
- **Humidity**: Not a concern since the sclerotia develop within the sealed
  (but breathable) jar environment.

## Harvesting

To harvest sclerotia from grass seed jars:

1. Open the jar over a clean surface.
2. Separate sclerotia from the grain substrate by hand or with a sieve.
3. Rinse sclerotia gently under cold water to remove adhering substrate.
4. Allow to air dry briefly on paper towels.
5. Fresh sclerotia can be consumed immediately, stored in the refrigerator
   for 1-2 months, or dried for longer-term storage.
