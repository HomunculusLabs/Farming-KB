## Fungal Transformation of Phenols in Soils

### Bound Residue Formation
Fungal transformation in soils produces metabolites that become covalently bound to soil organic matter (humus). While bound residues are less bioavailable and less toxic than parent compounds, they represent incomplete detoxification and may persist for extended periods.

### Degradation and Mineralization
Complete mineralization of phenols to CO2 is the ideal but often not fully achieved in soil environments. Factors limiting mineralization include:
- Adsorption of phenols to soil organic matter reducing bioavailability
- Oxygen limitation in compacted or waterlogged soils
- Competition with indigenous soil microorganisms
- Toxicity of transformation intermediates to degrading organisms
