## Vermiculite Compared to Perlite

Vermiculite and perlite are related horticultural products with fundamentally different water handling properties that make them suitable for different roles in the PF TEK system. Perlite does not actually absorb and hold water internally; rather, water adheres to its porous surface and evaporates gradually into the surrounding air. Vermiculite, by contrast, absorbs water into its expanded layered structure and holds it within the mineral itself.
