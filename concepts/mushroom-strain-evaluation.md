## Mushroom Strain Evaluation — Stamets 28-Feature Framework

## Overview

Evaluating and selecting mushroom strains is the foundation of any successful mushroom farm. Paul Stamets developed a comprehensive 28-feature framework for assessing the unique characteristics of each strain. Since tons of mushrooms can be generated from a few petri dish cultures in a matter of weeks, these features play essential roles in the success of every production run. A good strain is easy to keep but difficult or impossible to regain once it senesces.

## The Strain Selection Process

When a mushroom is brought into culture from the wild, little is known about its performance until trials are conducted. Even within the same species, mushroom strains vary to surprising degrees in growth rate, yield, flavor, appearance, and environmental sensitivity. The cultivator develops an intimate, co-dependent relationship with every strain, learning to recognize its unique "personality" through daily observation of the culture's transformations.
