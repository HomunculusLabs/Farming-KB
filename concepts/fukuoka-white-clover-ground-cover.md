## Clover Varieties and Selection

Fukuoka primarily used Ladino clover (Trifolium repens var. ladino), a large white clover
variety selected for vigorous growth and high forage production. Ladino clover produces
longer stolons and larger leaves than common white clover, giving it superior ground
coverage and competitive ability against weeds.
In cooler climates, Dutch white clover (a smaller, more compact variety) may perform better
and is more tolerant of close grazing or mowing. For orchard applications, strawberry clover
