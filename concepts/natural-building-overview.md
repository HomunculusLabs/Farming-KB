# Natural Building Overview

Updated: 2026-04-18

Natural building refers to construction methods that use locally available, minimally processed materials to create homes and structures. These methods have lower environmental impact than conventional construction, often cost less, and can be owner-built without specialized skills or heavy equipment.

## Principles

- **Local materials** -- use earth, stone, wood, and plant fibers from the building site or nearby
- **Minimal processing** -- materials are used in their natural state with minimal industrial processing
- **Owner-buildable** -- techniques can be learned and executed by non-professionals
- **[[powell-psilocybin-end-of-life-anxiety]], materials return to the earth

## Building Methods Compared

### Cob Building
See [[cordwood-construction]] for details.
- Material: short debarked log ends laid in mortar
- Walls: 12-24 inches thick
- Insulation: moderate to good depending on wood species and fill
- Cost: low (logs often free)
- Skill level: low
- Climate: versatile; thermal mass moderates temperature

### Slip Form Stone Construction
See [[comparison-cob-construction-vs-straw-bale-construction]]); hot dry climates benefit from thermal mass (cob, earthbag)
2. **Available materials** -- use what you have: clay soil (cob), straw (straw bale), sand/gravel (earthbag), stone (slip form), timber (cordwood)
3. **Budget** -- cob and earthbag are cheapest; underground is most expensive
4. **Building codes** -- straw bale and earthbag have the most code precedent; unconventional methods may require engineering review
5. **Owner involvement** -- all methods can be owner-built, but underground and stone require more expertise
6. **Time** -- slip form stone and cordwood are slowest; earthbag and cob are moderate; straw bale walls go up quickly

## Common Elements

All natural buildings benefit from:
- **Good foundation** -- raised above grade, protected from moisture
- **Wide roof overhangs** -- protect walls from rain
- **Natural plasters** -- earth or lime-based exterior and interior finishes
- **[[mollison-designers-composting-toilets-and-humanure]] and composting systems
