## Spatial Autocorrelation

Sporocarps of many species have clumped distributions. Contiguous subplots are more likely to contain the same species than those at a distance. Solutions:
- **Dispersed subsamples:** Separate plots with distance between them
- **Random plot placement:** Avoid clustering
- **Stratified random:** Sample across defined habitat types

## Convenience vs. Plot Sampling

### Convenience Sampling (Survey)
- Experienced individuals walk through area, document all taxa found
- Efficient for preliminary richness estimates
- Useful for rapid assessment of reserve sites
- Allows detection of more large, showy species than plot sampling
- **Cannot** provide standardized comparisons between sites or over time
- Collector bias may overlook some habitats or taxa

### Plot Sampling
- Standardized effort (defined area or defined time)
- All sporocarps in plots removed and examined
- Quantitative data: allows site comparisons
- Detects inconspicuous and cryptic species
- **Permanent plots:** Good for phenology, [[ectomycorrhizal-fungal-ecology]] richness than vegetation type
- Warm summers increase mycorrhizal sporocarp production in spruce forests but not pine
- Spring and fall are main [[oyster-mushroom-log-cultivation-temperate-regions-short-log-method]] with summer drought
- Excess moisture can also prevent fruiting in some species

### Vegetation
- Plant species composition directly affects [[fungal-habitat-diversity]] affects fungal richness more than geography per se
- Europe ranked lowest in fungal diversity (compared with other regions) despite most intensive collection — related to low tree diversity from Pleistocene extinctions
- Cortinariaceae and discomycetes most diverse at middle/high latitudes
- Saprobic Tricholomataceae most diverse at low latitudes

### Succession
- Substratum quality changes affect [[biodiversity-of-fungi-soil-fungal-communities-agriculture]] (early vs. late log decomposers)
- Stand age affects ectomycorrhizal communities (young vs. old stands)
- Ectomycorrhizal succession poorly documented outside plantations
