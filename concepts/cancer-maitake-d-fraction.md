# Maitake (Grifola frondosa) D-Fraction Cancer Research

## Overview

Grifola frondosa, commonly known as Maitake (Japanese for "dancing mushroom"), is a polypore mushroom found at the base of trees, particularly oaks. It has been valued as both a gourmet edible and medicinal mushroom in Japan and China for centuries. The D-Fraction, a bioactive beta-glucan extracted from Maitake, has emerged as one of the most promising mushroom-derived anticancer agents.

## Active Compounds

### Maitake D-Fraction (Grifron-D)
The D-Fraction is obtained from hot water crude extract by deproteination. It contains mainly beta-D-glucan with:
- 1-6 main chains and 1-4 branchings
- The more common 1-3 main chains and 1-6 branching
- A highly purified extract: beta-1,6 glucan branched with beta-1,3 linkages (Grifron-D, GD)

### Grifolan
A (1-3)-beta-glucan from G. frondosa, similar to Schizophyllan in primary structure. Grifolan enhances mRNA levels of IL-6, IL-1, and TNF-alpha in macrophages, functioning as a novel macrophage activator.

### Other Constituents
- Heteroglucan proteins, manogalactofucan, heteroxylan, and fucoxylan from mycelial extracts
- Acidic beta-glucan (Fa-1a-beta), hetero-beta-glucan (FIII-2c), xyloglucan

## Mechanism of Action

### Immune Modulation
Grifolan activates macrophages and increases cytokine production (IL-1, IL-6, TNF-alpha). GD has considerable immunomodulating and antitumour activities in animal models. Unlike Lentinan and Schizophyllan (which require intravenous or intraperitoneal administration), Maitake D-Fraction is orally bioavailable.

### Direct Cytotoxicity and Apoptosis
GD has cytotoxic effects on human prostate cancer cells (PC9) in vitro, acting through [[fungal-virulence-factors-host-immune-evasion]] responses that produce tumour regression and prevent metastasis.

## Clinical Trials

### Early Chinese Pilot Studies
An abstract-published pilot study involving 63 cancer patients reported response rates (partial and complete) of 95% for solid tumours and 90% for leukaemia (type unspecified).

### Japanese Non-Randomised Study (Nanba, 1997)
A clinical study using D-Fraction in 165 advanced cancer patients evaluated two treatment arms:
- **Arm 1**: Oral D-Fraction plus crude Maitake powdered tablets
- **Arm 2**: D-Fraction plus placebo tablets, plus chemotherapy

**Results**:
- 11 of 15 advanced hepatocellular carcinoma patients showed tumour regression or significant symptomatic improvement with D-Fraction plus Maitake
- When D-Fraction plus Maitake was combined with chemotherapy, overall response rates increased by 12-28% across all cancer types
- Amelioration rates by cancer type: liver (73.3%), breast (68.8%), lung (46.8%), brain (33.3%), prostatic (28.6%), leukaemia (25.0%), pancreas (20.0%), stomach (18.2%), bone (10.0%)
- Many patients recovered from severe chemotherapy side-effects when D-Fraction was given

### FDA IND Approval
The US FDA has approved Grifron-D (GD) for trial under an Investigational New Drug Application (IND) for patients with advanced cancer. Multiple US-based clinical trials are underway at various institutions. The FDA has also exempted Maitake polysaccharides from Phase I study due to limited side-effects.

### Current Trials
Phase I/II clinical trials in breast, prostate, lung, liver, and gastric cancers are underway in the US and Japan. Most are at an early clinical stage.

## Commercial Availability
Dried Maitake capsules are widely accepted dietary supplements. Other forms include Maitake tea, whole Maitake powder, and Maitake drink. The apparent success of crude Maitake powder by oral administration in cancer therapy and immune stimulation supports its suitability as a nutriceutical.

## Safety Profile
GD has few side effects. The FDA exemption from Phase I studies reflects the compound's established safety. Anecdotal clinical reports suggest GD may alleviate some chemotherapy side-effects, though this requires further controlled investigation.
