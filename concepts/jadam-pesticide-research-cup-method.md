# JADAM Pesticide Research Cup Method

## Overview

JADAM has developed a remarkably simple yet effective method for farmers to
conduct their own pesticide research using common household cups. Known as
the "cup method," this approach deliberately avoids expensive laboratory
equipment, instead relying on affordable tools that any farmer can access.
The entire research lab can be set up for approximately 50 dollars using
cups, dishes, and basic kitchen implements. This method enables farmers
to test germicidal and insecticidal properties of natural substances and
determine effective [[knf-imo-four-soil-foundation-and-field-application]].
