# Polm Gold

## Overview

Polm Gold is a sativa-dominant strain with a unique multinational heritage, created by Spliff Seeds of Holland and grown out by Poland's Satindi Seeds. The strain combines genetics from three distinct lineages: Viking, Early Pearl, and a rare Moroccan landrace from the Chefchaouen region of Northwest Africa. The name "Polm" refers to the traditional Moroccan term for hand-rubbed hashish, connecting this strain to Morocco's centuries-old cannabis culture.

The Moroccan Chefchaouen parent is particularly significant — Chefchaouen (also known as Chaouen) is a city in the Rif Mountains of Morocco, a region famous for its [[cannabis-hashish-production]]. The original sativa from this region brings unique [[cannabis-terpene-profiles]] and growing characteristics shaped by the Mediterranean [[mollison-valley-and-mountain-climate-effects]].

## Genetics and Lineage

- **Classification**: Sativa-Dominant
- **Genetics**: Moroccan Chefchaouen × Viking × Early Pearl
- **Original Creator**: Spliff Seeds, Holland
- **Current Distributor**: Satindi Seeds, Poland
- **Stability**: Highly stabilized through many generations, expressing only one phenotype
- **THC Content**: 17%

### Parent Strains

1. **Moroccan Chefchaouen**: A landrace sativa from the Rif Mountains region of Northwest Morocco. Chefchaouen is renowned as the "blue city" and sits at the heart of Morocco's traditional hashish-producing territory. This parent contributes the strain's exotic genetic heritage, robust outdoor performance, and connection to Moroccan cannabis culture.

2. **Viking**: A hardy European-adapted variety that contributes resilience to cold temperatures and short growing seasons. The Viking genetics help bridge the gap between the Moroccan parent's preference for warmth and the demands of Northern European cultivation.

3. **Early Pearl**: A classic early-finishing variety that helps reduce the flowering time, making the strain viable in regions with shorter summers. Early Pearl is known for its resin production and reliable outdoor performance.

### Stabilization Process

The offspring of these three parent strains has been stabilized through many generations of selective breeding. This stabilization means that Polm Gold crops should express only one consistent phenotype, giving growers predictable results. The stabilization process also transformed this former heat-loving plant into one that thrives in European climates, even those experiencing extreme temperature drops and heavy rainfall.

## Growing Characteristics

### Indoor vs. Outdoor

Polm Gold is emphatically **not suitable for indoor growing**. The plant is far too tall and leggy to be practical in indoor grow spaces. Even with [[cannabis-training-techniques]], the plant's natural growth habit would overwhelm most indoor setups.

Outdoors, however, Polm Gold truly excels. Given the space it needs, the plant will grow vigorously and seem like it will never stop reaching upward. This makes it an excellent choice for:

- Garden plots with ample vertical space
- Rural outdoor grows
- Mediterranean and temperate European climates
- Guerrilla growing in open areas

### Plant Structure and Root System

One of Polm Gold's most notable characteristics is its phenomenal root growth. The plant develops an extensive root system that:

- Lays down excellent foundations even on rocky soil
- Anchors the tall plant securely against wind
- Accesses nutrients and water from deep soil strata
- Contributes to the plant's overall vigor and resilience

This strong root development is partly inherited from the Moroccan landrace parent, which evolved in the rocky, mountainous terrain of the Rif Mountains where deep roots are essential for survival.

### Height and Growth Habit

- **Typical outdoor height**: 12 to 13 feet (3.7–4.0 meters)
- **Growth habit**: Tall, leggy sativa structure
- **Vegetative period**: 8 weeks recommended before flipping to flowering
- **Planting time**: Late May for September harvest

The plant's height makes it one of the taller sativa-dominant varieties available. Growers should plan accordingly, ensuring adequate spacing between plants and considering wind exposure in their site selection.

### Flowering and Harvest

- **Clone [[mollison-designers-day-length-and-flowering-response]]**: 2–3 weeks when placed under 24 hours of light
- **Recommended vegetative period**: 8 weeks
- **Planting timeline**: Late May planting for mid-September harvest
- **Expected yield**: Up to 600 grams per plant

The mid-September harvest time makes Polm Gold viable for many European climates, though the 8-week vegetative period means plants need to be started early. The yield of up to 600 grams per plant represents a substantial harvest from a single specimen.

### Cultivation Difficulty

Polm Gold requires more than a little experience to cultivate properly. The challenges include:

- Managing the extreme height of the plant
- Providing adequate support for tall, top-heavy colas
- Ensuring sufficient nutrition for such a large plant
- Protecting against wind damage at height

Experienced sativa growers will find the challenges rewarding, but new growers may find themselves overwhelmed by the plant's vigor and size.

### Climate Adaptation

Despite its Moroccan heritage, Polm Gold has been adapted through selective breeding to handle:

- Extreme temperature drops
- Heavy downpours and rainfall
- Variable European weather patterns
- Rocky and less-than-ideal soil conditions

The combination of Viking and Early Pearl genetics with the Moroccan landrace has created a plant that bridges the gap between tropical sativa preferences and temperate European growing realities.

## Aroma, Flavor, and Effects

### Appearance

Finished Polm Gold buds display the typical looser structure of sativa plants but with impressive resin coverage. The flowers are dense enough to be visually appealing while maintaining the airy quality characteristic of their sativa heritage.

### Aroma

The aroma of Polm Gold presents an unexpected combination. The scent carries prominent blueberry notes, which is unusual for a strain with Moroccan landrace parentage. The blueberry aroma likely comes from the Viking or Early Pearl lineage, which may carry indica-influenced terpene genetics.

### Flavor Profile

The taste experience is notably sweet — described as sweeter than apple pie. On the exhale, hints of earth and citrus fruit emerge, creating a complex and layered flavor profile. The combination of sweet blueberry upfront with earthy, citrusy undertones makes this one of the more uniquely flavored sativa-dominant strains.

The terpene profile likely includes:

- **Myrcene**: Earthy, herbal base notes
- **Limonene**: Citrus exhale characteristics
- **Pinene**: Possible contribution to the earthy undertones
- **Unknown blueberry terpenes**: Contributing the sweet berry aromatics

### Effects

The high from Polm Gold is described as exceptionally strong, combining two seemingly contradictory qualities:

1. **Trippy cerebral effects**: The sativa heritage delivers a potent, mind-altering experience characteristic of Moroccan landrace varieties
2. **Stoney body effects**: Unexpected physical sedation that grounds the cerebral intensity

This combination of trippy and stoney effects creates a balanced but powerful experience. The dual nature of the high reflects the three-way genetic heritage of the strain, with each parent contributing different aspects to the overall effect profile.

## Cultural Significance

### Moroccan Hashish Heritage

The name "Polm" directly connects this strain to Morocco's hashish tradition. "Polm" (also spelled "pollen" in European markets) is the term used for the fine, resinous powder collected from cannabis plants through sieving, which is then pressed into hashish. Morocco's Rif Mountains, where Chefchaouen is located, have been a center of hashish production for centuries.

By incorporating Moroccan Chefchaouen genetics, Polm Gold carries forward the genetic legacy of one of the world's most important cannabis-producing regions. The strain represents a bridge between traditional Moroccan landrace cultivation and modern European [[cannabis-breeding-techniques-acclimatization-trait-fixing-clarke]].
