### Spore Prints and Spore Syringes

Spore prints are created by pressing the gills of a mature mushroom onto
paper, capturing the spore pattern. Spore syringes are created by
transferring spores from a print into a syringe under sterile conditions.
The spores are then used to inoculate a substrate of nutrients free of
bacteria and molds. Just as a house plant is grown in soil, mushrooms can
be grown on a cake of substrate material. Spore prints are available for a
wide variety of magic mushroom species.
