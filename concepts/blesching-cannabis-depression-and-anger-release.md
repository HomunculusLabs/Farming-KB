## Strain-Specific Considerations

Sativa-dominant strains are preferred for depression due to their stimulating,
energizing, and uplifting potential. These strains have higher THC:CBD ratios.
Whole-spectrum cannabis with 70+ cannabinoids may work in concert through CB1
and CB2 activation plus whole-plant synergies.

- **THC** — binds CB1 and CB2 relatively equally
- **CBG** — binds both CB1 and CB2
- **CBD** — greater affinity for CB2
- **CBN** — higher affinity for CB2
- **CBC** — may not involve CB1/CB2
- **HU210** — higher affinity for CB1

## Mind-Body Medicine and Depression

The emotional realities underlying depression are critical. Depressed
individuals feel weighed down by layers of unexpressed hurt and anger,
resulting in low energy and lack of interest. The prospect of expending
energy for emotional processing can itself feel depressing, creating a
self-reinforcing cycle. Healing requires addressing and feeling the layers
of emotions that depress the body, mind, and spirit.

## The Emotional Architecture of Anger

Anger is often judged due to its association with violent expression.
However, discerning the complexities of anger reveals key distinctions:

- **Authentic anger** — felt and released. Communicates and focuses
  constructively on correcting an action.
- **Performed anger** — harbored and stored. Manipulates and destructively
  shames a person.

Few role models demonstrate how to express and release anger with intensity
while maintaining appropriate focus for positive impact. This deficit in
emotional literacy contributes to accumulated unprocessed anger that weighs
down the depressive individual.
