# Claviceps paspali Alkaloids

**Source:** The [[ololiuhqui-morning-glory-ergot-alkaloid-connection]].

The main constituents are:

- **[[bloomfield-ergot-claviceps-purpurea-ergotism]] (ergot of rye), which contains a mixture of water-soluble hallucinogenic alkaloids and water-insoluble medicinal alkaloids (ergotamine and ergotoxine groups), C. paspali contains only the hallucinogenic alkaloids. This means:

1. The entire sclerotium could potentially be used directly in powder form

2. No separation of soluble from insoluble alkaloids would be needed

3. There would be no risk of toxic non-hallucinogenic alkaloids contaminating the preparation

Hofmann wrote: "An easier method still would have been to have recourse to some kind of ergot like that growing on the grass Paspalum distichum, which contains only alkaloids that are hallucinogenic and which could even have been used directly in powder form."

## Mediterranean Distribution

"P. distichum grows everywhere around the Mediterranean basin." During the two millennia when the [[mckenna-discovery-and-impact-of-lsd]], differing primarily in potency:

- LSD: active at ~0.1 mg

- Lysergic acid amide: active at 1–2 mg

- Ergonovine: active at ~1.5–2 mg (confirmed by [[fukuoka-textdoc-barley-cultivation-three-stage-no-tillage-evolution]] and P. distichum growth.
