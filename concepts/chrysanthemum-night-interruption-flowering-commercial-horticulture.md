## Commercial Implementation

### Lighting Protocols

Standard chrysanthemum night interruption protocols include:

- **Duration**: 2-4 hours of light per night, or a series of brief cycles (e.g., 6 minutes on / 24 minutes off repeated through the night)
- **Intensity**: As low as 10-20 lux (approximately 2-4 foot-candles) at plant canopy level is sufficient
- **Spectrum**: Red-dominant light is most efficient; incandescent bulbs (which emit far-red) are less effective than fluorescent or LED fixtures providing pure red wavelengths
- **Timing**: Light is typically applied from 22:00 to 02:00 (midnight to 2 AM), centred in the dark period for maximum effectiveness

### Energy-Efficient Cyclic Lighting

Rather than providing continuous light for several hours, many commercial operations use cyclic lighting (short light pulses repeated throughout the night). Research has shown that cyclic protocols can reduce energy consumption by 70-80% compared to continuous night-interruption lighting while maintaining full flowering control. The minimum effective cycle varies by cultivar but typically requires at least 2-4 minutes of light per 30-minute period.
