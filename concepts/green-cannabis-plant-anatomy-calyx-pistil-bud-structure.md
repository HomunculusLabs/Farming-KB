# [[greg-green]]'s "The Cannabis Grow Bible" provides detailed descriptions of
each plant part and its role in growth and reproduction. The cannabis
plant can be divided into six main sections: bud, stem, branches, nodes,
leaves, and main cola. Beyond these broad categories, the flower
structures -- calyx, pistil, and resin glands -- are the most important
anatomical features for growers to understand.

## The Six Main Plant Sections

### Bud

The bud is the prime smokable part of the cannabis plant and contains the
highest concentration of THC and other cannabinoids. Buds develop at the
nodes and at the top of the plant during the [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-plant-training-pruning-and-yield]].

### Nodes

Nodes are the junction points where branches meet the stem. They are
critically important because this is where pre-flowers first appear,
allowing growers to determine plant sex before committing to the
flowering phase.

### Leaves

Leaves are the lesser potent part of the plant next to the stem and
roots. Fan leaves are large and serve primarily to gather light for
photosynthesis, producing sugars used in bud production. Smaller sugar
leaves are found within the buds and are often coated in trichomes.

### Main Cola

The main cola (also spelled kola) is the large central bud cluster at
the very top of the plant. It typically receives the most light and
produces the largest single mass of bud. See
[[green-cannabis-grow-bible-hash-making-and-trichome-extraction]] processes.
