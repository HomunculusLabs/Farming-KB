- **Blueberries**: Nectar

- **Cape lilac** (Melia azedarach): Valuable bee forage
- **Castor** (Ricinus communis): Valued bee forage
- **Chilean nut** (Gevuina avellana): Nectar and pollen
- **[[garden-plant-groupings]] for detailed [[comparison-companion-planting-reference-vs-jadam-monoculture-critique]] lists.

Key insectary companions:
- **Marigolds**: Repel nematodes, attract pollinators
- **Nasturtiums**: Repel aphids, attract predatory insects
- **Garlic**: Repels codling moth, Japanese beetles
- **Rosemary**: Repels cabbage moth, bean beetles, carrot fly
- **Thyme**: Repels cabbage worm
- **Borage**: Attracts pollinators and predatory wasps
- **Dill**: Attracts parasitic wasps, swallowtail butterflies
- **Fennel**: Attracts beneficial insects (but can inhibit some crops)
