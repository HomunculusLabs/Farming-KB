# Settlement Types and Village Design

> Source: [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-aquaculture-and-water-systems]] [[bill-mollison-permaculture-a-designers-manual-permaculture-design-principles]] from the start.

### Type 7: Specialised Coastal or Wilderness Settlements
Near wild areas or coasts. Needs careful environmental integration.

## Site Criteria for Village Development

Essential criteria:
- Must be able to catch, store, reticulate, and clean up its own water supply
- Wood, wind, solar, or high-pressure water available for energy production
- Clear ideas on clean energy development and maintenance
- Road, rail, boat, and not-too-distant air access for trade and travel
- Computer and telecommunications for data network
- Mixed ecologies: forest, aquatic, marine, agricultural, and market areas give many more options than a village in a simple ecology

## Village Development Financing

### Funding Sources
- Gift of land to a land trust intended for village development
- Option to buy suitable land, dependent on pre-sale to buyers
- Work on tribal or trust lands communally owned
- Investor or finance group partnership for staged development
- Convene a group wanting such a village and use their capital

### Title Distribution (Example: 100 Titles)
**Development Stage:**
- 60% sold to village group at best prices (30% covers all costs)
- 30% given to village group for later use
- 10% allotted as labour equity to surveyors, earthmovers, landscapers
