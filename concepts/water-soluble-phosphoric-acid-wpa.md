# Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-PA)

WS-PA is a [[complete-knf-input-reference]] extracted from sesame stem charcoal. Phosphoric acid is a component of every plant cell nucleus and is critical for root growth, flower formation, and energy transfer (ATP). WS-PA provides immediately bioavailable phosphorus during the changeover period when plants shift from vegetative to [[knf-reproductive-growth-stage]].

## Why Phosphorus?

- Composes the cell nucleus — present in DNA/RNA (phosphate backbone)
- Moves to vigorous buds, root tips, and seed fruit to increase cell production
- Involved in photophosphorylation and electron transport in photosynthesis
- Affects transport of anabolites and protein synthesis
- Phytic acid (storage form of phosphorus) is the major substance in seeds

## Deficiency Symptoms

- Symptoms appear on petioles and veins of old leaves first (P is mobile)
- New leaves stay small and turn dark green
- Flower growth noticeably suppressed, fewer fruits
- Symptoms often dormant internally — hard to recover once visible
- Fruit skin becomes thick, acid content increases

## Recipe

### Materials
- Sesame stems (dried)
- Water
- Clay jar or Japanese cedar bucket
- Hemp cloth bag
- Porous paper, rubber band

### Process
1. **Char the sesame stems**: Burn sesame stems and extinguish when large flames appear (not full ash — charcoal state)
2. **Extract**: Put 1-2 kg of sesame stem charcoal in a hemp cloth bag
3. **Soak**: Submerge bag in 100L of water
4. **Wait**: About 7 days (varies with temperature) — this produces crude liquid of natural phosphoric acid
5. **Strain**: Remove the bag; the liquid is WS-PA

### Environmental Conditions
- Optimum temperature: 23-25C
- Cool, shaded area with no direct sunlight
- Minimal environmental fluctuation

## Application Rates

| Use Case | Dilution | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| WS-PA alone | 0.7L crude in 20L water | Strong dose for cross-over |
| With WS-Ca | 1:1000 | Standard mixed application |
| Cross-over period | Either ratio above | Primary use window |

## When to Apply

- **Cross-over period**: Promotes flower bud differentiation, high fertility and yield
- **Dark green leaves**: Indicates possible phosphorus imbalance
- **Light green leaves**: Phosphorus may be deficient
- **Pre-harvest**: Improves sugar content of fruits

## WS-PA vs WS-CaPo

| Input | Source | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| WS-PA | Sesame stem charcoal + water | Pure phosphorus, fast extraction |
| WS-CaPo | Animal bones + BRV | Calcium + phosphorus, slower extraction |

Use WS-PA when you need phosphorus without additional calcium. See [[water-soluble-calcium-phosphate-wcp]] for the [[bionutrients-lab-calcium-phosphate-calamansi]] version.
