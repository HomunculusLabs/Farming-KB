## CookieWreck Strain Profile

## Overview

CookieWreck is a sativa-dominant hybrid bred by CannaVenture Seeds, crossing the legendary Arcata Trainwreck BX II with [[query-how-do-i-maximize-terpene-and-resin-production-in-cannabis]] that the Cookies family is known for. CookieWreck expresses two distinct phenotypes: a high-yielding Trainwreck-dominant pheno and a visually striking Girl Scout Cookies pheno with richer aromatics and colors.

## Lineage and Genetics

- **Breeder**: CannaVenture Seeds, USA
- **Genetics**: Girl Scout Cookies × Arcata Trainwreck BX II
- **Sativa/Indica Ratio**: Sativa-dominant
- **THC Content**: 18.97%
- **CBD Content**: 1.91%

### Parent Lineages

**Arcata Trainwreck BX II**:
- A backcrossed version of the famous Arcata Trainwreck clone
- The BX II designation indicates a second backcross to the original Arcata mother
- Preserves and intensifies the core Trainwreck traits: potency, energy, distinctive aroma
- Arcata Trainwreck is one of the most storied cuts from Northern California's cannabis history

**Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)**:
- The legendary cross of OG Kush × Durban Poison that defined a generation of West Coast genetics
- Known for extraordinary resin production, complex sweet/earthy flavor, and dense bud structure
- One of the most widely used breeding stock strains in modern cannabis

The combination brings together two of the most influential West Coast genetic lines of the past two decades.

## Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

- Tall and thin growth pattern typical of sativa-dominant hybrids
- Works equally well indoors and outdoors
- Stretch during [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]]
- Retains stronger Trainwreck structural characteristics
- Better choice for growers prioritizing production volume

**Girl Scout Cookies Pheno**:
- More visually interesting coloration (purples possible)
- More fragrant and aromatic
- Slightly lower yield but superior bag appeal
- Richer, more complex flavor profile
