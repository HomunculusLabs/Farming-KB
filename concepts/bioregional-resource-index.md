# Bioregional Resource Index

## Overview

The Bioregional Resource Index is a comprehensive catalogue system developed for mapping and organising all practical resources within a bioregion. Originally compiled by Maxine Cole and [[bill-mollison]] for the Northern Rivers Bioregional Association of New South Wales, Australia, it provides a structured framework for identifying every resource --- people, skills, machinery, services, and biological products --- essential to the functioning of a small region.

## Criteria for Inclusion

Resources must assist in:
- Conservation of resources
- Regional cash flow
- Survival of settlement
- Employment
- Community security

Security in this context means a cooperative neighbourhood and ample, sustainable resources for people. The index is best suited to computer retrieval and can be compiled topic by topic, sold initially by the page, and finally assembled as a loose-leaf notebook with volunteers entering local resource centres and addresses under each category.

## Category A: Food and Food Support Systems

**Criteria**: Native and economic species, organic and biocide-free, products of good nutritional value.

### A1. Plant Resources
- Nurseries and propagation centres, tissue culture, sources of inoculants, [[mycorrhiza]]
- Plant collections and botanical gardens, economic plant assemblies, aquatics
- Research institutes, horticultural and pastoral agencies
- Seed sources and seed exchanges
- Native species reserves and nurseries
- Demonstration farms and gardens, teaching centres, workshop conveners
- Government departments and their resources and regulations
- Voluntary agencies involved in plant protection, planting, and propagation
- Skilled people: botanists, horticulturists
- Publications, information leaflets, reference books, libraries, posters
- Contractors and consultancy groups for implementation of plant systems and farm designs
- Produce: products and producers in the region, growers
- Checklist of vegetables, fruits, and nuts that can be grown in the region

### A2. Animal Resources
- Breeders and stud or propagation centres, artificial insemination, hatcheries
- Species collections including worms and invertebrates
- Fish breeders and aquatic species
- Useful native species collections and reserves with cultivation potential
- Demonstration farms: free range, bee culture, teaching centres
- Government departments, regulations
- Voluntary agencies and animal protection societies
- Skilled people: farriers, vets, natural historians
- Contractors (shearers, etc.) and consultancy groups, farm designers
- Publications, posters, libraries for the region
- Produce: species and suppliers in region

### A3. Integrated Pest Management
- Insectaries and invertebrate predator breeders, suppliers of biological controls
- Suppliers of safe control chemicals and traps
- [[emcdda-hallucinogenic-mushroom-internet-information-sources]] on IPM
- Pest management of stored grains and foods
- References and libraries
- Checklist of common pests and predators, safe pest control procedures

### A4. Food Processing
- Suppliers of processing equipment
- Food Processing Centres (FPCs)
- Information sources on food processing and preservation
- Sources of yeasts, bacterial and algal ferment materials
- Processed-product producers in region

### A5. Markets and Outlets
- Local markets
- Delivery services
- Export markets and wholesalers
- Urban-rural co-op systems, direct marketing
- Retail outlets
- Market advisory skills and groups, contract and legal skills
- Roadside and self-pick sales
- Market packaging and package suppliers, ethical packaging systems
- Annual barter fair

### A6. Support Functions
- Residue testing services for biocides, nutrient, mineral, and vitamin content (food [[blesching-cannabis-contaminants-testing-quality-control]])
- Soil, water, and leaf analysis services for micronutrients and soil additives, water analyses, pH levels
- Hydrological and water supply services (dams, domestic water), design and implementation
- Fence and trellis suppliers and services, cattle grids and gates
- Suppliers of natural fertilisers, mulch materials, trace elements, soil amendments
- Farm machinery, garden and domestic tool suppliers, fabricators and designers, repair services, hire and contract services
- Land planning services
- Glasshouse, shadehouse, food dryers, suppliers, and appropriate materials
- Lime quarries and sources, stone dusts, local trace [[mineral-sources-for-soils]], regional geological resources

## Category B: Shelter and Buildings

**Criteria**: Energy-efficient house design and non-toxic materials only.

### B1. Construction Materials
- Timber growers and suppliers, community timber plantations
- Stone and gravel, earth materials
- Plumbing and piping, drainage, roofing
- Bricks and concrete products (tanks, blocks, etc.)
- Tiles and surfaces, paints (non-toxic)
- Furniture and fittings
- Tools and fasteners, tool sharpening services and repairs, glues and tapes
- Library and research resources
- Current state of housing in the region (numbers seeking housing, rentals available)
- Sources of toxins and unsafe materials in buildings, appliances, furnishings, paints, and glues

### B2. Energy Systems
- Home appliances for [[mollison-designers-home-energy-conservation-and-solar-heating]] efficiency, energy saving and insulation
- Hot water systems, solar systems
- Space heating and house design for the region
- Power generation systems for region: current and proposed
- [[appropriate-technology]] groups, research centres and demonstrations
- Designers of low-energy home systems and buildings
- Sources of information, publications, trade literature, library resources
- Reliable contractors and builders

### B3. Wastes and Recycling
- Sewage and greywater disposal (domestic)
- Compost systems and organics
- Solid wastes disposal and collection (boxes, bottles, plastics)
- Occupations based on waste recycling

## Category C: Livelihoods and Support Services

**Criteria**: Concept of right livelihood or socially useful work. Durable and well-made items.

### C1. Community Finance and Recycling
- Barter and exchange
- Small business loans
- [[mollison-local-currency-systems-and-community-banking]] and investment systems
- Land access systems, commonworks, leases, trusts
- Legal and information services

### C2. Livelihood Support Services
- Small business service centres
- Skills resource bank: business, legal and financial advisory services, volunteers and retired people
- Self-employment (work from fulfilling regional needs, job vacancy lists)
- Training courses in region

### C3. Manufacturing Services and Skills
- Clothing and cloth (spinning, weaving)
- Footwear and accessories, leatherwork
- Basketry and weaving, mats and screens
- Functional pottery
- Steelwork, fitting and turning, smithing and casting, welding
- Functional woodwork
- Engines and engine repairs
- Functional glasswork
