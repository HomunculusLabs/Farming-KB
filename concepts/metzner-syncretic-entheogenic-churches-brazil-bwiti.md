## Brazilian Ayahuasca Churches

Brazil has produced no fewer than three major organized churches in which
ayahuasca serves as the principal sacrament: Santo Daime, Uniao do
Vegetal (UDV), and Barquinha.

Each was founded in the 1950s by rubber tappers working in the Amazon who
came into contact with the hallucinogenic vine through mestizo or Indian
ayahuasceros.

The founders of each reported a significant vision instructing them to
organize a church using the brew as sacrament.

The UDV, the largest, follows the most formal structure: participants sit
in rows listening to sermons and songs from maestres, with a question-
and-answer period. Santo Daime involves congregational hymn-singing led
by women singers, sometimes with dancing in simple rhythmic steps.
