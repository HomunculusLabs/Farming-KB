Trial and error with small test batches is recommended when working with a

## Jar Preparation

After filling jars with hydrated seed:
1. Place lids on the jars but do **not** screw them tight — lids must remain
   loose to allow pressure equalization during sterilization
2. Cover with a double layer of crumpled aluminum foil over the lid and upper
   portion of the jar
3. The foil prevents water from entering the jar during pressure cooking

## Sterilization

Grass seed jars must be sterilized in a **pressure cooker or autoclave** — a
normal boiling pot will not reach sufficient temperature to kill all
contaminants. Standard sterilization is one hour at 15 PSI. Larger jars
(1.5 litre) and spawn bags require a full two hours of sterilization.

After the pressure cooker has depressurized, jars should be removed and shaken
to redistribute wet and dry kernels, creating a more uniform substrate. Always
inspect jars for cracks before shaking. Lids should remain loose during cooling
to prevent vacuum formation, which can suck contaminants into the jar.
