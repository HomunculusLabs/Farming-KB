### Growing Technique
- Seeds sown February 15 in plant-starting greenhouse
- Transplants grown on the floor in 30-inch-wide seedbeds (not soil blocks)
- Deep planting by dibbling transplants into holes (rather than hilling up soil)
- Allows intensive planting: three rows on a 30-inch bed, 4 inches apart in row
- Longer blanched shanks result from deep planting
- Six-row seed drill used for direct sowing in seedbeds

## Turnips

- 'Hakurei' is the primary variety — salad-quality baby turnips
- Available all winter in cool houses
- Plantings sown from mid-season onward hold well
- Crisp and mild in cold houses

## Beets

- Varieties: 'Red Ace', 'Merlin', 'Touchstone Gold'
- Grown for both roots and leaves
- 'Bull's Blood' grown primarily for leaves (deep red color, high in anthocyanins)
- Beet leaves from the winter greenhouse show much deeper red color than summer-harvested

## Radishes

- Varieties: 'Tinto', 'Cherriette', 'D'Avignon'
- Exceptionally mild and crisp in cold houses in fall and spring
- Will not grow in winter once continuous freezing occurs
- Strong demand at Thanksgiving and holiday dinners
- Succession-planted; sold into December from plantings as late as September 30
- Grown all winter in the cool greenhouse

## Onions and Scallions

- Winter scallions are extremely cold-hardy — can be harvested frozen and recover when thawed
- Overwintered onions ('Walla Walla Sweet', 'Olympic'): seed sown outdoors late August, covered with mobile greenhouse late November
- Five rows per bed; two intermediate rows sold as scallions in spring, three rows mature into bulbs five weeks ahead of spring-planted onions
- Vidalia-type short-day varieties grown in cool houses (set out first week January, harvested early May)

## In-Ground vs. Traditional Storage

Coleman's system favors in-ground storage because:
- Continuous freshness — crops are dug the same day they are sold
- Enhanced sweetness from cold soil exposure
- No infrastructure costs for root cellars or cold storage
- Green tops remain intact for premium presentation
- The "freshly harvested" identification allows premium pricing
