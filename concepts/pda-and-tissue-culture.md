# PDA and Tissue Culture

Potato Dextrose Agar (PDA) is the standard medium for mushroom tissue culture and spawn propagation. This page covers PDA preparation, tissue culture selection, and PDA-to-PDA transfer from the FAO training manual.

## PDA Medium Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Potatoes | 200 g |
| Dextrose | 20 g |
| Agar powder | 20 g |
| Water | 1 liter |

Buy dextrose and agar of commercial grade. Visually check potatoes for spots or rot before use.

## PDA Preparation Steps

1. **Prepare potatoes**: Wash and cut potatoes into 1-cm cubes (skin on or off)
2. **Cook**: Place potatoes in 1 liter of water. Simmer for 15-20 minutes
3. **Extract broth**: Remove potatoes, keep broth as clear as possible. Add water to broth to reach exactly 1 liter
4. **Add agar and dextrose**: Bring to stove. Add dextrose first, then agar slowly to prevent boiling over. Stir continuously at regular speed until completely dissolved
5. **Pour into bottles**: Fill small flat bottles (whiskey bottle size works) to 5-10 mm depth
6. **Plug**: Plug each bottle with cotton
7. **Sterilize**: Autoclave at 121°C for 20-30 minutes. Let cool to ~37°C
8. **Slant**: Place bottles at a slanted position to increase medium surface area. PDA should come close to the neck but must NOT touch the cotton plug
9. **Settle and check**: After PDA has set, transfer bottles to a clean shelf. Check for contamination daily (dark spots or lines = contaminated)
10. **Store**: Clean bottles with uncontaminated PDA are ready for tissue culture

## Tissue Culture Selection

### Materials Needed
- Special needle with insulated handle
- Alcohol lamp
- Alcohol (70%)
- Cotton/gauze
- Matches or lighter
- PDA bottles
- Laminar flow cabinet (or protected clean environment)
- UV lamp

### Mushroom Selection Criteria
Choose a strong, healthy mushroom for culture:
- Healthy appearance
- Not too mature, not too young
- Not too humid (wait 2-3 hours after watering)
- Stiff stalk
- Clean and far from any contaminated mushroom

### Tissue Culture Procedure

1. **Prepare clean room**: Clean room, all tools, inside and outside of laminar flow cabinet with alcohol. Transfer PDA bottles and tools into chamber
2. **Sterilize cabinet**: Turn on UV lamp and laminar flow for 10-15 minutes. Turn off UV but leave laminar flow running
3. **Prepare hands**: Clean both hands and bottles with alcohol, insert hands into cabinet
4. **Sterilize needle**: Hold needle with 2 fingers at 45° angle. Flame until needle turns red. Do not let it touch any surface after flaming
5. **Cool needle**: Wait 15-20 seconds (hold needle so it touches nothing, or place on clean glass surface)
6. **Open mushroom**: Using other fingers, tear mushroom lengthwise — DO NOT use a knife to cut (tearing exposes cleaner internal tissue)
7. **Excise tissue**: With cooled needle, cut a small piece (2mm x 2mm) of fleshy tissue from inside the mushroom (middle area between cap and stalk). Ensure it did not touch the outside
8. **Flame bottle mouth**: Flame around the mouth of the PDA bottle
9. **Open bottle**: Remove cotton plug in front of flame to prevent contamination
10. **Inoculate**: Insert needle and place tissue piece in the middle of the PDA surface. Bottom of bottle should always be lower than the mouth
11. **Close immediately**: Replace cotton plug near flame
12. **Label**: Record date, mushroom type, and mother spawn number

### Incubation
- Keep at room temperature
- Mycelium will take 10-15 days to fully cover the PDA surface (species dependent)
- Check for contamination daily
- After full growth, store in cool place or refrigerator vegetable compartment

## PDA-to-PDA Transfer

Because tissue culture has high contamination risk, do only a few bottles from fresh tissue. Then expand from those clean cultures to many bottles.

1. Follow steps 1-5 above (clean room, sterilize, prepare needle)
2. Cut a 5mm x 5mm square of mycelium from the source PDA (verify no contamination)
3. Flame the mouth of the new PDA bottle
4. Remove cotton plug near flame
5. Place mycelium piece in center of new PDA surface
6. Close immediately near flame
7. Label with date, mushroom type, and reference number
8. Incubate 10-15 days at room temperature
9. Store mature PDA cultures in refrigerator

## Contamination Management

- Expected loss rate: ~3% during spawn multiplication
- Separate contaminated bottles immediately
- Clean contaminated bottles thoroughly before reuse
- Keep detailed observation notes
- Never open a contaminated bottle inside the laminar flow cabinet
