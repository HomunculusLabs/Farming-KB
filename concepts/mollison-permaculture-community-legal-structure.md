# Mollison Permaculture Community Legal Structure

**Source:** Bill Mollison, Permaculture Design Course, Pamphlet XIII (1981)
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

Pamphlet XIII details the legal and financial structure used by the Tagari community in Australia. This model uses the same methods that integrate garden elements to integrate community, government, and business -- achieving surprisingly similar benefits for the whole social system. Maximum functional connections with minimal legal complexity. Works in Australia, applicable to America, Britain, and countries outside the Communist world.

## The Three-Entity Structure

### 1. The Community/Institute (Public Trust)

- A group of people agreeing to a set of principles and ethics: Earth care, people care, non-profiteering (not accumulating wealth beyond needs)
- Forms a publicly oriented company/trust working in health, education, and agriculture
- Usually immune from land taxes, rates, local government charges
- Cannot be raided by government (same protected status as schools, churches)
- **Advantages:**
  - Automatic member of World Free Seed Exchange (4,600 institutes, 680 botanical gardens)
  - Joint research with any similar institute worldwide
  - Tax deductible donations
  - No personal liability -- no one profits

### 2. The Trading Trust (Non-Profit Trading Corporation)

- A second trust, NOT connected with the Institute Trust
- Occupies structures loaned by the Institute at no cost
- Staffed free by community members
- Owns no buildings, employs no staff, has no chattels
- Borrows movable fixtures from a political party or external society
- **Appropriate businesses:**
  - Travel agencies (agents get 15% discount, free tickets)
  - Publishing companies (information is vital)
  - Consultancy (transmitting information globally)
  - Real estate operations
  - Seed companies (open-pollinated, self-reliance focus)

### 3. The Political Party

- Provides movable fixtures to the Trading Trust
- In Australia: need not declare income, membership, or pay traffic fines
- Not corporate = no income tax
- Receives minor flow equal to cost of typewriters, desks, vehicles, tractors

## Money Flow

### Government Aids
- Australian exporters automatically remitted 75%+ of travel fares
- Government fully refunds development of any salable invention
- No bureaucracy required -- automatic business aids

### Trading Trust Distribution (4 ways)
1. **Greatest amount** to the Research Institute (double tax exemption)
2. To public charity or ethnic groups (tax deduction)
3. Minor flow to political party (equals equipment costs)
4. Thin stream to workers ($21/week in Tagari -- all members equal regardless of age/sex)

### Land Accumulation
- No problem getting millions of dollars worth of land in 12 months
- Problem is time/cost of transferring it in ($4,000-$5,000 per property)
- Land comes from: public gifts, government, local government (unwanted land)
- Only fully owned, unencumbered property passes into the Institute
- Distributed as: limited tenancy, life tenancy (inheritable/transferable), or public land

## Seed Company Model

The Self-Reliance Seed Company (now Phoenix Seed Co.):
- Deals ONLY in open-pollinated seeds
- Only varieties from which people can save their own seed
- Growers collect seed using stamped envelopes, receive near-retail price
- Catalog lists plants by function: Bee Forages, Chicken Forages, Cattle Forages, Deer Forages, Fireproof Plants, by zone
- Every packet includes seed-saving instructions -- "selling self-reliance"
- Thompson and Morgan (world's largest seed company) operates from a small house with a few elderly women
- Free seed exchange in back of catalog; paid seed in front

## Key Principles

- "We give fishhooks, not fish" -- enablement, not money
- "We are the most heavily self-taxed group in Australia"
- All profit goes to the public; no individual power over others
- Community exists only if people like them; otherwise broke, dead, unloved
- Real estate gives measure of control over land without necessarily owning it
- Publishing: pre-order model = zero capital required
- Manufacturing: contract out components, assemble and distribute
