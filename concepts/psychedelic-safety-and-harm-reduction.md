## Psychedelic Safety and Harm Reduction

Throughout most of history, natural psychedelics have been considered gifts of the Gods, associated with healing of body, mind, and soul. Problems from psychedelic use are rare when proper precautions are observed. DM Turner's [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] is not compatible with split-second decisions required while driving
- Smaller doses produce similar but milder changes in consciousness
- Have a designated driver, take a cab, or stay put

### Medical Precautions
- Anyone with health problems or taking prescription/OTC medicines should be cautious
- Most psychedelics increase pulse rate and blood pressure
- Effects of using psychedelics with medical problems or prescription drugs is largely unknown
- Consult a knowledgeable doctor

## Mental Safety

Mental safety is individual and must be evaluated by each user. Each person's mind and capacity for handling psychedelics is different; psychedelics are not for everybody. Key principles:

### Set and Setting
- **Set**: Your mindset, mood, expectations, and mental state
- **Setting**: Physical environment, social context, and available support
- Both must be carefully prepared for a positive experience

### Optimal Setting
If choosing one ideal setting, Turner recommends the time-honoured methods: natural psychedelic (mushrooms or Peyote), outdoors in nature, away from city noise, with a group of respected friends or family. For novices, the session should be guided by trusted and experienced "elders."

### Recreational vs. Dedicated Use
The "recreational" use of smaller amounts in social settings (raves, concerts, parties) seldom provides a full spectrum of the psychedelic experience. To get the most from a psychedelic, one must put aside time completely devoted to the experience and take a dose that significantly alters perception.

### Difficult Experiences
- Anxiety about physical illness is a common mind-generated response during psychedelic transformation
- The Psychedelic Experience (Leary, Metzner, Alpert) describes techniques for navigating difficult periods
- Becoming aware of the body/mind relationship can help ease discomfort by releasing mental/emotional blocks
- "Bad trips" are often transformed into the most valuable experiences with proper navigation
- The main reason for negative experiences is lack of preparation, not anything inherent in the person or substance

### Reimprinting the Mind
Psychedelics can "reimprint the mind" — they dissolve identity and perceptual framework ("ego death"), then the user reassembles a new (hopefully improved) personality. This powerful process underscores why careful [[blesching-cannabis-safety-adverse-effects-evidence]] through natural psychedelics (mushrooms, [[trichocereus-mescaline-cacti-san-pedro-peruvian-torch-halpern]]) — users seem to "take it in stride and come back to center"
- People with schizophrenia or mental disorders beyond common neurosis should exercise extreme caution

### Integration
- Avoid returning to familiar routines immediately after a trip
- Extra recovery day helps retain insights from the journey
- Journaling and reflection amplify long-term benefits
