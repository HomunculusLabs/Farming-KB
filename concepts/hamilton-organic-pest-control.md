## The Natural Balance

In the organic garden with great diversity of planting, you attract the
complete spectrum of wildlife -- insects, birds, and small mammals --
that creates a natural balance. Hoverfly and ladybug larvae eat greenfly,
birds eat caterpillars, and no single pest species will ever dominate.
Hamilton stresses that it would be a foolish overreaction to think you
can do without pesticides completely, but you should only resort to the
few organic pesticides available when all else fails.

## Growing Healthy Plants

The very first rule of pest control is to adopt good cultivation
practices. Feeding the soil instead of the plant produces much stronger
growth that is less attractive to pests and better able to cope with an
attack. Always plant into fertile soil and ensure plants never go short
of water and food.
