# Cannabis Strain Selection and Genetics

**Source:** Marijuana Horticulture: The Indoor/Outdoor Medical Grower's Bible — Jorge Cervantes

## Taxonomic Classification

Technically and legally, all cannabis is classified as [[cannabis-indica]] (= C. sativa var. indica)
- [[cervantes-cannabis-ruderalis-autoflowering]] (= C. sativa var. spondanea)
- Cannabis afghanica (= C. sativa var. afghanica)

Each has distinct growth patterns, appearance, smell, taste, and effects.

## Cannabis Sativa

**Origin:** Asia, the Americas, and Africa

**General traits:**
- Tall, leggy stature with spacious internodal length
- Large sprawling root system
- Large narrow-bladed leaves
- Somewhat sparse flowers when grown indoors
- Blooms several weeks to months later than indica strains
- Outdoors can grow to 15+ feet; indoors often too tall too fast (up to 10 feet in 3 months)
- THC to CBD ratio produces a soaring, energetic, "speedy" high

**Regional varieties:**
- Mexican, Columbian, Thai, Jamaican — can be very potent
- Central African (e.g., 'Congolese') — tall, leggy, loosely packed buds
- [[kanna-sceletium-south-african-narcotic]] — 'Durban Poison' is the best-known strain
- Asian (Thai, Vietnamese, Laotian, Cambodian, Nepalese) — super THC-potent but difficult indoors, slowest to mature. Thai strains more prone to hermaphroditism
- Hemp strains — grown for fiber, very low THC

## Cannabis Indica

**Origin:** Pakistan and India

**General traits:**
- Prized by indoor growers and breeders
- Squat, bushy growth
- Condensed root system
- Stout stems
- Broad leaves
- Dense, THC-laden, fat heavy flowers
- Very dark green foliage; some strains have reddish to purple leaves
- Higher CBD to THC ratio — heavy, incapacitating "sit-on-your-head" stone
- Distinctive skunk or sweet/exotic odor
- Heavily resin-laden plants tend to be most pest and fungus resistant
- Few indicas with heavy, dense buds are resistant to gray (bud) mold

## Cannabis Ruderalis

**Origin:** Central Europe (brought to Amsterdam in early 1980s); also grows from Minnesota north through Manitoba and Saskatchewan, Canada

**General traits:**
- Short, weedy, scrubby plant
- Very low THC content — "ditch weed"
- Starts [[cannabis-sativa-flowering-cycle-management]] after a few weeks of growth (NOT photoperiod-dependent)
- Auto-flowering characteristic has been bred into other strains
- 'Lowryder' is one of the few C. ruderalis crosses that is auto-flowering and THC-potent

## Cannabis Afghanica

**Origin:** Near present-day Afghanistan

**General traits:**
- Quite short, seldom reaching six feet
- Distinctive broad, dark-green leaflets and leaves
- Dense branching and short internodes
- Long leaf stems (petioles)
- Cultivated exclusively for drugs; much resin made into hashish
- High cannabinoid content
- Often lumped with C. indica by growers and breeders

## Hybrid Strains

Most popular strains are combinations of two or more: C. sativa, C. indica, C. ruderalis, and C. afghanica. Many are bred specifically for [[comparison-cannabis-indoor-growing-basics-vs-cannabis-outdoor-growing]], greenhouse cultivation, or specific outdoor climates.
