# Beneficial Indigenous Microorganisms (BIM)

Gil Carandang's Philippine approach to indigenous microorganisms. Combines multiple microbial sources into a single blended serum, simpler and faster than Cho's 4-stage IMO process. See [[bionutrients-guide]] for the full bionutrient preparation system that pairs with BIM.

## Composition

50% lactic acid bacteria + 50% other microorganisms (1 part forest microbes, 1 part bamboo microbes, 1 part plant-specific microbes).

## Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB)

The major workhorse. See [[lactic-acid-bacteria-lab]] for full recipe. Key points:
- Collect from air using rice wash fermented 5-7 days at 20-25C
- Purify by adding 10 parts milk (skim/powdered okay, fresh best)
- Strain yellow serum after 5-7 days (carbohydrate/protein/fat float, discard)
- Store with equal amount crude sugar or molasses at room temperature
- Dilute 1:20 for basic concoction; 2-4 tablespoons per gallon for spray
- Never use refined sugar (chemically bleached, harms microbes)

## Forest Microbes (Carandang's Method)

Place cooked rice in a plastic container (wider surface area = better trapping), cover with metal netting for rat protection, bury in 2ft-1yd deep humus under a healthy old tree. In 2-10 days (temperature dependent), white and colored molds appear. Add 1/3 crude sugar/molasses, wait a week for sticky liquid. Add equal sugar for storage. Dilute 1:20 for use. Alternative: spread forest leaf mold/humus sparingly on top of cooked rice instead of burying.

## Bamboo Microbes

Bamboo roots exude sugary substances attracting powerful beneficial microbes. Bury cooked rice container in bamboo litter — same procedure as forest collection. Particularly useful for fermenting animal feeds.

## Plant-Specific Microbes

Invert cooked rice container over cut stems of desired crop. Microbes specific to that plant will be attracted to the rice.

## Rhizobium Nitrogen-Fixing Bacteria

Pull legume plants at flowering/fruiting stage, mix root-zone soil with equal ratio crude sugar. Rhizobia proliferate on sugar. Mix with next batch of legume seeds for inoculation.

## BIM vs. Cho's IMO — Key Differences

| Aspect | Carandang BIM | Cho's IMO |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Stages | Single blended serum | 4 sequential stages |
| Container | Plastic OK | Wood/bamboo boxes only |
| Sugar | Crude sugar or molasses | Brown sugar/jaggery |
| Fermentation | 1 week total | 3-5 weeks total |
| IMO-3/4 | Not used | Rice bran expansion + soil mixing |
| Use for animals | Pure LAB only | Not specified |
| Climate | Tropical-adapted (faster) | Temperate/tropical |
| Philosophy | Simplicity, self-reliance | Systematic, stage-specific |
