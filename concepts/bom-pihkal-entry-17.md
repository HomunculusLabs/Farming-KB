# BOM (beta-Methoxymescaline)

**[[2c-b-pihkal-entry-20]] #17**

## Chemical Identity

- **Full name:** beta-Methoxymescaline;
  3,4,5,beta-tetramethoxyphenethylamine
- **Class:** Phenethylamine (beta-methoxy derivative of mescaline;
  member of the BOX family)
- **Molecular formula:** C12H19NO4 (free base)
- **Hydrochloride salt:** white [[shirota-psilocybin-crystalline-product-catalytic-hydrogenolysis]],
  mp 198.5-199.5 °C
- **Nitroethane intermediate:** 1-methoxy-2-nitro-1-(3,4,5-
  trimethoxyphenyl)ethane, fine cream-colored crystals,
  mp 143-144 °C
- **Boiling point:** 120-150 °C at 0.3 mm/Hg (free base)

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage:** Greater than 200 mg
- **Duration:** Unknown (insufficient central activity to
  characterize)

## Qualitative Effects

Some indicators of central activity were noted at both 120 mg
and 180 mg, but nothing rated above a +1. The effects, while
present, were insufficient to characterize the compound as a
psychedelic at the doses tested.

If the relationship between mescaline and BOM follows the
pattern seen with the two active BOX members (BOD and BOB),
where the beta-methoxy compound is roughly equal in potency to
or up to approximately 2x the potency of the parent without the
methoxyl group, the active level for BOM might be in the 200-400
mg range. As of the time of writing, this remains undetermined.

## Synthesis Notes

### Step 1: Methoxy-nitroethane
beta-Nitro-3,4,5-trimethoxystyrene (9.0 g, from the "M" recipe)
was suspended in anhydrous MeOH (50 mL) and treated with sodium
methoxide solution (from 2.0 g Na in 50 mL MeOH). The bright
orange color faded to light cream as the nitrostyrene dissolved
over 3 minutes. Acetic acid (30 mL) was added, producing white
solids, followed by water (150 mL). The product was filtered,
washed, and recrystallized from boiling MeOH (150 mL) to give
6.9 g of 1-methoxy-2-nitro-1-(3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl)ethane.
TLC Rf was identical to the starting aldehyde.

### Step 2: Reduction
LAH (50 mL of 1 M solution in THF) was activated with H2SO4
(1.25 mL) at 0 °C. The nitroethane (6.0 g) was added as a solid
over 2 minutes. After 5 minutes stirring, the temperature was
raised to reflux. A gentle gas evolution was followed by a
vigorous exothermic reaction with copious gas evolution that
exceeded the condenser's capacity. After settling, reflux was
maintained for an additional 2 hours. The product (2.8 g) was
isolated as the hydrochloride salt.

The exothermic reaction during reduction is characteristic of the
BOX series (BOD, BOH, and BOM all show it), with BOB being the
notable exception.

## Safety and Pharmacology

BOM shows minimal central activity at doses up to 180 mg. No
specific safety concerns were noted at these levels, but the
compound remains incompletely characterized. The practical
question of whether administering 200-400 mg to reach threshold
is worthwhile remains open.
