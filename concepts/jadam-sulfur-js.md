
# JADAM Sulfur (JS)

JS is a natural germicide made by melting sulfur in caustic soda (NaOH) with water -- no external heating required. The exothermic reaction produces enough heat (~100C) to fully liquefy sulfur. Effective against [[powdery-mildew]], [[downy-mildew]], canker, black spot, pear rust, anthracnose, and most fungal diseases. Costs under $0.50/L. No expiration date. Unlike lime-sulfur, JS does not damage greenhouse plastic or steel pipes.

## Recipe: 100L (26 gal) Batch

**Ingredients**:
- Sulfur (99.9% pure): 25 kg (55 lb)
- Caustic soda (NaOH, 98%): 20 kg (44 lb) -- flakes preferred over powder
- Soft water: 82L total (50L initial + 32L added later; in summer >27C use 54L + 28L)
- Phyllite powder: 0.5 kg (1.1 lb) -- fine rock powder substitute OK
- Red clay powder: 0.5 kg (1.1 lb) -- fine rock powder substitute OK
- Sea salt: 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)

**Equipment**: 110L heat-resistant plastic container (NO aluminum), 1.2m wooden stick, gloves, goggles, mask, boots, jacket, 1-2L emergency water

### Step-by-Step Process (~20 min active, 24 hrs total)

1. Put sulfur, phyllite, red clay, and sea salt in container (sequence matters)
2. Add NaOH slowly (avoid dust)
3. Have 50L soft water ready in separate container
4. **Pour ALL water in at once** -- reaction begins immediately
5. Stir gently but thoroughly with wooden stick, especially the bottom
6. Temperature will exceed 80C and sulfur begins melting (~4 min)
7. Keep stirring for ~20 min while hot; check bottom for unmelted sulfur
8. **Watch for over-boiling**: if temperature too high or weather hot, it can boil over. Have 1-2L water ready to add if needed
9. Once all sulfur is melted (no granules on bottom), add second water (32L)
10. Stir well one final time
11. Close lid, let sit 24 hours

**After 24 hours**: Upper portion will be clear (25% sulfur content). Do NOT keep in making container -- upper/lower portions have different concentrations. Decant clear upper liquid into thick-walled storage bottles. Seal airtight. If crystals form on surface, the seal failed.

## Usage Rates (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

| Situation | JS Volume |
|-----------|----------|
| Greenhouse start | 0.5L (0.13 gal) |
| Open field start | 1L (0.26 gal) |
| Moderate disease | 1-1.5L |
| Serious disease | 1.5-2L (0.4-0.53 gal) |
| Winter dormant spray | 5L (1.3 gal) |
| Fog machine (17L tank) | 0.1L (0.026 gal) |

Increase in 0.2L increments only. Above 2.5L causes concentration damage (burned leaf edges). **Always use with JWA** -- without it, JS leaves marks and has reduced germicide effect.

## Disease Targets

- Powdery mildew and downy mildew (primary targets)
- Black spot, canker, pear rust, brown blotch
- Anthracnose, grey mold, green mold
- Rice blast, rice sheath blight, smut
- Leaf fungus, white rot, valsa canker
- Rice bakanae disease (seed soak: 5L JS in 500L cold water, 48 hrs)

## JS Germicide Recipe (JS + JWA)

**Mild fungal disease** (powdery/downy mildew):
- JWA 3L + JS 1.2L per 500L water

**Stronger diseases** (canker, black spot, pear rust):
- JWA 5L + JS 1.5L per 500L water

**Severe canker** (e.g., pepper):
- JWA 10L + JS 2L per 500L water; apply every 2 days, 2-3 total applications

**Flower thinning** (fruit trees, full bloom):
- JS 2L + JWA 3L per 500L -- risky, must test first

**Winter dormant spray** (fruit trees):
- JWA 10L + JS 5L + fine red clay powder 2kg per 500L

## Safety

- Produces VERY high heat -- more than JWA. Full safety gear mandatory
- Do NOT use aluminum containers or metal sticks
- Work in well-ventilated area
- Have emergency water ready for boil-over
- Never pour water gradually -- always all at once
- Do not reuse leftover spray containing JS (concentration stress)

## Sensitive Crops

Grapes, persimmons, walnuts, and perilla are sensitive to sulfur. Always do concentration test first. Start at lower doses.

## Troubleshooting

- **Sulfur won't melt**: Water was too little or weather too hot causing premature solidification. Once solidified, it will not re-melt
- **Black debris/oil film on top**: Remove particles and oil layer, use the clear liquid
- **White crystals in storage**: Seal failed (air contact). Decant clear portion
- **Concentration damage (burned leaf edges)**: Dose too high. Reduce JS by 0.2L increments
