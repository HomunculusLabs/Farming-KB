## Comparison with Traditional Evidence Hierarchies

Traditional evidence-based medicine (EBM) hierarchies typically rank evidence as: systematic reviews of RCTs > individual RCTs > cohort studies > case-control studies > case series > expert opinion > case reports. The CHI modifies this approach for cannabis-specific challenges:

1. **Blinding difficulty**: Whole-plant cannabis produces distinctive sensory effects (taste, smell, psychoactivity) that make true double-blinding difficult. The CHI therefore does not penalize open-label trials as heavily as traditional EBM hierarchies would.

2. **Schedule I barriers**: The legal classification of cannabis in many jurisdictions has restricted the number and scale of clinical trials. The CHI compensates by giving greater weight to preclinical evidence and patient-reported outcomes than traditional systems would.
