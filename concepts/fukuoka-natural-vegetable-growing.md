### Vegetable Disease and Pest Resistance

Fukuoka makes a crucial observation: "Judicious combinations of vegetables in a sensible mixed cropping scheme can be grown in fair yield, free of disease and pest damage, without resorting to pesticides."

This is possible because:

1. **Mixed cropping creates complexity:** When many vegetable species grow together, no single pest can build up damaging populations
2. **Natural enemies emerge:** Different vegetables attract different natural enemies at various times of year
3. **Healthy soil produces healthy plants:** Without fertilizer dependence, vegetables develop natural resistance
4. **Diverse ecology prevents outbreaks:** The complex web of interactions prevents any one species from dominating
