# Cannabis for Nausea and Appetite Stimulation

Cannabis has been recognized as an effective remedy to stimulate appetite and reduce nausea in almost every culture and medical tradition throughout history. Modern science has confirmed that the [[cannabis-endocannabinoid-system]] plays a central role in regulating hunger, eating behavior, and body weight.

## Evidence-Based Confidence Level

- **Total Studies Reviewed:** 11
- **Combined CHI Value:** 28

## Understanding Anorexia and Cachexia

**Anorexia nervosa** is a lack of interest in or refusal to eat sufficient food due to psychological reasons. **Cachexia** (wasting syndrome) refers to loss of body mass that cannot be replaced through nutrition, presenting as generalized weakness, malnutrition, and emaciation. Cachexia is usually a secondary condition in chronic diseases such as end-stage cancer, AIDS, multiple sclerosis, and COPD.

## The Science of Cannabis and Appetite

### CB1 Receptors and Eating Behavior

Endocannabinoid CB1 receptors are directly involved in eating behavior and body weight regulation. Key findings:

- **Madrid (2002):** Peripheral CB1 receptors play a complex role in regulating eating
- **New York (2005):** Reducing CB1 expression produces reduction in appetite, confirming CB1's role
- **University of Liverpool (2005):** Endocannabinoids acting at brain CB1 receptors "stimulate appetite and ingestive behaviours" with strong evidence for endocannabinoid involvement in "energy metabolism and fuel storage"

### Anandamide and THC

The endocannabinoid anandamide (AEA) and THC both stimulate appetite through CB1 receptor activation. Anandamide is produced naturally by the body, while THC from cannabis provides an exogenous source of CB1 stimulation.

## Clinical Evidence

### AIDS-Related Anorexia-Cachexia

**Tulsa, Oklahoma (1995):** 139 AIDS patients received Dronabinol or placebo:
- Increased appetite: 38% (Dronabinol) vs 8% (placebo)
- Improved mood: 10% vs -2%
- Decreased nausea: 20% vs 7%
- Body weight stable with Dronabinol vs 8.8 lb loss with placebo
- Side effects were "mostly mild to moderate (euphoria, dizziness, thinking abnormalities)"

**Orlando, Florida (2007):** 117 HIV/AIDS patients with appetite and weight loss received Dronabinol. The majority experienced quick appetite improvement and reduced weight loss.

### Elderly Weight Loss

**St. Louis, Missouri (2007):** 29 senior long-term care patients with weight loss received oral Dronabinol for 12 weeks. Average weight gain: almost 8 pounds.

### Hepatitis C

**Ottawa, Canada (2009):** 64% of patients undergoing interferon-ribavirin therapy experienced symptom improvements when also administered cannabinoids, demonstrating utility beyond AIDS and cancer.

### Cancer-Related Anorexia

**Cleveland, Ohio (1994):** 13 of 18 cancer patients reported increased appetite with THC 2.5mg orally three times daily. Researchers concluded "THC is an effective appetite stimulant in patients with advanced cancer."

**Toronto, Canada (2006):** Nabilone given to 82 cancer patients showed potential therapeutic benefit with improvement in pain, nausea, depression, anxiety, insomnia, and night sweats.

### Mixed Cancer Results

**Switzerland (2006):** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 164 cancer patients comparing cannabis extract, oral THC, and placebo showed no significant difference in appetite or quality of life, though no toxicity was reported. This highlights that individual responses vary.

## Strain-Specific Considerations

- Anorexia/cachexia patients often prefer **sativa strains** with higher THC relative to CBD and CBN
- Higher THC content stimulates CB1 receptors involved in appetite regulation
- Synthetic THC (Dronabinol/Marinol) is available by prescription
- Individual needs and preferences vary significantly

## Cannabinoids' Multi-Symptom Approach

Cannabis constituents address appetite stimulation through multiple mechanisms:
- Direct appetite stimulation via CB1 receptor activation
- Reduction of nausea and vomiting
- Gentle lifting of energy (body and mind)
- Reduction of depression and anxiety that may suppress appetite
- Improved sleep quality supporting overall recovery

## Key Considerations

While cancer anorexia studies show mixed results, AIDS-related and elderly-related appetite studies consistently demonstrate positive outcomes. The multi-symptom approach of cannabis (addressing nausea, mood, energy, and sleep alongside appetite) makes it particularly valuable for patients with complex, multi-faceted wasting conditions.

## Merged: Cannabis for Morning Sickness
An estimated 50% of all pregnant women develop some kind of morning sickness, usually starting in the middle of the first trimester (at about six weeks). Normally these episodes of nausea and vomiting are self-limiting and disappear toward the end of the first trimester (three months). The [[cannabis-endocannabinoid-system]] plays a crucial role in the earliest processes of conception and egg implantation. Levels of endogenous anandamide via [[cannabis-cannabinoid-receptor-pharmacology]] and CB2 play a deciding role in implanting the fertilized egg in the uterine wall. The cannabinoid [[cannabis-thc-therapeutic-profile]] binds to the same receptors and therefore may also play a significant role in early pregnancy processes.
