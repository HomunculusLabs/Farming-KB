# Cannabis Administration Methods

The therapeutic efficacy of cannabis depends not only on the choice of
cannabinoids and terpenes but critically on the method of administration.

- **Onset**: 15-45 minutes, faster than oral but slower than inhalation
- **Bioavailability**: 15-30%, higher than oral due to partial bypass of

  via the sublingual venous plexus
- **Duration**: 3-6 hours, intermediate between inhalation and oral
- **Advantages**: Faster onset than oral with longer duration than
  inhalation; better bioavailability than oral; easy dose titration by
  counting drops; no special equipment required; discrete
- **Considerations**: Requires holding liquid under the tongue for 60-90
  seconds for optimal absorption; swallowing before absorption negates
  the sublingual advantage; taste can be unpleasant with some preparations

## Topical Application

Topical cannabis preparations (creams, salves, balms, oils) are applied
directly to the skin for localized effects:

- **Onset**: 15-60 minutes for noticeable local effects
- **Bioavailability**: Primarily local; minimal systemic absorption (though
  transdermal patches can achieve systemic delivery)
- **Duration**: 2-6 hours for local effects; longer with transdermal patches
- **Advantages**: No psychoactive effects at typical doses; ideal for
  localized pain, inflammation, and [[blesching-cannabis-rheumatoid-arthritis]] pain
- **Considerations**: Limited to surface and near-surface tissue
  penetration; not effective for systemic conditions; efficacy depends on
  preparation quality and cannabinoid concentration

## Raw Cannabis Juice

Juicing raw, unheated cannabis provides a unique administration method
that delivers cannabinoids in their acidic (non-psychoactive) precursor
forms -- THCA and CBDA -- along with a concentrated source of terpenes
and other phytonutrients:

- **Onset**: 30-60 minutes
- **Bioavailability**: Similar to oral administration (4-20%)
- **Duration**: 4-6 hours
- **Advantages**: No psychoactivity (THCA does not activate CB1 receptors);
  delivers maximum anti-inflammatory benefits of acidic cannabinoids;
  provides concentrated nutrition including chlorophyll, vitamins, and
  minerals; high doses (multiple grams per day) are tolerable without
  intoxication
