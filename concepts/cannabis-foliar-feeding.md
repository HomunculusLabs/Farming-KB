# Cannabis Foliar Feeding

## Overview

Foliar feeding means spraying nutrients or bio-stimulants onto foliage to augment available nutrients, vitamins, and hormones. It is a quick-fix technique used when specific deficiency symptoms manifest. Foliar feeding should not be used as a primary nutrient delivery method -- it is a supplement only. Foliar feeding is a quick fix only and is easy to overdo. High levels of nutrients in the foliage stop the roots from taking in more; this is confusing for the plant.

### 6. Spray Concentration

- Never spray more than once every 10 days
- Keep spray concentration below 500 ppm or EC below 1.0
- Foliar spray concentration is cumulative; nutrients can build up in foliage
- High nutrient levels in foliage stop roots from taking in more nutrients
- Nutrients delivered via the foliage can cause a buildup of salts in and around the leaves, similar to the way salts accumulate in the soil
