## Holistic Management Decision Framework

The holistic management decision framework is the core decision-making
system in Allan Savory's HRM model. It consists of seventeen guidelines
organized into two categories: seven **testing guidelines** ("whether to")
that determine whether a tool should be used, and ten **management
guidelines** ("how to") that assist in using tools correctly. Together they
form a comprehensive filter through which all management decisions pass.

## The Two Categories

The testing guidelines help determine whether or not to use a given tool.
The management guidelines help apply the chosen tool in the right way. Some
people think of these as the "whether to" and "how to" guidelines. In
practice, the testing guidelines are used first: if a tool does not pass
these tests, you need not proceed with that tool but must consider and test
another.
