## Filial Breeding and Hybrid Generations in Cannabis

Filial breeding is a system where siblings of the same progeny lot and
generation are inter-mated to produce new generations. The progression
from F1 through subsequent filial generations is the most common method
for establishing pure or true-breeding populations when breeding
cross-pollinated species such as cannabis.

## The F1 Generation

The F1 (first filial) generation is obtained by crossing two unrelated,
true-breeding parent varieties. F1 hybrids are unique in that they are
uniform when grown from seed. Every F1 plant closely resembles every
other F1 plant from the same cross, providing predictable and consistent
results for the grower.

However, like all hybrids, F1 populations are genetically unstable. If
reproduced sexually by inbreeding within the F1 population, the
subsequent generation will be neither uniform nor similar to the F1
generation. This uniformity is lost when F1 plants are inter-bred.

## The F2 Generation

If two F1 siblings are bred, or the F1 population is allowed to open
pollinate, the resulting generation is labeled F2. The F2 generation
is where genetic segregation becomes visible. Traits that were uniform
in the F1 now show variation as recessive alleles recombine and express.

In the F2, plants that appeared identical as F1s now display a range
of phenotypes. This is where breeders discover the hidden genetic
diversity within a cross. It is also the generation where recessive
traits, masked by dominance in the F1, reappear in approximately 25
percent of the population.

## The Challenge of Selecting in the F2

The F2 generation presents the breeder with the widest range of
variation they will see from any single cross. Some plants may closely
resemble one grandparent, others the other grandparent, and some may
show entirely novel trait combinations not seen in either parent. This
is the generation where careful selection has the greatest impact.

Breeders should grow as many F2 plants as possible to maximize the
chance of finding exceptional individuals. Polygenic [[biodiversity-fungal-genetic-diversity]] in the process.
