# Cannabis Fire Ritual

The Feeding of the Flame is a fire celebration ritual from "[[marijuana-magick-cannabis-incense-and-preparation]]: A
Practitioners Guide" by Magus Sativus. This ceremony honors the element of
fire as a divine gift that sustains human life, drawing on the myth of
Prometheus while incorporating cannabis, grain alcohol, and incense into a
powerful outdoor ritual experience.

## Philosophy of Fire in Pagan Practice

Sativus frames fire as one of humanity's most important achievements, a force
"handed down to us from the gods by Prometheus." The Prometheus myth is
interpreted as fire representing knowledge itself: while fire is natural, the
ability to start and control it is a uniquely human accomplishment.

Fire is celebrated as a union between humans and nature, a reaction that
Mother Earth sustains. The ritual recognizes fire's dual nature: it is both a
force for good and a force capable of destruction, a duality the practitioner
is asked to meditate upon during the ceremony.

## Materials Required

- **An athame**: Ritual dagger for directing energy
- **5 sticks of incense**: For invoking the sacred smoke
- **A bottle of Everclear or other grain spirit**: Used as an offering to
  the fire
- **A bundle of wood and kindling**: For building the ritual fire
- **Salt**: Preferably from a large container, for drawing the circle
- **Cannabis**: In a pipe, to be lit from the ritual fire
- **A large open space**: Free from distractions, with a fire pit

## Preparing the Sacred Space

### Building the Fire

The practitioner places wood in the fire pit angled toward the sky with plenty
of kindling underneath. The salt is used to draw a large circle around the
fire pit. Unlike some other rituals in Sativus's guide, the star of the
pentagram is not drawn for this ceremony; only the circle is used.

### Opening Invocation

The practitioner addresses fire directly: "Fire, given from the gods to let us
live on this earth, in all places, we worship all places. Fire, you have
helped us to live, to eat, to stay warm. We thank you and the earth from
which you come. You allow us to smoke the joyous and healing herb. Thank you
element of the earth."

The fire is then started so that it burns only slightly, and the practitioner
says: "Man and womankind did not create you, o' flame, but we have learned to
sustain you and harness you. Please continue to help us thrive on this earth."

## Feeding the Flame with Grain Alcohol

As the fire grows slightly higher, the practitioner slowly pours a small
amount of grain alcohol onto it while chanting: "Fire, fire, of the earth, in
your flames we live rebirth." This chant continues for several minutes.

The grain alcohol offering represents a giving-back to the fire, a reciprocal
exchange between practitioner and element. The practitioner then pours
approximately one cup of grain alcohol slowly over the fire while repeating:
"I feed the fire of the earth, I ask you to give me rebirth." Safety is
emphasized throughout this process.

## Lighting the Cannabis with Sacred Fire

An incense stick is lit from the flame. Once the incense is burning, it is
used to light the cannabis in the pipe. The practitioner begins smoking, and
after each toke says: "From the fire we get the flame, we light the sacred
herb and begin again." This process continues until the cannabis in the pipe
is exhausted.
