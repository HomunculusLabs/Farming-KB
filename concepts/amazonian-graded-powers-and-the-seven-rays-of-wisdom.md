# Amazonian Graded Powers and the Seven Rays of Wisdom

Vision 49 of Pablo Amaringo's ayahuasca paintings reveals a cosmological framework of graded powers, organized as seven luminous rays ascending from the material to the spiritual. Each ray represents a level of esoteric knowledge accessible to the vegetalista, with specific beings, creatures, and qualities associated with each grade.

## The Seven Rays

### Ray 1: Humus (Light Green)

The lowest ray represents the earth itself — humus, the organic matter of soil. Here are found animals (quadrupeds and birds), plants, and the basic powers available to beginning vegetalistas. This is the foundation: the material world and its living inhabitants that the shaman first learns to work with.

This foundational ray corresponds to the everyday world of [[hallucinogens-and-shamanism-harner-anthology]] — the practical knowledge of plants and animals that every vegetalista must master before progressing to more esoteric domains.

### Ray 2: Topaz (Greenish-Yellow)

The second grade shows plants, reptiles, birds, unknown spectres, roses, and dark people. This represents one more grade of esoteric knowledge beyond the purely material. The appearance of spectres and unknown entities signals entry into the supernatural realm.

### Ray 3: Purple (Amethyst Realm)

The purple ray contains plants, animals, persons, sphinx-like beings, the black rose (which contains a great esoteric mystery), hypnotic animals, fortune tellers, sibyls, and persons endowed with a transcendental mind. The black rose represents hidden esoteric knowledge. This ray marks the transition from natural to supernatural mastery.

### Ray 4: Neutral Grey (Diamond)

This is the center that separates the visible from the invisible — the boundary between the material and spiritual worlds. Here are found animal persons (beings that are both animal and human). Many reach this grade but their journey ends here due to insufficient preparation in supranormal matters. The diamond represents the ability to test the knowledge one hides — a mirror that reveals truth.

### Ray 5: Turquoise Blue (Sapphire)

The sapphire ray shows angels or messengers that roam the vast universe, dwelling in different galaxies. They possess extrasensory wisdom and move with the speed of thought. They are the guardians appointed to the immense universe. This ray represents cosmic consciousness and intergalactic connection.

### Ray 6: Violet (Amethyst)

The violet ray contains sylphs, fairies, muses, kings, queens, princes, and princesses — all possessing great talent and wisdom. These are the spirit beings that populate the intermediate realms between earth and the highest heavens. They correspond to the spirit allies that master vegetalistas work with.

### Ray 7: Carbon and Silicon (The Dark Ray)

The final ray represents carbon and silicon — the fundamental elements of organic life and crystal technology. Here are found strange beings, sphinxes, animals, and witches. This ray is described as "the opposite of all the light and the enlightenment of the good." It represents the dark side of esoteric knowledge — the powers available to sorcerers and witches.

## The Graded Pulsation System

Complementing the seven rays is the system of pulsation levels, each represented by colored brain waves:

- **White and pink waves:** Apprentice pulsations
- **Blue waves:** Muraya (healer) pulsations — those who dedicate themselves mainly to curing
- **Red and yellow waves:** Banco (master of the three realms: atmospheric/sidereal, terrestrial, aquatic)
- **Black and red waves:** Evil bancos — witches and sorcerers who may cure but not often, usually doing harm
- **Pink layer:** Sumis — those able to enter the underwater realm
- **Red line with white waves:** Sumis who only heal

The pulsation system reflects the [[amazonian-vegetalista-shamanism-degrees-and-hierarchy]] of Amazonian shamanism, where different ranks of practitioners are associated with different types and levels of spiritual power. The colours of the pulsations correspond to visible spectrum phenomena, suggesting that the system maps altered states of consciousness onto a cosmological framework.

## The Three-Temple Initiation

In Vision 36, a three-tiered initiation structure appears:

1. **First temple:** Ordination on colored steps. A lilac horse with wings descends for admitted initiates.
2. **Second temple:** Deeper esoteric knowledge. A spaceship departs, symbolizing increased protection.
3. **Third temple:** Ordination as sublime masters with light-blue stairs. Two spaceships depart (one upward, one downward) — signifying full mastery of esoteric sciences.

The three-temple structure parallels initiation systems found in many spiritual traditions worldwide, from the [[eleusinian-mysteries-history-and-structure]] of ancient Greece to the degree systems of Masonic lodges. This cross-cultural pattern of graded initiation suggests a deep human impulse toward structured spiritual progression.

## The Graded Nature of Amazonian Shamanism

Amazonian shamanism is not monolithic but deeply stratified. The vegetalista hierarchy — from apprentice to banku (supreme master) — corresponds to progressively deeper access to the rays of wisdom:

- **Banku:** Highest level; master of all three realms (earth, water, sky). Swallows virotes to gain power. Can control weather, travel to other planets.
- **Muraya:** Specializes in underwater realm; can command yakurunas and water spirits. Powerful healer.
- **Sumiruna:** Can go underwater and to other dimensions. Master of transformation.
- **Banco:** Less specialized than banku; also a master healer.
- **Vegetalista:** The general practitioner of plant medicine.

## Symbolic Interpretation

The seven rays may correspond to:

- The seven chakras of Hindu/Buddhist cosmology
- The seven levels of consciousness
- The visible light spectrum (green -> yellow -> purple -> grey/white -> blue -> violet -> dark)
- The alchemical stages of transformation

The appearance of sphinxes, extraterrestrial beings, and beings from ancient civilizations (Egyptian, Mayan, Incan) in these visions suggests that Amazonian shamanism perceives all human spiritual traditions as interconnected through the same graded structure of consciousness.

## The Relationship to Plant Teachers

The graded power system is intimately connected to the [[amazonian-plant-teachers-doctores]] tradition. Each grade of esoteric knowledge is accessed through dieta — the disciplined practice of consuming specific plants under the guidance of a master vegetalista. The plants are not merely chemical agents but active participants in the educational process, each offering specific lessons and powers to the dedicated practitioner.
