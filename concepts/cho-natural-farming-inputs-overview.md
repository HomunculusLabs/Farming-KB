Dr. Cho Han Kyu's Natural Farming (NF) system uses a suite of
farm-made biological inputs to replace chemical fertilizers and
pesticides. All inputs are prepared from locally available natural
materials using fermentation processes driven by
[[cho-indigenous-microorganisms-collection-and-cultivation]] (IMOs). The system is built around the
[[knf-nutritive-cycle-theory]], which prescribes specific inputs at
specific growth stages.

## The Input Philosophy

In Natural Farming, we do not feed the plant -- we nurture the soil,
and the soil nurtures the plant through IMOs. This philosophy aligns with
[[fukuoka-natural-farming-method|Fukuoka's natural farming]] approach and
contrasts with conventional [[teaming-with-nutrients-organic-fertilizer-types-and-sources|fertilizer]]
regimes. The inputs fall into
several functional categories:

### Microbial Inputs
- [[cho-indigenous-microorganisms-collection-and-cultivation]]: The foundational input. Collected locally
  and cultured through four stages (IMO-1 through IMO-4)
- [[lactic-acid-bacteria-knf]]:
  Anaerobic microbes that improve soil ventilation and fruit quality
- Yeast: Completes the fermentation chain, producing vitamins and
  hormones

### Nutrient Inputs
- [[master-cho-knf-fpj-fermented-plant-juice-guide]]: Enzyme-rich extract from fast-growing plants; provides
  growth hormones
- [[master-cho-knf-ffj-fermented-fruit-juice-guide]]: Similar
  to FPJ but from fruits; provides activation enzymes
- [[cho-fish-amino-acid-preparation]]: Nitrogen-
  rich liquid from fish waste
- [[knf-water-soluble-potassium-detailed]]: Extracted from tobacco stems
- [[knf-water-soluble-phosphoric-acid-wspa]]: Extracted from sesame stem charcoal
- [[water-soluble-calcium-wca]]: Eggshells
  dissolved in [[knf-brown-rice-vinegar]]
- [[knf-water-soluble-calcium-phosphate]]: Animal bones dissolved in brown rice vinegar

### Plant Protection Inputs
- [[knf-ohn-oriental-herbal-nutrient-recipe]]:
  Made from garlic, ginger, and cinnamon; sterilizes and strengthens
  plants
- [[knf-brown-rice-vinegar]]: Sterilizes,
  enhances calcium absorption, promotes wax layer formation
- [[knf-loess-powder]]: Contaminated soil containing trace
  elements; used for disease prevention
- [[knf-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]]: Provides trace
  minerals and energy

### Compost and Soil Inputs
- [[knf-fermented-mixed-compost]]:
  Enriched compost incorporating IMO-4 and multiple NF inputs. This
  complements other composting approaches like [[composting-systems|conventional composting]]
  and [[natural-farming-guide-vermicomposting-and-worm-based-soil-building|vermicomposting]]
- [[knf-seed-treatment-and-soil-foundation]]:
  Combined liquid application for soil conditioning

### Pest Control
- Aromatic Insect Attractants (AIA): Traps using FFJ and rice wine
- Fluorescent Insect Attractants (FIA): Light traps with water basins
- Fish odour methods: Using FAA to control mites and whitefly

## Core Fermentation Principles

All NF inputs share common fermentation principles:

1. **Jaggery or brown sugar** is the universal fermentation agent.
  White refined sugar is never used. The closer the sugar is to
  its natural state, the more effective it is. Jaggery (crude,
  unrefined sugar) is preferred.

2. **Porous paper covers** are used instead of airtight seals. Paper
  allows air exchange while keeping insects and contaminants out.

3. **Clay pots or glass jars** are preferred over plastic containers.
  Wooden or bamboo containers are recommended for IMO collection.

4. **Temperature control**: The optimum range for most preparations
  is 23-25 degrees C. Storage at 1-15 degrees C extends shelf life.

5. **Do not wash ingredients**: Washing removes the useful
  microorganisms on plant and material surfaces.

6. **Standard dilution ratio**: Most NF inputs are diluted 1:1000
  with water for field application.

## Inputs by Growth Stage

The [[knf-nutritive-cycle-theory]] maps inputs to three plant growth
stages:

### Vegetative Growth (N stage)
Primary inputs: FPJ, FAA (nitrogen-rich). Support rapid growth of
roots and shoots. Dilution: 1:800 to 1:1000.

### Changeover Period / Morning Sickness (P stage)
Primary inputs: WS-PA, FFJ from less-ripe fruits (phosphoric acid).
Plants crave "sour nutrients" during flowering preparation.
Dilution: 1:1000.

### Reproductive Growth (K stage)
Primary inputs: WS-Ca, WS-K, FFJ from fully ripened fruits (calcium
and potassium for fruit quality). Dilution: 1:1000.

### Universal Inputs (all stages)
OHN, LAB, BRV, and seawater can be used throughout all growth stages.
OHN is considered the most important input and is always included.
