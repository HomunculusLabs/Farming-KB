# Cannabis Health Therapeutics

Medical cannabis applications organized by condition, with cannabinoid/terpene profiles, delivery methods, evidence levels, and dosing considerations. Based on Blesching's Cannabis Health Index.

Related: [[cannabis-plant-nutrition]], [[herbal-medicine]], [[cannabis-genetics-and-breeding]]

## Endocannabinoid System Overview

The endocannabinoid system (ECS) is a lipid-signaling system present in all vertebrates, regulating homeostasis across multiple physiological processes.

**Key components:**
- **CB1 receptors**: Concentrated in CNS (brain, spinal cord), also in peripheral tissues. Mediate psychoactive effects, pain modulation, appetite stimulation, motor control, memory processing.
- **CB2 receptors**: Primarily in immune cells (spleen, tonsils, immune system). Mediate anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory effects. Also found in peripheral nervous system and some brain areas.
- **Endogenous ligands**: Anandamide (AEA) -- binds primarily CB1, "bliss molecule," short half-life. 2-AG (2-arachidonoylglycerol) -- binds both CB1 and CB2, most abundant endocannabinoid.
- **Enzymes**: FAAH (degrades AEA), MAGL (degrades 2-AG). Inhibiting these enzymes increases endocannabinoid tone -- target for pharmaceutical development.

**ECS regulates**: Pain perception, inflammation, immune function, mood/stress response, appetite and digestion, sleep-wake cycles, memory and learning, reproductive function, neuroprotection, bone growth, cardiovascular function.

**Entourage effect**: THC and CBD work synergistically with terpenes and minor cannabinoids. Full-spectrum preparations often outperform isolated compounds at equivalent doses. This is why strain selection and whole-plant preparations matter clinically.

## Minor Cannabinoids and Breeding Targets

Emerging minor cannabinoids offer therapeutic potential distinct from THC and CBD. Selective breeding for these compounds is an active area of strain development (see [[cannabis-genetics-and-breeding]]):

| Minor Cannabinoid | Potential | Breeding Notes |
|-------------------|-----------|---------------|
| **THCV** | Appetite suppressant, anticonvulsant, anti-inflammatory | Found in African landrace sativas; breeding for high THCV requires selecting for the Bt/Bt allele |
| **CBG** | Antibacterial (MRSA), anti-inflammatory, neuroprotective | CBG is the precursor to THC/CBD; harvest early (week 6-8) for maximum CBG before conversion |
| **CBC** | Anti-depressant, anti-inflammatory, analgesic | Present in most strains at low levels; selective breeding can increase concentrations |
| **CBDV** | Anticonvulsant, anti-nausea | Found in landrace indica strains from NW India and Nepal |
| **THCA/CBDA** | Raw anti-inflammatory and anti-nausea (non-psychoactive) | Maximizing raw cannabinoids: harvest early, use whole-plant juicing |

### Strain Selection for Therapeutic Profiles

When selecting strains for specific therapeutic goals, consider the full cannabinoid-terpene matrix rather than single compounds:

- **Daytime functional**: High CBD, low THC, limonene/pinene dominant. Type II or Type III chemotypes
- **Pain management**: 1:1 to 2:1 THC:CBD, myrcene + caryophyllene dominant. Indica-dominant Type I or Type II
- **Sleep aid**: High THC + myrcene + linalool + CBN. Indica-dominant Type I, fully mature harvest
- **Anti-inflammatory**: High CBDA/THCA (raw), caryophyllene dominant. Harvest early for acidic cannabinoids
- **Anxiety/PTSD**: CBD-dominant, linalool + limonene. Type II or III with terpinolene

## Dosing Principles

- **Start low, go slow**: Begin with 1-2.5 mg THC. Wait 2+ hours (edibles) or 15 min (vaporized) before redosing.
- **Microdosing**: 0.5-2.5 mg THC for subtle therapeutic effects without intoxication. Effective for anxiety, inflammation, daytime pain management.
- **Therapeutic window**: Most conditions respond to 5-30 mg THC/day. Exceeding personal tolerance increases side effects without proportional benefit.
- **CBD moderates THC**: Adding CBD reduces THC's psychoactive and anxiogenic effects. 1:1 to 4:1 CBD:THC ratios provide medical benefits with minimal intoxication.
- **Tolerance management**: Tolerance to THC develops in 1-2 weeks of daily use. Strategies: take 2-day breaks weekly, "strain hop" between different cannabinoid profiles, use CBD-dominant preparations on tolerance breaks.
- **Cannabinoid ratio**: More important than absolute dose. Document your ratio (THC:CBD) and total dose for each effective session.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug interactions**: CBD inhibits CYP3A4 and CYP2C19 (affects ~25% of all pharmaceuticals). Consult pharmacist. Warfarin, valproate, clobazam require close monitoring.
- **Cardiovascular**: THC causes tachycardia and orthostatic hypotension. Caution with heart disease, especially in older adults.
- **Psychiatric**: THC can trigger psychosis in vulnerable individuals. Avoid high-THC in schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, or personal/family history of psychosis. CBD may be protective.
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: THC crosses placenta and enters breast milk. AAP and ACOG recommend against use.
- **Driving**: THC impairs driving for 3-5+ hours after use. Never drive under the influence.
- **Youth**: Brain development continues to ~25 years. Regular THC use in adolescence associated with cognitive deficits, increased psychosis risk, and potential addiction. Medical use in minors should be under specialist supervision.
