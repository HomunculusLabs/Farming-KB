## Human Uses of Fungi Throughout History

The relationship between humans and fungi spans millennia, encompassing food
production, medicine, industry, biotechnology, and spiritual practices across
every human culture. Fungi have shaped human civilization in ways that are often
underappreciated, from the leavening of bread to the production of life-saving
antibiotics. The extraordinary metabolic versatility of the fungal kingdom has
provided humans with an immense toolkit of biological processes and products.
