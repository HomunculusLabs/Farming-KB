## Nitrifying Bacteria

The conversion of ammonium to plant-available nitrate requires two specialized bacterial groups working in sequence:

1. **Nitrosomonas spp.**: Convert ammonium (NH4+) into nitrites (NO2-)
2. **Nitrobacter spp.**: Convert nitrites into nitrates (NO3-)

These nitrifying bacteria do not like acidic environments. Their numbers diminish when soil pH drops below 7. Bacterial biofilm (slime) has a pH above 7, so when enough bacteria are present, their slime keeps the local pH favorable for nitrification. If pH drops to 5 or lower, very little ammonium is converted to nitrate.
