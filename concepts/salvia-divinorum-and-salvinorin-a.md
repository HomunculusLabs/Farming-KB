# Salvia Divinorum and Salvinorin A

## Overview

Salvia divinorum is a psychoactive plant from the mint family
(Lamiaceae) that contains the neoclerodane diterpene salvinorin A.
Originally used by Mazatec Indians of Oaxaca, Mexico, it is now grown
and sold in the [[amanita-muscaria-pharmacology]]

## Taxonomy and Classification

Salvia divinorum belongs to the Lamiaceae (mint) family, which
includes many common ornamental and culinary plants. Despite its
relationship to common sage (Salvia officinalis), Salvia divinorum is
chemically distinct and is the only known Salvia species containing
salvinorin A. Other Lamiaceae ornamentals, including Coleus species,
have been chewed by Mazatec Indians as possible substitutes, but to
date only Salvia divinorum has been confirmed to contain the active
compound.
