## Double Cover Plastic Inflation: Trial Results

## The Experimental Setup

At [[coleman-cold-house-wind-protection-microclimate]]**: Covered with one sheet of greenhouse
plastic. This approach maximizes light transmission (critical during
short winter days) and minimizes both cost and complexity. No
electric blower is needed.

**Air-inflated double-layer house**: Two sheets of plastic with a
small electric blower continuously pumping air into the space between
them, keeping the layers separated. The trapped air layer provides
additional insulation. This system cuts approximately 10 percent of
ambient light but provides better thermal protection.
