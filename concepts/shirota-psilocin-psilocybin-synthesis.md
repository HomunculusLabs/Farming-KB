Preparative reversed-phase HPLC purification afforded a single compound
(8), analyzed by comprehensive 2D NMR. The proton and carbon-13 NMR spectra in
methanol-d4 showed signals for two sets of benzyl groups and a psilocin core.
One benzyl group showed a proton signal shifted to high field (delta 4.56)
compared to the other (delta 4.98, 4.96), while the methylene and N,N-dimethyl
signals of the psilocin core were shifted to low field compared to psilocin
itself. Phosphorus-31 NMR confirmed the phosphate moiety.

## Structural Elucidation

The HMBC (heteronuclear multiple bond correlation) experiment revealed that
one benzyl group had migrated to the nitrogen of the N,N-dimethyl group,
forming a quaternary ammonium ion. The NOESY (nuclear Overhauser effect
spectroscopy) experiment provided supporting evidence. These data established
that compound 8 is a zwitterionic N,O-dibenzyl phosphate derivative, formed by
intramolecular migration of one benzyl group from oxygen to nitrogen during
the aqueous workup.

Nichols and Frescas had previously observed a similar phenomenon, concluding
that hydrolytic cleavage of one O-benzyl group occurred rapidly. The Shirota
team showed that the conversion could be reliably achieved by simply suspending
the worked-up reaction mixture in dichloromethane overnight.
