## Jamaican Dream: Fast-Flowering Jamaican Heirloom Sativa

## Overview

Jamaican Dream is a 90% sativa strain bred by Eva Female Seeds of Spain from a Jamaican heirloom variety. It represents a remarkable achievement in [[oner-sativa-landrace-preservation]], ensuring that these valuable genetic resources remain available to future generations of growers and breeders.

## Genetics and Lineage

- **Type:** 90% sativa
- **Source:** Bred from a Jamaican heirloom variety
- **Landrace heritage:** Direct connection to Jamaican "Yarders" — the local [[growing-gourmet-chanterelle-truffle-cultivation-challenges]].

### Growth Pattern

- **Structure:** Christmas tree style growth pattern — compact and symmetrical
- **Ease of cultivation:** Easy enough that even newbie cultivators will be able to get the best out of it
- **Hardiness:** A hardy plant that can withstand common growing mistakes
- **Indica-like growth:** Despite its sativa-dominant genetics, the growth pattern resembles an indica

### Indoor Cultivation

- **Recommended flip height:** Force flowering at around 2 feet for a final height of approximately 3 feet
- **Lighting:** The breeders recommend growing under sodium lights
- **Nutrient strategy:** Going either medium or heavy with nutrients will help maximize yield
- **Expected yield:** Around 70 grams per plant indoors
- **Flowering time:** An incredibly quick 42 days — one of the fastest flowering times for any sativa-dominant strain

The 42-day flowering time is particularly remarkable. Most pure or near-pure sativas require 10-16 weeks of flowering, making Jamaican Dream's 6-week cycle an exceptional outlier. This rapid flowering makes the strain practical for growers who want sativa effects but cannot accommodate the long flowering periods typically required.

### Outdoor Cultivation

- **Height:** Can grow to around 6 feet or more when given full sun and root space
- **Harvest time:** End of September — early for a sativa-dominant variety
- **Expected yield:** At least 500 grams per plant outdoors
- **Climate suitability:** Performs well in temperate to warm climates
