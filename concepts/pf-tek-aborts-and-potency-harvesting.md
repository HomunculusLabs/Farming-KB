# PF TEK Aborts and Potency Harvesting

The PF TEK emphasizes that the potency of psilocybe-fanaticus
advocated harvesting mushrooms at a young, immature stage and collecting
aborts, the small malformed mushrooms that cease growing early in the fruiting
cycle. This approach, combined with cool [[jochen-gartz]] showed that [[cannabis-harvest-timing-visual-cues]] is
determined by the partial veil beneath the cap. The veil is a thin membrane
connecting the cap margin to the stem. As the mushroom matures, the veil
stretches and eventually tears. The PF TEK recommends harvesting before the
veil breaks, when:

- The mushroom cap is still roundish or conical, not yet spread flat.
- The gills on the underside of the cap are light in color, not yet darkening
  to deep brown.
- Sporulation has not yet begun (no purple spore deposits visible on the stem).

Harvesting at this stage yields smaller mushrooms but with significantly higher
potency by weight. The mature specimens with open caps, dark gills, and purple
spore deposits are beautiful and perfect for spore collecting and display, but
they are notably weaker in psychedelic potency.

## Harvesting Technique

[[pf-tek-psilocybe-fanaticus-cultivation-method]] recommended using a pointed knife blade for removing aborts
and small primordia from the cake. The pointed tip allows precise cutting
without damaging the surrounding mycelium or disturbing neighboring developing
mushrooms. Aborts should be harvested as soon as it can be determined that they
are not developing into normal specimens, and always before their heads begin
to turn black.

For normal fruiting specimens, grasp the mushroom at the base of the stem and
gently twist and pull. The goal is to remove the entire mushroom including the
base without tearing into the substrate cake. Damaging the cake surface can
open pathways for contamination.
