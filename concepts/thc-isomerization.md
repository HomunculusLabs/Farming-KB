### Dissolution

The oil from a prior petroleum ether extraction is dissolved in absolute
ethanol or pure methanol in a ratio of one gram of oil to ten grams of
solvent. The ethanol may be denatured but must not contain water.

### Acid Addition

One drop of 100% sulfuric acid is added to the alcohol/oil solution for
each gram of oil. The acid is added slowly with continuous stirring. Pure
sulfuric acid is extremely corrosive and causes severe burns. Safety
glasses, long rubber gloves, and clothing covering as much body surface
as possible are required. Burns are treated by immediate washing with
water and bicarbonate of soda.

### Refluxing

The solution is placed in a Pyrex pot in a refluxing apparatus. Pyrex is
substituted for stainless steel because of the reactive nature of sulfuric
acid. The apparatus is placed in a boiling water bath and refluxed for
two hours.

### Extraction

After refluxing, the pot is placed in an ice-water bath and opened. The
solution is poured into an equal amount of water and extracted with
petroleum ether. The ether solution is poured into four volumes of water
and gently inverted twenty-five times, releasing pressure each time. The
layers are allowed to separate, and the ether/oil layer is siphoned off.

### Washing

The ether/oil solution is poured into four volumes of 5% bicarbonate of
soda solution in water, mixed, separated, and siphoned. The bicarbonate
wash is discarded and the water wash is repeated twice.

### Evaporation

The ether is evaporated using a stew pot apparatus, collecting the pure
ether for reuse. The remaining oil now contains a much higher percentage
of THC in the high-rotating isomeric form, with all toxins removed.

closure pattern. Under acidic conditions, sulfuric acid catalyzes the
cyclization of the open-ring CBD molecule into the closed-ring THC
