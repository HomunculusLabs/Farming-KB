# 2C-I (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Iodophenethylamine)

## Chemical Identity

2C-I is a synthetic phenethylamine and a member of the 2C family of [[psychedelic-compounds-reference]]. Its
full chemical name is 2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodophenethylamine. It is classified as compound #33 in
Alexander Shulgin's PiHKAL (Phenethylamines I Have Known And Loved). The compound features the
characteristic 2C-family skeleton: a 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine with a halogen substituent at
the 4-position. In the case of 2C-I, this substituent is iodine.

## Synthesis

2C-I is synthesized from 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine (2C-H) through electrophilic aromatic
iodination. The free base of 2C-H is reacted with iodine monochloride (ICl) in warm acetic acid.
The intermediate phthalimide-protected compound is formed by first reacting 2C-H with phthalic
anhydride, followed by iodination at the 4-position, then deprotection with hydrazine hydrate.

The final hydrochloride salt is described as a white microcrystalline solid with a melting point
of 246-247°C. The position of iodination on the aromatic ring is verified by the presence of two
para-protons in the NMR spectrum.

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage**: 14-22 mg orally
- **Duration**: 6-10 hours
- **Onset**: 40-60 minutes, building gradually to peak at approximately 2 hours

2C-I occupies a middle ground in the 2C family in terms of both potency and duration. It is more
potent than 2C-C (20-40 mg) but less potent than 2C-B (12-24 mg) on a milligram basis.

## Qualitative Effects

### Visual Effects

The visual effects of 2C-I are frequently compared to those of 2C-B, with colorful, active
patterning described as "Persian carpet type" imagery. Color enhancement is pronounced, and
users report shadowed or darkened areas in the visual field that seem to shift around. Visual
activity tends to be more prominent in detailed environments (pictures, artwork) than in plain
surfaces such as walls.

### Emotional and Cognitive Effects

Users report a balanced emotional character, clear and insightful conversation, and a centered
feeling. The experience is generally described as easier and less dark in transition than 2C-B,
though some users note an absence of deep insight or revelatory content. A unique "contact high"
phenomenon was documented in PiHKAL, where an observer who consumed zero milligrams reported
reaching a plus 2.5 level simply by absorbing the ambience of others who had taken the compound.

### Physical Effects

2C-I is generally well-tolerated physically. Users report eating normally during the experience.
The comedown is described as abrupt at around 5-6 hours, followed by a gentle decline. Sleep
quality is typically good the following night, though users may feel unusually energetic the
next day and tired by evening.

## Comparison with 2C-B

The frequent comparisons between 2C-I and 2C-B are chemically logical. The two compounds have
truly analogous structures — in one, there is a strategically located iodine atom, and in the
other, an identically placed bromine atom. These halogens sit directly above and below each other
in the periodic table.

However, the compounds are not interchangeable in experience. Users describe 2C-I as having a
somewhat different "frequency" than 2C-B, with a less intensely sensual character but a longer
and more gradual onset. The transition period is notably less dark than with 2C-B.

## Radio-Labelling Applications

The presence of a heavy iodine atom makes 2C-I a natural candidate for radio-labelling experiments.
2C-I has been prepared with radioactive iodine for research purposes. The most significant
radio-labelling findings, however, have been made with the 3-carbon (amphetamine) analog, DOI,
which has been used extensively in [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]] binding studies.

## Chemical Extensions

### 2CI-2EtO (Iodotweetio)

One homologue of 2C-I is known: the 2-ethoxy analog (2-ethoxy-4-iodo-5-methoxyphenethylamine,
or 2CI-2EtO). The hydrochloride salt is a white crystalline product with a melting point of
175-175.5°C. The threshold of activity was observed at 5 mg orally, with effects dispersing
within a couple of hours. Unusually, larger doses (up to 50 mg) did not produce more intense
effects but simply extended the threshold duration by an additional couple of hours. At no tested
dose did 2CI-2EtO produce a plus-two response.

### Theoretical: 2C-A (Astatine Analog)

Shulgin speculated about the hypothetical astatine analog, 2,5-dimethoxy-4-astatophenethylamine.
Astatine is the fifth halogen, discovered in the nuclear age, and all its isotopes are
radioactive. The longest-lived isotope has a half-life of approximately 8 hours. Shulgin noted
that accumulating the necessary 10-20 mg of astatine would be virtually impossible (the maximum
ever produced at once was approximately 0.05 micrograms), and the resulting radiation would be
lethal to the user.

## Legal Status

2C-I has been scheduled as a controlled substance in many jurisdictions. In the United States, it
was placed under Schedule I of the [[controlled-substances-act-and-psychedelic-research]] in 2004 as part of the Emergency
Scheduling of [[shulgin-designer-drugs-analysis]]. It is also controlled in many other countries under analog drug
legislation or specific scheduling.

## Safety Considerations

As with all [[optical-isomers-psychedelic-phenethylamines]], set, setting, and dosage are critical safety factors.
The 6-10 hour duration requires users to commit a significant portion of their day to the
experience. The steep dose-response curve common to the 2C family means that small increases in
dosage can produce disproportionately intense effects.

## Relationship to the 2C Family

2C-I is part of the systematic exploration of the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted phenethylamines
that Shulgin undertook. The 4-position halogen series — 2C-F (fluorine), 2C-C (chlorine), 2C-B
(bromine), and 2C-I (iodine) — demonstrates the relationship between atomic weight, electron
configuration, and pharmacological activity. Generally, potency increases with the size of the
halogen substituent in this series. The 4-position is chemically constrained: unlike alkyl chains,
halogens cannot be "lengthened or shortened" — each is a fixed atom with unique properties. This
makes the halogen series particularly clean for structure-activity relationship studies.
