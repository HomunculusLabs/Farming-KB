## Bulk Substrate Preparation

For commercial or high-yield grows, substrate is often prepared in bulk.
Straw is chopped to 2-4 inch pieces, soaked or steeped in hot water, drained,
and loaded into bags or containers. Sawdust is mixed with supplements and
water, bagged, and sterilized. Proper bagging technique is critical: bags
must allow gas exchange (through filter patches) while preventing contaminant
entry.
