## Step 3: Filling the Bags

1. Firmly pack straw mixture into the bags until almost full
2. Leave enough room at the top to fit the filter can
3. Insert the filter can into the neck of each bag
4. Secure by wrapping wide masking tape around the neck of the bag three times, pressing it firmly onto both bag and can

## Step 4: Sterilisation

1. Place bags in the pressure cooker (1-2 bags depending on cooker size)
2. Add water and bring to maximum pressure (15 psi)
3. Sterilise for 60 minutes (can be done as two 30-minute cycles)
4. Allow bags to cool completely before inoculation

## Step 5: Preparing Bags for Inoculation

Two inoculation approaches are described:

**Short needle method**: Tape support strips from can to bag base. Mark 2 inoculation points per bag with tape, adding a looped piece to quickly seal after needle withdrawal.

**Long needle method (preferred)**: Tape the filter can on both sides of the bag. Inoculation passes the needle through the filter can itself — described as easier, quicker, and more trouble-free.

## Step 6: Preparing the Spore Syringe

1. Fill a Pyrex jug with 80 ml water, cover with tin foil
2. Wrap the connected syringe and needle in tin foil
3. Sterilise both in the pressure cooker at 15 psi for 20 minutes
4. Allow to cool, then add water to the spore jar
5. If spores do not readily mix, use a flame-sterilised flat-ended scraper to loosen them from the jar bottom
6. Load the spore suspension into the syringe
