# ultra low jadam fertilizer program

## Soil Foundation Builder

The most important application is the soil foundation builder, applied
before transplanting for annuals or before budding/sprouting for fruit
trees. For 0.33 hectares (0.8 acres):

- JMS: 500 L (132 gal)
- Sea salt: 1.5 kg (3.3 lb), or seawater 60 L (16 gal)
- Phyllite solution: 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF: 60 L (16 gal)

Mix with water and apply. For open fields, apply undiluted before rain
so inputs follow rainwater into the soil. For greenhouses, apply with
plenty of water to penetrate deeply. Repeat 3 to 4 times before
transplanting. This promotes root growth and strengthens resistance
against cold and frost.

## Seed and Seedling Treatment

Before planting, soak seeds or seedlings in leaf mold solution or JMS
dilution for 2 to 3 minutes. Use JMS 100 L or 10 kg of leaf mold per
500 L of water. This greatly improves root settlement and microbial
diversity around the young plant, preventing disease outbreaks.

## Basic Additional Fertilizer

For ongoing nutrition during growth (0.33 hectares):

- JMS: 500 L (132 gal)
- Seawater: 60 L (16 gal), or sea salt 1.5 kg (3.3 lb)
- Phyllite solution: 60 L (16 gal)
- GC-JLF: 30 L (8 gal)

Apply to soil, 3 to 4 times per month. Adjust GC-JLF dilution based on
observed plant growth. Use seawater or sea salt 3 to 4 times per month.

## Growth Promoters

When basic additional fertilizer is insufficient:

**Body growth promoter** (for vegetative growth): Add food waste JLF
60 L (can substitute urine, feces, or fish JLF) to the basic program.

**Reproductive growth promoter** (for flowering and fruiting): Add
calcium phosphate JLF 60 L to the basic program.

**Fruit growth promoter** (for fruit enlargement): Add potassium JLF.
To control excessive growth, remove potassium JLF and increase calcium
JLF. Calcium JLF diluted 100 times is used for growth control.

## Foliar Application Notes

For foliar application, reduce total inputs (JMS + JLF) to below 30 L
per 500 L of water. Add over 1.5 L of JADAM Wetting Agent for even
coating without marks. Do not use more than 20 L of JMS in foliar
applications as it breaks down JWA. Keep foliar application as
secondary; focus on soil application to feed roots.

## Key Principle: Same Back to Same

The fundamental rule is to give plants what is the same as or similar
to the plants themselves. This naturally provides the optimal
nutritional balance. Base fertilizer from cover crop and crop residue.
Additional fertilizer from crop residue and wild grass JLF. Keep using
these and you will eventually reach the ultimate nutritional balance.
It is easy, costs almost nothing, and rewards high quality and yield.
