# Shulgin's Rating Scale for Psychedelic Substances

## Overview

[[plant-sensory-perception]]. The subject is aware that something is happening but remains fully functional and oriented.

### ++

**Level 2**. Clear effects. Definitely an altered state. Visual distortions may be present, thought patterns are noticeably changed, but the subject maintains full contact with reality. Can be described as a "stoned" state. The subject can still perform ordinary tasks with concentration.

As one TIHKAL subject described: "I am up to a ++ within the hour and am feeling lazy."

### +++

**Level 3**. Strong psychedelic effects. True psychedelic experience with significant alterations of perception, thought, and emotion. Visual hallucinations with eyes closed (and sometimes open). Time distortion, ego softening. The subject may have difficulty with complex tasks but retains identity and awareness of surroundings.

From TIHKAL: "I struggle to put a name to it, just +++ smashed" -- indicating the overwhelming, nearly indescribable quality of this level.

### ++++

**Level 4**. Overwhelming experience. Complete [[psilocybin-ego-dissolution-mechanisms]], transcendent states, contact with what is perceived as the divine or ultimate reality. The subject may lose all sense of self, body, and ordinary reality. This is the level of mystical or near-death experiences.

From TIHKAL (5-MeO-[[dmt]]): "I beheld every thought that was going on everywhere in the universe and all possible realities... A definite ++++."

## Usage in TIHKAL

The scale is used in two contexts:

### 1. Individual Trip Reports
Subjects describe their experience level at various time points:
- "shooting up past a +++ in another quarter hour" (AL-LAD, 150 ug)
- "back to ++, still very [affected]" (4-HO-DET)
- "basically a +1 experience" (5-MeO-DMT, 0.7 mg i.v.)

### 2. General Characterization
Compounds are sometimes classified by the levels they typically achieve:
- 5-MeO-DMT: "positive, psychedelic, out-of-body" (B B rating)
- 5-MeO-DIPT: "positive, LSD-like psychedelic" (E E rating)
- 5-MeO-DET: "negative, vertigo, erotic" (C C rating)

## Limitations

Shulgin himself acknowledged the limitations of this scale:
- It captures only intensity, not quality or character of effects
- Individual variability is enormous ("it is different things to different folks")
- The same dose can produce widely different ratings in different subjects
- A compound's character (positive vs. negative, visual vs. conceptual, erotic vs. frightening) matters as much as its intensity level
- The scale cannot capture the full richness of psychedelic experience

## Complementary Characterization

Throughout TIHKAL, Shulgin supplements the numerical scale with qualitative descriptors:
- **Quality labels**: "positive," "negative," "neutral," "mixed," "complex"
- **Character descriptions**: "LSD-like," "erotic," "vertigo," "amnesia," "out-of-body," "[[turner-essential-psychedelics-lsd-cosmic-consciousness]]"
- **Duration markers**: Time of onset, peak, and return to baseline
- **Route-specific notes**: Differences between oral, smoked, and intravenous experiences

This multi-dimensional approach provides a richer characterization than the scale alone could convey.
