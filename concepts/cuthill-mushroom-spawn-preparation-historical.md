## Cuthill on Historical Mushroom [[french-flake-spawn-vs-brick-spawn]] was the standard product available to gardeners:

- A London brick was seven inches square and two inches thick, "like a
  small paving tile."
- Cuthill strongly objected to measuring spawn by the bushel: "Did any
  one ever hear of a bushel of bricks?" He preferred buying individual
  bricks at 4d. to 6d. each.
- One bushel of spawn covered approximately one hundred square feet.
- He bought first-rate spawn at 4d. to 6d. per square, noting it was
  made from pure materials with no "poison toadstool spawn" in its
  composition.
- Spawn-makers were described as "very particular" about their materials.
- Cuthill explicitly declined to explain how brick spawn was made: "It
  is quite out of my province to tell the public how to make brick
  spawn; that is a trade I do not wish to interfere with."
