# Jesus Christ and Jack 47

Two sativa-dominant hybrids from Oner's Volume 3 that represent
different approaches to breeding high-potency cannabis: Jesus Christ
is an accidental cross of two legendary American strains, while Jack 47
is a deliberate combination of two of the most celebrated genetics in
cannabis history.

## Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ was created by Green Dream Health Services in the USA
through a chance pollination when a Ghost OG plant got too close to a
towering [[cannabis-sour-diesel]]. Despite the accidental origin, the resulting
strain proved impressive enough to keep and stabilize. The name came
when the original gardener saw the seedling towering over its six
siblings and exclaimed "Jesus Christ, that plant is huge!" — and a
strain was born.

As a sativa-dominant hybrid with Ghost OG and Sour Diesel parentage,
Jesus Christ inherits the pungent, fuel-like aroma characteristic of
the Diesel family. The breeder recommends growing in organic soil with
minimal [[organic-nutrients-vs-synthetic-nutrients]] to preserve this tangy [[cannabis-terpene-profile]].
Quality rather than quantity is the stated goal with this strain.

The plant is described as not too fussy and not too demanding in
[[what-a-plant-knows-photoperiodism-flowering-time-measurement]], making it approachable for growers who lack extensive
sativa experience. This accessibility is unusual for a strain with such
potent genetics, as many sativa-dominant hybrids demand expert
management. The balanced growth pattern allows cultivation both
indoors and outdoors without requiring specialized [[cannabis-sativa-indoor-training-techniques]].

The high is described as beautifully balanced with abundant energy.
Smokers are warned that consuming enough may lead to grandiose
delusions of divinity — a fitting effect given the strain's name.

## Jack 47

Jack 47 was bred by Sweet Seeds of Spain, a breeder well known for
combining elite genetics into high-performance hybrids. The strain
merges two legendary lines: [[jack-herer]], named after the cannabis
activist and author, and AK-47, famous for its potency and yield.

Sweet Seeds used a proven male AK-47 as the stud plant — one that had
consistently produced offspring with huge yields and strong effects.
This was crossed with a female Jack Herer that had already proven its
worth in Sweet Seeds' popular Black Jack strain. The result is a
super-yielding hybrid with massive psychoactive power that is also
described as an excellent choice for [[cannabis-hashish-production]].

At 75% sativa, Jack 47 grows fairly tall but remains manageable
indoors for experienced cultivators. The strong sativa influence
creates some wildness in growth patterns, so this strain is not
recommended for beginners or those new to sativa cultivation. The plant
grows with great vigor and speed, thriving in almost any environment
or growing system. [[low-stress-training]] is recommended to optimize
canopy management regardless of setup. Cloning is notably easy, with
numerous potential cutting sites and root development in approximately
7 days under good conditions.

Despite the speed of growth and bud density, Jack 47 shows
extraordinary resistance to mold and [[bud-rot-cannabis]] — a valuable combination
of traits. The flowers are described as particularly gorgeous, growing
into themselves in every direction to create the impression of layered
velvet forming one giant bud. Indoor flowering takes approximately 9.5
weeks, while outdoor harvest occurs at the end of October. Yields are
described as phenomenal, especially outdoors.

Potency ranges from THC 18-24% with CBD at 1.6%, placing Jack 47
firmly in the high-potency category. The taste and aroma carry the
distinctive Jack Herer profile, but the effects are significantly more
intense — described as an "absolute annihilator" that transports users
to another plane entirely. The combination of extreme potency and heavy
[[cannabis-glandular-trichome-types-resin-production-clarke]] makes Jack 47 particularly well-suited for hashish and
concentrate production, a trait emphasized by Oner in the original
profile.
