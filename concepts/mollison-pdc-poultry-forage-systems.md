## Flock Management Principles

- Four hundred chickens is the break-even point for a pasture; 300 is
  workable
- Ideal flock size is 50 to 60 birds for social well-being
- About five roosters per flock prevent wandering
- Four different 60-bird flocks of different breeds can run on one acre
- Heavy breeds lay better in winter; light breeds are spring/summer
  layers
- Consult chicken fanciers, not poultrymen, for breed advice

Chickens in forage systems show remarkable health: "They seem to
maintain good health and they lay until they die. Some go six years.
They often die at roost, having laid the day before."
