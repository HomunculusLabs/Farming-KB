--- Enzyme System Architecture
The fungal cytochrome P450 monoxygenase system consists of two key
protein components working together in an electron transfer chain
localized in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane of fungal cells.
- **Cytochrome P450** is the terminal oxidase, a heme-containing
  enzyme that binds molecular oxygen and substrate, catalyzing
  insertion of one oxygen atom into the substrate molecule.
- **NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase (CPR)** shuttles electrons
  from NADPH to the P450 enzyme, enabling its catalytic cycle.
- Both microsomal and cytosolic P450 isoforms have been
  identified in biodegrading fungi, suggesting different
