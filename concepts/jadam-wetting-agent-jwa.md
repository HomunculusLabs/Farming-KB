## JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA)

JWA is the most important ingredient in [[jadam-methods]] pesticide system. It is a natural surfactant (soap) made from canola oil and caustic potash (KOH) without heating. It provides wetting, coating and penetrating power -- without it, no [[jadam-pesticide-dosage-guide]] works. It also has its own pesticide and germicide effect. Costs under $0.50/L to make. No expiration date.

Replaces: commercial synthetic surfactants, machine oil, neem oil. 99.9% biodegrades in 5 days.

## Water Quality Is Critical

JWA is extremely sensitive to water hardness. **Must use soft water** (low Ca, Mg, Fe).

**Water test**: Put a few drops of JWA in 0.5L water. Water should stay clear and produce lots of foam when shaken. If water turns murky or produces no foam, it is hard water -- do not use.

Solutions for hard water: collect rainwater (discard first 30 min), use a water softener (ion-exchange resin), or increase JWA dosage.

## Recipe: 100L (26 gal) Batch

**Ingredients**:
- Canola oil: 18L (4.76 gal) -- non-GMO preferred; sunflower, olive, or grapeseed oil also work
- Caustic potash (KOH, 90%): 3.2 kg (7.05 lb)
- Soft water: 82.5L total (2.5L initial + 80L added later)

**Equipment**: 110L heat-resistant plastic container (do NOT use aluminum), electric drill with clean paddle, gloves, safety goggles, mask, clean wooden stick

### Step-by-Step Process (4 days, no heating)

1. Do JWA water test on your water supply
2. Measure exactly 2.5L (0.66 gal) soft water into container
3. Add 3.2 kg KOH, close lid, roll container on its side to dissolve (small amount of gas released -- ventilate)
4. Heat is produced when KOH meets water -- ensure fully dissolved
5. Add 18L canola oil, mix with electric drill ~10 min until consistency of thin mayonnaise (longer in summer)
6. Close lid, let sit 3 days -- it will harden like butter. If it separates or doesn't harden, drill-mix again
7. During sitting, temperature rises from ~60C to ~83C, then declines
8. Add 20L soft water, drill-mix to remove lumps from walls (do NOT over-mix or it turns to white cream)
9. Add 60L soft water, stir thoroughly with clean wooden stick including the bottom
10. Lumps will dissolve over 24 hours. JWA is complete

**Storage**: Tightly sealed containers. No expiration -- quality improves with age. Do not freeze (thaw if frozen). Remove any white film from top before use. Becomes darker with age; this is normal.

## Safety

- Strong heat produced -- wear gloves, goggles, mask, boots, jacket
- KOH is caustic -- never touch with bare skin
- Work in well-ventilated area
- Never use aluminum containers
- Container must be 10%+ larger than batch volume

## Troubleshooting

- **Won't harden after 3 days**: Drill-mix again until mayo consistency; ensure container is warm enough
- **Separated layers**: Drill-mix again
- **Over-drilled (white cream)**: Add water to 3cm below rim, wait 2-3 days, clear JWA settles below. Remove white film
- **Lumps won't dissolve**: Need more water; add soft water and wait
- **Poor foaming in spray**: Water is hard -- switch to soft water or increase JWA
- **Murky spray solution**: Hard water -- change water source
