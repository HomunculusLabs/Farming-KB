## Overview

Gastro-esophageal reflux disease (GERD) is a chronic digestive condition in
which stomach acid or bile flows back into the esophagus, irritating its
lining. The primary mechanism involves transient lower esophageal sphincter
relaxations (TLESRs) -- inappropriate relaxations of the muscle between the
stomach and esophagus that allow acid to escape upward.

mistaken for cardiac pain), difficulty swallowing, chronic cough, hoarseness,
