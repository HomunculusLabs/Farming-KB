## JADAM Fertilizer Program Design

JADAM's approach to fertilizer program design represents a radical departure
from both conventional and mainstream organic methods. Founder Cho Han Kyu
(more widely known as Youngsang Cho) argues that the dominant paradigm of
dividing plant growth into stages and prescribing specific nutrient inputs
for each stage is fundamentally flawed — and primarily serves the commercial
interests of fertilizer manufacturers rather than farmers.

## Critique of Conventional Fertilizer Programs

The standard agricultural approach divides plant growth into stages and
designs separate fertilizer programs for each: high nitrogen during early
[[cervantes-chemical-fertilizers-guide]], NPK mixes,
    and customized [[jadam-nutritional-balance-fertilizer]].

### Base Fertilizer

Base fertilizer is supplied by:

-   **Cover crops** — The primary source. Focus on growing enough cover
    crop to supply 100% of base fertilizer needs.
-   **Crop residues** — The plant parts left after harvest.
-   **Animal manure** — From animals that ate grass (chicken and pig manure
    can also be used).
-   **Alternative [[jadam-soil-mineral-replenishment]] component:

-   **Sea water** — 20 liters per 0.1 hectare, providing 83 identified
    minerals.
-   **Phyllite solution** — 60 kg of fine-powdered phyllite in 500 liters
    of water, providing broad-spectrum rock minerals.
