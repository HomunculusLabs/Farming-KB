# Cannabis Topical Preparations

## Overview

Cannabis topicals are preparations applied to the skin for localized relief of pain, inflammation, and other conditions. While Cervantes devotes relatively little direct attention to topical preparations in "Marijuana Horticulture," the foundational extraction and infusion techniques described in the book provide the basis for creating effective topical cannabis products. The same cannabinoid extraction methods used for edibles and tinctures apply to topical formulations.

## Foundation: Cannabis-Infused Oils

The primary ingredient for most cannabis topicals is cannabis-infused oil, which serves as the carrier for cannabinoids. The psychoactive cannabinoids dissolve in butter, cooking oil, or alcohol, making them available for absorption by the body.

### Cannabis Vegetable Oil Base

The book provides a detailed recipe for making cannabis vegetable oil, which serves as the foundation for topical preparations:

1. Heat 1.6 quarts (1.5 L) of water in a large pan on the stove
2. Add 4 ounces (112 gm) of marijuana leaf and 1 quart (1 L) of vegetable oil
3. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low heat for 2 hours, stirring occasionally
4. Strain through a colander, press foliage to extract all liquid
5. Pour 0.5 quart (0.5 L) of boiling water over wet leaf to wash out remaining oil
6. Let cool; oil will separate and rise to the top
7. Place in freezer for 4-6 hours; water freezes, oil stays liquid
8. Pour off or scrape the cannabis-infused oil

### Oils and Lotions

Cannabis-infused oils can be applied directly to the skin or combined with other ingredients:

- Lighter oils (almond, jojoba, grapeseed) absorb more readily
- Lotions combine oil with water-based ingredients using emulsifiers
- The choice of carrier oil affects absorption rate and skin feel
- Thicker oils provide longer-lasting effects on the skin surface

### Compresses

Simple cannabis compresses can be made by soaking cloth in warm cannabis-infused oil and applying to affected areas. This method is useful for localized pain and inflammation.

## Preparing Input Material

For topicals, cannabis material should be carefully prepared:

- Clean stems and large debris from leaf material
- Ensure foliage is free of pesticide residues (use only water-based products before harvest)
- Flush with water for 7-10 days before harvest to remove fertilizers
