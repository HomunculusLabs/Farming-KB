# Cannabis Forms and Preparations

Cannabis medicine can be prepared and administered in numerous forms, each
offering distinct pharmacokinetic profiles, onset times, durations of effect,
and bioavailability characteristics. The choice of preparation significantly
influences therapeutic outcomes, making it one of the most important
decisions in medical cannabis treatment.

## Inhaled Preparations

Smoking cannabis remains the most common method of consumption, though it
introduces combustion byproducts including tar, carbon monoxide, and
[[cannabidiol-cbd-therapeutic-profile-blesching]] of the inhaled medicine.

The pulmonary bioavailability of THC through inhalation is estimated at
10-60%, with significant inter-individual variability based on smoking
technique, depth of inhalation, and breath-hold duration. Despite this
variability, inhalation remains the most predictable method for achieving
rapid therapeutic effects, making it particularly valuable for acute symptom
management such as breakthrough pain or nausea.
