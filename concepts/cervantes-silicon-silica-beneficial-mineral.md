# Silicon and Silica Benefits for Cannabis

Silicon is a beneficial mineral element that, while not classified as
essential for all plants, provides significant advantages for cannabis
cultivation. Available in most soils and water, silicon does not
typically cause cannabis complications due to deficiencies or excesses.
However, supplemental silicon can dramatically improve plant vigor and
resistance to stress.

## How Silicon Works in Plants

Silicon is absorbed by plants as silicic acid. Once absorbed, silicon
assists in keeping iron and manganese levels consistent within plant
tissues. It is found mainly in the epidermal cell walls where it
collects in the form of hydrated amorphous silica. Silicon also
accumulates in the walls of other cells throughout the plant.

The physical deposition of silica in cell walls creates a mechanical
barrier that strengthens the overall plant structure. This mineral
reinforcement is similar to adding rebar to concrete, making cell
walls thicker and more rigid.
