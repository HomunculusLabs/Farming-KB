# Cannabis Pests Prevention

From [[greg-green]]'s *The Cannabis Grow Bible* (4th ed.), Chapter 12.

## Critical Safety Warning

Only use repellents and pesticides that are clearly marked for FOOD
PRODUCTS USE on the label. If it is NOT FOR FOOD PRODUCT USE then DO
NOT USE IT WITH YOUR PLANTS. You are going to be smoking your plants
later. If it is safe for food product use then it will be safe to
smoke. Read the instructions carefully and do exactly as the label
says.

## The Golden Rule of Pest Prevention

**NEVER EVER BRING A PLANT INSIDE THAT HAS BEEN OUTSIDE.** People who
have done this have managed to kill nearly every plant in their house
because they brought a plant indoors that had spider mites.

Marijuana plants are very vulnerable to mite attacks because spider
mites love to suck marijuana plants dry. They are tiny -- about half
the size of a period dot. Always have a bottle of pesticide that kills
spider mites on hand.

## Common Cannabis Pests

### Spider Mites
**Deadly.** Can reduce your plant to trash in 2-3 days. Need a
pyrethrum-based insecticide. Ask your local grow shop for information.
This will also help keep away other insects.

### Whitefly
**Deadly.** Can reduce your plant to trash in days. Safer's soap helps
to kill whitefly. Available in most grow shops.

### Aphids
Small sap-sucking insects that cluster on stems and leaves. Ants farm
aphids, so ant presence often indicates aphid infestation. Treat with
pyrethrum-based insecticide.

### Ants
Will make a home in your grow for their nests. Also a sign of aphids.
Remove using Boric Acid or any popular colony killer pesticide.

### Powder Bugs (Thrips)
Like to lay eggs in your bud and stem. Can reduce your plant to nothing
in weeks. Pyrethrum-based insecticide recommended.

### Cutworms, Caterpillars, and Larvae
The only sure way to get rid of cutworms is to use a cutworm
repellent. Find a product that works on cutworms specifically. All
cutworm products work very well and can be bought in most grow shops.

### Slugs and Snails
Make a circle of table salt about 4 feet away from the base of your
plant. Make another circle a foot inside the first. Salt is deadly to
snails and slugs and will keep them out.

### Leaf Hoppers (Grasshoppers)
Harmless unless in large numbers. Treat same as powder bugs.

### Termites
They don't like water. Over-watering the soil around your plant will
make them leave. Also treat with pyrethrum-based insecticide.

### Gnats
Treat same as powder bugs -- pyrethrum-based insecticide.

### Scale
Treat same as powder bugs.

### Mealy Bugs
Treat same as powder bugs.

## Outdoor Predators

### Deer
Deer are so curious that even electric fences won't stop them over
time. Use predator urine from hunting shops. Spray the urine on your
patch to keep deer away. Find a urine type that does not attract other
plant-eating animals.

### Woodchucks
Nibble stems and collapse plants. Solutions: predator urine (same as
deer), build a small mesh fence around the bottom of plants. Use more
than one wrap of mesh and keep it tight. Planting marigolds nearby
also helps.

### Rabbits
Can reduce a crop to nothing in a couple of days and will continue
feeding from the same patch. Solutions are the same as for woodchucks.

### Groundhogs
Dry chlorine helps keep groundhogs away. Find groundhog holes near
your grow area and apply dry chlorine around the holes.

## Wilt Fungus ([[fusarium-oxysporum]])

Rare but still a problem in some parts of the world. This fungus wilts
your plants and will eventually kill them. You need a fungicide from
your grow shop. If they don't have it, order over the Internet.

## Pets and Pest Control

A cat for a garden grow is the #1 defense system against most small
predators. However, an indoor cat will bring unwanted pests into the
house. Cats also like to play with indoor plants, so be very careful.

## Cleaning the Grow Room (Full Reset)

Sometimes the bugs win. For a total infestation:

1. Set up another small grow room for cuttings and clones.
2. Take cuttings from your plants and move to the clean room.
3. Take all non-electrical equipment to the bathroom. Clean with
   bleach. Soak in a tub with water and bleach for a day.
4. Clean cervantes reflective materials with bleach. Paint if needed. Do corners,
   holes, extraction holes, fittings, pipes.
5. Clean around the floor rim. Lift floor panels if possible.
6. Use pest-killing smoke bombs following label instructions.
7. Clean the room again thoroughly.
8. Dust down electrical items before returning them.
9. Dry and return bleached equipment.
10. Grow cuttings for 1-2 weeks, checking daily for bugs.
11. Take new cuttings from the clean cuttings. Place in new soil and
    clean pots.
12. Move to the clean grow room and monitor for pests.

### Important Notes

- Eggs are usually safe from smoke bombs. Reapply 7-10 days after
  initial treatment.
- Some pests like powder bugs lay eggs inside stems and always come
  back. If you can't eliminate them, you may have to discard your
  genetics.
- Breeders should pay strict attention to keeping grow rooms clean
