## Fungal Bioweathering and Mineral Transformations

## Overview

Rocks and minerals represent a vast reservoir of elements, many of them
essential to life. Bulk biological metals such as sodium, potassium,
magnesium, and calcium are among the eight most abundant elements in the
Earth's crust and together make up over 11 percent of crustal rock.
Rocks and minerals also include essential trace metals (manganese,
molybdenum, iron, cobalt, nickel, copper, zinc) and, crucially for
microbial and plant growth, phosphorus. All these elements must be
released into bioavailable forms that can be assimilated by the biota.
Fungi play a major role in this mineral transformation process through
bioweathering activities.
