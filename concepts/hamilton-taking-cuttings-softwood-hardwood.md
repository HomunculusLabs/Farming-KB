## Softwood Cuttings

Softwood cuttings are taken from the current season's soft, new growth
during summer. This method is suitable for most shrubs and can be
performed any time during the summer months, though early in the season
is best. The [[maitake-fruiting-body-success-rate-management]] should be around eighty percent with proper
technique.

Select healthy shoots about four inches (10cm) long. Fill a tray with
peat-free propagation compost and trim each cutting just below a leaf
joint, removing about half the length. Carefully strip away all the side
leaves, then dip the cut end in copper fungicide solution followed by
organic rooting powder. Organic rooting powders contain no synthesised
hormones, making them perfectly safe for [[hamilton-vegetative-propagation]] and
