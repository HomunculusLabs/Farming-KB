# JADAM Phyllite Solution

## Overview

Phyllite is a fine-grained metamorphic rock that JADAM uses as a mineral supplement for soil. Derived from sedimentary rocks formed by the accumulation of ancient organic matter (dead plants and animals), phyllite contains a near-complete profile of inorganic nutrients required by plants. The solution can also be applied as a foliar spray at reduced concentration, providing a quick uptake of dissolved minerals through the leaf surface. Foliar application is particularly useful for correcting acute mineral deficiency symptoms such as interveinal chlorosis (indicating iron or [[knf-calcium-deficiency-symptoms]]).
For fruit orchards, JADAM recommends applying phyllite solution to the root zone in early spring before bud break and again after harvest during the root growth period. The slow-release nature of phyllite minerals means that a single application can provide benefits for several months, reducing the need for repeated applications compared to soluble mineral fertilisers.

## Phyllite and Soil Microbiome Interactions
The application of phyllite solution influences the soil microbiome in ways that extend beyond simple mineral nutrition. The fine mineral particles in phyllite solution provide surface area for microbial colonisation, creating microhabitats where bacteria and fungi can establish colonies protected from predation by soil microfauna. The silicate minerals in phyllite serve as substrates for silicate-dissolving bacteria such as Bacillus circulans and Pseudomonas fluorescens, which are known to enhance plant disease resistance through the production of antibiotics and the induction of systemic resistance responses in plant tissues.

Add the phyllite powder gradually while stirring vigorously to ensure even dispersion and to prevent clumping. A mechanical stirrer is preferable to hand stirring for large batches. After the initial mixing, allow the solution to settle for at least 24 hours. The upper portion of the liquid, which will be relatively clear with a slight cloudy tint, is the usable phyllite solution. The settled sediment at the bottom contains the larger mineral particles that have not dissolved and should be left in the container. As the solution is used, the container can be refilled with water, stirred again, and allowed to settle — the same phyllite powder will continue to release minerals for several months before it is exhausted.
The colour of the phyllite solution is a useful indicator of concentration and quality. A properly prepared solution should have a pale grey to light amber colour. If the solution is completely clear, the phyllite may be of poor quality or the water may be too hard (high in calcium and magnesium, which can precipitate other minerals out of solution). If the solution is very dark or opaque, the phyllite may contain excessive clay or silt content that does not contribute useful minerals.
