### Sprayer Maintenance

Add a surfactant ([[jadam-nutrient-management-wetting-agent-and-calcium]]) to sprayer solutions: a quarter teaspoonful
of mild liquid dishwashing soap per quart of spray. This quantity will not
burn or damage leaves. Before unscrewing the pump to fill the tank, wipe off
any grit lurking around the pump seal to prevent damage.

## Garden Knives

Two identical thin-bladed, sharp, pointed kitchen paring knives stuck into
the top of a short wooden stake in the garden are invaluable for:
- Thinning tiny seedlings by cutting (not pulling) crowded plants
- Weeding close to nearly emerged rows
- Harvesting

Keep a medium/coarse sharpening stone next to the stake. Hone the knife after
every five minutes of use. The stone can also hone a dull hoe in an emergency.
Bright red plastic handles improve visibility.
