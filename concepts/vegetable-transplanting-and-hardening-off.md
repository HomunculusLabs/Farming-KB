### Frost Protection Rules

- Do not allow the coverings to touch the plants, for they could
  injure the plants where contact is made. Use stakes or wire hoops to
  support coverings above the foliage.
- Remove the covers in the morning once temperatures rise above
  freezing to prevent overheating.
- Do not use metal or plastic sheets to protect plants from frost
  damage. They actually conduct cold to the plants and can cause more
  harm than no protection at all.
