# Cannabis, Food as Medicine, and Anti-Inflammatory Spices

## Overview

The therapeutic potential of cannabis extends beyond the plant itself.
Numerous foods and spices contain compounds that interact with the
endocannabinoid system, particularly CB2 receptors, to produce anti-
inflammatory and immune-modulating effects. Blesching's Cannabis Health
Index identifies dietary interventions that complement cannabinoid
therapy and support natural healing mechanisms.

## (E)-beta-Caryophyllene: The Dietary Cannabinoid

An international research team (Switzerland, Germany, Italy, U.S.; 2008,
2012) reported that spice-producing plants contain a functional non-
psychoactive CB2 agonist called (E)-beta-caryophyllene or (E)-BCP.
Scientists suggest CB2 activation via this dietary cannabinoid presents
a new therapeutic strategy for inflammation and [[blesching-cannabis-oxidative-stress-and-antioxidant-defense]]. (E)-BCP
may also protect against microbes, pain, and cancer.
