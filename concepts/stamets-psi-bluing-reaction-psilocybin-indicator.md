# Bluing Reaction in Psilocybin Mushrooms

## Overview

The bluing reaction is one of the most widely recognized features of
psilocybin-containing mushrooms. Many Psilocybe and Panaeolus
species will turn bluish or bluish green when bruised, either as a
normal response to growing conditions or while being handled during
collection. While the bluing reaction is of great interest to chemists
and pharmacologists, it has significant limitations as a field
identification tool. The blue pigmentation results from the degradation
of unstable psilocin (dephosphorylated psilocybin) into presently
unknown compounds by enzymes within the mushroom cells.
