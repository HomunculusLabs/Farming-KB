## Disadvantages of Bottle Cultivation

- **High initial investment**: Requires autoclaves, bottle racks, and often specialized filling equipment
- **Labor intensive for filling/emptying**: Despite automation potential, handling individual bottles is time-consuming
- **Limited substrate volume**: Small bottles restrict the total yield per container
- **Container cost**: Polypropylene bottles represent a significant recurring expense
- **Best suited to developed markets**: The economics work best where premium prices are available

## Suitability for Different Growers

Bottle cultivation is particularly suited to:
- Growers who already have enoki or shiitake production infrastructure
- Operations targeting premium markets with strict quality requirements
- Facilities with access to autoclave sterilization equipment
- Growers producing multiple mushroom species requiring different fruiting conditions
