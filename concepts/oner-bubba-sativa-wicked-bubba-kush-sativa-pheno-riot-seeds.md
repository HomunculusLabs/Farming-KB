# Bubba Sativa — Wicked Bubba × Bubba Kush Sativa Pheno

Bubba Sativa (also known as Wicked Bubba or Orgnkid's Sativa Pheno) is a
sativa-dominant hybrid created by Riot Seeds that bridges the gap between indica
and sativa experiences. By crossing Orgnkid's rare sativa-dominant phenotype
of the legendary Bubba Kush with their Wicked Bubba selection, Riot Seeds
produced a strain that delivers the telltale Bubba Kush flavor while offering
an unexpectedly versatile high encompassing both head and body effects.

This innovative cross represents a creative approach to exploring the genetic
diversity hidden within indica-dominant lines. It proves that even the most
indica-heavy genetic lines can produce sativa-leaning phenotypes when the right
genetic combination emerges.

## Breeder and Genetics

- **Breeder**: Riot Seeds, USA (riotseeds.nl)
- **Genetics**: Orgnkid's Bubba Kush Sativa Pheno × Wicked Bubba
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant
- **Potency**: THC 17–19%

The parentage is particularly interesting because Bubba Kush is classically
known as a heavy indica. Originally emerging from the California medical
cannabis scene in the 1990s, Bubba Kush became one of the most iconic indica
strains in existence — renowned for its sedative body stone, coffee-and-earth
[[cannabis-terpene-profile]], and compact growth habit.

The Orgnkid sativa pheno represents a rare expression of the Bubba Kush genetic
line that exhibits predominantly sativa characteristics:

- Thinner leaf structure typical of [[landrace-sativa-varieties]]
- Taller stature than standard Bubba Kush
- More cerebral effect profile than the classic indica expression

Orgnkid (a well-known breeder in the cannabis community) discovered and
stabilized this unusual sativa-dominant expression of Bubba Kush. By combining
this unique pheno with Wicked Bubba, Riot Seeds stabilized the sativa-leaning
traits while retaining the iconic Bubba Kush terpene profile that consumers
recognize and love.

## Growth Characteristics

### Plant Structure

Bubba Sativa displays a medium-height growth habit with thin, deep green
leaves — a clear visual indicator of its sativa heritage despite the Bubba
Kush lineage. The plants produce long, slightly loose buds that contrast with
the dense, tight nug structure typically associated with Bubba Kush phenotypes.

This bud structure is more characteristic of sativa-leaning plants and suggests
better airflow through the canopy compared to dense indica buds. Improved
airflow can reduce mold risk in humid growing conditions.

### Vegetative Stage

The [[cannabis-vegetative-growth-stage]] pattern is moderate — plants do not stretch aggressively
like pure sativa landraces, but they also do not stay compact like pure
indicas. This moderate growth habit makes Bubba Sativa relatively easy to
manage in indoor environments without extensive training.

Some low-stress training (LST) would likely improve light penetration and
overall yield by encouraging more even canopy development across the branch
structure.

### Flowering Stage

- **Flowering time**: 10 weeks — moderate for a sativa-dominant strain
- **Color profile**: Plants remain mostly green and yellow throughout flowering
- **[[cannabis-trichome-development-cannabinoid-biosynthesis-sites]]**: Adequate resin production for consumption and light
  hash-making
- **Difficulty**: Suitable for intermediate growers

The 10-week flowering period is reasonable for indoor operations and allows
growers to run multiple harvests per year. The moderate stretch after flipping
to 12/12 means plants won't quickly outgrow their space, making Bubba Sativa
more forgiving than many pure sativa strains.
