# Cannabis Potency-Enhanced Flowers

## Long-Term Storage of Enhanced Flowers

The stability of potency-enhanced flowers over time depends on [[snow-mold-fungi-cold-temperature-plant-pathogens]], darkness, and exclusion from oxygen minimizes the degradation of THC to CBN and preserves terpene content. Classical practitioners reported that properly stored cannabis extracts retain potency much longer than raw plant material, and the same principle applies to oil-impregnated flowers.

Vacuum-sealing in lightproof bags provides an additional layer of protection and is practical for medium to long-term storage. The vacuum removes oxygen that could contribute to cannabinoid oxidation, while the lightproof barrier prevents UV-induced degradation.

## Comparison with Direct Oil Consumption

Potency-enhanced flowers offer a different consumption experience compared to consuming the same oil directly. When oil is smoked through a glass pipe or vaporized on a nail, the full dose is delivered almost instantaneously. Enhanced flowers provide a more gradual release of cannabinoids as the flower material burns, producing an experience that more closely resembles smoking natural cannabis despite the dramatically increased potency.

This gradual release may be preferable for users who find the intensity of direct oil vaporization unpleasant. The plant material in the enhanced flowers acts as a buffer, moderating the rate of cannabinoid delivery and producing a smoother, more extended experience. The presence of the plant matrix also contributes familiar flavors and aromas that are absent from pure oil consumption.
