# JADAM Pesticide for Chewing Insects

Targets moths, caterpillars, armyworms, diamondback moth, cabbage butterflies, fruitworms, and other chewing larvae. Uses higher concentrations of [[jadam-herb-solution-jhs]] than the aphid/mite formulation, with [[jadam-wetting-agent-jwa]] for penetration through larval body walls. Developed by Hyunho Cha and Youngsang Cho.

## Formulation (per 500L / 132 gal spray tank)

**Standard moth/caterpillar control**:
- JWA: 8L (2.1 gal)
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS: 15L (4 gal)

**With disease control** (caterpillars + fungal disease):
- JWA: 8L + JS 1-2L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 15L

**Enhanced** (heavy infestation):
- JWA: 10L + Jerusalem artichoke JHS 15-20L

## Preparation Steps

1. Test water with JWA -- must be soft water
2. Mixture test: 0.5L water + 5cc each ingredient, shake, check foam + no clotting
3. Half-fill tank, add ingredients one by one stirring each
4. Top up with water to 500L
5. Filter through fine net
6. Use same day

## Pests Controlled

Tobacco moth, beet armyworm, diamondback moth, oriental fruit moth, fruitworm, cabbage moth, perilla leaf moth, slug moth larvae, citrus mealybug, arrowhead scale, and various caterpillars

## Application Timing

- **CRITICAL**: target larval stage -- JHS is contact insecticide, most effective on actively feeding larvae
- **Best time**: sunset or evening for nocturnal moths; early morning for diurnal feeders
- For heavy infestation: spray at sunset, re-spray early next morning
- Larvae are most vulnerable when small (early instar) -- scout and treat early

## Scouting

- Check undersides of leaves for eggs and small larvae
- Look for feeding damage: holes in leaves, chewed leaf margins, frass (droppings)
- Inspect 10-20 plants, focusing on growing tips and flower buds
- Pheromone traps can indicate moth flight activity -- treat when trap counts rise
- Action threshold: treat when small larvae are found on 5-10% of plants

## Key Application Notes

- For hairy pests like slug moth larvae (Thosea sinensis): spray repeatedly and wet them thoroughly -- the hair prevents good contact
- Adding 0.5kg fine red clay powder per 500L increases effectiveness
- Jerusalem artichoke JHS can be replaced with pokeweed, gingko, or Korean pasque flower root
- Without JS in the mix, can spray consecutively (every 1-2 days) for rapid control
- This formulation (no. 7) is stronger than the aphid/mite formulation (no. 6)

## JADAM vs. Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis)

JADAM does not use Bt as a separate input. Instead:
- JHS provides broad-spectrum contact insecticidal action from plant alkaloids
- JWA coats and penetrates the insect body, providing physical + chemical action
- JMS can be added to boost microbial competition on plant surfaces
- The JADAM approach is cheaper (self-made) and avoids resistance issues of single-strain Bt products
- Multiple active compounds from different herbs prevent resistance development

## Follow-Up

- After control, switch to preventive: JMS 20L + JHS 5L + JWA 3L per 500L
- Remove heavily damaged plant material to reduce re-infestation
- Rotate JHS types to prevent any pest adaptation
