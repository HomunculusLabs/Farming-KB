## Critique of Centralization

Fukuoka saw centralized systems -- whether political, economic, or agricultural -- as inherently destructive:

- Centralized food distribution requires preservatives, packaging, and long-distance transport, degrading food quality

- Centralized government makes decisions that ignore local ecological conditions

- Large-scale monoculture destroys [[fukuoka-view-of-nature-and-human-hubris]] of trying to improve upon nature.
