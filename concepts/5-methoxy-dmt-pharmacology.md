    10|# 5-Methoxy-DMT Pharmacology
    11|
    12|5-Methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine ([[5-meo-dmt]]) is a potent naturally occurring
    13|[[tryptamine]] psychedelic found [[allegro-death-and-resurrection-in-the-mushroom-cult]] venom [[allegro-star-of-the-morning-venus-and-the-sacred-mushroom]] Colorado River toad
    14|(Bufo alvarius, now Incilius alvarius) and in numerous plant species
    15|including Virola species used by indigenous peoples [[yakuruna-water-spirits-of-the-amazon]]. It is
    16|one [[allegro-colour-and-consistency-of-the-amanita-muscaria]] most powerful psychedelic substances known, producing profound
    17|[[weil-the-natural-mind-altered-states-normalcy]] at extremely low doses, and has attracted increasing research
    18|attention for its therapeutic potential.
    19|
    20|## Chemical Properties
    21|
    22|5-[[5-meo-dmt-compound-profile]] is structurally related to DMT, differing by the addition of a
    23|methoxy group at the 5-position [[allegro-names-of-the-gods-and-their-fertility-origins]] indole ring. This substitution
    24|significantly alters its pharmacological properties, increasing potency and
    25|changing its receptor affinity profile. The [[2c-t-7-compound-profile]] is a white crystalline
    26|solid at room temperature and can be administered via smoking, vaporization,
    27|insufflation, or intravenous injection.
    28|
    29|The effective dose of 5-MeO-DMT by vaporization is approximately 2 to 5
    30|milligrams, making it roughly five to ten times more potent than DMT by
    31|weight. The onset of effects is nearly instantaneous when vaporized, with
    32|the peak experience lasting 5 to 20 minutes and total duration of 30 to 60
    33|minutes. This extremely rapid onset and short duration distinguish it from
    34|most other psychedelic compounds.
    35|
    36|## Pharmacological Profile
    37|
    38|5-MeO-DMT acts primarily as a non-selective serotonin receptor agonist with
    39|high affinity for 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A receptors. Its high affinity for 5-HT1A
    40|receptors distinguishes it from other classical psychedelics, which
    41|typically show stronger selectivity for 5-HT2A receptors. This dual
    42|5-HT1A/5-HT2A agonism may contribute to its unique subjective profile.
    43|
    44|5-HT1A receptor activation is associated with anxiolytic effects, reduced
    45|anxiety, and a sense of calm and well-being. This may explain why 5-MeO-DMT
    46|experiences are frequently described as less anxiety-provoking than those
    47|of other psychedelics, despite their overwhelming intensity. The combination
    48|of 5-HT1A-mediated calm with 5-HT2A-mediated perceptual alteration creates
    49|an experience that users often characterize as blissful, ego-dissolving,
    50|and profoundly mystical.
    51|
    52|5-MeO-DMT also shows significant affinity for the sigma-1 receptor, which
    53|may contribute to its reported neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory
    54|effects. Additionally, it activates trace amine-associated receptors
    55|(TAARs), which may play a role in modulating monoamine release and
    56|contributing to the compound's overall pharmacological effects.
    57|
    58|## Subjective Experience
    59|
    60|The 5-MeO-DMT experience is qualitatively distinct from that of DMT and
    61|other classical psychedelics. While DMT typically produces richly detailed
    62|visual imagery, encounters with seemingly autonomous entities, and complex
    63|narrative content, 5-MeO-DMT is more frequently described in terms of
    64|complete ego dissolution, a sense of merging with a fundamental ground of
    65|being, and encounters with what is often described as pure consciousness or
    66|the void.
    67|
    68|Many users describe the experience as non-dual or mystical, characterized
    69|by the dissolution of boundaries between self and other, subject and object.
    70|Visual content, when present, tends toward abstract geometric patterns and
    71|intense fields of light rather than the representational imagery common in
    72|DMT experiences. The experience is frequently described as ineffable and
    73|beyond the capacity of language to convey.
    74|
    75|## Traditional and Modern Use
    76|
    77|Indigenous peoples of the Amazon basin have used 5-MeO-DMT-containing plants
    78|for centuries, primarily in snuff preparations such as yopo and epena made
    79|from [[anadenanthera-peregrina]] and Virola species. These snuffs are blown
    80|into the nostrils during ceremonial contexts and produce intense but
    81|relatively brief psychedelic experiences.
    82|
    83|The use of Bufo alvarius venom as a source of 5-MeO-DMT originated in
    84|the Sonoran Desert and has spread globally in recent decades through
    85|alternative spirituality communities. The practice of smoking dried toad
    86|secretions, sometimes called "the toad" or "bufotenine" (a misnomer, as
    87|the primary active compound is 5-MeO-DMT rather than bufotenine), has
    88|become controversial due to conservation concerns and animal welfare issues.
    89|
    90|## Therapeutic Research
    91|
    92|Emerging research has begun to investigate 5-MeO-DMT's therapeutic
    93|potential, particularly for depression, anxiety, and substance use
    94|disorders. Preliminary observational studies have reported significant
    95|reductions in depression and anxiety symptoms following a single 5-MeO-DMT
    96|experience, with effects persisting for weeks to months. Additionally,
    97|5-MeO-DMT has shown promise in reducing problematic alcohol and drug use.
    98|
    99|A 2019 survey study of 362 adults who had used 5-MeO-DMT found that
   100|approximately 80 percent reported improvements in depression and anxiety
   101|following their most meaningful experience. The mystical quality of the
   102|experience, as measured by validated scales, was found to mediate the
   103|therapeutic benefit. These findings mirror results obtained with psilocybin
   104|and support the hypothesis that mystical-type experiences underlie the
   105|therapeutic effects of classical psychedelics broadly.
   106|
   107|## Safety Considerations
   108|
   109|5-MeO-DMT carries similar risks to other classical psychedelics, including
   110|the potential for acute psychological distress, particularly in individuals
   111|with personal or family histories of psychotic disorders. The extreme
   112|potency and rapid onset of the compound when vaporized mean that dose
   113|control is critical, and accidental overdose is possible when the substance
   114|is handled in concentrated form.
   115|
   116|Physiological risks are generally low, but 5-MeO-DMT can cause transient
   117|increases in heart rate and blood pressure. Harm reduction practices include
   118|careful dose measurement, use of a trip sitter, and screening for
   119|psychiatric vulnerability. The interaction with MAO inhibitors, particularly
   120|in the context of [[ayahuasca-preparation]] analog preparations that combine 5-MeO-DMT with
   121|[[beta-carboline-chemistry]]-containing plants, requires additional caution.
