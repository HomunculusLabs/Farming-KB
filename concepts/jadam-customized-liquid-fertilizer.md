## Analyzed Crop-Specific JLF Recipes

### Wild Grass JLF
- Fill container with wild grass, fill with water, add leaf mold, close lid
- Ready in 10+ days
- Dilution: 20-300x
- Wild grass contains 1.5-2.5% nitrogen (dried weight); spring grass is higher
- Spring-collected grass has highest nitrogen content

### Purslane JLF
- One of the fastest-disintegrating wild grasses
- Decomposition almost complete in 10 days
- Effective fruit color enhancer
- Higher nitrogen content than most wild grasses due to soft tissue
- Dilution: 20-300x

### Strawberry JLF
- Fill half container with water, add leaf mold, add strawberry residues as produced
- Takes 1+ month
- pH 7.9, high in iron (5.291 mg/kg)

### Cherry Tomato JLF
- Fill half with water, add leaf mold, add cherry tomato residues
- Tomato peel is slow to disintegrate; add tomato leaves or branches to speed up
- Takes 10+ days

### Spinach JLF
- Fill container with spinach residues, fill with water, add leaf mold
- Dilution: 20-200x

## Food Waste JLF

JADAM strongly advocates recovering food waste as fertilizer. The claim that food waste is "too salty" is exposed as a fraud: after dilution and fermentation, salinity is lower than 0.002% -- far below the 2% salt content found in commercial compost bags. Food waste JLF has about 20 times more nitrogen than wild grass JLF.

A household can produce approximately 2,000L of food waste JLF per year. Use wild grass and fruit JLF as the main additional fertilizer; supplement with food waste JLF when plant growth weakens.

## Human Excreta JLF

JADAM advocates returning to the traditional use of human manure, which was treasured in Korean farming history. Human urine JLF is 30 times richer in nitrogen than wild grass JLF. One day's urine from an adult contains 30g of urea plus amino acids and inorganic salts. A family of four can produce: 1 ton human manure JLF, 2+ tons urine JLF, and 2 tons food waste JLF annually.

The Soil Association's 2010 report "Peak Phosphorus" warned that phosphate rock will peak in 2033, making human manure vital for future food security.

## Nitrogen-Rich JLF: Fish

Mackerel JLF has 3 times the nitrogen of human manure JLF and 50 times that of wild grass JLF. It has approximately 1/5 the nitrogen of urea fertilizer but contains a much wider variety of nutrients -- building a plant is like building a house, and you cannot build with cement (N) alone.

## The Nutritional Perspective

JADAM's fundamental insight: judge fertilizer from the nutritional perspective. Which fertilizer is best for tomatoes? Fertilizer made from tomatoes. The answer is that simple. Your crop residues are already the perfectly balanced nutrient source for your crops. The solution lies everywhere around you.
