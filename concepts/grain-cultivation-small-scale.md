## Seed Saving for Grain

You can save seed from your second crop of grain (unless using a hybrid). Select the best seed heads — unbroken and healthy. Let it dry in the shock for at least a month until totally dry. Then thresh and ensure it is free of leaves or twigs. Store with the same care as eating grain.
