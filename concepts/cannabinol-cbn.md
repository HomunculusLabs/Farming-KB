## Cannabinol (CBN)

Cannabinol (CBN) is a mildly psychoactive minor cannabinoid found in aged cannabis that is best known for its sedative and sleep-promoting properties. Unlike most cannabinoids, CBN is not produced directly by the cannabis plant's biosynthetic pathway. Instead, CBN is a degradation product of thc that forms through oxidative processes as cannabis flowers age and are exposed to light, heat, and oxygen over time. This unique origin — as the primary degradation product of THC — means that CBN content increases as cannabis ages and THC content decreases, making it the most abundant cannabinoid in old or improperly stored cannabis material.

## Formation and Chemistry

CBN is formed when THCA (tetrahydrocannabinolic acid) is exposed to [[serotonin-receptor-interference-psychedelic-mechanism]]**: CBN acts as an antagonist at this receptor, which may contribute to its sleep-promoting and anxiolytic properties

## Therapeutic Applications

### Sleep Enhancement

CBN is most widely used and studied for its sleep-promoting properties, making it one of the most popular minor cannabinoids in the consumer wellness market:

- Reduces sleep latency (time to fall asleep) in preclinical models
- May increase total sleep time and improve sleep quality when combined with THC
- Shows particular promise for patients with insomnia who have developed tolerance to other sleep aids
- The sedative effect is significantly enhanced when CBN is combined with THC, suggesting a synergistic interaction
- A 1975 clinical study found that a combination of CBN and THC produced greater sedation than THC alone, establishing the foundation for CBN's reputation as a sleep aid
- Unlike pharmaceutical sleep medications, CBN does not appear to produce significant next-day grogginess or impair sleep architecture at moderate doses

### Pain Management

CBN demonstrates meaningful analgesic properties through multiple receptor mechanisms:

- TRPA1 activation contributes to modulation of inflammatory and neuropathic pain pathways
- CB2 agonist activity reduces inflammatory pain through immune cell modulation
- Shows synergy with THC and CBD for pain management — the combination is often more effective than any single cannabinoid alone
- Particularly effective for chronic pain conditions when used in combination formulations
- May reduce opioid requirements in patients managing chronic pain

### Anti-inflammatory and Immune Modulation

Through CB2 receptor activation and TRP channel modulation, CBN provides significant anti-inflammatory effects:

- Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokine production including TNF-α, IL-1β, and IL-6
- Demonstrates efficacy in animal models of arthritis and [[cannabigerol-cbg]] in this regard
- Demonstrates antifungal properties against Candida species in vitro
- May be useful in topical formulations for wound care and skin infections

### Neuroprotective Effects

Emerging research suggests CBN may have neuroprotective applications:

- Delays the onset of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) symptoms in animal models
- Reduces markers of [[blesching-cannabis-neurodegenerative-diseases]]
- Shows potential for managing neurodegenerative conditions, though clinical evidence remains preliminary

### Appetite Stimulation

CBN has demonstrated appetite-stimulating effects that may complement THC's well-known hunger-inducing properties:

- Increases food intake in animal models
- May enhance the appetite-stimulating effects of THC when used in combination
