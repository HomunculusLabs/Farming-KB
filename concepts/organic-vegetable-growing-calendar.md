# Organic [[root-vegetable-storage-guide]] should be monitored for readiness

## July

### Outdoors
- Sow last batches of beans, beetroot, carrots
- Sow winter radishes, turnips
- Sow spring cabbage, spring onions (for overwintering)
- Sow lamb's lettuce, corn salad for winter

### Harvest Peak
- First early potatoes
- Broad beans
- Peas
- Courgettes (pick small and often)
- Early tomatoes (greenhouse)
- [[salad-crops-growing-guide]], herbs

## August

### Sow for Autumn/Winter
- Spinach (winter varieties)
- Oriental greens: mizuna, mibuna, mustard, pak choi
- Radishes (winter varieties)
- Turnips
- Spring onions (Japanese types)
- Lamb's lettuce, corn salad

### Harvest Peak
- Maincrop potatoes
- Tomatoes, peppers, aubergines
- Cucumbers, courgettes
- Sweetcorn
- Beans (runner, French, climbing)
- Onions, shallots, garlic (when tops fall over)
