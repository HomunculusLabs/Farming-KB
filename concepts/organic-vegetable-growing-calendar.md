# Organic [[vegetable-growing-guide]] Calendar

A month-by-month guide for temperate climate vegetable growing (USDA Zones 5-7 / UK equivalent). Adjust timing by 2-4 weeks for colder (sow later) or warmer (sow earlier) zones. This calendar pairs well with [[seed-starting-reference]] and [[coleman-season-extension-timeline-and-frost-protection]] for getting the most from your growing season.

## January-February

### Indoors (Under Cover)
- Onion sets and seeds
- Leeks
- Early cabbage (under heat)
- Tomatoes (late February, under heat)
- Peppers and aubergines (late February, under heat)
- Herbs: basil, parsley, chives

### Outdoors
- Prepare seed beds when weather permits
- Apply winter mulch to bare soil
- Prune fruit trees and bushes (dormant season)
- Plant bare-root fruit trees, bushes, and canes
- Order seeds for the season

## March

### Indoors
- Tomatoes, peppers, aubergines (if not started)
- Cucumbers, melons (under heat)
- Courgettes, squashes (late March)
- Brussels sprouts, summer cauliflower
- Celery, celeriac
- Flowers: marigolds, nasturtiums, calendula

### Outdoors (With Protection)
- Broad beans (direct sow)
- Early peas (under cloches)
- Onion sets
- Shallots
- Garlic (if not planted in autumn)
- Radishes, turnips
- Spinach, kale
- Parsnips (long taproot — sow in station sowing)
- First early potatoes (chitted)

## April

### Indoors
- Courgettes, squashes, pumpkins, cucumbers
- Sweetcorn (for transplanting in May)
- Climbing beans (for transplanting)

### Outdoors
- Second early and maincrop potatoes
- Carrots (under fleece for carrot fly)
- Beetroot
- Lettuce, rocket, salad leaves
- Radishes
- Spring onions
- Peas (direct sow)
- Swiss chard, perpetual spinach
- Turnips

## May

### Outdoors
- Sweetcorn (direct sow or transplant)
- Climbing beans, runner beans, French beans
- Courgettes, squashes, pumpkins
- Cucumbers (outdoor varieties)
- Tomatoes (plant out after last frost)
- Sweet potatoes (transplant)
- Basil (after frost)
- Melons (under cloches)
- Successional sowings of: lettuce, radish, beetroot, carrots, beans

### Succession Sowing
- Sow small batches every 2-3 weeks for continuous harvest
- Lettuce, radish, beetroot, carrots, beans, coriander

## June

### Outdoors
- Continue successional sowings
- Plant out Brussels sprouts, winter cabbage, leeks
- Sow Chinese cabbage, pak choi
- Sow Florence fennel
- Sow endive, chicory for autumn
- Earth up potatoes

### Maintenance
- Thin seedlings to final spacing
- Weed regularly
- Apply mulch around established plants
- Start harvesting: early potatoes, peas, broad beans, salad, radishes
- [[root-vegetable-storage-guide]] should be monitored for readiness

## July

### Outdoors
- Sow last batches of beans, beetroot, carrots
- Sow winter radishes, turnips
- Sow spring cabbage, spring onions (for overwintering)
- Sow lamb's lettuce, corn salad for winter

### Harvest Peak
- First early potatoes
- Broad beans
- Peas
- Courgettes (pick small and often)
- Early tomatoes (greenhouse)
- Soft fruit: strawberries, raspberries, gooseberries
- Salad crops, herbs

## August

### Sow for Autumn/Winter
- Spinach (winter varieties)
- Oriental greens: mizuna, mibuna, mustard, pak choi
- Radishes (winter varieties)
- Turnips
- Spring onions (Japanese types)
- Lamb's lettuce, corn salad

### Harvest Peak
- Maincrop potatoes
- Tomatoes, peppers, aubergines
- Cucumbers, courgettes
- Sweetcorn
- Beans (runner, French, climbing)
- Onions, shallots, garlic (when tops fall over)
