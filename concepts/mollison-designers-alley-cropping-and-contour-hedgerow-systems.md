---# Alley Cropping and Contour Hedgerow Systems

Alley cropping, also known as hedgerow intercropping, is an agroforestry
practice that integrates rows of perennial woody plants with annual or
perennial crops in the alleys between them. Mollison treats alley cropping as
a bridge between broadscale agriculture and permanent food forest systems,
offering a transition pathway that maintains crop production while building
soil, controlling erosion, and establishing the perennial framework of a
