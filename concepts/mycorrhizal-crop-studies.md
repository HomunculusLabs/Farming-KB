# Mycorrhizal Crop Studies

Research-validated results of [[mycorrhizal-inoculation]] on specific crops. Each entry notes the fungal species tested and measured outcomes. Based on [[jeff-lowenfels]]' *Teaming with Fungi*. See [[mycorrhizal-fungi]] for the biological basis and [[mycorrhizal-inoculum-production]] for making inoculum.

## Vegetables

### Cannabis (*Cannabis sativa*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus intraradices, [[funneliformis-mosseae]]
- **Results:** Increased growth, improved flower number and size, enhanced nutrient uptake. Full colonization takes up to 6 weeks from seed inoculation, but benefits begin before full colonization. Hydroponic studies show C. claroideum, C. etunicatum, F. geosporum, F. mosseae, G. microaggregatum, R. clarus, and R. intraradices all successful in rockwool — but R. intraradices alone produces similar results to multi-species mixes.

### Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus fasciculatus, R. intraradices
- **Results:** Increased growth, height, weight. R. fasciculatus improved nutrient uptake and reduced nematode gall size. Better drought and salt tolerance. More and larger fruit. Inoculation + high P resulted in earlier first flower and more flowers. Hydroponic: F. monosporus and F. mosseae increased height and dry weight significantly.

### Pepper (*Capsicum annuum*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus fasciculatus, Glomus ZAC-19, R. intraradices
- **Results:** R. fasciculatus more effective than ZAC-19, especially under reduced phosphorus. Hydroponic: increased root mass, larger fruit, more accumulated nutrients.

### Corn/Maize (*Zea mays*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus fasciculatus, R. intraradices
- **Results:** Root tissue +35% (F. mosseae), +98% (R. fasciculatus). Drought wilting delayed with R. intraradices. Better salt stress tolerance. Mycorrhizal hyphae bring more nitrogen to roots during drought.

### Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, [[rhizophagus-irregularis]], Claroideoglomus etunicatum, R. intraradices
- **Results:** Increased number and size of tubers (nearly 50% more in one study). Native Andean potatoes host 8 of 11 Glomeromycota families. Varieties show selectivity for fungal species — test strains for your variety.

### Lettuce (*Lactuca sativa*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Glomus deserticola, Rhizophagus fasciculatus
- **Results:** R. fasciculatus helped with salt stress. More nitrogen uptake overall. R. fasciculatus improved P uptake, F. mosseae improved N uptake in drought — suggesting a mixture is best.

### Onion (*Allium cepa*)
- **Fungi:** Various AMF
- **Results:** Improved phosphorus uptake. In low-P soils, uninoculated plants stunted. Better salt stress tolerance.

### Garlic (*Allium sativum*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus fasciculatus
- **Results:** Increased yields and larger bulbs in field studies. Roots readily colonized.

### Cucumber (*Cucumis sativus*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus intraradices, Claroideoglomus etunicatum, F. caledonium, R. clarus
- **Results:** Better salt stress tolerance, improved phosphorus uptake, larger cucumbers.

### Eggplant (*Solanum melongena*)
- **Fungi:** Claroideoglomus etunicatum, Gigaspora margarita
- **Results:** Protection against/delayed Verticillium wilt. C. etunicatum more effective — more and larger fruit.

### Basil (*Ocimum basilicum*)
- **Fungi:** Gigaspora margarita, Rhizophagus clarus, 13 other species
- **Results:** 400% growth increase after transplant to greenhouse. Better survival, drought-adjusting leaves. 15 species isolated from cultivated basil.

### Wheat (*Triticum spp.*)
- **Fungi:** Various AMF
- **Results:** Always [[mycorrhizae]]-dependent. Dry weight increases of 29-100%.

### Barley (*Hordeum vulgare*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus fasciculatus, Gigaspora margarita
- **Results:** +27% seed yield, +35% seed phosphorus content.

### Oat (*Avena sativa*)
- **Fungi:** Glomus T6, D13
- **Results:** Up to 70% root and 55% shoot growth increases. Less heavy metal translocation from soil to shoots.

## Legumes

### Alfalfa (*Medicago sativa*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus fasciculatus
- **Results:** Larger root mass, more nitrogen-fixing nodules. F. mosseae: twice the arbuscules, almost twice the biomass. Reduced Verticillium and Fusarium wilt incidence, fewer wilt spores in soil. More phosphorus uptake.

### Soybean (*Glycine max*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus fasciculatus, Funneliformis mosseae
- **Results:** R. fasciculatus improved yield, seed weight, pod and seed numbers. F. mosseae better drought tolerance. Slower colonization in tilled soils.

### Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*)
- **Fungi:** Funneliformis mosseae, Rhizophagus intraradices, R. fasciculatus, Scutellospora gilmorei
- **Results:** Reduced root galling and nematode populations. F. mosseae and R. fasciculatus most effective.

### Peanut (*Arachis hypogaea*)
- **Fungi:** Gigaspora margarita, Funneliformis mosseae
- **Results:** G. margarita: 6x more phosphorus uptake. Increased nematode tolerance. F. mosseae: better biomass, less root and pod rot.

## Fruits

### Strawberry (*Fragaria spp.*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus intraradices, Funneliformis mosseae, R. fasciculatus, Claroideoglomus etunicatum
- **Results:** Healthier plants, more runners, greater biomass. Better drought recovery. Reduced weevil damage. Hydroponic: better overall growth and productivity (varies by variety).

### Grape (*Vitis spp.*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus fasciculatus, Funneliformis mosseae, Glomus macrocarpum, G. deserticola
- **Results:** Increased dry weight, height, nutrient uptake, yields. Better drought stress tolerance. G. deserticola: earlier bud, flower, fruit set, and ripening.

### Raspberry (*Rubus spp.*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus intraradices, Funneliformis mosseae, R. fasciculatus, Gigaspora spp.
- **Results:** Better drought recovery. Reduced weevil damage. Note: Gigaspora species colonization depressed growth on raspberries specifically.

### Watermelon (*Citrullus lanatus*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus clarus
- **Results:** Mitigated drought stress.

## Herbs and Flowers

### Mint (*Mentha arvensis*)
- **Fungi:** Gigaspora margarita, Rhizophagus clarus, [[acaulospora-scrobiculata]], R. intraradices
- **Results:** Greatly increased size when phosphorus lacking. Essential oils and menthol levels lower without colonization. R. intraradices: greatly increased height and leaves.

### Rose (*Rosa spp.*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus intraradices
- **Results:** Improved growth of container-grown mini roses. For some varieties, as good as or better than rooting hormone.

### Petunia (*Petunia ×atkinsiana*)
- **Fungi:** Rhizophagus irregularis, Claroideoglomus etunicatum
- **Results:** 3x vegetative growth increase, 15 days earlier flowering. Nitrogen starvation triggered mycorrhizae formation even with high P availability.

### Marigold and Zinnia
- **Fungi:** Claroideoglomus etunicatum
- **Results:** Flowered faster, more flowers.

## Key Patterns

1. **Multi-species mixes often outperform single species** — different fungi excel at different functions (drought, P uptake, disease resistance)
2. **Benefits are most dramatic in low-nutrient soils** — mycorrhizae bridge the gap when P and N are limiting
3. **Disease resistance is a consistent benefit** — reduced nematode populations, wilt resistance, pathogen protection across most crops
4. **Excess phosphorus negates benefits** — high-P soils suppress mycorrhizal colonization
5. **Colonization takes 2-6 weeks** — benefits begin before full colonization but peak once established
6. **Crop variety matters** — some varieties show fungal selectivity (especially potatoes)
