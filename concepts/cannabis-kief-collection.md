## Cannabis Kief Collection

## Overview

Kief (also called resin powder, pollen, or dry-sift) consists of the detached resin gland heads of [[blesching-cannabis-resin-preparations-kief-hashish-concentrates]].

## Resin Gland Sizes

[[cannabis-microbiome-otu-abundance-vs-presence-cannabis-strains]]
- 135-150 microns: Necessary for many well-grown, very resinous strains

A micron is one millionth of a meter (1/1,000,000 m) or one-thousandth of a millimeter (1/1000 mm). This length is also referred to as a micrometer. Selecting the appropriate screen size is essential for collecting the most resin powder from specific strains being processed.

## Micron to Inch Conversion

Common sieve sizes and their approximate inch equivalents:

| Microns | Thousandths of an inch |
|---------|----------------------|
| 220     | 8.66                 |

| 160     | 6.30                 |
| 150     | 5.91                 |
| 120     | 4.72                 |

| 73      | 2.87                 |
| 66      | 2.60                 |
| 45      | 1.77                 |
| 43      | 1.69                 |
| 25      | 0.98                 |

Make sure the micron size is labeled on sieves. Color coding helps, but nothing is as reliable as the actual micron measurement of the screen.

## Dry Sieving Technique

### Equipment

- Silkscreen of appropriate micron size, stretched across a frame
- Glass surface or mirror placed below the screen
- Credit card or similar flat tool for working material
