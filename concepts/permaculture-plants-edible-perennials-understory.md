# Permaculture Edible Perennials and Food Forest Understory

The food forest understory layer contains shrubs, [[permaculture-plants-food-forest-ground-covers-bamboos]] that produce food, provide [[permplant-carob-drought-tolerant-food-tree]] and withstands some frost. In mountainous forest clearings but also grown as ornamental and fruiting hedge. Branches hang down and take root -- easy propagation.

## Understory Herbaceous Perennials

### Comfrey
Deep tap-rooted herbaceous perennial. Listed among [[permaculture-livestock-forage-systems]]. High mineral and protein content. Used as chop-and-drop mulch around fruit trees. Animals browse for self-medication.

### Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale)
Long-season bee forage providing both nectar and pollen. Edible leaves (raw or cooked), flowers (wine, fritters), and roots (coffee substitute). Deep taproot mines nutrients from subsoil. Livestock browse as self-medication.

### Chicory (Cichorium intybus)
Bee forage providing nectar and pollen. Deep taproot for nutrient mining. Edible leaves (bitter but nutritious). Important pasture species for extending flowering season and boosting [[comparison-scented-geraniums-vs-edible-flowers]] (cucumber flavour) and young leaves. Livestock self-medication herb.

### Plantain (Plantago spp)
Medicinal herb for livestock. Edible young leaves. Wound-healing properties (contains allantoin). Common in pastures worldwide.

### Yarrow (Achillea spp)
Woolly yarrow (A. lomentosa) listed as a ground cover. Medicinal herb for wound healing. Bee forage. Drought-tolerant.

## Understory Vines

### Passionfruit (Passiflora spp)
More than 40 edible species, mostly tropical to subtropical woody fruiting vines. Climb by tendrils on trellis or trees. Can inhibit supporting tree growth by reducing sunlight -- provide a clump of trees so the vine can spread. Key species:

- **Purple passionfruit (P. edulis)**: Best known, native to Brazil, subtropical
- **Sweet granadilla (P. ligularis)**: Large, sweet fruit
- **Giant granadilla (P. quadrangularis)**: Largest of the passionfruit
- **Banana passionfruit (P. mollissima)**: More cold-tolerant

## Nut Trees as Canopy with Edible Understory

### Walnut (Juglans regia)
Persian/English walnut provides canopy shade. Leaves are good butter-milk feed for livestock. Walnut is NOT a good companion to most species (allelopathic), so understory selection is important. Compatible companions include Albizia lophantha, tagasaste, garlic, lucerne, and mulberry.

### Chestnuts (Castanea spp)
- **Spanish chestnut (C. sativa)**: Major European nut crop
- **Japanese chestnut (C. crenata)**: Important Asian species
Both provide canopy and leaf litter for understory. Compatible with Acacia pulchella, A. suaveolens, Albizia lophantha, and garlic.

### Hackberry (Celtis australis)
Small to medium tree (25 m), useful shade tree. Fruits are pea-sized, sweet-flavoured, purple-black when ripe. Good addition to chicken forage systems. Understory-compatible.

### Irish Strawberry Tree (Arbutus unedo)
Evergreen tree/shrub to 7 m. Fruits take up to 12 months to ripen -- trees have flowers and fruits at all stages simultaneously. Edible red berries. Can be planted densely as a hedge. Bee forage value.

### Japanese Raisin Tree (Hovenia dulcis)
Deciduous tree to 18 m. The swollen fruit stem is edible (tastes like raisins). Used in China to annul effects of wine. Good spring honey plant.
