## Foliar Insects

**Anecdotal evidence is strong:** Nearly everyone working with AACT notes
that foliar insects "go away" when they start spraying with AACT. But
hard science data is still needed. SFI needs folks who will do tests,

## The Mechanism: Plant Quality Deters Pests

When plants have adequate biology in their soil, they produce complete
proteins and complex carbohydrates. Plants grown with healthy foodwebs in
the soil contain 3 to 10 times more protein than conventionally grown
plants. Incomplete proteins (free amino acids) attract pests -- the plant
chemistry signals vulnerability.

If free amino acids get stuck in that form and don't get moved into
protein synthesis, you will attract pests. The question is: why are amino
acids getting stuck? The answer lies in whether the soil biology can
deliver complete nutrition.
