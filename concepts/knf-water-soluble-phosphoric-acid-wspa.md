# Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-PA)

Water-Soluble Phosphoric Acid (WS-PA) is a Korean Natural Farming
input extracted from sesame stem charcoal. Phosphoric acid is a
substance of the cell nucleus and a major element of the reproductive
body of the plant. If the plant is deficient in phosphoric acid, cell
division is hindered and reproductive growth is impaired. WS-PA
provides a natural source of this essential nutrient through a simple
charcoal-percolation method.

## The Role of Phosphoric Acid in Plants

Phosphoric acid is an important element for the life of crops and is
contained in every living plant cell. It is a substance that composes
the cell nucleus and plays a fundamental role in cellular division.
When absorbed phosphoric acid moves to the bud, root tip, or seed, it
drives the cell division processes essential for growth and
reproduction.

The storage form of phosphoric acid, known as phytic acid, is the
major substance found in seeds. Beyond cellular division, phosphoric
acid is involved in photophosphorylation and electron transport in
photosynthesis and plays a role in protein synthesis.

In the context of the [[knf-nutritive-cycle-theory]], phosphoric acid
is particularly critical during the changeover period when plants
transition from vegetative to reproductive growth. This period, also
called "morning sickness," is when plants crave sour nutrients, and
phosphoric acid compounds fulfill this need.

## Why Sesame Stems

The stem of sesame (Zingly) contains large amounts of phosphoric
acid. Sesame stems are burned into charcoal, and the phosphoric acid
is percolated from the charcoal using water. The stems are burned and
the fire is put out when large flames appear, converting them to a
charcoal state that preserves phosphoric acid content.

## Preparation of WS-PA

Materials needed:
- Charcoal from sesame stems: 1 kg
- Water: 5 liters
- Jar or glass jar
- Cloth bag
- Porous paper and rubber band
- Air tube for aeration

Steps for preparation:
1. Make charcoal out of sesame stems. Burn the stems and put out
   the fire when large flames appear. Natural phosphoric acid is
   abundant in these stems.
2. Put 1 kg of sesame stem charcoal in a cloth bag and dip it in
   5 liters of water to dissolve phosphoric acid, creating a crude
   liquid of natural phosphoric acid.
3. Allow approximately seven days for extraction, though the time
   varies depending on the temperature of the surroundings.
4. Use an air tube to blow air into the water once every two days.
   This aeration helps the phosphoric acid dissolve more
   effectively into the water.

## How to Use WS-PA

WS-PA is applied with specific dilution ratios depending on whether
it is used alone or in combination with other inputs:

1. When used alone: Dilute 700 ml of crude WS-PA liquid with 20
   liters of water.
2. When used with water-soluble calcium: Dilute WS-PA with water
   in the ratio of 1:1000.
3. The mixture is applied primarily during the changeover period
   when crops transition from vegetative to reproductive growth.

## When to Apply WS-PA

Timing of WS-PA application is tied to the growth stage of the crop
according to the nutritive cycle:

1. Apply WS-PA during the changeover period. It promotes the
   differentiation of flower buds, resulting in high fertility and
   yield.
2. WS-PA improves the sugar content of fruits, making it valuable
   during reproductive growth.
3. Apply WS-PA when the colors of leaves turn light or dark green,
   which can indicate phosphoric acid imbalance.

## Symptoms of WS-PA Deficiency

The source provides detailed information about recognizing phosphoric
acid deficiency:

- WS-PA moves easily within the plant, going to vigorous new leaves
  first. Deficiency symptoms appear mainly on old leaf petioles and
  veins.
- New leaves grow poorly, becoming smaller and dark green.
- Flower growth is noticeably suppressed, bearing fewer fruits.
- Symptoms are often dormant within the body, making recovery
  difficult once exterior symptoms appear.
- Fruit skin becomes thick and acid content increases.

## Environmental Conditions

The source specifies optimal conditions for WS-PA preparation and
storage:

- Optimum temperature range: 23 to 25 degrees Celsius.
- Select a cool and shaded place with no direct sunlight for both
  preparation and storage.
- These conditions support the percolation process and maintain
  solution stability.

## Key Principles

- Phosphoric acid is essential for cell division and reproductive
  growth in all plants.
- Sesame stem charcoal is the preferred natural source of
  phosphoric acid in KNF.
- WS-PA is primarily applied during the changeover period to
  support the transition from vegetative to reproductive growth.
- Deficiency symptoms often remain hidden internally, making
  preventive application important.
- Aeration during preparation enhances the dissolution of
  phosphoric acid into water.

## Applications

- Promoting flower bud differentiation during the changeover period
- Improving fruit sugar content and quality
- Supporting cell division at root tips, buds, and seeds
- Enhancing photophosphorylation and photosynthesis efficiency
- Treating leaf color abnormalities indicating phosphoric acid
  imbalance
- Supporting protein synthesis and anabolite transport in crops
