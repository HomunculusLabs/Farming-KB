## Optimal Drying Methods

### Room Temperature Drying

In laboratory settings, mushrooms are usually dried at room temperatures. This is the safest method for preserving alkaloid content:

- Slow but gentle
- No heat damage to psilocybin
- Reduces risk of oxidation
- May take several days to weeks depending on humidity

### Freeze-Drying (Lyophilization)

Freeze-dried mushrooms have a highly porous texture that actually causes the alkaloids to [[query-can-fungi-break-down-plastic-pollution]] relatively quickly if stored at room temperature (20 C). For this reason:

- Freeze-dried samples for biochemical analysis are stored at -10 C (14 F) prior to alkaloid extractions or chromatography testing
- Freeze-drying is NOT recommended for long-term storage at room temperature
- Despite the convenience, freeze-drying is inferior to room-temperature drying for preservation of potency

### Oven Drying

Oven drying at temperatures below 50 C is acceptable but must be monitored carefully. The risk of exceeding the temperature threshold makes this method less reliable than air drying.
