## Improving Yield: Key Factors

### Spawn Rate

The best way to improve yields is simply to increase the spawn rate. Commercial growers
who generate their own spawn can afford to use 10-12%+ spawning rates instead of the
3-6% rates typical when purchasing spawn. Higher spawn rates narrow the window of
opportunity for contaminants and directly enhance yields. Using the spawn itself as the
vehicle of supplementation is far better than trying to boost the nutritional base of
the substrate prior to inoculation.

### Strain Selection

The strain is the most important key to success — all other factors pale in comparison.
Strains vary enormously in their conversion efficiency. A cultivator should evaluate
multiple strains of the same species and select the one that consistently achieves the
highest B.E. on their specific substrate and under their environmental conditions.
