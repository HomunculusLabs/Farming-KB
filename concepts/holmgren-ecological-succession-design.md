## Pulsing and Non-Equilibrium Dynamics
Holmgren emphasizes that many ecosystems do not follow linear
successional pathways toward a stable climax. Instead, many exhibit
pulsing dynamics — periodic disturbances that reset succession and
create opportunities for different species. Fire, flood, herbivore
pressure, and windthrow all create pulses that maintain landscape
diversity. In permaculture design, planned disturbance through grazing,
coppicing, controlled burning, and selective harvesting mimics these
natural pulses. The concept of pulsing also applies to social and
economic systems, where cycles of expansion and contraction create space
for innovation and renewal.
