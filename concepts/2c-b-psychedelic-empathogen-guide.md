# 2C-B Psychedelic Empathogen Guide

2C-B (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine) is a psychedelic/empathogen developed by [[mescaline-peyote-and-san-pedro-guide]] and [[turner-lsd]], [[tihkal-harmala-alkaloids]], and [[dm-turner]] rates intensity 2 to 7, varying with dosage and individual sensitivity.

## Dosage and Administration

2C-B should be ingested, preferably on an empty stomach. It is extremely dosage sensitive — amounts required are so small that a sophisticated scale is needed to accurately weigh a single dose.

With "street" 2C-B it is hard to know exactly how much is in a capsule, likely causing unexpected heavy trips if dosage information is not made available. People who have a quantity of 2C-B often dilute it in an inert powder before measuring individual doses for greater accuracy.

| Dose | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 8 mg | Starting test dose for new users |
| 12-16 mg | "Museum level" — enhanced perception, functional in public |
| 20-24 mg | Full [[leary-psychedelic-experience-bardo-mapping]] with visuals and intensified feelings |
| 25-35 mg | Strong psychedelic experience (standard for most users) |
| 40 mg | Unpleasant experience reported by experienced users (Gracie and Zarkov) |
| 50 mg | Required for low-sensitivity individuals (Turner's personal dose) |
| 100 mg | Frightening but no permanent damage (Shulgin overdose reports) |

In PIHKAL, Shulgin lists the dosage range as 16 to 24 mg. Another source lists 12 to 30 mg. No literature recommends a dose above 30 mg.

Individual sensitivity varies enormously — some people (including Turner) need 50 mg for effects others get at 25 mg. This is due to unusually low sensitivity to 2C-B and other substances in the phenethylamine family. Start with a small dose and work up.

## The Experience

### Low Dose ("Museum Level")
At a low dose, the experience resembles a speedy acid high — enhanced perception appropriate for going to a museum or moving about in public. Hardly worth discussing as a full psychedelic experience.

### Full Dose — Positive Qualities
- Definitely an "up" experience with lots of physical energy
- Extraordinary visuals: intense vivid colours, intricate patterning reminiscent of mescaline, charged with electricity
- Visuals can appear as chaos before becoming part of a vast cosmic order
- On a good dose, one can lie back for hours and trip on watching their own mind
- Unique awareness of the mind's "assembly" or "machine" language — seeing how neural signals process (80-90% connecting as expected, 10-20% going elsewhere)
- Deep, transformative empathogenic quality in ~25% of experiences — much more meaningful than ecstasy's "shallow emotional bliss"
- Simultaneously strengthens and dissolves aspects of ego/identity, often producing positive self-image changes, including the idea that the physical body is a sacred manifestation of creative consciousness
- Enhances sexual perception and performance; can maintain excited state for hours
- Allows continual exploration of different types of feelings, unlike ecstasy's repetitive or predictable emotional patterns
