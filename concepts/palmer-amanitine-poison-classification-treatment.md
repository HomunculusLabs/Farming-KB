## The Importance of Elapsed Time in Diagnosis

Palmer emphasized that the elapsed time between a suspected meal
and the onset of symptoms is the most important diagnostic
indicator available to physicians. Persons poisoned by mushrooms
are typically unable to identify the species that caused their
sickness, and amateurs frequently collect multiple species while
classifying them all as one kind.

The classification framework provides critical triage guidance:
- **Short elapsed time** (minutes to hours): Category one or two
poisoning. High hope for recovery with appropriate treatment.
- **Long elapsed time** (eight to fifteen hours): Amanitine
poisoning. The most common and most fatal form.
