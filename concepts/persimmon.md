### Diospyros dignya (Black Sapote, Chocolate Pudding Fruit)

An evergreen native of Central America, having a very sweet fruit with
chocolate brown flesh. This species extends the range of edible
Diospyros into tropical permaculture systems.

## Cultivation Tips

- Plant in full sun for best fruit production
- Mulch well to retain soil moisture
- Water regularly during the growing season
- Space trees at least 5 m apart
- Protect young trees from frost
- Non-astringent varieties are easier for fresh eating
- Astringent varieties need to be fully soft before eating
- Fruit can be dried for long-term storage
- Makes an excellent dual-purpose ornamental and food tree

## Uses and Processing

Fresh persimmons can be eaten when fully ripe and soft. The astringent
varieties must be allowed to ripen completely until the flesh has a
jelly-like consistency. Non-astringent varieties can be eaten while still
firm, like an apple. Persimmons can be dried, made into jams, jellies,
and preserves, or used in baking.

The fruit stores well and can be dried for long-term preservation.
Dried persimmons have a concentrated sweet flavour and were traditionally
valued in Asian cultures as a winter food. The fruit can also be frozen
successfully for later use.
