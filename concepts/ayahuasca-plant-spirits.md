## Overview

Central to Pablo Amaringo's visionary universe is the concept that every
plant possesses a conscious spirit — a sentient intelligence that
communicates with humans through dreams, visions, and ceremonial
experiences. These spirits, known as *madres* (mothers) or *dueños*
(owners), are the true teachers of Amazonian plant medicine. They reveal
healing knowledge, protective songs, and cosmological wisdom to
vegetalistas who diet with their species. Amaringo's paintings in
*[[icaros]] that cleanse the body and spirit of
contamination, making her particularly important for treating
conditions caused by sorcery or spiritual pollution.

### Renaquilla

*Renaquilla* (*Clusia rosea*) is a distinctive tree known for its
strangling fig growth pattern and powerful spiritual associations. The
renaquilla spirit mother is associated with binding, containment, and
treating conditions involving spiritual intrusion. She teaches
vegetalistas how to trap and neutralize harmful spiritual entities, and
her icaros are used to contain and extract sorcerous implants from
patients' bodies. In Amaringo's paintings, she often appears surrounded
by intricate root-like patterns representing her binding powers.

### Bobinzana

*Bobinzana* (*Calliandra angustifolia*) is a beautiful shrub with
distinctive pink powder-puff flowers. Its spirit mother is associated
with feminine healing energy, emotional balance, and the heart. The
bobinzana spirit appears as a gentle, nurturing feminine figure
radiating warmth and compassion. She teaches icaros that open the heart,
facilitate emotional release, and promote loving connections. Bobinzana
is considered one of the most accessible and gentle plant teachers,
making it an appropriate early dieta for apprentice vegetalistas.

## Spirit Hierarchies and Relationships

The plant spirit world depicted by Amaringo is a complex hierarchical
ecology. Some plant spirits serve others; some are subordinate to animal
or elemental spirits; and some exist in specific supernatural dimensions
accessible only through particular gateways. The spirit mothers
communicate with each other, forming alliances and hierarchies mirroring
ecological relationships of the physical forest. A vegetalista's
effectiveness depends partly on navigating these hierarchies and calling
upon the right spirits for each situation.

## Elemental and Cosmic Spirits

Beyond the plant mothers, Amaringo's visions reveal a vast population of
non-plant spirits: elemental beings associated with water, earth, air,
and fire; animal spirits serving as messengers and protectors; and
cosmic entities governing celestial movements and universal law. All
these entities interact with the plant spirit world, creating an
intricate web of relationships the vegetalista must understand and
navigate.

## The Visionary Landscape

In Amaringo's paintings, the spirit world is a structured landscape
with distinct regions, cities, rivers, mountains, and forests — all
existing in supernatural dimensions interpenetrating the physical
Amazon. Plant spirits inhabit specific locations, and the vegetalista
travels through these regions during ayahuasca visions, visiting the
homes of different spirit mothers and receiving teachings in their
supernatural environments. The richness of Amaringo's visionary
cartography suggests the spirit world is at least as complex and
geographically diverse as the physical forest it parallels.
