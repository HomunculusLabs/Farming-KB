## DOI (2,5-Dimethoxy-4-Iodoamphetamine)

## Overview

DOI is compound #67 in PiHKAL — an extremely potent, long-lasting psychedelic amphetamine with a 4-iodo substitution on the 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine scaffold. DOI holds particular significance in neuroscience as a radiolabelled research tool for mapping 5-HT2 receptor distribution in the brain. With active doses of 1.5-3.0 mg and durations of 16-30 hours, it represents one of the most powerful members of the DO (dimethoxy) psychedelic amphetamine family.

## Chemical Identity

- **Chemical name**: 2,5-Dimethoxy-4-iodoamphetamine
- **Molecular formula**: C11H16INO2
- **Molecular weight**: 321.16 g/mol
- **Structure**: Amphetamine with iodine at the 4-position, methoxy groups at 2 and 5 positions
- **Appearance**: White crystals (hydrochloride salt)
- **Melting point**: 200.5-201.5°C (hydrochloride)
- **Taste**: The water solution of the hydrochloride salt has a "slightly sweetish taste" (Shulgin)

The iodine atom is the heaviest halogen commonly used in psychedelic amphetamine chemistry, and its presence gives DOI unique properties as both a psychoactive compound and a radiopharmacological research tool.

## Dosage and Duration

- **Dosage range**: 1.5-3.0 mg orally
- **Threshold**: 0.5-1.0 mg
- **Common dose**: 1.5-2.5 mg
- **Strong dose**: 3.0-3.5 mg
- **Duration**: 16-30 hours
- **Onset**: 1-3 hours

At 3.0 mg, Shulgin rated DOI as a "clear, clean psychedelic" with excellent eyes-closed imagery. The duration is extraordinarily long — at 3.5 mg, users reported being at +++ for 3-4 hours with significant effects persisting past 18 hours. Even at the 16-hour mark, distinct residual awareness was common.

## Synthesis

The synthesis of DOI proceeds through a phthalimide protection-deprotection sequence:

1. **Phthalimide formation**: 2,5-DMA free base is heated with phthalic anhydride to ~150°C, forming N-(1-(2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-2-propyl)phthalimide as off-white crystals (mp 105-106°C).

2. **Iodination**: The phthalimide is dissolved in warm acetic acid and treated with iodine monochloride (ICl) at ~40°C for 2 hours. The product, N-(1-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-iodophenyl)-2-propyl)phthalimide, precipitates as fine white crystals with a slight purple cast (mp 103-105.5°C).

3. **Deprotection**: The iodinated phthalimide is treated with hydrazine hydrate in ethanol under reflux overnight, precipitating 1,4-dihydroxyphthalizine. The filtrate is extracted and the free base recovered.

4. **Salt formation**: The free base is dissolved in ether and saturated with HCl gas, yielding DOI hydrochloride as white crystals.

The phthalimide route was chosen to ensure clean, specific iodination at the 4-position without side reactions that could occur with direct bromination or iodination of the free amine.

## Qualitative Effects

### Perceptual Effects
- Excellent eyes-closed imagery with "clearly delineated patterns, pictures, and colors"
- Described as "perfect for an artist" — imagery is precise and paintable
- Slow-motion imagery persisting into the next day, even during sleep attempts
- Enhanced visual clarity without the "sparkle" characteristic of LSD

### Emotional and Cognitive Effects
- Effects tend toward melancholy or reflective sadness at lower doses
- At higher doses (3.0+ mg), a clear +++ with full psychedelic depth
- Capacity for "lateral movement with reality" — entering altered perspectives while retaining the body
- Some users report difficulty connecting with emotions at moderate doses
- One report described a unique "S-shaped" archetype of orgasm during erotic experience

### Dose-Response Characteristics
- **0.6 mg**: Mild spacey light-headedness, slow perception of time, generic sadness
- **1.6 mg**: Depressing experience, vague sadness, inability to connect with emotions
- **3.0 mg**: Clear, clean psychedelic, excellent imagery, total physical ease
- **3.5 mg**: Full crashing +++, ability to move "sideways with reality"

## Optical Isomer Studies

As with all psychedelic amphetamines possessing a chiral center, DOI"s isomers show dramatically different potency:

- **R-isomer (1.0 mg)**: Clear ++ from hours 2-8. "Sensible" and functional. Easy sleep at 15 hours.
- **R-isomer (2.3 mg)**: Unquestionable +++. Slight down mood towards end. Distinctly aware of residual effects well into the next day.
- **S-isomer (6.3 mg)**: Benign 1.5+ at 2 hours, leveling at ++. Would need 9-10 mg for a +++.

The R-isomer appears approximately 4x more potent than the S-isomer in humans, though rat discrimination studies suggest only a 2x difference. This discrepancy between animal and human data is noted by Shulgin as significant.

## Comparison with DOB and DOC

DOI, DOB, and DOC are the three halogenated members of the DO psychedelic amphetamine family:

- **DOB (bromine)**: 1-3 mg, 18-30 hours. Most widely known, most often associated with adverse events.
- **DOI (iodine)**: 1.5-3 mg, 16-30 hours. Cleanest subjective profile, premier research tool.
- **DOC (chlorine)**: 1.5-3 mg, 12-24 hours. Shortest duration of the three, "pretty much the same potency."

All three share the pattern of extreme potency, long duration, R-isomer dominance, and heavy-atom utility for radiolabelling studies. The choice of halogen affects potency, duration, and the specific receptor subtypes engaged.
