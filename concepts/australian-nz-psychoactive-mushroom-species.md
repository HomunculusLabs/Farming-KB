## Arrest Statistics and Enforcement Patterns

Dr. Hall reported that 60 people in Australia were charged with offenses involving possession or sales of psilocybin mushrooms during 1972. However, charges against several individuals were later withdrawn when no psychoactive compounds were found in the confiscated specimens. One year later, Hall stated that approximately 74 persons were charged in 1972. By 1973, only 27 persons were charged with psilocybin offenses, suggesting a decline in enforcement or use.
