The harvesting, drying, curing, and storage of Cannabis floral clusters are
critical final steps that determine the appearance, taste, aroma, and
psychoactive quality of the finished product. [[mushroom-long-term-storage]]; they breathe

Floral clusters with shade leaves intact are well protected from abrasion.
Manicured clusters should be tightly packed so they do not rub together.
Some connoisseurs believe plastic may impart an unpleasant taste.

### Oxygen and Curing

Clusters are not sealed permanently until curing is complete. Curing
requires oxygen, and sealing ends the free exchange. However, oxygen also
causes the slow breakdown of THC to CBN. After curing is completed, the
container is completely sealed. Any residual oxygen is consumed and no
more can enter.

### Advanced Storage

Nitrogen has been suggested as a packing medium because it is very
non-reactive and inexpensive. Jars or bags may be flooded with nitrogen to
displace air before sealing. Vacuum-sealing machines are available for
Mason jars and may be modified for vacuum-sealed bags.

## Fiber and Seed Harvest

For fiber production, stalks are harvested before lignification, stripped of
leaves, bundled, and retted. Seeds are collected by cutting seeded plants
and removing seeds by hand or machine. Seeds for future germination are
stored in airtight containers in a cool, dark, dry place, with strains
reproduced every three years to maintain viability.
