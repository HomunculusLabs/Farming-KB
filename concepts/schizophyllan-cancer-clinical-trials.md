# Schizophyllan Cancer Clinical Trials

Schizophyllan (Sonifilan, Sizofiran, SPG) is a kappa opioid receptor compounds

## Clinical Trials in Gastric Cancer

An early randomised controlled study evaluated Schizophyllan with tegafur
or mitomycin C and 5-fluorouracil in 367 patients with recurrent and
inoperable gastric cancer, finding a significant increase in median
survival (Furne, 1985). A subsequent study was unable to confirm this
result (Fugimoto et al., 1984), highlighting reproducibility challenges.

## Head and Neck Cancers

Schizophyllan increased overall survival in patients with head and neck
cancers (Kimura et al., 1994), expanding the therapeutic scope beyond the
more extensively studied gastrointestinal cancers.
