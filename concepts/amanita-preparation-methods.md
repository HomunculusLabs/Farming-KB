# Amanita Muscaria Preparation Methods

Practical [[duggar-mushroom-spawn-preparation-techniques]] for Amanita muscaria, drawn primarily from Donald E. Teeter's "Amanita Muscaria: [[amanita-muscaria-herb-of-immortality-soma-hypothesis]]" (2007) and Siberian ethnographic records. Proper preparation is critical: raw Amanita contains ibotenic acid (nauseating, agitating) which must be converted to muscimol (sedative, psychoactive) through drying and/or heating.

Related: [[amanita-muscaria-guide]], [[soma-hypothesis]]

## Fundamental Chemistry

- **Ibotenic acid** (C5H6N2O4): Glutamate receptor agonist. Causes nausea, confusion, visual distortion, agitation. Found in fresh and improperly prepared Amanita.
- **Muscimol** (C4H6N2O2): GABA-A receptor agonist. Produces sedation, dissociation, dreamlike states, euphoria. The desired active compound.
- **Conversion**: Drying at room temperature to 120F converts ibotenic acid to muscimol by decarboxylation (removal of CO2). Above 130F the mushroom "cooks" and chemicals begin degrading.
- **Muscimol content**: Approximately 1mg muscimol per gram of properly dried specimen.

## Drying Protocol

1. Harvest caps and stems separately
2. Dry at room temperature to 120F maximum
3. Shaded cool drying produces more muscimol than sun drying
4. Sun drying produces trace muscazone (less potent than muscimol)
5. Age dried material 2-3 months to allow hydrazine (present in all fresh mushrooms) to dissipate
6. Store in airtight container away from moisture and light

## Ambrosia (Cold Water Extract)

The simplest preparation. Place dried cap pieces upside-down in a jar; break stems into 1/2-1 inch pieces on top. Add cool drinking water (reverse-osmosis or distilled preferred) to just cover. Soak 3+ hours. Pour off amber liquid through a strainer. Repeat with fresh water for a second extraction. Press pieces to recover remaining liquid. Sip slowly.

## Sacred Wine

Substitute grape juice for water in the ambrosia method (~28g dried per quart juice). Soak 8-12 hours, strain and press. Add a few drops of vinegar per cup to increase potency. Dilute 2-4 parts water to 1 part wine before drinking.

**The dilution paradox**: Adding water to Amanita wine extract paradoxically increases potency and transforms the effect from sedative/dissociative to purely psychedelic (similar to psilocybin or LSD). Teeter theorizes muscimol's polar molecules form chains in concentrated solution that produce the sedative effect; water breaks these chains into individual psychoactive units. This matches ancient practice: Greek and Roman texts describe diluting wine 3:1 to 20:1 with water.

## Seer's Milk (Milk Fermentation)

Place small dried pieces in a bowl. Add 3/4 to 1 cup whole milk per 5-7g dried specimen. Cover with cloth. Leave at 60-70F. Ferments in approximately 12 hours (flies are attracted when ready -- a traditional indicator). Strain and sip slowly. Milk does not readily spoil for 24+ hours, which Teeter cites as evidence of antibiotic action. Significantly stronger than herb or ambrosia alone.

## Holy Oil (Topical)

Powder dried specimens finely, sift through cloth. Place powder in a vial, cover with olive oil to make a thin paste. Let soak 1-2 hours. Apply a fingertip to the center of the forehead. Effects begin in minutes. Also effective as a topical antibiotic for skin infections, insect bites, and infected wounds.

## Living Bread

Cooked pearled barley is inoculated with dried Amanita powder. The mycelium colonizes the barley in 60-72 hours at 65-72F in a sealed container. The resulting "fungus cake" tastes like malt, can be ground into flour, and functions as a renewable sacrament. One dried mushroom cap can produce several pounds of living bread in under a month. This preparation must be dried again before consumption to convert any ibotenic acid back to muscimol.

## Resurrection (Cultivation)

Dried Amanita muscaria resurrects into living mycelium within 3 days when rehydrated in liquid food sources. Teeter documented this with grape juice, honey water, and boiled barley water. The resurrected mycelium appears as aggressive, hairy, bluish-gray to white growth. Key observations:
- Requires oxygen (sealed jars produce slow/no growth and foul odors)
- Prefers 60-80F; 65-70F for 3-day resurrection
- Mycelium is pure snow white in darkness; turns bluish-gray on light exposure
- The original specimen pieces turn dark brown to almost black and grow back together
- Remarkably hardy: even powdered or crushed specimens can resurrect
- Subsequent resurrections happen faster than the first
- Must be re-dried before consumption (ibotenic acid reconversion)

The antibiotic action of Amanita is so strong that Teeter carried out all [[amanita-muscaria-resurrection-experiments]] in non-sterile conditions without any contamination appearing.

## Urine Recycling

Traditional Siberian practice documented among the Koryak and Chukchi peoples. After consuming Amanita muscaria, muscimol is excreted unchanged in the urine while ibotenic acid is metabolized. Drinking the urine produces psychoactive effects with reduced nausea. Multiple "pass-throughs" were common in ceremonial contexts. This practice matches Vedic descriptions of Soma urine being consumed, and is a key piece of evidence for the [[soma-hypothesis]].

## Critical Safety Warnings

- **Carbonated beverages**: Muscimol can be reconverted to ibotenic acid in the presence of carbonated water. Do not consume beer, soda, or sparkling wine before or after Amanita -- this reverses the drying conversion and causes nausea, vomiting, cramps, and stupor with no psychoactive effect.
- **Fresh mushrooms**: Contain hydrazine (also found in rocket fuel). Drying and aging for 2-3 months removes it.
- **Dosage**: Pleasurable range 3.5-10g dried (3-10mg muscimol). Maximum 14g per 200lb person. 1/8 oz per 50lb body weight. Accidental overdose is nearly impossible at approximately 1mg muscimol per gram.
- **Potency averaging**: Use pieces from multiple specimens for predictable effects; individual mushrooms vary widely.
- **Color varieties**: Red, orange, yellow, and white varieties all produce similar effects when identically dried. No significant chemical differences found between specimens from Italy, Holland, South Africa, Japan, [[guzman-psilocybin-mushrooms-north-america]], and Siberia.
- **NOT recommended for self-experimentation** without expert guidance.
