## Seed-Borne Endophytes
### In Grasses
Seed-borne transmission is the primary colonization route for
clavicipitaceous endophytes. The fungus is located in the seed
coat and near the embryo, ensuring that germinating seedlings are
immediately colonized from the outset of plant development.
### P-Endophytes
Species of Phialophora and Gliocladium are nonsystemic but also
seed-borne (An et al. 1993). These represent an intermediate
pattern: transmitted through seeds but not achieving systemic
colonization of the host plant.
