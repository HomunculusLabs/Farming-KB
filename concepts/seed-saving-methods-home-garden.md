# Seed Saving Methods for Home Gardeners

- Working from too small a gene pool, using too few plants, and making mistakes about which plants to take out will ruin the variety within two generations.
- Minimum population sizes vary by species:
  - Extremely vigorous outcrossers: as few as a dozen plants.
  - Corn: as low as 50 plants.
  - Most other outcrossing species: 200 plants minimum.
  - Self-pollinating species: as few as 5-10 plants (no inbreeding depression risk).

### Selection Methods

- Select wisely which plants contribute seed and which are removed.
- Selection is necessary to prevent the variety from showing too many unwanted characteristics.
- Don't hold the gene pool too rigorously to an exact form -- there's been too much inbreeding already.
- After only a few generations of inbreeding, a variety becomes so weak that its seeds barely sprout and grow so poorly that it becomes nonproductive.
- Make your selections within the first two weeks of emergence for traits like vigor and emergence rate.

## Isolation Distances

To prevent unwanted crossing between varieties of the same species:

- Self-pollinating crops (tomatoes, peppers, lettuce, beans, peas): minimal isolation needed, though 50 feet is recommended if perfect purity is a concern.
- Insect-pollinated outcrossers: isolation of half a mile (800 meters) or more.
- Bee-pollinated crops (carrots, okra, chicories): at least half a mile for pure seed.
- Brassicas: at least 50 feet between varieties of chicory; much more for cabbage family crops.

## Specific Crop Methods

### Beans and Peas

- Pick pods individually when the stem end withers.
- Finish drying in a large bowl or on a tarp.
- Store when completely dry.

### Carrots

- Overwinter selected roots in the ground or in moist sand.
- Replant in spring in a configuration about 6 by 4 feet (762 by 120 cm) [[comparison-chinampas-vs-raised-bed-gardening]].
- A 25-foot-long bed with a double row of carrots can produce enough seed for a neighborhood for a decade.
- Carrot seed dried down fully and kept dry can last many years.
- Need at least 50 plants in the seed-making population because the species is bee-pollinated.

### Lettuce

- Lettuce is self-pollinated; sow seed crop in mid-autumn and overwinter.
