## Results: Mycelial Growth Enhancement

On malt agar, the brassinosteroid-treated cultures showed mycelial growth
that was **2 to 3 times faster** than untreated controls. This dramatic
acceleration of the colonization phase has significant practical implications
for cultivation, where the colonization period represents one of the main
time bottlenecks.

The mechanism by which brassinosteroids accelerate fungal cell growth is not
fully understood, but is hypothesized to involve similar pathways to their
action in plant cells — likely modulation of cell wall extensibility and

## Morphological Changes

A notable and unexpected finding was that brassinosteroid treatment altered
the morphology of the resulting fruit bodies: treated mushrooms **lacked the
typical membranous annulus** (ring) on the stem. The annulus is a remnant of
the partial veil that covers the gills during development, and its absence
indicates that brassinosteroids may influence developmental gene expression
related to veil formation and rupture.
