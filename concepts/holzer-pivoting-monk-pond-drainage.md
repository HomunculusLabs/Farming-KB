# Holzer Pivoting Monk - Pond Drainage Invention

The Holzer Monk is a simple but cleverly designed mechanical device for
controlling pond overflow and drainage. It was developed by [[comparison-sepp-holzer-vs-permaculture-design]] needed a system that would be self-cleaning, simple to
construct, and require minimal maintenance.

## The Pivoting Monk Design

The Holzer Monk is essentially a pivoting pipe outlet set into the dam wall of
a pond or lake. The term "monk" comes from the traditional hydraulic
engineering term for a vertical outlet pipe through a dam.

### Construction

- A pipe is set into the dam body at the desired overflow height.
- The outer end of the pipe is fitted with a pivot mechanism that allows it to
  rotate or tilt.
- By pivoting the pipe, the water level at which overflow occurs can be
  adjusted. This gives the operator precise control over the water level.
- The design is mechanically simple, using the weight of the water and gravity
  to function, requiring no pumps or electrical power.

### Advantages

- Self-regulating: once set, the system works by gravity alone.
- Adjustable: the water level can be changed by pivoting the pipe to different
  angles or heights.
- Low maintenance: the simple mechanical design has few parts that can fail.
- Blockage-resistant: the open end and flow design minimise debris collection.
- No external power needed: works purely on hydraulic principles.
