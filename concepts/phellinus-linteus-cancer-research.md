## Clinical Applications in Digestive System Cancers

Practitioners of TCM have reported effects for oesophageal, duodenal,
colorectal, and hepatocellular cancers. These reports are consistent with
the mushroom's traditional association with digestive system health. Tumour
responses and symptomatic improvement have mainly been reported in
combination with conventional chemotherapy as an adjuvant or neo-adjuvant
(Mizuno, 2000).

## Korean National Research Programme

A major Korean collaboration involving industry, government, and academic
laboratories uses fermenter-cultivated mycelium from several P. linteus
strains (Aizawa, 1998). The major polysaccharide product has been approved
as a medicine and manufactured by Korean New Pharmaceutical Co. since 1997.
This programme represents one of the most comprehensive national-level
investments in a single medicinal mushroom species for cancer therapy,
comparable to Japan's development of Psk Polysacolaride K from
[[psk-psp-polysaccharide-peptides]], the strongest evidence supports adjunctive
use rather than monotherapy. A considerable number of Korean and Japanese
patents have been filed, and further [[medicinal-mushroom-extraction-preparation]]
methods used for commercial mushroom polysaccharide products.
