### GMO Concern

Most canola and soybean oils are genetically modified (GMO). In Korea, there is no law regulating GMO in natural oil. To avoid GMO, farmers can use:
- Non-GMO canola oil
- Sunflower oil
- [[youngsang-cho]] experimented extensively to find a radically easier method:
- Goal: no heating, only 10 minutes of mixing, 3 days of sitting
- Completely ignored temperature requirements
- Continued experimenting with different proportions of water, caustic potash, and oil
- Finally found the "magic ratio"

The key insight: "Maybe it was somebody's intention to make [soap-making] difficult after all. If people try to make natural soap for themselves but soon are exhausted, then they will resort to buying natural soap."

## JWA Recipe (100 L / 26 gal)

### Ingredients
- Canola oil: 18 L (4.76 gal)
- Caustic potash (KOH 90%): 3.2 kg (7.05 lb)
- Water: 82.5 L (21.8 gal) total
  - Initial water: 2.5 L (0.66 gal)
  - Added water later: 80 L (21 gal)

### Equipment
- 110 L (29 gal) heat-resistant plastic container (minimum)
- Electric drill with clean blade
- Gloves, safety goggles, mask, boots, jacket
- Clean long wooden stick
- Never use aluminum containers

### Step-by-Step Process

**Day 1 (about 10 minutes active work):**
1. Do JWA water test to confirm soft water
2. Put 2.5 L water in container, add 3.2 kg caustic potash
3. Close lid, roll container on side to melt (produces heat, small gas)
4. Verify complete dissolution
5. Add 18 L canola oil, mix with electric drill until thin mayonnaise (about 10 minutes)
6. Close lid, let sit for 3 days (temperature rises from 60C to 83C, then declines)

**Day 4:**
7. Open -- solid like butter
8. Add 20 L soft water, drill-mix to remove lumps from walls
9. Do NOT over-mix at this stage (can become white cream)
10. Add 60 L soft water, stir thoroughly by hand with wooden stick
11. Let melt for 24 hours
12. Pour into storage bottles, seal tightly

## Storage and Quality

- Tightly sealed, no expiration date
- Quality actually improves with age
- Store above freezing; can thaw and reuse if frozen
