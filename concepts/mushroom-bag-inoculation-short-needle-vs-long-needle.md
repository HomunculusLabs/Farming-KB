# Mushroom Bag Inoculation: Short Needle vs Long Needle Methods

The Jarrold boil-a-bag method describes two inoculation
techniques for introducing spore solution into straw bags.

The choice depends on available equipment and skill level.

Both methods require rigorous [[cultivator-sterile-technique-and-agar-culture]].

## Pre-Inoculation Preparation

Sterilised bags must be prepared before inoculation.

For the short needle method, tape supports run from the
filter can down to the bag base on each side.

These prevent bag collapse when the needle penetrates.

Tape is placed over two inoculation points below the
filter, with looped tape ready to seal holes after
the needle is withdrawn.

For the long needle method, only the filter can sides
need taping to secure the assembly to the bag.

## Short Needle Method

Used when only a standard 4 cm needle is available.

The needle penetrates directly through the bag wall
below the filter can level.

Each inoculation point is swabbed with propyl alcohol
using a cotton wool bud.

The needle tip is flame-sterilised until red hot, which
allows it to pierce the plastic bag wall easily.

5 cc of spore solution is injected into each of two
inoculation points on opposite sides of the bag.

As the needle is withdrawn, pre-positioned tape is
immediately rolled over the hole to seal it.

This sealing step is critical — without it, the
puncture becomes a contamination vector.

The short needle method is more labour-intensive and
carries higher [[fungicide-treated-seed-contamination-risk-mushroom-substrate]].

## Long Needle Method

Described as much easier, quicker, and more trouble-free
than the short needle approach.

A long custom needle passes through the vermiculite
filter can directly into the [[oyster-straw-substrate-preparation]].

The filter can lid is lifted, the flame-sterilised
needle is inserted through the filter medium, and
10 cc of spore solution is injected.

The needle is withdrawn and the filter lid closed.

The key advantage is that the filter medium acts as
a sterile barrier around the needle path.

Contaminants cannot easily travel through packed
vermiculite and rock wool to reach the substrate.

## Needle Construction

Standard needles at 4 cm are too short for the long
method.

A longer needle is fabricated by removing the original
needle from its plastic holder with pliers.

Thin stainless steel tubing from model shops is
inserted as a replacement.

Heating the tube tip before insertion aids fitting.
