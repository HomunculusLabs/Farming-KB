## Mushroom Bag Inoculation: Short Needle vs Long Needle Methods

The Jarrold boil-a-bag method describes two inoculation
techniques for introducing spore solution into straw bags.

The choice depends on available equipment and skill level.

Both methods require rigorous [[fungicide-treated-seed-contamination-risk-mushroom-substrate]].

## Long Needle Method

Described as much easier, quicker, and more trouble-free
than the short needle approach.

A long custom needle passes through the vermiculite
filter can directly into the [[oyster-straw-substrate-preparation]].

The filter can lid is lifted, the flame-sterilised
needle is inserted through the filter medium, and
10 cc of spore solution is injected.

The needle is withdrawn and the filter lid closed.

The key advantage is that the filter medium acts as
a sterile barrier around the needle path.

Contaminants cannot easily travel through packed
vermiculite and rock wool to reach the substrate.

## Needle Construction

Standard needles at 4 cm are too short for the long
method.

A longer needle is fabricated by removing the original
needle from its plastic holder with pliers.

Thin stainless steel tubing from model shops is
inserted as a replacement.

Heating the tube tip before insertion aids fitting.
