Unlike a moldboard plow, the keyline plow leaves the soil surface largely undisturbed while opening deep channels for water infiltration.

These channels allow rainfall to penetrate rather than run off, recharging groundwater and reducing erosion.

The plow is typically set to work at 4 to 6 inches below the surface on first pass, deepening over successive years as soil structure improves.
