### Biological Controls

Biological [[neem]] oil contains azadirachtin, which disrupts pathogen development
and strengthens plant cell walls. It has broad-spectrum activity against
fungal diseases like powdery mildew and rust, plus insecticidal
properties. Copper-based sprays (Bordeaux mixture, copper hydroxide)
are approved for organic use and provide protective barriers on leaf
surfaces, though copper can accumulate in soil with repeated use.
Sulfur-based products control powdery mildew and mites. Potassium
bicarbonate sprays raise leaf surface pH, creating an inhospitable
environment for many fungal pathogens.
