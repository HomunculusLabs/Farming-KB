### Humid, Rainy Climates

Best adapted to high humidity and disease pressure:
- **Satsuma mandarin** — Very disease-resistant
- **Valencia orange** — Handles humidity better than navels
- **Grapefruit** — Thrives in humid subtropics
- **Calamondin** — Virtually disease-free

Avoid: Lemons (prone to melanose, scab in high humidity).
