## The Precision Method (Oven Extraction)

For consistent, reproducible results:

1. **Preheat your oven** to 170 degrees Fahrenheit (77 degrees Celsius) — the lowest setting on most ovens.
2. **Combine herb and oil** in an oven-safe glass dish or mason jar (remove the metal lid).
3. **Cover loosely** with foil to prevent moisture from entering.
4. **Infuse for 2 to 3 hours**, checking occasionally that the oil is not bubbling or smoking.
5. **Remove, cool, strain, and bottle.**

## Preventing Rancidity and Spoilage

The most common failure in herbal oil making is spoilage. Follow these rules:

- **Start with dry herbs.** Fresh herbs (except St. John's wort) introduce water, which is the enemy of oil preservation. If you must use fresh, wilt them for 12 to 24 hours first to reduce moisture.
- **Add vitamin E oil** (1 percent of total volume) as a natural antioxidant. This significantly extends shelf life.
- **Store in amber glass** in a cool, dark place. Clear bottles on a sunny shelf will go rancid quickly.
- **Label with the date.** Most herb-infused oils last 6 to 12 months. Jojoba-based infusions can last 2 to 3 years. Smell your oil before each use — rancid oil smells like old paint or crayons.
- **Use a clean, dry jar** and avoid introducing moisture during straining. Even a drop of water in the finished oil can start microbial growth.
