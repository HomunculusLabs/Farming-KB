## How to Store Cannabis Seeds for Long-Term Viability

## Short Answer

Cannabis seeds remain viable longest when stored in a cool, dark, and dry environment.

For short-term storage of up to a year, a dark drawer in an airtight container with a desiccant pack works well. For medium-term storage of one to five years, the refrigerator provides ideal conditions. For long-term preservation beyond five years, freezing seeds in vacuum-sealed bags with silica gel can maintain high germination rates for a decade or longer.
