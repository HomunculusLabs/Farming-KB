## What are the biggest mistakes?
The biggest straw bale mistake is letting water reach and remain in the bales.
Other straw bale mistakes include using hay, using moldy bales, plastering wet walls, and omitting overhangs.
The biggest earthbag mistake is underestimating labor and wall weight.

## Can I combine them?
Yes.
Earthbags can form foundations, stem walls, retaining walls, root cellars, or thermal-mass elements.
Straw bales can form insulated above-grade walls protected by a good roof.
This hybrid approach can use the strengths of both systems.
It also increases design complexity, so transitions between materials need careful detailing.
## Bottom Line
Use straw bale for dry, well-roofed, highly insulated buildings.
Use earthbag for massive, durable, fire-resistant, low-cost structures where heavy labor is acceptable.
Use both only when each material is placed where its strengths actually matter.
When in doubt, build a small noncritical structure first and monitor how it handles rain, heat, cold, plaster, and maintenance.
