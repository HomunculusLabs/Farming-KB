## Harvesting at the Right Time

Harvesting age is critical for bamboo's structural integrity.

Poles should be harvested between three and five years of age for maximum strength.

Younger culms have not yet developed sufficient lignin and are prone to splitting and decay.

Older culms become brittle and lose their elasticity.

The best time to harvest is during the dry season when starch content is lowest.

Low starch content makes the bamboo less attractive to borers and other insects.

Cutting close to the ground with a sharp saw and removing branches immediately after harvest reduces damage.
