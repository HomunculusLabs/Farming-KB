# How to Make Korean Natural Farming Inputs

## Short Answer
Korean Natural Farming inputs are made by collecting and culturing beneficial
indigenous microorganisms and fermenting plant materials to extract their nutrients.
The core inputs include IMO (indigenous microorganisms), FPJ (fermented plant juice),
FAA (fish amino acid), OHN (oriental herbal nutrient), WCA (water-soluble calcium),
and LAB (lactic acid bacteria serum). Each input is made from locally available
materials using simple fermentation techniques, making KNF one of the most accessible
and affordable natural farming systems in the world for any scale of agriculture.

## Detailed Explanation

Korean Natural Farming, developed by Cho Han Kyu, is a complete agricultural system
that replaces purchased fertilizers and pesticides with farm-made biological inputs.
The [[complete-knf-input-reference]] offers quick
lookup for preparation details and application rates. The system is built on the
principle that nature already provides everything plants need; the farmer's job is to
facilitate natural biological processes rather than override them with synthetic
chemical inputs.

The foundation of KNF is indigenous microorganisms, or IMO. [[imo-collection-multi-stage-cultivation]] guide describes the progression through
IMO-2, IMO-3, and IMO-4 stages, each expanding the microbial population and adapting
it to your specific soils and crops through successive culturing steps.

Fermented plant juice, or FPJ, extracts growth hormones, amino acids, and minerals
from fast-growing plant tips. The [[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]] page covers variations using different plant species
selected for specific purposes such as flowering stimulation or pest resistance.

Fish amino acid, or FAA, provides nitrogen and amino acids through the fermentation
of fish waste. The [[cho-natural-pest-control-methods]] page details the preparation process and application rates for different
pest pressures. OHN can be combined with other inputs and applied as a foliar spray
to strengthen plant defenses against insects and disease throughout the growing season.

Water-soluble calcium, or WCA, addresses calcium deficiencies by extracting calcium
from eggshells or animal bones using natural fermentation acids. The
[[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] page describes how to culture lactobacillus from the natural environment. LAB is
used to boost beneficial microbial populations in the soil and on leaf surfaces,
suppress pathogenic organisms, and accelerate decomposition of organic matter in
compost piles and soil amendments. It can be combined with FPJ for enhanced foliar
feeding or added to compost piles to dramatically accelerate breakdown of organic
materials.

Brown rice vinegar, also known as BRV, is another important KNF input used primarily
as a natural herbicide and growth regulator. The [[cho-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]] guide explains how
diluted seawater can be applied to crops to supply the full spectrum of minerals
found in ocean water, including magnesium, sulfur, boron, and dozens of other
micronutrients that support enzyme function and plant health. This input is
particularly valuable for crops grown in depleted or leached soils where trace
mineral deficiencies limit crop quality and nutritional content.

The [[chos-global-natural-farming-input-reduction-methods]] page describes how experienced
KNF practitioners gradually reduce their reliance on purchased inputs over time as
their farm ecosystems become self-regulating and biologically self-sufficient.

## Key Points
- IMO is the foundation of KNF, collected locally and cultured through four progressive stages
- FPJ extracts plant growth hormones and nutrients from fast-growing tips using brown sugar
- FAA provides bioavailable nitrogen through fish waste fermentation for vegetative growth
- OHN acts as a natural pest deterrent and plant immune booster from fermented herbs
- All inputs are made from locally available materials at minimal cost using simple protocols
