# How to Make Korean Natural Farming Inputs

## Short Answer
Korean Natural Farming inputs are made by collecting and culturing beneficial
indigenous microorganisms and fermenting plant materials to extract their nutrients.
The core inputs include IMO (indigenous microorganisms), FPJ (fermented plant juice),
FAA (fish amino acid), OHN (oriental herbal nutrient), WCA (water-soluble calcium),
and LAB (lactic acid bacteria serum). Each input is made from locally available
materials using simple fermentation techniques, making KNF one of the most accessible
and affordable natural farming systems in the world for any scale of agriculture.

## Detailed Explanation

Korean Natural Farming, developed by Cho Han Kyu, is a complete agricultural system
that replaces purchased fertilizers and pesticides with farm-made biological inputs.
The [[cho-natural-farming-inputs-overview]] provides a comprehensive catalog of all
inputs and their functions, while the [[complete-knf-input-reference]] offers quick
lookup for preparation details and application rates. The system is built on the
principle that nature already provides everything plants need; the farmer's job is to
facilitate natural biological processes rather than override them with synthetic
chemical inputs.

The foundation of KNF is indigenous microorganisms, or IMO. [[indigenous-microorganisms-imo]] are collected from your local environment using a simple rice trap method.
Cooked white rice is placed in a wooden or bamboo box in a shaded area near
undisturbed natural land. Within three to five days, the rice will be colonized by
local fungi and bacteria that are naturally adapted to your specific climate and soil
conditions. This colonized rice is then mixed with brown sugar at a one-to-one ratio
by weight and fermented for about a week to produce IMO-1. The
[[imo-collection-multi-stage-cultivation]] guide describes the progression through
IMO-2, IMO-3, and IMO-4 stages, each expanding the microbial population and adapting
it to your specific soils and crops through successive culturing steps.

Fermented plant juice, or FPJ, extracts growth hormones, amino acids, and minerals
from fast-growing plant tips. The [[fermented-plant-juice-preparation-and-application]]
method involves harvesting actively growing shoot tips of plants like comfrey,
mugwort, or bamboo in the early morning when sugar content is at its peak. The plant
material is mixed with an equal weight of brown sugar and fermented in a covered
container for seven to fourteen days. The resulting liquid is diluted at roughly one
teaspoon per liter of water and used as a foliar spray or soil drench. The
[[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]] page covers variations using different plant species
selected for specific purposes such as flowering stimulation or pest resistance.

Fish amino acid, or FAA, provides nitrogen and amino acids through the fermentation
of fish waste. The [[cho-fish-amino-acid-preparation]] method uses fish scraps, heads,
and intestines mixed with brown sugar at a one-to-one ratio. The mixture ferments
for several weeks, breaking down proteins into bioavailable amino acids that plants
can absorb immediately through leaves or roots. FAA is particularly effective during
the vegetative growth stage when nitrogen demand is highest, but should be reduced
or eliminated during flowering to avoid excessive vegetative growth at the expense
of fruit and flower production.

Oriental herbal nutrient, or OHN, serves as a natural pest deterrent and immune
booster for crops. It is made by fermenting garlic, ginger, licorice, cinnamon, and
angelica in separate batches using different alcohol bases, then combining the
finished extracts into a single concentrated product. The [[cho-natural-pest-control-methods]] page details the preparation process and application rates for different
pest pressures. OHN can be combined with other inputs and applied as a foliar spray
to strengthen plant defenses against insects and disease throughout the growing season.

Water-soluble calcium, or WCA, addresses calcium deficiencies by extracting calcium
from eggshells or animal bones using natural fermentation acids. The
[[cho-water-soluble-calcium-and-potassium]] guide explains how to roast and crush
eggshells, then steep them in brown rice vinegar for several days to extract
bioavailable calcium that plants can absorb through foliar application or root
uptake. This is particularly important for preventing blossom end rot in tomatoes
and peppers, and for building strong cell walls in all crop plants.

Lactic acid bacteria serum, or LAB, is produced from rice wash water and milk through
a simple natural fermentation process. The [[beneficial-indigenous-microorganisms-bim]] page describes how to culture lactobacillus from the natural environment. LAB is
used to boost beneficial microbial populations in the soil and on leaf surfaces,
suppress pathogenic organisms, and accelerate decomposition of organic matter in
compost piles and soil amendments. It can be combined with FPJ for enhanced foliar
feeding or added to compost piles to dramatically accelerate breakdown of organic
materials.

Brown rice vinegar, also known as BRV, is another important KNF input used primarily
as a natural herbicide and growth regulator. The [[cho-brown-rice-vinegar-preparation]] method involves fermenting brown rice with beneficial microorganisms to produce
acetic acid. When diluted and sprayed on weeds, BRV acts as a natural contact
herbicide that burns weed foliage without harming established crop plants when
applied carefully. It can also be used in very dilute concentrations as a foliar
spray to stimulate plant metabolism and enhance nutrient uptake.

Seawater and fermented seawater provide trace minerals that are often deficient in
terrestrial soils. The [[cho-seawater-and-fermented-seawater]] guide explains how
diluted seawater can be applied to crops to supply the full spectrum of minerals
found in ocean water, including magnesium, sulfur, boron, and dozens of other
micronutrients that support enzyme function and plant health. This input is
particularly valuable for crops grown in depleted or leached soils where trace
mineral deficiencies limit crop quality and nutritional content.

The [[cho-plant-growth-stage-treatments]] page provides a complete schedule showing
which inputs to apply at each stage of plant development, from seed treatment through
vegetative growth, flowering, and fruiting. Following this schedule ensures that
plants receive the right nutrients and biological support at the right time. The
[[chos-global-natural-farming-input-reduction-methods]] page describes how experienced
KNF practitioners gradually reduce their reliance on purchased inputs over time as
their farm ecosystems become self-regulating and biologically self-sufficient.

## Key Points
- IMO is the foundation of KNF, collected locally and cultured through four progressive stages
- FPJ extracts plant growth hormones and nutrients from fast-growing tips using brown sugar
- FAA provides bioavailable nitrogen through fish waste fermentation for vegetative growth
- OHN acts as a natural pest deterrent and plant immune booster from fermented herbs
- All inputs are made from locally available materials at minimal cost using simple protocols
