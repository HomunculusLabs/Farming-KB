## Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Matters | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| **Wild-caught breeding stock** | Parasites, disease, poor genetics | Buy from a reputable heliculture farm |
| **No calcium supplement** | Thin shells, [[ingham-coli-compost-tea-food-safety]] risk | Always purge 5–7 days before processing |
| **No escape prevention** | Snails climb walls and disappear | Bury fencing, add outward lip at top |
