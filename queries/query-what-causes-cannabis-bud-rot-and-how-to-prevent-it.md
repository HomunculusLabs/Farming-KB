Bud rot is one of the most devastating diseases a cannabis grower can face,
capable of destroying an entire harvest in a matter of days if left unchecked.
The fungus responsible, Botrytis cinerea, is ubiquitous in the environment and
thrives in the cool, humid microclimates that develop inside dense cannabis
buds. Understanding what causes bud rot and how to prevent it is essential
knowledge for every cultivator, whether growing indoors, outdoors, or in a
greenhouse.

The primary environmental trigger for bud rot is excessive humidity combined
with inadequate airflow. During the [[query-what-causes-airy-loose-cannabis-buds-and-how-to-prevent-them]] become
