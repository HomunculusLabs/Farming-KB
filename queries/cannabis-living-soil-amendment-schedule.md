# Cannabis Living Soil Amendment Schedule

A synthesized week-by-week schedule combining [[knf-preparations]], [[nutritive-cycle-theory]], [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]], [[cannabis-flowering-stage]], [[cannabis-living-soil-guide]], and [[cannabis-plant-nutrition]]. Designed for indoor living soil beds with KNF supplement inputs.

## Pre-Plant (1-2 Weeks Before)

- Mix base soil (40% peat, 30% aeration, 20% compost, 10% castings) with amendments per cubic yard: 2c bone meal, 2c kelp meal, 2c rock phosphate, 2c gypsum, 1c oyster shell flour, 1c alfalfa meal, 1/2c malted barley, 1-2c neem/crab meal
- Inoculate with [[indigenous-microorganisms-imo]] (IMO-2 or IMO-4): soil drench at 1:500
- Apply [[fermented-mixed-compost-fmc]] at 1-2 kg/m\u00b2
- Mulch with straw/cardboard, moisten, let sit 7-14 days for microbial activation
- Environment: 65-75\u00b0F soil temp, 40-60% moisture

## Vegetative Weeks 1-2 (Seedling/Clone)

- **KNF**: FPJ 1:1000 foliar spray (mugwort/bamboo). OHN 1:1000 for immune support. LAB 1:1000 if conditions damp
- **Water**: Dechlorinated, room temp. Keep surface moist but not saturated
- **Light**: 18/6, PPFD 200-400 \u00b5mol, blue-heavy spectrum (30-50% blue)
- **Environment**: 70-82\u00b0F day, 60-70\u00b0F night, 60-70% humidity
- **Top-dress**: None \u2014 soil amendments carry through
- **Observe**: Cotyledon health, first true leaf color, root development

## Vegetative Weeks 3-4 (Established Growth)

- **KNF**: FPJ 1:800-1000 foliar or soil drench. FAA 1:1000 if leaves pale (N-deficient). LAB 1:1000 weekly
- **Compost tea**: First AACT application \u2014 vermicompost base + unsulfured molasses (1 tbsp/gal)
- **Training**: Begin LST, top at week 3-4 if desired (see [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]])
- **Top-dress**: Worm castings (1/2 cup per plant) if growth is slow
- **Observe**: Stem thickness, internode spacing, leaf color (dark green = adequate N)

## Vegetative Weeks 5-8 (Full Veg, Pre-Flip)

- **KNF**: FPJ 1:800 weekly. FAA 1:1000 every other week if needed. LAB 1:1000 biweekly. OHN 1:1000 for pest prevention
- **Compost tea**: Every 1-2 weeks, add kelp and [[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]] as microbial food
- **Top-dress**: Compost or castings at week 6 if vigor drops
- **Light**: PPFD 400-600 \u00b5mol. Plants should fill allotted space before flip
- **Observe**: Canopy evenness, root health, signs of nutrient needs
- **Decision**: Flip to 12/12 when plants have 6-8+ nodes and space is filled

## The Flip (Day 0 of Flower)

- Switch light to 12/12. Shift spectrum toward red-heavy over 3-5 days
- Ensure complete darkness during off period \u2014 light leaks cause hermaphroditism
- This is the start of Cho's **changeover period** \u2014 the most critical nutrient transition

## Flower Weeks 1-2 (Stretch + Changeover)

- **KNF \u2014 Changeover inputs**: WS-CaPo 1:500-1000 (calcium phosphate). WS-PA 1:30. Sour FFJ 1:1000 (unripe fruit). BRV 1:200-500. WS-Ca 1:1000
- **STOP**: FAA (nitrogen). Reduce FPJ. Reduce LAB
- **Compost tea**: Continue, but no nitrogen-rich additives
- **Environment**: Begin lowering humidity to 50-55%. PPFD 800-1000 \u00b5mol
- **Observe**: Stretch rate, early pistil formation, leaf yellowing (normal \u2014 "morning sickness")

## Flower Weeks 3-4 (Early Bud, P/K Top-Dress)

- **KNF**: FFJ from ripe fruits 1:1000. OHN 1:1000 for IPM. WS-Ca 1:1000
- **Top-dress**: 1/2c bone meal + 1/2c langbeinite/kelp + 1/4c oyster shell flour per plant
- **Compost tea**: Monthly, unsulfured molasses only (no nitrogen)
- **Defoliation**: Remove large fan leaves blocking bud sites (max 20% of foliage)
- **Environment**: 68-80\u00b0F day, 60-68\u00b0F night. Humidity 45-50%
- **Observe**: Calyx formation, resin production starting, pistil color

## Flower Weeks 5-6 (Mid Flower, Resin Production)

- **KNF**: FFJ 1:1000. OHN 1:1000. WS-K 1:30 (potassium for starch/sugar). WS-Ca 1:1000 (prevents overgrowth, hardens buds)
- **Seawater**: 1:30 for full-spectrum minerals
- **Top-dress**: Langbeinite + oyster shell flour if needed
- **Stop**: All nitrogen inputs, LAB (promotes leaf growth over flowers)
- **Environment**: VPD 1.0-1.5 kPa. Cooler nights (60-65\u00b0F) boost terpenes
- **Observe**: Bud density, trichome development, aroma intensity

## Flower Weeks 7-8+ (Late Flower)

- **KNF**: OHN 1:1000. WS-Ca 1:1000. Reduce all other inputs
- **Pre-harvest formula** (final 2 weeks): OHN 1ml + WS-Ca 2ml + Seawater 30ml per 1000ml water. Apply at 15 days and 2 days before harvest
- **Water**: Reduce frequency \u2014 let soil dry more between waterings
- **Environment**: 65-75\u00b0F day, 58-65\u00b0F night, 40-45% humidity
- **Observe**: Trichomes shifting clear \u2192 milky \u2192 amber. Pistils browning from tips inward
- **Harvest decision**: See [[cannabis-harvest-and-curing]] for trichome indicators

## Post-Harvest (Soil Recovery)

- Remove main stem and root ball (or leave roots to decompose for no-till)
- Top-dress: 1-2\" fresh worm castings, kelp meal, neem meal
- Re-mulch with fresh straw/cardboard
- Apply IMO-2 soil drench at 1:500 to re-inoculate
- Moisten lightly, let rest 2-4 weeks before next cycle
- Soil improves for 3-6 cycles; after 6+, blend 30% fresh base mix
