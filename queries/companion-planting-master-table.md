# Companion Planting Master Table

Comprehensive reference for 30+ common garden plants with companion relationships, pest-repelling properties, beneficial insect attraction, and nutrient contributions. Synthesized from [[companion-planting]], [[seed-starting]], and permaculture source texts.

## Tomatoes

| Plant | Good Companions (Reason) | Bad Companions (Reason) | Pest-Repelling | Attracts | Nutrient Contribution |
|-------|------------------------|------------------------|----------------|----------|----------------------|
| **Tomato** | Basil (repels hornworm, improves flavor); borage (repels hornworm, attracts pollinators); marigold (repels root-knot nematodes); carrots (shades carrots, carrots aerate soil); parsley (attracts parasitic wasps); onions (repel spider mites) | Fennel (inhibits growth); walnut (juglone); cabbage family (both heavy feeders, compete); potato (blight sharing) | Aromatic foliage repels some insects | Hoverflies (with parsley) | Moderate feeder — benefits from N-fixing neighbors |

## Herbs

| Plant | Good Companions (Reason) | Bad Companions (Reason) | Pest-Repelling | Attracts | Nutrient Contribution |
|-------|------------------------|------------------------|----------------|----------|----------------------|
| **Basil** | Tomato (improves flavor, repels hornworm); pepper; asparagus | None significant | Repels aphids, spider mites, hornworm | Bees, butterflies | Aromatic mulch when pruned |
| **Dill** | Cabbage, lettuce, onion, cucumber (attracts parasitic wasps) | Carrots (cross-pollination affects seed) | Attracts wasps that kill caterpillars | Parasitic wasps, hoverflies, bees | None — insectary plant |
| **Rosemary** | Beans, cabbage, carrots (repels carrot fly); sage | None significant | Repels cabbage moth, carrot fly, bean beetle | Bees | Woody perennial, deep roots |
| **Thyme** | Cabbage, tomato, eggplant | None significant | Deters cabbage moth, garden moth | Bees | Ground cover, soil holder |
| **Mint** | Cabbage, tomato (repels cabbage moth, flea beetle) | Plant in containers — invasive | Repels flea beetles, aphids, cabbage moth | Bees | Aggressive spreader — contain |
| **Oregano** | Tomatoes, peppers; all brassicas | None | General pest deterrent | Bees, beneficial insects | Perennial herb |
| **Cilantro** | Potatoes, tomatoes, anise; let bolt for flowers | None | Attracts hoverflies (aphid predators) | Hoverflies, parasitic wasps | Goes to seed = coriander |

## Flowers

| **Nasturtium** | Tomato, squash, cabbage, fruit trees (trap crop for aphids) | None significant | Repels cucumber beetle, squash bug; traps aphids away from crops | Aphids (trap), hummingbirds | Edible leaves/flowers; trap crop |

## Pest-Repelling Companion Quick Reference

| Pest | Repelled By | Companion Plant Near |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Aphids | Nasturtiums, garlic, chives, mint | Tomatoes, peppers, cabbage, fruit trees |
| Cabbage moth | Sage, rosemary, thyme, mint | All brassicas |
| Carrot fly | Onion, leek, sage, rosemary | Carrots |
| Colorado potato beetle | Horseradish, flax, catnip | Potatoes, eggplant |
| Cucumber beetle | Radish, nasturtium, tansy | Cucumbers, squash |
| Flea beetle | Garlic, mint, catnip | Eggplant, radish, brassicas |
| Japanese beetle | Garlic, tansy, rue, catnip | Beans, raspberries |
| Mexican bean beetle | Rosemary, savory, nasturtium | Beans |
| Spider mites | Garlic, onions, cilantro | Tomatoes, peppers |
| Squash bug | Nasturtium, tansy, radish | Squash, pumpkins |
| Tomato hornworm | Borage, basil, marigold | Tomatoes |
| Root-knot nematodes | Marigold (French type) | Tomatoes, peppers, eggplant |
| Slugs | Ducks (best), garlic, rosemary | All low-growing crops |
