# Can Fungi Break Down Plastic Pollution?

## Short Answer
Yes, certain species of fungi can break down various types of plastic, including
polyurethane (PUR), polyethylene terephthalate (PET), and polystyrene. These fungi
produce specialized enzymes such as cutinases, esterases, and lignin-degrading
peroxidases that can cleave the polymer bonds in plastic materials, converting them
into simpler, potentially biodegradable compounds. This capability is a growing
