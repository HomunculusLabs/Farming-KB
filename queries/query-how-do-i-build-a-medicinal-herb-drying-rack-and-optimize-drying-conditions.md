## How Do I Build a Simple Wall-Mounted Herb Drying Rack?

### Materials

- 2×4 lumber or 1×3 pine boards (frame)
- Hardware cloth or stainless steel mesh screen (¼-inch openings)
- Window screening (fiberglass or stainless) for fine-leaf herbs
- Wood screws or exterior-grade nails
- Screw eyes and S-hooks or paracord for hanging
- Small fan (optional but recommended)

### Frame Construction

1. **Cut frame pieces**: Four boards to your desired size. A 24"×36" rack dries a substantial harvest while fitting most walls. For larger operations, build multiple racks.
2. **Assemble the frame**: Butt-joint the corners and secure with 2-inch wood screws. Pre-drill to prevent splitting. Add a center cross-brace for racks wider than 30" to prevent sagging.
3. **Attach the mesh**: Staple hardware cloth tightly across the frame. Pull it taut—sagging mesh causes herbs to touch and blocks airflow. For fine herbs like thyme or chamomile flowers, layer window screening over the hardware cloth.
4. **Mount the rack**: Install screw eyes into the top corners. Use S-hooks to hang from a wall-mounted bracket, ceiling hook, or closet rod. The rack should hang freely for [[mycelium-running-forestry-practices-and-mushrooms]] in the same space**: Mushrooms release enormous amounts of spores during drying, which can contaminate herb batches and cause respiratory irritation.
