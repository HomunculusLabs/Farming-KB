## Week 1-2: Seedling

**Feeding**: None -- the pre-amended soil has sufficient nutrients. Water with dechlorinated water only. Mist surface to keep moist. No comparison-biofertilizers-and-soil-inoculants-vs-compost-tea-guide yet.

**Environment**:
- Temp: 72-80F day / 65-70F night
- Humidity: 65-80% (humidity dome for first 5-7 days)
- Light: 18/6 photoperiod, PPFD 200-400 umol/m2/s, blue-heavy spectrum
- Distance: 24-30 inches from seedlings (raise if stretching)

**What to watch for**:
- Stretching (move light closer, remove dome earlier)
- cervantes-damping-off-seedling-disease-prevention (reduce watering, improve airflow)
- Seed shell stuck on cotyledons (mist to soften, remove with tweezers)
- Yellow cotyledons after true leaves appear (normal senescence)

**Tasks**: Set up oscillating fan (gentle sway, no bending). Check soil moisture daily -- don't let it dry out completely but don't waterlog. Mark varieties.

---

## Week 3-4: Early Vegetative

**Feeding**: First compost tea (ACT) at week 3 if growth seems slow. 1 tbsp molasses/gallon in tea. Optional [[lactic-acid-bacteria-lab]] foliar spray for immune support. Still no heavy N inputs.

**Environment**:
- Temp: 70-82F day / 60-70F night (10F drop ideal)
- Humidity: 55-70% (gradually reduce)
- Light: PPFD 400-500 umol/m2/s, 18/6 or 20/4
- CO2: Ambient (400-600 ppm)

**What to watch for**:
- Nitrogen deficiency (pale lower leaves) -- top-dress worm castings
- Slow root development -- let soil dry slightly between waterings
- Uneven canopy -- begin LST to level the tops

**Tasks**: Top plants at 4th-5th node (week 3-4) if desired. Start LST with soft ties. Remove any dead or yellowing leaves. Scouting for pests on leaf undersides. See [[cannabis-topping-training]].

---

## Week 5-8: Late Vegetative / Transition

**Feeding**: Top-dress with compost or worm castings at week 5 if growth stalled. Compost tea every 1-2 weeks. [[fermented-plant-extract-fpe]] or [[fish-amino-acid-faa]] at 1:500 dilution as foliar if desired. [[arbuscular-mycorrhizal-colonization-patterns]] should be well established by week 6+.

**Environment**:
- Temp: 70-82F day / 60-70F night
- Humidity: 50-65%
- Light: PPFD 500-600 umol/m2/s, blue-heavy spectrum
- Airflow: Oscillating fans on all plants; stems should sway

**What to watch for**:
- Root-bound symptoms (rapid wilting, yellowing, [[cannabis-nutrient-lockout-and-ph]])
- Overwatering (droopy leaves, slow growth, algae on soil surface)
- Pest activity ([[cervantes-spider-mites-identification-control]], gnats, thrips) -- see [[cannabis-pest-management]]
- Plants outgrowing their space (flip to flower or train harder)

**Tasks**: Final LST adjustments. ScrOG tucking if using screen. Evaluate plant height -- indica flip at 12-18 inches, sativa at 18-24 inches. Ensure 6-8+ main branch nodes before flipping. Clean grow room thoroughly before the flip.

---

## Week 1-2 Flower: Early Flower (The Flip)

**Feeding**: Pre-amended soil carries through early flower. At week 2 flower, top-dress: 1/2 cup bone meal (P), 1/2 cup kelp meal (K + micronutrients), 1/4 cup oyster shell flour (Ca) per plant. Compost tea at week 2.

**Environment**:
- Light: Switch to 12/12. Shift spectrum toward red-heavy (50-70% red). PPFD 800-1000 umol/m2/s ramping up. Complete darkness during off period -- no light leaks.
- Temp: 68-80F day / 60-68F night (cooler nights boost terpenes)
- Humidity: 50% and dropping
- VPD: 1.0-1.2 kPa

**What to watch for**:
- Stretch phase: indicas +50-100%, sativas +200-300% -- adjust light height daily
- Hermaphroditism (bananas) from light leaks or stress -- remove immediately
- Nutrient demands shifting from N to P/K
- Leaves yellowing rapidly (could indicate N deficiency if too early)

**Tasks**: Last chance for defoliation (remove up to 20% of large fan leaves blocking bud sites). Stop all training after week 1. Support heavy branches with bamboo stakes or trellis netting. Add carbon filter if not already running.

---

## Week 3-6 Flower: Mid Flower

**Feeding**: Top-dress at week 4-5 with langbeinite (1/2 cup) + oyster shell flour (1/4 cup). Compost tea every 1-2 weeks. Stop N-rich inputs after week 5. [[lactic-acid-bacteria-lab]] foliar through week 6. Optional: [[water-soluble-calcium-wca]] foliar for Ca boost.

**Environment**:
- Temp: 68-78F day / 60-68F night
- Humidity: 40-50% (lower to prevent bud rot)
- Light: PPFD 800-1200 umol/m2/s, red-heavy, add low UV for trichome production
- VPD: 1.0-1.5 kPa

**What to watch for**:
- Bud rot (botrytis) -- brown mushy spots inside dense buds, especially in humid conditions
- [[knf-calcium-deficiency-symptoms]] (distorted new growth) -- add oyster shell or gypsum
- Tip burn from nutrient excess -- flush with plain water
- Strong aromas developing -- terpene production at peak
- Trichomes becoming visible -- monitor with loupe at 60-100x

**Tasks**: Stop all defoliation. Continue branch support as buds gain weight. Monitor for spider mites (peak pressure in warm conditions). Maintain strict dark period. Check carbon filter for saturation.

---

## Week 7-9 Flower: Late Flower / Flush

**Feeding**: Reduce all inputs. In living soil, simply reduce watering slightly -- no formal flush needed. Stop compost tea. If using bottled nutrients, flush with plain water for 7-14 days.
