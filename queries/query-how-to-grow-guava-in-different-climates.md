## Planting Instructions

1. Dig a hole 2× the width and equal depth of the root ball.
2. Loosen the sides and bottom of the hole to prevent root circling.
3. Place the tree so the root flare is level with or slightly above the soil line.
4. Backfill with native soil mixed with 20% compost; firm gently — do not compact.
5. Build a 3-inch berm around the drip line to retain water.
6. Apply 3–4 inches of mulch, keeping it 3 inches away from the trunk.
7. Water thoroughly at planting, then every 2–3 days for the first month.
