## What Are Mycelium Materials and How Are They Made?

## Quick Answer

Mycelium materials are products made by growing fungal mycelium (the root-like network of fungi) on organic substrates like agricultural waste. As the mycelium grows, it binds the substrate into a solid, self-supporting material that can be shaped, dried, and finished into everything from packaging foam and building insulation to leather alternatives and furniture. The process uses living organisms as tiny factories — no petroleum, no high-energy chemical synthesis, and the finished products are fully biodegradable.
