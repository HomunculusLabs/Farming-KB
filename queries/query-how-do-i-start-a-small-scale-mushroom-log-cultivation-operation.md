# How Do I Start a Small-Scale Mushroom Log Cultivation Operation?

A practical guide for beginners looking to [[query-what-are-the-best-mushroom-species-for-beginners]] risk as wild fungi colonize.
- Fresh-cut logs should have intact bark — do not peel or damage it.

## Yield Expectations

- **Shiitake:** 0.5–1.5 lbs per log/year at peak; ~2–4 lbs total over productive life.
- **Oyster:** 0.75–2 lbs per log/year; faster production but shorter log life.
- **100-log operation estimate:** 50–150 lbs/year mixed species.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using old or seasoned wood** — mycelium cannot compete with established wild fungi.
- **Waiting too long to inoculate** after cutting — aim for under 3 weeks.
- **Overcrowding holes** — spacing too close wastes spawn and slows colonization.
- **Poor wax sealing** — any gap invites competitor molds (Trichoderma, Hypoxylon).
- **Placing logs in full sun or standing water** — extremes kill mycelium.
- **Skipping soaking during droughts** — logs dry out and mycelium dies back.
- **Wrong spawn strain for your climate** — match spawn to your USDA hardiness zone.
- **Contaminated spawn** — always buy from reputable suppliers.
