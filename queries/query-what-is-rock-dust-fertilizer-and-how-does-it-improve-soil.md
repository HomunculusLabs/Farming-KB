# What Is Rock Dust Fertilizer and How Does It Improve Soil?

## Short Answer
Rock dust is finely ground rock powder sourced from volcanic basalt, granite, azomite, or
glacial deposits that contains a broad spectrum of minerals and trace elements. When
applied to soil, it slowly releases these minerals through weathering, replenishing
depleted soils and improving plant nutrition, microbial activity, and overall soil
structure. It is a long-term soil amendment rather than a quick-fix fertilizer.
