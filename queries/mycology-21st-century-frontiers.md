# 21st Century Mycology Frontiers

Modern mycology from genomics to conservation, covering the scientific advances, ecological insights, and applied mycology that define the current era. See [[history-of-mycology-timeline]] for the complete arc from prehistoric observations through the 20th century revolution.

## Science Communication and Popularization

- **2000s — Nicholas Money's science communication:**
  [[nicholas-money]] publishes Mr. Bloomfield's Orchard and other works
  popularizing fungal biology.
  Research on spore ballistics reveals that ballistospores
  launch at 100-20,000g acceleration.
  See [[fungal-spore-biology]].

- **2005 — Paul Stamets:**
  Published Mycelium Running, articulating the mycorestoration framework
  (mycoremediation, mycofiltration, mycoforestry, mycopesticides).
  Links mushroom cultivation to ecological restoration.
  See [[mycorestoration-overview]].

## Mycorrhizal Networks and Soil Biology

- **~2010s — Suzanne Simard:**
  "Wood wide web" research at the University of British Columbia
  demonstrates that mycorrhizal networks connect trees in forests,
  enabling nutrient sharing, chemical signaling, and communication.
  Mother trees preferentially feed their offspring through common mycorrhizal networks.
  See [[mycorrhizal-networks]].

- **2010s — Elaine Ingham's soil food web:**
  [[elaine-ingham]] popularizes the soil food web framework,
  demonstrating the critical role of fungi in soil health and plant nutrition.
  See [[soil-food-web]].

## Fungal Genomics Revolution

- **2010s — DNA phylogenetics and genome sequencing:**
  Reveal metabolic diversity,
  correct long-standing taxonomic errors, and elucidate biosynthetic pathways
  including psilocybin production.
  Molecular phylogeny establishes that fungi diverged from animals
  ~1.0-1.5 billion years ago.
  See [[fungal-genetics]] and [[fungal-evolution]].

## Applied Mycology and Industry

- **2010s — Mycelium materials industry:**
  Companies (Ecovative, Bolt Threads, Mylo) grow mycelium as packaging,
  leather substitutes, and building materials — a new sector of the bioeconomy.

- **2010s — Mycoremediation deployment:**
  Fungi deployed for oil spill cleanup,
  heavy metal sequestration,
  PAH degradation,
  and PCB/dioxin breakdown.
  See [[mycoremediation-basics]].

- **2010s — Medicinal mushroom clinical trials:**
  Research on turkey tail (PSK/schizophyllan for cancer immune support),
  lion's mane (erinacines for neuroprotection),
  reishi (ganoderic acids for anti-inflammatory effects).
  See [[medicinal-mushroom-guide]].

## Conservation and Deep Time

- **2020s — Oldest fungal fossil debate:**
  Ordovician-period fossils (~460 MYA) from Wisconsin and Sweden
  are the oldest widely accepted fungal fossils.
  Controversial Precambrian finds (~2.4 billion years ago)
  suggest fungi may be far older.
  See [[fungal-evolution]].

- **2020s — Conservation concern:**
  European mycorrhizal mushroom diversity has fallen over 50%
  in recent decades.
  Fungi increasingly recognized as indicator species
  of ecosystem health and bioindicators of soil quality.
  See [[fungal-biodiversity]].
