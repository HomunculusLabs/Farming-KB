# Psychedelic Research Renaissance (2000-Present)

The modern revival of psychedelic science from the first clinical trials through rescheduling efforts and mainstream acceptance. See [[psychedelic-research-timeline]] for the complete arc from 1938 through prohibition, and [[psychedelic-therapy-history]] for detailed therapy history.

## First Modern Clinical Trials (2000-2010)

- **2000s — First modern MDMA-PTSD trials:**
  MAPS sponsors the first clinical trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD.
  Initial results show significant symptom reduction.

- **2006 — Griffiths psilocybin landmark study:**
  Roland Griffiths publishes study at Johns Hopkins demonstrating
  that psilocybin can produce mystical-type experiences
  with lasting positive effects on personality, well-being,
  and attitudes toward death.

- **2009 — Psilocybin neuroimaging begins:**
  First modern fMRI studies of psilocybin's effects on brain activity
  begin at Imperial College London under Robin Carhart-Harris,
  revealing decreased activity in the default mode network.

- **2011 — Psilocybin cancer anxiety studies:**
  Johns Hopkins and NYU publish first modern studies of psilocybin
  for cancer-related anxiety and depression,
  showing significant reductions in death anxiety and depression.

- **2014 — First modern LSD study:**
  Peter Gasser publishes study of LSD-assisted psychotherapy
  for anxiety in terminal illness in Switzerland,
  showing positive trends.

## Breakthrough Therapy Era (2017-2020)

- **2017 — FDA Breakthrough Therapy for MDMA:**
  FDA grants Breakthrough Therapy designation to MDMA for PTSD
  (MAPS-sponsored Phase 3 trials).
  This designation accelerates regulatory review
  based on preliminary evidence of substantial improvement
  over existing therapies.

- **2018-2019 — FDA Breakthrough Therapy for psilocybin:**
  Compass Pathways and Usona Institute receive
  Breakthrough Therapy designations for psilocybin
  in treatment-resistant depression.

- **2020 — Oregon Measure 109:**
  First US state to legalize psilocybin-assisted therapy
  through ballot initiative.
  Creates regulated psilocybin services program.

- **2020 — Oregon Measure 110:**
  Decriminalizes possession of small amounts of all drugs including psilocybin.

- **2022 — Colorado Proposition 122:**
  Second state to establish regulated psychedelic therapy program,
  covering psilocybin, MDMA, DMT, mescaline, and ibogaine.

- **2023 — Australia reschedules:**
  First country to reschedule psilocybin and MDMA
  for therapeutic use by authorized psychiatrists.
  See [[psychedelic-therapy-history]].

## Current Research and Future Directions (2024-Present)

- **2024-2026 — Ongoing clinical trials:**
  Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials globally for psilocybin
  (depression, substance use, end-of-life anxiety),
  MDMA (PTSD), ibogaine (opioid use disorder), and 5-MeO-DMT.
  Esketamine (Spravato) already FDA-approved
  for treatment-resistant depression.

- **Research frontiers:**
  - Microdosing studies examining sub-threshold doses for cognitive enhancement
  - Combination therapy protocols pairing psychedelics with psychotherapy
  - Biomarker research identifying who responds best to treatment
  - Long-term follow-up studies tracking sustained benefits
  - New compound development targeting specific receptor subtypes

- **Institutional expansion:**
  - Psychedelic research centers at major universities worldwide
  - Training programs for psychedelic-assisted therapy practitioners
  - Pharmaceutical company investment in psychedelic drug development
  - Integration into mainstream psychiatric practice
