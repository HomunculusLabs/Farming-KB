# How Do I Fix Calcium Deficiency in Plants?

## Short Answer
Calcium deficiency causes distorted new growth, curling leaves,
brown spots, and tip burn in cannabis and other plants. Fix it by
ensuring soil pH is in the correct range (6.0 to 7.0 for soil,
5.5 to 6.2 for hydroponics), applying calcium-rich amendments
like limestone, gypsum, or cal-mag supplements, and improving
soil biology to enhance calcium availability. The root cause is
often pH-related nutrient lockout rather than actual calcium
deficiency.

## Detailed Explanation

Calcium is an essential secondary macronutrient that plays a
critical role in cell wall structure, membrane integrity, and
cell division. Unlike mobile nutrients like nitrogen, calcium is
immobile in plants, meaning it cannot be relocated from older
tissues to new growth. This is why calcium deficiency symptoms
always appear first on the newest, actively growing parts of the
plant: emerging leaves, shoot tips, and root tips.

In the short term, foliar sprays of calcium nitrate or calcium

composting, mulching, and avoiding excessive tillage improves

deficiency recurring.
