## What Is a Cannabis Mother Plant and How Do I Maintain One?

## Short Answer
A cannabis mother plant is a mature female kept in a perpetual vegetative state
specifically for taking cuttings (clones). By maintaining a mother, growers can
preserve exact genetic copies of their favorite strain indefinitely, ensuring
consistent quality, yield, and cannabinoid profiles across every harvest cycle.

A mother plant is the backbone of any serious cloning operation. Rather than
buying seeds every grow cycle or risking genetic drift through repeated seed
cultivation, a single well-maintained mother can provide hundreds of genetically
identical clones over several years. This is especially valuable for commercial
growers and dedicated hobbyists who want predictable results from every cycle.
