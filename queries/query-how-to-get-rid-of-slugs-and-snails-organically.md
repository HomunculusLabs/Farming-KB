## Key Points
- Use beer traps, copper barriers, and diatomaceous earth as physical and mechanical controls
- Handpick at night with a flashlight for immediate population reduction over 1 to 2 weeks
- Attract natural predators like ground beetles, frogs, and toads by providing habitat and avoiding pesticides
- Modify habitat by removing debris, reducing mulch near vulnerable plants, and watering in the morning
- Iron phosphate baits (Sluggo) are an effective organic option safe around pets, wildlife, and edible crops
