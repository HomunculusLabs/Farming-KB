## What Is the Best Light Schedule for Cannabis?

## Short Answer

Photoperiod cannabis plants need 18 hours of light and 6 hours of darkness during the [[cannabis-light-spectrum-and-color-bands]] matters as much as duration, with blue light favoring [[cannabis-vegetative-stage]], the standard recommendation is an 18/6 schedule:
- 18 hours of light
- 6 hours of uninterrupted darkness

This gives plants enough energy to:
- Build a robust root system
- Develop strong stems and branching
- Produce abundant foliage for photosynthesis

Some growers run 24 hours of continuous light during early veg to maximize growth rate, but most experienced cultivators prefer including a dark period.

The dark period allows plants to:
- Rest and perform root respiration
- Metabolize stored sugars
- Develop stronger cellular structures

## Vegetative Stage Management

The [[green-cannabis-grow-bible-flowering-phase-and-harvest]] events:
1. **Stretch phase** (weeks 1-3) - plants may double in height
2. **Bud formation** (weeks 3-6) - calyxes and pistils develop
3. **Ripening** (weeks 6-10+) - trichomes turn cloudy and amber

It is essential to maintain absolute darkness during the 12-hour off period.

Even brief light leaks can cause:
- Stress responses and slower flowering
- Hermaphroditism in sensitive genetics
- Reduced yield and lower potency
- Re-vegging if light leaks are severe

## Light Spectrum Considerations

Light spectrum interacts with photoperiod in important ways.

During vegetative growth:
- Blue-dominant spectrum (400-500nm) is preferred
- Encourages shorter internodal spacing
- Promotes compact branching
- Supports vigorous leaf production

During flowering:
- Red-dominant spectrum (620-700nm) promotes bud development
- Supports resin and terpene production
- Enhances flower density and size
