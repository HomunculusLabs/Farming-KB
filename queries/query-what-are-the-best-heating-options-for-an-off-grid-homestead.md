## Heating System Sizing Rules of Thumb

### Estimating Heat Load

1. **Calculate square footage** of the space to be heated.
2. **Adjust for climate**: Multiply by the heating degree-days factor for your zone:
   - Zone 3–4 (very cold): 50–60 BTU/sq ft
   - Zone 5–6 (cold): 40–50 BTU/sq ft
   - Zone 7 (mild cold): 30–40 BTU/sq ft
   - Zone 8+ (mild): 20–30 BTU/sq ft
3. **Adjust for insulation quality**:
   - Poor (old, uninsulated): +30%
   - Average: baseline
   - Good (R-30+ walls, R-50+ ceiling): -30%
   - Excellent (Passive House level): -50%
**Example**: A 1,200 sq ft home in Zone 5 with average insulation:
1,200 × 45 BTU = 54,000 BTU/hr peak heating load
