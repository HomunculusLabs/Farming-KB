## How Do Fungi Contribute to Carbon Sequestration?

## Short Answer
Fungi sequester carbon through multiple pathways including mycorrhizal
networks that transfer plant carbon into stable soil organic matter, production
of glomalin and other recalcitrant compounds that resist decomposition, and
direct incorporation of atmospheric carbon into fungal biomass. Mycorrhizal
fungi alone are estimated to store over 13 gigatons of carbon globally in
soils.
