# What Are the Stages of Mushroom Cultivation?

## Short Answer
Mushroom cultivation follows a multi-stage process that begins with obtaining
a clean culture, progresses through expanding that culture onto grain spawn,
inoculating a bulk substrate, inducing fruiting conditions, and finally
harvesting and storing the crop. Each stage requires specific sterile
techniques and environmental conditions, and mastering the fundamentals of
hygiene and timing at every step is the key to consistent, contamination-free
results.
