### Stem and Veil Characteristics
The stem (stipe) of Psilocybe cubensis is 4 to 15 centimeters long and 0.5
to 1.5 centimeters thick. It is typically white to yellowish and often has a
persistent partial veil that leaves a membranous ring (annulus) on the upper
stem. The stem is hollow and fibrous, and like the cap, bruises prominently
blue when handled. For deeper taxonomic understanding, the
[[psilocybin-mushroom-identification]] resource adds
that no single feature should be used in isolation. A reliable identification
requires a combination of all of the following:
- **Habitat confirmation:** Growth on dung in tropical grasslands
- **Blue bruising reaction:** Present on both cap and stem when damaged
- **Spore print color:** Dark purplish-brown to jet black
- **Veil ring:** Membranous annulus present on upper stem
- **Gill color progression:** Gray when young, darkening with maturity
If any of these features do not match, do not consume the mushroom. When in
doubt, throw it out.
