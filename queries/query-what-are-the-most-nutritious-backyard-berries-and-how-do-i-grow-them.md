- **Planting:** Space 6–8 ft apart in moist, fertile soil; full sun to partial shade.

- **Harvest:** August–September; harvest whole flower umbels or berry clusters.

## Regional Recommendations

### Cold Climates (Zone 3–4)

- **Best choices:** Haskap (zone 2!), aronia, elderberry, black currant, sea buckthorn.
- These berries are proven performers in harsh winters with minimal protection.
- Haskap is the standout — it survives to -40°F and fruits earlier than strawberries.

### Warm Climates (Zone 8–9)

- **Best choices:** Goji berry, blackberry, mulberry, rabbiteye blueberry, elderberry.
- Goji and mulberry handle heat and drought with minimal irrigation.
- Rabbiteye blueberries outperform highbush varieties in warm, humid climates.

### Small Spaces & Containers

- **Best choices:** Blueberry (dwarf varieties), haskap, aronia, raspberry (compact varieties).
- Use 15–20 gallon pots with well-draining [[solomon-potting-mix-and-seedling-soil-recipe]].
- Blueberries thrive in containers where pH is easier to control with peat-based mixes.

### Shade Tolerance

- **Best choices:** Elderberry, aronia, black currant.
- These tolerate 4–6 hours of sun; all others need full sun (6+ hours) for best production.
- Elderberry also doubles as a privacy hedge or windbreak in partially shaded sites.
