## Duck Species Selection

Choosing the right duck breed is essential for system success. Khaki Campbells
are the top choice for egg production (250-340 eggs per year), are excellent
foragers, and are relatively small and docile for easy management. Indian
Runners are the most efficient active foragers, spending hours grazing on
insects and vegetation, but they are less calm and can be flighty in
open spaces. Muscovy ducks are excellent for meat production, are quiet
(they do not quack), and are the best fliers, requiring wing clipping or
covered enclosures. They also consume large quantities of insects, including
mosquitoes, making them valuable for pest management.

## Predator Protection and Maintenance

Duck ponds attract predators including raccoons, foxes, herons, owls, and
domestic dogs. A secure night shelter is essential, and the pond area should
be fenced with hardware cloth buried at least 12 inches underground to prevent
digging. Floating cover (large sheets of Styrofoam or floating platforms)
provides ducks with refuge from aerial predators while on the water. Regular
maintenance includes removing excess vegetation to prevent the pond from
filling in, monitoring water quality during hot summer months, and periodically
harvesting fast-growing aquatic plants to maintain open water surface area.
