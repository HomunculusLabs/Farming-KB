## Practical Guidance for Selection

When choosing cannabis for specific purposes, consumers should focus on
chemotype data rather than the indica/sativa label. Look for products with
available certificate of analysis (COA) testing that reports cannabinoid
percentages and terpene profiles. For relaxation and sleep, seek strains high
in myrcene and linalool with balanced THC/CBD ratios. For energy and focus,
look for strains dominant in limonene, pinene, and terpinolene. For pain
relief, caryophyllene-dominant strains with higher THC may be most effective,
as caryophyllene is a selective CB2 receptor agonist that provides
anti-inflammatory benefits without psychoactivity.
