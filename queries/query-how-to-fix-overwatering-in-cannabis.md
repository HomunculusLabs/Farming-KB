## Detailed Explanation
Overwatering does not mean giving too much water at once. It means watering too
frequently, which deprives the root zone of the oxygen that roots need to
function. Cannabis roots are aerobic organisms that require air pockets in the
growing medium to breathe. When soil remains constantly saturated, water
displaces oxygen in the pore spaces, and roots begin to suffocate. This is why
overwatered plants often show symptoms that mimic underwatering: the drooping,
wilting leaves are actually a sign that roots cannot take up water efficiently
because they are drowning.

The earliest signs of overwatering are subtle. Leaves may appear slightly droopy
even though the soil is wet, and new growth will be pale green or yellowish. As
the condition worsens, older fan leaves begin to turn yellow from the center
