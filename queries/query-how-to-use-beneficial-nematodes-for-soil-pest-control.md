## How to Use Beneficial Nematodes for Soil Pest Control

## What Are Beneficial Nematodes?

Beneficial nematodes are microscopic, unsegmented roundworms that live in soil and actively hunt down insect pests in their larval or pupal stages. They are among the most effective [[cervantes-nematodes-root-maggots-soil-pests]]** — cabbage, onion, carrot, and seed corn maggots
- **Grubs** — Japanese beetle, chafer, and other white grub species
- **Wireworms** — click beetle larvae that damage root crops
- **Cutworms** — larvae that sever seedlings at the soil line
- **Thrips pupae** — the soil-dwelling pupal stage
- **Weevils** — black vine weevil, strawberry root weevil
- **Flea beetle larvae** — soil-dwelling stage before emergence

They are less effective against pests that spend most of their life cycle above ground.
