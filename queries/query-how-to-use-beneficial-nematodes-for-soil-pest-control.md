# How to Use Beneficial Nematodes for Soil Pest Control

## What Are Beneficial Nematodes?

Beneficial nematodes are microscopic, unsegmented roundworms that live in soil and actively hunt down insect pests in their larval or pupal stages. They are among the most effective biological control agents available to organic growers and homesteaders.

Unlike plant-parasitic nematodes (such as root-knot nematodes), entomopathogenic (insect-killing) nematodes are completely harmless to plants, earthworms, humans, and pets. The two most commonly used genera are **Steinernema** and **Heterorhabditis**, each carrying a symbiotic bacterium in their gut. When a nematode enters a host insect, it releases the bacteria, which multiply rapidly and kill the pest within 24 to 48 hours. The nematodes then reproduce inside the cadaver and emerge to seek new hosts.

## Which Pests Do They Control?

Beneficial nematodes are effective against a wide range of soil-dwelling pests:

- **Fungus gnat larvae** — the most common greenhouse and indoor grow target
- **Root maggots** — cabbage, onion, carrot, and seed corn maggots
- **Grubs** — Japanese beetle, chafer, and other white grub species
- **Wireworms** — click beetle larvae that damage root crops
- **Cutworms** — larvae that sever seedlings at the soil line
- **Thrips pupae** — the soil-dwelling pupal stage
- **Weevils** — black vine weevil, strawberry root weevil
- **Flea beetle larvae** — soil-dwelling stage before emergence

They are less effective against pests that spend most of their life cycle above ground.

## Choosing the Right Nematode Species

Not all nematode species target the same pests. Selecting the correct species is critical:

| Species | Best For | Behavior |
|---------|----------|----------|
| *Steinernema feltiae* | Fungus gnats, thrips pupae | Ambusher; works at cooler temps (50-68°F) |
| *Steinernema carpocapsae* | Cutworms, armyworms, flea beetles | Ambusher; waits near surface |
| *Heterorhabditis bacteriophora* | Grubs, wireworms | Cruiser; searches deeper soil; needs warmth (68-86°F) |
| *Steinernema kraussei* | Vine weevils | Effective at lower temperatures |

For broad-spectrum control, blend *S. carpocapsae* and *H. bacteriophora*. For greenhouse fungus gnats, *S. feltiae* is the standard.

## Purchasing and Storage

Nematodes are sold as live organisms on inert carriers (sponge, vermiculite, clay). They arrive dormant and must be kept refrigerated (38-42°F / 3-6°C) until use.

- **Order timing:** Apply within 1 to 2 weeks of delivery. Shelf life is typically 2 to 4 weeks refrigerated
- **Check viability:** Suppliers list expiration date and concentration (millions of infective juveniles per package)
- **Never freeze** or store at room temperature for more than a few hours

## Preparing for Application

### Soil Temperature

Each species has an optimal range. Applying outside it results in poor control:

- *S. feltiae*: 50-68°F (10-20°C), active down to 45°F
- *S. carpocapsae*: 60-85°F (15-29°C)
- *H. bacteriophora*: 68-86°F (20-30°C)

Use a soil thermometer at 2 to 4 inches depth. Do not guess from air temperature.

### Soil Moisture

Nematodes need a film of water to move through soil pores. Apply when soil is moist but not waterlogged:

- Water the area thoroughly 24 hours before application
- Keep the soil surface moist for at least 7 to 10 days after application
- In sandy soils, water lightly once or twice daily to maintain the active zone

### Timing

- **Evening or overcast days** — UV light kills nematodes in minutes
- **After light rain** is ideal — soil is moist and pests may be near the surface
- **Avoid heavy rain** — runoff washes nematodes away

## Step-by-Step Application

### Step 1: Activate the Nematodes

1. Remove from refrigerator, let sit at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes
2. Empty the carrier into a bucket of **non-chlorinated water** (let tap water sit open 24 hours, or use rainwater)
3. Use roughly 1 gallon per 5 to 10 million nematodes (follow supplier ratios)
4. Stir gently for 1 to 2 minutes — do not whisk aggressively
5. Let sit 5 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally to keep nematodes suspended

### Step 2: Apply

- Use a **watering can with a coarse rose** for small areas (fine nozzles damage nematodes physically)
- For larger areas, use a hose-end sprayer or backpack sprayer with filters removed (nothing finer than 50 mesh / 300 microns)
- Apply evenly at the recommended rate, agitating the bucket periodically
- For potted plants, drench until runoff appears at drainage holes

### Step 3: Post-Application Care

- Water lightly to wash nematodes into the top 1 to 2 inches of soil
- Keep soil surface moist for 7 to 10 days
- Avoid chemical pesticides, copper fungicides, and sulfur during this window
- Avoid heavy tillage for 2 weeks

## Application Rates

| Target Area | Rate | Frequency |
|-------------|------|-----------|
| Potted plants | 1-5 million per plant | Every 2 to 4 weeks |
| Raised beds (100 sq ft) | 5-10 million | Every 4 to 6 weeks |
| Garden plots (1,000 sq ft) | 25-50 million | 2 to 3 times per season |
| Lawns (5,000 sq ft) | 125-250 million | Spring and fall |

## Compatibility

**Safe to combine with:** [[mycorrhiza]]l inoculants, compost tea (on alternate days), BTi drenches, and beneficial fungi like *Trichoderma*.

**Avoid combining with:** Synthetic insecticides, copper-based fungicides, sulfur, high-nitrogen synthetic fertilizers, and soil solarization.

## Fungus Gnat Control for Indoor Growers

*Steinernema feltiae* is the preferred species for container growing because it thrives at moderate temperatures and moist conditions:

1. Apply 5 million per 100 square feet as a soil drench when introducing new plants
2. Reapply every 2 to 3 weeks during vegetative growth
3. Monitor adults with yellow sticky traps — increase frequency when counts rise
4. Combine with BTi drenches on alternating weeks for layered control
5. Allow the top inch of soil to dry between waterings to disrupt the gnat life cycle

## Troubleshooting Poor Results

- **Soil too dry:** Top 2 inches must stay moist for at least a week after application
- **Full sun application:** UV kills nematodes in minutes — apply at dusk
- **Chlorinated water:** Always dechlorinate or use alternative water sources
- **Wrong species:** *S. feltiae* will not control grubs — match species to pest
- **Expired product:** Check use-by date; old product has declining viability
- **Chemical residues:** Wait 2 weeks after any chemical pesticide or fertilizer application
