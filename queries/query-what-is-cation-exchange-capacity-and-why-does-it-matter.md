## Short Answer
Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC) is a measure of a soil's ability to hold and
exchange positively charged nutrient ions (cations) such as calcium, magnesium,
potassium, ammonium, and many micronutrients. Soils with higher CEC can retain
more nutrients against leaching and buffer against rapid pH changes, making them
