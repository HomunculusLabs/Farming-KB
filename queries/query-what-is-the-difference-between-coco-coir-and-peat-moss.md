# What Is the Difference Between Coco Coir and Peat Moss?

## Short Answer

Coco coir is a fibrous byproduct of coconut husk processing, while peat moss is partially decomposed sphagnum moss harvested from peat bogs. Both serve as soilless growing media with excellent water retention, but coco coir is renewable and has a near-neutral pH, whereas peat moss is acidic and its harvesting raises significant environmental concerns.
