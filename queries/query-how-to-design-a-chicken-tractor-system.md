## What Is a Chicken Tractor?
A chicken tractor is a portable coop, often built on a lightweight frame
with wheels or skids, that houses a small flock temporarily on a section
of ground. Unlike a fixed coop, it has no permanent floor, so birds
interact directly with the soil beneath them.
