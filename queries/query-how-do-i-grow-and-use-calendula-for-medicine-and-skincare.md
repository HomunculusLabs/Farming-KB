**Methods**:

**Quick method (stovetop)**:
1. Combine petals and oil in a double boiler or heat-safe jar set in a pot of simmering water.
2. Gently heat on low (40–50°C / 100–120°F) for 2–4 hours. Do not boil.
3. Strain through cheesecloth or a fine-mesh sieve. Squeeze out the petals.
4. Store in a dark glass bottle. Shelf life: 6–12 months.

**Slow method (solar infusion)**:
1. Pack a clean glass jar ⅔ full with dried petals.
2. Cover completely with oil, ensuring petals are submerged (add more oil if needed).
3. Cap tightly and place in a sunny window for 2–4 weeks.
4. Shake daily. Strain when the oil is deeply golden and fragrant.
5. Shelf life: 6–12 months.

### 2. Calendula Salve (All-Purpose Healing Balm)

**You need**: 1 cup calendula-infused oil, ¼ cup beeswax pastilles

1. Combine infused oil and beeswax in a double boiler or heat-safe jar.
2. Heat gently until beeswax is fully melted.
3. Test consistency: Place a few drops on a cold spoon. If too soft, add more beeswax (1 tsp at a time). If too hard, add more oil.
4. Pour into tins or small jars.
5. Let cool undisturbed until solid.
6. **Shelf life**: 12–18 months.

**Uses**: Minor cuts, scrapes, burns, rashes, dry/cracked skin, diaper rash, chapped lips, insect bites, mild sunburn, hemorrhoids.

### 3. Calendula Skin-Healing Cream (Lighter Than Salve)

**You need**: ½ cup calendula-infused oil, ¼ cup distilled water (or calendula tea), 2 tbsp emulsifying wax (e.g., Polawax or beeswax + borax), 5–10 drops vitamin E oil (preservative), optional: 10–15 drops lavender essential oil.

1. Heat oil and emulsifying wax together in one container until melted.
2. Heat the water/calendula tea in a separate container to the same temperature.
3. Slowly pour the water into the oil while whisking vigorously (or use an immersion blender).
4. Continue whisking as the mixture cools and thickens into a cream.
5. Add vitamin E and essential oils once below 40°C (100°F).
6. Spoon into jars.
7. **Shelf life**: 2–3 months (refrigerate to extend).

### 4. Calendula Tea (For Internal Use)

**For drinking**: Steep 1–2 tsp dried petals in 240 mL (8 oz) hot (not boiling) water for 10–15 minutes. Strain. Drink 2–3 cups daily.

**For compress**: Brew a strong tea (2–3 tbsp petals per cup), cool to comfortable temperature, soak a clean cloth, and apply to the affected area for 15–20 minutes.

**Uses**: Lymphatic support, digestive inflammation, menstrual cramp relief, sore throat gargle, mouthwash for gum inflammation.

### 5. Calendula Tincture

**You need**: Fresh or dried calendula petals, 80-proof (40%) vodka or brandy.

1. Fill a glass jar ½–⅔ full with dried petals (¾–full if using fresh).
2. Cover completely with alcohol, ensuring all plant material is submerged.
3. Cap tightly and store in a dark, cool place for 4–6 weeks.
4. Shake daily.
5. Strain through cheesecloth into amber dropper bottles.
6. **Dose**: 1–3 mL (20–60 drops) up to 3 times daily.
7. **Shelf life**: 3–5+ years.

## Using Calendula in Skincare

### Quick Reference: Which Preparation for What

| Condition | Best Preparation | How to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Minor cuts and scrapes | Salve or cream | Clean wound, apply thin layer, cover with bandage |
| Burns (minor) | Salve or cream | Apply after cooling the burn; reapply 2–3 times daily |
| Sunburn | Cream or tea compress | Cool compress first, then apply cream |
| Dry/cracked skin | Salve | Apply generously, cover with gloves/socks overnight |
| Diaper rash | Cream | Clean area, dry, apply thin layer at each change |
| Eczema/dermatitis | Cream or oil | Apply 2–3 times daily; combine with lavender oil |
| Acne | Oil (spot treatment) or tea compress | Anti-inflammatory; don't use heavy salve on acne-prone skin |
| Insect bites | Salve or tincture | Apply directly; tincture can be dabbed on neat |
| Radiation dermatitis | Cream (with doctor's knowledge) | Well-documented clinical use; apply 2–3 times daily |
| Aging skin | Oil or cream | Carotenoids provide antioxidant protection; apply nightly |
| Lip care | Salve | Apply as needed; combine with beeswax for extra protection |

### Pro Tips

- **Patch test** first: Apply a small amount to your inner wrist and wait 24 hours to check for allergic reaction, especially if you have Asteraceae (ragweed) allergies.
