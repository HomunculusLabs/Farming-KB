## Cannabis Legalization Era (2012-Present)

The end of prohibition: from Colorado and Washington's historic legalization votes through the Farm Bill, hemp revival, state-by-state legalization, and federal rescheduling efforts. See [[cannabis-prohibition-history]] for the complete arc from ancient use through the War on Drugs.

## State Legalization Begins (2012-2016)

- **2012 — Colorado and Washington legalize recreational use:**
  First states to legalize cannabis for adult recreational use
  through ballot initiatives.
  Both passed with approximately 55% support.
  Historic shift from prohibition to regulation and taxation.

- **2014 — Farm Bill (2014):**
  Section 7606 legalizes cultivation of industrial hemp
  (cannabis with <0.3% THC) for research purposes
  in states that have legalized it.

- **2014 — First legal recreational sales begin:**
  Colorado opens first retail cannabis stores on January 1, 2014.
  Washington follows in July 2014.
  Economic impact immediate and significant.

- **2016 — More states legalize:**
  California (Proposition 64), Massachusetts, Maine, and Nevada
  pass adult-use legalization.
  Over half of US states now have some form of legal cannabis.

## Hemp Revival and CBD (2018-2020)

- **2018 — Farm Bill 2018:**
  Legalizes industrial hemp (cannabis with <0.3% THC) nationwide.
  Removes hemp from the Controlled Substances Act entirely.
  Enables legal CBD production and hemp product sales across all 50 states.
  Major milestone separating hemp from marijuana in federal law.

- **2018 — CBD market explodes:**
  CBD products become widely available in retail stores nationwide.
  FDA approves Epidiolex (purified CBD) for treatment of seizures —
  the first FDA-approved cannabis-derived medicine.

- **2019-2020 — Further legalization wave:**
  Illinois legalizes recreational cannabis through legislation
  (first state to do so rather than ballot initiative).
  Michigan, Vermont, Arizona, Montana, New Jersey, South Dakota follow.

## Federal Rescheduling and International Shift (2020-Present)

- **2020 — UN reschedules cannabis:**
  UN Commission on Narcotic Drugs votes 27-25
  to remove cannabis from Schedule IV of the 1961 Single Convention,
  recognizing its medical value internationally.

- **2021-2022 — Further legalization:**
  New York, Virginia, Connecticut, New Mexico, Maryland, Rhode Island
  legalize adult-use cannabis.
  Over 20 states now have legal recreational cannabis.

- **2022 — Biden pardons federal cannabis convictions:**
  President Biden issues executive order pardoning all federal convictions
  for simple cannabis possession (~6,500 individuals).
  Directs HHS to review cannabis scheduling.

- **2023 — HHS rescheduling recommendation:**
  Department of Health and Human Services recommends
  moving cannabis from Schedule I to Schedule III
  based on scientific review.
  DEA begins formal rulemaking process.

- **2024 — Banking and expungement:**
  SAFE Banking Act passes the House multiple times but stalls in the Senate.
  State-level expungement programs continue to clear records.

- **2024-2026 — Rescheduling rulemaking:**
  DEA holds public hearings on rescheduling cannabis
  from Schedule I to Schedule III.
  If finalized, most significant change to federal cannabis policy since 1970.

- **Current federal status (2026):**
  Cannabis remains Schedule I at the federal level,
  but rescheduling to Schedule III is under active DEA rulemaking.
  24+ states and DC have legalized adult recreational use.
  All but a handful of states have medical or decriminalized cannabis.
  US cannabis market estimated at over $30 billion annually.
