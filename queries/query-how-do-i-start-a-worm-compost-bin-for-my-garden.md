# How Do I Start a Worm Compost Bin for My Garden?

## Short Answer
Starting a worm compost bin requires a container with drainage holes, bedding
material like shredded newspaper or coconut coir, red wiggler worms (Eisenia
fetida), and regular feeding with kitchen scraps. Keep the bin in a shaded
location between 55 and 77 degrees Fahrenheit, maintain moisture like a damp
sponge, and feed scraps in thin layers buried under the bedding. Finished
vermicompost is typically ready in 3 to 6 months.

coconut coir, and dried leaves all make excellent bedding. The bedding should be
moistened to the consistency of a damp sponge, wrung out so no water drips when
squeezed. Fill the bin with 6 to 8 inches of damp bedding before adding worms.
